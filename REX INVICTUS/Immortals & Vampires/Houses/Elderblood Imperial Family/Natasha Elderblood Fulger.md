Natasha Elderblood Fulger, also called Naty, is the former alternate personality of Annasthashia "Natashia" Elderblood Fulger who eventually developed into an entirely independent person. She originated from the spiritual and psychological abnormalities surrounding Natashia's damaged Soul, but after centuries of existing as a distinct consciousness and subsequently undergoing further Soul development, Natasha became capable of maintaining her own identity, Soul and eventually her own physical body.[2][3]
Natasha should not be confused with Natashia. Annasthashia/Natashia is the original Fulger woman and the biological mother of Sasha Elderblood Fulger and [[Angelina Elderblood]]. Natasha/Naty is a separate character after their separation and is the mother of [[Julia Elderblood]].

### Physical Appearance
Natasha is physically almost identical to Natashia, reflecting the fact that her first independent body was created from the existence she had previously shared with her. She is a tall and exceptionally beautiful woman with fair skin, long blonde hair and the distinctive features of the Fulger family. She stands approximately 183 centimeters tall.
Before becoming independent, Natasha naturally appeared through Natashia's body whenever she took control. After receiving a body of her own, she retained the same general appearance rather than becoming physically unrelated to her former host.[3]
As a Vampire, her natural blue eyes became red when drawing upon her Vampiric nature. Following her evolution into a True Dragon, her eyes developed the deeper Blood-red coloration characteristic of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Dragon family. Her later form additionally possesses the Draconic features of the Fulger branch, including golden Dragon horns and traits influenced by the Clan's Lightning-Spirit ancestry.[4]

### Personality
Natasha was historically regarded as the darker and more unstable personality that existed alongside Natashia. Whereas Natashia's resurfaced personality was shameless, energetic and openly affectionate, Natasha was far more ruthless, cruel, impulsive and willing to indulge destructive impulses.
During the period when Natasha was the dominant personality, she was responsible for much of the behavior that damaged the Fulger family's reputation. She was prone to gambling, treated those around her harshly and subjected Sasha to brutal training. Her existence was so dominant that outsiders simply believed this personality was Annasthashia herself until Natashia's original personality resurfaced during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s confrontation with the Fulger family.[5]
Despite being called the "evil twin", Natasha is not mindlessly malicious. She is highly perceptive, proud and capable of recognizing uncomfortable truths. When she and Natashia directly confronted each other internally, Natasha criticized Natashia's weaknesses and forced her to acknowledge parts of herself she had attempted to ignore.[6]
After receiving an independent Soul and body, Natasha became considerably more stable. The need to fight Natashia for control disappeared, allowing her personality to exist without constantly expressing itself through conflict. She retained her provocative humor, shamelessness, aggressive streak and love of causing trouble, but became much more openly affectionate toward the Fulger family and Victor's enormous household.
Natasha is particularly proud of the increasingly shameless personality Sasha developed over the years. Despite Sasha biologically belonging to Natashia, Natasha spent much of Sasha's childhood controlling the body of her mother and therefore possesses a complicated maternal attachment toward her. In the Imperial era, Natasha openly calls Sasha her "pride and joy" and celebrates how thoroughly she has adopted the eccentric personality associated with the Fulger women.
Unlike her older abusive behavior, modern Natasha is heavily invested in family life. She participates in the Elderblood household's gatherings, follows the development of the younger generation and has become protective of the children born into the dynasty.

### Synopsis

#### Vampire Society
Natasha initially existed as an alternate personality within Natashia. The woman known publicly as Annasthashia Fulger had lived for years with multiple personalities, although virtually nobody outside the family understood the extent of the division.
Natasha became the dominant personality for an extended period. While controlling Natashia's body, she developed the notorious reputation associated with Annasthashia during Sasha's childhood. Her recklessness, cruelty, gambling and poor decisions helped weaken Clan Fulger and severely damaged Sasha's relationship with the person she believed to be her mother.
During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s confrontation with Sasha's parents, the personality arrangement was finally exposed. Under the pressure of the battle and Victor's Blood, the original Natashia resurfaced. [[Scathach]] explained that the woman possessed two personalities, establishing Natasha and Natashia as distinct consciousnesses despite still inhabiting the same body.[5]
From that point forward Natasha remained inside their shared existence rather than disappearing.

#### A New Pillar
Natasha resurfaced internally during Natashia's battle against [[Niklaus Horseman]]. Inside their shared mental world, Natasha confronted Natashia directly.
The encounter demonstrated that Natasha was not simply an imaginary voice that could be discarded. She possessed her own thoughts, personality, memories and perspective and was capable of debating Natashia as an independent consciousness.[6]
Natasha also understood aspects of Natashia's mentality that Natashia herself refused to accept. Rather than allowing the original personality to completely suppress her, Natasha forced Natashia to confront their shared psychological problems.
Their relationship gradually shifted away from a simple battle for control. Both personalities remained distinct while functioning within the same Soul and body.

#### King Among Kings
Natasha's true nature was finally uncovered shortly before the Meeting of Supernatural Beings when Natashia suddenly suffered extreme spiritual pain.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] examined Natashia's Soul and discovered additional presences deep within it, including Natasha and [[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]].[2]
The condition of Natashia's Soul was connected to Carmila's desperate attempt to protect her daughter from the destructive consequences of the Fulger bloodline while Natashia was developing. The interference damaged and fractured the developing Soul. One part of those abnormalities would eventually develop into the personality known as Natasha.[2]
What had begun as a fragmented personality no longer remained merely psychological. Natasha had existed independently for too long and had undergone too much spiritual development. She had developed her own sense of self and a sufficiently complete Soul to be regarded as an individual.
Victor repaired the damaged spiritual structure rather than destroying Natasha. His intervention allowed Natasha to separate from Natashia and manifest through an independent body.[3]
For the first time Natasha no longer needed to share a body with her sister. She could speak, move and live entirely according to her own [[Will Solace|Will]].
The event also allowed Natasha, Natashia and Carmila to physically exist alongside one another rather than being trapped within one spiritual structure.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
With an independent body and Soul, Natasha became increasingly integrated into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family as herself rather than as a personality appearing through Natashia.
She pursued her own relationship with Victor Elderblood and eventually became one of his wives.[1]
Natasha was subsequently among the Fulger women affected by Victor's transformation of his family into [[True Dragons]]. Victor did not erase the abilities his wives already possessed when converting them. Their previous bloodlines and supernatural characteristics instead became foundations for their new Dragon forms.
For the Fulger family, this meant their hereditary Lightning abilities and ancient connection to Lightning Spirits evolved together with True Dragon physiology. Natasha consequently became a Lightning Spirit Dragon, preserving her elemental nature while gaining the vastly superior body, Soul and Energy of a True Dragon.[4]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Following the family's extensive development and the enormous periods experienced through accelerated Time, Natasha became fully established as an independent member of the Elderblood family.
Unlike Natashia, whose daughter with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is [[Angelina Elderblood]], Natasha eventually had her own daughter with Victor: [[Julia Elderblood]].
Julia inherited the Fulger family's blonde appearance and Victor's characteristic violet Dragon Eyes while strongly resembling her mother.

#### Those Who Rule Reality
During the rise of the [[Elderblood Empire]], Natasha continued acting independently from Natashia. Both women could simultaneously participate in the affairs of the Fulger branch, illustrating how complete Natasha's separation had become.
Natasha, Natashia, Carmila, Victoria and Sasha remained closely connected as the senior Fulger women within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family and increasingly participated in the management of the Empire's enormous civilization.[7]
Natasha also became a mother in her own right through [[Julia Elderblood]]. Julia is Natasha's daughter, while [[Angelina Elderblood]] belongs to Natashia. The two maternal lines should therefore not be merged.
Natasha remained heavily involved with the rest of the family as Victor's daughters grew into increasingly powerful Dragon Goddesses. Despite no longer being Sasha's literal mother after becoming a separate individual, Natasha continued to display strong maternal pride toward her due to having spent a substantial portion of Sasha's childhood occupying the maternal position.
By the later Imperial era, Natasha had transformed from an unstable personality trapped within another woman's body into an independent True Dragon, wife, mother and senior member of both the Fulger and Elderblood families.

### Skills & Abilities
Natasha originally developed while sharing Natashia's body and consequently inherited a supernatural foundation derived from the Fulger bloodline when she became independent. This does not mean every later feat, technique or development belonging specifically to Natashia should be attributed to Natasha. The abilities listed below concern powers Natasha retained or acquired as an independent entity.

#### True Dragon Physiology
Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s large-scale transformation of his family, Natasha became a True Dragon whose existence incorporated both Victor's Blood Dragon lineage and her pre-existing Fulger characteristics.[4]
- Blood Dragon Physiology: Natasha possesses the superior physiology of Victor's Dragon lineage, drastically surpassing her former Noble Vampire body in physical ability, Energy, regeneration and longevity.[4]
- Supernatural Strength: Her Dragon body possesses physical power far beyond that available to an ordinary Vampire.
- Supernatural Speed: Her natural Draconic speed combines particularly well with the Fulger family's Lightning-based acceleration.
- Supernatural Durability: Natasha can withstand physical and supernatural forces that would destroy considerably weaker supernatural beings.
- Enhanced Reflexes: Her reactions are sufficient to accompany her Lightning-enhanced movement.
- Enhanced Agility: Her True Dragon body possesses exceptionally precise physical control even at extreme movement speeds.
- Enhanced Senses: Draconic physiology grants senses far superior to her former Vampire body.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Natasha can recover from severe bodily damage through the vitality and Energy of a True Dragon.
- [[Immortality]]: Natasha possesses the extreme longevity and ageless nature associated with Victor's [[True Dragons]].
- Shapeshifting: Her Draconic nature permits alteration between humanoid and more overtly Draconic forms.
- Size Manipulation: Like other True Dragons, her full Draconic form is not limited to ordinary humanoid proportions.
- Dragon Breath: Natasha can release destructive Energy through the breath-based attacks natural to Dragons.
- [[Draconic Energy]]: Her True Dragon body possesses the powerful Energy system required to sustain Draconic abilities and enormously amplify her previous Fulger powers.[4]
- [[Dragon Heart]]: Natasha possesses the powerful internal source characteristic of True Dragons, supplying enormous quantities of Energy throughout her body.
- Dragon Soul: Her Soul was elevated alongside her body during her transformation, allowing it to support the supernatural burden of True Dragon existence.

#### Lightning Spirit Physiology
The Fulger bloodline descends from a Lightning-Spirit lineage. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Dragon transformation did not remove these characteristics; it elevated them into the foundation of Natasha's individual Dragon species.[4]
- Lightning Spirit Dragon: Natasha's True Dragon form incorporates the elemental nature inherited from the Fulger family's Lightning-Spirit ancestry.[4]
- Elemental Physiology: Natasha can express characteristics closer to living Lightning rather than remaining entirely dependent upon conventional biological matter.
- Spirit Transformation: Like the main-line Fulger women, Natasha can transform into a Lightning-Spirit state, converting her body toward its ancestral elemental nature.[4]
- Elemental Transformation: Natasha can shift portions or the entirety of her body toward Lightning.
- Elemental Intangibility: Transforming toward an elemental state makes conventional physical interaction considerably less effective against her.
- Elemental Movement: Natasha can use her Lightning nature to move without being restricted to ordinary biological locomotion.
- Lightning Resistance: Her evolved physiology possesses exceptional compatibility with electrical Energy, eliminating the severe self-damage that historically limited members of Clan Fulger.
- Electricity Resistance: Conventional electrical effects are drastically less effective against a being whose own nature is based around supernatural Lightning.

#### Electrokinesis
Natasha possesses the hereditary elemental ability of Clan Fulger, allowing her to generate and manipulate supernatural Lightning.
- Lightning Generation: Natasha can produce Lightning directly from her own Energy.
- Lightning Manipulation: She can freely direct and reshape generated electrical Energy.
- Electricity Manipulation: Her control extends to electrical currents and electrically charged phenomena.
- Lightning Aura: Natasha can surround her body with Lightning, turning proximity itself into a danger.
- Lightning Infusion: She can coat physical attacks with Lightning to increase their destructive potential.
- Lightning Empowerment: Lightning can be circulated through her body to enhance movement and combat performance.
- Lightning Acceleration: Natasha can explosively accelerate by combining physical movement with her elemental ability.
- Lightning-Speed Movement: Her ability allows her to achieve extraordinary movement speeds characteristic of the Fulger bloodline.
- Electrical Discharge: Natasha can release concentrated electrical Energy outward as ranged attacks.
- Area Lightning Attacks: Her control allows Lightning to be spread across a wider battlefield rather than being limited to direct-contact attacks.
- Draconic Lightning: Following her evolution, the same Fulger element is supported by a True Dragon's body and Energy, greatly increasing its overall potential.[4]

#### Time Difference
- Time Difference: Natasha inherited the Fulger family's high-speed perception ability, allowing her to process events in greatly increased detail while operating at extreme speed.
- Accelerated Perception: Her perception can keep pace with movements that would appear instantaneous to slower beings.
- Thought Acceleration: Natasha can process information much faster while using her speed.
- High-Speed Combat Processing: The ability prevents her Lightning-enhanced movement from exceeding her own ability to perceive and respond to surrounding events.
- Enhanced Reaction Speed: Time Difference works alongside her physical reflexes, allowing precise reactions during extremely fast combat.

#### Draconic Language
As a True Dragon, Natasha possesses instinctive access to the supernatural language of Dragons.
- Draconic Language: Natasha can communicate through the language naturally understood by [[True Dragons]].
- Speak of Power: Draconic speech carries supernatural authority rather than functioning as ordinary spoken language.
- [[Will Solace|Will]] Manifestation: A Dragon can infuse words with Will and Energy to influence the world around them.
- Reality Interaction: Sufficiently powerful Draconic commands can directly interact with Reality through the authority inherent in Dragon speech.
- Elemental Commands: Natasha can naturally combine Draconic expression with her affinity for Lightning.

#### Draconic Runes
- Draconic Runes: Natasha possesses access to the runic system associated with [[True Dragons]].
- Rune Manipulation: She can interact with and utilize supernatural Draconic inscriptions.
- Enchanting: Runes can be placed upon objects or structures to grant supernatural properties.
- Energy Regulation: Draconic inscriptions can control the movement and behavior of Energy.
- Barrier Creation: Runes can be arranged to establish supernatural defensive structures.
- Sealing: Runic systems can be used to restrict objects, areas or supernatural effects.
- Elemental Enhancement: Natasha can incorporate her Lightning affinity into runic constructions.

#### Vampiric Abilities
Natasha's former Vampire characteristics were not simply erased when she evolved. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Dragon transformations used the existing supernatural traits of each individual as part of the foundation of their new forms.[4]
- Vampiric Physiology (Former): Before becoming a True Dragon, Natasha possessed the physical and supernatural characteristics of an old Noble Vampire.
- Enhanced Strength (Former): Her Vampire body possessed strength far beyond Humans.
- Enhanced Speed (Former): Her natural Vampiric speed was further enhanced by Lightning.
- Enhanced Senses (Former): She possessed the heightened senses common to powerful Vampires.
- Regeneration (Former): Natasha's Vampire physiology could recover from significant injuries.
- [[Immortality]] (Former): As a Noble Vampire, she did not naturally age like a Human.
- Blood Consumption (Former): She could restore or strengthen herself through the consumption of Blood.
- Bat Transformation (Former): As with other sufficiently skilled Vampires, the foundational transformation ability could be developed into more advanced shapeshifting.
- Advanced Shapeshifting: Natasha retained extensive control over her physical appearance and has previously used shapeshifting for cosmetic alterations.

#### Soul Abilities
Natasha's existence is unusual even among [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s supernatural family because she developed from a personality into an independently sustainable Soul.
- Soul Development: Natasha's personality continued developing until it possessed a genuine spiritual existence rather than remaining only a psychological state.[2]
- Independent Soul: By Chapter 859, Natasha existed as a distinct presence within Natashia's Soul.[2]
- Soul Separation: Victor's repair of Natashia's spiritual foundation allowed Natasha to separate without either woman ceasing to exist.[3]
- Independent Manifestation: Natasha can maintain a physical body entirely separate from Natashia.[3]
- Independent Consciousness: She possesses her own thoughts, desires, personality and [[Will Solace|Will]].
- Memory Retention: Natasha retained memories and experience from the period during which she existed inside Natashia.
- Spiritual Resistance: Natasha survived an abnormal existence within a fractured Soul for years before eventually becoming capable of independent manifestation.
- Strong Life Connection: Even before complete separation, Natasha's existence had developed deeply enough that she could no longer be treated as a disposable personality fragment.

#### Combat Skills
- High-Speed Combat: Natasha's primary combat advantage comes from combining Fulger Lightning with enhanced perception and supernatural physical speed.
- Elemental Combat: She can use Lightning both as a direct weapon and as reinforcement for physical attacks.
- Close-Quarters Combat: Years spent controlling a powerful Noble Vampire body gave Natasha practical experience in supernatural physical combat.
- Combat Experience: Natasha retains the experience accumulated during the years in which she was the dominant personality.
- Battlefield Mobility: Her Lightning allows rapid changes of position, sudden acceleration and attacks from multiple angles.
- Killing Intent: Natasha's historically violent personality makes her far less hesitant about using lethal force than many ordinary supernatural beings.

#### Former Powers
- Noble Vampire Physiology: Natasha originally relied upon the physiology of the body she shared with Natashia before obtaining an independent form and later evolving into a True Dragon.
- Vampire Count-Level Power: While Natasha dominated Natashia's body, she had access to the supernatural foundation of a former Vampire Count. This should not be confused with Natashia's later individual feats after the personalities began operating separately.
- [[Vampire Count Form]]: The shared Fulger body possessed a powerful transformed Vampire state before the family's later Draconic evolution.
- Fulger Lightning (Vampiric): Natasha's original use of Lightning was limited by Vampire physiology. Dragonhood later eliminated many of those biological limitations and elevated the ability into part of her True Dragon nature.[4]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is Natasha's husband and the individual most responsible for making her independent existence possible.
Although Natasha existed long before meeting Victor, her Soul was initially bound within Natashia. Victor's influence, Blood and eventual direct intervention allowed the spiritual abnormalities surrounding the Fulger family to finally be resolved.[2][3]
Rather than destroying Natasha after discovering her inside Natashia's Soul, Victor recognized that she had become a genuine individual. He repaired the damaged spiritual structure and allowed Natasha to possess a body of her own.[3]
Natasha immediately regarded the new body as an opportunity to live for herself and pursue Victor independently. She later became one of his wives and was eventually transformed through his True Dragon lineage.[1][4]
Victor and Natasha later had a daughter, [[Julia Elderblood]].

#### Annasthashia Elderblood Fulger
Natashia is Natasha's sister and the original person from whose damaged spiritual structure Natasha developed.
For most of their existence, Natasha could not physically exist without Natashia because both personalities occupied the same body. Natasha frequently fought with Natashia for control and embodied traits that Natashia either suppressed or could not properly process.
By Chapter 230, the two could directly confront one another within their shared mental world, demonstrating that Natasha possessed an identity separate from Natashia even before gaining an independent body.[6]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] ultimately separated their Souls and gave Natasha an independent body.[3]
The distinction between them is important: Natashia is Sasha and Angelina's mother, while Natasha is Julia's mother.

#### Carmila Fulger Elderblood
[[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]] is Natasha's mother through her origin from the Fulger family and Natashia's damaged Soul.
Carmila's attempt to protect Natashia while she was still developing contributed to the unusual spiritual circumstances that eventually produced Natasha.[2]
Carmila herself remained inside Natashia's Soul for centuries before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] discovered both her and Natasha. Victor's intervention finally allowed all three Fulger women to coexist through separate bodies.[3]

#### Julia Elderblood
[[Julia Elderblood]] is the biological daughter of Natasha and [[Victor Elderblood]].
Julia is part of the Fulger branch of Victor's second generation and possesses the characteristic blonde hair and violet Dragon Eyes seen among many of Victor's daughters.
Julia should not be confused with [[Angelina Elderblood]]. Angelina is the daughter of Natashia, whereas Julia is the daughter of Natasha.

#### Sasha Elderblood Fulger
[[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] is biologically Natashia's daughter rather than Natasha's.
However, Natasha was the dominant personality occupying Natashia's body throughout much of Sasha's childhood. Consequently, Natasha spent years physically occupying Sasha's maternal position, including participating in the abusive upbringing that left Sasha traumatized.
Their later relationship became dramatically healthier. Natasha continued to regard Sasha with maternal affection even after obtaining an independent body and openly expressed pride in how Sasha's personality developed.
During the later Imperial era, Natasha happily described Sasha as her "pride and joy", while Natashia likewise celebrated Sasha's increasingly shameless Fulger behavior.

#### Angelina Elderblood
[[Angelina Elderblood]] is the daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and Natashia.
Angelina is therefore Natasha's niece rather than her daughter. She belongs to Natashia's direct maternal line and should not be merged with Natasha's daughter, [[Julia Elderblood]].
