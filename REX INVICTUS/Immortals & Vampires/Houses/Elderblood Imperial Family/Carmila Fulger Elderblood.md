Carmila Fulger Elderblood, formerly known as Carmila Fulger, is an ancient Noble Vampire Countess who became a True Blood Dragon, the historical matriarch of Clan Fulger, and one of the wives of [[Victor Elderblood]]. She was among the earliest recognized Vampire Countesses of [[Nightingale]], having earned her position through combat against powerful Elder Vampires during an age when leadership was determined by personal strength rather than the inheritance of an established title. Remembered as the Fulger Knight and the Strongest Fulger, Carmila was renowned for her extraordinary Lightning affinity, disciplined conduct, advanced martial techniques, and the influence she exercised over one of Nightingale's most important Noble Vampire families.[6][2]
Carmila is the mother of Natashia and Victoria, the grandmother of [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] and [[Tatsuya Rider]], and an important ancestor of the modern [[Fulger Bloodline]]. Her history is inseparable from the family's ancient relationship with Lightning Spirits, whose power became the foundation of the Clan's hereditary ability. The unusually spiritual nature of this inheritance created a biological weakness among certain descendants, as their physical Vampire bodies could not always withstand the increasing power of their ancestral Lightning. Carmila's attempts to protect Natashia from a fatal Spirit Evolution damaged her daughter's Soul, contributing to the circumstances from which [[Natasha Elderblood Fulger]] eventually emerged as a separate consciousness.[3][6]
Carmila was murdered centuries before Victor entered the supernatural world, but a surviving portion of her Soul remained attached to Natashia's spiritual existence. She subsequently observed much of her family's history through her daughter's senses without possessing a physical body of her own. When Natashia's damaged Soul approached a catastrophic breakdown, Victor discovered Carmila and Natasha within its spiritual core. Rather than destroy the additional presences, he repaired Natashia's Soul, separated their individual existences, and provided Carmila and Natasha with new physical bodies adapted to the unusual characteristics of the Fulger lineage.[6][1]
Her restoration reunited three generations of Fulger women who had been separated by death, guilt, and unresolved family conflicts. Carmila later joined the Elderblood Clan and underwent the transformation into a True Blood Dragon alongside Natashia, Natasha, Victoria, and Sasha. The new Draconic physiology removed the limitations that had constrained the family's original Vampire bodies, allowing Carmila to continue developing the martial techniques she had refined throughout her ancient life. She subsequently became Victor's wife and the mother of their daughter, Aline Elderblood Fulger, becoming an active member of the modern [[Elderblood Empire]].[4][2][5]

### Physical Appearance

#### Noble Vampire
Carmila originally possessed the appearance of an exceptionally beautiful Noble Vampire woman with long golden-blonde hair, fair skin, blood-red eyes, and an elegant, mature figure. Her appearance closely resembled the defining physical characteristics inherited by later generations of Clan Fulger, particularly Natashia, Victoria, and Sasha. She possessed a tall and voluptuous physique comparable to that of other exceptionally beautiful ancient Vampires, while her bearing reflected the discipline and confidence expected of a woman who had personally established herself among [[Nightingale]]'s earliest ruling Countesses.[8][9]
Her aristocratic appearance was complemented by a knight-like dignity that distinguished her from the more openly impulsive personalities of several later Fulger women. The family preserved portraits of Carmila within its ancestral mansion, allowing her descendants to recognize her appearance long after her original death. Sasha had grown up hearing accounts of her grandmother's character and historical accomplishments, making the surviving portrait an important part of her understanding of the family's past.[8][1]

#### Soul Form
Following her death, Carmila existed for centuries without an independent physical body. A portion of her Soul remained within Natashia's spiritual existence, retaining her identity, memories, and consciousness while being unable to interact normally with the material world. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] entered Natashia's Soul, Carmila appeared before him as a recognizable blonde woman whose appearance reflected her former identity rather than the condition of an ordinary disembodied Spirit.[6]
Her spiritual existence preserved the accumulated memories and experiences of her original life, including the circumstances surrounding Natashia's damaged Soul and the history of the [[Fulger Bloodline]]. However, the centuries she spent observing her daughter also influenced her mannerisms, leaving the once-formal Countess noticeably more playful and mischievous than her family remembered.[6]

#### Restored Body
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] reconstructed Carmila's physical existence after separating her Soul from Natashia. Rather than restoring her original corpse, he created a new body adapted to the spiritual characteristics of the [[Fulger Bloodline]]. The resulting form resembled her previous appearance but possessed a much stronger connection to her ancestral Lightning, allowing her to generate extraordinarily dense golden Lightning shortly after returning to life. Carmila recognized that the power available through her restored existence had already surpassed the strength she possessed during her original lifetime.[1]
Despite this increased potential, the new body did not possess the physical conditioning and accumulated muscle memory of her original form. Carmila retained her ancient combat knowledge, judgment, and experience, but the newly created vessel initially required further development before she could express those qualities with the same physical refinement she had achieved during her first life. This distinction became especially apparent during her reunion with Natashia, whose established body was considerably more developed than those of Carmila and Natasha immediately after their restoration.[1][7]

#### Blood Dragon
Carmila subsequently underwent another transformation when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] established the Blood Dragon Race and offered his family the opportunity to become [[True Dragons]]. Her new existence retained the recognizable appearance of the Fulger lineage while incorporating the distinctive features of Draconic physiology, including Dragon Horns, Draconic Eyes, and the extraordinary physical constitution associated with True Dragons.[4]
The transformation produced an especially pronounced reaction among the Fulger women because of their ancestral Lightning-Spirit heritage. As Victor's power reconstructed their bodies, Carmila and her descendants became enveloped in Lightning to such an extent that their forms temporarily resembled Beings composed of pure elemental Energy. Their former weakness, which originated from forcing immense spiritual power through comparatively limited Vampire flesh, was overcome through the vastly greater compatibility and developmental potential of their new Draconic bodies.[4]
Carmila's current appearance combines the elegance of her original Noble Vampire form with the supernatural features of a True Blood Dragon. During combat, her golden Dragon Horns and eyes can visibly glow with Lightning, while her body naturally accommodates the immense speed, power, and Energy output associated with the evolved [[Fulger Bloodline]]. Although her original life was constrained by the physical limits of her Vampire body, her current existence allows her to continue refining her abilities without that former restriction.[2]

### Personality
Carmila is a proud, disciplined, honorable, and exceptionally experienced woman whose personality reflects her ancient position as the matriarch of Clan Fulger. During her original life, she was remembered as a dignified Countess possessing the qualities of a noble knight, demonstrating considerable self-control, competence, and responsibility toward the people under her authority. Her reputation was not based solely upon inherited status; she had personally fought powerful Elder Vampires to establish herself as a Countess and continued maintaining her martial discipline even after becoming responsible for the administration of an entire Noble Clan. These circumstances cultivated a practical warrior's mentality beneath her formal aristocratic conduct, allowing her to distinguish between the personal desire for greater strength and the responsibilities imposed by leadership.[8][6][2]
Her devotion to martial refinement is one of her most enduring characteristics. Unlike many ancient Vampires who became complacent after reaching the limits of their physical development, Carmila continued improving her techniques despite being unable to substantially increase her original body's power. [[Scathach]] regarded her as an exceptionally experienced fighter who had never permitted her technique to stagnate, describing the older Countess as someone whose dedication to training resembled her own, although Carmila had been forced to divide her attention between combat and the responsibilities of ruling Clan Fulger. Her enjoyment of battle remained apparent immediately after her resurrection, when she eagerly sought an opportunity to fight inside the Tower of Nightmares rather than remain idle while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] attended to the rest of the family.[1][2]
Carmila's experiences as a mother profoundly influenced her understanding of responsibility, sacrifice, and regret. Her attempt to prevent Natashia's fatal Spirit Evolution was motivated by genuine love, but the damage inflicted upon her daughter's Soul produced consequences that endured for centuries. Carmila neither denies her involvement nor treats the outcome as an acceptable sacrifice simply because her intentions were protective. Instead, she recognizes that her original decision was made under circumstances in which she lacked the knowledge and power necessary to find a safer solution. Her restoration also forces her to confront the emotional consequences of her death, particularly Natashia's long-standing guilt and Victoria's unresolved resentment concerning their family history.[6][7]
Although she retains the instincts of a former Clan Leader, Carmila does not insist upon reclaiming authority merely because of her seniority. The passage of time has transformed Clan Fulger into an organization governed by descendants who possess their own responsibilities and relationships. Carmila accepts these developments and concentrates upon helping the family recover its unity, strengthen its techniques, and overcome weaknesses that prevented earlier generations from fully realizing their potential. Her decision to undergo the Blood Dragon transformation reflects the same outlook: rather than prioritize the preservation of her old identity, she willingly follows the rest of the family into a new existence that offers them a shared future.[4][2]
Centuries of spiritual coexistence with Natashia also altered Carmila's behavior. The legendary Fulger Knight became noticeably more mischievous, flirtatious, and playful after observing the experiences of her daughter from within the same Soul. She remains capable of maintaining the composure of an ancient aristocrat, but no longer feels compelled to preserve that dignity at every opportunity. Her conversations with Victor frequently display a combination of genuine respect, curiosity, and shameless teasing, while her interactions with Sasha reveal a particularly humorous sensitivity toward being treated as an elderly grandmother despite her youthful appearance. During an Imperial gathering, Carmila even insisted that Sasha refer to her as a sister rather than a grandmother, despite their obvious biological relationship.[6][10]
Beneath the humor and occasional childishness, Carmila remains highly perceptive and serious when circumstances demand it. She recognizes the responsibilities accompanying extraordinary power and possesses the patience required to train younger family members rather than merely overwhelm them through superior technique. Her willingness to acknowledge the progress of her descendants, accept her own past mistakes, and continue learning after thousands of years demonstrates that her pride is rooted more strongly in competence and the preservation of her family than in the need to maintain an unquestioned position above others.

### Background

#### Origins of the Fulger Bloodline
Clan Fulger possesses one of [[Nightingale]]'s most unusual Noble Vampire Bloodlines. Its hereditary Lightning originates from a connection with extremely powerful elemental Spirits, whose natural existence differs fundamentally from that of physical Vampires. The family's early traditions attributed this origin directly to Carmila, describing her as a Lightning Spirit of the highest order who became a Vampire through an agreement involving [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]]. Other family accounts emphasized the existence of an older Lightning-Spirit ancestor from whom Carmila and her descendants inherited their unusual supernatural characteristics.[8][3][6]
The earliest accounts preserved by Sasha described Carmila as a woman possessing close connections to Lightning Spirits associated with the Norse Gods. Sasha initially regarded the story as a legend because Spirits normally lacked the physical bodies necessary to undergo conventional Vampiric transformation. Natashia later gave a more direct account of her mother's origin, identifying Carmila herself as the Spirit who became a Vampire through Vlad's agreement. The deeper history of the Fulger lineage subsequently introduced an ancestral High-Level Lightning Spirit whose existence preceded the modern family's development. These accounts establish the Bloodline's spiritual origin, although they differ over whether the original conversion involved Carmila personally or an earlier ancestor.[8][3][6]
The conversion of Lightning-Spirit power into a Vampiric Bloodline created an important biological limitation. Spirits are naturally compatible with their own elemental Energy, whereas the bodies of Noble Vampires remain physical structures that must withstand the effects of the power passing through them. As the Fulger lineage grew stronger, the increasing concentration of Lightning and spiritual characteristics could place immense pressure upon the wielder's flesh. Descendants with exceptionally powerful ancestral potential were therefore vulnerable to a dangerous incompatibility between their physical bodies and the spiritual nature of their inheritance.[3][6]
Carmila represented one of the most accomplished historical manifestations of this Bloodline. Her exceptional Lightning affinity and accumulated combat experience allowed her to develop techniques that became central to the family's martial traditions, while her descendants inherited different proportions of its spiritual and Vampiric characteristics. The power could manifest very strongly in some individuals, remain comparatively dormant in others, or reappear through later generations. This variation is particularly apparent in the differences between Natashia, Victoria, Sasha, and [[Tatsuya Rider]].[8][3]

#### Rise of the First Fulger Countess
Carmila belonged to an ancient generation of [[Vampire Counts]] whose positions were established through direct competition rather than the hereditary political system familiar to later [[Nightingale]] society. During her era, individuals seeking the position of Count had to demonstrate sufficient strength to overcome other powerful Elder Vampires and earn recognition as the most capable representatives of their respective Clans. Carmila achieved this position through her own accomplishments, establishing herself as one of the oldest Countesses associated with the political foundations of Nightingale.[2]
Her reputation as the Fulger Knight reflected the extraordinary combination of martial refinement and noble conduct she maintained throughout her life. She became known as one of the strongest members in the history of her lineage, eventually taking responsibility for Clan Fulger's development and its position among Nightingale's principal ruling families. Her leadership included the administration of territory, preservation of the Clan's traditions, and the upbringing of successors capable of maintaining its standing after her death.[6][2]
The responsibilities of rulership limited the time Carmila could dedicate exclusively to personal training, but did not end her dedication to martial improvement. She continued refining her technique long after her body had reached the limits of its natural development, accumulating an extraordinary depth of combat experience that later generations could not reproduce through superior physical strength alone. This legacy became especially important following her restoration, when her knowledge remained intact despite the creation of an entirely new physical body.[2]

#### The Fulger Martial Tradition and Taranis
Carmila made lasting contributions to the martial foundations of Clan Fulger. She developed the secret combat traditions associated with the family's extraordinary Lightning speed, establishing techniques intended to exploit rapid movement, concentrated Energy, precise weapon control, and the ability to overwhelm enemies before they could respond. These methods were preserved and taught to talented descendants, eventually becoming one of the most important parts of the Clan's hereditary combat knowledge.[3]
Among the techniques derived from Carmila's teachings is Lynet, a named form employed by Sasha during the Demonic invasion of [[Nightingale]]. The method combined the [[Fulger Bloodline]]'s Lightning with extreme-speed movement and controlled attacks against multiple opponents. Natashia instructed Sasha in the family's secret martial tradition, explaining that its techniques originated with Carmila and could only be mastered completely by individuals possessing sufficient talent and bodily compatibility with the Lightning Bloodline.[3]
Carmila was also associated with the ancient Runic daggers later known as [[Taranis]], the Daggers of the Ancestral Spirit. The pair of golden weapons became one of Clan Fulger's most important hereditary treasures and were traditionally associated with Dwarven craftsmanship from Nidavellir. Their engraved Runes and supernatural enchantments were adapted to the Fulger Bloodline, allowing legitimate members of the family to channel and control Lightning while rejecting individuals who lacked the necessary hereditary connection.[11][12]
The daggers eventually passed into the possession of later Fulger leaders and became symbols of the family's inheritance. Their importance extended beyond their value as weapons because they represented the continuity of a martial tradition that Carmila had helped establish during the ancient era. They were preserved alongside other records and treasures that allowed the family's descendants to maintain a connection with their historical origins.[11]

#### Natashia's Spirit Evolution
Carmila's greatest personal crisis began when Natashia inherited an exceptionally powerful expression of the family's Lightning-Spirit heritage. Her ancestral characteristics developed to the point that her existence began naturally evolving toward a more complete spiritual and elemental state. Although the transformation represented the potential of the [[Fulger Bloodline]], Natashia's physical Vampire body lacked the compatibility necessary to survive the process. Without intervention, the increasing conflict between her spiritual foundation and her physical existence would eventually have killed her.[6]
Confronted with the imminent loss of her daughter, Carmila interfered directly with Natashia's Soul Core to prevent the evolution from continuing. The intervention succeeded in preserving Natashia's life, but damaged the spiritual structure that had been attempting to develop. Carmila lacked the means to reconstruct the damaged portions perfectly, leaving her daughter with a fundamental instability that persisted long after the immediate danger had passed.[6]
The consequences became more severe after Carmila's own death. Natashia's Soul contained both the damaged structures created by her mother's intervention and the remnants of Carmila's consciousness. The trauma surrounding the Countess' murder further contributed to the emergence of the personality who eventually became Natasha, producing a spiritual condition in which multiple distinct consciousnesses existed within Natashia's Soul. This arrangement remained unresolved for centuries and contributed to the complicated relationship between Natashia's identities.[6][1]

#### Death and Spiritual Survival
Carmila's original life ended through a betrayal involving the man who became Natashia's first love. The circumstances were especially devastating because Natashia believed that her own trust in the man had allowed him to approach the family and exploit Carmila's weakened condition. The ancient Countess was murdered, leaving her daughters without their mother and establishing a source of guilt that Natashia carried for centuries.[7]
Carmila's Soul did not disappear completely following her death. A surviving portion became attached to Natashia's spiritual existence, allowing Carmila to continue observing the world through her daughter's senses while remaining unable to participate openly in events. Carmila herself did not fully understand the mechanism that preserved her consciousness, remembering her death and subsequently awakening within a spiritual environment from which she could perceive Natashia's experiences.[1]
Her prolonged existence within Natashia meant that she witnessed the changes affecting her descendants, including the development of Natasha, the birth and upbringing of Sasha, the conflicts dividing the Fulger family, and the arrival of [[Victor Elderblood]]. Although she retained her identity, she lacked a physical body and possessed no ordinary means of revealing herself to the people she continued loving. Her presence remained hidden until the accumulated damage within Natashia's spiritual foundation became impossible to ignore.[6][1]

### Synopsis

#### A New Pillar
Although Carmila had died long before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became involved with [[Nightingale]]'s Vampire society, her reputation and possessions continued shaping Clan Fulger. Sasha retained memories of the stories told to her during childhood concerning the family's legendary former Countess, particularly accounts of Carmila's connection to powerful Lightning Spirits. These stories were preserved alongside the physical treasures maintained within the ancestral Fulger Mansion, where Sasha eventually returned to recover an important family inheritance.[8]
The old mansion contained a large portrait of Carmila associated with a hidden chamber protected through the Fulger family's Lightning. Sasha activated the concealed mechanism through her own supernatural power, opening a passage into a space filled with electrical Energy. The incident demonstrated how the ancestral Countess remained part of the family's identity even after her death, with her image, accomplishments, and supernatural inheritance preserved by descendants who had never personally known her.[8]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Carmila's historical reputation continued influencing the younger generation of [[Nightingale]]'s Count families. [[Scathach]], who had known of Carmila during her original lifetime, recognized Natashia's appearance and family relationship during their earlier encounters. The similarities between mother and daughter were particularly apparent in their beauty and physical characteristics, although Scathach also recognized considerable differences in temperament and personality.[9]
The ancient Fulger lineage remained connected to its preserved artifacts and genealogical records, which were considered important enough to be maintained as magical inheritances rather than ordinary historical documents. The family's ancestral records linked later descendants with earlier generations whose supernatural qualities had established the foundations of the modern Clan. These records, together with the Runic daggers and the martial traditions preserved by Natashia, continued transmitting Carmila's legacy long after the loss of her physical presence.[13]

#### Demonic War
The ancient Fulger martial tradition became particularly important during the Demonic invasion of [[Nightingale]]. While the Clan's territory faced an attack by forces associated with the Demonic Pillars, Natashia instructed Sasha in the secret techniques inherited from Carmila. The training was intended to teach her daughter how to fully exploit the family Bloodline, using the extraordinary compatibility of Sasha's body to express techniques that earlier generations could not always perform safely.[3]
Natashia explained that Carmila's martial arts originated from the family's spiritual Lightning heritage, whose full expression had once been restricted by the limitations of Vampire flesh. Sasha subsequently employed Lynet, a technique combining extraordinary movement speed with devastating Lightning attacks, to overwhelm the invading Demons. Her performance demonstrated that Carmila's teachings remained among the most formidable combat traditions preserved by Nightingale's ruling families.[3]
Carmila's influence also extended beyond the Vampire world. During Natashia's encounter with [[Viviane]], the Lady of the Lake, the Fairy recognized Carmila as an old friend. Viviane had not known of the former Countess' death and was deeply saddened to learn that someone she had once known personally had disappeared from the world. Their friendship demonstrated that Carmila's connections during her original life were not restricted to Nightingale and extended to ancient supernatural communities associated with the Fairies and the European mystical world.[14]

#### King Among Kings
Carmila's spiritual existence was finally discovered when Natashia suffered a catastrophic breakdown during her progression through the Tower of Nightmares. The crisis occurred shortly before the gathering of powerful supernatural factions, while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was occupied with preparations involving his expanding organization. Natashia suddenly collapsed after advancing through the Tower and drinking Victor's Blood, leaving [[Gaia]] and Nyx unable to determine the cause of the sudden instability. Victor immediately examined his wife and recognized that the problem originated within her Soul rather than her physical body.[6]
Entering Natashia's spiritual foundation, Victor discovered two additional presences embedded within its damaged structure. The first was Natasha, the independent consciousness who had developed from the fractured portions of Natashia's Soul, while the second was Carmila, whose surviving spiritual existence had remained hidden since her original death. Victor initially treated both women as potential threats because he found them occupying the deepest part of his wife's Soul. Carmila identified herself and explained the history of the [[Fulger Bloodline]], revealing the ancient relationship between Lightning Spirits and the Vampire family as well as the reason she had interfered with Natashia's spiritual development.[6]
The revelation exposed a crisis that had remained unresolved for centuries. Natashia's ancestral potential had once begun evolving toward a spiritual state that her Vampire body could not withstand, forcing Carmila to halt the transformation by damaging the Soul Core. Although the intervention had saved Natashia's life, the resulting instability never completely disappeared. Victor recognized that his wife's current condition resulted from the interaction between that ancient damage, her continued development, and the additional consciousnesses inhabiting her Soul.[6]
Rather than destroy Carmila or Natasha, Victor reconstructed the spiritual foundation itself. He used his increasingly sophisticated understanding of Souls and his Progenitor authority to strengthen Natashia's compatibility with the spiritual characteristics of the Fulger Bloodline, allowing her body to reject foreign spiritual presences without destroying them. Carmila and Natasha were consequently separated into independent Souls, while Natashia's damaged foundation was restored to a stable condition.[6][1]
Victor then created entirely new physical bodies capable of housing the separated Souls. Carmila and Natasha were restored as Vampires possessing stronger Spirit-like characteristics, allowing their new physical forms to support the unusual spiritual qualities inherited from the Fulger lineage. The process differed from ordinary resurrection because Carmila's original corpse was not restored. Instead, her surviving Soul received a newly created vessel, preserving her identity while establishing a fundamentally new physical existence.[1]
Carmila quickly discovered that her new body could generate far denser golden Lightning than the body she had possessed during her original life. The improvement impressed her, although her ancient combat instincts also made her eager to test its capabilities immediately. She persuaded Victor to allow her to fight within the Tower of Nightmares, rapidly leaving the room to experience combat with her restored body. Victor eventually retrieved her when the time came to explain the situation to the rest of the Fulger family.[1]
The reunion produced extraordinary emotional reactions. Natashia initially struggled to understand how her mother could be standing alive before her, while the appearance of Natasha as an independent individual caused even greater confusion. Overwhelmed by the simultaneous return of her mother and the physical manifestation of her former alternate self, Natashia briefly fainted. Victor subsequently brought Victoria and Sasha to meet Carmila, who introduced herself with the formality of an ancient Noblewoman and confirmed her identity as the former Leader of Clan Fulger and the mother of Natashia and Victoria.[1]
Sasha recognized her grandmother from the portrait preserved within the Fulger Mansion, while Victoria reacted with astonishment at the appearance of a woman she had long believed to be dead. The gathering brought together Carmila, Natashia, Victoria, Natasha, and Sasha, confronting several generations of Fulger women with a family history that had previously been divided by death and spiritual trauma.[1]
Natashia's emotional reunion with Carmila continued after the immediate shock subsided. Upon awakening and seeing her mother, she embraced Carmila with overwhelming force, unable to contain the grief and relief that had accumulated since the original murder. Carmila initially responded with gentle teasing before recognizing how deeply her daughter had been affected by the loss. Their conversation eventually returned to the circumstances of Carmila's death, particularly Natashia's belief that her own decisions had allowed the murderer to approach the family.[7]
Carmila firmly rejected Natashia's self-blame. The man responsible for the murder had deliberately exploited circumstances surrounding Carmila's weakened condition, and the Countess refused to treat her daughter's trust as equivalent to the killer's responsibility for the betrayal. Carmila also confronted Victoria's resentment and acknowledged that aspects of her parenting had caused pain within the family. Rather than use her former status to dismiss those grievances, she accepted responsibility for her mistakes and began rebuilding relationships that had remained unresolved for centuries.[7]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Carmila's restoration was followed by another fundamental transformation when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became the Dragon God of [[Chaos]] and began establishing the Blood Dragon Race. As Victor prepared to transform members of his family into [[True Dragons]], Carmila joined Natashia, Natasha, Victoria, and Sasha among those willing to accept his power. Their participation reflected a shared determination to remain together and continue developing as a family rather than preserve the racial limitations inherited from their original Vampire existence.[4]
When Victor questioned the presence of several Fulger women among the candidates, Natashia explained their collective decision through the importance of family. Carmila accepted the transformation despite having only recently regained an independent physical body, recognizing that the new existence would allow her to remain with her daughters and granddaughter while continuing her own development.[4]
Victor's Draconic power reconstructed the Fulger women into True [[Blood Dragons]], producing a powerful reaction within their Lightning-Spirit Bloodline. Their bodies became surrounded by immense concentrations of Lightning, temporarily resembling Beings of pure elemental Energy as their ancestral characteristics were fully awakened within their new Draconic foundations. The conversion removed the ancient incompatibility between spiritual Lightning and Vampire flesh, overcoming the limitation that had once forced Carmila to intervene in Natashia's Soul to preserve her life.[4]
The transformation was particularly significant for Carmila because it provided a physical foundation capable of continuing to grow beyond the limits of her original Vampire body. She retained the martial experience accumulated during her ancient life while gaining access to the extraordinary potential of a True Dragon. This combination allowed her to resume intensive training and begin recovering the physical familiarity that had been disrupted by the creation of her new body.[4][2]
Carmila subsequently participated in joint training with the other Fulger women. During one such session, she faced Sasha, Natashia, Natasha, and Victoria simultaneously within an arena reinforced to endure the powers of True Dragons. The confrontation involved movements and attacks performed at tremendous speeds, with the five women repeatedly disappearing from ordinary sight and leaving trails of Lightning as they exchanged blows. Their Draconic bodies allowed them to perform techniques that would have placed considerably greater strain upon their former Vampire physiology.[2]
Although Carmila was fighting against four powerful Blood Dragons at once, her combat experience allowed her to remain competitive through superior judgment, timing, and refined technique. She corrected Sasha's excessive Energy expenditure while responding to attacks from multiple directions, demonstrating that her ancient training remained relevant despite the enormous changes in their physical abilities. [[Scathach]] observed the battle and recognized that Carmila was simultaneously using her descendants to recover her own former combat instincts while forcing them to understand the limits and advantages of their new bodies.[2]
Scathach's assessment revealed the depth of Carmila's historical accomplishments. Carmila was older than Scathach and belonged to the ancient generation of Counts who had been required to defeat powerful Elder Vampires rather than inherit their positions. She had continued refining her martial arts throughout her reign, even after her physical body had ceased developing significantly. The Blood Dragon transformation consequently removed a long-standing restriction, allowing her accumulated technique to be supported by a body capable of continued evolution.[2]
The training reinforced Carmila's position as the martial matriarch of Clan Fulger. Although Natashia remained responsible for the family's modern political leadership, Carmila possessed a level of historical knowledge and technical refinement that made her invaluable to the younger generations. Her experience allowed her to guide their progress without demanding that they surrender the independence or authority they had acquired during her absence.[2]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Carmila became increasingly integrated into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household as the Elderblood family continued its development across the new territories and environments established by the Dragon God. Her relationship with Victor, initially founded upon his intervention in Natashia's spiritual crisis, gradually progressed into a romantic relationship. Carmila eventually became one of his wives, joining Natashia, Natasha, Victoria, and Sasha as part of the unusually extensive Fulger branch of the Elderblood family.[4][10]
The relationship also allowed Carmila to experience motherhood again after the destruction and restoration of her original life. She and Victor became the parents of Aline Elderblood Fulger, a daughter who strongly resembles Carmila and possesses the hereditary foundation of both the Fulger Lightning lineage and Victor's Blood Dragon family. Aline belongs to the later generation of Imperial children whose growth occurred during the accelerated developmental eras of the Elderblood household.[5]
Carmila's new family circumstances differed radically from the aristocratic household she had once governed in [[Nightingale]]. The descendants she had previously observed only through Natashia's Soul had become extraordinarily powerful [[Blood Dragons]], while Victor's growing civilization extended far beyond the scale of the original Vampire Kingdom. Nevertheless, Carmila continued identifying herself strongly with Clan Fulger, preserving its traditions and relationships even as its members assumed new positions within the Imperial Family.[2][5]
Her place within the family was further reinforced through the continued cooperation of Natashia, Natasha, Victoria, and Sasha. The old divisions involving Natashia's damaged Soul, Natasha's existence, and Victoria's resentment no longer prevented the women from living and developing together. Carmila could participate directly in their training, discussions, and daily lives rather than remaining an unseen observer attached to one of her daughters.[1][10]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
By the mature Imperial Era, Carmila regularly operated alongside the other principal women of Clan Fulger as a member of the wider Elderblood household. During an assignment conducted on the opposite side of a galaxy, she gathered with Natashia, Natasha, Victoria, and Sasha to discuss an organizational matter involving [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s expanding civilization. Victoria assessed the efficiency of the proposed arrangement, observing that the enormous number of extraordinary individuals available to the Empire reduced the need for Victor to personally manage every task.[10]
Carmila expressed satisfaction at seeing the Fulger women together again, reflecting how important their restored unity remained to her. Sasha responded that the women already spent considerable time together because they shared the same husband, turning the exchange into a familiar family argument. When Sasha addressed her as grandmother, Carmila protested that her appearance was far too youthful for the title and insisted upon being called her sister instead. The conversation demonstrated how completely the family had adjusted to relationships that would have been unimaginable during Carmila's original lifetime.[10]
Her continued involvement in Imperial affairs reflects the transformation of her role from an ancient Vampire Countess into an experienced member of an interstellar ruling family. Carmila no longer occupies her former position as the political head of [[Nightingale]]'s Fulger territory, but remains an important elder whose judgment, experience, and connection to the family contribute to the modern [[Elderblood Empire]]. Her position combines the responsibilities of a matriarch with the opportunities created by her new True Dragon existence, allowing her to participate in an era whose scale greatly exceeds the civilization she originally helped establish.[10]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
Carmila's relationship with [[Victor Elderblood]] began when he entered Natashia's Soul and discovered the additional consciousnesses inhabiting its damaged spiritual foundation. Victor initially regarded Carmila as a potential threat because he found her occupying the innermost region of his wife's existence. Carmila nevertheless remained composed enough to explain her identity and the circumstances surrounding Natashia's ancient Soul injury, recognizing that Victor's anger originated from his determination to protect his family.[6]
Victor's intervention accomplished something Carmila had believed impossible during her original lifetime. She had been unable to preserve Natashia without damaging her Soul, but Victor possessed the understanding and supernatural authority necessary to reconstruct the damaged foundation without destroying the individual consciousnesses associated with it. By repairing Natashia, separating Natasha, and restoring Carmila's physical existence, he resolved a family crisis that had endured for centuries and granted the ancient Countess an entirely new life.[6][1]
Carmila quickly developed considerable respect and affection for Victor. She admired his extraordinary accomplishments, his willingness to protect Natashia and her descendants, and his role in strengthening the Fulger lineage beyond its ancient limitations. His support allowed her to recover her original purpose as a warrior and mother while gaining opportunities she had never possessed during her first life. Her increasingly playful and flirtatious behavior also reflected the influence of centuries spent observing Natashia's relationship with him.[1]
Their relationship eventually became romantic, and Carmila joined the Elderblood family as one of Victor's wives. Their union produced Aline Elderblood Fulger, making Carmila the mother of another generation of descendants after her resurrection and transformation into a True Blood Dragon. Victor also occupies several other familial positions through his marriages to Natashia, Victoria, Natasha, and Sasha, creating a complex household in which Carmila remains both a historical ancestor and an active member of the Imperial Family.[5][10]

#### Annasthashia Elderblood Fulger
Natashia is Carmila's biological daughter and the person most directly affected by her decisions during the ancient Fulger era. Natashia inherited an extraordinarily powerful expression of the family's Lightning-Spirit Bloodline, causing her spiritual foundation to begin evolving beyond the limits her original Vampire body could withstand. Carmila damaged the developing section of her Soul to prevent the transformation from killing her, preserving Natashia's life at the cost of long-term spiritual instability.[6]
Their relationship became even more complicated after Carmila's murder. Natashia blamed herself for the tragedy because the killer had become her first love, while a surviving fragment of Carmila's Soul remained attached to her existence without her knowledge. Carmila consequently observed much of her daughter's suffering and development throughout the centuries, including the emergence of Natasha and the eventual arrival of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[1][7]
Their reunion after Carmila's restoration was intensely emotional. Natashia was initially unable to accept that her mother had returned, and later embraced her with overwhelming force after the immediate shock subsided. Carmila comforted her daughter and rejected the belief that Natashia should be held responsible for the murderer's actions. The restoration allowed them to rebuild their relationship without the barriers imposed by death and a damaged Soul.[7]
Following their transformation into [[Blood Dragons]], Carmila resumed directly training Natashia and helping her refine the techniques inherited from the ancient Fulger lineage. Their relationship consequently developed beyond its original maternal bond, allowing them to participate together as powerful members of the same evolved family.[2]

#### Victoria Fulger Elderblood
[[Victoria Fulger Elderblood]] is Carmila's biological daughter and one of the principal descendants of the ancient Fulger family. Although Victoria did not originally manifest the same Lightning ability that characterized Natashia, she retained the hereditary Bloodline and successfully passed its potential to her son, [[Tatsuya Rider]]. Her position demonstrated that the expression of the family's ancestral power could vary between generations without disappearing entirely from the lineage.[8]
Victoria's relationship with Carmila was marked by unresolved resentment stemming from the family's earlier history. Unlike Natashia, whose reunion with her mother immediately produced overwhelming emotional relief, Victoria retained grievances concerning the decisions and conduct of Carmila during her original lifetime. Carmila acknowledged those feelings after her restoration and accepted that her position as a former Matriarch did not excuse every mistake she had made as a mother.[7]
Their relationship improved as the family gradually recovered its unity. Victoria subsequently underwent the Blood Dragon transformation with Carmila and the other Fulger women, developing a new physical foundation capable of expressing the ancestral Bloodline more completely. They later trained together and participated in Imperial affairs, establishing a relationship no longer defined exclusively by the unresolved conflicts of their original lives.[4][2][10]

#### Natasha Elderblood Fulger
[[Natasha Elderblood Fulger]], also known as Naty, occupies an unusual position within Carmila's family. Unlike Natashia and Victoria, Natasha was not born through ordinary reproduction. Her independent existence developed from the damaged portions of Natashia's Soul, which had been altered by Carmila's intervention during the fatal Spirit Evolution. The trauma surrounding Carmila's death subsequently contributed to Natasha's emergence as a distinct personality with her own memories and experiences.[6][1]
Carmila's spiritual remnant existed alongside Natasha within Natashia's Soul for centuries, allowing the two to become familiar with one another before their separation. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually provided Natasha with an independent body rather than treating her as an abnormality that had to be destroyed. Carmila accepted the restored Natasha as part of her family, treating her with maternal affection despite the unconventional circumstances of her origin.[1]
Their relationship continued developing after both women became [[Blood Dragons]]. Natasha joined Carmila, Natashia, Victoria, and Sasha in their collective training and later became one of the principal Fulger women operating within the [[Elderblood Empire]].[4][2][10]

#### Sasha Elderblood Fulger
[[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] is Carmila's granddaughter and one of the most important inheritors of her martial traditions. Before Carmila's restoration, Sasha knew her grandmother through the stories preserved by the family and the portrait maintained within the old Fulger Mansion. She associated Carmila with the ancient greatness of Clan Fulger, its Lightning-Spirit heritage, and the martial accomplishments that established the family among [[Nightingale]]'s most powerful Nobles.[8]
Sasha inherited an unusually compatible physical foundation through the combination of her mother's [[Fulger Bloodline]] and the characteristics of her father's family. This allowed Natashia to train her in Carmila's secret martial arts, which demanded exceptional physical tolerance for the immense Lightning generated during their execution. Sasha's use of Lynet during the Demonic invasion demonstrated that the ancient Countess' techniques remained effective even against enemies far beyond the threats confronting ordinary Noble Vampires.[3]
When Carmila returned to life, Sasha recognized her from the preserved portrait and was astonished by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s restoration of the former Matriarch. Their relationship subsequently became much more personal as Carmila participated directly in her training and helped her adjust to the extraordinary potential of her Blood Dragon body. Carmila's ancient technical knowledge complemented Sasha's tremendous physical power and speed, allowing the younger woman to benefit from experience accumulated over thousands of years.[1][2]
Their later interactions became considerably more playful. Sasha freely teased Carmila about being her grandmother, while Carmila objected to the title because of her youthful appearance and insisted that being called a sister was more appropriate. Their humor reflected the comfortable relationship that had developed after Carmila's restoration and integration into the modern Elderblood family.[10]

#### Aline Elderblood Fulger
Aline Elderblood Fulger is the biological daughter of Carmila and [[Victor Elderblood]], born during the later period of the Elderblood family's development. She resembles a younger version of her mother and possesses the hereditary foundation associated with both the Fulger Lightning lineage and Victor's Blood Dragon Race.[5]
Aline's birth represents a profound change in Carmila's life. Having already spent millennia as a Vampire Countess, experienced the deaths and tragedies of her original family, and survived centuries as a disembodied Soul, Carmila received the opportunity to raise another child after being restored to life. Her relationship with Aline therefore belongs to a completely different period of her existence from the era in which she originally raised Natashia and Victoria.
Aline should not be confused with Aline Elderblood Valefar, another member of Victor's family who shares the same given name.

#### Clan Fulger
Clan Fulger is the family whose modern identity Carmila helped establish during the ancient history of [[Nightingale]]. Her accomplishments as a Countess, development of the ancestral martial tradition, and association with the family's hereditary artifacts became central elements of the Clan's historical reputation. Although the Lightning-Spirit lineage originated through an earlier ancestral connection or through the conversion attributed directly to Carmila in older accounts, her achievements made her one of its most important known representatives.[8][3][2]
Carmila's former position included the responsibilities of political leadership, territorial administration, and the preservation of the family's supernatural traditions. Those duties eventually passed to her descendants, particularly Natashia, whose leadership continued shaping the Clan during the events involving [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s rise. Carmila did not attempt to reclaim the political position after her resurrection, instead becoming an influential elder and martial instructor whose purpose was to strengthen the family while respecting the authority established by later generations.[1][2]
Her restoration and the Blood Dragon transformation fundamentally changed the future of the lineage. The family no longer needed to remain constrained by the incompatibility between Lightning-Spirit power and ordinary Vampire flesh, allowing its descendants to develop the ancestral inheritance within physical bodies far better suited to its extraordinary demands.[4]

#### Viviane
[[Viviane]], the Lady of the Lake, was an old friend of Carmila during her original lifetime. Their relationship connected the ancient Fulger Countess to the supernatural communities associated with the Fairies and the European mystical world, demonstrating that Carmila possessed personal connections extending beyond the political boundaries of [[Nightingale]].[14]
Viviane learned of Carmila's death when Natashia explained the developments that had occurred during her isolation. #### Scathach Elderblood Scarlett
[[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] was acquainted with Carmila's reputation and belonged to a later generation of [[Vampire Counts]]. Although both women were ancient warriors, Carmila was older and had earned her position during an era in which the Count title had to be secured through direct competition against other powerful Elder Vampires.[9][2]
[[Scathach]] possessed considerable respect for Carmila's martial refinement. While observing the restored Countess training against four Fulger descendants, she recognized that Carmila was using the confrontation both to recover her own ancient instincts and to teach the younger women how to properly employ their Draconic bodies. Scathach explained that Carmila had continued refining her technique throughout her original life despite the physical limitations that eventually prevented further increases in raw power.[2]
Their comparison is particularly significant because both women valued martial development, yet their original circumstances differed. Scathach had dedicated much of her existence to pursuing strength, while Carmila had been forced to balance combat training with the responsibilities of governing Clan Fulger. The Blood Dragon transformation allowed Carmila to resume the development that had previously been restricted by her physical body.[2]

### Legacy
Carmila's legacy extends across several distinct eras of the Fulger family's history. She was an ancient Vampire Countess who helped establish the Clan's political and martial reputation, an ancestor whose teachings survived through later generations, and a mother whose desperate attempt to save Natashia produced consequences that affected the family for centuries. Her original death deprived Clan Fulger of one of its greatest historical figures, while the survival of her Soul allowed her to witness the continuing development of the lineage without being able to participate directly in its affairs.[8][6]
Her restoration transformed that legacy from a collection of historical memories into a living connection between the Clan's past and present. Through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s intervention, Carmila received a new body, recovered her relationship with her descendants, and gained the opportunity to confront the mistakes and tragedies that had remained unresolved after her death. The emergence of Natasha as an independent individual further expanded the family, replacing a divided spiritual existence with separate lives capable of continuing alongside one another.[1][7]
The Blood Dragon transformation represents the most significant change in the history of the Fulger lineage since the original conversion of its spiritual Lightning into a hereditary Vampire power. Carmila's generation had been constrained by the limits of physical flesh, while later descendants inherited those weaknesses alongside extraordinary potential. Victor's creation of True Blood Dragon bodies removed that restriction and allowed the family's accumulated martial traditions to develop within a form capable of sustaining their full expression.[4][2]
Carmila consequently represents a living connection between the ancient [[Nightingale]] Count system and the modern [[Elderblood Empire]]. She witnessed the limitations of the original Vampire era, survived the prolonged spiritual crisis that divided her descendants, and returned to participate in an age characterized by [[True Dragons]], interstellar civilization, and the expansion of her family across generations. Her continued involvement in Clan Fulger preserves the historical knowledge and discipline that once made the family powerful, while her new existence allows her to contribute directly to its future rather than remain only a legendary figure remembered by her descendants.

### Abilities

#### Skills and Abilities
Carmila Fulger Elderblood is an ancient former Vampire Countess, the historical matriarch of Clan Fulger, and a True Blood Dragon belonging to the Elderblood Clan. As one of the oldest and most experienced members of the Fulger family, Carmila combines the supernatural characteristics of an ancient Lightning-Spirit lineage with thousands of years of martial training, exceptional control over her elemental Energy, and combat techniques developed during an era when the position of Vampire Count had to be earned through direct competition against powerful Elder Vampires. Her greatest specialties are [[Electrokinesis]], Lightning-Spirit Transformation, Dagger Mastery, Body Control, and Advanced [[Martial Arts]].[1][2][3]
Carmila's original supernatural foundation originated from the Fulger family's relationship with High-Level Lightning Spirits. Earlier accounts identified Carmila herself as an exceptionally powerful Lightning Spirit who became a Vampire through an agreement involving [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]], while later explanations traced the Bloodline to an ancestral Spirit whose elemental characteristics became hereditary among the Noble Vampires of Clan Fulger. Regardless of the precise ancestral sequence, Carmila possesses one of the oldest and most powerful known expressions of this lineage, granting her a natural relationship with Lightning that extends beyond ordinary elemental spellcasting.[4][1]
Her original Vampire physiology limited the full expression of this inheritance. The immense power of the ancestral Lightning-Spirit characteristics placed increasing strain upon physical flesh, preventing even exceptionally talented Fulgers from developing their abilities without restrictions. After Carmila's death, [[Victor Elderblood]] restored her surviving Soul and created a new body possessing enhanced Spirit-like compatibility. She subsequently underwent another transformation when Victor established the Blood Dragon Race, incorporating her spiritual and Vampiric characteristics into a True Dragon body capable of sustaining the extraordinary power of her ancestral lineage.[5][6]
Carmila's transformation into a Blood Dragon substantially increased her physical abilities, elemental compatibility, and potential for further development. However, her greatest advantage over many younger members of the family remains her accumulated martial experience. During training against [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]], Natashia, [[Natasha Elderblood Fulger]], and [[Victoria Fulger Elderblood]], Carmila demonstrated the ability to respond to four powerful [[Blood Dragons]] simultaneously, exploit their inefficiencies, and adapt to their immense speed without relying exclusively upon superior physical power. [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] regarded her technical skill exceptionally highly and estimated that Carmila had reached the limit of [[Poseidon|the Master]] Stage in dagger techniques, with enlightenment remaining the principal obstacle to entering the Grandmaster Stage. This assessment was explicitly [[Scathach]]'s judgment rather than a confirmed breakthrough.[2][3]

#### Blood Dragon Physiology
Carmila in her Blood Dragon form.
Carmila is currently a True Blood Dragon, having undergone the racial transformation performed by [[Victor Elderblood]] upon the members of his family. This transformation reconstructed her previous Vampiric and spiritual characteristics into a Draconic existence, preserving the distinctive nature of her Lightning-Spirit Bloodline while granting her the superior physical foundation, Energy capacity, and developmental potential of a True Dragon. Her transformation occurred alongside the other principal Fulger women, whose ancestral Lightning reacted especially strongly to Victor's Blood Dragon power.[6]
Unlike her previous Vampire bodies, which were restricted by their ability to withstand the immense power of her ancestral element, Carmila's Draconic physiology can accommodate the Lightning-Spirit characteristics naturally. This allows her to express her elemental power through her physical body without the same limitations that affected earlier generations of the Fulger family.[6][7]

##### Taranis, the Daggers of the Ancestral Spirit
[[Taranis]], the Daggers of the Ancestral Spirit is a pair of golden Runic daggers originally associated with Carmila and subsequently preserved as one of the most important hereditary treasures of Clan Fulger. The weapons were constructed through Dwarven craftsmanship associated with Nidavellir, the same supernatural tradition responsible for creating exceptional weapons such as Mjolnir. Their Runic enchantments were adapted specifically to the [[Fulger Bloodline]], allowing legitimate wielders to channel and control the family's volatile Lightning more effectively.[8]
The daggers eventually passed through later leaders of Clan Fulger and were entrusted to [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] during her development as the family's heiress. Although Carmila's historical reputation is closely associated with the weapons, their possession is not necessary for her to retain the martial skill she developed throughout her original lifetime.[8][4]
- Divine Runic Weaponry: Taranis consists of two supernatural golden daggers engraved with Runes and preserved as ancestral treasures of Clan Fulger.[8]
- Bloodline Recognition: The daggers recognize individuals possessing the Fulger Bloodline and are specifically adapted for use by legitimate members of the family.[8]
- Unauthorized Wielder Rejection: Individuals who do not possess the Fulger Bloodline are rejected by the weapons, which can release Lightning against those attempting to handle them without the necessary hereditary compatibility.[8]
- Lightning Control Enhancement: Taranis assists its wielder in controlling the powerful and volatile Lightning associated with the Fulger Bloodline.[8]
- Runic Enchantments: The daggers possess supernatural Runic engravings responsible for their inherited restrictions and elemental properties.[8]
- Dwarven Craftsmanship: The weapons originated from the supernatural Dwarven forging traditions associated with exceptional Divine artifacts.[8]
- Ancestral Weapon Inheritance: Taranis was preserved as a hereditary treasure and passed through successive generations of Clan Fulger, eventually reaching Sasha.[8]
