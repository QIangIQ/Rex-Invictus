### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 659
Helena Gremory Elderblood is a former half-Succubus Demon, the only known surviving heir of the deceased Duke Gremory, one of [[Victor Elderblood]]'s original Demonic Generals and later one of his wives. Introduced after the effective collapse of House Gremory, Helena was initially a deeply traumatized young Demon living among the ruins of her father's territory with little apparent desire to continue living. Her fortunes changed when [[Vine]] Elderblood recruited her into Victor's rapidly expanding forces and Victor became the first powerful figure Helena encountered who showed no interest in exploiting the prestige of the Gremory Bloodline. Instead, he asked what Helena herself could contribute.[3][4]
Helena answered that question by becoming one of the most important administrators and strategists of Victor's new order. She earned the title The Strategist through her ability to devise large-scale military operations and plans extending across years, eventually sharing the highest position beneath Victor in Hell with Vine and Vepar. Whereas Vine became the primary military commander and Vepar specialized in logistics and administration, Helena and four personally selected commanders became responsible for much of the new government's internal and external policy.[5]
Her loyalty eventually developed beyond ordinary service. Helena remained beside Victor through the destruction of [[Diablo]]'s old regime, participated in the liberation of [[Lilith Elderblood]], helped [[Lily Elderblood]] assassinate [[Bael]], assisted with the political and architectural reconstruction of Hell and eventually became recognized as one of Victor's Demonic wives.[6][7][1] She was later transformed into a Blood Dragon and True Dragon as Victor continued evolving his family, joining Clan Elderblood and the [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]].[8] Helena and Victor eventually had an unnamed daughter who is shown among the later generation of Elderblood Princesses.[2]

### Physical Description
Helena was introduced as an extraordinarily beautiful female Demon possessing the characteristic features of a Succubus mixed with the Gremory bloodline. She has long reddish-black hair, pale skin, dark crimson-red eyes surrounded by black eyeliner, two horns, a pair of Demonic wings and a tail. [[Vine]] immediately recognized her Succubus ancestry from her appearance despite Helena being only a half-breed rather than a pure Succubus.[3]
Her beauty is explicitly supernatural enough that an ordinary mortal seeing her would regard her as exceptionally attractive. Nevertheless, the most striking feature of Helena during her debut was not her appearance but her expression. Her eyes were described as lifeless or "dead", with Vine immediately concluding that Helena had experienced severe trauma. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] independently recognized the same expression and compared it to [[Eve Elderblood]] when he had first found Eve: the gaze of someone who had essentially lost the will to live.[3][4]
Helena normally retained the visible traits of her Demonic heritage while serving Victor in Hell, frequently appearing in armor when participating in military campaigns. Unlike many Succubi, whose presentation deliberately emphasizes seduction, Helena's bearing is generally formal and military. Her wings also provide natural flight, something she frequently uses while accompanying Victor and the other Generals through Hell.[9]
Following her transformation into a Blood Dragon, Helena's existence changed from that of a half-Succubus Demon into a Demonic True Dragon. She consequently gained the fundamental physical and spiritual characteristics of Victor's [[Blood Dragons]] while retaining traits derived from her original Demonic nature.[8] Her numerous official illustrations depict both less overtly Demonic humanoid appearances and forms retaining visible horns, wings and other supernatural characteristics.

### Personality and Traits
Helena was initially emotionally numb, cynical and deeply traumatized. When [[Vine]] found her, she spoke about her father's permanent death and the possibility of being killed or used as a breeding tool because of her Gremory Bloodline with almost complete emotional neutrality. Vine immediately recognized that such statements were not the reactions of a sheltered aristocratic heiress and concluded that Helena had survived enough hardship to leave lasting psychological damage.[3]
Despite this emotional detachment, Helena was never naive. She accurately judged Vine's intentions, revealed her identity only after determining that Vine probably did not intend to harm her and quickly understood the significance of the strange loyalty Vine displayed toward [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Her intelligence impressed Vine almost immediately, who considered Helena a "diamond in the rough" rather than merely the daughter of a fallen Pillar.[3]
Helena is extremely proper and disciplined for a Demon. Even after Vine repeatedly told her to dispense with formalities, Helena insisted upon calling her "Lady Vine" until her own accomplishments actually made them equals. Vine jokingly regarded this excessive politeness as Helena's only apparent flaw, remarking that she behaved more like an Angel than the stereotypically chaotic inhabitants of Hell.[3]
Her greatest personal pride is her strategic ability. Helena was raised as the heir of a Demon territory whose army had become comparatively weak, forcing her to solve problems through planning rather than overwhelming strength. As a result, she learned an unusually ruthless and pragmatic form of warfare that the narration compares more closely to Human cruelty than conventional Demonic battle. She became accustomed to achieving victory with insufficient forces, manipulating the battlefield and treating every available resource as part of the strategy.[4]
Victor's first conversation with Helena affected her profoundly because he deliberately separated her personal worth from her father's bloodline. Helena initially answered his question about her talents by beginning, "As a Gremory...", only for Victor to interrupt and explain that he had asked what she could do and did not care about the Gremory name. Helena realized that nearly everyone she had ever encountered—including people living in her own territory—had viewed her first as Gremory's descendant and only secondarily as Helena. Victor was the first ruler to reverse that priority.[4]
The change was small externally but significant internally. The lifeless quality in Helena's eyes gained a visible spark for the first time, and her determination to prove herself to Victor became considerably more personal than ordinary ambition. When Vepar later suggested that Victor's impossible zero-casualty conquest assignment might only be a test of her reasoning rather than something he genuinely expected, Helena insisted that she would attempt to fulfill the order literally because Victor had been the first person to recognize her as Helena rather than a bloodline.[4]
This became the foundation of Helena's exceptional loyalty. She is not mindlessly obedient; her loyalty originates from the fact that Victor gave her individual value, authority and an opportunity to use abilities that had been wasted beneath the old order. By Chapter 677, Helena, Vine and Vepar were infamous for reacting extremely harshly to insults directed at Victor, and Demons understood that disrespecting their King in front of his Generals could have severe consequences.[5]
Helena also develops a noticeable perfectionistic streak regarding Victor. She and [[Aline Elderblood Valefar]] repeatedly insist that anything belonging to their King must be sufficiently grand, technologically impressive and luxurious to reflect his status. Centuries later, Helena was still dissatisfied with structures that she considered too modest for Victor and argued that even his personal residences should be enlarged to dimensions appropriate for a Dragon King.[10]
Despite her normally calm demeanor, Helena can become flustered by Victor's personal affection. During the final Demonic War, Victor casually called her "Helen, dear", causing the usually composed strategist to blush before immediately carrying out his command.[6] Her original loyalty to Victor gradually evolved into genuine romantic attachment, and she ultimately became one of his wives rather than remaining solely a subordinate.[1]

### History

#### House Gremory
Helena is the daughter of the former Duke Gremory and an unnamed Succubus. Her existence surprised [[Vine]], who had known enough of Gremory's past to believe that he would never recover emotionally from the death of his former wife. Helena nevertheless inherited the Gremory Bloodline strongly enough to become his sole known surviving heir.[3]
The Gremory family had once possessed a far more prestigious position in Hell. After [[Sitri]] successfully schemed against Gremory, the Pillar lost much of his territory, influence and former standing. By the time Vine reached the Gremory domain, the once-beautiful city had deteriorated into something resembling a ruined slum and the palace itself was close to collapse.[3]
Helena grew up in the aftermath of that decline. The exact events responsible for her psychological trauma are not fully explained, but the novel makes the effects obvious: she expected powerful Demons to either kill her or exploit her bloodline reproductively, displayed almost no emotional reaction when discussing horrific possibilities and openly referred to her inheritance as a burden rather than a privilege.[3]
Gremory himself eventually died permanently under circumstances unknown even to Helena. She had only recently learned the truth through an emissary of the Demon King by the time Vine arrived. Helena did not know whether [[Diablo]], Sitri or another Demon had killed him and showed little grief when discussing the possibilities, instead speaking of her father and his ruined territory with open disdain.[3]
His death left Helena as the last known heir of the Gremory Pillar line and the de facto inheritor of a broken territory.

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s campaign through Hell, [[Vine]] was ordered to conquer or eliminate several lower-ranked Pillar territories. After defeating the Pillars between approximately Ranks 46 and 55, Vine reached the ruined domain belonging to Gremory expecting to demand his submission to her new master.[3]
Instead, she discovered Helena inside the decaying palace. Helena calmly informed her that Gremory was permanently dead. Vine initially considered the possibility that she was lying but could detect no deception in her statements.[3]
Once Helena revealed herself as the surviving Gremory heir, Vine immediately began evaluating her potential. Helena's dead expression revealed obvious trauma, but Vine also noticed intelligence, caution and an apparent willingness to commit herself completely if someone finally gave her something worth protecting. Vine therefore explained who Victor Alucard was and invited Helena to join his expanding faction.[3]
Helena accepted almost immediately. The remaining Demons of Gremory's territory followed their heiress, increasing Vine's forces and helping her continue the conquest of neighboring Pillar cities. Helena participated in these campaigns wearing full armor and quickly proved that she was far more useful as a strategist than as a symbolic aristocratic heir.[3]
Vine became particularly impressed by the combination of Helena's abilities. The Gremory Bloodline allowed her to increase or decrease the mass of physical objects, while her Succubus heritage gave her advanced aptitude in Dream [[Magic]]. Vine immediately realized that either ability could be extremely dangerous in the hands of someone sufficiently creative, and Helena possessed exactly that kind of strategic mind.[3]
Helena met Victor personally after Vine returned from eliminating the remaining lower-ranked Pillars. Although every other Demon reacted nervously beneath Victor's gaze, Helena remained outwardly composed and identified herself directly as the only living heir of Gremory.[4]
Victor immediately noticed the emptiness in her expression. Instead of discussing her bloodline, he asked what Helena herself knew how to do. When Helena automatically attempted to answer by describing what a Gremory could do, Victor corrected her and stated that he cared about Helena, not the name she inherited from her father.[4]
The simple distinction produced one of Helena's first visible positive emotional reactions. She realized that Victor was treating her as an individual rather than a surviving piece of the Gremory bloodline and finally explained what she considered her real talent: territory administration, warfare and above all the creation of strategies designed to guarantee victory.[4]
Victor decided to test that confidence immediately. Helena received command over 50,000 Lesser Demons together with Vine and was ordered to conquer the territory of the 39th-ranked Pillar without suffering a single casualty. The challenge was extreme because Pillars around that rank could command armies numbering in the millions.[4]
Rather than reject the assignment, Helena requested two weeks and promised Victor victory. Even after Vepar pointed out that the impossible casualty restriction might itself be part of the test, Helena decided that she would attempt to satisfy Victor's order exactly because he had been the first ruler to recognize her personal identity.[4]
Helena's performance exceeded the level expected from a newly recruited heir. Later accounts state that she proved her worth to Victor by successfully dominating multiple Pillar-ranked cities using comparatively tiny numbers of Demons, establishing herself as one of the most effective commanders produced during his conquest of Hell.[5]
Victor also empowered Helena, Vine and Vepar with his own Energy. By the time his army reached the lower levels of Hell, the three women were treated as the highest-ranking figures beneath Victor himself and were described as possessing strength comparable to members of the Top 10 Demon Pillars. Their military and administrative competence ensured that their authority was not based solely upon Victor's personal favoritism.[9]
Helena accompanied Victor through the final stages of the conquest and entered the deepest levels of Hell beside Vine and Vepar when he moved to claim the Demon King's throne. When Victor shattered the supposedly indestructible gate separating Hell's levels, Helena reacted with the tired disbelief of someone gradually becoming accustomed to watching her new King repeatedly destroy rules the inhabitants of Hell had considered absolute for millennia.[9]
Victor's conquest eventually made Helena one of the three original Generals of the new Demonic order: Vine the [[Destroyer]], Vepar the Proud, and Helena the Strategist. These titles emerged naturally among the Demons because of the reputations the three women developed rather than being personally assigned by Victor.[5]
Helena's role quickly extended far beyond battlefield command. Victor's reforms dismantled much of Hell's old hereditary political structure and replaced it with a merit-based hierarchy in which Demons could obtain rank through competence and service rather than ancestry alone.[5]
Within this system, Helena became one of the most important political officials in Hell. She personally selected four commanders and, together with them, became responsible for substantial portions of the new government's internal and external policy. Her plans could extend across several years and involved political, military and intelligence considerations rather than simple battlefield tactics.[5]
Helena also functioned as a gatekeeper for high-level access to Victor. Even influential figures such as Lily understood that individuals generally would not be allowed to speak privately with the King unless Helena judged the matter important enough. Lily herself occupied a major political position but was technically beneath Helena in the developing governmental hierarchy, and the two frequently worked together.[5]
Her responsibilities also included detecting threats operating in secret. Lily specifically considered Helena competent enough at tracking hidden political forces that concealing her son Luka's participation in an Ancient Pillar rebellion would eventually become impossible. This reputation made Helena one of the principal counter-conspiracy figures within Victor's new Hell.[5]
Helena remained beside Victor when the Demonic War returned to Earth and [[Diablo]]'s remaining order began collapsing. Together with Lily and Vepar, she participated in the operation that freed [[Lilith Elderblood]] from Diablo's control, allowing the former Demon Queen to regain her true form and directly confront her captor.[11]
She then helped eliminate [[Bael]], Diablo's Rank 1 Pillar and right hand. Helena and Lily simultaneously pierced Bael's body, while Lily used Sacred Dark Light to prevent him from resisting effectively. Once Lily finished explaining that their political marriage no longer benefited her, Helena shattered a vial of Victor's blood directly against Bael's chest.[6]
Victor manipulated the blood from a distance, causing Bael's body to collapse into a small sphere of blood. Helena recovered it and carried it directly to Victor, kneeling beside Vine, Vepar and Lily as the surviving elite Demons of Diablo's faction were systematically eliminated and converted into similar spheres for Victor to consume.[6]
Victor praised his Generals for their work, and Helena visibly took pride in receiving his approval. Shortly afterward, when Victor affectionately addressed her as "Helen, dear" while ordering the Demonic forces back to Hell, Helena briefly blushed before carrying out his command.[6]
The battle permanently destroyed the previous Demonic political hierarchy. Helena, who had begun the arc as the traumatized heir of a ruined Pillar family, ended it standing among the senior commanders of the new King of Hell.
Following Diablo's downfall, Helena became deeply involved in rebuilding Hell rather than simply governing the territory Victor had conquered. Her Gremory ability proved exceptionally useful on a civilizational scale because increasing or reducing mass could be used not only in combat but also in architecture and resource development.
One of the clearest examples was the construction of The Seven Pillars in Abaddon, Victor's new Royal Capital. [[Aline Elderblood Valefar]] and her development department created seven extraordinarily durable structures using some of Hell's strongest materials, Victor supplied the Energy necessary to reinforce them, and Helena used the Gremory ability to enlarge the completed buildings to skyscraper scale.[7]
The resulting structures contained tens of thousands of rooms and became prestigious residences for high-merit Demons. This gave Helena's inherited ability a significance the old Gremory family had never fully exploited: instead of merely creating wealth or converting small objects into heavy weapons, Mass Manipulation became a tool for rapidly constructing the infrastructure of an entire supernatural civilization.[7]
Helena remained one of the officials who helped turn Abaddon from a traditional Demonic city into a highly developed metropolis. As Hell incorporated technology, Divine materials, Magic and resources obtained from Victor's conquests, the Royal Capital eventually acquired skyscrapers, floating structures, advanced transportation and massive governmental complexes.[12]
Even after Hell became technologically advanced enough to impress Gods visiting from other Pantheons, Helena remained personally invested in ensuring its architecture reflected Victor's status. She and Aline repeatedly pushed for more extravagant palaces, fortresses and residences, considering ordinary dimensions inappropriate for a Dragon King.[10]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
By the time [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] consolidated the former Egyptian territories and reorganized [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]], Helena's relationship with him had developed far beyond that of General and King. Chapter 888 openly treats Helena, [[Vine]], Vepar, Lily and the other Demonic women accompanying them as Victor's wives, with [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood directly telling them that formal deference toward her was unnecessary because they shared the same husband.[1]
Helena arrived in the reclaimed Egyptian territory together with the other Demonic wives and complete Demonic Legions in order to establish a permanent base there. She immediately resumed her familiar command role, directing her commanders to spread throughout the territory and prevent conflicts during the enormous migration of Victor's allied populations.[1]
Her old form as a half-Succubus eventually ceased being the limit of her existence. During Victor's large-scale transformation of compatible members of his family, Helena was evolved into a Blood Dragon and True Dragon. Her original Demonic characteristics influenced the resulting species, creating a Demonic Dragon rather than erasing the traits that had defined her previous existence.[8]
As a True Dragon, Helena's potential, physical existence and natural understanding of Draconic power increased enormously. She became a formal member of Clan Elderblood and the increasingly Divine family forming the core of Dragons Nest.
Her position as Victor's wife was later further integrated into the Elderblood family's [[Soul Marriage]] structure. Although Helena began as a subordinate who followed Victor because he gave her purpose and recognition, she eventually became one of the women metaphysically connected to him as family rather than merely one of the Generals serving his throne.[13]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Helena continued serving the Elderblood civilization after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s transformation from a regional supernatural sovereign into God Emperor and Overlord. Her original specialty remained extremely valuable even in a civilization operating on planetary and Galactic scales: Helena had always been trained to think in terms of long-term strategy, political consequences, resource allocation and victory conditions rather than merely winning individual battles.
The later Elderblood family also gave Helena something she had lacked during her life as the heir of Gremory: a stable family that did not value her only because of her bloodline. Her position shifted from being the sole surviving heir of a ruined Demonic house to being one of many powerful women integrated into a much larger Clan where her strategic talent remained personally useful regardless of ancestry.
Helena and Victor eventually had an unnamed daughter. By Chapter 1120, that daughter is shown among the enormous younger generation of Elderblood Princesses and appears alongside the daughters of Pepper, Lacus, Siena and other wives.[2]
The existence of Helena's daughter completes one of the strongest contrasts in her character arc. When [[Vine]] first discovered Helena, the young Demon immediately assumed that a stronger faction might use her as a breeding tool because she possessed the Gremory Bloodline.[3] Thousands of years later, Helena forms a family by choice with the first ruler who explicitly told her that he did not care about the value of that bloodline and instead wanted to know what she herself could accomplish.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] fundamentally changed the direction of Helena's life from their first conversation. Prior to meeting him, every major relationship surrounding Helena had treated her through the political value of the Gremory name. Victor immediately rejected that framework and asked for Helena's own talents rather than those inherited from her father.[4]
Helena responded by dedicating herself to proving that the faith was justified. What began as the loyalty of a talented subordinate became increasingly personal as Victor rewarded competence, empowered her, trusted her with genuine authority and allowed her to become one of the architects of his new Hell rather than merely the surviving ornament of an old Demon family.
By Chapter 677, rumors already existed among Demons concerning the particularly intimate relationship between Victor and his three Generals, although the chapter treats those rumors humorously rather than as formal confirmation.[5] Later chapters remove the ambiguity: Helena is explicitly recognized as one of Victor's wives, eventually joins Clan Elderblood through [[Soul Marriage]] and has a daughter with him.[1][2]

#### Vine Elderblood
[[Vine]] was the person who discovered Helena and therefore indirectly responsible for her entire later future. Although Vine initially evaluated her in a calculating Demonic fashion, she rapidly developed genuine respect for Helena's talents and predicted that Helena would eventually become her equal.[3]
Helena initially refused to accept that equality before proving herself, continuing to formally call her "Lady Vine". Their relationship eventually developed into that of fellow Generals standing directly beneath [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], with Vine becoming the major military arm of the new government while Helena handled strategic policy.[5]

#### Vepar Elderblood
Helena and Vepar became close professional partners during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s conquest of Hell. Vepar's specialty in logistics and administration naturally complemented Helena's long-term strategic planning, and the two frequently discussed Victor's orders together while working out how seemingly impossible objectives could actually be implemented.[4]
They later became fellow Generals and eventually fellow wives within Clan Elderblood.

#### Lily Elderblood
Although Lily possesses significantly older political connections and an exceptionally prestigious lineage, Helena eventually became her superior within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s new governmental structure. The two worked together frequently because Lily managed much of the politics involving upper-class Demons while Helena supervised broader internal and external policy.[5]
Their cooperation became particularly direct during [[Bael]]'s assassination, when Helena and Lily personally restrained and wounded him before Helena introduced Victor's blood into his body.[6]

#### Aline Elderblood Valefar
Helena and Aline work particularly well together because their abilities solve different parts of large infrastructure problems. Aline supplies scientific and engineering knowledge while Helena's Gremory ability can alter the scale and mass of constructed objects. Their cooperation contributed directly to the rapid reconstruction of Hell and buildings such as the Seven Pillars.[7]
Both women also share an intense desire to build residences, fortresses and cities worthy of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s status, occasionally to the point where other members of the family consider their ambitions excessive.[10]

- Helena and Lily jointly attacked [[Bael]] immediately before his death.[6]
- Helena personally broke a vial of Victor's blood against Bael, allowing Victor to manipulate the blood inside his body and collapse him into a sphere.[6]
- Victor once affectionately called her "Helen, dear", causing Helena to blush despite her normally controlled demeanor.[6]
- Helena's Gremory ability was used during construction of the Seven Pillars skyscrapers of Abaddon, allowing completed structures to be increased to enormous size.[7]

#### Skills and Abilities
Helena is one of the most powerful Demons who submitted to [[Victor Elderblood]] during his conquest of Hell and later evolved far beyond the limits of her original species. She was born as a half-Succubus possessing the powerful Gremory Bloodline, inherited advanced Dream [[Magic]] from her Succubus ancestry and displayed exceptional strategic intelligence from a comparatively young age. After Victor personally strengthened Helena with his Energy, she, [[Vine]] and Vepar reached a level of power comparable to the former Top 10 Pillars of Hell.[1][2]
Her later transformation into a Blood Dragon and True Dragon further elevated every aspect of her existence, transforming her former half-Succubus nature into that of a Demonic Dragon while preserving and strengthening the racial abilities and Bloodline powers she already possessed.[3]

##### Blood Dragon / True Dragon Physiology
Blood Dragon Physiology: Helena was transformed by [[Victor Elderblood]] into one of his [[Blood Dragons]], an evolved Dragon race created through Victor's power. Her transformation elevated her Soul, body and pre-existing supernatural characteristics to the level of a True Dragon, while her original Demonic heritage influenced the resulting transformation and caused her to develop as a Demonic Dragon.[3]
As a Blood Dragon, Helena possesses the extraordinary physical and supernatural characteristics associated with Victor's Dragon lineage. Her former Demonic powers did not simply disappear; instead, they became incorporated into her new Draconic existence.
- Immense Strength: [[True Dragons]] possess tremendous physical power even without supernatural reinforcement. Helena's physical abilities increased enormously following her evolution, placing her far beyond the level she possessed as an ordinary half-Succubus Demon.[3]
- Immense Speed and Reflexes: Helena possesses supernatural movement speed, reaction time and combat perception consistent with beings of True Dragon physiology.
- Immense Durability: Her Draconic body possesses exceptionally dense and resistant flesh, bones and supernatural defenses capable of enduring levels of force that would destroy ordinary supernatural beings.
- Enhanced Stamina: Helena can function and fight for prolonged periods without experiencing the physical exhaustion characteristic of mortal species.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Blood Dragons possess extremely powerful regeneration derived from the combination of Victor's Blood-based nature and True Dragon physiology. Injuries that would be fatal to most species can be recovered from so long as Helena's existence has not been sufficiently damaged on a deeper metaphysical level.
- [[Immortality]] / Extreme Longevity: Helena no longer possesses the biological lifespan limitations of ordinary living species. Her previous Demonic longevity was already enormous, and her later existence as a True Dragon further elevated this characteristic.
- Enhanced Senses: Helena possesses greatly heightened sight, hearing, smell, Energy perception and supernatural awareness.
- Supernatural Energy Reserves: True Dragons possess naturally enormous amounts of Energy. Helena's already considerable Demonic reserves were further expanded by her Blood Dragon transformation.
- Flight: Helena possessed functional Demonic wings even before becoming a Blood Dragon and could naturally fly. Her Draconic evolution greatly enhanced this ability.[2]
- Shapeshifting: As a True Dragon, Helena can alter her body between more humanoid and Draconic states. Dragons naturally possess the ability to adopt humanoid forms despite the enormous size of their true bodies.[3]
- Dragon Form: Helena possesses a True Dragon form corresponding to her Demonic Dragon nature. The complete appearance and maximum size of Helena's individual Dragon Form have not yet been described in sufficient detail to assign exact measurements.
- Draconic Natural Weapons: In Dragon form, Helena possesses the natural offensive characteristics of Dragons, including claws, fangs, wings and an enormously powerful physical body.
- Dragon Breath: As a True Dragon, Helena possesses the racial capability to release supernatural breath attacks. The exact Energy nature and individual characteristics of Helena's breath have not yet been explicitly demonstrated.
- Draconic Presence: True Dragons naturally exert immense supernatural pressure upon weaker beings simply through their presence, especially when revealing greater portions of their true nature.
- Blood Dragon Compatibility: Helena's transformation did not eliminate her previous racial abilities. Instead, the qualities originating from her Demonic and Succubus bloodlines became incorporated into her new Blood Dragon species.[3]

###### Draconic Runes
Draconic Runes: After becoming a True Dragon, Helena gained the instinctive connection to the Draconic Language possessed by members of the race. Unlike ordinary supernatural beings who must painstakingly decipher the language, Draconic Runes are naturally comprehensible to [[True Dragons]] because the language is fundamentally tied to their species.[4][3]
Draconic Runes allow Helena to interact with supernatural principles through the language of Dragons, although the novel has not yet individually demonstrated Helena creating a specific named Rune or Runic technique.

##### Former Demon / Succubus Physiology
Half-Succubus Demon Physiology: Before becoming a Blood Dragon, Helena was a high-potential Demon born from Duke Gremory and an unnamed Succubus. Her mixed ancestry granted her both the supernatural traits of high-ranking Demons and the specialized abilities associated with Succubi.[1]
Although she is no longer biologically an ordinary Demon, these abilities form part of the foundation from which her Demonic Dragon nature developed.
- Demonic Strength: Even prior to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s empowerment, Helena possessed physical abilities vastly superior to ordinary Humans and Lesser Demons.
- Demonic Speed and Reflexes: Her supernatural physiology granted enhanced movement and reaction speed.
- Demonic Durability: Helena's body naturally possessed greater resistance to physical trauma than mortal organisms.
- Demonic Regeneration: Like other powerful Demons, Helena could recover from injuries beyond the capabilities of ordinary living beings.
- Demonic Longevity: Helena had already lived for an enormous period compared with Humans before her Draconic transformation.
- Miasma Manipulation: As a Demon of Hell, Helena naturally possesses affinity with Miasma, the primary Negative Energy used throughout Demonic society.
- Miasma Absorption: Demons can use the Miasma naturally produced throughout Hell as an Energy source.
- Miasma Reinforcement: Helena can reinforce her physical body and supernatural abilities using Demonic Energy.
- Demonic Wings: Helena naturally possessed two wings before becoming a Dragon and regularly used them for aerial movement.[2]
- Demonic Horns and Tail: Her original body possessed the characteristic horns and tail associated with her Demonic/Succubus lineage.
- Supernatural Beauty: Succubi possess supernatural physical attractiveness as an inherent racial trait. Helena is repeatedly described as extraordinarily beautiful even among supernatural women.
- Seductive Presence: Helena inherited the natural supernatural allure associated with Succubi, although unlike many members of that race she rarely relies upon seduction as her primary method of interaction or combat.
- Dream Affinity: Succubi possess an inherent connection to Dreams, and Helena demonstrated unusually advanced talent in this field even before joining Victor.[1]
Demonic [[Immortality]] (Formerly): Powerful Demons possess forms of survival associated with their Souls and Hell itself, allowing some Demons whose bodies are destroyed outside their native realm to eventually return under the proper circumstances. Helena's later transformation into a Blood Dragon fundamentally changed her species, so her current immortality should primarily be attributed to her Draconic existence rather than assuming every old Demonic resurrection mechanism continues to function identically.

##### Gremory Bloodline
Mass Manipulation: Helena inherited the signature supernatural ability of the Gremory Bloodline. She can freely increase or decrease the mass of physical objects, an ability whose applications range from economics and construction to potentially devastating battlefield attacks.[1]
Despite sounding simple, the ability was historically responsible for much of House Gremory's wealth and military power. Helena eventually surpassed the previous limitations demonstrated by her father and earlier users of the Bloodline.[5]

###### Mass Increase
Helena can drastically increase the mass of an object without requiring a corresponding amount of ordinary matter.[1]
The previous Gremory demonstrated how dangerous this principle could become: a small object such as a stone could be thrown normally and have its mass increased while travelling, effectively transforming an ordinary projectile into something comparable to a meteor upon impact.[1]
Helena inherited the same basic principle and later demonstrated substantially greater control over the Bloodline.

###### Mass Reduction
Helena can reduce the mass of physical objects, allowing extremely large or naturally heavy structures to become dramatically easier to transport, manipulate or reposition.[1]
This application is particularly valuable for logistics and construction because objects that would ordinarily require enormous supernatural strength or machinery can temporarily have their effective mass reduced.

###### Size Manipulation
Helena's mastery eventually developed beyond simply making objects heavier or lighter. By Chapter 698, the Gremory Bloodline is explicitly used to increase or decrease the physical size of objects.[5]
This allowed Helena to transform pre-built structures into enormous constructions after their basic form had already been completed.

###### Object Enlargement
Helena can enlarge physical objects to many times their original dimensions. One of the greatest demonstrated applications of her ability was the construction of the Seven Pillars of Abaddon. [[Aline Elderblood Valefar]] and the Technological Development Department first created seven identical structures using some of the strongest materials available in Hell, after which Helena used the Gremory Bloodline to cause the buildings to expand into gigantic skyscrapers containing more than 70,000 rooms collectively.[5]
This feat was particularly impressive because the materials involved were comparable to those used in some of Hell's strongest ancient constructions.

###### Object Reduction
The same principle allows Helena to reduce the physical dimensions of objects, giving the power considerable logistical potential for transporting or storing structures and equipment.
The maximum reduction Helena can achieve has not been explicitly established.

###### Permanent Physical Alteration
The Gremory ability is capable of producing changes that persist rather than functioning merely as temporary telekinetic effects. House Gremory historically exploited this property economically by increasing the mass of valuable substances such as gold, turning a small quantity of material into a much greater amount.[1]
Helena inherited this property together with the rest of the Bloodline.

###### Resource Multiplication
Because Helena can increase the mass of physical substances, her power can theoretically multiply the usable quantity of existing materials. This ability was one of the reasons the Gremory family historically possessed considerable wealth.[1]
It can also have enormous value within an industrial society by allowing strategically important materials to be increased after an initial sample has been obtained, provided the target material remains within the ability's current limits.

###### High-Density Projectile Creation
Helena can potentially transform mundane objects into devastating weapons by increasing their mass after they are thrown or accelerated. The Gremory Bloodline was explicitly described as capable of turning an ordinary stone into the equivalent of a meteor through this principle.[1]
Helena's exact maximum offensive output with this application has not yet been demonstrated directly.

###### Architectural Manipulation
Helena is exceptionally effective at applying Mass and Size Manipulation to construction. Rather than requiring every colossal structure to be manufactured at its final dimensions, engineers can construct a smaller but structurally complete object before Helena enlarges it afterward.[5]
Her cooperation with Aline and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] during the creation of the Seven Pillars demonstrates the ability's enormous potential for city development, fortress construction, logistics and megastructure engineering.

###### Evolved Gremory Bloodline
Helena eventually exceeded one of the greatest limitations of previous Gremory users. The former Duke Gremory was unable to freely manipulate objects containing enormous amounts of Energy or structures made from exceptionally resistant supernatural materials.[5]
Lilith specifically notes that the previous Gremory could not simply approach something comparable to the Final Gate of Hell and shrink it because the material and supernatural power involved exceeded the limitations of his ability.[5]
Helena, however, successfully enlarged the Seven Pillars despite their construction from some of the strongest materials available in Hell and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s enormous Energy being infused throughout the structures. Lilith consequently concluded that Helena had surpassed the former Gremory.[5]
Her advancement appears closely connected to both her natural talent and the enormous enhancement she received after joining Victor.[2][5]

##### Succubus Dream Magic
Dream Manipulation: Helena is explicitly described as an expert in the Dream [[Magic]] naturally associated with Succubi despite being comparatively young by the standards of ancient Demons.[1]
She can create and manipulate Dreams through supernatural means, and her expertise was important enough for [[Vine]] to identify it immediately as one of Helena's principal abilities alongside the Gremory Bloodline.
- Dream Creation: Helena can create artificial Dream environments.
- Dream Alteration: She can manipulate the contents and conditions of an existing Dream.
- Dream Entry: As a practitioner of Succubus Dream Magic, Helena can interact with supernatural Dream spaces rather than being limited to ordinary waking Reality.
- Dream Control: Within Dreams under her influence, Helena can alter what the affected individual perceives and experiences.
- Dream-Based Psychological Manipulation: Dream Magic can be used as a method of influencing, misleading, interrogating or psychologically pressuring targets without relying upon conventional physical force.
The precise limits of Helena's Dream Magic have not been extensively demonstrated in direct combat, so abilities such as unrestricted mind control, permanent memory alteration or automatic Soul manipulation should not be attributed to her without additional chapter evidence.

##### Victor's Empowerment
Energy Empowerment: After Helena submitted to [[Victor Elderblood]], Victor directly fed his Energy into Helena, [[Vine]] and Vepar and dramatically increased their overall supernatural power.[2]
The resulting enhancement was not minor. Demons observing the three women considered them comparable in raw standing to members of the Top 10 Pillars of Hell, despite Helena never previously holding a comparable Ars Goetia Rank.[2]
This empowerment improved far more than physical strength. The later evolution of Helena's Gremory Bloodline suggests that the increased quality and quantity of her Energy allowed her inherited ability to affect objects that previous Gremory users could not manipulate.[5]
- Power Amplification: Helena's overall Energy output increased tremendously.
- Physical Enhancement: Her strength, speed, durability and stamina increased alongside her Energy.
- Ability Amplification: The output and limits of her inherited and racial abilities were raised.
- Bloodline Evolution: Helena's Gremory ability eventually exceeded the limitations previously demonstrated by Duke Gremory.[5]

##### Strategic and Intellectual Abilities
Helena's greatest contribution to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction is not purely supernatural power. She is one of the most gifted strategists among the Demons of Hell and earned the title The Strategist specifically because of her talent for warfare and long-term planning.
Strategic Genius: Helena possesses exceptional ability to analyze military situations, identify victory conditions and create plans intended to minimize unnecessary losses. Her strategic talent was already recognized by [[Vine]] immediately after recruitment.[1]
Large-Scale Warfare: Helena is particularly talented at designing strategies involving entire armies and territories rather than merely individual duels.
Long-Term Planning: Her plans can extend across years and account for political, military and logistical consequences simultaneously.[6]
Casualty-Minimization: Victor's first personal test required Helena to conquer the territory of the 39th Pillar using only 50,000 Lesser Demons and Vine while suffering zero casualties. Helena accepted the seemingly impossible requirement and immediately began constructing a plan around it.[7]
Asymmetric Warfare: Helena was accustomed to operating with inferior military resources because the ruined Gremory territory could not compete directly with stronger Pillars. She consequently learned to win through planning, deception, positioning and efficient use of limited forces rather than simply relying upon numerical superiority.[7]
Military Command: Helena can command tens of thousands of Demons and later became one of Victor's principal Generals.
Territorial Conquest: Helena proved her value by assisting in and planning the conquest of multiple Pillar territories using comparatively small forces.[8]
Battlefield Analysis: She possesses exceptional observational ability and can rapidly identify how available powers, soldiers and environmental conditions can be combined into a viable strategy.
Political Strategy: Helena and four personally selected commanders eventually became responsible for major portions of the internal and external policy of Victor's new Hell.[9]
Counterintelligence: Helena developed a reputation for identifying hidden political factions and conspiracies operating inside Hell. Even [[Lily Elderblood]] believed that concealing large-scale rebellious activity from Helena indefinitely would be extremely difficult.[10]
Administration: Helena is capable of translating military conquest into stable government rather than functioning only as a battlefield commander. She became one of the principal officials responsible for the operation and expansion of Victor's reformed Hell.
Logistics and Infrastructure Planning: Helena's strategic thinking and Mass Manipulation make her particularly effective in large construction and infrastructure projects. Her involvement with the Seven Pillars demonstrates that she can combine supernatural powers with technological engineering rather than treating them as separate disciplines.[5]

##### Leadership
Generalship: Helena is one of the original Generals serving directly beneath [[Victor Elderblood]]. Alongside [[Vine]] and Vepar, she occupied one of the highest positions beneath the new King during his conquest and restructuring of Hell.[2][11]
Command Presence: Helena can maintain authority over large Demonic military formations despite originating from a previously ruined house.
Personnel Selection: She personally selected four commanders who later worked beneath her in managing the internal and external affairs of Victor's government.[12]
Meritocratic Administration: Unlike the old Pillar hierarchy, Helena adapted extremely well to Victor's merit-based system and became one of the people responsible for making that system functional.

##### Resistances
Demonic Resistance: Helena's original high-ranking Demon physiology granted substantial resistance to physical trauma, Miasma-rich environments and the hostile natural conditions of Hell.
Miasma Resistance: As a Demon born in Hell, she can naturally survive concentrations of Negative Energy and Miasma that would be highly dangerous to most mortal species.
Enhanced Mental Discipline: Helena's strategic temperament and extensive experience give her considerable emotional and psychological control despite the severe trauma present during her introduction.
Draconic Resistance: Following her Blood Dragon transformation, Helena gained the dramatically superior physical and supernatural resistance characteristic of [[True Dragons]].[3]
Enhanced Soul Durability: True Dragon transformation alters the entire existence of the target rather than merely modifying the physical body, substantially strengthening Helena's Soul alongside her body.[3]
