Liriel Elderblood is a Dragon Goddess, Imperial Princess of the [[Elderblood Empire]], and the biological daughter of Victor Elderblood and [[Lilith Elderblood]]. She is the first known Demonic Blood Dragon God, a unique True Hybrid created when Victor's Blood Dragon Progenitor lineage successfully preserved the complete racial characteristics inherited from Lilith, the Progenitor of the Demon Race.[2]
Although Liriel's name was not revealed until Chapter 1147, her existence had been established much earlier. Chapter 989 introduced Victor and Lilith's unnamed daughter alongside the daughter born between Victor and [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]], presenting the two children as unprecedented products of two separate Progenitor lineages successfully combining with Victor's Blood Dragon Race. Lilith's daughter became the first Demonic Blood Dragon God, while Tasha's daughter became the first Nature Blood Dragon God.[2]
Liriel appeared directly again in Chapter 1048, still without a personal name, where her Demonic nature created instinctive tension with [[Ariel Elderblood]]. She was explicitly described as simultaneously a Dragon, Goddess and Demon, capable of producing Hellish Miasma so toxic that even High-Level Demons could not withstand it. Her existence was characterized through Darkness and Miasma, forming a natural contrast with Ariel's Light and Positivity.[3]
Her identity was finally revealed in Chapter 1147 as Liriel Elderblood. By then she had matured into a formidable combatant whose exceptional hybrid foundation was supplemented by Immediate Evolution, powerful Miasma, Infernal Fire, Dragonfire, Dragon Language mastery and an unusual ability to become progressively stronger through negative effects imposed upon her enemies.[1]

### Identity
Liriel's chronology requires a distinction between her first mention, first direct appearance and first named appearance.
Her first confirmed mention occurs in Chapter 989.
At the time, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family had already experienced approximately two thousand years of accelerated development. Among Victor's wives, Lilith and [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]] were particularly unusual because both remained Progenitors and Goddesses of races fundamentally different from Victor's Blood Dragon Race. Unlike most of Victor's wives, neither was simply another conventional True Dragon whose original racial foundation had been completely replaced.[2]
Victor eventually had daughters with both women.
The result was something the characters themselves had never previously observed: children born from Progenitors belonging to different [[Races (Flamel)|Races]].[2]
Chapter 989 identifies the two daughters only through their mothers:
- Victor and Lilith's Daughter
- Victor and Tasha's Daughter
Both children were born as [[True Dragons]] while completely preserving the relevant characteristics of their mothers' races. The first became the Demonic Blood Dragon God; the second became the Nature Blood Dragon God.[2]
Lilith's daughter then appeared directly in Chapter 1048 during Ariel's growing integration into Victor's household. Ariel could perceive her displeasure, her Miasma and the natural contradiction between their existences, confirming that the previously discussed daughter was an active member of Victor's household rather than merely an off-screen biological fact.[3]
Only much later, in Chapter 1147, was the character finally named Liriel Elderblood.[1]

### Physical Appearance
As an adult, Liriel is an exceptionally tall and beautiful woman standing approximately 190 centimeters. She possesses a curvaceous yet powerful physique, slightly pale skin and deep-violet eyes inherited through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s lineage.[1]
Her most distinctive characteristic is her hair.
Liriel's long black hair is not described as ordinary biological hair but as being composed of pure Miasma. This gives her Demonic nature an immediately visible manifestation even while she maintains an otherwise Human-like appearance.[1]
Her overall facial structure strongly resembles Victor. Chapter 1147 compares her with [[Stella Elderblood]] in this regard and describes Liriel as appearing almost like a female version of her father.[1]
When drawing more heavily upon her Draconic nature during combat, her appearance becomes increasingly bestial. Her facial characteristics become more Dragon-like and she can manifest sharp claws suitable for close-quarters combat.[1]
The complete appearance of Liriel's full True Dragon Form has not yet been described in sufficient detail to catalogue separately.

### Personality
Liriel is proud, aggressive, highly competitive, family-oriented and intensely devoted to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Her personality is strongly affected by the unusual combination of her two racial inheritances.
Both [[True Dragons]] and True Demons possess exceptionally powerful senses of identity and pride. Liriel inherited the characteristics of both races simultaneously, resulting in an extreme level of pride that Lilith considers one of her daughter's greatest weaknesses.[1]
Her mother consequently devotes considerable effort toward teaching Liriel that pride is valuable only while it remains under control. Liriel should honor both the Dragon and Demon Blood within her, but she must never allow ideas such as racial superiority, honor or fairness to become more important than actually defeating an enemy.[1]
This lesson is demonstrated during Liriel's training with Lilith. After repeatedly failing to catch her mother, Liriel appears increasingly frustrated and begins gathering enormous quantities of Dragonfire and Infernal Fire. Her Draconic characteristics become more pronounced and Lilith assumes that Liriel has once again allowed emotion and pride to compromise her judgment.
Lilith moves behind her.
Liriel is already waiting.
Rather than genuinely losing control, she deliberately created an apparent opening and predicted exactly how Lilith would attempt to exploit it. Her claws are already positioned against her mother's neck before Lilith can complete the attack.[1]
The success demonstrates substantial growth in Liriel's mentality. She has learned that appearing emotional and actually surrendering control to emotion are different things.
Lilith immediately reverses the situation by distorting Reality and placing her own claws against Liriel's neck.
Liriel protests that using Reality distortion is unfair.
Lilith simply reminds her that a genuine enemy will never care whether a tactic is fair.[1]
Despite her enormous pride, Liriel is capable of accepting correction. She takes the lesson seriously rather than stubbornly rejecting it, and when Lilith praises her improvement, Liriel proudly responds that failing to learn after receiving so many lessons would make her unworthy of being Victor's daughter.[1]
Her earliest direct appearance also demonstrates that Liriel's instincts can influence her social relationships. Ariel's Holy, Positive and Light-oriented nature initially causes instinctive discomfort between them because Liriel's existence is deeply aligned with Darkness and Miasma.[3]
However, this instinctive contradiction does not make Liriel incapable of befriending beings associated with opposing Elements. Chapter 1048 explicitly states that she gets along very well with [[Amaterasu]]'s daughter and [[Gaia]]'s daughter, despite those girls being associated with Light and Life. Having grown up together allowed familiarity and family bonds to overcome differences in supernatural nature.[3]
This distinction is important. Liriel is naturally Demonic, but she is not inherently hostile toward Holy or Life-aligned beings.
Her attachment to Victor is considerably stronger than any racial instinct.
When Victor later addresses the Empire and mentions the theoretical possibility of his body being destroyed, Liriel immediately ignores the intended reassurance and fixates entirely upon the possibility that her father might die.[1]
She declares:
"Father will not die. I will not allow that! I will kill anyone who threatens him."[1]
Within moments, Liriel begins considering whether Victor should simply be taken into a secure hidden Dimension and guarded permanently by his daughters.
Lilith eventually strikes her on the head and orders her to stop behaving irrationally, reminding Liriel that Victor is not someone who needs to be hidden away and protected from the universe.[1]
Liriel accepts the correction.
Her conclusion is nevertheless characteristically Elderblood:
she simply needs to become stronger.

#### True Hybrid
True Dragon blood is normally overwhelmingly dominant when reproducing with weaker species. Chapter 989 explains that a child born between an ordinary Human and True Dragon would normally be born as a complete True Dragon rather than a conventional Human–Dragon Hybrid.[2]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood Dragon lineage possesses the same aggressive genetic dominance.
Liriel nevertheless became an exception because Lilith's racial foundation was itself backed by a Progenitor and Goddess. Her mother's side was powerful enough to survive the dominance of Victor's Draconic inheritance instead of being completely overwritten.[2]
The result was a True Hybrid.
Liriel was born as a True Dragon at her Core while retaining the full relevant Aspects of the Demon Race inherited from Lilith.[2]
The same phenomenon occurred through Tasha's daughter.
Thus, Chapter 989 identifies the births of:
- the first Demonic Blood Dragon God; and
- the first Nature Blood Dragon God.[2]
These were not ordinary mixed-race children. Their existence depended upon the combination of Victor's adaptable Blood Dragon Race and mothers whose own racial traits were reinforced by Progenitor-level foundations.

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Following the long period of accelerated development experienced by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family, two particularly unusual daughters were born.
The first was the daughter of Victor and [[Lilith Elderblood]].
The second was the daughter of Victor and [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]].[2]
Their births immediately attracted the attention of [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]], Aline Elderblood Fulger and [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]], whose scientific and analytical interests made the unprecedented hybrids enormously important research subjects. Nothing exactly comparable had previously existed because the supernatural world had never observed the successful reproduction of two compatible but distinct Progenitor lineages under these circumstances.[2]
Both girls were born fundamentally as [[True Dragons]].
However, the Blood Dragon Race's adaptation prevented their maternal racial traits from simply disappearing.
Lilith's daughter inherited the characteristics of the Demon Race.
Tasha's daughter inherited those of her mother's racial foundation.
They therefore became the first known examples of two entirely new Blood Dragon variations.[2]
Lilith's daughter became the first Demonic Blood Dragon God.
Even at this early stage, several of her natural abilities were already known.
She could create and control Infernal Miasma at extremely toxic levels, generate Infernal Fire that naturally mixed with her Draconic Fire and instinctively create portals leading toward Infernos—including Infernal locations she had never personally visited.[2]
Like other True Dragons, she also possessed compatibility with the broader Elements, but the Elements naturally corresponding to her hybrid nature were far more abnormal than ordinary elemental manipulation.[2]
Her next significant appearance occurred in Chapter 1048.
By then, [[Ariel Elderblood]] was spending increasing amounts of time inside Victor's personal mansion while gradually becoming part of the family.
Ariel's presence caused tension among several daughters descended from Demonic mothers, but the strongest reaction came from Lilith's daughter.[3]
Unlike sisters who had merely been born from women with Demonic origins, Lilith's daughter was simultaneously a Dragon, Goddess and genuine Demon.
Her Hellish Miasma was so toxic that even High-Level Demons were unable to withstand it comfortably.[3]
The problem with Ariel was not primarily personal.
Their fundamental natures opposed one another.
Ariel represented Light and Positivity.
Lilith's daughter represented Darkness and Miasma.[3]
Simply existing near one another initially produced instinctive discomfort.
Ariel recognized the displeasure but chose not to force a solution. Instead, she remained accessible and allowed time and familiarity to resolve the problem naturally.[3]
The narration itself demonstrates that their elemental contradiction was not absolute. Lilith's daughter already maintained excellent relationships with the daughters of [[Amaterasu]] and [[Gaia]] despite those girls being associated with Light and Life.
The primary difference was familiarity.
They had grown up together.[3]
This period also demonstrated an important aspect of Victor's second generation: whatever their species, Divinities or inherited powers, the daughters were primarily raised to see one another as family rather than as representatives of races that had historically been enemies.

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Tens of thousands of years later, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Lilith's previously unnamed daughter was finally identified as Liriel Elderblood.[1]
By approximately Imperial Year 100,000, Liriel had matured into an exceptionally dangerous combatant and was undergoing intensive training inside Frost Hell, an environment filled with pure Miasma.[1]
Chapter 1147 describes her as the daughter of two Progenitors from two different races and states that the available writings classified her as possessing "unlimited potential".[1]
This potential came from her successful inheritance of the powers of both a True Dragon and True Demon.
The inheritance was not entirely beneficial.
She also received the undesirable traits associated with both races, particularly their extreme pride, causing Lilith to regard Liriel as one of the daughters requiring especially careful attention and discipline.[1]
Lilith's method was practical.
She fought her daughter.
When Liriel was knocked down, Lilith ordered her to rise and try again.
Liriel obeyed while her injuries began rapidly healing.[1]
The chapter then reveals another of Liriel's inherited advantages: Immediate Evolution.
Victor had acquired the original ability through one of the Elder Gods he defeated. Liriel inherited a limited version through her father.[1]
Once Liriel experiences a physical attack, her body adapts to it.
Repeating the same physical attack afterward becomes ineffective.
Her version is inferior to Victor's because it operates only upon the physical level and cannot automatically evolve against attacks operating through Concepts.[1]
Even this ability is not treated as the center of Liriel's combat style.
Her primary danger comes from the interaction of her Demonic and Draconic powers.
Liriel can generate enormously toxic Hell Miasma, wield Infernal Fire, combine that fire with Dragonfire and create portals connected to Hell. She additionally specializes in imposing negative effects upon opponents.[1]
Every negative effect successfully imposed upon an enemy increases Liriel's own power.
Consequently, prolonged combat increasingly favors her.
While her opponent accumulates harmful conditions, Liriel herself becomes progressively stronger; meanwhile, Immediate Evolution reduces the usefulness of repeatedly applying the same physical attacks against her.[1]
The narration consequently compares fighting Liriel with confronting a raid boss built around accumulating negative status effects.
Liriel had also mastered the Dragon Language and was already highly proficient in using it.[1]
Her technical combat education came primarily from [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], while Lilith concentrated upon something equally important: giving Liriel genuine battle experience.
[[Scathach]] taught her.
Lilith fought her.[1]
The combination produced visible results.
During one training confrontation, Liriel deliberately appeared to become frustrated and overwhelmed by her instincts before preparing an enormous mixture of Dragonfire and Infernal Fire.
Lilith interpreted the behavior as a lapse in discipline and moved behind her daughter.
Liriel predicted the attack.
She had deliberately created the opening.
Her claws were waiting at Lilith's neck.[1]
Lilith praised the improvement but immediately demonstrated the remaining difference between them by distorting Reality, reversing the situation and placing her own claws against Liriel.
When Liriel protested that the tactic was unfair, Lilith reminded her that a real enemy would never restrict themselves according to Liriel's preferred rules.[1]
The lesson reinforced the primary objective of Liriel's training: pride should strengthen her identity, not control her decisions.
Liriel's ambitions extended beyond personal strength.
She wanted to actively serve the [[Elderblood Empire]] and eventually intended to become a Legion Commander.[1]
[[Siren Elderblood]] possessed similar military ambitions. Both consequently trained especially hard because they did not want to remain behind sisters such as [[Nero]], Ophis and [[Metis]], who had already established important positions in Victor's civilization, or [[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]], who had chosen a scientific path.[1]
Lilith nevertheless warned her daughter against allowing ambition to create isolation.
If Liriel encountered an enemy or problem beyond her ability, she should contact her family instead of attempting to resolve everything alone.
Liriel agreed:
"I will always rely on family."[1]
Their training was interrupted when Victor addressed the Empire.
The moment Liriel heard her father's voice, her attention shifted completely.
Victor spoke of the Empire's progress, the threats waiting beyond their protected Sector and the wars that would inevitably confront them once they entered the wider cosmic civilization.
He promised that even if his physical body was destroyed, the Souls of his people would remain protected through him.[1]
Liriel reacted only to the suggestion that Victor might die.
She immediately declared that she would kill anyone who threatened him and began constructing increasingly irrational plans to hide him within a protected Dimension guarded by herself and her sisters.[1]
Lilith was forced to physically knock sense into her daughter and remind her that Victor was enormously more powerful than Liriel herself.
After calming down, Liriel abandoned the idea.
She then resolved to train harder so that she could become strong enough to protect him anyway.[1]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Liriel's biological father and the origin of her Blood Dragon lineage.
Liriel physically resembles Victor so strongly that Chapter 1147 describes her as almost a female version of him.[1]
Her supernatural nature is equally connected to him. Victor's Blood Dragon Progenitor genetics are responsible for the Draconic Core of her hybrid existence, while the adaptability of the Blood Dragon Race allowed Lilith's Progenitor-level Demon characteristics to remain intact rather than being completely overwritten.[2]
Liriel also inherited a version of Victor's Immediate Evolution.[1]
Psychologically, Victor occupies the center of her worldview.
Her pride in her own progress is repeatedly expressed through being his daughter, and her desired future as a Legion Commander is directly connected to wanting to contribute to the Empire he created.[1]
Her protectiveness toward him borders on irrational obsession.
The possibility of Victor dying, even when mentioned only as part of a hypothetical reassurance during an Imperial speech, causes Liriel to immediately consider killing anyone responsible and hiding Victor somewhere no enemy could reach him.[1]

#### Lilith Elderblood
[[Lilith Elderblood]] is Liriel's biological mother, the Demon Progenitor from whom her True Demon nature originates, and one of her principal combat instructors.
Lilith is directly responsible for Liriel inheriting the racial characteristics that transformed her from an ordinary Blood Dragon into the first Demonic Blood Dragon God.[2]
From Lilith's lineage come Liriel's exceptionally strong connection with Miasma, Infernal Fire and Hell.
Their relationship is affectionate but demanding.
Lilith refuses to indulge Liriel's pride and deliberately places her daughter in genuine combat situations where mistakes are punished immediately. Her purpose is not cruelty but preparation: a real enemy will exploit every flaw Liriel possesses, so her mother intends to expose those flaws first.[1]
Lilith is also one of the few people capable of immediately interrupting Liriel's irrational behavior regarding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].

#### Ariel Elderblood
[[Ariel Elderblood]] was one of the first characters used to demonstrate Liriel's unusual supernatural nature before her name was revealed.
Their initial relationship was uncomfortable because their existences naturally opposed one another.
Ariel embodied Light and Positivity.
Liriel embodied Darkness and Miasma.[3]
Liriel therefore reacted negatively to Ariel's increasingly frequent presence within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s personal mansion.
Ariel understood that the reaction was largely instinctive and deliberately refused to provoke or force Liriel into accepting her. She instead remained approachable and allowed familiarity to develop naturally.[3]
The story explicitly rejects the idea that opposite elemental natures make peaceful relationships impossible. Liriel already got along well with [[Amaterasu]]'s and [[Gaia]]'s daughters despite their associations with Light and Life.[3]

#### Hinata Elderblood
[[Hinata Elderblood]], previously referred to simply as [[Amaterasu]]'s daughter, is one of the sisters with whom Liriel is explicitly stated to get along well despite their contrasting supernatural natures.[3]
Hinata's association with Light did not create the same lasting problem seen during Liriel's first interactions with Ariel because the sisters had grown up together and already regarded one another as family.

### Skills & Abilities

- Dragon Goddess Physiology: Liriel is simultaneously a True Dragon, Goddess and Demon.[2][3]

- Regenerative Healing Factor: Liriel rapidly begins recovering from injuries during her combat training with Lilith.[1]
- Natural Weaponry: She can manifest sharp Draconic claws during combat.[1]
- Partial Draconic Transformation: Her facial characteristics become more Dragon-like as she draws more heavily upon her Draconic nature.[1]
- Extreme Racial Pride: Liriel inherited the powerful pride associated with both Dragons and Demons. Lilith considers this one of her daughter's major weaknesses when uncontrolled.[1]
- Miasma Manipulation: Liriel possesses an exceptional natural affinity for the Miasma of Hell.[2][3]
- Miasma Generation: She can create Hellish/Infernal Miasma directly rather than merely manipulate existing concentrations.[2]
- Extreme Toxicity: Chapter 1048 states that Liriel's Miasma can reach concentrations so toxic that even High-Level Demons cannot withstand it.[3]
- Toxicity Inducement: Her Miasma can function as a source of harmful status conditions during battle.
- Miasma-Based Hair: Liriel's black hair is physically described as being composed of pure Miasma.[1]
- Miasmic Presence: Before her name was revealed, her very presence was described through Darkness and Miasma and caused discomfort to individuals outside her family.[3]
- Infernal Fire Manipulation: Liriel naturally possesses the flames associated with her Demonic heritage.[2]
- Infernal Fire Generation: She can create and control Infernal Fire through her True Demon nature.
- Dragonfire Compatibility: Her Infernal Fire naturally mixes with the Draconic Fire inherited from Victor.[2]
- Dragonfire Manipulation: As a True Dragon, Liriel possesses Draconic Fire and can use it offensively.[2]
- Breath Attack: Liriel can gather Draconic and Infernal Fire inside her mouth and discharge the combined Energy as a destructive beam.[1]
- Infernal Fire–Dragonfire Fusion: One of Liriel's earliest identified abilities is the seamless fusion of her maternal Infernal Fire with her paternal Draconic Fire.[2]
- Composite Elemental Attack: Both supernatural flames can be used simultaneously rather than interfering with one another.
- Destructive Beam: Chapter 1147 shows Liriel gathering both powers inside her mouth before releasing them as an extremely destructive beam.[1]
- Hell Portal Creation: Liriel possesses an instinctive understanding of creating portals connected to Infernal Realms.[2]
- Unknown-Destination Access: Chapter 989 specifically states that she can create portals toward Infernos even when she does not personally know the destination.[2]
- Inter-Realm Travel: This provides Liriel with direct movement between Hell-related spaces.
- Scope Limitation: The feat should not be generalized into unrestricted portal creation to every Dimension or Reality; the canonical statement concerns Infernos/Hells.
- Elemental Manipulation: As a True Dragon, Liriel possesses the broader elemental compatibility associated with the Race.[2]
- Multiple Elemental Affinities: Chapter 989 states that Liriel and Tasha's daughter can control other Elements in addition to those naturally corresponding to their unique hybrid lineages.[2]
- Natural Elemental Specialization: Her innate Demonic-associated Elements are considerably more abnormal than ordinary secondary elemental control.[2]
- Immediate Evolution: Liriel inherited a limited version of an adaptation ability Victor acquired from an Elder God.[1]
- Reactive Adaptation: After suffering a physical attack, Liriel's body evolves in response to it.[1]
- Repeat-Attack Resistance/Negation: Once adaptation has occurred, repeating the same physical attack becomes ineffective against her.[1]
- Physical-Level Limitation: Liriel's version operates upon physical phenomena rather than the broader scale available to Victor.[1]
- No Conceptual Evolution: Unlike Victor's superior version, Liriel cannot automatically adapt to Conceptual attacks.[1]
- Secondary Ability: Despite its danger, Chapter 1147 indicates that Immediate Evolution is not even the central specialty of Liriel's combat style.[1]
- Status Effect Inducement: Liriel specializes in imposing negative effects upon her opponents.[1]

A prolonged battle can therefore create two simultaneous problems for Liriel's opponent:
- repeated physical attacks become increasingly unreliable because of Immediate Evolution; and
- successful negative effects increase Liriel's own strength.[1]
This is the basis for Chapter 1147 comparing her fighting style to a raid boss centered around status effects.
The combination does not make Liriel invincible. Her Immediate Evolution has an explicitly stated limitation against Conceptual attacks, while no unlimited upper boundary has been established for her status-based empowerment.

### Potential
Chapter 1147 describes Liriel as possessing what existing writings call "unlimited potential".[1]
This wording refers to the extraordinary possibilities created by her genetic and supernatural foundation:
- two separate Progenitor lineages;
- a True Dragon Core;
- preserved True Demon characteristics;
- Demonic Blood Dragon physiology;
- Immediate Evolution;
- Miasma and Infernal powers;
- Dragon Language mastery;
- negative-effect-based growth during battle;
- and training from some of the most dangerous combatants in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family.
It should not be interpreted as a mechanically defined ability proving that Liriel possesses literally infinite power or can automatically surpass every possible limit.
The canonical statement is an assessment of her potential.

### Future Imperial Role
Liriel intends to serve the [[Elderblood Empire]] in a military capacity and is expected to eventually become a Legion Commander.[1]
Her chosen path distinguishes her from sisters who pursue scientific, administrative or comparatively peaceful lives.
Her development toward that objective includes:
- martial instruction from [[Scathach]];
- repeated live combat against Lilith;
- mastery over her dual racial instincts;
- suppression of self-destructive pride;
- tactical deception;
- learning when to depend upon family;
- and continuous practical combat experience.
By Chapter 1147, the position remains a future role rather than a confirmed office she already holds.
