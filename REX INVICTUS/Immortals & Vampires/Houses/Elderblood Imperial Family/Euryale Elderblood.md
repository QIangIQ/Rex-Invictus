### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 1169
Euryale Elderblood is the daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Medusa]] Elderblood, and one of the second-generation members of the Elderblood Clan. She is closely associated with [[Stheno Elderblood]], the daughter of [[Roberta Elderblood]], with the pair having originally been described as Roberta and Medusa's twins.[1]
Chapter 1169 revealed that this description was only an approximation. [[Stheno]] and [[Euryale]] are actually two different girls born to two different mothers. Their unusual twin-like relationship originates from Roberta and Medusa themselves, who were two independent people sharing the same body for much of their existence. Euryale is explicitly identified as Medusa's daughter.[2]
Her name was given in honor of Euryale, one of Medusa's deceased sisters.[2]

### Physical Appearance
[[Euryale]] closely resembles [[Stheno Elderblood]] physically, explaining why the two are so easily perceived as twins despite their different mothers. Her most clearly distinguished feature is her violet-black hair.[2]
Unlike [[Stheno]]'s ordinary black hair, Euryale's hair possesses a supernatural characteristic inherited from [[Medusa]] and can transform into snakes.[2]

### Personality
[[Euryale]] is affectionate toward [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] but notably proud of her identity as a Dragon. When Victor jokingly greeted [[Stheno]] and Euryale as his "little snakes", Euryale immediately protested:
"Papa! I'm not a little snake, I'm a dragon!"[2]
Her reaction suggests a more outspoken side to her personality than Stheno displayed during their introduction. Nevertheless, she quickly became absorbed in Victor's affection alongside her sister when he patted both girls on the head.[2]
Euryale also shares Stheno's playful behavior. The girls were sufficiently prone to experimentation that Roberta immediately warned them not to do anything foolish with Victor's scales. Euryale protested alongside Stheno but obeyed without argument once [[Medusa]] appeared and firmly ordered them not to play with the material.[2]

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
[[Euryale]]'s existence was initially established without a name in Chapter 998. During [[Leon Walker]]'s visit to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family, Leon witnessed the enormous number of daughters responding to Victor and asked Anna how many children her son possessed. [[Anna Elderblood]] attempted to estimate the number from Victor's wives and noted that certain wives had produced twins, explicitly citing Roberta/Medusa. She ultimately estimated more than thirty daughters before abandoning the calculation.[1]
Neither daughter's name, individual mother or powers were explained in that chapter. The pair consequently remained indexed simply as Roberta and [[Medusa]]'s unnamed twins.[1]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Chapter 1169 formally introduced the two girls as [[Stheno]] and [[Euryale]] and clarified the unusual circumstances surrounding their births. Despite having been called twins, they are not technically twins. Stheno is Roberta's daughter, while Euryale is [[Medusa]]'s daughter. Their mothers were nevertheless so closely connected through their shared body that their daughters developed extremely similar appearances.[2]
The girls were named after Medusa's deceased sisters. While Stheno inherited Roberta's appearance, Euryale inherited Medusa Elderblood's supernatural characteristics more directly. Her violet-black hair can transform into snakes, and the narration explicitly states that she inherited Medusa's abilities.[2]
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] greeted the pair as his "little snakes", Euryale immediately corrected her father and proudly declared herself a Dragon. Victor agreed and affectionately patted both daughters on the head, distracting them long enough that they briefly forgot the reason they had approached him.[2]
After Stheno remembered their purpose and asked Victor for a scale, Victor removed two black-and-purple scales from his primary body and gave one to each girl. Euryale and Stheno were shocked by their extraordinary weight and visibly struggled to lift them. Victor explained that the material had come directly from his main body before using violet Energy to make the scales light enough for them to carry.[2]
Roberta warned the girls not to misuse them, prompting the pair to protest. Medusa then emerged from Roberta and looked at her daughters with her characteristic snake-like eyes, firmly ordering them not to play with the scales. Euryale and Stheno immediately agreed. [[Scathach]] subsequently explained why the mothers were concerned: a single scale from Victor contained enough Energy to sustain an ordinary empire for more than one hundred thousand years.[2]

### Skills & Abilities
- Dragon Physiology: [[Euryale]] explicitly identifies herself as a Dragon. As one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters, her existence belongs to the supernatural Draconic second generation of the Elderblood family.[2]
- Inherited Gorgon Abilities: The narration directly states that Euryale inherited the abilities of her mother, [[Medusa]] Elderblood. The chapter does not individually enumerate every inherited ability, so powers demonstrated by Medusa should not automatically be attributed to Euryale unless later confirmed.[2]
- Snake Transformation: Euryale's violet-black hair is capable of transforming into living snake-like forms, visibly expressing the inheritance she received from Medusa.[2]
- Enhanced Draconic Physical Characteristics: As a Dragon daughter of Victor, Euryale possesses a supernatural Draconic body. Her individual physical limits have not yet been demonstrated, and no specific destructive scale should presently be assigned to her from Chapter 1169 alone.[2]

### Trivia
- [[Euryale]] and [[Stheno Elderblood]] were originally mentioned as Roberta/Medusa's unnamed twins in Chapter 998.[1]
- Chapter 1169 clarifies that the pair are not actually biological twins; they have separate mothers but are extremely similar because of the unusual relationship between Roberta and [[Medusa]].[2]
- Euryale is specifically Medusa's biological daughter, while [[Stheno]] is specifically Roberta's biological daughter.[2]
- She was named in honor of one of Medusa's deceased sisters.[2]
- Euryale is the sister who immediately objected when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] called the pair his "little snakes", insisting that she was a Dragon.[2]
- Of the two girls, Euryale is the one explicitly stated to have inherited Medusa Elderblood's abilities.[2]
- Victor gave both Euryale and Stheno one of the scales from his true body. The scales were initially too heavy for the girls to comfortably carry until Victor altered them with his Energy.[2]
