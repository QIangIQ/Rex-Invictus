### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 337: [[Hyakki Yakō]]
Otsuki Elderblood Haruna, formerly Otsuki Haruna and Otsuki Alucard Haruna, is a Blood Dragon, the leader of the [[Otsuki Clan]], the Supreme Commander of the Hyakki Yakō, and one of the wives of [[Victor Elderblood]]. Originally a Nine-Tailed Black Kitsune, Haruna established herself as one of the strongest and most influential Yokai in Japan, consolidating numerous supernatural factions under her leadership and challenging the traditional authority exercised over Yokai society by powerful Clan leaders and the [[Shinto Pantheon]]. Her rise transformed her from an exceptionally talented martial artist into the central political authority of a large portion of Japan's Yokai population, eventually bringing her faction into an alliance with Victor and the civilization that became the [[Elderblood Empire]].[6][7]
Haruna's philosophy of rulership developed from a childhood tragedy. After witnessing the murder of her father by an Oni, she became convinced that weakness allowed others to dictate an individual's life. She devoted herself to martial training under her grandfather, [[Otsuki Yoichi]], eventually killing the Oni responsible. However, the revenge failed to satisfy her because it merely continued the cycle of hatred that had caused her own suffering. Haruna consequently concluded that the supernatural factions of Japan required a single overwhelmingly powerful ruler capable of preventing their endless disputes through authority that none could safely challenge. This conviction became the foundation of her ambition to unify the Yokai and establish a lasting peace through absolute military superiority.[3]
Haruna is also the younger sister of the deceased [[Otsuki Hana]], the former wife of [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] and biological mother of [[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]]. Because Hana's existence had been concealed from her during childhood, Haruna only discovered the relationship after encountering Ophis and noticing the girl's resemblance to herself. Learning that Hana had been killed by the Elder God Shur transformed an unfamiliar historical tragedy into a deeply personal grievance, ultimately motivating Haruna to risk her own Soul during the battle against the Elder Gods in [[Nightingale]].[4][1]
Following her alliance and developing romance with Victor, Haruna gradually abandoned her isolation from the wider supernatural world without surrendering her independence or the leadership of her people. She became one of the principal Yokai authorities within [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]], preserving her direct responsibility for Yokai affairs while accepting Victor as the ruler of the greater faction. Her relationship with him later developed into marriage, and she eventually accepted transformation into a genuine Blood Dragon. Although the conversion ended her existence as a Kitsune in the ordinary racial sense, Haruna retained the Essence and fundamental characteristics of her former nature, allowing her to assume a Nine-Tailed Fox appearance instinctively and preserve the martial traditions she had developed throughout her life.[8][2][9][10]
Haruna's Draconic evolution also advanced her toward genuine Godhood. Before becoming a Dragon, she had temporarily forced the legendary tenth-tail metamorphosis during her confrontation with Shur, nearly destroying her Soul in the process. Victor subsequently used his authority over Beginnings to guide her development and weaken the metaphysical barrier obstructing her progress. Her eventual Blood Dragon transformation provided her with a Divine state of existence, but the development of her personal Divinity remained a separate process. Haruna later suspected that her emerging Concept was connected to Foxes and the Moon, reflecting the fundamental relationship between her Kitsune ancestry, her martial techniques and the celestial symbolism surrounding her evolution.[1][11][2][10]

### Physical Description

#### Nine-Tailed Kitsune
Before becoming a Blood Dragon, Haruna appeared as an exceptionally beautiful woman approximately 170 centimeters tall, possessing pale white skin, long black hair and black eyes. Her most distinctive physical characteristics were the two large black fox ears emerging from her head and the nine long, extraordinarily fluffy black tails extending from her lower back. These characteristics identified her as a Nine-Tailed Black Kitsune, one of the most advanced forms of Yokai evolution associated with the Otsuki lineage.[6][12]
Haruna's usual clothing reflected her preference for traditional Japanese aesthetics. She frequently wore a short black kimono combining the appearance of classical Japanese clothing with a more practical skirt-like design, allowing considerable freedom of movement during combat. Her preferred weapon was a black katana, which complemented the swordsmanship and ritualized martial techniques developed through the [[Otsuki Clan]]. The contrast between her elegant appearance and the dangerous aura surrounding her made it immediately apparent that she was far more than an ordinary noblewoman or Yokai administrator.[12]
Her fox ears and tails are particularly sensitive and expressive. The tails frequently react to her emotions, moving when she is pleased, irritated or embarrassed, while her ears can twitch involuntarily when touched. Haruna normally maintains a serious and composed demeanor, but the sensitivity of these features makes it difficult for her to completely conceal her feelings from people with whom she is comfortable. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually discovered how much she enjoyed having her ears and tails stroked or brushed, and the practice became one of the most recognizable expressions of affection within their relationship.[13][8]
Haruna can also assume the complete form of a gigantic black Nine-Tailed Fox. Unlike her ordinary humanoid appearance, this transformation emphasizes the supernatural nature of her original race, granting her the appearance of an enormous predatory fox with immense physical and spiritual presence. Her advanced Kitsune physiology allowed her to maintain control over multiple elemental affinities while utilizing her martial arts, Youki and the natural characteristics associated with her tails.[14][15]

#### Ten-Tailed Moonlight Fox
During the battle against Shur, Haruna forcibly accelerated the evolution that would ordinarily have required further development of her Kitsune existence. Her body expanded into an enormous black fox approximately 270 meters tall, and a tenth tail manifested behind her. The metamorphosis was accompanied by a profound change in her supernatural presence, producing an existence described through the symbolism of a dark fox ascending toward the Moon and becoming a Divine Moonlight Fox.[1]
Unlike her ordinary Nine-Tailed transformation, this state represented an unstable breakthrough beyond the limits her Soul was prepared to accommodate. Haruna acquired an extraordinary increase in power and demonstrated the ability to unleash the technique known as The Fall of the Stars, but the forced metamorphosis endangered her spiritual existence. Following the battle, the temporary tenth tail disappeared and Haruna reverted to her former state, exhausted and suffering from the consequences of having attempted an incomplete ascension.[1][11]
The Ten-Tailed Moonlight Fox was therefore a temporary transformation rather than Haruna's permanent race before Dragonization. Its significance lies in demonstrating both the potential of her original Kitsune bloodline and her willingness to risk her own survival in pursuit of an objective she considered more important than her personal safety.[1]

#### Blood Dragon
Haruna's conversion into a Blood Dragon reconstructed her original Yokai existence through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Draconic authority. Her new natural form acquired the physical and supernatural characteristics of a True Dragon, with the Draconic traits associated with Victor's lineage replacing her previous race as the foundation of her body and Soul. However, Victor's transformation did not completely eliminate the characteristics of her former Kitsune existence. A portion of that Essence remained integrated into her new foundation, allowing her previous abilities and appearance to survive in altered forms.[2][9]
Haruna's evolved Dragon-Kitsune appearance.
Haruna's Draconic manifestation possesses the distinctive features of a Blood Dragon, while her preference for black clothing and traditional Japanese aesthetics remains unchanged. During the migration of the Yokai into Victor's expanded Pantheon, she deliberately displayed her Draconic characteristics rather than relying exclusively upon her original Fox appearance. The decision publicly acknowledged her new relationship with Victor and the transformation of her existence into that of a Dragon Goddess.[10]
Nevertheless, assuming her former Nine-Tailed Fox appearance remained extraordinarily natural to her. Haruna discovered that the transformation felt almost instinctive, as though the Draconic and Kitsune aspects of her existence were both deeply connected to her identity. Although she was no longer technically a Kitsune by race, her retained Essence and developing Divinity allowed the Fox form to remain an important expression of herself. Haruna also continued using it because Victor enjoyed touching her ears and tails, and she had become openly fond of the affection he expressed toward those features.[10][16]

### Personality
Haruna is an exceptionally independent, proud, strong-willed and ambitious woman who considers freedom of choice one of the most important qualities an individual can possess. She dislikes being controlled, manipulated or pressured into decisions by external authority, regardless of whether that authority belongs to a powerful Clan leader, a God or a political ally. Her belief in personal responsibility extends to her own actions: Haruna prefers accepting the consequences of a difficult decision rather than surrendering the right to make that decision in the first place. Even after becoming [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wife, she remained insistent that her relationship with him developed through her own [[Will Solace|Will]] and was not the result of coercion, political necessity or pressure from the other women surrounding him.[12][8]
This independence developed into an intense obsession with strength after the murder of her father. Haruna had been unusually mature and self-reliant even before the tragedy, but witnessing his death while being powerless to intervene permanently altered her understanding of the world. She concluded that individuals without sufficient strength existed at the mercy of those willing to impose their desires through violence. Her subsequent training was therefore motivated not merely by revenge but by a determination never again to become helpless before another person's power. Even after killing the Oni responsible for her father's death, she continued pursuing strength because she recognized that revenge alone could not eliminate the circumstances that produced the conflict.[3]
Haruna's philosophy of rulership consequently differs from simple cruelty or the pursuit of authority for its own sake. She considers anarchy among competing supernatural factions fundamentally dangerous because it encourages repeated wars in which the victorious inevitably create new enemies among the descendants of the defeated. Her solution was to become a ruler whose strength made resistance impractical, forcing rival Yokai Clans to abandon their conflicts and exist beneath a common authority. Haruna openly described the role she intended to fulfill as that of a tyrant, but the purpose of her ambition was to end the endless cycle of warfare rather than indulge in destruction without purpose.[3]
Despite the harshness of her worldview, Haruna possesses considerable respect for individual courage and determination. She does not regard every weaker person as worthless, nor does she believe that a person without overwhelming power necessarily lacks value. Her contempt is directed primarily toward individuals who refuse to accept responsibility, surrender their freedom through cowardice or rely upon inherited authority without possessing the qualities necessary to justify it. This distinction is evident in her treatment of the child of the Oni who murdered her father. Rather than killing the child to prevent future retaliation, Haruna spared him and openly challenged him to become strong enough to confront her someday, acknowledging his right to pursue the same strength she herself had sought.[3]
Haruna also possesses an intense love of battle and personal improvement. She enjoys confronting opponents capable of threatening her and considers the possibility of defeat an opportunity to discover the limitations she must overcome. This mentality became particularly apparent in her relationship with Victor, whose extraordinary rate of development gave her an opponent she could continue pursuing even after her own power had reached a level sufficient to dominate much of Japan's supernatural society. Their duel awakened the sense of excitement Haruna had gradually lost after becoming the strongest authority among the Yokai, giving her a new goal beyond the maintenance of the political order she had established.[14][15]
Although she frequently appears selfish or excessively proud, Haruna is deeply protective of the individuals she accepts as her responsibility. During the conflict involving Japan's Yokai factions, she utilized a technique that connected her Energy to her subordinates and forced her to experience the suffering inflicted upon them. The ability allowed her to support the soldiers under her command, but required her to personally endure the pain produced by their injuries. Victor later recognized the contradiction between Haruna's insistence that she lived only according to her own wishes and her willingness to suffer for an entire army. Her behavior demonstrated that protecting those she considered hers was itself one of the desires guiding her decisions.[17][8]
Haruna's opposition to the Gods similarly originates from her rejection of inherited authority rather than a universal hatred of every Divine being. She resented the assumption that Yokai should automatically obey supernatural rulers simply because those rulers possessed Divine status, particularly when the interests of ordinary Yokai were neglected or sacrificed for the convenience of the existing hierarchy. Her political rise was accompanied by declarations rejecting the continued dominance of the Gods over the Yokai, while her later negotiations with [[Amaterasu]] demonstrated that peaceful relations became possible once the factions could interact through mutually accepted political authority instead of compulsory submission.[7][18]
Her strongest emotional vulnerabilities concern family. Learning that she possessed an older sister whose existence had been concealed from her produced intense anger, grief and resentment toward those responsible for the secrecy. Because Hana had died before Haruna could establish a normal relationship with her, Haruna became fiercely attached to Ophis as the surviving daughter of the sister she had never known. The possibility of restoring Hana later exposed an even more controlling side of Haruna's protectiveness, as she repeatedly expressed distrust toward Vlad and attempted to determine the safest possible future for her sister. Victor challenged this behavior by reminding Haruna that genuine protection should not eliminate another person's freedom to choose, although Haruna continued struggling to reconcile that principle with her fear of losing Hana again.[4][16]
Within Victor's household, Haruna displays a considerably softer and more affectionate personality than the one she presents to her political subordinates. Although she continues maintaining the dignity of the Supreme Commander, she frequently enjoys being pampered by Victor, especially when he strokes her fox ears or brushes her tails. Her embarrassment and possessiveness during these moments contrast with the composed and intimidating image she cultivated throughout Japan. She also becomes openly competitive with Victor's other wives, but her growing familiarity with the family allows her to express affection and humor without constantly preserving the emotional distance expected of an independent ruler.[13][8]
Haruna remains strongly attached to the traditions of her original race and culture. Even after becoming a True Dragon and living within an enormously advanced multiracial civilization, she retained a preference for classical Japanese architecture, traditional clothing and the martial disciplines developed through the [[Otsuki Clan]]. Her initial reluctance to accept Dragonization was partly motivated by the fear that abandoning her Kitsune race would invalidate the identity and accomplishments she had built throughout her life. Her eventual acceptance of the transformation reflected a recognition that preserving her people and continuing to grow were more important than refusing change solely because of personal pride.[19][2][10]

### Background
Haruna was born into the Otsuki lineage of Japan's Yokai society and raised without knowing that she possessed an older sister, [[Otsuki Hana]]. Her childhood was initially comparatively sheltered despite her unusually independent personality, and she was considered mature for her age even before undergoing formal martial training. This period ended when an Oni murdered her father directly in front of her, leaving the young Kitsune unable to prevent the death of someone she loved. The experience shattered her previous assumptions about safety and convinced her that the possession of overwhelming power was necessary to ensure personal freedom and protect one's family.[3]
Haruna subsequently devoted herself to martial training under her grandfather, [[Otsuki Yoichi]], an exceptionally accomplished swordsman associated with the [[Otsuki Clan]]'s traditional martial techniques. Her aptitude proved extraordinary, allowing her to master increasingly advanced disciplines and develop beyond the limitations that had constrained many practitioners before her. Her training culminated in the confrontation with the Oni responsible for her father's death, whom she eventually defeated and killed after becoming powerful enough to avenge the tragedy.[3]
The victory failed to provide the emotional resolution Haruna expected. Upon encountering the defeated Oni's child, she recognized that killing the parent had created another individual capable of inheriting the same hatred that had driven her own development. Rather than repeat the cycle by murdering the child, Haruna spared him and declared that he could pursue revenge when he became powerful enough to challenge her. The experience transformed her personal philosophy, convincing her that revenge could continue indefinitely unless someone acquired sufficient authority to prevent the underlying conflicts from recurring.[3]
Haruna's ambition gradually expanded from personal strength into political supremacy. Japan's Yokai society remained divided among numerous Clans, factions and powerful individuals whose rivalries produced repeated violence. The authority of the Shinto Gods and established supernatural leaders did not eliminate those disputes, leading Haruna to conclude that the existing political order could not provide the peace she sought. She therefore began consolidating followers beneath the Otsuki Clan, building a military organization capable of challenging the most powerful Yokai rulers and eventually unifying the independent factions through strength.[6][3]
Her efforts culminated in the organization of the [[Hyakki Yakō]], whose appearance in Tokyo became one of the most important demonstrations of the growing power of the Yokai factions. By the time [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] entered Japan, Haruna had already accumulated enough personal strength, political authority and military resources to challenge the established supernatural hierarchy openly, positioning herself as the principal rival of [[Hashimoto Genji]] and the traditional systems governing the Kitsune.[6]

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Haruna entered the main narrative during the crisis surrounding the disappearance of [[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]] from [[Nightingale]]. After accidentally transporting herself to Tokyo through her undeveloped spatial abilities, Ophis encountered [[Nero Elderblood]], who agreed to protect her until [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] could locate his daughter. Their arrival coincided with the preparations for the [[Hyakki Yakō]], bringing them into contact with numerous Japanese supernatural factions at a time when the existing political hierarchy was already experiencing considerable instability.[20][6]
Haruna publicly appeared during the enormous Yokai procession passing through Shibuya. The event involved thousands of supernatural beings and served as a demonstration of the military strength accumulated beneath her command. Her presence attracted considerable attention because a Nine-Tailed Black Kitsune capable of gathering such an enormous force represented a direct threat to the existing authority of the Japanese Yokai Clans. Haruna subsequently challenged [[Hashimoto Genji]]'s position as the supreme authority among the Kitsune and announced her intention to establish a new order rather than continue accepting the leadership of individuals she considered unworthy of ruling.[6]
During these events, Ophis encountered Haruna and was visibly affected by the woman's resemblance to her deceased mother. Although the girl did not yet understand the relationship connecting them, she instinctively associated Haruna's appearance with [[Otsuki Hana]], providing the first major indication that the Supreme Commander's ancestry possessed a direct connection to the Vampire Royal Family. Haruna herself remained unaware of the truth at the time, having grown up without knowledge of Hana's existence.[21]
Victor's arrival in Japan changed the balance of the conflict. Following his intervention to protect Ophis and [[Nero]] from the supernatural factions pursuing them, he became increasingly involved with Haruna's efforts to consolidate authority over the Yokai. Their first interactions revealed a strong similarity in their personalities: both possessed an intense desire for personal freedom, considerable confidence in their strength and a willingness to accept the consequences of their own decisions. Haruna refused to allow Victor to interfere unnecessarily in a battle she regarded as her responsibility, while Victor respected her determination because it resembled his own dislike of outsiders interrupting fights he had deliberately chosen.[22][12][17]
Haruna's confrontation with Genji demonstrated that her military organization was more than a collection of ordinary Yokai following the strongest available ruler. She possessed techniques specifically intended to protect and support the soldiers beneath her command, including a method of sharing Energy with her army while experiencing the injuries suffered by those linked to her. Victor recognized the significance of this sacrifice and became increasingly interested in the woman whose apparently ruthless ambitions concealed an intense sense of personal responsibility toward her followers.[17]
During the confrontation, Haruna also demonstrated her unusual swordsmanship through Mugetsu, an advanced technique capable of separating a target's body into independent sections without necessarily causing conventional physical injury. The ability proved effective in the conflict against Genji's forces, while Haruna's enormous Youki reserves and mastery of multiple supernatural disciplines established that her reputation was not based exclusively upon political intimidation.[7]
The conflict escalated when [[Inari-Omikami]] intervened against Haruna. The Fox Goddess's actions reflected the growing dispute between the traditional Divine authorities governing Japanese supernatural society and the increasingly independent Yokai factions organized beneath Haruna. Victor intervened to prevent Haruna from being killed, while [[Scathach]]'s involvement transformed the confrontation into a struggle between several of the most powerful supernatural beings present in Japan. Haruna's determination to reject the unquestioned authority of the Gods became one of the principal ideological foundations of the new Yokai order she intended to establish.[7][23]
Victor subsequently began investigating Haruna's resemblance to Ophis' deceased mother. The possibility of a familial relationship became increasingly significant after the events surrounding the Royal Family and the information available through his allies. Haruna eventually confronted her grandfather, [[Otsuki Yoichi]], demanding an explanation for the concealed history of their family. The confrontation revealed that Otsuki Hana had been Haruna's older sister, that Hana had married [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]], and that Ophis was therefore Haruna's biological niece.[4]
Yoichi brought Haruna to a secluded memorial associated with Hana. The location had been isolated through the extraordinary spatial abilities of [[Alexios Alioth]], creating a protected sanctuary dedicated to Hana's memory. Haruna paid her respects before the memorial and struggled to comprehend the fact that her sister had died without ever becoming part of her life. Although the place functioned as Hana's resting place, Yoichi privately knew that Hana's actual remains were not buried there, a fact he deliberately concealed because he feared Haruna would become obsessed with recovering them.[4]
The revelation profoundly changed Haruna's relationship with Ophis and the Vampire Royal Family. She recognized Ophis as a surviving part of the sister she had never known, while the discovery that an Elder God had been responsible for Hana's death created a new objective that would remain important throughout the subsequent conflicts. Haruna's desire for revenge was no longer abstract resentment toward the Gods or the supernatural hierarchy; it had become a deeply personal determination to confront the being who had destroyed her family.[4]
By the time representatives were selected for the larger gathering of supernatural factions, Haruna had become an authority whose decisions could no longer be ignored. Her rise forced the established political organizations to acknowledge the existence of a powerful independent Yokai faction, while her growing association with Victor placed her near one of the most rapidly expanding powers in the supernatural world.[24]

#### Demonic War
Haruna attended the Meeting of Supernatural Beings as the Supreme Commander of the [[Hyakki Yakō]], accompanied by her attendant [[Yotsuba Kuroka]]. Her arrival demonstrated the importance of her faction within the new political environment, while the magical properties of the meeting hall reflected her Japanese supernatural heritage through imagery resembling a traditional temple. Unlike representatives who approached the gathering as subordinates of ancient Pantheons, Haruna attended as an independent ruler responsible for negotiating the future of her people.[25]
During the negotiations, Haruna sought an arrangement that would protect the Yokai from external interference without requiring their complete submission to another supernatural authority. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] supported her position, proposing an alliance based upon cooperation and mutual interests rather than compulsory Soul Contracts. Haruna accepted the arrangement because it offered practical protection for her faction while preserving the political independence she had spent so long attempting to establish.[26][27][28]
The political relationship gradually developed into a personal one. When the possibility of strengthening their alliance through marriage arose, Haruna rejected the idea of becoming Victor's wife merely because the arrangement would benefit their respective factions. Her refusal reflected her long-standing insistence that personal relationships must result from her own decisions rather than political convenience. Victor similarly expressed little interest in marrying someone solely to secure an alliance, although he openly acknowledged that he found Haruna attractive and would welcome a genuine relationship if it developed naturally.[29]
Their subsequent interactions became increasingly direct. Victor expressed his interest in learning [[Senjutsu]] and Ki from Haruna, while Haruna responded positively to his willingness to speak openly rather than conceal his intentions behind complicated political maneuvers. Their discussions also caused Haruna to reconsider the experiences that had shaped her, including the murder of her father, her training under Yoichi and the failure of revenge to provide the satisfaction she had expected. Victor's concern for her feelings surprised Haruna because she was accustomed to being approached primarily as a powerful ruler rather than as an individual with personal vulnerabilities.[30][3]
Haruna eventually agreed to formalize the alliance between their factions and proposed a duel with Victor. The battle would serve both political and personal purposes, allowing their subordinates to witness the strength upon which the alliance rested while giving Haruna the opportunity to experience Victor's abilities directly. She regarded combat as one of the most effective ways of understanding another individual, particularly when the opponent possessed the confidence and determination she valued.[31]
Their duel became one of Haruna's most important demonstrations of power before her Draconic evolution. She released the enormous Youki reserves normally kept under control and employed the [[Otsuki Clan]]'s Four Dances of the Moon, a specialized martial system combining ritualized movements, moon-related manifestations and increasingly powerful attacks. Haruna demonstrated the capacity to adapt techniques beyond their original limitations, developing additional movements that surprised even Yoichi, the creator of the style. The battle included deceptive movement, multiple manifestations of Haruna, altered gravitational conditions and the creation of an additional Moon-like structure within the battlefield.[14][15]
Haruna's final attacks involved dividing herself into nine bodies and combining the elemental affinities associated with her tails. These included Fire, Water, Earth, Wind, Metal, Wood, Lightning, Darkness and Light, with her Light affinity being especially unusual for a Black Kitsune whose lineage was ordinarily associated with Darkness. The scale and complexity of the assault demonstrated that Haruna possessed far greater versatility than most other Yokai, although Victor ultimately endured the attacks and won the confrontation.[15]
Rather than becoming resentful, Haruna found the duel enormously satisfying. She had grown accustomed to possessing so much authority and strength that few opponents could challenge her meaningfully, causing the pursuit of power to become increasingly disconnected from the excitement she had experienced during her earlier development. Victor gave her a new objective: someone she could pursue without knowing whether she would ever completely surpass him. The battle consequently deepened their mutual attraction and established a personal foundation for their relationship beyond the political alliance between their factions.[15][32]
Their relationship continued developing during the following days, with Haruna becoming increasingly comfortable expressing emotions she normally concealed from the other Yokai. Victor's directness and willingness to respect her independence allowed her to relax without believing that affection required surrendering her identity as a ruler. Their growing intimacy also revealed the sensitivity of Haruna's ears and tails, creating a form of affection that remained important throughout their later marriage.[13][33]
Haruna subsequently became one of Victor's instructors, introducing him to the principles of Senjutsu and the use of [[Natural Energy]]. She brought him to the Ley Lines of Earth and explained the process through which practitioners absorb surrounding Energy, filter it and convert it into a form compatible with their own existence. Victor's relationship with [[Roxanne Elderblood]] gave him an extraordinary natural advantage because the World Tree connected to his Soul caused Natural Energy to react to him with unusual affinity, allowing him to progress far more rapidly than an ordinary student.[34][35]
When the Demonic War spread toward Japan, Haruna participated in organizing the defense of Yokai territory. Her forces cooperated with other powerful supernatural factions, including those associated with Morgana and Mizuki, while the Shinto Gods maintained their own responsibilities. Haruna's negotiations with [[Amaterasu]] demonstrated the political transformation that had occurred since her initial rebellion: she no longer approached the Divine ruler as a subordinate requesting permission or protection, but as an independent leader capable of negotiating on behalf of an organized Yokai population.[36][18]

#### King Among Kings
Haruna's relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] continued developing after the Demonic War, eventually reaching a point at which she no longer considered hesitation or concern over political appearances necessary. She explicitly rejected the suggestion that her involvement with him resulted from pressure by the other women in his household. Haruna insisted that she had remained beside Victor because she wanted to, accepting the consequences of that choice in accordance with the philosophy she had followed throughout her life. Victor recognized that her words reflected the same independence that had originally attracted him to her.[8]
Their intimacy produced subtle changes in Haruna's body and supernatural characteristics. Her connection to [[Natural Energy]] became increasingly efficient, making the practice of [[Senjutsu]] and Ki easier than before. Her hair grew darker and shinier, her tails became noticeably fluffier, and her body developed more pronounced physical definition. Haruna also became increasingly comfortable allowing Victor to touch her ears and tails, despite normally guarding those sensitive features from unwanted contact. Her behavior around him consequently contrasted sharply with the strict demeanor she maintained toward her subordinates.[8]
As Victor established the foundations of [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]], Haruna's political position developed alongside their relationship. Rather than simply absorbing the Yokai into a different supernatural institution and removing their traditional leadership, Victor permitted Haruna to retain responsibility for her people. This arrangement preserved the authority she had built through years of struggle while incorporating the Yokai into a much larger multiracial faction capable of protecting them against threats beyond the resources of Japan's supernatural society.[37]
Haruna's original Kitsune evolution also approached an important threshold. During the period surrounding the later supernatural gathering, [[Amaterasu]] recognized that Haruna was nearing the legendary tenth-tail stage, a transformation associated with the transition of an exceptionally powerful Kitsune toward a Divine form of existence. The process would ordinarily require further development and the replacement or elevation of the ordinary Youki foundation supporting the Kitsune's powers, but Haruna had not yet naturally completed that transition.[38]
The invasion of [[Nightingale]] by the Elder Gods finally provided Haruna with the opportunity to confront Shur, the being responsible for the death of her sister. Haruna and Vlad fought together despite the considerable resentment she felt toward the Vampire King for failing to protect Hana. Both possessed personal reasons for wanting Shur destroyed, transforming the battle into a confrontation shaped by centuries of grief rather than an ordinary military engagement.[39][1]
Haruna initially relied upon her established martial techniques, including Mugetsu, which separated Shur's enormous body into thousands of pieces and temporarily interfered with its ordinary physical regeneration. However, the Elder God's unusual existence allowed him to reconstruct himself through his connection to a greater source of power, making physical destruction insufficient to guarantee his death. Haruna and Vlad continued attacking while attempting to determine the source of his immortality, but Shur's increasingly dangerous abilities forced them to confront the possibility that conventional combat would not be enough.[1]
When Shur began releasing an extremely dangerous red power, Haruna and Vlad were forced to evade or counter attacks capable of causing severe damage even to beings of their level. The danger intensified Haruna's determination to end the confrontation. She eventually decided to force the transformation that her Kitsune existence had not naturally completed, disregarding the fact that her Soul was not prepared to support the transition.[1]
Haruna recited an incantation associated with the ascent of a dark fox toward the Moon, causing her body to expand into an enormous black fox approximately 270 meters tall. A tenth tail appeared behind her, completing a temporary metamorphosis into a Moonlight Fox whose supernatural presence approached that of a Divine existence. The transformation represented the culmination of her original racial development, but it was achieved through an extremely dangerous acceleration rather than the stable advancement she had previously been approaching.[1]
In this state, Haruna unleashed The Fall of the Stars, causing enormous celestial projectiles or star-like attacks to descend toward the battlefield. Their appearance was sufficiently dangerous that powerful observers prepared to protect the planet from collateral destruction, but Haruna redirected their trajectories toward Shur. The resulting bombardment struck the Elder God while the power sustaining his extraordinary immortality was weakening, contributing to the destruction of the enemy responsible for Hana's death.[1]
The victory came at a severe personal cost. Haruna's temporary tenth-tail transformation could not be maintained safely, and the attempt inflicted serious damage upon her Soul. She eventually reverted to her previous state and collapsed from exhaustion, having pushed herself beyond the limits she could support. Jeanne later explained that the forced transformation might have destroyed Haruna's Soul entirely, while the strengthening she had received through the Tower of Nightmare contributed to her survival.[1][11]
Victor subsequently examined the damage and used his newly developed authority over Beginnings to guide Haruna's future progress. Golden Energy appeared around her as the metaphysical barrier obstructing her development began to crack, opening a more favorable path toward the transformation she had attempted to force. However, Victor clarified that Beginnings could not simply grant an arbitrary Divinity without the individual discovering a compatible path. Haruna therefore received assistance toward her own development rather than acquiring Victor's Beginnings authority as a personal power.[11]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ascension as the Dragon God of [[Chaos]], Haruna became one of the clearest examples of the importance he placed upon allowing his family to choose their own development. Although Victor intended to transform many of the women surrounding him into genuine Dragons, Haruna initially refused. Her objection did not originate from ignorance of the benefits offered by his new race. Rather, she considered her Kitsune identity inseparable from everything she had accomplished, having dedicated most of her life to mastering the characteristics, traditions and martial disciplines inherited from the [[Otsuki Clan]].[19][2]
Haruna's opposition reflected the possibility that accepting a superior race might require abandoning the foundations of her previous existence. She had fought to become a Nine-Tailed Black Kitsune and approached the legendary tenth-tail state through her own efforts, making the idea of surrendering those accomplishments emotionally difficult. Victor did not attempt to compel her conversion, instead explaining that the transformation would preserve important aspects of her former Essence and that the shapeshifting capabilities of a True Dragon would allow her to continue expressing the physical characteristics she valued.[2]
The increasingly dangerous nature of the enemies threatening Victor's family eventually changed Haruna's assessment. She recognized that refusing a fundamental advantage solely because of pride could place her, her people and those she loved at unnecessary risk. Rather than allowing the emotional attachment to her original race to determine her future completely, Haruna accepted the transformation as another deliberate choice in pursuit of the strength she considered necessary to protect her responsibilities.[2]
During Dragonization, Haruna underwent a profound reconstruction of her existence. The transformation elevated her into a genuine True Dragon while preserving a portion of the Kitsune Essence underlying her former identity. Her previous supernatural characteristics became integrated into her Draconic foundation, allowing the abilities she had cultivated throughout her life to survive in a considerably enhanced form. The process also forcibly opened the path obstructing her ascension toward Godhood, producing another manifestation associated with the tenth tail while fundamentally differing from the unstable metamorphosis she had attempted during the battle against Shur.[2]
Although Haruna possessed the state of a Goddess following the transformation, she recognized that her personal Divinity remained incomplete. Acquiring an elevated race and Divine physiology did not automatically provide the understanding necessary to define her own mature Concept. She would still need to investigate the fundamental principles connecting her identity, racial heritage and supernatural development before the Divinity could be considered fully established.[2]
The advantages of her transformation became clearer as Haruna examined the interaction between her former Kitsune techniques and her new [[Draconic Energy]]. Rather than requiring her to relearn every Martial Art or separately reproduce the traditional Youki and [[Senjutsu]] processes, the abilities had merged with her Draconic foundation. She could now utilize the purer and more powerful Energy generated by her [[Dragon Heart]] directly, allowing her established techniques to function with far greater efficiency than before.[9]
Her extraordinary familiarity with [[Natural Energy]] produced another development. The connection she had cultivated through Senjutsu transformed Haruna into what was described as a type of Nature Dragon, allowing her to utilize Energy originating from [[World Trees]] with greater efficiency. Although this affinity was not equivalent to Victor's direct relationships with Roxanne and Amara, it represented a considerable advancement over the connection Haruna had possessed as a Kitsune.[9]
Haruna later supervised the integration of the Yokai into the expanding territories of Dragons Nest. Her people began receiving new settlements within the multiracial Pantheon, while she retained considerable freedom over their administration and cultural development. Rather than abandoning the traditions of her original race, Haruna favored districts built around classical Japanese architecture, preserving an environment familiar to the Yokai despite the advanced technologies and unfamiliar supernatural races now surrounding them.[10]
During the migration, Haruna deliberately displayed her new Draconic characteristics as a public acknowledgment of her identity as Victor's wife and a genuine True Dragon. However, the instinctive ease with which she could assume her former Nine-Tailed Fox form surprised her. She eventually suspected that her retained Kitsune Essence and the development of a personal Divinity associated with Foxes and the Moon explained the unusual relationship between her two appearances.[10]
Haruna's evolution also began influencing the Yokai connected to her. Her new existence possessed an exceptionally close relationship with the principles underlying their racial development, and the transformation gradually encouraged a movement away from complete dependence upon Youki toward a more natural relationship with Senjutsu and Natural Energy. The change suggested that Haruna's importance to the Yokai was no longer exclusively political, since her own evolution could influence the long-term development of the race she had originally unified through military strength.[10]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
The extensive periods of accelerated development experienced by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family eventually allowed Haruna to become a mother. She carried Victor's daughter for approximately 1,670 subjective years, reflecting the extraordinary gestation periods required for children inheriting the increasingly powerful characteristics of their parents. Haruna's pregnancy was especially frustrating because she remained an exceptionally active warrior who disliked prolonged restrictions upon her movements, training and personal development.[5]
Victor's protectiveness intensified the difficulty by preventing his pregnant wives from participating in dangerous activities or exerting themselves unnecessarily. Although Haruna understood the reasons behind his restrictions, spending such an enormous period unable to pursue her normal training was fundamentally incompatible with the lifestyle she had cultivated throughout her earlier existence. Following the birth of their daughters, Haruna and [[Scathach]] both declared that they would avoid becoming pregnant again until Victor discovered a means of shortening the gestation required for their extraordinarily powerful children.[5]
Haruna's daughter's complete name and individual supernatural abilities were not established in the cited birth account, but the child became part of the next generation of the Elderblood family. Haruna's transition into motherhood represented another profound change in her responsibilities, adding the upbringing of a child to her duties as Supreme Commander, Dragon Goddess and wife of Victor.[5]
Her unresolved feelings surrounding [[Otsuki Hana]] resurfaced when Victor created an artificial reality to assist Ophis in understanding herself and developing her Divinity. The constructed environment included a recreation of Hana based upon the information and memories available to Victor, allowing Ophis to interact with an image of the mother she had lost. The encounter held considerable emotional significance for the girl, whose early experiences and relationship with the Royal Family had been shaped by the absence of Hana.[40][41]
When Haruna learned about the artificial recreation, her immediate concern was Ophis. She regarded her niece as the living legacy of her older sister and remained deeply protective of the girl despite the extraordinary supernatural power Ophis had acquired since joining Victor's family. The possibility of eventually restoring Hana's genuine existence also revived Haruna's resentment toward Vlad, whom she blamed for failing to protect her sister when the Vampire Royal Family was attacked.[16]
Haruna expressed an intense desire to ensure that Hana would never suffer through the same circumstances again, even suggesting that she might restrict her sister's freedom if doing so guaranteed her safety. Jeanne challenged the unfairness of Haruna's judgment toward Vlad, acknowledging that Hana had been treated differently from several of the other women associated with the Vampire King. Haruna nevertheless insisted that she could not trust Vlad to protect her sister, arguing that the failure of his responsibilities remained unchanged regardless of the extraordinary circumstances surrounding Hana's death.[16]
Victor responded by reminding Haruna that Hana would need to make her own decisions if she were genuinely restored. Although he understood the fear motivating Haruna's behavior, he opposed the idea of controlling another person's future merely because the intention was protective. His response reflected the same principle that had attracted Haruna to him originally: individuals should possess the freedom to determine their own paths while accepting the consequences of those choices.[16]
Victor also explained that he intended to investigate the possibility of Hana's genuine restoration after resolving the more immediate problems surrounding Azathoth and the increasingly complicated affairs of the Primordial beings. Haruna consequently retained hope that her sister could eventually return, although the artificial recreation encountered by Ophis should not be confused with confirmation that Hana had already been permanently resurrected.[16]
Haruna continued pursuing martial development throughout this period. When Victor demonstrated the [[Seven Arts of the Blood Dragon Primogenitor]], she immediately understood the extraordinary significance of the system, which combined Draconic physiology, Energy control and the manipulation of Reality itself. Despite her advanced control over Energy, Haruna acknowledged that she had not yet attained the level of precision required to perform every component safely. She still needed to develop greater mastery of Reality Distortion before learning the complete Martial Art, demonstrating that becoming a True Dragon had not eliminated the need for continued training.[42]
Her position within the Elderblood family also allowed her to participate in the broader activities surrounding the expanding Empire. The household had become home to numerous extraordinarily powerful women, each possessing different responsibilities within the civilization Victor was constructing. Haruna remained connected to the Yokai administration while maintaining close relationships with Ophis, [[Amaterasu]] and the other women whose political backgrounds had originally placed them on opposing sides of Japan's supernatural hierarchy.[10][16]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
The Yokai were no longer confined to the old political wars of Japan. They sat inside a multiracial Empire whose reach already ran across worlds, and Haruna kept the people under her as a people rather than letting that identity dissolve into the larger civilization.[10]
She was with [[Amaterasu]] when the wives were already taking a star-eating animal apart for samples and for dinner. Haruna was the one who named it from the records: a being from space that fed on stars, and not yet fully grown.[43]
Parenthood did not yield to the same methods she used on warriors. When Ophis and [[Nero Elderblood]] were talking themselves into children, the examples that stopped them included Haruna, who was already losing the ordinary argument to a daughter as headstrong as she was. The child is not named.[44]
When the captured memories of Darkness named the six free Thrones, Kali left to see what the stronger Gods were actually like. Haruna went with her. Staying home, she said, would only make her rusty. Violet pitied the enemy. [[Scathach]] stayed, and hated staying.[45]
The counteroffensive was a reinforcement fleet, and two Thrones walked ahead of it without bothering with the ships. [[Aetherys]], Throne of Time, was a segmented body of crystalline armor and golden lines, a head of concentric rings around a golden core. [[Oryndal]], Throne of Space, was black plates, long extensions, and blue-violet rings. He did not cross a distance. He made the destination adjacent. Haruna met them over five meters tall, in full armor, with long black hair, two black horns, and Dragon eyes. She would not show the fox form. That shape was for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. She still thought of herself as a fox first and a Dragon Goddess only after that. Kali thought Haruna's presence was already more than the pair required, and recognized the habit: Haruna fought the way Victor did, damaging the opponent in every way she could, with provocation as a standard opening.[46]
Space and Time fused, and the Thrones tried to leave. Haruna was in front of Aetherys and kicked him in the face before he could react. They gave their names so the Thrones would look only at them. Hers was Haruna Elderblood, Goddess of War and Foxes, and wife of the Dragon Emperor. The delay was for the fleets. Once the real fight started, the region would be closed, and the effects could reach distant solar systems and threaten the galaxy. Natalia was already getting the Imperial forces out.[46]
Aetherys wrapped a Solar System in Time and called Victor a fool for sending his family, with the stated intention of capturing the two wives and killing them in front of him. Haruna changed her mind about making it quick. Her power rose as a deep-violet pillar, and her features sharpened into the Dragon. Ikari appeared in her hand, a violet katana, later an odachi matched to her height, marked with Draconic and Eldritch script. Victor had given it to her. The name meant wrath and anchor: he was the place she returned to. Drawing it made the clarity of both Thrones waver, and the razor edge of her aura was the proof that she had reached the master level of her art.[46]
She moved first. The dark-violet energy went into her legs, and she was across the distance before Aetherys could follow her in the flow of Time. His temporal barrier did not stop Ikari. The clash destroyed the rings around his body. He tried to put the blow into a time in which it never landed. She had already turned and opened a crack in the golden structure that held the core. Every cut after that forced his Divinity onto defense, so he could no longer run the battlefield through the temporal domain. When he increased the distance, she crossed the altered zone before the maneuver finished, broke the sequence his defense was holding, and threw him into a distant part of the field.[47]
He then split the course of events into overlapping sequences, so that many Harunas seemed to be striking from different times, some of them actions that had not happened yet. She did not chase the copies. She put her Divinity into the blade and cut the structure that bound them. The images went out one by one. She stepped in and struck the central structure before he could restore the domain. Oryndal opened a passage above her and sent extensions through it. Kali collapsed that passage. A joint domain of Time and Space held the two wives apart for a few moments. Haruna cut through one of the dimensional structures and opened a path. Kali went through it to Oryndal. Haruna reached the damaged core, and the synchronization died.[47]
Without Oryndal's spatial stability, Aetherys could not keep the distortions. He tried to reverse his condition with every hit. The cracks in the core would not allow a full restoration. Ikari broke another temporal barrier and struck the center. The remaining rings lost their timing, and the temporal area began to collapse. When he tried to withdraw, Kali stopped him by presence alone. Haruna hit him from behind and threw him into Oryndal. She then kept him under an uninterrupted attack while Kali destroyed every dimensional structure Oryndal tried to raise. The Darkness fleets used the wreck to leave. The fight had not been finished with beams. Scathach, watching, was satisfied with that. The Thrones had power and almost no martial foundation. Wives who had been taught both did not need to give them an opening.[47]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
Haruna's relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] began through political conflict and mutual curiosity. She initially regarded him as an extraordinarily dangerous Vampire capable of interfering with her plans to unify Japan's Yokai, while Victor became interested in both her strength and the resemblance between Haruna and Ophis' deceased mother. Their early encounters revealed similarities in their personalities, particularly their refusal to submit unnecessarily to other people's authority and their enjoyment of combat against powerful opponents.[22][12][4]
Their political alliance developed during the Meeting of Supernatural Beings, where Victor offered the Yokai protection without demanding their complete submission or requiring Haruna to sacrifice the independence she had fought to establish. Haruna valued the arrangement because it recognized her position as an independent ruler rather than treating her people as resources to be absorbed by a more powerful faction. The respect established through these negotiations became the foundation of their later personal relationship.[27][28]
Haruna rejected the suggestion that marriage should be used merely to formalize the alliance. Although she recognized the political advantages such a relationship might provide, she insisted that Victor would need to interest her personally and demonstrate the qualities she valued. Their subsequent duel fulfilled this purpose, allowing Haruna to witness his strength and determination directly. Victor's victory did not humiliate her; instead, it provided a new objective capable of restoring the excitement she had lost after becoming one of Japan's strongest Yokai.[29][14][15]
The relationship became increasingly intimate as Victor treated Haruna with affection while continuing to respect her independence. She eventually admitted that her decision to remain with him had been entirely voluntary, rejecting any suggestion that the other women or political circumstances had forced her into the relationship. Her trust allowed her to reveal a gentler personality normally concealed beneath the authority of the Supreme Commander, particularly when Victor stroked her ears or brushed her tails.[13][8]
Victor later played an important role in Haruna's spiritual development. After her dangerous attempt to force the tenth-tail metamorphosis, he examined the damage to her Soul and used his authority over Beginnings to guide her future advancement. He subsequently offered her the opportunity to become a Blood Dragon, but did not force the transformation upon her when she initially refused. Haruna eventually accepted after recognizing that the advantages offered by his lineage could strengthen her ability to protect the people she loved.[11][19][2]
Their marriage eventually produced a daughter, while Victor's continued support extended to Haruna's unresolved grief concerning [[Otsuki Hana]]. He promised to investigate the possibility of restoring her sister, but also challenged Haruna's desire to control Hana's future, reminding her that protecting someone should not eliminate that person's right to make decisions. Their relationship consequently combines affection, mutual respect and the willingness to confront one another when their personal convictions lead to disagreement.[5][16]

#### Otsuki Hana
[[Otsuki Hana]] was Haruna's older sister, whose existence was concealed from her throughout childhood. Hana had married [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] and given birth to Ophis, but was eventually killed during the conflict involving the Elder Gods. Haruna only learned the truth after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] investigated her resemblance to Ophis and she confronted her grandfather over the concealed history of their family.[4]
The revelation caused Haruna considerable emotional distress because she had been denied the opportunity to know her sister before Hana's death. She initially directed her anger toward the individuals responsible for the secrecy and the circumstances surrounding Hana's destruction, eventually recognizing Shur as the enemy whose actions had taken away someone she had never been allowed to protect. Her desire for revenge became an important motivation during the subsequent conflict with the Elder Gods.[4][1]
After confronting Shur, Haruna remained deeply affected by Hana's absence. The artificial recreation of her sister during Ophis' Divine trial reopened these feelings, particularly when Victor discussed the possibility of eventually restoring Hana's genuine existence. Haruna became intensely protective and expressed considerable distrust toward Vlad, whom she considered responsible for failing to protect his wife. Although Victor attempted to persuade her that Hana should be allowed to determine her own future, Haruna struggled to accept any possibility that might expose her sister to further suffering.[16]

#### Ophis Tepes Elderblood
[[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]] is Haruna's biological niece through [[Otsuki Hana]]. Their relationship was foreshadowed during the crisis in Japan, when Ophis instinctively reacted to Haruna's appearance and briefly associated her with her deceased mother. Although neither initially understood the reason for the resemblance, the encounter eventually contributed to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s investigation of the connection between the Otsuki family and the Vampire Royal Family.[21][4]
Haruna accepted Ophis as family immediately after learning the truth. The girl represented the surviving legacy of the sister she had never known, making her safety and happiness particularly important to Haruna. The relationship also strengthened Haruna's connection to Victor, whose decision to adopt and protect Ophis had already demonstrated the intensity of his commitment toward the members of his family.[4][26]
Following Haruna's marriage to Victor, Ophis became both her biological niece and stepdaughter. Haruna continued regarding her with intense affection and protectiveness, particularly during the events surrounding Ophis' developing Divinity. The possibility of restoring Hana through Victor's abilities was important to Haruna partly because it offered the hope of allowing Ophis to reunite with her birth mother.[16]

#### Otsuki Yoichi
[[Otsuki Yoichi]] is Haruna's grandfather, former Supreme Commander and principal martial instructor. After the murder of her father, Haruna devoted herself to the techniques developed through the Otsuki lineage, eventually mastering the martial foundations Yoichi had established. Her extraordinary talent allowed her to develop beyond aspects of his original system, including the addition of new applications to the Four Dances of the Moon that surprised him during her duel with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[3][14]
Their relationship is complicated by Yoichi's decision to conceal Hana's existence and death. Haruna regarded the secrecy as a betrayal because it prevented her from knowing an important member of her own family, leading to a confrontation during which she demanded an explanation from her grandfather. Yoichi nevertheless remained one of the few individuals whose experience and personal authority could influence Haruna's behavior, particularly when he insisted that Hana's memorial be treated as a place of remembrance rather than another resource for military expansion.[4]
Despite their disagreements, Yoichi played a decisive role in Haruna's development. Her martial foundation, political ambitions and willingness to challenge established traditions were all influenced by the skills and experiences she acquired under his instruction. Haruna ultimately exceeded many of the limitations associated with her grandfather's generation while continuing to preserve the traditions he had taught her.[3][14]

#### Hashimoto Genji
[[Hashimoto Genji]] was one of Haruna's principal rivals during her rise to leadership over Japan's Kitsune and wider Yokai community. As an established Nine-Tailed Kitsune possessing considerable political and commercial authority, Genji represented part of the older supernatural hierarchy Haruna intended to replace. Their conflict culminated in the struggle surrounding the [[Hyakki Yakō]], during which Haruna demonstrated the extraordinary personal strength and military resources supporting her ambitions.[6][17]
Haruna's Youki reserves were subsequently revealed to exceed Genji's by an enormous margin, with her capacity described as approximately ten times greater than his during their Nine-Tailed states. Her mastery of the [[Otsuki Clan]]'s martial arts further distinguished her from the traditional Kitsune ruler, allowing her to establish herself as the superior combatant and political authority.[14]
After Haruna consolidated her leadership, Genji's role changed from rival to subordinate administrator. Haruna recognized the practical value of his experience and connections, allowing him to contribute to the management of Yokai affairs rather than removing him merely because he had formerly opposed her authority. His continued involvement illustrates Haruna's willingness to utilize competent individuals when doing so benefits the stability of the society under her rule.[8]

#### Yotsuba Kuroka
[[Yotsuba Kuroka]] is one of Haruna's most trusted attendants and long-standing subordinates. She accompanied Haruna during major political events, including the Meeting of Supernatural Beings, and participated in the arrangements surrounding Haruna's duel with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Her responsibilities placed her among the individuals most directly involved with maintaining the Supreme Commander's schedule, military organization and diplomatic affairs.[25][14]
Haruna relies upon Kuroka's competence and familiarity with her intentions, allowing the attendant to assist with important administrative and political responsibilities. Their relationship reflects the trust Haruna places in capable subordinates, particularly those who remained loyal throughout her struggle to unify the Yokai.[8]

#### Amaterasu Elderblood
[[Amaterasu]] initially represented the Divine hierarchy Haruna had spent much of her life challenging. The Shinto Gods traditionally exercised authority over Japanese supernatural society, while Haruna believed that Yokai should not be required to obey Divine rulers merely because of their status. This difference created considerable tension between their factions during the period surrounding Haruna's rise to power.[7]
Their relationship changed after both became associated with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction. Haruna remained responsible for the Yokai, while Amaterasu continued serving as a principal authority among the Shinto Gods, allowing the two to interact as political representatives rather than through the former hierarchy of Divine command and Yokai obedience. Their eventual transformations into [[Blood Dragons]] further integrated them into the same extended family without eliminating the distinct responsibilities they held toward their respective peoples.[18][2]
Haruna and Amaterasu continued appearing together during later Imperial events, including gatherings involving Victor's wives and the examination of extraordinary creatures encountered through the Empire's expansion. Their cooperation demonstrates how the old antagonism between the independent Yokai and the Shinto hierarchy gradually became subordinate to the greater political and familial structure established by Victor.[43]

#### Vlad Tepes
[[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] is Haruna's brother-in-law through his marriage to [[Otsuki Hana]] and the father of her biological niece Ophis. Haruna's feelings toward Vlad are complicated by the death of her sister, whose protection she believes should have been one of the Vampire King's highest responsibilities. Although she recognizes his exceptional power and political importance, she resents his failure to prevent Hana's death and remains distrustful of his judgment concerning family matters.[4][16]
Their shared hatred toward Shur brought them together during the Elder God invasion, when both fought the being responsible for Hana's destruction. The alliance did not eliminate Haruna's grievances, but it demonstrated that they possessed a common objective significant enough to overcome their personal disagreements during the battle.[1]
Haruna's distrust became particularly evident when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] discussed the possibility of restoring Hana. She expressed concern that her sister might return to Vlad and encounter the same dangers again, prompting Victor and Jeanne to challenge the unfairness of attempting to control Hana's future. Haruna acknowledged that Vlad had changed in certain respects but remained unconvinced that the changes were sufficient to guarantee Hana's happiness or safety.[16]

#### Daughter
Haruna and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] have at least one daughter whose birth occurred after approximately 1,670 subjective years of pregnancy. The extraordinarily long gestation was particularly difficult for Haruna because her active lifestyle and dedication to training conflicted with Victor's determination to prevent his pregnant wives from exposing themselves to unnecessary danger. Following the birth, Haruna declared that she would avoid becoming pregnant again until a means of reducing the gestation period had been developed.[5]
Her daughter's individual personality later became a source of considerable difficulty for Haruna. During a discussion involving Ophis and [[Nero]], the younger women recalled that Haruna was struggling with a daughter who possessed an equally stubborn temperament. The resemblance demonstrates that the child inherited at least part of Haruna's strong will and resistance to authority, creating challenges even for a mother who had successfully commanded an enormous supernatural faction.[44]
The daughter's complete name and individual abilities have not been established in the cited passages and should not be assigned without direct confirmation from the novel.

### Abilities

#### Powers and Abilities
Otsuki Elderblood Haruna is an exceptionally powerful supernatural warrior, an accomplished martial artist, and one of the foremost practitioners of Kitsune techniques and [[Senjutsu]] introduced in the series. Before becoming a Blood Dragon, Haruna had already reached the pinnacle of the conventional Nine-Tailed Kitsune evolutionary path, possessing approximately ten times the Youki reserves of [[Hashimoto Genji]], a highly accomplished Nine-Tailed Kitsune and former leader of Japan's Fox Yokai. Her abilities combined the extraordinary Energy reserves of her original race with the martial system developed by [[Otsuki Yoichi]], advanced swordsmanship, illusions, elemental manipulation, [[Natural Energy]] cultivation, and techniques capable of supporting enormous supernatural armies.[1][2][3]
Haruna's most developed martial discipline is the Four Dances of the Moon, an elaborate combat system incorporating ritualized sword movements, Youki, illusions, gravitational pressure, lunar constructs and increasingly destructive attacks. Although her grandfather created the original system, Haruna's extraordinary talent allowed her to modify its established structure and develop continuations that should ordinarily have caused severe harm to an insufficiently skilled practitioner. She also mastered all nine elemental affinities available through her original Nine-Tailed Kitsune physiology, including Light, an element considered exceptionally unusual for a Black Kitsune whose racial characteristics were associated with Darkness.[2][3]
During the battle against Shur, Haruna temporarily transcended the limits of her original Kitsune state by forcibly developing a tenth tail and assuming the form of an enormous Moonlight Fox. The transformation granted her access to The Fall of the Stars, an extraordinary celestial attack that brought numerous beams or shooting-star-like projectiles from space toward the battlefield. However, the evolution had been forced before Haruna's Soul was sufficiently developed to sustain it, resulting in severe exhaustion and potentially fatal spiritual damage. Her tenth tail subsequently disappeared, making this an unstable temporary metamorphosis rather than a permanent evolution into a Ten-Tailed Fox.[4]
Haruna later accepted [[Victor Elderblood]]'s Blood Dragon transformation and became a genuine True Dragon. Although the change replaced her Kitsune race with Draconic physiology, her former racial Essence and the abilities developed throughout her life were preserved and incorporated into her new existence. Her old techniques became compatible with the superior Energy produced by her [[Dragon Heart]], allowing her to employ abilities formerly dependent upon Youki or Senjutsu without necessarily reproducing their original Energy-generation processes. Her long experience with Natural Energy also contributed to her development as a type of Nature Dragon, granting her greater efficiency when using Energy originating from [[World Trees]].[5][6]
Haruna's transformation also advanced her toward a personal Divinity. She acquired the qualitative existence of a Dragon Goddess before that Concept had finished forming. After sensing the first traces, she suspected the direction was Foxes and the Moon. That early reading is not the later title. By the war against Darkness she names herself Goddess of War and Foxes, and the Thrones of Time and Space recognize a divine energy of war coming off her. The Moon remains the foundation of the Otsuki sword art. It is not the name she gives her Divinity in that introduction, and neither title is a claim of unrestricted authority over every fox or every act of war.[5][7][8]

#### Current Powers

##### Blood Dragon Physiology
Blood Dragon Physiology: Haruna's current existence is that of a genuine Blood Dragon belonging to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s lineage. Her transformation replaced the biological foundation of her original Nine-Tailed Kitsune state with the vastly superior physiology of a True Dragon while preserving important characteristics of her former race. The result is a Dragon Goddess whose supernatural development incorporates the martial knowledge, Energy control, elemental affinities and [[Natural Energy]] techniques accumulated throughout her earlier existence.[5][6]
- True Dragon Physiology: Haruna possesses the physical and supernatural constitution of a genuine Dragon rather than merely manifesting temporary Draconic characteristics. Her former Kitsune Essence was retained as part of her transformed existence instead of remaining a separate mortal racial foundation.[5]
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics: Haruna possesses extraordinary strength, durability, speed, reflexes, agility and endurance through her Draconic physiology. Her original Nine-Tailed body was already capable of fighting extremely powerful supernatural opponents, while Dragonization provided a superior physical foundation for further development.[2][5]
- Enhanced Strength: Her new Draconic constitution provides substantially greater physical power than her previous Kitsune body. This improvement allows Haruna to employ her martial techniques through a stronger body without remaining subject to the same physical limitations that existed before Dragonization.[5]
- Enhanced Durability: Haruna's Dragon body possesses the resilience associated with True Dragon physiology. The transformation allows her to endure and circulate much greater concentrations of supernatural Energy than her former racial constitution.[5][6]
- Enhanced Speed and Reflexes: Haruna retains her exceptional combat reactions and high-speed martial movements through her new body. Even before Dragonization, she could disappear from an opponent's ordinary perception through speed and deceptive movement, while training within the Tower of Nightmare subsequently improved her reactions against unfamiliar attacks.[2][4]
- Enhanced Agility: Haruna retains the exceptional body control required by her katana techniques, evasive maneuvers and ritualized martial movements. Her Draconic transformation preserved the skills associated with her original Kitsune existence rather than forcing her to develop an entirely unrelated fighting style.[2][6]
- Enhanced Senses: Haruna possesses the supernatural perception associated with [[True Dragons]]. Her evolved senses allowed her to perceive structures and environmental phenomena that ordinary inhabitants of the newly constructed Pantheon could not yet detect.[7]
- Supernatural Longevity: Haruna had already lived for more than two millennia as a powerful Kitsune before becoming a True Dragon. Her new existence possesses a biological foundation far beyond the limitations of ordinary mortal aging.[5]
- Draconic Transformation: Haruna possesses a natural Draconic appearance and can manifest the physical characteristics associated with her new race. During the migration of the Yokai into Victor's Pantheon, she deliberately displayed these characteristics as a declaration of her relationship with Victor and her transformation into a Dragon.[7]
- Shapeshifting: Haruna can alter her physical appearance through her Draconic abilities, most prominently by assuming the appearance of her former Nine-Tailed Fox. The transformation feels almost instinctive despite her Kitsune race having been replaced.[7]
- Flight: Haruna possesses supernatural aerial mobility. She could already move through the air before Dragonization, and her new True Dragon physiology provides a Draconic foundation for this capability.[9][5]
- [[Dragon Heart]]: Haruna possesses a genuine Dragon Heart that produces Energy of substantially greater quality than the Youki and processed Natural Energy upon which she previously depended. This new Energy source is particularly important because her established techniques can function through it.[6]
- [[Draconic Energy]] Manipulation: Haruna can utilize her Dragon Heart's Energy to power techniques originating from her former Kitsune and [[Senjutsu]] systems. The preserved abilities became integrated with her Draconic foundation instead of being lost during her racial conversion.[6]
- Racial Essence Preservation: Haruna retained a portion of her former Kitsune Essence, allowing the characteristics and techniques she had cultivated throughout her life to remain deeply connected to her current existence.[5][7]
- Natural Energy Affinity: Her former mastery of Senjutsu interacts favorably with her Draconic physiology, allowing her to utilize Energy originating from [[World Trees]] more efficiently than she could as a Kitsune.[6]
- Draconic Reality Interaction: As a genuine True Dragon, Haruna possesses the racial foundation necessary to develop the control over Reality associated with advanced Draconic techniques. However, she explicitly acknowledged that her personal mastery of Reality Distortion remained insufficient to safely learn the complete [[Seven Arts of the Blood Dragon Primogenitor]].[10]
Dragon Breath, Draconic Runes and Regeneration: Haruna possesses a True Dragon's supernatural physiology, but the available passages do not individually establish the characteristics of a unique personal Dragon Breath, a named Draconic Rune technique or a specific regenerative feat exclusive to her. These should be distinguished from directly demonstrated abilities rather than automatically expanded into every possible power possessed by other [[Blood Dragons]].

##### Draconic Energy and Former Technique Integration
Haruna's Blood Dragon transformation fundamentally changed how her original supernatural abilities operate. Before Dragonization, she depended upon the Youki naturally generated by her Kitsune body and the [[Natural Energy]] obtained through [[Senjutsu]]. These systems required their own methods of cultivation, circulation and control. Following the transformation, Haruna discovered that her former abilities had merged with her Draconic foundation, allowing them to operate through the stronger and purer Energy generated by her [[Dragon Heart]].[6]
- Energy Manipulation: Haruna possesses advanced control over supernatural Energy, having developed extensive experience with Youki, Natural Energy and [[Draconic Energy]]. Her mastery allows her to regulate Energy output while performing complex martial techniques.[2][11][6]
- Draconic Energy Channeling: Haruna can circulate the Energy generated by her Dragon Heart through techniques originally designed for Youki or processed Natural Energy.[6]
- Technique Compatibility: The supernatural abilities she learned before Dragonization remained usable through her new physiology because their underlying characteristics were assimilated into the foundation of her Draconic existence.[6]
- Energy-System Adaptation: Haruna can employ old techniques without repeating every step of their original Energy preparation. This considerably improves the efficiency of abilities that formerly required separate Youki generation or Senjutsu cultivation.[6]
- Advanced Energy Control: Haruna possesses exceptional precision when controlling her Energy, demonstrated through both her earlier army-linking technique and the elaborate Energy requirements of the Four Dances of the Moon. Nevertheless, she acknowledged that her Energy control had not yet reached the perfection required for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s highest Martial Art.[1][2][10]
- Energy Suppression: Haruna developed the ability to restrain enormous portions of her power and release them when necessary. During her duel with Victor, she removed the restraints upon her Youki and revealed reserves approximately ten times greater than those possessed by Genji.[2]
- Energy Reinforcement: Haruna can coat her body and weapons with supernatural Energy to improve defensive and offensive performance. During the duel with Victor, she protected herself with Youki and concentrated Energy through her katana.[2][3]
- Energy Projection: Haruna can release enormous quantities of supernatural Energy through ranged attacks, including dark blasts, lunar-shaped sword attacks and the combined elemental assault of Destruction of the Nine Elements.[2][3]
- Multiple Energy-System Knowledge: Haruna's practical mastery of Youki and Senjutsu, followed by her acquisition of Draconic Energy, gives her considerable experience understanding and adapting techniques that rely upon different supernatural Energy sources.[11][6]
The ability to operate her former techniques through Draconic Energy does not establish unrestricted access to every supernatural Energy type. In particular, Haruna has not been individually demonstrated freely generating or controlling all forms of Positivity, Negativity or other specialized World Tree Energies merely because her Dragon Heart can interact with Natural Energy.[6]

##### Nature Dragon Characteristics
Haruna's long experience with [[Senjutsu]] produced an unusual interaction with her Blood Dragon transformation. Because she had devoted considerable effort to understanding [[Natural Energy]] before becoming a Dragon, her evolved existence acquired an especially strong affinity for the Energy associated with Nature and [[World Trees]]. The resulting condition was described as a type of Nature Dragon, although her connection remained less intimate than [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s direct relationship with Roxanne and Amara.[6]
- Natural Energy Manipulation: Haruna can sense and manipulate the supernatural Energy circulating through Nature. She possessed this ability before Dragonization through her mastery of Senjutsu and retained it after becoming a True Dragon.[11][6]
- Environmental Energy Absorption: Haruna can draw Energy from her surroundings rather than depending exclusively upon power generated inside her body. This is one of the fundamental principles of Senjutsu that she personally taught Victor.[11]
- Ley-Line Interaction: Haruna understands how to interact with the enormous currents of Natural Energy circulating through planetary Ley Lines.[11]
- Natural Energy Conversion: Before Dragonization, Haruna used Senjutsu to process Natural Energy into a form compatible with her existence. Following the transformation, her [[Dragon Heart]] and evolved racial characteristics allowed a more direct and efficient relationship with similar Energy.[11][6]
- World Tree Energy Utilization: Haruna can use Energy originating from World Trees more efficiently after becoming a True Dragon. The capability developed from the interaction between her previous Senjutsu mastery and her current Draconic physiology.[6]
- Enhanced Nature Affinity: Haruna's connection to Nature became an integrated characteristic of her Draconic existence rather than remaining solely a learned external Energy-manipulation technique.[6]
- Senjutsu Mastery: Haruna possesses advanced proficiency in Senjutsu, allowing her to understand and teach methods of absorbing, filtering and circulating Natural Energy.[11]
- Senjutsu Instruction: Haruna personally instructed Victor in the principles of Senjutsu and Natural Energy. His exceptional relationship with Roxanne caused the surrounding Energy to respond to him with unusual affinity, allowing him to progress much more rapidly than an ordinary practitioner.[11]
Haruna's Nature Dragon characteristics should not be confused with possessing the same depth of connection to World Trees as Victor. Her efficiency improved substantially, but the novel explicitly distinguishes her relationship from Victor's direct spiritual bonds with Roxanne and Amara.[6]

##### Developing Divinity
Haruna's path toward Godhood originally followed the natural evolutionary progression of an exceptionally powerful Kitsune approaching the legendary tenth-tail state. ##### Racial Evolution Influence
Haruna's transformation into a True Dragon produced consequences extending beyond her individual power. Because she had spent most of her existence as a Kitsune and remained the principal leader of the Yokai, the qualitative change in her existence began influencing the racial development of the people connected to her. The effect was described as accelerating a transition in which Yokai would gradually become less dependent upon Youki and more naturally compatible with [[Senjutsu]] and [[Natural Energy]].[7]
- Racial Evolution Acceleration: Haruna's existence as a True Dragon accelerates the long-term development of the Yokai associated with her, encouraging changes that would otherwise require a considerably longer period.[7]
- Energy-System Adaptation Influence: The Yokai are expected to become naturally more compatible with Senjutsu, eventually reducing their reliance upon Youki as their primary Energy source.[7]
- Racial Development Influence: Haruna's continued connection to the species she formerly belonged to gives her existence an influence upon its evolutionary direction.[7]
- Passive Evolutionary Acceleration: The phenomenon occurs through Haruna's transformed existence rather than being described as a conventional technique that she must repeatedly activate.[7]
This influence operates gradually and does not establish the ability to instantly convert every Yokai into a Dragon, immediately awaken Divinities among the population or forcibly rewrite the existence of every individual belonging to her former race.[7]

- Supernatural Longevity: Haruna had already lived for more than two millennia before her Dragonization, demonstrating the extraordinary lifespan associated with her original racial development.[2]
- Nine-Tailed Evolution: Haruna possessed the maximum conventional number of tails available to a mortal Kitsune and was approaching the threshold associated with the legendary tenth-tail Divine state.[12]
- Enormous Youki Reserves: Haruna possessed approximately ten times the Youki reserves of [[Hashimoto Genji]], an exceptionally powerful Nine-Tailed Kitsune considerably older than herself.[2]

Youki was Haruna's principal racial Energy before Dragonization. Through her Nine-Tailed Kitsune constitution, she possessed an extraordinary capacity for generating and controlling this power. During her duel with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], removing the restraints upon her Energy produced a massive dark column of Youki that shocked the spectators, including Genji, who recognized that Haruna's reserves were approximately ten times greater than his own.[2]

- Youki Manipulation: Haruna possessed exceptional control over the movement, concentration and release of her Energy, using it to support elaborate combat techniques and maintain simultaneous connections with numerous allies.[1][2]
- Youki Projection: Haruna could release concentrated Youki through ranged attacks, including dark blasts and crescent-shaped sword beams.[2]

Following Dragonization, Haruna no longer needed to rely upon Youki as an independent source of Energy for the preserved techniques. Their mechanisms were integrated into her Draconic foundation and became compatible with the Energy generated by her [[Dragon Heart]].[6]

##### Senjutsu and Natural Energy
Haruna is an accomplished practitioner of [[Senjutsu]], a supernatural discipline involving the absorption, processing and manipulation of [[Natural Energy]] circulating through the environment. She possesses sufficient technical understanding to instruct other supernatural beings and personally introduced [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to the principles of the system during their time together in Japan.[11]
- Senjutsu Mastery: Haruna possesses advanced proficiency in the manipulation of Natural Energy and understands the principles required for its safe cultivation.[11]
- Natural Energy Absorption: Haruna can draw Energy from her surrounding environment rather than relying exclusively upon the power naturally generated inside her body.[11]
- Ley-Line Manipulation: Haruna understands the relationship between Senjutsu and the enormous currents of Natural Energy circulating through the planet's Ley Lines.[11]
- Energy Filtering: Traditional Senjutsu requires a practitioner to process the absorbed Natural Energy into a form compatible with their body. Haruna possesses practical knowledge of this process and the dangers associated with improper control.[11]
- Energy Conversion: Haruna can convert environmental Energy into a usable form, allowing the power circulating through Nature to contribute to her supernatural techniques.[11]
- Environmental Energy Sensitivity: Haruna can perceive and interact with Natural Energy circulating through her surroundings, a capability essential to advanced Senjutsu practice.[11]
- Senjutsu Instruction: Haruna personally taught Victor the theoretical and practical foundations of Senjutsu. His relationship with Roxanne allowed Natural Energy to react to him with extraordinary affinity, making his progress unusually rapid compared with that of ordinary practitioners.[11]
- World Tree Energy Utilization: Following Dragonization, Haruna's Senjutsu mastery became integrated with her True Dragon characteristics, considerably improving her ability to utilize Energy associated with [[World Trees]].[6]
Haruna also possesses knowledge of Ki through her original martial and Energy cultivation background. However, Ki and Senjutsu should not be treated as identical powers, and the novel does not establish that every technique based upon one system automatically operates through the other without the necessary understanding or compatibility.

#### Support and Specialized Techniques

##### Unnamed Linking Technique
Haruna possesses an exceptionally advanced support technique allowing her to distribute her Youki among the soldiers under her command. During the struggle for leadership over Japan's Yokai, she activated the ability after observing the injuries suffered by her army. Dark Energy extended from her nine tails into numerous thread-like connections that spread throughout her forces, linking the soldiers directly to her supernatural power.[1]
The technique produced two major benefits. Injured soldiers began recovering from their wounds, while those in critical condition were restored sufficiently to continue fighting. Afterward, Haruna's Youki flowed through the links and formed an additional protective layer around the connected warriors, significantly reducing their likelihood of dying in battle. The ability demonstrated extraordinary Energy precision because maintaining the connections required Haruna to divide and control her power across an enormous number of individual subordinates simultaneously.[1]
Its primary disadvantage was the pain transmitted through the links. Haruna personally experienced the suffering inflicted upon her soldiers, effectively accepting part of the burden imposed upon her entire army. Genji recognized the technique as unusually difficult to master and explained that the individual developing it would require an exceptional combination of Youki reserves, Energy control and willingness to endure the consequences of maintaining the connections.[1]
- Mass Energy Distribution: Haruna can distribute her Youki among large numbers of soldiers simultaneously.[1]
- Energy Thread Creation: Dark thread-like extensions of her Energy connect Haruna to the warriors receiving her support.[1]
- Healing: Wounded soldiers began recovering after the technique was activated, while severely injured subordinates were restored from critical conditions.[1]
- Defensive Reinforcement: Haruna's Energy forms an additional protective layer around the connected soldiers, increasing their resistance to enemy attacks.[1]
- Survivability Enhancement: The combined restoration and protection substantially reduce the likelihood of the supported warriors dying during combat.[1]
- Pain Sharing: Haruna experiences the pain suffered by the individuals linked to her Energy, making the technique personally costly despite its benefits to her forces.[1]
- Multi-Target Energy Control: Maintaining the links requires the precise simultaneous distribution of Youki across numerous separate individuals.[1]
- Military Support: The technique allows Haruna to strengthen her entire army rather than restricting the benefits of her enormous Energy reserves to her personal attacks.[1]
The demonstrated effect concerns Energy distribution, restoration, reinforcement and the transmission of pain. It does not establish that every physical wound received by an ally is automatically reproduced upon Haruna's own body. Likewise, although her former techniques became compatible with [[Draconic Energy]] after Dragonization, the exact post-transformation output and maximum number of targets supported by this particular technique have not been independently demonstrated.[1][6]

##### Mugetsu
Mugetsu is one of Haruna's most unusual sword techniques, combining extraordinary speed with a supernatural cutting effect that does not necessarily inflict conventional wounds. Haruna demonstrated the technique during her confrontation with the Yokai opposing her leadership, releasing dark Energy before moving through the enemy formation and returning her katana to its sheath. The affected opponents initially appeared unharmed, but their bodies subsequently separated into multiple sections without the ordinary blood loss or fatal consequences associated with physical dismemberment.[16]
The separated pieces retained unusual characteristics. Rather than simply becoming dead flesh, the individual parts could continue moving, while dark smoke-like Energy appeared around the boundaries created by the technique. The result allowed Haruna to incapacitate a large group of opponents without immediately killing them, making Mugetsu particularly valuable during a campaign whose objective involved unifying the Yokai rather than exterminating the population she intended to govern.[16]
Haruna later employed Mugetsu against Shur during the invasion of [[Nightingale]]. The technique divided the Elder God's enormous body into thousands of pieces, and Shur discovered that the separated parts were not regenerating through the ordinary process he expected. However, his connection to an external source of immortality allowed him to abandon the damaged body and reconstruct himself through another manifestation, demonstrating that Mugetsu could interfere with his immediate physical regeneration without completely negating every mechanism sustaining his existence.[4]
- Supernatural Cutting: Mugetsu produces a cutting effect that can divide a target's body into separate sections without relying exclusively upon ordinary physical wounds.[16]
- Non-Lethal Dismemberment: Haruna can separate opponents into pieces without necessarily killing them or causing conventional bleeding.[16]
- Body Separation: The technique divides an affected target into numerous independent sections, leaving supernatural Energy around the boundaries of the separated pieces.[16]
- Containment: Mugetsu can incapacitate dangerous opponents without requiring their deaths, making it useful when Haruna intends to conquer or subdue an enemy faction.[16]
- Regeneration Interference: Against Shur, Mugetsu prevented the separated body parts from regenerating through their ordinary method, temporarily forcing the Elder God to reconstruct himself by another means.[4]
- Large-Target Application: Haruna can apply Mugetsu against enemies far larger than ordinary Humans or Yokai, as demonstrated when she divided Shur's enormous body into thousands of pieces.[4]
- High-Speed Iaijutsu: Mugetsu involves an extremely rapid movement and sword-sheathing sequence, with the affected opponents often understanding the results only after Haruna has completed the technique.[16]
- Dark Energy Application: Mugetsu is accompanied by dark supernatural Energy that remains visible around the affected body sections.[16]
The underlying metaphysical mechanism of Mugetsu has not been fully explained. Its effects should not automatically be equated with unrestricted Existence Erasure, universal Durability Negation, unlimited Spatial Cutting or complete negation of every form of immortality. Its demonstrated applications are extraordinary nonlethal disassembly and temporary interference with certain regenerative processes.[16][4]

#### Otsuki Martial Arts
Haruna is one of the most accomplished practitioners of the martial system developed by her grandfather, [[Otsuki Yoichi]]. The style combines conventional katana techniques with supernatural Energy, movement, deception and ritualized offensive sequences. Its principal offensive branch is the Four Dances of the Moon, while the supporting principles of Control and Deception can be integrated into other martial disciplines. The system therefore functions as more than a collection of isolated sword attacks: it is a structured martial philosophy in which individual movements, their order and the practitioner's Energy control determine the effectiveness and safety of the completed technique.[2]
Haruna's exceptional talent allowed her to develop beyond the original limitations imposed upon the system. Yoichi explained that the Dances operate through a rhythm resembling a continuous ritual or musical composition. Once the sequence has begun, the practitioner must maintain a compatible progression because improperly altering or interrupting the ritual can cause the accumulated Energy to harm the user. Haruna nevertheless succeeded in adding continuations that Yoichi had not originally considered possible, demonstrating a degree of understanding surpassing the simple reproduction of inherited techniques.[2]

##### Martial Arts Mastery
- Master Martial Artist: Haruna possesses extraordinary proficiency in supernatural [[Martial Arts]], having trained for more than two millennia and developed beyond important portions of the techniques inherited from the Otsuki lineage.[17][2]
- Katana Mastery: Haruna specializes in Japanese swordsmanship and performs most of her demonstrated martial techniques through a black katana.[16][2]
- Iaijutsu: Haruna can perform extraordinarily rapid draw-and-sheath techniques, including Mugetsu, which combines high-speed sword movement with an unusual supernatural cutting effect.[16]
- Ritualized Martial Arts: Haruna can perform complex sequences whose individual movements must maintain a precise relationship with one another. The Four Dances of the Moon demonstrate the interaction between physical movement, supernatural Energy and ritual structure.[2]
- Martial Arts Adaptation: Haruna can modify inherited techniques without triggering the damaging consequences normally associated with incorrectly altering their ritual sequence.[2]
- Martial Arts Creation: Haruna developed new continuations within the Otsuki system, including Waning Quarter, an extension of the First Dance that Yoichi had not originally created.[2]
- Combat Deception: Haruna can combine swordsmanship with deceptive movement and Kitsune illusions, making it difficult for opponents to identify the location and direction of her actual attacks.[2][3]
- Energy-Controlled Swordsmanship: Haruna can channel enormous amounts of Youki through her katana without disrupting the rhythm of her Martial Art.[2]
- Weak-Point Targeting: Haruna can direct precise sword attacks against vulnerable portions of an opponent's body. Moonlight Flower specifically combines six cuts into a pattern intended to destroy the target through attacks against multiple weak points.[3]
- Multiple-Body Combat: Haruna can coordinate attacks between several separate versions of herself, allowing simultaneous attacks and the use of different elemental affinities.[2][3]
- High-Speed Combat: Haruna can combine extraordinary movement speed with illusions and martial techniques, allowing her to attack from positions the opponent struggles to perceive accurately.[2]
- Adaptive Combat Strategy: Haruna can change the nature of her attacks as a battle develops. During the duel against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], she progressed from ordinary swordsmanship and deception into increasingly powerful lunar attacks, gravitational suppression, multiple bodies and simultaneous elemental attacks.[2][3]

##### Hadō and the Martial Rhythm
Haruna's martial development is closely connected to the principles taught by Yoichi concerning the rhythm and intention expressed through combat. During her duel with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], the deeper meaning of the martial system was described through the metaphor of music. The rhythm of a warrior's movements could communicate intent, character and philosophy to another sufficiently perceptive martial artist, allowing combat to become a method of understanding an opponent rather than merely defeating them.[18]
- Combat Rhythm: Haruna can maintain the precise sequence of movement and Energy required by the Four Dances of the Moon.[2]
- Martial Intent: Her fighting style expresses intent through the relationship between her movements, attacks and supernatural Energy.[18]
- Martial Understanding: Haruna possesses the technical knowledge required to understand the rhythm of advanced [[Martial Arts]] and develop compatible modifications to their structure.[2][18]
- Self-Development: The principles associated with the Otsuki system emphasize continued refinement, questioning and personal development rather than rigidly reproducing inherited movements.[18]
The metaphorical description of martial movements as music does not establish that Haruna literally generates or controls conventional sound waves whenever she fights.

##### May
May is a deception-oriented technique associated with Haruna's Kitsune martial arts. Upon activation, Haruna's tails move hypnotically while her body appears to vanish and reappear at unpredictable locations within the opponent's field of vision. The resulting effect combines deceptive movement with the illusion-related characteristics of her original Kitsune existence, making her actual position difficult to determine.[2]
- Illusion Manipulation: Haruna can produce deceptive visual effects that interfere with an opponent's perception of her position.[2]

- Evasive Deception: The technique allows Haruna to evade attacks through movements that the opponent initially perceives incorrectly. During her duel with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], he had to alter his perception to identify where she was actually standing.[2]
May should not be automatically classified as unrestricted control over every sense of every target. Its demonstrated effects concern deceptive visual perception, apparent movement and combat misdirection.

#### Four Dances of the Moon
The Four Dances of the Moon are the principal offensive techniques of the [[Otsuki Clan]]'s martial tradition, originally developed by Yoichi and subsequently refined by Haruna. The system consists of an escalating sequence of sword techniques associated with lunar imagery, Energy projection and progressively increasing gravitational pressure. The Dances are designed to restrict the opponent's ability to evade or counterattack while preparing increasingly dangerous finishing techniques.[2][3]
The ritualized structure imposes significant restrictions upon ordinary practitioners. Once the sequence begins, the techniques must progress through compatible movements because changing the rhythm incorrectly can cause the martial Energy to rebound upon the user. Haruna's technical talent allowed her to develop additional continuations while preserving the integrity of the system, an accomplishment that surprised Yoichi himself.[2]

##### First Dance: Waning Moon
Waning Moon is the First Dance of the Otsuki martial system. Haruna channels an enormous amount of Youki into her katana before releasing a gigantic crescent-shaped beam resembling a waning moon. The attack possesses enormous destructive power and can traverse the battlefield as a concentrated wave of supernatural Energy. During her duel with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], the attack was powerful enough that the spectators recognized it as an extraordinary display even by the standards of a Nine-Tailed Kitsune.[2]
- Lunar Energy Projection: Haruna releases a gigantic crescent-shaped attack resembling the waning phase of the Moon.[2]
- Concentrated Youki Manipulation: The technique requires Haruna to channel enormous quantities of Youki through her katana.[2]
- Long-Range Energy Attack: Waning Moon can travel across a large battlefield before striking the target or surrounding environment.[2]
- Area Destruction: The concentrated lunar attack possesses enough power to cause enormous destruction when it collides with the terrain.[2]

###### Continuation: Waning Quarter
Waning Quarter is Haruna's personally developed continuation of the First Dance. Unlike the original system, which proceeds directly into the Second Dance after Waning Moon, Haruna modified the ritual sequence to permit another coordinated attack without interrupting the martial rhythm. During the duel against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], she created multiple versions of herself and had four of them simultaneously release enhanced crescent-shaped attacks while the central Haruna maintained the formation.[2]
- [[Martial Arts]] Modification: Haruna created a continuation to the First Dance that was not part of Yoichi's original system.[2]
- Multiple-Body Coordination: Haruna can coordinate the attack between several versions of herself.[2]
- Simultaneous Lunar Attacks: Four Harunas released coordinated crescent-shaped beams during the demonstrated continuation.[2]
- Enhanced Attack Output: The continuation generated attacks described as faster and more powerful than the initial Waning Moon.[2]
- Ritual Integrity: Haruna maintained the necessary martial rhythm despite altering the sequence in a manner that would normally endanger the user.[2]

##### Second Dance: Moon Fall
Moon Fall is the Second Dance of the Otsuki martial system. Haruna increases the gravitational pressure affecting the battlefield and creates an enormous artificial Moon in the sky. The construct becomes a source of additional attacks, releasing numerous concentrated Energy beams toward the target while the increased gravity makes evasion progressively more difficult.[2][3]
- Moon Manifestation: Haruna creates an enormous artificial Moon above the battlefield.[2][3]
- Gravity Manipulation: The presence of the lunar construct greatly increases the gravitational pressure affecting the target and surrounding environment.[2]
- Movement Restriction: The increased gravity reduces the opponent's mobility and makes avoiding subsequent attacks more difficult.[2][3]
- Energy Barrage: The artificial Moon can produce thousands of concentrated Energy beams capable of striking the battlefield repeatedly.[3]
- Automated Target Pressure: The construct can continue threatening the opponent with additional attacks while Haruna prepares the following stages of her martial sequence.[3]
- Long-Range Suppression: The lunar beams and gravitational pressure allow Haruna to control the battlefield without remaining in immediate physical contact with the target.[3]
The artificial Moon is a supernatural construct created through Haruna's Martial Art. Its existence during the duel does not establish that she permanently created an ordinary natural satellite.

##### Third Dance: Moonlight Flower
Moonlight Flower is the Third Dance of the Otsuki martial system. The technique substantially intensifies the gravitational restrictions established by the previous stages while combining concentrated lunar Energy, deception and a precisely executed series of sword attacks. During the duel with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], the gravitational pressure reached approximately one hundred times Earth's gravity, greatly restricting his movement before Haruna attacked the vulnerable portions of his body through six coordinated cuts.[3]
- Gravity Amplification: The technique increases gravitational pressure to approximately one hundred times Earth's normal gravity.[3]
- Movement Suppression: The intensified gravity restricts the target's mobility and makes it difficult to evade Haruna's subsequent attacks.[3]
- Lunar Energy Projection: The artificial Moon releases highly concentrated beams capable of penetrating extremely durable terrain.[3]
- Illusory Concealment: Haruna uses deceptive movement and illusions to conceal her true location while the opponent is occupied by the lunar attacks.[3]
- Energy-Coated Katana: Haruna concentrates Youki around her sword until the blade appears as an exceptionally sharp dark weapon.[3]
- Sixfold Weak-Point Assault: Haruna performs six coordinated strikes aimed at vulnerable parts of the target's body, producing a flower-like pattern through the completed cuts.[3]
- Youki Poisoning: The attacks carry a poisonous or weakening application of Haruna's Youki. If the target survives the primary attack, this secondary effect is intended to reduce their ability to resist the next Dance.[3]
- Multi-Layered Assault: Moonlight Flower combines gravity, Energy projection, illusions, precise sword attacks and a secondary weakening effect within the same sequence.[3]
The poisonous effect is a secondary mechanism intended to weaken opponents who survive the direct attacks. Its presence does not establish universal poisoning of every biological or spiritual target, nor does it guarantee that the following Dance will succeed against every opponent.[3]

##### Fourth Dance: Full Moon
Full Moon is the highest established stage of the traditional Four Dances of the Moon. The technique intensifies the gravitational suppression to approximately three hundred times Earth's normal gravity while maintaining the destructive influence of the artificial Moon created through the previous stages. Haruna subsequently divides herself into nine bodies, preparing the conditions necessary to release the complete combination of her elemental affinities.[3]
- Gravity Amplification: Full Moon increases the gravitational pressure affecting the target to approximately three hundred times Earth's normal gravity.[3]
- Extreme Movement Restriction: The intensified pressure can force an opponent to the ground and make standing or moving extraordinarily difficult.[3]
- Lunar Energy Projection: The artificial Moon can continue releasing concentrated attacks while the gravitational suppression is active.[3]
- Ninefold Splitting: Haruna divides into nine combat-capable bodies, consisting of her original self and eight additional versions.[3]
- Elemental Preparation: Each of the nine Harunas can channel a different elemental affinity through the corresponding tail.[3]
- Finishing Technique Preparation: Full Moon establishes the conditions used by Destruction of the Nine Elements, combining gravitational suppression with simultaneous elemental attacks.[3]
The four Dances are not interchangeable isolated techniques when performed as the complete ritualized sequence. Their effectiveness depends upon the accumulated conditions created by the preceding stages and the practitioner's ability to maintain the necessary rhythm.[2][3]

#### Elemental Powers

##### Nine Elemental Affinities
Haruna mastered all nine elemental affinities associated with the tails of her original Nine-Tailed Kitsune existence. During her duel with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], she divided into nine bodies, each channeling a separate element. The affinities were explicitly identified as Fire, Water, Earth, Wind, Metal, Wood, Lightning, Darkness and Light. The final affinity was especially surprising because Haruna belonged to a Black Kitsune lineage whose inherent characteristics were associated with Darkness rather than Light.[3]
- Fire Manipulation: Haruna possesses an elemental affinity for Fire and can channel Fire-based Energy through her martial techniques. This affinity was individually manifested by one of the bodies participating in Destruction of the Nine Elements.[3]
- Water Manipulation: Haruna possesses an elemental affinity for Water, allowing the corresponding body to contribute Water-based supernatural Energy to her combined elemental attacks.[3]
- Earth Manipulation: Haruna possesses an elemental affinity for Earth, which can be incorporated into her elemental techniques and the combined finishing attack of her Nine-Tailed state.[3]
- Wind Manipulation: Haruna possesses an elemental affinity for Wind, allowing the associated body to channel Wind-based Energy during her simultaneous elemental assault.[3]
- Metal Manipulation: Haruna possesses an elemental affinity for Metal and can employ Metal-associated supernatural Energy as part of her nine-element system.[3]
- Wood Manipulation: Haruna possesses an elemental affinity for Wood, allowing her to employ Wood-associated Energy through the elemental techniques of her original Kitsune form.[3]
- Lightning Manipulation: Haruna possesses an elemental affinity for Lightning and can incorporate Lightning-based Energy into her attacks.[3]
- Darkness Manipulation: Darkness is a characteristic affinity associated with Haruna's Black Kitsune lineage. She demonstrated the ability to manipulate dark supernatural Energy through her racial techniques and elemental attacks.[2][3]
- Light Manipulation: Haruna possesses an unusual affinity for Light despite originating from a Black Kitsune lineage associated with Darkness. The manifestation of this element was treated as a remarkable anomaly by Yoichi.[3]
Haruna's acquisition of all nine elemental affinities confirms their availability within her original Kitsune system. However, individual affinities should not automatically be expanded into every possible specialized ability associated with an element. For example, possessing Wood Manipulation does not by itself demonstrate unrestricted control over all plant life, while possessing Light Manipulation does not establish complete authority over every form of electromagnetic radiation.

##### Light Affinity Anomaly
Haruna's Light affinity is one of the most unusual characteristics of her original Kitsune development. The Otsuki lineage consists predominantly of Black Kitsune whose natural characteristics are associated with Darkness. As explained during the duel against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], a Kitsune's elemental affinities develop as additional tails are obtained, but the emergence of an element directly opposing the individual's original racial nature was considered extraordinarily unlikely.[3]
Haruna nevertheless acquired Light as her ninth demonstrated affinity, completing a collection containing elements that would ordinarily be incompatible within a single Black Kitsune. Yoichi regarded the development as an anomaly, emphasizing the extraordinary nature of Haruna's racial potential and her ability to progress beyond traditional limitations.[3]
- Opposing Elemental Affinity: Haruna possesses both Darkness and Light affinities despite their opposition within the traditional characteristics of her racial lineage.[3]
- Elemental Compatibility: She can channel Light alongside the other eight elemental affinities during Destruction of the Nine Elements.[3]
- Anomalous Racial Development: Haruna's acquisition of Light contradicts the conventional expectations described for Black Kitsune of the Otsuki lineage.[3]

##### Splitting
Splitting is a Kitsune ability that allows Haruna to create multiple separate versions of herself. Unlike simple visual afterimages, the resulting bodies are capable of performing combat actions and channeling supernatural Energy. Haruna demonstrated this ability through the continuation of the First Dance and later used a complete nine-body formation during the Fourth Dance.[2][3]
- Body Duplication: Haruna can create multiple combat-capable versions of herself.[2][3]
- Ninefold Division: Her complete demonstrated formation consists of nine Harunas, including the original body and eight additional versions.[3]
- Independent Elemental Channeling: Each body in the ninefold formation can channel a different elemental affinity corresponding to one of Haruna's tails.[3]
- Simultaneous Attacks: Haruna can coordinate multiple bodies to perform attacks at the same time, increasing the difficulty of defending against her techniques.[2][3]
- [[Martial Arts]] Integration: Splitting can be incorporated into the Four Dances of the Moon and the preparation of Destruction of the Nine Elements.[2][3]
The continuation of the First Dance employed a smaller group of bodies, whereas the Fourth Dance demonstrated Haruna's complete ninefold formation. The distinction is important because the number of bodies used in a particular attack does not necessarily equal the maximum number available through her Kitsune physiology.[2][3]

##### Destruction of the Nine Elements
Destruction of the Nine Elements is Haruna's most powerful demonstrated conventional finishing technique from her original Nine-Tailed Kitsune state. After establishing the conditions created by the Four Dances of the Moon, Haruna divides into nine bodies and channels a different elemental affinity through each. The resulting attacks are released simultaneously and combine into an enormous multicolored pillar of Energy that connects with the artificial Moon above the battlefield.[3]
During her duel with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], the combined elemental attack caused the artificial Moon to descend toward the ground, producing an immense explosion that engulfed the battlefield. The technique was designed to take advantage of the extreme gravitational suppression imposed by Full Moon, leaving the opponent with increasingly limited opportunities to escape before the elemental attacks converged.[3]
- Nine-Element Manipulation: Haruna simultaneously employs Fire, Water, Earth, Wind, Metal, Wood, Lightning, Darkness and Light through her divided bodies.[3]
- Simultaneous Energy Projection: All nine bodies release their respective elemental attacks at the same time.[3]
- Elemental Combination: The separate attacks converge into an enormous multicolored pillar of supernatural Energy.[3]
- Lunar Integration: The combined elemental pillar connects with the artificial Moon created through the preceding Dances.[3]
- Moonfall Finisher: The interaction causes the lunar construct to descend toward the battlefield, greatly increasing the scale of the resulting attack.[3]
- Large-Scale Destruction: The technique produces an enormous explosion capable of devastating the surrounding combat area.[3]
- Gravity-Assisted Suppression: The technique is especially effective after Full Moon has severely restricted the target's movement through approximately three hundred times Earth's gravity.[3]
The demonstrated technique depends upon the combination of Haruna's elemental affinities, Splitting and the lunar conditions established by her Martial Art. It should not be interpreted as proof that she can spontaneously reproduce the same complete finishing attack under every possible circumstance without preparing the necessary conditions.

- Afterimage Generation: Her high-speed movement and deceptive abilities can produce multiple apparent versions of herself within an opponent's perception.[2]
- Combat Concealment: Haruna can conceal her true position while preparing a sword attack or maintaining pressure through the artificial Moon.[3]
- Illusion-Assisted Evasion: Haruna can use deceptive positioning to avoid direct attacks, including strikes from opponents whose physical speed and power exceed her own.[2][3]
- Multi-Layered Combat Deception: Her illusions can operate alongside gravitational suppression and Energy barrages, forcing opponents to respond to several different threats while attempting to locate her actual position.[3]
These feats establish sophisticated combat illusions and visual misdirection. They do not establish unrestricted control over every sense, unconditional Mind Manipulation or the ability to deceive every type of supernatural perception without limitation.

Haruna temporarily entered the legendary tenth-tail state during her battle against Shur. Her body expanded into an enormous Fox approximately 270 meters tall while a tenth tail manifested, and her power developed characteristics associated with a Moonlight Fox approaching Divine existence.[4]

- Forced Metamorphosis: Haruna temporarily crossed the threshold separating her Nine-Tailed Kitsune state from the legendary Ten-Tailed Divine Fox state.[4]

##### The Fall of the Stars
The Fall of the Stars is the most extraordinary attack Haruna demonstrated during her temporary Ten-Tailed Moonlight Fox metamorphosis. After completing the transformation, she directed her newly acquired power toward the sky, causing numerous beams or shooting-star-like attacks to appear from space. The successive impacts inflicted severe damage and forced Shur upward toward space while the external source sustaining his extraordinary immortality was failing. - Celestial Attack Manifestation: Haruna can produce an enormous bombardment of beams or shooting-star-like attacks descending from space while in her temporary Ten-Tailed state.[4]
- Long-Range Energy Projection: The attack originates from beyond the immediate battlefield and descends toward its target from the sky.[4]
- Multiple Projectile Generation: Numerous separate beams or projectiles can strike the target in succession.[4]
- Trajectory Manipulation: Haruna can redirect the approaching attacks, concentrating their movement upon a chosen target rather than allowing an uncontrolled planetary bombardment.[4]
- Large-Scale Destructive Potential: [[Shiva]] and Velnorah regarded the approaching attacks as sufficiently dangerous to prepare measures intended to protect the planet from their impact.[4]
- Elder-God Damage: The concentrated bombardment inflicted severe damage upon Shur after the power sustaining his immortality had become unstable.[4]
- Battlefield Targeting: Haruna demonstrated the ability to direct an enormous attack toward a specific enemy despite its apparent scale.[4]
The novel describes the attack through celestial imagery and beams arriving from space. It does not provide sufficient astronomical detail to establish the mass, size, composition or origin of each projectile as a literal star. Accordingly, the feat should not be expanded into unrestricted control over every star in the Universe, the creation of full-sized stellar bodies or automatic destruction of an entire planetary system.[4]
Likewise, Shur's loss of immortality was associated with the failure of the external Energy sustaining him rather than being established as a direct negation caused by The Fall of the Stars. Haruna's bombardment inflicted severe damage after that failure, while Vlad completed the Elder God's destruction.[4]

#### External Enhancements and Protection

##### Guidance of Beginnings
After Haruna injured her Soul through the premature Ten-Tailed transformation, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] examined her condition and used his recently awakened authority over Beginnings to assist her development. The intervention manifested as golden Energy surrounding Haruna, and she experienced the metaphysical barrier obstructing her progress as an enormous wall that had suddenly developed a crack.[13]
Victor explained that Beginnings could guide another being toward a compatible path of development but could not simply grant an arbitrary mature Divinity without the individual's own understanding and progression. Haruna consequently received assistance toward her eventual ascension rather than becoming a wielder of Victor's personal authority over Beginnings.[13]
- Divine Guidance (External): Victor used Beginnings to assist Haruna's development after her dangerous transformation.[13]
- Progression Guidance (External): The intervention helped Haruna move toward a more suitable path for her future Divine development.[13]
- Metaphysical Barrier Weakening (External): Haruna perceived the enormous wall obstructing her advancement developing a crack after Victor's intervention.[13]
- Recovery Assistance (External): Victor's intervention helped Haruna following the dangerous damage inflicted upon her Soul during the forced evolution.[13]
Beginnings is not Haruna's personal Divinity. The cited passage establishes that she was the recipient of Victor's assistance, not that she acquired unrestricted access to the Concept of Beginnings herself.[13]

##### Protection Provided by Victor
During the battle against Shur, Haruna encountered a dangerous red miasma capable of rapidly damaging and consuming supernatural bodies. Although she managed to retreat after her instincts warned her of the danger, part of her arm was affected by the substance. A protective measure previously provided by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] activated and concentrated Energy around the affected area, preventing the immediate consequences from progressing in the same manner observed when Vlad's infected arm was destroyed.[4]
- External Defensive Protection: Haruna possessed a protective measure provided by Victor that activated automatically when she was exposed to Shur's dangerous red miasma.[4]
- Danger-Triggered Activation: The protection responded to the harmful substance affecting Haruna and concentrated Energy around the endangered portion of her body.[4]
This protection should not be indexed as Haruna's native resistance to every form of Corruption, Existence Erasure or [[Eldritch Energy]]. The incident demonstrates the usefulness of Victor's external protection and the danger posed by Shur's attack, not absolute immunity inherent to Haruna's former Kitsune physiology.[4]

#### Combat Skills

##### Swordsmanship
Haruna is an accomplished swordswoman whose principal weapon is a black katana. Her fighting style combines Japanese swordsmanship with Youki manipulation, illusions, high-speed movement, elemental techniques and the ritualized martial principles developed through the Otsuki lineage. Rather than employing her abilities as unrelated attacks, Haruna coordinates their effects to restrict an opponent's movement and create increasingly favorable opportunities for decisive strikes.[16][2][3]
- Master Swordsmanship: Haruna possesses exceptional proficiency with a katana, developed through centuries of training and practical combat.[16][2]
- Iaijutsu Mastery: Haruna can perform rapid draw-and-sheath sword techniques, including Mugetsu.[16]
- Energy-Enhanced Swordsmanship: Haruna can channel Youki through her katana to greatly increase the supernatural effects of her attacks.[2][3]
- Lunar Sword Techniques: Haruna's Four Dances employ katana movements to produce enormous crescent-shaped attacks, lunar constructs and increasingly destructive finishing techniques.[2][3]
- High-Speed Sword Attacks: Haruna can execute sword movements rapidly enough that the effects are not immediately understood by the opponent, as demonstrated through Mugetsu and her duel with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[16][2]
- Weak-Point Targeting: Haruna can direct attacks toward vulnerable portions of an opponent's body, most prominently through the sixfold sword sequence of Moonlight Flower.[3]
- Illusion-Assisted Swordsmanship: Haruna can combine deceptive movement and visual illusions with direct katana attacks, making her true position and attack angle difficult to identify.[2]
- Multiple-Body Swordsmanship: Haruna can coordinate martial techniques between several versions of herself during her Kitsune formations.[2][3]
- Advanced [[Martial Arts]] Adaptation: Haruna possesses sufficient technical understanding to develop continuations to inherited Martial Arts without breaking the ritual rhythm required for their safe execution.[2]
- [[Draconic Energy]] Integration: Her former sword techniques remain compatible with the Energy generated by her [[Dragon Heart]] after Dragonization.[6]
- Master-Level Swordsmanship: By the fight with [[Aetherys]], drawing Ikari is treated as proof that she has reached the master level of her art.[8]
- Ikari: Her later principal weapon is the violet odachi Victor gave her. The older black katana is the weapon of the Four Dances. Ikari is the weapon of the War form.[8]

##### Combat Experience
- Master Combatant: Haruna possesses more than two millennia of supernatural combat experience and has confronted powerful Yokai, Divine beings and extraordinarily dangerous opponents.[2][4]
- Battlefield Adaptation: Haruna can alter her tactics in response to an opponent's capabilities. During her duel with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], she progressed from direct attacks into illusions, lunar constructs, increasing gravitational pressure and combined elemental attacks.[2][3]
- High-Speed Combat: Haruna can fight at extraordinary speeds, combining direct movement with deceptive techniques that make tracking her location difficult.[2]
- Combat Perception: Haruna possesses excellent combat awareness and can react to unfamiliar supernatural attacks. Training within the Tower of Nightmare improved her reactions sufficiently to evade an unexpected Energy beam from Shur, although part of her tails was burned.[4]
- Regeneration Countermeasures: Haruna understands the tactical value of preventing an immortal opponent from restoring their body. During the battle against Shur, she used Mugetsu to interfere with his immediate regeneration while coordinating her attacks with Vlad.[4]
- Cooperative Combat: Haruna can fight alongside other powerful supernatural beings despite differences in their abilities or personal relationships. Her cooperation with Vlad against Shur demonstrates this capability.[4]
- Battlefield Reading: Haruna can assess the actions and intentions of other combatants, using their behavior to determine when cooperation, withdrawal or an alternative attack is necessary.[4]
- [[Martial Arts]] Comprehension: Haruna can rapidly recognize the principles underlying complex Martial Arts. During Victor's demonstration of the Seven Arts, she understood the extraordinary requirements of the system and recognized the areas in which her own development remained insufficient.[10]

##### Seven Arts of the Blood Dragon Primogenitor
Haruna participated in the examination of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s [[Seven Arts of the Blood Dragon Primogenitor]], a Martial Art designed around the supernatural physiology and Reality-altering characteristics of [[True Dragons]]. Although she immediately recognized important principles governing the system, she acknowledged that her own capabilities were not yet sufficient to learn the complete Martial Art safely.[10]
The system requires exceptionally precise Energy control, advanced Reality Distortion and the ability to manipulate Energy and the Soul simultaneously while maintaining the necessary martial movements. Haruna explained that her Energy control was already highly developed but had not reached the required perfection, while her mastery of Reality Distortion needed substantial further training.[10]
- [[Martial Arts]] Analysis: Haruna can examine extremely advanced techniques and recognize the relationship between their individual components.[10]
- Energy Control Development: Haruna possesses advanced Energy control but acknowledged that it was not yet perfect enough to satisfy the requirements of the complete Seven Arts.[10]
- Reality Distortion Training: Haruna identified the need to improve her control over Reality Distortion before attempting to master Victor's Martial Art.[10]
- Advanced Martial Foundation: Her experience with the Otsuki system provides an extensive martial foundation, although her existing proficiency does not automatically grant mastery over techniques operating through entirely different supernatural principles.[2][10]
The Seven Arts should therefore be classified as a potential future development rather than a completed Martial Art Haruna has already mastered in the cited material.

#### Leadership and Non-Combat Skills
Haruna is not merely an accomplished personal combatant. Her greatest historical achievement before joining [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction was the unification of enormous portions of Japan's historically divided Yokai population. She accomplished this through a combination of military strength, political leadership, strategic planning and the ability to inspire or compel cooperation among supernatural beings who had previously resisted centralized authority.[19][20]
- Supreme Command: Haruna possesses extensive experience commanding large supernatural armies composed of different Yokai races and Clans.[19][1]
- Military Leadership: Haruna personally directs military operations and coordinates the actions of powerful subordinates during large-scale conflicts.[1]
- Strategic Planning: Haruna understands how to employ military force to achieve political objectives. Her original campaign sought to unify Yokai factions rather than destroy the people she intended to govern.[19][16]
- Faction Leadership: Haruna successfully established herself as one of Japan's principal supernatural rulers and retained responsibility for the Yokai integrated into Victor's larger faction.[20][7]
- Faction Administration: Haruna oversees the political and social affairs of the Yokai associated with her leadership, including their integration into new Imperial settlements.[7]
- Military Organization: Haruna possesses experience coordinating armies consisting of numerous supernatural species, each with different abilities and combat requirements.[19][1]
- Alliance Building: Haruna participated in negotiations establishing political protection for the Yokai while preserving their independence from other supernatural factions.[21][22]
- Diplomacy: Haruna is capable of negotiating with representatives of powerful supernatural factions, including Victor and the [[Shinto Pantheon]], while protecting the interests of her people.[23][24]
- Charismatic Leadership: Haruna's personal strength and determination earned the loyalty or recognition of numerous Yokai who had previously followed independent leaders.[19][20]
- [[Martial Arts]] Instruction: Haruna possesses the technical understanding necessary to teach advanced martial principles and Energy manipulation, including [[Senjutsu]].[11]
- [[Natural Energy]] Knowledge: Haruna understands the relationship between environmental Energy, planetary Ley Lines and the safe cultivation of Senjutsu.[11]
- Yokai Cultural Knowledge: Haruna possesses extensive familiarity with the traditions, physiology and political organization of the Yokai, developed through her leadership of Japan's supernatural factions.[19][7]
- Architectural Planning: Haruna participated in planning the settlements established for the Yokai within Victor's Pantheon, selecting traditional Japanese designs and examining the compatibility of the available infrastructure with her people's requirements.[7]
- Racial Development Awareness: Haruna recognized that her transformation into a True Dragon was influencing the long-term development of the Yokai, accelerating their transition toward greater reliance upon Senjutsu.[7]

#### Resistances and Defensive Abilities
Haruna possesses extraordinary defensive capabilities through her former Nine-Tailed Kitsune physiology, Energy reinforcement, training within the Tower of Nightmare and subsequent True Dragon transformation. However, the available material does not individually establish every resistance commonly associated with high-ranking Dragons, Gods or Kitsune. The following entries distinguish demonstrated defensive properties from general racial expectations.
- Enhanced Physical Durability: Haruna's original Kitsune body could withstand extremely powerful physical exchanges when reinforced with Youki. Her Dragonization subsequently provided a superior physical foundation through genuine True Dragon physiology.[2][5]
- Energy-Based Defense: Haruna can reinforce her body with supernatural Energy to reduce damage from physical attacks. During her duel with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], her Youki formed a protective layer around her body.[2]
- Defensive Illusion Use: Haruna can avoid attacks through deceptive movement and illusory positioning, making it difficult for an opponent to strike her true body.[2][3]
- Enhanced Soul Endurance: Haruna's Soul survived the extreme strain of her forced tenth-tail transformation, partly because it had previously been strengthened through the Tower of Nightmare. This demonstrates considerable spiritual endurance, but the transformation nevertheless left her severely exhausted and endangered her existence.[4]
- High-Speed Evasion: Haruna's enhanced reactions allowed her to dodge an unexpected attack from Shur following her training within the Tower of Nightmare, although the attack still burned part of her tails.[4]
- External Protection against Shur's Miasma: A protective measure previously provided by Victor activated when Haruna was affected by Shur's dangerous red miasma. This defense should not be classified as unrestricted native immunity to Corruption or Existence Erasure.[4]
- Enhanced Draconic Resilience: Her transformation into a True Dragon granted a far stronger physical and supernatural foundation than her former mortal Kitsune physiology.[5]
Unconfirmed Immunities: Haruna has not been individually established as immune to all elemental attacks, unrestricted Soul Manipulation, every form of Conceptual Manipulation, Positivity, Negativity, Existence Erasure or all supernatural poisons. The fact that she possesses a Divine racial foundation does not justify automatically attributing every possible Dragon resistance to her without an independently demonstrated feat or explicit statement.

#### Equipment

##### Black Katana
Haruna's principal weapon during her life as a Nine-Tailed Kitsune was a black katana possessing a dangerous supernatural presence. The weapon served as the principal focus of her swordsmanship and the [[Otsuki Clan]]'s ritualized [[Martial Arts]], including Mugetsu and the Four Dances of the Moon. Its construction and appearance reflected her preference for traditional Japanese weapons, while its effectiveness depended heavily upon Haruna's ability to channel enormous quantities of supernatural Energy through the blade.[16][23][2]
- Katana Mastery: Haruna possesses extensive proficiency in wielding the weapon through both conventional and supernatural Japanese swordsmanship.[16][2]
- Youki Channeling: Haruna can concentrate her Youki through the blade, producing powerful sword attacks and enabling the activation of her martial techniques.[2][3]
- Lunar Technique Catalyst: The katana serves as the principal focus through which Haruna performs the sword movements required by the Four Dances of the Moon.[2][3]
- Mugetsu Catalyst: Haruna performs Mugetsu through the movement and sheathing of her katana, producing the unusual body-separation effect associated with the technique.[16]
- Elemental Technique Compatibility: Haruna can combine her swordsmanship with the various elemental affinities associated with her original Nine-Tailed state.[3]
- [[Draconic Energy]] Compatibility: Haruna's former techniques remain compatible with Draconic Energy after her transformation, although the cited material does not establish that her original katana itself underwent a separate permanent transformation into a Divine Artifact.[6]
The katana's precise craftsmanship, supernatural durability and any independent enchantments have not been fully established in the cited passages. Its observed effectiveness should therefore be distinguished from the extraordinary power and skill of Haruna herself.

##### Ikari
Ikari is the violet katana [[Victor Elderblood]] gave Haruna, and the weapon she uses as Goddess of War. Draconic and Eldritch script covers the blade. At her height in that battle the drawn length is a giant odachi. She named it herself. The word means wrath and anchor. Victor is [[Phlegethon Wound|the anchor]]: the place she can return to, and the one who can calm her.[8]
- Divine Weapon: Ikari manifests in her hand at the start of the fight with the Thrones.[8]
- Draconic and Eldritch Script: The blade carries both scripts. Drawing it is followed at once by a waver in the Thrones' clarity.[8]
- Master-Level Catalyst: The razor aura that comes off her as she draws it is the stated proof of the master level.[8]
- Temporal Barrier Cutting: The blade goes through [[Aetherys]]'s temporal barriers and the structure that binds his overlapping sequences.[14]
- Dimensional Structure Cutting: She cuts a dimensional structure with it and opens a path Kali can cross.[14]
- Divinity Conduction: She can concentrate her Divinity in the blade for a single strike instead of releasing it as a beam.[14]
Ikari is not stated to be the same object as the older black katana. The Four Dances are not shown being cast through it.[2][8]

##### Tower of Nightmare Training
Haruna underwent training within the Tower of Nightmare, an environment designed to develop the capabilities of powerful supernatural beings. The experience improved her reactions and contributed to the strengthening of her Soul, both of which became important during the confrontation with Shur.[4]
- Combat Reaction Improvement: The training improved Haruna's ability to evade unfamiliar high-speed attacks.[4]
- Soul Strengthening: The strengthening and nourishment Haruna received through the Tower helped her survive the extreme strain of the forced tenth-tail metamorphosis.[4]
The Tower is a training resource rather than an innate ability or a piece of personal equipment permanently carried by Haruna.
