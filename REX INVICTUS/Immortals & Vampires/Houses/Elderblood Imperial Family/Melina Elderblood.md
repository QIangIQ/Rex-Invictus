### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 996
Hmm, Hmm, 1 hour without Daddy is like 1000 years without Daddy!
—Melina exaggerating how much she missed [[Victor Elderblood]], Chapter 997
Melina Elderblood is a Blood Dragon Goddess, Imperial Princess of the [[Elderblood Empire]], and the biological daughter of Victor Elderblood and [[Maria Elderblood]]. She belongs to Victor's naturally born second generation of [[True Dragons]] and is one of the daughters raised within his protected personal world following the expansion of the [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]].
Melina first appears in Chapter 996 while playing with [[Evelyn Elderblood]], [[Nikolina Nia Nefela Elderblood]] and [[Julia Elderblood]]. Her introduction presents her as one of the comparatively calmer members of the group: when Evelyn accidentally bites her tongue during their role-playing game, Melina matter-of-factly declares that the game has ended rather than continuing to indulge her sister's performance.[1]
Chapter 997 formally identifies her as Melina Elderblood, the daughter of Maria and Victor. The same chapter establishes her blonde hair, her physical resemblance to Maria, her playful relationship with her sisters and—most prominently—her extreme affection for Victor. The moment Melina learns her father is nearby, her calm demeanor disappears; she rushes toward him with her sisters, competes for his embrace and claims that even a single hour away from Victor feels like a thousand years.[2]

### Physical Appearance
Melina is a young-looking blonde girl who strongly resembles her biological mother, [[Maria Elderblood]]. During her introduction, she is one of two blonde girls playing alongside Evelyn and Nikolina. Chapter 997 subsequently explains that the daughters gathered there resemble their respective mothers and even inherited the same general hair colors, with Julia being the major exception because of the influence of her own Divine power.[2]
Melina therefore possesses blonde hair inherited from Maria. Her existing appearance also depicts the violet eyes characteristic of many of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s biological descendants.
When Melina becomes excited in Victor's presence, she manifests a pair of Dragon wings matching the color of her hair. Melina, Evelyn, Nikolina and Julia collectively lower their guard while being embraced by Victor, causing their Draconic wings to emerge instinctively as they excitedly seek his attention.[2]
Her complete adult appearance and full True Dragon Form have not yet received a dedicated description in the reviewed chapters.

### Personality
Melina is calm, playful, affectionate and intensely attached to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
Her first spoken interaction establishes a relatively composed side to her personality. Evelyn and Nikolina are enthusiastically role-playing a battle between the "Champion of Light" and "Herald of Darkness", while Melina and Julia watch nearby. When Evelyn bites her tongue while attempting to pronounce Nikolina's unusually long name, Melina calmly declares that the game has ended.[1]
Evelyn immediately complains that Melina and Julia are being petty by using the mistake against her.
The exchange demonstrates that Melina enjoys teasing her sisters but does so in a comparatively understated manner. She does not need to become loud or dramatic herself to participate in their games; instead, she casually joins Julia in dismantling Evelyn's performance with a simple observation.[1]
This composure disappears almost completely whenever Victor becomes involved.
After Anna tells the girls that someone is inside the room, Evelyn initially approaches cautiously. The moment Victor is discovered, however, the atmosphere changes completely. When Melina hears that her father is present, she immediately appears at the doorway and asks whether "Daddy" is really there.[2]
Julia reaches Victor first.
Melina and Nikolina immediately protest:
"AHHH! Aline, that's not fair!"[2]
Both then rush toward Victor.
Once gathered around him, Melina exaggerates that:
"1 hour without Daddy is like 1000 years without Daddy!"[2]
The narration explicitly notes that although the wording is exaggerated, it accurately represents how Melina feels.
Like many of Victor's daughters, her emotional attachment to her father is therefore enormous. Even after spending only a few hours apart, she behaves as though the separation has been intolerably long.
The affection is so intense that Melina and her sisters instinctively relax their defenses in Victor's arms and begin manifesting and flapping their Dragon wings like excited children seeking their father's attention.[2]
Despite this dependence upon Victor emotionally, Melina was raised according to Victor's broader philosophy that his daughters should eventually develop their own stories rather than remain permanently restrained by their parents. Victor specifically decides that his role should be to support whatever choices his daughters make while continuing to protect them from the background.[1]
Melina's more mature ambitions, personal career and long-term interests have not yet been individually revealed.

### Heritage
Melina is the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Maria Elderblood]].
Maria's place within Victor's family is unusual. She originally entered the story as an Inquisition hunter and enemy of Sasha before being transformed into a defective Ghoul. Over time, she became one of Victor's most loyal servants, entered Clan Elderblood and eventually became one of his wives.
Melina was born much later, after Maria had already become fully integrated into Victor's Draconic family.

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Melina first appeared during the period following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s return to his family after approximately two thousand years of development within his internal territories.
At the time, Victor was discussing the future of his daughters and how much freedom he should allow them as they matured.
His various internal thought-processes eventually reached the conclusion that restricting them excessively would contradict the philosophy he had used with the rest of his family. His daughters should be allowed to develop their own lives, make their own decisions and create their own stories while knowing that Victor would remain available whenever they required support.[1]
The conversation also concerned [[Leon Walker]].
Despite Leon technically belonging to Victor's own Vampire lineage by this point, Victor continued acknowledging him as his father and eventually agreed to allow Leon to meet his granddaughters.
Victor nevertheless scrutinized Leon's intentions before doing so.
Only after confirming that Leon genuinely wanted to meet the girls simply as their grandfather did Victor permit the meeting.[1]
Before Melina and the others were formally called into the room, their voices could already be heard nearby.
Evelyn, Nikolina, Julia and Melina had been playing together.
Evelyn was pretending to be the Herald of Darkness fighting a Champion of Light while attempting to introduce Nikolina as her minion. Her performance ended abruptly when Nikolina's long name caused Evelyn to bite her own tongue.[1]
Julia calmly observed that the game was finished.
Melina agreed:
"The game ended when you bit your tongue, Evelyn."[1]
Evelyn complained about both blonde sisters immediately exploiting her mistake.
The next chapter formally introduced all four girls.
Evelyn was identified as the daughter of Eve and Victor.
Nikolina was identified as the daughter of Nyx and Victor.
The two blonde girls were then identified as Melina Elderblood, daughter of Maria and Victor, and [[Julia Elderblood]], daughter of Natasha and Victor.[2]
Their appearances reflected their mothers.
The narration explains that the girls generally resembled their respective mothers and possessed the same hair colors, with Julia's unusual hair representing an exception caused by her special Divine nature.[2]
Anna eventually called out to the noisy children.
All four immediately became cautious.
Evelyn slowly opened the door and initially focused upon Anna.
Then she noticed Victor.
Her fear vanished immediately.
"Daddy!"
Evelyn rushed toward him.[2]
Melina heard the word and immediately appeared at the entrance.
"Daddy?"[2]
Nikolina followed.
Julia moved fastest and reached Victor first, throwing herself into his arms.
Melina and Nikolina immediately protested that Julia had gained an unfair advantage and rushed toward him as well.[2]
Victor embraced all four daughters and asked whether they were going to abandon their game simply because he was present.
They had absolutely no hesitation.
Being with their father was more important.
Victor pointed out that he had only been absent for a few hours.
Nikolina claimed that the period felt like several years.
Melina went significantly further:
"Hmm, Hmm, 1 hour without Daddy is like 1000 years without Daddy!"[2]
Although deliberately exaggerated, the narration confirms that the statement accurately reflected Melina's feelings.
As Victor continued pampering them, Melina and her sisters completely lowered their defenses. Dragon wings corresponding to their individual hair colors appeared from their backs and began flapping excitedly.[2]
Leon watched the scene in disbelief.
Rather than resembling terrifying supernatural Princesses, the girls appeared almost like baby birds competing for their father's attention.
Anna used the scene to demonstrate what she had previously tried explaining to Leon: Victor occupied such an overwhelming place in his daughters' emotional lives that gaining their attention as grandparents would be extremely difficult while they were still young.[2]
Melina's first appearance therefore establishes her less through combat than through the family dynamic defining Victor's second generation. She is a Dragon Goddess with an extraordinary supernatural foundation, but within the Elderblood household she is still a young daughter whose greatest concern at that moment is reaching her father's arms before her sisters.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Melina's biological father and the person toward whom she has displayed her strongest emotional attachment.
Melina's introductory storyline makes this extremely explicit.
She is capable of calmly teasing Evelyn and playing normally with her sisters while Victor is absent. The moment she hears that Victor is nearby, everything else becomes secondary.[1][2]
When Julia reaches Victor before her, Melina immediately protests and races toward him.
After Victor notes that he had been away for only a few hours, Melina claims that a single hour without him feels equivalent to one thousand years.[2]
The narration confirms that the exaggeration accurately reflects her emotional state.
Victor reciprocates this affection intensely. He openly describes Melina and the others as his beloved daughters and devotes his complete attention toward pampering them while they gather around him.[2]
His protectiveness extends far beyond ordinary parental concern. By Chapter 1052, Victor has constructed numerous supernatural safeguards around every one of his daughters, including Melina, to prevent Soul invasion, mental interference, parasitic attacks and other forms of supernatural corruption.[3]
He also retains a portion of each daughter's Soul within himself, allowing them to return through him should an enemy successfully kill them.[3]

#### Maria Elderblood
[[Maria Elderblood]] is Melina's biological mother.
Chapter 997 explicitly identifies Melina as Maria and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughter and states that the daughters present resemble their respective mothers, even inheriting their hair colors.[2]
Melina's blonde hair therefore directly reflects Maria's appearance.
Unlike several of Victor's daughters whose maternal relationships receive extended scenes, Melina and Maria have not yet received a dedicated mother-daughter storyline in the reviewed material.
Consequently, Melina should not automatically be assigned Maria's former Ghoul powers, Ghoul Queen characteristics or specialized abilities until the novel confirms that those traits were individually inherited.

#### Evelyn Elderblood
[[Evelyn Elderblood]] is one of Melina's earliest demonstrated companions.
The two play together alongside Nikolina and Julia during Chapters 996–997.
Melina displays a teasing relationship with Evelyn. When Evelyn bites her tongue while role-playing, Melina calmly announces that the game is finished, immediately reinforcing Julia's criticism and causing Evelyn to complain about both sisters being petty.[1]
Their interaction appears entirely playful rather than hostile.

#### Nikolina Nia Nefela Elderblood
[[Nikolina Nia Nefela Elderblood]] plays alongside Melina during her introduction.
Both girls react similarly after Julia reaches [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] first, simultaneously protesting that the situation is unfair before rushing toward their father.[2]
This places Melina and Nikolina together as part of the small sibling group frequently playing around Victor's residence during this period.

#### Julia Elderblood
[[Julia Elderblood]] is another of Melina's earliest companions.
Both are described as the blonde members of the four-girl group present during Melina's debut.[2]
Melina and Julia jointly tease Evelyn after she ruins the role-playing game by biting her tongue.
Their temporary alliance disappears the moment [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] appears: Julia reaches him first, causing Melina and Nikolina to immediately complain that she cheated them out of the first embrace.[2]

### Skills & Abilities
Melina has not yet received an individual combat showcase or a revealed personal Divinity. The following abilities are restricted to powers directly demonstrated by Melina or explicitly stated to belong to all of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters.
- Blood Dragon Goddess Physiology: Melina is a naturally born member of Victor's Blood Dragon lineage and one of his True Dragon daughters.
- True Dragon Physiology: Chapter 1052 explicitly distinguishes Victor's daughters as [[True Dragons]] and notes that even the daughters born from unusual mothers retain their Draconic nature.[3]
- Dragon Goddess Physiology: Melina belongs to Victor's second generation of Divine Dragons and is recognized as a Dragon Goddess.
- Dragon Wings: Melina can manifest blonde Draconic wings. They appear spontaneously while she relaxes in Victor's embrace alongside Evelyn, Nikolina and Julia.[2]
- Environmental Resistance: Victor's daughters possess the natural environmental resilience of Dragons. Temperatures around -60°C cause them no discomfort during Victor's later training exercise.[3]
- Exploratory Instinct: Young Dragons are described as naturally curious and exploratory, a trait Victor actively encourages by filling his personal world with hidden locations and Dimensions.[3]
- Passive Evolution / Adaptation: Melina inherited part of Victor's adaptive ability alongside his other biological daughters.[3]
- Environmental Adaptation: Conditions that initially cause physical discomfort gradually cease to bother her as her body adapts.
- Accelerated Adaptation: Victor's daughters adapt more rapidly than ordinary Dragons because they inherited part of his Elder-God-derived adaptation.[3]
- Inferior to Victor: Their version is explicitly described as much weaker than Victor's own evolution.
- No Confirmed Conceptual Adaptation: The demonstrated shared ability is passive adaptation to harmful or uncomfortable conditions and should not be expanded into Victor's much more extreme forms of Evolution.
- Soul Invasion Immunity: Victor's daughters are explicitly stated to possess total immunity to ordinary Soul invasion.[3]
- Soul Protection: A fragment of Victor's own Soul/existence remains attached to each daughter as an additional defensive mechanism.
- Emergency Defense: Should an opponent somehow bypass the ordinary protections, the Victor fragment within them can directly respond to protect the daughter's Soul.[3]
- Mental Invasion Immunity: Melina possesses the same protections Victor installed in all of his daughters against external mental interference.[3]
- Mind Alteration Resistance: Attempts to manipulate or rewrite her mind are opposed by Victor's protections.
- Adaptive Security: Victor continuously updates the protections whenever he conceives new methods through which an enemy might bypass them.[3]
- Parasite and Foreign-Body Protection: Melina is protected against hostile parasites, viruses and other invasive substances.[3]
- Beneficial-Disguise Countermeasures: The protections also account for hostile substances designed to deceive the body into recognizing them as beneficial.
- Automatic Defense: These protections operate without requiring Melina to consciously identify the threat.
- Soul Anchor / Resurrection Through Victor: Victor retains part of Melina's Soul within himself.[3]
- Post-Mortem Restoration: If Melina were successfully killed by an enemy, the part of her Soul retained by Victor could allow her to revive through him.
- External Soul Anchor: Her complete continued existence therefore does not depend exclusively upon the body currently occupied by her.
- Limitations Unexplained: The chapter does not establish that this mechanism can overcome absolutely every possible form of conceptual or existential destruction.
- Draconic Reality Influence (Shared Racial Capacity): Victor describes his daughters as Beings capable of bending Reality through words, reflecting the supernatural nature of Draconic language and authority.[3]
- Individual Mastery Unknown: Melina has not yet personally demonstrated advanced Draconic Rune or Dragon Language techniques.
- No Automatic Rune Mastery: Her True Dragon Race establishes compatibility with Draconic language, but this article does not assign her mastery comparable to Liriel, Victor or dedicated Runemasters without a direct feat.
- Divinity (Unrevealed): Melina is a Dragon Goddess, but her personal Divine Concept has not yet been identified.
- No Confirmed Personal Authority: Neither Chapter 996 nor Chapter 997 attributes a named Concept to Melina.
- No Inherited Ghoul Divinity: Maria's history as a Ghoul Queen does not establish that Melina possesses a Ghoul-related Divinity.
- No "Limitless Growth" Confirmation: The previous claim that Melina possesses limitless growth because her Divinity originates outside the Cosmology is not supported by the reviewed chapters and has therefore been removed.
