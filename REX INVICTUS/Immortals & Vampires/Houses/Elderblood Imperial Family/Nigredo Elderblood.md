Nigredo Elderblood is a recurring character in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]], an Imperial Princess of the [[Elderblood Empire]], and the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]]. She is a naturally born Blood Dragon Goddess and one of Victor's daughters whose supernatural existence is most closely associated with the [[Eldritch Pantheon]]. Born possessing an extraordinary form of the Concept of Darkness, Nigredo is classified as an Eldritch Primordial Goddess, combining the Divine and Draconic foundation of Victor's Bloodline with a Deity directly connected to the foreign Eldritch principles that exist beyond the native Universe.[1][2]
Nigredo's Darkness differs fundamentally from conventional elemental Darkness or the manipulation of ordinary Shadows. Her Divine nature is associated with the Eldritch Darkness of a black hole in which Being and Non-Being collapse together, a supernatural phenomenon whose characteristics extend beyond the absence of visible light. The extraordinary relationship between her existence and cosmic singularities became apparent immediately after her birth, when a black hole manifested within Victor's Personal Universe. Nigredo subsequently demonstrated a personal connection to that phenomenon by affectionately greeting it as her own black hole during an Imperial family banquet.[1][3]
Although her introductory description identifies her as a naturally born Primal Goddess of Darkness with the potential for further Primordial development, her actual classification is more specifically described as Eldritch Primordial Goddess. This distinction becomes important because all of Victor's biological daughters inherit Draconic and Eldritch characteristics to varying degrees, but only certain daughters possess Divinities directly aligned with the Eldritch Pantheon. Nigredo belongs to this exceptional group, possessing a particularly concentrated form of [[Eldritch Energy]] and the capacity to manifest an overt Eldritch Form. She is subsequently recognized alongside [[Yog-Sothoth]] Elderblood as one of the two [[Outer Gods]] participating in the experiments that produced the Great Old One [[Cthugha]].[4][5][6]
Despite the extraordinary nature of her existence, Nigredo possesses one of the most lethargic personalities among Victor's daughters. She is exceptionally quiet, enjoys sleeping and generally displays little enthusiasm for activities that do not interest her. During her first appearance, she is discovered sleeping near the ceiling while the remaining Imperial Princesses gather beneath her, refusing to wake when Albedo orders her to come down. Her disobedience contrasts sharply with her immediate compliance when Anna issues the same instruction, revealing the different degrees of parental authority established by her mothers.[1]
Nigredo becomes considerably more attentive when a subject genuinely interests her, particularly when it involves Eldritch existence, cosmic phenomena or the deeper principles governing Reality. She regards cosmic horrors that disturb her sisters as beautiful, displays an unusual degree of comfort around manifestations of foreign Eldritch power and participates in the creation of supernatural organisms possessing characteristics derived from multiple [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] and Divinities. Her partnership with Yog eventually results in a series of experimental Eldritch-Leviathan hybrids capable of surviving inside black holes and contributes to the creation of Cthugha, a new Great Old One recognized by Victor as a member of the Great [[Chaos]].[7][6]
Nigredo's supernatural potential should be distinguished from the complete governing position of a native Primordial Regulator. Although she is identified as an Eldritch Primordial Goddess and possesses the nature of an Outer God, her introduction does not establish that she occupies the native Universe's exclusive office governing every manifestation of Darkness. Her confirmed powers concern her Eldritch Darkness, singularity affinity, exceptional foreign Energy, Eldritch physiology and contributions to supernatural Life creation.

### Physical Appearance
Nigredo is an exceptionally beautiful young woman with extremely pale skin described as being as pale as paper and long black hair of such unnatural darkness that it resembles the pure conception of Darkness itself. Her appearance reflects the extraordinary supernatural characteristics of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s naturally born daughters while emphasizing the unusual Divine inheritance that distinguishes her from the rest of the family. The blackness of her hair is particularly significant because it visually expresses her conceptual relationship with Eldritch Darkness rather than merely representing ordinary biological pigmentation.[1]
Her ordinary form retains a predominantly Humanoid appearance despite the abnormal nature of her Divinity. Nigredo does not require conventional physical support to remain suspended in the air and is comfortable sleeping while floating near the ceiling. During her introduction, she remains motionless above the other daughters until Albedo calls her name, at which point she lazily opens one eye, observes her mother for several seconds and turns away without descending. Her effortless suspension and ability to change orientation while asleep demonstrate the supernatural nature of her movement, although the precise mechanism responsible for her levitation is not individually identified.[1]
Nigredo's appearance possesses a striking contrast between her apparently delicate Humanoid body and the cosmic phenomena associated with her existence. Her skin is extraordinarily pale, while her hair resembles an absolute blackness beyond conventional coloration. These features complement the Darkness Divinity inherited from Victor, whose Eldritch properties are associated with singularities and the collapse of fundamental distinctions between Being and Non-Being. Although Nigredo's ordinary eye color has not been individually described, her supernatural nature and appearance clearly distinguish her from ordinary Divine beings.[1]
Nigredo can also manifest a substantially more disturbing Eldritch Form. When she appears alongside Yog during their experiments with supernatural organisms, both sisters possess bodies that are grotesquely distorted while retaining a generally Humanoid outline. Numerous eyes cover their forms and stare in different directions, producing an appearance that would be deeply disturbing to ordinary observers. Their transformation reflects the foreign Eldritch characteristics inherited from Victor, whose daughters possess the potential to manifest cosmic-horror forms even when they normally appear Human or Draconic.[6]
The appearance of Nigredo's Eldritch Form is particularly significant because not every member of the second generation has learned to access such a transformation. Although virtually all of Victor's biological daughters possess Eldritch characteristics and the corresponding developmental potential, only certain individuals can manifest their forms deliberately. Nigredo belongs to those who have already achieved that capacity, placing her alongside Yog among the daughters whose foreign cosmic inheritance is most visibly developed.[6]
The grotesque characteristics of Nigredo's Eldritch Form do not prevent her sisters from recognizing her or interacting with her normally. [[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]], who encounters Nigredo and Yog in their transformed states, remains composed despite recognizing that their appearance would be terrifying to ordinary people. Velina's own Eldritch inheritance allows her to understand their nature, although she has not yet developed the same ability to access her individual Eldritch Form. The contrast reflects the differences in supernatural maturity and specialization among Victor's biological daughters.[6]
Nigredo's complete natural True Dragon Form has not been individually described. Her confirmed appearances therefore consist primarily of her pale-skinned Humanoid form, exceptionally black hair and grotesque multi-eyed Eldritch Form. Her exact height, weight, ordinary eye color and distinctive anatomical characteristics in her full Draconic state remain unidentified.

### Personality
Nigredo is lethargic, quiet, soft-spoken and exceptionally fond of sleeping. Her ordinary demeanor is considerably more subdued than that of many of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters, particularly those who compete openly for their father's attention or frequently become involved in impulsive activities. She generally prefers remaining comfortable rather than participating in unnecessary conversation, and her lack of interest can make her appear almost indifferent to events occurring around her. Nevertheless, her apparent laziness does not represent an absence of intelligence, supernatural awareness or affection for her family.[1][4]
Her introductory behavior establishes the unusual extent of her lethargy. While more than thirty-five of Victor's daughters gather for a traditional family reunion, Nigredo remains asleep near the ceiling and makes no effort to join them. When Albedo eventually notices that her daughter is missing and asks where she has gone, the other girls simply point upward. Nigredo responds to her biological mother's command by opening one eye, staring at her briefly and rotating away before returning to sleep. Her refusal is immediate and completely unembarrassed, demonstrating that she does not regard every parental instruction as something requiring her attention.[1]
However, Nigredo's reaction changes entirely when [[Anna Elderblood]] enters the room and calmly orders her to come down. She immediately answers "Yes, Mother" and obeys without resistance, leaving Albedo irritated by the obvious difference in their authority. Anna points out that Albedo's habit of spending nearly all her time in laboratories has prevented her from establishing the same relationship with Nigredo that the other mothers possess with their daughters. Although Nigredo does not openly challenge the explanation, the incident reveals that her obedience depends partly upon the authority and familiarity she associates with the individual giving the instruction.[1]
Nigredo is not hostile toward her biological mother, but her willingness to disregard Albedo reflects a comparatively distant relationship produced by the Witch Goddess's prolonged isolation. Albedo has devoted enormous periods to research, frequently placing scientific investigation above ordinary family activities. In contrast, Anna and several other mothers maintain direct involvement in the daughters' education and daily lives. Nigredo consequently responds more readily to Anna's instructions, although she accepts the reminder that all members of the younger generation are expected to respect their mothers regardless of their individual differences.[1][2]
Her lethargy does not prevent her from becoming interested in extraordinary supernatural phenomena. When Victor creates an artificial Eldritch galaxy populated by enormous cosmic horrors, most of the daughters react with alarm and instinctively retreat. Nigredo and Yog behave differently, observing the creatures with fascination rather than fear. Yog describes them as beautiful, and Nigredo immediately agrees. Their reaction reflects the compatibility between their Divinities and the foreign Eldritch characteristics of the creatures, demonstrating that phenomena considered grotesque or terrifying by ordinary beings are entirely natural to them.[7][4]
Nigredo also possesses a strong appreciation for the cosmic phenomena associated with her own Concept. During the family's celestial banquet, she affectionately greets the black hole created at her birth and subsequently selects a chair formed from a similar phenomenon. Her choices reflect a comfortable familiarity with singularities rather than an attempt to intimidate the other guests. While several sisters prefer furniture created from stars, nebulae or other visually attractive astronomical objects, Nigredo naturally gravitates toward the cosmic structures most closely associated with her Darkness Divinity.[3]
Although Nigredo rarely displays strong emotions, she becomes visibly annoyed when her contributions are ignored. During the experiments involving Eldritch-Leviathan hybrids, Yog initially refers to the creatures as her own creations. Nigredo responds by crossing her arms and silently expressing dissatisfaction, forcing Yog to correct the statement and acknowledge that the organisms belong to both sisters. Once properly recognized, Nigredo becomes visibly pleased and proudly explains that her contribution allows the fish to survive inside black holes. The interaction reveals a desire for recognition and personal accomplishment beneath her normally indifferent exterior.[6]
Nigredo also possesses considerable curiosity concerning supernatural principles that interest her. During Victor's lessons about Energy, Divinity and Reality, she becomes attentive despite her usual tendency to sleep. When the discussion turns toward Moira's abnormal Luck, Nigredo quietly raises her hand and asks what her youngest sister's power actually is. Her voice is so soft that an ordinary listener would barely hear it, but Victor immediately recognizes her question and responds gently. The exchange demonstrates that Nigredo is capable of serious intellectual engagement when confronted with an unfamiliar supernatural phenomenon.[8]
Her relationship with Victor is particularly effective in motivating her. During an earlier lesson, Victor expresses concern that several daughters are neglecting their studies despite possessing extraordinary intellectual potential. He affectionately strokes Nigredo's head alongside Priscillia's and reminds them that wasting their abilities would make him sad. The statement immediately motivates the girls to promise that they will study more seriously, while Anna privately observes that Victor has accomplished through a few affectionate words what the mothers have struggled to achieve through ordinary instruction. Nigredo's response confirms that her lethargy does not diminish her affection for Victor or her desire to receive his approval.[4]
Nigredo's behavior therefore combines profound laziness with exceptional supernatural curiosity, unusual emotional restraint and a strong affinity for Eldritch existence. She does not constantly seek conflict or display the intense possessiveness associated with certain sisters, but she becomes enthusiastic when experimenting with the principles governing cosmic Life, Darkness and foreign Reality. Her participation in the creation of new Eldritch organisms demonstrates that her intellectual interests can produce extraordinary achievements whenever she finds an activity sufficiently entertaining to overcome her natural preference for sleeping.

### Nature and Divinity

#### Eldritch Primordial Goddess
Nigredo possesses an extraordinary Divine nature that distinguishes her from conventional Gods of Darkness. She was born inheriting the Concept of Darkness from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], whose own supernatural existence had developed far beyond the limitations of the native Divine hierarchy. Her introductory description identifies her as a Primal Goddess of Darkness possessing the potential for further Primordial development, while later dialogue provides the more specific classification Eldritch Primordial Goddess. The distinction reflects the foreign conceptual foundation of her Divinity rather than merely its destructive potential or the amount of Energy she possesses.[1][2]
Nigredo's nature differs from that of a fully personified native Concept such as [[Kassandra Kashvi Elderblood]], who was born as the incarnation of Destruction itself. Nigredo possesses an exceptionally advanced Divine relationship with Darkness, but her introductory characterization does not establish that she has already become the native Universe's completed Primordial Regulator of that Concept. Instead, her supernatural inheritance combines primordial potential with Eldritch characteristics, producing a form of Darkness whose behavior does not correspond entirely to the ordinary Rules of the native Universe.[1]
The distinction becomes particularly important when the wives discuss the unusual powers emerging among Victor's daughters. Agnes describes Nigredo as an example of a naturally born Primordial Goddess, but Sasha immediately corrects the classification to Eldritch Primordial Goddess. Jeanne subsequently explains that all of Victor's biological daughters possess varying combinations of Eldritch and Primordial characteristics, although only a comparatively small number have developed Divinities belonging directly to the [[Eldritch Pantheon]]. Nigredo belongs to this exceptional group, possessing a Deity whose nature is fundamentally connected to the foreign Eldritch framework associated with Victor.[2]
Her status consequently represents more than ordinary compatibility with [[Eldritch Energy]]. The foreign characteristics of her Darkness are incorporated into her Divine foundation, allowing her to interact naturally with manifestations of cosmic horror that other daughters instinctively find disturbing. Her ability to access an Eldritch Form and contribute specialized properties to newly created Eldritch Life further demonstrates that her connection is an active component of her supernatural existence rather than a dormant inheritance.[4][6]

#### Outer God Status
Nigredo's connection to the [[Outer Gods]] becomes increasingly explicit as her Eldritch abilities develop. During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s lessons, he distinguishes the foreign Energy possessed by his daughters from the Energy native to their Universe and notes that certain daughters possess substantially higher concentrations than others. He directs particular attention toward Yog and Nigredo, recognizing them as examples of the second generation whose powers are especially closely aligned with the Eldritch side of his existence.[5]
Her status is subsequently reinforced during the experiments that produce [[Cthugha]]. Velina encounters Yog and Nigredo in their grotesque Eldritch Forms alongside Leviathans and other supernatural organisms, recognizing the dangers associated with combining the influence of two Outer Gods with creatures possessing fundamentally different supernatural characteristics. The two Outer Gods involved are Yog and Nigredo, whose experimental contributions provide the Eldritch foundation for the developing organism.[6]
Victor later explains the broader hierarchy of the Great [[Chaos]]. He and [[Azathoth Elderblood]] occupy the highest position as Creators, followed by the Outer Gods or Eldritch Gods associated with their direct lineage and creations. Beneath them are the Great Old Ones, Divine Eldritch beings created through the powers of the superior entities, while lesser organisms without Divinity are classified as Servants of the Outer Gods. Nigredo's position belongs to the Outer God tier, while Cthugha, whose creation she assists, occupies the subordinate Great Old One category.[6]
This classification should not be interpreted as confirmation that Nigredo possesses the complete powers of Victor, Azathoth or every other Outer God. Her individually established capabilities remain those demonstrated through her Eldritch Darkness, singularity affinity, supernatural physiology and creation of Eldritch Life. Likewise, the title of Eldritch Primordial Goddess does not independently establish that she governs every manifestation of native Darkness throughout the Universe or occupies the same position as its established [[Regulators of Existence]].

#### Concept of Darkness
Nigredo inherited the Concept of Darkness from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], but her manifestation differs fundamentally from conventional Darkness manipulation. Rather than merely controlling the absence of light, concealing objects within Shadows or producing dark-colored Energy, her Divinity expresses the Eldritch Darkness of a cosmic singularity. The Concept is associated with a black hole in which Being and Non-Being collapse together, describing a supernatural condition that involves the fundamental nature of existence rather than ordinary optical darkness.[1]
This existential characteristic is especially important because it distinguishes Nigredo from beings whose powers are associated with the physical properties of black holes alone. Her Darkness possesses a conceptual aspect connected to the collapse of ordinary distinctions between existence and nonexistence, while its Eldritch foundation reflects supernatural principles foreign to the native Universe. However, the complete offensive applications of this property have not been individually demonstrated through combat, so it should not automatically be treated as unrestricted Existence Erasure, universal-scale annihilation or mastery over every form of Nonexistence.[1]
Nigredo's Eldritch Darkness also differs from ordinary physical singularities because it forms part of a Divine existence whose supernatural powers can be transferred into other organisms. During the Eldritch-Life experiments, she contributes characteristics associated with her Concept to the developing hybrid creatures, granting them the ability to survive inside black holes. This accomplishment demonstrates that her relationship with singularities can influence other forms of Life rather than remaining restricted to her own body or the black hole formed at her birth.[6]
The full relationship between Nigredo's Darkness and the established native Concepts associated with Darkness, Void and other existential states remains incompletely explained. Her confirmed Divinity is Eldritch Darkness, and its supernatural properties are described through singularities and the collapse of Being and Non-Being. These statements establish an unusual conceptual foundation without proving that she possesses every ability associated with those separate principles.

#### The Black Hole of Her Birth
Nigredo's birth was associated with the manifestation of a black hole inside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Personal Universe. Her introductory narration identifies the singularity as a consequence of her extraordinary Darkness and suggests that its creation resulted from the unusual nature of the Concept she inherited. The connection is subsequently confirmed when Nigredo encounters the phenomenon again and affectionately greets it as "my black hole", establishing that the singularity genuinely exists within the family's cosmic environment.[1][3]
The existence of a black hole created through Nigredo's birth demonstrates how profoundly her conceptual inheritance can affect her surroundings. The phenomenon is not merely an ordinary object she happens to enjoy; it is directly connected to the circumstances of her existence and the extraordinary Darkness she possesses. Her familiarity with singularities becomes even more apparent when Victor constructs a celestial banquet using stars, nebulae, planets and other astronomical structures as furniture. Rather than selecting one of the more conventional seats, Nigredo immediately chooses a chair composed of a black hole, displaying complete comfort with an environment ordinarily considered catastrophically dangerous.[3]
Her later experiments further establish the practical significance of this affinity. The Eldritch fish developed by Nigredo and Yog possess supernatural characteristics derived from multiple [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] and Divinities, including the capacity to survive inside black holes through Nigredo's contribution. The feat demonstrates that her conceptual relationship with singularities can be incorporated into the biological or supernatural structure of created Life, although it does not independently establish that she can produce unlimited black holes at will or control every singularity throughout the Universe.[6]

### Synopsis

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Nigredo was formally introduced during an enormous gathering of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s biological daughters held shortly before a traditional family dinner. The reunion began as various Imperial Princesses arrived to discuss an incident involving Velina, gradually attracting more than thirty-five daughters alongside several members of the older generation. The gathering became increasingly lively as sisters from different maternal lineages joined the conversation, with [[Nero]], Ophis, [[Metis]], Akasha and Melida eventually appearing among the assembled family members.[1]
[[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]] arrived soon afterward, appearing exhausted despite possessing the supernatural constitution of a Dragon Goddess. Her attention initially turned toward Melida, whose extraordinary compatibility with Primal Energy and Victor's Soul made her naturally talented in fields Albedo continued studying through extensive research. The comparison reminded Albedo of the enormous potential possessed by Victor's descendants, but it also prompted her to remember her own daughter. When she looked around the gathering and failed to find Nigredo, she asked the other girls where the child had gone. They answered by silently pointing toward the ceiling, where Nigredo was sleeping while suspended in the air.[1]
Nigredo's appearance immediately revealed the unusual nature of her inheritance. She possessed paper-pale skin and black hair resembling the pure conception of Darkness itself, while her Divine existence was associated with a form of Eldritch Darkness comparable to a cosmic singularity. Her Darkness involved a black-hole-like phenomenon in which Being and Non-Being collapsed together, and the extraordinary circumstances of her birth had produced a corresponding black hole within Victor's Personal Universe. Despite possessing such overwhelming supernatural characteristics, Nigredo appeared completely unconcerned with the surrounding family gathering and continued sleeping without any intention of joining the others.[1]
Albedo ordered her daughter to wake up, but Nigredo merely opened one eye, looked at her mother for several seconds and rotated away before returning to sleep. Her refusal left Albedo speechless, particularly because she had expected her daughter to respond more seriously to a direct parental command. The situation changed when Anna entered alongside [[Aphrodite]] and Kaguya. Anna calmly ordered Nigredo to come down, and the girl immediately complied, addressing her as Mother without any of the resistance she had displayed toward Albedo.[1]
Albedo became irritated by the obvious difference in their authority, while Anna pointed out that her prolonged isolation in laboratories had prevented her from developing the same parental relationship maintained by the other mothers. Anna reminded Nigredo and the assembled daughters that their exceptional powers did not excuse disrespect toward their parents, and the girls accepted the instruction. [[Scathach]] and [[Dún Scáith|Dun Scaith]] subsequently arrived, immediately causing the younger generation to become nervous because of their experiences with Scathach's demanding training. Victor eventually joined the gathering and defended the importance of discipline, recognizing that daughters born with the ability to distort Reality could not safely mature without sufficient education, restraint and practical experience.[1]
Nigredo later participated in Victor's educational gathering concerning dimensional structures, Divine Concepts and the deeper nature of Reality. The lesson began when Norabell approached her father for assistance after repeatedly failing to create a stable dimension. Victor recognized that Norabell had unconsciously been attempting to produce an independent Reality instead of an ordinary subordinate dimension, prompting him to demonstrate the difference between structures supported by existing universal Rules and more complicated existences possessing higher conceptual foundations.[7]
Victor created a simple artificial dimension alongside a miniature galaxy formed through Negativity, Beginning and Eldritch powers. Although the galaxy appeared to contain ordinary astronomical phenomena, closer observation revealed enormous cosmic horrors resembling stars. Most of the assembled daughters instinctively recoiled when they examined the beings through their Divine senses, disturbed by the abnormal appearance and supernatural nature of the creatures. Nigredo and Yog were the exceptions, remaining entirely comfortable while observing the supposed stars. Yog described the creatures as beautiful, and Nigredo agreed without hesitation.[7]
Their reaction reflected the extraordinary nature of their inherited Divinities. Yog possessed a fully developed Eldritch Divine foundation through Victor and Azathoth, while Nigredo's Darkness Deity was also strongly connected to the Eldritch. Consequently, the foreign cosmic organisms did not provoke the same instinctive rejection experienced by sisters whose Eldritch inheritance remained less developed. Akasha was another daughter with an exceptional connection to Victor's Eldritch Divinity, although the immediate contrast during the galaxy demonstration centered upon Yog and Nigredo.[4]
Victor continued the lesson by explaining that the miniature galaxy was not a genuinely independent cosmic system. Its appearance, Energy and apparent inhabitants were extraordinarily convincing, but the structure lacked the complete conceptual foundation necessary to support independently existing Life. To demonstrate the distinction, he summoned genuine Eldritch beings, including [[Abhoth]], and instructed his daughters to compare them with the organisms created inside the artificial galaxy. The exercise revealed that reproducing the appearance of a cosmic structure was fundamentally different from establishing the Rules necessary for a genuine independent Reality.[4]
The gathering subsequently turned toward the nature of [[True Dragons]] and the extraordinary intellectual potential inherited by Victor's daughters. Victor explained that their Draconic lineage was exceptionally compatible with different forms of existence and that its predatory characteristics allowed supernatural inheritances to be assimilated rather than remaining incompatible. However, he became concerned when several daughters demonstrated insufficient knowledge of subjects that their education should already have covered. The revelation prompted him to criticize their laziness, particularly because their extraordinarily powerful minds allowed them to absorb complicated information with very little effort.[4]
Victor affectionately stroked Nigredo's head alongside Priscillia's and reminded them that wasting their natural potential would disappoint him. His words immediately motivated the girls to promise that they would study more seriously, with the surrounding daughters joining the declaration. Anna privately observed that Victor had managed to inspire even Nigredo and Priscillia, two of the family's most notoriously lazy daughters, through a few affectionate words. The incident demonstrated that Nigredo's lethargy could be overcome by the desire to make her father proud, particularly when he expressed genuine concern for her development.[4]
Nigredo subsequently joined the family during an extraordinary celestial banquet organized by Victor. The event began as an ordinary gathering intended to preserve the traditional communal meals shared by the household, but Victor transformed the surrounding environment into a cosmic celebration. He transported the family to their personal superplanet and manipulated nearby astronomical phenomena to create an appropriate setting, eventually producing furniture and ceremonial clothing from stars, nebulae, supernovae and other celestial structures. The extraordinary scale of the event reflected the overwhelming power possessed by the Emperor, although its underlying purpose remained the simple enjoyment of spending time with his family.[3]
Upon arriving at the superplanet, Nigredo immediately greeted the black hole created at her birth, affectionately addressing the singularity as her own. When Victor subsequently invited everyone to choose their seats, Nigredo selected a chair formed from a black hole, while other daughters chose furniture composed of stars and nebulae according to their individual preferences. Her natural comfort around singularities contrasted with the reactions ordinarily expected from beings confronted with such dangerous astronomical phenomena, reinforcing the intimate relationship between her supernatural existence and the Eldritch Darkness she inherited.[3]
The family's later discussions concerning Victor's increasingly abnormal descendants provided further clarification of Nigredo's status. As the wives speculated about the powers that might be inherited by Victor's future daughter with [[Tyche]], Agnes suggested that extraordinarily unusual children generally emerged from mothers possessing exceptional Divine characteristics. Sasha immediately reminded her of Nigredo, whose extraordinary Divinity had originated from the combined inheritance of Victor and Albedo. When Agnes described Nigredo as a naturally born Primordial Goddess, Sasha corrected the classification to Eldritch Primordial Goddess.[2]
Jeanne expanded upon the distinction by explaining that Victor's daughters possessed both Eldritch and Primordial characteristics to varying degrees, but only certain individuals had developed Divinities directly associated with the [[Eldritch Pantheon]]. Nigredo belonged to this smaller group of exceptionally unusual daughters, whose supernatural powers were not merely ordinary Divine abilities supplemented by dormant Eldritch ancestry. The discussion also reflected the importance of monitoring the daughters' developing powers, since extraordinary conceptual abilities could emerge through natural growth without following the conventional progression experienced by older Gods.[2]
The wives subsequently turned their attention toward Albedo's increasingly isolated lifestyle. Although Ruby, Velnorah and Aline were also dedicated researchers, they deliberately made time for their children and other family responsibilities. Albedo had instead spent enormous periods working alone, contributing to the absence of parental authority demonstrated during Nigredo's introduction. Anna and the other wives eventually concluded that Albedo needed corrective intervention, recognizing that the problem extended beyond one disobedient daughter and reflected a broader failure to balance research with family life.[2]
Their intervention resulted in Scathach confronting Albedo and dragging her into a reinforced arena for training. Although Albedo possessed the physical foundation of a Dragon Goddess, her excessive reliance upon spells and magical equipment had allowed her martial abilities to deteriorate. Victor and Scathach criticized her failure to develop the close-combat skills necessary to survive battles against exceptionally powerful opponents, explaining that high-ranking beings would not allow a mage to remain at a comfortable distance while preparing complicated techniques. Her continued dependence upon an enchanted sword as a magical focus further demonstrated that she had neglected the fundamental advantages of her Draconic physiology.[9][10]
During the confrontation, Victor emphasized that even younger members of the second generation had surpassed Albedo in relevant combat development. Scathach specifically invoked Nigredo as an example, stating that the daughter had already become capable of defeating her mother despite her comparatively young age. The comparison was particularly humiliating for Albedo because Nigredo was notorious for her laziness, yet had nevertheless developed enough practical strength to be cited by one of the family's greatest martial instructors. The statement establishes an important assessment of Nigredo's combat ability, although no complete individual battle between mother and daughter is depicted.[10]
Albedo subsequently underwent an intensive training program beneath Scathach, with Victor threatening to join the instruction personally if she failed to correct her habits. The intervention was intended to restore the martial foundation expected of a Dragon Goddess and encourage Albedo to take her responsibilities toward the family more seriously. Nigredo's earlier disobedience remained one of the clearest examples of the problems produced by her mother's isolation, while the comparison of their strength demonstrated that the daughter had continued developing even when her mother's personal combat skills had stagnated.[9][10]
Albedo later visited [[Evie Moriarty Elderblood]] on the Planet of Witches during the Empire's great merger. Although Evie ruled an entire world and possessed substantial magical power, Albedo initially struggled to appreciate the significance of her daughter's accomplishments. She had become so accustomed to Nigredo's overwhelming strength that her perception of ordinary supernatural power had become distorted, causing even the abilities of a powerful Witch Queen to seem comparatively unimpressive. Albedo recognized that her isolation within the Imperial household had undermined her understanding of the difference between naturally born Dragon Goddesses and the extraordinary but more conventional supernatural beings governing other civilizations.[11]
Nigredo later attended another gathering in which Victor adopted the role of an instructor and explained the principles governing Energy, Divinity and Reality. He described [[Eldritch Energy]] as a foreign force originating outside the native Universe, comparable to the attributeless Energy of another cosmological system. Because it does not belong to the native Reality, its expression can introduce corruption and alterations that follow foreign supernatural principles rather than the Rules ordinarily governing the surrounding environment.[5]
Victor explained that all of his daughters possessed Eldritch Energy to varying degrees, but certain individuals had inherited substantially stronger concentrations. He directed particular attention toward Yog and Nigredo, acknowledging the unusually advanced Eldritch nature of their existence. The comparison reinforced Nigredo's earlier classification as an Eldritch Primordial Goddess and established that her compatibility with foreign Eldritch phenomena was not simply a matter of personal preference or emotional indifference toward cosmic horrors.[5]
Nigredo remained attentive as the lesson continued into a discussion concerning the exceptional Luck possessed by [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]]. She softly raised her hand and asked Victor what her youngest sister's power actually was, speaking so quietly that an ordinary listener would barely have heard her. Victor nevertheless immediately recognized her voice and answered her question, explaining that Moira possessed Eldritch Luck while the extraordinary favor she received from the Universe appeared to be a separate passive blessing.[8]
Victor clarified that the Universe's apparent affection for Moira operated according to her sincere desires rather than requiring deliberate activation of a conventional supernatural technique. The precise limits of this favor remained uncertain, particularly because Moira was still a newborn and the family had not established how Reality might respond if she became frightened, upset or hostile toward another individual. Nigredo's question therefore prompted an important explanation concerning the distinction between an innate Eldritch ability and a potentially independent blessing granted through the native Universe.[8]
Nigredo's most significant demonstrated contribution to the development of Eldritch Life occurred during the experiments that eventually produced [[Cthugha]]. [[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]] was returning from her research when she encountered Nigredo and Yog beside a newly established river near the Imperial residence. Both sisters were using their grotesque Eldritch Forms, their distorted Humanoid bodies covered with numerous eyes staring in different directions. Their appearance would have been terrifying to ordinary people, but Velina remained composed because of her own inherited Eldritch characteristics and familiarity with the unusual powers possessed by her family.[6]
The scene became increasingly suspicious when Velina noticed that the river contained Leviathans and numerous small Eldritch organisms. She was certain that the river had not previously existed in that location, but Yog initially attempted to dismiss the matter as an ordinary conversation. Shortly afterward, Valentina, Leone, Hinata, Silvia, Rosemary and Stella arrived carrying additional supernatural creatures, revealing that the sisters were preparing a complicated experiment involving organisms whose characteristics originated from fundamentally different supernatural systems.[6]
Velina became especially concerned when she discovered that the girls intended to use one of Victor's scales as part of their experiment. The scale possessed extraordinary supernatural properties derived from their father's Draconic existence, making it a dangerous component to combine casually with the influences of two [[Outer Gods]], Leviathans and other foreign Eldritch beings. She initially attempted to discourage the experiment, but Stella reasoned that Victor was probably observing them and would intervene if they were about to cause a genuine disaster. Velina accepted the argument almost immediately, demonstrating that the daughters possessed extraordinary confidence in their father's protection even when dealing with unknown forms of Life.[6]
The river was already populated by experimental Eldritch fish produced through the combined efforts of Yog and Nigredo. The organisms incorporated characteristics associated with Leviathans and foreign Eldritch beings, creating hybrid Life capable of functioning under unusual supernatural conditions. Yog initially described them as her creations, but Nigredo became visibly annoyed and silently demanded recognition. Yog corrected herself and acknowledged that they had developed the fish together, allowing Nigredo to explain the characteristics contributed through her own powers.[6]
The hybrid fish possessed unusual spatial capabilities and could survive in environments ordinarily inaccessible to living organisms. Nigredo specifically emphasized that her contribution allowed them to survive inside black holes, demonstrating that the supernatural properties of her Darkness could be incorporated into the structure of another living being. Leone added a further adaptive characteristic allowing the creatures to exist under a much broader range of conditions, while Yog's Eldritch abilities formed part of the foundation supporting their unusual spatial characteristics.[6]
The sisters subsequently decided that the fish were not sufficiently dangerous and began proposing additional modifications. Valentina suggested incorporating Dragon Fire, Silvia offered Dragon Lightning, Rosemary contributed Ice, Hinata supplied the power of the Sun and Stella added her authority associated with Truth. Velina eventually joined the experiment by contributing intelligence, abandoning her earlier opposition once she recognized that the resulting creature might produce useful information concerning the interaction of different supernatural systems.[6]
Their combined powers, together with Victor's scale and the existing Eldritch-Leviathan foundation, produced an increasingly unstable mixture of foreign and native supernatural characteristics. Aphrodite arrived and recognized the danger, angrily demanding to know what the daughters were creating. The girls immediately gathered their experimental materials and fled, leaving behind the developing organism as the surrounding water began boiling. Victor appeared and reassured Aphrodite that he had been observing the experiment throughout its development and would have intervened if a genuinely dangerous situation had emerged.[6]
The experiment eventually produced Cthugha, a new Eldritch entity whose body manifested as an enormous sphere of plasma covered in eyes. Victor recognized the creature as a Great Old One rather than a true Outer God, explaining that only he and Azathoth possessed the necessary authority to create Eldritch Gods of the higher category. Cthugha nevertheless possessed genuine Divinity and combined characteristics originating from two different cosmological systems, making its existence an extraordinary example of supernatural hybridization.[6]
Cthugha inherited powers associated with stars and plasma alongside the foreign Eldritch characteristics supplied by the experimental organisms. Its existence also established it as the progenitor of a new Race of Fire Vampires, an unusual development because Fire ordinarily represented one of the principal weaknesses of conventional Noble Vampires outside the Snow lineage. Even as a newborn, Cthugha possessed enough natural power to destroy a Solar System, demonstrating the extraordinary consequences of combining the daughters' abilities with the supernatural foundation supplied by Victor's scale.[6]
Nigredo's contribution to the experiment was particularly important because the hybrid organisms already possessed the black-hole survival property she had bestowed before the sisters began adding their additional powers. Her involvement consequently formed part of the supernatural foundation from which Cthugha emerged, making her one of the co-creators of a genuine Divine Eldritch being. However, Cthugha's Solar System-level destructive potential should not automatically be attributed to Nigredo as an individual combat feat, since the creature resulted from the combined contributions of numerous exceptionally powerful daughters, multiple supernatural organisms and Victor's scale.[6]
Victor praised the daughters for their creativity and instructed them to conduct future experiments in locations farther from the family residence. Velina recognized that the creation of Cthugha might also provide important information concerning the Empire's attempts to understand and circumvent certain conceptual restrictions imposed by the native Universe. The existence of a hybrid Divine being incorporating characteristics from different cosmological systems demonstrated that some supernatural combinations could develop without provoking an immediate defensive response from Reality, even though the implications remained incomplete.[6]
Nigredo's participation in these experiments revealed a substantially more active side of her personality than her earlier appearances suggested. Although she ordinarily preferred sleeping and avoided unnecessary activity, she was willing to experiment with advanced Eldritch principles, cooperate with her sisters and develop genuinely new forms of supernatural Life. Her ability to transfer black-hole survival properties into another organism also provided one of the clearest practical demonstrations of the capabilities associated with her Eldritch Darkness Divinity.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Nigredo's biological father and the principal source of her Draconic, Divine and Eldritch inheritance. Through Victor, she possesses the advanced foundation of the Blood Dragon Race and an extraordinary connection to the Concept of Darkness, whose unusual manifestation reflects the foreign cosmic characteristics associated with his Eldritch existence. Her natural abilities make her one of the most unusual daughters within the Imperial Family, but Victor continues treating her with the same affection and concern he displays toward his other children.[1][4]
Nigredo's relationship with Victor is particularly important because of his ability to motivate her. Although she is notoriously lazy and frequently ignores ordinary activities, she becomes attentive when he expresses genuine concern for her development. During one of his lessons, Victor gently strokes her head and reminds her that her extraordinary intellect should not be wasted, explaining that seeing her neglect her potential would make him sad. Nigredo responds alongside her sisters by promising to study more seriously, demonstrating that her desire for Victor's approval can overcome her natural lethargy far more effectively than ordinary parental reprimands.[4]
Victor also supports Nigredo's unusual interests, allowing her to explore the supernatural principles associated with Eldritch existence while maintaining oversight of potentially dangerous experiments. His decision not to interrupt the creation of [[Cthugha]] reflects his confidence that the daughters can develop through experimentation when appropriate safeguards exist. Although [[Aphrodite]] objects to the risks involved, Victor explains that he has monitored the process and would have intervened if an actual disaster had become imminent. His response demonstrates an educational approach that encourages curiosity and creativity without entirely abandoning the responsibility to protect his family and the surrounding civilization.[6]
Nigredo's affection is also reflected in her participation during his educational gatherings. Despite speaking so softly that an ordinary listener would struggle to hear her, she raises questions when Victor discusses subjects that interest her, and he responds immediately with warmth and attention. Their relationship consequently combines the immense supernatural inheritance of an Outer God with the comparatively ordinary emotional bond between a father and his daughter.[8]

[[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]] is Nigredo's biological mother and the individual through whom she inherits a connection to the Moriarty family. Albedo becomes irritated by the obvious difference in their authority, while Anna explains that her excessive devotion to laboratory work has prevented her from establishing the same parental relationship maintained by the other mothers. The incident demonstrates a weakness in Albedo's family life rather than a complete absence of affection between mother and daughter.[1]
Albedo has also become accustomed to the extraordinary power possessed by Nigredo, causing her perception of ordinary supernatural strength to become distorted. When visiting Evie on the Planet of Witches, she initially struggles to appreciate the significance of her older daughter's accomplishments because Nigredo's natural abilities have become an everyday standard within the Imperial household. Albedo eventually recognizes that she needs greater experience outside the family to understand how exceptional her naturally born Dragon Goddess daughter truly is.[11]
Their relationship also becomes relevant when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and [[Scathach]] confront Albedo over her deteriorating combat skills. Nigredo's own development is cited as evidence that the Witch Goddess has neglected the martial foundation expected of a Dragon, with Scathach considering Nigredo capable of defeating her mother despite her younger age. Albedo consequently undergoes intensive corrective training intended to restore her practical abilities and encourage greater participation in family life. Although Nigredo does not personally fight her mother during this incident, the comparison establishes the extraordinary progress she has achieved.[10]

#### Evie Moriarty Elderblood
[[Evie Moriarty Elderblood]] is Nigredo's older maternal half-sister through Albedo. Unlike Nigredo, who was born directly into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood Dragon lineage, Evie's development occurred through the supernatural traditions of the Moriarty family and the Witch civilization associated with Arcane. She eventually became a powerful Witch Queen and ruler of the Planet of Witches, possessing substantial accomplishments and authority within her own world.
Their relationship is significant primarily through Albedo's perception of their different levels of power. Because Albedo has become accustomed to Nigredo's extraordinary abilities, she initially struggles to recognize how exceptional Evie's accomplishments are when compared with those of ordinary supernatural beings. The comparison demonstrates the immense gap between the naturally born daughters of Victor and individuals who developed through more conventional Divine and magical systems, although it does not diminish Evie's achievements within her own civilization.[11]

#### Cthugha
[[Cthugha]] is a Great Old One created through the combined experimental powers of Nigredo, Yog and several other daughters of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Although Nigredo is not the creature's sole creator, her participation in the development of the original Eldritch-Leviathan hybrids provides an important part of the supernatural foundation from which Cthugha emerges. Her contribution allows the experimental organisms to survive inside black holes, while other sisters subsequently add elemental, conceptual, adaptive and intellectual characteristics.[6]
The completed creature possesses genuine Divinity and an unusual combination of supernatural principles originating from the native Universe and the foreign Eldritch framework. Victor recognizes Cthugha as a Great Old One rather than an Outer God, establishing its position within the hierarchy of the Great [[Chaos]]. Its existence also represents an important example of the ability of Victor's daughters to create genuinely new forms of Divine Eldritch Life through cooperation, with Nigredo participating as one of the original contributors.[6]

### Trivia
- Nigredo is the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]], making her a naturally born Blood Dragon Goddess with an exceptionally advanced Eldritch inheritance.[1]
- Her name follows the terminology associated with the alchemical Magnum Opus. Nigredo represents blackness, while Albedo represents whiteness, reflecting the contrast between the daughter's extraordinary Darkness and her mother's name. Victor also considers the possibility of naming another daughter Rubedo to continue the alchemical sequence.[1]
- Nigredo's first confirmed named appearance occurs during the gathering of the Imperial Princesses in Chapter 1165, where she is discovered sleeping near the ceiling while her sisters assemble beneath her.[1]
- She possesses extremely pale skin and black hair described as resembling the pure conception of Darkness, visually reflecting the extraordinary nature of her Divine inheritance.[1]
- Nigredo refuses to wake when Albedo orders her to come down, but immediately obeys the same instruction from Anna. The incident reveals the difference in parental authority established by her mothers and becomes one of the reasons Albedo is later forced to confront her isolation from family life.[1][2]
- Nigredo's introductory description identifies her as a Primal Goddess of Darkness, while later dialogue specifies the more precise classification Eldritch Primordial Goddess. Her nature is therefore distinct from an ordinary native Darkness Divinity.[1][2]
- Her Darkness is associated with the Eldritch singularity in which Being and Non-Being collapse together, an existential characteristic whose complete offensive applications have not yet been individually demonstrated.[1]
- A black hole manifested within Victor's Personal Universe as a consequence of Nigredo's birth. She subsequently greets the singularity as "my black hole" during the family's celestial banquet, confirming its continued existence.[1][3]
- Nigredo selects a chair formed from a black hole during Victor's cosmic family banquet, while other daughters choose seats made from stars, nebulae and different astronomical phenomena.[3]
- Nigredo and Yog are the two daughters who do not retreat when they observe the cosmic horrors inside Victor's artificial Eldritch galaxy. Both consider the creatures beautiful because of their unusual compatibility with foreign Eldritch existence.[7][4]
- Nigredo's exceptional connection to [[Eldritch Energy]] is explicitly recognized by Victor, who singles her out alongside Yog when explaining that some daughters possess substantially stronger concentrations of the foreign Energy than others.[5]
- Despite her laziness, Nigredo possesses extraordinary intellectual potential. Victor encourages her not to waste her abilities and motivates her through affectionate praise, prompting Anna to observe how easily he inspires daughters who resist ordinary instruction.[4]
- [[Scathach]] considers Nigredo capable of defeating Albedo during the latter's corrective martial training, establishing that Nigredo has developed extraordinary practical power despite her comparatively young age. The statement is a comparative assessment rather than the outcome of a completed individual fight.[10]
- Albedo later realizes that her perception of ordinary supernatural strength has become distorted because she is so accustomed to Nigredo's extraordinary abilities. This becomes apparent when she visits Evie, whose accomplishments as a Witch Queen seem comparatively ordinary to someone accustomed to naturally born Dragon Goddesses.[11]
- Nigredo can manifest an Eldritch Form in which her Humanoid body becomes grotesquely distorted and covered with numerous eyes. Only a portion of Victor's biological daughters have learned to access these forms despite the broader generation possessing the potential for them.[6]
- Nigredo and Yog jointly create experimental Eldritch fish combining characteristics of foreign cosmic organisms and Leviathans. Nigredo becomes annoyed when Yog initially claims sole credit for the project, prompting her sister to acknowledge their shared work.[6]
- Nigredo's specific contribution allows the experimental Eldritch fish to survive inside black holes, demonstrating that properties associated with her Darkness Divinity can be incorporated into another living being.[6]
- Nigredo, Yog, Valentina, Leone, Hinata, Silvia, Rosemary, Stella, Velina and their sisters eventually combine their powers with Victor's scale to create [[Cthugha]], a genuine Divine Eldritch being.[6]
- Cthugha is classified as a Great Old One rather than an Outer God. Victor explains that only he and Azathoth possess the authority to create true Eldritch Gods, while the daughters' Divine creations belong to the subordinate Great Old One tier.[6]
- Cthugha becomes the progenitor of a new Race of Fire Vampires and possesses enough natural power to destroy a Solar System shortly after birth. This destructive potential belongs to the completed creature and should not automatically be attributed to Nigredo as an individual combat feat.[6]
- During Victor's educational lessons concerning Moira's extraordinary Luck, Nigredo quietly asks what her youngest sister's power actually is. Victor explains the difference between Moira's innate Eldritch Luck and the passive favor she appears to receive from the native Universe.[8]
- Nigredo is one of the relatively few members of Victor's second generation whose Divinity is explicitly aligned with the [[Eldritch Pantheon]] rather than merely containing dormant traces of foreign Eldritch heritage.[2]
- Her completed role as the native Universe's universal Regulator of Darkness has not been established. Her confirmed status is that of an Eldritch Primordial Goddess and Outer God possessing an extraordinary Darkness Divinity.
- Nigredo's exact chronological age, ordinary eye color, physical measurements, complete natural True Dragon Form and maximum individual combat capabilities remain unidentified.
