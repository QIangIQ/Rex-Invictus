**[[Victoria Fulger Elderblood]]**, formerly known as Victoria Rider and Victoria Alucard Fulger, is a True Blood Dragon, Dragon Goddess, businesswoman, member of the Fulger Clan, one of Victor Elderblood's wives, and the founder of the financial empire established under the Rider name. (Lore adapted from *My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires*.)

#### Debut
Chapter 81: Sly Fox
To be honest, I feel completely lost... A long time ago, I got used to being the 'black sheep' and 'failure' of the Fulgers.
—Victoria Fulger Elderblood reflecting on her past and the treatment she received from Clan Fulger, Chapter 728
Victoria Fulger Elderblood, formerly known as Victoria Rider and Victoria Alucard Fulger, is a True Blood Dragon, Dragon Goddess, businesswoman, member of the [[Fulger Clan]], one of [[Victor Elderblood]]'s wives, and the founder of the financial empire established under the Rider name. She is the younger daughter of [[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]], the younger sister of Natashia Fulger, the aunt of [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]], the mother of [[Tatsuya Rider]], and the biological mother of [[Svetlana Veronika Elderblood]], her daughter with Victor.[2][1]
Victoria was born into one of the most prestigious Noble Vampire families on [[Nightingale]], but her childhood and much of her adult life were shaped by an unusual hereditary condition: she did not inherit the Lightning power traditionally possessed by the main Fulger bloodline. Unlike Natashia, whose overwhelming talent embodied the ideals of the Clan, Victoria possessed only the fundamental abilities and physical characteristics of a Noble Vampire. Although she was physically powerful and possessed considerable intelligence, the absence of Lightning caused other members of the Clan to regard her as defective and repeatedly compare her unfavorably with her sister.[3][4]
The discrimination eventually escalated beyond ordinary social rejection. Some factions within Clan Fulger attempted to exploit Victoria's prestigious bloodline primarily for reproduction, treating her hereditary value as more important than her individual desires. Refusing to submit to that future, Victoria separated herself from the family, adopted the surname Rider, and created an independent financial empire. The decision was simultaneously an act of self-preservation and an attempt to prove that she could become successful without possessing the power her relatives believed defined the Fulger name.[4]
Over the following centuries, Victoria accumulated extraordinary wealth and established a massive network of businesses. Her financial influence eventually became comparable to or greater than that of many of Nightingale's most politically powerful figures, despite the fact that she did not personally hold a Vampire Count title. The Rider empire became one of the most significant economic organizations associated with Noble Vampire society, and Victoria developed a reputation for exceptional business management, strategic planning, and the ability to recognize profitable opportunities.[5][6]
Victoria's principal source of pride during her separation from Fulger was her son, Tatsuya Rider. Unlike his mother, Tatsuya inherited the Lightning of the Fulger bloodline together with the Illusion-related abilities of his father. Victoria regarded him as the continuation of everything she had been unable to achieve personally and became intensely protective of his development, sometimes placing expectations upon him that reflected her own unresolved feelings of inadequacy.[3][7]
Her relationship with the Fulger family gradually changed after Natashia regained control of her original personality and began restoring the Clan's former standing. Victoria's financial knowledge and organizational abilities proved indispensable to the reconstruction. Nevertheless, returning to her family did not immediately resolve the resentment accumulated through centuries of rejection, and she continued struggling with the desire to prove that she deserved the same recognition as her sister.[8][3][4]
Victoria's eventual incorporation into Victor's family fundamentally changed her circumstances. Through the strengthening of her Vampire existence and the influence of Victor's extraordinary Bloodline, she finally awakened the Fulger Lightning she had lacked throughout her life. However, her greatest development came from recognizing that she no longer needed the ability to earn acceptance. Victor and the other members of his family already considered her one of their own, regardless of her achievements, wealth, or inherited powers.[9][4]
Her transformation into a True Blood Dragon subsequently revealed the extraordinary benefits of her previous disadvantage. Because Victoria had spent centuries without Lightning, she had been forced to refine her physical movement and basic Vampiric abilities rather than depend upon elemental speed. Once she acquired Lightning, she combined it with those fundamentals, developing exceptionally precise control and a movement technique that allowed her to glide through the air through manipulated static electricity. Carmila eventually recognized Victoria as the most skilled Lightning controller among the Fulger women, potentially surpassed only by Victor himself.[10]
Victoria's importance within the Elderblood family is not limited to her supernatural abilities. Her independent financial empire, experience rebuilding Clan Fulger, and ability to make rational decisions led to her selection as one of the Noble Vampire representatives in Victor's developing government. During the later Imperial era, she remained among the women responsible for managing portions of the civilization's enormous administrative structure and openly supported Victor's decision to delegate responsibilities rather than personally control every aspect of the Empire.[11][12]
Despite her extraordinary political, financial, and supernatural accomplishments, Victoria's defining personal development remains her gradual reconciliation with the family that once rejected her and the establishment of a new home within Clan Elderblood. She is no longer primarily the defective Fulger who fled Nightingale's aristocracy or the independent businesswoman attempting to demonstrate her worth. She is a wife, mother of two children from different relationships, Dragon Goddess, administrator, and an established member of the Imperial family.[4][1]

### Physical Appearance

#### Vampire Form
Victoria is an exceptionally beautiful woman with long blonde hair, blue eyes, fair skin, and an elegant physique. She stands approximately 175 centimeters tall and possesses a strong familial resemblance to her older sister Natashia and her niece Sasha. Her appearance reflects the characteristics of the main Fulger bloodline despite her original inability to manifest the family's signature Lightning.[2]
Unlike Natashia, whose imposing physical presence and overwhelming supernatural power traditionally embodied the ideal image of a Fulger warrior, Victoria's appearance is associated with a more restrained and refined form of elegance. Her facial characteristics are comparatively gentle, and she ordinarily maintains the composed presentation expected of an experienced Noble Vampire businesswoman and administrator.[3]
Her resemblance to Natashia is particularly apparent when the two sisters are together. The differences between them are more evident through their expressions and behavior: Natashia is frequently animated, unpredictable, and openly affectionate toward [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], while Victoria normally attempts to maintain the dignity associated with her long-standing position as an independent financial leader.
Victoria's clothing traditionally reflects her professional responsibilities and aristocratic heritage. She favors elegant and practical attire suitable for meetings, formal occasions, and the supervision of large business organizations rather than clothing designed primarily for combat.
Her blue eyes were one of the recognizable characteristics of her original appearance. After developing more advanced supernatural powers and entering Victor's Bloodline, her eyes could display the red coloration associated with her Vampiric and later Draconic states.

#### Blood Dragon Form
Victoria's later Dragon Goddess appearance.
Victoria eventually evolved into a genuine True Blood Dragon through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s transformation of his family. The process fundamentally altered her racial foundation, replacing the limitations of her earlier Noble Vampire existence with the substantially greater physical and supernatural potential associated with [[True Dragons]].[10]
Her later humanoid representation retains her characteristic blonde hair and general facial appearance while displaying the Draconic features associated with the Fulger branch of Victor's family. These include prominent golden Dragon horns and the supernatural characteristics inherited through her new Blood Dragon physiology.[12]
The golden coloration of the horns became a recognizable feature of the Fulger women. During their gathering in the Imperial era, Carmila, Natashia, Natasha, Victoria, and Sasha were described as a group of blondes possessing golden horns, emphasizing both their common familial origin and the Draconic lineage they now shared.[12]
Victoria's new physical foundation greatly enhanced her former abilities, but her appearance remained sufficiently recognizable that her identity as Carmila's younger daughter was not obscured. Her Dragonization did not erase her previous characteristics; rather, it integrated the powers and developmental advantages she had acquired as a Vampire into a more advanced supernatural body.[10]
Although Victoria possesses the abilities and status of a Dragon Goddess, her most distinctive supernatural characteristic remains the exceptional precision with which she manipulates Lightning.

### Personality
Victoria is intelligent, independent, pragmatic, financially ambitious, level-headed, and exceptionally competent at managing complicated organizations. Her personality developed through centuries of experiences that differed substantially from those of most members of Clan Fulger. While her sister and many of her relatives relied upon exceptional combat power and inherited Lightning abilities, Victoria was forced to establish her position through economic influence, personal discipline, political judgment, and the cultivation of organizational talent. These qualities eventually made her one of the most dependable administrators within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s growing faction.[3][11]
Her public demeanor is usually restrained and practical. Victoria prefers assessing available resources, identifying the consequences of a decision, and arranging responsibilities in a manner that minimizes unnecessary risks. During the establishment of Victor's new government, she was specifically selected because her history of independently constructing a financial empire demonstrated extraordinary leadership, while her temperament made her more likely to reach logical decisions than many of the more impulsive members of the family.[11]
However, Victoria's composure conceals a considerable degree of emotional insecurity originating from her childhood. Her inability to manifest the Lightning of the Fulger bloodline caused relatives to regard her as inferior to Natashia, even though she possessed considerable talent in other areas. The comparison continued throughout her life, gradually creating the belief that she was fundamentally defective and needed to accomplish something extraordinary before she could deserve the same recognition as her sister.[3][4]
The rejection became especially damaging because Victoria's position as Carmila's daughter made her bloodline valuable even without the Lightning ability itself. Some members of Clan Fulger attempted to exploit that lineage for reproductive purposes, effectively reducing her potential future to producing descendants who might inherit the ability she lacked. Her eventual departure was therefore both a rejection of the Clan's treatment and an attempt to establish a life in which her worth would be determined by her own choices rather than her hereditary characteristics.[4]
Victoria's creation of the Rider financial empire reflects these motivations. She did not simply desire money for personal luxury, although she genuinely enjoys increasing her wealth and recognizes the pleasure associated with successful business ventures. Her economic accomplishments became evidence that she could surpass the limitations her relatives had assigned to her. Every successful investment, business expansion, and financial victory helped reinforce the identity she constructed after leaving Fulger.[5][4]
Her ambition can occasionally become excessive. Victoria is openly attracted to profitable opportunities and is willing to exploit the commercial potential of major public events. During the period surrounding the Vampire Count Games, she sold merchandise based upon prominent participants, including products using the images and clothing styles of Natashia and Niklaus. [[Hecate]] questioned the wisdom of provoking such powerful figures for additional profit, but Victoria remained enthusiastic about the business opportunity.[5]
Despite her apparent greed, Victoria places the safety of her family above financial gain. This priority became particularly evident during the Demonic invasion of Fulger territory. She initially worried about the destruction of buildings and the enormous cost of rebuilding, but explicitly informed Sasha that the preservation of her life was more important than any property loss. Victoria would rather lose enormous amounts of money than allow her niece or son to die while attempting to protect it.[13]
Her attachment to Tatsuya is especially intense. Victoria regards her son as the most important achievement of her earlier life and became deeply invested in his future after realizing that he had inherited the Lightning ability she lacked. She initially regarded his exceptional talent as evidence that he could achieve everything she had been denied, causing her to become possessive and excessively protective of his relationships and development.[3]
Natashia recognized that Victoria was placing her own unresolved expectations upon Tatsuya. Although Victoria insisted that she loved her son for himself rather than as a replacement for her own ambitions, her sister understood that the desire for him to become exceptional was closely connected to the psychological wounds created by their family. The conversation forced Victoria to confront the possibility that her protectiveness could become a burden rather than simply an expression of maternal affection.[3]
Even after Tatsuya became an accomplished supernatural warrior, Victoria continued treating him as a child. She remains particularly sensitive to discussions about his romantic relationships and the possibility of him establishing a family of his own. During the period when Tatsuya and Hecate were left responsible for Fulger affairs, Victoria attempted to return to the Clan out of concern for him, only to be stopped by Natashia and Sasha, who insisted that he was old enough to manage his own responsibilities.[7]
Victoria's relationship with Natashia is equally complicated. She loves her older sister, but the constant comparisons made during childhood caused deep resentment that could not be eliminated simply because Natashia herself had not intended to hurt her. Victoria's experiences during the period dominated by Natasha's alternate personality also contributed to the deterioration of the relationship, leaving her with memories of a family in which she had never felt entirely accepted.[3]
After Natashia regained control and began rebuilding Fulger, Victoria returned to assist her. However, she continued attempting to demonstrate her usefulness through the prosperity of the Clan. Victor eventually recognized that her ambition was motivated partly by a desperate desire for acceptance. By strengthening Fulger and proving that she could accomplish what other members could not, Victoria hoped to finally receive the recognition she had been denied.[4]
Victor's response helped change that mentality. He explained that Victoria had already been accepted by the people surrounding her and no longer needed to prove that she was worthy of belonging to her family. She had obtained the love and recognition she originally believed would come only after awakening Lightning or becoming indispensable to Fulger. Victoria gradually realized that her pursuit of the ability had lost much of its emotional meaning because the acceptance she wanted already existed.[4]
This realization did not erase the events of her childhood. Victoria could begin releasing the bitterness created by those experiences without pretending that the rejection had never occurred. The emotional change was therefore more complicated than simply forgiving everyone responsible; she began separating her present relationships from the expectations that had once governed her life.
Victoria's normally rational personality also contrasts with her surprisingly imaginative private thoughts. When Natashia involves her in Victor's family affairs, Victoria can become extremely embarrassed and construct elaborate scenarios in her imagination. These episodes reveal a more chaotic and emotionally reactive side that she generally conceals beneath her professional demeanor.[3]
Her discomfort with the unconventional structure of Victor's family became especially apparent after she entered his household. Victoria occasionally expressed disbelief that she had become one of his wives, particularly because the relationship involved sharing the same husband with her older sister and niece. Nevertheless, she had accepted the arrangement and signed the relevant marriage agreement, making her objections more a reflection of embarrassment than a serious desire to leave.[7]
Victoria is also less naturally obsessed with combat than several other members of Victor's family. She recognizes the importance of training and possesses considerable supernatural potential, but her preferred strengths involve control, preparation, organization, and the efficient application of resources. This approach also influences her Lightning techniques, which emphasize precision and movement rather than simply releasing the greatest possible quantity of destructive Energy.[10]
The same mentality governs her political decisions. During the later Imperial era, Victoria supported Victor's increasing willingness to delegate responsibilities, explaining that an Empire containing so many capable supernatural individuals did not require him to personally manage every matter. Her position reflected the philosophy she had followed throughout her business career: effective leadership involves building organizations capable of operating without constant intervention from their founder.[12]
Despite her professional seriousness, Victoria remains capable of playful behavior. She occasionally participates in the unusual activities of the other wives simply because the opportunity interests her. During the dispute involving Violet and the daughters, Victoria joined the musical accompaniment used to create a deliberately dramatic atmosphere, demonstrating that her comparatively restrained personality does not prevent her from enjoying the family's humor.[14]
By the mature Imperial era, Victoria has become considerably more comfortable with the relationships surrounding her. She remains ambitious, financially knowledgeable, protective of her children, and capable of becoming embarrassed by Natashia's behavior, but she no longer measures her entire existence against the Lightning she once lacked. Her value within the Elderblood family is recognized through her achievements, personal qualities, relationships, and the extraordinary control she eventually developed over the power once used to define her as inferior.

### History

#### Early Life
Victoria was born into the main bloodline of [[Fulger Clan]] as the younger daughter of [[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]] and the younger sister of [[Annasthashia Elderblood Fulger]]. Her family possessed an extraordinary supernatural heritage originating from Carmila, who had originally been a Lightning Spirit before becoming a Vampire through her relationship with Vlad. The Lightning associated with Carmila subsequently became the defining hereditary ability of the Fulger lineage.[3][13]
Unlike Natashia, Victoria did not inherit the elemental power associated with her mother's bloodline. Her existence was unusual because Noble Vampires ordinarily inherited at least some supernatural characteristics from their parents, while Victoria possessed only the fundamental abilities and physical advantages of a Vampire born into a Count Clan.[3]
Carmila's earlier position as the mother of a talented heir meant that Victoria's initial lack of Lightning did not automatically make her useless to everyone in the family. However, the circumstances changed after Carmila's absence and the deterioration of the Clan's internal relationships. Victoria increasingly became the target of comparisons, discrimination, and attempts to exploit the value of her hereditary bloodline.[3][4]
For much of her youth, Victoria existed in Natashia's shadow. Her sister possessed the extraordinary Lightning talent valued by Fulger, while Victoria's accomplishments in other fields were frequently dismissed. Although she could develop the basic physical and supernatural abilities of a Noble Vampire, the absence of Lightning created an inferiority complex that persisted throughout her later life.[3]
Some factions within Clan Fulger eventually attempted to use Victoria primarily as a means of producing descendants. The decision reflected the assumption that her bloodline could still generate powerful children even if she personally lacked the defining Fulger ability. Rather than submit to such an existence, Victoria left the family and adopted the surname Rider.[4]

#### Rider Financial Empire
After leaving Clan Fulger, Victoria began constructing an independent financial empire. Without the Lightning powers and political position traditionally associated with the main Fulger bloodline, she relied upon her intelligence, organizational ability, commercial judgment, and willingness to pursue profitable opportunities.[4]
Over several centuries, she established a massive network of businesses and investments that became known through the Rider name. Her economic influence eventually reached an extraordinary scale, with the wealth accumulated through her enterprises measured in trillions of dollars.[15][5]
The financial empire provided Victoria with something she had never obtained from her original family: a position whose value depended upon abilities she had personally developed. She could negotiate with powerful Noble Vampires, influence major political events, and support enormous supernatural operations without requiring the Lightning talent whose absence had defined her childhood.
Victoria eventually became the mother of [[Tatsuya Rider]], whose father was a Japanese Noble Vampire possessing an Illusion-related bloodline. Tatsuya inherited the Lightning of his mother's family together with the supernatural Illusion abilities of his paternal lineage, becoming an exceptionally talented young Vampire.[3][7]
Her son's development became another important part of Victoria's identity. She regarded Tatsuya as both her child and the continuation of the family she had established independently of Fulger. However, her unresolved inferiority complex also contributed to excessively high expectations, causing Natashia to eventually question whether Victoria was attempting to accomplish her own unrealized ambitions through him.[3]
By the time Victoria entered the main story, she was an established financial leader whose resources were substantial enough to participate in disputes involving Vampire Count titles and the political structure of [[Nightingale]].

### Synopsis

#### Vampire Society
Victoria was first mentioned during the discussion of the political circumstances surrounding Clan Fulger's loss of its former Vampire Count position. [[Niklaus Horseman]] and Clan Horseman had acquired the eastern territory and title previously associated with Fulger, creating a dispute whose outcome could affect the balance of political authority on [[Nightingale]].[15]
Victoria sought to challenge Niklaus through a formal Game rather than immediately initiating an unrestricted conflict between their Clans. She was prepared to wager the enormous financial empire she had accumulated under the Rider name against the political prize represented by the Vampire Count title.[15]
Her willingness to risk assets valued at several trillion dollars demonstrated both the extraordinary scale of her wealth and the importance attached to the title. Although the financial empire represented centuries of personal effort, Victoria recognized that becoming a Vampire Count would provide authority and opportunities unavailable through economic influence alone.
The proposed Game attracted considerable attention from Nightingale's Noble Vampires because it involved one of the most influential independent financial organizations and a Clan occupying one of the five principal political positions of Vampire society.[16][17]
Victoria selected her son, [[Tatsuya Rider]], as her representative. Tatsuya possessed exceptional swordsmanship, Fulger Lightning, and sophisticated Illusion abilities inherited from his father. Niklaus selected [[Einer Horseman]], whose own supernatural capabilities made him a dangerous opponent.[2][18]
The duel rapidly became one of the major attractions of the Royal Capital. Both representatives possessed abilities uncommon among ordinary young Noble Vampires, and their conflict offered the audience an opportunity to observe the potential heirs of two powerful families.[18]
However, the original confrontation was interrupted by [[Scathach]], who recognized that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] wanted to test his own progress after months of intensive training. She introduced a separate wager involving Victor's participation against both combatants, temporarily setting aside the original dispute between Victoria and Niklaus.[19]
Victor entered the arena and fought Tatsuya and Einer simultaneously. The encounter revealed that both young Vampires possessed exceptional potential, but they eventually struggled against Victor's increasingly refined physical strength, elemental abilities, and martial experience.[20][21][22]
Victor emerged victorious from his separate confrontation, but the result did not automatically resolve the original wager between Rider and Horseman. Scathach clarified that her arrangement concerned her disciple and was legally distinct from Victoria's challenge. Victoria consequently recognized that the original dispute remained unresolved despite the enormous public spectacle.[23]
After considering the changes created by Victor's appearance and his connection to several major Noble Vampire families, Victoria agreed with Niklaus to treat their original Game as a draw. She withdrew from the immediate attempt to obtain the title rather than continuing a dispute whose political circumstances had changed dramatically.[23]
The encounter nevertheless created a lasting connection between Victoria's family and Victor. Victor developed respect for Tatsuya after their battle and sought the swordsman out, regarding him as someone whose abilities made him a valuable potential companion and training partner.[24][25]
Victor subsequently visited Victoria's residence, where his presence contributed to the discovery of infiltrators and suspicious individuals operating within the estate. Victoria and her contracted Witch, [[Hecate Moriarty]], investigated the security breach while Tatsuya's relationship with Victor began shifting from rivalry toward friendship.[25][26]
Victoria's separation from Fulger eventually began changing after Natashia's original personality returned. Having regained control over her life, Natashia became determined to restore her family's former status and began involving Victoria and Tatsuya in the reconstruction of the Clan.[8]
Although Victoria possessed considerable reservations about returning to the environment she had fled, her sister's determination and the changing political circumstances gradually drew her back into Fulger affairs.

#### A New Pillar
Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s recognition as a Vampire Count and Natashia's return to active leadership, Victoria became increasingly involved in restoring Fulger's financial and administrative position.
Her expertise was particularly valuable because the Clan's previous standing had depended heavily upon its overwhelming martial power and the authority of its leadership. Victoria possessed centuries of experience in financial administration and the development of organizations capable of operating through complicated political and economic circumstances.[8]
Natashia recognized that her younger sister's talents complemented her own. Although Natashia possessed exceptional combat ability and the reputation associated with one of [[Nightingale]]'s strongest families, Victoria had demonstrated the ability to build an enormous financial organization from nothing. The reconstruction of Fulger therefore benefited from combining Natashia's political and martial influence with Victoria's administrative competence.
Victoria also continued pursuing commercial opportunities independently of her Clan responsibilities. During the events surrounding later Noble Vampire gatherings, she sold merchandise based upon prominent figures and major political conflicts, demonstrating that she remained deeply interested in business even after resuming her relationship with Fulger.[5]
Her activities included selling clothing and other products inspired by the appearances of Natashia and Niklaus. Although [[Hecate]] questioned the wisdom of exploiting powerful figures for commercial profit, Victoria regarded their public prominence as an opportunity too valuable to ignore.[5]
The period also demonstrated that Victoria had not abandoned her ambition to acquire a Vampire Count title. Although she accepted the outcome of the original Rider-Horseman Game, she continued recognizing the political significance of such a position and occasionally expressed frustration over having withdrawn from the opportunity.[5]
However, her involvement in rebuilding Fulger increasingly placed her in a position of influence without requiring her to become a Count herself. The Clan's financial recovery and expanding commercial relationships depended upon the organizational skills she had acquired during her independent life.

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
As [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family expanded, Victoria became increasingly involved in the relationships surrounding Natashia and Sasha. Her older sister actively encouraged Victoria to develop a closer personal connection with Victor, regarding him as the ideal individual around whom the women of the Fulger bloodline could establish a more secure and united family.
Victoria initially resisted much of Natashia's interference, particularly because she considered the relationship structure surrounding Victor exceptionally unconventional. Natashia nevertheless continued pushing her younger sister toward the household, sometimes ignoring Victoria's objections in favor of what she considered beneficial for their family.
Their interactions eventually exposed the deeper insecurity Victoria had carried throughout her life. When Natashia questioned her excessive concern for Tatsuya, Victoria admitted that her son possessed the abilities she had always lacked. He had inherited Fulger Lightning and his father's supernatural talent, while Victoria herself had been treated as inferior because she lacked the defining hereditary power of her family.[3]
Victoria explained that she wanted Tatsuya to possess the best possible future. She wished for him to receive the opportunities and recognition denied to her, including a suitable partner and the ability to develop his extraordinary talent without being restrained by the expectations of others.[3]
Natashia recognized the danger in her sister's mentality. Although Victoria genuinely loved Tatsuya, her protectiveness was influenced by her desire to see him accomplish the ambitions she had been unable to fulfill. Natashia warned that treating her son as a replacement for her own unrealized potential could place an unfair burden upon him.[3]
The discussion also revealed Natashia's opinion of Victoria's accomplishments. She considered Victoria an extraordinary leader whose business and administrative talent could surpass her own. The recovery of Clan Fulger depended heavily upon Victoria's efforts, and Natashia understood that her sister could have restored much of the Clan's prosperity even without her direct participation.[3]
After recognizing the depth of Victoria's inferiority complex, Natashia became more determined to involve her with Victor. She believed that her younger sister needed affection and recognition independent of the Lightning talent that had governed so much of her life.
Victoria's reactions frequently became a source of comedy because of her elaborate imagination. Natashia's attempts to involve her with Victor caused Victoria to imagine increasingly exaggerated scenarios, leaving her visibly embarrassed and occasionally prompting her to struggle against her sister's attempts to bring her into the household.[3]
During the subsequent interactions with Victor's family, Victoria continued experiencing a mixture of curiosity, embarrassment, and uncertainty. She was accustomed to controlling her professional life and maintaining a respectable public image, but Natashia's unpredictable behavior repeatedly placed her in situations she could neither anticipate nor easily escape.[27][28]
Nevertheless, the growing relationship between Victoria and Victor's family gradually provided opportunities for her to experience forms of affection and acceptance that differed from the expectations she had faced within the original Fulger household.

#### Demonic War
During [[Diablo]]'s large-scale invasion, the territories associated with Clan Fulger became targets of Demonic forces. Victoria was among the individuals responsible for organizing the Clan's response while Natashia participated in the wider gathering of supernatural leaders.[29]
Despite lacking the Lightning of her family at that time, Victoria was already an Elder Vampire possessing substantial physical strength. Her abilities made her dangerous compared with ordinary supernatural combatants, but her greatest contribution during the invasion concerned the coordination of Fulger's defenses rather than direct participation in the principal battle.[29]
Victoria helped organize the Clan's personnel and instructed non-combatants to seek shelter within protected underground areas. She subsequently entered the observation room maintained by [[Hecate]], whose [[Magic]] allowed them to monitor the condition of the territory and identify the Demonic forces responsible for the attack.[29]
The principal enemy was identified as Phoenix, a powerful Demon Duke associated with the Thirty-Seventh Key of Solomon. His Fire-based powers and regenerative characteristics made him an especially dangerous opponent for Clan Fulger, whose members traditionally relied upon Lightning and direct combat.[29][13]
Victoria and Hecate recognized that eliminating the invaders without collateral damage would be extremely difficult. Victoria was immediately concerned about the destruction of the territory's buildings and infrastructure, but also understood that the survival of the inhabitants and the defeat of the enemy were more important than preserving every property.[13]
She established communication with Sasha and Tatsuya, allowing the two young combatants to coordinate their efforts against the Demonic forces. Victoria initially requested that Sasha avoid unnecessary property damage, but made clear that the request was secondary to her niece's survival. She explicitly instructed Sasha to prioritize her own safety if preserving the buildings would place her in danger.[13]
The battle subsequently revealed how far Sasha had progressed under Natashia's instruction. Sasha employed advanced [[Martial Arts]] originating from Carmila Fulger's original fighting system, moving through the territory at extraordinary speed and eliminating large numbers of Demons through concentrated Lightning attacks.[13]
Victoria recognized the significance of these techniques because she possessed extensive knowledge of the Fulger family's history despite never being able to use its hereditary Lightning herself. She understood that the techniques were exceptionally difficult even for experienced members of the Clan and was astonished that Sasha could perform them at such a young age.[13]
Her surprise intensified when Sasha demonstrated a technique that Natashia herself had not been able to use properly until reaching the Elder Vampire stage and mastering the relevant transformation. Victoria rose from her seat in shock, recognizing that Sasha's development had progressed far beyond what ordinary Noble Vampires could reasonably achieve.[30]
The confrontation against Phoenix also demonstrated the extraordinary potential of Tatsuya. His Illusion techniques proved effective against numerous Demons, and he cooperated with Sasha to overcome enemies whose powers and durability greatly exceeded those of ordinary supernatural combatants.[31][30]
Despite their efforts, Phoenix's regenerative abilities made the conflict difficult to resolve permanently. Victoria and Hecate recognized that he possessed supernatural characteristics allowing him to recover from physical destruction, making direct attacks insufficient unless the combatants could damage his Soul or employ appropriate sealing techniques.[32]
Victoria nevertheless praised the younger Vampires for their accomplishments. She understood that fighting a powerful Demon Duke and surviving represented a remarkable achievement, even if Phoenix could not be permanently eliminated through conventional physical attacks.[32]
The invasion caused extensive financial damage. A report eventually revealed that businesses belonging to Victoria in Russia, China, Ukraine, and parts of the Middle East had been destroyed by Demonic attacks. The losses exceeded five hundred billion dollars, representing the destruction of an enormous portion of the assets she had accumulated through centuries of work.[32]
The news overwhelmed Victoria to the point that she lost consciousness. Her reaction reflected the importance of the Rider empire to her identity: the businesses were not merely possessions but the result of her independent efforts to establish a life outside the family that had rejected her.[32]
Despite the financial devastation, Victoria continued prioritizing the welfare of Sasha, Tatsuya, and the other members of Fulger. After the battle, she focused upon obtaining better protective equipment for Sasha and Natashia, recognizing that the growing scale of supernatural warfare required armor capable of withstanding considerably greater punishment.[32]
She proposed obtaining specialized equipment through the Adrasteia women, whose expertise and resources could provide armor better suited to the Lightning-based combat style of the Fulger family.[32]
The Demonic War therefore demonstrated both sides of Victoria's character. She was devastated by the destruction of her financial accomplishments, yet remained an effective administrator whose primary responsibilities concerned the survival and security of the family she had once abandoned.

#### King Among Kings
Victoria's relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually progressed beyond that of an ally connected through Natashia, Sasha, and Tatsuya. During the development of Victor's faction, she became increasingly involved in his personal household and eventually accepted membership in Clan Alucard.
Her incorporation into Victor's family had a profound effect upon her supernatural existence. After receiving the benefits of his Bloodline and establishing a closer connection with him, Victoria finally awakened the Fulger Lightning she had lacked throughout her life.[9]
The awakening was particularly important because the absence of Lightning had determined much of Victoria's childhood, relationship with Natashia, and decision to establish the Rider identity. For centuries, she had believed that acquiring the ability would prove she was a genuine member of the Fulger bloodline and establish that she possessed the same hereditary worth as her relatives.
Victor regarded the development as a natural consequence of her new connection to him. He explained that his Blood and the strengthening associated with joining his family made the awakening of her dormant hereditary power unsurprising.[9]
Victoria subsequently began training alongside Natashia and Sasha, allowing her to develop the elemental power she had previously only been able to observe and understand through the achievements of other Fulger women.[9]
However, the awakening did not resolve her emotional difficulties in the manner she had anticipated. Victor eventually recognized that Victoria continued feeling detached from Fulger despite possessing the ability she had once desired. Her attempts to strengthen the Clan and prove her usefulness were partly driven by a desire for acceptance rather than simply personal ambition.[4]
He explained that Victoria no longer needed Lightning to receive that acceptance. Her family already recognized her, and her position within Victor's household did not depend upon her wealth, status, or supernatural power. The realization was emotionally difficult because she had spent most of her life believing that possessing the traditional Fulger ability would finally make her worthy of the family's affection.[4]
Victoria gradually understood that obtaining Lightning after already finding a family could not provide the recognition she had originally expected from it. She had achieved acceptance through relationships and personal qualities rather than by satisfying the hereditary expectations that had once governed her life.[4]
Victor's reassurance allowed her to confront the bitterness attached to her past. Although the mistreatment could not simply be forgotten, she began recognizing that her current relationships were different from those that had caused the original trauma.[4]
Around the same period, Victor's faction began establishing a more formal governmental structure. The increasing number of supernatural [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] and organizations under his influence made it impractical for every decision to be handled informally through his wives and immediate subordinates.[11]
The faction therefore established plans for a central administrative residence where representatives of the different groups could gather and make decisions concerning their shared responsibilities. Victoria was selected as one of the three women qualified to represent the Noble Vampires, alongside Kaguya and Ruby.[11]
Her selection was based upon her independent accomplishments. Victoria had built a financial empire under the Rider name, demonstrated exceptional leadership, and possessed a comparatively level-headed personality. The arrangement allowed the three representatives to substitute for one another whenever their other responsibilities prevented them from attending administrative meetings.[11]
The political developments also led to the public announcement of Victor's intended marriages within the main Noble Vampire bloodlines. The Fulger women named in the announcement included Victoria and Annasthashia, while the corresponding Snow and Scarlett families announced their own members who would enter Victor's household.[11]
The arrangement elevated the political significance of the families associated with Victor, whose position as the Second Progenitor made marriage into his lineage an extraordinary honor within Noble Vampire society.
Victoria later demonstrated continuing embarrassment concerning her new marital status. During the preparations for training under Hassan, she questioned why she was required to participate and claimed not to remember becoming Victor's wife. Sasha reminded her that she had willingly signed the marriage agreement, leaving Victoria unable to deny that she had accepted the relationship.[7]
Her reluctance was primarily connected to the unusual nature of the arrangement. Victoria had to adjust to sharing her husband with both her older sister and niece, a circumstance that remained embarrassing despite her genuine affection toward Victor.
The training arrangements also required Victoria to delegate her administrative responsibilities. Natashia explained that Tatsuya and [[Hecate]] could manage the Clan while she was occupied, but Victoria immediately attempted to return out of concern for her son.[7]
Sasha and Natashia stopped her, reminding Victoria that Tatsuya was already a capable supernatural warrior and that his relationship with Hecate did not require his mother's constant supervision. Their reaction demonstrated that Victoria's maternal protectiveness remained one of the strongest influences upon her behavior.[7]
Victoria's training subsequently expanded beyond the techniques available through her original Fulger heritage. During the period surrounding Victor's experiments with the Nightmare Tower, she participated in the broader development of the family and encountered challenges designed to prevent combatants from depending excessively upon their strongest abilities.
During a discussion of the Tower, Victoria recalled having encountered a powerful gorilla and opponents capable of suppressing her speed. Her experience demonstrated that even after acquiring Lightning, she had to confront enemies designed to counter the advantages normally provided by elemental movement.[33]
Victor also transferred the useful knowledge and memories he had acquired from [[Zeus]] concerning Lightning to Natashia and Victoria. The knowledge included advanced understanding of electrical phenomena and techniques developed by one of the strongest Lightning Gods known to the family.[33]
Victoria was temporarily overwhelmed by the enormous quantity of information entering her consciousness, but subsequently began examining the memories and the possibilities they offered for improving her own power. The transfer expanded the theoretical foundation available to her beyond the techniques preserved within Clan Fulger.[33]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Victoria's existence changed fundamentally when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family underwent its transformation into genuine True [[Blood Dragons]]. Her previous Noble Vampire physiology was integrated into a far more advanced Draconic foundation, providing greater physical potential, improved supernatural compatibility, and new opportunities for developing the abilities she had accumulated throughout her life.[10]
The transformation was especially significant for Victoria because of the unusual path through which she had developed. Unlike the other Fulger women, she had spent most of her existence without Lightning and therefore could not depend upon the extraordinary speed and offensive power traditionally associated with the Clan.[10]
Instead, she had refined her fundamental Vampiric abilities to an exceptional level. Her physical movement, combat fundamentals, control over her body, and ability to coordinate supernatural actions had developed through centuries of effort rather than through continuous reliance upon an inherited elemental power.[10]
When Victoria finally acquired Lightning, those accomplishments became an enormous advantage. Her movement was not limited to the traditional Fulger method of surrounding the body with Electricity and moving at extreme speed. She developed a technique involving the careful manipulation of static electricity, allowing her to reduce resistance and effectively skate through the air.[10]
The technique demonstrated an extraordinary level of refinement because it combined the physical movement she had mastered before awakening Lightning with precise control over electrical forces. Rather than simply applying a larger quantity of Energy, Victoria employed a comparatively sophisticated interaction between her body and the surrounding environment.[10]
Carmila, who had returned to the family after her restoration, observed the development and recognized that Victoria possessed an exceptional talent for controlling Lightning. Despite lacking the ability throughout most of her life, she had achieved a level of precision that surpassed the other principal Fulger women.[10]
Carmila distinguished the strengths of the family according to different characteristics. Sasha possessed the greatest raw output, Victoria possessed the finest Lightning control, and Carmila herself retained the greatest experience. She considered Victoria the most refined Lightning controller among the Fulger women, with Victor being the only family member she regarded as potentially superior in that respect.[10]
The recognition had an enormous emotional effect upon Victoria. For most of her life, she had been treated as inferior because she lacked the power her mother had passed to the family. Receiving genuine praise from Carmila for her mastery of that same power represented a reversal of the expectations that had once governed her existence.[10]
Carmila also recognized the significance of Victoria's response. The praise caused her daughter to display a genuine smile that Carmila had not seen in an extremely long time, emphasizing how deeply the earlier rejection had affected Victoria's emotional development.[10]
The moment demonstrated that Victoria's apparent disadvantage had ultimately contributed to a form of mastery that the naturally talented Fulger women had not developed in the same way. Because she had lacked Lightning, she was forced to become exceptional at the fundamental techniques underlying physical movement. Once she finally possessed the element, she could apply it through a level of bodily control developed over centuries.[10]
Victoria's True Dragon evolution therefore did not simply increase her overall strength. It completed an important development in her character: the woman previously regarded as defective for lacking Lightning became one of the finest practitioners of the ability in the entire Fulger family.
Her later Dragon Goddess status did not eliminate her interest in administration or financial management. Although she possessed considerably greater combat potential, Victoria continued recognizing the value of her experience as a businesswoman and leader.

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
As [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family developed into an increasingly powerful Draconic household, Victoria became more deeply integrated into the Fulger branch of the Elderblood family.
Her earlier separation from the Clan no longer prevented her from participating in its affairs, while the restoration of Carmila and the coexistence of Natashia and Natasha created new opportunities for their relationships to develop.
Victoria's responsibilities increasingly concerned the administration of organizations connected to Victor's expanding civilization rather than the independent financial empire she had originally built under the Rider name.
The administrative experience acquired over centuries remained particularly valuable. Unlike many of Victor's wives, whose specialties involved combat, [[Magic]], Divine Concepts, or supernatural research, Victoria possessed extensive knowledge concerning the operation of large organizations, economic systems, personnel management, and the delegation of responsibilities.
Her experience helped distinguish her from women whose supernatural power greatly exceeded their interest in ordinary administrative work. Victoria understood that even an organization led by exceptionally powerful individuals required reliable management systems rather than depending exclusively upon the personal intervention of its ruler.
She also continued developing her Lightning techniques, now possessing the advanced control acquired through her Draconic transformation and the knowledge inherited from the Fulger family and [[Zeus]].[33][10]
Her position within the household changed further as the family expanded through new generations of children. Victoria eventually became the biological mother of another child with Victor, establishing a direct Blood Dragon lineage separate from Tatsuya's earlier birth.

#### Those Who Rule Reality
During the later Imperial era, Victoria remained one of the principal members of the Fulger family involved in administering the [[Elderblood Empire]].
During a gathering elsewhere in the Galaxy, she joined Carmila, Natashia, Natasha, and Sasha to discuss [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s increasingly complicated responsibilities as Emperor. All five women possessed golden horns, visibly demonstrating their shared Draconic heritage despite the different circumstances through which each had developed.[12]
The conversation concerned Victor's decision to delegate an increasing number of responsibilities to other capable individuals. Victoria supported the general arrangement, explaining that although the distribution of duties was not perfectly efficient, an Empire containing so many extraordinary Beings did not require Victor to personally manage every administrative matter.[12]
Her position reflected the experience she had accumulated while building the Rider financial empire. Victoria understood that a successful organization needed leaders capable of assigning work, supervising subordinates, and making decisions without becoming overwhelmed by every minor responsibility.
The gathering also demonstrated how thoroughly Victoria's relationship with the Fulger family had changed. The woman who once abandoned the Clan because of its treatment of her was now able to participate in ordinary discussions with her mother, older sister, Natasha, and niece, without allowing the experiences of her childhood to prevent their continued relationship.[12]
Victoria also retained her occasional playful disposition. During the confrontation involving Violet's decision to expand Victor's family, several of the wives joined an intentionally dramatic musical performance intended to accompany the escalating dispute. Victoria participated by playing the violin, apparently accepting the invitation because she considered the activity amusing.[14]
The event demonstrated that her practical and serious public demeanor had not eliminated her willingness to join the eccentric activities of the other members of Victor's family.
A particularly important later development was the confirmation of Victoria's biological daughter with Victor.
During the gathering of the Imperial Princesses, a group of blonde daughters appeared together, including Silvia, Julia, Ekaterina, Alisa, and [[Svetlana Veronika Elderblood]]. The narration explicitly identified Svetlana Veronika as the daughter of Victor and Victoria.[1]
The girls possessed blonde hair and violet eyes, reflecting the combination of the Fulger bloodline with their father's supernatural characteristics. Svetlana Veronika was among the daughters described as resembling Victor more closely than some of her sisters.[1]
Her appearance establishes that Victoria possesses at least two known biological children: [[Tatsuya Rider]] from her earlier relationship with a Japanese Noble Vampire, and Svetlana Veronika Elderblood from her marriage to Victor.
The distinction is important because Tatsuya was born long before Victoria entered Victor's family and retained his identity as a Noble Vampire rather than automatically accepting the Draconic lineage of his mother's later husband. By the Imperial era, he had continued developing through his original Race and was eventually recognized among the strongest Noble Vampires.[34]
Victoria's later existence therefore combines two different generations of supernatural development. Tatsuya represents the independent Rider and Fulger lineage she established before joining Victor, while Svetlana Veronika belongs to the new generation of Imperial Princesses born through the union between the Fulger family and the Blood Dragon Emperor.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Victoria's current husband, the father of her daughter Svetlana Veronika, and one of the most important individuals responsible for her eventual reconciliation with the Fulger family.
Their relationship began indirectly through Tatsuya. Victor fought Victoria's son during the interrupted Rider-Horseman Game and developed considerable respect for his swordsmanship and supernatural abilities. Rather than treating Tatsuya as a permanent rival, Victor sought him out afterward and gradually established a friendship based upon their shared interest in combat and personal development.[20][25]
Victoria initially viewed Victor through the lens of the political circumstances surrounding [[Nightingale]]'s Noble Vampire society. He was an extraordinarily talented young Vampire whose relationships with the Snow, Fulger, and Scarlett families could significantly change the balance of power among the Clans.
As Natashia became increasingly devoted to Victor, Victoria was drawn into his household through her sister's efforts. The arrangement initially caused considerable embarrassment because she had previously occupied a relatively independent position and was unaccustomed to the extraordinary relationship structure surrounding him.[3]
Their relationship eventually became personal, and Victoria accepted membership in his family. Through the strengthening associated with Victor's Bloodline, she finally awakened the Lightning she had lacked throughout her life.[9]
However, Victor's greatest contribution to Victoria's emotional development occurred when he recognized the insecurity underlying her desire for Lightning. He understood that she had spent centuries attempting to obtain the recognition of a family that had once rejected her, and he explained that her place among them no longer depended upon proving she possessed the expected hereditary power.[4]
Victoria gradually came to recognize that she was already loved and accepted. Victor's reassurance allowed her to begin separating her current identity from the expectations that had governed her childhood.[4]
Their relationship subsequently developed into marriage. Victoria occasionally displayed embarrassment concerning the arrangement, particularly because she shared Victor with Natashia and Sasha, but she had accepted the marriage agreement and remained part of his household.[11][7]
The later birth of Svetlana Veronika Elderblood established a direct biological connection between their two lineages, giving Victoria a daughter with Victor in addition to Tatsuya from her earlier marriage.[1]

#### Annasthashia Elderblood Fulger
Natashia is Victoria's older sister and one of the most influential individuals in her life.
For much of Victoria's childhood, Natashia represented everything the [[Fulger Clan]] valued. Her overwhelming Lightning talent, physical strength, and status within the family contrasted sharply with Victoria's inability to manifest the defining hereditary power of their bloodline.[3]
The constant comparison damaged Victoria's self-image, even though Natashia herself did not regard her younger sister as worthless. The deterioration of their family during the period dominated by Natasha's alternate personality contributed further to Victoria's separation from Fulger.[3]
After Natashia regained control of her original personality, she began rebuilding the Clan and actively sought Victoria's assistance. She recognized that her sister possessed exceptional administrative and financial talent, potentially exceeding her own abilities in these areas.[8][3]
Natashia also understood the inferiority complex created by Victoria's upbringing. She confronted her sister about the expectations she placed upon Tatsuya and repeatedly attempted to make Victoria recognize her accomplishments independently of the Lightning she lacked.[3]
Her efforts to bring Victoria closer to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] were motivated partly by her desire to unite the Fulger family within his household and partly by her belief that her younger sister deserved the affection and recognition she had been denied.[3]
Their later relationship is considerably healthier, although Natashia's unpredictable behavior and enthusiasm for involving Victoria in increasingly unconventional family situations frequently cause Victoria embarrassment.

#### Natasha Elderblood Fulger
[[Natasha Elderblood Fulger]] originated as the alternate personality associated with Annasthashia Fulger and eventually developed into an independent individual.
For much of Victoria's life, the presence of Natasha complicated her relationship with her older sister because the original Natashia was not continuously in control of their shared existence.
The later separation of the personalities created circumstances in which Victoria could interact with both women individually rather than interpreting their behavior as the actions of a single person.
By the Imperial era, Natasha participated alongside Victoria, Carmila, Natashia, and Sasha in the Fulger family's administrative gatherings, reflecting her established position within the wider Elderblood household.[12]

#### Carmila Fulger Elderblood
[[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]] is Victoria's mother, the original matriarch of Clan Fulger, and the source of the Lightning bloodline that became the defining supernatural characteristic of her descendants.
Victoria's original inability to inherit Carmila's Lightning contributed significantly to her childhood trauma. The Fulger family's expectations centered upon the power inherited from its founder, and Victoria's absence of the ability caused other members to overlook her accomplishments.[3]
The circumstances were further complicated by Carmila's absence and the deterioration of the Clan's internal relationships, leaving Victoria without the security and guidance she needed during much of her development.
Carmila eventually returned to the family after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s intervention and restoration of her existence. Her reunion with Victoria and Sasha created an opportunity for the women to establish relationships that had previously been impossible because of the enormous period separating them.[35]
After Victoria's evolution into a True Blood Dragon, Carmila observed her mastery of Lightning and recognized that her daughter possessed extraordinary technical control. She considered Victoria the finest Lightning controller among the Fulger women, potentially surpassed only by Victor himself.[10]
The recognition affected Victoria deeply because she had spent much of her life believing that her inability to use Lightning made her inferior. Receiving praise from her mother for mastering the same power represented a profound reversal of the circumstances that had defined her childhood.[10]
Carmila's observation of Victoria's happiness also emphasized the emotional cost of her daughter's earlier rejection. She recognized that Victoria had rarely experienced such genuine satisfaction and that the family had failed to acknowledge her worth for an enormous portion of her life.[10]
Their later relationship became more affectionate, and the two women eventually participated together in the administration and family activities of the [[Elderblood Empire]].

#### Sasha Elderblood Fulger
[[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] is Victoria's niece, the daughter of Natashia, and later one of her fellow wives through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
Victoria has long been protective of Sasha, particularly because the young Vampire inherited extraordinary Lightning power and became the principal heir to the Fulger lineage.
During the Demonic War, Victoria and [[Hecate]] monitored Sasha's battle against Phoenix and his forces. Victoria was repeatedly astonished by the advanced [[Martial Arts]] her niece demonstrated, recognizing techniques developed by Carmila that even experienced Fulger members struggled to master.[13][30]
Despite her concern over the destruction of the territory's infrastructure, Victoria explicitly placed Sasha's safety above financial considerations. She was willing to accept catastrophic property losses rather than demand that her niece fight under restrictions that could endanger her life.[13]
After the battle, Victoria focused upon obtaining improved equipment for Sasha and Natashia, demonstrating that her concern extended beyond financial management into the protection of her family.[32]
Their relationship became more unusual after both women entered Victor's household. Victoria occasionally expressed embarrassment over sharing her husband with her niece, while Sasha regarded the arrangement considerably more casually.[7]
By the Imperial era, their relationship combined familial affection, shared membership in Clan Elderblood, and participation in the administration of the Fulger branch of Victor's Empire.

#### Tatsuya Rider
[[Tatsuya Rider]] is Victoria's biological son from her earlier marriage to an unnamed Japanese Noble Vampire.
Tatsuya possesses an unusual combination of supernatural abilities inherited from both parents. He acquired the Lightning of the Fulger lineage and the Illusion-related talent of his paternal family, becoming an exceptionally capable young Vampire whose abilities attracted [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s attention.[3][7]
Victoria regards her son with enormous affection and pride. During the period following her departure from Fulger, Tatsuya represented the family she had established independently of the Clan and became the principal focus of her hopes for the future.
However, her attachment was influenced by her unresolved inferiority complex. She wished for Tatsuya to possess the opportunities and recognition she had been denied, sometimes interpreting his accomplishments as proof that her own bloodline still possessed value despite her inability to manifest Lightning.[3]
Natashia eventually questioned the extent to which Victoria was projecting her own ambitions onto her son. Although Victoria insisted that she loved him as an individual, the conversation revealed that her expectations were not entirely separate from the emotional wounds created by her childhood.[3]
Tatsuya's participation in the Rider-Horseman Game introduced him to Victor, eventually creating a friendship between the two men. Victor respected Tatsuya's martial talent and willingness to improve through personal effort rather than depending exclusively upon inherited power.[20][25]
During the Demonic War, Tatsuya fought alongside Sasha against Phoenix and the invading forces, demonstrating that he was capable of operating against enemies far more dangerous than those normally encountered by Noble Vampires of his age.[31][30]
Victoria continued treating him as her child despite these accomplishments. She was particularly sensitive to discussions concerning [[Hecate]] and the possibility that Tatsuya might establish a family of his own, reflecting her possessive maternal instincts.[7]
By the later Imperial era, Tatsuya had become one of the strongest remaining Noble Vampires without undergoing Victor's Dragonization. His decision to continue developing through his original Race distinguished him from many members of the Elderblood family who had accepted the benefits of the Blood Dragon lineage.[34]

#### Svetlana Veronika Elderblood
[[Svetlana Veronika Elderblood]] is Victoria's biological daughter with [[Victor Elderblood]] and a member of the younger generation of Imperial Princesses.[1]
Her existence is explicitly confirmed during the gathering of Victor's daughters in Chapter 1165. Svetlana Veronika appeared alongside several other daughters originating from the Fulger lineage, including Silvia, Julia, Ekaterina, and Alisa.[1]
The daughters possessed blonde hair and violet eyes, reflecting the combination of their mothers' Fulger characteristics with Victor's supernatural lineage. Svetlana Veronika was among those described as resembling Victor particularly closely.[1]
Her name distinguishes her from [[Natasha Elderblood Fulger]] and the other women belonging to the Fulger family. She is Victoria's daughter rather than an alternate name for Natashia, Natasha, or another member of the main bloodline.
Svetlana Veronika also represents a different generation of Victoria's descendants from Tatsuya. Tatsuya was born before Victoria entered Victor's family and inherited the original Fulger and paternal Illusion bloodlines, while Svetlana Veronika was born through Victoria's later union with the Blood Dragon Emperor.
The confirmed chapter establishes her parentage and appearance, but does not provide a complete independent account of her personal Divine Concepts or combat abilities. Those characteristics should be indexed separately when supported by additional chapter evidence.

#### Hecate Moriarty
[[Hecate Moriarty]] is Victoria's contracted Witch, long-standing professional associate, and one of the most important individuals involved in the management of her businesses and Clan responsibilities.
[[Hecate]] accompanied Victoria during the period when she operated primarily through the Rider identity, providing supernatural assistance and administrative support.
Their relationship includes a considerable degree of familiarity. Hecate frequently questions Victoria's more aggressive commercial decisions, particularly when the businesswoman attempts to exploit politically sensitive events for financial profit.[5]
During the period surrounding the Rider-Horseman Game, Hecate helped investigate the security problems affecting Victoria's residence after the discovery of infiltrators and hostile observers.[26]
She subsequently assisted Victoria during the Demonic invasion of Fulger territory, employing observation and communication [[Magic]] to monitor the battlefield and maintain contact with Sasha and Tatsuya.[29][13]
Their cooperation was particularly valuable because Victoria could organize the territory's defenses while Hecate supplied the magical infrastructure necessary to monitor enemies and coordinate the movements of allied combatants.
By the period preceding Victoria's later training, Tatsuya and Hecate were trusted with the practical management of Clan affairs whenever Victoria was occupied elsewhere.[7]
Victoria also became increasingly sensitive toward their developing personal relationship, occasionally attempting to interfere because of her protectiveness toward Tatsuya.

#### Fulger Clan
[[Fulger Clan]] is Victoria's birth family and the organization responsible for both the greatest traumas and some of the most important accomplishments of her life.
Victoria originally left the Clan because of the discrimination associated with her inability to manifest Lightning. Her departure and creation of the Rider financial empire allowed her to establish an independent identity, but did not eliminate her desire to receive recognition from the family she had abandoned.[4]
After Natashia regained control of her original personality, Victoria returned to assist in rebuilding Fulger. Her financial knowledge and organizational talent became essential to its recovery, demonstrating that the abilities for which she had received little recognition were extraordinarily valuable.[8][3]
Her eventual awakening of Lightning and later development into one of its finest controllers completed an important reversal of the Clan's earlier judgment.
By the Imperial era, Victoria no longer existed primarily as an outsider attempting to prove herself. She had become one of the principal members of the Fulger family and participated in its administration alongside Carmila, Natashia, Natasha, and Sasha.[12]

### Role in the Elderblood Empire
Victoria possesses an important administrative position within the [[Elderblood Empire]] because of her experience developing financial organizations and managing the resources of one of [[Nightingale]]'s strongest Noble Vampire families.

#### Financial Administration
Victoria's original financial empire provided the foundation of her administrative expertise. Unlike individuals whose political position originated primarily from military strength or inherited Divine authority, she acquired influence through economic development, investments, organization, and the ability to recognize commercial opportunities.[5][6]
Her accomplishments demonstrate that financial power could rival political authority within the supernatural world. At one point, her independent wealth was considered comparable to or greater than that of some rulers whose supernatural status greatly exceeded her own.[6]
This experience eventually became important to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s growing civilization, where enormous resources needed to be distributed among military, technological, infrastructural, and administrative projects.

#### Noble Vampire Representation
Victoria was selected alongside Ruby and Kaguya to represent the Noble Vampires in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s developing faction government.
The arrangement recognized that each woman possessed professional responsibilities that might prevent her from attending every meeting. Rather than requiring a single representative to be constantly available, the faction established multiple candidates capable of acting in one another's absence.[11]
Victoria's selection was specifically justified by her record as the founder of the Rider financial empire and her tendency to make rational decisions. Her administrative judgment therefore became valuable to the faction independently of her supernatural combat abilities.

#### Clan Fulger Reconstruction
Victoria played an important role in restoring Clan Fulger after the decline of its former leadership and the political disruption caused by its loss of the Vampire Count title.
Her financial resources, commercial connections, and experience managing personnel allowed Natashia to concentrate upon political and military responsibilities while Victoria supported the reconstruction of the Clan's infrastructure.[8][3]
During the Demonic War, this role expanded to include emergency administration. Victoria coordinated evacuations, monitored the destruction of property, maintained communication with combatants, and helped organize the response to the invading forces.[29][13]
Her concern for financial losses did not prevent her from placing the safety of the Clan's members above its material assets.

#### Imperial Delegation
Victoria's later political philosophy emphasizes the importance of delegation.
During the Fulger women's Imperial gathering, she supported [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s decision to distribute responsibilities among the capable individuals serving the Empire. She recognized that its size and the extraordinary abilities of its administrators made personal micromanagement by the Emperor unnecessary.[12]
Her position reflected the same principles that allowed her to establish and manage the Rider financial empire. A functioning organization requires reliable subordinates, clear responsibilities, and systems that continue operating without demanding constant intervention from the individual at its center.
Victoria's greatest administrative strength therefore lies in her ability to apply practical organizational principles to a civilization whose supernatural scale far exceeds the societies within which she originally developed.

### Legacy
Victoria's life demonstrates that the hereditary abilities valued by a supernatural family do not necessarily determine the ultimate potential of its members.
She was born into the main Fulger bloodline without the Lightning power that defined its social standing, causing relatives to regard her as inferior despite her many other talents. Rather than accept a life determined by those expectations, she abandoned the Clan and established a financial empire through her own intelligence and effort.[3][4]
Her success under the Rider name demonstrated that extraordinary influence could be acquired through finance, organization, and political judgment rather than personal combat power alone.
Her return to Fulger subsequently revealed that these accomplishments were essential to the Clan's reconstruction. Natashia recognized that Victoria possessed administrative talent potentially superior to her own, while the family's recovery depended heavily upon the abilities Victoria had developed during their separation.[3]
The awakening of Lightning represented an important personal achievement, but the circumstances surrounding it also changed her understanding of the acceptance she had spent centuries seeking. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] recognized that Victoria no longer needed to acquire the ability in order to belong to her family, allowing her to begin releasing the bitterness accumulated through her earlier experiences.[9][4]
Her later True Dragon transformation completed a remarkable reversal of the Fulger family's expectations. Because she had been forced to master basic Vampiric movement without Lightning, Victoria eventually developed a level of elemental control that Carmila considered superior to that of the other principal Fulger women.[10]
The trait originally used to define her as defective had therefore contributed indirectly to the development of her greatest individual supernatural advantage.
Victoria's legacy also extends through her children. Tatsuya represents the independent family and lineage she established before entering Victor's household, while Svetlana Veronika belongs to the later generation born through the union between the Fulger bloodline and the Blood Dragon Emperor.[7][1]
By the mature Imperial era, Victoria has become one of the most accomplished administrators and refined Lightning users within Victor's family. Her achievements reflect the value of abilities cultivated through experience, personal discipline, and independence rather than relying exclusively upon extraordinary inherited power.

### Abilities

#### Skills and Abilities
Victoria Fulger Elderblood is a True Blood Dragon, a Dragon Goddess, one of the most accomplished Lightning users of the [[Fulger Clan]], and an exceptionally skilled administrator who independently established the Rider financial empire. Her supernatural abilities are particularly unusual because she spent most of her existence without access to the hereditary Lightning power of her family. Rather than relying upon elemental abilities, Victoria developed the basic powers of a Noble Vampire to an exceptional degree, acquiring a level of physical control and movement proficiency that later became the foundation of her advanced Lightning techniques.[1][2][3]
Victoria's original condition was exceptional even among Noble Vampires. The Fulger family's bloodline descended from [[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]], an ancient Lightning Spirit transformed into a Vampire. Ordinarily, descendants of the main family inherited the ability to generate and manipulate Lightning, but Victoria did not awaken any elemental power during her early life. She consequently relied upon her physical abilities, basic Vampiric techniques, experience, and intelligence rather than the overwhelming elemental speed traditionally associated with her relatives.[1][4]
Her supernatural development changed after she became part of [[Victor Elderblood]]'s family. The strengthening produced through his Bloodline finally awakened Victoria's dormant Fulger Lightning, allowing her to combine the elemental power of her ancestors with centuries of refined physical technique. Unlike relatives who had used Lightning since childhood, Victoria possessed exceptional control over the movement of her own body before obtaining the element. This unusual foundation became her greatest advantage once she began developing Lightning-based movement.[5][3]
Victoria subsequently received Lightning-related knowledge and memories belonging to [[Zeus]], transferred to her by Victor. This provided an additional theoretical foundation encompassing the experiences of one of the strongest Lightning Gods of the [[Greek Pantheon]]. She later underwent Dragonization and became a genuine True Blood Dragon, acquiring superior racial characteristics while her [[Fulger Bloodline]] evolved toward the elemental nature of Carmila's original Lightning Spirit existence.[6][7][3]
Following her evolution, Victoria could transform her physical body into pure Lightning Energy while retaining the fundamental existence of a True Dragon. Her new abilities included improved metamorphosis, an exceptionally strong Draconic body, greater compatibility with Creation, and an enormous enhancement to her existing elemental abilities. However, her defining specialty remained Lightning Control. Carmila recognized that Victoria possessed the finest control among the principal Fulger women, distinguishing her technical refinement from Sasha's greater raw Lightning output and Carmila's own superior experience.[3][8]
Victoria's greatest demonstrated combat advantages arise from her combination of Lightning, precise movement, exceptional physical fundamentals, and the advanced racial foundation of a True Dragon. Her experience as an administrator also provides substantial non-combat abilities involving leadership, financial management, supernatural contracts, political negotiation, and the organization of extremely large institutions.[9]
