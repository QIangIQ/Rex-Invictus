#### Debut
Chapter 72: A Little Girl of Few Words
You will always be my father, even after I become your wife.
—Ophis to [[Victor Elderblood]], Chapter 1187
Ophis Elderblood, formerly Ophis Tepes, is the biological daughter of [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]], the First Vampire Progenitor and former King of [[Nightingale]], and [[Otsuki Hana]], a Nine-Tailed Kitsune of the Otsuki lineage. Originally the Third Princess of Nightingale, Ophis became the adopted daughter of Victor Elderblood after forming an unusually strong attachment to him during their first meeting in the Royal Capital. She is one of the oldest recognized daughters of Victor's household, the closest companion of [[Nero Elderblood]], a Blood Dragon, an Eldritch Goddess associated with Infinity, and one of the principal commanders of the Fifth Imperial Legion, [[The Imperial Guard]]. After reaching adulthood and experiencing an immense period of accelerated development, she also entered a marital relationship with Victor, while continuing to regard him as the father who had raised and protected her.[2][3][4][5]
Ophis was born with an extraordinary inheritance that distinguished her from nearly every other member of the Tepes Royal Family. She possessed approximately fifty percent of Vlad's [[Sanguis Noctis Regis]], the Blood of the Night King, and part of the unique spiritual foundation required for a genuine Vampire Progenitor. Although these characteristics did not make her a complete Progenitor, they granted her exceptional potential and established her as a possible natural successor to Vlad's position. Her mother's Nine-Tailed Kitsune Bloodline also survived within her, but its active expression was overwhelmingly suppressed by the dominance of her paternal Progenitor Blood.[6][7][8]
Despite being a Princess of the most powerful Vampire family in Nightingale, Ophis' childhood was defined by loneliness and the absence of consistent parental affection. Hana had died when Ophis was extremely young, while Vlad's grief and obsession with revenge made him increasingly distant from his surviving children. Ophis was heavily guarded because of her unusual Bloodline and royal status, but protection could not replace the affection and companionship she desired. Her first encounter with Victor consequently became a defining moment: rather than treating her as a politically important Royal Princess, Victor carried her through the Capital, bought her presents and accepted her company without expecting anything in return. Ophis began calling him Father almost immediately, establishing a relationship that neither the Vampire King's authority nor the subsequent transformations of their family would undo.[9][10][11]
Her early years within Victor's family were marked by a traumatic incident in Japan, where an uncontrolled attempt to teleport toward Victor left her stranded far from home. There she encountered [[Nero]], a persecuted Hybrid who repeatedly risked her life protecting Ophis from the supernatural factions attempting to capture them. Their escape ended in the event known as the Crimson Nightmare, during which Ophis was decapitated and Nero sustained life-threatening injuries. Victor's arrival, Ophis' recovery and his subsequent treatment of Nero transformed the connection between the two girls into one of the most enduring relationships within the Elderblood family.[12][13][14]
Ophis' supernatural development eventually moved beyond her original status as a Noble Vampire. Victor created artifacts that allowed her to access the dormant Nine-Tailed Kitsune heritage inherited from Hana, while his later transformation of the family into genuine [[Blood Dragons]] provided a stronger existence capable of supporting the extraordinary Progenitor and Youkai characteristics she already possessed. Her greatest transformation occurred during the awakening of her personal Divinity, Infinity. When the native Primordial Infinity attempted to establish itself as the source of her developing Divine power, Victor intervened and replaced that influence with Energy from his own Nightmare existence. The result was an Eldritch expression of Infinity originating from Victor rather than the ordinary native Primordial system.[15][3]
The enormous periods of accelerated development experienced by the [[Elderblood Empire]] transformed Ophis from a young Princess dependent upon the protection of others into a mature Dragon Goddess, experienced Imperial commander and one of the senior members of Victor's second generation. Nevertheless, she remains personally uninterested in warfare for its own sake. Although she possesses extraordinary combat talent and continues training to protect herself and her family, Ophis prefers peaceful activities, her responsibilities within the Imperial household and the company of Victor and Nero over the pursuit of increasingly dangerous battles.[16][5]

### Physical Appearance

#### Noble Vampire
Ophis during her childhood as the Third Princess of [[Nightingale]].
During her childhood, Ophis possessed the appearance of an extraordinarily beautiful girl approximately five years old, with a small body standing around 120 centimeters tall. Her skin was pale white, her eyes possessed a distinctive violet-purple coloration, and her long black hair framed a delicate face whose expression was generally calm or nearly emotionless. She frequently wore black Gothic dresses, establishing the dark and elegant clothing preferences she would maintain throughout her later development.[2][9]
Her restrained expression and unusually economical speech made Ophis appear innocent and harmless, but her small body concealed the abnormal supernatural characteristics inherited from Vlad. The enormous concentration of Progenitor Blood placed considerable demands upon her immature physiology, making ordinary physical contact potentially dangerous for individuals who were not prepared for the abilities she could activate without understanding them. Her appearance therefore contrasted sharply with the extraordinary Bloodline and spiritual potential contained within her body.[6][16]
Ophis' behavior also contributed to the impression she created. Rather than maintaining the elaborate public presentation expected from a Princess, she often moved quietly through the Royal Capital, taking the hands of people she liked or allowing herself to be carried without displaying concern for the political consequences. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] frequently carried her on his shoulder or allowed her to sit on his lap, a practice that became closely associated with their relationship during the early volumes.[9]
Despite acquiring additional clothing during her outings with Victor, Ophis retained her preference for Gothic dresses. Even after purchasing numerous new outfits, she continued wearing the familiar dark clothing she favored, demonstrating that her aesthetic preferences were already firmly established during childhood.[9]

#### Blood Dragon
Ophis following her transformation into a Blood Dragon.
Ophis' transformation into a genuine Blood Dragon changed her physical and spiritual existence while preserving many of the characteristics associated with her former Vampire appearance. Two black Dragon horns emerged from her head, and her previously violet eyes developed the blood-red coloration and narrow reptilian pupils associated with her new Draconic physiology. Her body also grew taller, although her long black hair, pale skin and preference for dark Gothic clothing remained recognizable.[15]
The transformation was considerably more significant than the appearance of horns or the alteration of her eyes. Ophis acquired a genuine True Dragon body capable of supporting the abnormal quantity of Progenitor power she had inherited from Vlad. The overwhelming racial imbalance that once placed her immature body under considerable strain became substantially easier to regulate through the superior Draconic foundation. Her maternal Kitsune characteristics also remained part of her existence instead of being erased by the transformation.[15][16]
Ophis continued wearing clothing that reflected her comparatively reserved personality. Unlike [[Nero]], who eventually favored athletic clothing that displayed her developed physique, Ophis generally preferred dresses, loose clothing and dark outfits covering most of her body. Even during combat training, she could be seen wearing a black tracksuit rather than the more revealing athletic clothing chosen by her sister.[16]
Her natural expression remained comparatively restrained, but the maturity of her new body and the predatory characteristics associated with her Dragon nature made her substantially more imposing than the child she had once been. When she allowed her Draconic features to become more pronounced, individuals who remembered her from childhood could recognize the enormous difference between the vulnerable girl of the past and the powerful Dragon Goddess she had become.[17]

#### Eldritch Goddess
Ophis underwent her most dramatic visible maturation during the awakening of her Divinity of Infinity. As the supernatural Energy associated with her developing Godhood gathered around her, her body rapidly developed into that of an adult woman approximately 185 centimeters tall. Her physique became fuller and more mature while preserving the dark-haired, pale-skinned appearance associated with her original Vampire identity.[3]
Kali observed that Ophis' appearance differed considerably from [[Nero]]'s simultaneous transformation. Whereas Nero developed a more athletic and warrior-like physique, Ophis retained the softer and more elegant characteristics associated with her Gothic appearance. The contrast reflected the different personalities and supernatural paths of the two women rather than a difference in their importance within the Elderblood family.[3]
During this transformation, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] also altered the nature of Ophis' emerging Divine power, replacing the connection to the native Primordial Infinity with Energy from his own Nightmare existence. The resulting Eldritch Divinity changed her supernatural foundation rather than merely producing an external physical transformation.[3]
Her hair continued growing throughout the accelerated Imperial era until it reached approximately her ankles, while she maintained a preference for floor-length Gothic dresses. Even after acquiring the appearance and authority of a mature Dragon Goddess, Ophis continued favoring the same general clothing style she had worn as a child.[18]
Ophis can also reduce her apparent physical size to resemble the child she once was. She uses this ability for personal reasons, particularly when she wants to sit upon Victor's shoulder and recreate the affectionate treatment she received during their earliest years together. The transformation demonstrates that her adult appearance is not an absolute limitation imposed upon her present Draconic existence.[19]

### Personality
Ophis is naturally quiet, observant, emotionally straightforward and strongly attached to the people she regards as family. During childhood, she rarely spoke more than necessary, frequently responding through single words, short statements or small physical gestures. Her reserved behavior was not an indication of limited intelligence; Ophis was unusually perceptive even as a child and could learn rapidly from the individuals surrounding her. Her silence instead reflected a combination of natural temperament, emotional isolation and a preference for expressing affection through direct actions rather than elaborate conversation.[2][9]
Her strongest defining characteristic is her attachment to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Ophis was born into the most politically powerful family of [[Nightingale]], but her childhood lacked the consistent parental presence she desired. Hana's death removed her primary maternal figure, while Vlad's grief and political responsibilities created considerable emotional distance between him and his youngest daughter. Victor's willingness to treat her as an ordinary child rather than a politically valuable Princess therefore became extraordinarily important to her. She rapidly began referring to him as Father and developed the belief that remaining beside him was the safest and happiest place she could be.[2][10]
Ophis is openly possessive of Victor's attention, but her possessiveness generally expresses itself through simple, almost childlike emotional reasoning rather than elaborate manipulation. She prefers sitting beside him, being carried, receiving affectionate gestures and accompanying him whenever possible. During an argument with [[Nero]] concerning the value of their Mothers and Father, Ophis concluded that Mothers were numerous while Father was unique, making Victor obviously more valuable to remain beside. The reasoning was deliberately simplistic, but it reflected the extraordinary significance she attached to the person who had consistently provided the affection missing from much of her childhood.[20]
Her attachment deepened considerably following the incident in Japan. Ophis had attempted to reach Victor through her teleportation because she associated his presence with safety, but her undeveloped control instead placed her in an unfamiliar country surrounded by dangerous supernatural beings. The resulting pursuit forced her to confront the limitations of her enormous inherited potential. She possessed Bloodline characteristics that should have placed her far above most of her pursuers, yet she lacked the experience and control necessary to use them effectively under pressure. As Nero accumulated injuries protecting her, Ophis became increasingly distressed by her inability to help.[12][21][13]
Her eventual decapitation created one of the most traumatic experiences of her childhood. Although her extraordinary physiology allowed Victor to restore her body, the pain and helplessness were unlike anything she had previously experienced. When Victor arrived, Ophis immediately became calmer through his presence, while her refusal to release him afterward demonstrated how profoundly the experience had affected her. The incident intensified her fear of separation and reinforced her certainty that Victor was someone who would always come to protect her.[13][14]
Ophis' personality is not exclusively dependent upon Victor, however. Her relationship with Nero became another emotional foundation of her life. Nero had repeatedly protected her despite possessing no obligation to do so, eventually suffering severe injuries and nearly dying during the Crimson Nightmare. Ophis recognized that loyalty and formed a bond with Nero that survived their adoption into the same family, their transformation into Dragons and the enormous periods of accelerated development that followed. The two became inseparable companions whose differing personalities created a natural balance between Nero's pride and combative temperament and Ophis' quieter, more openly affectionate disposition.[13][16]
The death of Hana remains another fundamental influence upon Ophis' personality. Because she was extremely young when her mother died, Ophis retained powerful emotional impressions without possessing the maturity necessary to understand the circumstances surrounding the loss. This explains why she instinctively associated Haruna with Hana during their first encounter and why Victor's later artificial recreation of her mother produced such an intense reaction. Even after centuries of development and the acquisition of Divine power, Ophis discovered that she had never fully processed the grief created by Hana's absence.[22][17]
Ophis' relationship with Vlad is considerably more complicated. She recognizes him as her biological father and retains affection for him, but she also understands that his prolonged emotional absence harmed their relationship. As she matured, Ophis became increasingly willing to criticize his decisions and express disappointment concerning his family life. She did not regard Victor's parental role as evidence that Vlad had ceased being her father, but she refused to treat their biological relationship as sufficient compensation for years of inconsistent attention.[23][19]
Her adulthood brought considerable emotional and intellectual development. Although Ophis continued speaking relatively little compared with many of her sisters, she became more capable of following Imperial affairs and understanding the political responsibilities associated with Victor's civilization. She learned to recognize when members of the younger generation were attempting to obtain positions or responsibilities within the Empire, often understanding their motives more quickly than Nero because she paid closer attention to administrative reports.[16]
Ophis also developed a surprisingly sophisticated understanding of combat. Despite her naturally quiet disposition, she learned to combine [[Martial Arts]], teleportation, Draconic movement and other supernatural abilities in ways that could surprise an opponent familiar with her usual behavior. During a training match with Nero, Ophis deliberately encouraged her sister to anticipate a conventional Dragon Breath before appearing behind her and attacking from an unexpected position. Her victory demonstrated that she had learned to use positioning, deception and tactical creativity rather than depending exclusively upon the enormous powers inherited from her parents.[16]
Nevertheless, combat is not something Ophis particularly enjoys. Unlike Victor, [[Scathach]], Nero, Siren and Gina, she does not regard increasingly dangerous battles as a personal source of excitement. She trains because she understands the importance of protecting herself and her family, not because she wishes to spend her life fighting. By the later Imperial era, Ophis openly stated that she did not intend to participate voluntarily in the coming war with the Upper Sectors unless circumstances or Victor gave her a compelling reason to reconsider.[5]
Her mature personality also retains a mischievous side. During Victor's lessons to his daughters, Ophis deliberately shrank herself into her childhood appearance and climbed onto his shoulder, exploiting the same paternal weakness that had allowed her to monopolize his attention during their earliest years together. Her sisters immediately recognized the effectiveness of the tactic and became jealous that they had not considered doing the same thing. Ophis was entirely satisfied with the result, demonstrating that centuries of maturity had not eliminated her willingness to seek affection through simple and direct methods.[19]
Ophis also possesses a practical understanding of the responsibilities associated with parenthood, shaped strongly by her own experiences as Vlad's daughter. When she briefly considered having a child because she wanted a younger version of herself, Victor reminded her that raising an exceptionally powerful Dragon child required enormous commitment and careful supervision. Ophis considered the difficulties experienced by the Mothers of her younger sisters and immediately recognized that her desire was insufficient. She chose to postpone motherhood until she was better prepared rather than repeat the neglect that had characterized much of her own childhood.[5]
Despite her Divine status, enormous experience and later marriage to Victor, Ophis continues to regard him as her Father. She makes the distinction explicitly during their private conversation in Chapter 1187, explaining that their new relationship has not erased the personal bond through which she originally entered his life. This continuity is one of the defining aspects of her characterization: her circumstances, powers and responsibilities change enormously, but the emotional connection formed when she first took Victor's hand in the Royal Capital remains central to her identity.[5]

### Background
Ophis was born into the Tepes Royal Family as the daughter of [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]], the First Vampire Progenitor, and [[Otsuki Hana]], a Nine-Tailed Kitsune originating from the Otsuki lineage. Her birth combined two extraordinarily powerful supernatural heritages, although their expression within her body was highly unequal. The dominant inheritance originated from Vlad, whose [[Sanguis Noctis Regis]] manifested in Ophis at a concentration of approximately fifty percent. The active portion of Hana's Kitsune Bloodline was comparatively small, leaving most of the maternal characteristics dormant beneath the overwhelming Progenitor foundation.[6][8]
Ophis' unusual inheritance made her fundamentally different from Vlad's other children. Although the descendants of a Progenitor could inherit considerable talent, the complete Blood and Soul structure associated with the position was normally unique. Ophis nevertheless possessed a substantial portion of the relevant foundation, making her an abnormal exception even among the Royal Family. Her existence represented a potential continuation of Vlad's racial position rather than merely another member of the Tepes bloodline.[24][7]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later confirmed that her Soul possessed traces of the structure associated with a genuine Vampire Progenitor. He considered developing that foundation until Ophis could become a complete Progenitor, but discovered that an external restriction prevented the transformation. He attributed the obstacle to the rules maintained through Balance, which normally permitted only one natural Progenitor of a particular race during a given era. Because Vlad remained alive and continued occupying that position, Ophis could not simply mature into a second simultaneous native Vampire Progenitor through the same mechanism.[7]
Her exceptional potential did not guarantee a happy childhood. Hana died while Ophis was still extremely young, and the loss profoundly affected Vlad. His grief contributed to an increasingly obsessive desire for revenge and a gradual withdrawal from his surviving children. Although he continued providing protection and material resources, he struggled to maintain the affectionate parental presence Ophis needed. Her royal status and unusual Bloodline also required extensive security, creating an environment where she was constantly guarded but often emotionally isolated.[11]
Ophis consequently developed a habit of escaping or wandering beyond the boundaries established by her guardians. Her actions were not primarily motivated by political rebellion or a desire to challenge Vlad's authority. She wanted companionship, freedom and opportunities to experience the world beyond the protection surrounding her. This habit eventually brought her into contact with Victor, whose treatment of her transformed the direction of her life.[2][9]

### Synopsis

#### Vampire Society
Ophis first became directly involved with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s life when he visited the Royal Capital of [[Nightingale]]. While exploring the city, Victor encountered a small black-haired girl with violet eyes who approached him without displaying the hesitation normally expected from a Royal Princess surrounded by strangers. Rather than treating her as a political figure, Victor responded with curiosity and allowed the child to accompany him. Ophis quickly took his hand and developed an attachment to him despite knowing almost nothing about his identity.[2]
Victor subsequently took Ophis shopping and allowed her to choose clothing and other items. He carried her through the Capital and treated her with the casual affection he would have shown an ordinary daughter. His companions recognized that the situation was far more politically complicated than he seemed willing to acknowledge, particularly once the child's identity became apparent. Ophis was not simply a wandering Noble Vampire; she was the youngest daughter of [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]], ruler of Nightingale and the First Vampire Progenitor.[2][9]
Learning her identity did not cause Victor to change his behavior. He continued allowing Ophis to remain beside him and accepted the increasingly affectionate way she addressed him. Ophis responded by treating him as her Father, a decision that placed Victor in an unusual position within the politics of the Royal Family. Elizabeth and the guards responsible for the Princess were understandably concerned by the developing relationship, but Victor refused to distance himself from a lonely child merely because her father was a powerful monarch.[9]
During one of their early encounters, Ophis bit Victor and established a supernatural Mark associated with her unusual inheritance. The connection allowed her to recognize and sense his presence, eventually becoming important during her later attempt to reach him through teleportation. Although the Mark initially appeared as an expression of childish affection, it demonstrated that Ophis possessed supernatural characteristics far beyond those ordinarily expected from a young Noble Vampire.[9][16]
Ophis' attachment became increasingly public during the Rider–Horseman competition, where she comfortably occupied Victor's lap in front of members of Nightingale's nobility. The spectacle further complicated the already tense political environment surrounding Victor, whose rapid rise within Vampire Society had attracted the attention of numerous powerful Clans. Nevertheless, Ophis displayed little concern for the reaction of the aristocracy, regarding Victor's attention as more important than the conventions expected from a Princess.[25][26]
The relationship became impossible to ignore during Victor's confrontation with Vlad. Ophis appeared before the King and openly referred to Victor as Father, creating an awkward situation because the individual she was addressing was not merely another member of the Royal Family but a newly rising Vampire whose political ambitions could eventually challenge Vlad's position. Victor continued treating her affection as genuine rather than attempting to exploit it against the King.[10]
Vlad subsequently explained the nature of [[Sanguis Noctis Regis]], revealing the significance of the Blood shared by himself and Victor. The discussion also provided important context for Ophis' unusual characteristics, since the girl possessed approximately fifty percent of Vlad's Progenitor Blood. The inheritance helped explain why she instinctively recognized Victor as someone whose supernatural nature resembled her father's despite the absence of an ordinary biological relationship between them.[6]
By the conclusion of Victor's first major visit to Nightingale, Ophis had formed a relationship that would remain central throughout the story. Although she continued belonging formally to the Tepes Royal Family, she regarded Victor as another father and repeatedly sought opportunities to remain near him. Victor accepted the responsibility accompanying that attachment rather than treating the relationship as a temporary childhood fascination.[10]

#### A New Pillar
Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s recognition as the newly appointed Vampire Count, Ophis remained closely connected to him despite the growing political complications surrounding his position. Her affection had not diminished during his absence from [[Nightingale]], and she continued approaching him with the same enthusiasm whenever he returned.[27]
During the conflict surrounding Natashia and the Horsemen, Ophis recognized Victor's arrival and loudly announced his presence by calling out to her Father. Her behavior reflected the fact that she did not distinguish between his increasingly imposing supernatural reputation and the affectionate individual who had accompanied her through the Royal Capital. To the other members of the Vampire aristocracy, Victor had become a rapidly developing political and military threat; to Ophis, he remained the person she wanted to carry her and spend time with her.[27]
Ophis also continued feeding upon Victor's Blood and occupying his lap whenever circumstances permitted. These interactions reinforced the unusual connection between their supernatural existences, while Victor openly referred to her as his daughter regardless of whether Vlad approved of the arrangement.[28]
Her relationship with Victor became one of the personal ties connecting the Tepes family with the increasingly independent Alucard faction. Although Ophis did not actively participate in the political disagreements surrounding the two Progenitors, her attachment meant that neither side could approach the situation as an ordinary conflict between rival Vampire authorities.[28]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Ophis' desire to remain beside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually caused one of the most dangerous incidents of her childhood. After obtaining permission to visit him, she attempted to use the supernatural Mark previously placed upon Victor as a destination for teleportation. However, her immature control could not reliably reproduce the precision required for such a journey. Instead of arriving beside Victor, she emerged in Tokyo, Japan, far from [[Nightingale]] and without the protection of her Royal guardians.[11][12]
Her unexpected arrival brought her into contact with [[Nero]], a young Hybrid whose life had been shaped by persecution and dangerous supernatural experiments. Nero initially regarded the unfamiliar Vampire child with suspicion, but her attitude changed after discovering that Ophis considered Victor Alucard her Father. Nero already possessed a connection to Victor through his earlier intervention in her life, giving her a reason to protect the stranded Princess while attempting to understand how she had arrived in Japan.[12]

The confrontation culminated in the incident known as the Crimson Nightmare. Victor then turned his attention toward Nero, recognizing that she was close to death because of the injuries and supernatural substances affecting her Hybrid body. Ophis watched the process while remaining close to Victor, trusting his assurance that he was helping the girl who had spent the previous week protecting her.[13]
The arrival of Victor's companions and the subsequent confrontation transformed the balance of power in Japan. The supernatural factions that had pursued Ophis and Nero became targets of Victor's retaliation, while the two girls were removed from immediate danger. Victor's reaction demonstrated that he considered the injury of his daughter an unforgivable attack upon his family rather than a political incident that could be resolved through ordinary compensation.[13][14]
Once Ophis had recovered, Victor refused to force her to return immediately to Vlad. The girl had spent days believing she might never escape and continued clinging to Victor because she feared another separation. He considered her emotional condition more important than a mechanical application of royal custody, deciding that she could remain with him until she was prepared to confront the circumstances of her return.[14]
The incident also transformed Nero's future. Her willingness to defend Ophis despite overwhelming danger had already created an extraordinary bond between the two girls. Victor's intervention removed the limitations imposed by her previous Hybrid condition and provided the foundation for her eventual incorporation into his family. Ophis welcomed Nero's presence, and their relationship developed from a temporary partnership between two endangered children into permanent sisterhood.[13][31]
When the surviving members of the Tepes Royal Family attempted to resolve Ophis' absence, Victor continued insisting that the girl's wishes mattered. Alexios eventually returned to Nightingale without immediately recovering the Princess, while Vlad was forced to acknowledge that Ophis had chosen to remain beside Victor after the trauma she had experienced.[32]
The Japanese incident permanently changed Ophis' position within the story. She was no longer merely the Third Princess of Nightingale who occasionally visited Victor; she had become an integral part of his developing household, with Nero emerging as the sister whose loyalty had been proven during one of the most dangerous periods of her life.[13][14]

Ophis' physical development continued throughout this period as her body matured beyond its earlier childhood limitations. #### Dragon God of Chaos
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ascension and the development of his Draconic powers provided new opportunities for strengthening his daughters. Recognizing that Ophis' maternal Kitsune inheritance remained almost entirely dormant beneath Vlad's overwhelming Progenitor Blood, Victor created a pair of special black gloves intended to awaken and support the suppressed characteristics.[8]
Unlike an ordinary weapon, the gloves were constructed through a combination of Victor's Soul Power, Draconic Runes and control over Creation. When Ophis wore them, red Magical Circles appeared upon her hands and her pupils narrowed like those of a fox. She instinctively manifested a Fire attack, demonstrating that the artifacts had successfully established access to a portion of the power inherited from Hana.[8]
The transformation continued as a dark mantle enveloped Ophis and two fox ears appeared upon her head. Nine tails composed of dark supernatural Energy emerged behind her, creating a manifestation associated with the Nine-Tailed Kitsune lineage. [[Nero]] admired the appearance, while Ophis immediately recognized the emotional significance of the gift and asked Victor whether the power belonged to her mother.[8]
Victor confirmed that the gloves were intended to help Ophis access Hana's heritage, explaining that the dormant portion of her Kitsune Bloodline had never been erased despite its extremely weak active expression. He also explained that the artifacts possessed additional functions Ophis would discover as she developed greater understanding and control.[8]
The gift affected Ophis more profoundly than its practical value suggested. Her mother had died before she could develop ordinary memories of their relationship, and much of Ophis' childhood had been shaped by the absence left behind. The gloves provided a tangible connection to Hana's supernatural heritage, allowing Ophis to experience a characteristic that had previously remained inaccessible within her own body. She thanked Victor sincerely and decided to seek further instruction from Haruna, whose experience with the Nine-Tailed Kitsune lineage made her a natural teacher.[8]
Victor also bestowed Blessings associated with Nature, War and Blood upon Ophis. The Blood Blessing was intended in part to help her understand the unusual Progenitor inheritance received from Vlad, while the other Blessings supported her continued development through Victor's increasingly complex Divine and Draconic existence.[8]
Ophis subsequently accompanied Victor, Nero, [[Metis]] and Ariel into the [[Biblical Pantheon]]'s Heaven during his meeting with the Heavenly Father. The journey exposed the daughters to another powerful supernatural civilization whose structure and Divine foundations differed from those of [[Nightingale]] and the developing [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]].[40]
The journey also illustrated Ophis' temperament. Rather than being impressed indefinitely by the grandeur of Heaven, she quickly became bored by the endless clouds and comparatively uniform environment. Her reactions contrasted with Ariel's attempts to maintain the dignity and formality expected from an Angel associated with the Biblical Pantheon.[41]
The development of Ophis' maternal characteristics became important again when Haruna encountered her after the awakening of the previously dormant Kitsune powers. Haruna immediately recognized the supernatural presence associated with the Otsuki lineage and was surprised because Ophis had not previously displayed the same manifestation. The encounter confirmed that Victor's intervention had genuinely restored access to aspects of Hana's inheritance rather than merely producing an unrelated visual effect.[42]
During this encounter, Ophis and Nero also recognized members of the Youkai factions associated with their pursuit in Japan. Their hostile reaction demonstrated that the experiences of the Crimson Nightmare remained emotionally significant despite their subsequent growth. Natalia intervened before the confrontation escalated, reminding the girls that the individuals before them were not necessarily responsible for the crimes committed by the original pursuers.[42]
The narration described Ophis and Nero as capable of confronting a Low-Level Combat God through the combined effects of Victor's training, his nourishing Blood and the Divine-quality artifacts they possessed. This was a statement concerning their combat capability at that point rather than proof that either girl had already completed the process of becoming a genuine God of that classification.[42]
The introduction of additional daughters also influenced Ophis' position within the family. When [[Yog-Sothoth]] appeared as another daughter connected to Victor's extraordinary Eldritch existence, Ophis and Nero reacted with jealousy rather than concentrating upon the cosmic significance of the newcomer. Their response reflected a familiar concern: every new sister represented another individual who might compete for the affection of the Father they wished to keep close.[43]
By the conclusion of this period, Ophis had become increasingly comfortable with her unusual inheritance and her place within the expanding Elderblood family. Her connection to Vlad, Hana and Victor remained distinct, but she no longer needed to view those relationships as mutually exclusive sources of identity.[8][42]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Following the transformation of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family into genuine [[Blood Dragons]], Ophis possessed a body and Soul capable of supporting the exceptional supernatural characteristics inherited from her parents. The superior Draconic foundation helped resolve the difficulties previously caused by the enormous concentration of Progenitor Blood within her immature Vampire body, allowing her to regulate powers that had once activated without sufficient control.[15][16]
Victor subsequently turned his attention toward the Divine development of Ophis and [[Nero]]. Although both daughters possessed extraordinary racial potential, he recognized that the acquisition of a genuine Divinity required more than the accumulation of Energy or the inheritance of powerful techniques. Jeanne assisted in explaining the relationship between a God and the Concept they sought to embody, emphasizing that true Divine advancement required personal understanding of the supernatural principle involved.[44][45]
Victor then constructed artificial Realities intended to challenge the emotional foundations of his daughters. Rather than placing them exclusively against stronger physical opponents, he designed circumstances in which their memories, attachments and unresolved fears would force them to confront questions they had previously avoided. Ophis' trial centered upon the loss of her biological mother.[46]
Within the constructed world, Ophis encountered a living representation of [[Otsuki Hana]], together with a younger version of Vlad. Despite possessing centuries of development and the extraordinary power of a Dragon Goddess, Ophis reacted emotionally to the sight of the mother she had lost as a child. The reunion exposed grief that had remained unresolved beneath the confidence acquired through her later growth.[47][17]
Hana initially struggled to reconcile the adult Dragon Goddess before her with the daughter she remembered. From Hana's perspective, Ophis was still a small child who needed protection, and the revelation that she had become a Dragon was almost impossible to accept. Vlad likewise found the situation confusing because the woman before him possessed the appearance and supernatural power of an adult who had clearly experienced a future far beyond the period represented by the artificial Reality.[17]
Ophis eventually explained that Hana would die in the future and that Victor had become her Father afterward. Vlad immediately recognized how the loss of Hana could transform his own behavior, understanding that his obsession with revenge might cause him to neglect the surviving children who needed his attention. The realization allowed Ophis to see the circumstances surrounding her father's emotional withdrawal from another perspective, although understanding his grief did not erase the pain created by his absence.[17]
The reunion also allowed Ophis to speak openly about her feelings toward Hana. She acknowledged that she had missed her mother throughout her life, despite being too young at the time of the death to understand what had happened. Hana responded with affection and grief, while Ophis reassured her that Victor possessed the extraordinary power necessary to pursue a future in which a genuine reunion might eventually become possible.[17]
Ophis recognized that the world surrounding them was an artificial Reality rather than the ordinary past. She nevertheless valued the opportunity to understand her mother more fully and to experience a conversation that had been impossible during her actual childhood. The meeting helped her acknowledge the significance of Hana's love without allowing the recreated environment to replace her current life.[17]
When the artificial Reality ended, Ophis returned to Victor visibly emotional and embraced him. The experience demonstrated that extraordinary supernatural strength did not eliminate the grief associated with her childhood, but it provided an opportunity to confront that grief and understand the relationships that had shaped her life.[17][48]
The emotional development occurred alongside the awakening of Ophis' personal Divinity. Kali detected traces of the Concept of Infinity emerging from Ophis as black supernatural Energy gathered around her body. Her existence began undergoing a Divine transformation, accompanied by the rapid maturation of her physical form into that of an adult woman approximately 185 centimeters tall.[3]
Victor perceived something more significant than the physical transformation. He recognized that the native Primordial Infinity was attempting to establish influence over Ophis through the Energy supporting her developing Divinity. Because Victor refused to permit an outside Being to acquire such influence over his daughter, he intervened before the process could fully mature.[3]
Using the Energy associated with his Nightmare existence, Victor replaced the native connection with his own supernatural foundation. The emerging Concept of Infinity consequently became an Eldritch Divinity supported by Victor rather than an ordinary Divine authority dependent upon the native Primordial system. Victor recognized that the intervention was possible because Ophis' Divinity remained in an early stage of development; performing an equivalent operation upon a completely matured Divine existence would be far more complicated.[3]
The transformation established Ophis as an Eldritch God of Infinity while preserving her existing Draconic and inherited supernatural characteristics. Her connection to the native Primordial Infinity had been redirected before becoming the completed foundation of her Godhood, distinguishing her eventual Divine status from that of a conventional native Goddess operating through the ordinary conceptual system.[3]
Ophis' development continued during the enormous periods of accelerated Time experienced by Victor's family. She eventually became one of the senior daughters whose practical experience, training and understanding of Imperial affairs exceeded those of many sisters born during the later expansion of the Empire.[16]
During a training session with Nero, Ophis demonstrated how thoroughly her combat ability had matured. The sisters exchanged powerful [[Martial Arts]] attacks within an Arena specifically constructed to endure their extraordinary strength. Ophis manipulated the surrounding Reality to change her position, used Draconic movement to maintain unusual combat angles and employed teleportation to surprise Nero despite her sister's extensive experience.[16]
At one point, Ophis appeared to prepare a conventional Dragon Breath from above. Nero anticipated the attack, but Ophis teleported behind her before releasing it at close range. The impact struck the Arena's defenses and demonstrated the danger created when Ophis combined her natural abilities with tactical deception. Their younger sisters observed the battle with fascination, recognizing that the two had developed combat skills far beyond those expected from ordinary young members of the Dragon race.[16]
The session also revealed how much Ophis' former physical limitations had changed. Her original Vampire body had struggled to support the enormous concentration of Progenitor Blood inherited from Vlad, occasionally causing her powers to activate in dangerous or poorly controlled ways. Her matured Dragon Goddess existence no longer suffered from those same difficulties, allowing her to use her abilities normally and interact with others without the constant danger associated with her childhood condition.[16]
Despite becoming a much more capable fighter, Ophis did not abandon her preference for a relatively peaceful life. She remained more interested in Victor, her family and the development of her own understanding than in seeking increasingly dangerous battles simply to prove her strength.[16]
The same period also marked the beginning of a more complicated personal relationship with Victor. Ophis had become fully adult through centuries of subjective development and gradually recognized feelings extending beyond the childhood attachment she had originally formed. She kept those feelings private, employing mental protections learned through her training to prevent other powerful members of the family from reading information she did not wish to share.[16]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
The accelerated Imperial era transformed Ophis' position within the Elderblood family and the Empire's military structure. Alongside [[Nero]], she became one of the principal commanders of the Fifth Imperial Legion, [[The Imperial Guard]]. The Legion possessed a highly specialized responsibility distinct from ordinary military conquest: protecting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], the Imperial Family, important Divine institutions, sacred territories, relics and other assets whose significance made them priority targets.[4]
The Imperial Guard included extraordinarily capable warriors and personnel selected for their ability to protect the Emperor's most important interests. Ophis and Nero jointly held command responsibilities, while [[Rose Adrasteia Elderblood]] could assume authority when the principal commanders were unavailable. Their appointment demonstrated the enormous confidence Victor placed in the two daughters who had once required constant protection.[4]
Ophis' personal relationship with Victor also continued developing throughout the accelerated period. After reaching adulthood and accumulating immense subjective age, she entered a marital relationship with him. The later Elderblood household recognized both Ophis and Nero among the daughters who had also become wives, sometimes referring to the pair as the family's sister-wives.[1][18]
The relationship did not erase the history through which Ophis had entered Victor's family. She continued addressing him as Father, while Victor retained the affection and protective concern he had shown since their first meeting. Ophis herself understood that her marital relationship had developed after enormous personal changes, but did not regard those changes as requiring her to abandon the parental bond that had defined her childhood.[5]
During the gathering of Victor's daughters, Ophis and Nero appeared among the more experienced members of the younger generation. Their arrival drew attention from the other Princesses, whose reactions reflected the complicated combination of sisterhood, marriage and competition for Victor's affection that had developed within the enormous family.[18]
Ophis retained her preference for floor-length Gothic dresses and long black hair, which by this period extended approximately to her ankles. Although she had become one of the Empire's senior Imperial Princesses, she continued displaying many of the habits associated with her original appearance and personality.[18]
During Victor's subsequent lessons, Ophis demonstrated this continuity by reducing herself to the childlike form she had possessed when they first met. She climbed onto Victor's shoulder and received his affectionate attention, immediately causing several of her sisters to regret not using similar methods to obtain the same treatment. Ophis remained completely satisfied with her position and ignored the jealousy surrounding her.[19]
The conversation also returned to her relationship with Vlad. Ophis explained that her biological father's newer household appeared more stable than it had been during her childhood, but she remained critical of his decisions and did not automatically accept his later partner as another mother. She believed that the woman's behavior toward her was influenced by fear of Victor and the circumstances surrounding the former Queen rather than genuine maternal affection.[19]
Victor recognized that Ophis' criticism was shaped by her own history, while Nero observed that conflicts of this kind were not unusual among royal families. Ophis' attitude demonstrated that she had become capable of evaluating her biological family from an adult perspective without forgetting the emotional consequences of her childhood.[19]
Later, Ophis received responsibility for preparing [[Nightingale]] for the merger of the Lower Sectors into Victor's developing Mid-Level Sector. Her assignment required returning to the world in which she had been born and communicating the necessary instructions to Vlad and the local authorities.[49]
The return revealed how thoroughly Nightingale had changed. Its Gothic architecture and longstanding Vampire culture remained recognizable, but the world had become a multicultural Imperial society inhabited by Vampires, Humans, Shadow Demons, Dark Elves, [[Ghosts]], Spectres and other supernatural races. The former divisions between many species had been reduced through Imperial law and technology, while advances in biotechnology allowed Vampires to survive without relying exclusively upon drinking Blood.[49]
Ophis traveled through the Royal Capital and the territories once associated with the major Count Clans, observing the extraordinary prosperity that had developed under Imperial administration. She recognized that the civilization had achieved conditions resembling a utopia compared with the political inequality and racial divisions of her childhood. The planet itself had become larger and more developed, reflecting the influence of the [[World Trees]] and other supernatural systems supporting the Empire.[49]
Her return to Vlad's castle produced a much less solemn encounter than the political importance of the assignment suggested. Ophis bypassed the castle's protections without difficulty and discovered Vlad privately playing video games. The Vampire King immediately attempted to recover his usual dignity after realizing that his daughter had entered the room unnoticed, but Ophis was thoroughly unimpressed by the performance.[49]
She informed Vlad that Nightingale would soon become part of the Elderblood Sector and instructed him to prepare the population to remain calm during the merger. Vlad accepted the information and ordered Alexios to begin the necessary preparations, allowing Ophis to complete her assignment without a political confrontation between the Royal Family and the Empire.[49]
The encounter demonstrated how far Ophis had progressed from her childhood dependence upon the Royal Family's protection. The girl who had once escaped her guards to wander through the Capital could now enter the King's most heavily protected residence without difficulty and deliver instructions concerning a cosmic event involving the reorganization of entire Sectors.[49]
During the later stages of Victor's great temporal project, Ophis and Nero visited him while he was conserving his physical Energy and operating primarily through his Soul. Both daughters were concerned about his condition and refused to accept his assurances without examining him personally. Ophis used her Draconic senses to check whether he had suffered damage, remembering that Victor sometimes minimized the danger to himself when protecting his family.[5]
Their conversation returned to the incident in Japan, with Victor recalling the danger Ophis had experienced and the consequences that might have followed if her rescue had failed. Ophis remained grateful for his intervention and acknowledged that the experience had shaped her understanding of family and protection.[5]
When Victor jokingly questioned her continued use of Father, Ophis explained that he would always remain her father even after becoming her husband. The statement summarized the continuity she perceived between her childhood and adulthood: although her circumstances had changed enormously, Victor remained the person who had given her security and affection when she had needed them most.[5]
Ophis then expressed an interest in having a daughter of her own, primarily because she liked the idea of raising a smaller version of herself. Victor reminded her that parenthood required substantially more preparation than merely finding the idea appealing. When Ophis considered the difficulties experienced by the mothers of her younger sisters, particularly the responsibility of supervising children who possessed extraordinary Draconic powers from birth, she recognized that her desire was insufficient and decided to postpone the possibility.[5]
The conversation also revealed her attitude toward the coming war with the Higher Sectors. Unlike Nero, who wished to participate in combat and improve her martial abilities, Ophis stated that she did not intend to fight voluntarily. She acknowledged that she possessed the talent and training necessary for dangerous battles, but explained through her behavior and decisions that warfare was not something she personally enjoyed. Victor continued encouraging her to maintain her abilities through training, while allowing her to choose the responsibilities she considered appropriate.[5]
By this period, Ophis had experienced an enormous span of subjective Time, become a Blood Dragon, awakened an Eldritch Divinity, acquired Imperial command responsibilities and developed far beyond the child who first approached Victor in Nightingale. Nevertheless, her affection for her Father and Nero remained central to her life, preserving the relationships that had shaped her identity long before the [[Elderblood Empire]] existed.[5]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Ophis' adoptive father, Blood Dragon Progenitor and later husband. Their relationship began before Victor knew that she was the daughter of the Vampire King, allowing Ophis to approach him without the political expectations surrounding her royal identity. Victor treated her as a lonely child who wanted companionship, and she responded by selecting him as another father almost immediately.[2][9]
Victor's acceptance became particularly significant because Ophis had grown up with inconsistent parental attention following Hana's death. While Vlad remained an extraordinarily powerful ruler and provided extensive protection for his daughter, Victor offered the ordinary affection and personal attention she had been missing. Ophis therefore came to associate his presence with safety, warmth and the freedom to express her desires openly.[10][11]
The relationship was tested during the Crimson Nightmare. Victor's reaction to Ophis' decapitation and [[Nero]]'s injuries demonstrated that he regarded her as his genuine daughter rather than a Princess temporarily entrusted to his care. He restored her body, protected Nero and subsequently confronted the individuals responsible for the attack, making clear that the mistreatment of his family would have consequences regardless of the political affiliations of those involved.[13]
Victor also encouraged Ophis to maintain a relationship with her biological heritage rather than abandoning it. He investigated the incomplete Progenitor foundation received from Vlad and created the black gloves that allowed her to access the dormant Kitsune power inherited from Hana. These actions demonstrated that Victor did not consider his own position as her Father a reason to erase the people and characteristics from which she had originally come.[7][8]
His involvement became even more significant during Ophis' Divine awakening. Victor created the artificial Reality through which she could confront her unresolved grief concerning Hana, then intervened when the native Primordial Infinity attempted to influence her developing Divinity. By replacing that connection with his own Nightmare Energy, Victor ensured that her emerging Godhood would develop through a supernatural foundation he considered safe from the outside influence he had detected.[3][17]
Following Ophis' adulthood, their relationship developed into marriage. Nevertheless, Ophis continued openly recognizing Victor as the Father who had raised and protected her. During their conversation in Chapter 1187, she explicitly affirmed that their marital relationship would not replace the parental bond through which she had originally entered his life.[5]

#### Vlad Dracula Tepes
[[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] is Ophis' biological father, the First Vampire Progenitor and the source of her extraordinary [[Sanguis Noctis Regis]] inheritance. Their relationship is unusually complicated because Vlad genuinely cares about Ophis, but his grief following Hana's death prevented him from providing the consistent emotional presence she needed during childhood.[6][11]
Ophis' disappearance demonstrated the depth of Vlad's concern. He ordered extensive efforts to locate her and became increasingly distressed as the search continued, while the Royal Family recognized the danger posed by the possibility that the Princess had fallen into hostile hands.[11]
Nevertheless, Ophis' decision to remain beside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] reflected a failure that Vlad could not simply correct through political authority or physical protection. She had found another individual willing to provide the affection she wanted, and the circumstances of the Crimson Nightmare made it impossible for Vlad to dismiss that relationship as a temporary childhood preference.[14]
As she matured, Ophis became increasingly critical of Vlad's family life. She feared that his growing household had replaced the children he already possessed and recognized patterns of emotional distance similar to those that had affected her own upbringing. The artificial encounter with the younger Vlad helped her understand how Hana's death had transformed his behavior, but the insight did not erase her disappointment.[23][17]
Their later relationship became more stable without developing into an uncomplicated reconciliation. Ophis continued referring to Vlad as Father and maintained sufficient trust to communicate important Imperial decisions directly to him. However, she remained willing to criticize his judgment and refused to accept his later partner as another mother merely because the woman occupied a position within his household.[19][49]
Her return to [[Nightingale]] during the Sector merger illustrated this mature balance. Ophis delivered Victor's instructions respectfully but without subservience, while Vlad accepted that his daughter had become an extraordinarily powerful adult whose responsibilities extended beyond the limits of the Kingdom he ruled.[49]

#### Otsuki Hana
[[Otsuki Hana]] is Ophis' biological mother and the source of her Nine-Tailed Kitsune heritage. Hana died while Ophis was extremely young, leaving her daughter with powerful emotional impressions but relatively few mature memories of their relationship.[8]
The absence created a lasting emotional wound. Ophis initially recognized Haruna through the resemblance between the two sisters and instinctively called her Mother, demonstrating the importance of the fragments of Hana she retained from childhood.[22]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later creation of artificial Realities allowed Ophis to encounter a reconstructed Hana as an adult. The meeting revealed that Ophis had never stopped missing her mother, despite the centuries of development separating her from the child who had experienced the original loss.[17]
Hana initially tried to protect the daughter she remembered, only to discover that Ophis had become a Dragon Goddess whose power exceeded the supernatural beings surrounding her. Their reunion therefore forced Hana to acknowledge Ophis' adulthood while allowing Ophis to recognize the affection her mother had felt toward her.[17]
Ophis ultimately understood that the artificial encounter could not replace the genuine Hana who had died. Nevertheless, the experience gave her an opportunity to understand her mother more fully and reinforced her hope that Victor might eventually accomplish a genuine restoration.[48]

#### Otsuki Elderblood Haruna
[[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]] is Ophis' maternal aunt and one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wives, making her both a biological relative through Hana and an adoptive maternal figure within the Elderblood family.[22][8]
Their first meeting occurred in Japan when Ophis recognized the resemblance between Haruna and her deceased mother. The reaction initially reflected Ophis' unresolved grief, but their relationship later developed into a genuine bond independent of that resemblance.[22]
Haruna became particularly important after Victor created the gloves that allowed Ophis to access her dormant Nine-Tailed Kitsune heritage. Ophis recognized that Haruna possessed the experience necessary to teach her about the abilities inherited from Hana, making her aunt a natural instructor in the supernatural characteristics that had remained suppressed throughout most of her childhood.[8]
The relationship also preserved Ophis' connection to the Otsuki lineage, ensuring that the development of her identity within Clan Elderblood did not require abandoning the maternal family from which she had originally descended.[8]

#### Nero Elderblood
[[Nero Elderblood]] is Ophis' closest sister, best friend and principal companion throughout much of her life. Their relationship originated during the Japanese incident, when [[Nero]] encountered Ophis after the young Princess accidentally teleported to Tokyo.[12]
Nero initially regarded the unfamiliar child with suspicion, but her connection to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] caused her to accept responsibility for protecting Ophis. Throughout their pursuit, Nero repeatedly placed herself in danger and continued fighting even after sustaining severe injuries. Her willingness to protect Ophis despite the absence of an ordinary familial obligation became the foundation of their relationship.[13]
Victor's eventual rescue allowed Nero to become part of the same family as Ophis. The two girls developed together under his protection, gradually acquiring the responsibilities and supernatural strength associated with his expanding household.[31][33]

### Abilities

#### Powers and Abilities
Ophis Elderblood, formerly Ophis Tepes, is a Blood Dragon, Dragon Goddess, and Eldritch God of <Infinity> whose supernatural existence combines several distinct racial and conceptual foundations. She was born as the daughter of [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]], the First Vampire Progenitor, and [[Otsuki Hana]], a Nine-Tailed Kitsune. From Vlad, she inherited approximately 50% of [[Sanguis Noctis Regis]] and part of the unique Soul structure associated with a genuine Vampire Progenitor. From Hana, she inherited a small but persistent Nine-Tailed Kitsune lineage, whose active expression was suppressed by the overwhelming dominance of her paternal Progenitor Blood.[1][2][3]
Even during childhood, Ophis possessed an abnormal collection of supernatural abilities that distinguished her from ordinary Noble Vampires. These included involuntary contact-based Charm and Memory Absorption, Vitality Absorption, limited Empathy, influence over other Vampires, the ability to mark an individual and subsequently detect their location, and Teleportation. Her immature body struggled to regulate these abilities, resulting in dangerous effects when other individuals touched her or when she attempted to employ her powers over considerable distances. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later theorized that most of these characteristics represented weakened or incomplete manifestations of the abilities naturally possessed by a genuine Vampire Progenitor, while Teleportation was more closely associated with the Nine-Tailed Kitsune inheritance received from Hana.[4][5][6]
Ophis' abilities underwent several important developments as Victor Elderblood continued strengthening his family. He created specialized black gloves that amplified the dormant Kitsune characteristics inherited from Hana, allowing Ophis to manifest fox ears, nine tails, a dark Energy mantle and elemental abilities associated with Fire and Wind. Her subsequent transformation into a genuine Blood Dragon incorporated and strengthened her existing Vampire and Youkai powers, while replacing the limitations of her former physiology with the superior foundation of a True Dragon. By adulthood, abilities that had once activated uncontrollably could be used deliberately without placing ordinary companions in danger.[3][7][6]
Her most significant advancement occurred through the awakening of her personal Divinity, <Infinity>. [[Kali Elderblood]] recognized the emerging Concept as extraordinarily dangerous because of the enormous variety of potential applications associated with Infinity. However, Victor discovered that the native Primordial Infinity was attempting to become the source of the Energy supporting Ophis' new Godhood. He intervened during the early stages of her awakening and replaced that connection with Energy from his own Nightmare existence, transforming Ophis into an Eldritch God with the Aspect of Infinity. The change gave her a supernatural foundation connected to Victor rather than one dependent upon the native Primordial Infinity.[8]
Ophis' current combat style combines her enormous Draconic physical characteristics with advanced [[Martial Arts]], specialized Teleportation, Reality Bending and destructive Dragon Breath. Her prolonged training alongside [[Nero Elderblood]] transformed her from a child possessing extraordinary but poorly controlled powers into a capable combatant who uses positioning, deception and combinations of supernatural techniques. Although she possesses the potential for considerably greater advancement through her Eldritch Divinity, her demonstrated abilities must be distinguished from theoretical applications of Infinity and the powers possessed by more advanced Eldritch entities.[9][6]

#### Racial and Existential Physiology

##### Blood Dragon Physiology
Blood Dragon Physiology is Ophis' current principal racial foundation. Unlike an ordinary Vampire possessing temporary Draconic enhancements, Ophis has become a genuine True Dragon whose body and Soul were fundamentally transformed through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Bloodline. Her former Vampire abilities and dormant Kitsune inheritance were not erased by this evolution. Instead, the transformation strengthened her existing powers while providing a more stable physical and spiritual foundation capable of supporting their continued development.[7][6]
The transformation was especially important because Ophis had suffered from a condition similar to Victor's earlier developmental difficulties: she possessed more supernatural power than her immature body could safely regulate. Her original Noble Vampire physiology struggled to contain the concentration of Progenitor Blood she had inherited from Vlad, causing several abilities to activate involuntarily. After acquiring her mature Draconic body and undergoing extensive training, Ophis no longer needed to fear that ordinary contact with another person would automatically trigger her abnormal powers.[4][6]

#### Divinity — <Infinity>
<Infinity> is Ophis' personal Divine Concept and the defining principle of her Eldritch Godhood. The emergence of the Divinity establishes her supernatural relationship with Infinity, while her individual feats determine which specific manifestations have been demonstrated.[14][8]

##### Potential Applications
The scope of Infinity suggests an enormous variety of possible supernatural effects. However, conceptual potential must be distinguished from demonstrated personal techniques. The reviewed chapters establish Ophis' possession of the Concept, its [[Eldritch Energy]] source and its developmental potential, but do not provide an independently demonstrated catalogue of named techniques through which she has fully mastered every application of infinitude.[8][9]
Consequently, the following should not be treated as confirmed active abilities merely because they are theoretically related to Infinity:
- Absolute Infinity Embodiment: A completed state in which Ophis becomes the unrestricted personification of every form of Infinity has not been independently demonstrated.
- Infinite Energy: Ophis has not been shown possessing an inexhaustible personal Energy reserve solely through the awakening of Infinity.
- Infinite Speed: Her extraordinary speed and Teleportation are documented, but they do not establish literally infinite movement speed.
- Infinite Physical Strength: The Concept does not automatically convert every physical characteristic into an infinite quantity.
- Unrestricted Spatio-Chronokinesis: Ophis' Teleportation and local Reality Bending are confirmed abilities, but full authority over every possible aspect of Space-Time has not been demonstrated.
- Universal Infinity Manipulation: The ability to control every infinite quantity, structure or characteristic throughout Creation requires further direct evidence.
- Automatic Infinity Barriers: No personal named defense functioning as an unrestricted infinite separation between Ophis and every opponent has been established.
- Absolute Rule Transcendence: Ophis' Eldritch foundation does not automatically make her immune to every supernatural Law or authority.
- Omnipotence or Omnipresence: Possession of Infinity is not sufficient evidence for either characteristic.

#### Partial Vampire Progenitor Soul

#### Progenitor-Derived Abilities

##### Contact-Based Psychic Condition
Ophis was born with an unusual condition causing supernatural effects whenever another individual physically touched her. During the Rider–Horseman event, Violet ignored [[Scathach]]'s warning and attempted to remove Ophis from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s arms. Upon contact, Violet immediately stopped moving and her eyes became blank, resembling someone whose consciousness had been temporarily overwhelmed. Scathach explained that Ophis' condition charmed the people who touched her and simultaneously allowed Ophis to perceive their memories.[4]
The condition was especially difficult for a young child because Ophis could access the memories of Vampires who had lived for hundreds or thousands of years. Such information was far beyond the ordinary emotional and intellectual experiences expected from someone of her age. The incident also demonstrated that the supernatural effect could incapacitate powerful Noble Vampires, since Violet remained affected until she forcibly recovered from the state.[4]
- Contact-Based Charm: Physical contact with Ophis could activate an involuntary supernatural charm effect.[4]
- Passive Psychic Influence: The condition originally activated without Ophis consciously selecting the target or attempting to attack them.[4]
- Memory Reading: Ophis could perceive the memories of individuals who made physical contact with her.[4]
- Memory Absorption: She could acquire information from another individual's memories through the contact effect.[4][6]
- Temporary Incapacitation: Violet became motionless with blank eyes immediately after touching Ophis.[4]
- Consciousness Disruption: The effect interfered with Violet's ability to remain consciously responsive to her surroundings.[4]
- Supernatural Charm Resistance Requirement: A target possessing sufficient resistance could recover from the effect. Violet eventually broke free, surprising Scathach because she recovered faster than expected.[4]
- Former Involuntary Activation: Ophis originally lacked sufficient control to prevent the condition from activating when individuals touched her.[4]
- Adult Contact-Power Control: By adulthood, Ophis could consciously regulate her inherited powers and no longer needed to avoid ordinary physical contact.[6]
