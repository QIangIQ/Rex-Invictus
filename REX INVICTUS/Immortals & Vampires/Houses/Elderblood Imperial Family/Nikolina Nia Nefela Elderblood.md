### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 996
And my name ended up being Nikolina Nia Nefela Elderblood!
—Nikolina explaining how Nyx chose her full name., Chapter 996
Nikolina Nia Nefela Elderblood is an Imperial Princess of the [[Elderblood Empire]], a naturally born Blood Dragon, a True Dragon Goddess and the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and Nyx Elderblood. She belongs to Victor's enormous second generation and inherited a Divine Concept centered upon <Star>, giving her the title Goddess of Stars.[2]
Nikolina was born under unusual circumstances even by the standards of Victor's children. Nyx carried her for approximately 1,900 subjective years during the family's two-thousand-year period of accelerated Time. As a consequence, Nikolina had existed for only roughly a century when the second generation began appearing prominently despite having spent almost two millennia developing within Nyx before birth.[1]
Her early appearances portray her less as a distant cosmic Goddess and more as an energetic young Dragon Princess. She role-plays with her sisters, argues over who receives the more impressive position in their games, proudly invokes Nyx's status as the Primordial Goddess of Night and becomes immediately interested when Victor offers his daughters a reward during their War Games.[2][3]
Despite her enormous inherited potential, Nikolina's War Games performance also demonstrates Victor's reason for training his daughters from childhood. She possesses knowledge and power but initially lacks the speed, battlefield awareness and command instincts of more naturally martial sisters such as [[Siren Elderblood]].[4]

### Physical Appearance
Nikolina is introduced as a young black-haired girl with pale skin and the deep-violet Dragon Eyes inherited by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s biological daughters.[2][5]
Chapter 997 explicitly groups Nikolina and [[Evelyn Elderblood]] together as the two black-haired girls among the four sisters playing with one another, contrasting them with the blonde members of the group.[5]
Her violet eyes become especially noticeable when Victor announces that the best-performing participant in his War Games will be permitted to make any request of him. Nikolina's eyes immediately sparkle upon hearing the offer, visually emphasizing both her excitement and her strong attachment to her father.[3]
As a naturally born True Dragon, Nikolina's childlike Humanoid body should not be treated as the full extent of her physical existence. Dragon development operates on radically different timescales from Human aging, and her physiology possesses the transformation and growth characteristics of Victor's Blood Dragon lineage.
No reviewed chapter currently provides a detailed description of Nikolina's mature adult Humanoid appearance, height or full Dragon form. Those details should therefore not be invented from fan art.

### Personality
Nikolina is playful, competitive, mischievous, proud and intensely attached to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
Her debut immediately establishes her tendency to participate enthusiastically in the games created by her sisters. While playing with Evelyn, Nikolina is assigned the role of Evelyn's subordinate. She immediately objects to being called a "minion" and insists that she should instead be the Herald of Darkness because her mother is the Primordial Goddess of Night.[2]
This argument also demonstrates that Nikolina has inherited at least some of Nyx's pride. She is perfectly willing to invoke her mother's cosmic status when it benefits her position in a childish role-playing game, prompting Evelyn to complain that their mothers should not be used as leverage in an ordinary sibling dispute.[2]
Nikolina quickly counters that Evelyn regularly does the same thing with [[Eve Elderblood]], leaving Evelyn unable to deny the accusation. Their exchange suggests that Nikolina is observant enough to recognize hypocrisy and sharp enough to turn an argument back upon her sister rather than simply becoming frustrated.[2]
She is also proud of being an Imperial Princess. Chapter 997 describes Nikolina and Evelyn as girls who proudly declared themselves Princesses, indicating that their royal identity is something they openly enjoy rather than a title treated with indifference.[5]
Like Victor's other daughters, Nikolina's greatest weakness is anything involving her father.
When Victor announces that the daughter with the best performance during the War Games may request anything from him, Nikolina immediately asks for confirmation. Victor recognizes the mischievous intention behind her reaction without needing her to explain it. Once he confirms the reward, Nikolina and the other daughters become dramatically more motivated.[3]
Nikolina is not portrayed as unintelligent. During the War Games she and Priscillia immediately begin applying lessons previously taught by their mothers, organizing forces, preparing supplies and attempting to approach the exercise seriously.[4]
Her problem is inexperience.
Nikolina becomes too focused upon preparation and her direct opponent. She does not properly account for the passage of Time, the possibility of intelligence gathering or the intervention of a third faction. Victor uses the resulting failure as a practical lesson rather than evidence that she lacks potential.[4]
This distinction is important to her characterization. Nikolina possesses extraordinary Divine and Draconic advantages but is still young enough to make strategic mistakes. Victor deliberately places her in circumstances where raw supernatural superiority cannot solve those mistakes for her.

### Name
Nikolina's unusually long name originates from a disagreement between her parents over what she should be called.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] originally wanted his daughter to simply be named Nikolina Elderblood. Nyx, however, became indecisive between the names Nia and Nefela. Rather than choosing between them, Nyx ultimately used both, producing the complete name:
Nikolina Nia Nefela Elderblood.[2]
The length of her name immediately becomes a recurring source of sibling comedy. During Nikolina's debut, Evelyn attempts to dramatically announce Nikolina's full name while role-playing, bites her tongue before she can finish and angrily blames Nikolina for possessing such a long name.[2]
Nikolina responds that Evelyn should blame Nyx rather than her.
The novel uses Nefela in Nikolina's own explanation of her name. The older spelling "Nefelia" found in previous versions of this article is therefore not used as her canonical full name.

### Heritage
Nikolina is the biological daughter of the Blood Dragon Primogenitor and one of the oldest Primordial Goddesses of Earth's supernatural history.
Her father, [[Victor Elderblood]], provides the dominant Blood Dragon and True Dragon foundation inherited by his biological children.
Her mother, Nyx Elderblood, is the original Primordial Goddess of Night, an ancient Goddess whose existence and authority are intrinsically connected to Night and who produced numerous Divine and Primordial descendants long before joining Victor's family.
Nikolina consequently possesses:
- Victor's Blood Dragon lineage;
- complete True Dragon physiology;
- a Divine nature from birth;
- Nyx's Primordial Divine heritage;
- the Divine Concept of <Star>;
- and the extensive racial adaptations and protections Victor deliberately passes to his daughters.[1][3]
The old article classified Nikolina directly as a Primordial Goddess. Her maternal lineage unquestionably gives her Primordial heritage, but no reviewed Nikolina-specific chapter explicitly establishes that she currently occupies the same cosmic office or state of existence as original [[Primordial Gods]] such as Nyx. Her confirmed rank is therefore indexed more conservatively as Dragon Goddess.

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Nikolina was born during the enormous period of accelerated Time experienced by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family following his rise as God Emperor.
Nyx's pregnancy lasted approximately 1,900 years. This left Nikolina among the comparatively younger members of Victor's early second generation, since around two thousand subjective years had elapsed during the accelerated family era and only approximately a century separated her birth from the period in which the daughters were introduced.[1]
Nikolina first appears while Victor is speaking with [[Leon Walker]]. Victor has reluctantly agreed to allow Leon to meet the girls as their grandfather, but only after examining his intentions and confirming that Leon possesses no inappropriate interest toward his daughters.[2]
Before the conversation can continue, the adults hear the children playing.
[[Evelyn Elderblood]] dramatically announces herself as the Herald of Darkness and attempts to introduce Nikolina as her loyal minion. Evelyn cannot finish Nikolina's full name before biting her tongue.[2]
Nikolina immediately protests.
She explains that Victor originally intended to call her simply Nikolina Elderblood but that Nyx could not decide between Nia and Nefela. Nyx's solution was simply to use both names, producing Nikolina Nia Nefela Elderblood.[2]
Nikolina then rejects Evelyn's attempt to make her the subordinate in their game and argues that she should be the Herald of Darkness because her own mother is the Primordial Goddess of Night. Evelyn objects to using their mothers as leverage, only for Nikolina to point out that Evelyn repeatedly invokes Eve in the exact same manner.[2]
The exchange continues into Chapter 997, where Nikolina and Evelyn are identified as the two black-haired girls among a group also containing [[Melina Elderblood]] and [[Julia Elderblood]]. Both Nikolina and Evelyn proudly identify themselves as Princesses.[5]
Their playful behavior helps Leon understand what Victor's enormous second generation is actually like. The girls possess powers, bloodlines and positions that would have made them terrifying beings almost anywhere else, yet inside Victor's household they behave primarily as sisters competing, playing and obsessing over their father.
Nikolina later participates in Victor's War Games, an extensive training exercise designed specifically to address the weaknesses created by his daughters being born extraordinarily powerful.[3]
Victor takes the girls into a hidden frozen region of his personal world. The environment is approximately -60°C, but none of the daughters are meaningfully affected due to their Dragon physiology and inherited adaptation.[3]
After using the encounter with a hidden Ice Dragon as another lesson about vigilance, Victor creates two headquarters and armies of humanoid soldiers. He announces that the daughters will participate in a war simulation in which their own overwhelming powers cannot simply be used to win. Instead, they must command the soldiers provided to them.[3]
The daughter who performs best will be allowed to make any request of Victor.
Nikolina immediately becomes interested.
Her violet eyes sparkle as she asks whether Victor truly means any request. Victor recognizes the mischievous thought behind her reaction but confirms the offer anyway.[3]
Victor selects Nikolina versus Priscillia as the first match.
Both girls are transported to separate headquarters and establish a direct connection with the soldiers beneath their command. Victor warns them to treat the exercise as a genuine war before beginning the simulation.[3]
The soldiers are subsequently given realistic autonomy. Nikolina and Priscillia immediately begin giving orders and applying the military lessons taught by their mothers. Their initial approach is competent: both organize forces and prepare for the coming conflict.[4]
However, Victor has constructed the scenario to punish narrow thinking.
Both girls spend too much Time preparing. They fail to recognize that an enemy does not wait indefinitely while an opposing army completes its organization and also fail to gather enough information regarding potential outside participants.[4]
[[Siren Elderblood]] understands Victor's warning more completely.
Victor allows Siren to enter the simulation as an unexpected third faction. Unlike Nikolina and Priscillia, Siren immediately mobilizes a smaller combat force and attacks the supply routes supporting both armies.[4]
Nikolina and Priscillia react too slowly. By the time the two leaders personally respond, Siren has already attacked vulnerable positions and withdrawn with stolen supplies.[4]
Victor uses their failure to explain one of the most important principles of warfare: a conflict rarely exists in isolation. Third parties can observe two enemies weakening one another and intervene specifically to gain something from both.[4]
The narration does not characterize Nikolina or Priscillia as incompetent. Both are explicitly described as capable, but neither naturally possesses the same leadership instincts Siren displays during the exercise. Victor views leadership and charisma as abilities that can develop through experience, making the loss exactly the kind of lesson the War Games were designed to provide.[4]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Nikolina's biological father and the individual around whom much of her early behavior revolves.
Victor originally chose the comparatively simple name Nikolina, although Nyx ultimately expanded it into Nikolina Nia Nefela Elderblood.[2]
Like Victor's other daughters, Nikolina is strongly motivated by the possibility of receiving something directly from him. During the War Games, the promise that the best performer can make any request immediately captures her full attention.[3]
Victor understands his daughter's intentions almost instantly, demonstrating the familiarity he has developed with the personalities of his children.
His relationship with Nikolina is nevertheless not merely indulgent. The War Games deliberately place her in a situation where her Blood Dragon body and Divine Concept cannot compensate for mistakes in judgment. Victor allows Nikolina to fail because the purpose of the exercise is to teach skills that inherited power cannot provide.[3][4]

#### Nyx Elderblood
Nyx Elderblood is Nikolina's biological mother.
Nyx carried Nikolina for approximately 1,900 subjective years before giving birth to her.[1]
She is also responsible for most of Nikolina's unusually long name. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] wanted "Nikolina", while Nyx could not decide between "Nia" and "Nefela". Her solution was to add both names.[2]
Nikolina is openly proud of her mother's identity as the Primordial Goddess of Night. During her game with Evelyn she uses Nyx's status as an argument for why she, rather than Evelyn, should be the Herald of Darkness.[2]
Her Divine Concept of <Star> also complements Nyx's night-associated nature, although the novel has not yet fully explained whether the specific Concept emerged directly from Nyx's Night Divinity or from the broader interaction between Nyx and Victor's existences.

#### Evelyn Elderblood
[[Evelyn Elderblood]] is one of Nikolina's closest demonstrated childhood playmates.
The two role-play together during Nikolina's introduction, with Evelyn initially attempting to make Nikolina her minion. Nikolina immediately refuses and argues for the more important role herself.[2]
Their argument is competitive but clearly playful rather than hostile. Nikolina is comfortable enough with Evelyn to accuse her of hypocrisy when Evelyn complains about invoking their mothers during the argument.[2]
Evelyn's inability to pronounce Nikolina's full name without biting her tongue also becomes the first joke associated with Nikolina in the novel.

#### Priscillia Penny Elderblood
[[Priscillia Penny Elderblood]] is Nikolina's first directly shown strategic opponent.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] selects the two sisters to begin his War Games and assigns each an army and headquarters.[3]
Both demonstrate that they have studied the lessons provided by their mothers, but their planning is too methodical and slow for an active battlefield. Neither properly anticipates Siren's intervention as an opportunistic third faction.[4]
Their match therefore becomes an educational failure rather than a straightforward contest of personal power.

#### Siren Elderblood
[[Siren Elderblood]] indirectly becomes Nikolina's first major battlefield lesson.
Siren enters the War Games as a smaller third faction after realizing the meaning behind [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s instruction to treat the scenario like a real war. While Nikolina and Priscillia focus upon one another, Siren raids both sides' supply routes.[4]
Victor later explains that Siren is better suited to leadership at that point in their development, while Nikolina and Priscillia remain competent but inexperienced commanders.[4]

### Divine Concept — Star
Nikolina's defining Divine authority is the Concept of <Star>.[2]
This makes celestial stars themselves the central subject of her Divinity rather than merely granting her access to an ordinary fire, light or plasma ability.
The previous version of this article expanded <Star> into unrestricted authority over galaxies, every astronomical superstructure containing stars and several individually named techniques that have not yet been directly demonstrated by Nikolina.
Those claims are too broad for the currently reviewed material.

#### Confirmed Scope
- Divine Concept — <Star>: Nikolina possesses a Star-based Divinity and is identified as a Goddess of Stars.[2]
- Star Manipulation: As a Goddess whose Concept is Star, Nikolina possesses conceptual authority over stars themselves. The exact upper scale, range and degree of control have not yet been demonstrated in a dedicated combat scene.[2]
- Stellar Affinity: Stars and stellar phenomena fall naturally within the conceptual domain represented by her Divinity.[2]
- Divine Power: Nikolina can express supernatural power through her personal Concept as a Dragon Goddess.
- Potential Conceptual Development: Like other young Elderblood Goddesses, Nikolina's command over her Divinity can increase through understanding, experience and training. No reviewed Nikolina-specific passage establishes a literal infinite current level of power.

- Enhanced Speed and Reflexes: Nikolina possesses the heightened movement and reaction capabilities of Dragonkind.

- [[Draconic Energy]]: Nikolina possesses the supernatural Energy characteristic of [[True Dragons]].
- [[Dragon Heart]]: Her physiology possesses the extraordinary Energy-generating foundation associated with Victor's Dragon lineage.
- Dragon Breath: Nikolina possesses the racial foundation for Dragon Breath, although her personal Breath manifestation has not yet been individually described.
- Draconic Language: The supernatural Language of Dragons is naturally compatible with her existence.
- Draconic Runes (Racial Foundation): Nikolina possesses the natural compatibility with Draconic Runes expected of a True Dragon. No reviewed Nikolina-specific scene yet establishes advanced Runemaster proficiency comparable to specialists such as Victor, Anna, [[Scathach]] or [[Dún Scáith|Dun Scaith]].

- [[Divine Energy]]: She possesses Divine power alongside her Draconic Energy.
- Divinity: Nikolina's Divine existence is centered upon <Star>.[2]
- Divine Longevity: Her Divine and Draconic physiology makes ordinary biological aging largely irrelevant.
- Divine Concept — <Star>: Nikolina possesses conceptual authority centered upon stars.[2]
- Star Manipulation: Stars fall within the direct conceptual subject of her Divinity.
- Stellar Affinity: She possesses an intrinsic supernatural relationship with stellar phenomena.
- Conceptual Development: Her control and understanding of <Star> can develop as she matures and trains.
- Environmental Adaptation: Nikolina possesses the passive adaptation inherited by Victor's daughters. When exposed to climates or environmental conditions uncomfortable to them, their bodies progressively adapt until those conditions cease to present the same problem.[3]
- Extreme Temperature Resistance: Nikolina remains unaffected while standing in an environment approximately -60°C during Victor's War Games.[3]
- Accelerated Adaptation: Victor's daughters adapt more rapidly than normal Dragons because they inherited portions of the adaptive abilities Victor obtained during his own evolution.[3]
- Mind Manipulation Resistance / Immunity: Victor placed extremely powerful safeguards against mental invasion and alteration upon his daughters.[3]
- Victor Soul Guardian: A fragment of Victor's Soul exists within his daughters as a final layer of defense against mental or spiritual intrusion.[3]
- Soul Manipulation Resistance / Immunity: Nikolina possesses direct safeguards against hostile Soul invasion.[3]
- Possession Resistance: Entities attempting to invade or take control of her body or Soul must overcome the protections Victor embedded within his daughters.[3]
- Parasite Resistance: Her body possesses safeguards against parasitic organisms and other invasive foreign entities.[3]
- Hostile Foreign-Body Rejection: Victor's protections recognize and reject substances or organisms harmful to his daughters.[3]
- Deceptive Biological Attack Countermeasures: The protections were specifically designed to account for hostile substances engineered to appear beneficial to the body while secretly causing harm.[3]
- Soul Backup / Resurrection: Victor preserves part of each daughter's Soul within himself. If Nikolina were somehow killed despite her other defenses, she could be restored through the Soul fragment maintained by her father.[3]
- Attack Detection / Paternal Warning: Hostile interference against Victor's daughters can alert him, meaning an enemy attacking Nikolina risks immediately attracting Victor's attention.[3]
- Military Education: Nikolina has received strategic and military lessons from the adults responsible for raising Victor's second generation.[4]
- Army Command: During the War Games she is placed in direct command of an autonomous military faction.[3]
- Logistical Organization: Nikolina immediately begins organizing her forces and supplies before engaging Priscillia.[4]
- Application of Maternal Lessons: The narration explicitly states that Nikolina and Priscillia begin applying what they had learned from their mothers.[4]
- Developing Strategic Awareness: Nikolina's initial failure to account for elapsed Time, intelligence gathering and third-party intervention becomes part of her practical education.[4]
- Leadership Potential: Victor considers Nikolina competent but not naturally suited to command at Siren's level during this stage. He treats leadership and charisma as skills capable of being developed through experience.[4]
