### Appearances

#### First Mention
Chapter 855
Siren was everything she had ever dreamed of: determined, full of potential, very much like her.
—Narration describing [[Scathach]]'s view of Siren, Chapter 988
Siren Elderblood is an Imperial Princess of the [[Elderblood Empire]], a naturally born Blood Dragon and Dragon Goddess, and the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]]. Unlike Scathach, who began life as a Vampire before being elevated into a True Dragon by Victor, Siren was born after the formation of the Elderblood Dragon lineage and consequently belongs naturally to the second generation of [[True Dragons]].[1]
Among Victor's enormous second generation, Siren is distinguished less by a currently revealed unique Divinity than by her exceptional martial inclination. Scathach describes her as determined, filled with potential and remarkably similar to herself, and Siren's mature behavior repeatedly validates that assessment. Even after living through enormous periods of accelerated Imperial development, she remains one of Victor's daughters most devoted to physical and martial training; by the later Imperial era Violet specifically uses Siren as an example of a daughter who prefers training rather than pursuing motherhood, while Siren herself willingly remains for Scathach's brutal lessons when most of her sisters immediately retreat from the opportunity.[2][3][4]
Siren's connection to Scathach is unusually personal. Her name originates from Siren, the identity Scathach herself used during the period in which she trained under [[Dun Scaith Elderblood]] before abandoning that name and becoming known as Scathach.[5] She is also the daughter Scathach had with the man she personally chose, loved, trained and eventually accepted as both husband and rival, giving Siren a different emotional significance from Scathach's earlier motherhood without diminishing her attachment to Ruby, Siena, Lacus or Pepper.[2]
Siren was foreshadowed long before her chronological birth. During a vision in Chapter 855, Victor witnessed a young daughter of himself and Scathach whose crying devastated an entire country, establishing the frightening potential expected of their eventual child.[6] The event occurred within a vision and therefore represents foreshadowed potential rather than a confirmed main-timeline combat feat; the novel has not identified the mechanism behind the destruction or connected it to a named Divinity or technique.

### Physical Appearance
Siren is one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s red-haired daughters, a characteristic directly acknowledged when Victor collectively addresses her and several of her sisters as his "redheads".[7] Her crimson coloration visually connects her particularly strongly to Clan Scarlett and her mother [[Scathach]], whose red hair remained one of her defining traits before and after becoming a Dragon.
Like Victor's other biological daughters, Siren possesses violet Dragon Eyes. The second generation inherited variations of Victor's supernatural perception through these eyes, allowing his daughters to perceive deeper aspects of Reality to differing degrees and further strengthen that perception through training.[8] Siren's exact personal level of ocular perception has not yet been separately demonstrated, and the novel has likewise not provided a sufficiently detailed description of her full Dragon Form to establish unique anatomical traits beyond her Blood Dragon and True Dragon nature.

### Personality
Siren is determined, highly competitive, extraordinarily training-oriented and temperamentally similar to [[Scathach]]. Her mother's earliest direct assessment after Siren's birth describes her as essentially everything Scathach had wanted in a daughter: determined, possessing enormous potential and very much like herself.[2] This resemblance becomes increasingly significant as Siren matures because Scathach's own identity is inseparable from constant improvement, combat, [[Martial Arts]] and the refusal to accept stagnation simply because she has already reached an extraordinary level of power.
Training is not a temporary interest for Siren but one of the clearest priorities of her mature life. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Violet discuss the future size of their family, Violet specifically notes that some daughters who train more, using Siren as the example, have no interest in having children at present.[3] Chapter 1173 demonstrates the same mentality directly. While Scathach's training of Albedo causes the surrounding daughters to withdraw rather than risk being included, Siren is the only one who remains behind, deliberately approaches her mother and asks if she can participate as well. She does so despite already having firsthand experience with how extreme Scathach's training can become.[4]
Her martial seriousness does not make her humorless or socially detached. During the large Elderblood family gathering in Chapter 1166, Siren is revealed to have participated alongside Erika, Priscillia, Rosemary, Sylvia and Lucrecia in the chaos surrounding Velina's temporary "kidnapping". Victor quickly recognizes that Velina could have escaped whenever she wanted and that the incident was fundamentally a game between sisters, but Siren nevertheless blushes with the rest when their father calls attention to what the redheads had done.[7] The scene establishes a mischievous and familial side to Siren that contrasts with the severe training mentality emphasized elsewhere.
Siren also appears unusually comfortable with physical hardship. By Chapter 1173 Scathach can use her as a standard against which another Dragon Goddess is judged, revealing that Siren had already endured gravity training modeled upon the conditions of a singularity.[4] Rather than avoiding the woman responsible for such training, Siren voluntarily asks for more. Her characterization therefore combines extraordinary inherited potential with the mentality Scathach values most: the willingness to repeatedly place that potential under pressure instead of relying upon birth alone.

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Siren enters the narrative before she is actually born. In Chapter 855, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] experiences a vision involving a small daughter produced by himself and [[Scathach]]. The child cries with such overwhelming power that the resulting disturbance devastates an entire country, presenting one of the earliest glimpses of the abnormal children Victor's future Dragon lineage could eventually produce.[6] Because the scene occurs through a vision rather than as an event performed by the present-day Siren, its country-scale destruction serves as foreshadowing of her potential and should not be treated as an independently verified combat feat or evidence for an unrevealed ability such as Sound Manipulation, Vibration Manipulation or a Destruction Divinity.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Years before Siren's eventual birth, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] learns an important part of [[Scathach]]'s history through [[Dun Scaith Elderblood]]. When Dun addresses her former disciple as Siren, Scathach reacts negatively and makes clear that she abandoned that identity after completing her old training and taking the name by which she became legendary.[5] The chapter does not establish "Siren" as Scathach's confirmed birth name; it establishes it as the old identity by which Dun knew her during her formative period. That discarded name would later be passed to Scathach and Victor's biological daughter, connecting the younger Siren directly to her mother's earliest known martial identity.

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
The future foreshadowed much earlier becomes reality after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household experiences enormous amounts of accelerated subjective Time. By Chapter 987, [[Scathach]] has spent approximately 1,670 subjective years pregnant with Siren, an extraordinarily long gestation even within the unusual reproductive biology surrounding Victor's newly established True Dragon lineage.[1] Siren is subsequently revealed among the second generation produced by Victor and his wives, born naturally into the Elderblood Dragon family rather than beginning as another species and later undergoing Victor's Dragon transformation.
Siren's birth changes Scathach in ways that raw power never had. When further extreme training and accelerated development are discussed, Scathach realizes that disappearing into another isolated period of training is no longer an uncomplicated decision because she does not want to remain separated from her daughter or lose the opportunity to watch her grow.[2] For a woman who had spent thousands of years placing battle and improvement near the center of her existence, this hesitation represents an unusually strong attachment.
Scathach considers Siren everything she had hoped for in a daughter: determined, enormously promising and similar to herself.[2] The emotional circumstances surrounding Siren are also different from those surrounding Ruby's birth. Scathach had genuinely wanted Ruby, but had selected Ruby's biological father principally because she considered him suitable for producing the child she desired. Victor instead became someone Scathach chose as a partner, disciple, rival and husband. Siren consequently becomes the child produced from a relationship Scathach herself considers emotionally meaningful in every respect, giving her a unique place in her mother's heart without replacing or diminishing Scathach's attachment to Ruby and her adopted daughters.

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Siren grows through the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s immense accelerated eras as part of a second generation raised under standards almost unrecognizable by the old supernatural world. The temporal project initiated by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his family was designed to grant the Empire approximately 200,000 years of internal development, and Victor deliberately left the younger generation free to use those millennia to mature rather than forcing them to sustain the dangerous ritual themselves.[9] Siren had already been born long before this project began and therefore develops across an enormous subjective lifespan in an environment populated by [[True Dragons]], Dragon Goddesses, ancient martial masters and increasingly powerful members of her own generation.
Her mature priorities become explicit during Victor and Violet's discussion of how enormous the Elderblood family may eventually become. Violet acknowledges that not every daughter is interested in producing another generation and specifically identifies daughters who train more, such as Siren, as examples of those who do not presently want children.[3] The statement establishes martial development as one of Siren's defining long-term choices rather than an activity imposed upon her because she is [[Scathach]]'s daughter.
Despite that severe focus, Siren remains closely integrated with her enormous family. During the Imperial Princess gathering, she joins Erika, Priscillia, Rosemary, Sylvia and Lucrecia in the group responsible for the mischief surrounding Velina. When Victor confronts his red-haired daughters about the chaos, Siren blushes with the others, while Victor quickly identifies that Velina had enough power to escape at any moment and had ultimately participated willingly in her sisters' game.[7] The incident provides one of the clearest glimpses of Siren away from training and shows that the intensely martial princess is still perfectly capable of joining the ordinary disorder created by Victor's daughters.
Chapter 1173 provides the clearest demonstration yet of how far Siren's training has progressed. While [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]] is being forced through fundamental True Dragon training under Scathach, the surrounding daughters retreat rather than volunteer for the same treatment. Siren alone remains. Ignoring the reactions of her sisters, she approaches Scathach because she actively wants to participate and directly asks whether she can train as well; Scathach immediately accepts.[4]
The scale of Siren's previous conditioning is revealed moments later. Albedo is equipped with gravity restraints calibrated through astronomical rather than ordinary numerical references, with progressively higher settings modeled after stellar bodies and the extreme endpoint associated with black-hole or singularity conditions. When Albedo protests the severity of the exercise, Scathach simply points toward Siren and reveals that her daughter had already withstood training corresponding to the theoretically overwhelming gravity of a singularity.[4] Scathach uses Siren as proof of the mentality she expects from Dragon Gods: what appears impossible is something to test rather than automatically accept as a permanent limitation.
Siren's mature characterization therefore fulfills the promise established at her birth without relying solely upon inherited talent. The child once foreshadowed through uncontrolled destructive potential becomes an ancient Dragon Goddess who deliberately seeks harsher training, has survived physical conditioning modeled upon singularity-level gravitational conditions and continues asking Scathach for more even when her sisters consider doing so madness.[4]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Siren's biological father and the origin of the Blood Dragon lineage into which she was born. Siren's existence is foreshadowed through Victor's own vision in Chapter 855, long before he and [[Scathach]] actually produce their daughter.[6] As with the rest of his children, Victor's influence extends far beyond genetics: Siren grows inside the civilization, family and Dragon lineage created around him, inheriting the violet Dragon Eyes shared by his biological daughters and developing during the enormous Imperial eras he establishes for the younger generation.[8][9]
Victor is intensely affectionate toward his daughters but does not attempt to reduce them to weapons simply because of their extraordinary natural abilities. Siren's decision to devote herself heavily to training therefore reflects her own preferences rather than a military role forced upon her by her father. Chapter 1166 also shows her participating freely in the chaotic family life Victor encourages among his daughters, while his handling of Velina's complaint makes clear that competition and mischief between sisters are acceptable so long as they never become genuine hatred or deliberate harm.[7]

#### Scathach Elderblood Scarlett
[[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] is Siren's biological mother, principal martial influence and the person Siren is most explicitly stated to resemble. [[Scathach]] carried Siren for approximately 1,670 subjective years and, after her daughter's birth, regarded her as determined, filled with potential and very much like herself.[1][2] Siren also occupies a particularly personal position in Scathach's life because she was conceived with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], the man Scathach herself chose as both partner and rival rather than someone selected merely for biological suitability.
The attachment does not cause Scathach to lower her standards. If anything, Siren becomes one of the strongest demonstrations of Scathach's philosophy toward raising extraordinarily powerful children. By the late Imperial era Siren has already endured training under gravity corresponding to singularity conditions, yet still voluntarily remains when Scathach begins another brutal training session and asks to participate.[4] Scathach responds happily, suggesting that Siren's enthusiasm for her methods is exactly the kind of mentality she values in a daughter.
Their bond is also symbolized by Siren's name. "Siren" was the identity Scathach used while training beneath [[Dún Scáith|Dun Scaith]] before deliberately abandoning it and becoming known as Scathach.[5] Passing the old name to her daughter connects Siren not merely to Clan Scarlett but to the formative period from which Scathach's own martial legend eventually emerged.

#### Ruby Elderblood Scarlett
[[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] is Siren's maternal half-sister and [[Scathach]]'s other biological daughter. The two were born during radically different stages of their mother's existence: Ruby was born while Scathach remained an ancient Noble Vampire, whereas Siren was born after Scathach had become a True Dragon Goddess and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had established the Blood Dragon lineage. Chapter 988 distinguishes the emotional circumstances surrounding their births without suggesting that one daughter replaced the other; Scathach loves Ruby deeply, while Siren represents the first biological child she produced with the man she personally chose as her husband.[2]

#### Scarlett Family
[[Siena Scarlett Elderblood]], [[Lacus Scarlett Elderblood]] and [[Pepper Scarlett Elderblood]] are [[Scathach]]'s adopted daughters and therefore Siren's immediate maternal sisters. Together with Ruby, they connect Siren to a family whose members had already spent centuries or millennia training beneath Scathach before Siren herself was born. Siren consequently inherits not only [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood Dragon lineage but the martial culture of Clan Scarlett, while later generations further blur the distinction between the Scarlett and Elderblood branches as Scathach's daughters establish children and families of their own.

### Skills & Abilities
- Blood Dragon Goddess Physiology: Siren is a naturally born Blood Dragon and Dragon Goddess produced after both of her parents had attained True Dragon existences. Unlike many first-generation members of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family, she did not require transformation from a Human, Vampire or another pre-existing species to obtain her Draconic foundation.[1]
- True Dragon Physiology: Siren possesses the body, Soul and supernatural foundation of a genuine True Dragon. By the late Imperial era, [[True Dragons]] are described as existences deeply compatible with Reality itself, capable of commanding ordinary Energy through their biology rather than depending upon external magical structures for every effect.[4]
- Enhanced Condition: Her True Dragon body provides immense supernatural strength, speed, durability, stamina, reflexes and physical resilience. Siren's personal conditioning is exceptional even among the Elderblood family, as demonstrated by [[Scathach]] using her performance as the standard another Dragon Goddess should aspire toward.[4]
- Regenerative Healing Factor: As a Blood Dragon, Siren possesses the regenerative biological foundation characteristic of Victor's Draconic lineage.
- Longevity: True Dragons possess extreme lifespans, and Siren has already personally experienced enormous amounts of subjective Time through the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s accelerated eras.
- [[Draconic Energy]]: Siren naturally possesses and manipulates the Energy produced by True Dragon physiology.
- [[Dragon Heart]]: As a complete Blood Dragon, Siren possesses the Draconic internal foundation responsible for sustaining and circulating her enormous reserves of Energy.
- Draconic Shapeshifting: Her True Dragon nature provides the racial foundation for changing between humanoid and Draconic manifestations, although Siren's complete Dragon Form has not yet received a detailed individual description.
- Dragon Breath: Siren possesses the racial foundation for Dragon Breath. The exact element, Concept or special properties of her personal Breath remain unrevealed.
- Dragon Language: Siren possesses the natural compatibility of True Dragons with their [[Will Solace|Will]]-bearing language, which allows sufficiently developed Dragons to impose commands upon Energy and Reality.
- Draconic Runes: Siren's race gives her natural compatibility with Draconic Runes. Chapter 1173 emphasizes that Dragon Runes are primarily used when imposing Concepts, laws or alterations upon Reality rather than as a crutch for ordinary actions that a True Dragon can already perform instinctively.[4] Her exact level of individual Runic mastery has not yet been stated.
- Exceptional Martial Potential: Siren's potential is one of the earliest qualities explicitly emphasized about her. Scathach, one of the greatest martial instructors in the setting, considers her daughter full of potential and strikingly similar to herself.[2] This is reinforced by Siren's willingness to continue training across immense periods of subjective Time rather than becoming dependent upon the power she inherited at birth.
- Extreme Training Drive: Siren is explicitly identified as one of Victor's daughters who trains more heavily and prioritizes that development over having children.[3] When given an opportunity to avoid Scathach's training alongside her sisters, she instead remains behind and asks to participate.[4]
- Extreme Physical Conditioning: Scathach has trained Siren under conditions severe enough that singularity-level gravitational pressure is treated as something her daughter has already endured.[4]
- Pain and Hardship Tolerance: Although no numerical pain threshold has been provided, Siren's willingness to repeat Scathach's training after experiencing its extreme conditions demonstrates exceptional tolerance for physical hardship.
- [[Martial Arts]]: Siren has undergone extensive martial training under Scathach across the accelerated Imperial eras. Her exact formal rank has not yet been identified, and she should not be listed as a Grandmaster until the novel explicitly establishes that level.
- Gravity Resistance / Extreme Durability: Siren has withstood a training setting described as reproducing the theoretically overwhelming gravity associated with a singularity.[4] This is a directly demonstrated training-endurance statement and one of Siren's clearest personal feats. It should not automatically be converted into unrelated offensive scaling, but it establishes extraordinary resistance to gravitational compression and physical stress.
- Dragon Eyes / Supernatural Perception: Victor's biological daughters inherit violet eyes containing variations of their father's ability to perceive deeper truths of Reality. The potency differs between daughters and can improve with continued training.[8] Siren possesses the same inherited foundation, although the novel has not yet individually demonstrated the exact range, depth or specialized applications of her version.
- Foreshadowed Destructive Potential: Victor's Chapter 855 vision depicts his future daughter with Scathach crying with enough force or supernatural effect to devastate an entire country.[6] The scene establishes extraordinary potential but occurs before Siren's chronological birth and has not yet been reproduced by present-day Siren.
- Unknown Mechanism: The novel does not state whether the destruction was produced by raw Energy, an emotional outburst, a Divine Concept, Dragon physiology or another ability. Siren should therefore not be assigned Sound Manipulation, Vibration Manipulation, Voice Manipulation, Destruction Manipulation or a named "destructive cry" ability solely from this scene.
- Divinity (Unrevealed): Siren is a Dragon Goddess, but the canonical identity of her personal Divine Concept has not yet been revealed. Her relationship to Scathach does not automatically grant her Scathach's <War>, <Teaching> or <Ice> authorities, nor has the novel confirmed that she inherited every Divine or elemental characteristic possessed by either parent.
- Immense Growth Through Accelerated Time: Siren was already born before the enormous Imperial temporal project that provided roughly 200,000 years of internal development.[1][9] Consequently, the late-volume Siren is not merely a young child possessing inherited potential but an ancient supernatural being who has had enormous subjective periods available for training. The exact total amount of time she personally experienced has not been individually stated.

### Notable Feats
- Before her chronological birth, a vision witnessed by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] depicts the future child of himself and [[Scathach]] causing country-scale devastation by crying. This remains a foreshadowed rather than confirmed present-timeline feat.[6]

### Name
The name Siren predates Siren Elderblood herself. Before she became known as [[Scathach]], her mother trained beneath [[Dun Scaith Elderblood]] while using the identity Siren. After completing that stage of her life she abandoned the old identity and adopted the name Scathach, to the point that Dun addressing her by the former name thousands of years later immediately irritated her.[5]
The novel has not established Siren as Scathach's original birth name, making it more accurate to describe it as her former identity or old name during her training beneath [[Dún Scáith|Dun Scaith]]. Its later reuse for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Scathach's biological daughter gives the name a new meaning within Clan Elderblood: an identity once associated with Scathach's own formative training became the name of the daughter who would eventually display the same extraordinary appetite for training.
