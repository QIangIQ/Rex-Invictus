#### Debut
Chapter 991
Where's my father? Where's my DADDY?
—Gina's thoughts while searching for [[Victor Elderblood]], Chapter 991
Gina Elderblood is a recurring character in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]], an Imperial Princess of the [[Elderblood Empire]], and the biological daughter of Victor Elderblood and [[Gaia]] Elderblood. She is a naturally born Blood Dragon Goddess possessing the exceptional biological foundation of a genuine True Dragon alongside an unusually advanced Divine connection to the Concept of Life. Explicitly described as a Primordial Dragon Goddess of Life, Gina inherited the dominant Draconic characteristics of Victor and the extraordinary Life-related potential of Gaia, producing a supernatural existence whose biological development differs considerably from that of ordinary Dragons and even several members of her own generation.[1]
Gina is considered an anomaly among Victor's naturally born daughters because of the extraordinary influence her Divine Concept exercises over her physical development. Although she had existed for only approximately twenty years when she was first introduced, her connection to Life accelerated the maturation of her Draconic body until she had already reached an adolescent developmental stage by True Dragon standards. As a result, she could naturally maintain a mature Humanoid appearance approximately 190 centimeters tall despite being considerably younger than sisters who had already experienced around a thousand years of subjective life. This development originated from the genuine growth of her biological existence rather than merely an artificial transformation intended to imitate an older appearance.[1]
Her supernatural inheritance places her among the daughters born from parents possessing particularly advanced Divine characteristics. Gaia's association with Life and Nature combines with Victor's evolved Blood Dragon physiology, giving Gina extraordinary potential for further biological and conceptual development. However, her status as a Primordial Dragon Goddess should not be confused with membership among the [[Regulators of Existence]]. Gina possesses a highly developed relationship with the Concept of Life, but she has not been established as the native Universe's completed Primordial Regulator responsible for governing every manifestation of Life throughout Creation.
Although her powers are exceptional, Gina's most prominent personal characteristic is her dedication to self-improvement. She possesses an intense, competitive and occasionally intimidating personality, but gradually develops a disciplined approach toward combat, practical responsibility and the development of her sisters. Rather than remaining satisfied with the extraordinary strength inherited through her parents, she trains regularly and insists that natural talent is meaningless unless its bearer learns to employ it properly. Her disagreement with [[Isabelle Elderblood]], who possesses overwhelming authority over Energy but initially places less importance upon martial development, demonstrates this philosophy particularly clearly.[2][3]
Gina also develops a genuine desire to contribute to the civilization established by Victor. Alongside [[Stella Elderblood]], she approaches [[Anna Elderblood]] and asks to become involved in Imperial administration rather than remain permanently sheltered within the family's private territories. Although Anna initially considers the girls insufficiently experienced for the responsibilities associated with government, Victor recognizes that their motivation has changed from ordinary boredom to a sincere desire to assist him. He consequently permits them to begin supervised administrative training with temporary authorization to observe Imperial operations classified at G-5 and below.[2]
Her abilities extend beyond physical power and biological growth. Gina possesses the advanced Dragon Eyes inherited by Victor's biological daughters, alongside exceptional supernatural senses and the potential to perceive deeper aspects of Reality. During Victor's lessons concerning Eldritch existence, she correctly recognizes that seemingly perfect replicas of cosmic horrors are not genuinely alive, distinguishing their outward appearance from the absence of the fundamental characteristics associated with independent Life. Her observation demonstrates an important application of her inherited perception and conceptual affinity, although her complete mastery over the underlying structure of Reality remains unidentified.[4]
Despite her serious disposition and formidable martial potential, Gina remains an affectionate daughter who is deeply attached to Victor. She frequently seeks his attention, treasures personal objects associated with him and can become almost comically possessive when competing with her sisters for his belongings. Her interests also include food, particularly unusual supernatural creatures, and she becomes visibly fascinated by the possibility of eating an enormous Star Beast during one of the family's later gatherings. These contrasting characteristics make Gina a distinctive member of the Elderblood family, combining the temperament of a competitive warrior with an affectionate and occasionally impulsive personality.[1][5]

### Physical Appearance
Gina is an exceptionally beautiful and physically imposing young Dragon Goddess with pale skin, long dark-green hair, prominent green Dragon horns and deep-violet eyes. Her mature Humanoid appearance stands approximately 190 centimeters tall, making her considerably taller than many of her younger sisters during their earliest appearances. Her coloration reflects her maternal inheritance from [[Gaia]], particularly through the green hair and horns associated with her extraordinary connection to Life and Nature, while her violet Dragon Eyes originate from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood Dragon lineage.[1]
Unlike several daughters who use supernatural metamorphosis to imitate more mature versions of themselves, Gina's advanced appearance is supported by genuine biological development. Her unusually powerful Concept of Life accelerated the natural maturation of her Draconic body, allowing her to reach a developmental stage comparable to that of an adolescent Dragon despite having lived for only approximately twenty years at her introduction. Her adult Humanoid form is therefore not simply a cosmetic disguise created through Reality manipulation, although her status as a True Dragon also provides the general transformative potential associated with the Race.[1]
Her physical development becomes increasingly apparent as she continues training throughout the later Imperial era. Gina possesses a powerful, athletic figure reflecting her dedication to martial development and physical conditioning. Rather than treating her extraordinary natural strength as sufficient, she actively improves her combat abilities and takes pride in maintaining a body suited to prolonged physical activity. Her imposing stature and warrior-like presentation distinguish her from sisters whose supernatural strength is expressed primarily through magic or conceptual authority, although her exact later physical measurements have not been independently established.[3]
Gina's eyes are among her most striking characteristics. Their deep-violet coloration is inherited from Victor, and they can produce an intimidating supernatural effect when she directs her attention toward individuals substantially weaker than herself. During her introductory appearance, her serious expression and intense gaze cause the castle's servants to move out of her path, while a Werewolf Maid becomes terrified when Gina briefly focuses her eyes upon her. The Maid experiences the gaze as though her life could end at any moment, despite Gina having made no threat and possessing no intention of harming her.[1]
The distinction between appearance and behavior is particularly important because Gina's imposing presence does not necessarily reflect hostility. She can appear furious even when her actual concern is simply locating Victor, and her supernatural senses allow her to notice details that ordinary observers would overlook. When questioning the frightened Maid, Gina merely asks where her father is, accepts the woman's answer and thanks her before leaving. The servant's reaction results from the overwhelming natural presence of a True Dragon combined with Gina's severe demeanor rather than a deliberate act of intimidation.[1]
Gina's natural appearance also expresses the different characteristics inherited from her parents. The green coloration of her hair and horns connects her visually to Gaia, while her violet eyes and powerful Draconic body reflect Victor's dominant Blood Dragon lineage. Her physical resemblance to her mother is contrasted with their personalities during a later family gathering, when Melina expresses surprise that the intense and physically imposing Gina is Gaia's daughter. Gaia reacts with embarrassment because she remembers behaving considerably more like Gina during an earlier stage of her own existence, revealing that their personalities possess greater similarities than their current demeanor initially suggests.[5]
As a naturally born True Dragon Goddess, Gina possesses a fundamentally Draconic biological and spiritual nature beneath her Humanoid appearance. However, the complete appearance of her individual True Dragon Form has not been established in sufficient detail to describe its precise size, scale coloration, anatomical characteristics or the properties of her Dragon Breath. Her confirmed individual features therefore remain her long dark-green hair, green horns, violet Dragon Eyes, pale skin, tall stature and unusually accelerated physical development.

### Personality
Gina is serious, determined, competitive, straightforward and intensely devoted to her family, particularly [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. She possesses a naturally explosive temperament influenced by the characteristics of her Draconic lineage and the more impulsive aspects of Victor's younger personality, but she is not ordinarily cruel or malicious toward weaker individuals. Her intimidating appearance frequently causes others to assume that she is angry, even when she has no intention of threatening them. Nevertheless, she can become genuinely irritated when circumstances frustrate her, and her dedication to improving herself sometimes leads her to speak harshly toward sisters whose attitudes she considers complacent.[1][2]
Her introductory behavior demonstrates the difference between her intimidating demeanor and her actual treatment of others. Gina searches the castle for Victor with such a severe expression that servants instinctively avoid her. When she questions a Werewolf Maid, her gaze terrifies the woman, but she responds to the answer with a simple acknowledgment and a polite expression of thanks. The Maid subsequently collapses from the accumulated tension, illustrating how Gina's natural presence can frighten weaker beings even without deliberate aggression. Her behavior reflects intensity and impatience rather than an inclination toward unnecessary violence.[1]
Like most of Victor's biological daughters, Gina possesses an extraordinary emotional attachment to her father. Her first appearance revolves around the frustration she experiences when she cannot locate him, and her internal thoughts become increasingly dramatic with every unsuccessful attempt to discover his whereabouts. She is distressed despite having been separated from him for only a few hours, revealing that her desire for his affection is not diminished by her extraordinary chronological experience or supernatural nature. This attachment also influences her relationship with her sisters, particularly when valuable personal belongings associated with Victor become involved.[1]
Gina possesses exceptional observational ability and is difficult to deceive when something concerning Victor attracts her attention. When she encounters Angelina near his private quarters, she immediately detects the residual scent of their father upon her sister. She recognizes that the smell is too weak to indicate recent direct contact and deduces that Angelina must be carrying one of Victor's belongings. Angelina initially denies the accusation, but Gina refuses to abandon the matter, eventually forcing her sister to negotiate rather than continue pretending that nothing has happened. The incident demonstrates Gina's combination of supernatural perception, persistence and possessiveness.[1]
Although Gina normally maintains a stern expression, her demeanor changes dramatically when Angelina presents Victor's shirt and a photograph of him sleeping. Gina becomes visibly greedy and struggles to conceal her excitement, eventually accepting Angelina's proposal that they keep the incident secret in exchange for the items. Her willingness to compromise reveals that her affection for Victor can overwhelm her usual seriousness, while Angelina's successful negotiation demonstrates that Gina is not immune to the same emotional weaknesses shared by her sisters. The exchange also reflects the family's unusually intense attachment to personal objects associated with their father.[1]
Gina is genuinely protective of her siblings. When Angelina attempts to redirect blame toward her after their mothers discover them near Victor's quarters, Gina becomes angry at the deception but does not abandon the principle that members of the family should support one another. During the subsequent conversation, she declares that she will not allow anything to happen to her sisters while she is present, reflecting the protective instincts encouraged by Victor and his wives. Her attachment therefore extends beyond possessiveness toward her father and includes a sincere desire to defend the younger generation from external danger.[1]
Her temperament gradually develops into a more mature form of determination. Gina becomes dissatisfied with remaining an Imperial Princess whose responsibilities consist primarily of training and enjoying the protection of her parents. Alongside Stella, she approaches Anna and asks to participate in governing the Empire, believing that she has reached a stage where she should begin contributing directly to the civilization Victor created. Although she lacks a complete understanding of the practical responsibilities associated with administration, her request is sincere, and she is willing to accept criticism rather than simply demand a prestigious position through her familial status.[2]
Anna recognizes that Gina possesses greater patience than Stella but remains concerned about her tendency to become angry when placed under prolonged pressure. The responsibility of governing an Empire involves endless reports, difficult decisions, complicated social relationships and the obligation to maintain a composed public image regardless of personal frustration. Gina initially finds the refusal disappointing, but she listens carefully when Anna explains that exceptional combat strength does not automatically translate into administrative competence. Her willingness to accept these limitations demonstrates that she has begun developing the judgment necessary for greater responsibilities.[2]
Gina also possesses an exceptionally competitive personality, particularly concerning martial development. She regularly trains and spars with her sisters, regarding practical combat experience as an essential complement to natural power. This philosophy becomes especially apparent through her rivalry with Isabelle, whose extraordinary authority over Energy provides one of the strongest inherited supernatural foundations among Victor's daughters. Gina rejects the suggestion that such power makes further physical training unnecessary, arguing that possessing overwhelming strength is meaningless if its owner does not understand how to apply it efficiently against a capable opponent.[3]
Her confrontations with Isabelle also reveal a mischievous and manipulative side. Gina understands that Isabelle particularly dislikes being described as weak, so she deliberately provokes her by emphasizing their previous sparring results and questioning her commitment to improvement. Isabelle recognizes that her sister is attempting to manipulate her into another training session, but becomes sufficiently irritated to accept the challenge anyway. Gina takes satisfaction in the success of her provocation, not because she wishes to humiliate Isabelle permanently, but because she considers the development of her sister's combat abilities more important than avoiding temporary irritation.[3]
Gina's competitive nature is closely connected to the principles Victor and [[Scathach]] emphasize throughout the younger generation's education. Their family possesses immense natural abilities, but the existence of enemies with comparable or greater supernatural power makes practical skill, adaptation and judgment essential. Gina internalizes this philosophy and eventually begins imposing it upon others, refusing to allow sisters with extraordinary talents to become complacent. Her own growth therefore demonstrates the difference between merely possessing advanced inherited powers and actively developing the experience necessary to employ them effectively.
Despite her serious disposition, Gina possesses a playful and occasionally gluttonous side. During one of Victor's later family gatherings, she becomes fascinated by the corpse of an enormous Star Beast previously hunted by the Empire's Leviathans. While Rosemary discusses the relevant military report and Melina comments upon her apparent differences from [[Gaia]], Gina remains almost entirely distracted by the possibility of eating the creature. She barely notices the conversation around her, revealing that unusual food can capture her attention just as completely as combat or the desire to find Victor.[5]
Her curiosity also extends to the fundamental structure of Reality. During Victor's educational lessons, Gina participates in discussions concerning genuine Life, Divine Concepts and the hierarchy of Primordial existence. She recognizes that artificial Eldritch organisms lack the essential characteristics of living beings and later asks questions about the relationship between the Creator, [[Primordial Chaos]] and the authorities established within different Pantheons. These interactions demonstrate that her serious personality is accompanied by intellectual curiosity and a desire to understand the deeper principles governing her family's extraordinary powers.[4][6]
Gina consequently combines the formidable presence of a naturally born Dragon Goddess with the emotional attachment, competitiveness and developing maturity of a young member of the Imperial Family. Although she possesses extraordinary inherited advantages, her desire to earn responsibility, improve her combat skills and encourage her sisters to become stronger distinguishes her from individuals who rely exclusively upon their natural power.

### Heritage
Gina is the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Gaia]] Elderblood, inheriting an extraordinary combination of Draconic physiology and conceptual affinity for Life. Victor is the Progenitor of the [[Blood Dragons]], whose evolved existence possesses an exceptionally dominant Bloodline capable of incorporating compatible characteristics from other supernatural beings without fundamentally weakening the natural True Dragon foundation of his descendants. Gaia is an ancient Goddess whose existence is closely associated with Earth, Nature and Life, possessing an exceptional relationship with the processes governing biological growth and the development of living beings.[1][4]
Their union produced a daughter whose Concept of Life was unusually developed from birth. Rather than manifesting solely as the potential for future healing or biological manipulation, Gina's Divine inheritance immediately influenced the maturation of her own body. Her accelerated growth allowed approximately twenty years of life to produce a developmental stage that would ordinarily require considerably longer periods for a naturally born Dragon, demonstrating that her Concept operates upon fundamental biological processes rather than merely altering outward appearance.[1]
Gina's inheritance also places her among the second-generation daughters born from parents possessing powers associated with fundamental aspects of existence. Her early development is compared with that of Stella and Isabelle, whose supernatural inheritances likewise produce unusual characteristics beyond those of ordinary young Dragons. Although each possesses a different conceptual foundation, their growth illustrates the exceptional results produced when Victor's Bloodline combines with the advanced Divine characteristics of his wives.[1]
The Blood Dragon lineage provides Gina with a natural True Dragon body, exceptional physical attributes, powerful supernatural senses, a [[Draconic Energy]] foundation and the inherited ocular potential associated with Victor's Dragon Eyes. Gaia's maternal influence contributes the unusual Life-related characteristics responsible for Gina's advanced maturation, while Victor's Eldritch heritage provides additional traits shared by his naturally born daughters. These inheritances interact within a single Draconic existence rather than producing an ordinary hybrid whose characteristics are divided equally between incompatible [[Races (Flamel)|Races]].[1][4]
Her title of Primordial Dragon Goddess of Life describes the extraordinary development of her Divine nature, but does not automatically establish that she occupies the completed cosmic office of a Primordial Regulator. The native Universe contains higher conceptual authorities responsible for maintaining fundamental aspects of Creation, and such positions are distinct from possessing a powerful affinity with the corresponding Concept. Gina has demonstrated remarkable control over her own biological development and exceptional perception of genuine Life, but the complete scope of her conceptual authority remains unidentified.

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Gina was born during the extraordinary period of accelerated development experienced by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his family following the establishment of his mature Blood Dragon lineage. Her birth resulted from the union of Victor and [[Gaia]], producing a naturally born True Dragon Goddess possessing an exceptionally advanced Concept of Life. Although several of her older sisters had already experienced approximately a thousand years of subjective life, Gina had existed for only around twenty years when she was introduced. Nevertheless, the influence of her Divine Concept accelerated the genuine maturation of her Dragon body until she had already reached an adolescent developmental stage, allowing her to maintain a mature Humanoid form approximately 190 centimeters tall.[1]
Gina's introduction begins while she is searching the family's castle for Victor. Her frustration increases because she cannot locate him, causing her already severe expression to become increasingly intimidating. The servants instinctively leave her path, recognizing that her temper can become explosive even though Victor forbids unnecessary violence toward those serving his household. When Gina stops a Werewolf Maid and demands to know where her father has gone, the woman becomes terrified beneath the intensity of her violet Dragon Eyes. She suggests contacting Kaguya, Violet or Velnorah because they are among the individuals most likely to know Victor's location, and Gina simply acknowledges the information before thanking her and continuing her search.[1]
The Maid collapses after Gina departs, overwhelmed by the pressure of being questioned by a naturally born True Dragon. Another servant approaches and discovers that the woman has become so frightened that she needs assistance returning to the bathroom. Gina remains unaware of the severity of her reaction, since she has neither threatened nor physically harmed the Maid. Her behavior demonstrates the difficulty ordinary supernatural beings can experience when interacting with Victor's daughters, whose overwhelming natural characteristics remain intimidating even when their intentions are harmless.[1]
Gina eventually encounters [[Angelina Elderblood]] near Victor's private quarters. Angelina claims that she entered the area because she was also searching for their father, but Gina immediately detects a faint trace of Victor's scent lingering around her sister. She recognizes that the scent is too weak to indicate direct physical contact and concludes that Angelina must be carrying one of his possessions. When Gina demands that the object be returned, Angelina initially pretends not to understand the accusation, but quickly realizes that her sister's supernatural senses have made further denial ineffective.[1]
Angelina eventually produces one of Victor's shirts from her personal dimensional storage. The item had been kept within an advanced storage space created through the combined technological and supernatural achievements of Victor, [[Hephaestus]] and Natalia, allowing the Imperial Princesses to carry personal belongings inside pocket dimensions. Gina immediately recognizes the stronger presence of Victor's scent upon the shirt and becomes visibly interested, struggling to maintain her stern expression while contemplating the possibility of obtaining it for herself.[1]
Rather than simply surrendering the item, Angelina proposes an agreement. She offers the shirt in exchange for Gina remaining silent about the unauthorized visit, but Gina hesitates because she recognizes that her sister is attempting to avoid responsibility. Angelina consequently produces an additional photograph of Victor sleeping, copied from a collection maintained by one of their mothers. The second item immediately changes Gina's attitude, and she enthusiastically accepts the bargain before taking possession of both objects. Her reaction reveals that the desire for rare belongings associated with Victor can overcome even her normally practical judgment.[1]
The sisters' arrangement is interrupted when Natashia, Natasha, Carmila and Victoria arrive outside Victor's quarters. Angelina immediately attempts to redirect responsibility toward Gina, claiming that she had encountered her sister already carrying Victor's shirt and had merely become involved afterward. Gina is outraged by the deception because Angelina has deliberately made it appear that she initiated the incident, although the adults quickly recognize that the explanation is incomplete. Rather than simply punishing the girls, their mothers use the opportunity to reinforce the principles governing the Elderblood family, particularly the importance of loyalty toward one's siblings and accepting responsibility for one's own actions.[1]
The women acknowledge that Angelina's attempt to avoid complete blame demonstrates a certain degree of cunning, but remind her that such behavior should not be used dishonestly against her own family. They distinguish the expectations governing relationships within the household from those applying when dealing with dangerous external enemies, explaining that survival and protecting one's relatives may justify considerably more deceptive methods under hostile circumstances. Gina declares that she will not allow anything to happen to her sisters while she remains present, revealing the protective side of her personality despite her irritation toward Angelina.[1]
Gina's own behavior also becomes a subject of amusement when Natasha notices that she is still holding Victor's shirt. The observation causes Gina to blush, and she hurriedly stores the precious items inside her personal dimensional storage belt. Their mothers subsequently instruct the girls to return to the daughters' quarters and wait for Victor, who is expected to visit them after completing his other responsibilities. Both Gina and Angelina immediately become excited and leave, demonstrating that their disagreement has not diminished their shared desire to spend time with their father.[1]
As Gina and her sisters continued developing, Victor became increasingly concerned that their extraordinary natural powers were preventing them from gaining practical experience. Most had been born with genuine True Dragon bodies, powerful Divine characteristics and the support of parents whose abilities surpassed those of nearly every established supernatural civilization within the Sector. Although these advantages provided exceptional safety and opportunities, Victor recognized that they could also produce adults who depended excessively upon raw power and struggled when confronting opponents capable of matching or countering their inherited abilities.[7]
Victor consequently organized a series of military exercises intended to develop adaptability, leadership, judgment and the ability to function under unfamiliar supernatural limitations. The initial War Games involved several daughters commanding opposing armies while Victor observed their decisions and explained the practical consequences of their mistakes. The soldiers were sophisticated artificial constructs capable of behaving according to realistic military and social expectations, allowing the Princesses to encounter difficulties involving fear, morale, resources, preparation and strategy without endangering genuine Imperial personnel.[8][9]
The exercise eventually expanded into a much more complicated Dream-based simulation through the upgraded Tower of Nightmare. Victor established an artificial world in which his daughters could receive unfamiliar [[Races (Flamel)|Races]], supernatural powers and political circumstances, deliberately reducing the advantages associated with their natural Blood Dragon identities. Their physical capabilities were initially restricted to levels appropriate for the simulated conflict, while their senses and supernatural knowledge were adjusted to prevent them from overcoming every problem through abilities unavailable to ordinary participants.[10]
Gina was selected alongside Rosemary, Stella and Yuno to participate directly in the expanded simulation. Victor assigned each daughter a different supernatural identity and transported them to separate locations, requiring them to respond to the new circumstances independently. Rosemary became a Fairy Queen surrounded by mischievous Fairies, Stella assumed the role of a Demi-Goddess within a Human civilization experiencing internal conflict, and Yuno became a Necromancer situated within a cemetery. Gina was transformed into a Dryad and placed within a tropical forest, losing the ordinary advantages associated with her natural Dragon body.[10]
Rather than reacting with panic or frustration, Gina examined the surrounding trees and calmly concluded that she could make the unfamiliar circumstances work in her favor. Her reaction was brief but demonstrated precisely the adaptability Victor wished to cultivate. Although Gina possessed extraordinary natural powers, she was capable of considering the opportunities presented by an unfamiliar Race instead of concentrating exclusively upon the abilities she had temporarily lost. Her willingness to participate in the exercise also reflected a developing awareness that practical skill and judgment remained valuable regardless of the supernatural foundation available to an individual.[10]
The War Games eventually expanded to include more daughters and several of Victor's wives, each receiving different roles within the Dream World. Victor intended the environment to provide experience involving political relationships, the management of resources, warfare and the consequences of decisions made by individuals lacking overwhelming natural strength. The simulation reinforced the importance of considering circumstances from the perspective of other Races and supernatural systems rather than assuming that every problem could be resolved through direct combat.[10]
Gina's participation became one of the experiences contributing to her desire for greater responsibility. As the Empire continued developing, she began recognizing that her exceptional natural abilities would mean relatively little if she remained permanently dependent upon her parents without attempting to contribute to their civilization. Her growing maturity consequently encouraged her to seek an active role within the Imperial structure rather than treating her status as a Princess as a reason to avoid difficult responsibilities.
Gina eventually approached Anna alongside Stella and directly requested permission to begin working within the [[Elderblood Empire]]. The two sisters explained that they wished to help Victor rather than remain indefinitely inside the family's protected territories, surprising Anna because their motivation appeared more serious than previous attempts to participate in administrative activities. Rather than immediately refusing, Anna asked them to explain how they expected to contribute to a civilization whose military and governmental institutions already possessed enormous numbers of experienced personnel.[2]
Anna acknowledged Gina's considerable combat ability and training, but explained that the Empire already possessed elite soldiers, powerful Dragons, Demons, Leviathans, the [[Blank Clan]], Victor's Heralds and exceptionally accomplished warriors such as [[Scathach]]. The existence of another powerful combatant would not automatically provide a meaningful administrative advantage, particularly when the Imperial Princesses lacked practical experience managing the enormous responsibilities associated with their father's civilization. She also reminded them that the behavior of a member of the ruling family carried political consequences far beyond those of an ordinary citizen.[2]
The expectations placed upon Imperial Princesses were particularly demanding because every public appearance represented Victor and the dynasty he had established. Their words, conduct and decisions could influence how the Empire was perceived across enormous populations, meaning that even a minor mistake might damage the reputation their parents had worked to create. Anna emphasized the importance of composure, diplomacy, appropriate presentation and the capacity to withstand endless reports and difficult decisions without allowing frustration to dictate behavior. Gina listened seriously despite finding the explanation discouraging.[2]
Anna privately recognized that Gina possessed greater patience than Stella, although both retained a tendency toward anger inherited partly from Victor's earlier temperament and the naturally volatile characteristics of Dragons. She therefore doubted that either daughter was ready to endure the continuous pressure associated with Imperial administration. Her concern was not that they lacked intelligence or strength, but that they had not yet developed sufficient experience interacting with the political and practical burdens of government. Gina nevertheless accepted the criticism, understanding that she was being treated seriously rather than dismissed through an arbitrary parental refusal.[2]
Victor, who had been listening through his connection with Anna, intervened after recognizing the sincerity of the daughters' disappointment. He understood that their previous interest in Imperial work had often originated from curiosity or boredom, whereas Gina and Stella now genuinely wished to contribute. Their experience in the Dream World had also improved their practical abilities, making supervised observation a reasonable next step. Victor consequently instructed Anna to organize a group of interested daughters, grant them temporary authorization to access Imperial operations classified at G-5 and below, and arrange for his Heralds to oversee their security while they traveled through the Empire.[2]
The authorization was deliberately limited. Gina and the other Princesses were expected to observe how their parents and experienced officials conducted government rather than immediately assume unrestricted authority over major departments. Anna warned that any behavior disrupting established operations, embarrassing the Imperial Family or interfering with subordinate officials would result in the girls being returned home for an extended period. Gina accepted the conditions with enthusiasm, recognizing that supervised participation offered an opportunity to acquire the knowledge and experience necessary for future responsibilities.[2]
Victor also considered the possibility that his daughters might eventually become responsible for governing entire planets or Galaxies depending upon their interests and demonstrated competence. He understood that their exceptional intelligence and supernatural abilities provided enormous potential, but that such responsibilities required practical experience rather than automatic appointments based upon their parentage. Gina's entrance into Imperial training consequently represented the beginning of her development as a possible future administrator rather than confirmation that she had already received a permanent executive or military position.[2]

#### Those Who Rule Reality

Gina takes satisfaction in the resulting confrontation because it encourages Isabelle to improve rather than rely exclusively upon her inherited power. Their rivalry is therefore competitive rather than genuinely hostile, reflecting Gina's conviction that the development of her sisters is an important responsibility shared by the entire family.

### Possessions
- Dimensional Storage Belt: Gina possesses a personal dimensional-storage device in the form of a belt. The equipment provides access to a private pocket-dimensional space in which she can store possessions without conventional physical containers. She uses the belt to store [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s shirt and a photograph obtained during her negotiation with Angelina, demonstrating its practical function and the sentimental importance of the objects she keeps within it.[1]
- Victor's Shirt: Gina obtains one of Victor's shirts from Angelina after discovering that her sister had taken it from their father's private quarters. The item is exceptionally valuable to Gina because it retains Victor's scent, making it a treasured possession despite its otherwise ordinary function.[1]
- Photograph of Victor: Angelina provides Gina with a photograph of Victor sleeping as an additional incentive during their negotiation. The image originates from a collection maintained by one of their mothers, and Gina immediately accepts the proposed bargain once it is offered alongside the shirt.[1]
- Imperial Administrative Authorization (Temporary): Gina receives permission to observe Imperial operations classified at G-5 and below as part of her supervised training. The authorization provides limited access to administrative activities rather than establishing independent executive authority over a specific territory or institution.[2]

### Development
Gina's supernatural development is unusual because her defining Divine Concept influences her physical existence from birth. Rather than beginning life with the ordinary developmental limitations of a young True Dragon, she possesses a highly developed relationship with Life that accelerates her natural biological maturation. This allows her to reach a developmental stage comparable to an adolescent Dragon after approximately twenty years, despite older sisters having accumulated considerably more chronological experience. Her growth therefore demonstrates how a fundamental Divine inheritance can alter the developmental characteristics of an already exceptional supernatural Race.[1]
Her early development is principally biological and conceptual, but her later experiences emphasize the importance of practical judgment. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s War Games temporarily remove her ordinary Blood Dragon advantages, forcing her to experience an unfamiliar Race and supernatural environment. Her assignment as a Dryad within a tropical forest provides an opportunity to develop adaptability without depending exclusively upon overwhelming natural strength. Gina accepts the situation calmly and begins considering how to employ the characteristics available to her, demonstrating an approach suited to the practical objectives of Victor's training.[10]
The desire to develop beyond inherited power eventually extends into Imperial administration. Gina approaches Anna alongside Stella and asks to participate directly in the government, recognizing that she wishes to become useful to Victor rather than remain permanently dependent upon his protection. Her initial request is rejected because she lacks sufficient practical experience and emotional restraint, but Victor recognizes the sincerity of her motivation and permits supervised observation under temporary G-5 authorization. This transition represents an important developmental milestone because Gina begins acquiring responsibilities requiring judgment, patience and political awareness rather than raw supernatural power.[2]
Her martial development continues alongside her administrative education. Gina becomes increasingly committed to physical training and regularly challenges Isabelle, criticizing her reliance upon overwhelming natural Energy. She recognizes that extraordinary inherited strength does not automatically produce competence against experienced opponents and deliberately provokes Isabelle into additional training. Her actions demonstrate that she has internalized the philosophy underlying Victor's War Games and now actively encourages other members of the family to develop through similar experiences.[3]
Gina's intellectual development also becomes apparent through Victor's lessons concerning Reality and Divine Concepts. She recognizes the absence of genuine Life within artificial Eldritch organisms and participates in discussions concerning the Creator, [[Primordial Chaos]] and the structure of the Cosmos. Her inherited Dragon Eyes provide the potential for increasingly advanced perception, while her Life Concept offers an unusual foundation for understanding living existence. Although the complete limits of these abilities remain unidentified, her continued curiosity demonstrates that her development extends beyond physical strength and formal responsibility.[4][6]
Gina consequently represents one of the clearest examples of Victor's intention for his second generation. She begins with extraordinary advantages that would already make her exceptional among ordinary supernatural beings, but gradually learns that natural strength alone is insufficient for confronting complicated realities. Her continued training, developing emotional restraint, administrative education and willingness to challenge her sisters demonstrate a progression toward becoming an independent and capable member of the Imperial Family rather than merely an extraordinarily powerful daughter protected by her parents.
