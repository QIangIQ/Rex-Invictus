Agatha Agnessa Elderblood, commonly known as Agatha, is a recurring character in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]], an Imperial Princess of the [[Elderblood Empire]], and the biological daughter of Victor Elderblood and [[Agnes Elderblood Snow]]. As a naturally born Blood Dragon Goddess, she belongs to Victor's second generation of [[True Dragons]] and possesses the extraordinary Draconic and Divine characteristics of her father's lineage. Through Agnes, she also belongs to the historical [[Snow Clan]], one of [[Nightingale]]'s original Noble Vampire Count families. Her unusual combination of discipline, focus and practical intelligence distinguishes her from several of her more impulsive sisters.[1]
Agatha is especially notable for her remarkably serious temperament. Although many of Victor's daughters possess energetic, mischievous or openly emotional personalities, Agatha generally approaches situations with restraint and concentration. Her demeanor is sufficiently similar to [[Hilda Snow Elderblood]] that Agnes has jokingly questioned whether her daughter resembles Hilda more closely than herself. The comparison reflects their shared seriousness and disciplined attitudes rather than an actual difference in Agatha's parentage. Nevertheless, Agatha possesses a more relaxed and affectionate side, which she generally reserves for Victor and other members of her family.[1]
Her practical judgment is demonstrated during the military exercises organized by Victor to educate his daughters. While [[Priscillia Penny Elderblood]] and [[Nikolina Nia Nefela Elderblood]] concentrate on organizing opposing armies, Agatha recognizes that their prolonged preparations are allowing valuable Time to pass. She understands that the commanders are failing to account for the consequences of delay, an especially important lesson for naturally born Dragons whose perception of Time differs considerably from that of ordinary soldiers. Her observation prompts Valentina to explain the importance of reconnaissance and rapid decision-making, demonstrating how Agatha's attention to practical details contributes to the collective understanding of her sisters.[1]
Agatha's earliest apparent depiction occurs considerably earlier in the novel's publication order, during a distant-future sequence connected to [[Violet Elderblood Snow]]'s supernatural vision. An unnamed, snow-white-haired Dragon woman appears in futuristic armor and arrives upon an enormous white feathered Dragon during a large-scale interstellar battle. Her crimson-violet eyes, serious demeanor and concern for the safety of the Dragon Empress closely correspond to Agatha's subsequently established characteristics. However, the future warrior is not explicitly identified by name within that scene, and the vision should not be treated as confirmation that every event depicted will necessarily occur in the main chronology.[2]
As one of Victor's biological daughters, Agatha possesses extraordinary natural physical abilities and extensive supernatural protections. Her True Dragon physiology provides exceptional resistance to hostile environments, while the passive Evolution inherited from Victor allows her body to gradually adapt to conditions that would otherwise remain uncomfortable. Victor also established multiple protections against Soul invasion, mental interference and biological contamination, retaining portions of his daughters' Souls within himself to provide an additional safeguard against death. These protections are shared by the second generation and should be distinguished from unique powers that Agatha has personally developed or demonstrated.[3]
Despite her exceptional natural potential, Agatha has not yet received an extensive individual combat demonstration establishing the complete nature of her personal Divinity, specialized elemental powers or maximum fighting strength. Her principal confirmed individual accomplishments concern perception, strategic reasoning and the ability to recognize practical problems that other characters initially overlook. Her future depiction suggests a possible development toward participation in advanced Imperial military operations, but her current formal military rank and the complete nature of any personal combat specialization remain unidentified.

### Name
Agatha possesses two given names because her parents disagreed over what to call her. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] wanted their daughter to be named Agatha, while Agnes preferred Agnessa, a name possessing historical significance within the Snow family because it had been used by one of their ancestors. Rather than continuing the disagreement, they chose to combine the two names, producing Agatha Agnessa Elderblood. Although the compromise satisfied both parents, Agatha herself strongly prefers the name selected by Victor and appreciates that he prevented her from being known exclusively as Agnessa.[1]
Her dislike of Agnessa is connected to her desire for an identity distinct from her mother's. She considers the name little more than Agnes with several additional letters and does not believe that its historical significance is sufficient reason to make it her preferred name. Agatha values individuality despite belonging to an ancient and prestigious supernatural family, and she does not wish to be defined solely through inherited traditions or comparisons with her mother. Her preference for Victor's choice consequently reflects both affection for her father and a personal desire to possess a name that feels uniquely her own.[1]
The existence of two given names does not indicate that Agatha and Agnessa are different individuals. Both names refer to the same daughter of Victor and Agnes, with Agatha being the form she personally prefers.

### Physical Appearance
Agatha is an exceptionally beautiful young woman with long snow-white hair, pale skin and striking crimson-violet Draconic eyes. Her appearance strongly reflects the Snow lineage inherited through Agnes, particularly the pale complexion and white hair associated with several members of the family. Although she possesses the extraordinary physical characteristics of a naturally born Blood Dragon Goddess, her Humanoid appearance remains elegant and distinctly recognizable through the combination of her Snow-family features and the unusual coloration of her eyes.[2][1]
Agatha bears an exceptionally strong resemblance to [[Valentina Victoria Elderblood]], the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Violet. Their appearance is described as nearly identical, with their contrasting personalities serving as one of the most reliable means of distinguishing them. Agatha is considerably more serious and focused, whereas Valentina possesses a different temperament despite their common ancestry and similar physical characteristics. The resemblance extends to the daughters born from [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]] and [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]], with the four girls appearing similar enough to be compared with quadruplets.[1]
This resemblance is not evidence that the four daughters were literally born as quadruplets. They possess different biological mothers, and their similarity reflects the overlapping physical characteristics of their maternal lineages alongside Victor's exceptionally influential inheritance. Among the group, [[Mirabelle Elizabeth Elderblood]], Maya's daughter, is particularly distinguishable through her light-brown complexion, which contrasts with the much paler skin of her sisters. Their overall resemblance nevertheless remains significant enough that their different personalities and individual features become particularly important for identification.[1]
Agatha's earliest apparent appearance depicts a considerably more mature military presentation. The unnamed white-haired Dragon warrior seen in the distant future possesses exceptionally long snow-white hair, visible Dragon horns and crimson-violet eyes. She wears a full suit of armor combining medieval and futuristic design elements, reflecting the advanced technological and supernatural capabilities of Victor's civilization during that possible future. The armor visually distinguishes her from the more informal appearances of the second-generation daughters during their early development.[2]
The same future warrior arrives riding an enormous white Dragon with sapphire-blue eyes and feathered wings. Unlike conventional Dragons possessing prominent reptilian characteristics and leathery wings, the creature is described as having a softer appearance, with feathers contributing to its distinctive appearance. The woman stands upon the Dragon with her arms crossed, maintaining a focused and hostile expression while observing an enormous enemy vessel. The scene establishes the appearance of her apparent mount, but does not demonstrate that the creature is Agatha's own Dragon Form or confirm its individual identity.[2]
Agatha's mature appearance in the future sequence should also be distinguished from her confirmed present-day physical development. The vision occurs in an uncertain future whose events may not unfold exactly as depicted, and the novel has not established the precise relationship between her future armored appearance and her current natural Humanoid form. Her exact height, weight and the complete appearance of her personal True Dragon Form remain unidentified.

### Personality
Agatha is serious, disciplined, observant, focused and considerably more reserved than many of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s other daughters. Her normal disposition is characterized by careful attention to circumstances and a preference for practical judgment over unnecessary theatrics. Although she possesses the overwhelming natural abilities of a Blood Dragon Goddess, she does not appear inclined to treat every situation as an opportunity to demonstrate her power or compete for attention. Instead, she concentrates upon understanding what is occurring and identifying details that may influence the outcome of an event.[1]
Her resemblance to [[Hilda Snow Elderblood]] is one of the most distinctive aspects of her characterization. Both possess a serious and controlled temperament that contrasts with the emotional intensity of several other members of the Snow family. Agnes has even joked that Agatha behaves more like Hilda's daughter than her own, an observation that reflects their considerable similarity in personality. The resemblance is striking enough that Hilda occasionally casts furtive glances toward Victor, creating an amusing situation within the family despite the fact that Agatha's biological parentage is unambiguous.[1]
Agatha's discipline does not mean she lacks affection or humor. She possesses a more relaxed side that becomes apparent when she is surrounded by Victor and her family. Her seriousness is therefore better understood as her habitual manner of approaching unfamiliar circumstances rather than emotional coldness or an inability to form close relationships. Within the protected environment of the Elderblood household, she can behave more comfortably and does not maintain the same rigid demeanor she displays when concentrating upon training or practical responsibilities.[1]
Her relationship with her own identity also reveals a strong individualistic streak. Although she belongs to one of [[Nightingale]]'s oldest supernatural families, she does not automatically appreciate every tradition associated with her ancestry. Her dislike of Agnessa originates from the belief that the name is too similar to Agnes and insufficiently distinct from her mother's identity. Rather than accepting it merely because it belonged to an ancestor, she prefers the name selected by Victor, which she considers more individual. Her reaction demonstrates that she values personal identity even while respecting the importance of her family and its history.[1]
Agatha's observational skills become particularly important during Victor's military exercises. Priscillia and Nikolina initially focus upon organizing their opposing armies, instructing soldiers to gather supplies and prepare for the expected conflict. Although these actions are necessary, Agatha recognizes that the commanders are overlooking the passage of Time. Their continued preparations gradually cause their soldiers to lose focus, while the absence of immediate reconnaissance creates opportunities for an enemy to exploit the developing situation. Agatha understands the danger before the commanders themselves fully recognize the consequences, demonstrating that she can identify problems extending beyond the immediate tasks being performed.[1]
Her realization is particularly important because naturally born Dragons experience Time differently from ordinary supernatural beings. A delay that feels insignificant to an individual accustomed to extraordinarily long lifespans may represent a dangerous period for soldiers operating under the pressures of an imminent battle. Agatha recognizes that Victor's warning to remain attentive to Time was not simply a theoretical instruction, but a practical principle capable of determining whether an army retains its readiness or becomes vulnerable. Her understanding prompts Valentina to expand upon the problem, explaining that the commanders should have been conducting reconnaissance while organizing their resources rather than allowing the enemy to act without interference.[1]
Agatha also possesses an awareness of her father's behavior that reflects her familiarity with Victor. When he becomes visibly enthusiastic about expanding the War Games, she and her sisters immediately recognize that he has developed another supposedly excellent idea. Rather than sharing his excitement without reservation, the girls become apprehensive because experience has taught them that Victor's ideas frequently result in considerably more complicated challenges for his children. Their suspicions prove justified when he expands the military simulation into a large-scale Dream World and assigns most of the remaining daughters unfamiliar roles and circumstances.[4]
The unnamed Dragon warrior associated with Agatha in the distant-future sequence displays a similarly disciplined temperament. She assesses the appearance of a dangerous enemy vessel, recognizes that the surrounding region is becoming a battlefield and insists that Violet withdraw rather than remain exposed to the escalating threat. Her concern is directed toward the strategic safety of the Empress rather than personal excitement over the impending battle. The behavior is consistent with Agatha's confirmed personality, although the woman's identity remains contextual rather than explicitly stated within the future sequence itself.[2]
Agatha's personality consequently combines natural discipline with affection for her family and a strong desire to understand practical consequences. She is capable of recognizing problems that other individuals overlook, takes Victor's lessons seriously and possesses an independent attitude toward her identity despite her prestigious ancestry. Her current characterization suggests an aptitude for military reasoning and organized responsibilities, but she has not yet been confirmed as possessing a particular present-day command position or a completed career within the Imperial military.

### Heritage
Agatha is the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Agnes Elderblood Snow]], inheriting the extraordinary Draconic characteristics of her father alongside the ancestry of the historical Snow family. Agnes originally belonged to one of the five Noble Vampire Count Clans of [[Nightingale]], whose supernatural influence extended across the political and economic organization of Vampire society. Through her mother, Agatha possesses a direct connection to this ancient lineage, while through Victor she belongs to the second generation of naturally born [[Blood Dragons]].[1]
Her birth occurred after Victor and his family had undergone extraordinary supernatural development, making her a genuine True Dragon Goddess rather than a conventional Noble Vampire. This distinction is important because the Blood Dragon Race possesses a fundamentally different biological and spiritual foundation from the original Vampire Clans. Although Agatha retains Snow-family ancestry and several visible characteristics associated with that lineage, she belongs primarily to Victor's evolved Draconic dynasty and possesses the extraordinary physical potential shared by his biological daughters.
Agatha's maternal ancestry also makes [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] her biological half-sister, since both are daughters of Agnes. Violet occupies an unusual additional position within the Elderblood family because she is also one of Victor's wives and the mother of [[Valentina Victoria Elderblood]]. Consequently, Agatha is related to Valentina through the extended Snow family as well as through their common father, Victor. Their complicated genealogical relationship reflects the unusual structure of the Elderblood household, whose members do not always fit conventional Human classifications of kinship.
The extraordinary resemblance between Agatha, Valentina, Leone and Mirabelle is partly connected to their overlapping inherited characteristics. Agatha and Valentina display especially similar physical appearances despite their different mothers, while the daughters of Leona and Maya possess enough comparable features that the group is described as nearly resembling quadruplets. These similarities do not mean that Agatha shares an identical supernatural development with her sisters, since each daughter also inherits the individual characteristics and potential associated with her biological mother.[1]
Agatha's maternal Snow heritage provides a connection to the family's traditional supernatural abilities, including its historical affinity with Fire and unusual resistance to the weaknesses ordinarily associated with Vampires. However, her individual possession of every specialized Snow technique has not been established. Her natural Blood Dragon physiology already provides advanced environmental resistance, and her connection to Agnes does not independently demonstrate mastery over her mother's complete personal Divinity or other powers that have not been individually attributed to Agatha.
Her inherited name also reflects the importance of this ancestry. Agnes selected Agnessa because it belonged to a distant ancestor of the Snow family, seeking to preserve an element of their history through her daughter. Agatha nevertheless prefers the name chosen by Victor, demonstrating that she values the identity created through both sides of her family while wishing to remain distinct from earlier generations.[1]

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
During the [[Supernatural Gathering]], [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] became increasingly conscious of the enormous difference between [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s rapidly developing abilities and those of the other supernatural powers surrounding them. Although she wished to become an Empress worthy of standing beside him, she understood that his growth had repeatedly surpassed conventional expectations. Her desire to understand the future eventually led her to disregard Victor's warnings against using her developing powers carelessly, allowing her to observe a distant period in which their civilization had undergone extraordinary changes.[2]
Violet's vision revealed a future version of herself who had become a far more mature and powerful Dragon Empress. The future Violet possessed a greater degree of supernatural development and appeared within an environment containing immense futuristic cities and advanced Draconic military forces. Her surroundings reflected a civilization that had expanded far beyond the comparatively limited supernatural territories controlled during the earlier stages of Victor's rise to power. The vision also demonstrated that future Violet possessed enough perception to recognize her younger self's observation, allowing her to interrupt the connection and prevent further uncontrolled interference with the future.[2]
The distant-future sequence continued after the younger Violet's direct observation ended. The mature Dragon Empress remained within a dangerous region while military forces were engaged in a large-scale interstellar conflict. Future [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] arrived and reprimanded Violet for remaining personally exposed to the battlefield, expressing concern over her willingness to take unnecessary risks despite her position as Empress. Their conversation was interrupted when an enormous vessel composed of bone, flesh and dark materials emerged through a portal, releasing grotesque creatures capable of contaminating the surrounding environment.[2]
The appearance of the enemy vessel caused the conflict to escalate rapidly. Additional portals opened nearby, allowing hundreds of Wyverns of various colors to emerge as reinforcements. A considerably larger portal subsequently appeared, and an enormous white Dragon with sapphire-blue eyes and feathered wings emerged from it. The creature possessed a softer appearance than conventional reptilian Dragons, making it visually distinctive despite its enormous size. Standing upon its back was an unnamed woman with extremely long snow-white hair, visible Dragon horns and crimson-violet eyes, dressed in a full suit of armor combining futuristic technology with medieval military aesthetics.[2]
The armored woman maintained a focused and hostile expression while observing the enemy vessel. Rather than immediately launching an attack, she assessed the developing tactical situation and warned that the surrounding region was about to become a major battlefield. She insisted that Violet should not remain there because the Empress's continued presence would expose her to unnecessary danger. Her warning prompted Violet to accept the assessment, while Sasha contacted Natalia and requested the opening of a portal through which they could withdraw from the increasingly dangerous location.[2]
The woman's appearance, Snow-family physical characteristics and disciplined military demeanor closely match Agatha's subsequently established identity, making the sequence her earliest apparent depiction. Nevertheless, the woman is not explicitly named within the distant-future scene, and the identification therefore rests upon contextual continuity rather than direct confirmation from the dialogue. The future itself is also presented as something that can potentially change, meaning that the armored appearance and military circumstances should not automatically be treated as events that have already occurred in Agatha's present chronology.[2]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Agatha was born during the extraordinary period of development experienced by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his family following the establishment of his Blood Dragon lineage. As the daughter of Victor and Agnes, she inherited the exceptional biological foundation of a naturally born True Dragon Goddess while retaining the maternal characteristics associated with the Snow family. Like the other members of Victor's second generation, she grew up surrounded by powerful parents and relatives whose abilities far exceeded those of most supernatural civilizations. Her upbringing provided extraordinary advantages, but Victor remained concerned that such privileges might prevent his daughters from developing the adaptability, judgment and practical experience necessary to confront unfamiliar dangers.
Victor consequently organized a series of military exercises intended to teach his daughters how to operate when their overwhelming natural abilities were insufficient to guarantee success. He selected a snowy region of his Personal Planet as the setting, where the surrounding temperature approached -60°C. The daughters were unaffected by the environment because of their natural Draconic resistance, while the passive Evolution inherited from Victor provided additional adaptation to conditions that might otherwise cause discomfort. The exercise demonstrated the fundamental differences between their extraordinary natural physiology and the limitations they would eventually be required to experience during the simulated conflicts.[3]
The daughters also possessed extensive supernatural protections established by Victor. His safeguards prevented ordinary Soul and mental intrusion, protected their bodies from parasites and other harmful invasive substances, and incorporated additional defenses against attempts to disguise biological threats as beneficial materials. He retained portions of his daughters' Souls within himself so that they could be restored through him if their bodies were destroyed, while fragments of his own existence within them provided a final defensive measure against attackers capable of bypassing their initial protections. These safeguards allowed Victor to conduct demanding training without treating the possibility of genuine harm to his children lightly.[3]
The military exercises began when Victor placed [[Priscillia Penny Elderblood]] and [[Nikolina Nia Nefela Elderblood]] in command of opposing forces. The soldiers initially existed as constructs produced through his Ice-related powers, but Victor altered Reality to grant them autonomy and the ability to behave like living soldiers. Although they appeared to possess ordinary biological characteristics and could respond independently, they remained artificial constructs rather than genuine living beings with complete Souls. Their realistic behavior nevertheless allowed the commanders to experience the practical complications of warfare without requiring actual soldiers to suffer injury or death.[1]
Priscillia and Nikolina immediately began organizing their armies, gathering supplies and issuing the commands necessary to prepare for the coming conflict. Their initial responses demonstrated that they had retained the lessons provided by their mothers and understood several principles of military organization. However, neither commander adequately considered the consequences of allowing too much Time to pass before taking action. Their soldiers gradually became less attentive, and the absence of immediate reconnaissance created opportunities for other forces to act without interference.[1]
Agatha recognized the problem while observing the exercise alongside her sisters. She realized that Victor's repeated warnings concerning Time had a practical significance that was easy for naturally born Dragons to underestimate. Unlike ordinary soldiers, her family experienced Time from the perspective of beings capable of living for extraordinary periods, making comparatively short delays seem insignificant. However, an army preparing for imminent conflict could not afford to ignore those delays, because every moment spent waiting created opportunities for its enemies and increased the possibility that its own soldiers would become distracted or unprepared.[1]
Her observation drew the attention of Valentina, who explained that their unusual perception of Time could become dangerous in situations requiring immediate action. She recognized that the commanders should have been organizing supplies while simultaneously sending reconnaissance units to observe the opposing army, allowing them to gather intelligence and react before the enemy gained an advantage. Agatha's realization therefore became part of a broader discussion concerning the importance of simultaneous preparation, information gathering and decisive action during warfare.[1]
The exercise soon demonstrated the consequences of the commanders' delay. [[Siren Elderblood]] recognized that their preparations had left both armies vulnerable and entered the battlefield as the leader of a third faction. Unlike the two original commanders, she wasted little time organizing a conventional military structure before acting. She assembled soldiers capable of fighting, ordered the remaining personnel to handle supplies and immediately began attacking the vulnerable positions of the opposing forces. Although her faction possessed fewer resources, its rapid intervention allowed her to exploit the weaknesses created by the other commanders' hesitation.[1]
Siren concentrated her attacks upon strategically important locations and supply routes, gaining resources while disrupting both opposing armies. Priscillia and Nikolina reacted too slowly, allowing the third faction to withdraw before they could respond effectively. Victor subsequently explained that conflicts frequently attract additional participants seeking to benefit from the confrontation, meaning that commanders must consider more than the actions of their immediate opponents. Agatha's earlier recognition of wasted Time consequently proved relevant to the wider lesson, since the original commanders' excessive concentration upon preparation had prevented them from responding effectively to an unexpected threat.[1]
The exercise also revealed that rapid action alone was insufficient for successful leadership. Although Siren achieved early victories, her soldiers later abandoned important responsibilities because they feared the retaliation of the opposing armies. Victor explained that leadership required understanding the motivations and circumstances of subordinates rather than assuming that orders would always be obeyed simply because the commander possessed greater strength. The developing simulation consequently exposed the daughters to the limitations of ordinary soldiers, the importance of morale and the need to balance decisiveness with practical knowledge of the forces under their command.[4]
After observing the initial results, Victor expanded the War Games into a considerably more elaborate exercise. He transported the upgraded Tower of Nightmare into the temporary dimension, using its supernatural functions to create an extensive Dream World in which the daughters could experience unfamiliar societies, powers and political circumstances. The simulation restricted their ordinary abilities and altered the characteristics of their temporary identities, forcing them to operate without relying exclusively upon the enormous advantages of their Blood Dragon physiology. Victor intended the exercise to develop the adaptability necessary for conflicts against enemies possessing comparable or superior supernatural power.[4]
The first group of daughters assigned new roles included Rosemary, Gina, Yuno and Stella. Rosemary became a Fairy Queen, Gina appeared as a Dryad, Yuno assumed the role of a Necromancer and Stella functioned as a Demi-Goddess within a Human civilization experiencing political conflict. Each daughter received a different environment and supernatural foundation, forcing her to consider the limitations and opportunities associated with the assigned role rather than immediately applying her normal abilities.[4]
Agatha initially remained among the daughters watching Victor expand the simulation. When he began displaying the familiar expression associated with developing another excellent idea, she and her sisters immediately became apprehensive. They had learned that Victor's enthusiasm frequently preceded complicated situations in which his children were expected to overcome increasingly difficult challenges, making them suspicious of his apparently innocent smile. Their concern was justified when Victor announced that he intended to make the exercise even more interesting.[4]
With a snap of his fingers, Victor transported nearly all the remaining daughters into the Dream World, leaving Yog and the recently born Akasha outside the simulation initially. The girls were assigned unfamiliar characteristics and circumstances, while Victor continued observing their progress through numerous supernatural displays. The exercise eventually expanded from a comparatively straightforward military competition into a large-scale world-building simulation involving independent factions, political conflicts and numerous supernatural systems.[4]
Agatha was among the daughters included in this expanded training, but her individual Race, faction and subsequent actions within the Dream World have not been described in sufficient detail to establish a specific personal role. Her participation nevertheless reflected Victor's broader approach to educating his children, requiring them to understand uncertainty, military organization, limited information and the difficulties faced by beings who could not simply overwhelm every obstacle through natural strength. Her earlier attentiveness to the passage of Time demonstrated that she was already capable of recognizing important practical lessons within the training environment.[1][4]
Agatha's developing character therefore reflects a combination of extraordinary inherited power and practical discipline. Although she possesses the natural foundation of a Dragon Goddess, her most significant confirmed individual contribution involves recognizing the strategic consequences of delay during warfare. Her willingness to observe carefully and apply Victor's teachings distinguishes her from sisters whose immediate reactions are more impulsive, while her affectionate relationships with the family ensure that her serious temperament does not prevent her from participating in the shared experiences of the second generation.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Agatha's biological father and one of the individuals around whom she reveals her more relaxed and affectionate personality. Although she ordinarily maintains a serious and focused demeanor, her behavior becomes less rigid when she is surrounded by Victor and other members of the family. Her affection is also reflected in her strong preference for the name Victor selected for her, since she appreciates that his choice preserved a distinct personal identity despite Agnes's desire to use an ancestral Snow-family name.[1]
Victor's relationship with Agatha also possesses an important educational dimension. Like his other biological daughters, she received extensive instruction intended to prepare her for situations in which overwhelming supernatural strength would not guarantee success. His War Games provide a particularly significant example, since Agatha recognizes the practical importance of his warnings concerning Time and military readiness while observing the commanders' difficulties. She understands that their unusual Draconic perception can create dangerous blind spots when interacting with soldiers whose priorities and reactions are governed by considerably shorter timescales.[1]
Victor also provides Agatha with the extraordinary protections shared by his daughters. His safeguards defend against Soul intrusion, mental interference, biological contamination and other threats that might bypass conventional physical durability, while portions of their Souls remain anchored within him as a measure against death. The presence of his protective Soul fragments within their own existences also allows him to respond if an opponent manages to circumvent their initial defenses. These arrangements reflect the exceptional importance Victor places upon his daughters' safety and his unwillingness to depend exclusively upon their natural power when confronting potentially dangerous circumstances.[3]
Agatha is familiar enough with Victor's personality to recognize when he is preparing another complicated activity for his children. During the expansion of the War Games, she becomes apprehensive alongside her sisters when Victor begins smiling with the expression associated with an excellent idea. Their reaction reflects affectionate familiarity rather than genuine hostility, since Victor's training frequently combines demanding practical lessons with the opportunity for his daughters to spend time together in unusual circumstances.[4]

#### Agnes Elderblood Snow
[[Agnes Elderblood Snow]] is Agatha's biological mother and the source of her historical Snow-family ancestry. Agnes initially wanted her daughter to be named Agnessa, a name used by one of their ancestors, but [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] preferred Agatha. Their eventual compromise gave the child both names, although Agatha later developed a strong preference for Victor's choice. She considers Agnessa too similar to Agnes and wishes to preserve an individual identity rather than appear to be merely another version of her mother.[1]
Their personalities also differ considerably. Agnes is openly emotional, expressive and capable of impulsive behavior, whereas Agatha generally approaches situations with restraint and careful concentration. The contrast is sufficiently pronounced that Agnes has jokingly described her daughter as resembling Hilda more closely than herself. The remark is affectionate rather than antagonistic and does not establish any actual uncertainty concerning Agatha's biological parentage. Despite their differences, both remain members of the tightly connected Elderblood family, whose relationships are shaped by shared supernatural development and devotion toward Victor.[1]
Agatha's appearance also reflects her maternal lineage. Her snow-white hair and pale complexion connect her visually with several members of the Snow family, while her extraordinary Draconic eyes and Divine characteristics originate through the more advanced supernatural foundation of Victor's naturally born descendants. Although she retains this family heritage, her personal combat specialization cannot automatically be identified with every power possessed by Agnes.

#### Hilda Snow Elderblood
[[Hilda Snow Elderblood]] is one of the individuals to whom Agatha's personality is most directly compared. Although Hilda is not her biological mother, both possess a serious, disciplined and focused disposition that distinguishes them from the more openly impulsive members of the Elderblood household. Agnes has jokingly suggested that Agatha behaves more like Hilda's daughter than her own, while the remarkable similarity apparently causes Hilda to direct furtive glances toward [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] on occasion.[1]
Their resemblance concerns temperament rather than confirmed shared supernatural abilities or an alternative biological relationship. Both tend to approach practical situations seriously, placing importance upon observation and discipline rather than unnecessary theatrics. Agatha's behavior during the War Games reflects these characteristics, since she quickly recognizes the consequences of the commanders' excessive preparations and understands the importance of Time management. The novel has not yet established an extended private relationship between Agatha and Hilda beyond the unusual similarity in their personalities.[1]

#### Valentina Victoria Elderblood
[[Valentina Victoria Elderblood]] is one of Agatha's sisters and the individual whose physical appearance is most directly compared with hers. The two resemble one another so closely that their different personalities become a particularly important means of identification. Their connection is further complicated by the Elderblood family's genealogy, since Valentina is [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughter with Violet, who is herself Agnes's daughter and Agatha's maternal half-sister.[1]
Agatha and Valentina demonstrate their complementary personalities during Victor's military exercises. Agatha recognizes that the commanders are failing to account for the passage of Time, while Valentina expands upon the observation by explaining how the perception of Time experienced by Dragons differs from that of ordinary soldiers. She identifies the absence of simultaneous reconnaissance as another important weakness, noting that the commanders should have been gathering intelligence while organizing supplies rather than allowing their opponents to act without interference. Their exchange reflects Agatha's attentiveness and Valentina's ability to apply that observation to broader strategic circumstances.[1]
The two also belong to the group of four daughters whose physical characteristics are compared with those of quadruplets. Despite their extraordinary resemblance, they were born to different mothers and possess individual supernatural inheritances. Their similarity is therefore primarily a matter of appearance rather than evidence that they share identical powers, personalities or developmental paths.

#### Violet Elderblood Snow
[[Violet Elderblood Snow]] occupies an unusual position within Agatha's family because she is both her maternal half-sister through Agnes and one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wives. Their shared maternal ancestry connects them through the historical Snow family, while the broader Elderblood household places them within different familial roles because Agatha belongs to Victor's generation of biological daughters.
Agatha's earliest apparent depiction is associated with Violet's distant-future vision. An unnamed white-haired Dragon warrior resembling Agatha arrives upon an enormous white feathered Dragon during a dangerous interstellar confrontation and insists that the future Dragon Empress withdraw from the developing battlefield. The woman's concern reflects a disciplined military attitude consistent with Agatha's later characterization, although the warrior is not explicitly named within the scene itself. The apparent relationship therefore supports a possible future role involving the protection of Violet, but does not establish a confirmed present-day military office or guarantee that the events of the vision will occur exactly as depicted.[2]

#### Leone Elena Luna Elderblood and Mirabelle Elizabeth Elderblood
Leone Luna Elena Elderblood and [[Mirabelle Elizabeth Elderblood]] are among the daughters whose appearances are compared with those of Agatha and Valentina. Through their respective mothers, Leona and Maya, the sisters possess several overlapping physical characteristics associated with the Snow and Lykos lineages. Their facial features and general appearance are sufficiently similar that the four are described as nearly resembling quadruplets, although they possess different mothers and distinct personalities.[1]
Mirabelle is especially recognizable through her light-brown skin, inherited from Maya, which contrasts with the paler complexions of the other girls. Their resemblance nevertheless demonstrates the extraordinary influence of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s inheritance upon the appearances of his biological descendants. No extended individual relationship between Agatha and either Leone or Mirabelle has been described beyond their shared familial connection and physical comparison.

### Future Appearance
Agatha's earliest apparent depiction is unusual because it occurs during a distant-future sequence published long before her named introduction within the main chronology. Violet's developing supernatural vision allows her to observe an older version of herself who has become a far more powerful [[Violet Elderblood Snow|Dragon Empress]] within an advanced interstellar civilization. The future contains enormous cities, large-scale military forces and technology integrated with Draconic supernatural abilities, reflecting a stage of development vastly beyond the Empire's earlier history. However, the vision also demonstrates that the future possesses uncertainty and that individuals capable of influencing Reality may interfere with its development.[2]
The unnamed woman associated with Agatha appears after future Sasha confronts Violet over her decision to remain on a dangerous battlefield. An enormous enemy vessel emerges through a portal, releasing grotesque creatures and causing the conflict to escalate. Additional portals subsequently open, allowing hundreds of Wyverns to arrive before a much larger white feathered Dragon emerges. Standing upon the creature is a snow-white-haired Dragon warrior with crimson-violet eyes, visible horns and futuristic armor. She assesses the enemy vessel and insists that the Empress leave because the region is becoming an active battlefield, prompting Violet and Sasha to prepare their withdrawal.[2]
The warrior's physical characteristics and serious military demeanor strongly correspond to Agatha's established appearance and personality. Nevertheless, the scene does not explicitly identify her by name, making the association a contextual identification rather than an independently confirmed statement. The future also remains a possible development rather than a fixed account of events that have already occurred. Consequently, the armor, mount and potential military responsibilities shown in the vision should be distinguished from Agatha's confirmed present-day abilities and possessions.[2]

### Trivia
- Agatha is the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Agnes Elderblood Snow]], making her a naturally born Blood Dragon Goddess belonging to the second generation of Victor's Draconic lineage. Through Agnes, she also possesses the historical ancestry of the [[Snow Clan]].[1]
- Her complete name resulted from a disagreement between her parents. Victor wanted Agatha, while Agnes preferred Agnessa, a name used by one of their distant ancestors. They eventually combined both names, although Agatha strongly prefers Victor's choice because she considers Agnessa too similar to her mother's name.[1]
- Agatha possesses a serious, disciplined and focused personality that strongly resembles [[Hilda Snow Elderblood]]. Agnes has jokingly described her as behaving more like Hilda's daughter than her own, while Hilda occasionally casts furtive glances toward Victor because of the resemblance.[1]
- Despite her normally reserved temperament, Agatha possesses a more relaxed and affectionate side that she reveals primarily around Victor and other members of her family.[1]
- Agatha strongly resembles [[Valentina Victoria Elderblood]] in physical appearance, while their different personalities provide a particularly important distinction. The daughters of Agnes, Violet, Leona and Maya are described as resembling one another enough to be compared with quadruplets, although they possess different biological mothers.[1]
- Among the four similar-looking sisters, [[Mirabelle Elizabeth Elderblood]] is particularly recognizable because of her light-brown complexion, inherited from Maya, which contrasts with the paler skin of the others.[1]
- Agatha's earliest apparent appearance occurs during Violet's distant-future vision, in which an unnamed white-haired Dragon warrior arrives upon an enormous white feathered Dragon during a large-scale interstellar conflict. The woman's crimson-violet eyes, armored appearance and serious demeanor correspond to Agatha's subsequently established characteristics, although the scene does not explicitly identify her by name.[2]
- The future warrior wears armor combining medieval and futuristic design elements, while her apparent mount possesses sapphire-blue eyes and feathered wings. The creature is distinct from conventional reptilian Dragons and should not automatically be identified as Agatha's personal Dragon Form.[2]
- During Victor's War Games, Agatha recognizes that Priscillia and Nikolina are allowing too much Time to pass while organizing their armies. Her observation leads to a discussion with Valentina concerning the dangers created by the different perception of Time experienced by Dragons and ordinary soldiers.[1]
- Agatha's tactical awareness is demonstrated through her recognition that excessive preparation can reduce military readiness and provide enemies with opportunities to attack. Valentina expands upon the lesson by explaining that reconnaissance should proceed alongside the organization of supplies.[1]
- Agatha is among the daughters who immediately become apprehensive when Victor begins smiling with the expression associated with developing another excellent idea. Their concern proves justified when he expands the War Games into a large-scale Dream World and assigns the remaining girls unfamiliar roles.[4]
- Her individual role within the expanded Dream simulation has not been established, although she participates in the broader training intended to improve strategy, adaptability and understanding of unfamiliar supernatural systems.[4]
- Like Victor's other biological daughters, Agatha possesses exceptional resistance to hostile environments and remains unaffected by the extremely low temperatures of the snowy training region, which approach -60°C.[3]
- She inherited a reduced form of Victor's passive Evolution acquired after he consumed the Elder Gods. The ability allows her body to adapt to environmental discomfort more quickly than ordinary Dragons, although it remains considerably less extreme than Victor's own adaptation.[3]
- Agatha possesses the Soul, mental and biological protections Victor established for his daughters, including safeguards against invasive parasites, harmful substances and attempts to bypass the body's ordinary recognition mechanisms.[3]

- Agatha's future appearance suggests a possible connection with advanced Imperial military operations, but she has not been confirmed as the present-day commander of a specific Legion or holder of an identified military rank.[2]
- The full appearance of Agatha's natural True Dragon Form, the name of her individual Divinity and the complete extent of her personal combat abilities remain unidentified.
