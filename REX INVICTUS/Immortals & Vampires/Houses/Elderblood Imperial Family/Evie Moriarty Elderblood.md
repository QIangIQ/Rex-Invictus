Evie Moriarty is the Witch Queen, the ruler of the Witches, the biological daughter and disciple of Albedo Moriarty Elderblood, the mother of [[Emily Moriarty]], and a Goddess whose Divinity embodies <Research>. By the time she became directly involved in the story, Evie had already spent almost two thousand years protecting and developing the Witch Race, transforming a people repeatedly persecuted by stronger supernatural factions into a wealthy, politically influential magical civilization whose knowledge, artifacts, information and services made the Witches valuable to virtually every major supernatural power.[8][5]
Unlike rulers who established their positions primarily through personal combat power, Evie's historical danger came from the combination of extraordinary Witchcraft, centuries of accumulated research, preparation, magical contracts, intelligence networks, economic influence, political manipulation and a willingness to exploit almost any useful opportunity. She was one of the most important independent rulers of the old supernatural order and defended that independence even while dealing with beings individually stronger than herself.[4][8][6]
Her position changed permanently after Emily Moriarty was afflicted by Divine Poison and Curses beyond Evie's ability to cure. Unable to save her daughter through Witchcraft, Evie offered Victor Elderblood herself, the Witches, her territory and everything she had accumulated throughout her long life in exchange for Emily's survival. Victor cured Emily, and Evie's civilization subsequently became part of the expanding [[Elderblood Empire]].[9] Victor later determined that Evie's Mortal Witch body and Soul had become inadequate for the increasingly dangerous knowledge she was expected to investigate. Rather than imposing an artificial Divinity upon her, he accelerated a Divine possibility already present within her Soul, awakening <Research> and transforming Evie into a Goddess of Research.[2]
Victor then entrusted Evie and the Witches with the expansion into the [[Magic]] World, the separate Lower Sector first reached through the dimensional experiments conducted from the original Arcane Realm. Over the following eras, the Witches conquered and transformed the Magic World into their principal planetary civilization. By Chapter 1179 the planet is referred to simply as Arcane and as the Planet of the Witches, with Evie recognized as its ruler.[10][1][2][3] This later use of Arcane refers to the Witch-controlled Magic World and should not be confused with the original Arcane Realm from which the dimensional expedition began.
During the merger of the Empire's subordinate Lower Sectors, the Magic World/Arcane was incorporated together with [[Nightingale]] and Samar into Victor's Mid-Level Sector. Evie participated directly by receiving one of Victor's power fragments and remaining on Arcane while the three smaller Realities were drawn into his Sector.[11] Afterward, Victor reorganized the imported galaxies and moved Arcane into the protected central region of the Empire alongside Nightingale and Samar.[12] As of Chapter 1182, Evie remains the Witch Queen and Goddess of Research; she has not been established as a Blood Dragon, Dragon Goddess or wife of Victor.

### Physical Appearance
Evie is an exceptionally tall woman with pale skin, a voluptuous and curvaceous build, and long black hair. Her appearance carries the elegance and authority associated with the Witch Queen while incorporating elaborate magical equipment befitting one of the greatest Witches of her era. During the [[Supernatural Gathering]], she wore a long black dress decorated with red accents. The lower portion of the outfit exposed dark-golden armor around her legs, with similar armor covering parts of her arms. The unusual shape of her armored hands was compared to something Draconic, but the feature belonged to her equipment and was not evidence of Dragon physiology.[5]
Evie also carried a long black magical staff and wore a crown formed from interwoven black branch-like structures surrounding a light-blue gemstone. Both objects emitted light-blue Energy mixed with green shades. Her most distinctive physical feature while using [[Magic]] is her eyes: complex magical circles, geometric structures and Rune-like patterns appear and rotate within them as she observes or analyzes supernatural phenomena. Golden magical patterns were visibly moving through her eyes when she examined the gathered supernatural rulers during her debut.[4][5]

#### Divine State
Evie's ascension in Chapter 1093 did not replace her Witch identity or transform her into a Blood Dragon. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] examined her Soul through his authority over the spiritual side of existence and accelerated the Divine future naturally compatible with Evie until the Concept of <Research> awakened.[2] Her Mana changed with the ascension, acquiring qualities comparable to [[Divine Energy]] while preserving the flexibility that makes Mana especially valuable to Witches. Victor consequently regarded the result as a form of Divine Mana. Her visual perception evolved at the same time: when fully activated, multiple magical circles manifest throughout her eyes and allow her to directly perceive Energy. Victor compared this perception to an inferior version of the sensory properties associated with Dragon Eyes, but the comparison concerned the function of her evolved sight rather than her Race.[2]

### Personality
Evie is ambitious, opportunistic, calculating, cautious, materialistic, intellectually curious and exceptionally pragmatic. Much of her apparent greed developed as a survival strategy rather than simple indulgence. The Witches had repeatedly been persecuted, exploited and driven toward extinction by stronger supernatural factions, and Evie's answer was to make her civilization too useful, wealthy, knowledgeable and difficult to attack casually. Under her rule the Witches became suppliers of magical services, artifacts, information and specialized knowledge while concentrating their most important resources inside the heavily defended Arcane Realm.[8] Evie therefore evaluates most political relationships transactionally: alliances are safest when both sides continue benefiting, contracts are preferable to informal promises, information is a form of power, and valuable resources should not be discarded when they can instead be acquired, studied or exchanged.
Her caution complements this opportunism. Even when meeting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] under ostensibly peaceful conditions, Evie covertly distributed numerous spells throughout their surroundings and prepared to trigger them through thought alone if the meeting became dangerous.[6] During negotiations involving Yama and [[Merlin]], she repeatedly examined the agreement being offered, searched for hidden magical interference and attempted to determine whether the people negotiating with her were concealing traps or information.[13][14] Evie is similarly willing to conceal components of her own spell construction so that another magician cannot easily identify and counter a technique before she activates it.[14]
Her greatest intellectual strength and one of her most serious flaws originate from the same characteristic: Evie has difficulty abandoning a mystery. Research that another ruler would consider too dangerous can become more attractive to her precisely because it has never been accomplished. This tendency contributed directly to the dimensional experiment that became the Arcane [[Black Hole|Singularity]]. Even as the connection to the unknown world became increasingly unstable, Evie's fascination with the scientific possibilities made her reluctant to abandon the project until the danger became undeniable.[15][10][16] She is nevertheless capable of changing direction when presented with sufficient evidence. During the first confrontation with the civilization beyond the portal, Emily's more immediate assessment of the threat helped push Evie away from treating every enemy primarily as a research specimen and toward eliminating dangers that could not safely be contained.[17]
Evie's reputation for greed does not extend above everything she values. Emily is the clearest exception. When no amount of [[Magic]], accumulated knowledge or political influence could cure her biological daughter, Evie abandoned the independence she had defended for nearly two millennia and offered Victor everything she possessed without hesitation.[9] Her later behavior toward Emily remains recognizably Evie—curious, intrusive and eager to investigate—but Chapter 1182 also shows that she can respect boundaries when they are stated directly. After discovering that Emily had developed romantic feelings for a young Werewolf from Tasha's family, Evie immediately demanded every detail and treated her daughter's love life as something worth celebrating; when Emily explicitly prohibited experiments and reserved the right not to answer sensitive questions, Evie accepted the restriction.[11][12]
Her relationship with Victor also illustrates that Evie remains capable of separating ambition, attraction and genuine love. Albedo repeatedly assumes that Evie's interest in the Emperor should naturally result in romance, but Evie explicitly rejects that conclusion in Chapter 1182. She acknowledges that she is interested in Victor—pointing out that he is the Emperor and that countless women would naturally be attracted to him—but argues that interest is not the same as being in love. According to Evie, she barely knows Victor personally and most of their direct interactions have been professional or political.[12] Even after Violet later forces Evie to reconsider how accustomed she has become to Victor's appeal, no romantic relationship or marriage is established.

### Synopsis

#### A New Pillar
Evie's influence enters [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s story before her physical debut. When the newly created Fifth Vampire Count becomes known to the wider supernatural world, the Witches immediately recognize the economic significance of his assigned role as an explorer: undiscovered territories could contain rare magical resources, materials and knowledge unavailable through existing markets. Evie therefore has [[Selena Moriarty]] investigate Victor and establish contact with his faction.[18] Reports from her agents gradually make him a much more serious object of interest. Victor routinely violates the behavior expected from a newly created Noble Vampire while accumulating the loyalty of powerful people rather than being destroyed by the established order, making Clan Alucard potentially valuable to a civilization whose economic power depends heavily upon access to unusual resources.[19][4]
Evie eventually attempts to investigate him through high-level Fate-based Witchcraft. The technique should allow the Witches to follow the Fate associated with a target and obtain information or a location depending upon the resources invested, yet Victor cannot be interpreted normally. Divination fails not only around Victor himself but increasingly around several people closely connected to him, revealing that something surrounding the young Vampire interferes with supernatural Fate observation far beyond what should have been possible for someone of his age.[4] Rather than reassuring Evie, the failure makes her more cautious and strengthens her interest in understanding what Victor actually is.

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Evie remains largely behind the scenes while the relationship between the Witches and Clan Alucard develops through intermediaries. Selena negotiates with [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]] and the members of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household while the Witches continue evaluating the economic possibilities created by Victor's exploration, rapidly expanding territory and growing collection of rare supernatural resources.[20][21][22][23] These exchanges establish the transactional foundation that later characterizes Evie's personal dealings with Victor: neither side trusts the other blindly, but both recognize that the other possesses resources, information and capabilities worth maintaining contact with.

#### Demonic War
Evie makes her physical debut during the [[Supernatural Gathering]], appearing before the rulers of Earth's major supernatural factions as the Witch Queen. Her clothing, enchanted equipment and rotating magical patterns make the scale of her Witchcraft immediately apparent, while a passive Seduction effect subtly influences the perception of people around her and causes observers without sufficient resistance to view her more favorably.[5] Her priorities during the Gathering are equally representative of her personality. When the factions discuss the disposition of Divine Artifacts recovered from enemy territory, Evie immediately objects to rules that would require the Witches to surrender valuable objects they successfully obtain.[24]
The situation changes when [[Diablo]] announces the Demonic War and Arcane itself is attacked. Evie's normally controlled demeanor deteriorates when she sees the Realm she spent centuries building burning and realizes that Demonic forces somehow penetrated defenses specifically designed to make Arcane one of the safest supernatural territories on Earth.[25][26] As the war expands, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually confronts Evie concerning the political arrangement linking the Witches and Diablo. Rather than trying to overpower one of Earth's most dangerous Witches and unnecessarily turning her entire Race into enemies, Victor approaches the confrontation as an information war. Evie secretly surrounds the meeting area with prepared [[Magic]], but Victor arrives with enough intelligence to repeatedly redirect the negotiation, exploit vulnerabilities in her position and prevent her from controlling the conversation in the manner she expected from a much younger ruler.[6][27][7]
By the end of their confrontation, Evie is forced to acknowledge that Victor has politically outmaneuvered her without needing a battle. The Witches do not become his subjects at this stage, but Evie can no longer regard Clan Alucard as an inexperienced faction she can manipulate from a distance.[7] Their negotiations also produce the deliberately unusual teacher-student dynamic through which Victor refers to Evie as his magical "Master". The relationship is never comparable to Victor's genuine apprenticeship under [[Scathach]]; it becomes partly political and partly a recurring joke between two people whose paths continue intersecting as Victor rapidly advances beyond the level at which conventional Witchcraft alone could define his development.[6][7]

#### King Among Kings
Evie's most consequential research project begins when she, Emily, Albedo and the Witches of the original Arcane Realm experiment with large-scale dimensional travel. Their work eventually opens a functioning connection to an entirely different inhabited world.[15][10][16] This destination is the world later known as the [[Magic]] World, a separate Lower Sector rather than another territory physically located within the Witches' original Arcane Realm. The experiment therefore represents a discovery on a much larger scale than the Witches initially understood.
The connection quickly proves to be two-way. Beings on the opposite side interact with the portal, turning a scientific breakthrough into a possible invasion route. Evie responds with high-level Witchcraft, combining Curses, Suggestions, restraints, environmental Magic and other prepared techniques against the foreign forces, but the encounter exposes how dangerous civilizations outside Earth's familiar supernatural hierarchy can be.[17] [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] crosses the portal and encounters Alfey, whose strength is supported by Blessings from several Gods belonging to his world's organized Pantheon. Although those Gods are not individually presented as overwhelmingly superior to every major deity Victor knows, their willingness to cooperate as a functional Divine military makes their civilization strategically dangerous.[17][28]
The real crisis emerges when the different temporal conditions of the two connected Realities begin affecting one another. The dimensional passage is no longer merely connecting two points in Space; incompatible Time conditions begin contaminating the connection itself. The phenomenon escalates beyond ordinary Witchcraft until [[The Universal Tree]] and [[The Judges of The Abyss]] intervene because continued instability could affect systems far larger than either laboratory.[29][1] The incident becomes both one of the greatest accomplishments of Witch research and one of Evie's greatest mistakes. It proves that other cosmic civilizations exist, demonstrates that even exceptional Witches can accidentally interfere with mechanisms far beyond their understanding, and permanently expands Evie's conception of what Magic and dimensional science can reach.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
The dimensional discovery makes the Arcane Realm strategically valuable to powers interested in reaching the foreign world. Evie attempts to preserve Witch autonomy while stronger factions begin surrounding the opportunity created by her research. Yama approaches seeking access, while [[Merlin]] operates behind the negotiations. Evie distrusts both and refuses to casually surrender control of the discovery even when faced with people capable of creating serious problems for the Witches.[13][14]
Her negotiations demonstrate the sophistication of her magical caution. Evie attempts to examine the people and [[Magic]] around her, recognizes when Merlin destroys one of her investigative attempts, hides portions of her own spell construction and repeatedly analyzes the contract offered to her before accepting that its clauses are legitimate.[13][14] The crisis simultaneously forces her to confront [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s earlier warning: sooner or later Arcane could encounter something powerful enough that preserving complete independence would become less important than finding an ally capable of protecting the Witches. Victor's rapidly growing faction increasingly appears to be one of the few powers capable of negotiating with Evie while having enough strength that it does not need to steal what it wants.

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Evie's political independence finally collapses through a threat she cannot solve with research. [[Emily Moriarty]] is afflicted by Divine Poison and Curses whose combined effects exceed Evie's accumulated Witchcraft. Every method she tries fails, leaving the Witch Queen unable to save the person she values most.[9] When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] confirms that he can cure Emily, Evie immediately offers him everything: herself, the civilization she built, her territory, her achievements and her future. Victor accepts her submission, heals Emily and brings the Witches beneath his authority. The decision ends the period in which Evie functions as a fully independent supernatural sovereign, although Victor continues using her as a ruler and specialist rather than stripping her of the position she spent centuries building.[9]
Evie's next major transformation occurs in Chapter 1093. Her research has begun bringing her into contact with information and Energies intended for beings vastly beyond Mortal Witches, and her body cannot safely sustain what her mind is trying to investigate. Victor considers that limitation wasteful and examines Evie's Soul through his authority over Negativity. He discovers that Evie already possesses the potential to become Divine and chooses to accelerate that natural possibility rather than constructing a foreign Concept for her.[2] The Concept that awakens is <Research>, elevating Evie into a Minor Goddess at the moment of ascension. Her Mana develops Divine properties, her body becomes capable of enduring information that previously threatened her, and her magical eyes evolve into a form capable of directly perceiving Energy.[2]
Victor subsequently assigns the newly ascended Goddess a task perfectly suited to her nature: investigate, develop and expand into the [[Magic]] World, the same separate Reality originally contacted through the Arcane dimensional experiment and a world deeply connected to the larger history of Magic and the Witches.[2] With the [[Elderblood Empire]] supporting her, Evie's earlier scientific discovery becomes an Imperial expansion project. The Witches cease merely observing the foreign world and eventually become its dominant civilization.

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Evie's assignment has transformed the [[Magic]] World beyond recognition. The chapter introduces the location as a Lower Sector: Planet of the Witches. Witch influence is omnipresent: magical devices line its cities, Witches fly through the air using magical circles, talking animals and enormous creatures move openly through society, magical blood has spread through the population and even Demigods exist among its inhabitants. The civilization appears exotic and chaotic at a glance but is administratively organized under Witch and Imperial control.[3] Evie remains the Witch Queen and is explicitly treated as the ruler of the entire planet. By this period the planet is referred to by Evie, Emily and other characters as Arcane, making clear that the late Imperial name refers to the Witch-controlled Magic World rather than creating an additional fourth Lower Sector.
[[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]] visits Evie shortly before the Empire's planned merger of its subordinate Sectors. Their reunion demonstrates how much Evie's circumstances have changed without erasing her personality. Albedo immediately questions why Evie has not become [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wife after so much time, arguing that remaining merely a Witch Queen is insignificant compared with becoming a Dragon Goddess. Evie rejects the suggestion that she should pursue him simply for status, although Albedo claims she can sense her daughter's envy.[3] The conversation is also one of the clearest pieces of evidence against older depictions of Evie as a Blood Dragon Goddess: Albedo explicitly contrasts her own Dragon Goddess condition with Evie's continuing status as Witch Queen.
During the visit, Emily arrives with a small white Dragon-like creature whom Evie identifies as the last Drakyo, a Race said to have created the world. When Albedo asks about the Paladin connected to the earlier struggle over the planet, Evie states that he is dead and confirms that she learned what she needed from him. She regards the conquest itself as so one-sided with Elderblood support that calling it a genuine "war" almost seems excessive.[3]
Albedo then gives Evie a dark-purple sphere required for the approaching Sector merger. Evie's instincts immediately drive her to analyze the object, but attempting to understand its nature causes a headache before she can properly perceive it. Albedo warns her to stop because forcing comprehension of the information involved could damage her mind. Evie already knows the larger operation includes Samar and [[Nightingale]] and accepts responsibility for keeping Victor's fragment within Arcane while the preparations are completed.[3]
The spheres are fragments of Victor's own power intended to saturate the three Lower Sectors. Once the process reaches the required threshold, the fragments activate simultaneously in Nightingale, Samar and Arcane. Victor begins pulling all three Realities toward his Mid-Level Sector while the Universal Tree stabilizes their passage and prevents unrelated smaller Sectors from being dragged into the movement.[11] Victor's Sector barrier expands as entire galaxies from the three Lower Sectors enter his sphere of influence, physically transforming Arcane from an external Lower-Sector domain into part of the enlarged cosmic Reality directly controlled by the [[Elderblood Empire]].[11]
While the merger proceeds, Albedo's attention returns to Evie's relationship with Victor. She decides that Evie should pursue the Emperor and attempts to involve Emily as well, only to discover that Emily already has romantic feelings for a young Werewolf belonging to Tasha's family. Evie is genuinely surprised because her daughter had never informed her. Albedo nevertheless drags both women toward the Elderblood household and states that entering Victor's harem requires more than attraction—it requires speaking with the Empress.[11]
Albedo brings the matter directly to [[Violet Elderblood Snow]]. Violet initially refuses because, from her perspective, the central issue is simple: Evie herself does not want a romantic relationship. Albedo argues that her senses clearly detect Evie's interest, prompting Evie to distinguish attraction from love. Evie admits that Victor interests her—he is the Emperor, and she considers attraction to him almost inevitable—but explains that she barely knows him personally and that their interactions have overwhelmingly been professional. She therefore rejects the idea that admiration or attraction should automatically be interpreted as romantic love.[12]
Violet eventually uses part of her private collection of photographs of Victor to demonstrate how strongly most women react to him. Evie is visibly affected and later admits that she understands the point Violet was trying to make, but the scene does not establish a marriage, confession of love or entry into Victor's harem.[12] After Albedo is summoned away for training and Violet returns to her duties, Evie describes the encounter as one of the strangest conversations of her life and blames her mother for creating the situation.
Evie and Emily are subsequently transported back to Arcane by a blonde servant whose effortless interplanetary movement causes them to suspect she was one of Victor's Empresses. Once home, Evie's focus immediately shifts toward her daughter's newly revealed romantic life. She insists that Emily tell her everything, celebrates the fact that her daughter is in love and displays enough scientific enthusiasm for Emily to immediately forbid experiments. When Emily also insists upon the right to refuse sensitive questions, Evie considers the demand and agrees.[12] The exchange shows that centuries of rulership, Divine ascension and Imperial expansion have not removed Evie's fundamental personality: she remains a researcher even when the subject is her own family, but she is capable of accepting limits when Emily directly establishes them.
At the same time, Victor completes the astronomical reorganization created by the merger. Nightingale and Samar must remain within their original Solar Systems, but Arcane's planet can be repositioned independently. Victor moves the newly incorporated territories into a protected central region of the Empire, placing them behind multiple Imperial checkpoints while reorganizing the younger galaxies around them.[12]

### Skills & Abilities
Evie was already one of the greatest living Witches before attaining Godhood. Her strength does not come from a single Element or combat technique; it comes from an enormous library of [[Magic]], the ability to prepare and combine spells for different circumstances, centuries of research and the analytical mentality that eventually became the foundation of her Divinity.
- Magic: As the Witch Queen and a Highest-Rank Witch, Evie possesses an exceptionally broad command of Witchcraft accumulated across almost two millennia of study. She can construct numerous spells simultaneously, alter their activation conditions and prepare magical effects in advance rather than relying entirely upon direct combat casting.[8][6]
- Mana Manipulation: Evie possesses extremely refined control over Mana and magical structures. Her equipment, magical circles and large-scale experiments demonstrate the ability to manipulate substantial quantities of Mana while maintaining complex spell formations.[5][15]
- Magic Circle Manifestation: Complicated magical circles and geometric structures appear inside Evie's eyes when she actively examines or manipulates Magic. Similar circles are used throughout Witch spellcasting and dimensional research.[4][5]
- Fate / Destiny Divination: Evie can employ advanced Witchcraft intended to investigate a target through Fate, potentially revealing information or location depending on the resources committed. Her attempt to use the method against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] fails because his relationship with Fate cannot be interpreted normally by the spell.[4]
- Seduction Magic: Evie can passively influence how others perceive her, encouraging susceptible observers to regard her more favorably. The effect can be resisted by sufficiently strong Wills.[5]
- Curse Manipulation: Evie's combat repertoire includes high-level Curses capable of being layered with other Witch techniques. She employs such Magic during the confrontation surrounding the foreign civilization reached through the Arcane portal.[17]
- Suggestion / Mental Influence: Evie possesses Magic capable of influencing the thoughts or decisions of targets, although sufficiently powerful or protected beings can resist such techniques.[17]
- Binding and Restraining Magic: Evie can create magical restraints and control effects intended to immobilize or contain hostile supernatural beings, frequently combining them with other prepared spells.[17]
- Environmental and Tempest Magic: Her magical repertoire includes techniques capable of manipulating the surrounding environment and producing large-scale elemental or tempest-like phenomena.[17]
- Memory Manipulation: Evie has demonstrated the willingness and ability to alter the memories of subordinates, including one of the Witches regarded as her daughters, when she considered the information involved dangerous or politically inconvenient.[30]
- Spell Preparation and Layering: Rather than waiting for an opponent to attack, Evie can discreetly establish numerous spells throughout an area and retain them for instantaneous activation. She prepared dozens of magical measures while privately negotiating with Victor despite outwardly maintaining a conversation.[6]
- Concealed Spell Construction: Evie can obscure portions of her magical preparation so that another practitioner cannot easily identify, intercept or destroy the complete technique before activation.[14]
- Contract Analysis: Her knowledge extends to magical contracts and supernatural agreements. Evie repeatedly examines contracts for hidden restrictions, magical interference and concealed clauses before allowing herself or the Witches to become bound by them.[13][14]
- Dimensional Magic and Portal Research: Evie was one of the principal researchers responsible for the Arcane project that opened a stable passage to another inhabited Lower Sector. The achievement required manipulating boundaries between separate Realities, although the later Arcane [[Black Hole|Singularity]] also demonstrated the danger of interacting with Space-Time systems whose underlying rules the researchers did not completely understand.[15][10][16][1]
- Magical Research and Analysis: Research is Evie's defining talent. She systematically studies unfamiliar powers, artifacts, species, magical systems and phenomena, and her instinctive response to an unknown object is often to analyze it immediately. This habit is powerful enough to become dangerous; in Chapter 1179 she attempts to analyze Victor's Sector-merger fragment despite being incapable of safely comprehending the information contained within it.[3]
- Divinity — <Research>: Victor awakened a Divine possibility that already existed within Evie's Soul, transforming her into a Goddess whose central Concept is Research. The Divinity reflects her lifelong drive to investigate, analyze, understand and develop knowledge rather than granting her an unrelated Divine attribute.[2]
- Divine Mana: Following her ascension, Evie's Mana acquired characteristics comparable to [[Divine Energy]] without losing the adaptability of conventional Mana. This allows her to retain the versatility of Witchcraft while operating through a higher-quality supernatural Energy.[2]
- Energy Perception: Evie's evolved eyes can directly perceive supernatural Energy. Multiple magical circles appear within them when the ability is active, and Victor compares the resulting perception to a weaker version of the perceptive function of Dragon Eyes.[2]
- Divine Physiology: [[Awakening]] as a Goddess raises Evie's body and Soul beyond their previous Mortal Witch limitations, allowing her to withstand forms of Energy and information that had begun physically and mentally overwhelming her before ascension.[2]
- Political and Economic Intelligence: Evie's greatest non-combat weapon is her ability to build systems. She transformed the Witches from a persecuted people into a civilization capable of surviving among Vampires, [[Werewolves]], Gods and Demons by making their Magic, information and services economically indispensable while maintaining extensive defenses and political leverage.[8]
- Planetary Administration: After receiving responsibility for the Magic World, Evie successfully oversaw the rise of a Witch-dominated planetary civilization supported by the [[Elderblood Empire]]. By Chapter 1179 the world contains widespread magical infrastructure, supernatural populations, Demigods and an organized society directly governed by her.[3]
Despite her Divine evolution, no currently available chapter establishes Evie as a Blood Dragon, True Dragon or Dragon Goddess. Albedo's Chapter 1179 comparison explicitly continues to distinguish her own Dragon Goddess status from Evie's position as Witch Queen, while Chapter 1182 still treats Evie as a separate Imperial ruler rather than one of Victor's transformed wives.[3][12]

### Relationships

#### Albedo Moriarty Elderblood
[[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]] is Evie's biological mother and original magical master. Albedo belongs to a much older generation of Witches and possesses knowledge that predates much of the civilization Evie eventually ruled; she is also the creator of the original Arcane Realm.[17] Evie's status as Witch Queen therefore never meant that she had surpassed Albedo in every field of [[Magic]]. Their specialties developed in different directions, with Evie becoming an administrator, researcher and ruler while Albedo expanded into [[Alchemy]], Soul-body research, Divine systems and eventually Draconic Magic.
Their relationship is unmistakably familial despite being unusually abrasive. Albedo frequently mocks Evie, interferes with her personal affairs or allows her to struggle rather than immediately solving a problem, while Evie has become comfortable enough to openly complain about her mother's behavior.[15][1] In Chapter 1179, Evie tells Albedo that her unannounced appearances are bad for her peace of mind and directly argues with her about [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. At the same time, she becomes genuinely concerned when Albedo's behavior seems unusual and asks what her mother has been doing, demonstrating that irritation does not translate into emotional distance.[3]
Chapter 1181–1182 provides perhaps the clearest example of their dynamic. Albedo decides on her own that Evie should consider Victor romantically, physically drags Evie and Emily into the matter and takes the situation directly to Violet. Evie later describes the resulting encounter as one of the strangest conversations of her life and Emily finally understands why her mother sometimes avoids Albedo.[11][12]

#### Emily Moriarty
[[Emily Moriarty]] is Evie's confirmed biological daughter and the person for whom she has demonstrated the strongest unconditional attachment. Evie personally trained Emily in Witchcraft and involved her in some of Arcane's most ambitious projects, including the dimensional experiment that led to contact with the [[Magic]] World.[15][10] Although Evie's parenting can appear clinical or excessively pragmatic, Emily's safety ultimately matters more to her than political independence, wealth or personal freedom. When Divine Poison and Curses placed Emily beyond Evie's ability to save, the Witch Queen immediately surrendered herself and everything she had built to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] in exchange for her daughter's survival.[9]
Their later relationship is considerably lighter. In Chapter 1181 Evie unexpectedly learns that Emily has developed romantic feelings for a young Werewolf from Tasha's family, information her daughter had successfully kept from her.[11] After returning to Arcane in Chapter 1182, Evie insists upon hearing every detail and declares that her beloved daughter being in love is something to celebrate. Emily, knowing her mother's personality, immediately prohibits experiments and asserts her right to refuse sensitive questions. Evie accepts the conditions after considering them, showing both the persistence of her obsessive curiosity and her willingness to respect Emily's boundaries when they are made explicit.[12]

#### Victor Elderblood
Evie's relationship with [[Victor Elderblood]] develops from distant political observation into rivalry, reluctant cooperation, submission and finally Imperial partnership. Evie first views Victor as an abnormal young Vampire whose exploration rights and rapid accumulation of resources could benefit the Witches, but failed Fate divination and reports of his increasingly impossible growth make her treat him as a potential danger as well as an opportunity.[18][4] Their first major direct political contest occurs during the Demonic War, when Victor confronts Evie concerning Arcane's relationship with [[Diablo]] and defeats her preferred methods of manipulation through preparation and superior information rather than physical force.[6][27][7]
The confrontation produces their unusual nominal teacher-student relationship, with Victor referring to Evie as "Master" in relation to [[Magic]]. The title survives largely because Victor enjoys teasing her rather than because Evie becomes to him what [[Scathach]] is in [[Martial Arts]].[6][7] Their later interactions become progressively less adversarial as the dangers surrounding the Arcane [[Black Hole|Singularity]] and foreign powers demonstrate that Victor possesses enough strength to protect the Witches without needing to deceive them into dependence.
Chapter 990 permanently changes their political relationship. Evie voluntarily places herself and her civilization under Victor when he becomes the only person capable of saving Emily.[9] Victor does not discard her authority; he instead integrates her expertise into his Empire and later personally elevates her to Godhood by awakening the Research Divinity already latent within her Soul.[2] He subsequently entrusts her with the conquest and development of the Magic World, giving Evie a civilization-scale research and expansion project perfectly aligned with her talents.[2]
Despite this closeness, Evie is not one of Victor's confirmed wives as of Chapter 1182. Chapter 1179 already has Albedo explicitly question why Evie has failed to marry him despite the enormous passage of Time.[3] Chapter 1182 goes further: when Albedo attempts to obtain Violet's approval, Evie personally explains that although she is interested in Victor as the Emperor, she does not consider that interest equivalent to romantic love. She says she barely knows him on a personal level and regards most of their relationship as professional.[12] Violet later succeeds in making Evie acknowledge Victor's attractiveness more openly, but no confession, marriage, [[Soul Marriage]] or entry into the harem occurs.

#### Violet Elderblood Snow
[[Violet Elderblood Snow]]'s most significant direct interaction with Evie occurs in Chapter 1182 when Albedo brings Evie's possible relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] before the Empress. Violet initially rejects the proposal because she believes Evie's own wishes matter more than Albedo's certainty that her daughter is attracted to Victor. Evie's explanation that admiration is not love satisfies the immediate objection, although Violet later uses photographs of Victor to demonstrate that Evie has become more accustomed to his presence than she realizes.[12] The meeting ends without Violet forcing any romantic outcome, leaving Evie's personal decision unchanged.

#### Honorary Daughters
Evie uses the designation of "daughter" for several elite Witches beneath her authority, but most of these relationships are not confirmed biological descent. Known examples include [[Selena Moriarty]], [[Alice Moriarty]], [[Hecate Moriarty]] and at least four additional Witches. Their positions appear to combine adoption, apprenticeship, political rank and personal loyalty within the Moriarty/Witch hierarchy. Evie historically treats these daughters more pragmatically than Emily and has been willing to use them as political or magical agents, or even interfere with their memories, when she considers the interests of the greater Witch civilization more important.[30][22]

#### Powers and Abilities
Evie is one of the most accomplished Witches produced by Earth's supernatural civilization and served as the Witch Queen for nearly two millennia. Her combat power has historically depended less upon overwhelming physical strength than upon enormous magical knowledge, versatility, preparation and the ability to select an appropriate spell for a situation.[1]
Following Chapter 1093, Evie additionally became a genuine Minor Goddess of Research. Her modern power therefore consists of two primary systems:
Witch [[Magic]] + Divine Authority over <Research>
Her ascension enhanced rather than replaced her Witchcraft.[2]

##### Witch Physiology
- Witch Physiology: Evie belongs to the supernatural Human-derived Race known as Witches. Witches possess the capacity to awaken and manipulate Mana, allowing them to use structured [[Magic]], magical circles, artifacts, contracts and numerous specialized systems.
- Mana Manipulation: Evie possesses enormous mastery over Mana and can shape it into extremely complex spells, magical formations, enchantments and ritual structures. Even before reaching Godhood, she was one of the greatest known Mortal users of Magic.[1][3]
- Magic: Evie's primary power is extremely advanced Witchcraft. Her repertoire includes offensive, defensive, mental, environmental, spiritual, contractual, divinatory and ritual Magic rather than being restricted to a single Element or school.[3][4]
- Magic Circle Creation: Evie frequently forms complicated magical circles to structure her spells. These formations can appear around her, beneath targets or inside her eyes depending upon the technique being performed.[3][4]
- Concealed Spellcasting: Evie is sufficiently skilled to conceal the construction or summoning of Magic from other supernatural observers. During the negotiations involving [[Merlin]], Evie identified herself as one of the people capable of hiding their summoning from a Being who would otherwise intercept ordinary Magic.[5]
- Thought-Based Spellcasting: Evie can establish prepared magical countermeasures and activate them with minimal physical action. During her private meeting with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], she distributed numerous spells throughout the room without displaying conventional visible circles and required only a thought to activate her preparations if Victor became hostile.[6]
- Magic Trap Creation: Evie can covertly place multiple countermeasure spells throughout an area and prepare them to respond to hostility or other predetermined conditions.[6]

##### Magic Perception and Analysis
- Energy Detection: Even before reaching Godhood, Evie's magical eyes and Witch senses allowed her to perceive supernatural Energy and spell activity around her.[7][3]
- [[Magic]] Analysis: Evie can use evaluation Magic to examine supernatural Beings and magical phenomena. She attempted to magically evaluate Yama and the disguised [[Merlin]], immediately realizing that her spell had been intercepted when Merlin destroyed it.[8][5]
- Information Analysis: Evie can use Magic to inspect magical documents and supernatural structures for hidden properties. She repeatedly analyzed Yama and Merlin's contract for concealed traps, deceptive clauses and magical interference before accepting it.[5]
- Magic Detection: Evie can recognize the activation, interception and alteration of Magic with sufficient accuracy to identify when another advanced magician has interfered with her technique.[5]

##### Mind Manipulation
- Mind Manipulation: Evie possesses several forms of [[Magic]] capable of influencing the thoughts, perceptions and behavior of other Beings.[9][3][4]
- Seduction Inducement: Evie's best-known passive mental effect is her Seduction Magic. Rather than simply increasing physical attractiveness, the Magic subconsciously pressures observers toward viewing Evie in a favorable or attractive manner.[3]
- The influence can function even through a televised or remote image rather than requiring direct physical proximity.[3]
- The effect is not absolute. Individuals possessing sufficiently powerful Wills can recognize or resist the influence.[3]
- Suggestion: Evie can magically implant or induce suggestive thoughts intended to influence the actions of another Being.[4]
- Mind Control: Evie possesses higher-order individual-control Magic capable of attempting to directly control another Being rather than merely influencing their mood.[4]
- Memory Manipulation: Evie has demonstrated the willingness and ability to tamper with the memories of one of the Witches she regarded as a daughter when doing so served her objectives.[9]

##### Contract Magic
- Contract Manipulation: Evie is among the greatest known specialists in the Witch art used to create magically binding supernatural contracts.
- Binding: Witch contracts can metaphysically enforce the agreements written within them and punish or restrict those who violate their conditions.
- Contract Creation: Evie can construct extremely high-level magical agreements appropriate even for rulers and ancient supernatural Beings.
- Contract Analysis: Her expertise also allows her to examine existing contracts for hidden clauses, traps or supernatural deception.[5]
- Contract Nullification: As Witch Queen, Evie possesses sufficiently high Authority within Witchcraft to interfere with and potentially undo even extremely high-grade Witch contracts. This should not be described as every contract she creates being absolutely unbreakable by everything in existence; sufficiently exceptional Authorities can circumvent ordinary Witch limitations.

##### Air Manipulation
- Air Manipulation: Evie can create and control enormous concentrations of moving air through Tempest-type spells.[4]
- Tempest: An offensive wind spell that releases a violent storm-like burst toward the chosen area.[4]
- Tempest Multiplox: A variation designed primarily around force rather than injury. Evie creates a powerful gust capable of forcing targets backward while minimizing direct damage.[4]
- Force Manipulation: Tempest Multiplox demonstrates that Evie can use moving air as controlled physical force without necessarily turning the attack into a lethal cutting or destructive effect.[4]

##### Curse Manipulation
- Curse Manipulation: Evie can create and apply high-order magical Curses capable of placing harmful supernatural conditions upon their targets.[4]
- Hex Casting: Evie is capable of using Hex [[Magic]] against other Beings.[10] The precise effects depend upon the spell used; the existence of Hex Magic should not be interpreted as an unrestricted ability to make literally every possible misfortune happen to a target.

##### Environmental Manipulation
- Environmental Manipulation: Evie's [[Magic]] can directly alter the environment surrounding a battlefield in order to restrict, attack or reposition another Being.[4]
- Plant Manipulation: Evie can use green Magic circles to cause tree roots and vegetation to rapidly grow and assume shapes she desires. She casually created a throne for herself from living roots while meeting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[6]

##### Binding and Restraint
- Chain Creation: Evie can materialize magical chains through Witchcraft.[4]
- Binding: The magical chains can restrain or immobilize targets as part of attempts to capture rather than immediately kill them.[4]

##### Fate-Based Divination
- Divination: Evie can use the high-level Witch spell referred to as Fate or Destiny, one of the greatest information-gathering and locating techniques available to the Witch Queen.[7]
- Fate Perception: The technique obtains information by reading the Fate associated with the chosen target rather than simply searching conventional physical space.[7]
- Tracking: By following the target's Fate, Evie can attempt to determine that individual's current location.[7]
- Clairvoyance: The spell can obtain information concerning the target beyond what Evie can perceive through ordinary senses.[7]
- Retrocognition: With sufficient Energy and resources, Fate/Destiny can reveal information associated with events in the target's past.[7]
- Precognition: Higher applications can examine possible future information associated with the target's Fate.[7]
- Future information should not be interpreted as an absolutely predetermined timeline. Fate in the setting contains possibilities and branches rather than one completely immutable future.
- Limit — Fate Resistance: The technique is not omniscient. It completely failed to properly perceive [[Victor Elderblood]], making him appear to the spell like an existence it could not locate or meaningfully read.[7]

##### Spirit Manipulation
- Spirit Manipulation: Evie can create, summon and manipulate supernatural cursed spirits for specialized magical tasks.[11]
- Cursed Spirit Creation: She can produce cursed spiritual entities rather than relying solely upon naturally occurring spirits.[11]
- Truth Detection: Evie can employ these spirits during interrogation to determine whether nearby individuals are speaking truthfully or lying.[11]

##### Remote Magic
- Remote Projection: Evie can manifest or communicate through magical circles without requiring her physical body to travel conventionally to the target location. This allows her to personally participate in negotiations while remaining inside Arcane.[8]
- Remote Communication: Magical projections and Witch devices allow Evie to communicate across great distances and dimensions.

##### Dimensional Magic
- Portal Creation: Evie possesses the knowledge required to participate in and direct extremely large-scale portal rituals. Working with Emily and the Witches of Arcane, she constructed the experiment that opened a passage toward another inhabited world.[12][13][14]
- Dimensional Travel: The completed Arcane portal allowed physical passage between separate worlds rather than merely transmitting information.[4][15]
- Dimensional Stabilization: Evie attempted to control and stabilize the [[Black Hole|Singularity]] after the connection began behaving unpredictably, adjusting and supplying enormous amounts of [[Magic]] to prevent the phenomenon from collapsing or becoming immediately catastrophic.[14][16]
These feats involved a major Arcane ritual, equipment and other Witches. They should not be represented as Evie casually opening inter-universal portals with no preparation.

##### God Physiology
Evie became a genuine Minor Goddess in Chapter 1093 after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] accelerated the Divine potential already contained within her Soul.[2]
- Divine Physiology: Evie's existence is no longer limited to that of an ordinary Mortal Witch. Her body and Soul were elevated to support genuine Divinity and [[Divine Energy]].[2]
- Supernatural Condition: Becoming a Goddess greatly improved Evie's physical and supernatural baseline compared with her previous Mortal body.[2]
- Enhanced Strength: Her Divine body possesses physical strength surpassing that of her former Witch body.
- Enhanced Speed: Her Divine transformation correspondingly increased her ability to move and react.
- Enhanced Durability: Evie can withstand supernatural stresses that previously harmed her Mortal body. Immediately before her ascension, attempting to comprehend the Empire's advanced information caused severe physical and mental pain; Victor's transformation corrected this inadequacy.[2]
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Divine physiology grants Evie supernatural recovery beyond that of an ordinary Human-based Witch.
- Longevity: As a Goddess, Evie is no longer restricted by a conventional Human lifespan. This does not make her absolutely impossible to kill.
- Divine Energy Manipulation: Evie can produce and use Divine power after awakening a genuine Divinity.[2]

##### Divine Mana
- Energy Manipulation: Evie's existing Mana fundamentally improved when she became a Goddess. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] observed that it was becoming an Energy possessing the characteristics of [[Divine Energy]] while preserving the versatility of Mana.[2]
- Divine Mana Manipulation: This combined quality allows Evie to use her Witchcraft through a substantially superior Divine magical Energy rather than remaining limited to ordinary Mortal Mana.[2]
This should not be confused with Evie gaining unlimited access to every form of Divine Energy in Creation.

##### Divinity — <Research>
- Conceptual Manipulation: Evie possesses genuine Divine Authority over the Concept of <Research>.[2]
The Concept was not randomly assigned to her. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] specifically used Negativity's Authority over the spiritual side of existence to push Evie's Soul toward a Divinity she possessed the natural potential to awaken in the future.[2]
Her lifetime of studying [[Magic]], developing Witch civilization, performing experiments and relentlessly seeking new knowledge made Research fundamentally compatible with her identity.
- Research Empowerment: The Divinity provides a conceptual foundation suited to investigation, experimentation and the pursuit of knowledge. The full limits of this Authority have not yet been demonstrated.
- Limitation: Possessing the Divinity of Research does not grant Evie omniscience or automatic knowledge of everything. Like other Gods, she must understand and develop her Concept through continued experience and comprehension.

##### Divine Eyes
- Energy Perception: Following her ascension, Evie's ocular [[Magic]] developed to the point where she could directly perceive Energy.[2]
- Enhanced Vision: Multiple magical circles can cover her eyes when she exerts the ability.[2]
- Energy Analysis: The sight provides a much more direct method of studying supernatural Energy than ordinary Witch senses.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] compared the function to Dragon Eyes, but specifically regarded Evie's version as considerably weaker.[2]
He suspected that this evolved form of her ocular ability was a manifestation associated with the newly awakened Research Divinity.[2]

#### Skills
- [[Magic]] Mastery: Evie is one of Earth's greatest Witchcraft specialists and possesses practical knowledge across an enormous number of magical disciplines.[1][17]
- Genius Intelligence: Her greatest advantage has always been intelligence rather than raw physical power. Evie united the Witches, constructed Arcane's modern political structure and repeatedly competed with physically superior supernatural rulers through preparation and knowledge.
- Research Expertise: Evie's lifetime of experimentation and investigation eventually made her Soul naturally compatible with a literal Divine Concept of Research.[2]
- Ritual Expertise: Evie can direct enormous multi-user magical experiments such as the Arcane [[Black Hole|Singularity]] project.[12][13][14]
- Magical Engineering: Evie understands the construction, stabilization and adjustment of large-scale magical systems, including dimensional portal infrastructure.
- Contract Expertise: Evie possesses extensive knowledge of supernatural contracts, their construction and methods used to detect hidden magical clauses.
- Strategic Planning: She habitually enters negotiations and dangerous encounters with several contingencies prepared beforehand.
- Political Manipulation: Evie transformed Witch society into an economically and politically relevant global supernatural power while preventing significantly stronger factions from simply absorbing it for centuries.
- Leadership: She unified the Witches beneath the authority of the Witch Queen and administered Arcane for approximately two millennia.
- Deception: Evie is highly capable of withholding information, manipulating negotiations and presenting situations in whichever manner most benefits the Witches.
- Business Acumen: Witch society under Evie became deeply integrated into the supernatural economy through magical artifacts, services and contracts.
- Teaching: Evie personally trained [[Emily Moriarty]] and was regarded as sufficiently knowledgeable that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later arranged for her to nominally become his teacher of Magic.[18]
