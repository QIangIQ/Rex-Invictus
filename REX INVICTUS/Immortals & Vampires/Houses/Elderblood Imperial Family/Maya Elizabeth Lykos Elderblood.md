**[[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]]**, formerly Maya Elizabeth Lykos, is an ancient former Werewolf, former Matriarch of the Lykos Clan, one of the leaders of the Werewolf Faction within Dragon's Nest, and one of the wives of Victor Elderblood. (Lore adapted from *My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires*.)

### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 739
Woman, stop thinking with your lower parts for a few seconds, okay? I thought your fire had cooled down because you already have so many husbands and children, but it seems that hasn't changed.
—[[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] to Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood, Chapter 788
Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood, formerly Maya [[Elizabeth Lykos]], is an ancient former Werewolf, former Matriarch of the [[Lykos Clan]], one of the leaders of the Werewolf Faction within Dragon's Nest, and one of the wives of [[Victor Elderblood]]. A descendant of Elizabeth Lykos and the first Werewolf Progenitor Licael, Maya inherited the exceptionally rare Elizabeth Trait and developed from an Alpha Werewolf into a True Werewolf capable of Complete Werewolf Transformation. Her strength and status among Samar's [[Werewolves]] earned her the title "The Strongest Female Werewolf."[6][1][7][8]
Maya had already attained genuine Godhood before joining Victor's family, possessing combat-oriented Divinities whose individual Concepts have never been named.[6] Her relationship with Victor developed from the instinctive clash of two dominant predators into genuine affection, eventually culminating in a [[Soul Marriage]].[4] After Victor became the Primogenitor of the [[Blood Dragons]], Maya accepted conversion into his Race and acquired the complete body of a True Dragon. The transformation did not simply erase what she had been; her Werewolf Essence and Elizabeth-derived characteristics were incorporated into her new Blood Dragon foundation, making her present existence a fusion of her ancient Lykos heritage, Godhood and Victor's evolved Draconic lineage.[9]
Although Maya eventually relinquished direct control of Clan Lykos, she remains one of its most important ancestral figures and continues to intervene whenever the Clan threatens its own stability or her descendants. Within Victor's wider faction she, Leona and Tasha were selected as the leaders of the Werewolf side of his developing multi-racial civilization.[4] During the Imperial era, Maya remains an active Dragon Goddess and senior member of the Elderblood family, eventually having a daughter with Victor, [[Mirabelle Elizabeth Elderblood]].[3]

### Physical Appearance
Maya is an exceptionally tall and physically imposing woman standing approximately 187 cm tall. She has chocolate-brown skin, vivid sky-blue eyes and snow-white hair that originally fell roughly to her shoulders. Her body reflects a lifetime centered around combat: she possesses a heavily conditioned athletic physique with prominent musculature and clearly defined abdominal muscles rather than the softer build associated with many supernatural nobles.[10]
When introduced, Maya wore tight black pants torn around the thighs, black high heels decorated with golden details and a short black upper garment bearing a silver wolf design. Her clothing emphasized both her confidence and the fact that she had never attempted to disguise the warrior-like nature of her body.[10]
Before becoming a Blood Dragon, Maya's appearance became progressively more feral as she released additional portions of her Werewolf power. Her teeth sharpened, fur appeared across parts of her body and her white hair became increasingly wild while her physical pressure rose dramatically.[6] She had progressed beyond ordinary Alpha transformation and achieved a Complete Werewolf Transformation, an advanced racial state compared in principle with the complete transformations used by the strongest Noble Vampires.[8]
Following her conversion in Chapter 918, Maya obtained the genuine body of a True Dragon and became a Blood Dragon Goddess. The new body did not completely remove the characteristics of her former Race; [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood Dragon conversion absorbed and reconstructed her previous racial Essence, allowing the Lykos and Elizabeth characteristics that defined Maya to remain part of her evolved existence.[9]

### Personality
Maya possesses an intensely predatory and martial personality shaped by more than a millennium of life within Werewolf society and amplified by the Elizabeth Trait. Strength, growth, competence and the [[Will Solace|Will]] to resist domination are fundamental standards by which she evaluates herself and others. Her first substantial interaction with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] therefore resembles an instinctive contest between apex predators rather than conventional diplomacy. Maya releases increasing amounts of her presence to test him, Victor refuses to submit, and the pressure generated between them becomes severe enough to affect weaker [[Werewolves]] in the surrounding area.[6][11]
Her view of romance historically followed the same logic. Maya accumulated many husbands over her long life, but continued interest depended heavily upon whether a partner remained capable of growing and attracting her instincts. Men who stagnated effectively ceased to interest her, while those who continued improving could regain her attention. Maya admits that the last partner before Victor who had genuinely satisfied the predatory part of her nature was Adam's father centuries earlier.[11] Victor attracts her because he represents virtually everything the Elizabeth-enhanced side of Maya had been seeking: overwhelming strength, continual growth, refusal to submit and the mentality of an Alpha who does not require an established hierarchy to make others instinctively acknowledge him.
The Elizabeth Trait is central to understanding Maya's behavior. Its accelerated instinctive development grants extraordinary potential but also makes the Werewolf side of a carrier increasingly forceful, emotional and difficult to suppress.[7][8] Maya's age and discipline allow her to control those impulses far better than an inexperienced carrier, but they remain visible in her enjoyment of powerful opponents, attraction toward dominant partners and immediate reactions to contests of hierarchy.
Maya is nevertheless much more than uncontrolled instinct. She ruled one of Samar's oldest and largest Clans for centuries, understands politics, recognizes long-term consequences and is capable of deliberately restraining herself when the survival of her people requires it. She distrusts rulers such as [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] because she understands how apparently beneficial agreements can become mechanisms of control, while her response to Victor gradually changes as she recognizes that his ambitions could provide Clan Lykos and Werewolf society opportunities unavailable under Samar's existing structure.[12][13]
Her treatment of family combines genuine affection with severe Werewolf standards. Maya cares deeply about her children and descendants, but does not believe love requires gentleness. She expects those claiming authority to possess enough strength, intelligence and resilience to justify it, and she later advises [[Edward Jonathan Elizabeth Lykos]] to be considerably harsher toward relatives who continually undermine his position as Clan Leader.[14] Edward chooses a less brutal style of government, which Maya accepts so long as it remains effective.
Threatening her family produces a much more violent response. When Lykos men plotted against young members of Victor's family, Maya personally punished them by repeatedly inflicting catastrophic bodily damage and then using Reality alteration to restore them so that the punishment could continue. The intervention stabilizes Edward's authority while showing that becoming a Dragon Goddess changed the scale of Maya's methods rather than softening her underlying personality.[14]
Despite her intimidating disposition, Maya is capable of humor, teasing, ordinary familial affection and surprisingly relaxed behavior around people she genuinely accepts. By the later Imperial era she can casually spend time with Tasha on a transformed Samar while discussing how drastically their civilization has changed, demonstrating that the woman once defined almost entirely through Clan hierarchy has become part of a much larger family and civilization without losing her original identity.[15]

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Maya enters the story during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s visit to Samar. His confrontation with [[Adam William Lykos]] draws the attention of Clan Lykos' ancient Matriarch, who immediately recognizes that the Vampire standing before her does not behave according to the ordinary hierarchy she expects. Rather than treating Victor primarily as a political guest or racial enemy, Maya tests him directly through [[Will Solace|Will]], instinct and supernatural pressure. As she releases progressively more of her Werewolf nature, her appearance becomes increasingly feral and her power rises into a genuinely Divine range. The encounter establishes that Maya is not merely an old Alpha: she is a True Werewolf, an existing Goddess with combat-oriented Divinities and one of Samar's strongest native [[Werewolves]].[10][6][11]
Their clash does not become a serious attempt to kill one another. Instead, Victor's refusal to submit immediately earns Maya's interest, while Victor becomes fascinated by a Werewolf capable of answering his own predatory presence without collapsing. Their continued interactions during his stay on Samar gradually become more personal. Walks through the Werewolf nation that begin as political conversations increasingly resemble dates, and Maya's attraction intensifies as she observes Victor repeatedly disrupt the assumptions of Samar's old hierarchy, including his ability to overwhelm [[Volk Fenrir]] in a manner that produces a powerful instinctive reaction from nearby Werewolves.[16][17][18]
The same period reveals Maya's deeper ancestry. Clan Lykos descends from [[Elizabeth Lykos]], wife of the first Werewolf Progenitor Licael, whose rare mutation occasionally reappears after several generations. The mutation dramatically accelerates development of the Werewolf's instinctive side while increasing both potential and the difficulty of controlling those instincts. Maya is one of its natural carriers and personally recognizes the same characteristic within [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]], explaining why Leona received the name "Elizabeth."[19][7][8][20] Maya's own development had already reached the Complete Werewolf state, and among the Wolves she carries the title The Strongest Female Werewolf.[1][8]
Maya's role extends well beyond her Bloodline. As Samar's political situation deteriorates, she attempts to protect Werewolf interests without blindly obeying Volk or accepting the designs of foreign powers. Her longstanding knowledge of rulers such as Vlad makes her skeptical of even apparently favorable agreements, while Victor's dealings with Samar and its World Tree increasingly demonstrate that his faction could become a transformative force for the planet.[12][13][21] At the same time, Victor's interest in [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]] forces Maya to confront her own attraction and the incompatibility between becoming part of Victor's household and continuing the network of former husbands she no longer truly respects.[22]
By Chapter 847, Victor addresses both issues directly. He explains that his control over Souls could help awaken dormant Elizabeth potential already existing within suitable Lykos descendants rather than artificially implanting a foreign mutation, offering Maya a method of strengthening her Clan without erasing its ancestry.[23] Their personal relationship is resolved shortly afterward. Maya abandons the obsolete relationships she had accumulated across centuries and enters a genuine [[Soul Marriage]] with Victor, formally becoming part of the Elderblood family.[4] During the same period, Maya, Leona and Tasha are selected as the principal leaders of the Werewolf Faction within Victor's developing Council of [[Races (Flamel)|Races]], preserving Maya's political importance even as her allegiance expands beyond Clan Lykos.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Maya's marriage brings her progressively deeper into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s emerging civilization. She remains closely connected to Samar but is now treated as part of Victor's inner family; while accompanying [[Anna Elderblood]] during work associated with the planet, she discovers that the large network of shadow protectors assigned to Anna also regards Maya as a protected member of the household.[24]
Her greatest personal transformation occurs after Victor establishes the [[Blood Dragons]]. Victor offers his family complete True Dragon bodies rather than partial Draconic characteristics, and Maya immediately recognizes how much greater the racial foundation of a genuine Dragon is compared with even a highly developed Werewolf. The new body offers vastly greater physical potential and an innate relationship with nature, Creation and supernatural Energy that [[Werewolves]] do not naturally possess.[9]
Maya accepts the transformation. Crucially, Victor's method does not simply overwrite her with a generic Dragon template. Her former Werewolf Essence, accumulated development and Elizabeth characteristics are incorporated into the Blood Dragon transformation, producing a True Dragon whose identity still contains the racial qualities that made Maya exceptional before conversion.[9] From this point onward she is no longer biologically classified as an ordinary Werewolf; she becomes a Blood Dragon Goddess while remaining one of the most important descendants and representatives of the Lykos lineage.

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
As [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s civilization expands, Maya eventually steps away from direct administration of Clan Lykos. [[Edward Jonathan Elizabeth Lykos]] becomes its active leader, allowing the ancient Matriarch to remain an ancestral authority without continuing to manage every internal dispute personally.[5][14] Maya does not become politically irrelevant, however. Her authority, personal strength and relationship with Victor make her someone ordinary members of the Clan cannot meaningfully disregard.
This becomes painfully clear after members of Clan Lykos begin plotting against daughters of the Elderblood family. Maya intervenes before the incident escalates into the kind of retaliation Victor or Leona would have delivered. By this stage she has developed the ability to shape Reality directly. Although explicitly less proficient than [[Anna Elderblood]], Maya can affect living supernatural beings, restore destroyed tissue and heal someone brought close to death with relative ease. She uses those abilities to repeatedly mutilate and reconstruct the conspirators until the lesson is understood, indirectly making Edward's later leadership much easier because relatives who had constantly challenged him become considerably more cooperative.[14]
The Imperial era also expands Maya's direct branch of Victor's family. She and Victor have a daughter, [[Mirabelle Elizabeth Elderblood]], who inherits Maya's dark complexion, white hair and athletic characteristics together with Victor's evolved Blood Dragon lineage.[3] During the 100,000th Imperial Year Maya appears alongside Leona, Leone and Mirabelle while several members of Victor's old Human circle reunite in [[Nightingale]]. Maya and Leona are powerful enough to perceive the mental conversation between the younger girls even though Leone and Mirabelle believe they are speaking privately.[3]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
By the latest era, Maya remains closely involved with Samar despite the enormous scale reached by the [[Elderblood Empire]]. During preparations for the merger of the connected Sectors, she is present with [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]] in Samar's transformed Royal Capital. The world is almost unrecognizable compared with the planet Maya once governed as a Clan Matriarch: Samar has expanded to roughly three times the size of old Earth, its capital alone exceeds the area once occupied by South America and the general Werewolf Bloodline has been purified to levels approaching the Race's ancient origins.[15]
Maya and Tasha observe these changes while waiting for the sector-merging ritual to begin. The scene places Maya in a notably different role from her introduction. She is no longer burdened with maintaining Clan Lykos as its sole Matriarch or competing within the old hierarchy built around Volk; she can simply walk beside Tasha and watch the civilization they once struggled to preserve develop under a vastly larger Imperial order.[15]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
Maya's relationship with [[Victor Elderblood]] begins through challenge. Victor's refusal to submit to her [[Will Solace|Will]], combined with his continual growth and instinctive dominance, immediately appeals to the predatory side of Maya created and intensified by the Elizabeth Trait.[6][11] Their attraction deepens throughout his stay on Samar as political conversations increasingly become personal encounters, particularly after Maya witnesses Victor overpower the assumptions upon which Werewolf hierarchy had traditionally been built.[16][18]
Victor also forces Maya to address an inconsistency in her life. She desires a partner capable of continuously challenging her yet continues maintaining formal relationships with men in whom she has long since lost genuine interest. Maya ultimately chooses Victor and enters a [[Soul Marriage]] with him, permanently joining his family.[23][4] She later accepts his Blood Dragon transformation and becomes a True Dragon Goddess through his lineage.[9]
Their union eventually produces [[Mirabelle Elizabeth Elderblood]], directly combining Victor's Blood Dragon lineage with Maya's Lykos and Elizabeth heritage.[3]

#### Lykos Clan
Maya served as Matriarch of [[Lykos Clan]] for centuries and is responsible for a substantial portion of its enormous modern family tree. Her authority was based not simply upon seniority but upon her status as one of the greatest natural [[Werewolves]] produced by the Clan, a True Werewolf, Elizabeth carrier and the woman known as the Strongest Female Werewolf.[10][1][8]
She eventually relinquishes direct leadership to [[Edward Jonathan Elizabeth Lykos]]. Maya does not attempt to undermine him simply because his style is less brutal than hers, although she remains willing to intervene when Clan politics threaten the family as a whole.[5][14]

#### Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos
[[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]] is Maya's granddaughter and one of the extraordinarily rare descendants to naturally inherit the Elizabeth mutation. Maya personally recognizes the mutation within Leona and marks her connection to the ancestral legacy through the name Elizabeth.[7][8]
Despite their close ancestry, Maya and Leona only develop a substantial personal relationship after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s arrival on Samar, reflecting the enormous size and unusual structure of Clan Lykos. Their later positions become even more interconnected after both enter Victor's family and, together with Tasha, assume leadership over the Werewolf Faction inside his wider civilization.[4]

#### Tasha Fenrir Elderblood
Maya and [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]] were already ancient figures within Samar's political structure before either woman became part of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family. Their positions initially differed—Maya led Clan Lykos while Tasha functioned as Samar's Queen—but both understood the weaknesses of the old Werewolf hierarchy and both eventually became deeply connected to Victor.
Alongside Leona, Maya and Tasha are recognized as leaders of the Werewolf Faction within Dragon's Nest.[4] Their relationship remains close into the latest Imperial era, with Maya accompanying Tasha through Samar while they await the Sector merger in Chapter 1179.[15]

#### Mirabelle Elizabeth Elderblood
[[Mirabelle Elizabeth Elderblood]] is Maya's biological daughter with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and the clearest direct union of Maya's personal Bloodline with the second generation of Elderblood Dragons. Mirabelle inherits Maya's darker complexion, white hair and athletic characteristics while also being born into Victor's exceptionally powerful Draconic lineage.[3]
Maya is present with Mirabelle during the 100,000th Imperial Year and can perceive the supposedly private mental conversation between Mirabelle and Leone, demonstrating the familiarity and supernatural connection maintained among the Lykos branch of the Elderblood family.[3]

### Abilities

#### Powers and Abilities
Maya possesses an unusually layered supernatural existence produced by several distinct stages of racial and Divine evolution. She began as an extraordinarily powerful Alpha Werewolf, naturally inherited the Elizabeth Trait, progressed into a True Werewolf capable of Complete Werewolf Transformation and independently attained Godhood with combat-oriented Divinities.[1][2][3] After joining [[Victor Elderblood]], Maya underwent conversion into a full Blood Dragon. Victor's transformation incorporated rather than discarded her previous racial Essence, causing her Werewolf and Elizabeth characteristics to become part of her new True Dragon foundation.[4]
Her present abilities consequently derive from four interconnected foundations: True Dragon/Blood Dragon physiology, retained Lykos and Elizabeth characteristics, pre-existing Godhood, and abilities developed after becoming a Dragon Goddess.

##### Blood Dragon and True Dragon Physiology
- Blood Dragon Physiology: Maya is a complete Blood Dragon created through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s evolved lineage. Rather than receiving merely Draconic characteristics, she obtained a genuine True Dragon body while preserving the significant racial characteristics she possessed before conversion.[4]
- Hybrid Racial Foundation: [[Blood Dragons]] incorporate the predatory qualities of Victor's original Vampiric lineage into a complete Draconic existence. Maya's personal transformation is further distinguished by the integration of her former True Werewolf and Elizabeth Essence.[4]
- Racial Essence Integration: The transformation reconstructed Maya's pre-existing racial traits within her new Dragon body rather than maintaining several unrelated biological systems beside one another.[4]
- Power Retention: Becoming a Blood Dragon did not make Maya lose the important abilities, instincts and hereditary characteristics accumulated during her Werewolf existence.[4]
- True Dragon Physiology: Maya possesses the complete biological and supernatural foundation of a True Dragon. She herself recognizes that the potential of a genuine Dragon body vastly exceeds even the naturally powerful physical structure of a developed Werewolf.[4]
- Supernatural Strength: Maya was already one of Samar's physically strongest [[Werewolves]] and experienced a major increase in bodily potential after acquiring a True Dragon body.[1][4]
- Supernatural Speed: Her physiology provides movement and combat speed vastly exceeding conventional supernatural species.[1][4]
- Supernatural Durability: Her current Dragon body possesses resilience far beyond the Werewolf physiology she previously considered exceptionally powerful.[4]
- Supernatural Stamina: Maya possesses the endurance of a True Dragon Goddess layered upon more than a millennium of prior Werewolf development.
- Enhanced Agility and Reflexes: Her old Werewolf combat instincts remain integrated with the bodily control and sensory capabilities of her Dragon physiology.[2][4]
- Enhanced Senses: Maya retains the powerful senses developed as a Werewolf while possessing the additional perception inherent to a True Dragon body.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Maya possesses powerful supernatural regeneration. Her current Blood Dragon physiology adds the regenerative capabilities of Victor's racial lineage to the healing foundation she already possessed as an ancient Werewolf.[4]
- Longevity / Extended Lifespan: Maya was already more than a thousand years old before becoming a Blood Dragon and survives into the 100,000th Imperial Year. Her current True Dragon Goddess physiology is no longer limited by ordinary mortal aging.[5][6]
- Self-Sustenance: As a developed True Dragon Goddess, Maya's body operates far beyond the biological requirements of ordinary mortal species. True Dragon Goddesses do not require ordinary food or sleep for survival, although they can still voluntarily eat, rest and participate in such activities for enjoyment.[7]
- Shapeshifting / Metamorphosis: [[True Dragons]] possess a naturally malleable physical existence, allowing Maya to maintain a Humanoid body despite possessing a complete Dragon physiology.[4]
- Humanoid Form: Maya can exist in the Human-like appearance she maintained before conversion without ceasing to be a True Dragon.
- True Dragon Form: Maya possesses a genuine Draconic body as a complete Blood Dragon rather than merely manifesting isolated scales, horns or wings.[4]
- Partial Transformation: Her current racial foundation can manifest individual Draconic characteristics without necessarily requiring complete transformation.
- Flight: Maya's True Dragon form possesses the natural aerial mobility of Dragonkind.
- Natural Weaponry: Her Draconic body can provide supernatural claws, fangs, scales, horns and the other biological weapons inherent to True Dragons.
- Dragon Breath: As a full True Dragon, Maya possesses the racial foundation necessary to produce Dragon Breath. The novel has not identified a unique elemental or conceptual property for Maya's individual Breath, so no specific variant should presently be assigned to her.
- [[Draconic Energy]] Manipulation: Maya possesses and can control the Energy naturally produced by her current Dragon physiology.[4]
- Energy Generation and Circulation: Her complete Dragon body possesses the internal supernatural infrastructure necessary to produce and sustain enormous quantities of Draconic Energy.
- Energy Manipulation: True Dragons do not require Runes merely to manipulate ordinary Energy; their ability to command supernatural Energy is inherent to their biology.[8]
- Nature and Creation Affinity: True Dragons possess an exceptionally close relationship with nature and the powers of Creation. Maya specifically recognized this as one of the advantages offered by Victor's transformation.[4]
- Elemental Compatibility: A sufficiently skilled True Dragon can interact with and manipulate a broad range of natural Elements rather than being biologically restricted to one conventional affinity.[4]
- Creation Energy Compatibility: Maya's current Race is naturally compatible with forces associated with Creation to a degree her previous Werewolf physiology was not.[4]
- Limitation: Racial compatibility with multiple Elements does not establish that Maya has individually mastered every Element or every application of Creation.
- Draconic Language: As a True Dragon Goddess, Maya possesses the racial foundation for the language and [[Will Solace|Will]]-based supernatural expression used by Dragonkind.
- Will Imposition: Draconic Language can impose the user's Will upon Energy and phenomena when the Dragon possesses enough understanding and power to support the command.
- Limitation: Maya has not yet demonstrated a unique advanced Dragon-Language technique comparable to the most specialized users in Victor's family
- Draconic Runes: Maya possesses the racial compatibility necessary to understand and employ Draconic Runic principles.
- Reality/Law Inscription: Dragon Runes are particularly useful when a Dragon wishes to impose a structured Concept, law or alteration upon Reality rather than merely manipulating Energy instinctively.[8]
- No Automatic Runemaster Status: Possessing True Dragon physiology does not make Maya an advanced Runemaster. Advanced Runecraft still requires individual understanding, study and practice.

##### Retained Werewolf Nature
Maya's Werewolf abilities predate her Blood Dragon transformation. Although she is no longer biologically classified as an ordinary Werewolf, Chapter 918 establishes that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s transformation incorporated her former Essence into the new Blood Dragon body rather than annihilating it.[4]
- Werewolf Physiology (Former): Maya was one of the strongest natural [[Werewolves]] produced by Samar, possessing the physical power, regeneration, heightened senses, predatory instincts and transformation abilities associated with the Race.[1][3]
- Alpha Werewolf Physiology: Maya developed as a dominant Alpha and ruled Clan Lykos for centuries. Weaker Werewolves instinctively recognized her position through both raw power and supernatural presence.[1][9]
- True Werewolf Physiology: Maya progressed beyond the conventional development of an Alpha by deeply exploring and accepting the primal Wolf side of her existence. She is directly described as someone who could be considered a True Werewolf.[1]
- Racial Mastery: The state represented a deeper integration with the instincts and nature of the Werewolf Race rather than simply a numerical increase in power.
- Predatory Development: Her True Werewolf nature allowed increasingly complete expression of the characteristics ordinarily suppressed within a Humanoid form.
- Complete Werewolf Transformation: Maya achieved the advanced complete manifestation of her Werewolf nature, an exceptionally rare state compared with the complete transformations used by the strongest Noble Vampires.[3]
- Before fully transforming, Maya could progressively release individual characteristics including sharpened teeth, fur, a more feral appearance and substantially increased power.[1]
- The racial Essence underlying this transformation survives inside her Blood Dragon existence, although the novel has not separately shown Maya recreating the exact pre-conversion Complete Werewolf Form after becoming a True Dragon.[4]
- Enhanced Predatory Instincts: Maya possesses extreme instinctive sensitivity toward strength, hierarchy, danger and dominance. These instincts were already exceptional for a Werewolf and are further intensified by the Elizabeth Trait.[9][2]
- Supernatural Aura / Presence: Maya can release her [[Will Solace|Will]] and predatory pressure strongly enough to physically and instinctively affect weaker supernatural beings. Her clash of presence with Victor placed significant pressure upon nearby Werewolves.[1][9]
- Indomitable Will: Centuries of warfare, leadership and control over her predatory nature have given Maya an exceptionally strong Will. During her first encounter with Victor she resisted his supernatural pressure instead of reflexively submitting.[1][9]
- Resistance to Supernatural Compulsion: Maya can resist forms of supernatural influence that attempt to force an instinctive or mental response from her, as demonstrated during her confrontation with Victor.[1]
- This is evidence of significant resistance rather than proof of absolute immunity to every possible form of Mind, Soul or Conceptual Manipulation.

##### Elizabeth Trait
Maya is one of the naturally occurring carriers of the Elizabeth Trait, a rare hereditary mutation descending from [[Elizabeth Lykos]], wife of Licael, the original Werewolf Progenitor.[2][3]
- Genetic Enhancement: The Elizabeth mutation increases the natural potential of the Werewolf carrying it, allowing physical and instinctive development beyond the ordinary limits reached by most members of Clan Lykos.[2]
- Accelerated Instinctive Development: The mutation causes the Werewolf side of its carrier to develop substantially faster than normal, accelerating access to characteristics and instincts that conventional [[Werewolves]] require much longer to awaken.[2]
- Enhanced Potential: Carriers possess exceptionally high developmental potential even by the standards of Clan Lykos. Maya's progression into a True Werewolf, Complete Werewolf and Goddess demonstrates the level a sufficiently mature carrier can reach.[2][3]
- Enhanced Instincts: The mutation greatly intensifies normal Werewolf instincts, contributing to Maya's extreme sensitivity to dominance, powerful opponents and Alpha-like individuals.[2][3]
- Bloodline Dominance / Resistance to Alteration: The Elizabeth characteristic behaves as an unusually predatory hereditary trait and resists being casually overwritten by other supernatural Bloodlines.[10]
- This resistance is not absolute. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later Blood Dragon transformation possessed enough racial dominance to integrate Maya's Werewolf and Elizabeth Essence into a superior Draconic foundation rather than being rejected by it.[4]
- Genetic Inheritance: The mutation can disappear for generations before naturally resurfacing within a descendant. Maya later identifies the same inherited mutation in [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]] and recognizes her as an important successor to Elizabeth's legacy.[2][3]

###### Side Effects
The Elizabeth Trait's extraordinary benefits are accompanied by equally intensified instincts.
- Instinctive Instability: The carrier's Werewolf instincts can become increasingly difficult to suppress as the mutation develops.[2]
- Emotional Amplification: Strong emotions can provoke more severe instinctive responses than in an ordinary Werewolf.[2]
- Predatory Mentality: An inexperienced carrier risks allowing instinct to overpower rational judgment if insufficient discipline is developed.[2]
- Experienced Control: Maya's centuries of experience allow her to control these effects far more effectively than a young carrier, although the trait remains visible in her reaction to exceptionally powerful Alphas and challenges involving dominance.[9][11]

##### Divine Abilities
Maya independently attained genuine Godhood before becoming a Blood Dragon. Her confrontation with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] establishes that she possessed combat-oriented Divinities while she was still a True Werewolf.[1]
- Goddess Physiology: Maya possesses the supernatural existence and Divine foundation of a genuine Goddess in addition to her racial physiology.
- [[Divine Energy]] Manipulation: Maya can generate, circulate and manipulate Divine Energy as part of her Godhood.[1]
- Divinity / Conceptual Authority (Unnamed): Maya possesses genuine Divine Concepts associated with combat.[1]
- The novel has not identified the actual names of those Concepts.
- Abilities such as War Manipulation, Battle Manipulation, Strength Divinity, Combat Divinity or similar powers should therefore not be assigned as individual named Divinities until a chapter explicitly identifies them.
- Power Augmentation: Releasing progressively greater portions of her Divine and Werewolf existence dramatically increased Maya's combat output during her initial confrontation with Victor.[1]
- Divine-Racial Integration: Maya retains her pre-existing Godhood after becoming a Blood Dragon, producing her current state as a Blood Dragon Goddess rather than replacing one supernatural system with another.[4]

##### Reality Manipulation
During the Imperial era, Maya developed the ability to shape Reality directly. Chapter 1116 explicitly identifies her as capable of the same general class of Reality alteration used by other developed Dragon Goddesses in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family, while establishing that her proficiency remains below specialists such as [[Anna Elderblood]].[12]
- Reality Warping: Maya can alter local Reality through direct supernatural manipulation.[12]
- Her demonstrated control is sufficient to directly affect living supernatural Beings.
- The known feats are localized and should not be inflated into unrestricted control over Reality at every scale.
- Healing: Maya can rewrite the condition of an injured target so that their body returns toward a healthy state. She considers restoring someone after bringing them close to death relatively easy.[12]
- Body Restoration: Maya can recreate or restore destroyed and severed body parts through Reality alteration.[12]
- Biological Restoration / Manipulation: By changing the physical state of a living target, Maya can reconstruct damaged biological structures directly rather than relying solely upon conventional regenerative healing.[12]
- Her demonstrated use is primarily restorative; broad unrestricted Biological Manipulation has not been established.
- Repeated Restoration: Maya can alternate catastrophic injury and Reality-based restoration repeatedly. She used this capability while punishing Lykos conspirators who threatened members of Victor's family.[12]

##### Other Abilities
- Telepathy / Mental Perception: During the 100,000th Imperial Year, Maya and Leona are able to perceive the supposedly private mental conversation between [[Mirabelle Elizabeth Elderblood]] and Leone Luna Elena Elderblood.[6]
- The scene demonstrates that Maya can access or perceive family mental communication under the relevant circumstances, although it does not establish unrestricted Mind Reading against every target.
- Supernatural Detection: Maya possesses highly developed perception originating from her former Werewolf senses, immense combat experience and present True Dragon physiology.
- Power Perception: Her predatory instincts allow her to evaluate another Being's strength and instinctive position with exceptional sensitivity, one of the qualities responsible for her immediate reaction to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[1][9]

#### Skills
- Master Combatant: Maya spent more than a millennium as one of Samar's greatest warrior Alphas before acquiring a True Dragon body. Her combat experience predates her introduction by centuries, and she instinctively evaluates worthy opponents through direct confrontation.[1][9]
- Extensive Combat Experience: Maya has fought, trained and survived throughout numerous generations of Werewolf history, giving her practical experience vastly beyond younger supernatural fighters.
- Predatory Instinct: Her Alpha Werewolf foundation and Elizabeth mutation provide exceptional instinctive judgment regarding strength, danger, hierarchy, aggression and weaknesses in another Being's confidence.[9][2]
- Leadership: Maya governed the enormous [[Lykos Clan]] as Matriarch for centuries and maintained authority within a society where leadership is constantly measured through personal strength and the willingness of subordinates to challenge weakness.[13][3]
- Werewolf Faction Leadership: After joining [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s organization, Maya, Leona and Tasha were selected as leaders of the Werewolf Faction within the Council of [[Races (Flamel)|Races]].[14]
- Political Experience: Centuries as Matriarch gave Maya extensive knowledge of Samar's Clan system, Werewolf Royal Family, international supernatural politics and the behavior of ancient rulers such as [[Volk Fenrir]] and [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]].[15][16]
- Clan Administration: Maya successfully managed an enormous Clan containing dozens of her own children and a vast number of later descendants for centuries.
- Intimidation: Maya understands Werewolf dominance intimately and can use raw presence, reputation and carefully applied brutality to force rebellious subordinates into obedience.[1][12]
- Knowledge of Werewolf Biology: Maya possesses extensive knowledge of Werewolf development, Alpha hierarchy, True Werewolf evolution, transformation and the hereditary structure of Clan Lykos.[2][3]
- Knowledge of the Elizabeth Trait: As an experienced natural carrier, Maya understands the mutation's development, hereditary behavior, advantages and dangers well enough to identify it within Leona.[2][3]
- Maternal and Clan Discipline: Maya is highly experienced in controlling descendants whose instincts are governed by Werewolf hierarchy. Her methods are often severe but highly effective within the cultural context from which she originated.[12]

#### Former Abilities and States
The following characteristics describe Maya before her Blood Dragon conversion. Their underlying Essence was incorporated into her current body, but the exact original forms have not all been demonstrated again after Chapter 918.
- Ordinary Werewolf Racial State: Maya is no longer biologically an ordinary Werewolf following her transformation into a Blood Dragon.[4]
- Alpha Werewolf Classification: Her original racial rank ceased to be the primary classification of her existence after becoming a True Dragon.
- True Werewolf State: Maya's former True Werewolf Essence remains part of her current racial foundation, but she now exists fundamentally as a Blood Dragon.[4]
- Complete Werewolf Transformation: Maya possessed this transformation before conversion. The novel has not directly demonstrated whether she can reproduce the exact old transformation independently of her current Draconic form.[3][4]

#### Limitations and Unconfirmed Abilities
- Maya's individual combat-oriented Divinities remain unnamed; no specific Concept should be invented for them.[1]
- Her individual Dragon Breath property has not been revealed.
- True Dragon compatibility with Creation and multiple Elements does not establish complete mastery over every Element.[4]
- Maya possesses the racial basis for Draconic Language and Runes, but advanced Runemaster-level proficiency has not been individually demonstrated.
- Her Reality manipulation is explicitly less proficient than Anna's and has only been demonstrated on a comparatively localized scale.[12]
- Her resistance to supernatural compulsion should not be treated as universal immunity to every Mind, Soul or Conceptual effect.
- The Elizabeth Trait's resistance to Bloodline alteration is powerful but demonstrably not absolute; [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood Dragon transformation successfully integrated it.[10][4]
- The exact pre-conversion Complete Werewolf form has not been shown again after Maya became a Blood Dragon.
