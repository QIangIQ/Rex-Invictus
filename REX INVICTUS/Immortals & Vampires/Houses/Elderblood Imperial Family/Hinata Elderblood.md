**[[Hinata Elderblood]]**, known within the Shinto Pantheon as Hinata-Omikami, is a recurring character in My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires, an Imperial Princess of the Elderblood Empire, a naturally born Blood Dragon Goddess and the biological daughter of Victor Elderblood and Amaterasu Elderblood. (Lore adapted from *My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires*.)

Hinata Elderblood, known within the [[Shinto Pantheon]] as Hinata-Omikami, is a recurring character in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]], an Imperial Princess of the [[Elderblood Empire]], a naturally born Blood Dragon Goddess and the biological daughter of Victor Elderblood and [[Amaterasu]] Elderblood. Through Victor, she belongs to the second generation of genuine [[True Dragons]] produced by the Elderblood lineage, while her maternal ancestry connects her directly to the ruling family of [[Takamagahara]] and the ancient Amatsukami. Her position combines the extraordinary supernatural inheritance of the Imperial Family with the religious and political prestige of the Shinto Divine dynasty, making her one of the highest-ranking Goddesses recognized within her mother's Pantheon.[1][2]
Hinata possesses a natural relationship with the power of the Sun, an inheritance subsequently demonstrated when she contributes Solar power to an experiment involving the creation of Eldritch Life. The Sun is one of the principal Divine authorities associated with Amaterasu, whose supernatural existence governs several important aspects of the Shinto Pantheon. Hinata's ability to provide Solar power confirms that she possesses access to this aspect of her maternal inheritance, although the novel has not explicitly established whether she holds an independent personal Divine Concept of the Sun, shares an inherited manifestation of Amaterasu's authority or possesses a differently developed Solar Divinity. Her contribution eventually becomes part of the supernatural foundation used to create [[Cthugha]], a new Great Old One possessing characteristics associated with stars, plasma and foreign Eldritch existence.[3]
Despite her immense supernatural status, Hinata displays little attachment to most of the ancient Gods belonging to her mother's family. She considers ordinary members of Takamagahara weak and excessively submissive, regarding their prestige as insignificant compared with the extraordinary power possessed by Victor and the individuals surrounding him. Although she occasionally interacts with her maternal relatives out of obligation, she generally refuses to treat biological connections as sufficient reason for affection or respect. The principal exception is her maternal grandmother, [[Izanami-no-Mikoto]], with whom Hinata develops an unexpectedly positive relationship despite Izanami's earlier conflict with Amaterasu. Their compatibility surprises both older Goddesses and becomes one of the most distinctive aspects of Hinata's personal history.[1]
Within Takamagahara, Hinata is known as Hinata-Omikami, a Goddess of the highest rank whose status commands extraordinary respect. Ordinary Gods would not casually insult or challenge her, partly because of her own supernatural inheritance and partly because she is the daughter of Amaterasu and Victor. However, the authority associated with her Shinto title should not be confused with an independently established position among the [[Regulators of Existence]]. She is a high-ranking Divine Princess and genuine True Dragon Goddess, but no confirmed event establishes that she has become a universal Primordial Regulator or independently governs the complete cosmological Concept of the Sun.[2]
Hinata's personality differs considerably depending upon the environment. In public, she imitates Amaterasu's aristocratic presentation, wearing elaborate Japanese royal clothing and maintaining the dignified mannerisms expected of a high-ranking Shinto Goddess. Within the Elderblood household, she becomes considerably more mischievous, shameless and openly possessive, particularly whenever an opportunity arises to obtain something connected to Victor. Her willingness to participate in elaborate schemes over photographs of his Human childhood demonstrates that her affection for her father outweighs the formal principles of dignity she normally claims to uphold.[2]
As the Empire continues developing, Hinata becomes increasingly involved in the ordinary activities, educational gatherings and supernatural experiments undertaken by Victor's daughters. She participates in a prank targeting [[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]], joins the family's celestial banquet, questions Victor about the origin of the Universe's Sector divisions and contributes Solar power to the creation of Cthugha. Her later activities reveal a combination of intellectual curiosity, extraordinary familial attachment and a willingness to experiment with supernatural principles extending beyond the traditional Divine structure of the Shinto Pantheon.[4][5][6][3]

### Identity
Hinata's identity is established through her direct biological relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and [[Amaterasu]]. She belongs to the Elderblood dynasty as an Imperial Princess and to the Shinto Divine family as a daughter of its ruling Goddess Queen. Her inherited Blood Dragon characteristics make her fundamentally different from conventional descendants of the Amatsukami, although her maternal lineage remains an important part of her public position and personal presentation.
Her earliest confirmed appearance in the narrative is an unnamed reference to Amaterasu's daughter during the period when Victor's children begin developing more independent relationships with the older families of their mothers. The daughter is distinguished by her contempt for ordinary [[Takamagahara]] Gods and her unusual affection for Izanami, characteristics subsequently associated with Hinata. Her personal name is formally revealed during the confrontation involving Stella and the missing photographs of Victor, establishing the identity of the previously unnamed daughter as Hinata Elderblood.[1][2]
The designation Hinata-Omikami is associated with her position in the [[Shinto Pantheon]]. The suffix reflects the elevated religious status recognized by Amaterasu's Divine society, where the ruling family occupies an exceptionally prestigious position. Hinata is explicitly described as a Goddess of the highest rank, and her status carries enough authority that ordinary Divine beings would not dare disrespect her casually. Her rank therefore involves more than an honorary title, although no evidence establishes that she possesses the complete governing authority of her mother or independently rules Takamagahara.[2]
Hinata also possesses a strong connection to the Solar inheritance of Amaterasu. Her ability to contribute the power of the Sun to [[Cthugha]]'s creation confirms that Solar abilities form part of her supernatural foundation. However, her exact personal Divinity has not been comprehensively explained, and the available material does not establish that she possesses every Divine Concept belonging to Amaterasu, including Light, Day and Sky. Those authorities remain distinct unless their individual manifestations are explicitly demonstrated or attributed to Hinata.[3]

### Physical Appearance
Hinata is an exceptionally beautiful young Dragon Goddess with fair skin, extremely long black hair and deep-violet eyes. Her violet Dragon Eyes reflect the supernatural characteristics inherited from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] rather than [[Amaterasu]]'s golden Solar eyes, while her black hair closely resembles the coloration associated with her mother's traditional appearance. Beneath one of her eyes is a distinctive birthmark that differentiates her from Victor's other biological daughters. Her overall appearance combines the elegance of the Shinto Divine aristocracy with the extraordinary physical characteristics of a naturally born Blood Dragon Goddess.[2]
Her clothing is strongly influenced by Amaterasu, whose traditional presentation reflects her position as the ruler of the [[Shinto Pantheon]]. Hinata wears an elaborate royal kimono, deliberately adopting the aristocratic style associated with the Japanese Divine court. She also arranges her long hair in the manner of a Japanese princess rather than allowing it to fall naturally around her body. The combination of her clothing, hairstyle and formal bearing makes her appear particularly similar to her mother despite the differences in their eye colors and supernatural characteristics.[2]
Hinata frequently carries a folding fan colored black and purple, decorated with a Dragon symbol that reflects her connection to the Elderblood lineage. The fan is among her most recognizable personal possessions, and she regularly raises it before the lower half of her face while speaking. The gesture is copied directly from Amaterasu, demonstrating how carefully Hinata imitates her mother's aristocratic mannerisms. Its use is particularly evident when she attempts to maintain a dignified appearance during conversations in which her actual behavior is considerably less respectable than the image she wishes to project.[2]
The contrast between Hinata's appearance and personality is most evident within the Imperial household. Although her royal attire and formal posture suggest a composed and authoritative Goddess, she frequently becomes embarrassed, irritated or mischievous when interacting with her sisters. Her folding fan provides an additional means of concealing facial expressions, allowing her to maintain an outwardly dignified appearance even when she is attempting to avoid responsibility or disguise her excitement over something connected to Victor.[2]
Her physical presentation during the later Imperial era remains closely associated with her maternal ancestry. Unlike daughters whose clothing reflects modern Imperial fashion or the martial traditions of their respective mothers, Hinata deliberately maintains the appearance of a Japanese Divine Princess. Her black hair, violet Dragon Eyes, distinctive birthmark and black-and-purple fan consequently form an unusually recognizable combination among the members of Victor's second generation.
As a naturally born Blood Dragon Goddess, Hinata possesses a fundamentally Draconic biological and spiritual existence beneath her Humanoid appearance. Her body provides the racial foundation associated with the transformations, supernatural strength and advanced compatibility of genuine [[True Dragons]]. However, the complete appearance of her individual True Dragon Form has not been described in sufficient detail to establish its precise dimensions, scale coloration or specialized anatomical characteristics. Her exact height and current subjective age also remain unidentified.

### Personality
Hinata is proud, aristocratic, aloof, mischievous and intensely devoted to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Her personality combines the formal bearing of a high-ranking Shinto Goddess with the possessive and occasionally shameless behavior common among several members of the Elderblood second generation. Although she generally presents herself as someone who values dignity, propriety and the traditions associated with her mother's Pantheon, those principles become considerably more flexible whenever an activity involves her father or another subject of personal interest. This contrast gives her a distinctive personality, allowing her to appear exceptionally dignified in public while behaving like an ordinary competitive daughter within the privacy of the Imperial household.[1][2]
Her pride is especially evident in her treatment of the older Shinto Gods. Hinata possesses little affection for most members of [[Takamagahara]] and considers them weak, excessively obedient and undeserving of automatic respect. Their age, Divine titles and ancestral relationships do not impress her because she was raised within a family containing [[True Dragons]], extraordinarily powerful Goddesses and beings capable of influencing fundamental aspects of Reality. Compared with the standards established by Victor and his wives, the prestige associated with ordinary Shinto Divinity appears almost insignificant to her.[1]
Hinata's attitude toward her maternal relatives reflects a broader characteristic of Victor's naturally born daughters. They frequently regard the members of the Elderblood household as their genuine family while remaining comparatively indifferent toward relatives they have barely encountered. Biological ancestry alone does not establish a meaningful emotional relationship for them, particularly when their mothers' earlier families contain individuals whose values and supernatural abilities differ substantially from those of the Draconic dynasty. Hinata therefore maintains occasional contact with her maternal relatives primarily because of obligation rather than genuine interest.[1]
The principal exception is her grandmother Izanami. Despite the conflict between Izanami and [[Amaterasu]], Hinata becomes genuinely comfortable around the ancient Goddess and continues visiting her while she remains sealed. Their compatibility surprises both women because the circumstances provide little obvious reason for such a positive relationship. Hinata's willingness to maintain contact demonstrates that her disdain toward Takamagahara does not extend indiscriminately to every member of the Pantheon. She is capable of developing sincere relationships with individuals she finds interesting or personally compatible, regardless of their complicated political history.[1]
Within the Elderblood household, Hinata adopts a considerably more playful attitude. She imitates Amaterasu's fan gestures and attempts to behave with the dignity expected of a Shinto Princess, but frequently participates in the same mischievous activities as her sisters. Her behavior during the dispute over Victor's photographs demonstrates how quickly she abandons her public principles when the opportunity arises to obtain something she considers valuable. Although she initially insists that stealing is beneath the dignity of a woman of her position, she subsequently participates in concealing the photographs and develops increasingly elaborate excuses when Stella discovers what happened.[2]
Hinata possesses enough intelligence to recognize that Stella's ability to perceive Falsehood makes ordinary lies ineffective, yet attempts to deceive her anyway. When Stella identifies the lie, Hinata first attempts to redirect blame toward Irene before claiming that she technically purchased the photographs rather than stealing them. Her explanations become increasingly contradictory, revealing a willingness to manipulate the wording of her actions even when she knows that her sister understands the situation perfectly. The incident reflects mischievous opportunism rather than serious hostility, since the dispute involves personal belongings rather than any genuine attempt to harm another member of the family.[2]
Her greatest emotional weakness is her attachment to Victor. Hinata values her father so deeply that photographs from his Human childhood are sufficient to make her disregard the rules of propriety she normally claims to uphold. When Stella accuses her of shamelessness, Hinata openly declares that she is capable of anything when their father is involved. The statement reveals that her devotion is not merely an inherited sense of loyalty toward the Emperor but a deeply personal affection that can override her desire to appear dignified or morally consistent.[2]
Hinata's ability to improvise excuses becomes especially humorous when Stella reminds her that she had insisted upon maintaining her dignity only moments earlier. Rather than attempting to reconcile the contradiction, Hinata simply claims that she has already evolved beyond the woman she was several minutes ago. The explanation demonstrates her willingness to embrace absurd reasoning when it allows her to avoid admitting defeat. Her humor also appears when she teases Irene by comparing her confidence in her own beauty to Boa Hancock, revealing that Hinata is familiar with entertainment from Victor's original Human culture despite her traditional Japanese Divine presentation.[2]
Although she can become shameless when Victor is involved, Hinata remains capable of embarrassment when he personally notices her misbehavior. After joining a prank targeting Velina, she becomes visibly uncomfortable when Victor discovers that even his usually dignified little Sun participated in the chaos. She turns her flushed face away and avoids looking directly at him, demonstrating that his opinion carries considerably more emotional weight than the reprimands or criticism of ordinary individuals. Her reaction resembles that of many of Victor's daughters, whose confidence can collapse immediately when they believe they have disappointed their father.[7]
Hinata also possesses intellectual curiosity extending beyond ordinary familial interests. During Victor's lessons concerning the origins of Creation, she asks whether the current division of the Universe into different Sectors was established by the original Creator or by the Primordial Beings who remained active afterward. The question demonstrates that she understands the distinction between the original act of Creation and the later development of its governing structure. She listens seriously when Victor explains his uncertainty and offers the possibility that the contemporary arrangement was established by the Primordial authorities after the Creator ceased participating directly in the Universe's affairs.[6]
Her curiosity occasionally takes a more experimental form. When her sisters begin combining different supernatural abilities to create Eldritch Life, Hinata willingly participates and contributes the power of the Sun. Rather than remaining restricted to the traditional duties of a high-ranking Shinto Goddess, she joins experiments involving foreign cosmic organisms, Leviathans and Draconic materials whose interactions are not fully understood. Her involvement demonstrates confidence in her family's ability to handle extraordinary supernatural phenomena and a willingness to cooperate with sisters possessing powers entirely different from her own.[3]
Hinata therefore possesses a complex personality shaped by Divine pride, inherited Draconic confidence, affectionate possessiveness and the extraordinary circumstances of her upbringing. Her public dignity is genuine in the sense that she understands the expectations associated with her position, but she does not always consider those expectations more important than her personal desires. Her continued development reflects the contrast between being a highly respected Goddess within an ancient Pantheon and remaining one of Victor's beloved, mischievous daughters within the Elderblood household.

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Hinata was born from the union of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Amaterasu]] Elderblood, combining the extraordinary Draconic foundation of Victor's later lineage with the Divine characteristics of the ruling family of [[Takamagahara]]. Amaterasu had already become one of Victor's wives and undergone the supernatural development associated with his Blood Dragon family, allowing their daughter to inherit the characteristics of a genuine True Dragon rather than beginning life as a conventional Shinto Goddess. Hinata consequently possessed a supernatural foundation considerably more advanced than that of ordinary Gods belonging to her mother's Pantheon, while retaining a direct connection to the traditions and Divine hierarchy of the Amatsukami.[1]
As Victor's daughters continued developing, their relationships with the older families of their mothers became increasingly complicated. Many of the younger Dragons possessed little interest in establishing connections with individuals whose existence had not been part of their daily lives, particularly when those relatives belonged to civilizations whose natural power and social traditions differed substantially from the Elderblood household. Although the children acknowledged their biological relationships, most did not automatically regard the older relatives of their mothers as members of their immediate family. Their closest emotional bonds remained centered upon Victor, their mothers and the sisters with whom they had grown up.[1]
Hinata was among the daughters who maintained occasional contact with their maternal relatives despite this general indifference. However, her interactions were primarily motivated by obligation rather than genuine affection. She viewed the majority of Takamagahara's Gods with contempt, considering their behavior excessively submissive and their supernatural abilities insignificant compared with the powers possessed by her own family. Their traditional prestige was insufficient to earn her respect, and she considered most of them so weak that they could be disposed of with negligible effort.[1]
The strength of Hinata's disdain was particularly notable because she was a direct descendant of the ruling family of the [[Shinto Pantheon]]. Although her maternal ancestry placed her within the Amatsukami, she did not consider that connection sufficient to create loyalty toward every member of its Divine society. Her upbringing within Victor's Draconic family had established a different standard of power and personal worth, making the ordinary distinctions between prestigious and insignificant Gods appear comparatively unimportant.[1]
Her relationship with [[Izanami-no-Mikoto]] developed in a substantially different direction. Despite the history of conflict between Izanami and Amaterasu, Hinata began visiting her maternal grandmother and established an unexpectedly positive connection with her. Their compatibility surprised both Goddesses because Izanami had only recently opposed Amaterasu in a struggle involving the Shinto Pantheon. Amaterasu consequently remained uncertain whether the relationship was desirable, particularly because the wounds caused by the earlier conflict had not completely disappeared.[1]
Victor saw no reason to prevent Hinata from visiting Izanami, while the ancient Goddess remained sealed and incapable of acting without restriction. Amaterasu therefore allowed the meetings to continue, choosing to monitor the interactions rather than forbid her daughter from maintaining a relationship she appeared to enjoy. The arrangement demonstrated the difference between Hinata's personal preferences and the political concerns inherited through her mother's family, since she did not automatically adopt Amaterasu's hostility toward an individual with whom she had developed genuine compatibility.[1]
Hinata's developing relationship with Izanami also established that her contempt toward ordinary Shinto Gods was not an indiscriminate hatred of the entire Pantheon. She was willing to spend time with an ancient member of the family despite the complicated circumstances surrounding their relationship, indicating that personal compatibility mattered more to her than traditional allegiance. Her continued visits consequently remained one of the most unusual aspects of her connection to Takamagahara during the earlier Imperial era.
Her upbringing otherwise continued within the extraordinary environment of Victor's family. Like his other biological daughters, Hinata possessed access to the advanced education, supernatural training and immense protective resources established by the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Her natural Draconic physiology provided a foundation superior to that of conventional Divine beings, while her maternal Solar inheritance offered a specialized supernatural domain closely connected to Amaterasu. However, her precise personal Divine Concept and complete range of combat techniques were not yet established during this period.

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Hinata's first named appearance occurs during a dispute involving photographs belonging to [[Anna Elderblood]]. The collection contains rare images of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] during his Human childhood and infancy, preserving a period of his life that most of his biological daughters never personally experienced. Because the images are among the few surviving records of his earlier existence, they possess enormous sentimental value within the Imperial Family. Anna protects the originals against unauthorized supernatural duplication, making the photographs unusually difficult to reproduce despite the extraordinary powers possessed by Victor's daughters.[2]
[[Stella Elderblood]] discovers that several photographs have disappeared from Anna's collection and begins searching for those responsible. Her investigation eventually leads her to Hinata's room, where she forcefully enters and confronts her sister. Hinata reacts indignantly to the intrusion, attempting to maintain the dignified manner expected of a Shinto Goddess despite the increasingly suspicious circumstances. The confrontation reveals her identity as Hinata Elderblood, daughter of Emperor Victor and Goddess Queen [[Amaterasu]], and establishes her public title of Hinata-Omikami, a Goddess of the highest rank within her mother's Pantheon.[2]
Hinata initially attempts to dismiss Stella's accusations by pretending that she does not understand what has happened. She raises her black-and-purple folding fan before her face in the manner copied from Amaterasu, attempting to preserve her usual aristocratic composure. However, Stella possesses a supernatural relationship with Truth and Falsehood that allows her to recognize deception even when she deliberately restrains her powers. Hinata's attempt to lie consequently fails almost immediately, leaving her unable to conceal the fact that she possesses information concerning the missing photographs.[2]
The confrontation becomes more complicated when Stella reveals that [[Irene Elderblood]] is also present. Irene has attempted to conceal herself within the room, but Stella recognizes the deception and forces her to abandon the hiding place. Hinata immediately complains that she had warned Irene against attempting to hide from someone capable of perceiving Falsehood, then tries to redirect responsibility for the scheme toward her sister. Her efforts are undermined by the obvious fact that she also wanted the photographs, making it impossible to portray herself as an entirely unwilling participant.[2]
Irene argues that Stella's possession of the photographs is unfair because their father belongs to the entire family and the other daughters also wish to preserve images of his earlier life. Hinata agrees that the pictures are valuable but insists that stealing should be beneath the dignity expected of women occupying their status. Stella recognizes the contradiction between the claim and Hinata's participation in the scheme, prompting her sister to revise the explanation. Hinata subsequently insists that she technically purchased the photographs from another daughter rather than stealing them directly, attempting to distinguish her actions through the wording of the transaction rather than the circumstances under which the objects were obtained.[2]
The photographs are eventually produced, revealing images of Victor during his Human youth. The collection is particularly important because the surviving pictures include rare examples of his appearance as an infant, providing the daughters with glimpses of a period they could never personally experience. Although the Imperial Family possesses extraordinary supernatural powers, Anna's protections prevent the photographs from being copied casually, increasing their sentimental and practical value. Stella consequently refuses to allow the unauthorized possession to continue and recovers the missing items.[2]
Hinata remains unapologetically interested in the photographs even after Stella exposes the scheme. When accused of shameless behavior, she declares that she is capable of anything when Victor is involved. Stella immediately points out that her declaration contradicts the dignity she had emphasized only moments earlier, but Hinata refuses to admit defeat and claims that she has already evolved beyond her previous beliefs. Her willingness to abandon her own reasoning demonstrates how completely her attachment to Victor can override the formal principles she normally uses to distinguish herself from her more mischievous sisters.[2]
The argument also includes playful teasing between Hinata and Irene. Hinata mocks Irene's confidence in her own beauty by comparing her to Boa Hancock, a character associated with the Human entertainment culture preserved within the Empire. Their interaction reveals the ordinary sibling relationships existing beneath the extraordinary status of the Imperial Princesses, who can engage in humorous arguments and share interests despite possessing the power to threaten entire supernatural civilizations.[2]
Stella eventually returns the photographs to Anna's control and warns Hinata and Irene against repeating their behavior. The girls remain dissatisfied because they still wish to possess copies for themselves, but directly confronting Stella through overwhelming supernatural power is not an acceptable option. Victor has established strict rules prohibiting his daughters from casually employing dangerous abilities against one another outside appropriate training, consensual duels or genuinely justified circumstances. The restrictions are intended to prevent ordinary disagreements from becoming supernatural conflicts and encourage the girls to develop methods of resolving disputes without simply overwhelming their sisters.[2]
Hinata and Irene continue considering how to obtain their own photographs until [[Yog-Sothoth]] Elderblood approaches them. Although Yog possesses extraordinary Eldritch abilities, she remains bound by the same family rules and cannot simply use her powers to seize the objects from Stella. Instead, she proposes a much simpler solution: asking Anna directly. The three girls consequently approach Anna in her office and request copies of the photographs, only to discover that she is perfectly willing to provide them when approached appropriately.[2]
The simplicity of the solution leaves Hinata and Irene stunned after the effort they had invested in their earlier scheme. They suspect that Yog's presence contributed to Anna's willingness to cooperate because she possesses a reputation as one of the more obedient daughters and generally avoids the unnecessary trouble associated with several of her sisters. Regardless of the reason, the incident ends without further confrontation and demonstrates that the extraordinary powers possessed by the Imperial Princesses often complicate situations that could be resolved through ordinary communication.[2]
Hinata subsequently participates in a prank targeting [[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]]. Velina has been occupied with scientific work involving her mother's advanced research, eventually returning to the family's residence after completing her responsibilities. Before she can settle into the ordinary preparations for dinner, she is ambushed by a group of sisters consisting primarily of the red-haired daughters associated with the Scarlett family, together with Hinata. The girls place a sack over Velina's head and carry her away toward Sylvia's room, transforming her return into an elaborate act of sibling mischief.[4]
The prank is particularly amusing because Velina possesses enough supernatural strength and intelligence to recognize that the situation presents no genuine danger. Although she initially protests, the other daughters treat the incident as an ordinary game, while several witnesses observe the scene with disbelief. Hinata's participation is significant because she ordinarily cultivates the appearance of a dignified Shinto Princess, yet willingly joins the more chaotic members of her family in carrying out an unnecessary prank. Her involvement reinforces the difference between the formal identity she presents to [[Takamagahara]] and her comparatively mischievous behavior within the Imperial household.[4]
The incident becomes a subject of discussion during the large family gathering that follows. More than thirty-five daughters assemble as the story spreads, eventually attracting Victor and several members of the older generation. Velina returns in a disheveled state, visibly irritated with the sisters responsible for embarrassing her. Victor recognizes the participants and begins affectionately questioning them, expressing particular amusement that even Hinata joined the chaotic activities associated with the red-haired girls.[8][7]
Victor refers to Hinata as his little Sun, an affectionate nickname reflecting her maternal Solar inheritance and the personal relationship between them. Hinata immediately becomes embarrassed when he acknowledges her involvement in the prank, turning her flushed face away and avoiding his gaze. Her reaction contrasts with the confidence she normally displays when confronting ordinary Gods or arguing with her sisters, demonstrating that the possibility of disappointing Victor remains particularly important to her.[7]
Victor subsequently reassures Velina that her sisters were merely playing and that no one had crossed the limits established by the family's rules. Velina acknowledges that she could have escaped the prank if she genuinely wished and had participated more willingly than her initial complaints suggested. The incident consequently ends as an ordinary demonstration of sibling affection rather than a serious disciplinary matter, although the adults remain concerned that the daughters' extraordinary natural powers could make less controlled mischief dangerous.[7]
The gathering continues as more members of the family arrive, eventually becoming another example of the enormous household maintained by Victor. Although the daughters differ in maternal lineage, Divine inheritance and personal interests, they remain closely connected through their shared upbringing and extraordinary affection for their father. Hinata's participation in the prank illustrates how readily those relationships can transcend the cultural differences separating the older Pantheons associated with their mothers.
Hinata later attends the family's celestial banquet, an extraordinary celebration organized by Victor using his immense supernatural powers. Rather than providing ordinary furniture and decorations, he manipulates astronomical phenomena to create a setting composed of stars, nebulae, black holes and other cosmic structures. The environment is designed to accommodate the preferences of his wives and daughters, allowing each individual to select a seat formed from a celestial phenomenon she finds especially appealing.[5]
Hinata selects a chair made from a yellow star, a choice that reflects her strong association with the Sun. Other daughters select objects corresponding to their individual supernatural natures, with Nigredo choosing a black hole and several members of the Scarlett family expressing enthusiasm for a sofa made from blue mist. Hinata's preference demonstrates her affinity for Solar imagery, although selecting a star-shaped or star-derived seat does not independently establish that she created the astronomical object herself or possesses unrestricted manipulation of every star throughout the Universe.[5]
The banquet also reinforces the importance of ordinary family traditions within the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Despite possessing enough supernatural power to manipulate cosmic structures, Victor continues gathering his family for communal meals and encourages his wives and daughters to spend time together. Hinata participates in these events as a member of the Imperial household rather than through the formal ceremonial role she occupies within Takamagahara, preserving a personal relationship with her family that exists independently of her public Divine status.
Hinata subsequently attends Victor's educational gathering concerning the nature of Energy, Divinity and the original structure of Creation. His lessons introduce the daughters to questions involving the [[Akashic Records]], the original Creator, Positivity, Negativity and the primordial forces responsible for maintaining the Universe. Although the girls have received extensive education, Victor encourages them to question what they already know rather than assume that the traditional explanations preserved by individual Pantheons are necessarily complete.[9][6]
During the discussion, Hinata asks whether the original Creator established the Universe's present division into different Sectors or whether the responsibility belonged to the Primordial Beings. Victor recognizes the difficulty of the question because the original events occurred beyond the period he can completely reconstruct through his accumulated knowledge. Rather than presenting an unsupported answer as fact, he explains that the Creator appears to have ceased participating directly in Creation after the ancient universal conflict, making it plausible that the existing Sector structure was developed by the Primordial authorities who remained active afterward.[6]
Hinata accepts the explanation and thanks Victor, recognizing the distinction between what he knows and what he considers a reasonable possibility. Her question demonstrates an awareness that the present Cosmology may reflect later interventions rather than the original arrangement of Creation, an important consideration because individual Pantheons frequently preserve incomplete or misleading interpretations of the cosmic hierarchy. The discussion also reflects the limitations imposed upon younger civilizations by the separation between Lower, Middle and Higher Sectors, which protects developing Life while creating substantial differences in knowledge between their inhabitants.[6]
Hinata's most significant personally demonstrated contribution to the development of supernatural Life occurs during an experiment organized by Yog-Sothoth Elderblood and Nigredo Elderblood. The two sisters begin creating hybrid organisms using Eldritch beings and Leviathan characteristics, developing a group of unusual fish capable of surviving under supernatural conditions that would be impossible for conventional living creatures. Their experiments attract several additional daughters, including Valentina, Leone, Silvia, Rosemary, Stella and Hinata, who become interested in combining their individual abilities to determine what kind of organism the resulting mixture could produce.[3]
The experimental foundation is already unusually advanced before the additional daughters become involved. Yog and Nigredo have produced Eldritch-Leviathan hybrids possessing extraordinary spatial compatibility, while Nigredo contributes characteristics allowing them to survive within black holes. Leone subsequently supplies a form of adaptability intended to allow the creatures to exist under a much broader range of environmental conditions. The combination creates organisms whose supernatural foundation incorporates properties derived from different [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] and foreign cosmic principles rather than following the natural development of a single species.[3]
The sisters decide to expand the experiment by incorporating additional elemental and conceptual properties. Valentina suggests Dragon Fire, Silvia proposes Dragon Lightning and Rosemary offers Ice, while Hinata declares that she will contribute the power of the Sun. Stella subsequently supplies Truth-related power, and Velina eventually contributes intelligence after deciding that the experiment is sufficiently interesting to justify participation. The mixture is further strengthened by the inclusion of one of Victor's scales, providing an exceptionally powerful Draconic foundation whose interaction with the other abilities makes the resulting organism particularly unusual.[3]
Hinata's Solar contribution establishes that her inheritance is not limited to the symbolic prestige associated with Amaterasu's position. She possesses an actual supernatural manifestation of the Sun's power that can be incorporated into another being through cooperative experimentation. However, the experiment does not isolate the complete mechanics of her individual ability, and it remains uncertain whether the Solar contribution operates through a separate personal Divinity, a directly inherited manifestation of Amaterasu's authority or another aspect of Hinata's Draconic supernatural foundation.[3]
The experiment attracts [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood, who becomes alarmed upon discovering that the daughters are combining dangerous supernatural characteristics near the family residence. The girls immediately attempt to escape with their experimental materials, while the surrounding water begins reacting to the developing organism. Victor appears and reassures Aphrodite that he has been observing the experiment throughout its development and would have intervened if a genuinely catastrophic outcome became imminent. He considers the daughters' creativity valuable and refuses to punish them merely for attempting an unusual form of supernatural research.[3]
Their combined abilities eventually produce [[Cthugha]], a new Divine Eldritch entity whose body manifests as an enormous sphere of plasma covered in eyes. The creature incorporates characteristics associated with stars and foreign Eldritch existence, representing an unusual fusion of the native Universe's supernatural principles with those originating from another cosmological framework. Victor identifies Cthugha as a Great Old One rather than a genuine Outer God, explaining that only he and Azathoth possess the necessary authority to create Eldritch Gods of the higher category.[3]
Cthugha possesses genuine Divinity and becomes associated with the emergence of a new Race of Fire Vampires. Its existence is particularly unusual because ordinary Noble Vampires outside the Snow lineage traditionally possess an important weakness toward Fire, whereas the new creature's supernatural foundation incorporates powers associated with stars and plasma. Even as a newborn, Cthugha possesses enough natural power to destroy a Solar System, making the experiment one of the most extraordinary achievements produced through the combined efforts of Victor's daughters.[3]
Hinata's involvement is significant because her Solar power becomes one of the contributions used to establish this unusual Divine existence. However, Cthugha's final abilities cannot be attributed exclusively to her, since the creature results from the combined powers of numerous sisters, foreign Eldritch organisms, Leviathan characteristics and Victor's own Draconic material. The completed Great Old One consequently represents a cooperative creation feat rather than proof that Hinata independently possesses every power displayed by the organism.[3]
The danger of approaching Hinata with inappropriate intentions becomes apparent through an incident involving a God from the [[Shinto Pantheon]]. The God publicly expresses his desire to take Hinata as his wife, apparently failing to appreciate the consequences of making such a proposal concerning one of Victor's biological daughters. He disappears without a trace the following day, and Amaterasu subsequently confirms that he is dead. The circumstances are treated as a warning that members of the older Pantheons cannot presume entitlement to the women of the ruling Imperial Family simply because they possess Divine status or political influence.[10]
The incident also affects Susanoo, who becomes alarmed by the severity of the response and recognizes that the God's disappearance involves consequences beyond an ordinary death. Amaterasu's attitude remains cold because she is furious that someone within her own Pantheon considered approaching her daughter in such a manner, particularly after Victor expressed his displeasure at home. The event reinforces the distinction between the traditional marriage politics of older Divine societies and the autonomy and protection granted to the Imperial Princesses.[10]
Although the God's death is confirmed, the precise individual responsible is not explicitly identified in the available account. The incident should therefore not be treated as a confirmed personal combat feat performed by Hinata, nor does it establish that she independently erased the God from existence. Its significance lies primarily in demonstrating the consequences of disregarding the position of Victor's daughters and the determination of their parents to prevent the older Pantheons from treating them as political or marital resources.[10]
Hinata remains an important member of the Elderblood second generation through her combination of high-ranking Shinto status, genuine Blood Dragon physiology and inherited Solar power. Her later participation in cosmological discussions and supernatural experimentation demonstrates a developing interest in subjects beyond the obligations associated with Takamagahara, while her continued devotion to Victor and companionship with her sisters preserve the personal characteristics established during her first named appearance.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Hinata's biological father, the Progenitor of her Blood Dragon lineage and the most important individual in her emotional life. Through Victor, she possesses the extraordinary supernatural foundation of a genuine True Dragon Goddess and belongs to the Imperial dynasty governing the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Her attachment to him strongly influences her behavior, particularly when she encounters situations involving his reputation, personal belongings or relationships with other members of the family.[2]
Hinata's extraordinary affection becomes especially apparent during the dispute involving photographs from Victor's Human childhood. She considers the images valuable because they preserve a period of his life she could never personally experience, making their sentimental significance greater than any ordinary material worth. Her desire to obtain them becomes so strong that she abandons the dignity she normally claims to maintain and participates in increasingly elaborate attempts to conceal their unauthorized possession. When Stella criticizes her conduct, Hinata openly declares that she is capable of anything when their father is involved.[2]
Victor's relationship with Hinata is also affectionate and comparatively informal. During the family gathering following the prank against Velina, he refers to her as his little Sun, recognizing both her Solar inheritance and her personal identity within the enormous household. Hinata becomes visibly embarrassed when he notices that she joined the mischievous daughters responsible for the incident, demonstrating that his opinion matters far more to her than the formal expectations of [[Takamagahara]]'s Divine society.[7]
Hinata also respects Victor as a teacher and source of supernatural knowledge. She participates in his lessons concerning Creation and questions him about whether the current division of the Universe into Sectors originated from the Creator or the Primordial authorities. Rather than simply accepting the traditional explanations inherited through the [[Shinto Pantheon]], she seeks a broader understanding of the Cosmos from her father and accepts his distinction between established knowledge and reasonable possibilities.[6]
Victor continues supporting Hinata and her sisters as they develop through experimentation. He observes the creation of [[Cthugha]] and allows the daughters to combine their supernatural abilities under his supervision, demonstrating a willingness to encourage their curiosity while maintaining sufficient control to intervene if their activities become genuinely dangerous. Hinata's participation in the experiment consequently reflects both her individual Solar inheritance and her confidence in the protective environment established by her father.[3]

#### Amaterasu Elderblood
[[Amaterasu]] Elderblood is Hinata's biological mother, the ruler of the [[Shinto Pantheon]] and the strongest visible influence upon her public appearance and ceremonial behavior. Amaterasu's long existence and position within [[Takamagahara]] connect Hinata to one of Earth's most influential Divine dynasties, while her later transformation into a Blood Dragon Goddess allows their daughter to inherit a supernatural foundation exceeding that of conventional Shinto descendants.[1][2]
Hinata deliberately models her appearance upon Amaterasu, wearing elaborate Japanese royal clothing, arranging her long black hair in a traditional aristocratic style and carrying a folding fan resembling those used by her mother. Her habit of raising the fan before her face during conversations is specifically copied from Amaterasu, demonstrating the extent to which she admires her mother's public bearing. These similarities make their maternal relationship immediately recognizable despite the violet Dragon Eyes inherited through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[2]
Their connection extends beyond appearance because Hinata also possesses access to the power of the Sun, one of Amaterasu's principal Divine domains. Her contribution of Solar power to the creation of [[Cthugha]] establishes a genuine supernatural relationship with this aspect of her maternal inheritance. However, the complete nature of Hinata's personal Divinity remains unidentified, and her mother's additional authorities over Light, Day and Sky should not automatically be assigned to her without direct confirmation.[3]
Amaterasu experiences considerable uncertainty concerning Hinata's relationship with Izanami. Her mother had recently opposed her during the conflict surrounding Takamagahara, making the unexpected friendship between grandmother and granddaughter politically and emotionally uncomfortable. Nevertheless, Amaterasu recognizes that Victor sees no reason to prevent the meetings and that Izanami remains sealed, allowing Hinata to continue visiting while keeping watch over their interactions.[1]
Amaterasu is also strongly protective of her daughter against inappropriate attention from older Shinto Gods. When a member of the Pantheon expresses his intention to marry Hinata and subsequently disappears, Amaterasu confirms his death with cold indifference. Her response reflects anger that a member of her own Divine society presumed the right to approach her daughter without respecting the boundaries established by the Imperial Family.[10]

#### Izanami-no-Mikoto
[[Izanami-no-Mikoto]] is Hinata's maternal grandmother and one of the most important figures in her early personal history. Unlike the majority of [[Takamagahara]]'s older Gods, toward whom Hinata feels little affection or respect, Izanami develops a surprisingly positive relationship with her granddaughter. Their compatibility is particularly remarkable because Izanami had recently fought against [[Amaterasu]], creating considerable uncertainty concerning whether the connection should be encouraged.[1]
Hinata continues visiting Izanami while the ancient Goddess remains sealed, apparently finding her company more appealing than that of the ordinary Gods associated with her mother's Pantheon. The relationship surprises both older Goddesses because their earlier conflict provides little obvious foundation for such an affectionate bond. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] does not interfere, while Amaterasu reluctantly tolerates the meetings and monitors them to ensure that the circumstances remain safe.[1]
The exact reason Hinata and Izanami get along so well has not been explained. Their relationship nevertheless demonstrates that Hinata's disdain toward Takamagahara is based upon her personal assessment of its members rather than automatic hostility toward every individual belonging to the older Shinto family.

#### Stella Elderblood
[[Stella Elderblood]] is one of Hinata's sisters and the principal participant in the confrontation concerning Anna's missing photographs. Stella possesses advanced perception of Truth and Falsehood, making her exceptionally difficult to deceive even when she deliberately restrains her supernatural abilities. Her position within the Elderblood family also means that she does not treat Hinata's Shinto rank as a reason to avoid confronting her directly.[2]
Their dispute begins when Stella enters Hinata's room and demands the photographs taken from Anna's collection. Hinata attempts to conceal her involvement through increasingly implausible explanations, but Stella immediately recognizes the lies and exposes Irene's concealed presence. The confrontation continues as Hinata attempts to justify her behavior through technical arguments about whether the pictures were stolen or purchased, eventually abandoning the pretense of dignity and declaring that she is capable of anything when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is involved.[2]
Although the argument becomes heated, it remains an ordinary example of sibling rivalry rather than genuine hostility. Victor's household rules prevent the sisters from using dangerous supernatural abilities against one another to resolve personal disagreements, encouraging them to settle such matters through conversation and appropriate parental intervention. Their relationship consequently illustrates how the extraordinary power possessed by the Imperial Princesses coexists with comparatively ordinary family disputes.

#### Irene Elderblood
[[Irene Elderblood]] is one of Hinata's companions during the dispute concerning [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s childhood photographs. The two cooperate in obtaining and concealing the pictures, sharing the desire to preserve images of their father from a period of his life they could never personally experience. Although Hinata initially attempts to blame Irene when Stella discovers the scheme, she eventually acknowledges that she also wanted the photographs and was not merely an unwilling participant.[2]
Their relationship possesses a playful and competitive quality. Hinata mocks Irene's considerable confidence in her own beauty by comparing her to Boa Hancock, while Irene objects to the suggestion that Stella should possess exclusive access to their father's photographs. The two continue working together after Stella recovers the originals and eventually accompany Yog to Anna's office, where their request for copies is granted without further confrontation.[2]

#### Yog-Sothoth Elderblood
[[Yog-Sothoth]] Elderblood is one of Hinata's sisters and an important participant in resolving the dispute over [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s photographs. Although Yog possesses extraordinary Eldritch power, she is subject to the same household restrictions governing the use of supernatural abilities against family members. Rather than attempting to overpower Stella, she recommends approaching Anna directly and asking for the photographs through ordinary communication.[2]
Hinata and Irene initially consider whether Yog's presence could help them overcome the obstacle created by Stella, but her straightforward solution proves considerably more effective than their earlier attempts. Anna simply provides copies when asked, leaving the two girls astonished by the ease with which the problem is resolved. Their experience demonstrates the value of Yog's comparatively obedient behavior and the contrast between her practical reasoning and the elaborate schemes developed by her sisters.[2]
Hinata later participates alongside Yog in the experiment that produces [[Cthugha]]. Yog and Nigredo provide much of the original Eldritch foundation through their experimental organisms, while Hinata supplies Solar power as one of the additional supernatural characteristics incorporated into the developing being. Their cooperation demonstrates that the sisters can combine abilities originating from very different cosmological systems without being limited by the traditional boundaries separating native Divine powers from foreign Eldritch phenomena.[3]

#### Anna Elderblood
[[Anna Elderblood]] is Hinata's paternal grandmother and one of the principal maternal figures responsible for maintaining discipline within the Elderblood household. She possesses the rare photographs of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Human childhood that become the subject of Hinata's dispute with Stella, including images from a period of his life preceding his transformation into a Vampire.[2]
Anna protects the photographs against unauthorized supernatural duplication because of their sentimental importance. Hinata initially participates in a complicated scheme to obtain them, assuming that the collection is too valuable or restricted to be shared freely. However, when Yog accompanies Hinata and Irene to Anna's office, Anna willingly provides several photographs after receiving a straightforward request.[2]
The interaction demonstrates that Anna's authority does not prevent her from showing affection toward her granddaughters. She expects them to respect the family's rules and the belongings of other individuals, but has no reason to refuse an appropriate request for objects whose sentimental importance is shared throughout the household. Hinata's surprise consequently reflects her own unnecessarily complicated approach rather than evidence that Anna intended to prohibit the daughters from possessing photographs of their father.

#### Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III
[[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]] is one of Hinata's sisters and a participant in several later family activities involving scientific research and supernatural experimentation. Their most prominent interaction begins when Hinata joins the Scarlett daughters in ambushing Velina after she returns from her work. The prank does not represent genuine hostility because Velina possesses sufficient strength and awareness to escape if she truly wishes, and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] subsequently recognizes that the girls were merely playing. Hinata becomes embarrassed when her father discovers her involvement, demonstrating that the desire for his approval remains more important to her than preserving the appearance of complete aristocratic dignity.[7]
Their paths cross again during the experiment involving Eldritch-Leviathan hybrids. Velina initially attempts to prevent the sisters from combining dangerous supernatural materials near the family residence, but accepts Stella's reasoning that Victor must already be observing the experiment. She eventually contributes intelligence to the developing organism, joining Hinata and the other girls in the cooperative creation of [[Cthugha]].[3]

#### Cthugha
[[Cthugha]] is a Great Old One created through the combined experimental abilities of Hinata and several other daughters of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. The creature's supernatural foundation includes foreign Eldritch characteristics, Leviathan properties, Draconic materials and multiple elemental or conceptual powers contributed by the participating sisters. Hinata specifically supplies the power of the Sun, demonstrating an active application of her inherited Solar abilities beyond the symbolic importance of her Shinto status.[3]
The completed organism possesses genuine Divinity and manifests as an enormous sphere of plasma covered in eyes. Victor recognizes it as a Great Old One rather than a true Outer God, placing it within the hierarchy of the Great [[Chaos]] beneath the Eldritch Gods. Cthugha subsequently becomes associated with the creation of a new Race of Fire Vampires, while its extraordinary natural strength allows it to threaten a Solar System even at birth.[3]
Although Hinata is one of its co-creators, the creature's completed abilities result from the combined contributions of numerous participants and should not automatically be attributed to her individually. Her confirmed contribution concerns Solar power, while the exact extent to which this influenced Cthugha's stellar and plasma-related abilities has not been separately quantified.

### Power and Status
Hinata possesses extraordinary natural power and elevated Divine status, but the two should be distinguished when assessing her position within the supernatural hierarchy. Her biological existence belongs to the genuine Blood Dragon Race, whose members possess physical and spiritual characteristics vastly beyond conventional supernatural beings. Her maternal inheritance places her within the ruling family of the [[Shinto Pantheon]], while her relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] grants the rank and protection associated with an Imperial Princess of the [[Elderblood Empire]].[1][2]
Hinata is explicitly considered a Goddess of the highest rank within [[Takamagahara]]. Her status commands considerable respect, and ordinary Gods would not casually insult her because of the political consequences and extraordinary supernatural power associated with her family. This does not automatically establish that she occupies the same cosmic position as the primordial creators of the Pantheon or that she possesses complete authority over every Divine domain associated with [[Amaterasu]].[2]
Her personal strength is described as overwhelmingly superior to that of ordinary Takamagahara Gods. Hinata regards many of them as weak and excessively submissive, believing that they could be disposed of with negligible effort. The assessment establishes the enormous difference between her naturally born Draconic foundation and the abilities of conventional Shinto Divine beings, although it does not provide an exact numerical comparison or demonstrate a dedicated battle against the strongest Gods of the Pantheon.[1]
Her inherited Solar abilities provide an additional supernatural specialization beyond the general advantages of True Dragon physiology. Hinata can contribute the power of the Sun to the creation of another Divine existence, demonstrating that her relationship with Amaterasu's principal domain possesses a practical manifestation. However, no individual combat scene establishes the maximum destructive scale of her Solar abilities or proves that she independently governs every star throughout the Universe.[3]
The experiment producing [[Cthugha]] is particularly significant because it demonstrates that Hinata can contribute to the creation of genuine Divine Eldritch Life. Nevertheless, the resulting creature possesses characteristics supplied by numerous exceptionally powerful daughters and Victor's own Draconic material, making it inappropriate to attribute the completed organism's full abilities to Hinata individually. Likewise, the disappearance of the Shinto God who expressed a desire to marry her demonstrates the danger of violating the Imperial Family's boundaries, but the available account does not establish that Hinata personally killed him.[3][10]
Hinata's present classification is therefore best understood as a high-ranking Shinto Dragon Goddess and Imperial Princess possessing confirmed Solar power, exceptional True Dragon physiology and considerable inherited conceptual potential. Her exact position relative to the strongest individual members of the Shinto Pantheon, the completed Primordial authorities and Victor's most advanced biological daughters remains unidentified.

### Trivia
- Hinata is the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Amaterasu]] Elderblood, combining the naturally born Blood Dragon physiology of Victor's second generation with the Divine ancestry of the Shinto ruling family.[1][2]
- Before her name was formally revealed, she was referred to as Amaterasu's daughter during the discussion of Victor's children and their relationships with the older families of their mothers.[1]
- Hinata's public Shinto title is Hinata-Omikami, and she is explicitly recognized as a Goddess of the highest rank within [[Takamagahara]].[2]
- Hinata possesses extremely long black hair, violet Dragon Eyes and a distinctive birthmark beneath one of her eyes. Her appearance combines the characteristics of Victor's Blood Dragon lineage with the traditional presentation of Amaterasu's Divine family.[2]
- She wears an elaborate royal kimono, arranges her hair in the style of a Japanese princess and carries a black-and-purple folding fan decorated with a Dragon symbol.[2]
- Hinata frequently raises her folding fan before the lower half of her face while speaking, a gesture deliberately copied from Amaterasu.[2]
- She regards most of the ordinary Gods of Takamagahara with disdain, considering them weak and excessively submissive compared with the extraordinary supernatural beings surrounding Victor.[1]
- Although Hinata possesses little genuine interest in most members of her maternal family, she unexpectedly gets along well with [[Izanami-no-Mikoto]] despite the earlier conflict between Izanami and Amaterasu.[1]
- Amaterasu permits Hinata to continue visiting Izanami while the latter remains sealed, but monitors their interactions because of the complicated history between the two older Goddesses.[1]
- Hinata's first named appearance involves Stella confronting her and Irene over photographs taken from Anna's private collection, including rare images of Victor during his Human childhood and infancy.[2]
- Hinata attempts to lie to Stella despite knowing that her sister possesses a passive ability capable of recognizing Falsehood, making ordinary deception largely ineffective.[2]
- She initially attempts to blame Irene for involving her in the photograph scheme before arguing that she technically purchased the pictures rather than stealing them.[2]
- Hinata insists that stealing is beneath the dignity of a woman occupying her position, but immediately abandons that principle when confronted about her desire to possess photographs of Victor.[2]
- When Stella accuses her of shamelessness, Hinata declares that she is capable of anything when their father is involved. After Stella points out the contradiction with her earlier insistence upon dignity, Hinata claims that she has already evolved beyond her previous attitude.[2]
- Hinata jokingly compares [[Irene Elderblood]] to Boa Hancock because of her sister's considerable confidence in her own beauty, demonstrating familiarity with Japanese entertainment preserved within the Empire.[2]
- Hinata and Irene eventually obtain photographs from Anna after Yog suggests simply asking for them, making their earlier attempts at theft and concealment unnecessary.[2]
- Hinata participates alongside the Scarlett daughters in ambushing [[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]], placing a sack over her head and carrying her away as part of an elaborate sibling prank.[4]
- Victor affectionately calls Hinata his little Sun when he discovers that she participated in the prank. Hinata becomes embarrassed and turns her flushed face away, avoiding her father's gaze.[7]
- During the family's celestial banquet, Hinata selects a chair created from a yellow star, reflecting her personal association with Solar phenomena.[5]
- Hinata asks Victor whether the original Creator divided the Universe into its present Sectors or whether the division was established by the Primordial Beings who remained active afterward.[6]
- Victor considers it plausible that the contemporary Sector structure was established by the Primordial authorities after the Creator ceased participating directly in Creation, although he does not claim complete knowledge of the original events.[6]
- Hinata possesses confirmed supernatural access to the power of the Sun, which she contributes during the experiment involving Eldritch organisms, Leviathans and Victor's Draconic material.[3]
- Her Solar contribution is incorporated alongside powers supplied by Yog, Nigredo, Valentina, Leone, Silvia, Rosemary, Stella and Velina, eventually producing the Great Old One [[Cthugha]].[3]
- Cthugha possesses genuine Divinity and combines native stellar characteristics with foreign Eldritch properties, but its complete powers belong to the cooperative creation rather than Hinata individually.[3]
- The existence of Hinata's Solar power does not automatically establish an independent completed Divine Concept of the Sun or possession of Amaterasu's additional authorities over Light, Day and Sky.
- A God belonging to the [[Shinto Pantheon]] disappears after expressing the desire to marry Hinata. Amaterasu subsequently confirms that he is dead, while the circumstances serve as a warning against inappropriate attempts to approach the Imperial Princesses.[10]
- The identity of the individual who killed the Shinto God is not explicitly confirmed in the available account, and the event should not be attributed to Hinata as an individual combat feat.[10]
- Hinata possesses inherited Eldritch characteristics through Victor, but no confirmed individual development establishes that she has attained the completed status of an Outer God or mastered every ability associated with the Great [[Chaos]].
- Her full True Dragon Form, exact current subjective age, personal Divine Concept classification, individual martial techniques, specialized Solar attacks and maximum combat capabilities remain unidentified.
