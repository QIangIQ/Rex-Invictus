Priscillia Penny Elderblood is a Blood Dragon Goddess, one of the Imperial Princesses of the [[Elderblood Empire]], and the biological daughter of Victor Elderblood and [[Pepper Scarlett Elderblood]]. As a naturally born descendant of Victor's mature Draconic lineage, she belongs to the second generation of [[True Dragons]] and possesses the exceptional biological and supernatural characteristics inherited by the Emperor's children. Through her mother, Priscillia is also a descendant of Clan Scarlett and a maternal granddaughter of [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], connecting her to one of [[Nightingale]]'s oldest and most powerful Vampire Bloodlines.[1]
Priscillia strongly resembles Pepper in temperament, inheriting her mother's playful disposition, enthusiasm, tendency toward laziness and fascination with entertainment. However, her behavior changes considerably when she assumes responsibility or becomes determined to accomplish something. In these circumstances, she demonstrates the seriousness, cunning and occasionally sadistic attitude associated with Victor. The combination of these characteristics makes her one of the more mischievous members of the Imperial Family, although her behavior is accompanied by considerable intelligence and genuine loyalty toward her parents and siblings.[1]
Despite her relatively carefree demeanor, Priscillia possesses exceptional proficiency in Draconic Runecraft. She demonstrated sufficient technical understanding to coordinate hundreds of Runes and circumvent the protective formations surrounding Pepper's private residence, an accomplishment that impressed Victor even after he recognized that the security system was using comparatively outdated Runic techniques. Her aptitude also extends to the practical manipulation of Reality through Runes, allowing her to perform precise alterations without relying exclusively upon destructive applications of supernatural power.[2][3]
Her interests are not confined to Runecraft. Priscillia is attracted to technological research, classified Imperial projects, fictional entertainment and unusual scientific inventions, particularly when their underlying mechanisms are concealed from her. Her investigation into Pepper's secret laboratory revealed an artificial star, advanced dimensional containment systems and numerous experimental weapons, including functional plasma swords inspired by fictional lightsabers. Although her curiosity frequently creates trouble for the Imperial household, she has also demonstrated the ability to understand complex supernatural systems and pursue technical objectives with considerable determination.[2][3]
Priscillia later participated in the educational discussions organized by Victor, where she questioned the relationship between Runes, [[Magic]], Divine Concepts and the fundamental abilities of different supernatural races. These interactions further established her interest in the underlying principles of supernatural power rather than merely its practical applications.[4]

### Name
Priscillia's name originated from the preferences of both her parents. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] wanted to name his daughter Priscilla, while Pepper preferred Penny. Since each liked the other's suggestion, they decided to combine both names rather than choose one over the other, resulting in Priscilla Penny Elderblood.[1]
The original English novel uses Priscilla, while the MTW wiki has historically used Priscillia. The established wiki title is retained, with the original spelling recognized as an alternative name. Penny is not a separate identity or additional character but the second personal name chosen by her mother.

### Physical Appearance
Priscillia is a beautiful young woman possessing the red hair characteristic of her maternal Scarlett lineage. Her appearance visibly connects her with Pepper and the wider Scarlett family, whose descendants share several recognizable characteristics inherited from their original Noble Vampire ancestry. Her complexion is fair, and she belongs to the second generation of [[Blood Dragons]] naturally born from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his wives. Like her siblings, she possesses the extraordinary biological development associated with [[True Dragons]], although her exact height and individual physical measurements have not been specified.[1][5]
As a True Dragon, Priscillia belongs to a race whose appearance is influenced by an unusually advanced supernatural physiology and the capacity to manipulate Energy directly. Her Draconic existence also permits extensive control over her outward presentation, although the precise range of transformations personally demonstrated by Priscillia has not been individually established.
Priscillia generally favors comfortable clothing suitable for her relaxed disposition, but she is equally willing to adopt theatrical outfits when pursuing an activity that interests her. During her infiltration of Pepper's residence, she dressed entirely in black and approached the protected territory as though performing an espionage mission. When Rosemary and Sylvia followed her, she used Draconic Runes to change their clothing into matching black infiltration uniforms, demonstrating her willingness to employ sophisticated Reality-altering techniques for comparatively ordinary purposes.[2][3]
Her visible resemblance to the Scarlett family is also acknowledged during the later family gathering, when Victor affectionately referred to Priscillia and several of her red-haired sisters as his troublesome redheads. Priscillia immediately protested the description, despite having recently participated in another act of mischief involving Velina.[5]

### Personality
Priscillia is playful, affectionate, energetic and exceptionally curious, having inherited much of Pepper's carefree attitude toward everyday life. She enjoys entertainment, games and spending time with her family, and she can appear lazy or unwilling to undertake ordinary responsibilities when something more enjoyable is available. Her enthusiasm changes quickly when an activity captures her interest, however, and she can become remarkably persistent once she decides upon an objective. This combination makes her behavior unpredictable: she may spend an extended period relaxing or reading manga before suddenly devoting her considerable intelligence to solving a problem that other members of the family would consider better left alone.[1][2]
Priscillia is especially affectionate toward [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and enjoys competing with her sisters for his attention. She openly clings to him, demands physical affection and enthusiastically participates in activities that allow her to spend time with her father. Her first appearance demonstrates this attachment when she immediately abandons her laziness upon hearing Victor announce that he wants to play with his daughters. She attaches herself to him and initially refuses to leave his embrace, only agreeing to change clothes when Victor promises they can perform the Fusion Dance afterward. Her smug reaction toward her jealous sisters reveals a mischievous competitiveness that remains part of her personality throughout her later appearances.[1]
Her everyday behavior strongly resembles Pepper's, but Priscillia becomes significantly more serious when placed in a position of authority. The narration explicitly identifies the commanding and somewhat sadistic side of her personality as an inheritance from Victor. She demonstrated this tendency during the Emperor's war games, where she immediately began directing her assigned soldiers and applying the military lessons taught by her mothers. Although initially inexperienced at leading a realistic army, she approached the exercise competitively and attempted to organize her faction according to the requirements of the simulated conflict.[1][6]
Priscillia's most prominent individual characteristic is her curiosity. She becomes fascinated by information that is deliberately concealed from her, particularly when the subject involves advanced technology or research carried out by a member of her family. The absence of details about Pepper's classified project made her determined to investigate, even though Rosemary and Sylvia repeatedly questioned the wisdom of becoming involved in a project whose purpose she did not understand. Rather than accept that certain information was restricted, Priscillia spent a considerable period considering her options before deciding to circumvent the defenses surrounding her mother's private territory.[2]
Her curiosity is supported by considerable intelligence. Priscillia can analyze technical problems, coordinate large numbers of Runes and develop solutions involving sophisticated supernatural systems. She does not always apply these abilities responsibly, but the effectiveness of her work is genuine. Victor recognized that her understanding of Runecraft had already reached a high level and was impressed that she could identify weaknesses in Pepper's older defenses. Her accomplishment also prompted him to consider strengthening the household's security systems because the same vulnerabilities might be exploited by others with comparable abilities.[2]
Priscillia possesses an enthusiastic appreciation for fictional entertainment and frequently interprets actual technology through familiar examples from anime, manga, games and other media. When she discovered Pepper's experimental plasma swords, she immediately treated them as lightsabers and enthusiastically imitated their movements and sound effects. Rosemary repeatedly attempted to explain the actual technical classification of the weapons, but Priscillia considered the distinction unimportant when the inventions so closely resembled objects she recognized from fiction. Her reaction to an advanced concealed blade containing Basilisk venom was similar, as she insisted upon identifying the device according to its familiar fictional counterpart rather than its more elaborate technical description.[3]
Despite her tendency to disregard restrictions, Priscillia is deeply loyal to her siblings. Her relationships with Rosemary and Sylvia demonstrate that the sisters are willing to accompany one another even when they recognize that a proposed activity is likely to end badly. When the two followed Priscillia into Pepper's restricted territory, she initially complained about their interference but quickly accepted their assistance. Her response reflected the broader solidarity of Victor's children, whose individual personalities differ considerably but whose family bonds remain exceptionally strong.[2][3]
She also possesses enough humility to recognize when another person has understood a situation better than she has. During the war games, Priscillia initially assumed that defeating her sisters was the principal objective of Victor's exercise. Akasha corrected the assumption by observing that Victor had promised to evaluate their overall performance rather than reward the winner alone. Priscillia accepted the reasoning once she realized that Akasha was correct, demonstrating that her competitiveness does not prevent her from learning from others.[7]
Priscillia's laziness is also recognized by Victor, who considers it a waste of the extraordinary intelligence and potential inherited by his daughters. Rather than simply punish her, he encourages Priscillia and the similarly lazy Nigredo to make greater use of their abilities and knowledge. His personal disappointment is something Priscillia takes seriously, and the prospect of failing to meet his expectations motivates her much more effectively than ordinary criticism or compulsory study.[8]
Although Priscillia frequently causes trouble, her actions generally arise from curiosity, enthusiasm and the desire to enjoy experiences with her family rather than malicious intentions. She is capable of accepting consequences when her behavior crosses established boundaries, particularly when the punishment comes from her parents. This is demonstrated when Pepper assigns her and her sisters six months of additional lessons after their unauthorized exploration of her private residence. Priscillia accepts the punishment enthusiastically because it is considerably less severe than the year of corrective training they feared receiving from [[Scathach]].[3]

### Heritage
Priscillia is the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]], the Blood Dragon Primogenitor, and [[Pepper Scarlett Elderblood]], one of the Scarlett sisters and a member of the Imperial Family. Her paternal lineage places her among the naturally born second-generation [[True Dragons]], while her maternal ancestry connects her to the original Vampire Bloodline of Clan Scarlett.
Pepper was originally a Noble Vampire descended from [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett|Scathach Scarlett]] before becoming a Blood Dragon Goddess through Victor's influence. Priscillia was born after the establishment of the mature Draconic lineage and consequently inherited the fundamental characteristics of a True Dragon rather than simply continuing the old Scarlett Noble Vampire bloodline.
The unusual compatibility of Victor's Draconic Bloodline allows his naturally born daughters to preserve important characteristics inherited from their mothers without suffering the biological incompatibilities traditionally associated with ordinary supernatural hybrids. His highly developed Soul authority and Divine Concept of Beginning contribute to this stability, allowing his descendants to maintain their True Dragon nature alongside additional inherited supernatural traits.[8]
Priscillia's connection with Clan Scarlett is therefore genealogical and cultural as well as supernatural. She is a descendant of one of [[Nightingale]]'s oldest Vampire families but belongs fundamentally to the Elderblood Blood Dragon lineage.

#### Dragon Lineage
Priscillia possesses the naturally developed physiology of a True Dragon, including the exceptional physical foundation, Energy compatibility and Draconic characteristics associated with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s second generation. The descendants of Victor's mature Bloodline possess extraordinary potential and are capable of using powers that ordinary supernatural races require substantial training or specialized methods to approach.
Her Draconic existence also provides the biological foundation necessary for her advanced Runecraft. Although understanding and technical precision remain essential, a True Dragon possesses exceptional compatibility with the Energy and language through which Draconic Runes interact with Reality.

#### Scarlett Lineage
Through Pepper, Priscillia belongs to the extended Scarlett family and is a granddaughter of [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]]. Her appearance and aspects of her personality reflect this heritage, particularly her red hair and the mischievous disposition shared with several of her maternal relatives.
Her membership in the Scarlett lineage does not automatically establish that she personally possesses every unique ability demonstrated by the original Scarlett [[Vampire Counts]]. Such powers must be distinguished from the general Draconic characteristics she inherited from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Priscillia first appears during a relatively peaceful period within the Imperial Family, shortly after the creation of [[Akasha Elderblood]]. Akasha's arrival had produced mixed feelings among [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters because she had been created directly through Victor's power and possessed an unusually close connection with him. Several daughters were concerned that Akasha might enjoy advantages in competing for their father's attention, while the more mischievous members of the family recognized that her dedication to protecting Victor could make their ordinary activities considerably more difficult.[1]
Victor subsequently decided to spend time with his children and appeared at the entrance to the portion of the Imperial residence containing their individual rooms. Priscillia was relaxing near the entrance when he announced that he wanted to play with them. She immediately responded and attached herself to him, clinging to her father so closely that several sisters became jealous. Victor affectionately stroked her head and formally introduced her as the daughter of himself and Pepper, with the narration identifying the similarities between her parents' personalities and explaining the origin of her combined name.
When Victor instructed his daughters to change into more durable clothing, Priscillia initially refused because she preferred remaining in his embrace. She excitedly agreed only after he promised they could perform the Fusion Dance together afterward, immediately abandoning her earlier laziness and returning to her room with a smug expression directed toward her sisters. The scene established her playful attachment to Victor and her willingness to use childish theatrics to obtain his attention.[1]
The daughters were subsequently transported to a snowy region of Victor's personal world, where he prepared a military exercise designed to evaluate their judgment, leadership, adaptability and ability to respond to unexpected situations. Although the environment was extraordinarily cold, their Draconic physiology protected them from the conditions. Victor explained that even greater resistance could develop through the passive adaptability inherited by his daughters, although their capabilities remained inferior to his own extraordinary evolutionary potential.[9]
The first major confrontation involved Priscillia and [[Nikolina Nia Nefela Elderblood]]. Rather than permitting the two to settle their competition through their immense personal strength, Victor assigned each of them a military force composed of artificially created soldiers. The daughters were expected to command their troops, organize supplies and apply the lessons they had received from their mothers. Victor's purpose was to force them to consider the responsibilities of leadership instead of treating warfare as a contest decided exclusively by supernatural power.
Priscillia immediately began issuing orders to her soldiers, while Nikolina organized the opposing force. Their initial preparations demonstrated their education but also exposed their inexperience in practical warfare. Both focused upon organizing their armies and supplies without adequately accounting for the movement of Time or the possibility that an unexpected third party might intervene. Victor had deliberately allowed their activities to develop under realistic conditions so the weaknesses in their assumptions would become evident through experience.[6]
The situation changed when [[Siren Elderblood]] entered the exercise as a third commander. Unlike the two original participants, Siren immediately recognized the importance of striking an opponent's logistical infrastructure and moved against the supply routes supporting both armies. Her attacks disrupted Priscillia's preparations before she could fully respond, demonstrating that a smaller and comparatively weaker force could produce decisive results through speed, initiative and superior operational judgment. Victor used the confrontation to demonstrate the importance of anticipating outside intervention, managing Time effectively and understanding that the strongest army does not always possess the best commander.[6]
The exercise subsequently expanded into a much larger simulation involving multiple factions, changing political circumstances and supernatural restrictions imposed upon the participants. Victor utilized the Tower of Nightmare and his authority over Dreams to create an increasingly realistic environment in which his daughters could experience the consequences of their decisions without suffering permanent harm. The resulting simulation introduced situations involving diplomacy, internal rebellion, resource management and the emergence of new political powers.
Priscillia continued participating through her own faction, whose activities eventually contributed to internal conflict within territory controlled by [[Stella Elderblood]]. The expanding simulation demonstrated how actions taken by one daughter could create problems for others, requiring the participants to respond to developments outside their original objectives.[7]
Victor later reorganized several participants and placed Akasha alongside Priscillia and other daughters connected with the Scarlett lineage. Priscillia welcomed Akasha and proposed that they cooperate to defeat their sisters, but Akasha recognized an important detail that the others had overlooked. Victor had never stated that victory alone would determine the outcome of the exercise; he intended to judge the overall quality of their performance. Priscillia and her sisters immediately recognized the accuracy of Akasha's observation, revealing that even their enthusiasm and competitive instincts could distract them from their father's actual instructions.[7]
The war games provided Priscillia with practical experience in military command and strategic decision-making, particularly the importance of evaluating situations beyond their immediate appearance. They also contributed to Victor's broader goal of preparing his children for the responsibilities associated with the [[Elderblood Empire]] without allowing them to rely solely upon the overwhelming power they had inherited at birth.

#### Those Who Rule Reality
During a later period of Imperial development, Priscillia became increasingly interested in the technological activities undertaken by her mother. While spending time in the manga section of the Imperial castle's library with [[Rosemary Lavina Elderblood]] and [[Sylvia Elderblood]], she discovered that Pepper was participating in a project whose details were not available through the ordinary Imperial databases. Although the Royal Family possessed access to extraordinarily sensitive information, certain projects remained restricted even among [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wives and daughters. These included major strategic developments such as Polaris, whose usefulness depended partly upon the secrecy surrounding its capabilities.[2]
Priscillia immediately announced that she wanted to participate in Pepper's project, despite knowing almost nothing about its purpose. Rosemary and Sylvia questioned her reasoning and pointed out that its absence from the ordinary information systems almost certainly meant that the research was deliberately restricted. Priscillia nevertheless insisted that her mother's participation should qualify her for involvement as well. After remaining unusually quiet for an extended period, she developed a plan and abruptly left the library, alarming her sisters, who recognized that her prolonged silence usually preceded something troublesome.
Rosemary and Sylvia eventually followed her because they were unwilling to abandon their sister even when they suspected that her plan would lead to punishment. Meanwhile, Victor detected Priscillia's approach toward the protected portion of the residence belonging to his wives. He chose not to intervene, observing her actions through his supernatural senses while recognizing the loyalty demonstrated by Rosemary and Sylvia.[2]
Priscillia approached Pepper's private residence wearing a black infiltration outfit and began manipulating the protective system surrounding it. She rapidly manifested hundreds of Draconic Runes, using their combined effects to analyze and circumvent the existing defensive formations. Victor recognized that Pepper's protections relied upon older methods, but he was nevertheless impressed by the technical sophistication demonstrated by his daughter. Her ability to penetrate the system through Runecraft rather than simply overwhelming it with raw Energy convinced him that the household's internal defenses required further improvement.
The security surrounding the mothers' private territories existed for reasons extending beyond ordinary protection against intruders. Victor's children possessed supernatural senses, spatial perception and Draconic abilities powerful enough to interfere with their parents' privacy if left unrestricted. As a result, entering the personal areas belonging to the Empresses and other wives without permission was forbidden, and the daughters understood that serious violations could result in corrective training under [[Scathach]].[2]
Priscillia successfully circumvented the initial defenses and entered Pepper's private residence. Rosemary and Sylvia arrived shortly afterward, nearly startling her into revealing their presence. Although she initially complained that she had never asked them to follow her, their explanation that they would not abandon their sister caused her to accept their assistance. She then used her Runic abilities to distort Reality around their clothing, creating matching black infiltration uniforms for the two girls so that all three could continue the investigation together.[3]
Inside Pepper's residence, the sisters discovered that the apparent private room was actually a spatially expanded villa possessing approximately 2.8 million square metres of interior space. The environment contained a forest, waterways, a waterfall and other natural features, making the residence comparable to an enclosed estate rather than an ordinary room. Priscillia continued searching until the group located a more heavily protected dimensional layer beneath the residence.
The sisters entered the concealed laboratory, inadvertently triggering a security notification that alerted Pepper. She attempted to return immediately, but Victor intercepted her teleportation and redirected her toward himself. Although Pepper demanded that he stop their daughters, Victor refused because he wanted to observe what Priscillia would discover and how she would react. Pepper accused him of spoiling their daughter far too much, particularly because he was deliberately permitting her to continue violating the household's restrictions.[3]
Within the laboratory, Priscillia and her sisters discovered an enormous white sphere suspended among scientific equipment and extensive Runic formations. The object was an artificial star created through Pepper's experimental research. Its development involved hundreds of Runes and assistance from a Sun Goddess, with the existing project having reached a stage at which the stellar object had been successfully stabilized.
The research documentation revealed that Pepper intended to continue increasing the artificial star's Energy until the completed experiment reached a projected output comparable to a billion exploding suns. This was the intended objective of the project rather than an already demonstrated final output. The laboratory's dimensional containment systems were designed to prevent a catastrophic failure from releasing that Energy into the surrounding environment, using multiple isolated layers to protect the outside world.[3]
The discovery considerably altered the sisters' understanding of Pepper. Although she was widely associated with entertainment and cultural development, her private research revealed extensive knowledge of advanced Runecraft, astrophysical manipulation and sophisticated containment technology. The realization also caused them to consider the extraordinary projects being undertaken by Ruby, Aline and Velnorah, whose contributions to Imperial technology were already well established.
Priscillia nevertheless remained determined to continue exploring Pepper's private territory. The sisters eventually discovered another hidden dimensional chamber identified as the Room for Fun Projects. Unlike the artificial-star laboratory, this chamber contained an enormous collection of experimental devices, weapons, armor, unusual materials and technologies inspired by fictional entertainment. Many of the objects were fully functional despite their resemblance to equipment traditionally associated with anime, films and games.
Priscillia immediately became fascinated by a collection of plasma swords that resembled lightsabers. Rosemary identified the weapons more precisely as plasma-based blades connected to Pepper's stellar-energy research, but Priscillia considered their resemblance to fictional lightsabers sufficient reason to identify them as such. She activated a purple-red weapon, adopted a dramatic fighting stance and began imitating the movements and sound effects associated with fictional sword combat. Her enthusiasm was especially notable because the weapon itself did not produce the sounds she was making.[3]
Her attention subsequently turned toward an advanced wrist-mounted weapon containing Basilisk venom and a mechanism associated with petrification. Rosemary explained its technical characteristics, but Priscillia again preferred the familiar description of a hidden blade. The exchange reflected her tendency to interpret advanced Imperial technology through fictional concepts, particularly when the similarities made the inventions more entertaining.
The sisters also discovered an extensive collection of historical photographs and recordings documenting Victor's earlier supernatural life. The archive contained material from his time in [[Nightingale]], including events involving the Scarlett family and his early public appearances. Priscillia and her sisters were delighted by the opportunity to observe a version of their father very different from the seemingly invincible God Emperor they had always known.[3]
Their exploration ended when [[Anna Elderblood]] discovered them inside the restricted collection. The three immediately recognized that they had violated the household's rules and feared they might be given to Scathach for an entire year of corrective training. Priscillia nevertheless maintained that she did not regret the experience, arguing that Pepper had been unfair to conceal so many interesting inventions.
Pepper subsequently intervened and imposed a substantially lighter punishment. Since the girls had entered her private residence without permission but had not destroyed her collection, she assigned them approximately six months of additional lessons and study. Priscillia, Rosemary and Sylvia accepted immediately, considering the punishment far preferable to Scathach's intensive training. Pepper also reminded them that they could have simply asked her about the collection rather than entering without permission.[3]
Priscillia's mischief continued during the preparations for the traditional Elderblood family dinner. Together with several of her sisters, she participated in an ambush against [[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]], who had been returning from her research activities. The girls placed a sack over Velina and carried her away to Sylvia's room, treating the incident as a family prank rather than a genuine attempt to harm her.[10]
The incident became apparent when Victor gathered the family and noticed that his intelligent daughter Velina and several of the troublesome Scarlett-line girls had not yet arrived. Velina eventually appeared in a disheveled state and angrily confronted her sisters, while Priscillia protested when Victor affectionately referred to the red-haired girls as troublemakers. Erika, Priscillia, Rosemary, Silvia, Siren and [[Lucrecia Lena Elderblood]] all reacted guiltily when their father questioned the latest disturbance.
Victor immediately recognized that Velina had possessed enough strength to escape from the prank whenever she wished. Rather than regard the incident as a serious attack, he understood that she had allowed herself to participate despite her complaints. He reminded the daughters that they were permitted to play and compete with one another as long as they did not genuinely harm their sisters or allow rivalry to become hatred.[5]
Priscillia subsequently participated in the educational discussions Victor held for his children. During one explanation of Divine authority and the limitations of higher supernatural beings, she questioned whether the descriptions promoted through certain books associated with the family were accurate. Victor used the opportunity to distinguish the powers of exceptionally developed Gods from the limitations of more ordinary supernatural beings, explaining that access to a Concept does not necessarily grant every possible application associated with it.[8]
The discussion also exposed gaps in the daughters' understanding of their own racial characteristics. Victor explained that the extraordinary compatibility of the True Dragon Race allowed his children to inherit beneficial characteristics from their mothers without losing their fundamental Draconic nature. When several daughters revealed that they had neglected important portions of their education, Victor expressed concern that their intelligence and potential were being wasted through laziness.
His response was particularly directed toward Priscillia and Nigredo, both of whom had demonstrated a tendency to avoid unnecessary study despite possessing extraordinary intellectual abilities. Victor affectionately stroked their heads and encouraged them not to waste their potential, explaining that their intelligence was an inheritance they should use rather than neglect. His encouragement immediately motivated them to promise greater dedication to their education.[8]
Priscillia later attended Victor's lessons concerning the fundamental structure of Energy and the different systems through which supernatural beings manipulate Reality. After he explained the relationship between Attributeless Energy, [[Divine Energy]], Demonic Energy and other supernatural manifestations, the discussion turned toward the nature of Runes.
Priscillia questioned whether every race, including Humans, could use Runes. Victor explained that the underlying principles were not restricted exclusively to Gods or Dragons, although the degree of authority available through a Runic system depended heavily upon the nature of the language and the being employing it. Ordinary magical enchantment could reproduce some comparatively simple Runic functions, while Draconic Runes possessed substantially greater potential because of the Dragon Race's exceptional compatibility with Reality.[4]
She then asked what a hypothetical God possessing the Divine Concept of Runes could accomplish. Victor clarified that Runic effectiveness depended more upon the user's understanding of the Universe and the supernatural language being employed than upon possession of a narrowly corresponding Divine Concept. Even a God whose Divinity concerned language would not automatically gain the unrestricted ability to reproduce the effects available through advanced Draconic Runes.
The exchange demonstrated Priscillia's continued interest in the theoretical foundations of Runecraft. Her questions were directly relevant to the specialty she had already demonstrated during the infiltration of Pepper's residence, but they also revealed her desire to understand the distinction between Runic proficiency, racial compatibility and Divine authority.[4]
Priscillia continued attending the lessons alongside her sisters when the discussion expanded to the extraordinary powers of the youngest members of the Imperial Family. During the explanation of [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]] and her unusual relationship with Luck, Priscillia compared her youngest sister to the exceptionally fortunate protagonists found in cultivation novels, reflecting her familiarity with fictional media and her tendency to use such comparisons when interpreting unfamiliar supernatural phenomena.[11]
These later appearances establish that Priscillia's abilities and interests continue developing beyond her original role as a mischievous daughter. Although she remains playful and easily distracted by entertainment, she has demonstrated advanced technical knowledge, curiosity concerning the foundations of Reality and a willingness to study subjects that directly contribute to her development as a Dragon Goddess.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Priscillia's biological father and one of the most important influences upon her personality and supernatural development. She is exceptionally affectionate toward him, openly competing with her siblings for his attention and enthusiastically accepting opportunities to spend time together. Her first appearance demonstrates this attachment when she clings to Victor and initially refuses to leave his embrace, only agreeing to follow his instructions after he promises to perform the Fusion Dance with her.[1]
Priscillia's relationship with Victor also reflects the qualities she inherited from him. Although her relaxed demeanor resembles Pepper, her commanding attitude, stubborn determination and willingness to overcome obstacles display characteristics associated with her father. Victor recognizes these similarities and appears particularly entertained by her resourcefulness, even when she uses her abilities to violate the household's rules.
His reaction to her infiltration of Pepper's residence provides an especially clear example. Victor detects the intrusion almost immediately but chooses to observe rather than interfere, becoming impressed when Priscillia bypasses the protective formations through sophisticated Draconic Runes. He also appreciates the loyalty displayed by Rosemary and Sylvia, whose decision to accompany their sister reflects the strong family bonds he encourages among his children.[2]
Victor's affection does not mean that he considers Priscillia's education unimportant. He recognizes her exceptional intelligence and later encourages her to take her studies more seriously. His disappointment at the possibility of her wasting her potential motivates her far more effectively than ordinary disciplinary measures. He ultimately wants his daughter to retain her playful personality while developing the knowledge and control necessary to utilize her extraordinary birthright responsibly.[8]

#### Pepper Scarlett Elderblood
[[Pepper Scarlett Elderblood]] is Priscillia's biological mother and the parent she most closely resembles in everyday behavior. Their similarities include their enthusiasm for entertainment, playful disposition, occasional laziness and tendency to become intensely interested in unusual inventions or activities. Pepper also chose the second half of her daughter's name, preferring Penny while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] preferred Priscilla.[1]
The resemblance between them extends into their interests. Although Pepper is publicly associated with entertainment and cultural development, she possesses considerable technical intelligence and maintains private research projects involving advanced Runecraft, dimensional engineering and artificial stellar systems. Priscillia's determination to investigate these projects reveals a similar curiosity and willingness to pursue subjects that capture her imagination.
Their relationship is also characterized by Pepper's comparatively lenient approach to discipline. When Priscillia and her sisters trespass within her restricted territory, Pepper initially becomes angry and demands that Victor intervene. However, after discovering that the girls have not damaged her collection, she assigns them six months of additional study rather than allowing them to receive the far harsher punishment they feared from [[Scathach]].[3]
Pepper's reaction demonstrates that she recognizes the distinction between genuine malicious behavior and the curiosity that led her daughter to explore the laboratory. Nevertheless, she also expects Priscillia to respect the family's boundaries and reminds her that permission should have been requested before entering the private residence.

#### Scathach Elderblood Scarlett
[[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] is Priscillia's maternal grandmother and one of the most influential martial instructors within the Elderblood family. As the mother of Pepper, [[Scathach]] represents an important part of Priscillia's Scarlett ancestry and the combat traditions inherited through that lineage.
Priscillia is well aware of Scathach's reputation for rigorous training and discipline. The possibility of spending an entire year under her corrective instruction is considered sufficiently severe that Priscillia and her sisters regard Pepper's six-month educational punishment as an exceptionally merciful alternative.[2][3]
Although Priscillia possesses notable technical abilities, the available material does not establish that she has surpassed Scathach in Runecraft or martial proficiency.

#### Rosemary Lavina Elderblood
[[Rosemary Lavina Elderblood]] is one of Priscillia's closest companions and frequently participates in activities with her. Rosemary possesses a relatively relaxed attitude toward ordinary life and enjoys entertainment, although she is considerably more cautious when Priscillia proposes an activity likely to violate the household's rules.
During the investigation of Pepper's classified project, Rosemary repeatedly questions Priscillia's reasoning and initially refuses to participate in her plan. However, she ultimately follows because she is unwilling to allow her sister to enter a potentially dangerous restricted area alone.[2]
Their partnership demonstrates the strength of the bonds between [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters. Rosemary does not necessarily agree with Priscillia's decisions, but she remains willing to accompany and protect her when necessary. The two subsequently explore Pepper's laboratory together and share responsibility for the resulting punishment.

#### Sylvia Elderblood
[[Sylvia Elderblood]] is another of Priscillia's principal companions and participates alongside Rosemary during the infiltration of Pepper's private residence.
Sylvia initially considers the plan unreasonable and recognizes that Priscillia's prolonged silence is a sign that she is developing a troublesome idea. Nevertheless, she follows her sister because she does not want Priscillia to face the consequences of the plan alone.[2]
During the laboratory exploration, Sylvia takes an active role in examining the technical records and identifying the purpose of Pepper's experiments. Her contributions complement Priscillia's ability to bypass the defenses and Rosemary's willingness to analyze the unusual discoveries.
The three share a close relationship despite their different attitudes toward risk and responsibility, and they accept Pepper's punishment together.[3]

#### Akasha Elderblood
[[Akasha Elderblood]] is one of Priscillia's sisters and an exceptional creation of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] whose existence is closely connected to the protection and administration of the Imperial Family.
Priscillia initially regards Akasha as another competitor for Victor's attention, reflecting the jealousy shared by several daughters when the newly created Akasha joins the family. However, she subsequently welcomes Akasha when Victor places them together during his war games.
Priscillia proposes that they cooperate to defeat their sisters, but Akasha immediately points out that their father has promised to evaluate performance rather than simply reward victory. Priscillia accepts the observation once she recognizes its accuracy.[7]
Their interaction illustrates the complementary nature of their personalities. Priscillia is energetic, competitive and inclined toward spontaneous action, while Akasha is methodical and highly attentive to the precise meaning of Victor's instructions.

#### Nikolina Nia Nefela Elderblood
[[Nikolina Nia Nefela Elderblood]] is Priscillia's initial opponent during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s war-game exercise. The two are assigned separate military forces and expected to command their soldiers rather than resolve their confrontation through personal strength.[9]
Both immediately begin organizing their armies and applying the lessons provided by their mothers. However, they initially fail to respond adequately to the passage of Time and the possibility that another faction might exploit their preparations, allowing Siren's intervention to disrupt both forces.
Their competition provides an important example of Priscillia's developing military education and the distinction between possessing extraordinary intelligence and applying that intelligence effectively under unfamiliar conditions.[6]

#### Siren Elderblood
[[Siren Elderblood]] participated in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s war games as a third commander whose intervention exposed weaknesses in the original conflict between Priscillia and Nikolina.
Siren adopted a more aggressive strategy, immediately attacking the logistical infrastructure supporting the two larger forces. Her initiative allowed her smaller faction to create serious problems for Priscillia and Nikolina before either could fully adjust to the changing situation.[6]
The exercise provided Priscillia with practical exposure to an opponent whose methods differed considerably from her own approach to military preparation.
Siren later participated alongside Priscillia and several other daughters in the prank involving Velina, demonstrating that their earlier competition did not prevent them from cooperating in ordinary family activities.[10][5]

#### Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III
[[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]] is one of Priscillia's highly intelligent sisters and an important contributor to Imperial scientific research.
Priscillia recognizes Velina's extraordinary technical competence, particularly her involvement in projects overseen by Velnorah, Ruby and Aline. Velina's access to advanced research represents something Priscillia finds especially interesting because she also wishes to participate in significant technological work.[2]
Despite recognizing Velina's achievements, Priscillia does not hesitate to participate in mischief directed toward her. She helps organize the prank in which Velina is captured and carried away shortly before the traditional family dinner.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] subsequently recognizes that Velina could have escaped whenever she wished and that the incident amounted to a voluntary participation in the sisters' ordinary games rather than genuine hostility. Their relationship therefore combines sibling rivalry, admiration and playful competition.[10][5]

#### Nigredo Elderblood
[[Nigredo Elderblood]] is one of Priscillia's younger sisters and shares her tendency toward laziness despite possessing extraordinary supernatural potential.
Both attract [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s attention during his lessons when several daughters demonstrate incomplete understanding of important aspects of their race and Divine development. Victor specifically encourages Priscillia and Nigredo to make better use of their inherited intelligence, reminding them that their potential should not be wasted through lack of effort.[8]
Their shared reaction reflects the importance of Victor's approval within the Imperial Family and the effectiveness of his personal encouragement.

#### Moira Eutychia Elderblood
[[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]] is one of Priscillia's youngest sisters and possesses exceptional powers associated with Eldritch Luck.
Priscillia encounters the explanation of Moira's abilities during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s educational discussions and compares her extraordinary fortune to that of favored protagonists from cultivation novels. The comparison reflects Priscillia's familiarity with fictional entertainment and her ability to recognize similarities between supernatural phenomena and familiar storytelling conventions.[11]

### Skills & Abilities
Priscillia possesses the fundamental supernatural characteristics of a naturally born Blood Dragon Goddess, including the physical and energetic foundation associated with [[True Dragons]]. However, her most clearly demonstrated individual specialty is Draconic Runecraft, and her documented feats should be distinguished from the full range of abilities potentially available through her racial inheritance.

#### Racial Abilities
- Blood Dragon Goddess Physiology: Priscillia is a naturally born daughter of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Pepper, possessing the Draconic physiology characteristic of the second generation of [[Blood Dragons]]. Her existence combines her father's mature True Dragon Bloodline with inherited maternal characteristics from the Scarlett lineage.[1][8]
- True Dragon Physiology: Her biological foundation belongs to the True Dragon Race, providing exceptional physical characteristics, Energy compatibility and supernatural development.
- Divine Physiology: Priscillia belongs to Victor's generation of naturally born Dragon Goddesses and possesses the corresponding Divine racial characteristics.
- Supernatural Longevity: Her lifespan and biological development operate according to the extraordinary standards associated with [[True Dragons]] and Gods. Her exact chronological age is not specified, although the later narrative describes her as older than multiple Human generations because of the accelerated periods experienced by the family.[2]
- [[Draconic Energy]] Compatibility: Her True Dragon nature grants substantial compatibility with supernatural Energy and provides the foundation for her use of advanced Draconic Runes.[4]
- Inherited Maternal Characteristics: Priscillia inherits characteristics associated with Pepper and the Scarlett lineage while retaining the fundamental biological structure of a True Dragon.[8]
- Passive Adaptation: Priscillia belongs to the generation of Victor's daughters who inherited a reduced version of the passive evolutionary adaptability associated with their father. Their bodies can gradually adapt to environmental conditions that would otherwise cause persistent harm or discomfort, although this characteristic is considerably less developed than Victor's own extraordinary adaptation.[9]
- Environmental Resistance: Her Draconic body possesses natural resistance to severe environmental conditions, including extreme cold.
- Progressive Adaptation: Persistent exposure to conditions capable of affecting her may result in increased resistance through her inherited adaptive characteristics.
- Limited Relative Adaptation: The ability is inferior to Victor's own evolutionary powers and should not be treated as an independently demonstrated capacity to adapt to every possible Conceptual attack.[9]
- Draconic Senses: Priscillia possesses the advanced senses characteristic of Victor's Draconic children, whose perception is sufficiently developed that the Imperial household requires specialized protective formations to maintain privacy within restricted territories.[2]
- Enhanced Perception: Her natural senses exceed the ordinary capabilities of Mortal supernatural races.
- Energy Sensitivity: Her Draconic physiology provides compatibility with the perception and manipulation of supernatural Energy.
- Individual Limits Unrevealed: The maximum range and complete characteristics of Priscillia's personal senses have not been established.

#### Soul Protection and Inherited Safeguards
- Soul Protection: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] maintains powerful safeguards around the Souls of his daughters, protecting them against hostile supernatural interference. These defenses arise through the relationship between the Imperial children and their father rather than through an independently demonstrated personal Divinity belonging to Priscillia.[9]
- Soul Invasion Resistance: The protective system is intended to resist attempts by foreign beings to enter or compromise the daughters' Souls.
- Mental Protection: Victor's safeguards provide protection against hostile interference with their minds.
- Protection Against Parasitic Intrusion: The daughters possess protections intended to prevent invasive entities from freely compromising their bodies.
- Protective Connection to Victor: Their relationship with Victor allows him to recognize serious threats directed toward their protected existences.
- Soul Anchor and Restoration: Victor maintains a connection with the Souls of his daughters, including the preservation of portions of their spiritual existence within himself. This arrangement provides an extraordinary safeguard against death and the loss of their existence.[9]
- Post-Mortem Restoration: The preserved Soul connection can provide a means of restoring a daughter after fatal injury under circumstances compatible with Victor's powers.
- Family-Based Soul Safeguard: The system belongs to the protections established by Victor for the Imperial Family rather than a unique power invented by Priscillia.
- Unestablished Absolute Limits: The available explanation does not demonstrate that the safeguard necessarily overcomes every possible form of complete Soul or Conceptual destruction.

#### Draconic Runecraft
- Advanced Draconic Runecraft: Priscillia possesses substantial proficiency in the manipulation of Draconic Runes, allowing her to construct sophisticated supernatural effects and interfere with existing Runic formations. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] personally recognized her understanding as exceptionally advanced during her infiltration of Pepper's private residence.[2]
- Large-Scale Rune Manifestation: She rapidly produced hundreds of Draconic Runes while analyzing and circumventing Pepper's security system.[2]
- Multi-Rune Coordination: Priscillia can operate numerous Runic symbols simultaneously as part of a complex technical procedure.
- Runic Formation Analysis: She can examine existing Runic protections and identify weaknesses that can be exploited without relying exclusively upon destructive force.[2]
- Barrier Circumvention: Her Runic techniques enabled her to bypass the defensive formations surrounding a protected private residence belonging to one of Victor's wives.[2]
- Runic Infiltration: She can employ sophisticated Runic effects to gain entry into restricted locations through their supernatural security systems.
- Advanced Rune Comprehension: Victor recognized that she had developed a high level of Runic understanding despite her relatively young age by Draconic standards.[2]
- Runic Reality Manipulation: Her mastery allows her to produce localized changes in Reality through properly constructed Runic effects.[3]
- Precise Runic Alteration: She demonstrated the ability to alter physical objects through Runes without generating large-scale destruction.
- Clothing Alteration: She transformed Rosemary's and Sylvia's clothing into matching infiltration outfits through localized Runic Reality distortion.[3]
- Runic Energy Control: The simultaneous operation of hundreds of Runes demonstrates substantial precision in the management of supernatural Energy.
- Formal Runemaster Rank Unrevealed: No numerical or officially recognized Runemaster grade has been individually assigned to Priscillia.

#### Technical and Intellectual Abilities
- Exceptional Intelligence: Priscillia possesses the highly developed Draconic intelligence inherited by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters, granting her substantial aptitude for complex technical information and supernatural systems.[2][8]
- Advanced Technical Analysis: She can study and manipulate sophisticated Runic systems through deliberate technical reasoning.
- Rapid Information Processing: Her Draconic brain permits the processing of complex information at speeds far exceeding ordinary Human capabilities.[2]
- Security-System Analysis: Priscillia identified methods of circumventing protected Runic formations despite their integration into the Imperial residence.

- Technical Interpretation: Her exploration of Pepper's laboratories demonstrates familiarity with advanced supernatural technology, although her personal contributions to formal Imperial research remain unestablished.

- Research Motivation: She is particularly interested in classified or unusual projects, although she has not been assigned a confirmed permanent position within the Empire's principal scientific institutions.
- Conceptual Understanding: Priscillia has received education concerning Divine Concepts, the structure of Reality and the differences between supernatural systems.[8][4]
- Understanding of Runic Systems: She recognizes that Runes possess applications extending beyond ordinary elemental [[Magic]].
- Theoretical Inquiry: She questions whether all supernatural races can utilize Runes and whether a Divine Concept associated with Runes would improve their effectiveness.[4]
- Developing Conceptual Knowledge: Her questions demonstrate an active interest in the relationship between supernatural language, racial compatibility and Divine authority.
- Individual Limits Unrevealed: The full extent of Priscillia's understanding of Conceptual Energy and the deeper structure of Reality has not been demonstrated.

#### Combat and Strategic Abilities
- Military Command: Priscillia has received education in military organization and battlefield leadership, including the command of soldiers and the preparation of forces for conflict.[9][6]
- Troop Command: She demonstrated the ability to organize and direct an assigned military force during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s war games.
- Logistical Preparation: Priscillia attempted to organize resources and preparations necessary for her simulated army.
- Application of Military Education: She applied lessons provided by her mothers when directing her forces.[6]
- Factional Strategy: Her faction later participated in a larger political and military simulation, contributing to internal conflict within territory associated with [[Stella Elderblood]].[7]
- Developing Battlefield Judgment: The war games exposed weaknesses in her understanding of Time management, reconnaissance and the intervention of third parties.
- Strategic Adaptability: Priscillia can recognize mistakes and adjust her interpretation of objectives when presented with more accurate reasoning, as demonstrated by Akasha's correction during the war games.[7]

- Runic Security Bypass: Her techniques allowed her to circumvent defensive formations without relying entirely upon overwhelming destructive force.
- Coordinated Infiltration: She directed Rosemary and Sylvia to assist with the search after accepting their participation.[3]
- Restricted-Area Exploration: Priscillia demonstrated the ability to navigate spatially expanded facilities and identify concealed research areas, although she did not independently defeat every layer of the laboratory's security.

#### Spatial and Technological Equipment
- Personal Dimensional Inventory: Priscillia possesses access to the spatial storage system distributed among [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters. The technology was developed through the capabilities of Victor and Natalia Elderblood Alioth, providing each daughter with a private pocket-dimensional inventory.[3]
- Dimensional Storage: Objects and equipment can be maintained within a personal spatial inventory.
- Portable Equipment Access: The system allows possessions to be stored and retrieved without conventional physical transportation.

#### Divinity
- Dragon Goddess: Priscillia is a naturally born Dragon Goddess whose existence includes the Divine characteristics associated with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s True Dragon lineage.[1][8]
- Divine Existence: Her racial foundation incorporates the characteristics of a True Dragon and Goddess.

- Runecraft and Divinity: Her demonstrated ability to manipulate Draconic Runes is a genuine personal specialty, but it has not been identified as her unique Divine Concept.
- Unconfirmed Exceptional Divinities: No verified passage establishes that Priscillia personally possesses an unrestricted Divine Concept of Limitless Growth or an independent cosmological authority beyond the native Universe.

### Runic Proficiency
Priscillia's most clearly demonstrated individual specialty is her advanced proficiency with Draconic Runes. Her ability to coordinate hundreds of Runic symbols and circumvent the security of an Imperial Empress's private residence establishes a level of technical understanding substantially beyond ordinary magical practice.
The significance of this accomplishment lies in the method she employed. Rather than simply destroying Pepper's barriers through superior Energy, Priscillia examined their structure and exploited the weaknesses of the existing defensive formations. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] recognized the technical sophistication involved and was impressed by the degree to which she had developed her abilities.[2]
The feat also illustrates the distinction between Draconic Runecraft and conventional [[Magic]]. Although both systems can produce supernatural effects, Draconic Runes employ the language associated with the True Dragon Race to issue commands that can directly alter the structure of Reality. Their effectiveness depends upon the user's understanding, control and compatibility with the underlying principles rather than exclusively upon the quantity of Energy supplied.
Priscillia's later questions during Victor's lessons confirm her interest in this theoretical foundation. She specifically questioned whether Humans could utilize Runes and whether possession of a Divine Concept associated with Runes would provide an advantage. Victor explained that Draconic Runes derive much of their effectiveness from the nature of the language and the exceptional compatibility of [[True Dragons]] with Reality, meaning a narrow Divine authority would not necessarily reproduce their complete potential.[4]
Her demonstrated capabilities currently include the simultaneous manifestation of hundreds of Runes, the analysis and circumvention of sophisticated protective formations, the precise manipulation of physical objects through Runic Reality alteration and a developing understanding of the relationship between Runes and Divine Concepts.
Although these accomplishments establish her as a highly talented practitioner, the available source material does not establish that she possesses a higher formal Runemaster rank than [[Scathach]], Victor or the most experienced dedicated Runemasters within the Empire.

### Imperial Education
Priscillia belongs to a generation deliberately raised to develop the knowledge and judgment necessary to govern an advanced supernatural civilization. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his wives recognize that their children possess extraordinary abilities from birth, but they do not consider inherited strength a substitute for practical knowledge, emotional maturity or personal responsibility.
Their education consequently encompasses military strategy, supernatural control, administrative responsibilities, political judgment, scientific knowledge and the relationships governing the fundamental structure of Reality. The daughters are expected to develop individual specialties while also maintaining enough general understanding to participate responsibly in the civilization their parents created.
Victor's war games demonstrate this philosophy. Priscillia and Nikolina were not permitted to settle their confrontation through overwhelming Draconic power; instead, they were required to command simulated soldiers, organize resources and respond to changing circumstances. The exercise exposed weaknesses in their practical understanding of warfare and illustrated the importance of experience even among beings possessing exceptional intelligence.[9][6]
The daughters' education also includes access to sensitive Imperial information. Victor believes that his children should understand the institutions, technology and policies governing their civilization, particularly because many of them may eventually occupy important positions within the Empire. However, this access does not extend to every strategic project or the private information belonging to their mothers. Priscillia's unauthorized investigation of Pepper's laboratory demonstrates the consequences of confusing educational access with unrestricted permission to enter protected personal territory.[2][3]
Her later participation in Victor's lessons further demonstrates the importance placed upon understanding rather than simply memorizing techniques. Priscillia's questions concerning Runes show that she is beginning to investigate the principles underlying powers she can already utilize. Her father regards this development positively but continues encouraging her to make greater use of her considerable intelligence rather than allowing laziness to prevent her from reaching her potential.[8][4]
