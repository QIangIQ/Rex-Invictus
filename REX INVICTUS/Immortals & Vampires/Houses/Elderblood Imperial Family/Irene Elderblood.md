### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 997
I won't give up. Our Father finally accepted our request, thanks to Stella, and we need to take advantage of this.
—Irene regarding the opportunity to help govern the [[Elderblood Empire]], Chapter 1120
Irene Elderblood is an Imperial Princess of the Elderblood Empire, a naturally born Blood Dragon, a True Dragon Goddess and the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood. She inherited Victor's exceptionally adaptable Draconic lineage together with the Divine heritage of one of the oldest and most powerful Goddesses associated with Love and Beauty. Irene's personal Divinity developed as <Obsessive Love>, a specialized aspect of Love centered upon possessive affection, fixation and emotional obsession.[2]
Irene is also one of the Elderblood daughters whose supernatural attractiveness possesses direct power implications rather than functioning as a purely aesthetic characteristic. Upon her appearance in Chapter 998, the narration explicitly states that she was born with an overwhelming Charm capable of destroying galaxies and immediately emphasizes that the statement was not an exaggeration.[3] This should be distinguished from Irene physically destroying a galaxy through raw strength; the statement concerns the supernatural potency of her Charm, which is reinforced by her Divine Concept, lineage and extraordinary beauty.
Her early appearances emphasize her shameless affection toward Victor and Aphrodite's enthusiasm for encouraging that side of her personality, while later chapters reveal considerably more maturity. Irene voluntarily begins learning Imperial administration, understands why Victor prohibits his daughters from casually using conceptual powers against one another and refuses to abandon difficult responsibilities simply because her inherited strength cannot solve them.[4][1]

### Physical Appearance
Irene inherited most of her appearance from [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood. Chapter 1120 describes her as essentially a sculpted copy of her mother with only a few characteristics inherited from her father.[4] She possesses extremely long pink hair, pale skin and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s characteristic deep-violet Dragon Eyes.
Even while still younger, Irene's appearance is treated as abnormal by supernatural standards. When Leon observes Victor's gathered daughters in Chapter 998, Irene immediately captures his attention despite the enormous number of beautiful Dragon girls present. The narration describes her as effectively "perfect", explaining that she was born from two beings whose beauty had already reached supernatural extremes and possessed an overwhelming Charm capable of destroying galaxies.[3] Leon falls into a stupor merely observing her, causing Anna to reinforce the protection around him and warn him to be extremely careful with his actions around Victor's daughters.[3]
By Chapter 1160 Irene has naturally matured into an adult woman standing approximately 188 centimeters tall. She possesses a curvaceous but delicate figure, extremely long pink hair and violet eyes, and the narration directly states that her supernatural beauty is comparable to both Aphrodite and Victor.[1] Her appearance therefore reflects more than genetic resemblance: she descends from Aphrodite, one of the setting's defining Beauty-associated Goddesses, and Victor, whose own evolved existence possesses an abnormal supernatural Beauty Concept and whose descendants repeatedly inherit exceptional physical perfection.
Irene's eyes can visibly react to her Divine authority. In Chapter 1001, a violet heart appears within them when she begins expressing her Love-related influence around Victor.[2]

### Personality
Irene is affectionate, seductive, mischievous, theatrical, vain and intensely possessive of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. She strongly reflects [[Aphrodite]]'s playful characteristics, although her fixation upon her father also embodies the obsessive emotional tendencies common throughout Victor's bloodline. Aphrodite actively encourages these traits rather than suppressing them, openly claiming that failing to properly teach seduction to a daughter who is herself a Goddess of Love would be disgraceful.[2]
Her obsession with Victor is considerable even compared with the already extreme affection displayed by many Elderblood daughters. During Chapter 1001 Irene casually removes [[Yuno Elderblood]] from Victor's lap and occupies the position herself, behaving provocatively while ignoring Yuno's jealousy. Anna notices immediately and orders her to behave; Irene obeys despite complaining internally, showing that her shameless personality does not remove her respect for the senior maternal figures responsible for disciplining the second generation.[2]
Irene's confidence extends to her appearance. In Chapter 1160 she refers to herself as a "perfect Goddess" and jokes that even if she occasionally does something wrong, her beauty will make everyone forgive her. [[Hinata Elderblood]] responds by mocking her with a comparison to Boa Hancock and reminding Irene that almost every one of their sisters inherited monstrous beauty from Victor.[1] Irene becomes visibly irritated by the comparison, demonstrating that her vanity is genuine even if it is generally portrayed humorously.
She is nevertheless capable of recognizing her own limitations. When Irene and several younger daughters finally receive permission to study the actual administration of the [[Elderblood Empire]], they discover that their previous Dream World education has not prepared them to manage a civilization whose planning occurs on Galactic scales. Irene acknowledges that their parents had good reason to repeatedly ask whether they genuinely wanted the responsibility, but when Valentina questions whether the difficulty will make them give up, Irene immediately refuses. Victor finally trusted them enough to grant the opportunity, and she does not intend to waste it.[4]
This persistence demonstrates that Irene's attachment to Victor has developed beyond simply competing for his attention. She wants to become useful to him and participate meaningfully in the civilization he has built. At the same time, Irene remains prone to ordinary sibling mischief. By Chapter 1160 she is an adult Dragon Goddess capable of influencing fundamental emotion yet still becomes involved in stealing rare childhood photographs of Victor and hiding from Stella when the theft is discovered.[1]
Irene also understands why Victor imposes restrictions upon his daughters. After Stella recovers the photographs, Irene admits that her Charm could easily be used to make the dispute easier, but Victor has forbidden the daughters from using overwhelming powers against one another in ordinary disagreements. Irene explicitly recognizes the educational purpose: relying upon supernatural force for every inconvenience would weaken their judgment and independence.[1]

### Heritage
Irene combines two unusually potent supernatural bloodlines. [[Victor Elderblood]] supplies the dominant Blood Dragon and True Dragon foundation possessed by his biological descendants, while [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood contributes one of the oldest Divine heritages associated with Love, Beauty, attraction and desire.
Rather than inheriting Aphrodite's entire Divine identity unchanged, Irene developed her own specialized authority. Her personal Concept is <Obsessive Love>, focusing Aphrodite's broad Love heritage into fixation, possessiveness and emotionally consuming affection.[2]
Victor's increasingly Eldritch existence also affected the nature of his later descendants. His biological daughters carry varying degrees of Eldritch and Primordial heritage alongside their Draconic foundation, although only certain children directly manifest those aspects as named Divinities. Irene therefore possesses this broader Elderblood heritage without currently being established as one of the original universal Primordial Beings.
Like Victor's other biological daughters, Irene also inherited developmental potential associated with his deeper perception of Reality. Victor later explains that his daughters possess variations of his ability to perceive the underlying Truth of existence, effectively allowing them to perceive portions of Reality's deeper structure or "source code".[5] Irene's individual degree of mastery has not yet been separately quantified.

### Divine Concept — Obsessive Love
Irene's defining Divine authority is <Obsessive Love>. [[Aphrodite]] explicitly identifies her daughter as a Goddess of Love whose Love is not ordinary Love but the obsessive form of the Concept.[2]
Aphrodite herself represents a much broader Love authority encompassing Beauty, attraction, desire and numerous expressions of affection. Irene instead specializes in the point where Love intensifies into fixation and possessiveness. This makes her Divinity particularly compatible with the Elderblood family's extreme emotional bonds and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s own association with obsessive or yandere-like Love.

#### Charm
Charm is one of Irene's most important confirmed traits and must not be treated as a merely cosmetic consequence of her beauty.
Chapter 998 explicitly describes Irene as having been born with an overwhelming Charm capable of destroying galaxies. The narration immediately clarifies that this is not an exaggeration.[3] The statement is therefore a canonical description of the magnitude of Irene's Charm rather than a fandom extrapolation.
The exact destructive mechanism has not yet been explained. Nothing in the statement requires Irene's physical punches, Dragon Breath or conventional Energy output to possess Galaxy-destroying force. Instead, the feat is specifically attributed to Charm, making it an abnormal hax-like or conceptual property of her presence and Love-associated existence.
Her Charm also possesses much smaller-scale directly demonstrated effects. Leon becomes entranced simply from seeing her among [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters in Chapter 998, forcing Anna to reinforce his protection and warn him to remain careful.[3] In Chapter 1001, Irene deliberately allows her Divine nature to surface while embracing Victor; although Victor remains unaffected, several girls nearby begin feeling physically hotter because of Irene's influence.[2]
By Chapter 1160 Irene herself treats Charm as a power she could intentionally use against another Goddess. While discussing how to deal with Stella, she states that the problem would be easy to solve through her Charm, but refuses to do so because Victor's rules prohibit using powers against sisters outside mutually accepted combat or training.[1]

#### Known Applications
- Love Manipulation: Irene possesses Divine authority over Love through the specialized Concept of <Obsessive Love>.[2]
- Obsessive Love Manipulation: Her Divinity specifically governs Love expressed through fixation, possessiveness and obsession rather than reproducing [[Aphrodite]]'s entire general Love domain.[2]
- Emotional Influence: Irene can influence the emotional and physiological state of nearby individuals through her Divine presence.[2]
- Attraction and Desire Inducement: Her presence can cause attraction and desire beyond the effect of ordinary physical beauty. Leon is immediately entranced by her in Chapter 998, while several girls experience Irene's influence in Chapter 1001.[3][2]
- Area Influence: Irene's Love-related influence can affect multiple nearby individuals simultaneously rather than being limited to a single selected target.[2]
- Intentional Charm: Irene possesses sufficient conscious control to consider using Charm against Stella during an ordinary dispute, although she chooses not to violate [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s rules.[1]
- Galaxy-Destroying Charm: The narration explicitly states that Irene's overwhelming Charm can destroy galaxies and emphasizes that the description is not an exaggeration.[3]
- Violet Heart Manifestation: A violet heart appears within Irene's eyes when her Love-oriented Divine nature becomes active.[2]
- Conceptual Development: As a young Goddess, Irene's understanding and practical applications of <Obsessive Love> can continue growing as she develops. The final scope of the Concept remains unrevealed.
The complete limits of <Obsessive Love> remain unknown. Her confirmed abilities support powerful Charm, emotional influence, attraction and obsessive Love authority, but generic abilities such as draining Love as Energy, automatically reading every emotional connection in existence or controlling every unrelated form of obsession should still be distinguished from feats Irene has personally demonstrated.

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Irene is introduced as part of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s enormous second generation after accelerated Time allowed his wives and daughters to experience centuries or millennia of development while relatively little Time passed in the outside world. She is identified as the biological daughter of Victor and [[Aphrodite]], combining a natural Blood Dragon body with one of the setting's most powerful Divine lineages associated with Love and Beauty.[6]
Chapter 998 provides the first major description of Irene's abnormal appearance and Charm. When Victor calls his daughters together, Leon observes the enormous group of Dragon girls surrounding him. Although all of Victor's daughters are extraordinarily beautiful, Leon's attention is drawn toward the pink-haired Irene. The narration describes her as essentially perfect and states that, as the daughter of two Beings renowned for supernatural beauty, she had been born with an overwhelming Charm capable of destroying galaxies. The text specifically adds that this was not an exaggeration.[3]
Leon immediately falls into a stupor while looking at her, prompting Anna to strengthen the barrier protecting him and warn him to be extremely careful. The scene demonstrates that Irene's Charm possesses an active supernatural effect even when she is not deliberately attempting to manipulate the observer.[3]
Her Divine nature becomes clearer in Chapter 1001. Irene approaches Victor during a family gathering, casually removes Yuno from his lap and occupies the position herself. She presses herself against Victor and begins behaving provocatively while a violet heart appears in her eyes. Victor remains unaffected and simply interprets the behavior as another daughter asking for affection, but the other girls nearby begin becoming physically affected by Irene's influence.[2]
Anna quickly intervenes and moves Irene away from Victor. The situation later becomes a topic inside the wives' communication group, where Aphrodite proudly explains that Irene is a Goddess of Love whose specific Divinity is <Obsessive Love>. Aphrodite also openly admits that she has been teaching Irene how to seduce properly because she considers such knowledge necessary for her daughter to understand her own nature.[2]
As Irene and the other daughters mature, Victor gradually begins allowing them to interact with increasingly serious responsibilities. Irene eventually joins the group of Imperial Princesses who ask to learn how to assist with management of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Their confidence initially comes from the enormous education they received through Dream Worlds, but Chapter 1120 demonstrates that this experience barely prepares them for the scale of Victor's actual civilization.[4]
The girls realize that their previous training taught them how to understand societies and interact responsibly without relying upon overwhelming powers, but Imperial administration requires planning for entire worlds and galaxies. Irene openly acknowledges that she now understands why their parents repeatedly asked whether they truly wanted the responsibility. When [[Valentina Victoria Elderblood]] asks whether the younger daughters intend to quit merely because the work is difficult, Irene immediately refuses and reminds everyone that Victor only recently accepted their request.[4]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
By Chapter 1160 Irene has matured into a 188-centimeter adult Dragon Goddess with extremely long pink hair, violet eyes and a curvaceous but delicate figure. The narration describes her beauty as exceptional even by Divine standards and directly places her alongside [[Aphrodite]] and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] in supernatural attractiveness.[1]
Despite this maturity, her fixation upon Victor remains unchanged. [[Stella Elderblood]] discovers that rare photographs from Anna's private collection depicting Victor during his Human childhood have disappeared. These images are so heavily protected that the daughters cannot simply duplicate them with their supernatural powers. Stella tracks the photographs to [[Hinata Elderblood]], immediately recognizes Hinata's attempt to lie and then detects Irene hiding nearby.[1]
When Stella orders Irene to appear, the concealment around her breaks with Reality itself appearing to shatter. Irene reveals herself and immediately complains that it is unfair for Stella to possess photographs that everyone wants. Irene and Hinata reluctantly surrender the collection, including an exceptionally rare picture of Victor as an infant.[1]
Irene privately considers Anna excessive for making the photographs impossible to copy, but then admits that she would probably protect the same treasures just as obsessively if they belonged to her. The admission demonstrates how naturally Irene understands possessive attachment, fitting her <Obsessive Love> Divinity even outside romantic or seductive situations.[1]
After Stella leaves, Irene explains that she could probably solve the problem using Charm but refuses because Victor has forbidden his daughters from using supernatural abilities against sisters during ordinary disagreements. Powers may be used during mutually agreed duels or training, but not simply because one sister wants something from another. Irene understands that unrestricted reliance upon their Concepts would make them mentally dependent upon overwhelming power.[1]
The conversation also exposes Irene's vanity. She jokingly describes herself as a perfect Goddess and claims that even if she occasionally makes mistakes, everyone will forgive her because she is beautiful. Hinata compares her attitude to Boa Hancock and points out that all of Victor's daughters inherited monstrous beauty, causing Irene to react with annoyance.[1]
[[Yog-Sothoth]] Elderblood eventually appears and offers to help them recover the photographs. Rather than using her extraordinarily dangerous authorities, Yog simply leads Irene and Hinata to Anna and asks for copies. Anna immediately agrees. Irene and Hinata are stunned that the entire problem could have been resolved by asking politely and subsequently praise Yog as their strongest, best and most beautiful sister for accomplishing through good behavior what their elaborate scheme failed to achieve.[1]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Irene's biological father and the primary focus of her strongest demonstrated obsession. Irene competes openly with her sisters for his affection, behaves provocatively around him and considers even photographs from his Human childhood valuable enough to become involved in theft.[2][1]
Victor responds with extreme paternal affection but does not appear vulnerable to Irene's Charm during their Chapter 1001 interaction. While nearby girls experience physical effects from Irene's Divine influence, Victor simply laughs, pets her and interprets the display as another daughter seeking affection.[2] This should not erase the separate Chapter 998 statement regarding Irene's Galaxy-destroying Charm; it demonstrates only that Victor himself can remain unaffected under the circumstances shown.
Victor also establishes the boundaries Irene follows. His prohibition against using conceptual powers, Charm or Reality alteration to settle ordinary sibling disputes directly prevents Irene from using one of her most dangerous advantages against Stella. Irene accepts the restriction because she understands that Victor wants his daughters to develop independence and judgment rather than becoming mentally dependent upon their abilities.[1]

#### Aphrodite Elderblood
[[Aphrodite]] Elderblood is Irene's biological mother and the source of her Love-associated Divine heritage. Irene resembles her so strongly that Chapter 1120 effectively describes her as a sculpted copy of Aphrodite with only limited physical traits inherited from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[4]
Their conceptual relationship is similarly direct but not identical. Aphrodite possesses a vast authority encompassing Love, Beauty, attraction and desire, whereas Irene developed the more specialized <Obsessive Love>.[2] Aphrodite is extremely proud of this development and actively teaches Irene seduction, arguing that a Goddess of Love should properly understand how to use her nature.

#### Anna Elderblood
[[Anna Elderblood]] is one of Irene's principal maternal authority figures. Anna immediately recognizes Irene's provocative intentions around [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] in Chapter 1001 and orders her to behave; Irene obeys without attempting to challenge her.[2]
Anna's collection of Victor's childhood photographs later becomes the center of Irene's Chapter 1160 mischief. Irene initially criticizes the extreme protections preventing supernatural duplication but privately admits she would probably do exactly the same thing if the photographs belonged to her.[1] When Yog eventually approaches Anna directly, Anna simply provides copies, leaving Irene and Hinata stunned that asking politely was more effective than their scheme.

#### Hinata Elderblood
[[Hinata Elderblood]] is Irene's primary partner during the photograph incident. Both desire copies of the rare images of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and cooperate after deciding that Stella and Anna possess an unfairly exclusive collection.[1]
Hinata repeatedly acts as the more skeptical member of the pair, warning Irene that concealment will not fool Stella and mocking Irene's exaggerated confidence in her beauty. Their interactions remain playful and demonstrate an easy sibling relationship despite their enormous supernatural power.

#### Stella Elderblood
[[Stella Elderblood]] exposes Irene's involvement in stealing the photographs. Stella's passive ability to recognize falsehood makes ordinary deception almost useless around her, and once Hinata lies, Stella quickly identifies Irene's hidden location as well.[1]
Irene believes Charm could resolve the confrontation but refuses to use it because [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] forbids powers between sisters outside accepted training and duels. Their disagreement therefore remains a mundane argument over photographs rather than escalating into conflict between Goddesses capable of manipulating fundamental Concepts.

#### Yog-Sothoth Elderblood
[[Yog-Sothoth]] Elderblood solves the photograph dispute by simply asking Anna for copies. Irene and Hinata initially expect another complicated plan but are stunned when Anna agrees immediately.[1]
The two subsequently praise Yog as the strongest, best and most beautiful sister for accomplishing through good behavior what they failed to achieve through theft and concealment.

#### Eros
[[Eros]] is Irene's maternal half-brother through [[Aphrodite]]. No substantial personal relationship between the two has yet been shown, so their connection is presently established primarily through genealogy.

### Power Scope

### Trivia
- Irene is the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood.[6]
- Her personal Divinity is <Obsessive Love>.[2]
- Chapter 998 explicitly states that Irene was born with an overwhelming Charm capable of destroying galaxies and immediately clarifies that this statement is not an exaggeration.[3]
- The Galaxy-destroying statement concerns Irene's Charm, not a direct statement that her ordinary physical attacks possess the same destructive output.[3]
- Leon becomes entranced simply by observing Irene, causing Anna to strengthen the barrier protecting him and warn him to be careful.[3]
- Irene inherited her mother's pink hair and strongly resembles Aphrodite, while her violet eyes reflect Victor's lineage.[4]
- By Chapter 1160 Irene stands approximately 188 cm tall and possesses extremely long pink hair, violet eyes and a curvaceous but delicate adult form.[1]
- Her beauty is described as extraordinary even by the standards of Gods and comparable to both Aphrodite and Victor.[1]
- A violet heart appears within Irene's eyes when her Love-related influence becomes active.[2]
- Irene's influence affects multiple girls around her in Chapter 1001 despite Victor being the primary focus of her attention.[2]
- Victor himself remains unaffected by that particular display.[2]
- Aphrodite openly teaches Irene seduction because she considers it necessary education for a Goddess of Love.[2]
- Irene participates in practical education designed to prepare Victor's younger daughters for positions within the [[Elderblood Empire]].[4]
- She initially underestimates the difficulty of Galactic administration but explicitly refuses to quit once Victor gives them the opportunity.[4]
- Irene and [[Hinata Elderblood]] become involved in stealing rare photographs of Victor from his Human childhood in Chapter 1160.[1]
- Irene hides while Stella confronts Hinata, but Stella identifies her position and forces her to appear.[1]
- The photographs are protected strongly enough that Victor's daughters cannot simply reproduce them with supernatural powers.[1]
- Irene admits that she could use Charm to make the Stella problem easier but refuses because Victor has prohibited powers against sisters in ordinary disagreements.[1]
- Irene jokingly calls herself a perfect Goddess and argues that her beauty will make people forgive her mistakes.[1]
- Hinata compares Irene's attitude to Boa Hancock, a comparison Irene strongly dislikes.[1]
- Irene, Hinata and Yog eventually obtain the photographs simply by asking Anna directly, leaving Irene and Hinata stunned by how easy the solution was.[1]
- Irene possesses the adaptive, mental, spiritual and resurrection safeguards Victor incorporated into his biological daughters.[7]
- Like Victor's other biological daughters, Irene possesses inherited potential to perceive deeper structures of Reality, although her individual specialization in this ability remains unrevealed.[5]
- Irene possesses Eldritch and Primordial heritage through Victor, but this does not by itself establish her as one of the original universal Primordial Beings.
- Her exact current subjective age is difficult to determine because the Elderblood family has experienced multiple extreme periods of Time acceleration.
