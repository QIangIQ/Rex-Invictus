#### Debut
Chapter 658
Vepar Elderblood, formerly known as Duke Vepar and the 42nd Key of Solomon, is a former member of the 72 Dukes of Hell, one of [[Victor Elderblood]]'s original Demonic Generals and later one of his wives. Unlike many Demonic Pillars who responded to Victor's conquest with suicidal Pride, Vepar survived because she paid close attention to events beyond Hell, recognized how abnormal Victor's growth had become and chose submission before he ever needed to force it from her.[2][3]
Although Vepar originally occupied only Rank 42, her comparatively modest position disguised an unusually broad range of skills. She was primarily a Water user specializing in terrain control, but centuries of survival had also taught her assassination, infiltration, cloning tactics, administration, information gathering and numerous other secondary trades. Victor eventually summarized her as someone who was competent at almost everything without having previously specialized deeply enough to become a true master of any one field.[4]
Victor rewarded this adaptability after Vepar successfully conquered a higher-ranked Pillar territory using an army of volunteers, Water-based terrain manipulation, clones and assassination tactics. His Energy permanently evolved her body, increasing her height from 180 cm to 186 cm, developing Ice upon her horns and tail and changing her original Water affinity into an affinity for both Water and Ice. Victor concluded that her existence had become so heavily aligned with Ice that she resembled an Ice Spirit more than an ordinary Demon and considered Demon of Frost an appropriate description of what she had become.[5]
Vepar subsequently became one of the three original Generals directly beneath Victor during his conquest of Hell alongside [[Vine]] and Helena. The Demonic population came to know them as Vine the [[Destroyer]], Vepar the Proud and Helena the Strategist.[6] Whereas Vine became responsible for warfare and military training and Helena for long-term internal and external policy, Vepar and four personally selected Commanders assumed responsibility for the enormous logistical and administrative systems required to govern Hell, including administration of its various Sinner Hells.[6]
Her importance continued long after the Demonic War. Vepar helped destroy the remaining elites of [[Diablo]]'s army, contributed to the reconstruction of Hell under Victor, worked alongside [[Aline Elderblood Valefar]] and Victor to regulate the Dimension's Miasma, supervised infrastructure capable of mobilizing billions of Demons and eventually became one of Victor's Demonic wives.[7][8][9][10]
Vepar was later transformed into a Blood Dragon and True Dragon. Her former Ice-Demon characteristics were preserved as part of the transformation, turning her into a Demonic Ice Dragon and raising the physical, spiritual and supernatural foundation underlying all of her existing abilities.[11]

### Physical Description
Vepar originally appeared as a tall female Demon approximately 180 centimeters in height. She possessed blue skin, a slim and well-proportioned body, prominent horns, a long sharply pointed Demonic tail and striking eyes composed of black sclerae surrounding Ice-blue irises.[3]
Her appearance lacked some of the exaggerated physical characteristics displayed by several other Pillars, matching Vepar's tendency to survive through caution and versatility rather than presenting herself as an overwhelmingly destructive brute. She nevertheless possessed the visibly supernatural anatomy of a high-ranking Demon and was immediately recognizable as one of Hell's more powerful inhabitants.
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] evolved her, Vepar's body underwent a subtler transformation than [[Vine]]'s. Her height increased from approximately 180 cm to 186 cm, her figure became more pronounced and Ice appeared along the tips of her horns and tail. Most noticeably, the surrounding atmosphere became unnaturally colder merely from Vepar's presence.[5]
Victor concluded that this was not simply Vepar learning Cryokinesis. Her body itself had become extraordinarily Ice-aligned, describing her as closer to an Ice Spirit than a conventional Demon. Her transformation was potentially influenced by [[Roxanne Elderblood]]'s Energy being involved in the enhancement process.[5]
Following her later Blood Dragon transformation, Vepar became a genuine True Dragon while preserving aspects of her Demonic/Ice foundation. Her current humanoid appearance can therefore coexist with a vastly more powerful Draconic form rather than representing the full scale of her biological existence.[11]

### Personality and Traits
Vepar's most defining characteristic is not raw Pride but caution. Unlike many Demonic Pillars of similar rank who paid attention almost exclusively to Hell's internal politics, Vepar deliberately followed important events occurring throughout the wider supernatural world. Information might reach Hell several days or weeks late, but she continued collecting it because she understood that ignorance could eventually get her killed.[3]
That habit is the principal reason she survived [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s conquest. Vepar had followed reports concerning the Second Vampire Progenitor long before he appeared in Hell and gradually concluded that Victor's growth was completely abnormal. When news reached her that he was actively conquering Pillar territories, she did not repeat the mistake of her neighboring Dukes. Among Pillars Rank 40 through 44, Vepar was the only one who voluntarily sided with him; the others were killed.[2][3]
Her decision was pragmatic but not entirely fearful. Vepar also recognized that Victor represented an opportunity. Breaking into the Top 20 Pillars had seemed effectively impossible because the Demons occupying those positions were vastly older Ancient Demons. Victor, however, had already demonstrated that he could completely overturn Hell's established hierarchy. The possibility that he could give her the Power she had been unable to obtain through ordinary Demonic advancement made serving him increasingly attractive.[3]
Vepar possesses considerable Pride, explaining the title later given to her by Hell's inhabitants, but unlike many Demons she usually keeps that Pride subordinate to self-preservation and reason. She does not need to die for the sake of appearances and has little difficulty acknowledging when another person is stronger or when another method is more efficient.
She is also exceptionally versatile. After observing Vepar's conquest, Victor asked whether assassination was her specialty. Vepar corrected him: her true specialty was controlling terrain through Water, while assassination was merely one skill she had learned over her long life. Asked how many secondary trades she possessed, Vepar admitted that she had lost count.[4]
Victor interpreted this as Vepar being good at almost everything but a master of nothing. Rather than dismissing that characteristic, he decided that her broad foundation could be refined later. Her later appointment to logistics and administration proved the value of that versatility because governing an entire Hell required far more than simple combat ability.[4][6]
Vepar is disciplined and comparatively studious for a Demon. Centuries after the conquest, she had read the historical records compiled by Albu and [[Zahal]] extensively enough to possess detailed information concerning powers belonging to ancient Demons such as Sloth. When Lily and [[Vine]] expressed surprise that she knew so much, Vepar criticized them for underestimating the historical responsibility Victor had assigned to the two [[Elders]].[12]
Her administrative temperament also makes her one of the people most willing to reprimand fellow Generals for carelessness. When Vine admitted she had ignored Vepar's written reports because rumors of a possible war had excited her, Vepar immediately scolded her for conduct unbecoming someone of her position.[9]
However, Vepar remains a Demon and is not completely immune to the corrupting temptations of authority. Victor specifically chose Helena rather than Vepar or Lily to act as overall ruler of Hell during his absence because both Vepar and Lily were judged more likely to become influenced by their Demonic nature if granted too much temporary power. This did not mean Vepar was incompetent—Chapter 756 explicitly states she was fully capable of ruling—but Victor considered Helena psychologically safer for unrestricted temporary sovereignty.[9]
Vepar is intensely loyal to Victor. Like Vine and Helena, praise from him produces obvious satisfaction and pride, and after the destruction of [[Diablo]]'s faction her devotion increasingly approached the fanaticism common among the King's closest Demons.[7] That loyalty eventually developed into marriage and membership within Clan Elderblood.[10][1]

### History

#### The Forty-Second Pillar
For most of her known existence, Vepar was the 42nd Key of Solomon. She understood better than many similarly ranked Demons how difficult advancement within the Ars Goetia hierarchy actually was. Meaningful Rank changes could require centuries, while the Top 20 were occupied by Ancient Demons whose accumulated power and experience made the upper hierarchy appear almost inaccessible.[3]
Rather than respond to this stagnation with reckless challenges, Vepar survived by remaining informed, acquiring numerous useful trades and avoiding battles she could not win. This temperament distinguished her sharply from Pillars such as [[Shax]] or [[Malphas]], whose refusal to submit to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] resulted in their deaths.

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Vepar was first mentioned after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] began systematically advancing through the territories of Hell's middle-ranked Pillars. By the time [[Diablo]] received reports concerning the invasion, Victor had already reached the region containing Ranks 40 through 44. Four rulers had refused his ultimatum and died; only Rank 42, Vepar, had chosen to join him.[2]
Her actual introduction occurred during Victor's assault upon the territory of [[Malphas]], the 39th Pillar. Vepar stood beside Victor as he approached the city and watched him offer the population a simple choice: abandon Malphas and submit, or remain and fight.[3]
Vepar had already chosen.
She had monitored Victor's rise in the outside world, knew about his increasingly impossible accomplishments and understood that his invasion of Hell was not an ordinary territorial dispute between Demons. To her, joining the abnormal Vampire Progenitor offered both survival and a path beyond the Rank 42 position she had occupied for so long.[3]
Victor subsequently destroyed Malphas, reinforcing Vepar's decision that submission had been the correct choice.
After the battle, Victor gave Vepar an opportunity equivalent to the one previously offered to [[Vine]]. He ordered her to conquer another Pillar-controlled city using whatever volunteers wished to follow her and promised that success would cause Vepar to become his completely and be reborn through the same kind of power that had transformed Vine.[3]
Vepar accepted eagerly.
Victor allowed Demons to volunteer rather than forcibly drafting them and gave Vepar approximately three days to prove herself. Roughly twenty thousand soldiers joined the operation, while Victor remained behind to observe whether Vepar actually deserved elevation into his emerging command structure.[3][4]
Vepar's resulting operation revealed her preferred style. Rather than attempting to defeat a stronger Pillar through direct frontal combat, she manipulated the terrain with Water, used Water-based clones to misdirect enemy forces and created opportunities for targeted assassination. Her clones lacked the complete physical substance of the original because they consisted entirely of Water, but Vepar used that limitation intelligently rather than expecting them to function as perfect duplicates.[4]
Her soldiers followed orders without seeking individual glory, allowing Vepar's plan to progress efficiently. She eventually assassinated the opposing Pillar, displayed the severed head before the surviving defenders and announced that their Lord was dead. The remaining Demons surrendered immediately.[4]
Victor was extremely satisfied.
He praised Vepar's efficiency, planning and assassin-like dexterity while also recognizing that assassination itself was not her real specialty. Vepar explained that she was fundamentally a terrain-control fighter who generated and manipulated Water to create advantageous conditions for her soldiers. Assassination was simply another skill accumulated during a long life spent surviving Hell.[4]
Her answer intrigued Victor enough that he decided Vepar would become his second General. He then rewarded both Vepar and the soldiers who had followed her by placing them inside Ice cocoons and infusing their bodies with purified Negative Energy.[4]
The transformation permanently altered Vepar.
Her height increased to 186 cm, her body became more pronounced, the tips of her horns and tail developed Ice and the environment around her became colder simply because she was present.[5]
The more important change was internal. Vepar's original supernatural power allowed her to create and control Water. After Victor's Energy changed her, that affinity expanded into Ice Manipulation comparable in principle to the supernatural Cryokinesis used by Clan Scarlett.[5]
Victor believed the transformation went much further than granting an extra element. Vepar had become so heavily composed of or aligned with Ice that he judged her closer to an Ice Spirit than an ordinary Demon. He suspected Roxanne's Energy had contributed to the unusual evolutionary result and concluded that Demon of Frost accurately described her new existence.[5]
Vepar immediately continued functioning as Victor's developing officer. When he discussed construction of a permanent territory in the deepest regions of Hell—territory belonging directly to Diablo—Vepar initially reminded him of the danger before quickly accepting that her new Lord genuinely intended to take it. Victor subsequently ordered her to organize his rapidly expanding forces and determine suitable military or logistical functions for his Demons.[5]
Vepar remained beside Victor through his confrontation with the Four Horsemen of The Apocalypse. As Victor's fight with War grew sufficiently destructive to rewrite the surrounding landscape, Vepar agreed with Vine that remaining nearby would only endanger the army and that the forces should withdraw, reorganize and prepare against possible ambushes rather than uselessly spectate.[13]
After War's defeat, Victor's conquest accelerated dramatically. Hundreds of thousands of Demons joined him, and Vine, Helena and Vepar visibly occupied the three highest positions beneath their King.[14]
Their status was justified by more than personal favor. Victor had strengthened all three enough that they were described as comparable to the former Top 10 Pillars, and each woman had already demonstrated competence deserving of her position.[14]
Vepar accompanied Victor into the deepest regions of Hell and was present when he shattered [[Lucifer]]'s supposedly indestructible gate, entered the city surrounding the Demon King's castle and finally moved to seize Diablo's throne.[14]
After Victor became the new King of Hell, the Demonic population created titles for his three original Generals:
Vine the [[Destroyer]], Vepar the Proud and Helena the Strategist.[6]
Victor reorganized Hell around their different competencies. Vepar and four personally selected Commanders became responsible for logistics and administration across the enormous Dimension. Their branch also administered the various Hells in which sinful Souls underwent punishment.[6]
The workload was immense. Vepar was eventually handling enough internal logistics and Sinner-Hell administration that Victor considered giving [[Lily Elderblood]] direct responsibility over the Sinner Hells specifically to reduce the burden on her.[6]
This position placed Vepar at the core of Victor's new government rather than merely making her a battlefield commander. Hell's enormous armies, Soul-processing territories, cities and administrative divisions could not function without the logistical systems her branch maintained.
Vepar returned to the Human World with Victor's forces during the final stage of the Demonic War.
After Helena and Lily incapacitated [[Bael]], Vine and Vepar attacked the remaining elites of Diablo's army and killed them. Victor subsequently converted the defeated enemies into spheres of blood for consumption.[7]
Vepar, Vine and Helena then appeared together before Victor and presented the remains of the enemies they had destroyed. Victor called the performance of his Generals impeccable, producing visible pride and satisfaction in all three women.[7]
When Lily completed her final mission by betraying and helping kill Bael, Victor formally promoted her as his Fourth General, expanding the original trio into the senior Demonic leadership that would govern Hell after the war.[7]

#### King Among Kings
Vepar became even more important once the war ended and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] began transforming Hell into a functioning civilization.
She and [[Vine]] were among the Generals responsible for protecting and supervising [[Lilith Elderblood]] after the former Demon Goddess awakened from her long mental exhaustion. They prevented Lilith from immediately entering the radically changed cities because her old understanding of Hell no longer matched the society Victor had created.[15]
Vepar explained that Abaddon and the other new cities had developed strict social rules, currencies and communal expectations. Even Lilith's former status would not allow her to violently take goods or disturb the Royal Capital without effectively turning the population of Hell against her.[15]
The statement demonstrated the success of the government's reforms. Vepar explained that the Three Cities had become secure enough that Vine's military forces were barely necessary for ordinary civilian protection because Demons themselves valued the peace and would oppose anyone threatening it.[15]
One of Vepar's four Commanders was [[Aline Elderblood Valefar]], daughter of former Rank 6 Pillar Valefar and an Ice Demon. Officially, Aline answered directly to Vepar as Head of Abaddon's Technological Development Department.[15]
This hierarchy was largely administrative rather than a reflection of their true value to Victor. Aline secretly became the new Ruler of Hell, and Victor regarded her importance as comparable to or even greater than that of the Generals because the Soul system depended upon her.[8]
Vepar nevertheless worked closely with Aline. Together with Victor, the two helped solve one of the greatest environmental problems facing the new Hell: excessive Miasma distorted Time and created dangerous conditions throughout the Dimension.[8]
By redistributing and containing the Energy rather than simply eliminating it, Victor, Vepar and Aline established a sustainable balance in which approximately one year on Earth corresponded to five years in Hell.[8]
The reform could not simply remove all excess Miasma because Miasma functioned as Hell's equivalent of [[Natural Energy]] and was required for the birth of Demons. Aline's technological solutions and Vepar's administrative knowledge helped preserve the specialized Sinner Hells while improving the wider environment.[8]
Several centuries later, Vepar remained one of the highest authorities in the Dimension.
When Victor's senior Demonic women gathered to prepare Hell's forces, Vine reported that the Hordes were militarily prepared while Vepar reported that internal logistics were complete and billions of Demonic Hordes could mobilize with a single command from the King.[9]
The same chapter demonstrates her attention to procedure. When Vine admitted she had ignored the reports Vepar sent because she became excited upon hearing a war might occur, Vepar reprimanded her and reminded Vine that making excuses was inappropriate for a person holding her rank.[9]
Vepar's knowledge also extends far beyond logistics. She studied the historical works written by Albu and [[Zahal]], allowing her to possess unusually detailed knowledge regarding the ancient Demons and former servants of [[Diablo]]. She correctly explained the passive defensive properties of the Sin of Sloth when even Lily did not understand them.[12]
When questioned about the source of her certainty, Vepar simply explained that she had actually read the [[Elders]]' books instead of treating their appointment as ceremonial.[12]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
By Chapter 888, Vepar's relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had evolved beyond that of General and King. She arrived in the former [[Egyptian Pantheon]] alongside Helena, [[Vine]], Lily and Lilith, and [[Aphrodite]] explicitly recognized the group as Victor's Demonic wives.[10]
The women brought entire Demonic Legions to establish a permanent base in the newly claimed territory. Vepar observed that the beings immigrating there already instinctively understood the danger of causing conflict inside Victor's domain, while Vine argued that an overt military presence remained useful for reinforcing that understanding.[10]
Marriage did not remove Vepar from administrative or military service. She remained one of the principal representatives of Hell while increasingly becoming integrated into the larger Elderblood family and Dragons Nest.
Vepar's greatest biological evolution occurred when Victor transformed another group of compatible wives into [[Blood Dragons]]. Vepar entered the transformation alongside Vine, Helena, Lily, Aline and several other women connected to his faction.[11]
Victor's method deliberately retained meaningful portions of each woman's original racial characteristics rather than producing identical Dragons. Vepar therefore retained the Ice-aligned and Demonic foundation created during her first evolution, transforming into a Demonic Ice Dragon rather than losing everything that had previously distinguished her.[11]
The transformation made Vepar a true member of the Dragon race, greatly strengthening her body, Soul, Energy reserves and existing supernatural abilities.

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Vepar subsequently became completely integrated into Clan Elderblood and the political structure that developed into the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Her old responsibilities within Hell remained relevant because the Dimension became one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s major Imperial territories rather than an isolated Biblical afterlife.
Her relationship with Victor was later formalized through the Clan's [[Soul Marriage]] structure, binding her to him not simply as an ancient subordinate but as family.[1]
Vepar's evolution from Rank 42 is particularly illustrative of Victor's meritocratic ideology. Under the old Ars Goetia system, she believed reaching the Top 20 was effectively impossible because the Ancient Demons above her possessed overwhelming advantages in age and accumulated power. Under Victor, the skills that had merely allowed Vepar to survive became the reason she was elevated beyond nearly every rank she once considered unreachable.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
Vepar chose [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] before he personally forced her to choose him. Her decision came from carefully observing his rise, recognizing the increasing impossibility of his accomplishments and understanding that opposing him merely because of Demonic Pride would accomplish nothing.[3]
Her initial motivation combined survival, ambition and attraction. Victor represented the opportunity to escape the stagnant Rank 42 position that had defined most of her existence.
Victor did not immediately reward her merely for surrendering. He demanded proof.
Vepar responded by conquering a superior Pillar's territory efficiently enough that Victor chose her as his second General and personally evolved her with his Energy.[4][5]
From that point onward, Vepar's pragmatic allegiance became increasingly emotional. Victor gave her access to power she had believed unreachable, entrusted her with an entire branch of Hell's government and repeatedly demonstrated competence far exceeding every ruler she had previously known. By the end of the Demonic War, simple praise from him visibly filled Vepar with pride and happiness.[7]
She eventually became one of his wives and later a Blood Dragon of his lineage.[10][11][1]

#### Vine Elderblood
[[Vine]] and Vepar are fellow original Generals whose personalities and responsibilities contrast strongly.
Vine is aggressive, direct and obsessed with military preparation, whereas Vepar is more cautious, procedural and attentive to information. Their exchanges often resemble those of colleagues accustomed to compensating for one another's shortcomings.
When Vine neglects administrative work because battle excites her, Vepar is usually the person who criticizes her.[9] Conversely, both women readily cooperate when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s safety or military objectives require action.

#### Helena Gremory Elderblood
Helena, [[Vine]] and Vepar formed [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s original trio of Generals and became the foundational leadership of his reformed Hell.
Their responsibilities were complementary: Helena handled strategy and policy, Vine warfare and training, and Vepar logistics and administration.[6]
Helena's greater resistance to the temptations of temporary authority eventually caused Victor to choose her as acting overall ruler whenever he was absent, although the narration explicitly emphasizes that Vepar remained entirely capable of governing.[9]

#### Aline Elderblood Valefar
Aline is officially one of Vepar's Commanders and initially works beneath her as Head of Abaddon's Technological Development Department.[15]
The hierarchy is mostly nominal. Aline's actual function as Ruler of Hell makes her one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s most indispensable subordinates, something Vepar and the other Generals understand.[8][9]
Vepar and Aline work particularly well together because their specialties overlap in infrastructure. Vepar understands Hell's administration, logistics and existing systems, while Aline provides technological and scientific solutions capable of transforming those systems.

- Vepar carefully studies the historical records written by Albu and [[Zahal]].[12]
- Her knowledge of those records allowed her to explain abilities belonging to the Sin of Sloth that even Lily did not know.[12]

#### Skills and Abilities
Vepar is one of [[Victor Elderblood]]'s original Demonic Generals and an exceptionally versatile supernatural combatant whose abilities developed through three major stages: her original existence as the 42nd Demon Pillar, her permanent evolution after receiving Victor's Energy, and her later transformation into a Blood Dragon and True Dragon. Before Victor changed her, Vepar's principal supernatural affinity was Water and her preferred combat methodology was terrain control, supplemented by Water clones, assassination, military command and a large number of secondary disciplines accumulated across her long life.[1]
Victor's first evolution significantly improved Vepar's body and altered her elemental nature. Her Water affinity expanded into Ice, Ice developed naturally along her horns and tail, the environment around her became colder and Victor concluded that her existence had become closer to an Ice Spirit than an ordinary Demon.[2] By Chapter 673, Vepar, [[Vine]] and Helena were described as having reached power comparable to the former Top 10 Pillars through Victor's enhancement and their own demonstrated ability.[3]
Her eventual transformation into a Blood Dragon elevated the entire foundation of her existence again, turning her into a Demonic Ice Dragon with True Dragon physiology while preserving the abilities and characteristics that had distinguished her previous forms.[4]

##### Blood Dragon / True Dragon Physiology
Blood Dragon Physiology: Vepar is currently a Blood Dragon belonging to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s lineage and consequently possesses the fundamental characteristics of a genuine True Dragon. Victor's transformation method preserves meaningful aspects of the transformed individual's previous species and abilities, allowing Vepar's Ice-Demon foundation to remain incorporated into her current Draconic form.[4]
- Immense Strength: True Dragon physiology grants Vepar tremendous physical strength vastly exceeding the already superhuman physical power she possessed as a Pillar Demon.
- Immense Speed and Reflexes: Her Draconic body provides highly enhanced movement speed, reactions and combat perception.
- Immense Durability: Vepar possesses the exceptionally resistant body characteristic of [[True Dragons]], granting far greater physical durability than her former Demonic physiology.
- Enhanced Agility and Stamina: Her body can sustain prolonged high-level combat and perform movements impossible for conventional living species.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: [[Blood Dragons]] possess extraordinarily powerful regeneration, further improving upon Vepar's previous Demonic healing.
- Extreme Longevity / [[Immortality]]: Vepar was already naturally long-lived as a Demon and is currently a True Dragon whose existence is removed even further from ordinary biological mortality.
- Enhanced Senses: Vepar possesses heightened physical and supernatural perception, including the ability to detect Energy and rapidly analyze changes in the battlefield.
- Immense Energy Reserves: True Dragons possess naturally enormous Energy reserves. This greatly expands upon the significant increase Vepar had already received during Victor's first evolution.
- Natural Flight: Vepar's current Draconic existence grants aerial mobility through her Dragon Form, while her former Demonic body already possessed supernatural movement capabilities.
- Shapeshifting: Vepar can assume a humanoid appearance despite possessing a considerably more powerful True Dragon body.
- Dragon Form: Vepar possesses a genuine Dragon Form influenced by her Demonic and Ice characteristics. The exact size and complete appearance of her individual form have not yet been described sufficiently to establish precise measurements.
- Draconic Natural Weapons: Her Dragon Form possesses the natural claws, fangs, scales and other biological weapons of the species.
- Dragon Breath: As a True Dragon, Vepar possesses the racial foundation necessary to use a supernatural breath attack. The exact elemental or metaphysical property of her personal breath has not yet been individually demonstrated.
- Draconic Presence: The quality of a True Dragon's existence naturally produces overwhelming pressure upon weaker beings.
- Enhanced Soul and Existence: Vepar's transformation strengthened more than her body. True Dragon evolution elevates the target's Soul and metaphysical foundation along with its physical form.[4]

###### Draconic Runes
Draconic Runes: Vepar possesses the natural comprehension of the Draconic Language associated with genuine [[True Dragons]]. Unlike ordinary [[Magic]] users who must study the language externally, Dragons possess an innate connection to it through their species.
The transformation therefore grants Vepar the ability to learn and employ Draconic Runes naturally. However, no individually named Rune or Runic technique has yet been demonstrated specifically by Vepar, so individual effects should not be assigned without additional evidence.

##### Former High-Ranking Demon Physiology
Demon Physiology: Before becoming a Blood Dragon, Vepar was already a high-ranking Demon and member of the Ars Goetia. Her Rank 42 status placed her vastly above Lesser Demons and granted the supernatural characteristics expected from ancient Pillar-class Demons.
- Superhuman Strength: Vepar possessed physical strength greatly exceeding Lesser and ordinary High-Level Demons.
- Superhuman Speed and Reflexes: Her Pillar physiology granted the speed necessary to fight other ancient supernatural beings and perform high-speed assassination maneuvers.
- Enhanced Durability: Her body could withstand levels of trauma far beyond ordinary mortal or low-level supernatural organisms.
- Enhanced Stamina: Vepar participated in prolonged conquests and large military campaigns without suffering normal physical exhaustion.
- Demonic Regeneration: As a high-ranking Demon, Vepar could naturally recover from severe physical injuries.
- Demonic Longevity: Vepar had occupied Rank 42 for a substantial period and does not suffer conventional Human aging.
- Miasma Manipulation: As a native Demon of Hell, Vepar possesses natural affinity with Miasma and can use the Energy of Hell to support her supernatural abilities.
- Miasma Resistance: Vepar can survive and function within extremely dense Miasma environments, including Hell's deeper regions.
- Demonic Energy Perception: Her long experience in Hell allows her to identify and analyze Demonic Energy signatures and changes in surrounding Miasma.
- Demonic [[Immortality]] (Former Racial Mechanism): High-Level Demons can under appropriate circumstances return to Hell after bodily destruction if their Souls remain intact. Vepar never personally required this process, and her later transformation into a True Dragon changed her underlying species, so her current resurrection mechanics should not be assumed to remain identical to an ordinary Demon Pillar.

##### Victor's First Evolution
Energy Empowerment: After Vepar proved herself by conquering another Pillar territory, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] infused her directly with purified Negative Energy, permanently changing her physiology.[1][2]
- Permanent Physical Enhancement: Vepar's baseline strength, durability, speed and supernatural capacity were permanently elevated.
- Height Increase: Her body increased from approximately 180 cm to 186 cm.[2]
- Ice Evolution: Her horns and tail developed Ice-tipped characteristics and the temperature of the surrounding environment dropped in response to her transformed existence.[2]
- Elemental Evolution: Her original Water Manipulation expanded into Ice Manipulation.[2]
- Ice-Spirit-Like Physiology: Victor judged that Vepar had become so closely aligned with Ice that she was closer to an Ice Spirit than an ordinary Demon.[2]
- Top-Pillar-Level Growth: By Chapter 673, Vepar, [[Vine]] and Helena were described as comparable in strength to former Top 10 Pillars.[3]

##### Hydrokinesis
Water Manipulation: Vepar's original signature supernatural ability was the creation and manipulation of Water. She explicitly identifies Water-based terrain control as her real specialty rather than assassination.[1]
Rather than primarily firing Water as simple projectiles, Vepar uses it to manipulate the environment and create tactical advantages for herself and allied soldiers.

###### Water Generation
Vepar can create Water supernaturally instead of requiring access to a pre-existing body of water.[1]
This greatly increases the reliability of her terrain-control style because a battlefield does not need to contain suitable natural Water before she begins modifying it.

###### Terrain Manipulation
Vepar can spread Water throughout the battlefield and alter the terrain to interfere with enemies while providing tactical advantages to allied forces.[1]
She specifically told [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] that this was her primary combat specialty and that she used the Water she produced to support her soldiers.

###### Water Clones
Water Cloning: Vepar can create humanoid duplicates composed entirely of Water.[1]
- Visual Duplication: The clones can imitate Vepar's appearance sufficiently to mislead opponents.
- Misdirection: She uses them to divide attention and disguise her true position.
- Tactical Diversion: During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s test, clone tactics contributed to Vepar maneuvering into position to kill the enemy Pillar.[1]
- Limited Substance: Victor observed that Vepar's clones lacked the same physical substantiality possessed by certain more advanced cloning abilities because they consisted purely of Water.[1]
- Specialized Use: Vepar compensates for their limited physicality by using them as tactical tools rather than expecting them to fight as full-power copies.

##### Cryokinesis
Ice Manipulation: After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Energy evolved her, Vepar's former Water affinity expanded into the ability to create and manipulate Ice.[2]
Victor compared the resulting ability to Clan Scarlett's supernatural Ice manipulation but considered Vepar's transformed existence unusual because her body itself had become closely aligned with the element.
- Ice Creation: Vepar can generate Ice through supernatural Energy.
- Ice Manipulation: She can shape and control Ice produced through her ability.
- Environmental Cooling: The temperature around Vepar naturally decreases as a consequence of her Ice-aligned existence.[2]
- Ice-Tipped Body Traits: Her horns and tail developed permanent Ice features following her transformation.[2]
- Water-to-Ice Conversion: Because her original Water affinity evolved rather than being replaced, Vepar can combine the two abilities and freeze Water she creates or controls.
- Ice Terrain Control: Her existing expertise in Water-based battlefield manipulation naturally extends to frozen terrain.
- Ice-Spirit Affinity: Victor described Vepar as closer to an Ice Spirit than a normal Demon after her evolution.[2]
The precise maximum temperature and lower limit of Vepar's Cryokinesis have not yet been established, so abilities comparable to Victor's Absolute Zero should not be attributed to her without explicit evidence.

##### Elemental Synergy
Water-Ice Manipulation: Vepar's Hydrokinesis and Cryokinesis function as connected powers rather than unrelated Elements.
This allows her to create Water to dominate terrain and subsequently convert sections of that Water into Ice, theoretically giving her much greater battlefield flexibility than a combatant restricted exclusively to one state of matter.
The novel has not yet provided a full direct battle showing the maximum complexity of Vepar's post-evolution combined elemental control, so specific advanced applications should remain conservative until demonstrated.

##### Cloning
Elemental Cloning: Vepar's cloning ability is fundamentally an application of her Water Manipulation rather than an unexplained independent biological power.[1]
Her Water clones are particularly useful because her combat style prioritizes positioning, deception and terrain control over straightforward exchanges of brute force.
- Multiple Bodies: Vepar can create more than one Water duplicate as tactical decoys.
- Visual Deception: Enemies can be misled regarding her real location.
- Assassination Support: Clones can draw attention while Vepar approaches a target from a less obvious direction.
- Battlefield Confusion: When used alongside Water covering the terrain, the distinction between Vepar and her constructs becomes tactically more difficult to exploit.

##### Assassination
Assassination Skill: Vepar is an experienced assassin despite explicitly stating that assassination is not her main specialization.[1]
During the conquest used to prove herself to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], she exploited Water control and clone-based diversions to approach the enemy Pillar and kill the ruler directly. Victor specifically praised the dexterity and execution of her strategy as worthy of an Assassin.[1]
- Stealth Tactics: Vepar understands how to move without making herself the focus of a battlefield.
- Misdirection: Water clones and terrain manipulation allow her to disguise her true approach.
- Decapitation / Target Elimination: Vepar successfully killed a superior Pillar and displayed the head to end resistance among the enemy army.[1]
- Opportunistic Killing: She can identify when removing the enemy commander is more efficient than destroying an entire army.

##### Terrain Control
Battlefield Manipulation: Vepar's defining tactical specialty is controlling the physical conditions of a battlefield through Water and later Ice.[1]
Her style focuses on making allied forces more effective rather than merely maximizing her own destructive output.
- Movement Restriction: Saturating or freezing terrain can impede enemy movement.
- Ally Support: Vepar explicitly states that she uses Water to give her soldiers an advantage.[1]
- Environmental Restructuring: Water and Ice allow her to change the terrain instead of simply adapting to whatever battlefield already exists.
- Tactical Preparation: Her abilities become particularly effective when combined with advance planning and troop coordination.
Only applications directly supported by the general mechanics of her demonstrated powers should be listed; highly specific claims such as continent-wide ocean creation or absolute freezing should not be added without further feats.

##### Combat and Technical Skills
Jack-of-All-Trades: Vepar accumulated an enormous variety of practical abilities during her long life in Hell. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] asked how many secondary trades she had learned, she admitted she had lost count.[1]
Victor characterized this as being capable in many areas without having previously mastered one to its absolute peak.
Assassination: Vepar possesses sufficient assassination skill to eliminate a Pillar superior in Rank through proper preparation.[1]
Espionage and Information Gathering: Her survival before Victor depended partly upon monitoring events occurring outside Hell rather than limiting herself to local Demonic politics.[5]
Historical Research: Vepar extensively studied records written by Albu and [[Zahal]], giving her detailed knowledge concerning Hell, ancient Demons and the abilities of former elites such as the Seven Deadly Sins.[6]
Threat Assessment: Vepar's defining survival trait is accurately judging threats. She was the only Pillar among Ranks 40–44 to surrender to Victor before forcing him to kill her, and later repeatedly recognized when battles had become too dangerous for ordinary forces to remain nearby.[7]

##### Leadership and Administration
Natural Leadership: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] considered Vepar an effective leader after observing how successfully she coordinated volunteer Demons during her conquest.[1]
Her soldiers obeyed instructions precisely rather than seeking individual glory, something Victor specifically praised after the battle.
Military Command: Vepar can command large Demonic forces and coordinate soldiers around terrain-control strategies.
Logistical Management: After Victor became King, Vepar and four personally selected Commanders became responsible for the logistics of the enormous Dimension known as Hell.[8]
Hell Administration: Her branch of the government oversaw ordinary administration in addition to supply systems.[8]
Sinner-Hell Administration: Vepar's responsibilities originally extended into the multiple Hells where sinful Souls received punishment, producing enough work that Victor later intended Lily to take direct responsibility for this area.[8]
Mass-Mobilization Logistics: By Chapter 756, Vepar's administrative systems were capable of preparing billions of Demonic Hordes to mobilize upon a single command from Victor.[9]
Organizational Discipline: Vepar maintains reports, expects fellow Generals to remain informed and reprimands carelessness even when dealing with friends such as [[Vine]].[9]
Infrastructure Management: Vepar works directly with Aline and Victor in managing environmental and governmental systems affecting the entirety of Hell.[10]

##### Miasma Regulation
Miasma Management: Vepar possesses enough understanding of Hell's infrastructure and natural Energy systems to work with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and [[Aline Elderblood Valefar]] on regulating the concentration of Miasma throughout the Dimension.[10]
Miasma is both dangerous and necessary: excessive amounts distorted Time and created hostile conditions, while reducing it too much would damage Hell because Miasma functions as the Dimension's equivalent of [[Natural Energy]].
Victor, Vepar and Aline established a new balance that reduced the temporal distortion to roughly one year on Earth for every five years in Hell while preserving sufficient Miasma for Hell's ecosystems and Demon-production systems.[10]
This should be treated as environmental management performed collaboratively rather than evidence that Vepar alone can directly manipulate all Miasma throughout Hell.

##### Knowledge of Demonic Powers
Demonology / Infernal Knowledge: Vepar possesses extensive knowledge concerning the abilities and history of ancient Demons.
Her study of Albu and [[Zahal]]'s historical records allowed her to explain that the Sin of Sloth possessed a passive protection capable of preserving the user's body from impurities and defending it even while asleep.[6]
Neither Lily nor [[Vine]] knew the details, demonstrating that Vepar's knowledge comes from deliberate study rather than merely common Demonic information.

##### Resistances
Miasma Resistance: As a former native High-Level Demon, Vepar can survive extremely dense concentrations of Infernal Energy.
Cold Resistance / Ice Affinity: Vepar's evolved body became so Ice-aligned that ambient temperatures fall around her. Conventional cold therefore poses little meaningful danger to her post-evolution existence.[2]
Demonic Environmental Resistance: Her former Demon physiology allowed her to survive Hell's hostile terrain and Energy conditions naturally.
Draconic Physical Resistance: Her Blood Dragon transformation dramatically strengthened her resistance to physical trauma and supernatural attacks.[4]
Enhanced Soul Durability: Becoming a True Dragon strengthened Vepar's metaphysical foundation and Soul in addition to her physical body.[4]

#### Former Abilities and Characteristics
The following characteristics primarily describe Vepar's former Pillar-Demon existence rather than abilities she necessarily lost outright:
- High-Ranking Demon Physiology
- Demonic Miasma Affinity
- Demonic Regeneration
- Demonic Longevity
- Ars Goetia Rank Authority (Lost after abandoning the Pillar hierarchy)
- Former Rank 42 Political Territory
Her Hydrokinesis, Cryokinesis, Water Cloning, terrain-control techniques and accumulated skills should not be considered lost. Nothing indicates her Blood Dragon transformation removed them.
