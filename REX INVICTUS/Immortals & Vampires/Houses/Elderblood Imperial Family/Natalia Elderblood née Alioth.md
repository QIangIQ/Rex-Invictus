### Appearances

#### Debut
[[Ch03 - Rex Natus I|Chapter 3]]: Violet Snow
You can't hold back a moving current… But that's not entirely the case for an Alioth.
—Natalia Elderblood Alioth explaining her power over Time before restoring [[Alexios Alioth]], Chapter 1077
Nataliana "Natalia" Elderblood Alioth, formerly known as Natalia Alioth, is a former Human Witch of [[Clan Alioth]], the daughter of Alexios Alioth, one of the wives of [[Victor Elderblood]], and a current Blood Dragon Goddess of Space and Time. Originally introduced as the personal maid of [[Violet Elderblood Snow]], Natalia served the [[Snow Clan]] as a transportation specialist, intelligence operative, and trusted household attendant. Her apparent position as an ordinary Human servant concealed an exceptionally rare hereditary ability: the supernatural Eyes of Clan Alioth, which allow their possessors to perceive and manipulate the structure of Space and eventually Time itself. What began as the practical ability to open portals between locations eventually developed into Divine authority capable of influencing planetary evolution, personal timelines, enormous regions of Space-Time, and the temporal development of the [[Elderblood Empire]].[4][8][1][2]
Natalia's importance to Victor's household became apparent long before her transformation into a Dragon. As a Human Witch, she possessed extraordinary talent for Spatial [[Magic]] and repeatedly performed operations that allowed Victor and his companions to circumvent the geographical, political, and dimensional restrictions separating different supernatural territories. Her work included transportation between Earth and [[Nightingale]], negotiations with information brokers, reconnaissance during the Demonic invasion, and the creation of extremely small spatial openings capable of observing conversations without physically entering the target location. Her control was so refined that even her father, an exceptionally experienced Alioth practitioner and one of Vlad's oldest advisers, acknowledged that she had surpassed him in certain applications of the family's hereditary power.[8][9][10]
Her relationship with Victor gradually developed from professional cooperation into personal trust and eventually marriage. Unlike many members of his household, Natalia initially hesitated to abandon her Human race, partly because of her attachment to Clan Alioth and her desire to preserve its unusual hereditary abilities through future generations. She later received Divine Blessings from Victor, underwent the transformation into a True Blood Dragon, and developed from an accomplished Space Mage into a Goddess whose authority encompassed both Space and Time. Her new physiology eliminated the principal physical limitations that had endangered Human practitioners of the Alioth Bloodline, allowing her to utilize temporal powers on a scale that had previously been impossible for her race.[6][11][1]
Natalia subsequently became one of the Elderblood Empire's most important technical specialists. Her accomplishments include helping to restore Alexios' deteriorating existence through a carefully controlled temporal procedure, contributing to the Empire's enormous Time-acceleration projects, creating a highly advanced fused Space-Time Rune, accelerating the geological development of an unfinished planet through billions of years, and stabilizing the surrounding Space-Time during the reconstruction of Mars into the Empire's new capital world. These undertakings established her as an essential contributor to the development of Imperial civilization, rather than simply an exceptionally powerful member of Victor's family.[5][12][13][3]
Natalia and Victor are the parents of [[Norabell Elderblood]], an Imperial Princess who inherited the Alioth family's affinity for Space-Time alongside the extraordinary perception and Draconic characteristics of her father. Norabell eventually developed into a Primordial Goddess associated with Space and Time, possessing Eldritch-influenced characteristics and one of the most powerful forms of inherited Reality perception among Victor's daughters. Their relationship demonstrates the continuing importance of Clan Alioth's ancient Bloodline within the considerably more powerful supernatural foundations of the Elderblood family.[1][7]

### Physical Appearance

#### Human Appearance
Natalia originally possessed the appearance of a beautiful young Human woman with long blonde hair, fair skin, blue eyes, and a comparatively modest figure. She stood approximately 170 centimeters tall and appeared to be somewhere between her early twenties and approximately thirty years of age. Her hair was ordinarily tied into a practical ponytail, while her posture and restrained expressions complemented the professional demeanor expected of Violet's personal maid. Compared with the extraordinarily voluptuous physiques and more conspicuous appearances of the ancient Noble Vampires surrounding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Natalia's original appearance was relatively understated, although she remained noticeably attractive.[4]
Her usual clothing consisted of the professional maid uniform associated with the Snow household. The attire reinforced her apparent position as a servant and concealed the importance of her supernatural heritage from individuals who did not recognize [[Clan Alioth]]. Although Natalia regularly interacted with powerful Vampires and other supernatural beings, her appearance differed little from that of an ordinary young Human woman until she activated the hereditary powers of her Eyes.
The most distinctive feature of her appearance was the Alioth Eyes. While her Eyes originally appeared blue under normal circumstances, their awakened state eventually became visually comparable to miniature galaxies or entire moving cosmoses. When Natalia exercised sufficiently advanced Space-Time techniques, celestial patterns could be perceived within her eyes, reflecting the extraordinary nature of the hereditary ability. The phenomenon became particularly pronounced after her evolution into a Dragon Goddess, when her Eyes were capable of expressing powers far beyond the original spatial techniques employed during her Human life.[1][5]

#### Dragon Goddess Appearance
Natalia's appearance changed dramatically following her transformation into a True Blood Dragon. Her previous Human proportions gave way to a much larger and more powerful Draconic body, while her blonde hair developed a brighter golden appearance. She retained the recognizable features of her former self, but acquired the greater physical presence, supernatural vitality, and Draconic characteristics associated with the members of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s True Dragon family.[5]
During the restoration of Alexios, Natalia appeared in a humanoid Dragon Goddess form measuring approximately 4.5 meters in height. Her appearance was comparable in scale to Victor's own humanoid form at the time, although she was slightly shorter than her husband. Her Eyes displayed the cosmic patterns associated with her fully developed Alioth inheritance, and the combination of her physical transformation and unusual ocular characteristics made her existence unmistakably supernatural even without actively releasing her Divine authority.[5]
As her development continued, Natalia's humanoid form was described as approximately five meters tall, with long golden hair, enormous Draconic wings, and Eyes resembling the Cosmos itself. During her planetary Time-acceleration work, she appeared above an unfinished world and unfolded her wings before manipulating the Time surrounding the planet. Her appearance reflected the enormous difference between the ordinary Human maid who once transported Victor between locations and the Dragon Goddess capable of altering planetary history.[2]
Her Draconic existence is not restricted to these humanoid dimensions. During the later Imperial reconstruction of Mars, Natalia manifested an enormous True Dragon body whose restrained size already exceeded the planet itself. Her unrestricted form was stated to be capable of reaching the scale of a Solar System, demonstrating that her earlier four-and-a-half- and five-meter appearances were controlled humanoid manifestations rather than the natural maximum dimensions of her evolved body.[3]
Natalia can retain smaller and more familiar appearances when interacting with Victor and the other members of the household. Her enormous Draconic dimensions are primarily relevant when undertaking operations requiring an astronomical physical scale or releasing powers whose effects extend across vast environments.

### Personality
Natalia is a calm, intelligent, practical, patient, and highly observant woman whose personality was shaped by her upbringing within [[Clan Alioth]] and her long service to the Snow family. During her Human life, she demonstrated the composure expected of a professional maid accustomed to serving powerful and frequently unpredictable Vampires. Violet's temperament, the conflicts among [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s original wives, and the political complications surrounding [[Nightingale]] demanded considerable discretion, and Natalia consistently handled her responsibilities without becoming overwhelmed by the behavior of the people around her. Her quiet demeanor concealed substantial independence, exceptional professional competence, and a willingness to act decisively whenever she believed the situation required intervention. Unlike individuals who sought recognition through displays of power, Natalia was content to remain outside the center of attention while ensuring that the necessary work was completed efficiently.
Her intelligence is particularly apparent in the creative manner through which she applies her hereditary abilities. Natalia rarely treats a supernatural technique as having only one possible purpose. Her original talent for opening portals was sufficient to make her an indispensable transportation specialist, but she later developed methods of using extremely small spatial openings for surveillance, bypassing restrictions that other Alioth practitioners considered difficult, and eventually manipulating personal timelines with extraordinary precision. Her accomplishments reflect an experimental and analytical mindset rather than reliance exclusively upon overwhelming Energy. This quality became especially important after she obtained a Dragon body, because the scale of her powers increased much faster than her ability to control them safely. Natalia willingly devoted extensive periods to training and experimentation instead of assuming that her new racial foundation automatically made her proficient in every application of Space-Time.[9][1]
Despite her outward seriousness, Natalia possesses a dry, mischievous, and occasionally ruthless sense of humor. She is accustomed to being asked to open portals whenever Victor or his wives require transportation, and her patience does not prevent her from making sarcastic observations about the amount of work entrusted to her. Violet's tendency to complain when other people relied too heavily upon Natalia was particularly amusing because Violet herself frequently used the same services without hesitation. Natalia's humor became more pronounced after her marriage, when she no longer felt the need to maintain the complete formality associated with her former position as a maid. She could tease members of the household, openly complain about unfair treatment, and occasionally embarrass individuals who had once occupied positions of unquestioned authority over her.[9][14]
Her loyalty is genuine but has never been equivalent to unquestioning obedience. Natalia deeply respects her father and the traditions of Clan Alioth, yet she was willing to circumvent spatial restrictions imposed by Alexios when she considered those restrictions harmful to Victor's family. She also chose to remain with Violet and Victor despite the growing political consequences of opposing Nightingale's older institutions. Such decisions demonstrate that Natalia prioritizes the people she trusts over preserving the formal authority of individuals whose positions she recognizes but does not consider infallible. Her relationship with Victor developed partly through this reliability, as he gradually came to regard her as a person upon whom he could depend rather than merely an attendant available to perform transportation.[8]
Natalia possesses considerable pride in her Human heritage. Unlike several members of Victor's household who eagerly pursued transformation into stronger supernatural races, she initially preferred remaining Human and preserving the distinctive identity of Clan Alioth. She understood that her family's hereditary [[Magic]] already provided opportunities for greatly extended life, and she hoped that Victor's unusual ability to interact with Souls could eventually allow them to produce a Human child capable of continuing the Alioth Bloodline. This preference did not originate from an inability to recognize the advantages of Vampiric or Draconic physiology; Natalia simply considered the preservation of her original lineage and personal identity sufficiently important that she was unwilling to abandon them without serious reflection.[6]
Her caution also extends to supernatural phenomena whose consequences remain incompletely understood. When Victor discovered that Natalia possessed a Soul with the quality of a God's Soul despite remaining biologically Human and lacking a corresponding Divine Concept, she did not demand an immediate transformation simply because the anomaly suggested extraordinary potential. She accepted Victor's decision to delay the procedure until he understood its dangers, recognizing that an irreversible change to her spiritual foundation could be far more dangerous than temporarily remaining weaker than the other women in the family.[15]
Natalia's affection toward Alexios is an especially important aspect of her personality. Although she eventually surpassed her father in several applications of Clan Alioth's power, she continued respecting his knowledge and the sacrifices he had made while serving Vlad. When those sacrifices threatened to destroy him, Natalia refused to treat his declining condition as an inevitable consequence of old age or excessive Magic. She developed a complex method of restoring a healthy state from his personal timeline, preserving the life he had already experienced instead of attempting to erase his history. The procedure demonstrated not only her technical development but also the emotional importance of her relationship with the person who had introduced her to the family's ancient supernatural inheritance.[1][5]
Her role as a mother further reinforces her attachment to family. Natalia regards Norabell as her daughter rather than merely an unusually powerful heir or a source of extraordinary Space-Time abilities. Although Norabell inherited some of the most remarkable supernatural characteristics available to Victor's children, Natalia remains connected to the personal and domestic responsibilities of motherhood. The development of her family also contributed to her growing confidence within the Elderblood household, where her earlier identity as Violet's maid eventually became only one part of a much longer personal history.
By the mature Imperial Era, Natalia had become noticeably more playful, affectionate, and outspoken while retaining her original practicality. She addresses Victor with the familiarity of a wife rather than the restraint of a servant, participates in the discussions and arguments of the household, and continues applying her abilities to problems whose scale greatly exceeds anything she encountered during her Human life. Her transformation into a Dragon Goddess altered her supernatural capabilities but did not erase the characteristics responsible for her original importance: reliability, loyalty, intelligence, discretion, and the ability to recognize an effective solution without demanding personal attention or recognition.

### Background

#### Clan Alioth
[[Clan Alioth]] is a Human supernatural family associated with unusually advanced manipulation of Space and Time. Its members possess hereditary Eyes through which they can perceive spatial structures and employ [[Magic]] capable of opening passages between distant locations and supernatural realms. The Bloodline's capabilities distinguish the Clan from ordinary Human Witches, whose magical abilities typically rely upon external techniques and conventional applications of Mana rather than the direct perception and manipulation of the underlying framework of Space.[8][1]
Natalia was born into this family as the daughter of [[Alexios Alioth]], a powerful Human Mage who served as one of [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]]' most trusted advisers. Alexios' position provided the family with considerable importance within [[Nightingale]] despite the kingdom being primarily governed by Noble Vampires. His abilities allowed Vlad to perform operations involving dimensional restrictions, the movement of individuals between locations, and alterations to the flow of Time that would otherwise have been extraordinarily difficult to accomplish.[8]
Unlike her father, Natalia entered the narrative as a relatively young Human woman employed within Violet Snow's household. Her service connected the Alioth family to the [[Snow Clan]] and placed her in regular contact with the supernatural figures who would eventually become the foundation of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction. The position required both considerable discretion and exceptional magical competence, allowing Natalia to develop practical experience far beyond the duties ordinarily associated with a domestic attendant.
Her aptitude for the hereditary Eyes eventually surpassed Alexios in certain applications, even before her Draconic transformation. This disparity demonstrated that the power of Clan Alioth was not determined exclusively by age or accumulated Energy. Natalia possessed an unusual capacity to interpret the possibilities of Spatial Magic and develop applications that her more experienced father had not necessarily pursued.[8]

#### The Mystery of the Alioth Eyes
The true origin of the Alioth Eyes remains uncertain. [[Clan Alioth]] historically regarded them as an exceptional hereditary mutation associated with Human [[Magic]], but this explanation became increasingly inadequate as Natalia's abilities developed toward Divine manipulation of Space and Time. Her Eyes eventually demonstrated characteristics far beyond the limits expected of ordinary Human Witches, particularly after receiving a sufficiently powerful physical and spiritual foundation.[1]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] investigated the anomaly and considered several possible explanations. One involved an ancient God associated with Space-Time whose power may have survived through descendants or supernatural inheritance. Another involved a powerful existence associated with the Higher Sectors whose abilities might have become embedded within the Alioth Bloodline. Victor could not establish either theory as fact, and the precise circumstances responsible for the emergence of the Eyes remained unresolved.[1]
Natalia should consequently not be regarded as a confirmed biological descendant of a Primordial God merely because her Eyes eventually developed extraordinary Conceptual properties. Their origin remains an open question separate from her personally demonstrated achievements and her later acquisition of Divine authority.

#### Life as Violet's Maid
Natalia entered the service of the Snow household before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s transformation into a Vampire. Her responsibilities extended beyond maintaining Violet's personal affairs, as she was also expected to provide supernatural transportation, maintain contact with external collaborators, and conduct sensitive tasks that required discretion. Her reliability made her particularly valuable because the temperaments of Violet and her closest companions frequently created circumstances that ordinary servants could not resolve safely.
Her status as a Human did not prevent her from functioning effectively within a household dominated by Noble Vampires. [[Clan Alioth]]'s hereditary abilities were sufficiently valuable that Natalia could perform tasks for which even considerably stronger supernatural beings possessed no practical substitute. Her ability to create portals between locations allowed the household to travel without depending exclusively upon the transportation systems or political permissions of other organizations.
Natalia's position also exposed her to the political structure surrounding [[Nightingale]]'s royal court. Through Alexios, she understood the authority exercised by Vlad and the importance of Clan Alioth to the monarchy; through Violet, she observed the interests and responsibilities of one of the Kingdom's principal Noble families. This combination of connections eventually became significant when Vlad's political decisions placed members of Victor's household in conflict with the restrictions enforced through Alioth [[Magic]].

### Synopsis

#### The Awakening
Natalia first appeared when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] arrived at Violet Snow's mansion following his transformation into a Vampire. She was one of the household attendants present during his introduction to the supernatural environment and the Noble Vampire society to which Violet belonged. Although Natalia did not initially participate in the principal conversation, Victor's unusual Vampiric perception allowed him to observe the distinctive signs of her Human physiology among the supernatural beings surrounding him. Her relatively ordinary appearance and quiet demeanor contrasted with the increasingly dangerous circumstances developing around her mistress.[4]
As Victor became acquainted with Sasha and Ruby, Natalia continued assisting the household during the rapid changes affecting Violet's domestic life. The arrival of additional women connected to Victor complicated the relationships within the mansion, while Natalia and the other attendants were expected to maintain order despite the possessiveness and unpredictable behavior of the newly formed family. Natalia generally remained composed, allowing the principal participants to resolve their personal disputes while performing the practical tasks necessary to keep the household functioning.
Her responsibilities soon expanded into supernatural information gathering. Victor's concerns regarding hostile Vampires and the activities of external organizations led the household to employ June, an independent Witch whose services included the acquisition of information. Natalia became involved in the arrangements surrounding these investigations, demonstrating that her duties were not restricted to transportation or ordinary domestic work. The ability to contact Witches, exchange sensitive information, and maintain professional discretion made her a useful intermediary between the Snow household and supernatural organizations operating outside the immediate authority of [[Nightingale]]'s Noble Clans.[16]

#### Vampire Society
Natalia's involvement in intelligence gathering increased as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his wives became entangled in the conflict surrounding Clan Horseman and the Inquisition. June's investigation into the rogue Vampire Lucy and his relationship with Karen provided information that could influence several of the family's developing problems. Natalia handled aspects of the transaction, including payments and communication with the Witch, helping the household obtain intelligence without requiring Violet or her companions to personally conduct every negotiation.[17]
The investigation also brought the [[Horseman Clan]]'s activities into greater focus. The information provided by June contributed to the family's understanding of the dangerous supernatural relationships developing around [[Nightingale]]'s Count families, while Natalia's work demonstrated the value of maintaining contacts outside the political networks controlled by the established Vampire nobility.[17]
Natalia subsequently provided transportation for Victor and Violet when the couple arranged a private date in [[Paris]]. The journey required a connection between the Vampire world and Human society, demonstrating the practical importance of Alioth Spatial [[Magic]] to individuals who wished to move between supernatural territories without depending upon ordinary geographical routes. What would otherwise have required extensive preparation could be accomplished through Natalia's direct assistance, allowing Victor's developing family relationships to continue despite the increasing demands of Nightingale's political world.[18]
Her presence within the Snow household also kept her involved in the personal developments surrounding Maria, Kaguya, Luna, and the other attendants associated with Victor's wives. Although she was rarely the center of those conflicts, Natalia developed considerable familiarity with the increasingly unusual structure of the household and continued performing the logistical work necessary to accommodate its members.

#### A New Pillar
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s recognition as the Fifth Vampire Count transformed his political position and expanded the responsibilities of the individuals supporting him. Natalia remained part of the household as its members adjusted to the formation of Clan Alucard and the growing influence of the young Progenitor. Her Spatial [[Magic]] became particularly valuable because the faction now needed to coordinate movement across multiple territories rather than remaining concentrated within the Snow family's established residences.[19]
Natalia accompanied Ruby during the investigation of suspicious activities associated with Clan Horseman and the wider supernatural world. Their search eventually brought them to [[The Lost Club]], a neutral establishment in Brooklyn where supernatural beings from different races could negotiate, exchange intelligence, and conduct transactions outside the ordinary political restrictions of their respective societies. Natalia's presence allowed Ruby to travel discreetly and maintain an effective means of withdrawal if the situation became dangerous.[20]
The visit exposed Natalia to the complicated arrangements through which Vampires, [[Werewolves]], Witches, and other supernatural races maintained contact despite their mutual distrust. Ruby pursued information connected to Victor's enemies, while Natalia provided the transportation and practical assistance required for the operation. Their cooperation demonstrated that the household's strength did not depend exclusively upon direct combatants; intelligence gathering, transportation, and the ability to maintain operational secrecy were becoming equally important.
Natalia's most consequential early political involvement occurred during the restrictions imposed upon [[Nightingale]] following Victor's rapid rise. Vlad relied upon Alexios' Spatial and temporal abilities to isolate the Vampire world and prevent Victor from freely returning. The resulting separation threatened to keep members of Victor's family apart for an extended period, while the manipulation of Time further complicated their attempts to reunite.[8]
Natalia assisted in circumventing the restrictions despite her father's involvement in establishing them. When Vlad later demanded an explanation, Alexios acknowledged that his daughter had surpassed him in certain applications of [[Clan Alioth]]'s power. The admission established Natalia's extraordinary talent and demonstrated that even an experienced Mage entrusted with the Kingdom's dimensional security could be surpassed by the younger generation of his own family.[8]
Her decision also revealed an important personal distinction: although Natalia respected Alexios and understood his responsibilities toward Vlad, she was willing to prioritize the safety and reunion of Victor's family over obedience to restrictions that she considered unacceptable.

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
As [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction expanded, Natalia's transportation services became increasingly indispensable. He regularly requested her assistance when moving between [[Nightingale]], Earth, and other supernatural locations, often employing his customary humor when asking her to open a portal. Although Natalia sometimes found the constant requests exhausting, she continued supporting the faction without abandoning the professional reliability that had originally made her so valuable to Violet.[21]
Her developing Spatial [[Magic]] also proved useful for reconnaissance. Natalia discovered that a portal did not need to be large enough for an entire person to pass through to serve a practical purpose. By opening an extremely small passage near a distant target, she could observe and listen to events without physically appearing at the location. The opening could be made subtle enough to escape the immediate attention of extraordinarily experienced supernatural beings, including [[Scathach]]. Rather than treat the technique as an exceptional achievement, Natalia regarded it as a natural application of the possibilities available through her hereditary Magic.[9]
The method significantly expanded her usefulness beyond transportation. Physical barriers, guarded entrances, and other ordinary means of restricting access became much less effective against an observer capable of placing an almost imperceptible opening within the protected area. Natalia's understanding of Space consequently established her as a valuable intelligence specialist as well as a trusted means of transportation.
Her relationship with Victor also became more personal during this period. When he required transportation to [[WarFall]] during his expedition involving Eleanor, Natalia provided the necessary passage. Before departing, Victor thanked her for the enormous amount of assistance she had repeatedly provided and promised that she could ask him for help whenever she genuinely needed it. The offer reflected the growing trust between them and acknowledged that Natalia's contributions had extended far beyond the duties originally expected of Violet's maid.[22]
Natalia continued accompanying members of the household during their movements between supernatural territories, including journeys involving Victor, [[Nero]], Ophis, Pepper, Lacus, and Leona. Her portals provided a reliable means of avoiding the delays and restrictions associated with ordinary travel, allowing the family to respond rapidly when dangerous circumstances developed elsewhere.[23][24]

#### Demonic War
Natalia's importance became especially apparent when [[Diablo]] launched his invasion of Earth and [[Nightingale]]. While remaining in the territory of Clan Snow alongside Violet, Hilda, and Oda, Natalia used her Spatial [[Magic]] to conduct reconnaissance across major Human regions. Her portals allowed her to investigate the condition of territories separated by enormous geographical distances without requiring a conventional journey through the areas occupied by the Demonic forces.[10]
The reconnaissance revealed the enormous scale of the invasion. Natalia observed major Human regions, including [[New York]] and Russia, where large Demon armies had already spread through populated areas. The environment itself was being altered by Demonic influence, with parts of the Human world beginning to resemble the conditions associated with Hell. During her investigation, Natalia encountered an enormous Dragon-like Demonic creature and withdrew rather than attempt to confront a powerful enemy whose defeat was outside her immediate responsibilities.[10]
Her findings established that the invasion threatened multiple Human territories simultaneously and could not be understood as an isolated attack upon Nightingale or a single supernatural stronghold. Natalia's ability to acquire information from distant locations helped the faction recognize the wider military situation, allowing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his allies to respond with a more complete understanding of the danger confronting the Human world.[10]
Natalia remained connected to the Snow family during the fighting, including the aftermath of Violet's confrontation with the Demon [[Sitri]]. Her continued presence allowed the household to coordinate movements and respond to the changing battlefield without becoming completely dependent upon conventional transportation.[25]
Her importance increased again after Victor became the new King of Hell and was temporarily separated from his wives. The extraordinary dimensional restrictions surrounding Hell prevented ordinary communication with him, leaving the family uncertain about his condition and the possibility of his return. Natalia immediately recognized that Alexios possessed the experience necessary to assist with the problem and brought her father directly to the gathering of Victor's wives and allies.[26]
Alexios employed his advanced Spatial abilities with the Energy provided by [[Jeanne Elderblood]], creating a connection that allowed the family to communicate with Victor despite the barriers separating their realms. Natalia's involvement was essential in bringing the participants together, while her understanding of [[Clan Alioth]]'s Magic allowed her to participate in the discussions surrounding the difficult operation. The success of the connection demonstrated both the practical importance of her relationship with Alexios and the limitations of her own abilities before she developed complete authority over Time.[26]
Natalia subsequently joined Natashia and Kaguya among the backup personnel prepared to support Victor's operation involving Diablo and the [[Key of Hell]]. Her presence reflected the importance of maintaining a reliable means of movement and extraction during a conflict involving powers capable of altering the structure of entire supernatural realms.[27]
By the conclusion of the conflict, Natalia had become an established operational specialist within Victor's expanding faction. Her role encompassed intelligence, transportation, interdimensional communication, and military support, although she still possessed the physical limitations of her Human race.

#### King Among Kings
Natalia's relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] continued developing as the family reorganized following the extraordinary events surrounding Hell and the collapse of several older supernatural political structures. She participated in the gatherings of Victor's wives and closest companions, who discussed the responsibilities and relationships developing within the increasingly extensive household. These discussions eventually established a more organized understanding of the roles occupied by Victor's wives, allowing former attendants such as Natalia to participate directly as members of the family rather than remain subordinate to the positions they originally held.[28][29]
Natalia's marriage to Victor became an established part of her identity. Although she had originally entered the household through Violet, the relationship was now recognized independently, and Victor was explicitly regarded as her husband. This development did not immediately cause Natalia to abandon her Human race or her attachment to [[Clan Alioth]]. She remained interested in preserving the family's supernatural heritage and believed that the unusual properties of her Bloodline might allow her to retain her youth far beyond an ordinary Human lifespan.[6]
The possibility of having children with Victor was especially important to her decision. Natalia hoped that her husband might use his extraordinary relationship with Souls to ensure that a future child could remain completely Human, allowing the Alioth lineage to continue without being assimilated into another supernatural race. Her attitude reflected both affection for Victor and considerable pride in the heritage she had received from Alexios.[6]
Natalia understood that her family's temporal abilities could extend Human life considerably, but the method carried serious risks. Alexios had repeatedly used Time manipulation to sustain his existence and serve Vlad, gradually subjecting his body to supernatural conditions far beyond what ordinary Human physiology could withstand. Natalia recognized that she could avoid many of those consequences by using her powers more conservatively, although maintaining her Human identity would necessarily impose limits upon the development of her abilities.[6][1]
After Victor obtained his new Divine authorities, he granted Natalia three important Blessings associated with Beginning, Nature, and War. The first improved her vitality and supernatural perception, the second strengthened her relationship with Positive [[Natural Energy]], and the third provided additional understanding connected to warfare and its development. These Blessings substantially improved Natalia's physical and spiritual condition, allowing her to employ Spatial [[Magic]] with a level of ease that had previously been impossible for her Human body.[11]
The enhancement also revealed an unexpected anomaly. When Victor examined Natalia's Soul, he discovered that it possessed a quality comparable to that of a God's Soul despite her continuing existence as a Human and the absence of a corresponding Divine Concept. Its structure remained unusually fragile, meaning that the similarity in quality did not establish that Natalia had already become a fully developed Goddess. Victor regarded the contradiction as dangerous and chose not to force an immediate racial transformation without first obtaining a more complete understanding of her condition.[15]
Natalia accepted his caution. Rather than risk destroying the unusual spiritual foundation responsible for her potential, she continued developing her inherited Magic while Victor investigated the possibilities available through his new Divine powers. The decision delayed her transformation but helped preserve the conditions necessary for the more extraordinary development that eventually followed.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Natalia eventually underwent the transformation into the True Blood Dragon Race, fundamentally altering the relationship between her hereditary abilities and her physical body. Her previous Human existence had restricted the quantity and quality of supernatural Energy she could safely manipulate, particularly when attempting advanced temporal techniques. The new Draconic foundation supplied a vastly more powerful body and Soul through which the exceptional abilities of [[Clan Alioth]] could continue developing without the same biological limitations.[1]
The transformation did not immediately grant Natalia perfect mastery over her new powers. Her Spatial abilities had become extraordinarily more powerful, and techniques that had once required manageable quantities of Energy could now produce effects whose scale was difficult to regulate. Natalia therefore devoted an immense period to training, spending approximately two thousand subjective years developing the control necessary to employ her transformed abilities efficiently.[1]
The training eventually produced a fundamental change in her supernatural classification. Natalia's hereditary Eyes began manipulating Time alongside Space, allowing her to develop authority over two closely connected aspects of Reality. Her specialization became so refined that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] recognized her greater efficiency in certain temporal applications. Although he possessed far more raw power, broader Divine authority, and an extraordinary relationship with Reality, Natalia could perform particular Space-Time techniques using substantially less Energy because of the innate precision provided by her Alioth Eyes.[1]
Natalia's development also exposed the inadequacy of the earlier explanations concerning Clan Alioth's origin. Victor considered whether the hereditary Eyes might descend from an ancient Space-Time God or another powerful existence associated with the Higher Sectors, but neither possibility could be confirmed. Natalia's extraordinary capabilities therefore remained an established fact even while the deeper origin of her family's inheritance remained unknown.[1]
During the same accelerated era, Natalia and Victor became parents to a daughter who inherited an extraordinary affinity for Space. The girl's name was not initially disclosed, but her natural talent already resembled Natalia's hereditary specialization. The resemblance established that the Alioth lineage continued within Victor's family despite Natalia's earlier concerns that her transformation into a Dragon might erase its distinctive characteristics.[1]
Natalia's most important personal application of her new authority involved Alexios. Her father had spent an enormous portion of his extended Human life manipulating Time, gradually transforming his body into a condition increasingly dependent upon temporal Energy rather than ordinary biological functions. The prolonged strain threatened his continued existence, creating a crisis that conventional physical healing could not easily resolve.[1]
Natalia refused to accept the deterioration as irreversible. Instead of simply reversing Alexios' entire timeline, which could alter important events and decisions associated with his history, she developed a more selective method. Her plan involved examining the Time belonging to Alexios, identifying a point at which he had possessed a healthy physical condition, and extracting that valid state for restoration within the present without erasing the experiences he had accumulated afterward.[5]
Natalia subsequently appeared before Alexios and Vlad with Victor to perform the procedure. Her fully developed Draconic form and cosmic Eyes demonstrated the extraordinary scale of her transformation, while the nature of the operation attracted considerable attention from Vlad, who recognized the sophistication required to manipulate a personal timeline in such a selective manner. Victor assisted by stabilizing the aspects of Reality necessary for the result to remain coherent, while Natalia's specialized Eyes provided the temporal precision upon which the operation depended.[5]
The procedure succeeded, allowing Alexios to recover from the dangerous condition threatening his continued existence. Natalia regarded the achievement as a personal accomplishment rather than simply another intervention performed by Victor on her behalf. When Vlad expressed his gratitude toward Victor, she playfully questioned why he had not also thanked her, drawing attention to the fact that the former Human maid had become one of the central reasons her father survived.[14]
The restoration also demonstrated how far Clan Alioth's abilities had developed through Natalia. Alexios had spent centuries using Time to prolong his own existence at considerable personal cost; his daughter now possessed the precision and supernatural foundation necessary to repair the consequences without simply negating the life he had lived.

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Natalia's responsibilities expanded from personal transportation and temporal intervention into the infrastructure of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. As [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] prepared to develop his civilization into a power capable of confronting civilizations from the greater Cosmos, he recognized that ordinary technological and supernatural progress would require enormous subjective periods. The Empire therefore began designing an extensive Time-acceleration system intended to provide its population, researchers, military forces, and institutions with the developmental opportunities normally available only to vastly older civilizations.[12]
Natalia was among the principal specialists responsible for the project. Her authority over Time provided the precision necessary to establish and regulate temporal conditions across areas whose scale greatly exceeded any location she had manipulated during her Human life. The undertaking also required Victor's immense Energy reserves, the support of the [[World Trees]], Draconic Runes, technological infrastructure, and the contributions of other members of the Imperial Family. Natalia's role was so important that her expertise became one of the principal foundations upon which the entire program depended.[12]
The original development of the project involved the construction of systems capable of altering the relationship between Time inside the affected territory and Time in the surrounding world. Such an arrangement could allow years, centuries, or millennia to pass inside the Empire while a much shorter interval elapsed elsewhere, providing an extraordinary advantage in scientific development, population growth, military training, and the accumulation of experience.[30][31][32]
Natalia's Runic knowledge also developed significantly during this period. She eventually succeeded in creating a fused Space-Time Rune, combining two extraordinarily complex supernatural principles within a single advanced Runic structure. The achievement attracted Victor's attention because it represented an unusually high level of understanding rather than merely the ability to release enormous quantities of Energy.[13]
Victor later employed the Rune as an example while instructing [[Dun Scaith Elderblood]] in advanced Draconic techniques. He identified Natalia's construction as belonging to the highest levels of Runic development, demonstrating how difficult it was to integrate Space and Time within a stable structure. Even Dun, an exceptionally talented and experienced supernatural practitioner, found the information difficult to process, emphasizing that Natalia's accomplishment was extraordinary within a family already containing numerous powerful Gods, Dragons, and Runic specialists.[13]
The development of the Rune established Natalia as one of the Empire's most important technical authorities. Her mastery of Space-Time was no longer limited to her own Eyes or personally performed spells; she possessed the understanding necessary to express these principles through complex Runic structures, creating opportunities for future systems whose operation could be sustained or reproduced through the broader infrastructure of Imperial civilization.

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Natalia became one of the central participants in the Empire's most ambitious period of accelerated development. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] initiated a temporal undertaking intended to provide approximately 200,000 subjective years of growth while only about one external year elapsed. Natalia controlled the temporal component of the project, while Victor supplied the overwhelming Energy necessary to sustain the affected region and the other Empresses contributed their respective supernatural resources.[33]
The operation required considerably more than placing an isolated room under accelerated Time. The Empire's planets, populations, ecosystems, technological systems, and astronomical structures had to continue functioning while the temporal conditions of the affected region diverged drastically from those outside. Natalia's specialization became essential because the process required the stabilization of vast numbers of interconnected phenomena rather than the simple acceleration of an individual object.[34]
The demands of the project gradually increased as the affected civilization continued developing. Victor supplied the greatest portion of the necessary Energy, while Jeanne assumed an increasingly important role in maintaining the system's stability. Natalia continued regulating the temporal framework through which the enormous developmental advantage was achieved, allowing other members of the ruling family to oversee the Empire's military, scientific, political, and cultural development.[34]
Natalia's authority also provided an alternative method of planetary development. During an investigation of the distribution of [[World Trees]], she examined approximately two hundred Solar Systems and identified worlds possessing World Trees in around eighty of them. The investigation contributed to the Empire's understanding of how natural planetary development and supernatural ecological foundations were distributed across the territories under its control.[2]
She then demonstrated the ability to accelerate the evolution of an unfinished planet. Appearing above the newly formed rocky body, Natalia unfolded her enormous Dragon wings and activated the fully developed Alioth Eyes. Victor supplied supporting Energy while Natalia altered the passage of Time around the planet, causing geological development that would ordinarily require billions of years to proceed within an extraordinarily short period from the perspective of the surrounding environment.[2]
The unfinished world rapidly matured as its own history was accelerated. Natalia maintained control over the temporal conditions until the planet reached the required stage of development, then restored the ordinary flow of Time. The achievement allowed the Empire to transform worlds that were otherwise too young for conventional habitation, providing an alternative to constructing every planetary environment entirely through direct Creation power or waiting for natural geological processes to complete.[2]
Her contribution was particularly significant because a civilization expanding across countless star systems required more than the ability to locate existing habitable planets. With Natalia's temporal authority, suitable planetary candidates could be developed through accelerated natural processes, reducing dependence upon the limited number of environments already possessing mature ecological conditions.
As the Imperial family grew, Natalia also contributed to technologies intended to make spatial storage more convenient for Victor's descendants. Alongside Victor, she provided the Imperial daughters with individual pocket-dimension inventories functioning as private spaces in which they could store their possessions. The system represented a more advanced development of the dimensional storage devices previously available in the supernatural marketplace and allowed the Princesses to maintain access to their belongings without transporting them through ordinary physical means.[35]
Natalia's daughter also became more prominent during this period. [[Norabell Elderblood]] was revealed as the child previously mentioned during Natalia's earlier development, having inherited an extraordinarily powerful affinity for Space and Time alongside the unusual perception associated with Victor's lineage. Although Norabell had already lived for more than one thousand subjective years, Victor continued treating her affectionately as his little girl, using the nickname "little cosmos" because of her exceptional relationship with Space-Time.[7]
Norabell's abilities illustrated the extraordinary potential created by the union of the Alioth and Elderblood lineages. Her Eyes combined Natalia's hereditary Space-Time affinity with Victor's ability to perceive deeper structures of Reality, allowing her to examine the Rules governing artificial dimensions and the Conceptual foundations separating ordinary spatial constructs from more complex independent environments. Her powers were further influenced by her father's Eldritch inheritance, making their eventual development difficult to predict through the characteristics of either parent alone.[7][36]
While receiving instruction from Victor, Norabell initially struggled to create an ordinary dimension because she was attempting to incorporate too many independent Conceptual characteristics into its foundation. Once she understood the distinction between manipulating Space within an existing Reality and constructing an entirely separate system of existence, she successfully produced the simpler dimension. Her progress demonstrated that the younger generation could develop toward applications of Space-Time that differed substantially from the more practical engineering and transportation specialties through which Natalia had originally become important.[36]
Natalia remained part of the family gathering that followed the lesson. During the enormous celestial banquet arranged by Victor, the Emperor reshaped astronomical phenomena into furniture and clothing for the assembled women. Natalia received a dress formed from the fabric of the Cosmos itself, with galaxies visibly incorporated into its appearance. The display reflected the extraordinary scale of the family's current existence, where astronomical structures could be treated as materials for personal artistic expression rather than distant celestial objects.[37]
Her most important later Imperial undertaking involved the reconstruction of Mars into the central populated world of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Victor had decided that [[Yggdrasil]], the original world associated with much of Imperial history, should eventually be preserved as a protected garden rather than continually enlarged to accommodate the Empire's expanding population. Mars offered an alternative location whose existing structure could be replaced and developed without exposing Yggdrasil to the risks associated with the experiment.[3]
Victor summoned Natalia, Anna, and Zaladrac to assist with the project because the operation required different forms of supernatural expertise. Natalia was responsible for stabilizing the surrounding Space-Time, while Anna reinforced the physical structures being transformed. Victor directed the actual planetary reconstruction and the more complex supernatural changes, with Zaladrac subsequently participating in the work. Their cooperation became particularly important because Victor had already committed more than eighty percent of his available strength to the continuing temporal development of the Empire.[3]
During the operation, Natalia and Anna manifested enormous True Dragon bodies that were already larger than Mars despite being deliberately restrained. Their unrestricted forms were described as capable of reaching Solar-System scale, establishing how dramatically Natalia's Draconic existence had developed since the period when her evolved humanoid body stood only several meters tall.[3]
Natalia maintained control over the Space-Time surrounding the reconstruction while Victor expanded Mars into a megaplanet capable of supporting trillions of inhabitants. Its environment was transformed into numerous regions possessing different physical conditions, including areas associated with extreme gravity, magma, cold, and other unusual natural or supernatural phenomena. Victor subsequently established a system for connecting the World Trees distributed throughout Imperial territory through the reconstructed planet, turning Mars into a central junction for communication and transportation across the Empire.[3]
The operation required approximately seven subjective months, but Natalia's temporal control prevented an equivalent period from elapsing outside the isolated construction process. Her participation therefore allowed a massive planetary engineering project to be completed without forcing the surrounding civilization to experience the ordinary passage of Time required for the work. The finished world became the new capital of the Elderblood Empire, while Yggdrasil retained its historical importance as the civilization's original home.[3]
Natalia's involvement in the project represented the culmination of a progression that had begun with the creation of simple portals for Violet's household. Her abilities had become a foundation of Imperial civilization, contributing to planetary engineering, spatial infrastructure, temporal development, and the continued expansion of a society operating on an intergalactic scale. Despite the extraordinary scale of her accomplishments, she remained closely connected to Victor, Alexios, Norabell, and the other members of the family whose relationships had originally motivated her development.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Natalia's husband, the father of her daughter Norabell, and the individual whose influence transformed her from a Human Witch into a Dragon Goddess. Their relationship began when Victor arrived at Violet's mansion and encountered Natalia as one of the household's attendants. Initially, their interactions were principally professional, with Natalia providing transportation and practical assistance while Victor became increasingly involved with the Snow family and the wider supernatural world.[4]
Victor gradually developed considerable trust in Natalia because she repeatedly assisted his family without demanding recognition or personal rewards. Her portals, intelligence gathering, and discretion became important to the growth of his faction, and her willingness to circumvent political restrictions demonstrated that her loyalty was genuine rather than based exclusively upon employment. During his expedition involving Eleanor, Victor explicitly thanked Natalia and promised to help her whenever she genuinely needed assistance, establishing a more personal relationship that continued developing afterward.[22]
Their relationship eventually became romantic and developed into marriage. Natalia was initially reluctant to undergo a racial transformation because she wished to preserve her Human identity and the hereditary characteristics of [[Clan Alioth]]. Victor respected this preference, and when her Soul displayed qualities that he did not fully understand, he refused to subject her to a dangerous procedure simply to increase her power. His caution reflected an important aspect of their relationship: he regarded Natalia's continued existence and well-being as more valuable than the immediate benefits of creating another powerful supernatural subordinate.[6][15]
After Natalia became a Blood Dragon, their abilities developed into an unusually effective combination. Victor possessed overwhelming power, extraordinary Divine authority, and an increasingly sophisticated relationship with the structure of Reality, while Natalia specialized in the precise manipulation of Space and Time. This distinction allowed her to perform certain technical procedures with greater efficiency than Victor could achieve through brute force alone, making their cooperation particularly valuable in operations such as Alexios' restoration and the development of Imperial temporal infrastructure.[1][5]
Their marriage also produced Norabell, whose inheritance combined the supernatural characteristics of both parents. Natalia continued participating in Victor's projects as one of his wives and trusted specialists, eventually becoming an essential member of the teams responsible for the Empire's most ambitious planetary and temporal engineering undertakings.[7][3]

#### Alexios Alioth
[[Alexios Alioth]] is Natalia's father, the historical head of [[Clan Alioth]], and one of Vlad's oldest and most trusted associates. Their relationship is founded upon a shared hereditary power that became increasingly important as Natalia's talents developed beyond those of ordinary Human Witches.[8]
Alexios possessed immense experience in Spatial and temporal [[Magic]], having spent much of his extended life performing extraordinary supernatural services for Vlad. He was responsible for establishing dimensional restrictions intended to isolate [[Nightingale]] during one of the political conflicts involving [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], but Natalia eventually circumvented aspects of those restrictions. When confronted by Vlad, Alexios acknowledged that his daughter possessed greater talent than himself in certain applications of Clan Alioth's abilities, demonstrating his pride in her development despite the practical consequences of her actions.[8]
The difference between father and daughter eventually became more substantial. Alexios remained Human and continued relying upon Time manipulation to prolong his life, while Natalia acquired the far greater physical and spiritual foundation of a True Dragon. The strain accumulated through Alexios' repeated temporal interventions eventually threatened to destroy his existence, forcing Natalia to confront the limitations of the very Bloodline she had inherited from him.[1]
Natalia refused to allow her father to disappear simply because he had overused the family's abilities. She developed a method of selecting a healthy state from his personal timeline and restoring it within the present, avoiding the dangerous consequences of reversing his entire history. Victor supported the procedure, but Natalia's specialized temporal perception was central to its successful execution.[5]
Her playful reaction when Vlad initially thanked Victor rather than her demonstrated that the achievement carried considerable personal importance. Natalia regarded Alexios' restoration as both an act of filial devotion and proof that her understanding of Clan Alioth's abilities had advanced beyond the limitations that had previously endangered her father.[14]

#### Violet Elderblood Snow
[[Violet Elderblood Snow]] was Natalia's original mistress and the person through whom she entered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s life. Natalia served her as a personal maid for years, performing household duties, maintaining external contacts, arranging transportation, and providing magical assistance whenever the Snow family required it.[4]
Their relationship developed considerable familiarity because Natalia was regularly exposed to Violet's possessiveness, impatience, and dramatic behavior. Although she maintained the outward professionalism expected of an attendant, Natalia was not incapable of recognizing the absurdities associated with her mistress. Violet occasionally complained that others relied too heavily upon Natalia's transportation services, despite frequently making comparable requests herself.[9]
Natalia's eventual marriage to Victor transformed their formal relationship. Rather than remaining Violet's subordinate, she became one of her fellow wives and an independent member of the Elderblood family. Their earlier familiarity nevertheless remained part of their personal history, with Natalia continuing to recognize Violet as an important figure in the circumstances that had brought her into Victor's household.

#### Kaguya Elderblood Blank
[[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]] is one of Natalia's oldest companions within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household. Both women originally served members of the family in capacities that concealed the importance of their supernatural abilities. Kaguya specialized in assassination, Darkness, and covert operations, while Natalia specialized in Spatial [[Magic]], transportation, and information gathering.
Their work placed them in regular contact during the early development of Victor's faction, including the period when Natalia helped coordinate the activities of the original household. Their respective abilities complemented one another, particularly when Kaguya's combat and assassination specialties required support from someone capable of transporting individuals or establishing routes between distant locations.
Natalia and Kaguya later became fellow wives of Victor and members of the True Blood Dragon Race, ending the formal distinction between their earlier service positions and their present status within the Imperial Family.

#### Norabell Elderblood
[[Norabell Elderblood]] is Natalia and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s biological daughter and one of the Imperial Princesses of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Her early existence was discussed before her name became publicly established, with Natalia explaining that the girl had inherited an extraordinary affinity for Space resembling her own hereditary ability.[1]
Norabell eventually developed into a Primordial Goddess associated with Space and Time, possessing an exceptional combination of maternal and paternal characteristics. Her Eyes incorporate the Alioth family's relationship with Space-Time alongside the unusually profound perception associated with Victor's lineage, allowing her to examine structures and relationships within Reality that ordinary spatial practitioners cannot easily comprehend.[7]
Although Norabell inherited extraordinary power, her development was not identical to Natalia's. The younger Dragon Goddess possessed additional Eldritch-influenced characteristics originating from Victor, and her attempts to create dimensions demonstrated that she sometimes approached problems through the manipulation of deeper Conceptual structures rather than ordinary Space. Victor personally instructed her in the distinction between creating a dimension inside an existing Reality and attempting to establish an independent environment possessing its own fundamental Rules.[7][36]
Natalia's relationship with Norabell represents the continuation of the Alioth lineage under conditions far beyond those its earlier Human members could have imagined. The hereditary Eyes survived Natalia's transformation into a Dragon and became part of a more powerful inheritance, allowing her daughter to develop abilities whose potential exceeds the original spatial techniques used by [[Clan Alioth]].

#### Vlad Tepes
[[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] was Natalia's father's longtime master and one of the central figures governing the supernatural world in which she was raised. Through Alexios, Natalia possessed a connection to the Royal Court that distinguished her from ordinary Human servants employed within Vampire households.
The relationship became politically important when Vlad relied upon Alexios to manipulate [[Nightingale]]'s dimensional access and temporal conditions. Natalia's decision to help circumvent those restrictions placed her personal loyalties in conflict with the objectives of the Vampire King, although Alexios acknowledged her talent rather than denying that she had surpassed him in the relevant application of Spatial [[Magic]].[8]
Their relationship became more personal during the restoration of Alexios. Natalia and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] performed the procedure while Vlad observed, allowing the Vampire King to witness the extraordinary development of a woman who had once been known primarily as the daughter of his trusted Human adviser. When Vlad initially thanked Victor, Natalia teasingly demanded recognition for her own contribution, demonstrating that her new power and position allowed her to interact with the ancient Progenitor on considerably more equal terms.[5][14]

#### Jeanne Elderblood
[[Jeanne Elderblood]] is one of Natalia's fellow wives and a major contributor to several of the supernatural projects in which Natalia participated. Their cooperation became especially important during the large-scale temporal programs intended to accelerate the development of the [[Elderblood Empire]].
Natalia's specialization allowed her to manipulate and stabilize Time, but operations of such enormous scale also required immense quantities of Energy. Jeanne's extraordinary power and efficiency made her an important participant in sustaining the temporal structure, particularly as the demands of the project increased and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s available strength became progressively more heavily committed.[12][34]
Their cooperation illustrates the highly specialized organization of the Imperial Family, whose members contribute different kinds of supernatural authority to projects that no single individual could perform as efficiently without assistance.

#### Anna Elderblood
[[Anna Elderblood]] is Natalia's fellow wife and one of the most important supernatural specialists involved in the later development of Imperial infrastructure. Their abilities complemented one another during the reconstruction of Mars into the new capital of the [[Elderblood Empire]].
Natalia was responsible for stabilizing the Space-Time surrounding the operation, while Anna provided the physical reinforcement necessary for the planet's structure to survive [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s reconstruction. Both women manifested enormous True Dragon forms during the undertaking, with their restrained bodies already exceeding Mars in size.[3]
Their cooperation allowed Victor to transform the planet without requiring every aspect of the process to be performed through his own increasingly depleted Energy reserves. The project established an important example of the coordination possible among the Empire's most powerful specialists.[3]

#### Clan Alioth
[[Clan Alioth]] is Natalia's original family and the source of her hereditary Eyes. Although she eventually became a True Blood Dragon and joined Clan Elderblood through marriage, her original lineage remains central to her supernatural identity.
Natalia's accomplishments demonstrate that the abilities inherited by Clan Alioth possess considerably greater potential than the ordinary Spatial [[Magic]] for which the family was originally known. Her development from a Human transportation specialist into a Goddess of Space and Time revealed the extent to which the Bloodline could evolve when supported by a sufficiently powerful physical and spiritual foundation.[1]
The inheritance also continued through Norabell, whose unusual supernatural characteristics demonstrate that the Alioth Eyes remain capable of developing within the direct descendants of the Elderblood family. Although the true origin of the Eyes remains uncertain, Natalia's development established Clan Alioth as one of the most extraordinary known Human supernatural lineages associated with [[Nightingale]].

### Abilities

#### Powers and Abilities
Nataliana "Natalia" Elderblood Alioth is a former Human Witch of [[Clan Alioth]], a True Blood Dragon, and a Dragon Goddess of Space and Time whose supernatural abilities are among the most specialized within the Elderblood Clan. Her powers originate from the hereditary Alioth Eyes, an extraordinary ocular Bloodline capable of manipulating the structure of Space and Time. These abilities were subsequently enhanced by three Divine Blessings granted by [[Victor Elderblood]], her awakening as a genuine Goddess possessing the Divinities of Space and Time, and her transformation into a True Dragon whose superior physical and spiritual foundation removed the limitations that had historically restricted Human members of Clan Alioth.[1][2][3]
Unlike supernatural beings who possess numerous unrelated Divinities, Natalia concentrates her development upon two closely connected Concepts. Her specialization provides exceptional precision and efficiency when manipulating Space-Time, allowing her to accomplish certain operations with considerably less Energy than Victor would require despite his overwhelmingly greater overall strength and wider collection of Divine authorities. Her advanced abilities extend far beyond conventional teleportation and Time acceleration, encompassing the manipulation of personal timelines, temporal-state extraction, localized Reality alteration through Space-Time, astronomical-scale temporal fields, and the creation of complex Draconic Runes incorporating both Concepts.[3][4][5]
Natalia originally served as the personal maid of [[Violet Elderblood Snow]], using Spatial [[Magic]] to transport members of the household, connect different supernatural territories, conduct reconnaissance, and bypass geographical restrictions. Even during her Human life, she possessed sufficient talent to surpass [[Alexios Alioth]], her father and the former head of Clan Alioth, in certain applications of their hereditary powers. Her development eventually made her an indispensable specialist in the construction of the [[Elderblood Empire]], whose enormous temporal engineering projects rely upon her ability to control Time across astronomical distances.[6][7][8]
Among her greatest accomplishments are the restoration of Alexios through the technique Time Cocoon, the creation of a highest-level Rune combining Space and Time, participation in the Galactic Time-acceleration ritual, the acceleration of billions of years of planetary evolution, the creation of private pocket-dimension inventories alongside Victor, and the stabilization of Space-Time during the reconstruction of Mars into the Imperial capital. These accomplishments establish Natalia as an exceptionally accomplished specialist in both supernatural engineering and Conceptual manipulation, although several of her largest feats required external Energy and cooperation with other members of the Imperial Family.[4][5][9][10][11]

#### Current Physiology

##### Blood Dragon Goddess Physiology
Natalia underwent a fundamental racial transformation when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] reconstructed her former Human existence into that of a genuine True Blood Dragon. Her inherited Alioth abilities were preserved rather than replaced, allowing the unusual ocular Bloodline to continue developing within a vastly more powerful Draconic body. This transformation was especially significant because the principal obstacle facing Human Alioth practitioners was not necessarily a lack of knowledge or magical talent, but the inability of mortal flesh to withstand the immense Energy and temporal strain required for higher-level applications of their powers.[12][3]
The transformation initially increased Natalia's Spatial abilities so dramatically that she required extensive training to control them. During the following two thousand subjective years, she devoted much of her time to mastering her new powers and adapting to the superior physical foundation of a True Dragon. Her later development as a Goddess of Space and Time demonstrates the extraordinary compatibility between the Blood Dragon Race and [[Clan Alioth]]'s previously restricted supernatural potential.[3]
- Blood Dragon Physiology: Natalia possesses the supernatural characteristics of Victor's Blood Dragon lineage, combining the fundamental qualities of Vampires and [[True Dragons]] within a new racial foundation.[12][3]
- True Dragon Physiology: Natalia possesses a genuine Draconic body capable of supporting extraordinary quantities of supernatural Energy and the increasingly powerful manifestations of her Space-Time authority.[3]
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics: Natalia possesses the greatly enhanced strength, speed, durability, agility, stamina, reflexes, and physical resilience associated with True Dragon physiology. Her present body is fundamentally superior to the Human physiology that originally restricted her supernatural powers.[12][3]
- Immense Strength: Natalia possesses the physical strength of a True Dragon Goddess. Although her individual maximum striking power has not been established through a dedicated combat demonstration, her current physiology greatly exceeds the limits of her former Human body.[3]
- Immense Durability: Natalia's Draconic body possesses extraordinary durability and can tolerate the supernatural conditions associated with high-level Space-Time manipulation. Her transformation removed the principal biological vulnerability that had previously endangered Alioth practitioners.[3]
- Immense Speed: Natalia possesses the extraordinary physical capabilities of a True Dragon, further complemented by her independent ability to manipulate Space and Time. Her combat and movement speed should be distinguished from instantaneous transportation performed through portals.[12][3]
- Enhanced Reflexes: Natalia's Draconic physiology provides substantially greater reaction speed and physical coordination than her original Human body.[12]
- Enhanced Stamina: Natalia can sustain prolonged supernatural activity through a more powerful physical foundation and considerably greater Energy capacity.[3]
- Enhanced Senses: Natalia possesses the heightened sensory characteristics associated with True Dragons, supplemented by the exceptional perception provided by her hereditary Eyes.[3]
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Natalia possesses the racial regenerative characteristics of [[Blood Dragons]]. Her individual maximum regeneration has not been demonstrated through a separate recovery feat.[12]
- Extreme Longevity: Natalia no longer possesses the ordinary lifespan limitations of a Human Alioth. Her Dragon physiology provides a foundation compatible with the extraordinary longevity associated with True Dragons.[3]
- Enhanced Energy Capacity: Her Draconic body can contain and channel substantially greater quantities of supernatural Energy than the Human body she originally possessed.[2][3]
- Enhanced Energy Tolerance: Natalia can perform advanced Space-Time operations without encountering the same immediate biological restrictions that previously threatened Human members of Clan Alioth.[3]
- Enhanced Spiritual Foundation: Natalia's abnormal Soul quality is now supported by her reconstructed True Dragon existence, allowing her supernatural development to progress beyond its former Human limitations.[2][3]
- [[Draconic Energy]] Compatibility: Natalia's Draconic body supports the supernatural Energy associated with her racial transformation while retaining her hereditary Spatial powers and later Divine authority.[12][3]
- Divine-Draconic Integration: Natalia's Divinities and Alioth Eyes operate through the same evolved existence rather than remaining restricted by an incompatible mortal body.[3]
- Flight: Natalia possesses enormous Draconic wings and can fly freely through planetary and astronomical environments. She unfolded her wings while accelerating the geological evolution of an unfinished planet.[9]

- Draconic Eyes: Natalia possesses the ocular characteristics of a True Dragon, although the Alioth Eyes remain her distinctive personal visual power and take precedence during advanced Space-Time techniques.[3]

- Draconic Metamorphosis: Natalia can alter her physical form to express different levels of her Draconic existence, including a humanoid Goddess appearance and an enormous True Dragon body.[4][11]

- Gigantic True Dragon Form: Natalia's later True Dragon body was already larger than Mars while deliberately restrained. Her unrestricted form was described as capable of reaching Solar-System scale.[11]

##### Divine Physiology
Natalia eventually became a genuine Goddess of Space and Time, developing authority over both Concepts rather than relying exclusively upon the inherited Witch [[Magic]] of [[Clan Alioth]]. Her Divine nature is particularly specialized because almost all of her supernatural development is concentrated upon the perception and manipulation of Space-Time. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] recognized that this specialization allowed her to perform certain temporal operations more efficiently than he could, despite his possession of greater overall Energy and substantially broader authority.[3]
- God Physiology: Natalia possesses the supernatural characteristics of a Divine being, including [[Divine Energy]] and a spiritual foundation capable of sustaining genuine Divine Concepts.[3]
- Divinity of Space: Natalia possesses Divine authority over Space, allowing her to manipulate spatial structures through more than conventional Witch Magic.[3]
- Divinity of Time: Natalia possesses Divine authority over Time, permitting advanced temporal manipulation beyond the ordinary techniques of Human Alioth practitioners.[3][9]
- Divine Energy Manipulation: Natalia can utilize the supernatural Energy associated with her Divine existence to perform advanced Space-Time techniques.[3]
- Divine Concept Manipulation: Natalia can interact directly with the Concepts of Space and Time through her Divine authority and hereditary Eyes.[3][4]
- Divine Perception: Natalia can perceive Divine and Conceptual phenomena through her developed spiritual foundation and the enhancement previously provided by Victor's Blessing of Beginning.[1]
- Specialized Conceptual Authority: Natalia's concentration upon two interconnected Concepts provides exceptional precision and efficiency compared with a broader but less specialized application of equivalent power.[3]
- Advanced Temporal Efficiency: Victor acknowledged that Natalia could perform certain manipulations of Time more efficiently than he could because her Eyes and specialized development were naturally suited to the task.[3]
- Space-Time Divine Integration: Natalia can employ Space and Time as related Divine principles, allowing the development of techniques that manipulate both simultaneously.[4][5]
- Divine-Draconic Synergy: Natalia's Divine authority operates through a True Dragon body whose physical and spiritual capacity is far greater than that of her original Human existence.[3]
Natalia had not completely mastered either Divinity at the time Victor examined her development in Chapter 1068, and the full potential of the Alioth Eyes remained inaccessible even after approximately two thousand years of training. Her subsequent achievements establish substantial further growth, but no statement has confirmed that she possesses absolute authority over all Space and Time throughout existence.[3]

##### God-Quality Soul
Natalia possessed an extraordinary spiritual foundation before acquiring genuine Godhood. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] examined her while she remained Human, he discovered that her Soul possessed the quality of a God's Soul despite lacking the Divine Concept normally associated with a fully developed God. The contradiction was sufficiently unusual that Victor delayed her transformation into a Dragon rather than risk destabilizing a spiritual foundation whose true origin and developmental potential remained unknown.[2]
- Abnormally High Soul Quality: Natalia's Soul possessed Divine-level quality despite her original Human classification.[2]
- Pre-Divine Spiritual Potential: Her abnormal Soul existed before she formally developed genuine Divinities over Space and Time.[2][3]
- Exceptional Spiritual Compatibility: Natalia's Soul possessed an unusually strong relationship with the powers inherited through [[Clan Alioth]], allowing extraordinary development once her physical restrictions were removed.[2][3]
- Spiritual Development: Natalia's later transformation into a True Dragon provided a foundation through which her abnormal Soul could continue developing without the former limitations of Human physiology.[3]
Her original Soul quality did not mean she had already become a complete Goddess. Victor specifically distinguished the quality of her Soul from the possession of an established Divine Concept, and the spiritual structure remained unusually fragile before her transformation.[2]

#### Eyes of Space-Time
The Alioth Eyes are Natalia's most distinctive hereditary ability and the supernatural foundation of [[Clan Alioth]]'s extraordinary relationship with Space and Time. Although the wiki designation [[Eyes of Space-Time]] accurately describes their developed functions, Natalia did not formally adopt the phrase as the definitive name of her power. She considered a name associated with Space and Time but regarded it as excessively dramatic, preferring the traditional identification of the Eyes belonging to the Alioth Clan Leader.[13]
The Eyes possess an appearance unlike conventional ocular abilities. When fully awakened, Natalia's eyes resemble continuously changing galaxies or miniature Cosmoses, with celestial patterns replacing their ordinary appearance. Their supernatural functions extend beyond enhanced vision, allowing Natalia to perceive and interact with the spatial and temporal structure of Reality. This connection became progressively more powerful after her transformation into a True Dragon, as her new body allowed her to exploit a much greater portion of the Bloodline's potential.[3][4]

##### Conceptual Efficiency
The Alioth Eyes are particularly valuable because they reduce the difficulty of manipulating phenomena that would otherwise require overwhelming quantities of [[Divine Energy]]. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] possessed sufficient power to perform extremely advanced temporal interventions, but the breadth of his authority did not provide the same specialized efficiency that Natalia achieved through her Eyes. This distinction is especially important when a technique requires extreme precision rather than merely a greater quantity of Energy.[3]

##### Rule Circumvention
Natalia's Eyes possess the unusual ability to interact with certain Rules of Reality in ways unavailable to ordinary Space-Time practitioners. While explaining the restoration of Alexios, Natalia acknowledged the conventional analogy of Time as a river whose flow cannot simply be stopped or reversed without consequences. However, she also explained that members of [[Clan Alioth]] possess Eyes allowing them to violate certain restrictions that would otherwise prevent the manipulation of historical states.[4]
- Rule Circumvention: The Alioth Eyes allow Natalia to bypass certain conventional Rules governing Space-Time through specialized applications of her power.[4]
- Temporal Rule Circumvention: Natalia can retrieve a past state and establish it within the present despite the ordinary progression of Time making the earlier condition no longer naturally valid.[4]
- Limited Reality Bending: Natalia can alter an existing condition of Reality through Space-Time manipulation, as demonstrated by the restoration of Alexios.[4]

These effects originate from Natalia's Space-Time authority and hereditary Eyes. They do not establish that she possesses a separate unrestricted Divinity of Reality or absolute control over Causality.

##### Origin and Development
The true origin of the Alioth Eyes remains unknown. [[Clan Alioth]] originally regarded them as an extraordinary hereditary mutation connected to [[Magic]], but their later development suggested a much more complicated supernatural foundation. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] considered the possibility that they originated from a deceased Primordial God of Time or another powerful existence associated with the Upper Sectors. However, he explicitly could not establish either explanation as fact.[3]
Even Victor, Alexios, Natalia, Albedo, and Jeanne lacked sufficient knowledge to conclusively explain the Eyes' origin. Their extraordinary potential is therefore established through direct demonstrations rather than through a confirmed connection to an unidentified ancient deity.[3]

#### Space Manipulation
Space was Natalia's original specialty and remains one of her principal Divine Concepts. During her Human life, the Alioth Bloodline allowed her to open portals, connect supernatural territories, and perform operations involving the direct manipulation of spatial relationships. Following her transformation into a True Dragon Goddess, these abilities developed into more advanced forms of spatial control capable of supporting astronomical engineering and the infrastructure of the [[Elderblood Empire]].[6][3][11]

##### Portal Creation
Portal Creation is Natalia's most frequently demonstrated early ability. She can open passages connecting two separate locations, allowing individuals and objects to move directly between them without crossing the intervening distance through ordinary physical movement. The versatility of this technique made Natalia indispensable to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction long before she acquired Divine authority, as it allowed the household to bypass enormous geographical distances and move between supernatural territories whose ordinary entrances were difficult or dangerous to access.[7]
- Portal Creation: Natalia can establish supernatural gateways connecting separate points in Space, allowing movement between them.[7]

- Teleportation: Natalia can transport herself and others across distances by opening passages connecting the intended locations.[7]
- Instantaneous Transportation: Individuals can enter a portal and emerge at another location without conventionally crossing the intervening distance.[7]
- Long-Distance Transportation: Natalia can connect locations separated by enormous geographical distances, allowing rapid travel across countries and other large territories.[7]
- Interworld Transportation: Natalia's portals can connect Earth, [[Nightingale]], and other supernatural environments when their locations are accessible through her [[Magic]].[6][7]
- Dimensional Transportation: Natalia can create passages connecting separate supernatural realms and dimensional environments, subject to the restrictions of the destination and the conditions surrounding the passage.[14]
- Group Teleportation: Natalia can transport multiple individuals through the same spatial gateway.[1]
- Mass Transportation: Natalia created an enormous portal through which large groups and substantial quantities of material could pass, demonstrating greatly enhanced capacity following Victor's Blessings.[1]
- Large Portal Creation: Natalia can create gateways substantially larger than ordinary personal transportation portals, including those suitable for the movement of extensive groups and large objects.[1]
- Rapid Portal Creation: Natalia became sufficiently experienced that opening a portal toward an ordinary known destination required comparatively little effort.[7]
- Portal Size Control: Natalia can regulate the dimensions of a portal, creating both large transportation gateways and extremely small openings suitable for observation.[7][1]
- Portal Positioning: Natalia can position a portal at a designated location when she possesses sufficient spatial information concerning the destination.[7]
- Portal Maintenance: Natalia can sustain spatial gateways long enough to accommodate the movement of individuals or groups.[1]
- Spatial Escape: Natalia can withdraw from dangerous environments through portals rather than depending entirely upon her own physical movement.[15]
- Transportation Support: Natalia can provide movement and extraction capabilities for allies whose individual powers are not suited to crossing the required spatial or dimensional barriers.[14][16]

##### Spatial Coordinates
[[Clan Alioth]]'s portal techniques do not ordinarily allow unrestricted transportation toward any arbitrarily imagined destination. The practitioner requires sufficient information concerning the target location, including coordinates or an established understanding of its spatial position. This restriction became particularly important when Natalia discussed the limitations of her inherited [[Magic]] before acquiring complete Divine authority.[17]
- Spatial Coordinate Recognition: Natalia can utilize spatial information to identify destinations suitable for portal creation.[17]
- Coordinate-Based Teleportation: Natalia can open passages toward locations for which she possesses adequate spatial information.[17]
- Known-Destination Transportation: Natalia can reliably connect familiar locations or destinations whose coordinates are known to her.[17]
- Spatial Destination Analysis: The precision of her transportation techniques depends upon correctly identifying and connecting the intended destination.[17]
A completely unknown destination cannot ordinarily be reached through a reliable portal without obtaining the necessary spatial information. Natalia's later Divine development greatly expanded her abilities, but no demonstrated technique has established that this fundamental requirement can be ignored under every possible circumstance.

##### Micro-Portal Manipulation
Natalia discovered that portals could be reduced to extremely small dimensions and used for purposes entirely different from ordinary transportation. By opening an almost imperceptible passage near a distant target, she could observe conversations and events without physically entering the location. Her control was sufficiently precise that an opening could remain unnoticed even in the presence of an exceptionally experienced supernatural combatant such as [[Scathach]].[7]
- Micro-Portal Creation: Natalia can create spatial openings small enough to escape ordinary attention.[7]
- Spatial Clairvoyance: Natalia can observe distant locations through a small portal, acquiring visual information without being physically present.[7]
- Remote Viewing: Natalia can use a spatial passage as an observation point connecting her current location with another environment.[7]
- Spatial Clairaudience: Natalia can listen to conversations through a portal connecting her with the target location.[7]
- Remote Hearing: Sound and conversational information can be acquired through her spatial openings without requiring physical proximity.[7]

- Spatial Espionage: Natalia can gather intelligence through concealed spatial connections, bypassing the need to enter guarded locations directly.[7]
- Remote Intelligence Gathering: Natalia can acquire information from distant locations through spatial surveillance rather than relying exclusively upon external informants.[7]
The concealment of Natalia's observation portals is exceptional but not absolute. The incident establishes that Scathach did not immediately detect the particular opening, rather than proving that every conceivable sensory ability or spatial authority is incapable of noticing it.

#### Time Manipulation
Time became Natalia's second principal Divinity after her Draconic evolution. Although [[Clan Alioth]]'s hereditary Eyes had always possessed the potential for temporal manipulation, Human users were restricted by the enormous strain associated with changing the passage of Time. Alexios prolonged his existence through temporal techniques, but every sufficiently dangerous use threatened the finite lifespan sustaining his body. Natalia's True Dragon transformation removed much of that biological restriction, allowing her to develop increasingly advanced applications of Time without suffering the same mortal deterioration.[18][3]
Her temporal abilities encompass localized Time dilation, accelerated subjective development, the manipulation of personal temporal histories, the retrieval of past states, advanced Space-Time isolation, and the regulation of Time across astronomical environments. These applications possess different technical requirements and should not be reduced to a single undifferentiated ability.[2][4][8]

##### Time Acceleration
Natalia can accelerate the passage of Time for a selected individual or region, causing the affected target to experience a longer interval than the surrounding environment. Her abilities developed sufficiently that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] could spend approximately five subjective months with his wives while only a single day elapsed outside the accelerated region. This feat occurred after Natalia received Victor's three Blessings, before her later development into a fully established Dragon Goddess.[2]
- Chronokinesis: Natalia can manipulate the passage of Time through her hereditary Alioth powers and later Divine authority.[2][3]
- Time Acceleration: Natalia can increase the rate at which Time passes for a selected target relative to the surrounding environment.[2][9]
- Localized Time Dilation: Natalia can create differences between the passage of Time inside an affected region and the ordinary passage of Time outside it.[2]
- Subjective Time Expansion: Natalia can provide individuals with a longer subjective period of activity while considerably less external Time passes.[2]
- Temporal Rate Manipulation: Natalia can modify the relationship between internal and external Time, allowing a target to progress through events at a different rate from its surroundings.[2]
- Five-Month Time Compression: Natalia enabled approximately five months to pass for Victor and his wives while only one day passed outside the affected environment.[2]
- Accelerated Training Environments: Natalia's Time manipulation can create conditions suitable for lengthy training and development without requiring an equivalent period to elapse externally.[2][8]
- Temporal Development Support: Natalia can use accelerated Time to assist the growth and development of individuals, organizations, and environments.[2][9]
- Temporal Field Regulation: Natalia can control the rate at which Time progresses within a designated field, an ability essential to her later Imperial projects.[8]

##### Temporal Stasis
Natalia can control Time to the extent necessary to isolate large-scale operations from the ordinary progression of the surrounding environment. During the reconstruction of Mars, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] selected her specifically to stop or control the Space-Time surrounding the participants, allowing the work to proceed without an equivalent period elapsing outside the affected region.[11]
- Temporal Stasis: Natalia can establish conditions in which the ordinary passage of Time is effectively suspended relative to an ongoing operation.[11]
- Time Stop: Natalia's role during the Mars reconstruction included stopping Time around the working environment while the participants continued their activities under controlled conditions.[11]
- Localized Temporal Isolation: Natalia can separate the temporal progression of a designated area from the surrounding environment.[11]
- Operational Time Lock: Natalia can maintain the temporal conditions necessary for a complex project to progress without forcing the surrounding civilization to experience the same elapsed Time.[11]
- External-Time Separation: Natalia can prevent an operation's subjective duration from corresponding to the passage of Time outside the affected region.[2][11]
This establishes controlled temporal isolation rather than proof that Natalia can indiscriminately stop all Time throughout an entire Universe without limitation.

##### Past Access

Natalia's powers possess the potential to alter established temporal histories. Natalia's Eyes make such techniques considerably easier for her than for less specialized beings, although the consequences of interfering with established history remain dangerous.[3]

##### Temporal-State Manipulation
One of Natalia's most important techniques involves retrieving a valid historical condition and applying it to the target's present existence. This differs from simply reversing Time, because an ordinary reversal could alter the events occurring between the chosen moment and the present. Natalia instead uses her specialized Eyes to identify a historical state, isolate it, and maintain the desired condition without necessarily destroying the intervening history.[4]
- Temporal-State Manipulation: Natalia can manipulate the condition of a target through the historical Time belonging to that individual.[4]
