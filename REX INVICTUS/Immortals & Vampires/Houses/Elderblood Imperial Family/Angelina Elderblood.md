### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 991
Ara, of course I came to look for my beloved Daddy... Too bad I didn't find him here.
—Angelina to [[Gina Elderblood]], Chapter 991
Angelina Elderblood is an Imperial Princess of the [[Elderblood Empire]] and the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Annasthashia Elderblood Fulger]]. Born directly into Victor's second generation, she is a natural Blood Dragon and True Dragon who inherited the defining Lightning of the [[Fulger Clan]] from her mother.[1]
Angelina strongly resembles a younger Annasthashia both physically and temperamentally. She inherited her mother's blonde Fulger appearance, extreme attachment toward Victor, mischievous personality and willingness to use cunning when necessary. Despite her small appearance at her debut, she belongs to the exceptionally rare True Dragon race and possesses the inherited protections and adaptive traits Victor placed within his daughters.[1][2]
A later chapter introduces Ekaterina Natasha Elderblood as a daughter of Victor and Annasthashia. Because Angelina had already been explicitly established as their daughter, and the later character is again presented as a blonde, violet-eyed member of the Fulger branch without any independent history separating her from Angelina, this wiki treats Ekaterina Natasha Elderblood as a continuity-error name for Angelina rather than as a separate daughter.[3]

### Physical Appearance
At her debut, Angelina is approximately 120 centimeters tall. She has pale skin, blonde hair arranged into twin tails and violet eyes inherited from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[1]
Her appearance strongly resembles a younger Annasthashia, particularly through her blonde Fulger features and mischievous expressions. Her small humanoid form is misleading, as Angelina is a genuine True Dragon whose apparent physical age does not accurately represent either her chronological age or supernatural development.[1]
When Angelina becomes serious, her expression can change considerably. During her confrontation with [[Gina Elderblood]], her violet eyes become completely lifeless and are compared to two violet black holes seemingly capable of swallowing everything.[1]
Chapter 1165 later describes the Fulger daughters gathered together as possessing blonde hair and the violet eyes characteristic of Victor's children. The character named Ekaterina Natasha Elderblood appears among this group and is identified as Annasthashia's daughter, further matching Angelina's established lineage and appearance.[3]

### Personality
Angelina is mischievous, cunning, shameless, affectionate, adventurous and intensely attached to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Much of this behavior is explicitly attributed to Annasthashia, with the narration noting that Angelina inherited her mother's madness toward Victor almost completely.[1]
Her attachment to her father extends even to ordinary belongings associated with him. Angelina enters Victor's private quarters hoping to find him and, when he is absent, steals one of his shirts. When Gina discovers what happened, Angelina first attempts innocent denial before realizing that continuing to lie will not resolve the situation.[1]
Angelina is capable of thinking beyond an immediate advantage. She knows she could use Fulger Lightning to escape from Gina, but correctly concludes that fleeing would only postpone the confrontation because they live together. Instead, she changes tactics and exploits Gina's own attachment toward Victor, offering her Victor's shirt and eventually a photograph of him sleeping in exchange for forgetting the incident.[1]
Her mischievous behavior therefore does not imply a lack of intelligence. Angelina quickly reads other people's motivations, adapts when one tactic fails and prefers solutions that permanently resolve a problem rather than merely delaying it.
The older Fulger women deliberately cultivate these qualities while also teaching her when they should and should not be used. [[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]] teaches Angelina that cleverness should not become an excuse for refusing responsibility around family. Annasthashia emphasizes that this restraint does not apply to genuine enemies: if someone stronger threatens Angelina, any deceptive or dishonorable tactic is acceptable if it allows her to survive. [[Victoria Fulger Elderblood]] similarly tells her to improvise rather than blindly follow rigid rules.[1]
Angelina nevertheless points out a practical problem with this training: Victor is so protective of his daughters that he is unlikely to willingly allow them to enter genuinely dangerous situations. The older women are forced to admit that she has a point.[1]

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Angelina belongs to the enormous second generation of the Elderblood family produced after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ascension and the accelerated development of his territories. Unlike Annasthashia and the original Fulger women, Angelina was not born as a Noble Vampire who later became a Dragon. She entered life directly as one of Victor's Draconic descendants, allowing the [[Fulger Bloodline]] to exist from birth inside a True Dragon body.[1]
Angelina properly debuts in Chapter 991 when [[Gina Elderblood]] wanders through the Elderblood residence looking for Victor. Gina eventually reaches the area surrounding Victor's private bedroom and encounters Angelina.[1]
Angelina is approximately 120 centimeters tall, with blonde twin tails and violet eyes. Despite the innocent appearance suggested by both her body and name, the narration immediately notes that she has inherited Annasthashia's obsession with Victor and is far from the stereotypical image of a harmless "angel."[1]
Angelina claims that she entered Victor's private room solely because she wanted to find her beloved father. Gina quickly becomes suspicious and detects Victor's scent around Angelina. The scent is too weak to indicate direct recent contact with Victor and instead resembles the residual scent left upon one of his possessions.[1]
Gina demands that Angelina return what she took. Angelina initially pretends not to understand, but Gina refuses to abandon the issue. Angelina's expression briefly becomes lifeless and threatening while she evaluates her options.[1]
Because she inherited the Lightning of the [[Fulger Clan]], Angelina believes she could simply accelerate away and leave Gina behind. She rejects the idea because it would solve nothing; Gina would still live beside her and eventually confront her again.[1]
Angelina instead reaches into her dimensional-storage artifact and produces one of Victor's shirts. Gina remains unconvinced that this is everything Angelina possesses, forcing her sister to reveal a second item: a photograph of Victor sleeping that Angelina copied from Annasthashia's collection.[1]
Angelina admits that she had tried to locate the more private photographs hidden by her mother but had been unable to discover them. The combination of Victor's shirt and the photograph destroys Gina's resistance, allowing Angelina to bargain her way out of the confrontation.[1]
Her escape is interrupted by the arrival of [[Annasthashia Elderblood Fulger]], [[Natasha Elderblood Fulger]], [[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]] and [[Victoria Fulger Elderblood]]. The women immediately understand what occurred, and Victoria's reaction implies that Angelina has developed a reputation for getting involved in troublesome schemes and potentially dragging her sisters into them.[1]
Angelina attempts to redirect some of the responsibility, prompting Carmila to explain that intelligence and cunning are useful but should not replace accountability when dealing with family. Annasthashia then gives Angelina the opposite lesson for enemies: if she ever faces someone sufficiently powerful to threaten her, she should use every possible method to survive rather than foolishly dying for the sake of honor. Victoria reinforces the point by teaching her to improvise according to the situation rather than allowing fixed rules to restrict her choices.[1]
Angelina accepts the lesson but questions how likely such situations are when Victor barely allows his daughters to leave the security of his territories. Her elders reluctantly recognize the validity of the argument.[1]
Once the Fulger women announce that Victor should be returning soon, Angelina and Gina immediately abandon their argument and race toward the daughters' room. Angelina takes the lead specifically through the speed granted by her inherited Lightning.[1]
Angelina later participates in one of Victor's War Games alongside several of her sisters. The daughters operate within an extremely cold snowy environment without being meaningfully impaired by the temperature, demonstrating the environmental resilience of their Draconic bodies.[2]
Victor eventually reveals an Ice Dragon concealed within the area. When the creature appears, Angelina immediately distinguishes it from herself and her sisters, observing that it is a Dragon but not a True Dragon like them. Her statement directly confirms her status as part of the highest Draconic lineage descending from Victor.[2]
The same period explains that Victor's daughters inherited portions of his adaptive characteristics and were deliberately given multiple protections against indirect methods of attack. Victor had no intention of allowing enemies to bypass their physical strength merely through possession, Soul interference, parasitic organisms or similar abilities.[2]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Angelina and the rest of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s immediate family subsequently experienced the enormous accelerated development of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. The Empire eventually accumulated approximately 200,000 years of internal development, causing members of the family who remained within the accelerated region to become vastly older subjectively than their earlier appearances suggested.[4][5]
By Chapter 1164, the True Dragon race remained extraordinarily rare despite the vast scale of Victor's civilization. Counting the Imperial Family, only approximately forty to fifty [[True Dragons]] existed throughout the entire Sector. Victor was capable of increasing their numbers but deliberately restricted the reproduction and spread of the race, both because of warnings concerning cosmic balance and because True Dragonhood had become one of the defining traits of the Imperial Family.[6]
In Chapter 1165, the disturbance surrounding [[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]] develops into an enormous gathering of Victor's daughters. More than thirty-five Imperial Princesses eventually assemble together.[3]
A group of Fulger descendants arrives during the gathering. The narration identifies [[Sylvia Elderblood]], [[Julia Elderblood]], [[Svetlana Veronika Elderblood]], [[Alisa Sasha Elderblood]] and a girl called Ekaterina Natasha Elderblood, who is stated to be the daughter of Annasthashia. The girls are collectively described as possessing blonde hair and violet eyes, with several strongly resembling their individual mothers.[3]
This creates a direct continuity contradiction. Angelina had already been introduced in Chapter 991 as Victor and Annasthashia's daughter and was later still identified among Victor's True Dragon daughters. No separate history, earlier appearance, different lineage or other distinguishing information establishes Ekaterina as another independent daughter of Annasthashia.[1][2]
For that reason, Ekaterina Natasha Elderblood is treated as a continuity-error name for Angelina Elderblood. She is not indexed as an additional daughter of Victor and Annasthashia, and Chapter 1165's information concerning the character is incorporated into Angelina's page instead.[3]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Angelina's biological father and the most important figure in her emotional life. Angelina's attachment toward him is extreme even by the standards of Victor's daughters and is explicitly presented as one of the characteristics she inherited from Annasthashia.[1]
Angelina searches Victor's private bedroom simply because she wants to see him. When he is absent, she steals one of his shirts, while photographs of him are valuable enough in her eyes to function as bargaining material.[1]
The announcement that Victor is expected to return home immediately overwhelms every previous concern Angelina and Gina had, causing both girls to race toward the daughters' room in anticipation of seeing him.[1]
Victor's relationship with Angelina also reflects his extreme protectiveness toward all of his children. Angelina herself recognizes that her father rarely allows his daughters enough freedom to encounter the kinds of dangerous situations for which the Fulger women attempt to prepare them.[1]
His protection is not merely political or physical. Victor placed extensive safeguards upon the bodies, minds and Souls of his daughters so that enemies cannot easily bypass their durability through indirect supernatural methods.[2]

#### Annasthashia Elderblood Fulger
[[Annasthashia Elderblood Fulger]] is Angelina's biological mother and the parent she most strongly resembles.
Angelina inherited Annasthashia's blonde Fulger appearance, Lightning affinity, mischievous behavior and extreme attachment toward [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. The similarities are strong enough that she is effectively described as a younger version of her mother in both appearance and personality.[1]
Annasthashia does little to discourage Angelina's obsession with Victor. Angelina even acquires a copy of a sleeping photograph of Victor from her mother's collection and admits that she unsuccessfully attempted to find the more private photographs Annasthashia keeps hidden.[1]
Annasthashia also participates directly in Angelina's upbringing and survival training, teaching her daughter that there is no reason to obey conventional ideas of honor when confronted by an enemy capable of killing her.[1]

#### Gina Elderblood
[[Gina Elderblood]] is one of Angelina's sisters and the person with whom Angelina interacts most extensively during her debut.
Their relationship is competitive and troublesome rather than genuinely hostile. Gina catches Angelina after she steals [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s shirt and refuses to let the matter disappear. Angelina initially considers escaping but realizes that avoiding Gina temporarily would only cause another confrontation later.[1]
She instead manipulates Gina's own affection for Victor and offers her the stolen shirt and a photograph of their father. Gina accepts almost immediately, illustrating that both sisters share many of the same weaknesses where Victor is concerned.[1]
Once told that Victor is returning, the two immediately abandon their dispute and run together toward him, with Angelina taking the lead through Lightning.[1]

#### Fulger Family
Angelina is raised within the wider Fulger branch of the Elderblood family rather than exclusively by Annasthashia. [[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]], [[Natasha Elderblood Fulger]] and [[Victoria Fulger Elderblood]] all participate in her upbringing and discipline.[1]
Carmila teaches Angelina that being intelligent does not mean refusing accountability. Within the family, she is expected to accept responsibility when clearly caught rather than continually invent excuses.
Against enemies, however, the Fulger philosophy changes. Annasthashia teaches her to prioritize survival over honor, while Victoria emphasizes flexibility and improvisation. An enemy powerful enough to threaten an Elderblood Princess is not owed fairness; Angelina is expected to survive by any means available and allow her family to retaliate afterward.[1]

### Skills & Abilities
- Blood Dragon Physiology: Angelina is a biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Annasthashia Elderblood Fulger]] and was born directly as a member of Victor's Blood Dragon lineage.[1]
- True Dragon Physiology: Angelina explicitly identifies herself and her sisters as [[True Dragons]] when distinguishing them from one of Victor's ordinary Dragons.[2]
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics: True Dragons possess overwhelming physical capabilities compared with ordinary supernatural species. Even the youngest members of the race are considered monsters in terms of their natural potential.[6]
- Environmental Adaptation: Angelina can comfortably operate inside extremely hostile environments. During Victor's War Games, she and her sisters remain functional in an environment around −60°C without the climate presenting a meaningful obstacle.[2]
- Passive Adaptation: Victor's daughters inherited a passive form of the adaptive characteristics he acquired through his own evolution. Their bodies gradually adjust to harmful or unfavorable conditions rather than remaining permanently vulnerable to them.[2]
- Exceptional Compatibility: True Dragons are described as extraordinarily well adapted to existence itself, allowing their bodies to interact with a wide variety of supernatural systems and environments.[6]
- Extreme Longevity: As a True Dragon and Goddess, Angelina possesses a lifespan vastly beyond ordinary mortal standards.
- Fulger Lightning: Angelina explicitly inherited the Lightning of the [[Fulger Clan]] through Annasthashia.[1]
- Electricity Manipulation: Angelina can generate and manipulate the supernatural Lightning inherited through her maternal Bloodline.[1]
- Lightning Manipulation: She possesses enough control over her Lightning to use it deliberately for movement rather than merely releasing it offensively.[1]
- Enhanced Speed: Lightning dramatically increases Angelina's movement speed.[1]
- Rapid Acceleration: Angelina is confident that she could use Lightning to escape from Gina before her sister could stop her.[1]
- High-Speed Movement: When racing toward Victor, Angelina takes the lead specifically through her Fulger Lightning.[1]
- Adaptive Evolution: The Elderblood daughters possess inherited adaptive characteristics originating from Victor. Harmful environmental conditions can progressively become less effective against them as their bodies adapt.[2]
- Soul Manipulation Immunity: Victor deliberately protected his daughters against hostile invasions or alterations of their Souls.[2]
- Mind Manipulation Immunity: The daughters possess protections preventing conventional mental invasion and hostile attempts to seize control of their minds.[2]
- Possession Immunity: Their combined mental and spiritual defenses make conventional supernatural possession ineffective against them.[2]
- Parasitic Infection Resistance: Victor's protections prevent malicious parasites and foreign organisms from simply infiltrating his daughters' bodies.[2]
- Disease and Biological Manipulation Resistance: The defensive system accounts for biological threats attempting to disguise themselves as harmless or beneficial processes, preventing enemies from easily bypassing the daughters' natural durability through infection or biological sabotage.[2]
- Soul Preservation: Victor preserves a portion of each daughter's Soul within himself as an additional safeguard against permanent death.[2]
- Resurrection: The preserved Soul fragments provide Victor with a method of restoring his daughters if their bodies are successfully destroyed.[2]
- Eldritch Soul Protection: A portion of Victor's own existence protects the Souls of his daughters, providing another defensive layer if an enemy somehow bypasses their ordinary protections.[2]
- Threat Detection: Victor's connection to his daughters allows serious attacks against them to alert him, making it exceptionally difficult for an enemy to target an Imperial Princess without eventually drawing his attention.[2]
- Draconic Perception: Angelina can identify differences within Victor's Draconic lineage. Upon observing an Ice Dragon during Victor's War Games, she immediately recognizes that the creature is a Dragon but lacks the status of a True Dragon like Angelina and her sisters.[2]
- Cunning: Angelina demonstrates considerable practical cunning during her confrontation with Gina.[1]
- Behavioral Analysis: She quickly recognizes that Gina's own attachment to Victor can be exploited.
- Negotiation: Angelina changes an escalating confrontation into a bargain instead of attempting to overpower her sister.
- Deception: She initially attempts to conceal her theft and has little hesitation using misdirection when useful.[1]
- Strategic Assessment: Angelina recognizes that escaping from Gina would not truly solve the problem because another confrontation would simply occur later.
- Improvisation: Victoria specifically encourages Angelina to alter her approach according to circumstances rather than mechanically follow rules.[1]
- Survival Training: Angelina has been personally instructed by the older Fulger women in methods for surviving against enemies capable of threatening a True Dragon.[1]
- Survival-First Mentality: Remaining alive takes precedence over conventional ideas of combat honor.
- Deceptive Combat: Deception and underhanded methods are explicitly acceptable when facing enemies strong enough to threaten her life.
- Flexible Rules of Engagement: Angelina is trained to improvise rather than obey predictable patterns.
- Family Retaliation: Her elders consider escaping from an overwhelming opponent preferable to dying against them, as surviving allows Angelina to return with the Elderblood family and retaliate collectively.[1]
- Divinity (Unrevealed): Angelina is a Dragon Goddess, but the reviewed chapters do not explicitly reveal the name of her individual Divine Concept. Her confirmed Fulger Lightning should therefore not automatically be treated as proof that she possesses a separate <Lightning> Divinity.

### Continuity
Angelina is explicitly established in Chapter 991 as the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Annasthashia Elderblood Fulger]]. Her appearance, inherited Fulger Lightning, personality and relationship with Annasthashia are all directly established during this chapter.[1]
Chapter 1165 later introduces Ekaterina Natasha Elderblood and identifies her as another daughter of Annasthashia during the gathering of Victor's Imperial Princesses. She appears among the blonde Fulger daughters and possesses the same violet Elderblood eyes.[3]
This produces a continuity contradiction because Angelina already occupies the established daughter position for Victor and Annasthashia, while Chapter 1164 explains that Victor and his wives deliberately limited themselves to approximately one child each during this period.[6]
Accordingly, this wiki treats Ekaterina Natasha Elderblood as a continuity-error name for Angelina Elderblood, similar to other naming inconsistencies within the later-generation cast. Ekaterina is therefore not counted as a separate daughter of Victor and Annasthashia, and information attributed to that name is indexed under Angelina.[1][6][3]
