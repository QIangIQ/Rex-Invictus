### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 1177 (First named physical appearance)Chapter 1124 (Prenatal existence)
Helvarya Elderblood is an Imperial Princess of the [[Elderblood Empire]] and the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Hela Elderblood]]. She belongs to Victor's second generation of natural-born Draconic descendants and is one of the most conceptually unusual of his daughters, having been born with the supernatural power of Death and the fundamental Concept of END.[1]
Helvarya's birth was initially believed to be a twin pregnancy. During Hela's pregnancy, two Soul-like presences could be detected within her, causing Victor and the family to believe that Hela was carrying two daughters.[2] As the pregnancy progressed, however, those presences merged into a single developing child and the resulting daughter's power grew considerably stronger. Later investigation revealed that the apparent second Soul had never been another ordinary living child. It was END itself, preparing Helvarya to become a suitable host for the Concept.[1]
Her unusual development closely parallels that of her paternal half-sister [[Ariella Victoria Elderblood]]. Ariella's apparent second twin was ultimately connected to Creation, while Helvarya's was associated with END. The two girls consequently represent complementary examples of Divine Concepts directly participating in the formation of Victor's descendants rather than merely being inherited as ordinary abilities.[1]

### Physical Appearance
Helvarya has blonde hair. During her first named physical appearance, she stands beside [[Ariella Victoria Elderblood]] and [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]]. The narration describes Ariella and Helvarya as one blonde sister and one dark-haired sister; Ariella is independently established as having black hair, identifying Helvarya as the blonde daughter in the scene.[1]
Her exact height, hairstyle, clothing, body proportions and Dragon Form have not yet received an individual description.
As a member of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s immediate biological family, Helvarya belongs to the generation in which [[True Dragons]] predominate. Later explanations identify Victor's immediate family as the core of the True Dragon population, although some mothers deliberately prevented their children from undergoing the complete True Dragon inheritance.[3] Helvarya has not yet displayed an individually described Dragon Form.

### Personality
Very little of Helvarya's personality has been individually explored.
Her first substantial appearance nevertheless establishes that, despite the extraordinary Concepts incorporated into her existence, Helvarya behaves naturally as one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters rather than as an emotionless embodiment of Death. When [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]] immediately monopolized Victor's attention, Helvarya and Ariella watched their younger sister with obvious envy.[1]
When Victor noticed them and invited both girls into a family embrace, Helvarya enthusiastically joined him without attempting to maintain the dignity expected of an Imperial Princess. The narration specifically notes that the sisters did not even pretend to behave like adults once their father offered them affection.[1]
This scene establishes a strong attachment toward Victor consistent with the wider Elderblood second generation, but Helvarya has not yet received enough individual dialogue to justify assigning her more specific traits such as cruelty, calmness, combat obsession or affinity with Hela's sadistic personality.

### Origin and Heritage

#### Hela's Pregnancy
Helvarya's existence was foreshadowed long before she received a name.
After [[Hela Elderblood]] became involved with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], their relationship developed across an extended period within manipulated Time. Hela eventually became pregnant, and Chapter 1124 explicitly presented the pregnancy as involving twins.[2]
The situation was already unusual because Victor's reproductive abilities do not follow the limitations normally experienced by extremely powerful supernatural Beings. As a Progenitor and God connected to Beginning, Victor can initiate conception despite the reproductive difficulties usually associated with increasingly powerful existences.[2]
Victor also explained that conception within his family involves more than conventional biology. The emotional and spiritual condition of both parents can influence the union of Souls during conception. He considered this one possible reason why the pregnancies of both Ariel and Hela produced apparent twins.[2]
For a considerable period, Hela was therefore understood to be carrying two daughters.
Chapter 1177 finally revealed what actually happened.
During the pregnancy, the two apparent Souls progressively drew together before ultimately merging into one. Rather than harming the developing child, the fusion caused the resulting existence to become stronger.[1]
Further investigation demonstrated that only one of the two presences had actually been the Soul of a child.
The second presence was associated with:
END.[1]
It had been preparing a compatible host.
The result of the fusion was Helvarya Elderblood.

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Helvarya's story begins before her birth through [[Hela Elderblood]]'s developing relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
Hela became increasingly involved with Victor's family during the later Imperial period. After spending an extended subjective period with Victor through Space-Time manipulation, she became pregnant.[2]
The pregnancy immediately attracted attention because two developing Soul-like signatures were present. Hela was consequently believed to be pregnant with twins, mirroring the earlier unusual pregnancy of [[Ariel Elderblood]].[2]
Victor and Violet discussed how such pregnancies might occur despite the unusual biology of extremely powerful Beings. Victor explained that his existence as both a Progenitor and God associated with Beginnings gave him extraordinary control over reproduction. He could determine conception directly if he wished, although he generally preferred allowing Nature to decide details rather than consciously designing every child.[2]
He also considered the mother's emotional state relevant because supernatural conception involved a deeper spiritual union between the parents. Hela's powerful emotions toward Victor were therefore considered a possible contributor to the unusual pregnancy.[2]
At the time, neither Victor nor the others knew that one of the apparent twins was not actually another child.

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Helvarya eventually survived the enormous period of Imperial development and became one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Imperial Princesses.
The full explanation of her existence did not emerge until Chapter 1177.
After a confrontation involving the Primordial Beings, Victor withdrew to a gigantic waterfall on the family's world to think. His solitude lasted only moments before [[Ariella Victoria Elderblood]], Helvarya and [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]] arrived.[1]
Moira immediately climbed onto Victor and claimed his attention, causing Helvarya and Ariella to watch their younger sister enviously.
Victor noticed and invited both girls closer.
Helvarya and Ariella immediately joined him in a family hug, abandoning any attempt to act like mature Imperial Princesses in favor of enjoying their father's affection.[1]
While holding them, Victor reflected upon how unusually Ariella and Helvarya had been born.
Both pregnancies had apparently contained twins.
In each case, however, the two detected presences eventually merged together and strengthened the remaining child.[1]
The family later discovered that the "second Souls" had never been ordinary Souls.
For Ariella, the presence was connected to Creation.
For Helvarya, it was END.[1]
Helvarya was ultimately born possessing the power of Death and END, while the Concept that had appeared to be her twin had effectively prepared her as its compatible host.[1]
Victor regarded both END and Creation as exceptional Concepts even compared with other extraordinarily powerful Divine powers.
The revelation finally resolved the mystery surrounding Hela's supposed twins first established in Chapter 1124.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Helvarya's biological father.
Only a small amount of direct interaction between them has currently been shown, but Helvarya is clearly affectionate toward him. She becomes jealous when Moira receives Victor's attention and immediately accepts his invitation to join a family embrace.[1]
Victor reciprocates this affection and treats Helvarya as a daughter rather than primarily as a holder of an extraordinary Concept.
His reflections regarding her unusual birth also show that he had closely followed the development of Hela's pregnancy and investigated what happened when the two apparent Souls merged.[1]

#### Hela Elderblood
[[Hela Elderblood]] is Helvarya's biological mother.
Hela's own supernatural nature is strongly associated with Death, dead realms and powers connected to END. Her pregnancy with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was explicitly identified as a twin pregnancy before Helvarya's birth.[2]
Helvarya ultimately inherited the power of Death, while the deeper Concept of END manifested through the extraordinary circumstances of her gestation.[1]
The novel has not yet shown a substantial direct mother-daughter conversation between Hela and Helvarya.

#### Ariella Victoria Elderblood
[[Ariella Victoria Elderblood]] is Helvarya's paternal half-sister and the daughter whose birth most closely parallels her own.
Both girls were initially believed to be products of twin pregnancies.
Both pregnancies later produced only a single daughter.
Both apparent twin Souls ultimately proved to involve an extraordinary Concept preparing a compatible host.[1]

### Skills & Abilities
Helvarya has not yet participated in an individually described battle. Her confirmed abilities are therefore primarily based upon the explicit explanation of her birth and conceptual nature rather than speculative applications.
- True Dragon Physiology: Helvarya belongs to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s immediate biological family, the group forming the core of the extremely limited True Dragon population. [[True Dragons]] are described as embodiments of nature with compatibility vastly exceeding that of ordinary Dragons.[3]
- Enhanced Physical Characteristics: As one of Victor's natural-born Draconic daughters, Helvarya possesses the superior biological and supernatural foundation characteristic of the second Elderblood generation.
- Supernatural Compatibility: True Dragons possess exceptional compatibility with supernatural systems and Concepts, an important trait for an existence carrying a Concept as extreme as END.[3]
- [[Draconic Energy]]: Helvarya possesses the natural Energy foundation associated with Victor's Draconic descendants.
- Extreme Longevity: Her Draconic and Divine existence is not bound by a conventional mortal lifespan.
- Death: Helvarya was explicitly stated to have been born with the power of Death.[1] The exact breadth of her personal Death Authority has not yet been individually demonstrated.
- Death Manipulation: Her innate connection to Death establishes direct supernatural authority over the Concept, although specific techniques remain unknown.
- Death Energy Affinity: Helvarya possesses inherent compatibility with Death-related supernatural Energy through her birth.
- Conceptual Death Affinity: Death is an intrinsic component of Helvarya's existence rather than merely a spell, learned technique or external Blessing.
- END: Helvarya was born possessing END, one of the most extreme terminal Concepts demonstrated in the series.[1]
- Conceptual Host: The apparent second Soul present during Hela's pregnancy was ultimately revealed to be END preparing Helvarya as its compatible host.[1]
- Innate END Compatibility: Helvarya's compatibility with END existed before birth and became integrated into her developing existence.
- Conceptual Fusion: [[True Death|The END]]-related presence merged with Helvarya during gestation rather than remaining an external power source.[1]
- Conceptual Empowerment: The merging of the two presences caused Helvarya's developing power to increase.[1]
- Finality Manipulation (Potential / Conceptual Foundation): END represents terminal cessation—the true conclusion of a target rather than merely ordinary physical destruction. Helvarya possesses the Concept, but the exact applications she personally controls have not yet been demonstrated.
- Existence Erasure (Potential / Conceptual Foundation): Established manifestations of END are capable of removing targets from conventional existence rather than simply destroying physical matter. Helvarya has not yet individually performed this feat, so it should be treated as an application supported by her Concept rather than a personal feat.
- Conceptual Erasure (Potential): END can operate beyond physical structures and affect deeper aspects of existence. No individual Helvarya feat has yet established the upper limit of her own version.
- Spatial/Temporal Termination (Potential): Advanced manifestations of END elsewhere in the series can terminate structures such as Space and Time. Helvarya should not automatically be given mastery equivalent to True Death until she directly demonstrates it.
- Death–END Synergy: Helvarya possesses both Death and END from birth.[1] Although related, the two should not be treated as identical.
- Death concerns the death of living existence.
- END represents a more fundamental terminal conclusion that can extend beyond conventional Life and Death.
- Helvarya's possession of both gives her an unusually coherent conceptual foundation centered upon cessation, but the novel has not yet shown how she combines them in combat.
- Special Conceptual Gestation: Helvarya's development was directly influenced by a fundamental Concept before birth.[1]
- Conceptual Soul Integration: A presence initially perceived as another Soul merged into Helvarya during pregnancy.
- Host Preparation: Further analysis revealed that the presence represented END preparing Helvarya as an appropriate vessel.[1]
- Power Amplification: The merger increased the power of the developing child.[1]
- Eldritch and Primordial Divine Heritage: Later discussion describes Victor's daughters as possessing unusually high Divine foundations incorporating Eldritch and Primordial characteristics through their father.[3] This should not be misrepresented as Helvarya already holding the complete cosmic office of a Primordial Entity.

Death governs the termination of Life.
END represents terminal cessation on a much deeper conceptual level.

Helvarya has both powers from birth.[1]
This does not mean she has automatically demonstrated every known feat performed by [[True Death]] or Apocalypse Beasts carrying derivative END powers.
Her current article should separate:
- Concept ownership/inheritance — confirmed;
- potential applications of END — supported by the nature of the Concept;
- personally demonstrated applications — currently almost nonexistent.
This distinction is necessary until Helvarya receives a battle or training scene.

#### Relationship to True Death
[[True Death]] possesses source-level Authority over Death and END and functions at a vastly higher established cosmological position.
Helvarya possessing END does not currently establish that she is equal to True Death, has replaced him, or automatically possesses his complete administrative Authority.
Chapter 1177 instead establishes Helvarya as an exceptionally compatible host of END whose exact future relationship with the Concept remains unexplored.[1]

### Limitations
- Minimal Combat Evidence: Helvarya has not yet received a proper individual battle scene.
- Unknown END Mastery: Possessing END from birth does not establish mastery comparable to [[True Death]].
- Unknown Death Mastery: Helvarya's Death power is explicitly confirmed, but its range and specialized applications remain unstated.
- Concept Ownership ≠ Primordial Office: Being born with an extraordinary Concept does not automatically mean Helvarya has inherited the complete cosmic administrative role of the corresponding Primordial Being.
- Potential Applications Are Not Feats: Existence Erasure, Spatial Erasure, Temporal Erasure and similar properties known from END elsewhere in the story should remain marked as potential until Helvarya performs them herself.
- Little Individual Characterization: Her currently established personality is limited primarily to affection toward [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and ordinary sibling jealousy.
