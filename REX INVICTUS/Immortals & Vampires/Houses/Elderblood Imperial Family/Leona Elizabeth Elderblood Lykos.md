### Appearances

#### Debut
- [[Ch21 - Rex Invictus XIV|Chapter 21]]
My instinct was not to want to cause chaos or to kill people but to mark someone who was my equal. You, Vic.
—Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos to [[Victor Elderblood]], Chapter 723
Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos, born Leona [[Elizabeth Lykos]] and previously known as Leona Elizabeth Alucard Lykos, is one of Victor Elderblood's oldest childhood friends, his wife, a member of the Elderblood Clan, and one of the highest-ranking Dragon Goddesses of the [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]]. She is the daughter of [[Adam William Lykos]], the younger sister of [[Edward Jonathan Elizabeth Lykos]], the younger half-sister of [[Johnny Lykos]], and a direct descendant of Elizabeth Lykos. Before becoming fully involved in supernatural society, Leona worked as a veterinarian and operated her own clinic.[2]
Although Leona spent most of her early life believing herself physically frail, she was actually a dormant Alpha Werewolf carrying the exceptionally rare Elizabeth Trait. Her Werewolf nature awakened unusually late, and on the same night that Victor's supernatural transformation began, her newly awakened instincts drove her toward the person she subconsciously regarded as her equal. She bit Victor and injected him with Werewolf venom in an attempt to mark and transform him; the venom subsequently became one of several supernatural influences acting upon Victor during the abnormal ritual that instead produced the second Vampire Progenitor.[3]
Leona and Victor eventually confronted the romantic feelings they had suppressed since their Human lives, gradually entering a relationship despite the traditional hostility between Vampires and [[Werewolves]].[4] Their bond was ultimately recognized through Leona's Werewolf Mark and [[Soul Marriage]], making her one of Victor's wives.[5] She later accepted Victor's large-scale Dragon transformation and was reconstructed into a genuine Blood Dragon and True Dragon, with the qualities of her former Lykos and Werewolf existence incorporated into her new Draconic body.[6]
During the Imperial era, Leona possesses the principal Divinities of Animals, Wildlife and Wolves, together with the lesser Divinities of [[Rex Invictus|Home]] and Nature. She is recognized as one of the principal Dragon Goddesses of the Empire and serves as the substitute commander of the Sixth Legion, [[The Silent Judgment]], whenever [[Jeanne Elderblood]] is unavailable.[7][8] Leona and Victor have a known daughter, Leone Luna Elena Elderblood, who inherited both the Lykos appearance and Victor's Draconic lineage.[9]

### Physical description
When first introduced, Leona possessed pure white hair, bright blue eyes, a slim build and extremely pale skin caused by albinism rather than Vampirism. She stood approximately 180 centimeters tall. Because her skin lacked ordinary pigmentation, sunlight was extremely uncomfortable for her during her Human-era life, causing her to spend most of her time inside her clinic or home and to prefer going outside at night.[2]
Leona's appearance changed substantially after her dormant Werewolf bloodline awakened. Her previously sickly and exhausted appearance disappeared, her thin limbs became more defined and lightly muscular, and training beneath the Lykos family developed her into a lean supernatural warrior. Her height increased from approximately 180 centimeters to 185 centimeters, and her greater appetite and supernatural physiology gave her a significantly healthier appearance.[10]
In her former Werewolf combat state, Leona's features became more feral, white fur grew across portions of her arms and legs, and sharp claws emerged from her limbs. Her Lykos heritage also produced the characteristic white coloration associated with the Clan.[11]
Following her evolution into a Blood Dragon Goddess, Leona's ordinary humanoid presentation became dramatically larger. By Chapter 1119 she is described as the same height as [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]], whose current Dragon Goddess body stands approximately 4.70 meters tall. Leona can wear Divine Raiment and one of her favored outfits is a tight white dress whose edges appear to be formed from stardust and faintly illuminate her surroundings as she moves.[7]

### Personality and traits
Leona is normally quiet, observant and relatively composed, and among [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s original Human-era friends she was consistently one of the more intelligent and perceptive members of the group. Her calm behavior is not the same as passivity. Even before her supernatural life became public, she was stubborn, independent and willing to disregard social expectations when they interfered with something she genuinely wanted. Her attempt to transform Victor into a Werewolf was ultimately rooted in this combination of attachment and determination: because relationships between ordinary Humans and [[Werewolves]] were forbidden within her society, she intended to solve the problem by making Victor one of her own race.[10][3]
Leona possesses a playful and spoiled side around her father and can be openly mischievous when surrounded by people she trusts. She has never shown much interest in the prestige normally associated with being born into an ancient Werewolf Clan and originally avoided creating a conventional pack despite possessing the authority of an Alpha. Her temperament changes most noticeably around [[Violet Elderblood Snow]]. Their mutual possessiveness toward Victor, old racial prejudices and highly competitive personalities can make Leona considerably more argumentative, sarcastic and aggressive whenever the two provoke one another.[12][13]
The awakening of Leona's Elizabeth Trait also revealed a much stronger instinctive side. The mutation naturally accelerates the development of a Werewolf's instincts and can produce emotional instability or increasingly wolf-like behavior when poorly controlled.[1] Leona is unusual because her Werewolf nature remained dormant until adulthood. She therefore developed a mature rational personality before being exposed to the mutation's full instinctive pressure, while her imprint upon Victor also satisfied one of the strongest impulses produced by her awakening.[14] Her Hybrid Form nevertheless makes her noticeably more aggressive and combative.[11]
Leona's relationship with Victor combines possessiveness with a comparatively grounded understanding of the person he was before becoming a supernatural monarch. She remembers the chronically ill boy who lived beside her long before the names Alucard, Elderblood, Dragon God or Emperor existed. This shared history grants Leona unusual standing within the family; by the Imperial era, the narration specifically distinguishes Violet, Leona and Anna as women who were beside Victor from the beginning, contributing to their unusually high position within the household.[9]
Her later behavior also demonstrates considerable political maturity. Despite possessing enough rank to overrule ordinary Imperial employees, Leona consciously refuses to abuse Victor's authority for personal convenience. When irritated by corpses resembling her brother, she briefly considers accelerating their destruction but rejects the idea because she is visiting as a civilian and does not want to establish a precedent in which the Emperor's family ignores the laws expected of everyone else. Leona understands that the conduct and reputation of the Dragon Goddesses affect how the entire Imperial system is perceived.[7]
Motherhood further emphasizes Leona's calmer and more responsible qualities. She regularly makes time for Leone, respects her daughter's privacy instead of casually reading her mind, and values humility, honor and familial loyalty despite the extraordinary privilege surrounding the Imperial Princesses.[15] At the same time, Leona remains mischievous enough to personally summon [[Scathach]] when Leone declares that she wants to reach Scathach's level, immediately volunteering her daughter for a year of brutal training and even providing dramatic music for the beginning of the ordeal.[16]

### Synopsis

#### The Awakening
Leona first reappeared in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s life when he visited the veterinary clinic where she worked. Alongside Edward and Andrew, she was one of the people who had known Victor throughout the years in which his physical illness made ordinary exercise and social life difficult. Leona's own albinism and unexplained physical frailty gave the two a shared understanding of living with bodies that limited them. The dramatic physical transformation produced by Victor's Vampirism was therefore immediately obvious to her and Edward.[2]
The reunion also revealed how much Victor's personal life had changed. Andrew disclosed Victor's sudden marriage to [[Violet Elderblood Snow]], and the group subsequently learned that he had already become involved with more than one extraordinarily possessive woman. Leona was surprised by the transformation of the quiet and sickly Victor she remembered into someone openly comfortable with dangerous supernatural relationships.[17]
The Lykos family's own secret emerged shortly afterward. [[Adam William Lykos]] recognized that Victor was no longer an ordinary Human, while the story revealed that Adam, Edward and Leona belonged to a Werewolf family. Victor had also carried Werewolf venom capable of triggering a transformation, although his simultaneous Vampire transformation prevented the normal result. Adam ordered Leona not to involve herself recklessly in Vampire affairs simply because Victor was her friend.[18]
Much later, Leona personally explained the missing part of the event. Her Werewolf side had abruptly awakened on the same night Victor became a Vampire. Rather than driving her toward random violence, her instincts pushed her to locate someone she considered an equal. She had already imprinted strongly upon Victor and consequently bit him, injecting the venom that should have begun his transformation into a Werewolf. Victor's body instead underwent the far more abnormal Vampire ritual involving Violet, Ruby and Sasha while simultaneously dealing with Leona's venom and the other forces surrounding his transformation.[3]

#### Vampire Society
While [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] trained in [[Nightingale]], [[Johnny Lykos]] returned to the supernatural underground and learned that his younger sister had feelings for the newly transformed Vampire. Johnny's protective instincts immediately made him investigate Victor. What he discovered—a young Vampire who was proud, vindictive and dangerous toward enemies but unusually loyal to people he regarded as family—did not completely reassure him, but those traits were considerably less alien to a Werewolf than they would have been to an ordinary Human.[19]

#### A New Pillar
Leona remained connected to Edward and Fred while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Human friends were progressively drawn into supernatural society. At the pet shop, the group dealt with the arrival of Mizuki, whose presence inside Werewolf territory created immediate tension. Fred eventually confronted Leona and Edward with evidence proving that Victor was a Vampire, causing the siblings to admit that they themselves were [[Werewolves]].[20]
Victor later met Leona and Edward directly and confirmed their supernatural nature through his own perception. The revelation did not meaningfully damage their relationship. Although Vampires and Werewolves traditionally treated one another as enemies, Victor had known the siblings before entering either society and saw no reason to allow an inherited racial feud to decide whom he considered a friend.[21]
The pet shop subsequently became an unusual meeting point for Victor, Violet, Mizuki, Fred, Edward and Leona. Their discussions introduced Fred more thoroughly to the hidden world and allowed Victor to compare the political hierarchy of the Werewolves with the structures he had encountered in [[Nightingale]]. Leona was identified as an Alpha Werewolf and the daughter of former Werewolf General Adam Lykos, making clear that her family occupied a considerably more important place in supernatural society than Victor had understood during their Human lives.[22]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
As the supernatural conflict surrounding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] expanded, Leona and the Lykos family became more directly involved with the Walkers. Leona, Adam, Edward and Victor's other Human-era friends gathered around Anna and the people preparing for the approaching Demonic threat. These meetings increasingly erased the boundary between Victor's former Human circle and the supernatural faction growing around him.[23]
By Chapter 492, Leona's dormant Werewolf bloodline had fully awakened. The transformation removed her sickly appearance, increased her height to approximately 185 centimeters and gave her the physique of a trained Lykos warrior.[10] More importantly, the change reopened a personal issue both Leona and Victor had avoided for years.
Victor and Leona finally spoke directly about their past in Chapter 493. Victor admitted that he had liked Leona when they were younger, while Leona's attachment had been even stronger. Their circumstances had prevented the relationship from developing: Victor's health made his future uncertain, Leona's family hid their Werewolf identity, and Leona herself had originally intended to awaken Victor as a Werewolf rather than pursue a forbidden Human-Werewolf relationship. Violet reached him first and transformed him into something else entirely.[4]
Victor refused either to pretend that he loved Leona as deeply as his established wives or to deny that feelings remained between them. Instead, the two agreed to proceed slowly. Leona would learn who Victor had become after entering the supernatural world, while Victor would learn who Leona had become after her own awakening. Their decision directly rejected the old rule that the hostility between Vampires and [[Werewolves]] should determine their private relationship.[4]
Leona's growing closeness to Victor immediately produced friction with Violet. When Violet detected the scent of a Werewolf on Victor, years of rivalry erupted into an exchange of insults between the two women. Their argument eventually became more serious as Leona questioned how someone as possessive as Violet could tolerate Victor's enormous family. Through the discussion, Leona began recognizing that Victor had not become an entirely different person after his transformation: his loyalty, possessiveness, pride, vengefulness and obsessive attachment to family had existed in less extreme forms throughout his Human life. Vampirism and [[Scathach]]'s training had amplified traits already present rather than creating them from nothing.[12][13]

#### Demonic War
Leona remained present when the unresolved relationship between Agnes and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became impossible to ignore. Because Victor had absorbed Adonis' memories and portions of his supernatural existence, Leona and Violet immediately understood the danger that Agnes might be responding to echoes of her deceased husband rather than Victor himself. Victor refused to allow himself to become a replacement for Adonis and demanded that any relationship with Agnes be built around who he actually was.[24]
Leona's own development became much more visible during the preparations surrounding [[WarFall]]. In Chapter 645 she demonstrated a Werewolf Hybrid Form in which white fur and claws appeared across her limbs. An almost imperceptible white Energy surrounded her body and reinforced it. [[Roxanne Elderblood]] recognized the Energy as similar to natural Energy, although less pure and mixed with Leona's Werewolf bloodline.[11]
During the enormous Monster assault on WarFall, Leona fought as one of the principal combatants defending the city. Using bladed gauntlets and the immense physical strength of her Hybrid Form, she mutilated Monsters at close range and casually threw a Behemoth approximately twenty meters tall. She subsequently concentrated the same white Energy inside her mouth and released a beam powerful enough to vaporize several Behemoths. Her sense of smell also detected an invisible attacker in time for her to warn Violet before the creature's ambush could succeed.[25]

#### King Among Kings
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] returned from centuries spent inside [[Biblical Hell]], Leona had become fully integrated into the enormous household gathered around him. The relationships surrounding Victor were no longer temporary attractions or isolated political arrangements; they increasingly functioned as a single extended family whose members understood that their individual positions carried obligations toward everyone else Victor had accepted.[26]
Chapter 723 finally revealed the circumstances surrounding Leona's own awakening and Victor's old Werewolf venom. Leona explained that her instincts had selected Victor as an equal and driven her to mark him on the night his life changed. The revelation established that, had Violet's ritual not intervened, Leona had already placed Victor upon a path toward becoming a Werewolf.[3]
Leona later accompanied Victor, Anna and Natalia into Samar, bringing Victor directly into the center of Werewolf civilization.[27] There, Leona openly demonstrated the dominance expected from a True Alpha. When she asserted her bloodline, her eyes glowed azure and surrounding [[Werewolves]] instinctively reacted to her status. Victor compared the phenomenon to the suppression weaker Noble Vampires experience in the presence of a vastly superior bloodline and noticed that Leona's authority had become stronger after their supernatural and physical bond developed.[28]
The visit also exposed the deeper history of the [[Lykos Clan]]. Bella Lykos explained that [[Elizabeth Lykos]], the strongest wife of the original Werewolf Progenitor, possessed an exceptionally rare genetic mutation that accelerated the instinctive development and overall potential of its bearer. Leona carried the same mutation.[1] Unlike most carriers, who awakened during early childhood, Leona's Werewolf nature had remained dormant until adulthood. The Matriarch had recognized the mutation when Leona was born and personally gave her the middle name Elizabeth, identifying her as a direct potential successor to the ancestral line.[14]
The unusual dominance of Lykos genetics became even clearer when [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] examined Maya and Leona. The Lykos lineage was described as predatory enough that its characteristic traits normally remained dominant even when mixed with Progenitor or Divine ancestry; Progenitor Blood tended to enhance the mother's lineage rather than erase it. Vlad considered Dragon blood the major known mortal exception capable of genuinely competing with the Lykos lineage.[29]
By this point Leona's relationship with Victor was openly treated as marital. When Victor discussed drawing Tasha into his family, Leona reacted with unmistakable jealousy despite enjoying his affection, demonstrating that integration into the larger household had done little to eliminate her possessive instincts.[30]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became the Dragon God of [[Chaos]], he offered members of his family the opportunity to undergo a far greater racial evolution. The process was voluntary and was specifically intended to preserve and elevate qualities the women already possessed rather than erasing their identities beneath a standardized Dragon template.[31]
Leona was among those who accepted. During the large-scale transformation of Chapters 917–920, Victor's Essence and Divinities reconstructed the participants at a fundamental level inside enormous Dragon eggs. Leona emerged as a genuine Blood Dragon and True Dragon, with the Lykos characteristics incorporated into her new Draconic existence rather than simply discarded.[6]
Her responsibilities also expanded with Victor's civilization. During Victor's temporary vacation, Leona led the group responsible for organizing the populations and other Beings who would relocate into territories belonging to the former [[Egyptian Pantheon]]. Her role demonstrated that she was no longer merely a warrior or Victor's childhood friend; she had become one of the women trusted to independently manage large portions of the civilization forming around Dragons Nest.[32]
When the existence of [[Yog-Sothoth]] Elderblood was revealed, Leona was among the wives confronting the implications of Victor somehow acquiring another extraordinarily powerful daughter through his connection with [[Azathoth Elderblood]]. The event produced the expected mixture of jealousy, protectiveness and familial competition, but the older women ultimately treated Yog as a child who needed a family rather than simply another source of cosmic power.[33]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
By the later Imperial period, Leona had completely transitioned from Alpha Werewolf into one of the principal Dragon Goddesses of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Pantheon. Chapter 1119 shows her moving through Imperial territory with the status of one of the highest-ranking Goddesses in Dragons Nest. Despite that authority, she deliberately visits an Imperial morgue as a civilian and follows its procedures rather than invoking Victor's name to bypass the institution.[7]
The same chapter provides the clearest statement of Leona's current Divine identity. Imperial records identify her as a Goddess of Animals, Wildlife and Wolves, with the additional lesser Divinities of [[Rex Invictus|Home]] and Nature. Her evolved perception is also demonstrated directly: she can hear another person's unspoken thoughts and can look through specialized isolation material and highly resistant coffins, immediately determining that a corpse resembling Edward contains no Werewolf genes and is biologically Human.[7]
Leona subsequently spends time with Leone Luna Elena Elderblood, whose upbringing demonstrates how seriously she and Victor take the education of their children. Leona refuses to casually invade her daughter's mind simply because she possesses the ability, considers privacy important, and is deeply pleased when Leone shows gratitude for her privileged life rather than behaving like a spoiled Imperial Princess. She and Victor want their children to preserve the household's principles of humility, honor and the old Alucard ideal that family always comes first.[15]
Leona remained part of the inner household observing Victor's increasingly dangerous dealings with the greater Cosmos, including the deployment of his younger clone and the confrontation with the [[Veillon Empire]]. By this point, the women surrounding Victor routinely shared information and observed threats together rather than relying upon Victor to explain every decision after the fact.[34]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
During the massive accelerated development of the [[Elderblood Empire]], Leona became directly involved in building its military and legal institutions. Alongside Ariel, Kaguya, Nyx and Tasha, she helped establish the Sixth Legion, [[The Silent Judgment]]. The Legion functions as the Empire's visible instrument of judgment in newly acquired or unstable territories, carrying out punishment, execution and the cleansing of corrupted or openly rebellious worlds. [[Jeanne Elderblood]] serves as its principal commander, while Leona is explicitly designated to assume command whenever Jeanne is occupied elsewhere.[8]
Chapter 1146 reunites Leona with the people who knew [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] before his rise to power. While a major Imperial parade takes place, Leona spends time with Maya, Bellatrix, Leone, Adam, Johnny, Edward, Andrew and Fred. The conversation emphasizes how unusual Leona's position has become: only Violet, Leona and Anna are singled out as women who were personally connected to Victor from the beginning of his life, long before power became a reason for anyone to seek him out.[9]
The same gathering demonstrates Leona's current telepathic perception. While Leone and Mirabelle silently discuss Victor's old friends, Maya and Leona hear their entire mental conversation and exchange amused looks. Johnny later asks Leona to arrange training for his children beneath [[Scathach]]. Leona agrees that she can obtain Scathach's attention but warns him that becoming one of Scathach's actual disciples cannot be granted through family influence; Scathach personally decides whether someone possesses the talent and [[Will Solace|Will]] she considers worthy.[9]
Leona later trains Leone personally inside a chamber where Time flows more quickly. When her daughter declares that she wants to become at least as formidable as Scathach, Leona explains that Scathach's danger is not simply a question of possessing more Energy. Thousands of years of warfare, martial refinement and the refusal to stop training are what make her exceptional. Rather than discouraging Leone from pursuing an almost impossible standard, Leona immediately contacts Scathach and asks her to train their daughter. Scathach agrees to an entire year, while Leona mischievously produces dramatic music for the beginning of the ordeal and promises to cheer Leone on.[16]

### Trivia
- The Elizabeth Trait is not merely a cosmetic Lykos characteristic. Chapter 787 describes the lineage as unusually predatory and genetically dominant: even Progenitor Blood tends to enhance rather than overwrite it, while ordinary Divine ancestry primarily affects the Soul rather than replacing Lykos blood. Vlad identified Dragons as the major known mortal lineage capable of genuinely competing with this genetic dominance.[29]
- Leona's own history is an exception to the normal development of the Elizabeth Trait. Most carriers awaken their Werewolf nature during early childhood, whereas Leona remained dormant until adulthood.[1][14]
- When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] stated that he intended to take Tasha for himself, Leona narrowed her eyes and squeezed Victor's thigh despite simultaneously enjoying his affection, using the gesture to vent her jealousy when she knew the physical pressure would not hurt him.[30]
- There is a private trade in rare photographs of Victor among members of his household. Violet, Leona and Natashia possess some of the rarest photographs and consequently dominate much of that informal market.[35]
- Leona and Victor's canonical daughter is indexed as Leone Luna Elena Elderblood. An earlier alternate rendering of her name was a continuity error and should not be counted as a separate daughter. The author explicitly acknowledged the naming continuity problem in the material surrounding Chapter 1160.[16]

### Abilities

#### Powers & Abilities
- Blood Dragon Goddess Physiology: Leona was reconstructed from an Alpha Werewolf into a genuine Blood Dragon through [[Victor Elderblood]]'s Dragon lineage. Victor's transformation process was designed to absorb and elevate the useful characteristics of the recipient's former existence instead of reducing every transformed woman to an identical Dragon template, allowing Leona's Lykos and Werewolf-derived qualities to remain incorporated into her new existence.[1]
- True Dragon Physiology: Leona currently possesses the body and existence of a genuine True Dragon rather than the Alpha Werewolf physiology she possessed before Victor's mass racial conversion.[1]
- Dragon Goddess Physiology: By the later Elderblood Imperial era, Leona is explicitly identified as one of the highest-ranking Dragon Goddesses of the [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]]. Dragon Gods occupy a rank above ordinary Gods within Victor's Pantheon because they belong directly to the Draconic line that founded it.[2]
- Enlarged Draconic Humanoid Form: Leona's current humanoid Dragon Goddess body is described as the same height as [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]], placing her at approximately 4.70 meters tall in Chapter 1119.[2]
- Werewolf-Trait Preservation: The conversion did not simply erase everything originating from Leona's previous race. Her Lykos heritage and preexisting supernatural characteristics were incorporated into the new Blood Dragon body, consistent with Victor's stated intention to preserve and strengthen the defining qualities of each transformed woman.[1]
- Divine Raiment: Leona can possess supernatural clothing appropriate to her existence as a Dragon Goddess rather than being restricted to mundane material clothing.[2]
- Stardust Dress: One of Leona's preferred Divine Raiments is a tight white dress whose edges appear to be composed of stardust and faintly illuminate the surrounding area as she moves.[2]
- Divinities: Leona possesses five confirmed Divine Concepts. Her three principal Divinities are <Animals>, <Wildlife> and <Wolves>, while <[[Rex Invictus|Home]]> and <Nature> are explicitly identified as lesser Divinities.[2]
- <Animals>: One of Leona's three principal Divinities and part of the Divine identity recorded for her within the [[Elderblood Empire]].[2]
- <Wildlife>: One of Leona's principal Divinities, reflecting her affinity with undomesticated life and the natural world.[2]
- <Wolves>: One of Leona's principal Divinities and the Concept most directly connected to her origin as an Alpha Werewolf of [[Lykos Clan]].[2]
- <Home>: One of Leona's two confirmed lesser Divinities.[2]
- <Nature>: One of Leona's two confirmed lesser Divinities. Its existence is consistent with the natural-Energy affinity already demonstrated by Leona during her Werewolf period, although Chapter 1119 does not establish every possible application of the Concept.[2]
- Telepathy: Leona possesses directly demonstrated mental perception and communication rather than merely being assumed to possess them because of her Divine status.[2][3][4]

- Enhanced Smell: Leona can distinguish supernatural beings, physiological reactions, emotions and concealed targets through scent with extreme precision.[6][7]

- Emotion and Physiological-State Detection: Changes associated with emotional or physical excitement can be identified through another Werewolf's scent.[6]

- Dominance Aura: Victor compared the response to the instinctive suppression ordinary Noble Vampires experience when confronted by a Vampire possessing a vastly superior lineage.[8]
- Werewolf Command Presence: Leona could force lower-ranking Wolves to instinctively acknowledge her status without physically attacking them.[8]
- Victor-Blood Empowerment: Victor noticed that Leona's Alpha influence had become more powerful after the development of their physical and supernatural relationship and concluded that his influence had strengthened her.[8]
- Nature Energy Manipulation (Werewolf-derived): As an Alpha Werewolf, Leona naturally produced a nearly invisible white supernatural Energy intimately associated with Nature.[9]
- Body Reinforcement: Leona circulated the white Energy through her body to reinforce her physical capabilities.[9]
- Physical Enhancement: [[Roxanne Elderblood]] directly recognized that the Energy surrounding Leona was being used to strengthen her body rather than simply being released wastefully into the environment.[9]
- Natural-Energy Affinity: Roxanne identified Leona's Energy as extremely similar to her own Nature Energy, although substantially less pure and mixed with qualities originating from Leona's Werewolf bloodline.[9]
- Werewolf-Nature Connection: The similarity caused Victor and Roxanne to recognize that high-level Werewolves possess an unusually close relationship with natural Energy.[9]
- Energy Projection (Werewolf-derived): Leona can directly release the supernatural Energy accumulated within her body as an offensive attack.[7]
- Energy Breath: During WarFall, Leona concentrated white Energy inside her mouth and discharged it forward as a destructive beam.[7]
- Behemoth Vaporization: A single discharge vaporized several enormous Behemoths rather than merely knocking them backward or injuring them.[7]
- Multi-Target Attack: The beam possessed enough effective width and output to destroy several giant opponents in a single release.[7]
- Werewolf Venom (Formerly demonstrated): During the night her Werewolf lineage awakened, Leona bit Victor and injected the transformative venom of her species into him.[10]
- Werewolf Transformation Inducement: Werewolf venom can initiate the conversion of a Human into a Werewolf if the target survives and the transformation is successfully completed.[10]
- Regenerative Induction: Leona explains that even the earliest stages of the Human-to-Werewolf transformation begin providing the victim with supernatural regeneration.[10]
- Contribution to Victor's [[Awakening]]: Victor identifies Leona's venom as one of the supernatural forces simultaneously acting upon his body and Soul during the abnormal night that produced his Vampire Progenitor existence.[10]
- Werewolf Marking Instinct: Leona's first overwhelming racial impulse after her abrupt Werewolf awakening was not indiscriminate violence but the need to locate an individual her instincts considered worthy of becoming her mate.[10]
- Mate Imprinting: Leona had already imprinted extremely strongly upon Victor, causing even her newly awakened Werewolf instincts to select him independently.[10]
- Equal Recognition: Her instincts specifically drove her toward someone she subconsciously recognized as an equal rather than simply the nearest Human or an easily dominated partner.[10]
- Werewolf Mark: Leona's bite and imprint ultimately became the Werewolf component of her supernatural marital connection to Victor, later existing alongside their [[Soul Marriage]].[10][11]
- Elizabeth Trait: Leona possesses the rare mutation originating from [[Elizabeth Lykos]], the strongest wife of the original Werewolf Progenitor. The mutation fundamentally changes the development of the carrier's instinctive Werewolf side and increases the potential available to the individual.[6][12]
- Accelerated Instinctive Development: The mutation causes the Werewolf's instinctive side to advance considerably faster than that of an ordinary member of the species.[6]
- Overall Ability Enhancement: The accelerated development of the instinctive side translates into a significant increase in the carrier's overall supernatural capability.[6]
- Exceptional Potential: Individuals possessing the mutation are regarded by Clan Lykos as having abnormally high future potential.[6]
- Rare Mutation: The Elizabeth Trait ordinarily resurfaces only once every three or four generations of Clan Lykos.[12]
- Direct Elizabeth Successor: The Lykos Matriarch detected the mutation within Leona at birth and personally gave her the middle name Elizabeth. Within the Clan's traditions, receiving that name directly from the Matriarch made Leona an intended potential successor to the ancestral line.[12]
- Lineage Enhancement: The mutation does not affect only its immediate carrier. Clan records describe descendants born from an Elizabeth carrier as inheriting substantially increased talent and potential.[12]
- Offspring Potential Increase: The next generation's talent and potential are described as being approximately one to three times greater depending upon the individual.[12]
- Predatory Genetic Dominance: The Lykos lineage is unusually dominant when mixed with foreign bloodlines and tends to preserve the Clan's characteristic traits instead of being overwritten.[13]
- Resistance to Progenitor-Blood Overwrite: Vlad explains that Progenitor Blood normally enhances the lineage inherited from the other parent instead of replacing it, allowing Lykos characteristics to remain intact.[13]
- Resistance to Divine-Lineage Overwrite: Because Gods are more spiritual than purely biological existences, ordinary Divine ancestry primarily alters the Soul while the Lykos bloodline remains dominant in the body.[13]
- Dragon-Blood Competition: Vlad considered Dragons the major known mortal lineage whose blood was powerful enough to genuinely compete with the predatory Lykos genes. Leona's later complete transformation into a Blood Dragon demonstrates that the Elizabeth lineage is not absolutely immune to all higher racial restructuring.[13][1]
- Elizabeth Instincts: The mutation dramatically intensifies the instinctive portion of its bearer.[6]
- Enhanced Predatory Instincts: As the mutation develops, its carrier can become far more instinct-driven than an ordinary Werewolf.[6]
- Emotional Intensification: The increasing dominance of instinct can cause pronounced emotional instability when the individual lacks appropriate training or control.[6]
- Reduced Rational Restraint (Potential Side Effect): An uncontrolled carrier may progressively behave more like an instinct-driven Wolf than a rational person.[6]
- Instinct-Control Requirement: Because of these risks, Clan Lykos considers early training particularly important for children carrying the mutation.[6]
- Leona's Delayed Development: Leona is an important exception to the normal pattern. Rather than awakening between approximately three and six years old, she remained physically sickly throughout much of her youth and experienced an abrupt Werewolf awakening only as an adult.[6][10]
- Environmental Suppression of Awakening: Bella theorized that Leona's sheltered life in the Human World, away from Samar's more instinct-oriented Werewolf environment, contributed to delaying the manifestation of the mutation.[12]
- Adult Rational Development: Because Leona developed her rational Human personality before her Werewolf instincts fully awakened, she avoided several psychological problems that can afflict carriers exposed to the mutation in early childhood.[12]
- Mate-Instinct Stabilization: Her strongest mate-related instincts were also directed toward and eventually satisfied through Victor, further reducing the conflict between her rational personality and her Werewolf impulses.[12]
- Supernatural Familial Bond: Through the combination of her Werewolf Mark, relationship with Victor and later Soul Marriage, Leona is integrated into the supernatural network connecting Victor's household. This gives her access to the family's direct mental communications, demonstrated when she contacts Scathach without an external device.[11][4]

Despite being capable of forcing ordinary Imperial employees to obey her, she deliberately refuses to misuse her status while acting as a civilian because doing so would undermine the laws and public image maintained by Victor's family.[2]

#### Limitations and Unconfirmed Applications
- Leona's confirmed Divinities should not automatically be treated as proof of every hypothetical power associated with their names. Chapter 1119 identifies <Animals>, <Wildlife>, <Wolves>, <[[Rex Invictus|Home]]> and <Nature>, but does not individually demonstrate unrestricted creation, evolution, devolution, possession, transformation or absolute control over all animals, Wolves, ecosystems, weather, geology, matter or Energy.[2]
- The broad Animal Manipulation and Nature Manipulation trees previously attached to Leona therefore should only be expanded when individual applications are directly demonstrated in the novel rather than being inferred solely from the names of her Divinities.[2]
- Elizabeth Form is not a confirmed transformation belonging to Leona. Chapters 772–773 establish the Elizabeth mutation, while the separate Complete Werewolf transformation discussed in that material belongs to Maya. Leona should not automatically inherit Maya's Complete Werewolf abilities simply because both women possess the same ancestral mutation.[6][12]
- The usual Elizabeth-carrier awakening age of approximately three to six years does not describe Leona. Her adulthood awakening is explicitly exceptional.[6][10]
- The Lykos lineage's genetic dominance is exceptional but not absolute. Chapter 787 specifically identifies Dragon blood as the major mortal lineage capable of competing with it, and Leona was later successfully reconstructed into a complete Blood Dragon.[13][1]
- Generic Dragon abilities such as unrestricted Shapeshifting, every possible Breath attack, automatic mastery of all Draconic Runes, arbitrary Creation or every ability possessed by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] should not be individually assigned to Leona solely from the fact that she is a True Dragon. They should be indexed when Leona demonstrates them herself or when the novel explicitly establishes them as part of her transformation.
- Leona's thought reading, telepathic communication, penetrating vision and genetic analysis are not speculative Dragon abilities; all have direct demonstrations in the later story.[2][3][4]
