### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 996
Show me, Champion of the Light! Show me your power! I will defeat you with my Sword of Darkness! I, the Herald of Darkness, Evelyn Elderblood! And your loyal minion Nikolina Nia Nef—...
—Evelyn while role-playing with Nikolina and her sisters, Chapter 996
Evelyn Elderblood is a recurring character, an Imperial Princess of the [[Elderblood Empire]], and the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Eve Elderblood]]. She belongs to Victor's second generation of naturally born [[Blood Dragons]], possessing a genuine True Dragon foundation from birth rather than undergoing the racial transformations experienced by many members of the previous Elderblood generation.[1][2]
Evelyn is introduced during the period in which Victor's enormous new family is revealed after roughly two thousand subjective years had passed for many members of the Elderblood household through accelerated Time. She is one of several daughters born from combinations of Victor's Blood Dragon existence and the supernatural characteristics of his wives, alongside figures such as [[Angelina Elderblood]], [[Gina Elderblood]], [[Nikolina Nia Nefela Elderblood]] and others.[1]
Unlike several of her sisters whose introductory chapters immediately explain their personal Divinities, Evelyn's individual Divine Concept and specialized supernatural inheritance have not yet been revealed in the reviewed material. Her earliest scenes instead focus almost entirely upon her personality and family life: she is imaginative, competitive, somewhat theatrical and intensely attached to Victor, spending her debut role-playing an exaggerated battle between Light and Darkness with Nikolina, [[Melina Elderblood]] and [[Julia Elderblood]].[3][2]
Her introduction also demonstrates the extreme father-centered attachment common throughout Victor's second generation. Evelyn's cautious attitude toward [[Anna Elderblood]] vanishes the instant she realizes Victor is nearby. She immediately abandons the game, rushes toward him and joins her sisters in competing for his attention, eventually manifesting her black Dragon wings as she relaxes in her father's presence.[2]

### Physical Appearance
Evelyn is introduced as a small young girl with black hair, pale skin and violet eyes.[2]
Chapter 997 notes that the four girls gathered together generally resemble their respective mothers, including sharing their mothers' hair colors. Evelyn consequently strongly resembles [[Eve Elderblood]], while her violet eyes reflect the characteristic Elderblood inheritance associated with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s descendants.[2]
Her exact natural height is not stated in the reviewed chapter material.
Despite her youthful Humanoid appearance, Evelyn is a complete True Dragon. When she relaxes around Victor, Dragon wings matching her black hair emerge from her back. She and her sisters excitedly flap their wings around him, leading [[Leon Walker]] to privately compare them to young birds competing for their father's attention.[2]
The contrast between Evelyn's childish appearance and her actual supernatural nature is characteristic of Victor's early second-generation daughters. Their bodies may appear young, but all possess Draconic foundations considered monstrous by the standards of the supernatural world.[2]

### Personality
Evelyn is playful, imaginative, dramatic, competitive and affectionate.
Her first scene is essentially an elaborate children's role-playing game. Evelyn casts herself as the Herald of Darkness, wields an imaginary Sword of Darkness and challenges a supposed Champion of Light while attempting to introduce Nikolina as her loyal minion.[3]
The performance collapses when Evelyn attempts to say Nikolina's complete name and bites her tongue halfway through it.
Rather than admitting defeat gracefully, Evelyn immediately complains that Nikolina's name is absurdly long and jokes that the supposed minion sounds more important than the person introducing her.[3]
The incident shows that Evelyn possesses a strong theatrical streak but does not take herself particularly seriously. Her sisters freely tease her, and Evelyn responds with huffing, complaints and internal grumbling rather than genuine anger.
She is also competitive with Nikolina.
When Nikolina objects to being called a minion and argues that she should instead be the Herald of Darkness because Nyx Elderblood is the Primordial Goddess of Night, Evelyn rejects the argument and tells her not to use their mothers as leverage.[3]
Nikolina immediately accuses Evelyn of doing exactly the same thing with Eve whenever it is convenient.
Evelyn visibly shrinks because she knows the accusation is accurate.[3]
This suggests that Evelyn is quite proud of her mother and is willing to invoke Eve's reputation when arguing with her sisters, although the exact supernatural abilities Evelyn herself inherited from Eve remain unstated.
Evelyn also enjoys directing the group's games.
When one of her sisters breaks character and begins discussing Nyx normally, Evelyn immediately orders her to remain in character. The other girls respond by declaring the role-playing game finished after Evelyn's tongue-biting mistake, leaving Evelyn frustrated that everyone is using the accident against her.[2]
Despite this bossiness, the interaction is clearly affectionate. Evelyn, Nikolina, Melina and Julia spend their time playing together rather than behaving as distant Divine Princesses.
Evelyn's attitude toward adults differs depending upon who is speaking.
When Anna tells the girls that they are being too loud, all four immediately groan. Evelyn cautiously approaches the room and asks whether Anna is there, behaving like a child who suspects she may be about to receive a reprimand.[2]
The instant Evelyn sees [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], all of that caution disappears.
She smiles broadly, throws the door open and leaps toward him while shouting "Daddy!"[2]
This reaction makes Evelyn's strongest demonstrated emotional trait very clear: she is enormously attached to her father.
Once Victor is present, the game she had been taking so seriously immediately becomes irrelevant. The girls themselves explain the priority openly—being with Victor matters more.
Evelyn eventually becomes so relaxed and happy in his presence that her defenses drop and her Dragon wings emerge unconsciously alongside those of her sisters.[2]
Her behavior contributes to Anna and Leon's realization that Victor's daughters are extraordinarily father-centered. Anna remarks that whenever their mothers interrupt them, the girls react like children caught misbehaving, while Victor's presence causes them to abandon whatever they are doing and immediately swarm around him.[2]
Evelyn also appears curious and eager to participate in anything Victor proposes. When Victor later remarks that the gathering may be a good opportunity for something, Evelyn immediately asks what he wants to do.[4]
Although her debut establishes a lively personality, the reviewed late-story chronology does not provide another Evelyn-centered arc comparable to the development given to daughters such as Stella, Leone, Ariella or Kassandra. Claims about Evelyn's mature personality, personal Divinity or specialized Imperial role should therefore remain limited until the novel directly provides them.

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Evelyn belongs to the enormous new Elderblood generation revealed after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family experienced approximately two thousand subjective years through accelerated Time.
By this period, many of Victor's wives had produced daughters carrying combinations of their own supernatural characteristics with Victor's Blood Dragon lineage. Evelyn was born from Victor and [[Eve Elderblood]], making her one of the naturally born [[True Dragons]] raised inside the new Elderblood civilization.[1]
Her first appearance begins near the end of Chapter 996.
Victor, Anna and Leon are discussing whether Leon should be permitted to meet Victor's daughters. Victor eventually agrees, although his extreme possessiveness leads him to scrutinize Leon's entire existence before accepting that his father's intentions are genuinely those of a grandfather rather than anything inappropriate.[3]
Before the conversation can continue, the adults hear the voice of a young girl dramatically declaring battle.
It is Evelyn.
She is role-playing as the Herald of Darkness and challenging an imaginary Champion of Light with her Sword of Darkness. Nikolina has been assigned the role of her loyal minion.[3]
Evelyn attempts to dramatically announce Nikolina's complete name:
"Nikolina Nia Nef—..."
She bites her tongue.
The dramatic confrontation immediately collapses.[3]
Evelyn covers her mouth in pain before angrily asking why Nikolina's name has to be so long.
Nikolina blames her mother.
According to her explanation, Victor had initially wanted the simpler name Nikolina Elderblood, but Nyx could not decide between the additional names Nia and Nefela and eventually used both.[3]
Nikolina then objects to being Evelyn's minion and argues that she should actually be the Herald of Darkness because her own mother is the Primordial Goddess of Night.
Evelyn tells her not to use their mothers to win the argument.
Nikolina immediately points out that Evelyn regularly invokes Eve when it benefits her.
Evelyn has no convincing response.[3]
The scene continues into Chapter 997.
The other two girls involved in the game—Melina and Julia—begin teasing Evelyn over biting her tongue. Evelyn internally complains that her sisters are petty and insists the entire accident is Nikolina's fault for having such an unnecessarily long name.[2]
The narration then identifies the group.
The two black-haired girls are Evelyn, the daughter of Eve and Victor, and [[Nikolina Nia Nefela Elderblood]], the daughter of Nyx and Victor. Their blonde sisters are Melina and Julia.[2]
All four physically resemble their mothers to varying degrees.
Their ordinary childhood game provides an unusual contrast with what they actually are: every girl present belongs to a generation of supernatural Beings whose baseline potential is monstrous by the standards of almost everyone outside Victor's family.[2]
The girls become loud enough to attract Anna's attention.
"Girls, you're making too much noise."[2]
All four groan simultaneously.
Evelyn becomes cautious and asks whether Mother Anna is actually nearby.
Anna confirms that she is and invites them inside.
Evelyn slowly peeks through the doorway, initially looking toward Anna.[2]
Then she notices Victor.
Everything else becomes irrelevant.
"Daddy!"[2]
Evelyn throws the door open and rushes toward him.
The other girls follow immediately.
What had been an important role-playing battle seconds earlier is abandoned without hesitation because seeing Victor takes complete priority.[2]
Victor happily embraces his daughters and asks whether they have finished playing.
The girls explain that they were enjoying themselves, but being with Daddy is more important.
Evelyn becomes almost completely absorbed in Victor's presence, happily repeating "Daddy" while remaining close to him.[2]
As the girls relax, Dragon wings corresponding to their hair colors emerge from their backs.
Evelyn consequently manifests black Draconic wings.[2]
Leon silently observes the scene.
To him, the daughters resemble young birds eagerly flapping their wings around their father.
Anna explains that this is normal.
The girls love Victor so much that their mothers often struggle to compete for their attention whenever he is present.[2]
The family moment continues into Chapter 998.
Victor eventually asks Anna to enlarge the room and calls all of his daughters across the planet to him. Evelyn and the other girls are joined by the rest of the enormous second generation, turning the area around Victor into what Leon perceives as an entire nest of young Dragons.[4]
The scene is less important for Evelyn's individual power than for establishing the family environment in which she grew up.
Despite being born as a Dragon Goddess in one of the most powerful families in Creation, Evelyn's early life is filled with games, arguments with sisters, teasing, maternal discipline and an overwhelmingly affectionate relationship with Victor.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Evelyn's biological father and the most important figure in her demonstrated emotional life.
Evelyn's behavior changes immediately when she realizes he is nearby.
She initially approaches Anna's room cautiously because she expects that the girls may have been caught making too much noise. The instant her eyes move from Anna to Victor, the hesitation disappears completely.[2]
She throws the door open and rushes directly toward him.
Evelyn subsequently loses interest in the role-playing game that had occupied all of her attention moments earlier and simply enjoys remaining beside her father.[2]
Her reaction mirrors that of several sisters.
Anna explains that Victor's daughters will frequently complain when interrupted by their mothers but immediately abandon their activities when Victor calls them.[2]
Victor reciprocates the affection.
He gathers the girls around himself, embraces them and openly describes them as his beloved daughters.
Evelyn's early characterization is consequently dominated by a highly affectionate father-daughter bond rather than by political or military ambitions.

#### Eve Elderblood
[[Eve Elderblood]] is Evelyn's biological mother.
Evelyn physically resembles Eve, most notably through her black hair. Chapter 997 establishes that the daughters playing together generally possess the same hair colors as their mothers.[2]
Her dialogue also suggests considerable pride in Eve.
Nikolina specifically accuses Evelyn of using Mother Eve as leverage whenever it becomes convenient during arguments between the sisters.[3]
Evelyn's embarrassed reaction strongly implies that Nikolina is correct.
However, the reviewed material does not yet identify the exact supernatural ability Evelyn inherited from Eve.
Eve herself possesses a highly unusual Corruption/Black-Fire-oriented Draconic nature, but that does not justify automatically assigning the same power to Evelyn without an explicit feat or statement.

#### Nikolina Nia Nefela Elderblood
[[Nikolina Nia Nefela Elderblood]] is one of Evelyn's closest demonstrated playmates and sisters.
Their debut consists primarily of the two arguing over an imaginary adventure.
Evelyn assigns herself the position of Herald of Darkness and Nikolina the role of loyal minion.
Nikolina refuses.
She argues that as the daughter of Nyx, she has a far better claim to Darkness-related titles than Evelyn.[3]
The argument quickly turns into the sisters accusing one another of using their mothers' reputations whenever convenient.
Evelyn's attempt to introduce Nikolina dramatically ultimately ends the game when she bites her tongue while trying to pronounce all of her sister's names.[3]
Despite their bickering, the interaction is entirely playful.
The moment [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] appears, both girls forget the argument and rush toward him together.

#### Melina Elderblood
[[Melina Elderblood]] participates in the same game alongside Evelyn and Nikolina.
Unlike Evelyn's theatrical behavior, Melina remains comparatively calm and is one of the sisters who immediately declares that the game is over once Evelyn bites her tongue.[2]
Evelyn internally complains that Melina and the others are being petty for refusing to ignore her mistake.

#### Julia Elderblood
[[Julia Elderblood]] is the fourth girl participating in Evelyn's introductory game.
Like Melina, Julia teases Evelyn once the attempted dramatic introduction fails.[2]
The four girls' ordinary play is one of the clearest demonstrations that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his wives deliberately allowed their daughters to experience childhood despite their overwhelming supernatural nature.

#### Anna Elderblood
[[Anna Elderblood]] functions as one of Evelyn's many maternal figures within the Elderblood household.
When Anna tells the girls they are making too much noise, Evelyn immediately becomes cautious and checks whether Anna is genuinely nearby.[2]
Evelyn nevertheless trusts her enough to enter when Anna invites them inside.
Her reaction also demonstrates the difference between Evelyn's behavior toward [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and the other parental figures around her: Anna can make her behave, but Victor can instantly make every other concern disappear.

### Skills & Abilities
- Blood Dragon Goddess Physiology: Evelyn is the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Eve Elderblood]] and was born directly into Victor's Blood Dragon lineage. She possesses the foundation of a genuine True Dragon rather than requiring a later transformation.[1][2]

- True Dragon Soul: Her spiritual existence possesses a natural Draconic foundation.
- [[Dragon Heart]]: As a complete Blood Dragon, Evelyn possesses the supernatural Energy-generating system characteristic of the Race.
- [[Draconic Energy]]: Her body naturally generates and circulates Draconic Energy.
- Enhanced Physical Abilities: Evelyn possesses the tremendous physical potential characteristic of Victor's naturally born Blood Dragon daughters.
- Enhanced Senses: Her Draconic body possesses supernatural sensory abilities beyond ordinary mortal limits.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Evelyn possesses the powerful regenerative foundation characteristic of [[Blood Dragons]].
- Extreme Longevity: Her development follows the lifespan and maturation of a True Dragon rather than an ordinary Human.
- Draconic Shapeshifting: Evelyn possesses the racial foundation for Draconic bodily transformation.
- Connection to Creation: As a True Dragon, she possesses exceptional natural compatibility with the supernatural powers and Energies of Creation.
- Draconic Language: Evelyn possesses the racial foundation for the supernatural [[Will Solace|Will]]-bearing language of Dragons.
- Draconic Runes: Her True Dragon nature gives her natural compatibility with basic Draconic Runic principles. This does not establish advanced Runemaster-level proficiency without an individual feat.
- Dragon Breath: Evelyn possesses the racial foundation for Dragon Breath, although the nature of her personal Breath has not yet been directly demonstrated.
- Draconic Wing Manifestation: Evelyn can manifest Dragon wings from her back.[2]
- Black Dragon Wings: Evelyn's wings match her black hair.[2]
- Emotional Manifestation: Her wings emerge while she and her sisters relax their defenses and become excited around Victor.[2]
- Flight (Racial): As functional True Dragon wings, they provide the racial foundation for aerial movement, although Evelyn's introductory scene focuses upon excited wing-flapping rather than an individual flight feat.
- Supernatural Perception (Elderblood Inheritance): As one of Victor's biological daughters, Evelyn belongs to the generation later established as inheriting the supernatural deep-violet eyes associated with Victor. The exact individual strength of Evelyn's version has not been demonstrated in a focused scene.
- Enhanced Vision: Her violet Draconic eyes possess a supernatural foundation beyond normal biological vision.
- Variable Inherited Potency: The inherited eye ability varies among Victor's daughters and develops through training; Evelyn's exact level should therefore not be assumed from the feats of sisters such as Mirabelle or Leone.
- High Intelligence (Draconic Baseline): Evelyn belongs to the generation of True Dragon daughters raised under the Elderblood family's advanced education and accelerated developmental environments.
- Imaginative Role-Playing: Her introductory scene demonstrates considerable creativity and enthusiasm for constructing fictional roles and scenarios.[3]
- Social Competitiveness: Evelyn quickly argues over status, roles and perceived advantages during games with her sisters.[3]
- Verbal Improvisation: She readily invents dramatic titles such as Herald of Darkness and Sword of Darkness as part of play.[3]
- Divinity (Unidentified): Evelyn is a Dragon Goddess, but the reviewed chapters do not reveal the canonical name or demonstrated abilities of her personal Divine Concept.
- Unknown Inheritance from Eve: Although Evelyn is Eve's biological daughter, the novel material reviewed for this page does not establish whether she inherited Eve's Corruption, Black Fire or another specialized ability.
- No Confirmed Darkness Divinity: Evelyn's self-proclaimed Herald of Darkness title and Sword of Darkness occur explicitly during a children's role-playing game and should not be treated as evidence that she possesses a Darkness Divinity or Darkness Manipulation.[3]
- No Confirmed Weapon: The Sword of Darkness is part of the role-play and is not presently established as a real named weapon belonging to Evelyn.[3]

### Early Development
Evelyn belongs to the first large generation of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters raised almost entirely after the Elderblood family had already become one of the strongest supernatural factions in existence.
This gives her upbringing a radically different structure from Eve's.
Eve experienced abuse, imprisonment and extreme hardship before Victor rescued her.
Evelyn was born directly into a family where she possessed:
- a True Dragon body;
- Divine status;
- extraordinary protection;
- numerous mothers and sisters;
- access to advanced magical and technological education;
- and a father whose possessiveness makes threats against his daughters almost unthinkable.
Her introductory chapter consequently does not revolve around survival.
It revolves around playing with her sisters.
This contrast is particularly meaningful for Eve's branch of the family. Evelyn represents a generation able to experience the carefree childhood Eve herself was denied.

### Limitations
- Unknown Personal Divinity: Evelyn's status as a Dragon Goddess does not establish the nature of her personal Divine Concept.
- Role-Playing ≠ Darkness Power: Her titles Herald of Darkness and Sword of Darkness are part of a game and cannot be used as supernatural feats.[3]
- Eve's Powers ≠ Automatically Evelyn's Powers: Eve possesses specialized Corruption and Black-Fire abilities, but biological descent alone is insufficient evidence that Evelyn possesses identical abilities.
- Basic Draconic Rune Compatibility ≠ Rune Mastery: Being a True Dragon establishes natural compatibility with Draconic Language and Runes, not mastery equivalent to specialists such as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], [[Scathach]] or [[Dún Scáith|Dun Scaith]].
- Dragon Breath Properties Unknown: No Evelyn-specific elemental or conceptual Breath has been demonstrated.
- No Confirmed Limitless Growth: The previous generic claim that Evelyn possesses limitless growth because her Divinity originates outside the Cosmology has been removed. No reviewed chapter directly establishes this as an individual ability.
- No Generic God Ability List: Powers such as Blessing Bestowal, Curse Bestowal or other broad Divine abilities should not be assigned solely because Evelyn is classified as a Goddess.
- Limited Individual Feats: Most of Evelyn's currently demonstrated scenes are family interactions rather than combat, so her actual specialized fighting style remains largely unknown.
