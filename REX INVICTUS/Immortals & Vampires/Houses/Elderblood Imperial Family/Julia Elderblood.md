### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 997
Julia Elderblood, also known as [[Julia Fulger]] through her maternal lineage, is a recurring character in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]], an Imperial Princess of the [[Elderblood Empire]], and the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Natasha Elderblood Fulger]], also known as Naty. She is a naturally born Blood Dragon Goddess belonging to Victor's second generation of genuine [[True Dragons]]. Her supernatural existence combines the extraordinary physical and spiritual characteristics of her father's Draconic Bloodline with the Divine inheritance of the Fulger family, producing a daughter whose natural abilities are exceptional even among the powerful members of the Imperial Family.[1]
Julia possesses the Divine Concept of Cultivation, an unusually broad authority associated with the process of cultivating, nurturing, developing and progressively improving something. Although the Concept naturally includes activities associated with agriculture, its meaning extends considerably beyond the growth of crops or the maintenance of farmland. Victor recognizes that agriculture represents only one manifestation of Cultivation, since the fundamental principle concerns the development of something through deliberate nurturing or improvement. This distinction gives Julia an exceptionally versatile conceptual foundation, although the exact limits and practical applications of her Divine Authority remain incompletely demonstrated.[1]
Her Divinity is particularly remarkable because Julia possesses abnormally large reserves of supernatural Energy, exceeding the already extraordinary natural reserves possessed by many of Victor's biological daughters. Her immense power creates difficulties during her early development because she has not yet acquired sufficient control to distribute her Divinity properly throughout her body. A considerable concentration of this Energy consequently accumulates within her hair, producing accelerated growth and making it unusually resistant to being cut. The resulting condition gives Julia one of the most distinctive physical appearances among the daughters of the Fulger family, with exceptionally long and curly orange-blonde hair extending approximately to her ankles.[1]
Julia also occupies an important position within the history of Clan Fulger because of the circumstances surrounding her name. Natasha deliberately named her after Julia, the Human Maid who raised [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] and effectively became her maternal figure during the period when Natasha failed to provide the emotional support her daughter required. The original Julia's kindness, loyalty and devotion had a profound influence upon Sasha's childhood, making her death one of the most painful events in the young Vampire's life. Naming her daughter Julia therefore represented an acknowledgement of the Maid's importance and Natasha's determination to avoid repeating the mistakes that had damaged her relationship with Sasha.[1]
Although Julia possesses extraordinary supernatural potential, relatively little of her individual combat development has been demonstrated. Her principal confirmed characteristics are her conceptual authority over Cultivation, exceptional Energy reserves, unusual Divinity-related physical development and natural Blood Dragon physiology. Her early appearances emphasize her relationships with her sisters and the emotional significance of her birth rather than individual military accomplishments. During the later Imperial era, she remains among the daughters associated with the Fulger lineage, retaining a strong resemblance to Natasha while participating in the traditional gatherings maintained by Victor and his enormous family.[1][2]
Julia's identity should be distinguished from that of Aline Elderblood Fulger and the other daughters associated with Clan Fulger. Julia is explicitly identified as the daughter of Natasha/Naty, while the later introduction of Ekaterina Natasha Elderblood and [[Alisa Sasha Elderblood]] contains inconsistent maternal labels that complicate the identification of several Fulger descendants. These discrepancies do not alter Julia's established parentage, but neither should the later names be dismissed automatically as nonexistent characters. The daughters' individual identities must be distinguished according to the relationships actually established for them rather than inferred solely from the similarity of their names.[1][2]

### Physical Appearance
Julia is an exceptionally beautiful young Dragon Goddess with fair skin, violet eyes and extremely long orange-blonde hair. Her facial features strongly resemble those of [[Natasha Elderblood Fulger]], making her appear almost like a younger version of her biological mother. Although both possess blonde hair, Julia's coloration has a noticeably stronger orange tint than Natasha's comparatively golden-blonde appearance. Her hair is also considerably curlier and less orderly, creating a distinctive appearance that separates her from the other blonde daughters belonging to the Fulger lineage.[1]
Her most recognizable physical characteristic is the extraordinary length of her hair, which naturally extends approximately to her ankles. Unlike ordinary long hair maintained primarily through personal preference, Julia's hair possesses an abnormal supernatural property caused by the concentration of her immature Divinity within it. Her exceptional Energy reserves are difficult for her to regulate properly during childhood, allowing substantial amounts of Divine power to accumulate in the strands and stimulate their continuous growth. The resulting condition makes her hair unusually difficult to cut, since the Energy saturating it provides additional supernatural resistance beyond the durability expected of conventional biological material.[1]
The unusual growth is considered a consequence of Julia's developing control rather than an immutable characteristic of her final supernatural state. As she learns to regulate her Divinity and distribute its power more efficiently throughout her body, the concentration within her hair is expected to become less troublesome. This does not mean that her hair will necessarily become ordinary or lose its distinctive length, but the underlying difficulty is associated with the incomplete control of her Energy rather than an independent permanent curse or a separate Divine Concept governing hair.[1]
Julia's violet eyes reflect the Blood Dragon lineage inherited through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Like his other biological descendants, she possesses the Draconic ocular foundation associated with the exceptional visual characteristics of the Race. Her eyes contrast with the orange-blonde coloration of her hair and reinforce the strong resemblance between her and the other Imperial Princesses whose mothers belong to Clan Fulger. Although Julia possesses the general inherited potential of Victor's Dragon Eyes, her individual mastery of the more advanced truth-perceiving characteristics demonstrated by some of her sisters has not been described in sufficient detail to establish its limits.
Her appearance during the later Imperial era continues to emphasize her maternal resemblance. When several blonde members of the second generation arrive together at a family gathering, Julia is specifically distinguished as one of the daughters whose facial features are almost identical to those of their mothers. Her resemblance to Natasha remains particularly striking despite the enormous supernatural development experienced by the family and the passage of accelerated Time. The other daughters present share several inherited characteristics, particularly blonde hair and violet eyes, but Julia's exceptionally long orange-blonde hair and maternal facial features remain important aspects of her appearance.[2]
As a naturally born True Dragon Goddess, Julia possesses a fundamentally Draconic physical and spiritual existence beneath her Humanoid appearance. Her actual age and developmental characteristics differ considerably from ordinary Human standards, and her extraordinary supernatural constitution provides the foundation for the transformative capabilities associated with genuine [[True Dragons]]. However, the complete appearance of her natural Dragon Form has not been individually described, and her exact height, weight, Dragon scale coloration and specialized anatomical characteristics remain unidentified.

### Personality
Julia possesses a comparatively relaxed and sociable personality, appearing comfortable in the company of her sisters and participating naturally in the ordinary games and conversations shared by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s children. Unlike several Imperial Princesses whose introductions emphasize aggressive tempers, extreme possessiveness or unusually demanding ambitions, Julia is initially presented through a lighthearted family interaction. Her behavior suggests that she is accustomed to the affectionate and informal atmosphere maintained within the Elderblood household, where extraordinary supernatural status does not prevent the daughters from enjoying ordinary childhood activities.[1]
Her first appearance places her alongside [[Evelyn Elderblood]], [[Nikolina Nia Nefela Elderblood]] and [[Melina Elderblood]], who are spending time together after a playful exchange involving Nikolina's unusually long full name. Evelyn becomes irritated because the other girls take advantage of her embarrassment after she accidentally bites her tongue while attempting to pronounce the name. Julia participates comfortably in the interaction, joining Melina in calmly teasing Evelyn rather than responding with the formal distance traditionally associated with an Imperial Princess. The scene establishes her familiarity with her sisters and her willingness to participate in ordinary family humor.[1]
Julia's relaxed disposition is especially significant because she was raised under circumstances fundamentally different from those experienced by Sasha during her own childhood. Natasha had previously been consumed by the problems surrounding the [[Fulger Bloodline]], treating the development of supernatural power as a priority while failing to provide Sasha with the affection and security she required. The Human Maid Julia consequently became the most important maternal figure in Sasha's life, providing the emotional support and everyday care that her biological mother neglected. Natasha's later decision to name her daughter after the Maid reflects the extent to which she has come to recognize the consequences of those earlier failures.[1]
Natasha becomes considerably more involved in the upbringing of her daughter Julia, deliberately attempting to provide the care she had withheld from Sasha. As a result, Julia grows up within a household where her mother is more attentive and where Victor and the other members of the family provide extensive emotional and practical support. This environment allows her to develop without the same isolation and resentment that characterized Sasha's early relationship with her parents. Her comfortable interactions with Evelyn, Nikolina and Melina demonstrate the more stable family life made possible by the extraordinary changes in Natasha's circumstances.[1]
Julia's personality should not be confused with the characteristics of her Divine Concept. Although she possesses authority over Cultivation, the available material does not establish that she is personally obsessed with agricultural work, martial cultivation, academic development or the pursuit of higher supernatural realms. Her Divinity provides the potential to influence such processes, but it does not automatically define her preferences or the activities she chooses to pursue. Likewise, her unusually large Energy reserves do not establish that she is aggressive or inclined toward combat merely because she possesses the strength necessary to become an exceptionally powerful fighter.
Her relationship with Natasha is among the most important aspects of her personal background. Julia represents an opportunity for her mother to demonstrate the emotional growth she achieved after confronting her past mistakes and obtaining a second chance at family life. Although their individual private conversations have not been extensively depicted, Natasha's deliberate involvement in Julia's upbringing establishes a substantially warmer environment than the one Sasha experienced. Julia consequently embodies an important change within the Fulger family, whose earlier generations had frequently prioritized supernatural power, political influence and Bloodline development above emotional wellbeing.
Julia remains a member of Victor's enormous second generation throughout the later Imperial era, participating in family gatherings alongside her sisters and maintaining the relationships established during her childhood. Her individual ambitions, specialized hobbies, preferred combat style and long-term professional interests remain largely unexplored. Her confirmed characterization is therefore best understood through her relaxed behavior, familial relationships and the significance of the circumstances surrounding her birth rather than through invented personality traits or achievements that have not been demonstrated.

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Julia was born during the extraordinary expansion of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family following the development of his Blood Dragon lineage into a genuinely Divine and Draconic dynasty. As the biological daughter of Victor Elderblood and [[Natasha Elderblood Fulger]], she inherited the supernatural foundation of a naturally born True Dragon Goddess rather than beginning life as a conventional Noble Vampire. Her maternal lineage connected her to one of [[Nightingale]]'s oldest and most influential Vampire families, whose members possessed a historical affinity with Lightning and the extraordinary characteristics associated with the [[Fulger Bloodline]]. Through Victor, Julia inherited the advanced Draconic foundation that made the second generation fundamentally different from the original Noble Vampire Clans.[1]
Natasha's ability to raise Julia personally was itself the consequence of extraordinary changes within the Fulger family. During the earlier period of her existence, Natasha had been an alternate personality originating from the fractured Soul of [[Annasthashia Elderblood Fulger]]. The two possessed conflicting temperaments and desires, with Natasha's actions contributing heavily to the deterioration of Clan Fulger and the suffering experienced by Sasha. Victor's later intervention allowed the personalities to become separate individuals, while also restoring [[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]] and transforming the family's supernatural foundation. The resulting independence gave Natasha the opportunity to establish a life distinct from Natashia's and eventually become the biological mother of her own daughter with Victor.
The decision to name her daughter Julia was deeply connected to Natasha's history with Sasha. During Sasha's childhood, Natasha had concentrated much of her attention upon the development of the Fulger Bloodline, failing to provide the emotional support expected of a mother. The Human Maid Julia consequently became the person who cared for Sasha, remaining beside her during difficult periods and providing affection, education and daily support. Sasha eventually regarded the Maid as her true maternal figure, making Julia's murder during an Inquisition attack one of the most devastating experiences of her early life.
Julia's death continued influencing the Fulger family long after Sasha had become one of Victor's wives. The loss forced Sasha to confront the emotional neglect she had experienced from her biological parents, while Victor recognized that the Maid had fulfilled the responsibilities of motherhood far more completely than the individuals who had brought Sasha into the world. Natasha's eventual recognition of these circumstances became an important part of her personal development, since she could no longer ignore the fact that another woman had provided her daughter with the affection she herself had withheld.
The original Julia's contribution to the family was eventually recognized through a formal memorial within the Fulger lineage. Her statue was placed among the family's honored dead, and her name was incorporated into the records of the Main Family despite her Human origins and previous position as a servant. The recognition established that loyalty, compassion and genuine familial devotion could carry greater significance than biological ancestry or inherited political rank, preserving the Maid's importance within the history of the Clan.
Sasha had also wished to preserve the memory of the original Julia by naming one of her own daughters after the woman who raised her. However, Natasha gave birth first and selected the same name for her daughter with Victor. The decision surprised Sasha because it demonstrated that Natasha had come to understand the importance of the Maid she had previously neglected to appreciate. The name therefore carried considerable emotional weight, serving not merely as a personal designation but as a permanent acknowledgement of the woman whose kindness had shaped Sasha's childhood.[1]
Natasha also deliberately changed her approach toward motherhood. Rather than repeating the emotional distance that had characterized her treatment of Sasha, she became substantially more involved in Julia's upbringing. The younger Julia consequently grew up surrounded by her parents, sisters and the extensive Elderblood household, receiving affection and attention in an environment where family unity was treated as one of the most important responsibilities of the ruling dynasty. Her childhood therefore contrasted sharply with the earlier Fulger tradition of prioritizing Bloodline development and political ambition over personal relationships.[1]
Julia's first individual appearance occurs during a gathering of four younger Imperial Princesses. Evelyn and Nikolina have been engaged in playful role-playing, while Melina and Julia observe the resulting argument. Evelyn becomes embarrassed after biting her tongue while attempting to pronounce Nikolina's unusually long name, and Melina and Julia take advantage of the incident to tease her. Their relaxed conversation reveals an ordinary family atmosphere beneath the overwhelming supernatural strength possessed by Victor's children, demonstrating that the daughters continue enjoying the games and interactions associated with childhood despite their extraordinary lineage.[1]
The gathering also provides an opportunity to examine the different Divine inheritances possessed by the younger generation. Julia is distinguished by the unusual characteristics of her Energy reserves and the nature of her personal Divinity. Although Victor's biological daughters already possess extraordinary amounts of supernatural Energy, Julia's reserves are exceptional even among them. Her natural power consequently exceeds what would ordinarily be expected of a young Dragon Goddess, giving her an unusually strong foundation for further development.[1]
Her personal Divine Concept is revealed to be Cultivation. The designation initially suggests abilities associated with agriculture, particularly the growing of crops and the nurturing of plant life. However, Victor recognizes that agriculture represents only one application of the underlying Concept. Cultivation itself concerns the deliberate process of developing, nurturing or improving something, meaning that its conceptual scope is potentially far broader than the agricultural Divinities traditionally associated with harvests or farmland.[1]
Victor considers the implications of this authority and finds them particularly amusing because of his familiarity with cultivation systems from fictional stories. In such settings, cultivators devote enormous periods to refining their abilities, increasing their Energy, improving their bodies and developing toward higher supernatural states. Julia's power represents a literal Divine relationship with the principle underlying those activities, making her Concept unusually appropriate for a civilization whose members possess extraordinary potential for continued growth. However, the full extent of the powers she can derive from the Concept remains unknown, since her individual training and combat applications have not yet been explored in detail.[1]
Julia's extraordinary Energy reserves also create an unusual developmental difficulty. A considerable portion of her Divinity accumulates within her hair rather than remaining properly regulated throughout her body, causing it to grow continuously and become exceptionally difficult to cut. The condition gives her extremely long, curly orange-blonde hair extending approximately to her ankles, a characteristic that distinguishes her from the other blonde daughters of the Fulger family. Her mother recognizes that the abnormal growth reflects Julia's immature control rather than a permanent defect in her existence, and the condition is expected to become more manageable as she develops greater mastery over her own Divinity.[1]
The interaction reveals that Julia's extraordinary natural abilities are accompanied by difficulties that require education and practice. Although she possesses more Energy than many advanced Divine beings, she must still learn how to regulate that power within her own body. Her condition therefore reflects one of the broader challenges experienced by Victor's second generation: being born with overwhelming potential does not automatically grant complete understanding or perfect control of every inherited supernatural characteristic.
Julia continues developing within the protected environment of the Elderblood family, where Victor and his wives provide extensive education concerning supernatural society, the responsibilities of Imperial status and the importance of understanding powers rather than relying exclusively upon their natural strength. Although her individual participation in later combat exercises has not been described in detail, she belongs to the generation whose extraordinary abilities require careful instruction to prevent their inherited potential from becoming a source of complacency or danger.

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Julia remains an active member of the Elderblood family throughout the extraordinary development of the Empire into a Galactic-scale civilization. The passage of accelerated Time allows [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wives and daughters to accumulate enormous subjective experience while the outside Cosmos progresses at a substantially different rate. The younger generation consequently matures under circumstances fundamentally different from those experienced by ordinary supernatural beings, gaining access to advanced education, technological resources and a civilization whose scale far exceeds the territories once governed by [[Nightingale]]'s [[Vampire Counts]].
The Empire's development also changes the responsibilities associated with the Imperial Princesses. Although their natural abilities already make them extraordinarily powerful individuals, Victor and his wives increasingly recognize the importance of providing practical experience beyond ordinary combat training. Their civilization governs enormous populations, possesses increasingly advanced technology and must prepare for conflicts involving opponents from more dangerous Sectors. The daughters are therefore expected to develop knowledge, judgment and restraint rather than assume that their inherited Blood Dragon physiology makes them automatically qualified to exercise political or military authority.
Julia's particular conceptual foundation remains relevant to this broader environment. Cultivation is associated with nurturing and improving something over time, an idea naturally connected to the developmental philosophy adopted by the Empire. Nevertheless, no confirmed scene establishes that Julia has personally taken charge of agricultural development, scientific cultivation projects, Imperial education or the improvement of supernatural races. Her Divinity provides a theoretical foundation compatible with such responsibilities, but her actual administrative position and long-term personal ambitions remain unidentified.
The extraordinary rarity of [[True Dragons]] becomes increasingly apparent during the Empire's expansion. Their numbers remain comparatively small despite the enormous size of Victor's family and civilization, with only approximately forty to fifty genuine True Dragons existing within the Sector during the relevant period. The restriction is deliberate, since Victor and his wives recognize that rapidly increasing the number of individuals possessing such extraordinary powers could destabilize the balance of their civilization and attract dangerous attention from higher cosmic authorities.[3]
The limited number of True Dragon births also reflects Victor's determination to preserve the Elderblood lineage as the defining supernatural foundation of the Imperial Family. He deliberately restricts the distribution of his Blood and avoids transforming ordinary citizens into genuine True Dragons merely to expand their numbers. Their scarcity is therefore not the result of conventional infertility or an inability to reproduce, but a conscious decision concerning the political and cosmological consequences of creating additional members of an overwhelmingly powerful Race.[3]
Julia subsequently joins a gathering of Victor's daughters alongside several members of the Fulger branch. The group arrives wearing the characteristic appearance of the younger Imperial Princesses, sharing blonde hair and violet Dragon Eyes. Among them are [[Sylvia Elderblood]], Ekaterina Natasha Elderblood, [[Svetlana Veronika Elderblood]] and [[Alisa Sasha Elderblood]], while Julia is specifically identified as a daughter associated with Natasha/Naty. The girls' arrival contributes to an increasingly large family reunion that eventually attracts more than thirty-five of Victor's daughters.[2]
The gathered Fulger descendants possess varying degrees of physical resemblance to their parents. Alisa and Svetlana are described as inheriting more of Victor's facial characteristics, whereas Julia and Silvia appear almost identical to their mothers. Julia's distinctive appearance therefore remains strongly associated with Natasha even after the immense passage of accelerated Time, reinforcing the close resemblance established during her original introduction.[2]
The reunion develops after several daughters become involved in a prank targeting [[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]]. Although the incident initially causes confusion, the girls treat it primarily as ordinary sibling mischief rather than genuine hostility. More daughters arrive to hear the account, eventually bringing together members of numerous maternal lineages who would otherwise be occupied with different responsibilities throughout the Empire. The gathering demonstrates how Victor's enormous family continues preserving ordinary familial relationships despite the extraordinary development of their civilization.[2]
[[Nero]], Ophis, [[Metis]], Akasha and Melida eventually join the gathering, followed by several members of the older generation. Their arrival produces a mixture of affection, teasing and competitive behavior, particularly because many of Victor's biological daughters possess unusually intense attachments to their father. Julia remains among the Fulger descendants participating in the larger reunion, although no additional individual confrontation or specialized use of her Cultivation Divinity is attributed to her during the event.[2]
The gathering also reinforces the extraordinary diversity of the second generation. Although the daughters share the fundamental Blood Dragon lineage of Victor, their individual mothers contribute different supernatural characteristics, producing descendants with distinct Divine Concepts, physical appearances and developmental paths. Julia's Cultivation authority and abnormal Energy reserves remain among the unusual manifestations of this inheritance, while her strong resemblance to Natasha preserves the visible characteristics of the Fulger family within the advanced Draconic dynasty.
Julia's later personal development remains largely unexplored. Her continued presence among the Imperial Princesses establishes that she remains part of the active Elderblood household, but her complete mastery of Cultivation, possible administrative responsibilities, individual combat achievements and current subjective age have not been explicitly demonstrated. Her established significance therefore remains centered upon her unusual conceptual foundation, extraordinary Energy constitution and the important emotional history associated with her name.

### Divinity

#### Cultivation
Julia possesses the Divine Concept of Cultivation, a broad conceptual authority associated with the deliberate nurturing, development and improvement of something. Unlike a conventional agricultural Divinity restricted primarily to the production of crops or the maintenance of farmland, Cultivation encompasses the underlying process through which something is progressively developed toward a desired condition. Agriculture is explicitly included within the Concept because planting, caring for and developing crops is itself a form of cultivation, but the principle is not limited exclusively to biological plant growth.[1]
The distinction is important because Cultivation operates as a general conceptual principle rather than a narrowly defined elemental ability. Cultivating a plant involves providing the conditions necessary for its growth, while cultivating a skill involves deliberate practice and refinement. Similar language can describe the development of supernatural Energy, martial ability, knowledge and other characteristics. However, the fact that these activities can be described as cultivation does not automatically establish that Julia possesses unrestricted control over every phenomenon associated with the word. The scope of a Divine Concept and the abilities an individual can actually perform through it remain dependent upon the nature of the authority, the user's understanding and the practical development of their powers.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] recognizes the unusual implications of Julia's Divinity because of his familiarity with the cultivation systems commonly found in fictional stories. Such systems frequently involve individuals refining their Energy, developing supernatural techniques and advancing through progressively higher states of existence. Julia's authority represents a literal Divine connection to the principle underlying those processes, making it potentially useful for applications extending far beyond conventional agriculture. Nevertheless, this comparison establishes the breadth of the Concept rather than proving that Julia can automatically grant cultivation realms, increase another individual's Divine rank or bypass the conceptual restrictions governing supernatural development.[1]
Julia's unusually large Energy reserves provide an exceptional foundation for applying her Divinity, but they also create difficulties during her childhood. Her immature control allows substantial amounts of Divine power to accumulate within her hair, causing continuous growth and unusual resistance to cutting. The condition demonstrates that her supernatural Energy can influence biological material even when she is not deliberately employing a specialized technique. However, the abnormal hair growth is primarily associated with the concentration of her immature Divinity rather than a confirmed individual ability to manipulate hair or impose identical effects upon external targets.[1]

The established evidence confirms the existence and general meaning of Cultivation, its relationship to agriculture and the unusual concentration of Divinity within her body. ### Skills & Abilities
Julia possesses the extraordinary biological and supernatural characteristics of a naturally born Blood Dragon Goddess, combined with her unusually large Energy reserves and the personal Divine Concept of Cultivation. Her known abilities are primarily innate rather than demonstrated through extended combat, and the available material provides relatively little information concerning her individual fighting style. The following abilities distinguish powers directly established through her personal characteristics from the broader racial foundation inherited by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s biological descendants.
- Blood Dragon Goddess Physiology: Julia is the biological daughter of Victor Elderblood and [[Natasha Elderblood Fulger]], possessing the advanced supernatural constitution of a genuine Blood Dragon and Dragon Goddess from birth. Her existence is fundamentally Draconic rather than a conventional Noble Vampire body that later acquired Divine power.[1]
- True Dragon Physiology: Julia possesses the biological and spiritual foundation of a genuine True Dragon, providing extraordinary physical potential, advanced supernatural senses, exceptional durability, longevity and compatibility with Draconic powers.[1][3]
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Her naturally born Draconic body provides exceptional strength, speed, reflexes, stamina and physical resilience compared with ordinary supernatural beings, although her individual maximum capabilities have not been demonstrated in a major battle.
- [[Draconic Energy]]: Julia possesses the supernatural Energy foundation associated with True Dragon physiology, providing the basis for advanced Draconic abilities in addition to her personal Divinity.
- [[Dragon Heart]]: Julia's natural True Dragon constitution includes the biological foundation associated with the generation and circulation of Draconic Energy, although the exact maximum output of her individual Dragon Heart remains unidentified.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Her Blood Dragon physiology provides the natural regenerative foundation characteristic of the Race, but the complete extent of her individual regeneration has not been established through a confirmed injury.
- Supernatural Longevity: Julia possesses the extraordinary lifespan and developmental characteristics of genuine [[True Dragons]], allowing her to survive extended periods of accelerated Imperial Time while retaining the characteristics of her supernatural Race.
- Draconic Transformation: Julia possesses the racial foundation for transformation between Humanoid and Draconic characteristics, although the complete appearance of her individual True Dragon Form remains undescribed.

### Family

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Julia's biological father and the source of her naturally born Blood Dragon lineage. Through Victor, Julia inherited the extraordinary biological and spiritual characteristics of a genuine True Dragon Goddess, making her fundamentally different from the conventional Noble Vampires who formed the original supernatural foundation of Clan Fulger. His advanced Draconic Bloodline provides her with exceptional natural strength and compatibility with Divine power, while the extraordinary resources of his Empire allow her to develop within a protected and highly advanced environment.
Victor recognizes the unusual nature of Julia's Cultivation Divinity and understands that its conceptual scope extends beyond conventional agricultural powers. His familiarity with fictional cultivation systems makes the inheritance particularly amusing to him, since Julia possesses literal Divine authority over a principle that forms the foundation of entire supernatural progression systems in such stories. Nevertheless, her existing power remains incompletely developed, and her extraordinary potential does not automatically grant mastery over every technique conceptually related to cultivation.[1]
Julia also grows up within a family whose guiding principles differ considerably from the supernatural society that existed during Victor's early life. Rather than allowing his daughters to be valued exclusively for their Bloodlines or treated as political resources, Victor emphasizes the importance of family unity, personal development and the freedom to pursue responsibilities suited to their abilities. Julia consequently benefits from an environment in which her extraordinary powers are recognized without being treated as the only measure of her value.

#### Natasha Elderblood Fulger
[[Natasha Elderblood Fulger]], commonly known as Naty, is Julia's biological mother and one of the most important individuals in her life. Natasha originated as an alternate personality within the fractured Soul of Natashia, eventually becoming an independent individual following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s intervention in the Fulger family's supernatural condition. Her independent existence allowed her to develop a life distinct from the original Natashia and eventually become Julia's mother.
Natasha's relationship with Julia is especially significant because of the mistakes she made during Sasha's childhood. Her obsession with Bloodline development and supernatural power had previously prevented her from providing Sasha with appropriate maternal affection, allowing the Human Maid Julia to assume the role of Sasha's principal caregiver. Natasha eventually recognizes that her neglect caused enormous suffering and deliberately attempts to avoid repeating the same behavior when raising her own daughter with Victor.[1]
The decision to name her daughter Julia represents an important acknowledgement of her past. Rather than selecting a name primarily associated with the Fulger family's political status or supernatural ancestry, Natasha chooses to honor the Human woman whose kindness had profoundly influenced Sasha's life. The name therefore preserves the memory of someone who possessed no extraordinary Bloodline but performed the responsibilities of motherhood more completely than the powerful Countess responsible for Sasha's upbringing.[1]
Natasha becomes considerably more involved in Julia's childhood, providing a level of attention and affection that Sasha had rarely experienced during her own early development. Julia consequently grows up with a more stable maternal relationship, surrounded by the support of Victor and the other members of the Elderblood household. Their relationship reflects Natasha's personal growth and the changes that transformed Clan Fulger from a family frequently dominated by Bloodline obsession into part of Victor's wider Draconic dynasty.[1]
Julia also strongly resembles Natasha in appearance, possessing a similar face, pale skin and blonde hair, although her own hair has a stronger orange tint and considerably curlier texture. Their resemblance remains obvious even during the later Imperial era, when Julia is recognized among the Fulger descendants as one of the daughters whose facial characteristics are almost identical to those of her mother.[1][2]

#### Sasha Elderblood Fulger
[[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] is Julia's older paternal half-sister and one of the individuals most closely connected to the circumstances surrounding her name. Sasha was raised within the original Fulger household during a period when her biological parents frequently prioritized supernatural development and political concerns over emotional wellbeing. The Human Maid Julia consequently became the person who cared for her most consistently, providing affection, education and support during the difficult experiences associated with her childhood.
Sasha's attachment to the original Julia was profound, and the Maid's murder became one of the most traumatic events of her early life. Because Julia had effectively become her mother, Sasha regarded the loss as far more significant than the death of an ordinary household servant. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later supported Sasha through her grief, while the Fulger family gradually came to recognize the importance of the woman whose loyalty and kindness had been taken for granted.
Sasha herself intended to preserve the Maid's memory by naming one of her daughters Julia. However, Natasha gave birth first and selected the same name for her child with Victor. The decision was emotionally significant because it demonstrated that Natasha had finally acknowledged the importance of the woman who raised Sasha and the mistakes that had originally made such support necessary. Julia Elderblood's name consequently represents a connection between the older and younger Fulger generations, preserving a memory that remains important to Sasha and her family.[1]

#### Julia Fulger
Julia was the Human Maid who raised Sasha and became her principal maternal figure during childhood. Although she possessed no extraordinary supernatural Bloodline, her kindness, loyalty and dedication made her one of the most important individuals in Sasha's life. She provided everyday care and emotional support while the young Fulger heiress struggled with the demands of her family's supernatural traditions, eventually becoming the person Sasha trusted most completely.
The original Julia was murdered during an Inquisition attack upon Sasha's residence, leaving Sasha devastated and contributing to the events that brought her closer to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Her death continued influencing Sasha's relationships long afterward, eventually prompting the Fulger family to acknowledge the Maid's importance through a memorial and the incorporation of her name into the Main Family records.
Julia Elderblood was deliberately named in honor of this woman. Although the two are separate individuals, their shared name preserves the historical significance of the original Julia within the Elderblood family. The younger Julia therefore represents both the continuation of the Fulger lineage and Natasha's recognition that genuine familial devotion cannot be measured exclusively through inherited supernatural power or political status.[1]

#### Aline Elderblood Fulger
Aline Elderblood Fulger is another daughter associated with the Fulger branch of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family and is traditionally indexed as the child of [[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]]. She should not be confused with Julia, whose established mother is Natasha/Naty and whose name was selected in honor of the Human Maid who raised Sasha.
Some versions of Julia's introductory material use the name Aline in passages that otherwise describe Natasha's daughter and the naming tribute to Sasha's caregiver. The currently available Webnovel identification names Julia explicitly, while the wider family index distinguishes her from Aline. The discrepancy is therefore treated as a naming inconsistency rather than proof that Julia and Aline are the same individual.[1]
The later introduction of [[Alisa Sasha Elderblood]] as Carmila's daughter creates an additional continuity question concerning the daughter previously indexed as Aline. Because the available material does not clearly establish whether these are separate daughters or different names referring to the same person, the relationship should remain qualified until their identities are directly distinguished. Julia's established parentage remains unaffected by the uncertainty.

#### Evelyn, Nikolina and Melina Elderblood
[[Evelyn Elderblood]], [[Nikolina Nia Nefela Elderblood]] and [[Melina Elderblood]] are among the sisters with whom Julia is first shown spending time. Their introductory interaction involves playful role-playing and teasing, with Evelyn becoming irritated after accidentally biting her tongue while attempting to pronounce Nikolina's unusually long name. Julia participates comfortably in the exchange alongside Melina, demonstrating her familiarity with the other daughters and the informal atmosphere maintained within the Elderblood household.[1]
Although the scene does not establish an exclusive friendship group or a permanent relationship distinct from Julia's connections with her other sisters, it provides an important example of her ordinary family life. The girls interact as siblings rather than maintaining the formal behavior traditionally expected of powerful Divine Princesses, reflecting the relatively affectionate environment in which [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s younger generation develops.

### Continuity
Julia's history contains several inconsistencies arising from changes in the names used for the Fulger daughters and the complicated relationship between Natasha, Natashia and their descendants. These inconsistencies are especially important because multiple women associated with Clan Fulger possess similar names, while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s second generation contains several daughters whose appearances and maternal characteristics closely resemble one another. Julia's explicitly established identity remains that of the daughter of Victor and Natasha/Naty, with the available later material confirming her continued presence among the Fulger descendants.[1][2]

#### Julia and Aline
Julia and Aline are traditionally indexed as separate daughters within the Fulger branch. Julia is identified as the daughter of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Natasha/Naty, while Aline is associated with [[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]]. Their similar appearances and the overlapping names used within some versions of the introductory material have caused confusion, particularly because Julia's name possesses a specific historical explanation connected to Sasha's Human caregiver.[1]
The naming tribute provides an important basis for identifying Julia. Natasha deliberately selected the name of the woman who had raised Sasha, giving her own daughter a name that acknowledged the emotional importance of the original [[Julia Fulger]]. Consequently, references to Natasha's daughter and the tribute to Sasha's caregiver refer to Julia Elderblood, not automatically to Aline.
However, the later appearance of [[Alisa Sasha Elderblood]] as Carmila's daughter creates an unresolved distinction between Aline and Alisa. Their names should not be treated as proof of two separate biological daughters without additional narrative confirmation, but neither should one be removed from the record solely because the names are similar. Julia's identity remains independent of this question.

#### Natasha, Naty and Annasthashia
[[Natasha Elderblood Fulger]] and [[Annasthashia Elderblood Fulger]] possess an unusually complicated relationship because Natasha originated as an alternate personality within Natashia's Soul. Their eventual separation allowed both women to exist independently, although similarities in their names continued producing confusion within the Fulger family and the surrounding narration.
Natasha is frequently called Naty, while Annasthashia is commonly called Natashia. These designations should be distinguished when establishing Julia's parentage because the daughter identified through Natasha's personal history and the naming tribute to the Human Maid is Julia Elderblood.[1]
The later family gathering uses the labels Annasthashia [Natasha] and Annasthashia [Naty] while introducing Ekaterina Natasha Elderblood and Julia Elderblood. The same passage also refers to Natasha and Naty as the same person, creating an apparent contradiction because the two daughters are presented separately despite the overlapping identification. The wording does not provide a sufficiently clear basis for treating Julia and Ekaterina as identical, nor does it invalidate Julia's earlier identification as Natasha's daughter.[2]
The most reliable approach is to preserve Julia's established maternal relationship while recording the later named daughters according to their individual appearances. Additional conclusions concerning whether the inconsistent labels refer to mistaken maternal names, alternative personal designations or separate daughters require direct clarification from the narrative rather than inference from the wording alone.
