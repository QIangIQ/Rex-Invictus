Erika Vesta Elderblood is a daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Hestia]] Elderblood. ### Physical Appearance
Erika is a young woman distinguished by her fiery red hair, strongly reflecting the coloration associated with her mother [[Hestia]] Elderblood. No detailed description of her height, clothing or other physical characteristics has yet been provided.[2]

### Personality
Only a small portion of Erika's individual personality has currently been shown. She appears gentle and approachable; when [[Ariella Victoria Elderblood]] happily greeted her during the gathering of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters, Erika calmly responded and smiled gently.[2]
She also appears comfortable among her numerous sisters, immediately joining the larger family gathering and listening as [[Valentina Victoria Elderblood]] explained the events that had brought everyone together.[2]

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Erika's existence was first established indirectly during the emergence of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s enormous second generation. [[Hestia]] Elderblood had spent approximately 1,900 years pregnant with Victor's daughter during the accelerated Time experienced by the family. At this stage the child was not given a name and was recorded only as Hestia's daughter.[1]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Erika made her first confirmed named appearance during the gathering of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters in Chapter 1165. Shortly after Ekaterina Natasha Elderblood, [[Svetlana Veronika Elderblood]], [[Alisa Sasha Elderblood]] and several of their sisters arrived, another fiery redheaded woman appeared and asked what had happened.[2]
[[Ariella Victoria Elderblood]] greeted her warmly by name, and Erika answered with a gentle smile. The narration then formally identified her as Erika Vesta Elderblood, daughter of Victor and [[Hestia]]. She subsequently remained with the increasingly large gathering as more than thirty-five of Victor's daughters assembled and exchanged stories.[2]

### Skills and Abilities
- Blood Dragon God Physiology: Erika belongs to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later generation of daughters, whose existences combine his Blood Dragon nature with the supernatural and Divine characteristics of their mothers. Her individual Divine Concept and specialized abilities have not yet been revealed.[1][2]
- Divinity: Erika's personal Divinity or Divine Concept has not yet been explained.

### Trivia
- Erika was previously known only as [[Hestia]]'s Daughter before Chapter 1165 revealed her full name.[1][2]
- Her middle name, [[Vesta]], directly mirrors another name associated with her mother, Hestia.
- Hestia carried Erika for approximately 1,900 years.[1]
- Erika's fiery red hair strongly resembles Hestia's characteristic coloration.[2]
