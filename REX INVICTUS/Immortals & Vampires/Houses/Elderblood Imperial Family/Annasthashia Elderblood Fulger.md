### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 99
No one can dominate a king. A king is one who stands above others and points the way for us common people to follow them... If a king is dominated... He will no longer be a king... but a puppet.
—Natashia to Natasha, Chapter 230
Annasthashia Elderblood Fulger, commonly known as Natashia, is a True Blood Dragon, the head and matriarch of Clan Fulger, a member of Clan Elderblood, and one of [[Victor Elderblood]]'s wives. She is the biological mother of [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] and [[Angelina Elderblood]].[5] Before joining Victor's family, Natashia was one of the ancient Vampire Countesses of [[Nightingale]] and the original holder of the Fulger Count title, one of the positions traditionally associated with the great Pillars of Vampire society. Renowned for her mastery of the hereditary Lightning of Clan Fulger, she acquired the historical reputation of the Fastest Woman Alive.[3]
Although Natashia initially appears to be an eccentric ancient Noble Vampire with extraordinary Lightning powers, her actual history is inseparable from the mysterious spiritual condition affecting her family. She is the original Annasthashia Fulger, while the woman who controlled her body for substantial portions of the earlier story was a second consciousness eventually identified as Natasha. The two originally shared a body and much of their history, but their personalities, emotional responses, and understanding of family differed substantially. The distinction was initially explained as a case of multiple personalities,[6] but later revelations established that Natasha had developed into a separate spiritual individual rather than remaining a temporary manifestation of Natashia's mind.[7]
The origin of this condition lay within the Fulger family's ancestry. Its Bloodline originated from powerful Lightning Spirits, and Natashia inherited sufficient compatibility to undergo an exceptional evolution toward the same elemental state. Her original Noble Vampire body, however, lacked the capacity to survive the complete transformation. Her mother, [[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]], intervened directly in her Soul Core to prevent the process, saving Natashia's life but damaging the foundation of her spiritual existence.[8] The consequences of that intervention persisted for nearly two thousand years, influencing her marriages, the upbringing of Sasha, the decline of Clan Fulger, and the relationship between her original consciousness and the personality eventually known as Natasha.
Natashia's circumstances finally changed when Victor repaired her damaged Soul and separated the spiritual presences contained within it. Carmila was restored to an independent body, while Natasha received her own body and the distinguishing name Naty, ending their dependence upon a single shared existence.[9] Natashia subsequently became a True Blood Dragon through Victor's transformation of his family, acquiring a superior biological foundation capable of sustaining the Lightning-Spirit heritage that had previously threatened her survival.[2]
After joining Victor's family, Natashia became one of the senior women of the Elderblood dynasty. Her original responsibilities as a Vampire Countess gradually developed into the much larger administrative obligations of the [[Elderblood Empire]], and she participated in the immense periods of accelerated Time through which the Imperial civilization evolved.[10] While her overwhelming affection for Victor and her eccentric behavior remain recognizable aspects of her personality, her later life is also defined by the restoration of her relationship with Sasha, the return of Carmila, her reconciliation with Naty, and the opportunity to raise Angelina without repeating the mistakes that shaped her first daughter's childhood.

### Physical Appearance

#### Noble Vampire
Natashia is an exceptionally beautiful woman with long blonde hair, pale skin, and a mature, voluptuous figure. During her life as a Noble Vampire, she stood approximately 183 centimeters tall and bore a particularly strong resemblance to her daughter Sasha, appearing much like an older and more mature version of her. Her natural eyes were blue, while the activation of her Vampiric characteristics could turn them red. She generally favored elegant clothing, especially white dresses and aristocratic garments that complemented her golden hair and reflected her position as the head of one of [[Nightingale]]'s ancient Noble Vampire families.
Despite her aristocratic appearance, Natashia rarely maintained the restrained demeanor traditionally expected from a Countess unless the situation genuinely required it. She frequently displayed exaggerated expressions, openly affectionate gestures, teasing smiles, and energetic movements that made her seem far younger than her actual age. This contrast became particularly striking when her original personality resurfaced after the confrontation involving [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and William Florence. Victor immediately recognized that her mannerisms, expressions, and atmosphere had changed so completely that she seemed like a different person, even though the body itself remained the same.[6]
The distinction between Natashia and Natasha was initially expressed through behavior rather than physical anatomy. Natasha generally presented herself as cold, calculating, composed, and emotionally detached, while Natashia displayed a much more animated and uninhibited disposition. Individuals familiar with the two could distinguish which consciousness controlled their shared body from changes in expression, voice, posture, and presence. Following Victor's eventual repair of the Soul, the two no longer needed to express themselves through the same physical vessel and could appear together as separate individuals.[9]
Natashia's appearance also changed as her body evolved through Victor's Blood. Her repeated Blood exchanges with him strengthened her supernatural constitution and allowed her to endure more of her own Lightning without injury. Later, when Victor deliberately enhanced the Fulger lineage through Progenitor Blood, Natashia and Sasha underwent additional physical refinement. Their already extraordinary beauty increased, their eyes acquired a permanent blood-red coloration, and their bodies became more closely aligned with Victor's evolving Vampire lineage.[11]

#### Vampire Count Form
As an ancient Vampire Countess, Natashia possessed the advanced transformation available to powerful Noble Vampires who had accumulated centuries of supernatural development. Her transformed state amplified her physical characteristics and the output of her hereditary Lightning, allowing her to fight at speeds that vastly exceeded those of ordinary members of Clan Fulger. In her confrontation with [[Niklaus Horseman]], she demonstrated the ability to unleash extraordinary quantities of attacks in extremely short intervals, with her Lightning becoming increasingly difficult for observers to follow.[12]
Her historical Count transformation possessed distinctive golden characteristics. Her skin developed a metallic golden appearance, powerful claws formed, enormous wings emerged from her back, and her long hair increasingly resembled supernatural Lightning. These features reflected her body's effort to accommodate the full expression of the [[Fulger Bloodline]], whose natural power could place considerable strain upon conventional Vampire flesh.[13] The transformation also provided the physical foundation necessary for her most destructive techniques, including the large-scale Lightning attack known as De Fulmine Judicii.
Her transformation later developed beyond the conventional characteristics of a Vampire Count. By the time she demonstrated her perfected state during the Fulger family events, Natashia had advanced toward a condition in which her body could become living Lightning. Rather than merely covering flesh with electrical Energy, she was capable of transforming the physical foundation of her existence toward the element itself while retaining her consciousness and supernatural identity.[14] This evolution was especially significant because it foreshadowed the later revelation that the deepest nature of the Fulger Bloodline was spiritual rather than purely biological.

#### True Blood Dragon
Natashia eventually abandoned the limitations of her original Noble Vampire physiology when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] transformed members of his family into genuine True [[Blood Dragons]]. The process reconstructed the participants at a fundamental level through Victor's Essence, producing individual Draconic existences that retained the characteristics of their previous races while acquiring the extraordinary physical and supernatural foundation of a True Dragon.[15][1]
For Natashia, the reconstruction preserved the Fulger Lightning-Spirit heritage rather than replacing it. Her new body possessed the enhanced strength, durability, sensory capacity, [[Draconic Energy]], transformative potential, and supernatural compatibility associated with Victor's Blood Dragon lineage.[2] The transformation also introduced the possibility of expressing conventional Draconic features such as horns, wings, and other manifestations of her new race, although the exact details of every personal transformation have not been individually described.
The most important consequence was the removal of the ancient incompatibility between her Bloodline and her physical body. As a Noble Vampire, Natashia's progression toward a high-level Lightning Spirit had threatened to destroy her because the original body could not safely sustain the transformation. As a True Blood Dragon, her supernatural foundation could accommodate those elemental characteristics much more naturally.[8][2] The change therefore represented more than an increase in combat strength; it resolved a biological contradiction that had shaped her existence since childhood.
Natashia's transformation also occurred alongside that of other women from the Fulger and Scarlett families. Each retained the elemental abilities, martial knowledge, and individual qualities developed before Dragonhood while receiving access to the Draconic Language and other powers inherent to Victor's race.[16] Her current existence consequently combines ancient Vampire experience, the Fulger Lightning-Spirit Bloodline, knowledge obtained from [[Zeus]], and the physiology of a True Blood Dragon.

### Personality
Natashia is intensely energetic, shameless, affectionate, possessive, impulsive, and emotionally extreme. Her behavior frequently appears irrational to outside observers because she openly expresses desires that most ancient aristocrats would conceal for reasons of dignity or political reputation. Nevertheless, her eccentricity should not be confused with a complete absence of judgment. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually describes her as a lucid crazy person, recognizing that Natashia is generally aware of how unusual her reactions can be and simply does not believe she should suppress her personality to satisfy ordinary social expectations.[17] She can transition from exaggerated affection or jealousy into serious combat, administrative work, and political calculation when circumstances demand it, reflecting the experience accumulated throughout her long existence as a Vampire Countess.
A central characteristic of Natashia's worldview is her admiration for individuals who possess genuine independence and the determination to act according to their convictions. Centuries among Noble Vampires exposed her to artificial relationships, calculated marriages, and rulers whose behavior changed whenever someone with greater authority appeared. Natashia came to despise people who surrendered their own [[Will Solace|Will]] while continuing to claim the dignity of a ruler. During her confrontation with Natasha inside their shared spiritual world, she expressed the belief that a King who allows another person to dominate his choices becomes a puppet rather than a genuine sovereign.[18] Her attraction toward Victor was closely connected to this philosophy, as he repeatedly challenged individuals who were older, stronger, or politically superior rather than permitting their status to determine how he should behave.
Natashia's affection for Victor originally manifested as aggressive romantic pursuit. Shortly after regaining control of her body, she openly expressed her desire for him and displayed little concern for the complications created by his existing marriage to Sasha. Victor, however, refused to indulge her simply because she was attractive or enthusiastic. He remained angry about the treatment Sasha had received from her parents and demanded that Natashia take responsibility for the consequences of the life conducted through their shared body.[19] Instead of accepting an easy romantic victory, Natashia found herself confronted by someone who expected her to become a better mother, restore her Clan, and demonstrate that her feelings were more than a temporary obsession.
Her response revealed a determination that her exaggerated behavior often concealed. Natashia began removing disloyal elements from Clan Fulger, reconnecting with Victoria, preparing to reclaim the Count title, and paying greater attention to Sasha's wellbeing.[20] She did not abandon her shamelessness or possessive instincts, but her priorities gradually expanded beyond obtaining Victor's affection. Reclaiming the Clan became a matter of personal pride and an opportunity to prove that she could still accomplish something worthy of her daughter's respect. Her willingness to challenge [[Niklaus Horseman]] despite the danger also demonstrated that she was prepared to place her position and life at risk rather than rely exclusively upon Victor to resolve her problems.
Natashia possesses a competitive nature shaped by centuries of combat. Her extraordinary speed is a particular source of pride, and she enjoys confronting capable opponents even when a battle begins through an argument or misunderstanding. Her relationship with [[Scathach]] illustrates this characteristic especially well. Natashia had challenged Scathach repeatedly during their earlier lives and continued regarding her as both a rival and a friend.[21] Even when Scathach attacked her in anger, Natashia could use her superior mobility defensively while attempting to understand what had caused the confrontation.
Despite her provocative manner, Natashia is capable of genuine empathy toward people she cares about. During the confrontation with Scathach, she realized that the ancient Countess had finally begun acknowledging her feelings for Victor. Rather than respond exclusively with jealousy, Natashia became excited that someone she considered a longstanding friend had recognized an important part of herself.[21] She hugged Scathach, celebrated the discovery, and attempted to encourage her to act upon those feelings. Although her advice was characteristically blunt, the interaction demonstrated that Natashia could sincerely desire another woman's happiness even when they were romantically interested in the same man.
Her treatment of Scathach also revealed that Natashia understood the importance of respecting another person's values, even when she considered those values unnecessarily complicated. Scathach insisted that Victor needed to defeat her before she would accept him as a husband, a requirement that Natashia found frustrating because it delayed a relationship both individuals appeared to desire. Nevertheless, when it became clear that the condition represented something important to Scathach's identity, Natashia recognized that simply dismissing it would accomplish little.[21] Her behavior combined teasing, emotional perception, and concern rather than being motivated solely by romantic competition.
Natashia's greatest personal weakness during her early life was her failure to establish a stable maternal relationship with Sasha. Although Natasha had controlled their shared body during important portions of Sasha's childhood, the original Natashia eventually recognized that she could not repair the situation by simply blaming the alternate consciousness. Sasha had suffered emotional neglect and severe training while [[Julia Fulger]] performed much of the role that her biological mother had failed to fulfill. When Natashia returned, her overwhelming affection initially confused Sasha because it contrasted so sharply with the cold behavior she remembered.[19] Victor's criticism forced Natashia to understand that biological motherhood did not automatically entitle her to forgiveness or emotional closeness.
Natashia's apology to Sasha became an important turning point. Rather than demand that her daughter immediately accept the original personality as her true mother, she acknowledged that the circumstances of their shared body did not erase the harm Sasha had experienced. She admitted that she had failed to become a good mother and expressed the hope that Sasha would eventually allow them to build a relationship.[19] The admission did not instantly repair centuries of emotional damage, but it established a foundation upon which Natashia could begin earning her daughter's trust through conduct rather than words.
Her later treatment of Julia Fulger demonstrated how far that understanding had developed. Julia had raised Sasha, protected her emotionally, and become the maternal figure Sasha trusted most. Natashia could have treated that bond as a threat to her own position, but she ultimately recognized Julia's contributions as something worthy of honor rather than jealousy. She posthumously inducted Julia into the Fulger Main Family and preserved her example as a lesson for future mothers and servants.[14] This decision was particularly meaningful because it represented Natashia acknowledging another woman's success in the very responsibility she had failed to fulfill.
Natashia's relationship with Natasha, later known as Naty, was similarly complicated. The two initially blamed one another for events that had occurred while sharing one body, particularly the damage surrounding Sasha and the decline of Clan Fulger. During their internal confrontation in Chapter 230, the dispute gradually moved beyond insults and resentment toward recognition that they were both products of the same fractured existence.[18] When Victor eventually repaired the Soul, Natashia did not insist that Natasha be destroyed simply because she had caused suffering. Their separation allowed both women to confront the past as distinct individuals rather than continuing an endless struggle for control.
Motherhood and family eventually became the strongest foundations of Natashia's modern identity. Her reunion with Carmila exposed an emotional vulnerability that centuries of life had never eliminated. Despite her age and position, Natashia immediately embraced her mother and cried when she realized Carmila was genuinely alive.[22] The moment contrasted sharply with the confident Countess who routinely provoked ancient rulers, demonstrating that the loss of her mother had remained a genuine wound throughout the centuries.
Natashia's acceptance of Blood Dragonhood was likewise connected to her attachment to Victor's household. The transformation offered extraordinary power, but its significance extended to remaining beside the people she loved and ensuring that her racial limitations would not prevent her from following the family's evolution.[1] By the time Angelina was born, Natashia possessed the maturity and emotional experience to raise another daughter under circumstances radically different from those surrounding Sasha. Her affectionate, possessive, and occasionally reckless temperament remained recognizable, but she had become much more conscious of the responsibilities associated with being a mother and a senior member of the Imperial Family.

### Background
Natashia was born more than nineteen centuries before the beginning of the main story as a descendant of the ancient Fulger Lightning-Spirit lineage and the daughter of [[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]]. The family's origins differed fundamentally from those of ordinary Noble Vampire Clans. Carmila had originally existed as a powerful Lightning Spirit before becoming a Vampire through an arrangement involving [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]], and her supernatural characteristics became embedded within the hereditary powers of Clan Fulger.[23] While later generations commonly regarded Lightning as their ancestral Vampire ability, the deeper nature of the Bloodline preserved a connection to the elemental existence from which the Clan originated.
Natashia inherited an unusually powerful expression of these characteristics. Her compatibility was sufficient for her existence to begin progressing toward Spirit Evolution, a process through which a descendant could approach the original elemental nature of the Fulger lineage. Unfortunately, her body remained that of a Noble Vampire and could not safely endure the complete transformation into a high-level Lightning Spirit. Carmila therefore intervened in Natashia's developing Soul Core to suppress the process and prevent her daughter's death.[8] Although the intervention succeeded in preserving Natashia's life, it damaged the structure of her Soul, producing abnormalities that neither ordinary Vampire knowledge nor conventional medical treatment could resolve.
The spiritual damage eventually gave rise to another consciousness. The cold personality who came to be known as Natasha developed through the fractured Soul and accumulated her own memories, experiences, and sense of identity. For many centuries, the two personalities shared the same body while remaining capable of expressing radically different behavior. The original Natashia was energetic, affectionate, and impulsive, whereas Natasha generally displayed a colder and more detached personality. The distinction became especially important during Sasha's upbringing, as the hostile personality controlled the body through significant portions of her childhood.[6]
Natashia nevertheless became one of [[Nightingale]]'s ancient Vampire Countesses and acquired considerable renown for her speed and Lightning abilities. As the head of Clan Fulger, she inherited responsibility not only for her family's military strength but also for an important part of Nightingale's internal administration. Vlad later identified her traditional Count responsibility as food management, demonstrating that the great Vampire Clans possessed specific institutional functions beyond maintaining hereditary armies and political prestige.[24]
The Fulger family's position eventually deteriorated through the wager involving Clan Horseman. The Clan possessed a living hybrid born from a Noble Vampire and a Werewolf, an extraordinarily unusual existence whose survival contradicted the expected incompatibility between the two races. Annasthashia wagered the Fulger Count title for the opportunity associated with the hybrid and lost, allowing Clan Horseman to replace Fulger among Nightingale's principal Count families.[25] The loss deprived the family of political authority and left its members in a considerably more vulnerable position than they had occupied previously.
During this period, Sasha grew up without a stable relationship with her biological parents. The severe training associated with Fulger Lightning placed enormous demands upon her, while the emotional distance maintained by Natasha and William Florence left her dependent upon Julia, the maid who became her principal maternal figure. When Julia was eventually murdered by the Inquisition, the loss devastated Sasha and contributed directly to her desire for revenge. The attack also reflected the weakened position of Clan Fulger, whose enemies no longer feared the retaliation traditionally associated with targeting the household of a reigning Vampire Count.[26]
The original Natashia remained largely absent from the immediate circumstances of Sasha's upbringing until [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] confronted the Fulger household. His actions destabilized the balance between the personalities, allowing the original consciousness to resurface and begin reclaiming control over the family whose decline had occurred during the centuries of their shared existence.[27][6]

### Synopsis

#### Vampire Society
Natashia's importance to the story was established through the political and emotional problems surrounding Sasha before the original personality appeared. The loss of the Fulger Count title had deprived one of [[Nightingale]]'s ancient Noble families of its former position, leaving Sasha in a household whose authority and protection had substantially declined. [[Scathach]] eventually explained that the loss had originated from Annasthashia's wager over a living Vampire–Werewolf hybrid kept by Clan Horseman.[25] The wager's consequences extended beyond aristocratic pride, as the Fulger family lost the privileges and influence associated with being one of Nightingale's principal Count families. Victoria attempted to reverse the situation by wagering an immense economic fortune against the title, placing the family's remaining resources at risk in the hope of restoring its former position.
The consequences of Fulger's decline became painfully personal when Sasha returned home and discovered that Julia had been murdered by agents of the Inquisition. Julia had raised Sasha during the years in which her biological mother failed to provide consistent affection, making her loss much more devastating than the death of an ordinary servant. The Inquisition had exploited the weakened political position of Clan Fulger, believing that its heiress could be targeted more safely after the family lost its Count status.[26] Sasha's grief and resentment toward her parents consequently became inseparable from the Clan's political decline.
The woman initially known to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] as Annasthashia appeared when Sasha's biological parents arrived at Scathach's mansion. [[William Salvatore Florence]] and the cold consciousness occupying Annasthashia's body entered the situation with the expectation that their age, parental position, and aristocratic authority would be respected.[28] Victor, however, had already heard Sasha's account of her childhood and possessed little patience for parents who treated their daughter's suffering as an acceptable consequence of harsh upbringing. Annasthashia's indifference toward Julia's death intensified his hostility because Julia had performed the maternal responsibilities that Sasha's biological parents had neglected.
Victor distinguished between training designed to make someone stronger and cruelty that served no meaningful purpose. He understood that supernatural combat could require extremely painful instruction because Scathach had personally subjected him to brutal training, but he considered the Fulger household's emotional neglect unacceptable.[29] Rather than defer to Annasthashia and William as his elders, Victor challenged them directly, making it clear that their biological relationship with Sasha did not automatically entitle them to her respect or forgiveness.
The confrontation escalated into a violent battle. Victor and Kaguya fought opponents who possessed centuries more experience than themselves, forcing Victor to rely upon his unusual regeneration, inherited elemental powers, and increasingly refined combat instincts.[30] The cold Annasthashia consciousness proved extremely dangerous, inflicting injuries that would have been fatal to an ordinary Vampire and displaying considerable familiarity with close-range combat. The struggle also exposed the extraordinary characteristics of Victor's Blood, which continued attracting the attention of the older Vampires even while he was fighting them.
William ultimately died during the confrontation. The accumulated violence, emotional pressure, and abnormal reactions surrounding Victor's Blood destabilized the consciousness that had been controlling Annasthashia's body.[27] What followed initially confused the people present, as the woman who had behaved with cold hostility suddenly displayed an energetic and affectionate personality completely unlike the one Victor had been fighting. Scathach recognized the change and explained that the person now speaking was the original Natashia, whereas the previous personality had originated from an incident in her past.[6]
The original Natashia immediately distinguished herself through her extraordinary enthusiasm. She showed little restraint in expressing her affection toward Sasha and displayed an almost immediate fascination with Victor. The sudden change overwhelmed Sasha, who had spent much of her life associating her mother's body with emotional distance and painful training.[19] Natashia embraced her daughter, praised her appearance, and behaved with a warmth that Sasha had rarely experienced from her biological mother, leaving the younger woman unable to reconcile the affectionate stranger with the person she remembered.
Victor remained unconvinced that the personality change eliminated the responsibility surrounding Sasha's upbringing. When Natashia argued that the other personality had been responsible for the earlier treatment, he insisted that the distinction did not make Sasha's suffering disappear.[19] The original Natashia eventually acknowledged his criticism. She apologized for failing to become a good mother and admitted that Sasha had no obligation to forgive her immediately. Rather than demand affection on the basis of blood, she expressed the hope that her daughter would allow them to rebuild their relationship over time.
The apology marked the beginning of Natashia's return to active leadership. Victor challenged her to protect Sasha, repair Clan Fulger, and behave as someone worthy of being regarded as the family's matriarch. Natashia accepted the expectations and began acting decisively. She removed disloyal elements from her household, rejected relationships she no longer valued, and started preparing to reclaim the Count title that had been lost during the period of her shared existence.[31]
Victor's Blood also produced an important change in her physical abilities. Natashia discovered that her Lightning could be used more freely without inflicting the same degree of damage upon her body, overcoming a weakness historically associated with the Fulger lineage. This improvement made her more capable of expressing the advanced principles of her Clan's inherited martial techniques and contributed to her determination to regain the family's former standing.
Natashia subsequently reunited with her younger sister Victoria and brought her into the effort to rebuild Clan Fulger. Victoria's economic resources and administrative competence complemented Natashia's combat strength and restored authority, while other relatives such as Tatsuya became involved in the family's affairs.[20]

#### A New Pillar
Natashia's restoration continued as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became the Fifth Pillar of [[Nightingale]]. After returning from his meeting with Vlad, Victor displayed significantly improved Fulger Lightning as a consequence of drinking Natashia's Blood.[32] [[Scathach]] recognized that his elemental control was no longer limited to producing electrical attacks. He had begun incorporating the acceleration and movement characteristics of Fulger Lightning into the martial principles she had taught him, demonstrating how his abnormal Bloodline could assimilate useful characteristics from the women connected to him.
For Natashia, Victor's growing strength reinforced the qualities that had originally attracted her. He had risen from a young Vampire with relatively little experience to a Count capable of challenging Nightingale's political hierarchy, yet his behavior remained guided by his own convictions rather than unquestioning obedience to established authority. Natashia increasingly regarded him as the man she wanted beside her, although her pursuit remained complicated by his marriage to Sasha and the unresolved consequences of the Fulger household's earlier history.
Her restoration of Clan Fulger became a more immediate concern when she approached Vlad to discuss the possibility of challenging Clan Horseman for the Count title.[33] The Vampire King explained that Natashia did not require his personal permission to attempt the challenge. Nightingale's existing rules allowed a sufficiently powerful Noble Vampire to contest a Count position, provided the challenger accepted the risks and consequences.[34] Natashia consequently began preparing to confront the family whose acquisition of Fulger's title had damaged her household for years.
The challenge developed alongside Victor's own conflicts. Natashia's preparations involved the recovery of family resources, the assessment of [[Niklaus Horseman]]'s abilities, and the need to demonstrate that Clan Fulger still possessed the strength expected of one of Nightingale's great families. The upcoming duel was therefore much more significant than a personal disagreement between two ancient Vampires. It represented the possibility of restoring the political position that had once protected Sasha and the people connected to her.
Victor encouraged Natashia to pursue the challenge for reasons that extended beyond prestige. He wanted her to become someone Sasha could respect and to reclaim the responsibilities she had abandoned or lost. Before the confrontation, Victor specifically encouraged her to defeat Niklaus and make Sasha proud.[35] Natashia responded in her usual manner by attempting to turn his encouragement into a discussion about the romantic reward she might receive afterward. Beneath the teasing, however, the prospect of earning her daughter's admiration became another source of motivation.
The formal challenge against Niklaus Horseman began before Nightingale's influential families and royal authorities.[36] Natashia entered the confrontation with a Western sword rather than relying exclusively upon the paired daggers traditionally associated with the Fulger fighting style, demonstrating the breadth of her martial experience. Her opponent initially appeared unable to match the speed generated by her Lightning, while Natashia repeatedly increased the intensity of her attacks to prevent him from establishing control over the battlefield.[12]
The battle demonstrated why Natashia possessed the historical reputation of the Fastest Woman Alive. Through the acceleration generated by her Bloodline, she could unleash approximately one million attacks within a single second, producing an extraordinary barrage that made conventional reactions almost useless.[12] Her Lightning was not limited to movement, as she could concentrate it into armor, weapons, and destructive projections. Nevertheless, Niklaus possessed abilities that prevented the fight from being decided through speed alone. His abnormal physiology and corrupted attacks allowed him to survive circumstances that would have defeated a more conventional Vampire opponent.[37]
As the confrontation continued, Niklaus revealed dangerous properties of his body and supernatural powers. His techniques interfered with Natashia's regeneration and forced her into increasingly desperate circumstances. Despite her enormous speed advantage, she could not simply avoid every consequence of his attacks, particularly once their supernatural effects began compromising her ability to recover.[38] The struggle culminated in injuries severe enough to threaten her life.
The danger produced an important internal confrontation between Natashia and Natasha. Rather than remaining completely separated through changes in control of the body, the two consciousnesses encountered one another within their shared spiritual world.[18] Natasha initially mocked the original for being caught by Niklaus' poison despite possessing superior power, while Natashia confronted the other personality over the consequences of their shared history. The exchange gradually exposed their conflicting interpretations of responsibility, particularly regarding Sasha.
Natashia also revealed the philosophy that shaped much of her attraction toward Victor. She rejected the idea that a true ruler could remain worthy of the title while allowing another person to dictate every decision. Her declaration about Kings and puppets was therefore not merely romantic praise for Victor but an expression of the independence she considered essential to genuine authority.[18] Their confrontation simultaneously showed that Natashia and Natasha possessed different desires and values despite originating from the same damaged Soul.
Victor intervened when Natashia's condition became genuinely life-threatening. He supplied his Blood to restore her body, allowing her to recover from injuries that had threatened to end the battle.[13] The exchange also amplified her Lightning and demonstrated the extraordinary restorative and evolutionary effects of his Blood upon members of the Fulger lineage. Natashia returned to the confrontation with renewed strength and a more developed appreciation for the danger posed by Niklaus.
The final outcome remained suspicious. Niklaus surrendered despite appearing to possess additional abilities that he had not completely revealed, returning the Count title to Natashia while leaving several observers uncertain whether the confrontation had been allowed to reach its natural conclusion.[39] Natashia nevertheless achieved the central objective of her challenge: Clan Fulger recovered its Count position, and Sasha's biological mother had demonstrated her willingness to fight for the family's restoration rather than remain content with its decline.
The confrontation also strengthened Natashia's emotional connection with Victor. His encouragement and intervention had helped her survive a battle that might otherwise have ended in her death, while his insistence that she take responsibility for her family had become an important influence upon her actions. She continued pursuing him aggressively, but their relationship had acquired a foundation extending beyond immediate attraction.
Natashia's understanding of Victor's desires was later demonstrated during his confrontation with Anderson. When Anderson used a dangerous Divine-enhanced Werewolf attack, Natashia became concerned enough to consider intervening, but Scathach restrained her because Victor had deliberately chosen to confront the attack himself.[40] Natashia ultimately accepted this reasoning, recognizing that protecting Victor against his wishes could amount to denying him the challenges he actively sought.

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] returned from the period during which [[Nightingale]] had been inaccessible to him, Natashia was among the women who learned how extensively his experiences had changed him. His separation from most of his family had lasted approximately eighteen months from his own perspective, during which Ruby had remained beside him while he fought, trained, and accumulated considerable combat experience.[41] Natashia's reunion with Victor reinforced the contrast between the comparatively short separation experienced by the rest of the household and the additional memories he had accumulated.
Natashia became involved in Victor's investigation of the mysterious blood-consuming tree later revealed to be connected to [[Roxanne Elderblood]]. The tree's unusual reactions to Victor's Blood attracted considerable attention, and Natashia accompanied him during portions of the investigation.[42] Her presence reflected both her desire to remain near Victor and the fact that she possessed enough experience to recognize when an apparently ordinary supernatural object concealed extraordinary potential.
The investigation eventually brought Victor and Natashia to Limbo, Nightingale's extradimensional prison for dangerous Vampires.[43] Rather than simply functioning as a conventional prison, Limbo possessed supernatural mechanisms capable of examining the nature of its visitors. Its analysis of Victor revealed titles and classifications that even Natashia found extraordinary, including the designation Second Progenitor.[44]
The gate's subsequent examination also produced predictions or extrapolations concerning Victor's relationships. Natashia and [[Scathach]] appeared among the women connected to his future despite neither relationship having been consummated at that point.[45] The incident added another unusual element to Natashia's developing relationship with Victor, although the information produced by Limbo could not be treated as an infallible statement of present circumstances.
Throughout this period, Natashia continued balancing her responsibilities as the restored head of Clan Fulger with her increasingly obvious affection for Victor. Their attraction was openly mutual by the time they returned from Limbo, and Natashia made little effort to conceal her desire for their relationship to develop further.[3][46] Her enthusiasm frequently complicated interactions with the other women surrounding Victor, but she was also becoming an accepted member of the extended household rather than merely Sasha's troublesome mother.
Natashia participated in the family's wider combat development and remained involved in Sasha's training. The original Fulger fighting tradition relied upon considerably more than raw speed, and Natashia possessed extensive knowledge of how Lightning could be incorporated into movement, weapon handling, and elemental transformation. Her daughter's increasing skill reflected the practical value of that instruction, while Victor's own extraordinary growth encouraged the older Countesses to refine their techniques rather than rely solely upon experience accumulated in the past.
A major development occurred during the events surrounding Natashia and Scathach. When Natashia announced her intention to visit Victor, Scathach reacted with violent jealousy, leading to a confrontation in which the two ancient Countesses fought within Scarlett territory.[21] Natashia initially attempted to understand why Scathach was attacking her, using her Lightning-enhanced speed to avoid the older Vampire's spear and Ice techniques. As the confrontation continued, she realized that Scathach had begun acknowledging her own feelings for Victor.
Rather than respond with anger at acquiring another romantic rival, Natashia became genuinely excited for her friend. She embraced Scathach, celebrated the revelation, and attempted to persuade her to stop allowing her complicated requirements for marriage to prevent her from pursuing the man she loved.[21] The interaction revealed that Natashia's friendship with Scathach was much more substantial than their history of rivalry might suggest. Although both women possessed competitive personalities and strong romantic feelings toward Victor, Natashia was capable of taking genuine pleasure in Scathach's emotional development.
The tension surrounding Victor eventually culminated in Natashia taking a decisive step in their relationship. After centuries of dissatisfaction with earlier marriages and relationships, she no longer wanted to postpone obtaining the man she considered worthy of her affection. Natashia and Victor consummated their relationship during the events of Chapter 425, following her long period of open pursuit.[47] The intensity of their reunion became another source of household complications, but the encounter was important because it transformed their attraction into an acknowledged physical relationship.
Natashia immediately sought to formalize the union rather than remain content with an unofficial arrangement. She brought Victor into the basement of the Fulger mansion, where the family's ritual facilities and supernatural materials were stored.[4] Because conventional Vampires did not possess the same magical foundation as Witches, the marriage ritual required prepared materials and catalysts. Natashia personally constructed the ritual circle and gathered the necessary components, displaying an unusual mixture of excitement, familiarity with ancient Vampire customs, and determination to complete the ceremony properly.
Victor recognized the ritual and understood what Natashia intended. He formally accepted Annasthashia Fulger as his wife, while Natashia enthusiastically agreed to spend eternity beside him.[4] The supernatural ceremony established a Blood bond between them and severed the remaining connections associated with Natashia's previous marriages. The new bond incorporated her into the network already linking Victor with Violet, Sasha, and Ruby, making Natashia his fourth official wife.
The completed marriage had consequences beyond social recognition. Natashia could now participate directly in the supernatural exchange of Blood that satisfied the needs of Victor's household, while Victor acquired another partner whose Blood formed part of his accepted marital bond.[48] Natashia's confidence in her place beside Victor increased accordingly, and she began imagining a future in which Clan Fulger would remain permanently connected to the expanding family surrounding him.
Her official marriage also changed her position among Nightingale's Countesses. She was no longer simply Sasha's mother or the restored head of an ancient Clan, but one of Victor's acknowledged wives and therefore a member of the household surrounding the Second Vampire Progenitor. The relationship further connected Clan Fulger to Victor's emerging political faction at a time when traditional loyalties within Nightingale were becoming increasingly unstable.
During this period, Vlad also identified the traditional administrative functions associated with the great Count Clans. Natashia's responsibility for food management demonstrated that the ancient political structure possessed an organized division of labor rather than consisting exclusively of powerful families controlling territory through military force.[24] This background would later become relevant as Natashia's role expanded from administering a Vampire Clan to participating in the government of an Imperial civilization.
Another decisive development concerned [[Julia Fulger]]. Natashia brought Sasha into the restricted Fulger memorial and confronted the memory of the woman who had raised her daughter during the years of maternal neglect.[14] Julia had been treated as a servant during her life, yet her loyalty and affection toward Sasha exceeded what Sasha had received from her own parents. Natashia recognized that Julia had fulfilled responsibilities that belonged to her and that the family could never properly repay what the woman had sacrificed.
Rather than merely expressing regret, Natashia used the Clan's ancestral records to formally recognize Julia as a member of the Fulger Main Family. The act granted her the family name posthumously and preserved her place within the genealogy of the household.[14] Natashia also arranged for Julia's memory to be honored among the family's most important figures, ensuring that later generations would understand why a former maid deserved recognition alongside ancient Noble Vampires.
The memorial possessed a second purpose. Natashia intended Julia's example to become a permanent lesson for Fulger servants and mothers, demonstrating the importance of loyalty, genuine affection, and responsibility toward children. Her own failures would likewise serve as a warning against allowing power, status, or personal problems to replace the obligations of motherhood.[14] The decision represented one of Natashia's most meaningful attempts to repair her relationship with Sasha because it acknowledged the woman Sasha had always regarded as her true maternal protector.
The same period illustrated the increasingly elemental nature of Natashia's powers. Her perfected Count transformation had progressed toward the condition of living Lightning, allowing her body to express characteristics associated with the original elemental state of the [[Fulger Bloodline]].[14] What initially appeared to be an unusually advanced Vampire technique would later be understood as part of the family's deeper Lightning-Spirit ancestry.

#### Demonic War
Natashia entered the Demonic War as the restored Countess of Clan Fulger and one of [[Nightingale]]'s principal ancient rulers. She participated in the preparations for the Meeting of Supernatural Beings alongside [[Scathach]] and Agnes, representing the old Count families whose political and military strength had helped sustain Vampire society for centuries.[49] Each Countess brought the ancestral Divine treasure associated with her lineage. Natashia carried the [[Taranis]] Daggers, while Scathach possessed [[Gae Bolg]] and Agnes carried the [[Blade of Fafnir]].
The Gathering assembled representatives of the major supernatural civilizations, including Vampires, Gods, [[Werewolves]], Witches, Angels, and the recently unified Youkai. Natashia's position as a Countess gave her a direct role in Nightingale's representation, but the political assumptions underlying the meeting were already becoming obsolete. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s rise had changed the relationship between traditional Vampire nobility and the rapidly growing faction surrounding the Second Progenitor.
The diplomatic meeting was interrupted when [[Diablo]] arrived and revealed that Hell's invasion had already begun.[50] Demonic armies had entered the Human World through numerous portals, while major supernatural territories were simultaneously threatened by forces that had spent years preparing for the conflict. Nightingale itself soon became a battlefield as Demonic portals opened throughout the lands controlled by its great Count families.
The invasion placed Clan Fulger under immediate threat. Sasha remained responsible for defending the family territory and displayed the results of the training she had received from Natashia.[23] Rather than simply attacking with uncontrolled bursts of Lightning, Sasha employed techniques built around the ancestral Fulger concept of returning the body toward the original state of a Lightning Spirit. Her use of Lynet, the first form of the Clan's Secret [[Martial Arts]], demonstrated the principles Natashia had preserved and transmitted to her daughter.
The larger defense of Nightingale revealed how much the old Count families had developed since Victor entered their lives. Clan Fulger's Lightning users fought alongside the elemental forces of Snow and Scarlett and the Shadow specialists of Clan Blank, confronting Demonic armies that had been prepared specifically to exploit the weaknesses of supernatural society.[51] As the conflict expanded, Fulger increasingly mobilized independently instead of relying exclusively upon Nightingale's Crown to coordinate every decision.[52]
Natashia's activities during the war also extended beyond conventional battlefield operations. She traveled through England with Sasha and [[Tatsuya Rider]] to locate [[Viviane]], the Lady of the Lake and an important figure among the surviving Fairies.[53] The mission held personal significance for Natashia because Viviane had known Carmila Fulger, whose death remained part of the family's unresolved past.
Viviane had isolated herself from much of the supernatural world and initially treated the arrival of Vampires with suspicion. Natashia approached her with the familiarity of someone who understood both Viviane's temperament and the history connecting her to Carmila.[54] The Fairy had remained unaware of several major developments, including the rise of the Second Vampire Progenitor and the changes taking place throughout Nightingale. Natashia consequently spent considerable time explaining the events that had transformed supernatural society during Viviane's isolation.
The conversation also exposed the emotional weight of Carmila's presumed death. Viviane had regarded the ancient Fulger founder as a friend, and learning what had happened to her affected the normally detached Lady of the Lake.[54] Natashia's presence therefore connected the Fairy mission to the history of Clan Fulger rather than reducing it to a purely strategic attempt to recruit another supernatural faction.
Natashia and her companions offered Viviane an alternative to continued isolation. The surviving Fairies had suffered from exploitation, persecution, and the ambitions of stronger supernatural races, while the Demonic War threatened the natural environments upon which many of them depended. The Fulger group sought to convince Viviane that cooperation with Victor's faction could provide a future in which the Fairies retained their identity without remaining defenseless against every powerful civilization that coveted their abilities.
The war also brought a major transformation to Natashia's own Bloodline. Victor began deliberately strengthening the women connected to him through his Progenitor Blood, altering their physical and supernatural foundations while preserving their original inherited characteristics. Natashia and Sasha underwent the process together, their Golden Lightning erupting uncontrollably as the power within their bodies increased.[11]
The transformation required precautions because the sudden amplification of their abilities produced enormous electrical discharges. Victor and Scathach maintained protective measures while the process continued, preventing the uncontrolled release of Lightning from endangering the surrounding area. When the transformation concluded, Natashia and Sasha discovered that their overall power output had increased substantially.[11]
The changes were not limited to raw strength. Their Lightning and perception became more pronounced, and dormant characteristics of the [[Fulger Bloodline]] were brought closer to the surface. These developments suggested that Victor's Blood could enhance hereditary qualities that the family itself had never fully understood, opening the possibility that their connection to Carmila's original nature could eventually become much more significant.
The enhancement also refined their physical appearance. Both women acquired permanent blood-red eyes and more developed characteristics associated with Victor's lineage, while retaining their recognizable Fulger identities.[11] The process therefore strengthened their relationship with Victor's Blood without simply replacing the original Lightning heritage that had defined their family for generations.
Natashia's relationship with Victor continued throughout the wider war, although the escalating conflict repeatedly separated members of his household as different factions confronted Demonic armies, infiltrators, and political conspiracies. Her position as a Countess and senior member of his family meant that the danger surrounding Victor affected her both personally and politically.
The war eventually culminated in Victor being forcibly banished into Hell. Natashia reacted with intense anger alongside Violet, Agnes, and Morgana, while Scathach considered increasingly extreme measures to recover him.[55][56] The event demonstrated how completely Natashia had become integrated into Victor's household. His disappearance was no longer simply the loss of a powerful political ally, but the separation of a husband from women who had placed their lives and futures beside him.
Victor eventually transformed the intended punishment into the conquest of Hell, spending approximately seven hundred subjective years fighting, developing his abilities, and ruling the infernal realm before returning to his family.[57] The reunion brought the women around him face to face with a husband who had experienced centuries of life while they had endured a comparatively short separation.

#### King Among Kings
After the Demonic War, Natashia continued functioning as one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s established wives while Clan Fulger became increasingly integrated into the faction he was constructing. The old distinctions between [[Nightingale]]'s Count families gradually lost much of their political importance as Snow, Scarlett, and Fulger came to operate within the same wider family structure. Natashia's longstanding rivalry with the other Countesses consequently developed into increasingly close cooperation, although none of the women's competitive personalities disappeared.
Victor's understanding of Natashia's behavior had also deepened. Rather than regard her exaggerated emotions as evidence that she lacked intelligence, he recognized that she remained capable of serious judgment even while behaving in ways that other people considered outrageous. His description of her as a lucid crazy person reflected this distinction.[17] Natashia herself continued displaying little concern for conventional dignity when interacting with her husband or the women closest to him.
Her affectionate eccentricity became particularly apparent through the household's unusual internal traditions. Natashia, Violet, and Leona possessed some of the rarest photographs circulating among Victor's wives, participating in an informal exchange of images and memorabilia involving their husband.[58] Although the activity was humorous, it also demonstrated how the household had developed its own private culture in which ancient Countesses, Goddesses, and other powerful women behaved much more like an extended family than representatives of competing political factions.
Natashia's concern for the younger generation also became more evident. Before Hassan was permitted to instruct members of the household, Natashia and Agnes investigated him carefully rather than accepting his usefulness as an assassin as sufficient proof that he could safely approach their children.[59] The caution contrasted sharply with Natashia's earlier behavior toward Sasha and demonstrated that she had become much more conscious of the dangers that could arise when powerful adults exercised authority over young members of the family.
[[The Tower of Nightmares]] period introduced another important development in Natashia's elemental knowledge. Victor possessed memories acquired from [[Zeus]], one of the [[Greek Pantheon]]'s most experienced Lightning users, and transferred useful portions of that accumulated knowledge directly to Natashia.[60] The exchange provided her with access to theories, techniques, and practical experiences developed over the lifetime of an ancient God-King, expanding her understanding beyond the traditions preserved exclusively within Clan Fulger.
The transferred knowledge possessed particular value because Fulger's Lightning had developed along lines that differed from conventional Divine electrical powers. The Clan's supernatural techniques were rooted in Lightning-Spirit ancestry, while Zeus' accumulated experience represented a separate tradition developed through Divine authority and centuries of combat. Natashia could therefore compare the two systems and identify areas in which their principles overlapped or differed.
One of the relevant subjects was the Fulger Static Field, a Lightning-based sensory technique developed to compensate for the limitations of ordinary perception during extremely rapid combat. Natashia explained how members of the Clan distributed Lightning through their surroundings and interpreted disturbances as information about nearby objects and opponents.[61] The method offered omnidirectional perception but required significant neurological adaptation because the amount of information collected could overwhelm the user's brain.
Natashia's explanation also established the limitations of the technique during her Vampire era. The ordinary Fulger Static Field extended approximately one hundred meters, while the activation of Count powers increased the effective radius to approximately five hundred meters. Natashia's perfected Count state reached approximately nine hundred meters, although she generally restricted her combat field to around fifteen meters to maintain greater practical precision.[61] Victor subsequently demonstrated methods for expanding the system further, but that larger application belonged to his own development rather than being a feat personally performed by Natashia.
Natashia's greatest personal crisis occurred shortly before the next Meeting of Supernatural Beings. While progressing through the Tower of Nightmares, she suddenly collapsed and began experiencing overwhelming pain that could not be explained through ordinary physical injury.[62] Victor immediately examined her condition and discovered that the problem originated within the core of her Soul.
The examination revealed two additional spiritual presences associated with Natashia's damaged existence: the consciousness previously known as Natasha and the surviving Soul of Carmila Fulger.[8] The discovery finally explained abnormalities that had affected the family for centuries. Carmila had not simply disappeared after attempting to save Natashia during childhood; part of her existence had remained connected to the damaged spiritual structure she created.
Carmila explained that the Fulger family descended from extremely powerful Lightning Spirits and that Natashia had possessed sufficient potential for a dangerous Spirit Evolution to begin naturally. Her original Vampire body would have been unable to survive the transformation, forcing Carmila to interfere with the Soul Core responsible for the process.[8] The intervention prevented Natashia's death but fractured the Soul, creating the conditions through which Natasha later emerged as a second consciousness.
Victor initially reacted with hostility to discovering foreign spiritual presences inside his wife, but the circumstances were fundamentally different from malicious possession. Carmila had attempted to save her daughter, and Natasha had developed enough individuality to become more than a temporary alternate personality. Destroying either presence would therefore have erased a being whose existence had become inseparable from the family's history.
Rather than choose that outcome, Victor reconstructed the damaged spiritual foundation. He altered Natashia's existence toward a Vampire possessing Spirit-like characteristics, allowing her Soul to reject presences that did not properly belong within it while remaining stable after their separation.[9] The process did not create entirely new Souls from nothing; it preserved and reorganized the existing spiritual individuals so they could continue independently.
Carmila and Natasha received separate physical bodies, ending the condition through which all three had remained connected to one damaged Soul. Victor subsequently renamed Natasha Naty to distinguish her from Natashia and prevent the old confusion between their identities from continuing.[9] The result transformed the Fulger family's circumstances. The original Natashia could finally exist without another consciousness competing for control, while Naty obtained the independence necessary to build her own relationships and future.
The unusual nature of Naty's existence surprised even powerful beings such as [[Gaia]] and Nyx. A personality originating from a fractured Soul was not ordinarily expected to accumulate enough individuality to survive as an autonomous spiritual being after separation.[7] Naty had nevertheless developed through centuries of experience, forming memories, desires, and a sense of identity sufficiently distinct from Natashia to be preserved.
The restoration also reunited Natashia with the mother she believed had died long ago. When Carmila appeared before her, Natashia initially struggled to comprehend what she was seeing. Once she understood that her mother was genuinely alive, her usual confidence collapsed into overwhelming emotion. She embraced Carmila and cried, while Carmila addressed her daughter by the full name Annasthashia.[22]
For Sasha and Victoria, the restoration was equally extraordinary. Three generations of Fulger women could now appear together as independent individuals rather than remaining separated by death, damaged Souls, and generations of misunderstanding. The circumstances did not instantly erase the emotional consequences of the past, but they finally allowed the family to confront those consequences without the additional complication of several consciousnesses occupying a single body.
The incident also fundamentally changed Natashia's relationship with Naty. The two no longer needed to struggle for control of their shared existence and could instead develop as separate women with their own identities. Their original conflict had been shaped by a condition neither completely understood, and Victor's intervention removed the physical and spiritual circumstances that had perpetuated it for centuries.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ascension as the Dragon God of [[Chaos]], his family began preparing for another stage of racial evolution. Victor had become capable of reconstructing members of his household into genuine [[True Dragons]] through his own Essence, although he refused to make the transformation mandatory for every woman connected to him. The decision remained personal because Dragonhood could fundamentally alter the biological and supernatural foundation of its recipients.
Natashia accepted the opportunity to become part of Victor's Blood Dragon Race. The transformation was important not only because it offered superior physical power but because her original Vampire body had always possessed a dangerous incompatibility with the Lightning-Spirit characteristics of her Bloodline.[1] The reconstruction therefore promised to resolve a problem that had persisted since Carmila's intervention during Natashia's childhood.
The transformation involved much more than acquiring visible Draconic traits. Victor's Essence and developing powers reconstructed the participating women at a fundamental level while preserving the qualities they had already developed through their previous races.[15][63] The process produced Dragon eggs whose individual characteristics reflected the person undergoing transformation, demonstrating that the resulting Dragons would not become interchangeable copies of Victor.
Members of the Fulger family retained their Lightning abilities, just as other transformed women preserved the powers associated with their original ancestry. Natashia consequently emerged with a True Dragon body capable of incorporating her hereditary elemental characteristics without being constrained by the weaknesses of her former Vampire physiology.[2] Her earlier knowledge, combat experience, and connection to the Fulger martial tradition were not erased by the transformation.
The reconstruction also changed the meaning of her Spirit Evolution. Before Dragonhood, the process had threatened her life because her body could not accommodate the transition into a high-level elemental existence. Her new Draconic foundation possessed much greater supernatural compatibility, allowing the ancestral Spirit characteristics to become part of her development rather than remaining a suppressed source of instability.
The family's subsequent training revealed additional abilities inherent to Dragonhood. Members of the Fulger and Scarlett branches discovered that words spoken with Draconic intent could impose comparatively simple commands upon Reality itself.[16] The development demonstrated that Natashia's new race possessed capabilities extending far beyond enhanced strength, speed, or elemental output.
For Natashia, the change united several previously separate stages of her life. She retained her ancient Vampire experience, the hereditary Lightning developed through Clan Fulger, the extraordinary knowledge obtained from [[Zeus]], and the restored Soul that Victor had repaired. These characteristics now existed within the superior foundation of a True Blood Dragon, allowing her to continue progressing alongside the family rather than remaining restricted by the racial limitations she had inherited.

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s development into the God Emperor, the Elderblood family underwent another enormous transformation through accelerated Time. Although only approximately two years passed in the outside world, Victor and many members of his household experienced roughly two thousand subjective years, allowing their abilities, relationships, and civilization to develop at an extraordinary rate.[64]
The changes within the Fulger family were particularly substantial. Carmila had returned to life, Naty possessed an independent existence, Victoria remained an important member of the restored household, and Sasha had grown into a powerful Blood Dragon. Natashia herself no longer suffered from the spiritual damage or physical incompatibility that had defined much of her former life. The family that had once been divided by neglect, death, and political decline now formed one of the branches of Victor's expanding dynasty.
Natashia and Victor also became the parents of [[Angelina Elderblood]], who inherited the Fulger family's Lightning and closely resembled her mother.[5] Angelina displayed an affectionate and possessive temperament consistent with the characteristics of the women surrounding Victor, becoming involved in the competition among his daughters for their father's attention.
Angelina's existence possessed considerable emotional significance for Natashia. Sasha had grown up during the period in which Naty controlled their shared body, and the original Natashia had been forced to rebuild a damaged relationship after returning. With Angelina, Natashia had the opportunity to participate in her daughter's upbringing from the beginning while possessing a much more developed understanding of the consequences of parental neglect.
One of Angelina's early appearances demonstrates the unusual environment in which the younger Elderblood generation was raised. She became involved in a dispute over Victor's belongings, including clothing that several daughters regarded as valuable personal treasures.[5] Her inherited Lightning gave her the mobility necessary to escape many ordinary situations, but her sisters possessed their own extraordinary abilities and were unwilling to abandon the disagreement.
The confrontation eventually attracted the attention of Natashia, Naty, Carmila, and Victoria, who appeared together in flashes of Lightning.[5] The scene was an especially important demonstration of the Fulger family's restoration. During the early story, the four women could not have gathered as independent individuals because Carmila was presumed dead, Naty remained within Natashia's Soul, and the family itself was divided. Their appearance together showed how thoroughly Victor's intervention and the passage of Time had transformed their circumstances.
The later generation also included [[Julia Elderblood]], the biological daughter of Victor and Naty.[65] Julia was named in honor of the deceased [[Julia Fulger]], whose care had been essential to Sasha's childhood. Her birth continued the complicated but ultimately restored history of the Fulger household, giving Naty a daughter of her own while preserving the memory of the woman whose death had helped expose the family's earlier failures.
Angelina and Julia are therefore separate daughters with different mothers. Angelina belongs to Natashia's biological line, while Julia is Naty's daughter. Although Natashia and Naty originated from the same damaged spiritual existence, their separation established them as independent women whose subsequent relationships and children must be distinguished.
The Imperial family continued expanding throughout this period, and Victor's daughters inherited different combinations of his Draconic nature, their mothers' powers, and the supernatural characteristics of the Elderblood lineage. Natashia's branch remained especially associated with Lightning, while the family's growing administrative and military responsibilities placed the older Fulger women within the wider Imperial structure rather than leaving them confined to the traditional Count system of [[Nightingale]].

#### Those Who Rule Reality
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually initiated a far greater period of accelerated Time as the [[Elderblood Empire]] prepared for conflict with civilizations existing beyond its familiar Sector. The undertaking was intended to grant approximately 200,000 years of internal development while a much shorter period passed outside the affected civilization.[66] The ritual involved enormous quantities of Energy and the cooperation of Victor's most powerful family members, allowing the Empire to develop its technology, population, military infrastructure, and supernatural systems across an immense subjective period.
Natashia participated in this era as a member of the ruling family rather than remaining outside the accelerated civilization. Her subjective age consequently increased far beyond the approximately nineteen centuries she had lived before the story began. Combining her original age, the earlier major accelerated period, and the subsequent two-hundred-thousand-year Imperial era establishes a conservative minimum exceeding approximately 203,900 subjective years, without attempting to calculate every smaller period of Time acceleration that may have occurred.
The growth of the Empire transformed the responsibilities of its ruling family. Victor could no longer personally supervise every administrative problem within a civilization spanning enormous portions of the galaxy, and he deliberately entrusted major responsibilities to his wives and trusted subordinates. Natashia's position as the head of Clan Fulger therefore developed into a role within a far larger Imperial government.
By Chapter 1143, Carmila, Natashia, Naty, Victoria, and Sasha were handling different administrative responsibilities within the Elderblood Empire.[10] Their participation demonstrated how completely the Fulger branch had moved beyond its former circumstances. The family no longer existed simply to preserve the Count title of [[Nightingale]] or maintain a Noble Vampire Bloodline. Its members had become part of the leadership responsible for sustaining an interstellar civilization developed across hundreds of thousands of subjective years.
The change also reflected Victor's own development as a ruler. During the early story, he frequently attempted to involve himself personally in every matter concerning his loved ones, but the scale of the Empire eventually made that approach impractical. Natashia and the other senior Fulger women consequently exercised greater independence, acting within their assigned responsibilities rather than waiting for Victor to decide every question.[10]
The later Imperial family gatherings further demonstrated the scale of Victor's dynasty. During the gathering involving more than thirty-five daughters, several members of the younger Fulger generation appeared alongside sisters from other branches of the family.[67] The chapter identifies [[Julia Elderblood]] as Naty's daughter and introduces several additional daughters associated with Sasha, Carmila, and Victoria.
The same chapter contains a naming inconsistency concerning Natashia's daughter. It introduces Ekaterina Natasha Elderblood as the daughter of Annasthashia, while the earlier Chapter 991 explicitly identifies [[Angelina Elderblood]] as Natashia and Victor's daughter.[5][67] Because the two chapters conflict and the later identification does not establish a separate birth, this article retains Angelina as Natashia's confirmed daughter rather than indexing Ekaterina as an additional child.
The increasingly powerful younger generation also reflected the extraordinary status reached by the family. Victor's descendants were no longer simply strong Vampires or Dragons by the standards of the old world. Many possessed abilities connected to Divinities, Draconic powers, and higher supernatural systems that ordinary civilizations could scarcely understand, making the careful upbringing of the Imperial Princesses an important concern for their mothers and instructors.
Angelina later participated in one of Victor's discussions with his daughters concerning the nature of Reality. During the conversation preserved in Chapter 1185, she asked her father about Positivity and Negativity and whether they were more fundamental than attributeless Energy. Victor explained their relationship to the Creator and the underlying structure of the Universe, using the question to teach the younger generation about the difference between the primordial foundations of Reality and the Energy systems operating within it.
The interaction demonstrated that Angelina's development extended beyond inherited Lightning and family competition. Like several of her sisters, she had become interested in understanding the metaphysical structure of the Cosmos and the powers possessed by her father. Her presence in these discussions also indicated the increasingly advanced education available to the Elderblood generation, whose members were being prepared to understand supernatural principles far beyond those known to most of the old Pantheons.
Natashia's own later appearances remain primarily connected to the Fulger household and its responsibilities within the Empire. Her established Lightning-Spirit heritage, repaired Soul, True Dragon physiology, and administrative position continue defining her place within Victor's family, while her daughters and relatives represent the continuation of the lineage she once struggled to restore.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Natashia's husband and the individual most responsible for the transformation of her later life. Their relationship began through conflict because Victor initially encountered her body while Natasha controlled it. He had already learned about Sasha's childhood suffering and refused to tolerate the behavior of the people he regarded as responsible for it.[28][29] The confrontation eventually contributed to the reappearance of Natashia's original personality, bringing the energetic woman back into active control of her body.
Natashia became attracted to Victor almost immediately, particularly because he possessed the confidence to challenge powerful ancient Vampires without permitting their status to determine his behavior. Victor, however, remained dissatisfied with the condition of Clan Fulger and the treatment Sasha had received. He refused to accept Natashia's affection as sufficient reason to overlook those problems and instead challenged her to repair her family and become a better mother.[19]
His influence became especially important during Natashia's attempt to reclaim the Count title. Victor encouraged her to challenge Niklaus and restore the family's position, but he did not simply attempt to resolve every problem on her behalf. When the confrontation placed her life in immediate danger, he supplied his Blood and allowed her to regenerate from devastating injuries.[38][13] The experience strengthened their attachment while demonstrating that Victor would support her without denying her the opportunity to fight for her own goals.
Their attraction gradually became mutual, and Natashia eventually took the initiative in transforming their relationship into marriage. After consummating their relationship, she personally prepared the Vampire marriage ritual and asked Victor to accept her as his wife.[47][4] The ceremony connected them through the supernatural Blood bond of Vampire marriage and established Natashia as Victor's fourth official wife.
Victor subsequently contributed to nearly every major stage of Natashia's development. His Blood strengthened her Lightning and removed important limitations on her ability to use it without injuring herself. He later deliberately evolved the [[Fulger Bloodline]] through his Progenitor powers, amplifying Natashia's existing hereditary characteristics rather than replacing them with unrelated abilities.[11]
His transfer of [[Zeus]]' accumulated Lightning knowledge expanded her understanding beyond the techniques preserved by Clan Fulger.[60] More importantly, Victor discovered the ancient damage within her Soul and repaired the spiritual foundation that had forced her to share her body with Natasha and Carmila's surviving presence.[9] The intervention restored Carmila to life, allowed Naty to become independent, and finally gave Natashia the opportunity to exist without the condition that had shaped her life for centuries.
Victor later reconstructed Natashia as a True Blood Dragon, giving her a body capable of sustaining the Lightning-Spirit evolution that had originally endangered her life.[2] Their relationship consequently encompasses emotional attachment, supernatural marriage, family restoration, racial evolution, and the creation of the next Elderblood generation. They later became the biological parents of [[Angelina Elderblood]].[5]

#### Sasha Elderblood Fulger
[[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] is Natashia's first biological daughter and the individual whose suffering forced Natashia to confront the consequences of her earlier life. Sasha grew up while Natasha controlled the shared body through important periods of her childhood, receiving harsh Lightning training and little stable maternal affection. [[Julia Fulger]] consequently became the person Sasha regarded as her principal mother, while her relationship with her biological parents deteriorated.
When the original Natashia resurfaced, she attempted to compensate through overwhelming affection, leaving Sasha confused and uncomfortable because the behavior contradicted nearly everything she remembered about her mother's body.[19] [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s intervention prevented Natashia from assuming that a personality change would automatically erase the past. Natashia apologized and acknowledged that Sasha had no obligation to forgive her immediately.
Their relationship gradually improved through Natashia's actions. She resumed her responsibilities as Clan head, recovered the Count title, and continued training Sasha in the hereditary martial techniques of Clan Fulger. The instruction preserved the family's Lightning-Spirit traditions and allowed Sasha to develop powers beyond the ordinary abilities expected of a young Noble Vampire.[23]
The memorial to Julia represented one of the most important stages of their reconciliation. Natashia recognized that Julia had loved and protected Sasha when she herself had failed, and she formally inducted the deceased maid into the Fulger Main Family.[14] The act demonstrated that Natashia had begun respecting Sasha's emotional history rather than attempting to replace it with her own newly expressed affection.
Mother and daughter later underwent Victor's Progenitor-Blood evolution together, increasing their Lightning output and hereditary potential.[11] They subsequently became members of his True Blood Dragon lineage, allowing the original Fulger powers to develop through a superior Draconic foundation.[1][2]

#### Natasha Elderblood Fulger
[[Natasha Elderblood Fulger]], later known as Naty, originated from the damage inflicted upon Natashia's Soul during Carmila's attempt to suppress Spirit Evolution. The two originally shared a body and much of their history, but developed contrasting personalities and conflicting interpretations of the responsibilities surrounding their family.[8]
Natasha controlled their shared body through significant portions of Sasha's childhood and was responsible for much of the emotional neglect associated with her upbringing. The original Natashia initially resented her for the consequences of those years, while Natasha regarded herself as the consciousness forced to live through circumstances that Natashia had avoided or failed to confront. Their disagreement became especially apparent during the internal confrontation produced by Natashia's battle with [[Niklaus Horseman]].[18]
The later revelation concerning their Soul established that Naty had developed enough independence to exist as a genuine spiritual individual rather than merely a temporary personality. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] repaired the damaged foundation and gave both women separate bodies, after which he renamed Natasha Naty to prevent continued confusion.[9][7]
Their separation permanently changed the relationship between them. Instead of competing for possession of one body, they could now exist as distinct women and develop their own futures. Naty subsequently became the mother of [[Julia Elderblood]], while Natashia became Angelina's mother.[5][65] The two women remain connected through their common origin, shared memories, and place within the Fulger family, but their identities and children must not be treated as interchangeable.

#### Carmila Fulger Elderblood
[[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]] is Natashia's mother and the origin of the Lightning-Spirit ancestry inherited by Clan Fulger. Her original nature as a powerful elemental Spirit established the characteristics that later generations inherited through the [[Fulger Bloodline]].[23]
Carmila's attempt to save Natashia during childhood shaped the lives of both women for centuries. Recognizing that Natashia's Vampire body would not survive Spirit Evolution, Carmila interfered with the Soul Core responsible for the process.[8] Although the intervention saved Natashia, the resulting damage produced the spiritual fracture from which Natasha emerged.
Natashia believed Carmila had died permanently and remained unaware that a surviving portion of her mother's Soul was still connected to her own existence. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] discovered that presence during Natashia's collapse and reconstructed Carmila into an independent body after repairing the damaged Soul.[9]
Their reunion was one of the most emotional moments of Natashia's life. Despite being an ancient Countess who frequently behaved with overwhelming confidence, she immediately embraced Carmila and cried when she realized her mother was genuinely alive.[22] The restoration allowed them to develop a relationship that no longer depended exclusively upon memories of the past.
Carmila later became part of Victor's extended family and participated in the affairs of the Fulger branch. By the later Imperial Era, she and Natashia were undertaking responsibilities alongside Naty, Victoria, and Sasha within the [[Elderblood Empire]].[10]

#### Victoria Fulger Elderblood
[[Victoria Fulger Elderblood]] is Natashia's younger sister and one of the most important individuals involved in the restoration of Clan Fulger. Whereas Natashia possesses a highly impulsive and combat-oriented personality, Victoria demonstrates greater patience for administration, finance, and long-term organization. Their contrasting qualities made them particularly effective when working toward the recovery of the family's political position.
Victoria had attempted to reclaim the Fulger Count title before Natashia's original personality returned, wagering an enormous portion of her economic resources against Clan Horseman.[25] Her actions demonstrated that she remained invested in the family's restoration despite the difficulties surrounding its leadership.
After regaining control of her body, Natashia brought Victoria more directly into Clan Fulger's affairs and relied upon her administrative skills while preparing to restore the family's position.[20] The reunion also provided Natashia with another opportunity to repair family relationships affected by the centuries of spiritual instability.
Victoria's personal insecurities were connected to the extraordinary standards established by the [[Fulger Bloodline]]. Her circumstances differed from those of her relatives whose Lightning developed more naturally, contributing to her determination to demonstrate her own worth through other accomplishments. The restored family gradually provided an environment in which those abilities could be valued without requiring Victoria to imitate Natashia or Sasha.
Their relationship continued into the Imperial Era, when Victoria became part of the administrative branch handling responsibilities throughout the [[Elderblood Empire]].[10]

#### Scathach Elderblood Scarlett
[[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] is Natashia's longstanding rival, friend, and fellow wife of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Their history extends across centuries, during which Natashia repeatedly challenged [[Scathach]] and treated [[Nightingale]]'s strongest female Vampire as a benchmark against which to measure her own abilities.
Although their personalities frequently clash, Natashia regards Scathach with considerable affection and respect. During the confrontation in which Scathach reacted violently to Natashia's intention to visit Victor, Natashia initially defended herself through superior mobility while attempting to understand the cause of the attack.[21] Once she realized Scathach had begun acknowledging her feelings for Victor, her reaction shifted from confusion to genuine excitement.
Natashia's celebration of Scathach's emotional development demonstrated that their friendship was not merely a convenient alliance between powerful Countesses. She wanted Scathach to experience happiness even though both women loved the same man, and she encouraged her to stop postponing the relationship unnecessarily.[21]
Their friendship eventually became part of the larger unity of the Elderblood household. Both women responded with intense concern when Victor was banished into Hell, while the expansion of his faction gradually reduced the importance of their former political rivalry.[55]

#### Agnes Elderblood Snow
[[Agnes Elderblood Snow]] is another ancient Vampire Countess whose relationship with Natashia developed through the political rivalries of [[Nightingale]] and the shared family surrounding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. The traditional separation between Clans Snow and Fulger gradually became less important as both women joined the same household.
Natashia and Agnes experienced many of the same crises surrounding Victor's rise, including the Demonic War and his later disappearance into Hell.[55] Their relationship consequently developed beyond aristocratic competition into cooperation founded upon their mutual attachment to Victor and the welfare of his family.
Their concern for younger members of the household was demonstrated when they investigated [[Hassan-i-Sabbah]] before permitting him to serve as an instructor.[59] The incident reflected their shared willingness to exercise maternal authority and protect the next generation rather than assuming that powerful subordinates were automatically trustworthy.

#### Julia Fulger
[[Julia Fulger]] was the maid who raised Sasha and became the most important maternal figure of her childhood. Although Julia was not originally recognized as a member of the Fulger Main Family, her care and affection toward Sasha were far more consistent than those provided by the girl's biological parents.
Julia's murder by the Inquisition devastated Sasha and contributed to the conflict that eventually brought [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] into direct confrontation with William Florence and Natasha.[26] The loss remained emotionally important long after the immediate circumstances had been resolved.
Natashia eventually recognized that Julia had fulfilled the responsibilities she herself had failed to perform. Rather than regard the maid's importance as a threat to her authority as Sasha's biological mother, she posthumously granted Julia the Fulger surname and entered her into the Main Family genealogy.[14]
The memorial established Julia's place within the family's history and served as a lesson to later generations about the value of genuine loyalty, affection, and responsibility. Her influence continued through the naming of [[Julia Elderblood]], the daughter of Victor and Naty, whose name commemorates the woman who had effectively raised Sasha.[65]

#### Angelina Elderblood
[[Angelina Elderblood]] is Natashia and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s biological daughter, born during the periods of accelerated development experienced by the Elderblood family.[5] She closely resembles Natashia and inherited the Fulger family's Lightning, combining those characteristics with the violet Draconic Eyes associated with Victor's daughters.
Angelina possesses an affectionate and possessive personality, displaying the competitive tendencies common among the Elderblood daughters when seeking Victor's attention. Her early involvement in disputes over Victor's belongings demonstrated both her inherited speed and the playful rivalry existing among members of the younger generation.[5]
Angelina represents a particularly meaningful development in Natashia's life because she was born after the restoration of the Fulger family. Unlike Sasha, whose childhood had been shaped by the conflict between Natashia and Naty, Angelina could grow up with her biological mother present as an independent individual.
Natashia therefore possessed an opportunity to apply the lessons learned through her earlier failures as a mother. The relationship also demonstrated that the Fulger branch had developed beyond simply recovering its ancient position, becoming part of the next generation of Victor's Imperial dynasty.
Angelina later appeared among Victor's daughters during discussions of higher supernatural principles. Her question concerning Positivity, Negativity, and attributeless Energy in Chapter 1185 demonstrated an interest in understanding the conceptual foundations of the Universe rather than remaining focused exclusively upon inherited combat powers.
The later name Ekaterina Natasha Elderblood introduced for Annasthashia's daughter in Chapter 1165 conflicts with Angelina's earlier identification. This article retains Angelina as Natashia's established daughter and does not create another child from the inconsistent identification.[67]

### Abilities

#### Powers & Abilities
Annasthashia Elderblood Fulger, commonly known as Natashia, possesses a combination of hereditary Fulger Lightning, ancient Vampire combat experience, Lightning-Spirit characteristics, knowledge acquired from [[Zeus]] and the abilities of a True Blood Dragon. Her present powers are the result of multiple transformations that preserved and developed her original Lightning specialization rather than replacing it with an unrelated set of abilities.
Her feats must be distinguished from those of Naty, who originated as her alternate consciousness but was eventually given an independent Soul and body.[1] Techniques that Natashia taught to Sasha are included as part of her martial knowledge, but Sasha's individual battlefield performances are not automatically feats performed by Natashia. Similarly, general True Dragon racial traits should not be treated as proof that Natashia has personally mastered every ability demonstrated by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
- True Blood Dragon Physiology: Natashia was reconstructed as a True Blood Dragon when Victor transformed the family members who accepted his new race. The process preserved her Fulger abilities while replacing the original Vampire body with a more powerful Draconic foundation.[2][3]
- Enhanced Physical Characteristics: True Dragonhood provided a substantial improvement in strength, speed, durability, stamina, reflexes and physical potential beyond her former Vampire body.[3]
- Enhanced Senses: Her Draconic physiology complements the already exceptional perception she developed through Fulger Lightning.[3]
- [[Draconic Energy]]: Natashia acquired the superior Energy system characteristic of Victor's True Dragon lineage, allowing her original elemental powers to operate through a more capable supernatural foundation.[3]
- Draconic Metamorphosis: Her modern body possesses the racial ability to express Draconic characteristics, including wings, horns and other manifestations associated with [[True Dragons]].[3]
- Flight: She possesses natural Draconic aerial mobility in addition to the Lightning-based flight technique already demonstrated during her Vampire life.[4][3]
- Draconic Eyes: Natashia acquired the specialized eyes of a True Dragon. Their exact individual capacity has not been established to be equivalent to Victor's highest ocular abilities.[3]
- Regenerative Healing: True Dragon physiology provides a superior regenerative foundation to the healing Natashia already possessed as an ancient Noble Vampire.[3]
- Supernatural Adaptation (Racial): True Dragons possess exceptional adaptability to supernatural conditions. This does not establish that Natashia can independently overcome every form of Reality alteration or Conceptual attack.[3]
- Creation Compatibility (Racial): Victor's True Dragon lineage possesses a natural relationship with the processes of Creation. Natashia's personal mastery of a complete Creation Divinity has not been established.[3]
- Spirit–Dragon Integration: Her Dragon body can sustain the ancestral Lightning-Spirit characteristics that once threatened the stability of her Vampire physiology.[5][3]
- Draconic Breath (Racial): Dragon Breath is a characteristic of the lineage, although Natashia has not demonstrated a separately named personal Dragon Breath technique with established output.[3]
- Draconic Language: Through Dragonhood, Natashia became compatible with the supernatural language through which Dragons express their [[Will Solace|Will]] and impose meaning upon Reality.[6]
- Reality Imposition: The Dragonized Fulger and Scarlett families demonstrated that simple spoken instructions accompanied by Draconic Will could directly influence Reality.[6]
- Limited Reality Warping: Draconic speech can reproduce effects by imposing an instruction upon the surroundings. Natashia's personal maximum has not been demonstrated to equal specialists such as [[Anna Elderblood]].[6]
- Limited Creation: Members of the transformed families demonstrated the creation of ordinary objects through sufficiently simple Draconic instructions.[6]
- Draconic Rune Compatibility: Natashia possesses racial compatibility with Draconic principles and supernatural language. This does not establish individual Runemaster proficiency comparable to Victor's leading specialists.[6]
- Fulger Lightning-Spirit Heritage: Clan Fulger's ancestral powers originated from [[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]], an immensely powerful Lightning Spirit who became a Vampire through an arrangement involving Vlad. The resulting Bloodline retained the capacity for descendants to approach their original elemental condition.[7]

- Living Lightning: Natashia perfected her Vampire Count transformation to the point that her physical body became living Lightning.[8]
- Elemental Physiology: While completely transformed, her body functions as an organized elemental phenomenon rather than ordinary biological flesh.[8]
- Elementalization: Natashia can replace her conventional bodily state with Lightning, making many ordinary physical limitations less relevant while transformed. This should not be mistaken for absolute immunity to every attack.[8]
- Elemental Mobility: Her living-Lightning state naturally complements the exceptional speed of her hereditary electrical powers.[8]
- Lightning-Recoil Resistance: Fulger Vampires historically suffered damage when their flesh could not withstand the output of their own inherited Lightning. Natashia stated that Victor's Blood had corrected this weakness, greatly improving her ability to use the secret martial techniques.[7]
