Valentina Victoria Elderblood is a recurring character in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]], an Imperial Princess of the [[Elderblood Empire]], a True Dragon Goddess, and the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and Violet Elderblood Snow. As one of Victor's naturally born [[Blood Dragons]], she belongs to the Imperial Family's second generation and possesses an extraordinarily powerful Draconic foundation accompanied by the supernatural heritage of the [[Snow Clan]].[1][5]
Valentina is distinguished among Victor's daughters by her exceptionally intense personality, which combines the defining characteristics of both parents. She inherited Violet's possessiveness, jealousy, aggressiveness and obsessive attachment to her loved ones alongside Victor's ruthlessness toward enemies, overwhelming pride and disregard for people who threaten his family. These characteristics manifested to such an extreme degree that outsiders could regard her as a sadistic and deeply unpleasant child. Within the Elderblood household, however, Valentina displays an entirely different disposition, treating her parents and sisters with affection, gentleness and fierce loyalty.[1]
Her temperament immediately attracted the attention of [[Lily Elderblood]], whose own sadistic interests made her particularly receptive to Valentina's personality. Lily was willing to teach the young Dragon Goddess torture techniques, although Valentina's later development encompasses considerably more than this initially emphasized disposition. She demonstrates a sharp analytical mind, familiarity with supernatural Energy systems, a strong sense of responsibility toward the Imperial Family and a growing desire to contribute personally to the Empire established by her father.[1][6][4]
Valentina is particularly close to [[Silvia Elderblood]] and [[Rosemary Lavina Elderblood]], the respective daughters of Sasha and Ruby. The three are practically inseparable and frequently participate in family activities together, reproducing many of the chaotic characteristics associated with Victor's original three wives. Their behavior eventually earns them recognition as a trio of chaos.[7][8]
Despite her naturally dangerous temperament, Valentina received the extensive education expected of an Imperial Princess. Victor and his wives deliberately ensured that their daughters understood responsibility, humility, the limitations of inherited strength and the consequences of using their enormous powers without restraint. Consequently, Valentina is capable of behaving with discipline and dignity when necessary, even though her private interactions with her family are frequently marked by possessiveness, mischief and arguments.[1][4]

### Physical Appearance
Valentina strongly resembles her biological mother, [[Violet Elderblood Snow]]. During her introduction, she appears as a beautiful young girl approximately 120 centimeters tall, possessing long white hair, pale skin, deep violet eyes and a pair of violet Draconic horns. Her appearance closely mirrors Violet's characteristic [[Snow Clan]] features, making their relationship immediately recognizable.[1]
Her violet eyes are characteristic of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s biological daughters and are associated with the supernatural perception inherited from their father. Her Draconic horns reveal her nature as a genuine True Dragon despite an otherwise largely Humanoid appearance. When introduced alongside Silvia and Rosemary, the three girls resemble miniature versions of their respective mothers, with Valentina distinguished by her white hair and violet horns.[1][6]
Although Valentina possesses the appearance of a small child, her natural body is extraordinarily powerful and bears characteristics that cannot be understood through ordinary Human physiology. [[True Dragons]] retain the immense weight of their Draconic bodies even when assuming Humanoid forms unless they actively control that characteristic. During Victor's meeting with Evie, the tremendous weight of the three young Dragon Goddesses is apparent when they settle upon the furniture, despite their small bodies.[6]
Valentina's youthful appearance is primarily a consequence of True Dragon maturation. Victor's children possess unusually prolonged developmental periods, with their physical growth occurring across millennia rather than the years ordinarily required by Humans. Research conducted by Velnorah, Ruby and Aline established developmental benchmarks of approximately two thousand years for the childhood stage, five thousand years for the adolescent stage and ten thousand years for full physical adulthood. Their mental development is not restricted to the same schedule, particularly because Victor began providing extensive education while his daughters were still physically young.[1]
Valentina also possesses the Draconic ability to modify her appearance. During the younger generation's attempt to involve itself in Imperial administration, she returns to her natural childlike form after having assumed an older appearance. Her sisters similarly possess the ability to adopt more physically mature forms, although the family maintains an informal rule against excessively altering their appearances merely to imitate their mothers.[4]
Her interest in resembling Violet more closely continues into the later Imperial era. While observing Ophis receiving affection from Victor after transforming herself into a childlike appearance, Valentina realizes that her own efforts to develop a more mature feminine figure have caused her to overlook the advantages of her naturally youthful appearance. This reaction reflects her ongoing desire to resemble her mother without diminishing her attachment to Victor's affectionate treatment of his daughters.[9]

### Personality
Valentina possesses an unusually intense personality characterized by possessiveness, sadism, intelligence, emotional attachment and a strong distinction between those she considers family and everyone else. Her temperament represents an almost perfect combination of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Violet's personalities, with their more extreme characteristics amplified considerably. Toward enemies, particularly anyone threatening her father, she can be extraordinarily ruthless and demonstrates little sympathy. Toward Victor, Violet and the wider Elderblood household, however, she is affectionate, loyal and capable of genuine tenderness.[1]
Her devotion to Victor is one of the strongest influences upon her behavior. Even individuals belonging to allied factions, whom Valentina might otherwise consider unimportant, are generally spared her cruelty because she does not wish to disappoint her father. This restraint demonstrates that her behavior is not indiscriminate; her sadistic inclinations coexist with a clear understanding of Victor's expectations and the importance of maintaining his approval.[1]
Valentina also possesses the intense jealousy associated with her mother's [[Snow Clan]] lineage. She is particularly sensitive to the possibility of additional women entering Victor's family and initially regards prospective new mothers with suspicion. Nevertheless, she is capable of separating emotional discomfort from practical judgment. During the encounter with Evie Moriarty, Valentina evaluates the Witch Queen's abilities and importance to Victor's faction despite personally disliking the possibility of her becoming another wife. Having already examined Evie's records, she recognizes the Witch Queen's competence and considers her potential place within the family alongside women such as Albedo, [[Dún Scáith|Dun Scaith]] and Kali.[6]
Her intelligence extends beyond political observations. Valentina is familiar with different supernatural Energy systems and demonstrates an understanding of [[Magic]] sufficient to explain its basic nature to her sisters. She also possesses considerable knowledge of Victor's history, including the circumstances surrounding Albedo granting him access to Magic. Her ability to obtain information, analyze relationships and communicate complex subjects reflects the extensive education provided to Victor's daughters.[6]
Valentina maintains a strong sense of humor and enjoys the entertainment culture preserved within the Empire. She is familiar with anime, movies and other media, although she is not as obsessively devoted to anime as Priscilla. During [[Nero]] and Ophis' training, she recognizes references to popular entertainment, discusses the creative value of fictional combat techniques and agrees with Rosemary that Human imagination can provide inspiration for developing supernatural abilities. Her familiarity with these subjects allows her to participate naturally in her sisters' increasingly elaborate jokes and discussions.[7]
Despite her inherited power, Valentina respects genuine combat experience. When observing Nero and Ophis fighting, she recognizes that their superior practical experience would make them difficult opponents even for extraordinarily talented members of the younger generation. Rather than dismissing their abilities because she possesses an exceptional bloodline, she acknowledges the considerable difference between having great potential and possessing the knowledge required to use it effectively.[7]
Valentina also demonstrates determination and growing maturity concerning her position as an Imperial Princess. When she and her sisters discover that Imperial administration is far more demanding than expected, she refuses to allow the difficulty to become an excuse for abandoning their ambitions. She encourages Irene and the others to continue learning rather than surrender simply because their first experience reveals how much they still need to understand. Her attitude emphasizes gradual progress, responsibility and the desire to become genuinely useful to their father's civilization.[4]
Her relationship with her older sisters contains a mixture of admiration and envy. Valentina respects Ophis and Nero's experience and understands that their familiarity with Victor's earlier life gives them advantages unavailable to children born after his ascension. She occasionally regrets not having been born earlier, wishing she could have accompanied her father through more of his history. However, these feelings do not prevent her from loving her older sisters or seeking their guidance.[7]
Within the family, Valentina is sociable, confident and capable of taking the initiative during discussions involving large groups of sisters. She also possesses the courage to challenge authority when she believes an important family principle has been violated. Her disagreement with Violet over the unilateral introduction of additional women into Victor's household develops into a comedic rebellion in which she openly proposes replacing her biological mother with Anna as Empress. The confrontation demonstrates that Valentina's loyalty is not equivalent to unquestioning obedience and that she considers consultation and respect between family members essential even when dealing with her own mother.[8]
Although she frequently behaves mischievously, Valentina does not lack emotional sensitivity. When Victor prepares to leave his daughters to attend to an important matter, she anxiously asks whether he will return. His promise that he will always come back reassures her, and she immediately accepts his request to look after her younger sister. Her concern reflects an enduring attachment to Victor that survives even after an immense period of accelerated Imperial development.[10]

### Name
Valentina's complete canonical name is Valentina Victoria Elderblood, incorporating names independently selected by both of her parents.[5]
Valentina was chosen by [[Victor Elderblood]], while Victoria was chosen by [[Violet Elderblood Snow]]. The latter is the feminine form of Victor's name, deliberately selected because Violet wanted their daughter to possess a name resembling her beloved husband. [[Scathach]] subsequently considers the combination particularly appropriate because Valentina developed into such a complete mixture of both parents' personalities.[5]

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Valentina was born during the extensive period of accelerated Time experienced by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his family following his evolution into a Dragon God. Although only approximately two years passed outside Victor's personal Reality, roughly two thousand years elapsed within it. The extraordinary duration allowed several of Victor's wives to complete the prolonged gestation required to produce naturally born [[True Dragons]], resulting in the emergence of an enormous second generation of exceptionally powerful daughters.[1]
Violet's pregnancy lasted approximately one thousand years. The duration was relatively short compared with several other pregnancies within Victor's family, some of which approached two thousand years. Valentina nevertheless possessed extraordinary potential from birth, establishing that a longer gestation period did not automatically indicate a more talented child. The differences in gestation were related to the conceptual characteristics inherited from the parents and the complexities involved in developing each child's existence.[1]
Valentina was approximately one thousand years old by the time Victor returned to deal personally with the deteriorating circumstances of the Witches. She accompanied him alongside two of her closest sisters, Silvia and Rosemary. All three stood approximately 120 centimeters tall and possessed the distinctive Draconic horns and appearances inherited from their mothers. Evie initially failed to notice their presence and was subsequently shocked to discover that Victor already had biological daughters despite the immense gestation periods ordinarily associated with Dragons.[1]
Victor's family had deliberately accelerated the mental development of its children rather than allowing them to remain psychologically immature for thousands of years. Valentina received an extensive education concerning supernatural society, morality, abilities and the responsibilities accompanying her extraordinary existence. Her temperament nevertheless remained a source of considerable attention. She displayed an extremely sadistic and twisted disposition toward outsiders, particularly those hostile to Victor, while simultaneously behaving with affection and gentleness toward her own family. [[Lily Elderblood]] found her personality exceptionally appealing and expressed an interest in teaching her torture techniques.[1]
Violet regarded Valentina's resemblance to herself and Victor with considerable satisfaction. When Violet later found [[Scathach]] reading in Victor's personal world and inquired about her husband's whereabouts, Scathach referred to Valentina and the two other girls accompanying him as troublemakers. Violet instinctively disliked the characterization but could not meaningfully dispute it. Their conversation also reflected the growing parental responsibilities faced by the women of the Elderblood household after centuries of accelerated development.[5]
Valentina's appearance alongside Victor became more significant during his meeting with Evie Moriarty. While the Witch Queen attempted to understand Victor's intentions and the immense changes to his power, Valentina, Silvia and Rosemary silently communicated through telepathy. Their conversation moved between Evie's supernatural abilities, the strange Energy surrounding her, Victor's historical relationship with Albedo and the possibility of the Witch Queen eventually becoming another member of their family.[6]
Valentina quickly identified the unfamiliar Energy as [[Magic]]. Although she regarded it as inferior to the extraordinary powers available to the Elderblood family, she acknowledged its versatility and explained that Albedo had previously granted Victor access to it. Her familiarity with the subject surprised her sisters and revealed that she had already studied information concerning supernatural powers outside their own racial foundation.[6]
Rosemary subsequently suggested that Evie might eventually become another of Victor's wives, provoking an immediate possessive reaction from Valentina and Silvia. Despite her displeasure, Valentina considered the possibility analytically, drawing upon Evie's records and Victor's existing relationships. Her thoughts became sufficiently intense that Silvia noticed portions leaking through their mental connection, forcing Valentina to acknowledge the mistake.[6]
When Victor formally introduced his daughters, he placed his hand upon Valentina's white-haired head and identified her as his daughter with Violet. Valentina immediately smiled in response to his affection. Evie meanwhile realized that the children were considerably more dangerous than their appearances suggested. Their enormous physical weight, exceptional sensory abilities and already-established status as Dragon Goddesses revealed how extraordinarily powerful Victor's biological descendants had become.[6]
The sisters continued observing their father's negotiations while treating several aspects of the encounter as entertainment. Valentina made an inappropriate comment through their telepathic conversation and immediately became uncomfortable when Victor turned his attention toward her, demonstrating that he remained aware of their behavior despite concentrating upon Evie. Later, when Victor's overwhelming superiority became increasingly apparent, the girls regretted not preparing cameras to record the event. They eventually remembered that Magic could reproduce memories as recordings, allowing them to preserve the experience without conventional equipment.[6]
The encounter further established the considerable confidence possessed by the second generation. Valentina regarded the Witch Queen's faction as insignificant compared with the power of the Elderblood family and understood that Victor did not require elaborate schemes to defeat or annex a much weaker organization. Nevertheless, she and her sisters refrained from interfering with his negotiations, respecting his authority while learning from his methods.[6]
As the Empire continued expanding, Valentina's interests developed beyond observing her father. Together with several sisters, she began seeking opportunities to contribute directly to Imperial administration. The girls had received education in government and supernatural society, but they quickly discovered that understanding the theory of administration was very different from managing an Empire whose policies affected entire Solar Systems and enormous populations.[4]
The difficulty initially discouraged several members of the younger generation. Valentina, however, refused to accept failure as an appropriate response. When Irene expressed frustration, Valentina questioned whether the challenge justified abandoning their objective and encouraged the sisters to continue learning at a manageable pace. Her determination reflected the family's broader expectation that an Imperial Princess should understand the consequences of exercising authority rather than merely enjoying the privileges of her birth.[4]
During this period, Valentina also demonstrated her ability to assume more mature appearances through Draconic Metamorphosis. She returned to her naturally childlike form while speaking with her sisters and participated in discussions concerning the family's informal rules against excessively imitating their mothers' appearances. The restrictions reflected the belief that each daughter should develop her own identity instead of attempting to become a physical copy of an older family member.[4]
Valentina later joined Stella, Silvia and Rosemary in observing [[Nero]] and Ophis during a training session. Their older sisters demonstrated extraordinary combat ability, combining advanced martial arts, supernatural mobility, Reality manipulation and Draconic techniques. Valentina acknowledged that even the most talented younger daughters would struggle to compete with their experience, understanding that inherited power alone could not replace centuries of practical combat training.[7]
The spectacle also revealed Valentina's familiarity with popular entertainment. She recognized the inspiration behind one of Ophis' techniques, discussed the usefulness of fictional combat ideas and agreed that Human imagination could inspire genuine supernatural techniques. She also warned Stella and Silvia about Priscilla's tendency to force her sisters into lengthy anime-viewing sessions, demonstrating how thoroughly Human entertainment had become integrated into the culture of Victor's daughters.[7]
After the training session, Valentina and her companions approached Nero and Ophis to ask about their responsibilities within the Empire. Ophis immediately recognized the reason for their interest, surprising Valentina with her perception. Valentina privately acknowledged how much experience Ophis had accumulated through her association with Vlad and Victor before the Empire's foundation. Although she envied the years her older sisters had spent beside their father, she set those feelings aside and enthusiastically requested information about Imperial administration.[7]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
As the [[Elderblood Empire]] entered an unprecedented period of accelerated development, Valentina continued maturing alongside her sisters. The enormous temporal project initiated by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Natalia was intended to provide the Empire with approximately two hundred thousand years of internal development while comparatively little Time elapsed beyond the affected region. This expansion transformed the Imperial Family's younger generation from children born during Victor's initial accelerated era into beings possessing extraordinary chronological ages, education and experience.[2]
One of Valentina's most memorable appearances during this era occurred when Violet attempted to arrange the addition of more women to Victor's household without adequately consulting the other members of the family. Kaguya and several of Victor's wives sensed the situation almost immediately, while daughters began gathering to confront Violet. Valentina was among the earliest to arrive and openly criticized her biological mother for making decisions affecting everyone without first communicating with them.[8]
Silvia and Rosemary quickly joined the confrontation. Their familiar relationship remained unchanged despite the enormous passage of Time, and the three again behaved as a chaotic group whose willingness to challenge authority matched the possessiveness inherited from their mothers. Violet attempted to defend her decisions by invoking her long history of managing Victor's family, but the daughters refused to accept seniority as sufficient justification for acting unilaterally.[8]
The situation escalated when approximately forty daughters surrounded Violet and demanded answers. Victor prevented the argument from becoming physically dangerous by forbidding aggressive action and suppressing the use of Violet's Authority over Order within his own Reality. Nevertheless, he refused to resolve the disagreement on Violet's behalf, leaving the Empress to confront the consequences of her actions.[8]
Valentina eventually escalated the confrontation into an openly comedic rebellion. Declaring Violet excessively authoritarian, she proposed removing her from the position of Empress and replacing her with Anna. Her declaration immediately received support from the surrounding daughters, who began chanting in favor of Anna and against the supposed tyrant. Violet reacted with disbelief that her own biological daughter would support such a proposal, but Valentina remained committed to the joke and the underlying criticism.[8]
The disagreement did not develop into a genuine struggle for Imperial authority. It nevertheless demonstrated the independence of the younger generation and emphasized that even Violet, as Victor's first wife and an Empress, was expected to respect the other members of the household when making important family decisions.
Valentina subsequently appeared during a gathering of Imperial Princesses following an incident involving Priscilla and several sisters attempting to investigate Pepper's secret research. When Ariella expressed concern about the apparent abduction of Velina, Valentina initially dismissed the situation as another example of her sisters' ordinary mischief. She then recognized its probable connection to recent events and helped bring the other daughters into the discussion.[11]
As additional daughters arrived, including members of the Fulger and Greek branches of the family, Valentina recounted the situation and became a central participant in the gathering. More than thirty-five daughters eventually assembled, exchanging information and reacting to the circumstances surrounding their sisters. The meeting also displayed the complicated relationships between the naturally born daughters and older sisters such as Ophis, [[Nero]], [[Metis]], Akasha and Melida, whose more intimate or unusual relationships with Victor occasionally provoked jealousy among the younger generation.[11]
Despite their disagreements, the Imperial Princesses remained closely connected. Their behavior changed immediately when Victor arrived for the family gathering, with the daughters rushing toward him and competing for his affection. Valentina's attachment to her father remained consistent with her earliest appearances, demonstrating that extraordinary chronological age had done little to diminish the emotional importance of her family.[11]
Valentina later observed Ophis using her powers to assume a childlike appearance and settle comfortably upon Victor's shoulder. Realizing that such an appearance encouraged the affectionate treatment she herself desired, Valentina regretted spending so much attention attempting to develop a more mature figure resembling Violet. Her reaction demonstrated the continuing conflict between her wish to resemble her mother physically and her enjoyment of being pampered by her father.[9]
Valentina also participated in Victor's extensive lessons concerning Divinity, Reality, Creation and the fundamental principles governing the Cosmos. During a discussion involving the hypothetical elimination of universal Concepts, Victor asked his daughters to consider the consequences of removing Infinity itself. Valentina correctly recognized that the disappearance of Infinity would fundamentally alter the Cosmos, depriving it of the limitless quality associated with that Concept. Victor approved of her response before continuing his explanation of Primordial Beings and the structures of Creation.[12]
These lessons exposed Valentina and her sisters to the distinction between possessing enormous Energy and genuinely understanding the Divine Concepts governing Reality. They also reinforced how radically their father's existence differed from that of ordinary Gods, particularly through his relationship with [[Eldritch Energy]] and the fundamental powers associated with Beginning and Negativity.[12]
Valentina's emotional attachment to Victor became especially apparent when he prepared to leave his daughters to handle an important matter. After Victor instructed one of his younger daughters to remain with her older sisters, Valentina anxiously asked whether he intended to return. He reassured her that he would always come back and affectionately stroked her head before asking her to look after her younger sister. Valentina accepted immediately, while the other daughters recognized the anger Victor was concealing over the circumstances requiring his attention.[10]
The sisters consequently acknowledged their need to continue becoming stronger so that they could eventually assist Victor more directly rather than remaining dependent upon his protection. Valentina's concern and the collective reaction of the younger generation reflected the continuing importance of family solidarity within the Empire.[10]
Valentina later became an active participant in another instructional discussion when Victor asked his daughters to consider the fundamental nature of Energy. She questioned the meaning of Attributeless Energy, explaining that although the subject was important, her sisters had not provided an entirely satisfactory explanation. Victor used her question to distinguish ordinary Divine, Demonic, Spiritual and other forms of Energy from the more fundamental source from which such attributes could emerge.[13]
When Victor asked her to distinguish [[Divine Energy]] from Demonic Energy, Valentina initially described them through their respective characteristics. He then complicated the distinction by referring to her sisters who simultaneously possessed Divine and Demonic foundations. After further explanation, Valentina understood that different forms of Energy represented manifestations or applications of a more fundamental principle rather than completely unrelated substances.[13]
Her understanding became clearer when Victor demonstrated the relationship between Attributeless Energy and the various Energy systems employed throughout Creation. Valentina compared Attributeless Energy to a blank canvas upon which different forms could be created, correctly recognizing its importance as a foundation capable of producing multiple attributed Energies. Victor approved of her interpretation before expanding the lesson into [[Primordial Energy]], Eldritch Energy and the principles underlying Runes.[13]
Valentina's later activities also reveal her participation in the younger generation's more dangerous experiments. She joined Yog, Nigredo, Leone, Hinata, Silvia, Rosemary and Stella in an attempt to develop unusual hybrid creatures through the combination of Eldritch influences, Leviathan characteristics and additional supernatural abilities. The group obtained a powerful Draconic scale belonging to Victor and began considering how the creatures could be further developed.[14]
Valentina participated by proposing the incorporation of Dragon Fire, while Silvia suggested Dragon Lightning, Rosemary requested Ice, Hinata offered Solar power and Stella contributed the power of Truth. The sisters eventually agreed to combine portions of their respective abilities into the experiment, with Velina joining after initially objecting to the risks. Their cooperation reflected the extraordinary versatility possessed by Victor's daughters and their tendency to approach dangerous supernatural experimentation with curiosity rather than caution.[14]
The incident also demonstrated that Valentina's original reputation for chaos had never disappeared. Although she had acquired immense experience, education and a greater understanding of her responsibilities, she remained willing to participate in unusual and potentially dangerous family projects alongside her sisters.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Valentina's biological father and the individual whose approval most strongly influences her behavior. She possesses an intense emotional attachment to him, characterized by loyalty, admiration, possessiveness and a desire to remain close to him whenever possible. Her father is exempt from the sadistic disposition she displays toward outsiders, and she responds to his attention with genuine affection.[1]
Victor deliberately ensured that Valentina and her sisters received an extensive education rather than allowing their extraordinary biological potential to replace training and discipline. He began teaching his naturally born daughters about supernatural society while they were still physically young and continued providing personal instruction as their abilities developed. Valentina consequently became familiar with numerous supernatural systems, the history of his powers and the philosophical principles underlying Divinity.[1][13]
Valentina particularly enjoys Victor's displays of power and is enthusiastic about preserving records of his activities. During the encounter with Evie, she regrets failing to bring cameras and eagerly supports the suggestion of reproducing recorded memories through [[Magic]]. Her attachment also explains her reaction whenever another woman is mentioned as a possible addition to Victor's family.[6]
Despite her immense inherited power, Valentina remains sensitive to Victor's disapproval. When she makes an inappropriate remark during a telepathic conversation with her sisters, his attention immediately causes her to behave. His approval likewise provides an important reason for her restraint around individuals whom she otherwise regards as insignificant.[1][6]
Her relationship with Victor continues developing as she becomes more independent. She seeks opportunities to assist in Imperial administration, requests explanations concerning sophisticated supernatural principles and recognizes the need to become stronger so that she can support him. Nevertheless, her underlying emotional attachment remains unchanged, as demonstrated when she anxiously asks whether he will return before one of his departures and is reassured by his promise.[4][10]

#### Violet Elderblood Snow
[[Violet Elderblood Snow]] is Valentina's biological mother and the parent she most strongly resembles in physical appearance. Valentina inherited her mother's white hair, [[Snow Clan]] characteristics, possessiveness and aggressive disposition, although those qualities were substantially influenced by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s own personality. The resulting combination is regarded as an exceptionally accurate reflection of both parents.[1][5]
Violet personally chose the Victoria portion of her daughter's name because it represented the feminine form of Victor. This decision reflected Violet's longstanding obsession with her husband and her desire for their daughter to carry a name directly associated with him.[5]
Valentina admires Violet's appearance and seeks to resemble her more closely, occasionally using her Draconic Metamorphosis to assume an older form. Her preoccupation with developing a more mature appearance demonstrates the influence Violet continues exerting upon her daughter's personal ideals even after thousands of years of development.[4][9]
Their relationship nevertheless includes substantial disagreements. Valentina is willing to directly oppose Violet when she believes her mother has disregarded the interests of the wider family. Her criticism of Violet's unilateral decisions concerning potential new wives culminates in her joking proposal to replace Violet with Anna as Empress. Although the confrontation embarrasses and irritates Violet, it occurs within a household whose members possess strong emotional bonds and are accustomed to expressing disagreement openly.[8]

#### Silvia Elderblood and Rosemary Lavina Elderblood
[[Silvia Elderblood]] and [[Rosemary Lavina Elderblood]] are Valentina's closest regularly demonstrated companions. Each is the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and one of his original three wives, creating a parallel between the relationships of the mothers and those of their daughters. The three are frequently found together, participate in telepathic conversations and share an enthusiasm for their father's activities.[6][7]
During the meeting with Evie, Silvia notices when Valentina's thoughts begin leaking through their mental connection, while Rosemary's curiosity provokes discussions about [[Magic]], the Witch Queen and potential changes to the family. Valentina frequently contributes prior knowledge and calculated observations, balancing the others' reactions with her own analytical approach.[6]
Their closeness continues throughout the Empire's development. Valentina, Silvia and Rosemary observe [[Nero]] and Ophis training together, discuss entertainment and supernatural techniques, and regularly participate in activities involving their other sisters. The three are considered practically inseparable, and their collective tendency toward mischief becomes particularly apparent during the confrontation with Violet.[7][8]
By the later Imperial era, their reputation as a trio of chaos extends beyond arguments and practical jokes. They also become involved in the unusual experimentation undertaken by Yog and Nigredo, proposing the combination of Draconic abilities with Eldritch creatures and demonstrating the adventurous tendencies common among Victor's daughters.[14]

#### Lily Elderblood
[[Lily Elderblood]] is one of the members of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household who finds Valentina's extreme temperament particularly entertaining. Lily's personal inclination toward sadism makes her unusually receptive to the young Dragon Goddess, and she expresses a willingness to teach Valentina torture techniques.[1]
Their connection is rooted in this compatibility of temperament. Although Lily's interest establishes her as a potential mentor in torture techniques, Valentina's complete training history and proficiency in Lily's personal methods have not been individually detailed.

#### Anna Elderblood
[[Anna Elderblood]] occupies an important maternal position within the Elderblood household and possesses considerable authority over [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters. Her influence is particularly significant because even exceptionally troublesome members of the younger generation generally obey her instructions, while [[Scathach]] maintains discipline through the formidable reputation of her training.[8][11]
Valentina demonstrates her regard for Anna during the family's dispute with Violet. When she proposes replacing her mother as Empress, Anna is the individual she nominates as the alternative. The proposal is comedic, but it reflects Anna's recognized position as one of the household's most authoritative maternal figures.[8]

#### Ophis Tepes Elderblood and Nero Elderblood
[[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]] and [[Nero Elderblood]] are among Valentina's older sisters and possess a unique relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] that predates the birth of his biological second generation. Their experience, familiarity with Imperial administration and extensive combat training distinguish them from many of the younger daughters.[7]
Valentina admires their abilities while occasionally envying the considerable amount of Time they spent with Victor before her birth. When observing their training, she recognizes that even extraordinarily talented Dragon Goddesses would struggle to overcome the experience and refined techniques possessed by the two older sisters.[7]
Her admiration eventually motivates her to approach them for guidance concerning Imperial responsibilities. Ophis correctly identifies the younger daughters' intentions, while Valentina eagerly requests information about the realities of working within the Empire. Despite their occasionally complicated family relationships, Valentina and her sisters genuinely care for Ophis and [[Nero]].[7]

#### Other Sisters
Valentina maintains close relationships with the wider group of Imperial Princesses. She frequently participates in family gatherings involving daughters from numerous branches of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household and is comfortable taking a leading role in discussions among them. During one Imperial family gathering, she recounts an incident involving Priscilla and Velina while more than thirty-five sisters assemble around her.[11]
She also participates in lessons alongside sisters such as Isabelle, Norabell, Yog, Nigredo and other descendants whose powers involve Reality, Space-Time, [[Eldritch Energy]] and unusually advanced Divine Concepts. The family encourages the daughters to exchange knowledge and develop their individual strengths while maintaining mutual loyalty.[12][13]
Her involvement with Yog and Nigredo's experiments demonstrates that she is willing to cooperate with sisters whose Eldritch characteristics differ considerably from her own demonstrated powers. Together with Leone, Hinata, Silvia, Rosemary and Stella, she participates in developing hybrid creatures through the combination of several extraordinary supernatural attributes.[14]

### Snow Clan Heritage
Valentina belongs to the biological continuation of the [[Snow Clan]] through her mother, [[Violet Elderblood Snow]], while simultaneously carrying the superior Draconic lineage established by [[Victor Elderblood]]. Her ancestry connects her to the family of [[Adonis Snow]] and [[Agnes Elderblood Snow]], although her personal identity and political status remain centered upon the Imperial Elderblood dynasty.

IMPERIAL PRINCESS
Valentina inherited the characteristic appearance of her mother's family, including white hair and the strong physical resemblance associated with Violet. Her unusual personality likewise incorporates several traits characteristic of Clan Snow, particularly intense possessiveness and emotional attachment.[1]
Her maternal ancestry must nevertheless be distinguished from personally demonstrated supernatural techniques. Her individual abilities are determined by the interaction of Victor's Blood Dragon foundation, Violet's characteristics and Valentina's own developing powers.

### Development
Valentina's development reflects the unusual combination of prolonged True Dragon maturation, inherited conceptual power, exceptional education and the enormous temporal acceleration experienced by the [[Elderblood Empire]].
Born after approximately one thousand years of gestation, Valentina already possessed a genuine True Dragon foundation and extraordinary potential. Her physical development remained slow compared with Humans, but [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] deliberately accelerated her education so that she could develop emotionally and intellectually without remaining psychologically immature throughout her extended childhood.[1]
Her early personality was characterized by intense sadism, possessiveness and devotion to her father. Through family education and experience, she developed considerable analytical ability, familiarity with supernatural systems and the discipline necessary to behave appropriately as an Imperial Princess. Her interactions with Evie demonstrated these qualities while she was still physically a small child.[6]
As the Empire expanded, Valentina became increasingly interested in contributing directly to its administration. Her initial experiences revealed the difficulty of governing enormous supernatural populations, but she responded by encouraging her sisters to continue learning. She also sought guidance from older sisters already familiar with Imperial affairs and demonstrated growing respect for experience, practical competence and specialized knowledge.[4][7]
The later Imperial era provided an unprecedented period for further development. The enormous temporal project affected the entire civilization, allowing Valentina and the other daughters to accumulate extraordinary chronological ages while continuing their education, training and personal growth. Her later appearances demonstrate involvement in family decisions, philosophical lessons concerning Reality and Divinity, and the increasingly elaborate experimentation undertaken by the younger generation.[2][12][13][14]
