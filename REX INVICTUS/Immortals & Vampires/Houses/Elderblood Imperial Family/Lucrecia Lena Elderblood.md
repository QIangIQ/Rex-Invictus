### Physical Appearance
Lucrecia has red hair, inheriting the characteristic coloration associated with Lacus and the wider Scarlett family. This is established when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] collectively referred to Erika, Priscillia, Rosemary, Sylvia, Siren and Lucrecia as his "redheads" during the family's gathering.[1]
No detailed description of Lucrecia's height, eye color, clothing or other physical characteristics has yet been provided.

### Personality
Lucrecia is described as being usually rather quiet, distinguishing her from several of her more openly mischievous sisters. Despite her normally reserved behavior, she had nevertheless become involved in the chaos caused by the other Elderblood daughters and visibly blushed when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] addressed the group about their behavior.[1]
Her participation indicates that although Lucrecia is comparatively quiet, she is still close enough to her sisters to occasionally become involved in their collective mischief.

### Synopsis

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Lucrecia was formally introduced during the continuation of the enormous Elderblood family gathering. After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] addressed the trouble caused by several of his daughters, he jokingly referred to the group of red-haired girls responsible for some of the chaos.[1]
Among them were [[Erika Vesta Elderblood]], [[Priscillia Penny Elderblood]], [[Rosemary Lavina Elderblood]], [[Sylvia Elderblood]], [[Siren Elderblood]] and Lucrecia Lena Elderblood. The narration immediately identified Lucrecia as the daughter of [[Lacus Scarlett Elderblood]] and Victor Elderblood, additionally noting that she was normally rather quiet. Despite her reserved nature, even Lucrecia blushed after being included among the daughters responsible for the disturbance.[1]
The incident occurred during the family's traditional gathering, where Victor's numerous daughters, wives and other relatives continued arriving and interacting with one another after the earlier commotion involving [[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]].[1]

### Skills and Abilities
Very little has currently been revealed about Lucrecia's individual powers.
- Blood Dragon Physiology: As the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Lacus Scarlett Elderblood]], Lucrecia belongs to the second generation of the Elderblood family's [[True Dragons]]. Her individual racial applications have not yet been demonstrated.
- True Dragon Physiology: Lucrecia belongs to Victor's generation of Dragon daughters, granting her the fundamental nature of a True Dragon. Her specific abilities have not yet been shown.
- Divinity: Like Victor's other Dragon daughters, Lucrecia possesses a Divine nature, although her individual Divine Concept, authorities and titles have not yet been revealed.
- Scarlett Heritage: Through Lacus, Lucrecia belongs to the extended Clan Scarlett lineage. No specific Scarlett-derived ability has yet been directly demonstrated by her.
