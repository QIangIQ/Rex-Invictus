Don't make him disappointed in us because we didn't listen to him.
—Isabelle Elderblood reminding her sisters to obey [[Victor Elderblood]], Chapter 999
Isabelle Elderblood is a recurring character in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]], an Imperial Princess of the [[Elderblood Empire]], and the biological daughter of Victor Elderblood and [[Jeanne Elderblood]]. She is a naturally born Blood Dragon Goddess possessing the complete biological and supernatural foundation of a genuine True Dragon, together with one of the most extraordinary Divine Concepts inherited by Victor's second generation. Isabelle was born possessing the Divinity of <Energy> at the Highest Level, an exceptionally broad authority governing the fundamental principle of Energy rather than a single elemental or supernatural Energy system. Her existence is considered an anomaly even among her extraordinarily talented sisters because the scope of her Concept potentially reaches the fundamental processes sustaining Creation itself.[1][2]
Unlike conventional Gods whose Divinities grant authority over particular manifestations or specialized applications of supernatural power, Isabelle's personal Concept concerns Energy itself. Her natural inheritance includes Primal Energy, which she possessed from birth, and her mature authority is described as encompassing what qualifies as Energy. This does not mean that Isabelle has already demonstrated every theoretically possible application of her Concept or possesses literally infinite usable Energy. Rather, her conceptual foundation provides exceptionally broad potential, while her practical abilities remain dependent upon comprehension, experience, the refinement of her authority and the limitations of her present development.[2][3]
The implications of Isabelle's Divinity extend far beyond ordinary combat. Her Concept possesses the potential to interfere with the distribution of Energy supplied by Creation, a function normally associated with the primordial mechanisms governing the Cosmos. If she prevented an entire Sector from receiving the Energy required to maintain its existence, the structures dependent upon that supply would eventually deteriorate until the region collapsed into cosmic dust. This represents a stated possibility of conceptual Energy deprivation rather than a direct demonstration of Isabelle destroying a Sector with the raw force of an attack. Victor recognizes the dangers associated with such an extraordinary inheritance and consequently restricts the most dangerous powers of his daughters during childhood, intending for their abilities to develop alongside their judgment and self-control.[1]
Isabelle's conception was itself extraordinary. Jeanne carried her for approximately 1,990 subjective years during the accelerated developmental period experienced by Victor's family, making her pregnancy one of the longest among the women who gave birth to the second generation. The exceptionally prolonged gestation was associated with the complexity and developmental level of Isabelle's Divine Concept, rather than a universal rule that a longer pregnancy necessarily produces a stronger child. By the time she was introduced alongside her sisters, Isabelle had existed for only approximately ten years, yet she already possessed an Energy-related foundation vastly beyond the natural starting point of most supernatural beings.[1]
Her abilities also extend beyond her personal Energy Divinity. Through Jeanne, Isabelle possesses a natural affinity with all Life, reflecting the unique supernatural existence of her mother and her maternal relationship with the Universal Tree. Victor's Blood Dragon lineage provides extraordinary physical characteristics, advanced supernatural compatibility, inherited Truth perception, [[Draconic Energy]] and protection against numerous forms of mental, spiritual and biological interference. Her Divine Concept additionally developed under the influence of Victor's Nightmare and Eldritch nature, granting an extra-cosmic conceptual foundation that does not follow precisely the same developmental restrictions imposed upon ordinary native Gods.[4][5][6]
Despite her extraordinary potential, Isabelle possesses a comparatively relaxed attitude toward martial training. She has received instruction from [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] and regularly spars with [[Gina Elderblood]], but initially considers the training she already receives sufficient because her natural authority encompasses Energy itself. Gina challenges this mentality, arguing that possessing overwhelming power is fundamentally different from understanding how to use it effectively. Their rivalry demonstrates one of the principal limitations of Isabelle's development: her innate authority is exceptional, but it does not automatically provide superior martial skill, perfect tactical judgment or victory against opponents who have devoted more effort to practical combat training.[2]
Isabelle is nevertheless intelligent, observant and deeply attached to Victor. During her early appearances, she helps restore order among the younger generation by communicating mentally with her sisters and reminding them that their father would be disappointed if they refused to obey his instructions. Her later intellectual development is equally significant, as she demonstrates considerable understanding of the Energy cycle, the relationships between fundamental Divine Concepts and the consequences of removing a Primordial force from Creation. Victor eventually identifies her as someone whose power could develop sufficiently to interfere with the very mechanisms sustaining the Universe, although that prediction concerns her future potential rather than an ability she has already demonstrated in full.[7][6]

### Physical Appearance
Isabelle is an exceptionally beautiful Dragon Goddess with pale skin, long golden-blonde hair and deep-violet eyes inherited through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood Dragon lineage. Her appearance combines the distinctive characteristics of her parents, particularly Jeanne's golden hair and naturally elegant physical features with the violet Dragon Eyes possessed by Victor's biological daughters. During her earliest appearances, Isabelle retains the youthful appearance expected of a naturally born True Dragon whose chronological age remains extremely small compared with the developmental timescales of the Race. Although she has already inherited an overwhelming Divine Concept, her physical immaturity remains genuine rather than reflecting a deliberate restriction upon an otherwise adult body.[1][7]
Jeanne's pregnancy lasted approximately 1,990 subjective years, leaving Isabelle only around ten years old when the two-thousand-year accelerated period ended. Her youthful appearance was consistent with the extraordinary developmental characteristics of [[True Dragons]], whose biological maturation occurs over timescales vastly longer than those experienced by ordinary Humans. The disparity between Isabelle's physical age and her supernatural power demonstrates that extraordinary conceptual development does not automatically produce an adult Dragon body, even when the individual possesses authority associated with fundamental aspects of Creation.[1]
During the later Imperial era, Isabelle appears as a fully developed young woman standing approximately 185 centimeters tall. Her golden hair extends all the way to the floor, giving her a distinctive appearance among the daughters of Victor and emphasizing the influence of Jeanne's maternal characteristics. She retains her pale complexion and violet Draconic eyes, and her preferred clothing consists of a comfortable white dress rather than elaborate armor or the martial attire favored by some of her more combat-oriented sisters. The simplicity of her clothing complements her relaxed personality and preference for comfort despite her extraordinary supernatural status.[2]
Isabelle's mature Humanoid appearance is partly a matter of personal choice. Her natural adult physique would more closely resemble the fuller proportions inherited from Jeanne, but Isabelle considers that body inconvenient during ordinary activities and prefers maintaining a slimmer figure. When Gina mocks her slender appearance, Isabelle responds that a Dragon Goddess can possess whatever form she wishes, demonstrating that the bodily alterations available through her Draconic nature allow her to adjust her physical presentation according to personal preference. Her appearance should therefore be distinguished from a permanent biological limitation or an indication that she possesses weaker physical attributes than sisters whose bodies appear more muscular.[2]
Her preferred Humanoid form also differs from the complete natural existence of a genuine True Dragon. Isabelle's Draconic physiology provides extraordinary supernatural strength, durability and compatibility with transformation, while her ordinary appearance does not reveal the full extent of her biological or Divine foundation. However, the novel has not described the complete appearance of her individual True Dragon Form in sufficient detail to establish its dimensions, scale coloration, specialized anatomical characteristics or the precise nature of her personal Dragon Breath. Her confirmed distinctive features therefore remain her golden hair, pale skin, violet Dragon Eyes, adult height and chosen slender Humanoid physique.

### Personality
Isabelle is intelligent, composed, observant, affectionate and considerably more relaxed than many of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters. She rarely displays the same constant aggression or mischievous behavior associated with several of her sisters, preferring to approach most circumstances calmly unless her personal pride or relationships become involved. Although she possesses extraordinary natural power, she does not feel compelled to demonstrate that strength constantly, regarding her abilities as an established part of her existence rather than something she must repeatedly prove to everyone around her. This confidence contributes to her generally controlled demeanor, but it also becomes a weakness when she begins relying excessively upon inherited superiority instead of pursuing continuous martial improvement.[7][2]
Isabelle's affection for Victor is one of her most important emotional characteristics. During the gathering involving [[Leon Walker]] and the numerous Imperial Princesses, the daughters become increasingly excited while surrounding their father, causing their behavior to grow more chaotic. Isabelle intervenes by reminding them to listen to Victor and subsequently addresses her sisters through their mental connection, warning them that they should not make their father disappointed merely because they refused to obey his instructions. Her approach demonstrates social awareness because she understands that Victor's approval is exceptionally important to every member of the younger generation and therefore represents a more effective motivation than an ordinary command to remain quiet.[7]
Unlike sisters who express their attachment primarily through attempts to monopolize Victor's attention, Isabelle initially demonstrates her devotion through obedience and concern for his feelings. She recognizes that disappointing him would be more painful to the girls than accepting a temporary restriction upon their excitement, allowing her to restore order without threatening or physically intimidating her siblings. The incident also reveals that she possesses enough emotional maturity to consider how the behavior of the entire group might affect their father, despite having lived for only a small fraction of the period normally associated with Draconic adulthood.[7]
As Isabelle matures, her extraordinary conceptual inheritance becomes a source of considerable personal confidence. She understands that her Divine Authority concerns Energy itself and that she possesses a supernatural foundation beyond the abilities of most conventional Gods. This awareness makes her less obsessive about continuous combat development than certain sisters who rely more heavily upon deliberate training and martial technique. Although Isabelle does not completely neglect her education, she initially considers the lessons received from [[Scathach]] sufficient for her needs, believing that her overwhelming natural authority substantially reduces the importance of acquiring greater physical combat experience.[2]
Gina strongly disagrees with this attitude. Having dedicated considerable effort to physical and martial development, she repeatedly points out that Isabelle's exceptional Energy abilities do not prevent her from losing their sparring matches. The difference between possessing power and knowing how to use it becomes the central issue in their rivalry, with Gina arguing that a sufficiently skilled opponent can overcome someone who possesses greater natural potential but lacks comparable practical control. Isabelle understands the reasoning and recognizes that her sister is deliberately attempting to provoke her, but her pride prevents her from ignoring the criticism when Gina describes her as weak.[2]
Isabelle's reaction demonstrates that she is not incapable of recognizing manipulation. She openly acknowledges that Gina is attempting to force another sparring session and understands that the insult is calculated to exploit her competitive instincts. Nevertheless, she accepts the challenge because she dislikes being treated as inferior, revealing that her relaxed personality conceals considerable pride. Their relationship remains competitive rather than genuinely hostile, since Gina's objective is to encourage the development of her sister's enormous potential rather than diminish her importance within the family.[2]
Outside direct competition, Isabelle generally prefers avoiding unnecessary trouble. When Gina notices a disturbance involving several sisters and asks what has happened, Isabelle simply suggests that it would be better not to become involved and encourages her to continue walking. The reaction contrasts with daughters who actively seek entertainment through mischief or confrontation, demonstrating that Isabelle is capable of recognizing situations in which curiosity is more likely to produce inconvenience than meaningful benefit. Her comparatively relaxed disposition therefore includes a degree of practical judgment, even though she can still be provoked when her own pride becomes involved.[2]
Isabelle possesses genuine intellectual curiosity and considerable understanding of the principles governing supernatural power. During Victor's lessons concerning the nature of Reality, she demonstrates enough prior knowledge that he prevents her from answering one of the questions intended for her sisters. The decision reflects his desire to test those who have not yet developed the same understanding rather than allow an already knowledgeable daughter to provide the solution immediately. Her grasp of the relationship between Divine Concepts, dimensional structures and the fundamental processes sustaining the Universe places her among the younger generation's more perceptive students.[6]
Her intelligence becomes especially apparent when Victor explains the cosmic role of the [[Regulators of Existence]]. Isabelle recognizes that removing Death would not merely prevent individuals from dying, but would also disrupt the Energy cycle and the natural processes through which matter and living beings develop, decline and return to the larger structure of Creation. She understands that the absence of such a fundamental principle would create enormous disorder, although she initially struggles with the apparent contradiction involved in killing Death itself. Victor uses the question to explain that sufficiently powerful existences can interfere with the fundamental mechanisms sustaining Reality, even when those mechanisms normally operate as essential components of the Universe.[6]
Isabelle is also protective of Jeanne and the Universal Tree. When Victor compares the Primordial forces to components in an enormous machine, she objects to the implication that her mother and uncle can be described in such an impersonal manner. Her concern is not based upon a failure to understand the analogy, but upon the belief that the individuals involved would dislike hearing themselves characterized as mechanical components. She promises not to repeat Victor's wording, prompting him to affectionately pick her up and pamper her. Despite her mature appearance and formidable conceptual inheritance, Isabelle responds with the delighted behavior of a daughter enjoying her father's attention.[6]
Her relationship with the Universal Tree reveals a playful side that contrasts with the extraordinary status of her maternal relative. Isabelle apparently introduces him to anime and the terminology associated with Japanese character archetypes. Victor discovers this when the Universal Tree demonstrates familiarity with expressions such as "tsundere" and "kuudere", knowledge that the ancient cosmic entity would have little reason to acquire through his ordinary responsibilities. Victor immediately suspects Isabelle, and the Universal Tree's refusal to deny the accusation reinforces the conclusion. Their relationship demonstrates that Isabelle interacts with her uncle as a familiar member of the family rather than treating him exclusively as a universal Primordial authority.[8]
Isabelle's personality therefore combines extraordinary confidence, genuine intelligence, familial affection and a degree of complacency produced by her exceptional natural abilities. Her most important personal limitation is not an absence of power or potential, but the need to develop practical mastery sufficient to match the fundamental scope of the Concept she inherited. The contrast between her relaxed attitude and Gina's relentless training reflects one of the central lessons Victor wishes to teach his children: possessing overwhelming strength does not eliminate the responsibility to understand and refine it.

### Heritage and Conceptual Origin
Isabelle possesses an exceptionally unusual supernatural inheritance because both of her biological parents had reached extraordinary states of existence before her birth. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s evolved Blood Dragon lineage provides a genuine True Dragon body, overwhelming biological potential, advanced compatibility with different supernatural systems and the predatory characteristics associated with his Bloodline. Jeanne's original nature is fundamentally different from that of an ordinary Goddess, since her existence is directly connected to the Universal Tree and the primordial mechanisms supporting Life. Their union consequently produced a daughter whose supernatural foundation combines Draconic physiology, an unusually broad Divine Concept and characteristics associated with the primordial Energy sustaining Creation.[1][3]
Jeanne's pregnancy lasted approximately 1,990 subjective years, one of the longest periods required for any of Victor's daughters to develop. The exceptional duration reflected the complexity of the Divine Concept forming within Isabelle rather than merely the difficulty of producing another True Dragon. Although a longer gestation could indicate the presence of a particularly advanced Concept, the relationship was not absolute, since other daughters had been born earlier while possessing extraordinary talents of their own. Isabelle and Stella nevertheless became prominent examples of children whose prolonged development was associated with the emergence of a Highest-Level Divine Concept.[1]
Isabelle's inheritance can be understood through several overlapping supernatural foundations. Her biological existence belongs to the Blood Dragon Race, while her Divine authority concerns Energy itself. Jeanne provides a natural connection to all Life and the primordial energetic processes associated with the Universal Tree, and Victor's Nightmare and Eldritch nature contributes characteristics that are not entirely restricted by the predetermined developmental framework of ordinary native Divinities. These characteristics coexist within Isabelle rather than forming separate incompatible [[Races (Flamel)|Races]], reflecting the extraordinary supernatural compatibility of genuine [[True Dragons]].[1][4][3]
The distinction between ordinary native Divinities and the Concepts inherited by Victor's later daughters is particularly important. During the long pregnancies, the mothers adapted to the Nightmare-related characteristics of Victor's supernatural existence, influencing the Divine foundations developing within their children. The resulting Concepts acquired aspects originating outside the ordinary native Cosmology, allowing their potential development to extend beyond the conventional ceiling imposed upon Gods whose powers remain fully governed by the original local framework. Isabelle's Energy Divinity belongs to this unusually advanced class, although conceptual developmental freedom does not establish unlimited current strength or immunity to every higher Authority.[1]
Jeanne later explains that the biological daughters of Victor possess Eldritch and Primordial characteristics to varying degrees, while only a comparatively small number have developed Divinities directly aligned with the [[Eldritch Pantheon]]. Isabelle is singled out as one of the most extraordinary daughters because she possessed Primal Energy naturally from birth and inherited a fundamental relationship with Energy rather than beginning with a comparatively narrow elemental specialization. Her innate advantages make comparisons with more conventionally developed descendants especially misleading, since other individuals may require immense periods of effort to obtain characteristics that Isabelle possessed before she could understand their significance.[3]
Her maternal ancestry also connects her to the Universal Tree, the primordial incarnation responsible for sustaining fundamental processes associated with Life and Reincarnation. Jeanne's unusual existence provides Isabelle with a natural affinity toward living beings even though her own personal Divine Concept remains Energy. This inherited affinity gives her potential sensitivity toward changes affecting Life, including the development of new life within a pregnant individual. However, the connection should not be confused with possession of a second personal Divinity governing Life or the complete authority exercised by the Universal Tree.[4]

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Isabelle's birth occurred after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his family experienced an extraordinary period of accelerated development lasting approximately two thousand subjective years. The pregnancies of his wives proceeded according to the unusual biological and supernatural characteristics of their evolved Draconic existences, with the most complicated Divine inheritances requiring exceptionally long gestation. Jeanne carried Isabelle for approximately 1,990 years, placing her alongside Anna among the women whose daughters required the longest periods to develop. The duration reflected the exceptional complexity of the Divine Concept forming within the child, which eventually manifested as <Energy> at the Highest Level.[1]
The emergence of such a broad Concept immediately raised serious concerns because Isabelle's natural abilities extended into a domain normally associated with the primordial mechanisms maintaining Creation. Energy is not merely one resource employed by supernatural beings during battle; it is fundamental to the continued existence and operation of physical structures, living organisms, Divine abilities and the processes sustaining entire cosmic regions. Isabelle's authority consequently possessed the potential to interfere with the distribution of Energy provided by Creation itself, making her existence exceptionally dangerous if the power were released without sufficient control.[1]
One possible application involved preventing a Sector from receiving the Energy necessary to maintain its existence. Without that supply, the processes supporting stars, worlds, Life and other structures would eventually fail, reducing the region to cosmic dust. The possibility did not require Isabelle to generate a conventional destructive attack powerful enough to overwhelm every celestial body individually, since the catastrophic outcome would follow from depriving the Sector of an essential energetic foundation. The distinction demonstrates the difference between raw destructive capacity and conceptual interference with the fundamental mechanisms upon which Reality depends.[1]
Victor recognized that a child could not reasonably be expected to understand every consequence of possessing such authority, particularly when ordinary emotional reactions might produce enormous collateral damage. He therefore restricted the most dangerous portions of the powers inherited by daughters such as Isabelle, allowing them to grow into their supernatural abilities as their intelligence, judgment and practical control developed. The restriction was not intended to punish the children or permanently suppress their potential, but to protect them and the surrounding civilization from the consequences of uncontrolled conceptual power.[1]
Isabelle's unusually advanced foundation also reflected Victor's developing Nightmare and Eldritch characteristics. During the prolonged pregnancies, the mothers adapted to aspects of his supernatural existence that did not belong entirely to the native Cosmos, allowing the Concepts inherited by their daughters to develop characteristics outside the ordinary restrictions governing local Divinities. Their potential growth was consequently described in exceptionally broad terms, but Victor continued distinguishing future conceptual development from the actual strength and control possessed by a child at any particular stage of life.[1]
Isabelle eventually joined the second generation of Imperial Princesses growing up within Victor's protected Personal World. Although the daughters differed considerably in appearance, age and supernatural characteristics, each possessed a genuine Blood Dragon foundation and access to the extraordinary educational resources maintained by their parents. Isabelle's childhood was comparatively brief at the time of the first major gathering, since her prolonged gestation had left her only approximately ten years old despite the family having experienced almost two thousand years of subjective development.[1]
Her first substantial named appearance occurs when Victor introduces the members of his enormous second generation during a family gathering involving [[Leon Walker]]. The daughters become increasingly excited while attempting to attract their father's attention, with several behaving possessively and competing to remain near him. Isabelle recognizes that the growing disorder risks disappointing Victor and intervenes by reminding the girls to obey his instructions. She subsequently addresses the gathered sisters through their mental connection, warning them not to make their father unhappy merely because they refused to listen.[7]
The intervention demonstrates both Isabelle's emotional intelligence and her ability to communicate directly with several individuals through a shared mental connection. She recognizes that Victor's approval is an exceptionally effective source of motivation because his daughters regard their relationship with him as more important than ordinary disciplinary concerns. Her warning consequently encourages the girls to restrain their behavior without requiring physical intimidation or the use of her extraordinary Energy authority.[7]
As the younger generation continued developing, Victor became concerned that their extraordinary natural abilities were preventing them from acquiring sufficient practical experience. Most had been born with genuine True Dragon physiology, powerful Divine characteristics and supernatural compatibility vastly beyond the abilities of conventional supernatural beings. Although these advantages provided exceptional safety, they also increased the danger of complacency, particularly for daughters who had never experienced situations in which their opponents possessed comparable power or could exploit deficiencies in their knowledge and practical control.[5]
Victor consequently organized the War Games, a series of military exercises intended to develop adaptation, judgment and an understanding of the limitations faced by ordinary soldiers and supernatural civilizations. Before beginning the training, the daughters encountered an environment cold enough to threaten many conventional beings, yet their natural Draconic physiology allowed them to remain comfortable without specialized preparation. Their bodies also possessed inherited adaptive characteristics derived from Victor's own development, allowing them to adjust gradually to unfamiliar environmental conditions and become increasingly resistant to circumstances that might initially cause discomfort.[5]
The same preparations revealed the extraordinary safeguards Victor had established for his children. Each biological daughter possessed advanced protection against Soul and mental invasion, parasitic organisms, biological contamination and other methods of interference that could bypass ordinary physical durability. Victor also maintained protective fragments of his own Soul within his daughters while preserving portions of their Souls inside himself, providing an additional defensive response and a means of restoration if their bodies were destroyed. These arrangements reflected his determination to prevent enemies from exploiting the vulnerabilities of his children even if their inherited supernatural abilities proved insufficient.[5]
During the exercise, an unfamiliar Ice Dragon appeared before the daughters, providing an opportunity to test their ability to analyze an unknown being. Isabelle quickly identified that the creature was female, while other sisters examined different characteristics of its existence and ancestry. Victor used the encounter to emphasize the importance of careful observation and maintaining awareness in unfamiliar circumstances, since even individuals possessing extraordinary senses and natural abilities could suffer serious consequences if they assumed that every situation was harmless.[5]
Isabelle's maternal inheritance later became relevant during the preparations surrounding [[Ariel Elderblood]]'s concealed pregnancy. Violet considered which daughters might discover the situation through their supernatural characteristics, identifying Gina, Irene and Isabelle as individuals possessing unusual sensitivity toward Life-related changes. Gina's connection originated through [[Gaia]] and her own Life Divinity, while Irene's relationship with [[Aphrodite]] provided another relevant foundation. Isabelle's sensitivity was specifically attributed to her status as Jeanne's daughter and her natural affinity with all Life, rather than the acquisition of an additional personal Divinity.[4]
The distinction reinforced the exceptional complexity of Isabelle's heritage. Her principal Divine Concept remained Energy, but the supernatural characteristics inherited from Jeanne provided a relationship with living existence beyond the ordinary abilities possessed by conventional Energy-related Gods. The potential to notice a pregnancy was consequently associated with her maternal affinity rather than an independently demonstrated ability to create Life, control biological development or govern the complete universal Concept of Life.[4]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
The [[Elderblood Empire]] subsequently underwent an enormous period of accelerated development intended to prepare the civilization for conflicts involving more advanced Sectors and enemies possessing extraordinary supernatural technologies. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Natalia, Jeanne and the other senior members of the family sustained the temporal undertaking while the younger generation continued growing and training within the affected territories. Isabelle eventually reached a mature Humanoid appearance and developed considerably greater practical access to her innate Energy authority, although the complete status of every restriction imposed upon her during childhood has not been individually described.[9][2]
By the later Imperial era, Isabelle had become an adult Dragon Goddess approximately 185 centimeters tall, possessing floor-length golden hair, pale skin and violet Draconic eyes. She preferred a comfortable white dress and deliberately maintained a slender Humanoid physique, regarding the fuller proportions inherited from Jeanne as inconvenient for ordinary activities. Her ability to alter her appearance demonstrated the extraordinary bodily control associated with genuine Dragons, whose Humanoid forms need not remain fixed according to the biological characteristics ordinarily possessed by Humans.[2]
Her supernatural authority had also developed considerably. Isabelle was recognized as one of the exceptionally powerful daughters of the Elderblood family, possessing control over Primal Energy and a Divine Concept identified with Energy itself. However, the gap between her natural potential and practical combat discipline had become increasingly apparent, particularly during repeated sparring sessions with Gina. Although Isabelle considered the training she received from [[Scathach]] sufficient, Gina argued that the ability to control overwhelming Energy did not guarantee victory against someone who understood how to employ lesser power more effectively.[2]
Their disagreement eventually developed into another challenge. Gina criticized Isabelle for neglecting opportunities to improve her martial abilities and deliberately described her as weak, knowing that the insult would provoke her sister's pride. Isabelle immediately recognized the manipulation and understood that Gina was attempting to force another sparring session. Nevertheless, she accepted the challenge because she refused to tolerate the implication that she was inferior. Their exchange demonstrated that Isabelle's enormous conceptual potential had not eliminated the importance of physical training, technique, experience and practical judgment.[2]
Isabelle's natural attitude contrasted with Gina's more demanding approach. Gina regarded continued combat development as an obligation accompanying extraordinary inherited strength, while Isabelle preferred relying upon the powers she already possessed and the instruction she received from Scathach. Although Isabelle was not entirely unwilling to train, she lacked the same relentless drive toward improvement displayed by her sister. The rivalry consequently reflected an important difference between their personalities rather than proof that Isabelle lacked intelligence or possessed an intrinsically weaker Divine Concept.[2]
Isabelle later participated in Victor's extensive educational gathering concerning dimensions, Divine Concepts and the underlying construction of Reality. The lesson began after Norabell encountered difficulties creating a stable dimension through her authority over Time and Space. Victor recognized that she was unconsciously attempting to produce an independent Reality rather than an ordinary subordinate dimension, prompting him to demonstrate the difference between a structure supported by existing universal Rules and an independent cosmic environment requiring additional conceptual foundations.[10][6]
Victor created a comparatively simple dimension and a miniature galaxy possessing a more complicated supernatural foundation. The latter contained apparent astronomical structures and Eldritch organisms resembling stars, although the supposed creatures were not genuine independent Life. He used the examples to demonstrate that the number and nature of Divine Concepts stabilizing a structure affected its existential weight and resistance to alteration. Isabelle was sufficiently knowledgeable about the subject that Victor prevented her from answering one of the questions intended for her sisters, recognizing that allowing her to provide the solution would undermine the exercise.[6]
The demonstration also provided a broader explanation of the perceptual inheritance possessed by Victor's biological daughters. Their Dragon Eyes and supernatural characteristics included variations of his ability to perceive the Truth underlying Reality, allowing sufficiently developed individuals to observe structures resembling the source code governing the surrounding world. Although the strength of this inheritance differed among the daughters, each possessed the potential to develop more advanced perception and eventually interact with the Rules sustaining particular dimensional structures. Isabelle belonged to the group already familiar with important aspects of the lesson, although her precise individual level of Truth perception was not quantified.[6]
Victor subsequently explained the function of the Primordial authorities maintaining the native Universe, beginning with the Universal Tree. He described Isabelle's maternal uncle as a fundamental incarnation of Life whose existence extended throughout the Cosmos and connected with the [[World Trees]] distributed across Creation. The Universal Tree possessed cosmic consciousness and an extraordinary capacity to perceive events across the Universe, while his continued existence remained essential to the operation of fundamental processes associated with Life. Isabelle was genuinely surprised to discover the full extent of her uncle's power, having previously interacted with him primarily as a familiar and comparatively gentle relative.[6]
The lesson became more serious when Victor asked what would happen if Death itself were eliminated. Isabelle correctly recognized that removing the fundamental principle of Death would prevent the ordinary conclusion of matter and living existence, disrupting the processes through which Energy circulates throughout the Universe. Without Death participating in the greater cycle, the natural relationship between living beings, matter, Energy and the return of existence to Creation would become profoundly unstable. Although she understood the consequences, Isabelle questioned how something that embodied the principle of Death could itself be killed without creating a contradiction.[6]
Victor explained that the Primordial authorities functioned as essential components of the mechanisms maintaining Reality. Their Concepts were not simply powerful abilities possessed by individual Gods, but fundamental principles necessary for the ordinary operation of the Universe. Overriding or removing such a principle would require authority capable of interfering with the underlying structure supporting it, rather than merely defeating a conventional Divine opponent through physical strength. He specifically identified the level of power required as something comparable to his own authority or to the power Isabelle could eventually possess after further development.[6]
The statement represented one of the most significant assessments of Isabelle's future potential. Her Concept concerns Energy itself, making it possible for her eventual development to reach the fundamental mechanisms upon which ordinary physical and supernatural processes depend. However, Victor's prediction did not establish that Isabelle already possessed the power to eliminate Death, erase a Primordial Regulator or rewrite the entire structure of Creation. Her present abilities remained subject to the difference between conceptual potential and developed mastery, a distinction reinforced by her continuing difficulties during martial training.[6]
Isabelle also displayed considerable concern for Jeanne and the Universal Tree during the explanation. When Victor compared the Primordial forces to components of a machine, she remarked that her mother and uncle would dislike being described in such a manner. Although she understood the comparison, she objected to its impersonal implications and promised not to repeat the wording. Victor responded by affectionately picking her up and pampering her, prompting Isabelle to react with the delighted affection of a daughter despite the extraordinarily serious subject they had been discussing.[6]
The exceptional circumstances surrounding Isabelle's birth later became a subject of discussion among Victor's wives. As the family considered the possible characteristics of another daughter born from a powerful conceptual Goddess, Natasha suggested that the child should possess an extravagant inheritance comparable to Isabelle or Kassandra. Agnes recognized that only a small number of daughters had been born with such unusual Divine foundations, while Jeanne explained that the entire second generation possessed Eldritch and Primordial characteristics to varying degrees. Isabelle remained one of the most extreme examples because her authority concerned one of the foundational principles of existence.[3]
Her natural advantages became particularly apparent through comparison with [[Adam Tepes]], Jeanne's son from her previous marriage to [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]]. Adam possessed enormous Soul potential and had undergone considerable supernatural development, eventually advancing from a Lesser Dragon into a more powerful Draconic state. Nevertheless, his practical growth remained far below the expectations associated with his lineage, particularly when compared with a younger sister who had possessed Primal Energy naturally from birth. Scathach considered the comparison inherently unfair because Isabelle's starting point was extraordinary even among Victor's daughters, while Jeanne became increasingly frustrated that Adam had not realized more of his own potential.[3]
Jeanne eventually concluded that Adam required considerably more demanding training and should become strong enough to protect Isabelle. Her expectation did not mean that Isabelle was helpless or possessed lesser power than her brother; rather, it reflected Jeanne's belief that Adam should develop into someone capable of fulfilling the protective responsibilities associated with an older sibling. His lack of progress was especially troubling because his enormous inherited Soul and subsequent Draconic development provided a foundation that should have allowed substantially greater accomplishments than he had achieved.[3]
Isabelle's relationship with the Universal Tree subsequently acquired a more humorous significance. During a private conversation, Victor discovered that the primordial incarnation of Life understood Japanese anime terminology, including expressions associated with common character archetypes. The Universal Tree's knowledge was sufficiently unusual that Victor immediately suspected Isabelle had introduced him to the subject, recognizing that the ancient cosmic entity would have little reason to seek out those terms independently. The Universal Tree refused to answer directly, effectively confirming the suspicion through his unwillingness to deny it.[8]
The incident demonstrated that Isabelle's relationship with her maternal uncle involved considerably more than formal reverence toward a Primordial authority. She interacted with him as a family member and was comfortable enough to introduce him to ordinary entertainment associated with Victor's former Human culture. The contrast between the Universal Tree's cosmic responsibilities and his familiarity with anime further emphasized the unusual domestic life maintained within the Elderblood family, where entities possessing universal significance could still participate in comparatively ordinary familial activities.[8]
Isabelle's extraordinary Energy affinity later became relevant during discussions concerning Victor's continued support of the Empire's massive temporal undertaking. The ritual required enormous quantities of Energy and exceptional control over the processes sustaining a Galactic-scale civilization, forcing Victor and several of his wives to devote substantial portions of their power toward maintaining the accelerated flow of Time. Albedo questioned why Victor refused to allow daughters such as Isabelle or Melida to assist, observing that their extraordinary supernatural abilities made them capable of contributing significantly to the process.[11]
Violet explained that Victor considered the maintenance of the ritual a responsibility belonging primarily to the adults. Although the daughters were already extraordinarily powerful and some had accumulated immense subjective experience, he wished to preserve their opportunity to develop independently rather than compel them to shoulder dangerous obligations merely because their inherited abilities made such assistance possible. He would consider involving them if absolutely necessary, but preferred searching for alternatives that allowed the older generation to maintain responsibility for the undertaking. Isabelle's inclusion among the daughters capable of assisting therefore established another assessment of her Energy-related potential without indicating that she had personally become one of the principal sources sustaining the ritual.[11]
Victor's subsequent educational lessons provided a more detailed explanation of the relationship between Attributeless Energy, Primal Energy and the fundamental construction of Creation. He explained that the original Creator employed Attributeless Energy as the basis from which existence emerged, while Primal Energy possessed characteristics associated with making the resulting structures function. The distinction was especially important for individuals whose supernatural understanding remained limited to particular forms of Energy, since beings capable of directly governing Energy at a more fundamental level could perceive the underlying relationship between these manifestations.[12]
The discussion also reinforced the extraordinary breadth of Isabelle's Concept. She had been born possessing Primal Energy and later developed broad authority over Energy itself, giving her a foundation relevant to the distinctions Victor was explaining. However, the lesson did not separately establish that Isabelle had already demonstrated complete personal control over every manifestation of Attributeless Energy or that she possessed every ability associated with Victor's more advanced understanding. Her potential remained extraordinary, but the complete development of her individual Energy authority had not yet been shown.[12]
Isabelle became the subject of another question when Stella asked Victor about the origin of the [[Akashic Records]] and the source nourishing Divine Concepts. Stella used Isabelle's Divinity as an example, questioning what supplied the fundamental Energy from which such an authority operated. Victor recognized the difficulty of the question and acknowledged that he did not possess complete knowledge concerning every aspect of Creation's original structure. The discussion demonstrated the importance of Isabelle's Concept as a subject of cosmological inquiry, since understanding Energy itself required examining principles reaching beyond the ordinary functioning of individual Gods and their Divinities.[13]
Throughout these events, Isabelle remained one of the most naturally exceptional daughters of the Elderblood family. Her authority over Energy, maternal affinity with Life, inherited Truth perception and advanced Draconic physiology provided an extraordinary foundation, while her personal development continued depending upon training, understanding and experience. Her existence demonstrated that possessing a Concept capable of affecting primordial cosmic functions did not eliminate the practical difficulties associated with learning how to employ such power responsibly.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Isabelle's biological father, the Progenitor of her Blood Dragon lineage and the principal authority responsible for regulating her supernatural development. Her extraordinary Divine Concept immediately attracts his attention because Energy is fundamental to the operation of both physical and supernatural Reality. Recognizing that a child capable of interfering with Creation's Energy supply could cause catastrophic consequences without intending to harm anyone, Victor restricts the most dangerous portions of her powers and ensures that her abilities develop alongside her maturity and practical control.[1]
Isabelle possesses extraordinary affection for Victor and is particularly concerned about disappointing him. During her early appearance, she reminds her sisters that their father would be unhappy if they ignored his instructions, using the family's mental connection to restore order among the daughters without resorting to intimidation. Her behavior demonstrates that she understands the importance of Victor's approval and considers his feelings more significant than the excitement of the surrounding gathering.[7]
Their relationship becomes increasingly educational as Isabelle matures. Victor explains the underlying Rules of Reality, the differences between dimensions and independent cosmic structures, the importance of Divine Concepts and the mechanisms governing Primordial existence. Isabelle demonstrates substantial understanding of these subjects and receives direct explanations concerning the Energy cycle and the consequences of interfering with fundamental principles such as Death. Victor eventually predicts that her power could reach a level relevant to the cosmic mechanisms he describes, reflecting his recognition of the exceptional potential possessed by her Concept.[6]
Victor nevertheless refuses to treat Isabelle primarily as a source of Energy for the Empire. Although Albedo recognizes that daughters such as Isabelle and Melida possess enough power to assist with the enormous temporal undertaking, Victor deliberately keeps the principal burden upon the older generation. He wishes his daughters to enjoy their lives and develop without being required to undertake dangerous responsibilities simply because their inherited abilities make them unusually useful. His protective attitude consequently shapes both Isabelle's personal upbringing and the limitations imposed upon her participation in the Empire's greatest projects.[11]
Despite the extraordinary scale of their supernatural relationship, Isabelle remains comfortable expressing ordinary affection toward Victor. When he praises her understanding during the lesson concerning Primordial Beings, he picks her up and pampers her, causing the adult Dragon Goddess to respond with open delight. Their interactions demonstrate that her formidable status and future cosmic potential do not diminish the personal importance of their father-daughter relationship.[6]

During the discussion concerning the unusual powers inherited by Victor's children, Isabelle is repeatedly mentioned as an extraordinary anomaly because she possessed Primal Energy naturally from birth. Isabelle is protective of Jeanne and considers her mother's feelings when Victor discusses the primordial mechanisms governing Creation. ### Divine Concept — Energy
Isabelle's defining supernatural authority is the Divine Concept of <Energy> at the Highest Level. Unlike a conventional Energy-related ability restricted to a particular manifestation, attribute or supernatural Race, her Concept is associated with Energy itself and the fundamental processes through which Energy supports physical and supernatural existence. This distinction grants Isabelle an exceptionally broad conceptual foundation, making her potentially capable of interacting with systems ordinarily governed by higher cosmic authorities. Nevertheless, possession of the Concept does not automatically establish complete mastery over every manifestation of Energy, unlimited current reserves or the ability to disregard all other Divine Concepts without resistance.[1][2]
Energy exists in numerous forms throughout the novel's Cosmology, including racial supernatural reserves, elemental Energy, Divine power, [[Draconic Energy]], Primal Energy and Attributeless Energy. Different [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] and civilizations develop methods of employing these Energies according to their biological foundations and understanding of supernatural principles. Isabelle's authority is exceptional because it concerns the broader principle shared by these manifestations rather than a single specialized form, although the exact mechanics through which she can manipulate each type have not been demonstrated independently.[2][12]

#### Primal Energy
Isabelle possessed Primal Energy from birth, an extraordinary characteristic explicitly recognized by [[Scathach]] during the later discussions concerning Jeanne's children. Most supernatural beings develop by gradually refining the Energy available through their racial or Divine characteristics, while Isabelle began life possessing a form of Energy associated with the foundational operation of Creation. This exceptional starting point contributed to her classification as an anomaly even among the naturally born True Dragon daughters of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[3]
Her mature Divine Concept is also directly associated with the manipulation of Primal Energy. Although the available material does not describe every specialized technique she has developed, Isabelle's authority over Energy itself provides a fundamentally different relationship with Primal Energy from that possessed by conventional Gods who merely employ it as a resource. Her power is conceptual rather than exclusively quantitative, allowing the potential for interaction with the principles governing Energy distribution and operation rather than merely increasing the amount of Energy discharged during an attack.[2]
Victor later clarifies the relationship between Primal Energy and Attributeless Energy during his lessons concerning the creation of the Universe. Attributeless Energy represents the fundamental Energy employed by the Creator to bring existence into being, while Primal Energy is associated with the operation of the structures produced through Creation. Although the distinction is important for individuals whose powers remain limited to particular energetic manifestations, Victor explains that beings capable of governing Energy at a more fundamental level can perceive a deeper relationship between the two.[12]
Isabelle's Concept makes these distinctions particularly relevant to her future development, but the explanation should not be treated as proof that she has independently demonstrated complete mastery over every application of Attributeless Energy. Her confirmed personal attributes include Primal Energy from birth and broad authority over Energy itself, while the full extent of her practical control remains incompletely described.

#### Creation's Energy Supply
One of the most extraordinary possibilities associated with Isabelle's Divinity concerns interference with the Energy distributed by Creation throughout the Sectors. The Universe depends upon fundamental energetic processes that allow matter, stars, living organisms and supernatural systems to continue functioning. Because Isabelle's Concept concerns Energy itself, her authority possesses the potential to interfere with this distribution rather than remaining restricted to the Energy contained within an individual opponent or localized supernatural technique.[1]
If Isabelle prevented a chosen Sector from receiving its necessary Energy, the region would eventually undergo catastrophic systemic collapse. Celestial structures and living beings would no longer receive the resources required for their continued operation, while the mechanisms supporting the Sector would gradually fail. The ultimate result would be the deterioration of the region into cosmic dust, demonstrating the extraordinary danger associated with conceptual interference even when the user does not directly generate an attack possessing the destructive force necessary to eliminate every structure individually.[1]
This possibility must be distinguished from an accomplished combat feat. Isabelle has not been shown personally disconnecting a Sector from Creation's Energy supply, nor has she demonstrated an unrestricted ability to erase every form of Energy throughout the Cosmos simultaneously. The described mechanism establishes the potential scope of her Concept and the danger [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] recognized during her childhood, while her actual ability to perform such an operation remains dependent upon further development and mastery.

### Power Restrictions
Isabelle's childhood powers were considered dangerous enough that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] refused to leave their full authority unrestricted. The fundamental nature of her Energy Concept made accidental misuse particularly serious because the consequences could extend beyond ordinary physical damage and affect the energetic processes sustaining entire regions of Creation. Victor consequently limited the most dangerous portions of the authority available to her during childhood, intending for the restrictions to protect both Isabelle and the surrounding civilization until she possessed sufficient understanding to employ her abilities responsibly.[1]
The restrictions were not designed to prevent Isabelle from developing or permanently remove her exceptional inheritance. Rather, they formed part of Victor's broader approach to raising children who were born with powers normally associated with extraordinarily advanced Divine beings. He recognized that a young Dragon could possess the intelligence necessary to understand complicated supernatural principles while still lacking the emotional experience required to anticipate every consequence of using them. Isabelle's ongoing education and martial training therefore remained essential regardless of the breadth of her natural authority.

### Development
Isabelle's supernatural development differs substantially from that of ordinary Gods because her principal difficulty has never been obtaining a sufficient amount of natural power. She was born possessing one of the most fundamental Divine Concepts available to a supernatural being and inherited Primal Energy before beginning conventional training. Her early development was therefore shaped primarily by the need to regulate an overwhelming inheritance rather than by the struggle to acquire basic supernatural abilities.[1][3]
Her initial restrictions protected the family and the surrounding civilization from the consequences of uncontrolled Energy authority while allowing Isabelle to continue growing according to the extraordinary biological and spiritual characteristics of a True Dragon. The extensive safeguards established by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] further reduced the danger of external interference, ensuring that her development occurred within an environment capable of resisting attacks that would threaten ordinary Divine beings. Nevertheless, these protections did not eliminate the need for education, since external safety and innate power could not automatically provide sound judgment or practical competence.[1][5]
Isabelle's later adulthood revealed the principal challenge associated with her unusual inheritance. She possessed mature authority over Primal Energy and an exceptionally broad relationship with Energy itself, but she lacked the obsessive dedication to martial improvement displayed by Gina. Their recurring sparring matches demonstrated that practical experience, control and technique remained essential even for individuals possessing powers associated with fundamental aspects of Creation. Isabelle's willingness to accept challenges despite recognizing Gina's provocations suggested that her competitive instincts could become an important motivation for further development.[2]
Her intellectual understanding continued expanding through Victor's educational lessons. Isabelle learned to distinguish the conceptual weight of different dimensional structures, recognize the relationship between fundamental Divine Concepts and understand the consequences of removing essential principles from the Universe. Her explanation of Death's role in the Energy cycle demonstrated that she had begun thinking about Energy as a foundational process rather than merely a resource employed during combat. Victor's prediction concerning her eventual ability to interfere with primordial mechanisms further emphasized the extraordinary developmental direction available through her Concept.[6]
The later explanation of Attributeless Energy and Primal Energy provided additional context for her future development. Isabelle's authority concerns Energy itself, making the relationship between the Creator's foundational Energy and the processes operating within Creation particularly relevant to her powers. However, the novel has not yet demonstrated the complete extent of her mastery over those processes, and her eventual ability to fulfill the predictions associated with her Divinity remains a matter of continued development rather than an accomplished transformation.[12]
Isabelle consequently represents an important example of Victor's philosophy toward his second generation. She possesses extraordinary supernatural potential, but her eventual achievements depend upon how effectively she develops the understanding, discipline and experience necessary to employ that potential. Her current existence combines conceptual authority reaching toward the primordial mechanisms of Creation with the personal characteristics of a daughter who remains affectionate, occasionally complacent and still capable of learning from the individuals surrounding her.
