Silvia Elderblood is a recurring character in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]], an Imperial Princess of the [[Elderblood Empire]], and the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]]. She belongs to Victor's naturally born second generation of [[Blood Dragons]], possessing the supernatural foundation of a True Dragon and Dragon Goddess from birth while simultaneously inheriting the hereditary Lightning characteristics of the [[Fulger Clan]].[1][4]
Silvia represents the continuation of one of [[Nightingale]]'s oldest Noble Vampire bloodlines through a fundamentally different racial existence. Unlike Sasha, who was originally born a Noble Vampire and subsequently evolved into a Dragon, Silvia was born directly as a Blood Dragon, inheriting the immense physical and supernatural potential of Victor's lineage alongside the elemental affinity of her mother. Lightning constitutes her principal inherited elemental characteristic, and her talent for manipulating its subordinate attributes distinguishes her among the younger members of the Fulger family. Her later participation in supernatural experimentation further establishes that she can contribute Draconic Lightning as a distinct elemental influence.[5]
Silvia is particularly associated with [[Valentina Victoria Elderblood]] and [[Rosemary Lavina Elderblood]], the respective daughters of Violet and Ruby. The three girls maintain an exceptionally close relationship resembling the bond between Victor's original wives. They frequently accompany one another, communicate through telepathy and display a shared fascination with their father. Their tendency toward mischief, bluntness and unpredictable behavior eventually earns them the collective description "the trio of chaos".[4][6][7]
Despite her troublesome behavior, Silvia possesses an observant, intelligent and surprisingly pragmatic personality. She demonstrates an understanding of the strategic advantages of preserving valuable people, knowledge and infrastructure rather than relying exclusively upon destruction. During Victor's negotiations with the Witch Queen, she recognizes that conquering a weaker civilization can be considerably more beneficial than annihilating it and specifically identifies the usefulness of incorporating competent specialists into the Empire's technological and magical institutions.[4]
Silvia also possesses considerable confidence in her inherited power and the position of her family, although she recognizes the difference between raw strength and practical combat experience. Her admiration for Victor coexists with genuine respect for her older sisters, particularly [[Nero]] and Ophis, whose extensive training and personal histories give them advantages that younger Blood Dragons cannot obtain merely through exceptional talent.[6]
By the later Imperial era, Silvia remains closely involved with her sisters and the enormous household established by Victor. She participates in family disputes, gatherings of the Imperial Princesses and increasingly unusual experiments involving the combination of supernatural abilities. Her personality retains the playfulness and irreverence of her childhood, even as the Empire's accelerated development grants its younger generation extraordinary chronological ages and opportunities to pursue their own interests.[7][8][5]

### Physical Appearance
Silvia possesses a beautiful, youthful appearance strongly resembling her biological mother, [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]]. During her introduction, she appears as a small girl approximately 120 centimeters tall, with pale skin, golden-blonde hair, deep violet eyes and a pair of golden Draconic horns. Her features are immediately recognizable as those of Sasha's daughter, while her violet eyes reveal the distinctive characteristics shared by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s biological descendants.[1][4]
When Silvia accompanies Victor alongside Valentina and Rosemary, the three girls display appearances strongly reflecting their respective mothers. Valentina possesses white hair and violet horns like Violet, Rosemary has vivid red hair reminiscent of Ruby, and Silvia is distinguished by her golden hair and horns. The resemblance is sufficiently pronounced that each girl can be associated with her maternal lineage almost immediately.[4]
Her childlike Humanoid appearance conceals the extraordinary physiology of a genuine True Dragon. Despite her small body, Silvia retains the immense physical mass associated with her Draconic nature. During the meeting with [[Evie Moriarty Elderblood]], Victor transforms the sofa provided for his daughters into a much stronger structure composed of Divine material before allowing them to sit. Even after this reinforcement, the furniture audibly creaks beneath the combined weight of Silvia, Valentina and Rosemary. Their weight contrasts sharply with Victor's own precise physical control, as he sits without producing a similar reaction despite possessing a vastly more powerful body.[4]
Silvia and her sisters also casually levitate through Evie's residence rather than walking. Their movements, supernatural eyes and Draconic horns contribute to the unsettling impression experienced by the Witch Queen, who initially struggles to understand the true nature of the three apparently innocent children.[4]
Her youthful body is not an accurate indication of her chronological age. Naturally born [[True Dragons]] possess extraordinarily prolonged developmental periods, allowing individuals to accumulate hundreds or thousands of years of experience while maintaining appearances that Humans would associate with childhood. Silvia belonged to a generation born during approximately two thousand years of accelerated development, and her biological maturation followed the exceptional standards of Victor's Blood Dragon lineage.[1]
As a member of that lineage, Silvia possesses the Draconic foundation associated with transformation between Humanoid and Dragon characteristics. The exact physical appearance, proportions, scale coloration and dimensions of her complete Dragon Form have not been individually established in her documented appearances. Her golden horns and inherited Lightning affinity are confirmed features of her Humanoid state, but they should not be used to invent additional anatomical characteristics for an otherwise undescribed transformation.
Silvia's resemblance to Sasha remains evident even during the later Imperial era. When a group of Fulger-descended Imperial Princesses gathers around Valentina and the other daughters, Silvia is recognizable through her blonde hair, violet eyes and strong physical similarity to her mother. Other daughters of the Fulger family share the general coloration but differ in how strongly their facial features resemble Victor or their respective mothers.[8]

### Personality
Silvia is playful, outspoken, intelligent, practical, highly confident and deeply devoted to her family. Her personality combines the pride and possessiveness characteristic of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s children with a more pragmatic disposition that distinguishes her from sisters who instinctively favor spectacular displays of destructive power. Although she frequently engages in teasing, arguments and mischief, she demonstrates a genuine understanding of the importance of preserving useful resources and avoiding unnecessary waste.[4]
Her affection for Victor is especially pronounced. Silvia repeatedly regards him with admiration and takes great pleasure in witnessing his displays of power, confidence and authority. During his meeting with Evie, she becomes visibly excited when his demeanor shifts from casual politeness to the commanding attitude of an Emperor. Rather than merely admiring his strength, she enjoys observing the way he controls conversations, exposes deception and forces powerful individuals to acknowledge his superiority.[4]
Silvia's devotion is accompanied by intense familial possessiveness. When Rosemary wonders whether Evie might eventually become another of Victor's wives, Silvia and Valentina immediately react with displeasure. Both are reluctant to accept the possibility of another woman entering the household, particularly because they are already surrounded by an enormous number of mothers and other women devoted to Victor. Nevertheless, Silvia is capable of setting aside her immediate emotional reaction when evaluating what another individual could contribute to the family.[4]
She also possesses a strong awareness of Victor's perception and parental authority. When Valentina makes an inappropriate joke during their telepathic conversation, Victor notices despite apparently concentrating upon Evie. Silvia immediately warns her sister to stop, recognizing that their father is likely to discipline them if they continue. Her reaction demonstrates that she understands the difference between the freedom enjoyed within their family and the limits imposed by Victor's expectations.[4]
Silvia is particularly practical when discussing military conquest and Imperial expansion. She has little doubt that Victor's faction possesses the power to overwhelm weaker supernatural societies, but she rejects the idea that overwhelming strength makes indiscriminate destruction desirable. When Rosemary suggests destroying Arcane through an orbital weapon, Silvia argues that conquering the territory and preserving its valuable inhabitants would be more efficient. She specifically considers Evie a competent individual who could contribute to the work of Velnorah, Ruby and Aline, revealing an appreciation for specialized knowledge and useful personnel.[4]
Her relationship with Valentina and Rosemary highlights another side of her personality. The three maintain an almost continuous familiarity with one another, discussing supernatural phenomena, family affairs and entertainment without needing to speak aloud. Silvia often contributes practical observations or notices problems overlooked by her companions. During their encounter with Evie, she recognizes Valentina's unintended thought leakage and warns her sister before the situation becomes more embarrassing.[4]
Despite their closeness, Silvia does not share all of her companions' interests to the same degree. She enjoys entertainment but is considerably less obsessed with anime than Rosemary, Valentina or especially Priscilla. Her personal preferences include cartoons, particularly SpongeBob, and reading books. She also watches anime and other media, but dislikes the expectation that everyone should become as devoted to the subject as her more enthusiastic sisters.[6]
This difference becomes apparent when Rosemary recognizes a fictional reference reproduced through Ophis' combat techniques. Silvia admits that she does not understand the reference, surprising Valentina and Rosemary, who recognize it immediately. Rather than becoming particularly interested in the explanation, she expresses frustration with the exaggerated enthusiasm surrounding anime and wonders why her sisters cannot simply enjoy cartoons. Her reaction reveals that the immense intellectual capabilities of Victor's daughters do not eliminate ordinary differences in taste and personality.[6]
Silvia possesses a realistic understanding of combat despite not being individually depicted engaging in the same extensive battles as [[Nero]] and Ophis. While observing their training, she recognizes that only the most talented younger daughters could reasonably hope to compete against them. Valentina further emphasizes that even exceptional talent might be insufficient because the two older sisters possess immense practical experience. Silvia accepts the significance of their abilities rather than assuming her own superior birthright makes experience irrelevant.[6]
Her confidence extends to her interactions with senior members of the Imperial Family. Silvia is willing to challenge [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] directly when she believes the Empress has made an unreasonable decision. During a disagreement over Violet's handling of new additions to Victor's family, Silvia rejects her justification that she has been managing such matters since before the daughters were born. She bluntly interprets the argument as proof that Violet is old and immediately protests when Violet attempts to dismiss her as too young to interfere.[7]
Although the exchange is comedic, it demonstrates that Silvia does not regard authority as protection from criticism. She respects Victor's position and the importance of the Imperial Family, but considers herself sufficiently mature to express disagreement when family decisions affect her and her sisters. The same confidence characterizes her interactions with Sylvia, her similarly named sister, whom she treats with the casual irreverence common throughout the household.[7][9]
Silvia remains capable of participating in increasingly elaborate mischief as the family develops. Her involvement in experiments with Eldritch creatures demonstrates curiosity, enthusiasm and a willingness to combine her own powers with those of her sisters even when the potential consequences are difficult to predict. When the group discusses enhancing experimental creatures with additional elemental characteristics, Silvia proposes Draconic Lightning and participates in the collective decision to contribute their abilities.[5]
Despite their troublesome behavior, Silvia and her sisters possess a strong sense of family solidarity. Their arguments generally occur within a household whose members understand that affection and loyalty remain more important than temporary disagreements. Silvia's confidence in her father, closeness with her sisters and desire to preserve the family's interests remain central to her characterization throughout her appearances.

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Silvia was born during the extraordinary period of accelerated Time experienced by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his family following his ascension as the Dragon God of [[Chaos]]. Although approximately two years passed in the outside world, Victor and many of his wives experienced roughly two thousand years within his personal Reality. Their development during this period included greater mastery of their Divine powers, continued refinement of their Draconic bodies and the births of numerous biological daughters whose supernatural foundations far surpassed those of ordinary newborn beings.[1]
As the daughter of Victor Elderblood and [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]], Silvia inherited two particularly significant supernatural lineages. Victor provided the foundation of a naturally born Blood Dragon, while Sasha contributed the [[Fulger Clan]]'s hereditary affinity for Lightning. The resulting combination produced a Dragon Goddess whose elemental potential was supported by a superior Draconic body rather than the conventional Noble Vampire physiology through which Fulger Lightning had historically manifested.[1]
Silvia belonged to the same generation as [[Valentina Victoria Elderblood]] and [[Rosemary Lavina Elderblood]], whose respective mothers were Violet and Ruby. The close relationship between the three developed into a nearly inseparable companionship, reflecting the longstanding bond between Victor's original three wives. Their shared upbringing also provided them with access to the extensive education, training facilities and supernatural resources of Victor's growing civilization.
Silvia's first substantial appearance occurs when Victor visits [[Evie Moriarty Elderblood]], the Queen of the Witches. He arrives accompanied by Valentina, Silvia and Rosemary, three small Dragon Goddesses whose appearance initially conceals their extraordinary nature. Although their father is the principal participant in the meeting, the daughters immediately begin observing Evie and discussing their surroundings through a private telepathic conversation.[4]
Evie finds their behavior unsettling from the beginning. The girls remain almost completely silent, watching her with violet eyes and small smiles while floating through the residence instead of walking. Their attention is especially noticeable when Evie uses [[Magic]] to close a door. Silvia and her sisters immediately perceive the unfamiliar Energy, briefly reacting before returning to their outwardly innocent expressions. Evie suspects that something unusual is occurring between the girls, but cannot determine the contents of their conversation.[4]
Victor subsequently reinforces the sofa prepared for them by transforming it into a structure composed of stronger Divine material. The reason becomes apparent when the girls sit and the furniture creaks beneath their immense Draconic weight. Evie recognizes that their Humanoid appearances do not eliminate the mass associated with their genuine Dragon bodies, further confirming that the apparently small children possess supernatural characteristics vastly beyond ordinary expectations.[4]
Victor then introduces his daughters individually. Valentina is identified as his daughter with Violet, while Silvia is introduced as his daughter with Sasha. The golden-haired girl smiles when Victor affectionately caresses her head, displaying the same happiness as her sisters. Rosemary is introduced afterward as Ruby's daughter, and all three greet Evie together before resuming their private mental conversation.[4]
Silvia begins examining the unusual Energy surrounding the Witch Queen and asks Rosemary whether she noticed it. Rosemary confirms that the Energy appears connected to Evie but originates through a different supernatural process from those normally employed by their family. Valentina identifies it as Magic and explains that it remains useful despite being comparatively inferior to the extraordinary Energies available to the Dragon Goddesses. Silvia recognizes the system as the one used by [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]], recalling that Albedo previously granted Victor access to Magic.[4]
Their discussion rapidly shifts toward Evie's potential relationship with Victor. Rosemary wonders whether the Witch Queen could eventually become another of their mothers, immediately provoking possessive reactions from Silvia and Valentina. Valentina considers the possibility by examining Evie's abilities and the women already closest to Victor, while Silvia shares her reluctance toward the possibility. When Valentina's increasingly intense thoughts begin leaking through their connection, Silvia notices and alerts her sister.[4]
Evie gradually becomes aware that the three girls are genuine Goddesses. Rosemary responds with pride in the extraordinary Blood they inherited from Victor, while Silvia enthusiastically declares through the telepathic connection that they are Super Dragon Goddesses. Victor confirms that they are Goddesses of Dragons, reinforcing the overwhelming difference between these naturally born daughters and the ordinary supernatural children Evie might have expected.[4]
Victor continues spoiling the girls even while conducting important negotiations. He creates three glasses of fruit juice, giving grape juice to Valentina, acerola juice to Rosemary and orange juice to Silvia. The girls enjoy their refreshments without understanding the extent of Evie's astonishment, as the cups are themselves Divine artifacts capable of continuously reproducing their contents. To the Witch Queen, the casual use of such extraordinary objects for ordinary drinks emphasizes the almost incomprehensible wealth and power surrounding Victor's family.[4]
As Victor questions Evie's explanation for seeking his assistance, Silvia becomes increasingly excited by his commanding demeanor. When he begins exposing the inconsistencies in the Witch Queen's statements, she urges Valentina to activate their recording equipment so they can preserve the scene. Valentina admits that she forgot the cameras, provoking Silvia's frustration, but Rosemary remembers that Albedo's Magic can convert memories into recordings. The solution satisfies the girls, who continue watching their father's demonstration with renewed enthusiasm.[4]
Their conversation also reveals Silvia's awareness of the political and military situation surrounding Arcane. When Evie raises the possibility that Victor's faction might be responsible for the illness affecting her daughter, Valentina rejects the suggestion because the Elderblood family has no reason to employ such indirect methods against a weaker opponent. Silvia agrees and argues that even relatively weak Dragonoids belonging to their faction could devastate Arcane without assistance from Victor or his wives.[4]
Rosemary then suggests that an orbital cannon could accomplish the same result without requiring anyone to enter the territory. Silvia immediately disagrees, explaining that simply destroying the kingdom would waste valuable resources. She favors conquering Arcane and preserving anything useful, particularly its population of skilled Witches and experienced specialists. She identifies Evie as an extraordinarily competent individual who could assist the work of Velnorah, Ruby and Aline, making her continued existence considerably more valuable than the destruction of her kingdom.[4]
Silvia's reasoning demonstrates that her immense confidence in the Elderblood faction does not eliminate an appreciation for efficiency. Although she believes the Empire can defeat Arcane militarily, she recognizes that conquest can provide personnel, knowledge and opportunities that destruction cannot recover. Her perspective complements Victor's own preference for incorporating valuable individuals and resources into his civilization when doing so serves his interests.
The negotiations eventually conclude with Evie accepting Victor's assistance in exchange for placing herself and Arcane beneath his authority. Victor cures her daughter, exposes the Divine conspirators responsible and transfers the Witch kingdom into Velnorah's territory. Throughout the encounter, Silvia and her sisters remain fascinated by his ability to accomplish extraordinary feats with casual ease.[4]
When Velnorah subsequently comments upon Victor's tendency to spoil his daughters, he affectionately strokes the heads of Silvia, Valentina and Rosemary and refers to them as his little treasures. The girls respond with delight, considering him the best father imaginable. Velnorah acknowledges that Victor's indulgence is balanced by the strict discipline imposed when necessary, although the mothers bear much of the responsibility for educating and controlling the younger generation.[4]
Silvia later appears alongside Valentina, Rosemary and Stella while observing [[Nero Elderblood]] and [[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]] training in one of the family's arenas. The two older sisters demonstrate extraordinary martial ability, exchanging attacks at speeds that ordinary observers would struggle to perceive. Their movements combine refined martial techniques with supernatural mobility and Reality manipulation, producing tremendous shockwaves that are safely contained within the specialized training environment.[6]
Silvia recognizes the enormous difference in experience between [[Nero]] and Ophis and the younger Dragon Goddesses. She observes that only the most talented daughters could potentially compete against them, while Valentina adds that even exceptional talent might be insufficient because of their superior combat experience. Their assessment reflects an understanding that natural power and inherited potential cannot automatically replace practical skill developed through extensive training.[6]
The atmosphere becomes more animated when Ophis performs an unusual combat maneuver resembling a technique from an anime. Rosemary immediately recognizes the reference and becomes excited, while Valentina also understands the inspiration. Silvia, however, admits that she does not recognize it, surprising her sisters. Stella shares Silvia's confusion, and the two become uncomfortable as Rosemary begins discussing the importance of watching particular anime and understanding the fictional techniques that inspire supernatural combat applications.[6]
Silvia expresses her preference for cartoons such as SpongeBob and reading books rather than following every anime recommended by her sisters. Stella similarly favors movies and physical training. Although neither girl dislikes anime, they are less devoted to it than Rosemary and Priscilla, whose enthusiasm occasionally overwhelms other family members. The conversation reveals the ordinary differences in entertainment preferences that persist despite the girls' extraordinary supernatural abilities.[6]
The training continues until Ophis surprises Nero with a combination of her supernatural mobility and Dragon Breath. The impact produces a destructive beam powerful enough to devastate an ordinary city, but the arena's protective barriers absorb the attack. Silvia and the other spectators witness how effectively their older sisters combine inherited abilities with refined combat techniques, further reinforcing their respect for experience and specialized training.[6]
After the sparring session, Nero approaches the girls and asks why they have been observing so closely. Silvia questions whether they require a particular reason to watch their older sisters train, while Valentina suggests that there is no harm in visiting them. Ophis nevertheless recognizes their intentions, realizing that the younger daughters have become interested in the administrative opportunities created within the Empire and wish to learn from sisters who already possess experience in those responsibilities.[6]
Valentina is surprised by Ophis' perception and privately reflects upon the advantages her older sisters possess through having accompanied Victor long before the birth of the second generation. Silvia participates in the gathering as the girls request information about the realities of Imperial work. Their interest reflects the growing ambitions of Victor's daughters, who increasingly wish to contribute directly to the civilization established by their family rather than remain solely protected members of the household.[6]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
As the [[Elderblood Empire]] entered its immense period of accelerated development, Silvia continued maturing alongside the younger generation. The temporal project established by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Natalia was intended to provide approximately two hundred thousand years of internal progress while only a comparatively short period passed outside the affected region. The Empire used this period to transform its technology, military, infrastructure and population, while the younger Imperial Family gained unprecedented opportunities for education and personal development.[2]
Silvia's close relationship with Valentina and Rosemary remained intact despite the passage of enormous amounts of subjective Time. The three continued appearing together and retained much of the irreverent behavior that had distinguished them during their childhood. Their personalities were particularly evident during a major family dispute involving [[Violet Elderblood Snow]], whose decisions concerning potential new members of Victor's household provoked the displeasure of both his wives and daughters.[7]
The disagreement began when members of the family discovered that Violet had acted without properly consulting everyone affected by the decision. The possessive instincts shared by Victor's wives and daughters caused numerous members of the household to converge upon the Empress. Valentina openly confronted her biological mother, arguing that she could not simply introduce additional women without first communicating with the rest of the family.[7]
Silvia joined the confrontation and immediately rejected Violet's attempt to justify her actions through her seniority. Violet argued that she had managed Victor's affairs since before most of the daughters were born and therefore understood such matters better than the younger generation. Silvia responded that this merely meant Violet was old and did not give her the right to make decisions without consulting everyone else.[7]
Violet became increasingly irritated and attempted to dismiss Silvia as too young to interfere. Silvia refused the characterization and insisted that she was no longer young, although her naturally youthful Draconic body made the argument particularly frustrating. Rosemary also complained about the slow physical maturation of their race, demonstrating that the enormous chronological ages of the daughters had not necessarily produced the adult physiques they desired.[7]
The confrontation eventually involved approximately forty daughters, with Valentina, Silvia and Rosemary behaving much as they had throughout their earlier appearances. Their close association was sufficiently established that Rosemary's presence beside Silvia and Valentina was regarded as almost inevitable, and the three were explicitly recognized as a trio of chaos.[7]
The argument expanded into a chaotic but ultimately comedic rebellion against Violet's decision. Valentina proposed removing Violet as Empress and replacing her with Anna, attracting enthusiastic support from the surrounding daughters. Silvia remained among those opposing Violet's unilateral behavior, demonstrating that she was willing to criticize even one of the family's highest authorities when she believed the circumstances justified it.[7]
Silvia continued appearing alongside the wider Imperial Family during the later gatherings of Victor's daughters. Following an incident involving Priscilla, Rosemary, Sylvia and their companions, several daughters assembled to discuss the apparent abduction of [[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]]. Valentina initially dismissed the disturbance as ordinary mischief, but the arrival of additional sisters revealed that the incident had attracted considerable attention throughout the household.[8]
Silvia arrived among a group of blonde-haired Imperial Princesses connected to the Fulger family. Her companions included Ekaterina Natasha Elderblood, [[Julia Elderblood]], [[Svetlana Veronika Elderblood]] and [[Alisa Sasha Elderblood]], whose appearances reflected different combinations of Victor's features and those of their respective mothers. Silvia and Julia particularly resembled the women from whom they inherited their maternal characteristics, while Alisa and Svetlana displayed more prominent features associated with Victor.[8]
The gathering eventually expanded to include more than thirty-five daughters, among them members of several branches of the Imperial Family. Their discussions were interrupted by the arrival of older sisters such as [[Nero]], Ophis, [[Metis]], Akasha and Melida, whose complicated relationships with Victor remained a source of occasional jealousy among the younger generation. Despite these tensions, the sisters continued treating one another as members of the same enormous family.[8]
The distinction between Silvia and her similarly named sister, [[Sylvia Elderblood]], became particularly apparent during the subsequent family gathering. Sylvia, who was born from Victor and Siena, had participated in the incident surrounding Velina and insisted that the girls would never deliberately harm their sister. Silvia responded with a snort, while their nearly identical names and similar manners were explicitly recognized as a source of confusion. The two were nevertheless separate individuals belonging to different maternal branches of the Elderblood family.[9]
Victor subsequently reassured Velina that he understood the incident had been playful rather than malicious, observing that she possessed enough power to escape whenever she wished. He also expressed affection toward all of his daughters, immediately diminishing their concern over the disagreement and provoking the familiar jealousy of several mothers who regarded Victor's indulgence as excessive.[9]
Silvia remained among the many daughters participating in the family's communal gatherings. During an extraordinary banquet prepared by Victor and Kaguya, the Imperial Family encountered furniture and decorations fashioned from cosmic materials, including celestial structures transformed into objects suitable for their celebration. Kaguya served the assembled family with such extraordinary precision that Silvia and Stella barely noticed their meals appearing before them. Silvia expressed surprise alongside her sister, further demonstrating the familiarity of the younger generation with supernatural spectacles that would be inconceivable to ordinary civilizations.[10]
Silvia's later activities extended into the unusual experimentation undertaken by several of her sisters. Yog and Nigredo began developing hybrid Eldritch creatures by combining the characteristics of Leviathans and other supernatural beings. Their experiments produced creatures with extraordinary adaptations, including the ability to survive in hostile cosmic environments and travel through Space. The project attracted additional participants, including Valentina, Leone, Hinata, Rosemary, Stella and Silvia.[5]
The sisters gathered around the developing creatures and discussed methods of further enhancing their capabilities. Their experimental materials included powerful Draconic scales belonging to Victor, whose supernatural properties made them extraordinarily valuable. Velina immediately recognized the potential danger of combining such powerful materials with the influences of Eldritch entities and Leviathans, but the other girls regarded the possibilities with considerable enthusiasm.[5]
As the group considered additional elemental characteristics, Valentina proposed introducing Dragon Fire. Silvia immediately suggested Dragon Lightning, while Rosemary requested Ice, Hinata offered Solar power and Stella proposed contributing the power of Truth. The sisters eventually agreed to combine portions of their respective supernatural abilities, with Velina adding intelligence so that the resulting creatures would possess more than overwhelming instinctive power.[5]
The experiment demonstrated that Silvia could participate directly in the manipulation and contribution of Draconic Lightning, providing a more specific example of her inherited elemental power than the general description of Fulger ancestry. It also revealed her willingness to cooperate with sisters possessing entirely different supernatural characteristics, including Eldritch, Solar and Reality-altering powers.[5]
Their activities eventually attracted the attention of [[Aphrodite]], who reacted with immediate alarm upon discovering what the daughters were attempting. The sisters fled with their materials rather than remain to face her anger, while Victor observed the situation with amusement. His reaction reflected the extraordinary normality of such incidents within a family whose children possessed abilities capable of producing entirely new supernatural creatures through collaborative experimentation.[5]
Silvia's involvement in these events demonstrates that her relationship with Lightning continued developing beyond simple racial inheritance. Although she was not individually shown engaging in a major battle during the available appearances, she remained an active member of the Imperial Family, continued participating in its collective activities and possessed the capacity to contribute her Draconic elemental power to supernatural creations of considerable complexity.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Silvia's biological father and the individual she admires most strongly. Her attachment to him is characterized by extraordinary affection, loyalty and pride in being his daughter. She enjoys receiving his attention and becomes particularly enthusiastic whenever he demonstrates the commanding personality or overwhelming power that distinguishes him from other supernatural rulers.[4]
During her introduction to Evie, Silvia immediately smiles when Victor affectionately caresses her head. Her devotion becomes even more apparent when he begins exposing the Witch Queen's deception, causing Silvia to demand that Valentina activate their recording equipment. She wishes to preserve such moments because she admires both his strength and the confidence with which he handles individuals who attempt to manipulate him.[4]
Silvia also respects Victor's authority as her father. She understands that his exceptional perception makes it difficult to conceal inappropriate behavior and warns Valentina when their telepathic conversation risks attracting his disapproval. Despite Victor's tendency to indulge his daughters, Silvia recognizes that he can become strict when necessary and that his expectations remain more important than the immediate desires of the younger generation.[4]
Her pride in Victor's existence influences her understanding of the wider supernatural world. Silvia regards the military strength of his faction as overwhelming compared with weaker civilizations and has little doubt that even relatively low-ranking Dragonoids could devastate Arcane. Nevertheless, she also demonstrates the practical judgment encouraged within his family, recognizing that conquering useful personnel and resources can provide greater benefits than simply destroying an opponent.[4]
As the Empire develops, Silvia becomes increasingly interested in the responsibilities carried by its older members. She joins her sisters in observing [[Nero]] and Ophis, whose experience in combat and Imperial affairs provides examples of the competence younger daughters must develop if they wish to contribute meaningfully to Victor's civilization.[6]
Victor's extraordinary affection remains a central feature of their relationship. He regards Silvia and her sisters as precious members of his family rather than merely powerful descendants or future military assets, and his presence frequently causes the younger generation to abandon their arguments in favor of competing for his attention.[4][9]

#### Sasha Elderblood Fulger
[[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] is Silvia's biological mother and the source of her hereditary Lightning affinity. Silvia strongly resembles Sasha in appearance, possessing the golden-blonde coloration and general facial characteristics associated with the Fulger family. Her supernatural heritage also reflects her mother's elemental nature, although Silvia was born directly as a Blood Dragon rather than undergoing the lengthy evolutionary process that transformed Sasha from a Noble Vampire into a True Dragon Goddess.[1][4]
The relationship between mother and daughter is particularly significant because the [[Fulger Clan]]'s Lightning power originally developed through its Noble Vampire Bloodline and the ancestral characteristics of its founding members. Through Silvia, that heritage continues within a naturally born True Dragon capable of combining elemental power with [[Draconic Energy]] and the supernatural potential inherited from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
Silvia's talent for manipulating Lightning into different subordinate expressions distinguishes her within the younger Fulger lineage. Her later participation in an experiment involving the contribution of Draconic Lightning demonstrates that the inherited element remains a central component of her supernatural abilities rather than merely a historical connection to her mother's Clan.[5]
Although Silvia shares Sasha's elemental heritage, the two should not be treated as identical combatants. Sasha possesses centuries of personal training, advanced Fulger techniques and her own refined Divine powers, while Silvia's individual abilities must be evaluated through her own demonstrations and statements. Her lineage does not automatically establish mastery over every technique developed by Sasha, Natashia, Victoria or the other members of the Fulger family.

#### Valentina Victoria Elderblood
[[Valentina Victoria Elderblood]] is one of Silvia's closest sisters and a constant companion throughout many of her appearances. As the daughter of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Violet, Valentina shares the same generational position as Silvia while possessing a substantially different maternal heritage. Their close relationship reflects the bond between Sasha and Violet, and they frequently accompany Rosemary as a nearly inseparable group.[4][6]
Silvia often displays awareness of Valentina's more extreme reactions and is comfortable correcting her when necessary. During their meeting with Evie, she notices when Valentina's thoughts become sufficiently intense to leak through their telepathic connection. She also warns Valentina against making inappropriate remarks that could attract Victor's attention, demonstrating familiarity with her sister's temperament and the limits of their father's patience.[4]
Their closeness survives the Empire's enormous accelerated development. Silvia and Valentina remain among the daughters who challenge Violet during a family dispute, while Rosemary's presence alongside them reinforces the established identity of their trio.[7]

#### Rosemary Lavina Elderblood
[[Rosemary Lavina Elderblood]] is the third member of Silvia's closest sibling group and the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Ruby. Silvia frequently addresses Rosemary as Lavina, particularly during their private conversations. The girls communicate telepathically, exchange information and share their enthusiasm for Victor's activities while maintaining distinct individual personalities.[4]
Rosemary is often more imaginative and impulsive in her suggestions, whereas Silvia tends to consider their practical consequences. This difference becomes especially visible when Rosemary proposes destroying Arcane with an orbital cannon. Silvia rejects the idea because it would eliminate useful territory and people, explaining that incorporating competent individuals into the Empire would provide greater advantages.[4]
Their differences also extend to entertainment. Rosemary enjoys anime and frequently recognizes references that Silvia does not understand, while Silvia prefers cartoons and books. These disagreements remain friendly and contribute to the group's familiar dynamic rather than weakening their relationship.[6]
Silvia and Rosemary continue appearing together during the mature Imperial era, including the sisters' collective confrontation with Violet and later experiments involving Eldritch creatures. Their shared participation in such incidents reinforces their longstanding reputation for creating trouble despite possessing the education and responsibilities expected of Imperial Princesses.[7][5]

#### Sylvia Elderblood
[[Sylvia Elderblood]] is Silvia's similarly named sister, born from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and [[Siena Scarlett Elderblood]]. Although their names differ by only one letter, they possess different biological mothers and belong to separate maternal branches of the Imperial Family. Silvia inherited the Fulger family's golden-blonde appearance and Lightning affinity, while Sylvia belongs to Siena's Scarlett lineage.[11][9]
The similarity between their names occasionally creates confusion, particularly during family gatherings involving many daughters. During an incident surrounding Velina, Sylvia insists that the girls would never deliberately harm their sister, prompting Silvia to snort in response. Their nearly identical names and manners are acknowledged despite their distinct parentage.[9]
Silvia should not be confused with Sylvia in accounts of the infiltration of Pepper's private residence. That incident involved Sylvia, Rosemary and Priscilla, rather than establishing Silvia as one of the original participants in the break-in. The distinction remains important when indexing the individual actions and achievements of Victor's daughters.[12][13]

#### Ophis Tepes Elderblood and Nero Elderblood
[[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]] and [[Nero Elderblood]] are among Silvia's older sisters and possess extensive experience with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s earlier life. Unlike his naturally born second-generation daughters, both were already members of his family before his ascension as a Dragon God, allowing them to witness much of his development and accumulate practical experience unavailable to children born afterward.[6]
Silvia respects their martial abilities and recognizes that their combination of natural talent and experience makes them formidable even among Victor's biological descendants. While observing their sparring session, she acknowledges that only exceptionally talented younger sisters could potentially compete with them. The intensity and refinement of their combat further reinforce the difference between inherited potential and mastered skill.[6]
Her relationship with them also extends into Imperial affairs. Silvia accompanies Valentina, Rosemary and Stella when the younger girls seek guidance concerning the responsibilities of working within the Empire. Ophis recognizes their intentions and agrees to discuss her experiences, allowing the younger generation to learn from someone familiar with the burdens associated with Imperial administration.[6]

#### Other Sisters
Silvia belongs to the enormous second generation of the Elderblood dynasty and maintains relationships with daughters from numerous maternal lineages. Her interactions with Stella, Leone, Valentina, Rosemary and the other Imperial Princesses demonstrate the close social structure of the family despite the considerable differences in their personalities and supernatural abilities.
She participates in gatherings involving the Fulger-descended daughters, including Julia, Ekaterina Natasha, Svetlana Veronika and Alisa Sasha. Their shared maternal heritage is reflected in their blonde hair, violet eyes and various degrees of resemblance to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and their respective mothers.[8]
Silvia also cooperates with sisters whose powers differ fundamentally from her own. During the creation of experimental Eldritch creatures, she works alongside Yog, Nigredo, Leone, Hinata, Valentina, Rosemary, Stella and Velina, contributing Draconic Lightning while the other girls propose abilities associated with Fire, Ice, Solar power, Truth and additional supernatural properties.[5]
The project demonstrates the collective potential of the Imperial Family, whose daughters possess access to numerous different supernatural systems and can combine their powers in ways that ordinary Gods or supernatural civilizations would struggle to reproduce.

### Fulger Heritage
Silvia represents the continuation of the [[Fulger Clan]] through the naturally born Blood Dragon lineage of [[Victor Elderblood]]. Her mother, [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]], descends from one of [[Nightingale]]'s ancient Noble Vampire families, whose hereditary power over Lightning has been preserved and refined throughout generations of supernatural development.
The Fulger Clan's ancestral inheritance originated through beings possessing an extraordinary affinity for Lightning, and its modern descendants developed numerous specialized applications of that power. The family's evolution into the Elderblood dynasty significantly expanded the physical and supernatural foundation supporting its elemental abilities, allowing the original Vampire Bloodline to coexist with True Dragon physiology and Divine power.[1]
Silvia inherited the elemental characteristics of her mother's family while possessing the superior biological foundation of a naturally born Blood Dragon. Her Lightning is therefore not restricted to the ordinary developmental limits of an untransformed Noble Vampire, although her personal proficiency and specific techniques remain dependent upon her own training and abilities.
Her inherited affinity is particularly notable because she is characterized as possessing an unusual talent for manipulating Lightning into different subordinate attributes. The individual derivatives have not all been identified, but her demonstrated participation in a later experiment involving Draconic Lightning establishes that her elemental power can be utilized through the Draconic framework inherited from Victor.[5]

### Lightning and Divine Concept
Silvia's principal supernatural inheritance is the [[Fulger Clan]]'s affinity for Lightning, which she received through [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]]. The original [[Fulger Bloodline]] developed its elemental abilities through the characteristics of ancient Noble Vampires and the inheritance of the family's ancestral Lightning power. Silvia possesses this heritage within a naturally born Blood Dragon, allowing her elemental characteristics to coexist with a fundamentally superior Draconic foundation.
The extent of Silvia's Lightning should nevertheless be distinguished from the complete abilities of her mother and other Fulger descendants. Sasha possesses extensive experience, specialized Lightning techniques and advanced Divine characteristics developed through her own evolution. Silvia's inherited affinity does not establish that she automatically possesses every technique or form mastered by Sasha, Natashia, Victoria or Carmila.
Silvia is characterized as possessing exceptional talent for manipulating Lightning into different subordinate attributes, indicating that her personal development extends beyond simply releasing conventional electrical attacks. The exact number, names and properties of these derivatives have not all been individually detailed.
Her participation in an experiment involving Eldritch creatures provides a more specific demonstration of this elemental foundation. When her sisters discuss strengthening the experimental beings through the addition of different supernatural characteristics, Silvia proposes Dragon Lightning and joins the collective process of contributing their respective abilities. The result establishes her ability to utilize Draconic Lightning as a supernatural attribute, although it does not provide a complete catalogue of her personal combat techniques.[5]
