Hilda Snow Elderblood, formerly known simply as Hilda Snow, is an ancient member of the [[Snow Clan]], its longtime Chief Maid, and the personal attendant of [[Agnes Elderblood Snow]]. Far more than a domestic servant, Hilda has historically functioned as one of the Clan's principal administrators, political advisers, combat instructors and disciplinarians. She helped raise and train [[Violet Elderblood Snow]], to whom she became a maternal figure, and was later explicitly described by Victor Elderblood as the woman second only to Agnes in strength within the Snow household.[6]
Hilda's responsibilities expanded considerably throughout the story. She temporarily managed the Snow Clan while Agnes was emotionally incapable of governing, helped educate Violet in international politics, analyzed enemy strategy during the Demonic War, and later became the personal instructor of [[Anna Elderblood]].[8][9][10][6] Eventually, Hilda's long-standing feelings toward Victor were confronted by Violet, leading Hilda to formally enter Victor's family. She became one of his wives and underwent an evolution into a True Dragon.[7][3]

### Physical description

#### Vampire
As a Noble Vampire, Hilda is described as a beautiful woman approximately 170 cm tall with pale skin, short white hair and blue eyes. Her usual appearance reflects her position as the [[Snow Clan]]'s Chief Maid, and she is most commonly seen wearing a formal maid uniform with white gloves. Her restrained posture and controlled expression complement the stern, professional demeanor with which she performs her duties.[4]
Maid Uniform
Despite appearing considerably younger than her actual age, Hilda is one of the oldest known members of the Snow household. Violet jokingly states that Hilda is older than Jesus and may even be older than [[Scathach]], demonstrating that her youthful appearance gives virtually no indication of the enormous length of her life.[11] Kaguya separately describes Hilda as being approximately within Agnes' age range, further establishing her as an extremely ancient Vampire rather than an ordinary maid.[12]

#### True Dragon
After becoming [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wife, Hilda underwent an evolution and explicitly identifies herself as a True Dragon.[3] The transformation gave her a fundamentally different physiology and awakened new senses and powers that she was initially still learning to use. Shortly after the transformation, Hilda momentarily forgot that she no longer needed to physically walk because she could simply float, with Violet deliberately taking her on an excursion as an opportunity to test her newly awakened senses and abilities.[3]
The novel has not yet provided the same detailed description of Hilda's personal True Dragon characteristics, unique Dragon form or individualized Divine Concepts that it has provided for several of Victor's more prominent wives. Consequently, abilities or visual traits belonging specifically to Violet, Agnes or other [[Blood Dragons]] should not automatically be attributed to Hilda without further evidence.

### Personality and traits
Hilda is defined primarily by her discipline, composure, responsibility and professionalism. While the Snow bloodline is infamous for producing emotionally intense individuals, Hilda normally presents herself as calm, rational and exceptionally controlled. She takes her responsibilities seriously enough that even Violet's status as the Clan heiress does not exempt her from Hilda's discipline. When ordered to bring Violet before Agnes, Hilda simply caught the fleeing heiress by her collar and dragged her toward her mother despite Violet's protests.[4]
This uncompromising attitude is even more obvious when Hilda is acting as an instructor. During Violet's early training, she punished the heiress immediately for becoming distracted by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], kicking her hard enough to launch her across a hill and bury her beneath rocks. Rather than regard this treatment as excessive, Hilda used the incident to teach Violet that the heir of a Count Clan could never afford to lower her guard merely because her attention had been drawn elsewhere.[13] Her later treatment of Anna followed the same philosophy. Victor specifically selected Hilda because he wanted someone capable of teaching his mother discipline, etiquette, Vampire customs and control over her newly awakened desires; Anna later admitted that Hilda "doesn't take it easy", but credited her training with helping Anna understand and control herself.[6][14]
Hilda possesses an extremely strong sense of loyalty and duty. Before joining the Elderblood Family herself, she was particularly devoted to Agnes and ordinarily accepted commands only from the Countess.[12] Her loyalty was nevertheless not blind incompetence: Hilda possessed enough political and administrative experience to independently run large portions of the Clan's affairs, and Victor considered her sufficiently reliable to place the [[Snow Clan]] under her practical management while Agnes recovered from Adonis' death.[8]
She is also highly intelligent, politically educated and observant. Hilda helped Agnes educate Violet regarding the leaders and political conditions of other supernatural factions because Clan Snow historically handled [[Nightingale]]'s domestic and foreign relations.[9] During the Demonic invasion she correctly evaluated [[Sitri]]'s strength and resources, recognized that his attack was deliberately designed to stall rather than conquer the Snow territory, understood the limitations of [[Clan Alioth]]'s spatial defenses and immediately identified the invasion of the Arcane Realm as suspicious.[10] Victor later explicitly called her a Master of etiquette and politics.[6]
Although normally stoic, Hilda is not emotionless. She shows irritation, embarrassment, amusement and affection around people she trusts, particularly Violet, Agnes and later Victor. Her maternal connection to Violet is strong enough that Victor directly calls her a mother figure to the younger Snow heiress.[6] By the time she formally became Victor's wife, Hilda had already lived beside his family for an enormous period and had developed deep feelings she had chosen not to openly pursue. Violet recognized those feelings and deliberately forced the issue rather than allowing Hilda's unspoken love to continue indefinitely.[7][3]
Even after becoming Victor's wife and attaining the same familial standing as the other women, Hilda initially struggled to abandon centuries of habit. She continued addressing Violet formally until Violet explicitly told her that they were now sisters of equal familial status and that such formalities were unnecessary in private.[3] This reflects Hilda's deeply ingrained respect for hierarchy, structure and proper conduct—qualities that remained part of her personality even after joining a family considerably less formal than the society in which she was raised.

### Synopsis

#### Vampire Society
Hilda first appears when Violet abruptly returns to the Snow residence after traveling through a portal. She is introduced as the personal maid of Agnes Snow and Head Maid of the [[Snow Clan]], possessing short white hair and blue eyes. Violet immediately attempts to leave in search of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], but Hilda catches her by the collar and informs her that Agnes had ordered Hilda to bring Violet before her. Hilda consequently drags the unwilling heiress through the residence regardless of Violet's complaints, immediately establishing both her authority within the household and her unwillingness to indulge Violet's impulsive behavior.[4]
Hilda subsequently becomes Violet's primary instructor while Victor trains under [[Scathach]]. When Violet allows concern for Victor to distract her, Hilda attacks without warning and kicks her hard enough to send her through the terrain. She reprimands Violet for dropping her guard and reminds her that being the heiress of the Snow Clan means living as a target for enemies as well as an object of admiration. Hilda's training consequently focuses not merely on increasing Violet's destructive power but on awareness, discipline, combat readiness and properly controlling the extraordinary abilities inherited through the Snow bloodline.[13]
Hilda's reputation as a combatant is later indirectly demonstrated when Victor seeks an experienced opponent capable of helping him improve his own Fire. Kaguya immediately recommends Hilda, describing her as an ancient Vampire approximately Agnes' age who is very proficient in the power of Fire. Kaguya also explains that Hilda normally accepts orders only from Agnes, emphasizing her longstanding loyalty to the Snow Clan's head.[12]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
After [[Adonis Snow]] asks [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to consume him in order to prevent his Soul from falling into [[Persephone]]'s hands, Agnes is left emotionally devastated by the loss of her husband and the supernatural bond that has partially transferred toward Victor. Hilda remains beside her mistress during the aftermath and helps remove the grieving Agnes from the immediate situation when Victor determines that she requires rest.[15]
The crisis ultimately reveals that Hilda's role in the Clan extends far beyond being Agnes' personal attendant. Victor begins reorganizing the Snow territory after exposing the conspirators hidden within it and decides to make the Clan capable of functioning without depending constantly upon Agnes. Because Agnes remains emotionally incapable of handling her normal responsibilities, Victor specifically chooses Hilda to take practical charge of the Clan until Agnes is ready to return.[8] Hilda consequently participates in the political and economic reforms introduced during this period, drawing upon her knowledge of the [[Snow Clan]]'s domestic administration and its international obligations.[16]
Later, Violet's teasing reveals how old Hilda actually is. During a conversation in which Hilda attempts to correct Violet, Violet responds by calling her extraordinarily old, claiming that Hilda predates Jesus and may even be older than [[Scathach]]. Although the statement is delivered mockingly rather than as a precise chronological measurement, it reinforces that Hilda has accumulated millennia of experience within Vampire society.[11]

#### Demonic War
During the [[Supernatural Gathering]], Hilda sits beside Violet and continues performing the educational role she has occupied since Violet's youth. When Violet casually describes [[Volk Fenrir]] as a beast, Hilda warns her not to underestimate a ruler who has maintained control over Samar for such a long period. Their conversation reveals that Agnes and Hilda had previously required Violet, as the Snow heir, to study the leaders of every major supernatural faction because Clan Snow was responsible for [[Nightingale]]'s domestic and foreign policy. Hilda similarly warns Violet regarding Tasha [[Fenrir]], whose manipulative and politically dangerous personality made her just as important to understand as her husband.[9]
When Demonic armies subsequently invade Nightingale, Hilda directly participates in assessing the defense of Snow territory. Observing [[Sitri]], she warns Violet not to dismiss him merely because the defenders appear to be winning and explains that a Demon's ranking depends upon wealth, influence, power, lineage and personal military strength. She recognizes that Sitri possesses enough forces to launch a far larger attack but is deliberately restraining himself, allowing Hilda and Violet to conclude that the Demonic army's actual objective is to keep the Snow forces occupied while another operation occurs elsewhere.[10]
Hilda's knowledge proves equally useful when June reports that the Arcane Realm has also been invaded. Hilda explains that even a Conceptual God possessing a Greater Space Divinity could potentially force a breach into Nightingale, yet invading the Witch Queen's personal realm should remain considerably more difficult. This allows the group to recognize that an unidentified power is assisting the Demons. When Violet finally moves personally against Sitri, Hilda covers her body in Fire and propels herself into the air to follow her.[10]
Hilda later receives an important new responsibility after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] transforms [[Anna Elderblood]] and [[Leon Walker]] into Vampires. Concerned that his parents remain unable to properly control their supernatural instincts, Victor summons Hilda together with [[Oda Blank]]. He introduces Hilda as Agnes' most loyal maid, a mother figure to Violet, the woman second only to Agnes in strength, a practitioner proficient in all Ninja Arts, and a Master of etiquette and politics. Victor assigns her to personally train Anna in Noble Vampire conduct, supernatural self-control, proper etiquette and the regulation of her new strength, talents and desires.[6]
Hilda approaches the assignment with her characteristic seriousness, regarding it as another responsibility she intends to complete with her full effort. Her success becomes apparent after Victor returns from Hell: Anna admits that Hilda's training has been extremely demanding but explains that it allowed her to understand herself far better and control the desires produced by her Noble Vampire nature.[14]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
By the time [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction has evolved into the [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]], Hilda is thoroughly integrated into the wider Elderblood household even though she has not yet formally become one of Victor's wives. She returns to the family mansion together with Violet and Agnes before the large-scale Dragon transformations, participating in the household discussions surrounding which individuals intend to undergo racial evolution and which prefer to preserve their original species.[17]
Her presence during this period reflects how completely the once separate Snow household has become integrated into Victor's expanding family and Pantheon. Nevertheless, unlike Agnes and Violet, Hilda does not initially undergo the transformation during the earlier waves of Dragonization, and her eventual evolution occurs only after her personal relationship with Victor changes.

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
After the passage of immense amounts of accelerated subjective time within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s territories, Hilda's relationship with Victor finally changes fundamentally. Although she had remained close to the Elderblood household for centuries and eventually millennia, Hilda kept her romantic feelings suppressed. Violet, having recognized Hilda's attachment to Victor, deliberately involves her in the relationship between Victor, Violet and Agnes rather than allowing those feelings to remain unresolved.[7]
The resulting union is not treated as a temporary affair. Hilda subsequently identifies herself as having become a True Dragon and understands that this transformation means she has formally become one of the Emperor's wives; Victor does not maintain casual lovers among the women who receive this level of connection, but wives and lifelong companions.[3] Violet likewise tells Hilda to stop addressing her as "[[Violet Elderblood Snow|Lady Violet]]" in private because their familial status is now equal and Hilda is one of her sisters.[3]
Despite accepting her new position, Hilda initially remains unaccustomed to her evolved body. When Violet invites her on an excursion, Hilda hesitates because her legs are still unstable, only for Violet to remind her that she can now simply float. Violet explicitly describes the excursion as an opportunity for Hilda to test her newly awakened senses and powers, marking the beginning of Hilda learning to operate as a True Dragon rather than an ancient Noble Vampire.[3]
Hilda nevertheless retains the same disciplined and organizational mentality that defined her life in Clan Snow. When discussing the enormous Elderblood Family, she readily understands why a hierarchy and internal system of responsibilities are necessary to prevent conflicts between so many extraordinarily powerful individuals. Her transformation therefore changes her species and place within the family without erasing the characteristics that originally made her indispensable to Agnes and Violet.[3]

#### Powers & Abilities
Hilda was one of the most powerful members of the [[Snow Clan]] even before becoming a Blood Dragon. Despite not holding the title of Countess, [[Victor Elderblood]] explicitly described her as the woman second only to Agnes in strength within the Snow household, while also identifying her as proficient in all Ninja Arts and a Master of politics and etiquette.[1] Her age, combat experience, Fire mastery and extensive political knowledge made her considerably more dangerous than her position as a maid initially suggested.

##### True Dragon Physiology
Following her union with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Hilda underwent an evolution and explicitly became a True Dragon.[2] Her new physiology possesses abilities and senses beyond those she held as an ancient Noble Vampire, although Hilda was initially unfamiliar with using many of them. The novel has not yet individually demonstrated every standard Blood Dragon ability through Hilda, so only directly demonstrated or explicitly established characteristics are listed below.
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics: Hilda already possessed exceptional physical strength, speed, reflexes and endurance as an ancient Noble Vampire. Victor later identified her as second only to Agnes in strength within the Snow household.[3][1] Her evolution into a True Dragon subsequently replaced her former Vampire physiology with a considerably higher form of supernatural existence.[2]
- Superhuman Strength: Hilda casually restrained the younger Violet by her collar despite Violet's resistance.[4] During training, a single kick from Hilda struck Violet hard enough to launch her across a hill and bury her under rock.[3]
- Superhuman Speed: Hilda possessed enough speed to overwhelm Violet during her early training and could appear beside her before Violet could properly react.[3]
- Superhuman Reflexes: As Violet's combat instructor, Hilda repeatedly punished openings and lapses in awareness quickly enough that Violet was forced to learn constant battlefield readiness.[3]
- Enhanced Stamina: Hilda is capable of prolonged supernatural training and combat far beyond Human limitations, first as an ancient Vampire and later as a True Dragon.[3][2]
- Flight / Levitation: After becoming a True Dragon, Hilda gained the ability to freely float. She initially forgot about the ability because of how recently her physiology had changed, prompting Violet to remind her that she no longer needed to rely upon her legs to travel.[2]
- Fire Propulsion (Formerly as a Vampire): Even before her Dragon transformation, Hilda could achieve aerial movement by covering herself in Fire and explosively kicking from the ground, flying after Violet during the Demonic invasion of [[Nightingale]].[5]
- Enhanced Senses: Hilda acquired new and enhanced senses after her transformation. Violet specifically proposes using their excursion as an opportunity for Hilda to test her newly awakened senses and powers.[2]
- Power Evolution: Becoming a True Dragon fundamentally upgraded Hilda's former existence and awakened abilities beyond those she possessed as a Noble Vampire. Hilda herself calls the change an "evolution", although the complete extent of her personalized post-transformation abilities has not yet been demonstrated.[2]

##### Former Vampire Physiology
Before her evolution, Hilda was an extremely old Noble Vampire of the [[Snow Clan]]. Consequently, she possessed the standard supernatural characteristics of Vampires in addition to her inherited Snow bloodline.[4]
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Like other ancient Vampires, Hilda possessed strength, speed, durability, stamina and reflexes immensely superior to ordinary Humans. Her personal level was especially high; [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] directly ranked her below Agnes but above the other Snow Clan members when introducing her to Anna.[1]
- Regeneration: As a Noble Vampire, Hilda possessed the regenerative physiology inherent to Vampires. Vampire regeneration allows severe physical injuries to be restored considerably faster than those of most mortal species, although the extent depends upon the Vampire's age and power.[6]
- Enhanced Senses: Vampires possess supernatural senses vastly superior to Humans. As an Elder Vampire, Hilda's perception was correspondingly developed and was sufficient for combat instruction and battlefield observation.[3][5]
- Longevity / Biological [[Immortality]]: Hilda lived for at least roughly two millennia while retaining the physical appearance of a young woman. Violet explicitly claims that Hilda predates Jesus and may even be older than [[Scathach]].[7]
- Blood Consumption: As a former Vampire, Hilda could replenish herself and obtain nourishment through blood, as is standard for her former race.

##### Snow Bloodline Abilities
As a member of the [[Snow Clan]], Hilda inherited the Clan's Fire-oriented Noble Vampire bloodline. Unlike speculative Snow abilities, Hilda's personal proficiency with Fire is directly confirmed: Kaguya describes her as an ancient Vampire who is very proficient in the power of Fire.[8]
- Fire Manipulation: Hilda can generate, control and surround herself with supernatural Fire. Her skill was sufficiently advanced that Kaguya suggested Hilda as an ideal opponent for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] when he specifically required an experienced Fire user capable of helping him improve through combat.[8]
- Fire Generation: Hilda can generate Fire directly from her body.[5]
- Fire Aura: Hilda can envelop her body in flames without harming herself.[5]
- Fire Propulsion: By combining physical movement with her Fire, Hilda can propel herself through the air at supernatural speed.[5]
- Fire Combat: Hilda is sufficiently experienced in Fire-based combat to instruct members of the Snow bloodline and was proposed as an experienced sparring partner for Victor when he needed to refine his own Fire.[3][8]
- Fire Control: Hilda possesses considerably better technical control than the younger Violet originally demonstrated. Her training of Violet focused heavily upon maintaining awareness and using inherited power intelligently rather than relying exclusively upon emotional output and raw destructive force.[3]
- Emotion Empowerment: The Snow bloodline grows stronger as the intensity of its user's emotions increases, with rage being an especially powerful amplifier. This is an inherited property of direct Snow bloodline Vampires rather than a Hilda-exclusive ability.[9]
- Sunlight Immunity: Direct members of the Snow bloodline possess the unusual ability to endure sunlight despite it being one of the traditional weaknesses of other Vampires. This bloodline characteristic was one of the traits Victor inherited through Violet when he was transformed.[10]
- Fire Immunity / Heat Resistance: Snow Vampires possess an innate affinity with their own element allowing them to operate within supernatural Fire that would normally be extremely dangerous to other Vampires. Hilda directly demonstrates that her own flames can completely cover her body without injury.[5]

##### Ninja Arts
Hilda possesses a major skill set that is almost completely absent from her old abilities page. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] introduces Hilda beside [[Oda Blank]], he first defines Oda's Ninja Arts as infiltration, assassination, Kenjutsu, Ninjutsu and interrogation, then explicitly states that Hilda is, "just like Oda," proficient in all Ninja Arts.[1] This establishes Hilda as considerably more versatile than a purely elemental fighter.
- Ninjutsu: Hilda is proficient in the supernatural and practical Ninja Arts used within [[Nightingale]].[1]
- Stealth: As part of her Ninja Arts training, Hilda is proficient in concealing her presence and operating without drawing attention.[1]
- Infiltration: Hilda is trained in entering defended or hostile locations covertly, one of the specific Ninja disciplines Victor associates with Oda immediately before describing Hilda as proficient in the same arts.[1]
- Assassination: Hilda possesses training in assassination techniques and the efficient elimination of targets.[1]
- Kenjutsu: Hilda is proficient in Japanese-style swordsmanship as part of her Ninja Arts education.[1]
- Interrogation: Hilda possesses interrogation training alongside her other Ninja disciplines.[1]
- Combat Stealth: Her combination of ancient Vampire speed and Ninja training makes Hilda particularly suited to exploiting openings before opponents can properly respond. Her early training session with Violet demonstrates her habit of immediately attacking lapses in attention.[3][1]

##### Combat Skills
- Master Combatant: Hilda possesses thousands of years of experience and was entrusted with personally training Violet, the heiress of one of [[Nightingale]]'s former Count Clans. Her physical combat ability was sufficient to repeatedly overwhelm the younger Violet during training, and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later described Hilda as the strongest woman of the Snow household after Agnes.[3][1]
- [[Martial Arts]] Proficiency: Hilda's combat training extends beyond elemental Fire use. She can employ direct physical attacks, rapid repositioning and disciplined battlefield awareness, and possesses the broader combat education implied by her proficiency in Ninja Arts.[3][1]
- Combat Instruction: Hilda is an experienced teacher capable of identifying weaknesses in other combatants and constructing harsh training around correcting them. She trained Violet to maintain awareness under emotional distraction and later trained Anna to control her physical strength, talents and predatory impulses.[3][1]
- Awareness Training: Hilda teaches fighters never to lower their guard and to remain prepared to activate supernatural powers immediately, particularly important for prominent Clan heirs who are constant targets.[3]
- Power-Control Training: Her instruction emphasizes controlling supernatural abilities rather than simply increasing output.[3][1]
- Instinct and Desire Control: Hilda helped Anna understand and regulate the intensified impulses produced by her new Noble Vampire physiology. Anna later directly credits Hilda's harsh training with helping her understand herself.[1][11]

##### Intellectual and Professional Skills
- Political Expertise: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly identifies Hilda as a Master of politics. Her knowledge extends across [[Nightingale]]'s domestic structure, foreign governments, major supernatural leaders and the political responsibilities historically handled by Clan Snow.[12][1]
- Etiquette Mastery: Victor explicitly calls Hilda a Master of etiquette and assigns her to teach Anna how a Noble Vampire is expected to behave in formal situations.[1]
- Administration: Hilda possesses sufficient managerial experience for Victor to place the [[Snow Clan]] under her practical control while Agnes recovered from Adonis' death. Victor intended to make the Clan self-sustaining and leave Hilda responsible until Agnes could return to her duties.[13]
- Economic Knowledge: As a senior administrator of the Clan responsible for Nightingale's economy and international relations, Hilda understands the authority, trade structure and political mechanisms available to the Snow Clan.[13]
- Clan Management: Hilda can maintain the operation of a former Count Clan when its official head is unable to govern.[13][14]
- Leadership: Although she rarely seeks formal political titles for herself, Hilda can issue instructions, supervise subordinates, train Clan heirs and manage Clan functions in Agnes' absence.[4][13]
- Tactical Analysis: Hilda can infer an enemy commander's objectives from the resources being committed to a battlefield. During the Demonic invasion, she recognized that [[Sitri]] possessed millions of Demons but was intentionally fielding only low-level forces, correctly concluding that the attack on Snow territory was a delaying operation rather than an attempt at conquest.[5]
- Strategic Analysis: Hilda quickly recognized that simultaneous breaches of Nightingale and the Arcane Realm could not be explained through ordinary Demonic portal abilities, leading her and Violet to suspect the involvement of a much more powerful hidden party.[5]
- Supernatural Knowledge: Hilda has accumulated extensive knowledge of Vampires, Demons, Gods, Witches, other supernatural factions and the political systems connecting them.[12][5]
- Demonology: Hilda understands the hierarchy of the Keys of Solomon, including how wealth and influence can determine lower rankings while the upper Keys additionally require personal power and powerful lineage. She correctly evaluates Sitri as dangerous despite his apparently passive conduct.[5]
- Dimensional and Divine Knowledge: Hilda understands the defensive significance of [[Clan Alioth]], the difficulty of breaching Nightingale, the effects of Greater Space Divinities and the exceptional dimensional protections surrounding the Witch Queen's Arcane Realm.[5]
- Foreign-Leadership Knowledge: Hilda helped educate Violet regarding rulers such as Volk and Tasha [[Fenrir]] because Snow Clan leaders were expected to understand the major political figures of other supernatural civilizations.[12]
- Observation: Hilda is highly perceptive in both combat and political situations. She noticed improvements in Violet's movement, evaluated her equipment and repeatedly identified inconsistencies in enemy behavior during the Demonic attack.[3][5]
- Emotional Discipline: Hilda possesses exceptional control over her emotions and outward behavior for a former Vampire, a species whose emotions are naturally intensified. Her stoicism and self-control are strong enough that Victor specifically entrusted her with teaching Anna how to control the predatory impulses of Noble Vampirism.[1][11]

##### External Enhancements
- [[Soul Marriage]]: Hilda ultimately became one of [[Victor Elderblood]]'s wives and entered his Family through the same deep spiritual framework used for his closest companions.[15][2]
- Victor's Soul Mark / Family Protection: Members of Victor's Family who are marked by his Soul are incorporated into an enormous contingency system designed to prevent hostile separation or tampering. If such a family member is forcibly kidnapped, the Soul connection can automatically transfer them into a protected Pocket Dimension controlled by Victor for quarantine and examination. Victor also created multiple countermeasures intended to prevent his Soul fragment inside family members from being influenced, erased or altered.[2]
- This should not be treated as blanket personal immunity to every form of Soul Manipulation or Existence Erasure; the explicit protection concerns Victor's Soul mark and the emergency systems attached to it.[2]

#### Resistances
- Resistance to Fire / Fire Immunity: Hilda can cover her entire body in her own supernatural Fire without suffering damage and belongs to the Snow bloodline, whose members possess innate resistance to Fire.[5]
- Immunity to Sunlight (Former Vampire): Unlike ordinary Vampires, direct members of the Snow bloodline are naturally capable of walking beneath sunlight without suffering the normal Vampire weakness.[16]
- Resistance to Emotional Influence: While not an absolute supernatural immunity, Hilda possesses exceptional psychological discipline and control over the intensified emotions associated with Vampirism, to the point that she was chosen to teach Anna how to prevent those instincts from controlling her behavior.[1][11]

#### Notable Scaling
- Before Hilda became a True Dragon, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly described her as the woman second only to Agnes in strength within the Snow household.[1]
- Kaguya describes Hilda as approximately within Agnes' age range and exceptionally proficient in Fire, which is why she considers Hilda an appropriate combat partner for Victor's elemental training.[8]
- Hilda could physically overwhelm the younger Violet during training and was trusted with molding the future [[Snow Clan]] leader's combat discipline.[3]
- Her later evolution into a True Dragon represents a fundamental racial advancement beyond the Noble Vampire state in which the preceding statements were made.[2]
