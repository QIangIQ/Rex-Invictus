**[[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]]**, formerly Eleanor Adrasteia and Eleanor Alucard Adrasteia, is a Blood Dragon, the former Vampire Countess responsible for the western territories of Nightingale, the leader of Clan Adrasteia, a disciple of Scathach Elderblood Scarlett, and one of the wives of Victor Elderblood. (Lore adapted from *My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires*.)

### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 67
You're mine, Eleanor. Do you think I would leave you in uncertainty for long? Just follow your heart and leave it to me. You can protect [[Nightingale]] even while living in my Pantheon.
—[[Victor Elderblood]] to Eleanor, Chapter 820
Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia, formerly Eleanor Adrasteia and Eleanor Alucard Adrasteia, is a Blood Dragon, the former Vampire Countess responsible for the western territories of Nightingale, the leader of Clan Adrasteia, a disciple of [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], and one of the wives of Victor Elderblood. For much of her life, Eleanor governed [[WarFall]], a heavily militarized settlement responsible for defending Vampire civilization against the seemingly endless population of immortal Monsters inhabiting Nightingale's unexplored western wilderness. Unlike the other [[Vampire Counts]], whose responsibilities involved politics, economics or administration, Eleanor's authority was fundamentally military, requiring her to oversee fortifications, soldiers, expeditions and the continuous containment of supernatural creatures that threatened the rest of Nightingale.[5][1]
Eleanor belongs to the Direct Bloodline of the First Adrasteia, an ancient Dragon Rider whose descendants inherited dormant Draconic characteristics. Her ancestry distinguishes her from ordinary Noble Vampires and even from most members of her own Clan, whose Monster traits developed through a secret ritual that incorporated the characteristics of slain Monsters into their bodies and Souls. Eleanor awakened the ancestral Dragon heritage and possessed an unusually draconic Monster transformation, eventually revealing that the creature used in her ritual possessed characteristics associated with gravity, fire and poison.[9][10][6]
Despite her imposing responsibilities, Eleanor developed a close relationship with Victor after he expressed interest in hunting the Monsters surrounding her territory. Their shared enthusiasm for combat gradually strengthened their attraction, while Victor's willingness to respect her responsibilities, Clan traditions and attachment to Nightingale distinguished him from individuals who might have regarded the Countess merely as a political or military asset. Eleanor eventually married Victor and joined his family, later undergoing a fundamental Draconic evolution that transformed her original Vampire-Monster hybrid existence into that of a genuine Blood Dragon.[6][11][4]
Her transformation did not erase the characteristics she had inherited through Clan Adrasteia. Instead, the Monster lineage was completely assimilated into the foundation of her new existence, producing a True Dragon associated with Space, Earth and Corruptible Fire. Her responsibilities likewise expanded beyond Warfall as she became involved in the military affairs of the [[Elderblood Empire]], participating in the conquest of extraterrestrial civilizations and eventually serving as a potential alternate commander of the First Legion, the Spartan Legion.[4][12][13]

### Physical Description

#### Vampire
Eleanor in her original Vampire appearance.
Eleanor is an exceptionally tall and physically developed woman with pale white skin, long flowing white hair and bright golden eyes. Standing approximately 190 centimeters tall in her original Vampire appearance, she possesses a muscular yet distinctly feminine physique shaped by her active military lifestyle. Unlike many members of [[Nightingale]]'s aristocracy, who spend much of their lives within luxurious mansions and political gatherings, Eleanor spent centuries fighting dangerous Monsters and training alongside the warriors entrusted with protecting Warfall. Her physical appearance consequently reflects the extraordinary strength and conditioning required of Clan Adrasteia's combat-oriented lineage.[5][14]
Despite her status as a Vampire Countess, Eleanor generally favors comparatively simple clothing when she is not participating in official ceremonies or combat operations. She has been described wearing ordinary shirts and jeans rather than elaborate Noble attire, demonstrating her preference for comfort and practicality. During military operations, however, she wears reinforced armor and carries an enormous greatsword whose size and weight make it unsuitable for ordinary Vampires. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] first encountered her, his ability to handle the weapon despite being a young Vampire surprised Eleanor and those accompanying her.[5]
Her imposing presence is further distinguished by the natural authority she developed through years of military command. Eleanor can appear calm and approachable during ordinary conversation, but her demeanor changes considerably when she assumes command of Warfall's defenders or prepares to confront a powerful enemy. Her expression, posture and predatory presence become much more intimidating, reflecting both her status as a Vampire Countess and the influence of the Monster bloodline incorporated into her body.[5][1]

#### Awakened Monster Form
Eleanor's original Adrasteia transformation originated from the Monster bloodline she incorporated through the Clan's secret ritual. Unlike the transformations of ordinary Noble Vampires, her Awakened Form possessed characteristics resembling a Dragon, reflecting the interaction between the Monster she consumed and the dormant Draconic heritage inherited from the First Adrasteia. The Monster used in her ritual possessed characteristics associated with gravity, fire and poison, which became part of the supernatural foundation of Eleanor's existence.[9][10]
In partial transformations, Eleanor can alter individual portions of her body without completely abandoning her humanoid appearance. One demonstrated manifestation involves transforming her arm into an enormous black monstrous claw, while her eyes acquire the predatory appearance associated with her Monster lineage. These changes allow her to utilize aspects of the transformation while retaining much of her ordinary Vampire form.[15]
Her complete Awakened Form is considerably more dramatic. Eleanor's body becomes covered in dark-green scales, two sharp horns emerge from her head, and Dragon-like wings extend from her back. A long tail develops from her lower body, while her face acquires reptilian characteristics and additional protective scales around her ears. Her eyes likewise change into those of a predatory reptilian creature. The transformation emphasizes the unusual nature of her bloodline, which was significantly closer to a Dragon than the ordinary Monster characteristics manifested by most other Adrasteias.[9]
This form was an important part of Eleanor's identity before her eventual evolution into a True Dragon. Rather than representing an ordinary Vampire Count transformation, it embodied the accumulated characteristics of her ancestry, Monster ritual and personal development as a warrior. The appearance also reinforced the reason she had been considered capable of fulfilling the Clan's hereditary leadership requirements despite the extraordinary restrictions imposed upon members of the Direct Bloodline.[9][6]

#### True Dragon
Eleanor's transformation into a True Dragon fundamentally reconstructed her previous existence. Instead of merely adding Draconic physiology to an existing Vampire-Monster hybrid body, the process assimilated her Monster lineage and transformed it into the foundation of her new Draconic nature. She emerged as a genuine Blood Dragon whose principal supernatural characteristics were associated with Space, Earth and Corruptible Fire, preserving the essential qualities she had developed throughout her earlier life while elevating them into a more advanced form.[4]
Her evolved appearance includes luminous neon-green Draconic eyes, horns and wings through which her distinctive green Energy can be perceived. The color of this Energy reflects the transformation of her previous Monster powers into a more advanced supernatural state, particularly the corruptive characteristics of her Fire. The evolution also granted her the vastly superior physical foundation associated with [[True Dragons]], allowing her to develop without remaining bound by the limitations of her former Vampire-Monster physiology.[4]
During later Imperial operations, Eleanor appeared in an enormous armored humanoid Draconic form alongside Rose and the Valkyries. The two commanders possessed imposing physiques and presences sufficient to immediately affect the atmosphere of a military command center, while Eleanor's appearance was accompanied by the visible manifestation of her authority over the surrounding environment. Their transformation demonstrated how drastically the former defenders of Warfall had changed after joining the Elderblood family.[12]

### Personality and Traits
Eleanor is an exceptionally dutiful, disciplined and responsible individual whose personality was shaped by the burden of governing one of [[Nightingale]]'s most dangerous territories. Unlike the other [[Vampire Counts]], whose responsibilities frequently involved politics, economics or the maintenance of aristocratic institutions, Eleanor spent much of her life protecting an entire civilization from creatures capable of devastating ordinary settlements. This responsibility developed her into a practical commander who prioritizes the safety of her soldiers, the stability of her territory and the fulfillment of her obligations above personal convenience. She is accustomed to making difficult decisions and does not regard authority as a privilege separate from the duties accompanying it.[5][1]
Her military experience also produced an unusually calm and patient temperament. Eleanor rarely allows ordinary insults, provocations or disagreements to interfere with her responsibilities, often maintaining a polite expression even when dealing with individuals whose behavior would irritate most other Noble Vampires. Her relationship with Violet demonstrates this patience particularly clearly. Although Violet initially regarded Eleanor with considerable hostility and referred to her through an insulting nickname, Eleanor generally treated the provocation as an inconvenience rather than allowing it to develop into a serious conflict. Her restraint is not a sign of weakness but a consequence of having spent centuries distinguishing between situations that genuinely require force and those that can be resolved through ordinary conversation.[2]
Beneath this composed exterior, however, Eleanor possesses an intense desire for battle. Like [[Scathach]] and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], she enjoys confronting powerful opponents and can become increasingly excited when an enemy proves capable of challenging her abilities. This side of her personality is particularly evident during hunts involving Nightingale's Monsters, where the ordinarily restrained Countess can display a much more aggressive and predatory temperament. Her Monster lineage intensifies these instincts, while her upbringing in Warfall provided an environment in which such enthusiasm could be expressed through constant combat without necessarily endangering innocent people.[16][17][15]
Eleanor also possesses a mischievous and occasionally sadistic sense of humor. She enjoys watching inexperienced individuals discover the unusual characteristics of Monsters through direct experience, particularly when their confidence causes them to underestimate dangers familiar to the veterans of Warfall. During Victor's early encounters with the creatures inhabiting her territory, she deliberately allowed him to learn certain lessons through fighting rather than immediately explaining every weakness and survival requirement. Her amusement was nevertheless accompanied by genuine interest in his unusual growth and willingness to learn from difficult experiences.[1]
Despite enjoying combat, Eleanor is not recklessly indifferent to the lives of her companions. Her decisions during the expedition beyond Warfall demonstrate an understanding that even exceptionally powerful warriors can be destroyed through exhaustion, poor planning or unnecessary engagements. When the group encountered approximately twenty enormous [[Titans]] deep within the unexplored territory, Eleanor and the others accepted that continuing forward would place the entire expedition at unnecessary risk. Their withdrawal reflected the discipline required of a commander whose responsibility was to bring her subordinates home rather than seek personal glory at their expense.[18]
Eleanor is deeply attached to Clan Adrasteia and the heritage inherited from her ancestors. She takes the traditions of the Main Lineage seriously, particularly those intended to preserve the Dragon Blood and Monster characteristics upon which the Clan's survival depended. Her affection for Rose reflects this connection, since the two women represent some of the few surviving members of their original bloodline. Eleanor was genuinely delighted when Rose finally awakened the dormant Dragon Blood she had failed to manifest throughout her earlier life, regarding the event as a restoration of something precious to their family rather than merely another increase in military strength.[6]
Her responsibilities also created considerable emotional conflict after she became romantically involved with Victor. Eleanor genuinely wanted to live beside him and the other members of his family, but she believed that abandoning Warfall or relinquishing her responsibilities to Nightingale would betray the duty she had accepted as Countess. For someone whose identity had been defined by protecting others, the possibility of choosing personal happiness at the expense of her people was deeply troubling. Victor eventually resolved the conflict by arranging for Clan Adrasteia to enter his Pantheon while permitting Eleanor to continue protecting Nightingale, demonstrating that he understood the responsibilities she could not simply discard.[11]
Following her marriage and Draconic evolution, Eleanor remained fundamentally the same disciplined and protective woman despite becoming vastly more powerful. Her military experience continued influencing her decisions during Imperial campaigns, where she preferred identifying and eliminating hostile leadership rather than destroying an entire civilization indiscriminately. She also maintained a sense of martial honor, recognizing that helpless civilians and armed enemies should not automatically receive identical treatment. This philosophy became particularly important when the Adrasteias confronted the descendants and followers of their historical enemies, requiring Eleanor to balance resentment, loyalty and the responsibilities of a commander.[19][12]
Outside warfare, Eleanor possesses quieter interests that contrast with her reputation as a Berserker. Reading books is one of her established hobbies, demonstrating that her personal life is not devoted exclusively to combat and military administration. Within the Elderblood household, she also displays considerable affection toward Victor and the younger members of the family, including the daughters who regard the women married to Victor as their collective mothers.[20][21]

### Background
Eleanor descends from the First Adrasteia, an ancient Dragon Rider whose bloodline eventually became the foundation of Clan Adrasteia. The descendants of this individual inherited dormant Dragon Blood, but the gradual weakening of the lineage and the dangers faced by successive generations required the Clan to develop unusual methods of preserving its supernatural characteristics. These circumstances ultimately led to the establishment of the Adrasteia Monster ritual, through which Vampires could incorporate the traits of powerful Monsters into their bodies and Souls.[6]
The Main Lineage maintained especially strict traditions surrounding marriage, reproduction and succession because only individuals capable of awakening the ancestral Dragon Blood could inherit the leadership of the Clan. This requirement contributed to the unusual position occupied by Rose, who belonged to the Direct Bloodline but failed to awaken her heritage during her earlier life. Eleonor's mother was Rose's niece, making the experienced swordswoman Eleanor's great-aunt and one of her closest surviving blood relatives.[6]
The history of Clan Adrasteia was also shaped by its conflict against the Monsters inhabiting [[Nightingale]]'s western wilderness. These creatures possessed extraordinary physical abilities and frequently displayed regenerative or immortal characteristics that prevented ordinary supernatural warriors from killing them permanently. The Clan consequently developed specialized military formations, unusual equipment and methods of incorporating Monster traits to strengthen its soldiers. The descendants of the First Adrasteia eventually became responsible for maintaining the western defensive frontier, transforming their territory into one of Nightingale's most heavily militarized regions.[1][10]
Eleanor received martial instruction from [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], one of the strongest Vampires in Nightingale and a legendary combat instructor. Her training helped develop the exceptional physical and martial foundation necessary for commanding Clan Adrasteia's forces. Eleanor eventually became a Vampire Countess at approximately twenty-seven years of age, an unusually young age for an office normally associated with ancient Noble Vampires possessing centuries of political and military experience. The achievement placed her among Nightingale's ruling Pillars and made her responsible for defending the territories surrounding Warfall.[1][7]
Her subsequent life remained closely tied to the defense of the western frontier. Warfall developed into a fortress civilization where ordinary citizens, merchants, craftsmen and soldiers were accustomed to the constant threat of Monster attacks. The Clan's economy relied heavily upon materials recovered from defeated creatures, while its military formations were trained to resist enemies capable of destroying ordinary Vampire settlements. Eleanor's position therefore required more than personal strength; she needed to maintain a functioning society whose continued survival depended upon the competence of its leadership and warriors.[22][1]

### Synopsis

#### Vampire Society
Eleanor first entered the main narrative near the conclusion of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s initial six months of training under [[Scathach]]. She arrived in [[Nightingale]] accompanied by a military contingent wearing black armor and carrying enormous weapons, immediately distinguishing her forces from the attendants and aristocratic retainers associated with the other [[Vampire Counts]]. The soldiers were mounted upon supernatural horses, whose unusual appearance and behavior attracted Victor's attention more than the formal political circumstances surrounding Eleanor's arrival. Rather than remaining intimidated by the heavily armed contingent, Victor approached the animals out of curiosity and quickly developed an attachment to a horse named Chloe.[5]
Eleanor herself demonstrated a more approachable personality than Victor expected from one of Nightingale's ruling Counts. Despite her military authority and considerable combat experience, she did not immediately treat the young Vampire with hostility or demand excessive formality. Their first meeting also revealed that Eleanor was a disciple of Scathach and possessed an exceptionally heavy greatsword, which Victor handled with surprising ease despite having only recently completed his initial training. His unusual physical strength and fearless behavior caught Eleanor's interest, particularly because most young Vampires would have struggled to even lift a weapon intended for an experienced warrior of her stature.[5]
Eleanor accompanied Scathach and the others toward the Royal Capital during preparations for the conflict involving Clans Rider and Horseman. Her soldiers helped escort the group, while Victor continued displaying the unusual mixture of enthusiasm, confidence and disregard for traditional Vampire etiquette that had already attracted considerable attention throughout Nightingale. Eleanor observed his interactions with the ancient Count families and gradually became interested in the young man whose rapid development had disrupted the established balance of Vampire society.[23]
Following the Clan Game and Victor's subsequent activities, Eleanor eventually invited him to visit Warfall. The invitation offered an opportunity to enter territory populated by dangerous Monsters and Demonic Beasts, making it immediately attractive to someone whose primary interest involved finding powerful opponents and improving through battle. Eleanor initially expected Victor to consider the offer carefully, but his enthusiasm for the prospect of fighting unfamiliar enemies convinced her that he possessed a mentality unusually compatible with the warrior culture of Clan Adrasteia.[24]

#### A New Pillar
Eleanor continued appearing among [[Nightingale]]'s senior Vampires following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s appointment as the Fifth Count. His new responsibility for exploration and the discovery of new territories naturally placed his interests near the dangerous frontiers traditionally guarded by Clan Adrasteia. Eleanor jokingly acknowledged that Victor had effectively become her neighbor in political terms, since his assigned duties would eventually bring him into contact with regions beyond the territories controlled by the established Vampire Clans.[25]
She also remained involved in the broader political events surrounding the [[Vampire Counts]], including the conflicts associated with Clan Horseman and the increasingly complicated relationships among Nightingale's ruling families. Although Eleanor generally maintained the responsibilities expected of a Countess, she displayed considerably more interest in Victor's combat development than in the ordinary political disputes occupying the other nobles. His exceptional growth, willingness to challenge dangerous opponents and developing relationship with [[Scathach]] made him a potentially valuable companion for the battles awaiting him beyond Warfall.[26][27]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Eleanor became more directly involved with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] when he returned to the wilderness surrounding [[Nightingale]] and requested her assistance in identifying a powerful gorilla-like creature guarding an unusual tree. Drawing upon her experience with Monsters and Demonic Beasts, Eleanor initially reacted aggressively toward the enormous creature before Victor explained their relationship and encouraged her to examine it more carefully. She subsequently recognized that the gorilla differed from the Monsters traditionally hunted by Clan Adrasteia, demonstrating the importance of distinguishing between creatures whose behavior, origins and supernatural characteristics were not necessarily identical.[16][28]
Victor's interest in Eleanor intensified as he recognized the contrast between her ordinarily restrained personality and the excitement she displayed while confronting dangerous enemies. He understood that Eleanor possessed a battle-loving temperament similar to his own and [[Scathach]]'s, although centuries of responsibility as Countess had encouraged her to suppress that side of herself whenever ordinary military duties required discipline. Victor consequently became interested in accompanying her on expeditions rather than merely observing her from a distance.[17]
During negotiations concerning the copied Hammers of [[Hephaestus]], Victor offered Eleanor sixty-nine replicas of the Divine Artifact. The weapons had been recovered through earlier events involving supernatural factions and possessed considerable destructive power despite being inferior to the original Hammer. Victor recognized that the replicas could become extremely valuable to Warfall, whose defenders continually faced enormous numbers of Monsters and needed equipment capable of destroying powerful enemies over large areas.[29][30]
When Eleanor asked what Victor wanted in exchange, he deliberately created an embarrassing misunderstanding by indicating that he wanted her. Eleanor initially interpreted the statement romantically, becoming increasingly flustered while Victor continued teasing her. His actual request was much simpler: he wanted Eleanor to accompany him on Monster hunts. Victor considered her an exceptional warrior whose experience and power could provide the challenges he wanted while avoiding the enormous gap separating him from Scathach. Eleanor ultimately accepted the arrangement, agreeing to allow Victor to participate in future expeditions while Warfall received the replicated Divine weapons.[30][31]
Victor later traveled to Warfall through the Gate of [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]], finally entering the territory Eleanor had spent much of her life protecting. He encountered a fortress settlement organized around permanent warfare, where civilians and soldiers were accustomed to carrying weapons and where the resources recovered from Monsters supported much of the local economy. During a confrontation with a Monster horde, Victor discovered that the creatures possessed regenerative characteristics capable of allowing them to survive attacks that would permanently destroy ordinary supernatural beings. Eleanor demonstrated why Clan Adrasteia remained capable of defending the region, using the Clan's specialized Energy and Monster-enhanced physiology to inflict injuries that the creatures could not simply regenerate.[32][22][1]
The experience revealed that Eleanor's position as Countess differed fundamentally from the political roles occupied by other Vampire nobles. Her authority was maintained through military competence and the continued ability to defend Warfall rather than relying exclusively upon ancestry or social prestige. Victor became increasingly interested in the unusual techniques and physiology possessed by the Adrasteias, while Eleanor recognized that his natural curiosity and rapid development made him an exceptional candidate for further exploration of the western wilderness.[1]
During the major expedition beyond Warfall, Eleanor led Victor, Rose and the Valkyries into increasingly dangerous territory populated by powerful Monsters and Demonic Beasts. Victor deliberately accepted the role of a squad member rather than attempting to assume command through his own political rank, acknowledging that Eleanor and her warriors possessed far greater knowledge of the terrain and its inhabitants. Their group traveled enormous distances, encountering creatures whose strength, intelligence and unusual abilities frequently exceeded the classifications familiar to ordinary Nightingale soldiers.[33][34]
Eleanor demonstrated the versatility of her battlefield-oriented abilities during the expedition. When the group needed to restrain an enormous flying Monster, she reshaped the surrounding earth into thousands of colossal stone hands capable of interfering with the creature's movements. Later, she used her authority over the terrain to construct an enormous fortified city, creating defensive walls and structures through the manipulation of the surrounding land. Although the feat displayed the extraordinary versatility of her abilities, producing such a large structure placed considerable strain upon her at the time, demonstrating that even exceptionally powerful Vampires possessed practical limitations.[35][36]
The expedition also exposed the enormous skill of Rose Adrasteia, whose swordsmanship could sever entire sections of the battlefield and destroy creatures possessing tremendous durability. Eleanor's relationship with Rose demonstrated the close cooperation between the Clan's leader and its senior commander, while the presence of the Valkyries emphasized the importance of specialized teamwork against enemies whose abilities could not always be overcome through raw power.[36]
The group eventually ventured approximately 42,000 kilometers beyond Warfall, reaching territory inhabited by increasingly dangerous creatures. When they encountered around twenty gigantic [[Titans]], each exceeding fifty meters in height, the expedition chose to withdraw rather than initiate a potentially disastrous confrontation. The decision reflected Eleanor's practical experience and Victor's developing understanding that surviving an expedition required more than defeating every enemy encountered along the way. Their group had already traveled far beyond established support positions, making unnecessary combat against numerous powerful opponents an unacceptable risk.[18]
During the return journey, Eleanor revealed the existence of Clan Adrasteia's secret ritual. The procedure allowed Vampires to incorporate the bloodline characteristics of a Monster into their bodies and Souls, but it could only be performed once because of the fundamental changes imposed upon the recipient. Its success depended upon the individual's compatibility, strength and the characteristics of the Monster used in the transformation, while Clan tradition normally required the recipient to personally defeat the creature whose bloodline would be absorbed.[10]
Eleanor further explained that her own Monster lineage originated from a Dragon-like creature associated with gravity, fire and poison. The ritual's connection to Clan Adrasteia was protected through Soul-level secrecy, preventing ordinary outsiders from freely obtaining the technique. When Rose suggested that Victor could marry Eleanor to satisfy the Clan's requirements, Victor rejected the implication that he should exploit Eleanor's feelings merely to acquire another supernatural ability. His reaction demonstrated genuine respect for Eleanor and became an important development in the affection forming between them.[10]

#### Demonic War
During the Demonic War, Eleanor remained responsible for Clan Adrasteia and its obligations toward [[Nightingale]], even as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] began constructing an increasingly independent coalition of supernatural factions. The existing political responsibilities of Clans Adrasteia and Alioth prevented them from entering the same formal arrangements as some of Victor's other allies, but this did not mean Eleanor opposed him. Victor deliberately avoided forcing the Clans into contracts that would conflict with their established duties, preferring to maintain cooperation through trust and mutual interests.[37][38]
Eleanor later participated in Victor's operation involving the surviving Amazons in Brazil. The mission required extensive control of the surrounding battlefield, particularly because the territory contained obstacles and hostile forces that could interfere with the planned confrontation. Eleanor demonstrated her mastery of battlefield manipulation by placing her hands upon the ground and invoking her authority over the surrounding terrain. Earthquakes spread through the area as the land began behaving almost like a fluid substance, swallowing obstacles, flattening irregular ground and producing an enormous open battlefield suitable for the forces preparing to engage the enemy.[3]
The operation ultimately developed into the trap that resulted in Victor being sent into Hell. Although the immediate events caused considerable suffering and temporarily separated Victor from much of his family, the experience subsequently became one of the most important stages of his development as a ruler and supernatural being. Eleanor remained among the allies connected to his growing faction throughout the conflict, while the responsibilities surrounding Warfall continued requiring the attention of Clan Adrasteia.[39][40]

#### King Among Kings
Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s return from Hell, Eleanor and Rose eventually approached him to discuss the traditions and origins of Clan Adrasteia. Their conversation revealed that the First Adrasteia had been a Dragon Rider, explaining the ancestral Dragon Blood preserved within the Clan's Main Lineage. Eleanor acknowledged that Victor's own connection to Dragons made his existence unusually compatible with the requirements historically imposed upon potential spouses of the Adrasteias, meaning the ordinary Monster ritual was no longer necessary to establish the relationship permitted by Clan tradition.[6]
Rose also revealed that she belonged to the same Direct Bloodline as Eleanor. Eleanor's mother had been Rose's niece, making the experienced swordswoman Eleanor's great-aunt rather than an unrelated subordinate. Despite belonging to the Main Lineage and possessing extraordinary martial ability, Rose had originally failed to awaken the ancestral Dragon Blood, preventing her from becoming Clan Leader. Eleanor explained that awakening this heritage was a fundamental requirement for succession, regardless of an individual's competence or seniority.[6]
The conversation became particularly significant when Eleanor revealed that Rose had finally awakened her dormant Dragon Blood through her relationship with Victor. Eleanor was delighted by the development because it represented the restoration of an important part of their ancestral heritage. Rose, having long since accepted her inability to awaken the bloodline and redirected her efforts into swordsmanship, was less concerned with the personal prestige of the transformation but appreciated the happiness it brought Eleanor.[6]
Victor subsequently accepted Eleanor and Rose as his wives, allowing them to join the family that would eventually become Clan Elderblood. Their relationships were not founded solely upon the political or biological advantages associated with the Adrasteia bloodline, since Victor had repeatedly demonstrated that he did not regard acquiring supernatural abilities as a sufficient reason to manipulate someone's emotions. Eleanor's marriage consequently represented both a personal commitment and the beginning of a closer relationship between Clan Adrasteia and Victor's growing faction.[6]
Eleanor later confronted a difficult decision concerning her responsibilities to [[Nightingale]]. Although she wanted to live with Victor, she remained deeply attached to Warfall and could not accept the idea of permanently abandoning the territory entrusted to her. Victor resolved the situation by arranging for Clan Adrasteia to become part of his newly established Pantheon while retaining the ability to continue protecting Nightingale. He also revealed that Vlad had accepted the arrangement through an alliance, removing the political obstacle that had prevented Eleanor from fully committing herself to her new family.[11]
As Victor began constructing the foundations of his Pantheon, the lands belonging to Clan Adrasteia were among the territories incorporated into his enormous project. Eleanor's responsibilities therefore continued within a new political and supernatural structure rather than disappearing. The decision reflected Victor's understanding that the people he loved possessed identities and obligations extending beyond their relationships with him.[11]
During the invasion of Nightingale by the Elder Gods, Eleanor confronted enemies whose existence was connected to the historical suffering endured by Clan Adrasteia. Enraged by the attack and the threat to her family, she entered her Awakened Form and unleashed an enormous display of destructive battlefield control. Earthquakes and landslides spread across an area exceeding ten thousand kilometers, while sections of the surrounding mountains were dismantled and transformed into enormous stone structures and warriors.[41]
Eleanor subsequently created an enormous stone greatsword and hundreds of gigantic stone soldiers, directing them against the invading Monsters. She then entered the sky surrounded by green flames and produced a massive funnel of Fire that drew numerous creatures into itself before destroying them. The battle demonstrated the enormous development of her original abilities, which had previously required considerable effort to construct a defensive city during the Warfall expedition but could now reshape vast regions of the battlefield during direct combat.[41]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ascension as the Dragon God of [[Chaos]], Eleanor became involved in the preparations surrounding the transformation of his family into genuine Dragons. Victor intended the process to preserve and elevate the distinctive characteristics of the women who accepted Dragonhood rather than erase their individual identities or replace their previous abilities with identical racial traits. Eleanor's unusually developed Monster lineage made her transformation particularly significant because the supernatural characteristics inherited from Clan Adrasteia had already been integrated deeply into her body and Soul.[42][43]
The transformation reconstructed Eleanor's existence through Victor's Draconic authority, incorporating her Monster bloodline into the foundation of a new True Dragon physiology. The resulting evolution was unusual even among the other women undergoing Dragonization, since Eleanor did not merely retain a collection of unrelated Monster abilities. Her previous lineage became part of her Draconic nature, producing a Dragon associated with Space, Earth and Corruptible Fire.[4]
The characteristics of Eleanor's green Fire became considerably more dangerous following the transformation. The flames were no longer restricted to ordinary destructive effects against physical targets, possessing the capability to corrupt the victim's Soul. Consequently, individuals capable of surviving the immediate effects of her Dragon Breath could still succumb to the spiritual corruption left behind. The transformation also altered her appearance, producing neon-green Draconic eyes and corresponding manifestations of power through her horns and wings.[4]
Her evolution represented the culmination of a process that had begun with the First Adrasteia. The Clan's founder had been a Dragon Rider, and generations of descendants had attempted to preserve and strengthen the inherited Dragon Blood through increasingly specialized Monster rituals. Eleanor eventually married Victor, whose own Draconic evolution surpassed the ordinary condition of a Dragon Rider, and became a genuine True Dragon herself. Rather than permanently remaining a Vampire-Monster hybrid carrying fragments of ancient Draconic ancestry, she acquired a new existence in which those characteristics were fully integrated into her being.[6][4]
Eleanor also continued maintaining her connections to the Valkyries and the surviving warriors of Clan Adrasteia. Victor later visited the group and provided its members with specially crafted Divine weapons adapted to their individual combat styles. The gifts reflected his recognition that the Valkyries remained essential to Eleanor and Rose's military strength, even though the Empire increasingly possessed armies and supernatural beings far more powerful than those available during the earlier [[Nightingale]] era.[19]
The encounter also addressed the fate of populations connected to the historical enemies of Clan Adrasteia. Although the Valkyries possessed understandable resentment toward those responsible for the suffering endured by their ancestors, Victor emphasized that Eleanor and Rose were honorable warriors capable of distinguishing between armed enemies and defenseless individuals. He encouraged their subordinates to trust the judgment of their leaders rather than allowing vengeance to dictate every decision, reinforcing the responsibilities that continued accompanying Eleanor's position as head of the Clan.[19]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
By the time [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family had entered its next era, Eleanor had become fully integrated into the Elderblood household while retaining her identity as a warrior and leader of Clan Adrasteia. Her responsibilities increasingly extended beyond the defense of Warfall, particularly as Victor's faction began expanding into foreign worlds and establishing the military infrastructure necessary to support an interstellar civilization. Eleanor continued working closely with Rose and the Valkyries, whose experience fighting dangerous Monsters remained valuable even as the enemies facing the Empire became far more sophisticated.[12]
Eleanor was also among the mothers of the next generation of Victor's family. By the period in which Jeanne began teaching the younger Elderblood daughters about Divinity and the nature of the Soul, the household had already produced numerous children from Victor's marriages. Eleanor's daughter was associated with this generation, although her complete individual identity has not been established in the relevant citation.[8]
The expansion of the Imperial Domain eventually brought Eleanor into direct contact with extraterrestrial civilizations. During one operation, [[Akasha Elderblood]] identified a foreign planet defended by several powerful Gods and a mature World Tree. The local civilization possessed sufficient Divine and military strength that the Imperial fleet commanded by John could not reasonably conquer it without reinforcements. Akasha consequently transferred Eleanor, Rose and the Valkyries directly into the command center of the operation.[12]
Their arrival immediately changed the atmosphere aboard the ship. Eleanor appeared in her imposing Draconic form and approached the commander's chair, using her supernatural authority to transform the surrounding structure into a dark-green throne with black accents. John recognized the weight of her status as a Dragon Goddess and experienced military commander, while the soldiers understood that members of the Imperial Family had personally assumed responsibility for the confrontation.[12]
After reviewing the information collected by Akasha, Eleanor concluded that the Empire could achieve victory more efficiently by targeting the enemy civilization's Divine leadership rather than relying exclusively upon a prolonged conventional invasion. She understood from her own experience that societies governed by arrogant supernatural rulers frequently contained dissatisfied populations whose loyalty depended heavily upon the continued dominance of their Gods. Removing the leadership could therefore produce a psychological collapse among the defenders, although Akasha warned that desperate populations might still resist despite losing their rulers.[12]
Eleanor assigned Rose responsibility for confronting the enemy's strongest Primordial God while she personally intended to pursue the World Tree. John and the Valkyries were ordered to engage the remaining Divine defenders, allowing the Imperial forces to concentrate upon the individuals maintaining the foreign civilization's resistance. The operation demonstrated Eleanor's development from a Countess defending a single region of [[Nightingale]] into a commander capable of directing campaigns against entire extraterrestrial civilizations.[12]
Her connection to ordinary interests and family life nevertheless remained visible. During discussions concerning the hobbies practiced by Victor's wives, Eleanor was identified as someone who enjoyed reading books, contrasting her quiet personal interests with the increasingly unusual activities pursued by other members of the Elderblood household. Her enjoyment of reading reflected a side of her personality not immediately apparent from her military reputation or aggressive behavior during combat.[20]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
The enormous expansion of the [[Elderblood Empire]] eventually required the reorganization of its military into specialized Legions. During the accelerated development of Imperial civilization, the First Legion, known as the Spartan Legion, was established through the efforts of [[Aphrodite]], [[Scathach]] and Rose. It was designed to serve as the Empire's principal offensive formation, responsible for invading hostile territory, overcoming planetary and stellar defenses, and leading large-scale military campaigns against external civilizations.[13]
Although John became the principal commander of the Spartan Legion, Eleanor was appointed as one of the individuals capable of replacing him when circumstances required it. Rose and Kali were similarly recognized as potential alternate commanders, demonstrating the confidence placed in their individual abilities and military experience. Eleanor's appointment represented a continuation of the leadership responsibilities she had carried throughout her life, but on a vastly greater scale than the defense of Warfall.[13]
The Spartan Legion's composition also demonstrated how dramatically Imperial warfare had changed. Its forces included War Dragons, heavily armored soldiers, siege specialists and advanced spacecraft capable of threatening planetary and stellar defenses. Eleanor's experience remained valuable because the principles of military discipline, identifying priority targets and coordinating specialized forces were applicable regardless of the technological scale of a particular conflict.[13]
Following the establishment of the Empire's extensive infrastructure, the territories historically associated with Clan Adrasteia continued existing within the larger Imperial civilization. [[Nightingale]] itself experienced enormous economic, technological and demographic development, gradually becoming home to numerous supernatural races that had previously remained separated through conflict or prejudice. The old Count territories remained recognizable despite the political changes, while their leaders were integrated into the broader system governed by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his family.[44]
Eleanor also remained present during important gatherings involving the Imperial Family. When [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]], Victor's youngest daughter with [[Tyche]], was introduced to her numerous mothers, Eleanor participated in the ensuing conversation concerning the child's extraordinary characteristics and the increasingly complicated family relationships surrounding Victor. Her observation that the situation would once again involve Ophis reflected the familiarity she had developed with the household's internal dynamics after many years of living alongside the other women.[21]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
Eleanor's relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] began through mutual interest in combat and gradually developed into a genuine emotional attachment. When they first met, Eleanor was intrigued by his unusual physical abilities and fearless curiosity toward the supernatural horses accompanying her military forces. Their subsequent encounters revealed that Victor possessed a temperament unusually compatible with the warriors of Clan Adrasteia, particularly his enthusiasm for dangerous opponents and willingness to improve through direct experience.[5]
Victor became more interested in Eleanor after recognizing the battle-loving personality hidden beneath her ordinarily composed demeanor. He later offered Warfall an enormous collection of replicated Divine weapons in exchange for Eleanor accompanying him on Monster hunts, deliberately teasing her by initially allowing her to believe that he was asking for something more personal. The arrangement brought them into closer contact and allowed Victor to experience the military environment that had shaped Eleanor's life.[30][31]
Their relationship deepened during the expedition beyond Warfall, where Victor respected Eleanor's superior knowledge of the territory and accepted her leadership despite possessing an independent political rank. He also demonstrated that he did not regard her Monster physiology or unusual transformations as something repulsive, instead approaching them with fascination. When Rose suggested marrying Eleanor as a method of obtaining Clan Adrasteia's secret ritual, Victor became angry because he refused to treat Eleanor's emotions as a convenient resource for acquiring another ability.[33][10]
Eleanor eventually married Victor after revealing the history of the First Adrasteia and the compatibility between Victor's Draconic existence and her Clan's ancestral traditions. Her greatest emotional conflict emerged when she realized that following Victor might require abandoning [[Nightingale]] and the responsibilities entrusted to her as Countess. Victor resolved the problem by arranging for Clan Adrasteia to enter his Pantheon while preserving her ability to continue protecting her former territory. His refusal to make her choose between love and responsibility demonstrated an understanding of the principles that had defined Eleanor throughout her life.[6][11]
Their marriage later resulted in Eleanor's evolution into a Blood Dragon, while her status within the family expanded alongside Victor's growing Empire. Despite the enormous changes in her existence, Eleanor retained the affection, discipline and sense of responsibility that had originally attracted Victor to her. She continued supporting his faction through military service, the leadership of Clan Adrasteia and the upbringing of the next generation.[4][8][13]

#### Rose Adrasteia Elderblood
Rose is Eleanor's great-aunt and one of her closest surviving blood relatives. Both women descend from the First Adrasteia, but their positions within the Clan were determined by the requirement that its leader awaken the ancestral Dragon Blood. Rose originally failed to meet this requirement despite possessing enormous martial experience, while Eleanor successfully manifested the bloodline and became the Clan's leader.[6]
Rose nevertheless served as Eleanor's most trusted senior commander and maintained the administration of Warfall whenever the Countess was absent. Their relationship combined military discipline with genuine familial affection, particularly because the Adrasteia Main Lineage had suffered enormous losses throughout its history. Eleanor was delighted when Rose finally awakened her Dragon Blood through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], regarding the development as an important restoration of their family's heritage.[22][6]
Both women eventually married Victor and became [[Blood Dragons]], continuing to serve together even after the establishment of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. During later military operations, they demonstrated exceptional coordination and mutual trust, including the planetary campaign in which Eleanor assigned Rose responsibility for confronting a powerful Primordial God while she personally pursued the enemy's World Tree.[12]

#### Scathach Elderblood Scarlett
[[Scathach]] is one of Eleanor's former martial instructors and an important influence upon her development as a warrior. Eleanor's exceptional physical abilities and battle-oriented mentality made her a suitable student for the legendary Vampire Countess, whose training emphasized discipline, combat experience and the pursuit of martial perfection.[5][7]
Their relationship also helps explain Eleanor's compatibility with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Both Eleanor and Victor possess an intense enthusiasm for confronting powerful opponents, while Scathach's influence contributed to the development of their respective martial foundations. Despite the similarities, Eleanor's responsibilities as Countess forced her to exercise greater restraint than the younger Victor initially possessed, allowing her to balance the enjoyment of combat against the obligation to protect her soldiers and territory.[17][33]

#### Vlad Tepes
As one of [[Nightingale]]'s original [[Vampire Counts]], Eleanor formerly served under the political authority of [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]], whose rule established the administrative framework governing the great Vampire Clans. Her duties as the western Countess required maintaining the defenses of Nightingale while preserving the obligations associated with her Clan's position.[5][1]
Eleanor's marriage to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] complicated this relationship because she wanted to join his family without abandoning Nightingale. Victor ultimately arranged an alliance with Vlad permitting Clan Adrasteia to relocate into his Pantheon while Eleanor retained the freedom to continue protecting Nightingale. The arrangement removed the conflict between her former political responsibilities and her new family allegiance.[11]

#### The Valkyries
The Valkyries are Clan Adrasteia's elite female warriors and some of Eleanor's most trusted subordinates. The group includes Rose, Dorothy, Alexa, Judith, Martha, Juliet and Anrietha, each possessing different combat specializations suited to the extraordinary enemies inhabiting [[Nightingale]]'s western wilderness. Their experience and loyalty allowed Eleanor to conduct expeditions deep into dangerous territory while maintaining an effective military organization capable of responding to numerous threats.[22][33]
Eleanor does not regard the Valkyries as disposable soldiers. Their long service and shared hardships created considerable personal trust, while Rose's position as both senior commander and blood relative strengthened the group's cohesion. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later recognized their importance and supplied them with personalized Divine Artifacts crafted to complement their individual fighting styles, allowing them to remain effective as the military threats facing the Elderblood family continued escalating.[19]

#### Children
Eleanor and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] have at least one daughter belonging to the next generation of the Elderblood family. During Jeanne's lessons concerning Divinity and the nature of the Soul, the daughters of Victor's various wives gathered to receive instruction intended to help them understand the supernatural characteristics inherited through their parents. Eleanor's child belongs to this generation, although her complete name and individual history remain unconfirmed by the currently cited passage.[8]
Eleanor also shares a maternal relationship with the other children of the Elderblood household. The daughters generally recognize Victor's wives as their mothers regardless of which woman gave birth to them, creating an unusually extensive family structure in which the women participate collectively in raising and protecting the younger generation. Eleanor's presence during Moira's introduction further demonstrates her continued involvement in the domestic affairs of the family despite her military responsibilities.[21]

### Abilities

#### Powers and Abilities
Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia is an exceptionally powerful supernatural warrior whose combat abilities originate from the ancient [[Adrasteia Bloodline]], her former Noble Vampire and Monster hybrid physiology, her extensive military and martial training, and her subsequent evolution into a Blood Dragon. Before becoming a True Dragon, Eleanor possessed the unusual physiology developed by Clan Adrasteia through centuries of incorporating Monster characteristics into its Vampire bloodline. This granted her exceptional physical capabilities, the ability to undergo partial and complete Monster transformations, and access to a specialized Energy capable of permanently killing the otherwise immortal Monsters inhabiting [[Nightingale]]. Her greatest individual specialization was her extraordinary control over the Earth, allowing her to restructure entire battlefields, create defensive fortifications and construct massive armies of animated stone.[1][2]
Eleanor belongs to the Main Lineage descended from the First Adrasteia, an ancient Dragon Rider whose descendants inherited dormant Draconic characteristics. Through the Adrasteia ritual, she had incorporated the bloodline of a Dragon-like Monster possessing powers associated with Gravity, Fire and Poison. These characteristics produced a particularly powerful Awakened Form with reptilian features, dark-green scales, horns, wings and a tail, alongside considerable enhancements to her physical capabilities. Her mastery of these abilities allowed Eleanor to fight enormous armies of Monsters, manipulate gravitational attraction and employ destructive techniques such as [[Black Hole|Singularity]], which combined her green supernatural flames with gravitational forces capable of drawing and consuming surrounding targets.[3][4][5]
Following her transformation through [[Victor Elderblood]]'s Draconic authority, Eleanor's previous Monster lineage was completely assimilated into the foundation of her new existence. She became a genuine True Dragon explicitly described as controlling Space, Corruptible Fire and Earth. Her original green Fire evolved into a far more dangerous form capable of corrupting the target's Soul, while her superior Draconic physiology allowed her supernatural abilities to operate through an existence no longer restricted by her former hybrid constitution. These three powers are confirmed as characteristics of her evolved Draconic nature; however, the novel does not explicitly classify all three as independent formal Divinities, and they should not automatically be assigned the complete range of abilities associated with such Concepts.[6]

##### True Dragon Physiology
After undergoing Dragonization, Eleanor became a genuine True Dragon belonging to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood Dragon lineage. Her transformation fundamentally reconstructed her original Vampire-Monster physiology, absorbing the Monster bloodline into the foundation of her new Draconic existence rather than maintaining it as an entirely separate source of power. The transformation granted her a superior biological and supernatural constitution capable of supporting her evolved control over Space, Corruptible Fire and Earth.[7][6]
- True Dragon Physiology: Eleanor possesses the physiology of a genuine Dragon rather than a Vampire temporarily imitating Draconic characteristics. Her former Monster bloodline was incorporated into her new existence during the transformation, preserving and elevating the abilities it had previously granted her.[6]
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Eleanor possesses extraordinary strength, speed, durability, reflexes, agility and physical endurance. Her original Adrasteia hybrid constitution was already physically superior to that of ordinary Noble Vampires, while her transformation into a True Dragon provided an even greater foundation for her supernatural development.[8][6]
- Enhanced Strength: Eleanor possesses sufficient physical power to wield exceptionally large and heavy weapons. Even before Dragonization, her greatsword was so heavy that ordinary Vampires required special training to handle it properly. Her subsequent Draconic transformation substantially enhanced the physiology supporting her combat techniques.[9][6]
- Enhanced Durability: Her True Dragon body possesses vastly improved resistance to physical damage compared with her former hybrid physiology. The transformation incorporated her Monster characteristics into a superior Draconic constitution rather than preserving the limitations of her original Vampire body.[6]
- Enhanced Speed and Reflexes: Eleanor possesses supernatural movement speed and combat reactions derived from her original Adrasteia bloodline and later Draconic physiology. Her experience fighting extremely dangerous Monsters further supports her ability to respond rapidly to battlefield threats.[8][6]
- Enhanced Senses: Eleanor possesses the superior senses associated with Clan Adrasteia and her evolved Dragon physiology. During an Imperial operation against an extraterrestrial civilization, she and Rose demonstrated Draconic perception capable of rapidly evaluating their surroundings and the beings inhabiting the target world.[8][10]
- Flight: Eleanor possesses Dragon-like wings and can fly independently. She previously demonstrated aerial mobility through her Awakened Monster Form, while her evolved Draconic manifestation possesses wings reflecting the green Power associated with her new existence.[4][11][6]
- [[Draconic Energy]]: As a genuine Dragon, Eleanor possesses the supernatural Energy associated with her Draconic existence. Her evolved green Power manifests through her eyes, horns and wings, while her individual characteristics are associated with Space, Corruptible Fire and Earth.[6]
- Dragon Breath: Eleanor possesses a Dragon Breath capable of releasing her evolved Corruptible Fire. Its effects extend beyond ordinary physical destruction because the flames can corrupt the Soul of a surviving target, potentially causing death even after the initial attack has been endured.[6]
- Draconic Eyes: Eleanor's eyes become neon green during her Draconic manifestation and visibly radiate the supernatural Power associated with her evolved existence. Their appearance reflects her transformation, although the chapter does not independently establish a unique perception ability exclusive to Eleanor's eyes.[6]
- Draconic Transformation: Eleanor possesses an evolved Dragon body incorporating her original Monster characteristics. During later Imperial operations, her physical appearance was considerably more imposing than her original Vampire form, reflecting the development of her Draconic physiology.[6][10]
- Supernatural Longevity: Eleanor already possessed the supernatural longevity of a Noble Vampire before becoming a Dragon. Her transformation elevated her into a race whose existence is not governed by ordinary mortal biological aging.[1][6]
- Monster-Lineage Assimilation: Eleanor's former Monster lineage was completely absorbed into the foundation of her Dragon existence. This distinguishes her evolution from an ordinary hybrid possessing several independent racial components and explains why her former abilities developed into the characteristics of her new Draconic nature.[6]

##### Draconic Authorities
Eleanor's evolved Draconic existence is explicitly associated with three principal powers: Space, Corruptible Fire and Earth. These characteristics originated from the interaction between her former Adrasteia heritage and the fundamental reconstruction of her existence during Dragonization. While Earth was already her most developed supernatural specialization, Corruptible Fire represented an evolution of her previous green Monster flames, and Space became another confirmed characteristic of her new Draconic nature.[6]

###### Space Manipulation
- Space Manipulation: Following Dragonization, Eleanor was explicitly described as a Dragon who controls Space. This establishes Spatial Manipulation as a confirmed characteristic of her current existence, although the full range of her individual applications has not been demonstrated.[6]
- Localized Environmental Alteration: During the Imperial operation in Chapter 1084, the Reality surrounding Eleanor's approach to the commander's chair visibly distorted, after which the chair transformed into a dark-green throne with black accents. The scene demonstrates a localized supernatural alteration of her surroundings, but does not explicitly identify whether the effect was produced through her Spatial authority, another Draconic power or a combination of abilities.[10]
Although Eleanor's control over Space is confirmed, unrestricted teleportation, dimensional creation, dimensional travel, spatial sealing and spatial erasure have not been individually established as applications of her personal authority. Such abilities should not be attributed to her merely because other Dragons or Space-related beings have demonstrated them.

###### Corruptible Fire Manipulation
Eleanor possesses an evolved form of supernatural Fire that originated from the green flames associated with her former Dragon-like Monster lineage. After becoming a True Dragon, this Fire developed the ability to corrupt the spiritual existence of its targets rather than merely inflicting physical damage. Its effects were explicitly compared with those of [[Eve Elderblood]]'s Mutant Fire, although the two powers were described as differing in their underlying nature.[4][6]
- Fire Manipulation: Eleanor can generate, control and direct powerful green supernatural flames. Before Dragonization, she used these flames during her Monster transformation and large-scale battles against [[Nightingale]]'s immortal Monsters. Her evolved Fire remains one of the principal powers associated with her Draconic existence.[4][11][6]
- Fire Generation: Eleanor can generate supernatural Fire directly from her body and weapons. During her Awakened Monster transformation, green flames manifested through her greatsword, while she later demonstrated the ability to release the flames directly through her own hands and Draconic body.[4][12][11]
- Fire Projection: Eleanor can project her flames toward distant targets and create large-scale attacks capable of striking multiple enemies. Her aerial green-fire funnel and evolved Dragon Breath demonstrate applications extending beyond direct physical contact.[11][6]
- Fire Shaping: Eleanor can manipulate the shape and movement of her flames, including creating rotating spheres and funnel-like formations capable of drawing surrounding enemies toward the attack.[4][11]
- Large-Scale Fire Attacks: Eleanor's supernatural Fire can affect enormous groups of enemies rather than being restricted to individual targets. Her [[Black Hole|Singularity]] technique expanded into a destructive sphere capable of consuming an approaching Monster army, while her later aerial fire funnel killed hundreds of enemies during the Elder God invasion.[4][11]
- Incineration: Eleanor can burn enemies through direct contact with her green Fire. During the invasion of Warfall, she seized a Monster and enveloped it in green flames, reducing its body to ash.[12]
- Soul Corruption: Eleanor's evolved Corruptible Fire can directly corrupt a target's Soul. This effect distinguishes her Dragon Fire from ordinary flames whose destructive properties are restricted primarily to physical matter.[6]
- Spiritual Damage: Because her evolved flames can affect the Soul, Eleanor can inflict damage extending beyond conventional physical injury. A target's bodily durability alone does not necessarily prevent the spiritual consequences of exposure to her Fire.[6]
- Delayed Death Inducement: A being who survives Eleanor's evolved Dragon Breath can subsequently die from the Corruption remaining within its Soul. The effect was explicitly explained following her Dragonization.[6]
- Dragon Breath: Eleanor can release her Corruptible Fire through her Draconic Breath, producing an attack whose destructive effects include both physical damage and spiritual Corruption.[6]
- Gravitational Fire Vortex (Former Monster application): Before Dragonization, Eleanor could combine her green flames with gravitational attraction to create attacks that drew surrounding enemies into concentrated Fire. Singularity represents the principal demonstrated application of this combination.[4]
The novel establishes that Eleanor's evolved Fire can corrupt Souls, but it does not provide a complete explanation of how the Corruption interacts with every type of spiritual resistance, resurrection, immortality or conceptual existence. Its demonstrated properties should not be treated as unrestricted Soul Erasure or universal negation of spiritual defenses without additional evidence.

Earth Manipulation is Eleanor's oldest and most extensively demonstrated supernatural specialization. Through the Adrasteia bloodline, she can manipulate earth, stone, minerals and terrain on an extraordinary scale, reshaping the environment to suit her military objectives. Unlike elemental powers employed exclusively for direct attacks, Eleanor's control over the land allows her to determine the structure of the battlefield itself, creating obstacles, fortifications, traps, weapons and animated constructs while removing advantages possessed by enemy forces. Her Earth authority remained one of the three principal characteristics of her existence after becoming a True Dragon.[2][13][6]
- Earth Manipulation: Eleanor possesses extraordinary control over the earth and the physical materials contained within it. She can reshape terrain, raise enormous structures, construct weapons and manipulate the battlefield through her Creation techniques.[2][14]
- Geokinesis: Eleanor can directly manipulate soil, stone, rock and other materials forming the surrounding terrain, altering their arrangement and movement according to her intentions. Her techniques include constructing pillars, hands, walls and large defensive structures.[2][14]
- Terrain Manipulation: Eleanor can completely restructure a battlefield, changing the distribution and shape of the land to eliminate enemy advantages. During the Amazon operation, she caused the ground to behave like a fluid substance and transformed the surrounding territory into a large plain suitable for combat.[13]
- Seismic Manipulation: Eleanor can generate powerful earthquakes through her control over the earth. Her Creation techniques repeatedly caused the surrounding terrain to shake violently, while her later battle against the Elder Gods produced earthquakes extending across enormous distances.[2][11]
- Landslide Generation: Eleanor can destabilize mountainous terrain and produce extensive landslides. During the Elder God invasion, her power caused earthquakes and landslides across a region exceeding ten thousand kilometers.[11]
- Mountain Manipulation: Eleanor can manipulate enormous mountain structures. During the attack upon Warfall, she dismantled the mountain dividing Clan Adrasteia's territory and transformed its material into hundreds of gigantic stone warriors.[11]
- Large-Scale Environmental Reconstruction: Eleanor can reshape enormous areas of land for military purposes. Her ability to flatten terrain, remove obstacles and construct defensive structures makes her particularly valuable during campaigns requiring extensive battlefield preparation.[14][13]
- Stone Manipulation: Eleanor can control and reshape stone into complex structures, including enormous hands, pillars, weapons, defensive walls and animated soldiers.[2][11]
- Mineral Manipulation: Eleanor's Creation techniques can incorporate minerals and other materials contained within the ground. During the construction of Babylon, the surrounding stones, minerals, trees and terrain were reorganized as the defensive city emerged.[14]
- Earth Constructs: Eleanor can create enormous structures formed from earth and stone. These constructs can perform different functions depending upon their design, including restraining opponents, protecting allied forces or physically attacking enemies.[2][11]
- Stone Hand Creation: Eleanor can generate thousands of colossal hands from the ground. These hands can seize, restrain, crush and repel enormous opponents, allowing her to control the movements of entire groups of enemies.[2][12]
- Restraint and Immobilization: Eleanor can immobilize targets through constructs formed from the surrounding land. During the Warfall expedition, her stone hands captured an enormous flying Monster and prevented it from escaping despite the creature attempting to burn through the restraints.[2]
- Earth-Based Weapon Creation: Eleanor can construct enormous weapons directly from the surrounding terrain. During the Elder God invasion, she created a gigantic stone greatsword and used it to direct her newly constructed army against the attacking Monsters.[11]
- Structure Creation: Eleanor can create large defensive structures by reshaping existing terrain. Her construction of Babylon demonstrates the ability to reorganize an enormous amount of material into a fortified city designed for military defense.[14]
- Fortress Creation: Eleanor can create fortifications and defensive positions suitable for protecting allied forces against large-scale attacks. Babylon was constructed specifically to provide shelter and strategic advantages during the Warfall expedition.[14]
- Pillar Creation: Eleanor can raise stone pillars from the ground to support structures, interfere with enemy movement or contribute to larger defensive formations. The pillars created during the expedition were subsequently used as supports for Babylon.[2][14]
- Ground Restructuring: Eleanor can alter the distribution of the surrounding land to remove obstacles and reshape the environment. During the Amazon operation, unnecessary structures were swallowed by the earth while the terrain became progressively flatter.[13]
- Golem Creation: Eleanor can construct enormous animated warriors from earth and stone. During the invasion of [[Nightingale]], she transformed an entire mountain into hundreds of giant stone beings possessing tremendous quantities of Energy.[11]
- Construct Animation: Eleanor's created stone giants are capable of moving and fighting rather than remaining stationary structures. The constructs demonstrated sufficient power and mobility to charge onto the battlefield and attack large numbers of Monsters.[11]
- Construct Command: Eleanor can direct the movements and actions of her animated stone soldiers. During the Elder God invasion, she ordered her newly created giants to attack, after which they entered the battlefield and began killing the invading creatures.[11]
- Mass-Crowd Control: Eleanor can use Earth constructs to interfere with enormous enemy formations. During the invasion of Warfall, her A Thousand Hands of Creation technique repelled a mass assault involving hundreds of thousands of Monsters, hurling attacking creatures away from the city's defenses.[12]
- Battlefield Fortification: Eleanor can transform an exposed battlefield into a fortified position, constructing defensive walls, pillars and structures that provide protection against overwhelming enemy numbers.[14]
- Environmental Obstruction: Eleanor's control over terrain allows her to remove favorable positioning from enemy forces by creating barriers, modifying the ground and disrupting movement across the battlefield.[13]
- Terrain-Based Military Support: Eleanor can prepare and maintain battlefield conditions favorable to allied soldiers. Her abilities are especially effective when supporting large armies because the advantages created through environmental restructuring can benefit numerous combatants simultaneously.[14][13]

##### Creation Techniques
Eleanor's Earth Manipulation is most prominently expressed through techniques associated with Creation. Unlike conventional attacks that simply project elemental Energy, these techniques alter the arrangement and movement of existing terrain, allowing Eleanor to construct enormous physical structures and impose her desired configuration upon the battlefield. Their effects became progressively greater as Eleanor developed, culminating in the large-scale manipulation of mountains and the creation of hundreds of animated stone giants.[2][14][11]

###### Creation
- Creation: Eleanor's principal terrain-shaping technique. By directing her Energy into the ground, she can restructure the surrounding earth and convert the environment into a configuration suited to her immediate objective. During the Warfall expedition, Creation was used to prepare the construction of Babylon, while the Amazon operation demonstrated its capacity to flatten and restructure entire battlefields. By Chapter 880, Eleanor's development allowed her to manipulate mountain formations and generate enormous numbers of animated stone constructs.[14][13][11]
- Battlefield Restructuring: Eleanor can transform irregular terrain into open ground, remove obstacles and alter the surrounding environment to improve the positioning of allied forces. During the Amazon operation, the earth behaved almost like a fluid before settling into a vast plain.[13]
- Seismic Generation: The activation of Creation can produce powerful earthquakes as the terrain is transformed. Eleanor's large-scale applications repeatedly generated tremors capable of affecting extensive regions.[14][11]
- Mountain Reconstruction: Eleanor can dismantle mountain structures and repurpose their materials for other applications. During the Elder God assault, an entire mountain was converted into an army of gigantic stone beings.[11]
- Earth Weapon Creation: Eleanor can use Creation to construct enormous weapons from stone, including a greatsword wielded during the battle against the Elder Gods.[11]
- Stone Soldier Creation: Eleanor can transform large quantities of earth and rock into animated giants capable of fighting under her direction. Hundreds of these constructs were created during the attack upon Clan Adrasteia's territory.[11]

###### A Thousand Hands of Creation
- A Thousand Hands of Creation: One of Eleanor's principal large-scale Earth techniques, generating thousands of enormous hands formed from stone, soil and other materials contained within the surrounding ground. The hands can rise toward airborne enemies, pursue their movements and restrain or crush targets that would otherwise possess the mobility necessary to escape conventional terrain-based attacks. During the Warfall expedition, Eleanor used the technique to capture the enormous flying Monster serving as an Alpha's mount. The creature attempted to destroy the restraints using Fire, but the reinforced stone hands endured the attack and subsequently crushed it.[2]
- Massive Stone Hand Generation: Eleanor creates thousands of enormous hands directly from the surrounding terrain, allowing simultaneous attacks against numerous targets or the concentration of overwhelming force upon a single large opponent.[2]
- Aerial Target Restraint: The hands can extend upward to capture flying enemies. Their size and number make them particularly effective against enormous creatures whose bodies are too large to evade the surrounding constructs easily.[2]
- Crushing Attacks: The stone hands can seize and crush trapped opponents. Eleanor's original demonstration destroyed the Alpha's enormous flying mount after preventing its escape.[2]
- Reinforced Constructs: The hands generated during the Warfall expedition displayed considerable durability, resisting the Fire released by the captured flying Monster before destroying it.[2]
- Mass-Army Repulsion: Eleanor later demonstrated the technique during the large-scale invasion of Warfall, raising thousands of hands that caught attacking Monsters, disrupted their formations and threw them away from the city's defenses. The technique was sufficiently powerful to repel an assault involving hundreds of thousands of Monsters, although the expenditure visibly exhausted Eleanor.[12]

###### Order: The City of Babylon
- Order: The City of Babylon: An advanced application of Creation that allows Eleanor to construct an entire fortified city from the surrounding terrain. During the Warfall expedition, she used previously created stone pillars as structural supports while the surrounding stones, minerals, trees and earth were reorganized into an enormous defensive settlement. The completed structure emerged from beneath the ground and provided the Valkyries with prepared positions from which to confront the approaching Monster army.[14]
- City Creation: Eleanor can raise an enormous settlement from the ground by reorganizing existing terrain into defensive structures.[14]
- Fortification Creation: The resulting city is specifically designed for defensive warfare, allowing allied forces to establish protected positions against overwhelming numbers of enemies.[14]
- Large-Scale Material Restructuring: Babylon incorporates stone, minerals and other materials contained within the affected terrain rather than being limited to the manipulation of loose soil.[14]
- Extreme Energy Expenditure: When Eleanor first demonstrated the technique, the expenditure was sufficiently severe that she became pale and collapsed from exhaustion. Anrietha subsequently assisted her recovery while the Valkyries prepared to defend the newly constructed city.[14]

##### Former Monster-Lineage Abilities
Before Dragonization, Eleanor possessed a specialized Monster bloodline acquired through the traditional Adrasteia ritual. The Monster used in her transformation was explicitly described as Dragon-like, with powers associated with Gravity, Fire and Poison. These characteristics formed part of her hybrid Vampire-Monster physiology and were especially prominent when she entered her Awakened Form.[5]
Her subsequent transformation into a True Dragon assimilated the Monster lineage into the foundation of her new existence. The novel thereafter identifies Space, Corruptible Fire and Earth as her principal Draconic powers. Although her previous abilities contributed to her development, Gravity and Poison have not been individually reconfirmed as separate authorities following Dragonization and should therefore remain distinguished from the powers explicitly established for her current form.[6]

###### Monster Physiology
- Monster Physiology: Eleanor formerly possessed the biological and supernatural traits of a powerful Dragon-like Monster incorporated through the Adrasteia ritual. These characteristics existed alongside her Noble Vampire physiology and contributed to the unusual physical development of her bloodline.[1][5]
- Hybrid Physiology: Her original body combined Noble Vampire characteristics with those of the Monster used in her ritual, producing a constitution fundamentally different from that of ordinary Noble Vampires.[1][8]
- Enhanced Physical Characteristics: Clan Adrasteia's hybrids possess strength, durability, speed, agility and senses superior to those of conventional Noble Vampires. Their physical characteristics were compared favorably with those of [[Werewolves]], whose natural bodies generally possess greater physical power than ordinary Vampires.[8]
- Partial Monster Transformation: Eleanor could transform individual body parts without assuming her complete Monster state. During the expedition beyond Warfall, she transformed one of her arms into an enormous black claw while retaining the rest of her humanoid body.[3]
- Full Monster Transformation: Eleanor could transform into a Draconic-looking creature covered in dark-green scales, possessing horns, wings, a tail and reptilian eyes. This form was an Awakened manifestation of her Monster lineage rather than an ordinary Vampire Count transformation.[4]
- Transformation-Based Power Amplification: Eleanor's complete Awakened Form dramatically increased her physical and supernatural capabilities. Its activation altered the surrounding atmosphere and was accompanied by a noticeable increase in the gravitational pressure affecting nearby individuals.[4]
- Claw Manifestation: Eleanor could transform her arm into a massive claw possessing the supernatural characteristics of her Monster lineage, allowing her to fight using natural weapons in addition to her greatsword.[3]
- Draconic Wings: Her Awakened Monster transformation produced Dragon-like wings that granted aerial mobility before her later evolution into a genuine True Dragon.[4]
- Reptilian Scales: Eleanor's complete Monster transformation covered portions of her body in dark-green scales, providing a visibly more armored and monstrous form than her ordinary Vampire appearance.[4]
- Enhanced Predatory Instincts: Eleanor's Monster transformation was accompanied by a more aggressive and battle-oriented temperament, reflecting the influence of the powerful creature incorporated into her bloodline.[3][4]

###### Gravity Manipulation
- Gravity Manipulation: Eleanor explicitly identified Gravity as one of the powers associated with the Dragon-like Monster incorporated into her original bloodline. Its effects were especially prominent during her complete Awakened Form.[4][5]
- Gravity Amplification: Eleanor's transformation could increase the gravitational pressure affecting her surroundings. When she began releasing her Monster power, nearby observers perceived the atmosphere becoming heavier, with the local gravity appearing to increase several times.[4]
- Gravitational Attraction: Eleanor could create forces that drew nearby objects and creatures toward a designated location. Her [[Black Hole|Singularity]] technique demonstrated this by pulling airborne Monsters toward a rotating sphere of green Fire.[4]
- Gravity-Based Crowd Control: Eleanor could use gravitational attraction to interfere with the movements of multiple enemies simultaneously. Singularity exploited this characteristic to draw approaching Monsters into a concentrated destructive attack.[4]
- Telekinesis-like Object Manipulation: Eleanor demonstrated the ability to move physical objects without touching them. During the Warfall expedition, she caused the corpse of a decapitated Ogre to levitate before hurling it toward another Monster. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] described the effect as Telekinesis, but Eleanor clarified that it was merely something similar and originated from her Monster bloodline. Its exact mechanism was not identified.[3]

###### Original Monster Fire
- Fire Manipulation: Eleanor's original Monster lineage allowed her to generate and manipulate dark-green supernatural flames. These flames could be projected through her weapon or body and combined with the gravitational characteristics of her Awakened Form.[4][5]
- Flame Generation: Eleanor could generate green Fire through her greatsword and concentrate the resulting flames into large destructive formations.[4]
- Flame Absorption: Her [[Black Hole|Singularity]] technique drew surrounding enemies into a green Fire sphere that grew larger as additional Monsters were consumed by the flames. The growth was directly associated with the creatures entering the attack.[4]
- Fire Expansion and Contraction: Eleanor could expand and reduce the size of the flame sphere formed by Singularity through deliberate commands, allowing the technique to transition from a concentrated sphere into a much larger destructive attack before being contracted again.[4]
- Fire Resistance: Her original Monster bloodline granted exceptionally high resistance to Fire, although Eleanor explicitly clarified that it was not complete immunity. Sufficiently powerful Fire could still injure her.[5]

###### Poison Manipulation
- Poison Manipulation: Eleanor identified Poison as one of the principal powers belonging to her original Dragon-like Monster lineage. Although the ability was confirmed as part of her inherited characteristics, the novel has not demonstrated enough individual applications to establish its exact potency, delivery methods, range or versatility.[5]
The existence of Poison within her former Monster bloodline does not establish universal Poison Immunity, resistance to every venom or control over all toxic substances. During the Demonic War, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] specifically warned Eleanor that a supernatural poisoned projectile targeting their group could have killed her if she had been struck, demonstrating that her original Monster constitution did not provide unrestricted protection against every form of poison.[15]

###### Singularity
- [[Black Hole|Singularity]]: Eleanor's most prominent demonstrated technique originating from her original Dragon-like Monster lineage. Upon activating the technique, the surrounding environment appeared to lose its ordinary colors, leaving Eleanor's green flames as the principal visible source of power. The flames concentrated into a rotating sphere resembling a miniature green sun, which subsequently generated a powerful attractive force that pulled surrounding Monsters toward it. As creatures entered the sphere, its Fire expanded and consumed them. Eleanor could then deliberately enlarge the sphere to engulf a much greater area before contracting it until the attack disappeared.[4]
- Flame Concentration: Eleanor condensed her green Fire into a rotating sphere suspended above the battlefield.[4]
- Gravitational Attraction: The sphere drew surrounding Monsters toward itself, preventing the approaching enemies from freely escaping its destructive influence.[4]
- Growth Through Consumption: The Fire sphere grew larger as Monsters were pulled into and consumed by its flames.[4]
- Expansion: Eleanor could command the sphere to expand, causing it to engulf additional Monsters, rocks, soil and other materials within the affected region.[4]
- Contraction: Eleanor could subsequently reduce the size of the sphere until it disappeared, ending the attack after the surrounding enemies had been destroyed.[4]
- Large-Scale Destruction: The technique was capable of eliminating an enormous Monster formation in a short period, demonstrating the destructive potential of combining her Fire with gravitational attraction.[4]
Despite its name and appearance, Singularity has not been explicitly identified as a literal astrophysical black hole. The demonstrated effects involve gravitational attraction, supernatural Fire and the manipulation of the attack's size.

##### Adrasteia Bloodline
Clan Adrasteia developed an unusual Vampire bloodline through the combination of ancient Draconic ancestry and specialized Monster rituals. Its members possess a unique Energy capable of permanently killing [[Nightingale]]'s immortal Monsters, while their hybrid physiology grants physical characteristics considerably stronger than those of ordinary Noble Vampires. Eleanor was one of the Clan's principal representatives and possessed both the general capabilities of this lineage and the extraordinary characteristics of the Direct Bloodline descended from the First Adrasteia.[1][8][16]
- [[Adrasteia Bloodline]]: Eleanor possesses the supernatural heritage of Clan Adrasteia, including the Monster-related physiological characteristics developed through its traditional ritual and the ancestral Dragon Blood inherited from the First Adrasteia.[1][16]
- Unique Energy Manipulation: Members of Clan Adrasteia possess specialized Energy effective against Nightingale's otherwise immortal Monsters. Eleanor can utilize this characteristic to inflict fatal damage upon creatures whose regenerative abilities would otherwise prevent permanent death.[1]
- Regeneration Negation: The special Adrasteia Energy can prevent Nightingale's Monsters from recovering from fatal injuries. This effect allows Eleanor and other members of the Clan to kill creatures that would ordinarily regenerate after being physically destroyed.[1]
- Limited [[Immortality]] Negation: Eleanor possesses a demonstrated means of permanently killing the specific immortal Monsters against which the Adrasteia bloodline was developed. The effect should not be interpreted as universal negation of every possible form of immortality.[1]
- Weapon Empowerment: Eleanor can employ her specialized Energy through conventional physical attacks and weapons. During her first demonstration against Nightingale's Monsters, she used her greatsword to inflict damage that prevented the creatures from simply regenerating.[1]
- Monster Bloodline Integration: Eleanor underwent the Clan's secret ritual to incorporate a powerful Monster into her body and Soul, developing a hybrid existence possessing additional characteristics beyond her original Vampire bloodline.[5]
- Monster Flesh and Blood Immunity (Former Hybrid Constitution): Adrasteia hybrids can safely consume the flesh and blood of Nightingale's Monsters despite the harmful effects those substances possess for ordinary Vampires. The Clan's members obtain nourishment primarily from Monsters and Demonic Beasts rather than Human blood.[8][17]
- Enhanced Physical Attributes: Clan Adrasteia's hybrid physiology provides physical strength, durability, speed and senses superior to those of ordinary Noble Vampires. Their bodies were compared with [[Werewolves]], whose natural physiques are generally stronger than those of conventional Vampires.[8]
- Ancestral Dragon Blood: Eleanor belongs to the Main Lineage of the First Adrasteia, a Dragon Rider whose descendants inherited dormant Draconic characteristics. Her original Monster transformation displayed an unusually Draconic appearance because of the interaction between this ancestry and the Monster incorporated through the Clan ritual.[16]
- Monster-Hunting Adaptation: Eleanor's heritage provides a physical and supernatural foundation particularly effective against Nightingale's immortal Monsters, allowing her to fight, kill and consume creatures that present severe dangers to other Vampire lineages.[1][8][17]
The Adrasteia bloodline's unique Energy and Monster-killing characteristics are confirmed through Eleanor's former hybrid state. Following her transformation into a True Dragon, the Monster lineage was absorbed into her new existence, but the novel has not separately explained every interaction between her former specialized Energy and the fundamental abilities of her evolved Dragon body.[6]

##### Former Noble Vampire Physiology
Before becoming a Blood Dragon, Eleanor was an ancient Noble Vampire and possessed the general supernatural characteristics of her race. These abilities existed alongside her Adrasteia Monster physiology and formed the foundation of her development before Dragonization.
- Noble Vampire Physiology: Eleanor possessed the supernatural biological characteristics of a Noble Vampire, including enhanced physical attributes, extraordinary longevity and regenerative capabilities.[1]
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Her Vampire body possessed strength, speed, agility, reflexes, durability and stamina far beyond those of ordinary Humans, which were further enhanced by her Adrasteia hybrid physiology.[8]
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Eleanor possessed the regenerative characteristics associated with Vampires, allowing her body to recover from injuries that would be fatal to ordinary Humans.[1]
- Supernatural Longevity: Eleanor possessed the extended lifespan associated with Noble Vampires and did not age according to ordinary Human biological limitations.[1]
- Enhanced Senses: Eleanor possessed superior Vampire senses that were further strengthened by the Monster characteristics of Clan Adrasteia.[8]
- Blood Consumption: Eleanor could obtain nourishment through blood as a Vampire, although members of Clan Adrasteia preferred Monster and Demonic-Beast blood rather than Human blood.[8]

##### Former Awakened Transformation
Eleanor's original Monster transformation was an Awakened manifestation of the bloodline incorporated through the Adrasteia ritual. Although it shared certain features with a Vampire Count transformation, Rose explicitly distinguished the two because Eleanor was manifesting the characteristics of her Monster lineage rather than merely accessing the ordinary ancestral form associated with other [[Vampire Counts]].[4]
- Awakened Monster Form: Eleanor could fully manifest the characteristics of the Dragon-like Monster incorporated into her body, acquiring scales, horns, wings, a tail and reptilian features.[4]
- Partial Transformation: Eleanor could manifest individual Monster characteristics without assuming her complete Awakened Form, including transforming an arm into a gigantic black claw.[3]
- Physical Amplification: Her complete transformation substantially increased her supernatural capabilities, allowing her to employ more powerful versions of her Monster-related abilities.[4]
- Gravity Amplification: The activation of her complete form increased the gravitational pressure affecting her surroundings, demonstrating the influence of the Monster's Gravity-related characteristics.[4]
- Green Fire Generation: Eleanor could generate enormous quantities of green supernatural Fire while transformed, using it directly or combining it with gravitational forces through [[Black Hole|Singularity]].[4]
- Winged Flight: The transformation produced Dragon-like wings that allowed Eleanor to move through the air without requiring an external means of transportation.[4]
- Predatory Transformation: Her complete form manifested visibly monstrous characteristics that intensified her already imposing presence as one of [[Nightingale]]'s Vampire Counts.[4]

#### Combat Skills
Eleanor is an exceptionally experienced military commander and greatsword combatant whose martial abilities were developed through years of training under [[Scathach]] and continual warfare against [[Nightingale]]'s Monsters. Unlike warriors who primarily rely upon personal combat strength, Eleanor combines direct fighting with the ability to control the environment and coordinate large formations, allowing her to influence battles far beyond the immediate reach of her weapon.[18][1][10]
- Master Combatant: Eleanor possesses extensive experience fighting supernatural Monsters, powerful Vampires and other dangerous beings. Her responsibilities as Countess required continual warfare rather than occasional participation in organized tournaments or political conflicts.[1][2]
- Greatsword Mastery: Eleanor specializes in an exceptionally heavy greatsword developed for combat against Nightingale's Monsters. She can wield the weapon with considerable skill and integrate its attacks with her Earth and Monster-related powers.[18][9][3]
- Weapon Handling: Eleanor possesses the strength and experience necessary to wield weapons considerably larger and heavier than those commonly used by ordinary Vampires. Her preferred greatsword was explicitly described as unsuitable for untrained individuals because of its extraordinary weight.[9]
- Close-Quarters Combat: Eleanor can fight directly against powerful supernatural opponents using her physical strength, greatsword and Monster transformation. She demonstrated this during her confrontations with the Monsters surrounding Warfall.[1][3]
- Monster Hunting Mastery: Eleanor possesses extensive knowledge of Nightingale's Monsters, including their biology, combat behavior, regenerative characteristics and the methods necessary to kill them permanently. This expertise developed through her responsibility for defending Warfall.[1][2]
- Demonic-Beast Recognition: Eleanor can distinguish between Nightingale's Monsters and other supernatural creatures such as Demonic Beasts. During her encounter with the gorilla protecting Roxanne's original tree, she recognized that the creature belonged to a different classification from the Monsters traditionally exterminated by her Clan.[19]
- Battlefield Manipulation: Eleanor can modify the environment during combat, eliminating advantageous positions held by enemies and creating defensive structures for her allies. This makes her especially dangerous during engagements involving large armies.[14][13]
- Area Control: Eleanor can influence enormous sections of the battlefield through Earth Manipulation, allowing her to affect multiple enemy formations instead of restricting herself to individual opponents.[12][11]
- Military Leadership: Eleanor governed Warfall and directed the forces of Clan Adrasteia, including the Valkyries and conventional military units responsible for the defense of Nightingale's western frontier.[1]
- Tactical Intelligence: Eleanor can evaluate battlefield conditions and make decisions based upon the survival of her forces. During the deep Warfall expedition, the group withdrew after encountering approximately twenty enormous [[Titans]] instead of pursuing an unnecessary confrontation in hostile territory.[20]
- Strategic Planning: Eleanor possesses experience planning military operations against powerful supernatural opponents. During the later Imperial campaign against an extraterrestrial civilization, she selected a strategy targeting the enemy's Divine leadership rather than relying exclusively upon prolonged conventional warfare.[10]
- Command and Coordination: Eleanor can organize and direct specialized military forces, assigning targets and responsibilities according to individual capabilities. During the Imperial operation in Chapter 1084, she assigned Rose to confront the enemy's strongest Primordial God while directing other forces against the remaining defenders.[10]
- Siege Warfare: Eleanor's abilities allow her to construct or destroy fortifications, reshape defensive positions and alter terrain surrounding fortified enemies. Her experience with Warfall and her creation of Babylon demonstrate the military applications of these techniques.[14]
- Defensive Warfare: Eleanor possesses extensive experience defending settlements against overwhelming Monster populations. Her abilities are particularly effective when fortifications, terrain control and coordinated military units can be used together.[1][12]
- Large-Scale Warfare: Eleanor has demonstrated the ability to influence battles involving enormous numbers of enemies, including the Warfall invasion in which she used A Thousand Hands of Creation to repel hundreds of thousands of Monsters.[12]
- Military Administration: As the former Vampire Countess responsible for Warfall, Eleanor possessed the organizational experience necessary to maintain a permanently militarized territory whose survival depended upon continuous defensive preparations.[1]
- Imperial Military Command: Following the expansion of the [[Elderblood Empire]], Eleanor was recognized as one of the alternate commanders of the First Legion, the Spartan Legion, which served as one of the Empire's principal offensive military formations.[21]

#### Non-Combat Skills
Eleanor's long experience as a ruler and military commander extends beyond personal combat. Her responsibilities required maintaining the infrastructure, defensive organization and social stability of Warfall, while her later Imperial position involved coordinating military operations across foreign civilizations.
- Leadership: Eleanor possesses extensive experience directing Clan Adrasteia and maintaining the authority necessary to govern a society organized around constant warfare.[1]
- Military Administration: Eleanor can oversee the organization and maintenance of military forces, fortifications and defensive operations. Warfall's continued survival depended upon the coordination of its soldiers and supporting civilian institutions.[22][1]
- Tactical Planning: Eleanor can assess enemy capabilities and determine appropriate combat strategies, including when to advance, establish defenses or retreat.[20][10]
- Battlefield Engineering: Eleanor can use her Earth Manipulation for non-offensive construction, including the creation of defensive cities, fortified positions and large-scale terrain alterations required for military operations.[14]
- Monster Biology Knowledge: Eleanor possesses detailed knowledge of [[Nightingale]]'s Monsters, their physical characteristics and the differences separating them from Demonic Beasts and other supernatural creatures.[19][1]
- Monster Bloodline Knowledge: Eleanor understands the principles and restrictions governing the Adrasteia ritual, including its biological consequences, compatibility requirements, limitation to a single successful incorporation and the secrecy imposed upon its use.[5]
- Supernatural Equipment Procurement: Eleanor possesses experience obtaining and distributing equipment appropriate for the extreme threats facing Warfall. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s offer of sixty-nine replicas of the [[Hammer of Hephaestus]] demonstrated the importance of powerful artifacts to the Clan's military infrastructure.[23]
- Equipment Provision: Eleanor provided Victor with specially made black armor designed around his abilities and protection requirements. The armor incorporated additional enhancements, including Rune-related improvements and systems suited to his particular style of combat.[24]
- Military Logistics: Eleanor possesses experience maintaining military operations in dangerous territory, including the preparation of expeditions and management of defensive resources necessary for Warfall's continued survival.[1][25]
- Interstellar Military Strategy: Eleanor later demonstrated the ability to adapt her experience to Imperial campaigns involving extraterrestrial civilizations, Divine defenders and planetary conquest.[10]

#### Resistances and Immunities
Eleanor possesses resistance to several forms of damage through her former Adrasteia hybrid constitution and later True Dragon physiology. However, her confirmed resistances must be distinguished from powers that have not been demonstrated individually, particularly because her original Monster bloodline did not provide absolute protection against every supernatural substance or attack.
- Fire Resistance: Eleanor's original Dragon-like Monster bloodline granted her exceptionally high resistance to Fire. She explicitly clarified that the resistance was not complete immunity and acknowledged that sufficiently powerful Fire could still injure her.[5]
- Resistance to Physical Damage: Eleanor's former Adrasteia hybrid body possessed physical durability superior to that of ordinary Noble Vampires and comparable to the strength of Werewolf physiology. Her later True Dragon transformation considerably enhanced her physical constitution.[8][6]
- Immunity to [[Nightingale]] Monster Flesh and Blood (Former Hybrid Physiology): Members of Clan Adrasteia can safely consume the flesh and blood of Nightingale's Monsters despite their harmful effects upon ordinary Vampires. This characteristic forms part of the hybrid physiology developed through the Clan's traditional ritual.[8][17]
- Resistance to Monster-Related Biological Hazards (Former Hybrid Physiology): Eleanor's Adrasteia constitution was adapted to the consumption and handling of Monster flesh and blood that ordinary Vampires could not safely consume. This resistance is specifically associated with Nightingale's Monsters rather than every biological toxin or supernatural poison.[8][17]
- Regenerative Recovery (Former Noble Vampire Physiology): Eleanor possessed the regenerative characteristics associated with Noble Vampires, allowing recovery from injuries within the limits of her supernatural constitution.[1]
- Enhanced Draconic Durability: Eleanor's transformation into a True Dragon provided a superior physical and supernatural foundation, incorporating her former Monster lineage into her new existence.[6]
The available chapters do not establish that Eleanor possesses universal immunity to Poison, Fire, Soul attacks, Spatial Manipulation or every form of physical damage. Her original resistance to Fire was explicitly described as incomplete, while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] warned that a poisoned projectile encountered during the Demonic War could have killed her if it had struck her directly.[5][15]

#### Equipment

##### Adrasteia Greatsword
Eleanor's principal weapon during her life as a Vampire Countess was an enormous greatsword designed for the extraordinary enemies inhabiting [[Nightingale]]'s western territories. The weapon was exceptionally heavy, and Eleanor explained that ordinary Vampires could not easily lift it without specialized training. Its unusual weight reflected its intended function: it was not originally constructed for conventional combat against other Vampires, but for the dangerous creatures that Clan Adrasteia had been entrusted with exterminating.[18][9]
- Greatsword Mastery: Eleanor can wield the weapon efficiently despite its enormous weight, combining conventional sword attacks with her superior physical strength and Monster-related abilities.[18][3]
- Anti-Monster Combat: The greatsword was designed for the enemies traditionally fought by Clan Adrasteia rather than ordinary Vampire opponents. Its use against Nightingale's Monsters demonstrates its role in the Clan's specialized military equipment.[9][1]
- Energy Channeling: Eleanor can employ her specialized Adrasteia Energy through weapon attacks, allowing fatal injuries against Monsters possessing extraordinary regenerative or immortal characteristics.[1]
- Monster Transformation Compatibility: Eleanor continued wielding her greatsword while partially manifesting her Monster traits, combining the weapon with her transformed physical characteristics.[3]
- [[Black Hole|Singularity]] Catalyst: During the demonstration of Singularity, Eleanor's green Monster flames were concentrated through her greatsword before forming the rotating sphere above the battlefield.[4]

##### Combat Armor
Eleanor traditionally employs combat armor suited to her responsibilities as the military leader of Warfall. The equipment reflects the Clan's emphasis upon practical protection and survivability rather than purely ceremonial appearances. Her forces are similarly associated with heavy black armor and weapons designed for prolonged engagements against powerful Monsters.[18][22]

##### Armor Provided to Victor
Eleanor provided [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] with a specially prepared set of black armor that he wore during the Demonic War. Unlike his earlier expedition equipment, this armor was designed around his particular supernatural capabilities and the need for protection during increasingly dangerous encounters. Its construction emphasized a close-fitting design that allowed Victor to utilize his powers without sacrificing mobility, while additional enhancements improved its effectiveness.[24]
The armor represented Eleanor's concern for Victor despite her inability to accompany him personally during that stage of the conflict. Its existence also demonstrated her familiarity with the specialized equipment required by powerful supernatural warriors. The Rune-based modifications and additional features associated with the completed armor should not automatically be treated as techniques Eleanor personally performed without confirmation of their individual creators.[24]

##### Hammer of Hephaestus Replicas
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] provided Clan Adrasteia with sixty-nine replicas of the [[Hammer of Hephaestus]] in exchange for Eleanor agreeing to accompany him on Monster hunts. Although the replicas were substantially weaker than the original Divine Artifact, they possessed exceptional destructive capabilities and were valuable as large-scale weapons against the Monsters threatening Warfall. The weapons became part of the Clan's military resources rather than being established as Eleanor's personal everyday equipment.[23][26]

##### Chloe
Chloe is one of the supernatural horses associated with Clan Adrasteia's military forces. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became particularly attached to Chloe during his first meeting with Eleanor, demonstrating the importance of specialized supernatural mounts to the Clan's military culture. Chloe should be classified as a living mount associated with Eleanor's forces rather than an ordinary artifact or personal weapon.[18]
