Dun Scaith is an ancient Ascended Goddess, legendary Runemaster, Rune Scholar and the original master of [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]]. Historically remembered as the Witch of Doncaster, Dun began as a Witch and dedicated her existence so completely to Runes that she eventually attained Divinity through her own effort and Enlightenment in the discipline, becoming the Goddess of Runes.[3][2][4]
Dun is one of the greatest specialists in Standard Runes introduced in the series. [[Scathach]] describes her old master as a "monster" in her craft, comparable to [[Merlin]] in Energy control and Victor Elderblood in combat talent.[5] By Chapter 1092, Scathach had already reached Grandmaster-level comprehension of the Standard Runic Script, yet Dun remained explicitly one level above her in that same Script.[9] Her mastery is the product of immense scholarship rather than an inherently Runic race: unlike [[True Dragons]], Dun had to study, inscribe and refine Runes manually throughout her life.
Her specialization is so complete that she can perform simpler Runic effects through speech rather than physically carving every symbol, something noted as an extreme ability even among experienced Rune Masters.[10] Complex high-level Artifacts remain far more demanding and require Dun to carefully engrave intricate Runic structures for extended periods, demonstrating that even her Runic Divinity does not eliminate the technical requirements of sophisticated Runecraft.[10]
Dun's knowledge profoundly shaped Scathach's development. Her training methods deliberately stripped her disciple of supernatural advantages so that talent, regeneration and Vampire physiology could not substitute for technique. Scathach later passed much of the same philosophy to Victor, meaning Dun indirectly influenced Victor's early development long before personally meeting him. The relationship eventually became circular: Dun taught Scathach, Scathach taught Victor, and Victor later became Dun's teacher after acquiring Draconic, Eldritch and other Runic systems inaccessible to her original existence.[11][7]
Dun's relationship with Victor initially developed through intellectual fascination rather than romance. Seeing him combine Draconic and Demonic Runic principles, write Runes through methods that violated conventional assumptions and develop technologies integrating multiple Runic Scripts reignited a scholarly obsession that centuries of accumulated knowledge had struggled to satisfy.[5][11] Over time, fascination became attraction. By Chapter 1123, Dun openly accepted [[Violet Elderblood Snow]]'s invitation to court Victor and declared that she intended to become the Emperor's wife, while acknowledging that genuine love would need to develop through continued interaction rather than being fabricated for political or intellectual convenience.[8]

### Physical Appearance
Dun is a beautiful woman with long light-brown hair, brown eyes and fair skin. Despite her immense age, she retains the youthful appearance characteristic of powerful Divine beings. When she first appears directly in Chapter 919, she is described as a brown-haired woman whose appearance Vlad recognizes from an encounter dating back to the period before her ascension into Godhood.[4]
Dun generally dresses considerably more simply than figures such as [[Scathach]] or the various Imperial Empresses. During her first direct appearance, her clothes are described as comparatively modern, emphasizing that despite her ancient origin and centuries of isolation she is not permanently attached to an archaic style.[4]
Her relatively ordinary humanoid appearance conceals an exceptionally powerful Soul and Divine existence. Chapter 1092 establishes that Dun's Soul is considerably stronger than those possessed by ordinary naturally born Gods and is stated to exceed even many [[Primordial Gods]], although it remains inferior to the Soul and Bloodline advantages possessed by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Dragon Gods.[9]
Official artwork depicts a later Dragon Goddess form for Dun. However, the novel explicitly establishes in Chapter 1092 that she had not yet become a Dragon Goddess at that point. Dun personally asked Victor to transform her after discovering that her body, Bloodline and Soul were beginning to limit how much higher Runic information she could process, but Victor refused because he only shared his Blood Dragon lineage with his wives.[9] Although Dun subsequently accepted the opportunity to court Victor and later appears increasingly integrated into his private family circle, a direct transformation scene has not yet been established in the reviewed chapter material through Chapter 1170. The Dragon Goddess artwork should therefore not by itself be treated as proof that the transformation has already occurred.

### Personality
Dun is defined above all by an obsessive desire to learn. Runes are not simply her preferred branch of [[Magic]]; they are the field around which she shaped her life, her identity and eventually her Divinity. She studied them intensely enough to attain Enlightenment and ascend into Godhood, yet becoming Divine did not reduce that hunger for knowledge.[2]
Her obsession becomes especially visible when she encounters Runic information outside her existing framework. During the Third [[Supernatural Gathering]], Dun watches [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] combine Draconic and Demonic principles and immediately recognizes that his methods violate several assumptions of ordinary Runecraft. Rather than rejecting the unfamiliar technique, she becomes excited by the existence of something she does not understand and leaves her isolation specifically to seek Victor out.[5][4]
Dun possesses the stereotypical greed for knowledge often associated with Witches but combines it with an unusually strong sense of personal honor. When Victor deliberately tempted her with access to his advanced Rune research, Dun became desperate enough to offer almost anything he wanted, but immediately drew a line when he jokingly asked about her body. Dun stated that she was an honorable woman, warrior and teacher and would only give herself to the husband and man she personally acknowledged.[11] Her thirst for knowledge is therefore extreme without completely overriding her principles.
She is also intensely competitive. Dun reacts poorly when limitations are described as something she simply cannot overcome. When Victor warned that her Divine body and Soul could not safely process the highest Runic information available to his Dragon Gods, Dun's first response was not resignation but a request to be transformed into a Dragon Goddess herself.[9]
At the same time, Dun lacks the intellectual arrogance that would prevent her from learning from someone younger. Once Victor demonstrates superior knowledge of Draconic and higher Runic systems, she willingly becomes his student despite being the woman who originally taught [[Scathach]]. She cares more about gaining genuine understanding than preserving the appearance of always being the greatest authority in the room.[7]
Dun is an extremely harsh teacher. Her training philosophy deliberately removes the advantages that allow talented students to hide deficiencies in technique. During Scathach's apprenticeship, Dun used limitation Runes to suppress her supernatural abilities toward Human levels and then forced her through dangerous survival and combat exercises. Scathach remembered experiences such as attempting to fight bears while stripped of her normal Vampire advantages.[11]
The brutality had a purpose. Dun wanted her disciple to understand that racial superiority, regeneration and raw strength could disappear or be countered. True competence had to remain even when every supernatural advantage was removed. Scathach later adopted a similar philosophy when training others, particularly Victor and the younger members of the Elderblood family.
Despite the severity of their history, Dun genuinely cares for Scathach. She takes visible pleasure from discovering that her old disciple still uses a proximity Rune Dun created for her centuries earlier and immediately begins teasing Scathach with her former name, Siren, when they reunite.[12][11] Their relationship has the familiarity of two ancient, stubborn women who understand exactly how far they can provoke one another.
Dun also possesses a scholarly form of absent-mindedness. When concentrating upon Runecraft she can become almost completely detached from her surroundings. Victor once observed her inscribing Runes upon an Artifact with such extreme focus that she was not even breathing normally, causing him to decide against interrupting her.[10]
Her feelings toward Victor develop from admiration, attraction and intellectual compatibility rather than instantaneous romance. Dun openly acknowledges in Chapter 1123 that she likes Victor and finds him attractive, but she does not pretend to possess the centuries of emotional attachment shared by several of his established wives. She accepts Violet's invitation because she wants the opportunity to know him more deeply and allows the possibility of love to develop naturally.[8]

### Background
Dun's precise birthplace, family, original Human identity and chronological age remain unknown.
She was originally a Witch and was already active long before the modern story. Chapter 919 reveals that Vlad encountered Dun in the distant past while she was still Human. At the time, rumors described her as a Human woman who trained heroes. The younger Vlad briefly investigated her but dismissed her because she appeared unremarkable to him, a judgment he later regretted after seeing what Dun ultimately became.[4]
Dun became historically associated with Doncaster and was remembered as the Witch of Doncaster.[3] She built a reputation as a teacher of warriors and a specialist in Runes before accepting the young Siren—later known as [[Scathach]]—as a disciple.
Her training of Siren became one of the most important educational relationships in the history of Earth's supernatural world. Dun placed limitation Runes upon the young Vampire to suppress the advantages of her Race and forced her to master combat, survival and Runic principles through genuine personal skill.[11]
After completing her training under Dun, Siren adopted the name Scathach herself. Dun did not bestow the name upon her; Scathach explicitly tells [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] that she changed her own name after finishing Dun's training.[11] Earlier interpretations that Dun personally renamed her disciple should therefore be treated as incorrect.
Dun continued pursuing Runic knowledge after Scathach departed. Her dedication eventually produced Enlightenment, allowing her to ascend naturally into Divinity. Unlike Gods born directly from Divine lineages, Dun's Godhood was achieved through personal development and realization of her chosen discipline.[2]
Runes consequently became more than something she used. They became the Concept through which her existence ascended, eventually establishing Dun as the Goddess of Runes.[4]

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Dun does not personally enter the main narrative during this period, but her influence is already visible through [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]]. [[Scathach]]'s extraordinary standards as both warrior and teacher originate partly from the mysterious master responsible for training her before she became the legendary Countess known throughout [[Nightingale]]. Dun's methods established the principle that extraordinary racial talent should never become an excuse for poor technique, a philosophy Scathach later carried into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s own training.[13]

#### Demonic War
Dun's name is first revealed when the history of [[Gae Bolg]] and Clan Scarlett's Runic tradition is discussed. [[Scathach]]'s spear was created through enormous numbers of synchronized Runes and derived its name from the tradition associated with the Witch who once served as her master, [[Dún Scáith|Dun Scaith]].[1] The connection establishes Dun as one of the foundational figures behind Scathach's Runic knowledge long before she personally enters the story.
Later discussions reveal more of Dun's reputation. [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood identifies Scathach's teacher as the Witch of Doncaster and describes Runes as an art sufficiently complex and dangerous that a master capable of using them freely with supernatural power could threaten even God-King-level beings.[3] Dun had already become legendary enough that ancient Divine beings such as Aphrodite knew of her history despite Dun remaining isolated from much of contemporary supernatural politics.

#### King Among Kings
The nature of Dun's ascension is clarified during discussions concerning Enlightenment and the different paths through which beings attain Divinity. Unlike Gods born Divine, Dun achieved Godhood through personal effort and Enlightenment in Runes, confirming that her Runic obsession had literally transformed her state of existence.[2]
Dun finally makes her direct debut during the Third [[Supernatural Gathering]]. She watches the broadcast from the comfort of an ordinary home while drinking tea and immediately becomes interested when she sees [[Scathach]] beside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Dun had long believed her disciple's standards were so unreasonable that Scathach might remain alone permanently, so discovering that she had finally chosen a man was already surprising. Victor's Runecraft quickly becomes far more important to her, however.[5]
Dun watches Victor manipulate Runes in ways that contradict normal practice. He combines Draconic and Demonic Runic principles, uses Creation to layer structures upon one another and performs effects that even ancient Rune specialists would normally regard as impossible. Dun recognizes that Victor is still developing his formal Runic knowledge but also understands that his natural aptitude and Draconic existence give him access to methods other Rune users simply do not possess.[5]
The spectacle reminds Dun why Scathach once described three individuals as monsters within their fields: Dun herself in Runes, [[Merlin]] in Energy control and Victor in [[Martial Arts]]. Victor's developing Runic ability is sufficient to reignite Dun's scholarly passion, and she decides she must meet him personally.[5]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Dun emerges from seclusion after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s transformation of his family begins producing numerous [[Blood Dragons]]. She enters [[Nightingale]] while concealing herself through Runes and encounters [[Kali Elderblood]], who has arrived for the same general reason: both women want to meet Victor.[4] Dun cannot initially locate Dragons Nest despite her expertise and notes that Nightingale's defensive structure is far more complicated than the systems she remembers. She and Kali eventually reveal themselves to [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] and demand assistance contacting Victor.
Vlad recognizes Dun from the distant past and recalls having met her while she was still Human. At the time, he had heard rumors about a Human woman who trained heroes but dismissed her after deciding she did not appear particularly special. Confronted centuries later with the Goddess of Runes, Vlad privately recognizes how badly his younger self misjudged her potential.[4]
Dun senses [[Scathach]]'s eventual approach through an old Rune she had given her disciple, taking private satisfaction in the knowledge that Scathach still uses her gift. When Victor, [[Aphrodite]] and Scathach begin speaking Draconic, Dun identifies the language despite being unable to understand it. Even for someone with extraordinary linguistic and Runic knowledge, Draconic Language is fundamentally different because the words of a Dragon impose the speaker's [[Will Solace|Will]] upon Reality rather than functioning as ordinary communication.[12]
Victor's deeper existence proves even more difficult to process. When he exposes aspects of the abnormal form associated with his [[Chaos]] nature while helping Kali, Dun is forced to close her eyes because her senses cannot properly comprehend what she is seeing.[12]
Afterward, Dun reunites properly with Scathach and immediately addresses her as Siren. Scathach angrily tells her to stop using the old name and explains to Victor that she personally changed it after completing Dun's training.[11] Their interaction quickly demonstrates that centuries of separation did little to alter the teacher-student relationship: Dun still knows exactly how to irritate Scathach, while Scathach is fully willing to threaten her former master when the teasing becomes excessive.
Victor then demonstrates experimental weapons containing interconnected Runic systems. Dun can identify the function of several structures through experience but cannot directly read the Draconic Runes. The sight of opposing Runic principles functioning together inside a single device drives her scholarly obsession into overdrive. She begs Victor to let her join his faction so she can study the technology and knowledge surrounding him.[11]
Victor deliberately tempts Dun with increasingly advanced projects, including concepts for Runic spacecraft and hybrid technology. Dun offers almost anything in return for access. When Victor asks whether "anything" includes her body, Dun immediately refuses and explains that intimacy is reserved for the husband and man she acknowledges. Victor respects the answer and recognizes that despite her obsessive personality she retains firm personal principles.[11]
Dun is eventually allowed access under strict conditions and enters Victor's personal territory alongside Kali. The newly created Blood Dragons shock both women because Victor has begun producing an entire civilization of [[True Dragons]] rather than merely transforming one or two exceptional individuals.[14][15]
Dun also becomes involved in [[Anna Elderblood]]'s training. Anna's newly awakened Dragon abilities allow spoken intention and Runes to distort Reality according to her subconscious understanding, making improper use extraordinarily dangerous. Victor therefore identifies Scathach, Zaladrac and Dun as the specialists best suited to train her: Scathach for control and discipline, Zaladrac for Draconic understanding and Dun for formal Runic knowledge.[6]
At this point Dun is not yet considered a complete member of Victor's family. Roxanne explicitly notes that Dun is not a "full ally" because she is not Victor's wife. Victor allows the training because Dun is bound through an extremely restrictive Soul- and Reality-based contract capable of erasing knowledge acquired through his faction if she betrays its terms.[6]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Dun remains within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s sphere as the Empire expands, devoting increasing amounts of time to advanced Runic research. By Chapter 1062, Victor observes her carefully engraving Runes into a high-level Artifact with such concentration that she is barely breathing. The scene clarifies the difference between normal Rune Masters and inherently Runic races such as Dragons: complex high-level Runecraft still requires Dun to spend hours carefully carving exact structures, while [[True Dragons]] can naturally invoke portions of Runic Language through speech.[10]
Dun nevertheless exists at an extreme level even among ordinary Rune users. The narration states that simpler Rune effects no longer necessarily require her to manually engrave every symbol; as the Goddess of Runes, she is capable of verbally invoking effects in a manner approaching Victor's own instinctive use.[10]
Her technical value becomes crucial during the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s increasingly ambitious projects. In Chapter 1092, Victor gives [[Scathach]] and Dun sections of a Runic system incorporating three different Scripts. Dun's Divine mind is immediately overwhelmed by the density of information, while Scathach's Blood Dragon physiology allows her to process the material much more easily.[9]
The scene provides the clearest direct comparison of their Standard Runic proficiency. Victor is described as a Master of the Standard Runic Script, Scathach as a Grandmaster, and Dun as one level above Scathach. No canonical title is given for Dun's level, so terms such as "Supreme Grandmaster" should not be treated as official ranks.[9]
Dun remains capable of finding flaws and possible improvements in Victor's Standard Runic work even while being unable to understand the Draconic and Eldritch portions. Victor consequently reinforces her mind because he values her opinion sufficiently to keep her involved in reviewing and refining the system.[9]
Their discussion exposes Dun's greatest current weakness. Victor explains that her problem is not intelligence but the limitations imposed by her body, Bloodline and Soul. Dun possesses an exceptional Soul—much stronger than the Souls of ordinary natural Gods and stated to exceed even those of many [[Primordial Gods]]—but the Soul of a Dragon God from Victor's lineage is still superior for processing information capable of distorting the mind.[9]
Dun immediately asks Victor to make her a Dragon Goddess.
Victor refuses because he does not distribute his Bloodline freely and restricts that transformation to his wives. He warns Dun to limit how long she studies the higher Runic texts and specifically tells her not to attempt to comprehend the unknown characters within the Scripts because doing so could drive her insane.[9]
The exchange gives [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] another reason to pressure Victor regarding Dun. Violet already considered Dun's talent far too valuable to leave outside the family and repeatedly grouped her alongside Kali, Albedo and Hela as women whose abilities would benefit enormously from complete integration into the Elderblood household.[9]
That possibility becomes formal in Chapter 1123. Violet invites Dun, Kali and Albedo to court Victor with the possibility of eventually joining the family. Violet makes clear that her permission does not itself make them Victor's wives; the final relationship still depends upon Victor's personal acceptance.[8]
Dun accepts. She acknowledges that much of her interest in Victor developed through his knowledge and through her relationship with Scathach, but she also genuinely likes Victor and finds him highly attractive. Rather than pretending to already possess deep romantic love, Dun considers affection something that can develop naturally through continued interaction. She therefore declares that she intends to become the Emperor's wife.[8]
Dun's relationship with Victor subsequently reaches its most ironic educational stage. During Chapters 1127–1128, Victor formally teaches her higher Runic principles, causing him to privately note that he has become his Master's Master's Master.[16][7]
Victor demonstrates a highly advanced Space-Time Rune associated with [[Natalia Elderblood née Alioth]]. Dun can understand the earlier layers of the construction but develops an intense headache when attempting to process the deeper Draconic characters. Victor clarifies that the demonstration is not intended to humiliate her by showing her limitations; he wants Dun to understand what the current peak looks like so she has something concrete to pursue.[7]
During the lesson, Victor describes Runes as the Source Code of the Universe. Beginners can use individual symbols to produce isolated effects, while true Rune Masters construct entire interconnected Runic texts whose components cooperate to produce stable, long-lasting changes in Reality.[7]
Dun immediately grasps the principle because it is the discipline through which she herself attained Divinity. She also demonstrates her existing mastery by casually creating a cleansing Rune upon her chair without interrupting the lesson.[7]
The experience changes the nature of Dun's attraction toward Victor. His ability to explain concepts that had remained beyond her understanding creates both intellectual admiration and unmistakable physical attraction, something Victor notices despite Dun attempting to conceal it.[7]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Dun remains present within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s private circle after the [[Elderblood Empire]] undergoes its enormous accelerated development. By the later Imperial Era she possesses access to sensitive information concerning Victor and his family under secrecy restrictions comparable to those previously applied to Albedo, Hela and Ariel.[17]
Her exact marital state during this period remains less explicitly documented than those of wives whose Soul Marriages are directly shown. The later chapters clearly portray Dun as an established member of the Emperor's intimate circle, but no reviewed chapter through Chapter 1170 provides a direct [[Soul Marriage]] or Dragon-transformation scene. Her status should therefore not be upgraded solely from implication or artwork.
Dun appears again during the enormous Elderblood family gathering in Chapter 1165, entering alongside [[Scathach]] while Victor's daughters, wives and close family members assemble inside the private family territory.[18] Her presence demonstrates how thoroughly she has been integrated into the family's private environment compared with the outsider who originally required contractual restrictions to enter Victor's territory.
She subsequently participates in the family's discussion surrounding a gigantic Star Beast brought back by the Leviathans. When Scathach reveals footage of an even larger specimen capable of swallowing a star, Dun reacts with open amazement at its size while Ruby and Velnorah begin analyzing the creature scientifically.[19]

### Relationships

#### Scathach Elderblood Scarlett
[[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] is Dun's most important known disciple.
Dun trained her when she still used the name Siren. The apprenticeship was exceptionally brutal, with Dun deliberately suppressing Siren's Vampire advantages through Runes before subjecting her to survival and combat training that could no longer be overcome through regeneration or superior physical attributes alone.[11]
This training established much of [[Scathach]]'s later philosophy. Scathach became one of the world's greatest warriors partly because Dun refused to allow talent or Race to substitute for genuine skill.
Their reunion demonstrates that their relationship remains affectionate beneath constant provocation. Dun immediately teases Scathach by calling her Siren, while Scathach responds with genuine threats when Dun attempts to reveal embarrassing details of her youth.[11]
Dun is visibly pleased that Scathach continues using an old proximity Rune she created centuries earlier.[12]
The balance between them later becomes unusual. In Standard Runic theory Dun remains above Scathach, but Scathach's Blood Dragon body allows her to process higher Draconic information that Dun's Divine body initially cannot safely withstand.[9]

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] first attracts Dun's attention through Runes rather than romance.
Watching him combine Draconic, Demonic and Creation-based principles during the [[Supernatural Gathering]] convinces Dun that Victor possesses knowledge unavailable anywhere else she has studied.[5] She subsequently leaves her isolation and actively seeks him out.
Their first direct relationship is essentially a negotiation between two Rune scholars. Victor deliberately tempts Dun with increasingly advanced research until she begs for access to his faction. Dun's refusal to trade her body for knowledge earns Victor's respect because it proves her obsession does not completely override her dignity.[11]
Dun later becomes one of the few people Victor trusts to review advanced Runic projects. He values her expertise enough to reinforce her mind when the information contained inside one of his multi-Script designs exceeds what her Divine physiology can naturally process.[9]
Victor eventually becomes Dun's teacher. Their educational relationship reverses the original lineage connecting them:

#### Anna Elderblood
[[Anna Elderblood]] became one of Dun's later students following her transformation into a Blood Dragon Goddess.
Anna possesses an extraordinary intuitive relationship with Runes and Reality. Her words can produce effects based upon what she subconsciously understands them to mean, making her dangerous until she learns precise control.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] selected Dun alongside [[Scathach]] and Zaladrac to teach Anna.[6]
Dun's role is specifically important because Anna already possesses enormous natural talent. What she lacks is the technical framework and disciplined understanding that Dun spent centuries developing.

#### Kali Elderblood
[[Kali Elderblood]] and Dun encounter one another while independently searching for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] after the emergence of the [[Blood Dragons]].[4]
Dun immediately recognizes how abnormal Kali is. Even with specialized restraints suppressing the Goddess of Destruction's Divinity, Dun can perceive enough of her Power to become extremely cautious.
The two nevertheless cooperate easily and approach Vlad together.
Their later circumstances remain parallel. Both are ancient women who had reached limitations in their original paths before discovering that Victor possesses knowledge or abilities capable of pushing them forward again. Violet later invites both Dun and Kali to court Victor in the same meeting.[8]

#### Violet Elderblood Snow
[[Violet Elderblood Snow]] becomes one of the greatest advocates for bringing Dun permanently into the Elderblood family.
By Chapter 1092, Violet already regards Dun's talent as something too valuable to leave outside the family and repeatedly signals to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] that he should pursue her.[9]
Violet later formalizes the matter by directly offering Dun, Kali and Albedo permission to court Victor. Dun accepts her proposal and states her intention to become Victor's wife.[8]
Violet's interest is partly strategic—Dun is one of the greatest Rune scholars alive—but she does not claim the ability to force Victor into accepting the relationship. The ultimate decision remains between Victor and Dun.

#### Vlad Dracula Tepes
[[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] first encountered Dun in the distant past while she was still Human.
The younger Vlad had heard rumors concerning a Human woman who trained heroes and visited her out of curiosity, but he ultimately dismissed Dun as unremarkable.[4]
When they meet again in Chapter 919, Dun has become the Goddess of Runes.
Vlad privately recognizes that his earlier judgment demonstrated a serious failure to identify long-term potential, especially when comparing his own reaction with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s tendency to discover and cultivate talented people.

### Etymology
[[Dún Scáith]] is a location in [[Celtic Pantheon|Irish mythology]] commonly translated as the Fortress of Shadows. It is traditionally associated with the warrior woman Scáthach and serves as the legendary fortress or school where heroes such as Cú Chulainn were trained.
The novel adapts the name differently by using Dun Scaith as the identity of [[Scathach]]'s own teacher rather than simply the name of her fortress.
The association remains thematically appropriate because Dun is herself a legendary teacher connected to the training of heroes and to the warrior later known as Scathach.
The old theory that Dun must therefore be the "original Scathach" is speculative and contradicted by Chapter 923's clarification that Siren personally chose the name Scathach after finishing her training.[11]

#### Powers & Abilities
[[Dún Scáith|Dun Scaith]]'s abilities revolve overwhelmingly around Runes. She was originally a Witch, but achieved Divinity through personal effort and Enlightenment in Runic study, becoming the Goddess of Runes.[1] Her strength does not come merely from possessing a large number of unrelated Divine abilities; Dun's defining advantage is the depth of her Runic knowledge, her unusually powerful Soul and her ability to translate Runic theory into persistent supernatural effects.

##### Ascended God Physiology
Dun is an Ascended Goddess rather than a naturally born God. Her original Witch existence evolved into Divinity after attaining Enlightenment through Runes.[1]
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Dun possesses the supernatural physical characteristics of a Divine being, including strength, speed, durability, stamina and reflexes greatly exceeding Mortal limitations.
- [[Immortality]] / Agelessness: Her Divine existence is no longer limited by ordinary Human aging.
- Regeneration: Dun possesses the supernatural survivability and recovery associated with Gods.
- [[Divine Energy]] Manipulation: She can generate and manipulate Divine Energy as part of her Ascended existence.
- Divine Senses: Dun can perceive supernatural Energy, Runes and other metaphysical phenomena that ordinary Mortals cannot properly perceive.
- Divine Soul: Dun possesses an extraordinarily powerful Soul even when compared with other Gods.[2]
- Exceptional Soul Strength: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] states that Dun's Soul is considerably stronger than those of most naturally born Gods and even stronger than [[Primordial Gods]] in the specific comparison being made.[2]
- Inferior to Dragon-God Souls: Despite her exceptional Soul, Victor explicitly states that she remains below a Dragon God, particularly one belonging to his Blood Dragon lineage and reinforced through his Essence.[2]
- Enhanced Information Tolerance: Dun's Divine mind and Soul allow her to perceive and analyze supernatural information that would completely destroy an ordinary Human. When exposed to Victor's multi-Script Runic text, the information overloaded her Divine brain; the narration states that a Human exposed to the same information would have been completely disintegrated.[2]
- Runic Information Overload: Her tolerance is not unlimited. High-density Draconic and other higher Runic Scripts can overwhelm her cognition, producing severe headaches and potentially driving her insane if she attempts to forcibly comprehend information beyond the limits of her body and Soul.[2][3]

##### Divinity — <Runes>
Dun reached Godhood specifically through Runic Enlightenment, making Runes the confirmed central Divinity of her Ascended existence.[1]
- Rune Divinity: Dun possesses Divine Authority directly associated with Runes.
- Runic Affinity: Her entire Divine path is built around understanding, creating and manipulating Runic structures.
- Runic Empowerment: [[Divine Energy]] can support and empower her Runic techniques.
- Runic Speech: Dun's mastery is sufficiently extreme that simple effects do not always require her to manually carve a Rune. Chapter 1062 states that in exceptional cases such as Dun, she can simply speak and cause the Runic effect to occur because she is the Goddess of Runes.[4]
- Runic Reality Manipulation: Runes function by imposing structured instructions upon Reality through supernatural language, Energy and intent. Dun can therefore alter Reality through the specific Runic effects she constructs.[3]
- Conceptual Growth: Achieving Rune Divinity did not represent the limit of Dun's development. She remains capable of learning higher Scripts, refining existing structures and expanding her understanding of Runes.[2][3]
Dun's Runic Reality Manipulation should not be confused with unrestricted Reality Warping. Her effects are mediated through the Runic language, structure, Energy and intent used in the particular construction.

##### Rune Mastery
Dun is one of the greatest confirmed Rune specialists in the series. [[Scathach]] describes her old master as an Unparalleled Master of Runes and one of the exceptional "monsters" she encountered within a specialized discipline.[5]

###### Standard Runic Script
Dun's greatest confirmed expertise lies in the Standard Runic Script.
Chapter 1092 directly establishes the following hierarchy:
User
Standard Runic Script
[[Victor Elderblood]]
Master
[[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]]
Grandmaster
[[Dún Scáith|Dun Scaith]]
One level above [[Scathach]]'s Grandmaster level
[2]
The novel does not provide a canonical name for Dun's rank above Grandmaster. Titles such as "Supreme Grandmaster" should therefore not be treated as official terminology.
Her Standard Rune abilities include:
- Rune Creation: Dun can create functional Runes by combining supernatural language, Energy and intent.
- Rune Inscription: She can engrave supernatural instructions onto objects and Artifacts.
- Rune Activation: Dun can provide the Energy or command necessary to activate completed Runic constructions.
- Runic Programming: She understands Runes as interconnected instructions rather than isolated magical symbols.
- Long-Form Rune Construction: Dun understands that true Rune Masters combine numerous symbols into long texts whose individual effects function together as a unified system.[3]
- Persistent Enchantment: Properly structured Runic texts can maintain their effects over extremely long periods rather than disappearing immediately after activation.[6][3]
- Multi-Layer Rune Construction: Dun can work with structures where multiple Runic effects interact, reinforce or modify one another.
- Rune Analysis: She can inspect unfamiliar Runic objects and infer their intended functions even when she cannot read every Script involved.[7]
- Rune Error Detection: Dun can detect inconsistencies and sections of advanced Standard Runic texts that can be improved.[2]
- Rune Refinement: She can suggest improvements intended to increase consistency and efficiency within Runic systems.[2]
- Runic Reverse Engineering: Her knowledge allows her to partially determine how unfamiliar Runic devices operate by studying their structure and interactions.[7]
- Runic Research: Dun continuously studies and develops new Runic knowledge instead of relying exclusively upon established formulas.
- Rune Teaching: She can teach Runic principles to others and was responsible for much of Scathach Elderblood Scarlett's original Runic education.[7]

###### Runic Source-Code Understanding
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later teaches Dun a more complete interpretation of Runes as the Source Code of the Universe.[3]
The principle explains that Runes are capable of controlling portions of Reality without the user necessarily possessing a matching Divinity. A Rune Master can encode commands into Reality through structured supernatural language.
Dun immediately understands the distinction between beginner and Master-level Runecraft:
- Beginners generally use individual Runic symbols for one effect.
- True Rune Masters write long Runic "texts" whose components function together.
- More advanced constructions can maintain persistent effects and manipulate larger portions of Reality.[3]
Dun's comprehension of this principle allows her to integrate Victor's higher theories into her own immense pre-existing knowledge.

###### Runic Speech
Most conventional Rune Masters must physically carve or write Runes, particularly when constructing complex Artifacts.
Dun is an exception.
Chapter 1062 states that for simpler applications, a Rune Master may simply write and activate a Rune, while Dun is advanced enough to speak and directly manifest the effect because she is the Goddess of Runes.[4]
This grants her:
- Verbal Rune Activation
- Runic Invocation
- Rapid Rune Deployment (Simple effects)
This ability does not remove all construction requirements. High-Level Artifacts containing complex Runic systems still require Dun to carefully engrave the necessary structures over long periods.[4]

##### Runecrafting
Dun is an exceptional creator and modifier of Rune-based Artifacts.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] observes her in Chapter 1062 engraving Runes upon a High-Level Artifact with such extreme concentration that she barely registers her surroundings.[4]
- Artifact Creation: Dun can create supernatural Artifacts through Runic inscription.
- Artifact Enchantment: She can grant objects supernatural properties through embedded Runes.
- Weapon Enchantment: Her Runic tradition formed part of the foundation later used by [[Scathach]] in Runic weapon construction.
- Persistent Enchantment: Properly written Runes can continue functioning long after Dun leaves the object.
- Complex Runic Engineering: High-Level Artifacts can contain large interconnected Rune structures requiring hours of careful work.[4]
- Runic Modification: Dun can review existing Runic structures and determine where they may be improved.[2]
- Runic System Optimization: Her Standard Runic knowledge is high enough that she can identify improvements within Victor's advanced Imperial projects despite being unable to understand every Draconic or Eldritch section.[2]

##### Limitation Runes
Dun can create Runes designed specifically to restrict another Being's supernatural characteristics.
During Siren's apprenticeship, Dun applied Limitation Runes to suppress the natural advantages of her Vampire physiology and force her to train under conditions approaching those of an ordinary Human.[7]
- Power Suppression (Runic)
- Supernatural Ability Restriction
- Physical Attribute Reduction
- Racial Ability Suppression
- Training Seals
The Runes were not intended to permanently destroy [[Scathach]]'s abilities. Dun used controlled suppression to prevent her disciple from relying upon superior Vampire strength, speed and regeneration instead of mastering technique.
The maximum level of being Dun can suppress with this method in the current era has not been demonstrated.

##### Tracking and Proximity Runes
Dun can create long-lasting Runes capable of identifying the approach of a specific person.
She previously gave [[Scathach]] a proximity Rune that remained functional centuries later. Dun sensed Scathach approaching because her disciple was still using the Rune she had created.[6]
- Presence Detection
- Proximity Detection
- Target-Specific Tracking
- Persistent Runic Marking
The fact that the Rune remained useful after an enormous passage of time demonstrates the durability of Dun's Runic constructions.

##### Cleansing Runes
Dun can casually create cleansing Runes capable of removing unwanted matter.
During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s lesson in Chapter 1128, she secretly created a cleansing Rune beneath herself which caused unwanted moisture on her chair to disappear completely.[3]
- Purification (Runic)
- Matter Removal (Limited; Runic)
- Contaminant Removal
- Rapid Rune Creation
The feat was performed discreetly during conversation, demonstrating that simple Runic applications require almost no meaningful preparation from Dun.

##### Runic Analysis
Dun possesses enough theoretical knowledge to understand the purpose of Runic devices even when parts of the construction use languages she cannot read.
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] showed her a failed prototype combining unfamiliar Draconic Runes with other structures, Dun immediately recognized that the opposing Runes should not normally have been compatible.[7]
- Structural Analysis
- Functional Inference
- Runic Weakness Detection
- Runic Error Detection
- Runic Optimization
- Foreign-Script Recognition
She later examined Victor's three-Script project and correctly determined that its Standard Runic sections could still be improved despite not understanding the Draconic and unidentified higher Runes mixed into the complete construction.[2]

##### Draconic Rune Study
Dun eventually becomes [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s student in the Draconic Runic Script.[8][3]
Unlike [[True Dragons]], Dun does not possess innate Draconic understanding.
- Draconic Language Recognition: Dun can recognize Draconic Language as a supernatural language even before she can understand its contents.[6]
- Limited Draconic Rune Comprehension: Through Victor's instruction, she begins understanding basic portions of Draconic Runic theory.[3]
- Partial Advanced-Rune Reading: When Victor presents a high-level Space-Time Rune created by [[Natalia Elderblood née Alioth]], Dun can understand only the initial portion before the deeper symbols become incomprehensible.[3]
- Space-Time Rune Study: Dun studies the principles behind Natalia's fused Space-Time Rune as an example of the current peak of advanced Runecraft.[3]
- Draconic Runic Theory: Victor teaches Dun how the Draconic Script relates to more conventional Runic structures and why True Dragon physiology grants an enormous learning advantage.[3]
Dun should not currently be indexed as possessing mastery of Draconic Runes. She is explicitly a student of the Script and lacks the innate understanding of a True Dragon.

##### Cross-Script Analysis
Dun is capable of examining projects containing multiple Runic Scripts even when she does not personally understand each language involved.
In Chapter 1092, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] gives her a Runic project written through three different Scripts. Dun cannot read the Draconic and other unfamiliar sections but can still identify flaws and possible improvements in the Standard Runic portions.[2]
This grants her:
- Multi-Script Structural Analysis
- Comparative Runic Analysis
- Partial Cross-Script Comprehension
This should not be confused with actual mastery of every Script present.

##### Exceptional Soul
Dun's Soul is one of her most important advantages and also the main reason she can interact with Runic information far above that of ordinary Gods.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explains that Dun is not a conventional Goddess. Her Soul is significantly stronger than those of most naturally born Gods and even exceeds [[Primordial Gods]] in the comparison being discussed.[2]
However, a Dragon God from Victor's Bloodline possesses an even stronger foundation because:
- the physical body is a genuine True Dragon body rather than the more spiritual Divine constitution of ordinary Gods;
- the Bloodline itself increases resistance to mind-distorting information;
- and Victor's wives additionally receive spiritual protection through [[Soul Marriage]].[2]
Dun's strong Soul therefore grants exceptional information-processing capacity but does not make her immune to all mental or spiritual effects.

##### Godslayer Reputation
Dun possesses an established reputation as a Godslayer and is historically known as a warrior as well as a scholar.
However, the currently reviewed chapters provide far more direct information about her Runic mastery than about individual God-killing battles.
The page should therefore distinguish between:
- Godslayer Reputation — Canonical title/reputation.
- Specific God-Killing Techniques — Not sufficiently demonstrated in the reviewed chapters to fabricate a separate anti-God power set.
Her Runic mastery is nevertheless dangerous enough that Runes in general are treated as capable of producing effects relevant even against high-level Divine beings when sufficiently advanced.

#### Skills
- Genius Intelligence: Dun is an exceptional supernatural scholar who reached Divinity through understanding and Enlightenment rather than Divine inheritance.[1]
- Runic Scholarship: She has accumulated immense knowledge of supernatural languages, Runic structures, enchantment, Artifact construction and the principles governing persistent supernatural effects.
- Research and Development: Dun continuously experiments with new Runic principles and willingly abandons old assumptions when new evidence contradicts them.
- Analytical Intelligence: She can rapidly separate familiar portions of a Runic design from unknown Scripts and determine where a construction can be improved.[2]
- Rapid Learning: Once [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explains higher Runic principles, Dun immediately connects them to her existing theoretical knowledge.[3]
- Exceptional Teaching Ability: Dun trained [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] during the period when she still used the name Siren and later assisted in teaching [[Anna Elderblood]] Runic control.[7][9]
- Training Methodology: Dun specializes in removing a student's natural advantages so weaknesses in technique cannot remain hidden.[7]
- Survival Training: She forced [[Scathach]] to operate under heavily restricted supernatural conditions in dangerous environments.[7]
- Combat Training: Dun identifies herself as a warrior and trained Scathach in practical combat as well as Runes.[7]
- Supernatural Language Knowledge: Her Rune scholarship requires extensive understanding of ancient and supernatural languages, although direct mastery of a particular Script should only be listed where explicitly demonstrated.
- Scholarly Adaptability: Dun is willing to become Victor's student despite previously being Scathach's teacher because acquiring genuine knowledge matters more to her than preserving seniority.[3]

#### Limitations
- Non-Draconic Physiology: Dun's greatest modern limitation is no longer intelligence or Standard Runic knowledge but her physical and spiritual foundation. As a non-Dragon Goddess, she cannot process the highest Draconic information as naturally as [[Scathach]] or [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[2]
- No Innate Draconic Language: Draconic Language does not come naturally to Dun because she is not a True Dragon.[6]
- Advanced Runic Information Overload: Victor's high-density three-Script text immediately produces a massive headache and overloads Dun's Divine brain.[2]
- Higher Draconic Character Rejection: Dun can understand the opening section of Natalia's advanced Space-Time Rune but the deeper Draconic characters produce severe mental strain.[3]
- Soul / Bloodline Bottleneck: Victor explicitly explains that some forms of knowledge are restricted not by Dun's intelligence but by the limitations of her body, Bloodline and Soul.[2]
- High-Level Artifact Preparation: Complex Rune Artifacts still require long periods of careful engraving even for Dun.[4]
- Cannot Naturally Perform All Dragon Runic Techniques: In Chapter 868, Dun is shocked when Victor places Runes inside other Runes through Draconic and Creation-based methods, demonstrating that this is not part of her conventional Runic capabilities.[5]
- External Mental Reinforcement: Victor can reinforce Dun's mind so she can temporarily inspect information that would otherwise overwhelm her, but this enhancement belongs to Victor and is not one of Dun's personal abilities.[2]
- No Confirmed Dragon Transformation: Dun requests transformation into a Dragon Goddess in Chapter 1092, but Victor refuses because his Bloodline is limited to his wives at that time.[2] No direct transformation scene has been confirmed through Chapter 1170.

#### Notes
- Dun's only directly confirmed named Divinity in the reviewed novel material is <Runes>. The older ability page's claims that she possesses minor Divinities over Death, Shadow and unspecified additional Concepts are not currently supported by a direct chapter citation and have therefore been removed.
- The old page classified Dun as a God-King largely by assumption. Her extraordinary Runic skill and Soul strength should not by themselves be used to invent an unconfirmed Divine combat rank.
- Dun being "one level above [[Scathach]]" applies specifically to the Standard Runic Script.[2]
- Scathach possesses a significant advantage over Dun in the Draconic Runic Script because her Blood Dragon physiology provides instinctive affinity and allows her to process information Dun's current body cannot safely withstand.[2]
- Dun's Soul comparison in Chapter 1092 refers specifically to the strength and information tolerance of her Soul. It should not be interpreted as proof that Dun possesses greater overall combat power than every Primordial God.[2]
- Dun's ability to verbally manifest simple Runes does not mean complex High-Level Artifacts can be instantly spoken into existence. Chapter 1062 explicitly distinguishes simple effects from high-complexity Runecraft requiring careful engraving.[4]
- Dun can analyze constructions containing Draconic or unknown Runes without necessarily understanding those Scripts themselves.[7][2]
- The ability to combine three different Runic Languages into a permanent large-scale Reality-distorting effect in Chapter 1128 is demonstrated and explained by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Dun learns the theory from him; the scene does not directly establish that she can already reproduce Victor's complete three-Script feat independently.[3]
- Likewise, Natalia's Space-Time Rune is used as a teaching example. Dun's partial comprehension of the Rune should not be indexed as independent Space-Time Manipulation until she directly demonstrates using it.[3]
- Claims that Dun directly uses Demonic Scripture and Divine Scripture should not be treated as confirmed Dun-specific abilities solely because those languages are used within the Runic tradition inherited by Scathach. A direct Dun feat or statement is required before indexing them as personal Script mastery.
- Dun's title as a Godslayer establishes her reputation, but named God-killing techniques should not be invented where the novel has not actually described them.
- Through Chapter 1170, no later material provides a confirmed Dragon Goddess transformation, new Runic Script mastery or new combat ability for Dun.
