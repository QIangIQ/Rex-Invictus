### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 1004
Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III is an Imperial Princess of the [[Elderblood Empire]], a naturally born Blood Dragon, True Dragon, Dragon Goddess, technomancer, engineer and one of the greatest scientists of the Elderblood civilization. She is the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]], and the granddaughter of [[Thaloria Xyphora]]. Her full name preserves the royal naming tradition of Velnorah's destroyed civilization while simultaneously marking her place within the Elderblood dynasty.[1]
Velina's importance lies primarily in science rather than conventional political or military leadership. She begins working alongside Velnorah from childhood and eventually develops into a researcher repeatedly grouped with Velnorah, Ruby and Aline as one of the principal figures responsible for the Empire's technological advancement. Her research encompasses Technomancy, Draconic Runes, Energy systems, artificial Life, artificial intelligence, genetics, cloning, racial engineering and eventually weapons designed to interfere directly with the underlying structure of Reality.[1][3][4]
Her earliest major contribution was the Energy-system prototype jointly developed with Velnorah that Victor subsequently transformed into the [[Dragon Heart]], one of the foundational Energy sources of the Empire. Velina later became one of the conceptual architects behind The Pillar and, after approximately two hundred thousand years of Imperial development, originated the idea that became the [[Absolute Paradox]]. The latter evolved from her interpretation of Schrödinger's cat into a proposed Reality-level weapon capable of forcing mutually exclusive truths to coexist simultaneously.[1][5]
Her later research also connects directly with the Elderblood family's increasingly advanced understanding of Reality. Victor establishes that his biological daughters inherited variations of his ability to perceive the deeper "source code" of Reality, and Velina later demonstrates this inheritance directly when examining [[Cthugha]]. She immediately recognizes that the Great Old One's coexistence of native and foreign Concepts provides an important clue toward solving the Absolute Paradox problem.[6][7]

### Physical Appearance
Velina strongly resembles her mother, [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]]. At her debut the two appear sufficiently similar to be mistaken for sisters or alternate versions of the same woman, with their most obvious differences being height, eyes and hairstyle. Velina possesses long blue hair, fair skin and deep violet eyes, and already stands approximately two meters tall despite still being shorter than Velnorah.[1]
Her height continues increasing throughout the accelerated Imperial era. By the large Elderblood family gathering, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] deliberately increases his normal humanoid height to approximately three meters because many of his adult daughters, including Velina, have grown beyond two meters and are more comfortable interacting with him at a larger scale.[2]
Velina's violet eyes are more than an inherited aesthetic trait. Victor explains that his daughters possess varying expressions of his ability to perceive the deeper truth and structure of Reality. This perception allows them, once sufficiently developed, to see the "source code" underlying dimensions and Reality and potentially manipulate what they understand.[6] Velina later demonstrates the perceptive side of this inheritance while examining [[Cthugha]]'s contradictory conceptual structure.[7]
While conducting research, Velina commonly surrounds herself with advanced technological interfaces, holographic systems, terminals and Imperial databases. Her personal room is considerably less austere than her professional image suggests, being filled with cute objects and prominently displaying a large family photograph. She also possesses access to a private laboratory Dimension constructed by Velnorah where experiments can be performed away from the environmental influence of a planet.[7]

### Personality
Velina is intensely intelligent, curious, analytical and perfectionistic. Even during her debut, she is dissatisfied with an experimental Energy source functioning with a mere two-percent margin of error and immediately begins calculating millions of possible failure conditions. Velnorah has to remind her that eliminating every possible unknown is impossible without possessing complete knowledge of the Universe.[1]
Her approach resembles that of a systems engineer rather than someone concerned only with whether an individual invention functions. Velina naturally considers redundancy, unforeseen variables, future applications, failure propagation and the consequences of integrating a new technology into civilization-scale infrastructure. This tendency remains one of her defining qualities even after almost two hundred thousand years of research experience.
Velina is confident enough to question Velnorah rather than automatically agreeing with her. When her mother proposes engineering a new population of technomages, Velina challenges whether the Empire actually requires additional researchers when Velnorah, Ruby, Aline and herself can already accomplish absurd amounts of work. Once Velnorah explains the value of compartmentalization, delegation and alternate perspectives, however, Velina accepts the reasoning rather than continuing the argument simply to protect her pride.[4]
Her principal character flaw is arrogance. Centuries and millennia of existing as a True Dragon within an overwhelmingly advanced Empire gradually distort her understanding of what ordinary weakness looks like. Velnorah becomes concerned that Velina and several of her sisters are beginning to regard absurd strength and intelligence as normal, and considers placing them in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Dream Worlds or dangerous missions where their normal advantages would disappear. Velnorah's concern is particularly personal because her own arrogance once contributed to the destruction of the civilization she ruled.[4]
Velina also inherits much of Velnorah's controlling nature. She prefers personally understanding systems and initially questions why important work should be delegated when she possesses the stamina and intellect to perform enormous amounts of it herself. As a True Dragon she experiences so little ordinary physical exhaustion that the necessity of conserving effort is difficult for her to appreciate.[4]
Her scientific curiosity can override caution and rules. Velina enters Velnorah's private room, learns about classified research she was not supposed to investigate and later disobeys instructions to remain home when she believes an old Human thought experiment may help solve the problem. Her gamble succeeds because the idea immediately fascinates Velnorah and ultimately becomes the foundation of the [[Absolute Paradox]] project.[5]
The same pattern appears around her sisters. Velina initially reacts with alarm when Yog, Nigredo and the others use one of Victor's scales, Eldritch creatures and Leviathans in an uncontrolled experiment. Once Stella points out that Victor is certainly watching and would intervene if the experiment became genuinely dangerous, Velina accepts the logic almost immediately and joins the project herself, contributing intelligence to the developing creature.[7]
Despite her scientific obsession, Velina does not normally neglect her family. Unlike Velnorah, Ruby and Aline during the most intense stages of the Absolute Paradox research, Velina generally returns home to sleep and attend family dinners. When Velnorah orders her away from the laboratory after prolonged research, Velina pouts and secretly continues testing theories inside her private laboratory, but stops immediately when a recording of Victor reminds her that it is time for dinner.[7]
Her greatest emotional bias concerns Victor. Velina idolizes her father to such an extent that when Velnorah cites Victor as evidence that perfection does not exist, Velina seriously challenges the premise. Her scientific fascination reinforces rather than replaces this affection: Victor's biology, Soul, Energy systems and continually evolving existence are among the most complicated phenomena she has ever attempted to understand.[1]
Around Victor personally, much of Velina's ancient scientific dignity disappears. During the family gathering, several sisters successfully carry her around inside a sack because she voluntarily allows the prank to continue. Victor immediately realizes she could have escaped whenever she wished, draws her into his lap and reduces the ancient Imperial scientist to behaving like an affectionate "Papa's little girl".[8]

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Velina made her direct debut while working beside Velnorah on a revolutionary Energy generator. Mother and daughter had combined Technomancy, Runes, supernatural engineering and advanced craftsmanship into an artifact intended to become a new central Energy source for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s growing civilization. Although the prototype already functioned with an extremely small failure margin, Velina remained dissatisfied and continued analyzing millions of possible error conditions.[1]
Their discussion revealed how much of Velnorah's scientific mentality Velina had inherited. Velnorah attempted to teach her that perfect engineering was impossible because they did not possess complete knowledge of every phenomenon that could interact with the machine, only for Velina to challenge the argument when Victor was used as an example of an imperfect existence. Velina had already spent significant time attempting to analyze Victor and knew firsthand that even the advanced technology available to her could interpret only a fraction of the information contained within his body and supernatural structure.[1]
Victor entered their laboratory without either researcher initially detecting him. Velina immediately embraced her father while her equipment continued collecting information from him, demonstrating that her affection and scientific curiosity could function simultaneously. Victor then examined the prototype she and Velnorah had created and reconstructed it through [[Junketsu]], Draconic Runes, his own understanding of Reality and a fragment of his Soul. The result was an enormous dark-violet icosahedral construct approximately ten meters tall that behaved less like a conventional reactor than a living supernatural heart.[1]
Velnorah named the artifact the [[Dragon Heart]]. Its Energy output vastly exceeded that of the original prototype while remaining sufficiently unaltered that the produced Energy could subsequently be given whatever properties a technological system required. Velina immediately recognized applications involving strategic weapons, artificial beings, combat constructs and other Energy-intensive projects that had previously been impractical.[1]
The distinction between their contributions remained important. Velina and Velnorah created the experimental Energy-system foundation, while Victor transformed that foundation into the final Dragon Heart. Velina is consequently one of the co-developers of the technology underlying the Dragon Heart rather than its sole creator.
Victor's use of his Soul within the artifact also gave Velina another idea. Artificial beings produced through systems ultimately powered by Victor could possess a natural connection to him, creating a safeguard against rebellion from future sentient technologies. This discussion developed into plans for The Pillar, a superior Imperial intelligence intended to possess genuine Life, a Soul and an independent consciousness rather than remaining an ordinary artificial intelligence. Velina proposed making the new Being one of Victor's daughters so that its loyalty would originate from family and existence rather than programming alone.[1]
The problem of creating a genuinely living central intelligence was later resolved through [[Akasha Elderblood]], whom Victor personally created as a daughter, Goddess and guardian linked to the Imperial System. Akasha should not be treated as Velina's personal creation, but the earlier Pillar discussions demonstrate that Velina and Velnorah were already exploring the same underlying problem of creating a truly living intelligence for an Empire too large for conventional administrative systems.

#### Those Who Rule Reality
The enormous temporal development of the [[Elderblood Empire]] transformed Velina from a talented young researcher into one of its principal scientists. By the later Imperial era, her sisters already used her as an example of a daughter who had built an important independent role within the Empire, and her proximity to Velnorah, Ruby and Aline became so well known that Priscillia, Rosemary and Sylvia regarded her as the most obvious sister to question if they wanted information about classified Imperial research.[9][3]
Velina's mature scientific status became explicit while she and Velnorah studied cloned species from Velnorah's former civilization. Their standards had changed enormously since the Thalorian era. Species once viewed as remarkable warriors now seemed deeply flawed beside [[True Dragons]], Bloodhunters and the genetically engineered inhabitants of the modern Empire. Velnorah nevertheless considered the extraordinary adaptability of the Yuurakyo valuable and decided to combine it with the Technomancy genes of her own people and useful characteristics from other races.[4]
Velina immediately participated in designing the resulting population. She asked what mentality the clones should possess, suggested whether opposed Angelic and Demonic traits might again be useful for balance and considered whether [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] or Melida should be brought into the project to elevate the creations further. Velnorah rejected the need for another supreme warrior species; she wanted technomages, researchers born with scientific knowledge, Technomancy and enough supernatural power to survive dangerous research environments without wasting resources turning them into elite soldiers.[4]
The project demonstrated the breadth of Velina's mature expertise. She was no longer limited to machines and Energy reactors but worked with cloning, genetic extraction, racial hybridization, psychological design, artificial knowledge implantation and the creation of entire purpose-built populations. During the same conversation, Velnorah, Ruby, Aline and Velina were identified collectively as the giants of Imperial research and development, with Velina's name appearing increasingly often on major projects as her career matured.[4]
Velnorah simultaneously recognized that Velina was becoming too arrogant. Her daughter's True Dragon body effectively eliminated ordinary fatigue, and generations of impossible Imperial technology had normalized standards that would have appeared absurd to earlier civilizations. Remembering how her own arrogance once contributed to the destruction of her kingdom, Velnorah considered placing Velina and several sisters in Dream Worlds or dangerous raids where they would once again understand what weakness felt like.[4]
Velina left the laboratory for the Elderblood family's traditional dinner only to be ambushed by several sisters, shoved into a sack and carried away. The prank eventually contributed to a family gathering containing more than thirty-five daughters. Velina initially appeared irritated when she reached Victor, but her father immediately realized she could have escaped at any moment and had voluntarily allowed the kidnapping to continue because she was enjoying herself. She eventually admitted the truth and settled comfortably into Victor's lap.[4][2][8]
Victor's later lessons on dimensions and Reality revealed another side of the abilities inherited by the Imperial Princesses. He explained that his daughters possess variations of his ability to perceive the truth underlying Reality. Properly developed, this sight can observe the "source code" from which dimensions and local structures are created and potentially manipulate what the user understands. Norabell and Melida were identified as particularly naturally gifted examples, but Victor stated that all of his daughters possess the potential.[6]
Velina's greatest scientific breakthrough began during the final portion of the Empire's enormous temporal-development project. While bored at home, she accessed the Imperial database through a bookshelf capable of reproducing archived works and encountered the old Human thought experiment known as Schrödinger's cat. Instead of focusing upon its original limited context, Velina immediately connected the idea of simultaneous contradictory states with a secret problem Velnorah had been investigating.[5]
Velina teleported directly to her mother despite having been told to remain home. Velnorah initially prepared to reprimand her for entering restricted areas and learning about classified research, but Velina proposed taking the contradiction represented by the thought experiment "to the extreme" and creating an actual large-scale paradox. Velnorah immediately recognized the potential, gathered her normal research group and began developing the concept with Velina, Ruby and Aline.[5]
The resulting project became the [[Absolute Paradox]]. Rather than attempting to overpower a target physically, the proposed weapon would force Reality to accept two states that logically cannot coexist. A person could simultaneously be alive and dead; an event could both have happened and never happened; two spatial positions could remain separate while also occupying the same point; and at its theoretical maximum the Universe itself could both exist and never have existed.[5]
Victor considered the idea important enough that its completion became one of the reasons he was willing to continue sustaining the extremely costly long-term ritual developing the Empire. He identified three principal requirements for the weapon: a Core of Contradiction capable of containing mutually exclusive states, an Energy source powerful enough to prevent Reality from immediately correcting the paradox and a mechanism capable of transferring the contradictory condition into a chosen person, object, Rule or Concept.[5]
The concept remained much easier to describe than to construct. [[Eldritch Energy]] was unsuitable for the original design because it distorted and corrupted the existing Rules rather than forcing Reality itself to accept a genuine internal contradiction. The researchers also realized that the Universe would immediately attempt to destroy a completed contradiction simply because such a state violated the system maintaining Reality. They therefore began with smaller experiments instead of attempting to construct the ultimate weapon immediately.[5]
By Chapter 1188, the Absolute Paradox had become an extended research program. Velina, Velnorah, Ruby and Aline had spent enormous amounts of time trying to find a method capable of producing a contradiction the Universe could not automatically resolve. The core problem was that using the Rules of the Universe to break those same Rules was effectively self-defeating. External Eldritch power could distort the local system more easily, but even that behaved more like foreign corruption than the absolute contradiction the researchers required.[7]
Velnorah eventually ordered Velina to leave the laboratory and return home. Velina complied outwardly but, still frustrated by the unsolved problem, entered the private laboratory Dimension her mother had constructed for her and used the remaining time before dinner to continue experimenting independently. In approximately half an hour she tested countless theories and simulations, including attempts involving contradictory Concepts, but every configuration failed because the Universe automatically corrected the inconsistency.[7]
Her experiments refined the family's understanding of the problem. Isolating an area from ordinary planetary influence was insufficient because the fundamental Rules of Reality still applied. Even a world contained within Victor's Soul remained part of the same Universe because Victor himself remained a native component of that Universe. Velina compared the problem to attempting to escape a living organism's immune system while still operating inside one of its organs. She also concluded from Velnorah's data that merely moving somewhere "outside" ordinary space would not necessarily solve the deeper conceptual problem.[7]
After ending her experiment for dinner, Velina encountered Yog, Nigredo and several sisters conducting an extremely dangerous Eldritch-Life experiment near the family castle. She initially objected strongly after realizing they were combining two [[Outer Gods]], a Leviathan, lesser Eldritch organisms and one of Victor's scales. Once Stella pointed out that Victor was certainly observing the experiment and would stop anything truly dangerous, Velina immediately became interested enough to participate.[7]
Yog and Nigredo had already created hybrid Eldritch-Leviathan fish capable of moving through Space and surviving inside black holes, while Leone had added extreme adaptability. Valentina, Silvia, Rosemary, Hinata and Stella proposed adding Dragon Fire, Dragon Lightning, Ice, Solar power and Truth. Rather than remain outside the experiment, Velina contributed intelligence, reasoning that the resulting creature should not be stupid.[7]
The combined powers, Victor's scale and the existing Eldritch organism eventually produced [[Cthugha]], whom Victor classified as a Great Old One. Cthugha contained an extraordinary mixture of mutually foreign properties: a native Concept of a star, Eldritch Space originating from another universal foundation, Draconic influence and recognition by the native Reality as the progenitor of Fire Vampires.[7]
For Velina, Cthugha's importance exceeded the creature's immediate combat power. Victor pointed out that the Great Old One was a living contradiction yet the Universe showed no obvious reaction against his existence. Velina used the inherited supernatural sight received from Victor to examine Cthugha's structure and immediately understood why the result mattered. The Great Old One was not the completed Absolute Paradox, but his stable coexistence of incompatible native and foreign properties provided the research group with its first important clue toward creating a contradiction Reality might tolerate instead of instantly correcting.[7]
Velina immediately left to report the discovery to Velnorah and the other researchers, connecting the Elderblood daughters' seemingly irresponsible cosmic experiment directly back to one of the Empire's most dangerous scientific projects.[7]

### Scientific Expertise

#### Technomancy
Velina inherited the technological tradition of Velnorah's civilization and became an advanced practitioner of Technomancy, a discipline in which supernatural Energy, Runes and technology are treated as compatible components of one engineering framework rather than separate systems.[1][4]
Her use of Technomancy extends beyond operating inherited technology. She participates directly in designing new power systems, artificial organisms, research species and Reality-level weapon concepts. By the late Imperial era, Technomancy has become one tool among many within a much broader scientific skill set.

#### Systems and Energy Engineering
Velina's debut establishes exceptional systems-engineering ability. She can model millions of possible failures, examine how Draconic Runes alter reliability, evaluate unknown variables and refuse to treat an invention as complete merely because it performs successfully under normal conditions.[1]
This mentality made her especially suited to the development of civilization-scale infrastructure. The prototype she created with Velnorah eventually became the technological basis from which [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] formed the [[Dragon Heart]], while Velina immediately recognized that the resulting Energy supply could transform the Empire's weapons, artificial Life and industrial development.[1]

#### Artificial Life and Intelligence
Velina's interest in artificial intelligence concerns genuine Life rather than simple automation. The Pillar was envisioned as an intelligence possessing an actual Soul, body and consciousness capable of administering an expanding Empire. Her proposal to make the entity one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters demonstrates that Velina approaches loyalty itself as part of biological, spiritual and social design rather than merely a software restriction.[1]
The later creation of Akasha solved many of the same problems through Victor's direct supernatural intervention, but Velina's earlier involvement remains an important example of her work at the intersection of technology, Souls and artificial Life.

#### Genetics and Racial Engineering
Velina's later work with Velnorah establishes extensive competence in biological engineering. She can evaluate cloned supernatural species, isolate valuable genetic characteristics, propose hybridization strategies, design mental dispositions and participate in creating entire populations optimized for particular functions.[4]
The technomage project combined Yuurakyo adaptability with [[Xyrra]] Technomancy and other selected characteristics. Knowledge itself was intended to be loaded directly into the new Beings, allowing them to begin functioning as scientists without spending ordinary lifetimes acquiring the necessary education.[4]

#### Conceptual Engineering
The [[Absolute Paradox]] represents Velina's progression beyond conventional engineering into the manipulation of the logical structure underlying Reality. Her contribution was not simply recognizing Schrödinger's cat but abstracting the idea of mutually exclusive states and asking whether the contradiction itself could become an engineered phenomenon.[5]
This field remains experimental. Velina is attempting to engineer effects upon Concepts and Reality through external systems rather than possessing a personal Divinity of Paradox, Science or Technology.

### The Absolute Paradox
Main article: [[Absolute Paradox]]
The Absolute Paradox is Velina's most important known theoretical contribution. Its foundational idea originated when she encountered the ancient thought experiment of Schrödinger's cat and recognized that the principle of mutually contradictory states could potentially be transformed from an intellectual exercise into a supernatural engineering problem.[5]
Velina brought the idea to Velnorah, who immediately recognized its implications. The project subsequently developed through Velina, Velnorah, Ruby and Aline, with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] contributing his own understanding of Reality and identifying several requirements that any functional implementation would need.[5]

#### Principle
The weapon would not operate through ordinary destruction or Eldritch corruption. Its intended effect is to make two logically incompatible conditions simultaneously true and prevent them from canceling each other.
Target
Proposed Contradiction
Life / Death
A target remains simultaneously alive and dead.
Existence
A Being or object simultaneously exists and does not exist.
History
A person was born and simultaneously was never born.
Events
An event occurred and simultaneously never occurred.
Space
Two locations remain separate while simultaneously occupying the same position.
Time
Something happened in history while simultaneously never having happened.
Objects
An object can simultaneously be in someone's possession and never have been there.
Laws / Concepts
A second mutually incompatible state is imposed directly upon a Law or Concept.
Universe
At its theoretical extreme, the Universe simultaneously exists and never existed, preventing Reality from determining a consistent state.[5]

#### Proposed Components
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] identifies three essential elements required for any functional [[Absolute Paradox]]:[5]
1. A Core of Contradiction capable of containing two normally mutually exclusive states without allowing one to eliminate the other.
2. An Energy source capable of sustaining the contradiction against Reality's automatic correction, with Primal Energy considered a potential candidate.
3. A transfer mechanism capable of imposing the contradiction upon a selected person, object, Law or Concept.

#### The Correction Problem
The greatest obstacle is that Reality naturally rejects a genuine contradiction. A Core of Contradiction would attract immediate correction simply by existing because the Universe's Rules do not permit mutually exclusive states to remain equally valid.[5]
Initial consideration therefore turned toward creating the weapon outside the Universe. Later research complicated even this possibility. Velina's own tests showed that simply isolating a laboratory, Dimension or world from ordinary environmental influence does not escape the fundamental framework of the Universe. Even the planets protected inside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul remain within the same cosmological system because Victor himself originates from and remains connected to that Universe.[7]
Velina tested numerous theoretical configurations and contradictory Concepts in her private laboratory, but the Universe repeatedly corrected the inconsistencies before they could become true Absolute Paradoxes.[7]

#### Eldritch Energy
[[Eldritch Energy]] initially appears attractive because it originates outside the native Universe and can influence local Reality more easily than powers relying entirely upon native Rules. The research group nevertheless concludes that Eldritch Energy by itself is not the desired solution: it produces distortion and corruption according to foreign Rules rather than forcing the native Universe to accept a genuine contradiction within its own logic.[5][7]

#### Cthugha — The First Major Clue
[[Cthugha]] provides the project's first major conceptual clue. The Great Old One is a hybrid of multiple systems, simultaneously possessing the native Concept of a star, foreign Eldritch Space, Draconic characteristics and the position of progenitor of a Fire Vampire race despite fire ordinarily being one of the principal weaknesses of most Noble Vampires.[7]
The resulting existence is described as a living contradiction, yet the Universe does not visibly attempt to erase or correct him. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] immediately points this out to Velina. Using her inherited supernatural sight, Velina analyzes Cthugha and recognizes that his stable cross-cosmological structure may provide a route toward understanding how incompatible truths can coexist without provoking immediate universal correction.[7]
Cthugha does not represent a completed [[Absolute Paradox]]. His existence is a clue demonstrating that a sufficiently unusual mixture of native and foreign principles can produce contradictions Reality tolerates.

### Cthugha Experiment
Velina was not originally part of the experiment that produced [[Cthugha]]. She encountered Yog, Nigredo, Valentina, Leone, Hinata, Silvia, Rosemary and Stella after leaving her own [[Absolute Paradox]] experiments for dinner. By then, the sisters had already gathered Leviathans, lesser Eldritch Beings and one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s scales while producing experimental hybrid organisms.[7]
Velina initially reacted as the responsible scientist of the group and questioned whether combining the influence of two [[Outer Gods]], a Leviathan, a lesser cosmic horror and Victor's scale so close to the family home was remotely sensible. Stella countered that Victor was certainly monitoring them and would prevent a genuine disaster. Velina found the argument convincing enough to stop opposing the project.[7]
The sisters then began contributing different abilities:
- Yog contributed characteristics related to Space.
- Nigredo contributed survival within black holes.
- Leone contributed extreme adaptability.
- Valentina proposed Dragon Fire.
- Silvia contributed Dragon Lightning.
- Rosemary contributed Ice.
- Hinata contributed Solar power.
- Stella contributed Truth.
- Velina contributed intelligence, specifically arguing that the new creature should not emerge stupid.[7]
The resulting Being developed Divinity and became the Great Old One Cthugha. Velina is therefore one of his co-creators, although the feat was collaborative and depended upon the combined powers of her sisters, Eldritch organisms, Leviathan characteristics and Victor's scale.[7]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Velina's biological father and the person she admires most strongly. Her emotional attachment is obvious from her debut, when she immediately embraces him despite being in the middle of professional research, and from her insistence that Velnorah provide evidence when claiming that Victor is not perfect.[1]
Victor is simultaneously one of Velina's greatest scientific mysteries. His body, Soul, Energy systems and continually evolving supernatural structure contain more information than even Imperial technology can completely analyze. Velina consequently studies him with genuine scientific fascination while never reducing him to an impersonal research subject.[1]
Victor treats Velina first as his daughter and only second as an Imperial scientist. During the family gathering he immediately realizes she allowed her sisters to abduct her because she secretly enjoyed the game, draws her into his lap and comforts her until her adult scientific demeanor collapses.[8]
He also respects her intellect. The [[Absolute Paradox]] concept is considered important enough that Victor is willing to prolong the extraordinarily draining Imperial-development ritual so the research group has more time to turn Velina's idea into something usable.[5]
Victor also serves as her teacher in subjects beyond formal science. His lessons concerning Reality, dimensions, Energy, Runes and the Cosmos give Velina access to principles that ordinary scientific civilizations could never test directly. Her inherited perception of Reality ultimately allows her to recognize [[Cthugha]]'s importance to the Absolute Paradox research.[6][7]

#### Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II
[[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]] is Velina's mother, principal mentor, long-term research partner and strongest intellectual influence. Velina resembles her strongly in both appearance and mentality: both instinctively approach supernatural phenomena as things that can eventually be measured, modeled, redesigned and integrated into technological systems.[1]
Their relationship develops from teacher and apprentice into something much closer to senior and junior peers. Velnorah clearly leads their Chapter 1004 research, but after the immense accelerated era Velina is named alongside Velnorah, Ruby and Aline as one of the Empire's R&D giants.[4]
Velnorah remains willing to correct her daughter's flaws regardless of Velina's accomplishments. She criticizes Velina's perfectionism, controlling behavior and later arrogance, particularly because she sees reflections of the mistakes that destroyed her original civilization.[1][4]
Their similarity also causes frequent boundary violations. Velina enters Velnorah's private space to investigate classified information and later avoids punishment only because the idea she brings back is too useful for Velnorah to ignore.[5]
During the prolonged [[Absolute Paradox]] research, Velnorah eventually has to order Velina away from the laboratory so that she will stop working and return home. Velina obeys technically, then spends the remaining hour before dinner experimenting secretly inside another laboratory.[7]

#### Ruby Elderblood Scarlett and Aline Elderblood Valefar
[[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] and [[Aline Elderblood Valefar]] are two of Velina's principal scientific collaborators. Together with Velnorah, they form the senior research circle whose work appears repeatedly throughout Imperial development. By the late era, Velina is sufficiently integrated into this group that her sisters assume she is the person most likely to know what the older researchers are secretly developing.[3][4]
The four become the core research team attempting to develop the [[Absolute Paradox]]. Their work continues long after Velina's initial idea, with the scientists spending years testing how Reality reacts to contradictions and how external cosmological forces interact with native universal Rules.[5][7]

#### Thaloria Xyphora
[[Thaloria Xyphora]] is Velina's maternal grandmother and the First Empress whose legacy survives within her full name. Velnorah's ancient Royal Family used a naming system in which descendants inherited their mother's name and the name connected to the First Empress. Velnorah preserves the custom through Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III.[1]
The name symbolically connects three civilizations: the destroyed Thalorian dynasty, Velnorah's surviving legacy and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s [[Elderblood Empire]].

#### Sisters
Velina is fully integrated into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s enormous second generation despite spending substantial periods working in laboratories. Her sisters regard her scientific career with enough respect that Priscillia's desire to join secret research is immediately contrasted with Velina, whom Sylvia describes as absurdly competent and as someone who has worked with Velnorah since childhood.[3]
Their relationship remains playful rather than exclusively professional. Several sisters successfully place Velina in a sack and carry her to the family gathering, despite Velina possessing more than enough power to escape. Victor later reveals that she voluntarily tolerated the prank because she was enjoying herself.[4][8]
Velina also participates readily in their collective experiments. Her initial attempt to act as the responsible voice during [[Cthugha]]'s creation collapses almost immediately once she is convinced Victor is supervising the situation, after which she contributes her own power to the project.[7]

### Skills & Abilities
- Blood Dragon Goddess Physiology: Velina is a naturally born daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and possesses the complete Blood Dragon foundation of the later Elderblood generation rather than beginning as a conventional Vampire or another species.
- True Dragon Physiology: She possesses a genuine True Dragon body and Soul, granting extraordinary supernatural compatibility, physical capability and adaptability.
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics: Velina possesses immense strength, speed, durability, reflexes and endurance even though scientific research rather than direct combat is her primary specialization.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Her True Dragon physiology grants the extreme regenerative properties characteristic of Victor's direct Blood Dragon lineage.
- Extreme Longevity: Velina remains physically youthful throughout at least approximately 190,000 subjective years of accelerated Imperial development.[5]
- Self-Sustenance / Extreme Stamina: Velnorah explicitly notes that Velina's True Dragon body has made ordinary fatigue largely irrelevant to her, contributing to her tendency to perform work personally instead of delegating it.[4]
- [[Draconic Energy]]: Velina naturally possesses and controls the Energy intrinsic to True Dragon physiology.
- [[Dragon Heart]]: As a complete True Dragon, Velina possesses the biological Draconic Energy system associated with the race. This should not be confused with the Imperial technological artifact also called the Dragon Heart.
- Draconic Shapeshifting: Velina possesses the racial foundation for Dragon metamorphosis and humanoid transformation, although her complete individual Dragon Form has not yet received a detailed description.
- Dragon Breath: Her True Dragon biology provides the racial foundation for Dragon Breath; the exact specialized properties of Velina's Breath remain unrevealed.
- Dragon Language: Velina possesses the biological and spiritual compatibility required to use the language of [[True Dragons]].
- Draconic Runes: Velina possesses practical mastery of Draconic Runes and integrates them into scientific and technological systems rather than treating them solely as combat [[Magic]].[1]
- Rune–Technology Integration: She combines Runes with Technomancy and machinery to improve technological performance and reliability.[1]
- Runic Failure Reduction: Velina specifically considers additional Draconic Rune implementation as a method of reducing the failure states of the Energy prototype created with Velnorah.[1]
- Runic Detection / Security: Velina uses multiple Draconic Runes to inspect her room for intruders before activating its defenses and beginning classified research.[7]
- Inherited Truth Sight / Reality Perception: Victor's biological daughters inherit variations of his ability to perceive the deeper truth underlying Reality. Victor describes this as seeing the "source code of Reality", including dimensions created according to the Rules of the Universe.[6]
- Conceptual Analysis: Velina directly demonstrates the inherited perception when examining [[Cthugha]] and rapidly understanding the significance of his contradictory native and foreign conceptual structure.[7]
- Potential Reality Manipulation: Victor states that his daughters can eventually alter the structures they perceive once they understand what they are doing, although Velina has not yet independently demonstrated the highest level of this capability.[6]
- Latent Eldritch Form: Like almost all of Victor's daughters, Velina possesses an Eldritch Form inherited through her father's cosmic nature. As of Chapter 1188 she explicitly does not yet know how to access it.[7]
- [[Eldritch Energy]]: Victor states that all of his daughters possess Eldritch Energy in varying quantities because of their inheritance from him. Velina therefore carries an Eldritch component despite not being explicitly identified as one of the daughters whose personal Divinity belongs to the [[Eldritch Pantheon]].[10]
- Teleportation: Velina can relocate herself supernatural distances effectively instantaneously, disappearing from her room to appear beside Velnorah and later teleporting herself home with a thought.[5][7]
- Technomancy: Velina is an advanced practitioner of the Technomancy inherited through Velnorah's civilization and uses it as one of the principal foundations of her research.[1][4]
- Technology Manipulation: She can operate and design systems where technological mechanisms and supernatural Energy function together.
- Technological Rune Integration: Draconic Runes can be incorporated directly into artificial systems rather than remaining separate magical effects.[1]
- Supernatural Engineering: Velina treats Divine, Draconic, Runic, technological and biological principles as compatible engineering components.
- Technomagic Engineering: Her later work extends to designing artificial Beings born with Technomancy as an inherent racial ability.[4]
- Supergenius Intelligence: Velina is one of the greatest scientists of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. By the mature Imperial era she is grouped directly with Velnorah, Ruby and Aline as one of the research giants responsible for major Imperial advancements.[4]
- Systems Engineering: She evaluates technologies as interacting networks of components and dependencies rather than isolated mechanisms.[1]
- Failure-State Analysis: Velina models millions of potential failure conditions and attempts to prepare contingencies for them.[1]
- Risk Analysis: Her engineering process explicitly considers unknown phenomena beyond currently modeled failures.
- Predictive Engineering: Velina attempts to anticipate how a system will behave after scaling, future modification or integration into civilization-level infrastructure.
- Rapid Learning: She can acquire and process technical information at extraordinary speed, using Imperial terminals and direct database access when manual reading would be inefficient.[5]
- Information Processing: Her Draconic cognition allows her to process amounts of technical information far beyond conventional biological intelligence.
- Interdisciplinary Research: Velina works across technology, physics, Energy systems, Runes, artificial Life, genetics, cloning, racial design and conceptual weapon research.
- Independent Theoretical Research: In less than an hour of private experimentation she can run enormous numbers of theories and simulations concerning Reality-level paradoxes.[7]
- Energy Engineering: Velina and Velnorah co-developed the prototype that became the technological foundation of the Imperial Dragon Heart.[1]
- Power-Source Design: She helped create an advanced Energy artifact intended to become a civilization-scale power source.
- Energy Analysis: After Victor transformed the artifact, Velina rapidly recognized that the new Dragon Heart produced vast quantities of essentially unaltered Energy.
- Energy Conversion Theory: She understands how a neutral or minimally attributed Energy source can subsequently be converted according to the needs of different supernatural technologies.
- Strategic Technology Applications: Velina immediately identified weapons, artificial beings and other Energy-intensive technologies that the Dragon Heart could enable.[1]
- Genetic Engineering: Velina directly participates in designing supernatural organisms from selected racial traits.[4]
- Genetic Trait Extraction: She works with Velnorah on extracting the exceptional adaptability of the Yuurakyo.
- Supernatural Hybridization: Velina assists in combining adaptability, Technomancy and selected properties of other supernatural species.[4]
- Racial Engineering: Her research extends from modifying individuals to constructing entire purpose-built populations.
- Cloning: Velina works directly with recreated members of species originating from Velnorah's former civilization.[4]
- Clone Design: She participates in deciding the biological and psychological parameters of artificial populations.
- Mental Design: Velina specifically asks what mindset newly created technomages should possess.[4]
- Knowledge Implantation Research: The planned technomages are designed to receive scientific knowledge directly rather than spending ordinary lifetimes learning their profession.[4]
- Artificial Life and Artificial Intelligence Research: Velina participates in planning artificial intelligences intended to possess genuine Souls, individuality and biological/supernatural Life rather than functioning as ordinary software.[1]
- The Pillar Project: She participates in the early planning of a living intelligence capable of helping administer the Empire.
- Biological–Technological Integration: Velina considers artificial technological architecture, Souls and biological Life parts of the same design problem.
- Loyalty Architecture: She proposes making the planned intelligence one of Victor's daughters, establishing loyalty through origin and family instead of relying solely upon programming.[1]

- Paradox Engineering: Velina originates the central concept behind the [[Absolute Paradox]].[5]
- Universal-Scale Theoretical Engineering: The project extends toward possible applications involving Life, Death, Space, Time, history, Laws, Concepts and the Universe itself.
- Conceptual Weapon Design: Velina co-develops a weapon whose intended effect is ontological contradiction rather than ordinary physical destruction.
- Universal Self-Correction Analysis: Her later tests establish experimentally that native contradictory Concepts are automatically reconciled by the Universe.[7]
- Cosmological Systems Analysis: Velina models the Universe as a self-correcting system and investigates whether an isolated region could escape its equivalent of an immune response.[7]
- Cross-Cosmological Analysis: Cthugha allows her to examine a stable Being simultaneously carrying native and foreign conceptual foundations, giving the project an important new research direction.[7]
- Collaborative Life Creation: Velina participated in the experiment that created Cthugha.[7]
- Intelligence Bestowal (Collaborative Creation): Velina explicitly contributed intelligence to the developing creature because she did not want the resulting Being to emerge unintelligent.[7]
- Great Old One Co-Creation: Cthugha ultimately developed Divinity and was classified as a Great Old One. The feat depended on the combined abilities of multiple sisters, Eldritch organisms, Leviathan properties and one of Victor's scales and should not be treated as Velina independently creating a Divine cosmic entity.[7]
- Research Leadership and Imperial R&D: Velina's name appears repeatedly on important Imperial projects, and she is recognized alongside Velnorah, Ruby and Aline among the greatest contributors to the Empire's scientific development.[4]
- Classified Research Familiarity: Her sisters regard Velina as the most likely person of their generation to know what the Empire's principal researchers are secretly developing.[3]
- Long-Term Research Experience: Velina possesses well over one hundred thousand subjective years of access to one of the most advanced scientific civilizations in her Sector.
- Research Delegation (Developing): Velina initially prefers performing work herself but is capable of accepting Velnorah's argument that delegation and outside perspectives are necessary for civilization-scale research.[4]
- Divinity (Unrevealed): Velina is explicitly a Dragon Goddess, but her individual Divine Concept has not yet been named. Her scientific expertise, the Absolute Paradox and her contribution of intelligence to Cthugha should not be converted into invented Divinities such as Technology, Science, Intelligence, Knowledge or Paradox without an explicit canonical statement.

### Weaknesses
Velina possesses few conventional biological weaknesses because of her True Dragon physiology, but several behavioral limitations have been identified.
- Arrogance: Velnorah believes Velina has become increasingly arrogant after spending immense spans of Time as part of an overwhelmingly powerful dynasty and civilization. She considers deliberately forcing her daughter to experience weakness again so that Velina does not repeat Velnorah's own historical mistakes.[4]
- Distorted Standards: Velina has become so accustomed to [[True Dragons]] and Imperial technology that species once considered extraordinary by Velnorah's former civilization can appear unimpressive to her.[4]
- Controlling Nature: She prefers personally understanding and supervising work and initially questions the necessity of subordinate research populations when the principal Imperial scientists can already perform enormous amounts of work themselves.[4]
- Scientific Obsession: Even after Velnorah explicitly orders her to leave prolonged [[Absolute Paradox]] research and return home, Velina secretly enters another laboratory and continues experimenting until dinner.[7]
- Curiosity / Boundary Crossing: Velina enters restricted spaces, investigates classified research and can ignore instructions when she believes she has discovered something scientifically important.[5]
- Father Bias: Her admiration for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is sufficiently intense that she treats claims of his imperfection as propositions requiring evidence rather than obvious statements.[1]
- Susceptibility to Interesting Ideas: Velina can move rapidly from criticizing an experiment as dangerously irresponsible to participating in it once she becomes convinced it is scientifically interesting and sufficiently supervised.[7]

### Notes
- Velina is not the sole creator of the [[Dragon Heart]]. She and Velnorah developed the Energy prototype; [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] then transformed it through his own powers, [[Junketsu]], Draconic Runes and a fragment of his Soul into the completed Dragon Heart.[1]
- Her most accurate credit is co-developer of the Dragon Heart's technological foundation.
- The artificial-intelligence discussions surrounding The Pillar should not be used to claim Velina personally created [[Akasha Elderblood]]. Akasha was later created directly by Victor.
- Velina's scientific status is explicitly supported rather than merely inferred: Chapter 1164 groups Velnorah, Ruby, Aline and Velina among the giants and greatest contributors of Imperial research.[4]
- Her work on the technomage population confirms expertise in cloning, genetic engineering and artificial racial design rather than restricting her scientific specialty to machines.[4]
- Velina possesses inherited Eldritch characteristics and a latent Eldritch Form, but the available material does not identify her personal Divinity as belonging specifically to the [[Eldritch Pantheon]].[7]
- Her inherited ability to perceive Reality's "source code" is now directly demonstrated through her analysis of [[Cthugha]] rather than existing only as a general racial statement about Victor's daughters.[6][7]
- The [[Absolute Paradox]] is a collaborative technological and conceptual research project, not Velina's personal supernatural ability.
- None of the Absolute Paradox's proposed effects should currently be used as Velina's personal Attack Potency, Existence Erasure, Concept Manipulation, Time Manipulation or Reality Warping.
- A completed Absolute Paradox does not yet exist. Native contradictions have repeatedly been corrected automatically by Reality.[7]
- Cthugha is an important research clue rather than proof that the Absolute Paradox has been completed.[7]
- Velina is one of Cthugha's co-creators, but his creation was a collective Elderblood experiment involving multiple sisters, Eldritch organisms, Leviathans and Victor's scale.[7]
- Her personal Divine Concept remains unidentified.
- No reviewed source establishes a dedicated Science, Technology, Knowledge, Intelligence or Paradox Divinity for Velina.
- Velina should not automatically be assigned the complete power set of an ordinary Vampire. She was born as a Blood Dragon and True Dragon rather than beginning as a conventional Noble Vampire.
- The old childhood-age estimates are obsolete. Velina was already alive by Chapter 1004 and subsequently experienced at least approximately 190,000 subjective years of accelerated Imperial development.[1][5]
