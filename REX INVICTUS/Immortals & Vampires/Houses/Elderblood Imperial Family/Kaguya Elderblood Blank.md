#### Debut
[[Ch04 - Rex Natus II|Chapter 4]]: An Incredibly Beautiful Woman
My Husband is a generous man; he will not do anything against Beings who take credit for his efforts for themselves... But we are different; his Wives exist to protect his pride... No, our pride. While he is focused on more important things for the future, we will protect it from all the trash.Anyone who threatens our pride will be eliminated, whether you are an ally or not.
—Kaguya, Chapter 986
Kaguya Elderblood Blank is a True Blood Dragon, Dragon Goddess of Darkness, Chief Maid and wife of [[Victor Elderblood]], Vice-Leader of the [[Blank Clan]], and Commander of the Third Legion of the [[Elderblood Empire]], the Emperor's Black Hand. Originally known as Kaguya Blank, she was born into a Noble Vampire family whose hereditary obligation was to serve the [[Snow Clan]] as maids, spies and assassins. Her exceptional training, mastery over Shadows, and unwavering dedication to becoming the perfect maid eventually brought her into the service of [[Violet Elderblood Snow]], through whom she first encountered Victor. What began as an obligation imposed upon her family ultimately became a freely chosen relationship that transformed Kaguya from a hereditary servant into one of the most trusted women in Victor's life.[5][6]
Although Kaguya originally served Violet, she was among the first people to recognize that Victor's abnormal transformation had created a situation requiring cooperation between the three Count families. Her early investigations into [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] and [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] helped prevent Victor's newly formed household from collapsing beneath jealousy, Bloodlust and political interference. Her intelligence and willingness to act independently of Violet's possessiveness distinguished her from an ordinary attendant, while Victor's habit of acknowledging her efforts gradually inspired a personal loyalty that her hereditary obligations had never produced.[7][8]
Kaguya subsequently became Victor's first personal maid and the founding senior member of his [[Maid Squad]]. She frequently remained concealed within his shadow, accompanying him into battle, gathering information, protecting his family and assisting with the development of his growing faction. Her service gradually developed into a romantic relationship, culminating in Victor offering her a permanent place within his Clan and marriage. Kaguya accepted both, choosing to retain her Blank heritage while entering Victor's direct Bloodline and eventually adopting the name Kaguya Elderblood Blank.[9][10][2][3]
The transformation into an Elderblood Vampire greatly strengthened Kaguya's inherited Darkness and awakened the dormant Fire characteristics of her Snow ancestry. Following Victor's ascension as the Dragon God of [[Chaos]], she evolved into a True Blood Dragon whose individual nature remained closely connected to Shadows. Her development continued until she became a full Dragon Goddess possessing an established Divinity of Darkness, which Victor further strengthened with a compatible copy of the Darkness power he acquired from [[Erebus]].[1][11][4]
Kaguya possesses a particularly unusual relationship with Darkness. As a Dragon Goddess whose supernatural existence is associated with Shadows, her sixth sense extends through phenomena related to Shadows and Darkness. The narration compares this perception to Nyx Elderblood's ability to extend her presence through the dark night sky, explaining that Kaguya can similarly be present through her Shadows. This gives her a form of limited Shadow-Based Omnipresence and greatly enhances her ability to conduct surveillance, observe distant events and operate through the Darkness associated with her element. The novel explicitly identifies these complementary abilities as a major reason Kaguya and Nyx are exceptionally effective at directing the Empire's intelligence operations.[12]
Kaguya's importance eventually extended far beyond Victor's household. Her authority over Clan Blank, experience coordinating assassins and intelligence operatives, and access to one of the Empire's most unusual supernatural surveillance abilities made her a natural candidate to organize Imperial intelligence. Together with Nyx and [[Persephone Elderblood]], she helped establish the Third Legion — Black Hand of the Emperor, becoming its Commander while Nyx serves as her deputy and alternate commander. The Legion specializes in espionage, infiltration, counterintelligence, psychological warfare, the investigation of treason, and operations that cannot be entrusted to conventional military forces.[13]
Despite becoming an Empress, Dragon Goddess, military commander and one of the most influential intelligence authorities in the Elderblood Empire, Kaguya continues identifying herself primarily as Victor's chosen maid. She voluntarily performs household duties even when Imperial technology and [[Magic]] could replace most ordinary service, believing that meaningful work, discipline and personal responsibility remain valuable regardless of status or power. She and Victor are also the parents of [[Yuno Elderblood]], a daughter whose personality and developing Divine characteristics have become subjects of considerable amusement within the Imperial family.[14][15][16]

### Physical Appearance

#### Noble Vampire
Kaguya is a pale-skinned woman of Japanese descent whose original appearance reflected both her Japanese Vampire ancestry and the standards of the Noble family she served. At her debut she possessed short black hair, dark eyes, a comparatively petite physique and a consistently composed facial expression. Her appearance was understated in comparison with the more extravagant Noble Vampires surrounding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], with her controlled demeanor and practical clothing emphasizing her responsibilities rather than attracting attention to herself.[17][6]
Kaguya ordinarily wore a traditional black-and-white maid uniform, which became one of her most recognizable characteristics. She preferred clothing appropriate for service and assassination rather than the elaborate dresses generally associated with members of [[Nightingale]]'s Noble families. Her restrained appearance also complemented the capabilities of her Shadow Bloodline, allowing her to pass unnoticed during ordinary household activities before disappearing completely into Darkness when the situation required it.[5][18]
Her early development was gradually influenced by consuming Victor's Blood. By the period preceding her direct incorporation into his Bloodline, Kaguya stood approximately 165 centimeters tall, possessing a slightly more mature appearance than she had during her first encounters with Victor. Nevertheless, she remained physically smaller than many of his wives and retained the unassuming presence characteristic of her original role.[3]

#### Elderblood Vampire
Kaguya underwent a dramatic transformation when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] incorporated her into his direct Bloodline. Her height increased from approximately 165 to 170 centimeters, her physique matured, and her previously short black hair grew considerably longer, flowing down her back. Its appearance became noticeably darker and more lustrous, resembling the night sky. Her eyes also changed permanently to the vivid Blood-red color associated with Victor's descendants and the Vampires directly incorporated into his lineage.[3]
Her Darkness reacted instinctively to the transformation, surrounding her body before forming a new Gothic maid uniform. The clothing reflected her supernatural abilities while preserving the identity she valued most. Despite the substantial increase in her beauty and physical development, Kaguya did not abandon the restrained presentation of a maid or attempt to distinguish herself from the other women through elaborate Noble attire.[3]
The new Bloodline also awakened characteristics inherited from her [[Snow Clan]] ancestors. For the first time, Kaguya could generate Fire in addition to Darkness, demonstrating that Victor's Blood had strengthened hereditary traits that had remained dormant throughout generations of Clan Blank. Her transformed appearance consequently represented more than an increase in physical maturity; it reflected a fundamental change in her supernatural ancestry and future developmental potential.[3]

#### Blood Dragon
Following her transformation into a True Blood Dragon, Kaguya acquired a considerably more imposing Draconic appearance. During the initial transformation, her height reached approximately 193 centimeters, and her physique became more mature. Her skin grew even paler, contrasting sharply with hair that appeared to be formed from Darkness itself. The supernatural darkness associated with her body made her presence feel increasingly disconnected from ordinary light, while her eyes could appear completely black and empty.[1]
Her hair became exceptionally long, extending toward the floor and moving with an appearance resembling living Darkness. Kaguya continued producing black clothing through her supernatural element, retaining a maid's appearance despite possessing a body fundamentally different from that of a Noble Vampire. Her Draconic evolution also granted the distinctive characteristics associated with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood Dragon lineage, including horns, wings and the altered physical structure of a True Dragon.[1]
The transformation did not remove the qualities that had defined Kaguya as a member of Clan Blank. Instead, her Draconic physiology magnified the Darkness and Shadows that had always been part of her existence. Her appearance consequently remained strongly associated with those elements, allowing her to differ visibly from other [[Blood Dragons]] whose individual natures were connected to Fire, Ice, Lightning or other supernatural characteristics.[1][11]

#### Imperial Goddess Form
As her Divine nature developed, Kaguya acquired an even more extraordinary form appropriate to her status as a Dragon Goddess and Empress. In Chapter 1104, she appeared approximately 4.70 meters tall, possessing long black Draconic horns, bright red Dragon Eyes and long black hair cascading toward her thighs. Her dress resembled the Darkness of the starry sky, making it appear as though the fabric had been created from the night sky itself.[4]
Her movements produced supernatural Darkness that remained beneath her feet for several seconds before disappearing. The material also surrounded her legs, making it difficult for observers to distinguish precisely where her body ended and her Divine element began. Although she was walking, the visual effect resembled floating through the air. This Darkness was a manifestation of her Divine power rather than an ordinary garment or decorative spell.[4]
By this stage, Kaguya possessed an established Divinity of Darkness. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had strengthened it by granting her a copy of the compatible Darkness power he previously acquired from [[Erebus]], allowing her to advance further in the Divine Concept connected to her existence. Her supernatural appearance therefore reflected her development from a Shadow-controlling Vampire into a genuine Goddess whose authority was associated with Darkness itself.[4]
Despite the magnificence of this form, Kaguya remained comparatively uncomfortable presenting herself ceremonially as Victor's wife. When Victor asked whether she would prefer remaining within his shadow, she answered immediately that she would. Nevertheless, she admitted that accompanying him openly as his wife was also pleasant, revealing that her attachment to her original role continued to coexist with genuine happiness in her position as an Empress.[4]

### Personality
Kaguya is disciplined, intelligent, observant, proud and intensely devoted to the people she considers her family. Her defining characteristic is the importance she places upon fulfilling her responsibilities properly, whether serving as a maid, protecting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], leading assassins, supervising Imperial intelligence or raising her daughter. She possesses an exceptionally controlled temperament and rarely allows her emotions to interfere with the immediate execution of her duties. Nevertheless, her restrained expression frequently conceals amusement, irritation, affection, embarrassment and a considerable capacity for ruthlessness.[6][3][19]
The most important contradiction in Kaguya's early personality was her simultaneous love of service and resentment toward compulsory servitude. She had been trained from childhood to obey the leader or heir of Clan Snow, learning that her personal preferences were secondary to the hereditary obligations imposed upon Clan Blank. Kaguya genuinely enjoyed being a maid, but believed that someone who had never been allowed to choose a master could not truly represent the ideal of a perfect servant. Her ambition was therefore not to abandon service, but to obtain the freedom necessary to make that service meaningful.[5][6]
Victor became important to Kaguya because he unintentionally fulfilled emotional needs that her upbringing had neglected. His early habit of praising her work, thanking her sincerely and treating her as a person rather than an interchangeable servant distinguished him from the Noble Vampires she had previously encountered. Even a simple acknowledgment that she had performed well could affect Kaguya deeply because recognition was something she had spent much of her childhood trying to obtain. Her initially professional interest in Victor gradually became admiration and an increasingly personal desire to remain near him.[5][6]
Her loyalty is accompanied by considerable independence. Even while she formally served Violet, Kaguya was willing to act against her mistress's immediate wishes when she believed those wishes endangered Victor or the household. Her decision to investigate Ruby and Sasha demonstrated that she did not confuse obedience with allowing her master to make avoidable mistakes. This quality eventually made her an indispensable adviser to Victor, who increasingly relied upon her judgment when he was too occupied with battle or personal matters to evaluate every political and practical concern himself.[5][8]
Kaguya's attitude toward personal sacrifice was fundamentally altered by Victor. When she suffered catastrophic injuries while protecting Sasha, she initially believed that successfully defending her master's wife should earn his approval. Victor instead reprimanded her for treating her own life as expendable. He wanted Kaguya to survive because she was precious to him, not because she could continue performing useful duties. His reaction challenged the values of her upbringing and helped transform her loyalty from professional devotion into a relationship based upon mutual emotional attachment.[20][21]
Although Kaguya generally maintains a serious demeanor, she possesses a dry sense of humor and frequently responds to the absurd behavior of Victor's family with restrained sarcasm. Her familiarity with the household allows her to recognize emotional changes that strangers would overlook, and she often understands Victor's intentions before he formally explains them. She can also be surprisingly possessive, particularly when another person receives attention that interferes with her accustomed position beside her husband. These tendencies are usually concealed beneath the outward discipline of a perfect maid rather than expressed through the explosive jealousy displayed by some of her fellow wives.[10][12]
Her association with the Sin of Pride reflects the enormous importance she places upon personal excellence, responsibility and the dignity of Victor's family. Kaguya does not regard pride as an excuse to avoid work or demand recognition without merit. Instead, she believes that individuals occupying important positions must behave in a manner worthy of their status. Her perfectionism can make her exceptionally strict toward herself and others, particularly when she believes carelessness or laziness could damage the reputation of the household.[15]
Kaguya's pride also produces a ruthless attitude toward people who deliberately insult Victor's accomplishments. The incident involving [[Ganesha]] demonstrated that she does not automatically share her husband's tolerance for insignificant political provocations. Victor might choose to ignore an attempt to claim credit for his work because he has more important responsibilities, but Kaguya regards such behavior as an insult toward the collective efforts and dignity of the entire Imperial family. She considers it the responsibility of Victor's wives to protect that shared pride even when their husband has no desire to retaliate personally.[19]
This ruthless mentality exists alongside a practical concern for the safety and stability of the Empire. Kaguya's responsibilities within Clan Blank and the Black Hand involve identifying threats before they reach the public, gathering intelligence, eliminating dangerous conspirators and preventing foreign factions from exploiting weaknesses in Imperial security. She considers these duties necessary rather than inherently enjoyable acts of cruelty, although she is capable of expressing obvious satisfaction when a particularly deserving enemy receives punishment.[15][13]
Despite becoming powerful enough to delegate virtually all ordinary service, Kaguya continues performing household work voluntarily. She believes that meaningful activity is essential for maintaining discipline and mental stability, particularly among immortal supernatural beings whose extraordinary lifespans and access to advanced technology could otherwise encourage complacency. This conviction explains why she still wakes early, prepares herself for work, supervises household rules and serves meals even when she has no material reason to continue acting as a maid.[15]
Her household authority extends to women who would ordinarily possess much higher social positions than a maid. Kaguya regularly corrects the behavior of her fellow wives when they disregard rules concerning privacy, clothing or the presence of children and servants. Although her strictness occasionally creates disagreements, she is capable of recognizing when she has overreacted and apologizing sincerely. The thousands of years she has spent living with Victor's family have made her sufficiently comfortable that discipline and affection can coexist without threatening their relationships.[15]
Motherhood has also deepened Kaguya's understanding of responsibility. She expects the children of the Imperial family to receive appropriate protection and guidance, but does not believe that extraordinary ancestry should excuse undisciplined behavior. During the later family discussions, she was among the mothers who considered the daily monitoring of their daughters' developing Divinities an essential responsibility, particularly because those daughters were growing into powerful Dragon Goddesses rather than ordinary children.[16]
Kaguya's behavior also demonstrates that she has not lost the possessive qualities associated with many of Victor's wives. During the celestial banquet, she firmly rejected Bruna's offer to assist with serving the family because she wanted to perform the task personally. Her refusal was so absolute that Bruna immediately withdrew, while Yuno regarded her mother's behavior with obvious pride. The incident illustrated how closely Kaguya's perfectionism and possessiveness remain connected to her identity as Victor's personal maid.[22]
Her unusual humor became particularly apparent during the family dispute in Chapter 1157. When the wives detected that Victor had attracted additional women, Kaguya jokingly referred to their shared possessive reaction as a form of supernatural instinct. Ruby objected to the terminology, leading to a discussion in which Kaguya distinguished the family's possessive reactions from her genuine Shadow-based sixth sense. The conversation revealed both her willingness to participate in her sisters' absurd discussions and her familiarity with the extraordinary sensory abilities she and Nyx employ during Imperial intelligence operations.[12]
Above all, Kaguya continues regarding service to Victor as something she freely chose rather than a position imposed upon her by birth. Her marriage, motherhood, Divine evolution and Imperial authority have given her opportunities that were unimaginable during her childhood, but none has diminished the significance of the decision that defined her life. She became the perfect maid not because Clan Snow ordered her to serve someone, but because she found a person whom she genuinely wished to serve and never abandoned that choice.[6][2][22]

### History
Kaguya was born approximately two centuries before the beginning of the story into the [[Blank Clan]], an ancient subsidiary family whose history was closely connected to the [[Snow Clan]]. The family originated through the union of a member of the Snow main family and a Japanese Noble Vampire whose Bloodline possessed supernatural authority over Shadows. Their descendants inherited aspects of both lineages, combining the Shadow powers of the Japanese ancestor with certain passive characteristics belonging to Clan Snow.[6]
The original family eventually lost the political protection that had accompanied its connection to the Snow main line. Following the death of the Japanese Vampire and the disappearance of her Snow Clan husband, the descendants were reduced to a subordinate branch whose members were expected to serve the Count family. Over generations, that obligation became central to Clan Blank's identity, transforming a family possessing rare supernatural abilities into a hereditary organization of servants, spies and assassins.[6]
Unlike the principal Snow family, Clan Blank generally did not inherit active Fire Manipulation. Its members retained the Snow lineage's unusual resistance to sunlight and Fire, but their principal supernatural specialization became Darkness and Shadows. This made them exceptionally useful for surveillance, assassination and covert operations, gradually establishing the family as one of the principal intelligence institutions serving Clan Snow.[6]
Kaguya was raised within this system from childhood. She received extensive instruction in household work, etiquette, intelligence gathering, assassination, the specialized Shadow techniques of her Clan, and the behavior expected of a personal attendant serving a powerful Noble family. Her training emphasized efficiency, emotional restraint, obedience and the ability to accomplish difficult assignments without attracting unnecessary attention.[5][6]
She eventually became the personal maid of [[Violet Elderblood Snow]], whose position as the heir and later Countess of Clan Snow made the assignment especially important. Kaguya carried out her duties with exceptional competence and developed a detailed understanding of Violet's possessive personality. However, she privately resented that her future had been determined by ancestry rather than personal preference. She believed a truly perfect maid should possess the freedom to select the person worthy of her service, even if doing so contradicted the traditions maintained by her birth Clan.[6]
The arrival of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] Walker, Violet's newly transformed husband, eventually gave Kaguya an opportunity to make that choice. Although she initially viewed him as another unusual responsibility accompanying her mistress's unpredictable behavior, Victor's gratitude, personal warmth and refusal to treat servants as expendable gradually changed her opinion. Her growing attachment to him ultimately became the foundation of a new life extending far beyond the hereditary position for which she had originally been trained.[5][6]

### Synopsis

#### The Awakening
Kaguya first appeared after [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] brought the newly transformed [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] Walker into her household. As Violet's personal maid, Kaguya was responsible for assisting the couple and ensuring that Victor's unfamiliarity with supernatural society did not interfere with his adjustment to becoming a Vampire. Her extraordinary speed, efficiency and composure immediately distinguished her from ordinary Human attendants, although Victor initially possessed little understanding of the extensive training required for her position.[17][5]
Kaguya's impression of Victor began changing through seemingly insignificant interactions. While preparing his clothing and performing other duties, she received sincere praise and affectionate recognition from a man who had no particular reason to value the efforts of a servant belonging to another Clan. Victor's habit of thanking her reminded Kaguya of the acknowledgment she had sought throughout childhood, gradually making her curious about the newborn Vampire who behaved so differently from the aristocrats she had previously served.[5][6]
At the same time, Kaguya recognized that Victor's transformation had created serious complications. Violet's Blood Ritual had unexpectedly connected Victor to Ruby Scarlett and Sasha Fulger, whose involvement could not be dismissed merely because Violet wanted to monopolize him. Kaguya understood that keeping the women separated could intensify the supernatural consequences of their incomplete relationships, especially as Victor's unusual Blood began producing changes in their conditions.[5][6]
Rather than blindly obey Violet's wishes, Kaguya independently investigated Ruby and Sasha. She first approached Ruby and her maid Luna, learning that Ruby was suffering from the same unusual Bloodlust connected to Victor. The investigation made it increasingly clear that the problem was not merely jealousy between three Noble Vampires but an abnormal supernatural bond whose consequences none of the participants fully understood.[7]
Kaguya subsequently investigated Sasha Fulger, whose situation had become considerably more dangerous. Following the massacre at the Fulger residence and the death of Julia, Sasha was overwhelmed by grief, humiliation and a desire for immediate revenge against the Inquisition. Kaguya recognized that the emotionally unstable Vampire was likely to pursue a suicidal confrontation if allowed to leave without intervention. She therefore incapacitated Sasha and the newly transformed Maria before transporting them to Victor.[23][8]
The encounter forced Kaguya to reveal the truth surrounding Victor's marriage. When Sasha came near him, their supernatural connection became impossible to ignore, and Kaguya explained that both Sasha and Ruby had become his wives through the abnormal transformation ritual. The revelation initially intensified Violet's possessiveness, but ultimately allowed the three women to begin confronting a situation that would otherwise have continued endangering their relationships and supernatural stability.[8]
Kaguya also participated in Victor's first major confrontation with the Inquisition. During the battle against Mizuki, the experienced hunter inflicted severe injuries upon Victor and attempted to exploit his weakened state to decapitate him. Kaguya emerged from the Shadows and intervened, providing the moment necessary for Victor's wives to respond and preventing the confrontation from ending in his death.[18][24]
The encounter demonstrated the importance of Kaguya's role as a hidden protector. Victor possessed extraordinary potential, but he remained a newly transformed Vampire whose regeneration and unusual powers could not compensate for every mistake. Kaguya's willingness to remain within his shadow allowed her to identify threats and act without requiring him to recognize the danger beforehand. This method of protection would become one of the defining aspects of their relationship throughout the following volumes.[18][24]

#### Vampire Society
Kaguya accompanied [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to [[Nightingale]] after [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] took him to the Vampire homeland for training. Rather than remain behind with Violet, Kaguya concealed herself within Victor's shadow, revealing her presence after his arrival. Her decision allowed her to continue protecting him while simultaneously observing his development within a civilization far older and more complicated than the isolated supernatural society he had previously encountered on Earth.[25]
During Victor's training, Kaguya witnessed the extraordinary speed with which he learned to control the abilities inherited from the three Count Bloodlines. His instinctive development, unusual Regeneration and emerging authority over other Vampires challenged her understanding of how a Noble Vampire should grow. Alongside [[Lacus Scarlett Elderblood]], Kaguya increasingly recognized that Victor could not be evaluated through the ordinary expectations applied to a newly transformed member of their Race.[26]
Her relationship with Victor became official when Violet released Kaguya from the hereditary arrangement connecting them and permitted her to enter Victor's direct service. Kaguya informed Victor that she was now his personal maid and attempted to establish a suitable contract. When Victor pointed out that he lacked enough money to employ her properly, she offered to lend him the necessary funds until he could become financially independent. The arrangement demonstrated that she valued remaining near Victor more highly than the ordinary benefits of her position.[9]
Kaguya consequently became Victor's first personal maid, establishing a direct master-servant relationship founded upon her own choice. Although she continued respecting Violet and the Snow family, her primary loyalty had shifted toward the person whom she genuinely wanted to serve. She increasingly occupied Victor's shadow for long periods, accompanying him through daily life, private discussions, political meetings and dangerous confrontations.[9]
Victor's return to Earth brought Kaguya into further contact with his parents and the increasingly complicated relationships surrounding his new existence. Her unusual ability to remain hidden close to him allowed her to observe circumstances that ordinary servants could not witness, making her one of the first people to recognize the full extent of his developing personality and the unusual behavior of the women surrounding him.[27]
Kaguya also participated in Victor's confrontation involving Annasthashia and William William. Their battle demonstrated how effectively Victor could combine his own powers with the actions of a hidden assassin. Kaguya emerged from his Shadows to attack William's throat while Victor assaulted Annasthashia through his Fire, Ice and Lightning. Their coordination helped Victor survive a confrontation against opponents whose accumulated experience greatly exceeded his own.[28]
By the end of this period, Kaguya had become considerably more than an attendant responsible for Victor's clothing and ordinary comfort. She functioned as his concealed bodyguard, intelligence gatherer, combat partner and trusted observer, establishing the relationship that would eventually make her indispensable to the development of his faction.[28]

#### A New Pillar
Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s promotion to the Fifth Vampire Count, Kaguya's responsibilities expanded alongside the newly founded Clan Alucard. She recognized that Victor could not maintain an independent Count family through personal strength alone. A proper Clan required reliable subordinates capable of protecting its territories, managing ordinary responsibilities and defending family members when Victor was absent. Her observations contributed to Victor's growing interest in gathering exceptional servants and establishing a more organized household.[29]
The most important personal development of this period occurred during the confrontation at the former Fulger residence. Kaguya entered the battle to protect Sasha but suffered catastrophic injuries from the Church's forces. Victor arrived and discovered his maid in a condition that threatened her life, causing him to react with overwhelming anger. He supplied his Blood to accelerate her recovery before retaliating against the individuals responsible.[20][30][31]
After Kaguya recovered, she expected that successfully defending Sasha would be considered proof of her dedication. Victor instead criticized her willingness to risk her life unnecessarily, explaining that her own safety was supposed to remain a priority. His anger originated from the possibility of losing someone precious to him, not from dissatisfaction with her performance. He nevertheless thanked Kaguya sincerely for protecting his wife, making clear that her actions had been appreciated even though the price she nearly paid was unacceptable.[21]
The conversation permanently altered Kaguya's understanding of their relationship. She had been raised to consider personal sacrifice a natural consequence of service, but Victor refused to accept that logic when it threatened someone he loved. Kaguya consequently developed an even stronger determination to improve herself, understanding that becoming the perfect maid required surviving and continuing to protect her family rather than merely dying while fulfilling an assignment.[21]
As Victor's household expanded, Kaguya naturally became the senior maid responsible for supervising the newcomers. [[Bruna Elderblood]], [[Eve Elderblood]], [[Maria Elderblood]] and other women joined his service, eventually forming the developing [[Maid Squad]]. Kaguya's seniority, training and exceptional efficiency made her the obvious person to coordinate their responsibilities, while her strict standards shaped the household's expectations concerning loyalty and discipline.[32][33]
Her combat abilities also improved considerably through her relationship with Victor. Repeated exposure to his Blood increased her strength and allowed her to maintain advanced Clan Blank techniques previously beyond her capabilities. During the confrontation against [[Werewolves]], she demonstrated Dance of Darkness, employing her Shadow weapons and transformed hair in a coordinated fighting style capable of attacking numerous enemies while protecting herself from retaliation.[34]
Kaguya continued serving beside Victor as his strength and political status increased, and by the time of his later confrontation against Anderson, four maids could emerge directly from his Shadows to prepare the battlefield. Kaguya employed her Darkness as a defensive territory while Roberta, Bruna and Eve participated in the preparations. The scene demonstrated how thoroughly Victor had begun integrating his personal attendants into an organized supernatural fighting force.[35]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
By the time [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s status as the Second Vampire Progenitor became widely recognized, Kaguya had established herself as one of his most dependable personal advisers. Her responsibilities increasingly included representing his interests in negotiations, evaluating visitors and participating in political exchanges involving the Witches and other supernatural factions. During meetings involving [[Selena Moriarty]], Kaguya demonstrated that she possessed the judgment necessary to negotiate with individuals whose interests extended far beyond the ordinary affairs of a Noble household.[36][37]
Kaguya's habit of remaining within Victor's shadow had become almost permanent. She rarely left his side unless specifically instructed to perform another responsibility and openly admitted that she enjoyed occupying his shadow because it was comfortable. The unusual arrangement allowed her to remain aware of Victor's circumstances without interfering in conversations unnecessarily, while her constant proximity made her one of the few people familiar with nearly every aspect of his daily life.[10]
The crisis following [[Adonis Snow]]'s death brought Kaguya back into direct contact with the political responsibilities of her birth family. Investigations exposed serious infiltration and betrayal within Snow territory, requiring coordinated intervention by Victor's supporters and Clan Blank. Kaguya participated in eliminating traitorous operatives, recognizing that her loyalty toward Victor did not excuse allowing members of her own family to endanger the people she had chosen to protect.[38][39]
Her progress impressed [[Oda Blank]], who recognized that she had developed several abilities normally requiring much longer periods of training. Oda also understood that Kaguya's relationship with Victor represented a departure from the traditions that had confined Clan Blank to compulsory service. Rather than condemn her choice, he acknowledged that a Shinobi should possess the right to select a worthy master and appointed Kaguya the Second Leader of Clan Blank.[40]
The appointment granted Kaguya access to restricted Clan techniques and authority over operatives belonging to one of [[Nightingale]]'s most experienced assassination networks. It also formally recognized the change in her relationship with Clan Snow: Kaguya remained a member of Clan Blank, but her primary allegiance now belonged to Victor, and the Clan's future could develop through a relationship founded upon personal choice rather than hereditary obligation.[40]
Kaguya also contributed to an important aspect of Victor's personal fighting style during this period. When he created an enormous Japanese-style sword from his Blood, she proposed the name [[Junketsu]], meaning Purity. The name reflected the extraordinarily pure Blood from which the weapon had been formed and established another connection between Kaguya's Japanese heritage and one of Victor's most recognizable possessions.[41]
As Victor's maids became increasingly powerful, [[Scathach]] took a more active interest in their combat training. She recognized that Kaguya, Eve, Bruna, Maria and the others could no longer depend entirely upon Victor to rescue them whenever danger appeared. Their training subsequently became more demanding, gradually transforming the maids into a genuine elite fighting force whose members possessed the ability and experience necessary to protect themselves and operate independently.[42]
The growing independence of the [[Maid Squad]] also reinforced Kaguya's position within Victor's household. She remained the most experienced member of the group, but the other women increasingly developed their own abilities, responsibilities and personal attachments to their master. The Squad was consequently becoming a family of capable supernatural individuals rather than a collection of ordinary domestic servants.[43][44]

#### Demonic War
During the Demonic War, Kaguya's Shadow abilities became one of the most important components of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s fighting style. She could conceal entire groups within her Darkness, transport allies without exposing their presence and provide support while occupying Victor's shadow. This allowed Victor to deploy his forces unexpectedly, protect vulnerable companions and combine the abilities of several maids during exceptionally dangerous confrontations.[45]
One of her greatest contributions occurred during the battle at the Vatican. Kaguya employed the secret Clan Blank technique known as Shadow Incarnation, allowing her to transform into pure Darkness and merge completely with Victor's shadow. The technique used Victor as a supernatural conduit, allowing its duration to benefit from his extraordinary stamina. Through this connection, Kaguya could support his movements and abilities while remaining directly integrated into his Shadow.[45]
Kaguya also employed Helheim, a large-scale Darkness technique creating a vast territory associated with her supernatural element. The technique helped contain and support the extraordinary powers being released during the confrontation, while the maids hidden within Kaguya's Shadows emerged to participate in the battle. The operation demonstrated that her value as Victor's personal maid extended beyond assassination: she had become a central component of the way his strongest allies could operate together.[45][46]
The Demonic War also brought the romantic development between Victor and his maids to a decisive point. Victor recognized that Kaguya, Bruna, Eve, Maria and Roberta had repeatedly fought and risked their lives alongside him, making it impossible to regard their relationships as ordinary employment. Their emotional attachments had already transformed them into members of his family, and he decided to offer them permanent places within his Clan rather than continue pretending that their bonds could be defined through service contracts alone.[47][2]
For Kaguya, the proposal represented the fulfillment of the ideal she had pursued since childhood. Victor was not demanding that she abandon Clan Blank or surrender the identity she valued. He was offering her the opportunity to become his wife and permanent family member while continuing to serve him freely. Kaguya accepted, recognizing that the relationship she had chosen was no longer limited to the distinction between master and servant.[2]
Victor subsequently performed a specialized Blood Ritual to incorporate her directly into his Progenitor Bloodline. The procedure removed the ancestral dependence upon the Bloodline of the old Vampire Progenitor and replaced it with Victor's own supernatural lineage. Kaguya's body matured, her eyes became permanently red, her Shadow powers grew considerably stronger and the dormant Fire element inherited through Clan Snow finally awakened.[3]
Kaguya initially considered different arrangements of her name before choosing Kaguya Alucard Blank. She wanted Victor's family name to precede her birth Clan because he had become both her husband and chosen master. Nevertheless, she refused to abandon the Blank name entirely, preserving her connection to the family whose traditions and abilities had shaped her life.[3]
Victor also granted Kaguya considerable authority within his household and faction. He informed her that she could speak for him whenever he was absent, a responsibility she initially found surprising because it could place her in a position of authority over other members of his family. Victor reminded her that she was now one of his wives and that his trust in her was not restricted by the title of maid.[3]
The marriage also strengthened Clan Blank's political position. Kaguya interpreted her union with Victor as an alliance between her birth family and Clan Alucard, ensuring that the relationship benefited the descendants of the family from which she had originated rather than requiring her to abandon them entirely.[48]

#### King Among Kings
Kaguya continued occupying an increasingly important position as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction expanded beyond a Noble Vampire Clan and began acquiring the characteristics of an independent supernatural civilization. Her responsibilities combined the management of his personal household, leadership over the [[Maid Squad]], authority within Clan Blank and the security obligations associated with protecting the family of a Vampire Progenitor.[49]
As Victor's religious and political influence increased, the women surrounding him began developing organizations capable of supporting his authority across different supernatural [[Races (Flamel)|Races]]. Kaguya participated in this growing administrative structure, alongside figures associated with the [[Blood God Religion]], the Noble Vampire families and Victor's expanding network of supernatural allies. Her experience managing covert operations made her especially valuable whenever ordinary political or military methods were unsuitable.[50]
Her military importance became particularly clear during the campaign against [[Olympus]]. Following Victor's victory over the principal Greek Gods, he placed the Maid Squad under Kaguya's command and ordered them to eliminate the remaining hostile Divine beings, excluding [[Athena]] and [[Poseidon]]. Because ordinary weapons might fail to permanently kill Gods, Victor coated the maids' weapons with his own Progenitor Blood, allowing their attacks to affect their targets beyond conventional physical injuries.[49]
Kaguya consequently directed some of Victor's most trusted combatants during the destruction of a major Pantheon. The assignment demonstrated how far she had developed from the hidden assassin who had originally protected a newborn Vampire on Earth. She was now a commander entrusted with the elimination of Divine enemies and the execution of decisions affecting entire supernatural civilizations.[49]
Kaguya also remained involved in the political struggle surrounding the Werewolf Kingdom. Her intelligence operations contributed to Victor's understanding of the conflict between [[Volk Fenrir]] and [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]], allowing his faction to obtain information concerning the King's attempts to undermine his wife. These developments reinforced the value of maintaining an intelligence network capable of gathering information before political disputes escalated into battles.[50]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ascension as the Dragon God of [[Chaos]] initiated a fundamental transformation among the individuals closest to him. Rather than remain limited to their original supernatural species, members of his family were offered the opportunity to enter his developing Draconic lineage. Kaguya was among those reconstructed as a True Blood Dragon, replacing the underlying limitations of her Noble Vampire body with the superior supernatural foundation of the Dragon Race.[1]
The transformation did not erase Kaguya's original Shadow abilities or the identity she had developed through Clan Blank. Her Draconic nature instead incorporated these characteristics, producing a Shadow Dragon whose body, senses and supernatural Energy possessed a far stronger connection to Darkness than her previous Vampire existence. Her appearance became taller and more imposing, while her hair and aura increasingly resembled living Darkness.[1]
Kaguya also acquired the broader supernatural characteristics of a True Dragon, including superior physical attributes, expanded metamorphic capabilities and an instinctive relationship with Creation. Her personal Shadow abilities developed additional applications through the transformation. She could transfer the weight of her Draconic body into her Shadow Space, allowing her to stand upon unusual surfaces without exerting the expected pressure upon the ordinary environment.[1]
More importantly, her new existence began developing traces of Divinity. Kaguya's unusual supernatural attraction became noticeable enough for other members of Victor's household to speculate that she might be developing a Concept connected to Attraction, Lust or Seduction. However, the precise nature of the emerging Divinity had not yet been established during these early discussions, and Kaguya's later confirmed Divine development centered upon Darkness.[11][4]
The transformation also reinforced the developing position of Victor's wives as Empresses and Divine authorities rather than merely spouses accompanying a powerful ruler. Kaguya's authority continued to originate partly from Victor's confidence in her competence, but her own supernatural development increasingly allowed her to operate independently against opponents who would previously have been far beyond her capabilities.[1]
Despite the extraordinary nature of her new existence, Kaguya continued participating in ordinary family activities. During Victor's vacation, she joined Ruby and Amara in a soccer match against Roxanne, Maria and Bruna, demonstrating that the family's increasing supernatural power had not eliminated its interest in recreation, competition and spending time together.[51]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Following the enormous transformations associated with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s expanding Empire, Kaguya became one of the senior women of a developing Divine dynasty. Her status as a Blood Dragon and wife of the Emperor granted her substantial personal authority, while her experience as Chief Maid and Clan Blank officer made her one of the most reliable individuals available to manage sensitive affairs.[19]
Her philosophy concerning Victor's dignity became particularly apparent following the restoration of Earth's environment. The influence of Victor's faction and [[Yggdrasil]] had transformed the planet, eliminating pollution and restoring damaged ecosystems. However, members of the [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]] attempted to claim credit for accomplishments belonging to the Emperor's people. Kaguya regarded the deception as an attack upon the honor of Victor and the collective efforts of his family.[19]
When [[Ganesha]] persisted in the deception, Kaguya personally intervened. Her arm assumed the scaled appearance of a Dark Dragon, and she generated Black Fire through the combination of her Darkness and Fire elements. The resulting attack destroyed Ganesha's existing Divine identity, preventing him from returning through ordinary reincarnation as the same individual. The underlying Divine Essence was not erased absolutely and could eventually form another God, but Ganesha's personal existence had been permanently ended.[19]
Kaguya subsequently warned the assembled supernatural authorities that Victor's wives would not tolerate attempts to steal credit for their husband's accomplishments. She emphasized that their family possessed collective pride and that insulting the Emperor through dishonesty or deliberate disrespect could provoke retaliation even when Victor himself considered the matter too insignificant to warrant personal intervention.[19]
By this period, Clan Blank had also undergone substantial development. Its operatives, once primarily Noble Vampires trained in assassination and intelligence, had been transformed into Dragonoids, allowing the family to continue serving Victor through a much stronger supernatural foundation. Kaguya remained an important authority within the Clan, overseeing its continued relevance as the Empire expanded beyond the political structures of [[Nightingale]].[19]
Kaguya and Victor also became the parents of [[Yuno Elderblood]], who inherited the extraordinary foundation of the Emperor's Blood Dragon lineage. Motherhood added another responsibility to Kaguya's already complicated life, but did not diminish her dedication toward Victor or her role as Chief Maid.[14]
Chapter 1065 provides an extensive account of Kaguya's ordinary routine after approximately two thousand years of development. She awoke in the family residence, used Darkness to form her maid uniform and employed Divine Sight to check the condition of the household. Discovering that Victor had prepared breakfast for his wives, she took time to enjoy the meal before returning to her responsibilities.[15]
The scene also demonstrated Kaguya's authority within the family. When Siena entered a shared area without appropriate clothing, Kaguya reprimanded her for disregarding the presence of children and staff. Siena explained that she would not behave that way while the children were nearby, prompting Kaguya to acknowledge that she had overreacted and apologize. Their disagreement ended peacefully, illustrating how thousands of years of living together had taught the women to correct one another without allowing minor conflicts to damage their relationships.[15]
Kaguya subsequently resumed her intelligence work with Clan Blank. She met with Oda and Mifune, examining information obtained from operations involving suspected traitors, foreign interference and the activities of rival supernatural factions. The organization had become responsible for investigating individuals whose actions threatened the security of the Emperor's territory, including cases involving attempts to compromise or exploit Imperial personnel.[15]
Her personal supernatural development also continued. When accompanying Victor in Chapter 1104, she appeared in her larger Imperial Goddess form and demonstrated an established Divinity of Darkness. Victor had strengthened the Concept by granting her a compatible copy of the Darkness power obtained from [[Erebus]], reflecting the Emperor's research into reproducing and distributing Divine powers without disregarding the compatibility requirements governing their use.[4]
Kaguya's reaction to appearing openly beside Victor revealed that her emotional development had not erased her original identity. Despite being an Empress and Goddess, she still preferred remaining concealed within his shadow, but acknowledged that accompanying him as his wife had become another meaningful way of expressing their relationship.[4]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
The creation of the mature [[Elderblood Empire]] transformed Kaguya's responsibilities from those of a household and Clan intelligence leader into the command of a civilization-scale institution. As [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] initiated the enormous accelerated development program intended to prepare the Empire for threats originating beyond its native Sector, he and his Empresses participated through avatars in major stages of its technological, military and administrative growth.[52][13]
Kaguya participated alongside other Imperial authorities in the creation of the early Legions, helping develop a system in which different military organizations possessed specialized responsibilities rather than forcing every branch of the armed forces to perform identical functions. Her experience coordinating Clan Blank made her especially suited to the establishment of an institution devoted to intelligence and covert operations.[13]
Together with Nyx Elderblood and [[Persephone Elderblood]], Kaguya helped establish the Third Legion — Black Hand of the Emperor. The Legion was assigned responsibility for espionage, intelligence gathering, infiltration, counterintelligence, psychological warfare, the identification of traitors and the manipulation of sensitive information when necessary to protect Imperial interests. Its personnel included Shadow assassins, telepaths, memory manipulators, shapeshifters and Shadow Demons, allowing the institution to employ a wide variety of specialized supernatural methods.[13]
Kaguya became its Commander, while Nyx was designated to act in her place whenever she was unavailable. The division of responsibility complemented their respective supernatural characteristics. Both possessed exceptionally advanced relationships with Darkness, allowing them to operate through elements that ordinary surveillance systems could not easily restrict.[13]
The Black Hand also developed an important relationship with the Empire's other security institutions. Whereas the Sixth Legion, [[The Silent Judgment]], publicly judged and punished major enemies of the Empire, the Third Legion operated in secrecy against threats that could be eliminated or contained before becoming matters of public concern. Its role consequently required extraordinary discretion, access to classified information and personnel capable of operating independently of conventional military formations.[13]
By Chapter 1157, Kaguya's Divine perception had developed into a particularly remarkable form of shadow-based awareness. She explained that her genuine sixth sense was associated with everything connected to Shadows and Darkness, distinguishing it from the possessive instinct jokingly discussed among Victor's wives. The narration directly compared her condition to Nyx's ability to extend her presence through the dark night sky, establishing that Kaguya could similarly use her own Shadows as extensions of her presence.[12]
This characteristic provided Kaguya with a form of limited Shadow-Based Omnipresence. She was not omnipresent throughout the entire Cosmos, but her connection to Shadows allowed her to extend her awareness and presence beyond the normal restrictions of a single physical body. Together with Nyx's corresponding relationship with the night sky, the ability made the two Goddesses exceptionally effective at gathering information and observing events throughout the areas accessible through their respective elements.[12]
The same chapter also demonstrated Kaguya's participation in the increasingly complicated domestic politics of the Elderblood family. When Violet concealed the addition of new women to Victor's household, Kaguya appeared alongside Nyx and Ruby after the wives' possessive instincts alerted them that something unusual was happening. Their exchange was partly humorous, but revealed that Kaguya retained enough independence to tease her fellow Empresses even while remaining one of the family's principal disciplinarians.[12]
Kaguya and Victor's daughter Yuno was among the first of the Imperial daughters to confront Violet after learning about the new additions. Her reaction demonstrated the strong possessive attachments developing among the younger generation and the willingness of Victor's daughters to challenge their mothers when they believed decisions affecting the entire family had been made without appropriate discussion.[12]
During the preparations for the Imperial family's celestial banquet, Kaguya again demonstrated that her household responsibilities remained important despite her enormous military and Divine authority. The family gathered an extraordinary collection of food suitable for Dragon Goddesses and other supernatural beings, including a gigantic Star Beast whose existence was associated with creatures capable of consuming stars. Kaguya immediately took responsibility for the practical arrangements surrounding the meal, even while the other Empresses discussed the unusual ingredients and supernatural circumstances involved.[22]
When Kaguya asked how the family should arrange the seating, Victor rejected the traditional royal practice of using banquet positions to represent rank or political seniority. He instead reshaped the surrounding celestial environment, creating seats and furnishings from astronomical phenomena and transforming the gathering into an enormous celebration beneath the stars. Kaguya accepted the extraordinary setting without abandoning her accustomed responsibilities, continuing to concentrate upon ensuring that every member of the family was properly served.[22]
Victor personally offered Kaguya the honor of serving the banquet, addressing her as his perfect maid. She accepted with obvious happiness, wearing clothing made from the same extraordinary material as Victor's ceremonial attire. When Bruna volunteered to assist, Kaguya immediately refused, making clear that she intended to complete the assignment personally. The intensity of her response surprised Bruna but delighted Yuno, who regarded her mother's behavior as something worthy of pride.[22]
Kaguya subsequently served the entire family so quickly and efficiently that the guests barely noticed her movements. In less than a second, the enormous banquet had been prepared and served, despite the extraordinary number of attendees and the unusual celestial setting. Victor praised her as his beloved wife, while Kaguya proudly responded that such performance was natural because she was the perfect maid.[22]
The event illustrated the continuity of Kaguya's character across the entire story. She had begun as a young Noble Vampire who wanted the freedom to choose her own master, and had eventually become an Empress, Dragon Goddess and Commander of one of the most important Imperial Legions. Yet she still considered personally serving her family an honor rather than a task beneath her station.[22]
During the later family discussions surrounding [[Tyche]] and the next generation of Victor's children, Kaguya also participated in debates concerning the inheritance of the Emperor's Bloodline. Some family members questioned why Victor did not simply transform every relative into a Dragon, but Kaguya joined Violet, Sasha, Ruby, Anna and [[Scathach]] in insisting that entry into the most powerful branches of the Imperial lineage needed to be earned through appropriate trials.[16]
Kaguya argued that even individuals entering the family through service underwent severe tests before receiving positions of responsibility. She therefore rejected the idea that distant Blood relationships alone should guarantee access to the Emperor's extraordinary supernatural lineage. Her position reflected the same principles governing her approach to household discipline and military command: status and ability should be accompanied by merit, responsibility and evidence that the recipient is worthy of the opportunity.[16]
The same discussions revealed another development involving Yuno. Sasha teased Kaguya about her daughter having developed what was described as a Yandere-related Divinity, suggesting that the supposedly perfect maid might possess more extreme personal characteristics than she normally displayed. Kaguya rejected the implication that her daughter's personality was uniquely unusual, arguing that possessive qualities existed among the other Imperial daughters as well.[16]
Kaguya further distinguished herself as one of the mothers who regularly examined the developing Divinities of their children. When Sasha questioned whether it was necessary to check their daughters so frequently, Kaguya joined Scathach, Natasha, Violet, Ruby and Jeanne in confirming that they did so daily. Their response reflected the unusual responsibilities involved in raising children whose supernatural development could produce dangerous or extraordinary Divine Concepts.[16]
By this point, Kaguya had also acquired an informal responsibility for maintaining order among several of the family's most intellectually driven women. Violet observed that without Kaguya's intervention, individuals such as Ruby, Velnorah and Aline could become so absorbed in their research that they neglected the passage of time and other responsibilities. The observation revealed that Kaguya's administrative efficiency remained valuable even among Beings whose intelligence and supernatural power exceeded ordinary comprehension.[16]
Kaguya's current position within the Empire consequently combines several roles that would ordinarily belong to separate individuals. She is Victor's wife and Chief Maid, a mother and senior member of the Imperial family, a Dragon Goddess possessing remarkable supernatural perception, and the commander of a Legion responsible for the Empire's most sensitive intelligence operations. Her development represents the transformation of Clan Blank's original tradition of service into a much greater institution, while preserving the personal choice that originally distinguished her from the hereditary obligations of her ancestors.[13][12][22][16]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Kaguya's husband, chosen master, Progenitor and the most important individual in her life. Their relationship began when Victor was newly transformed into a Vampire and Kaguya served his first wife, Violet. Although Kaguya initially regarded him as another responsibility associated with her mistress, Victor's behavior gradually convinced her that he was fundamentally different from the Noble Vampires she had previously served.[5][6]
Victor repeatedly acknowledged Kaguya's efforts and treated her as an individual rather than an expendable servant. His praise affected her deeply because she had spent much of her life receiving instruction and criticism without corresponding recognition. Kaguya eventually concluded that Victor represented the kind of master she had always wanted to choose for herself, and her loyalty continued developing even before Violet formally transferred her service.[6][9]
The near-death incident at the Fulger residence became a decisive turning point. Victor was furious that Kaguya had treated her own life as a resource that could be sacrificed without hesitation, explaining that he could not tolerate losing someone precious to him. His concern demonstrated that her value did not depend entirely upon her usefulness, giving Kaguya a form of emotional recognition that the traditions of Clan Blank had rarely permitted.[20][21]
Their relationship eventually became romantic, culminating in Victor offering Kaguya a permanent place within his Clan and marriage. She accepted without abandoning her identity as a maid, considering both roles genuine expressions of the relationship she had freely chosen. Victor granted her exceptional trust and authority, even declaring that she could speak for him whenever he was absent.[2][3]
As Victor's civilization developed, Kaguya continued regarding him as both husband and master. Although she became one of his Empresses and developed into a Dragon Goddess, she remained happiest when serving him personally. Victor respected this preference and repeatedly recognized her accomplishments, including her extraordinary performance during the celestial banquet, when he proudly addressed her as his perfect maid and beloved wife.[4][22]
Their marriage also produced their daughter Yuno, adding motherhood to the responsibilities Kaguya had accumulated throughout her life. Despite the immense difference between their original positions and their current existence within the [[Elderblood Empire]], their relationship continues expressing the same central principle established during Victor's earliest days: Kaguya serves him because she loves him and because the choice belongs to her.[14][16]

#### Violet Elderblood Snow
[[Violet Elderblood Snow]] was Kaguya's original mistress and the person whom Clan Blank's hereditary obligations required her to serve. Kaguya possessed a detailed understanding of Violet's possessiveness and unstable emotional reactions, allowing her to recognize situations in which obeying her mistress without question could produce unnecessary problems.[5]
Their relationship was complicated by Kaguya's private desire to choose her own master. Although she respected Violet and performed her duties competently, the service had originated from Clan tradition rather than independent preference. Kaguya's growing interest in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] therefore represented something Violet's hereditary authority could not provide: an opportunity for genuine personal choice.[6]
Violet eventually released Kaguya from the original arrangement and transferred her into Victor's direct service. The decision permitted Kaguya to achieve the freedom she had sought while preserving her relationship with the Snow family. Following her marriage to Victor, the two women also became fellow wives and members of the same household, allowing their relationship to develop beyond the previous distinction between mistress and servant.[9][2]
During the Imperial Era, Kaguya remained comfortable enough with Violet to participate in discussions challenging the Empress's decisions, including the dispute surrounding the addition of new women to the family. Her ability to question Violet without returning to their original compulsory hierarchy demonstrates how extensively their relationship has changed.[12]

#### Blank Clan
The [[Blank Clan]] is Kaguya's birth family and the source of her original Shadow abilities, assassination techniques and extensive training. Her relationship with the Clan remains complicated because she respects its traditions and accomplishments while rejecting the hereditary obligation that historically prevented its members from choosing their own masters.[6]
Kaguya's personal development eventually became an opportunity for the Clan itself. Oda recognized that her decision to serve [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] represented a legitimate expression of personal freedom and appointed her Second Leader. The position allowed Kaguya to command operatives, access restricted techniques and participate directly in determining the family's future.[40]
Her marriage to Victor did not end her connection with Clan Blank. She deliberately retained its name and described the union as a marriage alliance between her birth family and the newly established Elderblood lineage. The Clan later developed alongside the Empire, with its members becoming Dragonoids and acquiring increasingly important positions within the intelligence structure serving Victor.[3][48][19]
By the Imperial Era, Kaguya remained a major authority within Clan Blank while Oda and other senior members continued handling important operations when she was occupied with family or Legion responsibilities. Their cooperation allowed the original assassination family to become part of a much larger institution operating throughout the Empire.[15]

#### Oda Blank
[[Oda Blank]] is one of the most important senior figures in Kaguya's birth Clan and played a major role in recognizing her development. He possessed extensive experience in the traditions, techniques and political responsibilities of Clan Blank, making his acknowledgment of Kaguya's abilities especially significant.[39][40]
Oda eventually appointed Kaguya Second Leader because he believed she had earned the position through her accomplishments and independent choice of master. Rather than insist that her loyalty remain restricted to the [[Snow Clan]], he recognized that a genuine Shinobi should possess the freedom to serve someone worthy of their devotion.[40]
Their relationship continued into the Imperial Era, with Oda participating in intelligence operations and coordinating information with Kaguya. Their cooperation demonstrated the continuity between the original Clan Blank assassination network and the more advanced Imperial intelligence institutions established through her leadership.[15]

#### Maid Squad
The [[Maid Squad]] is the organization most closely associated with Kaguya's identity. As [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s first personal maid, she became its natural senior member and eventual Chief Maid. Her training, experience and disciplined personality made her responsible for supervising the other maids, teaching appropriate conduct and organizing their responsibilities.[33][53]
[[Bruna Elderblood]], [[Eve Elderblood]], [[Maria Elderblood]] and [[Roberta Elderblood]] were among the women whose development became closely connected to Kaguya's leadership. Although their personalities and original circumstances differed considerably, they gradually formed a cohesive group whose members shared loyalty and affection toward Victor.[32][47]
Kaguya's authority became sufficiently established that the other members frequently referred to her as Boss. She maintained strict standards concerning performance and discipline, but the relationships eventually became familial rather than purely professional. Victor's decision to marry the maids formally recognized the personal bonds that had already developed throughout their years of service.[47][2]
Even after the members of the Squad became exceptionally powerful women occupying important positions within the Empire, Kaguya continued treating their original identities as meaningful. Her refusal to allow Bruna to assist with the celestial banquet demonstrated the persistence of her pride in personally serving Victor and his family.[22]

#### Yuno Elderblood
[[Yuno Elderblood]] is the daughter of Kaguya and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], born into the powerful Blood Dragon lineage during the development of the Elderblood family. Her existence represents one of the most significant changes in Kaguya's life, transforming the woman who had been raised primarily for service into a mother whose child belonged to the family of the Emperor.[14]
Yuno shares several personality characteristics with her mother, including intense possessiveness and pride concerning the people she loves. During the dispute surrounding Violet's decision to introduce additional women into the family, Yuno was among the first daughters to demand an explanation, demonstrating her willingness to confront an Empress when she believed the interests of the family had been disregarded.[12]
Her admiration for Kaguya also became apparent during the celestial banquet. When Kaguya refused Bruna's offer to assist with serving, Yuno reacted with obvious satisfaction, proudly recognizing the perfectionism and authority that distinguished her mother.[22]
The later family discussions revealed that Yuno had developed an unusual Divinity described in connection with possessive or Yandere characteristics. Sasha used the development to tease Kaguya, suggesting that the supposed perfect maid might possess more extreme emotions beneath her composed exterior. Kaguya rejected the teasing and argued that possessive behavior was hardly unique among Victor's daughters.[16]
Kaguya also considers the continued observation of her daughter's supernatural development a parental responsibility. She is among the mothers who regularly examine the Divinities emerging among the Imperial daughters, recognizing the risks associated with raising children whose individual powers may eventually reach extraordinary levels.[16]

#### Nyx Elderblood
Nyx Elderblood is one of Kaguya's most important Imperial collaborators and the deputy commander of the Emperor's Black Hand. Their respective Divine natures provide unusually complementary methods of gathering intelligence. While Nyx's relationship with the night sky permits an extraordinary extension of her presence, Kaguya possesses a corresponding connection with Shadows and Darkness.[13][12]
The two consequently occupy central positions within Imperial intelligence, with their abilities allowing them to supervise operations and acquire information through methods inaccessible to ordinary spies. Nyx can act in Kaguya's place when the Commander is occupied with other responsibilities, allowing the Legion to continue functioning without depending upon her constant physical presence.[13]
Their relationship also extends beyond professional cooperation. Kaguya and Nyx participate in the discussions and domestic disputes of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wives, including the events of Chapter 1157, when they appeared together after the family realized that new women were being introduced into Victor's household.[12]

#### Persephone Elderblood
[[Persephone Elderblood]] participated alongside Kaguya and Nyx in the development of the Third Legion. Her involvement contributed to the establishment of an intelligence organization capable of performing functions beyond the traditional assassination and surveillance responsibilities of Clan Blank.[13]
The resulting institution became responsible for an enormous range of covert operations, including counterintelligence, infiltration and the identification of threats to the Empire. Kaguya assumed its formal command, while the organization retained the influence of the different Divine and supernatural authorities involved in its foundation.[13]

#### Ruby Elderblood Scarlett and Sasha Elderblood Fulger
[[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] and [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] were among the first women whose relationships with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became important to Kaguya. During his earliest days as a Vampire, Kaguya independently investigated both women to understand the abnormal marriage ritual and prevent Violet's possessiveness from endangering the emerging family.[7][8]
Their relationships gradually developed from professional observation into genuine familiarity and eventually sisterhood after Kaguya married Victor. Kaguya came to regard their emotional instability and possessiveness as ordinary characteristics of the household, while Ruby and Sasha increasingly accepted her authority as Chief Maid and her importance within Victor's family.[2][12]
During the later Imperial Era, Kaguya joined the two in discussions concerning Bloodline inheritance and the education of the Imperial daughters. Their agreement that entry into Victor's most powerful lineage must be earned demonstrates the extent to which they share important principles concerning responsibility despite possessing very different personalities.[16]

#### Scathach Elderblood Scarlett
[[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] became an important influence upon Kaguya's combat development as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household grew increasingly powerful. She recognized that the maids needed to become capable fighters who could protect themselves without depending entirely upon their master and consequently subjected them to demanding training.[42]
Kaguya respects [[Scathach]]'s extraordinary combat experience but does not place her above Victor within her personal hierarchy of loyalty. Their later relationship is nevertheless close enough for Kaguya to participate alongside Scathach in decisions concerning the discipline, training and development of the Imperial children.[16]

#### Anna Elderblood
[[Anna Elderblood]] is one of the principal maternal authorities within the Imperial family. Kaguya recognizes the extraordinary influence Anna possesses over [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters, who frequently obey her instructions even when they refuse requests from other mothers.[12]
During the later family discussions, Anna and Kaguya repeatedly cooperate in matters concerning household discipline, the protection of children and the requirements governing entry into Victor's Bloodline. Their shared insistence that extraordinary supernatural privileges must be earned reflects their concern that the younger generation should develop responsibility rather than depend exclusively upon the advantages of birth.[16]

#### Hindu Pantheon
Kaguya's relationship with the [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]] became openly hostile after its representatives attempted to claim credit for environmental restoration projects performed through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction. She interpreted the deception as an insult toward the Emperor and his family, regarding it as the responsibility of Victor's wives to defend the dignity of his accomplishments.[19]
Her confrontation with [[Ganesha]] ended with the destruction of the God's existing Divine identity through Black Fire. Kaguya subsequently warned the remaining authorities that similar attempts to exploit Victor's tolerance could provoke equally severe consequences. Her actions demonstrated the difference between Victor's willingness to overlook insignificant provocations and the more uncompromising attitude adopted by certain members of his household.[19]

### Abilities

#### Skills and Abilities
Kaguya Elderblood Blank is the Chief Maid of [[Victor Elderblood]], a senior member of [[Blank Clan]], Commander of the Emperor's Black Hand, and a powerful True Blood Dragon specializing in Darkness, Shadows, assassination, and supernatural infiltration. Originally a Noble Vampire belonging to a branch family of the [[Snow Clan]], Kaguya inherited the extraordinary Shadow abilities of her Japanese Vampire ancestors alongside the sunlight resistance of the [[Snow Bloodline]]. Her powers subsequently underwent several major developments through repeated exposure to Victor's Blood, her incorporation into his Progenitor lineage, her transformation into a True Dragon, and her eventual development into a Dragon Goddess of Darkness.[1][2][3][4]
Kaguya's greatest natural specialization is the manipulation of Darkness and Shadows. She can transform surrounding shadows into weapons, create large territories dominated by Darkness, move between shadows, conceal individuals within a separate Shadow Space, and merge her own existence with the shadow of a compatible partner. Her signature techniques, including Dance of Darkness, Shadow Incarnation, and Helheim, demonstrate how Clan Blank's assassination arts can develop into increasingly powerful supernatural abilities when supported by sufficient Energy and a compatible conduit.[5][6][7][8]
Her subsequent incorporation into Victor's Bloodline awakened the Fire Element associated with Clan Snow. This allowed Kaguya to combine her inherited Darkness with supernatural flames, eventually creating Black Fire, a destructive Draconic technique that permanently ended the Divine identity of [[Ganesha]]. Her transformation into a True Dragon also elevated her Shadow abilities to an entirely different level and granted her a deeper connection to Creation, [[Draconic Energy]], and the supernatural characteristics of Victor's Blood Dragon lineage.[9]
Kaguya's later development into a Goddess represents an additional advancement beyond her initial True Dragon transformation. By Chapter 1104, she possesses an established Divinity of Darkness, which Victor strengthened by granting her a compatible copy of the Darkness power he previously obtained from [[Erebus]]. This advancement should be distinguished from Chapter 921, when Kaguya had only begun manifesting traces of Divinity and the precise nature of her developing Divine domain remained uncertain.[10][4]
Beyond direct combat, Kaguya is an exceptionally accomplished assassin, intelligence operative, administrator, and military commander. Her training within Clan Blank originally prepared her for espionage and the elimination of dangerous targets, but her responsibilities expanded after becoming Victor's Chief Maid. By the Imperial Era, she commands the Third Legion — Black Hand of the Emperor, an organization specializing in intelligence gathering, infiltration, counterintelligence, psychological warfare, and the elimination of threats to the Empire.[11][12]

#### Racial Physiology

##### Noble Vampire Physiology
Kaguya was originally a Noble Vampire descending from two different Vampire lineages. Her ancestry combined the supernatural characteristics of Clan Snow with the Shadow powers of an ancient Japanese Noble Vampire family. Although she did not initially inherit Clan Snow's active Fire Manipulation, she retained several of the family's passive characteristics alongside her natural affinity for Darkness.[1][2]
- Noble Vampire Physiology: Kaguya originally possessed the supernatural constitution of a Noble Vampire, including physical abilities exceeding ordinary Humans, heightened senses, regeneration, longevity, and an inherited supernatural specialization.[1][5]
- Superhuman Strength: Her Vampiric body granted strength far exceeding that of ordinary Humans, allowing her to wield supernatural weapons and inflict severe injuries upon physically enhanced opponents.[5][6]
- Superhuman Speed: Kaguya possesses extraordinary movement speed, allowing her to attack, reposition, and eliminate enemies before they can respond effectively.[5][6]
- Enhanced Reflexes: Her Vampire physiology and assassin training provide exceptional reactions during high-speed combat. She can respond to attacks from experienced supernatural warriors while simultaneously manipulating her Darkness.[5][7]
- Enhanced Agility: Kaguya can perform precise and complex movements during battle, coordinating physical attacks with rapid Shadow Travel and changes in position.[6][7]
- Superhuman Durability: Her body possesses the enhanced resilience associated with Noble Vampires, allowing her to endure physical damage beyond the limits of ordinary Humans.[13][14]
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Kaguya possesses Vampiric regeneration. Her recovery became significantly more effective through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood, which helped her survive and recover from severe injuries.[13][14][15]
- Enhanced Stamina: Her supernatural constitution allows her to maintain prolonged physical activity and combat beyond ordinary Human limitations.[7]
- Enhanced Senses: Kaguya possesses heightened Vampiric perception, including extremely developed eyesight. During an early surveillance assignment, she observed Victor's university from a rooftop several kilometers away as though it were much closer.[1]
- Enhanced Vision: Kaguya can perceive distant objects with extraordinary clarity, allowing her to conduct surveillance without approaching the target directly.[1]
- Longevity: As a Noble Vampire, Kaguya possesses an extraordinarily extended lifespan and does not undergo ordinary Human aging.[1]
- Natural Weaponry: Her Vampiric physiology provides the predatory characteristics associated with Noble Vampires, while her Darkness Manipulation permits the creation of additional weapons suited to close-range combat.[5]
- [[Vampiric Energy]]: Kaguya originally possessed the supernatural Energy associated with Vampires, supporting her physical characteristics and inherited Shadow abilities.[7]
- Bloodline Empowerment: Repeated consumption of Victor's Blood greatly increased her strength and control over Darkness. This development allowed her to employ advanced Clan Blank techniques previously beyond her capabilities.[6][7]
- Progenitor Bloodline Integration: Victor eventually incorporated Kaguya more directly into his lineage, strengthening her existing traits and awakening the Fire Element associated with his [[Snow Clan]] inheritance.[2]

##### True Blood Dragon Physiology
Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ascension as the Dragon God of [[Chaos]], Kaguya underwent a transformation into a True Blood Dragon, with her particular Draconic nature associated with Darkness and Shadows. Her earlier Vampire abilities became part of the foundation of her new species rather than being discarded. The transformation strengthened her physical abilities, increased the effectiveness of her existing powers, and granted access to the broader supernatural characteristics of the Dragon Race.[3]
- Blood Dragon Physiology: Kaguya possesses the racial characteristics of a Blood Dragon descended from Victor. Her existence combines aspects of her previous Vampire ancestry with the superior supernatural constitution of a True Dragon.[3]
- True Dragon Physiology: Kaguya became a True Dragon rather than merely remaining an enhanced Noble Vampire. Her transformation established a new Draconic foundation for her previous powers.[3]
- Shadow Dragon Physiology: Her individual Draconic specialization is associated with Shadows and Darkness, reflecting the defining characteristics of Clan Blank.[3]
- Enhanced Physical Characteristics: Her True Dragon evolution substantially increased her strength, speed, durability, agility, reflexes, endurance, and other supernatural physical attributes.[3]
- Enhanced Regeneration: Her transformation improved the biological foundation supporting her existing regenerative abilities. This improvement is in addition to the benefits she previously obtained from Victor's Blood.[3]
- Enhanced Longevity: True Dragon physiology provides a form of existence far exceeding ordinary Human and Noble Vampire lifespans.[3]
- Draconic Transformation: Kaguya can assume a more complete manifestation of her Shadow Dragon nature, changing her physical appearance and the Darkness associated with her existence.[3]
- Partial Dragon Transformation: Kaguya can transform individual body parts without assuming a complete Draconic form. While confronting [[Ganesha]], she transformed one arm into a scaled Dark Dragon limb before releasing Black Fire.[9]
- Dragon Scales: Her transformed limbs possess Draconic scales, providing the anatomical characteristics associated with her evolved species.[9]
- Dragon Horns: Kaguya developed the horns associated with the newly transformed [[True Dragons]]. In her later Divine appearance, these manifest as large black horns.[3][4]
- Wing Manifestation: Kaguya possesses Draconic wings, as demonstrated by the physical characteristics shared among the women transformed into True Dragons.[3]
- Flight: Her Draconic physiology provides the capacity for aerial movement through her wings.[3]
- Enhanced Shapeshifting: The newly transformed True Dragons developed substantially improved metamorphic capabilities compared with their previous Vampiric forms.[3]
- Size Manipulation (Draconic Metamorphosis): Kaguya can assume physical appearances of significantly different sizes. Her Shadow Dragon manifestation and her later Goddess form possess dimensions far exceeding her original Noble Vampire body.[3][4]
- [[Draconic Energy]]: Kaguya's transformation connected her existence to the supernatural Energy associated with True Dragons, reinforcing her racial abilities and elemental powers.[3]
- Draconic Elemental Affinity: Her original Darkness and Shadow abilities became more powerful after the transformation, while her acquired Fire Element could be combined with Draconic power.[3][9]
- Creation Affinity: True Dragons possess a natural connection with Creation and Nature. The women transformed into True Dragons acquired the capacity to influence Creation on a smaller scale than Victor.[3]
- Limited Creation Manipulation: Kaguya belongs to the group of True Dragons capable of manipulating aspects of Creation through their racial nature. This does not establish independent mastery over the entire Concept of Creation.[3]
- Draconic Rune Compatibility: The Draconic Language and its Runes are naturally compatible with True Dragons, allowing Kaguya to benefit from the supernatural linguistic characteristics of her evolved species.[3]
- Draconic Instincts: Her True Dragon nature provides the innate relationship with Creation possessed by members of the species. The full range of her personal Draconic perception has not been separately demonstrated.[3]

##### Shadow Dragon Appearance
During her initial True Dragon transformation, Kaguya manifested a mature appearance standing approximately 193 centimeters tall. Her skin became extremely pale, while her eyes appeared empty and black. Her hair became a manifestation of Darkness rather than ordinary hair, creating the impression that supernatural shadows were flowing directly from her head. Her existence appeared to suppress the surrounding light, reflecting the deeper connection between her physical body and her natural element.[3]
Her later Goddess form differs substantially from this initial transformation. By Chapter 1104, Kaguya could appear approximately 4.70 meters tall, with long black horns, red Draconic eyes, and a dress resembling the Darkness of the starry sky. Her movements generated Darkness that briefly remained beneath her feet, giving the impression that she was floating even while walking. This appearance represents her later Divine development rather than the original measurements of her newly awakened Shadow Dragon state.[4]

#### Divinity and Divine Powers

##### Divinity of Darkness
Kaguya's development into a Dragon Goddess represents a major advancement beyond her earlier control over ordinary Shadows. Although she initially displayed only traces of emerging Divinity, her later development established a genuine Divine connection to Darkness. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] further enhanced this connection by granting Kaguya a compatible copy of the Darkness power he had obtained from the former Greek Primordial God [[Erebus]].[10][4]
The enhancement was possible because the copied Divine power was compatible with Kaguya's existing nature. Victor had spent considerable time researching how to reproduce and distribute Divine Concepts effectively, eventually determining that compatibility with the recipient's established Divine characteristics was essential. Kaguya's natural affinity with Shadows and Darkness made her an appropriate recipient for the power previously belonging to Erebus.[4]
- Divinity of Darkness: Kaguya possesses an established Divine authority associated with Darkness. Her previously inherited Shadow powers developed into a higher-order supernatural domain as she advanced into a Goddess.[4]
- Divine Darkness Manipulation: Kaguya can manifest and control Darkness associated with her Divine Concept rather than relying exclusively upon the inherited abilities of Clan Blank.[4]
- Divine Power Amplification: Victor strengthened Kaguya's Divinity by granting her a compatible copy of the Darkness power acquired from Erebus.[4]
- Conceptual Compatibility: Kaguya's natural affinity with Darkness allowed her to receive a copied Divine power compatible with her established supernatural existence.[4]
- Darkness Manifestation: Her Divine presence can produce Darkness around her body and beneath her feet. During her appearance as the Emperor's wife and Goddess, each step briefly generated pure Darkness.[4]
- Divine Darkness Clothing: Kaguya's later Goddess attire resembles a garment formed from the Darkness of the starry sky, reflecting the integration of her Divine nature into her appearance.[4]
- Divine Elemental Empowerment: Her Divine development strengthened her existing Darkness-related powers, allowing her to progress beyond the limitations of her original Noble Vampire abilities.[4]
- Higher-Order Darkness Affinity: The power inherited from Erebus further advanced Kaguya's relationship with the Divine Concept of Darkness.[4]
- Divine Metamorphosis: Kaguya possesses a distinct Goddess appearance significantly larger than her original Vampire form, demonstrating the continued development of her Draconic metamorphosis alongside her Divinity.[4]
Important distinction: Receiving a copy of Erebus's Darkness power does not automatically grant Kaguya every independent Divinity or technique previously possessed by Erebus. His Space, [[The Mist|Mist]], and Gravity-related abilities should not be listed as Kaguya's personal powers unless separate chapters demonstrate that she inherited those specific characteristics. Chapter 1104 explicitly establishes the strengthening of her Darkness Divinity.[4]

##### Divinity of Shadows
Kaguya's supernatural specialization originated with the Shadow Manipulation inherited from the Japanese Vampire lineage incorporated into Clan Blank. Her development into a Shadow Dragon strengthened the connection between these abilities and her Draconic existence, while her later Divine development established Darkness as a fundamental part of her supernatural identity.[3][4]
- Divine Shadow Affinity: Kaguya's mastery of Shadows became significantly stronger after her True Dragon evolution and later development into a Darkness Goddess.[3][4]
- Shadow-Based Divine Manifestation: Her Darkness can manifest through her body, hair, garments, and surrounding environment, reflecting the integration of her original powers with her later Divine nature.[3][4]
- Shadow Power Enhancement: Her evolved supernatural existence substantially improves the capabilities she originally possessed as a member of Clan Blank.[3]
- Darkness and Shadow Synergy: The close relationship between her inherited Shadow abilities and acquired Darkness Divinity allows her earlier techniques to develop within a much more powerful supernatural foundation.[3][4]
Although Kaguya is identified through titles such as Dragon Goddess of Darkness and Dragon Goddess of Shadow, those descriptions should not automatically be used to assign multiple separate Major Divinities without the corresponding development being established by the novel. Her Darkness Divinity is explicitly supported by Chapter 1104, while her Shadow specialization is supported by her earlier techniques and True Dragon transformation.[3][4]

Following Kaguya's transformation into a True Dragon, her appearance began producing an unusual passive attraction. The effect was strong enough to influence individuals whose ordinary preferences would not have predisposed them toward Kaguya, leading other members of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household to speculate that she might be developing a Divinity associated with Attraction, Lust, or Seduction.[10]
- Supernatural Attraction: Kaguya's evolved appearance produces an unusually powerful attraction that can affect nearby observers.[10]
- Passive Allure: Even her ordinary movements and restrained demeanor acquired a supernatural seductive quality following her Draconic transformation.[10]
- Attraction Inducement: Roberta was visibly affected by Kaguya's presence despite ordinarily directing her romantic interest toward Victor.[10]
- Nascent Divinity: The newly transformed Dragon women possessed emerging Divine characteristics, which could be perceived through their Draconic Eyes.[10]
- Possible Attraction Divinity: Carmila considered Attraction a plausible explanation for Kaguya's effect, comparing it to the supernatural influence produced by [[Aphrodite]].[10]
- Possible Lust or Seduction Divinity: Other observers speculated that Lust or Seduction might be associated with the same phenomenon, but these suggestions were not confirmed as fully established separate Divinities in Chapter 921.[10]
Classification note: Chapter 921 establishes an unusual passive attraction and the early development of Divinity. It does not confirm that Kaguya simultaneously possesses three independent, fully developed Divinities of Lust, Seduction, and Attraction. Her later confirmed Darkness Divinity should be indexed separately from these earlier interpretations.[10][4]

#### Shadow and Darkness Manipulation

##### Shadow Manipulation
Shadow Manipulation is Kaguya's original supernatural specialization and the foundation of Clan Blank's assassination techniques. She can generate Darkness, expand it across her surroundings, merge with shadows, create physical weapons and constructs, and move between different shadows without relying upon ordinary locomotion. These abilities make her exceptionally dangerous against opponents who depend upon direct visual observation or conventional defenses.[5][6][7]
- Shadow Manipulation: Kaguya can generate, shape, control, solidify, and move supernatural Shadows for offensive, defensive, and utility purposes.[5]
- Darkness Manipulation: She can produce and control supernatural Darkness extending beyond the shadows naturally present within an environment.[6][7]
- Shadow Generation: Kaguya can release Darkness from her body and spread it across the ground and surrounding environment.[5][6]
- Large-Scale Shadow Expansion: Kaguya can spread her Shadows across an extensive area. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] asked whether she could cover an entire mountain with Darkness, she confirmed that she possessed the necessary range.[16]
- Shadow Territory: Kaguya can cover the battlefield with Darkness and use the resulting territory to attack, move, and create supernatural constructs from numerous positions.[5][7]
- Territory Manipulation: She can control the location and movement of constructs emerging from the Darkness within her territory.[5][7]
- Shadow Solidification: Kaguya can make Darkness physically solid, allowing it to function as weapons, barriers, or restraints.[5][7]
- Shadow Weapon Creation: She can construct weapons from Darkness, including daggers, swords, spikes, and other sharpened structures.[5][6]
- Shadow Daggers: Kaguya routinely forms daggers from Darkness. She used one to pierce Mizuki's heart during their confrontation in Chapter 28.[5]
- Shadow Swords: Kaguya can enlarge her Darkness daggers into full-length swords suitable for close-range combat against powerful supernatural enemies.[6][7]
- Darkness Energy Slashes: She can release cutting attacks from her Shadow swords rather than requiring every strike to make direct physical contact.[7]
- Shadow Spike Generation: Kaguya can generate sharpened spikes of Darkness from her surroundings and use them to pierce multiple opponents simultaneously.[7]
- Shadow Construct Creation: She can generate numerous solid or semi-solid structures from her Darkness, including hands, blades, and other combat manifestations.[5][7]
- Shadow Hands: Thousands of black hands can emerge from Kaguya's territory and pursue targets within the affected region.[5]
- Binding and Restraint: Her Shadow Hands can seize and restrict opponents, preventing them from freely escaping or responding to further attacks.[5]
- Construct Transformation: Kaguya can transform manifested Shadow Hands into sharpened weapons, changing a restraining attack into a lethal strike.[7]
- Omnidirectional Attacks: Kaguya can launch attacks from multiple positions throughout her Shadow Territory, making it difficult for surrounded enemies to predict the direction of the next strike.[7]
- Darkness Explosion: She can release an explosive surge of Darkness while leaving a threatened position. This eruption can cut apart nearby opponents.[7]
- Shadow Infusion: Darkness can enter an opponent's body through injuries inflicted by her constructs and remain inside the target after the initial attack.[7]
- Internal Explosion: Kaguya can detonate Darkness previously introduced into an enemy's body. During the battle against [[Werewolves]], she ordered the Darkness inside several impaled opponents to explode, violently destroying their bodies.[7]
- Internal Damage: Her ability to detonate infused Darkness allows her to attack an enemy from within after her constructs have penetrated their defenses.[7]
- Matter Destruction Through Darkness: Kaguya can use Darkness to consume certain physical structures. She demonstrated this by destroying trees and wood generated across her battlefield by a Werewolf opponent.[7]
- Darkness Consumption: Physical materials caught within her Darkness can be swallowed and destroyed by the supernatural substance.[7]
- Barrier Generation: Kaguya can raise defensive barriers from the Darkness covering her territory. She used this method to intercept an attack during the confrontation with the Werewolves.[7]
- Shadow Shielding: Her solidified Darkness can function as a protective structure against physical and supernatural attacks.[7]
- Environmental Control: By spreading Darkness throughout an area, Kaguya can manipulate the terrain available for movement, concealment, and the creation of offensive structures.[5][7]
- Battlefield Obscuration: Her extensive Darkness can interfere with an opponent's ability to observe the surrounding environment and identify her position.[5][6]
- Shadow-Based Ambush: Kaguya can conceal her attacks until the moment of contact, making her Darkness particularly effective for assassination.[5]
The destructive effects of Kaguya's Darkness should not be confused with unrestricted conceptual absorption. Her early demonstrations establish the destruction of physical structures and supernatural opponents through Darkness, while her later Divine development provides a much higher supernatural foundation for the same general element.[7][4]

- Shadow Teleportation: Kaguya can enter one shadow and emerge from another, allowing her to change position without physically crossing the intervening distance through ordinary movement.[5]
- Shadow Travel: She can move while merged with Darkness, making her body difficult to detect during travel.[5]
- Shadow Merging: Kaguya can physically merge into shadows rather than merely hiding behind objects that cast them.[5][8]
- Presence Concealment: Kaguya can conceal her physical presence within Shadows, preventing experienced supernatural opponents from reliably locating her.[5]
- Stealth: Her concealment techniques allow her to approach targets from blind spots and attack before they recognize the danger.[5]
- Ambush Attacks: Kaguya can emerge beside an opponent and immediately strike a vulnerable area. Her attack against Mizuki pierced the hunter's heart before she could respond effectively.[5]
- Surprise Attack: Her ability to disappear and reappear through shadows allows her to disrupt an opponent's expectations concerning her position.[5]
- Evasion: Kaguya can retreat into Darkness to avoid attacks directed toward her previous position.[5][7]
- Shadow-Based Surveillance: She can remain hidden within a target's shadow and observe events without exposing her physical body.[17]
- Long-Term Concealment: Kaguya can remain within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s shadow for extended periods while performing her duties.[18]
- Rapid Shadow Transportation: Kaguya continues employing Shadow Teleportation for ordinary travel during the Imperial Era, including moving directly to her workplace.[19]
- Concealed Deployment: She can transport herself to a location while keeping other people hidden inside her Shadow Space, then release them when the situation requires their presence.[8]

#### Shadow Space

##### Pocket Dimension Manipulation
Kaguya possesses a separate supernatural space associated with her Shadows. This space is capable of holding objects and living individuals, making it considerably more versatile than ordinary concealment. The ability allows Kaguya to function as a transporter, hidden observer, storage specialist, and battlefield deployment point for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s forces.[8][3]
- Pocket Dimension Manipulation: Kaguya possesses an internal Shadow Space that functions as a separate supernatural realm linked to her Darkness.[8][3]
- Dimensional Storage: She can store equipment and other objects inside her Shadow Space.[8]
- Living Being Storage: Kaguya can conceal other people inside her Shadows without requiring them to remain physically visible in the outside environment.[8]
- Dimensional Transportation: She can transport individuals occupying her Shadow Space while moving through the outside world.[8]
- Mass Concealment: Kaguya can carry multiple allies within her Shadows, allowing groups to approach a battlefield or other dangerous location without being immediately detected.[8]
- Surprise Deployment: During the Vatican battle, Victor's maids and allied forces emerged from the Shadows in which Kaguya had concealed them.[8]
- Passenger Release: Kaguya can release individuals from her Shadow Space at an appropriate location.[8]
- Selective Ejection: She can forcibly expel an occupant from her Shadows. Before Victor employed a dangerous transformation at the Vatican, Kaguya removed Mizuki and deposited her outside the affected area.[8]
- Sensory Sharing: Individuals occupying another person's shadow can perceive information from the outside world through the relationship between the shadow and its owner.[8]
- Concealed Communication: Occupants can communicate with the owner of a shadow without necessarily producing audible speech detectable by outsiders.[16][8]
- Telepathic Communication: Kaguya can communicate mentally with Victor while occupying his shadow, allowing instructions and information to be exchanged during combat.[16][8]
- Shadow-Based Observation: She can observe events around Victor while remaining concealed within his shadow.[17][18]
- Safe Transportation: Kaguya can conceal more vulnerable companions inside her Shadows to reduce their exposure to external threats.[6][8]

##### Weight Transference and Surface Movement
Kaguya's Shadow Space acquired another important application following her transformation into a True Dragon. Rather than allowing the enormous mass associated with her Draconic body to rest entirely upon the physical environment, she can transfer its weight into the separate realm allocated within her Shadows. This allows her to move through ordinary environments without damaging their surfaces and creates unusual possibilities for movement.[3]
- Weight Transference: Kaguya can transfer the weight of her Draconic body into her Shadow Space rather than allowing it to act entirely upon the ordinary physical environment.[3]
- Weight Distribution: She can use the supernatural realm connected to her Shadows to support her body independently of conventional surfaces.[3]
- Environmental Weight Reduction: The technique prevents her enormous True Dragon mass from damaging floors or other surfaces merely through standing or walking upon them.[3]
- Surface Scaling: Kaguya can stand and move upon surfaces that would not ordinarily support a body of her mass or orientation.[3]
- Wall-Crawling: Her Shadow-based support allows her to move along walls and other vertical surfaces.[3]
- Ceiling-Walking: Kaguya demonstrated the ability by disappearing and reappearing while standing upon a ceiling.[3]
- Gravity-Defying Movement: Her movement can appear to disregard ordinary gravitational restrictions because the supporting weight is transferred into her Shadow Space.[3]
Mechanism distinction: Kaguya's demonstrated weight transference is an application of her Shadow Space. It does not independently establish unrestricted manipulation of gravity throughout the surrounding environment.[3]

#### Advanced Clan Blank Techniques

##### Dance of Darkness
Dance of Darkness is an advanced assassination technique belonging to Clan Blank. Kaguya initially lacked sufficient power to employ it properly, but repeatedly consuming [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood improved her strength and control until she could maintain the technique. She first demonstrated it during the battle against [[Werewolves]], combining her weapons, movements, hair, and surrounding Darkness into a coordinated offensive system.[6][7]
- Dance of Darkness: Kaguya enters an advanced combat state combining Shadow Territory, Dark weapons, hardened hair, and exceptionally rapid movement.[6]
- Prehensile Hair: Her hair grows longer and begins moving independently, resembling numerous tendrils of Darkness.[6]
- Hair Manipulation: Kaguya can control the movement of her Darkness-covered hair for offensive and defensive purposes.[6][7]
- Hair Blade Generation: Individual strands of hair become hardened through Darkness and function as sharpened weapons.[6]
- Hair-Based Cutting Attacks: Her hardened hair can cut through transformed Werewolves during close-range combat.[6]
- Independent Hair Attacks: The movement of her hair allows additional strikes while her arms remain occupied wielding swords.[6][7]
- Multi-Directional Offense: Kaguya can attack opponents from several directions simultaneously through her hair, Shadow swords, and surrounding Darkness.[7]
- Multi-Directional Defense: Her moving hair can intercept attacks while she continues performing offensive movements.[7]
- Enhanced Defensive Coverage: The combination of multiple independently moving blades was described as providing Kaguya with an almost perfect defense under the circumstances of the battle.[7]
- High-Speed Blade Combat: Kaguya moves through groups of opponents while continuously striking with her swords and hardened hair.[6]
- Simultaneous Weapon Control: She can coordinate conventional hand movements, Shadow weapons, and hair-based attacks without requiring every component to move in the same direction.[7]
- Levitation: During her deployment of Darkness, Kaguya can float slightly above the ground while performing her movements.[7]
- Battlefield Dominance: Dance of Darkness allows Kaguya to confront multiple opponents efficiently despite her original training emphasizing assassination rather than direct warfare.[6][7]
The technique's limitations depend upon the amount of power and control available to Kaguya. Its first successful use followed her improvement through Victor's Blood, establishing that the ability requires more than simply knowing its name or physical movements.[6]

##### Shadow Incarnation
Shadow Incarnation is one of Clan Blank's secret techniques and an important example of the synergy between Kaguya's powers and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s supernatural existence. The technique requires a compatible partner whose existence can function as a conduit. Kaguya's compatible partner is Victor, allowing her to transform into pure Darkness and merge directly with his shadow.[8]
- Shadow Incarnation: Kaguya can convert her body into pure Darkness and merge completely with Victor's shadow.[8]
- Darkness Physiology (Technique-Based): Her physical body becomes Darkness while the technique is active.[8]
- Shadow Fusion: Kaguya can merge her transformed existence directly with the shadow of a compatible individual.[8]
- Symbiotic Fusion: The technique establishes a close supernatural relationship between Kaguya and her partner, allowing their powers to operate through the same connection.[8]
- Conduit-Based Sustenance: Shadow Incarnation uses the compatible partner's existence as its sustaining conduit.[8]
- Stamina Dependency: The duration of the technique depends principally upon the stamina and continued existence of the partner supporting it.[8]
- Extended Transformation Duration: Victor's extraordinary stamina allows Kaguya to maintain the technique far longer than would normally be possible through her own reserves.[8]
- Master-Servant Power Synchronization: The fusion allows Kaguya to support Victor's supernatural actions while existing within his shadow.[8]
- Combined Power Channeling: The arrangement enables supernatural power to be channeled through the merged existence rather than requiring Kaguya and Victor to operate entirely separately.[8][20]
- Multi-Participant Energy Support: During the Vatican battle, the technique drew upon Victor's stamina and the additional maids concealed within his Shadows, allowing their participation in the combined supernatural state.[20]
- Allied Ability Channeling: The arrangement allowed Victor to employ abilities belonging to maids participating through Kaguya's technique. This is a specific combination involving multiple individuals rather than proof that Kaguya independently possesses every power of Victor's maids.[20]
Shadow Incarnation differs from ordinary Shadow Merging. The latter allows Kaguya to conceal herself or move within Shadows, while Shadow Incarnation creates a more complete supernatural fusion dependent upon the compatibility and stamina of another individual.[8]

##### Shadow-Based Sixth Sense and Omnipresence
As a Dragon Goddess associated with Shadows and Darkness, Kaguya Elderblood Blank possesses a supernatural sixth sense extending through her associated elements. Chapter 1157 explicitly establishes that her perception encompasses phenomena related to Shadows and Darkness and compares her ability to Nyx Elderblood's capacity to be present wherever she wishes through the dark night sky. Kaguya can similarly extend her presence through her own Shadows. The narration identifies these abilities as a principal reason Kaguya and Nyx are exceptionally effective at directing the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s intelligence operations.[21]
- Shadow-Based Omnipresence (Limited): Kaguya can extend her presence through her Shadows, allowing her to manifest at different locations connected to her supernatural element. This ability is explicitly compared to Nyx's ability to be present through the dark night sky.[21]
- Extrasensory Perception: Kaguya possesses a genuine sixth sense associated with Shadows and Darkness, operating beyond her conventional physical senses.[21]
- Darkness Sensing: Her sixth sense encompasses phenomena related to supernatural Darkness, providing a sensory connection to her Divine element.[21]
- Shadow-Based Spatial Awareness: Kaguya can use her relationship with Shadows to extend her awareness beyond her immediate physical surroundings.[21]
- Elemental Presence Extension: Kaguya can use her Shadows as extensions of her presence rather than relying exclusively upon the location of her physical body.[21]
- Enhanced Intelligence Gathering: Kaguya's shadow-based perception and presence extension make her exceptionally effective at gathering intelligence, an advantage explicitly recognized by the narration.[21]
Limitations: Kaguya's omnipresence is associated with Shadows and Darkness, not unrestricted presence throughout all existence. The chapter does not establish unlimited range across every Sector or dimension, nor does it confirm that Kaguya simultaneously perceives every event throughout the Cosmos.[21]

##### Helheim
Helheim is an exceptionally large-scale Darkness technique associated with Kaguya's Shadow Incarnation and her coordination with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. During the Vatican battle, Kaguya's Darkness spread across an enormous area and formed a domain through which the combined supernatural effects could be maintained and contained.[8]
- Helheim: Kaguya generates an extensive Darkness-dominated territory in conjunction with the power provided through her compatible conduit.[8]
- Large-Scale Darkness Generation: The technique can extend across an enormous battlefield, covering the surrounding area with supernatural Darkness.[8]
- Darkness Domain: Helheim establishes an environment dominated by Kaguya's Darkness, allowing Shadow-related techniques to operate across the affected territory.[8]
- Area Containment: The Darkness can serve as a containment structure for powerful supernatural phenomena occurring within the affected region.[8]
- Barrier Creation: Helheim can function defensively by restricting the spread of destructive effects beyond the territory.[8]
- Conduit-Based Amplification: The scale and duration of the technique can be supported by Victor's enormous supernatural reserves.[8]
- Combined Ability Support: Helheim can be maintained in conjunction with Shadow Incarnation and the contributions of other maids involved in the fusion.[8][20]
The numerous supernatural eyes manifested within the Darkness during the Vatican incident are associated with Victor's transformed state. They should not automatically be indexed as an independent Kaguya ability simply because the manifestations occurred within a territory supported by her Darkness.[8]

#### Fire and Elemental Powers

##### Fire Manipulation
Kaguya's original Clan Blank ancestry did not grant her active Fire Manipulation despite her descent from Clan Snow. This changed when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] incorporated her into his Progenitor Bloodline. The ritual strengthened her original powers and awakened the Fire Element associated with Victor's Snow inheritance, allowing Kaguya to produce flames independently of her Darkness.[1][2]
- [[Pyrokinesis]]: Kaguya gained the ability to generate and control supernatural Fire after her Bloodline was enhanced through Victor.[2]
- Fire Generation: She can create supernatural flames directly from her body without requiring an ordinary physical source.[2]
- Fire Projection: Kaguya can manifest flames outside her body, demonstrated when she opened her hand and produced Fire following her transformation.[2]
- [[Snow Clan]] Fire Affinity: Her active Fire ability originated from the Snow Clan characteristics present within Victor's Progenitor Blood.[2]
- Elemental Affinity Enhancement: Victor's Blood strengthened Kaguya's hereditary traits, allowing her original Darkness powers and newly acquired Fire to coexist.[2]
- Elemental Fusion: Kaguya can combine her Darkness and Fire Elements to produce an attack possessing characteristics different from either element used separately.[9]
- Draconic Fire: After becoming a True Dragon, Kaguya could combine her Fire Element with Draconic power, producing a greatly enhanced form of supernatural flame.[9]
Kaguya initially questioned whether she had inherited all the passive characteristics associated with the Snow Clan Bloodline, particularly the relationship between emotional intensity and increasing power. Her ability to generate Fire was confirmed, but the full extent of any additional inherited passive Snow abilities was not established during the ritual.[2]

##### Dark Fire / Black Flame Manipulation
Dark Fire, also called Black Fire, is Kaguya's most destructive individually demonstrated elemental technique. She creates it by combining her Darkness and Fire Elements through her evolved Draconic physiology. During the confrontation involving the [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]], she employed the technique against [[Ganesha]] after he attempted to take credit for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s restoration of Earth.[9]
- Dark Fire: Kaguya can generate a destructive black flame by combining Darkness and Fire.[9]
- Black Flame Manipulation: She can project and control supernatural Black Fire through her transformed Draconic body.[9]
- Elemental Fusion: The technique explicitly combines Kaguya's Darkness and Fire Elements.[9]
- Draconic Fire Manipulation: Her Black Fire possesses the destructive characteristics associated with Dragon Fire.[9]
- Partial Transformation Integration: Kaguya can release Black Fire while transforming only one arm into a scaled Dark Dragon limb.[9]
- Atomic Disintegration: Her Black Fire reduced Ganesha's head to atoms almost immediately.[9]
- Divine Destruction: The technique was powerful enough to destroy Ganesha's manifested Divine existence despite his status as a God.[9]
- [[Immortality]] Negation (Demonstrated Application): Kaguya's attack ended the continuity of Ganesha's existing Divine identity rather than merely inflicting a temporary physical injury.[9]
- Reincarnation Negation (Personal Identity): Ganesha could not return through ordinary reincarnation as the same individual after being destroyed by Kaguya's Dragon Fire.[9]
- Divine Identity Destruction: The attack destroyed the existing God's continuity of identity, ensuring that the same Ganesha could not return as the same person.[9]
- Destruction-Like Fire: The narration compared Dragon Fire to the power of Destruction, describing the effect as similar but somewhat weaker than genuine conceptual Destruction.[9]
Limit of the feat: Kaguya did not completely erase Ganesha's underlying Divine Essence. The chapter states that this Essence would eventually reform into a different God possessing another personality and identity. The attack therefore demonstrates the destruction of an individual Divine existence and the prevention of reincarnation as the same person, not absolute erasure of the Concept or Essence governing that God.[9]

#### Resistances and Immunities

##### Racial Resistances
- Sunlight Immunity: Kaguya inherited Clan Snow's unusual resistance to sunlight through her ancestry. Even before acquiring active Fire Manipulation, she could tolerate sunlight despite belonging to a Vampire branch family.[1]
- Fire Resistance: Clan Blank's Snow-derived ancestry provides resistance to Fire, a characteristic distinct from Kaguya's originally absent active [[Pyrokinesis]].[1][2]
- Enhanced Physical Resistance: Her Noble Vampire and later True Dragon physiologies provide increased protection against physical damage.[13][3]
- Regenerative Resistance to Injury: Kaguya can recover from substantial bodily injuries through her regenerative physiology, which was greatly enhanced by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood.[13][15]
- Longevity and Aging Resistance: Her supernatural racial constitution allows her to exist for extended periods without ordinary Human aging.[1][3]
- Draconic Physiological Enhancement: Her True Dragon evolution provides substantially greater physical resilience than her original Noble Vampire body.[3]
- Environmental Weight Compensation: Her ability to transfer bodily weight into Shadow Space prevents her True Dragon mass from damaging ordinary surfaces and allows movement along unusual surfaces.[3]

##### Defensive Techniques
- Shadow Barrier: Kaguya can create defensive structures from solidified Darkness to intercept attacks.[7]
- Shadow Evasion: She can retreat into Darkness and emerge elsewhere to escape attacks directed toward her former position.[5][7]
- Presence Concealment: Her Shadow abilities allow her to avoid ordinary visual detection and conceal herself from experienced supernatural opponents.[5]
- Multi-Directional Defense: Dance of Darkness provides additional protection through numerous moving hair blades that can intercept attacks while Kaguya continues fighting.[7]
- Helheim Containment: Kaguya's large-scale Darkness domain can contain powerful supernatural effects when supported through her compatible conduit.[8]
- Shadow Space Shelter: Kaguya can protect companions from immediate physical exposure by concealing them inside her Shadows.[8]
Resistance limitations: Kaguya's True Dragon and Goddess development substantially increased her abilities, but the available chapters do not establish unconditional immunity to every form of Conceptual Manipulation, Soul Destruction, Time Manipulation, Existence Erasure, or Divine attack. Her confirmed resistances should be distinguished from the broader supernatural potential of the Dragon Race and the more extensive immunities demonstrated by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] himself.

#### Combat Skills

##### Assassination and Martial Arts
Kaguya's fighting style originates from the specialized training of Clan Blank, a family historically responsible for espionage and assassination on behalf of Clan Snow. Her preferred methods emphasize concealment, sudden attacks, precise strikes against vital areas, and eliminating the target before a conventional duel can begin. Although her later transformations made her powerful enough to confront major supernatural beings directly, she continues to recognize assassination as the foundation of her combat training.[1][5][2]
- Master Assassin: Kaguya possesses extensive training in assassination and covert elimination, emphasizing rapid lethal strikes and the exploitation of vulnerabilities.[5][2]
- Assassination Arts: She can combine supernatural movement with precise attacks directed toward vital areas of an opponent's body.[5]
- Vital Point Targeting: Kaguya preferentially attacks vulnerable areas such as the heart and neck. During the encounter with Mizuki, she pierced the hunter's heart and immediately attempted to decapitate her.[5]
- Stealth Mastery: Her training allows her to combine conventional assassin techniques with supernatural Shadow Concealment.[5]
- Ambush Tactics: Kaguya can exploit an opponent's loss of visual contact to appear within striking distance and attack before an effective defense is established.[5]
- Dagger Mastery: She is highly skilled in the use of daggers, particularly those produced through her Darkness Manipulation.[5]
- Swordsmanship: Kaguya can fight with swords formed from Darkness and coordinate their use with her other supernatural abilities.[6][7]
- Dual Wielding: Kaguya has demonstrated the use of two Shadow weapons simultaneously during combat.[5][6]
- Unarmed Combat: Her assassin training and enhanced physical abilities allow her to confront enemies without relying exclusively upon conventional weapons.[7]
- Multi-Weapon Combat: Dance of Darkness allows her to attack through swords, bladed hair, and surrounding Shadow constructs simultaneously.[6][7]
- Multi-Target Combat: Kaguya can eliminate groups of opponents efficiently through territorial control and simultaneous attacks.[6][7]
- Battlefield Control: Kaguya can alter the combat environment using Shadows, barriers, weapons, and mobile constructs.[5][7]
- Combat Evasion: Her preferred style emphasizes avoiding direct confrontation with stronger opponents when stealth and precision provide a safer method of attack.[2]
- Combat Analysis: Kaguya understands the limitations of her training and can distinguish situations requiring assassination from those better handled through direct combat.[2]
- Tactical Adaptation: She can adjust her fighting methods to account for changing enemy abilities, including the deployment of additional Shadow techniques during prolonged battles.[7]
- Weaponized Hair Combat: She can integrate independently moving hair blades with ordinary martial movements during Dance of Darkness.[6][7]
- Supernatural Technique Control: Kaguya can coordinate several complex Shadow applications at once, including movement, barriers, weapon creation, and attacks against multiple enemies.[7]
- Onmyojutsu Recognition: Kaguya recognized the supernatural techniques and talismans employed by Mizuki, demonstrating familiarity with traditional Japanese magical systems.[5]
- Supernatural Historical Knowledge: Kaguya recognized the summoned spirit associated with Abe no Seimei through the characteristics of the ritual and its manifestation.[22]

##### Espionage and Intelligence
- Espionage: Kaguya was trained through Clan Blank to gather information, observe targets, and conduct covert operations.[1][17]
- Infiltration: Her Shadow abilities allow her to enter guarded environments without following conventional entrances or exposing her physical presence.[5][17]
- Surveillance: Kaguya can monitor people and locations while concealed through Shadow Merging or from distant observation points.[1][18]
- Long-Distance Observation: Her enhanced eyesight allows visual surveillance from distances of several kilometers.[1]
- Counterintelligence: Kaguya possesses extensive experience recognizing suspicious individuals, investigating possible threats, and supporting the security of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household.[23][11]
- Intelligence Analysis: She can interpret information obtained through covert observation and determine how it affects the security or political interests of her master.[23][24]
- Investigation: Kaguya can gather and organize evidence concerning political conspiracies. Her work provided Victor with evidence concerning Volk's actions against Tasha.[24]
- Covert Evidence Acquisition: She can obtain sensitive information without openly revealing that Victor's faction has been investigating a target.[24]
- Target Tracking: Kaguya can follow and monitor individuals through a combination of supernatural concealment and trained observational skills.[23]
- Operational Secrecy: Her experience within Clan Blank allows her to conduct assignments while minimizing the information available to outside observers.[5][24]
- Intelligence Network Management: Kaguya eventually acquired a senior leadership position within Clan Blank, providing her with authority over an established family specializing in intelligence and assassination.[25]
- Covert Personnel Deployment: Kaguya can conceal and transport allies through her Shadow Space, allowing groups to be deployed without exposing their numbers or identities beforehand.[8]
- Psychological Warfare: Her later Imperial responsibilities include the supervision of operations intended to destabilize enemies and influence the behavior of hostile factions.[12]

#### Leadership and Non-Combat Skills

##### Chief Maid and Household Administration
Kaguya's original training was not limited to assassination. From childhood, she was prepared to serve the leader or heir of Clan Snow, receiving extensive instruction in household management, personal service, discipline, and the responsibilities expected of a senior Noble Vampire attendant. After choosing to serve [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] directly, she became the senior member of his [[Maid Squad]] and eventually the Chief Maid responsible for organizing the household's attendants.[26][1][27]
- Master Maid: Kaguya possesses exceptional training in the duties of a personal maid, including preparation, clothing, etiquette, organization, and the efficient service of her master.[26][1]
- Household Administration: She can supervise the daily operations of Victor's household and coordinate the responsibilities of other maids.[27]
- Personnel Management: Kaguya possesses authority over members of the Maid Squad and can assign duties appropriate to their abilities and experience.[27]
- Maid Training: She has trained and instructed other members of Victor's household in service, discipline, and proper conduct.[28]
- Exceptional Efficiency: Kaguya can complete ordinary service duties with extraordinary speed and precision through a combination of training and supernatural physical abilities.[26][29]
- Rapid Clothing Preparation: She can change and prepare Victor's clothing with remarkable efficiency, including during his early life as a Vampire.[26]
- Royal Etiquette: Her background serving one of [[Nightingale]]'s most powerful Noble Clans provides experience with aristocratic customs and formal conduct.[1]
- Multitasking: Kaguya can coordinate service, security, supervision, and information gathering without treating these responsibilities as mutually exclusive.[27][19]
- Perfect Service: Even after becoming a Dragon Goddess, Kaguya continues performing her duties as Victor's personal maid. During the Imperial family's celestial banquet, she completed her service so efficiently that the guests barely noticed her movements.[29]
- Administrative Leadership: Kaguya possesses the judgment necessary to supervise personnel and organize operations while Victor concentrates upon responsibilities of greater scale.[19][12]

##### Clan Blank Leadership
- Clan Leadership: [[Oda Blank]] recognized Kaguya as the second leader of Clan Blank, granting her formal authority within the family's intelligence and assassination network.[25]
- Assassin Command: Kaguya can direct members of Clan Blank during covert and military operations.[11][9]
- Organizational Supervision: She can manage the responsibilities of a supernatural family whose members specialize in infiltration, surveillance, and the elimination of dangerous targets.[25]
- Bloodline Leadership: Kaguya later oversaw members of Clan Blank whose supernatural existence had been developed beyond their former Noble Vampire condition.[9]
- Dragonoid Personnel Command: By Chapter 986, the members of Clan Blank operating beneath Kaguya had been transformed into Dragonoids, allowing her to deploy an elite group of supernatural agents possessing Draconic characteristics.[9]
- Supernatural Security: She can oversee assassins and intelligence personnel assigned to protect [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s territories and family.[11][12]
- Political Judgment: Kaguya can act as a representative of Victor's interests during negotiations with outside supernatural groups, including Witches and other Noble factions.[23]
- Diplomacy: Her experience as a senior servant and political representative allows her to negotiate with outside parties rather than relying exclusively upon intimidation or force.[23]

##### Commander of the Emperor's Black Hand
During the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s accelerated development, Kaguya became Commander of the Third Legion — Black Hand of the Emperor, one of the Empire's principal intelligence and covert operations institutions. Nyx Elderblood serves as an alternate commander when Kaguya is occupied with other responsibilities.[12]
- Intelligence Command: Kaguya commands an Imperial Legion specializing in intelligence gathering and the identification of threats to the Empire.[12]
- Espionage Operations: The Black Hand conducts covert operations against hostile factions and individuals operating within or beyond Imperial territory.[12]
- Infiltration Operations: Kaguya's Legion employs specialists capable of entering protected environments and obtaining information without openly revealing Imperial involvement.[12]
- Counterintelligence: The Legion participates in identifying infiltrators, traitors, and individuals acting on behalf of hostile powers.[12]
- Psychological Warfare: Black Hand operations include influencing enemy behavior, destabilizing hostile organizations, and employing psychological pressure against threats.[12]
- Information Control: The Legion has responsibilities involving the protection and management of sensitive Imperial information and narratives.[12]
- Traitor Hunting: Kaguya's forces are assigned to locate and eliminate or apprehend individuals who betray the Empire.[12]
- Covert Enforcement: The Black Hand performs assignments that cannot appropriately be handled through the Empire's ordinary public military institutions.[12]
- Specialized Unit Coordination: The Legion employs Shadow assassins, telepaths, memory manipulators, shapeshifters, Shadow Demons, and other specialists suited to covert operations.[12]
- Operational Delegation: Kaguya can entrust responsibilities to Nyx when her personal duties prevent her from directly commanding the Legion.[12]
- Strategic Planning: Her position requires the coordination of intelligence assets, operational personnel, and covert objectives across an enormous Empire.[12]
- Imperial Security Leadership: Kaguya occupies one of the highest positions in the Empire's specialized security structure, with responsibilities extending beyond the protection of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s immediate household.[12]
Important distinction: The Black Hand employs telepaths, memory manipulators, shapeshifters, and other specialized operatives. Their individual racial and supernatural powers should not automatically be listed as personal abilities of Kaguya merely because she commands those personnel.[12]
