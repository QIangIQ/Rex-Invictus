### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 182: He's Coming for You
He is my master, my father, my creator, my god... He is my everything... Having him in my life is enough.
—Eve about [[Victor Elderblood]], Chapter 260
Eve Elderblood, formerly Eve Alucard, is a Blood Dragon, Dragon Goddess, one of Victor Elderblood's personal Battle Maids, his adopted daughter and later one of his wives. Originally an abused Human girl who had been denied even a personal name, Eve was rescued and transformed into a Noble Vampire by Victor during his early years as the newly appointed Fifth Vampire Count. He personally named her Eve Alucard, making her the first individual besides Victor himself to formally bear the Alucard surname and the second official member of the Clan that eventually became Clan Elderblood.[4][5]
Eve is distinguished by two unusual supernatural characteristics: Alter Eve, a counterpart originating from the companion she perceived during her traumatic Human childhood, and Mutant Black Fire, an inherited manifestation of Victor's Fire that developed corruptive properties through the influence of Eve's previous experiences. Unlike an ordinary descendant who inherits an established racial ability without substantial modification, Eve manifested her creator's Fire in an abnormal form whose properties were shaped by her individual history. Her original Black Fire was already capable of enormous destruction, while its subsequent evolution into Dragon Fire provided a far more dangerous corruptive characteristic.[6][7][1]
Alter Eve represents an equally important aspect of Eve's existence. Before becoming a Vampire, Eve regarded her as an imaginary companion who had remained beside her throughout her confinement. Victor nevertheless perceived an unusual feminine silhouette associated with Eve even while she was Human, suggesting that the companion possessed supernatural significance before its nature became fully apparent. After Eve's Vampire transformation, Alter Eve demonstrated the ability to communicate, influence their shared body and assume control during certain circumstances. Her existence became considerably more independent following Eve's transformation into a True Dragon, allowing her to leave Eve's body and function as a separate negative counterpart.[8][4][6][1]
Eve's life is closely connected to the formation of Victor's personal household. She became his third personal maid after Kaguya and Bruna, trained alongside the other women who eventually formed the [[Maid Squad]], and participated in numerous conflicts as Victor's faction expanded beyond [[Nightingale]]. Her extraordinary loyalty to Victor initially reflected the parental security she had never experienced during her Human childhood. Although Victor became the central figure in her life, he repeatedly encouraged Eve to develop her own identity and exercise the free will that had previously been denied to her. Her place within the family subsequently expanded through the formal acceptance of the Battle Maids into Clan Alucard, their transformation into [[Blood Dragons]] and the centuries of development experienced through accelerated Time.[9][10][1]
After becoming a True Dragon, Eve acquired the superior physiology of Victor's Draconic lineage while retaining the abilities and characteristics she had developed as a Vampire. Her Mutant Black Fire evolved into Dragon Fire with substantially stronger corrosive and corruptive properties, while Alter Eve gained the ability to operate externally rather than remaining limited to their shared consciousness. Eve eventually entered a marital relationship with Victor and became the biological mother of [[Evelyn Elderblood]], a member of the next generation of Clan Elderblood.[1][3][2]

### Physical Description

#### Human
Before meeting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Eve was an eighteen-year-old Human girl whose prolonged confinement, starvation and mistreatment had severely interfered with her physical development. Despite having reached adulthood, she stood only approximately 150 centimeters tall and possessed an extremely malnourished body that made her appear considerably younger than her actual age. Her pale skin was covered with injuries and scars accumulated throughout years of parental abuse, while her extraordinarily long black hair was dirty, tangled and reached almost to the floor. Her original eyes were sapphire blue, contrasting with the blood-red eyes she acquired after becoming a Vampire.[8][4][5]
The conditions in which Eve had lived were reflected in nearly every aspect of her appearance. She was kept inside a cage within a dark, neglected room, with little opportunity to move freely or care for herself. The unnatural smallness of her body was a direct consequence of malnutrition rather than a characteristic of her original physical constitution. Victor recognized that she had already reached adulthood and understood that the circumstances of her upbringing had prevented her body from developing normally.[4]
The most unusual aspect of Eve's Human existence was not immediately visible through ordinary observation. Victor's supernatural perception revealed a feminine silhouette floating near her, possessing a void-like quality that appeared to be awaiting something. Although Eve initially seemed to be an ordinary Human from a biological perspective, the presence suggested an abnormal characteristic that neither Victor nor Kaguya could immediately explain. The silhouette would later become significant when Eve revealed the existence of Alter Eve.[4][6]

#### Noble Vampire
Eve's original Vampire appearance.
Eve's transformation into a Noble Vampire immediately repaired much of the physical damage caused by her Human upbringing. Her previously malnourished body developed into a healthy adult physique appropriate to her age, while her height increased from approximately 150 centimeters to 168 centimeters. Her long black hair became healthier and more lustrous, and her eyes changed from sapphire blue to the blood-red coloration associated with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Vampiric lineage.[5]
The change was sufficiently dramatic that Eve scarcely resembled the girl Victor had discovered inside the cage. Her body now possessed the physical characteristics and supernatural capabilities of a Noble Vampire, allowing her to move, train and experience the world without the biological limitations imposed by years of deprivation. Victor was particularly interested in the potential concealed within her new existence, recognizing that her unusual psychological history and the mysterious silhouette he had previously detected might produce extraordinary results as she developed.[5]
Following her incorporation into Victor's household, Eve generally wore the black-and-white maid uniform associated with the [[Maid Squad]]. Although the individual members of the group possessed substantially different personalities and preferences, Eve's restrained demeanor and dark hair gave her an appearance noticeably more reserved than that of some of her more expressive companions. Her habitual expression was comparatively stoic, particularly around unfamiliar people, although her emotions became much easier to recognize when she was interacting with Victor or the other women she trusted.[6][10]

#### Blood Dragon
Eve following her transformation into a Blood Dragon.
Eve's transformation into a genuine Blood Dragon reconstructed her physical and spiritual existence through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Draconic lineage. Her new body acquired the horns, wings and other supernatural characteristics associated with [[True Dragons]], while her former Vampire powers became integrated into a stronger racial foundation. The transformation did not replace Eve's individual appearance or personality with those of an unrelated being; instead, it preserved the traits she had developed throughout her previous existence while allowing them to evolve through genuine Draconic physiology.[1]
Her black hair and red eyes remained characteristic aspects of her humanoid appearance, while the newly developed Draconic features reflected the influence of her evolved Black Fire. Like the other women transformed during the same period, Eve's horns and wings were associated with her individual coloration and supernatural characteristics. The transformation also increased the strength of her original abilities, allowing the Fire inherited from Victor to become a genuine form of Mutant Dragon Fire.[1]
The most important changes were not exclusively physical. Eve's mind became considerably more stable following the transformation, while Alter Eve's existence developed in the opposite direction from what might ordinarily be expected of a purely psychological phenomenon. Rather than disappearing, Alter Eve gained the ability to emerge from Eve's body and manifest as a separate negative copy. The two could therefore exist independently for periods of time despite Alter Eve having originated as an unusual component of Eve's own existence.[1]

#### Alter Eve
Alter Eve possesses an appearance closely resembling Eve's own, but with two particularly distinctive differences: white hair and sapphire-blue eyes. The latter preserves the coloration of Eve's original Human eyes before her transformation into a Vampire. Her visual characteristics emphasize her status as a contrasting version of Eve rather than a completely unrelated supernatural creature.[6]
Although Alter Eve's appearance is similar to Eve's, her expressions and behavior are considerably more animated. She frequently smiles provocatively, expresses irritation openly and behaves with a degree of confidence and aggression rarely displayed by Eve herself. Before the Blood Dragon transformation, the two could communicate internally and exchange control of their shared body, while Alter Eve could also appear separately under circumstances associated with her unusual supernatural nature.[6]
Following Dragonization, Alter Eve's existence became more concrete, allowing her to leave Eve's body and function as an independent negative counterpart. This development should be distinguished from ordinary illusion creation or the manifestation of a temporary visual projection, since the narration explicitly identifies the increased independence as one of the consequences of Eve's new Draconic existence.[1]

### Personality
Eve is naturally quiet, cautious, observant and emotionally guarded, possessing a strong distrust of unfamiliar people that originated from the severe mistreatment she experienced during her Human childhood. Having spent nearly her entire early life confined and abused by the individuals who should have protected her, Eve developed considerable difficulty trusting others or accepting affection without questioning the motives behind it. Her reserved demeanor is therefore not a simple preference for silence, but also reflects the emotional defenses she developed while living in circumstances where expressing her desires or resisting authority frequently resulted in punishment. Even after acquiring enough supernatural power to protect herself, Eve initially struggled to abandon habits formed during the years when she had been completely dependent upon her parents.[4][6]
Her behavior changes substantially around [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. He was the first person to recognize her circumstances, ask what she wanted and give her the opportunity to determine her own future rather than deciding everything on her behalf. Eve consequently developed an extraordinary attachment to him, regarding Victor simultaneously as her rescuer, Master, Father, creator and the most important person in her life. She places enormous value upon his presence and approval, frequently treating the possibility of remaining near him as more important than personal rewards, status or material possessions. Although her attachment gradually acquired additional dimensions as she matured, the paternal security Victor provided remained one of the foundations of her identity throughout her early development.[5][6][10]
Eve's dependence upon Victor is accompanied by a persistent tendency toward self-criticism. She often evaluates her own worth through whether she is capable of serving him adequately and can become distressed when she believes her abilities or behavior are insufficient. This disposition became particularly apparent when her Black Fire first awakened. Rather than interpreting the extraordinary manifestation as proof of her potential, Eve considered the flames inferior to Victor's beautiful Fire and believed their dark appearance reflected the corruption of her own existence. She associated the abnormal coloration with sins she believed she had committed, despite the fact that the suffering shaping those feelings had been inflicted upon her by others.[6]
Alter Eve directly opposes this pattern of self-condemnation. She frequently criticizes Eve for internalizing the guilt associated with her abuse, recognizing that their former circumstances had not been the result of Eve's own choices. The two therefore possess a complicated relationship in which Alter Eve often expresses the anger, confidence and willingness to pursue pleasure that Eve herself suppresses. Although their interactions frequently involve arguments, insults and disagreements, Alter Eve is fundamentally protective of Eve and repeatedly attempts to encourage her to enjoy the freedom gained after Victor rescued them.[6]
Despite Eve's emotional difficulties, she possesses considerable intelligence and an inquisitive personality. She quickly absorbs information about supernatural phenomena, observes the behavior of the people surrounding her and can recognize practical considerations that other members of Victor's household overlook. Her analytical tendencies became especially evident during the expansion of the [[Blood God Religion]], when she examined the clothing worn by its Acolytes and recognized the importance of visual presentation in shaping the public's perception of a new religious organization. Eve understood that white clothing created associations with cleanliness, medicine, safety and holiness, making it more suitable for public appearances than the dark clothing she had originally recommended for its practical advantages.[11]
This ability to evaluate situations logically provides a useful contrast with the more impulsive personalities of Bruna, Maria and Roberta. Eve is not incapable of humor or emotional expression, but she generally requires a greater degree of familiarity before displaying those qualities openly. Her quietness can occasionally make her an easy target for teasing, particularly when the other Battle Maids notice that she has become embarrassed or is attempting to conceal something. Although Eve frequently responds with irritation, these interactions also demonstrate the comfort she gradually developed within a household where disagreements and teasing did not automatically lead to rejection or punishment.[7]
Eve takes her responsibilities as Victor's maid seriously, but her understanding of service gradually developed beyond simply obeying instructions. Victor specifically warned her against attempting to become another Kaguya, explaining that the Head Maid's skills and personality had developed through her own circumstances and experiences. He wanted Eve to discover what kind of person and servant she wished to become rather than surrendering her new identity to an ideal copied from someone else. The advice was particularly important because Eve had previously been denied the opportunity to make even the most basic decisions concerning her own life.[12]
Victor reinforced this principle when he formally offered the Battle Maids permanent membership in his Clan. Eve was already an Alucard, but he recognized that the emotional significance of the event extended to her as well. He reminded Eve that she could rely upon him and the other members of the household, encouraged her to express her actual desires and emphasized that she possessed the freedom to decide how she wished to live. Eve acknowledged that what she wanted most was to remain beside him, explaining that she disliked being left behind whenever he traveled to dangerous locations. Victor accepted her decision while continuing to insist that her attachment should arise from her own [[Will Solace|Will]] rather than the restrictions imposed by her former circumstances.[10]
Eve's relationship with the rest of the [[Maid Squad]] became an important source of emotional stability. Kaguya provided discipline and professional guidance, Bruna and Maria offered contrasting personalities through which Eve could experience ordinary social interaction, and Roberta introduced another example of a person whose existence involved an unusual internal counterpart. Over time, Eve began considering the other maids her sisters, recognizing that she could turn to them when Victor was absent rather than remaining isolated until his return. The development was especially significant because Eve had never possessed a healthy family during her Human childhood.[10]
Her transformation into a Blood Dragon marked another stage in this progression. The narration explicitly states that Eve's mental condition became substantially more centered after Dragonization, indicating that the improvement in her physical and spiritual existence was accompanied by greater psychological stability. Alter Eve remained part of her existence, however, demonstrating that increased emotional maturity did not require eliminating the counterpart who had accompanied Eve during the most difficult period of her life. Instead, their relationship became more developed as Alter Eve acquired the ability to act independently.[1]
After centuries of accelerated development, Eve became more confident within the Elderblood family and gradually acquired responsibilities extending beyond the household duties originally assigned to her. Her relationship with Victor eventually became marital, and she became the mother of [[Evelyn Elderblood]]. Although Eve continued retaining elements of her reserved and easily embarrassed personality, the contrast between her early life and her later circumstances was considerable: she had developed from an isolated girl who barely possessed an identity of her own into a powerful Dragon Goddess, Battle Maid and mother within one of the largest supernatural families in existence.[3][2]

### Background
Eve was born to two ordinary Humans in the United States, but her early life was defined almost entirely by prolonged abuse and confinement. Her parents refused to provide her with a proper personal name, referring to their daughter by the degrading designation "Dog". They kept her imprisoned inside a cage within a dark room, provided insufficient nourishment and subjected her to repeated physical mistreatment. By the age of eighteen, the effects of these circumstances were visible throughout her body: she remained severely underdeveloped, possessed numerous injuries and scars, and had little experience with ordinary Human relationships or the world beyond the room in which she had been confined.[8][4]
The extreme isolation also shaped her psychological development. Eve became accustomed to expecting cruelty from the people surrounding her and gradually lost the ability to imagine a future in which she could live independently. Her life offered few opportunities to establish relationships, develop personal interests or receive the affection ordinarily associated with family. The books scattered throughout her room provided some evidence that she had attempted to occupy herself despite her captivity, but they could not compensate for the absence of genuine Human companionship.[4]
During this period, Eve developed the companion she later called Alter Eve. She initially understood the presence as an imaginary friend who kept her company throughout the years of confinement, providing someone with whom she could communicate when no one else offered comfort. The companion possessed a considerably more aggressive and expressive personality than Eve herself, frequently demonstrating the emotions Eve had learned to suppress. Its nature remained unexplained until [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] discovered Eve and observed an unusual feminine silhouette associated with her through his supernatural perception.[4][6]
Eve's life changed when Victor encountered her while traveling through [[New York]]. His attention was drawn by the abnormal supernatural presence associated with the imprisoned girl, leading him to investigate circumstances he had not originally expected to encounter. After discovering the conditions in which she was being kept, Victor questioned Eve about her name, age and treatment, eventually realizing the extent of the suffering inflicted upon her. He offered to remove her from the situation and provided her with a choice concerning the future she wanted to pursue.[8][4]
The encounter ended with the death of Eve's biological parents and her decision to accompany Victor. He subsequently transformed her into a Vampire, restored her physical development and gave her the name Eve Alucard. This act established the first genuine addition to Victor's newly founded Clan and provided Eve with a personal identity she had never previously possessed.[4][5]

### Synopsis

#### A New Pillar
Eve entered the narrative while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was traveling through [[New York]] with Kaguya after rescuing Bruna. During the journey, Victor perceived an unusual supernatural presence associated with a Human girl and followed the sensation toward the building in which she was being held. He discovered an eighteen-year-old woman imprisoned inside a cage, surrounded by evidence of prolonged physical abuse and neglect. Her parents had denied her a normal identity and referred to her only as "Dog", while her severely malnourished body and extensive injuries demonstrated that she had been subjected to these conditions for years.[8][4]
Victor's initial curiosity concerned the strange feminine silhouette his senses detected beside the girl. Although her physical body appeared Human, the unusual presence resembled a void-like figure whose characteristics he could not immediately identify. Kaguya recognized the mistreatment surrounding Eve, while Victor questioned the imprisoned girl and attempted to understand her circumstances. Eve responded with the subdued behavior of someone who had long ago learned that resistance was dangerous, but became increasingly distressed when she believed Victor might leave without taking her away from the room.[4]
After learning about Eve's circumstances, Victor brought her before her biological parents and allowed her to decide how they should be treated. Eve chose death, and Victor destroyed them with his Fire. The sight had a profound influence upon her because the flames represented the end of the people responsible for her suffering and the beginning of a life she had previously considered impossible. Rather than viewing the Fire primarily as an instrument of destruction, Eve associated its heat and appearance with freedom and the person who had finally answered her desire to escape.[4][5]
Victor then offered Eve the opportunity to accompany him. Their conversation developed into a discussion concerning the nature of Vampires and the freedom to pursue one's desires. Eve accepted the possibility of transformation and a new life beside him, while Victor approved of her willingness to claim the future she wanted rather than continuing to surrender decisions to others. He subsequently announced that she would become his personal maid and formally named her Eve Alucard.[5]
The transformation was immediate and dramatic. After Victor bit her and introduced his Blood into her body, Eve acquired the physiology of a Noble Vampire. Her body recovered from the severe consequences of malnutrition, increasing in height from approximately 150 to 168 centimeters while developing into a healthy adult form. Her eyes became blood-red, her hair regained its natural health and the injuries accumulated throughout her Human life ceased defining her appearance. Victor noticed that the unusual feminine silhouette previously associated with Eve was no longer externally visible, suggesting that the transformation had altered the relationship between Eve and the mysterious presence.[5]
The naming also carried major significance for Clan Alucard. Victor had only recently received the political title of Vampire Count, and the Clan itself had not yet acquired a substantial independent bloodline. By giving Eve his surname, he established her as the first formal member beneath him, making her the second official bearer of the Alucard name. Kaguya immediately recognized the political implications of the decision, since not even Victor's original wives had formally entered his newly founded Clan at that point.[5]
When Victor returned home carrying Eve and accompanied by Bruna, his household was surprised by the addition of two newly transformed Vampires. He introduced the women as members of his personal service and explained the organization he intended to create around Kaguya. Rather than surrounding himself with enormous numbers of ordinary servants, Victor wanted a comparatively small group of highly capable Battle Maids who could survive dangerous supernatural conflicts and protect his household even when he was absent. Kaguya became the standard toward which the newer maids were expected to develop, while Bruna and Eve began adjusting to their new identities and responsibilities.[9]
Eve's first experiences outside her former captivity were consequently shaped by the unusually powerful and complicated household into which she had entered. She was surrounded by Noble Vampires, powerful supernatural women and individuals whose relationships were difficult for an inexperienced Human to understand. Although the environment presented challenges, it also provided access to protection, education and companionship unavailable throughout her previous life. Her attachment to Victor developed rapidly, while Kaguya and the other maids gradually became the foundation of her new social world.[9]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Eve's development continued as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] expanded his responsibilities beyond the ordinary political affairs of Vampire Society. Rather than leaving the newly transformed girl permanently confined to domestic duties, he occasionally brought her along during his travels and expeditions, allowing her to encounter supernatural environments and experience the freedom of movement that had been denied throughout her Human childhood. During an expedition involving Eleanor, Victor selected Eve to accompany him, provoking jealousy among the other maids who also wished to remain close to their Master.[13]
The journey demonstrated that Eve still possessed much to learn about the supernatural world. Victor traveled through the use of his increasingly powerful Lightning, moving at a speed that was extraordinary even by Vampire standards. Eve had difficulty tolerating the experience and became visibly sick after reaching their destination, revealing the considerable difference between possessing enhanced physical characteristics and having the training or experience necessary to endure Victor's preferred methods of transportation.[14]
Victor also began addressing Eve's tendency to imitate Kaguya. Eve regarded the Head Maid as an extraordinary example of professional competence and attempted to reproduce aspects of her behavior, believing that becoming more like Kaguya would make her a better servant. Victor recognized that this could prevent Eve from developing an independent personality and explicitly instructed her to find her own style. Kaguya had spent centuries refining her skills through assassination, service and her particular upbringing, whereas Eve possessed a completely different history and an unusual supernatural characteristic whose potential remained unknown.[12]
Eve's individuality became apparent during a confrontation with powerful Demonic Beasts. As the group prepared to fight, Eve began interacting with the internal presence that had accompanied her since her Human childhood. The figure possessed Eve's general appearance but differed through white hair, sapphire-blue eyes and an aggressively expressive personality. Eve called her Alter Eve, treating the name as a practical designation for a companion whose precise supernatural nature remained uncertain.[6]
Alter Eve's behavior contrasted sharply with Eve's usual restraint. She spoke freely, insulted others, expressed excitement over their new life and complained that Eve was refusing to enjoy the freedom Victor had given them. Eve, by comparison, remained focused upon following Victor's instructions and ensuring that she did not disappoint him. Their exchange revealed that Alter Eve could communicate with Eve internally and influence their shared existence, while their differing personalities produced an ongoing conflict concerning how Eve should live after escaping her parents.[6]
During the same battle, Eve awakened an important characteristic of Victor's bloodline. A column of black Fire erupted from her body, surprising Eleanor and Sasha because the flames were clearly related to Victor's inherited Fire yet possessed an ominous coloration unlike the conventional powers of the [[Snow Clan]]. Eve immediately interpreted the dark appearance as a reflection of her own perceived impurity, while Alter Eve rejected the idea that the abuse Eve had endured made her responsible for the sins of the people who harmed her.[6]
Eve then demonstrated Supernova, a technique Victor had taught her. She concentrated the black flames into one hand and released them as a beam toward a fleeing Demonic Beast. The attack pursued its target and subsequently detonated, producing an explosion so powerful that Eleanor believed the surrounding forest could have been destroyed had the detonation occurred closer to the group. The feat established that Eve possessed considerable offensive potential despite her relative inexperience as a Vampire.[6]
The emergence of Black Fire was also important to the development of Clan Alucard. Vampire tradition allowed descendants created by a Progenitor to inherit characteristics associated with their creator, and Eve's Fire represented a direct manifestation of Victor's inherited bloodline power. Unlike a mere political subordinate carrying the Clan's name, Eve had demonstrated a genuine supernatural characteristic inherited from its founder. Her awakening therefore provided some of the earliest direct evidence that Victor's newly established Clan could produce descendants possessing unique variations of his abilities.[6]
Eve continued training with the other maids as the conflicts surrounding Victor's faction intensified. [[Scathach]] took greater responsibility for their martial development, recognizing that Victor's personal servants would require considerably greater strength if they were to survive the enemies he continued attracting. Kaguya remained the most experienced combatant among the maids, while Eve, Bruna, Maria and Roberta developed their own specialties and gradually became more capable of supporting Victor rather than relying entirely upon his intervention.[15]
Their relationship with Victor also developed beyond the ordinary obligations of service. The Battle Maids repeatedly placed themselves in danger for his sake, and Victor increasingly treated them as individuals whose safety and happiness were as important to him as those of the other members of his family. Eve's position was especially distinctive because she had possessed the Alucard name from the beginning and already regarded Victor as her Father, even while the formal relationship between the remaining maids and the Clan had not yet been established.[16][17]

#### Demonic War
During the Demonic War, Eve continued serving as one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Battle Maids while the strength and responsibilities of his faction expanded considerably. She participated in operations alongside Kaguya, Bruna, Maria, Roberta and Roxanne, whose abilities increasingly complemented one another. Their collective responsibilities involved protecting Victor's household, supporting his movements and confronting supernatural enemies who would have been beyond the capabilities of ordinary Noble Vampires.[18][19]
Eve was among the women who supported Victor during the large-scale conflict surrounding the Vatican. Kaguya's Shadow techniques allowed members of the [[Maid Squad]] to remain close to their Master and respond when necessary, while the group's combination of supernatural powers provided additional protection and tactical flexibility. The experience reinforced the importance of the Battle Maids as a specialized combat force rather than merely attendants responsible for domestic service.[18][19]
A major development occurred when Victor gathered the Battle Maids and discussed their position within his family. By that point, Kaguya, Bruna, Maria, Roberta and Eve had become indispensable companions whose relationships with him could no longer reasonably be reduced to employment. Victor offered the women formal membership in Clan Alucard, explaining that those who accepted would become permanent members of his family rather than individuals whose service could be ended like an ordinary contractual arrangement.[10]
Eve's circumstances differed from those of her companions. Victor had already named her Eve Alucard when he rescued her, and she had formally belonged to his Clan since the day of her transformation. He explicitly recognized this distinction, identifying Eve and Roxanne as existing examples of individuals who were already members of his family. For the other maids, the conversation represented an opportunity to enter the bloodline formally; for Eve, it became an opportunity to confront the insecurity and dependence that still affected her despite her established position.[10]
Victor embraced Eve and reminded her that she no longer needed to fear the darkness or the abandonment she had experienced during her childhood. He explained that she could rely upon him and the other members of his family, including the Battle Maids whom she had gradually come to regard as sisters. Eve admitted that what she wanted most was to remain beside him permanently and that she hated being left behind whenever he departed for dangerous locations. She continued addressing him as Father while explaining that his presence had become synonymous with the idea of home.[10]
Victor accepted her desire, but emphasized that her decision needed to originate from her own free will. He did not want Eve to replace the absolute control previously exercised by her biological parents with another form of compulsory dependence. Instead, he encouraged her to pursue the life she genuinely wanted while remembering that she would always have a family to return to. The conversation marked an important emotional development because Eve openly expressed her wishes rather than concealing them out of fear that they would be rejected.[10]
Eve also explained the existence of Alter Eve to the other Battle Maids during this conversation. She described Alter as a companion who had remained with her throughout the years of captivity and continued existing after her supernatural transformation. The revelation surprised the other women, particularly because Roberta also possessed an unusual internal existence through [[Medusa]]. Victor revealed that he had been aware of Eve's companion from the beginning, but rejected the implication that he had rescued Eve merely because of the unusual power he detected. His decision to intervene had been based upon her circumstances, even though he remained interested in the potential of her supernatural abilities.[10]
After formally incorporating the remaining maids into his Clan, Victor strengthened their bloodlines through his Progenitor Blood. The transformation granted them greater physical capabilities and, in some cases, additional inherited powers. Victor subsequently reflected upon why Eve and Bruna had awakened abilities that differed from those possessed by women who had already been supernatural before receiving his Blood. He concluded that their original Human experiences had strongly influenced the powers awakened through their transformations.[7]
In Eve's case, the connection was direct. She had admired the Fire Victor used to destroy her abusive parents and associated the heat of the flames with her release from captivity. This emotional association contributed to her awakening Fire as an inherited characteristic, while the accumulated trauma from her Human life caused the power to develop an abnormal, corrupted manifestation. Her Black Fire therefore resulted from the interaction between Victor's bloodline and Eve's individual experiences rather than representing an unrelated elemental power acquired by chance.[7]
The strengthening of the Maid Squad also elevated Eve's standing within the group. Although Kaguya remained the Head Maid and Victor's principal representative in his absence, Eve was one of the earliest and most important members of the Clan. Her continued training and development prepared her for the far greater conflicts that would emerge after the Demonic War, while her emotional connection to the other maids became increasingly important during periods when Victor was absent.[7]
Victor's descent into Hell eventually separated him from many of the women who normally remained beside him. The uncertainty surrounding his return had a considerable impact upon Eve and the other maids, who had come to depend upon his presence as the central figure connecting their household. Rather than remaining passive during the conflict, they redirected their anger and frustration toward the Demonic forces threatening Japan, continuing to fight while waiting for news of their Master.[20][21]

#### King Among Kings
Following the Demonic War, Eve's responsibilities expanded beyond the immediate protection of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household. Although she remained one of his personal Battle Maids, she also participated in discussions concerning the social and institutional development of the enormous supernatural faction forming around him. Her analytical tendencies proved useful in situations requiring an understanding of Human behavior rather than direct combat.[11]
One of the most important examples was the development of the [[Blood God Religion]]. The religion had originated through Bruna's extraordinary devotion to Victor after he rescued her from the institution that had betrayed her. Bruna's Faith gradually influenced Maria, Eve and Roberta, creating a circle of believers within Victor's household before the religion expanded into the Noble Vampire Clans, Hell, [[Nightingale]] and Earth. By the time its leadership was being formalized, the institution had become a major religious and political organization requiring professional administration, trained Acolytes and a carefully developed public identity.[11]
During the appointment of the religion's High Priestess, Eve examined the white uniforms worn by its Acolytes and recognized their psychological significance. She had originally considered black clothing more practical because it concealed stains and was suitable for covert movement, but concluded that white was more appropriate for an organization attempting to establish trust among ordinary Humans. She explained that white was associated with doctors, cleanliness, safety and holiness, making it more likely that observers would perceive the Acolytes positively rather than viewing them as dangerous supernatural cultists.[11]
The discussion demonstrated Eve's capacity for practical analysis. She understood that an institution's reputation depended not only upon the supernatural power of its members but also upon the impressions created through their appearance and behavior. Her contribution reflected the broader development of a woman who had once possessed almost no direct experience with ordinary Human society but had gradually become capable of evaluating its psychological and cultural conventions.[11]
Eve also participated in the conflict surrounding the [[Greek Pantheon]]. Before the assault against [[Olympus]], Victor placed the Battle Maids under Kaguya's command and provided them with weapons empowered by his Blood. The enhancement was particularly important because the enemies they were preparing to confront included Gods whose supernatural bodies and Divine characteristics could make ordinary physical attacks insufficient to achieve permanent destruction. Victor's Blood allowed the maids to overcome those limitations and kill Divine opponents rather than merely damage their temporary manifestations.[22]
The campaign represented another important stage in Eve's progression. Only a comparatively short period earlier, she had been an inexperienced Vampire learning to control a newly awakened elemental ability. By the conflict against Olympus, she had become a trained Battle Maid capable of accompanying an elite force whose assigned enemies included members of an entire Divine Pantheon. Her continued association with Kaguya and the other maids allowed her to develop within a disciplined combat organization while benefiting from Victor's increasingly powerful bloodline.[22]
Eve's personal relationship with Victor also continued developing as the family expanded. Although she had originally regarded him primarily as a paternal figure and her rescuer, their relationship eventually acquired additional dimensions after centuries of supernatural development. The position she held within his family was therefore not static, although her original Alucard identity and the circumstances of her rescue remained foundational aspects of her history.[2]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ascension as the Dragon God of [[Chaos]], he acquired the ability to transform members of his family into genuine [[True Dragons]]. Eve was among the women who accepted this evolution, abandoning the limitations of her previous Noble Vampire physiology while retaining the accumulated characteristics of her original existence. Her transformation provided a new Draconic body and Soul capable of supporting much greater quantities of supernatural Energy and more advanced manifestations of her existing abilities.[1]
The evolution was especially significant because Eve's Black Fire had already been unusual among the powers inherited by Victor's descendants. Its original manifestation resulted from her affinity for Victor's Fire being altered by the circumstances of her Human life, creating flames with a darker and more corrosive character than conventional [[Snow Clan]] Fire. After becoming a True Dragon, the ability evolved into genuine Dragon Fire, strengthening the corruptive aspect that distinguished it from ordinary elemental flames.[7][1]
The narration specifically compared Eve's Mutant Fire with the Corruptible Fire developed by [[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]]. Eleanor's evolved flames possessed the ability to corrupt a victim's Soul even if the initial attack failed to kill the target, while Eve's Fire displayed a comparable general nature despite possessing a different underlying essence. The comparison established that Eve's Black Fire had advanced beyond an ordinary inherited Vampire ability, although the exact interaction between its individual corruptive properties and every form of spiritual resistance was not separately explained.[1]
Alter Eve underwent an equally important transformation. Eve's mental state became considerably more stable after Dragonization, but the supernatural counterpart who had existed throughout her Human and Vampire lives did not disappear. Instead, Alter Eve acquired greater independence, gaining the ability to leave Eve's body and act as a separate negative copy. The development demonstrated that Alter Eve was not merely a temporary psychological state that would disappear when Eve became emotionally healthier, but a genuine supernatural component of her existence whose properties developed alongside her racial evolution.[1]
The new abilities also changed Eve's relationship with her original Human experiences. Her Fire had been shaped by the memories of escaping her abusive parents, while Alter Eve had accompanied her during the period when she possessed no other meaningful companionship. Rather than erasing these characteristics, Dragonization preserved them and allowed them to become considerably more powerful. Eve consequently retained the individual abilities that had defined her identity as the first member of Clan Alucard while acquiring the superior existence of a Blood Dragon.[7][1]
Following the transformation, Eve continued serving as one of Victor's Dragon Goddesses and Battle Maids. Her position within the household remained important because the maids were among the individuals most closely connected to Victor's daily life, while their increasingly powerful abilities allowed them to participate in responsibilities far beyond ordinary domestic service.[1]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
After the enormous periods of accelerated development experienced by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family, Eve's existence underwent further changes. Although only a comparatively short period passed in the external world, Victor and many of his closest companions experienced approximately two thousand subjective years through accelerated Time. During this period, Eve matured both psychologically and supernaturally while continuing to develop her relationship with Victor and the other women of Clan Elderblood.[23]
Eve eventually became one of Victor's wives, and their relationship acquired a marital dimension distinct from the parental role he had originally fulfilled during her rescue and adoption. The overlap between these relationships reflected the unusual familial arrangements established within Victor's enormous supernatural household, whose members possessed personal histories and relationships that did not always correspond to conventional Human family structures.[2]
The most important development associated with Eve during this period was the birth of her biological daughter, [[Evelyn Elderblood]]. Evelyn belonged to the increasingly numerous generation of children produced after Victor's family had acquired the supernatural power and opportunities for accelerated development associated with the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Her existence gave Eve a biological family of her own, representing a substantial change from the circumstances of her Human childhood.[3]
Evelyn was introduced among the younger Elderblood daughters participating in imaginative games together. She enthusiastically role-played as the Herald of Darkness, challenging an imaginary champion of Light while attempting to introduce herself and Nikolina in suitably dramatic fashion. The scene emphasized the individuality and playful personalities of Victor's children, whose enormous supernatural potential did not prevent them from behaving like members of an affectionate family.[3]
Eve's position as Evelyn's mother also introduced responsibilities extending beyond combat and household service. Her own upbringing had been defined by neglect, confinement and the denial of personal identity, whereas her daughter grew up surrounded by powerful relatives who competed to provide affection and attention. The contrast illustrated the profound change that had occurred in Eve's circumstances since Victor first discovered her in [[New York]].[5][3]
As Victor's household became increasingly complicated, Eve remained part of the private communications and organizational arrangements used by his wives. The group had developed into an enormous extended family whose members possessed different political, Divine and administrative responsibilities, requiring coordination that would have been unnecessary during the early years of Clan Alucard. Eve's continued participation reflected her permanent position within the family rather than merely the continuation of her original occupation as a personal maid.[2]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
During the later Imperial era, Eve continued serving as a member of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family and the [[Maid Squad]] while the scale of the [[Elderblood Empire]] expanded enormously. The civilization possessed advanced scientific and supernatural institutions capable of operating across multiple worlds, while Victor's wives occupied increasingly specialized positions within its administration, military forces and religious organizations. Eve remained among the women most closely associated with Victor's household rather than becoming one of the public-facing rulers responsible for governing an entire Pantheon.[24]
The development of the Empire's religion reflected this distinction. When the [[Blood God Religion]] established enormous public temples containing representations of the officially recognized Dragon Goddesses, several women who preferred avoiding public worship did not receive the same prominent treatment. Eve was among the women who did not regularly appear as a public Divine figure, alongside Kaguya, Bruna, Maria, Roberta and Maya. Although she possessed the existence of a Dragon Goddess, her personal responsibilities and preferences remained more closely connected to Victor's household than to maintaining a public religious identity.[24]
Eve continued participating in family gatherings involving the enormous number of women associated with Victor. During a later meeting, she appeared alongside Hela, Nyx, Bruna, Mizuki and Violet while the women discussed the activities of Victor and other members of the household. The gathering demonstrated the continued integration of the original Battle Maids into the broader family, where Eve now interacted with ancient Primordial Goddesses and other extraordinarily powerful beings as fellow members of the same household rather than distant supernatural authorities.[25]
The discussion also involved an enormous extraterrestrial creature whose body possessed a supernatural connection to Space. The women examined information concerning the creature's size, biology and predatory habits, comparing it with records obtained through Imperial exploration. Eve contributed to the discussion by observing the strength of the Empire's Leviathans and the fact that such enormous creatures hunted in groups, demonstrating familiarity with the extraordinary lifeforms encountered as the Empire expanded beyond its original territory.[25]
During a subsequent family celebration, Victor prepared an enormous banquet within an environment constructed from supernatural and celestial materials. Kaguya assumed responsibility for serving the guests with the extraordinary efficiency expected of her position as the Head Maid, while Bruna attempted to assist before being firmly refused. Eve participated in the surrounding conversation, commenting upon Kaguya's behavior and its resemblance to the personality displayed by Yuno. The exchange reflected the continuing familiarity among the original maids, whose interactions remained recognizable despite the centuries of development they had experienced and the extraordinary status they had acquired.[26]
The same period included an additional comparison concerning the unusual personality-related abilities appearing among the younger Elderblood generation. During Victor's interaction with the daughters of Roberta and [[Medusa]], the narration distinguished their relationship from the phenomenon associated with his daughter by Eve. The comparison specifically treated an age-related personality manifestation as part of the relevant daughter's abilities rather than automatically identifying every unusual personality phenomenon as an independently existing being. The distinction is important when considering Alter Eve, whose existence had explicitly developed into an independent negative counterpart following Eve's Dragonization.[26][1]
Eve also remained present during the later conflicts and investigations involving the Emperor and the members of his household. During one public confrontation, she was among the numerous wives observing the events through projections, while subsequent family conversations continued identifying her as one of the more reserved women whose demeanor contrasted with the extreme personalities of some of Victor's other companions.[27][28]
By this stage, Eve's life had changed beyond anything imaginable during her Human childhood. The girl who had been imprisoned without a personal name had become one of the earliest members of Victor's lineage, a powerful Dragon Goddess, an established Battle Maid, one of the Emperor's wives and the mother of a member of the next Elderblood generation. Her unusual abilities continued reflecting the circumstances of her origin, but her position within the family was no longer defined exclusively by her suffering or the need for another person to protect her.[5][1][3]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is the most important individual in Eve's life and occupies several distinct positions within her personal history. He originally became her rescuer after discovering her imprisoned inside a cage in [[New York]], recognizing the abuse she had experienced and providing the opportunity to leave her former life. He subsequently became her Vampire Progenitor and adoptive Father, personally naming her Eve Alucard and incorporating her into the newly founded Clan. These events established a bond that Eve regarded as far more significant than an ordinary relationship between Master and servant.[4][5]
Eve's attachment to Victor developed with extraordinary intensity because he was the first person she could remember treating her as someone whose desires and feelings possessed value. She viewed him as her protector, creator and the foundation of her new identity, openly referring to him as Father despite also addressing him as Master. During her first major demonstration of Black Fire, Eve explained to Alter Eve that Victor occupied virtually every important role in her life, making his continued presence her greatest source of emotional security.[6]
Victor reciprocated her attachment through behavior that was particularly paternal during the early stages of their relationship. He protected Eve, ensured that she received proper care after her transformation and repeatedly encouraged her to develop an independent identity. When he noticed her attempting to imitate Kaguya, Victor explained that she should become the kind of maid she personally wished to be rather than copying another individual's accomplishments. His concern reflected the recognition that Eve had spent her entire Human childhood without the freedom to determine her own life.[5][12]
Their relationship became especially important during the formal integration of the Battle Maids into Clan Alucard. Eve was already an official member, but Victor nevertheless addressed her emotional insecurity directly. He reminded her that she could always rely upon him and the other members of his family, including her fellow maids, and asked her to express what she truly wanted. Eve admitted that her greatest desire was to remain beside him permanently and that she disliked being separated from him whenever he traveled without her.[10]
Victor accepted her desire while emphasizing that her decision needed to belong to her. Eve subsequently recognized that the household Victor had established was more than a place where she performed duties; it was the first environment in which she possessed a family capable of supporting her without reducing her identity to obedience or fear.[10]
After centuries of supernatural development, their relationship eventually acquired an additional marital dimension, and Eve became one of Victor's wives. The later relationship did not erase the historical significance of Victor's original paternal role or the circumstances through which he had incorporated her into Clan Alucard. Their marriage eventually produced [[Evelyn Elderblood]], making Eve one of the mothers of Victor's next generation.[3][2]

#### Alter Eve
Alter Eve is Eve's oldest companion and one of the most unusual aspects of her supernatural existence. Eve originally perceived Alter as an imaginary friend who remained with her during her prolonged imprisonment, providing companionship when she had virtually no meaningful interaction with other people. Alter's existence subsequently demonstrated supernatural characteristics after Eve's transformation into a Vampire, confirming that she was more than an ordinary imagined presence.[6]
Their personalities are sharply contrasting. Eve tends to remain reserved, cautious and self-critical, while Alter Eve is aggressive, expressive, impulsive and far more willing to pursue personal enjoyment. Their disagreements frequently involve Eve's reluctance to trust others or her tendency to blame herself for the circumstances of her past, with Alter Eve opposing the idea that Eve was responsible for the abuse inflicted upon her.[6]
Despite these conflicts, Alter Eve possesses considerable affection for Eve and appears genuinely concerned with her happiness. She repeatedly encourages Eve to take advantage of the freedom [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] has given them, criticizing the tendency to treat service as an excuse for suppressing every individual desire. Their relationship therefore combines internal conflict with mutual dependence, making Alter Eve both a supernatural ability and an important emotional companion.[6]
After Eve became a Blood Dragon, Alter Eve acquired the ability to leave her body and operate as an independent negative counterpart. The development expanded their relationship beyond the earlier circumstances in which they communicated internally or alternated control of a shared body. Alter Eve nevertheless remained connected to Eve's existence, and their abilities continued developing through the same Draconic foundation.[1]
The later distinction between Alter Eve and other personality-related manifestations within the Elderblood family is important. Not every secondary personality described in the novel is necessarily a separate Soul or independent being, and the circumstances surrounding Eve's counterpart should not automatically be applied to unrelated cases merely because they display superficial similarities.[1][26]

#### Biological Parents
Eve's biological parents subjected her to prolonged physical and psychological abuse, refusing to provide her with a personal name and referring to their daughter by the degrading designation "Dog". They confined her inside a cage, denied her sufficient nourishment and prevented her from developing ordinary social relationships throughout much of her childhood. By the age of eighteen, the consequences had become severe enough that her body remained physically underdeveloped and covered with injuries and scars.[8][4]
Their treatment profoundly affected Eve's personality and shaped the circumstances through which Alter Eve developed. Eve learned to distrust unfamiliar people, suppress her desires and expect punishment rather than kindness, while Alter Eve became the only companion she believed had remained with her during the years of isolation.[6]
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] discovered Eve and offered her the opportunity to determine their fate, she chose death. Victor accepted the decision and killed her parents through his Fire, permanently ending their control over her life. The flames subsequently became an important emotional influence upon the supernatural power Eve awakened after becoming a Vampire, although their corruptive characteristics were shaped by the trauma associated with her upbringing rather than solely by the deaths of her parents.[4][7]

#### Maid Squad
The [[Maid Squad]] became Eve's first stable social group after leaving her Human captivity. Kaguya served as its Head Maid and principal instructor, while Bruna joined [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household shortly before Eve herself. Maria and Roberta subsequently became part of the group, creating a collection of women whose different supernatural abilities and personal histories gradually developed into an elite force directly serving Victor.[9][10]
Although Eve initially regarded the other maids primarily through the obligations of service, their shared experiences gradually established relationships extending beyond ordinary professional duties. They trained together, fought dangerous supernatural enemies and repeatedly supported Victor during major conflicts. Their different personalities also provided Eve with opportunities to experience ordinary companionship, teasing and disagreement without the fear that characterized her interactions with her biological parents.[10]
Victor eventually formalized the group's position within his family, explaining that the maids had become people he valued in their own right rather than servants whose responsibilities could be terminated through ordinary arrangements. Eve was already an Alucard, but he emphasized that she could rely upon her fellow maids as sisters and that their support would remain available even when he was absent.[10]

#### Kaguya Elderblood Blank
[[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]] is the Head Maid of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household and the most experienced member of the original [[Maid Squad]]. She possessed centuries of experience as an assassin and servant before Eve joined the household, making her the principal example of the competence Victor expected from his personal attendants.[9]
Eve initially admired Kaguya's skills and attempted to imitate her behavior, believing that becoming more like the Head Maid would improve her ability to serve Victor. Victor recognized that the effort risked suppressing Eve's developing individuality and instructed her to establish her own style rather than copying Kaguya's identity.[12]
Their relationship gradually developed into that of trusted companions within the same family. Kaguya continued providing professional guidance while Eve developed her own abilities, eventually becoming a powerful Battle Maid and Dragon Goddess. Even during the Imperial era, the contrast between Kaguya's perfectionism and the personalities of the other original maids remained a source of familiar household interactions.[10][26]

#### Bruna Elderblood
[[Bruna Elderblood]] joined [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household shortly before Eve and became the second personal maid within the developing [[Maid Squad]]. Both women had originally been ordinary Humans whose circumstances changed after Victor rescued and transformed them, but the experiences shaping their personalities were considerably different.[29][5]
Bruna had possessed an established personal identity and religious Faith before being betrayed by the institution she trusted, whereas Eve had been denied a normal childhood and even a personal name. Their shared transformation nevertheless created an important connection, allowing them to develop together under Kaguya's instruction and gradually adjust to the responsibilities of serving Victor.[9]
Bruna's extraordinary religious devotion also influenced Eve. After Victor rescued Bruna, the Faith she had previously directed toward the God she worshipped gradually became centered upon Victor himself. That devotion subsequently influenced Maria, Eve and Roberta, contributing to the formation of the small circle of believers from which the [[Blood God Religion]] eventually developed.[11]

#### Maria Elderblood
[[Maria Elderblood]] became one of Eve's closest companions through their shared service within the [[Maid Squad]]. Although Maria possessed a considerably more outspoken and irreverent personality, the two gradually developed familiarity through training, combat and their shared attachment to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[10]
Maria's behavior frequently contrasted with Eve's reserve, particularly during conversations involving the personal relationships among the maids. Eve could become embarrassed or irritated by Maria's teasing, but these exchanges also demonstrated that she had become comfortable enough within the group to participate in ordinary disagreements rather than remaining permanently withdrawn.[7]
Their relationship remained important during the development of the [[Blood God Religion]], when both women participated in discussions concerning the institution's organization and public identity. Maria immediately understood the psychological implications of Eve's observations about the Acolytes' clothing, allowing the two to develop a shared assessment of how the religion should present itself to ordinary Humans.[11]

#### Roberta Elderblood
[[Roberta Elderblood]] became part of the [[Maid Squad]] after Eve had already joined [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household. Her energetic and unconventional personality provided another contrast with Eve's reserved behavior, while the existence of [[Medusa]] created an unusual similarity between the two women.[10]
During the formal integration of the maids into Clan Alucard, Eve revealed Alter Eve's existence to her companions and compared the phenomenon with the unusual internal presence associated with Roberta. The comparison initially caused the maids to wonder whether Eve possessed something similar to a Heroic Spirit, although the subsequent development of Alter Eve established that the two situations should not automatically be treated as identical.[10][1]
Eve and Roberta continued serving together after their transformations into [[Blood Dragons]], with each retaining highly individualized supernatural abilities. Their relationship also formed part of the broader sisterhood that Victor emphasized when encouraging the maids to rely upon one another rather than depending exclusively upon his presence.[10]

#### Evelyn Elderblood
[[Evelyn Elderblood]] is the biological daughter of Eve and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], born after the family acquired access to extensive periods of accelerated development. She belongs to the younger generation of [[Blood Dragons]] whose inherited supernatural characteristics were shaped by the interaction between Victor's existence and the powers possessed by their mothers.[23][3]
Evelyn possesses an expressive and imaginative personality, demonstrated during her early appearance alongside several of her sisters. She enthusiastically role-played as the Herald of Darkness, challenging an imaginary champion of Light while participating in games with Nikolina and the other children.[3]
Her birth represented a profound change in Eve's personal circumstances. The woman whose biological parents had refused to recognize her individuality eventually became the mother of a child raised within an enormous family whose members possessed extraordinary power and affection for one another. Eve's relationship with Evelyn consequently forms an important part of her development beyond the circumstances that originally brought her into Victor's household.[5][3]

### Abilities

#### Skills and Abilities
Eve Elderblood is a True Blood Dragon, Dragon Goddess, and one of [[Victor Elderblood]]'s original Battle Maids. As the first individual formally incorporated into Clan Alucard through Victor's Blood, Eve originally possessed the physical and supernatural characteristics of a Noble Vampire directly descended from a Vampire Progenitor. Following Victor's ascension as the Dragon God of [[Chaos]], she underwent a fundamental racial transformation into a genuine Blood Dragon, acquiring the superior characteristics of True Dragon physiology while retaining and strengthening the abilities she had developed as a Vampire.[1][2]
Eve's individual supernatural characteristics are distinguished primarily by two abilities: Alter Eve and Mutant Black Fire. Although both are closely associated with her personal history, they originated through different mechanisms. Alter Eve was already connected to Eve during her Human childhood, originally appearing to her as an imaginary companion who remained beside her throughout her imprisonment. After Eve became a Vampire, this companion demonstrated genuine supernatural properties, including invisibility to powerful supernatural observers, independent interaction with the environment and the ability to physically attack enemies while Eve herself remained stationary.[1]
Eve's Black Fire, by contrast, was inherited through Victor's Progenitor Blood. As the founder of Clan Alucard, Victor possessed several powerful elemental characteristics, including Fire inherited from the [[Snow Clan]]. Eve acquired this characteristic following her transformation, but the power manifested in an unusual form influenced by the emotional experiences of her Human life. Victor's Fire had represented liberation from her abusive parents, while the accumulated trauma of her childhood corrupted the inherited element into sinister black flames possessing unusually destructive characteristics. The ability subsequently became known as Mutant Black Fire.[3]
Eve's Fire was already exceptionally destructive during her early years as a Vampire. She demonstrated the technique Supernova, taught to her by Victor, by concentrating the flames into a beam that pursued a fleeing Demonic Beast before detonating. The explosion destroyed the target, while [[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]] estimated that the surrounding forest could have been obliterated had the detonation occurred closer to their position. Eve later demonstrated the ability to engulf large battlefield areas in black flames during the Demonic War and burn Greek Gods during the assault upon [[Olympus]].[1][4][5]

Alter Eve is one of Eve's two defining supernatural abilities and the only one explicitly associated with her existence before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] transformed her into a Vampire. During the years Eve spent imprisoned by her biological parents, she regarded Alter Eve as an imaginary companion who remained beside her when no one else offered comfort. Following her transformation into a Vampire, this presence demonstrated genuine supernatural capabilities rather than functioning solely as an internal psychological experience.[1]
Alter Eve resembles Eve almost perfectly but possesses white hair and sapphire-blue eyes, preserving the color of Eve's eyes before her Vampire transformation. Her personality is considerably more aggressive, vulgar, confident and expressive than Eve's reserved demeanor. The two can communicate internally, argue with one another and coordinate their actions, while Alter Eve possesses sufficient independence to act without Eve consciously performing the same movements.[1]
During a battle against Demonic Beasts, Sasha observed Eve apparently standing motionless while the bodies of nearby monsters were torn apart. Although Sasha understood that something invisible was attacking them, she could neither see nor properly sense the entity responsible. The experience demonstrated that Alter Eve possessed genuine offensive capabilities and an unusual form of concealment capable of deceiving the supernatural senses of a powerful Noble Vampire.[1]
Eve subsequently approached Alter Eve and merged with her, demonstrating that the two were not permanently separated during Eve's original Vampire state. Their interaction also revealed that Alter Eve possessed her own thoughts and emotional reactions, frequently encouraging Eve to enjoy the freedom she had acquired rather than continuing to define herself through her former captivity.[1]

#### Mutant Black Fire
Mutant Black Fire, also referred to as Eve's Black Fire or Mutant Fire, is the elemental ability Eve inherited from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Progenitor Blood. The power originated from the Fire characteristic of Victor's lineage, but underwent an unusual mutation due to the experiences Eve had accumulated during her Human life.[1][3]
When Victor rescued Eve, the Fire he used to destroy her abusive parents became emotionally associated with her freedom from captivity. Eve remembered the warmth and beauty of the flames, and these experiences influenced the power that emerged after she became a Vampire. However, her long history of abuse had produced considerable psychological trauma, causing the inherited element to develop an abnormal and corrupted manifestation rather than reproducing Victor's Fire in its ordinary form.[3]
During her first major demonstration, Eve released a gigantic pillar of black flames from her body. The unusual color surprised both Sasha and Eleanor because they recognized the elemental characteristic inherited from Victor while observing a manifestation significantly different from the ordinary [[Snow Clan]] Fire. Eve believed the flames reflected her own perceived impurity, while Alter Eve recognized that the power's appearance did not make Eve responsible for the suffering she had endured.[1]

##### Known Racial Drawbacks
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explained that [[Blood Dragons]] are not a race possessing only the advantages of their component species. Although the robustness of the Dragon body removes many conventional Vampiric weaknesses, the combination of Dragon and Vampire instincts can intensify the emotional characteristics of the individual.[6]
- Amplified Emotions: Blood Dragons inherit the emotional amplification associated with Vampires and Dragons. Victor explained that the combination can intensify existing feelings far beyond ordinary Human experience.[6]
- Amplified Desires: The race possesses powerful instincts and considerably reduced inhibitions compared with ordinary Humans, making self-control especially important during early development.[6]
- Self-Control Requirements: Victor recognized that many members of his family would require considerable training to manage the instincts created or amplified by their new racial characteristics.[6]
- Psychological Stabilization: Eve's transformation into a True Dragon was specifically accompanied by an improvement in her mental stability, although this did not eliminate Alter Eve.[2]

##### Battle Armor
Eve possesses combat equipment appropriate to her position within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s [[Maid Squad]]. During the assault upon [[Olympus]], Eve and the other maids appeared wearing armor and carrying weapons designed for supernatural combat while preserving the visual characteristics associated with their maid uniforms.[13]
- Type: Battle Armor
- User: Eve Elderblood
- Affiliation: Maid Squad
- Combat Function: Provides equipment appropriate for combat operations involving powerful supernatural enemies.[13]
- Design: Maintains elements of the Maid Squad's characteristic clothing while serving as combat equipment.[13]

##### Victor's Blood-Infused Weapons
Before the assault upon [[Olympus]], [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] provided the [[Maid Squad]] with weapons coated or empowered with his Blood. The enhancement allowed the maids to threaten the permanent existence of hostile Gods whose Divine characteristics would otherwise make ordinary physical destruction insufficient.[13]
- Type: Blood-Infused Weapons
- Provider: Victor Elderblood
- Users: Members of the Maid Squad, including Eve
- Supernatural Enhancement: The weapons were empowered through Victor's Blood.[13]
- God-Slaying Capability: The enhancement was intended to allow the maids to permanently kill Divine enemies rather than merely destroy their temporary physical bodies.[13]
