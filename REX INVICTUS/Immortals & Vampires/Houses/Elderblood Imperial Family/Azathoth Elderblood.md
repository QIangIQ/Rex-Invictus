Everything we touch is distorted by our inherent characteristics, we are Eldritch [[Chaos]].

Azathoth Elderblood is an Outer God classified as an Eldritch Chaos, the wife and cosmic counterpart of Victor Elderblood, mother of [[Yog-Sothoth]] Elderblood, co-creator of [[Abhoth]], and the foundational matriarch of the [[Eldritch Pantheon]] connected to the [[Elderblood Empire]]. She is fundamentally different from the native [[Regulators of Existence]]: rather than originating as part of the cosmological machinery established by the current Universe's [[Primordial Chaos]], Azathoth entered the Creation as a foreign existence whose Essence, logic and development did not naturally belong to its Laws.[7][2][12]
Azathoth originally possessed neither a conventional name nor a humanoid identity. She emerged from foreign Chaos with little understanding of herself and explored existence primarily through curiosity, observation and consumption. By devouring powerful beings and absorbing their Energy, memories and accumulated knowledge, she developed intelligence and self-awareness while her physical existence expanded to astronomical proportions. Her growth eventually attracted the direct attention of the native cosmic administrators, whose inability to permanently eliminate the foreign Chaos led them to imprison her instead.[7][2]
The imprisonment prevented Azathoth's true body from freely moving through Creation but failed to completely isolate her. She scattered fragments of her Essence throughout the Cosmos, disguising their Eldritch foundation beneath powers acquired from beings she had previously consumed. Through these fragments she continued observing civilizations, collecting information and searching for a way beyond her confinement. One of these extensions eventually discovered Victor. Beneath his native Divine, Vampiric and Draconic existence, Azathoth recognized something she had never encountered before: another developing Eldritch Chaos.[7][6]
Finding Victor transformed Azathoth from a largely solitary intelligence driven by self-understanding into an individual capable of companionship, love, possessiveness and family attachment. She deliberately constructed a female humanoid identity modeled after Victor, chose the name Azathoth, imagined an Eldritch Pantheon created by the two of them and eventually combined a fragment of her own Essence with a minute amount of Victor's to produce Yog-Sothoth Elderblood.[6][13]
Although her true body remains imprisoned, Azathoth has become fully integrated into Victor's family and civilization through manifestations and her independently active consciousness. She is one of Victor's principal sources of information concerning [[Outer Gods]], foreign Cosmologies, Primordial Beings and the deeper architecture of Reality. By the late Imperial era she is no longer presented merely as the mysterious prisoner Victor intends to rescue: she participates in Imperial strategic planning, assists with Eldritch creations and defenses, and is explicitly described as the only person who truly equals Victor in both power and Authority.[14][8][3]

### Physical Appearance
Azathoth's natural existence cannot be accurately reduced to the anatomy of an ordinary biological species. Her original body developed from a concentration of foreign [[Chaos]] and Eldritch Essence whose scale increased as she accumulated Energy. By the period preceding her imprisonment, her body had already expanded across astronomical scales, and the surrounding Space and Reality distorted around her presence rather than behaving as they would around an ordinary creature.[7]

#### Humanoid Manifestation
Azathoth created her humanoid appearance only after discovering [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Seeing that her prospective companion possessed a male humanoid form, she deliberately chose the opposite sex and constructed herself as a feminine counterpart to him. The resulting manifestation is an extraordinarily beautiful pale-skinned woman with long black hair and facial characteristics strongly resembling Victor's. The novel describes her eyes as black, although several official illustrations depict them as blue.[6]
Her chosen sex is part of this constructed identity rather than a biological characteristic inherited from her original existence. Before developing an individual self, Azathoth had no reason to define herself according to ordinary concepts of male or female. Becoming a woman was one of several decisions—alongside choosing her name and identifying her personal desires—through which she transformed an instinctive cosmic existence into the individual known as Azathoth.[6]
Azathoth attempted to reproduce Victor's appearance closely, including his eyes, but was unable to completely alter the intrinsic characteristics expressed through her own existence. Her eyes therefore remained black in the novel's description. The resemblance between the two is intentional rather than coincidental: Azathoth wanted a body that visibly expressed her relationship with the first being she considered a genuine counterpart.[6]
Her humanoid manifestation does not completely suppress the nature underneath it. Strong emotion can cause her expression to distort into something visibly inhuman, particularly when excitement, anger or Eldritch instinct overwhelms the civilized presentation she normally maintains. When Victor identifies a viable method of reaching her imprisoned body in Chapter 1176, Azathoth's excitement causes her eyes to glow and her smile to become visibly distorted in a manner characteristic of the family's cosmic horrors.[4]

#### True Eldritch Form
Azathoth's true form is an enormous Eldritch existence fundamentally composed around foreign [[Chaos]] rather than conventional flesh. Its exact dimensions are not fixed in the manner of a normal organism because her physical development is connected to the quantity of Energy accumulated by the Eldritch Chaos. During her earliest growth she became progressively larger through consumption until planets, stars and other celestial structures ceased to represent the enormous scale differences they would to ordinary life.[7][2]
Her unrestricted nature also interacts abnormally with the surrounding environment. Azathoth is foreign to the native Laws through which the local Creation organizes ordinary existence, causing Reality itself to react to her presence. This is one reason her humanoid manifestation is important: it permits ordinary interaction with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family without continuously imposing the full informational and existential weight of her true nature upon comparatively smaller beings.[7][2]
Even when she is not deliberately hostile, traces of that nature can affect those around her. During the confrontation surrounding [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]] in Chapter 1175, Azathoth's irritation alone began degrading the mental stability of nearby Dragon Goddesses until [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] used Order to suppress the effect.[15]

### Personality
The oldest identifiable element of Azathoth's personality is curiosity. Before possessing a name, family, social morality or developed individual identity, she moved through existence because she wanted to know what she was and understand what surrounded her. Consumption initially served this objective as much as physical growth. Devouring beings gave her access to their Energy, but their memories and accumulated information were equally valuable because they allowed an entity with almost no initial cultural framework to understand civilizations, Gods and the larger Cosmos.[7]
Her emotional vocabulary developed through experience. Unfamiliar phenomena produced fascination; obstacles taught frustration; repeated attempts by the Regulators to suppress her produced anger; imprisonment developed into hatred. The discovery of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] produced a much more significant change. Azathoth had existed for an immeasurable span without finding another being she regarded as genuinely similar to herself, and recognizing another Eldritch [[Chaos]] transformed curiosity into intense fixation. She wanted to understand Victor, reach him, combine their Essences and create an entire family of beings possessing their nature.[6]
Azathoth's attachment to Victor is intensely possessive, but she is not blindly submissive to him. Their similarity frequently causes them to arrive at the same conclusions independently, especially regarding growth, Authority, family and the unreliability of cosmic institutions. At the same time, Azathoth can be more patient when Victor's protective instincts push him toward immediate confrontation. She has spent an extraordinary period imprisoned and understands that attacking a superior cosmological system before accumulating the necessary information and Authority could simply replace one problem with a worse one.[12][16][4]
Her morality is strongly centered upon the Elderblood family rather than abstract loyalty toward the native Universe. Azathoth does not defend Creation because she considers its existing order sacred. When discussing the consequences of destroying Primordial Beings, her concern is that recklessly eliminating the universal functions they administer could destroy the environment in which Victor and her family live. The Universe therefore matters because her loved ones exist within it, not because she feels obligated to preserve the authority of its administrators.[12]
Integration into Victor's family has also revealed a playful and unexpectedly ordinary side of Azathoth. She participates in household teasing, speaks casually with Victor's other wives and can become annoyed over subjects as mundane as jokes about her age. In Chapter 1157, the women jokingly salute her as the oldest woman in the Universe after establishing that she existed before its creation; Azathoth responds with visible irritation while simultaneously dismissing ordinary discussions of chronological age as largely meaningless for beings like them.[1]
Azathoth also displays genuine maternal affection toward the beings produced from her and Victor. Yog was created partly as a possible means of escape, but Azathoth nevertheless names and recognizes her as their daughter rather than treating her as an impersonal mechanism. When she and Victor later create [[Abhoth]], Azathoth immediately greets the malformed newborn affectionately, strokes him and objects when Ruby characterizes him according to the malevolent mythology associated with his name, insisting that he had only just been born and was not inherently evil.[6][8]

### Nature and Classification

#### Outer God
Azathoth is an Outer God, but this classification should not be simplified to mean merely "a powerful being born outside the Universe." [[Outer Gods]] are Eldritch existences whose fundamental nature does not conform normally to the native cosmological framework in which they are operating. Azathoth is the clearest foreign example because she originated beyond [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s native Creation, whereas Victor eventually became an Outer God despite having originally been born within that Creation.[2][12]
Consequently, Azathoth should not be classified as one of the native [[Regulators of Existence]]. Infinity, [[True Death]], the Universal Tree, the Judges of the Abyss and the Owner of Limbo occupy functions belonging to the administration of the local Universe. Azathoth entered that system from elsewhere. Her conflict with them arose precisely because she was an existence the native order had not produced and could not process in the same manner as its ordinary Gods and inhabitants.[7][12]

#### Eldritch Chaos
Within the Eldritch hierarchy, Azathoth is specifically an Eldritch [[Chaos]]. Chapter 1015 distinguishes this position from simply being an Eldritch God. Azathoth identifies herself as Chaos incarnate and the creator of a Pantheon, then recognizes [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] as another developing existence of the same fundamental class. An Eldritch Chaos functions as a foundational source from which an Eldritch Divine system and its descendants can develop.[2]
The term is especially important because Azathoth's relationship with Chaos is not equivalent to an ordinary native God receiving a Divine Concept called Chaos. Her existence itself is organized around a foreign Chaos framework. This allows the Pantheon emerging from Azathoth and Victor to possess an origin distinct from the native Divine hierarchy even when individual members simultaneously possess connections to Victor's original Creation.[2]
Azathoth is still not equivalent to the native [[Primordial Chaos]] from which Victor's Universe originated. Chapter 1171 describes her as the Primordial Chaos of the [[Eldritch Pantheon]] because she occupies the foundational position of that Pantheon and is described as its creator of cosmic horrors. The phrase identifies her position inside the Eldritch family hierarchy; it does not establish that she is the native Primal Chaos, an incarnation of it, or its biological daughter.[3]

#### The Eldritch Pantheon
The developing [[Eldritch Pantheon]] is centered upon foundational [[Chaos]] existences such as Azathoth and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Their descendants can develop as Eldritch Gods or higher Eldritch Divine beings whose powers are sourced through a framework distinct from ordinary native Divinity. [[Yog-Sothoth]] Elderblood is the most direct example because she was literally produced from the combined Essences of Azathoth and Victor.[6][2]
Victor's later daughters demonstrate that this foundation is not limited to children directly created with Azathoth. [[Nero Elderblood]]'s developing Apocalypse acquired Eldritch characteristics through Victor, while [[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]] and later [[Akasha Elderblood]] demonstrate additional ways Divine or Conceptual development can become connected to Victor's Eldritch foundation instead of relying entirely upon the native Divine hierarchy.[2]
By Chapter 1171, Azathoth's position is described in the strongest terms used for any member of Victor's Pantheon other than Victor himself. [[Scathach]] internally concludes that even Victor's extraordinarily talented daughters are unlikely ever to become another Victor and identifies Azathoth as the only existence who truly equals him in both power and Authority, specifically because she is Azathoth—the [[Primordial Chaos]] of their Eldritch Pantheon and creator of its cosmic horrors.[3]

#### Relationship with the Native Cosmology
Azathoth's existence provides direct evidence that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Universe is not the totality of Creation. She originated in another cosmic framework and later entered the one governed by the Regulators known throughout most of the story. Chapter 1075 establishes that separate Cosmologies can possess their own Laws, Primordials and forms of [[Chaos]] rather than every Reality being administered by one universal hierarchy.[12]
This distinction changes the meaning of "absolute" supernatural Laws. A Rule can be fundamental to one Creation without necessarily governing something whose origin precedes or exists outside that Creation's local structure. Azathoth's entire history is an example of the resulting conflict: the native administrators possess overwhelming Authority over the Universe they govern, while Azathoth carries an existence that did not originate from the system granting that Authority.[7][12]

### Synopsis

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Long before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] learned that she existed, Azathoth emerged from a foreign tangle of [[Chaos]]. She initially possessed little recognizable individuality beyond the impulse to understand herself and the Cosmos. Her Essence spread across immense distances while she observed unfamiliar phenomena and searched for information capable of explaining her own existence.[7]
Her earliest encounters with other life demonstrated how incompatible her nature could be with conventional beings. Civilizations encountered fragments or manifestations they did not properly understand, and attempts to interact with the foreign Essence could lead to corruption and mental instability. Azathoth herself initially approached these events analytically rather than maliciously. She was discovering the consequences of being something that followed a different existential logic from the beings around her.[7]
Azathoth eventually encountered an extraordinarily advanced celestial civilization populated by powerful beings. She consumed immense quantities of its Energy and numerous inhabitants, acquiring their memories alongside their supernatural characteristics. This accelerated both sides of her development: her physical existence expanded dramatically from the Energy while the memories provided knowledge she had previously lacked about civilization, Gods and the structure of Reality.[7]
Continuous growth made concealment impossible. The native [[Regulators of Existence]] eventually confronted the foreign intruder. The opposition included entities associated with fundamental systems such as [[The Universal Tree]], Infinity, [[True Death]] and Limbo. To Azathoth, these beings were not simply unusually powerful Gods; they possessed Authority embedded into the structure of the surrounding Creation itself.[7]
The Regulators likewise discovered that Azathoth could not be treated like an ordinary native entity. Their Authorities could restrict and counter her, but the foreign Chaos did not fit cleanly inside the mechanisms through which native life and death were administered. Chapter 1015 later explains the central problem directly: the Primordials had not spared Azathoth out of mercy; they had been unable to simply kill the Chaos existence confronting them.[7][2]
The solution was imprisonment. Rather than continue attempting to erase an entity whose foundation resisted their ordinary methods, the Regulators combined their advantages to confine Azathoth inside a space capable of suppressing her growth and movement. Attempts to enlarge herself, distort the prison or force a conventional escape were prevented by the restrictions surrounding the confinement.[7]
Azathoth responded by abandoning the idea that her main body needed to personally travel. She divided portions of her influence and distributed them throughout Creation, hiding the genuine Eldritch component beneath layers of Energy and powers accumulated from other beings. These fragments became remote observers and feeding points through which she could examine distant regions despite the imprisonment of her true form.[7]
One such fragment eventually allowed Azathoth to study civilizations inhabiting the comparatively lower portions of Creation. Their raw scale initially appeared unimpressive beside entities she had previously encountered, yet Earth and the worlds connected to it contained an unusual concentration of supernatural systems. Vampires, [[Werewolves]], Witches, Youkai, [[World Trees]] and Gods all expressed different relationships with Blood, Nature, Souls, [[Magic]] and Divine Concepts. The apparent backwater consequently became far more interesting than its position suggested.[6]
Her attention became completely focused upon the region when she discovered Victor Elderblood. Beneath the visible Blood Dragon and God stood something Azathoth recognized instinctively: an existence developing according to the same Eldritch principle as herself. After an immeasurable period of isolation, Azathoth believed she had finally encountered a genuine companion of her own kind.[6]
That discovery forced Azathoth to answer questions she had never needed to consider. She wanted a name because Victor possessed one. She wanted an individual appearance capable of standing beside him. She chose to become female because Victor was male and designed the manifestation as his counterpart. Most importantly, she recognized that she wanted Victor himself, wanted their Essences united and wanted the two of them to create a Pantheon together. The process of clearly defining those desires substantially increased her self-understanding.[6]
Her imprisonment immediately became more intolerable because she now possessed somewhere she actively wanted to go. Unable to personally reach Victor, Azathoth used an external connection that obtained a tiny quantity of his Essence. Instead of consuming it, she combined it with a portion of her own existence. The compatibility between their Essences allowed the creation of an independent child whom Azathoth named [[Yog-Sothoth]] Elderblood.[6]
Yog was sent toward Victor as their first daughter and as Azathoth's hope of eventually bridging the prison separating the two Eldritch Chaos beings. When Yog reached Victor, she identified both of her parents and explained the connection. Victor initially had almost no context for the absurd situation but quickly accepted the essential fact: Yog was his daughter. Whatever cosmic objective had been attached to her birth, he refused to treat her merely as an escape mechanism for her mother.[13]
The rest of the Elderblood household reached the same conclusion. Yog's existence was abnormal enough to interfere with Reality around her, and her future potential was obviously enormous, but the adults rejected the idea of immediately turning a newly born child into the solution for problems that had existed before her birth. Azathoth's hoped-for Eldritch family had therefore begun to exist, but under Victor it developed as an actual family rather than merely a hierarchy of cosmic weapons.[13][17]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Azathoth's role expanded substantially after Yog established a stable connection between her and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family. Her true body remained inaccessible, yet physical imprisonment became progressively less effective at isolating her socially. By Chapter 1000, Azathoth was capable of inserting a representation of herself into the private communication network used by Victor's wives, effectively becoming part of the household despite the impossible distance separating her body from them.[18]
The development of [[Nero Elderblood]] and [[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]] soon made Azathoth's knowledge essential. Victor's daughters were beginning to manifest characteristics that did not fit ordinary native Divinity, particularly when [[Nero]]'s developing Apocalypse responded to the Eldritch portion of Victor rather than functioning exactly like the conventional Concepts used by established Pantheons.[2]
Azathoth explained that Victor had become an Eldritch [[Chaos]]. He was not simply a Dragon God who had acquired an unusual secondary Energy source. His deeper nature was developing into the same broad category of existence represented by Azathoth herself. The distinction was complicated by Victor's origin: unlike Azathoth, who entered the Cosmology from elsewhere, Victor had been born inside the native Creation and simultaneously remained a high-level God recognized by it.[2]
She further explained that an Eldritch Chaos develops differently from conventional Gods. Energy, Divinities and other supernatural properties can be consumed and incorporated into its growth, while sufficient maturation can eventually produce an existence too great for an ordinary Reality to contain normally. This explained why native Primordials regarded entities like Azathoth as more than another hostile Pantheon: uncontrolled Eldritch development could eventually become incompatible with the structure intended to contain it.[2]
Azathoth's history also became a deterrent Victor could exploit. During his dealings with [[The Judges of The Abyss]], the mere identification of the ancient imprisoned Outer God produced a reaction very different from the dismissiveness normally directed toward younger Gods. The entities involved remembered that dealing with Azathoth had once required the combined attention of the native cosmic order.[19]
Victor subsequently questioned Azathoth directly about the nature of the Primordials. She corrected the assumption that they could be approached as merely exceptionally powerful Gods. A genuine Primordial is connected to an Aspect required for the Universe to function. Eliminating the complete existence of Life would not merely kill a Goddess called Life—it could erase or catastrophically damage Life as a universal function. The same logic applied to principles such as Soul, Death, Infinity and Balance.[12]
The critical distinction was between the Aspect and the consciousness administering it. Azathoth explained that destroying a hostile administrator did not necessarily require erasing the universal principle connected to that administrator. If the individual ego could be attacked while the underlying function remained intact, then a sufficiently prepared successor could potentially seize or replace the vacant Authority instead of destroying a necessary component of Reality.[12]
This fundamentally changed Victor's approach toward the Regulators. The objective no longer needed to be "become stronger and kill them." He and Azathoth instead began discussing the structure of their Authority, the conditions under which a cosmic office could change ownership and the means by which Victor could accumulate enough administrative control that the native hierarchy could no longer threaten his family.[12][16]
Their conversations also demonstrated how similar their thought processes had become. Azathoth could begin an explanation and Victor would follow the logic to essentially the same conclusion, or Victor would propose a direction that Azathoth had already considered. Their shared Eldritch foundation did not make their personalities identical, but it produced an unusually compatible approach toward growth and cosmic problems: understand the system, find what makes it work, acquire the necessary Authority and then stop treating the apparent limitation as absolute.[16]
Despite sharing Victor's ambition, Azathoth retained greater patience concerning her imprisonment. She had already survived confinement for an extraordinary period and did not see value in provoking every Primordial simply because Victor had become powerful enough to threaten some of them. Freedom mattered, but a failed rescue that exposed the family before they possessed the necessary preparation would accomplish nothing.[16]
Azathoth also remained one of Victor's benchmarks for genuine Eldritch knowledge. By Chapter 1092, when the Empire's increasingly ambitious Runic projects required understanding languages and principles beyond normal magical inscription, Azathoth remained among the relevant standards for advanced Eldritch Rune knowledge.[11]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
The enormous Imperial development preceding conflict with the Upper Sectors transformed Azathoth from a distant advisor into an increasingly active component of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s civilization. Beginning during the later stages of the Empire's accelerated development, Victor and his family transformed the Imperial galaxy itself into a defensive environment. Eldritch organisms were hidden within asteroids, stellar environments and even black holes, and both Victor and Azathoth participated in enhancing creatures intended to remain dormant until hostile forces entered protected regions.[14]
The project demonstrated the difference between the [[Elderblood Empire]] and the solitary Azathoth who had originally wandered into an unknown Cosmology. Azathoth had once entered a foreign system alone, attracted the attention of its administrators and ultimately been imprisoned. Victor's developing Eldritch civilization now possessed [[World Trees]], Gods, Dragons, technology, armies, afterlife infrastructure and an entire family of high-level beings preparing collectively for contact with stronger cosmic powers.[14]
The two-hundred-thousand-year Imperial development also gave the younger generation extraordinary time to mature. Yog, originally an infant created from two fragments of Essence, developed into one of the most dangerous members of the Elderblood second generation. Her continued growth retained particular significance for Azathoth because Yog's unusual relationship with Unreality and Nothingness offered a possible method of bypassing restrictions that ordinary native powers could not simply overpower.[5]
Chapter 1157 clarified Azathoth's chronological relationship with the current Universe. During a family discussion, [[Jeanne Elderblood]] was mocked for having existed before the Universe fully took shape, only for the others to remember that Azathoth had existed even before its creation. Azathoth herself regarded the comparison as largely meaningless; conventional age is difficult to apply to beings whose histories extend into conditions where ordinary measurements of Time are unreliable.[1]
A major strategic meeting in Chapter 1159 further demonstrated how naturally Azathoth handles information associated with cosmic horrors. Victor had extracted the memories of an Upper-Sector operative after repeatedly overcoming the existential contract designed to destroy forbidden information. Before sharing the memories with his family, he placed Eldritch Runic protections over the others because directly experiencing his unrestricted manifestation and the information surrounding it could damage their minds. Azathoth alone required no such protection.[8]
The gathered information provided the Elderblood leadership with a clearer image of the faction connected to Darkness, including a hierarchy of major Gods whose Divinities overlapped with powers already possessed by Victor's family. Azathoth reacted to the potential enemies in the same predatory manner as Victor and Violet: their Gods represented not only threats but possible sources of useful Divinities if defeated.[8]
During the same meeting, Victor decided that the Empire needed an additional defense against corruption and hostile control. He gathered several forces into his hand and asked Azathoth to assist him. Azathoth placed her hand upon Victor's and amplified the creation with her own power. The result was a malformed newborn Eldritch existence covered with eyes and built around controlled corruption.[8]
Victor named the newborn [[Abhoth]]. Despite the ominous implications associated with that name, Azathoth immediately treated him affectionately as a child of their Pantheon. When Ruby reacted as though they had just created an incarnation of impurity and evil, Azathoth objected that Abhoth had only just been born and had done nothing that made him inherently evil.[8]
Abhoth's creation represents another important stage in Azathoth and Victor's relationship. Yog had been created while the two were separated, using a tiny stolen fragment of Victor's Essence and Azathoth's own Essence. Abhoth was created after the two had developed a functioning partnership, deliberately combining their contributions for a specific Imperial need. Azathoth was no longer simply waiting for Victor to someday reach her; she was already creating and planning beside him through the portions of herself capable of acting beyond the prison.[6][8]
Chapter 1160 explicitly describes Yog as the daughter of two [[Outer Gods]] of the highest rank. The same passage continues to identify Yog as the principal trump card for freeing Azathoth, confirming that despite all of Victor's growth, the unusual nature of the prison remained a problem better solved through the correct Authority than through raw destructive output alone.[5]
The hierarchy of the Elderblood Pantheon increasingly placed Azathoth near its absolute summit. [[Scathach]] remained one of its most dangerous combatants and Victor's strongest conventional warrior, yet assessments during this era consistently placed Victor and Azathoth above her when judging total existential threat. This distinction did not diminish Scathach's martial superiority over many beings with larger reserves; it demonstrated that Azathoth represented a category of cosmic danger not reducible to combat technique alone.[5][20]
Victor's lessons concerning dimensions and existential weight produced another comparison. He constructed a miniature galaxy using high-order Concepts and allowed his daughters to attempt to interfere with its ownership. After ordinary dimensional methods failed, Victor stated that without his cooperation the people most likely capable of simply taking control of such a structure from him were Azathoth and Jeanne.[21]
The following lesson exposed an even larger possibility. Victor's miniature Eldritch galaxy could reproduce astonishingly convincing organisms, but the creatures were ultimately replicas because the artificial system lacked every Conceptual foundation required to establish independent Life. Victor privately acknowledged that directly combining himself with Azathoth could potentially produce a genuine Eldritch galaxy instead of an imitation. He refused to perform the experiment because creating such a structure would amount to broadcasting an enormous metaphysical signal to outside cosmic powers.[22]
Chapter 1171 finally states Azathoth's place beside Victor without relying upon indirect comparisons. While considering how exceptional Victor remained even among his own descendants, Scathach concluded that the only one who truly equals him in power and Authority is Azathoth. The narration connects this directly to Azathoth's identity as the [[Primordial Chaos]] of the [[Eldritch Pantheon]] and creator of its cosmic horrors.[3]
This description should not be confused with the native Primordial Chaos responsible for the current Universe. Azathoth's title concerns the foundational position she occupies inside the Eldritch Pantheon. She is the source-level [[Chaos]] from which that branch of cosmic horrors develops, while the local Primal Chaos is the primordial foundation that divided itself in the process associated with the native Universe's formation.[3]
Azathoth's relevance became immediate when [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]] was born. Moira's connection with Reality was sufficiently abnormal that her birth produced an impulse detectable throughout the Universe and attracted the direct attention of the System. When unfamiliar presences began gathering, Azathoth identified them as the Primordial Beings or entities extremely similar to them.[15]
The family recognized that most of Victor's wives had reached a level where they could dominate ordinary Divine civilizations yet still could not directly fight entities occupying universal administrative positions. Azathoth was treated differently. Violet's assessment was that only Victor or Azathoth could meaningfully act against the Primordial-class opposition itself, while the remaining family members would support them through Energy, preparation and their own specialized roles.[15]
The encounter ultimately brought the System, [[True Death]], Infinity, the Judges of the Abyss, the Owner of Limbo and the Universal Tree into direct confrontation with Victor. The System wanted Moira to assume the vacant archetypal position connected to Destiny because Reality responded to the newborn's desires in a manner unprecedented among beings born after Primordial Chaos. Victor categorically refused to surrender his daughter merely because the cosmological administration found her useful.[15][4]
After the confrontation ended without open war, Victor immediately turned his attention to Azathoth's unresolved imprisonment and asked where her true body was located. Her answer revealed that the circumstances had changed significantly since the original prison described in Chapter 979.[4]
Azathoth's body had been transferred to a dimension outside the Universe. The location barely qualified as an ordinary place and existed beyond the region where the native Creator's Rules applied. Azathoth herself could not accurately determine when the transfer had occurred because Time functioned unreliably or meaninglessly in the dimensionless conditions surrounding the prison. What felt recent to her could correspond to thousands of external years.[4]
The relocation demonstrated that the forces maintaining her imprisonment had not become complacent. Even after an immense period, precautions surrounding Azathoth were actively being changed to reduce the possibility of escape. Victor interpreted this as another problem to solve rather than a permanent limit.[4]
Azathoth also clarified that the prison restrained her body much more successfully than it restrained her consciousness. Her consciousness could break free and remain with her family whenever necessary. This was the principal reason she had never considered immediate physical rescue worth forcing Victor into an unprepared confrontation: from her perspective, she could already live beside the people she cared about while waiting for a safer opportunity to recover her true body.[4]
Victor nevertheless promised that her physical imprisonment would end. He identified Yog and Moira as the key to the current plan. Yog possesses the unique connection necessary to locate and interact with Azathoth's imprisonment, while Moira's abnormal relationship with Reality offers a possible way to overcome restrictions belonging to a place outside the native Universe's ordinary Laws. Azathoth immediately understood the implication and became visibly ecstatic at the possibility of finally regaining complete freedom.[4]
The family does not regard this as permission to sacrifice the children for Azathoth's liberation. Later discussion reiterates Victor's longstanding refusal to make his daughters solve adult problems simply because they possess useful powers. Yog was born with the liberation of Azathoth as one of her original purposes and Moira may guide the process, but Victor continues searching for alternatives and will not force a solution that unnecessarily endangers either daughter.[23]

### Current Status
Azathoth is currently alive and conscious. Her true body remains physically confined in the extradimensional prison outside the native Universe, but the confinement no longer prevents her consciousness from leaving and interacting with the Elderblood family. Describing her simply as an inactive "prisoner" is therefore incomplete: her body is imprisoned while Azathoth as an active consciousness remains involved in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household and Imperial affairs.[4]
Her exact physical location is deliberately difficult to reach. It exists outside the Universe in a dimension where the Rules of the native Creator do not normally apply, preventing ordinary spatial travel or the local cosmological administration from being treated as straightforward solutions. Yog's deep connection with Azathoth provides a route toward locating the body, while Victor believes the interaction between Yog and Moira creates a genuine method of breaching the confinement.[4]
Azathoth has no immediate desire to force the rescue before the family is prepared. She considers the ability of her consciousness to remain with Victor and their children sufficient reason to wait rather than begin a premature conflict with the unknown powers maintaining the prison. Victor is substantially less accepting of the arrangement and has made her eventual complete liberation one of his objectives.[4]
Within the Elderblood hierarchy, Azathoth currently occupies a unique position. She is neither one of Victor's ordinary subjects nor merely another Empress defined by political territory. She is his Eldritch counterpart, co-founder of the Pantheon to which several of their cosmic descendants belong, and the only person presently identified as his genuine equal in both power and Authority.[3]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Azathoth's husband, counterpart and the central figure around whom her modern identity developed. Before discovering Victor, Azathoth's existence revolved around curiosity, accumulation and self-understanding. Finding another Eldritch [[Chaos]] gave her something fundamentally new: a person she wished to reach rather than merely another phenomenon she wanted to study.[6]
Her humanoid identity was deliberately constructed around that relationship. She modeled her face and body after Victor, chose the opposite gender, selected an individual name after observing that he possessed one and defined one of her earliest personal ambitions as uniting their Essences to create a Pantheon. Victor therefore influenced Azathoth's development before the two could physically stand beside one another.[6]
Victor reciprocated the connection after learning the truth behind Yog's birth. He never treated Azathoth as simply a hostile foreign entity responsible for unexpectedly creating a child from his Essence. As he learned more about his own Eldritch nature, she became one of the few beings capable of explaining what he was becoming from direct experience.[13][2]
Their partnership is intellectual as much as romantic. Azathoth supplies firsthand information regarding foreign Cosmologies, Eldritch development and the structure of Primordial administration, while Victor continuously applies that information to problems the native Gods had accepted as immutable. Their conversations in Chapters 1075–1076 show them repeatedly arriving at the same conclusion: Authority is something that can be understood and eventually taken rather than an eternal privilege that must be respected because its current owner is ancient.[12][16]
Victor also provides Azathoth with something her earlier existence lacked completely—a family large enough that she is no longer fundamentally alone. Through him she gained Yog, [[Abhoth]], the broader Elderblood household and an enormous extended civilization whose survival she now values. Her relationship with Creation itself consequently changed because destroying the Universe would now destroy things she personally cares about.[12][8]
By Chapter 1171, Azathoth is the only person explicitly identified as Victor's true equal in both power and Authority. The comparison is especially significant because it occurs after Victor has spent hundreds of thousands of subjective years developing alongside an Empire filled with Primordial Goddesses, True Dragon Goddesses and exceptional cosmic beings.[3]
Despite this parity, neither is presented as subordinate to the other in their Eldritch relationship. Victor is Emperor of the civilization they inhabit, while Azathoth possesses her own foundational status in the [[Eldritch Pantheon]]. Their relationship functions as a partnership between two Chaos-level existences whose interests have become increasingly inseparable.[2][3]

#### Yog-Sothoth Elderblood
[[Yog-Sothoth]] Elderblood is Azathoth and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s first directly created daughter. Azathoth produced her after obtaining a minute portion of Victor's Essence and combining it with her own. Unlike ordinary reproduction, the process occurred while the parents' main bodies were separated by Azathoth's prison and created a child whose existence was fundamentally metaphysical and Eldritch from birth.[6]
Azathoth initially described Yog as both her first daughter and her hope of escaping confinement. That purpose does not diminish the familial relationship between them. Azathoth named her personally and regarded Yog as the beginning of the Pantheon she wanted to create with Victor.[6]
Victor later reinforced the distinction between Yog as a person and Yog as a useful power. Although he understood that her unusual nature might someday open the prison, he refused to raise her solely for that task. The rest of the family likewise insisted that Yog be allowed to grow as a daughter before being expected to solve cosmic problems belonging to her parents.[13][24]
After approximately two hundred thousand years of Imperial development, Yog has matured into one of the most extraordinary Elderblood daughters. Her connection to Unreality and Nothingness makes her the principal known means of interacting with restrictions that operate outside ordinary native Laws, and Chapter 1176 confirms that her connection to Azathoth can guide Victor toward the hidden prison itself.[5][4]

#### Abhoth
[[Abhoth]] is an Eldritch being created jointly by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Azathoth during the Empire's preparations for conflict with the Upper Sectors. Victor gathered the foundation of the new existence and explicitly asked Azathoth to assist him; she placed her hand over his and amplified the creation until a malformed creature covered in numerous eyes emerged.[8]
Azathoth's first response was maternal rather than strategic. She greeted the newborn gently, stroked him and regarded him as another member of their Pantheon. When Ruby reacted negatively to the implications of his name, Azathoth defended Abhoth on the grounds that a newborn could not be condemned as evil for something he had not done.[8]
Abhoth therefore differs from Yog in origin but remains another direct product of Victor and Azathoth working together. Yog was created from their Essences while Azathoth was still a distant observer desperately seeking connection with Victor; Abhoth was deliberately created after the two had become established partners within the same family and civilization.[6][8]

#### Moira Eutychia Elderblood
[[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]] is the daughter of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and [[Tyche]] Elderblood, making Azathoth one of her extended mothers rather than her biological parent. Her birth nevertheless becomes directly connected to Azathoth's future because Moira possesses an unprecedented relationship with Reality itself.[15]
The System's attempt to claim Moira for the vacant cosmological function associated with Destiny leads directly to Victor confronting the Primordial hierarchy. After the confrontation, Victor turns to Azathoth and realizes that Moira's existence provides the missing component of a possible rescue plan.[15][4]
Yog can provide the connection to Azathoth and interact with restrictions through her unusual Eldritch nature; Moira can potentially make Reality accommodate a result that conventional mechanisms would reject. Victor therefore describes both daughters together as the key to freeing Azathoth's body.[4]
This does not mean Moira is being raised as a tool for that purpose. The family later emphasizes that Moira would primarily guide or facilitate the solution and that Victor continues examining alternatives rather than forcing either daughter to carry an adult responsibility merely because their powers make them useful.[23]

#### The Elderblood Family
Azathoth initially wanted [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] because he was another existence like herself. Her attachment subsequently expanded beyond that original Eldritch fascination. Through Victor she became part of the enormous Elderblood household, whose members include beings from races, Pantheons and backgrounds radically different from her own.[18]
Her behavior among Victor's wives demonstrates that she no longer regards those women as irrelevant native creatures surrounding her companion. She speaks with them naturally, participates in their discussions and teasing, contributes to collective strategic decisions and accepts the family structure in which Victor's wives cooperate rather than constantly competing for exclusive ownership of him.[18][1][8]
Azathoth's presence is nevertheless still capable of reminding the others what she fundamentally is. Her emotional leakage can destabilize minds, her unrestricted compatibility with Victor's Eldritch information exceeds that of the Dragon Goddesses around her, and the family instinctively gives Victor and Azathoth greater distance when both begin combining their cosmic-horror powers.[8][15]
The contrast is characteristic of her current life: the same being who once forced the native Primordials to create an extraordinary prison can appear in the middle of a family argument, become annoyed when her sisters call her old and casually participate in household banter.[1]

#### Regulators of Existence
Azathoth's first relationship with the native [[Regulators of Existence]] was openly antagonistic. From their perspective she was an invasive foreign [[Chaos]] whose uncontrolled growth threatened the stability of the system they were responsible for maintaining. From Azathoth's perspective they were immensely powerful obstacles that attempted to destroy her and eventually imprisoned her when direct elimination failed.[7]
The conflict left a lasting impression upon the Primordial order. Much later, entities accustomed to treating ordinary Gods as insignificant react with genuine alarm to the possibility of confronting another existence related to Azathoth. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] recognizes that the fear attached to her history is itself strategically useful.[19]
Azathoth does not, however, treat every native Primordial as one unified personality. Chapter 1075 establishes that she understands the distinction between the consciousness occupying a cosmic position and the Aspect administered through that position. The later confrontation over Moira proves that the Regulators can disagree among themselves: the Universal Tree sides with Victor and openly opposes anyone attempting to take his niece, while other administrators question the System's unexplained intervention.[12][4]
Her hostility is consequently directed toward threats and hostile administrators rather than toward the abstract existence of every universal function. Azathoth understands better than most that recklessly destroying those functions could collapse the very Reality her family currently inhabits.[12]

### Future / Extra
The Extra chapters depict an enormously later Azathoth and should be separated from her currently imprisoned main-story incarnation. The amount of development occurring between the present narrative and those scenes has not been quantified.[9][10]
Future Azathoth is physically free and operates openly alongside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and their Eldritch descendants rather than interacting with the family while her main body remains confined. She participates in the larger defense of Victor's Reality and appears as one of the senior figures responsible for dealing with other cosmic horrors and foreign Eldritch threats.[9][10]
The future family has expanded well beyond Yog and [[Abhoth]], including Eldritch daughters such as [[Nyarlathotep]], [[Shub-Niggurath]] and [[Cthulhu]]. Their behavior demonstrates the logical conclusion of Azathoth's original desire in Chapter 980: the lonely foreign [[Chaos]] that once searched the Cosmos for another being like herself eventually becomes the matriarch of an enormous Eldritch family.[6][9][10]
Azathoth's personality remains recognizable despite the scale of her future responsibilities. She remains eccentric, attached to Victor and accustomed to the chaotic behavior of their descendants. Her increased freedom and development do not transform her into an impersonal cosmic mechanism; the individual identity she developed after discovering Victor remains central to her future self.[10]
Future statements and feats should not automatically be applied to Azathoth's present incarnation. The Extra version has experienced an undefined period of additional growth and, most importantly, is no longer limited by the prison still containing her main-story body.

### Abilities

As an Eldritch [[Chaos]], he is capable of feeding on Divinities, just as the remnants of Chaos in this Creation called Positivity & Negativity do. With time, the more he consumes this Creation to feed himself, there will come a point where he will become a Being outside of this Creation that not even this Reality can contain, and he will become a Being of a Higher Dimension. In simple words, he will become a Multiversal Being.
—Azathoth explaining the development of an Eldritch Chaos, Chapter 1015
Azathoth Elderblood is a natural Eldritch Chaos whose powers originate from a foreign Chaos rather than the Divine structure of the native Cosmology. Unlike an ordinary God, her existence was never created by the local Primordials, is not naturally integrated into their Rules, and develops through the accumulation and assimilation of Energy, Divinities, knowledge and increasingly complete self-understanding.[1][2]
Azathoth's abilities should therefore be distinguished from those of the native [[Regulators of Existence]]. Her enormous power allows her to contend with beings occupying Primordial positions, but she is not herself one of the native administrators. Likewise, powers demonstrated by her descendants should not automatically be copied onto Azathoth merely because she serves as one of their Eldritch sources; this page separates powers personally demonstrated or directly attributed to Azathoth from derivative abilities belonging specifically to beings such as [[Yog-Sothoth]] Elderblood.[3][4]

#### Powers & Abilities

##### Eldritch Chaos Physiology
- Eldritch [[Chaos]] Physiology: Azathoth is a naturally occurring Eldritch Chaos originating outside the native Creation. Her fundamental existence follows an external cosmological framework rather than the Laws through which ordinary Gods, Souls and supernatural species of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Universe were produced.[1][2]
- Foreign Existence: Azathoth's existence did not originate from the local [[Primordial Chaos|Primal Chaos]], native Primordials or Divine system. Authorities that possess overwhelming control over native existence therefore do not automatically possess the same degree of absolute control over her.[1][5]
- Non-Human Physiology: Her original form possesses no conventional Human anatomy, fixed biological sex or natural humanoid structure. Her female humanoid body was deliberately constructed after she discovered Victor.[3]
- Formlessness / Mutable Physiology: Azathoth's Chaos existence is not fundamentally restricted to one permanent anatomy. Her astronomical true body and compressed humanoid manifestation are expressions of the same Eldritch existence rather than separate species forms.[1][3]
- Foreign-Law Interaction: Azathoth can exist inside a Reality whose Rules were never designed to produce her. Her presence is consequently treated as an irregularity by the native order rather than as an ordinary component of the system.[1][2]
- Rule Incompatibility: Native Reality does not accommodate unrestricted Eldritch existence cleanly. Contact between Azathoth's Essence and ordinary Creation can produce distortion, corruption and madness rather than a normal interaction between compatible supernatural systems.[1][2]
- Energy-Based Maturation: Physical maturation for an Eldritch Chaos depends primarily upon accumulating immense quantities of Energy rather than simply surviving for a fixed chronological period.[1][2]
- Existential Growth through Self-Understanding: Development is not exclusively physical. Azathoth's realization of her own identity, desires, chosen name and relationship with Victor produced genuine maturation in her existence even without immediately providing enough Energy to complete her physical growth.[3]
- Extremely High Development Ceiling: Azathoth consumed an enormous world, powerful Divine beings and astronomical quantities of Energy while still remaining incomplete by the standards of a fully developed Eldritch Chaos.[1][2]
- Higher-Dimensional Evolution (Developmental potential): Azathoth explains that sufficiently prolonged growth allows an Eldritch Chaos to become too great for its current Reality to contain, eventually becoming what she describes as a Higher-Dimensional or Multiversal Being.[2]
- Chaos Assimilation (Racial mechanic): Eldritch Chaos can assimilate supernatural powers, Divinities and other suitable sources of Energy into its own development rather than being restricted to a fixed set of powers received at birth.[2]
- Inter-Cosmological Survival: Chaos-class existences are capable of operating beyond the normal protection of a single Cosmology and surviving within the dangerous regions separating separate Creations.[5]
- Inter-Cosmological Adaptation: Azathoth successfully entered and continued developing inside a foreign Cosmology whose laws, Primordials and metaphysical structure differed from those associated with her origin.[1][5]

##### Chaos
- [[Chaos]] Manipulation: Chaos forms the central foundation of Azathoth's existence. She explicitly identifies herself as Chaos Incarnate, while later narration describes her as the [[Primordial Chaos]] of the [[Eldritch Pantheon]].[2][4]
- Chaos Incarnation: Chaos is not merely a spell, Energy type or acquired Divinity used by Azathoth; it forms the existential basis from which her Eldritch nature develops.[2]
- Foreign Chaos: Azathoth's Chaos is separate from the native Primordial Chaos from which [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Universe originated. Chapter 1075 establishes that independent Cosmologies can possess distinct Chaos sources, Primordials and Rules.[5]
- Eldritch Pantheon Source: Azathoth occupies a foundational source position within the Eldritch Pantheon. Eldritch Gods and cosmic horrors descended from or existentially connected to her can develop Concepts belonging to an externalized Eldritch framework rather than functioning solely through the local Divine system.[2][4]
- Divinity Consumption: Eldritch Chaos can consume Divinities as nourishment. Azathoth specifically explains this property while discussing Victor's development and had already acquired powers from Divine beings she consumed during her earlier existence.[1][2]
- Conceptual Assimilation: Because a Divinity contains Conceptual properties, consumption can incorporate supernatural characteristics associated with Divine Concepts into the development of an Eldritch Chaos.[2]
- Unknown Acquired Divinities: Azathoth consumed numerous Divine beings during her travels and therefore possesses an unspecified collection of acquired supernatural characteristics. The story has not individually named most of these Divinities, so they should not be replaced by speculative lists of Concepts.[1][2]
- Cosmological Predation (Developmental potential): Azathoth explains that advanced Chaos entities can prey upon other Cosmologies in the greater Void, using the resources or existence of foreign Creations to strengthen their own development.[5]
- Cosmology Expansion (Developmental potential): A mature Chaos existence can develop together with the Cosmological structure associated with it rather than remaining permanently subordinate to a foreign Reality.[5]

##### Eldritch Energy
- [[Eldritch Energy]] Manipulation: Azathoth naturally possesses and manipulates the foreign Energy associated with [[Outer Gods]]. Eldritch Energy is not merely another variation of native Divine or magical Energy; its foreign nature allows it to interact with Creation in ways ordinary native powers do not.[2]
- Energy Generation / Projection: As an Eldritch [[Chaos]], Azathoth can manifest and supply enormous quantities of Eldritch Energy through her own existence.[2]
- Power Augmentation: Azathoth can directly add her power to another compatible being. During the creation of [[Abhoth]], she placed her hand over [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s and amplified the forces he was combining.[6]
- Eldritch Empowerment: Azathoth and Victor jointly strengthened Eldritch creatures used throughout the Imperial galaxy's defensive network, greatly increasing their usefulness as cosmic guardians.[7]
- Foreign Concept Empowerment: Eldritch Energy can sustain Concepts whose source does not belong normally to the native Divine structure. This principle is one of the foundations of the [[Eldritch Pantheon]] explained by Azathoth.[2]
- Rule Disruption: Eldritch Energy can interfere with native supernatural mechanics by imposing characteristics foreign to the Rules under which those mechanics ordinarily operate.[2]
- Conceptual Contamination: Concepts influenced by Eldritch Energy can develop abnormal properties not normally produced by their native cosmological equivalents.[2]
- Eldritch Compatibility: Azathoth possesses vastly greater compatibility with Eldritch power and information than ordinary Divine beings. When Victor shared memories involving his fully manifested cosmic-horror existence, every other woman required Eldritch Runic protection and filtering while Azathoth received the information without protection.[6]

##### Corruption and Madness
- Corruption Inducement: Corruption is an intrinsic consequence of Azathoth's foreign Eldritch nature. Her Essence can alter beings and supernatural systems that interact with it rather than merely damaging them physically.[1][2]
- Existential Corruption: Contact with genuine Eldritch Essence can affect a target's underlying existence because the foreign Energy operates according to principles incompatible with ordinary native beings.[1]
- Conceptual Corruption: Eldritch influence can distort the behavior of native powers and Concepts by forcing them to operate according to foreign supernatural logic.[2]
- Law Corruption: The fundamental danger of a cosmic horror is not limited to destructive output; Eldritch influence can corrupt the Rules supporting a phenomenon and make the affected structure behave according to another logic.[2]
- Madness Inducement: Contact with Azathoth's incomprehensible Essence can induce madness in beings unable to process it. During her early existence, beings attempting to interact with or exploit her Essence were driven insane by contact with something they did not understand.[1]
- Passive Mental Interference: Azathoth does not always need to consciously attack someone's mind. In Chapter 1175, her irritation alone caused the mental stability of nearby Dragon Goddesses to decline until Violet used Order to mitigate the effect.[8]
- Eldritch Presence: The above phenomenon demonstrates that sufficiently strong emotional leakage from Azathoth can carry the informational and psychological pressure of her underlying cosmic-horror nature.[8]
- Madness-Associated Aura: Later family descriptions associate the supernatural "flavor" of Azathoth and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s combined presence with madness and insanity, further reflecting the Eldritch qualities naturally emanating from them.[9]

##### Absorption and Assimilation
- Absorption: Consumption is one of Azathoth's principal natural mechanisms for acquiring Energy, information and supernatural development.[1]
- Lifeform Absorption: Azathoth consumed vast numbers of powerful beings during her early development.[1]
- God Absorption: The beings she consumed included extraordinarily powerful Divine entities whose Energy and supernatural characteristics contributed to her growth.[1]
- Energy Absorption: Energy acquired from other beings and astronomical structures can be consumed directly as nourishment.[1][2]
- Divinity Absorption: Azathoth can feed upon Divinities and incorporate their useful supernatural characteristics into herself.[2]
- Power Absorption: Powers belonging to consumed Divine beings can become part of the accumulated supernatural foundation from which Azathoth develops.[1][2]
- Memory Absorption: Devouring living beings allowed Azathoth to access memories contained within them.[1]
- Knowledge Absorption: She learned about civilizations, Gods, cosmic structures and supernatural systems by processing the memories of beings she consumed.[1]
- Mass Information Processing: Azathoth processed an enormous volume of foreign memories and information while constructing her understanding of herself and the Cosmos.[1]
- Cosmic Energy Consumption: Stars, planets, black holes and other massive Energy sources are described as potential nourishment for the continued physical maturation of an Eldritch [[Chaos]].[2]
- Cosmological Consumption (Advanced potential): Fully developed Chaos entities can eventually compete over and consume resources belonging to entire foreign Cosmologies.[5]
Note: Chapter 1159 names Divinities such as Night, Emptiness, Silence, Shadows, Death, Fear, Darkness and Oblivion as desirable nourishment belonging to enemy Gods. This demonstrates that Azathoth can regard such Divinities as consumable resources; it does not establish that she currently possesses every one of those Concepts.[6]

##### Reality and Law Manipulation
- Reality Warping / Reality Distortion: Azathoth's unrestricted existence naturally distorts surrounding Reality. This effect was already present during her early growth, before she possessed the refined knowledge and personality displayed in the modern story.[1]
- Passive Reality Warping: Azathoth does not need to consciously activate a spell for Reality surrounding her true form to become distorted; the incompatibility and scale of her existence can cause the effect simply through her presence.[1]
- Spatial Distortion: Space around Azathoth became distorted as her true body expanded through the accumulation of enormous quantities of Energy.[1]
- Environmental Distortion: The greater the portion of her unrestricted existence being expressed, the more heavily the local environment must accommodate an entity that does not naturally belong to its cosmological structure.[1]
- Foreign-Law Interference: Azathoth's Eldritch nature can interact with native systems without being completely bound by the assumptions those systems impose upon beings born inside the Cosmology.[2][5]
- High-Order Authority Interaction: In Chapter 1167, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] states that without his cooperation the only people he believed could likely seize control of the highly reinforced conceptual miniature galaxy he created were Azathoth and Jeanne.[10]
- Existential Weight: The above feat is particularly significant because Victor's miniature galaxy was reinforced by multiple high-level Concepts and possessed substantially greater existential "weight" than an ordinary subordinate dimension.[10]
- Cosmic Creation (Joint potential with Victor): Victor later admits that although his artificial Eldritch galaxy was an extraordinarily accurate imitation, directly merging his power with Azathoth could allow the two of them to create a true Eldritch galaxy possessing the deeper foundations his solo construct lacked.[11]
- Cosmological Signal Generation (Consequence): Victor specifically avoids performing the above experiment because a genuine Eldritch cosmic creation would act like a massive metaphysical signal announcing their activity to outside powers.[11]

##### True Form and Shapeshifting
- Enormous Size: Azathoth's true form exists on astronomical scales incomparably greater than ordinary biological beings.[1]
- Cosmic Growth: Her body expanded as she accumulated Energy, meaning size itself is partially connected to the physical maturation of her Eldritch existence.[1]
- Celestial-Scale Body: Azathoth's growth reached the point where stars and other cosmic structures were comparatively small features of the environment surrounding her existence.[1]
- Variable Size: Her manifested scale can range from a Human-sized woman to the astronomical proportions of her true Eldritch body.[1][3]
- Shapeshifting: Azathoth can radically alter the form through which she interacts with Reality.[3]
- Humanoid Transformation: After discovering [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], she deliberately constructed a female humanoid manifestation modeled upon him.[3]
- Gender Transformation: Azathoth's original existence possessed no conventional biological sex. She deliberately chose a female identity when designing her humanoid form.[3]
- Size Manipulation: She can compress her manifested appearance to a scale suitable for interacting with Victor and the Elderblood family despite the astronomical size associated with her genuine body.[3]
- Eldritch Transformation: Strong emotion can cause her humanoid manifestation to visibly distort as characteristics of her cosmic-horror nature become more pronounced.[12][13]
- Restricted Full Manifestation: Her complete true existence cannot be expressed casually inside every environment without severe consequences, and her true body is additionally restricted by its current prison.[1][13]

##### Essence Fragmentation and Remote Existence
- Essence Fragmentation: Azathoth can divide portions of her Essence from her main body and distribute them independently across enormous distances.[1]
- Distributed Extensions: Individual fragments can operate as remote extensions of Azathoth without requiring her complete body to travel to every location she wishes to observe.[1]
- Remote Observation: Her fragments allow Azathoth to observe civilizations and events far from the location containing her main body.[1]
- Remote Feeding: Distributed fragments can function as indirect channels through which she acquires Energy and information.[1]
- Extreme Range: Azathoth's distributed influence reached across enormous portions of Creation and ultimately allowed her to discover [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] despite her main body remaining imprisoned.[1][3]
- Hidden Essence: She can conceal the genuine Eldritch component of a fragment beneath supernatural characteristics obtained from previously consumed beings.[1]
- Persistent Influence: Imprisoning Azathoth's primary body did not eliminate every external manifestation or connection she had already established.[1]
Note: These should be described as Essence fragments rather than automatically as "Soul fragments". The exact relationship between Azathoth's Eldritch Essence and conventional native Soul mechanics is not sufficiently established to treat the terms as interchangeable.

##### Concealment and Perception Manipulation
- Concealment: Azathoth can disguise the genuine nature of her distributed power from supernatural beings attempting to analyze or exploit it.[1][3]
- Presence Concealment: Her true Eldritch source can be hidden beneath other supernatural properties.[1]
- Energy Concealment: The Energy presented by one of her remote extensions does not necessarily reveal the foreign [[Chaos]] hidden underneath it.[1]
- Deceptive Manifestation: A distributed extension can appear sufficiently different from Azathoth's true nature that another supernatural being may interact with it without immediately identifying what it actually represents.[1][3]
- Perception Manipulation: Azathoth can interfere with how another being interprets the supernatural information it receives.[3]
- False Sensory Information: A higher-level foreign God attempting to obtain power associated with [[Shiva]] was manipulated into believing it had acquired what it sought while Azathoth's actual Eldritch influence remained concealed.[3]
- Perceptual Deception: She can exploit the difference between what a target believes it has perceived and the genuine supernatural structure behind the interaction.[3]
Limitation: Concealment is not demonstrated to be absolutely undetectable by every possible cosmic system. It should therefore not be treated as universal immunity to detection.

##### Remote Manifestation and Consciousness Projection
- Avatar Creation / Remote Manifestation: Azathoth can manifest functional representations of herself despite the true body remaining imprisoned.[14]
- Remote Avatar: Her representation can appear among [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his wives, converse normally and participate in family interactions while her physical body remains elsewhere.[14]
- Remote Communication: Azathoth can communicate with Victor despite the extraordinary physical and dimensional separation between them.[14][5]
- Telepathy: She can participate in the supernatural communication network used by Victor's wives without being physically present beside them.[14]
- Consciousness Projection: Chapter 1176 reveals that Azathoth's consciousness can escape the confinement affecting her physical body and remain with her family whenever necessary.[13]
- Body-Consciousness Separation: Physical imprisonment therefore does not automatically imprison Azathoth's active consciousness to the same extent.[13]
- Extradimensional Operation: Her active consciousness continues operating even after her physical body is transferred into a dimension outside the Universe where the Rules of the native Creator do not apply.[13]

##### Cosmic Awareness
- Cosmic Awareness: Azathoth possesses perception appropriate to an ancient Eldritch existence that has directly experienced multiple cosmic systems.[1][5]
- Primordial Detection: When unknown presences gathered around the events involving Moira, Azathoth immediately identified them as the native Primordial Beings or entities effectively equivalent to them.[8]
- Extradimensional Body Awareness: Azathoth remained capable of sensing the condition and general location of her main body after it was secretly transferred outside the Universe.[13]
- Distributed Perception: Her scattered Essence fragments function as remote observation points capable of collecting information across enormous distances.[1]
- Eldritch Recognition: Azathoth was capable of recognizing the Eldritch nature developing beneath [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Vampire, Dragon and Divine characteristics despite his unusual status as a native-born being.[3]
- Cosmological Recognition: Her experience allows her to distinguish between ordinary Divine phenomena, native Primordial structures and powers originating from outside a Cosmology.[1][5]

##### Creation
- Creation: Despite her intrinsically corruptive nature, Azathoth is capable of creating genuinely independent Eldritch beings rather than being limited to destruction, consumption or avatars.[3]
- Life Creation / Being Creation: Azathoth created [[Yog-Sothoth]] Elderblood by combining part of her own Essence with a minute quantity of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Essence.[3][15]
- Essence Combination: Separate Eldritch Essences can be combined into an independent being possessing characteristics derived from both sources.[3]
- Autonomous Creation: Yog was not merely another remote fragment of Azathoth; she became an independent daughter possessing her own identity, powers and development.[3][15]
- Remote Creation: Azathoth performed Yog's creation while her principal body remained imprisoned and Victor was located extremely far away.[3]
- Eldritch God Creation: Yog's existence develops within the Eldritch Divine structure rather than functioning solely according to the native Pantheon system.[3][16]
- Power Bestowal / Concept Bestowal: Azathoth was capable of incorporating specific supernatural foundations into Yog when creating her.[3][15]
- Space Bestowal: Yog was created with an Eldritch Concept associated with Space.[15][17]
- Intangibility Bestowal: Intangibility was likewise one of the supernatural foundations possessed by Yog from birth.[15][17]
- Nullity Bestowal: Yog was also created with the abnormal Eldritch Concept associated with Nullity / Nothingness.[15][17]
Important: Azathoth's involvement in establishing these Concepts during Yog's creation confirms Conceptual bestowal and creative access to those foundations. It does not prove that every specialized application later demonstrated by Yog—such as invalidating entire laws, realities or effects—is automatically a personal technique Azathoth has demonstrated.
- Joint Eldritch Creation: Azathoth can combine her power with Victor's during direct creation. Their clearest current example is [[Abhoth]].[6]
- Living Corruption Creation: Victor and Azathoth created Abhoth as an autonomous Eldritch being centered upon controlled corruption and intended to serve as a defensive countermeasure against hostile corruption or possession.[6]
- Creation Amplification: Azathoth directly amplified Victor's developing creation by placing her power into the process.[6]
- Programmable Corruptive Function: Abhoth's corruption was designed so that its characteristics could be deliberately directed rather than functioning as uncontrolled contamination.[6]
Important: Abhoth possesses his own independent abilities. His later applications should not automatically be treated as personal powers of Azathoth merely because she participated in his creation.

##### Eldritch Pantheon Creation
- Pantheon Creation: Azathoth explicitly calls herself the creator of a Pantheon and occupies one of the foundational [[Chaos]] positions from which the Eldritch hierarchy develops.[2]
- Conceptual Source Generation: Eldritch descendants can develop supernatural Concepts whose fundamental source belongs to the Chaos foundation of their Pantheon rather than exclusively to the native Primordial hierarchy.[2][16]
- Foreign Concept Generation: Eldritch Concepts do not necessarily possess the same restrictions, limits or source relationships as apparently similar native Divinities.[2][16]
- Cosmic-Horror Progenitor: Chapter 1171 directly describes Azathoth as the creator of the cosmic horrors belonging to the [[Eldritch Pantheon]].[4]
- Foundational Authority: The same passage identifies Azathoth as the [[Primordial Chaos]] of the Eldritch Pantheon, describing her foundational position within that external Divine hierarchy.[4]
Important: "Primordial Chaos of the Eldritch Pantheon" does not mean Azathoth is the native Primal Chaos that produced [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Universe. The two are separate cosmological foundations.[5][4]

##### Eldritch Creature Empowerment
- Power Augmentation: Azathoth can strengthen existing Eldritch creatures. During the construction of the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s Galactic defenses, she and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] empowered creatures originating from Hela's previous wars and distributed them throughout asteroids, stars and black holes.[7]
- Cosmic Creature Empowerment: The empowered organisms became viable guardians of enormous astronomical regions.[7]
- Creature Influence: The enhanced Eldritch beings responded to Victor and Azathoth with the instinctive obedience expected toward vastly superior source-level beings.[7]
Important: This establishes empowerment and influence over specific Eldritch creatures. It does not establish the older claim that Azathoth personally created every Creature of the Abyss.

##### Inter-Cosmological Abilities
- Dimensional / Inter-Cosmological Travel: Azathoth entered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s native Creation from a foreign Cosmology during her earlier development.[1][5]
- Void Survival: She possesses the type of [[Chaos]] existence capable of surviving regions separating independent Cosmologies.[5]
- Foreign Reality Adaptation: Azathoth remained functional inside a Creation whose Laws were not the ones associated with her origin.[1]
- Cosmological Predation Knowledge: She understands how advanced Chaos existences can fight over or consume resources belonging to separate Cosmologies.[5]
- Cosmological Rule Knowledge: Azathoth understands that each independent Creation can possess different Laws, Primordials and originating Chaos rather than sharing one universally identical administrative system.[5]
- Cosmological Authority (Developmental potential): Azathoth understands the eventual relationship between a mature Chaos existence and the Cosmology associated with it.[5]
- Independent Cosmology Formation (Potential): A sufficiently developed Chaos may become the foundation around which a separate Cosmological structure and its Laws develop.[5]
- Cosmology Expansion (Potential): Such structures can be strengthened through resources consumed from other Cosmologies.[5]
Important: These are established developmental mechanics of Chaos-class existences and part of Azathoth's own knowledge. They should not be presented as proof that current imprisoned Azathoth already possesses a completed personal Universe identical to a fully matured Chaos.

##### Eldritch Runes
- Rune [[Magic]] / Eldritch Rune Mastery: Azathoth possesses exceptional proficiency and compatibility with Eldritch Runic systems. By Chapter 1092, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] treats her as a benchmark for extremely high-level Eldritch Rune knowledge.[18]
- Eldritch Language Understanding: Her knowledge extends beyond ordinary magical notation into the foreign language and principles underlying Eldritch inscriptions.[18]
- Conceptual Runic Knowledge: At the level relevant to Azathoth and Victor, Runes can serve as interfaces between meaning, supernatural Energy and the deeper Rules of Reality rather than functioning solely as conventional written spells.[18]
- Mental Protection Compatibility: Victor later uses Eldritch Runes to protect his wives from hazardous cosmic-horror information; Azathoth herself requires no such protection due to her natural Eldritch compatibility.[6]

#### Power and Scale
Azathoth's power should not be reduced to a single destructive measurement. The novel distinguishes between raw Energy, destructive scale, Authority, combat ability and ontological position; a being can be superior in one category without automatically being superior in all others.
- Outer God of the Highest Rank: Chapter 1160 explicitly describes Yog as the daughter of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Azathoth, two [[Outer Gods]] of the highest rank.[19]
- Superior Raw Energy: During the enormous Imperial Time ritual, Jeanne had recovered enough power to carry approximately thirty percent of the ritual comfortably and forty percent when necessary while approaching her former Primordial peak. Even then, the narration states that in pure Energy only Victor and Azathoth surpassed her.[20]
- Essential to Victor's Eldritch Ascension: The same period establishes that meaningful further advancement of Victor's Eldritch side required Azathoth rather than being something he could safely complete alone without provoking the native Primordial order.[20]
- Illustrative "Level Twelve" Existence: Chapter 1155 uses an intentionally simplified analogy in which [[Tyche]] corresponds to approximately ten, powerful True Dragon Goddesses to eleven, Victor, Jeanne, Azathoth and Yog to twelve, Primordial Beings to thirteen and Creators to fourteen. The narration explicitly warns that these values are illustrative examples of qualitative differences rather than formal power levels.[21]
- Multiple-Galaxy Destruction: Chapter 1164 identifies Victor, Azathoth, Jeanne and [[Scathach]] among the rare exceptions capable of annihilating multiple galaxies.[22]
- Galactic Threat Classification: The operational Imperial scale used in Chapter 1164 places galaxy-destroying beings within V-06, while emphasizing that the gap between the lower and upper portions of the same category can be enormous and that the classification measures overall threat rather than one attack statistic alone.[22]
- Above Scathach in Overall Threat: During Chapter 1160, Scathach is considered third in the Pantheon's overall threat hierarchy, with only Victor and Azathoth placed above her. The same discussion distinguishes Scathach's exceptional battle skill from Azathoth's much broader cosmic-horror threat.[19]
- High-Order Authority: Victor states that Azathoth and Jeanne are the people he considers capable of forcibly taking control of his Conceptually reinforced miniature galaxy without his voluntary cooperation.[10]
- True Eldritch Galaxy Creation (Joint): Victor states internally that merging his power with Azathoth would allow the creation of a genuine Eldritch galaxy rather than the highly accurate but incomplete replica he produced alone.[11]
- Victor-Level Power and Authority: Chapter 1171 gives Azathoth's clearest direct comparison: the only one who truly equals Victor in power and Authority is Azathoth. The narration attributes this to her status as the [[Primordial Chaos]] of the [[Eldritch Pantheon]] and creator of its cosmic horrors.[4]
- Primordial-Class Combat Relevance: When the family detects approaching Primordial Beings in Chapter 1175, Violet concludes that the other wives cannot directly intervene in a confrontation on that level and that only Victor or Azathoth can act directly against them.[8]
- Passive Primordial-Level Presence: Azathoth's irritation during the same crisis destabilizes the mental state of nearby Dragon Goddesses without a deliberate attack, requiring Violet's Order to counter the effect.[8]
- Current State versus Full Maturity: Despite her present standing, Chapter 1015 establishes that an Eldritch [[Chaos]] can continue developing far beyond the state presently demonstrated by Azathoth. Her current main-story incarnation should therefore not be equated automatically with a completely matured or Future/Extra Azathoth.[2]

#### Resistances
- Resistance to Death Manipulation: [[True Death]] participated in the attempt to eliminate Azathoth, yet native Death Authority failed to permanently terminate the foreign [[Chaos]] existence.[1]
- Resistance to Existence Erasure: The native cosmic powers were unable to simply erase Azathoth from existence and ultimately resorted to imprisonment instead.[1]
- Resistance to Conceptual Erasure (Native Death / END): The failure of native Death/End authority to permanently eliminate her demonstrates substantial resistance to Conceptual termination operating through the local Creation's framework.[1]
- Resistance to Primordial Authorities: Multiple native Regulators acting together could suppress and imprison Azathoth but could not permanently destroy her.[1]
- Resistance to Native-Law Authority: Powers possessing overwhelming authority over native beings do not automatically exercise equivalent absolute control over Azathoth because her existence originated outside their system.[1][2]
- Resistance to Eldritch Corruption: Azathoth naturally tolerates Eldritch Essence and cosmic-horror information that would corrupt or destabilize ordinary native beings.[6]
- Resistance to Madness Inducement: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] needed to protect his other wives with Eldritch Runes before transmitting memories of his unrestricted cosmic-horror manifestation. Azathoth alone received those impressions without protection.[6]
- Resistance to Information Hazards: The same feat demonstrates exceptional tolerance toward information whose direct perception was considered psychologically dangerous even for Victor's extremely powerful Dragon Goddess wives.[6]
- Resistance to Reality-Warping Suppression (Survival): Azathoth continued to exist after the native Primordials suppressed or circumvented the Reality distortion generated by her presence. This does not mean she could ignore their suppression completely.[1]
- Extradimensional Consciousness Persistence: Moving her true body into a dimension outside the Universe did not prevent Azathoth's consciousness from continuing to operate beyond the prison and interact with her family.[13]

##### Resistance Limits
Azathoth's resistances are extremely high but should not be presented as absolute immunities. The Regulators were unable to kill her, yet their combined Authorities were still sufficient to contain her.[1]
- Infinity is not negated automatically: Infinity successfully maintained an effectively endless separation that Azathoth could not simply cross by distorting Space or expanding her body.[1]
- Conceptual confinement can affect her: The prison combining the Authorities of the native cosmic administrators successfully prevented Azathoth's physical escape.[1]
- Reality Warping can be countered: Her passive Reality distortion did not automatically overwrite the superior restrictions imposed around the prison.[1]
- Foreign existence does not equal immunity: Being external to the native Laws makes those Laws less absolute against Azathoth; it does not mean that sufficiently powerful native Authorities cannot influence, suppress or restrain her.[1]

#### Limitations
- Physical Imprisonment: Azathoth's greatest current limitation is that her main body remains imprisoned despite her consciousness being able to leave the confinement.[13]
- Primordial Confinement: The native Regulators successfully contained Azathoth after discovering that direct destruction was ineffective.[1]
- Infinity-Based Separation: Infinity's Authority prevented Azathoth from simply reaching the Regulators by crossing or distorting the intervening Space.[1]
- Reality-Warping Suppression: Her natural distortion of Reality was insufficient to immediately overcome the combined structure of the original prison.[1]
- Current Prison Outside the Universe: Azathoth's true body has since been moved away from its former location into a dimension outside the Universe where the normal Rules of the native Creator do not apply.[13]
- Difficult Location: Ordinary native spatial mechanics cannot be assumed to provide a straightforward route toward a place existing outside the Universe's normal Rules. Yog's deep connection to Azathoth is currently one of the principal methods [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] intends to use to locate her body.[13]
- Temporal Disorientation: Azathoth's perception of Time is unreliable in the dimensionless environment associated with her prison. Something she experiences as having happened recently could correspond to thousands of external years.[13]
- Incomplete Physical Development: Azathoth remains capable of further Eldritch development and should not be treated as having reached the final possible state of an Eldritch [[Chaos]].[2]
- Enormous Energy Requirement: Further physical maturation requires astronomical quantities of Energy. Stars, planets, black holes and eventually resources on cosmological scales are discussed as meaningful nourishment for advanced Chaos development.[2][5]
- Danger of Unrestricted Manifestation: Azathoth's genuine nature can distort Reality and damage the minds of surrounding beings, requiring control when interacting with substantially weaker life.[1][8]
- Unknown Acquired Arsenal: Although she consumed numerous Gods, the novel has not named every power or Divinity she acquired. Unidentified powers cannot be converted into arbitrary specific abilities simply because they are theoretically possible.[1]
- Derived Powers are not automatically personal powers: The abilities of Yog, [[Abhoth]] and other descendants can demonstrate what Azathoth's Eldritch foundation is capable of producing, but their specialized techniques remain their own unless Azathoth separately demonstrates or is stated to possess them.

#### Growth
Azathoth's growth differs from ordinary biological evolution. Eldritch development consists of several interacting components: Energy accumulation expands her physical capacity, consumption provides new supernatural characteristics, memories provide information, and self-understanding develops the individual identity capable of controlling those resources.[1][3][2]
- Growth through Energy Absorption: Greater quantities of Energy increase the capacity and physical development of her Eldritch existence.[1][2]
- Growth through Divinity Consumption: Divine powers can be consumed as nourishment and incorporated into continued Eldritch development.[2]
- Growth through Knowledge: Absorbed memories accelerated Azathoth's understanding of civilizations, supernatural systems and herself.[1]
- Growth through Experience: Azathoth was not born possessing perfect knowledge. Encountering unfamiliar beings, frustration, imprisonment and eventually companionship continuously expanded her mentality.[1][3]
- Growth through Self-Understanding: Choosing the name Azathoth, developing a female identity, recognizing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] as her desired companion and defining her personal goals represented meaningful existential development.[3]
- Physical and Existential Growth are Distinct: Increased self-understanding can mature Azathoth psychologically and conceptually even when her main body has not accumulated sufficient Energy to complete an equivalent physical maturation.[3][2]
- Victor-Azathoth Synergy: By Chapter 1148, further meaningful development of Victor's own Eldritch side is stated to require Azathoth, demonstrating that interaction between compatible [[Chaos]] existences can become relevant to their higher evolution.[20]
- Higher-Existence Endpoint: According to Azathoth, enough continued consumption eventually produces a being that its original Reality cannot contain, resulting in a Higher-Dimensional or Multiversal existence.[2]
- Exact Current Formal Stage Unknown: The novel establishes that current Azathoth is not the ultimate possible maturation of an Eldritch Chaos, but the reviewed main-story material does not securely assign her present body a formal racial label such as Child, Teenager or Adult. Those labels should not be used as definitive current classifications without a direct chapter statement.

#### Skills & Knowledge
- Cosmological Knowledge: Azathoth possesses firsthand knowledge of multiple Cosmologies, foreign [[Chaos]] entities, native Primordial systems and the greater structure outside an individual Creation.[5]
- Primordial Knowledge: Her direct conflict with the [[Regulators of Existence]] gives her practical knowledge concerning their functions, Authorities and relationship with the Universe.[1][5]
- Conceptual Knowledge: Consuming Gods and absorbing their memories provided extensive experience with Divinities and supernatural Concepts.[1]
- Eldritch Knowledge: Azathoth understands the biology, feeding requirements and developmental trajectory of an Eldritch Chaos substantially better than [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] during the early stages of his Outer-God development.[2]
- Inter-Cosmological Knowledge: She knows that separate Creations can possess entirely different Rules, Chaos sources and Primordials.[5]
- Void Knowledge: Azathoth understands the region separating Cosmologies and the danger presented by other Chaos entities operating there.[5]
- Mass Information Processing: She processed enormous quantities of acquired memories while constructing a functional understanding of civilizations and Reality.[1]
- Adaptive Learning: Azathoth developed language, emotion, identity, strategic reasoning and social understanding through observation rather than beginning as an omniscient entity.[1][3]
- Strategic Concealment: After brute force failed to immediately defeat her prison, Azathoth changed methods and distributed concealed Essence throughout Creation instead of repeatedly wasting power against the same restriction.[1]
- Long-Term Planning: She tolerated continued physical imprisonment because her consciousness could remain with her family and because provoking the forces maintaining the prison before Victor was prepared would provide little strategic benefit.[13]
- Primordial Analysis: Azathoth understands that a Primordial consists of more than raw power; its consciousness occupies a cosmic function. She explains why destroying the entire underlying Aspect could damage the Universe while attacking or replacing the administrator itself represents a fundamentally different objective.[5]
- Eldritch Mentorship: Azathoth acts as one of Victor's principal teachers regarding his own Outer-God nature and the implications of becoming an Eldritch Chaos.[2]
- Rune Mastery: Her knowledge of Eldritch Runes is treated as a benchmark even by the increasingly advanced Victor.[18]
- Threat Recognition: Azathoth immediately identified the pressure gathering after Moira's birth as belonging to native Primordial or Primordial-equivalent beings.[8]
- Strategic Judgment: Despite personally desiring freedom, Azathoth refuses to encourage a premature rescue operation when she believes the family is not prepared for the resulting confrontation.[13]

#### Joint Abilities and Creations
The following abilities or feats specifically involve Azathoth combining her existence with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] rather than acting entirely alone.
- Creation of [[Yog-Sothoth]]: Azathoth used her own Essence together with a minute amount of Victor's to produce their first independent Eldritch daughter.[3]
- Creation of [[Abhoth]]: Victor assembled the developing existence while Azathoth directly added and amplified her own power, resulting in a living Eldritch corruption designed for Imperial defense.[6]
- Eldritch Creature Empowerment: Victor and Azathoth jointly empowered the cosmic creatures incorporated into the Imperial galaxy's defensive infrastructure.[7]
- True Eldritch Galaxy Creation (Stated potential): Victor states internally that combining himself directly with Azathoth would allow them to create a genuine Eldritch galaxy containing foundations his solo simulation could not reproduce.[11]
- Mutual Eldritch Development: Victor later determines that further significant advancement of his Eldritch side requires Azathoth rather than isolated training.[20]
- Compatible [[Chaos]] Interaction: Their unusual compatibility allows powers originating from two Eldritch Chaos existences to be combined without producing the destructive incompatibility expected between many unrelated supernatural systems.[3][6]

#### Future / Extra Abilities
The following material belongs to Azathoth's vastly later Future/Extra incarnation and should not be retroactively applied to the currently imprisoned main-story Azathoth.
- Multiversal Existence: Future Azathoth has developed far beyond the physical condition shown during the current main story and operates on the Multiversal scale associated with the oldest developed [[Outer Gods]].[23][24]
- Unrestricted Physical Presence: Unlike the current incarnation, future Azathoth is no longer depicted as a consciousness acting while her true body remains physically imprisoned.[23]
- Reality / Layer Protection: She operates alongside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and their Eldritch descendants within the defenses protecting his later Reality from external cosmic interference.[23]
- Large-Scale Eldritch Regulation: Future Azathoth is one of the senior authorities capable of monitoring and restraining younger Outer Gods when their behavior threatens unacceptable cosmic damage.[24]
- Superior Eldritch Control: Unlike several younger cosmic horrors whose emotions can unintentionally produce catastrophic phenomena, future Azathoth possesses much greater control over the manifestations produced by her Eldritch nature.[24]
- True-Form Reality Strain: The unrestricted manifestations of highly developed Outer Gods place enormous pressure upon ordinary Reality merely through existing, with future Azathoth belonging to the senior generation of these beings.[23][24]
Important: Future Azathoth has undergone an undefined and potentially enormous period of additional development. Future feats, status and freedom should remain separate from the current main-story incarnation.
