Pepper Scarlett Elderblood, formerly known as Pepper Scarlett and Pepper Scarlett Alucard, is a major supporting character in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]], the youngest adopted daughter of [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], one of the wives of [[Victor Elderblood]], and the biological mother of [[Priscillia Penny Elderblood]]. Originally a Noble Vampire descended from a powerful Water-based Clan of [[Nightingale]], Pepper eventually became a genuine Blood Dragon, True Dragon and Dragon Goddess through the extraordinary development of Victor's family. Her abilities combine exceptional physical strength, inherited Water Manipulation, extensive martial training, advanced Draconic Runecraft and an increasingly sophisticated understanding of supernatural technology. Although she is frequently regarded as an eccentric woman more interested in entertainment than serious responsibilities, her later accomplishments establish her as an exceptionally capable researcher whose creations include an artificial star, advanced dimensional-containment systems and functional weapons inspired by fictional technology.[1][2][3]
Pepper was not born into [[Scathach]]'s Bloodline. She originated from another influential Noble Vampire Clan whose supernatural inheritance was associated with Water rather than the Ice abilities possessed by Scathach and several of her biological descendants. During Pepper's childhood, extremist [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] infiltrated Nightingale and massacred much of her family, leaving the young heiress surrounded by destruction and the bodies of her parents. Scathach arrived before the remaining attackers could kill her, exterminated the Hunters and rescued several survivors. Rather than merely providing temporary protection, Scathach adopted Pepper and raised her alongside Siena and Lacus, allowing the surviving child to become part of the Scarlett household.[1]
The circumstances of Pepper's adoption profoundly influenced her personality. Although she appeared cheerful, playful and frequently absent-minded, the destruction of her biological family left her with an intense fear of losing the people who provided her with security. Scathach became more than an ordinary adoptive mother: Pepper regarded her as the person who had rescued her from the destruction of everything familiar and eventually described her as her refuge. Even after centuries of growth and martial training, Pepper found extended separation from Scathach emotionally difficult, especially when circumstances forced her to travel far beyond the territory in which she had grown up.[1]
Pepper's relationship with Victor began when he arrived in Nightingale as a newly transformed Vampire. Initially, she possessed vastly greater practical experience and helped explain the supernatural abilities he had inherited without understanding. As Victor developed beneath Scathach's training and eventually surpassed the limitations of ordinary Noble Vampires, their relationship gradually changed. He recognized that Pepper's extraordinary natural strength had become both an advantage and a limitation because Scathach's daughters had developed complementary specializations without fully learning the foundational principles required to function independently. Victor consequently began completing Pepper's martial education while encouraging her to become emotionally independent without diminishing her affection for Scathach.[4]
As their relationship deepened, Pepper became one of the women who entered Victor's expanding romantic family in her own right rather than remaining merely Ruby's adoptive sister. She was publicly identified alongside Ruby, Siena and Lacus as one of the Scarlett women who would marry him, later receiving the Blood Dragon transformation that reconstructed her former Vampire existence into the supernatural foundation of a genuine True Dragon. The transformation preserved compatible elements of her earlier Bloodline and developed abilities, allowing her inherited Water power and martial specialization to continue evolving rather than being replaced by an unrelated Draconic identity.[5][2]
Pepper subsequently attained Godhood and became publicly recognized as a Dragon Goddess. However, her Divine development also acquired an unusual cultural dimension following the transformation of Earth and the establishment of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Rather than concentrating exclusively upon combat or government, she dedicated considerable effort to restoring the modern entertainment lost during civilization's upheaval, recovering anime, manga, games, films and other cultural works while also developing altered continuations of fictional stories whose original conclusions she disliked. Her contributions became enormously popular among Imperial citizens, earning her the public titles Goddess of Culture, Goddess of Art and Goddess of Passion.[6]
These cultural titles possess religious significance because the Faith generated by the public can influence the development of a God's Divinities. [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood explains that sufficiently widespread worship associated with a particular Concept can eventually contribute to the awakening of corresponding Divine authority, while Victor deliberately guides the public presentation of his wives to cultivate desirable future Divinities. Nevertheless, the original recognition of Pepper as the Goddess of Culture, Art and Passion does not establish that all three Concepts had already awakened independently. They are confirmed public titles and prospective Faith-based developments rather than three separately verified completed Divinities.[6]
Pepper's importance within the Empire extends well beyond cultural preservation. As the civilization advanced through vast periods of accelerated Time, she quietly developed substantial expertise in Draconic Runes, supernatural technology, artificial stellar structures, Energy containment and experimental weapons. Her private research archive eventually accumulated hundreds of millions of data points, while her laboratories became sufficiently advanced to contain artificial stars and catastrophic Energy experiments inside multiple protected dimensions. Victor personally recognized the value of her unconventional creativity and ordered the researchers responsible for Polaris to involve her in improving the Empire's Galaxy-[[Destroyer]]-class strategic vessel, despite Scathach's concern that her daughter's ideas were frequently too dangerous to implement without supervision.[7][3]
Her extraordinary versatility distinguishes her from women whose importance is concentrated within a single field. Pepper is simultaneously an accomplished martial artist, a planetary-scale Water user, a Dragon Goddess, a cultural patron, a mother, an inventor and a researcher capable of manipulating supernatural Energy through advanced Runic systems. Her continued eccentricity and youthful behavior do not diminish these accomplishments. Instead, they reflect her unusual approach to development, since many of her most ambitious projects originate from a desire to recreate something entertaining or discover whether an apparently impossible fictional idea can be transformed into a functional supernatural technology.

### Physical Appearance
Pepper is an exceptionally beautiful woman with pale skin, long red hair and green eyes. During her original Noble Vampire period, she stood approximately 170 centimeters tall, making her taller than Lacus despite being younger. She possesses a curvaceous figure and youthful facial features, with an energetic manner of moving and speaking that often makes her appear considerably younger than her actual chronological age. Her green eyes also distinguish her from [[Scathach]] and several other members of the Scarlett family, whose traditional appearance is associated with red eyes and the inherited characteristics of their particular Vampire Bloodline.[8]
Pepper's general appearance reflects her position as an adopted member of the Scarlett family rather than a biological descendant of Scathach. Although she possesses the characteristic red hair associated with the household, her green eyes and Water-based supernatural inheritance originate from a different Noble Vampire family. Her individual features therefore preserve aspects of her original identity despite the enormous amount of Time she has spent living beneath Scathach's protection and eventually joining [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood Dragon dynasty.
Unlike Scathach, whose clothing frequently reinforces her image as an ancient warrior, Pepper is comfortable adopting modern styles and informal clothing. Her interests in games, anime, manga and entertainment influence her everyday presentation, while her facial expressions and body language generally appear more playful than those of her adoptive mother or older sisters. This relatively approachable appearance conceals considerable physical power, particularly because Pepper possessed exceptional natural strength even before she became a Dragon.[9]
Her appearance changed following Victor's transformation of his family into genuine [[Blood Dragons]]. Rather than merely acquiring temporary Dragon characteristics, Pepper's body and supernatural foundation were reconstructed into those of a True Dragon while retaining compatible features of her earlier existence. She consequently gained access to the physical transformations and Draconic manifestations associated with the Race, although she continues maintaining a familiar Humanoid appearance resembling the red-haired woman who originally lived in Scathach's mansion.[2]
Pepper later appears publicly in a much larger Dragon Goddess manifestation approximately 4.7 meters tall. She and Siena wear specially designed Divine clothing that emphasizes their status as wives of the Emperor, combining Draconic horns, wings and supernatural eyes with elegant ceremonial garments. The clothing is manufactured using advanced Imperial materials and designed to avoid restricting movement despite its elaborate appearance. The manifestation represents their public Divine and Imperial presentation rather than a permanent replacement of Pepper's ordinary Humanoid form.[10]
Despite the enormous periods of accelerated Time she experiences, Pepper retains a particularly youthful demeanor. During the incident involving her secret laboratory, Sylvia observes that Pepper still lacks the immediately recognizable maternal presence developed by Siena, Ruby and Lacus. The difference does not establish that Pepper is biologically younger than the other women or has failed to mature physically; it reflects the playful behavior and spontaneous expressions she continues displaying despite becoming an ancient Dragon Goddess and mother.[3]

### Personality
Pepper is cheerful, energetic, affectionate, eccentric, imaginative and frequently absent-minded. Her apparent simplicity is one of the most misleading aspects of her character because her unusual behavior coexists with exceptional intelligence and an increasingly sophisticated understanding of supernatural technology. She can become distracted by games, forget mundane responsibilities or leave objects scattered throughout an enormous residence while simultaneously maintaining experiments involving artificial stars, complex Draconic Runes and advanced dimensional barriers. Her absent-mindedness therefore concerns everyday organization and personal habits rather than a lack of intellectual ability.[3]
Pepper has been interested in entertainment since her earliest appearances. She enjoys anime, manga, games and fictional technology, sharing many of these interests with Ruby and eventually [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Unlike individuals who regard such activities as distractions from supernatural development, Pepper consistently treats them as valuable forms of creativity and recreation. Her enthusiasm becomes especially important after the establishment of the [[Elderblood Empire]] because she refuses to allow the enormous cultural achievements of the previous Human civilization to disappear during the transition toward a Galactic civilization.[6]
Her playful demeanor originally concealed severe childhood trauma. Pepper was present when extremist [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] attacked her biological Clan, murdered her parents and destroyed the home in which she had grown up. Although the passage of Time caused the exact faces and details of the incident to become increasingly indistinct, the emotional consequences remained. The memory of screams, destruction and the burned bodies of her family continued influencing her behavior, while [[Scathach]]'s arrival became inseparably associated with rescue and safety.[1]
Pepper's attachment to Scathach developed beyond the ordinary dependence of a young daughter upon her mother. She openly regarded Scathach as her refuge, meaning that the older Vampire represented the security and stability she had lost during the massacre. Long journeys away from the Scarlett territory consequently caused genuine emotional discomfort despite Pepper's considerable physical strength. Her reluctance was not simply laziness or unwillingness to explore unfamiliar places; it originated from an earlier experience in which her entire world had been destroyed without warning.[1]
Victor's understanding of the problem became important to Pepper's development. Rather than demanding that she abandon her attachment to Scathach, he recognized that loving her mother and relying upon her for emotional security were different matters. He encouraged Pepper to develop enough confidence and independence that remaining near Scathach would become a choice motivated by affection rather than a necessity created by fear. His training therefore addressed both Pepper's martial limitations and the psychological insecurity that had accompanied her for centuries.[1][4]
Pepper possesses considerable determination when something genuinely captures her interest. Although her earlier training suffered from a tendency to depend upon the strengths of her sisters, she became increasingly willing to experiment with unconventional techniques and develop new applications of her Water ability. Her interest in fictional powers provided an unexpected source of inspiration, allowing her to consider approaches that conventional martial artists might dismiss as impractical. She consequently accumulated ideas for new techniques and eventually began employing greater creativity in the development of her combat abilities.[11]
The same tendency eventually defines her approach to research. Pepper frequently begins with the assumption that a fictional object or apparently impossible idea would be enjoyable to reproduce, then applies genuine scientific and Runic knowledge to determine whether it can function. This mentality produces results considerably different from those of more conventional researchers because her initial objective is often entertainment rather than military usefulness. However, the resulting inventions can possess extraordinary practical value, particularly when fictional concepts are combined with advanced Imperial Energy systems and supernatural materials.[3]
Her creativity also creates genuine concerns. Scathach understands that Pepper possesses enough imagination to develop ideas capable of producing serious consequences for the Empire and its enemies, while Ruby and Velnorah have learned to record her suggestions without necessarily implementing every proposal. Victor nevertheless considers that imagination valuable and deliberately gives Pepper greater freedom during the development of Polaris. His decision reflects confidence in her abilities, but Scathach immediately joins the project to ensure that enthusiasm does not replace caution when working with a strategic weapon capable of affecting entire Galaxies.[7]
Pepper's cultural influence reflects another side of her personality. She does not simply reconstruct old entertainment without personal involvement; when she dislikes the ending of a fictional story, she sometimes develops an alternative conclusion that better reflects her own preferences. Her restorations are therefore partly preservation and partly creative reinterpretation. This behavior occasionally surprises Imperial audiences, but it also demonstrates her belief that entertainment should remain something enjoyable and emotionally meaningful rather than an untouchable historical artifact.[6]
Her attachment to Victor remains similarly affectionate and playful. Pepper preserves recordings and photographs of him dating back to his earliest supernatural life, including his first experiences in [[Nightingale]] and his participation in Vampire tournament battles. These materials become particularly valuable to the younger generation because they reveal a version of Victor who had not yet become the immensely powerful Emperor familiar to his daughters. Pepper's decision to preserve them demonstrates genuine sentimentality beneath her eccentricity and her appreciation for the personal history shared by the family.[3]
Motherhood does not fundamentally change her outward personality. Pepper remains playful and easily distracted, but she is capable of enforcing rules and recognizing dangerous behavior when her daughter becomes involved. She is generally one of the more lenient mothers in the household, preferring additional study and moderate restrictions rather than the extreme physical training imposed by Scathach. Nevertheless, she becomes irritated when Victor encourages Priscillia's recklessness, recognizing that his affection for their children can make discipline unnecessarily difficult.[12][3]
Pepper therefore possesses a personality defined by emotional warmth, eccentric creativity, unresolved memories of loss and extraordinary intellectual potential. Her development does not involve abandoning the playful interests that originally distinguished her from Scathach's more severe household. Instead, those interests become an important source of scientific achievement and cultural influence, demonstrating that her apparently childish enthusiasm can coexist with the responsibilities of an ancient Dragon Goddess, mother and Imperial researcher.

### Background
Pepper was born as the heiress of an influential Noble Vampire Clan in [[Nightingale]] whose hereditary ability was associated with Water. The family's strongest members possessed enough supernatural power to generate tidal waves capable of devastating entire countries, making its Bloodline both powerful and feared. Unlike the [[Scarlett Clan]]'s Ice-based inheritance, Pepper's original supernatural ability involved creating and controlling Water, a distinction that remained important throughout her later martial development.[1]
Her childhood was violently interrupted when extremist [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] infiltrated Nightingale and attacked the Clan. The assault resulted in the deaths of Pepper's biological parents and the destruction of much of the family residence. Although the exact number of victims is not established, Pepper personally remembered the bodies of her parents and the overwhelming destruction surrounding her, while the attackers continued searching for survivors after the initial massacre.[1]
[[Scathach]] arrived during the aftermath and immediately intervened. Her supernatural power froze the surrounding area, while the Hunters responsible for the massacre were eliminated. Pepper was rescued before the attackers could finish their work, and several additional members of her original Clan survived under Scathach's protection. Scathach subsequently exterminated the extremist department responsible for the attack, ensuring that the organization could no longer continue its operations against the surviving family.[1]
The remaining members of Pepper's original Clan eventually settled within Scarlett territory. Some maintained ordinary lives under the protection of the Countess, and Pepper continued meeting them occasionally despite having accepted Scathach as her mother. One survivor subsequently became Ruby's father, creating an additional connection between Pepper's original biological family and the household into which she had been adopted. The surviving Clan members therefore did not disappear completely, although Pepper herself permanently embraced the Scarlett surname and the identity of Scathach's daughter.[1]
Pepper became the third adopted daughter of Scathach, following Siena and Lacus. Ruby, Scathach's biological daughter, was born afterward, making Pepper the youngest adopted daughter but not the youngest of the four original Scarlett sisters. Each girl possessed different supernatural characteristics, and Scathach attempted to develop them through a foundational martial system intended to encourage cooperation and eventual individual mastery.[8][4]
Scathach divided four principal martial foundations among her daughters: Pepper received Strength, Siena Power, Lacus Speed and Ruby Defense. The complete system also included Control, which Scathach expected the sisters to discover after teaching one another their respective foundations. However, the girls instead developed a highly effective cooperative fighting style in which each relied upon the others to compensate for individual limitations. Although the arrangement made them extremely dangerous as a group, it prevented them from becoming the complete martial artists Scathach had originally intended.[4]
Pepper's specialization was particularly appropriate because she had possessed exceptional natural physical strength since childhood. Rather than relying primarily upon the supernatural amplification normally employed by Vampires, much of her strength originated from her own body. Scathach consequently emphasized the development of this characteristic, producing a fighter whose physical attacks were unusually powerful even when compared with the abilities of other Noble Vampires. However, her specialization also contributed to deficiencies in speed and overall combat balance, limitations [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually recognized and began correcting.[9][11]

### Synopsis

#### Vampire Society
Pepper entered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s life shortly after [[Scathach]] brought him to [[Nightingale]] for training. Victor had only recently become a Vampire and possessed considerable supernatural potential without the practical knowledge necessary to employ it efficiently. Pepper and Lacus helped introduce him to the Scarlett household and the basic abilities associated with Vampirism, while Scathach assumed responsibility for developing his martial foundation. Pepper's cheerful behavior and occasional absent-mindedness immediately distinguished her from the more severe members of the family, causing Victor to regard her as one of the household's least intimidating personalities.[13][14]
Despite her relatively relaxed behavior, Pepper possessed extensive practical knowledge of Noble Vampire abilities and was already a powerful combatant. Scathach's training had developed the extraordinary physical strength inherited from her original Clan, while her Water manipulation provided abilities that differed substantially from those of Ruby and Siena. Her role in Victor's early education consequently combined genuine supernatural expertise with the casual atmosphere she helped maintain inside the Scarlett residence.
Pepper and her sisters gradually became increasingly aware of the unusual relationship developing between Victor and Scathach. Although Victor originally entered the household as Ruby's husband and Scathach's disciple, the older Countess devoted extraordinary attention to his training and personal development. This complicated the existing family relationships, particularly because Siena, Lacus and Pepper had previously regarded Scathach as an almost untouchable maternal authority whose emotions rarely became visible to outsiders.
As Victor became more powerful and increasingly involved in the affairs of Nightingale's Noble Vampire families, Pepper began regarding him as part of the Scarlett household rather than simply an inexperienced outsider connected to Ruby. Their relationship developed through shared training, conversation and his growing recognition of the different personalities and abilities possessed by Scathach's daughters.

#### A New Pillar
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s growing responsibilities eventually brought the Scarlett family into the Human World. Following the threats involving the Inquisition, [[Scathach]] arrived with Siena, Lacus and Pepper and temporarily established themselves around the extensive underground complex beneath Victor's family residence. Their presence reunited the different branches of his growing household and provided additional opportunities for martial training.[8]
Victor subsequently fought Ruby, Siena, Lacus and Pepper simultaneously, confronting the cooperative combat system they had developed beneath Scathach's instruction. The sisters compensated for one another's weaknesses through their individual specializations, forcing Victor to respond to multiple attacks and defensive arrangements rather than fighting a single opponent. Pepper contributed the exceptional physical strength she had developed over centuries, while her sisters employed their own specialized techniques to support the formation.[15]
The battle demonstrated both the effectiveness and limitations of their training. Individually, each sister possessed extraordinary abilities, but the reliance upon complementary specializations prevented them from expressing the complete martial foundation Scathach intended them to acquire. Victor began recognizing that the sisters would benefit from developing the principles they had not originally been assigned, particularly because their individual survival could not always depend upon having every member of the formation nearby.
Pepper continued accompanying Victor and the Scarlett family during the political disputes surrounding [[Nightingale]]'s Count families, the Horseman and Rider Clans, and the increasing instability affecting the supernatural world. Her growing familiarity with Victor allowed their relationship to develop beyond the formal boundaries originally established by his marriage to Ruby.

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became increasingly involved in the development of [[Scathach]]'s daughters as his own martial abilities advanced. Recognizing that ordinary training was no longer sufficient, he began challenging Siena, Lacus and Pepper under unfamiliar conditions and forcing them to respond to techniques outside the patterns they had practiced for centuries. His training included changing weapons, alternating supernatural applications and emphasizing the importance of adapting to an opponent whose abilities could not be predicted through ordinary experience.[16][17]
Victor also recognized that Pepper's fighting style could benefit from specialized long-range equipment. Rather than attempting to transform her into a duplicate of Scathach, he commissioned Esther to create a pair of anti-material rifles suited to Pepper's exceptional physical strength and supernatural combat preferences. The weapons provided a method of attacking distant enemies without relying exclusively upon Water manipulation or close-quarters strikes, complementing her natural advantages while encouraging her to develop a broader approach to combat.[18][19]
Pepper's most important early personal development occurred when she spent an extended period away from Scathach while accompanying Victor and [[Nero]]. Although she had become an exceptionally powerful Vampire, she admitted that she had rarely remained so far from the Scarlett territory for such a long time. The separation forced her to confront the emotional insecurity that had remained since the destruction of her biological family, eventually encouraging her to explain the circumstances of the massacre and the importance Scathach possessed in her life.[1]
Pepper recalled the deaths of her parents, the destruction of her Clan and the appearance of Scathach among the ruins. Although many details had faded during the intervening century, the fear associated with the attack remained deeply rooted in her memories. She explained that Scathach had become her refuge, while Victor recognized that Pepper's emotional dependence upon her mother was connected to the same insecurity limiting her independent development.[1]
Victor responded by encouraging Pepper to distinguish affection from dependence. He did not consider her desire to remain close to Scathach inherently wrong, but believed that she should possess enough strength and confidence to live independently if circumstances required it. This principle extended into her martial education, where Pepper had become reliant upon the specialized abilities of her sisters instead of developing the complete foundation Scathach originally intended.[1][4]
Victor consequently began teaching Pepper the martial foundations she lacked. He explained that Scathach had deliberately divided Strength, Power, Speed and Defense among her daughters so that they would eventually teach one another and discover Control as the final integrating principle. Pepper's natural specialization in Strength was valuable, but the incomplete system limited her overall performance, particularly because her speed remained insufficient compared with what her physical development should have permitted.[4]
His demonstrations revealed that the footwork taught to Lacus could produce movement far faster than Pepper expected, even without relying upon Lightning or another elemental acceleration ability. Victor explained that the technique involved controlled breathing and precisely applied bursts of force through the legs, while more advanced practitioners could combine the movement with their own supernatural characteristics. Pepper recognized how much she still needed to learn, marking an important step toward becoming a more complete martial artist.[4]
The training also strengthened her personal relationship with Victor. He had become someone capable of understanding the fear beneath her playful behavior while providing practical guidance without treating her as weak or incapable. Pepper consequently began developing greater independence and confidence, allowing her attachment to Scathach to become less restrictive without diminishing the affection between them.

#### Demonic War
Pepper's developing martial abilities were tested during the Demonic invasion of [[Nightingale]]. [[Diablo]]'s forces opened portals across territories belonging to the [[Vampire Counts]], attacking settlements and attempting to exploit the temporary absence of several powerful supernatural leaders. Clan Scarlett became a direct target of the invasion, forcing Pepper, Ruby, Siena and Lacus to defend their territory alongside allied combatants.[20][21]
Siena had already evacuated the population into protected shelters before the attack, allowing the defenders to concentrate upon destroying the invading Demons without endangering the ordinary inhabitants. When [[Zagan]] and his legion entered Scarlett territory, the sisters combined their supernatural abilities into a coordinated area attack. Lacus produced an enormous cloud-like mass through her [[The Mist|Mist]] ability, while Pepper supplied the Water necessary to generate rainfall across the territory.[21]
Siena and Ruby subsequently incorporated their Ice powers into the formation, converting the Water and surrounding precipitation into a lethal battlefield-wide attack. The resulting snowfall froze Demons on contact, while additional thin Ice spikes descended upon the invading forces. The technique allowed the sisters to control enormous portions of the battlefield simultaneously, eliminating or incapacitating large numbers of enemies before they could approach the defended settlements.[21]
The attack demonstrated the extraordinary compatibility of their different powers. Pepper's Water provided the necessary material, Lacus supplied the cloud-like distribution system, Siena employed her Ice authority to dominate the battlefield, and Ruby contributed the precise supernatural control required to coordinate the effects. The combined technique was considered capable of destroying large cities and possessed considerable potential for controlling enemy movement across a wide area.[21]
The result also demonstrated that Pepper remained an important member of the Scarlett combat formation even after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] began developing her independent abilities. Her Water power complemented the elemental characteristics of her sisters, while the additional training she had received improved her ability to contribute effectively without relying exclusively upon physical strength. The battle reinforced the value of cooperation without eliminating the importance of developing individual mastery.
Pepper's physical strength continued attracting attention during this period. Ruby later explained that her own training had allowed her to lift approximately thirty tons with considerable effort, while Pepper possessed a lifting capacity of approximately forty tons before becoming a Dragon. More importantly, Ruby clarified that much of Pepper's extraordinary strength originated from her natural body rather than exclusively through supernatural amplification, distinguishing her from most Noble Vampires.[9]
Further development revealed that Pepper's exceptional strength remained accompanied by a comparatively weak speed foundation. Her attacks possessed enormous force, but her movement had not improved sufficiently to match the standards of a complete martial artist. She consequently concentrated more heavily upon training her speed and developing creative applications of Water, drawing inspiration from fictional abilities and techniques she had previously considered entertaining but had neglected to explore seriously.[11]
Pepper continued participating in battles and training alongside the Scarlett family as the supernatural world underwent further conflict. The experience gradually transformed her from an exceptionally strong but relatively sheltered Vampire into a combatant capable of operating under the pressure of large-scale warfare while retaining the interests and personality that distinguished her from the older warriors surrounding Victor.

#### King Among Kings
Pepper's relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] continued developing as [[Nightingale]]'s political structure became increasingly centered upon his growing power and the alliance of its major supernatural families. The original distinctions separating the Snow, Fulger and Scarlett Clans remained important historically, but the women who led those families gradually became part of a much larger household united through their relationships with Victor.
Pepper's personal growth was evident in her relationship with [[Scathach]]. Although she continued loving her adoptive mother deeply, her experience traveling beyond Scarlett territory and developing a more complete martial foundation had reduced the dependence associated with her childhood trauma. She was increasingly capable of acting independently and participating in the conflicts surrounding Victor without requiring Scathach's constant physical presence.
Her place within Victor's romantic family eventually became public when the women surrounding the original three wives were formally included in his expanding household. The Scarlett branch specifically included Ruby, Siena, Lacus and Pepper, establishing Pepper as one of Victor's wives in her own right rather than merely an adoptive sister connected to his existing marriage.[5]
The transition represented the culmination of a relationship that had developed over several stages. Pepper had initially assisted Victor during his earliest supernatural education, while he later became her martial instructor, emotional confidant and source of encouragement. Their growing affection was therefore based upon extended personal experience rather than an immediate political arrangement, and marriage allowed them to continue developing those relationships within the wider Elderblood family.
Pepper's personality remained recognizable despite the change in status. She continued enjoying anime, games and entertainment, while her unusual creativity increasingly influenced her approach to Water manipulation and combat. Her place among Victor's wives did not require her to imitate the behavior of Scathach or abandon the interests that had always distinguished her from her more severe relatives.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Pepper underwent a fundamental transformation when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] began converting his family into genuine [[Blood Dragons]]. The process involved more than granting temporary Draconic characteristics to existing supernatural beings; their bodies, Souls and racial foundations were reconstructed into the advanced existence of [[True Dragons]] through Victor's Essence and supernatural authority.[22][2]
Pepper successfully underwent Dragonization, replacing the former Noble Vampire foundation of her existence with a genuine Blood Dragon body. Her compatible abilities were preserved during the process rather than eliminated, allowing the Water inheritance of her original family, the martial techniques acquired beneath [[Scathach]] and Victor, and her accumulated supernatural knowledge to continue developing within the far more advanced Draconic framework.[2]
The change was particularly significant because Pepper had spent much of her early existence relying upon the protection of one of [[Nightingale]]'s strongest Vampires. Becoming a True Dragon provided a supernatural foundation vastly beyond the limitations of the society in which her first family had been destroyed, giving her the potential to confront dangers that would have been impossible for her original Clan to withstand.
The transformation also introduced unfamiliar supernatural systems. True Dragons possess compatibility with Draconic Language and Runes, allowing them to impose meaning upon Energy and interact with Reality through the supernatural characteristics of their Race. Pepper and the other newly transformed women consequently began learning abilities that had not existed within their former Vampire physiology, while their individual characteristics continued influencing the ways those powers developed.[23]
Pepper's later research demonstrates that this new compatibility eventually became one of the most important aspects of her supernatural development. Although she was already inventive as a Vampire, the combination of Draconic intelligence, Runic compatibility and the resources of Victor's Empire provided a foundation for projects extending far beyond conventional elemental techniques or combat equipment.

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Thousands of subjective years of accelerated development transformed Pepper's responsibilities within the expanding Elderblood family. She and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually had a biological daughter, [[Priscillia Penny Elderblood]], whose personality combined Pepper's enthusiasm and imaginative interests with Victor's confidence, cunning and tendency to pursue entertaining ideas despite obvious complications.[24]
Pepper also attained genuine Godhood and became recognized as a Dragon Goddess. Her public identity acquired an unusual cultural dimension after Victor began integrating and transforming the civilizations under his control. The destruction and reconstruction associated with Earth's transformation had caused enormous quantities of Human entertainment, art and historical material to become inaccessible or disappear entirely. Pepper dedicated herself to restoring modern popular culture, while Siena concentrated more heavily upon classical art, historical writings, ancient music and traditional cultural heritage.[6]
Pepper's work included recovering or reconstructing films, anime, manga, games and numerous fictional series associated with the Human civilization preceding the Empire. However, she did not always reproduce their original conclusions unchanged. When she disliked an ending, she sometimes developed an alternative version that provided what she considered a more satisfying conclusion for the characters. The changes reflected her personal investment in entertainment and her belief that fictional stories should produce enjoyment rather than exist exclusively as historical records.[6]
Her restoration of adult-oriented entertainment also attracted considerable attention, contributing to the public's humorous association of Pepper with Passion. Citizens began calling her the Goddess of Culture, Art and Passion, while Siena received overlapping titles connected with Art, Culture and Knowledge. The distinction between them reflected their different contributions: Pepper became associated primarily with modern entertainment and popular culture, while Siena preserved the historical and classical achievements of earlier civilizations.[6]
The titles possessed genuine supernatural potential because Faith can influence the development of Divine Concepts. [[Aphrodite]] explained that concentrated worship associated with particular ideas could eventually help a sufficiently powerful God awaken corresponding Divinities. Victor was deliberately cultivating such associations among his wives, recognizing that cultural influence and public perception could become sources of Divine development rather than merely political prestige.[6]
Pepper and Siena later appeared publicly in approximately 4.7-meter Dragon Goddess forms, wearing specially designed Divine clothing and displaying the advanced physical characteristics of their Blood Dragon existences. Their images circulated through Imperial media, contributing to the enormous popularity they had acquired through cultural restoration. Pepper's appearance became especially recognizable as the public face of modern entertainment, while ordinary citizens established devotional practices associated with the Goddesses responsible for recovering the lost works of Human civilization.[10]
Imperial technological development also accelerated the production of new cultural material. Advanced systems reduced the time required to create anime and related entertainment, with some twelve-episode productions becoming possible within only a few weeks depending upon their complexity. Pepper remained actively involved in the development of these industries because her work originated from genuine personal enthusiasm rather than an obligation to maintain her religious titles.[25]
Despite her increasingly civilian public identity, Pepper remained an exceptionally powerful combatant. Victor explicitly identified her as an example of why none of his wives should be considered weak, stating that she possessed enough Water power to flood an entire planet if she chose. This represented a substantial progression from the country-devastating tidal waves associated with the strongest members of her original Clan, demonstrating that her inherited Water ability had continued evolving after Dragonization and Godhood.[26]
The statement should be understood as an assessment of her available power rather than an accomplished planetary destruction feat. Although the capacity to flood a planet would allow catastrophic environmental devastation, no individual battle establishes that Pepper actually submerged an inhabited planet or destroyed one through Water manipulation. Her status as a Dragon Goddess and her inherited Water Bloodline therefore remain distinct from hypothetical or unstated achievements.

#### Those Who Rule Reality
The later Imperial era revealed that Pepper's cultural activities represented only one part of her increasingly extraordinary development. As [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s civilization advanced through immense periods of accelerated Time, she quietly accumulated extensive technical knowledge and began undertaking independent research projects involving supernatural materials, advanced Runes, artificial stellar structures and experimental weapons. Her public reputation remained associated with entertainment, but her private activities increasingly resembled those of the Empire's most advanced researchers.
Her inventiveness attracted Victor's attention during the development of Polaris, one of the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s most classified strategic weapons. Polaris had been constructed as a Galaxy-[[Destroyer]]-class Divine Artifact in the form of an enormous spacecraft, possessing systems capable of interacting with Reality, Space, Time and Destruction-related concepts. Its most dangerous weapon could channel the Concept of <END> through designated supernatural conductors, allowing the vessel to erase targets through an Authority far beyond ordinary destructive Energy.[7]
The principal engineering difficulty involved the firing mechanism itself. A complete discharge of END would erase the cannon through which the attack passed, making conventional reinforcement ineffective. Rather than attempting indefinitely to create an indestructible component, the researchers adopted a system of replaceable cannons that could be exchanged after use. Victor subsequently directed Velnorah, Aline and Ruby to continue increasing Polaris's lethality and specifically instructed them to involve Pepper in the project.[7]
Pepper reacted with enormous enthusiasm to the opportunity. [[Scathach]], however, immediately questioned whether allowing her daughter unrestricted access to such a dangerous project was wise. Pepper had developed numerous ideas that experienced researchers considered extremely effective but too dangerous to implement without substantial supervision. Ruby and Velnorah had learned to preserve her proposals for later consideration while refusing to pursue some of the more extreme possibilities directly.[7]
Victor nevertheless trusted Pepper's creativity and gave her broad permission to develop additional systems. He also instructed the researchers to produce another Polaris for his personal use, reminding Pepper that he might eventually be the person operating the weapons she designed. Scathach consequently volunteered to join the development team herself, largely to ensure that Pepper's imagination did not produce consequences that even the experienced Imperial researchers would struggle to contain.[7]
Pepper's involvement in highly classified Imperial research eventually attracted the attention of her daughter Priscillia. During a gathering in the castle library, Priscillia revealed to Rosemary and Sylvia that Pepper was working on a secret project unavailable through the normal Imperial databases. Because many members of the Royal Family possessed access to enormous quantities of classified information, the absence of meaningful details immediately suggested that the undertaking belonged to the Empire's most restricted category of projects.[12]
Priscillia became convinced that her mother's involvement provided an opportunity for her to join the project, despite lacking any direct invitation. She consequently decided to investigate Pepper's private residence, attempting to discover the nature of the research before the adults could intervene. Rosemary and Sylvia initially warned her that approaching such a heavily protected area was dangerous, but eventually followed because they refused to abandon their sister once she had committed herself to the scheme.[12]
Victor detected the intrusion almost immediately. Rather than stopping the girls, he observed their progress with amusement and recognized that their willingness to support one another reflected the solidarity he had encouraged throughout the younger generation. Priscillia employed hundreds of advanced Draconic Runes to penetrate the security surrounding Pepper's residence, demonstrating extraordinary skill in manipulating the supernatural structures used to protect the private areas of the Imperial household.[12]
The effectiveness of Priscillia's techniques also revealed that Pepper had not updated some of her older defensive systems despite possessing the knowledge necessary to do so. Victor recognized that his daughter's success exposed potential weaknesses within the family's private security and considered strengthening the barriers. Nevertheless, the defenses remained exceptionally sophisticated compared with ordinary supernatural security systems, since the Elderblood household required protection against the advanced senses and conceptual powers possessed by its own residents.[12]
Pepper's apparent private room was revealed to be an enormous spatially expanded residence measuring approximately 2.8 million square meters. The area contained forests, a river, a waterfall, natural springs and an extensive self-contained ecosystem, functioning more like a private world than a conventional room. Despite the extraordinary infrastructure available to her, Pepper had allowed considerable disorder to accumulate throughout the residence, prompting Sylvia to complain that someone possessing such advanced Runic knowledge should be capable of maintaining perfect cleanliness without depending upon ordinary household assistance.[3]
The girls eventually entered a more heavily protected dimensional area beneath the residence. The deeper laboratory possessed additional security measures and automatically alerted Pepper once its boundary was crossed. Pepper immediately attempted to teleport back, but Victor intercepted the movement and redirected her to himself, deliberately preventing her from ending the investigation before the girls discovered the extent of her hidden research.[3]
Pepper reacted with frustration when she realized that Priscillia, Rosemary and Sylvia had entered her private territory. Although she was accustomed to Victor indulging their daughters, she considered the intrusion a serious violation of personal boundaries and objected to his decision to encourage it. Victor nevertheless remained amused and persuaded Pepper to observe the situation, revealing how much he enjoyed witnessing the intelligence and curiosity displayed by their children.[3]
The girls discovered an enormous white sphere of Energy surrounded by complex scientific equipment and thousands of Runes. Their examination revealed that Pepper had been working on an artificial stellar experiment, recorded in her research notes under the title "Creating a supernova in the palm of my hand." Rather than being a harmless theoretical proposal, the project had already reached a functioning stage in which Pepper had successfully created and stabilized an artificial star using hundreds of carefully arranged Runes.[3]
Pepper had temporarily obtained assistance from an unnamed Sun Goddess during the development process. She subsequently erased the helper's memory to preserve the secrecy of the experiment, acknowledging in her own notes that [[Amaterasu]] possessed considerably greater expertise concerning Solar phenomena but that the artificial star she had produced was sufficient for her intended purpose. This distinction demonstrates that Pepper's accomplishment relied upon technological and Runic engineering rather than an independently established personal Divinity governing the Sun.[3]
Her next planned stage was substantially more dangerous. Having stabilized the artificial star, Pepper intended to feed it additional Energy until the resulting system could produce an output comparable to the Energy of one billion exploding suns. The figure represented her intended Phase Four target rather than a completed demonstration, since the available notes establish that the artificial star had been stabilized but do not confirm the successful achievement of the projected billion-sun output.[3]
Pepper had nevertheless prepared extensive safeguards. The artificial star existed inside a dedicated dimensional containment structure designed to absorb a catastrophic release of Energy if the experiment failed. An additional outer dimension provided another protective layer, intended to prevent any surviving Energy from reaching the inhabited world outside. Her design demonstrated advanced understanding of high-energy containment and the practical dangers associated with stellar-scale experimentation, contradicting the assumption that her research was merely reckless curiosity without scientific preparation.[3]
The laboratory's research archive was similarly extraordinary. Rosemary examined Pepper's computers and discovered hundreds of millions of individual data points containing technical calculations, experimental records and complicated Runic arrangements. Although Rosemary possessed the advanced cognition of a genuine True Dragon and could understand unfamiliar technology with extraordinary speed, she recognized that the amount of information was too extensive to absorb casually. The discovery revealed that Pepper had been accumulating sophisticated technical knowledge over an enormous period rather than suddenly developing these abilities during a brief interval.[3]
The girls subsequently discovered another hidden dimension containing a chamber explicitly named the "Room for Fun Projects". The space housed a massive collection of experimental weapons, armor, alien machinery, rare ores, exotic crystals and supernatural artifacts acquired throughout Pepper's travels and research. Some objects were preserved because she found them interesting, while others represented fully functional devices created through the combination of Imperial technology, supernatural materials and ideas inspired by fiction.[3]
Among the objects were plasma swords resembling fictional energy blades. Unlike harmless replicas, these weapons used Energy derived from Pepper's artificial star and produced genuine plasma blades. The collection also contained a wrist-mounted weapon combining a concealed blade with Basilisk venom and supernatural Eyes capable of petrifying targets. The devices demonstrated Pepper's ability to transform fictional weapon concepts into functional supernatural technology rather than merely reproducing their outward appearance.[3]
The chamber also contained photographs and recordings documenting Victor's earlier life. The material included images from his first experiences in [[Nightingale]], his travels through different supernatural territories and footage of his earlier tournament battles. Priscillia and her sisters regarded the collection as exceptionally valuable because they had grown up knowing Victor primarily as the God Emperor, making records of his comparatively inexperienced Vampire years a rare opportunity to understand the history of their family.[3]
Anna eventually discovered the girls inside Pepper's private collection. Entering another mother's protected territory without permission was a serious violation of the household's rules, particularly because the chamber contained genuine weapons and dangerous supernatural technology. Anna considered imposing approximately one year of Scathach's personal training, a punishment the daughters regarded with considerable fear because of the extraordinary severity associated with her martial instruction.[3]
Pepper intervened and reduced the punishment to six additional months of lessons, reasoning that the girls had not actually damaged anything within her collection. Although she objected to their intrusion, she preferred correction over an excessively severe punishment and reminded them that they should simply ask if they wished to examine something belonging to her. Her decision reinforced her reputation as one of the more lenient mothers of the Elderblood household, while also demonstrating that she was capable of setting boundaries when her daughter behaved irresponsibly.[3]
The incident revealed the extraordinary breadth of Pepper's achievements. Her public identity as the Goddess of Culture concealed an extensive history of supernatural research, while her private hobbies had developed into functioning technologies capable of manipulating stellar Energy and containing catastrophic experiments. Her work demonstrated that cultural imagination and scientific achievement were not mutually exclusive, since many of her most unusual inventions originated directly from the fictional stories she had spent centuries enjoying.
By the later Imperial era, Pepper had consequently become far more than the young Vampire Victor originally encountered in Scathach's mansion. She remained affectionate, playful and occasionally absent-minded, but her accumulated accomplishments included planetary-scale Water abilities, genuine Dragon Godhood, advanced Runecraft, artificial stellar engineering, cultural restoration and participation in the development of some of the Empire's most dangerous strategic weapons.

### Equipment and Possessions

Pepper possesses a pair of specialized anti-material rifles commissioned by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] from Esther. The weapons were designed to complement her exceptional physical strength and provide a ranged combat option capable of functioning alongside her Water manipulation and close-quarters martial techniques. Their acquisition represented Victor's recognition that Pepper's abilities were sufficiently distinctive to justify equipment suited specifically to her rather than the weapons traditionally favored by [[Scathach]] and the other Scarlett women.[18][19]

#### Private Research Laboratory
Pepper maintains a heavily protected laboratory within a nested dimensional structure located inside her spatially expanded private residence. The laboratory contains advanced scientific equipment, sophisticated Runic arrangements, artificial stellar systems and multiple defensive layers intended to prevent catastrophic experimental failures from spreading into the surrounding inhabited environment. Its existence is concealed behind additional security measures that automatically alert Pepper when unauthorized individuals enter.[3]

One of Pepper's most ambitious private projects is an artificial star created and stabilized through hundreds of carefully arranged Runes. The successful stabilization represents a confirmed achievement, while the subsequent objective involved supplying enough additional Energy to produce an output comparable to approximately one billion exploding suns. The proposed maximum output had not been confirmed as completed when the project was discovered, and the experiment therefore should not be treated as proof that Pepper personally released such an attack.[3]

Pepper maintains an enormous private collection of experimental weapons, armor, rare minerals, alien machinery, exotic supernatural materials and objects inspired by fictional entertainment. Although the chamber's name reflects her playful personality, many of its contents are genuine functional weapons developed through advanced Imperial technology and Runecraft. The collection includes stellar-powered plasma blades and a wrist-mounted device incorporating Basilisk venom and supernatural petrification effects.[3]

Pepper possesses photographs and recordings documenting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s early supernatural life, including his first experiences in [[Nightingale]], journeys through other territories and historical tournament battles. These records preserve a period of his existence preceding the establishment of the [[Elderblood Empire]] and consequently possess enormous sentimental value for his biological daughters.[3]

### Relationships

#### Scathach Elderblood Scarlett
[[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] is Pepper's adoptive mother and the defining figure of her childhood. [[Scathach]] rescued Pepper after extremist [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] destroyed her biological Clan, eliminated the attackers and adopted the surviving girl into the Scarlett household. The rescue created an emotional connection considerably deeper than ordinary gratitude because Pepper had lost her home and biological parents immediately before Scathach appeared.[1]
Pepper consequently regarded Scathach as her refuge, associating her mother's presence with safety and stability. Even after more than a century of training, extended separation from Scathach remained emotionally difficult, particularly when Pepper traveled to unfamiliar places far from Scarlett territory. Their relationship therefore combined genuine maternal affection with an insecurity created by Pepper's traumatic early experiences.[1]
Scathach also developed Pepper's exceptional natural physical strength through her foundational martial system, assigning her the Strength specialization while her other daughters developed complementary principles. Pepper eventually began correcting the limitations of this training with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s assistance, allowing her to become a more independent fighter without diminishing her respect for Scathach.[4]
Their relationship remains recognizably maternal throughout the later Imperial era. Scathach understands Pepper's eccentric creativity and becomes concerned when Victor grants her broad freedom to develop weapons for Polaris. She consequently joins the research team largely to supervise her daughter, demonstrating that Pepper's extraordinary intelligence and Divine status have not eliminated the affectionate and occasionally exasperated dynamic between them.[7]

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] originally entered Pepper's life as Ruby's newly transformed husband. During their earliest interactions, Pepper possessed greater practical Vampire knowledge and helped explain his supernatural abilities, while he remained an inexperienced fighter learning beneath [[Scathach]]. Their relationship gradually reversed as Victor developed extraordinary martial skill and began providing instruction to Pepper and her sisters.[13][4]
Victor recognized both the practical limitations of Pepper's specialization and the emotional insecurity associated with her childhood. He encouraged her to develop Speed, Defense, Power and Control without abandoning her natural advantage in Strength, while also helping her become capable of functioning independently from Scathach. His support established a degree of trust that allowed Pepper to discuss the painful memories she normally concealed beneath her cheerful personality.[1][4]
Victor also supported Pepper's individual fighting preferences. His decision to commission anti-material rifles from Esther demonstrated that he wished to strengthen her existing combat style rather than force her to imitate Scathach's preferred weapons or techniques. Their growing companionship eventually developed into romance, and Pepper was publicly included among the Scarlett women who joined Victor's marriage and expanding Clan.[18][19][5]
Their later relationship remains affectionate and playful despite enormous periods of accelerated Time. Victor appreciates Pepper's inventiveness and encourages her experimental interests, eventually involving her in Polaris despite the concerns expressed by more conventional researchers. Pepper, in turn, trusts Victor's judgment but occasionally criticizes his tendency to indulge their daughter excessively, particularly when Priscillia becomes involved in dangerous or unauthorized activities.[7][3]

#### Ruby Elderblood Scarlett
[[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] is Pepper's adoptive younger sister and one of her closest companions within the Scarlett family. Although Pepper was the youngest child adopted by [[Scathach]], Ruby was born afterward, making her the youngest of the four original sisters overall. The women share interests in anime, manga, games and modern entertainment, providing a cultural connection distinct from the martial traditions that dominate much of the Scarlett household.[8]
Pepper's original biological Clan also possesses an additional connection to Ruby because one survivor of the massacre eventually became Ruby's father. Their relationship therefore involves both their upbringing as daughters of Scathach and a historical connection between Pepper's original family and the later [[Scarlett Bloodline]].[1]
Their interests eventually develop into complementary professional roles. Ruby becomes one of the Empire's principal technological researchers, while Pepper undertakes independent experiments inspired by fictional technology and advanced Runic systems. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later places both women within the expanded Polaris development team, allowing their expertise to contribute to one of the Empire's greatest strategic projects.[7]

#### Siena Scarlett Elderblood
[[Siena Scarlett Elderblood]] is Pepper's eldest adoptive sister and one of the more mature members of [[Scathach]]'s household. Their martial specializations differ, with Siena originally receiving Power while Pepper concentrated upon Strength. These complementary foundations made them effective teammates but also contributed to the incomplete individual development [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later attempted to correct.[4]
Their later cultural contributions likewise become complementary. Siena concentrates upon classical art, historical works, traditional music and ancient cultural heritage, while Pepper focuses primarily upon modern entertainment and popular culture. Their combined efforts help preserve the diverse cultural achievements of Humanity during the Empire's transformation into a much more advanced civilization.[6]

#### Lacus Scarlett Elderblood
[[Lacus Scarlett Elderblood]] is Pepper's older adoptive sister and one of her principal early training partners. Lacus specialized in Speed while Pepper developed Strength, creating a natural contrast between their preferred fighting styles. Their cooperation allowed the sisters to compensate for individual weaknesses, but [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually recognized that both would benefit from learning the martial foundations they had not originally mastered.[4]
Pepper and Lacus trained together beneath Victor's instruction, confronting increasingly complex techniques and opponents capable of exploiting their habitual reliance upon teamwork. Their relationship remained affectionate throughout the subsequent development of the Scarlett family, with both women eventually becoming wives of Victor and members of his expanded Blood Dragon lineage.[17][5]

#### Priscillia Penny Elderblood
[[Priscillia Penny Elderblood]] is Pepper and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s biological daughter. She inherits Pepper's enthusiasm and fascination with entertaining ideas alongside Victor's cunning, confidence and willingness to pursue unconventional opportunities. Her unusual personality becomes particularly apparent when she learns that Pepper is involved in a secret Imperial research project and immediately decides that she should participate despite lacking permission.[24][12]
Priscillia eventually penetrates the security surrounding Pepper's private residence through hundreds of advanced Draconic Runes, accompanied by Rosemary and Sylvia. Their investigation reveals the extent of Pepper's scientific abilities, including the artificial star, extensive research archive and private collection of functional supernatural weapons. Priscillia is astonished by the discovery, realizing that the playful woman she knows as her mother possesses technical knowledge far beyond what she previously imagined.[12][3]
Pepper initially attempts to prevent the intrusion but is deliberately distracted by Victor, who wishes to observe the girls' progress. When Anna eventually catches them, Pepper intervenes to reduce their punishment from approximately a year of [[Scathach]]'s training to six additional months of lessons. Her decision demonstrates a comparatively lenient approach to motherhood, although she still insists that Priscillia respect the privacy and dangerous equipment belonging to other members of the family.[3]

#### Rosemary Lavina Elderblood and Sylvia Elderblood
[[Rosemary Lavina Elderblood]] and [[Sylvia Elderblood]] are Pepper's daughters within the communal structure of the Elderblood household, although their biological mothers are Ruby and Siena respectively. Both accompany Priscillia during the investigation of Pepper's private research, initially warning her that the plan is likely to end badly but ultimately refusing to abandon their sister.[12]
The three girls discover Pepper's artificial star, hidden weapons collection and historical photographs of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], becoming increasingly interested in the scientific and supernatural achievements concealed within her residence. Pepper treats all three as her daughters when deciding their punishment, reinforcing the communal maternal relationships maintained among Victor's wives and biological children.[3]

Her abilities developed through several distinct stages, beginning with her original Water-based Noble Vampire Bloodline, continuing through extensive martial training beneath [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] and Victor, and eventually expanding through her transformation into a genuine True Dragon and attainment of Godhood. Following the destruction of that Clan, Scathach adopted Pepper and trained her alongside Siena, Lacus and Ruby. Pepper specialized in Strength within Scathach's foundational martial system, allowing her to develop exceptional natural physical power while leaving deficiencies in Speed and the other complementary principles. Pepper's Dragonization fundamentally increased her racial potential without eliminating the compatible powers she possessed as a Vampire.
