(Lore adapted from *My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires*.)

#### Debut
Chapter 297
Calling our kind Gods is an insult to True Gods like [[Hestia]], who is kind to everyone. We are just another species of opportunistic, greedy, lustful, and naively prideful Demons.
—[[Persephone]] to Nyx Elderblood, Chapter 641
Persephone Elderblood, formerly known as Persephone and later Persephone Alucard, is an Olympian Goddess, the former Queen and eventual True Ruler of the [[Greek Underworld]], a Blood Dragon Goddess, and one of the Soul-Married wives of [[Victor Elderblood]]. She is the daughter of [[Zeus]] and [[Demeter]], the former wife of [[Hades]], and the mother of [[Melinoë]], [[Macaria]] and an unnamed daughter with Victor.
For much of her Divine life, Persephone possessed the title of Queen of the Underworld without truly governing it. Hades controlled the realm while severely restricting Persephone's freedom, and her isolation contributed heavily to the possessiveness, arrogance and emotional immaturity she displayed during her earliest appearances. Her obsessive relationship with [[Adonis Snow]] eventually connected her to Victor after Adonis deliberately allowed himself to be consumed rather than permit Persephone to claim his Soul after death.[3][4]
Persephone's greatest personal transformation occurred during the Demonic War. After Hades abandoned both [[Olympus]] and the responsibilities attached to the Greek Underworld, [[The Judges of The Abyss]] subjected Persephone to an extreme trial intended to determine whether she possessed the impartiality required of a legitimate Ruler of an afterlife.[5][6] Persephone passed and ceased being a ceremonial Queen, becoming the true Ruler recognized and supported by the Underworld itself.[7]
The collapse of the old Greek order subsequently forced Persephone to confront the damage created by her relationships with Hades and Adonis. She acknowledged that her attachment to Hades had originated from abduction and captivity, described it as a form of Stockholm Syndrome, and accepted responsibility for the way she had treated Adonis.[8] After receiving closure regarding Adonis and joining Victor's expanding family, she was later reconstructed as a genuine Blood Dragon while retaining the Divine identity and experiences that defined her.[9] Her relationship with Victor culminated in a permanent [[Soul Marriage]] in Chapter 930.[1]
In the later Imperial era, Persephone's experience administering the dead became useful far beyond the Greek Underworld. She participated in the administration of the enormous infernal and Soul-processing systems incorporated into Victor's civilization and remained an active member of the Elderblood household as it developed into the ruling dynasty of the [[Elderblood Empire]].[10]

### Physical Appearance
[[Persephone]] is an exceptionally beautiful Goddess with long black hair, black eyes and pale skin. Her appearance carries the naturally regal atmosphere associated with an ancient Olympian Goddess and Queen. During her earlier life in the [[Greek Underworld]] she was not constantly dressed for war or ceremony and could be seen within her residence wearing comparatively ordinary dark clothing.
Persephone's appearance became considerably more imposing after completing the Judges' trial. The change reflected the distinction between possessing the title of Queen and actually becoming a Ruler acknowledged by the Underworld.[7]

#### True Ruler of the Underworld
Upon awakening as the legitimate Ruler of the [[Greek Underworld]], [[Persephone]]'s clothing was replaced by a combination of elegant black attire and dark armor. [[The Helm]] of [[Hades]] manifested around her head before changing into a dark crown with a red jewel at its center. Her already pale complexion became even paler, while her black hair appeared darker beneath the influence of the Underworld.[7]
Her transformation was accompanied by a drastic change in presence. The previously comparatively ordinary Goddess now carried the unmistakable authority of the realm's sovereign, to the point that the Underworld itself reacted to her awakening.[7]

#### Blood Dragon Goddess
[[Persephone]] later underwent [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s large-scale Dragon transformation alongside numerous members of his family. Rather than simply receiving superficial Draconic traits, her body and existence were reconstructed into that of a genuine Blood Dragon and True Dragon.[9][11]
The transformation did not erase the Goddess Persephone had been. Her Divine nature, identity and accumulated characteristics were incorporated into the new Draconic foundation, resulting in a Blood Dragon Goddess rather than an entirely different person replacing the Olympian Goddess.[12]

### Personality
[[Persephone]]'s personality underwent one of the more pronounced developments among the surviving members of the former [[Greek Pantheon]]. Her earliest behavior was heavily shaped by isolation, Divine privilege and relationships in which possession was repeatedly confused with affection.
Despite having lived for an enormous period of time, early Persephone was emotionally immature in several important respects. [[Hades]] abducted her while she was young, forced her into the [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] and restricted her ability to leave. Her life therefore developed within an extremely limited environment dominated by a possessive husband and the culture of an ancient Pantheon whose Gods rarely had to answer to anyone for their behavior. Persephone herself later recognized that her feelings toward Hades had developed from those circumstances rather than from a healthy beginning.[8]
Her treatment of [[Adonis Snow]] demonstrated the worst expression of that mentality. Persephone became intensely possessive of him and regarded her own desire as sufficient justification to interfere with his freedom, share control over his life with [[Aphrodite]] and place a Curse upon his Soul so that he would eventually return to her after death.[4][13] Adonis' refusal to accept that situation was initially something Persephone struggled to comprehend because she had spent so long treating Divine desire as authority.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was one of the first people capable of confronting that worldview without being intimidated by her status. He possessed Adonis' memories but refused to become a substitute for him, rejected Persephone's claim over Adonis' remains and repeatedly treated her as a person whose behavior could be challenged rather than as an untouchable Goddess. Their early conversations were openly hostile, but they forced Persephone to encounter interpretations of Adonis she had avoided for centuries.[13][14]
Her first major step toward genuine change occurred before she became the True Ruler. The pressure of the wider supernatural war forced her to actually perform the work attached to being Queen, while comments from Aphrodite regarding self-respect remained in her thoughts. Persephone eventually realized that endlessly pursuing Adonis through Victor was humiliating both herself and the memory she claimed to love. She voluntarily abandoned that pursuit and removed the inherited Curse from Victor.[15][16]
The trial of [[The Judges of The Abyss]] completed the transformation that ordinary reflection had begun. Persephone was deliberately placed in situations where love, status and personal preference could not determine her judgment. The trial forced her to choose between people such as [[Demeter]] and Adonis and later confronted her with her own daughters, making the position of Ruler emotionally real rather than ceremonial.[17] By the time she awakened, Persephone understood that governing the dead required placing the function of the afterlife above the whims of the Goddess sitting on its throne.
Afterward, she became considerably more critical of the entitlement common among Gods. Her condemnation of Olympian hypocrisy explicitly included herself; she did not portray her own past behavior as morally superior simply because she had changed. This capacity for self-criticism became particularly important after the fall of [[Olympus]], when she admitted to Agnes and the others that her treatment of Adonis had been wrong and accepted that they had every reason to hate her.[7][8]
Persephone nevertheless did not become mild or submissive. She remains proud, sharp-tongued, possessive, jealous and fully capable of behaving theatrically when something catches her interest. Her friendship with [[Hecate]], for example, includes open arguments and childish disputes despite Persephone technically being Hecate's Queen.[18] What changed was not the disappearance of her personality but her willingness to recognize limits, responsibility and the autonomy of others.
Her relationship with Victor reflects this distinction. Persephone is attracted to Victor's own extreme possessiveness, but Victor is capable of resisting her and possesses no need to submit to her authority. Their eventual marriage was therefore entered into knowingly by both rather than produced through abduction, dependence or an attempt to trap the other person's Soul.[19][1]
By the later Imperial era, Persephone had become thoroughly integrated into the Elderblood household. During a gathering of Victor's enormous family, she and Aphrodite agreed with [[Gaia]]'s observation that the old Greek Pantheon's endless family problems had largely been caused by the Gods themselves, showing how far Persephone's view of her former society had changed.[20]

### Early Life
[[Persephone]] was born to [[Zeus]] and his sister [[Demeter]], making her a member of the younger Olympian generation descended from the primordial and Titan lineages of the [[Greek Pantheon]]. Despite her Divine birth, much of her early life became defined by the [[Greek Underworld]] rather than [[Olympus|Mount Olympus]].
While Persephone was still comparatively young, her uncle [[Hades]] abducted her and brought her into the Underworld. He forced Persephone into marriage and was intensely possessive, strongly restricting when and how she could leave his territory. Persephone eventually developed emotional attachment toward him and spent an enormous portion of her life as his Queen, but she later looked back upon the origin of their relationship with considerably less romanticism, describing the attachment created by her captivity as Stockholm Syndrome.[8]
Persephone and Hades eventually had at least two daughters, [[Melinoë]] and [[Macaria]]. Despite the royal title, however, Persephone herself later admitted that she had never truly functioned as the Underworld's sovereign. Hades remained the actual Ruler while Persephone possessed a position and multiple Divine titles without the experience, mentality or authority required to administer the realm independently.[7]
During one of the periods in which she left the Underworld, Persephone encountered [[Adonis Snow]]. His extraordinary beauty immediately attracted her attention. [[Aphrodite]] had already discovered Adonis and had enhanced his beauty further, creating a rivalry between the two Goddesses. Rather than beginning a direct Divine conflict over him, they reached an arrangement that divided Adonis' time between them.
The arrangement considered the desires of the Goddesses far more than those of Adonis. Persephone eventually placed a supernatural Curse upon him so that his Soul would return to her after his death, ensuring that even mortality would not allow him to permanently escape her possession.[4][13]
Adonis came to despise the control exercised over him by both Goddesses. That resentment remained one of the defining experiences of his life and eventually became the reason he made an extraordinary final request of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]: rather than die normally and risk his Soul falling into Persephone's hands, Adonis wanted Victor to consume him completely.[3]

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
[[Persephone]]'s direct involvement in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s story began through [[Adonis Snow]]. When Adonis' deteriorating condition made his death unavoidable, he explained the Curse Persephone had placed upon him and asked Victor to devour him. Adonis preferred being absorbed into Victor—where his memories and parts of his existence could remain without independent continuity—to the possibility of spending eternity under Persephone's control.[3]
Victor reluctantly honored the request. Because his Progenitor nature allowed him to absorb far more than ordinary blood, Adonis' Blood, memories, experiences and Soul became incorporated into Victor deeply enough that Persephone could no longer simply collect Adonis as an independent dead Soul.[3]
The Curse did not disappear with Adonis. It instead transferred to Victor as part of what he had absorbed. When Victor eventually slept, the connection activated and dragged his consciousness into a spiritual domain associated with Persephone.[4]
Persephone expected the remnants of Adonis and instead encountered the Second Vampire Progenitor who had consumed him. The situation immediately departed from what she anticipated. Victor's Soul was already enormous and abnormal, preventing Persephone from effortlessly dominating him within a space defined through spiritual interaction. More importantly, Victor possessed Adonis' memories and therefore understood details of Persephone's relationship with him that an outsider should never have known.[4]
Victor deliberately used those memories against her. He reproduced elements of Adonis' appearance, exposed the resentment Adonis had developed toward his treatment and emphasized the fact that Adonis had willingly chosen absorption by Victor specifically to prevent Persephone from claiming him after death. Persephone reacted with anger and attempted to reinforce her authority, but Victor refused to treat her Divine status as proof that Adonis had belonged to her.[4][13]
The confrontation also revealed the long rivalry between Persephone and [[Aphrodite]]. Victor understood from Adonis' memories that, despite resenting both Goddesses, Adonis had ultimately preferred Aphrodite. Victor openly exploited that information to provoke Persephone and destabilize the certainty with which she had interpreted her relationship with the dead man.[13][14]
Their first meeting ended without friendship or reconciliation, but the Curse continued connecting them. Victor's later periods of sleep repeatedly returned him to Persephone's domain, and their encounters gradually changed from outright hostility into strange conversations between two people who still distrusted one another but had become familiar enough to speak without immediately trying to dominate the exchange.[21][22]
Victor eventually regarded those meetings as sufficiently irritating that avoiding Persephone became one reason he almost stopped sleeping entirely. His supernatural vitality allowed him to remain physically active for extremely long periods, although Ruby warned that his mind still required genuine rest regardless of what his body could tolerate.[23]
Persephone, meanwhile, was changing for reasons unrelated to Victor alone. The spreading supernatural war caused severe instability throughout the [[Greek Underworld]], forcing her to exercise her authority as Queen in a practical manner for the first time in thousands of years.[15] The administrative crisis confronted her with responsibilities she had previously been able to leave largely to [[Hades]] and his system.
A conversation with Aphrodite also remained with her. Aphrodite had bluntly criticized Persephone for continuing to chase a dead man who had rejected her and argued that Persephone needed enough self-respect to stop defining herself through Adonis. After months of reflection, Persephone finally accepted that the criticism was correct.[16]
During another meeting with Victor, Persephone declared that she was finished pursuing Adonis through him and voluntarily removed the Curse connecting them. Rather than continuing to chase a relationship that had already ended, she turned her attention toward the increasingly unstable Underworld.[16]
Her timing proved important. [[Cerberus]], one of the Underworld's essential guardians, disappeared amid the growing crisis. Persephone placed [[Thanatos]] in the role of temporary guardian, sent [[Hypnos]] to communicate with [[Olympus]] and deployed the Furies as the realm's internal situation deteriorated.[16] The woman who had once behaved primarily as Hades' isolated wife was beginning to act like an actual Queen before she possessed the full Authority of one.

#### Demonic War
The Demonic War pushed the [[Greek Underworld]] into a crisis its existing political structure could no longer survive. [[Hades]] had abandoned his responsibilities and sided with forces opposed to [[Olympus]], leaving [[Persephone]] and the remaining Gods of the Underworld to confront a realm whose supernatural machinery had been built around a Ruler who was no longer performing his function.
By Chapter 623, Persephone had been badly affected by the fighting and the burden of governing a system she did not truly control. Nyx approached her with a solution: Persephone could take the Helm of Hades and replace Hades as the legitimate Ruler of the Greek Underworld.[5]
Persephone did not blindly accept. Becoming an actual Ruler was not equivalent to putting on Hades' crown. The authority attached to an afterlife was tied to larger systems governing death and Souls, and the position could only be inherited by someone recognized as capable of performing its function.
Persephone eventually accepted the opportunity and came before [[The Judges of The Abyss]]. The Judges explained that Hells and Heavens were components of a greater process through which the dead were judged, punished, purified and eventually returned to the cycle. A Ruler was responsible for maintaining the system inside the territory entrusted to them; personal affection could not be allowed to override that responsibility.[24][6]
The resulting trial was psychological rather than a conventional battle. Persephone was repeatedly forced to judge circumstances in which no outcome would preserve everyone she cared about. The tests escalated until they involved the people closest to her.[25][26]
One decisive test forced Persephone to choose between [[Adonis Snow]] and her mother [[Demeter]]. Despite the obsession that had defined centuries of her life, Persephone was unable to sacrifice Demeter and chose Adonis to die. The trial then continued rather than giving her relief, eventually involving her daughters [[Melinoë]] and [[Macaria]] and forcing Persephone to experience the emotional cost of decisions a true Ruler might be required to make.[17]
The suffering was not meaningless cruelty. The trial stripped away the assumption that ruling an afterlife meant simply possessing enough power to command it. Persephone had to understand that a Ruler was a component of an impartial system whose decisions could involve enemies, strangers, friends, family and Gods alike.
Persephone ultimately passed.
When she awakened in Chapter 641, the distinction was immediate. Before the trial, Persephone had possessed numerous Divine titles but had never sufficiently developed herself to stand among the highest Gods of the Pantheon. Afterward, the Greek Underworld itself recognized and supported her as its legitimate sovereign. The increase elevated her into the class of a genuine God-King while transforming her authority from a ceremonial title into the function of a Ruler.[7]
Her appearance transformed with the position, producing black armor, an elegant dark dress and a crown originating from the Helm. Persephone was able to perceive Nyx despite the Primordial Goddess' concealment and struck her hard enough to sever an arm. She then ordered Nyx out of the Underworld, and the realm enforced its Ruler's command.[7]
The experience changed more than Persephone's strength. She openly condemned the hypocrisy of the Greek Gods and included herself among those responsible for it, contrasting their greed, lust, pride and opportunism with Gods such as [[Hestia]], whom Persephone considered far closer to what the title of a genuine God ought to represent.[7]
Persephone had begun the war as Hades' abandoned Queen. She emerged from the trial as the sovereign Hades had failed to remain.

#### King Among Kings
By the time [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s attention returned to the [[Greek Pantheon]], [[Persephone]] had spent enough time as the actual Ruler for the change in her administration to become visible.
When Nyx, [[Aphrodite]], [[Scathach]] and Morgana entered the [[Greek Underworld]], the realm was no longer simply [[Hades]]' former kingdom waiting for his return. Persephone controlled the central authority and the former Domain of Hades, while still respecting the independent territories of powerful Underworld Gods such as [[Hecate]], [[Hypnos]], Nyx and [[Erebus]].[27] Her status as Ruler did not mean every ancient Divine Being living within the dimension had been reduced to a personal servant.
[[Thanatos]] became particularly important to her administration. He acted as Persephone's most reliable direct subordinate and coordinated with Hypnos, [[Nemesis]], the Furies and other beings connected to the dead. By this stage even Thanatos, who had known the old Persephone, recognized that she had matured into a substantially more capable Queen.[28]
Her relationship with Hecate reflected the less rigid side of that rule. Hecate remained a powerful independent Goddess and an old friend rather than a servant who obeyed every royal demand. When the two argued over a portrait of Victor, Persephone attempted to invoke her position as Queen to order Hecate to surrender it, only for Hecate to reject the command with complete irreverence.[18] Their interaction demonstrated that Persephone's later court was not built entirely upon the fearful submission characteristic of many Olympian relationships.
The war that destroyed the old Olympian order eventually reached Persephone's realm directly. Persephone initially intended to support Victor largely through her control over the Underworld rather than personally turn the conflict into another Greek civil war. [[Zeus]] changed that decision when he broke [[Poseidon|the Master]] Bolt and used the enormous Influence stored within it to declare part of the battlefield the Territory of [[Olympus]], imposing his authority inside Persephone's realm.[29]
Persephone regarded the act as a direct violation of her sovereignty. She immediately ordered Thanatos to prepare the Underworld's army and entered the war.[29]
Her forces consisted of numerous ancient Gods associated with death, dreams, revenge and other powers historically rooted in the Underworld, including children of Nyx and Erebus and their descendants. The appearance of Persephone's army placed the surviving Olympian forces between Victor's coalition and an Underworld that no longer recognized Zeus' right to interfere with it.[30][31]
Persephone ordered her people to attack the remaining Greek forces while avoiding Victor's Demon King faction. She then concentrated on dismantling the foreign Territory Zeus had imposed upon the Underworld, with Thanatos assigned to protect her while she restored the authority of her own realm.[32]
The destruction of the old Greek political order finally removed many of the structures that had defined Persephone's life. Instead of using the opportunity merely to celebrate victory, she was forced to confront the people harmed by her own past.
Persephone openly acknowledged to Agnes and Violet that she regretted what she had done to Adonis. She admitted that Aphrodite had been correct when she told Persephone to let him go and recognized that her refusal had harmed the person she claimed to love. Persephone did not demand forgiveness. She accepted that Agnes and Violet had legitimate reasons to hate her and asked only that any punishment directed toward her not be extended to her children.[8]
The same discussion forced her to reassess Hades. Persephone acknowledged that she had genuinely felt something for him, but also recognized that those feelings originated after being kidnapped, isolated and forced into marriage. She described the relationship for what it had been: affection produced under circumstances resembling Stockholm Syndrome.[8]
Victor nevertheless recognized that the Persephone standing before them was not identical to the woman who had cursed Adonis centuries earlier. By this point her connection to the Greek Underworld allowed the realm to support her so heavily that, while operating within her proper territory, her practical strength could reach the same broad class as the original [[Primordial Gods]] of the Greek Pantheon.[33] This did not change Persephone's birth into that of a First Generation Primordial God; it represented the amplification granted to a legitimate Ruler by the realm supporting her.
Victor later provided something none of them believed possible: an opportunity to speak with Adonis again without undoing his death. Using information preserved within the [[Akashic Records]], Victor reconstructed an informational echo based upon Adonis shortly before his death.[34]
The result was not the true resurrection of the continuous Soul Victor had absorbed. It was a reconstruction carrying Adonis' memories, personality and recorded identity, sufficient for the people left behind to finally speak with a version of him capable of answering for himself.[35][36][37]
For Persephone, the encounter provided the closure her obsession had prevented for centuries. Adonis no longer needed to exist as an object she was trying to reclaim, and Persephone no longer needed to define herself by whether he had loved her. The chapter of her life connecting her to Adonis could finally end.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
[[Persephone]] remained within the enormous coalition and family developing around [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] after the collapse of [[Olympus]]. The next fundamental change to her existence came when Victor began transforming members of his family into genuine [[True Dragons]].
Persephone was among the Goddesses who accepted the transformation. Because a God's identity, Divinities and Soul were deeply connected, Victor's method was not treated as a simple physical mutation. The participants were reconstructed into [[Blood Dragons]] while their existing identities and compatible characteristics were incorporated rather than indiscriminately erased.[9][11]
Persephone consequently emerged as a genuine Blood Dragon Goddess. She remained Persephone—the former Olympian, mother, [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] Ruler and Goddess she had always been—but now possessed a True Dragon foundation belonging to Victor's newly created lineage.[12]
She remained close enough to Victor during the following period that she was among those alarmed when the internal restructuring of Victor's Soul briefly caused his body to lose consciousness and cough blood. What appeared externally like an injury was actually the result of a rapid purification and reorganization occurring inside an existence that had already exceeded ordinary mortality.[38]
Persephone's personal relationship with Victor then moved beyond political cooperation and mutual fascination. She understood that Victor desired her and openly confronted the possessiveness behind that desire rather than pretending not to notice it.[19]
Their circumstances differed fundamentally from Persephone's first marriage. Victor had not abducted her, did not require her to mistake captivity for love and could not be manipulated through the type of unequal relationship that had characterized her obsession with Adonis. Persephone knew what Victor was, understood the intensity with which he treated those he accepted as his own and chose to enter that family anyway.
In Chapter 930, Persephone and Victor completed a genuine [[Soul Marriage]]. Their Souls became permanently connected and Persephone formally became one of Victor's wives and a member of the family that would later be known as Clan Elderblood.[1]
Her responsibilities as a Ruler remained relevant after the marriage. When an enormous influx of dead Souls overwhelmed the infernal systems controlled by Victor's faction, [[Lily Elderblood]] was forced to call Persephone, Aline and [[Lilith Elderblood]] for assistance. The three women were specifically relied upon as Rulers capable of increasing the number of Souls being judged and processed.[10]
The crisis demonstrated why Persephone's history in the Greek Underworld remained useful even after she had outgrown the old Olympian political system. Thousands of years spent around an afterlife, followed by the Judges' trial and her actual reign as its Ruler, gave her experience directly applicable to the vastly larger infernal administration Victor was constructing.[10][39]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
The increasingly extreme time manipulation used within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s territories transformed the scale of the Elderblood family. While relatively little time passed externally, Victor's wives could experience centuries or millennia during which their civilization, personal development and pregnancies progressed according to supernatural rather than ordinary Human timescales.
[[Persephone]] was among the Divine wives whose pregnancy lasted exceptionally long. Her daughter with Victor required approximately 1,900 years of gestation, placing Persephone among a group of Goddesses whose children took far longer to develop than those born from many of Victor's other wives.[2]
The length of the pregnancy was associated with the extremely high-level Divine potential involved in the development of such children rather than functioning as a simple measurement in which a longer pregnancy automatically guaranteed a stronger daughter. Other children born after shorter periods could still possess extraordinary talent.[2]
The daughter born from Victor and Persephone has not yet been given a confirmed name in the currently indexed material and should therefore remain listed as Unnamed Daughter rather than receiving a speculative identity.
By this period, Persephone's old identity as [[Hades]]' Queen had become almost entirely historical. She was now a Blood Dragon Goddess, one of Victor's wives, a mother within the second Elderblood generation and an experienced Ruler inside a civilization whose afterlife infrastructure increasingly extended beyond any single Pantheon.

#### Those Who Rule Reality
The [[Elderblood Empire]]'s enormous period of accelerated development turned the administration of death into a civilization-scale institution. Populations eventually reached levels at which older afterlife systems designed around comparatively limited planetary civilizations were insufficient. Dedicated regions for judgment, punishment, preservation, rest and reincarnation had to be expanded and reorganized as the number of Imperial Souls continued to grow.[40][41]
The Empire also preserved the cultures of civilizations that had been absorbed or politically superseded. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] created an enormous world for the Greek members of his family, allowing temples, architecture and Divine traditions to be rebuilt without resurrecting [[Olympus|Mount Olympus]] as the independent political structure that had produced so many of their previous conflicts.[42] For [[Persephone]], the project represented another distinction between preserving Greek identity and restoring the dysfunctional order of [[Zeus]] and the old Pantheon.
That difference became particularly visible during the Elderblood family's traditional gathering in Chapter 1166. Persephone arrived alongside [[Gaia]], Morgana, Lilith and Lily while a large portion of the Imperial family prepared food together. When Gaia remarked that at least the Elderblood family did not suffer from the same problems as their former Pantheon, Persephone and [[Aphrodite]] agreed, recognizing that many of Greece's greatest disasters had been created by the Gods themselves.[20]
The scene demonstrated how thoroughly Persephone's concept of family had changed. The former Olympian Queen who had once existed inside an isolated [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] household was now one member of an enormous dynasty containing Vampires, Dragons, Demons, Goddesses, [[World Trees]], former [[Primordial Gods]] and generations of children who nevertheless treated one another as relatives before treating one another as representatives of separate species or Pantheons.
Persephone also appears in the later explanation of how Victor's Draconic bloodline was distributed throughout the extended family. True Dragon status was not automatically granted to every relative merely because they were connected to Victor. Persephone, Gaia, Aphrodite and Jeanne were specifically named among women who had prevented their children from automatically becoming [[True Dragons]], although those children could still receive lower Draconic lineages.[43]
This policy reflected a wider Elderblood principle that even relatives were not automatically entitled to the highest form of Victor's lineage. Different levels of Draconic inheritance existed, and admission into the stronger portions of the Imperial bloodline could be subjected to standards established by Victor's wives.[43]
Persephone remained actively connected to the household rather than withdrawing into an administrative domain. When [[Scathach]] later dragged [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]] into an arena to confront her insufficient participation in family affairs, Persephone was among the large group of Victor's wives watching through projections.[44] By this stage her position within the Elderblood family had become completely normalized: she was no longer an outside Greek Goddess orbiting Victor's faction, but one of the women expected to participate in the family's internal life.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Persephone]] began as adversaries connected through another man's death.
Victor inherited Persephone's Curse when he consumed [[Adonis Snow]], causing his sleep to repeatedly draw him into Persephone's spiritual domain.[4] Persephone initially approached Victor through her unresolved fixation upon Adonis, while Victor immediately rejected the possibility that inheriting Adonis' memories made him either Adonis himself or property Persephone could claim.
Their early conversations were confrontational. Victor deliberately used Adonis' memories to force Persephone to acknowledge perspectives she had spent centuries refusing to consider, including the possibility that Adonis had hated being controlled by her and had deliberately chosen Victor as a means of escaping her after death.[4][13]
Repeated encounters gradually produced familiarity without immediately creating affection. Victor saw Persephone change as the [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]]'s crisis forced her to take responsibility, while Persephone became increasingly interested in a man who could confront her without fear and who refused to accept Divine status as an excuse for abusive behavior.[21][22]
Their relationship could only progress after Persephone independently abandoned her pursuit of Adonis. She removed the Curse from Victor in Chapter 467 and chose to concentrate upon her own life and responsibilities rather than use Victor as a substitute for a dead lover.[16]
Victor later witnessed the results of her Ruler trial, the collapse of [[Olympus]] and Persephone's attempts to take responsibility for her past. He defended the distinction between the Persephone who had harmed Adonis and the woman who had genuinely changed afterward without pretending her earlier actions had been acceptable.[8][33]
After becoming a Blood Dragon, Persephone moved fully into the family surrounding Victor.[9] By Chapters 929–930, both openly understood the attraction and possessiveness existing between them. Persephone accepted Victor with complete awareness of those traits rather than being trapped into the relationship.
Their commitment culminated in a [[Soul Marriage]], permanently connecting their Souls and establishing Persephone as one of Victor's wives.[1] They later had an unnamed daughter whose gestation lasted approximately 1,900 years under the accelerated chronology experienced by the family.[2]
Unlike her relationships with [[Hades]] and Adonis, Persephone's relationship with Victor was entered into after she had already acquired independence, political authority and the ability to refuse him. That distinction is central to the development separating her later life from her past.

#### Hades
[[Hades]] was [[Persephone]]'s uncle, abductor and first husband.
He kidnapped Persephone while she was young, brought her into the [[Greek Underworld]] and forced her into marriage. Hades was extremely possessive and strongly restricted Persephone's ability to leave the realm. Although she eventually developed emotional attachment toward him and spent an enormous portion of her existence as his Queen, the relationship began without Persephone's freedom or consent.
Persephone herself eventually stopped romanticizing that history. After the destruction of [[Olympus]], she acknowledged that whatever genuine feelings she later developed had originated through captivity and emotional dependence, describing her love for Hades as an example of Stockholm Syndrome.[8]
Hades' eventual abandonment of the Greek Underworld destroyed what remained of their old arrangement. He left behind both Persephone and the duties attached to being the realm's Ruler, forcing Persephone to either allow the Underworld's administration to collapse or become the sovereign Hades had ceased to be.[5]
Ironically, Hades' abandonment became one of the events that finally allowed Persephone to stop existing beneath him. By passing the Judges' trial and becoming the True Ruler herself, she acquired the authority and independence she had never possessed as the [[Iron]] Queen of Hades.[7]

#### Adonis Snow
[[Adonis Snow]] was the center of [[Persephone]]'s most destructive obsession.
Persephone became attracted to Adonis after encountering him outside the [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] and entered a rivalry with [[Aphrodite]] over him. The Goddesses eventually divided his time between themselves rather than asking whether Adonis wished to belong to either of them.
Persephone went further by placing a Curse upon Adonis that would return his Soul to her after his death.[4] From Persephone's early perspective this guaranteed that she could never permanently lose him. From Adonis' perspective it meant that even dying would not allow him to escape a Goddess who considered his existence hers.
His resentment eventually became so severe that, when death approached, Adonis asked [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to devour him completely. He specifically preferred absorption into another being to the possibility that his independent Soul could be claimed by Persephone.[3]
Persephone initially had difficulty accepting what that decision said about their relationship. Victor's possession of Adonis' memories repeatedly forced her to confront the issue, while Aphrodite later told Persephone directly that endlessly pursuing someone who wanted nothing to do with her was degrading to both of them.
Persephone finally listened. She stopped pursuing Adonis through Victor, removed the Curse and later admitted that refusing to let Adonis go had harmed the man she claimed to love.[16][8]
Victor's temporary reconstruction of Adonis' recorded identity later allowed Persephone one final opportunity for genuine closure.[34][37] Adonis ceased being the object around which she organized her emotional life, allowing Persephone to move into relationships built upon who she had become rather than what she had refused to release.

#### Aphrodite Elderblood
[[Aphrodite]] Elderblood and [[Persephone]] share a relationship that changed almost as drastically as Persephone herself.
They were originally rivals for Adonis. Their mutual desire led them to arrange a division of his time rather than fight directly, making both Goddesses participants in the circumstances Adonis eventually came to resent.
Despite the rivalry, Aphrodite was also responsible for one of the most important interventions in Persephone's life. Her criticism that Persephone needed self-respect and should stop pursuing a dead man remained in Persephone's thoughts for months. Persephone eventually admitted that Aphrodite had been correct and abandoned the obsession.[16]
Both women later became wives of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and members of the same Elderblood family. By the Imperial era their old conflict over Adonis had become part of a shared past rather than an active rivalry. In Chapter 1166, Persephone and Aphrodite even responded together when [[Gaia]] criticized the disastrous family politics of the old [[Greek Pantheon]].[20]

#### Demeter
[[Demeter]] is [[Persephone]]'s mother and one of the people she values most deeply.
The strength of that bond became undeniable during the Judges' trial. Persephone was forced to choose between Demeter and Adonis under circumstances where she could not preserve both. Despite the obsession that had dominated her relationship with Adonis for centuries, Persephone could not sacrifice her mother and chose Demeter.[17]
The decision became one of the clearest demonstrations that Persephone's attachment to Adonis, however extreme, had never completely replaced her deeper familial bonds.

#### Melinoë and Macaria
[[Melinoë]] and [[Macaria]] are [[Persephone]]'s daughters with [[Hades]].
They were among the people used during Persephone's Ruler trial precisely because the Judges needed to determine whether her impartiality could survive when the Souls involved were not strangers but her own children.[17]
The ordeal brought Persephone close to despair and taught her that legitimate rulership could demand decisions no parent or Goddess would willingly choose to face.
In the later Imperial era, Persephone is specifically included among mothers who did not automatically transform their children into [[True Dragons]] merely because [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family possessed access to that lineage. Lower Draconic lineages remained possible, but the highest form was not distributed indiscriminately.[43]

#### Unnamed Daughter
[[Persephone]] eventually had a daughter with [[Victor Elderblood]] during the enormous accelerated periods experienced by the Elderblood family.
Her pregnancy lasted approximately 1,900 years, placing her alongside several other highly Divine wives whose daughters required unusually long gestation periods.[2]
No canonical name for Persephone and Victor's daughter has been confirmed in the currently indexed material. She should therefore remain listed as Unnamed Daughter until the novel explicitly identifies her.

#### Magic goddess Hecate
[[Hecate]] is one of [[Persephone]]'s oldest and closest Divine associates in the [[Greek Underworld]].
Unlike ordinary subordinates, Hecate possesses her own territory and considerable independence. Persephone deliberately did not absorb the domains belonging to Gods such as Hecate and [[Hypnos]] after becoming the Underworld's Ruler, recognizing both their assistance and their established positions within the realm.[27]
Their friendship allows Hecate to behave toward Persephone in ways an ordinary servant would never attempt. Their argument over a portrait of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] escalated until Persephone invoked her royal status and ordered Hecate to hand it over, only for Hecate to categorically refuse despite the command.[18]
The interaction illustrates a relationship based upon familiarity rather than rigid royal hierarchy.

#### Thanatos
[[Thanatos]] became [[Persephone]]'s most dependable direct subordinate as she transitioned from ceremonial Queen to actual Ruler.
When [[Cerberus]] disappeared, Persephone entrusted Thanatos with the role of temporary guardian of the [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]].[16] After her ascension, he continued coordinating many of the realm's military and administrative responses on her behalf.
By Chapter 778, Thanatos personally recognized how dramatically Persephone had changed and considered her to have matured into a worthy Queen.[28] During the fall of [[Olympus]] he commanded portions of her forces and remained beside Persephone while she concentrated upon dismantling [[Zeus]]' imposed Territory.[32]

#### Hypnos, Nemesis and the Furies
[[Hypnos]], [[Nemesis]] and the Furies form part of the network of Divine beings serving [[Persephone]]'s [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] administration.
Hypnos was trusted with diplomatic and operational responsibilities as early as the period following [[Cerberus]]' disappearance, while Nemesis and the Furies later operated as direct combatants beneath Persephone's command.[16][32]
Their continued service demonstrates that Persephone did not govern the Greek Underworld alone. Her successful reign depended upon an established structure of death-related and Underworld Gods whose loyalties increasingly centered upon the Ruler actually maintaining their realm rather than the husband who had abandoned it.

#### Nyx Elderblood
Nyx Elderblood played a contradictory but essential role in [[Persephone]]'s rise.
It was Nyx who offered Persephone the opportunity to take the Helm and replace [[Hades]] as the legitimate Ruler when the [[Greek Underworld]] began collapsing without him.[5] Persephone initially distrusted both Nyx's motives and the cost of the offer but eventually accepted the trial.
After surviving the Judges' ordeal, Persephone was furious that Nyx had known what the process would involve without explaining its full severity. Her first actions as a newly recognized Ruler included attacking Nyx and ordering her out of the Underworld.[7]
Their relationship did not remain permanently defined by that confrontation. Both eventually entered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s much larger family and became members of the same Elderblood civilization, although their earlier interactions remained an example of the complicated histories carried into Victor's household by ancient Goddesses who had known one another for enormous periods before meeting him.

### Abilities

#### Skills and Abilities
Persephone Elderblood possesses several distinct supernatural power systems that overlap without being identical. She was born an Olympian Goddess with multiple Divinities, most prominently those associated with Spring, the [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] and Souls, alongside lesser agricultural Concepts such as Harvest, Vegetation and Fertility. For most of her early life, however, [[Persephone]] possessed many Divine Concepts without having developed them to a particularly advanced degree. [[Hades]] was the true Ruler of the Greek Underworld, while Persephone was largely a ceremonial Queen.[1]
This changed after Persephone accepted the Helm of Hades and successfully passed the test imposed by [[The Judges of The Abyss]]. [[The Helm]]'s Authority tested whether she could remain impartial when dealing with Souls; after passing, Persephone became the legitimate True Ruler of Greek Hell, connected to the greater supernatural system involved in processing Souls and received the knowledge required to perform a Ruler's duties.[2][3] Greek Hell itself began supporting her, immediately elevating Persephone into the realm of a God-King while she operated through her Ruler Authority.[1]
Persephone later underwent [[Victor Elderblood]]'s Dragonization and became a genuine Blood Dragon. The transformation did not erase her Divine existence: her Olympian nature, Divinities, spiritual characteristics and Ruler powers were preserved and incorporated into a complete True Dragon foundation.[4][5][6] She therefore currently combines three principal sources of power:
- Blood Dragon / True Dragon physiology
- Olympian Divine physiology and personal Divinities
- True Ruler Authority and the Greek Underworld's Soul-processing System
These sources should be distinguished when describing Persephone's abilities. Her personal Concept of Soul, for example, belongs to Persephone herself, while her ability to judge and administrate vast populations of deceased Souls is substantially augmented by the Authority granted to a legitimate Ruler of Hell.

##### Blood Dragon God Physiology
- Blood Dragon God Physiology: [[Persephone]] was reconstructed into a genuine Blood Dragon by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] rather than merely receiving superficial Draconic characteristics. Her existing Divine nature was incorporated into the new Race instead of being discarded.[4][5][6]
- True Dragon Physiology: Persephone possesses the physical and supernatural foundation of a complete True Dragon.[4]
- Enhanced Physical Characteristics: Her Dragonization vastly improved her physical strength, speed, durability, stamina, reflexes and overall bodily capability compared with her former Olympian body.[4][6]
- Enhanced Senses: Her True Dragon physiology provides senses capable of perceiving supernatural phenomena far beyond conventional physical perception.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Persephone combines the supernatural recovery of a Divine Being with the vastly improved vitality of a Blood Dragon.
- Longevity: Persephone was already effectively ageless as a Goddess and retains this characteristic as a True Dragon.
- True Dragon Transformation: Her Dragonization created a complete True Dragon existence rather than a partial hybrid transformation. Persephone can maintain a humanoid body while possessing the foundation necessary to assume her genuine Draconic form.[4][6]
- Flight: Her True Dragon form possesses the natural aerial mobility associated with Dragons.
- Natural Draconic Weapons: In a complete Draconic state she possesses the biological armaments characteristic of a True Dragon, such as reinforced scales, claws, fangs, horns and other natural weapons.
- [[Draconic Energy]]: Persephone can utilize the supernatural Energy belonging to her Draconic existence alongside her pre-existing [[Divine Energy]].
- Power Integration: Victor's transformation did not standardize Persephone into an ordinary Dragon. Her pre-existing Divinities, spiritual characteristics and powers were incorporated into the Blood Dragon foundation, allowing her former abilities to operate through a considerably superior body.[4][6]
- Draconic Adaptability: [[True Dragons]] possess exceptional compatibility with supernatural systems and different forms of Energy. Persephone consequently gained a far broader foundation for learning Draconic abilities after her transformation.
- Dragon Breath: As a complete True Dragon, Persephone possesses the racial foundation for Dragon Breath. Her specific Breath element or individualized variation has not yet been directly demonstrated and should not be assigned a property without further evidence.

###### Draconic Vision
- Draconic Vision: [[Persephone]] developed supernatural ocular abilities after becoming a Blood Dragon.[7]
- Energy Perception: Persephone can visually perceive supernatural Energies and distinguish different Energies operating simultaneously within the same phenomenon.[7] While observing [[Anna Elderblood]]'s Reality-altering ability, Persephone saw numerous distinct Energies gathering and working together to fulfill Anna's desire.[7]
- Supernatural Process Perception: Her Draconic Eyes allow her to examine processes that cannot be understood through ordinary physical sight alone.[7]
- Developing Mastery: Persephone's eyes visibly glowed while she deliberately activated the ability. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explained that this was because she had not yet mastered the ocular Technique; more experienced users could maintain similar perception naturally without requiring such an obvious activation.[7]
- Limitations: Persephone has not demonstrated every ocular ability possessed by Victor or his daughters. Only the functions actually shown through Persephone should be attributed to her.

###### Draconic Language and Runes
- Draconic Language Affinity: As a genuine True Dragon, [[Persephone]] gained access to the racial foundation necessary to learn the Draconic Language, in which Energy, intent and words can be combined to impose instructions upon Reality.[7][8]
- Draconic Rune Affinity: Persephone possesses compatibility with Draconic Runes, described as a supernatural language capable of interacting with the fundamental mechanisms of Creation.[7]
- Creation Compatibility: The basic nature of [[True Dragons]] grants them unusual compatibility with the processes of Creation, forming the basis of Dragon Runes and their ability to interact with Reality.[7]
- Training Requirement: Dragonization grants the foundation for these systems rather than instant mastery. Persephone was still learning even her basic Draconic Vision shortly after becoming a Dragon, and the newly transformed Dragon family subsequently required dedicated training in their bodies, powers and Draconic Language.[7][8]

##### Olympian God Physiology
- Olympian Goddess Physiology: [[Persephone]] was born a genuine Goddess of the [[Greek Pantheon]] and retained her Divine existence after Dragonization.
- [[Divine Energy]] Manipulation: Persephone can generate and manipulate Divine Energy through her existence and Divinities.
- Divinity: Persephone possesses multiple genuine Concepts through which she can interact with phenomena corresponding to her Divine domains.
- [[Divine Presence]]: Persephone can release overwhelming supernatural pressure proportional to her Divine Power.
- Divine Longevity: Persephone is unaffected by conventional Mortal aging and natural lifespan.
- Divine Soul: Her Soul possesses the supernatural quality and resilience appropriate to an ancient Goddess, later reinforced further by Dragonization and her status as a legitimate Ruler.
- Divine Regeneration: Her Divine existence grants supernatural recovery beyond ordinary biological healing.
- Miasma Generation: Persephone can release a dense supernatural Miasma when angered or exerting her power.[9]
- Miasma Aura: Her Miasma can spread throughout the surrounding spiritual environment together with her killing intent.[9]

##### Divinities
[[Persephone]] possesses several Divine Concepts. The most consistently established are Spring, the [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] and Soul, while Harvest, Vegetation and Fertility are also associated with her Divine identity.[10][11]
Unlike some Gods whose individual Concepts have been extensively demonstrated in combat, several of Persephone's agricultural Divinities have received very little direct usage. Their existence should therefore be distinguished from speculative applications that the novel has never shown.

###### Spring
- Spring Divinity: [[Persephone]] possesses a genuine Divine Concept corresponding to Spring.[11]
- Seasonal Authority: Phenomena conceptually belonging to Spring fall within the potential jurisdiction of this Divinity.
- Renewal and Growth Association: The Concept naturally connects Persephone to the renewal and growth associated with Spring.
- Limitations: The novel has not directly demonstrated the full environmental or combat applications of Persephone's Spring Divinity. It should not automatically be expanded into unrestricted Life Manipulation, resurrection or universal seasonal control.

###### Underworld
- [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] Divinity: [[Persephone]] possesses a personal Divine association with the Underworld independent of the additional Authority she obtained after becoming its legitimate Ruler.[11]
- Underworld Affinity: Her Divine nature makes her naturally compatible with infernal environments, dead Souls and the supernatural systems of the Greek Underworld.
- Distinction from Ruler Authority: The enormous territorial powers Persephone obtained after the Judges' trial derive from her status as the True Ruler and the corresponding System connection. They should not all be attributed solely to her personal Underworld Divinity.

###### Soul
- Concept of Soul: [[Persephone]] explicitly possesses and wields the Divine Concept of Soul.[10]
- Soul Manipulation: Her Divinity allows her supernatural power to directly interact with Souls rather than being restricted to physical matter.
- Soul Perception: Persephone can perceive Souls and spiritually significant characteristics within them.
- Soul Intent Perception: After observing Souls for thousands of years, Persephone became capable of sensing the intentions and emotions carried within a Soul even when the individual's expression or behavior attempted to conceal them.[12]
- Soul Attack Infusion: Persephone recognized that an attack infused with the Concept of Soul could directly threaten the spiritual existence of a target rather than merely damaging their body.[10]
- Soul Targeting: Her powers can operate directly upon spiritual structures, as demonstrated most clearly by the Curse she placed upon [[Adonis Snow]].[13]
- God-Soul Interaction: Persephone's later Ruler Authority additionally allows her to judge [[Divine Souls]] that legitimately enter the afterlife system under her jurisdiction.[1]

###### Harvest
- Harvest Divinity: [[Persephone]] possesses a Divine association with Harvest.[11]
- Agricultural Authority: Harvest-related phenomena fall conceptually within this Divinity.

###### Vegetation
- Vegetation Divinity: [[Persephone]] possesses a Divine Concept associated with vegetation and plant life.[11]
- Plant Affinity: Vegetation belongs within the conceptual jurisdiction of this Divinity.

###### Fertility
- Fertility Divinity: [[Persephone]] possesses a Divine association with Fertility.[11]
- Fertility Authority: Reproductive and natural fertility fall conceptually within this Divine domain.

##### Curse Manipulation
- Curse Manipulation: [[Persephone]] is capable of constructing highly persistent supernatural Curses that attach directly to the Soul.[13]
- Soul-Binding Curse: Persephone placed a Curse upon [[Adonis Snow]] that remained bound to his spiritual existence and was designed to ensure that his Soul ultimately returned to her after death.[13]
- Post-Mortem Soul Claim: The Curse was specifically intended to interfere with what happened to Adonis after death rather than merely afflicting his physical body.[13]
- Curse Inheritance: When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] consumed Adonis and incorporated his Soul and memories, the Curse transferred with what he absorbed, demonstrating that its attachment existed at a spiritual level.[13]
- Spiritual Trigger: The inherited Curse eventually connected Victor's Soul to Persephone and drew him into the spiritual environment generated through it.[13]
- Curse Removal: Persephone can voluntarily remove her own Soul-based Curse. She eventually abandoned her pursuit of Adonis and severed the Curse connecting her to Victor.[14]
- Limitations: Persephone's Curse is not absolute. Victor's abnormal Soul prevented the encounter from functioning as Persephone expected, and the Curse's intended killing mechanism was poorly suited to a target who could not be killed through the expected method.[13][9]

##### Shared Spiritual Domain
- Spiritual Domain Creation: [[Persephone]]'s Curse can create a spiritual environment connecting her Soul to the Soul carrying the Curse.[13]
- Soul Manifestation: Persephone and the affected individual can interact directly through their spiritual existences inside this space.[13]
- Spiritual Communication: The domain allows direct conversation and interaction without ordinary physical proximity.
- Domain Influence: Because the space is created through Persephone's Curse and Soul, she normally possesses substantial influence over its environment.[13]
- Soul-Based Resistance: Control of the domain depends partly upon the strength and quality of the Souls involved. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul was powerful enough to remain fully aware, resist Persephone's expected control and influence the space himself.[13][9]
- Non-Absolute Control: The spiritual domain should therefore not be treated as an automatic victory condition against every target.

##### Miasma Manipulation
- Miasma Manipulation: [[Persephone]] can generate dark supernatural Miasma associated with her Divine and [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] nature.[9]
- Miasma Generation: Large quantities of Miasma can emerge directly from her spiritual existence.
- Miasma Aura: Intense anger and killing intent can cause the surrounding environment to become saturated by her Miasma.[9]
- Killing Intent: Persephone can project enough hostility that her intent becomes a tangible supernatural pressure.
- Limitations: No direct feat establishes every possible poisonous, corrosive, madness-inducing or Soul-destroying property sometimes associated with other forms of Miasma in the setting.

##### True Ruler of the Greek Underworld
[[Persephone]]'s greatest pre-Dragon transformation came from becoming the legitimate Ruler of Greek Hell. The power should be treated as a supernatural office with its own Authority, System connection, territorial jurisdiction and limitations rather than as another ordinary Divinity.

###### Ruler Authority
- True Ruler Authority: [[Persephone]] obtained legitimate Authority over Greek Hell after accepting the Helm of [[Hades]] and successfully passing the test of [[The Judges of The Abyss]].[2][3][1]
- Afterlife-System Connection: Passing the test connected Persephone to the supernatural system responsible for managing the Souls processed through the afterlife.[2]
- Ruler Knowledge: After completing the trial, Persephone received an extensive body of memories and knowledge containing the information required to perform the work of a legitimate Ruler.[1]
- Territorial Empowerment: Greek Hell itself supports Persephone when she operates through her legitimate Ruler Authority.[1]
- God-King Empowerment: Nyx initially expected Persephone to rise merely to the level of a High-Level God, but after the trial Persephone entered the realm of a God-King while backed by Greek Hell.[1]
- Superior Ruler Reserves: Her awakening produced Divine Power exceeding what Nyx expected from Persephone's former level and transformed her into an existence even Nyx could no longer casually manipulate.[1]
- Ruler Soul Protection: Legitimate Rulers receive substantial protection against Soul-based attacks because their Souls are supported by the supernatural status of their office. Soul attacks from ordinary Gods of Death have greatly reduced effectiveness against a Ruler, although significantly superior spiritual existences can still bypass this protection.[15]
- Ruler Regalia: Persephone's successful recognition caused black armor, an elegant black dress and a crown derived from Hades' Helm to manifest together with her new Authority.[1]

###### Territorial Authority
- [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] Control: Within the administrative territory legitimately belonging to the Ruler, [[Persephone]] can exert supernatural control vastly greater than that of an ordinary Goddess.[1]
- Territorial Awareness: Persephone can sense significant supernatural presences and disturbances occurring within territory under her influence.[16]
- Territorial Law Enforcement: Commands issued through legitimate Ruler Authority can become supernatural Rules enforced by the Underworld itself.[1]
- This is a territorial power and should not be treated as unrestricted universal Law Manipulation.
- Banishment: Persephone can forcibly expel Beings from the Greek Underworld when they are subject to her legitimate Ruler jurisdiction.[1] Persephone used this Authority to banish Nyx, an Original Greek Primordial Goddess.[1]
- Access Denial: After banishing Nyx, Persephone's Authority prevented her from simply re-entering the legitimate Hell through an ordinary route. Nyx would require Persephone's permission or an alternate condition capable of bypassing the prohibition.[1]
- Territorial Suppression: Persephone can interfere with hostile Gods attempting to freely exercise their Divinities inside territory governed by her.[17] During the war against [[Olympus]], interference from the Ruler of the Greek Underworld was explicitly one of the factors limiting the Olympian Gods' ability to freely deploy their powers.[17] This represents suppression and interference, not proof that Persephone can universally nullify any power regardless of difference in strength or location.
- Foreign Authority Removal: Persephone can dismantle supernatural territorial influence imposed upon her legitimate domain by another Ruler or God.[18]
- After [[Zeus]] sacrificed [[Poseidon|the Master]] Bolt to declare part of the battlefield the Territory of Olympus, Persephone focused her Ruler Authority on breaking and removing the foreign Territory.[17][18]
- The foreign Territory still existed inside the Greek Underworld, allowing Persephone's legitimate Authority to oppose it, although the enormous amount of Energy Zeus supplied through the Master Bolt meant removing it required considerable time and Energy.[18]
- Administrative Core Access: Persephone possesses access to the Core of the Greek Underworld because she is one of its legitimate Rulers. Entry to the Core is restricted to the King or Queen and those permitted by them.[19]
- Core Synchronization: Restoring Persephone's complete connection to the Underworld Core caused her power to increase explosively, demonstrating how strongly her maximum territorial strength depends upon synchronization with the realm.[19]

###### Soul Judgment and Afterlife Administration
- Soul Judgment: Kings and Rulers of Hell possess the Authority to judge Souls to a lesser degree than [[The Judges of The Abyss]].[2]
- God-Soul Judgment: [[Persephone]] explicitly declared that her role includes judging the Souls of Gods when those Souls fall legitimately under her afterlife jurisdiction.[1]
- Soul Evaluation: A Ruler receives access to the information necessary to determine the circumstances surrounding a Soul and make an appropriate judgment.[2]
- Contextual Judgment: Persephone's trial taught her that judging an action requires understanding intent, motive and circumstances rather than merely observing the final result.[2][3]
- Impartial Judgment: The Judges repeatedly forced Persephone to evaluate situations involving people emotionally important to her so that personal attachment would not supersede her responsibilities as a Ruler.[3]
- Soul Processing: Persephone can participate in directing deceased Souls through the appropriate punishment and purification processes belonging to Hell.[2]
- Soul Recycling Administration: Hells function as part of a larger system that punishes and recycles Souls before they proceed to the next stage of the cycle.[2]
- Afterlife Maintenance: As a legitimate Ruler, Persephone possesses both the Authority and technical knowledge required to keep an afterlife's Soul-processing functions operating correctly.[2][1]
- Not Reincarnation Authority: The Soul-recycling function of Hell does not establish a personal Divinity of Reincarnation for Persephone. After a Soul has been processed and recycled, Chapter 629 explicitly identifies a separate Primordial Being responsible for Reincarnation and Life as the existence that grants the Soul its next beginning.[2]
- Not Universal Soul Ownership: Persephone does not possess unrestricted jurisdiction over every Soul in Creation. The Judges of The Abyss exist above individual Hell Rulers within the broader system.[2]

###### Limits of Ruler Authority
[[Persephone]]'s Ruler Authority is exceptionally powerful within its proper jurisdiction but is neither universal nor completely absolute.
- Territorial Limitation: Persephone's greatest Ruler powers depend upon operating within a Hell or administrative territory legitimately connected to her Authority.[16]
- Independent Divine Territories: The entire [[Greek Underworld]] is not one homogeneous domain completely controlled by Persephone. Ancient Gods such as [[Hecate]], [[Hypnos]], Nyx and [[Erebus]] possess their own territories inside the greater Underworld.[16]
- Nyx's Territory: Persephone explicitly could not control Nyx's personal territory despite being the Ruler of Greek Hell because that region functioned as Nyx's residence rather than part of the Soul-processing Hell directly subject to Persephone's administrative Authority.[16]
- Central Domain Advantage: Persephone's influence becomes considerably stronger near the portions of the Greek Underworld directly governed by her. She believed even Nyx would struggle to remain hidden if she approached Persephone's central territory.[16]
- Primordial Concealment Limitation: Outside those strongest zones, Persephone could not automatically detect Nyx or Erebus when they seriously used their respective concealment-related Divinities.[16]
- Foreign Authority Resistance: A sufficiently powerful outside Authority can temporarily contest Persephone's territorial control. [[Zeus]]' [[Master Bolt]] allowed him to establish the Territory of [[Olympus]] inside the Underworld until Persephone dismantled it.[17][18]
- Energy Requirement: Removing a powerful foreign Territory can require substantial Energy and concentration even when Persephone possesses the superior territorial claim.[18]
- System Dependency: Persephone's Ruler powers depend upon her connection to the afterlife System. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s actions temporarily blocked that System during the collapse and reconstruction of the Greek Underworld, Persephone lost access to the corresponding Authority until the connection could function again.[20]
- Higher Spiritual Authorities: Ruler status grants powerful Soul protection, but it does not make Persephone immune to Beings standing fundamentally above normal Rulers in the hierarchy of Souls, such as [[The Judges of The Abyss]].[2][15]

##### Ruler Regalia and Divine Weapon

###### The Helm of Hades
- Helm of [[Hades]]: A Divine Ruler Artifact containing the Authority required to test and recognize a candidate for legitimate rulership of Greek Hell.[2]
- Ruler Selection: Putting on the Helm initiated [[Persephone]]'s evaluation as a potential Ruler.[2]
- Judges' Trial Access: The Artifact transported Persephone into the trial administered by The Judges of The Abyss.[2]
- System Connection: Persephone only obtained complete Ruler Authority after successfully passing the trial and being connected to the Soul-processing System.[2][3]
- Regalia Transformation: After Persephone was recognized, the Helm manifested as part of her Ruler regalia, becoming a dark crown containing a red jewel.[1]
- Scythe Transformation: The Artifact can transform into Persephone's enormous dark Scythe.[1]
- Legitimacy Requirement: Merely possessing the Helm is not equivalent to possessing complete Ruler Authority. The candidate must first pass the trial imposed through the Artifact.[2][3]

###### Dark Scythe
[[Persephone]] wielding her Dark Scythe.
- Dark Scythe: The combat manifestation of the Helm of [[Hades]] and Persephone's recognized status as Ruler.[1]
- Divine Weapon: Nyx initially assumed the weapon was [[Thanatos]]' Scythe before realizing that it was Persephone's own Divine Weapon produced from the Helm.[1]

- Weapon Concealment: Persephone can cause the Scythe to disappear from normal perception while it remains physically present, allowing her to conceal the weapon and the timing of her attack.[1]
- Authority Conduction: The weapon functions as an extension and combat expression of Persephone's recognized Ruler status.

##### Primordial-Level Interactions
- Primordial Concealment Breach (Momentary): The enormous burst of power accompanying [[Persephone]]'s awakening temporarily penetrated the concealment surrounding Nyx, something Nyx noted should ordinarily require an existence on the level of a Greek Primordial God.[1]
- Primordial Physical Harm (Dark Scythe): Persephone's Ruler weapon was capable of severing Nyx's arm.[1]
- Primordial Banishment (Within Ruler Territory): Persephone successfully expelled Nyx from the [[Greek Underworld]] through a territorial command recognized by the realm.[1]
- Limitations: These feats are contextual. Chapter 775 later establishes that Persephone cannot automatically perceive or control Nyx everywhere in the greater Greek Underworld and cannot freely govern Nyx's independent territory.[16]

##### Soul Marriage
- [[Soul Marriage]]: [[Persephone]] completed a genuine Soul Marriage with [[Victor Elderblood]] in Chapter 930.[21]
- Permanent Spiritual Bond: The ritual establishes a direct Soul-level connection between Persephone and Victor rather than merely a ceremonial or political marriage.[21]
- Soul Representation: Completion of the marriage caused a new internal representation associated with Persephone to appear within Victor's Personal Universe, allowing [[Roxanne Elderblood]] and [[Amara Elderblood]] to immediately recognize that another Soul Marriage had taken place.[21]
- Conceptual Expression: Persephone's representation inside Victor's spiritual world reflected major portions of her identity, combining an eternally spring-like region with an [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]]-like region.[21]
- Limitations: More advanced mechanics associated with Victor's later use of Soul Marriage should not automatically be back-scaled to Persephone unless the corresponding chapter establishes that the same effect applies to her.

##### Underworld Army and Command Authority
- [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] Army Command: [[Persephone]] can mobilize the Divine and supernatural forces politically and spiritually aligned with the Greek Underworld.
- [[Thanatos]]: Thanatos recognizes Persephone as his Queen and serves as one of the principal executors of her orders.
- [[Hypnos]]: Hypnos operates as one of the senior Underworld Gods within Persephone's wider administration.
- [[Nemesis]]: Nemesis fought as part of Persephone's army during the war against [[Olympus]].
- The Furies: The Furies can operate as combatants under the command structure of Persephone and Thanatos.
- Chthonic Divine Forces: Her forces include numerous Divine beings connected to Death, Dreams, Revenge and other Underworld Concepts, including descendants of Nyx and [[Erebus]].[22]
- Mass Mobilization: Persephone entered the war against Olympus with an enormous force after ordering Thanatos to prepare the armies of the Underworld.[23][22]
- Battlefield Command: Persephone can issue large-scale military orders while simultaneously devoting her own concentration to Ruler-level tasks such as dismantling [[Zeus]]' imposed Territory.[18]

##### New Hell and Soul Administration
[[Persephone]]'s expertise as the former Ruler of the [[Greek Underworld]] remained useful after she became part of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family.
- Afterlife Administration: Persephone possesses extensive practical knowledge regarding the operation of a functional Hell, the movement of Souls and the responsibilities of supernatural Rulers.[2][1]
- Mass Soul Management: During an enormous influx of dead Souls into Victor's infernal system, Persephone was among the experienced Rulers called to help reorganize and process the dead.[24][25]
- Soul Distribution and Processing: She can assist with organizing how large populations of deceased Souls are judged, routed and processed through an afterlife structure.[24][25]
- Ruler Expertise: The memories and information granted after her trial make Persephone one of the Elderblood family's most experienced specialists in supernatural afterlife administration.[1]
- Administrative Scaling: Persephone's usefulness in these systems is not dependent solely upon raw combat power; her specialized understanding of Soul logistics and legitimate Hell governance gives her knowledge that ordinary Gods do not automatically possess.

##### Combat Capabilities
- God-King-Class Combatant: After passing the Judges' trial, [[Persephone]] immediately entered the realm of a God-King while supported by Greek Hell.[1]
- Ruler-Enhanced Combat: Persephone becomes substantially more dangerous while fighting inside territory capable of directly supporting her legitimate Authority.
- Scythe Proficiency: She can effectively use the Dark Scythe produced through the Helm as a combat weapon, successfully surprising and severing the arm of Nyx immediately after acquiring her Ruler state.[1]
- Soul-Based Combat: Persephone possesses the Concept of Soul and understands how attacks infused with Soul can threaten targets beyond conventional physical damage.[10]
- [[Divine Energy]] Control: She can release and control the enormous Divine reserves acquired through her Godhood and Ruler empowerment.
- Miasma Combat Application: Persephone can fill the surrounding environment with Miasma and killing intent when seriously angered.[9]
- Large-Scale Warfare: She has experience commanding armies of Gods and [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] entities in conflicts involving other major Divine factions.[23][22][18]
- Mounted Combat: Persephone entered the war against [[Olympus]] mounted upon a supernatural black winged horse while leading her Underworld forces.[23]

##### Power
[[Persephone]]'s effective strength has changed dramatically throughout the story and should not be represented by a single static classification.

###### Before Becoming a True Ruler
Despite possessing numerous Divinities, [[Persephone]] was described as comparatively weak and poorly developed as a Goddess because she had not mastered the Concepts available to her. Her position as Queen was largely ceremonial while [[Hades]] held the actual Ruler Authority.[1]

###### True Ruler
Passing the Judges' trial transformed [[Persephone]]'s position completely. Nyx initially expected the process to merely elevate Persephone into the range of a High-Level God; instead, Persephone entered the God-King realm and became supported by Greek Hell itself.[1]
Her greatest advantage is territorial. The stronger her synchronization with the legitimate [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] system and Core, the more power Greek Hell can supply to its recognized Ruler.

###### Fully Connected to the Greek Underworld
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] restored [[Persephone]]'s complete connection to the [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] Core, her power increased dramatically enough to place her effective strength within the same broad class as the ancient first-generation Greek [[Primordial Gods]] while she was fully supported by her realm.[19]
This means:
Persephone can reach First-Generation Greek Primordial-class power while fully synchronized with and supported by the Greek Underworld.
It does not mean:
Persephone is a First Generation Primordial God.
Persephone remains a later-generation Olympian Goddess by origin. The comparison concerns the effective power provided by her legitimate Ruler Authority and territorial connection, not a retroactive change to her birth, generation or fundamental Divine origin.

###### Blood Dragon Goddess
Dragonization subsequently raised the quality of the body, Soul and Energy foundation supporting [[Persephone]]'s existing powers. She became a full Blood Dragon while preserving her Divinities and Ruler Authority, meaning that her former God-King and territory-based abilities now operate through a much stronger Draconic existence.[4][6]
The story has not yet provided a new isolated maximum-output feat for current Imperial-era Persephone sufficient to assign a precise modern ceiling independent of her older Ruler feats.

##### Judgment, Knowledge and Non-Combat Skills
- Soul Evaluation: [[Persephone]] possesses extensive experience examining Souls, spiritual intentions and the circumstances surrounding the dead.[12][2]
- Impartial Judgment: The Judges' trial fundamentally reshaped her capacity to separate personal feelings from the decisions required of a Ruler.[3]
- Contextual Reasoning: Persephone understands that identical actions may require different judgments depending upon motive, intent, coercion and circumstance.[2][3]
- Afterlife Expertise: The memories granted upon becoming a Ruler gave her detailed technical understanding of Soul processing, punishment, recycling and the maintenance of a functioning Hell.[1]
- Soul Intent Reading: Thousands of years observing the dead made her extremely skilled at perceiving hostility, emotional intent and spiritual abnormalities through the Soul itself.[12]
- Divine Political Experience: Persephone possesses extensive knowledge of Greek Divine politics, the [[Greek Underworld]] and relationships among numerous ancient Gods.
- Administration: She has direct experience governing a supernatural afterlife and later assisting with vastly larger Soul populations inside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s civilization.[24][25]
- Military Leadership: Persephone can command large supernatural armies and delegate responsibilities among powerful Gods such as [[Thanatos]], [[Hypnos]] and [[Nemesis]].[23][18]

##### Resistances
- Soul Manipulation Resistance: Legitimate Ruler status significantly reduces the effectiveness of ordinary Soul attacks against [[Persephone]] because her Soul is protected by the supernatural structure supporting a Ruler.[15]
- This is powerful resistance rather than absolute immunity. Beings of sufficiently superior spiritual status can still overcome a Ruler's protection.[15]
- Enhanced Soul Resilience: Persephone combines the Soul of an ancient Goddess, the reinforcement of legitimate Ruler Authority and the spiritual foundation of a True Dragon.
- Divine Longevity: Conventional aging and ordinary natural death do not affect her in the manner they affect Mortals.
- Draconic Durability: Her Blood Dragon body possesses vastly greater resilience than her original Divine body.[4][6]
- Territorial Reinforcement: Greek Hell actively reinforces Persephone while she is properly connected to the realm as its Ruler.[1][19]
