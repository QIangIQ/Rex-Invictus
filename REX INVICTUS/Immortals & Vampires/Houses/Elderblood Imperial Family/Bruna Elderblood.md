### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 175: An Angel Sent by God
You are not guilty, the world is guilty. And if the world is wrong, you just need to burn it.
—[[Victor Elderblood]] to Bruna, Chapter 177
Bruna Elderblood, born Bruna Francesca and formerly known as Bruna Alucard, is a True Blood Dragon, a Dragon Goddess of Dragon's Nest, one of Victor Elderblood's wives, and a member of his original [[Maid Squad]]. Originally a Human Catholic nun from California, Bruna became the first Human directly transformed into a Vampire by Victor, establishing the earliest known example of a Vampire created through his own Progenitor Blood rather than through the descendants of [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]]. She possesses the individual supernatural ability of Telekinesis, which developed from the regret she experienced over her inability to rescue her childhood friend, Leticia, and eventually became powerful enough to physically manipulate Gods and simultaneously move the inhabitants of multiple Vampire territories.[3][4][5][6][7]
Bruna's introduction is closely connected to the corruption of the religious institution she trusted throughout her Human life. Father Fernando, a priest associated with her parish, secretly sold women to a Werewolf tribe that abducted Humans for a reproductive ritual intended to produce an Alpha. Bruna's childhood friend Leticia had been among the previous victims, and her disappearance remained unresolved despite Bruna's attempts to obtain help from the police. When Fernando attempted to sell Bruna to the same [[Werewolves]], Victor intervened and exposed what had happened. Rather than simply killing everyone responsible himself, he gave Bruna the opportunity to confront them and choose whether she would take revenge.[3][8]
After Bruna killed the perpetrators, Victor offered her the opportunity to enter the supernatural world as his personal maid. Bruna accepted, and Victor transformed her into a Noble Vampire. The transformation was completed within approximately three hours, a dramatic contrast to Victor's own week-long maturation following his transformation. Bruna's early development demonstrated that Vampires personally created through Victor's Blood could possess extraordinary potential even without previous supernatural experience.[4]
Bruna's religious upbringing had a lasting influence upon her interpretation of Victor. She originally regarded him as an Angel sent by God, but his intervention, extraordinary power, and willingness to help her reclaim control over her life eventually convinced her that he himself was worthy of worship. Victor repeatedly objected to being addressed as God and instructed her to call him Master, but Bruna's conviction remained unchanged. Her devotion subsequently influenced other members of the Maid Squad and eventually spread into the Noble Vampire Clans, establishing the earliest foundation of the [[Blood God Religion]].[4][9]
Her relationship with Victor eventually developed beyond that of master and servant. After years of training and service, Victor formally offered Bruna and several other maids permanent membership in his Clan. Bruna revealed that she had secretly worried about being insufficiently important compared with the powerful women surrounding him, and Victor reassured her that her value was not determined by their status or supernatural strength. His recognition established her place within his family, while her later relationship with him developed into marriage and eventually produced their unnamed biological daughter.[10][11][2]
Bruna's supernatural abilities continued developing throughout Victor's rise to power. During the invasion of [[Olympus]], she demonstrated Telekinesis capable of violently manipulating Divine opponents, while the subsequent construction of Victor's new world required her to lift and transport the inhabitants of the Snow, Fulger, and Adrasteia territories simultaneously. Her later transformation into a genuine Blood Dragon further enhanced her Telekinesis to a level explicitly described as astonishing, placing her among the Dragon Goddesses of Victor's growing family.[6][7][1]
Despite the enormous changes to her Race, power, and social status, Bruna remains closely associated with the original Maid Squad. She continues respecting Kaguya's authority concerning household service, maintains her devotion toward Victor, and participates in ordinary family activities rather than seeking an independent political or religious position. Her life therefore encompasses several distinct identities: former nun, rescued Human, first direct Vampire descendant, Battle Maid, foundational religious devotee, Clan member, wife, mother, and Dragon Goddess.

### Physical Description

#### Human
During her Human life, Bruna was a beautiful young woman with long black hair, sapphire-blue eyes, fair skin, and an approximately 170 cm tall figure. Her appearance contrasted with the conservative clothing associated with her occupation as a Catholic nun. At her introduction, she wore a traditional religious habit, although the clothing had been damaged by the [[Werewolves]] attempting to inspect her before the transaction arranged by Father Fernando.[3][4]
Her original appearance included a slender yet naturally developed figure. Chapter 177 explicitly describes Bruna as approximately 170 cm tall before becoming a Vampire, with her subsequent transformation producing a substantial increase in height and physical maturity. Because [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] initially knew little about her identity, he frequently referred to her simply as "Nun" rather than using her actual name.[4]
Bruna's Human appearance was accompanied by a relatively gentle demeanor reflecting her sheltered religious background. Although she could become outspoken when confronting injustice, her earliest reactions to supernatural beings were characterized by fear and confusion, particularly after discovering that the men involved in her trafficking were Werewolves rather than ordinary Humans.[3]

#### Noble Vampire
Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s direct transformation, Bruna's physical characteristics changed dramatically. Her height increased from 170 cm to 183 cm, her sapphire-blue eyes became blood red, and her skin acquired the paler complexion associated with Vampires. Her black hair became considerably longer, extending approximately to her lower back, while her body developed the enhanced proportions and supernatural beauty characteristic of Noble Vampires.[4]
The transformation was exceptionally rapid. Victor compared Bruna's development with his own experience and recognized that she had attained her new physical condition within approximately three hours, whereas he had required an entire week to complete his transformation. The difference attracted his attention because Bruna was the first Vampire created directly through his own Bloodline rather than through the conventional lineage originating from Vlad.[4]
Bruna subsequently adopted the maid uniform associated with Victor's household. The outfit replaced the religious clothing she had worn during her Human life and became closely connected with her new identity as a Battle Maid. Her appearance was often a source of teasing from Kaguya and the other women, who enjoyed pointing out the contrast between her former occupation and the dramatic physical changes caused by Victor's Blood.[4][5]
Although her appearance changed, several familiar characteristics remained. Bruna retained her long black hair, expressive facial features, and tendency to become embarrassed when the other maids made provocative remarks. Her physical maturation therefore did not immediately eliminate the habits or emotional characteristics associated with her Human upbringing.

#### Blood Dragon
Bruna following her evolution into a True Blood Dragon.
Bruna later became one of the women transformed into a genuine True Blood Dragon through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Draconic lineage. Her height increased again to approximately 197 cm, while her body acquired the supernatural characteristics associated with [[True Dragons]]. Unlike some of the other women, whose transformations produced considerable changes to their overall proportions, Bruna's appearance remained comparatively recognizable because her Noble Vampire body had already undergone substantial development.[1]
Her new form acquired the characteristic Draconic features associated with Victor's Race, including Dragon horns, wings, and the altered supernatural eyes of a True Dragon. The transformation also provided a more advanced physical foundation, allowing her body to support considerably greater quantities and qualities of supernatural power.[1]
The most important transformation concerned her individual ability rather than her outward appearance. Bruna's Telekinesis experienced an extraordinary qualitative improvement, being specifically identified among the powers that had advanced most dramatically following the creation of Victor's new True Dragons.[1]
Bruna's current appearance therefore combines the recognizable features of her earlier Vampire form with the height, presence, and Draconic characteristics of her evolved existence. Although she possesses the status of a Dragon Goddess, she continues frequently appearing in clothing associated with the household rather than exclusively wearing ceremonial Divine garments.

### Personality
Bruna is affectionate, compassionate, intensely loyal, emotionally expressive, and deeply devoted to the people she considers family. Her personality was shaped by an upbringing without known biological relatives, sincere religious belief, the loss of her closest childhood friend, and the traumatic discovery that the institution she trusted had knowingly participated in the exploitation of innocent women. Unlike individuals who entered supernatural society after growing up around Vampires, Witches, Gods, or other supernatural beings, Bruna possessed an ordinary Human understanding of morality and religion before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] changed her life.[3][8][4]
During her Human life, Bruna frequently hesitated when confronted with difficult decisions. Leticia recognized this trait and encouraged her to become more assertive, believing that Bruna needed to stop allowing circumstances to determine her future. Bruna's indecision was not simply cowardice; she feared making choices that contradicted the moral principles she had been taught and was accustomed to seeking reassurance through the rules of the Church. Her encounter with Victor forced her to recognize that those principles could be manipulated by people who had no genuine concern for the suffering of others.[8]
The revelation concerning Leticia created a fundamental conflict between Bruna's religious convictions and her desire for revenge. Although she regarded killing as sinful, the men responsible had deliberately abducted and abused innocent women for an unproven supernatural objective. Victor rejected the idea that their actions deserved forgiveness merely because Bruna's former moral framework condemned violence, instead encouraging her to accept responsibility for the decision she wished to make. Bruna eventually chose revenge, discovering that she was capable of considerable anger and satisfaction when punishing those responsible for Leticia's death.[8][4]
Her subsequent emotional turmoil demonstrated that the decision had not been easy. Bruna initially feared that killing the perpetrators meant she had become evil or abandoned everything she once believed. Victor reassured her that she was not responsible for the cruelty inflicted upon her and that the people who exploited her could not determine the moral value of her future. His words provided the emotional justification she needed to abandon the institution that had betrayed her and establish a different life.[4]
Bruna's loyalty toward Victor consequently developed with unusual intensity. She interpreted his arrival as divine intervention and regarded him as the person who had answered the prayers left unanswered by the Church. After he transformed her into a Vampire, she concluded that he deserved the Faith she had previously directed toward the [[Heavenly Father]]. Her devotion was sincere rather than a calculated attempt to obtain greater supernatural power, and she continued addressing Victor with reverence even when he requested that she use the more ordinary title of Master.[4]
This devotion became one of Bruna's defining characteristics. She is willing to obey Victor's instructions, place herself in danger for his sake, and oppose individuals who threaten the household. However, her loyalty is not restricted to a formal servant-master relationship. Bruna loves Victor romantically, values his affection, and regards him as the foundation of the family she had never possessed during childhood. Her feelings therefore encompass religious Faith, gratitude, romantic love, and the trust of someone who believes she has finally found a permanent home.[10]
Despite her extraordinary devotion, Bruna can be insecure about her own importance. Victor's family contains ancient Noble Vampires, experienced warriors, powerful Goddesses, and supernatural rulers whose accomplishments and origins considerably exceed those of an ordinary Human. Bruna sometimes compared herself unfavorably with these women, secretly believing that she lacked the status or power necessary to deserve the same recognition. Her fears became especially apparent when Victor offered the maids permanent membership in his Clan, causing her to become emotional enough to abandon her customary title of Master and address him by name.[10]
Victor immediately recognized the insecurity she had struggled to express. He assured Bruna that she was important and sufficient as she was, emphasizing that his wives, family, and trusted companions remained essential to his life regardless of his increasingly enormous responsibilities. Bruna's response demonstrated how deeply she desired explicit familial recognition, particularly after spending much of her Human life without the security of a conventional family.[10]
Bruna's religious background remains visible in her daily behavior. She frequently becomes embarrassed when the other maids discuss provocative subjects, and she sometimes insists that she remains a nun despite having abandoned her former religious institution and established a romantic relationship with Victor. Kaguya, Maria, and Roberta enjoy teasing her about this contradiction, occasionally describing her as a puritan or deliberately provoking her embarrassment. Bruna can become outspoken during these exchanges, especially when she believes that the others are being disrespectful in front of Victor.[5]
Her reactions should not be confused with an inability to understand the circumstances of her current life. Bruna gradually became comfortable with the unusual relationships surrounding Victor's household and willingly accepted the affection and companionship available within it. Her embarrassment became a recognizable personality trait rather than evidence of continuing loyalty to the Church that had betrayed her.
Bruna also possesses considerable respect for professional discipline. Kaguya began training her shortly after Victor transformed her and immediately demonstrated that exceptional physical strength was insufficient without technique, experience, and the willingness to confront danger. Bruna learned to follow Kaguya's instructions even when the older maid used harsh methods or teased her relentlessly. The relationship eventually developed into genuine familial affection, with Bruna continuing to recognize Kaguya as the senior member of the [[Maid Squad]] despite their later transformation into Dragon Goddesses.[12][13][14]
Her loyalty extends to the other maids as well. Eve, Maria, Roberta, Roxanne, and Kaguya became an important part of the family Bruna established after leaving her Human life. Although they possess radically different backgrounds and personalities, they share the experience of being accepted by Victor and developing relationships that eventually surpassed their original status as servants. Bruna's Faith influenced several of these women, while their friendship and shared experiences helped her gain confidence in her place within the household.[9]
Bruna's devotion does not mean that she seeks worship for herself. After becoming a Dragon Goddess, she was explicitly identified among the women who did not maintain prominent public religious representation or regularly appear before worshippers. Her original religious influence remained directed toward Victor, even though her own power and status had become sufficient for her to be recognized as a Goddess.[15]
By the Imperial era, Bruna's personality remains recognizable despite the passage of enormous periods of time and her development into an exceptionally powerful True Dragon. She continues participating in ordinary household activities, assisting with family gatherings, respecting Kaguya's professional pride, and reacting warmly to the younger generation. Her relationship with Victor is now supported by the security of marriage, motherhood, and permanent Clan membership rather than the uncertainty that accompanied her earliest days as a rescued Human.[16][14][17]

### History

#### Early Life
Bruna Francesca was born Human and spent her childhood in an orphanage. She did not possess a known biological family, and the institution in which she was raised eventually closed, leaving her without an established household to return to. The circumstances contributed to her desire for belonging and made her later acceptance into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family especially important.[4]
One of the most significant people in her early life was Leticia, a childhood friend who possessed a considerably more decisive personality. Leticia frequently criticized Bruna's tendency to hesitate and encouraged her to make choices for herself rather than remain uncertain about her future. She also dreamed of establishing a large family connected by Blood, believing that such a family would provide something different from the relationships available within the orphanage.[8]
Bruna supported Leticia's ambitions and believed that her friend deserved to experience the happiness she imagined. Their relationship remained important after childhood, making Leticia's subsequent disappearance one of the most distressing events in Bruna's Human life.
Bruna eventually entered the Catholic Church and became a nun. Her religious devotion appears to have been genuine; she regarded the Church as a source of moral guidance and believed that its representatives could be trusted to act according to the principles they publicly preached. This belief made the later actions of Father Fernando particularly devastating.
Several months before Bruna encountered Victor, Leticia disappeared. Bruna searched for her and attempted to obtain assistance from the police, but the investigation produced little progress. She remained unaware that Fernando had sold Leticia to a Werewolf tribe through an arrangement involving the trafficking of women from his parish.[3]
The truth emerged when Fernando attempted to sell Bruna herself. Her discovery that the priest had knowingly participated in Leticia's disappearance destroyed the remaining security associated with her former religious life and created the circumstances that led to her transformation into Victor's first direct Vampire descendant.[3][8]

### Synopsis

#### A New Pillar
Bruna entered the narrative during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s visit to California, where Father Fernando had arranged to sell her to a small Werewolf tribe in exchange for valuable jewels. The group arrived at the Church and inspected Bruna while Fernando treated the transaction as an ordinary business arrangement. When the priest mentioned Leticia as a previous example of someone who had been sold, Bruna immediately recognized that her friend's disappearance was connected to the men surrounding her.[3]
The revelation caused Bruna to confront Fernando, demanding to know why he had betrayed the women who trusted him. Rather than expressing remorse, the priest attempted to justify the arrangement through the financial needs of the Church. He also indicated that he could provide additional women in the future, demonstrating that the transaction was not an isolated occurrence.[3]
Victor had been moving through California with Kaguya when he heard Bruna's distress. His supernatural senses revealed the presence of the [[Werewolves]], and he entered the Church before the transaction could be completed. After overwhelming the attackers, he created an Ice barrier preventing their escape and forced them to explain why they had been purchasing Human women.[3][8]
The Werewolves revealed that their tribe lacked an Alpha and believed that producing a large number of offspring at approximately the same time might result in one being born. To pursue this possibility, they had been abducting women and using them in a reproductive ritual. The tribe did not know whether the belief was accurate, yet continued carrying out the practice despite the deaths and suffering it caused.[8]
Bruna learned that Leticia had been one of the victims. Remembering her friend's dream of establishing a family, she was devastated by the circumstances of her death. Victor recognized her grief and encouraged her to decide personally how the perpetrators should be punished. He created an Ice sword and placed the responsibility for revenge in Bruna's hands rather than eliminating the men without involving her.[8]
Bruna initially hesitated because murder contradicted the religious principles she had spent much of her life practicing. However, she remembered Leticia's encouragement to act decisively and recognized that the men before her had deliberately exploited innocent women. She eventually accepted the weapon and attacked the perpetrators, killing the priest and the Werewolves involved in the transaction.[8][4]
Afterward, Bruna experienced conflicting feelings of satisfaction, hatred, fear, and guilt. She had never previously killed another person, and the realization that she had enjoyed punishing the people responsible frightened her. Victor refused to condemn her for her actions, explaining that the people who had victimized her were responsible for the circumstances that forced the decision. His words helped Bruna accept that she could choose a different future rather than remain controlled by the betrayal she had suffered.[4]
Victor subsequently offered Bruna a place within his household. She could attempt to return to ordinary Human society or enter the supernatural world as one of his personal maids. Bruna accepted the latter, having already lost her confidence in the institution that had previously provided her purpose and belonging.[4]
Without knowing every detail of the conventional Vampire transformation ritual, Victor followed his instincts and bit Bruna, allowing his Progenitor Blood to initiate the change. Her body transformed rapidly, acquiring the enhanced physical characteristics of a Noble Vampire. Approximately three hours later, Bruna stood beside Victor in a maid uniform, already possessing a completed Vampire body whose development had required far less time than his own.[4]
The transformation was historically significant because Bruna became the first Vampire created directly through Victor's Bloodline. Unlike Victor himself, whose transformation originated through Violet and the older Vampire lineage, Bruna represented the beginning of a new generation whose supernatural foundation was derived directly from him.[4]
Bruna immediately began worshipping Victor. She initially referred to him as God, believing that the man who had rescued her and provided a new existence was the supernatural answer to her prayers. Victor instructed her to call him Master instead, but her conviction remained unchanged. Kaguya, meanwhile, began supervising the new maid and quickly gave her the nickname "Lusty Maid," initiating a relationship characterized by professional discipline and frequent teasing.[4]
Victor then traveled with Kaguya and Bruna to the Werewolf settlement associated with the perpetrators. He intended to test Bruna's newly acquired strength and determine whether she possessed the determination necessary to survive the supernatural world she had chosen to enter. The operation also allowed Bruna to confront the wider group responsible for the abduction and exploitation of women like Leticia.[18]
Bruna's physical abilities proved exceptional even by the standards of a newly transformed Noble Vampire. Her attacks possessed enough force to overpower Werewolves who would ordinarily have been dangerous opponents for someone with almost no combat experience. However, her movements lacked the efficiency of a trained supernatural fighter, and she initially struggled to employ her new strength without unnecessary hesitation.[12]
Kaguya demonstrated the enormous difference between natural power and combat mastery. Through the assassination techniques of Clan Blank, she rapidly eliminated opponents while controlling the battlefield far more efficiently than Bruna. The encounter became an important part of Bruna's education, establishing the level of skill Victor expected his maids to develop.[12][13]
Victor subsequently encountered [[Eve Elderblood]], another Human whose life had been defined by severe abuse. He transformed Eve and brought both women into his household, where they became part of the developing [[Maid Squad]]. During their introduction to the family, Victor clarified their positions: Kaguya was his first maid, Bruna his second, and Eve his third. Eve also became the first formally named member of Clan Alucard besides Victor himself, despite Bruna having been transformed earlier.[19][20]
Victor explained that he wanted a limited number of exceptionally capable maids rather than an enormous household of ordinary servants. Bruna and Eve consequently entered a demanding environment in which their extraordinary natural potential would be refined through combat instruction, practical experience, and the guidance of the more accomplished members of the family.[20]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Bruna continued developing as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household expanded. Her original transformation had provided exceptional physical strength, but her lack of experience remained a significant limitation. Kaguya continued supervising her combat education, while [[Scathach]] gradually became involved in training the maids as Victor's faction encountered increasingly dangerous opponents.[21]
The growing importance of the [[Maid Squad]] changed Victor's expectations of its members. He did not want the women to depend entirely upon his ability to intervene whenever danger appeared, particularly because their proximity to him made them potential targets of hostile supernatural factions. Bruna therefore continued developing practical combat abilities rather than relying solely upon the strength granted by his Bloodline.[21]
Victor also personally participated in her martial instruction. During his training of [[Nero]], he brought Bruna into the lesson and explained how a powerful physical strike should be performed. He demonstrated that an effective punch begins with the positioning of the legs, transfers force through the hips and back, and only then releases that accumulated power through the shoulder and arm. The explanation was particularly relevant to Bruna because her early fighting had depended heavily upon raw strength rather than the coordination of her entire body.[22]
Her relationship with the other maids became increasingly familiar as the household developed. Kaguya remained the most experienced and disciplined member, while Bruna, Eve, Maria, Roberta, and Roxanne gradually became capable of participating in dangerous missions rather than remaining passive residents of Victor's mansion. Their shared experiences helped establish the familial relationships that would later be formally acknowledged by Victor.[23][24]
Bruna's religious devotion also continued influencing the household during this period. Her worship of Victor initially appeared to be a personal eccentricity originating from her former occupation, but other women gradually adopted similar convictions. The growing Faith directed toward Victor would eventually become a significant religious movement, although its full scope remained unknown to him for some time.[9]

#### Demonic War
During the Demonic War, Bruna remained an established member of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Battle Maid group. By this period, the organization had developed into a collection of trusted combatants whose responsibilities extended beyond domestic service and included the protection of Victor's family, cooperation during supernatural operations, and direct participation in dangerous conflicts.
A major personal development occurred when Victor gathered Kaguya, Bruna, Maria, and Roberta and offered them permanent membership in his Clan. He explained that their relationships had progressed beyond ordinary employment and that the women standing before him were people he regarded as family. The proposal carried considerable significance because becoming a permanent member of a Vampire Clan represented a lasting commitment rather than a temporary professional arrangement.[10]
Bruna's reaction revealed feelings she had concealed for a long time. She became visibly emotional and addressed Victor by name, admitting that she had wondered whether she possessed enough value to deserve such recognition. Victor immediately understood her insecurity and assured her that she was important to him regardless of the increasingly powerful women surrounding him. He emphasized that the people who continued helping him through wars and political responsibilities remained among the most important parts of his life.[10]
Bruna accepted the offer and received the name Bruna Alucard. The moment established her place within the Clan and gave her the familial security she had desired since childhood. Her emotional attachment to Victor now included an explicit recognition that she belonged to his family rather than merely serving him because he had saved her.[10]
Victor subsequently strengthened the maids through his Bloodline, improving their physical and supernatural characteristics. During his examination of the powers awakened by Vampires he had personally created, he considered how the emotional histories of Bruna and Eve had influenced their individual abilities. Bruna's Telekinesis originated from her regret over being unable to reach Leticia in time, while Eve's altered Fire was connected to the circumstances surrounding her own traumatic past.[25][5]
The explanation established that Bruna's Telekinesis was not simply a random ability produced by becoming a Vampire. Her deepest regret had shaped the supernatural power she awakened, granting her the ability to affect distant objects and individuals without physically crossing the space separating them. The power therefore possessed a direct thematic relationship with Leticia's death and Bruna's desire to prevent similar failures in the future.[5]
Bruna's permanent place within the family was reaffirmed when Victor reorganized Clan Alucard. He established that the Clan would not become an ordinary aristocratic House filled with distant relatives and political dependents. Instead, it would consist of the people he personally considered family, with membership representing a lasting relationship rather than a convenient title.[11]
When Victor questioned Bruna, Maria, and Roberta concerning their decision, Bruna immediately reaffirmed her willingness to follow him. She explained that Victor had been her God since the day he rescued her from the [[Werewolves]] and that she would remain beside him even if his future led into Hell. Her answer confirmed the same loyalty she had expressed during their first encounter, now supported by years of personal experience and affection.[11]
These events establish Bruna's formal admission and continued commitment to the Clan. They should not be confused with the depiction of a separate wedding ceremony. Her later status as Victor's wife belongs to the subsequent development of their relationship rather than a specifically documented marriage ritual in the Clan-admission scene.

#### King Among Kings
Bruna's religious influence became increasingly important after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s victories began changing the supernatural political structure of Earth, [[Nightingale]], and Hell. His conquest of [[Biblical Hell]], public reputation, and the decline of confidence in older religious institutions created conditions in which growing numbers of people began directing their Faith toward him.
Victor eventually learned that this worship had already developed into a substantial organized religion. Its earliest foundation was Bruna, whose sincere devotion had influenced Maria, Eve, Roberta, and later members of the Fulger and Snow Clans. Kaguya eventually became involved in organizing the growing movement, while Ruby, Violet, Sasha, Roxanne, and other members of the family subsequently helped establish the administrative structures necessary to support its expansion.[26][9]
By the time Victor attended the ceremony introducing the religion's High Priestess, the [[Blood God Religion]] had developed followers throughout Hell, Nightingale, and large portions of Earth. Its organizers had established temples, internal ranks, membership requirements, disciplinary rules, and a system through which dedicated followers could obtain rewards and opportunities for advancement.[9]
Victor recognized the irony that an enormous institution had formed around him without his direct involvement. Although his accomplishments attracted worshippers, the practical development of the religion resulted primarily from the work of his wives and trusted subordinates. Bruna occupied a particularly important place in this history because she had been his first genuine devotee and had helped inspire the original group of believers from which the movement expanded.[9]
Bruna did not become the religion's administrative High Priestess. That responsibility was eventually entrusted to [[Valeria Alekerth]], while other women helped manage the institution's growth. Bruna's importance remained foundational: her original Faith preceded the formal religious structure and provided one of the earliest examples of worship directed toward Victor as a Divine Being.[9]
Her personal combat abilities also developed significantly. During the invasion of [[Olympus]], Victor's [[Maid Squad]] entered the battlefield under Kaguya's command, accompanied by other powerful members of his faction. The maids possessed weapons and supernatural abilities capable of harming Divine opponents, allowing them to participate in a conflict far beyond the scale of Bruna's earliest battles against [[Werewolves]].[27]
During the fighting, Bruna used Telekinesis to seize and violently twist Gods across the battlefield. The narration compared the affected opponents to rag dolls, demonstrating that her ability had advanced sufficiently to physically dominate Divine beings whose defenses were unable to resist its force.[6]
The feat represented a dramatic increase from her first days as a Vampire. Bruna had originally possessed exceptional raw physical strength but little combat technique, while her Telekinesis had been associated with the regret of being unable to save one Human friend. By the assault against Olympus, that same power had become effective against supernatural opponents whose existence greatly exceeded the limits of ordinary Vampires.[5][6]
A more extensive demonstration followed during Victor's construction of a new planetary territory. He intended to relocate the lands belonging to the Snow, Fulger, and Adrasteia Clans, requiring the simultaneous movement of enormous sections of terrain and the protection of the inhabitants residing within them.[7]
Victor called upon Bruna because Telekinesis was particularly suitable for removing large numbers of people without physically disrupting their homes or the land itself. After she touched Victor, Bruna temporarily perceived the affected region through his senses, allowing her to identify the inhabitants and their possessions. She then released a large telekinetic field, causing the Vampires within the three territories to rise into the air.[7]
Bruna maintained control while Victor moved the terrain and other members of the family supported the larger planetary project. The inhabitants were transported to a designated safe location near the [[Snow Clan]], together with their personal belongings. Bruna's role therefore involved the protection and movement of the population rather than the creation or transportation of the planetary landmasses themselves.[7]
The effort exhausted her because she had never previously employed Telekinesis across so many targets and such an extensive area. Nevertheless, the experience provided new ideas for developing the ability, demonstrating that her power could serve enormous logistical and protective purposes in addition to direct combat.[7]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Bruna's existence changed fundamentally when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] developed the ability to transform members of his family into genuine [[True Dragons]]. She became one of the women converted into the True Blood Dragon lineage, acquiring a considerably more advanced physical foundation while retaining the powers associated with her previous Vampire existence.[1]
Her transformation increased her height to approximately 197 cm and provided the supernatural characteristics associated with True Dragons. The process did not erase the abilities she had already developed; instead, their previous Bloodlines and personal powers became foundations upon which the Draconic transformation could produce further improvements.[1]
The newly created True Dragons were described as possessing a closer relationship with Creation, allowing them to influence its supernatural structures on a smaller scale than Victor. They also acquired improved metamorphosis and elemental affinities, while several individuals developed unusual combinations of powers originating from their former abilities and new Draconic characteristics.[1]
Bruna's individual development was specifically highlighted because her Telekinesis reached an astonishing level. This statement establishes a substantial increase in the quality and potential of her unique power following Dragonization, although the chapter does not provide a precise measurement of its maximum range, output, or the greatest number of targets she could subsequently manipulate.[1]
Her new racial existence eventually contributed to her recognition as a Dragon Goddess within the supernatural hierarchy associated with Victor's family. However, Bruna did not abandon the personal identity she had developed through the [[Maid Squad]]. She continued serving Victor alongside the women who had accompanied him during his earliest adventures, maintaining the familiar relationship that had existed long before the emergence of his later Empire.
During the family's vacation, Bruna participated in a soccer match alongside Roxanne and Maria against Kaguya, Ruby, and Amara. The recreational event demonstrated that even after becoming enormously powerful supernatural beings, the women continued participating in ordinary activities and enjoying the informal relationships established through their family.[28]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
As [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s civilization developed and his family began experiencing enormous periods of accelerated time, Bruna continued living as one of his wives and personal Battle Maids. The expansion of the Elderblood family also introduced a new generation of daughters, many of whom possessed supernatural potential exceeding that of ordinary Gods.
Bruna eventually became the biological mother of an unnamed daughter with Victor. The child's existence is directly established during the description of the daughters' residential area in Chapter 1051, which identifies her among the younger members of the family.[2]
The daughters occupied a separate residential section of Victor's enormous mansion. Their individual rooms could be expanded or reduced through Runes, allowing each child to select an environment suited to her preferences. While some preferred exceptionally large and luxurious rooms, Bruna's daughter was specifically identified as favoring accommodations that were comparatively small, functional, simple, and elegant.[2]
The detail provides one of the clearest individual characteristics presently established for Bruna's child. Her name and complete supernatural abilities have not been identified in the reviewed passages, so they should not be inferred from her mother's Telekinesis or assigned without additional evidence.
Bruna's motherhood also represents an important development when considered alongside her Human history. Leticia had dreamed of creating a family connected by Blood, while Bruna herself had been raised without known biological parents. Through Victor, Bruna ultimately became part of an enormous household and established a biological family of her own.
Her position as a Dragon Goddess developed alongside the expansion of the [[Blood God Religion]]. Temples erected throughout the Empire displayed representations of Divine figures associated with Victor's Pantheon, but not every Dragon Goddess appeared publicly or maintained a personal group of worshippers.[15]
Bruna was specifically identified among the Goddesses whose images were absent from certain public religious displays because they did not seek prominent worship or regularly appear before the population. Kaguya, Eve, Roberta, Maria, and Maya were included in the same general category.[15]
This distinction is particularly meaningful because Bruna had helped originate the religious movement surrounding Victor. Even after becoming a Goddess herself, she did not attempt to transform the religion into an institution primarily devoted to her own status. Her Faith remained directed toward Victor rather than the acquisition of personal religious authority.
Bruna continued maintaining her place within the original [[Maid Squad]] and the larger Imperial household while the Empire's scale expanded far beyond the supernatural societies she had known when she first became a Vampire.

#### Those Who Rule Reality
During the mature Imperial era, Bruna continued participating in the ordinary activities and traditions of the increasingly enormous Elderblood family. Although the household now included cosmic-level supernatural beings, numerous daughters, and women whose responsibilities extended across galaxies, the original maids retained their familiar relationships with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and one another.
During the family gathering preceding the Heavenly Feast, Bruna arrived alongside Hela, Nyx, Eve, Mizuki, and Violet. The women participated in the organization of a traditional family meal, reflecting their shared desire to preserve familiar customs despite the extraordinary development of their civilization.[16]
Violet instructed Bruna to summon their remaining sisters and requested that she bring [[Tyche]] and the Valkyries as well. Bruna noticed Violet's unusual interest in their participation and questioned what she had seen, but accepted the instruction and proceeded with the task.[16]
The gathering eventually involved the preparation of an enormous Star Beast. Its size and unusual biological characteristics attracted the attention of several members of the family, transforming an ordinary household meal into an event requiring considerable supernatural coordination. Bruna assisted in bringing additional participants, including Aline, whose ongoing research was interrupted by the summons.[16]
During the subsequent Heavenly Feast, Bruna offered to help Kaguya serve the family. Kaguya immediately refused, regarding the opportunity to perform her duties as Victor's perfect maid as a matter of personal pride. Bruna recognized that the older maid would not accept assistance and returned obediently to her seat rather than challenging her decision.[14]
The exchange demonstrated the continuing familiarity of their relationship. Bruna had long since become a Dragon Goddess, yet her experiences under Kaguya's strict supervision remained sufficiently memorable that the older maid's determination could still intimidate her.
Bruna also participated in the family gathering following the birth of [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]]. As the youngest daughter interacted with her mothers and reacted to their distinctive characteristics, Bruna observed the familiar preference displayed by Victor's children for their father. Her reaction reflected her growing comfort within the household and the ordinary affection she shared with the younger generation.[17]
These later appearances establish the continuity of Bruna's personality and family relationships rather than a newly demonstrated individual supernatural technique. Her greatest confirmed power development remains the enormous enhancement of her Telekinesis following Dragonization, while her later life reflects her established position as Victor's wife, mother of his daughter, and one of the original Battle Maids.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Bruna's husband, the father of her daughter, and the individual responsible for transforming her from an ordinary Human nun into a Vampire and eventually a True Blood Dragon. Their relationship is one of the earliest examples of Victor personally changing the life of someone outside supernatural society through direct intervention rather than an inherited political or familial obligation.
Bruna originally regarded Victor as an Angel sent by God after he intervened during her attempted trafficking. His willingness to reveal the truth about Leticia and place the decision of revenge in her hands profoundly affected her because it contrasted with the behavior of the institution she had trusted. Victor subsequently offered her a new existence rather than leaving her alone with the consequences of the betrayal, establishing the foundation of her loyalty toward him.[3][8][4]
After becoming a Vampire, Bruna increasingly interpreted Victor as her God. He initially objected and requested that she call him Master, but her devotion remained sincere. Although this religious interpretation influenced the formation of the [[Blood God Religion]], it also developed into genuine personal affection as Bruna spent years serving him and becoming familiar with his family.[4][9]
Victor recognized Bruna's importance through both his actions and the responsibilities he entrusted to her. He arranged her combat education, placed her under Kaguya's supervision, and personally instructed her in [[Martial Arts]]. Her later participation in the relocation of the Count territories also demonstrated that Victor trusted her individual power during operations affecting enormous numbers of civilians.[22][7]
Their emotional relationship deepened when Victor offered Bruna permanent membership in his Clan. Her response revealed that she had feared being less important than the more powerful women surrounding him, and Victor directly reassured her that she was essential to his family. His recognition provided the security she had been seeking since childhood.[10]
Bruna subsequently became one of Victor's wives and the mother of his unnamed daughter. Her feelings toward him encompass gratitude, Faith, romantic love, trust, and the absolute loyalty of someone who regards him as the foundation of the family she never previously possessed.

#### Leticia
Leticia was Bruna's childhood friend and one of the most important people in her Human life. The two shared an upbringing connected to the absence of ordinary parental support, but possessed different personalities. Leticia was outspoken and determined, while Bruna frequently hesitated when making decisions and preferred following the principles she had been taught.[8]
Leticia dreamed of establishing a large family connected by Blood. She hoped to create relationships that would provide the sense of belonging she had lacked during childhood, and Bruna encouraged her ambition. Their friendship remained important after they became adults, making Leticia's subsequent disappearance particularly distressing.[8]
Bruna attempted to discover what had happened and sought help from the police, but the investigation remained unresolved. She eventually learned that Father Fernando had sold Leticia to the [[Werewolves]] responsible for the abduction and exploitation of women from the parish.[3]
The revelation became the immediate cause of Bruna's decision to kill the perpetrators. More importantly, her regret over failing to save Leticia continued influencing her existence after becoming a Vampire. Chapter 592 identifies that regret as the emotional foundation of her Telekinesis, linking one of her most important supernatural abilities directly to her Human history.[5]
Leticia therefore remains significant to Bruna's character despite dying before her friend's introduction to supernatural society. Bruna's transformation, personal motivations, and extraordinary individual ability are all connected to the circumstances of Leticia's death.

#### Kaguya Elderblood Blank
[[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]] is Bruna's senior within the [[Maid Squad]], one of her closest companions, and the principal figure responsible for her earliest training as a Battle Maid. Kaguya began supervising Bruna almost immediately after her transformation, recognizing that the new Vampire possessed exceptional raw strength but almost no experience using it effectively.[4][12]
Their relationship is characterized by a combination of discipline, teasing, and genuine affection. Kaguya frequently mocks Bruna's reactions to provocative subjects and originally gave her the nickname "Lusty Maid." Bruna often protests against these remarks, particularly when she believes they are inappropriate in front of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[4][5]
Despite the teasing, Bruna recognizes Kaguya's extraordinary combat and professional abilities. During her first battle against the Werewolf tribe, Kaguya demonstrated the difference between natural power and trained assassination techniques, establishing a standard toward which Bruna was expected to develop.[12]
Their relationship continued throughout Victor's rise to power. Both women entered his family, developed into extraordinarily powerful supernatural beings, and maintained their association with the original Maid Squad. During the Heavenly Feast, Bruna's immediate acceptance of Kaguya's refusal to share the serving duties demonstrated that their familiar professional hierarchy remained recognizable even after both became Dragon Goddesses.[14]

#### Maid Squad
The [[Maid Squad]] became Bruna's primary social and professional group after leaving her Human life. Its original members possessed different backgrounds and supernatural abilities but were united through their loyalty toward [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and their commitment to protecting his family.
Bruna's closest companions include Kaguya, Eve, Maria, Roberta, and Roxanne. Each woman entered Victor's household under different circumstances, but their shared training and experiences gradually transformed their relationships beyond ordinary employment. Victor eventually acknowledged this development by offering permanent Clan membership to the maids who had continuously supported him.[10]
Bruna's religious influence was especially important during the group's early history. Her Faith affected Maria, Eve, Roberta, and others, creating the first circle of worshippers whose beliefs eventually contributed to the development of the [[Blood God Religion]].[9]
Although the maids later became Dragon Goddesses and members of an Imperial family, they continued identifying with the household organization that originally brought them together. Bruna's willingness to assist during family gatherings and her continued respect for Kaguya demonstrate the persistence of these relationships.

#### Eve Elderblood
[[Eve Elderblood]] joined [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household shortly after Bruna's transformation. Both had been ordinary Humans whose lives were dramatically altered by Victor's intervention, giving them a shared emotional foundation despite the differences in their individual histories.[19][20]
Bruna's unique Telekinesis originated from her regret concerning Leticia, while Eve's altered Fire was connected to the traumatic events surrounding her own Human life. Their development provided Victor with examples of how the strongest emotions and experiences of a Human could influence the individual abilities awakened through direct transformation into a Vampire.[5]
The two subsequently trained under Kaguya and became established members of the [[Maid Squad]]. Their shared devotion toward Victor and continued participation in his household helped establish a close familial relationship that persisted into the Imperial era.

#### Maria Elderblood
[[Maria Elderblood]] is one of Bruna's fellow Battle Maids and among the women influenced by her original devotion toward [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Maria possesses a more openly provocative personality than Bruna and frequently enjoys teasing her, particularly concerning the contrast between Bruna's former religious profession and her current life.[5]
Despite their differences, the two women share an unusually strong loyalty toward Victor and became permanent members of his family. Their friendship continued through the expansion of the [[Maid Squad]] and the development of the [[Blood God Religion]], whose earliest circle of followers included both women.[9]

#### Roberta Elderblood
[[Roberta Elderblood]] is one of Bruna's fellow Battle Maids and another member of the household whose devotion was influenced by Bruna's example. Roberta possesses a distinctive supernatural existence connected with [[Medusa]], creating a contrast with Bruna's simpler development as a Human transformed directly through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Bloodline.
The two women became closely associated through their shared service and participation in Victor's Clan. They formally reaffirmed their desire to remain within his family during the same reorganization, demonstrating their commitment to maintaining the relationships established through years of shared experiences.[10][11]

#### Blood God Religion
Bruna occupies a foundational position in the [[Blood God Religion]]. Unlike later administrators and High Priestesses, she began worshipping [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] before a formal institution existed around him. Her devotion originated from the belief that he had acted as the Divine savior who rescued her from the people responsible for Leticia's death.[4]
The Faith she directed toward him influenced Maria, Eve, Roberta, and eventually Noble Vampires from the Fulger and Snow Clans. As Victor's accomplishments attracted greater attention, the movement continued expanding until the household was compelled to establish an organized institution capable of managing its followers.[9]
Although the religion subsequently developed an administrative hierarchy, temples, and a High Priestess, Bruna remained important as its earliest genuine devotee. Victor himself recognized that the movement had begun with her and the maids, even though he had not personally established its original structure.[9]
Her later status as a Dragon Goddess did not cause her to seek prominent worship for herself. She remained among the women whose Divine status did not translate into the maintenance of a public cult, consistent with the Faith she had directed toward Victor since their first encounter.[15]

#### Unnamed Daughter
Bruna and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] possess an unnamed biological daughter belonging to the younger generation of Clan Elderblood. Her existence is confirmed during the description of the individual residences assigned to Victor's daughters in Chapter 1051.[2]
The daughters occupied an extensive residential area containing personalized rooms whose dimensions and defensive characteristics could be altered through Draconic Runes. The arrangement allowed each child to maintain a personal environment suited to her preferences while remaining protected by the supernatural systems maintained by Victor and the family.[2]
Bruna's daughter was specifically associated with a preference for smaller, simpler, functional, and elegant accommodations rather than the enormous luxurious rooms preferred by some of her sisters. This detail provides an individual characteristic without requiring an invented name, Divine Concept, or supernatural power.[2]
For Bruna, motherhood represents a significant personal development because of her childhood as an orphan and the loss of Leticia, whose greatest ambition had been to establish a biological family. Bruna eventually obtained the family and belonging she had once lacked, becoming both the wife of the man who transformed her life and the mother of a daughter within his household.

### Legacy
Bruna is significant to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s history for several reasons extending beyond her personal combat abilities. Her transformation represents the first known creation of a Vampire directly through his Progenitor Blood, establishing the beginning of a new lineage whose supernatural development could differ from ordinary Vampires descended from [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]].[4]
Her individual Telekinesis also provides an important example of the relationship between a person's Human history and the supernatural power awakened through transformation. The ability originated from Bruna's regret over failing to save Leticia, but eventually developed into a power capable of affecting Gods and moving entire populations during extraordinary supernatural operations.[5][6][7]
Bruna's religious devotion had an even broader influence. Her conviction that Victor deserved worship became the earliest foundation of the [[Blood God Religion]], which eventually developed into an enormous organization with political, military, and supernatural significance across multiple worlds. Although she did not personally construct the institution's administrative system, the movement originated through the Faith she expressed long before Victor formally occupied the Divine position associated with his later Empire.[9]
Her development also illustrates the evolution of the [[Maid Squad]] from a group of trusted servants into a permanent part of Victor's family. Bruna began as an ordinary Human who lacked combat experience and had no surviving household to return to, but eventually received permanent Clan membership, developed into a Dragon Goddess, and became one of Victor's wives and the mother of his daughter.[10][1][2]
Despite the extraordinary scale of these developments, Bruna's emotional foundation remains closely connected to the experiences that originally brought her into the supernatural world. She continues valuing the affection of Victor and her fellow maids, demonstrating that the family she obtained is as important to her identity as the overwhelming Telekinesis and Draconic power she subsequently acquired.

### Abilities

#### Skills and Abilities
Bruna Elderblood is a True Blood Dragon, one of [[Victor Elderblood]]'s original Battle Maids, and the first Human personally transformed into a Vampire through his Progenitor Blood. Her supernatural abilities developed through several successive stages, beginning with an unusually powerful Noble Vampire body and the awakening of her unique ability, Telekinesis. Following her formal incorporation into Victor's Clan and subsequent evolution into a genuine True Dragon, both her physical capabilities and the quality of her supernatural powers increased substantially.[1][2][3][4]
Unlike Vampires who inherit their principal abilities from an established Noble Clan, Bruna developed Telekinesis through the circumstances of her Human life. Victor concluded that the power originated from her regret over being unable to reach and save her childhood friend, Leticia. Her subconscious desire to reach someone before it was too late manifested as the ability to influence distant objects and individuals without physically crossing the intervening space. This interpretation was part of Victor's wider observation that the personalities and experiences of Humans directly transformed through his Blood could influence the supernatural abilities they awakened.[3]
Bruna's earliest combat performances also revealed an extraordinary physical characteristic: despite possessing virtually no experience as a supernatural fighter, she was considerably stronger than the [[Werewolves]] encountered during her first mission. Her punches launched opponents across the battlefield, and her strength was sufficiently exceptional that even experienced Werewolves struggled to understand how a newly created Vampire could physically overpower them. However, her initial attacks were inefficient because she lacked the technique and experience necessary to exploit that strength fully.[2]
Under the instruction of [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]], [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], and Victor himself, Bruna developed into a trained Battle Maid capable of participating in conflicts against increasingly powerful supernatural enemies. By the invasion of [[Olympus]], her Telekinesis had become effective against Divine Beings, allowing her to twist Gods as though their bodies were rag dolls. She subsequently demonstrated exceptional large-scale control when she simultaneously lifted and transported the inhabitants and belongings of the Snow, Fulger, and Adrasteia territories during Victor's construction of a new world.[5][6][7][8]
Her evolution into a True Blood Dragon further transformed her capabilities. The new [[True Dragons]] possessed improved metamorphosis, elemental affinities, a greater relationship with Creation, and enhancements to the abilities inherited from their previous [[Races (Flamel)|Races]]. Bruna's Telekinesis was specifically identified as reaching an astonishing level, establishing that her transformation improved her personal specialty considerably beyond its earlier state. The novel does not provide a numerical maximum range, lifting capacity, or destructive output for her post-Dragonization Telekinesis.[4]
The following sections distinguish Bruna's personally demonstrated abilities from the general characteristics of her current Race, powers acquired through Bloodline development, and techniques that remain unconfirmed for her individually.

#### Telekinesis
Telekinesis is Bruna's principal supernatural ability and the most extensively demonstrated power in her personal repertoire. It allows her to exert force upon physical targets without making direct bodily contact, enabling her to move, suspend, restrain, and violently manipulate objects or living beings from a distance.[3][7][8]
Her Telekinesis is not restricted to manipulating ordinary inanimate objects. She has demonstrated sufficient force to distort the bodies of Gods and enough simultaneous control to lift the inhabitants of several Vampire territories while separately manipulating their possessions. Its effectiveness depends upon Bruna's supernatural power, control, and the scale of the operation, with exceptionally demanding applications requiring considerable Energy and concentration.[7][8]

##### Origin and Development
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s examination of his transformed maids established a connection between their original Human experiences and the powers they awakened. Bruna had regretted failing to reach Leticia before her death, and her transformation produced an ability that overcame the ordinary necessity of physically approaching something in order to influence it.[3]
- Psychokinesis: Bruna can interact with physical targets through supernatural force rather than direct physical contact.[3][7]
- Telekinesis: Her primary ability allows the remote manipulation of objects and living beings.[3][8]
- Remote Force Application: Bruna can apply physical force to a target without striking it with her body.[7]
- Non-Contact Manipulation: Her ability eliminates the need to physically touch an object in order to move or influence it.[3]
- Supernatural Ability Development: Her Telekinesis originated through her Vampire transformation and continued improving as her Bloodline and overall supernatural strength developed.[3][4]
- Dragon-Enhanced Telekinesis: Her transformation into a True Blood Dragon caused her original ability to reach an extraordinary new level.[4]

##### Object Manipulation
Bruna can use Telekinesis to move objects while remaining physically separated from them. The ability is sufficiently precise to manipulate personal belongings independently of their owners and surrounding structures, an important characteristic demonstrated during the relocation of the Vampire Count territories.[8]
- Remote Object Manipulation: Bruna can move physical objects through Telekinesis.[8]
- Telekinetic Lifting: She can lift objects without using her hands or other physical means of support.[8]
- Telekinetic Levitation: Bruna can suspend targets in the air against the ordinary effects of gravity.[8]
- Telekinetic Transportation: She can move affected targets from one location to another through sustained telekinetic control.[8]
- Telekinetic Positioning: Bruna can control the position of objects within the effective area of her ability.[8]
- Selective Object Manipulation: She distinguished personal possessions from the homes and terrain surrounding them during the relocation operation.[8]
- Multi-Object Manipulation: Bruna can influence numerous separate objects during the same supernatural operation.[8]
- Sustained Object Control: She can maintain Telekinesis long enough to transport large collections of targets over a significant distance.[8]

##### Living-Target Manipulation
Bruna has demonstrated that her Telekinesis can directly affect living supernatural beings. Her power does not require an opponent to be an ordinary Human, and it remains effective against sufficiently vulnerable Divine targets.[7]
- Living-Target Telekinesis: Bruna can directly move and manipulate living beings.[7][8]
- Body Manipulation: She can exert telekinetic force upon an opponent's physical body.[7]
- Telekinetic Restraint: Bruna can restrict a target's movement by subjecting its body to external supernatural force.[7]
- Telekinetic Immobilization: Her power can interfere with an opponent's ability to move normally when the target cannot overcome her control.[7]
- Forced Movement: Bruna can move another individual without that individual's voluntary participation.[7][8]
