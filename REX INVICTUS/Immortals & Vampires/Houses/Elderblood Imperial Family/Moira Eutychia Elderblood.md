Moira Eutychia Elderblood is a recurring character in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]], the biological daughter of Victor Elderblood and [[Tyche]] Elderblood, and an Imperial Princess of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. She is the youngest known biological daughter of Victor during the events surrounding her birth and the youngest member of the Elderblood household during her subsequent appearances. Born as a True Dragon Goddess, Moira possesses the Draconic and Divine inheritance of her father alongside the extraordinary Luck of her mother, producing a supernatural existence whose relationship with Reality is exceptional even among Victor's naturally born descendants.[1][2]
Moira's defining characteristic is a unique Divinity named I Am the Privileged One, whose underlying power is identified as Eldritch Luck. This ability originated through the combination of Tyche's authority over Luck and several supernatural characteristics inherited from Victor, particularly his Eldritch nature. Unlike conventional probability manipulation, Moira's existence possesses an abnormal relationship with Reality itself. The Universe naturally favors her, influences circumstances for her benefit and may directly alter its own operation in response to her sincere desires. Its protection is sufficiently aggressive that attacks against her can provoke the spontaneous manifestation of Eldritch entities or the elimination of the aggressor through Reality's distortion.[2]
Her exceptional characteristics extend beyond ordinary Luck. Moira can receive objects without performing a conventional creation technique, acquire knowledge through desire and experience alterations to her own supernatural development that would normally violate the established principles governing Divinity. Her powers also affect other beings, as demonstrated when she wished for her exhausted father to recover and the Universe supplied enough Primal Energy to replenish approximately twenty percent of Victor's enormous Energy reserves.[2][3]
Although the name Eldritch Luck accurately describes her inherited power, Victor later distinguished it from the extraordinary favor she appears to receive from the Universe itself. He tentatively described this additional phenomenon as a passive blessing, while acknowledging that he did not know whether the classification was correct. The exact relationship between her innate Eldritch Luck and the Universe's apparent affection remains unresolved. Their effects overlap in practice, but the two should not automatically be treated as identical supernatural mechanisms.[4]
Moira is also remarkable for possessing a degree of governing Authority naturally from birth. According to the System, no Being born after [[Primordial Chaos]] had naturally possessed comparable Authority, not even the established Primordial Beings, whose governing powers had originally been granted to them. The System considered Moira an ideal candidate to occupy the vacant Primordial function associated with Destiny, believing that her unusual relationship with Reality could restore a fundamental mechanism that had disappeared from the Universe. Victor categorically rejected the proposal, refusing to sacrifice his daughter's independence, emotions and personality for the stability of Creation.[5][6]
Despite her extraordinary potential, Moira is not an established Primordial Being and does not possess unrestricted command over the Universe. Her governing influence is incomplete, functions primarily through passive responses to her desires and can be substantially restricted by Victor. Her greatest present difficulty is not a lack of strength but the inability to fully control the consequences of ordinary thoughts and emotions. She remains emotionally a newborn despite inheriting the knowledge and intellectual foundation of an Ancient Dragon's descendant.[2][5]
Victor describes her condition as possessing "omnipotence, omnipresence, and omniscience on a small scale", emphasizing that these characteristics are limited, largely passive and capable of being suppressed. Moira does not constantly know everything, cannot consciously exercise complete universal Authority and has not demonstrated absolute control over every aspect of existence. Her unusual powers nevertheless allow her to accomplish feats that are ordinarily impossible even for highly developed Divine beings.[2][7]
Beyond her supernatural importance, Moira is an affectionate, cheerful and energetic little girl who adores Victor and enjoys spending time with her mothers and older sisters. She is generally kind, attentive and well behaved, but becomes exceptionally stubborn whenever her father is involved. The strong protection afforded by her family and the Universe's unusual affection for her have made her the center of considerable attention, although Victor remains determined to provide her with the discipline and self-control necessary to grow into an independent individual.[2][3]

### Physical Appearance
Moira is an exceptionally beautiful young True Dragon Goddess with the appearance of a girl approximately ten years old. She possesses extremely long golden-brown hair reaching her ankles, fair skin, a pair of Dragon horns and deep violet Dragon Eyes. Her hair reflects the appearance of her biological mother, [[Tyche]] Elderblood, while her distinctive violet eyes are inherited from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and are shared by his other biological daughters. During her introduction, Moira wore a beautiful white gown that complemented her youthful appearance.[2]
Unlike ordinary Human infants, Moira was born directly with a developed childlike body. This is not unusual for Dragon Gods, whose initial physical forms can vary considerably according to their supernatural characteristics and the powers inherited from their parents. Some naturally manifest as children, while others may possess adult bodies immediately after birth. Moira's comparatively mature physical appearance therefore does not indicate that she had already experienced a conventional childhood or possessed the emotional development of an older individual.[2]
Her physical appearance also conceals the extraordinary supernatural nature of her body. As a naturally born True Dragon Goddess, she possesses the characteristics of Victor's Draconic lineage and can instinctively manifest more pronounced Dragon features. When food materialized before her shortly after her birth, Moira opened her mouth and temporarily assumed a more Draconic facial appearance before consuming it. Her transformation demonstrated that her body could express its underlying Dragon characteristics without requiring prolonged training or conscious mastery.[2]
Moira's violet Dragon Eyes provide another indication of her inherited nature. Victor's biological daughters possess different degrees of his exceptional visual perception, allowing them to observe deeper aspects of Reality that ordinary senses cannot detect. The strength of this inheritance varies between individuals, and its applications can be developed through training. Moira possesses the same fundamental ocular inheritance, although the complete extent of her personal perception has not been individually established.[8]
Her appearance is particularly striking when contrasted with her mother. Tyche had developed into a True Dragon Goddess by the time of Moira's birth, possessing golden Dragon Eyes and her own Draconic horns. Moira instead inherited Victor's violet eyes, retaining Tyche's golden-brown coloration while visibly displaying the characteristics of the Elderblood lineage.[2]
Despite her youthful and seemingly harmless appearance, Moira possesses enough innate supernatural Energy to threaten an ordinary planet through an uncontrolled bodily reaction. Her first sneeze released a gigantic beam of purple Dragon plasma, which [[Amaterasu]] estimated could have easily destroyed a planet. The incident demonstrated the immense difference between Moira's childlike exterior and the destructive potential of her Draconic body.[2]
The full appearance of Moira's complete Dragon Form has not been individually described in sufficient detail to establish its size, scale coloration or other anatomical characteristics beyond those already displayed through her Humanoid and partial Draconic transformations.

### Personality
Moira is cheerful, affectionate, energetic, curious, kind and exceptionally attached to her father. Despite possessing abnormal supernatural abilities and inherited knowledge, she retains the emotional characteristics of a newborn child. She enjoys physical affection, becomes easily excited by new experiences and frequently seeks [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s attention, often climbing onto his shoulders or demanding that he carry her. Her innocent personality contrasts sharply with the extraordinary consequences that can arise from even her simplest desires.[2]
Her attachment to Victor became apparent immediately after her birth. Upon seeing him, she enthusiastically demanded that he pick her up. Victor responded by lifting her into the air and throwing her into the stratosphere, an action that alarmed [[Tyche]] but delighted Moira. She returned like a small meteor, landed unharmed in her father's arms and immediately demanded that he repeat the experience. Rather than feeling frightened, she considered the event an exciting game and reacted with wholehearted enthusiasm.[2]
Moira's affection is accompanied by unusual stubbornness whenever Victor is involved. When Tyche instructed her to leave her father, she refused and climbed onto his shoulder instead. The behavior frustrated Tyche, who initially believed her daughter might simply possess an exceptionally disobedient personality. However, her later observations revealed that Moira was normally obedient, gentle and attentive, even though almost every member of the family spoiled her. Her resistance was concentrated primarily upon situations involving separation from Victor or instructions that interfered with her desire to remain near him.[2][3]
Although Moira strongly favors her father, she sincerely loves the other members of the Elderblood family. When Victor asked her to introduce herself to his wives, she greeted the assembled women affectionately, expressed happiness at meeting them and openly declared that she loved them all. She immediately added that she wanted to remain with Victor, provoking amusement and resignation among the experienced mothers, who recognized the familiar attachment shared by many of his daughters.[2]
Moira displays a particularly affectionate and imaginative manner of addressing her mothers. She called [[Rose Adrasteia Elderblood]] "Mama Muscle" in response to Rose's athletic physique and then complimented her beauty and hair. She described [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood as "Shining Mama" because of Aphrodite's natural radiance, while [[Hestia]] Elderblood became "Cozy Mom" after Moira climbed onto her shoulder and embraced her. Even while affectionately hugging Hestia, Moira immediately announced that her father was better, revealing the strength of her preference for Victor without diminishing her affection toward the rest of the family.[2]
Her curiosity and enthusiasm occasionally produce unusual requests. After observing Aphrodite's radiant appearance, Moira decided that she wanted to shine in the same manner. She asked Victor for a star and swallowed the stellar object he created for her, causing considerable alarm among her mothers. When she subsequently developed a minor Solar Divinity, she was initially pleased with her new appearance. However, after Victor suggested that permanently glowing might become uncomfortable, she immediately decided that remaining close to him was more important and no longer wanted the power. Her spontaneous change of mind caused the newly acquired Divinity to disappear.[2]
Moira's emotional innocence is also evident when confronted with situations that other members of the family consider extremely serious. When Victor prepared for a possible confrontation with the Primordial Beings after her birth, his older daughters recognized his anger and the danger surrounding the family. Moira remained confused despite possessing considerable inherited knowledge, because she lacked the practical experience necessary to understand the implications of the threat. Her intellectual foundation therefore does not eliminate the need for ordinary emotional development and education.[5]
Her concern for Victor is genuine and can overcome her usual carefree behavior. When she noticed that her father seemed unusually exhausted while teaching his daughters, Moira immediately asked whether something was wrong. Victor attempted to reassure her, but she remained worried because she sensed that he was not behaving normally. Her desire for him to recover was sufficiently sincere that Reality itself supplied him with an enormous quantity of Primal Energy. Moira became delighted when she perceived his condition improving, demonstrating that her powers can respond to genuine affection rather than merely childish demands.[3]
Moira also possesses an extraordinary determination to remain near Victor. When he withdrew alone to a distant waterfall to recover his composure, she found him almost immediately and proudly declared that she would always be able to locate him. Her sisters were astonished because even Yuno, one of Victor's most devoted daughters, ordinarily could not locate him when he genuinely wished to remain hidden. Moira treated the accomplishment as entirely natural and immediately claimed her familiar position upon his shoulder.[9]
Despite the constant attention she receives, Moira has not developed the intentionally cruel or dismissive behavior that might be expected from someone whose wishes receive such extraordinary supernatural support. Tyche specifically regards her as obedient, kind and attentive under ordinary circumstances. Her most difficult traits arise from emotional immaturity, attachment to Victor and the fact that the Universe may respond disproportionately to desires she does not yet understand.[3]
Moira's curiosity extends to ordinary animals and small experiences. During Victor's lessons, she became absorbed in playing with a cat that inexplicably transformed into an unusually calm snow leopard. She remained largely unconcerned with the astonishment of her sisters, becoming confused when everyone began staring at her. Her innocent reaction highlights how unusual supernatural events can occur around her without her necessarily understanding or consciously causing them.[4]
Victor recognizes that her greatest developmental challenge is self-control. Her emotional responses, sincere desires and ordinary bodily reactions possess the potential to alter Reality or release destructive Energy. He consequently intends to supervise her early education personally, emphasizing discipline, emotional regulation and the careful control of thoughts and abilities before entrusting later training to [[Scathach]]. Moira's affection and openness make her receptive to Victor's attention, but the unpredictability of her passive powers demands considerably greater caution than ordinary childhood behavior.[2][4]

### Synopsis

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Moira's existence originated through the developing relationship between [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Tyche]] Elderblood, the Greek Goddess of Luck. Tyche had previously remained relatively distant from Victor despite belonging to the same expanding supernatural civilization. Her authority over Luck had grown enormously during the Elderblood era, eventually making her a potentially valuable ally whose supernatural fortune could influence circumstances on a scale beyond conventional probability manipulation.
Violet recognized the strategic and personal significance of Tyche's growing power and encouraged her to become closer to Victor. Although Tyche respected him as Emperor, she initially knew relatively little about him as an individual. Victor consequently chose to spend time with her without forcing their relationship to progress according to an artificial schedule. Their developing intimacy attracted considerable attention from the women of the Elderblood household, who were already familiar with the extraordinary characteristics inherited by Victor's biological children.
Approximately two years passed as Victor and Tyche's relationship developed. His wives eventually began anticipating the possibility of another daughter, considering pregnancy almost inevitable because of Tyche's extraordinary Luck, [[Aphrodite]]'s blessing and Victor's supernatural lineage. Agnes predicted that their child would inherit Tyche's hair and Victor's eyes, while Natasha hoped that the birth would produce another unusual daughter comparable to the more exceptional members of the family.[1]
The discussions also revealed the broader nature of Victor's descendants. His biological daughters were described as possessing half-Eldritch and partial Primordial characteristics, although only some had developed Divinities that directly expressed those inheritances. Their status as naturally born [[True Dragons]] further distinguished them from lesser Draconic races, whose members possessed only portions of the extraordinary compatibility and supernatural foundations characteristic of genuine True Dragons.[1]
Tyche's relationship with Victor continued developing until she became pregnant, and the family began preparing for the arrival of another Dragon Goddess. Her own supernatural development progressed substantially throughout the period. By the time Moira was born, Tyche had become a True Dragon Goddess with golden Dragon Eyes and horns, while her authority over Luck had advanced until she was only one step away from becoming one with her Concept, a stage compared with Kali's extraordinary progress toward complete union with Destruction.[2]
Moira's gestation was abnormal even by the standards of Victor's daughters. Most of the family's naturally born True Dragon Goddesses had required more than one thousand years to develop before birth, whereas Moira emerged after only three hundred years. This made her the fastest-born True Dragon Goddess within the family at that time. Victor believed that the shortened gestation was not merely a biological anomaly; the Universe's extraordinary affection for Moira had seemingly accelerated her development so that she could enter the world sooner.[2]
When Moira finally appeared, she already possessed the appearance of a ten-year-old girl. Her golden-brown hair reached her ankles, two Dragon horns adorned her head and her violet eyes reflected the appearance of Victor's other biological daughters. She wore a beautiful white gown and initially appeared no more threatening than an ordinary child. However, Violet, Ruby and Sasha immediately recognized the extraordinary nature of her existence and reacted with resignation, understanding that caring for this particular newborn would present unprecedented difficulties.[2]
Moira immediately demanded Victor's attention, asking him to pick her up. Victor affectionately complied before throwing her into the stratosphere, allowing her to experience the extraordinary capabilities of her own body. Moira fell back toward the ground like a meteor and landed safely in his arms, laughing enthusiastically and demanding another turn. Tyche was horrified by the danger she believed Victor had exposed their daughter to, but he explained that Moira's safety was supported by a supernatural mechanism far beyond ordinary durability.[2]
Moira had been born possessing the Divinity I Am the Privileged One, produced through the fusion of Tyche's Luck and Victor's inherited supernatural characteristics, particularly his Eldritch nature. Her true ability was identified as Eldritch Luck, an aggressive and chaotic manifestation of fortune that fundamentally altered the relationship between the individual and the surrounding Universe. Rather than simply ensuring favorable probabilities, Reality itself acted to protect Moira and satisfy her desires.
The distinction became apparent when Victor compared her power with Tyche's mature Luck. Tyche could passively cause attacks to miss and, when ordinary probability was insufficient, cause Reality to alter circumstances so that she remained unharmed. Moira's inherited ability went further, allowing the environment to act directly against an aggressor. Hostile beings could be eliminated through Reality's distortion or devoured by spontaneously manifested Eldritch creatures before successfully harming her. The surrounding world could effectively become an extension of her passive protection.[2]
Victor likened the phenomenon to a vast machine representing the Universe. Under ordinary circumstances, beings were subject to the mechanisms governing that machine, regardless of how powerful they became. Moira instead possessed something resembling administrative privileges, allowing the machinery of Reality to alter its own operation so that circumstances naturally favored her. The comparison demonstrated why her Divinity could not be adequately described through the conventional rules governing ordinary Luck or Divine Concepts.
Moira's earliest interactions demonstrated how unpredictable this protection could become. When Tyche instructed her to leave Victor, Moira simply refused and climbed onto his shoulder. Her stubbornness immediately frustrated her mother, while Pepper jokingly compared the situation to an excessively powerful character requiring a reduction in strength. Anna questioned whether any force could reliably restrain such an abnormal ability, recognizing the difficulty of raising a child whose desires were continually supported by Reality itself.[2]
Victor reassured the family that he could substantially restrict Moira's passive influence. Although her authority was exceptionally unusual, his superior power and control over his Personal Reality allowed him to suppress most of its effects. However, he soon discovered that she could partially disregard even Rules personally imposed by him, requiring additional restrictions to prevent her powers from affecting the wider Empire.
Moira subsequently announced that she was hungry, causing food to materialize directly before her. The manifestation occurred without any observable Energy expenditure, spell or Rune. Anna and the other women were astonished because the phenomenon differed from the creation techniques available through Draconic Runes. Rather than manipulating Energy to produce an object, the Universe had simply supplied what Moira wanted.[2]
The event also revealed the inherited knowledge possessed by Victor's True Dragon descendants. As an Ancient Dragon with an extraordinary legacy, Victor passed general knowledge of the world and dormant information concerning techniques and abilities to his biological daughters. Moira consequently possessed an intellectual foundation vastly greater than that of an ordinary newborn, although the inherited techniques ordinarily still required deliberate effort to unlock and develop.
Her lack of physical control became apparent when Victor's hair brushed against her nose and caused her to sneeze. A gigantic beam of purple Dragon plasma erupted into the sky, prompting [[Amaterasu]] to estimate that the discharge could easily have destroyed a planet. The incident demonstrated that Moira's [[Draconic Energy]] could become dangerous through involuntary reactions even when she was not deliberately employing an offensive technique.[2]
Victor subsequently instructed Moira to introduce herself to the assembled mothers. She greeted them affectionately and declared that she loved them, only to immediately return to her father's shoulders. Ruby reassured the bewildered Tyche that she would eventually become accustomed to such behavior, recognizing the intense attachment that had developed in many of Victor's biological daughters.
Moira proceeded to interact with several mothers individually. She called Rose Adrasteia Mama Muscle and complimented her appearance, described Aphrodite as Shining Mama and affectionately embraced [[Hestia]] after calling her Cozy Mom. Her warmth delighted the women, although her continued preference for Victor reinforced the concern that the youngest daughter would become exceptionally difficult to separate from her father.[2]
The family soon began discussing the dangers associated with her existence. Moira was not the only daughter whose unusual powers might attract the attention of the Primordial Beings, but her abnormal relationship with Reality presented a particularly serious concern. Victor refused to allow her to travel through the wider Empire until she had learned to control her emotions and supernatural abilities, recognizing that ordinary childish impulses could produce catastrophic consequences when supported by the Universe itself.
Her ability to obtain information through desire further complicated her education. Victor believed that Moira could simply wish to know something and receive the corresponding knowledge, possibly through a direct relationship with the [[Akashic Records]]. Because she already possessed inherited Draconic knowledge, conventional lessons alone would not address her greatest weaknesses. Victor concluded that the priority must be teaching her how to regulate her thoughts, emotions and powers so that the Universe did not constantly respond to impulsive desires.[2]
He described her condition as possessing limited forms of omnipotence, omnipresence and omniscience, while emphasizing that their present effects were relatively small and could be suppressed. The abilities remained partly active even under restriction because they formed an intrinsic characteristic of her existence. Victor therefore decided to personally supervise her earliest training before eventually entrusting her further development to [[Scathach]].
Moira soon provided another demonstration of her ability to disregard conventional supernatural development. Having become fascinated with Aphrodite's radiant appearance, she asked Victor for a star. He created a perfect replica of a yellow star from the Milky Way and allowed her to swallow it, provoking alarm from Ruby and Amaterasu. Although sufficiently developed Dragons could consume stars or even black holes under suitable conditions, a newborn normally lacked the maturity and compatibility required to safely absorb such objects.[2]
Moira's abnormal relationship with Reality overcame the difficulty. Approximately two minutes after swallowing the star, she began glowing and developed a genuine minor Divinity associated with the Sun. Amaterasu recognized the extraordinary achievement, while the other women struggled to understand how an infant had acquired an entirely new conceptual characteristic without following the normal process of Divine development.
When Victor suggested that remaining permanently radiant might become inconvenient, Moira immediately decided that she no longer wanted the power. The newly acquired Solar Divinity disappeared as though it had never existed, astonishing Aline and the other women familiar with the established principles governing Divine Concepts. Victor explained that the development and disappearance of the ability had not followed the conventional process of acquiring or abandoning Divinity. Reality itself had simply altered the relevant conditions to accommodate Moira's sincere desires.[2]
The extraordinary implications of Moira's existence soon extended beyond the Elderblood household. Despite remaining concealed within Victor's Eldritch Soul, her birth released a powerful impulse throughout the entire Universe. Her relationship with Reality was sufficiently fundamental that the surrounding cosmological structure reacted to her existence, attracting the attention of the System.[5]
Victor immediately detected the unfamiliar attention directed toward his daughter and became furious. After affectionately asking Moira to play with her older sisters, he entrusted her to Valentina and the other girls before departing to confront the source of the disturbance. His wives sensed his anger through their shared Soul connection, while the daughters recognized that something serious had occurred. Moira remained confused because she lacked the experience necessary to understand the threat.
Victor ordered the Elderblood leadership to prepare for the possibility of a Universal-scale extinction event. Violet immediately organized the family's defensive preparations, assigning roles to the senior wives and coordinating their available supernatural resources. Tyche wished to accompany the others but was ordered to remain behind because she had only recently become a Dragon Goddess and lacked sufficient experience in combat at that level.[5]
Victor confronted the System in the space between galaxies, assuming his enormous Dragon Form and demanding an explanation for its interest in his daughter. The System manifested as a genderless being of pure Energy and admitted that Moira's birth constituted an unprecedented anomaly. According to its explanation, no Being within the Universe had naturally been born possessing comparable governing Authority, not even the established Primordial Beings, whose corresponding powers had originally been granted to them by their Creator.
The System also admitted that it had attempted to observe Moira but had failed. Although it possessed extraordinary administrative capabilities, it could not penetrate Victor's Eldritch Soul, where his daughters resided. This prevented the System from directly examining Moira despite the enormous influence her birth had exerted upon the Universe.[5]
Victor recognized that the System was withholding information and demanded the truth. The System eventually revealed that it considered Moira essential to restoring the Universe's missing Primordial function of Destiny. Since the disappearance of the previous Progenitor associated with Destiny, the System believed that the Cosmos lacked a stable mechanism governing its future. Although other forces continued maintaining Reality, the System feared that the absence of Destiny would eventually compromise the stability of Creation.
The System therefore proposed that Moira become the new archetypal Primordial force responsible for Destiny. Her extraordinary relationship with Reality made her an ideal candidate in its assessment, since she had naturally acquired an influence over the Universe that other Beings could only obtain through the granting of formal Authority.[5]
The proposal was particularly significant because Moira had not inherited the Time-related component the System associated with the complete office of Destiny. Nevertheless, the System believed that her natural compatibility and extraordinary power would allow her to acquire the missing characteristic with sufficient assistance. It also stated that, in the specific hierarchy of Moira's governing relationship with Reality, only [[Primordial Chaos]] stood above her.
Moira's authority remained incomplete despite these exceptional characteristics. She could not consciously issue unrestricted commands to the Universe because she had not become an established Primordial force and possessed no corresponding cosmic office. Instead, her desires passively influenced how Reality behaved around her. The System considered this incomplete power extraordinary enough to exceed the natural authority of every Being born after Primordial Chaos.[5]
Victor rejected the proposal immediately. He had observed that several Primordial Beings behaved less like completely independent individuals and more like cosmic mechanisms whose personalities and decisions were constrained by the purposes for which they had been created. He refused to allow Moira to risk losing her emotional independence or becoming an instrument dedicated primarily to maintaining the Universe's functions.
When the System questioned whether Victor would endanger countless lives and the stability of Creation for a single daughter, he answered without hesitation that he would. From his perspective, the safety and freedom of his family remained more important than the demands of external powers, regardless of their cosmic significance.[5]
Victor nevertheless did not accept the implication that protecting Moira necessarily required sacrificing the inhabitants of his Empire. His Personal Reality and enormous Eldritch Soul possessed the capacity to shelter tremendous populations, and he believed he could provide refuge for his citizens if the greater Universe became hostile. He was prepared to expand his Personal Universe and create additional worlds or dimensions rather than submit to an arrangement that would take his daughter away.
The confrontation escalated when the System recognized that Victor would not negotiate over Moira's freedom. His authority over Negativity and his Eldritch existence made him capable of threatening the fundamental structure of Reality, even if the consequences would also injure him. The System eventually withdrew, declaring that it would not pursue Moira but would inform the established Primordial Beings of the disturbance.[6]
The System deliberately omitted terminology associated with Destiny when informing the Primordial authorities, revealing that its earlier explanation had not been entirely transparent. Five Primordial Beings subsequently appeared: [[The Universal Tree]], the Judge of the Abyss, the Being associated with Limbo, Death and Infinity.
Victor repeated his refusal and warned that any attempt to seize his daughter would provoke catastrophic retaliation. He demonstrated his willingness to destabilize Negativity itself, threatening to corrupt or damage a fundamental component of the Universe through the interaction between his conceptual Authority and [[Eldritch Energy]]. The Primordial Beings recognized that the consequences of such an action would extend throughout Creation, since Negativity existed alongside Positivity as one of the fundamental components underlying Reality.[6]
Although Victor understood that destroying or corrupting such an important part of his own existence would severely weaken him, he considered the sacrifice acceptable if necessary to protect Moira. The conflict therefore threatened not merely an ordinary battle between powerful individuals but the potential destabilization of the cosmic mechanisms that sustained the Universe.
The Universal Tree eventually intervened and declared that nobody would approach the Dragon Emperor's daughter. He rejected the idea that the Primordial authorities should interfere directly with Victor's family merely because Moira possessed unusual potential. The Being associated with Limbo also attempted to de-escalate the confrontation, while the other Primordial Beings began questioning the System's motivations.[6]
Their suspicions increased when they realized that the System had apparently been seeking another Primordial Being without properly informing the authorities responsible for maintaining the Universe. The Universal Tree considered the System's behavior sufficiently abnormal to warrant investigation and promised to examine the circumstances surrounding its attempt to claim Moira.
With the immediate threat resolved, Victor returned to the Elderblood family. The incident nevertheless left his wives deeply angered. Several recognized that the Primordial Beings possessed powers beyond their present level and that their family could not rely exclusively upon Victor's willingness to sacrifice himself whenever an external authority targeted one of their children.[9]
Violet and Sasha consequently began considering the development of a weapon capable of threatening the entire Universe. They intended to involve the Empire's greatest researchers and Azathoth, potentially incorporating Eldritch Energy and other exceptionally dangerous supernatural principles. Their objective was to ensure that the Elderblood family possessed an independent deterrent against Primordial-level threats rather than relying upon Victor to damage his own Soul and Divinity in the event of another confrontation.
Victor withdrew to a distant waterfall on the family's personal planet to regain his composure and consider the implications of the encounter. His solitude lasted less than two seconds before Moira arrived alongside [[Ariella Victoria Elderblood]] and [[Helvarya Elderblood]]. Victor immediately understood that Moira had located him despite his attempt to remain alone.[9]
Moira proudly announced that she would always find her father and immediately climbed onto his shoulder. Ariella and Helvarya were astonished because even Yuno normally could not locate Victor when he genuinely wished to remain hidden. Her success revealed another unusual application of her Reality-favoring existence, although the specific supernatural mechanism responsible for locating Victor remained unidentified.
Victor abandoned his attempt to remain alone and embraced the three daughters together. The encounter reinforced his determination to protect Moira and the rest of his family, particularly after witnessing how casually the System had attempted to assign his newborn daughter a cosmic responsibility without considering her personal freedom.[9]
The incident also affected Victor's discussions with [[Azathoth Elderblood]]. Azathoth had revealed that her imprisoned true body had been transferred into a dimension outside the native Universe, where the normal Rules established by the Creator did not apply. Her daughter Yog possessed a sufficiently deep connection to her to provide a means of locating the hidden prison. Victor recognized that Moira's abnormal relationship with Reality might help overcome the remaining obstacles and identified Yog and Moira together as potential keys to freeing Azathoth.[6]
The family nevertheless remained cautious about involving children in responsibilities belonging to adults. Violet later explained that Yog had been created with a specific purpose, while Moira's intended role would merely involve guiding the way. Victor continued searching for alternative methods that would allow him to free Azathoth without relying upon either daughter, reflecting his belief that children should not be required to solve problems their parents were responsible for addressing.[3]
Moira continued enjoying the attention of her family while the Empire underwent further development. Tyche gradually recognized that her daughter's stubbornness was concentrated almost entirely upon matters involving Victor. Under ordinary circumstances, Moira remained kind, obedient and attentive despite being spoiled by nearly everyone in the household. Her behavior therefore differed significantly from the uncontrolled defiance Tyche had initially feared.[3]
During a period of particularly demanding Imperial activity, Victor began conducting lessons for his daughters while his physical body rested. He was sustaining an enormous Sector-merging ritual whose Energy requirements had become so immense that even his extraordinary reserves were insufficient to eliminate the resulting exhaustion. His Soul could continue functioning and teaching while his physical body remained in an Energy-saving state.
Moira attended one of these lessons alongside her older sisters. When she noticed that Victor seemed unusually tired, she immediately questioned whether he was feeling well. Her concern attracted the attention of the other daughters, who used their inherited Dragon Eyes to examine his condition and discovered the extraordinary degree of exhaustion he was experiencing.[3]
Victor attempted to reassure Moira that he was merely tired from working too much, but his explanation failed to eliminate her concern. She sincerely wished for her father to become well again, prompting an immediate supernatural response. Primal Energy began entering Victor's body, rapidly replenishing his reserves until approximately twenty percent of his total Energy had been restored.
The effect occurred despite Victor's automatic defenses. He recognized that his protections had not prevented the phenomenon because the Energy was not being forced into him through an ordinary technique. The Universe itself had simply supplied what Moira desired.[3]
The restoration was substantial enough that Victor could have awakened his physical body immediately. However, the ongoing Energy expenditure required to sustain the Sector-merging ritual continued draining his reserves, making it more efficient to remain in his resting state. Moira nevertheless became delighted when she sensed that he had recovered significantly, while Anna and Tyche were astonished by the scale of the assistance her simple wish had produced.
Moira subsequently joined the other daughters for Victor's lesson. Because she was the smallest member of the gathering, she sat at the front so she could see properly. Victor invited the girls to ask questions concerning supernatural principles, allowing them to discuss the nature of Energy, Divinity, the Akashic Records and the structure of Reality.[3]
The lesson revealed an important distinction between Moira's inherited intelligence and her practical knowledge. Although Victor believed that she could obtain information through sincere desire, she did not automatically possess complete knowledge of every supernatural subject. When he questioned his daughters about the Akashic Records, the older girls understood the subject, while Moira did not because she had only recently been born. Victor consequently began explaining the principle from the beginning so that his youngest daughter could follow the discussion.[7]
This limitation demonstrated that her information-related powers were not equivalent to continuous unrestricted omniscience. Moira could acquire extraordinary knowledge under suitable circumstances, but she still possessed gaps in her understanding and required education concerning subjects she had not yet learned or consciously sought to understand.
During a subsequent lesson, [[Nigredo Elderblood]] asked Victor to explain the unusual nature of Moira's abilities. The youngest daughter was occupied with a cat that had inexplicably transformed into a docile snow leopard, apparently unconcerned with the astonishment of her sisters. The animal's fur tickled her nose and caused her to sneeze, producing another dangerous accumulation of supernatural Energy. Victor immediately suppressed the discharge before it could erupt.[4]
Victor explained that Moira's innate power was Eldritch Luck, distinguishing it from ordinary Luck through the Eldritch characteristics inherited from his own existence. He then clarified that the extraordinary treatment she received from the Universe appeared to be something additional, tentatively describing it as a passive blessing. The distinction was important because the phenomenon did not require Moira to consciously activate a technique, perform a spell or manipulate an established supernatural system.
According to Victor, the Universe genuinely favored Moira, and this favorable influence responded to her sincere desires. He considered the phenomenon particularly powerful during her newborn stage but acknowledged that he had not yet established its complete limits or even determined whether blessing was the correct classification.[4]
Victor remained uncertain whether the effect could injure beings stronger than Moira, fulfill desires that were objectively impossible through normal means or respond violently if she became seriously upset. He consequently warned his daughters against playing jokes that might provoke their youngest sister, recognizing that ordinary childhood mischief could have consequences far beyond those expected from conventional supernatural abilities.
The explanation astonished the assembled daughters. Rosemary compared Moira to a Mary Sue, while Priscillia, Sylvia and Lucrecia discussed her resemblance to exceptionally fortunate protagonists from fictional cultivation stories. They observed that Moira had been born into one of the most powerful families in existence, inherited extraordinary abilities and enjoyed the affection of the entire Universe itself.[4]
Despite their astonishment, the girls ultimately regarded Moira's extraordinary fortune as another example of the unusual characteristics shared by Victor's descendants. Their youngest sister remained an affectionate newborn surrounded by a family determined to protect and educate her, even while her existence continued raising questions concerning the fundamental workings of Reality.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Moira's biological father and the individual toward whom she displays the strongest attachment. Despite spoiling Moira, Victor recognizes that her abilities make discipline essential. Moira's affection for Victor also produces extraordinary supernatural effects. - [[Rose Adrasteia Elderblood]]: Moira calls Rose "Mama Muscle" in response to her athletic appearance. She also compliments Rose's beauty and hair, receiving an affectionate response from her mother.[2]
- [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood: Moira calls Aphrodite "Shining Mama" because of her natural radiance. Her desire to shine in the same manner later causes her to temporarily acquire a minor Solar Divinity after swallowing a star.[2]
- [[Hestia]] Elderblood: Moira calls Hestia "Cozy Mom" and affectionately embraces her while sitting upon her shoulder. She nevertheless immediately insists that Victor is better, demonstrating her strong attachment to her father.[2]
- [[Valentina Victoria Elderblood]]: Victor specifically entrusts Moira to Valentina and her other older sisters before confronting the System, asking them to take care of the newborn while he handles the danger threatening their family.[5]
- [[Ariella Victoria Elderblood]] and [[Helvarya Elderblood]]: These sisters accompany Moira when she locates Victor at the waterfall. The three subsequently share an affectionate family embrace with him.[9]
- [[Yuno Elderblood]]: Moira's ability to locate Victor astonishes her sisters because even Yuno, one of the daughters most obsessively devoted to him, ordinarily cannot find him when he genuinely wishes to remain hidden.[9]
- [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]]: Victor intends to supervise Moira's earliest instruction before entrusting additional self-control and combat training to [[Scathach]]. Her precise future curriculum has not been established.[2]
- [[Nigredo Elderblood]]: Nigredo becomes curious about Moira's unusual power and asks Victor to explain it during a family lesson, prompting him to distinguish Eldritch Luck from the Universe's potentially separate passive favor.[4]
- [[Rosemary Lavina Elderblood]]: Rosemary compares Moira's extraordinary fortune to the characteristics of a Mary Sue, recognizing the extent to which Reality itself appears to support her youngest sister.[4]
- [[Priscillia Penny Elderblood]], [[Sylvia Elderblood]] and [[Lucrecia Lena Elderblood]]: These sisters compare Moira's unusual advantages with the extremely fortunate protagonists of fictional cultivation stories, observing that she enjoys extraordinary parents, inherited power and the affection of the entire Universe.[4]
- [[Yog-Sothoth]] Elderblood: Yog possesses a deep connection to Azathoth's imprisoned true body, while Moira's Reality-favoring existence may provide assistance in overcoming the abnormal conditions surrounding that imprisonment. Victor identifies the two daughters as potentially important to freeing Azathoth, although he continues seeking alternatives that would avoid involving his children.[6][3]
The attempt to claim Moira as the Primordial force of Destiny also strengthened the family's determination to prepare for future cosmic threats. Violet and Sasha recognized that Victor's willingness to sacrifice himself could not remain the household's only means of resisting enemies possessing Universal-scale Authority. They therefore began considering the development of weapons capable of threatening the fundamental structure of Reality, ensuring that future attempts to target Moira or another member of the family would encounter a more comprehensive defensive response.[9]
