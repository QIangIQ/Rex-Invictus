### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 1104: A New Talented Daughter
Her mind does not remember, but her soul does. She has already walked this path once in the past.
—[[Victor Elderblood]] about Melida Charlotte Elderblood, Chapter 1148
Melida Charlotte Elderblood, commonly called Melida or Charlotte, is a Blood Dragon, an Imperial Princess of the [[Elderblood Empire]], and one of Victor Elderblood's daughters. She was created through an extraordinary process of reincarnation involving [[The Universal Tree]], [[Jeanne Elderblood]] and Victor, using a profoundly reconstructed Soul that originally belonged to [[Merlin]]. Following her creation, Melida became one of the most naturally gifted members of the Elderblood family in the fields of Energy Manipulation, Soul Manipulation and the perception of Reality's underlying structure. By the later Imperial era, after reaching adulthood, she was also counted among Victor's wives.[2][3][1]
Although Melida originated from Merlin's former Soul, she is not merely Merlin resurrected in a different body. At Jeanne's request, the Universal Tree employed his authority over Life and Reincarnation to reconstruct the Soul's Core of Existence, altering its fundamental identity rather than simply erasing memories or providing a new physical vessel. The modification was sufficiently profound that Victor, despite possessing an extraordinary understanding of Souls and existence, could comprehend only approximately seventy percent of the process. The resulting Soul retained a connection to its former foundation but was fundamentally different from Merlin, allowing Melida to begin life as a distinct individual.[2]
The Universal Tree also created Melida outside the conventional Soul System. Although ordinary Souls were regulated through the arrangements associated with the Judges of the Abyss and the supernatural mechanisms governing death and reincarnation, Melida's reconstructed existence did not follow the standard registration process. Jeanne explained that her brother had not informed Primordial Soul of his intervention, and that if Melida died, her Soul would return directly to the Universal Tree rather than automatically entering the ordinary reincarnation system. This arrangement demonstrated that the system governing Souls was not an indispensable prerequisite for their existence, since a sufficiently powerful Being with the appropriate authority could sustain an alternative process.[2]
After examining the reconstructed Soul, Victor accepted the new girl as his daughter and gave her the name Melida Charlotte Elderblood. Because words spoken with sufficient [[Will Solace|Will]] possess genuine supernatural power, Victor's naming caused another transformation. His influence enveloped Charlotte and reconstructed her existence through his Bloodline, granting her the characteristics of a genuine Blood Dragon. Her hair became black, her horns acquired dark-violet coloration, Draconic wings emerged from her back and her eyes developed an extraordinary kaleidoscopic appearance reflecting the numerous Energies circulating through her body.[3]
Melida's defining talent is her nearly instinctive understanding of Energy. Unlike conventional supernatural beings who generally learn to absorb and manipulate already differentiated Energies, Melida naturally approaches Energy through its underlying, unassigned state. She can work with Attributeless Energy and impose different characteristics upon it, allowing the same foundational Energy to acquire properties associated with distinct supernatural systems. Shortly after her rebirth, she demonstrated compatibility with Demonic Miasma, Angelic Light, [[Divine Energy]], Positive Energy, Negative Energy and [[Magic]]. Even a portion of the [[Primordial Energy]] controlled by Jeanne was drawn into Melida's body when she took her first breath outside the incubation chamber.[3]
Her ability directly inspired one of Victor's most important breakthroughs. Watching Melida manipulate Energy revealed that the many supernatural Energies he had previously treated as separate systems could be understood through a more fundamental principle: Energy without assigned characteristics could be given different attributes according to the practitioner's understanding and Will. Victor rapidly developed this insight through his accumulated experience, achieving a substantial advancement in [[Creator's Energy Mastery]]. Although Victor possessed vastly greater knowledge and power, his newborn daughter supplied the missing perspective that allowed him to understand an essential principle underlying the Energies of Creation.[3]
Melida subsequently developed far beyond her extraordinary beginnings. During the Empire's accelerated development, she acquired advanced control over Souls and began channeling Energies associated directly with Victor. By Chapter 1165, her abilities were described as sophisticated enough to manipulate Victor's own Soul, an extraordinary accomplishment given the fundamental nature of his existence. She also possesses exceptionally powerful inherited perception, with later chapters identifying her among the daughters capable of perceiving Reality's underlying structure at a particularly advanced level.[4][5][6]
Her continued development demonstrates the unusual relationship between her reconstructed Soul and former existence. Melida does not consciously remember Merlin's life, but Victor explains that her Soul retains a deeper form of experiential familiarity. Consequently, certain disciplines she studies are not entirely unfamiliar to the foundation of her existence, even though her conscious mind must still learn and develop them. This distinction preserves Melida's individuality while explaining why her progress in Energy and Soul-related disciplines became extraordinarily rapid.[4]

### Physical Appearance

#### Before Victor's Naming
Melida's first appearance occurred while she was developing inside a large incubation chamber containing nourishing green liquid. Her newly reconstructed body possessed long brown hair and horns of a similar coloration, distinguishing her initial appearance from the predominantly dark features she would later acquire through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Bloodline. Although the Soul used in her creation had once belonged to [[Merlin]], her body already belonged to a newly formed individual whose fundamental existence had been altered by the Universal Tree.[2]
The incubation chamber served as the environment in which her reconstructed Soul could stabilize and develop a compatible physical vessel. Her appearance was therefore not simply Merlin's former body restored through ordinary resurrection. [[The Universal Tree]] had altered the Soul's Core of Existence, and the new body was constructed around that altered foundation rather than preserving the exact physical identity of its previous owner.[2]

#### Blood Dragon Rebirth
Melida after entering [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood Dragon lineage.
Victor's naming produced an immediate and dramatic transformation. After he declared her name and directed his [[Will Solace|Will]] toward her existence, a violet supernatural influence enveloped Charlotte and initiated another rebirth. Her body developed into a humanoid form approximately 180 centimeters tall, her previously brown hair became black like Victor's, and her horns changed into black Draconic horns possessing dark-violet nuances. Large Dragon wings emerged from her back with sufficient force to damage the surrounding incubation chamber.[3]
Melida's eyes are one of her most distinctive features. Unlike the bright violet eyes commonly inherited by Victor's daughters, Melida possesses a constantly shifting kaleidoscope of colors, primarily violet, red and blue. These colors sometimes blend into additional combinations, visually reflecting the numerous supernatural Energies circulating within her body. Jeanne and Kaguya could perceive several distinct Energy types within Melida, and the unusual movement of these Energies appeared closely related to the changing appearance of her eyes.[3]
Shortly after emerging from the chamber, Melida began observing Victor and attempting to imitate him. She altered the Energy radiating from her eyes until their appearance approached his characteristic crimson-violet coloration, demonstrating that she could modify the supernatural properties associated with her own body. The imitation was not initially perfect, since traces of other Energies remained mixed into the effect, but the accomplishment was remarkable for a newborn who had received almost no formal instruction.[3]
Melida also inherited Victor's unusually captivating voice. When she first addressed him, Victor immediately recognized the characteristic and expressed surprise because relatively few of his daughters possessed the same quality. He specifically associated the trait with his existence as a Progenitor and regarded its inheritance as another indication of Melida's exceptional potential.[3]
Although her newly transformed humanoid body initially stood approximately 180 centimeters tall, Melida found the height inconvenient when looking up at her enormous Draconic relatives. She consequently employed her powers to enlarge herself until reaching approximately 4.75 meters. The adjustment was performed almost instinctively, demonstrating that the newly reborn Dragon possessed considerable control over her physical characteristics despite her inexperience with more refined Energy manipulation.[3]

#### Later Imperial Appearance
An alternate depiction of Melida Charlotte Elderblood.
Melida retains the distinctive characteristics acquired through her rebirth into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Bloodline, including her black hair, Draconic horns and extraordinary eyes. Her appearance can change according to the Energy characteristics she chooses to reproduce, making her physical presentation unusually flexible even among the [[True Dragons]] of Clan Elderblood.[3]
Her enormous initial growth should not be understood as a permanent increase in the size of her default humanoid body. The transformation demonstrates Melida's capacity to modify her physical proportions through supernatural means, while her ordinary humanoid appearance remains distinct from the enlarged form she adopted to interact more comfortably with her family.[3]

### Personality
Melida possesses an exceptionally curious, intelligent and observant personality, with a natural tendency to study unfamiliar phenomena and reproduce the principles behind them. Unlike individuals whose development depends primarily upon memorized techniques, Melida approaches new abilities by attempting to understand their underlying mechanisms. This disposition became apparent immediately after her rebirth, when she observed [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] controlling supernatural Energy and began imitating his actions without requiring detailed explanations. Her first instinct was not to demonstrate overwhelming power for its own sake, but to discover how the abilities used by her father functioned and whether she could reproduce them through her own understanding.[3]
Despite her extraordinary intelligence, Melida initially possessed the emotional behavior of a newborn. She was gentle, quiet and highly responsive to Victor's attention, displaying considerable affection for him even while attempting to understand her newly acquired powers. Her immense natural talent did not grant immediate emotional maturity, and she could become frustrated when her experiments failed to produce the exact results she desired. During her first lesson concerning Attributeless Energy, she attempted to create a sphere containing only one chosen Energy attribute, but other Energy characteristics repeatedly contaminated the construct. The failure irritated her because she instinctively understood what she wanted to achieve without yet possessing the practical control necessary to perform it perfectly.[3]
Victor addressed this frustration by explaining that extraordinary talent did not eliminate the need for patience, discipline and repeated training. Melida accepted his instruction and quickly calmed, responding positively when he affectionately patted her head. The interaction established an important aspect of their relationship: although Melida possessed knowledge capable of inspiring Victor himself, she remained his daughter and required the same emotional guidance and personal attention that he provided to the other members of his family.[3]
Melida's intelligence is highly specialized rather than uniformly distributed across every possible discipline. Victor compared her development to a character whose greatest abilities were concentrated in Intelligence, Wisdom and the unique quality of understanding Energy. He recognized that her talent was comparable to his own unusual affinity for understanding supernatural systems, but centered upon a particular field in which she possessed almost instinctive comprehension. This specialization made her exceptionally well suited to researching the nature of Energy, while also providing the potential to develop related disciplines involving Souls and the underlying laws of Reality.[3]
Her natural curiosity also gives her a strongly experimental disposition. Melida does not appear satisfied merely because an ability functions once; she seeks to understand its fundamental principles, reproduce the result and improve her control. By the later Imperial era, this tendency had developed into a sophisticated research mentality. Her experiments were progressing so successfully that she became frustrated by their lack of difficulty, having expected the advanced disciplines she was studying to present substantially greater obstacles.[4]
Victor attributed part of her extraordinary progress to the residual experience contained within her reconstructed Soul. Although Melida consciously possesses no ordinary memories of [[Merlin]]'s former life, her Soul retains familiarity with certain paths of development it had previously traversed. This allows her to approach advanced subjects with a form of underlying recognition, although she must still develop the conscious understanding and technical precision necessary to control them properly.[4]
Melida also possesses a competitive and occasionally possessive side, particularly when interacting with her sisters. During Victor's later lessons concerning dimensional structures and Reality, she displayed irritation when Ophis occupied her father's attention and refused to move away from him. Her reaction demonstrated that even after enormous periods of development, Melida retained the affectionate desire for Victor's attention that had appeared during her first moments of life. She was also visibly displeased when Victor prevented her from answering questions whose solutions she already understood, preferring to participate directly rather than remain silent while her sisters worked through problems she considered comparatively simple.[6]
Her behavior among the younger Elderblood generation demonstrates that immense intellectual ability does not necessarily produce a detached or unemotional personality. Melida can recognize complicated situations quickly, but she still participates in ordinary familial rivalries and reacts when her sisters receive attention she wishes to claim for herself. Alongside [[Metis]] Elderblood and [[Akasha Elderblood]], she is among the daughters whose intelligence makes them particularly difficult to deceive, often recognizing the intentions behind the schemes and arguments occurring within the family.[5][6]
The contrast between her intelligence and emotional attachments remains central to her characterization. Melida is capable of comprehending Energy at a level that challenges the understanding of powerful Gods and advanced researchers, yet she continues regarding Victor as her principal teacher and source of affection. Even after reaching adulthood and becoming one of his wives, her relationship with him retains the personal significance established during the moment he first recognized her as a daughter.[3][1]

### Origin

#### Merlin's Soul Foundation
Melida's existence originated from the Soul previously belonging to [[Merlin]], whose former identity and supernatural development had left a complicated spiritual foundation. Merlin's existence had become heavily affected by Sin and the characteristics associated with his Demonic nature, creating limitations that ordinary reincarnation would not necessarily eliminate. Simply restoring his Soul within a different body could have preserved too much of the previous existence, including the conditions that restricted its future development.[2]
[[Jeanne Elderblood]] therefore requested assistance from her brother, [[The Universal Tree]], whose authority over Life and Reincarnation allowed him to perform operations upon Souls at a level far beyond conventional reincarnation. Because Merlin's former Soul foundation already existed, the Universal Tree did not need to create an entirely new Soul through the ordinary supernatural mechanisms. Instead, he used the available foundation and reconstructed it through his own authority, altering the underlying structure sufficiently to support the emergence of a different individual.[2]
The intervention reached the Core of Existence, the fundamental structure through which a Being's identity and supernatural characteristics are established. Unlike modifications that merely alter memories, appearance or racial characteristics, changes to the Core can affect what the individual fundamentally is. Such operations are extraordinarily difficult because the Core is closely connected to the stability and continuity of the Being itself. Improper modifications can produce severe defects or destabilize the existence being altered.[2]
The Universal Tree nevertheless completed the reconstruction successfully. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later examined the resulting Soul, he recognized superficial connections to Merlin's previous foundation but could not regard the new existence as identical to its former owner. The intervention had changed the Core so extensively that the resulting individual possessed a new identity and an independent path of development. Melida therefore retained a connection to Merlin through the material from which her Soul had been reconstructed without simply continuing his previous life under a new name.[2]

#### Reconstruction of the Core of Existence
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] personally investigated the reconstructed Soul after Jeanne explained the Universal Tree's intervention. His examination revealed an operation considerably more sophisticated than an ordinary resurrection, reincarnation or modification of spiritual information. [[The Universal Tree]] had not merely eliminated memories or removed undesirable racial traits; he had reconstructed the essential structure defining the Being's existence.[2]
Victor could understand approximately seventy percent of the process. The remaining portion exceeded his knowledge of the relevant supernatural mechanisms at that point, impressing him because he already possessed considerable authority over Souls, racial transformations and the structure of existence. The accomplishment demonstrated that the Universal Tree's relationship with Life and Reincarnation allowed him to perform operations that even Victor could not yet completely reproduce or explain.[2]
The reconstruction also established an important distinction between the continuity of a Soul's foundation and the persistence of an individual's identity. Although Melida was created using the Soul formerly belonging to [[Merlin]], the alteration of its Core prevented the previous identity from simply continuing unchanged. The resulting existence could therefore be regarded as a genuine new life rather than a continuation in which Merlin had merely acquired a different appearance.[2]
The distinction became especially significant when Victor later recognized that Melida's Soul retained a form of spiritual familiarity with its former development. Her conscious mind did not possess Merlin's ordinary memories, but the underlying Soul could still benefit from paths previously traversed. Consequently, the reconstruction broke the continuity of personal identity without necessarily eliminating every trace of experience contained within the spiritual foundation.[4]

#### Existence Outside the Conventional Soul System
[[The Universal Tree]] performed Melida's reincarnation without incorporating her into the ordinary Soul System governing most inhabitants of Creation. Jeanne explained that the intervention had occurred without notifying Primordial Soul, whose jurisdiction normally involved the supernatural arrangements responsible for Soul regulation. Nevertheless, the larger System did not immediately reject Melida's existence or prevent the newly reconstructed Soul from developing.[2]
This became important to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s understanding of the relationship between Souls and the systems created to manage them. The ordinary Soul System was not the fundamental source from which every Soul necessarily originated. Rather, it functioned as a regulatory structure established to organize the processes of death, reincarnation and the circulation of Souls through Creation. Beings possessing sufficiently fundamental authority could perform operations upon Souls without relying entirely upon those conventional mechanisms.[2]
Melida's Soul was therefore placed under an unusual arrangement. Jeanne explained that if Melida died, her Soul would return directly to the Universal Tree rather than entering the ordinary reincarnation process overseen through the standard supernatural system. This established an alternative post-death destination associated with the Being responsible for reconstructing her existence.[2]
The arrangement should not be confused with absolute immunity to Soul Manipulation or the inability to suffer spiritual damage. Melida still possesses a Soul capable of interacting with supernatural phenomena; the difference lies in its reconstruction, registration and intended post-death process. The existence of an alternative destination does not, by itself, prove that no sufficiently powerful enemy could ever threaten or destroy her spiritual foundation.

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Melida entered the story while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was resolving matters connected to [[Merlin]]'s former existence. Accompanied by Kaguya, Victor visited the facility where Jeanne had arranged for a new individual to develop from a Soul reconstructed by the Universal Tree. Jeanne explained that she had requested her brother's intervention because merely reincarnating Merlin through ordinary methods would not have produced the outcome she wanted. [[The Universal Tree]] had instead used his authority over Life and Reincarnation to reconstruct the Soul's Core of Existence, creating an individual sufficiently different from Merlin that the former identity could no longer be considered an adequate description of the new Being.[2]
Victor examined the Soul personally and discovered that its reconstruction was extraordinarily sophisticated. Although he could recognize traces of the original foundation, the fundamental Core had been changed so extensively that he considered the resulting girl a different person. The reconstruction had also been performed outside the ordinary Soul System, leaving her future existence subject to arrangements established by the Universal Tree rather than the conventional process governing reincarnation. Victor recognized the accomplishment as another demonstration of the extraordinary authority possessed by the Being responsible for maintaining Life throughout Creation.[2]
After accepting the reconstructed Soul as a new individual, Victor chose to recognize her as his daughter. In Chapter 1105, he formally gave her the name Melida Charlotte Elderblood. His words carried enormous supernatural significance because they were spoken through the [[Will Solace|Will]] of a Being whose existence had already developed beyond the ordinary limits of the Gods. The declaration initiated another rebirth, with Victor's power enveloping Charlotte and reconstructing her body and spiritual foundation through his Bloodline.[3]
The transformation caused Charlotte to acquire the physical characteristics of a genuine Blood Dragon. Her body developed to approximately 180 centimeters, her hair became black, and her horns acquired the dark coloration associated with Victor's Draconic lineage. Large Dragon wings expanded from her back, breaking portions of the incubation chamber as her transformed body emerged. Her eyes opened to reveal an unusual kaleidoscope of violet, red and blue, constantly shifting in response to the numerous Energies circulating through her existence.[3]
Jeanne and Kaguya immediately recognized that the girl possessed extraordinary Energy characteristics. Within her body they could perceive Demonic Miasma, Angelic Light, [[Divine Energy]], Positive Energy, Negative Energy and [[Magic]], despite the fact that she had only just undergone the transformation. When Melida took her first breath outside the chamber, the surrounding Energy was drawn toward her and absorbed into her body. Even a portion of the [[Primordial Energy]] controlled by Jeanne was affected, astonishing the former guardian of the Universal Tree because such compatibility was exceptional even among extremely talented supernatural beings.[3]
Jeanne compared Melida's achievement with the development of her own daughter, recognizing that an ability of this nature would ordinarily require extensive training before it could be used safely. Rather than reacting with jealousy, she was delighted by the addition of another exceptionally talented individual to the Elderblood family. Melida's existence promised to expand the family's understanding of supernatural Energies and potentially contribute to developments that even its most accomplished researchers had not yet considered.[3]
Melida's attention quickly turned toward Victor. Observing the Energy surrounding him, she attempted to imitate its characteristics and altered the appearance of her eyes until they resembled his crimson-violet gaze. The imitation was not entirely accurate, but Victor recognized that she was reproducing the underlying properties of his Energy despite possessing almost no conscious training. When she first addressed him, he also discovered that she had inherited his unusually captivating voice, a rare characteristic that relatively few of his daughters possessed.[3]
The newborn continued examining Victor's abilities and began reproducing them through her own Energy. She created a feminine version of the armor he manifested around himself, altered her hair until it acquired characteristics associated with Hell's Miasma and increased her physical size to approximately 4.75 meters because she disliked looking upward at the enormous Draconic members of her family. These demonstrations were performed through instinctive experimentation rather than the application of techniques she had previously studied.[3]
Victor gradually realized that Melida's approach to Energy differed fundamentally from his own. He normally obtained an existing form of Energy and manipulated the properties it already possessed, or drew upon the numerous Energies contained within his body. Melida instead worked with an underlying Energy lacking assigned characteristics and transformed that Energy into whatever specialized form she wanted to produce. Victor identified this foundation as Attributeless Energy and began considering whether the many supernatural Energies he had encountered were different manifestations of the same deeper principle.[3]
The realization became the catalyst for a major breakthrough. Victor possessed enormous accumulated knowledge and experience, allowing him to develop the theoretical implications of Melida's instinctive methods far more rapidly than she could. By recognizing that Energy could exist without an assigned characteristic, he began understanding how different supernatural Energies could be generated through the addition of specific properties. This transformed his approach to Energy manipulation and became the conceptual foundation of [[Creator's Energy Mastery]].[3]
Victor explained the principle through the comparison between a blank canvas and the colors applied to it. Attributeless Energy represented the underlying foundation, while the characteristics imposed upon it produced specialized Energies such as Fire, Magic, Miasma, Divine Energy and other supernatural forces. Although the individual systems possessed different applications and interactions, they could be understood as differentiated expressions of a more fundamental Energy state. Melida had demonstrated this principle naturally, whereas Victor required the perspective she provided to connect it with his accumulated knowledge.[3]
The breakthrough produced an enormous reaction throughout the surrounding environment. Victor's Energy underwent a transformation that briefly made its ordinary supernatural presence disappear from perception, despite the tremendous existential weight remaining within him. When he released his newly organized power, the resulting pressure was felt across enormous distances, forcing even powerful observers to recognize that he had achieved another major advancement. The disturbance attracted the attention of the Imperial authorities, who initially reacted as though an extraordinary supernatural incident had occurred within the Empire.[3]
Victor nevertheless continued concentrating upon Melida's development. He instructed her to create a sphere containing only Attributeless Energy before assigning it a specific characteristic. Melida could understand the intended result instinctively, but her practical control was not sufficiently refined to prevent unrelated Energies from entering the construct. Victor explained that mixing different Energies could be useful, but the ability to isolate a single property was necessary for greater precision and efficiency. The lesson established that Melida's extraordinary natural understanding did not eliminate the importance of patient technical development.[3]
Jeanne and Kaguya attempted to reproduce the same technique but discovered that knowing the principle was different from understanding it instinctively. Their attempts continued drawing upon the specialized Energies already available to them rather than producing the pure Attributeless foundation demonstrated by Victor and Melida. This highlighted the unusual nature of Melida's talent: even extremely powerful Dragon Goddesses possessing extensive experience could struggle with a process that she had approached almost naturally from birth.[3]
The aftermath of the transformation also revealed that Attributeless Energy could interfere with supernatural effects under particular circumstances. When Jeanne attempted to repair the damaged incubation chamber through Reality Distortion, portions of the structure resisted her efforts. She suspected that the Attributeless Energy involved in Melida's transformation was interfering with the specialized Energy used to perform the restoration, but she did not immediately consider her explanation complete. Recognizing the importance of the phenomenon, Jeanne preserved the relevant observations for further examination by the family's researchers.[3]
The event concluded with Melida being recognized as another Imperial Princess of Clan Elderblood. Her birth had not merely added a new member to Victor's family; it had introduced a Being whose natural understanding of Energy could inspire developments previously inaccessible even to individuals possessing extraordinarily advanced supernatural knowledge. The Empire's response to the disturbance soon gave way to recognition of the new daughter and the breakthrough associated with her creation.[7]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Melida continued developing during the enormous periods of accelerated Time experienced by the [[Elderblood Empire]]. The Empire's expansion provided her with access to increasingly advanced research facilities, supernatural materials and knowledgeable instructors, allowing her natural talent to develop into practical expertise. While many of her sisters specialized in combat, administration, Divine Concepts or the development of particular supernatural systems, Melida concentrated upon the fundamental behavior of Energy and the advanced manipulation of Souls.[4]
By Chapter 1148, her progress had become exceptional even by the standards of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family. During a discussion concerning the development of his wives and daughters, Victor asked about Melida's experiments and learned that their results were overwhelmingly positive. She had acquired full control over Souls and had recently developed the ability to channel Energies associated directly with Victor. Her progress was so consistent that she had become frustrated by how little difficulty the experiments presented, having expected her research to require more effort.[4]
Victor explained that Melida's extraordinary progress was partly connected to her reincarnated Soul foundation. Although her conscious mind possessed no ordinary memories of [[Merlin]]'s life, her Soul retained familiarity with paths previously traversed. This deeper spiritual experience could assist her in relearning disciplines associated with Souls and Energy, allowing her to progress more naturally than an individual encountering the same principles for the first time. The observation did not mean that Melida had reverted to Merlin's former personality; her conscious identity remained that of Victor's daughter.[4]
Her later development represented a substantial advancement from the abilities demonstrated immediately after her birth. Chapter 1105 had emphasized that none of Victor's daughters had inherited his innate Soul-control characteristic at that time, making it one of the rarer abilities associated with his existence. Melida's later acquisition of advanced Soul Manipulation demonstrated that her capabilities could develop beyond the characteristics immediately available through her Bloodline rebirth.[3][4]
By Chapter 1155, Melida had reached adulthood and was explicitly counted among Victor's wives alongside Ophis, [[Nero]], [[Metis]] and Akasha. Her inclusion reflected the considerable passage of experienced Time during the Imperial era and the development of her relationship with Victor beyond its original father-daughter form. She nevertheless retained her established position as one of the daughters created or reincarnated into the Elderblood family.[1]
During the incident involving Velina and the gathering of the Imperial Princesses, Melida appeared alongside Metis and Akasha after numerous other daughters had already assembled. Their arrival was treated differently from that of the younger sisters because several of the Princesses recognized the unusual position occupied by these women within the family. Melida, Metis and Akasha quickly understood the circumstances surrounding the gathering rather than being deceived by the explanations offered by the others.[5]
Melida's extraordinary talent became a subject of particular frustration for [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]]. Albedo had been studying advanced Energy manipulation and recognized how difficult the relevant principles should have been, especially when applied to the increasingly complex powers associated with Victor. However, Melida possessed an instinctive understanding of these systems, allowing her to progress rapidly through subjects that required others to undertake extensive research. The narration also identified Melida as capable of manipulating Victor's Soul, emphasizing the extraordinary sophistication of the Soul Manipulation she had developed.[5]
Albedo's reaction reflected the difference between accumulated intellectual achievement and the exceptional natural specialization possessed by Melida. Although Albedo was an accomplished researcher, she recognized that Melida had inherited an advantage that made certain Energy-related disciplines almost instinctive. The comparison did not diminish Albedo's own abilities, but demonstrated that Melida belonged to the exceptionally small group of individuals whose understanding of fundamental Energy systems exceeded the expectations normally applied even to advanced Dragon researchers.[5]
Melida later participated in Victor's lesson concerning Reality, dimensions and the differences between ordinary alternate spaces and independently established cosmic structures. The discussion began when [[Norabell Elderblood]], the daughter of Victor and Natalia, attempted to construct a dimension possessing independent rules and discovered that the process was far more difficult than creating a subordinate pocket space within the existing Universe. Victor gathered several daughters and demonstrated the distinction between a dimension supported by the ordinary laws of Reality and a more substantial cosmic structure possessing its own higher-order foundations.[6]
During the lesson, Melida displayed impatience because she already understood much of the material. When Victor presented a question concerning the differences between the structures he had created, he specifically instructed Melida not to answer. Isabelle was similarly prevented from responding, allowing the other daughters to examine the demonstration and develop their own conclusions. Melida reacted with visible displeasure because she wanted to participate despite possessing an advantage in the relevant subject.[6]
The lesson also revealed another major aspect of Melida's inherited abilities. Victor explained that his daughters possessed different degrees of compatibility with the power to perceive and alter the underlying structure of Reality. The ability was described through the analogy of observing and changing Reality's source code, allowing a sufficiently developed practitioner to understand and manipulate dimensions created according to the fundamental rules of the Universe. Melida was specifically identified alongside Norabell as possessing an unusually strong natural manifestation of this characteristic, with Norabell's eyes having not yet reached Melida's level.[6]
The distinction is important because Melida's extraordinary understanding of Energy is not her only advanced perceptual ability. She also possesses a particularly developed manifestation of the perception inherited from Victor, allowing her to recognize structures and supernatural principles that other daughters require considerably more effort to interpret. Nevertheless, the lesson did not establish that Melida possessed Victor's complete ability to dominate every level of Reality without limitation; it confirmed that her inherited perception was exceptionally advanced and continued developing.[6]
Melida's expertise became relevant again during the preparations for Victor's later ritual involving the merger of enormous supernatural systems. Albedo questioned why Victor did not simply request assistance from exceptionally capable daughters such as Melida and Isabelle, whose talents would make them valuable contributors to the work. Violet explained that Victor deliberately avoided placing unnecessary burdens upon his daughters, preferring that the older generation assume responsibility for dangerous undertakings whenever possible. Although some of his children possessed power sufficient to assist, Victor regarded their continued development and freedom from unnecessary responsibilities as more important than the convenience of relying upon their abilities.[8]
Violet also explained that Melida and Metis differed from creations such as Akasha, whose existence had originally been designed around a specific Imperial function. Melida had not been created to serve a predetermined administrative or military purpose. Victor regarded her as a daughter whose future should not be restricted by an obligation imposed at birth, even though her extraordinary talents could undoubtedly contribute to the Empire's development.[8]
During Victor's subsequent lessons concerning Energy, the Emperor returned to the same fundamental principles that Melida had helped him understand shortly after her birth. He explained that [[Divine Energy]], Demonic Energy, Spirit Energy, Mana, Ki and numerous other supernatural forces could be regarded as different manifestations of Energy, distinguished by the attributes imposed upon them. Attributeless Energy represented the unassigned foundation from which differentiated forms could emerge, while Primal Energy possessed its own characteristics despite occupying an extraordinarily fundamental position within Creation.[9]
Victor clarified that the distinction between Attributeless Energy and Primal Energy mattered considerably to ordinary practitioners, but far less to individuals possessing a fundamental understanding of Energy itself. He specifically identified Melida as one of the Beings for whom the difference was almost trivial, since both he and his daughter could manipulate Energy at a level where assigning or altering its characteristics presented relatively little difficulty. The explanation reinforced Melida's position as one of the foremost Energy specialists within the Elderblood family.[9]
Victor also explained that Attributeless Energy occupied a foundational position in the creation of the Universe, with differentiated Energies emerging through the assignment of particular characteristics. The lesson connected this principle to the operation of Draconic Runes and the commands through which powerful beings could influence Reality. Melida's presence within this discussion emphasized the extent to which her original specialization had matured from instinctive Energy imitation into a sophisticated understanding of the fundamental processes governing supernatural existence.[9]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Melida's father, Bloodline creator, principal teacher and later husband after she reaches adulthood. Their relationship began when Victor examined the Soul reconstructed by the Universal Tree and recognized that the resulting Being was different from [[Merlin]]. Rather than treating Charlotte as the continuation of a historical individual, he accepted her as a new daughter and personally gave her the Elderblood surname.[2][3]
Victor's naming carried extraordinary supernatural significance. By directing his [[Will Solace|Will]] through the declaration of Melida's identity, he caused her physical and spiritual existence to undergo another rebirth, integrating her into his Blood Dragon lineage. The transformation established their relationship through both personal recognition and a genuine supernatural connection, making Melida his daughter by name, Will and Bloodline.[3]
Their first interaction also established Victor as Melida's principal teacher. The newborn immediately began observing him and attempting to reproduce his abilities, including his armor, Energy signature and methods of bodily transformation. Victor encouraged her experimentation but corrected her when she became frustrated by the difficulty of isolating Attributeless Energy. His guidance demonstrated that he valued her development beyond simply obtaining another powerful member of the family.[3]
The relationship was unusually reciprocal from its beginning. Victor possessed far greater accumulated knowledge and experience, but Melida's instinctive understanding of Energy revealed a fundamental principle he had previously overlooked. By studying her methods, he achieved the breakthrough that became the foundation of [[Creator's Energy Mastery]]. Melida therefore occupies an unusual position among his daughters as someone whose natural talent directly inspired one of his major developments rather than merely benefiting from the powers and instruction he provided.[3]
Victor continued supporting Melida throughout her development. He followed the progress of her Soul-related research, recognized the contribution of her reconstructed spiritual foundation and remained attentive to the ways in which her talents could be cultivated. By the later Imperial era, Melida had reached adulthood and was included among his wives, while retaining the significance of her original creation and adoption into the Elderblood family.[4][1]
Her affection for Victor remained visible during the later family gatherings and lessons. Despite her advanced abilities, Melida could become irritated when her sisters monopolized his attention or when he prevented her from answering questions that she already understood. These interactions demonstrated that her enormous intellectual and supernatural development had not eliminated the personal attachment established during her earliest moments of life.[6]

#### The Universal Tree
[[The Universal Tree]] is responsible for the foundational reconstruction that allowed Melida to exist as a new individual. At Jeanne's request, he took the Soul formerly belonging to [[Merlin]] and used his authority over Life and Reincarnation to rebuild its Core of Existence. The intervention altered the Soul so profoundly that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] could no longer regard the result as the same Being merely restored through conventional reincarnation.[2]
The Universal Tree also established an unusual relationship with Melida's post-death existence. Because her Soul was reconstructed outside the ordinary Soul System, Jeanne explained that it would return directly to the Universal Tree if she died. His role therefore extends beyond providing the circumstances of her birth: he remains connected to the fundamental arrangements governing her reconstructed Soul.[2]
Although Melida became Victor's daughter through his naming and Bloodline influence, the Universal Tree's intervention remains the reason she could begin life as an independent individual rather than merely continuing Merlin's previous existence.

#### Jeanne Elderblood
[[Jeanne Elderblood]] was the principal individual responsible for requesting Melida's reincarnation. She approached her brother, the Universal Tree, and arranged for the reconstruction of [[Merlin]]'s former Soul, allowing the creation of a fundamentally new individual through Life and Reincarnation.[2]
Jeanne was present when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] named Melida and observed the extraordinary consequences of her Blood Dragon rebirth. She recognized the numerous supernatural Energies circulating within the newborn's body and was particularly astonished when Melida absorbed a portion of the [[Primordial Energy]] Jeanne controlled. The feat exceeded what Jeanne expected from a newly created Being and demonstrated the extraordinary compatibility Melida possessed with fundamental Energy systems.[3]
Rather than reacting negatively to Melida's exceptional potential, Jeanne was delighted that the family had acquired another individual capable of advancing its understanding of supernatural power. She recognized that Melida's talent would become an enormous advantage for the Elderblood family, especially once supported by the resources and instruction available through Victor's Empire.[3]
Jeanne continued participating in discussions concerning Melida's development during the later Imperial era. When Victor asked about their daughter's Soul-related research, Jeanne and Natalia were among the women familiar with the results, recognizing that Melida had progressed far beyond the capabilities demonstrated during her initial rebirth.[4]

#### Merlin
[[Merlin]] is the former owner of the Soul foundation used in Melida's creation. The relationship is essential to understanding her unusual abilities, but it does not mean that Melida simply possesses Merlin's previous identity under a different name.[2]
[[The Universal Tree]] fundamentally reconstructed the Soul's Core of Existence, creating a new individual whose conscious mind did not retain Merlin's ordinary memories. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] recognized the distinction immediately after examining the result, refusing to treat the new Soul as merely the resurrection of the previous individual.[2]
Nevertheless, Melida's relationship with Merlin is not entirely severed. Victor later explained that the reconstructed Soul retained a form of experiential familiarity with paths previously traversed, even though Melida herself did not consciously remember those experiences. The distinction helps explain her extraordinary development in Soul-related disciplines without requiring her to possess Merlin's former personality or memories.[4]
Melida is therefore best understood as a new individual created from a profoundly reconstructed Soul foundation that retains certain deeper traces of its previous development. The continuity lies in the underlying spiritual material and experiential familiarity, while her personal identity belongs to Melida Charlotte Elderblood.

#### Metis Elderblood
[[Metis]] Elderblood is one of Melida's sisters and another member of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family whose existence originated through reincarnation. Both women were created through supernatural processes that transformed previous Soul foundations into new individuals, distinguishing their origins from those of daughters born through ordinary biological reproduction.[4]
Metis possesses extraordinary intelligence associated with Wisdom, while Melida specializes in the fundamental understanding and manipulation of Energy. Their abilities therefore complement one another despite the differences in their primary disciplines. During the later Imperial era, the two were frequently associated with other exceptionally intelligent daughters such as Akasha, whose responsibilities involved the administration and technological infrastructure of the Empire.[4][5]
When the Imperial Princesses gathered during the incident involving Velina, Metis and Melida were among the women who quickly recognized the circumstances behind the gathering rather than being misled by the explanations circulating among their sisters. Their reactions emphasized the exceptional intelligence possessed by both women.[5]

#### Akasha Elderblood
[[Akasha Elderblood]] is another of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s created daughters, although the circumstances of her creation differed from those surrounding Melida. Akasha was developed with specific responsibilities connected to the protection and administration of the [[Elderblood Empire]], while Melida was accepted into the family without being assigned a predetermined institutional function.[4][8]
The two frequently appear alongside [[Metis]] as representatives of the exceptionally intelligent younger members of Clan Elderblood. Akasha's abilities are centered upon the administration and protection of the Empire, whereas Melida possesses a more specialized talent for the fundamental nature of Energy and Souls.[5]
Their different purposes were later emphasized when Violet explained why Victor preferred not to involve his daughters unnecessarily in dangerous Imperial projects. Akasha had originally been designed with a specific function, whereas Melida and Metis had been created as daughters whose personal development was not intended to be restricted by predetermined obligations.[8]

#### Albedo Moriarty Elderblood
[[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]] is one of the Elderblood family's most accomplished researchers, possessing extensive knowledge of supernatural systems and advanced Energy-related disciplines. Her relationship with Melida is particularly notable because Melida's natural talent challenged Albedo's expectations concerning the difficulty of manipulating fundamental Energies.[5]
During the gathering of the Imperial Princesses, Albedo complained about the difficulty of handling the relevant Energy systems before remembering that Melida could manipulate them almost instinctively. She recognized that Melida's talent extended to extraordinarily advanced Soul-related applications and was frustrated by the apparent ease with which the younger Dragon approached subjects that required extensive study from other researchers.[5]
Albedo's reaction did not develop into genuine hostility. Instead, she acknowledged Melida's exceptional specialization while remaining determined to continue her own research. The comparison demonstrates how unusual Melida's natural understanding remains even among researchers possessing enormous supernatural knowledge and resources.[5]

#### Norabell Elderblood
[[Norabell Elderblood]] is the daughter of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Natalia, possessing exceptional inherited abilities associated with Time, Space and the perception of Reality. Her relationship with Melida is especially relevant because both are identified among the daughters possessing unusually powerful eyes capable of recognizing the underlying structure of existence.[6]
During Victor's lesson concerning dimensions and cosmic laws, Norabell struggled to distinguish between a conventional alternate dimension and a more substantial structure possessing its own higher-order foundations. Victor used the opportunity to explain the significance of the different Divine Concepts and rules supporting such constructs. Melida was prevented from answering the questions immediately because her superior understanding would have deprived Norabell and the other daughters of the opportunity to reach their own conclusions.[6]
The narration explicitly compared Norabell's Eyes with Melida's, explaining that Norabell had not yet reached the same level of development. This establishes Melida as one of the family's most accomplished inheritors of Victor's Reality-perception abilities, although Norabell possesses her own exceptional potential through the combination of her parents' powers.[6]

### Powers and Abilities
Melida possesses the physical and supernatural characteristics of a genuine Blood Dragon, but her greatest strengths lie in Energy Manipulation, Soul Manipulation and the perception of Reality's underlying structure. Her unusual origin also grants her a reconstructed Soul existing outside the conventional Soul System, while her natural specialization allows her to understand supernatural Energies at a level that challenges the knowledge of exceptionally powerful Gods and advanced researchers.[2][3][4][6]

#### Blood Dragon Physiology
Blood Dragon Physiology: Melida became a genuine Blood Dragon after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] gave her the Elderblood name and reconstructed her existence through his Bloodline. Her transformation provided a complete True Dragon body rather than merely imitating the physical characteristics of the race.[3]
- True Dragon Physiology: Melida possesses the physical and spiritual foundation of a genuine True Dragon.[3]
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics: Her Dragon body possesses extraordinary strength, speed, durability, reflexes, agility and endurance beyond those of ordinary mortal beings.[3]
- Draconic Wings: Large Dragon wings emerged from Melida's back during her rebirth, damaging the incubation chamber surrounding her.[3]
- Flight: Melida possesses the Draconic wings and supernatural mobility associated with her True Dragon physiology.[3]
- Draconic Horns: Her original brown horns became black with dark-violet nuances after Victor reconstructed her Bloodline.[3]
- Draconic Metamorphosis: Melida can modify her physical characteristics, including enlarging her humanoid form from approximately 180 centimeters to 4.75 meters.[3]
- Enhanced Senses: Melida possesses the advanced sensory foundation of a True Dragon, supplementing the extraordinary perception associated with her individual abilities.[3][6]
- [[Draconic Energy]]: Her Blood Dragon physiology provides the supernatural foundation necessary for producing and manipulating Draconic Energy.[3]
- Dragon Soul: Melida's spiritual existence was integrated into Victor's Bloodline through the supernatural rebirth caused by his naming.[3]
- Supernatural Longevity: Melida possesses the extraordinary longevity associated with genuine [[True Dragons]] and continued developing throughout the Empire's enormous accelerated periods.[4]
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Her Dragon physiology provides the extraordinary biological resilience associated with the Blood Dragon race, although her individual regeneration limits have not been separately quantified.
- Draconic Language Compatibility: Melida possesses the racial foundation associated with the supernatural language of True Dragons, through which [[Will Solace|Will]] and Energy can be expressed as commands affecting Reality.[6][9]

Understanding of Energy is Melida's principal natural specialization. Melida can intuitively recognize the properties of Energy and reproduce effects without requiring the same extensive theoretical preparation normally necessary for a newly created Being.[3]

- Energy Analysis: Melida can recognize the characteristics of different Energies and attempt to reproduce their behavior.[3]

- Energy Conversion: Melida can convert Attributeless Energy into differentiated forms by assigning specific characteristics.[3]
- Multiple Energy Affinities: Her body contained several different Energy types immediately after her Blood Dragon rebirth, including Miasma, Angelic Light, Divinity, Positive Energy, Negative Energy and [[Magic]].[3]
- Advanced Energy Compatibility: Melida can interact with extraordinarily powerful Energies, including the [[Primordial Energy]] controlled by Jeanne.[3]
- Advanced Energy Research: By the later Imperial era, Melida had developed sufficient expertise to conduct sophisticated experiments involving Souls and fundamental Energies.[4]
- Fundamental Energy Understanding: Victor later identified Melida as one of the rare Beings for whom the distinction between Attributeless Energy and Primal Energy presented little practical difficulty.[9]

Attributeless Energy Manipulation: Melida possesses an instinctive understanding of Energy before the assignment of a particular supernatural characteristic. - Attributeless Energy Manipulation: Melida can manipulate Energy lacking an assigned supernatural attribute.[3]
- Attribute Assignment: She can impose specific characteristics upon Attributeless Energy, allowing it to behave as a differentiated Energy type.[3]
- Energy Conversion: Melida can transform an Attributeless foundation into Energy possessing particular properties.[3]
- Energy-Type Reproduction: Melida can reproduce Energy characteristics she understands through the manipulation of Attributeless Energy.[3]
- Multi-Energy Manipulation: Her abilities are compatible with numerous supernatural Energy systems rather than being restricted to a single elemental affinity.[3]

- Fundamental Energy Affinity: Her specialization allows her to interact with Energy at a level closer to its underlying foundation than that normally available to supernatural beings.[3][9]

- Primordial Energy Absorption: Melida absorbed a portion of the Primordial Energy controlled by Jeanne.[3]
- Multiple Energy Compatibility: Her body can contain and interact with several different forms of supernatural Energy simultaneously.[3]

#### Existence Outside the Soul System
Melida's Soul was reconstructed by the Universal Tree without being incorporated into the ordinary regulatory mechanisms governing Souls. This status is a fundamental characteristic of her origin rather than a technique she consciously activated.[2]
- Alternative Soul Registration: Melida exists outside the conventional registration process associated with the ordinary Soul System.[2]

- Reconstructed Core of Existence: Melida's fundamental spiritual identity was rebuilt through the Universal Tree's authority over Life and Reincarnation.[2]
This status should not be interpreted as absolute immunity to Soul-based attacks or proof that Melida is incapable of dying. Its confirmed significance concerns the structure and regulation of her Soul rather than unrestricted resistance to every supernatural authority.

- Energy Compatibility Does Not Equal Authority Ownership: Melida's ability to reproduce or channel different Energies does not automatically grant her every Divine Concept or specialized technique associated with their original users.
- [[Primordial Energy]] Absorption Does Not Equal Primordial Status: Absorbing Jeanne's Primordial Energy demonstrates extraordinary compatibility but does not establish that Melida possesses the full authority of a Primordial Entity.[3]
- Advanced Soul Manipulation Was Developed Later: Her later control over Souls should not be backdated to the earliest moments of her existence without supporting evidence.[3][4]
- Reality Perception Requires Understanding: The ability to perceive and alter the underlying structure of Reality depends upon the practitioner's development and understanding of the target's supernatural foundations.[6]
- Existence Outside the Soul System Is Not Absolute Immunity: Melida's alternative post-death arrangement does not prove that she cannot suffer Soul damage, conceptual interference or permanent destruction.

### Identity and Reincarnation
Melida's existence represents an unusual distinction between the continuity of a Soul and the continuity of the person associated with it. The Soul used in her creation once belonged to [[Merlin]], but the Universal Tree altered its Core of Existence so extensively that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] regarded the result as an independent individual rather than a conventional resurrection. Melida therefore possesses an origin connected to Merlin without being identical to the former magician.[2]
The process involved two major stages. First, the Universal Tree reconstructed the original Soul foundation through Life and Reincarnation, establishing a new identity and an alternative relationship with the conventional Soul System. Second, Victor named the resulting individual Melida Charlotte Elderblood and reconstructed her body and spiritual foundation through his Bloodline, transforming her into a Blood Dragon.[2][3]

### Role in Victor's Development
Melida possesses a distinctive position among [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters because her natural talent directly inspired a major breakthrough in his understanding of supernatural Energy. Before meeting her, Victor already possessed extraordinary control over numerous Energy systems, including [[Divine Energy]], [[Magic]], Miasma, elemental Energies, Positive and Negative Energy, [[Draconic Energy]] and several other supernatural forces. However, his methods generally involved obtaining, absorbing or manipulating Energy that already possessed a particular characteristic.[3]
Melida demonstrated a fundamentally different approach. She worked with Energy lacking a predetermined attribute and imposed the characteristics required by the desired effect. Victor recognized that this implied a common underlying foundation from which different supernatural Energies could emerge, transforming his understanding of their relationship with one another.[3]
The discovery was not a transfer of superior accumulated knowledge from Melida to Victor. Rather, Melida possessed an instinctive perspective that Victor had not previously considered, while Victor possessed the experience necessary to develop that perspective into a more comprehensive system. Their interaction therefore demonstrated how a naturally gifted specialist could inspire a more experienced Being to recognize principles hidden beneath familiar supernatural mechanisms.[3]
The later explanation in Chapter 1185 reinforced this development. Victor described Attributeless Energy as the underlying foundation from which specialized Energies could be formed, while distinguishing it from Primal Energy, which already possessed attributes. He identified Melida as one of the rare individuals capable of manipulating Energy at a level where this distinction was comparatively insignificant, demonstrating how far her specialization had developed since the first lesson she received after birth.[9]
Melida's importance therefore extends beyond the acquisition of powerful individual abilities. Her existence revealed a fundamental principle concerning the nature of Energy, contributed to Victor's development of [[Creator's Energy Mastery]] and subsequently produced one of the Empire's foremost researchers in advanced Energy and Soul-related systems.[3][4][9]
