#### Debut
Chapter 990
We are not just humble Goddesses. We have our Father's Blood; we are super Goddesses!
—Rosemary's telepathic reaction to [[Evie Moriarty Elderblood]] discovering the girls' Divine nature, Chapter 990
Rosemary Lavina Elderblood is a recurring character in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]], an Imperial Princess of the [[Elderblood Empire]], a Blood Dragon Goddess, and the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]]. Born into Victor's second generation of naturally occurring [[True Dragons]], Rosemary possesses an extraordinarily powerful Draconic foundation while simultaneously continuing the maternal lineage of Clan Scarlett. She is also the granddaughter of [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], connecting her to one of [[Nightingale]]'s oldest and most formidable martial families.[1][4]
Rosemary possesses several characteristics inherited from her parents, including Ruby's exceptionally crimson-red hair, Victor's violet Dragon Eyes, an extraordinary Draconic intellect and the immense physical capabilities of a naturally born Blood Dragon. Despite these similarities, her personal ambitions differ considerably from Ruby's. Whereas her mother became one of the Empire's foremost scientists and researchers, Rosemary prefers combat training, military activities, entertainment and spending time with her siblings. Her stated ambition is to eventually command one of the Empire's military Legions, allowing her to contribute directly to its defense and expansion rather than pursue a career in scientific research.[5]
She is especially close to [[Valentina Victoria Elderblood]] and [[Silvia Elderblood]], the respective daughters of Victor and his original wives Violet and Sasha. The three frequently accompany one another, communicate telepathically and share an intense fascination with their father. Their unpredictable behavior, mischievous personalities and constant association have earned them recognition as the "trio of chaos".[4][6][7]
Although Rosemary has little interest in spending her life conducting scientific experiments, she possesses the intellectual abilities characteristic of Victor's biological daughters. Her Draconic mind allows her to process enormous quantities of information, understand highly advanced technological systems and analyze unfamiliar supernatural phenomena. She demonstrates these capabilities when examining Pepper's private research, correctly interpreting the protective mechanisms surrounding an artificial star and rapidly understanding scientific documentation despite lacking extensive specialized experience.[8]
Rosemary also demonstrates considerable adaptability and an enthusiasm for exploring unfamiliar supernatural systems. She participates in Victor's large-scale military simulations, temporarily assuming the role of a Fairy Queen after losing the advantages of her natural Dragon physiology. Her later activities include participating in the development of an Eldritch being alongside several sisters, contributing to an experiment that ultimately results in the creation of the Great Old One [[Cthugha]].[9][10]
Despite her extraordinary chronological age and supernatural abilities, Rosemary retains a playful and affectionate personality. She enjoys manga, anime, movies and spending time with her sisters, frequently becoming involved in troublesome activities despite understanding the consequences. Her close relationship with the Imperial Family remains one of the most important aspects of her character, particularly her attachment to Victor and the affection she receives from [[Nero]] and Ophis, both of whom played important roles throughout her upbringing.[6][5]

### Physical Appearance
Rosemary is an exceptionally beautiful young True Dragon Goddess with fair skin, vivid crimson-red hair and deep violet eyes. Her facial features and distinctive hair strongly resemble those of [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]], making her maternal ancestry immediately recognizable. Her hair is particularly noteworthy because of its unusually intense crimson coloration, which [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] specifically notices while introducing her to Evie Moriarty. Her violet eyes, meanwhile, are characteristic of Victor's biological descendants and reflect the supernatural perception inherited from their father.[4]
During her introductory era, Rosemary possesses the Humanoid appearance of a small girl standing approximately 120 centimeters tall. She has visible Draconic horns and the youthful facial characteristics shared by several of Victor's naturally born daughters. When Victor accompanies Rosemary, Valentina and Silvia into Evie's residence, the three resemble miniature versions of Ruby, Violet and Sasha respectively, with Rosemary immediately distinguished by her exceptionally red hair.[1][4]
Her violet eyes are considerably more than an inherited physical characteristic. All of Victor's biological daughters possess a form of supernatural perception derived from his ability to observe the deeper truths of Reality. The initial strength of this inheritance differs between individuals, but every daughter possesses the potential to further develop her Eyes through training. Rosemary therefore shares the general foundation of the inherited Dragon Eyes, although the exact level of her personal perception and any specialized applications remain distinct from Victor's complete abilities.[11]
Rosemary's small Humanoid body conceals the extraordinary physiology of a genuine True Dragon. Dragons naturally retain the enormous mass associated with their Draconic bodies even when assuming Humanoid appearances unless they actively regulate that characteristic. During Victor's meeting with Evie, he reinforces the sofa intended for his daughters by reconstructing it from exceptionally durable Divine material. Despite this reinforcement, the furniture audibly creaks beneath the combined weight of Rosemary, Valentina and Silvia. Evie realizes that the girls are considerably heavier than their appearances suggest, while Victor's ability to sit without producing a similar reaction demonstrates his vastly superior control over his own physical characteristics.[4]
Rosemary also casually levitates through Evie's residence rather than walking. The effortless manner in which she and her sisters remain airborne contributes to the Witch Queen's growing realization that the apparently harmless children possess abilities vastly beyond those of ordinary supernatural beings.[4]
Her youthful appearance does not accurately represent her chronological age. Naturally born [[True Dragons]] develop physically over periods vastly longer than those experienced by Humans or conventional Vampires, allowing individuals to accumulate centuries or millennia of life while retaining childlike Humanoid bodies. Rosemary was already approximately one thousand years old when introduced, having been born during the enormous period of accelerated Time experienced by Victor and his family.[1]
Her slow physical maturation occasionally frustrates her. When Violet attempts to dismiss the younger daughters by emphasizing their youthful appearances, Rosemary complains about the Draconic characteristics preventing her body from developing more quickly. The exchange reveals that she is conscious of the disparity between her chronological age and physical appearance, particularly when comparing herself with the mature figures of her mothers.[7]
As a naturally born Blood Dragon, Rosemary possesses the supernatural foundation associated with Draconic transformation and the ability to assume different physical characteristics. However, the complete appearance of her individual Dragon Form has not been described sufficiently to establish its precise size, scale coloration, anatomical distinctions or other unique features. Her confirmed visual characteristics remain her crimson-red hair, violet eyes, visible Draconic horns and strong resemblance to Ruby.

### Personality
Rosemary is playful, curious, intelligent, affectionate, mischievous and highly confident, possessing an enthusiasm for both supernatural combat and entertainment. Her personality reflects the unusual upbringing of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s second-generation daughters, who were born with extraordinary abilities and raised within a family whose power vastly exceeds that of ordinary supernatural civilizations. Although she is conscious of her privileged position, she remains capable of accepting corrections, recognizing the competence of others and acknowledging subjects in which her sisters possess greater experience.[4][5]
Her attachment to Victor is particularly strong. Rosemary idolizes her father and takes considerable pleasure in observing his displays of power, authority and confidence. She proudly regards herself and her sisters as superior to ordinary Goddesses because they inherited his Blood, and she becomes visibly excited whenever he demonstrates the overwhelming superiority that distinguishes him from other supernatural rulers. During his negotiations with Evie Moriarty, Rosemary and her companions frequently treat the encounter as an opportunity to admire their father rather than merely a political meeting.[4]
Rosemary is also fascinated by Victor's earlier life. She values photographs and recordings depicting him before he became the God Emperor, particularly because she was born long after many of his earliest adventures. She knows that Violet and Anna possess unusually extensive private collections documenting his history and expresses interest in obtaining access to those materials. Her fascination becomes especially apparent when she discovers Pepper's archive of photographs and recordings from Victor's arrival in [[Nightingale]], including material documenting his battles and earlier encounters with members of the supernatural world.[4][8]
Her curiosity sometimes leads her to make observations that provoke strong reactions from others. During her introduction to Evie, Rosemary wonders whether the Witch Queen might eventually become another of Victor's wives. Valentina and Silvia react possessively, but Rosemary approaches the possibility partly through Evie's competence and the usefulness of her abilities. Although she shares the intense attachment to Victor common among his daughters, she does not automatically reject every potential addition to the family without considering the circumstances.[4]
Rosemary occasionally proposes direct and overwhelmingly destructive solutions to problems. When Evie suggests that Victor's faction might be responsible for the supernatural illness affecting her daughter, Rosemary dismisses the accusation because the Empire possesses sufficient strength to destroy Arcane openly. She casually suggests that an orbital cannon could eliminate the territory without requiring complicated political schemes. When Silvia argues that conquest would preserve valuable inhabitants and knowledge, Rosemary initially questions the usefulness of acquiring material resources that Victor could create himself. However, she immediately recognizes that competent people cannot be treated as interchangeable physical resources and accepts Silvia's reasoning without unnecessary argument.[4]
This willingness to acknowledge a better explanation distinguishes Rosemary from individuals who regard their overwhelming strength as proof that their initial judgment must always be correct. She possesses considerable confidence in the Elderblood family, but she is not incapable of learning from her sisters or recognizing the limitations of her own perspective.
Rosemary's personal interests differ substantially from Ruby's despite their striking physical resemblance and similarly advanced intellects. Ruby possesses an intense fascination with science, research and technological development, while Rosemary prefers activities that provide entertainment, excitement or opportunities for physical training. She enjoys reading manga, watching anime and movies, and participating in activities with her sisters. Although she can understand complex scientific subjects, she dislikes spending extended periods conducting research and prefers allowing sisters with a genuine enthusiasm for such work to pursue it.[5]
Her interest in anime is particularly visible during [[Nero]] and Ophis' training. Rosemary recognizes a fictional technique when Ophis reproduces a similar maneuver during their sparring session and becomes exceptionally excited at seeing something inspired by entertainment used in a genuine supernatural battle. She believes fictional stories can provide useful inspiration for developing combat techniques because Human imagination frequently produces ideas that can be reproduced or improved through supernatural abilities. She encourages her sisters to experience different forms of entertainment rather than restricting themselves exclusively to conventional training.[6]
Despite enjoying entertainment, Rosemary possesses serious martial ambitions. She intends to eventually command one of the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s military Legions, believing that military service and leadership would allow her to make a meaningful contribution to the civilization established by her father. This goal distinguishes her from sisters who prefer scientific research, administration or other specialized occupations. Rosemary recognizes that her extraordinary natural abilities provide enormous potential, but she must still acquire the training and experience necessary to use that potential effectively.[5]
Rosemary demonstrates particular affection toward her older sisters Nero and Ophis. She grew up treating both as genuine older sisters through their close relationship with Ruby, and they reciprocated by giving her considerable attention. Nero, who regards Ruby as her mother, is especially indulgent toward Rosemary, while Ophis follows Nero's example and similarly spoils her. Rosemary's enthusiastic reaction to Ophis reproducing a fictional combat maneuver illustrates the warmth of their relationship, with Ophis acknowledging her younger sister's excitement through an affectionate gesture during the sparring session.[6]
Rosemary's loyalty toward her sisters frequently overrides her preference for avoiding unnecessary trouble. When Priscillia becomes determined to infiltrate Pepper's private residence, Rosemary immediately recognizes the danger and attempts to discourage her. Nevertheless, she ultimately accompanies Priscillia alongside Sylvia because neither is willing to abandon a sister who is clearly preparing to do something reckless. Their decision reflects the principle that members of the Elderblood family support one another even when they disagree with each other's judgment.[5]
Her loyalty does not prevent her from challenging senior family members. Rosemary joins Valentina and Silvia in criticizing Violet when the Empress makes an important decision concerning Victor's family without adequately consulting everyone affected. She is willing to express her displeasure openly, demonstrating that her attachment to the household does not require unconditional agreement with every decision made by its older members.[7]
Rosemary also possesses a mischievous streak that persists despite the Empire's extraordinary accelerated development. She participates in the prank involving Velina and later contributes to an unauthorized experiment involving Eldritch creatures and Victor's Draconic scales. Although she recognizes the possibility of punishment, she is frequently drawn into such activities through curiosity, excitement and the enjoyment of cooperating with her sisters. Her behavior reflects a family in which immense supernatural power and exceptional intelligence coexist with the ordinary desire to play, explore and have fun.[12][10]

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Rosemary was born during the extensive period of accelerated Time experienced by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his family following his ascension as the Dragon God of [[Chaos]]. While approximately two years passed in the outside world, Victor and many of those closest to him experienced roughly two thousand years within his personal Reality. His wives continued developing their Divine and Draconic abilities, while the family produced a new generation of biological daughters combining Victor's Blood Dragon nature with the characteristics inherited from their mothers. Rosemary was among these children, born from Victor and [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] and possessing the supernatural foundation of a genuine True Dragon Goddess.[1]
Her existence represented the continuation of the Scarlett family's lineage within Victor's newly established Blood Dragon dynasty. Through Ruby, she inherited a connection to the ancient Scarlett family and its martial traditions, while Victor provided the extraordinary Draconic physiology and potential characteristic of his biological descendants. Unlike Ruby and [[Scathach]], who were originally Noble Vampires before experiencing their later transformations, Rosemary was born directly into a much more advanced supernatural existence.
Rosemary developed a close relationship with [[Valentina Victoria Elderblood]] and [[Silvia Elderblood]], whose respective mothers were Violet and Sasha. Their upbringing reflected the enduring relationship between Victor's first three wives, with the daughters becoming almost inseparable companions who regularly shared activities, conversations and their enthusiasm for their father.
Rosemary made her first substantial individual appearance when Victor visited [[Evie Moriarty Elderblood]], Queen of the Witches, during the crisis affecting the Arcane Realm. He arrived accompanied by Valentina, Silvia and Rosemary, whose outwardly innocent appearances initially concealed their extraordinary supernatural nature. Although the girls remained quiet, Evie immediately sensed that something unusual was occurring because all three watched her with violet eyes and sweet expressions while appearing to communicate without speaking.[4]
Their behavior became even more unsettling when Evie used [[Magic]] to close the door of her chambers. The three girls immediately noticed the unfamiliar Energy, reacting with curiosity before returning to their composed expressions. Evie also realized that they had been floating rather than walking, an otherwise casual behavior that demonstrated how accustomed the daughters were to their extraordinary physical abilities.
When Evie offered them a place to sit, Victor reinforced the furniture by transforming it into a much stronger structure composed of Divine material. The reason became apparent when the girls settled upon the sofa and its frame audibly creaked beneath their immense Draconic weight. Evie understood that their small Humanoid appearances did not eliminate the physical mass associated with their genuine Dragon bodies, while Victor's ability to sit without producing a similar reaction demonstrated his exceptional bodily control.[4]
Victor subsequently introduced his daughters individually. Valentina was identified as his daughter with Violet, Silvia as his daughter with Sasha, and Rosemary as his daughter with Ruby. He placed his hand upon Rosemary's extraordinarily crimson-red hair while introducing her, and she smiled in response to his affectionate treatment. The three greeted Evie in unison before resuming their private telepathic conversation.
Silvia questioned Rosemary about the unusual Energy surrounding the Witch Queen, and Rosemary observed that it appeared connected to Evie while originating from somewhere beyond her immediate body. She also recognized that the Energy was weaker than the powers normally employed by the Dragon Goddesses. Valentina identified the phenomenon as Magic and explained that it remained useful despite possessing a lower level of power than their familiar supernatural systems. The girls then recalled that Albedo employed the same Energy and had previously granted Victor access to it.[4]
Rosemary soon began wondering whether Evie might eventually become another member of their family through marriage to Victor. Her suggestion caused Valentina and Silvia to react with immediate displeasure, as both were highly possessive of their father. Rosemary nevertheless considered Evie's competence relevant, reasoning that a woman capable of ruling the Witches might possess qualities valuable to the Elderblood family. Valentina assessed the possibility against Victor's existing relationships, while Silvia eventually noticed that her sister's thoughts were leaking through their telepathic connection.
Evie gradually realized that the three girls were genuine Goddesses. Rosemary reacted proudly, emphasizing that their inherited connection to Victor placed them beyond ordinary Divine beings. Silvia enthusiastically added that they were Super Dragon Goddesses, prompting Victor to confirm that they were Goddesses of Dragons. The exchange further astonished Evie, who had initially struggled to comprehend that Victor already possessed biological daughters born with such extraordinary powers.[4]
Victor continued conducting his negotiations while indulging the girls. He created three cups containing their preferred fruit juices, giving Valentina grape juice, Silvia orange juice and Rosemary acerola juice. The cups continuously replenished their contents, allowing the daughters to drink without interruption. Evie eventually recognized that the seemingly ordinary containers were extremely valuable Divine artifacts capable of endlessly reproducing their contents, an extraordinary application of power that Victor treated as a simple convenience for his children.
Rosemary and her sisters became increasingly excited as Victor began exposing the true reason behind Evie's request for assistance. When his demeanor became more commanding and he started dismantling the Witch Queen's arguments, the girls wished to record the interaction. Valentina admitted that she had forgotten their cameras, provoking considerable frustration from Silvia. Rosemary quickly remembered that Albedo's Magic could convert memories into recordings and proposed using it to preserve the encounter, satisfying her sisters and allowing them to continue enjoying Victor's display of authority.[4]
The negotiations became particularly serious when Victor revealed that Evie's daughter was suffering from a mysterious illness of Divine origin. Evie questioned whether Victor's own faction might have been responsible, prompting the girls to dismiss the suggestion through their telepathic conversation. Valentina argued that their family possessed no reason to employ such indirect methods, while Silvia claimed that even comparatively weak Dragonoids belonging to their faction could devastate Arcane without requiring Victor or his wives to intervene.
Rosemary suggested that an orbital cannon could accomplish the same objective more directly. Silvia disagreed, arguing that destroying an entire civilization would waste potentially valuable people and resources. Rosemary initially questioned the importance of material possessions when Victor possessed the ability to create practically anything he wanted, but Silvia clarified that competent inhabitants and skilled specialists represented a different kind of resource. She specifically identified Evie as someone who could contribute to the technological and magical research performed by Velnorah, Ruby and Aline. Rosemary recognized the distinction and accepted the explanation, acknowledging that her initial observation had overlooked the importance of preserving talented individuals.[4]
The discussion concluded with Victor securing Evie's agreement to place Arcane beneath his authority in exchange for resolving the crisis affecting her daughter. Rosemary and her sisters witnessed the negotiations with overwhelming admiration, repeatedly reacting to Victor's confidence and the ease with which he handled matters that had threatened an entire supernatural civilization.
Victor's affection toward the girls remained evident throughout the encounter. He continued providing them with attention and treating their ordinary desires as important despite his overwhelming responsibilities as Emperor. Rosemary, Valentina and Silvia were delighted by his behavior, while Velnorah recognized that their father's tendency to indulge them was balanced by the strict education and discipline maintained throughout the family.
Rosemary later became involved in the extensive military training exercises organized by Victor for his daughters. The Emperor recognized that children born with such extraordinary strength could develop dangerous misconceptions about warfare if they were never forced to confront opponents possessing comparable or superior abilities. He therefore developed simulations intended to teach strategy, leadership, adaptation and the importance of understanding circumstances rather than simply overpowering every obstacle.
The exercises initially involved several daughters placed in unfamiliar military situations, including Priscillia, Nikolina and Siren. Victor observed their decisions and explained the consequences of relying excessively upon natural strength or charisma without understanding the circumstances of their subordinates. He subsequently expanded the exercise into a much larger Dream-based simulation incorporating the Tower of Nightmare, allowing its supernatural systems to create an environment in which the participants could experience complicated military and political situations without permanently endangering themselves.[9]
Rosemary was selected alongside [[Gina Elderblood]], [[Stella Elderblood]] and [[Yuno Elderblood]] for the next stage of the exercise. Victor assigned each girl a separate group and placed them in different circumstances, deliberately altering their available powers and sometimes their entire [[Races (Flamel)|Races]]. The participants were restricted to the strength of a Mid-Level [[Ares|God of War]] while their supernatural senses were severely weakened, ensuring that their natural advantages could not completely eliminate the challenges of the simulation.
Rosemary's transformation was particularly dramatic. Instead of retaining her Blood Dragon body, she was temporarily changed into a Fairy Queen and placed among a group of small Fairies. She immediately questioned how she was expected to improve her position using such an unfamiliar Race, recognizing the enormous difference between her ordinary Draconic foundation and the abilities available within the exercise.[9]
The purpose of the transformation was to force Rosemary to consider problems that could not be solved through overwhelming personal strength. As a Fairy Queen, she needed to understand the capabilities of her new Race, the resources available to her and the difficulties associated with governing beings far weaker than her original form. The simulation also required the participants to develop judgment concerning subordinates, alliances and conflicts within a larger political environment.
Victor continued expanding the Dream World until the War Games developed into a large-scale civilization-building simulation involving numerous daughters and members of the family. The environment incorporated distinct Races, political organizations and competing supernatural systems, allowing the participants to experience situations beyond those ordinarily encountered by members of the extraordinarily powerful Elderblood dynasty.
Rosemary's participation in the War Games established an important foundation for her later military ambitions. Although she possessed the natural strength of a True Dragon Goddess, Victor deliberately ensured that his children understood the difference between strength and the ability to lead others. Her Fairy Queen state remained a temporary transformation imposed by the simulation rather than a permanent alteration of her Race.
Rosemary continued spending time with her sisters as the Empire expanded. She was particularly close to [[Nero]] and Ophis, both of whom had become important figures in her upbringing. Because Nero regarded Ruby as her own mother, she treated Rosemary as an especially precious younger sister and spoiled her considerably more than most of the other daughters. Ophis, who was almost always beside Nero, gradually adopted the same behavior and became equally indulgent toward Rosemary.[6]
Their relationship was demonstrated when Rosemary joined Stella, Silvia and Valentina in observing Nero and Ophis training within one of the family's arenas. The two older sisters exchanged attacks at extraordinary speeds, combining refined martial techniques with supernatural movement and Draconic power. Rosemary compared the spectacle to watching a fighting anime, while the others acknowledged that Nero and Ophis possessed combat experience beyond that of most younger daughters.
The training reached an especially exciting moment when Ophis began preparing her Dragon Breath before suddenly using teleportation to reposition herself behind Nero. She released the attack at close range, catching her opponent by surprise and producing a destructive blast whose impact was absorbed by the arena's protective barrier. Rosemary immediately recognized the maneuver as resembling a technique from an anime she had previously watched and became so excited that she jumped repeatedly while celebrating Ophis' success.[6]
Ophis noticed Rosemary's reaction and responded with an affectionate smile and a victory gesture. Rosemary enthusiastically praised her older sister, revealing the close bond between them. Stella and Silvia, however, failed to recognize the fictional reference, provoking disbelief from Rosemary and Valentina.
Their subsequent discussion revealed the different entertainment preferences among Victor's daughters. Rosemary believed anime and other fictional media could provide genuine inspiration for combat techniques, while Valentina agreed that Human imagination was exceptionally useful. Silvia and Stella were less interested in anime and preferred cartoons, books, movies or other activities. Rosemary accepted these differences while continuing to recommend entertainment as a source of imaginative ideas.
After Nero and Ophis concluded their sparring session, they approached the younger girls and asked why they had gathered to observe them. The sisters initially attempted to avoid revealing their intentions, but Ophis correctly recognized that they were interested in the administrative opportunities recently opened within the Empire. Rosemary and her companions sought information concerning the responsibilities carried by their older sisters, hoping to understand how they could eventually contribute to the civilization established by their father.[6]
The encounter reinforced Rosemary's appreciation for experience and competence. Nero and Ophis possessed extraordinary inherited potential, but their strength was also supported by the training and practical knowledge they had accumulated over many years. Rosemary's respect for them reflected her own interest in developing a future military role rather than remaining dependent solely upon the abilities she possessed from birth.

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Rosemary continued developing alongside the Imperial Family during the enormous temporal acceleration initiated by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Natalia. The project was intended to grant the Empire approximately two hundred thousand years of internal development while only a comparatively short period elapsed outside the affected region. The resulting transformation allowed Imperial civilization to advance across enormous spans of subjective Time, developing new military institutions, technological systems, infrastructure and populations while members of Victor's family continued pursuing their individual ambitions.[2]
Despite the passing of these immense periods, Rosemary retained the close relationship she had established with Valentina and Silvia. Their personalities remained playful and confrontational, while their attachment to Victor continued influencing their reactions to changes within the family.
A major disagreement arose when Violet attempted to introduce additional women into Victor's household without adequately consulting the other wives and daughters. The decision provoked an immediate reaction throughout the family, with numerous women becoming aware that Violet had acted on her own initiative. Valentina was among the earliest daughters to confront her mother, criticizing the decision and demanding that important changes to the household be discussed with everyone affected.[7]
Silvia quickly joined the confrontation and rejected Violet's justification that she had managed Victor's affairs since before the daughters were born. Rosemary naturally appeared alongside them, and their constant association was recognized through the familiar description of the trio of chaos. She openly criticized the Empress for irritating everyone through an unnecessarily provocative decision and argued that Violet should have consulted the family beforehand.
Violet attempted to dismiss the younger generation by emphasizing her seniority and their comparatively youthful appearances. Silvia protested that she was no longer young, while Rosemary expressed frustration with the slow physical maturation of [[True Dragons]]. Her complaint reflected the considerable chronological experience she had accumulated despite maintaining a small Humanoid body.
The disagreement eventually attracted approximately forty daughters and numerous mothers, transforming the private dispute into a chaotic gathering. Valentina escalated the argument by jokingly proposing that Violet be replaced by Anna as Empress. Rosemary remained among the daughters expressing their displeasure, demonstrating that even enormous amounts of Time had not diminished the confrontational and mischievous characteristics of the three sisters.[7]
Rosemary later spent time in the manga section of the Imperial castle's library alongside [[Priscillia Penny Elderblood]] and [[Sylvia Elderblood]], the daughter of Victor and Siena. The girls occupied a comfortable reading area surrounded by books and manga, providing a place where they could relax away from their training and other responsibilities. Their conversation turned toward the enormous number of technological projects being developed throughout the Empire, particularly those involving their mother Pepper.[5]
Priscillia excitedly announced that Pepper was participating in an important Imperial project and declared that she intended to become involved as well. Rosemary pointed out that she had already read the relevant Imperial updates, while Sylvia complained that the database changed so frequently that keeping track of every announcement was exhausting. Rosemary dismissed the complaint because their Draconic minds could process extensive information with extraordinary speed, making ordinary reading requirements trivial compared with the capabilities of their race.
The discussion revealed that the Imperial Family possessed access to highly classified information concerning the Empire's administration, military and research. Victor deliberately allowed his daughters to learn about many sensitive matters because he wanted them to understand the civilization they would eventually help govern. However, projects whose effectiveness depended upon secrecy remained restricted, including exceptionally important strategic undertakings such as Polaris. Pepper's involvement in an unidentified project consequently attracted Priscillia's curiosity because its details could not be accessed through the ordinary Imperial database.
Rosemary displayed considerably less enthusiasm for pursuing the project. When Sylvia questioned why she had not followed Ruby into scientific research, Rosemary explained that she simply did not enjoy spending her time in laboratories. Her lack of interest did not reflect inadequate intelligence; naturally born Dragon Goddesses possessed extraordinary mental capabilities and received extensive education from their parents. Rosemary simply preferred reading manga, watching anime and movies, training and pursuing activities that suited her own interests.[5]
Her long-term ambition was to eventually command one of the Empire's Legions. She regarded military leadership as a more desirable way to contribute to the Empire, allowing sisters with a genuine enthusiasm for science to handle the complicated technological research necessary for its development.
Priscillia nevertheless remained determined to discover the nature of Pepper's classified project. Rosemary and Sylvia attempted to discourage her, recognizing that she did not even know what the research involved or whether she possessed the qualifications necessary to participate. Their objections failed, and Priscillia became unusually quiet as she concentrated upon developing a plan.
Rosemary and Sylvia became increasingly concerned because prolonged silence was unusual for their highly energetic sister. They understood that her exceptional Draconic intelligence made her capable of developing highly sophisticated schemes whenever she concentrated seriously upon an objective. After Priscillia finally announced that she had an idea and abruptly left the library, the two girls concluded that something troublesome was inevitable.
Although they initially considered remaining behind, Rosemary and Sylvia ultimately decided to follow her. Both understood that Priscillia was preparing to do something reckless, but neither was willing to abandon a sister after becoming involved in the situation. Their decision reflected the close family solidarity cultivated among Victor's daughters.[5]
Priscillia infiltrated the protected territory surrounding Pepper's private residence by constructing hundreds of Draconic Runes capable of circumventing the security barriers. Victor observed her actions almost immediately but chose not to intervene, finding the girl's ingenuity entertaining. Rosemary and Sylvia followed the trail and arrived after Priscillia had already entered the restricted area, startling her and nearly exposing their presence.
The three girls were aware that entering the private quarters belonging to their mothers without permission was expressly forbidden. The restrictions existed primarily to preserve privacy within a household whose inhabitants possessed extraordinary supernatural senses and the ability to bypass ordinary physical security. Depending upon the circumstances, unauthorized intrusion could result in anything from a reprimand to an entire year of disciplinary training beneath [[Scathach]], a punishment the younger generation regarded with considerable apprehension.
Priscillia used her Runic abilities to provide disguises for Rosemary and Sylvia before directing them to search for information concerning Pepper's project. Although all three possessed access to personal dimensional inventories containing their belongings, Priscillia preferred employing supernatural techniques even for comparatively trivial tasks.[8]
The sisters discovered that Pepper's private residence had been expanded through sophisticated spatial engineering into a vast interior environment containing approximately 2.8 million square meters of space. Rather than resembling an ordinary bedroom, the area contained its own ecosystem, forests, waterfalls and waterways, providing the comfort and privacy of an enormous estate within the Imperial castle.
Their exploration eventually led them into a concealed laboratory protected through additional dimensional barriers. Inside, they encountered an enormous white sphere surrounded by scientific equipment and thousands of Runes. Rosemary immediately became curious and began examining the laboratory's technological systems.
The sisters discovered that Pepper was developing an artificial star through a combination of advanced technology, Runic engineering and Divine assistance. Her research records described the process of stabilizing the stellar object through hundreds of Runes and supplying it with additional Energy. The ultimate objective was to create a system possessing an output comparable to approximately a billion exploding suns, although this represented the intended result rather than the power already achieved by the unfinished experiment.[8]
Rosemary quickly recognized the sophistication of the protective measures surrounding the laboratory. The artificial star was contained within a heavily shielded dimension designed to absorb the Energy of a catastrophic failure. An additional dimensional layer existed beyond it, providing further protection to prevent any escaping Energy from threatening the surrounding planet. Her assessment demonstrated an understanding of advanced dimensional containment despite her comparatively limited personal interest in scientific research.
She also began examining the technological documentation maintained within Pepper's systems. Rosemary used her extraordinary Draconic intelligence to process unfamiliar material at enormous speed, consulting the Imperial network whenever she encountered terminology requiring further explanation. Within a short period, she acquired a general understanding of research that ordinary specialists might require considerable time to comprehend.
Nevertheless, the volume of information eventually exceeded what she could casually absorb. Pepper had accumulated hundreds of millions of research records and technical observations, reflecting a project developed through extensive effort rather than an experiment that could be completely understood after a few minutes of reading. Rosemary recognized that her mother's sister possessed far greater scientific expertise than her playful public personality suggested.
The discovery also caused the girls to reconsider the technological capabilities of the Empire's principal researchers. If Pepper, whose public responsibilities revolved around entertainment and culture, could casually conduct such extraordinary stellar experiments, the projects being developed by Ruby, Velnorah and Aline were likely to be even more complicated and dangerous.
The sisters continued exploring and discovered another concealed laboratory known as the Room for Fun Projects. Unlike the artificial-star facility, this chamber contained experimental devices inspired by fictional media, including unusual armor, sophisticated weapons and technological objects resembling equipment from games, anime and films.[8]
Priscillia immediately became fascinated by a collection of glowing plasma swords that resembled lightsabers. Rosemary examined their documentation and explained that the weapons used plasma powered by the Energy of the artificial stellar system they had discovered earlier. Priscillia dismissed the technical distinction and insisted that any glowing plasma blade was sufficiently similar to a fictional lightsaber to deserve the same name.
A similar exchange occurred when Priscillia discovered an unusual wrist-mounted weapon resembling a hidden blade. Rosemary examined the device and identified its incorporation of Basilisk venom and a mechanism capable of producing petrification through the supernatural characteristics associated with the creature's eyes. Although she attempted to explain the differences between the experimental weapon and its fictional inspiration, Priscillia continued treating it as a hidden blade, eventually causing Rosemary to abandon the argument.
Their exploration continued until Sylvia discovered an enormous archive containing photographs and recordings from Victor's earlier life. Rosemary immediately became excited when she realized that the collection included material from the period following his arrival in [[Nightingale]]. The archive also contained recordings of his battles, visits to other supernatural territories and interactions with individuals who had known him before his ascension as God Emperor.
The discovery was particularly meaningful to Rosemary because she had been born long after many of those events. Although she knew her father's history through education and family stories, the opportunity to see authentic recordings of his earlier adventures allowed her to observe a version of Victor she had never encountered personally.[8]
The girls' enthusiasm was interrupted when [[Anna Elderblood]] discovered their unauthorized intrusion. Rosemary, Priscillia and Sylvia immediately realized that they might receive severe punishment, particularly because Anna possessed considerable authority over the younger generation and maintained the family's disciplinary standards.
Pepper intervened before the situation escalated and decided that the girls would receive a comparatively mild punishment because they had not seriously damaged anything. She assigned them approximately six months of additional lessons and study rather than sending them to Scathach for an entire year of corrective training. Rosemary and her sisters were immensely relieved, regarding the punishment as extraordinarily merciful compared with the alternative.
Rosemary continued participating in family activities following the incident. She later became involved in a prank targeting [[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]], one of the Empire's most talented young researchers. Together with Erika, Priscillia, Silvia, Siren and [[Lucrecia Lena Elderblood]], she helped intercept Velina and carry her away as part of a playful kidnapping carried out shortly before a traditional family gathering.[13][12]
The prank caused considerable confusion when Velina's absence became known to other members of the household. Several sisters began gathering to discuss the incident, while the extraordinary size of the Imperial Family meant that news spread quickly among daughters belonging to different maternal lineages.
The situation eventually reached Victor, who questioned the girls responsible for the disturbance. Rosemary and her companions reacted with embarrassment, recognizing that their father understood what had occurred. Velina demanded punishment, but Victor recognized that she possessed enough power to escape whenever she wished and had effectively allowed the prank to continue. He treated the event as ordinary sibling mischief rather than a genuine attack, although he reminded his daughters that family games must not involve deliberately harming one another.[12]
Rosemary subsequently joined the enormous gathering of Imperial Princesses and other family members preparing for the traditional meal. The banquet involved the preparation of an extraordinary creature known as a Star Animal, whose body was so enormous that ordinary planetary cooking facilities would have been completely inadequate.
When several sisters questioned the nature of the creature, Rosemary identified it as a Star Animal previously killed by the Empire's Leviathans. She was familiar with a report prepared by Scathach concerning the incident and casually referred to the available Imperial records while explaining its origin. Her familiarity with such extraordinary creatures demonstrated how thoroughly cosmic-scale phenomena had become normalized within the everyday life of Victor's family.[14]
Rosemary also participated in the educational gatherings Victor organized for his daughters, where he discussed the nature of supernatural power, Divine Concepts and the fundamental characteristics of Reality. These lessons were intended to provide the younger generation with a deeper understanding of the powers they had inherited and prevent them from confusing extraordinary natural talent with genuine mastery.
During one such gathering, the discussion turned toward [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]], Victor's youngest daughter with [[Tyche]]. Moira possessed an extraordinary relationship with Luck, giving her a level of supernatural good fortune that immediately attracted the attention of her sisters. Rosemary compared her youngest sister to a Mary Sue, recognizing the similarity between Moira's exceptional advantages and fictional protagonists who appear to receive extraordinarily favorable circumstances.[15]
The comparison reflected Rosemary's familiarity with storytelling and popular entertainment. She also acknowledged that extraordinary natural advantages were not unique to Moira, since Victor's biological daughters had all been born with exceptional potential and powers that ordinary supernatural beings could scarcely imagine.
Rosemary later became involved in an unusual experiment undertaken by several of her sisters. The project originated through [[Yog-Sothoth]] Elderblood and [[Nigredo Elderblood]], who had begun creating hybrid Eldritch creatures by combining the characteristics of Leviathans and other supernatural beings. Their creations possessed extraordinary adaptations, including the ability to travel through Space and survive conditions that would ordinarily destroy conventional life.[10]
The experiment attracted the attention of additional sisters, including Valentina, Leone, Hinata, Silvia, Rosemary and Stella. The group assembled near a river containing the developing Eldritch organisms and began considering how their supernatural characteristics could be further improved.
Some of the existing creatures had already acquired remarkable capabilities through the contributions of different sisters. Yog and Nigredo had developed organisms capable of surviving within extremely hostile environments, while Leone contributed exceptional adaptability. The group also possessed access to powerful Draconic scales belonging to Victor, whose supernatural characteristics provided an extraordinary foundation for further experimentation.
Velina discovered the gathering and immediately recognized the dangers associated with combining such powerful materials. The involvement of Eldritch beings, Leviathans and Victor's Draconic Essence created the possibility of producing a creature whose nature exceeded the expectations of ordinary supernatural biology. Although Velina initially attempted to discourage the experiment, the other girls remained enthusiastic.[10]
Valentina suggested introducing Dragon Fire into the developing organism, while Silvia proposed the addition of Dragon Lightning. Rosemary asked whether Ice could also be incorporated, contributing another elemental possibility to the discussion. Hinata offered Solar power, while Stella proposed adding the influence of Truth. The sisters eventually agreed to combine portions of their supernatural powers and observe what kind of being would emerge.
Velina ultimately abandoned her objections and joined the experiment herself, deciding that the creature should possess intelligence rather than simply becoming an instinct-driven organism. The group proceeded with the combined influences of their powers, Victor's scale and the Eldritch materials already assembled.
Their activities attracted the attention of [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood, who became alarmed upon discovering that the daughters were conducting such an extraordinary experiment within the family residence. The girls immediately fled with their materials rather than remain to face her anger, while Victor observed the situation with amusement.
The experiment nevertheless continued developing. The water began reacting to the supernatural influences introduced by the sisters, eventually producing a sphere of plasma covered in numerous eyes. Victor recognized that the being emerging from the experiment represented a stable combination of characteristics originating from the native Cosmos and the foreign Eldritch framework associated with his own existence.[10]
The creature became [[Cthugha]], a newly formed Great Old One possessing the Concept of a Star alongside Eldritch characteristics. It was also recognized as the Progenitor of a new lineage of Fire Vampires, a supernatural contradiction because ordinary Vampire characteristics and the nature of Fire were traditionally associated with incompatible weaknesses and limitations.
Despite having only recently come into existence, Cthugha possessed enough power to destroy a Solar System, with the potential for further growth as it matured. Its creation demonstrated how the combined abilities of Victor's daughters could produce a fundamentally new supernatural existence rather than merely a conventional hybrid creature.
Cthugha belonged to the hierarchy of Eldritch beings beneath the greater [[Outer Gods]], possessing Divinity and therefore occupying the category of Great Old One rather than that of an ordinary servant without Divine characteristics. Its existence also provided Victor and Velina with valuable information concerning the possibility of creating stable beings whose characteristics would ordinarily be considered contradictory under the established Rules of Creation.[10]
When Victor summoned the daughters responsible, Rosemary and her sisters approached expecting punishment. Instead, he praised their creativity and instructed them to conduct similarly dangerous experiments farther away from the family residence in the future. Aphrodite was exasperated by his leniency, but Victor considered the outcome valuable because it revealed possibilities for combining different supernatural systems and creating new forms of existence.
Rosemary's participation represented one of her most significant contributions to supernatural experimentation. Although she personally preferred training and military pursuits over scientific research, she had nevertheless participated in the collective creation of a Great Old One through cooperation with her sisters. The resulting creature's powers belonged to Cthugha and arose from the combined experiment rather than from an independent ability allowing Rosemary to create such beings alone.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Rosemary's biological father and the most important individual in her life. She possesses an intense emotional attachment to him, characterized by affection, admiration, possessiveness and overwhelming pride in being his daughter. Her admiration extends beyond his supernatural abilities to his personality, particularly the confidence and authority he displays when confronting other rulers or dealing with individuals attempting to deceive him.[4]
Rosemary enjoys receiving Victor's affection and reacts happily whenever he strokes her head or spends time with her. During her introduction to Evie, she proudly emphasizes that her connection to Victor places her and her sisters beyond ordinary Goddesses. She is equally fascinated by his ability to dominate political negotiations, regarding such interactions as events worth preserving through recordings and photographs.
Her interest in Victor's history is especially pronounced. Rosemary wishes to observe the man her mothers knew before he became an extraordinarily powerful Emperor, making old photographs and recordings particularly valuable to her. She expresses interest in the collections maintained by Violet and Anna and later becomes excited upon discovering Pepper's archive of material from Victor's early life in [[Nightingale]].[4][8]
Victor's approach to fatherhood has also influenced Rosemary's personal development. Although he possesses the power to protect his daughters from most threats, he deliberately refuses to raise them as individuals dependent entirely upon inherited strength. His War Games place Rosemary in unfamiliar circumstances where her ordinary Draconic advantages are restricted, forcing her to acquire the judgment and adaptability necessary to confront enemies on more equal terms.[9]
Rosemary's ambition to become an Imperial Legion Commander reflects this upbringing. She wishes to contribute directly to the Empire rather than remain a protected Princess whose only achievement is possessing extraordinary natural powers. Victor's willingness to provide extensive training and allow his daughters to develop their own interests supports her desire to pursue a military future distinct from Ruby's scientific career.
Despite recognizing the dangers associated with his daughters' enormous abilities, Victor remains affectionate and indulgent toward them. After Rosemary participates in the creation of [[Cthugha]], he praises the group's ingenuity rather than simply punishing them, while reminding them to conduct dangerous experiments away from the family residence. His response reflects the confidence he places in their potential and the importance he assigns to encouraging creativity alongside responsibility.[10]

#### Ruby Elderblood Scarlett
[[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] is Rosemary's biological mother and the principal source of her maternal characteristics. Rosemary possesses Ruby's exceptionally crimson-red hair and strongly resembles her in facial appearance, making their relationship immediately recognizable. Through Ruby, she also inherits the ancestral lineage of Clan Scarlett and its connection to [[Scathach]]'s martial traditions.[4]
Despite their striking resemblance, Rosemary and Ruby possess different personal ambitions. Ruby developed into one of the Empire's most accomplished scientific researchers, participating in technological development and extraordinary projects alongside Velnorah and Aline. Rosemary possesses the intellectual potential necessary to understand such work but does not share her mother's enthusiasm for spending extended periods conducting experiments.[5]
Rather than treating her differences from Ruby as a deficiency, Rosemary is comfortable pursuing her own interests. She enjoys training, entertainment and military activities, with the long-term objective of becoming an Imperial Legion Commander. Her ability to understand Pepper's artificial-star research demonstrates that she is not incapable of scientific work; she simply prefers a different occupation.[8]
Ruby's relationship with [[Nero]] also contributes to Rosemary's family connections. Because Nero considers Ruby her mother, she treats Rosemary as an especially precious younger sister and spoils her more extensively than many of the other daughters. The relationship reinforces Rosemary's connection to the wider Scarlett family while allowing her to receive affection from siblings who entered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household before her birth.[6]

#### Scathach Elderblood Scarlett
[[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] is Rosemary's maternal grandmother and one of the most important martial figures in the [[Elderblood Empire]]. As an exceptionally experienced warrior and instructor, [[Scathach]] represents the martial traditions of the Scarlett lineage and provides training for members of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family who wish to develop their combat abilities.
Rosemary is familiar with Scathach's reputation as a demanding instructor and understands the severity of her disciplinary methods. When she and her sisters are discovered inside Pepper's restricted residence, the possibility of undergoing a year of corrective training beneath Scathach causes considerable alarm. Rosemary regards Pepper's alternative punishment of six months of additional lessons as an enormous relief, illustrating how formidable Scathach's training has become in the eyes of the younger generation.[5][8]
Rosemary also pays attention to the reports prepared by her grandmother concerning extraordinary creatures and military matters. She identifies the enormous Star Animal being prepared for the Imperial banquet through information preserved in Scathach's records, demonstrating familiarity with the research and reports maintained by the Empire's military leadership.[14]
Her ambition to command an Imperial Legion makes Scathach's martial example particularly relevant, although no confirmed appointment establishes Rosemary as a permanent Legion Commander or as the holder of any specific senior military office.

#### Valentina Victoria Elderblood
[[Valentina Victoria Elderblood]] is one of Rosemary's closest sisters and an inseparable companion throughout many of her appearances. As the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Violet, Valentina shares Rosemary's position within the naturally born second generation while possessing a different maternal lineage. Their relationship closely resembles the companionship between Ruby and Violet, with both girls frequently appearing alongside Silvia.[4]
Rosemary and Valentina communicate telepathically and openly discuss the circumstances surrounding their father, including the possibility of additional women joining the family. Valentina is generally more suspicious and possessive toward such possibilities, while Rosemary sometimes approaches them through curiosity and practical considerations. Their differing perspectives contribute to the lively conversations that characterize the trio.
Their closeness continues throughout the later Imperial era. When Violet makes a controversial family decision, Rosemary and Valentina join Silvia in criticizing her behavior, demonstrating their shared willingness to challenge senior family members when they believe an important principle has been disregarded.[7]
The two also cooperate during the Eldritch experiment that produces [[Cthugha]]. Valentina proposes introducing Dragon Fire into the developing creature, while Rosemary raises the possibility of incorporating Ice. Their participation reflects both their enduring companionship and their willingness to investigate unfamiliar supernatural applications alongside other sisters.[10]

#### Silvia Elderblood
[[Silvia Elderblood]] is Rosemary's other closest sister and the third member of their longstanding trio. As the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Sasha, Silvia possesses the hereditary Lightning characteristics of the [[Fulger Clan]] alongside her Blood Dragon physiology.
Rosemary and Silvia complement one another through their different approaches to problems. Rosemary often raises questions or proposes imaginative solutions, while Silvia tends to examine the practical consequences of those suggestions. During the meeting with Evie, Silvia explains why conquering a civilization and preserving its competent inhabitants can be more useful than simply destroying everything, causing Rosemary to reconsider her initial position.[4]
The two also possess different entertainment preferences. Rosemary enjoys anime and frequently recognizes fictional references used by her older sisters during combat training, while Silvia prefers cartoons such as SpongeBob and reading books. Despite occasionally disagreeing over entertainment, both remain affectionate companions who participate in many of the same activities.[6]
Their closeness remains evident throughout the later Imperial era, particularly during the confrontation with Violet and the experiment that produces [[Cthugha]]. Together with Valentina, they are recognized as the trio of chaos because of their persistent companionship and tendency to become involved in troublesome situations.[7][10]

#### Nero Elderblood
[[Nero Elderblood]] is one of Rosemary's older adoptive sisters and occupies an especially important position in her upbringing. When observing Nero sparring with Ophis, Rosemary recognizes the difference between the practical knowledge possessed by her older sisters and the extraordinary natural potential of younger Dragon Goddesses.
Nero's familiarity with Imperial responsibilities also makes her a potential source of guidance. Rosemary accompanies Valentina, Silvia and Stella when the younger generation seeks information concerning administrative opportunities within the Empire, demonstrating her willingness to learn from an older sister whose experience extends beyond ordinary combat training.[6]

#### Ophis Tepes Elderblood
[[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]] is another of Rosemary's older adoptive sisters and shares an exceptionally affectionate relationship with her. Because Ophis spent much of her time beside [[Nero]], she gradually adopted Nero's indulgent treatment of Rosemary and became equally willing to spoil her younger sister.[6]
Their closeness becomes especially apparent during a sparring session between Ophis and Nero. The relationship combines admiration for an extraordinarily powerful older sister with the warmth of someone who participated directly in her upbringing.

#### Sylvia Elderblood
[[Sylvia Elderblood]] is another of Rosemary's Scarlett-lineage sisters and the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and [[Siena Scarlett Elderblood]]. She should not be confused with [[Silvia Elderblood]], the daughter of Sasha, despite the similarity between their names.

#### Priscillia Penny Elderblood
[[Priscillia Penny Elderblood]] is Rosemary's sister through their common father, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], and her cousin through the original Scarlett family. Priscillia's biological mother, Pepper, is Ruby's sister, creating an unusually close maternal relationship between the two daughters.

#### Pepper Scarlett Elderblood

#### Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III

Her inherited abilities include the physical foundation of a True Dragon, Divine characteristics, advanced Draconic senses and the exceptional intellectual potential shared by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s biological descendants. Her individually demonstrated capabilities include levitation, telepathic communication, supernatural Energy perception, rapid technical comprehension and participation in highly advanced collective supernatural experiments.

### Military Ambition
Rosemary's desired future is centered upon the military structure of the [[Elderblood Empire]] rather than the scientific institutions in which Ruby has achieved extraordinary prominence. Although Rosemary possesses the intelligence necessary to understand advanced technology and research, she finds considerably greater enjoyment in training, combat-related activities and entertainment.[5]
Her stated ambition is to eventually command one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Imperial Legions, allowing her to participate directly in the protection and development of the civilization established by her family. She does not regard scientific research as inherently unimportant, but prefers leaving that work to sisters who possess a genuine enthusiasm for it while pursuing a role better suited to her own interests.
Rosemary's martial ambitions are supported by the extraordinary biological potential of her Blood Dragon physiology and the extensive training resources available to the Imperial Family. However, she also recognizes the importance of experience and technical mastery, particularly when observing [[Nero]] and Ophis, whose practical combat abilities distinguish them from younger daughters possessing similarly exceptional natural potential.[6]
Victor's War Games provide an important foundation for her development. By temporarily transforming Rosemary into a Fairy Queen and restricting her ordinary Draconic abilities, he forces her to confront situations in which power alone cannot guarantee success. The exercise encourages her to consider leadership, military organization, resources, unfamiliar [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] and the consequences of decisions affecting subordinates.[9]
Although Rosemary has expressed an ambition to lead one of the Empire's Legions, no confirmed appointment establishes that she currently possesses permanent command over a specific military formation. Her ambition therefore remains a personal objective rather than an already demonstrated military office.
