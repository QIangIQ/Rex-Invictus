Morgana Elderblood, formerly known as Anna, is an ancient Demon-born warrior, former General of [[Lilith Elderblood]], former Fifth Queen of [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]], and one of the wives of [[Victor Elderblood]]. Originally a High-Level Succubus who earned the title The Reaper through thousands of years of warfare in Hell, Morgana subsequently became a Noble Vampire, the first known Demonic Vampire subspecies, and eventually a genuine Blood Dragon Goddess whose two principal Domains are Dreams and Radiation.[1][2][3]
Long before becoming associated with [[Nightingale]], Morgana had already established herself as one of Hell's most formidable military figures. Born as an insignificant Succubus with little power or protection, she survived the predatory environment surrounding Lilith's territory through repeated battles, consumption of stronger Demons and relentless self-improvement. Her achievements eventually attracted Lilith's attention, allowing her to rise into the ranks of the Mother of Demons' Generals. At the height of her Demonic career, she was powerful enough to contend with [[Baal]] and defeated five Demon Dukes simultaneously, an achievement that made the name The Reaper infamous among Hell's elite.[4][1]
Morgana eventually encountered Vlad Tepes, a Vampire Progenitor who offered her a life beyond Hell. Their relationship led to her departure from Lilith's army, her transformation into a Noble Vampire, and her position as Nightingale's Fifth Queen. Although the new body awakened the extraordinarily destructive Bloodline of Radiation, it also reduced the efficiency of the Dream powers she had spent millennia mastering. Her marriage produced two daughters, [[Lilith Tepes]] and [[Elizabeth Tepes]], the latter being an extraordinary life created through Morgana's Dream abilities and the combined Essence of herself and Vlad.[5][1]
Her attachment to her daughters ultimately became stronger than the loyalty she owed either Lilith or Vlad. When her marriage deteriorated, Morgana abandoned the name Anna and accepted a new identity given by Victor. Her subsequent relationship with the Second Vampire Progenitor restored the independence, ambition and martial pride that had become buried beneath centuries of marital frustration. Victor's Progenitor Blood later transformed her mixed Demonic and Vampiric constitution into an entirely new Vampire subspecies, while her evolution into a True Dragon finally reunited the Dream and Radiation powers that had defined separate stages of her life.[6][7][2][3]

### Physical Description
Morgana is an exceptionally beautiful woman with long black hair, milky-white skin and a mature, voluptuous figure. Her original appearance possessed the seductive characteristics of a High-Level Succubus, including violet eyes, Demonic horns, a long tail and large bat-like wings. These features could become considerably more pronounced when she released her Demonic power, altering the shape and coloration of her body as her supernatural strength increased.[8][9]
Following her transformation into a Noble Vampire, Morgana acquired a complete mortal body while retaining the spiritual foundation of a Demon. Her eyes became Vampiric red, but the Demonic characteristics remained accessible through her transformations. In her enhanced Count state, her skin could become deep red, her eyes change to golden hues, and her horns, wings and tail become larger and more threatening. Her limbs developed formidable Demonic characteristics, creating a monstrous appearance that reflected the power of the former General rather than an ordinary Vampire Countess.[8][9][1]
Her appearance underwent another change after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] incorporated her into his Progenitor Bloodline. Her horns became darker, her tail lengthened, and her Demonic features became more prominent without losing the elegance of her humanoid form. Her eyes developed permanently blood-red pupils surrounded by black sclera, while the supernatural attraction inherited from her Succubus nature was amplified. Victor observed that the combination of her original racial characteristics, Vampiric evolution and other influences had made her attractiveness considerably more dangerous than before.[7]
After becoming a True Dragon, Morgana's appearance acquired unmistakably Draconic characteristics. Her horns and wings developed scale-like structures, and her red eyes became reptilian. Unlike her previous hybrid-like appearance, these changes represented a genuine reconstruction of her existence as a Dragon whose Soul and body naturally incorporated the Dreams and Radiation she had previously wielded through separate racial foundations.[3]

### Personality
Morgana is a direct, ruthless and fiercely independent woman whose personality was shaped by surviving Hell for thousands of years. Her earliest experiences taught her that weakness invited exploitation, and she became accustomed to a world where hesitation could cost an individual their life. She developed into someone who respects strength, competence and decisiveness, but her respect is not based solely on overwhelming power. Morgana values individuals who accept responsibility for their own decisions and refuses to remain blindly obedient to a ruler merely because that ruler occupies a position of authority. Her original loyalty to Lilith was founded upon the opportunities the Mother of Demons had given her, while her eventual resentment toward Vlad developed from his failure to reconcile the responsibilities of a King with those of a husband and father.[4][1][10]
Her temperament is openly emotional and frequently vulgar. Morgana swears without hesitation, insults people who irritate her and often responds to complicated situations with blunt remarks rather than elaborate diplomacy. She possesses a mischievous, flirtatious side inherited from her Succubus nature and enjoys provoking the people around her, particularly Jeanne and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Nevertheless, this playfulness does not undermine her military discipline. Morgana spent centuries commanding Demons, surviving hostile territory and fighting powerful enemies, making her capable of abandoning frivolous behavior immediately when circumstances demand serious action. During the Greek campaign, [[Scathach]] specifically entrusted her with protecting and scouting for the assault group because Morgana possessed exceptional experience in large-scale warfare.[11]
Morgana's defining emotional attachment is her love for her children. She initially believed that her Demonic upbringing would allow her to raise a powerful daughter and surrender that child to Lilith in fulfillment of an old agreement. The birth of [[Lilith Tepes]] destroyed that assumption. Morgana discovered that her feelings as a mother were stronger than her willingness to obey the ruthless traditions of Hell, and she could not willingly sacrifice the daughter she had carried and raised. Her later decision to create Elizabeth through Dream power was motivated partly by the hope that an artificially created child would be easier to surrender, yet Elizabeth's individuality immediately awakened the same maternal attachment. Morgana refused to abandon either daughter, demonstrating that the affection she discovered through motherhood had become more important than the mentality she had cultivated as a Demonic General.[5][12]
This attachment also governs her loyalty to those she considers family. Despite the complicated relationship she maintained with [[Lilith Elderblood]], Morgana continued regarding the Mother of Demons as a maternal figure. Learning that [[Diablo]] had captured and possibly exploited Lilith enraged her because the woman she once served remained emotionally important even after their political relationship had ended. Her reaction to Vlad's refusal to intervene immediately in the crisis surrounding their daughter was even more severe. Morgana could tolerate enemies, hardship and danger, but the deliberate abandonment of family responsibilities was something she could no longer excuse.[1][10]
Victor attracted her because his priorities contradicted the behavior she had come to despise in Vlad. Where the Vampire King repeatedly weighed personal matters against political consequences, Victor was willing to confront established powers and disrupt entire factions to protect those he acknowledged as his own. He also encouraged Morgana to rediscover the person she had been before her transformation into Vlad's Queen, returning to her the sense of identity and ambition she had lost. Her affection for Victor consequently developed through more than physical attraction: he represented the possibility of belonging to a family without surrendering the independence that had allowed her to survive Hell.[6][4][13]
Morgana's pride is inseparable from her martial nature. Although she can wield weapons, she prefers fighting directly through her claws and fists, employing the close-combat mentality she developed as a Demon. She does not regard overwhelming destructive power as a substitute for training and willingly participates in Victor's increasingly demanding martial exercises even after becoming a Dragon Goddess. Her blunt criticism of the [[Seven Arts of the Blood Dragon Primogenitor]] also demonstrates that she is willing to challenge Victor's ideas rather than flatter him; ironically, her observations concerning the pressure his techniques placed upon the body and Soul helped him identify ways to improve the system.[14][15]

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Morgana entered the story during the crisis in [[Nightingale]]'s Royal Capital, when the long-dormant former Queens of [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] began awakening and questioning their relationship with the Vampire King. Her appearance accompanied that of [[Jeanne Elderblood]], another woman whose past had been tied to Vlad for centuries. Their awakening occurred amid increasing instability within the Royal Family, exposing tensions that had existed long before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became involved in Vampire politics.[16]
Unlike the Noble Vampires born within Nightingale, Morgana had originated in Hell. She had once possessed a Demonic name that she refused to reveal and had later received the name Anna from Vlad. Her first encounters with Jeanne following their awakening emphasized how unfamiliar the changed supernatural world had become. Both women had spent an extraordinary period asleep and were eager to experience an existence outside the restrictions imposed by their former husband. Morgana retained connections to Lilith, her original Demonic ruler, while Jeanne's spiritual nature remained connected to the [[Heavenly Father]] despite the Vampiric body she had received from Vlad.[5][17]
The circumstances of Morgana's former marriage were inseparable from an agreement she had made with Lilith before leaving Hell. Morgana had risen through the ranks of the Demonic army until becoming one of Lilith's Generals, but she eventually became fascinated by the possibility of living beyond the realm where she had spent thousands of years fighting for survival. Vlad offered her that opportunity, and Lilith agreed to release Morgana on the condition that she eventually provide a successor possessing exceptional potential. The agreement was originally intended to secure a replacement General for the position Morgana had abandoned.[5][12]
After marrying Vlad, Morgana became his Fifth Queen and received a Noble Vampire body. The transformation gave her a complete mortal existence but disrupted the efficiency of her old Dream abilities. Nevertheless, her new body awakened a previously dormant Bloodline associated with Radiation, granting her destructive powers radically different from the spiritual techniques she had used as a Succubus. Morgana subsequently bore her first daughter, [[Lilith Tepes]]. Although the child possessed the potential expected from the union of an ancient Demon and a Vampire Progenitor, Morgana discovered that she could not fulfill the agreement by surrendering her newborn daughter to [[Lilith Elderblood]].[5][1]
Attempting to find another solution, Morgana used the power of Dreams alongside Vlad's Progenitor Essence to create [[Elizabeth Tepes]]. Rather than producing a temporary illusion or a mindless construct, the process created a genuine life possessing independent thoughts and an identity of her own. Elizabeth's spiritual resemblance to Morgana was extraordinary, but her existence was not simply an obedient extension of her mother's consciousness. Morgana had hoped that creating a daughter through supernatural means would prevent her from forming the same emotional attachment; instead, she loved Elizabeth no less than Lilith and refused to surrender her as well.[5][12]
Following her awakening, Morgana's attention increasingly shifted toward the Second Vampire Progenitor. She and Jeanne approached Victor after his confrontation with [[Aphrodite]] and began interacting with his household. During these meetings, Victor grew irritated by the fact that Morgana used the name Anna, which was also his mother's name. Morgana refused to reveal her true Demonic name because such names possessed enormous spiritual significance to High-Level Demons and were ordinarily disclosed only to individuals receiving exceptional trust. Victor therefore selected an alternative name for her: Morgana. She accepted it, and the new identity gradually replaced the name Vlad had given her.[6]
The change also created an unexpected emotional response. Morgana was already attracted to Victor's strength and personality, but receiving a name from someone she had begun admiring made her more receptive to him. Her flirtatious response immediately provoked Violet, whose possessiveness toward her husband remained intense. Despite the resulting confrontation, Morgana continued remaining near Victor and became increasingly involved in his activities.[6]
Morgana and Jeanne subsequently proposed that Victor establish a faction capable of opposing Vlad and eventually removing him from power. Both women wanted greater independence from the Vampire King and were particularly concerned about recovering their children from his influence. Victor refused to begin a political rebellion merely because two former Queens desired it. He pointed out that neither had properly considered the wishes of their children, who were old enough to possess opinions of their own, and that overthrowing Vlad would create responsibilities Victor had no immediate interest in assuming. His refusal disappointed Morgana, but it also demonstrated that Victor would not casually exploit family relationships as excuses for political ambition.[18]
Victor later used his unusual abilities to reconnect Morgana with memories of her original Demonic existence. The experience returned her consciousness to the period when she had been a weak Succubus struggling to survive the environment surrounding Lilith's territory. Succubi possessed an instinctive awareness of the Mother of Demons' location, but the region surrounding Lilith was protected by exceptionally dangerous Demons. Countless weaker creatures attempting to reach her were destroyed before they could approach.[4]
Morgana and the companions accompanying her repeatedly attempted to overcome these obstacles. Their failures involved severed limbs, grievous wounds and near-death experiences, with a strange red river providing the means to recover between attempts. After 101 attempts, they finally defeated a High-Level Imp by using Morgana's Dream [[Magic]] to keep the creature asleep while the group killed it with their claws. They subsequently consumed the Imp, obtaining the strength necessary to continue advancing through Hell's hierarchy.[4]
The victory established a pattern that defined Morgana's early life. She hunted Demons stronger than herself, defeated them through patience, deception and increasingly refined Dream abilities, and consumed their remains to grow. Her appearance became more monstrous as her strength increased, while repeated exposure to violence gradually eliminated many of the weaknesses she had possessed as a newly born Succubus. Lilith eventually recognized Morgana's potential and elevated her into a High-Level Demon, beginning the military career that would later earn her the title The Reaper.[4]
Recovering these memories reminded Morgana of the determination and independence she had possessed before marrying Vlad. Her relationship with Victor deepened as she came to associate him with the recovery of her former identity rather than another authority attempting to restrict her freedom. She also became increasingly involved with [[Nero]] and the younger members of the household, demonstrating a maternal temperament considerably different from the ruthless reputation she carried in Hell.[19]
Morgana eventually encountered her daughters again and was forced to confront the influence their father's household had exerted upon them. During their meeting with Victor, both Lilith and Elizabeth were visibly overwhelmed by the difference between the people they remembered and the powerful individuals now surrounding them. Morgana responded by reprimanding her daughters for allowing themselves to become paralyzed by fear, insisting that children born from an ancient Demonic General and the Vampire King should possess greater composure. Although the response appeared harsh, it reflected her belief that surviving the supernatural world required courage and the ability to remain functional before overwhelming power.[20]

#### Demonic War
The Demonic invasion of [[Nightingale]] forced Morgana to confront the civilization she had abandoned. As [[Diablo]]'s armies attacked the Vampire territories, Morgana and Jeanne fought in the Royal Capital against high-ranking Demons who initially regarded the former Queens as targets to be eliminated. Morgana's previous position within Lilith's army gave her extensive knowledge of Hell's military hierarchy and the nature of powerful Demonic opponents, making her one of the most experienced defenders participating in the conflict.[8][9]
During the battle, Morgana confronted [[Dantalion]], a Demon Duke whose abilities and confidence were initially sufficient to threaten the defenders. Morgana activated a mutated Vampire Count Transformation incorporating the Demonic characteristics preserved within her Soul. Unlike an ordinary Noble Vampire transformation, the process caused her body to become progressively more monstrous, developing larger horns, a longer tail, altered skin coloration and increasingly pronounced Demonic structures.[8][9]
Her old Dream [[Magic]] proved especially dangerous to Dantalion. Morgana could manipulate a target's perception through dreams and nightmares, imposing illusions powerful enough to affect the victim both mentally and physically. She demonstrated that the difference between a dream and an attack upon Reality could become dangerously blurred when sufficiently advanced supernatural power was involved. Nevertheless, Morgana acknowledged that her Dream techniques remained less efficient than they had been during her existence as a pure Demon, since her transformation into a Vampire had fundamentally changed the way she interacted with the power.[9][1]
Morgana pushed her Count Transformation further, releasing an even greater portion of the Demonic nature encoded within her existence. Dantalion's confidence began collapsing once he realized the strength of the opponent confronting him, but he attempted to destabilize Morgana by revealing that Lilith had become a subordinate of Diablo. His words instead provoked Morgana's fury. She understood that Lilith would never willingly accept such humiliation without extraordinary circumstances and became increasingly suspicious that Diablo had captured, manipulated or exploited the Mother of Demons.[1]
The confrontation awakened Morgana's other great supernatural power. Rather than continue fighting primarily through Dreams, she unleashed the Radiation Bloodline acquired after becoming a Vampire. Crimson-orange Energy enveloped her body, and the surrounding Demons and structures were annihilated by the extraordinary destructive power she released. Radiation had remained dormant and accumulated within her body for an enormous period before its nature was properly understood. Even Vlad had difficulty classifying the Bloodline, eventually recognizing it as an unusual form of pure Radiation possessing nuclear destructive characteristics.[1]
Morgana's awakened Radiation allowed her to overwhelm Dantalion's remaining forces. She projected destructive Energy through her eyes and used the heat and destructive pressure surrounding her body to incapacitate the Duke. When Dantalion finally recognized her identity, he remembered the reputation she had earned in Hell: The Reaper, the former General who had fought [[Baal]] directly and defeated five Demon Dukes without assistance. The encounter demonstrated that Morgana's transformation into a Vampire had not erased the warrior who once terrified the elite of Hell.[1]
The information recovered from the battle eventually convinced Morgana that Lilith had been captured and manipulated by Diablo. Her concern was not merely political. Despite recognizing Lilith's cruelty and the danger of trusting powerful Demons, Morgana continued regarding her former ruler as a maternal figure. The possibility that Diablo was exploiting Lilith enraged her, especially because Lilith's position as the Progenitor of Demons made her valuable to those attempting to control Hell's population.[1]
The war also endangered Morgana's own daughter. When Morgana confronted Vlad over the crisis, she expected him to act decisively as a father and husband. Vlad instead evaluated the consequences of intervention for Nightingale and the supernatural balance, prompting Morgana to accuse him of placing his Kingdom above his family once again. Centuries of accumulated resentment erupted as she openly condemned his behavior and abandoned whatever hope she still retained that their relationship could be restored.[21][10]
Jeanne understood Morgana's anger because she had experienced similar disappointment with Vlad. Nevertheless, Morgana's reaction was particularly severe because she had once genuinely loved the man and believed leaving Hell with him would produce a life worth protecting. She admitted that she regretted becoming involved with him, but also recognized that she would never erase the past entirely because their relationship had given her Lilith and Elizabeth.[10]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] responded to Morgana's distress differently. Rather than accept the limitations imposed by Vlad's political calculations, he removed Morgana from the immediate confrontation and made clear that the safety of the people connected to him would not be subordinated to Nightingale's existing arrangements. His willingness to challenge powerful factions without waiting for royal approval became one of the decisive reasons Morgana's loyalty shifted toward Victor.[13]
As the conflict continued, Morgana became increasingly integrated into Victor's personal faction. The rescue operations involving hostages and the confrontation with Diablo's forces further demonstrated Victor's willingness to protect his allies even when doing so created political consequences. Morgana's relationship with him consequently evolved beyond admiration for his power and became an attachment based upon trust, gratitude and a shared understanding of the importance of family.[22]
Morgana underwent another major transformation when Victor began incorporating his wives and companions into his Progenitor Bloodline. Her existence presented an unusual problem because she had originally been a Demon who subsequently received a Noble Vampire body. Although the Vampiric transformation had made her a more complete mortal Being, remnants of her previous Demonic nature remained encoded within the Core of her Soul.[7][2]
Victor's Bloodline resolved this distinction by causing Morgana's Noble Vampire side to absorb the Demonic characteristics completely. Rather than producing an unstable Hybrid or erasing the abilities she possessed as a Demon, the process incorporated the Demonic Traits directly into her Vampiric foundation. Her Soul Core became unified, allowing her to retain the characteristics of both histories without the former incompatibility that had limited her powers.[2]
[[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] initially questioned whether Morgana had become an entirely new Race. Victor and the others clarified that the more accurate classification was a Noble Vampire subspecies. Unlike a completely new Progenitor-created Race, Morgana remained fundamentally a Noble Vampire whose lineage now possessed complete Demonic characteristics. This new existence was named the Demonic Vampire, making Morgana the first known representative of the subspecies.[2]
The distinction was particularly important because Morgana was not a balanced Hybrid in the same sense as beings such as [[Viviane]]. Her Vampiric nature had subsumed the Demonic nature rather than maintaining two separate racial foundations in equilibrium. As a result, her original Succubus abilities remained part of her existence while benefiting from the complete Soul and developmental potential provided by Victor's Blood.[2]

#### King Among Kings
Morgana's military experience remained valuable after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s conquest of Hell and the formation of his expanding faction. Although she no longer served Lilith's Demonic army, Morgana retained intimate knowledge of the realm's military hierarchy, dangerous territories and the behavior of ancient Demons. Her familiarity with Hell allowed her to understand threats and circumstances that were unfamiliar to younger members of Victor's household.
During this period, Victor addressed the unresolved agreement between Morgana and [[Lilith Elderblood]]. Morgana had once promised to provide a replacement General possessing exceptional potential in exchange for her freedom. Although she had initially intended to fulfill the arrangement through [[Lilith Tepes]] and later [[Elizabeth Tepes]], her feelings as a mother had prevented her from surrendering either daughter.[12]
Victor encouraged Morgana to speak with Lilith directly about the agreement rather than allowing it to remain a source of anxiety indefinitely. Lilith no longer commanded Hell's armies as she had during Morgana's early life, and her political position had changed drastically beneath Victor's rule. Morgana agreed to approach Lilith and attempt to resolve the matter. The conversation established her intention to confront the old promise, although it did not itself confirm that the agreement had already been formally cancelled.[12]
The discussion also addressed Morgana's lingering resentment toward Vlad. Jeanne argued that continuing to express hatred gave their former husband unnecessary importance in their lives, while indifference would demonstrate that they had genuinely moved beyond him. Morgana initially rejected the idea because of the anger she still felt, but the conversation forced her to consider whether maintaining that resentment served any meaningful purpose.[12]
Morgana later participated in Victor's campaign involving the [[Greek Pantheon]]. Alongside Nyx Elderblood, [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood and [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], she entered the [[Greek Underworld]], wearing armor reminiscent of her former position as a Demonic General. The environment reminded her of the Hell she had once inhabited, although the organization and metaphysical structure of the Greek afterlife differed substantially from the Demonic realm.[23]
When the campaign escalated into open warfare, Victor personally strengthened Morgana's combat capabilities. Recognizing that her greatest weapons were Dreams and Radiation, he temporarily equipped her with the Demonic claws of Marbas, the former Fifth-Rank Pillar whose existence had become part of Victor's immortal army. The claws were formed over Morgana's hands through Victor's Blood and influenced her Demonic characteristics, sharpening her horns, tail and wings while enhancing the natural weaponry available to her.[24]
Victor instructed Morgana to use her Dream powers to confuse enemies, Radiation to overwhelm their defenses and the claws to finish them. The combination was designed around her established fighting style rather than forcing her to abandon the techniques she had spent thousands of years developing. Morgana responded with the discipline of a General receiving orders from a trusted ruler, accepting the role of The Reaper once again beneath Victor's command.[24]
During the ensuing battle, Morgana attacked a Greek God wearing ancient Spartan equipment. Her Radiation allowed her to project destructive beams while pursuing the target, and the claws supplied by Victor enabled her to engage the God directly at close range. When the opponent attempted to invoke conventional notions of honor, Morgana rejected the idea that warfare was governed by the etiquette of a duel. Her response reflected the survival mentality she had cultivated in Hell, where victory and survival were the only reliable guarantees.[24]
As the conflict continued, [[Scathach]] placed Morgana in the combined role of Guardian and Scout for the assault group. The decision was based upon Morgana's superior experience with large-scale war compared with the other women participating in the operation. Rather than treating the mission as an opportunity to pursue personal battles, Morgana accepted responsibility for observing the surroundings, identifying threats and helping ensure the survival of the group.[11]
The operation demonstrated how Morgana's identity had changed without erasing her past. She remained The Reaper, an experienced combatant capable of overwhelming enemies through brutal force, but her violence now served the protection of a family and faction she had consciously chosen rather than the ambitions of Lilith or Vlad.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Morgana completed her transformation into a genuine True Dragon during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s early development as the Blood Dragon Primogenitor. Unlike several women who underwent Dragonization during the later large-scale ritual, Morgana's transformation had already finished by the time Lilith encountered her in Chapter 828.[3]
Her new appearance immediately revealed the change. Morgana's eyes had become reptilian, while her horns and wings possessed visibly Draconic, scale-like characteristics. Lilith recognized that Morgana had undergone a complete racial transformation and questioned what type of Dragon she had become.[3]
Morgana explained that her new Domain was Radiation and Dreams. Both attributes had fused naturally into her True Dragon existence, becoming stronger and more effective than before. This evolution was particularly significant because the two abilities had originally developed through different stages of her life. Dreams belonged to her original Succubus nature, whereas Radiation had awakened only after Vlad transformed her into a Vampire. The Draconic transformation removed the separation between those foundations by making both attributes natural expressions of the same Dragon Soul.[3]
Lilith was especially surprised by the existence of a Dream Dragon because Dreams had traditionally been associated with spiritual creatures such as Succubi rather than the Dragon Race. Morgana herself could not completely explain why those attributes had become her personal Draconic Domain. Victor had informed her that a Dragon's awakened characteristics depended upon the individual's nature, personality and potential, and she suspected that her new powers reflected the abilities she had already cultivated.[3]
Her transformation also restored the efficiency of her Dream powers beyond the limitations she had endured as a Noble Vampire. The abilities that had once been weakened by her conversion into a mortal body now existed as part of a complete True Dragon foundation, supported by a much more powerful physiology and the exceptional supernatural compatibility of the Race.[3][25]
Morgana subsequently participated in the conflict involving the Elder Gods and the defense of [[WarFall]]. During the fighting, she coated her body in destructive Radiation and moved directly through concentrations of hostile Monsters, annihilating enemies through the Energy surrounding her while using beams projected from her eyes and attacks released through her hands.[26]
Her Radiation reached a scale far beyond conventional nuclear technology. Morgana compressed nuclear Energy into small green-and-yellow spheres and launched them against enemy concentrations, creating gigantic mushroom-cloud explosions. The resulting attacks devastated the battlefield and were described as possessing the greatest offensive power among the fighters present during that particular engagement. This comparison referred to the immediate group and circumstances rather than establishing Morgana as the strongest offensive combatant in the entire Elderblood faction.[26]
The nature of her Radiation also differed from ordinary Human nuclear weapons. Because the attacks used a purer supernatural form of nuclear Energy, their destructive effects did not leave the surrounding planet poisoned by conventional radioactive contamination. Morgana subsequently created approximately twenty additional spheres and launched another bombardment, demonstrating that the technique could be repeated rather than functioning as a single desperate attack.[26]
The battle established the extraordinary destructive potential of Morgana's Dragon Domain. Dreams provided unusual spiritual and psychological applications, while Radiation allowed her to annihilate enormous numbers of enemies through concentrated destructive Energy. Her two Domains complemented one another, making her dangerous both against opponents vulnerable to supernatural mental interference and against armies relying upon conventional durability.[3][26]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Following the accelerated periods of development experienced by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family, Morgana became a mother again. Alongside the children she had with Vlad, she eventually had an additional daughter with Victor, expanding her personal branch of the Elderblood dynasty. The daughter remains unnamed in the currently established material and belongs to the younger generation of [[Blood Dragons]] born within Victor's rapidly expanding family.[27]
Morgana continued refining her martial abilities rather than relying solely upon the overwhelming destructive potential of her Dragon physiology. When Victor demonstrated the [[Seven Arts of the Blood Dragon Primogenitor]], she joined [[Scathach]], Rose and Haruna in studying the new system.[14]
The Seven Arts differed fundamentally from ordinary martial techniques because they required the practitioner to control not merely their body and Energy, but also the surrounding Reality and the relationship between Energy and the Soul. Even as a Dragon Goddess, Morgana recognized that the system's requirements exceeded her current level of technical mastery. She acknowledged that she still needed much greater control over Reality Distortion and Energy before she could safely employ the complete Arts, while Scathach identified herself and Rose as the only women present who then satisfied the fundamental requirements.[14]
Morgana nevertheless contributed to the system's development through her observations. While examining Victor's demonstrations, she pointed out the unreasonable pressure his techniques placed upon the body and the enormous difference between his capabilities and those of other Dragons. Victor realized that the Arts needed further refinement to prevent practitioners from damaging themselves through the interaction between physical force, supernatural Energy and Soul defenses. Her criticism therefore helped identify an important flaw in the system rather than merely expressing disbelief at its power.[14]
Victor subsequently trained Morgana and Haruna personally. The two attacked him together, coordinating their movements and attempting to exploit openings created by their combined pressure. Victor defeated their approaches through superior technique and overwhelming control, explaining that coordination alone could not compensate for openings an opponent sufficiently skilled could recognize and exploit.[15]
The training clarified Morgana's preferred method of fighting. Although she possessed the knowledge necessary to wield weapons, she naturally favored claws and fists, employing her body as a close-combat weapon rather than relying upon a sword or spear. Her fighting style retained the aggression and directness cultivated during her Demonic career while benefiting from the physical advantages of her True Dragon physiology.[15]
Morgana continued participating in Victor's martial training alongside the other powerful women of his family. These exercises formed part of the broader effort to push their combat abilities beyond the limitations imposed by merely possessing enormous quantities of Energy or naturally powerful racial abilities.[28]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
During the Empire's later development, Morgana remained an established member of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s inner family and one of the ancient warriors whose perspective had been shaped by supernatural civilizations predating the [[Elderblood Empire]]. The enormous acceleration of Imperial development transformed the scale of ordinary life, warfare and technology, but Morgana's personal relationships and direct manner of interacting with the other wives remained recognizable.
Morgana appeared among the women gathering around Victor during the preparations involving the Imperial daughters and the unusual celestial creature introduced into the family's activities. When the others reacted with surprise to the increasingly absurd level of Divine and technological development surrounding them, Morgana treated the spectacle with relative composure, reflecting how accustomed the household had become to phenomena that would once have been regarded as impossible.[29]
Her close friendship with Jeanne likewise remained evident during the family's later gatherings. When Jeanne attempted to maintain a stern attitude toward Victor and his daughters before inevitably softening, Morgana teasingly encouraged her friend to remain firm. Their exchange demonstrated that the companionship established during their time as Vlad's wives had survived the changes in Race, allegiance, family and civilization that followed.[30]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Morgana's current husband, the origin of her Blood Dragon existence and the person most responsible for her development after separating from Vlad.
Their relationship began while Morgana was attempting to recover the independence she had lost during her marriage to the Vampire King. Victor's irreverent personality, rapidly increasing strength and refusal to submit automatically to existing supernatural authorities attracted her interest. His decision to give her the name Morgana was particularly meaningful because it allowed her to abandon the name associated with Vlad without needing to reveal the true Demonic name she considered deeply personal.[6]
Victor subsequently helped Morgana recover memories and aspects of her identity that had become buried beneath centuries of frustration. The experience reminded her of the young Succubus who had survived Hell through intelligence, determination and violence until becoming The Reaper. Rather than attempting to make Morgana dependent upon him, Victor encouraged the return of the pride and ambition she had possessed before entering [[Nightingale]]'s Royal Family.[4]
Their relationship deepened considerably during the Demonic War. Morgana was devastated when Vlad once again prioritized his Kingdom over intervening immediately to protect their family. Victor's willingness to disregard political convenience when the safety of his own people was involved established a contrast that Morgana could not ignore. His actions reinforced the belief that she could trust him to protect those he acknowledged as family.[10][13]
Victor later incorporated Morgana into his Progenitor Bloodline, transforming her into the first known Demonic Vampire subspecies. He also provided the foundation for her evolution into a genuine True Dragon, allowing the Dream and Radiation abilities that had developed separately throughout her life to become part of one complete Draconic existence.[7][2][3]
Morgana eventually had another daughter with Victor, strengthening their family relationship beyond the marriage itself. She also became one of the experienced warriors he trained personally while developing the [[Seven Arts of the Blood Dragon Primogenitor]].[27][14][15]

#### Vlad Tepes
[[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] is Morgana's former husband and the father of [[Lilith Tepes]] and [[Elizabeth Tepes]].
Morgana originally admired Vlad enough to abandon the world in which she had spent thousands of years fighting. Their relationship represented an opportunity to experience freedom beyond Hell, and Vlad's transformation gave her a genuine mortal body together with the advantages and limitations of a Noble Vampire.[5][1]
Their marriage gradually deteriorated because Morgana increasingly regarded Vlad as a ruler incapable of prioritizing the people closest to him. His concern for [[Nightingale]] and the supernatural balance repeatedly superseded his responsibilities as a husband and father, leaving Morgana emotionally isolated despite her position as a Queen.
After awakening from her prolonged sleep, Morgana celebrated the opportunity to escape Vlad's control and eventually abandoned the name Anna. Her final major confrontation with him occurred during the Demonic War, when she condemned his refusal to act immediately in the crisis surrounding their daughter. Although she admitted that the relationship had become a source of profound regret, she also recognized that she would not erase it completely because Lilith and Elizabeth were born from their union.[17][6][10]

#### Lilith Elderblood
[[Lilith Elderblood]] is the Progenitor of Demons, Morgana's former ruler, original master and one of the most important maternal figures in her life.
Morgana originally reached Lilith's territory as a weak Succubus attempting to survive Hell. The lethal obstacles surrounding the Mother of Demons forced Morgana to develop the skills that would eventually make her one of Hell's greatest Generals. Lilith recognized her potential and elevated her into a High-Level Demon, giving Morgana the opportunity to rise far above the condition into which she had been born.[4]
Their relationship nevertheless remained complicated. Lilith permitted Morgana to leave Hell only after receiving a promise that Morgana would eventually provide a replacement General possessing exceptional potential. Morgana's refusal to surrender either of her daughters left the agreement unresolved for a considerable period, creating anxiety even after she no longer served Lilith politically.[5][12]
Despite understanding Lilith's ruthless nature, Morgana retained genuine affection for her former ruler. Learning that [[Diablo]] had captured and exploited Lilith provoked her anger and concern because she continued regarding the Mother of Demons as someone resembling family.[1]
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became ruler of Hell, the balance of authority between Morgana and Lilith changed drastically. Victor encouraged Morgana to discuss the old agreement directly with Lilith rather than continue fearing that one of her children would eventually be claimed. Morgana agreed to pursue the conversation, although its formal resolution was not established in that exchange.[12]

#### Lilith Tepes
[[Lilith Tepes]] is Morgana's firstborn daughter with Vlad and the child whose birth first demonstrated that Morgana could no longer live according to the purely ruthless mentality of Hell.
Morgana had originally believed that she could surrender her daughter to [[Lilith Elderblood]] in fulfillment of the agreement allowing her to leave the Demonic army. However, the emotional attachment she developed after giving birth made such a decision impossible. Morgana chose to protect her daughter rather than treat her as an obligation or replacement military asset.[5]
Lilith inherited powerful characteristics from her parents, including a Vampiric foundation and unusual Demonic potential. Nevertheless, her development was affected by the circumstances of [[Nightingale]]'s Royal Family, and Morgana later criticized the degree to which Vlad's influence had limited the independence and growth of their children.[31][12]
The danger surrounding Lilith during the Demonic War became one of the principal reasons Morgana's relationship with Vlad collapsed. Her daughter's safety mattered more to Morgana than the political considerations Vlad continued prioritizing.[10]

#### Elizabeth Tepes
[[Elizabeth Tepes]] is Morgana's second daughter with Vlad and one of the most extraordinary demonstrations of Morgana's original Dream abilities.
Unlike Lilith, Elizabeth was not conceived through an ordinary biological process. Morgana used the powers of a High-Level Succubus to combine her own Essence with Vlad's Progenitor Essence and partially transform a Dream into an independent living existence. Elizabeth developed thoughts, emotions and individuality of her own, although her spiritual nature strongly resembled Morgana.[5]
Morgana initially created Elizabeth in the hope that an artificially produced daughter would be easier to surrender to [[Lilith Elderblood]]. The attempt failed because Elizabeth was not merely a construct or disposable imitation. Morgana recognized her as a genuine daughter and came to love her just as strongly as Lilith.[5][12]
Elizabeth's unusual origin also makes her an important example of the creative potential of extremely advanced Dream powers when supported by sufficient supernatural Essence. Nevertheless, her creation required both Morgana's abilities and Vlad's Progenitor Energy, and should not be treated as proof that Morgana can freely create limitless independent beings without comparable conditions.[5]

#### Unnamed Elderblood Daughter
Morgana eventually had an additional daughter with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] after joining the Elderblood family. Unlike [[Lilith Tepes]] and [[Elizabeth Tepes]], who belong to her former marriage with Vlad, this daughter descends directly from Victor's Blood Dragon lineage and Morgana's reconstructed True Dragon existence.[27]
The daughter's name and individual abilities have not yet been established in the currently indexed material. She should therefore remain listed as an unnamed daughter rather than be assigned an unsupported name or speculative Divinities.

#### Jeanne Elderblood
[[Jeanne Elderblood]] is Morgana's oldest and closest established friend.
Both women were formerly married to Vlad, possessed highly unusual supernatural origins and endured long periods of separation from the world. Their friendship allowed them to confront the changes surrounding their awakening together, and Jeanne frequently acted as a more restrained counterpart to Morgana's impulsive behavior.[16][5]
Their shared frustrations with Vlad eventually led them to seek [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s assistance in establishing a faction capable of opposing the Vampire King. Although Victor refused their original political proposal, the experience brought both women closer to his household.[18]
Jeanne and Morgana subsequently entered Victor's lineage together, although their transformations produced different results because of the extraordinary characteristics of their original Souls. Their friendship survived those changes, and the two continued teasing, advising and supporting one another throughout their lives as members of Victor's family.[7][3][30]

#### Scathach Elderblood Scarlett
[[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] is one of Morgana's fellow wives and an ancient warrior whose experience and martial knowledge Morgana respects.
Both women possess extremely violent histories, but their expertise developed through different circumstances. [[Scathach]] is particularly distinguished by her mastery of martial techniques and individual combat, while Morgana spent thousands of years surviving Hell's armies and large-scale conflicts. During the Greek campaign, Scathach deliberately assigned Morgana Guardian and Scout responsibilities because of the latter's extensive experience in warfare.[11]
Morgana later participated in martial training alongside Scathach while studying [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s [[Seven Arts of the Blood Dragon Primogenitor]]. Despite her immense power as a Dragon Goddess, she accepted Scathach's assessment that her control over Energy and Reality still required improvement before she could safely employ the complete system.[14]

### Abilities

#### Skills & Abilities
Morgana Elderblood is an ancient warrior, former High-Level Succubus, former General of [[Lilith Elderblood]], former Noble Vampire, the first known Demonic Vampire, and a current Blood Dragon Goddess whose supernatural existence combines several distinct stages of racial evolution. Originally renowned throughout Hell as The Reaper, Morgana possesses thousands of years of combat experience and two extraordinary supernatural attributes: Dream and Radiation. The former originated from her Succubus nature, while the latter awakened after [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] transformed her into a Noble Vampire. Both powers subsequently became integrated into her current Draconic existence through [[Victor Elderblood]]'s Blood Dragon lineage.[1][2][3]
Morgana's development differs considerably from that of most conventional supernatural beings. Her original Succubus abilities allowed her to manipulate Dreams, create illusions capable of physically and psychologically harming opponents, and temporarily materialize dream phenomena. At the height of her Demonic existence, she possessed enough mastery over this power to partially create an independent living being by combining her own Essence with Vlad's Progenitor Essence. Her Vampiric transformation subsequently awakened an extraordinarily destructive Radiation Bloodline, but simultaneously weakened the natural efficiency of her Dream abilities. Victor's Progenitor Blood later resolved the incompatibility between her Demonic and Vampiric foundations by transforming her into a new Noble Vampire subspecies possessing complete Demonic Traits.[4][5][6][2]
Her evolution into a True Dragon completed this process. Morgana explicitly identifies her new Dragon Domain as Radiation and Dreams, explaining that both of her original attributes had fused completely and become stronger. Her current powers therefore function as characteristics of one integrated Draconic existence rather than as separate, incompatible abilities inherited from a Demon and a Vampire. Although she possesses the extraordinary physical foundation of a Blood Dragon Goddess, Morgana remains particularly distinguished by her destructive Radiation, versatile Dream abilities, battlefield experience and close-quarters combat expertise.[3][7][8]

#### Blood Dragon Goddess Physiology
Morgana is a genuine True Dragon belonging to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood Dragon lineage. Her evolution fundamentally reconstructed her supernatural existence, granting her a Draconic body and Soul while preserving the information and powers inherited from her former Demonic Vampire state. Her reptilian eyes, transformed horns and scaled Draconic wings demonstrate that the change was a genuine racial evolution rather than a temporary enhancement or the manifestation of superficial Dragon characteristics.[3]

##### Physical Characteristics
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Morgana possesses the extraordinary physical constitution associated with genuine [[True Dragons]], supported by the supernatural foundation accumulated throughout her previous lives. Her current physiology is superior to that of her former Demonic Vampire existence, although her exact physical statistics have not been individually quantified.[3][7]
- Immense Strength: Morgana possesses the enormous physical strength characteristic of powerful True Dragons. Even before becoming a Dragon, she demonstrated sufficient physical power to overwhelm high-ranking Demons and brutally incapacitate [[Dantalion]] during their confrontation in [[Nightingale]].[1][3]
- Immense Speed: Her movement and combat speed greatly exceed ordinary supernatural standards. During her battle with Dantalion, she crossed the distance between them almost instantaneously from the opponent's perspective, seized him by the neck and subsequently flew through sections of the Royal Castle while carrying him.[1]
- Superhuman Reflexes: Morgana possesses exceptional combat reactions developed through thousands of years of warfare. Her ability to combine aerial movement, deception and close-range attacks allows her to respond to powerful opponents while maintaining offensive pressure.[5][9]
- Immense Durability: Her True Dragon body possesses the supernatural toughness associated with the Race. The exact amount of damage her current body can withstand has not been separately measured through a sustained battle against an equally powerful Dragon.[3]
- Enhanced Agility: Morgana's fighting style relies upon rapid changes in position, aerial maneuvering, claws and fists. She demonstrated this flexibility when attacking Dantalion through successive movements and later while fighting a Greek War God with the assistance of Marbas' claws.[5][9]
- Superhuman Stamina: Her Demonic, Vampiric and Draconic foundations provide extraordinary endurance. She possesses extensive experience participating in prolonged warfare, although a precise upper limit for her current stamina has not been established.[1][3]
- Enhanced Senses: Morgana possesses the supernatural perception associated with a high-ranking Demon and a genuine True Dragon. Her combat awareness allows her to recognize dangerous individuals, notice changes in supernatural power and react to transformations occurring during battle.[5][3]
- [[Immortality]]: Morgana survived thousands of years as a Demon before becoming a Noble Vampire and subsequently a True Dragon. Her current existence possesses the exceptional longevity associated with the Blood Dragon Race and is not subject to ordinary mortal aging.[1][3]
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Her current Draconic constitution possesses the restorative potential associated with True Dragons and incorporates the supernatural characteristics of her previous Vampiric foundation. Her individual regeneration limits should not be assumed identical to those of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], whose Progenitor physiology possesses exceptional properties beyond those of ordinary members of his lineage.[2][3]
- Supernatural Energy Manipulation: Morgana can generate, circulate, concentrate and release tremendous quantities of supernatural Energy. Her demonstrated applications include maintaining powerful transformations, forming offensive Dream constructs, projecting Radiation and repeatedly producing nuclear-scale explosions.[5][1][7]
- Energy Concentration: Morgana can condense enormous amounts of destructive power into relatively small objects. During the defense of [[WarFall]], she formed spheres of concentrated nuclear Energy before launching them across the battlefield.[7]
- Flight: Morgana possesses functional supernatural wings and can fly at extraordinary speed. Her wings were originally Demonic and later developed a Draconic appearance, becoming incorporated into her current True Dragon anatomy.[1][3]
- Aerial Combat: Morgana is capable of fighting while airborne, combining flight with ranged Radiation attacks, rapid pursuit and close-range assaults. Her engagement with the Greek War God demonstrates her ability to alternate between aerial positioning and melee strikes.[9]
- Natural Weaponry: Morgana possesses claws, horns, wings and a tail that can participate in physical combat. Her use of Marbas' Demonic claws demonstrated the potential to sharpen her wings into blade-like structures and her tail into a spear-like weapon, although those particular enhancements originated from external equipment.[9]
- Shapeshifting and Metamorphosis: Morgana can maintain a humanoid appearance while possessing a genuine Draconic existence. Her former transformations also demonstrated extensive alterations to her anatomy, including changes in horns, wings, tail, claws, body size and protective structures.[10][1][3]
- Dragon Transformation: Morgana possesses the racial foundation of a full True Dragon rather than a lesser Dragonoid. Her complete Draconic nature is confirmed, although the exact dimensions and appearance of her unrestricted Dragon Form have not been individually documented.[3][11]
- Dragon Breath: Dragon Breath belongs to the supernatural physiology of True Dragons. Morgana possesses the racial foundation required for such attacks, but a specific Breath technique or independently confirmed attribute unique to Morgana has not been established in the relevant scenes.[3][11]
- Draconic Heart: As a genuine True Dragon, Morgana possesses the Draconic physiological foundation associated with the Race, including the extraordinary capacity required to sustain enormous supernatural Energy output. Her individual [[Dragon Heart]] has not been assigned a distinct power ranking or unique function comparable to Victor's.[3]

##### True Dragon Soul
Morgana's current spiritual structure belongs to the True Dragon Race, but her existence retains information accumulated through her earlier Demonic and Vampiric transformations. Her development is therefore not simply the replacement of one body with another; it represents the integration of several previous supernatural foundations into a coherent Draconic nature.[2][3]
- True Dragon Soul: Morgana possesses the genuine Draconic spiritual foundation associated with complete [[True Dragons]]. This distinguishes her from Dragonoids, who lack the complete Dragon Soul and its corresponding instinctive knowledge.[3][11]
- Racial Power Integration: Her True Dragon transformation incorporated Dreams and Radiation as natural attributes of her new existence instead of discarding them or maintaining their previous racial incompatibility.[3]
- Soul Core Integration: Morgana's earlier transformation into a Demonic Vampire demonstrated the ability of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Progenitor Blood to alter her Soul Core. The Vampiric foundation subsumed her former Demonic nature while retaining its supernatural characteristics.[2]
- Supernatural Adaptability: Morgana's racial evolution demonstrates that powers originating from fundamentally different supernatural species can be incorporated into one coherent existence through Victor's Bloodline. This does not establish unrestricted instantaneous adaptation to arbitrary attacks or Concepts.[2][3]
- Instinctive Draconic Knowledge: True Dragons possess an innate connection to Draconic Language and Runes through their complete racial foundation. Morgana consequently possesses this natural aptitude, although her personal technical mastery has not been comprehensively demonstrated.[11]

#### Draconic Domains
Morgana possesses two explicitly confirmed Draconic attributes: Dream and Radiation. These powers were not newly invented during her Dragon transformation. Dreams originated from her Succubus existence, while Radiation awakened after she became a Noble Vampire. Their combination reflects the integration of Morgana's history into her current Dragon nature.[1][3]
The novel identifies them as Morgana's Domains or attributes. They should therefore be recorded under those terms rather than automatically classified as fully matured conventional Godly Divinities without an independent statement establishing that classification.

##### Dream Domain
Dream is Morgana's original supernatural specialty and one of the most extensively developed powers from her former existence as a High-Level Succubus. Her mastery developed through thousands of years of surviving Hell, fighting stronger Demons and refining the ability to deceive, incapacitate and destroy opponents through Dream phenomena. At the height of her Demonic existence, she could temporarily bring Dreams into Reality, a feat later recalled by [[Vine]] Elderblood when evaluating the possible development of other Dream-related powers.[12][13]
Her initial transformation into a Noble Vampire greatly reduced the efficiency of Dream power because her new mortal body was not naturally suited to the abilities of a pure Succubus. Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Progenitor Blood ritual and her subsequent Dragon evolution, this incompatibility was progressively corrected, culminating in Dreams becoming one of the two principal attributes of her True Dragon existence.[1][2][3]

###### Dream Manipulation
- Dream Manipulation: Morgana can generate, shape, project and weaponize supernatural Dreams. Her powers extend beyond ordinary sleeping experiences and can interact directly with the waking world when sufficiently developed.[4][5]
- Dream Generation: She can produce supernatural Dream phenomena rather than relying exclusively upon Dreams already being experienced by another person.[5]
- Dream Projection: Morgana can project Dream constructs into the surrounding environment, making opponents experience phenomena originating from her supernatural abilities.[5]
- Dream Materialization: At advanced levels, her Dreams can acquire a temporary physical presence. During the battle with [[Dantalion]], Dream-generated phenomena were capable of interacting with the target and inflicting genuine harm.[5]
- Dream-Reality Interaction: Morgana's projected Dreams are not restricted to harmless mental images. Her combat against Dantalion demonstrates that knowing an attack originates from a Dream does not necessarily prevent it from producing physical or psychological consequences.[5]
- Temporary Reality Materialization: At the height of her former Succubus powers, Morgana could temporarily bring Dreams into Reality for several minutes. [[Vine]] later identifies this achievement as a benchmark for the possible development of Dream-related abilities.[13]
- Dream Construct Creation: Morgana can produce supernatural manifestations through Dream power, including terrifying creatures, false bodies and offensive constructs that interact with the target.[5]
- Dream Environment Manipulation: Her manifestations can alter the circumstances an opponent believes they are experiencing, forcing the victim to react to the events established by the Dream.[5]
- Dream-Based Offensive Power: Her Dreams can inflict physical and mental damage simultaneously, distinguishing her advanced Succubus techniques from ordinary illusions that affect perception alone.[5]

###### Illusion Manipulation
Morgana's Dream powers include powerful illusions capable of deceiving supernatural beings and disguising the distinction between an actual combatant and a projected manifestation. During her confrontation with [[Dantalion]], she repeatedly exploited this distinction to mislead his attacks and create opportunities for counterattacks. Her illusions subsequently improved following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s influence, allowing her to conceal herself even from Jeanne until Victor exposed her location.[5][14]
- Illusion Creation: Morgana can generate convincing supernatural illusions through Dream power. These manifestations are capable of deceiving powerful enemies during combat.[5]
- Visual Illusion Manipulation: She can alter the appearance of herself or a projected target, making opponents respond to a visual representation that does not correspond to her true physical position.[5]
- Perception Manipulation: Morgana can interfere with the information perceived by an opponent, making it difficult to distinguish her actual presence from the Dreams and illusions surrounding the battlefield.[5]
- Illusory Doppelgangers: She can produce false versions of herself to misdirect attacks. During the battle against Dantalion, apparently defeated versions of Morgana dissolved or behaved unexpectedly, revealing that the Duke had attacked Dream-based manifestations rather than reliably eliminating her.[5]
- Illusory Substitution: Morgana can exploit a false body to redirect an enemy's attention while her actual attack approaches from another position. Her battle against Dantalion demonstrates that such deceptions can remain effective even against high-ranking Demons.[5]
- Illusory Invisibility: Morgana can conceal her presence through an illusion that creates the appearance of invisibility. After Victor's Bloodline ritual, she hid near a pillar without Jeanne initially detecting her. Victor identified her location and revealed the deception, after which Jeanne recognized that Morgana's illusion abilities had improved.[14]
- Supernatural Concealment: Her illusions can conceal her apparent location from powerful supernatural observers, although the technique is not absolute and can be penetrated by superior perception.[14]
- Physicalized Illusions: Some of her advanced Dream manifestations can inflict genuine injuries rather than merely misleading an opponent's senses.[5]
- Illusionary Combat Tactics: Morgana can combine false appearances with genuine attacks, forcing opponents to waste time identifying the actual source of danger while she exploits the resulting openings.[5]
The illusions Morgana employs are more versatile than ordinary visual deception, but they should not automatically be interpreted as unrestricted Mind Control. Her confirmed abilities primarily involve manipulating perception, Dreams and the temporary physical manifestation of imagined phenomena.

###### Nightmare Manifestation
Morgana can use Dream power offensively by manifesting horrifying creatures and experiences that exploit the fears of her opponents. Her encounter with [[Dantalion]] demonstrates that these attacks are particularly effective against enemies who understand that the manifestations are unreal but remain vulnerable to their psychological influence.[5]
- Nightmare Manipulation: Morgana can create terrifying Dream manifestations based upon the psychological vulnerabilities of her target.[5]
- Fear Inducement: Her projected nightmares can cause intense fear even in high-ranking Demons. Dantalion became terrified by the grotesque manifestation confronting him despite recognizing it as a Dream.[5]
- Psychological Manipulation: Morgana's nightmares directly interfere with a target's mental state, creating panic and reducing the effectiveness of rational responses.[5]
- Psychological Warfare: She can exploit fear, confusion and emotional instability while maintaining direct physical pressure upon the opponent.[5]
- Nightmare Projection: Morgana can manifest frightening creatures and other hostile Dream phenomena into the environment experienced by her target.[5]
- Mental and Physical Damage: Her advanced projected Dreams can simultaneously affect the target psychologically and physically, making them more dangerous than harmless hallucinations.[5]

###### Sleep Manipulation
Morgana can induce or maintain a sleeping state through her Succubus Dream abilities. One of her earliest documented achievements involved using this power to defeat an opponent considerably stronger than herself during her struggles to reach Lilith's territory.[12]
- Sleep Inducement: Morgana can use Dream [[Magic]] to place a target into a supernatural sleeping state. She employed this ability against a High-Level Imp during the repeated attempts made by her group to survive the dangerous region surrounding Lilith.[12]
- Sleep Maintenance: Her Dream power was effective enough to keep the Imp asleep while Morgana and her companions attacked it directly.[12]
- Combat Incapacitation: Sleep-based interference can render an enemy temporarily unable to defend itself, creating opportunities for Morgana or her allies to attack without confronting the opponent's full strength.[12]
The effectiveness of this power against beings vastly superior to Morgana's current level has not been established. The original feat demonstrates successful incapacitation of a High-Level Demon under specific circumstances.

###### Dream Weapon Creation
Morgana can form offensive weapons from supernatural Energy associated with her Dream abilities. During her battle against [[Dantalion]], she created a Western-style sword from concentrated dark power and used it in direct combat.[5]
- Energy Weapon Creation: Morgana can create a sword from supernatural Energy rather than relying exclusively upon conventional physical equipment.[5]
- Supernatural Weapon Materialization: Her created weapon possesses sufficient solidity and offensive capability to clash with a Demon Duke's claws.[5]
- Energy Weapon Combat: Morgana used the manifested sword to attack Dantalion at close range and exploit opportunities created by her illusions.[5]
- Weapon-Assisted Illusions: Her ability to combine false bodies, misleading movements and a materialized weapon allows her to threaten opponents even when they believe they have successfully identified her position.[5]
The chapter establishes the creation of a combat-capable supernatural sword, but does not confirm that Morgana can automatically reproduce every kind of weapon or Divine Artifact through this ability.

###### Partial Life Creation
One of Morgana's greatest feats from her former Succubus existence was the creation of [[Elizabeth Tepes]]. Unlike an ordinary Dream illusion or temporary physical manifestation, Elizabeth developed into an independent living being possessing thoughts, emotions and an individual identity. Morgana achieved this by combining her own Essence with the Progenitor Essence of Vlad and using her advanced Dream abilities to partially transform the resulting concept into life.[4]
- Partial Life Creation: Morgana produced an independent living being through advanced Dream power and the combined supernatural Essence of herself and Vlad. The process was explicitly regarded as bordering upon the domain of Divine creation.[4]
- Essence Manipulation: Morgana could use her own supernatural Essence and the Progenitor Essence supplied by Vlad as components of the creation process.[4]
- Dream Materialization: Elizabeth's existence demonstrates that Morgana could use Dream power to produce something substantially more permanent than an ordinary temporary illusion.[4]
- Spiritual Replication: Elizabeth inherited spiritual characteristics resembling Morgana to such an extent that she could be compared to a younger spiritual clone of her mother.[4]
- Independent Consciousness Creation: The process resulted in a being possessing independent thoughts rather than a mindless construct directly controlled by Morgana.[4]
- Biological and Spiritual Creation: Elizabeth possesses a genuine existence created through the interaction between supernatural Essence and Dream power. The process does not establish that Morgana could accomplish the same result without the extraordinary Energy and Essence involved.[4]
Limitations: Elizabeth's creation required Vlad's Progenitor Essence, Morgana's own Essence and the power she possessed as a highly developed Succubus. The feat should therefore be classified as a conditional form of Life Creation rather than unrestricted creation of life from nothing. Elizabeth is also not a disposable Dream construct: she possesses an independent identity and cannot be treated as an extension of Morgana's consciousness.

##### Radiation Domain
Radiation is Morgana's second principal supernatural attribute and the source of her greatest demonstrated destructive output. The power awakened after Vlad transformed her into a Noble Vampire, despite having apparently remained dormant and accumulating within her existence beforehand. Its nature was unusual enough that even Vlad had difficulty classifying it after examination. Unlike Dreams, which became less efficient following her Vampiric transformation, Radiation was naturally compatible with her new body and provided Morgana with an extraordinarily destructive alternative to her former Succubus techniques.[1]
Following her evolution into a True Dragon, Radiation became one of Morgana's two Draconic Domains and was explicitly strengthened alongside Dreams. The destructive capabilities demonstrated during the defense of [[WarFall]] illustrate the enormous difference between Morgana's early Vampiric use and her later Dragon Goddess state.[3][7]

###### Radiation Manipulation
- Radiation Manipulation: Morgana can generate, control, concentrate and release extraordinarily destructive supernatural Radiation. Her Bloodline was described as Radiation in a particularly pure form, although its exact classification was not completely understood even by Vlad.[1]
- Radiation Generation: Morgana naturally produces the destructive Energy associated with her Bloodline rather than depending upon external radioactive materials.[1]
- Radiation Accumulation: Before Morgana fully awakened the Bloodline, the Radiation was described as having remained dormant and accumulating within her body.[1]
- Radiation Projection: Morgana can release concentrated Radiation through her body, eyes and hands, allowing attacks at different distances and scales.[1][7]
- Radiation Concentration: She can compress her Energy into destructive projectiles instead of releasing it exclusively as an uncontrolled aura or blast.[7]
- Radiation Release: Morgana can discharge an enormous quantity of stored Radiation in a sudden outburst. Her initial major display during the fight with [[Dantalion]] annihilated large numbers of enemies together with nearby structures.[1]
- Crimson Radiation: Her earlier Vampiric Radiation manifested as crimson Energy containing orange tones, surrounding her body after the release of her awakened Bloodline.[1]
- Energy Control: Morgana can regulate the delivery of Radiation through several distinct methods, including body coating, eye beams, hand beams, concentrated spheres and large-area explosions.[1][7]

###### Radiation Aura
Morgana can coat her body in destructive Radiation, turning her immediate surroundings into a dangerous environment for enemies attempting to approach or intercept her. The technique was demonstrated during her confrontation with [[Dantalion]] and later on a much greater scale during the invasion of [[WarFall]].[1][7]
- Radiation Aura: Morgana can envelop her body in the destructive Energy of her Bloodline.[1][7]
- Radiation Coating: The aura covers her body while she moves, allowing her to combine ordinary physical attacks with destructive Energy.[7]
- Contact Destruction: Morgana can fly directly through enemy formations while the Radiation surrounding her destroys Monsters along her path.[7]
- Proximity Damage: The Energy surrounding her can threaten nearby enemies without requiring every target to be struck by a separate projectile.[7]
- Radiation-Enhanced Combat: Morgana can maintain destructive Radiation around herself while executing physical movements, making close-range combat especially dangerous for opponents unable to withstand her Energy.[7]
The aura surrounds Morgana and can discourage close-range attacks, but it should not automatically be classified as an impenetrable barrier or proof that she possesses unlimited resistance to every form of Radiation.

###### Ocular Radiation Projection
Morgana can project destructive beams of Radiation directly from her eyes. The ability was prominently demonstrated after awakening her Bloodline during the Demonic invasion and was later employed during the defense of [[WarFall]].[1][7]
- Ocular Energy Projection: Morgana can release concentrated Radiation through her eyes as powerful destructive beams.[1]
- Heat Projection: Her eye beams possess sufficient destructive intensity to burn through enemies and surrounding material.[1][7]
- Piercing Energy Attacks: The beams can penetrate groups of Demons and continue affecting targets along their path.[1]
- Continuous Beam Projection: Morgana can sustain beams while tracking moving opponents, allowing her to maintain pressure against enemies attempting to evade the attack.[9]
- Aerial Beam Combat: She can use her ocular Radiation while flying, combining speed, pursuit and sustained offensive projection.[9][7]
- Wide-Area Destruction: Releasing the beams at considerable intensity can destroy multiple enemies and nearby structures, making careful control necessary in populated areas.[1]
During Morgana's earlier Vampiric use, the destructive output caused substantial collateral damage. Her later Dragon Goddess feats demonstrate increased versatility and scale, but do not establish that every beam is automatically precise enough to avoid all unintended damage.

###### Hand Radiation Projection
Morgana can emit concentrated Radiation through her hands, providing a direct ranged attack that does not require creating an explosive sphere or projecting Energy through her eyes.[7]
- Hand Energy Projection: Morgana can release pure Radiation directly from her palms.[7]
- Concentrated Energy Beams: She can channel the Energy into powerful beams capable of destroying enemies across the battlefield.[7]
- Ranged Combat: Her hand beams allow Morgana to attack targets without requiring direct physical contact.[7]
- Multiple Energy Delivery Methods: Morgana can alternate between ocular beams, hand projection and concentrated projectiles according to the circumstances of combat.[7]

###### Crimson Radiation Release
During the battle against [[Dantalion]], Morgana's anger over the apparent fate of Lilith caused her to unleash an enormous quantity of accumulated Radiation. The release briefly manifested as a crimson phenomenon surrounding the battlefield, annihilating the Demons summoned by Dantalion and destroying nearby structures. The attack marked one of the first occasions on which Morgana openly used the destructive Bloodline awakened through her Vampiric existence.[1]
- Massive Energy Release: Morgana can discharge a substantial quantity of Radiation in a single overwhelming attack.[1]
- Area-of-Effect Destruction: Her initial major release destroyed multiple Demons and surrounding structures simultaneously.[1]
- Mass Target Destruction: The attack eliminated the army that Dantalion had summoned from his Soul, demonstrating effectiveness against large numbers of supernatural enemies.[1]
- Destructive Energy Burst: The Radiation can be released outward as a rapidly expanding phenomenon rather than only as a narrow projectile.[1]
- Overwhelming Offensive Pressure: The sudden increase in destructive power allowed Morgana to overpower the immediate battlefield and isolate Dantalion after his supporting forces were destroyed.[1]
The disappearance of enemies within the attack should not automatically be interpreted as absolute Existence Erasure. The chapter establishes extraordinary physical and supernatural destruction, but does not demonstrate that Morgana erased the victims' metaphysical Records, Souls or Concepts from all of Reality.

###### Nuclear Energy Manipulation
Morgana's Radiation possesses explicitly nuclear destructive characteristics. During the Elder-God conflict, she demonstrated the ability to condense her Energy into small spheres that produced enormous explosions resembling nuclear detonations. These attacks were formed from her own supernatural Energy rather than conventional technological weapons.[7]
- Nuclear Energy Generation: Morgana can generate concentrated supernatural nuclear Energy as an application of her Radiation Domain.[7]
- Nuclear Energy Manipulation: She can compress and deploy this Energy as destructive projectiles.[7]
- Energy Sphere Creation: Morgana can form small spheres of nuclear Energy within her hands, with the spheres appearing green and containing yellow tones.[7]
- Energy Compression: She can concentrate an extraordinary quantity of destructive Energy into a small projectile before releasing it.[7]
- Nuclear Explosion Inducement: The spheres produce enormous mushroom-cloud explosions after being launched against the target area.[7]
- Remote Detonation Attacks: Morgana can throw the spheres into enemy formations and cause destructive explosions at a distance.[7]
- Multiple Projectile Generation: Morgana produced approximately twenty additional nuclear spheres in rapid succession after demonstrating the initial attack.[7]
- Nuclear Bombardment: Repeated detonations allow her to devastate large concentrations of enemies across an enormous battlefield.[7]
- Mass Destruction: The offensive output of her nuclear Energy was sufficient to terrify powerful observers, including Gods, Angels and other supernatural combatants witnessing the explosions.[7]
The demonstrated ability concerns the generation and manipulation of Morgana's own supernatural nuclear Energy. Direct manipulation of arbitrary atomic nuclei, all forms of radioactive decay or every scientifically possible nuclear reaction remains unconfirmed.

###### Purified Nuclear Energy
An important characteristic of Morgana's nuclear attacks is the unusually pure nature of the Energy used to produce them. Unlike conventional Human nuclear weapons, the explosions demonstrated during [[WarFall]] did not poison the planet with the lingering radioactive contamination normally associated with nuclear detonations.[7]
- Purified Nuclear Energy: Morgana can produce supernatural nuclear Energy whose destructive effect does not necessarily create the same residual contamination as ordinary nuclear weapons.[7]
- Reduced Environmental Contamination: Her attacks can produce enormous nuclear explosions without automatically leaving the affected planet poisoned by conventional radioactive fallout.[7]
- Repeated Nuclear Bombardment: The absence of ordinary lingering contamination allows Morgana to employ successive nuclear attacks without the specific environmental consequence described for Human nuclear weapons.[7]
This characteristic does not mean the explosions are environmentally harmless. Their immediate destructive effects remain enormous, and the chapters do not establish that Morgana can restore terrain, resurrect victims or eliminate every secondary consequence of the explosions.

###### Radiation-Based Destruction
- Heat-Based Destruction: Morgana's Radiation can produce intense heat capable of burning and destroying supernatural enemies.[1][7]
- Material Destruction: Her attacks can destroy physical structures and surrounding material through overwhelming destructive Energy.[1]
- Biological Destruction: Her Radiation can annihilate living supernatural creatures through direct exposure to sufficiently powerful attacks.[1][7]
- Large-Scale Offensive Power: During the defense of [[WarFall]], Morgana was described as possessing the greatest offensive power among the fighters present in her immediate battlefield group.[7]
- Continuous Battlefield Suppression: Morgana can combine her destructive aura, energy beams and nuclear projectiles to maintain pressure upon large enemy formations.[7]

##### Dream-Radiation Fusion
Morgana's evolution into a Blood Dragon unified Dreams and Radiation within a single supernatural existence. This fusion corrected the incompatibility that had reduced the effectiveness of her old Succubus powers after she became a Vampire and strengthened both attributes through her Draconic nature.[1][3]
- Dual Domain Integration: Dreams and Radiation function as principal attributes of Morgana's True Dragon existence rather than remaining disconnected powers from separate racial foundations.[3]
- Racial Power Evolution: Her Dragon transformation strengthened both of her previous specialties instead of replacing them with unrelated Draconic abilities.[3]
- Dream Power Restoration: The natural incompatibility between her Vampiric physiology and former Succubus Dream abilities was corrected through their incorporation into her new Draconic foundation.[1][3]
- Enhanced Radiation: Radiation became a principal True Dragon attribute and subsequently demonstrated nuclear-scale attacks far beyond Morgana's earlier recorded Vampiric applications.[3][7]
- Combined Offensive Potential: Dreams provide concealment, psychological interference and unusual manifestations, while Radiation supplies overwhelming destructive output. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly instructed Morgana to use Dreams to confuse opponents and Radiation to overpower them during the Greek campaign.[9]
The precise technical limits of a simultaneous conceptual combination between Dreams and Radiation have not been fully demonstrated. Their fusion as Draconic attributes is confirmed, but individual hybrid attacks possessing entirely new properties should not be invented without textual evidence.

#### Draconic Language and Runes
Morgana's True Dragon status grants her the natural connection to Draconic Language and Runes associated with genuine members of the Race. Dragon Language differs from ordinary communication because words can carry supernatural [[Will Solace|Will]], Energy and authority, allowing sufficiently knowledgeable Dragons to impose commands upon Reality.[15][11]
- Draconic Language Comprehension: As a genuine True Dragon, Morgana possesses the instinctive relationship with the language of her Race.[11]
- Runic Affinity: Morgana possesses the natural aptitude for Draconic Runes associated with a complete True Dragon Soul.[11]
- Instinctive Rune Knowledge: Genuine [[True Dragons]] possess inherent knowledge of their supernatural language and Runic system, unlike lesser Dragonoids who must acquire that knowledge through study.[11]
- Supernatural Language: Draconic speech is capable of carrying commands that interact with Reality when supported by sufficient authority, Energy and understanding.[15]
- Rune [[Magic]] Potential: Morgana possesses the racial foundation necessary to employ Draconic Runes, although her personal repertoire and highest level of technical mastery remain incompletely documented.[11]
The possession of instinctive Draconic knowledge does not automatically establish mastery equal to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], [[Zaladrac Zeovnur Elderblood]] or [[Dun Scaith Elderblood]]. Racial comprehension and the ability to construct advanced Runic systems are separate achievements.

#### Reality Distortion
As a True Dragon, Morgana possesses the racial foundation associated with the ability to interfere with surrounding Reality. However, her personal mastery is not complete. During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s demonstration of the [[Seven Arts of the Blood Dragon Primogenitor]], Morgana acknowledged that she still lacked the necessary precision in Reality Distortion and Energy control to perform the complete martial system safely.[16]
- Reality Distortion: Morgana possesses the True Dragon racial foundation required to interact with Reality beyond ordinary physical movement. The exact range and complexity of her individually mastered applications have not been completely established.[16]
- Developing Reality Control: Morgana is actively improving the precision necessary to employ more advanced martial techniques involving direct manipulation of the surrounding world.[16]
- Energy Control Training: She continues refining her ability to regulate supernatural Energy while fighting, particularly because advanced Dragon martial techniques demand extremely precise coordination.[16]
- Reality-Enhanced Martial Potential: Her racial abilities make her theoretically compatible with martial systems that rely upon Reality Distortion, but she had not reached the necessary standard when the Seven Arts were first introduced.[16]
Morgana's Reality Distortion should not be equated with unrestricted Reality Warping or the advanced abilities personally demonstrated by Victor.

#### Former Racial Abilities
Morgana's current Draconic existence incorporates several earlier supernatural foundations. Her previous powers did not all vanish when she evolved, but their original racial classifications and transformations should remain distinguished from the abilities of her present True Dragon state.

##### Former Succubus Physiology
Morgana originated as a low-level Succubus born within Hell and developed into a High-Level Demon through thousands of years of survival, combat and service beneath Lilith. Her original existence possessed the spiritual characteristics common to Demons and an exceptional affinity for Dreams.[12][1]
- Demon Physiology: Morgana originally possessed the supernatural existence of a Hell-born Demon before being transformed into a Noble Vampire.[12][2]
- Succubus Physiology: Her original Race possessed supernatural characteristics associated with Dreams, spiritual perception and an unusually attractive humanoid appearance.[4][17]
- Supernatural Beauty: Morgana possesses the unnatural attractiveness associated with her Succubus origin, a characteristic that remained recognizable throughout her later transformations.[17]
- Natural Dream Affinity: Dreams were part of Morgana's original racial foundation. She refined this affinity into one of her greatest powers during her rise through Hell.[4][12]
- Demonic Claws: Morgana could fight using natural claws during her early development, including the confrontation in which her group defeated a High-Level Imp.[12]
- Demonic Wings: Her former body possessed wings that supported aerial movement and became more pronounced during her transformations.[10][1]
- Demonic Tail and Horns: Morgana retained recognizable Succubus characteristics through her subsequent transformations, with these structures becoming increasingly formidable when she released greater power.[10][1]
- Demonic Power Growth: Morgana developed her strength through repeated combat and the consumption of stronger Demons. Her early survival in Hell demonstrates the importance of this process in her original evolution.[12]
- Demonic Spiritual Existence: Demons possess an unusual spiritual nature tied to Hell. Morgana explains that an ordinary Demon killed without the appropriate weakness being exploited can return to the Hell associated with its existence, although this former racial property should not automatically be attributed unchanged to her current Draconic Soul.[2]
- True Demonic Name: Morgana possesses an original Demonic name that she refuses to reveal casually. Such names are deeply connected to the existence of High-Level Demons, and her current name does not replace the significance of that original spiritual identity.[18]

###### Demonic Regeneration
During Morgana's early life, she and her companions repeatedly recovered from catastrophic wounds by using a supernatural red river in Hell. The injuries they survived included severed limbs and severe bodily mutilation.[12]
- External Regeneration: The red river restored injuries suffered by Morgana and her companions during repeated attempts to defeat stronger Demons.[12]
- Recovery from Mutilation: Morgana survived grievous damage through access to the river's supernatural restorative effects.[12]
These early feats depended upon an external supernatural resource. They should not be interpreted as proof that Morgana independently regenerated from identical injuries without assistance at that stage of her life.

###### Miasma Compatibility
As an ancient High-Level Demon, Morgana possessed the racial background associated with Hell's Miasma and Demonic Energy. However, her individually documented powers primarily concern Dreams, followed by the Radiation she acquired after becoming a Vampire.[1][2]
- Demonic Energy Compatibility: Morgana's original existence belonged to a Race naturally associated with Demonic supernatural Energy.[2]
- Miasma Affinity: Her former High-Level Demon status provides the racial context for interaction with Hell's Miasma, but the specific amount she can personally generate or manipulate has not been independently quantified.
Unlimited Miasma manipulation, arbitrary corruption and mastery over every Demonic technique should not be assumed solely from her former General rank.

##### Former Noble Vampire Physiology
Vlad transformed Morgana into a Noble Vampire, granting her a complete mortal-type body and Soul while preserving remnants of her former Demonic existence. The transformation awakened Radiation but caused her previously refined Dream powers to become considerably less efficient.[1][2]
- Noble Vampire Physiology: Morgana received the enhanced physical and supernatural characteristics associated with Noble Vampires.[1]
- Vampiric [[Immortality]]: Her new existence possessed the extraordinary lifespan associated with high-ranking Vampires.[1]
- Vampiric Regeneration: Her Noble Vampire foundation possessed the racial regenerative characteristics associated with the species.[2]
- Complete Mortal Soul: Vlad's transformation gave Morgana a complete mortal-type Soul, an important distinction from the spiritual nature of ordinary Hell-born Demons.[2]
- Radiation Bloodline: Morgana's Vampiric body awakened the destructive Radiation that had remained dormant within her existence.[1]
- Demonic Trait Retention: Her Soul Core retained a remnant of her original Demonic identity, allowing her Count Transformation to manifest characteristics of her former Succubus nature.[10][2]

##### Former Demonic Vampire Physiology
Morgana became the first known Demonic Vampire following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Progenitor Blood ritual. Unlike a balanced Hybrid, she was fundamentally a Noble Vampire whose Vampiric nature had subsumed and incorporated the traits of her former Demonic existence. Her Soul Core became unified rather than continuing to maintain two incompatible racial foundations.[6][2]
- Demonic Vampire Physiology: Morgana became the first known representative of a Noble Vampire subspecies possessing complete Demonic Traits.[2]
- Racial Trait Assimilation: Her Vampiric side absorbed the remaining Demonic information in her Soul Core without eliminating the supernatural powers associated with that origin.[2]
- Soul Core Reconstruction: Victor's Bloodline altered the fundamental racial structure of Morgana's Soul, producing a coherent new subspecies rather than merely strengthening her body.[2]
- Demonic Power Preservation: Her former Succubus abilities remained available despite the complete subsumption of her Demonic racial foundation.[2]
- Enhanced Dream Compatibility: Following the transformation, Morgana demonstrated improved illusion abilities, including the capacity to conceal herself from Jeanne through an illusion.[14]
- Enhanced Vampiric Foundation: The transformation strengthened her Noble Vampire existence through direct incorporation into Victor's Progenitor Bloodline.[6][2]
- Potential for Divine Development: The discussion surrounding her new Soul established that Morgana's complete mortal-type Soul and enhanced supernatural foundation provided possibilities that would not normally apply to an ordinary incomplete Demonic Soul.[2]
The Demonic Vampire transformation did not establish Morgana as the Progenitor of a completely independent Race. The novel explicitly classifies the result as a Noble Vampire subspecies, and she later evolved beyond that stage by becoming a True Dragon.

##### Former Vampire Count Transformation
Before becoming a True Dragon, Morgana possessed a mutated [[Vampire Count Form]] influenced by the Demonic information preserved within her Soul Core. Her transformation developed into a succession of increasingly monstrous states, reflecting her former Succubus physiology rather than the conventional appearance of a Noble Vampire Count.[10][5][1]

###### First Form
Morgana's first demonstrated Count Transformation dramatically altered her appearance and increased the prominence of her Demonic characteristics.[10]
- Demonic Metamorphosis: Her body transformed into a state resembling a powerful Succubus rather than an ordinary Vampire.[10]
- Hair Growth: Her long black hair extended until it reached her feet.[10]
- Reinforced Hair: Individual strands became unusually strong and appeared sharp enough to resemble blades.[10]
- Gravity-Defying Hair: Her hair floated unnaturally rather than remaining subject to ordinary gravity.[10]
- Wing Transformation: Her wings developed a darker appearance as her Demonic nature became more pronounced.[10]
- Skin Transformation: Her normally pale skin changed to deep red.[10]
- Eye Transformation: Her eyes acquired a golden appearance during the transformation.[10]
- Demonic Claw Formation: Her hands transformed into powerful Demonic claws suitable for direct combat.[10]
- Forearm Spikes: Two spikes emerged from her forearms as part of the transformation.[10]
- Natural Armor Formation: Thick protective structures formed across portions of her arms, legs, torso and other areas, giving the appearance of biological armor.[10]
- Enhanced Demonic Power: The transformation increased the supernatural pressure and combat capabilities associated with her former Demonic nature.[10]

###### Second Form
Morgana pushed her Count Transformation to a more advanced stage while fighting [[Dantalion]]. The resulting form became substantially more monstrous and increased the scale upon which she could employ Dream power.[5][1]
- Advanced Demonic Transformation: Morgana's body became larger and more visibly monstrous than during the first stage.[1]
- Enhanced Tail: A large, thick Demonic tail developed behind her.[1]
- Additional Horn Growth: Another horn appeared as the transformation progressed.[1]
- Demonic Aura: An oppressive dark pressure surrounded Morgana, affecting Dantalion's perception of the danger she represented.[1]
- Enhanced Dream Power: The second transformation allowed Morgana to employ Dreams on a greater scale than before, although their efficiency remained inferior to the power she possessed as a pure Succubus.[1]
- Enhanced Combat Capability: The form increased Morgana's ability to contend with the empowered Dantalion, whose supernatural abilities had been strengthened through external curses.[1]
Both Count stages belong to Morgana's former Noble Vampire existence. Her current True Dragon state supersedes them as her principal racial foundation.

#### Combat and Martial Abilities
Morgana possesses thousands of years of experience fighting Demons, Demon Dukes, Divine Beings and other powerful supernatural creatures. Her development began as a weak Succubus forced to survive Hell through repeated combat and eventually carried her into the rank of General within Lilith's army. Her combat expertise is distinguished by aggressive close-range fighting, supernatural deception and extensive experience with large-scale warfare.[12][1][19]

##### Close-Quarters Combat
Morgana is explicitly identified as a close-combat specialist. Although she understands conventional weapons, she prefers using claws and fists, maintaining a fighting style consistent with the natural weaponry of her original Demonic existence.[8]
- Expert Hand-to-Hand Combat: Morgana possesses extensive experience fighting directly with her body, using punches, claws and other close-range attacks.[5][8]
- Claw Combat: Her preferred natural weapons are her claws, which she uses for slashing, piercing and close-range offensive pressure.[9][8]
- Unarmed Combat: Morgana can fight without a conventional weapon and prefers using her hands and body as the principal means of attack.[8]
- Aerial Close Combat: Her wings allow rapid movement and changes of direction while engaging opponents at close range.[5][9]
- Tail Combat: Morgana can employ her tail as a natural weapon when sufficiently enhanced. With Marbas' claws equipped, her tail became sharp enough to function like a spear and was used offensively against a Greek War God.[9]
- Natural Weapon Integration: Morgana can combine claws, wings and tail attacks, turning multiple parts of her body into offensive instruments.[9]
- Radiation-Enhanced Physical Combat: Morgana can coat her body in destructive Radiation while moving through opponents, allowing physical contact to be accompanied by supernatural Energy damage.[7]
- Energy Weapon Combat: She demonstrated the ability to use a manifested sword during her fight against [[Dantalion]], despite her normal preference for claws and fists.[5]
- Combat Deception: Morgana frequently combines misleading movements and illusions with genuine physical attacks. Her use of false bodies against Dantalion demonstrates the effectiveness of this approach.[5]

##### Tactical Combat
- Combat Experience: Morgana's thousands of years of battles provide extensive familiarity with the techniques and behavior of supernatural enemies.[1]
- Tactical Intelligence: She can exploit the particular weaknesses of opponents instead of relying exclusively upon superior raw power. Her early victory over a High-Level Imp through Sleep Manipulation demonstrates this principle.[12]
- Combat Analysis: Morgana recognizes unfamiliar changes in an opponent's supernatural power, including the cursed inscriptions that appeared upon [[Dantalion]]'s body.[5]
- Weakness Exploitation: She can combine deception, psychological interference and direct attacks to exploit the openings created by an opponent's reactions.[5][9]
- Unorthodox Combat: Morgana does not restrict herself to conventional dueling etiquette. During the Greek campaign, she attacked a War God through a combination of overwhelming Radiation, unnatural weaponry and opportunistic strikes.[9]
- Interrogation: Morgana possesses experience extracting information from dangerous opponents. During the battle with Dantalion, she attempted to obtain information concerning Lilith's condition rather than simply eliminating the Duke immediately.[1]
- Supernatural Threat Recognition: Her familiarity with Hell's hierarchy allows her to identify powerful Demons and recognize the significance of their techniques or affiliations.[5][1]

##### Martial Arts Training
Morgana continued developing her martial capabilities after becoming a Dragon Goddess. She participated in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s demonstrations of the [[Seven Arts of the Blood Dragon Primogenitor]] and later trained directly against him alongside [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]].[16][8]
- [[Martial Arts]] Knowledge: Morgana possesses practical martial experience accumulated throughout her career, although she has not been established as possessing the same formal mastery as Victor, [[Scathach]] or Rose.[8]
- Coordinated Combat: Morgana and Haruna demonstrated excellent coordination while attacking Victor from different directions during their training.[8]
- Combat Adaptation Through Training: She actively practices against stronger opponents and attempts to correct weaknesses revealed through direct confrontation.[8]
- Reality-Based Martial Training: Morgana is developing the Energy control and Reality Distortion precision necessary for the more advanced martial principles used by [[True Dragons]].[16]
- Technical Evaluation: Morgana recognized the enormous strain the Seven Arts placed upon the practitioner's body and the difficulty of reproducing Victor's movements without his exceptional control.[16]

###### Seven Arts of the Blood Dragon Primogenitor
Morgana is acquainted with the principles of the Seven Arts but had not mastered the complete system when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] introduced it. The techniques require extremely precise Energy control, advanced Reality Distortion and the ability to coordinate supernatural Energy with the defenses and deeper structure of the Soul.[16]
- Reality Distortion Training: Morgana needs to refine her ability to manipulate the surrounding world with considerably greater precision before safely attempting the complete Arts.[16]
- Perfect Energy Control Training: Her existing Energy control was insufficient for the highest requirements of Victor's martial system.[16]
- Soul-Energy Coordination: The more advanced stages involve directing Energy toward the Soul and partially integrating it with the practitioner's physical body. Morgana has not demonstrated mastery of these stages.[16]
- Martial Development: Morgana continues training under Victor, improving the foundations necessary for more advanced combat techniques.[8]
The Seven Arts should not be listed among Morgana's fully mastered techniques until a later chapter explicitly establishes that she has met their requirements.

#### Military Skills
Morgana's military experience is one of the most important characteristics distinguishing her from other members of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family. She spent thousands of years fighting in Hell and eventually became one of Lilith's Generals, earning the title The Reaper through her military achievements. Her reputation included the ability to contend with [[Bael]] and the defeat of five Demon Dukes of Ranks 21, 26, 34, 44 and 69.[1]
During the Greek campaign, [[Scathach]] specifically identified Morgana as possessing the greatest experience in large-scale warfare among the women participating in the assault group and entrusted her with the responsibilities of Guardian and Scout.[19]
- Military Strategy: Morgana possesses extensive experience with warfare involving Demonic armies, high-ranking commanders and supernatural military forces.[1][19]
- Large-Scale Warfare Experience: She has participated in wars involving numerous powerful combatants and is familiar with the demands of fighting beyond individual duels.[1][19]
- Military Leadership: Morgana formerly served as one of Lilith's Generals and consequently possesses experience associated with high-ranking military command.[1]
- Battlefield Awareness: Morgana understands the need to monitor threats affecting an entire group rather than concentrating exclusively upon the opponent directly in front of her.[19]
- Scouting: Scathach entrusted Morgana with reconnaissance responsibilities during the Greek campaign.[19]
- Guardian Duties: Morgana accepted responsibility for protecting the other participants in her assault group while continuing to contribute offensively.[19]
- Formation Protection: Her experience allows her to recognize situations in which preserving the safety and cohesion of a group takes priority over pursuing an individual opponent.[19]
- Demonic Warfare Expertise: Morgana possesses intimate knowledge of Demon armies, commanders, ranks and the tactics employed by Hell's military powers.[5][1]
- Demonic Hierarchy Knowledge: Her former position allowed her to recognize the status and capabilities of important Demon Dukes and other high-ranking members of Hell.[1]
- Demonic Curse Recognition: During her fight with [[Dantalion]], Morgana recognized that the unusual inscriptions appearing upon his body were connected to an external supernatural system rather than ordinary Demonic techniques.[5]
- Survival Expertise: Morgana survived the dangerous environment of Hell when she was still a comparatively weak Succubus, developing the ability to recover from failure, exploit stronger enemies and use available resources to continue advancing.[12]
- Interrogation and Intelligence Gathering: Morgana can prioritize obtaining information from enemies when their knowledge is more valuable than immediate execution. Her confrontation with Dantalion was partly motivated by the need to discover what had happened to Lilith.[1]

#### Equipment

##### Marbas' Demonic Claws
During the Greek campaign, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] temporarily equipped Morgana with a pair of Demonic claws originating from Marbas, the former Fifth-Rank Pillar of Hell. Rather than functioning as ordinary weapons, the claws resonated with her supernatural nature and altered several parts of her body, making them more dangerous in close combat. Victor instructed her to combine Dreams, Radiation and the claws into a single fighting strategy.[9]
- Demonic Claw Weapons: The claws provide Morgana with additional supernatural natural weaponry suitable for close combat.[9]
- Demonic Trait Enhancement: The equipment strengthened the visible Demonic characteristics retained within Morgana's existence.[9]
- Enhanced Horns: Her horns became sharper while the claws were equipped.[9]
- Blade-Like Wings: Her wings developed edges sharp enough to resemble blades.[9]
- [[Spears|Spear]]-Like Tail: Her tail became pointed and sharp, allowing it to function as a piercing weapon.[9]
- Enhanced Close-Range Offense: The claws provided an effective method of finishing opponents after using Dreams to confuse them and Radiation to weaken or overpower their defenses.[9]
- Multi-Weapon Combat: Morgana could threaten opponents with claws, wings and tail attacks in addition to her supernatural Energy.[9]
The claws were lent to Morgana through Victor's power and should not be treated as permanently owned equipment unless their continued possession is independently established.

##### Demonic General Armor
Morgana wore black armor reminiscent of her former position as one of Lilith's Generals during the expedition into the [[Greek Underworld]]. The armor reflects her former military identity, although the novel does not provide a comprehensive technical description of its individual enchantments or defensive capabilities.[20]

#### Resistances and Survivability
Morgana possesses exceptional survivability through her current True Dragon physiology, previous supernatural foundations and extensive combat experience. Nevertheless, her personally demonstrated resistances should be distinguished from the full range of abilities theoretically associated with every Dragon, Demon or Vampire.
- Supernatural Durability: Her current True Dragon body possesses the extraordinary physical resilience characteristic of the Race.[3]
- Immortal Physiology: Morgana's existence has endured for thousands of years and subsequently evolved into a True Dragon, granting longevity beyond ordinary mortal limitations.[1][3]
- Regenerative Capacity: Her current Draconic existence incorporates the restorative potential of her supernatural racial foundations, but the exact limits of her individual regeneration have not been independently demonstrated.[2][3]
- Soul Core Stability: Morgana's Soul Core successfully assimilated her former Demonic information into her Vampiric foundation through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Bloodline, resolving the previous incompatibility between the two racial structures.[2]
- Supernatural Power Compatibility: Her True Dragon transformation successfully incorporated Dreams and Radiation without reproducing the incompatibility that had previously limited her Succubus abilities.[3]
- Exceptional Pain Tolerance: Morgana's thousands of years surviving Hell and participating in violent warfare demonstrate her ability to continue fighting despite severe danger and physical suffering.[12][1]
- Psychological Resilience: Morgana has extensive experience confronting terrifying supernatural enemies and surviving repeated defeats. This does not establish absolute immunity to fear or mental interference.[12][5]
- Environmental Combat Adaptability: Morgana can fight in hostile supernatural environments, including Hell, the [[Greek Underworld]] and battlefields devastated by large-scale supernatural attacks.[1][20][7]
- Radiation Compatibility: Morgana can generate and envelop her body in her own destructive Radiation without displaying the immediate self-destructive effects suffered by surrounding enemies. The feat establishes compatibility with her own Energy, not immunity to every conceivable form of Radiation.[1][7]

##### Limitations
- Anti-Dragon Vulnerability: Morgana belongs to the True Dragon Race and is consequently subject to the racial vulnerabilities associated with Anti-Dragon properties. Her individual protection against specialized Anti-Dragon weapons has not been separately quantified.[21]

#### Power Progression

##### High-Level Succubus
Morgana's original state was that of a High-Level Succubus and eventually a General of Lilith. Dreams were her principal supernatural specialty, and she possessed enough power and combat experience to contend with [[Bael]] and defeat five Demon Dukes during her career in Hell.[12][1]
Her Dream abilities were at their most naturally efficient during this period, including the temporary materialization of Dreams and the extraordinary circumstances surrounding Elizabeth's creation.[4][13]

##### Noble Vampire
Vlad's transformation gave Morgana a complete mortal-type body and Soul while awakening the destructive Radiation Bloodline. The transformation strengthened her mortal supernatural foundation but reduced the efficiency of her original Dream abilities.[1][2]
During this period she possessed the mutated Demonic Vampire Count Transformation, including multiple stages that progressively strengthened her Demonic characteristics and supernatural power.[10][1]

##### Demonic Vampire
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Progenitor Blood transformed Morgana into the first known Demonic Vampire, a Noble Vampire subspecies whose Vampiric foundation had completely subsumed her former Demonic nature. The process preserved her supernatural Demonic Traits while resolving their former incompatibility with her Noble Vampire Soul Core.[6][2]
Her Dream-related abilities subsequently displayed improvement, including illusion-based concealment capable of initially deceiving Jeanne.[14]

##### Dream and Radiation Dragon
Morgana's current and most advanced confirmed racial state is a genuine True Dragon whose Domains are Dreams and Radiation. Both attributes were fused into her Draconic foundation and became stronger, giving her an integrated supernatural existence that incorporated the principal powers developed throughout her previous lives.[3]
Her later demonstrations during [[WarFall]] established the extraordinary offensive capabilities of her Radiation, including repeated nuclear-scale attacks, while her continued martial training beneath [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] demonstrated that her technical development remained an ongoing process rather than ending with her racial transformation.[7][16][8]
