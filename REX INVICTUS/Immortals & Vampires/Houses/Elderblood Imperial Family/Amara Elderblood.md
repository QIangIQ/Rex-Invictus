Amara Elderblood, formerly Amara Alucard, is an Elder World Tree of Positivity, the original Positive World Tree of [[Nightingale]], the older sister and natural counterpart of [[Roxanne Elderblood]], one of [[Victor Elderblood]]'s wives, and the biological mother of [[Amaya Elderblood]]. Originally responsible for sustaining the Positive aspect of Nightingale's supernatural environment, Amara later became permanently integrated into Victor's Soul, where she serves as one of the principal sources of pure Energy supporting his physical body and the increasingly complex structure of his internal existence.[4][5]
As a Positive World Tree, Amara belongs to a species created by [[The Universal Tree]] to establish and maintain the supernatural foundations required for Life. A Positive World Tree ordinarily develops first when a new planet begins forming, providing the nourishment necessary for Nature, biological evolution and the emergence of living civilizations. Its Negative counterpart emerges later, when Life, death, conflict and accumulated emotions have produced the conditions necessary for the development of the planet's spiritual and Negative systems. The two Trees eventually sustain complementary aspects of the same world, allowing the planet to develop both physical and supernatural ecosystems.[5]
Before encountering Victor, Amara resided within Nightingale's Wildlands and possessed a close relationship with the [[Elder Gods Pantheon]]. She was proud of the Divine civilization associated with her territory and frequently boasted to other [[World Trees]] that she possessed an entire Pantheon of Gods who worshiped her. Her arrogance earned her a reputation among her sisters, particularly Aurora, who openly called Amara narcissistic when discussing the World Tree of Nightingale with Victor.[1]
Her original existence changed after Victor defeated the Elder Gods and forcibly incorporated her into his Soul. Separated from Nightingale, Amara initially lost access to the planetary foundation supporting her ordinary World Tree functions. She was placed under Roxanne's supervision and confronted with an environment radically different from the world she had sustained for eons. What initially appeared to be captivity became the foundation of an extraordinary evolution because Victor's Soul already contained Roxanne, whose Negative Energy naturally complemented Amara's Positivity.[6][7]
The continuous interaction between the two World Trees caused their opposing Energies to move toward Balance. This process accelerated Amara's development by thousands of years, allowing her to become an Elder World Tree within an extraordinarily short period. Her humanoid appearance matured considerably, while her supernatural foundation acquired the capacity to nourish an existence vastly more complicated than the planet she originally sustained.[4]
Amara and Roxanne subsequently assumed complementary responsibilities within Victor. Roxanne concentrated principally upon nourishing his Soul and regulating the Negative aspects of his existence, while Amara devoted more of her attention to his physical body and the Positive characteristics of his supernatural foundation. Their continuous Energy production, together with the [[Dragon Heart]], made them three exceptionally important sources of pure supernatural Energy within Victor's existence.[4][5]
Her relationship with Victor eventually became voluntary and permanent. Although he had originally brought her into his Soul against her wishes, Amara subsequently chose to extend her own roots into his deeper spiritual foundation. She understood that reaching the same depth as Roxanne would make separation impossible, yet deliberately completed the process. Their relationship developed into marriage and eventually produced their daughter Amaya, whose existence demonstrated that World Trees could develop an entirely new biological lineage through Victor's extraordinary nature.[3][2]
By the Imperial era, Amara's main body had become integrated with an enormous white stellar structure within Victor's internal cosmos, representing the Positive side of his supernatural system. She and Roxanne continually organized the powers circulating through that environment, supported the development of the larger World Tree network and maintained their connection to Victor's deepest spiritual structures. Her roots eventually extended close enough to his Soul Core that she and Roxanne were identified as two of the only individuals, apart from Victor himself, possessing access to that region.[3][8]
Despite her enormous supernatural importance, Amara's daily life is surprisingly domestic. She remains competitive with Roxanne, frequently complains when her younger sister teases her, openly expresses affection toward Victor and takes an active role in supervising Amaya and the younger members of the family. Her development has therefore transformed her from an isolated and arrogant planetary World Tree into a central member of an enormous family whose responsibilities extend across galaxies.[9]

### Physical Appearance

#### Original Appearance
Amara's original humanoid appearance.
Amara originally appeared as an exceptionally beautiful woman with long golden-blonde hair, luminous golden eyes and fair skin. Her appearance reflected the Positive alignment of her World Tree existence and contrasted with the crimson-red hair and Blood-associated characteristics of Roxanne, her Negative counterpart. Although the two sisters possessed recognizable similarities, their original physical development was different because Amara had matured through the ordinary World Tree system while Roxanne had undergone several accelerated transformations through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[10]
When Amara first approached Victor's family, she immediately recognized Roxanne despite the changes to her younger sister's humanoid body. The extraordinary development Roxanne had undergone surprised Amara, who had expected her younger sister to remain less mature. Her reaction was influenced partly by their familial relationship and partly by her pride as the older Positive World Tree.[10]
Amara's golden hair and eyes remained among her most recognizable characteristics during the period following her first appearance. Her humanoid manifestation possessed the beauty and supernatural presence associated with an ancient World Tree, although the form represented only one part of her existence rather than the totality of her true body.

#### Elder World Tree
Amara underwent a substantial physical transformation after being placed inside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul. The continuous balancing of her Positive Energy with Roxanne's Negativity accelerated her World Tree growth to an extraordinary degree, allowing her to complete development that would ordinarily have required thousands of years.[4]
Her humanoid body became more mature and increasingly resembled Roxanne's. The resemblance was sufficiently pronounced that the narration compared the sisters to twins, with their different hair coloration providing one of the clearest ways to distinguish them.[4]
This similarity did not mean that they were biologically twins. Amara remained the older Positive World Tree, while Roxanne was the younger Negative counterpart. Their comparable appearances resulted from extraordinary supernatural development rather than simultaneous creation.

#### Blood Dragon Appearance
Amara's later Dragon Goddess representation.
Following her integration into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood Dragon lineage, Amara's humanoid manifestation developed characteristics associated with her current hybrid existence. Her appearance retained the elegance and golden coloration associated with her World Tree identity while reflecting the Draconic development of the supernatural foundation to which she was connected.
Her later illustrations depict the more elaborate and imposing appearance associated with a Dragon Goddess. Nevertheless, the fundamental nature of Amara's existence remains that of a Positive World Tree, and her humanoid or Draconic presentation should not be confused with the enormous true body rooted within Victor's Soul.

#### White Energy Manifestation
Amara's most significant later visual transformation occurred when she deliberately extended her roots deeper into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul. The process brought her into direct contact with the Positive supernatural structures forming within his internal cosmos, causing her connection to the corresponding white stellar core to become increasingly pronounced.[3]
As the restructuring intensified, Amara's golden hair transformed into pure white Energy. Roxanne underwent a corresponding change on the Negative side, her crimson hair becoming red Energy. The transformation reflected the increasing integration of the two sisters with the supernatural foundations they were organizing, rather than an ordinary alteration in hair color.[3]
Within Victor's inner cosmos, Amara's genuine World Tree manifested at a scale far exceeding the size of her humanoid projection. Her branches stretched across enormous portions of the environment, while the immense white stellar structure associated with her Positive side became one of the defining features of Victor's evolving internal system.[3]

### Personality
Amara is proud, competitive, intelligent, emotional and occasionally vain, with a personality that changed considerably after she joined [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family. During her original existence on [[Nightingale]], she regarded herself as an especially important World Tree because of the powerful Pantheon surrounding her. Her pride was reinforced by the fact that the Elder Gods depended upon the supernatural environment she maintained, allowing her to regard their worship and the prosperity of their civilization as evidence of her superiority.[1]
Her reputation for narcissism was sufficiently well established that Aurora openly complained about it when discussing her sister from Nightingale. Amara regularly communicated with other Positive [[World Trees]] and used those conversations to boast about the number and power of the Gods associated with her domain. Her behavior reflected not only personal vanity but also the enormous importance World Trees possess within the planetary systems they sustain.[1]
Amara's relationship with Roxanne initially involved the assumption that she, as the older Positive World Tree, should possess greater maturity, experience and authority. This expectation was challenged when she discovered that Roxanne had evolved considerably through her connection with Victor. Roxanne's more mature body, increasing spiritual strength and unusual knowledge left Amara both surprised and jealous, creating a rivalry that continued even after the two began living within the same Soul.[10][4]
The rivalry became particularly intense after Victor captured Amara. Roxanne took considerable pleasure in reversing their expected familial hierarchy, treating her older sister like an irresponsible younger sibling and scolding her whenever she complained or behaved arrogantly. Amara disliked the treatment but struggled to oppose Roxanne because her younger sister possessed greater familiarity with Victor's unusual existence and the supernatural environment surrounding them.[7]
Amara's arrogance was also challenged by her first direct experience of Victor's deeper Soul. After being placed within his internal world, Roxanne warned her not to look upward toward the extraordinary existence visible beyond the surrounding environment. Although Amara recognized the warning as a deliberate attempt to provoke her curiosity, she looked anyway. Her senses could not comprehend what she was observing, and the resulting existential pressure overwhelmed her to the point of collapse.[7]
The experience demonstrated the limits of her knowledge despite her extraordinary age. Amara had spent eons sustaining a planet and communicating with other World Trees, but Victor possessed characteristics that exceeded the ordinary framework through which she understood supernatural existence. Her subsequent development required her to accept that her previous position did not automatically provide an answer to every phenomenon she encountered.[7]
After becoming an Elder World Tree, Amara gradually developed a more affectionate relationship with Victor. She initially complained whenever he used her Positive Energy, expressing annoyance at being treated as a source of supernatural power. However, she continued supplying that Energy while recognizing the enormous benefits the connection provided to her own existence. Roxanne frequently mocked the contradiction between Amara's protests and her actual willingness to help.[4]
Her competitiveness eventually became a source of personal development. When Amara discovered that Roxanne's roots reached much more deeply into Victor's Soul, she refused to accept the difference as permanent. She explained that Positive World Trees could consciously deepen their relationship with the supernatural foundation sustaining them and proceeded to extend her roots until they reached the same depth as Roxanne's.[3]
The decision was especially important because Amara knew that completing the fusion would make separation impossible. She nevertheless accepted the consequence, demonstrating that her relationship with Victor had progressed beyond the involuntary circumstances through which she originally entered his Soul. Although jealousy contributed to her decision, the action also represented a deliberate commitment to the life and family she had acquired.[3]
Amara remains jealous of Roxanne on occasion, but the nature of their rivalry has changed. After their deeper integration, both sisters possessed enormously developed connections to Victor, yet Amara recognized that Roxanne had shared experiences with him that could never be reproduced simply by increasing the depth of her roots. Roxanne had accompanied Victor through fundamental stages of his life and understood parts of his existence that Amara had encountered only after entering his Soul.[9]
This realization did not destroy their relationship. The two sisters continue bickering and teasing one another while working together to sustain Victor. Their Positive and Negative powers are complementary by nature, making cooperation not merely convenient but a fundamental part of the supernatural system in which they now exist.[4][5]
Amara has also become considerably more affectionate toward Victor. By the later Imperial era, she freely refers to him as her husband and participates in the ordinary conversations of the household. She enjoys teasing him and discussing the unusual circumstances surrounding his enormous family, while her initial resentment at being captured has given way to genuine attachment.[9]
Motherhood brought another important change. After giving birth to Amaya, Amara became responsible for raising a daughter whose supernatural potential originated from the unusual combination of World Tree and Blood Dragon characteristics. She takes this responsibility seriously, supervising Amaya's studies and correcting her behavior when necessary.[2]
During a conversation between Amaya and Rosalyn about the possibility of nurturing an entire Galaxy, Amara immediately recognized that the girls were neglecting their lessons. She interrupted them and ordered them to return to their education, demonstrating that her maternal responsibilities remain important despite the enormous supernatural systems she continuously supports.[2]
Amara is also protective of the younger members of the household. She has demonstrated the ability to prevent the girls from perceiving inappropriate situations occurring within Victor's internal world, choosing to shield them rather than allowing their supernatural senses to reveal everything happening around them.[9]
Although she is less associated with formal military command than many of Victor's other wives, Amara possesses considerable intelligence and understands complicated supernatural systems. She can analyze the relationship between Positive and Negative Energy, recognize the significance of unusual artifacts, explain the developmental processes of World Trees and identify how different supernatural phenomena fit into the broader systems supporting Creation.[5]
Her current personality therefore combines the pride and competitiveness that originally defined her with affection, responsibility and an increasingly sophisticated understanding of Victor's existence. She remains capable of jealousy and childish arguments with Roxanne, but she is also one of the most dependable supernatural foundations sustaining the Emperor and his family.

### Background
Amara originated as the Positive World Tree associated with [[Nightingale]], a planet inhabited by Vampires, Monsters, Gods and numerous other supernatural beings. Her creation belonged to the wider process established by the Universal Tree, which plants the seeds responsible for developing the supernatural foundations required by living worlds.[5]
Under the ordinary World Tree cycle, a Positive Tree is the first to mature. It nourishes the developing planet and supports the emergence of Nature and biological evolution. Only after the world accumulates sufficient Life, death, conflict and negative emotions does a corresponding Negative World Tree emerge, allowing the planet's physical and spiritual systems to develop together.[5]
As Nightingale's Positive Tree, Amara occupied the older position within the planetary pair. Her younger counterpart was Roxanne, whose Negative World Tree existence initially developed in a forest associated with Vampire territory. Under conventional circumstances, Amara would have been responsible for guiding her sister through the knowledge and responsibilities necessary for further maturation.[11]
However, Nightingale's unusual political and supernatural circumstances separated the two Trees. Amara developed within the Wildlands and became associated with the Elder Gods, while Roxanne remained comparatively isolated. This separation became more significant after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] encountered Roxanne and caused her to evolve through Progenitor Blood, producing an abnormal developmental path that no longer depended upon the ordinary guidance of her Positive counterpart.[1][11]
Amara remained connected to Nightingale throughout much of the conflict between its supernatural inhabitants. The Elder Gods' desire to remove the Vampires from the planet placed her politically and emotionally closer to their faction, although her fundamental racial responsibilities still concerned the maintenance of the planet rather than the ambitions of any particular civilization.
The destruction of the Elder Gods' previous position eventually removed the foundation upon which Amara had built much of her pride. Victor acquired the power and authority necessary to control Nightingale's supernatural environment and forcibly incorporated Amara into his own Soul, transforming her existence from that of an independent planetary World Tree into a participant in his rapidly developing internal cosmos.[6][7]
The subsequent integration with Roxanne changed Amara's developmental path. Through the continuous interaction of Positivity and Negativity, she reached the Elder World Tree stage much earlier than ordinary growth would have permitted. Her relationship with Victor also developed into a permanent spiritual bond, ultimately resulting in marriage, motherhood and responsibilities extending far beyond the original planetary assignment she had once considered the center of her existence.[4][3]

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Amara was first indirectly introduced during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s investigation of the [[World Trees]] associated with Samar. While speaking with [[Aurora Seraphina Evergreen]], Victor learned that Positive World Trees maintained relationships and communication with one another across enormous distances. Aurora explained that one of her older sisters lived on [[Nightingale]] and frequently informed the other Trees about events occurring there.[1]
Aurora did not speak favorably of this sister's personality. She described her as narcissistic and complained that she regularly boasted about possessing an entire Pantheon of Gods who worshiped her. The description referred to Amara, Nightingale's Positive World Tree, establishing her personality and association with the Elder Gods before she appeared directly in the story.[1]
Amara made her first direct appearance during Victor's efforts to create a new world. While the family was gathered around Nightingale, a woman with golden hair and golden eyes sensed an extraordinary supernatural disturbance originating from her younger sister. She recognized Roxanne's Energy and approached to investigate the situation.[10]
What she discovered challenged her understanding of ordinary World Tree development. Roxanne had become closely connected to Victor and no longer depended upon Nightingale as the principal foundation of her existence. Amara recognized that the relationship between her sister and the Vampire Progenitor was fundamentally different from the conventional connection between a World Tree and its planet.[10]
Amara was also surprised by Roxanne's physical maturation. Her younger sister had acquired an unusually mature humanoid form through Victor's influence, and the difference offended Amara's pride as the older Positive Tree. The encounter established the rivalry that would become increasingly prominent after both sisters entered Victor's Soul.[10]
During the conflict against the Elder Gods, Amara remained connected to the Wildlands and the Divine civilization associated with the region. Although her principal role concerned planetary support rather than direct military command, the defeat of the Elder Gods eventually affected the entire supernatural system surrounding her.[6]
After Victor completed his ascension and consolidated his control over Nightingale, he turned his attention toward the Positive World Tree that had remained beyond his immediate influence. Amara was forcibly removed from her sanctuary and brought under Victor's authority. Rather than personally supervising her adjustment, he instructed Roxanne to deal with her older sister and teach her discipline.[6]
Amara was subsequently placed inside Victor's Soul. The experience immediately revealed that her normal planetary relationship had been severed. She attempted to access her powers and discovered that the connection to Nightingale no longer functioned as it previously had. The realization frightened her because World Trees ordinarily sustain the planets to which they are bound, and Amara believed the loss of her support could cause Nightingale to deteriorate.[7]
Roxanne dismissed much of her concern, explaining that Victor possessed more than enough power to support the planet through his own existence. She then introduced Amara to the internal world in which her main World Tree body had already been rooted for a considerable period.[7]
Roxanne repeatedly warned Amara not to look upward toward the mysterious presence visible above the internal environment. Amara understood that her younger sister was intentionally exploiting her curiosity, but she nevertheless attempted to examine the phenomenon. What she perceived was connected to Victor's deeper spiritual existence, whose qualities exceeded anything she could comprehend through her ordinary World Tree senses.[7]
The encounter caused extreme fear and physical strain. Amara's attempt to analyze the incomprehensible structure overwhelmed her senses, producing bleeding and eventually causing her to collapse. Although she was an ancient Positive World Tree, she had encountered an existence that operated beyond the limits of the supernatural framework she had previously understood.[7]
The episode also revealed an important limitation of her power: extraordinary age, planetary authority and superior perception did not grant the ability to understand every phenomenon contained within Victor's Soul. Amara would need to adapt to an existence fundamentally different from the planet she had once sustained.[7]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Amara's development changed rapidly after she began living within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Her Positive Energy continuously interacted with the Negative Energy produced by Roxanne, causing the two opposing forces to move toward Balance. Rather than destabilizing the environment, the interaction produced a self-correcting process through which Amara's World Tree foundation began evolving at an extraordinary rate.[4]
The consequences were immediate. Development that ordinarily required thousands of years occurred within an extremely short period, and Amara matured into an Elder World Tree. Her humanoid body changed accordingly, becoming more physically developed and resembling Roxanne so closely that the sisters could reasonably be compared to twins.[4]
Amara was astonished by the result. Despite initially resenting the circumstances of her captivity, she recognized that Victor's influence had accelerated her growth far beyond anything she could have accomplished within the ordinary planetary life cycle. Roxanne used the opportunity to mock her complaints, reminding her that the new arrangement benefited her as well as Victor.[4]
Their unusual coexistence also produced a significant change in Victor's Energy economy. [[World Trees]] normally nourish planetary environments, but Roxanne and Amara were both rooted within a living Soul connected to [[Nightingale]] and Victor's developing personal world. Their supernatural production therefore flowed into the same Being, allowing the two complementary sources to support his existence continuously.[4]
Together with the [[Dragon Heart]], the sisters became three exceptionally powerful sources of pure Energy within Victor. Under ordinary circumstances, the continuous replenishment made conventional exhaustion increasingly difficult because his supernatural reserves were being sustained by multiple immense sources rather than by his own regeneration alone.[4]
Amara's growing relationship with Victor also exposed her to the development of new supernatural systems. When Victor began investigating the unusual Dagger associated with Cain, both World Trees participated in the examination because the artifact contained properties associated with Positivity and Negativity.[5]
The Dagger had been affected by the Holy Light of the [[Heavenly Father]] while also carrying the supernatural consequences of the First Murder. These opposing characteristics gave it an unusual relationship with Balance, making it particularly suitable for examination by two World Trees whose fundamental Energies represented complementary aspects of the same cosmic system.[5]
Amara recognized that the Dagger's properties resembled the natural relationship between Positive and Negative World Trees. She explained that the two sides were not simply enemies whose powers canceled one another, but necessary aspects of the larger system responsible for the development and maintenance of living worlds.[5]
The discussion led to a detailed explanation of the World Tree life cycle. A seed planted by the Universal Tree produces a Positive World Tree, which nourishes the developing planet and allows Nature and evolution to begin. After the emergence of living civilizations and the accumulation of death and negative emotions, a Negative World Tree develops and begins supporting the planet's spiritual systems.[5]
Amara explained that Positive World Trees eventually create Heavens for filtered Souls, while Negative World Trees produce corresponding Hells. The development of these spiritual environments provides suitable conditions for Divine Beings and Pantheons to emerge, making World Trees fundamental components of the planetary supernatural ecosystem.[5]
The explanation also clarified why Nightingale's development had become unusual. Roxanne was originally intended to mature alongside Amara within the planet's ordinary World Tree system, but Victor had caused her to merge with him before that process reached its normal conclusion. Amara's own incorporation into Victor subsequently transformed the arrangement even further, placing both sides of the planetary system within one living Soul.[5]
The sisters eventually developed complementary responsibilities. Roxanne concentrated on refining Victor's Soul and managing the Negative aspects of his spiritual existence, while Amara devoted more of her attention to his physical body and the Positive supernatural characteristics supporting it. Their continuous cooperation increased the stability and quality of an existence that was already developing beyond the ordinary limits of the supernatural [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] known to them.[5]
Amara's involvement became especially important when the sisters began examining the accumulated powers within Victor's inner cosmos. Roxanne discovered that certain supernatural abilities could be incorporated into the enormous red structure associated with Negativity, allowing compatible powers to become part of a larger and more organized system.[12][3]
Amara immediately noticed that Roxanne possessed an advantage. Her younger sister's roots had been connected to Victor's Soul much longer and reached more deeply into its fundamental structure. This gave Roxanne easier access to certain powers and allowed her to demonstrate abilities that Amara could not initially reproduce through the shallower connection she possessed.[3]
Jealous of the difference, Amara decided to deepen her own fusion. She explained that consciously extending one's roots into the supernatural foundation supporting the Tree was a basic ability available to Positive World Trees, then began forcing her roots deeper into Victor's Soul.[3]
The process increased Amara's connection to the Positive powers circulating through Victor. As her roots extended farther, the white stellar structure associated with her side of the system became stronger, while her ability to access compatible Energy improved. She eventually reached the same depth as Roxanne, arriving at a point beyond which separation from Victor would no longer be possible.[3]
Roxanne questioned whether Amara understood the consequences. Amara nevertheless continued, accepting the permanent nature of the connection. The decision represented a significant change from her earlier situation because she now chose to remain part of Victor's existence rather than merely enduring a condition imposed upon her.[3]
The sisters then began reorganizing the supernatural powers stored within Victor's Soul. Amara directed positively aligned powers toward the white stellar structure associated with her existence, while Roxanne handled the Negative side. Among the positive characteristics examined were those connected to Michael and other Angelic powers, demonstrating that the system could incorporate attributes acquired from extraordinarily powerful supernatural Beings.[3]
Their experiment eventually produced consequences greater than either had anticipated. The enormous quantity of accumulated power began reorganizing automatically, with compatible characteristics moving toward the corresponding Positive and Negative structures. The process expanded beyond their immediate control, affecting the fundamental organization of Victor's internal cosmos.[3]
The restructuring was powerful enough to injure Victor's physical body and temporarily render him unconscious. Nevertheless, his extraordinary spiritual foundation survived the process, and the sisters continued protecting and restoring him as his internal system settled into a more organized configuration.[3]
When Victor regained consciousness, he discovered that Amara and Roxanne had become deeply integrated with enormous stellar structures inside his Soul. Their true World Tree bodies had expanded to extraordinary proportions, while their humanoid appearances reflected the corresponding changes. Amara's golden hair had become pure white Energy, matching the immense Positive stellar core associated with her existence.[3]
The result permanently changed the relationship between the sisters and Victor. Their powers were no longer functioning merely as independent sources of nourishment; they had become organizing components of the supernatural structures through which his accumulated Energy and abilities operated.[3]
Although Victor recognized the benefits, he also warned the sisters that altering such deep portions of his existence without informing him could create unnecessary risks. Both acknowledged the problem, establishing that their intimate spiritual connection did not eliminate the need for caution when experimenting with powers capable of affecting his entire existence.[3]
Outside these responsibilities, Amara continued developing ordinary relationships with the rest of the family. During Victor's vacation, she participated in a soccer match alongside Kaguya and Ruby against a team consisting of Roxanne, Maria and Bruna. The activity reflected her growing familiarity with the relaxed domestic environment of a household whose members possessed extraordinary supernatural responsibilities but still enjoyed ordinary recreation.[13]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
As the Elderblood family continued expanding, Amara became increasingly integrated into its domestic and parental responsibilities. Her relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually produced [[Amaya Elderblood]], a daughter who inherited extraordinary World Tree characteristics despite the reproductive limitations traditionally associated with Amara's species.[2]
The birth represented an important exception to the ordinary origin of [[World Trees]]. These Beings were normally created through the Universal Tree rather than through biological reproduction between individuals. Victor's extraordinary nature allowed both Amara and Roxanne to produce daughters possessing genuine World Tree characteristics, establishing a new familial lineage within the species.[2]
Amaya grew within the environment maintained by the World Trees inside Victor's personal universe. Together with Rosalyn, Roxanne's daughter, she possessed an exceptional understanding of the relationship between World Trees and the celestial environments they could nourish.[2]
During a conversation with Victor, Rosalyn suggested that she and Amaya could help him nurture an entire Galaxy. Amaya expanded upon the proposal, explaining that a properly developed Galactic ecosystem would nourish Victor in return, creating a reciprocal exchange of supernatural Energy between the World Trees, the environment and their father.[2]
Amara immediately interrupted the conversation after realizing that the two girls had neglected their lessons. She ordered them to return to their education, demonstrating that her role as a mother involved more than admiring their enormous inherited power. She expected them to develop the knowledge and discipline necessary to use their abilities responsibly.[2]
Her relationship with Victor continued becoming increasingly affectionate. By the events of Chapter 1032, Amara and Roxanne openly referred to him as their husband, teasing one another about his behavior and discussing the complicated relationships that had become ordinary within the Elderblood household.[9]
The same period revealed that Amara continued feeling jealousy toward Roxanne despite their comparable structural fusion. Amara recognized that the depth of their roots did not represent the entirety of their relationships with Victor. Roxanne had accompanied him through numerous earlier transformations and possessed shared memories and experiences that could not be reproduced merely through the passage of Time or further supernatural development.[9]
Amara also participated in protecting the younger generation from inappropriate supernatural observations. [[The Universal Tree]] is the Primordial progenitor responsible for creating the World Tree species and is therefore recognized as Amara's father.[5]
Amara's understanding of planetary development originates partly from the supernatural system established through his actions. The Universal Tree plants the seeds that begin the World Tree life cycle, allowing Positive Trees to nurture developing planets before their Negative counterparts emerge.[5]
Although Amara possesses extraordinary power and knowledge, she does not hold the complete administrative authority of her progenitor over Life, Reincarnation and the maintenance of the Universe.

#### Aurora Seraphina Evergreen
[[Aurora Seraphina Evergreen]] is one of Amara's younger World Tree sisters and an early source of information concerning her personality.
Before Amara's direct appearance, Aurora explained that her sister on [[Nightingale]] frequently boasted about possessing a Pantheon of Gods. Aurora regarded the behavior as narcissistic and openly complained about Amara's vanity.[1]
Their relationship also demonstrates the communication network maintained among Positive [[World Trees]] across enormous astronomical distances.

#### The World Trees
Amara belongs to the enormous family of [[World Trees]] originating from the Universal Tree. Their individual planetary assignments differ, but their shared racial foundation gives them a common responsibility for maintaining living worlds.[5]
Amara's current relationship with the species is unusual because she no longer depends upon an independent planetary root system. Her main body is permanently connected to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], and her responsibilities have become part of the larger network supporting the [[Elderblood Empire]].[3][17]
Her Positive specialization complements Roxanne's Negative role, allowing the two sisters to participate in supervising World Trees whose supernatural functions remain connected to their original planetary alignments.[15]

#### Elder Gods Pantheon
The [[Elder Gods Pantheon]] was the Divine civilization associated with Amara's original territory on [[Nightingale]].
Amara regarded their existence as an important source of personal pride and frequently informed other [[World Trees]] that she possessed an entire Pantheon of Gods worshiping her.[1]
Her relationship with the Elder Gods ended after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] defeated their faction and removed her from Nightingale. She subsequently became part of the Elderblood family and no longer occupied her former position as the independent Positive World Tree supporting their Divine civilization.[6]

### Role in the Elderblood Empire
Amara occupies an important position within the [[Elderblood Empire]] because her responsibilities concern the supernatural infrastructure supporting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s existence. Although she possesses enormous power, her greatest contributions are associated with continuous Energy production, physical refinement, spiritual integration and the maintenance of Positive World Tree systems.

#### Nurturing Victor's Physical Existence
Amara's principal responsibility is the continuous nourishment and refinement of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s physical body. While Roxanne concentrates more heavily upon his Soul, Amara channels Positive Energy into the physical foundation sustaining his increasingly extraordinary supernatural powers.[5]
This process operates continuously through her roots rather than requiring her to remain physically beside him. Her Energy helps support the body that contains Victor's enormous spiritual foundation and contributes to his recovery when severe supernatural changes place strain upon his physical existence.[5][3]

#### Positive Energy Infrastructure
Amara is one of three exceptional internal sources of pure Energy supporting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], alongside Roxanne and the [[Dragon Heart]]. The combination provides an extraordinary level of supernatural replenishment that allows him to perform operations exceeding the ordinary resources of most Gods and Dragons.[4]
However, the later Imperial era demonstrates that their output is not unlimited. Amara remains a critical contributor during enormous cosmic rituals, but operations involving entire Sectors can consume Energy more rapidly than even the collective World Tree network can replace it.[16]

#### Internal Cosmic Organization
Following the restructuring of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s inner cosmos, Amara became responsible for the Positive side of its developing supernatural system. Her white stellar core serves as a principal structure through which compatible powers can be integrated and organized.[3]
This responsibility is essential because Victor possesses an enormous collection of supernatural characteristics acquired through different [[Races (Flamel)|Races]], Divine powers and absorbed enemies. Without a coherent internal structure, the accumulated powers would remain considerably more difficult to organize and utilize efficiently.
Amara and Roxanne therefore function as complementary organizers of his internal Energy system, with each concentrating upon characteristics compatible with her respective alignment.[3]

#### World Tree Network
As the Empire expanded, [[World Trees]] became increasingly important to its planetary and communication infrastructure. The supernatural connections established among them eventually allowed entire Galactic territories to communicate through a shared network maintained by their roots.[17]
Amara remains one of the principal Positive World Trees connected to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], providing support to the wider system while continuing to fulfill her personal responsibilities within his Soul.
Her importance therefore extends beyond [[Nightingale]] and its former Pantheon. She now contributes to a civilization whose supernatural infrastructure spans enormous numbers of planets and galaxies.

### Legacy
Amara's existence demonstrates the fundamental role played by Positive [[World Trees]] in the development of inhabited planets. Her explanation of the World Tree life cycle establishes that these Beings are not merely sources of supernatural Energy, but essential components of the processes through which Nature, evolution, afterlife systems and Divine civilizations emerge.[5]
Her relationship with Roxanne provides one of the clearest examples of the complementary relationship between Positivity and Negativity. Rather than representing powers that must destroy one another, the two aspects operate together to sustain Life and maintain equilibrium. Their interaction inside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul accelerated Amara's evolution and eventually contributed to the organization of his increasingly complex supernatural existence.[4][3]
Amara also represents a major exception to the ordinary relationship between a World Tree and its planet. She abandoned her original connection to [[Nightingale]] and deliberately established an irreversible bond with a living Being whose Soul contained an expanding cosmos. This transformed her from an independent planetary guardian into a permanent component of Victor's existence.[3]
The birth of Amaya further expanded the significance of her development. Through Victor, Amara became the biological mother of a genuine World Tree, creating a familial relationship that did not originate through the normal method by which the Universal Tree establishes new planetary Trees.[2]
By the later Imperial era, Amara's importance extends across several distinct responsibilities: the nourishment of Victor's physical body, the production of pure Positive Energy, the organization of the Positive characteristics of his inner cosmos, the supervision of the younger generation and participation in the World Tree network supporting the Empire.[3][17]
Her greatest personal development, however, concerns the transformation of her relationship with Victor and Roxanne. What began through resentment, captivity and sibling rivalry eventually became a permanent familial bond, allowing an ancient World Tree who had once measured her worth through the Gods surrounding her to find a different place within a family whose existence had grown beyond the limits of her original world.
