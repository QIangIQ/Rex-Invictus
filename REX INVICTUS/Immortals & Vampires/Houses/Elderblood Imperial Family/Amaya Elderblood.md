### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 1007
Amaya Elderblood is the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Amara Elderblood]], making her a unique descendant of both Victor's Blood Dragon lineage and the World Tree race descended from [[The Universal Tree]].[1]
Unlike ordinary [[World Trees]], which normally arise in connection with a developing planet, Amaya was born as a World Tree directly from Victor and Amara. She consequently possesses a far more unusual relationship with her father than conventional planetary World Trees. Together with [[Rosalyn Elderblood]], she resides primarily within Victor's Soul and is naturally linked to his Inner World, although the two daughters possess substantially greater freedom of movement than their mothers.[2]
Amaya first appeared when she and Rosalyn contacted Victor while he was considering the enormous scale of his Energy reserves. Rosalyn proposed that they could help Victor nurture an entire Galaxy, while Amaya immediately understood the reciprocal effect such a system would create: the Galaxy would subsequently nourish Victor in return, producing a continuously self-sustaining Energy cycle.[1]
Although extremely young by the standards of her species, Amaya is already more developed than an ordinary World Tree of the same age because of her birth as Victor's daughter. Even so, she is still considered an immature World Tree compared with her mother and the ancient planetary Trees elsewhere in the Universe.[2]

### Physical Appearance
Amaya appears as a miniature version of her mother, [[Amara Elderblood]]. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] called Amaya and Rosalyn out of his Soul to participate in his War Games, the narration described the two girls as essentially smaller copies of their respective mothers.[2]
Amaya therefore closely resembles Amara in her humanoid form. Her exact height, clothing style, hair length, and additional Draconic traits have not yet been described in sufficient detail to establish a complete physical profile.
Her young appearance reflects her developmental stage as a newly born World Tree rather than the mature adult forms displayed by older [[World Trees]] such as Amara, [[Yggdrasil]], or [[Aurora Seraphina Evergreen]].

### Personality
Amaya is cheerful, intelligent, affectionate, mischievous, and deeply attached to her father.
Her first appearance immediately demonstrates her analytical nature. When Rosalyn suggested that the two of them could help [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] nurture a Galaxy, Amaya did not simply agree; she immediately recognized the larger energetic consequence and explained that the nurtured Galaxy could then nourish Victor in return, creating a perpetual feedback system.[1]
This indicates that even while still extremely young, Amaya instinctively understands the fundamental symbiotic principle upon which [[World Trees]] operate: a World Tree nourishes a larger living system, while that system provides Energy and environmental support in return.
Amaya is also highly enthusiastic regarding Victor. When Victor later assumed an enormous Dragon Form that grew beyond the scale of an entire Solar System, Amaya and Rosalyn reacted with excitement rather than fear. Amaya exclaimed in admiration at her father while Rosalyn immediately instructed her to record everything so that they could show their sisters.[3]
Like many of Victor's daughters, Amaya strongly prefers remaining close to him whenever possible. When Victor ordered her and Rosalyn to leave the Inner World of his Soul and participate in the War Games with their sisters, both girls were visibly unhappy because they wanted to remain inside their father's Soul instead.[2]
She also possesses a mischievous side. During her debut, Amaya and Rosalyn were supposed to be attending lessons but had instead contacted Victor. Their conversation ended when Amara discovered that both girls were skipping their studies and immediately ordered them to return.[1]
Despite possessing intelligence far beyond an ordinary child, Amaya therefore still behaves like a young daughter around her parents and sisters.

### Nature

#### Born World Tree
Amaya differs fundamentally from many naturally developed [[World Trees]] because she was born as a World Tree from the beginning.[2]
Ordinary World Trees emerge through planetary processes or mature through long periods of development. Amaya instead inherited World Tree nature directly from [[Amara Elderblood]] while simultaneously being born as one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s biological daughters.
This gives her a hybrid existence that does not completely follow the normal developmental model of her species.

#### Positive World Tree Lineage
Amara is a Positive World Tree, meaning Amaya inherits the Positive branch of the World Tree family through her mother.
This places her opposite [[Rosalyn Elderblood]], whose mother [[Roxanne Elderblood]] belongs to the Negative World Tree branch.
The pair consequently reflect the World Tree duality within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s second generation:
- [[Amara Elderblood]] → Amaya Elderblood — Positive World Tree lineage.
- Roxanne Elderblood → Rosalyn Elderblood — Negative World Tree lineage.
Unlike ordinary Positive and Negative [[World Trees]], however, Amaya and Rosalyn were not independently produced by a planet. They were born as sisters within the Elderblood family.

#### Connection to Victor's Soul
Amaya's natural home is within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul and Inner World.[2]
Because she is a World Tree, she cannot fully separate herself from Victor in the same manner as an ordinary independent person. However, her connection is considerably less restrictive than the bond possessed by Amara and Roxanne.
Victor's older World Tree wives possess a much deeper integration with him because they actively help sustain his Soul and its internal system. Amaya and Rosalyn do not yet perform that same essential function, allowing them significantly greater freedom of movement outside Victor's Inner World.[2]

#### Developmental Stage
Despite Amaya's extraordinary heritage, she remains extremely young by World Tree standards.
During Chapter 1051, she and Rosalyn were described as being little more than immature sprouts from the perspective of their species. They had not yet accumulated the tremendous Energy reserves expected from mature [[World Trees]].[2]
However, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters were simultaneously described as being far more developed than normal World Trees of equivalent age.[2]
This means Amaya's current state combines two apparently contradictory traits:
- She is extremely immature compared with adult World Trees.
- She is exceptionally advanced compared with ordinary World Trees of her own age.

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Amaya first appeared while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was training with [[Kali Elderblood]]. After Victor absorbed the excess Energy generated by Kali, he noted that the entire amount had restored only approximately three percent of his reserves. Kali was stunned and asked Victor how much Energy he actually possessed, but Victor admitted that even he did not know how to accurately quantify his total capacity.[1]
While Victor considered the question, [[Rosalyn Elderblood]] suddenly contacted him mentally and suggested that she and Amaya could help him nurture the entire Galaxy.[1]
Amaya immediately developed the concept further. She explained that if they nurtured the Galaxy, the Galaxy would eventually nourish Victor in return. The resulting exchange would function as a continuous cycle in which Victor supplied Energy to the Galactic system while receiving Energy back from the living environment he had empowered.[1]
For Amaya, the idea was simple enough that she concluded there was effectively nothing her father could not accomplish.
The conversation also demonstrated how naturally Amaya and Rosalyn could communicate with Victor from within his Soul. Neither daughter needed to physically appear beside him to participate in the discussion.[1]
Before Victor could properly respond, Amara suddenly entered the conversation and discovered that both daughters were skipping their lessons. She immediately demanded that Amaya and Rosalyn return, abruptly ending their attempt to help their father.[1]
Amaya later appeared while Victor experimented with the maximum scale of his Dragon Form. Victor expanded until his body became several times larger than [[Jupiter]] and continued growing until his size surpassed an entire Solar System. From his perspective, planets resembled small objects that could easily be manipulated.[3]
Amaya and Rosalyn watched the transformation and simultaneously expressed their admiration. Rosalyn immediately ordered Amaya to begin recording Victor so they could show their sisters later, and Amaya enthusiastically agreed.[3]
Amaya later became more directly involved with the rest of Victor's daughters when he organized War Games as a mixture of play and training. Initially, Amaya and Rosalyn remained inside Victor's Soul while their sisters gathered physically around him.[2]
Victor specifically called the two World Tree daughters out, telling them they could not remain "at home" while everyone else participated. Amaya and Rosalyn then manifested before him as miniature copies of Amara and Roxanne. Both responded reluctantly because they preferred remaining inside their father's Soul.[2]
The scene revealed additional information regarding their nature. Because Amaya and Rosalyn were born as [[World Trees]], they remained naturally connected to Victor and could not become completely independent from him. Their connection was nevertheless less restrictive than that of Amara and Roxanne, allowing the girls far greater autonomy outside Victor's Inner World.[2]
Victor also acknowledged that the girls were still immature by World Tree standards. They had not yet developed the massive internal Energy reserves associated with mature Trees and did not need to sustain either Victor's Soul or a planet. However, because they were Victor's daughters, they had developed significantly faster than ordinary World Trees born at the same stage.[2]
Amaya subsequently joined the War Games alongside the rest of her sisters, marking one of the first occasions where she was deliberately pushed away from the security of Victor's Inner World so that she could interact and develop more independently.[2]

### Skills & Abilities
World Tree–Blood Dragon Hybrid Physiology: Amaya is the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Amara Elderblood]]. Her existence combines Victor's supernatural Draconic lineage with the World Tree nature inherited from Amara.[1]
Because Amaya remains extremely young and has not yet received a dedicated combat sequence, her article should distinguish between powers she has actually demonstrated and broader abilities she may inherit later.

#### World Tree Abilities
- Galactic Nurturing: Amaya can participate with Rosalyn in nurturing an entire Galaxy for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[1] The statement establishes that her developing World Tree functions can already be applied on a scale vastly larger than that of an ordinary planetary ecosystem.
- Energy Feedback Creation: Amaya recognized that a Galaxy nurtured through World Tree Energy could subsequently nourish Victor in return, creating a continuous reciprocal Energy cycle.[1]
- World Tree Nourishment: Amaya possesses the fundamental nurturing nature inherent to [[World Trees]]. Even at her young age, she can contribute to the development and energetic support of a much larger living system.[1]
- Positive World Tree Lineage: Through [[Amara Elderblood]], Amaya belongs to the Positive branch of the World Tree family.
- Mental Communication: Amaya can communicate directly with Victor through their spiritual connection without physically manifesting beside him.[1]
- Remote Observation: Amaya can observe events involving Victor while remaining connected to his Inner World, as demonstrated when she and Rosalyn watched his enormous Dragon transformation.[3]
- External Manifestation: Although naturally connected to Victor's Soul, Amaya can leave the Inner World and physically manifest outside it. Victor explicitly ordered her and Rosalyn to do so before the War Games.[2]
- Autonomous Movement: Amaya possesses substantially more freedom of movement than her mother. Unlike Amara, who is deeply integrated into Victor's Soul and participates directly in sustaining its structure, Amaya can operate outside the Inner World with considerably fewer restrictions.[2]
- Accelerated Development: Amaya is significantly more developed than an ordinary World Tree of equivalent age because she is Victor's daughter.[2]
- World Tree Instinctive Knowledge: Amaya naturally understands the relationship between World Trees, their environments and reciprocal Energy exchange despite being young enough to still require formal lessons.[1]

#### Current Limitations
Amaya has not yet reached the maturity of an adult World Tree.
By World Tree standards, she remains a young sprout whose internal Energy reserves have not yet developed into the enormous quantities associated with adult and Elder Trees.[2]
She also does not yet perform the same structural function as Amara. Amaya is not responsible for sustaining [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul or an independent planet, which is one of the reasons she possesses more freedom of movement than her mother.[2]

#### Blood Dragon Heritage
As [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s biological daughter, Amaya belongs to the Elderblood second generation and carries his Draconic lineage.
However, the novel has not yet shown a dedicated scene in which Amaya personally uses:
- Draconic Breath.
- Draconic Runes.
- A full Dragon Transformation.
- A named Divinity.
- Divine Blessing or Curse Bestowal.
- A personal combat technique.
These abilities should therefore not be attributed individual feats until Amaya demonstrates them directly.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Amaya's father and the central figure of her life.
Amaya is deeply attached to him and shows enormous confidence in his potential. During her debut, she concluded that there was effectively nothing Victor could not accomplish once she considered how World Tree nourishment could expand his Energy system.[1]
She also strongly prefers remaining inside Victor's Soul. When Victor told her and Rosalyn that they needed to leave their "home" and participate in the War Games, both girls were visibly reluctant because they wanted to continue staying within him.[2]
Amaya's connection to Victor is both emotional and biological. Her World Tree nature makes Victor's Soul her natural environment, while her identity as his daughter makes that connection considerably more personal than the normal relationship between a World Tree and a planet.

#### Amara Elderblood
[[Amara Elderblood]] is Amaya's biological mother and the source of her World Tree heritage.
Amaya physically resembles Amara so strongly that she is described as a miniature copy of her mother.[2]
Amara is also involved in Amaya's education. During Chapter 1007, she discovered that Amaya and Rosalyn were skipping their lessons and immediately ordered both girls to return.[1]
Unlike Amaya, Amara is deeply integrated into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul and actively participates in sustaining its internal structure. This gives Amaya greater physical autonomy than her mother despite both being [[World Trees]].[2]

#### Rosalyn Elderblood
[[Rosalyn Elderblood]] is Amaya's sister and the daughter of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and [[Roxanne Elderblood]].
The two are extremely close and frequently appear together. Rosalyn first proposes the Galactic nurturing plan in Chapter 1007, while Amaya immediately expands the idea by explaining the reciprocal Energy cycle it would create.[1]
They also skip lessons together, communicate with Victor together, observe his Dragon transformation together, and manifest from his Soul together when participating in the War Games.[1][3][2]
Their World Tree heritages are complementary. Amaya descends from Amara's Positive lineage, while Rosalyn descends from Roxanne's Negative lineage.

#### The Universal Tree
[[The Universal Tree]] is the Progenitor and creator of the World Tree race and therefore Amaya's maternal ancestor through Amara.
Amaya differs from The Universal Tree's directly created daughters because she belongs to a later biological generation produced through the union of a World Tree and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
