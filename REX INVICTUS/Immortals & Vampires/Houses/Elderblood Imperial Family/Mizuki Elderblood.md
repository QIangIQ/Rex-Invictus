Mizuki Elderblood, born Aikyo Mizuki, is a former General of The Inquisition, a master Onmyōji, a Blood Dragon Goddess, and one of the wives of Victor Elderblood. Originally introduced as one of the most dangerous Human hunters opposing the newly transformed Victor, Mizuki was an experienced supernatural combatant and disciple of the Heroic Spirit [[Abe-no-Seimei]].[1][2] Her relationship with Victor gradually changed from hostility to reluctant cooperation, friendship and attraction; she eventually became one of his instructors, formally entered the Elderblood family through an Onmyō-based ritual and [[Soul Marriage]], and later accepted transformation into Victor's Blood Dragon Race when doing so could strengthen rather than invalidate the supernatural arts she had spent her life developing.[3][4][5][6]
Mizuki's life is defined heavily by her childhood experiences with predatory Vampires and by the gradual destruction of the absolute worldview those experiences initially created. Her hatred of Vampires originated from genuine trauma rather than religious doctrine, yet over time she became increasingly unwilling to condemn entire races for the actions of individuals. Her eventual place beside Victor consequently represents one of the clearest examples of a former enemy becoming part of the Elderblood Clan without Mizuki abandoning the principles that originally motivated her to become a hunter.[7][8][9][10]

### Physical Appearance
Mizuki is a tall, exceptionally attractive Japanese woman with long black hair, black eyes and a mature, athletic physique developed through more than a century of supernatural combat and Onmyōjutsu training. During her Human years, her body visibly reflected that history. By Chapter 311 she possessed a strongly conditioned abdomen and numerous old battle scars, including three scars across her abdomen and six across her back, two of which originated from previous stabbing wounds.[7] These scars were not permanent, however. After Mizuki later consumed [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood, the restorative properties within it returned her body to its physical prime, rejuvenated her cells and healed the accumulated damage to the point that [[Abe-no-Seimei]] described her body as restored and scar-free.[8]
Victor's Blood also temporarily altered several of Mizuki's physical characteristics without actually transforming her into a Vampire. Her eyes could become blood-red and her body acquired greatly increased vitality and limited characteristics associated with Victor's Blood, but Mizuki remained Human during this stage.[11][8]
After becoming a Blood Dragon, Mizuki acquired the overt Draconic characteristics of Victor's Race, including horns and other traits associated with her Dragon state. She is nevertheless sufficiently skilled with the shapeshifting inherited from the Vampire side of the Blood Dragon Race to suppress these characteristics almost completely. When meeting Victor in the mountains near Kyoto, Mizuki deliberately concealed her Dragon nature: her eyes returned to black, her horns disappeared, and her usually loose long hair was tied in a modest style while she wore a traditional kimono. In that state she appeared externally like an ordinary—albeit extraordinarily beautiful—Japanese woman rather than a supernatural Dragon Goddess.[9]

### Personality
Mizuki is disciplined, proud of her own accomplishments, blunt and strongly independent. Decades of hunting supernatural beings made her highly practical in dangerous situations, and she has little patience for incompetence or behavior she considers foolish. Her seriousness is frequently contrasted with the eccentric personalities around [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]; teasing from Victor, Fred or Abe can quickly provoke irritation or embarrassment, although this becomes increasingly playful after she grows comfortable around them.[12][13][8] Beneath this severity, Mizuki is naturally curious and considerably less emotionally rigid when she feels safe. Victor notices this directly in Chapter 611 when a relaxed Mizuki secretly watches his parents' conversation simply because she finds it interesting, revealing a side of her personality that her former identity as an Inquisition General rarely allowed others to see.[4]
The central principle behind Mizuki's morality is the protection of people who cannot defend themselves, particularly children. Although her childhood initially caused her to regard Vampires as monsters that should be exterminated, she never derived satisfaction simply from killing members of another Race. This becomes explicit after leaving the Inquisition. Mizuki condemns its experimentation on Humans and Vampires, recruitment and indoctrination of children, and attempts to justify atrocities as necessary for humanity's protection; she tells Abe that nothing can justify the deaths of children.[7] Around the same period she becomes unable to kill an infant Noble Vampire after killing its father, arguing that the child had done nothing personally to deserve execution. Abe ultimately kills the child himself, causing a serious ideological dispute between master and disciple.[7]
Mizuki is also highly capable of changing her beliefs when experience proves them wrong. She openly recognizes that her earlier view of the supernatural world was naive and that Humanity is no more inherently righteous than the [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] she once hunted. Her disillusionment with the Inquisition affected her deeply because she had believed she was serving an organization protecting innocent Humans; discovering laboratories in which the organization itself experimented on children left her ashamed and prone to blaming herself for having been deceived. Abe explicitly observes that she punishes herself excessively over this failure.[8] Her experiences with Victor and his family gradually teach her to judge individuals by their actions rather than their species, although this growth does not erase her memories or automatically eliminate the resentment created by her childhood.[7][14][4]
Despite being emotionally guarded, Mizuki possesses considerable vulnerability concerning her past. She attempted to destroy traces of what happened during her childhood and feared that Victor would regard her with disgust if he learned what she had been forced to do to survive. When Victor eventually revealed that he knew, Mizuki briefly fell into despair before he assured her that survival as a powerless child was not something he would condemn. His complete refusal to judge her became an important turning point in their relationship; Mizuki openly thanked him, embraced him and accepted that she no longer had to confront those memories entirely alone.[4] Victor's acceptance is particularly meaningful because Mizuki understands that he himself is capable of extreme violence yet still retains firm personal principles.
Mizuki is notably pragmatic concerning Race and power. Contrary to the assumption that her refusal to become a Vampire came from pride in Humanity, Mizuki explicitly states that she is not proud to be Human. She rejected Vampirism because she did not wish to spend eternity dependent upon Blood and, more importantly, because changing into a Vampire would make the Onmyō techniques she had trained throughout her life incompatible with her new physiology, effectively weakening her by invalidating her greatest expertise. Becoming a Blood Dragon presented the opposite situation: Dragons possess vastly stronger bodies and enormous Energy affinity while Victor's Race can adapt to many Energy systems, allowing Mizuki to preserve and improve her existing path. She therefore accepted the transformation despite [[Abe-no-Seimei]]'s reservations.[6] Her memories also lead her to bluntly acknowledge that Humans are sometimes capable of behavior worse than Noble Vampires or Demons, demonstrating how completely her mature worldview differs from the one that originally led her into the Inquisition.[6]

### Background
Mizuki was born as Aikyo Mizuki around 1893 to an impoverished farming family living in the mountains near Kyoto. Although her family possessed very little materially, Mizuki remembered her early childhood as happy and secure. That life ended in 1899, when she was six years old and a Vampire attacked her family. Her father was murdered, her mother was brutalized, and Mizuki was abducted and taken to the estate of a Japanese Noble Vampire family.[9]
Mizuki discovered that numerous other children had been abducted and kept inside the estate. The Vampires treated their captives as disposable prey and Mizuki spent approximately three years observing children disappear as they grew older. During this period she became close to another captive named Koyuki, whose friendly behavior caused Mizuki and many of the other children to trust him. Rather than remaining passive, however, Mizuki carefully studied the routines of every Vampire in the mansion. By the age of nine she had memorized their schedules sufficiently well to discover a recurring opportunity to escape when the Noble Vampire responsible for the estate was absent.[9][10]
Mizuki made the mistake of revealing the escape plan to Koyuki and asking him to flee with her. Koyuki betrayed her, attacked her from behind and revealed that his apparent kindness had concealed his willingness to cooperate with their captors for his own benefit. Desperation triggered Mizuki's latent supernatural affinity, allowing her to instinctively use Faith without a prepared talisman and escape despite being seriously wounded. She fled through the surrounding forest until collapsing at a temple connected to the descendants of the legendary Onmyōji [[Abe-no-Seimei]], whose Heroic Spirit was present there.[10]
Abe recognized Mizuki's exceptional affinity for Onmyōjutsu and became her master. Mizuki devoted herself obsessively to training, using the supernatural arts both to survive and to ensure that she would never again be helpless before beings like the Vampires who destroyed her family. After reaching adulthood she returned for those responsible for her childhood, destroyed the Noble Vampire organization involved and personally killed Koyuki, who had himself become a Vampire servant by that time.[10] Her campaign eventually expanded until Mizuki and Abe had eradicated ten Japanese Noble Vampire families.[1][8]
Believing that The Inquisition genuinely existed to protect Humanity from predatory supernatural beings, Abe encouraged Mizuki to broaden her horizons by joining the organization. Mizuki eventually became one of its four Generals and one of the most dangerous Human hunters operating internationally.[1][8] The relationship ended when Mizuki discovered that the organization she trusted was itself conducting experiments on Humans—including children—and creating artificial supernatural abominations. She destroyed facilities connected to those experiments and abandoned the Inquisition, losing access to the organization's support systems and returning to independent hunting alongside Abe.[7][8]

### Synopsis

#### The Awakening
Mizuki is first mentioned during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s earliest encounter with The Inquisition. Jimmy identifies her as one of the organization's most dangerous Generals, an experienced Japanese hunter capable of confronting Vampires centuries older than herself and already notorious for destroying multiple Noble Vampire families in Japan.[1] She physically enters the story as part of the Inquisition force that corners Ruby Scarlett and Luna while searching for Lucy. When Victor senses Ruby's injuries and arrives in his first Count-like transformation, his enormous raw power initially overwhelms the ordinary hunters, but his almost complete lack of combat experience leaves him vulnerable to Mizuki's vastly superior technique.[15]
Mizuki combines Onmyōjutsu, talismans, blessings and the support of [[Abe-no-Seimei]] to fight Victor, successfully injuring and poisoning him with attacks specifically effective against supernatural beings. At one point she comes close to finishing the newborn Vampire before Kaguya intervenes and wounds her. The confrontation expands when Violet and Sasha arrive; with Victor already badly injured and Sasha attacking at extreme speed, Mizuki and the surviving hunters are ultimately unable to prevent the Vampires from escaping. Abe also recognizes that prolonging the battle risks attracting intervention from [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett|Scathach Scarlett]].[2][16] The incident indirectly provokes [[Scathach]]'s retaliation against the Vatican after she learns that the hunters injured Ruby.[17]

#### Vampire Society
Mizuki does not take an active role during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s six-month stay in [[Nightingale]]. The significance of their first confrontation nevertheless changes dramatically during this period: Victor enters Nightingale as an inexperienced newborn whom Mizuki could nearly kill through superior technique and leaves as [[Scathach]]'s disciple and the newly recognized Fifth Vampire Count. Their next meeting consequently occurs under completely different circumstances.

#### A New Pillar
Mizuki returns to the Human World storyline when she pursues Fred, whose growing involvement with supernatural affairs attracts the attention of both hunters and Onmyōji. She demonstrates the continued breadth of her techniques while trying to prevent Fred from simply escaping the supernatural consequences surrounding him.[18] She subsequently reunites with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Edward, Leona and Fred after Victor's return from [[Nightingale]]. Mizuki has difficulty reconciling the absurdly powerful Fifth Count standing before her with the inexperienced Vampire she nearly killed only months earlier, and becomes even more incredulous when Victor confirms that he had actually been an ordinary Human only a short time before their original battle.[19][20]
Mizuki and Abe also become important to Fred's own entry into the supernatural world. While Mizuki explains aspects of Faith and the Hunter system, Fred rapidly loses enthusiasm for joining the Church once he learns about its restrictions. Abe instead offers to teach him Onmyōjutsu, placing Fred on a path that eventually makes Mizuki one of his primary teachers.[12][13] During these interactions Mizuki's hostility toward Victor is already becoming far less straightforward; she is irritated by his constant teasing but also admits that he resembles her ideal type of man.[13]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Mizuki's largest personal development before the Demonic War begins after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] encounters her badly wounded inside an abandoned church. Having broken with the Inquisition, she is once again hunting dangerous supernatural beings independently and has lost much of the institutional support that previously sustained her body. Their conversation reveals both the reason for her defection and how much her worldview has changed: Mizuki condemns the Inquisition's Human experimentation and exploitation of children, while simultaneously arguing with Abe over whether innocent Vampire children deserve death simply because they might become threats in the future.[7] Victor values precisely this willingness to question absolute answers and treats Mizuki as an ally rather than the enemy who once nearly killed him.
Mizuki's injuries have also been contaminated by Demonic miasma that her normal recovery cannot properly remove. Victor ultimately gives her his Blood, purging the contamination and rejuvenating her body without converting her into a Vampire.[21][11] The experience further destabilizes Mizuki's old prejudices because she now owes her life and renewed physical condition to the very Vampire she had once tried to kill. Victor deliberately encourages her to spend more time around Vampires and other supernatural beings rather than judging an entire Race by its worst members, and Mizuki increasingly accepts that the world is far more morally complicated than the doctrine she once followed.[14]
Mizuki subsequently contributes her knowledge as a hunter and Onmyōji to Victor's growing circle. Her technical knowledge contributes to Victor's development and to the initial weapon that eventually becomes [[Junketsu]], while she continues expanding her own incomplete Onmyō training under Abe.[3] By Chapter 366 Abe recognizes how greatly she has changed, describing her earlier self as naive while also criticizing her tendency to blame herself for having trusted the Inquisition. Victor's Blood has returned Mizuki to her physical prime and broadened the range of techniques she can realistically pursue, prompting Abe to begin teaching her areas of Onmyōjutsu associated with his old rival Ashiya Dōman.[8]
Later, Mizuki's path crosses directly with [[Scathach]]. The ancient Countess naturally treats meeting another capable fighter as an excuse for training, testing Mizuki's skills and forcing her to demonstrate increasingly advanced Onmyō techniques.[22] Mizuki employs techniques including Nurarihyon, Order: Tanuki, Order: Kamainu and Yami Art: Hyakki Yagyō, using illusions, summoned beings and restrictive effects rather than relying solely on raw destructive power. Scathach subsequently becomes another of Mizuki's martial instructors, adding the Countess's combat experience to the supernatural education Mizuki already received from Abe.[23]

#### Demonic War
With the conflict against [[Diablo]] and the Demons approaching, Mizuki becomes a regular member of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s operational circle rather than an outside hunter. The threat of an approaching Demonic invasion initially provokes her protective instincts, but Victor challenges her to decide whom she truly wishes to defend instead of fighting merely because she believes herself obliged to be heroic.[24] When directly questioned about her willingness to fight, she confirms that she intends to participate according to her own desires rather than disguising her motivation behind abstract claims of heroism.[25] During Victor's operations against the Vatican and the conspirators within the Inquisition, Mizuki is deployed through Kaguya's shadows as an infiltrator and intelligence operative. She uses the Jorōgumo technique to mark and imprison the wounded General Kurtz for interrogation,[26] and later successfully emerges alone from a heavily defended concentration of Angels and [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] after investigating the conspiracy for Victor. Victor's willingness to let her perform the mission reflects his confidence in how much stronger Mizuki has become through her own effort and training under both him and [[Scathach]]; Kaguya's hidden shadow is retained only as an emergency extraction measure.[27]
Her relationship with Victor also becomes increasingly personal during this period. Mizuki spends enough time within his household to converse casually with [[Leon Walker]], analyze the differences between Humans and supernatural beings, and participate in the family's increasingly complicated domestic life.[28] Victor later confronts the resentment Mizuki still carries from childhood. After revealing that he already knows what happened during her captivity, he refuses to judge the actions of a desperate child trying to survive and tells Mizuki that she cannot allow hatred of individual Vampires to poison the rest of her existence. Mizuki, who had feared disgust more than anger, is deeply relieved by his response and accepts his promise that she does not have to face the past alone.[4] During the subsequent preparations surrounding Leon and Anna’s changes, Mizuki demonstrates a protective invocation absent from many accounts of her abilities. By invoking Shisa, the Sea Dragon, through five talismans, she creates a red barrier over an enclosed room and marks its walls with protective Japanese script, combining principles from Seimei’s and Ashiya Dōman’s arts.[29]
Mizuki's military importance grows alongside the intimacy of their relationship. Mizuki also takes part in Victor’s wider wartime operations as an experienced covert hunter, with her successful reconnaissance among the Angels and Inquisition forces demonstrating the practical value of her intelligence work.[27] During the enormous assault on [[WarFall]], Mizuki fights from the city's defenses in full battle equipment and unleashes one of her largest Onmyō techniques. Calling upon Susanoo, she uses eight talismans to produce Wrath of the Heavens, generating violent storm conditions and eight enormous water warriors equipped with different weapons. The constructs enter the monster army and become one of the principal reasons the defenders can slaughter the invading forces at such scale.[30] By the concluding stages of the war, Mizuki is firmly part of the coalition surrounding Victor rather than an independent Human observer, remaining with the defensive forces when other combatants advance farther into Hell.[31]

#### King Among Kings
Mizuki's place within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family is eventually formalized. The woman who originally tried to kill him as an Inquisition General and later became his ally and instructor joins Victor through an Onmyō-based ritual and [[Soul Marriage]], becoming one of his wives and a permanent member of the Elderblood family.[5]
Her former Human identity also makes her politically useful to the rapidly expanding [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]]. When Victor's faction is reorganized around representatives for its many species, Mizuki is selected as the representative of the Human population. The initial Council of [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] selects her because she is the strongest Human then serving Victor and is trusted as his wife; she is also expected to supervise the Humans living in Snow territory who have not fully integrated into his authority.[5] Although later administrative reforms eventually place other individuals in more specialized Human leadership positions, this appointment demonstrates how far Mizuki has moved from an isolated hunter into the governing structure surrounding Victor.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] establishes the Blood Dragon Race, Mizuki is offered another fundamental choice concerning her existence. Her response directly distinguishes her from Haruna. Haruna hesitates because becoming a Dragon would sacrifice the 9-Tailed Fox lineage upon which her identity and techniques depend; Mizuki instead explains that she has no comparable attachment to being Human. She had refused previous opportunities to become a Vampire because permanent Blood dependence and Vampiric physiology would interfere with the Onmyōjutsu she had spent her entire life developing, making conversion a net loss rather than an advancement.[6]
Blood Dragon physiology offers the opposite outcome. Dragons possess immensely superior bodies, exceptional affinity for Energy and the ability to adapt to different Energy systems, while Victor's [[Blood Dragons]] combine those characteristics with the flexibility inherited from Vampires. Mizuki therefore decides that refusing the transformation merely for the sake of remaining Human would be irrational. [[Abe-no-Seimei]] dislikes her decision but accepts that his disciple is old enough to determine her own future.[6] By the later Dragon-development period Mizuki has completed her transition into a Blood Dragon, and her Onmyōjutsu itself has begun evolving alongside her new physiology, allowing her to analyze and approach supernatural phenomena through abilities unavailable to her former Human body.[32]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Thousands of years of accelerated development eventually transform Mizuki from one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s newest wives into an established member of the Elderblood dynasty. By Chapter 987, Victor and Mizuki have an unnamed biological daughter, confirming that their relationship has produced its own branch of the next Elderblood generation.[33]
Mizuki later returns to the center of the narrative during one of the most personal sequences of Victor's later life. Their relationship has by this point changed beyond recognition from the hostility of their first meeting; Victor treats Mizuki as unquestioned family and she trusts him sufficiently to invite him into the landscape of her childhood.[34][35] In the mountains near Kyoto she deliberately suppresses her Dragon characteristics, returning her eyes to black, hiding her horns, tying her long hair and wearing a traditional kimono before leading Victor through the story of Aikyo Mizuki.[9]
Mizuki recounts her peaceful childhood, the destruction of her family, three years of captivity, the other abducted children, Koyuki's betrayal and the moment her latent supernatural power allowed her to escape. She also explains how collapsing near the temple associated with [[Abe-no-Seimei]] began the relationship that would define most of her life, and how she eventually returned as an adult to destroy those responsible for what happened to her.[9][10] Victor listens without dismissing either the cruelty she experienced or the moral complexity of what she later became. Their conversation reinforces a principle both now share: neither Humans nor Vampires deserve automatic moral protection because of their Race; an individual's actions, choices and treatment of others are what ultimately determine how Victor and Mizuki judge them.[36]
Mizuki’s relationship with her first master also reaches an important turning point during Victor's consolidation of Earth. Abe-no-Seimei, who had remained a Heroic Spirit beside Mizuki for most of her life, tells Victor that he wishes to continue observing what kind of civilization his disciple's husband will create. Victor responds by giving Abe a living High Human body, biological immortality and enough mental durability to survive the immense lifespan required to witness that future. Abe accepts, while acknowledging that Victor was also the person who finally helped pull his disciple out of the emotional state in which her past had trapped her.[37]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
During the Imperial expansion into the wider Cosmos, the Onmyō traditions Mizuki preserved become a prominent profession throughout the [[Elderblood Empire]]. The practice gains support from Mizuki as an Empress and from [[Amaterasu]] as the Shinto Goddess Queen, while her long-standing pupil [[Frederick Winter]] becomes the most famous and highest-ranking Onmyō warrior.[38] On a newly contacted world in Andromeda, Fred uses an invocation calling upon the Emperor and a [[Ares|God of War]] to reinforce himself and transform his sword into an Energy greatsword, illustrating how the old faith-and-invocation system has adapted to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Imperial religion.[38] These techniques are demonstrated by Fred rather than Mizuki, but their prominence establishes her continuing importance as a teacher and patron of Onmyōjutsu after thousands of years of Imperial development.[38] Mizuki also joins the gathering of Victor's wives during the family events preceding the traditional banquet, appearing alongside Hela, Nyx, Bruna, Eve and Violet.[39]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
Mizuki first encounters [[Victor Elderblood]] as an enemy and nearly kills him because his extraordinary raw power cannot compensate for his inexperience against a veteran Onmyōji.[2] Later, their relationship grows through shared training, cooperation and conversations in which Victor refuses to condemn her either for her past as a Hunter or for the choices she made as an abused child.[7][4] Their marriage follows the Onmyō-based rituals associated with the expansion of Victor's family, while their later return to Kyoto demonstrates that Mizuki trusts him with the details of her earliest trauma.[5][9]

#### Abe-no-Seimei
[[Abe-no-Seimei]] rescues and raises Mizuki after her escape, eventually becoming her principal teacher in Onmyōjutsu and a parental figure in everything but blood.[10] Their relationship includes persistent arguments, especially concerning the moral treatment of innocent Vampire children and Mizuki's decision to become a Blood Dragon.[7][6] In later years, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] grants Seimei a living High Human body and biological immortality, allowing the legendary Onmyōji to continue observing the Empire and the life of the disciple he once protected.[37]

#### Scathach Elderblood Scarlett
[[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] becomes Mizuki's martial instructor after testing her techniques and discovering the unusual versatility of Onmyōjutsu.[22] Mizuki's combinations of concealment, decoys, restraints, Youkai invocations and swordsmanship interest [[Scathach]], whose training forces her to address the weaknesses created by telegraphed incantations and limited close-combat experience against superior opponents.[23]

#### Frederick Winter
[[Frederick Winter]] is Mizuki's student and an accomplished practitioner of Imperial Onmyōjutsu. His position becomes particularly important after the establishment of the [[Elderblood Empire]], where Mizuki and [[Amaterasu]] Elderblood sponsor the growing tradition of Onmyō warriors.[38] Fred serves as the most prominent leader among these practitioners and can invoke Imperial-era Onmyō techniques without depending on a prepared talisman, although his own accomplishments remain distinct from abilities personally demonstrated by Mizuki.[38]

#### Koyuki
Koyuki is Mizuki's childhood companion during her captivity. She trusted him enough to share a carefully prepared escape plan, but he betrayed her to the Vampires who imprisoned them.[10] After Mizuki survived, trained and returned as an adult, she killed Koyuki, who had become a Vampire servant, during her campaign against the families responsible for the abuse.[10]

### Current Status
Mizuki remains alive as one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wives, a Blood Dragon Goddess and a senior figure associated with the traditions of Imperial Onmyōjutsu. Her former career as an Inquisition General is now part of the history she has overcome rather than an indication of ongoing loyalty to that organization.[10][38] Her martial and religious legacy continues through her disciple Fred and the growing Onmyō-warrior profession recognized throughout the Empire.[38] Her abilities remain centered on her personal development as an Onmyōji and Dragon Goddess; the extraordinary battlefield feats later performed by Fred belong to her disciple rather than to Mizuki herself.

### Trivia
- Mizuki's birth name is Aikyo Mizuki.[10]
- Before joining [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Mizuki was one of the four elite Generals of The Inquisition and was already credited with participating in the eradication of ten Japanese Noble Vampire families.[1][8]
- Mizuki once admitted that Victor resembles her ideal type of man, long before they formally became husband and wife.[13]
- [[Abe-no-Seimei]] remained with Mizuki as a Heroic Spirit for a large portion of her life despite being capable of manifesting independently; Victor later recreated him as an immortal High Human.[8][37]
- Contrary to the assumption that she remained Human because of racial pride, Mizuki explicitly states that she is not proud of being Human. Her objection to becoming a Vampire was based upon Blood dependence and the incompatibility of Vampirism with her lifetime of Onmyōjutsu training.[6]
- Mizuki and Victor have at least one confirmed biological daughter, first mentioned in Chapter 987.[33]
- Mizuki's earliest relationship with Victor is almost the exact opposite of their current one: she was one of the first people in the series who came genuinely close to killing him, but later became his ally, teacher, wife and fellow Blood Dragon.[2][5]
- Mizuki created a Shisa-based protective barrier by casting five talismans against the walls of a room, combining protective methods associated with both Abe-no-Seimei and Ashiya Dōman.[29]
- Her disciple [[Frederick Winter]] becomes the leader of the Empire's Onmyō warriors; Mizuki and [[Amaterasu]] are identified as the tradition's principal sponsors during the Imperial Era.[38]

### Abilities

#### Powers & Abilities
Mizuki combines a complete Blood Dragon foundation with more than a century of personal Onmyōjutsu experience before her racial transformation. Her individual specialties are talismanic rituals, Shinto invocations, illusion and restriction techniques, support magic, swordsmanship and sustained large-scale battlefield spells; techniques performed by her disciple [[Frederick Winter]] are not automatically her own.
- Blood Dragon God Physiology: Mizuki ultimately accepted [[Victor Elderblood]]'s offer to abandon her Human body and become a genuine Blood Dragon and Dragon Goddess. Her transformation greatly increased the supernatural foundation through which she uses Onmyojutsu, with her already exceptional Onmyōji abilities evolving further after her racial change.[1]
- Enhanced Onmyōji Abilities: Mizuki explicitly possesses special abilities derived from being an Onmyōji which evolved further following her transformation into a Blood Dragon.[1]
- Supernatural Analysis: Mizuki can use her evolved Onmyōji perception to analyze supernatural creations and determine properties that are not immediately apparent through ordinary senses; she examined the objects produced through Anna's Reality power after becoming a Dragon.[1]
- Onmyojutsu: Mizuki is an exceptionally powerful Onmyō Mage and the disciple of [[Abe-no-Seimei]]. Onmyōjutsu is a supernatural system comparable to the Faith techniques used by the Inquisition, but Mizuki's branch draws upon Faith associated with the Gods and supernatural traditions of Japan rather than the [[Heavenly Father|Biblical God]].[2]
- Faith Manipulation: Onmyōjutsu is powered through Faith, described as an unshakable belief capable of producing supernatural change. Mizuki channels this principle through her spells and talismans.[2]
- Shinto Faith Channeling: Mizuki's traditional Onmyōjutsu invokes and draws supernatural effects through the Gods and beliefs of the [[Shinto Pantheon]].[2]
- Talisman [[Magic]]: Mizuki can store, prepare and activate supernatural techniques through paper talismans containing different spells and invocations.[3][4][5]
- Incantation-Based Magic: Many of Mizuki's Onmyō techniques require a verbal invocation corresponding to the supernatural Being or effect being called upon.[3][4]
- Spell Combination: Mizuki can prepare several different talismans simultaneously and combine multiple Onmyō techniques during a single battle.[5]
- Spell Catalyst Usage: Mizuki can channel Onmyōjutsu through a weapon and use it in a role comparable to a magician's wand, causing the weapon's supernatural properties to become incorporated into the resulting spells.[6]
- Odachi Catalyst: During the defense of [[WarFall]], Mizuki used an Odachi lent to her by Eleanor as the main catalyst for an enormous Onmyō spell.[6]
- Levitation / Flight: Mizuki can remain suspended in the air through her supernatural techniques while casting large-scale Onmyōjutsu.[6]
- Yami Arts: Mizuki possesses knowledge of the darker branch of Onmyōjutsu associated with supernatural techniques traditionally connected to Youkai and the methods explored by [[Ashiya Doman]]. Abe-no-Seimei explicitly encouraged Mizuki to broaden her understanding beyond the narrower branch she had previously practiced.[7][5]
- Yami Art — Nurarihyon: Mizuki alters the surrounding environment so that her presence becomes visually concealed rather than merely making her physical body transparent.[5]
- Environmental Illusion: [[Scathach]] identified Nurarihyon as an illusion imposed upon the environment itself rather than a conventional mental illusion directly affecting the target's brain.[5]
- Visual Invisibility: While Nurarihyon is active, an opponent can look directly toward Mizuki's location without being able to visually perceive her.[5]
- Non-Visual Detection Limitation: Nurarihyon does not erase Mizuki's supernatural presence completely; Scathach remained capable of sensing her location through her powers despite being unable to see her.[5]
- Order — Tanuki: Mizuki can summon ten supernatural tanuki which immediately assume her appearance, creating a group of visually identical Mizukis surrounding the opponent.[5]
- Illusory Duplication: The additional Mizukis generated through Tanuki are illusions rather than independent physical clones, as Scathach was able to disperse the false images through environmental disruption.[5]
- Misdirection: Tanuki allows Mizuki to obscure which body represents her actual position and forces an opponent to divide their attention between multiple identical targets.[5]
- Order — Kamainu: Mizuki can invoke a dog-like supernatural entity through a talisman which imposes a restriction preventing the selected target from using their powers to deliberately harm another Being.[5]
- Violence Prohibition: Kamainu prevents the affected target from performing actions whose purpose is to directly harm another person.[5]
- Attack Nullification: When Scathach attempted to throw an Ice sword at Mizuki, Kamainu's eyes activated and the weapon disappeared before the attack could be completed.[5]
- Power-Use Restriction: Kamainu does not completely remove the victim's supernatural abilities; Scathach could still create Ice, but could not use that Ice offensively against another person.[5]
- Intent-Based Restriction: The technique distinguishes between merely activating an ability and attempting to use that ability to inflict harm, demonstrating that the restriction operates according to the intended action rather than simply suppressing Energy.[5]
- Yami Art — Hyakki Yagyō: Mizuki can establish a ten-pointed magical formation and invoke multiple Youkai-related supernatural effects to immobilize and trap an enemy.[5]
- Ten-Pointed Magic Circle: A ten-pointed supernatural formation appears beneath the intended target when Mizuki activates Hyakki Yagyō.[5]
- Movement Restriction: Once caught within the formation, the victim can be prevented from physically moving from their position.[5]
- Youkai Manifestation: Mizuki invokes multiple supernatural Youkai manifestations as part of Hyakki Yagyō rather than relying upon a single creature or effect.[5]
- Paralysis: One of the manifested creatures possesses a female or [[Medusa]]-like head whose supernatural influence contributes to immobilizing the target.[5]
- Ocular Magic Circle: Mizuki's eyes turn blood-red and manifest the same ten-pointed magical formation while the technique is active.[5]
- Layered Restraint: Hyakki Yagyō combines multiple restrictions and Youkai manifestations, making escape substantially more difficult for an opponent unfamiliar with the technique.[5]
- Jorōgumo: Mizuki can invoke a Jorōgumo through a black talisman to mark and restrain another individual.[8]
- Youkai Summoning: Activating the talisman physically manifests a spider-woman Youkai beside the selected target.[8]
- Target Marking: Mizuki commands the Jorōgumo to mark the chosen individual as its target and prisoner.[8]
- Parasitic Spider Implantation: The manifested Jorōgumo produces a small spider which enters the target's body through the mouth.[8]
- Physical Restraint: The Jorōgumo can physically hold the victim in place while the smaller spider enters their body.[8]
- Prisoner Control: Mizuki uses the technique to convert a dangerous target into a controlled prisoner rather than immediately killing them, as demonstrated on General Kurtz.[8]
- Shisa — Sea Dragon Protective Invocation: Mizuki can invoke Shisa, a guardian associated with both protective deity traditions and Youkai, to erect a sanctified barrier over an enclosed location.[9]
- Five-Talisman Barrier: She places five talismans upon the walls of a room and activates them simultaneously, producing red glowing script and a barrier covering the enclosure.[9]
- Protective Magic: The barrier is created to seal and safeguard the protected room during a dangerous supernatural ritual, rather than to attack or disable a specific opponent.[9]
- Syncretic Onmyōjutsu: Abe-no-Seimei recognizes Mizuki's invocation as combining methods drawn from his school and the arts of his rival Ashiya Dōman.[9]
- Susanoo Invocation: Mizuki can invoke Susanoo through Onmyōjutsu to generate and control enormous weather phenomena associated with storms, rain and Lightning.[6]
- Weather Manipulation: Mizuki can create an artificial localized storm over a chosen battlefield through the Susanoo invocation.[6]
- Storm-Cloud Generation: Dark storm clouds rapidly gather above the affected region after Mizuki calls upon Susanoo.[6]
- Rain Manipulation: Mizuki can generate torrential rainfall confined specifically to the area affected by her spell.[6]
- Lightning Manipulation: Lightning bolts generated within Mizuki's artificial storm can be directed downward against enemy forces.[6]
- Mass Electrocution: The storm released repeated Lightning strikes across the battlefield, electrocuting numerous attacking Monsters simultaneously.[6]
- Water Generation and Manipulation: Mizuki can dramatically increase the amount and volume of Water produced by her supernatural rainfall.[6]
- Water-Sphere Creation: Individual droplets of rain expanded until they became Water spheres approximately two meters in diameter.[6]
- Battlefield Flooding: The enormous quantity of Water produced through the technique rapidly flooded the battlefield.[6]
- Wrath of the Heavens: Mizuki's large-scale Susanoo-based Onmyōjutsu used during the invasion of WarFall. The technique combines weather manipulation, Water control, Lightning and supernatural summoning into a sustained battlefield spell.[6]
- Mass Water Manipulation: Water spread across the battlefield is gathered and reshaped by Mizuki into gigantic humanoid constructs.[6]
- Eight Water Giants: Wrath of the Heavens creates eight gigantic warriors composed primarily of Water, each approximately 20 meters tall.[6]
- Samurai Construct Creation: The Water giants manifest wearing armor modeled after ancient Japanese samurai.[6]
- Weapon Creation: Each giant manifests with a different functional weapon as part of its supernatural construction.[6]
- Katana Creation: Some of Mizuki's Water giants wield giant Japanese blades suitable for close combat.[6]
- Firearm Creation: At least one Water giant manifested carrying a functional rifle despite the otherwise ancient samurai aesthetic of the constructs.[6]
- Independent Combat Constructs: The Water giants can act as combatants against the invading Monster army rather than functioning merely as stationary environmental structures.[6]
- Mass Monster Extermination: The combined storm, Lightning and Water constructs allowed Mizuki to destroy large numbers of Monsters across WarFall's battlefield.[6]
- Extended Spell Maintenance: Mizuki could maintain Wrath of the Heavens for more than thirty minutes. Abe-no-Seimei stated that even at his own peak he had only been capable of maintaining comparable Magic for approximately one minute.[6]
- Victor-Blood Enhancement: Abe-no-Seimei attributed Mizuki's enormous increase in sustained Onmyō output partly to the changes caused by previously consuming Victor's Blood.[6]
- [[Tsukuyomi]] — Peace of the Night: Mizuki can invoke Tsukuyomi through her fan and release white supernatural Energy over allied fighters.[3]
- Calmness Inducement: The technique causes affected allies to become substantially calmer even while confronting an overwhelmingly dangerous enemy.[3]
- Fear Suppression: Mizuki used the enchantment to reduce the terror Victor's killing intent was producing among the less experienced [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] accompanying her.[3]
- Area Support: The white Energy produced through Tsukuyomi affected multiple Hunters surrounding Mizuki simultaneously rather than requiring individual casting.[3]
- Bishamonten — Weapon Bestowal: Mizuki can invoke Bishamonten and request a weapon suitable for destroying her enemies.[3]
- Weapon Transmutation: After Mizuki's invocation, the fan she was holding transformed into a roughly two-meter-long Odachi.[3]
- Supernatural Weapon Creation: The resulting Odachi possessed sufficient supernatural properties to be used against extremely powerful creatures of the night such as Victor.[3]
- Abe-no-Seimei — Wisdom Enchantment: Mizuki can invoke the knowledge and spiritual assistance of her master, Abe-no-Seimei, to enhance her combat capabilities.[3]
- Mental Enhancement: After invoking Abe-no-Seimei's wisdom, blue Energy covered Mizuki's body and a supernatural circle appeared within her eyes.[3]
- Combat Analysis Enhancement: The technique assists Mizuki in reading and exploiting an opponent's fighting behavior, something particularly effective against the extremely inexperienced early Victor.[3]
- Flame Talisman: Mizuki can activate a talisman that releases a wave of Fire toward an opponent.[3]
- [[Pyrokinesis]] (Onmyōjutsu): Through a prepared talisman, Mizuki generated a large wave of flame and launched it directly at Kaguya.[3]
- Anti-Supernatural Poison: Mizuki possesses access to poison capable of severely debilitating Noble Vampires.[3]
- Vampire Paralysis / Debilitation: The poison used against early Victor caused him to cough black Blood, lose control of his transformation and become increasingly incapacitated.[3]
- Count-Class Effectiveness: Although Mizuki acknowledged that the poison was not powerful enough to directly kill a Noble Vampire of Victor's class by itself, it could weaken one sufficiently for a Hunter to deliver a killing blow.[3]
- Innate Faith Affinity: Mizuki possessed an extraordinary natural affinity with Faith and supernatural power even before receiving formal Onmyōji training.[10]
- Talismanless Faith Manifestation: At approximately nine years old and without formal training, Mizuki instinctively released Faith-based supernatural power without using a talisman while escaping from the Vampires who had imprisoned her.[10]
- Physical Empowerment through Faith: Despite being a badly wounded Human child, Mizuki's latent supernatural power allowed her to overpower Koyuki long enough to escape captivity.[10]

##### Limits of Attribution
Mizuki's Dragon Godhood grants a powerful Draconic physiology and significantly improves the foundation through which she channels Onmyōjutsu, but the novel does not identify a unique personal Divine Concept for her in the verified chapters. Her ability to analyze Anna's Runic creations is an evolved individual sensory skill, not proof that she can reproduce Anna's Reality-altering feats.[1] Although Fred later manifests extraordinarily powerful Imperial Onmyō techniques without talismans, those feats are explicitly his and should not be added to Mizuki's personal technique list without a separate demonstration.[11]

#### Skills
- Master Onmyōji: Mizuki is one of the greatest modern practitioners of Onmyōjutsu and the personal disciple of [[Abe-no-Seimei]]. Her talent was considered extraordinary even compared with the legendary Onmyōji of the past.[12][10]
- Exceptional Onmyō Talent: Abe-no-Seimei recognized Mizuki as possessing extraordinary natural aptitude for the Onmyō Arts after she arrived at the temple during childhood.[10]
- Rapid Learning: Mizuki absorbed the knowledge of Onmyōjutsu and Faith at an exceptional rate after beginning her formal training.[10]
- Light and Dark Onmyōjutsu: Mizuki eventually broadened her training beyond the narrower Faith techniques she originally favored and began using darker Yami Arts associated with other Onmyō traditions.[7][5]
- Elite Hunter: Before joining [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction, Mizuki was one of the four Generals of the Inquisition and was already recognized as one of Humanity's most dangerous supernatural hunters.[13]
- Vampire Hunting: Mizuki was explicitly stated to be capable of fighting Vampires more than 500 years old.[13]
- Clan Extermination: Before the beginning of the story, Mizuki had destroyed approximately ten Vampire Clans operating in Japan.[13]
- Anti-Supernatural Combat: Mizuki possesses extensive practical experience fighting Vampires and other creatures whose physical abilities greatly exceed those of ordinary Humans.[3]
- Weakness Exploitation: Against early Victor, Mizuki immediately recognized that his tremendous raw strength was undermined by his complete lack of combat experience and adjusted her tactics around that weakness.[3]
- Odachi Mastery: Mizuki is highly skilled in the use of extremely long Japanese swords and routinely employs an Odachi together with her Onmyōjutsu.[3][4]
- High-Speed Swordsmanship: Mizuki can combine physical acceleration, supernatural reinforcement and Odachi attacks when fighting creatures substantially stronger than ordinary Humans.[3]
- Defensive Swordsmanship: During her training with [[Scathach]], Mizuki instinctively used an Odachi to defend against Scathach's sudden attacks.[4]
- Spell–Sword Integration: Mizuki can attach talismans directly to an Odachi and incorporate the weapon into the preparation and execution of her Onmyō techniques.[4][6]
- Catalyst Swordsmanship: Mizuki understands how to use a weapon not merely as a melee tool but as a magical catalyst through which her spells can be amplified.[6]
- Combat Adaptation: Mizuki modified her casting habits after training with Victor and Scathach, learning that experienced opponents would not simply allow her to complete obvious incantations uninterrupted.[4]
- Distraction Casting: Victor taught Mizuki to conceal or disguise the preparation of her mandatory incantations by creating distractions during combat.[4]
- Silent/Low-Voice Incantation: Mizuki learned to avoid loudly announcing every technique and instead murmurs portions of her incantations while distracting the opponent.[4]
- Talisman Preparation in Motion: Mizuki can place talismans on weapons and prepare a spell while simultaneously moving or attacking.[4]
- Exceptional Tactical Versatility: Scathach specifically found Mizuki's Onmyōjutsu interesting because it allowed one fighter to alternate between illusion, restraint, environmental control, summoning and direct combat within the same engagement.[5]
- Behavioral Analysis: Even as a child, Mizuki demonstrated an extraordinary ability to observe and memorize patterns of behavior over long periods.[10]
- Routine Memorization: During her captivity, Mizuki memorized the habits and schedules of the Vampires controlling the mansion in which she was imprisoned.[10]
- Security-Gap Identification: By analyzing those patterns, Mizuki identified a specific recurring opening in the Vampires' defenses and created an escape plan around it.[10]
- Imperial Onmyō Patronage: The professional discipline of Imperial Onmyō warriors expands under Mizuki's sponsorship as an Empress, alongside [[Amaterasu]]'s patronage of the Shinto tradition.[11]
- Master of [[Frederick Winter]]: Fred remains Mizuki's disciple and is recognized as the best-known, highest-ranking Onmyō warrior in the Empire. His later feats are his own and do not automatically establish new abilities for Mizuki.[11]
- Instructor:
Mizuki later became one of the people responsible for broadening Victor's combat education beyond Vampire martial arts.[4]
- - Hunter-Method Instruction: Mizuki taught Victor how professional [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] approach Vampires, [[Werewolves]] and other creatures of the night.[4]
- Odachi Instruction: Mizuki trained Victor in the practical use of the Odachi and its differences from other bladed weapons.[4]
- Limited Blacksmithing / Weapon Crafting: Mizuki possesses enough practical forging and supernatural crafting knowledge to construct highly unusual anti-supernatural weapons.[14]
- [[Junketsu]] Creation: Mizuki created the original version of Junketsu after Victor proposed combining his Blood with anti-monster Blessed Hunter Metal.[14]
- Hybrid Material Forging: Mizuki successfully combined Victor's abnormal Blood with supernatural metal originally designed to kill creatures of the night, producing a weapon capable of safely conducting his power.[14]
- Progenitor-Compatible Weapon Crafting: The resulting weapon remained compatible with Victor despite the Blessed material normally being harmful to Vampires, demonstrating Mizuki's ability to adapt supernatural weapon materials around the intended wielder.[14]

#### Former Powers and Enhancements
- Human Onmyō Mage Physiology: Before becoming a Blood Dragon, Mizuki was biologically Human and relied upon Onmyōjutsu, Faith, training and supernatural equipment to fight creatures physically superior to Humanity.[13][2]
- Inquisition Support [[Magic]] (Former): While affiliated with the Inquisition, Mizuki had access to limited supernatural support originating from the organization even though she personally followed the Shinto Gods and did not genuinely believe in the [[Heavenly Father|Biblical God]].[15]
- Physical Reinforcement: The Inquisition's support Magic increased Mizuki's physical capabilities while she remained affiliated with the organization.[15]
- Regeneration Enhancement: The same external support increased Mizuki's ability to recover from physical injuries.[15]
- Loss after Betrayal: Mizuki permanently lost access to this support after abandoning and betraying the Inquisition.[15]
- Faith Incompatibility: Mizuki refused to use the Biblical God's power directly because her genuine religious Faith remained with the Gods of Japan.[15]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood Enhancement (Temporary Human State): While Mizuki was still Human, consuming Victor's Blood temporarily altered her body and restored it toward its physical prime.[7]
- Rejuvenation: Victor's Blood healed Mizuki's body and restored it to a peak biological condition.[7]
- Cellular Aging Suspension: [[Abe-no-Seimei]] determined that Mizuki's cells had temporarily stopped aging after she consumed Victor's Blood.[7]
- Vitality Enhancement: Her cells became filled with greatly increased Vitality as a result of the Blood.[7]
- Physical Restoration: Old injuries and scars across Mizuki's body were healed as her biological condition improved.[7]
- Physical Empowerment: Mizuki noted that she physically felt stronger following the changes caused by Victor's Blood.[7]
- Minor Fire Resistance: A strand of Mizuki's hair exposed directly to supernatural Fire resisted burning for several seconds and suffered only minor damage afterward.[7]
- Enhanced Onmyō Potential: Abe-no-Seimei later concluded that the changes created by Victor's Blood had dramatically increased Mizuki's potential as an Onmyō Mage, allowing her to sustain Wrath of the Heavens for more than thirty times longer than he could at his own peak.[6]

#### Equipment
- Onmyō Talismans: Mizuki routinely carries numerous prepared paper talismans containing different invocations, Yami Arts, restraints and summoning techniques.[3][5][8]
- Multi-Spell Loadout: Different talismans can contain completely different supernatural effects, allowing Mizuki to rapidly change tactics without relying upon one fixed spell.[5]
- Colored Talismans: During complex Onmyōjutsu, Mizuki can maintain multiple talismans glowing in different colors simultaneously.[6]
- Onmyō Fan: Mizuki formerly used a traditional fan as one of her primary ritual tools when invoking Shinto-based techniques.[3]
- [[Tsukuyomi]] Catalyst: Mizuki used the fan to channel the white Energy of her Tsukuyomi calming enchantment across the surrounding [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]].[3]
- Bishamonten Transformation: Through the invocation of Bishamonten, the fan transformed into a two-meter Odachi suitable for direct combat.[3]
- Odachi: Mizuki specializes in unusually long Japanese blades and frequently integrates them into both swordsmanship and Onmyōjutsu.[3][4]
- Spell Catalyst: An appropriately enchanted Odachi can be used like a magical wand to channel and amplify Mizuki's Onmyōjutsu.[6]
- Eleanor's Odachi: During [[WarFall]]'s defense, Mizuki used an Odachi lent by Eleanor whose supernatural characteristics were transmitted into Mizuki's spells while serving as their catalyst.[6]
