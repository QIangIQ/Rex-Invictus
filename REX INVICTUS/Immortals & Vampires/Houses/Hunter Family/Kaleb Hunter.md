Although outwardly classified as a Dragonoid, Kaleb's actual physiology is considerably more complex. ### Physical Appearance
Kaleb is an enormous pale-skinned man with white hair and gray eyes. His face is usually completely expressionless, making his emotional state difficult to determine from ordinary observation. Despite this, Clare was able to recognize the intense admiration he held for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] simply by looking into his eyes.[4]
His physique occupies a middle ground between two of his fellow Emperor's Soldiers. He is considerably more muscular than the comparatively slender, swimmer-like [[Caelus Hunter]], but lacks the exaggerated, barbarian-like musculature displayed by Conan. John possesses a similarly balanced body type.[4]
The original Emperor's Soldier template created by Victor possessed a natural height of approximately four meters. This size was required because the engineered bodies contained too many supernatural characteristics to comfortably maintain within an ordinary Human-sized vessel while preserving maximum performance. However, Victor deliberately retained Vampiric metamorphosis within their physiology, allowing the soldiers to alter their size whenever their normal enormous stature would be inconvenient.[1]
Consequently, Kaleb's exact visible height can vary rather than remaining permanently fixed at four meters.

### Personality
Kaleb is outwardly quiet, emotionally restrained and difficult to read. His expression is naturally blank enough that Clare initially considered him strange in a manner comparable to Kiana. Unlike Kiana, however, Kaleb's unusual nature was not immediately expressed through smiling or overtly threatening behavior. His most obvious emotion was the reverence he felt toward [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[4]
His loyalty to Victor borders on fanaticism. All seven original Emperor's Soldiers instinctively recognize Victor as their Creator, Father and Emperor, but Kaleb demonstrated an especially absolute interpretation of that relationship. During his first combat exercise, rather than initially examining the invading monsters or approaching the technologically advanced Empire, Kaleb immediately began walking toward the leader of the Empire based around physical strength. His eyes took on a maniacal look, and the thought was: No one can be called Emperor in front of me other than The Dragon Emperor.[3]
This response caused [[Scathach]] to identify Kaleb as one of the more openly dangerous personalities among the seven. She specifically grouped him together with Kiana when considering the types of maniacs contained among Victor's new soldiers. The others behaved as she expected. Conan's charge did not make him stupid. Kaleb's walk was the specific case.[3]
Kaleb nevertheless should not be reduced to a mindless berserker. Victor deliberately designed the Emperor's Soldiers to possess intelligence, adaptability and the ability to think independently rather than function as obedient machines incapable of making decisions. Their implanted memories exposed them to warfare, strategy, tactics, politics and the experiences of past heroes, while their future training was specifically intended to develop wisdom alongside raw power.[4][2]
What distinguishes Kaleb is therefore not a lack of intelligence, but the extreme priority he places upon Victor's authority. He also judges his own work against people above him. After building a virus in more than a minute, he said he needed to improve, and he agreed when Clare set Aline and Ruby at less than thirty seconds.[6] The same priority is what he does with the word justice. A chief god's public tie to it does not impress him. Spoken by powers that pursue their own interest first, the word is a way to keep a good image. His thought, over the captured leader of the [[Varkhess League]], was mocking. The justice they preach, and in the end they are only enemies of the Emperor.[9]

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Kaleb's existence originated from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s attempt to create a military force belonging directly to himself rather than relying entirely upon the armies, servants and followers already attached to his wives and allies. Victor considered two possibilities: mass-producing large numbers of merely competent soldiers or investing far greater resources into a smaller population of elite warriors capable of functioning as weapons of mass destruction. He ultimately chose the latter.[1]
The Dragonoid race became the foundation of the project because its physiology possessed a stronger connection to Victor while providing the durable body necessary to survive further modification. Victor then incorporated several additional racial characteristics: Human adaptability and ingenuity, Angelic characteristics intended to counterbalance darker impulses, Demonic aggression and combat-oriented traits, and diluted Fairy Essence that granted an Elf-like connection to Nature. Because that Nature was ultimately connected to Victor himself, the Fairy element further strengthened their bond to their Creator.[1]
The final and most dangerous ingredient was Victor himself. Each soldier received approximately 0.0000001% of Victor's Soul, a fragment intended to grow together with the individual over time. Victor also placed Blessings associated with his Combat Divinities upon the developing Souls. His Essence then blended the otherwise incompatible racial characteristics together around the Dragonoid foundation, creating beings who outwardly registered as Dragonoids while being far more complicated internally.[1]
The resulting bodies reached approximately four meters in height while retaining extraordinary agility. Victor also preserved Vampiric regeneration and metamorphosis so that his soldiers would not become overly specialized giants incapable of handling delicate missions. His intention was to create flexible war machines capable of operating effectively regardless of the environment.[1]
Victor created seven original Souls to serve as the prototypes from which future soldiers would eventually be derived. Kaleb was one of those seven alongside John, Clare, Kiana, Laura, Conan and Caelus.[1]
The seven originals spent approximately ten years developing before formally meeting Victor as mature soldiers. Their growth was not purely physical. Victor implanted memories belonging to warriors and heroes of the past into them, exposing the developing soldiers to countless examples of warfare, tactics, strategy, intelligence and the consequences of conflict long before they personally experienced a battlefield.[4]
These memories did not erase their individuality. The soldiers understood that the memories were lessons rather than their own lives, allowing them to learn from past experiences without losing their own developing identities.[4]
When the seven finally emerged from their creation chambers, Kaleb appeared as a white-haired, gray-eyed man with an emotionless face and a physique between Conan and Caelus. Although his expression revealed almost nothing, Clare immediately noticed the adoration in his eyes when he looked toward Victor.[4]
Like the others, Kaleb immediately knelt before Victor and addressed him as Emperor. Victor acknowledged their rapid development but warned them that accumulated memories did not automatically make them genuine heroes or warriors. Knowledge without personal experience was insufficient, and their talents still needed to be refined through actual training.[4]
Victor then brought the seven before the individuals selected to complete their education. Hassan-i Sabbah was assigned to teach assassination and methods of killing, [[Julius Caesar]] was assigned to teach politics, [[Abe-no-Seimei]] would instruct them in philosophy and wisdom, while [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] would personally polish their combat talents.[2]
[[Scathach]] initially considered the seven unimpressive because years of living beside Victor and raising True Dragon children had catastrophically distorted her standards. After deliberately comparing them to the great heroes and Gods of previous generations instead, her judgment changed. She recognized that the seven possessed talent comparable to the exceptional individuals traditionally regarded as Gods and heroes rather than ordinary soldiers.[2]
Victor then explained why he was investing so many resources into them. He had not created them to become mediocre subordinates. The seven were his Creation, his children, his [[Spears|Spear]] and Shield, and the soldiers through whom his [[Will Solace|Will]] would eventually be exercised.[2]
Victor formally identified John, Clare, Kiana, Laura, Conan, Kaleb and Caelus as the first of the future seven Legions and his seven Imperial Commanders. Each would eventually serve directly beneath General Scathach while their experiences became the foundation used to develop the soldiers belonging to their respective Legions.[2]
However, Victor made clear that their positions represented his plans for their future rather than an achievement they had already earned. If they failed to develop sufficiently, they would not meet his expectations. Their immediate responsibility was therefore training.[2]
Scathach began their practical education by throwing all seven directly into the family's personal Nightmare Tower. Rather than gradually teaching them through controlled exercises, she created a large-scale war simulation in which two rival Empires were already fighting one another while simultaneously suffering an invasion from another dimension.[3]
One Empire relied primarily upon advanced technology but possessed comparatively weak bodies. The other possessed extremely powerful physical combatants but comparatively poor intelligence. Dimensional monsters attacked both sides indiscriminately. Scathach gave the seven one objective: pacify both sides and eliminate the invading threat.[3]
Each soldier experienced the scenario individually rather than being placed together. Their only external support consisted of Imperial equipment: adaptive armor created by Victor, a plasma firearm and a sword forged from metals obtained from the deepest regions of Hell, where exposure to dense Miasma had given the weapon dangerous poisonous properties.[3]
Scathach intentionally denied them conventional preparation. The seven already carried heroic memories and tactical knowledge; what they lacked was personal experience with pain, danger, uncertainty and the possibility of death. She therefore forced them to discover their own weaknesses through warfare before she began refining them more precisely. Her last instruction was that there are no rules in war, that only those who win are considered just, and that the strong are what dictate history.[3]
The responses of the seven immediately revealed their individual personalities. Conan charged into battle, Laura approached the Technological Empire, Caelus remained behind to observe the situation, while Kiana disappeared after apparently formulating her own plan. Kaleb chose a considerably more personal target.[3]
Without hesitation, he began walking directly toward the ruler of the physically strongest Empire. His expression changed from its usual emptiness as his eyes developed a maniacal gleam. The thought was: No one can be called Emperor in front of me other than The Dragon Emperor.[3]
Scathach, who was observing all seven from a distance, immediately understood Kaleb's nature. Having encountered countless warriors throughout her extraordinarily long life, she categorized both Kaleb and Kiana as particular varieties of maniacs, distinguishing them even among Victor's already unusual elite soldiers. John and Clare were the two who interested her most.[3]
The exercise represented the beginning of Kaleb's actual development as a warrior. Until that point he had inherited the knowledge and experiences of others; from then onward, he would begin creating experiences of his own that would eventually be recorded and inherited by the soldiers of his future Legion.[2][3]
On Clare's planet the order was to take it in two days, by striking the hive's reproductive capacity and leaving the hive mind unconscious while the other hostiles were dealt with. Kaleb's answer was that he could create a virus to make that possible, and that he needed samples from the hive for the virus to be effective. He started the simulation as he said it. Laura's warning sat on that plan: a simple virus was not enough, and a mutating virus was too risky. Clare and John are the natural leaders. Conan, Caelus, and Kiana are the berserkers. Kaleb and Laura are the minds, and the narration says the two of them could even be future gods of tactical warfare, [[Athena]]'s place, which is vacant. Beside John, Conan, and Clare they can look ordinary. Outside that group they are not, and battle intelligence is where they match those three.[5]
Clare's division of the work was that she would collect the samples, Kaleb would prepare his equipment, and Conan would see that no one approached him. The two of them held the air. The hostiles could climb and jump, and the air was still safer than the ground. When Conan named the swarm as theirs, and more specifically the Emperor, Kaleb's deduction was that the hive had sensed the danger when the Emperor looked at the planet, and had started producing more beings because of it.[5]
That virus is what he then made. Akasha cut the communication once the samples were acquired and the data sent. Clare left the hive, reached him at speed, and put the sample in his hands. "Kaleb, prepare the virus." He took it in gear made of Divine Material. "2 minutes… No, give me 1 minute." The information was read at high speed. In less than two seconds he was already preparing it. Clare and Conan held the hostiles off while he worked. When he finished he judged the time himself: "I took more than 1 minute, I need to improve further." Clare's answer was that Aline or Ruby would have finished in less than thirty seconds. She said it to push him, not to cut him down. He nodded. The result was a vial of green liquid. Asked what to do with it, he said, "Just inject it into the core." Clare, Conan, and Kaleb went in together, their bodies covered in Dragon Fire, the two of them charged with keeping her safe, and she injected it. The hive screamed. Its activity dropped, and what remained of its effort turned to defending against the virus. Clare asked how long that would last. "24 terrestrial hours, I can make more, but the effects will diminish with each dose... I predict we can keep it dormant for 7 terrestrial days. After that, it will become completely immune to the virus and all its variants."[6]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
The Emperor approved an offensive on several fronts against the sectors nearest The Black Eclipse, with priority on the systems that held the faction's transit and supply routes. The work was divided among the Originals. John took the reconnaissance of the border. Kaleb coordinated the attacks on military installations. Conan assembled the troops who would occupy and hold what was taken, and Kiana and Caelus watched the movement between the fronts so reinforcement arrived where it was needed. The force was not to push in only one direction.[7]
John was the first to find a concentration of Black Eclipse ships around a system that controlled an important passage. The engagement that followed put an Imperial presence on that route without destroying the enemy squadrons. Kaleb used the result. He proposed an attack on a military station in a neighboring solar system, a maintenance and supply base for the ships of the region. Taking it would narrow the room enemy reinforcements had to move through, and it would give the Empire an outpost. John looked at the system and gave the call. The ships fired. The planetary defenses resisted, the fire was stronger, and a planet exploded. Kaleb then said that the neighboring worlds were still active. John ordered the undefended worlds conquered first. The First Legion's victory on that field, which had no super-elite on it, is John's, and the principal battlefield was [[Scathach]]'s.[7]
A weapon of Darkness then appeared in Solar System 1-1. The first estimate was V-05, a threat to several solar systems. What the image showed was a mass devouring the matter of the region and making smaller monsters that spread as a swarm. Conan called it a pile of flesh. John saw that a pile of flesh could spread, raised the threat to V-04, a danger to an entire galaxy, and ordered every Imperial troop out of the system. No one was to attack it. He looked at his brother. Kaleb's judgment was a predatory organism. It devours biomass, uses the strange divinity of Darkness, and from that makes further beings of biomass. A gigantic swarm. If it was allowed to devour still more matter, it would become uncontrollable. He took it for a secret weapon of the enemy commander. John nodded, and the call went to Akasha. The Emperor already knew. The souls of the soldiers swallowed by the thing were trapped inside it, and that was what brought Velnorah and Hela.[8]
The [[Varkhess League]], an alliance of rulers near an Imperial border, used the reorganization to take three newly incorporated worlds for their minerals and their industry. An expeditionary force took the worlds back. Two days later Conan led elite soldiers, the kind who can annihilate solar systems, into the League's own system and finished it in less than thirty minutes. In the ruins of the capital, Kaleb and Conan stood over a white-skinned alien with octopus-like eyes and six arms. Conan threw the leader down in front of him. The one who had financed them was a god named [[Auralion]].[9]
Kaleb placed the office. Auralion serves as one of the chief gods of one of the four enemy factions, the being known as <Emperor>. No one in the Empire knew that leader's own name, so the record kept the codename the System assigns. The chief gods were the exception. Their names were already public, because their activity stood in connection with justice. The League was only being used. Its database had nothing on the faction beyond the names of the ten chief gods, each standing for one aspect of justice. The word drew his contempt. The justice they preach, and in the end they are only enemies of the Emperor. Conan called it a recurring pattern. They knew the financier only because the captive's mind was weak. He wanted the man sent to the girls, the B.H.E. under [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]], the third legion, who do the dirtier work in secret. Kaleb told him not to say that aloud, or he would get into trouble.[9]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is Kaleb's Creator, Father, Emperor and the central figure around which Kaleb's existence is organized. The original Emperor's Soldiers were not merely cloned military personnel commissioned by Victor; he personally designed their bodies and Souls, supplied a fragment of himself to stabilize their existence, Blessed them and remained present throughout their development.[1][4]
Kaleb's response to Victor appears particularly intense even compared with the other original soldiers. When Clare first observed him, his face remained expressionless but his gray eyes openly revealed adoration for their Creator.[4]
His devotion became even clearer during his first independent combat exercise. After learning that one of the simulated factions was ruled by an individual who claimed the title of Emperor, Kaleb immediately advanced toward that ruler. The thought was that no one can be called Emperor in front of him other than the Dragon Emperor.[3] The same line is what he applies to [[Auralion]]'s faction. A public claim of justice does not make them anything but enemies of the Emperor.[9]
Victor, in turn, places enormous expectations upon Kaleb and the other originals. He explicitly called them his Creation, his children, his [[Spears|Spear]] and his Shield, intending for his [[Will Solace|Will]] to eventually be exercised through the Imperial Soldiers they command.[2]

#### Scathach Elderblood Scarlett
[[Scathach]] is Kaleb's General and principal combat instructor. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] appointed all seven Imperial Commanders to work directly beneath her and entrusted her with polishing their natural talents into genuine martial ability.[2]
She initially underestimated Kaleb and his siblings because years of living beside Victor and their True Dragon daughters had severely distorted her standards. After reassessing them against the heroes and Gods she had trained throughout history, Scathach acknowledged their exceptional talent and became interested in personally developing them.[2]
During their first Nightmare Tower exercise, Scathach quickly identified Kaleb as one of the more psychologically extreme members of the group. Watching him immediately target the ruler of the Strength-focused Empire solely because of his devotion to Victor caused her to classify Kaleb alongside Kiana as one of the seven's particular varieties of maniac.[3]

She brings him the sample, tells him to prepare the virus, and sets Aline and Ruby as the speed he is measured against. He takes the correction. She carries the vial in, and he and Conan are there to keep her safe.[6]

#### The Emperor’s Soldiers
Kaleb is one of the seven original Emperor's Soldiers alongside John, [[Clare Bloodhunter]], [[Kiana Bloodhunter]], [[Laura Bloodhunter]], Conan and [[Caelus Hunter]]. They are not merely members of the same military organization; the seven serve as the original templates whose accumulated experiences will be used to create and educate the soldiers belonging to the future Imperial Legions.[2]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] deliberately preserved their individuality despite planning to use them as genetic and experiential foundations for future soldiers. The clones descended from the originals would inherit useful combat, political and strategic memories while developing their own identities and appearances.[1][2]
On the hive he works with Clare and Conan, and with Laura he is one of the two minds.[5][6] On the Black Eclipse border he coordinates the installation attacks beside John's reconnaissance, Conan's occupation, and the reinforcement work of Kiana and Caelus.[7]
