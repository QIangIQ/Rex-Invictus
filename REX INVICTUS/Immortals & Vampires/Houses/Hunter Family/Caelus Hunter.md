Caelus Hunter is one of the seven Original Emperor's Soldiers personally created by [[Victor Elderblood]] to form the first generation of elite Imperial Commanders of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Like his six siblings, he was built on a Dragonoid foundation, with traits from several other races, Victor's Blessings, and a minute fragment of his Soul.[1][2]
His first act in a war was to watch the whole field before he chose.[3] The wars after that put him somewhere else. He and Kiana are the two who most inherited the Demonic side, and with Conan they are the berserkers who destroy everything with the fury of the Emperor in their hearts.[4] On the hive world he came down from orbit on Dragon wings and landed in the fire.[5] On the offensive against The Black Eclipse he and Kiana watched the movement between the fronts so the reinforcements arrived where they were needed.[6]

### Physical Appearance
Caelus possesses a noticeably androgynous and comparatively delicate appearance when contrasted with the other male Original Emperor's Soldiers. He has black hair and bright golden eyes, with facial features considerably softer than those of the heavily masculine Conan. His body nevertheless remains distinctly masculine and athletic, possessing the lean, highly developed musculature of a professional swimmer rather than the massive muscular frame characteristic of Conan.[2]
Caelus effectively represents one physical extreme among the male Originals. Conan possesses the largest and most heavily developed muscular build, while John and [[Kaleb Hunter]] are described as possessing physiques approximately halfway between Conan's bulk and Caelus' slender athletic body.[2]
Like the other Emperor's Soldiers, Caelus possesses a naturally enormous Dragonoid-derived body. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] deliberately incorporated the Vampiric ability of metamorphosis into the species so his soldiers would not be permanently restricted to approximately four-meter-tall bodies during infiltration, diplomatic interaction or missions performed among smaller races.[1]
On the hive world he unfolds Dragon wings. He and Kiana open them two hundred meters above the burning surface and slow the fall. The regular soldiers on that drop use the thrusters in their armor.[5]
Caelus normally wears the advanced black Imperial uniform and combat armor developed for the Emperor's Soldiers, decorated with dark-violet and red details and bearing the Dragon symbolism of Victor's Empire.[3]

### Personality
Caelus is initially presented as calm, observant and methodical. During the seven Originals' first practical battlefield evaluation, he did not immediately attack the nearest enemy, pursue one of the warring Empires or begin experimenting with his equipment. He assumed a different posture from the others and decided to observe everything before making a decision.[3]
Conan went straight at the fight. [[Kaleb Hunter]] walked at the ruler of the strongest Empire. [[Laura Bloodhunter]] went to the Technological Empire. [[Kiana Bloodhunter]] disappeared on a plan of her own. Caelus watched.[3]
The later wars show the other half. Among all the siblings born that day, Kiana and Caelus were the ones who most inherited the Demonic side, and they were killing machines. Inside the seven, Clare and John are the natural leaders, Conan, Caelus, and Kiana are the berserkers who destroy everything with the fury of the Emperor in their hearts, and Laura and Kaleb are the minds.[4]
The fury is trained. When Clare told them not to forget their subordinates, because they would not be fighting alone, both of them answered "Yes." That was one of the lessons [[Scathach]] had etched into them. They would not focus on the fight and forget everything around them. They were soldiers, not bloodthirsty beasts.[4]
His loyalty is the same as the others. He knew [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] on sight and knelt.[2]

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Caelus was created as part of [[Victor Elderblood]]'s effort to establish an elite professional military directly subordinate to the Emperor. Although Victor already possessed numerous armies, supernatural followers and forces connected to his wives, he wanted soldiers whose primary institutional loyalty belonged directly to the future [[Elderblood Empire]].[1]
The resulting Emperor's Soldiers used Dragonoid physiology as their biological foundation because Dragonoids possessed the robustness, strength and compatibility with Victor's Draconic existence necessary for the project. Victor then incorporated advantageous characteristics from numerous additional races. Human traits provided adaptability and ingenuity. Angelic characteristics contributed obedience and benevolent tendencies. Demonic characteristics provided aggression and cruelty. Diluted Faerie Essence granted a connection with Nature. Vampiric physiology supplied regeneration and metamorphosis. He Blessed them through his combat-related authorities and put a minute fragment of his Soul into the Originals. His Essence is what holds the contradictory pieces as one species.[1]
The first generation consisted of [[Clare Bloodhunter]], [[Laura Bloodhunter]], [[Kiana Bloodhunter]], Conan, Caelus, [[Kaleb Hunter]] and John. These seven were the first Imperial Commanders, and the templates later soldiers would come from.[1][7]
Caelus spent approximately ten subjective years developing before leaving his creation chamber. Victor implanted the memories of heroes and warriors, from wars between nations to supernatural and apocalyptic ones, as lessons rather than as a finished life. When he came out he was the androgynous one: black hair, bright golden eyes, a swimmer's body, masculine and not packed with muscle. He knelt with the others. Victor's warning was that the memories did not make them heroes.[2]
The four teachers were Hassan-i Sabbah for the taking of a life, [[Julius Caesar]] for politics, [[Abe-no-Seimei]] for philosophy, and [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] for the fight. Set against the heroes and Gods she had trained, rather than against Victor's daughters, the seven sat in that company. Victor then named all seven, Caelus among them, as the first Imperial Commanders under [[Scathach]].[7]
The first test was the family's Nightmare Tower. Two Empires were already at war, one technological and one built on physical strength, and interdimensional monsters had come in on both. The order was to pacify both sides and eliminate the invaders. The equipment was the armor, a plasma firearm, and a sword from the deepest metal of Hell. There are no rules in war. Only those who win are considered just.[3]
Caelus did not charge and he did not pick a ruler. He assumed a different posture and decided to observe everything before he made a decision.[3]
On the hive world, Clare's order to the pair was that they were responsible for eliminating all hostiles on the planet once the first objective was achieved, and that they should do what they do best. Among the siblings born that day, Kiana and Caelus were the ones who most inherited the Demonic side, and they were killing machines. Beside John, Conan, and Clare they can look like less. Outside that group they are not, and the place Laura and Kaleb match those three is battle intelligence. The roles are the ones the narration gives. Clare and John lead. Conan, Caelus, and Kiana are the berserkers. Kaleb and Laura are the minds. Clare told them not to forget their subordinates. They would not be fighting alone. Both answered "Yes." Scathach had already etched that lesson in. They were soldiers, not bloodthirsty beasts.[4]
The surface was burned from orbit first. Kiana ordered the fire, five minutes of it, until the planet glowed and the creatures on the surface were dead. She took eighteen thousand soldiers with her. The rest stayed on the Slayers. Caelus was on another spacecraft. The two of them went to the launch hangars and jumped from orbit, the same way Clare, Conan, and Kaleb had. The sky filled as if meteors were falling. At two hundred meters, Kiana and Caelus unfolded their Dragon wings. The regular soldiers' thrusters came on by themselves. They landed in a rumble and ignored the fire. They were Beings of fire, and the armor protected them as well. The orders that split the landing into squads of fifty, and the order of no mercy, were Kiana's.[5]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
The Emperor approved an offensive on several fronts against the sectors nearest The Black Eclipse, with priority on the systems that held the faction's transit and supply routes. John took the reconnaissance of the border. Kaleb coordinated the attacks on military installations. Conan assembled the troops who would occupy and hold what was taken. Kiana and Caelus watched the movement of troops between the fronts and made sure reinforcements arrived wherever they were needed. The force was not to advance in only one direction. While part of the fleet put pressure on the defensive installations, other units looked for positions from which the offensive could be widened.[6]

### Skills & Abilities
The headers follow Superpower Wiki terminology. Each entry is what he is built as, or what he does with it.

#### Hybrid Physiology
- Dragonoid Foundation: The durable frame, chosen for robustness and for compatibility with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. The lean swimmer's build is the visible body. It is still that frame.[1][2]
- Racial Composite: Human adaptability and ingenuity, Angelic obedience and kinder tendencies, Demonic aggression and cruelty, a Faerie connection to Nature, and Vampire regeneration and metamorphosis, fused by Victor's Essence.[1]
- Soul Fragment: A minute portion of Victor's Soul, meant to grow with the soldier and to keep the racial pieces together.[1]
- Combat Blessing: Blessings from Victor's combat-related authorities.[1]
- Demonic Inheritance: Among the siblings born that day, he and Kiana are the ones who most inherited the Demonic side. Clare's word for the pair is killing machines.[4]

#### Supernatural Condition
- Supernatural Strength: He is the lean extreme of the four men. John and Kaleb are the builds between him and Conan. The leanness is not a lack of the Dragonoid's strength. At ten, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] already treats a mistake from one of them as something that can erase a city.[1][2]
- Supernatural Agility: The design stands about four meters and is still as agile as an Elf.[1]
- Supernatural Durability: The Dragonoid frame is the one built to carry the modifications and then the war.[1]

#### Regenerative Healing Factor
- Vampiric Regeneration: Kept in the design on top of the Dragonoid durability.[1]

#### Shapeshifting
- Size Shifting: He can leave the four-meter frame. The change is there so that size does not make infiltration, or work among smaller people, impossible.[1]

#### Wing Manifestation
- Dragon Wings: On the drop he unfolds them. At two hundred meters above the hive world, he and Kiana open their Dragon wings and slow the fall. The regular soldiers on the same drop use thrusters.[5]

#### Flight
- Orbital Entry: He jumps from a spacecraft in orbit and comes down with the landing force. The wings are what bleed the last of the fall.[5]

#### Fire Immunity
- Beings of Fire: He lands in a planet the fleet has set burning and ignores the fire. The soldiers of that drop are Beings of fire, and such a thing cannot affect them. The armor protects them as well.[5]

#### Berserker Mode
- Fury of the Emperor: Conan, Caelus, and Kiana are the berserkers who destroy everything with the fury of the Emperor in their hearts.[4]
- The Kill: Clare's order is that he and Kiana eliminate every hostile on the planet once the first objective is done, and that they do what they do best.[4]
- The Limit: Told not to forget the subordinates, because they will not be fighting alone, he answers "Yes." [[Scathach]]'s lesson is that they are soldiers, not bloodthirsty beasts.[4]

#### Enhanced Intelligence
- Observation: In the first war he takes a different posture from the others and watches the whole field before he decides.[3]
- Inherited Memory: Ten years of other men's wars, tactics, and strategy, kept as lessons and not as his own life.[2]
- God-Class Talent: Judged against the heroes and Gods [[Scathach]] had trained, the seven sit in that company.[7]
- Assassination: Hassan-i Sabbah, on the ways of taking a life.[7]
- Politics: [[Julius Caesar]], on rule.[7]
- Philosophy: [[Abe-no-Seimei]], on judgment.[7]
- Martial Training: [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], built on how each of them entered the first war.[7][3]

#### Leadership
- The Drop: He comes down with Kiana and the eighteen thousand. The squad orders on the ground are hers. The assignment to clear the planet is Clare's, and it names both of them.[4][5]
- The Fronts: On the Black Eclipse border he and Kiana watch the movement between the fronts and see that reinforcements arrive where they are needed, so the offensive is not one push.[6]

#### Weapon Proficiency
- Plasma Firearm: Issued for the first war. The batteries recharge through sunlight or other Energy.[3]
- Miasma Sword: Forged from metals of the deepest Hell, where the Miasma is dense and toxic. A cut poisons.[3]

#### Power Suit
- Adaptive Armor: The Imperial suit issued to the Originals. It changes with the role and carries an assistance system based on [[Akasha Elderblood]] for the body, the vitals, and the field.[3]
- Environmental Protection: On the burning world the armor is the second reason the fire does not take him. The first is that they are Beings of fire.[5]
