Frederick Winter, commonly known as Fred, is a recurring supporting character in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]], one of [[Victor Elderblood]]'s closest childhood friends and the first individual to become a Daywalker, a superior variation of Noble Vampires created through Victor's powers. Originally an ordinary Human from the United States, Frederick became involved with supernatural society after finding a Divine spear belonging to the Inquisition. His ability to handle the weapon attracted the attention of the Church, bringing him into contact with [[Mizuki Elderblood]] and the legendary Onmyō master [[Abe-no-Seimei]]. Their encounter revealed his extraordinary aptitude for [[Onmyoujutsu]], a supernatural discipline based upon Faith, spiritual techniques, talismans and the invocation of Divine power.[1][2][3][4]
Frederick belongs to the small circle of individuals who knew Victor before his transformation into a Vampire. Alongside [[Andrew Walter]], [[Edward Jonathan Elizabeth Lykos]] and [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]], he maintained a friendship with Victor throughout their childhood and adolescence, when Victor's illness prevented him from participating normally in many activities enjoyed by his peers. The relationship survived the discovery of supernatural society, Victor's evolution into a Vampire Progenitor, his ascension to Godhood and the establishment of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Despite the increasingly extraordinary circumstances surrounding their lives, Frederick continues treating Victor with the informal familiarity of a childhood companion rather than restricting their relationship to the reverence ordinarily shown toward the Emperor.[5][6]
Although Frederick initially lacked any supernatural characteristics, his natural athleticism and remarkable aptitude for spiritual techniques allowed him to progress far beyond ordinary Human limitations. Abe-no-Seimei recognized his talent, while Mizuki became responsible for much of his practical education. Frederick eventually completed foundational training that ordinarily required prolonged instruction beginning during childhood in less than a year. His accomplishments included supernatural concealment, infiltration, talisman-based spellcasting and increasingly sophisticated applications of [[Divine Energy]]. Even [[Amaterasu]] Elderblood, whose knowledge of Japanese Divine traditions far exceeded that of ordinary practitioners, was impressed by the speed of his development.[7][8]
Frederick's development underwent a fundamental transformation when Victor reconstructed his existence into a new variation of Noble Vampire. Rather than converting him into a conventional Plebeian Vampire or granting him the restricted Blood Dragon lineage, Victor created the Daywalker, an advanced Vampiric variation possessing superior racial characteristics, immunity to the ordinary sunlight weakness, an enhanced Count Form and the ability to obtain nourishment from conventional food. Frederick retained the need to consume blood periodically and remained vulnerable to opposing Holy and Light-related powers. His conversion was also unusually complete: unlike ordinary Humans transformed into Vampires, whose Souls retained a residual Human component, Frederick became a fully Vampiric existence while preserving the Onmyō abilities he had previously cultivated.[9][8]
The preservation of his Onmyoujutsu was particularly important because his supernatural development had originally depended upon Faith and the invocation of Gods associated with Japanese spiritual traditions. Victor deliberately designed the Daywalker variation to accommodate those powers rather than forcing Frederick to abandon years of training. His Vampiric physiology consequently functions alongside his religious techniques, providing an unusually powerful foundation through which Divine Energy can be channeled without making Frederick a God or granting him independent ownership of the Divinities he invokes.[8]
As Victor's Empire expanded, Frederick's relationship with the Imperial Divine system became increasingly important. He eventually began directing his Faith toward Victor himself, allowing him to invoke the Emperor's numerous Divine authorities through Onmyoujutsu. His first demonstrated application of this connection employed Victor's Concept of Fear, producing an enormous sphere of crimson Negativity through a basic talisman technique. The result demonstrated that the power of a religious invocation depended not merely upon the practitioner's personal strength but also upon the authority of the Divine Being supplying the Energy.[10][11]
Following the Empire's prolonged period of accelerated development, Frederick became the highest-ranking and most famous practitioner among its Onmyō warriors, serving as their leader under the patronage of Mizuki and Amaterasu. His direct relationship with Victor, exceptional talent and accumulated accomplishments gave him extraordinary religious prestige, with many worshippers regarding him as an unofficial Pope or saint-like figure. His position does not supersede that of [[Valeria Alekerth]], the High Priestess responsible for the Empire's official religious organization, but it places Frederick among the most prominent representatives of its Onmyō tradition.[12]
Frederick's mature combat abilities include planetary-class physical and supernatural capabilities, advanced Onmyoujutsu and the ability to invoke Divine authorities belonging to several Pantheons. He is classified as a V-05 threat within the Imperial system, indicating Solar System-level maximum destructive potential through the Divine powers available to him. This classification accounts for his complete available arsenal, including powers borrowed from Gods vastly stronger than himself, rather than establishing that his ordinary physical attacks independently possess Solar System-level destructive capacity. Despite his extraordinary development, he retains the humorous, irreverent and enthusiastic personality that distinguished him during his Human life.[12][13]

### Physical Appearance
Frederick is a handsome man who originally stood approximately 180 centimeters tall, with black hair, black eyes and a relatively ordinary Human complexion. During his introduction, he wore casual clothing, including a shirt depicting an anime girl, reflecting his enthusiasm for Japanese animation and otaku culture. Although his original appearance was comparatively ordinary, he possessed considerable natural athleticism and enough agility to navigate urban obstacles through parkour while attempting to escape Mizuki.[2]
His appearance changed considerably during his training. Mizuki's demanding instruction strengthened his body and improved his physical conditioning, gradually transforming him into a more muscular and athletic young man. His hairstyle and clothing also became more refined. During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s reunion with him in the former [[Egyptian Pantheon]]'s territory, Frederick wore stylish clothing that emphasized his improved physique, and his hairstyle bore a resemblance to Victor's earlier appearance. His overall presentation was considerably more mature than that of the young Human who had previously fled through [[New York]] wearing anime merchandise.[14]
Frederick underwent a more fundamental transformation after becoming the first Daywalker. His formerly black eyes became blood-red through the Vampiric lineage inherited from Victor, while his skin developed a noticeably pale complexion. His body acquired the supernatural attractiveness and physical characteristics of a superior Noble Vampire, retaining enough of his original appearance to remain recognizable. The transformation also fundamentally reconstructed his Soul and racial foundation rather than merely adding Vampiric abilities to an otherwise Human body.[9][8]
Although Frederick resembles an ordinary Noble Vampire externally, his biological and spiritual structure is distinct from conventional transformed Vampires. Victor's modifications produced a complete Vampiric Soul and a body capable of supporting both superior Noble Vampire characteristics and [[Onmyoujutsu]]. His appearance therefore conceals a far more advanced supernatural existence than that of an ordinary converted Human, although his exact physical dimensions and the complete appearance of his enhanced Count Form have not been fully established.[8]
During the later Imperial era, Frederick wears advanced black combat armor decorated with gold and crimson accents. The equipment is suited to the military and technological standards of the [[Elderblood Empire]] and accompanies a technologically advanced Western-style sword. Through Onmyoujutsu, he can surround his weapon with supernatural Energy and transform its effective reach into that of an enormous Energy greatsword. His military appearance contrasts sharply with his earlier casual attire, reflecting his development into an elite Imperial warrior and commander.[12]

### Personality
Frederick is cheerful, humorous, irreverent, intelligent, resourceful and frequently immature. He possesses an intense enthusiasm for anime, manga, games, fictional women and Japanese popular culture, often interpreting unfamiliar supernatural situations through references to the entertainment he enjoys. This behavior earned him a reputation as a Man of Culture, although his enthusiasm frequently produces exaggerated or inappropriate reactions that irritate those surrounding him. Beneath his foolish exterior, Frederick possesses considerable intelligence, exceptional learning ability and an instinctive talent for adapting to unfamiliar circumstances.[2][4]
His fascination with supernatural women is especially pronounced when they resemble characters or creatures associated with Japanese fiction. During a gathering of major supernatural powers, Frederick becomes extremely excited upon seeing [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]], a Nine-Tailed Kitsune, and subsequently reacts with equal enthusiasm when [[Yotsuba Kuroka]] appears. He repeatedly photographs the women and behaves with such intensity that Edward struggles to restrain him, while Pepper shares much of his excitement. His reactions demonstrate how strongly his recreational interests influence his behavior even after he has become accustomed to the existence of supernatural beings.[15]
Frederick also shares strong opinions about entertainment with members of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family. Alongside Ruby, Pepper, Leona and Eve, he considers the continuation of a famous anime series about a blond ninja to be an unacceptable sequel. Their shared enthusiasm contributes to the informal relationships that survive within Victor's increasingly complicated household, illustrating how ordinary interests remain important even among individuals whose lives have become dominated by supernatural warfare and Divine politics.[16]
Despite his immaturity, Frederick possesses considerable practical intelligence. His initial escape from Mizuki demonstrates athletic coordination, spatial awareness and the ability to improvise under pressure. Rather than attempting to confront an opponent whose strength he cannot match, he uses obstacles, rooftops, environmental distractions and hiding places to prolong his escape. His pursuit of supernatural abilities similarly reveals that he can recognize opportunities capable of improving his circumstances, although his motivations are often mixed with romantic fantasies and expectations borrowed from fiction.[2][4]
His intelligence becomes particularly apparent through his achievements as an Onmyō practitioner. [[Abe-no-Seimei]] recognizes his potential, while Frederick's rapid mastery of foundational techniques demonstrates exceptional comprehension and adaptability. The discipline ordinarily requires years of training and spiritual preparation, yet Frederick develops the necessary foundations in less than a year despite beginning as an adult. His natural talent impresses [[Amaterasu]] and establishes that his apparently foolish behavior conceals a genuinely exceptional aptitude for supernatural learning.[8]
Frederick's resourcefulness is equally evident in his infiltration of an Inquisition installation. He employs supernatural concealment to bypass the facility's external defenses and subsequently disguises himself as a priest after entering the premises. By separating the initial infiltration from his later impersonation, he avoids the identity checks normally conducted upon people entering from outside. Ruby and Kaguya regard the achievement as remarkable because even experienced supernatural assassins would find the operation difficult, particularly when Frederick is still biologically Human.[7]
His personal ambitions are often considerably more mundane than those of the powerful supernatural beings surrounding him. Frederick initially becomes interested in joining the Inquisition because obtaining supernatural abilities resembles the progression of fictional protagonists he admires. However, the restrictions associated with the Church's religious institution quickly discourage him, particularly those conflicting with his desire for romantic relationships. Abe-no-Seimei's alternative offer proves considerably more attractive because [[Onmyoujutsu]] provides access to spiritual techniques without requiring Frederick to adopt the lifestyle he associates with the Church.[3][4]
His romantic interests eventually develop beyond jokes and unrealistic fantasies. The influence of [[Aphrodite]]'s authority over Love helps him find the courage to confess his feelings, and he later establishes a genuine relationship with a Noble Vampire woman. Frederick becomes deeply satisfied with this development, regarding the end of his prolonged single life as an achievement worthy of celebration. Nevertheless, his Human physiology initially creates difficulties because his partner possesses the supernatural endurance and regenerative characteristics of a Noble Vampire, eventually contributing to his decision to accept Victor's offer of Vampiric transformation.[17][14][9]
Frederick is genuinely loyal to Victor and the people who have remained important throughout his life. He recognizes that Victor's increasingly extraordinary power provides his family and friends with protection against threats they could never reasonably confront as ordinary Humans. Although he often teases Victor and envies his romantic circumstances, Frederick values their friendship deeply and is willing to assist him when necessary. His familiarity with Victor also allows him to recognize that the Emperor's overwhelming power has not completely changed his underlying personality; many of the qualities now displayed on a cosmic scale were already present during their childhood.[7][5]
That familiarity initially becomes an obstacle when Frederick attempts to direct religious Faith toward Victor. Although he intellectually understands that his friend has become an immensely powerful God, he continues perceiving him primarily as the Human companion he knew before supernatural society entered their lives. The emotional difficulty of worshipping a childhood friend prevents him from immediately developing the conviction needed for powerful Divine invocation. After witnessing a fraction of Victor's true existence, Frederick begins reconciling those identities and recognizes that his personal affection for Victor can coexist with acknowledgement of the Emperor's Divine authority.[11]
Frederick's preference for comfort and enjoyment occasionally interferes with his supernatural development. Even after acquiring a superior Daywalker body, he initially neglects to explore its full potential and remains satisfied with strength far below what Victor considers appropriate. His attachment to his wife and comparatively peaceful lifestyle contributes to this complacency. Victor becomes irritated upon discovering that Frederick has not properly developed the enhanced Count Form included within his racial transformation, recognizing that the problem is not inadequate talent but insufficient motivation to make use of the opportunities available to him.[10]
His later accomplishments demonstrate that this complacency does not permanently define his development. Frederick becomes the highest-ranking Onmyō practitioner in the Empire, accumulates extraordinary combat experience and acquires enough authority to command Imperial forces during dangerous operations. He also learns to perform supernatural techniques without relying upon spoken incantations, although he deliberately continues using them because he enjoys the dramatic presentation. Even Andrew's criticism fails to discourage him, with Frederick openly embracing the idea that a person should not abandon harmless aspects of their childhood simply because they have become powerful or mature.[12]
Frederick is capable of adopting a serious and authoritative demeanor when circumstances demand it. During a planetary military operation, his behavior changes immediately upon the arrival of Imperial Spartans. He stops exchanging jokes with Andrew and begins issuing operational orders, directing the soldiers to secure important continental regions and prepare for extraction. His conduct demonstrates that his humor is a personal preference rather than an inability to recognize military responsibility, while his willingness to employ Divine authority for pacification rather than continued destruction reveals a practical understanding of the objectives assigned to him.[12]
His loyalty becomes particularly significant as the Empire prepares for war against civilizations from beyond its original Sector. Frederick understands that Victor would never willingly submit to another ruler and regards resistance as preferable to surrendering the freedom of their civilization. Nevertheless, when the possibility of a major intergalactic war becomes unavoidable, his first priority remains protecting his family rather than pursuing personal glory. He accepts that greater strength may be necessary and seriously considers entering the exceptionally dangerous Tower of Nightmares after Victor provides him with the opportunity.[18]

### Synopsis

#### The Awakening
Frederick belonged to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s childhood friendship group alongside [[Andrew Walter]], [[Edward Jonathan Elizabeth Lykos|Edward Lykos]] and Leona Lykos. Their friendship developed during the years when Victor was still an ordinary Human suffering from a debilitating medical condition, while Leona experienced her own difficulties because of her unusual appearance and Werewolf heritage. The group remained closely connected despite these circumstances, providing Victor with some of the most important relationships of his early life.[1]
Fred is mentioned among the childhood friends associated with Leona's school years. His friendship with her, Andrew and Victor contributed to the attention Leona received from other students, including girls who disliked her association with the group. Victor later reassures Leona that she can rely upon Edward, Andrew, Fred and himself whenever she requires assistance, establishing Frederick as part of the support network surrounding the people Victor cared about long before becoming a Vampire.[1]
Frederick initially remains outside the supernatural conflicts surrounding Victor's transformation. Unlike the Noble Vampires, [[Werewolves]] and other supernatural beings increasingly entering Victor's life, Fred lacks inherent supernatural protection and possesses little understanding of the hidden world. His eventual discovery of supernatural society begins independently of Victor's transformation, bringing him into contact with the Inquisition and Japanese spiritual traditions.

#### A New Pillar
Frederick's direct involvement with supernatural society begins after he finds a Divine spear connected to the Inquisition. The Church becomes interested in the fact that an apparently ordinary Human can handle the weapon and sends Mizuki to recruit him as a Hunter. The circumstances surrounding the spear include the intervention of Church personnel and interference with Frederick's memories, which Mizuki subsequently helps restore. What initially appears to be a routine recruitment effort becomes considerably more complicated because Frederick possesses abilities and personal characteristics that neither Mizuki nor her master expects from an ordinary Human.[2]
While being pursued through [[New York]], Frederick relies upon his natural athleticism and knowledge of parkour to escape. He runs through narrow streets, climbs walls and fire escapes, navigates rooftops and throws obstacles behind him in an attempt to delay Mizuki. His physical abilities impress her enough that she recognizes him as unusually fast and agile for an ordinary Human. Nevertheless, his efforts are insufficient against a trained supernatural practitioner, and Mizuki invokes the Lightning of [[Takemikazuchi]] to continue the pursuit.[2]
Frederick becomes increasingly terrified as the encounter exposes him to supernatural techniques he cannot explain. His attempts to escape include hiding in a dumpster and using the urban environment to conceal himself, while his exaggerated reactions and references to fictional characters demonstrate the eccentric personality that will continue defining him throughout his life. The pursuit also reveals the presence of [[Abe-no-Seimei]], whose spiritual existence Frederick can perceive and interact with despite lacking formal supernatural training.[2]
The ability to interact with a spiritual existence is particularly noteworthy because it suggests that Frederick possesses a natural aptitude extending beyond ordinary physical talent. Abe-no-Seimei takes an interest in him, while Mizuki gradually realizes that the apparently foolish young man may possess considerably more potential than his behavior suggests. Their interactions nevertheless remain difficult because Frederick repeatedly makes inappropriate remarks and approaches dangerous situations through the mentality of an enthusiastic anime fan.[2]
The confrontation eventually brings Frederick into contact with members of his childhood friendship group, including Edward and Leona. At one point, Edward intervenes when Mizuki's increasingly violent reaction threatens Fred, demonstrating the difference between an ordinary Human and a developed Alpha Werewolf. Frederick's behavior also provokes Edward, who understands how to make his friend cooperate by threatening his treasured manga collection. These encounters reinforce the unusual combination of genuine loyalty and constant teasing that characterizes their relationship.[2][19]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s reunion with Mizuki further connects Frederick's circumstances to the increasingly complicated supernatural world surrounding the Walker household. Mizuki initially struggles to accept Victor's status as a Vampire Count because his rapid development contradicts her previous understanding of Vampirism. Frederick, meanwhile, becomes increasingly fascinated by the possibility of obtaining powers comparable to those he has previously encountered only through fiction.[19]
Frederick initially considers joining the Church as a Hunter. However, the religious and personal restrictions associated with the Inquisition quickly discourage him, particularly because he hopes to eventually establish romantic relationships and acquire the freedom he associates with powerful fictional protagonists. The possibility of becoming a supernatural warrior remains attractive, but he does not wish to sacrifice every other ambition simply to obtain power.[3]
Abe-no-Seimei offers Frederick an alternative by recognizing his aptitude for [[Onmyoujutsu]]. The Japanese spiritual discipline allows practitioners to employ talismans, prayers, Faith and Divine authority without requiring them to become conventional Church [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]]. Victor immediately encourages Frederick to accept the opportunity, recognizing both its practical value and its compatibility with his friend's personality. Victor is also interested in the unusual techniques associated with Japanese spiritual traditions and wants Frederick to become strong enough to protect himself as supernatural conflicts increasingly endanger ordinary Humans.[4]
Mizuki becomes Frederick's principal instructor, while Abe-no-Seimei's recognition of his potential establishes the foundation for his future supernatural development. What begins as a failed attempt at conventional Church recruitment consequently leads Frederick toward an entirely different path, one that eventually allows him to become among the most accomplished practitioners of Onmyoujutsu in the [[Elderblood Empire]].

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Frederick's education under Mizuki develops rapidly. Although he begins formal training as an adult rather than receiving the childhood preparation ordinarily required by Japanese spiritual traditions, he displays exceptional aptitude for absorbing supernatural knowledge. His physical conditioning improves considerably alongside his spiritual techniques, producing a Human whose strength, endurance and practical capabilities increasingly exceed those of ordinary civilians. His development also allows him to become more directly involved in the activities of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s expanding supernatural circle.[8]
One of Frederick's most impressive accomplishments occurs when he successfully infiltrates an Inquisition installation and steals confidential information. Rather than attempting to overpower the guards or defeat the facility's defenses through direct confrontation, he casts a concealment technique and bypasses the external security systems. Once inside, he disguises himself as a priest and proceeds through the installation without undergoing the identity checks ordinarily imposed upon individuals arriving from outside.[7]
The operation demonstrates Frederick's understanding of the relationship between supernatural techniques and ordinary security procedures. His concealment spell allows him to penetrate the facility, while his later impersonation provides a convincing explanation for his presence once he is already inside. The combination proves effective because the guards do not treat someone apparently belonging to the organization and already present within the installation as an unauthorized visitor. His achievement is especially remarkable because he remains Human and does not possess the natural stealth abilities, durability or speed ordinarily associated with advanced supernatural races.[7]
Ruby and Kaguya acknowledge the difficulty of the accomplishment. Ruby recognizes that successfully entering a protected Inquisition facility and obtaining information is something very few individuals can perform, while Kaguya confirms that such an operation would challenge even experienced supernatural assassins. Despite considering Frederick's behavior frequently foolish, both women acknowledge his competence and the practical value of his Onmyō abilities.[7]
The incident also strengthens Frederick's confidence in the supernatural path he has chosen. His abilities are not restricted to offensive spells or religious ceremonies; they can be employed for infiltration, concealment, intelligence gathering and unconventional problem-solving. The versatility of [[Onmyoujutsu]] allows him to overcome obstacles that would be difficult to solve through physical strength alone, establishing a foundation for the broader Divine invocation techniques he develops later.
As supernatural conflicts begin threatening the Human World more directly, Frederick becomes increasingly aware of the danger surrounding Victor's family and their childhood friends. He understands that ordinary Humans have little ability to protect themselves against supernatural armies, ancient Vampires and Divine beings, and he recognizes the practical importance of remaining connected to Victor's increasingly powerful faction. His friendship with Andrew, Edward and Leona remains important throughout these developments, while his personal training allows him to participate in situations that would previously have been beyond his capabilities.[7]

#### Demonic War
Frederick remains connected to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household as the conflict involving the supernatural factions of Earth escalates. His training has already provided him with enough strength to participate in increasingly dangerous activities, although his personality continues making him one of the most conspicuous and unpredictable members of the childhood friendship group.
During the gathering of major supernatural powers, Frederick watches the arrival of prominent representatives from the different races and Pantheons. The appearance of Evie Moriarty, Queen of the Witches, impresses the assembled observers, while the arrival of the Japanese Youkai attracts Frederick's particular attention. When the Nine-Tailed Fox [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]] appears, Frederick reacts with overwhelming excitement, repeatedly taking photographs and celebrating the opportunity to witness a genuine Kitsune.[15]
His enthusiasm intensifies when [[Yotsuba Kuroka]] appears alongside Haruna. Frederick and Pepper become equally excited by the presence of a supernatural cat girl, while Edward attempts unsuccessfully to restrain Frederick's exaggerated reactions. The incident demonstrates that his increasing familiarity with supernatural society has not diminished the enthusiasm he possesses for creatures and characters associated with Japanese popular culture.[15]
Despite his eccentric behavior, Frederick remains a loyal member of Victor's social circle during the escalating Demonic conflict. The dangers surrounding the Human World reinforce the importance of his supernatural education, while his ability to use Faith-based techniques offers a practical means of contributing without requiring the biological advantages possessed by Vampires or [[Werewolves]].

#### King Among Kings
Frederick's personal life begins developing alongside the supernatural transformation of the world. The increasing influence of [[Aphrodite]]'s Divine authority over Love affects numerous individuals, and Frederick receives the courage necessary to express his romantic feelings toward a woman who interests him. This development gradually leads toward the relationship that becomes one of the most important aspects of his later life.[17]
His connection to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family also continues through shared interests outside combat. Frederick belongs to the group of anime enthusiasts who strongly dislike certain sequels to popular fictional franchises. His opinions are shared by Ruby, Pepper, Leona and Eve, illustrating how ordinary entertainment and recreational interests continue providing common ground among people whose lives are otherwise dominated by extraordinary supernatural responsibilities.[16]
Frederick's supernatural progression nevertheless remains distinct from the biological evolutions experienced by many members of Victor's family. He continues pursuing [[Onmyoujutsu]] through Mizuki's instruction, strengthening his ability to invoke Divine powers while retaining his Human physiology. Although the discipline allows him to perform increasingly impressive supernatural feats, his original Race continues imposing limitations upon the amount of power he can safely endure and the physical demands he can sustain.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually encounters Frederick while travelling through territories belonging to his developing civilization. By this period, Frederick has established a relationship with an unnamed Noble Vampire woman and possesses a considerably more developed physique than during his early Human life. He lives in territory associated with the former [[Egyptian Pantheon]], where his relationship with Victor provides him with privileges and access unavailable to ordinary residents. His improved clothing, hairstyle and muscular development reflect both his training under Mizuki and the influence of his romantic partner.[14]
Frederick is delighted by the progress of his romantic life and remains characteristically irreverent when discussing Victor's appearance and extraordinary attractiveness. Even the supernatural Charm surrounding Victor produces uncomfortable thoughts that Frederick attempts to dismiss through humor and the informal rules governing friendship between the men. Their conversation nevertheless demonstrates that Frederick continues regarding Victor as a childhood companion rather than approaching him exclusively as the Dragon God whose authority now extends across multiple worlds.[14][9]
The reunion reveals an important limitation in Frederick's continued existence as a Human. His wife possesses the supernatural endurance and regenerative abilities of a Noble Vampire, making their intimate relationship physically demanding despite Frederick's considerable training. He can reinforce himself through Onmyō talismans, but those methods cannot permanently eliminate the fundamental difference between Human physiology and an advanced Vampiric body. The situation causes Frederick to reconsider the advantages of remaining Human, particularly because his supernatural abilities have already become essential to his lifestyle.[9]
Victor recognizes that Frederick's continued Human existence will become an increasingly serious limitation as the surrounding civilization confronts stronger supernatural and extraterrestrial powers. He possesses the knowledge and authority necessary to create or modify supernatural [[Races (Flamel)|Races]], but he does not intend to distribute the Blood Dragon lineage casually. Rather than granting Frederick the same racial foundation reserved for members of his restricted Draconic family, Victor proposes creating a superior variation of Noble Vampire that will provide the necessary improvements without making Frederick a True Dragon.[9]
The proposed variation becomes known as the Daywalker. Its foundation includes superior Noble Vampire physiology, immunity to ordinary sunlight, the ability to survive on conventional food and an enhanced version of the Count Form. Frederick will still need to consume blood periodically, and the variation retains vulnerabilities associated with its fundamental position as a creature of the night. Holy and Light-related powers remain opposing influences capable of threatening the Daywalker, preventing the racial improvement from eliminating every traditional Vampiric weakness.[9][8]
Frederick is particularly concerned about losing his Onmyō abilities through the transformation. His spiritual techniques depend upon Faith and Divine power, while ordinary Vampires possess an existence associated with the darker side of the supernatural balance. Victor refuses to create a variation that would discard an important portion of Frederick's accomplishments and assures him that the new Race will preserve his previous supernatural development.[8]
Victor's intervention reconstructs Frederick's body and Soul into a complete Vampiric existence. Unlike the conventional conversion of a Human into a Vampire, which ordinarily preserves a residual Human component, Frederick's transformation produces a fully Vampiric Soul. His eyes become blood-red, his complexion becomes pale and his appearance acquires the enhanced attractiveness associated with developed Noble Vampires. The new body provides him with an improved supernatural foundation while allowing the continued use of [[Onmyoujutsu]].[8]
The preservation of Frederick's spiritual powers impresses [[Amaterasu]], who recognizes the unusual compatibility achieved through Victor's intervention. She is equally surprised to discover how rapidly Frederick completed his foundational training, since the discipline ordinarily requires extensive education and preparation beginning during childhood. His accomplishments confirm [[Abe-no-Seimei]]'s earlier recognition of his talent and demonstrate that Frederick's potential extends considerably beyond what his earlier Human limitations suggested.[8]
Victor subsequently involves Amaterasu, [[Tsukuyomi]] and Susanoo in Frederick's further development. Their expertise provides additional opportunities to improve his techniques, while his new Daywalker physiology allows him to endure supernatural development that would have been difficult or impossible for his previous Human body. Frederick becomes the first member of the Daywalker variation, establishing a new Vampiric lineage distinct from conventional Noble Vampires and the restricted Blood Dragon Race.[8]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Frederick continues developing as the [[Elderblood Empire]] expands beyond the supernatural territories that originally defined [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s civilization. His Daywalker body provides a far greater foundation for supernatural development than his former Human physiology, but his personal priorities initially prevent him from exploiting its full potential.
When Victor reunites with Frederick, Andrew and Edward, he discovers that Fred possesses approximately Mid-Level God-class overall power. Although this would once have represented extraordinary strength compared with the ancient supernatural races of Earth, Victor considers it inadequate for an individual possessing a superior Noble Vampire body personally created through his powers. Frederick has become accustomed to a comparatively comfortable life and has not seriously explored every characteristic of his new Race.[5][10]
Victor becomes particularly dissatisfied upon discovering that Frederick has neglected the enhanced Count Form included within the Daywalker variation. The problem is not a lack of talent, knowledge or supernatural potential but Frederick's preference for enjoying his personal life rather than enduring the demanding training required to develop his abilities further. Victor consequently threatens to correct his friend's complacency through personal instruction, making it clear that extraordinary opportunities are meaningless when their recipient refuses to make use of them.[10]
Their reunion also demonstrates how differently the members of Victor's childhood friendship group have developed. Andrew possesses abilities connected to the Inquisition, Edward has become an important member of the Lykos Werewolf lineage, and Frederick relies upon [[Onmyoujutsu]] supported by his new Daywalker body. Their supernatural paths differ considerably, yet their conversations retain the humor, arguments and informal familiarity that characterized their relationships before Victor became Emperor.[5][10]
Frederick recognizes that Victor's immense power has not entirely changed his personality. The Emperor remains protective of his family, intolerant of betrayal, aggressive toward threats and unusually generous toward those he considers worthy of his loyalty. What has changed is the scale of his abilities and the consequences of his decisions. Frederick therefore continues treating Victor as the same individual he knew during childhood, even though that familiarity eventually becomes an obstacle to developing religious Faith in him.[5]
Victor redirects Frederick's attention toward the foundations of Onmyoujutsu. Because the discipline operates through Faith and the invocation of Divine beings, Frederick has traditionally relied upon the Gods associated with Japanese supernatural traditions. Victor possesses numerous Divinities of his own, including authorities related to Blood, War, Fear, Despair, Murder, Strength and Destruction. The possibility of invoking Victor directly provides Frederick with access to an extraordinarily powerful source of [[Divine Energy]], potentially allowing even basic techniques to achieve results far beyond their previous limitations.[11]
Frederick initially struggles with the proposal because he does not naturally regard Victor as an object of worship. Their childhood friendship makes it psychologically difficult to perceive him as a Divine Being, even though Frederick understands that Victor possesses strength surpassing the traditional Gods of their original world. Victor recognizes the obstacle and demonstrates a fraction of his true existence, manifesting an imposing Imperial form before allowing the three men to witness an enormous Dragon whose apparent size exceeds entire Galaxies.[11]
Victor transports his friends through extraordinary environments, including the space between galaxies and worlds possessing radically different characteristics. The experience exposes Frederick, Andrew and Edward to the overwhelming scale of the Emperor's existence, allowing them to perceive what Victor has become rather than relying upon abstract descriptions of his power. The demonstration does not erase their friendship but establishes the emotional foundation Frederick requires to genuinely acknowledge Victor as a God.[11]
Frederick subsequently prepares a talisman and invokes Victor as the God of Fear. The invocation draws upon the Emperor's Divine authority and manifests an enormous concentration of crimson Negativity. Frederick releases the resulting sphere through his talisman, sending it beyond the planet in seconds before it detonates in space. The attack surprises Frederick himself because he has employed only a basic Onmyō technique rather than a sophisticated invocation designed to request a greater portion of a God's power.[11]
Edward recognizes the danger posed by the attack, acknowledging that it possesses enough power to threaten some of his strongest warriors even though he believes he could personally avoid or counter it. Frederick understands that the result is principally attributable to the extraordinary authority of the Divine Being supplying the Energy, with his Daywalker body providing an additional foundation through which that power can be employed. The technique demonstrates the difference between a practitioner's personal strength and the supernatural authority they can temporarily borrow through Faith.[11]
Frederick also recognizes the destructive possibilities offered by Victor's other Divinities, including Despair, Murder, Strength and Destruction. These authorities provide potential sources for future invocations, although the demonstrated technique involves Fear and its associated Negativity rather than independent spells employing every Concept mentioned during their conversation.[11]
Victor personally blesses Frederick, Andrew and Edward, reinforcing the protective relationship he maintains with his oldest friends. The Blessing establishes a connection to the Emperor that remains meaningful regardless of the worlds or circumstances they eventually encounter. Frederick's new understanding of Victor and his Divine authority becomes an important foundation for his subsequent religious development, eventually contributing to his unusually privileged position among the Empire's supernatural practitioners.[11]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
The Empire's enormous period of accelerated development transforms Frederick's position within supernatural society. As Imperial civilization advances through successive millennia, Fred accumulates extraordinary experience and continues strengthening his Onmyō techniques while maintaining his relationships with Andrew, Edward and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Their friendship remains recognizable despite the transformation of their surroundings into an interstellar civilization governed by the Elderblood dynasty.
During the Empire's hundred-thousand-year celebration, Frederick gathers with Andrew, Edward and members of the Lykos family, including several of Victor's daughters. Among those present are Leone Luna Elena Elderblood, daughter of Leona, and [[Mirabelle Elizabeth Elderblood]], daughter of Maya. The gathering highlights how drastically the standards of the younger generation have changed, since even individuals possessing enormous supernatural strength can appear comparatively ordinary when judged against True Dragon Goddesses and the most powerful members of Victor's family.[6]
Frederick, Andrew and Edward continue behaving much as they did during their childhood, exchanging jokes and arguments despite the immense amount of subjective Time they have experienced. Their familiarity contrasts with the public reverence surrounding the Emperor, whose overwhelming power and Divine status make ordinary Imperial citizens unlikely to address him with the casual intimacy maintained by his oldest companions.[6]
As the Empire's religious institutions develop, [[Onmyoujutsu]] becomes an important supernatural discipline supported by the patronage of Mizuki and [[Amaterasu]]. Frederick rises to become the highest-ranking and most famous practitioner among the Imperial Onmyō warriors, assuming a leadership position within the tradition. His exceptional aptitude, accumulated accomplishments and personal connection to Victor give him extraordinary religious prestige, with many worshippers regarding him as an unofficial Pope or saint-like figure.[12]
The Imperial Onmyō warriors and the reorganized Imperial Inquisition operate within the larger religious system governed by High Priestess [[Valeria Alekerth]]. Both traditions employ Faith and borrow Divine power, but their methods and limitations differ. Onmyoujutsu can invoke compatible Gods from different Pantheons, provided the respective Divine authorities permit the connection, while the techniques of the Imperial Inquisition draw more directly upon Victor and the Gods formally belonging to the [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]].[12]
This distinction allows Frederick to employ an exceptionally versatile religious system. His Onmyō techniques can request power from Divine beings outside the immediate membership of Dragons Nest, while Andrew's Inquisition techniques are more closely restricted to the authorities of the Emperor's own Pantheon. Both systems ordinarily impose demanding requirements involving genuine Faith, the permission of the invoked God and years of training, but Frederick and Andrew enjoy unusually broad privileges because they are personally blessed by Victor.[12]
Frederick's mature abilities are demonstrated during an Imperial operation within the Andromeda Galaxy. The expedition concerns a planet already belonging to Imperial territory whose World Tree has been secured. A newly emerged extraterrestrial species has spread across the planet, displaying rapid reproduction, considerable adaptability and the potential to threaten entire planetary environments. The Fourth Legion requests a biological sample for research, prompting the deployment of Imperial Spartans and the preparation of a fleet containing more than fifty ships, including an Explorer-class vessel.[12]
Although the fleet possesses orbital weapons and sufficient military personnel to conduct the operation, Frederick volunteers to descend personally. Andrew accompanies him, and both men deploy in advanced black Imperial armor decorated with gold and crimson accents. Their decision reflects a mixture of confidence, boredom and the desire to experience direct combat despite the availability of considerably safer methods for securing the planet.[12]
Upon landing, Frederick encounters enormous alien creatures exceeding ten meters in height. He draws a technologically advanced Western sword and effortlessly cuts through one of the attackers before confronting the larger swarm. Rather than relying entirely upon his physical strength, he invokes Victor's authority as the [[Ares|God of War]], surrounding his body with violet supernatural Energy and transforming his sword into an enormous Energy greatsword.[12]
Frederick proceeds to annihilate the surrounding creatures through a combination of close-range swordsmanship and Divine empowerment. His attacks tear through their bodies, while a particularly powerful Energy discharge catastrophically alters the surrounding geography. The attack divides a continental region, effectively creating a new geographical separation. Imperial operators recognize that the damage will require environmental reconstruction, although their technology is sufficiently advanced to repair the affected terrain after the operation.[12]
Andrew criticizes Frederick for the excessive destruction, but soon demonstrates similarly overwhelming power through the techniques of the Imperial Inquisition. He invokes the Emperor's authority, manifests supernatural weapons and rapidly eliminates enemies across the planet. Frederick responds to Andrew's complaints with his usual irreverence, and their argument demonstrates how little their personal relationship has changed despite possessing powers capable of devastating entire worlds.[12]
The encounter establishes the extraordinary strength of both men. Their fundamental physical and supernatural abilities have reached planetary scales, providing immense strength, stamina, durability and flight. Their religious techniques raise their maximum threat potential considerably further because they can invoke Divine beings whose powers vastly exceed their own. Within the Imperial system, Frederick and Andrew are classified as V-05 threats, indicating the capacity to annihilate an entire Solar System when employing the full destructive potential available through their supernatural techniques.[12][13]
The V-05 classification reflects their maximum available threat rather than establishing that every ordinary attack possesses Solar System-level destructive capacity. Frederick's own Daywalker physiology and accumulated combat experience provide the foundation for his abilities, while the most extreme effects depend upon successfully channeling the authority of vastly stronger Divine beings. The distinction is particularly important because the Empire evaluates threats according to the complete capabilities and dangerous powers available to an individual rather than relying exclusively upon their ordinary physical output.[13]
Frederick also demonstrates considerable military authority when Imperial Spartans arrive on the planet. He immediately abandons his joking attitude and directs the soldiers to secure the largest continental regions and prepare them for extraction. The troops obey his commands, demonstrating that his position includes practical military leadership rather than merely honorary religious prestige.[12]
Rather than continuing to destroy the hostile species, Frederick and Andrew eventually decide to employ the Divine authority of Order. Their intention is to pacify the planet and bring its hostile inhabitants under control without unnecessarily exterminating them. Both men invoke [[Violet Elderblood Snow]], whose Divine authority can impose Order upon supernatural and physical phenomena. Their prayer unexpectedly attracts Violet's direct attention, causing her to manifest a projection before the Imperial soldiers.[12]
Violet examines the situation, including Frederick and Andrew's memories, and surveys the surrounding Solar System before performing the intervention. Her projection grows enormously, and she directly imposes Order upon the planetary environment. The hostile creatures become docile, the previously dangerous climate stabilizes and the atmosphere improves, accomplishing the intended objective without requiring the continued destruction of the species.[12][13]
The intervention is performed directly by Violet rather than representing Frederick independently controlling her Divinity of Order. His contribution consists of establishing the supernatural connection through Onmyoujutsu and participating in the invocation alongside Andrew. The incident nevertheless demonstrates the extraordinary value of his relationship with the Imperial Divine system, allowing him to request the intervention of beings whose powers vastly surpass his personal abilities.[13]
Frederick reacts with amusement and mild disappointment when Violet personally completes the operation, complaining that she has taken the dramatic moment he and Andrew originally intended for themselves. His reaction demonstrates that he has retained the same humor and enthusiasm that characterized him during his Human life. Despite becoming an elite Imperial commander and religious leader, he continues approaching extraordinary supernatural events with the informal mentality of the young man who once viewed the world through the conventions of fictional adventure stories.[13]
As the Empire's confrontation with the civilizations beyond its original Sector becomes increasingly unavoidable, Frederick faces the prospect of participating in a war far greater than the supernatural conflicts of his youth. During the large-scale military mobilization, he walks through Imperial territory alongside Andrew and Edward while the Empire prepares fleets, armies, supplies and other resources for the approaching conflict. Andrew expresses concern about the enormous number of lives potentially lost during an intergalactic war, while Frederick argues that surrendering to an enemy seeking domination would leave their civilization with no meaningful freedom.[18]
Frederick openly supports Victor's refusal to submit to another ruler. He also recognizes that the Empire possesses supernatural systems capable of preserving and reincarnating Souls, allowing citizens to receive another opportunity after death under appropriate circumstances. His characteristically humorous attitude resurfaces when he compares the possibility of reincarnation to the adventures found in fictional otherworldly stories, although Andrew has no desire to experience death merely to discover what such a future might involve.[18]
Victor unexpectedly appears before the three men while they discuss the situation. Rather than asking them to recklessly enter the coming war, he questions what they intend to do. Frederick, Andrew and Edward agree that protecting their families takes priority over pursuing personal glory or attempting to confront enemies beyond their abilities. Their answer satisfies Victor, who understands the importance of ensuring that his oldest companions do not endanger themselves unnecessarily.[18]
Victor subsequently presents the three men with Imperial badges shaped like Dragons and formed from his own power. The badges grant them access to the strongest version of the Tower of Nightmares, located on Victor's personal world. The facility has been modified for battles involving the Dragon Goddesses, making it extraordinarily dangerous even for powerful supernatural beings. Rather than forcing his friends to undergo the training, Victor offers them the opportunity to improve through their own efforts, respecting the distinct paths each has chosen.[18]
Frederick recognizes the immense danger associated with the Tower and initially expresses hesitation. Andrew likewise considers the consequences, while Edward announces that they will meet at the entrance. Their reactions suggest an understanding that greater strength will be necessary for the future, although their subsequent progress within the Tower has not been established. The opportunity represents another expression of Victor's relationship with his childhood friends: rather than arbitrarily granting them additional Divinities or transforming their existences again, he provides them with the means to develop their own abilities and allows them to decide how far they are willing to progress.[18]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is one of Frederick's oldest and closest friends. Their relationship predates Victor's transformation into a Noble Vampire, allowing Frederick to remember him as a Human whose daily life was limited by illness rather than the extraordinarily powerful Emperor known throughout the Cosmos. Their friendship survives numerous supernatural transformations, political conflicts and enormous periods of accelerated development without losing the informal familiarity established during childhood.[1][5]
Frederick frequently teases Victor about his romantic relationships, appearance and extraordinary luck, although he also acknowledges his friend's loyalty and willingness to protect the people he cares about. Victor, in turn, recognizes Frederick's eccentric personality and exceptional supernatural potential. Their relationship allows them to exchange jokes and criticism without the strict formality ordinarily imposed upon individuals addressing the Emperor.[14][10]
Victor's concern for Frederick eventually leads him to create the Daywalker variation. Recognizing that Frederick's Human physiology will become increasingly inadequate as the surrounding civilization confronts stronger enemies, Victor reconstructs his friend's body and Soul into a complete superior Noble Vampire. He deliberately preserves Frederick's Onmyō abilities, refusing to eliminate the spiritual techniques his friend has spent years developing.[9][8]
Their relationship also involves occasional disagreements over Frederick's complacency. Victor becomes irritated when he discovers that Frederick has failed to properly explore the full potential of his Daywalker body, particularly the enhanced Count Form. Although Frederick attempts to avoid the resulting criticism, the encounter demonstrates Victor's expectation that his friend should become capable of protecting himself rather than relying indefinitely upon the Emperor's personal intervention.[10]
Frederick's Faith in Victor eventually becomes an important aspect of their supernatural relationship. Initially, he struggles to perceive Victor as a God because he continues regarding him primarily as his childhood friend. After witnessing a fraction of the Emperor's true existence, he becomes capable of invoking Victor's Divine authorities through [[Onmyoujutsu]]. Victor also personally blesses Frederick and his other childhood companions, reinforcing the bond between them.[11]
Their friendship remains significant even as the Empire prepares for intergalactic war. Victor offers Frederick access to the most demanding Tower of Nightmares rather than simply granting him additional Divine abilities, recognizing that his friend possesses his own ambitions and path toward greater power. Frederick, for his part, openly supports Victor's refusal to submit to hostile foreign rulers while maintaining that protecting his family remains his foremost responsibility.[18]

#### Unnamed Noble Vampire
Frederick eventually establishes a romantic relationship with an unnamed Noble Vampire woman. The relationship develops after he gains the courage to express his feelings, with [[Aphrodite]]'s influence over Love contributing to his willingness to pursue romance seriously rather than restricting his ambitions to fictional fantasies and jokes.[17]
By the time [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] encounters him in the former [[Egyptian Pantheon]]'s territory, Frederick is living happily with his partner. His relationship has significantly improved his confidence and personal satisfaction, and her influence contributes to his more mature appearance and clothing. Frederick regards their relationship as one of the most important achievements of his personal life, especially given his previous frustration with remaining single while Victor acquired numerous wives.[14]
The biological differences between Frederick and his partner initially create difficulties. As a Noble Vampire, she possesses supernatural endurance and regenerative abilities greatly exceeding those of an ordinary Human. Frederick compensates through Onmyō talismans and physical training, but the limitations of his original species remain increasingly apparent. These circumstances contribute to his willingness to become a Vampire when Victor offers him the opportunity.[9]
Victor later refers to the woman as Frederick's wife and considers her a suitable partner for his friend. Her identity, original Clan, exact age and complete supernatural capabilities remain undisclosed. Although Frederick occasionally expresses the romantic fantasies associated with his otaku personality, no additional spouses have been individually established through the available narrative information.[20]

#### Mizuki Elderblood
[[Mizuki Elderblood]] is Frederick's principal supernatural instructor and one of the most important individuals responsible for his development. Their relationship begins when Mizuki pursues him through [[New York]] after the discovery of a Divine spear connected to the Inquisition. Frederick's evasive behavior, inappropriate remarks and unusual personality repeatedly frustrate her, although his natural athleticism and supernatural aptitude attract the attention of her master.[2]
Mizuki's training is exceptionally demanding and contributes significantly to Frederick's improved physique, spiritual knowledge and practical combat abilities. Through her instruction, he develops the foundations necessary to become an accomplished Onmyō practitioner, eventually mastering techniques that ordinarily require extensive preparation and years of training.[8]
Their relationship changes considerably as Mizuki becomes one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wives and later a Dragon Goddess associated with the Imperial Onmyō tradition. Frederick retains considerable respect for her expertise while continuing to remember the harshness of his earlier training. His later position as leader of the Imperial Onmyō warriors reflects the enormous development of the discipline under Mizuki's patronage, with Frederick regarded as her foremost disciple and representative among its practitioners.[12]

#### Abe-no-Seimei
[[Abe-no-Seimei]] recognizes Frederick's exceptional aptitude for Japanese spiritual techniques and plays an essential role in redirecting his supernatural future away from the Inquisition. His spiritual existence is perceptible to Frederick even before formal training, demonstrating unusual compatibility with supernatural phenomena that ordinarily remain inaccessible to Humans without appropriate abilities.[2]
Rather than allowing Frederick's potential to remain confined to conventional Church recruitment, Abe-no-Seimei proposes that he become an Onmyō practitioner. The alternative offers access to Faith-based techniques and Divine power without requiring Frederick to accept the same religious and personal restrictions associated with the Inquisition. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] strongly supports the proposal, while Seimei regards Frederick as someone capable of becoming an outstanding practitioner.[4]
Frederick's rapid mastery of foundational techniques ultimately validates Seimei's recognition of his talent. His accomplishments are sufficiently extraordinary to impress [[Amaterasu]], whose knowledge of the discipline allows her to appreciate the difficulty of achieving such progress in less than a year.[8]

#### Amaterasu Elderblood
[[Amaterasu]] Elderblood becomes one of the most important Divine authorities associated with Frederick's supernatural development. As a Goddess deeply connected to Japanese spiritual traditions, she possesses extensive knowledge of the Faith-based techniques employed through [[Onmyoujutsu]]. Her expertise allows her to recognize the unusual nature of Frederick's talent and the significance of the racial modifications [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] performs upon him.[8]
Amaterasu is particularly impressed by Frederick's ability to retain his Onmyō powers after becoming a complete Vampire. She also recognizes how extraordinary his original learning speed was, considering the difficulty of mastering the discipline even among Humans trained from childhood. Her involvement in Frederick's continued development provides access to knowledge and Divine support far beyond that normally available to independent practitioners.[8]
During the later Imperial era, Amaterasu and Mizuki become the principal patrons responsible for the growth of the Empire's Onmyō tradition. Frederick rises to become its highest-ranking practitioner and recognized leader, maintaining a position that reflects both his own accomplishments and the Divine institutions supporting the discipline.[12]

#### Andrew Walter
[[Andrew Walter]] is one of Frederick's closest childhood friends and an important companion throughout his supernatural development. Both men retain the humorous and informal relationship established during their Human lives, frequently exchanging jokes and criticisms despite eventually acquiring extraordinary supernatural powers.[1][5]
Their supernatural paths develop through different religious traditions. Frederick specializes in [[Onmyoujutsu]] and the invocation of compatible Gods from multiple Pantheons, while Andrew follows techniques derived from the Inquisition and the Divine authorities belonging to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s own Pantheon. Both receive unusual privileges because of their direct relationship with the Emperor, eventually acquiring planetary-class fundamental abilities and V-05 maximum threat classifications.[11][12]
Their friendship becomes particularly apparent during the planetary operation in the Andromeda Galaxy. Both voluntarily descend to the surface despite the availability of Imperial military support, and both demonstrate overwhelming combat abilities while arguing about unnecessary destruction and dramatic spellcasting. Their disagreements continue when they jointly invoke Violet's authority over Order, only for the Empress to personally complete the intervention they intended to perform through borrowed Divine power.[12][13]
Frederick and Andrew remain close as the Empire prepares for intergalactic war. Both recognize the need to protect their families and seriously consider the extraordinary training opportunity offered by Victor through the Tower of Nightmares. Their reactions demonstrate a continued willingness to pursue greater strength despite understanding the danger involved.[18]

#### Edward Jonathan Elizabeth Lykos
[[Edward Jonathan Elizabeth Lykos]] belongs to the same childhood friendship group as Frederick, Andrew, Leona and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Their relationship develops long before either becomes an important supernatural figure, allowing them to continue interacting with the familiarity of their earlier Human lives despite pursuing fundamentally different paths.[1]
Edward frequently becomes exasperated by Frederick's exaggerated reactions and enthusiasm for Japanese fictional characters. During the gathering of supernatural powers, he attempts to restrain Frederick after the appearance of Haruna, only to discover that his friend's supernatural training has made him considerably stronger than expected.[15]
Edward also witnesses Frederick's first major invocation of Victor's Divine power. He recognizes that the resulting attack possesses enough destructive power to threaten some of his strongest warriors, although he considers himself capable of avoiding or countering it. Their shared experience further demonstrates how dramatically the original childhood group has developed since becoming involved in supernatural society.[11]
Their friendship survives the Empire's enormous accelerated development, and Edward continues serving as a comparatively serious counterbalance to Frederick and Andrew's humor. During preparations for the coming intergalactic war, Edward is the first of the three to openly announce that they will meet at the Tower of Nightmares, understanding that his friends are likely to pursue the same opportunity despite their apparent hesitation.[18]

#### Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos
[[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]] is one of Frederick's childhood friends and a longstanding member of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s original social circle. Their relationship dates back to their school years, when Leona spent considerable time with Victor, Andrew and Frederick despite hostility from other students. The friendship survives the discovery of her Werewolf heritage and her eventual marriage to Victor.[1]
Frederick continues interacting with members of the Lykos family during the Imperial era. Their gathering during the hundred-thousand-year celebration includes members of Victor's younger generation, demonstrating how thoroughly the original childhood friendship group has become connected to the increasingly enormous Elderblood dynasty.[6]

#### Violet Elderblood Snow
[[Violet Elderblood Snow]] becomes an important Divine authority available through Frederick's mature [[Onmyoujutsu]]. Her position as Dragon Goddess and Empress gives her access to extraordinary powers associated with Order, allowing her to influence entire environments and impose stability upon hostile supernatural conditions.[12]
During the planetary operation undertaken with Andrew, Frederick participates in an invocation requesting Violet's authority over Order. The intended objective is to pacify the hostile extraterrestrial species and restore stability to the environment rather than continue destroying the planet through direct combat.[12]
Violet unexpectedly answers the invocation personally, manifesting a projection and examining the circumstances surrounding the expedition. She subsequently imposes Order upon the planet, pacifying the hostile creatures and stabilizing the environment. The resulting transformation is accomplished directly through Violet's Divine authority rather than an independent manifestation of Order belonging to Frederick, although his successful invocation demonstrates the extraordinary value of his access to the Imperial Divine system.[12][13]

#### Powers and Abilities
Frederick Winter possesses an unusual combination of two advanced supernatural systems: the physiology of a Daywalker, a superior variation of Noble Vampires created by [[Victor Elderblood]], and the Faith-based magical discipline of [[Onmyoujutsu]]. He originally learned Onmyoujutsu as a Human through the instruction of [[Mizuki Elderblood]] and [[Abe-no-Seimei]], developing an exceptional aptitude that allowed him to complete foundational training in less than one year. Victor subsequently reconstructed his physical and spiritual existence into a complete Vampiric form while deliberately preserving his previously acquired Onmyō abilities, allowing both systems to operate together despite the incompatibilities ordinarily associated with certain Divine techniques and darker supernatural [[Races (Flamel)|Races]].[1][2][3]
Fred's present combat abilities extend considerably beyond those available through ordinary Noble Vampire physiology. His Daywalker body provides advanced physical characteristics, supernatural longevity, improved racial abilities and a superior Count Form, while his Onmyō techniques allow him to request and channel Divine power from Gods belonging to multiple Pantheons. The effectiveness of these invocations depends upon the supernatural strength of the chosen God, the quality of the practitioner's Faith, the amount of power granted and the requirements governing the particular technique. Fred's personal Blessing from Victor gives him privileges unavailable to ordinary practitioners, substantially increasing his ability to access powerful Divine authorities.[4][5]
By the late Imperial era, Fred possesses planetary-scale fundamental capabilities and a maximum threat classification of V-05. The latter represents the ability to annihilate an entire Solar System through the unrestricted use of the extraordinary Divine powers accessible to his combat system. It does not mean that Fred's ordinary physical attacks or every basic Onmyō technique independently possess Solar System-level destructive power. His demonstrated continental-scale sword attack, personal physical characteristics and borrowed Divine authorities must therefore be distinguished when evaluating his complete capabilities.[5][6]

#### Daywalker Physiology
Daywalkers are a superior variation of Noble Vampires created directly through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s advanced Progenitor abilities. Fred was the first individual transformed into this Race, receiving a complete Vampiric body and Soul rather than retaining the residual Human component ordinarily associated with conventional Human-to-Vampire transformation. His new existence preserves the fundamental supernatural characteristics of Noble Vampires while correcting certain racial weaknesses and providing greater potential for future development.[2][3]
- Superior Noble Vampire Physiology: Fred possesses the advanced physical and supernatural foundation of a Daywalker, combining the established characteristics of Noble Vampires with modifications introduced by Victor.[2]

#### Onmyoujutsu
[[Onmyoujutsu]] is Frederick's principal supernatural discipline and the foundation of his position within the [[Elderblood Empire]]. He began learning the system after [[Abe-no-Seimei]] recognized his unusual aptitude and Mizuki undertook his practical instruction. Although Fred entered supernatural society as an adult Human, he mastered foundational techniques normally taught across years of childhood training in less than one year, establishing an exceptional natural capacity for Faith-based magic.[1][9][3]
The discipline operates through a relationship between the practitioner and a Divine Being. Onmyō techniques can employ talismans, incantations, rituals, enchantments and other supernatural mediums, but their most important higher-level applications depend upon the Faith connecting the user to the God being invoked. A practitioner can request access to portions of a God's Divine authority rather than needing to possess the corresponding Divinity independently. The amount of power available depends upon the strength of the practitioner's Faith, the supernatural characteristics of the invoked God, the authority granted and the complexity of the technique.[4]
Fred's development eventually carries this system beyond its traditional association with Japanese Divine authorities. Mature Imperial Onmyoujutsu allows qualified practitioners to invoke Gods belonging to multiple Pantheons when the relevant conditions are satisfied. Fred possesses exceptional access because of his relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his personal Blessing, giving him privileges far beyond those ordinarily available to religious practitioners within the Empire.[5]

Its acquisition attracted the attention of Church authorities and eventually Mizuki, but the weapon's later permanent ownership and present status remain unidentified.[7]
- Imperial Religious Access: Fred possesses an unusually privileged connection to Divine authorities through his relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his personal Blessing. This is a spiritual advantage rather than an independent physical item.[4][5]

#### Ability Classification and Limitations
Fred's most important distinction is the coexistence of a superior Noble Vampire body and an exceptionally versatile Faith-based magical system. His individual Daywalker abilities include enhanced physiology, sunlight immunity, reduced blood dependency, improved Vampiric transformations and the physical development accumulated throughout the Empire's accelerated era. His Onmyō abilities allow far broader effects through Divine invocation, but the associated Divinities remain properties of the Gods providing their power rather than Fred's permanent personal authorities.
