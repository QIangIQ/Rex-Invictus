#### Debut
Chapter 469
HAHAHAHAHAHA~, What!? My little Hunter, you can still fight, right? RIGHT!? Now, take your weapon, and fight! Fight! Let's have fun!
—[[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], as remembered by Liena during their earlier encounter, Chapter 470
Liena Walter is a supporting character in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]], a former Inquisition Hunter, an exceptionally experienced supernatural combatant, and the mother of [[Andrew Walter]], one of [[Victor Elderblood]]'s oldest childhood friends. Originally a Human living in [[New York]], Liena spent an undisclosed period serving the Inquisition before becoming disillusioned with the organization's concealed activities and abandoning supernatural affairs. Despite her retirement, she retained considerable knowledge of Vampires, [[Werewolves]], Demons, Hunter procedures and supernatural combat. Her reputation as a former Hunter is particularly noteworthy because she is explicitly identified as one of the few Humans who successfully injured Scathach Elderblood Scarlett, an achievement sufficiently unusual that the ancient Vampire continued remembering her long after their encounter.[1][2]
Unlike [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] who continued serving the Inquisition despite its corruption, Liena ultimately became dissatisfied with the organization and attempted to withdraw from the supernatural world entirely. Her retirement was motivated by the discoveries she made concerning the activities of its leadership and by her increasing psychological exhaustion from a life involving dangerous supernatural beings. She subsequently established an apparently ordinary existence, maintained friendships with other parents in New York and raised Andrew without revealing the full extent of her former occupation. However, her retirement did not eliminate her supernatural knowledge or her willingness to investigate individuals who might threaten her family.[1][3]
Liena's association with Victor's family originated through Andrew, who had known Victor since childhood. She was already acquainted with [[Anna Elderblood]] and [[Adam William Lykos]], the parents of two members of her son's friendship group, although she concealed her former Hunter background from the younger generation. Her knowledge extended well beyond what Andrew suspected. Liena had investigated Adam and established that the Lykos family were Werewolves long before her son became aware of the supernatural world, yet allowed him to continue visiting them because she considered Adam peaceful and did not regard Werewolves as an inherent danger to Humans.[4][1]
Her peaceful life ended when Andrew accidentally revealed that Victor had become a Vampire. The discovery alarmed Liena because her Hunter experience had taught her that Vampires were predators possessing supernatural methods of manipulating and controlling their prey. Although she trusted Victor as an individual and remembered him as a harmless Human child, she refused to assume that his transformation had left him unchanged without investigating the circumstances. Her efforts to protect Andrew eventually brought her into contact with Victor's growing supernatural family and forced her to confront the world she had tried to leave behind.[1]
Liena's reunion with [[Scathach]] was particularly significant. Their original encounter had involved a battle in which Liena was forced to reveal an unidentified technique or trump card while attempting to survive and escape the ancient Vampire. Instead of discouraging Scathach, the attack injured her and attracted her interest, causing her to remember Liena as an unusually entertaining Human opponent. The experience left Liena deeply frightened of Scathach, whose delight in combat made her more dangerous to someone attempting to escape rather than challenge her. When the two encountered one another again through Victor, Scathach immediately recognized Liena and openly expressed her continued fascination with the technique she had used.[1][2]
The precise nature of Liena's trump card remains unidentified. The novel confirms that her technique successfully injured Scathach and that the Vampire considered it fascinating, but does not establish whether the attack involved a specialized weapon, a personal supernatural ability, an unusual application of Hunter equipment or another method. Its effectiveness should therefore be distinguished from Liena's general combat strength. Injuring Scathach through an extraordinary technique does not independently establish that Liena possessed physical strength, speed or durability comparable to the strongest Vampire Countess.[2]
Liena also possessed an unusual relationship with longevity before becoming a High Human. Her appearance was considerably younger than expected for her apparent age, and Scathach directly challenged her claim that she was not immortal. Liena clarified that she was referring primarily to her mind, expressing the belief that Human consciousness was not naturally suited to endless existence even when the body could continue living. The exchange indicates that her former condition was more complicated than ordinary Human aging, but the mechanism responsible for her longevity was never identified. It remains uncertain whether she possessed a supernatural treatment, an unusual biological condition, a specialized technique or another source of extended life.[4][3]
Her existence underwent a more clearly established transformation when Victor personally changed her Race into a High Human. This superior variation of Humanity possesses an enhanced biological and spiritual foundation and no longer suffers from the ordinary lifespan restriction governing natural Human aging. Victor transformed both Liena and Andrew, ensuring that mother and son could continue living throughout the extraordinary periods of accelerated Time associated with the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Although this removed her ordinary biological expiration date, it did not make Liena absolutely immortal or incapable of being killed.[5]
Liena's importance to the narrative consequently rests upon more than her former status as a Hunter. Her history demonstrates that Humans possessing exceptional training and specialized methods could threaten supernatural beings far beyond their ordinary physical capabilities, while her disillusionment reveals the psychological consequences of discovering corruption within institutions claiming to protect Humanity. Her relationship with Andrew and Victor also connects the earlier Human world to the later Elderblood Empire, preserving the perspective of someone who experienced the supernatural world long before Victor transformed its political and religious structure.

### Physical description
Liena is a beautiful, mature woman with long blonde hair, green eyes and a youthful appearance unusual for someone of her apparent chronological age. At her introduction, she was described as being in her late fifties, yet physically resembled a woman around thirty years old. Small wrinkles became visible only upon closer inspection, providing one of the few indications that her actual age differed considerably from her youthful presentation. Her appearance was sufficiently remarkable to attract attention despite her deliberate efforts to maintain an ordinary life outside supernatural society.[4]
Her youthful appearance also distinguished her from the other parents associated with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s childhood friendship group. Although Liena lived as an apparently ordinary woman in [[New York]], her earlier supernatural experience and the circumstances surrounding her age made her fundamentally different from someone possessing an entirely conventional Human history. However, the origin of her unusual appearance remained unidentified, and the narrative does not establish that she possessed the biological characteristics of a Vampire, Werewolf or another supernatural Race before Victor transformed her.[4][3]
Liena generally maintained a restrained and unremarkable presence during her retirement. Victor had encountered her during visits to Andrew's home but remembered little about her because she deliberately avoided drawing attention to herself. This contrasted with the commanding demeanor she displayed after returning to supernatural affairs. Her posture, expression and professional awareness became much more noticeable when she was confronted with situations requiring her old Hunter experience, suggesting that her earlier inconspicuous behavior was a deliberate aspect of her attempt to maintain an ordinary identity.[2]
Despite having retired from combat, Liena remained physically capable of participating in dangerous supernatural engagements. During the Demonic invasion of Scarlett territory, she carried a large rifle across her back and moved between buildings without hesitation, displaying the practiced confidence of someone accustomed to operating on active battlefields. Her movements and reactions reflected years of combat experience rather than the uncertainty expected from someone encountering Demons for the first time.[6]
Her usual appearance changed in supernatural foundation rather than becoming an entirely different racial form following Victor's intervention. The High Human transformation reconstructed her biological and spiritual existence into a superior version of Humanity while preserving her fundamentally Human nature. The exact physical alterations experienced by Liena were not individually described, but the characteristics of the Race include enhanced physical potential and freedom from ordinary biological aging. Her hair color, eye color and recognizable Human appearance remain the established features of her character, while no dedicated description confirms a separate transformation with the visible racial traits of a Vampire or Dragon.[5]
Liena's exact current height, individual High Human measurements and later Imperial attire have not been established. Although her new Race provides an extraordinarily improved physical foundation, the absence of a dedicated later appearance prevents any precise description of her current combat equipment, armor or preferred clothing beyond the rifle she employed during her earlier Hunter-related activities.

### Personality and traits
Liena is cautious, intelligent, pragmatic, protective and psychologically experienced. Her personality reflects the responsibilities of a mother attempting to protect her child and the accumulated knowledge of a former Hunter who has witnessed the dangers associated with supernatural society. Although she prefers an ordinary life and deliberately withdrew from the Inquisition, her retirement did not eliminate her instincts or willingness to intervene when circumstances threaten her family. She approaches unfamiliar situations through investigation and contingency planning rather than assuming that powerful supernatural beings are trustworthy simply because they possess friendly appearances or impressive reputations.[1]
Her protectiveness toward Andrew becomes particularly apparent when he accidentally reveals [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s transformation into a Vampire. Liena recognizes that her son does not understand the full implications of the information and immediately questions him about the circumstances. Although she has known Victor since childhood and does not believe that he would casually endanger Andrew, her experiences with supernatural predators make her unwilling to rely exclusively upon personal trust. She therefore seeks to determine whether Victor has retained his original personality and whether Andrew might be suffering from supernatural manipulation or another form of influence.[1]
Liena's approach to the Lykos family reveals that she does not indiscriminately hate supernatural beings. She secretly investigated Adam long before Andrew learned the truth about [[Werewolves]] and discovered the family's supernatural identity. Nevertheless, she allowed her son to maintain his friendship with Edward and Leona because Adam had demonstrated peaceful intentions and no desire to prey upon ordinary Humans. Her decision reflects a distinction between the inherent dangers associated with supernatural power and the actual behavior of the individual possessing it.[1]
This distinction became increasingly important after Liena lost confidence in the Inquisition. The organization publicly claimed to defend Humanity against dangerous supernatural beings, yet Liena discovered concealed activities that contradicted the principles she had been expected to serve. Her disillusionment was sufficiently profound that she eventually abandoned the institution rather than continue participating in its operations. The experience reinforced her distrust of organizations relying upon religious or political authority to justify their actions, particularly when their actual behavior contradicted their professed objectives.[3]
Liena's response to corruption differed from that of individuals who attempted to reform supernatural society after discovering its darker aspects. Rather than remain involved and attempt to confront the organization directly, she withdrew into an ordinary Human existence and deliberately stopped following supernatural politics. Her decision appears to have been influenced by psychological exhaustion as much as moral opposition, since she no longer possessed the enthusiasm necessary to remain surrounded by conflict, deception and dangerous supernatural beings.[3]
Victor recognizes this exhaustion when they eventually discuss her retirement. He compares her circumstances with those of Mizuki, who had also become disillusioned after discovering corruption within the Inquisition. Although the comparison is intentionally harsh, it reflects his belief that Liena has become tired of confronting the world rather than completely losing the ability to fight. Liena does not deny the emotional burden associated with her experiences, and her later comments concerning immortality reveal that her weariness extends beyond the particular organization she once served.[3]
Her conversation with [[Scathach]] demonstrates a particularly important aspect of her worldview. When challenged about her claim that she was not immortal, Liena explains that she is speaking of her mind rather than exclusively her body. She believes that ordinary Humans are not naturally suited to existing indefinitely, since an extended lifespan may impose psychological burdens that the Human mind was never designed to withstand. Her statement reflects an understanding that longevity is not necessarily beneficial when the individual must continue accumulating memories, suffering and emotional exhaustion without relief.[3]
Liena's attitude toward immortality distinguishes her from many supernatural characters who pursue an extended lifespan primarily because they fear death. She does not regard endless existence as an unquestionable advantage, having already experienced enough of the supernatural world to understand that survival can create problems beyond those associated with the body. Her perspective also provides a meaningful contrast with Scathach, whose extraordinary lifespan and enthusiasm for combat allow her to continue seeking increasingly dangerous battles rather than withdrawing from conflict.[3]
Her fear of Scathach represents one of the few circumstances capable of seriously disrupting her composure. Liena remembers the ancient Vampire as a combat-obsessed monster who became more excited when confronted with resistance. During their earlier encounter, Liena apparently attempted to escape, only to discover that employing her most effective technique merely made Scathach more interested in continuing the fight. The experience left her with an enduring psychological aversion to the Vampire Countess, whom she hoped never to encounter again.[1]
The intensity of this reaction becomes apparent when Liena hears that Ruby is Scathach's daughter. Ruby's identity reminds her of the earlier encounter and causes her to relive memories of Scathach laughing while demanding that she continue fighting. Her normally controlled behavior begins to deteriorate, illustrating how deeply the experience affected her despite her considerable history of supernatural combat.[1]
Her fear becomes even more obvious when Scathach personally recognizes her during Victor's family gathering. Liena visibly trembles and privately expresses the hope that Scathach might have forgotten her. The opposite proves true: Scathach remembers her unusually well because she had succeeded in causing an injury. While the ancient Vampire regards their reunion as an opportunity to encounter a fascinating opponent again, Liena considers the attention deeply undesirable.[2]
Nevertheless, Liena is not cowardly or incapable of fighting. Her reaction to Scathach concerns an opponent whose extraordinary power and battle obsession made the prospect of another encounter terrifying even to an experienced Hunter. When Demons later invade Scarlett territory, Liena retrieves her rifle and moves toward the battle without hesitation, casually acknowledging that she has not fought a Demon for some time. The contrast establishes that her aversion to Scathach is rooted in the particular experience they shared rather than a general inability to withstand supernatural danger.[6]
Liena also possesses considerable practical judgment concerning political information. During the meeting with Ruby and Victor's allies, she recognizes that the younger members of the group cannot reasonably expect access to every strategic secret merely because they possess personal relationships with Victor. Although she wishes to understand the developing Demonic invasion and the threats facing Andrew, she recognizes that the supernatural factions involved are operating under circumstances requiring discretion. Her response reflects the experience of someone accustomed to military organizations and the consequences of revealing sensitive information to individuals who have not formally chosen an allegiance.[7][8]
Her relationship with Andrew combines affection, caution and a desire to prepare him for dangers she cannot always prevent personally. Although she originally concealed her Hunter background to protect his ordinary childhood, she eventually assumes responsibility for training him once supernatural society becomes part of his life. Her instruction emphasizes survival rather than unnecessary displays of honor, reflecting her understanding that ordinary Humans cannot reliably overcome superior supernatural beings through straightforward physical exchanges. She therefore teaches Andrew to exploit weaknesses and employ practical methods capable of compensating for the disadvantages of his original Race.[9]
Liena's characterization ultimately reflects the distinction between strength and willingness to continue fighting. She possesses exceptional Hunter experience, has survived encounters with some of the most dangerous beings in the supernatural world and retains the courage necessary to return to battle. Nevertheless, her accumulated experiences have made her more interested in preserving the safety and wellbeing of her family than in seeking the excitement of conflict. Her later High Human transformation provides the possibility of an extended future, but the philosophical reservations she expressed concerning psychological immortality remain an important aspect of her established personality.

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Liena became directly involved in the story when Andrew accidentally revealed that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had transformed into a Vampire. Until that point, she had maintained an apparently ordinary life in [[New York]] and concealed her previous occupation as an Inquisition Hunter from her son. Although Andrew regarded Victor's transformation primarily through the personal familiarity of their childhood friendship, Liena immediately recognized its supernatural implications and demanded further information before allowing herself to believe that the circumstances were harmless.[4][1]
Her knowledge of Vampires made the revelation especially concerning. Ordinary Humans could be threatened through physical strength, supernatural Charm, psychological manipulation and the predatory instincts associated with various Vampiric lineages. Although she trusted Victor personally and remembered him as a sickly child who had maintained a long friendship with Andrew, Liena understood that supernatural transformation could produce consequences far beyond ordinary changes in appearance. She consequently prepared herself to investigate whether Victor had genuinely retained his identity and whether Andrew remained safe around him.[1]
Liena's preparations brought her into contact with [[Adam William Lykos]] and the other members of the original childhood group. Although Adam's Werewolf nature became an immediate subject of concern among the younger visitors, Liena already knew his secret because she had investigated him years earlier. She had deliberately concealed that knowledge after establishing that Adam possessed no desire to harm Humans and intended to live peacefully with his family. Her attitude demonstrated that she was willing to distinguish supernatural creatures who posed genuine threats from those whose unusual biology did not make them enemies.[1]
Liena accompanied Andrew, Frederick, Adam, Edward and Leona to Anna's home, arriving at a time when Victor's family was already preparing for the escalating supernatural conflict. Anna recognized Liena as an old acquaintance and became surprised when she learned that another parent associated with Victor's childhood friends possessed a hidden supernatural background. The revelation added another complication to the increasingly unusual relationships surrounding Victor, whose family already included Vampires, [[Werewolves]] and individuals connected to several supernatural organizations.[4]
The gathering subsequently moved into Ruby's secure underground facilities, where the visitors were introduced to the developing situation. Ruby explained the threats associated with the Demonic invasion and the increasingly dangerous political conflicts surrounding the supernatural factions. Liena listened carefully, recognizing that the information involved organizations and individuals whose capabilities were substantially greater than those Andrew had encountered during his ordinary Human life.[1][7]
Liena's greatest immediate discomfort arose when Ruby identified herself as a daughter of [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett|Scathach Scarlett]]. The name reminded her of a previous encounter with the ancient Vampire, during which Liena had attempted to escape and eventually been forced to reveal a technique capable of injuring her opponent. Rather than weakening [[Scathach]]'s enthusiasm, the attack had provoked greater excitement and caused the battle-obsessed Countess to demand that Liena continue fighting. The memory remained vivid enough that Liena reacted with genuine fear despite having spent years distancing herself from supernatural conflicts.[1]
The presence of Ruby made another encounter with Scathach seem increasingly likely. Liena understood that the Countess maintained an exceptionally close relationship with her daughters and possessed little reluctance to involve herself in conflicts offering the possibility of a challenging battle. Although Liena hoped the ancient Vampire might have forgotten her, the memory of their previous encounter made the prospect of a reunion deeply unpleasant.[1]
As Ruby continued explaining Victor's activities, Liena discovered that his supernatural development had progressed far beyond what she originally expected. He had acquired considerable political influence within [[Nightingale]], defeated powerful Vampire opponents and become involved in incidents causing enormous casualties throughout the supernatural world. The scale of the events surprised the assembled visitors, including Liena and Adam, who possessed considerably more experience with supernatural society than the younger Humans.[8]
One revelation attracted Liena's particular attention: Victor had attacked several Inquisition facilities and had become an exceptionally dangerous target in the eyes of the organization. The information suggested that he might have discovered the same concealed activities that originally caused her to abandon the institution. Liena consequently became interested in speaking with him directly, recognizing that his conflict with the Inquisition might provide answers to questions she had avoided confronting since her retirement.[8]
Although the visitors wished to know more about Victor's plans, Ruby refused to reveal certain information because they had not yet formally chosen an allegiance. Liena and Adam understood the political reasoning behind the decision despite their desire for greater clarity. Both possessed sufficient supernatural experience to recognize that family connections and friendships did not automatically justify unrestricted access to strategic information during an approaching war.[7][8]
Victor eventually reunited the visitors with the other members of his expanding household. During the introductions, he revealed the identities and relationships of several individuals surrounding him, including his adopted daughters, Maids, wives and Scathach. Liena recognized the danger of the situation as soon as the ancient Countess was named, knowing that Scathach had the kind of personality that made old adversaries especially interesting when they had previously survived or demonstrated unusual abilities.[2]
Scathach immediately recognized Liena and expressed delight at their reunion. The reaction surprised Victor because his teacher rarely remembered opponents who had failed to make a meaningful impression. Scathach openly explained that Liena had employed a fascinating technique and was among the few Humans who had actually managed to hurt her. The admission established the extraordinary nature of Liena's earlier accomplishment, particularly because Scathach was already renowned as one of the most dangerous Vampires in existence.[2]
Liena responded with visible fear rather than pride. Her body trembled, and she regretted having attracted Scathach's attention during their earlier encounter. She had hoped to remain forgotten, but the Countess's enthusiastic reaction made it clear that the unusual technique had permanently established her as an interesting opponent in Scathach's memory. The conversation also demonstrated how differently the two women understood their previous fight: Scathach remembered an exciting battle, while Liena remembered a terrifying encounter she had struggled to survive.[2]
The reunion also revealed how effectively Liena had concealed her supernatural identity during retirement. Victor remembered having visited Andrew's home but possessed only a vague recollection of his friend's mother because she had deliberately maintained a forgettable presence. Her current demeanor differed considerably from that ordinary appearance, with her Hunter experience becoming increasingly obvious as she interacted with the powerful individuals surrounding Victor.[2]
Victor later addressed the concerns of Liena, Andrew and Frederick directly. Although Liena had initially approached him because she feared for Andrew's safety, the information she had gathered convinced her that Victor had not become a danger to her son merely through his transformation into a Vampire. Her remaining questions concerned the approaching Demonic invasion, the political situation surrounding the Inquisition and the larger supernatural conflicts developing across the world.[3]
Liena admitted that her years of retirement had left her largely uninformed about recent supernatural developments. She had deliberately avoided following the affairs of the organizations she once knew, preferring the stability of ordinary life after becoming disillusioned with the Inquisition. Her withdrawal had been sufficiently complete that she possessed little knowledge of the full circumstances surrounding the Demonic War, despite having once belonged to a professional organization dedicated to confronting supernatural threats.[3]
Victor recognized that Liena's retirement involved psychological exhaustion rather than a complete inability to fight. He understood that she had discovered the Inquisition's corruption and responded by withdrawing from the supernatural world rather than confronting the consequences directly. His comparison with Mizuki emphasized the similarity between their experiences: both women had discovered information contradicting the principles they had previously served, but their responses to that knowledge had developed in different directions.[3]
The conversation became more personal when Scathach challenged Liena's claim that she was not immortal. Liena clarified that her objection concerned her mental endurance, explaining that Humans were not naturally suited to living indefinitely and that consciousness could deteriorate under the psychological burden of an extraordinarily long existence. Scathach understood the distinction because she had witnessed numerous supernatural beings lose their sanity after surviving for centuries, particularly individuals whose existence had been unnaturally prolonged.[3]
The exchange also confirmed that Liena's former longevity was not entirely ordinary. Scathach directly rejected the claim that Liena was not immortal in some sense, although the narrative did not explain the origin of the condition or identify its limitations. Liena's clarification therefore concerned the psychological consequences of prolonged survival rather than an explicit denial of every unusual biological characteristic she possessed.[3]
Victor ultimately decided to help her obtain the information necessary to understand the current supernatural situation. He provided Liena with a phone containing Mizuki's contact information, believing that another former Inquisition member who had confronted the organization's corruption could address her concerns more directly. He also arranged for Ruby to provide reports concerning the approaching Demonic invasion, allowing Liena to make an informed decision about the danger facing Andrew and the wider community.[3]
The assistance effectively ended Liena's attempt to remain completely uninvolved in supernatural affairs. Although she continued preferring a peaceful existence, the approaching invasion threatened her family and the surrounding world, leaving her unwilling to remain passive while the people she cared about faced danger.

#### Demonic War
Liena returned to active supernatural combat when the Demonic invasion reached territories belonging to [[Nightingale]]'s major Vampire families. The forces of [[Diablo]] launched attacks across the regions controlled by the [[Vampire Counts]], attempting to exploit the absence and divided attention of several important supernatural leaders. Among the threatened territories was Clan Scarlett, whose legendary founder [[Scathach]] possessed a reputation that made an attack upon her household particularly audacious.[6]
The invading force included the 61st Pillar Demon, [[Zagan]], who led a legion of Demons into Scarlett territory. The defenders had already received warnings concerning the possibility of an invasion, allowing Siena and the household's Maids to prepare the population and move civilians into protected underground shelters. Ruby, Pepper, Lacus, Siena and their allies subsequently gathered to confront the invading forces, while Liena accompanied several of the visitors who had become involved with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction.[6]
Liena's reaction to the arrival of Demons demonstrated the extent of her previous experience. Rather than panicking when explosions and monstrous roars spread across the territory, she retrieved the large rifle she carried and casually acknowledged that she had not fought a Demon for a long time. Her response established that Demon hunting belonged to her earlier professional experience and that the years spent outside active service had not eliminated her willingness to fight when circumstances required it.[6]
Her actions contrasted with those of individuals who had only recently discovered supernatural society. Liena approached the situation with the practical familiarity of a veteran Hunter, recognizing the significance of the invading creatures without allowing their appearance to prevent her from acting. She moved through the defended territory carrying her rifle, demonstrating that she remained physically capable of participating in a battlefield involving opponents far stronger than ordinary Humans.[6]
The defense of Scarlett territory also exposed Liena to the extraordinary capabilities of Scathach's daughters. Although she was already aware of the Countess's power, she had not expected Ruby, Siena, Pepper and Lacus to possess such extensive large-scale battlefield control. The sisters combined their different supernatural abilities into an attack involving widespread precipitation, Ice and carefully controlled environmental effects capable of freezing Demons across enormous areas.[6]
The scale of the technique astonished Liena, Edward and Leona, who watched as the invading creatures were immobilized or destroyed through the combined powers of the Scarlett women. Liena recognized that the daughters of an extraordinarily powerful Vampire were themselves dangerous combatants, but the effectiveness of the attack exceeded her expectations. The technique also displayed remarkable control because the destructive snowfall avoided friendly individuals rather than freezing every person within the affected territory indiscriminately.[6]
Liena observed this precision directly while moving across the battlefield. When snowflakes approached her position, they altered their movement and avoided contact rather than subjecting her to the freezing effects experienced by the Demons. The phenomenon demonstrated that the attack's users could distinguish allies from enemies while maintaining extraordinary destructive coverage, a level of elemental coordination that even an experienced supernatural Hunter found remarkable.[6]
Although Liena was present among the defenders and retained her weapon throughout the invasion, the narrative does not provide a dedicated individual battle describing the exact number of Demons she killed or the destructive output of her rifle. Her confirmed actions establish readiness for combat, participation in the defense, mobility across the battlefield and prior experience fighting Demons, while the massive environmental attack belongs to the coordinated abilities of the Scarlett sisters rather than to Liena personally.[6]
Her participation nevertheless represented a decisive return to supernatural affairs. Having attempted to remain outside that world for years, Liena found herself once again confronting the same categories of threats she had previously fought as a Hunter. The difference was that she now operated alongside a growing alliance centered upon Victor rather than the Inquisition, allowing her to participate without returning to the institution whose corruption had originally caused her retirement.

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Liena continued living within the civilization eventually established beneath [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s authority, maintaining her relationship with Andrew as the supernatural world underwent enormous political and technological changes. Although she had abandoned the old Inquisition, her experience remained valuable because ordinary Humans and newly trained combatants still required methods of surviving encounters with supernatural beings possessing far greater natural strength.[9]
She eventually became Andrew's principal Hunter instructor. Her training drew upon the practical methods developed during her former career, emphasizing the importance of exploiting weaknesses and employing unconventional tactics against enemies that ordinary Humans could not realistically defeat through straightforward physical force. Andrew subsequently described himself as an Inquisitor in training, although he preferred the less institutionally restrictive title of Hunter because Liena was no longer a member of the organization.[9]
Her instruction reflected the fundamental circumstances of Human supernatural combat. Vampires, [[Werewolves]], Demons and other [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] frequently possessed superior physical strength, durability, regenerative abilities or supernatural powers, placing ordinary Humans at an immediate disadvantage. Liena therefore taught Andrew that survival required preparation, practical knowledge and a willingness to employ methods that more powerful beings might consider dishonorable. The purpose was not to encourage unnecessary cruelty but to compensate for biological limitations that could otherwise make a confrontation impossible to survive.[9]
Despite receiving training from an experienced Hunter, Andrew continued suffering from the limitations of his natural Race. His physical condition remained inferior to that of most developed supernatural beings, while the Faith-based techniques associated with the Inquisition were difficult for him to employ because he could not sincerely worship the Divine authority traditionally associated with the organization. His skepticism toward the supernatural Gods he had encountered prevented him from developing the religious conviction necessary to use those powers to their fullest extent.[9][10]
Liena's own condition was also unusual. Although she had displayed signs of abnormal longevity before joining Victor's civilization, the exact mechanism and limitations of that condition remained unidentified. Her earlier conversation with [[Scathach]] had established that prolonged existence could become psychologically exhausting, particularly for someone who had already spent a substantial portion of life confronting dangerous supernatural beings and witnessing the corruption of supposedly protective institutions.[3]
Victor eventually transformed Andrew into a High Human as an expression of gratitude for their long-standing friendship. The transformation reconstructed Andrew's physical and spiritual foundation into a superior version of Humanity, greatly increasing his natural capabilities and removing the ordinary biological expiration date of his Race. Edward's supernatural senses confirmed that Andrew remained fundamentally Human despite becoming vastly more powerful, establishing that the transformation did not simply replace his original biology with Vampire or Dragon physiology.[5]
During the same conversation, Victor revealed that he had already transformed Liena into a High Human as well. The disclosure surprised Andrew because he had not expected Victor to extend the intervention to his mother. His immediate reaction was characteristically humorous, as he invoked the brothers' code of conduct and warned Victor against becoming romantically involved with Liena. The exchange reflected their familiar relationship despite the extraordinary significance of the supernatural transformation being discussed.[5]
For Liena, the change represented a fundamental alteration of her established racial classification. Her earlier existence had involved a form of unusual longevity whose origin was never identified, but her new High Human physiology possessed an explicitly established biological foundation. The Race no longer suffered from the ordinary natural lifespan limit of Humanity and provided enhanced physical and spiritual potential, allowing Liena to survive the enormous periods of accelerated Time experienced by the Empire without relying upon the unknown condition that had previously distinguished her from ordinary Humans.[5]
The transformation also resolved the immediate problem created by Andrew's own longevity. As a High Human, Andrew could continue surviving for an extraordinarily long period unless killed, while Liena would ordinarily have faced separation from her son through the biological limitations of natural Humanity. Victor's intervention allowed mother and son to retain a shared racial foundation and potentially continue their relationship throughout the Empire's extended future.[5]
Although the High Human transformation established an extraordinary increase in Liena's biological potential, the novel has not provided a dedicated subsequent battle establishing the precise extent of her new strength, speed or supernatural techniques. Her original Hunter experience remained an important part of her background, while the transformation supplied a new foundation upon which those abilities could theoretically continue developing. However, the extraordinary planetary-scale and Divine-invocation feats later performed by Andrew should not automatically be assigned to Liena merely because they share the same Race.[5]
Liena's later status consequently reflects the combination of two distinct periods of development. Her Human-era achievements establish her as an exceptionally experienced Hunter capable of surviving encounters with dangerous supernatural beings and employing an unidentified technique that injured Scathach. Her High Human existence provides superior physical potential and biological agelessness, although the exact extent of her individual development after the transformation remains unestablished.

### Skills & Abilities
Liena possesses extensive Hunter experience, supernatural knowledge, practical combat proficiency and an unidentified technique exceptional enough to injure [[Scathach]]. Her former Human abilities were developed through training and specialized methods associated with the Inquisition, while her later High Human transformation improved her biological and spiritual foundation. The two aspects of her supernatural existence should be distinguished because her demonstrated achievements against Scathach and during the Demonic invasion occurred before the confirmed High Human transformation, whereas the precise extent of her new racial capabilities has not been established through individual combat feats.[1][2][6][5]

### Relationships

#### Andrew Walter
[[Andrew Walter]] is Liena's biological son and one of the most important people in her life. Her attempt to withdraw from supernatural society was partly intended to provide him with an ordinary childhood free from the dangers associated with her former Hunter career. She concealed the existence of Vampires, [[Werewolves]] and the Inquisition for many years, allowing Andrew to develop friendships and interests without becoming involved in the conflicts she had previously experienced.[1]
Liena's protectiveness became especially apparent when Andrew revealed [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s transformation into a Vampire. Although she trusted her son, she recognized that he lacked the experience necessary to assess every danger associated with the supernatural world. She consequently demanded information and prepared contingency plans before allowing him to continue interacting with Victor without further investigation. Her response demonstrated genuine maternal concern rather than hostility toward Victor personally.[1]
Once the supernatural world became part of Andrew's life, Liena assumed responsibility for teaching him the practical methods of Human [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]]. Her instruction emphasized survival and the exploitation of enemy weaknesses, providing him with a foundation that eventually contributed to his development within the Imperial Inquisition. Victor later transformed both mother and son into High Humans, allowing their relationship to continue without the ordinary biological lifespan limitation of Humanity.[9][5]

#### Anna Elderblood
[[Anna Elderblood]] is one of Liena's established Human-era acquaintances and a close friend associated with the families of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s childhood companions. Their relationship predates the supernatural revelations involving Victor and Andrew, with the two women maintaining enough familiarity that Liena could approach Anna directly when concerns arose about her son's safety.[4]
Anna became surprised when she discovered that Liena had once served as an Inquisition Hunter. The revelation demonstrated that another parent associated with Victor's childhood friendship group possessed concealed supernatural experience, adding to the increasingly complicated history of the adults surrounding his original Human life. Their relationship nevertheless continued as Liena became involved with Victor's family and the developing supernatural conflicts.[4]

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is one of Andrew's oldest childhood friends and the individual responsible for Liena's eventual transformation into a High Human. Liena knew Victor during his earlier Human life and therefore understood that his personality could not be judged exclusively through the characteristics associated with Vampirism. Nevertheless, her knowledge of supernatural predators initially made her cautious after learning that he had become a Vampire.[1]
Their relationship developed after Victor addressed Liena's concerns and explained the dangers associated with the approaching Demonic invasion. He recognized that her retirement originated partly from disillusionment with the Inquisition and arranged for her to contact Mizuki, another former member who had confronted the organization's corruption. His willingness to assist demonstrated that he regarded Andrew's family as individuals deserving protection rather than merely outsiders connected through an old friendship.[3]
Victor eventually transformed Liena into a High Human alongside Andrew, establishing an extraordinary improvement in her biological and spiritual existence. The transformation allowed her to survive without the ordinary age-related limitations of Humanity and continue living within the civilization he had created.[5]

#### Scathach Elderblood Scarlett
[[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] is the most terrifying opponent Liena is confirmed to have encountered during her former Hunter career. Their previous confrontation involved an unidentified technique that successfully injured [[Scathach]], making Liena one of the rare Humans whose attacks had an effect significant enough to attract the ancient Vampire's attention.[1][2]
The two women remember the encounter in radically different ways. Scathach regards the injury as evidence that Liena is an interesting opponent and remains enthusiastic about the possibility of fighting her again. Liena, however, remembers the Countess's laughter, relentless demands for another battle and extraordinary predatory behavior, causing her to experience considerable fear whenever the subject arises.[1]
Their reunion through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] demonstrates the lasting consequences of the encounter. Scathach immediately recognizes Liena and expresses delight, while the former Hunter visibly trembles and regrets having attracted the Countess's attention. The encounter remains the strongest established evidence of Liena's exceptional former combat abilities, although its exact mechanics remain unidentified.[2]

#### Adam William Lykos
[[Adam William Lykos]] is another parent associated with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s childhood friendship group and a former supernatural combatant who attempted to live peacefully outside the conflicts of his Race. Liena secretly investigated Adam during Andrew's childhood and discovered that the Lykos family were [[Werewolves]], but did not consider them a threat because Adam had demonstrated peaceful intentions.[1]
Their similar backgrounds contribute to the mutual understanding displayed during the meeting organized by Ruby. Both possess enough experience to recognize the importance of political secrecy and the danger of revealing sensitive information to individuals who have not formally established their allegiance. Their relationship consequently reflects practical respect between experienced supernatural veterans who sought ordinary lives after withdrawing from their former responsibilities.[7][8]

#### Mizuki Elderblood
[[Mizuki Elderblood]] is a former Inquisition General whose experiences possess important similarities to Liena's disillusionment with the organization. Both women discovered information contradicting the Inquisition's public principles and eventually abandoned their previous institutional loyalties, although their responses to those discoveries differed.[3]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] recognized the similarity and provided Liena with Mizuki's contact information, believing that someone who had personally confronted the same corruption would be better suited to explain the organization's hidden activities. The arrangement established a possible means of resolving Liena's remaining questions, although the available narrative does not confirm the full details of any subsequent conversation between the two women.[3]

#### Ruby Elderblood Scarlett
[[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] became one of Liena's important contacts when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s supernatural family gathered to explain the approaching Demonic invasion. Ruby provided information concerning the political situation, the dangers facing Humanity and the extraordinary conflicts involving Victor, while refusing to reveal sensitive strategic details that the visiting group had not yet been authorized to know.[1][8]
Ruby's relationship with [[Scathach]] also caused Liena considerable emotional discomfort because the daughter reminded her of the ancient Vampire she had once fought. Nevertheless, Liena recognized Ruby's authority and understood the reasons she restricted certain information. Victor subsequently arranged for Ruby to provide Liena with reports concerning the Demonic invasion, allowing her to remain informed without relying upon the organization she had abandoned.[1][3]
