Andrew Walter is a recurring supporting character in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]], a High Human, an elite combatant of the Imperial Inquisition, and one of [[Victor Elderblood]]'s oldest childhood friends. Originally an ordinary Human from [[New York]], Andrew was a naturally athletic, charismatic and flirtatious young man whose relationships with Victor, [[Frederick Winter]], [[Edward Jonathan Elizabeth Lykos]] and [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]] predated their involvement in supernatural society. His life changed after discovering that his mother, [[Liena Walter]], was a retired Hunter of the original Inquisition, introducing him to the supernatural profession that eventually developed into his position within the religious military institutions of the [[Elderblood Empire]].[1][2][3]
Unlike several members of Victor's childhood circle, Andrew did not inherit a powerful supernatural Bloodline. He initially developed through physical conditioning and Hunter techniques taught by his mother, whose experience fighting dangerous supernatural beings allowed her to instruct him in methods that compensated for Humanity's comparatively fragile physiology. Although he trained in the techniques associated with the Inquisition, Andrew referred to himself as a Hunter because Liena had already retired from the organization. His training was further complicated by his inability to develop genuine religious Faith in the [[Heavenly Father|Biblical God]]. Andrew's experiences with supernatural society made him skeptical of Divine authority, particularly because the Gods he encountered did not consistently demonstrate the moral qualities their followers attributed to them.[4][5]
Victor eventually reconstructed Andrew into a High Human, a superior variation of Humanity possessing dramatically enhanced physical and spiritual characteristics. Unlike Frederick, whose transformation produced the first Daywalker Vampire, Andrew remained fundamentally Human while acquiring a body whose potential surpassed the natural limitations of his original species. The transformation removed the ordinary biological expiration date associated with Human aging, allowing him to survive without dying from natural age-related deterioration. Victor subsequently revealed that he had also transformed Liena, preserving the lives of both mother and son throughout the enormous periods of accelerated Time experienced by the Empire.[6]
Andrew's supernatural development changed further after Victor personally bestowed The Emperor's Blessing upon him, Frederick and Edward. Through a direct demonstration of his Divine existence, Victor established himself as a source of Faith that Andrew could sincerely trust without abandoning his practical worldview. Rather than worshipping a distant God whose character he did not understand, Andrew could place his Faith in the childhood friend whose principles he had known throughout his life. The connection provided the foundation for his later mastery of the Imperial Inquisition's religious techniques, allowing him to channel [[Divine Energy]] originating from Victor and compatible members of the [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]].[5]
During the late Imperial era, Andrew became one of the most prominent combatants associated with the reorganized Imperial Inquisition, an institution operating beneath the religious hierarchy administered by [[Valeria Alekerth]]. His direct relationship with Victor granted him extraordinary prestige among believers, with some regarding him as an unofficial Pope or saint-like figure. Although he did not formally replace Valeria as the head of the Empire's religion, his personal Blessing and privileged access to Divine power distinguished him from ordinary Inquisitors. Andrew's mature combat abilities included planetary-class fundamental capabilities, independent flight, extraordinary physical strength and the ability to temporarily become a Divine instrument through which the supernatural powers of the Emperor could manifest.[7]
Within the Imperial threat classification system, Andrew is designated V-05, indicating a maximum destructive potential sufficient to annihilate an entire Solar System when his complete available supernatural arsenal is considered. This classification depends significantly upon the extraordinary Divine authorities he can invoke rather than establishing that his ordinary High Human body independently produces Solar System-level attacks. His demonstrated abilities include Divine Lightning, temporary Vampiric characteristics, supernatural Energy weapons and borrowed applications of Fire, Ice and Blood, while his successful invocation of [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] establishes his ability to request the authority of Order through the Imperial religious system.[7][8]
Despite his enormous supernatural development and the passage of vast amounts of subjective Time, Andrew remains recognizable as the young man who befriended Victor before the latter's transformation. His playful behavior, romantic interests, practical intelligence and loyalty remain defining characteristics. Although the scale of his responsibilities has expanded to encompass planetary operations and preparations for intergalactic warfare, Andrew continues valuing his oldest relationships above the prestige associated with his position, regarding the protection of his family and companions as more important than personal glory.[9][10]

### Physical Appearance
Andrew is a handsome and athletic man who originally stood approximately 185 centimeters tall. He possesses short, spiky blond hair, a distinctive asymmetrical V-shaped face and striking green eyes compared to green sapphires. His facial features are generally accompanied by a gentle smile, giving him a charismatic and approachable appearance. Even before becoming involved with supernatural society, Andrew maintained a toned and well-developed physique through regular participation in sports, making him considerably stronger and more athletic than [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had been during the years when his illness severely restricted physical activity.[1]
His appearance reflects the confidence and social ease that characterize his personality. Andrew generally carries himself casually, displaying little hesitation when approaching unfamiliar individuals or teasing his friends. His natural attractiveness contributes to his reputation as a womanizer, although his physical appearance is not described as possessing the supernatural beauty ordinarily associated with Vampires or the Divine characteristics later acquired by Victor.
Andrew's transformation into a High Human preserves his recognizable Human appearance while fundamentally altering his biological and spiritual nature. His blond hair, green eyes and facial structure remain characteristic of his original identity, but his body becomes supernaturally superior to its previous condition. Edward immediately recognizes the transformation through his supernatural perception, observing that Andrew is still Human despite possessing an entirely improved foundation. He does not acquire the pale complexion, crimson eyes or permanent Vampiric characteristics associated with Frederick's Daywalker transformation.[6]
The sudden increase in Andrew's physical capabilities initially exceeds his instinctive control. His strength and speed become so much greater that movements previously performed without difficulty require readjustment. Following Frederick's unexpectedly powerful Divine invocation, Andrew attempts to flee but misjudges the capabilities of his transformed body and collides with a wall. The incident demonstrates the abrupt nature of his evolution rather than a permanent weakness of High Human physiology, since his subsequent development provides extensive opportunities to master his new physical characteristics.[5]
During the later Imperial era, Andrew wears advanced black combat armor decorated with gold and crimson details, resembling the equipment employed by Frederick during their joint planetary expedition. The armor reflects the military and technological sophistication of the [[Elderblood Empire]] and accompanies Andrew during direct engagements with hostile extraterrestrial creatures. Its precise defensive mechanisms and technological specifications have not been individually established, although its appearance and operational use identify it as advanced Imperial combat equipment.[7]
Andrew undergoes a more dramatic temporary change when invoking Victor through the Imperial Inquisition's Divine Instrument technique. His body becomes surrounded by a golden radiance, and an extraordinary bolt of golden Divine Lightning descends upon him, enveloping him in the Emperor's power. The resulting state grants him Vampiric characteristics reminiscent of Victor while allowing him to manipulate borrowed supernatural Energy and manifest two plasma-like swords. His attacks release Fire, Ice and Blood-related effects alongside overwhelming Lightning, making his transformed appearance resemble a smaller manifestation of the God Emperor. The state is an invocation-based empowerment rather than a permanent racial evolution, and Andrew's underlying existence remains that of a High Human.[7]

### Personality
Andrew is cheerful, confident, humorous, flirtatious, pragmatic and deeply loyal to his closest friends. His personality combines considerable social intelligence with an irreverent attitude toward authority, allowing him to remain comfortable joking with individuals whose supernatural positions would intimidate ordinary Humans. Although his behavior occasionally appears immature or irresponsible, Andrew is capable of understanding complicated personal situations and recognizing when the actions of another individual conceal deeper motivations. His long relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] gives him particular insight into the Emperor's temperament, allowing him to distinguish the characteristics Victor possessed during childhood from those developed through supernatural experience.[1][11]
Andrew's playful nature is particularly evident during his first appearance. Upon encountering Victor after the latter's transformation, he immediately notices the enormous changes in his friend's physique and begins questioning whether Victor has been using steroids or other performance-enhancing substances. Rather than reacting with admiration, Andrew threatens to inform Anna if his suspicions prove correct, provoking a familiar exchange of insults between the two men. His reaction demonstrates the absence of unnecessary formality in their friendship and his willingness to criticize Victor when he believes his friend may be behaving recklessly.[1]
His romantic behavior is one of his most prominent characteristics. Andrew possesses considerable confidence around women and openly discusses relationships that his friends regard as irresponsible or dangerous. During his early Human life, he admits to having been involved with a married woman who was thirty-two years old when he was twenty, acknowledging the potential consequences while presenting the experience through humor. Although his romantic confidence contributes to his popularity, it also produces serious personal conflicts, particularly when his conduct affects people close to his childhood friends.[1]
The most significant example involves Leona's only close female friend during their school years. Andrew previously dated the girl but cheated on her with an older woman, destroying the relationship and causing Leona to resent him. The incident was especially damaging because Leona already experienced considerable social difficulties and possessed very few genuine friendships outside Victor's circle. Andrew later acknowledges that he did not understand how important the relationship was to Leona and admits that he would have behaved differently had he recognized the consequences. His regret demonstrates that his irresponsible romantic behavior does not prevent him from accepting responsibility when he realizes he has hurt someone.[2]
Despite his reputation, Andrew strongly values the boundaries established among his closest friends. Victor, Andrew, Frederick and Edward maintain a brothers' code of conduct prohibiting romantic involvement with a friend's current girlfriend, former partner, sister or mother. The agreement reflects their desire to prevent romantic complications from destroying relationships established during childhood. Andrew continues respecting the principle even as the circumstances surrounding Victor's family become increasingly extraordinary, and he frequently refers to the code when teasing his friends about their complicated relationships.[1][6]
Andrew possesses a practical understanding of loyalty and personal independence. He was aware of Victor's childhood illness and the bullying he suffered, and he became genuinely angry when remembering the individuals responsible. Nevertheless, Andrew respected Victor's refusal to allow others to resolve his personal grievances. Although he wanted to intervene, he understood that doing so without permission would undermine Victor's determination to confront his own problems. His approach to friendship therefore emphasizes remaining available when needed while allowing the people he cares about to make their own decisions.[2]
His romantic experience also gives him considerable insight into possessiveness and jealousy. When Victor reveals photographs of his wives and describes their behavior, Andrew recognizes characteristics resembling the fictional yandere archetype. He warns that women possessing extreme jealousy and obsessive attachment can become dangerous, particularly when they perceive betrayal or romantic competition. Andrew's reaction becomes increasingly incredulous when Victor admits that he genuinely enjoys those characteristics, since the traits his friends consider alarming are precisely those Victor finds attractive.[12]
Andrew's practical worldview becomes particularly important after he discovers the existence of supernatural Gods. Although he understands that Divine beings possess genuine supernatural powers, he does not consider such abilities proof of moral superiority. His encounters with supernatural society reveal that Gods can behave selfishly, cruelly or irresponsibly despite demanding unquestioning devotion from their followers. Andrew consequently finds it difficult to place genuine Faith in the [[Heavenly Father|Biblical God]] associated with the original Inquisition, since he has never personally experienced the assistance or protection he would expect from a Being worthy of worship.[5]
This skepticism limits Andrew's original religious abilities, but it also reveals the distinction he draws between belief and trust. He is not opposed to recognizing supernatural power or acknowledging the existence of Divine beings; rather, he refuses to offer genuine devotion merely because an individual claims Divine status. Victor eventually becomes the exception because Andrew possesses extensive personal knowledge of his friend's character. His Faith in the Emperor develops from loyalty, trust and direct experience rather than acceptance of an unfamiliar religious doctrine.[5]
Andrew's understanding of power is particularly philosophical. He rejects the idea that supernatural strength automatically corrupts an individual, instead believing that power reveals characteristics already present beneath the restrictions imposed by society. He considers most people likely to become more selfish or abusive when they possess enough strength to act without consequences, while a smaller number retain their fundamental principles regardless of their circumstances. Andrew regards Victor as belonging to the latter group, believing that his childhood friend always responded to kindness with kindness and hostility with hostility, even when illness prevented him from acting upon those principles.[11]
His familiarity with Victor allows him to recognize that the Emperor's enormous development has changed the scale of his actions more than the fundamental motives behind them. Victor possesses extraordinary power, commands immense civilizations and can confront beings far beyond the old Gods of Earth, yet Andrew continues perceiving many of the same traits that characterized him during childhood. From Andrew's perspective, the supernatural transformation has removed the limitations that previously prevented Victor from expressing his natural temperament rather than replacing him with an entirely different person.[11]
Andrew's humor remains important throughout the enormous passage of Imperial Time. He frequently mocks Frederick's enthusiasm for anime and Edward's complicated Werewolf family relationships, while his friends respond through equally exaggerated insults. Their conversations during the Empire's celebrations demonstrate that they remain comfortable behaving foolishly in one another's company despite occupying positions of considerable power and prestige. Such behavior reflects the security of relationships maintained over immense periods rather than a complete absence of maturity.[9]
His later military experiences reveal a more competitive aspect of his personality. During an expedition alongside Frederick, Andrew criticizes his friend for unleashing an unnecessarily destructive attack that alters continental geography. However, after invoking Victor and obtaining access to an extraordinary Divine empowerment, Andrew begins annihilating hostile creatures across the planet with comparable enthusiasm. Frederick immediately notices the contradiction, demonstrating that Andrew's preference for dramatic combat remains strong despite his attempts to present himself as the more reasonable member of the pair.[7]
Andrew is nevertheless capable of exercising practical judgment during military operations. He understands the objectives assigned to Imperial forces, cooperates with Frederick's commands and participates in an invocation intended to pacify hostile creatures rather than continuing their extermination. His accumulated experience allows him to distinguish between combat undertaken for enjoyment and situations requiring the successful completion of a mission, even though his personal behavior frequently retains the humor and competitiveness of his earlier life.[7][8]
His compassion remains an important quality despite the enormous changes surrounding him. As the Empire mobilizes for an intergalactic war, Andrew becomes troubled by the possibility of catastrophic casualties, openly questioning how many people might die in a conflict between civilizations possessing such extraordinary power. He recognizes that resistance is necessary to prevent the Empire from becoming subordinate to a hostile ruler, but this understanding does not eliminate his concern for the people who may suffer during the war. When Victor asks what he intends to do, Andrew immediately identifies the protection of his family as his foremost responsibility, demonstrating that his personal attachments remain more important than military prestige or the pursuit of glory.[10]

### Background
Andrew was born into a Human family in the United States and grew up in the same social environment as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Frederick, Edward and Leona. His mother, [[Liena Walter]], possessed a secret history as a Hunter of the original Inquisition, although Andrew spent much of his childhood unaware of her involvement in supernatural affairs. Consequently, he initially developed without the specialized education ordinarily available to children raised within supernatural families, maintaining an ordinary lifestyle based upon school, sports, friendships and romantic relationships.[1][3]
His family history includes a custody dispute involving his father. During Andrew's childhood, his father attempted to obtain custody of him, and [[Anna Elderblood]], who was already an accomplished lawyer, assisted Andrew's family. The incident established considerable respect for Anna within the Walter household and contributed to the close relationship between Andrew and Victor's family. Although his father's identity and subsequent circumstances have not been fully established, the dispute represents one of the important events connecting the two families before the discovery of supernatural society.[1]
Andrew's friendship with Victor developed during the period when the latter suffered from a debilitating medical condition caused by his rare blood. Unlike Andrew, Edward and the other physically active members of their group, Victor could not participate freely in sports or strenuous activities without risking serious medical consequences. Andrew understood how much his friend resented those limitations and supported his determination to live as independently as possible, although he occasionally wished Victor would allow others to intervene when he faced bullying or harassment.[2]
Andrew also maintained relationships with the Lykos family despite initially lacking knowledge of their supernatural heritage. Edward and Leona were among Victor's closest companions, while their father, Adam, was another important adult associated with the group. Andrew's relationship with Leona became strained because of his betrayal of her only close female friend, although he remained connected to the wider circle and later acknowledged his responsibility for the conflict.[2]
Liena's past was considerably more dangerous than Andrew initially understood. She had belonged to the Inquisition and possessed enough ability to survive confrontations with powerful supernatural beings. Among her most extraordinary accomplishments was an encounter with [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], during which she employed a technique capable of injuring the legendary Vampire Countess. [[Scathach]] remembered the experience long afterward because very few Humans had managed to wound her, while Liena regarded the attention she attracted from the battle-obsessed Vampire as something she would have preferred to avoid. This history established that Andrew's mother was far more accomplished than an ordinary retired Hunter, providing important context for the practical knowledge she later passed to her son.[13]

### Synopsis

#### The Awakening
Andrew first appears when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] encounters him while travelling to retrieve his cat, Zack, from Leona's veterinary clinic. Having recently spent time away with his mother, Andrew has not seen Victor since the latter's transformation into a Noble Vampire. He is immediately surprised by the dramatic changes in his friend's appearance, noticing his increased height, muscular development and newfound confidence. Unable to reconcile these characteristics with Victor's previous medical condition, Andrew initially assumes that he has begun using steroids or other performance-enhancing substances.[1]
Andrew questions Victor repeatedly and jokingly threatens to inform Anna if his suspicions prove correct. Their conversation quickly becomes a familiar exchange of insults, demonstrating the ease with which they interact despite the changes in Victor's appearance. Andrew's reaction also reveals that he understands the severity of Victor's former illness, since the physical development he observes should have been impossible within such a short period under ordinary Human circumstances.[1]
The reunion introduces the brothers' code of conduct maintained by Victor and his friends. Andrew and Victor discuss the importance of avoiding romantic relationships with the female relatives or current and former partners of their companions, recalling how such complications could damage friendships established over many years. Andrew also speaks openly about his history with older women, including his previous involvement with a married woman, while Victor reacts with his usual mixture of amusement and exasperation.[1]
Andrew becomes even more surprised when Victor reveals that he has married. Because Victor previously suffered from a severe illness and possessed limited romantic experience, Andrew initially considers the announcement implausible. Rather than accepting the information without explanation, he accompanies Victor to the veterinary clinic, where their conversation eventually reunites them with Edward and Leona.[1]
The meeting exposes the lingering tension between Andrew and Leona. Andrew remains aware that she resents him because he previously dated and betrayed her only close female friend. He discusses the matter with Edward and acknowledges that he did not understand how much the relationship meant to Leona. Although he regrets his behavior, Andrew recognizes that reconciliation cannot be demanded merely because he now understands his mistake.[2]
Andrew and the others also discuss Victor's childhood illness and the bullying he experienced. Andrew expresses anger toward the individuals responsible and reveals that he had previously wanted to confront them, but Victor's insistence upon resolving his own problems prevented him from interfering. Andrew nevertheless reaffirms his loyalty and makes clear that Victor can rely upon his friends whenever assistance is genuinely required.[2]
Victor subsequently shows photographs of his wives, leaving Andrew and the others astonished by their beauty and the extraordinary speed with which Victor has acquired romantic partners. Their admiration changes when Victor begins describing the possessive and jealous behavior of the women. Andrew recognizes the similarities to the fictional yandere archetype and warns Victor about the dangers associated with obsessive romantic attachment. Victor's admission that he genuinely prefers such women only increases his friends' concern, with Andrew struggling to understand why someone would willingly pursue the characteristics normally treated as warning signs in romantic relationships.[12]
The gathering concludes without Andrew understanding the supernatural circumstances surrounding Victor's transformation. Nevertheless, his friendship with Victor remains unchanged, preserving an important connection between the newly transformed Vampire and the ordinary Human life he had known before supernatural society became central to his existence.

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Andrew initially remains outside the major supernatural conflicts surrounding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], continuing his ordinary life while the influence of Vampires, [[Werewolves]], [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] and other supernatural beings expands around his childhood friends. However, the separation becomes increasingly difficult to maintain as the dangers associated with Victor's growing faction begin affecting people whose lives had previously remained disconnected from supernatural politics.
Andrew eventually reveals enough information concerning Victor's transformation to attract Liena's attention. Unlike her son, Liena possesses extensive experience with supernatural society through her former career as a Hunter, and she quickly recognizes that Victor's changes cannot be explained through ordinary biology. Andrew attempts to reassure her that his friend is not dangerous, but Liena refuses to accept the situation without further investigation because she understands the risks associated with allowing inexperienced Humans to become involved with powerful supernatural creatures.[3]
Rather than immediately forbidding Andrew from interacting with Victor, Liena evaluates the circumstances surrounding the Walker and Lykos families. She already possesses information concerning Adam's Werewolf nature and understands that his family is comparatively peaceful despite belonging to a supernatural species historically opposed to Vampires. Her investigation reflects the caution and practical judgment acquired through her former profession, particularly because she considers her son's safety more important than the prejudices traditionally maintained by the old Inquisition.[3]
Liena subsequently visits Anna while accompanied by Andrew and members of the childhood friendship group. The gathering eventually brings them into contact with Ruby, Kaguya and other members of Victor's supernatural household. Andrew is exposed to a considerably broader range of supernatural beings than he previously understood, while Liena recognizes the significance of the powerful Vampire families associated with Victor.[3]
Their meeting within the underground residence places Andrew alongside his childhood companions and several adults possessing supernatural experience. The presence of Adam, Liena and the Lykos family contributes to a discussion about the dangers surrounding Victor and the increasingly unstable supernatural world. Andrew responds to many of these revelations with disbelief and humor, although he gradually recognizes that the people he has known for years possess histories and abilities far beyond ordinary Human expectations.[3][14]
Ruby's explanation of the supernatural world exposes Andrew to the consequences of Victor's earlier actions and the scale of the threats surrounding his faction. He learns about the destruction caused by major conflicts, the existence of powerful Divine and Demonic beings and the extraordinary influence Victor has acquired in a relatively short period. Andrew's initial reactions reflect the difficulty of reconciling these revelations with his memories of Victor as an ordinary sickly young man.[15]
Frederick's accomplishments during the same period demonstrate the practical value of specialized supernatural techniques. His successful infiltration of an Inquisition installation through concealment and impersonation impresses Ruby and Kaguya, while Andrew recognizes that his friend has acquired abilities far beyond those available through ordinary athletic training. The growing supernatural competence of the people surrounding Andrew contributes to his eventual interest in developing his own combat capabilities.[15]
Andrew also encounters Victor's increasingly complicated household. When Victor introduces members of his family, including his adopted daughters [[Nero]] and Ophis, his wives, maids and the Scarlett sisters, Andrew is surprised by the number and diversity of supernatural beings gathered around his childhood friend. His reactions are particularly exaggerated when he discovers that several women whose appearances resemble young adults are actually centuries old.[13]
The encounter becomes even more extraordinary when [[Scathach]] recognizes Liena. The ancient Countess remembers her from a previous confrontation and expresses particular interest in the unusual Human technique that managed to injure her. Liena immediately becomes uncomfortable because she understands Scathach's obsession with battle and has no desire to attract her attention again. Andrew, Frederick, Edward and Leona recognize the danger implied by the exchange, while Victor reacts with characteristic enthusiasm toward the possibility of confronting exceptionally strong opponents.[13]
Andrew is also affected by Victor's extraordinary supernatural attractiveness. Alongside Frederick, he attempts to resist the influence of Victor's unconscious Charm, reminding himself of his romantic preferences rather than allowing the overwhelming beauty of the Vampire to influence his thoughts. The incident emphasizes how completely Victor's appearance has surpassed ordinary Vampiric standards, while Andrew continues responding through the irreverent humor familiar to his childhood companions.[13]
As the threat of a Demonic invasion becomes increasingly serious, Victor begins evaluating the safety of the Humans connected to his family. Liena, Andrew and Frederick are among the individuals required to consider whether they should remain on Earth or relocate to territory capable of providing greater protection. The danger extends beyond ordinary political conflict, since the approaching Demons possess overwhelming physical advantages and the capacity to kill or devour Humans who cannot defend themselves.[16]
The discussion forces Andrew and the others to recognize that ordinary confidence and limited supernatural training are insufficient against the forces threatening Earth. Frederick openly acknowledges that he cannot realistically protect himself and his family against a large-scale Demonic invasion without help, while Andrew and Liena are similarly included in Victor's preparations. The gathering demonstrates Victor's determination to provide his oldest friends and their families with the means to survive rather than leaving them vulnerable merely because they were born Human.[16]
Liena eventually begins personally training Andrew in the methods of the old Inquisition. Her experience provides him with practical combat techniques designed around the weaknesses of physically superior supernatural opponents. Because ordinary Humans are far more fragile than most supernatural [[Races (Flamel)|Races]], the training emphasizes preparation, deception, exploiting vulnerabilities and unconventional fighting methods rather than relying exclusively upon strength or conventional martial honor.[4]
Andrew's natural athleticism provides a useful foundation, but he remains comparatively inexperienced beside veteran Hunters who have spent years or decades developing their abilities. He trains in techniques associated with the Inquisition while referring to himself as a Hunter because his mother is no longer formally affiliated with the organization. His supernatural development is also constrained by his inability to develop the genuine Faith necessary for the more advanced religious abilities employed by traditional Inquisitors.[4][5]
Although Andrew understands that Gods exist, his practical worldview prevents him from sincerely worshipping the [[Heavenly Father|Biblical God]]. He has witnessed enough supernatural corruption and selfishness to recognize that Divine status does not automatically establish moral superiority, and he cannot comfortably direct his Faith toward an entity whose character he does not trust. Consequently, his early combat development depends more heavily upon the practical techniques taught by Liena than upon the full religious potential of the old Inquisition.[5]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Andrew continues developing as the [[Elderblood Empire]] expands beyond the supernatural civilizations that originally defined [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s life. Although several of his childhood companions have acquired extraordinary racial characteristics, Andrew remains Human during the earlier Imperial period. His training under Liena allows him to participate in supernatural affairs, but his biological limitations remain substantial compared with the powerful Vampires, [[Werewolves]] and Divine beings increasingly common within the Empire.[4]
Andrew eventually reunites with Frederick and Edward atop a skyscraper in the Imperial Capital. While waiting for his friends, he reflects upon the enormous transformation experienced by Victor and the civilization surrounding them. The Emperor now possesses power far beyond anything imaginable during their childhood, yet Andrew considers his fundamental personality remarkably consistent with the man he knew before the discovery of supernatural society.[11]
Andrew rejects the belief that power inevitably corrupts. In his view, individuals who acquire extraordinary strength often begin openly expressing characteristics they previously concealed because they no longer fear the consequences of their actions. He considers Victor different from most people because his friend has retained the same fundamental principles throughout his development, continuing to reward kindness and respond to hostility with overwhelming force. Andrew recognizes that the scale of Victor's retaliation and generosity has changed, but he regards the underlying motivations as familiar.[11]
The reunion quickly returns to the informal relationship maintained by the childhood group. Andrew greets Frederick and Edward, jokes about Frederick's superior Daywalker body and questions Edward concerning the increasingly complicated romantic traditions of the [[Lykos Clan]]. Their conversations also reflect the cultural changes brought about by Imperial technology, including the development of entertainment systems capable of producing anime and other media at extraordinary speed.[11][4]
Andrew's continued Humanity becomes particularly important when the differences between the three men are considered. Frederick has become a Daywalker, while Edward possesses the powerful characteristics of the Lykos Werewolf lineage. Andrew remains an Inquisitor trainee whose abilities are limited by ordinary Human biology and incomplete religious development, placing him at a substantial disadvantage compared with companions whose racial foundations provide far greater opportunities for growth.[4]
Victor eventually recalls an offer Andrew made during their youth. His friend had promised to help him escape if he ever encountered circumstances serious enough to require leaving his ordinary life behind, even suggesting that he could arrange for Victor to disappear to another country. Although Andrew made the offer long before Victor possessed supernatural power, the gesture remained meaningful because it reflected the loyalty of someone willing to assist him when he was vulnerable.[6]
Victor decides to repay that friendship by transforming Andrew's racial foundation. With a simple gesture, he reconstructs Andrew into a High Human, immediately producing an enormous improvement in his physical and spiritual characteristics. Edward recognizes the transformation through his supernatural perception, observing that Andrew remains Human despite possessing a supernaturally superior existence. The alteration demonstrates Victor's ability to manipulate the information contained within the Soul, allowing him to reconstruct a person's Race without relying upon the conventional limitations of natural reproduction or ordinary biological evolution.[6]
The transformation grants Andrew a body whose strength, speed, durability and potential vastly exceed the natural limitations of ordinary Humanity. He also acquires biological agelessness, removing the ordinary expiration date imposed by Human aging. Andrew initially interprets this development as immortality, but Victor clarifies that freedom from natural age-related death does not make him invulnerable. Andrew can still be killed through sufficiently powerful attacks or supernatural effects, just as Gods and other long-lived beings can be destroyed despite possessing extraordinary lifespans.[6]
Victor also reveals that Liena has received the same racial improvement. The transformation preserves both mother and son without requiring them to abandon their fundamental Human nature, allowing them to survive the enormous periods of accelerated Time that increasingly characterize the Empire's development. Andrew reacts with gratitude, although he quickly returns to teasing Victor through references to the brothers' code and the attractiveness of his mother.[6]
The sudden improvement in Andrew's body does not immediately provide complete mastery over his new characteristics. His strength and speed increase much faster than his physical instincts can adapt, creating problems when he attempts ordinary movements. This temporary limitation becomes especially apparent after Frederick releases an unexpectedly powerful supernatural attack, causing Andrew to misjudge his own speed while attempting to escape and collide with a nearby wall.[5]
Victor subsequently begins explaining the relationship between religious techniques and Divine power to his childhood companions. Frederick's [[Onmyoujutsu]] and Andrew's Inquisition techniques rely upon related principles, employing Faith to establish a connection with a Divine source and temporarily borrow supernatural power. However, the precise structure of their techniques differs, with Onmyoujutsu possessing a broader range of possible invocations while the traditional Inquisition relies upon a more restricted Divine framework.[5]
Andrew acknowledges that his lack of genuine Faith has prevented him from properly developing the religious techniques inherited from the old Inquisition. His experiences with supernatural Gods have made him increasingly skeptical of claims to Divine authority, particularly because many of those beings have behaved selfishly or maliciously. Victor recognizes that the difficulty does not originate from Andrew's inability to understand the supernatural system but from his unwillingness to offer sincere devotion to an individual he does not trust.[5]
Victor provides an alternative by offering himself as the Divine source supporting Andrew's Faith. He possesses an enormous collection of Divinities and authorities, including numerous Concepts acquired through his own development and the absorption of other Gods. Although Andrew and his companions already know that Victor is extraordinarily powerful, the Emperor decides that merely describing his abilities will not adequately demonstrate the scale of his existence.[5]
Victor transforms into an imposing Imperial form and allows his Divine presence to affect the surrounding Reality. He then transports Andrew, Frederick and Edward into the space between galaxies, where they witness an enormous Draconic manifestation whose size exceeds entire Galactic structures. The experience exposes the three men to the incomprehensible scale of Victor's existence, revealing that the childhood friend they remember has become a Being whose natural characteristics surpass those of the Gods they originally associated with supernatural society.[5]
The Emperor's demonstration continues as he transports them through several extraordinary environments, including worlds covered in lava, snow and water, as well as desolate regions and planets inhabited by unfamiliar extraterrestrial species. Andrew is shocked by the experience, particularly because the environments appear genuine rather than simple illusions. The demonstration establishes the difference between merely knowing that Victor possesses Divine power and personally witnessing the consequences of that power.[5]
Victor concludes by bestowing The Emperor's Blessing upon Andrew, Frederick and Edward, establishing a direct spiritual connection between himself and his oldest companions. The Blessing assures them of his continued protection across different worlds and Realities while encouraging them to survive, develop and live meaningful lives. Rather than granting them every possible supernatural ability directly, Victor provides a foundation through which they can continue advancing along their own paths.[5]
Frederick immediately tests the connection through Onmyoujutsu, invoking Victor's Concept of Fear and producing an enormous sphere of crimson Negativity. The resulting attack is far stronger than Frederick expects from a basic technique, demonstrating that the Divine Being supplying an invocation can dramatically alter its destructive power. Andrew witnesses the manifestation and questions whether he can eventually achieve something similar through the Inquisition's techniques.[5]
Frederick explains that Andrew's religious system follows comparable principles despite employing different methods. Both traditions depend upon Faith and borrowed [[Divine Energy]], making Victor's Blessing particularly valuable because Andrew now possesses a Divine source he can sincerely trust. The experience establishes the foundation for Andrew's subsequent development into a powerful religious combatant capable of channeling the Emperor's authority through the Imperial Inquisition.[5]
Andrew's High Human transformation and new Divine connection fundamentally alter his future. He is no longer confined to the biological limitations of ordinary Humanity, while the religious techniques previously restricted by his skepticism can now be supported by genuine Faith in Victor. His progression nevertheless remains dependent upon training, experience and the continued development of his own abilities rather than automatically receiving the complete power of the Emperor.

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Andrew continues living and developing throughout the enormous periods of accelerated Time experienced by the [[Elderblood Empire]]. The civilization expands across Galactic territory, develops increasingly sophisticated technology and integrates numerous supernatural [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] into a unified Imperial society. Andrew's High Human physiology allows him to survive these developments without suffering ordinary biological aging, while his religious training gradually transforms him into an elite combatant associated with the Imperial Inquisition.
During the Empire's hundred-thousand-year celebration, Andrew gathers with Frederick and Edward in a bar overlooking an Imperial parade. The three continue exchanging insults and jokes much as they did during childhood, mocking one another's supernatural characteristics and personalities. Andrew and Frederick repeatedly tease Edward through comparisons to fictional Vampires, provoking his irritation because he remains a Werewolf despite their persistent jokes.[9]
The gathering also includes members of the Lykos family, including Adam, Johnny, Leona, Maya and Bellatrix. Edward's responsibilities as an Alpha become another source of humor, with Andrew and Frederick taking advantage of their familiarity to mock the increasingly serious positions occupied by their childhood companions. Their conduct reflects the freedom associated with personal relationships established long before supernatural status became an important aspect of their lives.[9]
The arrival of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters, Leone Luna Elena Elderblood and [[Mirabelle Elizabeth Elderblood]], introduces Andrew to the extraordinary second generation of the Elderblood dynasty. Both possess characteristics inherited from their mothers and Victor, including the distinctive violet eyes associated with the Emperor's descendants. Andrew becomes impressed by their appearance and inadvertently provokes the protective members of the Lykos family when his comments appear to imply that their beauty originates exclusively from Victor.[9]
Andrew quickly attempts to correct the misunderstanding, recognizing that provoking Adam, Edward and Johnny over their female relatives would create unnecessary trouble. Frederick deliberately contributes to the confusion through exaggerated jokes and theatrical behavior, while Andrew becomes increasingly frustrated by his friend's refusal to behave seriously. Their interaction demonstrates that even after enormous periods of supernatural development, the original childhood group remains comfortable engaging in the same immature arguments that characterized their youth.[9]
The passage of Time also alters Andrew's ordinary biological responses. During the gathering, Frederick tricks him into drinking a transparent alcoholic beverage disguised as water, but Andrew recognizes that his improved High Human physiology makes ordinary intoxication considerably more difficult. The incident provides another example of the physiological advantages acquired through Victor's transformation, although it does not establish absolute immunity to every form of intoxication or supernatural substance.[9]
Andrew, Frederick and Edward have become influential figures within the Empire. Their personal relationship with Victor makes them highly respected and attractive to potential romantic partners, while their supernatural accomplishments give them considerable authority outside their private gatherings. Although the three behave casually among trusted companions, ordinary Imperial citizens regard them with substantially greater respect. The narrative also acknowledges that their positions allow them to maintain harems, although Andrew's individual spouses, romantic arrangements and exact number of partners remain unidentified.[9]
Andrew's religious development becomes particularly important as the Empire's institutions mature. The original Inquisition has been reorganized into the Imperial Inquisition, a Faith-based military tradition serving the Empire and operating beneath its official religious hierarchy. Although its supernatural methods retain principles associated with the old Hunter system, its principal Divine sources now include Victor and the Dragon Goddesses belonging directly to the [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]].[7]
Andrew rises to become one of the organization's most prominent combatants. His exceptional position is supported by the Emperor's personal Blessing, his enormous accumulated experience and his ability to invoke Divine authorities that ordinary practitioners can access only after extensive training and spiritual preparation. His prestige becomes comparable to that of Frederick, who has risen to become the leader and most famous practitioner among the Imperial Onmyō warriors.[7]
The two religious traditions operate beneath the larger system administered by High Priestess [[Valeria Alekerth]]. Although Andrew and Frederick are informally treated as unofficial Popes because of their proximity to Victor, neither holds the formal position of supreme religious administrator. Valeria remains responsible for the organization of the Empire's official religion, while Andrew's authority derives from his prominence within the Imperial Inquisition and his exceptional relationship with the God Emperor.[7]
The supernatural methods employed by Andrew and Frederick differ despite relying upon comparable principles. Frederick's [[Onmyoujutsu]] can invoke compatible Gods from multiple Pantheons when the appropriate permission exists, giving it considerable versatility. Andrew's Imperial Inquisition techniques draw more directly upon Victor and the Divine beings formally belonging to Dragons Nest, providing access to extraordinary power while restricting the ordinary range of compatible Divine sources.[7]
The distinction becomes important when considering allied Pantheons. The Gods of the Shinto, Hindu and Biblical traditions exist within the political sphere dominated by the Empire, but this does not automatically establish direct membership within Dragons Nest. Andrew can invoke powers associated with [[Amaterasu]] Elderblood and [[Kali Elderblood]] because both are Dragon Goddesses belonging directly to Victor's Pantheon. In contrast, Gods such as Susanoo and [[Shiva]] remain outside the standard Imperial Inquisition invocation framework because their political subordination does not make them direct members of the Emperor's Pantheon.[7]
Both Andrew and Frederick possess extraordinary privileges because of their personal Blessings and relationship with Victor. Ordinary practitioners require substantial Faith, Divine permission and years of instruction to develop advanced religious techniques, but the Emperor's childhood friends possess unusually broad access to compatible powers. These privileges contribute to their exceptional reputations among Imperial believers and allow them to employ supernatural abilities far beyond the normal limits of their respective Races.[7]
Andrew's mature strength is demonstrated during a military operation in the Andromeda Galaxy. An Imperial fleet consisting of more than fifty vessels arrives near a planet afflicted by a dangerous extraterrestrial species. The planet has belonged to the Empire for an extended period, and its World Tree has already been secured. However, the newly emerged species possesses rapid reproduction, considerable adaptability and enough destructive potential to threaten the planetary environment, prompting the Fourth Legion to request biological samples for research.[7]
Rather than leaving the expedition entirely to the Imperial Spartans and the fleet's orbital weapons, Frederick volunteers to descend personally. Andrew decides to accompany him, with both men treating the operation as an opportunity for direct combat after prolonged periods of relative peace. The fleet's commanding operator is alarmed by their decision because their status as close friends of the Emperor makes their safety an important responsibility, and additional military support is prepared to prevent unnecessary casualties.[7]
The two deploy in advanced black Imperial armor decorated with gold and crimson accents, descending from the atmosphere and striking the planetary surface with enough force to create enormous craters. Their arrival attracts hostile creatures exceeding ten meters in height, immediately providing the opportunity for the direct combat they originally sought.[7]
Frederick begins fighting through Onmyoujutsu, invoking Victor as the [[Ares|God of War]] and transforming his advanced Western sword into an enormous supernatural Energy weapon. His attacks tear through the alien creatures, while a particularly powerful Energy discharge catastrophically alters the surrounding geography and divides a continental region. Andrew observes the destruction and criticizes Frederick for creating unnecessary environmental damage when the mission requires capturing specimens rather than exterminating the entire species.[7]
Andrew subsequently employs the techniques of the Imperial Inquisition. Directing his Faith toward Victor, he offers his body as a Divine instrument through which the Emperor's supernatural power can manifest. Golden radiance surrounds his body, and an extraordinary bolt of golden Divine Lightning descends directly upon him, enveloping his High Human existence in borrowed supernatural Energy.[7]
The invocation temporarily grants Andrew Vampiric characteristics reflecting Victor's existence. He manifests two swords composed of plasma-like [[Divine Energy]] and wields them through reverse grips, attacking the surrounding creatures at extraordinary speed. His movements are accompanied by supernatural Lightning, while Fire, Ice and Blood-related effects erupt around the enemies he destroys. The combination makes Andrew resemble a smaller manifestation of Victor despite remaining fundamentally Human beneath the temporary transformation.[7]
Andrew's speed and offensive capabilities allow him to travel repeatedly around the planet while eliminating hostile creatures across enormous distances. Frederick immediately recognizes the irony of the situation, since Andrew has begun causing similarly excessive destruction after criticizing his own attack. Their reactions demonstrate that both retain the same competitiveness and enthusiasm for dramatic combat that characterized their earlier friendship.[7]
The operation establishes the extraordinary fundamental strength of both men. Their physical and supernatural foundations provide enormous strength, stamina and independent flight, placing their ordinary combat capabilities at planetary scales. When their Divine invocations are considered, their maximum available threat potential increases considerably further because the Gods supplying those powers are vastly stronger than the practitioners themselves.[7]
The Empire consequently classifies Andrew and Frederick as V-05 combatants, indicating Solar System-level maximum destructive potential through their complete available supernatural arsenal. This classification is not equivalent to granting Andrew an independent Solar System-level Divinity or establishing that every ordinary physical attack can destroy an entire Solar System. Instead, it reflects the danger posed by an individual capable of channeling extraordinary Divine authorities through Faith-based combat techniques.[7][8]
After the Imperial Spartans arrive, Frederick assumes command and orders the troops to secure the largest continental regions and prepare for extraction. Andrew joins him in considering a method of ending the planetary threat without causing additional destruction. Rather than continuing to annihilate the creatures through direct combat, the two decide to request the authority of Order from [[Violet Elderblood Snow]].[7]
Andrew begins the invocation by addressing Violet as the Goddess and Empress, requesting that the chaotic environment become a world governed by Order. Frederick simultaneously employs his Onmyō techniques, allowing their different religious systems to cooperate in the same supernatural objective. Golden radiance surrounds both men, and the invocation produces a crown accompanied by a translucent manifestation of Violet.[7]
The Empress unexpectedly answers their prayers personally through a Divine projection. Andrew and Frederick are shocked because they intended to request a portion of Violet's power rather than attract her direct attention. The surrounding Imperial soldiers immediately recognize the projection and offer their respects, while Violet reassures them that she is not physically present and has merely manifested a portion of her existence.[7]
Violet examines the situation through the memories of Andrew and Frederick, allowing her to understand the nature of the expedition and the danger posed by the alien creatures. She also surveys the surrounding Solar System to determine whether unrelated Life exists nearby before completing the intervention. Her projected body expands enormously, and she imposes Order directly upon the planetary environment.[7]
The resulting effect pacifies the hostile species, rendering the creatures docile and eliminating their immediate aggression. Violet's authority also stabilizes the harsh climate and improves the planetary atmosphere, allowing the Imperial forces to continue their mission without unnecessarily exterminating the newly discovered Race. The scale of the intervention demonstrates how completely the Dragon Goddesses surpass the ordinary supernatural powers available to most Imperial combatants.[8]
Although Andrew participates in the successful invocation, the planetary pacification is performed directly by Violet. His accomplishment consists of establishing the Divine connection and requesting the appropriate authority through the Imperial Inquisition's religious system. The distinction is important because Andrew's ability to borrow supernatural powers does not make the invoked Divinities permanent components of his own existence.[8]
Following Violet's departure, the Imperial soldiers resume their assigned tasks while Andrew and Frederick return to their familiar arguments. Frederick complains that the Empress has taken the dramatic moment they intended to create, while Andrew responds that another opportunity will eventually arise. Their behavior demonstrates that even centuries and millennia of supernatural development have not fundamentally changed the personalities established during their Human lives.[8]
Andrew's responsibilities expand further as the Empire prepares for war against civilizations beyond its original Sector. During the enormous mobilization ordered by Victor, fleets, military bases, industrial worlds, research facilities and logistical organizations begin preparing for a conflict on a scale far beyond the wars previously experienced by Earth and [[Nightingale]]. Andrew observes the preparations while walking through Imperial territory with Frederick and Edward, becoming increasingly concerned about the potential casualties of an intergalactic conflict.[10]
Although Andrew possesses extraordinary combat experience and has become a much stronger individual, he has not abandoned his compassion. The thought of countless soldiers and civilians dying in a war between Galactic civilizations troubles him, and he openly questions how many lives may be lost. Frederick argues that the Empire has little meaningful choice because submission would place its people under the control of hostile rulers, while Andrew acknowledges that Victor would never willingly surrender his sovereignty.[10]
The discussion also turns toward the Empire's control over death and reincarnation. Frederick reminds Andrew that the Imperial afterlife system can provide another existence to those whose Souls remain within its protection, joking about the possibility of receiving an adventure in another world. Andrew refuses to romanticize the prospect of dying and makes clear that he prefers remaining alive, demonstrating that his extraordinary longevity and access to supernatural protection have not eliminated his desire for an ordinary, meaningful personal life.[10]
Victor unexpectedly appears before the three men while they discuss the coming war. His manifestation resembles a partially translucent spiritual projection, and the surrounding citizens do not appear to notice his presence. Edward asks whether the Emperor is suffering from the effects of the enormous ritual he has recently performed, but Victor reassures them that he is capable of enduring far greater burdens.[10]
Rather than immediately recruiting his friends into the Imperial armies, Victor asks what they intend to do during the war. Andrew responds that protecting their families is the obvious priority, while Frederick and Edward agree that nothing is more important. Victor is relieved by their answer because he does not want his oldest companions recklessly entering battles against enemies whose power could threaten even the Empire's strongest warriors.[10]
Victor subsequently provides each man with an Imperial badge shaped like a Dragon and formed from his own power. The badges grant access to the strongest version of the Tower of Nightmares, located on Victor's personal world. The facility has been modified for battles involving the Dragon Goddesses, making it extraordinarily dangerous even for powerful supernatural combatants. Nevertheless, Victor offers access without forcing his friends to participate, explaining that the opportunity is available if they genuinely desire further strength.[10]
Andrew recognizes the danger associated with the Tower and initially hesitates. Frederick shares his concern, while Edward confidently announces that they will meet at the entrance. Their reactions suggest that Andrew and Frederick are already seriously considering the opportunity despite attempting to appear indifferent. Victor's decision reflects his refusal to simply grant every friend additional Divinities or Eldritch transformations, preferring to provide them with the means to strengthen themselves through their own efforts and decisions.[10]
Andrew's subsequent progress within the Tower remains unconfirmed. Nevertheless, his willingness to consider the extraordinarily demanding training demonstrates that he continues seeking personal development despite already possessing immense supernatural strength. His final expressed priorities remain consistent with the loyalty and practicality that defined him during childhood: he intends to protect his family, preserve the freedom of his civilization and acquire whatever strength is necessary without abandoning the relationships that have remained important throughout his life.[10]

His racial transformation grants extraordinary physical potential, biological agelessness and the capacity to continue developing beyond ordinary Human limitations, while his religious abilities allow him to channel supernatural power belonging to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and compatible members of the [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]]. These sources must be distinguished when evaluating his capabilities because his maximum V-05 threat classification includes borrowed Divine authority rather than representing the destructive output of his unenhanced physical body alone.[6][7][8]

### Power
Andrew's power progression begins with ordinary Human physiology and athletic conditioning, followed by Hunter training beneath his mother and a fundamental racial transformation performed by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. His original limitations included the physical vulnerability of Humans and an inability to access the complete potential of Faith-based Inquisition techniques. The High Human transformation removed his natural biological expiration date and provided extraordinary physical potential, while the Emperor's Blessing established the spiritual foundation necessary for advanced Divine invocation.[4][6][5]
Immediately following his transformation, Andrew possessed strength and speed vastly exceeding his previous capabilities but lacked the instinctive control necessary to employ them efficiently. His subsequent development across enormous periods of accelerated Imperial Time allowed him to adapt to the superior body and accumulate considerable supernatural combat experience. By the late Imperial era, his fundamental physical and supernatural characteristics had reached planetary-class combat levels, with independent flight, extraordinary endurance and the ability to participate directly in dangerous planetary operations.[6][5][7]
Andrew's V-05 classification represents a maximum threat potential sufficient to annihilate an entire Solar System through the complete powers available to him. This classification is substantially influenced by his ability to invoke Divine beings whose powers vastly exceed his own racial foundation. Consequently, his ordinary High Human body, temporary Divine-instrument empowerment, compatible Divine sources and demonstrated attacks must be evaluated separately rather than treated as interchangeable expressions of personal destructive power.[7][8]

### Weaknesses
- Killable: Andrew's High Human physiology removes natural biological aging but does not make him invulnerable. He remains capable of dying through sufficiently powerful attacks or supernatural effects.[6]
- Faith Dependency: His advanced Imperial Inquisition techniques depend upon genuine Faith and compatible external Divine authority. These techniques do not grant him permanent personal ownership of the powers he invokes.[5][7]
- Invocation Compatibility: Andrew cannot automatically invoke every God politically subordinate to the [[Elderblood Empire]]. His religious system operates primarily through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Divine beings belonging directly to Dragons Nest, excluding Gods whose only connection is political allegiance through another Pantheon.[7]
- External Divine Source: His maximum V-05 threat potential depends significantly upon extraordinary Divine power borrowed from beings much stronger than his ordinary High Human body.[7][8]
- Variable Available Power: The scale and nature of Andrew's supernatural techniques depend upon the compatible Divine authority being invoked and the power accessible through the religious system. Compatibility with a particular God does not establish that every ability possessed by that God has been independently demonstrated by Andrew.[7]
- Inferiority to the Highest Imperial Powers: Although Andrew is an elite V-05 combatant, his demonstrated power and classification do not place him on the same level as the strongest Dragon Goddesses or Victor himself. His ability to request their intervention does not make their full power a permanent component of his own existence.[7][8]

#### Former Limitations
- Ordinary Human Physiology: Andrew originally possessed the biological limitations of ordinary Humanity, making direct confrontation with stronger supernatural beings extremely dangerous. The limitation was overcome when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] transformed him into a High Human.[6]
- Lack of Religious Faith: Andrew initially struggled to employ advanced Inquisition techniques because he could not sincerely worship the [[Heavenly Father|Biblical God]]. His personal trust in Victor eventually allowed him to develop genuine Faith through the Emperor's Divine authority.[5]
- Unfamiliar High Human Strength: Andrew initially lacked the instinctive control necessary to use his dramatically enhanced physical abilities efficiently. His subsequent training and prolonged development overcame this early adjustment problem.[5][7]
- Limited Hunter Experience: Andrew began his supernatural training comparatively late and initially lacked the experience of veteran Inquisitors. His later development through extensive accelerated Time allowed him to progress far beyond the limitations of his original training.[4][7]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Andrew's oldest and closest childhood friend, and the principal individual responsible for his racial transformation and later Divine development. Their friendship originated while Victor was still an ordinary Human suffering from a severe medical condition, allowing Andrew to understand his personality independently of the supernatural powers and political titles he eventually acquired. Although Andrew frequently mocks Victor and participates in immature jokes, he genuinely values their relationship and remains protective of his friend's independence.[1][2]
Andrew's loyalty was particularly meaningful during Victor's childhood because he understood the limitations imposed by his illness. He wanted to confront the individuals who bullied Victor but respected his friend's refusal to allow others to resolve his personal grievances. Their relationship consequently developed through mutual trust rather than dependence, with Andrew remaining available whenever Victor genuinely required assistance.[2]
Victor eventually repays that loyalty by transforming Andrew into a High Human, remembering a promise Andrew had made during their youth about helping him escape serious trouble. The transformation provides a superior physical and spiritual foundation while allowing Andrew to preserve his fundamental Human identity. Victor also transforms Liena, demonstrating that his concern extends to the families of his closest companions rather than exclusively to the individuals themselves.[6]
Their relationship becomes the foundation of Andrew's Faith. Rather than requiring Andrew to worship an unfamiliar God whose motives he cannot verify, Victor allows him to establish a supernatural connection through the trust developed during their childhood. The Emperor's Blessing gives Andrew access to extraordinary Divine authority while preserving his practical worldview and longstanding friendship with Victor.[5]
Even during the Empire's preparations for intergalactic war, Victor continues respecting Andrew's independence. Rather than forcing him to confront enemies beyond his capabilities or arbitrarily granting him additional Divinities, Victor provides access to the strongest Tower of Nightmares and allows Andrew to decide whether he wishes to pursue the dangerous training. Andrew's response demonstrates that his loyalty remains grounded in protecting his family and supporting the freedom of the Empire rather than blindly pursuing power or personal glory.[10]

#### Frederick Winter
[[Frederick Winter]] is another of Andrew's closest childhood companions and his principal partner during several important Imperial appearances. Their friendship involves frequent jokes, exaggerated arguments and a shared willingness to behave immaturely despite the extraordinary supernatural powers they eventually acquire. Frederick's enthusiasm for anime and fictional entertainment often becomes a source of amusement or irritation for Andrew, while both remain comfortable exchanging insults because of the familiarity established during their Human lives.[11][9]
Their supernatural development follows different paths. Frederick becomes the first Daywalker and eventually rises to become the highest-ranking practitioner among the Imperial Onmyō warriors, while Andrew retains his High Human nature and develops through the Imperial Inquisition. Both employ Faith-based techniques capable of invoking Divine power, although Frederick possesses broader cross-Pantheon versatility while Andrew's religious abilities connect more directly to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and members of Dragons Nest.[5][7]
Their joint planetary expedition demonstrates both the rivalry and cooperation characterizing their relationship. Andrew criticizes Frederick for causing unnecessary geographical destruction, then immediately produces comparable devastation after invoking Victor. The two later combine their religious techniques to request Violet's authority over Order, demonstrating that their humor and competitiveness do not prevent effective teamwork when circumstances require it.[7][8]
As the Empire prepares for intergalactic war, Andrew and Frederick continue discussing the consequences of the conflict and the importance of protecting their families. Both receive access to the strongest Tower of Nightmares from Victor and recognize the danger associated with the opportunity, although their reactions suggest that neither intends to abandon the possibility of further development without serious consideration.[10]

#### Edward Jonathan Elizabeth Lykos
[[Edward Jonathan Elizabeth Lykos]] is one of Andrew's oldest childhood friends and another member of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s original social circle. Their friendship survives Edward's development through the Lykos Werewolf lineage and Andrew's transformation into a High Human, allowing both men to continue interacting through the familiar humor and informal disagreements established before the discovery of supernatural society.[2][9]
Edward understands the reasons behind Leona's resentment toward Andrew, since his sister suffered considerable emotional harm after Andrew betrayed her only close female friend. Although Edward recognizes that Andrew regrets his actions, he also understands that reconciliation cannot be forced and that Leona's feelings should be respected.[2]
Their relationship becomes a source of frequent humor during the Imperial era. Andrew repeatedly mocks Edward's complicated family relationships and the unusual romantic traditions of the [[Lykos Clan]], while Edward responds with irritation at the apparent refusal of his friends to behave seriously. Nevertheless, their friendship remains strong enough that Edward confidently assumes Andrew and Frederick will eventually accept Victor's invitation to pursue further training within the Tower of Nightmares.[4][9][10]

#### Liena Walter
[[Liena Walter]] is Andrew's biological mother and the individual responsible for his original supernatural education. Although Andrew spent much of his childhood unaware of her connection to the supernatural world, Liena previously belonged to the Inquisition as an accomplished Hunter. Her experience allowed her to recognize the danger surrounding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s transformation and motivated her to investigate the increasingly complicated circumstances affecting her son and his childhood friends.[3]
Liena possesses considerable experience confronting supernatural creatures. Among her most remarkable accomplishments was an encounter with [[Scathach]], during which she employed a technique capable of injuring the legendary Vampire Countess. Scathach retained an intense interest in the incident because very few Humans had ever managed to wound her, while Liena regarded the experience as an unfortunate source of attention from a dangerous opponent. The incident demonstrates that Andrew's mother possessed skills far beyond those expected of an ordinary retired Hunter.[13]
Liena eventually trains Andrew in the practical methods of the old Inquisition, emphasizing tactics designed to overcome enemies possessing much greater strength than ordinary Humans. Her instruction becomes the foundation of his later development as an Imperial Inquisitor, even though his initial lack of genuine Faith prevents him from employing the complete supernatural potential of the traditional religious system.[4][5]
Victor subsequently transforms Liena into a High Human alongside Andrew, removing the ordinary biological expiration date associated with their original Race. The transformation allows both mother and son to survive the enormous periods of accelerated Time experienced by the Empire, preserving their relationship without requiring either to become a Vampire or another supernatural species.[6]

#### Anna Elderblood
[[Anna Elderblood]] is [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s mother and an important parental figure associated with Andrew's childhood. Before her supernatural transformation, she was an accomplished Human lawyer who assisted Andrew's family during a custody dispute involving his father. Andrew consequently possesses considerable respect for Anna, regarding her as someone who helped protect his family long before Victor became an Emperor.[1]
Their relationship is also connected to the brothers' code of conduct maintained by Victor and his friends. Andrew recognizes the importance of respecting the female relatives of his companions and avoids romantic involvement that could threaten the relationships established within the group. Victor likewise acknowledges Andrew's respect for Anna, although the subject occasionally becomes a source of jokes when the increasingly complicated relationships within the Elderblood family make the original code appear difficult to apply consistently.[1][6]

#### Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos
[[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]] is one of Andrew's childhood friends, although their relationship was complicated by his earlier romantic behavior. During their school years, Leona experienced considerable isolation because of the bullying associated with her appearance, making her limited friendships especially important. Andrew previously dated the only close female friend she possessed but cheated on her with an older woman, causing Leona to become deeply resentful toward him.[2]
Andrew later acknowledges that he failed to appreciate the importance of the relationship and expresses genuine regret over his actions. Although Leona does not immediately forgive him, their shared friendships and family connections ensure that they continue interacting throughout the development of the supernatural world and the eventual establishment of the Empire.[2]
Their relationship becomes more comfortable during the later Imperial era, although Andrew remains cautious when making comments about the beauty of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Leona's daughter, Leone. The protective reactions of Edward, Adam and Johnny remind him that his close relationship with Victor does not automatically protect him from provoking the other members of the Lykos family, particularly when his jokes concern the younger generation.[9]

#### Valeria Alekerth
[[Valeria Alekerth]] is the High Priestess of the Empire's official religion and the superior religious authority beneath whom the Imperial Inquisition and Imperial Onmyō warriors operate. Andrew's position as a prominent Imperial Inquisitor places him within the wider religious and military system administered through Valeria's authority, while Frederick possesses a comparable position among the Onmyō practitioners.[7]
Although Andrew enjoys extraordinary prestige because of his personal relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and the Blessing he received from the Emperor, his informal recognition as a Pope-like figure does not replace Valeria's official position. The distinction reflects the difference between Andrew's exceptional religious influence and the administrative responsibilities of the institution's established High Priestess.[7]

#### Violet Elderblood Snow
[[Violet Elderblood Snow]] is one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s principal wives, an Empress of the [[Elderblood Empire]] and a Divine authority accessible through Andrew's Imperial Inquisition techniques. Her position within Dragons Nest allows Andrew to request her supernatural power through Faith-based invocation, particularly in circumstances requiring the application of Order rather than destructive force.[7]
During the planetary expedition undertaken with Frederick, Andrew participates in a joint invocation requesting Violet's authority over Order. Their objective is to pacify the dangerous extraterrestrial species and restore environmental stability without continuing the destruction caused by their earlier combat.[7]
Violet personally answers the invocation through a projection, examining the circumstances before imposing Order upon the planet. Her intervention pacifies the creatures, stabilizes the climate and improves the atmosphere, demonstrating the extraordinary power available through the Divine authorities associated with Victor's Pantheon. Andrew's role involves successfully establishing the request and participating in the invocation rather than independently performing the completed planetary transformation.[7][8]
