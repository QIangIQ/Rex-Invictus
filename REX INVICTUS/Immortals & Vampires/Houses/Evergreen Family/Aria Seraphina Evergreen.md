Aira Seraphina Evergreen is the Negative World Tree of Samar, a young sprout-class World Tree and the negative counterpart of [[Aurora Seraphina Evergreen]], Samar's Positive World Tree.[2][3] She is one of the daughters of [[The Universal Tree]] and exists as the second half of Samar's planetary World Tree system, having been born after Samar developed enough sentient Life and accumulated enough Negative Energy to sustain a Negative World Tree.[2]
Among the [[World Trees]] introduced in the story, Aira is notable for still being at the sprout stage, making her far younger and less mature than Aurora, [[Yggdrasil]], Qiliphoth, and the older World Trees encountered elsewhere. Her existence helped [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] properly understand that a developed planet does not possess a single neutral World Tree, but instead a pair composed of one Positive and one Negative Tree working as complementary parts of the same planetary structure.[2]

### Physical Appearance
Aira appears as a little girl with black hair, red eyes, and a dark red dress.[2] Her youthful appearance sharply contrasts with the mature and elegant form of her elder counterpart [[Aurora Seraphina Evergreen]], visually emphasizing that Aira is still a newly born or young World Tree rather than a fully matured planetary guardian.
Her small body and childlike appearance do not indicate weakness in the ordinary sense, but rather reflect her developmental stage as a sprout World Tree. Like other [[World Trees]], her humanoid appearance represents her personified self rather than the entirety of her true planetary-scale nature.

### Personality
Aira has the mentality expected of a young World Tree. Compared to older sisters such as Aurora, [[Yggdrasil]], or Qiliphoth, she is much less ancient in bearing and carries a more childlike presence.[2]
As Samar's Negative World Tree, Aira is not "evil" in the simplistic moral sense. Rather, she exists as the natural negative counterpart required for Samar's planetary system to fully mature. Her existence is tied to the Negative Energy generated by sentient Life, making her a necessary part of Samar's balance rather than a corruption of Aurora.[2]
Aira also demonstrates the broader instincts shared by [[World Trees]]. Like her sisters, her existence is ultimately tied to the continuation of her planet rather than the temporary interests of any one civilization living upon it. This larger World Tree worldview was made clear through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s explanation of how Positive World Trees prioritize the planet over its current inhabitants, a principle that applies to the planetary system as a whole and therefore frames Aira's role as well.[4]
Because she is still a sprout, Aira should be understood as much more immature and less fully developed than older World Trees. Her personality and abilities are therefore likely more limited and less refined than those of the adult or elder Trees.

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Aira first appeared during [[Victor Elderblood]]'s stay on Samar, when he investigated the planet's World Tree and encountered both [[Aurora Seraphina Evergreen]] and Aira.[2]
During this encounter, Aurora explained the actual lifecycle of [[World Trees]]. A Positive World Tree appears first and spends millions of years nurturing a planet and developing the conditions necessary for Life. Once sentient Life emerges and produces enough Negative Energy, a corresponding Negative World Tree begins to form. Aurora identified Aira as Samar's newly born Negative World Tree, making her the younger counterpart to the older Positive Tree.[2]
This revelation was important because it clarified the real structure of planetary World Trees throughout the setting. Rather than being a single neutral existence holding every polarity at once, a developed planet possesses a Positive Tree and a Negative Tree functioning as complementary aspects of the same system.[2]
Aira's appearance also demonstrated the developmental differences possible among World Trees. Whereas Aurora had already reached adulthood and carried herself with calm ancient dignity, Aira was still a sprout and appeared as a little girl, highlighting how recently she had emerged compared with the other established World Trees.[2]

#### Later Developments
Although Aira has had much less direct screen time than older [[World Trees]] such as [[Yggdrasil]] or Aurora, her existence remains cosmologically important. She stands as one of the clearest examples of a newly born Negative World Tree and helps define how the planetary systems on worlds like Samar function.[2][3]
Her existence also provides a useful contrast with older Negative World Trees such as Qiliphoth and [[Roxanne Elderblood]], who represent later or more developed stages of the same general type of being.

### Skills & Abilities
Sprout Negative World Tree Physiology: Aira is a sprout-class Negative World Tree, making her a young but fundamentally planetary-scale being whose existence is connected to Samar's natural and supernatural structure.[2]
- Negative World Tree Nature: Aira was born as the Negative counterpart to [[Aurora Seraphina Evergreen]] after Samar's sentient Life generated sufficient Negative Energy for a Negative World Tree to emerge.[2]
- Negativity Absorption: As a Negative World Tree, Aira is sustained by and associated with Negative Energy, which forms the basis of her existence.[2]
- Negative Emotion/Intent Perception: As the negative counterpart to a Positive World Tree, Aira can be associated with the sensing and resonance of negative emotional and energetic states, paralleling how Positive [[World Trees]] can perceive positive emotions.[3]
- Nature Manipulation: Like all World Trees, Aira possesses authority over non-sentient natural elements. World Trees inherently possess control over plant life and related natural structures, with their influence extending beyond simple vegetation.[5]
- Plant Manipulation: World Trees possess natural affinity with trees, plant life, and other green forms of non-sentient Life.[5]
- Terrain Manipulation: The natural authority of a World Tree is not limited to plants and includes non-sentient natural matter such as earth and rock.[5]
- [[Natural Energy]]/Senjutsu Relation: World Trees are fundamentally tied to the natural energy systems of their planets. As one half of Samar's World Tree pair, Aira is part of the larger energetic structure underlying the planet's natural and supernatural development.[5][2]
- Planetary Connection: As Samar's Negative World Tree, Aira is not merely a being living on the planet, but a direct component of the world's life-energy framework.[2]
- World Tree Communication/Family Link: As a daughter of The Universal Tree, Aira belongs to the wider family of World Trees spread across multiple worlds, though she is much younger than most of the sisters already introduced.[1]
- Immense Longevity: Even as a sprout-class World Tree, Aira is still a supernatural existence operating on a vastly longer timescale than ordinary mortals, though she is much younger than the adult and elder Trees.[2]
- Dimensional Significance: World Trees are closely tied to the planetary and dimensional structure of advanced worlds. Through Aurora's explanation, it is clear that Aira, as Samar's Negative Tree, forms part of the same fundamental support system underlying Samar's supernatural framework.[2][3]

#### Negative World Tree Traits
Aira exists because Samar reached the developmental stage necessary to generate a Negative World Tree. This means she was born later than Aurora and is naturally younger and less mature.[2]
Her existence demonstrates that Negative [[World Trees]] are a natural stage in the growth of a living world rather than a corruption or distortion of the Positive Tree.

#### Developmental Stage
Aira is still only a sprout World Tree. This is a major distinction between her and most of the other [[World Trees]] seen in the story.
Because of this stage:
- She is much younger than Aurora.[2]
- She presents as a child rather than a mature woman.[2]
- Her abilities and maturity should be considered less developed than those of adult or elder World Trees.

### Relationships

#### Aurora Seraphina Evergreen
[[Aurora Seraphina Evergreen]] is Aira's older sister and Samar's Positive World Tree.[2]
Aurora explained Aira's nature directly to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], describing how a Negative World Tree emerges after a Positive Tree has already spent millions of years cultivating a world. The two are separate individuals, but together they form Samar's complete World Tree system.[2]
Aurora's mature, serene and ancient demeanor contrasts heavily with Aira's youthful sprout-like existence.

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] met Aira during his investigation of Samar's World Tree.[2]
Through Aira and Aurora, Victor came to understand the true structure of [[World Trees]] and the difference between Positive and Negative Trees. Even if Aira has had comparatively little direct involvement with Victor afterward, she remains important to his cosmological understanding of the Universe's planetary systems.

#### The Universal Tree
[[The Universal Tree]] is Aira's creator, father and progenitor.[1]
Like all [[World Trees]], Aira is one of the daughters created as part of the greater system through which The Universal Tree sustains Life-bearing worlds throughout Creation.

#### Yggdrasil and Qiliphoth
[[Yggdrasil]] and Qiliphoth are older World Tree sisters who represent the corresponding Positive and Negative pair of Earth.[2]
Aira's existence serves as a Samar parallel to their arrangement, making her one of the clearest examples of how younger planetary World Tree pairs compare with older and more mature ones.

#### Roxanne Elderblood and Amara Elderblood
[[Roxanne Elderblood]] and [[Amara Elderblood]] are fellow World Tree sisters. Compared to Aira, both are much more directly involved in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s life and in the Elderblood family's internal affairs.
Aira's comparatively limited direct interaction with the main cast emphasizes how young and undeveloped she still is compared with more mature or better-integrated [[World Trees]].
