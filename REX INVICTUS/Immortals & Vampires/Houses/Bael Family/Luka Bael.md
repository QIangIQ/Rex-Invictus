Luka Bael, also rendered as Luka [[Baal]], is a high-ranking Demon, the son of former First Duke of Hell [[Bael]] and Lily Bael née [[Lucifer]], and a descendant of both Lucifer Morningstar and [[Lilith Elderblood]]. Through Lily, he is a half-Succubus and possesses one of the most prestigious Demonic bloodlines in Hell.[1]
Luka was raised within the old hierarchical culture of Hell and inherited much of the pride associated with his father and Ancient Demon ancestry. After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] Alucard conquered Hell and dismantled the old Pillar system, Luka repeatedly rejected the new order and became involved with remnants of the former Demonic aristocracy who intended to overthrow and kill the new Demon King.[3]
His mother had previously protected him from the consequences of an earlier act of rebellion and personally punished him in an attempt to force him to abandon his resistance. When Luka repeated the same mistake and joined another conspiracy against Victor, Lily refused to save him in the same manner. She exposed the conspiracy herself, crushed the rebels and publicly delivered Luka to Victor's judgment.[3][1][2]
Rather than execute him, Victor accepted Lily's proposal to exploit Luka's valuable bloodline and strength. Luka was sentenced to 10,000,000 years as the slave and puppet of his own mother, with Lily permitted to remove his free will through an ancient Demonic mind-control technique historically used by Lilith Elderblood.[2] No later chapter has established that the sentence was cancelled or that Luka regained his freedom.

### Physical Appearance
Luka strongly resembles a male version of his mother, [[Lily Elderblood]]. He has short, spiked black hair with red tips and a much more muscular physique than Lily.[1]
The narration directly identifies him as a half-Succubus, although his overall appearance and bloodline lean more heavily toward his father [[Bael]] than toward the [[Lucifer]] side of his family.[1]
By the time Lily publicly brought him before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] in Chapter 679, Luka had been subjected to extensive torture. He was covered in open wounds, severely beaten and was explicitly described as missing an arm. His condition was so poor that he could barely process the voices of the Demons surrounding him.[2]
It is unknown whether his missing arm was later restored. His mother had already demonstrated the ability to partially heal him enough to prevent his death, but the novel does not provide a later physical description confirming his complete recovery.[1]

### Personality
Luka is proud, arrogant, rebellious and heavily influenced by the old political culture of Hell. He was particularly attached to the idea that ancient bloodlines and established Demonic houses possessed an inherent right to power and therefore reacted poorly when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] dismantled the previous hierarchy and replaced it with a merit-based system.[3][1]
His pride is closely connected to his ancestry. Luka is descended from [[Lucifer]], Lilith and [[Bael]] and was accustomed to belonging to one of Hell's most politically important families. He initially regarded Victor as an illegitimate ruler precisely because Victor was not originally a Demon. During Lily's interrogation, Luka angrily referred to Victor as a half-breed and objected that he was not even a Demon, believing that this alone undermined his legitimacy as King of Hell.[1]
Luka was not completely unintelligent. Once Lily forced him to examine the political situation rationally, he understood why Victor's reign had become almost impossible to overthrow. He correctly recognized that Victor's meritocratic system had transferred opportunities away from a small number of Ancient Demons and given ordinary Demons the ability to improve their status through personal effort. As a result, countless Lesser Demons had become fanatically loyal to Victor because the new King had directly improved their lives.[1]
Luka himself summarized the danger of the system: power was no longer monopolized by the ancient Demonic houses, and every Demon empowered by Victor would become another loyal supporter of his regime. He further recognized that Victor's charisma had produced loyalty comparable to that historically inspired by Lucifer.[1]
Despite understanding the situation intellectually, Luka's pride repeatedly overrode his judgment. Lily had already warned and punished him for rebellious behavior once before Chapter 677, yet he later joined another group of former Pillar Demons conspiring to attack Victor and overthrow the new government.[3]
Luka also possesses considerable fear of his mother. When Lily personally disciplined him, he understood that fighting back was pointless and eventually resigned himself to whatever punishment she had planned because he knew he had no chance of defeating her.[1]
At the same time, he understood Lily well enough to know that she still cared for him in her own Demonic manner. Even while she brutally broke his body, Luka believed she would not actually allow him to die.[1] This assumption proved correct, although the fate Lily ultimately chose for him was something Luka considered substantially worse than death.
When Lily proposed transforming him into a mind-controlled puppet, Luka was horrified. He attempted to protest and internally concluded that he would prefer death ten thousand times over to continuing to exist without his own will.[2]

### Background
Luka was born from the marriage between [[Bael]], the First Duke and highest-ranked Pillar Demon, and Lily, daughter of [[Lucifer]] Morningstar and [[Lilith Elderblood]].
The marriage itself had originally been motivated by political power. Lily later explained to Luka that she had chosen Bael because becoming his wife gave her enormous influence throughout Hell.[1]
Consequently, Luka was born into one of the most politically and biologically prestigious lineages available to a Demon. His ancestry included:
- Lucifer Morningstar, the First Demon King and first Fallen Angel;
- Lilith Elderblood, Mother of Demons;
- Bael, the former First Duke of Hell;
- and [[Lily Elderblood]], a powerful Succubus descended directly from Lucifer and Lilith.
Despite this ancestry, Luka did not inherit every ability possessed by his mother's bloodline.
Most notably, he failed to inherit Lily's Dark Light.[1]
Dark Light was an unusual Demonic power carrying remnants of Lucifer's original Holy Light. It was particularly deadly toward Demons and had passed from Lucifer's lineage into Lily.
The narration explicitly states that this power did not pass into Luka because he resembled Bael's side of the family much more strongly than Lucifer's.[1]
This distinction became painfully relevant when Luka considered fighting his mother and immediately recognized that her Dark Light gave him effectively no chance against her.
Luka nevertheless inherited a valuable and powerful bloodline. Lily later emphasized to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] that although her son was useless politically, his descent from Lucifer, Lilith and Bael made his blood too valuable to casually waste, while also directly stating that Luka himself was strong.[2]

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] Alucard's conquest of Hell, the traditional Demonic hierarchy was rapidly dismantled. Victor abolished the old Pillar system, centralized authority beneath himself and reconstructed Demonic society around a meritocratic system in which strength, capability and useful service determined advancement rather than ancestry alone.[3]
The reforms were enormously popular among Lesser and ordinary Demons, many of whom had possessed almost no path toward political or supernatural advancement under the old system. Victor's ability to directly strengthen those who served him further transformed successful soldiers, administrators and specialists into fanatically loyal supporters of the new regime.[3][1]
Luka reacted very differently.
As the son of [[Bael]] and grandson of [[Lucifer]], he had been born near the top of the old hierarchy. The destruction of the Pillar system therefore represented not merely a change in government but the collapse of the privileged world into which Luka had been born.
Luka had already displayed rebellious tendencies against Victor's rule before his primary appearance. Lily later revealed that she had previously requested mercy for her son and personally punished him severely in the hope that he would learn from the experience and stop challenging the new King.[3]
The lesson failed.
Luka joined a faction composed primarily of former Pillar Demons who rejected Victor's reforms and began planning an attack intended to kill the new Demon King.[3]
Lily discovered the conspiracy before it could develop into a meaningful threat.
Rather than conceal her son's involvement, she personally approached Victor and informed him that Luka had joined the rebels. Victor initially questioned whether Lily intended to ask for leniency again, but Lily refused. She was furious that Luka had repeated the same mistake despite her previous intervention and requested permission to personally deal with the entire rebellion.[3]
Victor accepted.
He ordered Lily to handle the matter alone and make an example so severe that his previous punishments would appear childish by comparison. If she impressed him, he promised her practically any reward within his authority.[3]
Lily subsequently captured Luka.
Before taking him to the public judgment, she personally attempted to force him to understand why his rebellion was doomed. She kicked him in the stomach, punched him in the face, shattered his leg and repeatedly slammed or stomped him into the ground while demanding that he abandon his resistance.[1]
Even while being tortured, Luka initially remained angry.
He could not understand why his mother had accepted Victor as ruler and protested that Victor was not even a real Demon.[1]
Lily responded by explaining the political reality of Hell.
Victor had accomplished feats even Lucifer, Lilith and the strongest historical rulers of Hell had failed to achieve. He had damaged or destroyed structures thought impossible to break, conquered Hell alone, defeated a [[Demon God]] and completely reorganized the realm after [[Diablo]] abandoned it.[1]
More importantly, Victor possessed something the old rulers did not: the genuine support of the Demonic masses.
Lily forced Luka to explain the consequences himself. By replacing bloodline privilege with merit, Victor had created a civilization in which Lesser Demons could gain strength and status through service. Every Demon whose life improved because of the King's system consequently gained a personal reason to defend his reign.[1]
Luka realized the implication.
Even if the Ancient Demons could individually slaughter enormous numbers of Lesser Demons, they could not indefinitely fight billions of fanatically loyal supporters while simultaneously confronting Victor, his Generals, the oldest Demons in Hell and every new generation born into the King's system.[1]
Luka was especially shocked to learn that [[Zahal]] and Albu, the [[Elders]] of Hell, had personally recognized Victor as King. According to Lily, they had never displayed the same submission even toward Lucifer or Diablo.[1]
By the end of the confrontation, Luka understood that the rebellion was impossible.
Understanding did not save him.
Lily told him that he would help her correct the damage he had caused. Luka considered resisting but immediately abandoned the thought because the difference between them was overwhelming. Lily's Dark Light was particularly dangerous to Demons, while Luka had failed to inherit the ability himself.[1]
Lily only healed his injuries enough to ensure that he would survive what came next.
She subsequently crushed the remaining rebellion and brought hundreds of captured traitors before the Demon King's Castle for public judgment.[2]
Luka was among them.
The assembled Demons were shocked by his condition. Luka was severely tortured, covered in wounds and missing an arm, while Lily personally escorted the prisoners toward Victor's throne.[2]
The sight carried enormous political significance because Lily had previously been well known for protecting her son and repeatedly covering for the arrogant descendant of Lucifer. Seeing her personally deliver Luka for execution dramatically increased the perception of her loyalty toward Victor.[2]
Luka was initially expected to die with the other traitors.
Victor, however, deliberately left Lily room to propose another punishment.
Lily reminded him that Luka possessed the combined blood of Lucifer, Lilith and Bael. Whatever his personal failures, both his blood and his strength remained useful resources.[2]
She suggested transforming him into a puppet.
The method was an ancient technique used by Lilith during her civil war against Diablo. Enemy Demons—or even mindless beasts—could be captured and subjected to mind control, removing their free will and converting them into obedient war puppets.[2]
Luka was horrified.
He attempted to protest but was physically incapable of properly speaking. Internally, he concluded that he would rather die ten thousand times than exist as a puppet without any will of his own.[2]
Victor accepted Lily's proposal.
Before the assembled population of Hell, he formally declared:
Luka Bael would serve 10,000,000 years as the slave and puppet of Lily Bael.
[2]
The sentence simultaneously served several political purposes. Luka remained alive as a valuable Demonic resource, Lily publicly demonstrated absolute loyalty to Victor by sacrificing her own son, and Victor avoided appearing merciful toward a traitor while still preserving Luka's usefulness.[2]
Lily's actions elevated her reputation throughout the new Demonic government and earned her an additional personal reward from Victor.
Luka, meanwhile, lost the freedom he had attempted to preserve through rebellion.
His current individual activities after receiving the sentence have not been described in detail.

### Skills & Abilities
Luka is a powerful Demon descended from several of the most significant bloodlines in Hell. However, the novel provides very few direct combat feats because nearly all of his scenes occur while he is being defeated or restrained by his mother.

#### Demon Physiology
As a high-ranking Demon, Luka possesses the supernatural physiology characteristic of Hell's Demonic population.
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Luka possesses strength, speed, durability and stamina vastly beyond ordinary Humans.
- Enhanced Longevity: Demons possess extremely long supernatural lifespans.
- Demonic Physiology: Luka's body is naturally adapted to Hell and Demonic Energy.
- Supernatural Durability: Luka survived extensive physical torture from Lily, including broken bones, repeated blows to the skull and having his body slammed hard enough to create a crater.[1]
- High Pain Tolerance: Despite severe injuries, Luka remained conscious and capable of speaking through much of Lily's punishment.[1]
- Demonic Bloodline: Luka possesses the combined ancestry of [[Bael]] and Lily's [[Lucifer]]/Lilith bloodline.[1][2]
The novel does not individually demonstrate every generic Demon ability through Luka, so abilities such as Demonic Possession, specialized Miasma techniques or dependent forms of [[Immortality]] should not automatically be assigned solely from his Race.

#### Half-Succubus Physiology
Luka is explicitly described as a half-Succubus.[1]
His mother is a powerful Succubus, but Luka's appearance and supernatural inheritance lean more heavily toward [[Bael]].
No direct feat establishes that Luka personally possesses:
- Succubus Charm;
- Sexual Energy Absorption;
- Dream Manipulation;
- or other specialized Succubus abilities.
Those powers should therefore not be added without a direct demonstration.

#### Powerful Demonic Bloodline
Luka's greatest confirmed natural advantage is his lineage.
He descends from:
- [[Lucifer]] Morningstar;
- [[Lilith Elderblood]];
- [[Lily Elderblood]];
- and [[Bael]].
Lily explicitly argued that Luka should not be executed because his blood remained extremely useful and because he was personally strong.[2]
This gives him:
- Enhanced Demonic Potential
- High-Quality Bloodline
- Powerful Ancestry
The exact abilities inherited specifically from Bael have never been detailed.

#### Dark Light — Not Inherited
Luka notably did not inherit his mother's Dark Light.[1]
Dark Light originated from the combination of Lilith's Demonic nature and the remnants of Holy Light still existing within [[Lucifer]] after his fall. The result was a Demonic power containing attributes particularly dangerous to other Demons.
The ability passed to Lily but not Luka.
The narration directly explains that Luka took more strongly after [[Bael]] than Lucifer.[1]
Consequently, Luka should not be indexed with:
- Dark Light Manipulation;
- Holy Light;
- Light-Darkness Fusion;
- or Lily's anti-Demon Light abilities.

#### Intelligence and Political Awareness
Luka initially displays poor strategic judgment by repeatedly attempting rebellion against an overwhelmingly superior ruler.
However, once forced to seriously examine [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s reforms, he correctly identifies how the merit system generates self-sustaining political loyalty.[1]
He understands that ordinary Demons empowered through Victor's system would become permanent supporters because the King personally changed their social and supernatural prospects.
This demonstrates that Luka is capable of political analysis even though pride frequently interferes with his decisions.

#### Mind-Controlled Puppet (Current Condition)
Following Chapter 679, Luka was sentenced to become Lily's slave and puppet for ten million years.[2]
Lily's proposed method was an ancient technique inherited from [[Lilith Elderblood]] which used mind control to remove the free will of captured enemies and convert them into obedient war puppets.[2]
This condition grants Luka no additional independent Power.
Instead, it represents:
- Mind Control (Victim)
- Loss of Free [[Will Solace|Will]]
- Forced Obedience
- War-Puppet Conversion
The ability belongs to Lily/Lilith's technique, not Luka.

### Power
Luka is repeatedly treated as a naturally powerful Demon whose bloodline would be wasteful to discard.
Lily directly tells [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] that although Luka is personally useless as a political actor, his blood is useful and he is strong.[2]
His ancestry alone gives him enormous potential. He combines the bloodlines of [[Bael]]—the former First Duke of Hell—with Lily's descent from [[Lucifer]] and Lilith.
However, Luka is far weaker than his mother.
When Lily began punishing him in Chapter 678, Luka considered fighting back but immediately understood that resistance was pointless. Lily's superior physical ability and Dark Light made the difference between them overwhelming.[1]
Luka is also completely irrelevant as a threat to Victor by this stage. The entire purpose of Lily's lecture was to force him to recognize that rebellion against Victor would require overcoming:
- Victor himself;
- the Demon Generals;
- the [[Elders]] of Hell;
- the new government;
- and billions of Demons benefiting from Victor's rule.
Luka eventually acknowledged that the situation was impossible.[1]
No direct feat establishes him at:
- Duke level;
- Deadly Sin level;
- Combat God level;
- or any Divine tier.
His exact placement within the Demonic power hierarchy therefore remains unknown beyond being described as strong and possessing an exceptional bloodline.

### Relationships

#### Lily Elderblood
[[Lily Elderblood]] is Luka's mother and the most important relationship in his story.
Despite Lily's ruthless personality, she had historically been unusually protective of Luka. The Demons observing his public trial were shocked precisely because Lily normally protected her son and repeatedly covered for his arrogant behavior.[2]
Lily had already intervened once after Luka displayed rebellious behavior against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s rule. She requested mercy and personally punished him so severely that she expected he would never repeat the mistake.[3]
Luka nevertheless rebelled again.
This destroyed much of Lily's remaining patience.
She reported her own son to Victor, personally captured him and brutally beat him while attempting to make him understand why resistance against the new King was suicidal.[3][1]
Even then, Lily did not actually want Luka dead.
Luka himself understood this during the beating. Despite calling his mother insane, he knew she still cared about him enough to prevent his death.[1]
At the public trial, Lily deliberately proposed an alternative to execution: stripping Luka of his free will and turning him into her puppet.[2]
The solution saved his life while simultaneously subjecting him to a punishment Luka considered worse than death.
Victor subsequently sentenced Luka to remain Lily's slave and puppet for ten million years.[2]
Their relationship therefore combines genuine maternal attachment with the extreme cruelty expected from Hell's Ancient Demonic culture.

#### Bael
[[Bael]] was Luka's father and one of the most powerful Pillar Demons in Hell.
Luka appears to have inherited more from Bael's side of the family than from his maternal grandfather [[Lucifer]]. Chapter 678 explicitly states that Luka failed to inherit Lily's Dark Light because he was more Bael than Lucifer.[1]
Lily also blamed Luka's rebellious and foolish mentality partly on traits inherited from his father.[3]
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] seized Hell, Bael was absent alongside [[Diablo]] in the Human World and could not protect his son. Lily repeatedly emphasized this reality while forcing Luka to recognize that the political order supporting his father's position had already collapsed.[1]
Bael was subsequently killed during the final stages of the Demonic War with Lily herself assisting Victor's faction.
No direct scene shows Luka learning of his father's death or reacting to it.

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]], known in Hell at the time as Victor Alucard, became Luka's King, enemy and eventual stepfather.
Luka initially rejected Victor's authority because Victor was not originally a Demon and because his reforms destroyed the hereditary privileges possessed by families such as [[Bael]]'s.[1]
He eventually joined a rebel faction planning to attack and kill Victor.[3]
Victor regarded Luka's conspiracy as little more than another minor rebellion among old Demons unable to accept the new political system. Rather than personally handling it, he gave Lily complete authority to eliminate the conspirators and demonstrate her loyalty.[3]
At Luka's trial, Victor could have ordered his death but instead deliberately gave Lily an opportunity to present another solution. He then sentenced Luka to ten million years as Lily's slave and puppet.[2]
The judgment simultaneously preserved Luka as a useful resource and transformed his punishment into political propaganda strengthening both Victor and Lily's positions.
Victor later married Lily, making Luka one of his stepchildren.
There is no evidence that Victor developed a conventional father-son relationship with him.

#### Lucifer Morningstar
[[Lucifer]] Morningstar is Luka's maternal grandfather.
Luka's pride is strongly connected to being one of Lucifer's descendants. Lily herself repeatedly reminds him that he carries the blood of both Lucifer and Lilith and therefore expects him to behave with dignity even while suffering.[1]
However, Luka inherited less from Lucifer's supernatural side than his mother.
Most notably, he did not inherit the remnants of Lucifer's Holy Light which became Lily's Dark Light.[1]

#### Lilith Elderblood
[[Lilith Elderblood]] is Luka's maternal grandmother and one of the creators of the Demonic Race.
Luka inherits part of his valuable bloodline through Lilith.
Ironically, the punishment ultimately chosen for him was based upon an ancient technique created or historically used by Lilith herself. During the old civil war in Hell, Lilith captured hostile Demons and beasts and used mind control to transform them into obedient war puppets.[2]
Lily proposed applying the same technique to Luka.
