### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 251
Bael, also known as [[Baal]], was the First Key of Solomon, Rank 1 among the Demon Pillars and one of the most important members of [[Diablo]]'s faction. He served as Diablo's right hand and acted as one of the Demon King's principal commanders, administrators and political representatives. His position as Rank 1 placed him above the ordinary Pillars and granted him special authority over the Ars Goetia, including the ability to locate the other Pillars throughout [[Biblical Hell]].[1][2]
Bael was significantly more dangerous than an ordinary Duke of Hell. During the invasion of [[Nightingale]], he independently attacked the Royal Castle, killed six of Vlad's Royal Guards while they were using their Vampire Count transformations and defeated [[Adam Tepes]], [[Saul Tepes]], [[Lucas Tepes]], [[Elizabeth Tepes]] and [[Lilith Tepes]] despite suffering devastating injuries in the process. He ultimately escaped with Lilith because an external contract with one of the [[Regulators of Existence]] protected him from Vlad's retaliation, allowing Bael to withstand even an attack intended to erase him from existence.[3][4]
His loyalty to Diablo eventually placed him directly against [[Victor Elderblood]]. Bael participated in several plans intended to contain or kill Victor before the young Progenitor became impossible to control, but Victor's rise as the true King of Hell ultimately destroyed the political order Bael had spent millennia protecting. During the final stage of the Demonic War, Bael was betrayed by his own wife Lily and Victor's General Helena. After Helena introduced Victor's blood directly into his body, Victor remotely destroyed Bael's vessel, compressed his remains into blood and consumed him together with his Soul.[5]

### Physical Description
Bael normally appeared through a tall and unusually elegant Human vessel, possessing long golden hair tied into a ponytail that extended almost to the ground and bright green eyes. He generally wore a clean white suit, giving him an appearance dramatically different from the monstrous or deliberately intimidating forms commonly associated with high-ranking Demons. His refined presentation reflected his status as one of Hell's senior political figures rather than an ordinary battlefield Demon.
Like other ancient Demons, however, Bael's Human appearance should not be mistaken for his true biological form. High-ranking Demons frequently operated through possessed mortal vessels rather than exposing their real Demonic bodies outside Hell, and Bael could abandon or replace such bodies when necessary.
During the attack upon [[Nightingale]], the difference between Bael's elegant appearance and the violence required to stop him became particularly evident. Six Royal Guards using their Count transformations and several members of Vlad's Royal Family were required to seriously damage him. By the time Jeanne and Morgana reached the Royal Castle, Bael had lost his right hand, right leg and left eye, yet remained conscious, capable of using his powers and sufficiently dangerous to hold Lilith hostage while confronting both women.[3]

### Personality
Bael embodied many of the characteristics traditionally associated with Hell's ancient political elite: ruthless ambition, opportunism, caution and the willingness to treat relationships as transactions rather than sources of genuine affection. Unlike more impulsive Demons, however, Bael was rarely reckless without a strategic reason. He could recognize when an opponent exceeded his ability to defeat directly and preferred manipulation, preparation and political leverage over meaningless displays of pride.
His absolute loyalty to [[Diablo]] was one of his defining characteristics. Bael understood that Diablo's commands within his faction were effectively absolute and acted as the Demon King's principal intermediary when important matters required investigation. When Bael questioned [[Belial]] regarding the destruction of his territory, Belial immediately understood that refusing to cooperate with Bael effectively meant refusing an order directly from Diablo.[1]
Bael's loyalty did not make him unintelligent or blindly confident. His assessment of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] Alucard became increasingly cautious as Victor accumulated power. During the conspiracy organized to eliminate Victor, Bael openly agreed that confronting him directly was no longer practical. Victor had already survived a confrontation with [[Agares]], acquired increasingly powerful allies and continued becoming more dangerous every time his enemies failed to kill him. Bael therefore chose to prepare a trap and delegated its practical construction to James, demonstrating that he understood the danger of allowing personal pride to override strategy.[6]
He also took Diablo's assessments seriously even when they contradicted his own assumptions. After the [[Nightingale]] operation, Bael suggested exploiting the captured [[Lilith Tepes]]' heritage to produce powerful Demonic offspring. Diablo immediately rejected the idea because harming Vlad's daughter could provoke the Progenitor into abandoning every political restriction simply to destroy them. Although Bael was surprised that Diablo's opinion of Vlad had changed so dramatically, he accepted the order rather than risk acting independently.[4]
At the same time, Bael possessed an arrogant and sadistic streak. He casually threatened hostages, used injured members of the Royal Family as leverage against Jeanne and Morgana, and treated political violence as an ordinary tool. His initial target during the Nightingale invasion was Ophis, whose status as Vlad's youngest daughter would have made her an exceptionally valuable hostage, although Ophis successfully avoided capture.[3]
Bael's marriage to Lily illustrates his transactional worldview particularly clearly. Their marriage was never primarily based upon love. Lily later explained that Bael benefited politically from marrying the daughter of [[Lucifer]] Morningstar and [[Lilith Elderblood]], because possessing such a prestigious wife helped make his Rank 1 position effectively permanent. Lily, in return, gained access to Bael's influence and status and could use the marriage to discourage other unwanted political suitors.[5]
Despite this, Bael showed a moment of genuine surprise when Lily personally betrayed him. He questioned whether their marriage truly meant nothing to her, only for Lily to remind him that both of them had always known exactly what the relationship was. Rather than reacting with prolonged outrage, Bael ultimately laughed at the ruthlessness of her decision, recognizing it as the kind of behavior expected from a Demon.[5]

### Synopsis

#### Early History
Bael was one of the oldest and highest-ranked members of the Ars Goetia and eventually obtained the position of Rank 1 Pillar. His status gave him extraordinary political authority among the Dukes of Hell and earned him the informal position of King of the Pillars.[2]
At some point before the beginning of the series, [[Sitri]] attempted to challenge Bael's political position by invading his territory. Bael's Rank 1 Authority allowed him to automatically determine the positions of other Pillars throughout Hell, meaning Sitri's intrusion could not remain hidden from him. The conflict ultimately failed to remove Bael from his position.[2]
Bael also entered into a political marriage with Lily, daughter of [[Lucifer]] Morningstar and Lilith. The marriage strengthened both parties: Bael gained the prestige associated with possessing Lucifer and Lilith's daughter as his wife, reinforcing the permanence of his Rank 1 title, while Lily gained the influence of Hell's highest-ranked Pillar. Their son [[Luka Bael]] was born from the union.[5]
Bael later became one of [[Diablo]]'s most important subordinates and effectively acted as his right hand. His administrative importance meant that Bael frequently handled matters involving other Dukes when Diablo did not consider the situation worthy of personal intervention.

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Bael entered the main story more directly after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s activities began destabilizing relationships between several Demonic factions. Victor had previously destroyed [[Belial]]'s castle while disguising himself as [[Beelzebub]], deliberately creating suspicion and hostility between the two ancient Demons.[1]
[[Diablo]] ordered Bael to investigate the incident. Bael summoned Belial and Beelzebub to a formal meeting and demanded an explanation regarding what had occurred. His involvement immediately demonstrated his authority: Belial understood that although Bael was the one asking the questions, the command originated directly from Diablo, making refusal effectively equivalent to disobeying the Demon King himself.[1]
Bael's investigation gradually contributed to Hell recognizing that an unknown outside actor was deliberately destabilizing the existing Demonic order. Although Victor's exact role was not immediately understood, the incident helped establish him as an increasingly serious problem for Diablo's faction.

#### Demonic War
When [[Diablo]] began the Demonic War, Bael was one of the high-ranking Pillars deployed against [[Nightingale]]. Bael, [[Paimon]], [[Sitri]], Phoenix, [[Zagan]] and [[Dantalion]] were assigned different strategic targets intended to cripple Vampire intervention before the larger conflict between Demons, Angels and Humanity fully escalated.[7]
Bael, Paimon and Dantalion attacked the Royal Capital. Their operation had several objectives: pressure Morgana and the Royal Family, acquire politically useful hostages and prevent Vlad from freely committing Nightingale's power against Diablo's faction. Ophis represented the most valuable prospective hostage because she was the Vampire King's youngest daughter.[7][3]
While Paimon and Dantalion confronted Morgana, Jeanne and other defenders, Bael infiltrated Vlad's Royal Castle. His arrival forced the Royal Guards and Vlad's children into direct combat. Six Royal Guards entered their Vampire Count transformations and attacked him together with members of the Royal Family.[3]
The resistance badly wounded Bael but failed to stop him. When Jeanne and Morgana reached the castle, six transformed Royal Guards were already dead and [[Adam Tepes]], [[Saul Tepes]], [[Lucas Tepes]] and [[Elizabeth Tepes]] had been incapacitated. Bael stood among them missing his right hand, right leg and left eye while using [[Lilith Tepes]] as a hostage.[3]
Bael admitted that the Royal Guards had performed far better than ordinary opponents, while also mocking Vlad's children for failing to completely stop him. His original attempt to capture Ophis had failed because the young princess concealed herself and later used the opportunity created by Jeanne and Morgana to remove several of her siblings from danger.[3]
Jeanne attempted to attack Bael directly but discovered one of his most troublesome defensive abilities. While Bael remained in a defensive state, her physical attack passed completely through him. Morgana explained that ordinary physical attacks were effectively useless under these circumstances unless they possessed a Holy attribute or could directly damage Bael's Soul. Alternatively, an opponent could force Bael to attack and thereby temporarily lower the defense.[3]
Morgana's Succubus abilities were capable of bypassing the defense through less conventional means. She briefly inflicted an apparent burn upon Bael using a dream-based attack, although Bael quickly recognized that the damage originated from a Succubus illusion and dispelled it with Demonic power.[3]
Bael continued using the incapacitated Royal children and Lilith as human shields to prevent Jeanne and Morgana from freely attacking. When Vlad finally returned to the Capital, Bael prioritized completing the political objective rather than continuing an unnecessary fight and transferred Lilith through a Demonic portal.[3]
Vlad immediately attempted to erase Bael using his Blood Domain, but the attack inexplicably failed. A protective barrier appeared when Vlad tried physical force as well, preventing even the Vampire Progenitor from destroying him.[3]
Bael revealed that Diablo's faction had made a transaction involving one of the Five [[Regulators of Existence]]. The protection was so powerful that even Vlad acknowledged only another Being comparable to the Regulators could directly interfere with the authority sustaining it. When Vlad attacked again with enough power to erase everything in front of him from existence, Bael remained protected.[4]
Using the Regulator protection, Bael delivered Diablo's terms. Vlad was ordered not to actively intervene against the Demons during the developing war, and Bael presented a Demonic contract signed by Diablo guaranteeing that Lilith would remain physically unharmed and be returned after less than a year so long as the conditions were respected.[4]
Bael then returned to Diablo and reported the operation as a partial failure. He had failed to acquire Ophis or several other desired Royal hostages, but Lilith's capture remained sufficient for Diablo's political purpose. During the subsequent conversation, Bael suggested exploiting Lilith's Succubus heritage to produce powerful Demonic descendants. Diablo immediately prohibited any such action because harming Vlad's daughter could provoke the Progenitor into abandoning political restraint entirely and launching a suicidal campaign of revenge.[4]
Bael accepted the order. Diablo simultaneously instructed him to monitor [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] constantly and kill him only if an opportunity appeared where success was effectively certain. Diablo believed Victor possessed the qualities of a "Hero": if an attempted assassination failed, Victor would inevitably return stronger than before. Bael took the warning seriously rather than arrogantly dismissing it.[4]

#### Old Enemies
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s continuous growth eventually caused [[Diablo]] to authorize a dedicated operation specifically intended to eliminate him. Bael became one of the central organizers of this effort alongside [[Niklaus Horseman]], Fenir and James.[6]
By this point, the conspirators possessed considerably more information regarding Victor's history. They knew that he had originally been Human, had somehow become a Vampire Progenitor and had continued accumulating abilities and allies at a pace no normal supernatural being could reproduce. James in particular became obsessed with discovering what had allowed Victor's transformation to occur.[6]
Bael listed Diablo's order as one of the principal reasons Victor needed to be killed and arranged for comprehensive information concerning Alucard to be distributed to the conspirators. Even with additional allies, however, Bael rejected the idea of a simple frontal confrontation. Victor had fought [[Agares]] and survived, had become stronger since that encounter and now possessed ties to Vlad's wives and numerous other powerful figures.[6]
Bael therefore ordered that a trap be constructed instead and placed James, identified as the operation's second seat, in charge of preparing the practical assassination plan. His behavior during the meeting demonstrates that Bael's arrogance was tempered by significant strategic caution whenever he believed an opponent represented a genuine existential threat.[6]

#### King of the Pillars
While [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was conquering [[Biblical Hell]], Bael remained one of [[Diablo]]'s principal sources of information concerning the Ars Goetia. His Rank 1 position possessed an Authority unavailable to ordinary Pillars: the King of the Pillars could determine the locations of the other ranked Demons throughout Hell.[2]
The Authority was sufficiently secret that only Bael and Diablo were stated to know of its existence. Bael had previously used it to discover [[Sitri]] invading his territory.[2]
Victor's conquest exposed an important limitation. When [[Vine]] underwent her unusual evolution after submitting to Victor, Bael completely lost the ability to detect her position. Diablo reacted with disbelief because Vine should have been easy for the Rank 1 Authority to locate, but Bael explained that she had effectively disappeared from his senses following the transformation.[2]
This was one of the signs that Victor's influence was producing changes in Hell that the old Ars Goetia system could no longer reliably understand or control.

#### Final Demonic War
By the time [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] returned from Hell as its recognized King, [[Diablo]]'s political position had deteriorated catastrophically. Many former Pillars had submitted to Victor, the Horsemen had been defeated, Lilith was no longer under Diablo's secure control and entire Demonic Legions now regarded Victor as their sovereign.
Bael nevertheless remained with Diablo during the final war. He entered combat against Ariel, one of Heaven's highest-ranking Angels and an experienced commander of the Biblical forces.[8]
The overall war rapidly turned against Diablo. Victor's newly organized Demonic forces entered the conflict, former enemies changed sides, Yama abandoned Diablo once their political agreement became invalid and several of the Demon King's remaining elite subordinates were systematically isolated.
Bael's death occurred in the middle of this collapse. Lily and Helena, both Succubi and now loyal to Victor, suddenly attacked Bael together, piercing his body with their bare hands.[5]
Bael initially reacted with shock upon recognizing his wife among his attackers. Lily told him directly that he was simply no longer useful to her. When Bael questioned whether their marriage had meant anything, Lily rejected his attempt to frame himself as the betrayed husband and reminded him that neither of them had ever entered the marriage for love.[5]
Lily explained the original political arrangement: Bael acquired the daughter of Lilith and [[Lucifer]] as his wife, helping ensure the permanence of his Rank 1 position, while Lily acquired Bael's political influence and protection from other suitors. Victor's rise had made Bael's influence unnecessary, while service beneath the new King offered Lily a substantially greater future.[5]
Lily increased the output of her Dark Light while she and Helena kept Bael restrained. Helena then broke a vial containing Victor's blood directly against Bael's chest, introducing the Emperor's blood into his body.[5]
Victor did not need to personally approach. Once his blood existed inside Bael, he remotely manipulated it and collapsed the Demon Duke's body into a compact sphere of blood. Helena recovered the sphere and presented it to Victor together with the remains of other defeated Demons.[5]
Victor swallowed Bael's remains and consumed his Soul. Lily's participation in the assassination completed the final mission Victor had given her and earned her appointment as his [[Lily Elderblood|Fourth General]].[5]

#### After Death
Bael's death did not make the knowledge accumulated across his millennia of existence disappear. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ability to consume Souls allowed him to acquire information belonging to the beings he devoured.
Together with the memories and knowledge later obtained from [[Diablo]], Bael's accumulated information became useful when Victor began investigating the network of older conspiracies surrounding [[New Dawn]]. By Chapter 701, the memories obtained from those ancient Demons helped connect several previously isolated enemies and organizations that had operated against Victor and his family.[9]
Bael therefore indirectly contributed to the destruction of the conspiratorial network he had once helped support: after failing to eliminate Victor while alive, the knowledge contained within his consumed Soul became another resource used by Victor against his surviving enemies.

### Skills and Abilities
Bael was one of the strongest members of the old Ars Goetia and possessed significantly greater authority than an ordinary Demon Pillar. His position as [[Diablo]]'s right hand reflected both political reliability and combat ability. During the [[Nightingale]] invasion, Bael killed six Royal Guards while they were using their Vampire Count transformations and incapacitated several of Vlad's children before finally retreating, although the battle cost him his right hand, right leg and left eye.[3]
Demon Physiology: As an ancient high-ranking Demon, Bael possessed the enhanced physiology and supernatural characteristics of powerful members of his race. These included immense physical ability, Miasma, regeneration, extreme longevity, Demonic transformation and the unusual forms of survival possessed by beings native to Hell.
- Superhuman Strength: Bael possessed sufficient physical strength to fight transformed Royal Guards and multiple members of Vlad's family within the Royal Castle.[3]
- Superhuman Speed: Although Jeanne ultimately surpassed his perception during portions of their encounter, Bael possessed combat speed vastly beyond ordinary supernatural beings and was capable of reacting to high-ranking Vampires and Demons.[3]
- Superhuman Durability: Bael remained active after losing a hand, leg and eye while fighting the Nightingale Royal Guard and Vlad's children.[3]
- Enhanced Stamina: Severe mutilation did not immediately prevent him from continuing his mission, maintaining hostages or using his supernatural abilities.[3]
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Like high-ranking Demons, Bael possessed supernatural regeneration, although sufficiently powerful Holy or Soul-targeting damage could bypass the normal survival mechanisms of his race.
- Extreme Longevity: Bael had existed for millennia and belonged to the ancient political hierarchy of [[Biblical Hell]].
- Miasma Manipulation: As a high-ranking Demon, Bael could manipulate Miasma, the principal supernatural Energy used by Demons.
- Demonic [[Immortality]]: Demons possess an unusual form of dependent immortality tied to the nature of Hell and their Souls. Conventional physical destruction is often insufficient to permanently kill a powerful Demon unless the attack possesses Holy properties or directly damages the Soul.[5]
- Demonic Possession: Like other Demons, Bael could operate through deceased Human bodies by establishing a supernatural connection through the dead person's Soul.
- Soul Interaction: Bael could perceive and interact with Souls through the natural metaphysical abilities possessed by high-level Demons.
Density Shift / Defensive Intangibility: Bael possessed a specialized defensive state capable of making ordinary physical attacks pass through his body. When Jeanne attempted to seize him directly, her hand simply passed through him despite her overwhelming physical ability.[3]
- Physical Intangibility: Conventional physical attacks cannot properly strike Bael while the defensive state is active.[3]
- Holy Vulnerability: Holy-attributed attacks can bypass the defense and harm Bael directly.[3]
- Soul Damage Vulnerability: Attacks capable of directly damaging the Soul can likewise circumvent the protection.[3]
- Attack-State Limitation: Morgana explained that opponents unable to use Holy or Soul-based attacks could instead force Bael to attack, causing the defensive state to disappear temporarily and creating an opening.[3]
Rank 1 Authority — King of the Pillars: Bael's position as Rank 1 granted him a unique Authority over the Ars Goetia. As the King of the Pillars, he possessed supernatural awareness of the locations of the other ranked Demon Pillars throughout Hell.[2]
- Pillar Detection: Bael could determine where other Pillars were located even across the enormous territory of Biblical Hell.[2]
- Intrusion Detection: Bael had previously discovered [[Sitri]] invading his territory because the Rank 1 Authority exposed the other Pillar's location.[2]
- Secret Authority: The existence of this ability was known only to Bael and Diablo among the relevant characters.[2]
- Detection Limitation: The ability failed to locate [[Vine]] after her evolution under [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Bael described her as completely absent from his senses despite continuing to search Hell for her.[2]
Regulator Contract Protection: During the Nightingale operation, Bael possessed external protection obtained through a deal involving one of the [[Regulators of Existence]]. The exact Regulator responsible is not explicitly identified in Chapters 537–538, making the older page's specific attribution to the Judges of the Abyss uncertain.[3][4]
- Existence Erasure Protection: Vlad attempted to erase Bael through his Blood Domain, but the attack completely failed.[3]
- Absolute Defensive Barrier: When Vlad attempted direct physical destruction, a mysterious barrier appeared and prevented the attack from reaching Bael.[3]
- Resistance to Progenitor-Level Power: Vlad increased the power of his attack to the point that everything directly in front of him was erased from existence, yet the Regulator protection continued shielding Bael.[4]
- External Power: This protection was not shown to be an innate racial ability belonging to Bael. Bael explicitly described the result as something obtained through a transaction involving the Regulators.[4]
- Unknown Cost and Source: Neither the exact "currency" paid nor the specific Regulator responsible for granting the protection is revealed in these chapters.[4]
Demonic Contracts: As one of Diablo's highest-ranking representatives, Bael could carry and enforce high-level Demonic contracts. During the Nightingale invasion he presented Vlad with a contract personally signed by Diablo establishing terms for Lilith's captivity and Nightingale's non-intervention.[4]
Strategic Intelligence: Bael was not merely a combatant. As Diablo's right hand, he investigated internal problems, coordinated other Pillars and helped construct plans against dangerous supernatural targets. His decision to avoid fighting Victor directly and instead organize a specialized trap demonstrates strong threat assessment and strategic caution.[1][6]
Political Influence: Bael's Rank 1 position made him one of the most influential political figures in old Biblical Hell. His marriage to Lily further reinforced this standing because she was the daughter of [[Lucifer]] Morningstar and Lilith, two of the most historically important Beings associated with Hell.[5]
