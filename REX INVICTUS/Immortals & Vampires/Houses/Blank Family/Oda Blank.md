### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 63
Oda Blank is an ancient assassin, the First Master of [[Blank Clan]], one of the Clan's senior leaders, and a long-serving subordinate of the [[Snow Clan]]. Originally a Noble Vampire, Oda served as the personal butler and shadow operative of [[Agnes Elderblood Snow]], while simultaneously overseeing the training and deployment of the assassins belonging to the Snow Clan's intelligence network. He is a Ninja Master and is explicitly stated to have mastered infiltration, assassination, Kenjutsu, Ninjutsu and interrogation.[1]
Oda was one of the primary teachers responsible for developing [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]] into an assassin and later trained [[Adonis Snow]] in the "Art of being a Ninja", particularly teaching him to observe his surroundings and search the shadows for information other people overlook.[2][3] Much later, [[Victor Elderblood]] personally entrusted Oda with training his father, [[Leon Walker]], after Leon's transformation into a Vampire, demonstrating the extremely high regard placed upon Oda's experience as an instructor.[1]
Following Victor's rise, Oda and Clan Blank gradually developed from the Snow Clan's private assassination organization into part of the intelligence and internal-security apparatus surrounding Victor's faction and eventually the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Victor personally strengthened the Blanks with his Blood, allowing even ordinary Clan members to access forms and levels of Vampiric power that should normally have been inaccessible to them.[4] Following the establishment of the Empire, Clan Blank was further transformed into Dragonoids, becoming considerably more powerful than the Noble Vampires they had once been.[5]
By the Imperial era, Oda remained an active senior leader beneath Kaguya, participating in investigations, interrogations and counterintelligence operations against anyone attempting to compromise the Empire. Even after centuries of development and the rise of vastly more powerful beings around him, Oda continued to be considered exceptionally competent and retained considerable authority among the Blanks.[6]

### Physical Appearance
Oda is an East Asian-looking man with a stern and disciplined appearance. When he was formally introduced to [[Leon Walker]] in Chapter 619, he was described as an Oriental-looking man with a severe expression, immediately giving the impression of a professional rather than an ordinary servant.[1]
By the later Imperial era, Oda is described as an older man. Despite his outward age, the description does not reflect frailty; Kaguya explicitly considered him as competent as ever and dismissed his joking references to his "old back", since there was effectively nothing physically decrepit about him.[6]
Oda's original Vampire form has not been extensively described. During combat operations by the later stages of the story, however, he is capable of entering a Level 2 [[Vampire Count Form]], enveloping himself in the Shadow Power characteristic of Clan Blank.[4]
Following the evolution of Clan Blank, Oda became a Dragonoid. The exact physical alterations caused by his Dragonoid transformation have not yet been individually described, although members of Clan Blank were noted to possess appearances and powers reflecting the same general Shadow-aligned evolution associated with Kaguya.[5]

### Personality
Oda is disciplined, observant and highly professional, possessing the mentality expected from someone who has spent much of his existence working as an assassin, intelligence operative and servant of one of [[Nightingale]]'s major Noble houses. He rarely wastes words during missions and is accustomed to receiving orders from his superiors before immediately carrying them out. Even when communicating entirely through shadows, he maintains proper etiquette and addresses members of the Snow household according to their station.[7]
His professionalism does not mean he is emotionless. Oda possesses a considerably more casual side when he is away from an active operation and is capable of dry humor, personal indulgence and even mild shamelessness. This became particularly obvious when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] investigated the [[Snow Clan]]'s finances and discovered that Oda spent approximately 500 million dollars every year purchasing alcohol despite Vampires being incapable of normally consuming it. Oda defended the expenditure by claiming that every Japanese man needed Sake in his house and that the bottles were part of his collection and open bar. Victor confiscated the entire collection and ordered Kaguya to auction it in the Human World, leaving Oda visibly devastated.[8]
Oda takes his position as an instructor seriously. As leader of Clan Blank, he personally evaluates developing assassins and appears to maintain clear expectations regarding what members should be capable of at different ages. When Kaguya dramatically surpassed the normal development of the Clan, Oda immediately recognized the abnormality rather than dismissing it. He remembered her as an exceptional apprentice but had originally expected her to require until approximately five hundred years of age before awakening most of Clan Blank's mature abilities. Seeing her accomplish comparable feats centuries early therefore genuinely surprised him.[2]
He is similarly demanding toward people outside his Clan when entrusted with their training. Upon seeing Leon after Victor appointed Oda as his instructor, Oda was privately unimpressed that the Human father of the Vampire Progenitor appeared so unremarkable. He nevertheless kept the judgment to himself and accepted Victor's order professionally, taking responsibility for transforming Leon into someone capable of properly controlling his new supernatural abilities.[1]
Despite his stern nature, Oda is loyal to those above him and takes considerable pride in the organizations he serves. By the Imperial era he had accepted the role of one of the Emperor's "shadows", treating threats against Victor's family or Empire as matters requiring immediate investigation, capture, interrogation or elimination. His attitude while pursuing [[Agares]] demonstrated this clearly: once Victor personally took command of the operation, Oda immediately informed his subordinates that failure was unacceptable and ordered every operative to use their complete strength.[4]
Oda has also retained a somewhat playful warrior's desire for action despite his age. Thousands of years into the future, after reporting to Kaguya that another security matter had emerged, he casually expressed anticipation regarding Victor's response because he wanted an opportunity to "exercise" himself again. Kaguya considered the statement ridiculous because Oda remained as capable as ever despite presenting himself as an old man.[6]

### Synopsis

#### Vampire Society
Oda was first introduced indirectly during Violet Snow's training. While Hilda was supervising Violet, she noticed the shadow of a man concealed within her own shadow and addressed him as Oda. Oda answered without physically emerging, demonstrating the ability of Clan Blank members to communicate and operate while remaining merged with darkness.[7]
Hilda ordered Oda to locate Violet and subsequently contact Kaguya, whom Hilda wanted to return to the [[Snow Clan]]. Oda immediately raised the issue that Kaguya had already been dismissed from her service by Violet, demonstrating his familiarity with both the Clan's internal procedures and the changing arrangement surrounding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Hilda explained that Kaguya was still formally a member of Clan Blank and therefore needed to pass through the proper procedures before permanently ending her position. Oda questioned why Victor required servants from both Clan Snow and Clan Blank, but accepted Hilda's explanation and immediately carried out his mission.[7]
The scene established several characteristics that would later define Oda's role. He operated from the shadows, maintained awareness of the Snow Clan's internal personnel, questioned orders when clarification was necessary rather than mindlessly obeying, and served as part of the invisible intelligence structure supporting Agnes and her household.[7]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Oda played a considerably larger role during the crisis surrounding [[Adonis Snow]] and the traitors embedded throughout Clan Snow. After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] absorbed Adonis' soul and discovered the extent of the conspiracy operating within the Count Clans, he ordered Oda and Kaguya to mobilize Clan Blank and eliminate the traitors hidden throughout Snow territory.[2]
The resulting purge demonstrated the effectiveness of Oda's assassins. Clan Blank systematically eliminated members of the organization known as The Eye, which had spent years infiltrating the Snow and Fulger Clans while planning to replace the existing Countesses. By the time Victor met Oda and Kaguya in the city, the streets had effectively been painted with the blood of the conspirators. When Victor asked whether any of the targets had caused difficulty, Oda answered that every enemy had died before realizing what was happening.[2]
During the operation, Oda paid particular attention to Kaguya. As the leader responsible for training newly inducted assassins, he remembered Kaguya as one of his own apprentices and had always considered her exceptionally talented. However, he had expected her to require centuries before developing the mature techniques of Clan Blank. Watching her use abilities that should normally have belonged to an adult Vampire caused Oda to realize that her service to Victor had accelerated her development to an extraordinary extent.[2]
Following the purge, Oda continued participating in the restructuring of the [[Snow Clan]] while Victor temporarily handled its administration. Victor discovered extensive financial waste throughout the Clan, including Oda's annual expenditure of approximately five hundred million dollars on Sake. Oda attempted to defend his collection as decoration, only for Victor to confiscate the alcohol and order Kaguya to sell it at auction. Oda's visible despair amused the other Blanks and provided a rare glimpse of his personality outside assassination work.[8]
After the immediate political crisis was brought under control, Oda appointed Kaguya as the second leader of Clan Blank. The promotion was strategically significant: Kaguya already belonged primarily to Victor's personal household, but her new position allowed her to access additional confidential information and Clan techniques that could be used when supporting Victor.[9]

#### Demonic War
During the Demonic invasion of [[Nightingale]], Oda remained within Snow territory as one of its principal defenders. When the Capital came under direct attack, Violet gathered with Hilda, Natalia and Oda to assess the situation. At this point the narration directly identifies Oda as a Master Assassin, confirming that his status went considerably beyond simply leading Clan Blank administratively.[10]
His presence alongside Hilda during the invasion demonstrated the position he occupied in Clan Snow's defensive command structure. While most of the ancient Vampires had concentrated around the Capital, Oda remained one of the senior combat and intelligence specialists available to Violet in Snow territory.[10]

#### King Among Kings
Oda's true level of martial specialization was explained when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] selected instructors for his newly transformed parents. Victor summoned Hilda and Oda from the shadows and formally introduced Oda as the First Master of Clan Blank and a Ninja Master. Victor specifically stated that Oda specialized in infiltration, assassination, Kenjutsu, Ninjutsu and interrogation, describing him as a Master in every one of these fields.[1]
Victor assigned Oda to personally train [[Leon Walker]]. The decision placed Oda beside Hilda, who was assigned to train Anna, and demonstrated that Victor considered the two among the best instructors available for turning inexperienced Vampires into competent members of supernatural society. Oda privately regarded Leon as surprisingly unimpressive for the Human father of a Progenitor but did not openly disrespect him and simply accepted the responsibility.[1]
Oda's abilities had previously been used to train [[Adonis Snow]] as well. When Victor later drew upon Adonis' memories, he remembered that Oda had been responsible for teaching Adonis the Art of being a Ninja. One of Oda's most important lessons was to constantly observe one's environment—particularly the shadows—and search for information hidden outside obvious lines of sight. Victor explicitly retained those lessons after absorbing Adonis, demonstrating that Oda's instruction remained valuable even to Victor himself.[3]
Oda eventually considered Leon sufficiently competent to take part in real intelligence operations. When Victor's forces discovered another enemy base, Clan Blank and allied Demons were placed on standby and Leon was included in the operation. Those around Victor understood the significance: because Oda was effectively Leon's Master, Leon's inclusion meant that Oda believed his student had reached a level where he could contribute rather than becoming a liability.[11]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
By the time [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s conflict with his remaining enemies intensified, Oda had become one of the commanders used to hunt major supernatural targets. Clan Blank eventually located a base being used by Radu Tepes, formerly known as [[Niklaus Horseman]]. Oda and a squad of Blank assassins attacked the location, forcing Radu to abandon his position and flee.[12]
Oda pursued him closely enough that Radu was eventually forced to reveal information he had hidden for centuries. Before escaping, he declared his true identity as Radu Tepes, brother of [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]], and told Oda to carry his declaration of revenge to Vlad. Although Oda did not successfully capture Radu during this confrontation, forcing the ancient Vampire into retreat and becoming the recipient of his revelation demonstrated the level of trust placed in Oda for operations against major enemies.[12]
Oda's combat development became far more apparent during the pursuit of [[Agares]]. When Agares infiltrated Samar while [[Anna Elderblood]] and [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] were present, Oda immediately activated a defensive protocol created under Victor's instructions. Clan Blank assassins and Shadow Demons emerged throughout the area, and Oda deployed a device that isolated the room while simultaneously turning Agares' successful escape into a means of tracking him.[4]
Oda divided his forces with military precision. Squads Two and Three remained behind to protect Anna and Maya, while Squads One, Four and Five followed him in pursuit. The sheer number of shadows involved revealed the enormous scale of Victor's security network, with Oda functioning as one of the commanders capable of coordinating it.[4]
Victor contacted Oda personally during the chase and ordered Agares to be captured alive because his dimensional abilities could prove useful. Oda immediately accepted the order. After Victor informed him that [[Zaladrac Zeovnur Elderblood]] would reinforce the operation, Oda warned his subordinates that the Emperor was personally observing the mission and that failure was therefore unacceptable.[4]
Oda then ordered every operative to stop holding back. The Shadow Demons released their full Miasma, while the members of Clan Blank activated their Vampire Count transformations. Oda himself entered a Level 2 [[Vampire Count Form]]. Agares was shocked that every Blank pursuing him appeared capable of accessing Count-level transformations, something explained by Victor having personally given his Blood to the Clan in order to strengthen them.[4]
Although Oda and the Blanks were not ultimately the beings who physically overpowered Agares, their pursuit prevented him from disappearing, forced him to continuously expend his powers and guided the operation until Zaladrac and Nyx Elderblood completely sealed his escape routes.[4]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
The development of Clan Blank continued after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became God Emperor. By Chapter 986, the Blanks were explicitly no longer ordinary Noble Vampires. They had evolved into Dragonoids, beings described as second only to [[True Dragons]] in potential. Under Kaguya, the transformed Blanks acted as the Emperor's shadows and were deployed against Gods who attempted to claim credit for Victor's achievements.[5]
Oda remained an active part of the organization throughout the Imperial period. By Chapter 1065, Kaguya visited one of the Empire's internal-security facilities and found Oda supervising the interrogation and "rehabilitation" of suspected traitors. Oda was described as an older man and one of the leaders of Clan Blank, possessing enough authority to use his true name directly before Kaguya while most lower operatives instead operated under codenames.[6]
Oda and another operative, Mifune, reported that an Imperial citizen had exposed individuals attempting to sell information about the Empire to Gods. Oda participated directly in the investigation and discussed wider political developments with Kaguya after the formal report was completed. Despite the enormous amount of time that had passed, Kaguya considered Oda as competent as ever, while Oda joked that he hoped Victor's next move would allow him to exercise his supposedly aging body.[6]
By this period Clan Blank had become considerably more than the private assassination family originally serving Clan Snow. Its members functioned as the shadows, eyes and ears of the Emperor, investigating treason, protecting sensitive information and suppressing internal threats throughout the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Oda remained one of the senior figures responsible for maintaining that system.[6]

### Skills and Abilities
Oda is one of the foremost assassination specialists produced by [[Nightingale]]. Even before being empowered by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], he was explicitly recognized as the First Master of Clan Blank, a Ninja Master and Master Assassin. His combat style emphasizes stealth, information, precision and ending confrontations before an opponent can properly respond rather than relying exclusively upon direct displays of raw power.[1]
After receiving Victor's Blood, Oda's Vampiric abilities dramatically improved, eventually allowing him to access a Level 2 [[Vampire Count Form]]. Clan Blank was subsequently transformed into a Dragonoid organization, greatly increasing the potential and fundamental nature of its members.[4][5]
- Dragonoid Physiology: During the Imperial era, the Blanks were explicitly stated to have ceased being ordinary Noble Vampires and become Dragonoids, beings second only to [[True Dragons]] in potential.[5] The precise individual characteristics of Oda's Dragonoid evolution have not yet been shown in detail, so abilities unique to his personal Dragonoid form remain unknown.
- Noble Vampire Physiology: Oda was originally a Noble Vampire and possessed the standard supernatural traits of his race, including enhanced strength, speed, durability, senses, longevity, regeneration and [[Vampiric Energy]]. His long career and Master-level training allowed him to apply those abilities far more efficiently than inexperienced Vampires.
- Victor's Blood Empowerment: Victor personally gave his Blood to members of Clan Blank in order to strengthen them. The result was a tremendous increase in the Clan's overall combat potential, with [[Agares]] becoming shocked that all of the Blanks pursuing him could access Count-level transformations.[4]
- Vampire Count Form: Oda can use the advanced transformation normally associated with extremely powerful Vampires. During the pursuit of Agares, he activated a Level 2 Vampire Count Form while surrounded by Shadow Power.[4]
- Enhanced Physical Attributes: Victor's Blood dramatically increased the physical power of Clan Blank's members beyond what was expected from ordinary Noble Vampires.[4]
- Enhanced Vampiric Potential: The empowerment allowed Clan Blank members to reach forms and levels of Vampiric power that would ordinarily have been far more difficult or impossible for them to access.[4]
- Shadow Manipulation: The hereditary ability of Clan Blank allows its members to manipulate shadows and use darkness as the foundation of their fighting and infiltration methods.[7][2]
- Shadow Melding: Oda can merge his body into shadows, allowing him to remain concealed while observing his surroundings. His debut showed him hidden directly within Hilda's shadow while communicating with her without emerging physically.[7]
- Shadow Travel: Clan Blank operatives can enter and move through shadows to rapidly relocate. Oda repeatedly uses the ground and surrounding darkness as though they were an alternative medium of movement.[7][4]
- Shadow Concealment: Oda can suppress his physical presence while remaining inside shadows, making his abilities extremely well suited to surveillance and assassination.[7]
- Shadow Communication: While concealed within a shadow, Oda was capable of communicating directly with Hilda, with his voice being heard despite his body remaining hidden.[7]
- Shadow Empowerment: Oda can envelop himself in Shadow Power while transforming and fighting at higher levels.[4]
- Master Assassin: Oda is explicitly called a Master Assassin. His expertise encompasses the complete process of locating, infiltrating, interrogating and eliminating targets rather than simply fighting them openly.[10][1]
- Assassination Arts: Oda is one of Clan Blank's foremost specialists in silently killing targets. Under his leadership the Clan massacred the [[Snow Clan]] traitors so efficiently that most died before realizing they were under attack.[2]
- Silent Killing: Oda's training philosophy centers around eliminating a target before conventional battle becomes necessary. This principle is reflected throughout Clan Blank's operations.[2]
- Target Elimination: Oda can coordinate multiple assassins across an entire territory to eliminate dispersed targets simultaneously, as shown during the purge of The Eye.[2]
- Ninjutsu Mastery: Victor explicitly describes Oda as a Master of Ninjutsu and as a Ninja Master. His techniques incorporate stealth, misdirection, environmental awareness and the specialized supernatural methods of Clan Blank.[1]
- Art of the Ninja: Oda personally taught this discipline to Adonis. One of its principles is to continuously inspect areas that conventional observers neglect—especially the shadows—in order to identify hidden information, enemies and opportunities.[3]
- Environmental Awareness: Oda teaches his students to search beyond the obvious and pay attention to details hidden around them. These lessons remained valuable enough that Victor continued applying them after inheriting Adonis' memories.[3]
- Stealth: Oda possesses advanced training in remaining undetected while approaching, observing and infiltrating enemy-controlled environments.[1]
- Infiltration: Oda is explicitly a Master of infiltration, making the penetration of defended or restricted locations one of his primary fields of expertise.[1]
- Kenjutsu Mastery: Oda is explicitly stated to be a Master of Kenjutsu. Although his individual sword style has not been extensively displayed on-screen, swordsmanship is one of the disciplines Victor lists when explaining Oda's qualifications.[1]
- Interrogation Mastery: Oda is a Master interrogator and later remains directly involved with the Empire's interrogation and rehabilitation operations. His organization extracts intelligence from traitors, spies and compromised citizens before deciding their fate.[1][6]
- Espionage: Clan Blank acts as the intelligence and espionage arm of the Snow Clan and later the [[Elderblood Empire]]. As its First Master and one of its leaders, Oda possesses extensive experience gathering information, tracking political enemies and identifying hidden threats.[7][6]
- Counterintelligence: During the Imperial era, Oda supervised investigations into citizens secretly communicating with foreign Gods and attempting to sell information concerning the Elderblood Empire. His responsibilities include identifying leaks before they can become serious security breaches.[6]
- Intelligence Analysis: Oda can interpret field reports and determine how threats should be handled rather than functioning solely as an executioner. He regularly acts as one of the senior people through whom Clan Blank's information reaches Kaguya and higher authorities.[6]
- Tracking: Oda possesses considerable expertise in pursuing escaping targets. During the Agares operation, he coordinated the use of Victor's tracking technology and maintained pursuit even after Agares moved through alternate dimensions.[4]
- Military Command: Oda is capable of commanding large groups of assassins and allied supernatural troops. During the Agares incident he divided the available forces into five squads, assigned defensive and pursuit roles and later coordinated both Clan Blank members and Shadow Demons.[4]
- Tactical Coordination: Oda rapidly adjusts his plans according to new orders. When Victor changed the objective from merely stopping Agares to capturing him alive, Oda reorganized the operation and synchronized his forces with the reinforcements Victor sent.[4]
- Security Protocols: Oda is familiar with defensive procedures designed personally by Victor. When Agares infiltrated Samar, he activated the appropriate protocol almost immediately and used specialized equipment to turn Agares' escape attempt into a method of tracing him.[4]
- Leadership: Oda historically led Clan Blank and remains one of its highest-ranking figures even after Kaguya became the principal leader during the Elderblood Empire era. He trains assassins, allocates missions, evaluates their development and commands field operations.[2][6]
- Teaching: Oda is an accomplished instructor whose students include [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]], [[Adonis Snow]] and [[Leon Walker]]. His ability to train individuals with radically different levels of experience demonstrates that his expertise extends beyond personal combat into transmitting his knowledge to others.[2][1][3]
- Assassin Evaluation: Oda possesses enough familiarity with the development of Clan Blank's bloodline and techniques to judge the expected progression of younger assassins. He immediately recognized that Kaguya had reached the capabilities of an adult Vampire centuries earlier than expected.[2]
- Pain Tolerance and Combat Experience: Oda has operated as an assassin for an extensive period and remains comfortable participating personally in dangerous missions involving enemies vastly beyond ordinary Vampires. Even in the Imperial era he continued requesting opportunities to fight rather than retiring from active service.[4][6]
- Sunlight Immunity: Clan Blank descends partially from the Snow bloodline. Although the branch did not inherit the Snow Clan's [[Pyrokinesis]], its members inherited the unusual ability to endure sunlight without suffering the weakness affecting most Vampires.[13]
- Fire Resistance: The same Snow ancestry grants Clan Blank unusually high resistance to fire. This trait originated when the descendants created through the union between Snow and Japanese Vampire blood failed to inherit flame manipulation but retained the Snow lineage's defensive characteristics.[13]

### Relationships

#### Agnes Elderblood Snow
Oda has traditionally served Agnes as her personal butler and one of the principal agents through which the [[Snow Clan]] gathers information. His position as head of Clan Blank made him one of the invisible figures responsible for maintaining Agnes' territory, identifying threats and carrying out actions the Countess could not personally handle.
Unlike an ordinary domestic servant, Oda's butler role encompasses intelligence gathering, assassination and security. He frequently operates outside Agnes' immediate presence while still serving the broader interests of her Clan.

#### Kaguya Elderblood Blank
Kaguya was originally one of Oda's apprentices. As the leader responsible for training Clan Blank's younger assassins, Oda recognized her exceptional potential long before she reached adulthood. He nevertheless expected her powers to develop according to the normal progression of their Clan and was genuinely shocked when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s influence allowed her to achieve adult-level capabilities centuries early.[2]
Following the purge of the [[Snow Clan]] traitors, Oda promoted Kaguya to the position of second leader so she could access sensitive Clan information and advanced techniques while continuing to serve Victor.[9]
Their hierarchy eventually changed substantially. By the [[Elderblood Empire]] era, Kaguya had become the central authority of the Blanks while Oda functioned as one of the Clan's senior leaders whenever she was occupied elsewhere. Oda reports directly to her during Imperial intelligence operations, although their conversations remain considerably more relaxed than those between Kaguya and lower-ranking operatives. Kaguya continues to regard her old teacher as exceptionally competent.[6]

#### Victor Elderblood
Oda's relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] evolved from curiosity toward his mistress' son-in-law into direct service beneath Victor's expanding faction. His first known discussion concerning Victor occurred when he questioned Hilda about why the newly transformed Vampire required servants from both Clan Snow and Clan Blank.[7]
After Victor assumed temporary control of Snow territory, Oda followed his commands during the purge of The Eye and allowed Victor to reorganize the territory despite the latter ruthlessly investigating Clan expenditures—including Oda's own enormous Sake collection.[2][8]
Victor eventually placed considerable trust in Oda's abilities. He personally selected him to train Leon, strengthened the entire [[Blank Clan]] with his Blood and later entrusted Oda with command responsibilities protecting members of his family. During the [[Agares]] operation, Victor contacted Oda directly and issued detailed instructions concerning the target's capture.[1][4]
By the Imperial era, Oda and the Blanks explicitly regarded themselves as the Emperor's shadows, eyes and ears, maintaining internal security and acting against threats that Victor did not need to address personally.[6]

#### Leon Walker
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] appointed Oda as Leon's personal trainer following Leon's transformation into a Vampire. Oda initially appeared unimpressed by Victor's father but accepted responsibility for correcting his weaknesses and teaching him the skills needed to control his supernatural existence.[1]
Leon eventually developed sufficiently that Oda allowed him to participate in operations connected to Victor's enemies. His inclusion was treated as evidence that Oda believed Leon had become useful enough to contribute to a genuine mission rather than requiring continued protection.[11]

#### Adonis Snow
Oda was one of Adonis' Masters and personally instructed him in the Art of being a Ninja. His lessons focused not merely on physical techniques but on awareness, observation and searching for information where enemies would not expect someone to look.[3]
These teachings indirectly survived Adonis' death. After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] absorbed Adonis and inherited his memories and experiences, he retained the lessons Oda had taught him and consciously used them himself.[3]
