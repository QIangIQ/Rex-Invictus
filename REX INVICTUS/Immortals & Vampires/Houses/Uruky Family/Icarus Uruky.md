### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 737
Icarus Uruky is an Alpha Werewolf of the [[Uruky Clan]] and a grandson of its Patriarch, [[Leônidas Uruky]]. He was introduced alongside [[Zaion Uruky]] shortly before [[Victor Elderblood]] confronted the Clan over Zaion's intentions toward [[Natalia Elderblood née Alioth]].[1]
Compared with Zaion, [[Icarus]] is considerably more cautious and politically aware. He was the first of the younger Uruky [[Werewolves]] to recognize how dangerous the situation involving Natalia could become and attempted to prevent Zaion from provoking a conflict with an opponent far beyond their Clan's ability to handle.[1]

### Personality
[[Icarus]] is intelligent, cautious and more politically aware than Zaion. When Zaion claimed that he had seen a member of [[Clan Alioth]] on Samar, Icarus immediately rejected the idea because the Clan was directly protected by [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]]. Once Zaion revealed that Anderson had also been accompanying the woman, Icarus became considerably more serious and demanded a complete description of everyone present.[1]
After hearing Zaion's description, Icarus concluded that the mysterious man accompanying the group might have been Vlad using shapeshifting and immediately recognized that attempting to abduct an Alioth could cause an international conflict.[1]
Although relatively intelligent, Icarus does not always remain composed under overwhelming pressure. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] threatened Clan Uruky, Icarus attempted to argue that destroying the family would create an international incident. Leônidas internally considered the comment foolish under the circumstances, despite otherwise regarding Icarus as intelligent, because Victor had already demonstrated that conventional political threats were unlikely to restrain him.[1]
Icarus is also practical and strongly concerned with his own survival. After escaping Victor's confrontation and hearing Zaion being tortured, he felt sympathy for his relative but privately acknowledged that Zaion had caused his own downfall. He was relieved when Leônidas refused to retaliate against Victor.[2]
He remains loyal to his Patriarch. Once Leônidas ordered Clan Uruky to abandon its mansion, Icarus immediately recovered from his shock and helped enforce the Patriarch's commands among the surrounding [[Werewolves]].[2]

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
[[Icarus]] debuted in Chapter 737 while arguing with [[Zaion Uruky]] inside the Uruky residence. Zaion insisted that he had seen a woman from [[Clan Alioth]] accompanying Anderson's group, while Icarus initially dismissed the claim because members of Clan Alioth were normally protected directly by the Vampire King.[1]
Zaion eventually declared that he intended to capture the woman and deliver her to the First Prince. When he mentioned that Anderson had personally been travelling with her, Icarus immediately stopped him and demanded a complete description of the group.[1]
Zaion described Anderson, several of his subordinates, a member of [[Lykos Clan]] and a tall, exceptionally handsome man whom Zaion had incorrectly assumed was weak. Icarus' expression darkened as he heard the details. Knowing that Vlad was an experienced shapeshifter and closely connected to Clan Alioth, Icarus concluded that the unidentified man was probably the Vampire King.[1]
He angrily accused Zaion of risking a war and decided that the incident needed to be reported immediately to their Patriarch.
Unknown to both younger [[Werewolves]], [[Leônidas Uruky]] had already overheard their conversation. He entered the room and condemned Zaion's actions, agreeing that even the possibility of insulting Clan Alioth could become a serious diplomatic incident.[1]
Before the Clan could resolve the matter internally, [[Victor Elderblood]] arrived.
At nearly the same moment, [[Volk Fenrir]] announced throughout Eclipse Ventus that the King of Hell, Victor Alucard, was visiting Samar as Anderson's protected guest and ordered the Werewolves not to antagonize him. The announcement immediately revealed that Icarus' earlier theory had been incorrect in one important respect: the powerful stranger with Natalia had not been Vlad, but Victor himself.[1]
Victor isolated the room from the rest of the Uruky residence and confronted Leônidas over Zaion's intentions toward Natalia. When Icarus attempted to warn that attacking Clan Uruky would create an international incident, Victor dismissed the argument and explained that sufficiently powerful individuals were not meaningfully restrained by ordinary political consequences.[1]
Victor revealed that he had perceived Zaion's intentions clearly: Zaion wanted Natalia for himself and intended to exploit her Clan Alioth abilities. Icarus and Leônidas looked toward Zaion, and his refusal to meet their eyes confirmed that Victor's accusation was accurate.[1]
Leônidas ultimately chose to surrender Zaion rather than risk the destruction of the entire Clan. After securing a contract intended to protect the rest of the family, the Patriarch physically dragged Icarus away from the room and left Zaion behind with Victor.[1]
In Chapter 738, Zaion's screams began echoing throughout the mansion as Victor punished him. The surrounding members of Clan Uruky were initially frozen by the situation until Leônidas ordered everyone to abandon the residence. Icarus quickly regained his composure and reinforced the command:
"...Didn't you listen to the Patriarch!? Back to work!"[2]
Icarus then accompanied Leônidas while the Clan prepared to relocate to its Eastern Mansion in the capital. He cautiously asked whether Clan Uruky intended to retaliate against Victor. Leônidas immediately rejected the idea and questioned whether Icarus was foolish enough to seriously consider fighting someone of Victor's power.[2]
Internally, Icarus was relieved.
Although he felt sorry for Zaion, he recognized that Zaion's fate resulted from his own actions and had no desire to involve himself in a suicidal conflict with Victor.[2]
Icarus subsequently asked whether they should contact the First Prince, whose faction Clan Uruky had supported. After considering the situation, Leônidas announced that the Clan would withdraw its support from the First Prince entirely. He also decided to personally resume active leadership of Clan Uruky and ordered the current Clan Leader to step down.[2]
Icarus was visibly shocked by the scale of these changes but immediately submitted when Leônidas made clear that he would personally retake command and challenge anyone who disputed his authority.[2]
Icarus has not received another substantial individual role in the currently documented material.

### Skills & Abilities
[[Icarus]] has not participated in a direct battle or demonstrated a unique supernatural technique. His confirmed capabilities primarily derive from his Alpha Werewolf physiology.

#### Alpha Werewolf Physiology
- Alpha Werewolf Physiology: [[Icarus]] belongs to the Alpha class of [[Werewolves]] and possesses the enhanced supernatural characteristics associated with that status.
- Superhuman Strength: Alpha Werewolves possess physical strength considerably beyond ordinary Humans and lower-ranking Werewolves.
- Superhuman Speed: Icarus possesses supernatural movement speed and reflexes.
- Superhuman Durability: His Werewolf body possesses greater physical resilience than ordinary beings.
- Enhanced Stamina: Alpha Werewolves possess significantly enhanced endurance.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Icarus possesses the accelerated healing characteristic of Werewolves.
- Enhanced Senses: His Race grants highly developed smell, hearing and predatory perception.
- Longevity: Werewolves possess substantially greater lifespans than ordinary Humans.
- Werewolf Transformation: Icarus possesses the racial capacity to manifest more pronounced Werewolf characteristics, although his individual transformation has not been shown.
- Natural Weapons: Werewolf physiology provides enhanced claws and fangs.
- Alpha Presence: Icarus can exert considerable pressure upon other Werewolves. When he seriously questioned Zaion, the pressure emanating from him was sufficient to make Zaion respond fearfully.[1]
- Predatory Instincts: As an Alpha Werewolf, Icarus possesses instinctive sensitivity toward threats, hierarchy and supernatural dominance.

#### Intelligence and Political Awareness
- Threat Assessment: [[Icarus]] rapidly recognized that a member of [[Clan Alioth]] travelling with Anderson indicated a situation far more dangerous than Zaion initially understood.[1]
- Political Knowledge: He knew about Clan Alioth's relationship with the Vampire King and understood that attacking one of its members could potentially trigger a conflict.[1]
- Analytical Reasoning: Using Zaion's description and his knowledge of Vlad's shapeshifting, Icarus reasonably concluded that the unidentified powerful man could have been the Vampire King.[1]
- Pragmatism: After experiencing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s presence firsthand, Icarus accepted that retaliation would be suicidal and supported Leônidas' decision to avoid further confrontation.[2]

### Relationships

#### Leônidas Uruky
[[Leônidas Uruky]] is [[Icarus]]' grandfather and Patriarch.
Icarus respects Leônidas' authority and immediately follows his commands. After Zaion was surrendered to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Icarus assisted the Patriarch in organizing the evacuation of the Uruky mansion and accepted his decision to withdraw the Clan from the First Prince's faction.[2]
Leônidas appears to regard Icarus as more intelligent than Zaion, although he becomes frustrated when Icarus speaks recklessly during situations where remaining silent would be safer.[1]

#### Zaion Uruky
[[Zaion Uruky]] was another grandson of Leônidas and a close relative of [[Icarus]].
The exact biological relationship between Icarus and Zaion has not been explicitly established. The novel calls both men Leônidas' grandsons but does not directly state that they are brothers.[1]
Icarus repeatedly attempted to stop Zaion from escalating the situation involving Natalia. After Zaion's death became inevitable, Icarus felt some sympathy for him but ultimately concluded that Zaion had caused his own downfall.[2]

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Icarus]] first encountered [[Victor Elderblood]] during Victor's confrontation with Clan Uruky.
Although Icarus initially attempted to invoke the possibility of an international incident, Victor's overwhelming position quickly convinced him that ordinary political protections would not save the Clan.[1]
After escaping the confrontation, Icarus had no desire to retaliate and was relieved when Leônidas reached the same conclusion.[2]
