Zaion Uruky was an Alpha Werewolf of the [[Uruky Clan]] and a grandson of its Patriarch, [[Leônidas Uruky]]. He belonged to the bloodline of Leônidas' eldest son and was one of the younger Alpha descendants being raised within the powerful Uruky family.[1]
Zaion's role in the story was brief. Shortly after [[Victor Elderblood]] arrived on Samar, Zaion noticed [[Natalia Elderblood née Alioth]] and developed the intention of capturing her for himself before delivering her to the First Prince. Unknown to him, Natalia was Victor's wife. Victor perceived Zaion's intentions and personally entered the Uruky residence to punish him.[2][1]
After Leônidas learned exactly what Zaion had intended, he chose the survival of the entire Uruky Clan over protecting his grandson and surrendered Zaion to Victor. Victor subsequently subjected him to prolonged torture before killing him.[1][3]

### Physical Appearance
Zaion was a tall man with olive-colored skin and short black hair.[2]
No exact height, eye color, clothing description or complete Werewolf transformation was provided before his death.

### Personality
Zaion was arrogant, impulsive, entitled and heavily influenced by the status of his Alpha bloodline. He demonstrated little understanding of the political consequences his actions could create and initially dismissed the warnings of [[Icarus Uruky]] when informed that a member of [[Clan Alioth]] appearing on Samar could potentially be connected to [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]].[1]
His arrogance became particularly clear when he declared that he intended to capture Natalia regardless of the potential consequences. Zaion believed his position within the [[Uruky Clan]] and his intended connection to the First Prince would allow him to act without meaningful opposition.[1]
He was also highly lustful and exploitative. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s empathic perception revealed that Zaion did not merely find Natalia attractive; he wanted to possess her, take her for himself and exploit the abilities associated with Clan Alioth for his own purposes.[1]
Zaion was nevertheless significantly less courageous once confronted by individuals who clearly outranked him. When Leônidas pressured him for answers, his previous arrogance quickly disappeared and he submitted immediately to the Patriarch's authority.[1]
His grandfather regarded this behavior as the result of excessive protection. Leônidas admitted that he had shielded Zaion too much whenever he made mistakes and believed this indulgence had allowed Zaion's arrogance and recklessness to develop unchecked.[1]
Zaion also inherited behavioral similarities to his father. Leônidas described him as a brute like his deceased father, who had similarly attempted to claim another man's woman based upon his confidence in the Uruky Clan's Alpha status and was ultimately killed for it.[1]

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Zaion first appeared shortly after [[Victor Elderblood]], [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]], [[Natalia Elderblood née Alioth]] and Anderson's group entered Samar. While observing the newly arrived group from a distance, Zaion noticed Natalia and looked at her with obvious greed, lust and malicious intent.[2]
Victor immediately sensed Zaion's emotions. As Victor's awareness of emotions and hostile intentions had become extraordinarily developed, Zaion's interest in Natalia was obvious to him even though Zaion had taken no direct action yet. Victor immediately separated from the others and went after the Werewolf responsible.[2]
Some time later, Zaion argued with [[Icarus Uruky]] inside the Uruky residence. Zaion insisted that the woman he had seen belonged to [[Clan Alioth]]. [[Icarus]] initially refused to believe him because Clan Alioth was famously protected by the Vampire King and warned that interfering with one of its members could provoke a major conflict.[1]
Zaion remained dismissive. He declared that he would capture Natalia and deliver her to the First Prince, claiming that not even Second Prince Anderson would stop him.[1]
This statement immediately alarmed Icarus, who demanded a detailed description of everyone accompanying Natalia. Zaion described Anderson, his subordinates, a member of Clan Lykos and an exceptionally handsome man whom Zaion had incorrectly judged as weak. Icarus concluded that the mysterious man could potentially be Vlad in disguise because of the Vampire King's known connection to Clan Alioth.[1]
Their grandfather [[Leônidas Uruky]] had overheard the entire conversation. His supernatural instincts had already begun warning him that something was terribly wrong, and he entered the room before Zaion could continue with his plan.[1]
Leônidas immediately condemned Zaion's behavior. He stated that Zaion's actions were serious enough to create a diplomatic incident and even declared that if the Vampire King demanded Zaion's head, Clan Uruky would surrender it rather than endanger the entire family.[1]
Zaion protested that he had done nothing except look at Natalia, but Leônidas considered even this fortunate because any direct attempt to abduct her would have made the situation substantially worse.[1]
Before their discussion could continue, Victor appeared.
The [[Werewolves]] initially realized that the newcomer was not Vlad. Victor revealed himself as the newly recognized King of Hell, while [[Volk Fenrir]]'s announcement simultaneously echoed throughout Eclipse Ventus, declaring that Victor Alucard was Anderson's protected guest and warning every Werewolf on Samar not to antagonize him.[1]
Zaion immediately understood that he had targeted the wife of someone considerably more dangerous than the Vampire King he had originally feared.
Victor isolated the room from the rest of Clan Uruky and confronted Leônidas directly. He made it clear that he could destroy the family if he wished but became interested in the Patriarch's determination to protect his descendants even against an opponent he could not defeat.[1]
Victor then revealed exactly what he had sensed from Zaion when the latter observed Natalia. Zaion wanted Natalia for himself, intended to violate her and planned to exploit her Clan Alioth abilities for whatever objective he was pursuing.[1]
Leônidas and Icarus looked toward Zaion for confirmation. Zaion's refusal to meet their eyes effectively confirmed Victor's accusation because both men knew his behavior well enough to recognize when he was hiding something.[1]
Victor offered Leônidas a simple arrangement: surrender Zaion and the rest of Clan Uruky would be spared.
After several moments of consideration, Leônidas accepted. He determined that Zaion alone was responsible for the crisis and refused to sacrifice the entire Clan for one reckless descendant.[1]
Leônidas nevertheless demanded some assurance that Victor would not attack the family after receiving Zaion. Victor respected his willingness to endure humiliation for his Clan and produced a Demonic Contract promising that Clan Uruky would remain unharmed unless one of its members attacked Victor's family.[1]
Once the agreement was signed, Leônidas took Icarus with him and left Zaion behind.
Before departing, Leônidas finally revealed the fate of Zaion's father. Zaion's father had similarly attempted to take a woman who belonged to another man, assuming that his status as a member of an Alpha Werewolf Clan would protect him. The woman's husband belonged to the pack of a powerful Alpha, who intervened and caused Zaion's father to die without accomplishing anything.[1]
Leônidas bitterly observed that Zaion had repeated almost exactly the same mistake.
After the Patriarch left, Victor began punishing Zaion.
The torture continued long enough for Zaion's screams to echo throughout the Uruky mansion. Leônidas ordered the entire Clan to abandon the residence because he refused to continue living in a building stained by his grandson's blood.[3]
Icarus questioned whether Clan Uruky intended to retaliate. Leônidas immediately rejected the idea and ordered every member of the family to remain as far away from Victor as possible. The incident also caused Clan Uruky to withdraw its political support from the First Prince.[3]
When [[Adam William Lykos]] and Volk Fenrir later entered the room, Zaion had been mutilated so extensively that neither man could easily recognize what remained as a living Werewolf. Even Adam, an experienced General, immediately left the room and vomited upon seeing him, while Volk was visibly nauseated by the scene.[3]
Victor explained that Zaion's punishment was specifically for targeting one of his wives. He confirmed that the rest of Clan Uruky had been left unharmed and privately acknowledged that Leônidas' character was the principal reason the Clan had survived the incident intact.[3]
At Volk's request, Victor finally ended Zaion's suffering. With a gesture, blood poured from Zaion's mutilated body and he died, ending his brief involvement in the conflict.[3]

### Skills & Abilities
Zaion received no dedicated combat scene and demonstrated no unique supernatural technique before his death. His confirmed capabilities primarily derive from his Alpha Werewolf physiology.

#### Alpha Werewolf Physiology
- Alpha Werewolf Physiology: Zaion was born into one of Samar's hereditary Alpha Werewolf families and possessed the superior physiology associated with that status.[1]
- Superhuman Strength: Alpha [[Werewolves]] possess physical strength considerably exceeding ordinary Humans and lower-ranking Werewolves.
- Superhuman Speed: Zaion possessed the enhanced movement speed and reflexes inherent to his Race.
- Superhuman Durability: His Werewolf body possessed supernatural physical resilience.
- Enhanced Stamina: Alpha Werewolves possess substantially greater endurance than ordinary Humans.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Zaion possessed the accelerated recovery natural to Werewolves.
- Enhanced Senses: Werewolves possess highly developed smell, hearing and predatory perception.
- Longevity: Werewolves possess lifespans significantly exceeding those of ordinary Humans.
- Werewolf Transformation: Zaion possessed the racial capacity to manifest more pronounced Werewolf characteristics, although his individual transformed state was never shown.
- Natural Weapons: His Werewolf physiology provided enhanced claws and fangs when transformed.
- Predatory Instincts: Zaion possessed the instinctive responses toward hierarchy, territory and dominance characteristic of Werewolves.

#### Alpha Bloodline
- Hereditary Alpha Lineage: Zaion descended from the [[Uruky Clan]]'s long lineage of Alpha [[Werewolves]].[1]
- Patriarchal Subordination: Despite being born an Alpha, Zaion remained one of [[Leônidas Uruky]]' supernatural Betas while under the authority of the Clan Patriarch. Chapter 737 establishes that every Werewolf within Leônidas' family occupies this subordinate position relative to him, even naturally born Alphas.[1]
Zaion has not been shown possessing:
- True Werewolf physiology.
- Ancient Werewolf physiology.
- True Alpha status.
- Complete Werewolf Transformation.
- Nature Energy techniques.
- An individual Element.
- Godhood or Divinity.
- A named combat technique.

### Relationships

#### Leônidas Uruky
[[Leônidas Uruky]] was Zaion's grandfather and Patriarch.
Leônidas had protected Zaion repeatedly during his younger years and later admitted that this excessive protection had likely contributed to his grandson becoming arrogant and reckless.[1]
When Zaion's actions placed the entire [[Uruky Clan]] in danger, however, Leônidas prioritized his responsibilities as Patriarch over his feelings as a grandfather. He surrendered Zaion to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] rather than allow every member of the family to die for one individual's actions.[1]
Despite this decision, Leônidas did not enjoy Zaion's fate. Hearing his grandson's screams during the torture caused him considerable guilt, and he believed that a Wolf who committed a crime should ideally be punished by his own Clan rather than an outsider.[3]

#### Icarus Uruky
[[Icarus Uruky]] was another grandson of Leônidas and a close relative of Zaion. The novel does not directly establish that the two were biological brothers.
[[Icarus]] was significantly more cautious than Zaion. He immediately recognized the danger of targeting a member of [[Clan Alioth]] and repeatedly attempted to force Zaion to understand the political consequences of his actions.[1]
Zaion ignored those warnings until [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] arrived.

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] killed Zaion after perceiving his intentions toward Natalia.
Victor did not punish Zaion merely for looking at Natalia. Through his empathic perception, Victor understood that Zaion wanted to possess her, sexually exploit her and use her [[Clan Alioth]] abilities for his own purposes.[1]
Victor subsequently tortured Zaion in retaliation and ultimately killed him in Chapter 738.[3]

#### Natalia Elderblood née Alioth
[[Natalia Elderblood née Alioth]] was the target of Zaion's interest.
Zaion saw Natalia shortly after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s group arrived on Samar and immediately became interested in both her beauty and the usefulness of her [[Clan Alioth]] abilities. He intended to capture her and eventually deliver her to the First Prince while also wanting her for himself.[2][1]
Zaion was unaware that Natalia was Victor's wife until Victor personally explained the relationship.

### Trivia
- Zaion first appeared in Chapter 735, while his name was revealed in Chapter 737.[2][1]
- He was a descendant of [[Leônidas Uruky]]'s eldest son.[1]
- The older article described Zaion as the "eldest of the Clan's bloodline", but Chapter 737 more specifically establishes that he descended from Leônidas' eldest son's bloodline rather than explicitly calling Zaion the eldest living Uruky descendant.
- [[Icarus Uruky]] is another grandson of Leônidas, but the reviewed chapters do not directly confirm that [[Icarus]] and Zaion were biological brothers.
- Zaion intended to capture Natalia and deliver her to the First Prince.[1]
- His actions indirectly caused the [[Uruky Clan]] to withdraw its support from the First Prince.[3]
- Leônidas stated that Zaion's father had died after committing essentially the same mistake: attempting to take another man's woman because he believed his Alpha Clan status would protect him.[1]
- Zaion's arrogance largely disappeared whenever Leônidas exerted his authority over him.[1]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] originally considered destroying everyone closely connected to Zaion but changed his decision after respecting Leônidas' willingness to sacrifice himself for his Clan.[1]
- Zaion's punishment was sufficiently gruesome to make [[Adam William Lykos]], an experienced Werewolf General, immediately vomit after seeing him.[3]
- [[Volk Fenrir]] also considered the punishment excessively severe and asked Victor to end Zaion's suffering.[3]
- Zaion was still alive after Victor's prolonged torture and was ultimately killed only when Victor deliberately ended his life.[3]
