### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 737
Leônidas Uruky is an ancient Alpha Werewolf, the Patriarch of the [[Uruky Clan]], and one of the most influential senior [[Werewolves]] of Samar. More than a thousand years old, Leônidas spent much of his life developing the Uruky Clan's wealth, military strength and reputation until it became powerful enough to directly rival the much older [[Lykos Clan]].[1]
By the time of [[Victor Elderblood]]'s arrival on Samar, Leônidas had effectively retired from the Clan's day-to-day leadership and left most active responsibilities to younger generations. He instead devoted himself to training his descendants while continuing to serve as the family's Patriarch and ultimate Alpha.[1]
His most significant appearance occurred after his grandson [[Zaion Uruky]] developed predatory intentions toward [[Natalia Elderblood née Alioth]], unknowingly attracting Victor's personal retaliation. Faced with an enemy he understood he could not defeat, Leônidas nevertheless remained willing to sacrifice himself for the rest of his family. This determination earned Victor's respect and ultimately prevented the destruction of the Uruky Clan.[1]

### Physical Appearance
Despite being more than a thousand years old, Leônidas possesses the appearance of a man still within the prime of his physical life. He stands more than 190 cm tall and has a large, heavily muscular physique.[1]
His body is covered with numerous scars accumulated throughout his long life. Several of these scars remained visible despite the naturally powerful regenerative ability of [[Werewolves]], reflecting the severity of the battles and injuries he survived during his rise to power.[1]
The novel has not provided a detailed description of his hair color, eye color, skin tone or usual clothing.

### Personality
Leônidas is proud, experienced, pragmatic and intensely protective of his family. He possesses considerable pride as both an Alpha and the Patriarch responsible for transforming Clan Uruky into one of Samar's major Werewolf families. In his youth he even considered becoming one of [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]]'s many partners because of the extraordinary strength of the Lykos Bloodline, but ultimately rejected the idea because he refused to accept becoming merely another man within Maya's enormous harem.[1]
Rather than relying upon another Clan's power, Leônidas chose to elevate his own. Through centuries of personal effort, he increased the wealth and reputation of Clan Uruky until it could rival Clan Lykos, giving him considerable personal satisfaction and reinforcing his belief in self-reliance.[1]
Age and experience have also made him extremely cautious. When he overheard Zaion and [[Icarus Uruky]] discussing an unknown woman from [[Clan Alioth]], Leônidas immediately felt that something was wrong. As an ancient Werewolf, he had learned to trust his instincts, which had repeatedly saved his life throughout the centuries.[1]
Unlike his younger descendants, Leônidas maintained his composure even when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] invaded the Uruky residence and displayed power sufficient to terrify everyone around him. He understood that provoking Victor further would only endanger his Clan and attempted to communicate directly rather than respond through empty threats.[1]
His defining trait is his responsibility as Patriarch. Victor could perceive that Leônidas was genuinely prepared to fight him despite knowing victory was virtually impossible if doing so became necessary to protect his family. He was even prepared to sacrifice himself merely to inflict whatever damage he could before dying. Victor respected this determination and explicitly called Leônidas a splendid warrior.[1]
Leônidas is nevertheless capable of making extremely harsh decisions when the survival of the Clan requires them. Once he learned that Zaion had deliberately targeted Victor's wife, he abandoned his grandson to Victor rather than risk the lives of every other Uruky member. The decision deeply conflicted with his Werewolf instincts and familial loyalty, but he chose the entire Clan over a single descendant responsible for the crisis.[1][2]

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Leônidas was introduced shortly after [[Victor Elderblood]] arrived on Samar. At the time, he had already lived for more than a thousand years and had largely withdrawn from active leadership of the [[Uruky Clan]]. Having spent centuries raising the family into one of Samar's most renowned Alpha bloodlines, Leônidas left much of its current administration to the younger generation and concentrated instead on training his descendants.[1]
His peaceful retirement was disrupted when he overheard his grandsons [[Zaion Uruky]] and [[Icarus Uruky]] arguing. Zaion claimed to have encountered a member of [[Clan Alioth]] on Samar and intended to capture her and deliver her to the First Prince. [[Icarus]] immediately recognized the danger in this plan because Clan Alioth had historically been protected by the Vampire King, while Leônidas' own instincts warned him that the situation was even worse than his grandsons understood.[1]
Zaion described the group he had seen, including Anderson, several [[Werewolves]] and an apparently weak but extraordinarily handsome man. Before the situation could be resolved internally, Victor entered the Uruky residence personally. At almost the same moment, [[Volk Fenrir]]'s announcement echoed throughout the city, declaring Victor Alucard—the King of Hell—a protected guest of Second Prince Anderson and ordering every Werewolf not to antagonize him.[1]
Leônidas immediately understood the severity of the situation. Victor isolated the room from the rest of the Clan, leaving Zaion and Icarus terrified that he had casually killed everyone outside. Leônidas remained calmer because his supernatural connection to the members of his family had not disappeared and his own power had not weakened, allowing him to realize that they were still alive.[1]
Victor explained the reality of their position. As the Demon King who had killed [[Diablo]] and inherited the armies of Hell, he possessed enough personal and military power that ordinary political restrictions could not realistically protect Clan Uruky if he decided to destroy it. Leônidas did not attempt to deny this because he understood the central law governing the supernatural world: overwhelming power could render conventional political consequences meaningless.[1]
Despite this enormous disparity, Leônidas refused to completely submit emotionally. Victor sensed that if a battle became unavoidable, the Patriarch would fight until death to protect his family even while knowing that he had virtually no possibility of victory. This determination impressed Victor, who praised Leônidas as a genuine warrior and explained that it was precisely because of the Patriarch's character that he was willing to spare the Clan.[1]
Victor then revealed the reason for his anger. Zaion had looked upon [[Natalia Elderblood née Alioth]] with the intention of taking her for himself and exploiting her abilities. Natalia was Victor's wife, and Victor had originally intended to kill Zaion together with everyone closely connected to him.[1]
Leônidas questioned Zaion and quickly realized that Victor's accusation was true. He then recalled that Zaion's own father had died under remarkably similar circumstances after pursuing another man's wife. Recognizing that his grandson had repeated the same fatal mistake, Leônidas chose the survival of Clan Uruky over defending Zaion.[1]
Before leaving Zaion to Victor, Leônidas demanded a guarantee that the rest of the Clan would remain safe. Victor initially reminded him that he was in no position to make demands, but Leônidas replied that as Patriarch he was obligated to secure his family's survival even if doing so humiliated him. Victor respected the answer and created a Demonic Contract stating that the Uruky Clan would not be attacked unless one of its members attacked Victor's family.[1]
Leônidas carefully read the contract before signing it with his Blood. Unknown to him, the exact wording technically bound only Leônidas because the agreement had been written to restrain the "Demon" Victor Alucard while Victor was actually a Vampire. Victor nevertheless intended to honor his promise regardless of that loophole.[1]
Leônidas then dragged Icarus away and abandoned Zaion to face the consequences of his actions. The decision weighed heavily upon him. In Chapter 738, as Zaion's screams echoed throughout the mansion, Leônidas struggled with guilt because Werewolf instincts emphasized that Wolves should stand together. Even so, between one reckless grandson and the survival of the entire Clan, he knew there had never been a real choice.[2]
Victor later confirmed to Volk that no other member of Clan Uruky had been harmed. He explicitly acknowledged that Leônidas' character was the reason the rest of the family had been spared.[2]
Leônidas later participated in the gathering of major Werewolf Alphas concerning Victor's relationship with [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]]. Unlike [[Vincent Demetriou]] and his supporters, Leônidas did not join the attempt to interfere with Victor's family. When Victor punished the hostile Alphas, Leônidas remained among the senior Werewolves left outside the condemned group alongside Volk, Maya and [[Anthony Bianchi]].[3]
When Victor summoned one of Hell's creatures to discipline Vincent's faction, Leônidas and the other unaffected Alphas prepared themselves in case Victor intended to attack them as well. No battle followed. The encounter also highlighted how deeply Samar's traditional hierarchy had shaped Leônidas: like Anthony, he was so accustomed to answering primarily to Volk that interacting with an independent ruler vastly more powerful than himself did not come naturally.[3]

### Skills & Abilities
Leônidas is an ancient Alpha Werewolf whose power is strongly tied to his position as Patriarch of the [[Uruky Clan]]. Unlike several minor Alpha characters, Chapter 737 provides direct information about the mechanics of his authority and the power he receives from his family.

#### Alpha Werewolf Physiology
- Alpha Werewolf Physiology: Leônidas belongs to a long hereditary family of Alpha [[Werewolves]] and possesses the enhanced physiology associated with that status.[1]
- Superhuman Strength: His large muscular body possesses physical strength vastly exceeding ordinary Humans and lower-ranking Werewolves.
- Superhuman Speed: Leônidas possesses the enhanced movement speed and reflexes of an Alpha Werewolf.
- Superhuman Durability: He survived centuries of dangerous battles severe enough to leave permanent scars across his body.[1]
- Enhanced Stamina: His ancient Werewolf physiology allows him to remain physically formidable despite being more than a thousand years old.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Werewolves possess powerful natural regeneration, although some of Leônidas' historical wounds were severe enough to leave lasting scars.[1]
- Enhanced Senses: Leônidas possesses the heightened sensory abilities of an experienced Alpha Werewolf.
- Longevity: He is explicitly more than 1,000 years old while retaining the appearance and physique of a man in his prime.[1]
- Werewolf Transformation: Leônidas can release more of his Werewolf nature, causing his features to become visibly wilder when threatened or preparing for battle.[1]
- Natural Weapons: His Werewolf physiology grants supernatural claws and fangs when transformed.

#### Patriarch Authority
As Patriarch of Clan Uruky, Leônidas possesses a particularly powerful form of Werewolf pack authority.[1]
- Pack Dominance: Every member of Leônidas' family is considered one of his Betas within the Clan hierarchy, even individuals who were naturally born as Alpha [[Werewolves]].[1]
- Pack Empowerment: The number of Werewolves subordinate to him directly strengthens Leônidas. The enormous size and power of Clan Uruky therefore contribute to his own supernatural strength.[1]
- Pack Connection: Leônidas maintains a supernatural connection to the Werewolves under his authority. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] isolated the Uruky residence and caused every voice outside the room to disappear, Leônidas recognized that his Clan was still alive because he had not lost his connections to his Betas.[1]
- Pack Status Perception: He can notice changes in those connections strongly enough to determine whether the members empowering him remain alive.[1]
- Hierarchical Superiority: Even natural Alphas within his family remain subordinate to Leônidas while operating under his Patriarchal authority.[1]

#### Enhanced Instincts
- Danger Perception: Centuries of experience taught Leônidas to trust his supernatural instincts. He sensed that something was terribly wrong before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] arrived simply from overhearing Zaion and [[Icarus]]' discussion.[1]
- Predatory Awareness: His Werewolf instincts assist in identifying threats and shifts in supernatural hierarchy.
- Experience-Based Intuition: Leônidas states that listening to these instincts had saved him repeatedly throughout his life.[1]

#### Combat Skills
- Veteran Combatant: Leônidas has survived more than a thousand years in Samar's violent supernatural society and carries numerous permanent battle scars.[1]
- Strong Willpower: Even while confronting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Leônidas remained mentally capable of preparing himself for battle rather than collapsing under fear.[1]
- Warrior's Resolve: Victor directly recognized that Leônidas was prepared to fight until death to protect his family despite understanding that victory was effectively impossible.[1]
- Emotional Control: Unlike Zaion and [[Icarus]], Leônidas maintained enough composure under Victor's pressure to analyze the situation and negotiate for his Clan's survival.[1]

#### Leadership
- Clan Leadership: Leônidas personally transformed Clan Uruky from a respected Alpha family into a Clan wealthy and powerful enough to directly rival Clan Lykos.[1]
- Political Experience: More than a millennium of life has given him extensive familiarity with Samar's social and supernatural hierarchy.
- Training: After retiring from active leadership, Leônidas focused much of his time upon training his descendants.[1]
- Pragmatic Decision-Making: He was capable of sacrificing Zaion when defending him would have endangered every other member of Clan Uruky.[1][2]
Leônidas has not been confirmed to possess:
- True Werewolf physiology.
- Ancient Werewolf physiology as a formal racial class.
- True Alpha status.
- Complete Werewolf Transformation.
- Godhood or Divinity.
- An individualized Element.
- A named supernatural combat technique.

### Power and Standing
Leônidas is one of Samar's ancient Alpha [[Werewolves]] and considerably more influential than an ordinary Alpha. His power is enhanced by his position as Patriarch because the entire Uruky family functions beneath him as a large supernatural pack, with even natural Alphas counting as his Betas.[1]
His greatest accomplishment is not an isolated destructive feat but the Clan he constructed. Through his own efforts, the Uruky family became wealthy and powerful enough to rival the ancient [[Lykos Clan]], which was renowned for repeatedly producing Generals for the [[Lycaon|Werewolf King]].[1]
This does not place Leônidas on the level of exceptional anomalies such as [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]], [[Volk Fenrir]], [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]] or current [[Victor Elderblood]]. Leônidas himself fully understood that fighting Victor directly would be hopeless. His willingness to fight was a demonstration of courage and duty rather than evidence that their strength was comparable.[1]

### Relationships

#### Zaion Uruky
[[Zaion Uruky]] was one of Leônidas' grandsons and a senior descendant within Clan Uruky's bloodline.[1]
Leônidas was deeply disappointed in Zaion after discovering that he had targeted Natalia despite the obvious political and personal risks. He compared Zaion's behavior with that of Zaion's father, who had similarly died after attempting to take another man's wife.[1]
Although Leônidas cared about his grandson and later suffered emotionally while hearing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] torture him, he ultimately prioritized the survival of the entire Clan. He regarded sacrificing one guilty descendant as preferable to allowing every member of the family to die for Zaion's actions.[1][2]

#### Icarus Uruky
[[Icarus Uruky]] is another of Leônidas' grandsons. Unlike Zaion, [[Icarus]] immediately recognized that attempting to abduct a member of [[Clan Alioth]] could create a major political crisis and repeatedly attempted to make Zaion explain exactly what he had witnessed.[1]
Leônidas ultimately removed Icarus from the room before leaving Zaion with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], ensuring that his innocent grandson was not included in Victor's punishment.[1]

#### Victor Elderblood
Leônidas first met [[Victor Elderblood]] under extremely hostile circumstances after Zaion targeted Natalia.
Despite Victor being overwhelmingly stronger, Leônidas' determination to protect his family earned Victor's respect. Victor explicitly praised him as a splendid warrior and spared Clan Uruky primarily because of Leônidas' willingness to stand against an impossible opponent for his family's sake.[1]
Leônidas subsequently signed a Demonic Contract intended to prevent further conflict between Clan Uruky and Victor's family.[1]

#### Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood
Leônidas has known of [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] for centuries. At one point he was sufficiently impressed by both Maya and the exceptional Lykos Bloodline that he considered becoming one of her partners.[1]
His pride ultimately prevented him from doing so. Leônidas refused to become merely another man within Maya's enormous harem and instead dedicated himself to building Clan Uruky into a family capable of rivaling hers.[1]

#### Volk Fenrir
[[Volk Fenrir]] was Leônidas' King during most of the known Samar era. Like many senior Alpha [[Werewolves]], Leônidas had spent centuries operating beneath the [[Lycaon|Werewolf King]]'s hierarchy and became deeply accustomed to responding to Volk's authority.[3]
Chapter 750 notes that this conditioning made it difficult for Leônidas and Anthony to naturally adapt when dealing with an independent ruler such as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], whose authority did not originate from Samar's established political system.[3]
