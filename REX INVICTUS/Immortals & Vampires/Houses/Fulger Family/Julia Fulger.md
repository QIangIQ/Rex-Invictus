### Appearances

#### Debut
[[Ch16 - Rex Invictus IX|Chapter 16]]
Julia, posthumously recognized as Julia Fulger, was a Human maid of the [[Fulger Clan]] and the personal caretaker of [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]]. Although she possessed neither Noble Vampire Blood nor supernatural power, Julia became one of the most important people in Sasha's life. During the years in which Sasha was subjected to dangerous training and experimentation by her parents, Julia cared for her injuries, educated her, played with her and gave her the affection Sasha felt she never received from her biological mother. Sasha consequently regarded Julia not simply as a servant but as her true maternal figure.[1]
Julia was murdered alongside the other servants of Sasha's residence when [[Carlos Reiss]] and Maria, members of the Inquisition, attacked the weakened Fulger household shortly after the Clan lost its Count status.[2] Her death became one of the defining traumas of Sasha's early life and directly contributed to the grief, rage and recklessness she displayed when she first became properly involved with [[Victor Elderblood]].[2][1]
Julia's importance was eventually acknowledged even by Natashia. Long after Julia's death, Natashia used the supernatural ancestral record of Clan Fulger to place Julia Fulger within the Clan's Main Family Lineage, granting a Human maid one of the greatest honors the ancient Vampire house could bestow.[3]

### Physical Appearance
Julia was a Human woman who appeared to be in her thirties at the time of her death. She had fair skin, brown eyes and long black hair extending past her shoulders. While working as a maid, she generally kept her appearance neat and practical, often arranging her hair so that it would not interfere with her duties.
Unlike the supernatural women surrounding the Fulger household, Julia possessed no ageless Vampire physiology and aged normally as a Human.

### Personality
Julia was gentle, patient, hardworking and deeply caring. These traits distinguished her sharply from the environment in which Sasha was raised. While Sasha's parents often treated their daughter's extraordinary abilities as something to test and develop, Julia consistently treated Sasha as a child who required affection, guidance and ordinary emotional support.[1]
Her devotion went considerably beyond the professional responsibilities of a personal maid. Sasha remembered returning from her parents' dangerous training sessions severely injured or close to death, only for Julia to personally treat her wounds and care for her as though she were her own daughter.[1] Julia never appears to have treated Sasha's position as the heiress of an ancient Vampire Clan as more important than Sasha herself.
Julia was also responsible for much of Sasha's ordinary upbringing. She taught her etiquette, general education, the customs and rules of Noble Vampire society, arithmetic, geography and Vampire history. At the same time, Julia made an effort to give Sasha experiences unrelated to Bloodline development or Clan obligations. She introduced her to Human games and regularly played chess with her.[1]
The combination of teacher, caretaker, confidante and parental figure caused Julia to occupy a place in Sasha's life that neither of her biological parents successfully filled during her childhood. Sasha explicitly described Julia as the mother her real mother had never been to her.[1]

### Synopsis

#### The Awakening
Julia had served as Sasha Fulger's personal maid for years before the beginning of the story. Sasha's childhood had been dominated by the extraordinary expectations attached to the [[Fulger Bloodline]]. Because she inherited both the Clan's powerful Lightning and the physical characteristics of her father [[William Salvatore Florence]], Sasha possessed greater resistance to the destructive effects of Fulger Lightning than previous generations. Her parents consequently subjected her to repeated and extremely dangerous experiments intended to develop that potential.[1]
Sasha recalled being placed in an electric chair to test her resistance, thrown beneath the sea and ordered to escape using Lightning, and subjected to other training that repeatedly brought her close to death. Her Noble Vampire regeneration allowed her to survive treatment that would have killed ordinary Humans, but surviving did not make the experiences harmless.[1]
Julia became Sasha's personal maid after her parents began losing interest in continuously supervising those experiments. From then onward, she became the person who actually raised Sasha. Whenever Sasha returned from training severely injured, Julia healed and cared for her. She taught Sasha the knowledge expected from a noble heiress, including etiquette, arithmetic, geography, education, Vampire history and the customs of Noble society, but also spent ordinary recreational time with her. Sasha particularly remembered playing chess with Julia and simply being able to talk with her.[1]
These apparently ordinary experiences became extremely important to Sasha because Julia was one of the only people in her childhood who consistently provided affection without demanding that Sasha prove the value of her Bloodline.
Shortly before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s transformation, Clan Fulger suffered a major political decline after Annasthashia lost the family's Vampire Count position to [[Niklaus Horseman]]. The loss made the Clan appear considerably more vulnerable to hostile organizations that would previously have hesitated to attack the household of one of [[Nightingale]]'s great Pillars.[2]
On January 31, shortly after the ritual that transformed Victor into a Vampire, Sasha returned to her residence and discovered that it had been attacked by members of the Inquisition. The servants had been slaughtered and Julia had been killed, her body having been decapitated during the massacre.[2]
The attackers, [[Carlos Reiss]] and Maria, had targeted Sasha after taking advantage of Fulger's weakened political position. Julia and the other servants were casualties of the operation intended to capture the Clan's heiress.[2]
Julia's death immediately destabilized Sasha. Already suffering from the abnormal Blood bond created during Victor's transformation, Sasha reacted to the destruction of her home and the murder of her maternal figure with overwhelming rage. She forced her Lightning beyond the level she normally used, escaped the hunters' restraint and captured Maria.[2]
Rather than killing Maria, Sasha forcibly transformed her into a Vampiric servant, turning a member of an organization devoted to hunting Vampires into one herself. Sasha then intended to pursue further revenge against the Inquisition despite being emotionally compromised and injured. [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]] recognized that Sasha's grief over Julia was making her dangerously reckless and incapacitated her rather than allow her to launch what could have become a suicidal attack.[2][4]
After being taken to Victor, Sasha eventually recounted her childhood and Julia's role in it. The conversation revealed just how profound the loss had been. Sasha described Julia as the person who treated her wounds, educated her, played with her and loved her as a daughter. While recounting those memories, Sasha finally broke down and admitted that she did not simply miss a servant—she missed her mother.[1]
Victor, connected strongly enough to Sasha to experience portions of her emotions directly, developed enormous respect for Julia despite never having met her. He also became increasingly furious toward Sasha's parents after understanding that an ordinary Human maid had provided the parental care they had failed to give their own daughter.[1]
Julia's memory remained central to Victor's later judgment of Sasha's family. When he eventually confronted William and Annasthashia, he rejected the idea that biological parenthood automatically entitled them to Sasha's respect. From Victor's perspective, Julia had fulfilled the responsibilities of Sasha's mother far more completely than either of Sasha's biological parents had during the most vulnerable period of her life.[5][6][7]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Years of emotional development eventually forced Natashia to acknowledge the same reality.
By Chapter 473, Natashia had greatly changed from the emotionally detached woman Sasha remembered from childhood. Inside her private room within Clan Fulger, she maintained paintings and reminders of the people she valued and had created a statue honoring Julia.[3]
Natashia called Julia the woman who had taken care of her "little treasure" when she herself had failed to do so and decided that ordinary gratitude was insufficient.[3]
She summoned the ancestral family record of Clan Fulger, a supernatural book created by an earlier Fulger ancestor and considered an artifact comparable in significance to the Clan's ancestral daggers. The book recorded the genuine Main Family Lineage beginning with Carmila Fulger, followed by Natashia, Victoria, Sasha and [[Tatsuya Rider]].[3]
Sasha initially pointed out that what Natashia intended violated the normal rules governing the Main Family record. Julia was Human, unrelated by Blood and had served the family as a maid.
Natashia disregarded the restriction on the grounds that, as the current Clan Leader, she possessed the authority to change the rule. She declared that caring for Sasha was sufficient reason for Julia to receive the greatest honor Natashia and Clan Fulger could grant her.[3]
Using her authority as Clan Leader and her fully developed Fulger power, Natashia ordered the ancestral record to recognize Julia. The book responded, and Julia Fulger was formally entered into the Primary Family Lineage of Clan Fulger.[3]
Her name was placed alongside the generation containing Natashia and Victoria rather than among ordinary initiated servants. By the Clan's supernatural record, Julia therefore became a posthumously recognized member of Fulger's Main Family. Genealogically, this places her in an aunt-like position toward Sasha, although emotionally Sasha continued to regard Julia as her mother.[3]
The gesture deeply affected Sasha. Julia had died believing herself to be a Human servant caring for the girl entrusted to her; centuries later, the ancient family she had served formally recognized that her role had been far greater than employment.

### Relationships

#### Sasha Elderblood Fulger
Sasha was the most important person in Julia's life shown in the story. Julia was officially her personal maid, but their relationship developed into that of mother and daughter.
Julia entered Sasha's life after years of dangerous training and experimentation had left the young Vampire emotionally isolated. She treated Sasha's injuries whenever she returned from training, educated her, taught her how to conduct herself as a noblewoman and spent ordinary leisure time with her.[1]
Sasha later remembered that Julia taught her virtually everything she needed to function outside combat: etiquette, formal education, arithmetic, geography, Vampire history and the rules of Noble society. Julia also introduced Human games into Sasha's life, with chess becoming one of the activities Sasha specifically remembered sharing with her.[1]
Her death devastated Sasha to such a degree that merely discussing Julia with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] caused her to collapse into grief. Sasha openly referred to Julia as her mother and contrasted Julia's affection with the emotional absence of her biological parents.[1]
Natashia's eventual decision to recognize Julia as Main Family gave supernatural and legal Clan recognition to a relationship Sasha had emotionally accepted long before.[3]

#### Annasthashia Elderblood Fulger
Julia served within the household ruled by Annasthashia Fulger, although there is little direct interaction between them shown before Julia's death.
Their significance to one another emerges primarily through Sasha.
During Sasha's childhood, Julia performed many of the maternal responsibilities Annasthashia neglected. When Annasthashia initially learned of Julia's death, her apparent indifference deeply hurt Sasha because it reduced the woman Sasha considered her mother to a replaceable servant.[5]
After Natashia's original personality resurfaced and she spent considerable time attempting to repair her relationship with Sasha, her attitude changed dramatically. She eventually acknowledged that Julia had raised and protected her daughter when she herself had failed to do so.[3]
Natashia's posthumous adoption of Julia into Fulger's Main Family was therefore simultaneously an act of gratitude toward Julia and an acknowledgment of Natashia's own failure as Sasha's mother.

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] never met Julia while she was alive.
He learned about her almost entirely through Sasha's memories, grief and later discussions concerning her childhood. Nevertheless, Victor developed profound respect for Julia after understanding the role she had played in his wife's life.[1]
Through his bond with Sasha, Victor could directly feel much of the pain associated with Julia's death. He consequently regarded Julia as Sasha's genuine maternal figure and later used her as the standard against which he criticized Sasha's biological parents.[1][7]
Victor's respect was not based upon Julia possessing power, status or a supernatural Bloodline. From his perspective, she had earned the position of Sasha's mother by actually raising, protecting and caring for her.

#### Maria Elderblood
Maria was one of the Inquisition hunters responsible for the attack in which Julia died.[2]
Julia's death therefore became one of the principal reasons Sasha initially hated Maria. Instead of simply killing her after escaping the ambush, Sasha inflicted a punishment she considered particularly appropriate for an Inquisition hunter by transforming Maria into a Vampire servant.[2]
Maria's later integration into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household occurred long after Julia's death and does not erase the role she played in the event that caused Sasha's trauma.

### Skills & Abilities
Julia was an ordinary Human and has never been shown possessing a supernatural Bloodline, magic, enhanced physiology or combat ability. Her demonstrated talents were domestic, educational and interpersonal rather than supernatural.
- Professional Maid Training: Julia was capable of serving as the personal maid of the heiress of one of [[Nightingale]]'s great Noble Vampire families.
- Caretaking: She regularly treated and cared for Sasha after the young Vampire returned severely injured from training and experimentation.[1]
- Education: Julia taught Sasha arithmetic, geography, Vampire history, etiquette and the rules governing Noble Vampire society.[1]
- Etiquette: She was sufficiently knowledgeable in aristocratic customs to prepare Sasha for her responsibilities as the heiress of Clan Fulger.[1]
- Medical Care (Mundane): Julia repeatedly tended Sasha's injuries after training. No supernatural healing ability is attributed to her.[1]
- Childcare: Julia effectively raised Sasha throughout a substantial portion of her childhood and adolescence.[1]
- Chess: Julia regularly played chess with Sasha and introduced her to recreational games created by Humans.[1]
- Knowledge of Vampire Society: Despite being Human, Julia possessed enough knowledge of Vampire history, Noble customs and Fulger society to educate the Clan heiress in those subjects.[1]

### Legacy
Julia's narrative importance is disproportionate to the amount of time she physically appears.
Her treatment of Sasha directly influenced the woman Sasha became. While the Fulger household taught Sasha power, endurance and the expectations of a Noble Vampire heiress, Julia taught her ordinary affection, education, patience and the experience of being cared for without being treated as an experiment.
Her murder became one of the emotional catalysts behind Sasha's earliest development in the story. It contributed to Sasha's hatred of the Inquisition, her treatment of Maria, her emotional vulnerability when first bonding with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Victor's subsequent hostility toward Sasha's parents.[2][1][7]
Most significantly, Julia eventually altered the traditions of an ancient Vampire Clan despite having died as an ordinary Human.
The Fulger Main Family record was traditionally reserved for the central Bloodline descending from Carmila. Natashia deliberately overrode that convention and ordered the supernatural artifact itself to recognize Julia as family because Julia had earned the position through her treatment of Sasha.[3]
Julia therefore became one of the clearest examples within Clan Fulger that family could be recognized through actions rather than Blood alone.
