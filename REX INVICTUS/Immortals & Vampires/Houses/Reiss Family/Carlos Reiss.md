### Appearances

#### Debut
[[Ch16 - Rex Invictus IX|Chapter 16]]
Carlos Reiss was a member of the Inquisition, an artificial Human-Vampire Hybrid, and one of the earliest recurring enemies of [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] and [[Victor Elderblood]]. He was the childhood companion and former lover of Maria, with the pair having survived together as impoverished orphans in Camden, New Jersey before being recruited by the Church.[1]
Carlos and Maria were responsible for the ambush against Sasha's residence shortly after Victor's transformation. Carlos personally slaughtered the servants inside the mansion while Maria decapitated Julia, the woman Sasha considered her true mother, before the pair attempted to capture the weakened Fulger heiress for the Inquisition.[2]
After Sasha abducted Maria and transformed her into a defective Vampire servant, Carlos became increasingly obsessed with recovering her and exterminating Vampires. His indiscriminate killing eventually attracted the attention of General James, who promoted him to Commander and subjected him to the Inquisition's experimental Hybrid program. Carlos willingly accepted the transformation, retaining his ability to utilize Faith while gaining several Vampire-derived characteristics and an unidentified black supernatural power.[3][4]
His pursuit of Maria ultimately became part of a larger trap devised by James to collect information on Victor's strength. Carlos was overwhelmingly defeated by Victor, captured by the Elderblood household and subsequently tortured and studied. Victor later removed the Vampire component from Carlos' altered body in order to access the memories contained within his blood, uncovering Carlos' connection to James and the wider artificial Hybrid program.[5][6]

### Physical description
Carlos was originally described as a tall, bald, white-skinned man. During his first appearance, he wore priestly clothing that had been modified until it resembled a black suit, along with black gloves used during combat. His build and posture reflected his role as the physical fighter of his partnership with Maria.[2]
Following his artificial Hybrid transformation, Carlos' appearance became considerably more Vampiric. His skin grew noticeably paler, his eyes became blood-red and his teeth developed pronounced sharpness. When activating his powers, his right arm became covered by an ominous black aura while his left arm released brilliant golden Faith energy.[4]
Carlos could spread the black power from his right arm over a much larger portion of his body. During his battle with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], this energy could additionally manifest into a large black wing behind him, giving his transformed state an increasingly unnatural appearance.[5]

### Personality and traits
Carlos was originally a pragmatic survivor rather than an ideological fanatic. According to Maria, the two had no particular hatred toward Vampires during the beginning of their careers and regarded Vampire hunting primarily as employment. They had spent thirteen years surviving on the streets of Camden together, stealing and even killing when necessary simply to remain alive.[1]
Even during childhood Carlos demonstrated unusual physical potential. Despite being only around ten years old, he was stronger and faster than ordinary Humans of his age and repeatedly protected Maria from the dangers they encountered while living on the streets. Maria credited Carlos with protecting her "tooth and nail", and her emotional dependence upon him became so strong that Carlos himself eventually became the object of her Faith.[1]
Their partnership consequently functioned through complementary roles. Carlos possessed considerably greater raw power and Hunter potential, while Maria compensated for her weaker Faith by specializing in restraint and planning. Maria summarized their relationship simply: she was the brain and Carlos was the muscle.[1]
Carlos also genuinely loved Maria. Before the attack against Sasha, the two intended to marry and have children the following year.[2] Losing her therefore caused an extreme psychological deterioration. Carlos experienced repeated nightmares concerning the night Sasha took Maria and became obsessed with acquiring enough power to ensure that he would never again be helpless in such a situation.[4]
His grief gradually transformed into hatred. By the time [[Victor Elderblood]] investigated him, Carlos had developed a reputation for killing every Vampire he encountered without discrimination. This behavior attracted General James, who recognized both Carlos' combat ability and his willingness to undergo increasingly extreme experimentation.[3]
Carlos ultimately accepted becoming the very type of creature he had sworn to destroy. He was completely aware of the contradiction and even invoked Friedrich Nietzsche's warning about fighting monsters while explaining himself to Maria. Rather than considering the transformation a betrayal of his beliefs, Carlos argued that Humanity possessed clearly defined natural limits and that he therefore needed to become a monster himself in order to defeat other monsters.[4]
His fanaticism did not eliminate his Faith. Carlos interpreted his continued ability to produce golden Faith from his left arm as proof that God had not abandoned him despite his Hybrid transformation. This strengthened his belief that his actions remained justified.[4]
Carlos' feelings toward Maria became severely distorted following her transformation into a Ghoul. Although the woman before him retained Maria's memories and personality, Carlos refused to recognize her as the person he had loved. He repeatedly insisted that the "real" Maria had died and that the Vampire standing before him was merely a Demon occupying her place. From his perspective, killing her would allow his beloved to finally rest in peace.[4]
Despite this fanaticism, remnants of Carlos' former feelings remained. Maria's bloody tears briefly shook his resolve during their confrontation, and his later experiences showed that he had never completely stopped regretting what happened between them.[4][6]
When Carlos later entered the [[World of Unconsciousness]], his regrets became explicit. He lamented not having been strong enough to protect Maria from Sasha and regretted never being honest enough about his feelings to simply abandon the Church and leave with her. At the same time, Carlos acknowledged that such a future had probably never been realistic because he had accepted the life of a Hunter as something he would continue until death.[6]
Carlos was therefore not portrayed as completely devoid of positive qualities. His memories included Humans he had protected and people for whom he had functioned almost like a savior, contrasting sharply with the Vampires and supernatural beings he had slaughtered. His final arc consequently emphasized the blurred boundary between the person he had once been and the extremist he eventually became.[6]

### Synopsis

#### The Awakening
Carlos was first identified as one of the more dangerous [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] operating in the United States during the Inquisition's search for Lucy. Jimmy listed Carlos alongside Maria and Mizuki while explaining the composition of the hunting force to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Violet.[7]
Shortly afterward, Carlos and Maria targeted [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]]. The Inquisition had learned that Clan Fulger had lost its position as a Count Clan and believed that capturing its heiress while the family was politically weakened would further diminish its power.[2]
The pair infiltrated Sasha's residence before her return. Carlos directly attacked the mansion and killed the servants inside while Maria personally killed Julia and prepared a web of Faith-enhanced wires to immobilize Sasha. When Sasha entered the mansion, Carlos launched the initial ambush and attempted to engage her with his Faith-enhanced fists.[2]
Carlos demonstrated awareness of his own limitations during the confrontation. He openly admitted that Clan Fulger's lightning was badly matched against his fighting style and depended upon Maria's restraining abilities to capture Sasha alive rather than attempting to overwhelm the Vampire by himself.[2]
Their plan initially succeeded. Maria's wires restrained Sasha in a crucifix-like position while Carlos prepared to attack. Sasha, however, forced her lightning beyond its normal limits and broke free at supersonic speed.[2]
Rather than immediately kill Carlos, Sasha identified his emotional attachment to Maria and chose a far more personal form of retaliation. She seized Maria, bit her and escaped before Carlos could save her. Carlos' desperate screams followed them as Sasha transformed Maria into the very species she had spent her life hunting.[2]

#### Vampire Society
Several months later, Maria recounted her history with Carlos. The two had grown up together as orphans in one of the poorest areas of Camden, New Jersey, surviving on the streets for approximately thirteen years.[1]
Carlos possessed abnormal physical capabilities even as a child and repeatedly protected Maria from the dangers surrounding them. A priest eventually approached the pair and provided them with shelter. The Church subsequently discovered that Carlos possessed exceptional aptitude for accessing supernatural energy through Faith.[1]
Maria attempted the same training because she was afraid of being separated from Carlos. Although her raw aptitude was considerably weaker, she discovered that her personal faith in Carlos himself was sufficient to access Hunter abilities. The pair subsequently entered the Inquisition together, with Carlos functioning as the principal fighter and Maria as the strategist who restrained enemies for him.[1]
According to Maria, neither originally possessed a personal vendetta against Vampires. Hunting supernatural beings was simply the profession that allowed two former street orphans to survive comfortably.[1]

#### A New Pillar
The loss of Maria drastically changed Carlos. Instead of moving on after the failed mission, he became increasingly consumed by the memory of what Sasha had done. He threw himself into acquiring strength and began indiscriminately killing Vampires wherever he encountered them.[3]
His behavior attracted General James, who had already been conducting extensive research into artificial Human-Vampire Hybrids. James promoted Carlos to Commander and incorporated him into his experimental program.[3]
Carlos voluntarily accepted extensive modifications. The process transformed him from an ordinary Human Hunter into an artificial Hybrid capable of retaining Human Faith while simultaneously displaying Vampire characteristics. His transformation gave him blood-red eyes, paler skin, sharp teeth, greatly enhanced physical attributes, regeneration and access to an unidentified black power distinct from his golden Faith.[4]
Carlos eventually contacted Maria and arranged a meeting inside an abandoned town in Texas. Maria correctly suspected that the invitation was a trap, and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household deliberately used the opportunity to locate both Carlos and the forces operating behind him.[4]
When Maria entered the mansion, Carlos initially welcomed her gently and admitted that he had continued suffering nightmares about the night he lost her. He then revealed his Hybrid transformation, explaining that he had willingly crossed the boundary between Human and monster because ordinary Humanity's limitations were no longer sufficient for what he wanted to accomplish.[4]
Carlos demonstrated that the two halves of his altered nature remained separately accessible. His right arm generated black supernatural energy while his left arm produced the golden Faith of a Hunter. He considered the continued existence of his Faith proof that God still approved of him despite his transformation.[4]
He then attacked Maria. A Faith-enhanced strike from his left arm produced an enormous golden explosion that severely burned and injured her, while an attack from his right arm sent her crashing through the mansion's exterior. Carlos refused to recognize Maria as his former lover, repeatedly insisting that she had already died and that the Ghoul standing before him was merely a Demon that needed to be destroyed.[4]
Victor initially intended to allow the confrontation to end with both Carlos and Maria dying as revenge for Julia. Sasha's increasingly complicated feelings toward Maria changed the situation, and Victor ultimately intervened according to his wife's true wishes.[4]
After removing Maria from the fight, Victor confronted Carlos personally. The difference between them proved overwhelming. Victor accelerated using Fulger lightning and repeatedly struck Carlos at speeds the Hybrid could not properly follow, launching him continuously through the air.[5]
Carlos attempted to answer by expanding the black power from his right arm across his body and firing a massive black beam at Victor. Victor simply avoided it. Carlos then manifested the black energy into a wing, while Victor observed that the same power noticeably accelerated Carlos' regeneration.[5]
Carlos continued using golden Faith offensively, generating powerful pressure and energy from his left fist, but Victor remained largely unimpressed. Victor eventually combined his own elemental abilities and produced an enormous fire attack resembling a miniature sun, causing a catastrophic explosion that overwhelmed Carlos.[5]
The explosion did not kill him. While Victor became occupied with the additional experimental combatants deployed by James, Ruby and the others located Carlos' surviving body. Recognizing that his Hybrid physiology might allow him to survive injuries that would kill an ordinary Human, Ruby insisted they confirm his condition and ultimately had him frozen and transported away rather than leaving him behind.[8][9]
Carlos was subsequently imprisoned within Victor's underground residence. Sasha and Maria were eventually permitted to confront him directly. Carlos defended the Inquisition's hostility toward supernatural beings by pointing to the enormous disparity between ordinary Humans and supernatural races, describing many of the Church's actions as the practical response of a weaker species attempting to survive among monsters.[10]
Sasha rejected his justification, arguing that Humanity's persecution routinely extended even toward supernatural beings who had done nothing to them. Maria, meanwhile, finally stopped viewing Carlos as the man she once loved.[10]
Driven partly by the hunger of her incomplete Ghoul physiology, Maria fed directly upon Carlos. Carlos did not meaningfully resist and appeared to accept what was happening while remembering the life they had shared together. The act left him severely weakened but did not immediately kill him.[10]
Shortly afterward, Carlos entered the [[World of Unconsciousness]], where he confronted memories of both the people he had helped and the supernatural beings he had killed. He reflected upon his relationship with Maria and admitted his regret over the choices that had ultimately separated them.[6]
Upon awakening, he was confronted by Victor. Carlos was shown Maria after Victor had completed her transformation into a healthy Vampire, but Maria now regarded her former lover with little beyond disgust.[6]
Victor then experimented upon Carlos' altered blood. He extracted a sphere of black blood from the Hybrid, purified it until it turned red and absorbed it into himself. The process allowed Victor to view fragments of Carlos' memories, including references to Project Hybrid, Maria, S-000 and Carlos' first direct meeting with General James.[6]
This finally allowed Victor to identify the person directing the experimental program from the shadows. After extracting Carlos' Vampire component, however, Victor discovered that the remaining body was effectively Human and rapidly deteriorating. Rather than allowing him to die immediately, Victor decided Carlos remained more useful alive and intended to hand him over to [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] as a research subject.[6]
Carlos' exact final moments were not shown. Later narration nevertheless refers to Commander Carlos as deceased when discussing the Inquisition's subsequent command structure, confirming that he eventually died sometime after his capture and experimentation.[11]

### Skills and abilities
Carlos was naturally gifted even before becoming a Hunter and was substantially stronger than ordinary Humans from childhood. After joining the Inquisition he developed into a powerful close-range combatant specializing in Faith-enhanced martial attacks. His later artificial Hybrid transformation considerably increased his abilities.

#### Human Abilities
- Faith Manipulation: Carlos possessed significant natural aptitude for accessing Faith, the supernatural energy employed by Human [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]]. His potential was considered exceptional enough that the priest who discovered him and Maria specifically recognized Carlos as unusually gifted.[1]
- Faith Empowerment: Carlos could channel Faith directly into his body, particularly his fists. During his first battle against Sasha, a prayer caused his hand to glow with golden energy before he entered a martial stance.[2]
- Enhanced Strength: Carlos was abnormally strong even before receiving formal Hunter training. At approximately ten years old, he was already stronger than ordinary Humans around him and used that strength to protect himself and Maria while living on the streets.[1]
- Enhanced Speed: Maria similarly described Carlos as unnaturally fast from childhood, with his innate physical abilities later increasing further through his training as a Hunter.[1]
- Hand-to-Hand Combat: Carlos primarily fought at close range using his fists and displayed formal combat training. Before attacking Sasha, he assumed a martial stance and empowered his fist through Faith.[2]
- Hunter Training: Carlos was an experienced member of the Inquisition long before his promotion to Commander and possessed the combat knowledge, religious techniques and supernatural awareness expected from professional Vampire Hunters.

#### Hybrid Abilities
- Artificial Human-Vampire Hybrid Physiology: Through James' experiments, Carlos became an artificial Hybrid combining Human and Vampire traits. Unlike ordinary Humans, he developed blood-red eyes, sharp teeth, pale skin, significantly improved physical capabilities and Vampire-derived regeneration while retaining his ability to use Faith.[4]
- Superhuman Strength: Carlos' Hybrid transformation drastically amplified his physical power. His punches were capable of launching Maria through reinforced structures and creating powerful shockwaves.[4]
- Superhuman Speed: Carlos became capable of moving rapidly enough to seemingly disappear before reappearing directly in front of Maria. His speed remained vastly inferior to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] using Fulger lightning, however.[4][5]
- Superhuman Durability: Carlos survived Victor's repeated high-speed physical attacks as well as an enormous elemental explosion that devastated the surrounding area. Although he was defeated, the attack failed to kill him outright.[5][8]
- Regeneration: Carlos inherited regenerative characteristics from his Vampire side. His unidentified black power could be spread across his body to accelerate this regeneration even further.[5]
- Faith Retention: Unlike a normal Vampire, Carlos retained access to Human Faith after his transformation. He concentrated this golden energy primarily through his left arm.[4]
- Golden Energy Projection: Carlos could release his Faith as destructive golden energy. A single punch against Maria generated a massive golden blast powerful enough to burn most of her skin away and launch her across the mansion.[4]
- Black Energy Manipulation: Carlos possessed an unnamed black supernatural power concentrated primarily in his right arm. Its exact origin was never fully explained, though it appears to have been associated with the Vampire component of his Hybrid transformation.[4][5]
- Energy Projection: Carlos could fire the black energy outward as a massive beam.[5]
- Regeneration Enhancement: Covering his body in the black power significantly increased the speed of his healing.[5]
- Wing Manifestation: Carlos could shape the black energy into a large wing resembling the wing of a dark angel.[5]
- Dual-Energy Usage: Carlos could wield golden Faith and his black Hybrid energy separately through opposite arms, allowing him to retain powers associated with both sides of his altered existence.[4]
- Enhanced Senses: As an artificial Vampire-Human Hybrid, Carlos possessed enhanced supernatural perception beyond that of ordinary Humans.
- High Pain Tolerance: Carlos survived severe punishment from Victor, subsequent capture and torture, being fed upon by Maria and later having the Vampire component of his blood forcibly extracted before finally deteriorating.[5][10][6]

### Weaknesses
- Incomplete Hybrid Physiology: Carlos was not a naturally stable Hybrid. Ruby's later examination of artificial Hunter-Vampire Hybrids established that their genetic structures were severely incomplete, and Carlos himself was missing a substantial portion of the genetic information necessary for a properly stable Hybrid body.[12]
- Dependence on Vampire Component: When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] extracted the Vampire-derived portion of Carlos' blood and abilities, Carlos reverted toward an ordinary Human state and immediately began dying because his altered body could no longer properly sustain itself.[6]
- Inferiority to True High-Level Vampires: Although the Hybrid procedure made Carlos vastly stronger than his former Human self, his new power remained insignificant compared with genuinely elite Vampires such as Victor. Carlos was unable to properly perceive Victor's speed and was overwhelmingly defeated once Victor fought seriously.[5]

### Trivia
- Carlos and Maria were both orphaned children raised on the streets of Camden, New Jersey.[1]
- The pair survived together for approximately thirteen years before being taken in by a priest.[1]
- Maria's personal Faith was centered upon Carlos rather than God. She explicitly described Carlos as her "god" during their time together as [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]].[1]
- Carlos and Maria originally planned to marry and have children before their failed mission against Sasha destroyed their relationship.[2]
- Carlos originally treated Vampire hunting as a job rather than a personal crusade. His indiscriminate hatred of Vampires developed primarily after Sasha transformed Maria into a Ghoul.[1][3]
- General James became interested in Carlos after learning that he was killing every Vampire he encountered without discrimination.[3]
- Carlos consciously recognized the hypocrisy of becoming a Human-Vampire Hybrid despite dedicating himself to killing Vampires. He justified the decision by believing Humanity's natural limits prevented Humans from defeating monsters without becoming monsters themselves.[4]
- The golden energy in Carlos' left arm was his Human Faith, while the right arm produced his unidentified black Hybrid power. The previous article incorrectly confused the two sides.[4]
- Carlos was promoted to Commander, not General. Later chapters explicitly refer to him as "Commander Carlos".[3][11]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] did not kill Carlos during their battle in Chapter 192. Carlos survived the fight, was captured, tortured and subsequently used as a source of information and research material.[5][6]
- Carlos' precise death was never directly depicted. By Chapter 341, however, the narration explicitly treats Commander Carlos as deceased.[11]
