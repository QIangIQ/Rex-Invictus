### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 119
Alexios Alioth is a High Human, the patriarch of [[Clan Alioth]], father of Natalia Elderblood Alioth, and the longtime right-hand man and Royal Advisor of [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]]. Originally a Human born into the anomalous Alioth bloodline, Alexios inherited the Clan's mysterious Galaxy Eyes, granting him extraordinary authority over Space and Time.[2][3]
Despite being a Human serving within the court of the Vampire King, Alexios became one of Vlad's most trusted companions and advisers over the centuries. Their relationship considerably exceeds that of a conventional ruler and subordinate: Alexios is descended from the First Alioth, one of Vlad's oldest Human friends, and Vlad later demonstrated that Alexios' life was important enough to him that the normally prideful Vampire King openly bowed his head to [[Victor Elderblood]] in gratitude after Victor helped save Alexios from death.[4][5][1]
Alexios is among the most accomplished Space-Time specialists of his era. His abilities allow him to create interdimensional portals, manipulate and expand space, produce spatial runes, isolate entire regions or dimensions, alter temporal flow, and perform large-scale effects capable of interfering with travel between worlds. However, the Time aspect of his power was historically extremely dangerous to use: large-scale Chronokinesis consumed his own lifespan, eventually causing Alexios to sustain himself by replacing his mortal existence with increasingly greater quantities of pure Time Energy.[3][6]
By the events of Chapter 1068, Alexios' body had ceased being ordinary flesh and had effectively become a mass of pure Time Energy. Natalia attempted to restore him through a Time Cocoon, but the process inadvertently began transforming Alexios into an existence resembling a Time Elemental. Before that transformation could become permanent, Victor stabilized Alexios' Soul and strengthened his Human side, transforming him into a High Human capable of safely retaining the Time Energy already integrated into his existence.[5][1]

### Physical description
Alexios is a tall, mature-looking man with bright golden hair who commonly dresses in an immaculate white suit. Despite his true age being measured in centuries, his appearance remained that of a comparatively young middle-aged man due to the abnormal vitality of the Alioth bloodline and his manipulation of Time.[2][3]
His most distinctive characteristic is his eyes. Alexios habitually keeps them closed, to the point that he can converse, navigate and observe his surroundings without visibly opening them. When the eyes are revealed, ordinary sclera, irises and pupils are absent. Instead, each eye resembles a miniature galaxy filled with stars and cosmic structures.[2][3]
The Galaxy Eyes are directly connected to the hereditary Space-Time power of [[Clan Alioth]]. Opening them is especially important when Alexios performs powerful temporal techniques, and their appearance marks him as one of the descendants of the First Alioth. Vlad himself was reminded of his original Human friend when looking directly into Alexios' eyes.[4]
Near the end of his original Human existence, Alexios' internal condition became radically different from his outward appearance. Centuries of extending his lifespan through Time had gradually replaced his physical body until [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] determined that Alexios was no longer fundamentally composed of flesh, but rather of pure Time Energy.[6]
After Natalia and Victor restored him, Alexios retained a living Human-based form but was transformed into a High Human. Victor prevented the Time Energy from rewriting Alexios' Soul into that of an Elemental while reinforcing his Human existence strongly enough to coexist with the Time already accumulated inside him.[1]

### Personality and traits
Alexios is calm, professional, observant and exceptionally loyal. His centuries of service beside Vlad have made him capable of interpreting the King's intentions from extremely subtle changes in expression or behavior. During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s first royal audience, Alexios stopped the guards from interfering with Victor because he noticed that Vlad was interested in seeing what the newborn Vampire would do, correctly determining his master's intentions without needing an explicit order.[7]
His loyalty should not be mistaken for blind obedience. Alexios considers advising Vlad—including openly criticizing him—to be part of his responsibility. When Vlad's neglected former partners confronted him, Alexios bluntly informed the King that the resulting hostility was entirely his own fault, listing his neglect, arrogance, greed, disdain and apathy as causes of his family's collapse.[4]
Alexios is one of the relatively few people capable of speaking to Vlad in this manner without fear. Rather than flattering his ruler, he repeatedly provides assessments Vlad does not necessarily want to hear. He reminded Vlad that several of his children could never forgive him for what happened to their mothers and advised him to repair the relationships that could still be salvaged.[4]
His relationship with Vlad has developed into something approaching familial affection. Alexios has served him for so long that he worries not only about Vlad's kingdom but about Vlad himself as a person. He believes that even an immortal ruler eventually requires rest and repeatedly encourages Vlad to prepare a successor rather than remain King indefinitely.[8]
When Vlad complained about the problems surrounding Victor, Alexios immediately seized upon Vlad saying that he was tired and enthusiastically suggested preparing [[Lucas Tepes]] as his successor. Although he maintained his usual expressionless professionalism when Vlad accused him of trying to remove him from the throne, Alexios privately admitted that this was exactly what he wanted.[8]
Alexios' loyalty also extends to Vlad's growth as a person. Centuries later, when Vlad displayed considerably more maturity regarding coexistence and politics, Alexios became emotional enough to shed tears because he felt as though he were watching someone he had helped advise for centuries finally grow beyond his old habits.[9]
He is similarly protective of his daughter Natalia Elderblood Alioth. Although Natalia eventually grew vastly stronger than her father, Alexios continues regarding her foremost as his daughter rather than simply as another powerful supernatural being.
Alexios initially maintained a largely professional relationship with Victor Elderblood, but he gradually developed considerable respect for him. Victor frequently leaves Alexios bewildered by the scale and violence of his actions, yet Alexios repeatedly recognizes that Victor possesses qualities of leadership that many older rulers lack.[10][11]
His fundamental nature remained important during his near-transformation into a Time Elemental. Victor concluded that allowing Alexios to become a Spirit-like manifestation of Time would fundamentally contradict who he was. Alexios was, in Victor's assessment, a comparatively simple man whose greatest desire was to remain beside and support his King. Becoming an Elemental would bind him to the greater laws of Creation instead.[1]

### Biography

#### Vampire Society
Alexios was introduced within the Royal Castle of [[Nightingale]] during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s first formal audience with the Vampire King. Standing directly beside Vlad's throne, Alexios reprimanded the arguing Vampire Princes and reminded them to maintain the dignity expected of royalty.[2]
His presence immediately stood out because the trusted right-hand man of the King of all Vampires was himself a Human. Alexios was identified as both Vlad's closest adviser and the father of Natalia Elderblood Alioth, establishing [[Clan Alioth]]'s unusual position within Nightingale's political hierarchy.[2]
When Victor ignored Vlad's pressure and continued approaching the throne, the Royal Guards attempted to stop him. Alexios raised his hand and ordered them to stand down. Having served Vlad for centuries, he had noticed the nearly imperceptible amusement upon the King's face and understood that Vlad wanted to see how far Victor would go.[7]
The incident established Alexios as more than merely a powerful transporter or court official. His position depended heavily upon his ability to understand Vlad's intentions and preserve the functioning of the Royal Court without requiring constant direct instruction.

#### A New Pillar
Alexios' abilities became increasingly important during the political conflict following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s rise as [[Nightingale]]'s fourth Vampire Count.
When Vlad decided to isolate Nightingale from Earth, the normal gate connecting the two worlds was completely closed. The Alioth Clan responsible for transportation ceased responding to those outside, leaving Victor and Ruby incapable of returning to Nightingale.[12][13]
The closure demonstrated the extraordinary scale at which Alioth spatial techniques could operate. Rather than merely opening personal portals, Alexios' side of the bloodline possessed the ability to interfere with the connection between entire worlds and prevent ordinary dimensional transportation from functioning.
The resulting separation lasted long enough for substantial time to pass in Victor's Human-world life before the gate finally became accessible again.[13]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Alexios later encountered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] during the aftermath of the Japanese supernatural conflict.
After Victor recovered Ophis and refused to immediately return the frightened girl to Vlad, Alexios became involved in the political dispute surrounding the Vampire Princess.[10]
The conversation developed beyond Ophis herself and became an exchange concerning kingship. Victor explained that a ruler needed to establish a clear path for his people but that, if he were ever forced to choose between his family and his kingdom, his family would take precedence. Alexios listened to the philosophy of the increasingly influential new Progenitor while evaluating the consequences Victor's presence would have for Vlad and [[Nightingale]].[10]
Alexios later participated in Victor and Vlad's discussions surrounding the development of a planned supernatural city. The conversation revealed applications of Alioth power that had previously been largely ignored because Vlad primarily used Alexios for transportation and intelligence work.[3]
Alexios explained that he could use specialized runes to enlarge the internal space of houses far beyond their external dimensions. These spaces required Energy to remain stable, and because Alexios could not personally power thousands of structures indefinitely, he proposed using condensed Mana Crystals as batteries.[3]
The potential scale surprised Victor. Alexios stated that, given sufficient Energy, he could easily construct approximately one kilometer of internal space and could potentially create an area comparable to countryside within the spatial boundaries of a single house using sufficiently powerful Mana Crystals.[3]
Victor immediately criticized Vlad for possessing someone with such useful abilities yet primarily treating him as a transportation and espionage asset.[3]
The same conversation exposed the differences between Alexios' Space and Time abilities. Spatial manipulation was comparatively straightforward and could be sustained through external Energy sources. Manipulating Time was significantly more dangerous.[3]
When Victor asked him to perform a major temporal effect, Alexios opened his Galaxy Eyes and explained the price involved. [[Clan Alioth]] members possessed unusually powerful vitality and could naturally live for extremely long periods, but using their Time ability required sacrificing their own lifespan.[3]
The hereditary ritual associated with inheriting the Galaxy Eyes allowed members of Clan Alioth to exceed the normal limits imposed upon their Human bodies, but every serious temporal manipulation still carried a personal cost.[3]
Alexios' responsibilities as Vlad's adviser continued alongside these supernatural duties. When Vlad's former wives confronted him over the consequences of centuries of neglect, Alexios refused to protect his master from criticism. He explicitly blamed Vlad's own behavior for the hostility now directed toward him and urged him to repair what remained of his family relationships.[4]

#### Demonic War
Alexios remained one of Vlad's most important support assets throughout the Demonic War.
His primary role involved using [[Clan Alioth]]'s Space abilities to rapidly transport major combatants across otherwise impossible distances and between supernatural regions. He accompanied powerful figures such as [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] through spatial gates and repeatedly acted as the Royal Court's principal means of long-distance supernatural transportation.
During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s campaign against [[Diablo]]'s forces, Alexios eventually encountered the aftermath of one of Victor's massacres. The scale of the slaughter left even the centuries-old adviser momentarily unable to understand how Victor could have exterminated so many Demonic forces within such a short period.[11]
When Alexios demanded to know what Victor had done, Victor answered simply that he had killed them "like pigs to the slaughter" and considered the massacre what was necessary.[11]
The exchange reinforced Alexios' increasingly complicated opinion of Victor. He recognized Victor as a potential stabilizing ruler and valuable ally, but also understood that his son-in-law was capable of levels of violence that even experienced supernatural politicians found disturbing.
Alexios simultaneously continued advising Vlad regarding succession. By Chapter 605, he was actively encouraging Vlad to prepare another King and reminding him that "no King rules forever".[8]
Although Vlad argued that an immortal God-King did not face the ordinary restrictions of aging, Alexios privately believed the issue was psychological rather than biological. An immortal body did not mean an immortal mind could govern forever without eventually requiring rest.[8]

#### King Among Kings
Alexios remained at Vlad's side while the scale of supernatural politics expanded beyond Earth and [[Nightingale]].
During the negotiations concerning Samar, he accompanied Vlad's faction and participated in discussions involving [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], the [[Werewolves]] and other supernatural powers.[9]
These events further demonstrated the limits of Alioth teleportation. Alexios explained that even [[Clan Alioth]] could not simply create a portal to an arbitrary unknown location. For a portal to be opened safely, the user generally needed either to have visited the destination previously or possess sufficiently precise spatial information about it.[9]
The limitation is particularly important for interplanetary and interdimensional travel. The Alioth power can cross enormous distances, but it still requires a destination that the user can meaningfully identify rather than functioning as unrestricted teleportation to any imagined location.[9]
By this period Natalia had become far stronger than her father, while Alexios' own history of using the Alioth power on enormous scales had significantly affected his longevity. Natalia's much more advanced physiology later allowed her to use the Galaxy Eyes without suffering the same mortal limitations.[9][6]
Alexios' expertise remained respected even after Victor's own powers grew far beyond conventional supernatural standards. When Victor forcibly locked the entire Greek dimension through his modified Strategic-Class [[Magic]], Alexios immediately recognized what had happened and was visibly shocked by the scale of the dimensional closure.[14]
For one of the world's foremost Space specialists to react in this manner emphasized how abnormal Victor's growing control over dimensional space had become.

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
The true consequences of Alexios' centuries of temporal manipulation became apparent much later.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] discovered that Alexios had survived far beyond the lifespan his original Human body should have possessed by continuously using his own Time powers to extend his existence.[6]
The method was fundamentally dangerous. Alexios had effectively bathed himself in pure Time Energy to prevent his mortal lifespan from reaching its natural conclusion. Whenever he used powerful Time abilities, this accumulated Energy was depleted and the mortality his power had delayed began returning.[6]
The process continued for so long that Alexios' physical existence changed completely. By Chapter 1068, Victor determined that Alexios' entire body was no longer ordinary flesh but pure Time Energy.[6]
This condition was beyond Victor's ability to casually repair despite his own Divinity over Time. Alexios had not simply suffered physical damage; his very existence had become inseparably mixed with one of Creation's fundamental forces.[6]
The investigation also revealed that the true origin of [[Clan Alioth]]'s Galaxy Eyes remained unknown.
Even Alexios, Natalia and Albedo did not know where the bloodline's abilities had actually originated. The old theory claimed that the First Alioth had experienced a mutation after coming into contact with [[Magic]], but Victor concluded that this explanation was incorrect.[6]
Victor speculated that the Eyes might have once belonged to a dead Primordial God associated with Space and Time or possibly a being originating from the Higher Sectors, but he possessed no evidence sufficient to establish either theory as fact.[6]
This meant that classifying Alioth power as ordinary Witch Magic was, at best, an incomplete historical interpretation. The Galaxy Eyes instead represented a unique hereditary authority over Space-Time whose full potential remained inaccessible even to Natalia after her evolution into a True Dragon.[6]
Alexios' condition continued deteriorating until Vlad was informed that the descendant of his oldest Human friend was dying. The situation affected Vlad deeply because the death of the First Alioth had already been one of the few losses from his ancient past that remained emotionally significant to him.[5]
Natalia intervened by placing her father inside a Time Cocoon. The technique restored Alexios' vitality and drew upon a moment in his personal timeline where his body remained healthy, effectively bringing that state into the present and maintaining it through her extraordinary connection to Time and Space.[5]
The procedure succeeded in restoring Alexios' physical condition, but an unexpected complication emerged.
The vast quantities of Time Energy surrounding Alexios began entering his Soul. Victor immediately recognized that, if the process continued, Alexios would cease being Human and become something comparable to a Time Elemental.[1]
Such a being would be almost completely synchronized with Time itself. Victor theorized that the resulting existence could perceive the recorded past through the [[Akashic Records]] and potentially observe a limited distance into possible futures, approaching a form of temporal near-omniscience.[1]
However, the transformation would fundamentally change Alexios as a person. Becoming an Elemental would bind him to the natural order and responsibilities associated with a fundamental force of Creation rather than allowing him to continue living according to his existing Human priorities.[1]
Victor knew Alexios well enough to reject that outcome.
He used his mastery over Souls to prevent the Time Energy from altering Alexios' Soul and instead reinforced Alexios' Human nature strongly enough to coexist with the temporal Energy already accumulated within him.[1]
Natalia immediately understood what Victor intended: Alexios would become a High Human.[1]
Victor confirmed that the transformation would allow Alexios' existing powers to continue becoming somewhat stronger over time without producing the extreme and personality-altering consequences that becoming a Time Spirit would have caused.[1]
Victor's reasoning was ultimately based upon Alexios' personality. Alexios was not someone who wanted to become an abstract representation of Time. He was a loyal adviser whose fundamental desire was to remain himself and continue supporting Vlad for as long as he could.[1]
Vlad's reaction demonstrated how deeply he valued his old adviser. After Alexios was saved, the ancient Vampire King lowered his pride and bowed his head toward Victor in genuine gratitude, surprising even those familiar with Vlad's normally unyielding personality.[1]
Alexios therefore entered the modern Imperial Era not as a Time Elemental, but as a High Human retaining both his individuality and the extraordinary Space-Time powers of Clan Alioth.

### Skills and abilities
Alexios is one of the greatest known Space-Time specialists originating from Earth. His abilities derive primarily from the mysterious hereditary Galaxy Eyes possessed by [[Clan Alioth]], rather than ordinary Witch [[Magic]]. Although the family was historically classified alongside Witches, later revelations established that even the Alioths themselves do not properly understand the true origin of their abilities.[3][6]

#### Space-Time Eyess
- Galaxy Eyes: Alexios possesses the hereditary supernatural eyes of [[Clan Alioth]]. When opened, his eyes contain no visible normal sclera or pupils and instead resemble living galaxies filled with stars. The Eyes act as the principal medium through which Alexios accesses his extraordinary control over Space and Time.[3]
- Space-Time Affinity: The Galaxy Eyes grant Alexios an extremely powerful innate affinity with both Space and Time. This affinity is not comparable to ordinary elemental [[Magic]] and remained mysterious even to the most knowledgeable members of the family.[6]
- Spatial Perception: Alexios possesses an exceptional ability to perceive distortions, barriers and changes involving dimensions. He immediately recognized when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] forcibly sealed the Greek dimension despite the enormous scale and complexity of the effect.[14]
- Dimensional Awareness: His familiarity with Space allows Alexios to understand dimensional coordinates and connections on a level far beyond ordinary Witches, making him one of [[Nightingale]]'s primary experts in dimensional transportation.

#### Space Manipulation
- Spatiokinesis: Alexios possesses extremely advanced control over Space. He can distort distance, create portals, enlarge enclosed spaces, isolate regions and establish dimensional barriers.[3][9]
- Portal Creation: Alexios can open spatial gates connecting distant locations almost instantaneously. These portals can cross enormous terrestrial distances and connect separate supernatural worlds or dimensions.[3][9]
- Interdimensional Travel: Alexios can use his portals to move himself and others between separate dimensions, making [[Clan Alioth]] one of [[Nightingale]]'s principal methods of transportation between Earth and supernatural territories.
- Interplanetary Travel: Alioth portals are not inherently restricted to a single planet. With the appropriate destination information, their Space manipulation is capable of bridging astronomical distances.[9]
- Portal Destination Restriction: Alexios cannot simply open a safe portal to a completely unknown location. The user generally needs to have visited the destination previously or possess sufficiently accurate information and coordinates to establish the connection.[9]
- Spatial Expansion: Alexios can expand the internal dimensions of an enclosed location so that its interior is vastly larger than the structure's external dimensions.[3]
- Expanded-Space Construction: Through specialized Alioth runes, Alexios can construct houses and rooms containing enormously enlarged internal spaces. Given sufficient Energy, he stated that producing approximately one kilometer of internal space would be easy for him.[3]
- Large-Scale Spatial Expansion: With sufficiently powerful Mana Crystals acting as an external Energy source, Alexios stated that he could potentially place an area comparable to countryside inside the spatial boundaries of a house.[3]
- Pocket Space Creation: His spatial techniques allow him to produce artificially expanded or isolated spaces functioning similarly to pocket dimensions.
- Spatial Runes: Alexios can encode his Space manipulation into specialized runes rather than needing to personally maintain every effect. These runes can permanently or semi-permanently sustain expanded spaces when supplied with an adequate Energy source.[3]
- Spatial Energy Substitution: Alexios can personally fuel portals and spatial distortions with his own Energy, but permanent large-scale applications require an external source. Mana Crystals can function as batteries for these constructs.[3]
- Dimensional Isolation: Alexios is capable of separating locations from normal dimensional access and establishing barriers around extremely large regions.
- Dimensional Lockdown: Clan Alioth's abilities can prevent ordinary entry into or exit from a dimensional region. Alexios' powers were involved in the period during which the passage connecting Earth and Nightingale was completely closed.[12][13]
- World-Gate Sealing: Alexios can interfere with established dimensional gateways strongly enough to prevent supernatural beings outside the target world from using their usual transportation routes.[13]

#### Time Manipulation
- Chronokinesis: Alexios possesses direct control over Time through his Galaxy Eyes. He can alter temporal flow within targeted regions and perform effects impossible through conventional [[Magic]].[3]
- Time Dilation: Alexios can manipulate the rate at which Time passes inside a location relative to the outside world.
- Time Slow: He can dramatically slow the progression of Time over large areas. His temporal abilities have been used on scales far beyond ordinary combat applications.
- Temporal Isolation: Alexios can combine Space and Time manipulation to isolate an area from ordinary temporal progression.
- Large-Scale Time Manipulation: The scale of Alexios' abilities is sufficient for temporal manipulation involving extremely large territories rather than merely individual targets. However, such applications historically demanded a severe price from his Human body.[3]
- Lifespan Conversion: Before becoming a High Human, Alexios' temporal powers were directly connected to his lifespan. Using major Time techniques could consume portions of his remaining life.[3]
- Life Extension Through Time: Alexios eventually inverted this relationship and used Time Energy to continuously extend his own existence beyond the point at which his mortal body should naturally have died.[6]
- Mortality Suppression: By bathing his body in pure Time Energy, Alexios prevented his natural Human lifespan from reaching its conclusion. This allowed him to survive years beyond the point at which he otherwise should have died.[6]
- Time-Energy Physiology (Formerly unstable): Near the end of his original Human life, so much Time Energy had replaced his mortal body that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] determined Alexios was effectively composed entirely of pure Time Energy rather than conventional flesh.[6]

#### Runic Expertise
- Runic [[Magic]]: Alexios possesses advanced knowledge of runes specifically designed to reproduce the spatial effects of [[Clan Alioth]]'s powers.[3]
- Runic Enchantment: He can apply spatial properties to buildings and other structures through runes, allowing the effect to persist without Alexios continuously remaining nearby.[3]
- Magical Engineering: Alexios is capable of combining his hereditary Space manipulation with Mana-powered technology and magical materials to create practical supernatural infrastructure.[3]

#### High Human Abilities
Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s intervention in Chapter 1079, Alexios became a High Human.[1]
- High Human Physiology: Alexios possesses the superior physiology of a High Human, reinforcing the Human aspect of his existence beyond the limitations of his former mortal body.[1]
- Enhanced Physical Attributes: His transformed body is physically superior to that of an ordinary Human and capable of safely containing the enormous quantity of Time Energy already integrated into his existence.
- Enhanced Vitality: Alexios' High Human body possesses vastly greater vitality than his original Human physiology, removing the immediate physical collapse caused by centuries of manipulating Time.
- Agelessness: As a High Human, Alexios is no longer limited by the ordinary biological lifespan that originally forced him to continually extend his existence through Chronokinesis.
- Enhanced Adaptability: Victor strengthened Alexios' Human side specifically so that his body and Soul could adapt to the Time Energy already present rather than being rewritten by it.[1]
- Soul Stabilization: Victor used his mastery over Souls to prevent the accumulated Time Energy from transforming Alexios' Soul into that of a Time Elemental.[1]
- Progressive Space-Time Growth: Victor stated that Alexios' powers would continue becoming somewhat stronger following his stabilization, although his growth would remain far less extreme than the hypothetical power he would have obtained as a complete Time Spirit.[1]

#### Other Skills
- Supernatural Knowledge: Having served the Vampire King for centuries, Alexios possesses extensive knowledge concerning [[Nightingale]], Vampires, Witches, dimensional travel and the wider supernatural world.
- Political Expertise: Alexios has advised Vlad through centuries of Nightingale politics and is capable of analyzing both immediate political developments and the long-term consequences of a ruler's behavior.[4][8]
- Leadership: As patriarch of [[Clan Alioth]] and Vlad's Royal Advisor, Alexios commands considerable political authority within Nightingale.
- Observation and Insight: Alexios is exceptionally skilled at reading Vlad's intentions. He can understand the King's desires through minor changes in behavior without requiring explicit instructions.[7]
- Strategic Judgment: Alexios regularly advises Vlad against decisions motivated by pride or stagnation and actively advocates succession planning and institutional change when he believes they are necessary.[4][8]

### Weaknesses
- Destination Knowledge Requirement: Alexios' portals require meaningful information regarding their destination. Attempting to teleport to a completely unknown location without accurate spatial data is not normally possible.[9]
- High Energy Consumption: Large or persistent spatial effects require immense quantities of Energy. Although Alexios can personally power temporary portals, large permanent projects require external sources such as Mana Crystals.[3]
- Former Lifespan Cost: Before becoming a High Human, powerful manipulation of Time consumed Alexios' lifespan, making repeated large-scale use inherently dangerous.[3]
- Former Mortal Limitation: Alexios' original Human body could not safely withstand the true potential of the Galaxy Eyes. His mortality restricted how extensively he could exploit Time compared with Natalia after she became a Dragon.[6]
- Time-Energy Destabilization (Formerly): Alexios' attempt to indefinitely extend his life through Time gradually replaced his mortal body and eventually threatened to transform both his body and Soul into an Elemental existence.[6][1]

### Trivia
- Alexios is a descendant of the First Alioth, one of [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]]' oldest Human friends.[4][5]
- Vlad's relationship with Alexios extends considerably beyond an ordinary master-servant relationship. Vlad's helplessness over Alexios' deteriorating condition reminded him of the grief he experienced when the First Alioth died.[5]
- Despite serving the King of Vampires, Alexios was originally Human.[2]
- Alexios' eyes resemble galaxies and normally remain closed. Opening them is especially associated with the use of his Time abilities.[3]
- The true origin of the Galaxy Eyes remains unknown. Even Alexios, Natalia and Albedo did not know where [[Clan Alioth]] originally obtained them.[6]
- The traditional explanation that the First Alioth developed the power as a simple mutation after interacting with [[Magic]] was eventually revealed to be incorrect.[6]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] theorized that the Eyes might originate from a deceased Primordial God of Space-Time or a powerful being from the Higher Sectors, but he explicitly lacked enough information to confirm either possibility.[6]
- Because of this revelation, describing Alioth power simply as ordinary Witch Magic is inaccurate. Their hereditary Space-Time authority is a unique ability whose actual origin remains unresolved.[6]
- Alexios' spatial abilities were so versatile that Victor criticized Vlad for wasting much of their potential by primarily using him as transportation and an intelligence operative.[3]
- Alexios secretly wants Vlad to retire and establish a successor, believing that an immortal mind requires rest even when the body itself does not age.[8]
- Despite his desire for Vlad to retire, Alexios remains extraordinarily loyal to him. Victor specifically cited this loyalty when deciding that becoming a Time Spirit would be fundamentally incompatible with Alexios' personality.[1]
- Alexios never actually became a Time Elemental. Natalia's treatment began pushing him toward that transformation, but Victor prevented his Soul from changing and instead transformed him into a High Human.[1]
- Had the Elemental transformation continued, Victor theorized that Alexios could have become completely synchronized with Time and gained the ability to observe the recorded past of the [[Akashic Records]] and limited portions of the immediate future.[1]
- Victor deliberately rejected this outcome because such an existence would be bound to the natural order of Creation and could fundamentally alter Alexios' personality.[1]
- After becoming a High Human, Alexios retained the Time Energy already integrated into his existence, and Victor predicted that his abilities would gradually strengthen with time.[1]
- Vlad was so grateful to Victor for saving Alexios that he bowed his head toward him, an exceptionally rare display of humility from the Vampire King.[1]
