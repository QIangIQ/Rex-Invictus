### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 81: Sly Fox
Tell Vlad Dracul Tepes, I, Radu Tepes, will return to get my revenge, and when I return, he will not be able to protect himself behind the back of his beloved Progenitor forever...
—Radu Tepes to [[Oda Blank]], Chapter 870
Radu Tepes, formerly known as Niklaus Horseman, is an ancient Noble Vampire, the leader of the [[Horseman Clan]], a former Vampire Count of [[Nightingale]], and one of the principal conspirators associated with [[New Dawn]]. He is the biological younger brother of the original [[Vlad III Tepes]], the historical Vlad the Impaler, whose existence was subsequently absorbed by the present Vampire Progenitor. Radu's resentment over his brother's fate developed into a centuries-long vendetta against the Vampire King, motivating an extensive series of political conspiracies, supernatural experiments, and alliances intended to weaken Vlad's position before eventually destroying him.[2][3]
Operating under the identity of Niklaus Horseman, Radu rose to prominence within Vampire society after obtaining the Count title previously held by [[Annasthashia Elderblood Fulger]]. His victory in a wager against the former Countess allowed Clan Horseman to replace Clan Fulger among Nightingale's ruling families, granting him considerable political influence and access to resources that could support his wider ambitions. His temporary reign ended when Annasthashia, after recovering her original personality as Natashia, challenged him to reclaim the position. Although Radu possessed unusually dangerous abilities and had prepared methods of surviving attacks capable of destroying his entire body, he deliberately surrendered after [[Victor Elderblood]] intervened in the confrontation, recognizing that continuing the battle would expose him to risks that outweighed the value of retaining his title.[8][9][10][4]
Beyond his public position, Radu was deeply involved in New Dawn, an organization whose membership extended across several supernatural races and political factions. His collaborators included James, Fenir, individuals associated with [[Diablo]], and representatives connected to the [[Elder Gods Pantheon]]. Through these relationships, he gained access to information, experimental research, and supernatural resources far beyond those ordinarily controlled by a single Noble Vampire Clan. His activities included experiments involving the Blood of Vampire Progenitors, the development of unusual Hybrid beings, and participation in conspiracies capable of threatening Vlad's authority and the existing supernatural political order.[2][11]
Although Radu repeatedly opposed Victor, his personal hatred remained directed toward Vlad. He regarded the younger Progenitor as unpredictable and increasingly dangerous, but also recognized that Victor's rise had inadvertently accomplished several of Radu's own political objectives by reducing the Vampire King's influence over the Count Clans. This unusual perspective allowed him to appreciate Victor's actions even while cooperating with organizations seeking his death.[2]
Following Victor's declaration of war against New Dawn, Radu abandoned the organization and escaped through a mysterious portal after entrusting his biological daughter, [[Jessica Horseman Snow]], to the protection of her maternal family. During his escape, he publicly revealed his true identity and declared that he would eventually return to avenge his brother. His survival was confirmed much later when he appeared among an unidentified faction operating within the Upper Sectors, having undergone a supernatural transformation that left his body enveloped in Darkness and his eyes possessing an inhuman appearance. Despite his association with a powerful civilization beyond the native Sector, Radu continued pursuing revenge against Vlad and openly warned his new associates against underestimating Victor Elderblood.[7][1]

### Physical Appearance
Radu's original appearance as Niklaus Horseman was that of a tall, muscular man standing approximately 190 centimeters in height. His physique was sufficiently developed that his physical strength remained apparent even beneath the aristocratic clothing he habitually wore. His fashion resembled that of a medieval nobleman, reinforcing the impression of a man whose appearance had changed very little despite centuries of supernatural existence. His skin was pale, his hair black, and his eyes possessed an exceptionally cold and lifeless quality that made his expression disturbing even when he remained completely composed.[5]
His appearance changed drastically when he released his Vampire Count transformation. His body became almost entirely black, with dark-gray skin, elongated claws, large bat-like wings and a spiked tail. His eyes turned blood red, while his increasingly monstrous physique reflected the corruptive supernatural characteristics of the Horseman Bloodline. The transformation was accompanied by the manifestation of a black, liquid-like substance that played an important role in his ability to survive physical destruction and reconstruct himself from separated portions of his body.[9][6]
By the time Radu reappeared in the Upper Sectors, his physical appearance had undergone another substantial transformation. His entire body was shrouded in an unidentified Darkness, obscuring many of his former physical features. His eyes were no longer recognizable as those of an ordinary Noble Vampire, possessing completely black sclera surrounding red pupils. The resulting appearance was distinctly monstrous, suggesting a fundamental alteration of his supernatural constitution. Whether this transformation represents an evolution of his original Vampiric Bloodline, the influence of a foreign Entity, or another process remains unknown.[1]

### Personality
Radu is an exceptionally intelligent, calculating, patient, and secretive individual who prefers careful manipulation over direct confrontation. His reputation as the Sneaky Fox reflects his tendency to conceal information, observe opponents from a distance, and prepare contingencies before committing himself to a dangerous situation. Unlike warriors who regard personal pride and the satisfaction of combat as important principles, Radu evaluates confrontations according to their strategic value. He is willing to retreat, surrender, or sacrifice political influence when doing so preserves his opportunity to pursue a more important objective. His surrender to Natashia demonstrated this mentality particularly clearly: although he still possessed additional means of continuing the battle, he recognized that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s interference and Vlad's willingness to tolerate it had transformed the confrontation into an increasingly dangerous political trap.[10][4]
His outward composure conceals considerable perceptiveness. Radu frequently recognizes developments that others overlook, particularly changes in the behavior and motivations of powerful individuals. During the conspiracy involving the assassination attempt against Victor, he understood the danger represented by the younger Progenitor's temperament more clearly than [[Theo Tepes]]. Victor's unpredictability, rapid development, and increasingly close relationship with [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] threatened to create an enemy whose strength could eventually exceed the expectations of established Vampire society. Radu recognized that provoking such individuals without understanding their personalities was an extraordinarily dangerous mistake.[12]
Radu's willingness to abandon unfavorable positions is not accompanied by a lack of ambition. He is capable of pursuing extraordinarily complicated objectives across centuries, patiently assembling resources, cultivating alliances, and waiting for circumstances that might allow his plans to succeed. His political ambitions toward the Fulger and Snow Clans were not isolated attempts to acquire wealth or prestige, but part of a much larger strategy intended to weaken Vlad's effective control over [[Nightingale]]. Even after losing the Count title, he continued searching for alternative means of accomplishing his objectives rather than surrendering his vendetta.[2][7]
His greatest motivation is his hatred of Vlad, which originated from the disappearance of his biological elder brother following the Vampire Progenitor's absorption of the original [[Vlad III Tepes]]. This resentment gradually became the central purpose of his existence. Unlike an ordinary political rivalry, his vendetta is intensely personal and persists regardless of changes in his circumstances. Radu has demonstrated that he is willing to abandon entire organizations, sacrifice centuries of preparation, and flee beyond his native Universe if necessary, provided he remains alive long enough to eventually confront the man he blames for his brother's fate.[7][3]
Despite his ruthlessness, Radu is not incapable of affection. His relationship with Jessica reveals a protective and genuinely paternal aspect of his personality. Although he initially intended to exploit her inherited [[Snow Bloodline]] for political advantage, he ultimately abandoned that ambition and prioritized her survival when [[New Dawn]] came under attack. His willingness to entrust her to the Snow family, despite his history of hostility toward Nightingale's ruling Clans, demonstrates that his attachment to Jessica could outweigh his desire for political control. He also understands the personalities of his enemies well enough to recognize that Victor's extreme protectiveness toward his family made him a more reliable guardian for Jessica than many of Radu's former allies.[2][7]
His attitude toward Victor is unusually complicated. Although Radu cooperated with conspirators attempting to kill the younger Progenitor, he openly admitted that he did not genuinely hate him and even found him somewhat likable. Victor repeatedly destroyed Radu's plans, but his growing influence over Nightingale also weakened Vlad's authority and created a second center of political power within the Kingdom. Radu consequently regarded Victor as an enemy who had unintentionally accomplished much of what he had been attempting for centuries. This appreciation never prevented him from plotting against Victor when their objectives became incompatible.[2]
His cautious temperament remained unchanged after his arrival in the Upper Sectors. Although his new associates belonged to an ancient civilization whose territory extended across hundreds of galaxies, Radu did not allow their confidence to distort his judgment. He openly warned them that Victor was capable of destroying their forces and potentially devouring their entire faction, recognizing that the [[Chaos]] Dragon God could not be evaluated through ordinary assumptions concerning the relative strength of civilizations. When other participants mocked his assessment, Radu simply ignored them, considering his responsibility fulfilled once he had delivered the warning. His restraint reflected the same survival-oriented mentality that had characterized his conduct throughout his previous life in Nightingale.[1]

### Background
Radu was originally a Human born into the family of the historical [[Vlad III Tepes]], who would later become known as Vlad the Impaler. Unlike his elder brother, Radu possessed an exceptionally weak constitution and suffered from severe physical frailty. The original Vlad maintained a relationship with the Being who would eventually become [[Nightingale]]'s Vampire King and requested that he transform Radu into a Vampire. The transformation saved Radu from his previous weakness and granted him the considerably longer existence associated with Noble Vampires.[3]
The relationship between the original Vlad and the Vampire Progenitor eventually ended when the latter absorbed the former's existence. As a Vampire Progenitor, Vlad possessed the ability to consume Souls and incorporate individuals he considered sufficiently important into his own existence. The original Vlad III Tepes became part of the Progenitor through this process, creating an unusual situation in which the present Vampire King contained Radu's biological brother without literally being the same individual who had been born alongside him. Vlad later acknowledged that describing Radu as his brother was therefore simultaneously correct and incorrect.[3]
Vlad suspected that Radu might have witnessed the absorption, although the precise circumstances surrounding Radu's discovery of his brother's fate remain uncertain. Regardless of how he learned what had happened, Radu came to regard the Vampire Progenitor as the Being responsible for taking his brother from him. This grievance developed into a vendetta that would influence his activities for centuries.[3]
Radu eventually adopted the identity Niklaus Horseman and established himself within Nightingale's Noble Vampire society. His appearance and supernatural presence had changed sufficiently that the present Vlad failed to recognize him as the Human he had transformed long before. The disguise allowed Radu to interact with the Vampire King's political system without immediately exposing his true identity or the personal motive behind his hostility toward the monarchy.[3]
Over time, Radu accumulated political connections and supernatural resources, including access to the Blood of the Vampire Progenitor. His research contributed to the creation of [[Einer Horseman]] and [[Zwei Horseman]], experimental beings produced using Vlad's Blood rather than through ordinary biological reproduction. He also became the father of [[Jessica Horseman Snow]] through his relationship with [[Alienova Constantine]], whose ancestry connected her to the main Snow family. These relationships placed several extraordinary Noble Vampire bloodlines within Radu's sphere of influence and provided additional opportunities for his political ambitions.[7][3]
His rise to the position of Vampire Count originated from a wager against Annasthashia Fulger involving a rare Vampire–Werewolf Hybrid. By defeating the Countess under the agreed conditions, Radu obtained the title and territory associated with Clan Fulger, allowing Clan Horseman to temporarily replace it among Nightingale's ruling families. The victory gave him considerable political authority, but also placed him in direct opposition to the Fulger family and its allies, creating a conflict that would eventually draw [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] into his schemes.[8]

### Synopsis

#### Vampire Society
Radu entered the narrative publicly as Niklaus Horseman, the newly established Vampire Count whose Clan had replaced the Fulger family as one of [[Nightingale]]'s ruling houses. His title originated from an earlier wager involving Annasthashia Fulger and a rare Vampire–Werewolf Hybrid controlled by Clan Horseman. The former Countess' defeat transferred her political position to Niklaus, leaving the Fulger family without the title that had traditionally established its position among Nightingale's four principal Noble Clans.[8]
The dispute resurfaced when [[Victoria Fulger Elderblood|Victoria Rider]] arranged another competition against Clan Horseman. The proposed wager placed Victoria's enormous commercial fortune against Niklaus' Count title, creating an opportunity for the Fulger family to reclaim its position without requiring Annasthashia to fight personally. Both sides selected representatives rather than participating directly: Victoria relied upon [[Tatsuya Rider]], while Niklaus chose [[Einer Horseman]]. The event attracted considerable attention because its outcome could once again alter the Kingdom's established political hierarchy.[13][14]
Niklaus appeared before the assembled Vampires as an imposing aristocrat whose cold demeanor contrasted with the more expressive personalities of the other nobles. His unusual appearance immediately attracted [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s attention, although the younger Vampire had not yet developed a complete understanding of the danger represented by the [[Horseman Clan]]. Niklaus' calm behavior reflected his preference for allowing subordinates and carefully prepared representatives to act on his behalf rather than exposing his abilities unnecessarily.[5]
The competition began with Tatsuya confronting Einer, whose abilities demonstrated the unusual characteristics associated with the Horseman family's experimental Bloodline. Their battle became an important public demonstration of the capabilities possessed by both families' younger members.[15][16]
The confrontation changed when Victor entered the competition under a separate wager arranged by [[Scathach]]. Rather than merely watch the representatives fight, Victor confronted both Einer and Tatsuya, demonstrating an abnormal combination of inherited Vampire abilities and combat potential for someone who had only recently entered supernatural society. His participation exposed the difference between his rapid development and the capabilities expected of ordinary young Noble Vampires, leaving the assembled observers with a considerably greater appreciation of the danger he might eventually represent.[17]
The result did not restore Clan Fulger's Count title because Victor's participation belonged to a separate arrangement rather than the original wager between Victoria and Niklaus. Clan Horseman therefore retained its position within Nightingale's ruling hierarchy, while the consequences of the competition further strengthened Victor's growing reputation among the Kingdom's nobility.[18]

#### A New Pillar
Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s recognition as the Fifth Vampire Count, Radu became involved in an operation intended to investigate the young Progenitor's behavior and combat capabilities. Working with [[Theo Tepes]], he participated in a conspiracy involving Clan [[Amon]], an organization specializing in assassination, and Clan Astharoth, whose members operated as information brokers. Their objective was not simply to eliminate Victor through an ordinary assassination attempt, but also to observe the response of an increasingly influential Vampire whose temperament remained difficult to predict.[19][12]
The operation produced catastrophic consequences for the participating Clans. Victor captured the assassins sent against him, identified their affiliation, and retaliated by destroying Clan Amon with his Snow Fire. During the destruction, he detected a black magical owl being used to observe the events remotely and deliberately addressed the individual responsible before eliminating the construct. [[Scathach]] separately traced the conspiracy to Clan Astharoth and destroyed the organization after discovering another observation construct associated with the same operation.[19][12]
Niklaus and Theo observed the outcome from a distance. While Theo was disturbed by the destruction of their assets, Niklaus recognized a more important danger: Victor possessed the same uncompromising mentality that had made Scathach almost impossible to manipulate throughout Vampire history. His increasing strength, personal attachment to Scathach, and refusal to tolerate attacks upon his family made him an opponent whose future development could become disastrous for anyone attempting to control him through conventional political methods.[12]
Meanwhile, Natashia began preparing to reclaim the Count title lost to Clan Horseman. Having recovered the personality that had previously been suppressed within her, she approached Vlad to determine whether the King would permit another challenge for the position. Vlad informed her that no special royal permission was required and reminded her of the ancient principle governing Vampire society: the strong possessed the right to determine the outcome of their conflicts. Natashia consequently began arranging a formal wager against Niklaus.[20]
Natashia offered the Fulger family's Divine Treasure as the stake necessary to secure a rematch, placing an inheritance of enormous value against the Count title held by Clan Horseman. Niklaus accepted the wager, and the confrontation was scheduled to occur three days later. Before the battle, he also arranged for members of his family to evacuate [[Nightingale]], demonstrating that he anticipated the possibility of circumstances extending beyond an ordinary formal duel.[21][22]
The rematch attracted considerable attention from Nightingale's ruling families. Natashia entered with the intention of reclaiming her former title and restoring Clan Fulger's political standing, while Niklaus prepared to confront an opponent possessing extraordinary speed, destructive lightning and centuries of combat experience. Both understood that defeat could result in death, and the arena was reinforced to withstand the power expected from two ancient Nobles fighting seriously.[22][23]
Natashia initially dominated the confrontation through her overwhelming speed and destructive power. Her lightning repeatedly destroyed Niklaus' visible body, seemingly reducing him to a condition from which an ordinary Noble Vampire could not recover. However, Niklaus had anticipated that his opponent would rely upon direct physical destruction and had prepared countermeasures against precisely that strategy. Before the battle reached its most dangerous stages, he had concealed portions of his body beneath the arena, allowing him to regenerate from surviving fragments even after attacks that appeared to annihilate him completely.[24][9]
As Natashia continued attacking, Niklaus spread additional fragments of his body throughout the battlefield and gradually introduced his corruptive supernatural substance into the confrontation. His abilities began impairing her physical condition, interfering with her movement and weakening her regeneration. Although Natashia continued demonstrating superior speed and direct combat power, she became increasingly vulnerable as the effects accumulated. Niklaus eventually released his monstrous Count transformation, revealing dark skin, claws, large bat-like wings and a spiked tail while his opponent struggled against the growing corruption.[9][6]
Niklaus eventually struck Natashia with an attack that pierced her heart and placed her life in immediate danger. The crisis forced Natashia and Annasthashia to confront one another within their shared consciousness, gradually recognizing that their divided personalities could cooperate rather than continue struggling for exclusive control of their existence. Victor returned during the climax of the duel and intervened after recognizing the danger facing Sasha's mother.[6][25]
Victor supplied Natashia with his Blood, rapidly strengthening her regeneration and allowing her to recover from the debilitating effects of Niklaus' corruption. The intervention fundamentally altered the balance of the duel. Niklaus immediately challenged the legality of Victor's actions, pointing out that assistance from an outside participant violated the conditions under which the fight had been arranged. Rather than ignore the political implications, he recognized that Victor's interference had provided an opportunity to abandon a confrontation whose outcome had become increasingly uncertain.[25][10]
Niklaus ultimately surrendered. Although he still possessed unrevealed abilities and additional means of surviving Natashia's attacks, he considered retaining the Count title less important than avoiding unnecessary exposure and possible destruction. By invoking the authority of Vlad and the principle that the strongest Being present could determine how the rules were enforced, he placed the King in a position where refusing an openly declared surrender would carry considerable political consequences. Vlad accepted the decision, transferring the Count title and former Fulger territory back to Natashia.[10][4]
The surrender left Victor furious. He regarded Niklaus' withdrawal as cowardice and considered his conduct incompatible with the pride expected of a genuine warrior. His anger also reflected the earlier attempt against his family, which he connected to Niklaus after recognizing similarities between the Count's supernatural power and the black owl used to observe Clan Amon's destruction. Victor confronted him personally and made clear that surrendering the Count title had not resolved their conflict.[4]
Natashia regained the position of Vampire Count, restoring Clan Fulger among Nightingale's ruling families. However, Scathach and Agnes remained suspicious because they recognized that Niklaus had deliberately concealed part of his true capabilities. His defeat therefore represented the collapse of his immediate political position rather than the complete destruction of the threat he posed.[26]
Following the confrontation, Niklaus met Theo outside Nightingale and discussed the failure of their plans. Both recognized that Victor's development had become increasingly dangerous, while several projects involving supernatural experimentation had failed to achieve their intended results. Their surviving research included Experiment [[Nero Elderblood|Number Zero]], an undertaking whose possible applications extended beyond the original Hybrid experiments toward weapons capable of affecting entire supernatural populations. Niklaus understood that the rapidly changing political situation left them with fewer opportunities to act before Victor's influence became overwhelming.[27]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Despite losing the Count title, Radu remained involved in conspiracies threatening [[Nightingale]]'s political stability. His previous activities had created connections among members of the Royal Family, the Noble Vampire Clans and several organizations operating beyond the Kingdom's ordinary political structure. These networks continued influencing events even when Niklaus was no longer publicly active as one of the ruling Counts.[28]
The instability surrounding Clan Fulger and Nightingale's Royal Family continued developing while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] expanded his influence across the supernatural world. Earlier conspiracies and betrayals contributed to the political crisis surrounding the Fulger sisters and the Vampire King's authority, creating further opportunities for factions seeking to weaken the established monarchy.[29][30]
Radu's wider ambitions extended beyond the temporary possession of Fulger territory. His relationship with [[Alienova Constantine]] had produced a daughter carrying hereditary connections to Clan Snow, providing a potential means of expanding his influence over another of Nightingale's major Noble Vampire families. Had his control over Clan Fulger remained secure, Jessica's ancestry could have offered a second avenue through which he might gradually reduce Vlad's political authority. Victor's repeated interference ultimately forced Radu to abandon the original sequence of these plans.[7]

#### Demonic War
Radu's role within [[New Dawn]] became increasingly important as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s enemies began recognizing the danger posed by his abnormal growth. During a secret meeting, Niklaus gathered several of the organization's principal collaborators, including James, Fenir and [[Bael]], to discuss the consequences of allowing Victor Alucard to continue developing without opposition. He argued that Victor's death had become a strategic priority because his unpredictable behavior threatened plans that had required centuries of preparation.[2]
Radu identified several reasons Victor was especially dangerous. His actions could not be reliably predicted through established political customs, his supernatural growth was accelerating at an abnormal rate, and his influence had expanded beyond [[Nightingale]] through relationships with individuals and factions possessing extraordinary power. Victor had also become increasingly proactive, seeking out enemies and interfering in their activities rather than merely defending himself when threatened. [[Diablo]]'s desire to eliminate him further reinforced the conclusion that New Dawn could not continue treating Alucard as a temporary inconvenience.[2]
The discussion also exposed the importance of James' experimental research. The former Inquisition General possessed considerable knowledge concerning supernatural biology and had investigated the origins of Victor's extraordinary existence, including the relationship between his rare Blood and his transformation into a Vampire Progenitor. Although attempts to reproduce Victor's circumstances had not produced the desired results, the research strengthened New Dawn's belief that he represented an unprecedented threat whose future development might eventually exceed the combined expectations of its members.[2]
Radu entrusted James, whom he addressed as the organization's Second Seat, with responsibility for preparing the operation against Victor. He recognized that confronting Alucard directly without reliable information or carefully prepared countermeasures would be extraordinarily dangerous, especially given the rapid development of Victor's powers and his increasingly powerful personal alliances.[2]
Radu's private conversation with Jessica revealed another important component of the conspiracy. Vlad had already been affected by a poison exploiting several of the Vampire race's most dangerous weaknesses. The condition weakened the Vampire Progenitor sufficiently to prevent him from exercising his complete power, although his extraordinary regeneration would eventually overcome its effects. Radu estimated that Vlad could recover within approximately two years, leaving New Dawn with a limited period in which to exploit the King's weakened condition.[2]
Jessica also questioned her father's hostility toward Victor, expecting him to express the hatred ordinarily associated with their repeated conflicts. Radu instead admitted that he did not truly hate Alucard and even regarded him with a degree of appreciation. Victor's rise had weakened Vlad's political authority by attracting the Count Clans toward a second powerful Progenitor, accomplishing much of what Radu had spent centuries attempting through his own schemes. He recognized the contradiction but remained committed to removing Victor if his existence continued threatening their plans.[2]

#### King Among Kings
Following the destruction of [[Diablo]] and the absorption of several important members of the Demonic faction, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] gained access to information exposing additional details about [[New Dawn]]. Through [[Bael]]'s memories, he discovered that the Demon had infiltrated the organization on Diablo's behalf and identified Niklaus, James and Fenir among its most important members. The intelligence also revealed the location of one of New Dawn's bases and confirmed repeated contact between the organization and messengers associated with the Elder Gods.[11]
The discovery established that Victor's enemies had maintained a network of supernatural cooperation extending across several factions that ordinarily possessed incompatible interests. New Dawn's research concerning Hybrid blood, supernatural poisons and experimental organisms made the organization particularly dangerous because its methods could potentially affect entire populations rather than merely producing individual assassins. Its connections to the Elder Gods also raised concerns regarding the nature of the additional resources and supernatural knowledge available to its members.[11]
Victor eventually declared war against several organizations that had threatened his family, including the [[Egyptian Pantheon]], the Elder Gods and New Dawn. He openly identified Niklaus and James among the enemies he intended to confront, ending the possibility that Radu could indefinitely rely upon secrecy to avoid retaliation.[7]
Radu immediately understood that defending New Dawn's headquarters against Victor's forces would be suicidal. Rather than attempt to preserve the organization through a direct confrontation, he chose a strategic retreat. He informed James that his ultimate objective remained vengeance against his brother and that he was willing to flee as far as another Universe if necessary. As long as he survived, his plans could continue; if he died defending an organization that had already attracted Victor's full attention, centuries of preparation would become meaningless.[7]
Before escaping, Radu encountered Jessica, who refused to accompany him to the destination he had prepared. Recognizing that he could not force her to accept the dangers awaiting him, he finally revealed the truth of her maternal ancestry. Jessica's mother was [[Alienova Constantine]], the illegitimate daughter of [[Alienova Snow]], who belonged to the Snow family's main bloodline and was related to the family of [[Agnes Elderblood Snow]]. Jessica had therefore inherited two powerful Noble Vampire bloodlines naturally, unlike Einer and Zwei, whose existences originated from experiments conducted with Vlad's Blood.[7]
Radu entrusted Jessica with a protective collar that had belonged to her mother. The artifact was an ancient [[Snow Clan]] relic created using the heart of the Dragon [[Fafnir]], while the corresponding sword contained the Dragon's Soul. Although the sword was considered the more powerful of the two artifacts individually, the collar possessed considerable supernatural value and could be used properly only by someone belonging to the Snow family's main lineage. When employed together, the relics were reputed to allow their wielder to manipulate fire in a manner resembling Fafnir himself.[7]
The collar also provided Jessica with proof of her heritage and a means of obtaining protection from the Snow Clan. Radu recognized that his earlier ambition to use Jessica's Bloodline to control the family had become irrelevant. Despite his hostility toward Victor, he understood that the Dragon's obsessive protectiveness toward his family made him the person most likely to ensure Jessica's safety. He therefore considered [[Nightingale]] a safer destination for his daughter than the unknown territory to which he intended to escape.[7]
When New Dawn's headquarters came under attack, Radu deliberately struck Jessica unconscious, activating the collar's protective mechanism. Flames enveloped her body, her inherited Snow characteristics manifested, and she disappeared from the collapsing headquarters before the approaching forces could reach her.[7]
Radu then entered his monstrous Vampire Count transformation and attempted to escape. [[Oda Blank]] and members of the [[Blank Clan]] intercepted him, attacking normally vital areas such as his heart and brain. However, Radu survived because he had developed countermeasures against conventional Vampiric weaknesses, allowing him to continue moving despite injuries that would ordinarily be fatal even to powerful Noble Vampires.[7]
Reaching a prepared escape method, Radu destroyed a mysterious black egg-like object. A dark-red portal manifested behind him, distorting the surrounding Space while tentacle-like appendages extended from the opening and seized his body. The supernatural influence associated with the portal prevented Oda's assassins from interfering effectively, allowing Radu to escape despite their attempts to capture him.[7]
Before disappearing, he discarded the identity of Niklaus Horseman and publicly announced his true name. Addressing Oda, he declared himself Radu Tepes and ordered the assassin to deliver a message to Vlad: he would eventually return to complete his revenge, and the Vampire King would not be able to hide behind Victor's protection forever.[7]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Radu's true identity became the subject of a direct conversation between [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Vlad after the collapse of [[New Dawn]]'s operations. Victor questioned the Vampire Progenitor about the name Radu Tepes, prompting Vlad to reveal the existence of the original [[Vlad III Tepes]] and his sickly younger brother. The Vampire King admitted that he had transformed Radu into a Noble Vampire at the original Vlad's request because the young Human had possessed an exceptionally weak constitution.[3]
Vlad also clarified the complicated relationship between himself and the historical Vlad the Impaler. The original Vlad had been absorbed through the Progenitor's ability to consume and incorporate another existence, meaning that the present Vampire King possessed aspects of Radu's brother without literally being the same individual who had originally been born alongside him. Vlad consequently acknowledged that describing Radu as his brother was both correct and incorrect.[3]
Victor questioned why Vlad had failed to recognize Niklaus during their earlier encounters. Vlad explained that Radu's appearance and supernatural presence had changed considerably since his original transformation, preventing him from identifying the Noble Vampire as the person he had known long ago. Vlad further suspected that Radu had witnessed the absorption of his brother, providing a possible explanation for the intensity of his resentment, although the precise circumstances remained uncertain.[3]
The conversation also exposed the extraordinary experiments conducted through the Horseman family. Victor revealed that Einer and Zwei were not ordinary biological descendants of Niklaus, but beings created through experiments using Vlad's Progenitor Blood. The Vampire King's genuine surprise demonstrated that he had been unaware of the experiments and had failed to detect the distant use of his own Blood. Victor found the situation particularly strange because his own connection with Blood allowed him to perceive and control Blood belonging to him even when it was located in another Dimension.[3]
These revelations established the personal motive behind Radu's conspiracies and clarified how extensively his plans against Vlad had developed before Victor entered Vampire society. His experiments, political infiltration and association with New Dawn were connected through a common objective rooted in his family's distant past.[3]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Radu reappeared within the Upper Sectors during the escalating crisis surrounding the barrier separating the [[Elderblood Empire]] from civilizations beyond its native Sector. His appearance had changed substantially following his escape from [[New Dawn]]. His body was enveloped in an unidentified Darkness, while his eyes possessed black sclera and red pupils, giving him an appearance fundamentally different from that of the Noble Vampire who had once ruled Clan Horseman.[1]
He was present during a meeting involving powerful members of an unidentified Upper-Sector faction. The discussion concerned repeated attempts to interfere with the Elderblood Empire through mysterious spheres containing fragments of Divine Concepts. One participant acknowledged that another fragment had been neutralized, while others observed that the barrier between their territories was weakening but continued resisting their attempts to force an opening.[1]
The faction's members regarded the appearance of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Sector as an opportunity to acquire valuable territory and strengthen their civilization. They believed that consuming the Sector could increase their own power and potentially allow them to confront enemies they otherwise considered troublesome. An older man presiding over the meeting ordered his subordinates to continue deploying traps and saturating the target territory with hostile interference until the barrier finally collapsed.[1]
The other participants demonstrated considerable confidence in their civilization's superiority. They claimed that their ruler had existed since before the formation of the Middle Sectors and that their Empire extended across hundreds of galaxies. From their perspective, a civilization recently emerging from a Middle Sector could not possibly compete with the strength accumulated by their ancient faction.[1]
Radu openly disagreed. When asked whether he feared the destruction of his former Sector, he calmly denied any personal concern for its survival. His only remaining objective was revenge, and the preservation of [[Nightingale]] or the surrounding territories did not determine his judgment. Nevertheless, he warned the faction that attacking Victor's Sector through a straightforward invasion was foolish because the Being they called [[Chaos]] was not an ordinary opponent.[1]
His warning was exceptionally severe. Radu believed that Victor could destroy the invading forces and potentially devour their entire faction. The assembled participants initially reacted with disbelief and ridicule, insisting that their civilization's history, territorial influence and accumulated power made such an outcome impossible. One individual reminded Radu that the Great Darkness had recognized his value, but argued that this recognition did not grant him the authority to make claims they considered absurd.[1]
Radu refused to engage in further argument. He closed his eyes, folded his arms and ignored their mockery, considering his responsibility fulfilled once he had delivered the warning. His personal conflict remained directed toward Vlad rather than Victor, and he saw no reason to sacrifice himself attempting to correct the arrogance of individuals who refused to recognize a dangerous opponent.[1]
The older man presiding over the meeting nevertheless took Radu's assessment seriously. Rather than immediately order a full-scale invasion, he instructed his subordinates to continue deploying traps while monitoring developments more carefully. The decision reflected a recognition that the Elderblood Empire might present a greater danger than the faction's initial estimates suggested. Radu's experience with Victor consequently influenced the strategic response of a civilization whose members possessed far greater territorial resources than the former Nightingale Kingdom.[1]
The meeting confirmed that Radu had survived his escape from New Dawn and established a relationship with forces operating beyond his original Sector. However, the precise nature of his transformation, his current supernatural classification, his formal position within the faction and the identity of the Great Darkness remain unknown. His vendetta against Vlad remains unchanged despite his altered circumstances.[1]

### Relationships

[[Vlad III Tepes]] was Radu's biological elder brother and the original Human associated with the historical identity of Vlad the Impaler. Their relationship existed before Radu became a Vampire, and the original Vlad's concern for his physically frail younger brother led him to request the intervention of the Vampire Progenitor. Radu's subsequent transformation saved him from his previous weakness and allowed him to survive far beyond the ordinary limitations of Human life.[3]
The original Vlad was later absorbed by the present Vampire King, becoming part of his existence through the supernatural properties of Progenitor Soul absorption. This event became the foundation of Radu's resentment and contributed to the vendetta that continued shaping his objectives for centuries. Although the circumstances surrounding the absorption are not fully known, Radu regards the disappearance of his brother as an offense requiring eventual revenge.[3]

#### Vlad Tepes
[[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] is Radu's principal enemy and the ultimate target of his vendetta. The Vampire Progenitor contains the absorbed existence of the original Vlad III Tepes, creating a complicated relationship in which Radu treats the present Vampire King as both a brother and the person responsible for taking that brother from him.[3]
Radu's efforts against Vlad involved political infiltration, attempts to control [[Nightingale]]'s principal Noble Vampire Clans, experiments using stolen Progenitor Blood and participation in conspiracies that successfully weakened the Vampire King's supernatural power. His patience reflected his understanding that defeating an ancient Progenitor through direct combat would be extraordinarily difficult, particularly while Vlad retained the loyalty of Nightingale's strongest families.[2][3]
His revenge remained his principal objective after fleeing [[New Dawn]] and reaching the Upper Sectors. Even while associating with a faction preparing to invade [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s territory, Radu explicitly distinguished his personal conflict with Vlad from the ambitions of his new associates.[7][1]

#### Alienova Constantine
[[Alienova Constantine]] was Radu's spouse and the mother of his biological daughter, Jessica. Her family descended from the [[Snow Clan]] through [[Alienova Snow]], establishing a hereditary connection to one of [[Nightingale]]'s principal Noble Vampire bloodlines.[7]
Alienova's ancestry provided Radu with a potential means of extending his political influence into Clan Snow. However, the relic that had belonged to her eventually became an instrument for protecting their daughter rather than fulfilling Radu's original ambition to control the Snow family.[7]

#### Jessica Horseman Snow
[[Jessica Horseman Snow]] is Radu's only confirmed naturally born child. Through her father and mother, she inherited the characteristics of two powerful Noble Vampire bloodlines, distinguishing her from her experimental brothers, Einer and Zwei.[2][7]
Radu recognized Jessica's intelligence and developing competence, although he was concerned that her comparatively gentle disposition made her poorly suited to the cruelty surrounding [[New Dawn]]. She demonstrated considerable skill in covert observation, including successfully planting a listening device upon her father. Rather than react angrily, Radu acknowledged her talent and expressed approval of her growing abilities.[2]
His paternal affection became most apparent during the collapse of New Dawn. He revealed Jessica's maternal ancestry, entrusted her with Alienova's protective collar, and deliberately activated its defensive mechanism to send her away from the headquarters. His confidence in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s devotion to his family allowed him to regard Clan Snow as the safest destination available to his daughter, despite his previous hostility toward its members.[7]

#### Einer and Zwei Horseman
[[Einer Horseman]] and [[Zwei Horseman]] are experimental creations produced through research involving Vlad's Progenitor Blood. Although Radu refers to them as his children, their origins differ from Jessica's natural biological descent.[7][3]
Einer appeared as Clan Horseman's representative during the competition against [[Tatsuya Rider]], publicly demonstrating the unusual characteristics associated with the family's supernatural experiments. The brothers' origins became particularly important after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] revealed to Vlad that his Blood had been used to create them without his knowledge.[15][3]

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is one of Radu's most dangerous enemies, although their hostility differs significantly from his vendetta against Vlad. Victor repeatedly interfered with Radu's political objectives, beginning with the competition involving Clan Horseman and continuing through the assassination conspiracy, the Fulger rematch and the eventual destruction of [[New Dawn]].[17][12][4][7]

Their radically different attitudes toward combat further intensified the hostility. Victor despised Niklaus' willingness to surrender rather than finish a battle, while Radu considered survival more important than satisfying the expectations of an opponent who valued warrior pride.[4]
Despite these differences, Radu recognized that Victor's growing political influence had weakened Vlad's control over [[Nightingale]]. He openly admitted that he did not genuinely hate Alucard and even appreciated the consequences of his rise, although this never prevented him from cooperating with individuals seeking Victor's death.[2]
His respect for Victor's danger persisted after reaching the Upper Sectors. While his associates confidently dismissed the [[Elderblood Empire]] as an inferior civilization, Radu warned that the [[Chaos]] Dragon God could destroy their forces and consume their faction. [[Theo Tepes]] was one of Radu's principal collaborators within [[Nightingale]]'s Royal Family. Their cooperation involved political conspiracies intended to undermine Vlad's authority and investigate [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s increasingly dangerous existence.[12]

[[Diablo]] and [[Bael]] maintained connections with [[New Dawn]] through the Demonic faction's opposition to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Bael infiltrated the organization on Diablo's behalf, gathering intelligence while participating in discussions concerning the young Vampire Progenitor.[2][11]

### Abilities

#### Powers & Abilities
Radu Tepes, formerly known as Niklaus Horseman, is an ancient Noble Vampire, former Vampire Count, and the head of the [[Horseman Clan]]. His supernatural abilities are primarily associated with Corruption, an unusual Vampiric power that allows him to generate and manipulate a black liquid-like substance, produce debilitating effects, construct weapons and defenses, and distribute fragments of his own body throughout the surrounding environment. Unlike Vampires who depend primarily upon overwhelming physical strength or destructive elemental abilities, Radu specializes in weakening his opponents, controlling the battlefield, and surviving damage that would ordinarily be fatal even to powerful Noble Vampires.[1][2][3]
Radu's combination of regeneration and bodily fragmentation was particularly dangerous during his confrontation with Natashia Fulger. Despite being considerably inferior to her in direct speed and destructive strength, he repeatedly survived the apparent destruction of his physical body by reconstructing himself from fragments he had secretly distributed throughout the battlefield. His Corruption also inflicted powerful poisoning effects capable of suppressing Natashia's regeneration, impairing her senses, and weakening her sufficiently to allow Radu to inflict otherwise devastating injuries.[4][1][2]
Radu subsequently developed additional countermeasures against the conventional weaknesses of his Vampiric physiology, enabling him to survive the simultaneous destruction of vital organs during the assault upon [[New Dawn]]'s headquarters. Following his escape, he underwent an unidentified supernatural transformation associated with the Upper Sectors and the faction of Darkness. His body is now enveloped in an unnatural Darkness, and his eyes have changed into a monstrous configuration with black sclera and red pupils. Although this alteration demonstrates a substantial change in his existence, its precise supernatural properties and the extent of his newly acquired power have not yet been revealed.[5][6][7]

#### Vampiric Physiology

##### Noble Vampire Physiology
Radu was originally a Human possessing an exceptionally frail constitution before being transformed into a Noble Vampire by [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]]. His subsequent development allowed him to survive for centuries and acquire supernatural characteristics far beyond those of ordinary Vampires. He eventually accumulated enough strength and influence to obtain the position of Vampire Count, although his principal advantages in combat remained his unusual Bloodline abilities and extraordinary survivability rather than superiority in direct physical confrontation.[8][4]
- Vampire Physiology: Radu possesses the supernatural characteristics of a Noble Vampire, including greatly enhanced physical abilities, regeneration, longevity, and the ability to nourish himself through Blood consumption. His original Human constitution was fundamentally altered when Vlad transformed him into a member of the Vampire race.[8]
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics: Radu possesses strength, speed, reflexes, durability, stamina, and agility vastly exceeding those of ordinary Humans. His physical capabilities allowed him to engage a former Vampire Countess in close combat despite her considerable advantages in direct destructive power.[4][1]
- Superhuman Strength: Radu can exchange powerful physical blows with ancient Noble Vampires and wield weapons capable of withstanding tremendous supernatural forces. His strength becomes considerably greater after assuming his full Vampire Count transformation.[1][2]
- Superhuman Speed: Radu possesses exceptional movement and combat speed associated with his status as an ancient Noble Vampire. However, Natashia's superior Lightning-based speed allowed her to repeatedly attack before he could adequately respond.[4][1]
- Enhanced Reflexes: Radu can respond to high-speed supernatural combat and defend himself against powerful opponents, although his reactions are insufficient to consistently match the extraordinary speed of the [[Fulger Bloodline]].[1]
- Superhuman Durability: Radu can endure powerful physical and supernatural attacks. His transformed state possesses particularly formidable durability, allowing him to resist a Lightning-enhanced sword strike from Natashia.[2]
- Enhanced Stamina: His ancient Vampiric constitution allows him to continue fighting despite substantial physical destruction. His survival methods further reduce the effectiveness of exhausting him through repeated attacks against his visible body.[4][3]
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Radu possesses advanced Vampiric regeneration, enabling him to recover from extensive physical injuries. His ability to reconstruct himself from distributed fragments greatly increases his survivability beyond ordinary regeneration.[4][1]
- Longevity: Radu has lived for centuries after being transformed by Vlad, possessing the extended lifespan characteristic of ancient Noble Vampires. His precise age has not been established.[8]
- Blood Consumption: Radu can consume Blood to satisfy his Vampiric nature. He demonstrated this by tearing out Natashia's heart, destroying it, and drinking the Blood contained within it, displaying intense satisfaction at the quality of an Elder Vampire's Blood.[2]
- Enhanced Vampiric Senses: Radu possesses the heightened sensory characteristics associated with his race and extensive combat experience. He is capable of analyzing the movements and physical condition of powerful supernatural opponents during battle.[1]

##### Vampire Count Transformation
Radu possesses a complete Vampire Count transformation, which substantially changes his appearance and enhances his physical and supernatural abilities. His body becomes almost entirely black, with dark-gray skin, blood-red eyes, elongated claws, large bat-like wings, and a spiked tail. The transformation emphasizes the monstrous qualities of his Vampiric existence while greatly increasing his capacity to withstand and inflict physical damage.[2]
- Transformation: Radu can enter and leave his complete [[Vampire Count Form]], altering his physiology and supernatural characteristics. He retained access to the transformation after surrendering the political title of Vampire Count.[2][5]
- Physical Empowerment: His transformed body possesses substantially greater strength and durability than his ordinary appearance, allowing him to perform attacks and withstand punishment that would otherwise place him at a disadvantage against other ancient Vampires.[2]
- Enhanced Strength: Radu struck Natashia with sufficient force to cave in her face and render her unconscious, subsequently tearing her heart from her chest and violently throwing her body into the ground. The impact created a crater and inflicted extensive additional injuries.[2]
- Enhanced Durability: Natashia attempted to cut Radu with her golden sword while it was enveloped in the [[Fulger Clan]]'s Lightning, but the blade stopped against his transformed skin instead of penetrating his body.[2]
- Accelerated Regeneration: Radu's regeneration becomes particularly formidable in his transformed state. Natashia repeatedly damaged and destroyed portions of his body during their confrontation, yet he continued recovering and remaining combat-capable.[3]
- Natural Weaponry: The transformation produces elongated claws and a spiked tail, granting him additional natural weapons for close-range combat.[2]
- Wing Manifestation: Radu develops long black bat-like wings as part of his monstrous Vampire Count physiology.[2]
- Enhanced Predatory Characteristics: His transformed appearance includes more pronounced monstrous features and a heightened physical capacity for direct attacks involving his hands, claws, and other natural weapons.[2]
- Transformation Reversal: Radu can deliberately cancel his complete Vampire Count Form and return to his ordinary appearance. He immediately reversed the transformation when announcing his surrender to Natashia.[9]

#### Corruption Manipulation
Corruption is the defining supernatural characteristic associated with the [[Horseman Clan]]. Unlike conventional elemental Bloodline abilities such as the [[Fulger Clan]]'s Lightning, Corruption is a more unusual power capable of expressing itself through bodily substances, debilitating conditions, and destructive effects. Its characteristics vary among members of the Bloodline, with [[Einer Horseman]] demonstrating powers associated with Decay while Radu's own abilities are primarily expressed through a black liquid-like substance and an exceptionally dangerous poison.[1]
During the confrontation between Radu and Natashia, the ancient Vampires observing the battle struggled to classify the Horseman family's power precisely. [[Agnes Elderblood Snow]] identified Corruption as the Clan's specialty, while [[Adonis Snow]] and others recognized similarities between its different manifestations. Pepper compared the family ability to supernatural debilitation effects often associated with Necromancers and [[Warlocks]], emphasizing that Corruption could affect an opponent's condition rather than merely inflict direct physical damage.[1]

##### Black Water Generation and Manipulation
Radu's Corruption manifests most prominently through an unnatural black water produced from his supernatural power. The substance can spread across enormous portions of a battlefield, respond to his commands, form offensive appendages, and provide defensive protection. Its behavior is not limited to ordinary liquid physics, as Radu can control its shape and movement and utilize it as a medium for his supernatural abilities.[1][9]
- Corruption Manipulation: Radu possesses the supernatural ability associated with the Horseman Bloodline, allowing him to generate and control an abnormal black substance that can damage, restrain, and weaken other beings.[1]
- Black Water Generation: Radu can produce substantial quantities of supernatural black water from his power. During his duel with Natashia, the substance spread outward from his position and covered a large portion of the arena.[1]
- Liquid Manipulation: He can control the movement, distribution, and structure of his Corruption, transforming it into different forms according to his needs.[1][9]
- Environmental Manipulation: Radu can saturate his surroundings with Corruption, transforming the affected terrain into an environment where he can attack and restrain opponents through the substance distributed across the ground.[1]
- Area Control: After spreading his black water over the battlefield, Radu utilized the contaminated region as his personal combat territory, immediately attempting to restrain Natashia when she entered it.[1]
- Tentacle Manifestation: Radu can shape his Corruption into numerous tentacle-like appendages. These tentacles can emerge from the surrounding black water, wrap around a target, and attempt to penetrate their defenses.[1]
- Binding: The tentacles generated by his Corruption can restrain an opponent by wrapping around their body. Natashia was briefly caught within the black liquid, although her Lightning armor prevented the tentacles from successfully piercing her.[1]
- Impaling Attacks: His Corruption tentacles are capable of attempting to pierce an opponent's body rather than functioning exclusively as restraints. Their effectiveness depends upon the durability and defensive powers of the target.[1]
- Shield Creation: Radu can instantly shape his black water into a defensive shield. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] attempted to stab him with a Blood dagger, he generated a barrier of Corruption between himself and the attack.[9]
- Barrier Generation: His controlled liquid can be concentrated into protective structures capable of intercepting physical or supernatural attacks, demonstrated by the shield he created against Victor.[9]
- Supernatural Weapon Creation: Radu can manifest a completely black sword using his own power, allowing him to generate a weapon without depending exclusively upon a conventional physical blade.[2]
- Corruption Weaponry: His constructed sword contains the same unusual supernatural characteristic as his other Corruption manifestations. Victor recognized its distinctive nature as matching the black owl he had previously consumed during his retaliation against Clan [[Amon]].[9]
- Supernatural Energy Projection: Radu can externally manifest his Corruption rather than maintaining it solely within his body. His black water, sword, shield, and other constructs demonstrate different applications of this projected power.[1][2][9]
The territory saturated by Radu's black water was described by him as his domain. Its demonstrated properties involve the manipulation of the Corruption distributed throughout an ordinary battlefield; no independent spatial dimension or separate Reality was created through this ability.[1]

##### Poison Manipulation
Radu's Corruption possesses an exceptionally dangerous toxic component capable of affecting even ancient Noble Vampires. His poison gradually weakened Natashia during their battle, interfering with her perception, bodily coordination, and regenerative capabilities. Rather than instantly overwhelming an opponent through destructive power, its effectiveness increased as the victim's condition deteriorated, allowing Radu to exploit weaknesses that became more pronounced over time.[2]
- Poison Generation: Radu can produce a supernatural poison associated with his Corruption. The substance was powerful enough to affect Natashia despite her advanced Vampiric physiology and exceptional physical capabilities.[2]
- Poison Manipulation: Radu possesses control over a toxic supernatural substance that can be used to compromise the physical condition of an opponent during combat.[2]
- Healing Suppression: The poison drastically reduced the effectiveness of Natashia's regeneration. Injuries that should have recovered rapidly instead healed abnormally slowly, demonstrating its ability to interfere with the regenerative mechanisms of an ancient Noble Vampire.[2]
- Regeneration Inhibition: Radu's poison weakens the victim's capacity to restore physical damage, making successive injuries increasingly dangerous even when the target ordinarily possesses powerful regeneration.[2]
- Sensory Impairment: Natashia experienced blurred vision and difficulty accurately perceiving her surroundings as the poison progressed through her body.[2]
- Auditory Distortion: The poison affected Natashia's perception of sound, causing Radu's voice to appear distant despite his actual proximity.[2]
- Disorientation Inducement: The poisoned Natashia experienced the sensation of the world spinning around her, making it increasingly difficult to maintain her balance and combat awareness.[2]
- Weakness Inducement: Continued exposure to the poison progressively reduced Natashia's physical ability to resist Radu, allowing him to overpower her despite her earlier superiority in direct combat.[2]
- Motor Function Impairment: The poison interfered with Natashia's bodily coordination and capacity to maintain control of her weapon, eventually causing her to lose her grip on her sword.[2]
- Physical Debilitation: Radu's poison produces cumulative deterioration of the target's physical condition, combining sensory disruption, impaired regeneration, and loss of strength into an increasingly dangerous state.[2]
- Status Effect Inducement: Radu's Corruption can impose debilitating supernatural conditions rather than functioning solely through direct injury. His confirmed applications include poisoning, sensory impairment, regeneration suppression, disorientation, and physical weakness.[1][2]
- Black Miasma Emission: A dark miasma-like substance emerged from Radu as he displayed his predatory nature and continued exerting his Corruption. This manifestation is associated with his supernatural power, although it has not been established as a separately mastered system of Miasma manipulation.[2]
The poison proved especially effective because Natashia initially relied upon her superior speed and regenerative abilities to overcome Radu through repeated attacks. By suppressing her recovery and gradually impairing her senses, Radu created an opportunity to inflict severe injuries despite remaining inferior to her in direct speed and destructive power.[1][2]
The Horseman Bloodline has been associated with other debilitating characteristics, including Decay, but Radu has not personally demonstrated every individual application attributed to his Clan. His confirmed toxic effects should therefore remain distinct from the abilities inherited or developed by Einer, Zwei, and other members of the lineage.[1]

##### Supernatural Constructs and Surveillance
Radu's Corruption is not restricted to liquid structures or close-range weapons. His involvement in the conspiracy against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] also established a connection between his supernatural power and the black owl-like constructs used to observe the destruction of the Noble Vampire Clans that participated in the assassination attempt. Victor detected and consumed one of the constructs, later recognizing that its supernatural properties matched those of Radu's sword during their confrontation after the Fulger rematch.[10][11][9]
- Supernatural Construct Creation: Radu possesses the ability to manifest supernatural structures from his Corruption, including weapons and defensive barriers. The black owl connected to his surveillance operation also possessed the distinctive characteristics of his power.[10][2][9]
- Corruption-Based Constructs: His supernatural power can assume forms other than ordinary liquid, demonstrated by his black sword and the observation construct associated with his earlier conspiracy.[10][2][9]
- Remote Surveillance: Radu participated in the covert observation of Victor and [[Scathach]] through black owl-like constructs positioned near the Noble Clans involved in the assassination conspiracy. The operation allowed the conspirators to evaluate their enemies without exposing themselves directly.[10][11]
- Information Gathering: The observation constructs were used to gather intelligence concerning the combat abilities, temperament, and reactions of Victor and Scathach. The supernatural method complemented Radu's preference for indirect confrontation and calculated preparation.[11]
- Supernatural Power Concealment: Radu was able to operate through concealed observation methods without initially revealing his identity to the targets. Victor eventually identified his involvement after comparing the supernatural properties of the constructs with Radu's own Corruption.[10][9]
The exact limits of Radu's surveillance method remain unknown. Although the observation constructs were connected to his Corruption, their demonstrated purpose was reconnaissance; they have not been shown possessing independent combat abilities comparable to his transformed body.[10][11][9]

#### Propagation and Regeneration

##### Self-Fragmentation
One of Radu's most dangerous abilities is the capacity to separate portions of his own body and distribute them throughout the environment. Unlike ordinary Vampiric regeneration, which depends upon the surviving body repairing itself, Radu can prepare separated fragments capable of serving as the foundation for the reconstruction of his entire physical form. This makes the destruction of his visible body insufficient to guarantee his death, particularly when his opponent does not know where the surviving fragments have been concealed.[1][3]
- Self-Fragmentation: Radu can divide portions of his body into separate fragments while preserving their usefulness for his survival and regeneration.[1]
- Body Part Separation: He can deliberately separate physical portions of himself without permanently compromising his ability to maintain or reconstruct his main body.[1]
- Fragment Distribution: Radu can disperse pieces of himself throughout a battlefield, preventing his survival from depending entirely upon the destruction or preservation of one visible body.[1][3]
- Body Reconstruction: Radu can recreate his entire body from surviving fragments, allowing him to return after the apparent destruction of his previously manifested form.[4][1]
- Regeneration from Fragments: Surviving separated portions of Radu's body can provide the foundation for his restoration even when his primary body has been reduced to pieces or otherwise destroyed.[1][3]
- Regenerative [[Immortality]]: Radu's distributed body fragments make him extraordinarily difficult to kill permanently. As long as a suitable fragment survives, the destruction of his current physical form does not necessarily end his existence.[3]
- Conditional Immortality: His extraordinary survival depends upon the continued existence of the fragments through which he can regenerate. This protection is therefore conditional rather than evidence of absolute immunity to every possible method of destruction.[1][3]
- Survival Anchoring: Radu can deliberately conceal fragments before or during a confrontation, establishing hidden points from which his body can be reconstructed after a fatal attack.[1]
- Distributed Regeneration: His regeneration can operate through pieces of himself spread across multiple locations, rather than requiring the immediate restoration of the body originally attacked.[1][3]
- Total Body Destruction Survival: Radu survived repeated attacks that effectively destroyed his visible body, returning through fragments he had distributed elsewhere within the arena.[4][1][3]
- Regeneration-Based Combat Endurance: His ability to return from dispersed fragments allows him to withstand repeated defeats of his visible body and continue participating in a confrontation against an opponent with considerably greater direct destructive power.[1][3]
Radu's preparation was particularly effective against Natashia because he had concealed a portion of himself underground before the most destructive attacks occurred. As she continued attempting to eliminate him through overwhelming Lightning and physical strikes, he distributed additional pieces, making it increasingly difficult to ensure that every part capable of regenerating had been destroyed. [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] recognized the danger of this strategy and understood that ordinary destruction of his current body would not necessarily eliminate him.[1][3]

##### Black Liquid Regeneration
Radu's regeneration is closely associated with the black liquid-like substance forming part of his supernatural constitution. Rather than reconstructing himself exclusively through the conventional healing of flesh and bone, his dispersed remains can gather and take shape until a complete body has been restored.[4][1]
- Liquid-Based Regeneration: Radu's physical restoration can manifest through the gathering and reconstitution of black liquid-like material associated with his Corruption.[4][1]
- Regenerative Reconstruction: His supernatural body can reform after catastrophic injury, restoring functional organs, limbs, and other physical structures.[4][3]
- Advanced Regenerative Healing Factor: His restoration capabilities substantially exceed ordinary physical healing, allowing him to recover from forms of destruction that would otherwise eliminate the body available for regeneration.[1][3]
- Regeneration Under Sustained Attack: Radu can repeatedly reconstruct portions of his body while an opponent continues inflicting severe injuries, making it difficult to incapacitate him permanently through a conventional succession of physical attacks.[3]
The survival of distributed pieces remains important to this form of regeneration. Radu has not demonstrated that he can restore himself after the complete destruction of every fragment of his existence, and his ability should not be treated as independent resurrection from absolute nonexistence.[3]

#### Survival and Weakness Countermeasures

##### Vampiric Weakness Countermeasures
Radu eventually developed methods for surviving damage to the normally fatal weak points of a Vampire's body. These preparations became apparent when members of the [[Blank Clan]], led by [[Oda Blank]], intercepted him during the destruction of [[New Dawn]]'s headquarters. Despite attacks that destroyed both his heart and brain, Radu remained alive, demonstrating that he had prepared additional measures against methods ordinarily capable of permanently killing even powerful Vampires.[5]
- Weakness Countermeasures: Radu possesses prepared methods of surviving attacks aimed at the conventional vital weaknesses of Vampiric physiology.[5]
- Heart Destruction Survival: Radu survived the destruction of his heart during the confrontation against the Blank Clan assassins.[5]
- Brain Destruction Survival: Radu continued existing after the destruction of his brain, despite its status as one of the most dangerous weak points of a Vampire.[5]
- Simultaneous Vital Organ Destruction Survival: Radu endured attacks that destroyed multiple critical organs during the same confrontation, demonstrating that his survival methods were not limited to protecting a single vulnerable location.[5]
- Enhanced Survivability: The combination of his advanced Vampiric regeneration, bodily fragmentation, and prepared countermeasures makes him exceptionally difficult to kill through conventional physical means.[1][5]
- Pain Tolerance: Radu remained capable of reacting and continuing his escape despite sustaining catastrophic damage to vital portions of his body.[5]
- Damage Tolerance: Radu can continue functioning after injuries that would ordinarily prevent a Vampire from maintaining a coherent physical body or surviving the destruction of essential organs.[5]
The precise mechanism underlying these countermeasures has not been disclosed. His survival establishes resistance to specific forms of otherwise fatal physical destruction, but not unconditional immunity to every Vampiric weakness or every method of destroying his body, Soul, or existence.[5]

##### Omnidirectional Energy Release
Radu possesses the ability to release supernatural power outward in a violent burst. During his attempted escape from the [[Blank Clan]], he was surrounded and attacked by multiple assassins, but responded by explosively discharging his power and throwing the nearby attackers away. The attack created the opportunity necessary for him to activate his prepared escape artifact.[5]
- Energy Projection: Radu can release a powerful burst of supernatural Energy from his body, affecting multiple opponents in his immediate surroundings.[5]
- Shockwave Generation: The release of his power created an explosive force that propelled the surrounding assassins away from him.[5]
- Omnidirectional Attack: Radu can discharge his supernatural power across the surrounding area rather than directing all offensive attacks toward a single opponent.[5]
- Area-of-Effect Attack: His explosive release can affect several nearby opponents simultaneously, making it useful for escaping encirclement.[5]
- Crowd Dispersal: The attack successfully forced multiple assassins away from Radu, interrupting their attempt to restrain and eliminate him.[5]

#### Current Altered Existence

##### Upper-Sector Darkness Transformation
Radu's current appearance differs fundamentally from his previous Noble Vampire and Vampire Count forms. Following his escape from [[New Dawn]], he became associated with an unidentified faction belonging to the Upper Sectors, whose greater authority is connected to Darkness. His body is now covered by an unusual Darkness, while his eyes possess black sclera and red pupils rather than their original Vampiric appearance. Other members of the faction acknowledged that the Great Darkness had recognized his value, indicating a relationship with the supernatural authority governing their civilization.[6][7]
- Supernatural Transformation: Radu underwent a substantial alteration of his supernatural existence, resulting in an appearance markedly different from his previous Noble Vampire physiology.[7]
- Darkness-Enveloped Physiology: His current body is shrouded in an unidentified supernatural Darkness. The substance is a confirmed characteristic of his present appearance, although its exact composition and independent applications have not been established.[7]
- Monstrous Eye Transformation: Radu's current eyes possess completely black sclera and vivid red pupils, differing from his previous Vampiric appearance.[7]
- Foreign Supernatural Influence: Radu possesses a connection to a powerful Upper-Sector faction associated with Darkness. His altered appearance and the recognition he received from the Great Darkness demonstrate his association with that authority, but the specific powers derived from it remain unknown.[7]
The connection between the Horseman Bloodline and powers originating beyond the native Sector was further suggested during the investigation of [[Nikolai Francis Nichev]]. [[Victor Elderblood]] encountered foreign Conceptual corruption that resembled, at a much greater level, the abnormal abilities associated with Radu's experimental descendants. He considered the possibility that Radu had obtained a foreign artifact or previously established contact with Upper-Sector forces, explaining how those characteristics might have entered the Horseman experiments. The precise origin of the Bloodline's abnormal properties was not conclusively established.[6]
The supernatural darkness enveloping Radu's current body has not yet been demonstrated as an independently controlled offensive or defensive ability. His association with the Great Darkness does not establish that he personally possesses the full Authority, Divinity, or powers of the Being responsible for his transformation.[7]

#### Skills

##### Combat and Weapon Proficiency
Radu possesses considerable combat experience accumulated throughout his existence as an ancient Noble Vampire. Although his preferred methods rely heavily upon Corruption and preparation, he is capable of direct armed confrontation against powerful supernatural opponents. During his duel against Natashia, he demonstrated enough swordsmanship to engage her briefly despite the considerable speed advantage provided by the [[Fulger Bloodline]].[1]
- Swordsmanship: Radu is experienced in the use of swords and can engage ancient Noble Vampires in armed combat. His blade clashed directly with Natashia's Lightning-enhanced weapon, producing a powerful shockwave.[1]
- Weapon Proficiency: Radu can utilize conventional swords and weapons manifested from his own supernatural power, allowing him to switch between physical and Corruption-based attacks.[1][2]
- Armed Combat: Radu possesses substantial experience engaging opponents with bladed weapons, combining physical attacks with his supernatural abilities.[1]
- Close-Quarters Combat: Radu can employ punches, claws, and other physical attacks effectively, particularly while using his full Vampire Count transformation.[2]
- Combat Strategy: Radu plans confrontations around the abilities and habits of his opponents, using preparation and gradual debilitation to compensate for disadvantages in physical strength or speed.[1][3]
- Combat Analysis: Radu can evaluate an opponent's supernatural abilities and determine whether their current power exceeds previous expectations. His assessment of Natashia recognized that her capabilities had changed considerably from their earlier confrontation.[1]
- Tactical Adaptation: Radu can modify his approach as a battle develops, distributing additional fragments when direct confrontation becomes too dangerous and exploiting opportunities created by his opponent's deteriorating condition.[1][2]
- Defensive Combat: Radu can combine physical evasion, regeneration, battlefield control, and Corruption barriers to survive attacks from opponents with greater offensive power.[1][9]

##### Intelligence and Strategy
Radu's most consistently demonstrated noncombat strength is his strategic intelligence. His plans frequently involve several overlapping contingencies, and he is capable of recognizing when preserving his resources is more valuable than achieving an immediate victory. This mentality allowed him to escape situations that threatened opponents who relied excessively upon physical superiority or personal pride.[3][9]
- Genius Intelligence: Radu possesses an exceptionally calculating mind and has successfully maintained complex political and supernatural conspiracies across centuries. His plans involved multiple Noble Vampire Clans, external supernatural factions, and hidden experimental operations.[12]
- Master Strategist: Radu can construct long-term plans involving political infiltration, supernatural research, and the manipulation of powerful individuals whose objectives overlap with his own.[12]
- Analytical Prediction: Radu can evaluate the likely consequences of an opponent's behavior, recognizing that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s unpredictability and abnormal development made conventional methods of controlling him increasingly unreliable.[11][12]
- Contingency Planning: Radu routinely prepares alternate means of survival and escape, including distributing pieces of his body before battle and maintaining an extraordinary artifact capable of opening a route beyond his native Sector.[1][5]
- Political Manipulation: Radu understands the power structures and customs governing [[Nightingale]]'s Noble Vampires, allowing him to exploit formal rules and the reputational concerns of powerful rulers.[9]
- Strategic Withdrawal: Radu can recognize when continued confrontation would endanger his long-term objectives and is willing to abandon valuable positions or resources rather than risk destruction.[9][5]
- Psychological Analysis: Radu possesses considerable insight into the personalities and motivations of others, demonstrated by his assessments of Victor, [[Scathach]], and the members of [[New Dawn]].[11][12]
- Threat Assessment: Radu can evaluate the danger posed by individuals whose strength exceeds the expectations of their political or supernatural position. He warned an ancient Upper-Sector faction that Victor could destroy its forces and potentially devour its entire organization despite their confidence in overwhelming territorial superiority.[7]
- Diplomatic Judgment: Radu is capable of addressing powerful rulers without surrendering his independent assessment of a situation. His warning concerning Victor influenced the Upper-Sector faction's decision to delay an immediate invasion.[7]
- Emotional Composure: Radu can remain calm when threatened or ridiculed, prioritizing the preservation of his objectives rather than responding impulsively to insults or challenges to his pride.[9][7]
- Intelligence Gathering: Radu utilizes covert observation, collaborators, and supernatural constructs to acquire information before acting, rather than relying exclusively upon direct personal encounters.[10][11]
- Organizational Leadership: Radu led the [[Horseman Clan]] and exercised substantial influence within New Dawn, coordinating individuals from different supernatural races and political factions.[12]
- Supernatural Research Coordination: Radu maintained connections to experimental projects involving Vampire Blood, Hybrid physiology, and other abnormal supernatural characteristics. The experiments involving Einer and Zwei demonstrate his access to Progenitor Blood and advanced research resources, although the precise procedures were not all performed personally by him.[12][8]

#### Equipment

##### Corruption-Based Weaponry
Radu can create supernatural weapons through his Corruption, reducing his dependence upon conventional armaments during combat. His manifested black sword is directly associated with his Bloodline ability rather than being an independently established enchanted artifact.[2][9]
- Black Sword: Radu manifested a completely black sword from his supernatural power during the confrontation against Natashia, preparing to use it to inflict a fatal attack.[2]
- Corruption Shield: Radu created a defensive shield from black water when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] attempted to stab him with a Blood dagger. Its supernatural signature matched the abnormal Corruption Victor had previously encountered.[9]

##### Black Egg
Radu possessed a mysterious black egg-like artifact bearing unnatural tentacle-like characteristics. The object served as an emergency escape contingency and was destroyed during the assault upon [[New Dawn]]'s headquarters. Its destruction opened a dark-red portal behind Radu, while tentacle-like appendages extended from the other side and seized him. The surrounding Space became distorted by the influence of the Entity connected to the portal, preventing [[Oda Blank]]'s forces from successfully interfering with his escape.[5]
- Dimensional Transportation (Artifact-Based): Destroying the black egg activated a portal that allowed Radu to escape beyond the immediate reach of the [[Blank Clan]] assassins. The portal was associated with an external supernatural influence rather than a personally demonstrated innate ability.[5]
- Escape Portal Activation: Radu could activate his prepared escape method by destroying the black egg, initiating the appearance of the portal and the intervention of the Entity on its opposite side.[5]
- External Spatial Distortion: The supernatural influence associated with the portal distorted the surrounding Space and prevented the nearby assassins from interfering. This effect originated from the external force connected to the artifact rather than independently demonstrated spatial manipulation by Radu.[5]
- External Tentacle Manifestation: Dark tentacle-like appendages extended from the portal and seized Radu, dragging him into the opening. These appendages belonged to the phenomenon associated with the portal and have not been established as another manifestation of his personal Corruption.[5]
The black egg was consumed in the activation of the escape mechanism. Its origin, the identity of the Entity connected to it, and whether Radu possesses another equivalent artifact have not been confirmed.[5]

##### Snow Clan Relic
Radu previously possessed a protective collar belonging to [[Alienova Constantine]], an ancient relic associated with the main Bloodline of the [[Snow Clan]]. The artifact was created using the heart of the Dragon [[Fafnir]] and possessed extraordinary supernatural characteristics connected to the family's inheritance. Before escaping [[New Dawn]], Radu entrusted it to [[Jessica Horseman Snow]], whose biological descent allowed her to make use of the relic's protection.[5]
- Protective Relic (Former Possession): The collar possessed a defensive mechanism capable of protecting Jessica when she was rendered unconscious, activating and transporting her away from the threatened headquarters.[5]
- Hereditary Bloodline Recognition: The relic was associated with the Snow Clan's main family and could be properly used by individuals possessing the appropriate inherited Bloodline.[5]
The relic's abilities belong to the artifact and its eligible wearer. Radu's possession of the collar does not establish that he personally possessed the power to reproduce its protective teleportation or its Draconic properties without the artifact.[5]
