### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 64
Zwei Horseman is a young Noble Vampire, the older twin brother of [[Einer Horseman]], elder brother of [[Jessica Horseman Snow]] and one of the sons of [[Niklaus Horseman]]. He is a prominent member of the [[Horseman Clan]] who first appeared alongside Einer during the Clan's involvement with Lucy and Karen's attempt to artificially transform Plebeian Vampires into Noble Vampires.[1]
Zwei's personality contrasts almost completely with that of his twin. Whereas Einer is loud, sadistic, excitable and constantly smiling, Zwei is quiet, emotionally restrained and difficult to read. His mannerisms are those of an impeccably trained Noble Vampire, but his perpetual disinterest is so extreme that Lucy described him as appearing almost dead inside.[1]
Despite his limited dialogue, Zwei was involved in several of the Horseman Clan's early schemes. He accompanied Einer while dealing with Lucy and Karen, was aware of the Clan's secret Hybrid experiments and remained beside Niklaus during the political game against [[Victoria Fulger Elderblood|Victoria Rider]]. His concern when Einer activated the [[Vampire Count Form]] also shows that his apparent emotional emptiness should not be interpreted as complete indifference toward his family.[2][3]

### Physical Appearance
Zwei bears a strong resemblance to his twin brother [[Einer Horseman]], but several features make the two immediately distinguishable. He possesses pale white skin, bright blood-red eyes and long, straight black hair reaching approximately to his shoulders.[1]
Unlike Einer's messy hairstyle and exaggerated expressions, Zwei's hair is kept considerably straighter and his face normally remains completely neutral. His expression conveys almost total disinterest in his surroundings, contributing to Lucy's impression that Zwei looked emotionally empty despite being perfectly alive.[1]
Zwei also dresses considerably more formally than his younger brother. During his first appearance, his clothing and behavior reflected exceptionally refined Noble etiquette. He calmly sat upon a couch drinking blood from a teacup while Einer moved around the room laughing and teasing everyone present.[1]
The twins' similar faces combined with their opposite presentation make them visually striking counterparts: Einer appears uncontrolled and openly unstable, while Zwei resembles a composed aristocrat whose thoughts are almost impossible to determine.

### Personality
Zwei is quiet, restrained, observant and almost unnaturally calm. He rarely speaks unless he has something directly relevant to say and commonly communicates using only a few words. During his introduction, Lucy attempted to greet him and received only a dry "Hello" before Zwei immediately returned to drinking his blood.[1]
This extreme lack of outward emotion makes him difficult to understand. Lucy considered Einer easy to read despite his insanity because the younger twin constantly displayed his feelings. Zwei was the opposite; his blank expression and dry speech revealed almost nothing about what he actually thought.[1]
Zwei nevertheless possesses excellent manners. His posture, clothing and behavior were explicitly described as displaying impeccable noble etiquette. His cold demeanor is therefore not the result of poor social education or awkwardness. He simply appears to have very little interest in unnecessary conversation.[1]
His emotional restraint persists even during abnormal situations. When everyone felt supernatural pressure rising from beneath Lucy's base, Zwei calmly identified its origin with a single word—below—before continuing to drink from his teacup as though nothing particularly important were happening.[1]
Zwei is also remarkably direct. When Karen emerged from her failed transformation ritual and instinctively attacked Einer, Zwei effortlessly intercepted her fist. Rather than praise her, mock her or explain what had happened, he simply stated that she had become stronger.[1]
Although outwardly apathetic, Zwei is not completely emotionless. During the Rider–Horseman game, Einer activated the [[Vampire Count Form]] and began releasing considerably more Power. Zwei immediately addressed Niklaus in his normal neutral voice, but the narration specifically noted a warning within his tone. His father understood what he meant and reassured him that Einer's transformation was under control.[3]
This moment indicates that Zwei does care about his younger twin even if he rarely displays those emotions openly.
Unlike Einer, Zwei has not demonstrated the same recklessness or battle obsession. He appears more cautious and analytical, preferring to observe rather than immediately involve himself in unnecessary confrontation. However, the novel has not shown enough of Zwei in direct combat to determine how his personality changes during a serious battle.

### Synopsis

#### Vampire Society

##### Lucy and Karen's Noble Vampire Ritual
Zwei first appeared alongside [[Einer Horseman]] when the twins arrived earlier than expected at Lucy and Karen's hidden base in the Human World. The Horseman brothers had been looking forward to traveling there, although Zwei's completely indifferent appearance made Lucy question whether the older twin was actually interested at all.[1]
While Einer immediately began laughing and casually speaking with Lucy, Zwei sat on a nearby couch drinking blood from a teacup. Lucy quickly noticed the dramatic difference between the twins. Einer possessed messy hair and constantly displayed his emotions, whereas Zwei had shoulder-length straight black hair, perfect aristocratic manners and an expression showing almost no interest in anything around him.[1]
Einer jokingly attempted to prove that Zwei was secretly excited by physically forcing his brother's face into a smile. Zwei did not meaningfully react, leaving Lucy unsure how he was supposed to interpret their relationship.[1]
The gathering was interrupted when supernatural pressure began emanating from beneath the base. Einer became curious, while Zwei calmly identified that the disturbance originated from below. Lucy explained that Karen had become impatient and begun their transformation ritual without waiting for him.[1]
The group descended toward the ritual chamber and discovered Karen emerging from the coffin after believing she had failed to change at all. Einer approached her while laughing and mocking her disappointment. Karen instinctively attempted to punch him, but Zwei immediately intercepted the attack by catching her fist.[1]
After briefly assessing Karen, Zwei stated that she had become stronger. Einer agreed but explained that the ritual had nevertheless failed to turn her into a Noble Vampire.[1]
Einer subsequently handled most of the explanation while Zwei remained nearby. The Horseman brothers revealed that Lucy and Karen's ritual was incomplete because changing from Plebeian Vampire physiology into Noble Vampire physiology required something significantly more powerful than the blood of an ordinary Noble Vampire.[1]
The true missing component was the blood of a Vampire Progenitor.
A Horseman servant produced a case containing a vial of [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]]' blood. The reaction from Lucy and Karen was immediate, with both Plebeian Vampires displaying overwhelming thirst mixed with instinctive terror at the blood's quality.[1]
Although Einer performed most of the taunting and deliberately denied Lucy and Karen immediate access to the vial, Zwei's presence establishes him as directly involved in the [[Horseman Clan]]'s manipulation of the two Plebeian Vampires and their attempted racial evolution.[1]

##### Horseman Hybrid Experiments
Zwei later appeared with Niklaus and Jessica during preparations for the game against Clan Rider. When Niklaus selected which of his children would fight, Einer casually asked whether the participant would be himself, his older brother or Jessica.[2]
Niklaus immediately stated that Zwei could not fight and refused to risk Jessica in such an important match. Einer was therefore chosen as Clan Horseman's representative.[2]
The reason Zwei could not participate was not explained.
After Einer departed, Niklaus asked Zwei about the condition of an unnamed boy connected to their experiments. Zwei responded in his characteristic short manner, explaining that the boy was dying, was sick and did not have much time remaining.[2]
Niklaus regarded the situation as a significant problem because creating a Hybrid was extremely difficult and he could not afford to lose the subject before their objective had been completed. The exchange confirms that Zwei was aware of and sufficiently involved with the [[Horseman Clan]]'s Hybrid program to report directly to Niklaus regarding the health of one of its subjects.[2]
However, the chapter does not establish that Zwei personally created, modified or experimented upon the Hybrid. His exact role in the project remains unspecified.

##### The Rider–Horseman Game
Although unable to participate personally, Zwei remained beside Niklaus and Jessica while Einer fought [[Tatsuya Rider]] in the arena.
Einer's opponent proved considerably more troublesome than expected. Tatsuya possessed a strange perception-related Power capable of deceiving Einer's senses, forcing the younger Horseman twin to escalate the battle.[4]
Eventually, Einer entered the [[Vampire Count Form]] despite being only 105 years old. The transformation released enough Power to shock a significant portion of the audience and caused even experienced Vampires to question how someone so young had already reached that state.[3]
Zwei immediately responded.
Although his voice remained neutral, the narration specifically noted a warning within his tone when he addressed his father. Niklaus understood the concern without requiring Zwei to explain himself and responded that everything was fine, noting that transformations of this kind were not unheard of among the children of [[Vampire Counts]].[3]
Jessica watched Einer closely because she herself had not yet completely grasped the transformation. Zwei instead remained focused upon the potential danger to his brother.[3]
The battle eventually became a three-way confrontation after [[Victor Elderblood]] entered the game. Einer and Tatsuya cooperated against Victor but were ultimately defeated.[5][6]
Zwei did not directly interfere in the fight.

### Skills & Abilities
Zwei is a naturally born Noble Vampire and possesses the supernatural characteristics inherent to that race. However, unlike Einer, he has not yet participated in a substantial on-page battle. His personal Power and full combat style therefore remain unknown.

#### Noble Vampire Physiology
- Noble Vampire Physiology: Zwei is a naturally born Noble Vampire rather than a Human transformed through the ordinary Vampire ritual. As such, he possesses the superior physical constitution associated with Vampire nobility.
- Superhuman Strength: Zwei was capable of effortlessly stopping Karen's punch immediately after her failed ritual had increased her physical strength.[1]
- Superhuman Reflexes: Karen attacked Einer instinctively and without warning, yet Zwei intercepted the strike before it could reach his brother.[1]
- Superhuman Speed: His ability to react quickly enough to stop Karen's sudden attack demonstrates physical speed beyond an ordinary Human.
- Enhanced Durability: As a Noble Vampire, Zwei possesses a supernatural body significantly more durable than that of a Human.
- Regeneration: Zwei possesses the regenerative properties inherent to Vampires, although no individual regeneration feat has yet been demonstrated.
- Enhanced Senses: Zwei possesses the sharpened senses expected of a Noble Vampire and immediately identified the origin of the supernatural disturbance beneath Lucy's base.[1]
- Longevity: Zwei was already approximately 105 years old during the Vampire Society arc while retaining the appearance of a young man.

#### Observation
- Keen Observation: Zwei quickly evaluated Karen following her ritual and correctly recognized that her physical strength had increased despite the transformation failing to change her race.[1]
- Situation Awareness: He noticed the supernatural pressure originating beneath Lucy's base and remained aware of the risks surrounding Einer's Vampire Count transformation.[1][3]

#### Noble Etiquette
- Noble Etiquette: Zwei is explicitly described as possessing impeccable aristocratic etiquette. His posture, speech and mannerisms strongly reflect his upbringing as a high-ranking Noble Vampire.[1]
- Emotional Control: He demonstrates exceptional outward composure, rarely allowing surprise, concern or irritation to visibly affect his behavior.

### Unconfirmed Abilities
- Vampire Count Transformation: Zwei is the son of a Vampire Count and Niklaus stated that it was not unusual for the sons of Counts to possess access to the transformation. However, only [[Einer Horseman]] has directly demonstrated the [[Vampire Count Form]] on-page. Until Zwei personally uses the transformation or the novel explicitly confirms that he possesses it, it should not be listed as one of his established abilities.[3]
- [[Horseman Clan]] Corruption Power: Various members of the Horseman bloodline possess unusual Powers associated with corruption or related supernatural effects, but Zwei's own individual Power has never been properly demonstrated. The abilities displayed by Einer or Niklaus should therefore not be copied onto Zwei's page without evidence.

### Relationships

#### Einer Horseman
[[Einer Horseman]] is Zwei's younger twin brother and the person with whom he is most frequently shown.
Their personalities could hardly be more different. Einer is loud, constantly laughing, openly sadistic and highly expressive. Zwei is silent, emotionally restrained and extremely difficult to read. During their introduction, Einer jokingly grabbed Zwei's face and physically forced him to smile while claiming that his older brother was secretly excited about visiting the Human World. Zwei simply maintained his blank expression.[1]
Despite their differences, Zwei appears to care about Einer. He immediately protected him when Karen suddenly attacked and later expressed subtle concern when Einer activated the dangerous [[Vampire Count Form]] against Tatsuya.[1][3]

#### Radu Tepes
[[Niklaus Horseman]] is Zwei's father and creator.
Zwei appears obedient to Niklaus and reports important information directly to him. During the Rider–Horseman game, Niklaus asked Zwei for an update concerning one of the Clan's Hybrid subjects, and Zwei immediately provided the boy's deteriorating condition.[2]
Niklaus also understands Zwei well enough to recognize concern hidden beneath his otherwise neutral speech. When Zwei spoke only the word "Father" while watching Einer's Count transformation, Niklaus immediately understood the warning and reassured him without requiring further explanation.[3]

#### Jessica Horseman Snow
[[Jessica Horseman Snow]] is Zwei's younger sister. The two were shown together beside Niklaus during the Rider–Horseman game and were both excluded from representing the Clan in the duel, though for different reasons. Niklaus stated that Zwei could not fight, while he simply refused to entrust such an important match to Jessica.[2]

#### Lucy and Karen
Zwei encountered Lucy and Karen through the [[Horseman Clan]]'s involvement in their attempted evolution into Noble Vampires.
Unlike Einer, Zwei showed little interest in tormenting the Plebeian pair verbally. He mostly observed while Einer handled the conversation. However, he immediately intervened when Karen attempted to punch his brother and confirmed that her failed ritual had increased her strength.[1]

### Trivia
- Zwei is the older twin of [[Einer Horseman]]. Chapter 82 explicitly has Niklaus refer to him as Einer's older brother.[2]
- The twins were approximately 105 years old during the early Vampire Society storyline.
- Zwei and Einer strongly resemble each other physically, but Zwei possesses straight shoulder-length hair while Einer's hair is considerably messier.[1]
- Lucy described Zwei as possessing impeccable Noble etiquette while simultaneously appearing as though he were dead inside.[1]
- Zwei speaks extremely little. Several of his early lines consist of only one or two words.[1][2]
- Einer can apparently understand and tolerate Zwei's emotionless behavior better than outsiders, frequently speaking for or teasing his brother.[1]
- Zwei effortlessly stopped Karen's punch immediately after her ritual had already increased her physical strength.[1]
- Niklaus explicitly stated that Zwei was unable to participate in the game against Clan Rider, but the novel never explained why.[2]
- Zwei knew about the [[Horseman Clan]]'s Hybrid experiment and directly reported the failing health of one experimental subject to Niklaus.[2]
- His otherwise neutral voice carried enough concern when Einer entered the [[Vampire Count Form]] that Niklaus immediately understood Zwei was warning him about the danger.[3]
- The old version of this page listed Zwei as the Heir of the Horseman Clan. The early novel instead explicitly presents Einer Horseman as the Clan's heir during the Rider–Horseman game, so that title has been removed from Zwei's infobox.[7]
- The old page also lacked a debut field despite Zwei first appearing alongside Einer in Chapter 64.[1]
- Zwei's personal supernatural Power has not yet been clearly revealed. It would be inaccurate to copy Einer's black-thorn abilities or Niklaus' corruption-based techniques onto him without direct evidence.
