### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 64
Einer Horseman is a young Noble Vampire, the younger twin brother of [[Zwei Horseman]], elder brother of [[Jessica Horseman Snow]], and one of the children of [[Niklaus Horseman]]. During the early period of the story, Einer was publicly identified as the heir of the [[Horseman Clan]] and represented his father during the game between Clan Horseman and [[Victoria Fulger Elderblood|Victoria Rider]], fighting [[Tatsuya Rider]] before [[Victor Elderblood]] interrupted the match and challenged both participants.[1][2]
Although only 105 years old during the game, Einer was already capable of using the [[Vampire Count Form]], an achievement notable enough to surprise much of the audience. [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett|Scathach Scarlett]] later judged him an exceptionally talented Vampire, specifically noting his ability to access the transformation at such a young age and the considerable control he displayed while using it, although his limited stamina prevented him from maintaining the form indefinitely.[3][4]
Einer's personal Power manifests as a strange black energy primarily expressed through thorns, weapons and a decaying effect. He can generate black weapons, create enormous thorn structures, trap opponents, construct defensive enclosures and even channel his Power into another person to temporarily enhance them. During his battle with Tatsuya and Victor, this ability proved versatile enough to serve both offensive and supportive purposes.[1][5][6]

### Physical Appearance
Einer is a young-looking male Vampire with pale skin, messy black hair and red eyes. His most immediately recognizable characteristic is his expression: he habitually wears an exaggerated, distorted smile and frequently possesses a crazed look in his eyes. When Lucy first met him, Einer's appearance and behavior immediately gave him the impression that the young Noble Vampire had "several screws loose".[7]
During the game against Tatsuya, Einer wore comparatively ordinary clothing consisting of a black sweatshirt and black jeans. His casual appearance strongly contrasted with Tatsuya's traditional black Yukata and more composed demeanor, visually reinforcing the enormous difference in their personalities.[1]

#### Vampire Count Form
Einer's appearance changes drastically when entering his [[Vampire Count Form]]. His body grows taller, his skin changes into an extremely dark black coloration, his ears lengthen, his hair rises as though defying gravity and his red eyes become significantly deeper in color. His teeth also become considerably sharper.[5]
The most distinctive feature of the transformation is the appearance of large black wings composed of pure Power behind him. These wings allow Einer to take to the air and also function as extensions of his supernatural ability, from which he can generate and launch large quantities of black thorns.[5][3]

### Personality
Einer is eccentric, sadistic, excitable and extremely playful. He frequently laughs regardless of the seriousness of the circumstances around him, normally using a distinctive "Kakakaka" laugh, and enjoys provoking or tormenting other people for his own amusement. During his debut, he deliberately toyed with Lucy and Karen after revealing that their attempted transformation ritual was incomplete, repeatedly presenting the vial of [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]]' blood they desperately wanted before abruptly closing the case and refusing to give it to them simply because he found their reactions entertaining.[7]
His behavior often makes him appear unstable. Einer sings or hums to himself during tense situations, maintains his abnormal smile while discussing serious business and can transition between playful mockery and genuine violence with little warning. Lucy found Einer considerably easier to understand than Zwei, but simultaneously believed the younger twin appeared mentally unhinged.[7]
Einer possesses a strong sadistic streak during battle. He becomes excited when his attacks appear to connect, reacts with visible pleasure when he believes he has injured an opponent and initially attempted to kill or severely injure Tatsuya despite the rules prohibiting permanent death. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later joined the match, Einer immediately attempted to pierce his head rather than cautiously studying the new opponent.[5][2]
However, Einer is not simply a mindless berserker. When his usual tactics fail, he is capable of becoming surprisingly analytical and pragmatic. Against Tatsuya, Einer recognized that maintaining the [[Vampire Count Form]] while blindly throwing attacks was wasting Energy. He voluntarily abandoned the transformation, returned to his Rapier and changed to a more efficient close-range strategy after beginning to understand how Tatsuya's unusual perception-based Power functioned.[3]
He can also cooperate effectively when necessary. Although Einer initially fought Tatsuya as an enemy, both men immediately understood that defeating Victor required teamwork. Einer protected and empowered Tatsuya, coordinated their attacks and willingly took the more dangerous role of directly holding back Victor's massive ice attack so that Tatsuya could destroy it.[8][6]
Einer possesses considerable pride in his abilities and initially underestimated opponents who appeared to be near his own age. This arrogance had previously contributed to the failure of a mission involving the capture of an Alpha Werewolf, something Niklaus had not forgiven. When his father selected him to represent Clan Horseman against Tatsuya, Niklaus explicitly informed Einer that this would be his final opportunity to prove himself. Einer immediately became serious and promised not to disappoint him again.[9]
His father's approval therefore appears highly important to him. Although normally carefree and mocking, Einer understood that repeated failure was unacceptable and treated the game against Clan Rider as an opportunity to restore Niklaus' confidence in him.[9]
Einer is also extremely stubborn. Even after exhausting himself fighting Victor and losing his transformation, he refused to admit defeat and attempted to stand alongside Tatsuya to continue fighting. This determination earned the respect of both Victor and [[Scathach]].[4]

### Synopsis

#### Vampire Society

##### The Noble Vampire Ritual
Einer made his first appearance when he and [[Zwei Horseman]] arrived in the Human World to meet Lucy and Karen. The twins' arrival occurred earlier than Lucy expected, causing the Plebeian Vampire to panic because Karen had already begun the experimental ritual they intended to use to transform themselves into Noble Vampires.[7]
Einer immediately contrasted with his older twin. Zwei remained quiet, formal and emotionally detached while drinking blood from a teacup, whereas Einer greeted Lucy by laughing loudly, constantly talking and physically forcing his brother's face into a smile as a joke. When supernatural pressure began emanating from beneath the base, the twins quickly realized that Karen had already activated the ritual.[7]
Karen eventually emerged from the blood-filled coffin and initially believed the procedure had accomplished nothing. After she attempted to attack Einer and Zwei casually intercepted her strike, Einer confirmed that her body had become somewhat stronger but bluntly informed Lucy and Karen that the ritual had nevertheless failed to change her species.[7]
Einer mocked them for believing that transforming a Plebeian Vampire into a Noble Vampire could be accomplished simply by stealing a sacred Church artifact, hiring a Witch, filling a coffin with innocent Human blood and adding the blood of an ordinary Noble Vampire. He explained that Plebeian and Noble Vampires were fundamentally different species and contemptuously described Plebeians as an imperfect subspecies created as weapons.[7]
However, Einer also admitted that the ritual itself was fundamentally correct. Karen had failed because she lacked its decisive final ingredient: the blood of a Vampire Progenitor.[7]
A Horseman servant then brought forward a briefcase containing a small vial of blood belonging to [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]], the First Vampire Progenitor and King of Vampires. Merely seeing the blood produced overwhelming thirst mixed with instinctive fear in Lucy and Karen. Einer deliberately exploited this reaction, repeatedly asking whether they wanted it until both desperately reached for the vial.[7]
He then slammed the case shut and laughingly refused to give it to them, openly enjoying the frustration and desperation he had created. The exchange demonstrated both the [[Horseman Clan]]'s possession of extremely valuable Progenitor blood and Einer's tendency to treat manipulation as entertainment.[7]

##### The Game Between Clan Rider and Clan Horseman
Einer later became a central participant in the political dispute between [[Niklaus Horseman]] and [[Victoria Fulger Elderblood|Victoria Rider]]. The conflict concerned the position formerly held by Clan Fulger and culminated in an official game sanctioned under [[Nightingale]]'s system for resolving conflicts between major Vampire families.[9]
The agreed game was a one-on-one duel. Clan Horseman wagered its newly acquired Vampire Count title, while Clan Rider wagered Victoria's enormous corporate conglomerate. Victory would be determined if a participant surrendered or had their heart destroyed, while permanent death remained prohibited.[9]
Niklaus chose Einer as his representative because Zwei was unable to participate and Niklaus refused to entrust such an important matter to Jessica. He also used the opportunity to remind Einer of his previous failure to capture an Alpha Werewolf, stating that arrogance had caused the earlier mission to fail despite the resources provided to him. Because his new opponent was expected to be a Vampire close to his level and restricted from using his full Power, Niklaus declared that Einer would have no acceptable excuse for losing.[9]
Einer responded much more seriously than usual and promised his father that he would not fail again.[9]
When the participants were publicly revealed, Einer was identified as the 105-year-old heir to Clan Horseman. His opponent was [[Tatsuya Rider]], publicly presented as a ninety-year-old mercenary despite secretly being Victoria's son.[1]
The battle began with both fighters immediately disappearing from ordinary sight and colliding in the center of the arena. Einer created a completely black Rapier with his Power while simultaneously attempting to immobilize Tatsuya's feet using a black substance emerging from the ground. He also generated black thorns capable of attacking from unexpected angles.[1]
Tatsuya initially appeared to be caught by these abilities, but the entire exchange was revealed to have occurred inside the strange perceptual phenomenon created by his own Power. Even many young Vampires in the audience had unknowingly perceived the false sequence of events as reality.[1][5]
Einer gradually began recognizing that his perception was being manipulated. Tatsuya repeatedly forced him to question whether wounds, attacks and even Tatsuya himself were real. Einer responded by generating large numbers of black thorns and attempting to overwhelm the battlefield rather than rely upon accurate perception, but Tatsuya continued slipping through his attacks.[5]
Increasingly frustrated, Einer finally unleashed his [[Vampire Count Form]]. A pillar of black Energy erupted from him as his body darkened, grew larger and manifested wings composed of Power. The transformation was extraordinary for a Vampire only 105 years old, and even Victoria—herself familiar with the abilities of Count families—was alarmed by how young Einer was while already capable of accessing it.[5][3]
Using the enormous increase in Power provided by the transformation, Einer overwhelmed Tatsuya's perceptual manipulation through sheer force. His black Energy spread throughout the surroundings and caused the apparent Reality created around them to decay, forcing Tatsuya's true position to become visible.[3]
Einer then generated black thorns from his wings and bombarded the arena. The thorns possessed a visibly decaying effect wherever they struck. When Tatsuya continued evading them, Einer created a massive Cocoon of black thorns around the battlefield and used it to continuously attack from multiple directions.[3]
Tatsuya countered with a powerful lightning-based sword technique inherited from his father. The resulting dragon-shaped attack destroyed Einer's cocoon and forced him to evade. Recognizing that remaining transformed while failing to hit the faster swordsman was wasting his limited stamina, Einer voluntarily released the Count Form and returned to close-range Rapier combat.[3]
The change demonstrated considerable adaptability. After observing Tatsuya long enough, Einer became capable of distinguishing his opponent from the false images created by his Power and began engaging him directly with his Rapier. Their duel was still unresolved when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s desire to fight became impossible to contain.[3][10]

##### Victor vs. Tatsuya and Einer
[[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett|Scathach Scarlett]] arranged for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to enter the game as a separate participant, transforming the duel into a three-way confrontation. Einer immediately disliked Victor's excited smile and, once the match began, rushed directly at him while creating a black Rapier and attempting to pierce his head.[2]
The difference between them was immediately demonstrated. Victor covered one finger with ice and stopped Einer's Rapier using that single finger before striking the air with a martial arts punch. The resulting blast hurled Einer across the arena, forced him to cough blood and began freezing his body.[2]
Einer nevertheless survived and returned to the fight. Realizing that neither he nor Tatsuya could realistically confront Victor alone, the former enemies silently agreed to cooperate.[8]
Einer immediately returned to his [[Vampire Count Form]]. He generated an enormous black thorn beneath Victor, successfully impaling his stomach and lifting him into the air, then launched a barrage of additional thorns from his wings. Victor's regeneration and elemental defenses rendered the damage temporary, but Einer's attack succeeded in demonstrating that his Power could injure him if it connected.[8]
Victor retaliated with simultaneous barrages of ice and lightning, with struck areas subsequently bursting into unnatural fire. Tatsuya's superior speed allowed him to evade many attacks, but Einer's comparatively poor aerial mobility forced him to create barriers of black thorns for defense. Victor's combined assault eventually broke through those defenses and struck him repeatedly.[8]
The confrontation escalated when Victor generated an enormous spike of ice large enough to cover the arena and then saturated the entire structure with lightning. Faced with an attack neither could stop individually, Einer and Tatsuya developed a coordinated response.[6]
Einer extended his hand toward Tatsuya and covered him in his own dark Power. Tatsuya's golden lightning became black lightning, and he immediately felt physically stronger. Einer explained that the enhancement would increase Tatsuya's strength while also helping protect him from Victor's fire.[6]
Einer then generated several enormous black thorns from beneath the arena. The thorns rotated, combined and formed a gigantic structure that collided directly with Victor's descending ice spike. The recoil severely damaged Einer's own body, but he continued holding the attack back long enough for Tatsuya to perform his strongest sword strike.[6]
Once Tatsuya attacked, Einer withdrew his thorns and allowed the massive ice structure to descend. Empowered by Einer's Power, Tatsuya successfully cut Victor's attack in half, producing one of the few moments during the battle when the two participants managed to overcome one of Victor's major techniques through teamwork.[6]
The effort exhausted both of them. Even after Einer's Count Form disappeared and he struggled to remain standing, he still refused to admit that the fight was over. Tatsuya likewise forced himself back onto his feet, and both prepared to continue confronting Victor.[4]
Victor was deeply impressed by their determination and openly declared that he liked both fighters. However, because neither was in a condition to continue providing a meaningful fight, he approached them and rendered both unconscious with simple flicks to their foreheads. Victor was consequently declared the winner.[4]
[[Scathach]]'s assessment afterward was notably favorable toward Einer. She considered both young Vampires talented, but specifically highlighted Einer as particularly impressive because he could already access the Vampire Count Form and possessed substantial control over it despite his age. His principal limitation was his stamina, as he could maintain the transformation only for a limited period before exhaustion became severe.[4]
Victor's intervention did not determine the original wager between Clan Rider and Clan Horseman. Scathach had technically entered Victor into a separate game, meaning the original political duel remained unresolved. Victoria and Niklaus ultimately agreed to treat their dispute as a tie because the circumstances surrounding Victor had changed the political situation too dramatically to continue recklessly.[4]

### Skills & Abilities
Einer is a highly talented Noble Vampire who, despite being only 105 years old during his principal battle, had already achieved a transformation normally associated with the descendants of [[Vampire Counts]]. His abilities primarily revolve around a strange black Power with thorn-like and decaying properties.

#### Vampire Physiology
- Noble Vampire Physiology: Einer is a naturally born Noble Vampire and possesses the enhanced physical abilities, senses, durability and regeneration associated with his species.
- Superhuman Strength: Einer can engage other highly trained Noble Vampires in direct combat and becomes drastically stronger in his [[Vampire Count Form]].[1][3]
- Superhuman Speed: He can move fast enough to disappear from the perception of ordinary observers and engage Tatsuya in high-speed sword combat.[1]
- Enhanced Durability: Einer survived being struck across the arena by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s martial arts technique, partially frozen and repeatedly hit by Victor's elemental attacks before continuing to fight.[2][8]
- Enhanced Endurance: Even after suffering significant recoil from directly contesting Victor's enormous ice-lightning attack, Einer remained conscious and attempted to continue the battle.[6][4]
- Regeneration: As a Noble Vampire, Einer possesses the regenerative abilities of his race, although no extraordinary regeneration feat comparable to Victor's is individually attributed to him.

#### Black Power
Einer possesses an unusual supernatural Power manifesting as black Energy. Its exact formal name has not yet been stated. The Power is repeatedly associated with black thorns, physical deterioration and a decaying effect, so those properties are described directly rather than assigning it an unsupported Divinity or conceptual classification.
- Black Energy Manipulation: Einer can create, project and shape a dark supernatural Energy for numerous offensive and supportive applications.[1][3]
- Weapon Creation: Einer can condense his Power into a completely black Rapier and use it as his primary close-range weapon.[1]
- Black [[Dr. Thorn|Thorn]] Creation: His most frequently demonstrated technique is the generation of black thorns from the ground, his surroundings or his own wings.[1][3]
- Remote Thorn Attacks: Einer can cause thorns to emerge at distance and attack an opponent from directions separate from his own position.[1]
- Binding: He can create a black slime-like substance from the ground to immobilize an opponent's feet, preventing immediate movement.[1]
- Decay Effect: Surfaces struck by Einer's thorns visibly begin deteriorating or decaying. Tatsuya consequently avoided allowing the thorns to touch his Katana.[3]
- Power Field: In [[Vampire Count Form]], Einer can spread his Power throughout a large surrounding area. The pressure of this Power was strong enough to forcibly break through Tatsuya's perception-based ability and cause the false Reality surrounding them to collapse.[3]
- Cocoon: Einer can surround an area with an enormous enclosure composed of black thorns. He used the structure to restrict Tatsuya's movement and continuously launch attacks throughout the enclosed battlefield.[3]
- Thorn Barrage: Using the wings manifested in his Count Form, Einer can fire large quantities of thorns as projectiles.[3]
- Thorn Barrier: Einer can concentrate his thorns defensively to shield himself from incoming attacks, although [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s combined elemental assault eventually overwhelmed these defenses.[8]
- Giant Thorn Formation: Einer can summon enormous thorns from beneath the ground and combine them into a massive rotating structure. He used this technique to directly contest Victor's arena-sized ice spike long enough for Tatsuya to destroy it.[6]
- Power Bestowal: Einer can channel his black Power into another person.[6]
- Physical Enhancement: When he coated Tatsuya in his Power, Tatsuya immediately felt stronger.[6]
- Energy Alteration: Tatsuya's normally golden lightning changed into black lightning while empowered by Einer.[6]
- Elemental Protection: Einer stated that his Power would help protect Tatsuya from Victor's fire while simultaneously increasing his strength.[6]

#### Vampire Count Form
- Vampire Count Transformation: Despite being only 105, Einer had already unlocked the powerful transformation associated with the children and descendants of [[Vampire Counts]].[5][3]
- Massive Physical Amplification: Einer's raw physical and supernatural Power increases drastically while transformed, allowing him to break through Tatsuya's unusual Power through overwhelming force.[3]
- Power Amplification: His black Energy expands enough to affect large portions of the battlefield and substantially increases the size and quantity of his thorn attacks.[3]
- Black Wings: The form manifests large wings composed of black Power behind Einer.[5]
- Flight: Einer can fly while transformed, although his aerial mobility was noticeably inferior to Tatsuya's lightning-enhanced movement.[3][8]
- Enhanced [[Dr. Thorn|Thorn]] Generation: His wings can directly generate enormous barrages of black thorns.[3]
- Transformation Control: [[Scathach]] specifically recognized Einer as possessing substantial control of the [[Vampire Count Form]] despite his young age.[4]
- Limited Duration: Einer's principal weakness is the amount of Energy required to sustain the form. During his fight with Tatsuya, he recognized that he could maintain it only for a few minutes before becoming severely exhausted and voluntarily reverted to conserve strength.[3][4]

#### Combat Skills
- Rapier Proficiency: Einer is skilled with the Rapier generated from his Power. After adapting to Tatsuya's perceptual ability, he abandoned broad Energy attacks and successfully engaged the swordsman directly in close combat.[3]
- Adaptive Combat: Despite his impulsive demeanor, Einer is capable of recognizing when a strategy is ineffective and changing tactics accordingly. He stopped relying on his Count Form when it became inefficient and eventually learned to distinguish Tatsuya from his false images.[3]
- Battlefield Analysis: Einer rapidly recognized that neither he nor Tatsuya could defeat [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] independently and cooperated with his former opponent without wasting time on pride.[8]
- Teamwork: Einer proved unexpectedly effective in cooperative combat. He empowered Tatsuya, protected him and directly stalled Victor's strongest attack so Tatsuya could deliver the decisive strike against it.[6]
- High Pain Tolerance: Einer continued fighting despite being struck by Victor, suffering injuries and enduring severe recoil from his own large-scale technique.[2][6]

### Weaknesses
- Limited Stamina: Einer cannot maintain the [[Vampire Count Form]] indefinitely. At the age of 105, the transformation consumed enough Energy that only several minutes of continuous use risked leaving him completely exhausted.[3][4]
- Impulsiveness: Einer frequently allows irritation or excitement to dictate his decisions. He wasted Energy attempting to overwhelm Tatsuya with attacks he could not reliably land and immediately attacked [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s head when their fight began.[3][2]
- Arrogance: Niklaus directly blamed Einer's arrogance for an earlier failed mission involving an Alpha Werewolf.[9]
- Limited Aerial Mobility: Although his Count Form grants flight, Einer was noticeably less maneuverable in the air than Tatsuya and was forced to rely upon his thorns defensively during Victor's aerial bombardment.[8]

### Relationships

#### Niklaus Horseman
[[Niklaus Horseman]] is Einer's father and former head of the [[Horseman Clan]]. Einer appears to genuinely care about his father's opinion despite his normally unserious behavior.
Niklaus was deeply disappointed after Einer failed an earlier mission involving the capture of an Alpha Werewolf and explicitly warned him that the battle against Tatsuya represented his final opportunity to redeem that failure. Einer immediately abandoned his usual joking tone and promised his father that he would succeed.[9]
Niklaus nevertheless trusted Einer enough to make him the Horseman Clan's representative in a game where the family's Vampire Count title itself was at stake.[9]
Following Einer's defeat against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Niklaus did not irrationally blame his son. He acknowledged that the circumstances had moved beyond Einer's control and privately recognized that his son had still learned from the confrontation.[4]

#### Zwei Horseman
[[Zwei Horseman]] is Einer's older twin brother. The twins possess almost opposite personalities. Einer is loud, playful and constantly expressive, whereas Zwei is emotionally subdued, formal and speaks only when necessary.[7]
Einer frequently attempts to provoke reactions from Zwei, including physically manipulating his brother's face into a smile while joking that Zwei was secretly excited. Zwei normally tolerates the behavior without visible emotion.[7]
Despite their differences, Zwei closely watches Einer's battles and showed concern when his brother activated the [[Vampire Count Form]] against Tatsuya.[3]

#### Jessica Horseman Snow
[[Jessica Horseman Snow]] is Einer's younger sister. During his battle with Tatsuya, Jessica carefully observed Einer's Vampire Count transformation because it was an ability she herself had not yet completely mastered and hoped to understand better by watching him.[3]

#### Tatsuya Rider
[[Tatsuya Rider]] began as Einer's opponent during the game between Clan Rider and Clan Horseman. Their personalities and fighting styles were almost complete opposites: Einer relied upon aggressive supernatural attacks, improvisation and overwhelming Power, while Tatsuya remained calm, precise and difficult to perceive.[1][5]
Einer initially found Tatsuya's Power infuriating, but gradually adapted to it and earned enough understanding to fight him directly.[3]
Their rivalry immediately turned into cooperation once [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] entered the battle. Both recognized that continuing to fight each other would be meaningless against the stronger opponent and coordinated surprisingly well despite having been enemies moments earlier.[8][6]

#### Victor Elderblood
Einer's first direct battle with [[Victor Elderblood]] was overwhelmingly one-sided. He initially disliked Victor's smile and attempted to kill or seriously wound him immediately, only for Victor to stop his Rapier with a single ice-covered finger.[2]
Despite the difference in strength, Einer repeatedly returned to the fight and eventually worked with Tatsuya to counter Victor's attacks. Their combined effort successfully destroyed one of Victor's largest techniques.[6]
Victor came away from the battle with considerable respect for Einer and Tatsuya's determination. Even when exhausted, Einer still attempted to continue fighting, prompting Victor to declare that he liked both men before knocking them unconscious because they were no longer capable of giving him the fight he wanted.[4]
[[Scathach]] similarly recognized Einer's potential and specifically considered him the more noteworthy talent of the pair in terms of raw Vampire development.[4]
