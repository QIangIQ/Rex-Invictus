### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 59
Jessica Horseman Snow is a Noble Vampire and the youngest child of Radu Tepes, formerly known as [[Niklaus Horseman]], and [[Alienova Constantine]]. Originally raised as a member of the [[Horseman Clan]], Jessica was unaware for most of her life that her mother descended directly from the main line of the [[Snow Clan]]. Unlike her brothers, whose origins were tied to Radu's experiments and the blood of his brother, Jessica was naturally born carrying two exceptionally powerful Noble Vampire bloodlines: the blood of the Tepes/Horseman lineage through her father and the direct Snow lineage through her mother.[1]
Jessica first appeared as one of [[Johnny Lykos]]' lovers and an associate of the supernatural community surrounding [[The Lost Club]]. Her relationship with Johnny was complicated by the ancient hostility between Vampires and [[Werewolves]], with Jessica gradually realizing that Johnny unconsciously treated her differently from his other women because she was a Vampire.[2] Years later, during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s war against [[New Dawn]], Radu revealed Jessica's maternal ancestry and entrusted her with a Snow Clan Relic forged from the heart of [[Fafnir]]. The Relic awakened her dormant Snow bloodline, transformed her black hair into platinum white and automatically transported her to the protection of her maternal Clan.[1]
Jessica thereafter adopted the name Jessica Snow and began living among the Snow family. Her awakening exposed the characteristic temperament of the Snow main line while simultaneously giving her access to its Fire alongside the corruptive Black Blood inherited from her father. Although initially deeply suspicious of Agnes, Violet and Victor's faction, she was ultimately accepted as family rather than treated merely as the daughter of an enemy.[3]

### Physical Description
Jessica is a relatively short and slender young woman standing 168 centimeters tall. Before awakening her maternal bloodline, she possessed pale skin, long slightly curly black hair, dark-blue eyes and a comparatively modest figure with B-cup breasts.[4]
Her original appearance differed considerably from the women of the Snow main line because the characteristics inherited from her mother had remained dormant throughout most of her life. This changed when the Relic containing [[Fafnir]]'s heart activated to protect her during [[New Dawn]]'s destruction. Flames covered Jessica's body as her dormant Snow lineage awakened, and her black hair rapidly changed into the characteristic platinum-white coloration associated with the Snow family.[1]
After joining the [[Snow Clan]], Jessica consequently came to resemble her maternal relatives much more strongly. Despite the newly awakened coloration, she retained her original dark-blue eyes and overall facial characteristics.

### Personality and Traits
Jessica is intelligent, independent and considerably more perceptive than her early status as the [[Horseman Clan]]'s "third heiress" would initially suggest. Radu and her brothers rarely included her directly in their plans, but rather than remaining ignorant, Jessica independently discovered information by listening to conversations and examining documents. This allowed her to uncover the Horseman Clan's involvement with Lucy, their interest in [[Adam William Lykos]]' Alpha blood and the experiments involving artificial Hybrids.[2]
She is capable of genuine loyalty even when that loyalty places her against her own family. Upon discovering that her brothers intended to target Adam, Jessica personally warned Johnny despite knowing that doing so meant revealing secret Horseman information. When she learned that Adam was Johnny's father, her concern immediately became more personal, and she specifically warned Johnny not to die if he became involved.[2]
Jessica's relationship with Johnny also exposes a more insecure side of her personality. Although she cared for him, she became increasingly convinced that a Vampire and Werewolf could never truly form a stable relationship. Johnny claimed not to care about the conflict between their species, yet Jessica noticed that he unconsciously behaved differently toward her than toward his other lovers. Their relationship had lasted only approximately two months by Chapter 60 and had made little progress, leaving Jessica increasingly uncertain about whether Johnny truly accepted her.[2]
She is also prideful and possesses a distinctly Noble Vampire mentality. During her early life she regarded Hybrids negatively, only to immediately realize the hypocrisy of that attitude because any hypothetical child between herself and Johnny would itself be a Vampire-Werewolf Hybrid. This demonstrates that Jessica is capable of recognizing contradictions within her own prejudices even if abandoning deeply ingrained supernatural attitudes is difficult.[2]
Jessica can be surprisingly innocent in personal situations despite her upbringing. When [[Victor Elderblood]] accidentally entered her bedroom while she was undressed, Jessica initially froze completely before becoming furious and embarrassed. Victor's complete indifference to her nudity paradoxically offended her even further, and after he left she panicked over the idea that she could no longer marry because a man had seen her naked.[4]
Following the awakening of her Snow lineage, Jessica began displaying the supernatural emotional characteristics associated with the Clan's main line. Snow women are naturally extremely temperamental, and sufficiently intense emotions directly influence their flames and surroundings. Jessica consequently developed much more drastic mood swings after awakening, becoming rapidly irritated, embarrassed or anxious while her Fire reacted to those emotions.[3]
She is initially horrified upon learning that the Snow main line also tends to develop extreme obsessions. Having witnessed Agnes and Violet's personalities from the outside, Jessica desperately hoped this characteristic was not hereditary. Yuki's refusal to reassure her only increased her anxiety. The narration nevertheless establishes that women of the Snow main lineage inevitably develop some powerful obsession or passion toward something.[3]
Despite her temper, Jessica remains cautious and highly survival-oriented. When she first arrived among the [[Snow Clan]], she assumed the worst possibilities: political imprisonment, arranged marriage or exploitation of her bloodline. She even resolved that she would rather kill herself than be reduced to breeding stock. At the same time, she was practical enough to recognize when she was surrounded by beings vastly stronger than herself and avoided recklessly attacking Agnes or Violet despite becoming defensive over her mother's Relic.[3]
Jessica places enormous emotional value upon the few people she considers family. Before discovering the Snow Clan connection, she had effectively regarded Radu and her brothers as her only genuine relatives. Her mother's necklace therefore became exceptionally precious to her because it represented one of the only remaining connections she possessed to Alienova. When Agnes temporarily took the Relic from her, Jessica immediately entered a fight-or-flight state and internally resolved to recover it even if doing so killed her.[3]

### Synopsis

#### Vampire Society
Jessica first appeared at [[The Lost Club]], where she was associated with [[Johnny Lykos]], Jinsei, Judy and Roberta. At this point she was known exclusively as Jessica Horseman and was one of Johnny's newer lovers, having known him for approximately two months.[5][2]
Unlike Johnny's longer relationships, Jessica's position within his group was already emotionally unstable. Although Johnny openly disregarded the traditional prohibition against Vampires and [[Werewolves]] associating with one another, Jessica perceived that his instincts still caused him to unconsciously treat her differently. When Johnny became aggressive after hearing that members of the [[Horseman Clan]] intended to target his family, Jessica interpreted his reaction as further evidence that their racial difference would eventually make the relationship impossible.[2]
Jessica nevertheless demonstrated loyalty toward Johnny by warning him about her own family's activities. She had discovered that her brothers, [[Zwei Horseman]] and [[Einer Horseman]], were cooperating with Lucy's operation and intended to hunt [[Adam William Lykos]] for the blood of an Alpha Werewolf. The Alpha blood was apparently intended to help stabilize the unnatural Hybrid being developed through the operation. Jessica was not officially included in the Horseman Clan's plans; as the third heir, her father and brothers generally withheld sensitive information from her, forcing her to uncover it independently.[2]
When Johnny revealed that Adam was his father, the situation became much more serious. Jessica warned Johnny that becoming involved would place him directly against her brothers but understood immediately that he could not simply abandon his family. Although she attempted to hide her emotions, she ultimately left after simply telling Johnny not to die. The incident further exposed the instability in their relationship, with the people around Johnny warning him that if he continued failing to address Jessica's feelings he would eventually lose her.[2]
Jessica later returned to the Horseman Clan's Eastern territory. After the game involving [[Tatsuya Rider]], Einer Horseman and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Victor personally traveled to the Horseman mansion looking for Einer. Due to incorrect directions from the butler James, Victor instead accidentally entered Jessica's bedroom while she was completely undressed.[4]
Jessica froze in embarrassment while Victor merely examined her appearance and asked for her identity. Upon hearing that she was Einer's sister, Victor immediately ignored the awkward circumstances and began questioning her about him. Jessica informed Victor that Einer had fallen into a coma after exhausting himself during the game and was still recovering.[4]
Despite their strange introduction, Jessica quickly became curious about Victor. She was surprised when he simply accepted her explanation regarding Einer and later helped him by explaining where the giant apes inhabiting the Eastern territory could be found. Victor thanked her and left casually, only for Jessica to remember after the door closed that he had seen her naked. She consequently screamed that she could no longer get married.[4]
Jessica's early story remained closely tied to the Horseman family and her relationship with Johnny, but she was never deeply involved in Radu's larger conspiracy. This distinction became extremely important much later, when Radu's enemies finally closed in upon [[New Dawn]].

#### Dragon God of Chaos
During the [[Supernatural Gathering]], [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] publicly declared war against the [[Egyptian Pantheon]], the Elder Gods operating in [[Nightingale]] and [[New Dawn]]. He specifically addressed [[Niklaus Horseman]] and James, making it clear that he had never forgotten their previous actions. Radu immediately understood the implication and began preparing to escape rather than attempt to confront Victor directly.[1]
Jessica was living within New Dawn's headquarters at the time and had become visibly exhausted by the life her father intended for the family. When Radu prepared to flee into another universe, Jessica refused to accompany him, explaining that she did not want to escape to the place he intended to take her and her brothers.[1]
Rather than force her to follow, Radu made an unexpected decision. He ordered Jessica to return to Nightingale and seek protection from the [[Snow Clan]]. Jessica had no idea why he would send her to another Vampire family, prompting Radu to finally reveal the truth surrounding her mother.[1]
[[Alienova Constantine]] had been the illegitimate daughter of [[Alienova Snow]], herself the sister of Agnes Snow's mother. Jessica was therefore not merely connected distantly to Clan Snow: she was a direct descendant of a woman belonging to its main lineage. Combined with Radu's own blood, this made Jessica the natural carrier of two of the most exceptional lineages existing among Noble Vampires.[1]
Radu further revealed that this distinction separated Jessica from her brothers. Jessica had been born naturally from Radu and Alienova, inheriting both powerful bloodlines directly, whereas his sons had been created through different circumstances involving the blood of Radu's brother.[1]
Despite his hatred for Victor, Radu admitted through his actions that he trusted Victor's protection of family more than virtually any other option available to him. Since the Snow Clan belonged to Victor's family, Radu believed that Jessica would be safe once accepted among them. He therefore preferred entrusting his daughter to the family of his greatest enemy rather than bringing her into whatever dangerous future awaited him.[1]
Before sending her away, Radu placed a collar around Jessica's neck that had belonged to her mother. The collar was a lost Snow Clan Relic created from the heart of [[Fafnir]]. Unlike the Clan's sword, which contained Fafnir's Soul, the necklace contained the Dragon's heart. Although the sword was considered individually superior, using both Relics together was said to allow their users to wield flames resembling those of Fafnir himself.[1]
The Relic could only be properly used by members of the Snow main family and therefore served as both protection and proof of Jessica's ancestry. Radu had originally intended to exploit Jessica's Snow blood to eventually interfere with or control the Clan after consolidating power over Clan Fulger, but Victor's rise destroyed those plans. The Relic consequently became the means by which he could save his daughter instead.[1]
As Victor's forces attacked New Dawn, Radu deliberately struck Jessica hard enough to render her unconscious. The attack triggered the Relic's protective function. Fafnir's flames covered Jessica, awakened her dormant Snow lineage and changed her black hair to platinum white. The Relic then transported her away from the collapsing New Dawn headquarters and toward the protection of her maternal family.[1]

#### The Snow Clan
Jessica subsequently found herself within the territory taken from the defeated [[Egyptian Pantheon]] and occupied by the [[Snow Clan]]. The sudden transition left her deeply uncomfortable. She had spent her life believing that Radu and her brothers represented essentially her entire family; now she was surrounded by white-haired Vampires calling themselves her relatives and discovering that her supposedly missing mother had concealed an enormous secret.[3]
The necklace containing [[Fafnir]]'s heart remained around her neck. Jessica regarded it primarily as a precious memory of Alienova rather than merely a powerful Artifact, and the idea of losing it caused immediate distress. She also realized that her awakened lineage had removed one of the greatest weaknesses associated with ordinary Vampires: she no longer needed to fear sunlight because the Snow bloodline's affinity with Fire provided its characteristic immunity.[3]
Her integration was complicated by the psychological traits accompanying the Snow blood. Jessica initially remained awake for long periods because she feared what her new relatives might do with her. She considered possibilities ranging from political marriage to being used for reproduction because of the exceptional value of her two bloodlines. Although some of her thoughts became deliberately ruthless—such as considering killing a hypothetical political husband and claiming his inheritance—they primarily reflected her uncertainty and survival instincts rather than actual Snow Clan plans.[3]
Stress also began activating the emotional properties of her awakened Fire. When Jessica became increasingly irritated while thinking about [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Johnny, Radu and her missing brothers, the environment around her began heating up. Fafnir's Heart dramatically amplified her flames, causing the surrounding material to melt before Jessica even realized what she was doing.[3]
[[Yuki Snow]] confronted her about the damage and became one of Jessica's first genuine connections within the Clan. Jessica found the idea of calling Yuki her cousin deeply unfamiliar because she had never possessed an extended family before. When the damage became obvious, Jessica demonstrated that her paternal inheritance remained active alongside her newly awakened Snow power: she released a corruptive black liquid from her hands using Radu's bloodline and temporarily reconstructed the damaged surroundings.[3]
Yuki then explained one of the less convenient aspects of Jessica's maternal heritage. Members of the Snow main line were notoriously temperamental, and their emotional instability could directly affect their surroundings through their Fire. Main-line Snow women also possessed tendencies toward psychotic or obsessive behavior, with each eventually developing some powerful obsession. Jessica was horrified at the idea that she might eventually resemble Agnes or Violet in this respect.[3]
Yuki subsequently escorted Jessica to meet [[Agnes Elderblood Snow]], [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] and [[Hilda Snow Elderblood]]. Jessica formally presented herself as Jessica Snow, acknowledging the new family even while remaining deeply wary of them. Agnes immediately teased her for omitting the Alucard name that Agnes and Violet themselves had adopted through Victor.[3]
The conversation became serious when Violet asked about Jessica's lost Snow Relic. Jessica instinctively became defensive, but Violet explained that Clan Relics normally chose their wielders; the fact that Fafnir's Heart remained with Jessica even in the presence of Agnes and Violet already demonstrated its recognition of her bloodline.[3]
Agnes nevertheless showed that, as Clan Leader, she could still command the Relic. She summoned the necklace directly from Jessica and compared its resonance with the Sword of Fafnir. Jessica immediately entered a defensive state and was prepared to risk death rather than permanently lose the only object she still possessed from her mother. The matching resonance finally removed Agnes and Violet's remaining doubts: Jessica genuinely was a direct descendant of the Snow main lineage.[3]
Agnes returned the necklace and declared that Jessica would remain with them because she was family. Violet agreed, noting that Jessica had not personally played a meaningful role in Radu's conspiracy and therefore should not simply be punished for her father's actions. Her blood nevertheless made her extraordinarily valuable: Jessica simultaneously carried the Snow main lineage and the lineage of the Vampire King's family through Radu.[3]
Violet also recognized Jessica's usefulness as leverage should Radu ever return, but her deeper plan was considerably more personal. Rather than imprison Jessica or force her loyalty, Violet intended to give her a safe and healthy environment, allow her to form genuine relationships with the Snow family and gradually give her reasons to consider the Clan her home. Violet trusted that the famously intense emotional attachments of the Snow bloodline would eventually make betrayal nearly impossible if Jessica developed an obsession centered upon someone within their family.[3]
When Hilda questioned why Violet was so interested in Jessica's eventual integration, Violet openly compared the situation to the [[Fulger Clan]], whose main-line women had all become bound to Victor. She believed the Snow Clan should eventually achieve a similarly unified family structure. This is the basis for Jessica's status as a potential future love interest for Victor, but Chapter 897 does not establish that Jessica herself was already romantically interested in Victor or that any marriage had occurred.[3]
Jessica's fate therefore changed completely following [[New Dawn]]'s destruction. She went from the isolated daughter of Radu Tepes and a largely secondary Horseman heir to one of the rare Vampires possessing two powerful Noble bloodlines simultaneously, recognized by a lost Dragon Relic and accepted into the main family of one of [[Nightingale]]'s oldest Count Clans.

- Agnes and Violet chose to accept Jessica as family despite her father's actions because Jessica herself had not significantly participated in Radu's conspiracy.[3]
