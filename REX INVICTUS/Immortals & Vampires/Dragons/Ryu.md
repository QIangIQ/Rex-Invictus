### Appearances

#### First Mention
Chapter 1189 (Individual ancestor; Ryuji lineage established much earlier)
Ryu is an indexing designation for the currently unnamed Ryuji Dragon God from whom [[Ryujin Ikaros]] descends. The novel does not presently establish that "Ryu" is the individual's personal name; Chapter 1189 identifies Ryujin as descended from "a Ryu", immediately explaining the term as a Japanese Dragon God. The Ryuji themselves are not a newly introduced Dragon lineage, having appeared much earlier as one of Japan's established Yokai Dragon Clans. Chapter 1189 instead provides the first direct information about Ryujin's particular Divine ancestry and confirms that his family descends from a Japanese Dragon who had attained Godhood.[1]
Earlier material identifies the Ryuji as Japanese Dragons belonging to the highest tier of traditional Yokai clans. They existed alongside the Oni and Kitsune as one of the major supernatural bloodlines under Japan's Yokai society, possessed their own organized Dragon Clan and participated in the development of the supernatural arts later used by [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]]'s faction.[2] This earlier information provides important context for Chapter 1189: Ryujin's "Japanese Dragon side" is not an unidentified form of generic Dragon ancestry, but belongs to a Dragon lineage already established within Japan centuries earlier.
By Ryujin's generation, however, the Divine quality of that ancestry had become heavily diluted. [[Victor Elderblood]] recognized the weakened Dragon lineage within him and purified it until its quality once again approached that of his ancestors. Once restored, the bloodline gave Ryujin the potential—with training and the resources of the [[Elderblood Empire]]—to eventually become an elite warrior capable of destroying entire Solar Systems.[1] This provides indirect evidence of the exceptional quality preserved within the original Ryuji Divine lineage, but it does not establish the exact personal strength of the unnamed Dragon God indexed on this page.

### Physical Appearance
The individual Ryuji Dragon God from whom Ryujin descends has never appeared directly and possesses no confirmed personal physical description. The character's humanoid appearance, Dragon form, height, coloration, eyes, horns, scales and clothing remain unknown.[1]
Earlier material does establish that the Ryuji are a form of Japanese Dragon rather than members of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later Blood Dragon lineage. They existed within Japanese supernatural society long before Victor became a Dragon and were already treated as an established Dragon Clan among the Yokai.[2]
The older Yokai material associates Ryuji with Draconic physical characteristics and identifies them as particularly durable among Japanese supernatural beings.[3] These are racial characteristics of the Ryuji lineage and should not be used to invent a specific design for Ryujin's Divine ancestor.
Any artwork currently used for this character should therefore be treated as an artistic interpretation.

### Personality
Ryu has no direct dialogue or on-page characterization, so no detailed personality can presently be established.
Some characteristics of the ancestral bloodline remain visible through [[Ryujin Ikaros]]. Most prominently, Ryujin possesses an unusually powerful attraction toward shiny and valuable objects. His compulsive collecting is explicitly identified as a product of his Japanese Dragon side, demonstrating that recognizable Draconic instincts persisted even after the lineage became substantially diluted.[1]
This does not establish that his ancestor personally behaved like Ryujin. Ryujin's laziness, promiscuity and preference for pleasure over training are his own personality traits and should not be retroactively assigned to the Ryuji Dragon God.
The modern Japanese Dragon Clan is also described as extremely proud of its ancestry. The name Ryujin is not given freely: only descendants considered worthy are permitted to carry it, and Ryujin Ikaros possessing the name is itself treated as an indication that he was considered talented by his clan.[1] Whether this naming tradition originated directly with the Divine ancestor remains unknown.

### Background

#### Ryuji — Japanese Dragons
The term Ryuji had already been associated with Japanese Dragons long before [[Ryujin Ikaros]] appeared. Within the traditional Yokai structure, the Ryuji were one of the principal Japanese supernatural bloodlines and possessed an organized Dragon Clan.[2]
They belonged to the upper level of Yokai society alongside the Oni and Kitsune clans. The Three Great Clans were distinguished from ordinary Yokai by their greater supernatural foundations and their ability to produce and manipulate Youki rather than depending entirely upon ambient Energy.[2]
Earlier descriptions also identify the Ryuji as exceptionally durable compared with other conventional Yokai, with their natural Draconic bodies standing above Oni, Kitsune and lesser Yokai in this particular physical characteristic.[3]
The Ryuji should therefore be understood as an established Japanese Dragon lineage rather than another name for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s [[Blood Dragons]], the Western Dragons encountered elsewhere in the story or the later [[True Dragons]] belonging to the Imperial family.

#### The Three Great Yokai Clans
During the period in which [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]] was reorganizing and strengthening the Yokai, the Dragon Clan existed alongside the Oni and Kitsune as one of Japan's major supernatural clans.[2]
These clans represented the strongest organized Yokai bloodlines and possessed advantages that ordinary Yokai did not. One of the most important was the ability to generate their own Youki. Ordinary Yokai largely relied upon Youki produced from the supernatural and emotional conditions associated with Japan, creating a serious weakness whenever they moved too far from their homeland. The Great Clans possessed a greater degree of independence because they could generate the Energy themselves, although Japan remained their natural environment.[2]
The existence of the Ryuji Dragon Clan centuries before Chapter 1189 makes [[Ryujin Ikaros]]' ancestry considerably more specific. His Japanese Dragon blood belongs to one of the old supernatural lineages that had already been active during the conflicts surrounding Haruna and the Yokai.

#### Dragon Veins and the Search for a New Energy
The Ryuji Clan was present during one of the most important developments in modern Yokai history: the discovery and study of the planetary Energy later used for [[Senjutsu]].[2]
The Yokai suffered from a fundamental limitation involving Youki. Their traditional Energy was closely connected to Japan and the negative emotional Energy generated by Humans, restricting how effectively most Yokai could operate away from their homeland. Haruna therefore sought another form of Energy that could be cultivated more universally.
The investigation led the Yokai to enormous currents of neutral Energy running through the planet, referred to as the Dragon Veins. These were later more clearly described as the planet's ley lines or "Mother Earth's Blood Veins", through which the Energy sustaining the natural world circulates.[2][4]
Haruna eventually developed a method of drawing this Energy into the body while maintaining a state of mental equilibrium. The resulting art became known as Senjutsu. Incorrect absorption was extremely dangerous because losing control of the foreign Nature Energy could damage the practitioner's body and spiritual foundation.[2]
Chapter 607 later clarified the deeper nature of the phenomenon when Haruna brought [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] directly to one of the ley-line chambers. The Energy flowing through the Dragon Veins was the natural life-supporting Energy of the planet, closely associated with the power produced by a World Tree. Victor immediately recognized its similarity to [[Roxanne Elderblood]]'s Energy because Roxanne was his own personal World Tree.[4]
There is currently no evidence that Ryujin's Divine ancestor personally discovered Senjutsu or the Dragon Veins. These events belong to the later history of the Ryuji Clan and wider Yokai civilization, but they demonstrate that the Dragon lineage continued functioning as a major Japanese supernatural faction long after the original Divine ancestor's era.

#### Divine Ancestry
Chapter 1189 adds information that the earlier Yokai material never explicitly provided: at least one branch of the Ryuji lineage traces its ancestry to a genuine Japanese Dragon God.[1]
[[Ryujin Ikaros]] is specifically described as descended from a Ryu, a Japanese Dragon God. He also belongs to a clan described as having been founded by a God, allowing him to immediately understand the seriousness of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] identifying the object attached to his Soul as the power of hostile Divine invaders.[1]
The exact relationship between the unnamed Ryuji Dragon God and the entire historical Ryuji Clan remains unclear. Several possibilities remain open:
- the Dragon God may have founded the entire Ryuji lineage;
- the Dragon God may have founded only Ryujin Ikaros' particular family branch;
- several ancient Ryuji may have eventually become Gods;
- or "Ryu" may itself refer broadly to a class of Divine Japanese Dragons rather than one unique ancestral individual.
Until later chapters provide further information, only Ryujin's direct descent from a Japanese Dragon God should be treated as certain.[1]

#### Dilution of the Divine Bloodline
Despite the prestige attached to Ryujin's ancestry, the Dragon blood present within him had become significantly diluted by Chapter 1189.[1]
The dilution was not severe enough to erase his heritage completely. Ryujin retained strong Draconic instincts, enough natural talent to receive the prestigious Ryujin name and a recognizable Dragon lineage that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] could immediately perceive. However, the quality of his blood no longer approached that possessed by his ancient ancestors.
This demonstrates that Japanese Dragon ancestry can persist over many generations even when the descendants are no longer equivalent to the original Divine progenitors of the line.

#### Restoration by Victor Elderblood
After saving Ryujin from the Divine fragment attached to his Soul, [[Victor Elderblood]] decided that the young Dragon was wasting the potential inherited through his ancestry. Victor secretly strengthened Ryujin's Dragon side while repairing his damaged Soul.[1]
Victor later described what he had done as a small improvement, but the narration clarifies that his definition of "small" was misleading. He had effectively purified Ryujin's diluted bloodline until it reached a quality comparable to that of his ancestors.[1]
The restoration did not transform Ryujin into a Blood Dragon, True Dragon or direct member of Victor's own lineage. Victor strengthened the Japanese Dragon blood Ryujin already possessed.[1]
With the ancestral lineage restored and the resources of the [[Elderblood Empire]] available to him, Ryujin gained the potential to become an elite Imperial warrior capable of destroying entire Solar Systems. Victor nevertheless made it clear that potential was not achievement; Ryujin would still have to train, develop and earn that strength himself.[1]

### Skills & Abilities
The individual Dragon God indexed as Ryu has never fought on-page. His personal techniques, Divine Concepts, elemental affinities and combat style remain unknown. The following should therefore distinguish between traits confirmed for the individual through Chapter 1189 and characteristics historically associated with the broader Ryuji lineage.

#### Dragon God Physiology
- Dragon Physiology: Ryu is explicitly identified as a Japanese Dragon God and therefore belonged to the same broad Draconic ancestry later inherited by [[Ryujin Ikaros]].[1]
- God Physiology: The ancestor had attained genuine Godhood. The generation, exact Divine ranking and circumstances of that ascension remain unknown.[1]
- Hereditary Draconic Bloodline: His Dragon nature was inheritable and survived through numerous generations despite significant dilution.[1]
- Draconic Instinct Inheritance: Ryujin's powerful attraction toward shiny objects is explicitly associated with his Japanese Dragon ancestry, proving that instinctive traits of the bloodline remained active long after its Divine origin.[1]
- High Ancestral Potential: Once Ryujin's bloodline was purified back toward ancestral quality, his potential increased enough for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to consider him a possible future Solar-System-class elite.[1]

#### Divine Nature
- Divinity: Ryu was a genuine Dragon God and therefore possessed a Divinity, although its Concept has never been named.[1]
- [[Divine Energy]]: As a God, Ryu possessed the Divine Energy associated with deities. No individual use has been depicted.
- Divine Soul: His existence necessarily included a Divine spiritual foundation capable of supporting Godhood. No unique Soul technique has been attributed to him.
- Divine Longevity: As a Dragon God, Ryu was not subject to ordinary Human aging. His actual age and whether he remains alive are unknown.

#### Ryuji Lineage Characteristics
The following abilities are associated with the broader Ryuji/Japanese Dragon lineage in earlier material. They provide context for the ancestor's race but should not automatically be treated as individually demonstrated feats.
- Youki Manipulation: Ryuji belong to the traditional Yokai supernatural system and can manipulate Youki.[2]
- Youki Generation: As one of the Three Great Yokai Clans, the Ryuji possess the ability to produce their own Youki rather than depending entirely upon ambient Youki from Japan.[2]
- Enhanced Durability: Earlier classifications identify Ryuji Dragons as possessing particularly durable bodies among conventional Yokai, exceeding the durability normally associated with Oni, Kitsune and lesser Yokai.[3]
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: As one of the strongest Yokai lineages, Ryuji possess physical capabilities considerably beyond ordinary Humans and weaker supernatural species.
- Draconic Instincts: Animalistic and treasure-oriented instincts are associated with the lineage, with Ryujin providing a later direct example of such inherited behavior.[1]
- [[Senjutsu]] (Learnable): Ryuji were part of the Yokai civilization that developed Senjutsu through the Nature Energy carried by the Dragon Veins. Senjutsu is a learned art rather than an inherent racial power and therefore should not be assumed to have been possessed by the ancient Dragon God himself.[2][4]

#### Unconfirmed Abilities
Older descriptions of Japanese Dragons sometimes attribute control over water, rain, storms, floods, clouds, transformation and other abilities to Ryuji. Much of this material appears to derive from general East Asian Dragon mythology rather than clearly demonstrated novel scenes.
Until the novel itself connects these abilities directly to the Ryuji, they should not be assigned to Ryu as personal powers merely because he is a Japanese Dragon God.
Likewise, no personal Dragon Breath, water Divinity, weather Divinity, flight method, shapeshifting technique, weapon, martial art or unique Divine Authority has yet been directly demonstrated for the individual ancestor.

### Power
Ryu's exact personal level of strength remains unknown. No battle, opponent, threat classification or direct comparison involving the Dragon God has been shown.[1]
His status as a genuine Dragon God nevertheless establishes that he stood above ordinary members of the diluted lineage inherited by his descendants. The strongest indirect evidence comes from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s purification of [[Ryujin Ikaros]]. When Ryujin's Dragon blood was restored to a quality comparable to that of his ancestors, Victor judged that he could eventually develop into an elite warrior capable of destroying Solar Systems.[1]
This does not mean the unnamed ancestor should automatically be classified as Solar System-level. Ryujin's potential exists within the enormously advanced [[Elderblood Empire]] and can be cultivated through resources, training systems and supernatural knowledge that were unavailable during earlier Japanese history.
Ryu should therefore remain at an Unknown Divine Dragon level until the story provides an individual feat or ranking.

### Relationships

#### Ryujin Ikaros
[[Ryujin Ikaros]] is a distant descendant of the Japanese Dragon God indexed as Ryu.[1]
Ryujin inherited enough of the ancestral lineage for his Dragon nature to remain obvious despite extensive dilution. His Draconic instincts, natural talent and position within the Japanese Dragon Clan all survived, although his bloodline no longer possessed its original quality until [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] restored it.[1]
There is no evidence that Ryujin personally met his Divine ancestor.

#### Ryuji Dragon Clan
The Ryuji Dragon Clan is the broader Japanese Dragon lineage with which Ryujin's ancestry is associated. It existed long before his birth and was already one of the major clans of Japanese Yokai society during Haruna's era.[2]
The clan participated in the supernatural development of Japan, including the period when the Yokai investigated the Dragon Veins and developed [[Senjutsu]] as an alternative to their traditional dependence upon Youki.[2][4]
Chapter 1189 reveals that Ryujin's particular branch possesses a Divine ancestral origin, but it does not yet establish whether the unnamed Dragon God founded every historical Ryuji family or only one branch of the larger clan.

#### Otsuki Elderblood Haruna
There is no confirmed personal relationship between Ryu and [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]]. Haruna existed far later in the known history of the Japanese Dragon lineage and eventually became the central leader of the broader Yokai faction in which the Ryuji Clan operated.
Her work with the Three Great Clans and the development of [[Senjutsu]] demonstrates that the descendants of the ancient Ryuji Dragons remained an active part of Japan's supernatural civilization.[2]

#### Amaterasu Elderblood
No direct relationship between Ryu and [[Amaterasu]] Elderblood has been established.
By [[Ryujin Ikaros]]' era, however, the modern Dragon Clan was connected to a Japanese supernatural hierarchy capable of escalating serious supernatural matters toward Amaterasu. When the clan could not understand the Divine fragment attached to Ryujin, they sought assistance from a friendly God, who contacted a higher-ranking deity, with the matter eventually progressing toward Amaterasu before Akasha and the Imperial Family intervened.[1]
Ryu should therefore not currently be listed as a member or subordinate of the [[Shinto Pantheon]] solely because he was a Japanese Dragon God.

### Indexing Notes
The individual is currently indexed as Ryu because Chapter 1189 describes [[Ryujin Ikaros]] as being descended from "a Ryu, a Japanese Dragon God".[1] Earlier chapters make it clear that Japanese Dragons already existed as the Ryuji Dragon lineage, making it considerably more likely that "Ryu" is functioning as a racial or cultural designation rather than being the ancestor's confirmed personal name.
The distinction can presently be understood as:
Term
Current Use
Ryuji
Established Japanese Dragon/Yokai lineage appearing in earlier volumes.[2]
Ryuji Dragon Clan
Organized Japanese Dragon Clan belonging to the major Yokai bloodlines.[2]
Ryu / Japanese Dragon God
Unnamed Divine ancestor specifically connected to Ryujin Ikaros in Chapter 1189.[1]
Ryujin
Prestigious name used by worthy descendants within Ryujin Ikaros' modern clan.[1]
This page should therefore remain primarily an index for Ryujin Ikaros' unnamed Divine ancestor. A separate Ryuji or Japanese Dragons race article should contain the broader history, biology, clan structure, Youki, [[Senjutsu]] and other characteristics belonging to Japanese Dragons as a species.
