Etherea is the central physical world of Victor Elderblood's private internal Reality and the principal family home of the Elderblood Clan. It began as an artificial planet constructed after Victor's destruction of the old Greek Pantheon, but its later history is inseparable from Victor's own Soul. The Universal Tree transferred the completed world into Victor's Personal Dimension, where it merged with the earlier planetary structure already existing within Victor and became a vastly larger Soul-bound world.[4][5]
The planet is metaphysically real. It possesses genuine terrain, a planetary core, ley lines, atmosphere, oceans, ecosystems and an actual Sun within its surrounding Dimension. Serious damage to its deepest foundations can affect Victor himself because the world is part of his internal existence rather than a detached illusion or symbolic visualization.[5]
Etherea's appearance deliberately resembles a natural paradise, yet its physical conditions are extreme enough to make it lethal to most ordinary beings. Surface gravity commonly reaches hundreds of times that of Earth, deeper areas possess far greater gravitational pressure, parts of the world approach Absolute-Zero conditions, oceans are extraordinarily deep and violent, and volcanic regions are directly influenced by Victor's Dragon Fire.[8][6]
The world was not designed simply as a comfortable residence. Victor intentionally allowed its environments to reflect different aspects of his own Powers and personality. Paradise, infernal landscapes, enormous oceans, lethal cold, extreme gravity and supernatural wilderness coexist across the same planet, making Etherea simultaneously a home, sanctuary, natural wonder and training world.[6]

### History

#### Predecessor Inner World
Etherea was not the first planetary structure inside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
Following Victor's evolution into the Blood Dragon Primogenitor, his Inner World had already undergone a massive transformation and developed into a planetary environment described as approximately one hundred times larger than Earth.[12]
This earlier world should be understood as a developmental predecessor within Victor's Soul.
The artificial planet created in Chapters 821–822 later entered Victor and merged with the existing internal planetary structure, producing the much larger world that became Victor's permanent Personal World.[5]

#### Creation of Etherea
Following the fall and replacement of the Greek divine order, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] decided to create an entirely new world rather than simply inherit [[Olympus]].
The project involved several of the most powerful members of his growing family and faction.[4]

##### Celestial Foundation
Nyx Elderblood located an enormous asteroid containing sufficient material to serve as the foundation of the new planet.
Natalia Elderblood Alioth used her mastery of Space together with Nyx to isolate, transport and stabilize the celestial body while construction proceeded.[4]
[[Gaia]] subsequently began reshaping the object into a genuine world.

##### Terrain and Planetary Geography
[[Gaia]] Elderblood played the central role in producing the physical landscape.
She reshaped the developing object's internal and external geography, establishing:
- continents;
- mountains;
- valleys;
- forests;
- enormous bodies of water;
- frozen territories;
- volcanic territories;
- and the general foundations required by a functional planetary ecosystem.[4]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] assisted with Creation-related powers while Gaia performed the specialized planetary shaping for which her Nature-related existence was exceptionally well suited.

##### Relocation of Existing Life
Bruna participated in the logistical stage by moving living beings, possessions and other material associated with the territories being transferred into the developing world.[4]
This should be distinguished from creating the planet itself.
Bruna helped relocate what would inhabit and furnish the emerging world rather than establishing its fundamental planetary structure.

##### Artificial Energy Veins
One of the largest challenges was reproducing systems that normally develop through the long-term influence of a World Tree.
[[Roxanne Elderblood]] saturated the forming world with enormous amounts of her Energy, while [[Jeanne Elderblood]] added [[Primordial Energy]] and helped accelerate the process.[4]
Together they established an artificial network comparable to the supernatural Energy veins that would normally emerge naturally around a mature World Tree.
The process that would ordinarily require an enormous period of natural development was compressed into hours through their combined power.[4]

##### Dragon Fire Core
After the planet's main structure had stabilized, [[Gaia]] opened an enormous cavity in the central region.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] filled the cavity with vast quantities of his characteristic violet Dragon Fire.
Gaia then sealed it, creating a planetary core directly infused with Victor's draconic power.[4]
The core was not merely decorative.
Its supernatural Energy would influence the planet over time, enriching the world and potentially producing unusual materials and resources associated with Victor's draconic nature.
Gaia immediately recognized that such resources could eventually become valuable enough to attract the greed of Gods and other supernatural powers.[4]

#### System Registration
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] did not simply create an isolated ball of matter.
He deliberately followed the larger cosmic process necessary to register a genuine supernatural world.
The System recognized the creation and temporarily assigned the new divine structure the numerical designation:
Pantheon 7475909777[4]
The System drew an enormous amount of Energy during the registration and expansion process, requiring Victor to provide approximately 95% of the necessary Energy expenditure associated with completing the new structure.[4]
This event is important because a genuine supernatural planet required more than a physical surface.
It needed integration with:
- Material existence;
- higher spiritual structures;
- Life;
- Souls;
- World Tree infrastructure;
- and the cosmological systems responsible for maintaining those functions.

#### Intervention of the Universal Tree
The creation of a legitimate new Life-bearing world attracted The Universal Tree.[5]
Normally, the Universal Tree would provide or assign an appropriate World Tree to a new world.
Etherea created an unusual problem.
[[Roxanne Elderblood]] already functioned as the relevant World Tree, but she was permanently rooted within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul rather than existing as an ordinary World Tree attached exclusively to an external planet.
Victor therefore intended to support Roxanne personally with the assistance of [[Gaia]], the Fairies and his family instead of treating Roxanne as the sole administrator of the entire world.[5]

#### Integration into Victor's Soul
[[The Universal Tree]] solved the structural problem by transferring the newly created planet into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
The event established one of the most important facts concerning Etherea:
the planet is physically real, but it exists inside a separate Dimension contained within Victor's Soul.[5]
Roxanne immediately felt her internal environment expand to accommodate the incoming world.
The artificial planet then began merging with the planetary structure already present inside Victor.
The resulting merged world became a Megaplanet several hundred times larger than Earth.[5]
The Universal Tree accelerated and stabilized the process.
He additionally created a genuine Sun inside the Dimension, causing the surrounding internal Space to expand sufficiently to contain the celestial system.[5]

#### Early Exploration
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his family explored the completed world after the merger stabilized.
They discovered a landscape dramatically different from the violent circumstances surrounding its creation.
Enormous forests, pristine valleys, waterfalls, streams, brilliant flowers and a clear blue sky produced an atmosphere so peaceful that the world was immediately compared with Paradise.[8]
[[Gaia]] herself regarded it as one of the purest environments she had observed.
The family quickly began discussing keeping the world private rather than allowing uncontrolled settlement.[8]
At this stage the world still lacked a complete animal ecosystem.
The wildlife and more complex ecological populations were added later.

#### Planetary Defense Project
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] and [[Zaladrac Zeovnur Elderblood]] subsequently traveled across the entire world installing enormous numbers of Draconic Runes.[6]
The Rune network was intended as the beginning of a continuously expanding planetary defense system rather than the final defensive arrangement.
[[Gaia]] was surprised by how quickly the three moved across the enormous planet while establishing the network.[6]

#### Development into the Family World
Over time Etherea became increasingly inseparable from the private life of the Elderblood household.
The world gained:
- permanent residences;
- extensive training infrastructure;
- created wildlife;
- specialized environments;
- numerous associated smaller Dimensions;
- additional celestial structures;
- and facilities intended specifically for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wives and daughters.
Victor deliberately used the safety of his Personal World to satisfy the exploratory instincts of his Dragon daughters without immediately allowing them to wander unsupervised throughout the external Universe.[13]

#### Expansion of the Personal Dimension
The Dimension containing Etherea did not remain at its original scale.
By Chapter 1071, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] observed:
- his planet;
- its surrounding Solar System;
- and finally the Galaxy containing them.
The narration explicitly states that the size of this Personal Dimension increased alongside Victor's power.
It had once been approximately Solar-System-sized and had grown into an entire Galaxy.[1]
This statement concerns the Dimension surrounding Etherea rather than the physical diameter of Etherea itself.

#### Victor's Personal Paradise
By Chapter 1100, the internal territories of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul had become clearly differentiated.
Hell and Elvenorah functioned as major divine and afterlife territories associated with the wider [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]].
Etherea/Victor's Personal World served a different purpose.
It was described as Victor's Personal Paradise for himself and his family.[7]
Of these major internal territories, the Personal World possessed the strongest privacy and defensive isolation.
Hell and Elvenorah retained connections to the external cosmological system through which sufficiently authoritative entities such as the Primordials could potentially enter.
Victor's Personal Paradise was described as connected to nothing except Victor himself.[7]

#### Naming of Etherea
The world remained unnamed for a surprisingly long period.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] simply called it [[Rex Invictus|Home]].[11]
During his conversation with Kali, he finally accepted the name Etherea.
The decision transformed what had previously been a descriptive wiki designation into the canonical name directly chosen by Victor.[11]

#### The Personal Superplanet
By Chapter 1169, the Elderblood family's home is explicitly described as their personal superplanet.[3]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] transported the entire Dimension in which [[Scathach]] and the family had been gathered to the family world.
The surrounding celestial environment demonstrated how drastically the Personal Dimension had advanced since its earlier Galaxy benchmark.
Victor casually manipulated astronomical phenomena as material for a family banquet:
- stars;
- supernovae;
- nebulae;
- lifeless planets;
- cosmic clouds;
- and nearby large-scale celestial phenomena.[3]
He created tables, chairs and other scenery from these structures.
Natalia Elderblood Alioth received a dress formed from the fabric of the cosmos, within which entire galaxies visibly shimmered.[3]
Nyx received clothing formed from the starry night.
[[Amaterasu]]'s clothing was woven from stellar light.
Violet wore a gown formed from supernova light.
[[Tyche]], observing the spectacle, interpreted Victor as casually turning stars and the entire cosmos into furniture.[3]
The scene should not be used to claim that the physical planet Etherea itself is Universe-sized.
Instead, it demonstrates the enormous modern scale and astronomical complexity of the Personal Dimension surrounding the superplanet.

#### Family Sanctuary in the Modern Era
Chapter 1175 clarifies [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s long-term intention for his Soul and Personal Universe.
Victor states that his enormous Soul could accommodate tens of trillions of beings if he wished.
He deliberately does not use Etherea and his deepest private domain in this way.
The internal world is intended to remain a safe haven for his family.[14]
If the Empire ever required additional protected population space, Victor could instead expand his Personal Universe and create additional planets or Dimensions.
Etherea therefore remains primarily:
[[Rex Invictus|Home]] first, civilization center second.

### Cosmological Nature

#### A Real Planet inside a Soul
Etherea is not a dream, illusion, Soulscape visualization or symbolic representation.
It is a genuine physical world existing inside a real Dimension contained by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul.[5]
The distinction is important.
Victor's Soul does not simply contain the planet as though ordinary matter were floating directly within spiritual substance.

#### Soul-Bound World
Etherea is metaphysically connected to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
Damage is therefore divided into different levels.
Ordinary environmental damage does not mean every broken tree or destroyed mountain directly injures Victor.
However, sufficiently catastrophic damage to the planet's foundational systems—particularly structures such as its Core and major Energy/ley-line network—can harm Victor because the world exists as part of his internal system.[5]
The correct model is therefore:
SURFACEOrdinary local damageDoes not automatically injure Victor.
PLANETARY SYSTEMMajor environmental destructionCan destabilize the world.
FOUNDATIONCore / ley-line catastropheCan directly affect Victor through the Soul connection.

#### Independent Time and Space
The Personal Dimension containing Etherea possesses genuine Space and its own internal temporal operation.
By Chapter 1071 it could be deliberately separated from the wider System infrastructure, although [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] normally chose not to fully perform such an isolation because doing so would affect the normal reincarnation process and attract the attention of the [[Regulators of Existence]].[1]
Victor later considered even more extreme isolation methods capable of sealing his Inner World in a protected Dimension disconnected from the System and ordinary cosmological access.[15]
These are contingency capabilities.
Etherea should not be described as permanently disconnected from the external Cosmos throughout its history.

#### Victor's Authority
Within his Personal Dimension, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] possesses extraordinarily direct territorial Authority.
Chapter 1071 describes him essentially as an all-powerful God within the environment he personally controls.[1]
This does not mean Victor is absolutely omnipotent in every Reality.
It means his control inside his own Soul-contained territory is dramatically greater than his control over an unrelated external world.
On Etherea he can:
- move across the planet through intent;
- alter environmental conditions;
- protect chosen individuals from lethal planetary forces;
- manipulate local natural phenomena;
- create new celestial structures;
- produce or connect new Dimensions;
- repair damage;
- alter gravity;
- create matter and terrain;
- and reshape the surrounding Personal Dimension.[6][1]

#### Planetary Authority
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s control is sufficiently direct that natural events need not occur independently of his [[Will Solace|Will]].
Environmental phenomena such as major earthquakes can be permitted, suppressed or modified depending upon what Victor wants for the world.
Etherea is therefore not merely a planet Victor owns politically.
It is a world existing inside a Reality where Victor possesses profound metaphysical sovereignty.

### Appearance
Despite its extreme physical conditions, Etherea was intentionally created to resemble an untouched supernatural Paradise.[8]
The landscape initially encountered by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family contained immense trees whose complex branches and foliage formed enormous natural canopies.
The forest floor was covered by soft grass and unusual flowers with brilliant, almost jewel-like appearances.
Streams flowed through hills and valleys before forming clear waterfalls.
The water possessed extraordinary clarity, while the surrounding terrain appeared unnaturally healthy and vibrant.[8]
The atmosphere contained dense revitalizing Energy.
Merely existing within the environment produced a strong sense of peace and physical/spiritual refreshment.
Above the world stretched a pristine blue sky beneath a genuine Sun whose light illuminated the forests without harming the Vampires living there.[8]
This protection was a property of the world and Dimension rather than proof that the Sun itself was fake.

#### Paradise and Violence
The serene appearance represents only one side of Etherea.
The world also contains territories that appear infernal or catastrophically hostile.
This contrast is deliberate.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] did not want an artificial garden that expressed only gentleness.
Etherea incorporates:
- beauty;
- serenity;
- predation;
- violence;
- extreme environments;
- opportunity;
- danger;
- and adaptation.
Victor explicitly regarded the world as a physical representation of his own Powers and personality.[6]

#### Gravity
Etherea's gravity varies drastically by region.
Across substantial portions of the planet, gravity ranges from approximately 100 to 300 times Earth's gravity.[6]
More extreme regions reach approximately 1,000 times Earth's gravity.
The deepest known regions can reach pressures equivalent to approximately 50,000 times Earth's gravity.[6]
Without supernatural adaptation or [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s direct protection, these conditions would make normal travel effectively impossible for ordinary beings.
Victor can exempt individuals from these effects when necessary.

#### Frozen Regions
The planet possesses multiple frozen and snow-covered regions associated with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Ice-related Powers and [[Gaia]]'s environmental construction.
One later inhabited snowy region possesses temperatures around approximately −60°C.[13]
This is not the lower limit of the planet.
Chapter 824 describes some of Etherea's extreme frozen environments as reaching temperatures close to Absolute Zero.[6]
The −60°C environment should therefore be treated as one specific cold region rather than a measurement of Etherea's most extreme cryogenic zone.

#### Volcanic and Infernal Regions
Etherea contains enormous volcanic territories.
Some are heavily influenced by the Dragon Fire forming the planet's Core.[6]
The deeper infernal environments include:
- extreme heat;
- volcanic activity;
- red or blood-like terrain;
- violent storms;
- and landscapes visually resembling infernal worlds.
These regions are not evidence that [[Biblical Hell]] physically occupies the planetary surface.
They are native Etherean environments expressing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Powers.

#### Oceans
Etherea's oceans are substantially more extreme than Earth's.
They possess:
- exceptional depth;
- supernatural pressure;
- powerful currents;
- and environments suitable for extremely durable aquatic creatures.[6]
The oceans consequently serve both ecological and training functions.

#### Forests
Etherea's forests express the influence of Nature-related powers and the World Tree system.
The vegetation is enormous, Energy-rich and unusually resilient.
Later wildlife was deliberately integrated so that herbivores, predators and supernatural species each contributed to the ecological cycle.

#### Planetary Diversity
The harsh regions were not accidents caused by incomplete creation.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] intentionally accepted and encouraged environmental extremes.
His family consisted increasingly of Dragons and Gods for whom hostile conditions could become:

### Ecosystem
Etherea did not begin with a complete naturally evolved biosphere.
When the family first explored the world in Chapter 823, animal life had not yet been fully introduced.[8]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and the Goddesses subsequently developed wildlife suited to the planet's supernatural conditions.

#### Designed Ecology
The ecosystem was constructed around ecological functions rather than simply filling the world with powerful monsters.
[[Gaia]] and the other Goddesses designed creatures so that individual species contributed to maintaining the planet's natural Balance.[16]
Herbivores controlled vegetation.
Predators controlled animal populations.
Supernatural creatures contributed Energy and draconic influence to the environments around them.

#### King's Bison
One confirmed species is the King's Bison.[16]
King's Bison resemble vastly enlarged supernatural bison.
Known characteristics include:
- bodies approximately twice the size of an elephant;
- six legs—four toward the front and two toward the rear;
- a long tail;
- a large horn structure emerging from the skull and forming a crown-like shape;
- herbivorous behavior;
- movement in groups of approximately ten;
- and extreme physical size despite generally non-aggressive behavior.[16]
Their ecological function is clearing older vegetation and trees.
This creates space for younger trees and plant life to develop, preventing the enormous forests from stagnating.

#### Draconic Influence
Many creatures created for Etherea possess Dragon-related characteristics.
This reflects both [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s own nature and the planet's intended population.
Even apparently ordinary wildlife exists within an environment saturated by energies vastly beyond those present on conventional mortal worlds.

#### Planned Mythical Species
During the earliest development of the world, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] discussed populating Etherea with creatures such as:
- dinosaurs;
- Basilisks;
- giant spiders;
- Unicorns;
- and other supernatural animals.[6]
These early ideas should be distinguished from species explicitly confirmed later.
The conversation establishes Victor's intended ecological direction but does not prove that every creature he mentioned was eventually created in exactly that form.

### World Tree System
[[World Trees]] are fundamental to the operation of ordinary supernatural Life-bearing planets.
Etherea possesses an abnormal relationship with this system.

#### Roxanne
[[Roxanne Elderblood]] is the primary World Tree associated with the original Personal World.[4]
However, she is not simply rooted to Etherea in the same manner that an ordinary World Tree is rooted to one external planet.
Roxanne's fundamental connection is to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] himself.[5]
The planet subsequently entered that same Soul structure.
This reverses the conventional relationship.
Normally:
World Tree → Planet
In Victor's system:
Roxanne → Victor's Soul → Etherea

#### Artificial Planetary Veins
During construction, Roxanne and Jeanne artificially established the supernatural vein network normally produced naturally by a World Tree over an immense period of time.[4]
Roxanne provided vast Negative/Life-related Energy.
Jeanne added [[Primordial Energy]] and stabilization.
The process allowed the unfinished world to rapidly develop the internal Energy circulation necessary to support supernatural Life.

#### Roxanne's Planetary Manifestation
After the world entered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Roxanne could manifest an enormous portion/projection of herself within the new planet.
Her colossal form extended across the horizon, with branches appearing to embrace the world.[6]
This planetary manifestation should not be confused with Roxanne's complete fundamental existence.
Her true root remains bound to Victor.

#### Shared Administration
Roxanne was not expected to maintain every aspect of the world alone.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly intended to assist through:
- his own enormous Energy;
- [[Gaia]];
- the Fairies;
- his Divine family;
- and other nature-related beings.[5]
This produced a distributed environmental system rather than forcing one World Tree to carry every responsibility.

#### Later World Tree Network
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later relationship with [[World Trees]] expanded far beyond Etherea.
By Chapter 1136, thousands of World Trees associated with planets throughout his Sector had entered and become organized within his Soul.[17]
These Trees reside in a deeper World Tree region within Victor, almost reaching his Soul Core.[2]
They should not be described as thousands of physical Trees all rooted on Etherea.
Etherea remains one world within the much larger World Tree/Soul infrastructure.

### Clarifications

#### Etherea Is Not Victor's Entire Personal Universe
Correct.
Etherea is the physical planet/superplanet.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul contains multiple independent Dimensions and deeper regions beyond the Personal Dimension containing Etherea.[23][2]

#### Etherea Is Not Galaxy-Sized
No chapter directly states that the planet itself became the size of a Galaxy.
Chapter 1071 states that the Personal Dimension surrounding it expanded into an entire Galaxy.[1]
The planet itself is explicitly a Megaplanet/superplanet whose exact present diameter remains unknown.

#### The Personal Dimension Did Not Stop Growing at One Galaxy
Chapter 1071's Galaxy size is a historical measurement rather than a permanent cap.
The chapter itself states that the Dimension's size increases according to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s power.[1]
Chapter 1169 presents a substantially more mature cosmic environment containing Galactic phenomena and numerous astronomical structures.[3]

#### Natalia's Galaxy Dress Does Not Mean Etherea Is a Universe
Natalia's Chapter 1169 dress was made from the fabric of the cosmos with galaxies visible inside it.[3]
This demonstrates [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s control over the cosmic environment around the family world.
It does not change Etherea's classification from planet to Universe.

#### Hell Is Not a Continent on Etherea
Correct.
Later chapters explicitly establish Hell as a separate Inner Dimension within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s greater Soul structure.[23][7]

#### Elvenorah Is Not a City on Etherea
Correct.
Elvenorah exists as another major internal territory.
It should not be listed among Etherea's ordinary planetary cities.

#### World of Water Is Not an Etherean Ocean
Correct.
It is a separate complete Dimension created inside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wider Soul domain.[24]

#### The World Tree Region Is Not on Etherea's Surface
Correct.
Chapter 1124 places the World Tree region spiritually deeper than [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Personal World and almost at his Soul Core.[2]

#### The Tower of Nightmare Was Not Originally Created on Etherea
Correct.
The Tower predates Etherea's construction.
Its later primary version is associated with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Personal World, but the original creation event occurred earlier.[19][4]

#### The −60°C Region Is Not Etherea's Coldest Location
Correct.
Chapter 1052 provides a −60°C environment, while Chapter 824 describes more extreme frozen areas approaching Absolute Zero.[6][13]

#### The Current Planet Has a Confirmed Exact Diameter
Incorrect.
The world was described as several hundred times Earth's size during the Chapter 822 merger and later as a Megaplanet/superplanet.
No definitive modern diameter has been established.
