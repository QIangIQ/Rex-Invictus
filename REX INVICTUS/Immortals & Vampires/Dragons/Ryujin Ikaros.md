### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 1189
Ryujin Ikaros is a male Dragon and citizen of the [[Elderblood Empire]] descended from a Ryu, a Japanese Dragon God. His ancestry combines Japanese and Greek roots, while his Japanese Dragon lineage belongs to a proud clan in which the name Ryujin is reserved only for members considered worthy of carrying it. Although his inherited Dragon blood had become considerably diluted by the time of his debut, Ryujin was still regarded as unusually talented by the standards of his clan.[1]
Ryujin came directly to the attention of the [[Elderblood Imperial Family]] after his compulsive attraction toward valuable and shiny objects caused him to acquire an apparently harmless luminous object that attached itself to his chest and then became entangled with his Soul. The object was eventually identified by [[Victor Elderblood]] as a fragment of a Light Divinity connected to forces attempting to infiltrate the Empire. Had the fragment remained unnoticed for much longer, Ryujin's body would eventually have been converted into a vessel for the invading power without his awareness.[1]
His decision to seek help ultimately prevented that outcome and unintentionally gave the Empire an intact sample of the hostile Divine fragment. Victor therefore considered Ryujin to have performed a significant service despite having discovered the threat entirely by accident. As his reward, Victor purified the Dragon blood within Ryujin, restoring his diluted lineage to a quality comparable to that of his ancestors and granting him the potential to eventually become one of the Empire's elite warriors capable of reaching Solar System-class levels of power. Victor deliberately provided only the potential and path toward such strength; whether Ryujin ever reaches that level depends upon his own effort.[1]

### Physical Appearance
Ryujin is a male Dragon, but his detailed physical appearance has not yet been described. His height, hair color, eye color, clothing and complete Dragon form remain unknown. During his audience with [[Violet Elderblood Snow]], [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], he was physically small enough in comparison to the Imperial women that their more-than-five-meter Imperial forms were deeply intimidating to him.[1]
The Light fragment he discovered visibly attached itself around his chest before extending its influence into his Soul. Victor later removed it as a small luminous sphere and completely sealed its power.[1]

### Personality
Ryujin possesses several personality traits closely associated with the Dragon heritage established for his lineage. Most prominently, he has an obsessive attraction toward valuable and shiny objects. He internally describes himself as unable to stop collecting such things, while the narration characterizes the behavior as a form of natural kleptomania produced by his Dragon side.[1] This hoarding instinct directly caused the event that brought him before the Imperial Family, as he picked up the mysterious Light fragment simply because it attracted his attention.
Despite this recklessness, Ryujin is not shown to be suicidally stubborn when confronted with something he does not understand. Once the unknown object attached itself to him, fear overcame his acquisitive instincts and he approached his clan for assistance. When his relatives failed to identify the phenomenon, they continued escalating the matter through increasingly powerful Divine contacts until it reached the attention of [[Amaterasu]] Elderblood. This willingness to seek help ultimately saved his life.[1]
Ryujin is also openly characterized as extremely lustful and lazy. He personally believed that his strongest desire would involve women and admitted internally that he would prefer being surrounded by a hundred women to entering battle. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] regarded this attitude as an embarrassing waste of Draconic potential, comparing Ryujin's behavior more closely to that of a lust-driven Demon than to the proud Dragon Victor believed he could become.[1]
Beneath that lifestyle, however, Ryujin possesses a considerably stronger desire for Power than even he realized. When Victor used his abilities to force Ryujin's deepest desire to be expressed honestly, Ryujin answered:
I want to become stronger.
—Ryujin Ikaros, Chapter 1189
The answer surprised Ryujin himself. His conscious interests revolved far more heavily around pleasure, but the deeper foundation of his personality still desired strength. Victor took advantage of that buried ambition when deciding how to reward him.[1]
Ryujin also demonstrates considerable reverence toward Victor. Before their direct meeting, he had only observed the Emperor from a distance, yet that limited exposure left a lasting impression. Victor's arrival during the investigation excited him even while the entire situation terrified him. In contrast, he was considerably more frightened of Violet, whose reputation and overwhelming presence made him consciously avoid drawing the wrong kind of attention from her.[1]

### Heritage

#### Mixed Ancestry
Ryujin's full name reflects a combination of Greek and Japanese ancestry.[1] The exact identities of his parents and the point at which the two bloodlines became connected have not yet been revealed.
His Draconic ancestry comes through Japan. Ryujin descends from a Ryu, explicitly described as a Japanese Dragon God, placing his family within an old Divine Draconic lineage rather than among the [[Blood Dragons]] descended from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[1]

#### Japanese Dragon Clan
Ryujin belongs to a proud Japanese Dragon Clan whose traditions place considerable significance upon names and inherited worth. The clan does not permit every descendant to use the name Ryujin; only those regarded as worthy may bear it. His possession of that name is therefore treated as evidence that he displayed significant natural talent even before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] altered his lineage.[1]
The clan is sufficiently integrated into the supernatural hierarchy of the [[Elderblood Empire]] that when Ryujin encountered an unknown metaphysical phenomenon, his relatives were able to consult a friendly God, who then contacted a still higher-ranking Divine authority. The information continued upward until it was headed toward [[Amaterasu]] and the Imperial Family.[1]
The clan's exact founder, leadership structure, population, territory and relationship with the modern [[Shinto Pantheon]] have not yet been identified.

#### Diluted Dragon Blood
Although Ryujin was genuinely descended from a Ryu, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] determined that his Dragon lineage had become heavily diluted over successive generations.[1] Enough remained to influence Ryujin's nature—particularly his attraction toward treasure and shiny objects—but his inherited foundation was far below the quality possessed by his Divine Dragon ancestors.
Victor considered that condition a waste. Rather than transforming Ryujin into a member of the Imperial Blood Dragon lineage, he strengthened what was already present and purified Ryujin's existing Japanese Dragon ancestry.

#### Purified Ancestral Lineage
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] described his intervention casually as strengthening Ryujin's Dragon side "a little", but the actual result was substantially greater. Ryujin's diluted Dragon lineage was purified until its quality became comparable to the bloodline of his ancestors.[1]
This distinction is important. Ryujin was not transformed into a True Dragon, Blood Dragon or member of Clan Elderblood. Victor restored the potential contained within Ryujin's own ancestral Dragon lineage rather than replacing it with Imperial Dragon blood.
The purification dramatically raised Ryujin's potential. With proper training and Imperial resources, he could eventually become an elite warrior with the capacity to threaten or destroy an entire Solar System.[1] This is a statement about his potential after purification rather than proof that Ryujin already possesses Solar System-level combat ability at his debut.

### Synopsis

#### Those Who Rule Reality
By the mature Imperial era, Ryujin lived as one of the countless citizens incorporated into the vast [[Elderblood Empire]]. Despite carrying a respected Japanese Dragon lineage and enough talent to earn the prestigious name Ryujin, he had largely failed to develop that potential. His interests were concentrated around pleasure, women and his instinctive Draconic compulsion to collect attractive objects rather than serious training.[1]
That habit eventually led him to discover a strange luminous object. Unable to resist his natural attraction toward shiny treasures, Ryujin took it, only for the object to attach itself directly to his chest. He quickly realized that something was seriously wrong and sought assistance from his clan. His relatives could not identify the object, so they approached a friendly God; that God sought someone of higher rank, and the inquiry continued upward through the Divine hierarchy toward [[Amaterasu]] Elderblood.[1]
The Empire had already detected the anomaly before the traditional chain of consultation could finish. [[Akasha Elderblood]] recognized that something unusual was occurring, while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Heralds had already placed Ryujin under observation. Akasha alerted [[Violet Elderblood Snow]], who summoned [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] because of her scientific expertise. Victor received the information almost immediately afterward, demonstrating the extraordinary speed of the mature Empire's information network centered upon Mars.[1]
Ryujin was subsequently brought before Violet and Ruby. Both women appeared in Imperial forms exceeding five meters in height, leaving him deeply intimidated. Ruby examined the object and determined that it had not merely attached itself physically to Ryujin: the fragment had wrapped itself around his Soul. Because directly separating something fused to a Soul required greater expertise than ordinary medicine or magical extraction, they deferred to Victor.[1]
Victor arrived personally and identified the object as a fragment of a Light Divinity. The discovery could not immediately be blamed upon Light or the [[Veillon Empire]], because Light-aligned Gods existed naturally throughout Reality and sufficiently talented descendants could inherit similar Divinities. Furthermore, the object contained only a fragment rather than a complete foreign presence.[1]
Victor nevertheless recognized its function as hostile. Using his authority over Souls, he precisely separated the fragment from Ryujin. The operation necessarily damaged a portion of Ryujin's Soul because the foreign power had already become embedded within it, but Victor immediately repaired the resulting injury through his authority over Souls and Negativity. The fragment emerged as a sealed luminous sphere which Victor himself could not simply force into submission through ordinary manipulation.[1]
Only after the removal did Victor explain how dangerous the situation had been. The fragment belonged to forces attempting to infiltrate the Empire. If Ryujin had delayed seeking assistance, his body eventually would have become an enemy vessel while his consciousness remained unaware of the gradual takeover.[1]
Ryujin's panic was replaced by confusion when Victor praised him for his service. From Victor's perspective, intent was secondary to outcome: Ryujin had brought an otherwise hidden hostile fragment directly into Imperial custody before it could successfully infiltrate society. By seeking help rather than hiding the problem, he had simultaneously saved himself and prevented a possible breach of the Empire. Victor therefore declared that Ryujin would receive an appropriate reward.[1]
When asked what he wanted, Victor used his abilities to ensure that Ryujin's deepest desire—not merely the answer he consciously intended to give—would emerge. To Ryujin's own surprise, he answered that he wanted strength. Victor accepted the request and promised to create a path through which he could become stronger, but deliberately refused to simply hand him completed Power. Ryujin would receive the foundation and opportunity; accomplishing anything with it would remain his responsibility.[1]
Four Imperial Guards then escorted him away. Only after Ryujin left did Ruby realize that Victor had manipulated the situation further than the young Dragon understood. Victor disliked seeing genuine Draconic potential wasted on laziness and promiscuity, so while repairing Ryujin he had also strengthened his Dragon side. What Victor initially called a small enhancement had actually purified Ryujin's diluted lineage until it approached the quality of his Japanese Dragon ancestors.[1]
Victor then ordered Akasha to monitor Ryujin's future development. With his restored lineage and access to the Empire's resources, Ryujin now possessed the potential to become an elite Solar-System-class warrior, although Victor emphasized that potential alone guaranteed nothing. The Empire already contained hundreds of elite warriors; Ryujin would have to earn his place among them through his own effort.[1]
The extracted fragment was given to Ruby as a new research subject for the Empire's scientific establishment.[1]

### Skills & Abilities
Ryujin has not yet participated in an on-page battle, meaning most of his specific combat techniques remain unknown. His confirmed characteristics are derived from his Draconic lineage and the transformation [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] performed upon it.

#### Dragon Physiology
- Dragon Physiology: Ryujin is repeatedly identified as a Dragon whose ancestry originates from a Japanese Ryu. His Draconic characteristics were initially weakened by the dilution of his ancestral lineage, but they remained strong enough to influence his instincts and personality.[1]
- Draconic Instincts: Ryujin possesses powerful instinctive behavioral traits inherited from his Dragon ancestry, most visibly an extreme desire to acquire shiny and valuable objects. This impulse became strong enough to resemble compulsive kleptomania.[1]
- Natural Talent: The Japanese Dragon Clan reserves the name Ryujin for members considered worthy of it. His ability to bear the name is treated as evidence that he possessed notable potential even before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] purified his bloodline.[1]

#### Purified Dragon Lineage
- Bloodline Purification (Granted by [[Victor Elderblood]]): Victor purified Ryujin's heavily diluted Dragon ancestry, restoring the quality of his lineage until it matched that of his ancestral Japanese Dragons.[1]
- Enhanced Draconic Potential: The restoration massively increased the ceiling of Ryujin's development. With Imperial support and sufficient personal effort, he possesses the potential to enter the Empire's elite warrior class.[1]
- Solar System-Class Potential: Ryujin's purified lineage is stated to provide a possible path toward becoming an elite warrior capable of destroying an entire Solar System. He has not yet demonstrated this level of Power; it represents his possible future ceiling rather than his current combat rating.[1]

#### Former Condition
- Light Divinity Fragment (Removed): A fragment associated with a Light Divinity attached itself to Ryujin's chest and fused with his Soul after he discovered it. The fragment was not one of Ryujin's natural powers and was actively transforming him into a possible vessel for hostile invaders. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] completely removed and sealed it before the process could finish.[1]

### Potential
Ryujin's most important known characteristic after his debut is not his current strength but the enormous difference between what he was and what he could become. Before meeting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], his Dragon ancestry had degraded substantially from the level of the Ryu from whom his clan descended. Victor reversed that decline by restoring the bloodline's ancestral quality.[1]
The resulting potential places Ryujin within reach of the Empire's elite military class. By the standards of the mature [[Elderblood Empire]], an elite of this category can reach threat levels sufficient to destroy a Solar System. This still would not make Ryujin exceptional among the Empire's absolute strongest warriors: Victor notes that the Empire already contains hundreds of elites, while truly rare individuals such as War possess the ability to threaten an entire galaxy.[1]
Ryujin's future therefore depends almost entirely upon discipline. Victor explicitly refused to give him completed strength. He purified the foundation, made the path available and then left the actual achievement to Ryujin himself.[1]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
Before their direct meeting, Ryujin had only seen [[Victor Elderblood]] from a distance, yet the Emperor already left an overwhelming impression upon him. He reacted with genuine excitement when Victor personally appeared during the investigation of the Light fragment.[1]
Victor saved Ryujin from eventual possession by removing the fragment fused to his Soul and restoring the damage created during the extraction. He also chose to reward Ryujin for unintentionally exposing a possible infiltration route into the Empire.[1]
Their interaction nevertheless reveals considerable differences in personality. Victor views Draconic pride, ambition and self-improvement as fundamental qualities and was openly disappointed that someone possessing Dragon ancestry would waste his life indulging in women and laziness. This disappointment motivated him to secretly amplify the reward: Ryujin asked for strength, and Victor responded by restoring the Dragon lineage that gave him the potential to pursue it.[1]
Victor subsequently placed Ryujin under observation through Akasha to determine whether he would actually develop into an elite warrior.[1]

#### Violet Elderblood Snow
[[Violet Elderblood Snow]] personally oversaw the initial Imperial response after Akasha detected the anomaly surrounding Ryujin. Ryujin was considerably more frightened of Violet than [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], despite recognizing the Emperor's vastly greater position and Power. Violet herself recognized that his fear was reasonable.[1]

#### Ruby Elderblood Scarlett
[[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] examined the fragment and confirmed that it had become entangled with Ryujin's Soul. She later received the extracted fragment from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] for scientific research.[1]

#### Akasha Elderblood
[[Akasha Elderblood]] detected the abnormal situation before Ryujin's clan's request had completed its progression through the Divine hierarchy. After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] purified Ryujin's bloodline, Akasha was instructed to continue monitoring his development and determine whether he possessed the discipline necessary to turn his new potential into genuine strength.[1]

#### Japanese Dragon Clan
Ryujin sought the assistance of his clan almost immediately after realizing that the object he had collected could not be removed normally. Although the clan could not identify the foreign fragment, its members did not ignore the danger and continued seeking progressively higher Divine authorities until the matter approached [[Amaterasu]].[1]
The clan holds sufficient pride in its lineage that the name Ryujin is restricted to descendants considered worthy of bearing it.[1]
