Fafnir is an ancient Fire Dragon who once ruled as the King of the Fire Dragons and whose power was feared throughout the supernatural world. Although Fafnir's original Dragon body died long before the beginning of the story, his existence did not completely disappear. Parts of his body, power and Soul were preserved through artifacts belonging to the [[Snow Clan]], most importantly the [[Blade of Fafnir]], the Clan's greatest ancestral weapon.[3][4]
The Blade was constructed from Fafnir's remains, particularly his scales, and was bathed in the Blood originating from his [[Dragon Heart]]. Rather than functioning as a simple weapon forged from the corpse of a powerful creature, it retained a direct connection to Fafnir himself. His Soul remained associated with the sword, allowing the ancient Dragon King to persist as a conscious spiritual existence long after the death of his original body.[3][2]
Fafnir's surviving existence underwent two major transformations through [[Victor Elderblood]]. In Chapter 810, Victor directly modified the Dragon Soul within the Blade, changing Fafnir from male to female because he refused to leave a male Dragon Spirit permanently attached to his wife [[Agnes Elderblood Snow]].[2] The alteration initially appeared limited to Fafnir's spiritual gender and temperament, but the consequences continued developing over the following centuries. By Chapter 1070, Fafnir had evolved beyond an ordinary weapon-bound Dragon Soul into a genuine Fire Elemental, capable of entering Agnes' body, covering her in an Elemental armor of flame, enabling her to become a Fire Elemental herself and allowing the ancient Dragon's substance to be reshaped into multiple weapon forms.[1]
Fafnir therefore occupies an unusual state in the modern era. The original Fire Dragon King is dead, but the continuous spiritual existence preserved inside the Snow Clan's weapon remains alive, sentient and capable of further evolution. Rather than being merely a relic from a dead Dragon, Fafnir has effectively become Agnes' living Elemental partner and one of the most unusual sentient weapons associated with Dragons Nest.

### Physical Appearance

#### Original Dragon Form
The novel has never provided a complete physical description of Fafnir's original Dragon body. His precise size, coloration, number of horns, wing structure and other anatomical details remain unknown. What is established is that Fafnir was an exceptionally powerful Fire Dragon who held the rank of Dragon King and was recognized as the King of the Fire Dragons.[3]
Fafnir's physical remains survived his death in sufficient condition to be turned into several extraordinarily powerful artifacts. His scales were incorporated into the [[Blade of Fafnir]], while material connected to the Blood of his [[Dragon Heart]] was likewise used in its construction.[3] Another relic associated with the main Snow family preserved Fafnir's Heart or power originating from it and later came into the possession of [[Jessica Horseman Snow]].[5]
The artwork depicting Fafnir's former Dragon form is therefore an artistic interpretation rather than a completely canonical anatomical reference.

#### Blade / Spirit Form
For most of the story Fafnir exists through the [[Blade of Fafnir]], a large red Western sword and the greatest ancestral treasure of the [[Snow Clan]]. The weapon was forged from Fafnir's scales, bathed in the Blood of his [[Dragon Heart]] and carries a surviving portion of his power and Soul.[3]
Fafnir is sentient within the weapon. Rather than communicating through ordinary physical speech during the scenes in which the Spirit is directly acknowledged, Fafnir responds through the sword and its flames. In Chapter 810, the flames surrounding the weapon reacted playfully when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] directly addressed Fafnir, demonstrating that the Dragon Soul could understand him and consciously answer despite lacking a separate physical body.[2]

#### Current Fire Elemental Form
Fafnir's modern existence is considerably more advanced than the simple weapon-bound Soul first encountered by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. By Chapter 1070, the Blade itself had evolved into a Fire Elemental. When commanded by Agnes, Fafnir could enter her body instead of remaining externally held as a sword. Flames then spread across Agnes' body and formed a complete suit of Elemental armor.[1]
No canonical chapter has provided a detailed independent humanoid appearance for Fafnir's current female Spirit. The female artwork used by the wiki should therefore be understood as an artistic visualization of her current gendered spiritual identity rather than a confirmed description of a separate Human-shaped body.

### Personality
Very little is known about Fafnir's personality before death. The original Dragon King lived in an ancient era, and no direct conversation involving him before his death has been shown. His enormous reputation indicates that he inspired fear, but this alone is insufficient to establish whether the original Fafnir was cruel, benevolent, territorial or simply feared because of the destructive power associated with a Dragon King.
Much more can be inferred from Fafnir's modern spiritual existence. By Chapter 810, Fafnir was fully capable of understanding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and responding to him. When Victor directly addressed the Dragon Spirit after changing her gender, the flames surrounding the Blade reacted in an almost playful fashion. Agnes immediately noticed that the sword also felt significantly more docile and comfortable in her hands than it had before Victor's alteration.[2]
Fafnir appears to accept Agnes as her primary wielder and partner. There is no indication that the Spirit resists Agnes' commands; centuries later, a simple order from Agnes caused the Blade to enter her body and merge with her without hesitation.[1] The relationship is therefore considerably closer than that between an ordinary warrior and an enchanted sword. Fafnir functions as a cooperative sentient entity whose existence has become deeply synchronized with Agnes.
Her relationship with Victor is similarly unusual. Victor treats Fafnir less like an inanimate artifact and more like a living member of the supernatural family surrounding him. In Chapter 810 he directly called Fafnir his daughter while altering the Dragon Soul, and Fafnir reacted to him rather than behaving as an unconscious magical object.[2] Victor's intervention appears to have been one of the principal catalysts that later caused Fafnir's Soul to evolve beyond the state in which the [[Snow Clan]] originally preserved it.

### History
Fafnir lived in an ancient era as the Dragon King who ruled the Fire Dragons. The circumstances surrounding his life, reign and eventual death have not yet been shown directly. Although the [[Snow Clan]] eventually obtained large portions of his remains, the novel has not established that the Clan itself personally killed Fafnir, nor has the canonical material examined here identified his killer or the exact weapon responsible for his death.
What is known is that Fafnir's death did not completely destroy him. His scales, [[Dragon Heart]]-related materials and Soul became the foundation of powerful artifacts later associated with the Snow main family. The most important of these was the [[Blade of Fafnir]], which preserved enough of Fafnir's Soul and power for the Dragon King to continue existing as a sentient spiritual entity.
The exact period in which the Blade was created is unknown, but by the modern era it had become the greatest Clan Treasure held by the Snow family and an heirloom traditionally associated with its leader. The weapon's power was so closely connected to the Clan's inherited Fire that successive Snow leaders developed an entire martial tradition around wielding it.[3][4]

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Fafnir was first introduced indirectly through the [[Snow Clan]]'s greatest ancestral treasure. Following the death of [[Adonis Snow]], [[Agnes Elderblood Snow]] spent a period emotionally devastated and largely withdrew from the responsibilities she had previously carried. Violet eventually forced her mother to confront how badly she had allowed herself to stagnate, and Agnes decided to regain the strength she had neglected.[3]
As part of that decision, Agnes retrieved the [[Blade of Fafnir]]. The weapon was revealed to be a red Western sword created from the remains of Fafnir, the ancient King of the Fire Dragons. Its construction incorporated Fafnir's scales and the Blood of his [[Dragon Heart]], causing the completed weapon to retain a portion of the Dragon King's extraordinary Fire power.[3]
The Blade was not simply powerful because it contained rare Dragon materials. It possessed extreme compatibility with the elemental Bloodline of Clan Snow and could channel and amplify the Fire generated by its legitimate wielder. Successive Clan leaders had developed a specialized martial tradition around the sword, making it simultaneously an ancestral relic, Divine-class weapon and physical representation of the Snow family's accumulated history.[3]
The reputation surrounding Fafnir himself was similarly enormous. He was remembered as the Dragon King whose name once inspired despair, while information surrounding the Blade described the ancient Fire Dragon as possessing enough power to devastate or destroy entire countries.[3] The Snow Clan consequently developed a superstition that whenever its leader was forced to seriously draw upon the Blade of Fafnir, tremendous chaos would soon follow.

#### Demonic War
When the great supernatural factions assembled for the [[Supernatural Gathering]], Agnes brought the [[Blade of Fafnir]] as the ancient treasure representing Clan Snow. The three principal Vampire Countess families displayed the greatest relics associated with their respective houses: Clan Snow possessed Fafnir's Blade, Clan Scarlett possessed [[Gae Bolg]], and Clan Fulger possessed its ancestral [[Taranis]] Daggers.[4]
The presence of Fafnir's remains was significant even among a gathering containing [[God-Kings]], ancient Vampires, [[Werewolves]] and other exceptionally powerful supernatural rulers. Figures familiar with Divine and Draconic artifacts immediately understood that Agnes was carrying a weapon containing the power of an ancient Dragon King.[6]
Fafnir did not independently manifest during the Demonic War, but the Blade remained one of Agnes' principal weapons and continued preserving the ancient Dragon King's spiritual existence.

#### King Among Kings
Fafnir's status changed dramatically following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ascension as a Blood Dragon Primogenitor and the destruction of the old [[Greek Pantheon]]. When Agnes summoned the [[Blade of Fafnir]] in Chapter 810, she immediately noticed that something about the weapon had changed. The sword felt more obedient and unusually comfortable compared to its previous state.[2]
Victor took the weapon from Agnes and surrounded it with violet flames before directly speaking to the consciousness inside. He addressed the Spirit as Fafnir and called her his daughter, receiving an obvious reaction from the flames. Agnes quickly realized that Victor had changed more than the sword's power and demanded to know whether he had actually altered Fafnir's gender.[2]
Victor openly admitted that he had. From his perspective, Fafnir might have existed only as a Soul attached to a weapon, but he still had no intention of allowing a male Dragon Spirit to remain permanently in the hands of his wife. Fafnir was therefore transformed from the male spiritual identity retained after his original death into a female one.[2]
The modification was initially treated humorously, but it produced deeper consequences. Victor did not merely alter Fafnir cosmetically; his intervention exposed the Dragon Soul to the power of an evolved Dragon Progenitor. The weapon continued changing over the following centuries, eventually developing characteristics that neither Agnes nor Victor had originally planned.[1]
Fafnir's original body had also left behind another important Snow heirloom. While Radu Tepes attempted to save [[Jessica Horseman Snow]] during the destruction of [[New Dawn]], he revealed that Jessica's mother, [[Alienova Constantine]], had secretly descended from the Snow main family. As proof, Radu gave Jessica a collar inherited through Alienova's branch that contained or had been created from power associated with Fafnir's Heart.[5]
Radu explained that the collar and the Blade represented two connected portions of Fafnir's legacy. The Blade retained the Dragon's Soul and martial power, while the other relic carried the Heart-related component. The sword was individually regarded as the superior combat weapon, but the combination of both relics was said to allow a compatible Snow descendant to reproduce Fire approaching the ancient Dragon King's own flames.[5]
The collar immediately recognized Jessica's hidden [[Snow Bloodline]]. Flames covered her body, her dormant lineage awakened and the relic transported her away from New Dawn toward the family from which her maternal Bloodline originated.[5] Although the collar was separate from Fafnir's surviving Soul within Agnes' sword, its existence demonstrated how extensively the original Dragon King's body had been incorporated into the heritage of Clan Snow.

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Centuries after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s initial intervention, Agnes revealed that the [[Blade of Fafnir]] had undergone a transformation nobody had originally expected. When Velnorah examined the weapon, she immediately recognized that it was no longer merely a sword containing a Dragon Soul. Fafnir had become a Fire Elemental.[1]
Agnes explained that the weapon had remained comparatively normal for several years after Victor enhanced it, only to begin changing gradually afterward. Victor himself was surprised by the result and described Fafnir as something resembling an Elemental Dragon, recognizing that the surviving Dragon Soul had developed along a path distinctly different from ordinary Dragons, Spirits or Divine Artifacts.[1]
The transformation became much clearer when Agnes ordered Fafnir to come to her. Instead of simply flying into Agnes' hand, the sword entered her body. Fire immediately spread across Agnes and formed a complete suit of Elemental armor. The temperature surrounding her briefly reached approximately 6,000 degrees, forcing Victor to isolate the immediate region so the released heat would not damage their surroundings.[1]
The fusion provided more than protection. Agnes could mold Fafnir's flames and substance into different weapons, reproducing forms with the characteristics of the Dragon-derived material composing Fafnir's relic. More importantly, Agnes could completely transform herself into a Fire Elemental while synchronized with Fafnir.[1]
Victor compared the phenomenon with the evolution of [[Junketsu]], his own sentient Soul Weapon. Both weapons had developed beyond the limitations of conventional artifacts and could become integrated into their wielders, but their essential natures remained different. Junketsu was fundamentally based upon Victor's Blood and possessed extreme adaptive versatility, while Fafnir's evolution was far more specialized around Fire and Elemental existence.[1]
Victor suspected that Fafnir's unusual evolution originated from what he had done to the Dragon Soul centuries earlier. His intervention had apparently provided the catalyst that allowed the already sentient remnant of a Dragon King to continue evolving after death rather than remaining permanently trapped in the state of an ancestral weapon.[1]
By this point Fafnir could no longer accurately be described simply as a deceased Dragon whose Soul happened to occupy a sword. She had become an active supernatural entity in her own right: a sentient Fire Elemental capable of independent growth, weapon transformation and complete synchronization with Agnes.

### Skills & Abilities
Fafnir's abilities must be separated between those belonging to the original Fire Dragon King and those belonging to her modern existence as a Spirit and Fire Elemental. The original Fafnir never receives a direct on-page battle, meaning abilities that are merely common among Dragons should not automatically be presented as personally demonstrated feats.

#### Former Dragon King
- Dragon Physiology: Fafnir was a genuine ancient Fire Dragon and held the rank of Dragon King. His original body possessed the supernatural physical, spiritual and energetic characteristics associated with powerful Dragons.[3]
- Dragon-King Power: Fafnir stood at the head of the Fire Dragons and was powerful enough for his name to inspire fear long after his death. Chapter 304 associates the Dragon King with power sufficient to devastate or destroy countries.[3]
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: As a Dragon King, Fafnir's original body possessed supernatural strength, speed, durability, vitality and senses vastly beyond ordinary mortal species. No individual numerical limits are provided.
- Fire Affinity: Fire was Fafnir's defining element. Even after death, the materials and Soul preserved from him remained extraordinarily compatible with Fire, and artifacts produced from his remains dramatically amplified the [[Snow Clan]]'s flames.[3]
- Fire Generation and Manipulation: Fafnir's surviving power is explicitly associated with high-level Fire. The [[Blade of Fafnir]] retains enough of that elemental nature to channel and enhance the Fire of its wielder, while the later Elemental state is entirely constructed around flame.[3][1]
- Extreme Heat: Even a transformed spiritual remnant of Fafnir could produce temperatures of approximately 6,000 degrees while merging with Agnes, demonstrating the extreme thermal output preserved from the Dragon King's Fire nature.[1]
- [[Dragon Heart]]: Fafnir possessed a genuine Dragon Heart. Its Blood and other remains became central components of Snow Clan artifacts following his death.[3][5]
- Powerful Dragon Soul: Fafnir's Soul survived the destruction of his original body and remained coherent enough to persist for an immense period inside a weapon, retain sentience, respond to others and eventually undergo further evolution.[2][1]
- Draconic Scales: Fafnir's scales remained valuable supernatural material after his death and were used to create the Blade bearing his name.[3]
- Post-Mortem Power Retention: Fafnir's physical death did not erase the power contained within his remains. His scales, Heart-related materials and Soul continued retaining enough Draconic properties to produce multiple extraordinarily powerful artifacts.[3][5]
Abilities such as unrestricted illusion immunity, universal telepathy, automatic Primordial-God-level Soul strength or other generalized Dragon traits have not been directly demonstrated by Fafnir and should not be presented as unique confirmed feats without additional evidence.

#### Spirit Physiology
- Spirit Physiology: Following the death of his original body, Fafnir continued existing through his Soul within the Blade. The modern Fafnir is therefore a spiritual rather than biological Dragon existence.[2]
- Soul Survival: Fafnir's identity persisted for an enormous period despite the destruction of the original Dragon body.[2]
- Sentience: Fafnir understands speech, recognizes individuals and consciously reacts to external stimuli. Her flames visibly responded when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] directly addressed her.[2]
- Weapon-Bound Existence: For most of her post-mortem existence, Fafnir's Soul remained connected to the [[Blade of Fafnir]], allowing the weapon to function as the physical vessel of the Dragon Spirit.[2][1]
- Spiritual Evolution: Fafnir was capable of continuing to grow even after physical death. Following Victor's intervention, the Spirit gradually transformed from a Dragon Soul sealed inside a weapon into a Fire Elemental.[1]
- Gender Alteration: Fafnir's spiritual identity was successfully altered from male to female by Victor in Chapter 810. This was performed by Victor rather than being an inherent Fafnir ability, but the fact that the change became permanent demonstrates the malleability of Fafnir's post-mortem spiritual form.[2]

#### Fire Elemental Physiology
By Chapter 1070, Fafnir's evolution had progressed far enough that Velnorah identified the sword itself as having become a Fire Elemental.[1]
- Fire Elemental Transformation: Fafnir evolved from a weapon-bound Dragon Soul into a supernatural existence whose current body and power can be expressed directly as Fire.[1]
- Elemental Body: Fafnir no longer depends exclusively upon maintaining the fixed material form of a Western sword and can manifest through an Elemental state.
- Fire Embodiment: Her current existence is so deeply integrated with Fire that she can convert herself into flames and merge those flames with Agnes.[1]
- Elemental Reshaping: Fafnir's substance can be reshaped into multiple forms rather than remaining permanently restricted to the Blade's original geometry.[1]
- Fire Generation: The Elemental state continuously produces immense supernatural heat and flame.
- Extreme Fire Resistance / Immunity: As a Fire Elemental created from an ancient Fire Dragon Soul, Fafnir naturally exists within temperatures and flames that would destroy conventional biological beings.
- Non-Biological Existence: Destruction of an ordinary biological organ is no longer applicable to Fafnir in the manner it was to the original Dragon body; her modern form is spiritual, Elemental and weapon-based rather than conventional flesh and blood.

#### Fusion with Agnes
Fafnir's greatest modern ability is her synchronization with [[Agnes Elderblood Snow]].[1]
- Internalization: At Agnes' command, the Blade can enter Agnes' body instead of remaining externally held.[1]
- Spirit-Wielder Fusion: Fafnir can merge directly with Agnes, integrating the Fire Elemental into Agnes' body rather than functioning as a separate weapon.[1]
- Fire Elemental Armor: After merging, Fafnir's flames cover Agnes' entire body and form a complete suit of Elemental armor.[1]
- Elemental Empowerment: The fusion dramatically increases the amount, density and versatility of Fire available to Agnes.
- Complete Fire Elementalization: Agnes can use the fusion to completely transform herself into a Fire Elemental rather than merely coating her Vampire/Dragon body in flames.[1]
- Elemental Weapon Generation: While merged with Fafnir, Agnes can mold the surrounding flames and Dragon-derived substance into different weapons.[1]
- Material-Property Reproduction: The generated weapons can reproduce characteristics associated with the material making up Fafnir's weapon-body, allowing the transformation to provide functional weapons rather than purely decorative constructs.[1]
- Instant Form Transition: Fafnir can transition from sword to internal Elemental fusion rapidly after receiving Agnes' command.[1]

#### Fire Manipulation
- [[Pyrokinesis]]: Fafnir possesses exceptionally powerful control over Fire as the former Fire Dragon King and current Fire Elemental.[3][1]
- Fire Amplification: The [[Blade of Fafnir]] can amplify the Fire generated by a compatible [[Snow Clan]] wielder, causing the Clan's already powerful flames to increase considerably.[3]
- Fire Channeling: The sword acts as a supernatural conductor through which the wielder can direct their own Fire more efficiently.[3]
- High-Temperature Flame: Fafnir's fusion with Agnes generated approximately 6,000-degree heat during its demonstrated activation.[1]
- Flame Armor: Fafnir can shape Fire around Agnes into protective supernatural armor.[1]
- Flame Weapon Construction: Fafnir's Elemental Fire can be condensed and shaped into weapons.[1]
- Fire Synchronization: Fafnir possesses exceptionally high compatibility with Clan Snow's inherited Pyrokinesis, making the Dragon Spirit particularly effective when paired with Agnes.[3][1]

#### Sentient Weapon Physiology
- Sentient Divine Weapon: The [[Blade of Fafnir]] is not merely an enchanted sword but a Divine-class weapon containing a conscious Dragon Spirit.[3][2]
- Wielder Recognition: Fafnir recognizes and responds to Agnes as her wielder rather than functioning indiscriminately for anyone who physically possesses the sword.
- Autonomous Response: The flames surrounding the sword can respond to conversation and emotional interaction without Agnes manually directing every reaction.[2]
- Transformation: Fafnir can cease functioning as a conventional sword and become an Elemental extension of Agnes' body.[1]
- Growth: Unlike ordinary fixed Divine Artifacts, Fafnir continued evolving after centuries of use and exposure to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s power.[1]
- Soul-Weapon Integration: The surviving Soul, weapon and elemental power have become increasingly difficult to separate into independent components, making modern Fafnir simultaneously a person, Spirit, Elemental and weapon.

### Power and Threat Level
The original Fafnir was remembered as the King of the Fire Dragons and one of the great Dragons of the ancient world. Chapter 304 associates him with enough destructive capacity to eliminate countries, an extraordinary level of power by the standards of the period in which the Blade was first introduced.[3]
This does not provide enough information to precisely place the original Fafnir relative to later beings such as Nidhoggr, [[Zaladrac Zeovnur Elderblood]], [[Victor Elderblood]] or the modern True Dragon Goddesses. The series' Draconic power scale expanded enormously after Fafnir's initial introduction, and his historical title of Dragon King should not automatically be converted into later classifications such as Progenitor, Primordial Dragon or Dragon God without direct evidence.
His remains nevertheless demonstrate that the original Dragon possessed exceptional supernatural quality. A sword created from his scales and Heart-Blood became a Divine-class Clan Treasure capable of remaining relevant to Agnes thousands of years later, while a separate Heart-related relic could awaken and protect the dormant [[Snow Bloodline]] of Jessica.[3][5]
Modern Fafnir's threat is even more difficult to isolate because she now operates primarily through synchronization with Agnes rather than as an independent combatant. Chapter 1070 demonstrates that their fusion can immediately release temperatures of approximately 6,000 degrees and transform Agnes into a Fire Elemental, but the total combat output of this state is inseparable from Agnes' own enormously evolved power.[1]

### Remains and Relics
Fafnir's death resulted in his body and spiritual existence becoming the foundation of more than one [[Snow Clan]] artifact. These relics should not be treated as identical objects.

#### Blade of Fafnir
The [[Blade of Fafnir]] is the greatest traditional treasure of the [[Snow Clan]] and the primary vessel of Fafnir's surviving Soul.[3]
The weapon is a red Western sword forged from Fafnir's scales and bathed in Blood from the Dragon King's Heart. It retained part of Fafnir's Fire power and possessed extreme compatibility with Clan Snow's inherited [[Pyrokinesis]], allowing the wielder to channel and amplify their flames through the weapon.[3]
Successive leaders of the Snow Clan developed a martial tradition around the Blade. The family also developed a superstition that whenever its leader was forced to seriously wield Fafnir's weapon, a period of enormous chaos was about to begin.[3]
By Chapter 810, the weapon was conclusively shown to contain a sentient Fafnir capable of understanding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and responding to him.[2] By Chapter 1070, this consciousness had evolved further and the Blade itself had become a Fire Elemental capable of merging directly with Agnes.[1]

#### Fafnir's Heart Collar
A second relic of Fafnir was preserved through an older branch of the Snow main family. The artifact eventually belonged to [[Alienova Constantine]], descendant of [[Alienova Snow]], and was preserved by Radu Tepes following Alienova's death.[5]
The object takes the form of a collar and contains or was created through Fafnir's Heart-related power. Unlike the Blade, which became the primary vessel of Fafnir's Soul and fighting power, the collar was strongly associated with the Dragon King's Heart and the Bloodline-recognition functions of the Snow main family.[5]
When Radu placed the collar on [[Jessica Horseman Snow]], it immediately recognized her as a legitimate Snow descendant. Flames surrounded Jessica, her dormant Snow lineage began awakening, her hair started becoming white and the Artifact transported her away from [[New Dawn]] to safety.[5]
Radu stated that the Blade was individually the superior weapon, but the two relics were intended to complement one another. According to the information preserved concerning the artifacts, a compatible user possessing both Fafnir's Soul-related Blade and Heart-related relic could wield Fire approaching that of the original Dragon King.[5]

### Relationship with Clan Snow
Fafnir's post-mortem existence became inseparably connected to the [[Snow Clan]]. The family transformed his remains into its greatest Divine weapon, built martial traditions around that weapon and preserved multiple relics connected to his [[Dragon Heart]] and Soul.[3][5]
The exact circumstances by which the Clan originally acquired Fafnir's corpse are unknown. Older versions of this page listed the Snow Clan as Fafnir's killer, but the currently established chapters only confirm that the family possesses artifacts made from his remains; they do not directly establish who killed the Dragon King.
Fafnir's Fire also became unusually compatible with the [[Snow Bloodline]]. The Blade amplifies Snow [[Pyrokinesis]], while the Heart Collar recognized Jessica's main-family ancestry and contributed to the awakening of her dormant Bloodline.[3][5]
In the modern era this relationship has become considerably less like a dead creature being exploited for materials. Fafnir is now a conscious Spirit who willingly responds to Agnes and has evolved into an active member of the supernatural system surrounding the family.

### Relationships

#### Agnes Elderblood Snow
[[Agnes Elderblood Snow]] is Fafnir's current master, primary wielder and Elemental partner. As leader of Clan Snow, Agnes inherited the Blade and initially used it as the family's greatest martial treasure.[3]
The relationship changed after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] altered and empowered Fafnir. By Chapter 1070 the sword could enter Agnes' body, surround her in Fire Elemental armor and allow both existences to fight as an integrated supernatural system.[1]
Fafnir responds immediately to Agnes' commands and has shown no hostility toward her. Their modern bond is therefore significantly closer to a Spirit contract or symbiotic weapon partnership than ordinary ownership of an artifact.

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] was responsible for the most important transformation in Fafnir's post-mortem existence. In Chapter 810 he took the Blade from Agnes, surrounded it in his own power and directly altered the Dragon Soul inside it, changing Fafnir from male to female.[2]
Victor's motivation was characteristically possessive: he refused to tolerate the idea of his wife continuously carrying a male Dragon Spirit, even if that Dragon existed only in Soul Form. He subsequently addressed the transformed Fafnir as his daughter, and the sword's flames responded to him.[2]
Centuries later Victor concluded that the much more significant Fire Elemental evolution was probably connected to this earlier intervention. His power had apparently altered or stimulated the Dragon Soul enough that Fafnir continued developing beyond the limits of the original Divine weapon.[1]

#### Jessica Horseman Snow
[[Jessica Horseman Snow]] does not possess Fafnir's primary Soul, but she inherited another important portion of the Dragon King's legacy through her mother [[Alienova Constantine]]. Radu gave her the Heart-related collar associated with Fafnir after revealing Jessica's hidden Snow ancestry.[5]
The Artifact recognized Jessica's Bloodline, protected her and participated in awakening the dormant Snow traits she had inherited from Alienova. Jessica therefore represents the second modern Snow branch directly connected to Fafnir's surviving relics.

#### Junketsu
[[Junketsu]] and Fafnir are not personally related, but [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] directly compared their evolutions in Chapter 1070. Both began as weapons and eventually developed living characteristics capable of integrating with their wielders.[1]
Their fundamental natures remain different. Junketsu originates from Victor's Blood and Soul and possesses extraordinary adaptability, while Fafnir began as the Soul and remains of an ancient Fire Dragon and evolved specifically toward Fire Elemental existence. Victor considered Fafnir more specialized in this respect, while Junketsu retained greater versatility in reproducing different material properties.[1]

### Current Status
Fafnir's original Dragon body is dead, but Fafnir as an individual is not completely deceased. The Soul survived within the [[Blade of Fafnir]], retained sentience and continued evolving over thousands of years.
By Chapter 1070 Fafnir had become a Fire Elemental capable of abandoning the fixed external form of the sword, entering Agnes' body and merging with her as armor, weapon material and Elemental power.[1]
Her current existence is therefore best classified as Alive as a Spirit / Fire Elemental rather than simply Deceased.

- Victor directly addressed Fafnir as his daughter.[2]
- Fafnir's flames reacted to Victor when he spoke to her, confirming that the Spirit inside the weapon possesses awareness.[2]

- Victor suspected his Chapter 810 intervention was responsible for starting this later transformation.[1]

- Fafnir can enter Agnes' body rather than remaining physically held as a sword.[1]
- Agnes can completely become a Fire Elemental while synchronized with Fafnir.[1]
- The fusion can reshape Fafnir's Elemental substance into different weapon forms.[1]
- Victor compared Fafnir's evolution to [[Junketsu]], although he emphasized that the two sentient weapons possessed fundamentally different Essences and specialties.[1]
- Fafnir should not automatically be classified as a True Dragon, Dragon God, Primordial Dragon or Progenitor solely because later Draconic classifications became more detailed. The confirmed material establishes Fafnir as a Dragon King and King of the Fire Dragons.
- Likewise, generic Dragon abilities such as absolute illusion immunity, unrestricted telepathy or Primordial-level Soul power should not be assigned to Fafnir without direct evidence.
- Although the current Spirit is female, Fafnir's historical male existence remains the reason older records and dialogue may use masculine language.
- Fafnir's present affiliation with Dragons Nest derives through Agnes and the Elderblood family rather than from membership in the Pantheon during the original Dragon King's lifetime.
