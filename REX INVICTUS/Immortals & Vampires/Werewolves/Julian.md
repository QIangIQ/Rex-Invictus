Julian is a Beta Werewolf from Samar and one of the long-standing members of Anderson's personal group alongside Liza, Juan and Yuran. He specializes in raw physical strength and serves as the group's primary Tank, using his large and powerful body as its principal frontline fighter.[1][2]
Julian is considerably quieter than several of Anderson's other subordinates. He rarely displays strong outward emotion and generally observes situations before speaking, although his occasional comments demonstrate an understanding of Werewolf pride and Alpha mentality.[1]

### Physical Appearance
Julian is a tall, dark-skinned and heavily muscular man with long black hair normally tied into a ponytail.[1][2]
His large physique is not merely cosmetic. Anderson explicitly states that Julian's body reflects his combat specialization in physical strength and describes him as the group's "Tank".[2]
The novel has not provided Julian's exact height, eye color, age, clothing style or transformed Werewolf appearance.

### Personality
Julian is stoic, quiet and observant. During most of his appearances he maintains an almost unchanged expression, speaking primarily when he has something relevant to contribute.[2]
He appears to understand the importance of pride and [[Will Solace|Will]] within Werewolf society. After Juan suggested that [[Johnny Lykos]] would become a more useful subordinate if his arrogance were removed, Anderson rejected the idea because an Alpha stripped of his Will would cease being a true Alpha. Julian was interested enough in Anderson's reaction to directly ask why, allowing Anderson to explain that such a Werewolf would be little more than a lion deprived of its claws and fangs.[1]
Julian is also capable of appreciating confidence and dramatic behavior. After [[Victor Elderblood]] departed from [[Adam William Lykos]]' home in spectacular fashion, Julian casually admitted that Victor knew how to make an impressive exit.[3]
He is not particularly reckless. During the Vampire tournament, Julian agreed with Liza's assessment of [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] as a monster, demonstrating that he readily recognizes beings far beyond his own level rather than allowing Werewolf pride to blind his judgment.[4]
Julian remains notably composed even when affected by unusual circumstances. When Anderson formally introduced his group to Victor years later, Julian maintained the same neutral expression despite repeatedly glancing toward Victor and being affected by his supernatural Charm.[2]

### Synopsis

#### A New Pillar
Julian first appeared as one of the four [[Werewolves]] accompanying Anderson when the Second Prince confronted [[Johnny Lykos]] near [[Adam William Lykos]]' residence.[1]
Anderson approached Johnny openly while his four subordinates surrounded the area without being detected. Johnny eventually noticed Liza watching from above, Julian positioned in an alley and the other Werewolves observing from surrounding locations. The fact that four Betas had surrounded him without his immediate awareness demonstrated how effectively Anderson's group operated together.[1]
After warning Johnny about the consequences of separating completely from Adam's pack, Anderson withdrew and discussed the situation with his subordinates from a distance.
Anderson asked Juan for his assessment of Johnny. Juan believed Johnny possessed significant potential because of his ancestry but considered his arrogance a hindrance. He suggested that removing this flaw would make Johnny more useful to the [[Lycaon|Werewolf King]].[1]
Anderson immediately rejected that conclusion. When Julian questioned why, Anderson explained that pride and [[Will Solace|Will]] were fundamental characteristics of an Alpha Werewolf; removing them would make Johnny more obedient but would also destroy much of what made him valuable as an Alpha in the first place.[1]
Julian subsequently accompanied Anderson to Adam's home during the confrontation involving [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and the Werewolves. While the conflict was primarily centered around Victor, Anderson and Liza, Julian remained with Anderson's group throughout the encounter.[5][3]
When Victor eventually departed while carrying the exhausted Liza and openly challenged Anderson to a future fight, Julian watched Victor's dramatic exit and remarked that the new Vampire Count undeniably knew how to leave in style.[3]
Julian later accompanied Anderson to [[Nightingale]] during the tournament between [[Annasthashia Elderblood Fulger]] and [[Niklaus Horseman]]. While observing [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]], Liza described the Vampire King as a monster. Julian and Juan silently agreed with her assessment.[4]

#### King Among Kings
Julian reappeared many years later when Anderson met [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] before travelling to Samar. By this point Victor had become the Demon King and Vampire Progenitor, while Anderson's original group of subordinates remained together.[2]
Anderson formally introduced each member of his group and explained their individual specialization. Julian was the first:
"This brute here is Julian. He is our 'Tank'. His big body is not for show; he specializes in strength."[2]
Julian simply nodded toward Victor's group. His expression remained unchanged throughout the introduction, although he repeatedly glanced at Victor. The women accompanying Victor noticed the looks and concluded that even Julian was being affected by Victor's overwhelming supernatural Charm.[2]
Anderson's explanation also established how Julian functions within the team. While Juan specializes in speed and tracking and Liza possesses superior tracking senses, Julian fills the physically defensive and strength-oriented role, giving Anderson's group a dedicated frontline combatant.[2]
Julian subsequently accompanied the group through the portal to Samar but did not receive another major individual scene during Victor's initial visit.

### Skills & Abilities
Julian is a Beta Werewolf whose primary specialization is physical strength. Anderson specifically identifies him as the group's Tank, distinguishing his role from the tracking and speed specializations of Juan and Liza.[2]

#### Beta Werewolf Physiology
- Werewolf Physiology: As a Beta Werewolf, Julian possesses the supernatural physiology characteristic of his Race.
- Superhuman Strength: Julian's primary specialization is strength. Anderson specifically states that his unusually large and muscular body is not merely for appearance and identifies him as the physically powerful member of the team.[2]
- Superhuman Speed: Despite specializing in strength, Julian possesses the supernatural speed and mobility common to [[Werewolves]]. Anderson's group was able to surround Johnny without him immediately detecting their movements.[1]
- Superhuman Durability: Julian's role as the group's Tank indicates that his fighting style emphasizes using his powerful Werewolf body to endure direct physical confrontation.[2]
- Enhanced Stamina: Werewolves possess supernatural physical endurance allowing them to maintain strenuous activity beyond Human limits.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Julian possesses the accelerated healing inherent to Werewolf physiology.
- Enhanced Senses: His Race possesses greatly enhanced smell, hearing and general predatory perception.
- Longevity: Werewolves possess significantly greater natural lifespans than ordinary Humans.
- Werewolf Transformation: Julian possesses the racial ability to manifest stronger Werewolf characteristics, although his individual transformation has not been shown.
- Natural Weapons: Werewolf physiology provides supernatural claws and fangs when transformed.

#### Strength Specialization
- Enhanced Strength: Julian is explicitly specialized in physical strength rather than speed or tracking.[2]
- Tank Role: Anderson identifies Julian as the Tank of his group, indicating that Julian is expected to engage threats directly and provide the team's heavy physical presence.[2]
- Powerful Physique: His tall and heavily muscular body reflects the physical specialization he developed as a member of Anderson's group.[2]

#### Stealth and Teamwork
- Stealth: Julian and Anderson's other subordinates were capable of surrounding Johnny without the Alpha Werewolf initially realizing that four additional [[Werewolves]] had taken positions around him.[1]
- Team Coordination: Julian has operated alongside Anderson, Liza, Juan and Yuran for years, with the group using members with different specializations to cover one another's weaknesses.[1][2]
Julian has not been confirmed to possess:
- Alpha Werewolf physiology.
- True Werewolf physiology.
- True Alpha status.
- Complete Werewolf Transformation.
- An individual Element.
- Nature Energy techniques.
- Godhood or Divinity.
- A named supernatural technique.
- Specialized tracking abilities comparable to Liza or Juan.

### Relationships

#### Anderson
Anderson is Julian's Alpha, Prince and commander.
Julian has accompanied Anderson since at least Chapter 187 and appears to be one of the core members of his regular team.[1] Anderson trusts him enough to bring him on missions outside Samar and later explicitly identifies Julian's role within the group as its Tank and strength specialist.[2]
Julian is comfortable enough around Anderson to question his reasoning when he disagrees or does not understand something, as demonstrated during their discussion concerning Johnny's pride as an Alpha.[1]

#### Liza
Liza is one of Julian's fellow pack members and has accompanied him during every major appearance of Anderson's original group.
Julian agreed with Liza when she described Vlad as a monster during the Vampire tournament.[4]

#### Juan
Juan is another of Julian's long-standing companions.
Their combat roles are fundamentally different. Julian specializes in strength and serves as the Tank, while Juan specializes in speed and tracking.[2]

#### Yuran
Yuran regularly operates beside Julian as part of Anderson's group, although Yuran technically belongs to [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]]'s personal pack rather than being exclusively Anderson's subordinate.[2]
