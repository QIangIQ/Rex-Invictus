### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 186
Liza is a Beta Werewolf, a member of Anderson's personal pack, and one of his most trusted subordinates. She specializes in tracking and possesses unusually powerful senses even compared with the other trackers serving Anderson. During [[Victor Elderblood]]'s first encounters with the [[Werewolves]], Liza was responsible for identifying him after examining the remains of a destroyed Werewolf settlement, and she later accompanied Anderson during his visits to [[Nightingale]] and Victor's journey to Samar.[1][2]
Liza is notably connected to Samar's Positive World Tree system. A branch-like supernatural mark on her arm allows her to interact with its [[Natural Energy]] and create a passage leading to Samar, making her useful not only as a tracker but also as part of Anderson's transportation between worlds.[2]

### Physical Appearance
Liza is a tall, dark-skinned woman with long black hair and a powerful, highly developed athletic physique. Her body is visibly muscular, with particularly defined abdominal muscles, giving her the appearance of a seasoned warrior rather than an ordinary civilian.[1]
At her debut, Liza wore clothing fashioned from animal skins that exposed much of her abdomen and allowed considerable freedom of movement. The combination of her muscular physique, clothing and generally intense demeanor caused the men travelling with Anderson to compare her appearance and attitude to that of an Amazon.[1]
Years later, when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] reunited with Anderson before travelling to Samar, Liza retained essentially the same athletic appearance. She wore jeans and an upper garment resembling a sports bra, again leaving her developed abdomen visible.[2]
When accessing the supernatural mechanism connected to Samar, a black tattoo resembling numerous intertwined tree branches appears across Liza's arm. The mark glows green while active.[2]

### Personality
Liza is serious, proud and generally restrained. She normally speaks in a neutral manner and displays considerably less outward emotion than some of Anderson's other companions. Her confidence is partially derived from her history as a capable fighter; prior to encountering [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], her companions stated that she had never lost a fight against a man, particularly not against a Vampire.[3]
She is highly loyal to Anderson and consistently follows his orders. Anderson later identifies Liza as his most trusted subordinate, showing that her position within his group extends beyond ordinary pack membership.[2] She respectfully addresses him as Prince Anderson and performs tracking and transportation duties on his behalf without hesitation.
Liza's pride makes humiliation particularly difficult for her to tolerate. After Victor effortlessly countered her attack and incapacitated her using his Lightning-based massage technique, she became intensely resentful toward him and repeatedly thought about taking revenge.[4][3] Even much later, Liza continued looking at Victor as though she wanted to stab him, demonstrating that she had not forgotten the incident.[2]
Her hostility toward Victor is nevertheless mixed with recognition of his strength. When exposed directly to Victor's Killing Intent in Chapter 189, Liza was physically paralyzed by the pressure but simultaneously found his overwhelming strength impressive.[3] She therefore does not underestimate Victor even while personally disliking him.
Liza also possesses the practical mentality expected from a member of Samar's Werewolf society. When examining the slaughtered Omega settlement in Chapter 186, she showed little emotional reaction toward the corpses and simply asked Anderson what should be done with them.[1]

### Synopsis

#### A New Pillar
Liza was introduced shortly after [[Victor Elderblood]] destroyed a settlement of rogue Omega [[Werewolves]]. Anderson, acting under orders connected to his father and the Werewolf Kingdom, arrived at the ruined settlement with several subordinates in an attempt to locate [[Nightingale]]'s newly appointed Vampire Count.[1]
Anderson asked Liza to investigate what had happened. She placed her hand against a damaged tree and addressed it as her "little child", asking it to tell her what had occurred. Liza's eyes glowed gold as she received information through the tree and reconstructed the attack, identifying three individuals: a tall man dressed in a black suit accompanied by two maids.[1]
One of Anderson's men displayed recorded footage of Victor fighting [[Tatsuya Rider]] and [[Einer Horseman]]. After examining the recording, Liza confirmed that Victor was the same man whose presence she had detected through the tree.[1] Her successful identification provided Anderson's group with the first reliable trail leading toward the new Count.
Liza later accompanied Anderson and the rest of his group to [[Adam William Lykos]]' home. While Anderson and Adam spoke with Victor, Liza suddenly became irritated and charged toward Victor at high speed, her eyes glowing gold as she attempted to strike him with her full strength.[4]
Victor intercepted the punch with one hand and used a martial movement to redirect its force, immediately destroying Liza's balance. Before she could recover, Victor pulled her into his control and decided to use a Lightning-based massage technique that he had learned through his martial training.[4]
Small electrical currents were applied directly through Victor's fingers to Liza's muscles and nerves. The technique overwhelmed her bodily control and rendered her incapable of continuing the fight despite her attempts to resist. Victor continued until Liza became physically exhausted and eventually lost consciousness.[4][3]
The incident was particularly humiliating for Liza because she had never previously lost against a man in such a manner. Her companions understood that her pride as a strong and confident warrior had been badly wounded by the encounter.[3]
Approximately an hour later, Liza regained consciousness beside the sleeping Victor. Remembering what had happened immediately filled her with embarrassment and anger, and she repeatedly considered attacking him while he slept. She nevertheless hesitated each time she looked at him.[3]
Victor eventually awakened after sensing the approach of another confrontation. The Killing Intent released from his body was powerful enough to temporarily paralyze Liza, who had never personally experienced such overwhelming hostility before. Despite her resentment, she was impressed by the sheer power behind it.[3]
After Victor left, Liza memorized his scent and privately promised herself that she would eventually defeat or kill him in retaliation for the humiliation she had suffered.[3]
Liza later accompanied Anderson during the events surrounding the Vampire tournament. While observing [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]], she openly called the Vampire King a monster, with Julian and Juan agreeing with her assessment.[5]
She was also present during the long-awaited battle between Anderson and Victor. Liza witnessed the increasingly violent exchange between the two and could perceive the enormous pressure created when Anderson released more of his Alpha Werewolf power.[6] During the battle she briefly interacted with [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] after Ruby intervened to protect her from collateral danger, though Liza stubbornly refused to thank the Vampire.[6]
As Anderson began displaying techniques inherited through his unusual lineage, Liza and the other members of his group became increasingly shocked by Victor's ability to directly confront them. When Victor physically redirected one of Anderson's strongest supernatural attacks, even Anderson's companions regarded the feat as irrational.[7]

#### King Among Kings
Liza reappeared many years later when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] met Anderson before travelling to Samar. Despite the passage of time, Liza had clearly not forgotten their previous encounter and watched Victor with open hostility during Anderson's introductions.[2]
Anderson formally introduced her as:
"This beautiful woman is Liza, my most trusted subordinate. She is an expert in tracking."[2]
When [[Anna Elderblood]] questioned why Anderson's group required two trackers, Anderson explained that Liza was superior to Juan in the role. Liza's senses were stronger, making her the better tracker overall, although Anderson normally deployed Juan and Liza together so that each could support the other if unexpected circumstances arose.[2]
Once the introductions were complete, Anderson arranged for Victor to receive official guest status on Samar. When the group was ready to depart, he ordered Liza to open the passage to the Werewolf world.[2]
Liza extended her arm and activated a black tattoo resembling numerous tree branches. The mark spread across her arm and began glowing green before branches materialized in empty space and formed a supernatural doorway.[2]
Victor examined the phenomenon with his enhanced perception and recognized that the Energy contained within Liza's mark was associated with a Positive World Tree. The Energy resembled the [[Natural Energy]] used by [[World Trees]] but possessed a distinctly Positive alignment.[2]
Liza then used the doorway to facilitate the group's passage to Samar, demonstrating that her role within Anderson's pack included access to supernatural transportation connected to the planet's World Tree system.[2]

### Skills & Abilities
Liza is a Beta Werewolf whose individual specialization lies primarily in tracking and sensory abilities rather than overwhelming direct combat.

#### Beta Werewolf Physiology
- Werewolf Physiology: As a Werewolf, Liza possesses supernatural physical capabilities considerably exceeding those of ordinary Humans.
- Superhuman Strength: Liza is physically powerful enough to attack with force expected from a trained Werewolf warrior, although [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was able to redirect her full-force punch effortlessly.[4]
- Superhuman Speed: She can move at supernatural speed and rapidly closed the distance between herself and Victor before attacking him.[4]
- Superhuman Durability: Liza possesses the natural resilience of Werewolf physiology.
- Enhanced Stamina: Her heavily conditioned physique and supernatural Race provide significant physical endurance.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Like other [[Werewolves]], Liza possesses accelerated recovery from injury.
- Enhanced Senses: Liza possesses exceptionally developed senses even by the standards of Anderson's other trackers.[2]
- Longevity: Werewolves possess substantially greater lifespans than ordinary Humans.
- Werewolf Transformation: Liza possesses the normal racial capacity to manifest more pronounced Werewolf characteristics, although her individual transformed appearance has not been shown.
- Natural Weapons: Her Werewolf physiology provides enhanced claws and fangs when sufficiently transformed.

#### Enhanced Tracking
- Supernatural Tracking: Liza is an expert tracker and is explicitly identified by Anderson as his best subordinate in this field.[2]
- Enhanced Scent: Her Werewolf sense of smell is sufficiently developed that she can memorize and recognize individual scents. After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] departed in Chapter 189, Liza deliberately memorized his scent.[3]
- Superior Sensory Perception: Anderson directly states that Liza's senses are stronger than Juan's and that she is consequently the superior tracker.[2]
- Cooperative Tracking: Liza normally works together with Juan when pursuing important targets, allowing the two trackers to support one another if unexpected circumstances arise.[2]

#### Plant Communication
- Plant Communication: Liza can communicate with or obtain information through plant life. At the destroyed Werewolf settlement, she placed her hand against a broken tree and asked it to tell her what had happened.[1]
- Environmental Information Gathering: Through this connection, Liza was able to identify the number and general appearance of the individuals responsible for attacking the settlement.[1]
- Retrocognitive Tracking: The information obtained through the tree effectively allowed Liza to reconstruct events that had already occurred in the surrounding area.[1]
- Golden Eyes: Liza's eyes glow gold while using this form of supernatural perception.[1]

#### World Tree Mark
Liza possesses a supernatural mark connected to the [[Natural Energy]] system used by Samar's Positive World Tree.[2]
- World Tree Energy Connection: When activated, a black tattoo resembling branching tree limbs appears across Liza's arm and glows green.[2]
- Positive Natural Energy: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] identified the Energy within the mark as belonging to the Positive side of a World Tree's Natural Energy.[2]
- Portal Creation: Liza can use the mark to manifest a doorway composed of tree branches that provides passage to Samar.[2]
- Dimensional Travel: The resulting doorway allows individuals to travel between the Human World and Samar.[2]
- Limitation: The chapter establishes that the mark is connected to the Positive World Tree system, but it does not establish that Liza personally possesses every ability or Blessing of Samar's World Tree.

#### Combat Skills
- Experienced Combatant: Liza possesses considerable fighting experience and, according to her companions, had never lost a fight against a man prior to her encounter with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[3]
- Unarmed Combat: She primarily attacks through her natural Werewolf physical abilities and direct strikes.
- Strong Fighting Pride: Her previous undefeated record contributed heavily to her confidence and made her defeat by Victor particularly humiliating.[3]

### Relationships

#### Anderson
Anderson is Liza's Alpha, Prince and immediate superior. She is one of the core members of his personal pack and consistently accompanies him during missions outside Samar.[1]
Their relationship is built upon strong loyalty and trust. Anderson immediately accepts Liza's identification of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] during Chapter 186 without demanding additional evidence, and by Chapter 733 he openly calls her his most trusted subordinate.[1][2]
Liza addresses him respectfully as Prince Anderson and obeys his commands, including opening the passage to Samar when requested.[2]

#### Victor Elderblood
Liza initially encountered [[Victor Elderblood]] indirectly while tracking the destruction of a rogue Werewolf settlement. Using information acquired through a damaged tree, she identified Victor as the individual Anderson had been searching for.[1]
Their first direct confrontation ended disastrously for her. Liza attempted to attack Victor and was immediately countered before being incapacitated by his Lightning-based massage technique.[4][3]
The humiliation created a lasting grudge. Liza privately promised herself that she would eventually defeat or kill Victor and continued displaying open resentment toward him years later.[3][2]
Despite this hostility, Liza recognizes Victor's extraordinary power. His Killing Intent temporarily paralyzed her in Chapter 189, and she later witnessed his high-level battle against Anderson firsthand.[3][6][7]

#### Juan
Juan is another member of Anderson's pack and one of its trackers. Anderson describes Liza as superior to Juan specifically because her senses are stronger, but the two are normally deployed together during tracking missions so that they can support one another.[2]

#### Julian and Yuran
Julian and Yuran are fellow members of Anderson's group and frequently accompany Liza during missions. Both were present during her early encounters with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], and Julian openly explained that much of Liza's anger following the incident came from the fact that she had never previously lost a fight to a man, especially a Vampire.[3]
