### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 749
Anthony Bianchi is an Alpha Werewolf from Samar and a member of an established Werewolf bloodline. He first appeared during a gathering of influential Alphas discussing [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]]' relationship after several Werewolf families attempted to interfere with the possibility of a Vampire–Werewolf Hybrid being born.[2]
Anthony's family descends from a lineage of Alpha [[Werewolves]], although its history is explicitly stated to be less extensive than the ancient Lykos and Demetriou bloodlines.[1] Unlike [[Vincent Demetriou]], Anthony approached the situation cautiously and recognized that confronting Victor as though he were merely another Noble Vampire was extremely dangerous.

### Physical Appearance
Anthony is described as a middle-aged man.[1]
No further canonical description of his height, hair color, eye color, skin tone, clothing or Werewolf transformation has currently been provided.

### Personality
Anthony is considerably more cautious and pragmatic than several of the other Alphas present during his introduction. Although he was among the [[Werewolves]] gathered because of concerns surrounding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Leona's relationship, he disagreed with [[Vincent Demetriou]]'s reckless approach and openly told him to calm down.[1]
Unlike Vincent, Anthony understood that Victor could no longer be evaluated simply as a Vampire. By this point Victor was already the Demon King and ruler of an enormous supernatural faction, and Anthony specifically reminded Vincent that they were dealing with a King.[1]
His behavior suggests that Anthony is capable of separating personal discomfort from political reality. Even if he shared some of the traditional Werewolf concerns regarding Hybrids, he recognized that provoking a ruler vastly more powerful than themselves would accomplish nothing except place their families in danger.
Anthony is nevertheless deeply accustomed to Samar's traditional hierarchy. After Victor dealt with Vincent and his supporters, the narration notes that Anthony and [[Leônidas Uruky]] were so accustomed to responding to [[Volk Fenrir]] that neither naturally knew how to interact with a superior foreign ruler such as Victor. This contrasted with [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]], who adapted to Victor's presence far more easily.[3]

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Anthony first appeared during the political backlash surrounding the relationship between [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]]. Although Maya had restrained the [[Lykos Clan]] and the families under her influence, she did not possess the authority to command all [[Werewolves]] on Samar. Several independent Alpha bloodlines therefore attempted to interfere.[1]
A number of influential Werewolves gathered before [[Volk Fenrir]]. Among them were Maya, [[Vincent Demetriou]], [[Leônidas Uruky]] and Anthony. Vincent argued that Victor was still fundamentally a Noble Vampire despite becoming the Demon King and described the possibility of a child between Victor and an Alpha Werewolf from Leona's lineage as an abomination that should be prevented.[1]
Maya immediately condemned Vincent's reasoning. Anthony reluctantly agreed with her, telling Vincent that he was being excessively obstinate.[1]
Vincent questioned whether Anthony truly intended to remain passive. The narration then identified him as Anthony Bianchi, a middle-aged man descended from a lineage of Alpha Werewolves. His family's history was respected but considerably less extensive than the ancient Lykos and Demetriou lineages.[1]
Anthony responded by urging Vincent to calm down and reminding him that the individual they were discussing was not an ordinary supernatural Being:
"We are not dealing with just anyone but a King."[1]
Vincent rejected this warning and continued insulting both Victor's relationship with Leona and the Lykos lineage itself. The dispute became increasingly hostile until Volk ordered the assembled Alphas to stop.[1]
Leônidas Uruky, who had already personally encountered Victor, reinforced Anthony's warning. Although Leonidas admitted that he personally disliked the idea of a Hybrid, he distinguished that emotional reaction from the logical reality of the situation and warned Vincent that confronting Victor could result in the destruction of the entire Demetriou lineage.[1]
Victor soon arrived personally.
His presence immediately transformed the meeting. The Alpha Werewolves were exposed to the despair-inducing pressure associated with Victor's Demon King identity, and the difference in their positions became impossible to ignore.[1]
In Chapter 750, Victor offered Vincent and his supporters a simple choice: abandon their interference and humiliate themselves by apologizing, or be taken to Hell and subjected to punishment under his authority as Demon King.[3]
Anthony was not included among Vincent's supporters who were ultimately taken away. Alongside Maya, Volk and Leonidas, he remained outside the group Victor specifically punished.[3]
When Victor began summoning additional demonic power, Anthony, Volk, Leonidas and Maya nevertheless prepared themselves in case Victor decided to attack them as well.[3] No battle occurred.
After Vincent's faction was removed, Anthony, Volk and Leonidas watched Maya interact casually with Victor. The narration specifically contrasts Maya's social flexibility with the three men, who were accustomed to Samar's rigid hierarchy and primarily responding to Volk as their King. None of them immediately knew how to behave around a foreign ruler who possessed substantially greater personal power and political influence than the structures they were accustomed to.[3]
Anthony has not received another substantial individual role in the currently documented story.

### Skills & Abilities
Anthony has never participated in a dedicated battle and has not demonstrated any unique supernatural technique. His confirmed abilities are therefore limited primarily to his status as an Alpha Werewolf.

#### Alpha Werewolf Physiology
- Alpha Werewolf Physiology: Anthony belongs to a hereditary lineage of Alpha [[Werewolves]] and possesses the enhanced supernatural physiology associated with that rank.[1]
- Superhuman Strength: Alpha Werewolves possess physical strength greatly exceeding ordinary Humans and lower-ranking Werewolves.
- Superhuman Speed: Anthony possesses supernatural movement speed and reflexes.
- Superhuman Durability: His Werewolf physiology grants enhanced resistance to physical damage.
- Enhanced Stamina: Alpha Werewolves possess significantly greater endurance than ordinary Humans.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Anthony possesses the accelerated healing inherent to Werewolf physiology.
- Enhanced Senses: His Race provides highly developed smell, hearing and predatory perception.
- Longevity: Anthony was among the senior Alphas Maya described as having lived for more than five centuries.[1]
- Werewolf Transformation: As an Alpha Werewolf, Anthony possesses the racial ability to manifest more of his Werewolf characteristics, although his individual transformed appearance has not been shown.
- Natural Weapons: Werewolf transformation provides enhanced claws and fangs.
- Predatory Instincts: Anthony possesses the instinctive sensitivity toward strength, danger and hierarchy characteristic of Alpha Werewolves.
- Alpha Presence: His Alpha status grants him greater supernatural authority and presence than ordinary members of the Race.

#### Political Experience
- Supernatural Politics: Anthony is important enough within Samar's Werewolf society to participate personally in a gathering involving [[Volk Fenrir]], Maya, [[Vincent Demetriou]] and [[Leônidas Uruky]].[1]
- Threat Assessment: Unlike Vincent, Anthony correctly recognized that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s status as a King and Demon ruler made direct confrontation extremely dangerous.[1]
- Pragmatism: He was capable of disagreeing with another senior Alpha when he believed that Alpha's actions risked provoking an overwhelmingly stronger enemy.

### Relationships

#### Vincent Demetriou
[[Vincent Demetriou]] and Anthony were both senior Alpha [[Werewolves]] present during the dispute surrounding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Leona.[1]
Anthony disagreed with Vincent's insistence on interfering. He repeatedly attempted to make him recognize that Victor was no longer someone Samar's individual Alpha families could casually threaten, but Vincent refused to listen.[1]
Anthony was ultimately proven correct when Victor personally appeared and overwhelmed Vincent's faction without requiring a conventional battle.

#### Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood
Anthony agreed with [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] that Vincent was behaving irrationally, although he admitted his agreement reluctantly.[1]
Unlike Maya, however, Anthony lacked experience interacting casually with someone operating outside Samar's traditional hierarchy. After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] punished Vincent's supporters, the narration notes that Anthony, Leonidas and Volk were all considerably less socially adaptable around Victor than Maya.[3]

#### Volk Fenrir
[[Volk Fenrir]] was Anthony's King during his introduction. Anthony was accustomed to Samar's hierarchy and responding to Volk's authority.[3]
When Volk ordered the arguing Alphas to stop, Anthony obeyed immediately along with the others.[1]

#### Victor Elderblood
Anthony first encountered [[Victor Elderblood]] during the crisis caused by [[Werewolves]] attempting to interfere with Victor and Leona's relationship.
Before Victor even arrived, Anthony already regarded confronting him as an extremely dangerous decision and reminded Vincent that Victor was a King rather than an ordinary Noble Vampire.[1]
Anthony did not become part of the faction Victor specifically punished in Hell. He nevertheless remained prepared to defend himself when Victor's increasingly oppressive supernatural presence made his intentions temporarily unclear.[3]
