#### Debut

Juan is a Beta Werewolf from Samar and one of Anderson's long-standing subordinates. He operates alongside Liza, Julian and Yuran as part of Anderson's regular group and specializes in speed and tracking. Anderson later describes Juan as a talented hunter, with his mobility and sensory abilities forming an important part of the team's pursuit and reconnaissance capabilities.[1][2]
Unlike Yuran, Juan is not identified as one of [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]]'s personal Betas. Chapter 733 specifically identifies Yuran as belonging to Tasha's pack, while Juan is introduced by Anderson as the group's speed and tracking specialist.[2]

### Physical Appearance
Juan is a tall, thin man with golden hair and black eyes.[2]
His lean build contrasts noticeably with Julian, whose much larger and more muscular physique reflects his role as Anderson's strength specialist and Tank. Juan's lighter frame instead suits his specialization in speed, tracking and hunting.[2]
No canonical description has yet established Juan's exact height, skin tone, usual clothing or transformed Werewolf appearance.

### Personality
Juan is observant, analytical and relatively pragmatic. Rather than simply accepting another Werewolf's status or ancestry as evidence of competence, he evaluates individuals according to their useful qualities and weaknesses.
This is demonstrated during his first major scene when Anderson asks for his opinion regarding [[Johnny Lykos]]. Juan acknowledges Johnny's potential as the eldest son of [[Adam William Lykos]] but considers his Alpha pride and arrogance a serious problem. He argues that if Johnny's arrogance could be removed, he could become a useful asset to the [[Lycaon|Werewolf King]].[1]
Anderson disagrees with this conclusion because he considers pride and [[Will Solace|Will]] essential components of an Alpha Werewolf's nature. Juan does not argue aggressively with his Prince after hearing the explanation, suggesting that he is comfortable offering honest assessments while still accepting Anderson's authority.[1]
Juan is also capable of recognizing beings drastically stronger than himself without allowing racial pride to interfere with his judgment. During the Vampire tournament, Liza describes [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] as a monster, and Juan silently agrees with her assessment alongside Julian.[3]
Years later, Juan greets [[Victor Elderblood]] and his companions with a disarming smile when Anderson formally introduces the members of his group, displaying a considerably more approachable demeanor than several other [[Werewolves]] present.[2]
Juan can additionally appreciate impressive displays even from Vampires. After Victor departed from Adam's home by launching himself into the sky with Lightning and producing multiple sonic booms, Julian remarked that Victor knew how to leave in style, and Juan openly agreed.[4]

### Synopsis

#### A New Pillar
Juan was introduced as one of the [[Werewolves]] serving under Anderson during the Second Prince's investigation of the newly transformed Vampire Count [[Victor Elderblood]]. Anderson's group had been sent into Human territory following the destruction of an Omega Werewolf settlement and the growing political attention surrounding Victor.[1]
Juan later participated in the confrontation with [[Johnny Lykos]]. Anderson approached Johnny while several of his Betas secretly positioned themselves around the surrounding area, demonstrating that the group had been observing Johnny before openly revealing themselves.[1]
After Anderson warned Johnny about the dangers of fully abandoning his father's pack, he withdrew with his subordinates and asked Juan what he thought about Adam's eldest son.
Juan believed Johnny possessed considerable potential because of his heritage but considered his personality a significant obstacle. According to Juan, Johnny had become excessively arrogant because of his status as an Alpha Werewolf and believed he could act however he wished. Juan suggested that eliminating this arrogance could make Johnny a valuable force for the [[Lycaon|Werewolf King]].[1]
Anderson rejected the suggestion. He explained that an Alpha Werewolf who lost his [[Will Solace|Will]] and pride would no longer truly function as an Alpha, comparing such an individual to a lion deprived of its natural weapons. Juan and the other Betas accepted Anderson's explanation.[1]
Juan subsequently accompanied Anderson to [[Adam William Lykos]]' residence during the meeting between Adam and Victor. He remained largely an observer as the situation escalated when Liza attacked Victor and was immediately countered.[5]
Victor eventually challenged Anderson to fight him during a future meeting before leaving the area through an explosive burst of Lightning. His acceleration produced several sonic booms and left a golden trail across the sky. Julian commented that Victor knew how to make an impressive exit, with Juan agreeing with him.[4]
Juan later travelled with Anderson's group to [[Nightingale]] during the tournament involving [[Annasthashia Elderblood Fulger]] and [[Niklaus Horseman]]. While observing [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]], Liza described the Vampire King as a monster. Julian and Juan silently agreed, while Yuran stated that he personally found [[Volk Fenrir]] more frightening.[3]

#### King Among Kings
Juan reappeared many years later when Anderson met [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] before the latter's journey to Samar. By this point, Anderson's original team remained together despite the enormous changes that had occurred throughout the supernatural world.[2]
Anderson formally introduced the members of his group to Victor, [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]], [[Natalia Elderblood née Alioth]] and [[Anna Elderblood]]. Each member possessed a distinct specialization.
Julian was identified as the team's Tank and strength specialist, while Anderson introduced Juan as the group's specialist in speed and tracking, describing him as a talented hunter.[2]
Juan greeted Victor's group with a friendly smile rather than displaying open hostility.
Anderson's later explanation of the group further clarified Juan's role. While Juan is a capable tracker, Liza possesses stronger senses and is therefore considered the superior tracker overall. Anderson nevertheless normally deploys both of them together because combining two trackers gives the group greater security when facing unexpected circumstances.[2]
Juan therefore fills a hybrid role within the team. Liza possesses the superior raw tracking senses, while Juan supplements her with greater emphasis on speed and conventional hunting ability.[2]
The same conversation also clarified the distinction between Juan and Yuran. Yuran belongs directly to [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]]'s personal pack and ultimately prioritizes the Queen's orders. Juan is not given this qualification and remains one of Anderson's own regular subordinates.[2]
Juan subsequently accompanied Anderson and the others through the supernatural passage to Samar.

### Skills & Abilities
Juan is a Beta Werewolf whose principal specialization lies in speed, tracking and hunting. Unlike Julian, who emphasizes physical strength and durability, Juan uses his mobility and senses as part of Anderson's reconnaissance and pursuit capabilities.[2]

#### Beta Werewolf Physiology
- Werewolf Physiology: As a Beta Werewolf, Juan possesses the supernatural physical characteristics inherent to his Race.
- Superhuman Strength: Juan possesses physical strength considerably greater than an ordinary Human, although strength is not his primary specialization.
- Superhuman Speed: Speed is explicitly one of Juan's specialties, making him one of the more mobile members of Anderson's group.[2]
- Superhuman Reflexes: His enhanced speed is accompanied by supernatural reactions and bodily coordination.
- Superhuman Durability: Juan possesses the enhanced physical resilience inherent to Werewolf physiology.
- Enhanced Stamina: [[Werewolves]] possess considerably greater endurance than ordinary Humans.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Juan possesses the accelerated biological recovery characteristic of Werewolves.
- Enhanced Senses: His Race grants enhanced smell, hearing and predatory perception, which Juan further applies toward tracking targets.
- Longevity: Werewolves possess substantially longer natural lifespans than ordinary Humans.
- Werewolf Transformation: Juan possesses the racial ability to manifest more pronounced Werewolf characteristics, although his personal transformed appearance has not been shown.
- Natural Weapons: His Werewolf physiology provides enhanced claws and fangs when sufficiently transformed.

#### Supernatural Speed
- Speed Specialization: Anderson directly identifies speed as one of Juan's principal specialties.[2]
- Enhanced Mobility: Juan's fighting and hunting role is built around moving more quickly than physically focused members of the team.
- Pursuit: His speed complements his tracking abilities by allowing him to rapidly pursue supernatural targets once they have been located.

#### Tracking
- Supernatural Tracking: Juan is one of Anderson's dedicated trackers and can use his enhanced Werewolf senses to pursue supernatural targets.[2]
- Enhanced Scent: His Werewolf sense of smell allows him to follow scents that ordinary Humans could not perceive.
- Hunter's Perception: Anderson specifically describes Juan as a talented hunter, indicating that his tracking ability involves practical pursuit skills rather than relying solely upon supernatural senses.[2]
- Cooperative Tracking: Anderson commonly deploys Juan alongside Liza, allowing the two trackers to support one another during dangerous missions.[2]
- Inferior Tracking to Liza: Liza possesses stronger senses and is considered the superior tracker between the two. Juan compensates through his greater emphasis on speed and hunting ability.[2]

#### Stealth
- Concealment: Juan participated with Anderson's group when several Betas surrounded [[Johnny Lykos]] without the Alpha initially identifying every Werewolf positioned around him.[1]
- Ambush Positioning: His speed and hunting skills make him well suited for reconnaissance and surrounding targets as part of a coordinated pack.

#### Analytical Skills
- Combat Assessment: Juan quickly identified both Johnny's substantial potential and his arrogance as one of the main factors limiting his usefulness.[1]
- Threat Recognition: He readily recognized [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] as a monstrously dangerous individual rather than underestimating him because he was a Vampire.[3]
Juan has not been confirmed to possess:
- Alpha Werewolf physiology.
- True Werewolf physiology.
- True Alpha status.
- Complete Werewolf Transformation.
- An individualized Element.
- Nature Energy techniques.
- Godhood or Divinity.
- A named supernatural technique.
- Membership within [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]]'s personal pack.

### Relationships

#### Anderson
Anderson is Juan's Prince, commander and immediate superior.
Juan has accompanied Anderson since his early investigation of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and serves as one of the core members of his regular field team.[1]
Anderson values Juan primarily for his speed, tracking and hunting capabilities. He also asks Juan for tactical opinions, demonstrating that Juan's role is not limited exclusively to following orders.[1][2]

#### Liza
Liza is Juan's fellow tracker and one of his most frequent partners.
Although both specialize in tracking, Liza possesses stronger senses and is considered superior in pure tracking ability. Anderson nevertheless prefers using the two together because having multiple trackers provides greater flexibility and protection during unpredictable missions.[2]
Juan's specialization in speed gives him a different tactical emphasis from Liza despite the overlap in their responsibilities.

#### Julian
Julian is one of Juan's fellow members of Anderson's group.
Their specializations complement one another. Julian functions as the team's Tank and focuses upon raw strength, while Juan specializes in speed and tracking.[2]
The two also share similar reactions toward powerful supernatural figures. Both silently agreed with Liza when she described Vlad as a monster and later openly acknowledged [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s dramatic style.[3][4]

#### Yuran
Yuran regularly operates alongside Juan, but their positions within the Werewolf hierarchy are not identical.
Yuran is explicitly one of [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]]'s personal Betas and technically belongs to the Queen's pack. Juan is instead presented as one of Anderson's regular subordinates and is not identified as a member of Tasha's personal pack.[2]
