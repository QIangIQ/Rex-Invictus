Anderson is an Alpha Werewolf, a [[Demigod]], and the Second Prince of Samar. He is the second son of [[Volk Fenrir]], the former King of the [[Werewolves]], and [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]], a former Egyptian Goddess who eventually ascended as the Progenitor of the Werewolf Race. Anderson belongs to the Werewolf Royal Family and possesses both the powerful physical constitution of a royal Alpha and the Divine ancestry inherited from his mother. He is the younger brother of Fenir and the older brother of Thomas Fenrir.[1][2]
Anderson initially entered the conflict surrounding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] Alucard after travelling to the Human World to investigate the newly promoted Vampire Count. Although Werewolves and Vampires traditionally maintained a hostile relationship, Anderson became intrigued by Victor's personality, extraordinary growth and enthusiasm for combat. Their subsequent confrontation developed into a genuine friendship and martial rivalry, with Anderson becoming one of the first opponents of Victor's apparent generation capable of forcing him into an enjoyable and sufficiently demanding battle.[3][4]
Unlike his elder brother Fenir, who became involved with [[New Dawn]], Anderson maintained his allegiance to Samar and remained concerned with the welfare of its Werewolves. His dissatisfaction with the country's political stagnation and the continued rule of his father nevertheless caused considerable frustration. Following the exposure of Fenir's conspiracy, Anderson became involved in the investigation of New Dawn and later witnessed the collapse of Volk's authority when Tasha assumed control of the Werewolf Race. Her subsequent [[Soul Marriage]] with Victor made Anderson the stepson of a man who had already been his friend and former combat rival for years.[5][6][7]

### Physical Appearance
Anderson is a tall, dark-skinned man standing approximately 200 centimeters in height, with short white hair, blue eyes and an exceptionally muscular physique. His broad shoulders, well-developed muscles and imposing stature reflect his Alpha Werewolf heritage. Unlike his father Volk, whose enormous physique and wild appearance are particularly distinctive, Anderson generally maintains a more conventional Humanoid appearance. His physical presence nevertheless distinguishes him from ordinary Humans and lower-ranking supernatural beings.[1][8]
Anderson possesses the ability to transform individual portions of his body without abandoning his Humanoid form. During combat, his teeth become elongated and sharpen into lupine fangs, animal fur develops across his skin, and his hands acquire powerful claws. These changes accompany the release of his supernatural power and allow him to employ his Werewolf characteristics while retaining a more Human fighting posture.[9]
His complete transformation produces a gigantic, heavily muscled, bipedal Werewolf standing more than two meters tall. His eyes shine with an intense azure-blue light, his body becomes covered in fur, and a large tail emerges behind him. A prominent tattoo is visible on his right arm, while numerous small golden lights appear throughout his fur. These golden manifestations are associated with the Divine bloodline inherited from Tasha and immediately attract the attention of powerful observers familiar with his mother's nature. The emergence of his full form is accompanied by an overwhelming Alpha presence capable of provoking instinctive unease among nearby Vampires.[10][2]

### Personality
Anderson is a proud, straightforward and fiercely competitive warrior who genuinely enjoys confronting powerful opponents. Like his father, he values strength, courage and the willingness to face danger directly, particularly when a battle promises to test his abilities. He is dissatisfied with effortless victories and becomes considerably more enthusiastic when an opponent proves capable of resisting his attacks. His fascination with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] originated from recognizing another individual who derived intense personal satisfaction from combat, yet Anderson's attitude toward fighting does not make him entirely reckless. He can assess an opponent's strength, consider the political consequences of his actions and postpone a confrontation until more favorable circumstances arise.[3][8][4]
Despite his martial temperament, Anderson possesses a strong sense of responsibility toward his Race. He regards the [[Werewolves]] belonging to his father's Pack as members of an extended family rather than merely as expendable subordinates. This outlook was instilled through Volk's teachings and contributes to Anderson's concern for the safety and future of Samar. His treatment of [[Johnny Lykos]] illustrates both the protective and authoritarian aspects of this philosophy. Although Anderson was disappointed by Johnny's arrogance and rebellious conduct, he still recognized his potential and initially attempted to warn him against abandoning the Royal Pack. He understood that an independent Alpha could become a political threat and that the Werewolf monarchy traditionally eliminated those who openly defected. Anderson's concern for Johnny therefore coexisted with a willingness to enforce the harsh rules of his father's society.[1]
Anderson also places considerable value upon an individual's pride and determination. He does not believe that stripping an Alpha of all independent will would necessarily make that individual a better warrior or leader. When his subordinate Juan suggested that Johnny could become a useful servant if his arrogance were removed, Anderson rejected the implication that an Alpha deprived of ambition and self-respect could retain the qualities that made him valuable. His ideal of strength involves more than physical power; it also requires the will to act independently and confront danger rather than allowing stronger individuals to make every decision.[1]
His willingness to judge individuals according to their personal qualities allows him to maintain friendships across racial boundaries. While many Werewolves hold deep prejudices against Vampires, Anderson does not automatically regard every Vampire as an enemy. His friendship with Victor developed naturally through their shared competitive personalities, and he openly defended that association despite Fenir's criticism. The Prince is sufficiently confident in his own identity that he does not consider friendship with another Race a betrayal of his people.[3][11]
Anderson is also critical of Samar's political structure, particularly the stagnation caused by Volk's continued control over the Werewolf monarchy. Although his father possesses extraordinary strength and commands the respect of countless Wolves, Anderson believes that the older generation has allowed the kingdom to remain trapped beneath traditions that do not adequately serve its people. His dissatisfaction initially manifested primarily through complaints and frustration rather than decisive political action. Victor challenged this tendency by encouraging him to take responsibility for changing the conditions he disliked instead of waiting indefinitely for someone else to do so. Anderson interpreted the advice through Werewolf succession customs, considering the possibility of challenging Volk himself, even though Victor's intended lesson was broader than a direct struggle for the throne.[4]
Anderson's political outlook gradually became more mature as he confronted problems beyond personal combat. When the Beast of Ragnarok became ill, he did not automatically accept Tasha's assumption that Demons were responsible. Instead, he considered the possibility of betrayal from within Samar and even the prospect of an unknown natural condition affecting the Beast. His reasoning impressed Tasha, who recognized how much her son had developed. Nevertheless, Anderson retains a practical desire to avoid unnecessary domestic complications. When Tasha suggested that he should acquire a second wife for political reasons, he hastily departed rather than risk angering his existing spouse.[12]
Although generally more approachable than Fenir, Anderson is not without ambition or resentment. During the struggle between Tasha and Volk, he wanted an opportunity to participate in the conflict and potentially benefit from the political changes it would produce. His mother anticipated this possibility and confined him to prevent interference. Anderson resented being treated as a child, but the overwhelming authority Tasha acquired as the Werewolf Progenitor made any direct challenge dangerous. His reaction revealed the continuing tension between his pride as an adult Alpha and his position as a son subject to a vastly more powerful ruler.[6]

### Synopsis

#### A New Pillar
Anderson travelled to the Human World with his followers during the growing conflict between the [[Werewolves]] and Vampires. His arrival coincided with increasing attention surrounding the newly promoted Vampire Count [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] Alucard and the instability affecting members of the [[Lykos Clan]]. As the Second Prince of Samar, Anderson represented a significantly more powerful political and military presence than the ordinary Werewolves operating on Earth. He was accompanied by trusted members of his Pack, including Liza, Yuran, Julian and Juan, whose abilities and loyalty allowed them to operate independently while maintaining the authority of the Werewolf Royal Family.[13][1]
One of Anderson's earliest activities involved confronting [[Johnny Lykos]], the son of the respected former Werewolf General [[Adam William Lykos]]. Johnny had become increasingly independent of his father's Pack and was behaving arrogantly despite lacking the political strength necessary to resist the Werewolf monarchy. Anderson appeared before him without difficulty while his four subordinates surrounded the area, demonstrating that Johnny had failed to detect their approach. He criticized the young Alpha's conduct, questioned the direction of his life and warned him that abandoning the Royal Pack would have severe consequences. Anderson nevertheless urged him to reconsider rather than immediately attacking, recognizing that Johnny possessed valuable potential and that killing a Werewolf of his bloodline would be wasteful.[1]
Anderson's warning reflected the harsh political traditions of Werewolf society. An Alpha who completely abandoned the King's Pack could establish an independent power base capable of challenging the throne, making such defections unacceptable to Volk's regime. Anderson understood that Johnny's father had avoided formally reporting his son's rebellion partly to protect him from those consequences. Although the Prince openly threatened to hunt Johnny if he became an enemy, he expressed disappointment rather than satisfaction at the possibility of killing him. In his subsequent conversation with Juan, Julian and Liza, he explained that the members of the Royal Pack were effectively his family, even when their actions frustrated him.[1]
Anderson eventually visited Adam's home while Victor was present. The Vampire Count had been spending time with his former professor, whose identity as an Alpha Werewolf did nothing to diminish their longstanding relationship. When Anderson arrived with his subordinates, Victor immediately drew the Prince's attention through his unusual behavior and the ease with which he interacted with powerful Werewolves. Victor also became interested in Liza after noticing something unusual about her supernatural constitution. Anderson prevented his followers from escalating the situation unnecessarily, allowing Victor to investigate without treating the Count's curiosity as an immediate threat. His observations suggested that Victor possessed the independent temperament and disregard for conventional racial hostility that Anderson himself admired.[14]
The encounter gradually developed into mutual recognition. Anderson had initially regarded Victor as an unusually promising young Vampire whose rapid promotion to Count deserved investigation, but his personal interest soon exceeded the political purpose of the visit. The two shared a love of combat and a willingness to challenge stronger opponents without allowing tradition to dictate their relationships. Although Anderson wanted to test Victor's abilities immediately, the circumstances surrounding their first meeting made an unrestricted fight inappropriate. Both men consequently agreed to arrange a proper confrontation later, beginning a friendship founded upon their competitive personalities and mutual respect.[3]
After Victor returned to [[Nightingale]] and reunited with his family, Anderson travelled to [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett|Scathach Scarlett]]'s territory to claim their promised duel. His subordinates were understandably uneasy about challenging a Vampire Count inside the estate of one of the most dangerous Vampires in Nightingale, particularly when several other powerful individuals were present. Anderson nevertheless insisted upon confronting Victor openly, following the warrior principles he had learned from Volk. Victor welcomed his arrival, and preparations were made for the fight while members of the Scarlett family, Victor's wives, his allies and Anderson's followers gathered to observe.[8][15]
Their battle immediately demonstrated the differences between their combat styles. Anderson relied upon the immense physical power, durability and regenerative characteristics of an Alpha Werewolf, while Victor employed the versatile [[Martial Arts]] and elemental techniques he had developed during his prolonged training. Victor repeatedly changed the weapons created through his Ice Manipulation, switching between swords, spears, shields and other forms according to the circumstances. Anderson countered through powerful close-range attacks, sharp claws and his ability to withstand physical punishment. Both warriors became increasingly enthusiastic as their exchanges grew more destructive, while the spectators struggled to reconcile the intensity of the battle with the obvious enjoyment displayed by its participants.[15][9]
When Victor condensed a tremendous quantity of fire into a greatsword and unleashed his attack, Purge, Anderson responded by releasing a powerful supernatural technique. His eyes glowed sapphire blue, white Energy spread across his body, and patches of fur appeared as he began partially transforming. After inhaling deeply, he released an extraordinarily powerful roar that concentrated his Energy before his mouth and discharged a massive beam of white power. The resulting blast collided with Victor's wave of fire, producing an enormous release of Energy. Their confrontation continued as both men employed progressively greater strength, with Anderson forming gigantic wolf-like claws out of white power while Victor combined Fire, Lightning and Ice through his weapons and movements.[9]
The duel became increasingly destructive as both fighters exchanged blows at speeds difficult for ordinary spectators to perceive. Anderson's claws and physical strength allowed him to injure Victor, while Victor's superior versatility and regeneration enabled him to endure the Prince's attacks. Their clashes produced shockwaves, damaged the surrounding battlefield and repeatedly forced the spectators to reconsider the level of power being demonstrated. Anderson's regenerative capabilities were especially important because he continued fighting despite accumulating injuries that would have incapacitated lesser supernatural beings. Victor likewise took advantage of the confrontation to demonstrate the progress of his training and encourage those closest to him to pursue further improvement.[9][10]
As the battle escalated, Anderson abandoned his predominantly Humanoid fighting state and entered his complete Werewolf transformation. His bones shifted, his body expanded, and thick fur spread across his increasingly muscular frame while a large tail appeared. When the pillar of supernatural Energy surrounding him was torn apart, Anderson emerged as an immense bipedal Werewolf with glowing azure-blue eyes, a prominent tattoo on his right arm and small golden lights emerging from his fur. The pressure surrounding his body became particularly oppressive, provoking instinctive reactions among several Vampire spectators. The golden manifestations also revealed the Divine inheritance he had received from Tasha, causing [[Scathach]] and Natashia to recognize the nature of the power concealed within his bloodline.[10]
Anderson subsequently demonstrated a technique directly connected to his Divine ancestry. After parrying a powerful kick from Victor, he seized the Count's leg and hurled him into the sky. White Energy concentrated around his claws as he raised both arms and performed a movement resembling the jaws of a predatory animal closing around its prey. He then unleashed The Beast Devours the Heavens, creating an enormous white wolf's head that flew toward Victor. The technique required a special Divine bloodline associated with a deity closely connected to animals and wolves, allowing Anderson to wield a supernatural power beyond conventional Werewolf attacks. Scathach immediately recognized the technique, while Natashia understood that a direct hit could inflict serious damage even upon a powerful Vampire.[2]
Rather than evade the enormous wolf-shaped manifestation, Victor confronted it directly. Anderson caused the projected jaws to close around his opponent, but Victor resisted the attack and ultimately struck the manifestation with sufficient force to repel it and return the Energy toward its owner. The result astonished Anderson's followers, who understood the significance of the Divine technique and had expected it to place Victor in a much more dangerous position. Anderson nevertheless remained delighted rather than discouraged, recognizing that he had finally encountered an opponent capable of providing the challenge he desired.[2]
Despite the increasingly intense combat, Victor ultimately chose to end the confrontation before it developed into a lethal struggle. His decision surprised nearly everyone present, since the Count was normally reluctant to abandon an entertaining battle. Victor had already accomplished his principal objectives: testing his progress, demonstrating his improved combat techniques and encouraging his companions to continue developing their abilities. Anderson was dissatisfied because he wanted to continue fighting, but accepted Victor's promise that they would arrange another contest in a location better suited to their destructive power.[4]
Their conversation after the duel strengthened their friendship. Anderson expressed frustration with Samar's government and Volk's continued rule, explaining that the political structure of the Werewolves had remained stagnant despite the considerable potential of their Race. Victor challenged him to stop merely complaining about circumstances beyond his control and instead take action to change what he disliked. Anderson interpreted those words through the Werewolf tradition of challenging a ruling Alpha, causing a misunderstanding about the precise form of action Victor intended. Nevertheless, the Prince departed with a renewed determination to pursue his own ambitions rather than remain indefinitely beneath his father's authority.[4]

#### Demonic War
Anderson later accompanied [[Volk Fenrir]] and Fenir during the [[Supernatural Gathering]]. While Volk was introduced as the powerful King of the [[Werewolves]], Anderson and his elder brother received comparatively modest announcements identifying them primarily through their relationship to the monarch. Anderson responded humorously to the unimpressive introduction, but Fenir reprimanded him for damaging their dignity as members of the Royal Family. Their interaction revealed a longstanding difference in temperament, with Anderson comparatively relaxed about such matters while Fenir placed greater importance upon appearances and formal respect.[16]
Anderson's existing friendship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became a source of further disagreement between the brothers. When Victor approached him, Anderson greeted the Vampire naturally and treated him as a familiar companion. Fenir, who already regarded Victor as an enemy because of his connections with Niklaus and the emerging [[New Dawn]] conspiracy, disapproved of his younger brother associating with a Vampire. He openly criticized Anderson, but neither Anderson nor Victor considered his hostility sufficient reason to abandon their friendship. The encounter illustrated how differently the two Princes approached relationships with other supernatural [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] and foreshadowed the consequences of Fenir's political activities.[11]
During the Demonic crisis, Anderson became involved in the events surrounding the disappearance and return of his youngest brother, [[Thomas Fenrir]]. After Thomas was rescued from the circumstances involving [[Diablo]]'s forces, Anderson accompanied him back to Samar and reported to their mother. Tasha initially welcomed Thomas with considerable affection before returning to her responsibilities as Queen and demanding a complete account of everything that had occurred. Anderson explained the events from his perspective, including Victor's involvement, but could not provide the details of Victor's private conversation with Evie Moriarty. His inability to obtain that information frustrated Tasha, whose experience in supernatural politics caused her to regard seemingly insignificant conversations as possible sources of intelligence.[12]
Anderson soon learned that a far greater problem was developing within Samar. The legendary [[Fenrir]], the Beast of Ragnarok and guardian of the Werewolf Race, had mysteriously become ill following the Demonic invasion. Its condition was deteriorating despite the attention of Samar's medical specialists, while Volk and Tasha remained close to the Beast because of their connection through its Blessing. Anderson reacted with disbelief, having never considered that a Being of Fenrir's nature could suffer from an illness that ordinary medical methods could not identify. The situation threatened the foundations of Samar's security, since the Beast was one of the greatest sources of supernatural protection available to the Werewolf kingdom.[12]
Tasha suspected that the Demons had somehow caused Fenrir's condition, but Anderson questioned whether an external attack was necessarily responsible. He pointed out that an ordinary Demon would have been unable to approach the Beast without being detected or destroyed, raising the possibility of an internal traitor familiar with its circumstances. At the same time, Anderson warned against assuming that the illness had been deliberately inflicted. Fenrir's biology remained poorly understood even by those closest to it, and an unusual natural condition could not be ruled out. His reasoning impressed Tasha, who recognized that her second son had become considerably more perceptive and mature than she had previously acknowledged.[12]
Their conversation also revealed developments in Anderson's personal life. He was already married and appeared satisfied with his relationship, a circumstance Tasha viewed positively given her own increasingly troubled marriage with Volk. However, when she suggested that Anderson should acquire a second wife to strengthen Samar's political relationships, he hastily excused himself. Anderson feared that his existing wife would react violently to such an arrangement and preferred to avoid the domestic consequences rather than obey his mother's political suggestion. Tasha found his behavior amusing, recognizing that despite his growth, he still disliked unnecessary personal complications.[12]
Following Anderson's departure, Tasha ordered her loyal intelligence operatives to investigate the Royal Castle and the individuals associated with its political factions. Fenir was specifically included among those subject to suspicion, while Anderson and Thomas were excluded. Tasha regarded her second son as relatively straightforward and trustworthy, noting that he had not displayed the same capacity for concealing his true intentions from her as more politically deceptive individuals. Although Anderson was capable of making independent judgments, his concern for Samar and his relationship with his mother distinguished him from the conspiracies developing around Fenir.[12]

#### King Among Kings
As [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s influence continued expanding, Anderson again became an intermediary between the Vampire ruler and the Werewolf kingdom. He accompanied Victor and members of his family during their journey to Samar, helping them enter the Werewolf homeworld through the transportation abilities available to his followers. Liza opened a supernatural gateway using markings resembling branches that spread across her arm and manifested a passage formed from trees. Victor recognized an unusual Positive Energy associated with the gateway, reinforcing his understanding of the relationship between the Werewolf Race and the supernatural foundations of Samar. Anderson's position as a royal Prince allowed him to facilitate the visit despite the political sensitivity surrounding Victor's arrival.[17]
The visit placed Anderson in an increasingly complicated political situation. Victor had become vastly more powerful than the Count Anderson once fought, while his growing relationships with Samar's influential families challenged the traditional authority of Volk. Anderson maintained his friendship with Victor despite these developments, but the instability within the Royal Family made his position difficult. His father's pride, Tasha's political ambitions and Fenir's concealed associations with [[New Dawn]] increasingly threatened the unity of the kingdom, while Thomas remained the focus of expectations connected to the Beast of Ragnarok.[17][18]
The exposure of Fenir's conspiracy brought the crisis within the Royal Family to a decisive point. Fenir had attempted to acquire New Dawn's assistance in obtaining the throne, offering the organization an opportunity to establish influence over Samar and its [[Werewolves]]. Volk regarded the arrangement as treason and wanted his eldest son executed, while Tasha intervened to preserve Fenir as an important source of information. Their disagreement nearly escalated into open combat, allowing Fenir an opportunity to attempt escape. Victor prevented his departure and subjected him to an extraordinarily powerful form of Vampiric Charm, forcing the First Prince to answer questions that he would otherwise have concealed.[18]
During the interrogation, Victor requested the presence of Maya Lykos, Adam Lykos and Anderson. Tasha initially questioned why her second son was necessary, but Victor defended Anderson's intelligence and remarked that he could be as cunning as his mother. His assessment surprised Tasha, who began questioning whether the burden of ruling Samar had caused her to overlook the personal development of her older children. Anderson joined the investigation alongside Maya and Adam, hearing the information extracted from Fenir concerning New Dawn's structure, its secretive operations and the existence of a seven-member council representing powerful figures from different supernatural communities.[5]
Fenir's testimony established that he occupied Seat No. 2 on New Dawn's council as the Werewolf representative, while the numbered positions themselves did not represent a hierarchy of power. The organization had developed from the cooperation of Fenir, [[Niklaus Horseman]] and General James, subsequently expanding through contacts with Demons and other supernatural powers. Anderson recognized the seriousness of his brother's actions and did not attempt to excuse him merely because of their shared bloodline. The possibility that New Dawn had penetrated the Royal Family also caused him to question the security of his own followers and political connections.[5][19]
Although Fenir had demonstrated political ambition and possessed administrative competence, his willingness to cooperate with an outside organization threatened Samar's independence. Anderson considered his brother foolish for allowing the conspiracy to develop so far and became concerned that similar infiltrators could have entered his own faction. While Tasha continued examining Fenir's associations and ordered him imprisoned, Anderson moved to investigate possible compromises among those serving him. His actions reflected the growing importance he placed upon national security and the possibility that threats to Samar could originate from powerful Werewolves rather than only from traditional external enemies.[19]
The political situation deteriorated further as Tasha resolved to remove Volk from power and establish a new order for the Werewolf Race. Anderson and Thomas were prohibited from intervening in the confrontation between their parents, but Anderson did not accept the restriction willingly. He was an adult Alpha with political ambitions of his own and wanted the opportunity to participate in the struggle, potentially improving his position within the kingdom. Tasha anticipated this possibility and had him confined alongside his wife and most loyal subordinates, preventing him from influencing the outcome. Thomas was likewise excluded from the struggle so that neither son could complicate a conflict already threatening the stability of Samar.[6]
Tasha ultimately defeated Volk and acquired the supreme authority of the Werewolf Progenitor, becoming the Alpha among Alphas. Her new position granted her control over the racial hierarchy of the Werewolves, including the ability to awaken or remove an individual's Alpha status. Volk was stripped of his former authority and reduced to an Omega, while the political structure of Samar was reorganized beneath Tasha's leadership. Anderson retained his position as a Prince but now served under a ruler whose supernatural authority over the Race was far greater than Volk's previous power.[20][21][6]
Following her victory, Tasha met with Anderson and Thomas to establish their positions within the new political order. Anderson cautiously expressed dissatisfaction with her decision to confine him during the confrontation, arguing that he was no longer a child. Tasha rejected his objections and made clear that her authority as the Progenitor was not open to casual dispute. Anderson understood that she could remove his Alpha status through the racial power she now possessed and withdrew his criticism rather than provoke a confrontation he could not hope to win. Thomas, meanwhile, learned that he would no longer be forced to succeed Volk as King, although Tasha continued insisting that he develop his strength and fulfill his responsibilities as a member of the Royal Family.[6]
Anderson also witnessed the immense transformation Victor had undergone since their early friendship. The familiar Vampire Count had become a Being possessing powerful Draconic characteristics and a supernatural presence that Anderson found difficult to comprehend. The Prince was astonished that Victor could have advanced so far within a comparatively short period, recognizing that the difference between their former combat levels had become enormous. Victor nevertheless greeted him naturally and maintained the friendly attitude that had characterized their relationship from the beginning. Anderson responded with a resigned smile, acknowledging both his friend's extraordinary growth and the absurdity of trying to measure Victor by ordinary standards.[6]
Tasha subsequently entered a [[Soul Marriage]] with Victor, formally joining the rapidly expanding Elderblood family. Through that union, Anderson became Victor's stepson despite already possessing an established friendship and combat rivalry with him. Their previous relationship was not founded upon parental authority, and Victor had never attempted to replace Volk in Anderson's life. The marriage instead added a familial connection to an existing bond, making Anderson one of the unusual members of Victor's extended family who had known and fought their new stepfather long before the marriage occurred.[7]

### Skills & Abilities
Anderson is a powerful Alpha Werewolf whose combat abilities combine the physical characteristics of his Race with the Divine heritage inherited from Tasha. His transformations, supernatural Energy projections and exceptional physical resilience enable him to engage powerful opponents directly, while his Divine bloodline allows him to employ a specialized technique associated with a deity closely connected to wolves. Alongside his combat abilities, Anderson possesses considerable experience in royal military leadership, political judgment and the management of a personal Pack.

#### Racial Abilities
- Alpha Werewolf Physiology: Anderson belongs to the royal lineage of the Werewolf Race and possesses the supernatural physical characteristics of an Alpha. His body is exceptionally powerful even before transforming, granting him the strength, speed, durability, regenerative capabilities and predatory characteristics associated with high-ranking [[Werewolves]].[1][9][10]
- Supernatural Strength: Anderson can exchange powerful physical attacks with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] during the Count's earlier development, using punches, kicks, grappling and claw strikes capable of causing significant damage. His physical strength is sufficient to overpower Victor during individual exchanges, although the outcome of their wider battle remains unresolved.[9][2]
- Supernatural Speed: Anderson can move at tremendous speeds, exchanging attacks too quickly for ordinary observers to follow. He can intercept Victor's movements and respond to attacks performed at or around the speed of sound.[10][2]
- Supernatural Durability: His Alpha physiology provides an extremely tough physical constitution capable of surviving repeated impacts and powerful elemental attacks. His body remained functional despite sustaining considerable damage during his fight with Victor.[10]
- Regeneration: Anderson possesses an advanced regenerative capacity that allows him to recover from substantial combat injuries. He continued fighting after receiving wounds that would incapacitate ordinary supernatural beings, although regeneration does not render him immune to pain or physical damage.[10]
- Enhanced Endurance: His physiological resilience allows him to continue fighting through repeated injuries, exhaustion and intense physical exchanges without rapidly losing his effectiveness.[10]
- Enhanced Senses: Anderson possesses the heightened sensory characteristics associated with Werewolves, including their natural ability to perceive supernatural presences and physical threats. No unique sensory technique or fixed detection range has been established for him.[1]
- Natural Weaponry: Anderson can develop sharp fangs and claws, allowing him to inflict serious damage through bites, slashes and other close-range attacks.[9]
- Werewolf Transformation: Anderson can transform his Humanoid body into an enormous bipedal Werewolf, substantially increasing his already formidable physical characteristics. His transformation develops fur, an elongated muzzle, powerful claws, sharp fangs and a tail while releasing an increasingly oppressive supernatural presence.[10]
- Partial Transformation: Anderson can manifest individual Werewolf traits without completing his transformation, including sharp claws, elongated teeth and animal fur. He uses these changes during combat to enhance his natural weapons while maintaining his ordinary fighting posture.[9]
- Physical Amplification: His full transformation greatly increases his physical power and releases the overwhelming presence associated with his Alpha status.[10]
- Alpha Aura: Anderson can release an oppressive supernatural presence that affects nearby individuals and emphasizes his position within the Werewolf hierarchy. The pressure surrounding his full transformation was compared to a substantial increase in gravity, although this description does not independently establish control over gravitational forces.[10]
- Longevity: As an Alpha Werewolf with Divine ancestry, Anderson possesses the extended lifespan characteristic of powerful supernatural beings.

#### Divine and Supernatural Abilities
- [[Demigod]] Physiology: Anderson inherited Divine blood through [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]], who originated as an Egyptian Goddess. This heritage grants him supernatural characteristics beyond those of an ordinary Alpha Werewolf and allows him to employ powers associated with his mother's Divine nature.[10][2]
- [[Divine Energy]]: Anderson possesses Divine power inherited through his maternal bloodline. The golden lights manifesting from his fur during his full transformation were recognized as evidence of that inheritance.[10]
- Divine Power Manipulation: Anderson can channel inherited supernatural power into offensive techniques, including an attack that requires the blood of a deity closely associated with animals and wolves.[2]
- Energy Manipulation: Anderson can surround his body with brilliant white supernatural Energy and concentrate that power into physical attacks, projected constructs and ranged techniques.[9]
- Energy Aura: He produces a visible white aura around his body and limbs, particularly when increasing the intensity of his attacks or releasing greater amounts of power.[9]
- Energy Projection: Anderson can discharge concentrated supernatural Energy toward an opponent rather than relying exclusively upon physical attacks.[9]
- Energy Constructs: He can form enormous wolf-like claws from white Energy, increasing the range and destructive potential of his claw attacks.[9]
- Concussive Roar: Anderson can unleash a deafening roar that produces a powerful supernatural attack. His roar can concentrate Energy before his mouth and release it as a massive beam toward an opponent.[9]
- Energy Beam: By focusing white Energy through his mouth, Anderson can unleash a powerful ranged blast capable of directly contesting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s fire-based techniques.[9]
- Divine Wolf Manifestation: Anderson can create an enormous wolf-shaped manifestation from concentrated white Divine power. The construct possesses a gigantic head and jaws capable of closing around an opponent, allowing him to attack at a considerable distance.[2]
- The Beast Devours the Heavens: A powerful inherited Divine technique in which Anderson concentrates white Energy around his claws, raises his arms and releases an immense wolf's head toward his target. By closing his hands, he can cause the manifestation's jaws to close around the opponent. The technique requires the bloodline of a deity closely associated with wolves and animals, and its power was considered dangerous even to powerful Vampires.[2]
- Oppressive Presence: His full Werewolf transformation releases intense supernatural pressure capable of provoking instinctive fear among nearby Vampires. The sensation resembles a sudden multiplication of gravitational force, but no independent ability to manipulate gravity as a fundamental force has been established.[10]

#### Combat and Other Skills
- Hand-to-Hand Combat: Anderson is a skilled close-range fighter who uses punches, kicks, grappling, claw attacks and rapid movement to overwhelm opponents. His confrontation with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] demonstrates an ability to continue fighting despite frequent changes in his opponent's weapons and techniques.[9][2]
- Combat Instinct: Anderson possesses powerful martial instincts and can recognize dangerous attacks, adjust to changing circumstances and determine when an opponent is restraining their full power.[9]
- Combat Adaptation: He can adjust his fighting style against different forms of physical and supernatural attacks, combining his Werewolf transformation with Energy-based techniques.[9][10]
- Extraordinary Reflexes: Anderson successfully intercepted rapid attacks from Victor and reacted to movements that could not be properly followed by ordinary spectators.[2]
- Leadership: As the Second Prince of Samar, Anderson commands his own Pack and possesses experience supervising powerful [[Werewolves]]. His subordinates respect his strength, authority and concern for their welfare.[1]
- Political Awareness: Anderson understands the political consequences of Werewolf succession customs, the importance of protecting royal authority and the danger posed by infiltrators within Samar. His judgment was sufficiently respected by Victor that he was included in the investigation of Fenir and [[New Dawn]].[1][5][19]
- Analytical Thinking: Anderson can examine political and supernatural problems without relying exclusively upon obvious explanations. His reasoning concerning [[Fenrir]]'s illness demonstrated an ability to consider internal betrayal, outside interference and unknown natural causes.[12]
- Strong [[Will Solace|Will]]: Anderson possesses considerable pride and determination, allowing him to continue fighting through pain and pressure while maintaining confidence against powerful opponents.[10]
- Tactical Judgment: Despite his enthusiasm for combat, Anderson can delay unnecessary confrontations, assess the strength of potential enemies and recognize when direct action would create greater political problems.[3]

### Power
Anderson's primary classification is Alpha Werewolf / [[Demigod]]. His royal Werewolf physiology grants him exceptional physical characteristics, while his Divine bloodline gives him access to powers unavailable to ordinary members of his Race. He demonstrated sufficient strength, speed, durability and regenerative ability to challenge [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] during the Count's earlier development, forcing him to rely upon his physical training, elemental powers and rapidly improving [[Martial Arts]]. Anderson was able to contest powerful supernatural attacks, endure substantial physical punishment and retaliate with techniques capable of seriously injuring the Vampire Count.[9][10][2]
His inherited Divine technique, The Beast Devours the Heavens, is one of the clearest demonstrations of his unusual bloodline. The ability produces a massive wolf-shaped manifestation possessing sufficient power to threaten a powerful Vampire, while its use requires Divine ancestry connected to wolves. Anderson's ability to employ such an attack distinguishes him from an ordinary Alpha and confirms that the Divine component of his existence possesses practical combat applications rather than merely providing a genealogical connection to Tasha.[2]
Although Anderson could challenge Victor during their early confrontation, the two became increasingly separated by the extraordinary speed of Victor's subsequent evolution. Anderson's abilities should consequently be evaluated relative to Victor's early Vampire Count state rather than his later Progenitor, Dragon or Divine transformations. His surprise upon encountering Victor's later Draconic presence reflected the enormous disparity that had developed between their respective levels of power.[6]
Anderson remains identified as an Alpha Werewolf and Demigod. His ability to wield inherited Divine power does not automatically make him a fully developed God, nor does his royal birth establish an evolution into an Ancient Alpha or Werewolf Progenitor. Tasha's ascension granted her racial authority over the [[Werewolves]], but that authority belongs to her rather than Anderson.[20][21][6]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Anderson's close friend, former combat rival and eventual stepfather. Their relationship began when Anderson became interested in the rapidly growing Vampire Count and recognized a personality similar to his own. Both enjoyed combat, valued personal freedom and admired opponents willing to challenge powerful enemies. Their subsequent battle strengthened this mutual respect and developed into a friendship that remained intact despite the traditional hostility between Vampires and [[Werewolves]]. Victor also influenced Anderson's political outlook by encouraging him to take responsibility for improving Samar rather than waiting indefinitely for Volk to change. Although Anderson interpreted that advice through the traditions of Werewolf succession, it nevertheless motivated him to reconsider his own position within the kingdom.[3][4]
As Victor's power increased, their relationship ceased to represent a rivalry between approximately comparable fighters. Anderson eventually witnessed the extraordinary Draconic transformation his friend had undergone and recognized that the difference between them had become immense. Victor nevertheless continued treating him naturally, and their existing friendship remained unaffected by his new status. Following Victor's [[Soul Marriage]] with Tasha, Anderson became his stepson, creating an unusual familial relationship founded upon a friendship that had existed long before the marriage.[6][7]

#### Tasha Fenrir Elderblood
[[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]] is Anderson's biological mother and the former Queen who subsequently became the Werewolf Progenitor. Anderson respects her intelligence, political competence and concern for the kingdom, although their approaches to problems frequently differ. His reasoning concerning [[Fenrir]]'s illness impressed Tasha and made her recognize that her second son had developed considerably beyond the expectations she had formed during his childhood. She regarded Anderson as comparatively honest and trustworthy, even while acknowledging that he possessed political ambitions and could become troublesome when the interests of the Royal Family conflicted.[12]
Their relationship became more complicated following Tasha's decision to remove Volk from power. Anderson wanted to participate in the struggle between his parents, but Tasha confined him to prevent his interference. He resented the restriction and later questioned her decision, only to recognize that her authority as the new Progenitor made resistance practically impossible. Despite these tensions, Anderson remained within her political hierarchy and continued to acknowledge her position as the supreme Alpha of Samar.[6]

#### Volk Fenrir
[[Volk Fenrir]] is Anderson's biological father and the former King of the [[Werewolves]]. Anderson inherited much of Volk's enthusiasm for combat and his preference for warriors who confront opponents directly. Volk also taught him that the members of the Royal Pack should be regarded as family, contributing to Anderson's concern for the welfare of Samar's Werewolves. However, Anderson became dissatisfied with his father's continued rule and the stagnation of the kingdom's political institutions, believing that meaningful change was necessary for the Race to progress.[1][4]
Although Anderson considered challenging Volk's authority, his dissatisfaction never developed into the same form of foreign conspiracy pursued by Fenir. The conflict between Volk and Tasha ultimately ended with the former King's defeat and removal from the position of supreme Alpha. Anderson was prevented from taking part in the confrontation and subsequently served beneath the new hierarchy created by his mother.[6]

#### Fenir
Fenir is Anderson's elder brother and the former First Prince of Samar. The two possess substantially different personalities: Fenir is more concerned with political appearances and acquiring the throne, while Anderson is comparatively direct and places greater importance upon combat, personal relationships and the welfare of his followers. Fenir frequently criticized Anderson's friendship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], regarding his association with a Vampire as inappropriate for a member of the Royal Family. Anderson, conversely, considered his brother overly arrogant and was unwilling to allow racial prejudice to determine his friendships.[16][11]
Their relationship deteriorated further after Fenir's involvement with [[New Dawn]] was exposed. Anderson regarded his brother's arrangement with the organization as foolish and dangerous, particularly because it threatened to place Samar under the influence of external powers. Rather than defending Fenir solely because of their shared bloodline, Anderson became concerned with identifying possible infiltrators among his own followers and protecting the kingdom from further conspiracies.[19]

#### Thomas Fenrir
[[Thomas Fenrir]] is Anderson's youngest brother and the royal son most closely associated with the Beast of Ragnarok. Thomas received considerable attention from their parents because of his exceptional potential and the recognition granted to him by [[Fenrir]]. Anderson remained protective of his younger brother and was involved in the circumstances surrounding Thomas' return from the Demonic War, subsequently reporting the events to Tasha.[12]
Both brothers became subject to their mother's authority after she assumed control of the Werewolf Race. Anderson was prevented from intervening in Tasha's confrontation with Volk, while Thomas was also excluded from the struggle. Following her victory, Tasha permitted Thomas greater freedom concerning his future but continued requiring him to develop his strength and prepare for his responsibilities within the Royal Family.[6]

#### Wife
Anderson is married to an unnamed woman whose personal identity and abilities have not been established. Their marriage predates Tasha's ascension as Werewolf Progenitor, and Anderson appears to value the relationship considerably. When his mother suggested obtaining a second wife for political reasons, he immediately attempted to avoid the conversation, fearing that his current wife would react badly to such an arrangement. His behavior suggests that although he is a powerful Alpha and royal Prince, he prefers domestic stability over unnecessary political marriages.[12]
During Tasha's struggle against Volk, Anderson's wife was confined alongside him and his loyal subordinates to prevent his involvement in the conflict. Her existence and marriage to Anderson are confirmed, but her membership in the [[Lykos Clan]], as listed in earlier wiki material, has not been independently established through the available dialogue.[6]

#### Subordinates
Anderson commands a personal Pack that includes Liza, Yuran, Julian and Juan. They accompanied him during his activities in the Human World and later witnessed his battle against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Anderson values the abilities and loyalty of his followers, choosing individuals whom he considers capable of representing the Royal Family rather than merely surrounding himself with large numbers of weaker [[Werewolves]]. His subordinates also recognize his comparatively compassionate treatment of other Wolves, particularly his willingness to give individuals such as [[Johnny Lykos]] an opportunity to reconsider their actions before enforcing the harsher traditions of Werewolf society.[1][8]

### Current Status
Anderson remains alive and belongs to the Werewolf Royal Family under the political order established by Tasha. He retains his identity as the Second Prince and an Alpha Werewolf, while the supreme authority of the Race passed from Volk to his mother following her ascension as the Werewolf Progenitor. His marriage to an unnamed woman remains part of his established personal life, and Tasha's subsequent union with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] brought Anderson into the extended Elderblood family through his new stepfather.[6][7]
Although Anderson possesses considerable combat potential and inherited Divine power, his later activities and any further personal evolution have not been individually established. His confirmed standing remains that of an Alpha Werewolf and [[Demigod]] rather than an Ancient Alpha, full God or Werewolf Progenitor. His relationship with Victor continues to be defined by friendship and mutual respect, despite the immense difference in power that developed between them.
