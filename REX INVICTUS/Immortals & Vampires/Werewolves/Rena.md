### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 711
Rena is an Egyptian Werewolf, one of the Archbishops of the [[Blood God Religion]] and one of [[Valeria Alekerth]]'s most trusted high-ranking subordinates. Despite possessing no formal surname, Rena descends from the [[Lykos Clan]] lineage, with [[Victor Elderblood]] believing her to most likely be a descendant of his wife [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]]. Unlike many members of that lineage, however, Rena chose a path outside the traditional Lykos Clan structure and eventually devoted herself entirely to the Blood God Religion.[1]
Rena was already one of the religion's seven Archbishops before Valeria Alekerth was appointed High Priestess and therefore occupied one of its highest offices during the period when Victor formally acknowledged the religion growing around him.[2] Together with [[Lizbet Greygrave]], she later became one of Valeria's closest representatives and helped design the religion's expansion throughout Human and supernatural society.[1][3]
Victor eventually personally rewarded Rena's service by reconstructing her Werewolf existence. He evolved her from an ordinary Werewolf into a True Werewolf resembling the ancient ancestral state of her species, removed several traditional Werewolf limitations and incorporated traits derived from an Alpha Monster from [[Nightingale]]. The resulting evolution gave Rena an independent transformation, an animal form, environmental adaptation and complete invisibility, turning her into one of the most biologically advanced non-Progenitor [[Werewolves]] shown in the story.[3]

### Physical Appearance

#### Original Appearance
Rena is an attractive woman with light chocolate-brown skin, snow-white hair and sapphire-blue eyes. Although her skin tone was described as resembling that commonly associated with Latinas, Rena is explicitly Egyptian.[1]
She possesses delicate, graceful and noble-looking features that contrast dramatically with her nature as a dangerous Werewolf. During the ceremony in which [[Valeria Alekerth]] was selected as High Priestess, the narration specifically noted that someone looking only at Rena's refined appearance would never expect the ferocious Werewolf concealed beneath it.[2]

#### True Werewolf Evolution
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s modification of Rena substantially altered her appearance. Like most Beings personally evolved by him, she became visibly more beautiful, taller and physically more developed after the process.[3]
The most obvious biological change was that her ordinary Human ears disappeared and were replaced by wolf ears positioned upon her head. Her existing wolf tail also became thicker and more prominent. These traits no longer represented a temporary hybrid transformation; after Victor's reconstruction, features that ordinary [[Werewolves]] normally manifested only while transforming became part of Rena's base form.[3]
Her teeth also became significantly sharper. When Rena opened her mouth immediately after the transformation, those present could see rows of extremely sharp predatory teeth, further emphasizing how strongly her physiology had shifted toward the ancient form of the Werewolf race.[3]

### Personality
Rena is normally gentle, calm, graceful and noble. Her composed demeanor is sufficiently natural that people who observed only her ordinary behavior could easily underestimate the ferocity concealed beneath her elegant appearance.[4]
Her interactions with [[Victor Elderblood]] are a notable exception. Rena's devotion toward the God Emperor is so intense that she becomes visibly less composed in his presence. The narration explicitly clarifies that this should not be mistaken for Rena normally being clumsy; even a disciplined woman such as her can lose her composure while personally standing before the Being she worships as God.[4]
Rena is intensely religious and completely loyal to Victor. Her devotion extends beyond ordinary respect for a ruler. She regards him as the rightful supreme authority and reacts extremely aggressively to what she considers disrespect toward him. During negotiations in Celtic territory, a representative referred to Victor dismissively as "this Dragon". Although the remark was relatively minor, Rena immediately crushed the table beneath her grip, released her aura and furiously condemned the representative for insulting the God Emperor.[4]
Beneath her religious fanaticism, however, Rena is a competent administrator and political operator. She can listen calmly to negotiations, evaluate proposals and coordinate religious expansion without immediately resorting to violence. Her loss of restraint in the Celtic negotiations occurred specifically because Victor was insulted rather than because Rena lacked diplomatic ability.[4]
She also possesses a strong desire to protect ordinary people, although this compassion exists alongside the expansionist ideology of the [[Blood God Religion]]. When designing the religion's future strategy, Rena advocated protecting innocent civilians from supernatural predators, intervening where injustice occurred and forming trained Acolytes capable of fighting supernatural threats while simultaneously expanding Victor's influence.[3]
Rena strongly believes that Victor's rule would improve the world. Alongside Lizbet, she therefore does not interpret the religion's expansion merely as conquest. From her perspective, spreading the God Emperor's authority, removing corrupt rulers and protecting populations under his influence are complementary objectives.[3]

### Synopsis

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Rena first appeared as one of the seven Archbishops attending the ceremony where the newly organized [[Blood God Religion]] would receive its first High Priestess. By this period the religion had already expanded throughout large portions of Earth, [[Nightingale]] and Hell, while its hierarchy had developed a sophisticated merit system regulating advancement, education, discipline and rewards.[2]
The Archbishops occupied the highest ordinary positions within this structure. They were responsible for evaluating Acolytes, granting rewards and administering major portions of the religion beneath the future High Priestess. Unlike ordinary worshippers, members of the official hierarchy were extensively educated in subjects such as politics, economics, etiquette, medicine, architecture, warfare and assassination depending upon their specialization.[2]
During [[Roxanne Elderblood]]'s announcement that a High Priestess would finally be selected, Rena personally asked what method would be used to determine the successful candidate. Roxanne explained that unlike Archbishops, who advanced through the religion's system, the High Priestess would be chosen personally by their God.[2]
The revelation that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] himself would physically appear before them immediately overwhelmed the composure of many of the assembled women. Rena nevertheless remained one of the established senior members present when Victor formally acknowledged the religious organization built around him and [[Valeria Alekerth]] was elevated as its High Priestess.[2]
From that point onward, Rena served beneath Valeria as one of the principal Archbishops responsible for translating Victor's religious authority into practical administration.

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
By the era of the [[Elderblood Empire]], Rena remained one of Valeria's most important Archbishops. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] temporarily assumed direct responsibility for portions of the [[Blood God Religion]] during Valeria's reunion with [[Vanessa Alekerth]], Rena and [[Lizbet Greygrave]] were the two Archbishops immediately available to brief him on the religion's administration and future plans.[1]
Victor recognized Rena's Lykos heritage almost immediately. Despite lacking a surname, her snow-white hair, sapphire eyes and Werewolf bloodline made her origin obvious to him. He concluded that she was likely descended from Maya's branch of the Lykos lineage but had chosen not to follow the traditional path of the Clan.[1]
Victor then listened as Rena and Lizbet explained how they intended to expand the Blood God Religion's influence. Their objective was not merely to preach publicly or force populations into conversion. Instead, the Archbishops proposed establishing influence through community involvement, social aid, protection and tangible benefits supplied by Victor's religion.[5][3]
Rena specifically proposed creating groups of Acolytes capable of protecting innocent people from supernatural predators. Wherever injustice occurred, the religion would intervene and secure the prosperity of the people involved. The Blood God Religion would present itself as a force of peace and protection while slowly bringing communities into Victor's sphere of influence.[3]
Her ambitions quickly became much more aggressive. Rena openly declared that Victor should ultimately possess supreme authority and that political leaders attempting to secretly maintain control would eventually need to be removed. She also proposed controlling information so thoroughly that by the time outside authorities realized a transfer of power was occurring, the process would effectively already be complete.[3]
Victor was extremely pleased. He praised Rena and Lizbet for rapidly understanding his intentions and concluded that the two Archbishops were exactly the kind of representatives necessary to enforce his [[Will Solace|Will]] in other territories.[3]
Because he refused to allow individuals representing him before Gods and supernatural powers to remain weak, Victor decided to personally reward both women for their service to Valeria.[3]
Lizbet was evolved first, becoming a Spriggan and High Dark Elf. Victor then turned his attention toward Rena.[3]
Using his authority over Life, Blood and biological development, Victor fundamentally reconstructed Rena's Werewolf physiology. Her Human ears disappeared, wolf ears became permanent features of her base body and her tail grew thicker. Victor explained through the transformation that ordinary [[Werewolves]] historically required hybrid transformations to access greater portions of their racial power, whereas Rena now naturally possessed characteristics associated with that state even while untransformed.[3]
Rena had evolved into what the narration explicitly called a True Werewolf, placing her within the same fundamental species as [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]] but without possessing Tasha's status as a Progenitor. Rather than being transformed into a new racial origin, Rena had reached the ancient form from which modern Werewolves descended.[3]
This evolution removed several of the weaknesses historically attached to Alpha Werewolves. Rena no longer required the full moon to achieve her complete transformation and could transform whenever she desired. Her strength also ceased being dependent upon the number of subordinate Beta Werewolves connected to her pack, making her an independent Werewolf rather than one whose peak output relied upon external pack conditions.[3]
Victor then revealed that he had gone considerably further.
As an experiment, he incorporated blood and characteristics derived from an Alpha Monster he had encountered in [[Nightingale]] and restructured those characteristics so that they would function within a Werewolf Soul. When Rena examined herself, she discovered that she now possessed a complete animal form in addition to her humanoid and Werewolf states.[3]
The incorporated predator traits also granted her the ability to adapt to any environment and become completely invisible. Victor described Rena's new existence as that of a predator capable of continuously approaching greater perfection if she possessed the determination to continue developing herself.[3]
Victor deliberately chose not to give her every possible useful characteristic. He could have incorporated additional traits from monsters and opponents he had encountered in the past, but believed that granting everything immediately would remove Rena's motivation to continue growing. Instead, he gave her a vastly superior foundation and left room for her to advance through her own effort.[3]
Overjoyed by the transformation, Rena promised to work even harder to satisfy Victor's expectations. Victor ordered Rena and Lizbet to leave and mold the world according to his Will, officially beginning their wider campaign of religious expansion.[3]
Rena soon traveled into Celtic territory on an extremely important mission. The Blood God Religion's growing activity had begun alarming a High-Level God of the [[Celtic Pantheon]], who used his influence among Mortals to obstruct the Acolytes operating in his lands. Rena was therefore sent as the Archbishop responsible for dealing with the dispute.[4]
Although formally presented as negotiations, Rena understood that Victor had already ordered the expansion. Her actual task was to listen to the local representative's request and determine how the religion should proceed rather than ask permission to enter the territory.[4]
The Human representative, himself directly Blessed by a Celtic God, attempted to reach an agreement whereby Rena would temporarily restrain the activities of her Acolytes while the local government dealt with the panic generated by Victor's enormous Dragon manifestation.[4]
Rena initially listened with her normal gentle and graceful demeanor. The representative remained respectful until he referred to Victor dismissively as "this Dragon".[4]
Her composure vanished immediately.
Rena crushed the table beneath her hand, released her predatory aura and furiously accused the representative of insulting His Imperial Majesty. Her hair began floating under the pressure of her power, her eyes became predatory and her sharpened teeth made the otherwise elegant Archbishop resemble a monster preparing to devour the Humans before her.[4]
The confrontation demonstrated both sides of Rena's character. Under ordinary circumstances she was a capable diplomat and composed noblewoman, but disrespect toward Victor could instantly overwhelm that restraint. It also demonstrated why individuals occupying the office of Archbishop required far more than simple Faith: they represented the God Emperor before foreign Gods, governments and supernatural powers and therefore needed strength, education, authority and the ability to enforce the religion's interests themselves.[4]

### Skills & Abilities
Rena was originally a conventional Werewolf of the Lykos lineage. After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] personally reconstructed her in Chapter 1029, she became a True Werewolf with an independent Alpha state and several artificial predator characteristics derived from [[Nightingale]]'s Monsters.

#### True Werewolf Physiology
- True Werewolf Evolution: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] evolved Rena from an ordinary modern Werewolf into a True Werewolf, restoring characteristics associated with the ancient ancestral form of the species. The narration directly compares her species to [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]], although Rena is not a Progenitor.[3]
- Ancestral Werewolf State: Rena's new body represents the original form from which modern [[Werewolves]] descended rather than an entirely unrelated mutation.[3]
- Permanent Hybrid Characteristics: Traits ordinary Werewolves normally display only while accessing stronger hybrid transformations—most visibly wolf ears and a pronounced tail—became part of Rena's ordinary base form.[3]
- Enhanced Physical Attributes: As a True Werewolf, Rena possesses supernatural strength, speed, stamina, durability and agility superior to her former Werewolf state. Her casual physical strength after evolution was sufficient to crush a negotiating table beneath her hand and subsequently throw it hard enough to destroy both the table and part of the wall behind it.[4]
- Enhanced Senses: Rena retains the heightened predatory senses inherent to Werewolves, further refined by her superior physiology.
- Enhanced Healing: Her advanced Werewolf body possesses the regenerative characteristics of her race at a level superior to her former state.
- Predatory Teeth and Claws: Rena possesses exceptionally sharp teeth following her evolution, reflecting the increasingly predatory nature of her body.[3]
- Longevity: As a supernatural Werewolf, Rena possesses a lifespan considerably beyond that of an ordinary Human.

#### Independent Alpha Physiology
- True Alpha State: Rena possesses an advanced Alpha Werewolf state that no longer depends upon several traditional limitations affecting ordinary Alpha [[Werewolves]].[3]
- Moon Independence: Rena no longer needs the full moon to achieve her complete Werewolf transformation. She can transform whenever she chooses.[3]
- Pack Independence: Her peak strength is no longer limited by or dependent upon the number of Beta Werewolves belonging to her pack.[3]
- Independent Power Source: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s reconstruction made Rena a self-sufficient Werewolf whose racial power belongs entirely to herself rather than relying upon an external lunar or pack condition.[3]

#### Werewolf Transformation
- Complete Werewolf Transformation: Rena can transform into the complete combat form of a Werewolf without requiring a full moon.[3]
- True Ancestor Form: Her transformation accesses the ancient ancestral characteristics restored through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s reconstruction rather than merely the incomplete state of an ordinary modern Werewolf.[3]
- Voluntary Transformation: Rena can enter or leave her stronger form according to her own will.[3]

#### Animal Transformation
- Animal Form: After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] incorporated Alpha Monster traits into her reconstructed Soul and Werewolf physiology, Rena discovered that she possessed a complete animal form separate from her ordinary humanoid state.[3]
- Predatory Physiology: Victor deliberately designed this form around the characteristics of a predator rather than simply giving Rena another cosmetic transformation.[3]

#### Alpha Monster Characteristics
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] incorporated the blood and characteristics of an Alpha Monster from [[Nightingale]] into Rena while altering its spiritual structure so that the traits functioned properly inside a Werewolf.[3]
- Environmental Adaptation: Rena can adapt herself to different environments, a characteristic derived from the predator Victor used as part of her evolution.[3]
- Survival Adaptation: The ability allows Rena's biology to respond to hostile environmental conditions rather than remaining constrained to a single natural habitat.[3]
- Invisibility: Rena can become completely invisible, another characteristic incorporated during Victor's reconstruction.[3]
- Predatory Concealment: Victor specifically associated this trait with Rena's development as a perfected predator rather than an illusion-based form of concealment.[3]

#### Growth Potential
- Adaptive Evolution Potential: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] deliberately left Rena's new body incomplete in the sense that it retained room for further self-directed growth. He refused to simply add every useful characteristic available to him because doing so would eliminate the motivation necessary for Rena to continue pursuing strength.[3]
- Predator Toward Perfection: Victor described Rena as a predator capable of walking toward greater perfection if she continued actively developing herself.[3]

#### Religious Authority
- Archbishop Authority: Rena is one of the highest-ranking members of the [[Blood God Religion]] beneath [[Valeria Alekerth]]. She possesses authority over Acolytes, missionaries and religious operations assigned to her.[2][4]
- Rite of Passage Authority: Official Acolytes of the Blood God Religion are accepted through rites performed by an Archbishop, giving Rena the authority to formally induct new members into the institution.[2]
- Missionary Command: Rena participates in directing Emissaries and Acolytes responsible for spreading [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s religious and political influence.[3]
- Diplomatic Authority: She is trusted to independently negotiate with governments and representatives of foreign Pantheons on behalf of the Blood God Religion.[4]
- Religious Enforcement: Her status is not merely ceremonial. Rena possesses sufficient supernatural strength and institutional authority to protect the religion's operations when diplomacy fails.[4]

#### Education and Skills
The [[Blood God Religion]] requires its senior personnel to possess education extending far beyond theology. Its Acolytes are trained in practical disciplines, while Archbishops represent the highest products of that system beneath the High Priestess.[2]
- Leadership: Rena commands lower-ranking members of the Blood God Religion and is trusted with independent missions involving the expansion of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s influence.[3][4]
- Administration: She participated directly in the strategic planning of the religion's expansion and was one of only two Archbishops immediately briefing Victor while Valeria was absent.[1][3]
- Diplomacy: Rena is capable of calmly conducting formal negotiations with representatives of foreign Gods and governments when Victor's honor is not being challenged.[4]
- Political Strategy: She understands indirect methods of expansion including community involvement, information control, social protection and the removal of political leadership hostile to Victor's authority.[3]
- Religious Propagation: Rena is an experienced missionary leader capable of designing methods by which the Blood God Religion can spread without relying exclusively upon direct coercion.[3]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
Rena worships [[Victor Elderblood]] as her God Emperor and demonstrates an exceptionally intense degree of personal devotion toward him. Although normally graceful and composed, she becomes noticeably less controlled when directly interacting with Victor.[4]
Victor in turn recognizes Rena as one of [[Valeria Alekerth]]'s most competent subordinates. After listening to Rena and Lizbet's plans for spreading his influence, he openly praised their understanding and personally rewarded both women by improving their species.[3]
Rena's reaction to this reward was overwhelming gratitude. She immediately promised to work harder to meet Victor's expectations and accepted his command to spread his [[Will Solace|Will]] throughout the world.[3]
Her loyalty borders on fanaticism where Victor's honor is concerned. A comparatively minor disrespectful reference to him during negotiations was enough to cause Rena to abandon her normal diplomacy and nearly attack the people responsible.[4]

#### Valeria Alekerth
[[Valeria Alekerth]] is Rena's High Priestess and direct superior within the [[Blood God Religion]]. Rena is one of Valeria's most trusted Archbishops and remained among the officials capable of administering the religion while Valeria temporarily withdrew to spend time with her resurrected daughter.[1]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s decision to strengthen Rena was explicitly framed partly as a reward for the work she had performed in support of his disciple.[3]

#### Lizbet Greygrave
[[Lizbet Greygrave]] is Rena's closest known fellow Archbishop. The two repeatedly operate together and served as the principal Archbishops briefing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] during Valeria's temporary absence.[1]
Rena and Lizbet jointly designed the [[Blood God Religion]]'s future expansion strategy, combining missionary work, public assistance, supernatural protection, political influence and eventual control beneath the God Emperor.[5][3]
Victor subsequently evolved both women simultaneously: Lizbet became a High Dark Elf and Spriggan, while Rena became a True Werewolf enhanced with Alpha Monster characteristics.[3]
