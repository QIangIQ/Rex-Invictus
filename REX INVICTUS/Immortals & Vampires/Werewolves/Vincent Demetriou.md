### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 749
Vincent Demetriou is an elderly Alpha Werewolf and an influential member of Clan Demetriou, one of Samar's old Alpha Werewolf bloodlines. Clan Demetriou possesses a history comparable to the ancient [[Lykos Clan]] and was among the families involved in the early development of Samar.[1]
Vincent was one of the principal [[Werewolves]] who opposed the relationship between [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]], believing that the birth of a Vampire–Werewolf Hybrid from their union should be prevented. His refusal to recognize Victor's current position as the Demon King ultimately caused him and his supporters to be publicly humiliated and taken to Hell for punishment.[1][2]

### Physical Appearance
Vincent is described as an aged-looking man, reflecting his status as one of Samar's older Alpha [[Werewolves]].[1]
No detailed description of his height, hair color, eye color, skin tone, clothing or Werewolf transformation has been provided.

### Personality
Vincent is proud, traditionalist, stubborn and strongly prejudiced against the mixing of Vampire and Werewolf Bloodlines. His opposition to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Leona's relationship was not merely political; he genuinely considered the possibility of their child an unacceptable Hybrid and believed the situation needed to be stopped before it progressed any further.[1]
His pride in Clan Demetriou is particularly pronounced. When [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] mocked his reasoning and suggested that old age had made him senile, Vincent immediately defended his family by declaring that Clan Demetriou stood on the same level as Clan Lykos.[1]
Vincent's greatest flaw is his inability to adapt his worldview when confronted with changes in the balance of power. He acknowledged that Victor had become the Demon King but continued treating him primarily as a Noble Vampire, believing his Race mattered more than the political and military power he had accumulated. Maya and [[Anthony Bianchi]] both criticized this reasoning, while [[Leônidas Uruky]] warned Vincent directly that antagonizing Victor could result in the complete destruction of Clan Demetriou.[1]
He is also deeply attached to Samar's traditional hierarchy. Vincent believed that because Clan Demetriou had helped establish Samar, [[Volk Fenrir]] would naturally protect the Clan against an outsider regardless of the circumstances. He was visibly shaken when Volk refused to promise such protection.[1]
Although extremely proud, Vincent is not completely fearless. Once Victor demonstrated that Volk would not intervene and effortlessly sent one of Vincent's allies directly into Hell, Vincent's resistance collapsed. Faced with the choice between humiliation and eternal punishment, he eventually knelt and apologized.[2]
Even then, however, his apology was visibly insincere. Victor immediately recognized the anger and desire for revenge remaining within Vincent and his supporters and decided that a harsher lesson was necessary.[2]

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Vincent first appeared during the political backlash caused by [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]]' relationship. Although Maya had restrained the [[Lykos Clan]] and the families under her direct influence, several independent Alpha Werewolf families remained strongly opposed to the possibility of a child being born between Victor and Leona.[1]
Vincent became one of the most vocal members of this faction.
During a gathering attended by [[Volk Fenrir]], Maya, [[Anthony Bianchi]], [[Leônidas Uruky]] and other senior [[Werewolves]], Vincent argued that the situation needed to be "nipped in the bud". He maintained that although Victor was the Demon King, he was still fundamentally a Noble Vampire and therefore considered his relationship with an Alpha Werewolf unacceptable.[1]
Maya immediately mocked Vincent for focusing upon Victor's former racial status while ignoring his current authority. Vincent responded angrily, insisting that Clan Demetriou was equal in standing to Clan Lykos.[1]
The chapter established that Clan Demetriou, like Clan Lykos, descended from a long lineage of Alpha Werewolves. Its history was considerably more extensive than that of younger Alpha families such as Anthony Bianchi's lineage.[1]
Anthony nevertheless agreed with Maya rather than Vincent. He urged Vincent to calm down and reminded him that they were dealing with a King, not an ordinary Vampire.[1]
Vincent dismissed the warning. He argued that a respectable King would not contaminate his lineage through such a relationship, causing Maya to interpret the comment as an insult toward her own family. Their confrontation nearly escalated into violence before Volk ordered the Alphas to stop.[1]
Volk then asked Leônidas for his opinion because the Uruky Patriarch had recently encountered Victor directly. Leônidas admitted that he personally disliked the idea of a Hybrid, but distinguished his personal feelings from the logical reality of the situation. He warned Vincent that Victor was someone Clan Demetriou could not afford to provoke and that the family's ancient lineage could end that very day if Vincent refused to accept the consequences of his actions.[1]
Vincent remained confident that Volk would protect him. He emphasized that Clan Demetriou had helped create Samar and directly asked the [[Lycaon|Werewolf King]] whether he intended to abandon one of his people's founding families.[1]
Volk remained silent.
His refusal to answer immediately caused Vincent to begin sweating. As King, Volk understood that protecting Vincent would mean potentially bringing the entire Werewolf civilization into conflict with Victor's united Hell, an enemy Samar lacked the military strength to confront successfully.[1]
Victor then arrived personally.
The atmosphere changed immediately as Victor released the oppressive presence associated with his position as the Demon King of Tyranny. His maids surrounded the room while the gathered Alpha Werewolves were forced to confront the enormous difference between their own political importance and Victor's current power.[1]
Victor did not attempt to negotiate with Vincent.
Instead, he summoned a black throne, opened a portal into Hell and gave Vincent's faction two choices: continue interfering and be dragged into Hell for endless punishment, or kneel and publicly apologize for involving themselves in a matter that did not concern them.[2]
One elderly member of Vincent's faction attempted to argue that Victor could not do such a thing. Before the man could finish speaking, Victor sent him through the portal, where Demonic hands immediately dragged him deeper into Hell.[2]
The display destroyed much of the confidence among Vincent's supporters.
Vincent desperately turned toward Volk and demanded that his King intervene. Volk again refused. Maya openly told Vincent that Volk could not protect him even if he wanted to, and the King's continued silence confirmed her statement.[2]
Realizing that no protection was coming, Vincent finally fell to his knees. His supporters followed him, and together they repeated the humiliating apology Victor demanded.[2]
Victor immediately recognized that none of them meant it.
Their anger, humiliation and desire for revenge remained obvious, so Victor decided that an apology was insufficient. He summoned Lily's Toy, a grotesque Demonic subordinate created by Lily to administer punishments within the Lust Sector of Hell.[2]
Victor ordered the creature to teach Vincent and his supporters humility. When one of the Werewolves continued protesting, Lily's Toy summoned numerous arms, restrained the group and received Victor's permission to employ Hell Hounds as part of their punishment.[2]
Vincent and the other offending Werewolves were subsequently dragged through the portal into Hell.
Victor clarified that they would not be killed. Lily's Toy was taking them to a region where the concentration of Miasma could be reduced enough for living beings to survive, meaning the purpose of the punishment was discipline and humiliation rather than execution.[2]
Vincent has not received another substantial individual role in the currently documented material.

### Skills & Abilities
Vincent has not participated in an individual battle and has demonstrated no unique Element or named supernatural technique. His confirmed abilities primarily derive from his status as an Alpha Werewolf born into one of Samar's ancient Alpha lineages.

#### Alpha Werewolf Physiology
- Alpha Werewolf Physiology: Vincent belongs to the Alpha class of [[Werewolves]] and possesses supernatural physical characteristics superior to ordinary Werewolves.
- Superhuman Strength: Alpha Werewolves possess physical strength considerably beyond ordinary Humans and lower-ranking members of their Race.
- Superhuman Speed: Vincent possesses supernatural movement speed and reflexes.
- Superhuman Durability: His Alpha physiology provides greatly enhanced resistance to physical damage.
- Enhanced Stamina: Werewolves possess supernatural endurance.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Vincent possesses the accelerated healing inherent to Werewolf physiology.
- Enhanced Senses: His Race provides heightened smell, hearing and predatory perception.
- Longevity: Vincent is visibly elderly even by Werewolf standards and belongs to one of Samar's old generations of Alpha Werewolves.[1]
- Werewolf Transformation: Vincent possesses the racial ability to manifest more pronounced Werewolf characteristics, although his transformed state has not been individually shown.
- Natural Weapons: Werewolf physiology provides supernatural claws and fangs.
- Predatory Instincts: As an Alpha, Vincent possesses instinctive sensitivity toward hierarchy, strength and dominance.
- Alpha Presence: His position among Samar's senior Alpha Werewolves gives him greater instinctive authority than lower-ranking Wolves.

#### Ancient Alpha Bloodline
- Demetriou Bloodline: Clan Demetriou descends from a long lineage of Alpha [[Werewolves]] comparable in age and prestige to Clan Lykos.[1]
- High Social Standing: Vincent possessed enough political influence to participate directly in meetings involving [[Volk Fenrir]], Maya, Leônidas and other important Alphas.[1]
- Founding-Clan Heritage: Vincent states that Clan Demetriou participated in the creation or early establishment of Samar's Werewolf society, explaining his confidence that the King could not simply abandon his family.[1]

- Godhood or Divinity.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
Vincent strongly opposed [[Victor Elderblood]]'s relationship with Leona because of Victor's Vampire origins and the possibility of the couple producing a Hybrid child.[1]
His greatest mistake was continuing to view Victor through the standards used for ordinary Vampires. Vincent acknowledged Victor's title as Demon King but failed to understand that his military and personal power had grown far beyond anything Clan Demetriou could oppose.[1]
Victor ultimately forced Vincent to kneel and apologize before sending him and his supporters into Hell for punishment.[2]

#### Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood
[[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] openly despised Vincent's reasoning.
Their argument became particularly hostile after Vincent implied that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s relationship with Leona would contaminate or "soil" his lineage. Maya interpreted the statement as an insult against the Lykos Bloodline and threatened to wipe out Vincent's family herself.[1]
Vincent responded by insulting Maya and referring dismissively to her numerous partners, nearly provoking a direct confrontation before Volk intervened.[1]

#### Anthony Bianchi
[[Anthony Bianchi]] was considerably more cautious than Vincent. Although both were senior Alphas present during the dispute, Anthony agreed with Maya that provoking [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was irrational and repeatedly attempted to convince Vincent to calm down.[1]
Vincent rejected Anthony's advice.

#### Leônidas Uruky
[[Leônidas Uruky]] understood some of Vincent's personal discomfort regarding the possibility of a Hybrid but considered acting upon that discomfort suicidal.
Having already encountered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] directly, Leônidas explicitly warned Vincent that continuing the confrontation could result in the extinction of Clan Demetriou.[1]

#### Volk Fenrir
[[Volk Fenrir]] was Vincent's King.
Vincent expected Volk to protect Clan Demetriou because of the Clan's ancient contribution to Samar. Volk instead prioritized the survival of the broader Werewolf civilization and refused to enter an unwinnable conflict with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s forces merely to defend Vincent's actions.[1][2]
Volk's refusal to intervene was the moment Vincent fully realized how isolated his faction had become.
