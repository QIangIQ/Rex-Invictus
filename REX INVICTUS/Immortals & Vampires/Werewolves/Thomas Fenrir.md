### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 538
Thomas Fenrir is an Alpha Werewolf, [[Demigod]], and the youngest known son of former [[Lycaon|Werewolf King]] [[Volk Fenrir]] and [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]]. He is the Third Prince of Samar, the younger brother of Fenir and Anderson, and one of the few [[Werewolves]] personally recognized and Blessed by [[Fenrir]], the Beast of Ragnarok.[1]
Thomas was already considered an Alpha while still a child, although his Alpha nature had not yet completely awakened when he was twelve years old. His natural potential was considered exceptional even among members of Samar's Royal Family, combining the Royal Werewolf lineage inherited from Volk, Divine ancestry inherited through Tasha, and Fenrir's personal Blessing.[1]
Unlike his older brothers, Thomas was raised from an early age with the expectation that he could eventually become a future ruler of Samar. Tasha subjected him to extensive training in politics, leadership, combat and self-discipline, teaching him never to accept mediocrity even if his position as a Prince could have allowed him an easier life.[2]
Thomas became directly involved in the wider supernatural conflict during [[Diablo]]'s invasion when [[Agares]] infiltrated Samar and kidnapped him. He was subsequently imprisoned alongside [[Emily Moriarty]] and [[Lilith Tepes]], beginning an unusual relationship with Emily that eventually developed into romantic interest on Thomas' side.[3][4]

### Physical Appearance
Thomas is a young boy with chocolate-brown skin, black hair and black eyes. During his introduction he wore clothing appropriate for a young Royal Prince, while his later imprisonment left him dressed much more simply.[3]
Despite being only twelve years old at the time of his kidnapping, Thomas was already physically superior to an ordinary Human child because of his Werewolf and [[Demigod]] heritage.
His complete mature Alpha Werewolf appearance has not yet been shown because his Alpha nature was explicitly stated to have remained incompletely awakened during his childhood.[1]

### Personality
Thomas initially appears rude, blunt, impatient and spoiled. He frequently swears, complains when ordered around and has little interest in behaving politely merely because Royal etiquette expects him to.
His interactions with [[Emily Moriarty]] emphasize this side of his personality. Emily repeatedly became irritated by his language and attitude, while Thomas frequently mocked her in return. Their constant arguments eventually resulted in Emily calling him a "stupid dog".[3]
However, Thomas is considerably more intelligent and disciplined than his initial behavior suggests.
During his captivity by powerful Demons, he remained calm enough to:
- observe his surroundings;
- keep track of time;
- read rather than panic;
- assess the other prisoners;
- and understand the seriousness of their circumstances.[4]
Thomas' maturity is largely the result of Tasha's upbringing.
Rather than allowing her youngest son to become an incompetent Royal child, Tasha trained him from childhood with the expectation that he could someday become King. Thomas was required to study politics, combat, leadership and the responsibilities of an Alpha.[2]
One of the primary lessons she repeatedly impressed upon him was:
"Never accept mediocrity."
—[[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]], Chapter 855
Thomas internalized this mentality even though he frequently complained about the amount of work involved.
His later reaction after being freed from the expectation of becoming King also revealed that Thomas had sacrificed much of a normal childhood for his Royal education. Once he realized he would have more free time, he became genuinely excited about being able to play games, watch movies and enjoy hobbies he had previously missed.[2]
Thomas is also cautious around his mother.
Although Tasha openly spoils and loves him, Thomas understands that she can instantly switch from affectionate mother to extremely dangerous ruler. Her serious tone is enough to make him reconsider lying or attempting to avoid her questions.[2]
Thomas eventually developed romantic feelings for Emily Moriarty. His attraction was not purely physical; he also considered her intelligence, talent, supernatural strength and political position appropriate for someone who might eventually become part of his pack.[2]

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Thomas became directly involved in the Demonic War when [[Diablo]] launched coordinated attacks against multiple supernatural factions.
Rather than attempting a direct conquest of Samar, the Demonic forces created widespread chaos to distract the Werewolf Kingdom.
During the disturbance, [[Agares]], the Second Duke of Hell, infiltrated Samar and kidnapped Thomas.[1]
The kidnapping immediately caused a crisis inside the Werewolf Royal Family.
Unlike Anderson and Fenir, who were already powerful adults capable of protecting themselves, Thomas was only twelve years old.
Volk explained that although Thomas had possessed Alpha status from childhood, his Alpha nature had not yet completely awakened.[1]
Despite this limitation, Thomas' potential was already considered extraordinary.
Volk revealed that Thomas had been personally recognized and Blessed by:
[[Fenrir]], the Beast of Ragnarok.
[1]
This made Thomas' kidnapping particularly serious.
He was not merely a Royal hostage, but one of Samar's most promising future [[Werewolves]].
Tasha responded immediately by mobilizing the Werewolves directly loyal to her and beginning preparations to recover her son.[1]

##### Captivity
Thomas was imprisoned alongside [[Emily Moriarty]].
Despite the danger surrounding them, the two children quickly began arguing with one another.
Emily found Thomas rude and annoying.
Thomas found Emily equally irritating.
Their relationship rapidly developed into constant insults and childish competition despite the fact that they were prisoners of extremely powerful Demons.[3]
[[Lilith Tepes]] was later placed in the same prison.
When she returned after being isolated for approximately two days, Thomas was sitting calmly on a bed reading a book.
He immediately informed her how long she had been gone.[4]
Emily attempted to force Thomas to introduce himself properly.
Thomas reluctantly identified himself as:
Thomas Fenrir, third son of [[Volk Fenrir]], the [[Lycaon|Werewolf King]], and Tasha [[Fenrir]], the Werewolf Queen.
[3]
His interactions with Emily and Lilith demonstrated that Thomas remained surprisingly composed during captivity.

##### Rescue
[[Victor Elderblood]] eventually reached the prison during his attack against [[Diablo]]'s forces.
Victor and his allies recovered Thomas, Emily and Lilith while dealing with [[Agares]] and the surrounding Demons.[5]
Thomas' rescue did not immediately end the danger.
After escaping, his physical condition deteriorated severely.
The group discovered that Thomas had been deliberately poisoned using a specialized form of:
Wolfsbane.
[6]
The poison was dangerous even to Thomas despite his Royal Alpha lineage and [[Demigod]] heritage.
[[Emily Moriarty]] helped investigate his condition and participated in treating the poison. By the conclusion of the treatment, she confirmed that Thomas was no longer in immediate danger.[7]
The event became one of the foundations of Thomas' later feelings toward her.
The two spent most of their captivity insulting one another, yet Emily immediately helped save him once his life was genuinely threatened.

##### Return to Samar
Thomas was eventually returned safely to Samar.
[[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]] reacted immediately upon seeing him again.
She embraced Thomas, referred to him affectionately as her darling and briefly abandoned her usual Queenly composure before returning to the investigation surrounding his kidnapping.[8]
Tasha subsequently ordered that Thomas receive:
twenty-four-hour protection.
[8]
The successful kidnapping of a Royal Prince had demonstrated that Samar's internal defenses could no longer be trusted completely.
Thomas therefore remained under significantly heavier protection while Tasha investigated the possibility of internal betrayal.

#### King Among Kings
Thomas' relationship with [[Fenrir]] gained additional context after [[Victor Elderblood]] personally encountered the Beast of Ragnarok.
For generations, the Werewolf Kingdom had treated Fenrir as though he were naturally obligated to protect Samar.
Victor rejected this assumption.
Fenrir did not operate according to normal political responsibility.
Instead, the ancient Beast protected what existed within his territory and developed simple but genuine companionship toward specific individuals he personally recognized.
Victor specifically identified Thomas among the people Fenrir had accepted in this manner.[9]
This makes Fenrir's Blessing especially important.
It was not simply a random supernatural enhancement.
Fenrir had personally acknowledged Thomas.
However, the Blessing does not automatically mean Thomas possesses Fenrir's:
- <End> Divinity;
- Conceptual Erasure;
- Existence Erasure;
- God-King power;
- or complete abilities.
No such inheritance has been demonstrated.

##### Werewolf Royal Crisis
As the political conflict between Tasha and Volk intensified, Thomas was deliberately kept away from the confrontation.
Tasha refused to force her sons to participate in a struggle between their parents.
Thomas therefore did not directly fight during the conflict that eventually ended Volk's reign.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Tasha ultimately defeated Volk and became the:
Werewolf Progenitor
and the new supreme Alpha of the Race.
This development dramatically changed Thomas' future.
Before Tasha's rise, Thomas had spent most of his childhood training under the assumption that he might eventually inherit Samar's throne.
By Chapter 855, Thomas finally asked whether his mother's transformation into the immortal Progenitor of the Werewolf Race meant he no longer needed to prepare himself to become King.[2]
Tasha confirmed that the situation had changed.
However, she refused to allow Thomas to become lazy.
She reminded him:
Never accept mediocrity.
[2]
Thomas would still be required to:
- train;
- become stronger;
- continue political education;
- learn leadership;
- and eventually establish his own pack.
The major difference was that he could finally choose more of his own future.
Thomas immediately became excited by the possibility of experiencing things his training had prevented him from enjoying.
For the first time he could seriously think about:
- games;
- entertainment;
- movies;
- hobbies;
- and a life that was not completely centered around becoming King.[2]

##### Emily Moriarty
Tasha also informed Thomas that he had reached the point where he needed to begin thinking about his future pack and potential partners.
Thomas initially disliked the conversation.
However, when he considered possible candidates, one person immediately came to mind:
[[Emily Moriarty]].
[2]
Thomas believed Emily would be an unusually suitable partner because she possessed:
- exceptional talent;
- considerable supernatural strength;
- intelligence;
- prestigious lineage;
- and the status of being the daughter of an important supernatural ruler.
Thomas cautiously asked his mother:
"Can I pursue a Human?"
—Thomas Fenrir, Chapter 855
Tasha immediately realized that he already had someone in mind.
Thomas eventually admitted:
Emily.
When Tasha asked for her surname, Thomas reluctantly answered:
Moriarty.
[2]
Tasha rejected the idea because of the historical complications between [[Werewolves]], Humans and Witches.
Thomas' interest nevertheless remained.
As of his latest confirmed development, Emily has not been shown reciprocating those feelings directly.
The relationship should therefore be classified as:
Thomas' romantic interest in Emily
rather than a confirmed romantic relationship.

#### Victor and Tasha
After becoming Werewolf Progenitor, Tasha entered a [[Soul Marriage]] with [[Victor Elderblood]].
Victor consequently became Thomas' stepfather.[10]
Unlike Anderson, who had already developed a close friendship and rivalry with Victor before Tasha joined him, Thomas' personal relationship with Victor is less extensive.
Victor nevertheless played a major role in Thomas' life.
He:
- participated in rescuing Thomas from [[Diablo]]'s forces;
- helped reveal the true nature of [[Fenrir]]'s relationship with Samar;
- helped Tasha during her rise to Progenitor;
- and later became part of Thomas' family.

### Demigod Heritage
Thomas is a [[Demigod]] through his mother.
[[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]] was originally an Egyptian Goddess before later becoming a Werewolf Queen and eventually the Werewolf Progenitor.
Thomas consequently possesses Divine ancestry in addition to his Royal Werewolf Bloodline.
His supernatural foundation therefore combines:
- Alpha Werewolf physiology;
- Royal [[Fenrir]] lineage;
- Divine ancestry;
- and Fenrir's Blessing.
However, Thomas has not yet demonstrated the same mature control over [[Divine Energy]] shown by his older brother Anderson.
His Demigod classification therefore confirms his nature and potential but should not be used to automatically grant him every Divine technique demonstrated by other Demigods.

### Skills and Abilities
- Alpha Werewolf Physiology: Thomas was born as an Alpha Werewolf despite his young age.[1]
- Incomplete Alpha [[Awakening]]: Thomas' Alpha nature had not completely awakened when he was twelve years old.[1]
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Thomas possesses physical characteristics above ordinary Humans due to his Werewolf physiology, although the upper limit of his mature strength has not yet been demonstrated.
- Enhanced Senses: As a Werewolf, Thomas possesses superior sensory capabilities.
- Enhanced Healing Factor: Thomas possesses the regenerative characteristics of [[Werewolves]], although specialized anti-Werewolf substances can overwhelm this ability.
- Longevity: Thomas possesses the supernatural lifespan associated with Werewolves and Demigods.
- Werewolf Transformation: Thomas possesses the racial potential for Werewolf transformation, although his mature form has not yet been shown.
- [[Demigod]] Physiology: Thomas inherited Divine ancestry through [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]].
- Divine Potential: Thomas possesses the potential to access Divine power, although no mature personal Divine technique has yet been demonstrated.
- [[Fenrir]]'s Blessing: Thomas was personally Blessed by Fenrir, the Beast of Ragnarok.[1]
- Supernatural Blessing: Fenrir's Blessing provides Thomas with an additional supernatural connection beyond his natural Bloodline.
- Recognition by Fenrir: Fenrir personally acknowledged Thomas rather than treating him as merely another Werewolf.[9]
- Royal Werewolf Potential: Thomas combines Volk's powerful Alpha Bloodline with Tasha's Divine heritage.
- Intelligence: Thomas possesses significant intelligence despite his abrasive personality.
- Political Education: Tasha trained Thomas from childhood in politics and rulership because he was considered a possible future King.[2]
- Leadership Training: Thomas has received education intended to prepare him to eventually establish and command his own pack.[2]
- Combat Training: Thomas has received Royal combat instruction from childhood.
- Observation: Thomas remained highly observant during his imprisonment and accurately tracked how long Lilith had been separated from the group.[4]
- Mental Discipline: Thomas remained calm enough to read and think rationally despite being imprisoned by high-ranking Demons.[4]
- Caution: Thomas generally understands when he is unable to meaningfully fight a stronger opponent and did not recklessly attack his captors.
- Brutal Honesty: Thomas has little interest in hiding his immediate opinion and frequently speaks without concern for politeness.

### Resistance and Vulnerabilities

#### Wolfsbane
Thomas remains vulnerable to Wolfsbane.
Following his rescue, he became dangerously ill because [[Diablo]]'s forces had poisoned him with a specialized Wolfsbane compound.[6]
The poison was capable of threatening his life despite his:
- Royal Werewolf Bloodline;
- Alpha status;
- [[Demigod]] heritage;
- and [[Fenrir]]'s Blessing.
[[Emily Moriarty]] participated in treating him and eventually confirmed that he was no longer in immediate danger.[7]
This establishes that Thomas' exceptional lineage does not automatically negate standard anti-Werewolf substances.

### Relationships

#### Tasha Fenrir Elderblood
[[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]] is Thomas' biological mother and the primary person responsible for his upbringing.
Thomas is one of Tasha's greatest emotional weaknesses.
His kidnapping caused her to immediately mobilize forces and begin planning retaliation against [[Diablo]]'s faction.[1]
After Thomas returned safely, Tasha embraced him affectionately before immediately arranging permanent protection and investigating the failure that allowed him to be taken.[8]
Tasha also personally structured Thomas' education.
She trained him relentlessly as a potential future King and repeatedly taught him not to accept mediocrity.[2]
After becoming Werewolf Progenitor, Tasha freed Thomas from the obligation to inherit Samar's throne.
However, she still required him to:
- train;
- study;
- become stronger;
- and eventually build his own pack.

#### Volk Fenrir
[[Volk Fenrir]] is Thomas' biological father and the former [[Lycaon|Werewolf King]].
Volk was deeply concerned when Thomas was kidnapped because his youngest son had not yet fully awakened his Alpha nature.[1]
He was nevertheless extremely proud of Thomas' potential and specifically cited [[Fenrir]]'s Blessing as proof of how exceptional his youngest son was.[1]
Thomas was originally raised as one possible future successor to Volk.
That expectation effectively ended when Tasha became the Werewolf Progenitor.

#### Anderson
Anderson is Thomas' older brother.
Anderson helped ensure Thomas' safe return during the Demonic War and later personally reported the circumstances surrounding his rescue to Tasha.[8]
His attitude toward Thomas reflects the Werewolf principle that:
"The Pack always comes first."
—Anderson, Chapter 580
Both Anderson and Thomas were later kept outside Tasha's direct conflict with Volk.

#### Fenir
Fenir is Thomas' older brother.
Their family relationship later became complicated by the conspiracies surrounding Samar and [[New Dawn]].
Thomas himself remained largely protected from those events because of his age.

#### Emily Moriarty
[[Emily Moriarty]] is Thomas' childhood rival and current romantic interest.
The two met while imprisoned by [[Diablo]]'s forces.
Their relationship initially consisted almost entirely of arguments and insults.
Emily repeatedly called Thomas:
Stupid Dog.
[3]
Thomas regularly irritated her in return.
Despite their constant fighting, the two gradually became familiar with one another.
When Thomas became dangerously ill from Wolfsbane, Emily immediately participated in saving him.[6][7]
Years later, when Tasha required Thomas to consider potential partners, Emily was the first person he seriously considered.[2]
Thomas considered her appealing because she was:
- intelligent;
- talented;
- powerful;
- politically important;
- and capable of standing beside a high-ranking Werewolf Prince.
He eventually admitted his interest to Tasha.
Tasha initially opposed the idea because Emily was Human and a Witch.[2]
Emily has not yet directly reciprocated Thomas' romantic feelings.

#### Fenrir
[[Fenrir]] is Thomas' supernatural patron.
Fenrir personally recognized Thomas' potential and Blessed him while he was still a child.[1]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later confirmed that Thomas was also among the limited number of individuals toward whom Fenrir possessed a genuine sense of companionship.[9]

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Thomas' rescuer and later stepfather.
Victor participated in recovering Thomas from [[Diablo]]'s forces and later became involved in the transformation of Samar's political structure.
He also helped clarify the true nature of [[Fenrir]]'s relationship with Thomas and the Werewolf Kingdom.
After marrying Tasha through a [[Soul Marriage]], Victor became Thomas' stepfather.[10]

### Current Status
Thomas remains alive and retains his position as the Third Prince of Samar.
Following Tasha's rise as Werewolf Progenitor, he is no longer expected to dedicate his entire existence toward eventually replacing Volk as King.[2]
He remains required to continue his development as:
- an Alpha;
- a Prince;
- a future pack leader;
- and a member of Samar's Royal Family.
At the same time, Thomas has been given considerably greater freedom to decide what he personally wants from his future.
His developing interest in [[Emily Moriarty]] represents one of the clearest examples of Thomas beginning to make decisions based upon his own preferences rather than solely upon Royal expectations.
