### Physical Appearance
Yuran is a dark-skinned man with hair described as black as night and equally black eyes.[1]
During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later reunion with Anderson's group, Yuran is described as having chocolate-colored skin, black hair and black eyes.[2]
The novel has not provided his exact height, clothing, age or transformed Werewolf appearance.

### Personality
Yuran is generally calm, reserved and straightforward. During the Vampire tournament, while Liza, Julian and Juan reacted strongly to [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]]' presence, Yuran remained comparatively composed and simply stated that he personally found [[Volk Fenrir]] more frightening.[1]
His answer reflects the perspective of a Werewolf raised beneath Samar's Royal Family. To Yuran, Volk was the ruler whose strength and authority directly affected his own species, making the [[Lycaon|Werewolf King]] more intimidating than even a legendary Vampire monarch.[1]
Yuran is also loyal to Anderson despite technically belonging to Tasha's pack. When Anderson later jokingly warns [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s group that Yuran is one of his mother's Betas and therefore cannot always be treated as Anderson's personal responsibility, Yuran immediately protests that he is still faithful to Anderson—most of the time.[2]
This interaction suggests that Yuran is comfortable enough with Anderson to argue with him openly while still respecting him as a Prince and commander.

### Synopsis

#### A New Pillar
Yuran's first confirmed named appearance occurred during the tournament surrounding the succession dispute between [[Annasthashia Elderblood Fulger]] and [[Niklaus Horseman]]. Anderson attended the event alongside Liza, Julian, Juan and Yuran.[1]
While observing [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]], Liza called the Vampire King a monster, with Julian and Juan silently agreeing. Anderson noticed Yuran had not reacted and directly asked for his opinion.[1]
Yuran calmly replied:
"To me, your father is scarier."[1]
Anderson pointed out that Yuran naturally felt that way because he was a Werewolf. He then observed that both Vlad and Volk possessed a similar quality: their own people deeply respected them but were also afraid of them. Yuran and the other subordinates silently agreed with his assessment.[1]
Yuran remained with Anderson's group throughout this period but did not receive an individual combat scene.

#### King Among Kings
Yuran appeared again many years later when Anderson met [[Victor Elderblood]], [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]], [[Natalia Elderblood née Alioth]] and [[Anna Elderblood]] before their journey to Samar.[2]
Anderson formally reintroduced his companions and clarified their individual roles. Julian specialized in raw physical strength, Juan specialized in speed and tracking, Liza was Anderson's most trusted subordinate and superior tracker, while Yuran occupied a somewhat unusual position within the group.[2]
Anderson introduced him as:
"This idiot here is Yuran, and as you can tell by his appearance, he's one of my mother's Betas, so be careful what you say in front of him."[2]
Yuran immediately protested Anderson's warning, insisting that despite belonging to Tasha's pack, he remained loyal to Anderson. The narration clarified that Yuran was a member of the Queen's pack, described as a group of highly loyal operatives. Consequently, if Tasha ever issued an order conflicting with Anderson's wishes, Yuran's ultimate loyalty would remain with the Queen.[2]
When Victor introduced Natalia by her Clan name, Yuran reacted with immediate surprise upon hearing Alioth. He recognized [[Clan Alioth]] as one of the families involved in [[Nightingale]]'s founding and was clearly familiar with its historical importance.[2]
Later, once Anderson formally accepted Victor as his guest on Samar, Yuran personally approached Victor carrying the golden guest identification token. The token bore the image of a roaring Wolf and contained supernatural Energy used to authenticate its legitimacy.[2]
Yuran handed the token to Victor and explained that he should keep it with him while travelling through Samar. Any Werewolf questioning Victor's presence could identify him as an officially protected guest of the Second Prince through the token.[2]
Yuran then accompanied Anderson's group through the portal opened by Liza for their journey to Samar.

### Skills & Abilities
Yuran has not received an individual battle or demonstrated a unique supernatural technique. His confirmed abilities are primarily those of a Beta Werewolf and his position within Tasha's pack.

#### Beta Werewolf Physiology
- Werewolf Physiology: As a Beta Werewolf, Yuran possesses the supernatural physical characteristics inherent to his Race.
- Superhuman Strength: [[Werewolves]] naturally possess physical strength far exceeding ordinary Humans.
- Superhuman Speed: Yuran possesses enhanced movement speed and reflexes.
- Superhuman Durability: His Werewolf physiology grants significantly greater physical resilience than a Human body.
- Enhanced Stamina: Werewolves possess high levels of endurance and can sustain intense physical activity for extended periods.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Yuran possesses the accelerated healing characteristic of Werewolves.
- Enhanced Senses: His Race possesses heightened smell, hearing and predatory perception.
- Longevity: Werewolves possess significantly extended lifespans compared with ordinary Humans.
- Werewolf Transformation: Yuran possesses the racial capacity to transform and manifest more pronounced Werewolf characteristics, although his individual transformed form has not been shown.
- Natural Weapons: Werewolf transformations provide enhanced claws and fangs.
- Predatory Instincts: Yuran possesses the instinctive awareness of danger and supernatural hierarchy common among Werewolves.

#### Royal Pack Membership
- Beta Bond: Yuran is one of [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]]'s Betas and therefore belongs directly to the Werewolf Queen's pack.[2]
- Royal Loyalty: His ultimate allegiance belongs to Tasha even while he operates under Anderson's command.[2]
- Royal Operative Training: Yuran's inclusion among Tasha's personal Betas and Anderson's travelling group establishes him as a trusted operative, although the novel has not revealed his individual specialization.[2]

#### Knowledge
- Supernatural Society Knowledge: Yuran immediately recognized the importance of the Alioth name and knew of the Clan's historical role in the foundation of [[Nightingale]].[2]
- Samar Protocol: Yuran was responsible for presenting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] with the official guest token used to identify protected foreign visitors on Samar.[2]
Yuran has not been confirmed to possess:
- Alpha Werewolf physiology.
- True Werewolf physiology.
- Nature Energy techniques.
- An individualized Element.
- Godhood or Divinity.
- A named combat technique.
- A specialized tracking ability comparable to Liza or Juan.
- Julian's specialization in physical strength.

### Relationships

#### Tasha Fenrir Elderblood
[[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]] is Yuran's Alpha and the leader of the pack to which he truly belongs.[2]
Although Yuran frequently follows Anderson, the narration clarifies that his primary supernatural loyalty is to Tasha. Anderson therefore warns others that actions taken by Yuran under the Queen's orders should not necessarily be attributed to him.[2]

#### Anderson
Anderson is Yuran's Prince and regular field commander.
Yuran accompanies Anderson alongside Liza, Julian and Juan during multiple appearances and clearly considers himself one of Anderson's loyal subordinates despite technically belonging to Tasha's pack.[1][2]
Their interaction is informal enough that Anderson openly calls Yuran an idiot while introducing him, causing Yuran to immediately complain.[2]

#### Liza, Julian and Juan
Liza, Julian and Juan are Yuran's fellow members of Anderson's group.
The four regularly accompany Anderson during missions and important supernatural events. During the Vampire tournament, they collectively discussed the intimidating presence of Vlad and Volk, while years later Anderson individually explained each subordinate's role to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s group.[1][2]
