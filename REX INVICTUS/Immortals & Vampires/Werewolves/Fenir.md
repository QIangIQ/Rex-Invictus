Fenir, also spelled Fanir in numerous later chapters, is an Alpha Werewolf and [[Demigod]] from the Werewolf kingdom of Samar. He is the eldest son of the former Werewolf monarchs [[Volk Fenrir]] and [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]], and the elder brother of Anderson and [[Thomas Fenrir]]. Once the First Prince of the [[Werewolves]], he was an influential political figure whose impatience with his father's continued rule ultimately led him to join [[New Dawn]], a clandestine cross-racial organization seeking to remove [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] Alucard. Fenir was part of New Dawn's original three-person nucleus alongside [[Niklaus Horseman]] and General James, and subsequently occupied Seat No. 2 on its seven-member council as the representative of the Werewolves.[1][2][3]
Although Fenir wanted to succeed Volk as King, his actions extended beyond the ordinary succession struggles permitted by Werewolf society. To obtain outside assistance, he gave New Dawn an avenue of influence over Samar and its people, provoking the anger of both parents. His faction was dismantled, he was captured when Victor Elderblood interrupted an attempted escape, and Tasha placed him in the royal dungeon following an interrogation. His last individually confirmed scene establishes him as alive but imprisoned; a later release, execution, reconciliation or restoration of his royal position has not been established in the chapters verified for this page.[4][3]

### Physical Appearance
Fenir is a tall, dark-skinned man with light-black hair marked by dark-blue undertones. He closely resembles his father, [[Volk Fenrir]], but does not share Volk's extraordinary bulk, disproportionately large physique or wild outward manner. At the [[Supernatural Gathering]], Fenir and Anderson were described as comparatively more civilized versions of their father, wearing conventional clothing while Volk appeared in animal skins. His manner and bearing suited the public role of a royal prince, although neither an exact height nor a detailed description of his ordinary outfit was given.[1]
His dark-blue eye color is recorded in the character profile, but a precise eye-color description was not independently established in the reviewed debut passage. His full Werewolf transformation has likewise not been individually described. The illustration on this page is an artistic interpretation rather than a confirmed depiction of every physical detail.

### Personality
Fenir is proud, ambitious, intelligent and politically minded, but also impatient, resentful and increasingly consumed by the belief that his rightful future has been denied him. As the eldest son of the monarchs, he considered the continuation of his father's reign an injustice. He saw little purpose in a hereditary monarchy if the reigning King could indefinitely retain authority without offering his descendants a realistic opportunity to succeed him. His resentment was not directed solely at his family: he considered Samar's political and cultural institutions stagnant, criticizing the older generation for preserving incompatible traditions and resisting meaningful reform. From his perspective, the kingdom possessed tremendous potential but suffered under rulers unwilling to let younger [[Werewolves]] shape its future.[4]
Fenir's discontent was rooted partly in his upbringing. Unlike Thomas, whose extraordinary potential attracted the attention of the Beast of Ragnarok, Fenir and Anderson grew up without the same parental expectations or recognition. By the time [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] visited Samar, Fenir appeared to have stopped seeking his parents' approval, choosing to pursue his own interests instead. However, his later interrogation revealed that resentment over the unequal attention had never entirely disappeared. He bitterly regarded Thomas' privileged standing and the right to bear the Beast's name as evidence that the youngest brother was respected more than the eldest prince.[5][4]
His outward conduct could be dismissive and insulting. During the [[Supernatural Gathering]], he rebuked Anderson for joking about their unimpressive introduction. Later he openly disapproved of Anderson's friendship with a Vampire and disparaged his brother in front of Victor. Nevertheless, Fenir was not merely an ineffectual or spoiled prince. Tasha acknowledged that he was competent and regularly helped her administer Samar, even though he was not among her most naturally gifted children. His presence among [[New Dawn]]'s original organizers and his ability to maintain a faction in Samar further demonstrate political initiative, networking ability and a willingness to operate secretly.[1][6][4][3]
Fenir was sufficiently calculating to recognize dangers greater than his own abilities. When New Dawn discussed assassinating Victor, he judged a direct confrontation suicidal and supported setting a trap instead. His skepticism toward reports of Victor's extraordinary growth was accompanied by an attempt to explain that rise through hidden intervention rather than pure coincidence. He could be envious, especially regarding Victor's looks, power and rapidly expanding influence, but envy did not make him forget the practical necessity of preparation.[7]
His greatest failing was the gap between his professed loyalty to Samar and the consequences of his ambitions. He wanted to become King and modernize Werewolf society, yet accepted assistance from an organization that stood to gain control over Samar's affairs. The question of how fully he understood that arrangement remains more complicated than his parents initially assumed. Adam Lykos, who knew the royal children well, suspected that New Dawn might have manipulated Fenir after securing his cooperation; he believed Fenir would not knowingly surrender the Werewolves to outsiders. This is an in-story suspicion, not proof that Fenir was magically controlled before his capture. His ambitions, established participation and disastrous political choices remain confirmed.[3]

### Background
Fenir was born to [[Volk Fenrir]] and [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]], the leading figures of Samar's Werewolf monarchy. His biological father was an exceptionally powerful Alpha whose authority rested heavily upon personal strength and the traditions of Werewolf rule; his mother was both a Goddess and an exceptionally capable political administrator. As their eldest son, Fenir held the title of First Prince, but birth order did not automatically guarantee succession in a society that prioritized strength and the capacity to command. While his youngest brother Thomas received recognition from the legendary Beast of Ragnarok, Fenir developed into a more conventional political operator, assisting Tasha and establishing his own influence within the kingdom.[8][5][4]
A significant distinction in Fenir's political circumstances is that Volk did not oppose succession challenges in principle. According to the narration, competition for the throne was considered a natural means of progress, and a son seeking to supplant his father was not automatically a traitor. What crossed the line was obtaining support from a concealed foreign organization in return for letting it exploit Werewolf resources and influence. Fenir's actions created an issue of national sovereignty, not simply a quarrel about inheritance.[4]
Before his eventual exposure, Fenir had become associated with [[Niklaus Horseman]] and General James. Together they formed the early nucleus of the organization later known as [[New Dawn]]. His involvement predated the open collapse of the Demonic alliance and ultimately drew him into dealings with [[Baal]], [[Diablo]] and other supernatural powers, bringing external conspiracies directly into Samar's internal politics.[6][3]

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Fenir was mentioned before his on-page debut when [[Aphrodite]] identified the powerful guests expected at the [[Supernatural Gathering]], explaining that Volk would attend with his sons Fenir and Anderson. The scene established Fenir as part of Samar's royal delegation before readers saw him directly.[9]
He appeared in person when Volk arrived at the gathering. The King was given an imposing announcement emphasizing his strength and blessing from the Beast of Ragnarok, while Fenir and Anderson were presented merely as his first and second sons. Volk privately disapproved of this comparatively unremarkable introduction, recognizing that his older sons lacked publicly celebrated accomplishments. Anderson joked that they had been overlooked, but Fenir reprimanded him for embarrassing the family. Their appearance also contrasted with that of their father: both sons resembled him physically but wore ordinary clothing and projected a more controlled, civilized image.[1]
The differences between the brothers became apparent when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] approached Anderson at the gathering. Anderson greeted him warmly, having developed respect and friendship through combat, while Fenir watched with concealed hostility. Unlike Volk and Anderson, Fenir was already familiar with Victor as the enemy of his organization. He had received information from Niklaus and knew that [[Werewolves]] he had provided to the conspirator had been killed by Victor. Anderson formally introduced the two, but Fenir responded by criticizing his brother for associating with Vampires and treating him as a disappointment to the family. Victor and Anderson mocked his attitude and largely ignored him, although Victor noticed that Fenir's animosity was strange for someone he had apparently never met before.[6]
This encounter is particularly important because it demonstrates that Fenir's participation in the conspiracy was not a sudden development after Victor's rise to the position of Demon King. He was already linked to Niklaus and had supplied Werewolf personnel to the latter before his treason was exposed. The exact identities and number of those Wolves are not specified in this scene and should not be invented.[6]
Fenir later attended a secret council meeting organized by Niklaus with General James and [[Baal]]. Their immediate purpose was to plan Victor's assassination. The organization considered Victor dangerously unpredictable, exceptionally fast-growing and likely to obstruct its future plans. James presented information documenting Victor's Human origins, his rare blood and his transition from a sick young man into the Second Vampire Progenitor. Fenir questioned whether such a chain of events could truly be accidental and suspected the possibility of a hidden group influencing Victor's development. The revelations also underscored that Victor had already become too powerful to confront without planning.[7]
Despite his hostility, Fenir argued against attacking Victor directly. He recommended preparation and supported the notion of a trap, remembering that Victor had survived conflicts involving formidable Demons such as [[Agares]]. His envy of Victor's appearance was noted alongside his practical understanding of the threat. At the meeting, Fenir functioned as an active conspirator rather than an uninformed royal observer, while Niklaus and James coordinated the effort to eliminate Victor.[7]

#### King Among Kings
After returning from his extended stay in Hell, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] examined memories taken from [[Diablo]] and his associates. These revealed connections between previously separate opponents and pointed again toward [[New Dawn]], with Niklaus, General James and Fenir among the recognizable participants. Fenir's earlier hostility thus became part of a larger network of threats rather than an isolated Werewolf dispute.[10]
When Victor subsequently arrived on Samar, he learned that the First Prince still maintained contact with New Dawn and was attempting to obtain its assistance in taking the throne. Victor's knowledge of Diablo's memories gave him a clearer understanding of the danger posed by that alliance and an opportunity to exploit the political division within the royal household.[11]
During Victor's visit, the tensions within the [[Fenrir]] family became increasingly visible. As the narration later clarified, Thomas had grown up bearing extraordinary expectations as the Beast of Ragnarok's favored heir, while Fenir and Anderson had spent years without comparable parental recognition. Fenir had outwardly abandoned the pursuit of their praise and was focused on his own political affairs. That apparent indifference hid a much older frustration which would emerge under Victor's mental influence.[5][4]
Several days after Victor arrived, Fenir's conspiracy was uncovered and his political infrastructure collapsed. Tasha and Volk jointly destroyed his possessions and dismantled his entire faction, severely damaging New Dawn's established influence over Samar. Volk was prepared to execute him for offering Werewolf sovereignty to outsiders. Tasha intervened, arguing that their son was the principal source of information about the organization and that she herself would decide his punishment. Fenir feared his mother's intentions enough to regard a quick death at Volk's hands as potentially preferable.[4]
The disagreement nearly provoked a violent confrontation between Samar's monarchs. Volk demanded obedience and began transforming under an oppressive green aura, while Tasha answered with the golden-tinged power associated with her own Divine nature. As both focused upon their struggle, Fenir attempted to slip away. Victor, who had sensed the sudden eruption of power, interrupted the incident and froze half of Fenir's body, stopping the escape without killing him.[4]
Fenir immediately accused Victor of ruining his schemes, pointing to Victor's interference in [[Nightingale]], Diablo's affairs and now Samar. Rather than retaliating through direct violence, Victor subjected him to an unusually potent form of Vampiric Charm. Fenir lost the normal ability to conceal his thoughts and began expressing deeply suppressed grievances about his father, mother, youngest brother and the political condition of the Werewolf kingdom. He called Volk incompetent and tyrannical, complained that the throne would never be passed to his descendants, condemned the old generation's refusal to modernize, and accused Tasha of favoring Thomas because the Beast of Ragnarok had recognized his potential. The combination of magical compulsion and emotional release briefly reduced Fenir to tears.[4]
The interrogation also revealed a more revealing contradiction. Fenir believed Tasha would be a better ruler than Volk, yet resented her role in the family. He admitted that he had feared Victor and, had he understood him earlier, might have sought Victor's support instead of opposing him. He even suggested that Victor could have become one of New Dawn's leaders, helping the organization confront an unspecified future threat. When Victor demanded to know what that threat was, Fenir could not reveal it: a powerful Privacy Spell prevented disclosure. Victor compared the mechanism to curses used in infernal contracts and found that even his Draconic Eyes could not detect its workings. He suspected the involvement of beings at the level of [[Primordial Gods]], but their precise identities were not established by this observation.[4]
Tasha conducted the substantive political questioning while Victor maintained Fenir's compliance. The investigation established that New Dawn was ruled by a seven-member council, with representatives drawn from different [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] and communities. Fenir was Seat No. 2, representing the [[Werewolves]]. The council's numbered seats were not a ranking from strongest to weakest or commander to subordinate: all seven representatives were formally equal within its stated structure. The privacy restriction still prevented Fenir from giving away the organization's most carefully protected secrets.[2]
From Fenir's testimony and the information already held by Victor, the group reconstructed New Dawn's development. Its original membership consisted of Niklaus, General James and Fenir, whose conflicts with Victor encouraged them to look for stronger allies. Contacts with [[Baal]] and Diablo connected them to the Demonic Army and its broader infernal network. Following the collapse of that alliance during the Demonic War, New Dawn sought further assistance from other powerful supernatural groups against the rapidly ascending Victor.[3]
The revelations left the Werewolf leadership divided over how to interpret Fenir's betrayal. Maya and Tasha considered his conduct remarkably foolish given his intelligence and political competence. Adam, however, pointed out that the presence of a powerful information-suppression spell suggested that New Dawn possessed the resources to manipulate Fenir. Based on his knowledge of the royal children, he suspected Fenir originally intended to use the organization to obtain the throne and then abandon it, without realizing that a faction powerful enough to make him King would never willingly release such a valuable asset. This remained a theory; the scene did not prove that a separate mind-control spell had forced him to betray Samar.[3]
Victor stated that he could break the Privacy Spell, but doing so would require killing Fenir. He refused out of consideration for Tasha, correctly understanding that she intended to punish her son but could not bring herself to execute him. After Victor ended the magical compulsion, Fenir awakened confused and still partly immobilized by ice. Anderson left to investigate whether traitors had entered his own faction, while Tasha ordered her Betas to take Fenir to the dungeon and obtain the names of everyone with whom he had been involved. That imprisonment is Fenir's last individually verified disposition in this storyline.[3]

### Skills & Abilities
Fenir's confirmed abilities and competencies are primarily matters of racial heritage, position and political conduct, not demonstrated Divine techniques. Although his royal and Divine ancestry should not be disregarded, he has not received a substantial individual battle in the verified chapters. Powers displayed by Volk, Tasha, Thomas or the Beast of Ragnarok should not automatically be attributed to him.

#### Racial and Divine Heritage
- Werewolf Physiology: Fenir is a member of the Werewolf Royal Family and an Alpha. He belongs to a species possessing supernatural physical characteristics, predatory senses and transformative traits. Their general racial capabilities provide context for his heritage; the novel has not individually measured Fenir's physical attributes.[1]
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics: Werewolf physiology entails physical capabilities beyond those of ordinary Humans, but no numerical strength, speed, stamina or durability level has been established specifically for Fenir.[1]
- Enhanced Senses (Racial trait): Fenir shares the Werewolf species' sensory heritage. There is no distinct scene establishing a unique sensory technique, detection range or resistance belonging to him.[1]
- Regeneration (Racial trait; unquantified): [[Werewolves]] possess supernatural physical recovery, but Fenir has no individually documented high-level healing feat in the indexed scenes.[1]
- Transformation (Racial capacity; personal form unrevealed): He is an Alpha Werewolf, although his personal Beast Form, Hybrid Form and potential transformations have not been depicted or described in sufficient detail to index their features or powers.[1]
- [[Demigod]] Physiology: Fenir is the son of the Goddess [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]] and the Werewolf [[Volk Fenrir]], giving him both Divine and Werewolf ancestry. No unique Divinity, Divine Form or personal Divine spell has yet been attributed to him in the examined chapters.[4]
- Royal Werewolf Bloodline: Fenir is a direct descendant of the reigning royal couple, and his status as First Prince provided political legitimacy and access to powerful Werewolf circles. Birth order alone, however, did not establish him as Samar's inevitable successor.[1][4]

#### Leadership and Non-Combat Skills
- Political Administration: Tasha considered Fenir capable and relied on him for administrative assistance despite judging his natural talent inferior to that of some other children.[4]
- Political Influence: Fenir built and maintained a personal faction on Samar substantial enough that Tasha and Volk had to dismantle it to eliminate [[New Dawn]]'s leverage in the kingdom.[4]
- Conspiratorial Networking: He was among New Dawn's original organizers and developed connections with former Vampire, Human Inquisition and Demonic figures, eventually representing the [[Werewolves]] on its ruling council.[6][3]
- Political Scheming: Fenir pursued a plan to replace Volk with foreign support. The strategy reveals initiative but also serious misjudgment of the risks to Samar's independence.[11][4]
- Military Resource Access: Fenir supplied Niklaus with Werewolves before their loss at [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s hands, indicating access to followers or forces of his own. Their exact numbers and relative power were not specified.[6]
- Tactical Assessment: He identified the danger of an open battle with Victor and advocated a planned trap against him during the New Dawn meeting.[7]
- Political Analysis: Fenir criticized Samar's fragmented historical institutions and the unwillingness of entrenched rulers to permit reform. These ideas represent his stated judgment, not confirmation that all his proposed reforms would have been successful.[4]

#### Limitations and Unknown Abilities
- Vampiric Charm Susceptibility: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s enhanced Charm overrode Fenir's resistance and compelled him to speak openly. This is a demonstrated vulnerability under a vastly more powerful supernatural ability, not proof that all forms of mind control would affect Fenir equally.[4][2]
- Privacy Spell (Imposed restriction, not a personal ability): A powerful spell associated with [[New Dawn]] prevented Fenir from disclosing protected information, even while controlled by Victor. Its origin and the caster's identity remain unspecified in the reviewed scene.[4][2]
- Unrevealed Combat Techniques: The verified chapters do not establish named attacks, martial arts, magical spells, individual Divine Concepts, a unique [[Fenrir]] Blessing or a confirmed personal combat tier. His brother Thomas' recognition by the Beast of Ragnarok must not be applied to Fenir.[5][4]

### Relationships

#### Volk Fenrir
[[Volk Fenrir]] is Fenir's biological father and the principal target of his political resentment. Fenir believes Volk possesses overwhelming strength but insufficient rationality and administrative judgment to govern a modern society. Above all, he resents his father's refusal to retire or create a path for the elder children to succeed him. Volk, conversely, considered Fenir's acceptance of external influence an intolerable betrayal and was prepared to execute him. Their conflict exposes the distinction between acceptable rivalry for the crown and selling the country's independence for foreign support.[4][2]

#### Tasha Fenrir Elderblood
Tasha is Fenir's mother and a Goddess who helped govern Samar. Fenir recognizes her political competence and privately considers her better qualified to rule than Volk. He nevertheless believes she overlooked her older sons and favored Thomas. Tasha considered Fenir a capable helper whose impatience had led him into a disastrous conspiracy. Despite her anger and disappointment, she protected him from Volk's intended execution, supervised the investigation of [[New Dawn]] and ultimately ordered his imprisonment rather than his death.[4][2][3]

#### Anderson
Anderson, the second prince, is Fenir's younger brother. Fenir treats him with condescension, particularly after Anderson befriends [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and openly disregards his elder brother's hostility toward Vampires. During the [[Supernatural Gathering]], Anderson mocked Fenir's stiff behavior and then introduced him to Victor despite knowing their personalities would clash. Later, after Fenir's betrayal, Anderson treated his brother as a fool and became concerned that [[New Dawn]] might have infiltrated his own supporters as well. The narrative also shows that both brothers suffered from a relative lack of parental recognition while growing up.[1][6][5][3]

#### Thomas Fenrir
[[Thomas Fenrir]] is Fenir's youngest brother. Thomas received the attention of the legendary Beast of Ragnarok and was permitted to bear its name, becoming the most promising royal successor despite his youth. Fenir considered the status and respect granted to Thomas unfair and interpreted his parents' treatment of their youngest son as an insult to the older princes. The narration in Chapter 743 qualifies this rivalry: Thomas also endured enormous parental expectations, even if Fenir perceived him primarily as the favored child.[5][4]

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] was the target of Fenir's early conspiracy and the man responsible for exposing the political weakness of [[New Dawn]]'s foothold on Samar. Fenir had already supplied followers to Niklaus and learned they were killed by Victor before personally encountering him at the [[Supernatural Gathering]]. His resentment deepened as Victor's influence expanded and culminated in the failed attempt to escape royal custody. Under Victor's Charm, however, Fenir admitted he might have approached Victor for help against Volk if he had not been so frightened of him. Victor later spared Fenir because forcibly breaching the Privacy Spell would kill him and Tasha did not want her son dead. Victor subsequently became Fenir's stepfather through his relationship with Tasha, but no personal reconciliation between the two men is established in the reviewed material.[6][7][4][3]

#### Niklaus Horseman and General James
[[Niklaus Horseman]] and General James were Fenir's earliest known collaborators in [[New Dawn]]. Niklaus supplied Fenir with intelligence concerning [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], received Werewolf personnel from him and subsequently led assassination planning. James contributed research, intelligence and logistical abilities acquired through his former position in the Inquisition. The three were specifically identified as the original nucleus of New Dawn before the organization sought wider Demonic and Divine connections.[6][7][3]

### Current Status
Fenir was alive and detained in Samar in his last directly confirmed scene, when Tasha instructed her subordinates to imprison him and investigate all his past contacts. He had lost his independent political faction and its [[New Dawn]] connections had been compromised. The verified chapter material does not establish a subsequent trial, a formal sentence, liberation, restoration to the throne's succession line, death or further political role. Imprisoned therefore describes his last confirmed disposition rather than an independently verified permanent condition.[3]

### Trivia
- Fenir and Fanir are two spellings used for the same character. Chapter 509 uses Fenir, while Chapters 513 and 761–762 use Fanir in their narrative passages.[1][6][2]
- His name was mentioned in Chapter 397 before he appeared at the [[Supernatural Gathering]] in Chapter 509.[9][1]
- In Chapter 513, Fenir was already associated with Niklaus and had supplied him with Werewolf personnel. His participation therefore preceded the meeting of conspirators in Chapter 595.[6][7]
- He belonged to [[New Dawn]]'s original nucleus with [[Niklaus Horseman]] and General James. [[Baal]] and [[Diablo]] were drawn into the group's broader alliances afterward.[3]
- Seat No. 2 was Fenir's New Dawn council position, but the seven seats were officially equal; the number did not automatically denote second-in-command or second-strongest.[2]
- Volk did not oppose Fenir's pursuit of the throne in principle. The treason lay in exposing Samar and its [[Werewolves]] to foreign manipulation.[4]
- Fenir once believed Tasha would make a better supreme Alpha than Volk, even though he resented her treatment of the older princes.[2]
- He was no longer actively seeking his parents' approval by Chapter 743, but Chapter 760 revealed that the childhood resentment remained deeply rooted.[5][4]
- The powerful spell suppressing Fenir's ability to reveal New Dawn's secrets was not shown to be a technique he personally cast.[4]
- Adam suspected that New Dawn might have manipulated Fenir or exploited his plans to abandon the organization after becoming King, but the suspicion was never confirmed in the verified interrogation scene.[3]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] did not break the Privacy Spell because doing so would have required Fenir's death, an outcome he knew Tasha could not accept.[3]
- Fenir's personal Werewolf transformation, individual Divine powers and named combat techniques have not been revealed in the reviewed chapter material.
