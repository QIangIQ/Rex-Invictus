Volk Fenrir is the former King of the [[Werewolves]], former supreme Alpha of Samar, and the ex-husband of [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]]. A descendant of the ancient Werewolf lineage originating from Licael, the First Werewolf Progenitor, Volk was one of the most powerful members of his Race before his downfall. His natural abilities as a True Alpha, the enormous strength accumulated through his position at the head of Samar's Werewolf hierarchy, and a personal Blessing granted by [[Fenrir]], the Beast of Ragnarok, collectively established him as a formidable supernatural monarch. His formal introduction recognized him as the Strongest Alpha, the [[Lycaon|Werewolf King]], and The One Blessed by the Beast of Ragnarok.[4][5]
Unlike the Vampire Progenitor [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]], Volk was not himself the original Source of his Race. His extraordinary status arose from his bloodline, personal strength, authority over the Werewolf packs, and Fenrir's favor. Nevertheless, Volk possessed the qualifications necessary to become a new Werewolf Progenitor. His wife Tasha independently fulfilled the same requirements, producing an unusual situation in which two candidates were simultaneously eligible for the position. Neither was selected until their final confrontation established the superior candidate, allowing Tasha to ascend as the new Progenitor and acquire authority over the entire Werewolf Race.[6]
Volk's reign was built around the traditional Werewolf values of strength, courage, individual pride, hierarchy, and the right of a stronger Alpha to challenge an existing ruler. He was recognized as a powerful warrior and legitimate monarch even by individuals who criticized his behavior, including [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] and Vlad. His greatest political advantage, however, was inseparable from his marriage. While Volk provided the overwhelming military authority needed to govern a warrior society, Tasha possessed the intelligence, diplomatic ability, organizational experience, and willingness to operate through covert methods that kept Samar functioning as a stable nation. Their differences created a government divided between the visible authority of the King and the more secretive political apparatus maintained by the Queen.[7][8]
This arrangement eventually collapsed when Volk became increasingly hostile toward Tasha's independence and her growing association with [[Victor Elderblood]]. His jealousy and fear of losing authority led him to remove Tasha from the position of Queen and activate divorce documents secretly prepared at the beginning of their marriage. The betrayal ended their political partnership and drove Tasha into a confrontation in which Volk lost not merely his throne but the supernatural foundations of his identity.[9][2]
Following his defeat, Tasha forcibly reclaimed the Werewolf Power Volk had accumulated, removed his Alpha status, and expelled him from the Pack hierarchy through her Authority as Progenitor. The severing of his connection to the Source of the Werewolf Race damaged his Soul and left him weaker than an ordinary Human child. Fenrir subsequently withdrew the Blessing that had previously distinguished him from other True Alphas. Volk survived the ordeal but was reduced to an Omega, deprived of his former supernatural strength, pack, political standing, and ability to command the Werewolf Race.[1]

### Physical Appearance
At the height of his power, Volk was an enormous dark-skinned man standing approximately 220 centimeters tall. His body was extraordinarily muscular, possessing unusually broad shoulders, powerful arms, a massive chest, and a physique considerably larger than that of an ordinary Human. His proportions emphasized the overwhelming physical development associated with his True Alpha nature, creating an appearance more reminiscent of a wild predator than a conventional monarch.[4]
Volk possessed long, light-black hair containing noticeable shades of blue, extending approximately to his waist. He generally wore his hair loose and untamed, complementing his rugged appearance. His eyes were a striking bright blue, possessing an intensity that made his gaze uncomfortable for weaker individuals to endure. A small scar was visible on the left side of his chin, adding to the impression of a warrior accustomed to physical confrontation.[4]
His clothing reflected the primitive aesthetic associated with the strongest traditional Werewolf Alphas. Rather than the elaborate uniforms, ceremonial robes, and luxurious garments worn by many supernatural rulers, Volk preferred clothing fashioned from the hides of different animals. During the Third [[Supernatural Gathering]], he appeared barefoot, with his enormous body and animal-skin garments distinguishing him immediately from the more formally dressed representatives. Even his sons Fenir and Anderson presented comparatively civilized appearances despite their obvious physical resemblance to their father.[4]
His appearance changed dramatically following his defeat by Tasha. When she began reclaiming his accumulated Werewolf Power, Volk's body visibly withered, losing the extraordinary supernatural strength that had supported his enormous physique. The process was accompanied by the extraction of green Power, which gathered into a sphere in Tasha's hand. Although she deliberately prevented the procedure from killing him, the destruction of his Alpha status and the severe injury to his Soul left him physically and spiritually crippled.[1]
No detailed account establishes whether Volk subsequently recovered his former muscular appearance. His original physical description represents his condition as the reigning King and True Alpha, rather than a confirmed depiction of his weakened Omega state.

### Personality
Volk is a proud, aggressive, competitive, and strongly instinct-driven individual whose personality reflects the traditions of ancient Werewolf society. He values physical strength as the foundation of authority and considers the willingness to challenge a superior opponent an essential quality of a powerful Alpha. His worldview is governed by the belief that competition forces individuals and civilizations to develop, while excessive protection and the absence of meaningful challenges produce stagnation. This philosophy shaped his approach to leadership, relationships, and the upbringing of his children, making him a ruler who was far more comfortable commanding warriors than negotiating complicated political agreements.[7][10]
Despite his apparent savagery, Volk was not merely a mindless brute. He had ruled Samar for an extended period and possessed enough personal ability to remain at the top of a political system in which exceptionally powerful Alphas could openly challenge one another. Hilda recognized this distinction when she warned Violet that a man could not maintain such a position for so long through physical strength alone. Volk understood the practical importance of loyalty, military organization, political reputation, and recognizing threats that could endanger his Race. Although he frequently approached these matters more crudely than Tasha, his reign could not be reduced entirely to incompetence.[7]
One of his defining characteristics was an intense commitment to the Werewolf tradition of earning authority through strength. Volk did not believe that a ruler should remain forever unchallenged simply because he currently occupied the throne. When Fenir's ambition to replace him became known, Volk did not condemn the idea of succession through competition. He regarded such challenges as a natural part of Werewolf society and believed that a son sufficiently strong or capable to defeat his father could legitimately become King. What he found unacceptable was Fenir's decision to involve [[New Dawn]], an external organization whose assistance could place Samar beneath the influence of individuals who possessed no loyalty toward its people.[10]
Volk's relationship with his Pack was similarly complex. His authority depended upon his position as Alpha, but Werewolf society was not composed exclusively of warriors. Ordinary civilians, craftsmen, merchants, and less powerful members of the Race also belonged to the Pack whose existence sustained his strength. The King therefore depended upon the continued survival and prosperity of a much larger population than the elite warriors surrounding him. This connection gave his position a practical responsibility extending beyond the pursuit of battle, even if his personal temperament was rarely suited to ordinary administration.[5]
His greatest political weakness was his inability to appreciate the importance of methods that did not fit his preferred approach. Volk generally favored open confrontation, the assertion of authority, and direct demonstrations of strength, whereas Tasha relied upon preparation, deception, intelligence networks, diplomacy, and the manipulation of opposing interests. This contrast initially allowed them to compensate for one another's weaknesses, but it also produced growing resentment as Volk became increasingly uncomfortable with the influence his wife exercised independently of him. He mistook the ability to dominate an opponent directly for proof that he could similarly control more complicated political situations.[7][9][2]
Volk's pride also made apologizing exceptionally difficult. He ordinarily refused to submit to anyone or acknowledge that another person possessed sufficient authority to demand humility from him. However, he was capable of restraining this impulse when the survival of someone important required it. During his negotiations with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] concerning the illness of the Beast of Ragnarok, Volk recognized the difference in their strength and apologized for reacting aggressively to Victor's description of [[Fenrir]]. The gesture surprised the surrounding Alphas because apologizing was extraordinarily uncharacteristic of their King. Volk's willingness to do so demonstrated that he could subordinate his pride to protecting someone he considered family.[11]
This protective instinct extended toward his children. Although Volk was a severe father who believed that hardship and competition were necessary for growth, he retained moral boundaries concerning the deliberate sacrifice of innocent family members. When Vlad later suggested kidnapping [[Thomas Fenrir]] and using his life as leverage against Tasha, Volk immediately rejected the proposal. He refused to threaten a son who had done nothing wrong merely because the strategy might preserve his political position. The decision revealed a genuine paternal attachment that survived his increasing desperation and willingness to employ ruthless methods against political opponents.[12]
Volk's personal relationships were nevertheless marked by substantial hypocrisy. He maintained intimate relationships with numerous women throughout his marriage and fathered children outside his publicly recognized family. Several of those children were not formally acknowledged because he feared Tasha discovering the extent of his infidelity. Despite this history, he became increasingly possessive and jealous when Tasha developed a closer relationship with Victor. He interpreted her growing independence and private interactions with the Demon King as evidence of betrayal, even though Tasha denied having been sexually unfaithful before their divorce.[13][14]
His pride was particularly vulnerable when confronted with individuals who embodied the qualities he valued more successfully than himself. Victor possessed overwhelming physical strength, the respect of powerful supernatural warriors, an expanding network of allies, and a family whose members had chosen to remain with him despite the dangers involved. The Vampire's ability to command beings across racial boundaries increasingly exposed how limited Volk's own authority had become. What initially resembled admiration for another battle-loving ruler gradually transformed into insecurity, resentment, and fear.[15][16]
Volk could also become surprisingly dependent upon people he considered his equals. His long acquaintance with Vlad survived the historic rivalry between Vampires and [[Werewolves]], and he contacted the Vampire King for advice once his situation with Tasha became increasingly dangerous. Although Volk disliked being corrected, he eventually recognized that Vlad understood the political circumstances better than he did and accepted an uncomfortable explanation of his mistakes. His ability to seek advice demonstrated that even his considerable arrogance possessed limits when faced with the possibility of losing everything.[13][8][12]

### Background
Volk originated from an ancient Werewolf Alpha lineage descending from the original Progenitor of the Race, Licael. His family possessed a bloodline associated with exceptionally powerful Alphas and the older Werewolf traditions that emphasized fully embracing the predatory nature of their species. The original family eventually declined or disappeared, leaving Volk among the remaining inheritors of its ancient characteristics.[17][3]
As a True Werewolf, Volk belonged to the rare individuals who embraced their inner Wolf rather than suppressing it beneath a conventional Humanoid mentality. This condition increased his natural strength and reinforced the animalistic instincts influencing his behavior. Such development was associated with other exceptional [[Werewolves]], including [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]], the Ancient Alphas, and the ancestral members of the Race. The advantage came with a corresponding change in psychology, making individuals who followed this path more susceptible to predatory impulses and the behavior of intelligent animals.[17]
At some point before his introduction, Volk earned the recognition of [[Fenrir]], the Beast of Ragnarok. The legendary Wolf regarded Volk and Tasha favorably enough to Bless them personally and permit them to carry his name. Such recognition was extraordinary even among supernatural beings, since receiving a powerful entity's name and Blessing generally indicated a relationship extending beyond an ordinary temporary agreement. Fenrir's favor substantially elevated Volk's standing, while the Beast's presence on Samar provided the Werewolf nation with protection against enemies that its conventional armies could not reasonably defeat.[5][11]
Volk's natural physical strength, ancient lineage, and connection to Fenrir helped establish his authority over the Werewolf Race. However, the power of the Werewolf monarchy was not derived exclusively from the King himself. Every Werewolf belonged to the broader hierarchy of Packs, Alphas, and Betas, and the authority Volk exercised as King allowed him to benefit from the accumulated strength of the population under his command. The resulting amplification placed him considerably above ordinary Alphas, although the contribution of individual Pack members varied according to their own abilities and development.[5]
Volk eventually encountered Tasha, a former Egyptian Goddess who had lost her former position and required protection after leaving the [[Egyptian Pantheon]]. Despite their radically different personalities, the two entered a partnership that allowed each to benefit from the other's strengths. Volk possessed the natural Werewolf authority and military strength necessary to rule Samar, while Tasha possessed knowledge, political intelligence, administrative expertise, and experience surviving the dangerous politics of the Egyptian Gods. Their union allowed Volk's emerging monarchy to develop into a more stable and influential civilization.[2][14]
Even before their marriage was completed, Volk secretly prepared a means of separating from Tasha. During their first night together, he persuaded her to sign documents that could eventually be used to dissolve the marriage. Tasha trusted him sufficiently that she failed to recognize the significance of the agreement. Volk later justified his deception as a contingency against the possibility that his wife would eventually betray him or become impossible to control. His decision established a concealed foundation of distrust beneath a partnership that would otherwise endure for centuries.[2]
During their reign, Volk served as the visible King and supreme military authority of Samar. His reputation, physical strength, and status as the strongest Alpha helped maintain order among the various Werewolf clans, while Tasha handled much of the kingdom's diplomacy, foreign relationships, intelligence, and practical administration. The division of responsibility was significant enough that outsiders familiar with Samar recognized Tasha as the principal figure responsible for numerous aspects of its political stability.[7][8]
Their marriage produced three publicly recognized sons: Fenir, Anderson, and [[Thomas Fenrir]]. Volk also fathered additional children through other relationships, although the identities and precise number of these children have not been established. His children grew up beneath the expectations of a powerful warrior monarchy, where strength, succession, and recognition from the Beast of Ragnarok carried enormous social importance. Thomas eventually received special recognition from Fenrir, while the older brothers followed different paths shaped partly by their dissatisfaction with the family hierarchy.[18][13]
The partnership between Volk and Tasha remained politically effective despite their increasing emotional distance. Tasha maintained her own networks and supporters, while Volk continued to command the more public military structure and the masses who recognized him as King. Their separation into different centers of authority made the kingdom powerful when both cooperated, but also created a dangerous vulnerability once they became enemies.[8]

### Synopsis

#### A New Pillar
Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] Alucard's unexpected promotion to Vampire Count, news of the young Vampire's achievements spread throughout supernatural society. His rapid advancement, unusual behavior, and provocative decision to establish a Clan named Alucard attracted attention far beyond [[Nightingale]]. Among those interested was Volk, who regarded the emergence of another unusually strong and battle-loving individual as an opportunity rather than an immediate political threat. Upon learning that Vlad had recognized Victor as a Count, Volk enthusiastically ordered his son Anderson to investigate the newcomer. His reaction reflected the same instinct that made him seek powerful opponents and respect individuals willing to challenge established authorities.[19]
The assignment eventually brought Anderson into direct contact with Victor, beginning a friendship that would later complicate the relationship between the Vampire Progenitor and the Werewolf monarchy. Although the two [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] had historically been rivals, Volk did not consider the existence of an exceptionally strong Vampire sufficient reason to dismiss the possibility of a worthwhile encounter. His interest was primarily motivated by the potential strength and unusual personality of the newly established Count rather than by a carefully developed diplomatic plan.[19]

#### Demonic War
Volk made his physical introduction during the Third [[Supernatural Gathering]], arriving as one of the most influential rulers of supernatural society. His entrance was formally announced through the titles of Strongest Alpha, [[Lycaon|Werewolf King]], and the individual Blessed by the Beast of Ragnarok. He appeared accompanied by his sons Fenir and Anderson, whose relatively modest introductions contrasted sharply with the enormous reputation surrounding their father. Volk's imposing physique, wild clothing, and unmistakably predatory presence distinguished him immediately from the other representatives gathered for the meeting.[4]
His appearance prompted discussion among the Vampires observing the proceedings. Violet initially regarded him as little more than a powerful savage, but Hilda warned against assuming that someone capable of maintaining a kingdom for so long possessed no other qualities. Their discussion also revealed the importance of Tasha's role. Whereas Volk represented the strength and public authority of Samar, Tasha was the ruler responsible for the covert and political activities supporting the kingdom. She was similarly Blessed by [[Fenrir]], but her methods were almost the opposite of her husband's, allowing the two to form a government in which direct power and sophisticated manipulation operated together.[7]
Volk participated in the ensuing negotiations alongside the other supernatural rulers. He maintained the confrontational behavior expected from a Werewolf King, displaying little hesitation toward figures such as Vlad or representatives of powerful Pantheons. During discussions concerning the ownership of Divine Artifacts, he questioned proposals that would automatically recognize the claims of the Gods, particularly when those same Pantheons had historically taken resources or possessions from weaker groups. His objections reflected a worldview in which authority and ownership were ultimately enforced through power rather than guaranteed by universal principles.[20]
The discussion became increasingly contentious as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] questioned the hypocrisy of supernatural laws. When the possibility of Vlad acting without facing meaningful consequences was raised, Volk acknowledged that a ruler with sufficient strength could escape punishment simply because the people opposing him lacked the means to retaliate. Victor used the answer to expose the practical reality underlying much of supernatural diplomacy: rules were often upheld only when someone possessed enough power to enforce them. Volk's straightforward acknowledgment distinguished him from politicians who preferred concealing the same principle beneath elaborate arguments about legality and tradition.[21]
Although Victor had previously killed [[Werewolves]], Volk was not immediately driven into uncontrollable rage by the revelation. He was irritated by the young Vampire's provocations, particularly when Victor mocked his masculinity, but also recognized qualities he could respect. Victor's willingness to admit his actions openly and accept the consequences of confronting powerful enemies appealed to Volk's martial mentality. Their interaction contained the beginnings of mutual recognition, although the disparity between their political priorities would eventually destroy any possibility of a stable relationship.[21]
The arrival of [[Diablo]] and the revelation that the Demonic invasion had already begun forced the gathered rulers to reconsider their priorities. Volk rejected the suggestion that the conflict could be treated merely as a private war between Demons and Angels, recognizing that an invasion on such a scale threatened every supernatural faction. He was suspicious of Diablo's explanations and believed the Demonic leader possessed objectives beyond those publicly announced. Despite his preference for direct confrontation, Volk did not recklessly begin a battle inside the neutral meeting ground and instead continued assessing the threat.[22][23]
Volk's dealings with Evie Moriarty, the Witch Queen, also exposed the economic vulnerabilities of Samar. Many ordinary Werewolves depended upon magical talismans capable of reproducing the influence of a full Moon, allowing them to access transformations that would otherwise require specific lunar conditions. Powerful Alphas such as Volk and his sons did not share this dependence, but the talismans remained important to weaker Werewolves, especially Betas. The Witches controlled a substantial portion of this supply, giving Evie considerable leverage over the kingdom.[24]
During an exchange with Evie, Volk provoked the Witch Queen sufficiently that she threatened to triple the base price of the talismans supplied to Werewolves. Volk objected, recognizing the consequences such an increase could have upon the kingdom's resources, but Evie reminded him that her control over the product allowed her to determine its price. She offered to restore the previous arrangement if he apologized. His refusal demonstrated the extent to which his personal pride could interfere with matters affecting ordinary citizens, while the dispute illustrated why Tasha generally handled Samar's commercial and diplomatic relationships.[24]
The wider Demonic War placed further pressure upon Samar. Volk's strength and the protection provided by Fenrir made the kingdom difficult to threaten directly, but the Beast of Ragnarok eventually suffered from a mysterious condition that weakened him despite his extraordinary nature. The Werewolf Royal Family became increasingly concerned about the possibility of Demonic interference, internal betrayal, or an unknown supernatural illness. Fenrir was not merely a powerful ally; his continued existence was fundamental to the kingdom's defense against enemies that its ordinary army could not oppose.[18]
Volk remained one of the principal military authorities available to the Werewolf Race during the conflict, while Samar cooperated with other anti-Demon forces as the threat expanded. His personal strength was especially significant because the Werewolf hierarchy allowed the Alpha controlling a sufficiently large Pack to receive additional Power from the members beneath him. As the King of every Werewolf on Samar, Volk benefited from an enormous population, although the relatively small contribution of ordinary civilians prevented that relationship from making him infinitely powerful.[5][25]

#### King Among Kings
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s eventual journey to Samar brought the Demon King directly into the center of Werewolf political society. By this point, Victor had developed far beyond the Vampire Count Volk originally found interesting. He possessed the authority of a Vampire Progenitor, controlled vast Demonic forces, and had accumulated personal strength that the [[Lycaon|Werewolf King]] could no longer dismiss through traditional Alpha intimidation. Volk nevertheless initially approached the foreign ruler with the pride and possessiveness expected of someone accustomed to commanding an entire Race.[26][27]
Their relationship became increasingly tense because Victor had connections to Samar that extended beyond conventional diplomacy. He was a close friend of Anderson, the husband of [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]], and an individual whose influence had already attracted the attention of several important Werewolf families. Volk was not accustomed to a foreign ruler entering his territory while refusing to acknowledge the normal superiority of the reigning Alpha. Victor, however, had no intention of treating Samar's political traditions as restrictions upon his personal actions or the safety of his family.[27][28]
Volk demonstrated the unusual nature of his power when Victor and Maya became involved in an intense confrontation of Wills. The clash between the two supernatural predators produced enough pressure to overwhelm most of the surrounding [[Werewolves]], leaving only a handful of exceptional individuals capable of remaining standing. Volk was among those who endured the confrontation and eventually intervened by releasing an ominous presence associated with the Beast of Ragnarok.[29]
The presence generated a sensation resembling an unavoidable End, disturbing the instincts of even exceptionally powerful observers and interrupting the conflict. Its source was the Blessing Volk had received from [[Fenrir]] rather than an independent Divinity belonging to Volk himself. He could borrow an impression of the apocalyptic Beast's nature, but he had not become the Conceptual embodiment of End or acquired unrestricted authority over the termination of existence.[29]
The central purpose of Victor's negotiations involved Fenrir's deteriorating condition. Volk regarded the ancient Beast as family and was deeply concerned by the possibility of losing the Being who had protected Samar for generations. When Victor offered the possibility of investigating and treating Fenrir, the Werewolf King initially struggled with the Demon King's apparent disregard for the dignity of the Beast and the kingdom. Nevertheless, he recognized that Victor possessed power and knowledge beyond anything the Werewolves could provide independently.[11]
Volk ultimately restrained his hostility and apologized for his reaction, surprising the Alphas present because of his notorious refusal to display submission. The apology was motivated by his concern for Fenrir, demonstrating that the Beast occupied a far more personal position in Volk's life than that of an ordinary military patron. However, Victor refused to provide such an important service without compensation and demanded an equivalent exchange. When the Demon King named Tasha as the person he wanted, the negotiations became a direct threat to the political and marital arrangement upon which Samar's government depended.[11][30]
The demand provoked confusion and outrage because Tasha was simultaneously Volk's wife, the Queen of Samar, and one of the most important administrators in the kingdom. Volk could not simply surrender her without consequences for his authority, while Tasha herself possessed no intention of allowing another man to treat her as property. Nevertheless, the situation exposed the reality that Victor regarded Tasha as an exceptional individual whose value was far greater than the narrow role she occupied within Volk's political hierarchy.[30][31]
During the same visit, Victor raised suspicions concerning Fenir's connections to [[New Dawn]]. Volk initially resisted the accusations because they concerned his own son, but he eventually recognized that an investigation was necessary. Tasha moved through her intelligence network while Volk gave instructions to the authorities beneath him, beginning the destruction of the conspiracy's influence over Samar. The incident demonstrated how effectively the King and Queen could act when their priorities temporarily aligned, even though their wider relationship was rapidly deteriorating.[11][10]
The exposure of Fenir's faction created another confrontation within the Royal Family. Volk was not angered merely because his eldest son wanted to replace him, since Werewolf traditions encouraged strong Alphas to challenge the ruling hierarchy. Instead, he regarded Fenir's willingness to accept support from New Dawn as a betrayal of the nation. The organization possessed its own ambitions and could eventually use the resources of Samar without respecting its independence. Volk and Tasha consequently cooperated to dismantle Fenir's political structure and remove his ability to continue operating through compromised followers.[10]
Volk wanted Fenir executed for endangering the kingdom, but Tasha refused to surrender control over their son's punishment. She regarded the First Prince as an important source of information concerning New Dawn and insisted that the investigation continue before any final decision was made. Their disagreement nearly developed into a direct confrontation, revealing the increasingly unstable relationship between the two rulers. When Fenir attempted to exploit the distraction and escape, Victor intervened and prevented him from leaving.[10]
Victor's involvement became personally humiliating for Volk. When the Demon King was summoned during the investigation, the Werewolf King reacted with anger and attempted to assert his authority over the situation. Victor responded by releasing a terrifying Demonic presence that overwhelmed Volk's instincts and reduced the powerful Alpha to fearful submission. To the surrounding observers, the spectacle represented a reversal of the traditional hierarchy of predators: the ruler who had spent centuries occupying the highest position among Werewolves was being dominated by a foreign Being whose power transcended the limits of Samar's political system.[16]
Volk's relationship with Victor continued deteriorating after the latter healed Fenrir and developed a personal connection with the Beast. The King recognized that Fenrir's growing respect for Victor made it increasingly unrealistic to seek the ancient Wolf's assistance against him. He also became disturbed by the Vampire's relationships with extraordinarily rare Beings, including [[World Trees]], whose influence Volk regarded as far beyond the reach of ordinary supernatural monarchs.[15]
The decisive diplomatic confrontation occurred when Volk interfered in negotiations that Tasha would ordinarily have conducted. His approach endangered Samar's position and provoked criticism from Maya, who acknowledged his strength and considered him a capable King but believed that he should have allowed Tasha to manage the situation. Volk's unwillingness to remain within the political role best suited to him transformed an already difficult negotiation into a threat to the stability of the Werewolf monarchy.[9]
Following the confrontation, Volk contacted Vlad for advice. Their conversation demonstrated that the two monarchs possessed a relationship extending beyond the traditional hostility between their [[Races (Flamel)|Races]]. Vlad recognized the situation almost immediately, understanding the distinctive frustration Victor caused in rulers who were accustomed to controlling their own affairs. Volk explained that Fenrir's condition had been resolved but remained deeply unsettled by Victor's behavior, political influence, and growing connection to Tasha.[13]
The discussion exposed Volk's concealed infidelity and the existence of children he had fathered outside his marriage. Vlad understood that the Werewolf King's position was far weaker than he admitted and warned that provoking Victor or targeting the Vampire's family would lead to disastrous consequences. Volk's anger and jealousy did not alter the reality that the Demon King had become powerful enough to retaliate against Samar without requiring the cooperation of its monarch.[13][8]
Vlad presented Volk with different political options. The relationship between Samar's rulers was divided between the King's public authority over the masses and Tasha's control over the darker institutions and much of the Noble hierarchy. Volk could attempt to remove Tasha's influence and preserve the throne, but the decision would destroy their marriage and risk dividing the kingdom. Alternatively, he could seek reconciliation and preserve the partnership that had allowed Samar to function successfully. Volk chose to maintain his own authority rather than accept the growing independence of his wife.[8]
His decision was influenced by Victor's increasing presence in the political struggle. The Demon King's faction had captured several influential Werewolves belonging to Volk's supporters, and Victor subsequently handed those captives to Vlad, giving the Vampire King potential leverage over elements of Samar's internal politics. This development further complicated Volk's belief that his longtime acquaintance with Vlad guaranteed a reliable political relationship. The old rivalry between Vampires and Werewolves remained useful to people capable of exploiting it, even while individual rulers maintained private friendships.[32]
Volk eventually moved against Tasha's power base. He used loyal Betas and the influence of the throne to weaken her political position before confronting her directly. Tasha responded by emphasizing how much of Samar's development had resulted from her efforts. She had created foreign relationships, maintained essential institutions, negotiated with dangerous factions, and handled responsibilities Volk routinely left to her. The King acknowledged her usefulness but insisted that she was no longer necessary to the government he intended to control personally.[2]
Volk then revealed the divorce documents Tasha had unknowingly signed during the beginning of their relationship. The arrangement allowed him to dissolve their marriage and strip her of the position of Queen, reducing her nominally to the status of an exiled Egyptian Goddess without legitimate authority over Samar. His decision represented a calculated betrayal of the trust Tasha had once placed in him and destroyed the personal relationship beneath their political partnership.[2]
The consequences rapidly exceeded Volk's expectations. Tasha considered his actions a betrayal that demanded retaliation, while Victor's growing interest in her provided the opportunity and support necessary for her to pursue a different future. The Werewolf King meanwhile sought further advice from Vlad, who openly criticized the manner in which Volk had approached the confrontation. Vlad argued that an individual familiar with Tasha's experiences should have understood that direct hostility would provoke her defensive instincts and make reconciliation almost impossible.[12]
Vlad warned Volk that Tasha was exceptionally dangerous when given sufficient preparation and that Victor's involvement only increased the threat. The Werewolf King began considering his remaining options but discovered that many traditional sources of support were no longer reliable. Fenrir respected Victor, Samar's World Trees favored the Demon King, and foreign Pantheons would demand substantial compensation before intervening in a domestic struggle threatening the entire kingdom.[12]
Among the strategies discussed was using Thomas as a hostage to force Tasha to withdraw. Volk immediately rejected the proposal, declaring that his youngest son had done nothing wrong and would not be sacrificed for the sake of political advantage. The decision demonstrated that his desperation had not entirely overcome his paternal instincts or his remaining sense of honor.[12]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
The struggle between Volk and Tasha eventually escalated into an open confrontation over the future of Samar. By this point, the former Queen had developed significantly beyond her previous limitations and was prepared to challenge the husband who had deprived her of her political position. Their conflict represented far more than a conventional marital dispute. The outcome would determine which of them could legitimately claim the supreme authority of the Werewolf Race and whether Samar would continue beneath the hierarchy Volk had maintained for centuries.[33][3]
Volk initially relied upon his overwhelming physical strength, position as a True Alpha, and the assumption that Tasha remained fundamentally beneath him in direct combat. Tasha refused to fight according to his expectations. She exploited his anger, distracted him through personal accusations, and used the intelligence and preparation that had always distinguished her from her former husband. Her transformation into a more complete Wolf Goddess further altered the confrontation, granting her access to supernatural characteristics that Volk had underestimated.[3][14]
During the battle, Tasha employed a full Moon and increasingly powerful wolf-related abilities while confronting Volk. The conflict reflected the differences between their fighting philosophies: Volk favored direct confrontation and the overwhelming physical capabilities of his body, while Tasha combined supernatural power with preparation, deception, and assistance from loyal followers. His anger prevented him from adequately accounting for opponents who did not intend to respect the conventions of an honorable duel.[3][14]
The decisive injury occurred when a concealed sniper struck Volk in the chest with a specialized projectile. The bullet contained Wolfsbane and Demon metal, substances capable of interfering with his exceptional Werewolf regeneration. Volk attempted to force the wound closed but discovered that his body was failing to respond normally. Recognizing the nature of the projectile, he associated the weapon with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and reacted furiously to the possibility that the Demon King had supplied Tasha with the means to defeat him.[14]
Tasha rejected his accusation that using hidden weapons constituted cowardice. She reminded him that he had been the first to betray her trust, employing secret preparations and political manipulation to remove her authority while she continued supporting the kingdom. From her perspective, Volk had no right to demand an honorable confrontation after undermining their marriage through deception. She intended to destroy the political and personal identity he had constructed rather than allow him to escape the consequences of his actions.[14]
Their confrontation exposed Volk's central accusation: he believed Tasha had betrayed him romantically through her growing association with Victor. Tasha denied having slept with Victor before the divorce and condemned Volk for allowing jealousy to replace the trust and communication necessary for a lasting relationship. She maintained that his own decisions had created the circumstances he most feared, transforming a potentially recoverable political disagreement into an irreversible personal conflict.[14]
Tasha's growth culminated in the awakening of her complete Divine nature as a Goddess associated with the Wolf Race and its subspecies. Her existence was no longer confined to the ordinary status of a powerful Werewolf Queen or former Egyptian Goddess. She had developed into a Being whose Divine authority was intrinsically connected to wolves and the supernatural foundations of the Race itself.[6]
The confrontation also resolved the dormant competition surrounding the next Werewolf Progenitor. Both Volk and Tasha had fulfilled the necessary qualifications, but the existence of two viable candidates had prevented either from being selected. By defeating Volk and demonstrating her superiority as an Alpha, Tasha triggered the intervention of a higher system of Balance that recognized her as the appropriate candidate. She consequently ascended as the new Werewolf Progenitor, becoming the Source through which the entire Race's supernatural hierarchy was organized.[6]
This ascension fundamentally changed Volk's position. Until that moment, he had existed as the King and supreme Alpha of Samar, benefiting from the strength of the Pack beneath him. Once Tasha became the Progenitor, every Werewolf—including Volk—became subordinate to her within the deeper racial hierarchy. His political title, ancient bloodline, and accumulated strength could no longer override the Authority of the individual recognized as the Source of his species.[6]
Tasha immediately demonstrated the consequences by reclaiming the Power Volk had accumulated. He became unable to resist as green Energy emerged from his body and gathered into a sphere within her hand. The process caused his physical form to wither and stripped him of the tremendous strength he had possessed as King. Tasha absorbed the sphere, adding Volk's accumulated Power to her own without requiring his death or the consumption of his Soul.[1]
Unlike the method through which a Vampire Progenitor could obtain abilities, memories, and other characteristics by consuming an opponent, Tasha's racial Authority granted her Volk's Power without automatically obtaining his knowledge, experiences, or individual techniques. This distinction reflected the underlying differences between Vampire and Werewolf Progenitors. The Werewolf hierarchy operated through the relationship between an Alpha and the Packs beneath them, allowing the Source of the Race to reclaim strength from individuals subject to that Authority.[1]
Tasha then pronounced Volk's formal expulsion from the Werewolf hierarchy. She removed his Alpha status, denied him the lesser position of Beta, and declared that he would become an Omega, a solitary wolf without a Pack or recognized place within the Race. The decree carried genuine supernatural authority rather than functioning as a symbolic political sentence. [[Werewolves]] present immediately sensed their connections to Volk disappearing, while Victor could perceive the metaphysical thread binding him to Tasha being severed.[1]
Because Tasha was the Source of the Werewolf Race, the expulsion inflicted catastrophic damage upon Volk's Soul. He suffered intense pain as the connection was broken, and the resulting spiritual injury prevented him from maintaining or recovering the supernatural strength he had previously possessed. Victor determined that even repairing the damaged Soul would be insufficient to restore Volk's original power while he remained disconnected from the Source. His effective strength had fallen below that of an ordinary Human child.[1]
The final loss came from [[Fenrir]] himself. [[Aurora Seraphina Evergreen]], Samar's World Tree, had observed Volk's destruction through her connection to the planet and concluded that he could no longer fulfill the responsibilities of one of its protectors. She instructed Fenrir to remove the Blessing previously granted to the defeated King. The Beast regarded Volk with contempt and withdrew his favor, severing the last known source of the exceptional power that had distinguished Volk from ordinary members of his Race.[1]
Volk survived the confrontation but lost his throne, wife, Pack, accumulated Power, ancestral status as the supreme Alpha, and the Blessing of the Beast of Ragnarok. The man who had once governed Samar through physical dominance was left powerless before the woman he had attempted to remove from the kingdom. Tasha intended for him to remain alive and witness the destruction of the legacy he believed belonged exclusively to him, regarding survival without power or influence as a punishment more fitting than death.[1]

### Current Condition
Volk's last confirmed condition is that of an Omega Werewolf, alive but severely crippled following Tasha's ascension as the new Werewolf Progenitor. He no longer possesses his former position as the King of Samar, and the supernatural hierarchy that once amplified his power has been severed from him. His connection to Tasha as the Source of the Race was destroyed, causing damage to his Soul that left him unable to recover the strength he had previously accumulated.[1]
The loss of his Alpha status is distinct from ordinary political exile. An ordinary Werewolf deprived of a position might theoretically rebuild a Pack, develop physical abilities, or challenge another ruler. Volk's punishment involved direct intervention by the Race's Progenitor, who removed the supernatural relationship upon which his powers depended. Even restoration of his damaged Soul would not independently return his former strength unless the loss of his connection to the Source were also resolved.[1]
[[Fenrir]]'s subsequent withdrawal of his Blessing permanently ended Volk's previously demonstrated access to the apocalyptic power borrowed from the Beast of Ragnarok. The overwhelming End-like presence he had used to intervene during the confrontation between [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Maya was therefore no longer available to him.[29][1]
His precise whereabouts, treatment following his defeat, and subsequent personal activities have not been established. No confirmed restoration of his Alpha status, reconciliation with Tasha, recovery of Fenrir's Blessing, or return to the Werewolf throne is recorded. His former combat abilities remain part of his history, but they should not be treated as powers currently available to him.

### Former Skills & Abilities
Before his downfall, Volk belonged to the highest levels of Werewolf society, combining the natural abilities of a True Alpha with an enormous Pack-based strength amplification and a personal Blessing from the Beast of Ragnarok. His racial heritage, battle experience, and extraordinary physical constitution made him one of Samar's most dangerous warriors. The abilities below describe his former capabilities; his present condition does not support treating them as active powers.

#### True Alpha Werewolf Physiology
- True Alpha Werewolf Physiology (Formerly): Volk belonged to an ancient Werewolf bloodline descending from the original Progenitor and fully embraced his inner Wolf. His condition placed him among the exceptional True [[Werewolves]] who had developed beyond ordinary Alphas through their acceptance of their predatory nature.[17][3]
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics: Volk possessed immense physical strength, speed, durability, stamina, and other characteristics inherent to his status as a powerful True Alpha. His physical abilities greatly exceeded those of ordinary Werewolves and formed the foundation of his reputation as Samar's strongest Alpha.[4][17]
- Superhuman Strength: Volk possessed a massive and exceptionally powerful body capable of applying tremendous force in direct combat. His position as King further amplified his natural strength through the enormous Pack subordinate to him.[4][5]
- Superhuman Speed: His advanced Werewolf physiology granted him physical speed and reactions far beyond ordinary supernatural beings. His ability to perceive and react to conventional projectiles was sufficiently developed that he regarded the failure to avoid Tasha's specialized ammunition as unusual.[14]
- Superhuman Durability: Volk possessed a highly resilient body capable of enduring powerful supernatural attacks and physical punishment. His eventual defeat required Tasha to exploit his psychological vulnerabilities and employ weapons specifically designed to interfere with his regeneration.[3][14]
- Regeneration: Volk possessed the regenerative abilities characteristic of powerful Werewolves. He could ordinarily recover from severe physical injuries, but the combination of Wolfsbane and Demon metal used against him interfered with his healing, preventing the wound in his chest from closing normally.[14]
- Enhanced Stamina: His True Alpha physiology allowed him to maintain prolonged physical activity and combat without the limitations experienced by ordinary Humans. The exact maximum duration of his stamina has not been measured independently.[17]
- Enhanced Senses: Volk possessed the heightened sensory capabilities associated with powerful Werewolves, including supernatural perception of threats and other predators. No unique named sensory technique has been established.[17]
- Predatory Instincts: By fully embracing his inner Wolf, Volk developed the instinctive awareness and animalistic mentality associated with ancient True Werewolves. This provided advantages in recognizing powerful opponents while contributing to his aggressive and prideful behavior.[17]
- Werewolf Transformation: Volk could embrace the transformed state associated with True Werewolves, although the exact physical characteristics and complete appearance of his personal transformed form have not been described sufficiently to establish a definitive design.[17]
- Longevity: Volk possessed the extended lifespan of an ancient Werewolf. Ordinary Alphas could naturally live for approximately one thousand years, with the maximum lifespan of Werewolves extending toward three thousand years depending upon strength. Volk's Blessing from [[Fenrir]] complicated his natural longevity, but the exact extent to which it prolonged his life has not been measured.[5]
- Ageless Appearance: Despite his great age, Volk maintained a powerful and youthful physical condition during his reign, consistent with the longevity and supernatural constitution of high-ranking Werewolves.[4][5]

#### Alpha Authority and Pack-Based Power
- True Alpha Authority (Formerly): Volk stood above the ordinary Werewolf hierarchy as the recognized King and supreme Alpha of Samar. His status provided authority over the Packs subordinate to the monarchy and allowed him to command the military forces acknowledging his rule.[5][1]
- Pack Empowerment: Werewolf Alphas gain additional strength through the members of their Packs. As the King of every Werewolf on Samar, Volk benefited from an enormous racial population, producing a substantial amplification above his personal physical strength.[5]
- Power Amplification: The strength obtained through a Pack depends upon the number and quality of its members. Although Volk commanded an immense population, ordinary civilian [[Werewolves]] contributed much less than exceptionally powerful warriors, preventing the amplification from becoming unlimited.[5]
- Pack Hierarchy: Volk's position granted him authority within the existing Werewolf social structure, including the ability to command Betas and lesser Alphas beneath the royal hierarchy. This authority depended upon his continued standing within the Race and was superseded when Tasha became its Progenitor.[5][1]
- Dominating Presence: Volk possessed an intimidating Alpha aura capable of exerting pressure upon weaker Werewolves and other supernatural beings. His presence was reinforced by the enormous power accumulated through his position as King.[4][29]
- Royal Military Command: Volk possessed the authority to mobilize the military forces and Generals of Samar, including members of the powerful Werewolf Clans that recognized his reign.[25][9]
- Alpha Succession: Volk could maintain his position through the traditional Werewolf system in which powerful Alphas challenged the existing hierarchy. His willingness to accept such contests reflected the competitive foundation of the Race's political structure, although this was primarily a social institution rather than a separate supernatural technique.[10]

#### Blessing of the Beast of Ragnarok
- Blessing of [[Fenrir]] (Formerly): Volk received a personal Blessing from Fenrir, the Beast of Ragnarok. The Blessing distinguished him from ordinary True Alphas and granted access to an aspect of Fenrir's apocalyptic nature. It also permitted Volk and Tasha to bear the Fenrir name.[4][5][29]
- End-Like Aura: Volk could release an extraordinarily ominous presence causing observers to experience a sensation resembling their unavoidable End. He used this manifestation to interrupt the overwhelming clash between [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Maya, forcing both opponents to acknowledge the danger associated with Fenrir's Power.[29]
- Fear Inducement: The apocalyptic presence associated with his Blessing could provoke instinctive fear and awareness of overwhelming danger. The effect originated from Fenrir's nature rather than from a separate fear-related Divinity belonging to Volk.[29]
- Borrowed Conceptual Influence: Fenrir possesses an extraordinary relationship with End, but Volk merely borrowed a limited manifestation through the Blessing. He did not independently possess the Concept of End, absolute cessation, or the ability to erase any target without resistance.[29]
- Blessing-Based Enhancement: Fenrir's favor contributed to Volk's unusual standing and potentially affected the limits of his natural Werewolf existence. The exact numerical enhancement and full list of characteristics provided by the Blessing have not been established.[5]
- Blessing Removal: Following Volk's expulsion, Fenrir withdrew his Blessing at Aurora's request. Volk consequently lost access to its supernatural effects, including the End-like presence formerly available to him.[1]

#### Combat and Non-Combat Skills
- Hand-to-Hand Combat: Volk was an experienced warrior whose fighting methods reflected the direct physical traditions of Werewolf society. His reputation depended primarily upon strength, close-range confrontation, and the ability to dominate other powerful Alphas.[4][3]
- Ancient Combat Experience: Volk had lived for thousands of years and maintained his position within a civilization where powerful challengers could threaten the throne. His longevity and reputation demonstrate considerable combat experience, although the complete record of his individual battles has not been established.[5][10]
- Combat Instinct: Volk possessed highly developed predatory instincts through his True Werewolf nature. These instincts helped him recognize threats and evaluate the danger posed by exceptional supernatural opponents.[17][11]
- Pain Tolerance: Volk endured severe bodily damage during his confrontation with Tasha and attempted to continue functioning despite injuries that interfered with his regeneration.[14]
- Leadership: Volk commanded the Werewolf kingdom for an extended period and maintained the respect of the Royal Army, powerful Alphas, and substantial portions of the population. Maya considered him a capable King even while criticizing his political mistakes.[9]
- Military Leadership: Volk served as Samar's principal military authority and was responsible for maintaining the strength and reputation of the kingdom through direct force and its established warrior hierarchy.[7][25]
- Political Judgment: Despite his frequent impulsiveness, Volk could recognize dangerous situations, understand the importance of powerful alliances, and seek advice from experienced rulers when necessary. His limitations became most apparent in matters requiring the subtle methods employed by Tasha.[11][8]
- Intimidation: Volk's physical appearance, supernatural strength, and Alpha status allowed him to exert considerable psychological pressure upon weaker opponents and subordinates.[4]
- Supernatural Predation: Volk possessed the predatory traits associated with ancient [[Werewolves]] and threatened opponents with being devoured. However, the precise mechanics of any independent power-acquiring Devouring ability have not been demonstrated; his threats should not be treated as proof that he could automatically absorb an enemy's abilities or Divinities.[21]

#### Weaknesses and Limitations
- Wolfsbane Susceptibility: Volk's supernatural healing could be disrupted by specially prepared Wolfsbane. The projectile used against him prevented a severe chest wound from regenerating normally.[14]
- Demon Metal: The specialized ammunition used by Tasha's forces incorporated Demon metal, contributing to its effectiveness against Volk's regenerative defenses.[14]
- Dependence on Pack Structure: Much of Volk's former strength resulted from the supernatural hierarchy beneath him. His position as King provided enormous amplification but also created a vulnerability once a higher racial Authority became capable of severing his connections.[5][1]
- Dependence on [[Fenrir]]'s Blessing: The special End-like aspect of Volk's power was borrowed from Fenrir and could be withdrawn by the Being who granted it. Fenrir ultimately removed the Blessing completely.[1]
- Racial Authority: Once Tasha became Werewolf Progenitor, Volk was unable to oppose her position as the Source of his Race. Her authority allowed her to reclaim his accumulated Power and expel him from the Werewolf hierarchy.[6][1]
- Soul Damage: Tasha's expulsion severed the metaphysical connection supporting Volk's existence within the Race, inflicting catastrophic damage upon his Soul. He could not recover his former strength merely through ordinary physical healing.[1]
- Emotional Vulnerability: Volk's jealousy, possessiveness, and difficulty controlling his anger were repeatedly exploited by Tasha, making him susceptible to psychological manipulation during their final confrontation.[2][14]
- Strategic Limitations: Volk's preference for direct confrontation and his distrust of Tasha's political methods contributed to several disastrous decisions. His inability to properly anticipate her retaliation ultimately cost him his throne and power.[9][12]

### Power
At his peak, Volk was regarded as the Strongest Alpha and one of the most powerful [[Werewolves]] living on Samar. His standing came from the combination of an ancient True Alpha bloodline, extensive physical development, the enormous Pack supporting him as King, and [[Fenrir]]'s personal Blessing. He was powerful enough to remain standing during the tremendous clash of supernatural Wills between [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Maya when the surrounding Werewolves were being overwhelmed, and his borrowed End-like presence was sufficient to interrupt that confrontation.[4][5][29]
The most distinctive aspect of his power was the amplification provided by Werewolf hierarchy. Ordinary Alphas could gain additional strength through their Betas, with the amount obtained depending upon the number and quality of their followers. Volk's position as King connected him to the entire Werewolf population of Samar and therefore supplied a much greater increase than could ordinarily be obtained by an independent Alpha with a small Pack. The enormous size of his kingdom did not make him infinitely powerful because the benefits of such amplification became subject to diminishing returns and the different strengths of individual members.[5]
Volk's personal strength was further distinguished by Fenrir's Blessing. Although the Beast of Ragnarok possessed powers associated with the End of entire Divine systems, Volk did not independently control Fenrir's full authority. The sensation he could produce represented a borrowed fraction of the Beast's nature and should not be used to classify Volk as the actual embodiment of End or as an unrestricted Conceptual Being.[29]
Volk was sufficiently powerful and qualified to stand as one of the candidates for the next Werewolf Progenitor. His failure to acquire that position did not mean he lacked the necessary foundation; the competition remained unresolved because both he and Tasha possessed the required qualifications. Once Tasha defeated him and was recognized by the Balance as the superior candidate, she ascended and gained an authority surpassing every existing Werewolf, including Volk.[6]
His defeat was not simply a conventional comparison of physical strength. Tasha used Divine development, superior preparation, psychological warfare, specialized weapons, and ultimately racial Authority to overcome him. Her ascension then transformed the nature of their relationship from a struggle between powerful individuals into a confrontation between the Source of the Werewolf Race and one of its subordinate members.[14][6][1]

### Relationships

#### Tasha Fenrir Elderblood
[[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]] was Volk's wife, co-ruler, political partner, and ultimately the individual responsible for ending his reign. Their relationship began when Tasha was a displaced former Egyptian Goddess and Volk possessed the ancient Werewolf bloodline and physical strength necessary to establish authority over Samar. Their union allowed each to contribute what the other lacked. Volk provided military security and the physical authority expected from a [[Lycaon|Werewolf King]], while Tasha developed the political institutions, diplomatic relationships, and covert networks through which the kingdom became more stable and influential.[2][14]
Despite the advantages of their partnership, Volk never trusted Tasha completely. He secretly arranged divorce documents at the beginning of their marriage and concealed the existence of several children fathered through other women. Tasha likewise maintained independent sources of influence because her experiences in the [[Egyptian Pantheon]] had taught her that entrusting her entire future to another individual was dangerous. Their mutual distrust remained manageable while both benefited from the arrangement, but eventually became destructive once Volk felt threatened by Tasha's increasing independence.[13][2]
Volk's jealousy over Tasha's association with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] transformed these tensions into open hostility. He accused her of betraying him despite his own history of infidelity and moved to weaken her political position before invoking the hidden divorce agreement. Tasha interpreted his actions as a deliberate betrayal of the trust she had once extended to him, and her subsequent revenge was designed to destroy the entire identity Volk had built around his status as King and Alpha.[2][14]
Their final confrontation ended with Tasha ascending as the new Werewolf Progenitor, reclaiming Volk's accumulated Power, and removing him from the Race's hierarchy. She deliberately spared his life, considering the humiliation of survival without strength, influence, or recognition more appropriate than a quick death.[6][1]

#### Vlad Dracula Tepes
[[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] was Volk's longtime acquaintance and one of the few supernatural rulers he considered sufficiently familiar to approach privately. Despite the ancient hostility between Vampires and [[Werewolves]], their interactions demonstrated a degree of mutual understanding developed through their respective experiences as monarchs. Volk contacted Vlad for advice when his relationship with Tasha began collapsing, believing that the Vampire King could understand the difficulties of maintaining political authority against powerful individuals who refused to submit.[13]
Vlad responded with unusual familiarity, openly criticizing Volk's mistakes and explaining how his dependence upon direct confrontation had made him vulnerable to Tasha's retaliation. He recognized [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] as an extraordinary threat and repeatedly warned Volk that attempting to harm the Vampire's family would be disastrous. Their conversations also exposed the political difference between them: Vlad maintained relatively centralized control over his own society, whereas Volk shared Samar's authority with Tasha and depended upon powerful Werewolf Clans whose loyalties were not exclusively his own.[8][12]
Although Vlad proposed extreme strategies to preserve Volk's political position, including threatening Thomas as a hostage, Volk rejected methods that would deliberately endanger an innocent son. Their friendship was therefore not founded upon identical moral principles or political methods, but upon familiarity, mutual recognition, and the experience of ruling hostile supernatural civilizations.[12]

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] was initially a young Vampire whose extraordinary growth attracted Volk's interest. The [[Lycaon|Werewolf King]] respected Victor's willingness to challenge established authorities and his refusal to conceal his violent actions behind political excuses. That admiration gradually transformed into hostility as Victor became powerful enough to disregard Samar's conventional hierarchy and developed increasingly close relationships with the people Volk considered essential to his own authority.[19][21]
Volk found Victor's influence especially threatening because it extended into his own family. Anderson had become the Vampire's friend, powerful members of the Lykos lineage regarded him favorably, [[Fenrir]] developed respect for him, and Tasha increasingly recognized qualities in Victor that she no longer perceived in her husband. The Demon King's overwhelming supernatural strength further damaged Volk's pride, particularly when Victor intimidated him into submission during the investigation of Fenir's conspiracy.[16][15]
Volk's insecurity culminated in his suspicion that Tasha was romantically betraying him with Victor. He interpreted their private interactions as evidence that he was losing his wife, even though she denied having slept with Victor before their divorce. Volk's attempts to preserve his authority accelerated the very outcome he feared by driving Tasha away and giving her reason to reject the marriage permanently.[2][14]
Victor did not need to personally defeat Volk in their final conflict. Tasha overcame her former husband through her own development, preparation, and racial ascension, while Victor observed the emergence of the new Werewolf Progenitor and recognized the severity of Volk's punishment.[6][1]

#### Fenrir
[[Fenrir]], the Beast of Ragnarok, was Volk's patron and the source of his most distinctive supernatural ability. Fenrir recognized Volk and Tasha sufficiently to grant them powerful Blessings and allow them to carry his name. The Beast's presence on Samar provided an extraordinary deterrent against powerful outside enemies, making his protection one of the most valuable assets available to the Werewolf monarchy.[4][5]
Volk regarded Fenrir as family rather than merely as a source of strength. When the Beast became severely ill, Volk was willing to abandon his usual pride and apologize to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] in order to obtain assistance. His reaction demonstrated genuine concern for Fenrir's survival and a personal attachment that extended beyond the political value of possessing an apocalyptic protector.[11]
Their relationship deteriorated after Fenrir recovered and developed his own connection with Victor. Once Volk had been stripped of his Alpha status, Aurora concluded that he could no longer serve as a meaningful protector of Samar. Fenrir obeyed her request to remove his Blessing, looking upon his former beneficiary with contempt before withdrawing the final source of Volk's exceptional borrowed power.[12][1]

#### Fenir
Fenir is Volk's eldest publicly recognized son and the former First Prince of Samar. Fenir inherited his father's ambition and dissatisfaction with the stagnation of Werewolf political structures but pursued authority through methods Volk considered unacceptable. Although the King did not oppose the possibility of being replaced by a stronger or more capable successor, he condemned Fenir's cooperation with [[New Dawn]] because the arrangement threatened to surrender Werewolf resources and influence to outsiders.[10]
Volk cooperated with Tasha to dismantle Fenir's political faction after the conspiracy was exposed and initially wanted his son executed. Tasha opposed the immediate execution because Fenir possessed valuable information concerning New Dawn's members and activities, contributing to the growing conflict between the King and Queen over the treatment of their children and the security of Samar.[10]

#### Anderson
Anderson is Volk's second publicly recognized son and the Second Prince of Samar. Anderson inherited much of his father's love of battle, strength, and willingness to confront powerful opponents, but developed a comparatively more flexible attitude toward individuals from other [[Races (Flamel)|Races]]. His friendship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] demonstrated a willingness to judge a Vampire according to personal qualities rather than the longstanding hatred between [[Werewolves]] and Vampires.[19][4]
Anderson also grew increasingly dissatisfied with the political stagnation of Samar, believing that his father's continued reign prevented necessary changes. Nevertheless, he did not become involved in Fenir's foreign conspiracy and remained concerned with the security of the kingdom. Tasha eventually prevented Anderson from interfering in her confrontation with Volk, recognizing that her son possessed ambitions of his own and might attempt to influence the outcome.[34]

#### Thomas Fenrir
[[Thomas Fenrir]] is Volk and Tasha's youngest publicly recognized son and the child most closely associated with the Beast of Ragnarok. Thomas received extraordinary attention because of his potential and the recognition granted to him by [[Fenrir]], creating resentment among his older brothers concerning the unequal expectations within the Royal Family.[18]
Volk's attachment toward Thomas became especially apparent during his private discussions with Vlad. When the Vampire King suggested kidnapping Thomas and threatening his life to pressure Tasha, Volk refused immediately. He considered Thomas innocent of the conflict between his parents and would not deliberately sacrifice him for the sake of retaining his political position.[12]

#### Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood
[[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] was one of Samar's most powerful Ancient Alphas and the Matriarch of the [[Lykos Clan]]. She respected Volk's strength and considered him a capable King, but did not regard his position as sufficient reason to tolerate political incompetence. Her own status as a powerful True Werewolf allowed her to evaluate him without the same submissiveness that characterized many ordinary Pack members.[17][9]
Maya became openly critical of Volk after he interfered in negotiations that Tasha would normally have handled. She believed that his unwillingness to trust the Queen's superior diplomatic ability had placed the entire kingdom in unnecessary danger. Her criticism represented one of the clearest assessments of Volk's reign: he possessed the qualities necessary to serve as the military center of Samar, but his mistakes emerged when he attempted to govern every aspect of the kingdom through the same methods.[9]

#### Evie Moriarty Elderblood
Evie Moriarty, the Witch Queen, maintained important economic dealings with Samar because ordinary [[Werewolves]] relied upon magical talismans capable of reproducing the influence of the full Moon. Although powerful Alphas such as Volk did not require the devices to access their transformations, the kingdom's weaker members depended upon their continued availability.[24]
Volk's confrontational behavior toward Evie led her to threaten a substantial increase in the price of those talismans. His refusal to apologize demonstrated how easily his personal pride could create difficulties for the wider population, while Evie's ability to exploit the dispute reflected the economic influence of the Witches over supernatural societies.[24]

#### Aurora Seraphina Evergreen
[[Aurora Seraphina Evergreen]] is Samar's World Tree and one of the supernatural foundations supporting the planet. Her relationship with [[Fenrir]] and the wider environment placed her in a position to evaluate individuals who served as protectors of the world. After Volk lost his Alpha status and accumulated Power, Aurora concluded that he could no longer fulfill that role and instructed Fenrir to withdraw his Blessing.[1]
Her decision permanently ended the protection previously granted to Volk by the Beast of Ragnarok and completed the removal of his former supernatural advantages.[1]

- Volk was not himself the original Werewolf Progenitor. Both he and Tasha later qualified as candidates for the next Progenitor, with the competition resolved in Tasha's favor.[6]
- Volk's strength was substantially amplified by his position as King because the entire Werewolf population of Samar belonged to the hierarchy supporting his Pack.[5]
- An Alpha Werewolf's lifespan could extend with strength, while the general upper limit discussed for the Race was approximately three thousand years. Volk's Blessing from [[Fenrir]] complicated the ordinary limitations of that lifespan.[5]
- Volk and his sons could access their Werewolf transformations without the artificial full-moon talismans required by many weaker [[Werewolves]]. Those talismans were an important component of Samar's trade with the Witches.[24]
- Volk once provoked the Witch Queen sufficiently that she threatened to triple the price of those talismans unless he apologized.[24]
- He was capable of remaining standing during the powerful clash of Wills between Victor and Maya when most of the surrounding Werewolves were overwhelmed.[29]
- His End-like aura originated from Fenrir's Blessing. Volk did not independently possess the Concept of End.[29]
- Volk considered Fenrir family and was willing to apologize to Victor in order to obtain help treating the Beast's mysterious condition.[11]
- Volk did not object to Fenir's ambition to replace him as King in principle, but regarded his willingness to involve [[New Dawn]] as a betrayal of Samar.[10]
- He secretly prepared the documents that would later divorce Tasha at the beginning of their marriage, intending them as a contingency against losing control over his wife.[2]
- Volk fathered additional children outside his marriage but concealed or refused to publicly acknowledge several of them because he feared Tasha discovering his affairs.[13]
- Despite the historic rivalry between Vampires and Werewolves, Volk maintained a longtime personal acquaintance with Vlad and sought his advice during his political crisis.[13]
- Victor captured influential members of Volk's faction and transferred them to Vlad, giving the Vampire King political leverage over the rival Werewolf kingdom.[32]
- Volk refused Vlad's proposal to use Thomas as a hostage against Tasha because he considered his youngest son innocent of their conflict.[12]
- Tasha defeated Volk using a combination of Divine development, psychological manipulation, superior preparation, and specialized ammunition incorporating Wolfsbane and Demon metal.[14]
- Both Volk and Tasha fulfilled the conditions necessary for Progenitor ascension, but the existence of two candidates prevented either from being selected until Tasha defeated him.[6]
- After becoming Progenitor, Tasha reclaimed Volk's accumulated Power without needing to kill him or consume his Soul.[1]
- Tasha obtained Volk's Power but did not automatically acquire his memories, battle experience, or individual techniques through the process.[1]
- Volk was formally expelled from the Werewolf hierarchy as an Omega, and the severing of his connection to the Race's Source caused catastrophic damage to his Soul.[1]
- Even if his Soul were repaired, Volk could not regain his former strength while the connection to Tasha as the Source of the Werewolf Race remained severed.[1]
- After losing his accumulated Power and Fenrir's Blessing, Volk became weaker than an ordinary Human child.[1]
- Volk remains alive in his last confirmed appearance, but his subsequent whereabouts and any possible restoration of his former powers have not been established.
