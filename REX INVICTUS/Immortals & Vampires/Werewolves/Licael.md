#### First Mention
Chapter 772
Licael is the first known Werewolf Progenitor and one of the foundational figures of Werewolf history on Samar. He lived during the earliest known period of the modern Werewolf race and was the husband of [[Elizabeth Lykos]], who was remembered as the strongest of the Progenitor's wives and the ancestor of the [[Lykos Clan]].[1]
Very little is currently known about Licael personally. Most information concerning him comes indirectly through the history of Elizabeth and the ancient Lykos bloodline rather than through a direct appearance.

### Personality
Very little is known about Licael's personality.
His position as the original Werewolf Progenitor placed him at the highest level of ancient Werewolf society, and Elizabeth being specifically described as his strongest wife establishes that he had multiple wives during his lifetime.[1]
Beyond this, the novel has not yet revealed enough information to determine his temperament, leadership style or personal relationship with his wives.

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Licael was first meaningfully referenced during [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]]' exploration of the historical records of the [[Lykos Clan]]. While explaining the origin of the Clan and the Elizabeth Trait, the records identified [[Elizabeth Lykos]] as the Strongest Wife of the Progenitor of the [[Werewolves]].[1]
Elizabeth lived during the era of the original Werewolf Progenitor and became one of his wives. More importantly, she possessed a rare genetic mutation that even Licael himself did not possess. The mutation accelerated the development of a Werewolf's instinctive side and greatly increased the individual's overall potential.[1]
Elizabeth eventually became the strongest Werewolf of her era, and her mutation continued to survive among her descendants. This hereditary characteristic later became known as the Elizabeth Gene or Elizabeth Trait.[1][2]
Through Elizabeth, Licael became one of the principal ancestral figures connected to the Lykos Clan. However, the exceptional mutation that distinguishes the Elizabeth lineage originates specifically from Elizabeth rather than from Licael.[1]
Later information concerning Clan Lykos demonstrated how unusually dominant this ancient bloodline became. [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] observed that Lykos Blood could remain dominant even when mixed with powerful supernatural lineages, including the Blood of Gods and Progenitors.[3]
Licael has not yet appeared directly in the modern storyline.

### Skills & Abilities
The full extent of Licael's powers has not been revealed. His status as the first Werewolf Progenitor nevertheless establishes him as the ancestral source of the Werewolf lineage associated with Samar.

#### Werewolf Progenitor Physiology
- Werewolf Progenitor Physiology: Licael was the original Werewolf Progenitor and therefore possessed a form of Werewolf existence fundamentally superior to ordinary Alpha and Beta [[Werewolves]].[1]
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: As a Progenitor, Licael possessed supernatural strength, speed, durability, stamina and reflexes far beyond ordinary Werewolves.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Werewolves naturally possess powerful regenerative abilities, with a Progenitor representing the ancestral pinnacle of that racial physiology.
- Enhanced Senses: Licael possessed the heightened smell, hearing and predatory perception characteristic of Werewolves.
- Longevity: As an ancient Progenitor, Licael possessed an exceptionally long lifespan.
- Werewolf Transformation: Licael possessed the racial ability to manifest the full predatory characteristics of a Werewolf.
- Natural Weapons: His Werewolf physiology provided supernatural fangs and claws.
- Progenitor Status: Licael existed at the origin of the modern Werewolf lineage associated with Samar and occupied the highest position within the ancient racial hierarchy.[1]
- Bloodline Progenitor: Numerous later Werewolf bloodlines ultimately trace their ancestry back to the era of Licael, including the [[Lykos Clan]] through [[Elizabeth Lykos]].[1]

#### Unconfirmed Progenitor Abilities
Several abilities associated with Progenitors in later portions of the story should not currently be assigned directly to Licael without additional evidence.
- Pack-Based Power Amplification: Modern Werewolf hierarchy demonstrates that powerful Alphas can gain strength from subordinate Betas, but the precise mechanics of Licael's Progenitor-level version have not yet been directly explained.
- Power Bestowal: It has not been directly confirmed that Licael's bite possessed the same special enhancement properties later associated with other exceptional supernatural bloodlines.
- Inherited Knowledge: No chapter currently confirms that Licael possessed Dragon-like inherited knowledge.
- [[Sanguis Noctis Regis]]: Sanguis Noctis Regis, or Golden Blood, is explicitly associated with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Human bloodline and later Vampire history. It should not automatically be assigned to Licael without a direct statement.
- Mind Manipulation Resistance: No individual feat of Licael resisting Mental Manipulation has been shown.
- Absolute Authority over all [[Werewolves]]: Although he was the original Progenitor, the exact supernatural mechanics of his authority over every Werewolf have not yet been detailed.

### Legacy
Licael's principal importance is historical.
As the first Werewolf Progenitor, he stands at the beginning of Samar's known Werewolf lineage. His descendants and the families that emerged during and after his era eventually developed into the Clans that shaped modern Werewolf civilization.
The most important known branch connected to him is the [[Lykos Clan]]. Through his wife [[Elizabeth Lykos]], this lineage inherited one of the most unusual Werewolf mutations ever recorded.[1]
Elizabeth's mutation did not originate from Licael. The historical record specifically emphasizes that it was a characteristic even the Progenitor himself did not possess.[1]
This distinction is important because the exceptional strength later displayed by members such as [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] and [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]] comes from the combination of ancient Werewolf ancestry and Elizabeth's unique mutation rather than from Licael alone.[1][2]

### Relationships

#### Elizabeth Lykos
[[Elizabeth Lykos]] was one of Licael's wives and the strongest among them.[1]
Elizabeth possessed a genetic mutation that Licael himself lacked. Through that mutation she became the strongest Werewolf of her time and established the hereditary line that eventually became the [[Lykos Clan]].[1]
No detailed account of their personal relationship has yet been provided.
