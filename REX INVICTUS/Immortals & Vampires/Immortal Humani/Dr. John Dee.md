## Rex Invictus Integration

In [[Rex Invictus]] the role Dee plays in the [[Flamel Series|Flamel series]], the immortal sorcerer who hunts the Flamels for his masters, belongs to [[Robespierre]]. [[Robespierre]] serves "the Triumvir" (very probably the Roman Triumvirate Holdings named by [[Asmodeus]]), hunts the Philosopher's Stone rather than the Codex, and attacks [[Nicholas Flamel|Perenelle]] in the wrecked Small Book Shop in [[San Francisco]] ([[Ch17 - Rex Invictus X|Ch17]]). Dee himself has not appeared. Read the Dee material below as the source pattern Robespierre follows, not as Rex Invictus history.

## Real-World Background (IRL)

John Dee (1527–1608/9) was an English mathematician, astronomer and occultist who advised Elizabeth I, built one of England's largest libraries and, with Edward Kelley, held angelic séances using a scrying mirror of obsidian now in the British Museum.

Dr. John Dee is one of the primary antagonists of the series. He is a magician, alchemyst, and necromancer in service to the Dark Elders Isis and Osiris. He once was an advisor to Queen Elizabeth I. He is also called the 'English [[Magician (Flamel)|Magician]]' due to his background and practice.

### The Alchemyst
He is first introduced in the Alchemyst, invading the bookstore of [[Nicholas Flamel|Nicholas]] and Perenelle Flamel, stealing a majority of the Codex of [[Abraham the Mage]], and taking Perenelle Flamel hostage. He is stopped from taking the final two pages of the Codex, which are the Final Summoning by [[Josh Newman]]. After the battle, Nicholas and Josh escape with Josh's twin sister [[Sophie Newman]]. Dee responds by sending golems to track down Flamel and the twins. When it is discovered that the twins and Flamel are meeting with [[Scathach]] the Shadow, a powerful [[Next Generation]] Elder to enlist her as a possible ally, Dee takes control of some of the animals outside of Scathach's dojo and uses their eyes to spy on them. When his golems arrive at the dojo and attack, Scathach, destroys all of them quickly and decides to ally with Flamel. While the twins, Flamel, and Scathach go in search of someone who can awaken the twin's powers, Dee meets with the Dark Next Generation Elder, [[The Crow Goddess|the Morrigan]]. During the meeting, he learns that the Flamel and his allies are located in the Shadowrealm of an Elder known as [[Hecate|Hekate]]. Although Dee is worried that they will not be able to defeat Hekate by themselves, the Morrigan is confident, stating that she knows where a powerful Elder, [[Bastet]], is located. After Dee and the Morrigan enlist the help of Bastet, they invade Hekate's shadowrealm. While Hekate is preoccupied with awakening Sophie Newman, Bastet and the Morrigan gather their forces (crows, cats, cat-human hybrids), and Dee gathers some magical items. The trio attack the center of Hekate's shadowrealm, a seed of the [[Yggdrasil]]. Hekate's [[Torc Allta]] clash with the forces assembled to defeat them, and Nicholas, Scathach, and Josh Newman watch the battle unfold. Dee surprises Nicholas and Scathach by freezing a wereboar into solid ice with his sword, [[Excalibur]]. Meanwhile, Hekate has finished awakening Sophie, and attacks the remaining enemy forces. Realizing that they will lose the battle unless he acts otherwise, Dee plunges Excalibur into the Yggdrasil, destroying it, Hekate, Hekate's shadowrealm, and several other adjourning shadowrealms. As Dee and his allies escape, Bastet points out that they have to continue to hunt Flamel and the twins, seeing as they had escaped as well. They locate them near [[Ojai]], California, trying to persuade the [[Zephaniah|Witch of Endor]] to teach Sophie [[Air magic]]. On the way to Ojai, Dee discovers the prophecy about the mystical twins with gold and silver auras (i.e. Josh and Sophie), fated to either save or destroy the world. He tempts Josh to join him, while using necromancy to raise thousands of corpses to assault Witch of Endor, Flamel, Scathach, and Sophie with. Josh almost agrees, but at the last moment he realizes he will lose Sophie if he agrees, and attempts to hit Dee with a Hummer, distracting the necromancer to allow him to escape with Scathach, Sophie and Nicholas by using a leygate, opened by the Witch of Endor, to escape to [[Paris]]. The Witch of Endor then destroys the leygate and forces Dee to flee when he is slightly injured.

### The Magician
Dee spends most of his time in [[The Magician]] trapped in [[Ojai]], after [[Nicholas Flamel|Nicholas]], [[Scathach]], [[Sophie Newman|Sophie]], and [[Josh Newman|Josh]] escape through the leygate. Much of his time is spent waiting for his flight to [[Paris]]. He expresses anger when he finds out that his old rival Niccolò [[Niccolo Machiavelli|Machiavelli]] is being sent to capture the twins while he is preoccupied. Later, he receives a call from his Dark Elder Master informing him that Flamel, Scathach, and the twins are located inside the home of Comte Saint Germain, a master of fire magic. His masters also orders Dee to work with Machiavelli to obtain the last two pages of the Codex. When Dee finally arrives in Paris, Machiavelli informs him of his plan to unleash the monster [[Nidhogg]] upon Saint Germain's house. Dee calls his plan insane, but is reassured that everything will go according to plan and that the three [[Disir]] will remain in control of Nidhogg after it destroys their enemies. When Nidhogg flees from Saint Germain's house with Scathach after being wounded by Josh Newman, Machiavelli and Dee chase them down. They witness Josh destroy Nidhogg with the elemental sword of Fire, [[Clarent]], and the stone monster plunges into the Seine. Dee approaches Josh and convinces him to join them to find an Elder who will awaken Josh's powers. The trio then escape into the catacombs underneath Paris, where they find the Elder, [[Mars Ultor]]: He has been cursed by the [[Zephaniah|Witch of Endor]] so that his aura is forever hardening into stone over his body. While Mars is awakening Josh, Dee fights off Flamel and [[Joan of Arc]] with Machiavelli. Mars then becomes infuriated with Sophie after learning that she possesses the knowledge of how to free him from his stone imprisonment, yet refuses to release him. Dr. Dee then uses alchemy to encase Mars in the liquified bones of the Catacombs after being threatened by the enraged Elder, and escapes with the others. Dee and Machiavelli then ambush Flamel, the Twins, Joan of Arc, and Saint Germain outside Notre Dame by animating thousands of gargoyles and grotesque. Dee then watches as the twins combine their powers to destroy the statues and escape. An infuriated Dee enlists the help of the many demons, creatures, [[Elders]], and immortal humans within [[London]] to track down the group.

#### The Sorceress
In [[The Sorceress]], Dee does not appear until later in the story, but is mentioned several times. When the twins are talking to [[William Shakespeare]], it is revealed that Dee was once his mentor, but accidentally killed Shakespeare's son, Hamlet, during the awakening process (believing him to be one of the legendary twins). Dee is later seen arriving in [[London]], where he learns that [[Nicholas Flamel|Flamel]] and the twins are hiding in [[Palamedes]]'s castle. He meets the Archon, [[Cernunnos]] the Horned God, and is in awe of its power, but quickly theorizes that Cernunnos possesses little or no auric abilities. They, along with [[Bastet]], attack Palamedes's junkyard fortress with Cernunnos's [[Wild Hunt]]. During the battle, Dee wields [[Excalibur]] and douses the wall of fire, allowing the Wild Hunt to attack the twins and the immortals; however, even with the Sword of Ice, Dee is repelled by [[Josh Newman|Josh]], who uses [[Clarent]], the Sword of Fire. Flamel and the twins later escape from the battle, and Dee and the rest of the Wild Hunt are attacked by creatures created by Shakespeare. Dee is later on the run, afraid to ask his Dark Elder masters for help because of the potentially lethal consequences for him. He later enlists the help of Cernunnos again, in exchange for Clarent and Excalibur.In actuality, Dee only agrees because he believes he can kill the Archon with the Twin Blades and obtain its millennia of knowledge. They corner Flamel and the twins as they are learning Water [[Magic]] from the longest-living immortal human, [[Gilgamesh]]. As Flamel and the twins attempt to flee, Josh tries to stop Dee from attacking them by throwing Clarent directly at him. Dee throws up a shield made of his aura, but the sword is too powerful and knocks him unconscious. Josh is distracted by the outbreak of the rest of the Wild Hunt to retrieve the sword, so it remains beside Dee. When Dee regains consciousness, he finds that Clarent is right beside him. He claims the sword along with Excalibur, which he kept under his coat, and laughs hysterically with delight as he finally has both swords. The swords then fuse together to form one sword. Gilgamesh then appears and when he sees the sword, he touches it, repeating a part of the prophecy, "The two that are one, the one that is all".

#### The Necromancer
Dee has now been declared a [[Utlaga]] (outlaw) by his Dark Elder masters and every creature in [[London]] who he had called upon to hunt [[Nicholas Flamel|Flamel]] has now turned against him. He does not dare to use his aura to ward off enemies in fear of drawing his powerful Elder masters. He meets up with [[Virginia Dare]], one of a very small number of Immortals to ever kill their Elder masters. Dee is attacked by [[Cucubuths]], but Dare saves him with the flute; the pair are then attacked by [[Odin]]'s (an Elder) ravens, who seeks revenge for the destruction of his Shadowrealm. The two immortals enter an abandoned Shadowrealm in [[Traitor's Gate]] Pool, and Dee learns the ultimate secret of the [[Swords of Power]]: Combined, [[Clarent]] and [[Excalibur]] can create a leygate, which he uses to take him and Virginia to [[San Francisco]]. With this secret in his hands, Dee formulates a master plan: He will create a leygate using the Swords to [[Coatlicue]]—most powerful of all the [[Archons]]—in her Shadowrealm, and draw her forth to Earth. He will send her to [[Xibalba]], which can lead her to every Shadowrealm; as she rampages throughout the Myriad [[Shadowrealms]], destroying all [[Dark Elders]], Dee will cut off all leygates leading to earth, ruling as an emperor. All he needs to do is appease Coatlicue by feeding her a pure Gold aura (a.k.a. [[Josh Newman|Josh]]). Dee calls in a favor with Mars, who controls Josh since he Awakened him. The Elder hypnotizes the boy and leads him to Dee, who convinces Josh to raise Coatlicue by tricking him into believing the Archon can teach him [[Necromancy]]. Coatlicue is raised, and Dee places a spell on Josh that makes him believe the Archon is a beautiful woman; as Dee is about to feed Josh to the Archon and achieve his plan, [[Niten]], [[Sophie Newman|Sophie]], and [[Aoife]] ruin by saving Josh. However, the boy remains on his side, thinking Sophie has been turned evil by Flamel. Dee is still the most powerful Immortal on the planet as he possesses all four Swords of Power, the Book of [[Abraham the Mage]], and the Gold Twin, as he exits the burning building.

#### The Warlock
With the aid of [[Josh Newman]], Dee escape across San Fransico even while being chased by Sack Men sent by Hel. They manage to get to [[Treasure Island]], where they steal a boat to get them to [[Alcatraz]]. In the bay, they are attacked by [[Nereids]] (vicious mermaid daughters of [[Nereus]]), and are only saved by Josh's quick thinking. Dee then creates another master plan: He will release the monsters into the city as planned, creating a diversion for his Elder masters to watch. While they are preoccupied, he will create a leygate 10,000 years into the past to [[Danu Talis]]. Once there, he will ensure all [[Elders]] died when the island sank, making him the ruler of the world. When [[Niccolo Machiavelli|Machiavelli]] and Billy reveal they intend to go against their masters, Virginia knocks them unconscious and proceeds to unleash the monsters for Dee. Dee, Virginia and Josh then get cornered by [[Sophie Newman|Sophie]], Mars, Hel, and [[Odin]], so he creates a portal with the Four [[Swords of Power]], and Virginia, Josh and Sophie journey back to a changeable timeline on Danu Talis before it sank. On the island, he is confronted by his Elder masters, [[Isis]] and [[Osiris]], who also turn out to be Josh and Sophie's parents.

#### The Enchantress
As Dee faces his Elder Masters, his immortality is stripped, leaving him a withering old man. On the verge of death, his health is restored by [[Marethyu]], and he is taken to see [[Abraham the Mage]]. On the flight there, Marethyu explains he is not truly alive and that Dee inadvertently made him immortal. The Mage talks with Dee, rebuking him for his cruelty and arrogance, and gives him a jade tablet meant for [[Virginia Dare]]. On the streets of [[Danu Talis]], Dee meets Virginia and gives her the tablet, which sparks her to start a revolt. He becomes an advisor to Virginia, stating that he has come back full circle, becoming an advisor to a queen, who is, in this case, Virginia. He uses the last of his aura to support Virginia in redirecting the anpu's arrows fired upon a crowd of helpless humans. His aura completely depleted, he then dies with [[Scathach]] and Virginia at his side, the freed [[Aten]] at his feet. Dee had regained his humanity right before he died since he realized what it truly meant to be human once he lost his immortality. Through his actions, (revealing the power of the Four Swords to [[Josh Newman|Josh]]), Josh gets a hook on his hand, which is a mix of all 4 ancient swords and becomes Marethyu.

### Appearance
John Dee is a small, neat, well dressed Englishman. He dresses in a classical style, that hints at being old-fashioned but not out of place. His hair is long and iron gray, yet neatly kept in a ponytail, and he has a small, pointy beard.

### Personality
Dee has been seen as a heartless, cold, ruthless man willing to do and sacrifice anything to achieve his goals. It is mentioned several times by [[Nicholas Flamel]] that Dee caused many catastrophic events throughout history such as the Great [[London]] Fire or the Earthquake of Tokyo; killing many innocent people. Dee has also been very manipulative, such as when he tricked [[Josh Newman]] into summoning [[Coatlicue]], using a spell to make her seem beautiful, and earlier to get Josh Awakened by [[Mars Ultor]], getting [[William Shakespeare]] to awaken his children despite the dangers, and getting [[Virginia Dare]] to join him by promising her that they would rule the Earth and all the myriad [[Shadowrealms]]. It is noted that Dee was very analytical, being able to survive for a long time in Queen Elizabeth I's court and serving as her adviser, astronomer and astrologer. However, even at such times, Dee still had a level of humanity, as he himself noted he cried when his wives and sons died. After being stripped of his [[Immortality]], Dee's humanity grew far more prevalent. He came to regret how ruthless he was and how he was not a good husband or father to many of his spouses and children. He even sacrificed himself to save what Virginia was trying to build.

### Powers & Abilities
Over the course of the books, Dee has been seen as an extremely powerful [[Magician (Flamel)|Magician]], Necromancer, and Alchemyst. He has displayed a variety of powerful abilities and is respected worldwide as a formidable practitioner of the magical arts and has been mentioned by many powerful beings as not wanting to make an enemy of him.
[[Elders]] and Immortals often underestimate Dee due to his small stature, but he is truly a formidable opponent. He has killed the extremely powerful Elder [[Hecate|Hekate]] and beaten [[Mars Ultor]] by trapping him in bone. When [[Niten]] attempted to kill him, the Japanese Swordsman's "unbreakable swords" shattered upon contact. [[Isis]] and [[Osiris]] were terrified at the prospect of Dee returning to [[Danu Talis]] because they thought he truly had the power to wipe out all the Elders.
[[Magic]] Mastery: Dee has displayed that he is incredibly powerful in the ways of magic. [[Nicholas Flamel]] has stated that he is infinitely more powerful than himself, and he is widely known by many to be one of the most dangerous and powerful men on the planet. He has mastered several magical arts throughout his vast life. Through magic, his powers include the following:
- Legendary swords: Has wielded a sword of power.
- Awakened Senses: Dr. John Dee has been awakened, or has learned to harness the power of their aura. He has significantly sharper senses, better reflexes, and greater stamina than most humani.
- Aura Manipulation: Dr. John Dee has learned to control his aura to a certain extent. He is able to shape the aura around his body, and uses it to power his magic. He is capable of mystically manipulating vast quantities of energy, including casting energy spears.
- Polyglottism: One of the many advantages of a long life is having the opportunity to travel the world and learn many languages. Dr. John Dee is fluent in {{{languages known}}}.
- [[Immortality]]: Dr. John Dee gained Immortality from his Dark Elder masters, and therefore does not age (though he is vulnerable to injury and poison). Unless his masters choose to take his immortality away, Dr. John Dee may live for thousands or millions of years.
Alchemyst
- Transmutation: With preparation, Alchemysts can transform one substance into another. Alchemysts can reshape or restructure atoms and molecules, and can even turn lead into gold.
- Transformation: Alchemysts can alter the configuration of substances on a chemical or physical level. Large objects can be easily moulded into new shapes, or transmogrified into a new allotrope or state of matter. He used this power to trap Mars Ultor in solid bone.
- Biological manipulation: Alchemysts can tap into the very essence of life, and can manipulate DNA to modify or enhance organic matter.
- Elixir of Life: Through careful research, substances capable of extending the human lifespan may be created. Some recipes - such as the Philosopher's Stone - are only temporary, but permanent elixirs may exist.
- Telekinesis: Many alchemysts can use their control over alchemy to influence objects at a distance.
- Disintegration: As shown in the Alchemyst and mentioned in the Sorceress, he has the ability to cause things to turn to dust by touching them or causing someone to explode by overloading their Aura.
- Photokinesis: Dee is able to create a very large flash of light to blind his enemies.
- Telekinesis: He was able to shatter a window telekinetically.
- Clairvoyance: He was able to see things through [[Scrying]].
- Animal Manipulation: Dee is able to manipulate a swarm of rats from miles away.
- Animation & Deanimation: He was capable of creating and animating [[Golems]] and animating the gargoyles of Notre Dame.
- Illusion-Casting: He can create incredibly lifelike illusions, such as how he changed [[Coatlicue]]'s appearance in [[Josh Newman|Josh]]'s mind.
- [[Pyrokinesis]]: He was able to create a solid wall of flame.
- Solid Energy Constructs: Dee is able to create things, such as a sword, out of energy.
Magician
- Practitioner of the Magical Arts: The powers wielded by magicians are never clearly distinguished from those of a Sorcerer or from other magic users, but known Magicians are able to create servants such as Golems and [[Tulpa]], and are able to use Scrying.
Necromancer
- [[Necromancy]]: Does whatever it is necromancers do. Dee has been shown to be a master of Necromancy, being able to call forth the spirit of an Elder and raise thousands of dead animals and people to serve him in [[Ojai]]

### Allies
- Niccolò [[Niccolo Machiavelli|Machiavelli]]: Forced to work together throughout the course of [[The Magician]], they developed a grudging respect for one another.
- The [[The Crow Goddess|Morrigan]]: The Morrigan and Dr John Dee have been able to help each other but Dee doesn't trust her. He even threatened to kill her to get an answer.
- [[Bastet]]: Bastet's relationship with Dee is similar to the one he has with the Morrigan. They have been forced to work together in order to capture the twins, but they both don't trust each other.
- [[Virginia Dare]]: Former lover, because he was in need of allies, Dee went in search of her to aid in his quest to destroy the [[Dark Elders]]. Helped him summon [[Coatlicue]] and capture [[Josh Newman]].

### Enemies
- [[Nicholas Flamel]]: Dr. John Dee and Nicholas Flamel have not always been enemies. Their relationship began with Dee being an apprentice to Flamel, however the [[Dark Elders]] got to Dee and turned him against Flamel. He tried to take the Codex but failed and has chased them around the globe for centuries, every encounter a failure. Until he finally succeeds in capturing the Codex.
- Perenelle Flamel: Dislikes him for turning against her and her husband and chasing him and her across the world, as well as imprisoning her inside [[Alcatraz]].
- [[Scathach]]: Dislikes him for turning against the Flamels.
- [[Zephaniah|Witch of Endor]]: Hates him for attacking her home in [[Ojai]] and desecrating the bones of its citizens.
- [[Odin]]: Hates him for killing [[Hecate|Hekate]], his lover.
- Dark Elders (as a majority): Dislikes him for repeatedly failing to kill the Flamels, capturing the twins, and recovering the final two pages of the Codex.
- [[Mars Ultor]]: Hates him for imprisoning him in a solid tomb of bone by the use of [[Alchemy]] Transmutation Spell underneath [[Paris]].
- [[Niccolo Machiavelli|Machiavelli]]: Mutual dislike and grudging respect (when he had recorded what Dee said at the end of Book 2, chapter 54).

### Abilities
Dee is a master manipulator. While not nearly on the same level as [[Niccolo Machiavelli|Machiavelli]], he comes pretty close, as he was able to convince both [[Virginia Dare]] and [[Josh Newman]] to join him and he owns his own personal company, [[Enoch Enterprises]], implying that he is also a very shrewd business man. He is also a very proficient linguist, able to speak hundreds of languages fluently, as well as a proficient swordsman. While not on the same level as [[Niten]], he was able to hold his own against him for a short time.

### Equipment
[[Excalibur]]: The famed Sword of Ice, Dee wields it with deadly force. He used it in the Fight of [[Yggdrasil]] and to kill [[Hecate|Hekate]] through the destruction of Yggdrasil. He also used it in the siege on Shakespeare's realm to fight against [[Josh Newman|Josh]], who was wielding [[Clarent]], the sword of Fire.
Clarent: The corrupting sword of Fire. As of the third book, Dee has had possession of this sword and used it with as much deadly proficiency as Excalibur.
Codex: The ancient book of power written by [[Abraham the Mage]], Dee stole this in the Alchemyst and has kept it to himself for the entire series. He has it but not the last two pages, the final summoning.
[[Joyeuse]]: The sword of Earth. Another one of the legendary swords that Dee has had possession of over the years.
[[Durendal]]: The sword of Wind. Another one of the legendary swords that Dee has had possession of over the years.
Cell-phone: Dee possesses a cell-phone that he uses frequently in the series to call allies. It's ringtone is the theme from X-Files.
