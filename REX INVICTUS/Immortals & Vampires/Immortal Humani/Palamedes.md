## Real-World Background (IRL)

Sir Palamedes is a Saracen knight of the Round Table in Arthurian romance, who pursues the Questing Beast and loves Iseult.

### Appearance
Palamedes is described as very tall and built "like a linebacker". He has a smooth shaven head, with brown eyes, brown skin, and very white teeth. On each cheek he has three short, horizontal scars.

### Abilities
- [[Immortality]]: Palamedes gained Immortality from his Elder master, and therefore does not age (though he is vulnerable to injury and poison). Unless his master chooses to take his immortality away, Palamedes may live for thousands or millions of years.

#### The Sorceress
Palamedes is recruited by his friend Saint-Germain to bring [[Nicholas Flamel|Nicholas]] and the Twins to [[Gilgamesh]]. He brings Flamel and the twins to his lair in [[London]], where they are pursued by [[Cernunnos]] and the [[Wild Hunt]]. After the battle, he takes the group to Gilgamesh the King and aids them in [[Stonehenge]].

#### The Necromancer
In [[The Necromancer]], Palamedes goes with [[William Shakespeare]] and Saint Germain to [[Tammuz]] for help to save Joan and [[Scathach]]. When he arrives at [[Marethyu]]'s Shadowrealm, he is recruited alongside the Shadow, [[Joan of Arc]] and her husband, as well as William Shakespeare, to go back to the isle of [[Danu Talis]] and save the [[Humani]] from the Island's destruction.

### Relationships
Palamedes is good friends with [[William Shakespeare]]. He is also friends with Gabriel, who leads the ratchets (also called the werewolves or the [[Torc Madra]]).
He is also said to have loved Iseult, but Iseult loved Tristram, who also loved her.
He doesn't like [[Nicholas Flamel]] much, saying that Flamel could have hidden somewhere remote so that people wouldn't be hurt by [[Dr. John Dee|Dee]]'s attempts to kill him (the Great Fire of [[London]], famine and plague in Ireland, etc.). However, he helps Flamel and the twins and takes a large amount of risk for them.
His master is the Green Man; however, they are not on good terms. [[Tammuz|The Green Man]] made him immortal as punishment and cursed him to wander the [[Shadowrealms]] but he gave Palamedes the ability to travel through Shadowrealms without being affected by their alternate times.
He is also good friends with the [[Comte de Saint-Germain]].
Note: Palamedes and his comrades fought with [[King Arthur]] and saw him fall to [[Cernunnos]] and the Coward's Blade.

### Trivia
