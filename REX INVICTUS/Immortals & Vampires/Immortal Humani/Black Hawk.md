## Real-World Background (IRL)

Black Hawk (Ma-ka-tai-me-she-kia-kiak, 1767–1838) was a Sauk war leader who fought the United States in the Black Hawk War of 1832 and dictated one of the first Native American autobiographies.

Ma-ka-tai-me-she-kia-kiak, more commonly known as Black Hawk, is a Sauk Indian chief and warrior born in the late 1700s on North American soil. The Elder Quetzalcoatl later made him an immortal. He aids [[Billy the Kid]] and Niccolò [[Niccolo Machiavelli|Machiavelli]].

### History
It is mentioned that he knows [[Niten]] from the Battle of the Greasy Grass. Niten stated it as "a day for warriors." Black Hawk also once tried to kill [[Billy the Kid]], but they were still friends even then.
Black Hawk appears in [[The Necromancer]], sent by his Dark Elder master to help Billy and [[Niccolo Machiavelli|Machiavelli]] release the monsters from [[Alcatraz]]. He later appears in [[The Enchantress]], helping [[Nicholas Flamel|Nicholas]] and Perenelle defeat the monsters. While fighting a giant crab, Black Hawk got knocked into the water. It is unsure whether he survived the encounter because, on the one hand, the book stated: "And they all knew that the [[Nereids]] were waiting in the water," but later on pointing out that the Nereids had left after Black Hawk killed [[Nereus]]. He’s later confirmed alive in [[The Lost Stories Collection]] in a 512-bit encrypted email from Niccolo Machiavelli to Billy the Kid. It is also revealed in the previous encrypted email from Billy to Machiavelli that Black Hawk's first cooperation with Billy, Black Hawk's true allegiance was always an agent of [[Marethyu]].

### Abilities
- [[Immortality]]: Black Hawk gained Immortality from his Dark Elder master, and therefore does not age (though he is vulnerable to injury and poison). Unless his master chooses to take his immortality away, Black Hawk may live for thousands or millions of years.
