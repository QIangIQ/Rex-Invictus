## Real-World Background (IRL)

King Arthur is the legendary British king of Camelot and the Round Table, first named in Welsh sources and shaped by Geoffrey of Monmouth (1136) and Thomas Malory's *Le Morte d'Arthur* (1485).

King Arthur was a legendary humani who was notable for pulling [[Excalibur]] from its stone. He also wielded its legendary twin, Clarent, which was later used by Mordred (Aurthur's son) to kill the King; this deed earned the blade's nickname of the "Coward's Blade".  He was also one of the few humani to have a pure gold aura.

### Trivia
- King Arthur was said to be very close to [[Gilgamesh]]. Following Arthur's death, Gilgamesh's sanity began to suffer.
- [[Scathach]] claimed to have played an instrumental role in his ascent to Kinghood, though she later regarded this as a mistake.
- [[Cernunnos]] claimed to have killed both Arthur and Mordred
