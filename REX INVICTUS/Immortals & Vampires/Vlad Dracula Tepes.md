## Definition

**[[Vlad Dracula Tepes]]** is a **[[Vampire|Vampire Progenitor]]**, reigning **Vampire King**, and one of the oldest extant **[[Primordial|Primordial Immortal Entities]]**, predating or rivaling early [[God|God-Class Entities]]. He is the foundational origin of the majority of modern [[Vampire|Vampire Lineages]], and his existence represents a continuity of a far older, pre-divine power structure that once rivaled the dominant entities of ancient eras.

## Origin & Existence

[[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] is not the original mortal figure associated with the title “Impaler,” but rather a **[[Primordial|Progenitor Entity]]** that subsumed and integrated that identity, selectively retaining its characteristics. This results in a hybridized existence:

- Simultaneously:
    - Original and non-original
    - Singular and composite
- Comparable to:
    - [[Divine Single Event Effects (DSEE)|Identity Overlay Phenomena]]
    - Multi-Form Entity Structures

His lineage and species are implied to originate from **extra-planetary systems**, with independent evolutionary and cultural histories separate from Earth-based [[Divine Being|Divine Hierarchies]].

Vlad Dracula Tepes is an Ancient Vampire, one of the surviving Vampire Progenitors, patriarch of the [[Tepes Clan]], and the long-reigning King of [[Nightingale]]. Having existed for more than six thousand years and governed the modern Vampire Kingdom for approximately three thousand, Vlad established himself as one of the most influential supernatural rulers associated with Earth. Before the rise of [[Victor Elderblood]], he was widely regarded as the strongest known Vampire and one of the most dangerous creatures within the supernatural world. His extraordinary affinity with Blood, authority over Souls, mastery of the ancestral Vampire physiology, and immense collection of consumed Souls allowed him to challenge powerful Gods, high-ranking Demons, and other exceptional supernatural beings.[8][9][1]
Although commonly called the First Vampire Progenitor, Vlad is not the first Vampire or the first Progenitor to have existed. His Race originated long before the modern supernatural civilizations associated with Earth, and other ancient Vampire Progenitors possessed Bloodlines predating his own. Vlad's historical prominence instead derives from his success in surviving, reproducing his Bloodline, preserving his descendants, and creating a major civilization when many older Vampire lineages had disappeared or declined into isolated remnants. Before Victor's emergence, supernatural society generally referred to him simply as The Progenitor; the designation of First Progenitor became more prominent after another living Vampire Progenitor appeared.[2]
Vlad's authority over modern Vampires is both biological and political. The great majority of Nightingale's Noble Vampires descend directly or indirectly from his Blood, while the kingdom's historical structure placed the Count Clans beneath a Crown governed by the Tepes Royal Family. His existence therefore represented the surviving foundation of an entire civilization rather than merely the strongest individual occupying its throne. At the same time, the enormous influence of his Bloodline never meant that every ancient Vampire belonged to his lineage or that his commands possessed unconditional authority over other Vampire Progenitors.[4][2]
The name Vlad Dracula Tepes is not his original identity. The Ancient Progenitor lived under other names before encountering the Human ruler later remembered as Vlad Dracul Tepes. The two became friends, but their relationship ended when the Progenitor killed and consumed the Human Vlad, incorporating his Soul and characteristics into his own existence. The Progenitor subsequently adopted the dead man's identity, becoming the Vlad Dracula Tepes known throughout supernatural history. The Human ruler's younger brother, Radu Tepes, witnessed the killing, creating a grudge that endured across the following millennia.[10][11]
Vlad's relationship with Victor profoundly changed his position within supernatural society. Initially treating the newborn Vampire as a potential successor and exceptional subordinate, Vlad eventually recognized him as another Progenitor possessing an independent Bloodline and ambitions incompatible with permanent submission to Nightingale. Their rivalry repeatedly brought them into conflict, yet both gradually developed genuine respect for one another. Victor's eventual ascension as the Dragon God of [[Chaos]] and establishment of the [[Elderblood Empire]] transformed their relationship further, leaving Vlad as the ruler of a historic Vampire civilization operating within a vastly greater Imperial order.[12][13][14]
Within the Imperial Era, Vlad continued developing rather than remaining dependent upon his historical achievements. Imperial science and biological enhancement allowed him to approach the ancestral condition of his Race more completely, overcome several conventional Vampire limitations, and develop his combat power far beyond what he possessed during his reign over the old Nightingale. He eventually became one of the Elderblood Empire's elite combatants, retaining superiority over [[Tatsuya Rider]], who was identified as the second strongest Noble Vampire. Both possessed sufficient destructive capability to destroy entire Solar Systems, while Vlad was already capable of independently threatening or destroying conventional Pantheons before receiving later Imperial support.[15]

### Physical Appearance
Vlad possesses no permanently fixed appearance in the conventional sense. His mastery over his Vampire physiology allows him to alter his face, apparent age, height, hair, complexion, voice, physical proportions, and even the fundamental structure of his body. These changes are genuine transformations rather than simple visual Illusions, and his ability to reproduce the appearance of other individuals is sufficiently refined that ordinary observers cannot reliably identify his true identity through physical characteristics alone.[16]
During his first personal meeting with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Vlad initially appeared as a tall, pale-skinned man approximately 210 centimeters in height. His apparent age was around thirty, and his black hair was combed backward in a neat style. Despite his extraordinary position as the Vampire King, the appearance was relatively ordinary, resembling an unusually tall Human professional rather than a visibly supernatural monarch. The contrast between his mundane appearance and the tremendous pressure radiating from his existence reinforced the danger posed by a Being who required no elaborate physical display to inspire fear.[4][16]
Vlad subsequently demonstrated several other appearances. He covered himself in Blood and assumed the form of a medieval monarch wearing black armor, adopting the deep voice and commanding manner of an ancient warrior accustomed to leading armies. He then transformed into a blond-haired man with green eyes, resembling a holy warrior who had fought in service to a God before being betrayed. These identities reflected the enormous variety of appearances, experiences, and characteristics Vlad had accumulated throughout his existence.[16]
His shapeshifting also extends to individuals known personally by others. During the same demonstration, Vlad imitated Victor's father and [[Andrew Walter]], accurately reproducing their physical appearances and voices. The ability allows him to disguise himself as another person without depending exclusively upon conventional [[Magic]] or Illusions. His vast collection of Souls further complicates the distinction between transformation and identity, because certain individuals he consumed became incorporated into the characteristics of his own existence.[16][10]
Vlad's most historically significant adopted appearance belongs to the Human Vlad Dracul Tepes. After consuming the man, the Progenitor incorporated enough of his characteristics to adopt his name and identity. However, the novel does not explicitly establish that every medieval appearance demonstrated by Vlad represents the original Human Impaler; his transformations are sufficiently diverse that appearances should not automatically be identified with particular consumed individuals without direct confirmation.[10][11]

#### Ancient Vampire True Form
Vlad's most significant transformation is the Ancient Vampire True Form, representing the ancestral state of the Vampire Race before its ancient decline. Unlike his various humanoid disguises, this transformation expresses the original predatory morphology and supernatural nature of ancient Vampires. Vlad revealed the form during his confrontation with Yama, explaining that the modern [[Vampire Count Form]] represents an incomplete return toward the original condition of the species rather than the ultimate stage of Vampire evolution.[9]
In this state, Vlad's skin becomes greyish, his sclerae turn completely black, and his irises shine with an intense crimson glow. His ears become slightly pointed, while his mouth transforms into a lipless maw filled with sharp teeth. His hands and feet develop long, monstrous claws, and large wings composed entirely of Blood emerge from his back. Black tattoo-like patterns move across his body as though possessing lives of their own, producing an appearance radically different from the civilized monarch he ordinarily portrays.[9]
The Ancient Vampire True Form is not simply a larger or more muscular version of a normal Vampire. It represents a return toward the supernatural characteristics possessed by the ancient Race, whose original powers were sufficiently dangerous that other powerful beings united against them. Vlad can combine this transformation with his exceptional control over Blood and Souls, allowing portions of his body to dissolve, reform, extend, and change shape during combat.[9]
Vlad can also abandon a stable Humanoid shape and fight as an amorphous mass of dark crimson and black Blood. In this condition, his body can become liquid, avoid conventional physical attacks, and produce new limbs, predatory heads, eyes, and other structures as required. Even while appearing to possess no recognizable martial stance, Vlad continues performing highly refined combat movements through the flowing substance of his transformed body.[17][9]

#### Imperial-Enhanced Ancestral State
Following the enormous technological development of the [[Elderblood Empire]], Vlad received biological enhancements that advanced him closer to the ancestral or primal condition of the Vampire Race. He had already approached this state through his own development, but Imperial assistance allowed him to complete additional improvements that were previously beyond his reach. The exact physical appearance of the enhanced condition has not been described in enough detail to establish a completely separate transformation design.[14]
The enhancement also removed numerous restrictions normally associated with Noble Vampires. Vlad became capable of consuming ordinary food rather than relying exclusively upon Blood for nourishment, and most of the fundamental biological weaknesses associated with his former Vampire condition were eliminated. The complete list of removed weaknesses was not individually identified, so abilities such as unconditional immunity to every form of [[Holy Energy]], conceptual attack, or racial weakness should not automatically be attributed to the modification.[14]

### Personality
Vlad is proud, calculating, pragmatic, possessive, and extraordinarily patient, with a worldview shaped by more than six thousand years of supernatural existence and approximately three millennia of kingship. He considers strength, competence, loyalty, and long-term survival essential qualities in individuals who intend to occupy positions of authority. His immense experience allows him to analyze political conflicts from a perspective inaccessible to younger rulers, while his status as a Vampire Progenitor grants him the confidence to disregard ordinary threats. Vlad rarely acts without evaluating possible consequences, but his enormous pride and habitual emotional detachment have repeatedly caused him to underestimate problems that cannot be resolved through political influence or overwhelming strength.[8][18]
As King, Vlad understands that a civilization cannot survive merely because its ruler is individually powerful. His greatest accomplishment was establishing a stable Vampire society capable of enduring through centuries of conflict, competition between Noble Clans, and interference from foreign supernatural powers. He values capable subordinates, organized institutions, and the preservation of his Bloodline, recognizing that the survival of his descendants represents something greater than his personal reputation. Nevertheless, his success created a dangerous tendency toward complacency. After occupying the throne for thousands of years, Vlad gradually began treating his kingship as a permanent extension of his identity rather than a position he had freely chosen to maintain.[8][2]
His attachment to the throne became apparent when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] questioned why he intended to continue ruling indefinitely. Vlad had spent approximately three thousand years governing [[Nightingale]] and assumed that an immortal King should naturally continue doing so. Victor challenged the assumption by asking who had actually required Vlad to remain on the throne forever. The question forced Vlad to confront the possibility that his responsibilities had transformed into an obligation he imposed upon himself rather than something demanded by the people around him.[8]
Vlad's treatment of his children initially reflected the contradiction between his ideals and his actions. He expected the members of the Tepes Royal Family to become powerful, independent Vampires capable of defending themselves, yet his constant presence as an overwhelming source of protection allowed several of them to grow dependent upon him. Their political privileges and the security of the Royal Castle insulated them from challenges that might otherwise have forced them to develop. When Victor emerged as a young Vampire willing to challenge stronger opponents without hesitation, Vlad recognized qualities absent from several of his own descendants and began reconsidering how he had raised them.[19][8]
His domestic relationships reveal an even greater weakness. Vlad frequently neglected his wives and children while concentrating upon the responsibilities of kingship, treating their continued presence as something guaranteed by marriage, Bloodline, or political obligation. He could provide wealth, protection, and extraordinary supernatural opportunities, but often failed to provide the affection and personal attention necessary to preserve genuine relationships. When his household eventually collapsed, [[Alexios Alioth]] directly confronted him about the arrogance, emotional distance, and selfishness that had contributed to the disaster.[20][18]
Three of Vlad's earlier wives betrayed him romantically, including Stella, whose affair involved their son Theo. His response was ruthless: after uncovering the betrayals, he executed the women involved and Theo, permanently damaging his relationships with surviving children such as Lucas and Saul. Although their actions constituted betrayals within the marriage, Vlad's own failures were not ignored by those closest to him. Alexios regarded the collapse of the household as a consequence of centuries of neglect, while several of Vlad's children came to resent him for killing their mothers.[20][21][18]
[[Jeanne Elderblood]] and [[Morgana Elderblood]] followed a different path. Rather than betraying Vlad through affairs, they became unwilling to continue their marriages and eventually demanded freedom from the supernatural bonds connecting them to him. Vlad ultimately released them, demonstrating an ability to accept the dissolution of relationships even when doing so meant losing individuals whose presence had once belonged to his established household.[22]
Despite these failures, Vlad is capable of profound attachment. His relationship with [[Otsuki Hana]] was the most important romantic bond of his life, and her death left an emotional wound that persisted for an extraordinary period. When he finally confronted Shur, the Elder God responsible for her death, his habitual restraint collapsed beneath centuries of grief and hatred. Rather than approaching the battle as an ordinary military operation, Vlad sought the permanent destruction of the Being who had taken away the woman he loved most.[23]
Vlad also values loyalty extending beyond political usefulness. His relationship with Alexios originated through an ancient connection to the Alioth family but eventually became something approaching friendship and familial attachment. Alexios possesses enough personal importance to criticize Vlad openly without destroying their relationship, while Vlad trusts him with responsibilities that would be dangerous to entrust to an ordinary servant. When Alexios' body and Soul began deteriorating because of centuries of Time manipulation, Vlad was genuinely distressed by the possibility of losing him. After Victor saved Alexios, the Vampire King voluntarily bowed in gratitude, placing his friend's survival above the preservation of his pride.[1][24]
His relationship with Victor underwent a similar transformation. Vlad initially regarded the young Vampire as an exceptionally troublesome subordinate whose independence threatened the orderly political structure of Nightingale. However, he gradually recognized that Victor's determination, loyalty toward his family, and refusal to compromise his personal principles were qualities worthy of respect. Although the two repeatedly clashed over authority and political interference, Vlad eventually trusted Victor to protect members of the Tepes family without requiring contractual guarantees or the threat of retaliation.[12][13]
Vlad remains fundamentally ambitious, but he has gradually learned that ambition does not always require remaining above every other individual. The creation of the [[Elderblood Empire]] provided him with a civilization so far beyond Nightingale's former scale that attempting to compete with Victor for supreme authority would have been meaningless. Rather than obsessively seeking to reclaim superiority, Vlad became increasingly interested in the possibilities available within the Empire, including advanced technology, unfamiliar opponents, and opportunities to strengthen his own existence.[14]
His enormous age has also produced a dangerous familiarity with boredom. Vlad deliberately avoided personally experiencing the Empire's two-hundred-thousand-year accelerated developmental era, preferring to remain outside the temporal process and discover the resulting civilization afterward. Having already existed for thousands of years, he considered the excitement of encountering something unfamiliar more valuable than personally living through another vast period of controlled development. This decision was not a rejection of progress; he accepted Imperial enhancements and benefited from the resulting civilization while deliberately preserving his curiosity about its future.[14]
Despite becoming more tolerant, Vlad remains capable of ruthlessly reorganizing his family when necessary. His current marriage has not eliminated every problem associated with his earlier domestic life, and Ophis remains skeptical that his present wife genuinely loves him. Vlad himself recognizes that an immortal Progenitor possesses enough time to begin again if a family or political arrangement becomes impossible to maintain. His increased willingness to tolerate change therefore coexists with the same capacity for decisive and potentially merciless action that historically made him such a dangerous ruler.[25]

### History
Vlad was born more than six thousand years before the modern events of the story. His early existence was marked by uncertainty, danger, and an incomplete understanding of the extraordinary abilities he possessed. Although he eventually became one of the most powerful creatures associated with Earth, he was not born possessing complete mastery of his Bloodline. His early helplessness remained sufficiently memorable that the deterioration of Alexios' condition reminded him of a time when he had been unable to protect the people important to him.[1]
His species originated long before the modern supernatural order associated with Earth. Ancient Vampires possessed characteristics far beyond those ordinarily displayed by their descendants, and their original power frightened other exceptional supernatural beings enough to provoke a coalition against them. The resulting decline damaged the species and left later Vampires dependent upon incomplete expressions of their ancestral nature. The modern Vampire Count transformation represents one stage of recovering those lost characteristics.[9]
Vlad's birth name and the complete circumstances surrounding his earliest development remain unknown. Over thousands of years he adopted numerous appearances and identities, accumulating the memories, Souls, and supernatural characteristics of individuals he defeated. His exceptional Bloodline enabled him to transform Humans into Vampires and produce descendants whose existence would eventually become the foundation of modern [[Nightingale]].[16][10]
During his travels, Vlad encountered the Human ruler later remembered as Vlad Dracul Tepes. The two developed a genuine friendship, and the Human eventually requested the Progenitor's assistance when his younger brother Radu became gravely ill. Vlad saved Radu by transforming him into a Noble Vampire through his own Blood, allowing the younger brother to survive a condition that would otherwise have killed him.[10]
The friendship ended when the Progenitor killed and consumed the original Vlad. Radu witnessed the act and subsequently developed an enduring hatred toward the supernatural creature who had taken his brother's appearance and name. The Progenitor incorporated enough of the Human ruler's characteristics to adopt his identity, eventually becoming recognized as Vlad Dracula Tepes. Despite the killing, he continued regarding the Human Vlad as a friend and preserved a coffin bearing his name in a heavily protected private dimension.[10][11]
Vlad's growing Bloodline eventually became the dominant foundation of modern Vampire society. Unlike earlier Progenitors whose descendants vanished, declined, or survived only in small communities, Vlad expanded his lineage and organized the Vampires associated with Earth into a functioning civilization. Nightingale became their principal homeland, and the Tepes Royal Family assumed authority over the Noble Clans and the institutions governing their society.[2]
The Count Clans developed into the principal political Pillars supporting the kingdom. Their authority was subordinate to the Crown but sufficiently extensive to preserve meaningful internal autonomy, competition, and conflict between Noble families. Vlad's rule therefore depended upon more than the personal obedience of every Vampire. The monarchy had to maintain a balance between powerful Noble Clans, the Royal Family, and outside supernatural factions whose interests could threaten Nightingale.[4][8]
Over the centuries, Vlad also encountered and defeated exceptionally powerful supernatural opponents. His collection of consumed Souls expanded to include hundreds of thousands of individuals, with some victims possessing abilities far beyond those of ordinary Vampires. Among the most extraordinary was an Ancient Dragon whose Soul he retained and whose Dragon Breath he later reproduced during his confrontation with Yama. The event demonstrated that Vlad's Progenitor abilities allowed him to preserve dangerous characteristics obtained from defeated beings rather than relying exclusively upon the natural abilities of his Race.[9]
Vlad's longevity and success did not prevent personal tragedy. His marriages produced numerous children, but his emotional neglect gradually undermined the relationships he had created. Several wives betrayed him, others rejected their supernatural marriages, and the death of Hana left a loss he could never fully repair. His domestic failures eventually became one of the principal reasons that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s rapidly developing household appealed to members of the Tepes family who had grown dissatisfied with their biological father's distant rule.[20][18][23]

### Synopsis

#### Vampire Society
Vlad's authority was already fundamental to [[Nightingale]] when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] Walker became a Vampire. As the ruler of the Race and the individual whose Bloodline dominated the majority of Noble society, the King possessed the power to determine whether the appearance of an abnormal Vampire represented a threat, an opportunity, or a reason to intervene directly. Victor's extraordinary development immediately attracted his attention because the young man possessed characteristics that should not ordinarily coexist within a newly transformed Vampire.[3][4]
Victor's eventual arrival at the Royal Castle brought the two face to face. Vlad deliberately exposed the young Vampire to his overwhelming supernatural presence, expecting the pressure to demonstrate the difference between an inexperienced Noble and a genuine Progenitor. Victor recognized the danger but responded with curiosity and a desire to challenge the King rather than unconditional submission. His reaction distinguished him from individuals whose fear of Vlad was sufficient to suppress any thought of resistance.[4][16]
Vlad's subsequent demonstration of shapeshifting further revealed the extent of his mastery over his body. He moved between different Human appearances, reproduced familiar individuals, and assumed forms connected to his vast experiences. Victor remained interested in the capabilities being displayed rather than intimidated by the implication that the King possessed powers far beyond his own. The meeting convinced Vlad that the young Vampire possessed the temperament and potential necessary to become an exceptional member of the Race.[16]
Their conversation eventually revealed the significance of Victor's unusual Blood. His former Human condition, associated with RH-null blood, corresponded to the supernatural designation [[Sanguis Noctis Regis]], or the Blood of the Night King. Vlad possessed the same fundamental characteristic, which granted extraordinary potential for growth, adaptation, learning, and supernatural authority. The discovery explained why Victor's existence differed so drastically from ordinary converted Vampires and made the possibility of another Progenitor increasingly difficult to dismiss.[26]
Vlad ultimately recognized Victor as a Vampire Count and permitted him to establish an independent Clan. Victor selected the surname Alucard, deliberately reversing Dracula as a provocation toward the King and a declaration of his refusal to remain permanently beneath another ruler. Rather than react with simple hostility, Vlad found the audacity amusing and recognized that the young Vampire's personality could eventually produce either an extraordinary successor or a serious political problem.[27]
The young Progenitor's emergence also influenced Vlad's treatment of his own children. Having watched Victor willingly challenge a vastly superior opponent, Vlad became dissatisfied with the complacency displayed by several Royal heirs. He summoned them and criticized their lack of development, recognizing that centuries of privilege and protection had prevented some members of his family from acquiring the ambition necessary to survive as independent supernatural rulers. Adam appeared particularly promising because he retained greater potential and willingness to develop than several older members of the family.[19]

#### A New Pillar
Vlad formally announced the creation of [[Nightingale]]'s Fifth Pillar, recognizing the newly founded Clan Alucard as an additional Count family. The appointment was extraordinary because Count Clans traditionally possessed ancient Bloodlines, enormous territories, military forces, and accumulated political influence. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had existed as a Vampire for only a comparatively short period, making Vlad's decision one of the most significant departures from established Nightingale tradition in centuries.[28]
The recognition did not eliminate the existing political laws governing the Noble Clans. Vlad continued permitting Count titles to be challenged under Nightingale's established system, understanding that the hierarchy remained viable partly because ambitious Vampires possessed formal methods of seeking advancement. His willingness to acknowledge Victor therefore coexisted with a determination to preserve the broader structure of the kingdom.[29]
Victor's rapid progress eventually caused Vlad to employ a familiar strategy for controlling exceptionally troublesome Vampires. The King used Nightingale's dimensional circumstances and the difference between internal and external Time to restrict Victor's movements, leaving him separated from much of his family for a prolonged period. The approach resembled an earlier measure Vlad had employed against [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], whom he had once kept away from Nightingale for approximately twenty years after her conduct became excessively destructive.[30][31]
[[Scathach]] warned Vlad that attempting to control Victor through the same methods was dangerous. Unlike her, Victor possessed little sentimental loyalty toward Nightingale's King, and his intensely possessive nature made enforced separation from his wives especially provocative. The conflict demonstrated the difference between Vlad's established methods of political control and Victor's increasingly uncompromising approach to personal independence.[31]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s continued development and relationship with [[Roxanne Elderblood]] increasingly convinced Vlad that the young Progenitor had become far more dangerous than his political title suggested. Roxanne's existence as a World Tree provided Victor with a connection to a supernatural power whose value extended well beyond [[Nightingale]]'s conventional institutions. Vlad recognized that any attempt to eliminate or subordinate Victor could provoke consequences involving powerful Vampires, foreign supernatural factions, and Beings whose influence transcended the planet itself.[32][33]
The stability of the Tepes Royal Family collapsed when Vlad uncovered betrayals involving several of his wives and children. Among the most devastating revelations was the relationship between his first wife Stella and their son Theo. Vlad responded by killing the individuals he considered responsible, including Theo and the wives involved in the affairs. His actions preserved his authority as King but devastated the surviving family, particularly Lucas and Saul, who resented their father for executing their mothers.[20][21]
The disappearance of Ophis created another crisis. Vlad initially attempted to recover his daughter through the royal authorities, but the young Princess increasingly regarded Victor's household as a safer and more emotionally supportive environment than her father's court. Vlad eventually accepted that Ophis had chosen to remain with Victor, although the decision exposed how much the King had neglected the emotional needs of the youngest member of his family.[34]
Victor's growing status as another Vampire Progenitor further complicated the relationship. The two possessed independent Bloodlines and supernatural authority derived from their existences rather than solely from political titles. Vlad recognized that Victor could not remain permanently subordinate to the Crown, while Victor had little intention of allowing Nightingale's traditional rules to determine the future of his family.[12][8]
Vlad also demonstrated why his existence remained a threat to Gods. His authority over Souls allowed him to attack the fundamental existence of supernatural beings rather than merely their physical bodies. Individuals such as [[Tsukuyomi]] recognized that Vlad possessed the means to kill supposedly immortal Gods permanently, making him dangerous even to opponents who could ordinarily regenerate from conventional injuries.[35]
As the Royal Family's domestic conflicts continued, Jeanne and Morgana became increasingly unwilling to remain bound to Vlad. Their rejection exposed the limitations of a marriage maintained through supernatural obligation without corresponding personal attachment. Alexios also confronted Vlad about his role in destroying his own family, refusing to treat the King as blameless merely because some members of the household had betrayed him.[18]
The approaching Demonic War eventually forced Vlad to return his attention toward Nightingale's survival. He gathered the kingdom's major authorities and ordered preparations for a conflict of extraordinary scale, recognizing that the existing balance between supernatural factions was approaching collapse. Despite the domestic crisis and his increasingly complicated relationship with Victor, Vlad continued fulfilling his responsibilities as King.[36]

#### Demonic War
Vlad represented [[Nightingale]] during the Third [[Supernatural Gathering]], appearing alongside the rulers of other major supernatural civilizations. His participation reflected the historic importance of the Vampire monarchy, although [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] increasingly operated as an independent Progenitor whose decisions were not automatically subordinate to the King. Their contrasting attitudes toward authority remained apparent throughout the meeting and the events surrounding the Demonic invasion.[5]
When Demonic forces attacked Nightingale's Royal Capital, Vlad personally intervened against the invading army. He manifested the technique Ima-Ku-Roi, transforming into a dark, winged, nearly formless state and exerting control over the Blood of large numbers of enemies. The affected Demons underwent catastrophic bodily dissolution as their flesh, bones, and other structures were destroyed or converted, leaving Blood that flowed toward Vlad and was absorbed into his wings. The demonstration revealed how efficiently a Vampire Progenitor could destroy ordinary supernatural armies when their defenses were insufficient to resist his authority.[37]
The war also brought Vlad's remaining relationships with Jeanne and Morgana to a conclusion. Both women sought release from the supernatural marriages connecting them to him, and Vlad eventually severed the bonds. Although the separation ended part of the original Royal Family's structure, it also demonstrated that Vlad was beginning to accept the consequences of his treatment of the people closest to him.[22]
Vlad's relationship with Victor remained politically unstable. The two possessed competing forms of authority over Vampires, and disputes involving Nightingale's territory and individuals protected by Victor threatened to escalate into confrontation. However, both eventually recognized that an unrestricted conflict between Progenitors would endanger far more than their personal pride, preventing the disputes from developing into a destructive civil war.[38][39]
Vlad subsequently acknowledged that his title as First Vampire Progenitor did not indicate literal chronological primacy. Older Vampire Progenitors had existed before his birth, but their Bloodlines had either disappeared or failed to establish civilizations comparable to Nightingale. His own success had resulted from surviving and preserving a lineage that eventually became the dominant Vampire population associated with Earth.[2]
His confrontation with Yama became one of the clearest demonstrations of the abilities possessed by a genuine Ancient Vampire. Yama initially attempted to exploit the weaknesses traditionally associated with Vampires, preparing methods to resist direct manipulation of the Blood inside his body. Vlad responded by fighting through living Blood, abandoning a stable Humanoid shape and using refined [[Martial Arts]] despite the fluidity of his transformed body.[17][9]
As the confrontation intensified, Vlad transformed the battlefield into an enormous domain of Blood filled with emerging eyes, limbs, monstrous heads, and projections connected to the Souls accumulated throughout his life. He produced numerous Blood spikes, created clouds that rained Blood across the surrounding area, and used his supernatural control to attack from directions difficult for a conventional combatant to anticipate. His techniques combined physical destruction with attacks capable of affecting the Soul, forcing Yama to rely upon advanced Demonic defenses and assistance from [[Merlin]].[9]
Vlad then revealed the Ancient Vampire True Form and explained the history of his Race. The form represented the original condition of Vampires before ancient supernatural powers united to cripple the species. He emphasized that the modern Vampire Count transformation was merely a step toward regaining that lost ancestry, while his own body possessed characteristics far closer to the true origin of the Race.[9]
Among the Souls stored within Vlad was an Ancient Dragon he had defeated and consumed long before the battle. He manifested the Dragon's head through his living Blood and reproduced its Breath, unleashing an attack sufficiently dangerous that Merlin intervened to prevent Yama from receiving it directly. The technique demonstrated the extraordinary versatility of a Vampire Progenitor capable of preserving the powers and characteristics of beings whose Souls had become part of his collection.[9]
Vlad described the essential nature of a Vampire Progenitor as the ability to negotiate between Life and Death through Blood and Souls. Blood was not merely nourishment or a physical weapon; it was the medium through which a Progenitor interacted with the deeper existence of living beings and acquired Souls. His authority therefore differed fundamentally from the ordinary biological and elemental abilities possessed by lower-ranking Vampires.[9]

#### King Among Kings
Following the Demonic War, Vlad began reorganizing [[Nightingale]] to account for a supernatural world increasingly dominated by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s expanding faction. He recognized that restoring the previous political arrangement without modification would leave the kingdom vulnerable to a new generation of powerful beings and civilizations. His response involved strengthening Noble Vampires, encouraging competition and development, and forming relationships that would have been politically unthinkable during the earlier period of Vampire-Werewolf hostility.[40]
One major development was Vlad's decision to establish a new marriage with a woman descending from an ancient Vampire Progenitor Bloodline. The lineage had been believed lost for thousands of years, making the union both a political arrangement and a potential means of strengthening the diversity of Nightingale's surviving Vampire heritage. The woman became the current [[Vampire Queen]], bringing an additional ancient lineage into the Tepes Royal Family.[40]
Vlad also altered his traditional approach toward distributing Progenitor Blood. Rather than restricting access exclusively through birth or exceptional personal favor, he introduced a system in which sufficiently capable Vampires could earn a drop of his Blood. The arrangement encouraged greater development among his descendants and offered a path through which talented individuals could improve their Bloodlines without depending entirely upon inherited political privilege.[40]
His political attention eventually extended toward Samar. Vlad recognized that the long-established rivalry between Vampires and [[Werewolves]] had become increasingly impractical, especially when Victor was building alliances across numerous supernatural [[Races (Flamel)|Races]]. He sought opportunities to acquire influence within Werewolf society and cooperated with individuals who possessed reasons to oppose or negotiate independently of Victor's faction.[13][41]
[[Volk Fenrir]] contacted Vlad for assistance when his relationship with Tasha began collapsing. Their conversation revealed the unusual friendship between the historic Vampire and Werewolf rulers, although Vlad was considerably more experienced in political manipulation. He explained the weaknesses of Samar's divided monarchy, warned Volk about Victor's overwhelming power, and presented strategies through which the [[Lycaon|Werewolf King]] might preserve his position.[13]
Victor's faction subsequently captured seven influential Werewolves belonging to Volk's political network and transferred them into Vlad's custody. Vlad recognized the enormous opportunity and used his Progenitor-level Vampiric Charm to overcome their natural mental resistance. He placed the Alphas beneath his influence, implanted concealed instructions, and ordered them to forget the meeting before returning to their ordinary activities. [[Alexios Alioth]] used his Time Manipulation to restore their physical appearances and remove signs of captivity, allowing the operation to proceed without immediately revealing the interference.[42]
The operation demonstrated the difference between the abilities of an ordinary Noble Vampire and those of a Progenitor. Werewolves naturally possess resistance to Vampiric Charm, but that protection was insufficient against Vlad's overwhelming supernatural power. Exceptionally powerful Alphas such as Volk, Tasha, and Maya remained distinct cases whose resistance could potentially prevent the same method from working. Vlad therefore acquired influence over important figures within Samar without requiring an open military confrontation.[42]
Vlad's negotiations eventually secured approximately five hundred Werewolves for Nightingale, further weakening the historic separation between the two Races. He also continued encouraging his own descendants to become stronger rather than remain permanently sheltered. During a later [[Supernatural Gathering]], he brought [[Adam Tepes]] personally despite the dangers involved, intending for the younger Vampire to witness powerful supernatural beings and develop a more realistic understanding of the world beyond Nightingale.[43][44]
The conflict against [[New Dawn]] and the Elder Gods eventually brought Vlad into direct confrontation with Shur, the Being responsible for Hana's death. Accompanied by [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]], Vlad repeatedly attacked the Elder God's body and Soul, but Shur's unnatural immortality allowed him to recover from injuries that would ordinarily have caused permanent death. Vlad initially considered sealing his opponent because killing him remained impossible while the external source supporting his immortality continued functioning.[23]
Shur subsequently released a dangerous red Miasma capable of consuming whatever it contaminated. When Vlad's arm became infected, he immediately severed it to prevent the corruption from spreading through his body. He continued supporting Haruna with Blood constructs and defensive barriers while attempting to find an opportunity to end the battle.[23]
The confrontation changed when Shur's source of immortality began failing. Haruna unleashed a powerful attack that exploited the resulting weakness, and Vlad immediately abandoned the objective of sealing the Elder God in favor of killing him permanently. He impaled Shur and tore apart both his physical body and Soul through his Progenitor authority, preventing his enemy from returning through ordinary regeneration or reincarnation.[23]
The victory carried extraordinary personal significance. Vlad had pursued revenge for Hana across an enormous portion of his life, and the destruction of Shur finally ended the unresolved grievance. He shed bloody tears while remembering his wife, revealing the emotional intensity concealed beneath the ordinarily calculating behavior of Nightingale's King.[23]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Following the destruction of [[New Dawn]], [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] investigated the history of [[Niklaus Horseman]] and the identity of Radu Tepes. Vlad revealed that Radu was not his biological brother but the younger brother of the original Human Vlad Dracul Tepes. The Vampire Progenitor had transformed Radu into a Noble Vampire at his friend's request, only for their relationship to collapse when Radu later witnessed the Progenitor consume his brother.[10]
The explanation provided important insight into the limitations of Vlad's Soul consumption. A Vampire Progenitor could obtain memories and characteristics from consumed Souls, but complete integration required exceptional circumstances and involved risks that differed from Victor's more advanced predatory abilities. Vlad had consumed the original Human Vlad deeply enough to incorporate his identity, demonstrating that Souls could become part of a Progenitor's personal existence rather than remaining merely stored resources.[10]
Vlad also explained that his control over Blood differed from Victor's. Although he possessed extraordinary authority over Blood in his immediate surroundings and could manipulate it on a tremendous scale, he could not automatically sense and command his Blood across distant dimensions in the same manner as Victor. The difference established that shared Progenitor ancestry did not guarantee identical manifestations of supernatural abilities, and that Victor's development had produced characteristics outside the conventional limits of Vampire Progenitors.[10]
After the conversation, Vlad entered a heavily protected private dimension containing several coffins. One bore the name of the original Human Vlad Dracul Tepes, whom the Progenitor continued remembering as a friend. His preservation of the coffin demonstrated that consuming the man's Soul had not erased the significance of their earlier relationship, even though the circumstances of his death had created the hatred motivating Radu's subsequent actions.[11]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Vlad continued ruling [[Nightingale]] while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] expanded his faction into an increasingly powerful civilization. The old Vampire King remained deeply attached to the society he had built, but the rise of the [[Elderblood Empire]] made it increasingly clear that Nightingale no longer occupied the highest position in the regional supernatural hierarchy. Victor's authority extended across planets, Pantheons, and territories that previously operated independently, while the capabilities of his family surpassed those of many of Earth's most powerful ancient beings.[1][14]
A major personal crisis arose when [[Alexios Alioth]] began dying from the accumulated effects of manipulating Time throughout his life. The deterioration threatened both his body and Soul, reminding Vlad of his inability to save the First Alioth thousands of years earlier. Despite all the supernatural strength he had acquired, Vlad could not independently repair the damage without risking the loss of the person Alexios had become.[1]
Natalia attempted to save Alexios through her authority over Time, but the process risked transforming him into something resembling a Time Elemental rather than preserving his original identity. Victor intervened, repaired the underlying problem, and transformed Alexios into a superior Human state while preserving his memories, personality, and individuality.[24]
Vlad's response surprised everyone present. The normally arrogant Progenitor voluntarily bowed his head to Victor and thanked him sincerely for saving Alexios. [[Scathach]] and Natalia were shocked to witness someone so proud lower himself in gratitude, while Victor recognized that Vlad's attachment to Alexios extended far beyond that of an ordinary ruler toward a useful servant.[24]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
During the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s enormous accelerated developmental era, Vlad deliberately remained outside the primary temporal ritual. He had already lived for more than six thousand years and did not wish to experience another two hundred thousand years merely to witness technological and social progress gradually. Instead, he preferred allowing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s civilization to develop before personally exploring the results. Only a comparatively short period passed from his perspective while Imperial society underwent extraordinary changes.[14]
By Imperial Year 100,000, [[Nightingale]] and the wider Empire had changed beyond anything Vlad once considered possible. Watching the massive Imperial military parade alongside Alexios, Vlad witnessed forces possessing levels of power and technology that would have allowed him to conquer the old supernatural world repeatedly without meaningful resistance. Rather than resent the development, he became increasingly excited by the existence of enemies dangerous enough to justify the Empire's unprecedented military preparations.[14]
Vlad himself had benefited from Imperial technology. His body became stronger, his ancestral Vampire characteristics developed further, and numerous traditional limitations of his Race were removed. He could consume ordinary food rather than depending exclusively upon Blood, while additional biological improvements supported his continued advancement. His growth demonstrated that the Empire's benefits were not restricted to Victor's immediate family or to individuals who abandoned their original species.[14]
The political transformation was equally significant. Vlad understood that the Elderblood Empire possessed effective control over Nightingale and the World Tree supporting its existence, while he continued ruling largely because Victor respected his authority and the civilization he had preserved. Their relationship had become one of genuine friendship and mutual respect, with Vlad increasingly willing to acknowledge the achievements of the man he had originally imagined as a potential successor.[14]
Vlad also began reconsidering his relationship with Ophis. His daughter had developed into an immensely powerful Dragon Goddess and Imperial Legion Commander, possessing a degree of independence impossible for him to deny. Although the prospect of her eventually marrying Victor remained uncomfortable, Vlad recognized that Ophis genuinely wanted the relationship and no longer considered himself entitled to determine her future simply because he was her biological father.[14]
His personal development did not completely resolve the problems surrounding his household. Ophis remained suspicious of the current [[Vampire Queen]] and believed that the woman's apparent restraint resulted primarily from fear of Victor and concern about repeating the mistakes of earlier members of Vlad's family. She regarded the household as less stressful than before but did not consider it an example of the affectionate and stable family life she had found among the Elderbloods.[25]
Vlad's continued development also affected his standing within the Vampire Race. [[Tatsuya Rider]] became extraordinarily powerful despite remaining a Noble Vampire rather than transforming into a True Dragon. However, Vlad retained superiority over him, remaining the strongest of the two. Both were recognized among the Elderblood Empire's elite combatants and possessed sufficient destructive power to destroy entire Solar Systems. Vlad's later enhancements further strengthened an individual who had already been capable of single-handedly destroying conventional Pantheons.[15]
As the Empire prepared to incorporate the smaller Sectors associated with Nightingale, Samar, and Arcane, Vlad received a personal visit from Ophis. Nightingale had developed into a much larger and more prosperous world, with the Royal Capital retaining its traditional Gothic architecture while supporting a population composed of Vampires and numerous other supernatural [[Races (Flamel)|Races]]. Shadow Demons, Dark Elves, [[Ghosts]], Specters, and ordinary Humans moved through the capital, reflecting the integration of species that had once existed within separate or hostile societies.[45]
The technological transformation of Vampire society was also visible in ordinary life. Vampires were no longer restricted to drinking Blood for nourishment and could consume conventional food, while Imperial laws prohibited the predatory practices that had previously characterized portions of supernatural society. Nightingale's economy and infrastructure had expanded enormously, with the territories of its major Noble families benefiting from their connections to Victor's household.[45]
Ophis found Vlad inside his private quarters playing video games while wearing a headset. The ancient monarch was sufficiently absorbed in a competitive match that he failed to notice his daughter's arrival, despite the enormous powers ordinarily available to him. When Ophis announced her presence, Vlad immediately abandoned the game, restored the room's dignity, and attempted to appear as the imposing King she remembered. His behavior revealed a comparatively relaxed side of the Progenitor, whose private interests were no longer restricted to government and supernatural conflict.[45]
Ophis informed him that Nightingale would soon participate in the merger with Victor's greater Sector. Vlad accepted the announcement and asked whether any preparations were required. Ophis instructed him to keep the population calm until the ritual was completed, prompting Vlad to order Alexios to coordinate the necessary arrangements. His response demonstrated that, despite his extraordinary pride, he now treated major Imperial initiatives as legitimate developments requiring cooperation rather than challenges to his personal authority.[45]
The merger subsequently began under Victor's direct supervision. The smaller Sectors associated with Nightingale, Samar, and Arcane were drawn toward the Elderblood Empire's central Sector through an extraordinary manipulation of the surrounding cosmic structure. Vlad's kingdom consequently became part of a vastly larger astronomical arrangement rather than remaining isolated within its former region of existence.[46]
Victor reorganized the newly integrated Galactic territories to prevent destructive interactions between celestial systems and provide stable conditions for their development. Because Nightingale and Samar possessed unusual characteristics, both planets remained within their respective Solar Systems rather than being relocated independently. Victor moved Nightingale's system into a protected region of the Empire and positioned Samar's system several hundred light-years away, creating a secure central area accessible only after passing through multiple layers of Imperial defense.[47]
The integration marked another transformation in Vlad's historical role. His reign had begun within a civilization whose principal concerns involved survival, territorial disputes between Noble Clans, and the dangers posed by Earth's supernatural factions. Nightingale now existed within a Galactic Empire whose ruler could reorganize entire Solar Systems and Sectors, while Vlad remained responsible for preserving the Vampire civilization whose foundation he had established thousands of years earlier.[47]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Vlad's fellow Vampire Progenitor, former political subordinate, longtime rival, and eventual friend. Their relationship began when Vlad recognized the extraordinary potential of the young Vampire and granted him the position of Count. Victor's independent personality immediately complicated the arrangement, since he refused to accept that the King possessed an inherent right to dictate every aspect of his life. Their conflicts over political authority became increasingly serious as Victor's Bloodline and supernatural power developed beyond the limits of an ordinary Noble Vampire.[4][12]
Despite their disagreements, Vlad gradually recognized qualities in Victor that he deeply respected. Victor maintained fierce loyalty toward his family, willingly confronted powerful opponents, and refused to abandon his principles merely to preserve political convenience. By the later stages of their relationship, Vlad trusted Victor to protect members of the Tepes family even when those individuals no longer wished to remain beneath [[Nightingale]]'s Crown.[13]
Victor's rescue of Alexios became particularly significant because Vlad voluntarily bowed in gratitude, something almost unimaginable during their earliest encounters. The rise of the [[Elderblood Empire]] eventually placed Victor far above Vlad in political and supernatural authority, but the former Vampire King increasingly regarded the change as an opportunity for development rather than merely a humiliation.[24][14]

#### Alexios Alioth
[[Alexios Alioth]] is Vlad's most trusted subordinate and one of the few individuals capable of criticizing him directly. Their relationship originates from Vlad's connection to the original Alioth family, whose history predates much of modern [[Nightingale]]. Alexios served as the King's adviser, administrator, and trusted companion while maintaining enough personal independence to challenge Vlad whenever he believed his master was making a serious mistake.[18][1]
Alexios was particularly important during the collapse of the Tepes Royal Family. Rather than defend Vlad unconditionally, he criticized the King's neglect and arrogance, making clear that many of his problems were consequences of his own behavior. Vlad nevertheless retained his loyalty because their relationship was built upon centuries of shared experience rather than obedience alone.[18]
When Alexios began dying because of accumulated Time-related damage, Vlad experienced genuine helplessness. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s intervention saved Alexios while preserving his individuality, prompting Vlad to bow in gratitude. The event demonstrated that Alexios was not merely a useful servant but someone whose loss would have deeply affected the Progenitor.[1][24]

#### Ophis Tepes Elderblood
[[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]] is Vlad's youngest known daughter and the child whose relationship with him underwent the greatest transformation. Vlad initially treated her as a vulnerable Royal Princess requiring protection, but his emotional distance and the instability of the Royal Family made his household an increasingly unsuitable environment for her development. Ophis consequently became deeply attached to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and eventually regarded him as her true paternal figure.[34]
Vlad initially struggled to accept her attachment to another man, but gradually recognized that Ophis possessed extraordinary power and could make decisions independently. By the Imperial Era, she had become a Dragon Goddess and Legion Commander, while Vlad accepted the possibility of her eventually marrying Victor despite his paternal discomfort.[14]
Their relationship improved sufficiently that Ophis could visit [[Nightingale]] and interact with him without the same level of hostility that characterized her earlier life. Nevertheless, she remained critical of his family arrangements and openly considered Victor a better example of fatherhood. Her discovery of Vlad privately playing video games also revealed that their interactions could now include ordinary and embarrassing moments rather than only political conflict.[25][45]

#### Otsuki Hana
[[Otsuki Hana]] was Vlad's most beloved wife and the woman whose death affected him most deeply. Unlike relationships primarily shaped by politics or obligation, Vlad's attachment to Hana survived even after thousands of years of separation. Her death at the hands of Shur became an unresolved grievance that continued influencing his actions long after his other marriages had collapsed.[23]
When Vlad finally confronted Shur, his accumulated grief and hatred became the driving forces behind his attacks. After Shur's external immortality failed, Vlad used his Soul Authority to kill him permanently, shedding bloody tears while remembering Hana. The victory represented the completion of one of the most personal objectives in his entire existence.[23]

#### Jeanne Elderblood
[[Jeanne Elderblood]] was one of Vlad's wives and eventually became one of the women who rejected the continuation of their supernatural marriage. Their relationship deteriorated through Vlad's emotional neglect and his treatment of the family as an extension of his political responsibilities. Jeanne eventually demanded freedom from the bond connecting them, and Vlad released her.[22]
Jeanne later became closely connected to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and the greater cosmological order through her relationship with the Universal Tree. Her subsequent importance demonstrated that Vlad had once possessed access to individuals and opportunities whose true significance he had failed to recognize during his marriage.[14]

#### Morgana Elderblood
[[Morgana Elderblood]] was another of Vlad's former wives and one of the individuals whose relationship with the King deteriorated through his emotional distance. Like Jeanne, she rejected the continuation of their marriage rather than betraying him romantically. Vlad eventually severed the supernatural bond connecting them and allowed her to leave the Royal Family.[22]

#### The Vampire Queen
The current [[Vampire Queen]] is Vlad's later wife and a descendant of another ancient Vampire Progenitor Bloodline believed lost for thousands of years. Their marriage provided a connection to a lineage older than much of modern [[Nightingale]]'s established nobility and became part of Vlad's attempts to reorganize his kingdom after the Demonic War.[40]
Although the marriage remains intact in his later appearances, Ophis distrusts her stepmother and believes that the woman's behavior is influenced primarily by fear of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and the consequences of betraying the Tepes Royal Family. The precise extent of the Queen's genuine attachment to Vlad remains uncertain.[25]

#### Theo Tepes
[[Theo Tepes]] was Vlad's son and one of the central figures in the collapse of the original Tepes household. His affair with Stella, Vlad's first wife, constituted a betrayal that Vlad considered intolerable. Vlad ultimately killed Theo after discovering the relationship, contributing to the permanent fragmentation of the Royal Family.[20]

#### Lucas Tepes and Saul Tepes
[[Lucas Tepes]] and [[Saul Tepes]] are among Vlad's surviving sons. Their relationships with their father were deeply damaged when Vlad killed their mothers following the revelation of marital betrayals. Although Vlad regarded his actions as punishment for unforgivable offenses, the surviving sons did not simply accept his authority as justification for the executions.[21][18]

#### Adam Tepes
[[Adam Tepes]] is one of Vlad's younger sons and an individual whom the King regarded as possessing considerable future potential. Vlad eventually began taking a more direct interest in his development, bringing him to an important [[Supernatural Gathering]] despite the dangers involved. The decision reflected Vlad's growing recognition that excessive protection had weakened several members of his family and that younger descendants needed genuine exposure to powerful opponents and difficult circumstances.[19][44]

#### Vlad III Tepes
Vlad Dracul Tepes was a Human ruler who became Vlad's friend before the Progenitor adopted his identity. Their relationship was sufficiently close that the Human requested help saving his younger brother Radu, whom the Progenitor transformed into a Noble Vampire. The friendship nevertheless ended when the Progenitor killed and consumed Vlad Dracul Tepes, incorporating his characteristics and name into his own existence.[10]
Despite consuming the Human, Vlad preserved a coffin bearing his name and continued regarding him as an old friend. This unusual attachment demonstrates the complexity of a Vampire Progenitor's relationship with the Souls he consumes, particularly when their characteristics become deeply integrated into his identity.[11]

#### Radu Tepes
Radu Tepes was the younger brother of the original Human Vlad Dracul Tepes. The Vampire Progenitor saved Radu's life by transforming him into a Noble Vampire, but their relationship ended when Radu witnessed the Progenitor killing and consuming his brother. Radu subsequently developed a centuries-long grudge toward the creature wearing his brother's face and name, eventually becoming involved in the conspiracies surrounding [[New Dawn]].[10]
Radu is not the Vampire Progenitor's biological brother. Their apparent familial relationship exists through the Progenitor's adoption of the original Human Vlad's identity.[10]

#### Volk Fenrir
[[Volk Fenrir]] is the former King of the [[Werewolves]] and one of Vlad's longtime acquaintances. Despite the historic hostility between Vampires and Werewolves, the two rulers developed a relationship combining rivalry, personal familiarity, and mutual understanding of the burdens of kingship. Volk contacted Vlad when his marriage to Tasha began collapsing, trusting that the Vampire King possessed enough experience to provide useful advice.[13]
Vlad explained the political weaknesses of Samar's divided monarchy and warned Volk about the consequences of provoking [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Their relationship did not prevent Vlad from pursuing opportunities to gain influence among Werewolves, illustrating the distinction between personal friendship and the interests of competing supernatural civilizations.[42]

#### Shur
Shur was an Elder God responsible for Hana's death and one of the individuals Vlad hated most deeply. Their conflict eventually culminated in a battle during the campaign against [[New Dawn]], in which Vlad and Haruna repeatedly attempted to overcome Shur's unnatural immortality. Once the Elder God's external resurrection mechanism failed, Vlad permanently destroyed his Soul, completing his revenge for Hana.[23]

### Current Status
Vlad remains alive and continues serving as the historic ruler of [[Nightingale]] within the wider [[Elderblood Empire]]. His personal power has increased substantially through Imperial assistance, while his authority over the Vampire Race remains connected to the Bloodline and civilization he established thousands of years earlier. He is among the Empire's elite combatants and retains superiority over [[Tatsuya Rider]], who is identified as the second strongest Noble Vampire.[14][15]
His position is fundamentally different from that of an independent monarch governing a civilization outside Imperial authority. Nightingale and its associated Sector have been incorporated into the Elderblood Empire, with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] personally relocating the planet's Solar System into a protected region of the greater Imperial territory. Vlad continues exercising responsibility over the Vampire Kingdom, but the ultimate political and cosmic authority surrounding it now belongs to Victor.[45][47]
Vlad's immense age and extraordinary abilities continue making him dangerous even among the advanced supernatural populations of the Imperial Era. His history as a Vampire Progenitor, accumulated Soul collection, mastery over Blood, and advanced ancestral physiology distinguish him from most Noble Vampires. Nevertheless, his present standing does not place him above Victor's highest Draconic, Divine, or Eldritch states, and no confirmed transformation into an Outer God or equivalent cosmic existence has been established for him.[15]

### Abilities

#### Powers & Abilities
I'm Blood.And the blood is me.
—Vlad Dracula Tepes to Yama, Chapter 670
Vlad Dracula Tepes is an Ancient Vampire and Vampire Progenitor who has existed for more than six thousand years. His abilities originate from the extraordinary physiology of the ancient Vampire Race, his possession of [[Sanguis Noctis Regis]], his mastery of Blood Manipulation, and the innate authority Vampire Progenitors possess over Souls. Throughout his life, Vlad has developed these abilities to an exceptional degree, accumulating hundreds of thousands of Souls and mastering a combat style in which Blood functions simultaneously as his physical body, weapon, defense, sensory medium, and instrument for interacting with the spiritual existence of other beings.[1][2]
Unlike ordinary Noble Vampires, whose abilities are primarily determined by their inherited Clan characteristics, Vlad possesses the fundamental qualities of a Vampire Progenitor. His control over Blood and Souls extends beyond ordinary biological manipulation, allowing him to attack entities whose immortality would otherwise render conventional physical destruction ineffective. His Soul-based abilities were considered particularly dangerous by the Gods, and he has demonstrated the capacity to permanently destroy an Elder God's Soul when the external mechanism responsible for restoring that Being was no longer functioning.[3][4]
Vlad's strength has continued increasing since the collapse of the old supernatural hierarchy. With the support of the [[Elderblood Empire]], he reached a more developed ancestral state of Vampire physiology and overcame several traditional racial limitations. He remains stronger than [[Tatsuya Rider]], the second strongest Noble Vampire, and belongs to the Empire's elite combatants. His present destructive capabilities extend to entire Solar Systems, representing a substantial advancement beyond the power he displayed during his earlier battles against Yama and Shur.[5][6]

#### Racial Powers

##### Vampire Progenitor Physiology
As a Vampire Progenitor, Vlad possesses the supernatural characteristics of Vampires at an exceptionally advanced level. His physiology retains properties belonging to the ancestral Vampire Race, whose original power was considerably greater than that of most modern Noble Vampires. These characteristics provide the foundation for his physical abilities, longevity, regeneration, transformation, and extraordinary affinity with Blood.[2]
- Vampire Physiology: Vlad possesses the biological and supernatural characteristics of an ancient Vampire, including superior physical abilities, accelerated regeneration, heightened senses, longevity, natural predatory traits, and the capacity to manipulate his own supernatural body.[1][2]
- Progenitor Physiology: Vlad is one of the surviving Vampire Progenitors and stands at the origin of the dominant modern Vampire Bloodline. His nature grants powers unavailable to ordinary members of his Race, particularly those concerning the creation of descendants and the manipulation of Souls.[7][2]
- Superhuman Strength: Vlad possesses immense physical strength and can engage exceptionally powerful supernatural opponents directly. His physical capabilities allowed him to exchange attacks with Yama, a Ruler of Hell, and Shur, an Elder God.[8][4]
- Superhuman Speed: Vlad can move and attack at extraordinary speeds, engaging opponents whose movements are far beyond the perception of ordinary supernatural beings. His exchanges with Yama became progressively faster as both increased their combat intensity.[8][2]
- Enhanced Reflexes: His physiology and accumulated combat experience allow him to recognize and react to extremely rapid attacks, including supernatural projectiles and complex combinations performed by powerful Demons and Gods.[8][4]
- Superhuman Durability: Vlad possesses an exceptionally resilient body capable of surviving powerful physical and supernatural attacks. His durability is further supported by his ability to transform his body into living Blood and regenerate damaged portions.[8][4]
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Vlad can rapidly restore damage to his physical body, including severe wounds sustained during combat. Injuries inflicted by Yama healed almost immediately, while Vlad later demonstrated the ability to continue fighting after deliberately severing an arm contaminated by Shur's supernatural Miasma.[8][4]
- Enhanced Stamina: His Progenitor physiology permits sustained combat and supernatural activity for periods far exceeding the limitations of ordinary Humans and lesser Vampires.[8][2]
- Supernatural Endurance: Vlad can continue functioning despite substantial injuries, physical strain, and dangerous supernatural effects, relying upon his regeneration and experience to maintain combat effectiveness.[4]
- [[Immortality]]: Vlad possesses the supernatural longevity and agelessness associated with high-ranking Vampires. He has existed for more than six thousand years without undergoing ordinary biological aging.[9]
- Self-Sustenance: Fully developed Vampire Progenitors can endure extremely prolonged periods without ordinary Blood consumption. Vlad's mature physiology substantially exceeds the nutritional limitations of ordinary Noble Vampires, while his later Imperial enhancements further reduced these restrictions.[5]
- Enhanced Senses: Vlad possesses the heightened visual, auditory, olfactory, and supernatural perception associated with Vampires. His experience and Progenitor nature allow him to detect powerful presences and react to threats beyond ordinary sensory capabilities.[1][8]
- Night Vision: As an ancient creature of the night, Vlad possesses the ability to perceive his surroundings under conditions of darkness associated with Vampire physiology.
- Natural Weaponry: Vlad can produce monstrous claws, elongated predatory teeth, and other biological weapons through his ancestral Vampire transformation.[2]
- Flight: Vlad can fly through supernatural means and produce large wings composed entirely of Blood while employing his Ancient Vampire True Form.[2]
- [[Vampiric Energy]] Manipulation: Vlad can generate and control the supernatural Energy associated with his Vampire existence, utilizing it to reinforce his body and sustain his extraordinary abilities.[8]
- Racial Evolution: Vlad possesses the ability to develop the characteristics of his Vampire physiology beyond ordinary Noble Vampire limits. His Ancient Vampire True Form represents a return toward the original racial condition, while later Imperial enhancements advanced his body even further.[2][5]

##### Sanguis Noctis Regis
[[Sanguis Noctis Regis]], also known as the Blood of the Night King, is the extraordinary Bloodline possessed by Vlad and [[Victor Elderblood]]. The Blood provides abilities associated with the highest potential of nocturnal species and represents one of the foundations of Vlad's status as a Progenitor. Its benefits extend beyond ordinary physical enhancement, affecting the bearer's capacity for learning, development, adaptation, mental resistance, and supernatural domination.[10]
- Enhanced Learning: Sanguis Noctis Regis greatly increases the rate at which its bearer acquires knowledge and develops new capabilities.[10]
- Accelerated Development: The Bloodline allows its bearer to grow stronger more efficiently than ordinary members of the same Race, increasing the effectiveness of training and supernatural development.[10]
- Enhanced Adaptation: Vlad possesses extraordinary adaptive potential, allowing him to develop in response to increasingly demanding circumstances and forms of supernatural power.[10]
- Enhanced Potential: The Blood of the Night King provides the potential to reach the highest levels of the nocturnal Race to which its bearer belongs.[10]
- Enhanced Mental Resistance: The Bloodline protects its bearer against attacks directed at the mind, including ordinary mental manipulation and supernatural interference. Exceptionally powerful Divine authorities remain a possible means of bypassing such protection.[10]
- Enhanced Vampiric Charm: Sanguis Noctis Regis strengthens the bearer's natural Vampiric Charm, allowing the manipulation of individuals who would ordinarily resist conventional Vampire mental abilities.[10]
- Inherited Knowledge: Night Kings possess a form of inherited ancestral knowledge comparable in principle to the information naturally available to Dragons. Unlike the extensive magical knowledge inherited by Dragons, this information primarily concerns the nature of supernatural species, beings, and aspects of the world once considered common knowledge. Vlad's possession of this racial characteristic does not imply unrestricted knowledge of every event or access to the complete [[Akashic Records]].[10][11]
- Bloodline Empowerment: Vlad's Blood can increase the supernatural capabilities of Vampires who receive it, improving their potential and providing access to a stronger connection with the Progenitor lineage.[12]
- Power Bestowal: Vlad can deliberately grant portions of his Blood to other Vampires. He established a system through which exceptional individuals could earn a drop of Progenitor Blood, allowing them to advance beyond the limits of their previous development.[12]
- Bloodline Limit Expansion: Progenitor Blood can enhance the developmental limits of subordinate Vampires and improve their capacity for further growth.[10][12]
- Restorative Blood (Shared Night King property): Blood of the Night King possesses powerful restorative characteristics when willingly given to another individual. Its regenerative properties can accelerate the recovery of injuries and damaged tissue. The exceptionally extensive healing and purification effects demonstrated specifically by Victor should not automatically be treated as identical to every application available to Vlad.[10]
- Bloodline Creation: Vlad can produce new Vampires through his Progenitor Blood, establishing descendants whose supernatural characteristics originate from his own lineage.[10][13]
- Noble Vampire Creation: Individuals transformed directly through Vlad's Progenitor Blood can become Noble Vampires. Radu Tepes was transformed into a Noble Vampire after the original Human Vlad requested that the Progenitor save his dying younger brother.[13]
- Conversion Rule Bypass: Vlad can transform Humans who do not satisfy the restrictions governing ordinary Vampire conversion. [[Adonis Snow]] was transformed despite circumstances that would have prevented a conventional Vampire from creating a Noble Vampire through the usual rules.[14]
- Progenitor Bloodline Authority: Vlad possesses an intimate supernatural connection with the Bloodline originating from him, allowing him to create and strengthen descendants and influence the development of his Race.[7][12]

#### Blood-Based Powers

##### Blood Manipulation
Blood is my Power. Blood is my Authority. A means to reach the Soul.
—Vlad Dracula Tepes, Chapter 670
Blood Manipulation is Vlad's principal supernatural specialization. His authority allows him to produce and control Blood on an enormous scale, transform it into weapons, reshape his body through it, and affect the Blood of opponents whose supernatural defenses are insufficient to resist him. Unlike conventional biological Blood, the substance manipulated through his Progenitor abilities can behave as a living extension of his existence, incorporating sensory organs, offensive constructs, and manifestations associated with the Souls he has consumed.[8][2]
- Hemokinesis: Vlad can generate, manipulate, move, compress, shape, disperse, and weaponize Blood with exceptional precision.[8][2]
- Large-Scale Blood Manipulation: Vlad can spread Blood throughout an enormous battlefield, filling the surrounding terrain and atmosphere with supernatural material under his control.[2]
- Blood Generation: Vlad can produce tremendous quantities of supernatural Blood through his abilities, creating enough material to overwhelm large areas and sustain numerous simultaneous attacks.[2]
- Living Blood: Vlad's Blood behaves as a supernatural extension of his existence. Eyes, mouths, limbs, predatory creatures, and other structures can emerge from it, functioning as parts of his larger combat system.[2]
- Blood Mimicry: Vlad can transform his body into liquid Blood, abandoning ordinary anatomical restrictions and assuming a dark crimson or black fluid state.[8][2]
- Liquid Physiology: While transformed into living Blood, Vlad can disperse or reshape portions of his body to evade conventional physical attacks.[8]
- Body Liquefaction: Vlad can dissolve his Humanoid body and reconstruct it through supernatural Blood, allowing him to avoid attacks directed against specific parts of his anatomy.[8]
- Blood Hardening: Vlad can solidify his Blood into durable structures and weapons rather than maintaining it exclusively in a liquid state.[8][2]
- Blood Weapon Creation: Vlad can construct weapons from Blood. He created a sword while preparing to fight Yama and used it in direct combat against the Ruler of Hell.[8]
- Blood Swordsmanship: Vlad can combine conventional sword techniques with weapons created through Blood Manipulation, integrating supernatural transformation into his martial style.[8]
- Blood Spikes: Vlad can generate numerous spikes and launch them as projectiles. During his Ancient Vampire transformation, he produced thousands of spikes and accelerated them to extremely high speeds.[2]
- Blood Projectile Manipulation: Vlad can control the movement, direction, and formation of Blood-based projectiles, allowing attacks to be launched from different locations within his Blood-filled battlefield.[2]
- Blood Constructs: Vlad can create hands, claws, jaws, teeth, limbs, and numerous other structures from his living Blood.[2]
- Blood Monster Creation: He can transform portions of his Blood into monstrous creatures capable of attacking enemies through physical or supernatural means.[8][2]
- Blood Beast Manifestation: Vlad can create enormous predatory manifestations from his fluid body. During his confrontation with Yama, a wave of his Blood assumed the appearance of a monstrous beast and attacked the Demon King.[8]
- Blood Hands: Giant hands can emerge from his Blood Sea to seize opponents, immobilize them, or drag them beneath the surrounding Blood.[2]
- Blood Tendrils: Vlad can extend Blood-based appendages capable of grasping, striking, and piercing opponents from unexpected directions.[2]
- Blood Wings: He can produce enormous wings from Blood, allowing flight while providing additional surfaces through which the substance can be controlled or absorbed.[15][2]
- Blood Barrier: Vlad can form defensive walls and barriers of Blood. He used a large Blood barrier while protecting Haruna during the conflict against Shur.[4]
- Blood Rain: Vlad can spread Blood into the atmosphere, generate crimson clouds, and cause Blood to rain across the battlefield. The falling Blood remains connected to his larger supernatural network.[2]
- Blood Sea: Vlad can transform the battlefield into an enormous sea of living Blood, allowing attacks to emerge from practically any direction within the affected region.[2]
- Blood Eyes: Vlad can manifest eyes throughout his Blood, including enormous eyes overlooking the battlefield and smaller sensory organs embedded within the substance.[2]
- Distributed Perception: Because his living Blood can generate sensory organs, Vlad can observe events from multiple positions within the area occupied by his Blood constructs.[2]
- Omnidirectional Attacks (Within Blood-Controlled Areas): Vlad can generate attacks from numerous directions by using the Blood spread throughout his surroundings. This does not represent literal omnipresence or unlimited range.[2]
- Internal Blood Manipulation: Vlad can influence the Blood contained within another living being when the target's supernatural defenses cannot resist his control. Yama specifically prepared a countermeasure against this ability.[8]
- Internal Blood Rupture: Vlad can cause an opponent's Blood to violently rupture from within the body. He used such an attack against Yama, temporarily destroying the Demon King's body before his Ruler-based resurrection restored him.[8]
- Internal Biological Disruption: His authority over an opponent's Blood can produce destructive effects inside the body rather than requiring an external projectile to penetrate the target's defenses.[8]
- Blood Absorption: Vlad can draw external Blood toward himself and absorb it into his supernatural body. His wings absorbed the Blood remaining after his technique annihilated Demonic troops in [[Nightingale]].[15]
- Battlefield Saturation: Vlad can progressively increase the quantity of Blood surrounding an opponent until conventional positioning becomes disadvantageous and the battlefield itself functions as his weapon.[2]
- Corpse Manipulation (Through Blood): Vlad can animate bodies contained within his Blood and use them alongside monstrous constructs. The corpses under his control moved as though restored to life during his battle against Yama.[2]
- Limited [[Necromancy]] (Blood and Soul-Based): Vlad can produce an army-like manifestation of animated corpses and Soul-associated creatures through his Blood. This should not be interpreted as unrestricted resurrection of the deceased as independent living beings.[2]
- Blood-Based Soul Interaction: Vlad uses Blood as a medium through which his Progenitor Authority can access and manipulate Souls, connecting his principal racial powers into a single combat system.[2]

##### Blood Manipulation Limitations
Vlad's control over Blood is extraordinarily advanced but differs from the characteristics eventually developed by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Although Vlad can control his own Blood and affect weaker targets within his range, he cannot naturally perceive and manipulate Blood across enormous distances or separate dimensions in the same manner as Victor. The distinction exists because different Vampire Progenitors possess different talents and levels of proficiency concerning the same underlying racial powers.[13]
- Range-Dependent Blood Control: Vlad's ability to sense and manipulate separated portions of his Blood depends upon their proximity and the conditions supporting his connection to them.[13]
- Resistance from Stronger Opponents: Beings possessing sufficient supernatural strength or specialized defenses can prevent Vlad from controlling the Blood inside their bodies.[8]
- Blood Destruction: Supernatural attacks capable of destroying or evaporating Blood can remove portions of Vlad's constructs and temporarily reduce the area available for his techniques.[2]
- Distributed Soul Vulnerability: Souls manifested through Vlad's living Blood remain vulnerable to sufficiently powerful Soul-targeting techniques. Yama's Judgment destroyed Souls contained within a portion of the Blood-based army.[2]

#### Soul-Based Powers

##### Vampire Progenitor Soul Authority
The Progenitor of Vampires is the one who negotiates between Life and Death using Blood as a bargaining chip to obtain the Soul. That is the essence of the Progenitor.
—Vlad Dracula Tepes, Chapter 670
The fundamental power distinguishing Vampire Progenitors from ordinary Vampires is their authority over Souls. Blood serves as the medium through which this authority is expressed, allowing the Progenitor to obtain the Souls of defeated beings, store them within a spiritual space, access their characteristics, and employ them for additional supernatural effects. Vlad has developed this racial capability through thousands of years of combat and Soul consumption, maintaining an internal collection numbering in the hundreds of thousands.[2]
- Soul Manipulation: Vlad can directly interact with Souls through his Progenitor Authority, accessing spiritual characteristics beyond an individual's ordinary physical existence.[2]
- Soul Perception: Vlad can perceive and examine Souls, allowing him to recognize spiritual characteristics and connections that would not necessarily be visible through ordinary senses.[8]
- Soul Detection: Vlad can recognize the presence of Souls associated with supernatural beings and identify spiritually significant characteristics during combat.[8]
- Soul Damage: Vlad can inflict injuries upon an opponent's Soul rather than limiting his attacks to the physical body.[4]
- Soul Destruction: Vlad can destroy Souls through his Progenitor abilities, eliminating the spiritual foundation required for ordinary resurrection or reincarnation.[3][4]
- Permanent Death Inducement: By destroying the Soul, Vlad can permanently kill supernatural beings whose immortality depends upon the continued existence of their spiritual foundation.[3][4]
- [[Immortality]] Negation (Soul-Based): Vlad can bypass forms of immortality that protect or restore only the physical body. This ability does not automatically overcome independent resurrection systems that continue restoring the target's Soul.[4]
- Reincarnation Negation: The complete destruction of a Soul prevents its ordinary return through reincarnation mechanisms.[4]
- Soul Consumption: Vlad can consume the Souls of defeated beings and incorporate them into his supernatural existence.[2][13]
- Soul Absorption: Vlad can absorb Souls or Soul fragments and retain supernatural characteristics associated with their former owners.[2][13]
- Soul Storage: Vlad can preserve consumed Souls instead of immediately destroying or completely assimilating them.[2]
- Soul World: Vlad possesses an internal spiritual repository containing the Souls accumulated throughout his existence. His collection contains hundreds of thousands of Souls.[2]
- Soul Manifestation: Vlad can manifest stored Souls through his Blood, producing creatures and bodily structures associated with the beings he has consumed.[2]
- Soul Army: Vlad can use numerous stored Souls simultaneously as components of a supernatural army manifested through his living Blood.[2]
- Soul Construct Manifestation: The characteristics of consumed Souls can emerge as hands, heads, monsters, and other forms within the Blood controlled by Vlad.[2]
- Power Manifestation Through Souls: Particularly powerful stored Souls can retain characteristics sufficient for Vlad to reproduce supernatural abilities associated with their original owners.[2]
- Soul Assimilation: Vlad can integrate consumed Souls more deeply into his own existence rather than simply storing them as separate spiritual resources.[13]
- Identity Assimilation: When a consumed Soul possesses exceptional personal significance to Vlad, its characteristics can become incorporated into his own identity. The original Vlad Dracul Tepes is the most important established example.[13][16]
- Memory Assimilation: Vlad can acquire memories and experiences from Souls that he consumes, including information associated with individuals whose characteristics become part of his own existence.[13]
- Memory Reading (Vampire Progenitor Trait): Vampire Night Kings possess a Soul-based method of obtaining memories from personally defeated individuals through the absorption of Soul fragments. The ordinary requirements of this racial ability distinguish it from unrestricted access to the memories of every dead being.[13]
- Soul-Based Knowledge Acquisition: Vlad can obtain information from consumed Souls, supplementing the knowledge accumulated through his own personal experiences.[13]
- Soul Record Affinity: Vampire Progenitors possess a natural relationship with Soul information. Their Authority resembles a highly restricted form of the [[Akashic Records]], limited to Souls rather than the complete record of events throughout Reality.[11]
- Soul Editing (Racial Potential): Vampire Progenitor Authority can theoretically support profound alterations to Souls when its wielder possesses sufficient understanding and precision. The underlying racial capability is exceptionally versatile, but [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later specialized applications should not be automatically attributed to Vlad without separate demonstrations.[11]
- Soul Information Manipulation (Racial Potential): The relationship between Progenitor Authority and Soul records provides a foundation for modifying spiritual characteristics. Vlad's demonstrated specialization concerns Soul consumption, storage, manifestation, and destruction rather than every possible application of Soul editing.[11]

##### Soul Authority and the Akashic Records
The connection between Vampire Progenitors and the [[Akashic Records]] is limited in scope. A Progenitor does not automatically possess unrestricted access to the universal record of all events, nor does the racial ability provide complete knowledge of every Soul that has ever existed. Instead, the Progenitor's supernatural existence functions as a considerably smaller Soul-focused system, providing a foundation for accessing and manipulating spiritual information.[11]
The potential of this Authority depends heavily upon the user's knowledge, control, and development. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later ability to perceive increasingly fundamental information about Souls and Reality is supported by additional Draconic, Divine, and conceptual characteristics. Those advancements do not retroactively become powers possessed by Vlad merely because both originally belonged to the Vampire Progenitor category.[11]

##### Ancient Dragon Soul
Among the Souls accumulated by Vlad is that of an Ancient Dragon he defeated and consumed during his earlier life. The Dragon's Soul retained supernatural characteristics powerful enough for Vlad to reproduce a form of its original Breath through Blood-based manifestation.[2]
- Ancient Dragon Soul Storage: Vlad possesses the Soul of an Ancient Dragon within his spiritual collection.[2]
- Dragon Manifestation: He can manifest the head of the consumed Dragon from his living Blood, producing a supernatural construct capable of attacking independently of his ordinary body.[2]
- Dragon Breath: Vlad can reproduce the Dragon's Breath through the manifested Soul. The resulting attack was considered sufficiently dangerous that [[Merlin]] intervened to prevent Yama from receiving it directly.[2]
- Draconic Fire Projection: The manifested Dragon can release an intense Breath attack possessing the dangerous supernatural characteristics associated with ancient Dragons.[2]
- Cursed Damage (Inherited Dragon Breath Property): Ancient Dragon Breath can inflict supernatural injuries that are exceptionally difficult to heal. Merlin explained that victims lacking appropriate protection could require the removal of affected body parts or assistance from exceptionally rare healing powers. The attack originates from the consumed Dragon's Soul rather than Vlad independently possessing a Dragon's natural physiology.[2]
- Borrowed Draconic Characteristics: Vlad can access abilities belonging to the Dragon Soul through manifestation, but this does not make him a Dragon Progenitor or establish that he possesses the complete natural powers of an independent Ancient Dragon.[2]

#### Ancient Vampire Powers

##### Ancient Vampire True Form
Vlad possesses the Ancient Vampire True Form, a transformation representing the ancestral condition of the Vampire Race before its supernatural decline. This form is fundamentally different from the incomplete [[Vampire Count Form]] available to talented modern Vampires. According to Vlad, the ancient Race originally possessed extraordinary abilities that caused powerful beings of the distant past to cooperate in suppressing its development. The True Form represents the recovery of those ancestral characteristics rather than merely an ordinary numerical increase in physical strength.[2]
- Ancient Vampire Transformation: Vlad can transform into the original morphology of the ancient Vampire Race, developing greyish skin, black sclerae, glowing crimson irises, slightly pointed ears, a monstrous mouth, elongated claws, and wings made entirely of Blood.[2]
- Enhanced Physical Characteristics: His ancestral form allows fuller expression of the physical and supernatural characteristics associated with ancient Vampires.[2]
- Enhanced Blood Manipulation: Vlad can employ enormous Blood-based techniques while using his ancestral combat state, including a Blood Sea, Blood Rain, thousands of projectiles, and large-scale Soul manifestations.[2]
- Enhanced Soul Manifestation: Vlad can access the Souls within his collection through the living Blood surrounding him, producing numerous spiritual and monstrous manifestations.[2]
- Natural Claws: His hands develop monstrous claws, while his feet acquire elongated talons resembling bladed weapons.[2]
- Predatory Maw: His mouth becomes an unnatural structure filled with sharp teeth and lacking conventional Human lips.[2]
- Blood Wings: Enormous wings formed entirely from Blood emerge from his back and function as extensions of his supernatural body.[2]
- Living Body Markings: Black tattoo-like patterns move across Vlad's body as though alive, reflecting the unusual characteristics of his ancestral transformation.[2]
- Elbow Spikes: Black spikes possessing crimson tips protrude from his elbows while using the Ancient Vampire True Form.[2]
- Ancestral Racial Power: The form expresses characteristics belonging to Vampires who existed before the modern generation of Earth's Gods, representing a closer return toward the origins of the Race.[2]

##### Shapeshifting and Body Manipulation
Shape and looks don't make any sense to me. I can be whatever I want, whenever I want.
—Vlad Dracula Tepes to [[Victor Elderblood]], Chapter 120
Vlad possesses exceptional control over his physical appearance and biological structure. His transformations are genuine alterations of his body rather than conventional Illusions, allowing him to assume identities accumulated throughout his lifetime or reproduce individuals personally known by others.[17]
- Advanced Shapeshifting: Vlad can alter his physical form at will, changing his face, proportions, body structure, hair, complexion, and other characteristics.[17]
- Appearance Alteration: He can assume drastically different Humanoid appearances, including those of ancient warriors, medieval Kings, and individuals possessing unrelated physical characteristics.[17]
- Age Shifting: Vlad can modify his apparent physical age without changing his actual chronological existence.[17]
- Identity Mimicry: He can imitate other people, reproducing their physical appearance with sufficient accuracy to resemble the individual directly.[17]
- Voice Mimicry: Vlad can reproduce the voices of other individuals, including people personally familiar to Victor.[17]
- Body Restructuring: Vlad can modify his anatomy, creating or removing physical structures without remaining bound to an ordinary Human shape.[17][8]
- Partial Transformation: He can alter individual portions of his body without necessarily transforming his entire physical existence.[17]
- Amorphous Physiology: Vlad can abandon a stable Humanoid structure and transform into an irregular mass of dark, living Blood.[8]
- Biological Structure Manipulation: His transformed body can generate eyes, teeth, mouths, limbs, claws, and other structures according to his intentions.[8][2]
- Liquid-Body Evasion: Vlad can avoid conventional physical attacks by dispersing or reshaping the portion of his body targeted by an opponent. This represents a transformation-based defensive mechanism rather than proven unrestricted intangibility against every possible form of attack.[8]

#### Mental and Psychic Powers

##### Vampiric Charm
Vlad possesses Progenitor-level Vampiric Charm, allowing him to dominate the minds of supernatural beings whose mental resistance is insufficient to oppose his power. His ability is significantly stronger than that of ordinary Vampires and can overwhelm members of [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] that naturally resist Vampiric mental manipulation.[18]
- Mind Manipulation: Vlad can influence the thoughts and behavior of susceptible targets through his supernatural Charm.[18]
- Mind Control: He can impose commands upon affected individuals, forcing them to obey instructions that they would ordinarily reject.[18]
- Mental Domination: Vlad can overcome the resistance of multiple Alpha [[Werewolves]] and bring them under his control simultaneously.[18]
- Memory Manipulation: Vlad can alter or suppress memories belonging to affected individuals. He erased the memories of Werewolves subjected to his Charm and replaced the events with a fabricated explanation.[18]
- Post-Hypnotic Suggestion: Vlad can implant instructions intended to influence an individual after the immediate period of mental domination has ended.[18]
- Resistance Negation (Limited): His Charm can overcome the natural resistance Werewolves possess toward Vampire mental abilities. This effect depends upon the relative strength of the target and does not establish absolute control over every Being.[18]
- Multiple-Target Mind Control: Vlad successfully dominated several Alpha Werewolves during the operation involving members of Volk's faction.[18]

##### Other Psychic Abilities
- Telepathy: Vlad can communicate mentally, transmitting information and receiving messages without ordinary speech. His exchanges with Alexios during the confrontation against Yama demonstrate supernatural communication even while the two are physically separated.[8]
- Mental Communication: Vlad can coordinate actions with trusted subordinates through supernatural mental contact, allowing information to be exchanged during dangerous situations without alerting nearby opponents.[8]
- Passive Fear Inducement: Vlad's overwhelming supernatural presence can trigger instinctive terror in other beings. [[Tsukuyomi]] recognized the danger posed by the Vampire King and experienced fear associated with the possibility of permanent Soul death.[3]
- Intimidating Aura: Vlad can release an oppressive presence capable of affecting the confidence and instincts of weaker supernatural beings.[1][8]

#### Immortality Negation and Spiritual Offense
One of Vlad's greatest advantages is his ability to attack the Soul independently of the physical body. This makes him exceptionally dangerous to opponents who rely upon regeneration or ordinary Divine immortality. His authority nevertheless possesses limits, particularly against Beings whose Souls are protected by higher supernatural statuses or restored through external mechanisms.[3][8][4]
- God Killing: Vlad possesses attacks capable of permanently killing Gods by destroying their Souls. This danger was recognized by members of the Divine Pantheons who understood that conventional immortality did not guarantee survival against him.[3]
- Soul-Based [[Immortality]] Negation: Vlad can bypass immortality that depends upon the regeneration of the physical body by destroying the Soul supporting that existence.[3][4]
- Permanent Soul Destruction: He can inflict damage upon an opponent's Soul until the spiritual foundation of their continued existence is completely destroyed.[4]
- Reincarnation Negation: Complete Soul destruction prevents the ordinary reincarnation of the defeated individual.[4]
- Elder God Killing: Vlad permanently killed Shur after the external system sustaining the Elder God's immortality was removed. His final attack destroyed both the physical body and Soul.[4]
- Spiritual Defense Bypassing: Vlad can attack the Soul through supernatural methods rather than depending exclusively upon physical damage, rendering ordinary bodily durability insufficient as a complete defense.[3][2]
- Soul-Targeting Attacks: His Blood-based combat style permits direct interaction with the Souls of enemies when their supernatural protections can be overcome.[2][4]
Soul Destruction is not equivalent to unrestricted Existence Erasure. Vlad can permanently kill an opponent through the destruction of their Soul, but this does not establish the ability to erase every aspect of a Being across all possible forms of existence. Shur's continued resurrection while his external immortality remained functional demonstrates that Vlad's attacks can be resisted or circumvented by mechanisms operating beyond ordinary bodily and Soul regeneration.[4]

#### Defensive Abilities and Resistances

##### Natural Resistances
- Mental Manipulation Resistance: [[Sanguis Noctis Regis]] provides substantial protection against supernatural attacks directed at the mind. Vlad described the Blood of the Night King as granting immunity to mind-related damage, although particularly powerful Divine authorities remain potential exceptions.[10]
- Hypnosis Resistance: Vlad's Progenitor Bloodline makes him highly resistant to conventional hypnotic and Charm-based supernatural effects.[10]
- Fear Resistance: Vlad possesses exceptional mental fortitude developed through his ancient existence and Progenitor nature. His considerable experience allows him to confront beings capable of intimidating ordinary supernatural rulers.[1][8]
- Physical Damage Resistance: His Vampire physiology provides exceptional durability and regenerative protection against ordinary physical injuries and attacks lacking sufficient supernatural power.[8]
- Regenerative Defense: Vlad can recover from severe bodily injuries and continue fighting after damage that would incapacitate ordinary supernatural beings.[8][4]
- Liquid-Body Evasion: His ability to dissolve into living Blood allows him to evade numerous conventional physical strikes by dispersing or restructuring the affected area.[8]
- Blood Barrier: Vlad can create defensive structures from Blood to protect himself or allies from dangerous supernatural attacks.[4]
- Pain Tolerance: Vlad can continue functioning despite extraordinary physical suffering, demonstrating sufficient control to sever an infected limb immediately when supernatural corruption threatens his body.[4]
- Supernatural Corruption Containment (Through Amputation): Vlad can prevent some dangerous effects from spreading by removing contaminated bodily material before the corruption reaches the remainder of his body. This is a defensive response rather than immunity to the underlying attack.[4]
- Resistance to Aging: Vlad's ancient Vampire physiology prevents ordinary biological aging and allows him to maintain his physical existence across thousands of years.[9]

##### Imperial-Era Resistance Improvements
The [[Elderblood Empire]]'s advanced biological technology significantly improved Vlad's ancestral physiology and eliminated many restrictions previously associated with Noble Vampires. These changes strengthened his body without requiring him to abandon his original Vampire lineage.[5]
- Racial Weakness Removal: Imperial biotechnology eliminated most of the fundamental weaknesses associated with Noble Vampire physiology from Vlad's body.[5]
- Dietary Independence: Vlad no longer depends exclusively upon Blood for nourishment and can consume ordinary food.[5]
- Enhanced Biological Stability: The development of his ancestral Vampire state strengthened his body and improved its compatibility with further supernatural growth.[5]
- Enhanced Physical Resilience: Imperial modifications increased his physical condition beyond the level displayed during his earlier existence as King of [[Nightingale]].[5]
The complete list of weaknesses removed through Imperial modification has not been identified individually. Vlad should therefore not be credited with absolute immunity to every possible form of [[Holy Energy]], Faith Energy, Sacred Weaponry, Soul Manipulation, conceptual attack, or Divine authority merely because most ordinary Noble Vampire weaknesses were eliminated.[5]

#### Sealing Abilities
- Sealing (Stated Capability): Vlad possesses a method capable of sealing exceptionally dangerous supernatural beings. During the battle against Shur, he stated that he could seal the Elder God if Haruna created a sufficiently large opportunity.[4]
- Immortal Containment (Proposed Application): Vlad considered sealing Shur when repeated destruction of his body and Soul proved insufficient to overcome the external mechanism restoring him.[4]
The exact mechanics, duration, conditions, and limitations of Vlad's sealing technique have not been demonstrated. Shur's external immortality was removed before Vlad needed to complete the proposed seal, allowing him to pursue permanent destruction instead.[4]

#### Combat Skills

##### Martial Arts
Vlad possesses more than six thousand years of combat experience and has refined his martial abilities to the point that they remain recognizable even when his body no longer possesses ordinary Humanoid anatomy. His confrontation with Yama demonstrated that the absence of a conventional physical stance does not prevent him from applying deliberate technique, timing, and movement through his amorphous Blood body.[2]
- Master Martial Artist: Vlad possesses extremely refined combat technique developed through thousands of years of fighting powerful supernatural opponents.[2]
- Master Swordsman: Vlad can wield swords against powerful Demons and Gods, including Yama and Shur.[8][4]
- Unarmed Combat: Vlad can fight through physical strikes, close-range attacks, and supernatural extensions of his body.[8]
- Weapon Creation and Proficiency: He can produce Blood-based weapons and immediately integrate them into his fighting style.[8]
- Amorphous [[Martial Arts]]: Vlad can perform refined martial techniques while existing as living Blood rather than maintaining a conventional Humanoid body.[2]
- Unorthodox Combat: His shapeshifting allows attacks to emerge from body parts and positions impossible for ordinary martial artists to reproduce.[8][2]
- Battlefield Control: Vlad specializes in spreading Blood throughout the battlefield and turning the environment into an extension of his attacks.[2]
- Combat Adaptation: Vlad can alter his fighting methods when opponents prepare countermeasures against his abilities. He changed tactics after discovering that Yama could resist direct manipulation of the Blood inside his body.[8]
- Combat Analysis: His experience allows him to recognize dangerous abilities, identify unusual defenses, and evaluate opponents through observation during battle.[8][4]
- Combat Instinct: Vlad can identify threats before their full effects are revealed. He recognized that Yama's unfamiliar Judgment technique represented a dangerous development and immediately increased his offense.[2]
- Extreme-Speed Combat: Vlad can maintain refined offensive and defensive movements while exchanging attacks with extremely powerful supernatural opponents.[8]
- Multiple-Attack Coordination: He can simultaneously control Blood constructs, projectiles, and Soul-based manifestations while maintaining his own combat movements.[2]
- Defensive Adaptation: Vlad can respond to dangerous conditions through regeneration, Blood transformation, protective barriers, and the deliberate removal of compromised tissue.[8][4]
- Combat Experience: Vlad has fought Humans, Vampires, Demons, Gods, Dragons, and other supernatural beings over more than six millennia.[2][4]

##### Military, Political and Non-Combat Skills
- Leadership: Vlad established and maintained the modern Vampire Kingdom of [[Nightingale]], governing its Noble Clans and Royal institutions for approximately three thousand years.[19]
- Military Leadership: Vlad possesses extensive experience commanding supernatural forces, coordinating defenses, and organizing large-scale responses to threats against Nightingale.[15]
- Strategic Planning: Vlad can evaluate conflicts over extended periods and anticipate political consequences affecting entire supernatural civilizations.[19][20]
- Political Manipulation: Vlad possesses extensive experience exploiting the rivalries, ambitions, and vulnerabilities of powerful supernatural factions. His operations involving Volk's followers demonstrate his willingness to employ covert influence rather than direct war.[18]
- Diplomacy: Vlad can negotiate with rulers from traditionally hostile species, including [[Werewolves]] and members of foreign Pantheons.[20]
- Psychological Warfare: Vlad understands how intimidation, reputation, supernatural power, and political pressure can influence an opponent's behavior.[1][18]
- Intelligence Gathering: Vlad can employ Nightingale's Royal Shadows and other intelligence resources to investigate threats and monitor supernatural developments.[19]
- Interrogation: His Charm, Soul-related capabilities, and political experience provide methods of obtaining information from individuals who would otherwise refuse cooperation.[18][13]
- Knowledge of Ancient History: Vlad possesses extensive knowledge concerning supernatural civilization, Vampire ancestry, ancient Bloodlines, and powerful beings from periods predating the modern Pantheons.[7][2]
- Knowledge of Vampire Biology: Vlad understands the supernatural characteristics of Vampire Bloodlines, including the Blood of the Night King and the ancestral morphology associated with Ancient Vampires.[10][2]
- Knowledge of Souls: His Progenitor nature and accumulated experience provide advanced understanding of Soul consumption, spiritual characteristics, and the relationship between Blood and the Soul.[2][13]
- Multilingualism: Vlad possesses knowledge of ancient languages, including the forgotten language employed when activating Ima-Ku-Roi.[15]
- Political Administration: Vlad maintained a functioning Vampire monarchy for thousands of years while overseeing Noble Clans, Royal institutions, and important territorial relationships.[19]
- Long-Term Planning: Vlad can evaluate the consequences of decisions across generations rather than relying exclusively upon immediate political advantage.[12]
- Technological Adaptability: Vlad can adapt to technological developments introduced by the [[Elderblood Empire]] and has incorporated Imperial enhancements into his personal development.[5]
- Strong Willpower: Vlad possesses exceptional personal resolve developed through his ancient existence and his status as a Progenitor. His willingness to confront powerful enemies and endure dangerous circumstances reflects this characteristic.[4]

#### Techniques

##### Ima-Ku-Roi
Ima-Ku-Roi is an ancient Blood-based technique whose invocation uses a language largely forgotten by modern civilization. It represents one of Vlad's most dangerous large-scale abilities and can be employed through different manifestations of his supernatural Blood and Soul authority.[15][2]
During the Demonic invasion of [[Nightingale]], Vlad entered a dark, winged, nearly formless state and invoked the technique against the invading forces. The affected Demons began dissolving completely, with their flesh, bones, and other bodily structures transforming until only Blood remained. The resulting Blood rose from the battlefield and flowed toward Vlad's wings, where it was absorbed. The technique annihilated the Demonic soldiers throughout the affected area and demonstrated the overwhelming advantage of Progenitor-level Blood authority against opponents unable to resist it.[15]
Vlad also invoked Ima-Ku-Roi during his confrontation with Yama, controlling the enormous river of Blood and bodies formed during their battle. Corpses within the Blood began moving as though restored to life, accompanied by thousands of different monstrous creatures. The animated bodies and Soul-associated manifestations formed a large supernatural fighting force capable of engaging the Ruler of Hell's techniques.[2]
The demonstrated applications of Ima-Ku-Roi therefore include large-scale bodily dissolution into Blood, Blood absorption, manipulation of a river of Blood and corpses, and the animation of monstrous or corpse-like forces. These applications are distinct but belong to the same named technique as demonstrated in different circumstances.[15][2]

##### Blood Sea
Vlad can saturate the surrounding battlefield with an enormous mass of living Blood. The Blood Sea contains sensory organs, attacking limbs, monstrous manifestations, and Souls obtained throughout his life. Once established, it allows him to attack from numerous directions and makes ordinary movement across the battlefield dangerous for opponents who cannot destroy or repel the surrounding Blood.[2]

##### Blood Rain
Vlad spreads supernatural Blood throughout the atmosphere, creating crimson clouds that release Blood over the affected battlefield. The rain remains part of his larger Blood network and can serve as a medium for attacks, supernatural manifestations, and the expansion of his area of influence.[2]

##### Blood Weapon Creation
Vlad can condense his Blood into physical weapons, including swords and projectiles. His Blood sword can be wielded through conventional martial techniques while remaining connected to his supernatural abilities. He can also produce thousands of spikes, accelerate them to extraordinary speeds, and launch them toward opponents with sufficient force to damage powerful supernatural defenses.[8][2]

##### Blood-Soul Manifestation
Vlad can combine his Blood Manipulation with the Souls contained within his internal spiritual collection. Hands, monstrous heads, predatory creatures, and other manifestations emerge from the living Blood and attack opponents. Their appearance reflects the Souls previously consumed by Vlad, allowing the Blood Sea to function as a medium through which his accumulated spiritual resources become active combatants.[2]

##### Ancient Dragon Manifestation
Vlad can manifest the Soul of an Ancient Dragon he defeated and consumed during his earlier existence. The Dragon's head emerges from his supernatural Blood and releases a powerful Breath attack containing dangerous Draconic properties. [[Merlin]] considered the attack sufficiently threatening that he intervened to redirect it rather than allow Yama to suffer a direct hit.[2]

##### Internal Blood Rupture
Vlad can attempt to seize control of the Blood inside a living opponent, causing catastrophic internal damage. He successfully used this method against Yama during their initial exchanges, producing a violent rupture that destroyed the Demon King's body. Yama's external Ruler-based resurrection subsequently restored him, demonstrating the difference between physically defeating an opponent and permanently destroying their continued existence.[8]

##### Soul Destruction
Vlad can direct his Progenitor Authority against an opponent's Soul, inflicting spiritual damage capable of producing permanent death. During the confrontation against Shur, he repeatedly damaged the Elder God's physical body and Soul, but the external mechanism supporting Shur's immortality continued restoring him. Once that mechanism was removed, Vlad completed the destruction of Shur's Soul, permanently ending the Elder God's existence through ordinary resurrection and reincarnation methods.[4]

##### Sealing
Vlad possesses an undisclosed sealing method intended for opponents whose immortality prevents conventional elimination. He proposed using the technique against Shur if Haruna could provide a sufficient distraction. The method was not completed because Shur's external source of immortality failed, allowing Vlad to kill him permanently instead.[4]

#### Current Imperial-Era State
Vlad's development continued after the [[Elderblood Empire]] began transforming the former supernatural civilizations of Earth and [[Nightingale]]. He received advanced biological and technological support that strengthened his original Vampire physiology, helping him reach a condition closer to the ancestral state of his Race. The enhancements preserved his existence as a Vampire rather than converting him into a True Dragon.[5]
- Ancestral Vampire Advancement: Imperial support allowed Vlad to approach a more complete primal condition of Vampire physiology, improving characteristics associated with the ancient Race.[5]
- Enhanced Physical Characteristics: His body became considerably stronger after benefiting from Imperial biotechnology and resources.[5]
- Power Augmentation: Vlad increased his overall combat strength beyond the level he possessed before receiving Imperial assistance.[6]
- Biological Optimization: Imperial modifications eliminated numerous limitations associated with Noble Vampire physiology and improved the functioning of his body.[5]
- Dietary Independence: Vlad gained the ability to consume ordinary food without depending exclusively upon Blood for nourishment.[5]
- Racial Weakness Reduction: Most conventional weaknesses associated with Noble Vampire physiology were removed, although their complete individual list has not been specified.[5]
- Imperial Elite Combatant: Vlad belongs to the Elderblood Empire's elite forces, possessing combat strength far beyond the standards of Nightingale's earlier supernatural hierarchy.[6]
- Solar-System Destructive Capacity: Vlad possesses enough power to destroy an entire Solar System.[6]
- Pantheon-Level Destructive Capability: Vlad was already capable of single-handedly destroying conventional Pantheons before becoming stronger through later Imperial development.[6]
- Continued Racial Supremacy: Vlad remains stronger than [[Tatsuya Rider]], who is identified as the second strongest Noble Vampire.[6]
The exact formal name of Vlad's enhanced ancestral condition has not been provided. The enhancement should therefore not be assigned an invented racial designation or treated as proof of a transformation into a Dragon, Outer God, or unrelated species.[5]

#### Power
Vlad's historical combat status was that of one of Earth's strongest supernatural creatures and the dominant known Vampire Progenitor before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s emergence. His strength was not restricted to conventional physical combat, since his Blood and Soul authority allowed him to challenge beings possessing extraordinary durability, immortality, and resistance to ordinary supernatural attacks.[1][3]
His battle against Yama demonstrated the effectiveness of his advanced Blood Manipulation, martial arts, Soul manifestations, and ancestral Vampire physiology. Vlad could transform his body into living Blood, attack from numerous directions, manifest Souls numbering in the hundreds of thousands, and reproduce the dangerous Breath of an Ancient Dragon. His techniques were powerful enough that the surrounding Demonic forces required substantial supernatural barriers to prevent their battle from destroying the surrounding region.[8][2]
Against Shur, Vlad demonstrated his ability to inflict damage upon an Elder God's Soul and continue fighting through dangerous supernatural attacks. Although Shur's external immortality initially prevented his permanent death, the failure of that mechanism allowed Vlad to destroy him. The confrontation established that Vlad's Soul-based abilities can permanently eliminate exceptionally powerful beings when the specific mechanisms protecting their continued existence are overcome.[4]
With later Imperial assistance, Vlad advanced beyond the standard of strength he possessed during those encounters. He remains above [[Tatsuya Rider]] and belongs to the elite combatants capable of destroying entire Solar Systems. This advancement should not be confused with the cosmic level attained by Victor and the highest-ranking members of the [[Elderblood Empire]], whose later Draconic, Divine, and Eldritch powers belong to fundamentally different stages of development.[6]
