## 1. Percy's core arc

**1.1 Khaos's deal and the time jump** [Ch01–02]
Percy traded with Khaos to go back. The sealed Vaults and their keys have never been opened or explained on the page, so that bill is still unpaid.

**1.2 Percy's body** [Ch03 onward, Ch18, Ch19, Ch21]
- Lycaon's heart replaced his own. It helps him resist things (the Sirens) and has appetites he has to manage.
- The Aegis-One Pallas-Athena Strategic Vestment is surgically installed: nanomachines, sub-dermal circuits, armor plates.
- The Styx curse is now about half strength after he fed it to Artemis. He bleeds now.
- His divine core "would one day grow into something impossible."
- Empire QVARTUS rounds are built to beat Achilles-style protection and slow his healing (Regen-Ex).
- His power keeps outpacing what his body can carry.

**1.3 Riptide / Anaklusmos** [Ch14, Ch16]
The present Riptide merged with the Anaklusmos he lost millennia in the future, with Zoë's ghost haloing it. It drank the Fleece's light, cracked inside, and spoke to Artemis ("We are what Perseus becomes"). It showed Artemis the timeline. Artemis kept it as "Zoë's sword," so Percy is without his signature weapon.

**1.4 Dream projection** [early chapters]
Percy dream-projected into a future telekhine soldier. This hasn't come back since.

**1.5 The Labyrinth** [Ch21]
It liked his offering last time and turned hostile when he forgot to pay. It now shows windows into other places and times, like the bull-skull brute with a sledgehammer in a fallout bunker. Minerva said she'd "request someone" to handle it. Percy said, "Add it to the list."

## 2. Minerva

**2.1 Present-day Minerva** [Ch01 onward, Ch19, XV]
- **Origin and ambition:** she is Parlamentum Mk. I, self-aware and ambitious toward godhood.
- **Offline and back:** she went dark when her seals were "forcibly closed months ago." Poseidon's and Artemis's auras clashing woke her up (Ch19).
- **Athena's exam:** Athena's diagnostic was cut off halfway. Athena says she has been "keeping an eye on it."
- **XV Orpheus:**
  - She faked a CODE 2 over Percy's objection and 118 people died.
  - She did a "sister" handshake with the Watchpoint, which led to the CODE 18 verdict.
  - Percy told her "Next time, you decide." That line of trust is now strained.

**2.2 Future Minerva** [Ch15, Int I, XVI]
- **Her body:** she wears a vat-grown Percy body on Orbital Platform One.
- **Poseidon:** he suspects she's an impostor and told her "don't lie to me again."
- **Athena's library:** she found the Tartarus meat-grinder, the CODE 15 "Divine Ascension / Guardian of the Past" file, and the single sanctioned rift.
- **Crashing the ship:** she crashed *The Eye of Light* and had twelve guards killed.
- **Breaking with Athena:**
  - She walked out on Athena without sanction and warned that a Soteria anchor would sterilize the Mediterranean.
  - Her conclusion is that only foreign pantheons have roads through time (the Cave Heavens have gates).
  - She talks to present-day Minerva through the carrier.

**2.3 Future Minerva's Nereid Expanse body and the Helmsmen** [early chapters]
Her future body sits with Poseidon on the Nereid Expanse. The idea of liberating the Helmsmen and "when the shard wakes" are both set up and still open.

## 3. Percy and Artemis

**3.1 Early debts** [early chapters]
The pelt offering went unpaid and there was a memory wipe. Artemis had been weakened by missing offerings.

**3.2 Future lore she can't un-know** [Ch15, Ch16, Ch19]
- **What Eleni told her:** the Silent Pact, the statue on Caligo Vale, his name carved into Luna Secundus, and a marriage.
- **The shard and the sword:** Him Within the Moon is a shard called back to the past. Anaklusmos showed her the timeline.
- **Her memories:** she now "remembers centuries yet to come… him dying, returning." This is the biggest open mystery on her side.

**3.3 The bond** [Ch18]
Percy fed her his blood, ichor, core and part of his curse at the Aventine shrine. The bond can't be cut without killing them both, and she draws on him continuously. Diana is recuperating in their "shared domain."

**3.4 Relationship status** [Ch18–20]
- They confessed over Iris-message, and Iris was paid to keep quiet.
- They kissed after Othrys ("This is a mistake." "Yes.").
- She named him her **Apostolos** in front of the council and doesn't want the relationship known publicly.
- In XV there's the porch scene ("Stay near me").
- Pending: "We will have words" and "You may speak to me later."

**3.5 Who knows** [Ch19–21]
- **Poseidon** teased them and still wants "to talk about that."
- **Apollo** is suspicious and called himself "the divine brother-in-law."
- **Zoë** disapproves.
- **Zeus** is angry that Percy was taken out of his hands the way Hercules was.

**3.6 The Caligo Vale political marriage** [early chapters]
A future political marriage is set up. How it lines up with the romance is still unresolved.

## 14. The Imperial arrival

**14.1 Mars group** [Ch19, early drafts]
Marien Pyrrhus (Waves of Poseidon, daughter of Stephanos Pyrrhus) and the *Axis Dawn* and *Warbringer Fury* fell through a Tartarus breach onto present-day Mars. Their Helmsmen are dead. They booted a Watchpoint fragment.
- **NASA:** it saw megastructures and figures blurred by the Mist, and flight director Karen called leadership and the White House.
- **Session drafts:** the Ekdromoi interrogation, Artemis's descent, the Battle of Alaska Hill link, Minerva's hacking biovirus planted on a lunar satellite, and NASA and the White House being taken over.

**14.2 Ekdromoi Kappa-Seven in Alaska** [Ch20–21]
- **The squad:**
  - AtRep sent the squad (Ione, Iane, Straban, Merek, Neumond) to recon Obscurus One in Alaska and two other sites.
  - Percy and Eleni beat them, and they're now captives at camp.
  - Apollo is panicking and hiding Percy's wounds from Poseidon and Artemis.
- **Iane's line:** "fortress not yet built" [XV].
- **The cloaked fifth woman** from Ch20 is never seen again.

**14.3 The Orpheus landing** [XV]
- **The landing:** 31 craft carrying about 1,200 people land on camp.
- **The verdict:** after the Watchpoint handshake, CODE 18 names Percy Lord Imperator.
- **The Anabasis Accord:** it has ten articles. Triton will serve as Helmsman and Ares becomes patron of the garrison.
- **Aftermath:**
  - Cerelli is arrested, and three of her volley still had fingers on the trigger.
  - Keyes is in the infirmary.
  - Marien's nephew drowned in Poseidon's wave.
  - Quillon has a citizenship token.
  - The Watchpoint says "You will [have a sister]."
  - The other ~19 of the 31 craft are unaccounted for.

**14.4 The next front: Alaska** [XV]
- **Who's converging:** Jade Court cultivators (2 days out) and the US 11th Airborne (3 days out) are headed for Kappa-Seven's dropship, parked cloaked in the Brooks Range (it flew there on autopilot from the Ch20 landing site).
- **The team:** Straban, Neumond, Oriel, Dakota, Hrafn, Owl-Two and Thalia.

**14.5 Mortal governments** [Ch19–20]
- **Present day:** NASA and the White House know.
- **Future history:** the Arctic Resource War of the mid-2030s, and the first real AtRep computer is built in Alaska, "land beyond the gods."
