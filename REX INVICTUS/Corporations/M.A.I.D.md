Military Advanced Infantry Directorate
"Mors Arma Invicta Dirigit"
### Establishment

[[M.A.I.D]] was formally established in 1958 Old Terra Standard, within a decade of the founding consolidation of [[Institute Academy]]. The formation occurred during early terrestrial geopolitical instability when Institute leadership determined that technological superiority required a disciplined, loyal, and symbolically unified ground enforcement arm.

Unlike conventional militaries of the era, MAID was built as a purpose-designed force: technologically synchronized, ideologically aligned, and culturally engineered for psychological presence.

From its inception, MAID was structured not as a temporary security apparatus but as a permanent expeditionary corps.

---

### Force Size and Composition

Current Estimated Strength:

- ~186,000 active-duty operators
    
- ~42,000 reserve and rapid-deployment cadre
    
- 12 operational divisions
    
- 3 urban pacification corps
    
- 1 ceremonial and strategic honor guard detachment
    

Personnel selection criteria prioritize:

- Exceptional physical resilience
    
- Neural compatibility with Institute battlefield overlays
    
- Psychological stability under prolonged mechanized assault conditions
    
- Absolute loyalty to [[Institute Academy]]
    

Recruitment is voluntary but highly selective. Attrition rates during qualification are severe.

### Hierarchy Structure (French Military Rank Doctrine)

MAID employs a distinctly French-inspired command hierarchy, preserved as institutional tradition from its Old Terra origins.

**Commissioned Command Ranks:**

- [[Maréchale-Commandante]] — Supreme Operational Commander of MAID
    
- [[Générale de Corps]] — Corps-level commander
    
- [[Générale de Division]] — Division-level commander
    
- [[Générale de Brigade]] — Brigade-level commander
    
- [[Colonelle]] — Regiment commander
    
- [[Lieutenante-Colonelle]] — Deputy regiment commander
    
- [[Commandante]] — Battalion commander
    
- [[Capitaine]] — Company commander
    
- [[Lieutenante]] — Platoon leader
    
- [[Sous-Lieutenante]] — Junior platoon officer
    

**Non-Commissioned Structure:**

- [[Adjudante]]
    
- [[Sergente-Majore]]
    
- [[Sergente]]

### Organizational Divisions

MAID is divided into:

1. [[Armored Assault Divisions]] — Heavy mechanized infantry
    
2. [[Urban Tactical Corps]] — Dense-environment pacification and enforcement
    
3. [[Expeditionary Shock Units]] — Rapid planetary deployment
    
4. [[Honor Guard of Athena]] — Ceremonial and elite combat formation
