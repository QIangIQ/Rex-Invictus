**[[Private Military Company (PMC)]]** is a firm that sells armed security, combat or military support services.

---

## History (IRL)

Mercenary companies are old, from Greek hoplites hired by Persia to the condottieri of Renaissance Italy (see [[Condottiero]]). The modern industry grew after the Cold War with firms such as Executive Outcomes, Sandline, Blackwater and the Wagner Group. The Montreux Document (2008) summarizes the international law that applies to them.

## UOE (far future)

Mercenary Liaison Organization (MLO) aggregators such as [[Akvantages Mercenary Liaison Organization]] and [[Aldra Mercenary Liaison Organization]] broker PMC contracts, and [[Mercenary Solutions and Advisory Corporation (MSAC)]] is a major firm.
