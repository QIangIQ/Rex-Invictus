> **Rex Invictus canon.** Flamel material is adapted into the Rex Invictus present (c. 2021). Rex Invictus wins any conflict; [[Robespierre]] fills [[Dr. John Dee]]'s role and hunts the Philosopher's Stone rather than [[The Codex]]. See [[Flamel Series#Integration rules]].

## Real-World Background (IRL)

A vetala is a spirit of Hindu folklore that animates corpses in cremation grounds; the *Vetala Panchavimshati* tells 25 riddles a vetala poses to King Vikramaditya.

### The Sorceress
While [[Nicholas Flamel]] was trying to contact his wife Perenelle, who at the time was imprisoned on [[Alcatraz]], two Vetala attacked Pernelle. Despite [[Juan Manuel de Ayala]]'s best attempts to scare them away, they all failed. It was only when the Sphinx discovered where Perenelle was, that the Vetala fled.
The Vetala, as stated by Perenelle, are from a different branch of Vampire and drink the blood of their victims. There are apparently a few of them imprisoned on Alcatraz.

### Appearance
They stand at least as tall as [[Josh Newman|Josh]] and have a completely human head with long fangs and red eyes. Below their head, however, they are skeletally thin with bones clearly visible beneath their flesh. They possess razor sharp claws on their hands and feet and large, leathery bat wings grow out of their backs.

### Powers & Abilities
- [[Immortality]]: Like most [[Next Generation]], the Vetala do not age.
- Superhuman Strength: Despite their appearance, they are able to break through a solid stone wall.
- Superhuman Speed: Their speed is paranormal.
- Fangs & Claws: They wield razor-sharp claws and fangs.
- Flight: The Vetala can use their wings to fly.
