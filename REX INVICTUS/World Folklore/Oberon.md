**[[Oberon]]** is the king of the fairies in medieval and Renaissance literature.

---

## Folklore (IRL)

He first appears as the dwarf-king Auberon in the 13th-century French romance *Huon of Bordeaux*, a son of [[Julius Caesar]] and Morgan le Fay who aids the hero with a magic horn and cup. The name descends from Alberich, the dwarf of Germanic legend. [[William Shakespeare]] made him the jealous fairy king of *A Midsummer Night's Dream* (about 1595), quarreling with his queen Titania over a changeling boy and sending Puck to fetch the love-juice flower. Spenser's *Faerie Queene* makes him the father of Gloriana.
