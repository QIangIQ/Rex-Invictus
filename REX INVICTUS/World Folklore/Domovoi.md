**[[Domovoi]]**: The Domovoi are small, hairy, dwarf-like creatures which speak in an unknown and baffling language. (Lore adapted from *The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** Flamel material is adapted into the Rex Invictus present (c. 2021). Rex Invictus wins any conflict; [[Robespierre]] fills [[Dr. John Dee]]'s role and hunts the Philosopher's Stone rather than [[The Codex]]. See [[Flamel Series#Integration rules]].

## Real-World Background (IRL)

The domovoi is the household spirit of Slavic folklore, who protects the home if treated well.

| Field | Value |

The Domovoi are small, hairy, dwarf-like creatures which speak in an unknown and baffling language. In the series, they are one of the many creatures to be imprisoned by [[Dr. John Dee]] on [[Alcatraz]]. In The Enchantress, they, along with many of the other monsters freed from the prison, battle against [[Odin]], Mars, and Hel.
