**[[Lammergeier]]** is the bearded vulture (*Gypaetus barbatus*), a large Old World vulture of high mountains from the Pyrenees to the Himalaya.

---

## Natural History (IRL)

Its wingspan reaches nearly three metres. It is the only bird that lives almost entirely on bone; it swallows bones whole and drops larger ones from height onto rocks to crack them. Adults stain their pale feathers rust-red by bathing in iron-rich mud. The German name means "lamb vulture", from the false belief that it carried off lambs. The ancient story that Aeschylus was killed by a tortoise dropped by an eagle is usually taken to describe this bird.
