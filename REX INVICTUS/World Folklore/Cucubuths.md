A Cucubuth is a hybrid cross between a [[Torc Madra]] and a Vampire. Their males, as seen in The Necromancer, are described as shaven-headed youths. They are immensely strong and fast and can transform at will into enormous wolf-like creatures, though retaining a human face. They tend to travel in very large packs.

### Race Information

#### Status
Extant

#### Related races
Vampires, [[Torc Madra]]

### Traits

#### Lifespan

#### Powers
Superhuman strength and speed, immortality

#### Population
At least a few dozen

### Trivia
- They are enemies of the [[Dearg Due]].
- According to [[Palamedes]], they are "dumb as dirt".
