Areop-Enap is a huge, spider-like Elder goddess worshiped by the Nauru people as a goddess of creation. In recent history she was imprisoned by [[Dr. John Dee]] on [[Alcatraz]] with symbols that poison Next Generations, luckily not being an next generation, and falling asleep, until she was released by [[Nicholas Flamel|Perenelle Flamel]], with whom she trapped [[The Crow Goddess|the Morrigan]], making her "fall asleep," and releasing Morrigan's two sisters.

### Appearance
Areop-Enap was at one point considered beautiful: however, like most of her kind her appearance was warped by the Change. [[The Change]] influenced Areop greatly, transforming her body into that of a huge spider coated with gray-tipped purple hair. Although her head retains a fairly human shape, she lacks nose, ears, eyebrows, her mouth is a simple vertical slit with fangs and flanked with mandibles on each side - both of which cause her  to speak with pronounced lisp - and she possesses eight eyes said to be the color of an old bruise. Each of her legs was as wide as [[Nicholas Flamel|Perenelle]]'s waist. She took great offense when Abraham said that the change was a reflection of her true self.

### Abilities
Areop-Enap is described as being a very powerful Elder. Her powers are included, but not limited to
- Animal Command: Areop-Enap is a master or god of spiders. She can summon spiders at will, and can order vast armies of them to follow basic commands.
- Web-Spinning: Areop-Enap is able to spin incredibly tough spider's web that is inches thick. She uses this to help trap [[The Crow Goddess|the Morrigan]].
- Magical Strength: As an Elder, Areop-Enap likely has considerable magical strength.
- Resilient: Areop-Enap can survive in extreme conditions for long periods of time. She survived a mountain being dropped on her by [[Nicholas Flamel|Perenelle]], and has "survived worse" (likely during the fall of [[Danu Talis]]).
- Self-Augmentation: By hibernating inside a cocoon made of her own webs, Areop-Enap managed to grow into a gigantic size, and become powerful enough to easily defeat [[Karkinos]], a colossal monstrous crab.
- [[Immortality]]: Areop-Enap is Immortal and does not age. Though an immortal may be poisoned or slain in battle, they may live for thousands or millions of years.

### The Magician
Following [[Nicholas Flamel|Perenelle]]'s temporary escape from the Sphinx, [[Juan Manuel de Ayala]] scouts the island of [[Alcatraz]] to search for anything that may help her leave the island without being followed or at least deal with the Sphinx. Ayala finds an area deep beneath the Island that he cannot enter, and suspects whatever is hidden there must be a threat to [[Dr. John Dee|Dee]], and thus potentially of use to her. Following a trail of spiders leads her to a room containing the Spider goddess, using ancient symbols to keep her in place and restrict her power. Despite Perenelle having encountered her in the past (worshippers of Areop-Enap had tried to sacrifice her to activate an extinct volcano; Perenelle in turn fought back and brought down an entire mountain on her), Perenelle forms an alliance with the Elder until they are both free. Together they manage to lure [[The Crow Goddess|the Morrigan]] and trap her using the same symbols that detained the Spider Goddess.

### The Sorceress
Following the incapacitation of [[The Crow Goddess|the Morrigan]], [[Billy the Kid]] sends a wave of poisoned flies towards the island. Thousands of Areop-Enap's spiders die defending their master, while [[Nicholas Flamel|Perenelle]] lures the flies away from the island with the scent of garbage, but the Elder is still harmed enough by the poison that she needs to sleep and heal from the poison.

### The Enchantress
Areop-Enap was hibernating on [[Alcatraz]] while the monsters were lose on the island. [[Nicholas Flamel|The Flamels]], [[Billy the Kid]], [[Black Hawk]], and [[Niccolo Machiavelli]] needed to awaken her to defeat [[Karkinos]]. She awoke at the very last moment and easily defeated the crab.
