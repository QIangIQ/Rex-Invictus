> **Rex Invictus canon.** Flamel material is adapted into the Rex Invictus present (c. 2021). Rex Invictus wins any conflict; [[Robespierre]] fills [[Dr. John Dee]]'s role and hunts the Philosopher's Stone rather than [[The Codex]]. See [[Flamel Series#Integration rules]].

## Real-World Background (IRL)

A wight is an old English word for a living being; in later fantasy it means an undead creature, from the barrow-wights of Tolkien and the Norse draugr.

Wights are creatures of unknown origin that wield or control maladies to destroy populations. In The Alchemyst, [[Nicholas Flamel]] claims that the wights of Hunger and Disease were sent after Perenelle and himself by [[Dr. John Dee|John Dee]] while they were staying in Ireland, causing the 1845 Great Famine.

### Race Information

#### Status
Unknown

#### Controller(s)
[[Dr. John Dee]]

### Traits

#### Powers
Some control over a particular malady

### Powers and Abilities
- Artisans of ailment: Wights have great control over their respective aspects of malady. Together, Hunger and Disease were able to create the potato blight, causing mass starvation and severe outbreaks of disease. The magnitude of the epidemic suggests that the wights are able to control their powers over large distances and attack many targets at once.
