**[[Superior Human Physiology]]** is strength, speed, durability and endurance beyond any human. Gods have it by nature; in PJO canon demigods show a lesser form, and [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] gains near-invulnerability from the Curse of Achilles in *The Last Olympian*.

**Listed for:** [[Apollo]], [[Gaia]], [[Hades]], [[Hephaestus]], [[Percy Jackson]], [[Poseidon]], [[Zeus]]

See [[Pyrokinesis]], [[Hydroknesis]], [[Geoknesis]], [[Electrokinesis]] and [[Ability Cassification]].
