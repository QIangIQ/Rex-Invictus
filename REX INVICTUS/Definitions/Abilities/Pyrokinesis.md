**[[Pyrokinesis]]** is the power to create, shape and command fire and heat. In PJO canon it is rare among children of [[Hephaestus]] (Leo Valdez is the first in centuries) and belongs to gods of fire and forge such as [[Hephaestus]], [[Hestia]] and [[Apollo]] in his solar aspect.

The sub-powers below are the ones the vault lists for gods and demigods. Each section names who has it.

---

## Fire Generation

Creating fire from nothing or from the body.

**Listed for:** [[Frank Zhang]], [[Hestia]], [[Leo]], [[Phlegethon]]

## Fire Projection

Throwing fire as blasts, streams or bolts.

**Listed for:** [[Hestia]], [[Leo]], [[Phlegethon]]

## Fire Attacks

Fire-based combat techniques in general.

**Listed for:** [[Hestia]], [[Leo]], [[Mrs. O'Leary]], [[Phlegethon]]

## Fire Armor

Wrapping the body in protective flame.

**Listed for:** [[Hestia]], [[Leo]], [[Phlegethon]]

## Fire Immunity

Immunity to burning by fire.

**Listed for:** [[Hestia]], [[Leo]], [[Phlegethon]]

## Heat Immunity

Immunity to extreme temperatures, including molten metal.

**Listed for:** [[Apollo]], [[Hades]], [[Hephaestus]], [[Hestia]], [[Leo]], [[Phlegethon]]

## Heat Absorption

Drawing heat out of surroundings or attacks to grow stronger or cool an area.

**Listed for:** [[Apollo]], [[Hephaestus]], [[Mrs. O'Leary]]

## Self Combustion

Igniting one's own body without harm.

**Listed for:** [[Apollo]], [[Frank Zhang]], [[Hephaestus]], [[Hestia]], [[Leo]], [[Phlegethon]]

## Fire Infusion

Charging weapons or objects with fire.

**Listed for:** [[Apollo]], [[Hephaestus]]

## Greek Fire Manipulation

Control of Greek fire, the Byzantine incendiary that burned on water; in PJO canon a green liquid fire used by demigods.

**Listed for:** [[Apollo]], [[Hades]], [[Hephaestus]], [[Hestia]], [[Leo]], [[Phlegethon]]

## Magma Manipulation

Shaping molten rock.

**Listed for:** [[Hestia]], [[Leo]], [[Phlegethon]]

## Incineration

Burning a target to ash.

**Listed for:** [[Zeus]]

## Energy Manipulation

Shaping raw energy, here as the source of flame.

**Listed for:** [[Apollo]], [[Hades]], [[Hephaestus]]

## Purification

Cleansing corruption by fire, as the hearth fire of Hestia purifies.

**Listed for:** [[Apollo]], [[Hades]], [[Hephaestus]]
