**[[Electrokinesis]]** is the power to generate and control electricity and storms. In PJO canon it is the gift of children of [[Zeus]] and [[Jupiter]], such as [[Thalia Grace]] and [[Jason Grace]].

The sub-powers below are the ones the vault lists for gods and demigods. Each section names who has it.

---

## Electricity Generation

Producing electric current or lightning.

**Listed for:** [[Zeus]]

## Electricity Attacks

Lightning-based combat techniques.

**Listed for:** [[Zeus]]

## Electrical Telekinesis

Moving metal objects with electromagnetic force.

**Listed for:** [[Zeus]]

## Electricity Detection

Sensing currents and electrical devices.

**Listed for:** [[Zeus]]

## Electrical Regeneration

Healing by absorbing electricity.

**Listed for:** [[Zeus]]

## Electrical Immunity

Immunity to electric shock and lightning.

**Listed for:** [[Zeus]]

## Thunderstorm Creation

Summoning thunderstorms.

**Listed for:** [[Zeus]]

## Storm Manipulation

Controlling storms and weather.

**Listed for:** [[Zeus]]

## Light Manipulation

Shaping light, including the flash of lightning.

**Listed for:** [[Zeus]]
