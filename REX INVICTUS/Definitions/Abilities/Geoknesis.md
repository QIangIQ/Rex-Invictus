**[[Geoknesis]]** is the power to command earth and stone. It belongs to [[Gaia]] and her children, and in PJO canon to Earth-related powers such as Hazel Levesque's command of precious metals.

The sub-powers below are the ones the vault lists for gods and demigods. Each section names who has it.

---

## Crystal Manipulation

Shaping crystals and gemstones.

**Listed for:** [[Gaia]]

## Density Manipulation

Altering an object's density, making earth hard as steel or soft as sand.

**Listed for:** [[Gaia]]

## Gravitational Manipulation

Altering gravity, a planetary trait of the Earth Mother.

**Listed for:** [[Gaia]], [[Parzival]]

## Earth Constructs

Raising walls, golems and weapons from the ground.

**Listed for:** [[Gaia]]

## Earth Purification

Cleansing poisoned soil and land.

**Listed for:** [[Gaia]]
