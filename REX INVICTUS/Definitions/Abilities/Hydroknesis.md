**[[Hydroknesis]]** is the power to control water. In PJO canon it is the signature gift of children of [[Poseidon]], displayed by [[Percy Jackson]] from *The Lightning Thief* onward.

The sub-powers below are the ones the vault lists for gods and demigods. Each section names who has it.

---

## Hydro-Telekinesis

Moving water at will, from waves to single drops.

**Listed for:** [[Percy Jackson]], [[Poseidon]]

## Water Detection

Sensing water and moisture nearby, including in living bodies.

**Listed for:** [[Percy Jackson]], [[Poseidon]]

## Hydrokinetic Regeneration

Healing faster when touching or immersed in water, as Percy does.

**Listed for:** [[Percy Jackson]], [[Poseidon]]

## Property Manipulation

Changing water's properties, such as pressure or temperature.

**Listed for:** [[Percy Jackson]], [[Poseidon]]

## Viscosity Manipulation

Making water thicker or thinner, as when Percy hardens it into a platform.

**Listed for:** [[Percy Jackson]], [[Poseidon]]

## State Manipulation

Turning water to ice or steam.

**Listed for:** [[Percy Jackson]], [[Poseidon]]

## Blood Manipulation

Controlling the water in blood. In *The House of Hades* Percy chokes the goddess Akhlys with poison this way and frightens Annabeth.

**Listed for:** [[Percy Jackson]], [[Poseidon]], [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]]
