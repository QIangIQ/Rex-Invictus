**[[Pandora's Pithos]]** is the jar (*pithos*, mistranslated as "box" by Erasmus) that Pandora, the first woman made by [[Hephaestus]], opened, releasing every evil into the world. Only [[Elpis]], hope, remained inside ([[Hesiod]], *Works and Days*). In [[The Last Olympian]] [[Kronos]]'s forces use it to tempt [[Percy Jackson]], who gives it to [[Hestia]] for safekeeping.
