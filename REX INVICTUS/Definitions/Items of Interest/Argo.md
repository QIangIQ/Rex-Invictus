**[[Argo]]** was the ship of [[Jason (Argonaut)|Jason]] and the Argonauts.

---

## Greek History (IRL)

The shipwright Argus built it with [[Athena]]'s help, and she set into the prow a beam of the oracular oak of Dodona that could speak. The crew included [[Hercules|Heracles]], Orpheus, Castor and Pollux, [[Peleus]] and in some versions [[Atlanta|Atalanta]]. It sailed through the Clashing Rocks to Colchis to bring back the [[Golden Fleece]], and was afterwards dedicated to [[Poseidon]] and set among the stars as the constellation Argo Navis.

## PJO Canon

The *Argo II*, built by Leo Valdez of [[Hephaestus' Cabin]] with the bronze dragon [[Festus]] as its figurehead, carries the seven demigods of [[The Heroes of Olympus]] to Greece.
