**[[Human Neuroanatomy]]** is a reference for the brain structures named in [[Metaphysical Null Phenomenon]].

- **Cerebrum.** The two cerebral hemispheres, joined by the **corpus callosum**, a band of about 200 million axons.
- **Frontal lobe.** Planning, decisions, movement (primary motor cortex) and speech (Broca's area).
- **Parietal lobe.** Touch and body position (somatosensory cortex) and spatial attention.
- **Temporal lobe.** Hearing, language comprehension (Wernicke's area) and memory, with the **hippocampus** (forming new memories) and the **amygdala** (fear and emotional salience) inside it.
- **Thalamus.** Relays sensory signals to the cortex. The **hypothalamus** below it controls hormones, temperature, hunger and sleep.
- **Cells.** **Neurons** carry signals. **Oligodendroglia** wrap axons in **myelin** for fast conduction in the brain and spinal cord; **astrocytes** support neurons and the blood-brain barrier.
- **Spinal cord.** Carries signals between brain and body, protected by the vertebrae.
- **Blood supply.** Two **internal carotid arteries** and two **vertebral arteries** feed the brain and join in the **circle of Willis**, a ring at the base of the brain that lets flow reroute if one vessel is blocked.
