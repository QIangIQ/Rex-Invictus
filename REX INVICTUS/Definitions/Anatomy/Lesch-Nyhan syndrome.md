**[[Lesch-Nyhan syndrome]]** is a rare inherited disorder caused by a lack of the enzyme HGPRT (hypoxanthine-guanine phosphoribosyltransferase).

---

## Medicine (IRL)

It is X-linked, so it almost always affects boys. Without the enzyme the body overproduces uric acid, causing gout, kidney stones and joint damage, and the basal ganglia develop abnormally, see [[Human Neuroanatomy]]. Children show severe movement disorders and, most strikingly, compulsive self-injury such as biting the lips and fingers, which they cannot stop despite feeling pain. Michael Lesch and William Nyhan described it in 1964. Allopurinol controls the uric acid but not the neurological symptoms.
