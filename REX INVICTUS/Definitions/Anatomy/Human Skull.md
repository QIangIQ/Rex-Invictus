The **[[Human Skull]]** has 22 bones. The cranium enclosing the brain has eight: one frontal bone (forehead), two parietal bones (top and sides), two temporal bones (holding the ear canals), one occipital bone (back, with the foramen magnum where the [[Human Neuroanatomy|spinal cord]] enters), plus the sphenoid and ethmoid. The other fourteen form the face. Sutures join the cranial bones and fuse in adulthood. See [[Human Neuroanatomy]] and [[Metaphysical Null Phenomenon]].
