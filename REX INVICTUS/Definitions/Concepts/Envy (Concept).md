### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 682
Envy was a powerful Demon, one of the Seven Deadly Sins serving [[Diablo]] and the Demon occupying the position of the Sin of Envy. Unlike several members of Diablo's forces whose abilities were only implied through their titles, Envy directly demonstrated a specialized supernatural power capable of rapidly stealing another Being's Energy. During the final stage of the Demonic War, Diablo deployed him together with Sloth against Michael, combining Sloth's supernatural lethargy with Envy's Energy-draining ability to weaken the Archangel.[1]
Envy survived that encounter long enough to participate in the wider battle against Heaven, but he, Wrath and Pride were eventually overwhelmed and severely injured by the Seven Virtues. The three could no longer continue fighting and were removed from the active battlefield by other Demons. Shortly afterward, the [[Heavenly Father]] unleashed Heavenly Judgment, covering the battlefield with overwhelming Holy Light. Envy was among the Deadly Sins completely evaporated by the attack, with Sloth being the sole surviving Sin caught within the Judgment.[2]

### Physical Description
Envy's complete physical appearance is not described in sufficient detail within his brief appearance. The novel does not establish his height, build, hair color, eye color, clothing, horns, wings or any distinctive True Form before his death.[1]
Consequently, the artwork currently associated with Envy should be treated as an artistic interpretation rather than a definitive canonical design.

### Personality and Traits
Envy receives very little individual characterization because he appears only during the final stages of the Demonic War. He remained obedient to [[Diablo]] and immediately participated in the coordinated attack upon Michael when ordered, demonstrating that he was capable of working tactically with another Deadly Sin rather than behaving as a purely independent combatant.[1]
His behavior against Michael was opportunistic and efficient. Rather than challenging one of Heaven's greatest Archangels through a straightforward exchange of physical attacks, Envy took advantage of the opening created by Sloth's lethargy-inducing power and attached his own draining ability to Michael. The combination forced Michael to divide his attention between resisting supernatural sleepiness and dealing with the rapid loss of his Energy reserves.[1]
Beyond this, the novel never explores Envy's personal ambitions, motivations or the psychological influence of the Sin he represented. Although his title identifies him as the Sin of Envy, characteristics such as obsessive jealousy, copying another person's powers through envy or becoming stronger by coveting others should not be treated as canon unless directly demonstrated.

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Envy entered the conflict during the increasingly chaotic final phase of the Demonic War, when Heaven and Hell had committed many of their strongest remaining combatants. At [[Diablo]]'s command, Envy and Sloth moved against Michael, one of the most powerful Archangels participating in the battle. Sloth initiated the coordinated assault by speaking to Michael in her characteristically exhausted manner, causing an abnormal feeling of sleepiness to spread through him and forcing the Archangel to consciously resist the urge to sleep.[1]
Envy immediately exploited the distraction. He released a dark supernatural manifestation that attached itself to Michael and began consuming or draining his Energy. Michael quickly noticed that his reserves were decreasing at an abnormal rate, making Envy's contribution fundamentally different from a conventional damaging attack: the ability directly reduced the power Michael had available for continued combat. Combined with Sloth's lethargy, the attack placed Michael under simultaneous pressure from two different supernatural effects and demonstrated why Diablo deployed the Sins in coordinated groups against Heaven's elites.[1]
The wider battle continued after their attack on Michael. Heaven's remaining elites eventually counterattacked with the Seven Virtues, and Envy fought alongside the other Sins opposing them. By the following stage of the conflict, Envy, Wrath and Pride had accumulated numerous wounds from the Virtues and were no longer capable of continuing active combat. Other Demons carried the injured Sins away from the immediate fighting while Heaven similarly suffered catastrophic casualties among its own forces.[2]
Their removal from the front lines was ultimately meaningless. The losses suffered by Heaven caused Ariel to appeal directly to the [[Heavenly Father]], who ordered the remaining Angels to retreat before invoking Heavenly Judgment. Vast quantities of Holy Light descended across the battlefield, evaporating approximately half of the remaining Demonic forces and destroying the surviving Deadly Sins caught within the attack. Envy was completely erased alongside the other Sins, while Sloth survived only because the enormous quantity of Miasma surrounding her prevented the Judgment from fully reaching her.[2]
Because Envy was destroyed through overwhelming Holy Light, his death bypassed the normal ability of Demons to eventually reconstruct themselves within Hell after conventional death. Powerful Demons can ordinarily require long periods to reform, but Holy Light and certain other methods can prevent that process entirely.[3]

### Skills & Abilities
Envy was one of [[Diablo]]'s Seven Deadly Sins and consequently belonged among the elite Demonic forces participating in the war against Heaven. His brief appearance prevents a complete reconstruction of his power set, but unlike several other Sins, Envy directly demonstrated the mechanics of at least one individualized supernatural ability.
- Demon Physiology: Envy was a high-ranking Infernal Demon capable of participating in battles involving Angels, Virtues and other elite supernatural combatants. His Demonic physiology granted supernatural physical abilities, longevity and compatibility with the infernal Energies of [[Biblical Hell]].
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics: As one of the Seven Deadly Sins, Envy possessed physical and supernatural attributes vastly beyond ordinary Demons. He remained involved in the battle against Heaven's elite forces until the Seven Virtues inflicted enough damage to incapacitate him.[2]
- Demonic Energy: Envy possessed the supernatural Energy system associated with powerful Demons. The exact nature of his ordinary Demonic techniques is not individually explored.
- Conditional [[Immortality]] / Demonic Reconstitution: Demons killed through conventional means can eventually reconstruct themselves within Hell, with stronger Demons generally requiring greater amounts of time to return. Holy Light, specialized anti-Demon weapons and complete absorption can bypass this mechanism.[3] Envy's destruction through Heavenly Judgment consequently became permanent.[2]
- Sin of Envy: Envy held the supernatural position associated with the Deadly Sin of Envy. The novel does not explicitly explain whether his Energy-draining ability was the direct manifestation of this Sin or merely one of his personal Demonic powers. Other envy-themed abilities should therefore not be added without evidence.
- Energy Drain: Envy can rapidly remove supernatural Energy from another Being. When his ability was used against Michael, the Archangel's reserves began decreasing at an extreme rate, temporarily reducing the amount of power available to him during the battle.[1]
- Rapid Energy Depletion: The drain operates quickly enough for Michael to immediately notice that his reserves were disappearing at an abnormal rate.[1]
- Power Weakening: By reducing the Energy available to a supernatural opponent, Envy can indirectly weaken their ability to sustain techniques, continue fighting or maintain maximum output.
- High-Level Target Effectiveness: Envy's Energy Drain successfully affected Michael, establishing that the ability can function against an extremely powerful Archangel rather than only against ordinary opponents.[1]
- Parasitic Energy Manifestation: Envy's draining ability manifested through a dark supernatural entity or mass that attached itself to Michael and began consuming his Energy.[1]
- Parasitic Attachment: The manifestation can latch onto a target rather than requiring Envy to maintain continuous direct physical contact.
- Sustained Energy Consumption: Once attached, the manifestation continues reducing the victim's Energy reserves until the effect is interrupted or removed.
- Remote Drain Application: Envy does not need to personally remain in hand-to-hand contact with the victim for the draining process to function after the manifestation has attached itself.
- Energy Absorption (Limited/Unclear): Envy demonstrably removes Energy from his target, but the chapter does not sufficiently establish whether the stolen Energy is transferred into Envy himself, stored within the parasitic manifestation or simply dissipated. It is therefore more accurate to classify the demonstrated effect primarily as Energy Drain rather than unrestricted Energy Absorption.
- Status-Effect Coordination: Envy effectively combined his ability with Sloth's supernatural lethargy. While Michael concentrated on resisting sleep, Envy attacked his Energy reserves, forcing the Archangel to simultaneously defend against multiple different supernatural effects.[1]
- Team Combat: Envy demonstrated effective coordination with another Deadly Sin rather than interfering with Sloth's ability or independently attacking without strategy.[1]
- Demonic Resilience: Envy survived the initial engagements of the final war and remained alive after receiving numerous wounds from the Seven Virtues, although those injuries eventually became severe enough to incapacitate him.[2]

#### Energy Drain
Envy's most important demonstrated ability is his capacity to forcibly reduce another Being's supernatural Energy reserves. The technique is especially dangerous against opponents whose combat capability depends upon maintaining large quantities of Energy over a prolonged battle.
Against Michael, Envy did not merely strike the Archangel with a conventional projectile. A dark manifestation originating from Envy attached itself to Michael and immediately began draining his reserves at an extreme rate.[1] Because Michael was simultaneously resisting Sloth's supernatural sleep effect, the combination prevented him from focusing entirely upon removing or countering Envy's attack.
The ability therefore functions as a form of supernatural attrition. Even an opponent physically stronger than Envy can become progressively less capable of maintaining high-output techniques if the drain remains attached long enough. Its successful use on Michael demonstrates considerable potency, although the chapter does not establish that Envy could completely empty the Archangel's reserves before Michael found a countermeasure.
The exact relationship between this ability and the Sin of Envy is unknown. It is possible that stealing what another Being possesses thematically corresponds to Envy, but the novel never directly states this connection. The ability should consequently remain indexed by its demonstrated function rather than speculative conceptual mechanics.

#### Power
Envy's exact position relative to the other Seven Deadly Sins is never explicitly ranked. His status nevertheless places him far above ordinary Demonic soldiers, and he was trusted by [[Diablo]] to attack Michael directly alongside Sloth.[1]
The successful Energy Drain against Michael should not be interpreted as proof that Envy possessed greater overall combat power than the Archangel. The attack occurred as part of a coordinated ambush in which Sloth had already imposed supernatural lethargy upon Michael, creating the opportunity Envy exploited.[1]
Similarly, Envy's later incapacitation does not prove that one particular Virtue individually surpassed him. Chapter 683 describes Envy, Wrath and Pride as heavily wounded during the broader engagement against the Seven Virtues rather than documenting a conclusive one-on-one defeat for each Sin.[2]

#### Weaknesses
- [[Holy Energy]] / Holy Light: As a Demon, Envy possessed an inherent disadvantage against sufficiently powerful Holy Energy. The Seven Virtues were able to inflict numerous injuries upon him during the Demonic War.[2]
- Permanent Destruction by Holy Light: Ordinary Demonic reconstitution can be prevented by Holy Light. Envy was completely evaporated by the [[Heavenly Father]]'s Heavenly Judgment and therefore did not reform within Hell.[2][3]
- Higher Divine Power: Envy remained vulnerable to sufficiently powerful Divine opponents despite being one of the Seven Deadly Sins. The Seven Virtues collectively incapacitated him, and the Heavenly Father's mass Judgment completely destroyed him.[2]
- Energy Drain Requires Successful Application: Envy's principal demonstrated ability depended upon attaching the draining manifestation to his opponent. The novel does not establish that he could automatically drain Energy from any target regardless of distance, defense or resistance.
- Unknown Drain Limit: The maximum amount of Energy Envy could consume, the duration for which his parasite could remain active and whether sufficiently powerful opponents could forcibly remove it are not explained.

### Relationships

#### Diablo
Envy served [[Diablo]] as one of his Seven Deadly Sins and obeyed his commands during the final Demonic War. Diablo personally deployed Envy and Sloth against Michael, indicating that he considered their combined abilities useful against one of Heaven's most powerful combatants.[1]
Envy remained with Diablo's faction until Heaven's counterattack incapacitated him and Heavenly Judgment permanently destroyed him.[2]

#### Sloth Abyss Verneila
Sloth was Envy's principal on-page combat partner. Their abilities complemented one another particularly well: Sloth imposed supernatural fatigue and the desire to sleep upon Michael, while Envy attached his Energy-draining manifestation to the distracted Archangel.[1]
The encounter demonstrates that the Seven Deadly Sins were capable of coordinated combat rather than functioning exclusively as isolated elite warriors.

#### Michael
Michael was Envy's only individually identified target. Envy and Sloth attacked him together under [[Diablo]]'s orders, with Envy successfully attaching his draining ability and rapidly reducing Michael's Energy reserves.[1]
The encounter demonstrates the potency of Envy's specialized ability but does not establish that Envy could defeat Michael alone.

#### Seven Deadly Sins
Envy was one of the Seven Deadly Sins alongside Pride, Wrath, Greed, Gluttony, Lust and Sloth. During the final Heavenly counterattack, Envy, Wrath and Pride were severely injured by the Seven Virtues and carried away from the immediate battle. Heavenly Judgment subsequently destroyed the Sins caught within its range, with Sloth being the only one to survive.[2]

### Trivia
- Envy is one of the few original Seven Deadly Sins whose individualized supernatural ability is directly demonstrated before his death.[1]
- His first direct appearance occurs in Chapter 682 when [[Diablo]] deploys him alongside Sloth against Michael.[1]
- Envy's demonstrated specialty is the rapid depletion of another Being's supernatural Energy reserves.[1]
- His Energy Drain was powerful enough to affect Michael, one of Heaven's strongest Archangels.[1]
- The draining effect was delivered through a dark parasitic manifestation that attached itself to Michael.[1]
- The novel does not clarify whether Energy removed from a victim is transferred into Envy, stored by the parasitic manifestation or simply consumed.
- The relationship between Envy's Energy Drain and his position as the Sin of Envy is not explicitly explained.
- Envy, Wrath and Pride were all incapacitated by injuries received during the battle with the Seven Virtues.[2]
- He was permanently destroyed by the [[Heavenly Father]]'s Heavenly Judgment.[2]
- Sloth was the only one of the Deadly Sins caught in Heavenly Judgment to survive the attack.[2]
- Envy's complete canonical physical appearance is never described in detail before his death.
- No canonical evidence currently supports assigning abilities such as Power Copying, Jealousy Manipulation, Envy Empowerment or unrestricted Ability Theft to him solely because he represents the Sin of Envy.
