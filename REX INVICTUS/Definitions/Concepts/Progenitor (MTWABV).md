Progenitors are exceptionally rare existences that function as the beginning, central origin or metaphysical foundation of a race or Bloodline in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]]. "Progenitor" is not a species by itself and does not grant one identical collection of powers to every holder. Instead, each Progenitor possesses a racial function associated with the nature of the lineage they represent: Vampire Progenitors govern Blood and Souls, the Werewolf Progenitor connects and exerts the collective power of the pack, the Demon Progenitor can directly create Demons, a Dragon Progenitor possesses extraordinary authority over the Energies and structure of Creation, and other Progenitors establish different racial foundations.[1][2][3][4][5]
A Progenitor is fundamentally a Beginning. Vlad explains the principle through Adam, the first Human: Humanity's remarkable Potential and Adaptability originated in Adam at their fully developed foundational level, while later Humans inherited smaller portions of those characteristics through his lineage.[1] The same principle applies more broadly to Progenitors. Their characteristics can become the foundation of a Bloodline, allowing mutations, racial traits and supernatural characteristics originating around the Progenitor to continue through later generations.
The term must not be confused with Creator. Heavenly Father created Adam, but Adam became the Human Progenitor.[1] [[Athena]] caused the transformation that produced [[Medusa]] Elderblood, but Medusa became the first known Gorgon Progenitor.[6] Conversely, a Being may simultaneously be creator and Progenitor, as demonstrated by [[The Universal Tree]] in relation to ordinary [[World Trees]] and Victor Elderblood in relation to the Blood Dragon lineage.[7][4]

### Terminology

#### Progenitor
A Progenitor is a Being recognized as the beginning or central racial source of a lineage. This can mean the first member of a newly established species, the foundation of a new Bloodline within an older species, or an exceptional racial existence through which a lineage's defining characteristics become established.[1][8]
The title therefore does not necessarily mean "the chronologically first member of this broad species to ever exist." This is explicitly demonstrated by Vampire history. Vlad is traditionally called the First Vampire Progenitor, yet he personally explains that Vampire Progenitors existed before him. His title reflects the extraordinary success and survival of his Bloodline rather than proving that no previous Vampire Progenitor ever existed.[8]

#### Primogenitor
Primogenitor describes the first Progenitor of a newly established lineage and is most prominently applied to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] after the birth of the [[Blood Dragons]].
Victor demonstrates the difference clearly:
- As a Vampire Progenitor, Victor became another Progenitor within the already existing Vampire species while Vlad remained alive.[9]
- As the Blood Dragon Primogenitor, Victor became the first and foundational Progenitor of an entirely new race created through the fusion and evolution of his Vampiric and Draconic existence.[4]
Primogenitor should therefore not automatically be interpreted as a separate combat tier above every other Progenitor. It describes the individual's position as the first foundational Progenitor of that lineage.

#### Night King
Night King is a designation associated with certain Progenitors among the Creatures of the Night, particularly Vampire and Werewolf-related Progenitors. It should not be applied to Adam, [[Medusa]], the Universal Tree, War or every other Progenitor indiscriminately.
Night Kings are associated with the [[Sanguis Noctis Regis]], commonly called the Blood of the Night King or Golden Blood.[10]
Known properties include:
- Accelerated Learning: greatly improved ability to learn techniques, Powers and applications of supernatural abilities.[10]
- Enhanced Adaptability: exceptional capacity to adapt biologically and supernaturally to new circumstances.[10]
- Growth Enhancement: the Night King's Blood possesses extraordinary potential for continued development.[10]
- Racial Empowerment: Blood or direct power received from a Night King can significantly strengthen compatible members of the race and help develop their potential.[11]
- Mental Resistance: Progenitors among the Night Creatures possess extremely strong resistance to ordinary mental domination and manipulation, although sufficiently powerful conceptual or Divine mental authorities can potentially overcome this depending upon comparative power.[12]
- Inherited Racial Knowledge: Night Kings can possess inherited "common knowledge" about the supernatural world. Unlike the extensive technical inheritance associated with Dragons, this functions more as foundational racial knowledge intended to prevent a new Progenitor from being completely ignorant of the supernatural world.[13]

### Nature of a Progenitor

#### The Beginning of a Lineage
The defining characteristic of a Progenitor is not simply exceptional power. A Progenitor is a racial beginning.[1]
Vlad's analysis of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] demonstrates what this means. Adam did not merely possess greater Human potential than ordinary Humans; Potential and Adaptability existed in Adam at their original foundational magnitude. His descendants inherited those characteristics in reduced forms. Victor, being descended from Adam before becoming a Vampire Progenitor, retained Humanity's Potential and Adaptability and had those characteristics enormously amplified when his Progenitor state emerged.[1]
A Progenitor can therefore become the source from which future descendants receive racial or hereditary characteristics. This principle is important enough that the unusual mutations of later Bloodlines can ultimately be traced to characteristics introduced or preserved through the Progenitor.

#### Racial Function
Every Progenitor does not possess the same special power.
The series repeatedly presents Progenitors as racial functions:
Lineage
Central Progenitor Function
Vampires
Accumulation and administration of Souls through Blood; functioning between Life and Death.[14][3]
[[Werewolves]]
Exertion and distribution of power through a species-wide pack hierarchy.[13][5]
Demons
Direct creation of Demons without relying upon the normal process that naturally produces them.[15]
Humans
Foundational manifestation of Humanity's Potential and Adaptability.[1]
Dragons
Extreme interaction with Creation, Reality, Runes and virtually every native Energy beneath Positive and Negative Energy.[4][16][17]
[[World Trees]]
Generation and continuation of the World Tree lineage.[7]
[[Gorgons]]
Foundation and continuation of the Gorgon lineage; a separate exclusive Progenitor authority has not yet been fully explained.[6]
[[Heralds of Chaos]]
Creation of additional Heralds belonging to War's lineage.[18]
For this reason, abilities demonstrated by one Progenitor must not automatically be copied to another.

#### Progenitor-exclusive Characteristics
Some abilities exist specifically because the individual is the Progenitor rather than merely a member of the race. This distinction becomes explicit during later discussion of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s children. Although his daughters inherited extraordinary Blood Dragon qualities and varying levels of talent with Runes, Shapeshifting and other racial abilities, they did not automatically inherit the powers that belonged specifically to Victor's Progenitor status. These abilities could potentially be learned or approximated where the racial system allowed it, but the innate Progenitor functions themselves belonged to the Progenitor.[16][17]
This establishes an important distinction between:
- Racial abilities — characteristics inherited by ordinary members or descendants of the species.
- Progenitor-enhanced racial abilities — ordinary racial abilities expressed at an extraordinary level because the user is the Progenitor.
- Progenitor-exclusive authority — abilities produced specifically by the individual's position as the racial Beginning.

#### Soul
Progenitor Souls are abnormal even before Godhood.
During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s internal development, it is explained that Progenitors possess Souls of enormous size. A Progenitor's Soul can exceed the Soul of a God in sheer capacity, although an ordinary Progenitor remains inferior to a Divine Soul in quality because the Progenitor is still fundamentally a mortal existence rather than automatically a God.[2]
This difference between size and quality is important. Progenitor status can produce God-class or God-King-class power without automatically granting Divinity.
Vampire Progenitors represent the most extreme demonstrated connection between Progenitor status and Soul structure. Their existence is designed to contain numerous foreign Souls, something described as fundamentally contrary to the normal rules governing living Beings.[19]

#### Reproduction and Bloodline Continuation
Progenitors possess physiology particularly suited to continuing a Bloodline. Their reproductive characteristics can be abnormally powerful and are repeatedly associated with the fundamental function of creating or continuing a race.[20]
This does not mean every Progenitor reproduces through the same method.
- Humans primarily propagate through ordinary reproduction.
- Vampire Progenitors can directly create members of their lineage by transforming compatible Beings.[21]
- Demon Progenitors can directly create Demons.[15]
- Werewolf lineages expand through reproduction and transformation, adding additional members to the Progenitor's pack structure.[5]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] can directly create or transform Beings into Draconic/Vampiric derivatives after becoming the Blood Dragon Primogenitor.[22]
- War can directly raise new [[Heralds of Chaos]].[18]
Therefore, the statement that every Progenitor can rewrite every humanoid into their own species is incorrect. Such conversion is demonstrated for specific lineages but has never been established as a universal Progenitor power.

### Creation and Emergence of Progenitors
There is no single biological ritual through which every Progenitor appears.
A Progenitor can emerge through several demonstrated mechanisms.

#### Natural or Systemic Emergence
Some Progenitors arise when the requirements of their race are fulfilled. Tasha and Volk both possessed qualifications associated with the modern Werewolf Progenitor position, but neither could fully establish the position while another viable claimant remained. Tasha's victory and complete subjugation of Volk resolved the conflict and allowed her to emerge as the uncontested Progenitor and Alpha of Alphas.[13][5]

#### Transformation into a New Racial Beginning
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ascension into the Blood Dragon Primogenitor represents another method. His abnormal Vampire Progenitor existence, Draconic evolution and accumulated characteristics fused into a completely new species. The event was so significant that the Primordial Beings immediately sensed the birth of a new Progenitor.[4]
The Judges stated that the anomaly surrounding Victor had been corrected and Balance preserved, while the Universal Tree identified him as a Progenitor standing above the Dragon and Vampire subspecies from which his new race originated.[4]

#### Creation by a God of Beginning
Progenitors can also be deliberately produced by sufficiently developed Gods associated with Beginning.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] demonstrated this when transforming War. After War's reconstruction and later evolution, Victor used his authority and Essence to create a new type of Being. Roxanne explicitly explained that Victor was not the only possible Being capable of deliberately creating a Progenitor: Gods sufficiently connected to Beginning can perform such acts, while Progenitors may also emerge through the larger systems governing existence.[23][24]
War later demonstrated that his new Progenitor state was genuine by creating the [[Heralds of Chaos]].[18]

#### Relationship with Balance
The appearance of a Progenitor can interact with Balance, but the story has not established that every Progenitor is personally selected in an identical manner by Positivity and Negativity.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood Dragon transformation corrected an anomaly and restored Balance.[4] Tasha's emergence depended upon the resolution of competing candidates within the Werewolf hierarchy.[13][5] War was deliberately created by Victor and accepted by the larger system despite containing contradictory characteristics of Positivity and Negativity.[23][24]
The safest interpretation is therefore that Progenitor emergence is constrained by larger cosmological rules, while the exact method differs according to lineage and circumstance.

### Vampire Progenitors
Main article: Vampires
The defining authority of a Vampire Progenitor concerns Blood, Souls, Life and Death.
[[Loki]] describes the Vampire Progenitor as a Being walking between Life and Death, possessing authority over the Blood of the living and Souls of the dead.[14] Vlad later gives an even more direct explanation: the Vampire Progenitor operates between Life and Death while using Blood as the bargaining medium through which Souls are acquired.[3]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually describes the metaphysical function another way: a Vampire Progenitor behaves like an extremely limited version of the [[Akashic Records]], except its archive and authority are restricted primarily to Souls.[25]

#### One Progenitor per Age
Vampire Progenitors possess a special restriction related to their function over Souls.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s internal Progenitor manifestation explains that storing numerous Souls inside a living Being fundamentally violates the normal order through which Souls are handled. Because Vampire Progenitors are existences that repeatedly break those spiritual rules, there is ordinarily only one Vampire Progenitor in each age.[19]
Victor and Vlad existing simultaneously was therefore not evidence that this restriction did not exist. Victor was an anomaly. His abnormal coexistence with Vlad continued until Victor evolved into the Blood Dragon Primogenitor, at which point the larger system treated his previous anomaly as corrected.[4]

#### Historical Vampire Progenitors
Chapter 628 establishes that Vlad was not literally the first Vampire Progenitor in history.
The Asanbosam preserve the clearest evidence of an older lineage. Their ancestral Vampire Bloodline predates Vlad's dominant lineage.[8]
Vlad explains that Progenitors had appeared in previous ages. The reason he became known throughout supernatural society as the First Progenitor was that he survived, protected his descendants and established a powerful, enduring Vampire civilization, whereas previous Progenitor Bloodlines either collapsed, disappeared or remained comparatively insignificant.[8]
The names and individual abilities of those earlier Progenitors have not been revealed. They should therefore be indexed as historical unnamed Vampire Progenitors rather than given invented identities.

#### Vlad Dracula Tepes
Main article: [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]]
[[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] is the established Vampire Progenitor of the dominant modern [[Nightingale]] lineage.
His Progenitor-related abilities include:
- Vampire Progenitor Blood Authority — complete access to the standard Progenitor relationship with Blood.[3]
- Soul Authority — interaction, acquisition, preservation and destruction of Souls.[3]
- Soul World — storage of accumulated Souls within his spiritual existence.
- Soul Assimilation — Vlad has deliberately merged selected Souls into himself rather than merely storing them, an advanced personal application of Vampire Progenitor Soul authority.[3]
- Soul-Based Army Creation — techniques such as Ima-Ku-Roi weaponize the Souls accumulated by Vlad. This is a technique developed by Vlad using Progenitor resources rather than a technique automatically granted to every Vampire Progenitor.[3]
- Limited Akashic Function — like other Vampire Progenitors, his spiritual existence can serve as a repository of Soul information.[25]
- Night King Characteristics — [[Sanguis Noctis Regis]], exceptional adaptability, learning speed, mental resistance and racial empowerment.[10][11]
Vlad's Blood Rain, Blood Barriers, particular transformations and other named combat techniques are personal developments and should remain on his individual abilities page rather than being listed as universal Progenitor powers.

#### Victor Elderblood — Former Vampire Progenitor
Main article: [[Victor Elderblood]]
Victor became the Second Vampire Progenitor despite Vlad already occupying the Progenitor position of his era.[9]
Victor possessed the standard Vampire Progenitor functions over Blood and Souls but differed from Vlad in several important ways.
- Human Progenitor Heritage: Victor was born Human and retained Humanity's Potential and Adaptability after becoming a Vampire. His Progenitor Blood amplified rather than erased those characteristics.[1]
- Multiple Noble Characteristics: his abnormal conversion absorbed the Snow, Fulger and [[Scarlett Bloodline]] characteristics rather than forcing only one lineage to dominate.
- Soul Authority: Victor naturally acquired the Vampire Progenitor function of interacting with Souls and developed it progressively throughout the series.
- Bloodline Characteristic Transmission: Vampires created directly through Victor could inherit characteristics originating from him, as demonstrated by [[Eve Elderblood]] manifesting black fire.[26]
- Exceptional Soul Capacity: Victor accumulated huge quantities of Souls, with Roxanne becoming essential to stabilizing and refining the burden placed upon his spiritual existence.[19][2]
- Akashic-like Soul Function: Victor later explicitly identifies Vampire Progenitors as limited Akashic repositories specialized in Souls.[25]
Victor ceased being classified purely as a Vampire Progenitor when his existence evolved into the Blood Dragon Primogenitor in Chapter 803. His Vampire Progenitor authorities were not lost; they were fused into the new Blood Dragon Progenitor structure.[4]

### Werewolf Progenitors
Main article: [[Werewolves]]
Werewolf Progenitors function almost as the inverse of Vampire Progenitors.
Vampire Progenitors primarily accumulate Souls and information within themselves. The Werewolf Progenitor's racial system instead exerts and distributes power through a collective pack structure. The population of Werewolves becomes part of an enormous racial network linked back to the supreme Progenitor.[13][5]

#### Tasha Fenrir Elderblood
Main article: [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]]
Tasha Fenrir Elderblood became the Progenitor of the modern Werewolf lineage after defeating and completely subordinating [[Volk Fenrir]]. Both had possessed qualifications connected to the position, but the existence of two competing supreme candidates prevented the hierarchy from resolving until Tasha established herself as the uncontested Alpha.[13][5]
Her Progenitor powers form the most complete explanation of Werewolf Progenitor mechanics currently available.
Ability
Description
True Alpha / Alpha of Alphas
Tasha occupies the supreme position of the Werewolf hierarchy. Ordinary Alphas cease being equivalent independent apexes in relation to her and exist beneath her Progenitor authority.[5]
Universal Pack Bond
[[Werewolves]] belonging to her modern racial lineage become connected to the Progenitor's pack regardless of their former local pack structure. The connection is represented through spiritual threads reaching the members of the race.[5]
Racial Authority
Tasha's commands carry instinctive authority over connected Werewolves, superseding lesser Alpha hierarchies.[5]
Pack Power Exertion
The Werewolf Progenitor does not simply store the race inside herself. Her authority operates outward through the pack, allowing the collective existence of her followers to contribute to the Progenitor's effective power.[5]
Pack Amplification
The number and strength of Werewolves beneath the Progenitor contribute additional power to her racial system. Expanding and improving the race consequently improves the power available through the Progenitor network.[5]
Power Withdrawal
Tasha can withdraw power from a Werewolf connected to her authority and reclaim it through their racial bond.[5]
Pack Expulsion
Tasha can expel a Werewolf from the racial hierarchy, severing the target's connection to the Progenitor and reducing them to the position of an Omega. Volk demonstrates the catastrophic weakness that can result when this support is removed.[5]
Soul-Connection Severance
Expulsion affects more than social rank. The connection exists at a spiritual level, and forcibly severing it can damage the target's Soul and racial foundation.[5]
Alpha Appointment
As Alpha of Alphas, Tasha can elevate another Werewolf into the position of Alpha even when that individual was not naturally born with the normal qualification.[27]
Hierarchy Reorganization
The Progenitor can redefine the functional structure of the Werewolf race, replacing lesser leadership relationships beneath the universal pack.
Lineage Expansion
Additional Werewolves strengthen the collective system because every compatible member can become another component of the greater pack network.[5]
The powers above should not be confused with Tasha's personal Egyptian Divine abilities, later Positive Energy support, Goddess physiology or abilities derived from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Those are Tasha's own Powers rather than innate Werewolf Progenitor functions.

#### Licael
Main article: Licael
Licael is historically remembered as an ancient Werewolf Progenitor and ruler connected to the ancestry of the [[Werewolves]] later established on Samar.[13]
However, later information complicates the simple interpretation that Licael and Tasha are merely two holders of an identical racial office. Licael originated from a foreign lineage, and later discussion identifies his underlying species as fundamentally different from the native lineage represented by Tasha.
For that reason, the following distinction should be maintained:
- Licael is historically identified as a Werewolf Progenitor.
- He was associated with the Night-Creature/Alpha structure from which later Werewolf civilization developed.
- The modern Progenitor mechanics demonstrated directly by Tasha should not automatically be copied one-for-one to Licael without further chapter evidence.
- His exact personal Progenitor authority has not been demonstrated directly in sufficient detail.
The previously common practice of listing every one of Tasha's modern pack functions as confirmed Licael abilities therefore overstates the available evidence.

### Human Progenitor

#### Adam
Main article: Adam
Adam is the first Human and Progenitor of Humanity. He was created by [[Heavenly Father]], making the distinction between a Creator and a Progenitor particularly clear.[1]
Adam's Progenitor significance centers upon the traits that became foundational to Humanity.
Trait
Relationship to Adam's Progenitor Status
Potential
Humanity possesses exceptional capacity for improvement and development. In ordinary Humans this exists as a comparatively small "seed", while Adam possessed the original foundational form at full Progenitor magnitude.[1]
Adaptability
Humans possess an exceptional ability to adapt to different circumstances. Adam possessed the original form from which later Human adaptability descended.[1]
Free [[Will Solace|Will]]
Free Will was granted by the Heavenly Father to the earliest Humans and became one of Humanity's most important characteristics. It is a creator-bestowed Human trait carried by Adam rather than an ability demonstrated to originate solely from Progenitor status.[15]
Racial Propagation
Adam became the ancestral foundation from which Humanity continued through biological reproduction.
No Demonstrated Racial Conversion
Unlike Vampire, Demon, Blood Dragon and several other Progenitor systems, Adam has not been demonstrated possessing an ability to rewrite another species into Humans.[28]
Potential, Adaptability and Free Will should therefore be described as foundational Human characteristics represented in their original form by Adam, not as proof that every unrelated Progenitor automatically gains those abilities.

### Demon Progenitor

#### Lilith Elderblood
Main article: [[Lilith Elderblood]]
Lilith Elderblood is the principal known Demon Progenitor and the Mother of the Demon lineage.
The defining Demon Progenitor function is unusually straightforward compared with the complex Soul system of Vampires or pack system of [[Werewolves]]: a Demon Progenitor possesses the ability to create Demons directly without relying upon the normal process through which Demons arise.[15]
Ability
Description
Demon Progenitor Physiology
Lilith possesses the foundational Demonic existence from which her Demon lineage is propagated.
Direct Demon Creation
The defining Demon Progenitor authority. Lilith can create members of the Demon race without requiring the conventional spiritual process through which ordinary Demons naturally arise.[15]
Demonic Lineage Generation
Demons created from the Progenitor become members of a lineage derived from her foundational existence.
Hereditary Racial Transmission
Progenitor-level Demonic characteristics can survive strongly in descendants. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Lilith's daughter later demonstrates an unusually complete preservation of both parental Progenitor lineages.[29]
Lilith's Miasma abilities, Hell-related Powers, Divine abilities, personal [[Magic]] and other later developments are not universal Demon Progenitor powers and should remain on her own abilities page.

### Gorgon Progenitor

#### Medusa Elderblood
Main article: [[Medusa]] Elderblood
Medusa Elderblood explicitly identifies herself as Medusa, Queen of the [[Gorgons]], a Divine Spirit and a Progenitor. She later clarifies that she is the first known Progenitor of the Gorgon species.[6]
Unlike the Vampire, Werewolf, Demon and Dragon Progenitor systems, the story has not yet supplied a detailed separate mechanism exclusive to a Gorgon Progenitor.
Progenitor-related Trait
Description
Gorgon Lineage Foundation
Medusa is the foundational Progenitor from whom the known Gorgon lineage descends.[6]
Racial Propagation
Gorgon characteristics continued through Medusa's descendants despite Medusa herself eventually surviving as a Divine Spirit rather than an independent physical body.[6]
Progenitor Soul
Medusa retains the metaphysical status of a Progenitor despite existing as a Divine Spirit within her descendant.[6]
Petrification, snake-related abilities and Medusa's later Divine Powers are Gorgon or personal abilities, not confirmed Gorgon Progenitor-exclusive authorities. Until the novel identifies a distinct Gorgon Progenitor function, those abilities should not be presented as equivalent to Vampire Soul Authority or Tasha's universal pack system.

### World Tree Progenitor

#### The Universal Tree
Main article: [[The Universal Tree]]
The Universal Tree is both a creator and the foundational Progenitor of the ordinary [[World Trees]].[7]

### Dragon Progenitor Authority
Main article: Dragons
The Dragon Progenitor system is one of the most powerful racial Progenitor functions described in the series.
Chapter 803 explains that ordinary Dragons are Beings of Nature deeply connected to existence. As Dragons become stronger, their senses and compatibility expand until they can interact with Energies outside the element with which they were originally born. The true Dragon Progenitor takes this principle to its extreme.[4]
The chapter describes the true ability of the Dragon Progenitor as control over essentially every ordinary Energy beneath the fundamental Positive and Negative Energies sustaining Creation.[4]

#### Identity of the Ancient Dragon Progenitor
The novel uses the concept of the Dragon Progenitor when explaining these powers, but the checked chapter material does not conclusively identify a separate named historical Dragon Progenitor before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
[[Tiamat]] is associated strongly with the ancient Dragon race and is recognized as a major maternal figure among Dragons, but this alone should not be used to state that she was definitively the historical Dragon Progenitor unless a chapter explicitly assigns her that title.
For this reason, the general Dragon Progenitor authority should be indexed separately from Tiamat's personal page until stronger direct evidence appears.

#### Limits of Inheritance
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters do not automatically inherit every Progenitor-exclusive authority.
They can inherit:
- Blood Dragon physiology.
- Exceptional Draconic compatibility.
- Superior Runic talent.
- Enhanced Shapeshifting.
- Parental racial traits.
- Characteristics inherited from their mothers.
However, the innate powers belonging specifically to Victor's status as Progenitor—such as the complete Progenitor-level Reality affinity and highest innate racial authorities—are not simply reproduced in every child.[16][17]
This distinction is one of the clearest demonstrations that being a descendant of a Progenitor is not equivalent to being another Progenitor.

#### Beginning
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later reached Godhood, his status as a racial Beginning contributed directly to the awakening and development of his Divinity of Beginning.[34]
This is a Divine development derived from his nature as a Progenitor, not a universal automatic power every mortal Progenitor possesses.

### Progenitors and Godhood
A Progenitor and a God are two different kinds of existence.
A Progenitor represents a racial Beginning. A God possesses Divinity and a metaphysical connection to one or more Concepts.
An ordinary Progenitor can possess immense power without being Divine. Progenitor Souls can exceed [[Divine Souls]] in size while remaining inferior in qualitative Divine nature.[2]
Godhood is therefore not automatically granted by Progenitor status.
Several Progenitors eventually reached Divine states through exceptional circumstances, demonstrating that the separation is not an absolute prohibition. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Tasha, Lilith, [[Medusa]] and War all eventually exist with Divine characteristics in addition to their Progenitor-related nature.
The important distinction is:
Classification
Fundamental Meaning
Progenitor
Beginning and racial foundation of a Bloodline or species.
Primogenitor
First foundational Progenitor of a newly established lineage.
God
Divine Being possessing Divinity and conceptual authority.
Primordial God
Extremely advanced Divine Being whose power and Conceptual development approach the upper limits of Godhood.
Primordial Entity / Regulator
Being whose existence is integrated into a fundamental Aspect or function of the Cosmos.
A Being can possess more than one of these classifications simultaneously.

### Progenitor Lineages and Inheritance

#### Direct Descendants
Being directly descended from a Progenitor can dramatically affect the potential of the offspring, but descendants are not automatic copies of the Progenitor.
They may inherit:
- stronger racial traits;
- unusual mutations;
- portions or variants of parental abilities;
- superior Bloodline potential;
- hybrid characteristics when another sufficiently powerful Bloodline survives the inheritance process.
This becomes particularly clear among [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s children.
When both parents possess overwhelming Bloodlines—especially when both are Progenitors—the mother's racial characteristics can survive the normally dominant inheritance of Victor's Blood Dragon lineage. Victor's daughter with Lilith developed into a Demonic Blood Dragon, while his daughter with Tasha developed as a Blood Dragon retaining an extraordinary relationship with Nature inherited through the Werewolf Progenitor side.[29]
This does not make the children additional Progenitors. It demonstrates the exceptional strength of Progenitor-origin Bloodlines.

#### Created Descendants
A Progenitor can also imprint characteristics upon individuals directly created through their racial system.
[[Eve Elderblood]] is an important early example. After being created from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood, she manifested black fire that Victor identified as a characteristic inherited from his own developing Bloodline.[26]
This establishes that a Progenitor does not merely create a generic member of the race; the specific characteristics of the Progenitor can influence the lineage produced from them.

### Historical and Unnamed Progenitors

#### Ancient Vampire Progenitors
Earlier Vampire Progenitors existed before Vlad.[8] Their names remain unknown.
The Asanbosam demonstrate that at least one ancient Vampire Bloodline survived long enough for descendants to remain in the modern era.[8]
The exact abilities of these historical Progenitors are unknown beyond what can reasonably be associated with the Vampire Progenitor racial function.

#### Dragon Progenitor
Chapter 803 explicitly explains the Dragon Progenitor as a racial category possessing powers far beyond those of ordinary Dragons.[4]
However, the checked novel material does not yet provide a sufficiently direct statement identifying a separate named ancient Dragon as the definitive historical Dragon Progenitor.
The following should therefore be avoided unless supported by a direct chapter citation:
- Automatically identifying [[Tiamat]] as the original Dragon Progenitor.
- Treating every Ancient Dragon as a Progenitor.
- Treating ordinary True Dragon abilities as equivalent to full Dragon Progenitor authority.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood Dragon Primogenitor state is presently the clearest directly demonstrated Progenitor-level Draconic existence.

### Trivia
- Progenitors are described as Beginnings rather than simply unusually strong racial members.[1]
- A Vampire Progenitor normally appears once per age because their ability to carry numerous Souls within a living existence violates the normal spiritual order.[19]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Vlad existing simultaneously was an anomaly that was ultimately resolved when Victor became the Blood Dragon Primogenitor.[4]
- Vlad's traditional title of First Vampire Progenitor is not literal chronological proof that he was the first Vampire Progenitor in history.[8]
- The Asanbosam descend from a Vampire Bloodline older than Vlad's dominant lineage.[8]
- Progenitor Souls can possess greater size than [[Divine Souls]] while remaining inferior in Divine quality.[2]
- Victor's Blood Dragon evolution was immediately perceived by the Primordial Beings.[4]
- [[The Universal Tree]] called Victor a Progenitor above the Dragon and Vampire subspecies from which his new existence originated.[4]
- Dragon Progenitor abilities are explicitly described as partly exclusive to the Progenitor; Victor's daughters did not inherit the complete innate package merely by being his children.[16][17]
- Gods associated with Beginning can deliberately create a new Progenitor under appropriate circumstances, as Victor demonstrated through War.[23][24]
- War subsequently demonstrated genuine lineage creation by producing the [[Heralds of Chaos]].[18]
- War remains identified as a Progenitor during the Empire's much later Upper-Sector conflict.[35]
- Victor's later Blood Dragon lineage can include [[True Dragons]], lesser Draconic descendants and other branches while remaining subordinate to the Progenitor at the root of the line.[31]
- Progenitor and God are independent classifications; an individual can possess both without the terms becoming synonymous.
- Victor's Divine Concept of Beginning was heavily influenced by his nature as a Progenitor, but his highly developed Divine authority should not be copied to every Progenitor.[34]
