Youki (妖気), also known as Youkai Energy or Yokai Energy, is a supernatural form of Energy used primarily by the Yokais of Japan. It originates from the accumulation of negative emotional Energy produced by Humans, including fear, hatred, suffering and despair. Youkai absorb this Energy from their surroundings and convert it into supernatural power, using it to strengthen their bodies, perform racial techniques and sustain the extraordinary abilities associated with their respective species. Unlike internally cultivated energies such as Ki, most Youkai depend upon environmental concentrations of Youki, making the availability of compatible Energy an important limitation upon their strength and geographical independence.[1][2]
Youki is particularly concentrated within Japan, where an unusual supernatural history created environmental conditions favorable to its accumulation. This concentration allowed Youkai civilization to develop powerful racial techniques, but also created a long-standing dependence upon the country's supernatural environment. Most ordinary Youkai cannot freely generate Youki without an external supply, while the three principal races—Kitsune, Oni and Ryuji—possess exceptional members capable of generating the Energy independently. Powerful Kitsune and Ryuji can also transform other forms of surrounding Energy into Youki, greatly reducing their dependence upon a naturally concentrated environment.[1]
Although Youki is derived from negative emotions, its applications are not restricted to emotional influence or simple destructive attacks. Skilled users can manipulate it for physical reinforcement, energy projection, swordsmanship, illusions, protective barriers, specialized martial techniques and even the maintenance of artificial battlefields. Otsuki Elderblood Haruna provides one of the most extensive demonstrations of its capabilities, combining immense Energy reserves with the sword techniques of the [[Otsuki Clan]], her innate Kitsune abilities and extraordinary combat skill.[3][4]
The limitations of Youki eventually motivated the unified Youkai civilization under Haruna to investigate alternative supernatural energies. Their research resulted in the discovery of [[Senjutsu]] and Ki, two related disciplines that offered access to planetary [[Natural Energy]] and internally cultivated Vitality. These developments provided potential alternatives to Youki without eliminating the importance of the traditional racial Energy or the techniques developed around it.[2]

### Nature and Origin

#### Negative Emotional Energy
Youki originates from the negative emotions generated by Humanity. Fear, hatred, despair and similar emotions release supernatural influences that accumulate within the environment, eventually becoming a source of power accessible to Youkai. This process distinguishes Youki from systems such as conventional [[Magic]], which normally employ Mana, and [[Senjutsu]], which draws upon the [[Natural Energy]] circulating through planetary Ley Lines.[1][2]
The Energy generated through Human emotions is not automatically available in equal quantities throughout the world. Despite Humans existing on every continent, the exceptional concentration of Youki in Japan makes the country uniquely suitable for ordinary Youkai cultivation. The connection between emotional Energy and environmental accumulation therefore involves more than the simple presence of Human populations; geographical conditions and supernatural history influence the availability of usable Youki.[1]
Youki possesses negative characteristics, but this classification does not mean that every Youkai using it is inherently evil or malicious. The Energy's origin reflects the emotions through which it is generated, whereas the intentions and actions of its wielder depend upon the individual. Youkai can use the same supernatural resource to defend their companions, sustain barriers, perform martial techniques or attack their enemies.
- Negative Energy Absorption: Youkai can absorb accumulated negative emotional Energy from their surroundings and utilize it as supernatural power.[1]
- Emotion-Based Energy: Youki originates from emotional influences generated by Humans, including fear, hatred and despair.[1][2]
- Energy Manipulation: Absorbed Youki can be directed through the user's body and employed in supernatural techniques.[3]
- Environmental Energy Absorption: Ordinary Youkai obtain much of their power from the naturally accumulated Youki present within their environment.[1]
- Energy Empowerment: The Energy can reinforce the user's existing physical and racial characteristics, allowing them to perform supernatural feats beyond their ordinary bodily capabilities.[3]

#### The Supernatural Environment of Japan
Japan possesses an exceptionally high concentration of Youki compared with other regions of Earth. The country's unusual relationship with this Energy became one of the principal reasons the Youkai developed such a powerful and geographically concentrated civilization. Its environment supported the accumulation and circulation of Youki, allowing numerous supernatural species to develop abilities that would be considerably harder to sustain elsewhere.[1]
The precise origin of Japan's exceptional concentration was initially uncertain. After investigating information obtained from Japanese Noble Vampires, Violet Elderblood identified evidence that Youki had existed long before its dramatic increase in potency. At some point in supernatural history, the Energy became substantially more concentrated, strengthening the Youkai inhabiting the region.[1]
Violet proposed that the death of an exceptionally powerful supernatural creature might have altered the surrounding environment. Such a phenomenon was consistent with the established ability of powerful creatures, particularly Dragons, to transform an area through the residual supernatural properties of their remains. The resulting concentration could have encouraged further Youkai development, with the deaths of powerful Youkai contributing additional Energy to the environment and reinforcing the cycle.[1]
Kaguya Elderblood considered the legendary Yamata no Orochi a possible explanation, since its mythology appeared compatible with the proposed circumstances. However, the identification was speculative rather than a confirmed account of the event. The established facts are that Japan possesses an unusual concentration of Youki and that its environmental conditions became significantly more favorable to Youkai over time.[1]

#### Environmental Accumulation
Youki can accumulate within a region and remain available for absorption by supernatural beings possessing the appropriate racial characteristics. The concentration of Energy influences the sustainability of Youkai populations and their ability to maintain powerful techniques.[1]
The existence of naturally concentrated Youki also explains why some Youkai are comparatively dependent upon particular territories. A powerful practitioner may possess tremendous skill and exceptional physical abilities but still suffer limitations when separated from the environment supplying their supernatural Energy.
The historical accumulation of Youki in Japan made the country a natural center of Youkai civilization. This advantage simultaneously became a strategic weakness, since ordinary practitioners lacked the same freedom to operate at full capacity in regions where comparable environmental concentrations did not exist.[1]

### Generation and Absorption

#### Ordinary Youkai
Most Youkai can manipulate Youki but lack the ability to generate significant quantities independently. They instead absorb the Energy naturally present in their surroundings and employ it to power their supernatural abilities.[1]
For these beings, the amount of available Youki depends heavily upon the surrounding environment. Regions containing abundant negative emotional Energy provide suitable conditions for the continued use of their abilities, while areas without comparable concentrations can severely restrict their capabilities.
The limitation is particularly important during prolonged combat or military campaigns. A supernatural warrior who cannot replenish expended Energy may be unable to maintain the same level of performance indefinitely, even if their physical abilities and martial training remain exceptional.
- Energy Absorption: Ordinary Youkai absorb naturally occurring Youki instead of generating all of their supernatural Energy internally.[1]
- Energy Manipulation: Absorbed Youki can be employed for racial techniques, physical reinforcement and combat applications.[1][3]
- Power Source Dependency: The effectiveness of their Youki-based abilities is influenced by the available environmental Energy.[1]
- Energy Depletion: Techniques that consume substantial quantities of Youki can exhaust the available reserves of the practitioner, limiting sustained combat performance.[5]

#### The Three Great Youkai Races
The three principal Youkai races—Kitsune, Oni and Ryuji—possess an exceptional relationship with Youki. Their highest-ranking members can generate it independently, reducing the limitations ordinarily imposed by environmental dependence.[1]
The differences between these races are especially important because Youki generation is not necessarily identical among them. High-ranking Kitsune possess specialized anatomical characteristics that permit the conversion of surrounding Energy into Youki, while powerful Ryuji possess a comparable ability. Oni also belong to the exceptional races capable of producing the Energy, although the precise mechanism is not described in equivalent detail.[1]
This capacity grants the strongest members of the three races considerable advantages over ordinary Youkai, particularly when operating outside Japan. Their ability to sustain powerful techniques without depending entirely upon locally accumulated Youki makes them more adaptable during travel, warfare and other activities beyond their traditional territories.

##### Kitsune
Kitsune are among the most capable natural users of Youki. Their tails possess the ability to absorb surrounding Energy and transform it into Youki, allowing sufficiently powerful members of the race to replenish their reserves independently of the ordinary environmental supply.[1]
The number of tails possessed by a Kitsune is closely associated with its supernatural development. Powerful Nine-Tailed Kitsune possess extraordinary quantities of Youki, and the Energy available to exceptionally gifted individuals may vastly exceed that of other members of the same racial class.
Haruna's reserves provide a particularly extreme example. Upon releasing her suppressed power during her duel with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], [[Hashimoto Genji]], himself an exceptionally powerful Nine-Tailed Kitsune, recognized that Haruna possessed approximately ten times his own available Energy. Her strength therefore greatly surpassed what could be expected from an ordinary Nine-Tailed Kitsune, demonstrating the differences in potential that can exist even among elite members of the same race.[3]
- Youki Generation: Powerful Kitsune can generate Youki independently of the natural environmental supply.[1]
- Energy Conversion: Their tails can absorb other forms of surrounding Energy and convert them into usable Youki.[1]
- Energy Distribution: Kitsune capable of generating sufficient Youki can share it with other beings, including members of their military forces.[1]
- Energy Manipulation: Kitsune can employ Youki to reinforce their bodies and activate their supernatural techniques.[3]
- Energy Projection: Highly trained Kitsune can release concentrated Youki as destructive energy attacks.[3]
- Energy Coating: Youki can envelop the user's body or weapons, increasing their durability and offensive power.[3][4]

##### Oni
Oni are one of the three major Youkai races capable of generating Youki independently. This characteristic distinguishes their highest-ranking members from ordinary Youkai who must rely almost entirely upon ambient environmental Energy.[1]
Oni possess exceptionally powerful physiques and racial abilities suited to close-quarters combat. Their capacity to generate Youki provides an additional supernatural resource that can be employed alongside those innate physical characteristics.
Although the race's independent generation of Youki is established, the precise anatomical mechanism by which Oni produce the Energy is not described as extensively as the Energy-conversion abilities of Kitsune tails. Their racial powers should therefore be distinguished from specific techniques demonstrated by individual Oni rather than attributing every Kitsune application to the Oni species.

##### Ryuji
Ryuji, the Dragon-like Youkai of Japan, also possess the ability to generate Youki. Powerful members of the race can influence the surrounding environment and produce supernatural Energy independently, granting them advantages similar to those of high-ranking Kitsune.[1]
The Ryuji are especially notable for their exceptional physical strength and destructive racial abilities. Their possession of Youki-generation capabilities provides a foundation for sustained combat without the same environmental dependence affecting ordinary Youkai.
Their relationship with supernatural Energy also contributed to the discovery of [[Senjutsu]]. During the efforts to develop alternatives to Youki, the Ryuji revealed knowledge of the planetary pathways known as Dragon Veins, leading Haruna and the other Youkai leaders toward an energy source that did not depend upon accumulated negative Human emotions.[2]

### Properties and Characteristics

#### Negative Alignment
Youki is naturally associated with negative emotional influences, making it an impure energy system when compared with the neutral [[Natural Energy]] employed in [[Senjutsu]]. Its relationship with fear, hatred and despair gives it characteristics superficially resembling other negative-aligned supernatural energies.[1][2]
[[Morgana Elderblood]] identified similarities between Youki and Demonic Miasma while distinguishing their potency. According to her comparison, Youki resembled Demonic Energy but possessed considerably weaker qualities. The resemblance reflects their negative characteristics rather than establishing that they are identical supernatural substances.[1]
The distinction is also important when comparing Youki with pure Negativity. Although Youki originates from negative emotions, its users do not automatically possess authority over the fundamental cosmic Concept of Negativity. Their powers remain associated with a specialized form of racial Energy rather than unrestricted manipulation of every negative phenomenon.

#### Appearance
Youki commonly manifests as dark supernatural Energy. When released in large quantities, it can envelop the user's body, form a visible aura or accumulate into a dense column extending into the surrounding environment.[3]
Haruna's unrestricted release produced an enormous dark pillar rising from her body, immediately revealing the extraordinary quantity of Energy she possessed. The same substance could be concentrated around her katana, transformed into powerful sword attacks or circulated around her body to reinforce her defenses.[3][4]
The appearance of Youki can also vary according to the technique being employed. Haruna's attacks frequently take on lunar forms, while her elemental abilities produce effects corresponding to the particular elements associated with her tails. These manifestations arise through the combination of Youki with specialized Kitsune abilities and martial techniques rather than every ordinary user possessing identical powers.

#### Energy Quantity and Potency
The effectiveness of Youki-based techniques depends upon the quantity of Energy supplied, the practitioner's individual strength and the control exercised over its application. A particularly powerful user can produce attacks requiring immense quantities of Youki, while inexperienced practitioners may be unable to reproduce the same techniques even when possessing comparable racial characteristics.
Haruna demonstrated exceptional Energy reserves compared with other Nine-Tailed Kitsune. Her unrestricted Youki substantially exceeded Genji's, while the strength of her attacks surprised even [[Otsuki Yoichi]], the originator of the [[Otsuki Clan]]'s distinctive sword techniques.[3]
Nevertheless, an enormous reserve does not eliminate the limitations imposed by Energy expenditure. Repeatedly producing extremely demanding constructs and attacks can exhaust even a powerful Nine-Tailed Kitsune. Haruna's repeated recreation of the artificial Moon during her duel consumed enough Youki to leave her severely fatigued.[5]

#### Compatibility
Youki possesses distinctive characteristics that can interfere with other forms of supernatural Energy. The difficulties become particularly evident when attempting to combine Youki with [[Senjutsu]], whose neutral Nature Energy is fundamentally different from the accumulated negative emotional Energy used by Youkai.[2]
The conflict between these systems initially prevented ordinary practitioners from freely combining them. Haruna's early experiments required considerable effort, and she was the only member of her group who had achieved even a limited degree of control over both Energies.[2]
Compatibility is therefore not determined solely by the quantity of supernatural Energy available. Effective manipulation also depends upon understanding the nature of the particular source and preventing conflicting characteristics from harming the user.

### Abilities and Applications

#### Youki Manipulation
The fundamental application of Youki is the generation, absorption and manipulation of supernatural Energy. Its users can direct it through their bodies, project it outward and incorporate it into techniques suited to their racial characteristics.[1][3]
- Energy Manipulation: Users can control accumulated Youki and employ it to activate supernatural techniques.[1]
- Energy Absorption: Ordinary Youkai can absorb environmental Youki to supplement their available supernatural reserves.[1]
- Energy Generation: Exceptional members of the Kitsune, Oni and Ryuji races can generate Youki independently.[1]
- Energy Conversion: Powerful Kitsune and Ryuji can convert surrounding Energy into Youki, reducing their dependence upon naturally concentrated regions.[1]
- Energy Projection: Youki can be released as concentrated blasts or beams capable of damaging powerful supernatural opponents.[3]
- Energy Aura: Users can surround themselves with visible Youki, increasing their combat capabilities and providing the foundation for other techniques.[3]

#### Physical Reinforcement
Youki can be circulated around a practitioner's body to reinforce its defensive and physical capabilities. Haruna demonstrated this application during her duel with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], surrounding herself with a protective layer of Energy immediately before receiving an exceptionally powerful kick. Although she suffered a minor injury, the reinforcement helped her withstand the impact and continue fighting.[3]
- Statistics Amplification: Youki can reinforce the user's physical capabilities when circulated through the body.[3]
- Enhanced Durability: Concentrated Youki can protect the user against physical attacks by providing an additional defensive layer.[3]
- Energy Coating: Users can envelop their bodies in Youki to reinforce their defenses.[3]
- Impact Resistance: Defensive Youki can mitigate the damage inflicted by powerful physical attacks.[3]
- Physical Reinforcement: Youki can assist in regulating and supporting exceptional movement, including controlled landings after high-speed travel.[6]
The defensive properties of Youki depend upon the strength of the user and the amount of Energy employed. Haruna's successful resistance to Victor's attack does not establish that ordinary Youkai possess equivalent durability.

#### Energy Projection
Youki can be projected outward as concentrated supernatural Energy. This application is particularly effective when combined with powerful martial techniques or a practitioner's natural racial abilities.[3]
- Energy Blast: Haruna can release dark Youki directly from her body as an offensive attack.[3]
- Energy Beam: Her First Dance, Waning Moon, produces an enormous crescent-shaped attack formed through concentrated Youki.[3]
- Energy Wave: Youki can be channeled through a weapon and released as a large destructive projection.[3]
- Energy Combination: Haruna can employ Youki alongside her elemental affinities and martial techniques to produce more elaborate attacks.[4]
The scale of Haruna's attacks demonstrates the offensive potential of an exceptionally powerful Youki user, but the associated swordsmanship, control and racial abilities remain essential to reproducing those results.

#### Weapon Empowerment
Youki can be concentrated around weapons to increase their offensive capabilities. The [[Otsuki Clan]] developed sophisticated martial techniques incorporating this property, allowing their swordsmanship to exploit the unique characteristics of Kitsune Energy.[3][4]
- Weapon Enhancement: Youki can be circulated through a weapon to increase its supernatural effectiveness.[3]
- Energy Coating: Haruna can envelop her katana in dense Youki, transforming its effective cutting surface into concentrated supernatural Darkness.[4]
- Energy Projection: Weapon techniques can release accumulated Youki as destructive beams or crescent-shaped attacks.[3]
- Poison Manipulation: Haruna can incorporate poisonous Youki into her sword strikes, weakening opponents who survive the initial physical attack.[4]

#### Poison Manipulation
Youki can possess harmful properties beyond ordinary physical damage. One of the clearest demonstrations appears in Haruna's swordsmanship, which employs poisonous Youki to weaken targets that survive an otherwise lethal strike.[4]
Her Third Dance was designed to inflict fatal injuries through a sequence targeting vulnerable points. If the opponent survived the initial strikes, the Youki carried by the katana was intended to poison and weaken the target, creating an advantage for the subsequent attacks. This reveals an additional offensive mechanism in which Youki interferes with a victim's condition rather than merely increasing the cutting force of a weapon.[4]
- Poison Manipulation: Youki incorporated into a weapon can produce a poisonous effect upon the victim.[4]
- Statistics Reduction: The poison is intended to weaken surviving opponents and reduce their ability to withstand subsequent attacks.[4]
- Damage Over Time: The harmful effect can continue developing after the initial strike, creating an additional threat beyond immediate physical injury.[4]
- Weapon Enhancement: Concentrated Youki permits the delivery of supernatural poison through conventional sword attacks.[4]
This application is directly demonstrated through Haruna's specialized martial technique. It should not be assumed that every manifestation of Youki automatically produces an identical poison or that all Youkai possess the same degree of control over its harmful properties.

#### Energy Sharing and Distribution
Powerful Kitsune possess the ability to distribute their Youki to other beings. This characteristic is particularly valuable in military organizations, where the strength and endurance of ordinary soldiers can depend upon the quantity of Energy available to them.[1]
Haruna possesses an exceptional technique allowing her to establish an energetic connection with her subordinates. By sharing her Youki with her forces, she can improve their chances of surviving combat, although the technique imposes a serious burden upon its user. Injuries suffered by the connected soldiers are transferred to Haruna, forcing her to endure the damage sustained across the linked force.[7]
The technique reflects Haruna's extraordinary commitment to her followers and illustrates the potential of Youki manipulation beyond simple destructive attacks. Instead of using Energy solely to overwhelm opponents, a sufficiently powerful leader can employ it to sustain an entire military formation.
- Energy Transfer: Powerful Kitsune can distribute Youki to other beings.[1]
- Power Bestowal (Limited): Sharing Youki can provide additional supernatural support to recipients whose own reserves are insufficient.[7]
- Energy Linking: Haruna can establish a connection between herself and members of her military force.[7]
- Damage Transferal: The linked technique transfers the injuries sustained by connected soldiers to Haruna, reducing the immediate danger faced by her subordinates at considerable personal cost.[7]
- Military Support: Concentrated Youki can be distributed across a coordinated army rather than remaining exclusively within the body of its principal wielder.[7]
The technique's ability to transfer injuries is not an inherent property of ordinary Youki. It represents a specialized application developed and employed by Haruna.

#### Illusion Manipulation
Youki can be incorporated into Kitsune techniques involving deception, sensory interference and the manipulation of an opponent's perception. Haruna's fighting style repeatedly exploits these abilities, preventing enemies from accurately following her movements or identifying her true position.[3][4]
Her technique May combines rapid movement with deceptive appearances, using the movements of her tails and her supernatural abilities to create a sequence in which opponents struggle to distinguish her actual position. Additional illusions can interfere with perception during the later stages of the [[Otsuki Clan]]'s sword techniques, allowing Haruna to attack while the target is unable to identify her precise location.[3][4]
- Illusion Manipulation: Haruna can produce deceptive supernatural experiences that interfere with her opponent's perception.[3][4]
- Perception Manipulation: Her techniques can confuse an opponent's senses and make it difficult to determine her true position.[4]
- Afterimage Creation: Rapid movement and deceptive techniques can produce multiple apparent positions, overwhelming an opponent's ability to track her.[3]
- Stealth Mastery: Illusions can conceal Haruna's movements during combat, allowing her to reposition without being accurately observed.[4]
These are specialized Kitsune applications performed using the practitioner's supernatural abilities. Youki supplies part of their power, but its possession does not automatically grant every Youkai the same degree of Illusion Manipulation.

#### Dimensional Barrier Creation
Youki can sustain exceptionally complex supernatural environments. During the duel between Haruna and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], [[Yotsuba Kuroka]] and her subordinates maintained an artificial Inverse World through the expenditure of enormous quantities of Youki. The technique provided an isolated battlefield where the two combatants could fight without directly devastating the surrounding real-world environment.[3]
The Inverse World was not independent of the Energy maintaining it. When Victor released a particularly powerful attack, the resulting destruction threatened the stability of the artificial environment. Kuroka immediately ordered her subordinates to channel additional Youki into the structure, demonstrating that the barrier required a continuing supply of Energy to remain functional.[4]
- Barrier Creation: Youki can be employed to establish protective or isolating supernatural boundaries.[3]
- Pocket Reality Manipulation (Technique-Based): Kuroka and her supporting practitioners maintained an artificial Inverse World suitable for containing high-level combat.[3]
- Environmental Isolation: The artificial battlefield prevents the destructive effects of the enclosed confrontation from spreading directly into the surrounding ordinary environment.[3]
- Energy Maintenance: The constructed environment requires a continuing supply of Youki, with additional Energy necessary when powerful attacks threaten its stability.[4]
- Cooperative Energy Manipulation: Multiple practitioners can contribute Youki toward maintaining a shared supernatural structure.[4]
The Inverse World demonstrates that highly developed techniques can utilize Youki for effects involving an artificial space. It does not establish unrestricted dimensional creation or universal Space manipulation for ordinary Youkai.

#### Supernatural Marking
Kitsune possess a racial method of marking their partners through the use of Youki. The process involves biting the chosen individual and injecting a quantity of Youki into the mark, establishing a supernatural indication of their relationship.[8]
Haruna employed this method with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] as part of their relationship, using a supernatural process comparable in social purpose to the mate-marking practices of [[Werewolves]] while differing in its underlying Energy. The presence of Youki distinguishes the Kitsune technique from an ordinary physical bite or a purely symbolic mark.[8]
- Supernatural Marking: Kitsune can mark a partner by injecting Youki through a bite.[8]
- Energy Imbuement: The mark incorporates the Kitsune's supernatural Energy into the affected individual.[8]
The mark's demonstrated purpose is the identification and establishment of a partner relationship. Additional abilities such as unrestricted location tracking, mind control or damage transfer should not be attributed to the mark without separate evidence.

### Youki-Based Techniques

#### The Four Dances of the Moon
The Four Dances of the Moon are a collection of sword techniques developed by [[Otsuki Yoichi]] and associated with the martial traditions of the [[Otsuki Clan]]. They combine specialized swordsmanship, Kitsune abilities and substantial quantities of Youki, allowing the practitioner to employ increasingly elaborate attacks against powerful supernatural opponents.[3][4]
Although Yoichi developed the original techniques, Haruna's exceptional talent enabled her to surpass his own ability to execute them. She refined the techniques to a degree that astonished her grandfather, incorporating additional applications that greatly increased their destructive potential. Their advanced use requires considerable control because adding new components to an already established martial technique can destabilize its intended operation and injure the practitioner.[3]

##### First Dance: Waning Moon
First Dance: Waning Moon is an offensive sword technique in which the practitioner channels concentrated Youki through a katana and releases it as an enormous crescent-shaped attack. Haruna's execution produced a beam requiring an extraordinary amount of Energy, exceeding what Yoichi himself could reproduce despite being the original creator of the technique.[3]
- Energy Projection: The technique releases concentrated Youki in the form of a gigantic crescent-shaped attack.[3]
- Weapon Enhancement: The user channels substantial quantities of Youki through a katana before releasing the attack.[3]
- Destructive Energy Manipulation: Its power depends upon the exceptionally concentrated Energy supplied by the practitioner.[3]
Haruna developed an additional application known as Waning Quarter, incorporating her splitting abilities to produce multiple variations of the original attack simultaneously.[3]

##### Second Dance: Moon Fall
Second Dance: Moon Fall produces an enormous artificial Moon above the battlefield. The construct serves as an offensive platform capable of projecting numerous energy beams toward the target. Its presence changes the conditions of the battle by allowing Haruna to threaten opponents from above while preparing subsequent techniques.[3][4]
- Energy Construct Creation: Haruna can produce an enormous Moon-like construct through her supernatural techniques.[3]
- Energy Projection: The artificial Moon can release large numbers of destructive energy beams toward enemies below.[4]
- Area-of-Effect Attack: The construct can threaten a substantial surrounding area instead of being limited to a single direct projectile.[4]
- Power Combination: The Moon functions as part of a larger martial sequence rather than being an entirely independent source of power.[4]
The Energy necessary to create and maintain the artificial Moon is enormous. Repeatedly recreating it substantially depleted Haruna's reserves, demonstrating the limitations imposed even upon exceptionally powerful Youki generators.[5]

##### Third Dance
The Third Dance combines precise sword attacks, sensory interference and a gravitational effect associated with the artificial Moon. During Haruna's duel with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], the technique imposed increasingly powerful gravity upon the battlefield, eventually reaching approximately one hundred times Earth's normal gravity. Its purpose was to restrict the opponent's movement while the practitioner delivered lethal attacks.[4]
Haruna's attacks concentrated upon vulnerable points, while the Youki surrounding her katana carried a poison intended to weaken enemies who survived the initial strikes. The combination of restraint, deception and persistent poisoning made the technique considerably more dangerous than an ordinary sword attack.[4]
- Gravity Manipulation (Technique-Based): The technique produces powerful gravitational pressure that restricts the opponent's movements.[4]
- Weapon Enhancement: Haruna concentrates Youki around her sword, producing a sharp supernatural cutting surface.[4]
- Poison Manipulation: The Youki carried by her katana is intended to poison and weaken surviving opponents.[4]
- Perception Manipulation: Illusions prevent the target from accurately identifying Haruna's position during the attack sequence.[4]
The gravitational effect belongs to the specialized martial technique rather than representing an ordinary property of every form of Youki.

##### Fourth Dance: Moonlight Flower
Fourth Dance: Moonlight Flower incorporates multiple powerful sword attacks aimed at an opponent's vulnerable points. Haruna combines precise movements with concentrated Youki, enhancing her katana's cutting power while exploiting the restrictive effects of her preceding techniques.[4]
- Swordsmanship: The technique employs multiple highly precise attacks against vulnerable parts of the target's body.[4]
- Energy Coating: Haruna envelops her katana in concentrated Youki, increasing its cutting power.[4]
- Weakness Exploitation: The attacks target locations intended to cause fatal damage against ordinary opponents.[4]
- Power Combination: The technique benefits from the restrictions and distractions established by the previous Dances.[4]

#### Destruction of the Nine Elements
Destruction of the Nine Elements is one of Haruna's most powerful techniques, combining the Energy and elemental characteristics associated with all nine of her Kitsune tails. Through her splitting abilities, Haruna creates multiple manifestations of herself, each channeling a different element. Their attacks converge into a massive multi-elemental pillar that interacts with the artificial Moon previously established above the battlefield.[4]
The nine elements demonstrated through Haruna's tails are Fire, Water, Earth, Wind, Metal, Wood, Lightning, Darkness and Light. Her possession of Light is particularly extraordinary because the [[Otsuki Clan]] is associated with Darkness, and other members of the Clan were not expected to manifest its opposing element. Yoichi regarded her ability to use Light as an anomaly contradicting the established expectations of the Clan's racial development.[4]
- Elemental Manipulation: Haruna can channel nine distinct elemental affinities through the supernatural abilities associated with her Kitsune tails.[4]
- Energy Projection: Each manifestation releases an elemental attack that converges upon the target.[4]
- Cloning / Splitting: Haruna uses her Kitsune splitting abilities to coordinate multiple simultaneous elemental attacks.[4]
- Power Combination: The nine attacks merge into a single massive column of Energy.[4]
- Energy Construct Manipulation: The combined attack interacts with Haruna's artificial Moon, drawing it into the final destructive sequence.[4]
The technique depends upon Haruna's exceptional racial abilities and control over her nine elements. The ability to manipulate all nine elements should not be assigned to every Youki user.

### Relationship with Other Energy Types

#### Youki and Miasma
Youki possesses similarities to Miasma because both are associated with negative supernatural characteristics. Morgana identified this relationship while regarding ordinary Youki as considerably weaker than Demonic Energy.[1]
The two energies nevertheless remain distinct. Youki originates from accumulated Human emotions and is employed primarily through Youkai physiology, while Miasma is associated with Demonkind and the supernatural environment of Hell. Their similar characteristics do not establish that they can be manipulated through identical methods or that all of their racial techniques are interchangeable.

#### Youki and Senjutsu
[[Senjutsu]] utilizes [[Natural Energy]] circulating through the environment and the planetary systems maintained by [[World Trees]]. Unlike Youki, which depends upon accumulated negative emotional Energy, Senjutsu draws upon an external source connected directly to planetary Life.[2]
The discovery of Senjutsu offered the unified Youkai civilization an alternative to the geographical limitations imposed by its traditional Energy system. However, the neutral properties of Natural Energy initially conflicted with the negative characteristics of Youki, making simultaneous control difficult.[2]
Haruna demonstrated exceptional adaptability by beginning to master both systems, although she acknowledged that fully controlling their interaction would require considerably more training.[2]

#### Youki and Ki
Ki is an internally cultivated energy associated with the Vitality naturally existing within living beings. Its source differs fundamentally from Youki, since the practitioner utilizes personal Life Energy instead of relying upon accumulated negative emotions.[2]
The distinction is particularly important for ordinary Youkai. Ki can potentially be developed outside regions naturally saturated with Youki, offering additional independence from Japan's supernatural environment. Its use nevertheless presents separate dangers because excessive expenditure can consume the practitioner's own Vitality and shorten their lifespan.[2]

#### Youki and Magic
Youki and [[Magic]] represent distinct conventional methods of employing supernatural Energy. Magic generally relies upon Mana and magical techniques, whereas Youki is associated with the racial characteristics and inherited supernatural traditions of Youkai.
Certain techniques used by Youkai can resemble magical spells, including illusions, barriers and elemental projections. However, reproducing a similar effect does not mean the Energy employed is identical. The techniques must be understood through the practitioner's actual abilities rather than their superficial resemblance to another supernatural system.

#### Youki and Divinity
Youki is not a [[Divine Energy]] and does not independently grant Godhood. Powerful Kitsune can possess extraordinary amounts of Youki while remaining mortal supernatural beings, and Haruna's original Nine-Tailed Kitsune physiology did not automatically confer the Divinity associated with attaining a higher state of existence.[3][9]
The distinction became particularly important when Haruna temporarily forced her evolution into a Ten-Tailed Kitsune and manifested powers associated with a higher Divine existence during the battle against Shur. Her transformation involved a dangerous change in her Soul and supernatural state rather than a simple increase in ordinary Youki reserves.[9]

#### Youki and Draconic Energy
The later development of the Youkai under [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction introduced access to powers associated with [[Blood Dragons]] and [[Draconic Energy]]. Haruna's own transformation into a Dragon fundamentally changed the Energy available to her, allowing her to use her [[Dragon Heart]] as a source of power superior to the conventional Youki and [[Senjutsu]] she had previously employed.[10]
This development did not necessarily eliminate her knowledge of traditional Youkai techniques. Rather, it changed the foundation through which those abilities could be sustained, demonstrating that the same technique may eventually be powered by a different supernatural Energy when its user possesses sufficient control and an appropriate source.

### Limitations and Weaknesses

#### Geographical Dependence
The principal limitation of traditional Youki is its dependence upon environmental concentrations of negative emotional Energy. Although Humans produce negative emotions throughout the world, Japan possesses the unusual conditions necessary for ordinary Youkai to obtain and employ Youki at their full potential.[1]
Most Youkai cannot freely generate the Energy required to maintain their strongest techniques outside suitable environments. Consequently, travel beyond Japan can substantially restrict their supernatural abilities unless they possess an alternative source or belong to one of the exceptional races capable of independent generation.

#### Racial Generation Restrictions
Independent Youki generation is not a universal characteristic of all Youkai. The ability belongs principally to powerful members of the three great races—Kitsune, Oni and Ryuji—while ordinary Youkai generally rely upon the environmental supply.[1]
The highest-ranking members of these races can overcome many geographical limitations, but their existence does not eliminate the dependence affecting the rest of the Youkai population.

#### Energy Depletion
Powerful techniques can consume enormous quantities of Youki. The artificial Moon created through Haruna's martial techniques provides a direct example, since recreating the construct repeatedly consumed sufficient Energy to leave her exhausted despite her extraordinary reserves.[5]
The same limitation applies to techniques requiring the continuous maintenance of artificial environments. Kuroka and her subordinates needed to channel additional Youki into the Inverse World when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s attacks threatened the stability of the battlefield.[4]

#### Energy Incompatibility
Youki's negative characteristics can conflict with other forms of Energy. The introduction of [[Senjutsu]] initially created difficulties for Haruna and the Youkai attempting to master it because the neutral properties of [[Natural Energy]] were incompatible with those of their traditional racial Energy.[2]
The problem demonstrates that acquiring another supernatural power does not automatically grant the ability to combine it safely with Youki. Successful integration requires substantial understanding, control and compatibility.

#### Specialized Techniques
Numerous applications demonstrated by powerful Youkai depend upon their racial abilities and martial training rather than arising automatically from ordinary Youki manipulation. Haruna's illusions, elemental techniques, gravitational attacks and artificial Moon are specialized abilities that employ Youki as a power source, not guaranteed characteristics of every practitioner.[3][4]

### Development of Youki Usage

#### Traditional Youkai Civilization
Youki historically served as the principal supernatural Energy sustaining much of Youkai civilization. Its concentration within Japan supported the development of powerful Clans, martial techniques and specialized racial abilities. However, the same geographical concentration that provided a favorable environment also restricted the independence of ordinary Youkai and contributed to their reliance upon traditional territories.[1]
The three great races possessed significant advantages through their ability to generate or convert Youki, reinforcing the differences in military and political strength between the most powerful Clans and ordinary supernatural groups.

#### Unification Under Haruna
Haruna's unification of the Youkai brought the major factions into a single political and military structure. The establishment of a unified civilization created opportunities for cooperation between the three great races and encouraged the development of supernatural techniques that could overcome limitations inherited from the older fragmented system.
The discovery of [[Senjutsu]] and Ki emerged from these efforts. Haruna gathered representatives of the great Clans to investigate alternative power sources, with the Ryuji revealing knowledge of the planetary Dragon Veins and the resulting research identifying [[Natural Energy]] as a possible replacement or supplement for ordinary Youki.[2]

#### Alternative Energy Systems
[[Senjutsu]] and Ki offered a means of reducing the dependence upon environmental Youki. Their discovery did not immediately invalidate the existing system because the new disciplines possessed different requirements and dangers. Haruna herself preferred employing Youki during her duel with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] rather than relying upon the unfamiliar energies she had only recently begun to cultivate.[2][3]
The development of alternative powers nevertheless marked a significant advancement in Youkai civilization, creating a path toward greater independence from the conditions that had historically restricted their supernatural strength.

#### Draconic Evolution
The later integration of the Youkai with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s evolving faction introduced a further transformation in their energy systems. Access to Draconic power provided a stronger foundation for racial techniques, and Haruna's own development enabled her to employ the Energy of her [[Dragon Heart]] instead of depending exclusively upon the traditional reserves of Youki or [[Senjutsu]].[10]
Youki therefore remains an important historical and supernatural characteristic of the Youkai even where certain individuals eventually obtain more powerful sources of Energy.
