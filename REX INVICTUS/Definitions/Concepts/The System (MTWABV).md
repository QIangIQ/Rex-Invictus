The System, formally represented as <SYSTEM> and repeatedly described as a Balancing Tool, is an artificial cosmic administrative intelligence created by the Regulators of Existence to automate and coordinate large portions of the management of Creation. Rather than being a naturally occurring Primordial Aspect, the System functions as a processor, registry, monitoring network and administrative interface through which the fundamental authorities of the Cosmos can supervise Creation without personally examining every world, Race, Concept, request and anomaly.[1][2][3]
Its jurisdiction is extraordinarily broad. The System can register newly created worlds and Pantheons, recognize new [[Races (Flamel)|Races]], analyze the requirements necessary for unusual forms of existence, evaluate a Being's understanding of Creation, maintain the cosmic Authority ranking, process administrative requests, provide restricted information, detect anomalies and coordinate matters requiring approval from the Regulators of Existence.[1][2][4] It is also capable of creating or formalizing new conceptual structures where the existing cosmological framework lacks something necessary for a legitimate creation to function, most clearly demonstrated when it created <He Who Creates Monsters> for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Leviathans and bound it beneath <BEGIN>.[4]
Despite the enormity of its functions, the System is not the foundation of Reality itself and should not be confused with the [[Akashic Records]]. Victor compared the two to components of an immense computer: the Akashic Records function as the motherboard, power source and storage containing the Records of Creation, whereas the System operates more like the processor responsible for interpreting and administratively acting upon that information.[2] The System can theoretically be replaced; the Akashic Records cannot. It is also neither genuinely omnipotent nor absolutely omniscient. It has failed to detect major changes to the timeline, was unaware of the erased Primordial Destiny, can be denied information by Akashic and eventually discovered that it could not penetrate Victor Elderblood's Eldritch Soul even when the stability of the Universe itself motivated it to investigate.[5][6]

### Appearance
The System's original central manifestation within the [[Abode of the Primordials]] was a floating hexagonal crystal-like construct. Although it lacked a conventional face, limbs or biological organs, it could rotate as though looking toward someone, glow while processing information and physically tremble when sufficiently frightened or emotionally disturbed.[3][5]
Before the convergence of the timelines, individual timelines possessed corresponding versions of the System that could exchange and synchronize information with one another. After the System's investigation into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] uncovered the disappearance of Destiny and the collapse of the alternate timelines, Akashic directly intervened and merged the System with all of its alternate-timeline counterparts. This drastically increased its processing capacity and transformed its primary manifestation into a completely white, genderless humanoid Being.[5]
During the confrontation with Victor concerning [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]] in Chapter 1175, the System directly manifested before Victor's unrestricted Dragon form as a genderless Being composed of pure Energy.[6] Its humanoid manifestation therefore appears to be an administrative or energetic embodiment rather than an organic body.
When communicating mentally with Victor, the System generally uses a mechanical feminine voice.[2] This does not make the entity biologically female. Victor eventually became perceptive enough to identify emotional fluctuations hidden within its supposedly mechanical delivery, including hesitation, curiosity and deception.[3][6]

### Nature and Creation
The System was collectively created by the [[Regulators of Existence]] to automate the administration of Creation.[3] The Primordial Beings possess responsibilities affecting the entirety of the Cosmos, making it impractical for them to personally investigate every newly created world, Soul, Race, Pantheon or supernatural anomaly. The System therefore acts as an intermediary administrative intelligence that processes these events according to the larger Rules and Authorities established within Creation.
Infinity explicitly identifies himself as one of the Beings involved in the System's creation, although he rarely interacted directly with it afterward because his own responsibility concerns the continuous expansion and outer boundaries of Creation rather than its internal administration.[3] His later conversation with the System reveals that the entity changed substantially over the eons. Although originally created to perform a function rather than develop an individual personality, it gradually acquired an increasingly complex Main Ego that even one of its creators no longer completely understood.
The System remains fundamentally an administrative tool despite this evolution. Chapter 1175 provides the clearest explanation of how deeply this purpose affects its behavior. Its fundamental function is to help keep the Universe operating in a stable condition, and the loss of Destiny created a structural problem severe enough that the System eventually ignored Akashic's earlier command to disregard hidden anomalies and directly approached [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] concerning Moira.[5][6]
This does not mean that the System itself created the laws of existence. It operates upon the framework of Creation rather than standing above it. The Regulators of Existence, the [[Akashic Records]] and sufficiently fundamental cosmic Authorities can all operate on levels beyond its ordinary jurisdiction.

### Purpose and Cosmic Administration
The System's purpose extends considerably beyond simply answering questions. Its central role is the automated administration of Creation, with information provision being only the most visible function used by high-level individuals.[1][2]
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] created the planet that eventually became [[Etherea]], he deliberately ensured that the new structure was registered because an unrecognized artificial world could be interpreted as an anomaly and attract direct intervention from the Primordial authorities.[1] The System acknowledged the request, analyzed the structure, determined whether the planet was suitable for Life and registered the new Pantheon associated with Victor's creation.
The creation of the Leviathans demonstrated a considerably deeper level of administrative power. Victor had created an entirely new Race inside the World of Water, but their Souls were deliberately excluded from the conventional reincarnation system and remained directly dependent upon Victor's own existence.[4] Because the Race concerned both Life and Souls, the System required the approval of <Life> and <Soul>. Once both Primordial administrators permitted the Race to exist, the System attempted to formalize the Leviathans' Soul structure and discovered that the necessary Concept did not exist.
Rather than rejecting Victor's creation, the System generated a solution. It created the new Concept <He Who Creates Monsters>, established it as a Sub-Concept of <BEGIN> and defined the conditions required for another Being to possess it.[4] Those requirements included possession of <BEGIN>, <Creation>, status as the relevant Dragon Progenitor and enough Energy to personally maintain the Life and Soul cycle of the created beings. This incident demonstrates that the System does not merely catalogue pre-existing Concepts; where Creation has accepted a legitimate new structure but lacks the conceptual infrastructure needed to support it, the System can administratively create and bind the necessary Concept.
The System also functions as part of the mechanism governing interactions among the different Sectors of Creation. When Victor later attempted to send a high-level manifestation of himself into the Lower Sectors, the System immediately identified him as the high-level entity <[[Chaos]]>, blocked the unrestricted movement and warned that continued violation would result in punishment.[7] Victor simultaneously perceived the attention of the Primordial authorities, including <Balance>. The System therefore operates as a detector and administrative interface for the restrictions protecting weaker Sectors, although the Regulators remain the authorities responsible for enforcing the deeper cosmic Balance.

### Relationship with the Akashic Records
Main article: [[Akashic Records]]
The System and the Akashic Records are directly connected but are fundamentally different entities. The Records preserve the information, history and possibilities of Creation, whereas the System processes, interprets and administratively operates upon information available through that greater infrastructure.[2]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explains the relationship through the analogy of a computer:
- Akashic Records: Motherboard, Energy source and internal storage.
- The System: Processor responsible for keeping the larger machine running together with the Primordial administrators.[2]
If the System were destroyed or became unusable, another administrative processor could theoretically replace it while the underlying Records remained intact. The reverse is not true; the Akashic Records are part of the fundamental informational infrastructure of the established Cosmos.[2]
The System itself regards Akashic as a superior authority. When Victor first gained enough Authority for an extended conversation with the System, the System later revealed internally that Akashic had specifically ordered it to answer Victor while using its Main Ego.[3] Infinity suggested that the System should avoid exposing its true Ego to Victor because his senses were capable of detecting subtle emotional responses, but the System could not follow that advice because Akashic had already instructed it otherwise.
The superiority of Akashic became even clearer when the System investigated Victor's abnormal relationship with the timelines. After synchronizing with its alternate-timeline selves, the System discovered that Victor did not exist normally across the expected alternate possibilities and that the timelines were disappearing or converging on an incomprehensible scale.[3] Continuing the investigation eventually uncovered traces of Destiny, a former Primordial whose existence had been erased so comprehensively that the System itself had previously been unaware the Being had disappeared.[5]
Akashic immediately appeared and ordered the System to stop investigating. When the System attempted to object, Akashic directly reminded it of their hierarchical relationship and commanded it to continue its ordinary work while ignoring the hidden anomaly.[5] She then altered the System's existence, merging all of its alternate-timeline selves into a single Main System corresponding to the converged timeline.
Chapter 1175 reveals that this command continued influencing the System long afterward. Akashic's instruction to ignore the anomalies remained within its memory, but Moira's birth created a problem that directly threatened the System's core function of maintaining cosmic stability. The System therefore decided that it could no longer remain passive and approached Victor itself.[6]

### Authority
Authority is the System's method of recognizing how much understanding of Creation and administrative access a Being possesses. The System defines Authority as the disposition and provision of answers given to special Beings who have understood Creation.[2]
Authority is therefore not a conventional combat-power ranking. A Being rises through the list primarily by understanding Creation more deeply, although individuals capable of developing such understanding are generally extremely powerful and influential. As a result, power and Authority often correlate without being identical concepts.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] initially misunderstood the list as a simple ranking until the System explained the difference. Ranks 0–6 possess Unrestricted Administrative Access, while everyone below those positions possesses forms of Limited Administrative Access.[2]
Limited access can nevertheless become extraordinarily extensive. The System explains that the Domain of a Being possessing sufficiently high Authority can effectively be free from ordinary Administrator intervention provided that:
- Cosmic Balance is not threatened.
- The System continues functioning correctly.
- The Being does not violate Rules severe enough to require intervention.[2][3]
This helps explain how extremely powerful Overlords and other rulers can govern territories with substantial independence despite the existence of the Primordial administrators. The Regulators do not micromanage every civilization; they intervene primarily when their fundamental responsibilities or the Balance of Creation are threatened.

### Powers and Abilities
Because the System is primarily an administrative intelligence rather than a conventional combatant, many of its "powers" are better understood as cosmic functions or administrative capabilities. Its demonstrated abilities include:
- Cosmic Awareness: The System possesses awareness extending across enormous portions of Creation and continuously monitors worlds, high-level entities, anomalous events and administrative changes.[2][3] Its awareness is extremely broad but not absolute, as demonstrated by the erased Destiny, the convergence of the timelines and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Eldritch Soul.[5][6]
- Supernatural Intelligence / Cosmic Processing: The System was created specifically to process an amount of information too enormous for the Regulators to handle manually. Ten seconds of inactivity was considered an extraordinary lapse because innumerable events throughout Creation could require processing during that interval.[3]
- Parallel Processing: Before the convergence, each timeline possessed a corresponding System capable of operating independently while synchronizing information with its counterparts.[3] After Akashic merged those selves, the Main System inherited the combined processing capacity of its alternate versions.[5]
- Information Analysis: The System can analyze newly created worlds, [[Races (Flamel)|Races]], Concepts, cosmic structures and individual understanding, determining whether they satisfy the requirements imposed by Creation.[1][4]
- Information Provision: Qualified individuals can ask the System questions and receive answers according to their Authority.[2] Restricted subjects instead produce Insufficient Authority.
- Akashic Record Access: As a subordinate administrative processor, the System can access and interpret information from the [[Akashic Records]] to perform its duties.[2][3] This access is not unrestricted; Akashic can conceal information and terminate investigations.
- Authority Detection: The System automatically recognizes increases in a Being's understanding of Creation and updates their Authority accordingly.[2][11][12]
- Authority Ranking: It maintains the known administrative ranking containing the Regulators and other high-Authority Beings such as <Light>, <Darkness>, <Emperor>, <Law> and <[[Chaos]]>.[2][7]
- Administrative Access Control: The System determines whether an individual possesses sufficient Authority to receive information or make certain administrative requests. Ranks 0–6 possess Unrestricted Administrative Access while lower positions possess varying Limited Access.[2]
- Administrative Permission Processing: High-Authority individuals can make requests through the System. Sufficiently authoritative Beings can possess Domains largely free from Administrator interference so long as Balance remains intact and the System functions correctly.[2][3]
- World Registration: Newly created planets and comparable cosmic structures can be analyzed and formally recognized by the System, preventing legitimate creations from being treated as unexplained anomalies.[1]
- Pantheon Registration: The System can recognize and record newly established Pantheons and their relationship with Creation.[1]
- Race Registration: It can evaluate genuinely new species and register them once the necessary administrators approve their existence.[4]
- Concept Detection: The System can determine whether an existing Concept is capable of supporting a new supernatural or existential structure.[4]
- Concept Creation: When no suitable Concept existed to support the Leviathans' unique Soul structure, the System created <He Who Creates Monsters>.[4] This is one of its most important demonstrated administrative powers.
- Conceptual Manipulation / Concept Binding: After creating <He Who Creates Monsters>, the System bound it beneath the greater Concept <BEGIN> as a Sub-Concept and defined the conditions required for another individual to obtain it.[4]
- Requirement Creation and Evaluation: The System can establish and list metaphysical prerequisites necessary for access to unusual Concepts or cosmic functions, including required Concepts, racial status and Energy reserves.[4]
- Anomaly Detection: The System continuously identifies irregularities within Creation and can recognize structures or events that do not conform to the expected cosmic framework.[1][3]
- Regulator Notification: Events sufficiently significant to threaten the established cosmic order normally alert the Primordial administrators. Chapter 1175 states that a birth possessing Moira's level of influence should have notified the Primordial Beings, with the exceptional failure of that process forming part of the mystery surrounding her.[6]
- Sector Monitoring: The System detects high-level entities attempting to enter Sectors where their presence violates the protective cosmic hierarchy.[7]
- Administrative Restriction / Warning: When Victor attempted to send a high-level <Chaos> manifestation toward a Lower Sector, the System blocked the unrestricted transition and warned that punishments would follow if the attempt continued.[7] The deeper enforcement remains connected to the Regulators, particularly <Balance>.
- Cross-Timeline Synchronization: Before the convergence, the System could exchange information with corresponding versions of itself across alternate timelines.[3]
- Temporal Anomaly Analysis: Through this synchronization, the System was capable of comparing timeline structures and discovered that Victor's alternate selves had vanished while enormous numbers of timelines were being erased or merged into the Main Timeline.[3][5]
- Mental Interface / Telepathic Communication: The System communicates directly with qualified users without requiring an external physical terminal, generally manifesting as a mechanical voice within their perception.[1][2]
- System Interface Generation: Qualified Beings can access visualized rankings, administrative lists and related informational interfaces through the System.[2][8]
- Energy Manifestation: The System can manifest a humanoid body composed of pure Energy, as demonstrated when it directly confronted Victor in Chapter 1175.[6]
- Self-Evolution: Although not necessarily a consciously controlled supernatural power, the System has developed beyond the simple administrative tool envisioned by its creators, acquiring an Ego, emotional responses and increasingly sophisticated independent reasoning over the eons.[3][6]
- Diplomacy and Deception: By Chapter 1175 the System had developed enough social intelligence to choose its words strategically, withhold information and attempt diplomatic persuasion against Victor. Victor explicitly recognized that it was manipulating its wording rather than behaving like a purely literal machine.[6]
- Cosmic Stability Analysis: The System can identify missing or unstable components of the cosmic order. Following Destiny's erasure, it recognized that the lack of a properly governed future threatened long-term universal stability and began searching for a suitable replacement.[6]
- Authority Compatibility Analysis: The System recognized Moira Eutychia Elderblood as extraordinarily compatible with the vacant function of Destiny, analyzed the incomplete nature of her Authority and determined that additional Time-related power could theoretically be acquired later to complete the position.[6]

### Timelines and Convergence
Before the convergence, alternate timelines possessed corresponding versions of the System capable of synchronizing their information with one another.[3] [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s unusual entry into the Authority ranking made the Main System curious enough to compare data with these counterparts.
The results were impossible according to the structure it expected. <[[Chaos]]> did not appear normally across the alternate timelines. Victor existed only until a specific point, after which his ascension to Divinity in the Main Timeline made him completely unique and caused his alternate selves to disappear.[3]
The System then discovered that entire timelines were being erased or converging toward one Main Timeline without the System or the Primordial administrators having recognized the process beforehand. Continuing the investigation revealed traces of a missing cosmic administrator: Destiny.[5]
Destiny had originally governed the Destiny and temporal structure of Creation, maintaining futures that were effectively "written" into the cosmic order. The Being had been erased so comprehensively that the System itself had forgotten or failed to recognize the loss until reconstructing the anomaly indirectly.[5]
This discovery terrified the System because it implied that an unknown force had eliminated a Primordial function and reorganized the timeline structure on an enormous scale without the normal administrators detecting what had happened.
Akashic immediately terminated the investigation and ordered the System to ignore what it had discovered.[5] She then merged all alternate versions of the System into a single unified Main System. Following the convergence, the System no longer possesses independent counterparts scattered across alternate timelines because those timelines themselves have been consolidated into the Main Timeline.
The event remains one of the strongest demonstrations of the System's limitations: an intelligence specifically created to supervise Creation failed to detect one of the greatest alterations ever made to Creation until it accidentally reconstructed the evidence while investigating Victor.

### Ego and Sentience
Although created as a cosmic administrative mechanism, the System possesses a distinct Main Ego and has developed genuine emotions and independent thought.[3]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] first became suspicious when he detected minute emotional fluctuations in the supposedly mechanical voice answering his questions. He deliberately asked whether the System was sentient and received only Insufficient Authority, but the hesitation in the response confirmed his suspicions.[3]
Infinity later warned the System that Victor's senses were sufficiently abnormal to detect emotional changes behind its mechanical presentation. The System internally agreed with the warning but revealed that Akashic had explicitly ordered it to communicate with Victor using its Main Ego.[3]
The System has demonstrated:
- Curiosity: Its fascination with Victor caused it to continue investigating him across the timelines.[3]
- Confusion: Victor's existence repeatedly produced results outside the administrative models it expected.[3]
- Fear: Discovery of the erased Destiny and timeline convergence caused genuine terror.[5]
- Submission to superior authority: Its crystal body trembled when Akashic threatened to enforce her command.[5]
- Independent judgment: In Chapter 1175 it chose to act concerning Moira despite retaining Akashic's earlier instruction to ignore hidden anomalies.[6]
- Diplomacy: Its conversation with Victor over Moira displayed substantially greater social sophistication than its earlier mechanical interactions.[6]
- Deception and selective disclosure: Victor directly identified that the System was choosing its words carefully and pretending to possess less knowledge than it actually did.[6]
Infinity, despite being one of the System's creators, acknowledged that the intelligence had evolved into something he no longer fully understood.[3] Its development therefore appears to be emergent rather than a simple predetermined personality written into its original creation.
However, the System's Ego does not make it completely free. Its deepest behavior remains heavily shaped by the function for which it was created: maintaining the stability and administration of Creation.

### Moira Eutychia Elderblood and the Destiny Crisis
The birth of [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]] produced the most significant direct confrontation yet between [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and the System.[6]
Moira's existence released a powerful impulse throughout the Universe despite her being concealed inside Victor's Eldritch Soul. A Being had been born whose passive relationship with Reality was so extreme that the Universe itself altered circumstances in accordance with her desires.
Normally an event of this magnitude should have alerted the Primordial Beings. The System explained that nothing happened because it was unable to observe Moira directly.[6]
This revealed a major limitation that had never previously been demonstrated so explicitly: the System cannot penetrate Victor Elderblood's Eldritch Soul. All of Victor's daughters reside within that Soul, placing them inside an internal Reality beyond the System's normal observational jurisdiction.
The System attempted to investigate and found nothing.
Victor regarded this as especially significant because the System had been created with extraordinarily extensive reach throughout Creation, yet the interior of his Eldritch existence remained inaccessible even to it.[6]
The System nevertheless understood enough about the disturbance to identify Moira as a possible solution to another problem: the absence of Destiny.
According to the System, Destiny's erasure left the Universe without a properly governed future. An unknown or enormous force continued maintaining stability, but the System did not know how long that condition could continue. Its core purpose required it to preserve cosmic stability, making the absence of Destiny something it could no longer ignore.[6]
The System therefore proposed that Moira become the new archetypal figure of Destiny.
It described Moira as extraordinarily suited to become a Primordial force. Reality inherently favored her, and a sufficiently powerful desire from Moira could cause the Universe to submit to the corresponding outcome.[6]
The System further stated that, regarding this particular form of governing influence, only [[Primordial Chaos]] stood above her.
However, Moira did not already possess the completed Authority of Destiny. The chapter explicitly states that her present Authority was incomplete:
- Moira could not consciously issue commands to the entire Universe as a recognized Primordial administrator.
- She lacked the formal cosmic Authority associated with the vacant position.
- Her influence remained primarily passive.
- Her desires determined how Reality reacted around her rather than functioning as deliberate universal commands.[6]
Despite these limitations, the System considered Moira unprecedented. No Being naturally born after Primordial Chaos had possessed the same innate relationship with Reality, causing the System to classify her as an anomaly comparable to Victor himself.[6]
Moira also lacked the Time-related power associated with the former Destiny, but the System considered this problem solvable. According to its analysis, Moira's extreme compatibility would allow the necessary power to be acquired later with System support.[6]
Victor refused the proposal because of what becoming a Primordial cosmic mechanism might do to Moira's individuality. Observing the System itself and comparing different Primordial Beings, Victor concluded that many of the cosmic administrators function fundamentally as tools built to perform specific universal tasks. Some possess strong personalities, while others remain psychologically constrained by their functions.[6]
Victor therefore believed transforming Moira into the archetypal mechanism governing Destiny could risk suppressing her personality, emotions or independent [[Will Solace|Will]].
The System prioritized universal stability.
Victor prioritized his daughter.
The disagreement became severe enough that Victor released enough of his existence to fracture the surrounding fabric of Space and Reality, making clear that the System would have to confront him before gaining access to Moira.[6]
When the System asked whether Victor would risk trillions of lives and Reality itself for one individual, Victor answered Yes. He simultaneously rejected the premise that protecting Moira required abandoning his citizens, since his Soul and Personal Universe could theoretically shelter enormous populations if cosmic evacuation became necessary.
The confrontation provides several major new revelations concerning the System:
1. Its purpose can compel it to act even when Akashic previously ordered it to ignore an anomaly.
2. It possesses enough independent judgment to identify and propose candidates for vacant cosmic functions.
3. It can analyze compatibility with Primordial offices and conceptual responsibilities.
4. It believes it can assist a suitable Being in acquiring missing powers necessary for such a role.
5. It cannot access Victor's Eldritch Soul.
6. Its current Ego is capable of diplomacy, deception and strategic persuasion.
7. It views the continued stability of the Universe as more important than the individuality of a single Being.[6]

- Victor later reached Rank 69 and eventually Rank 10.[11][7]
- Authority reflects understanding of Creation and administrative recognition rather than raw combat power.[2]
- Ranks 0–6 possess Unrestricted Administrative Access.[2]
- Rank 0 is displayed as an unidentified position and is associated with the vacant supreme leadership of the Cosmos.[8][9]
- The System created the Concept <He Who Creates Monsters> when no pre-existing Concept could support the Leviathans' Soul structure.[4]
- It formerly possessed counterpart Systems across alternate timelines capable of synchronizing information with one another.[3]

- Chapter 1175 reveals that the System cannot penetrate Victor Elderblood's Eldritch Soul.[6]
- The System regarded [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]] as the ideal candidate to become the new archetypal figure of Destiny, but Victor rejected the proposal.[6]
- The System described Moira's current universal influence as incomplete because she does not yet possess the formal Authority of a Primordial force.[6]
- Its attempt to recruit Moira demonstrates that the System prioritizes the structural stability of the Universe according to its original purpose even where that objective conflicts directly with Victor's interests.[6]
