Primordial Entities occupy a state where Concept, Soul and existence cease to be cleanly separable. Chapter 725 first gives the transformation a precise mechanism: a Primordial God approaching the next state must produce a qualitative change in the Soul until that Soul becomes the Concept and the individual becomes part of an Aspect of Existence.[1] Later explanations expand the model. A completed native Primal Force is not merely a person who has maximized a Divinity; it is a fundamental component of the Universe, with Gods sharing the same Concept ultimately drawing through the greater conceptual foundation represented by that Primordial.[4]
The ancient native Primordials are consequently described as abstract Concepts given form, Pillars of Existence and fundamental pieces required for the Universe to function.[5][6] Their significance is ontological before it is destructive: eliminating a local manifestation is not equivalent to eliminating the universal function, while genuine removal of the underlying Aspect can alter Reality itself. If Destruction were completely removed, destruction would cease to function throughout that Universe; if Death disappeared, things would lose the fundamental mechanism by which they end; if Infinity disappeared, the Cosmos would cease to possess Infinity as a native property.[6][3][4]

### Primordial Nature
The first detailed explanation of Primordial ascension appears through [[Kali Elderblood]]. By Chapter 725, Kali had penetrated so deeply into Destruction that the distinction between Goddess and Concept was beginning to collapse. The chapter explicitly separates a Primordial Goddess from a Primordial Entity by the condition of the Soul: an ordinary God or Primordial God possesses a higher-quality Divine Soul, but the next transition requires transforming that Soul into a Concept until the Being becomes part of an Aspect of Existence.[1]
PRIMORDIAL TRANSFORMATION
A God possesses a Concept. A Primordial Entity progressively becomes the Concept.
The distinction concerns the underlying Soul and identity, not merely the amount of [[Divine Energy]] available to the individual.[1]
Chapter 1074 later describes the established Primordials as abstract Concepts taken form and Pillars of Existence.[5] Chapter 1075 explains why this matters: these Beings are fundamental pieces of the Universe representing Aspects of Creation required for the whole structure to function.[6] Chapter 1185 expresses the same principle through the later term Primal Force. A God of Destruction can wield Destruction; a Primal Force of Destruction would cease being merely its Divine user and become Destruction itself.[4]
This produces a qualitative difference between Primordials and enormously powerful ordinary Gods. Destructive capacity, Energy reserves, age and combat mastery can all increase without changing the fundamental ontology of the user. Primordial development instead alters what the Being is. The Concept becomes an intrinsic part of its Soul, its existential weight and eventually its cosmic function.[1][10]

#### Primordial God and Primordial Entity
The term Primordial God predates the later cosmological explanations and does not by itself mean that the individual has become a true Primordial Entity. A Primordial God can possess tremendous age, [[Divine Energy]] and Conceptual proficiency while remaining fundamentally a God. Kali is the clearest demonstration: despite being among the greatest users of Destruction and standing one step from the Primordial transition, the story continues to distinguish her Goddess state from completed Primordial existence.[1]

### Divine Concepts and the Conceptual Field
The late cosmology establishes that Divine Concepts are not created only when a God happens to awaken them. In Chapter 1186, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explains that the Universe itself nourishes Divine Concepts and that Concepts can exist throughout Reality without ever having been used by a current God.[11] This makes the conceptual field broader than the known Divine pantheons and far broader than the established Primordial offices.
Victor consequently theorizes that an enormous variety of ideas could potentially exist as Divine Concepts. He suggests that perhaps every adjective could correspond to a Concept, using his own unusual Yandere Divinity and the highly specialized Gods of the [[Shinto Pantheon]] as evidence for how specific Concepts can become.[11] This particular extension is explicitly Victor's deduction rather than a formally demonstrated exhaustive rule. What is directly established is that unused Divine Concepts exist and are nourished by the Universe itself.[11]
The Concepts are not all equal in cosmological importance. Victor distinguishes highly specific or personal Concepts from primary Concepts such as Life, Death, Time, Space and Nature.[11] The known Primordial offices consistently correspond to universal functions whose absence would alter the operation of Reality itself. The existence of a minor or specialized Divinity therefore does not require the existence of a corresponding Primordial administrator.

#### Concepts as Foundations of Reality
Chapters 1167–1168 provide a practical demonstration of how deeply Concepts can anchor an existence. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] constructs an ordinary subordinate dimension and compares it with a miniature galaxy into whose foundation he has incorporated genuine Divine Concepts. Although the comparison is not about physical mass, [[Norabell Elderblood]] perceives the Concept-rich structure as vastly "heavier". Victor identifies this difference as existential weight.[10][3]
Divine Concepts are compared to metaphysical keys or portions of Reality's deepest operating code. A sufficiently strong Being may sometimes force an effect without possessing the appropriate key, but direct Authority over the relevant Concept allows much more natural interaction with the corresponding Rules.[3] Primordial embodiment applies this principle at the universal level: the Being is not simply using a key but has become one of the foundational components through which that part of Reality functions.

### Conceptual Competition Across Sectors
Chapter 1185 gives the clearest explanation of how ordinary Gods sharing the same Concept interact across the wider Universe. The Concept itself does not become a different Concept merely because the God enters a larger Sector. A God of Destruction remains a God of Destruction. What changes is the user's relative influence over that Concept.[4]
A normal God who dominates a Concept in a Lower Sector may encounter numerous competitors upon entering a Mid-Level Sector and can therefore fall from the dominant user to only one of many. In the Higher Sectors, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explains that there can be hundreds of Gods sharing the same Concept, creating what he compares to a gigantic tug-of-war stretching across the Universe.[4] Strength, understanding and the user's relationship with the Concept determine how much influence an ordinary God can exercise within that competition.

This also clarifies why destroying the actual Primordial Aspect is so catastrophic. If a God of Destruction dies, other users and Destruction itself remain. If the universal Primal Force and its fundamental Aspect were somehow erased at the conceptual level, Destruction itself would cease to operate across that Universe.[4]

#### Soul Transformation
Kali's route demonstrates the Soul requirement most clearly. As her understanding of Destruction deepened, the Concept began merging progressively with her Soul. By Chapter 1033, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] observes that she is actively forcing this process, with her existence preventing the Soul from simply collapsing beneath the strain.[2] Her later designation as a Quasi-Primordial confirms that she had genuinely entered the transitional state rather than merely possessing an unusually strong Divinity.[8]

#### Compatibility with the Cosmic Function
Kali also demonstrates why Conceptual development is not the only requirement of the native route. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] concludes in Chapter 1033 that she already possesses the necessary strength, Soul and mentality to have progressed further, yet Creation continues preventing completion.[2] The problem is the role itself.
The established Primordials are expected to perform their functions according to the needs of the Universe rather than according to ordinary personal attachment. A Primordial of Destruction must destroy when Destruction is required so that Creation and the universal cycle can continue. Kali, however, remains personally devoted to her Pantheon and would not willingly destroy what she loves merely because a cosmic mechanism demanded it. Victor therefore concludes that the native system refuses to promote her because her personal values conflict with the impartial responsibility of the office.[2]
This does not mean every Primordial possesses no individuality. [[The Universal Tree]] is openly emotional and biased toward his family, while the Judges of the Abyss behave far more mechanically. The evidence instead shows that the office places functional obligations upon the Being and that severe failure to perform the required role can prevent or complicate native ascension.[2][7]

#### One Primordial per Concept
The native Cosmos currently follows a rule allowing only one completed Primordial Being for a given conceptual office.[4] The three Judges of the Abyss do not violate the rule because they are fundamentally one Soul Primordial divided into three expressions.[4]
The restriction belongs to the present native cosmological framework rather than existing above the Creator. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly states that [[Primordial Chaos]] can change the rule if it wishes.[4] Independent cosmologies can also support analogous incarnations without competing for the same native position, which is why Kassandra can potentially develop into an Eldritch Primal Force of Destruction without necessarily taking the native Destruction office intended for Kali.[4]

### Primordial Chaos
Main article: [[Primordial Chaos]]
Primordial Chaos is not merely another specialized Primordial. It is the Creator-class foundation from which [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s native Universe originated and occupies Rank 0 within the known Authority structure.[12][4] Where a normal Primal Force embodies one fundamental function, Primordial Chaos is described as the embodiment of the Universe itself.[4]
RANK 0 • CREATOR FOUNDATION
PRIMORDIAL CHAOS
Complete embodiment of the native Universe and the supreme Authority from which its divided Duality and subordinate cosmic functions derive.
Chapter 1185 states that a Universe requires a Primordial Chaos and describes a Universe lacking its complete active embodiment as unprotected.[4] Within the Universe it embodies, complete Primordial Chaos is described as omnipotent, omniscient and omnipresent. These statements are explicitly tied to its own cosmological realm and should not be expanded into unrestricted supremacy over every independent Universe or foreign Cosmology.[4]
All Creation belonging to the native Universe exists within the greater Reality represented by Primordial Chaos. A clash between two complete Primordial Chaoses is therefore compared to a collision between embodiments of two different Universes.[4] Victor explains that at this scale ordinary comparisons involving martial technique, localized advantages or conventional power levels stop being useful because the combatants represent entire cosmological foundations.

#### The Division of the Creator
[[Primordial Chaos]] presently does not exist in its complete Rank 0 condition. [[The Universal Tree]] confirms that the original conscious Being was divided into Positivity and Negativity, which now survive as two semi-conscious halves.[13] Chapter 1176 further clarifies that the Creator is not simply dead in an ordinary sense: its consciousness survives through the divided state, and the two supreme Concepts themselves select appropriate successors.[14]
The exact ancient event responsible for this condition remains incompletely explained. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] deduces from the Creator's damaged state and the wider cosmological defensive structure that another Universe or another Primordial Chaos may have been involved in an ancient inter-universal conflict.[13] The Universal Tree refuses to provide a direct historical confirmation. Chapter 1185 adds another important qualification: when Victor concludes that the native Primordial Chaos must have lost, Negativity immediately reacts against that conclusion and indicates that the opposing side was in an even worse state.[4] The exact victor and exact circumstances therefore remain unresolved.

#### Creator Authority
Rank 0 stands above the present one-Primordial-per-Concept restriction. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly states that the Creator can alter that rule.[4] [[Primordial Chaos]] therefore does not merely occupy the highest position within an immutable system; its Authority reaches the framework establishing at least some of the cosmological Rules beneath it.
The current Creator is nevertheless too damaged or divided to exercise complete Rank 0 administration normally. The System continues to recognize a superior Authority above the present Primordials, and later dialogue indicates that the Creator has not relinquished enough of that Authority for the remaining administrators to simply replace it at will.[14][15]

#### Origin of the Other Primordials and Akashic Records
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] considers it logical that [[Primordial Chaos]] created the other Primordial Beings and the [[Akashic Records]], particularly because [[Jeanne Elderblood]] and the Universal Tree remember the Creator as their origin.[11] He explicitly states, however, that he does not know this for certain. The origin of the Akashic Records and the exact creation history of every Primordial remain deductions based upon known cosmological relationships rather than fully confirmed historical facts.[11]
The present large-scale arrangement of the Universe is likewise uncertain. Victor theorizes that the Creator left active construction too early because of the ancient cosmological conflict and that the surviving Primordials may consequently have organized substantial portions of the present structure themselves.[11] He again presents this as a reasoned possibility rather than confirmed history.

### Positivity and Negativity
Main article: Positivity & Negativity
Positivity and Negativity are the divided Rank 1 halves of [[Primordial Chaos]], not ordinary specialized Primordial Concepts.[12][14][13] Together they represent the split state of the original Creator and permeate the entire native Universe.
Chapter 1185 places this Duality above even Attributeless Energy in the conceptual hierarchy of the Creator's existence: Primordial Chaos created the Energy, whereas Positivity and Negativity are the present divided incarnation of the Creator itself.[4] [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explains that everything in the Universe contains both halves. His examples describe spiritual, emotional and non-physical phenomena as leaning more strongly toward Negativity, while material and concrete phenomena lean toward Positivity; even fundamental Divine Concepts such as Death, Destruction, Time and Space contain both aspects.[4]

The percentages Victor uses in his Chapter 1185 lesson are best understood as an explanation of dominance rather than as a replacement for the broader principle that both halves are present throughout Creation. He describes heavily spiritual or abstract phenomena as carrying a greater Negativity share and physical phenomena as carrying a greater Positivity share, while stressing that neither half operates independently from the other.[4]
The consequence is enormous conceptual reach. During the Chapter 1176 confrontation, the Judges explain that catastrophically damaging Negativity would affect approximately half of the Universe directly and throw the remainder into disorder because Positivity and Negativity function as two parts of one greater structure.[14] Victor's inheritance therefore grants him extraordinary Authority over Concepts without automatically making Negativity itself synonymous with his personal combat ability. Victor explicitly distinguishes the Authority inherited from Negativity from the strength he personally accumulated through his Blood Dragon lineage, martial skill, Eldritch nature and mastery of his other powers.[4]

#### Chosen Successors
Positivity and Negativity cannot be acquired by ordinary Conceptual training in the same manner as normal Divinities because their semi-conscious primordial foundations select the recipient.[5][14] Negativity chose [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], making him its successor; Positivity has chosen Light as the corresponding successor in the Higher Sectors.[14]
Victor has consequently inherited half of Rank 0's Authority, but he has not restored complete [[Primordial Chaos]] and has not yet become the full embodiment of Negativity.[14] Inheritance, mastery and complete embodiment are therefore separate stages.

### Authority and the System
Authority represents recognized access to Reality at the level of Concepts, Rules and administrative functions. It is related to power but is not identical to Energy reserves or destructive output. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] compares high Authority to administrator rights over an immense machine: rather than destroying every individual component, a sufficiently authorized Being can interact with the deeper rules governing the system itself.[3]

#### System
The System is an administrative intelligence rather than a Primordial Entity. It manages Creation and possesses extensive access throughout the native Universe, but its permissions remain subordinate to still greater cosmological Authorities. Chapter 964 reveals that the [[Akashic Records]] can directly order the System and that the System considers itself a subordinate of that structure within Creation.[12]
Chapter 1175 demonstrates another limitation. Despite its universal administrative access, the System is unable to penetrate [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Eldritch Soul to inspect Moira.[7] The System therefore possesses extraordinary administrative reach without being synonymous with unlimited Authority over every foreign or Eldritch structure.
The System's interest in Moira also demonstrates the distinction between recognition and completed office. It identifies her as almost perfectly suited to Destiny, but recognizing that compatibility does not itself make her the completed Primal Force.[7]

The native Primordial functions operate as an interconnected system rather than isolated supernatural Domains. Chapter 1181 gives the clearest description of this universal cycle. Life begins through the Universal Tree and the structures connected to it; Souls pass through the Judges; information is preserved through the Akashic system; Death governs the ending and decay of existence; the released Energy is recycled so that Life can continue; Infinity sustains cosmic expansion; and Balance prevents internal actors from destabilizing the whole.[13]

The amount of information and Energy contained in an existence affects how long this recycling can require. The Universal Tree explains that ordinary Beings may take comparatively manageable spans, while an existence carrying energy on [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s scale could theoretically require trillions of years to process completely.[13]

#### Distributed Expression of Life
[[The Universal Tree]] demonstrates that a Primordial function can manifest through an enormous distributed network. Every World Tree functions as a node extending Life throughout its world, and the Universal Tree treats those Trees as extensions of its greater system.[15] Destroying one World Tree does not destroy Life itself; replacement nodes can arise while the greater Primordial network remains.
This gives the Universal Tree tremendous awareness through Life's distributed infrastructure. Chapter 1168 describes him as connected throughout the Cosmos through the [[World Trees]] and able to access information and locations through that relationship.[3] This mechanism should not automatically be copied to every Primordial because the network is specifically demonstrated as part of Life's function.

#### Primordial Jurisdiction
Established Primordials possess jurisdiction corresponding to their functions. When the Universal Tree formally places [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family beneath its protection, other Primordials cannot casually ignore Life's claim without escalating the situation into a dispute between cosmic Authorities.[5] Primordial Domains are therefore not merely geographical territories; they represent conceptual and administrative jurisdiction.
The principle also explains why Primordial conflict is dangerous independently of conventional attack scale. The combatants are parts of the same machine they would be damaging. A war between universal Concepts can compromise the system around them even when neither side attempts to destroy the Universe directly.[6][14]

#### Possible Defensive Architecture
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] concludes from the arrangement of the Primordial offices that the system may double as a defense against threats originating from other Universes.[13] The World Tree network covers living worlds, Soul examines incoming spiritual information, Infinity occupies the expanding frontier, Death watches the terminal cycle, Balance monitors internal destabilization and the System searches for irregularities.
The individual functions are confirmed; the claim that they were deliberately designed as one coordinated anti-invasion defense is Victor's inference.[13] [[The Universal Tree]] never provides a complete design document for the Creator's original intentions.

### Existential Weight and Higher-Dimensionality
The novel uses higher-dimensional language to explain qualitative differences between levels of existence, but later dialogue explicitly warns against treating those analogies as a literal numbered tier system. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] refers to models describing higher beings as pure Energy, pure intelligence or consciousness beyond ordinary Space-Time and observes that such imagery resembles attempts by Mortals to conceptualize Primordial Beings.[18][3]
He nevertheless rejects simplistic equations such as "Primordial Being = tenth-dimensional Being."[3] A dimensional location, a higher plane, conceptual embodiment and Primordial ontology are separate properties. [[Agares]], for example, obtained enormous tactical advantages by occupying a dimensional layer ordinary opponents could not perceive, but that did not make him a Primordial; once opponents could perceive and interact with the same layer, the advantage ceased being absolute.[10]
The more important late-story concept is existential weight. Divine Concepts incorporated into an existence make that existence metaphysically more entrenched in Reality.[10][3] Primordials possess extreme versions of this principle because the Concept is not merely added as an ability—the Concept has become part of the Being's identity and universal function.

### Foreign and Eldritch Primal Forces
The native route is not the only possible path toward Conceptual embodiment. The discovery of [[Azathoth Elderblood]] and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s maturation as an Eldritch [[Chaos]] establishes that independent cosmologies can possess fundamentally different Rules and conceptual foundations.[19] A Being whose existence originates outside the native Universe is not automatically bound to every administrative limitation established by its [[Primordial Chaos]].
This becomes directly relevant to Kassandra and Kali in Chapter 1185. Kassandra can potentially take the native Destruction office, but doing so would prevent Kali from occupying the same native Concept under the current one-Primordial rule. Victor instead explains that Kassandra's Eldritch heritage permits another possibility: she can become an Eldritch Primal Force of Destruction, essentially embodying Destruction through another cosmological foundation rather than replacing the native Destruction office.[4]
Kali may eventually become capable of the same alternative route, although Victor states that she cannot do so yet because her Eldritch integration is much less complete than Kassandra's.[4] The decisive factor again involves the Soul. Kassandra is Eldritch in both Soul and body, giving her a much more natural route toward a foreign Primordial foundation.

Chapter 725 describes a Primordial Entity as genuinely immortal and capable of persisting until the End of Time because its Soul has become part of an Aspect of Existence.[1] Chapters 1074–1075 reinforce this by explaining that established Primordials are integrated into the Universe itself.[5][6]

This explains why the phrase "killing a Primordial" can refer to very different events. Destroying a body, suppressing consciousness, replacing an administrator and erasing the actual Concept are not equivalent outcomes.

#### Conceptual Erasure
Chapter 1075 directly explains that true removal of a Primordial Aspect would have universal consequences. Removing Soul would cause Souls themselves to cease functioning; removing Life would eliminate the foundation of living existence; removing Balance would remove one of the systems preserving cosmic equilibrium.[6] Chapter 1168 extends the example to Death and Infinity, while Chapter 1185 applies the same logic to a hypothetical Primal Force of Destruction.[3][4]

#### Destiny's Erasure
Destiny is the clearest demonstration that an established Primordial function can nevertheless be removed under sufficiently extreme circumstances. The erasure is so thorough that the other Primordials lose normal memory of Destiny, while the System discovers only traces revealing that the missing Regulator once existed.[12][16]
Destiny's disappearance removes the old foundation that made predetermined futures cosmologically inevitable.[12] The later collapse of the branching timeline system should, however, be separated from the original erasure itself. Chapter 965 shows Akashic directly intervening, synchronizing the timeline-specific Systems and consolidating the remaining active structure into the Main Timeline.[16] Destiny's erasure created the missing cosmic function; Akashic's later action completes the timeline convergence.
The vacancy remains cosmologically relevant long afterward. In Chapter 1175, the System seeks Moira precisely because the Destiny office is still empty and the System fears that the present arrangement may not remain stable indefinitely.[7]

#### Moira and Natural Governing Compatibility
Moira demonstrates the largest inversion of the conventional path currently shown. The established Primordials were not born possessing their completed governing permissions, yet Moira emerges with Reality responding directly to her wishes before she has accepted any office.[7] The System describes her as born to be a Primal Force, with only [[Primordial Chaos]] above her in governing compatibility.
Her influence is nevertheless passive. She cannot consciously issue universal administrative commands because she lacks the completed Authority and office.[7] The distinction is crucial: extraordinary Reality response does not automatically mean she already is Destiny.
The System's interest in her also raises a cost [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] considers unacceptable. Established Primordials can be heavily conditioned by their function, and Victor fears that accepting the office could subordinate his daughter's individuality to the cosmic job she would be expected to perform.[7] Whether every Primordial office necessarily causes such a loss of individuality has not been demonstrated; the Universal Tree retains a strong personality, whereas the Judges display much more mechanical behavior.

#### Kassandra: Destruction vs END
Kassandra's birth also clarifies that Conceptual embodiment does not make two related Concepts identical. She is born as Destruction itself, yet Chapter 1150 explicitly distinguishes Destruction from END.[9] Destruction breaks down matter and structures so they can participate in Creation's recycling; END represents a more absolute terminal erasure. Kassandra's natural personification therefore concerns Destruction rather than the deeper terminal function associated with END.

#### Office-Dependent Characteristics
Not every capability displayed by one Primordial is automatically shared by the others. [[The Universal Tree]]'s awareness through all [[World Trees]] belongs to Life's distributed network; Soul judgment belongs to the Judges; universal expansion belongs to Infinity; Death's terminal function belongs to Death; Balance's regulatory actions belong to Limbo's Primordial. Their common ontology allows them to interact on similar conceptual levels, but their individual powers remain shaped by the Aspect and Authority they administer.
Likewise, unrestricted omniscience, omnipotence and omnipresence should not be assigned to every specialized Primordial merely because complete [[Primordial Chaos]] is described that way within its own Universe.[4] The Creator embodies the complete cosmological realm; the subordinate Primal Forces embody individual fundamental components within it.
