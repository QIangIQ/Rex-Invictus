Primordial Energy, also called Primal Energy, is a fundamental, exceptionally high-order form of Energy originating from the native structure of Creation and associated principally with the Primordial Beings. It is the Energy produced by [[Primordial Chaos]] and employed by Primordial existences, possessing a quality that places it above the ordinary Positive and Negative Energies and the individual Divine Energies of Gods in the native Universal hierarchy. Unlike conventional Elemental, Magical or [[Divine Energy]], Primordial Energy is not defined by one particular supernatural phenomenon. It is an extraordinarily versatile form of power capable of supporting creation-related processes, accelerating development, maintaining supernatural structures, interacting with Concepts and sustaining effects that ordinary Energy cannot easily reproduce.[1][2][3]
Its association with Primordial Beings does not mean that its users must themselves occupy the position of a native Primordial Entity. Jeanne Elderblood, Guardian of [[The Universal Tree]], possessed Primordial Energy through her extraordinary spiritual nature and connection to one of the Universe's fundamental administrators. Victor Elderblood subsequently learned to reproduce it through his control over Attributeless Energy, while Melida Charlotte Elderblood and Isabelle Elderblood demonstrated exceptional natural compatibility with the Energy. Their existence established that Primordial Energy could be accessed outside the original group of Primordial regulators, although such users remained extraordinarily rare.[4][5][6][7]
The most important distinction involving Primordial Energy was clarified during Victor's explanation of Creation to his daughters. Although Primordial Energy and Attributeless Energy are so closely related that individuals capable of manipulating Energy at its fundamental level may consider their differences almost insignificant, they perform distinguishable functions. Attributeless Energy is the propertyless foundation from which Creation is formed, whereas Primal Energy is the attributed power that supports the operation of what exists. Victor summarized their functional relationship by explaining that Attributeless Energy brings things into existence, while Primal Energy makes those things function. Attributeless Energy therefore occupies the more fundamental position, although both are essential to understanding the workings of Creation.[3]

### Nature

#### Origin in Primordial Chaos
The origin of Primordial Energy lies in the fundamental structure established by [[Primordial Chaos]], the Being responsible for the original Creation of the native Universe. During the confrontation between [[Shiva]] and Nur, the narration explained that Primordial Energy was produced by Primordial Chaos and used by Primordial Beings. It occupied the first position in the Universal Rule of Energy known at that point, above the Positive and Negative Energies associated with the great duality and above the individual Concepts employed by Gods.[1]
This description establishes Primordial Energy as an extraordinarily fundamental power, but it does not mean that it was the undifferentiated substance from which all other Energy originated. Later discoveries demonstrated that an underlying state exists before Energy acquires any distinguishing characteristics. That state is Attributeless Energy, which [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] ultimately identified as the original medium used by the Creator to form the Universe.[5][6]
The distinction was made especially clear in Chapter 1185. Victor explained that the Creator employed Attributeless Energy to shape everything that existed, and that referring to this original power as Primordial Energy was understandable in a broad sense because it was the Energy involved in the beginning of Creation. However, when distinguishing the two technically, Attributeless Energy lacks attributes while Primordial Energy possesses them. Their difference concerns the state and function of the Energy rather than two completely unrelated substances.[3]

#### The Energy That Makes Creation Function
Primordial Energy's most important cosmological function is associated with the operation of Creation.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] described Attributeless Energy as the medium through which existence is brought forth, while Primal Energy supports the operation of what has been established. Under this explanation, the distinction resembles the difference between forming a structure and supplying the power necessary for that structure to function according to its intended nature.[3]
This does not establish that every individual object, organism or supernatural phenomenon must contain a separately identifiable reserve of Primordial Energy. Instead, the explanation concerns the fundamental relationship between the Energy used to construct Creation and the Energy associated with its functioning.
The practical significance becomes apparent when considering the applications demonstrated by Jeanne. Her Primordial Energy could accelerate planetary development and isolate an environment while its internal supernatural structure was being established. Later, Victor identified Primal Energy as a possible means of sustaining a contradiction that Reality would otherwise attempt to resolve. Both applications concern maintaining, supporting or directing the operation of supernatural processes rather than merely producing an elemental attack.[2][10]

#### Attributed Rather Than Propertyless
Primordial Energy possesses identifiable characteristics despite its extraordinary versatility.
This was demonstrated directly in Chapter 1107, when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] created a sphere of pure Primordial Energy by assigning the appropriate properties to his Attributeless Energy. He was able to accomplish this because he had observed Jeanne, his daughter and genuine Primordial Beings using the Energy, giving him sufficient knowledge of the characteristics that needed to be reproduced.[6]
The demonstration establishes two important facts. First, Primordial Energy can be reproduced through sufficiently advanced Energy manipulation rather than existing as a fundamentally inaccessible substance. Second, the ability to reproduce it requires understanding its characteristics; merely possessing large quantities of ordinary Energy does not establish the ability to generate Primordial Energy.
This also clarifies an important difference between Primordial Energy and Attributeless Energy. Attributeless Energy can receive different attributes and become distinct forms of Energy, including Primordial Energy. The ability to freely assign arbitrary characteristics is therefore most directly established as a property of Attributeless Energy and its manipulation, rather than an automatic ability of every Primordial Energy user.[6][3]

#### Energy Quality and Metaphysical Superiority
Primordial Energy possesses sufficient quality to oppose certain supernatural effects that would otherwise overwhelm conventional Divine or Elemental powers.
During [[Shiva]]'s battle against Nur, ordinary elemental attacks were easily destroyed by Shiva's Concept of Destruction. The narration nevertheless identified several means of opposing that power, including Creation, sufficiently exceptional Positive and Negative Energy, the Concepts of Beginning and End, and superior Divine Energies such as Primordial Energy.[1]
The important distinction is that Destruction's effectiveness against ordinary elemental manifestations did not guarantee identical effectiveness against all higher-order powers. Primordial Energy possessed the quality necessary to interact with the Concept on a different level.
However, the passage does not establish that every quantity of Primordial Energy automatically overcomes every possible Concept, nor does it mean that a weak user necessarily defeats a stronger conceptual opponent merely by possessing the Energy. The demonstrated distinction concerns the superior nature of the Energy and its compatibility with exceptionally powerful conceptual phenomena.

#### Relationship with Primordial Beings
Primordial Beings possess a fundamental relationship with the Energy generated by [[Primordial Chaos]]. The Universal Rule of Energy described in Chapter 881 characterized Primordial Energy as an Energy used by these Beings, distinguishing it from the Positive and Negative Energies associated with [[World Trees]] and the Divine Energies associated with Gods.[1]
Nevertheless, later examples demonstrated that Primordial Energy was not absolutely restricted to individuals holding a formal office among the Regulators. Jeanne possessed it as the Guardian of the Universal Tree, while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] learned to reproduce it without becoming a native Primordial Regulator. Melida and Isabelle demonstrated further exceptional forms of access.[4][6][7]
The distinction is therefore between an Energy's cosmological classification and the ontological status of an individual capable of using it. A Being may possess Primordial Energy without occupying the office of Death, Infinity, Destiny or another fundamental regulator.

The hierarchy began with Primordial Energy, followed by Positive and Negative Energy, and then the Divine Concepts of Gods, among which Beginning and End occupied exceptionally important positions.[1]

#### The Discovery of a More Fundamental State
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s investigation into the origin of Energy revealed that the hierarchy described by [[Shiva]] did not include the propertyless Energy preceding all differentiated forms.
During [[Melida Charlotte Elderblood]]'s rebirth, Victor observed her extraordinary ability to manipulate different forms of Energy. Unlike his previous methods of converting or modifying already existing Energies, Melida could directly transform Attributeless Energy into whatever characterized form she needed. Victor recognized that this underlying state represented the point from which the differentiated forms of Energy originated, including Primordial Energy itself.[5]
His later demonstration in Chapter 1107 confirmed that understanding. By producing Primordial Energy from Attributeless Energy, he established a direct relationship between the original propertyless state and the higher-order attributed Energy.[6]

Attributeless Energy is the state of Energy before any specific characteristics have been assigned.
Ordinary supernatural beings usually encounter Energy after it has acquired recognizable properties. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] illustrated this relationship to his daughters by creating different spheres of Energy around a central representation of Attributeless Energy.[3]
The central Energy was the propertyless foundation from which the surrounding characterized forms could be produced. Victor demonstrated the process by drawing Energy from that foundation and assigning it the characteristics of Divine Energy. The demonstration explained why distinct forms of Energy could share a common origin despite possessing different functions and properties.

#### Why the Difference Can Become Negligible
Although Primordial Energy and Attributeless Energy are distinguishable, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explained that the difference becomes comparatively insignificant to individuals who can control Energy at its fundamental level.
For someone such as Victor or Melida, who can manipulate Energy before and after characteristics are assigned, the distinction between the two does not represent an insurmountable division. Their mastery allows them to interact with Energy at the underlying level rather than being restricted to a small number of predetermined manifestations.[3]
This does not mean the two forms are literally identical in every application. Victor continued distinguishing their states and functions, specifically identifying the importance of Attributeless Energy as the foundation used to construct Creation.

#### Artificial Production of Primordial Energy
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] demonstrated artificial production of Primordial Energy in Chapter 1107 while instructing [[Scathach]] in the manipulation of Attributeless Energy.
After Scathach attempted to reverse the characteristics of her Ice and experienced a dangerous failure, Victor explained that propertyless Energy could be assigned other characteristics. He then produced a sphere of pure Primordial Energy, demonstrating that even this exceptionally high-order power could be reconstructed through sufficient understanding and control.[6]
The process was possible because Victor had closely observed several individuals who already possessed the Energy, allowing him to identify and reproduce its necessary properties. His accomplishment should therefore be understood as advanced Energy manipulation rather than evidence that every supernatural being can easily create Primordial Energy.

### Relationship with Other Energies

#### Positive and Negative Energy
Positive and Negative Energy represent the great duality of the native Universe. The Universal Rule of Energy described in Chapter 881 places them beneath Primordial Energy and above the ordinary Concepts of Gods.[1]
They are not merely weaker elemental variations of Primordial Energy. Positive and Negative Energy possess their own cosmic significance because they encompass the foundations of enormous families of Concepts and participate in the balance of Creation.
An individual possessing sufficient quantities of these Energies can oppose effects that ordinary Divine or Elemental power cannot resist. [[Shiva]]'s analysis of Destruction expressly recognized extraordinarily large quantities of Positive or Negative Energy as potential counters to the effects of his Concept.[1]

#### Divine Energy
[[Divine Energy]] is associated with the powers of Gods and the Concepts they embody. Although certain Divine Energies possess extraordinary destructive, creative or conceptual capabilities, ordinary Godhood does not automatically grant access to Primordial Energy.
Similarly, Primordial Energy does not automatically give a user every Divinity belonging to a native Primordial Entity. The possession of a particular Concept or governing office remains distinct from the ability to manipulate a high-order Energy.
Jeanne's access through her Guardian nature and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ability to reproduce Primordial Energy provide examples of individuals whose manipulation of the Energy does not depend upon possessing every corresponding Primordial Authority.[4][6]

#### Eldritch Energy
[[Eldritch Energy]] originates from outside the native Universe and possesses a fundamentally foreign nature.
During his explanation in Chapter 1185, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] described it as comparable in principle to the Attributeless Energy of another Universe. Because its origin and governing logic are foreign to native Creation, it can distort the environment according to the rules of the Reality from which it originates.[3]
This establishes an important difference between Eldritch Energy and Primordial Energy. Primordial Energy belongs to the native cosmic framework and is associated with its functioning, whereas Eldritch Energy introduces influences originating beyond that framework.
Neither should automatically be treated as merely an elemental variation of the other.

### Properties and Applications
The following abilities are associated with demonstrated uses of Primordial Energy or clearly identified applications proposed for it. The effects depend upon the user's skill, quantity of Energy, compatibility and the supernatural process being supported.

#### Energy Manipulation
Primordial Energy can be controlled, directed and incorporated into supernatural techniques by individuals possessing sufficient affinity or mastery.
Jeanne demonstrated direct manipulation of the Energy through environmental engineering, protection and concealment. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] reproduced it by assigning the relevant characteristics to Attributeless Energy, while Melida displayed the ability to absorb and manipulate exceptional forms of Energy from the moment of her rebirth.[2][5][6]

#### Energy Projection
Primordial Energy can be projected offensively or defensively.
During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s assessment of Velnorah and the exceptional individuals accompanying him, he recognized that Jeanne's use of Primordial Energy provided sufficient offensive potential to threaten extraordinarily powerful opponents. Her Energy was one of the reasons he considered her among the few individuals present capable of confronting Velnorah.[11]

#### Defensive Energy Projection
Jeanne demonstrated defensive use of her Energy during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s confrontation with Ra. When Victor's emotional instability began producing an increasingly dangerous supernatural manifestation, Jeanne positioned herself between him and the surrounding group and spread a colorless protective force around them.[11]
The barrier allowed those nearby to withstand the dangerous environment while Victor's power continued escalating. Velnorah immediately recognized the extraordinary nature of Jeanne's Energy, reinforcing its exceptional quality.[11]

#### Process Acceleration
Primordial Energy can dramatically accelerate ongoing supernatural and environmental processes.
During the construction of Dragons Nest, Jeanne supplied Primordial Energy to an artificially forming planet. Her contribution accelerated the processes required for the world's development and helped establish the conditions necessary for a functioning planetary environment.[2]
This application is especially important because it demonstrates that Primordial Energy is not restricted to combat. It can support large-scale engineering and development by increasing the rate at which an established process advances.
However, the chapter does not establish that a user can accelerate absolutely any process without considering the characteristics of the target, the available Energy or the user's understanding.

#### Environmental Isolation
Primordial Energy can isolate a developing environment while supernatural processes are taking place.
During the creation of Dragon's Nest, Jeanne's Energy simultaneously accelerated planetary development and isolated the environment being manipulated. This was particularly important because the developing world required the construction of Energy pathways and environmental systems capable of supporting life.[2]
The demonstrated effect differs from ordinary physical shielding in that the isolation functioned as part of the world-building process rather than simply blocking an enemy's attack.

#### Planetary Development
Jeanne's participation in the creation of Dragon's Nest provides the clearest demonstration of Primordial Energy in large-scale environmental engineering.
[[Roxanne Elderblood]] supplied Negative Energy to enrich the developing planet and establish conditions favorable to life, while Jeanne's Primordial Energy accelerated the process and isolated the developing environment. Together, their powers reproduced processes that a World Tree would ordinarily perform gradually.[2]

#### Energy Reinforcement
Primordial Energy can reinforce supernatural processes and provide additional power to an ongoing operation.
Jeanne demonstrated the capacity to support other individuals while performing complex tasks, including stabilizing Natalia during the construction of Dragon's Nest. However, the chapter does not explicitly establish that every form of Energy Jeanne transferred during that sequence consisted exclusively of Primordial Energy, so these applications should be understood within the broader context of her powers.[2]
The later restoration of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s reserves through Moira's influence provides a separate example of Primal Energy being transferred into another Being, rather than merely projected outward as an attack.[12]

#### Energy Absorption
Primordial Energy can be absorbed by individuals possessing exceptional Energy-related abilities.
During [[Melida Charlotte Elderblood]]'s rebirth, she instinctively absorbed multiple forms of supernatural Energy from her surroundings. Jeanne was particularly surprised when her own Primordial Energy was drawn toward Melida, despite the latter having only recently been reborn.[5]
The amount absorbed was not especially large by Jeanne's standards, but the feat demonstrated Melida's extraordinary compatibility with a form of power ordinarily inaccessible to most supernatural beings.
Jeanne also observed that her own daughter had not demonstrated comparable control immediately at birth, requiring additional development before gaining similar capability.[5]

#### Presence Concealment
Primordial Energy can conceal its user's presence from supernatural perception.
Jeanne demonstrated this ability by remaining undetected during an encounter involving Naty. After revealing herself, she explained that Primordial Energy was exceptionally versatile and could be employed to suppress her detectable presence.[13]
She further explained that genuine Primordial Beings employed the same general principle more effectively than she could. Her concealment therefore demonstrated a real application of Primordial Energy without establishing that Jeanne possessed absolute undetectability against every conceivable form of observation.

#### Supernatural Perception Suppression
Jeanne's concealment was not simply ordinary visual invisibility. She remained present while supernatural observers failed to detect her, demonstrating that the Energy could interfere with or suppress the signatures through which other beings perceived her presence.[13]
The exact method was not described as the erasure of her existence. It is more accurately classified as concealment from supernatural senses through advanced Energy manipulation.

#### Conceptual Energy Interaction
Primordial Energy can interact with powers operating through high-order Concepts.
The confrontation between [[Shiva]] and Nur established that Primordial Energy belonged among the powers capable of opposing Destruction, despite ordinary elemental attacks being erased by the Concept. This was one of the earliest explicit demonstrations of the Energy's exceptional position within the native supernatural hierarchy.[1]
Its capacity to oppose a specific conceptual manifestation does not amount to universal immunity or automatic negation of every Concept. The strength and nature of the opposing power remain important.

#### Energy Attribute Reproduction
Primordial Energy possesses identifiable characteristics that can be reproduced through advanced manipulation of Attributeless Energy.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] demonstrated this by producing pure Primordial Energy after obtaining sufficient knowledge of its properties from observing established users. The resulting sphere confirmed that Primordial Energy could be generated through manipulation of the original propertyless Energy.[6]
This should not be confused with granting every ordinary user of Primordial Energy unrestricted access to the entire spectrum of differentiated Energies. The demonstrated ability belongs to the underlying manipulation of Attributeless Energy.

#### Paradox Stabilization
One of the most significant proposed applications of Primal Energy emerged during the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s research into an advanced paradox-based weapon.
The project originated when [[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]] introduced an idea inspired by the thought experiment commonly known as Schrödinger's cat. Her proposal led [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]] and the Empire's researchers to consider creating a contradiction in which two normally incompatible states could coexist simultaneously.[10]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] subsequently identified three principal components required for such a weapon.

This application is especially consistent with the later explanation that Primal Energy supports the functioning of Creation. A contradiction cannot normally remain stable within the native framework, so the proposed weapon requires a high-order Energy source capable of maintaining an otherwise prohibited process.[10][3]
However, Chapter 1183 presents this as a theoretical design and developmental proposal rather than a completed and successfully demonstrated weapon. Its proposed capabilities should not be indexed as an already proven unrestricted ability to negate every Law or Concept.

#### Restoration of Energy Reserves
During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s period of extreme exhaustion following the enormous temporal and Imperial development rituals, [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]] inadvertently caused Primal Energy to enter his body.
Victor had depleted an extraordinary portion of his available power while maintaining and expanding the structures supporting the Empire. When Moira became concerned for her father's condition, her desire for his recovery produced a supernatural response from Reality.[12]
Anna and [[Tyche]] observed Primal Energy entering Victor, while Victor himself recognized that his reserves were recovering despite his ordinary defensive mechanisms being unable to prevent the intervention. The influx restored approximately twenty percent of his Energy reserves, a substantial improvement under the circumstances.[12]
This incident is important because the Energy did not originate from a conventional attack or deliberate technique performed by Moira. Her extraordinary relationship with Luck caused Reality to supply the Energy necessary to fulfill her desire.
The event therefore demonstrates Primal Energy being supplied by Reality in response to a conceptual influence, rather than proving that Moira possesses the same independent Energy manipulation as Jeanne, Isabelle or Melida.

### Primordial Energy and Primordial Existence

#### Energy and State of Existence
Possessing Primordial Energy and becoming a Primordial Entity are not identical accomplishments.
A native Primordial Entity is fundamentally integrated with the Concept or function it represents. Rather than merely employing a Divine Concept as an ordinary God would, such an existence embodies a fundamental aspect of the Universe and performs a corresponding role within its structure.[3]
Primordial Energy, by contrast, is a form of Energy that can be manipulated, reproduced, absorbed or employed by suitably exceptional Beings.

#### The Office of a Primordial Being
Chapter 1185 clarified that a true Primordial Being becomes the incarnation of its Concept rather than remaining merely a God capable of invoking it.
Once a Being occupies the corresponding position within the native Universal system, that existence becomes a fundamental component of Reality. Gods associated with the same Concept consequently draw upon a structure in which the Primordial Being serves as its ultimate incarnation and governing authority.[3]
This distinction is crucial when evaluating the abilities of individuals such as Jeanne, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] or Isabelle. Their capacity to employ Primordial Energy does not automatically mean they have occupied the formal cosmic position of a native Primordial Regulator.

#### The Distinction Between Primal Force and Primal Energy
The late Imperial narrative also uses expressions such as Primal Force and Primordial Being when discussing the fundamental entities responsible for governing Reality.
These terms should not automatically be treated as alternate names for Primordial Energy.
A Primal Force in discussions concerning Destiny, Death or Destruction refers to the Being or governing existence associated with a fundamental cosmic function. Primal Energy refers to the Energy itself.[14][3]
This distinction is particularly important in the case of Moira. Her extraordinary suitability to become a Primal Force associated with Destiny does not mean that her passive influence over Luck is identical to consciously controlling every function of Primordial Energy.

### Control and Compatibility

#### The Importance of the User's Vessel
The ability to manipulate Primordial Energy is influenced by the nature of the user's body and Soul.
Following Jeanne's transformation into a True Dragon, she discovered that controlling her Primordial Energy had become substantially easier. Her new Draconic vessel also allowed her to perceive aspects of Souls that she previously could not observe with the same clarity.[8]
Jeanne attributed part of this improvement to the extraordinary relationship between Dragon physiology and Creation. Her transformation did not create her Primordial Energy from nothing; rather, it improved the compatibility of her physical vessel with the extraordinary Energy already associated with her spiritual existence.[8]
This distinction is especially important because Jeanne's Soul was far more substantial than an ordinary Dragon Soul, while her previous physical bodies had limited how completely she could express her power.

#### Natural Affinity
Natural affinity can drastically influence the difficulty of controlling Primordial Energy.
[[Melida Charlotte Elderblood]] demonstrated an extraordinary instinctive understanding of Energy from the moment of her rebirth. She could absorb Primordial Energy and manipulate Attributeless Energy with a proficiency that surprised even Jeanne.[5]
By the late Imperial era, this affinity had developed into an exceptional natural mastery of Primal Energy. Melida's knowledge was so instinctive that other talented individuals considered comparison with her unreasonable.[9]

#### Understanding and Technical Mastery
Natural affinity is not the only route to manipulating Primordial Energy.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] acquired the ability to reproduce it through understanding the properties of Energy itself. His accomplishment relied upon extensive observation, accumulated knowledge, precise Energy control and his extraordinary ability to interact with Attributeless Energy.[6]
[[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]] provides an important contrasting example. Despite her extraordinary intelligence, research abilities and Dragon Goddess physiology, manipulating Primal Energy remained difficult and exhausting for her. During the late Imperial era, she explicitly regarded Melida's instinctive mastery as an unfair comparison because Melida possessed knowledge and compatibility that Albedo was still attempting to develop through study.[9]
This demonstrates that possessing an exceptionally powerful body does not automatically grant complete mastery over every form of Energy.

#### Control of Attributeless Energy
Manipulation of Primordial Energy becomes considerably more flexible when the user understands Attributeless Energy.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] demonstrated that the properties of Primordial Energy could be reproduced directly from the original propertyless state. Melida displayed a similarly extraordinary relationship with this underlying Energy, manipulating its characteristics with an ease that ordinary users of differentiated supernatural Energies could not reproduce.[5][6]
However, Chapter 1186 clarified that Victor himself did not claim to possess complete understanding of Attributeless Energy. Although he could manipulate it effectively, his broader explanations of Creation remained partly based upon deduction and experimentation rather than omniscient knowledge.[15]
Therefore, even the most advanced demonstrated users should not be assumed to possess unrestricted knowledge of every possible consequence arising from the Energy.

### Inheritance and Exceptional Users

#### Jeanne's Original Explanation
Chapter 620 established an important early restriction upon the inheritance of Primordial Energy.
Jeanne explained that her power as the Guardian of the Universal Tree was bound directly to her Soul. As a Higher Spirit who had obtained different physical bodies, she possessed a spiritual existence that remained distinct from the ordinary hereditary characteristics of the race represented by her current vessel.[4]
When Ruby asked whether Jeanne's children could inherit the exceptional Energy associated with her position, Jeanne explained that ordinary descendants would inherit the characteristics of her physical race rather than the powers bound exclusively to her spiritual nature.
She additionally stated that the original Primordial Beings possessed comparable restrictions preventing their governing powers from passing indiscriminately to descendants. These limitations existed to protect the balance of Creation.[4]

#### The Later Elderblood Exceptions
The later existence of Isabelle and Melida demonstrated that the earlier inheritance explanation could not be applied as an absolute restriction upon every possible descendant or individual incorporated into an extraordinary supernatural lineage.
Isabelle, daughter of Jeanne and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], was explicitly described as possessing Primal Energy from birth. Melida, who was incorporated into Victor's lineage through an extraordinary rebirth, demonstrated instinctive control over Energy that ordinary supernatural beings required extensive training to manipulate.[5][7][16]
These developments do not necessarily mean that Jeanne's original explanation was false regarding ordinary reproduction through her former physical vessel. They establish that Victor's exceptional lineage, the conditions surrounding his descendants and the extraordinary transformations of his family produced cases not adequately covered by the earlier generalization.
The exact hereditary or metaphysical mechanism through which Isabelle obtained Primal Energy from birth has not been fully explained.

#### The Importance of Spiritual Compatibility
Jeanne's case demonstrates that Primordial Energy can be connected directly to the user's Soul and spiritual identity.
Her original connection to the Universal Tree existed independently of the physical race she inhabited, while her later Dragon body improved the compatibility between her vessel and her underlying power.[4][8]
Melida represents another unusual form of compatibility, possessing an extraordinary capacity to interact with multiple Energies through her reborn existence.
These examples establish that spiritual nature, racial physiology and individual understanding can all influence the manipulation of Primordial Energy, but the precise contributions differ between users.

#### Jeanne Elderblood
Jeanne is among the earliest explicitly demonstrated users of Primordial Energy and remains one of its most significant representatives.
Her relationship with the Energy originates from her spiritual existence as the Guardian of the Universal Tree. This connection grants her access to an exceptionally pure form of power that even knowledgeable Gods recognize as extraordinary.[4]
She demonstrated the Energy's versatility through planetary development, supernatural shielding, concealment and the ability to apply high-order power in combat. Her transformation into a True Dragon substantially improved her control, allowing her to interact more efficiently with an Energy already associated with her Soul.[2][8][11][13]

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] initially lacked the specialized access to Primordial Energy naturally possessed by Jeanne. His later understanding of Attributeless Energy allowed him to overcome that distinction.
After studying the characteristics of multiple Energies and observing Primordial users, Victor demonstrated the artificial creation of pure Primordial Energy. Rather than inheriting it through a Primordial office, he reproduced its properties by manipulating Energy at a more fundamental level.[6]
His mastery also enabled him to consider Primal Energy as a component of increasingly advanced Imperial technology, including the proposed paradox-stabilization mechanism developed in Chapter 1183.[10]

#### Melida Charlotte Elderblood
Melida possesses an extraordinary natural affinity for Energy and an instinctive understanding of its fundamental characteristics.
During her rebirth, she absorbed multiple forms of supernatural power, including Jeanne's Primordial Energy. Her ability to manipulate Attributeless Energy and assign characteristics to it provided one of the inspirations that allowed [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to develop a deeper understanding of the origin of differentiated Energies.[5]
Later, Melida was described as possessing instinctive knowledge of Primal Energy, which continued improving naturally. Her proficiency contrasted sharply with Albedo's difficulty in attempting similar manipulation.[9]

#### Isabelle Elderblood
Isabelle is the daughter of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and [[Jeanne Elderblood]] and possesses an extraordinary relationship with Energy.
Chapter 1160 identifies her Divine Concept as Energy itself, granting her power associated with the mastery of energetic phenomena. She is also explicitly described as controlling Primal Energy, distinguishing her from ordinary Gods whose abilities operate through narrower forms of characterized power.[7]
Chapter 1171 further establishes that Isabelle possessed Primal Energy from birth. Her natural compatibility was considered an exceptional anomaly even among Victor's extraordinary descendants.[16]
Her abilities should nevertheless be distinguished from automatic possession of every other fundamental Concept. Her relationship with Energy does not, by itself, establish that she occupies every Primordial office in the Universe.

#### Albedo Moriarty Elderblood
Albedo demonstrated the difficulty of mastering Primal Energy through conventional research and training.
Despite her formidable knowledge, Dragon Goddess physiology and exceptional magical ability, she found the Energy exhausting to manipulate. Her struggle contrasted with Melida's instinctive mastery and demonstrated that advanced intelligence and enormous power reserves did not automatically provide complete control over Primal Energy.[9]
Albedo's inclusion therefore represents a user developing the relevant knowledge and manipulation rather than an individual already established as possessing mastery comparable to Melida.

### Additional Demonstrations and Distinctions

#### Shiva's Recognition of Primordial Energy
During the confrontation involving Nur, [[Shiva]] recognized the ability of Primordial Energy to oppose Destruction and was disturbed when he perceived traces of it appearing through Jeanne's actions.
The narration clarified that Jeanne was not deliberately releasing the Energy at full power; portions of it were leaking naturally because of her emotional state.[1]
This demonstrates that extraordinary Energy can become perceptible through a user's uncontrolled output, even when the user is not consciously performing a specialized Primordial technique.

#### Scathach's Primal Force
During [[Scathach]]'s confrontation with Albedo in Chapter 1172, the narration described one of her physical attacks as being delivered through Primal Force. Her overwhelming martial control allowed her to shatter the surrounding Reality through direct physical combat while overwhelming Albedo's elaborate magical techniques.[17]
However, the phrase alone does not establish that Scathach had acquired the same distinct Primordial Energy mastery as Jeanne, Melida or Isabelle. The scene emphasizes the extraordinary nature of Scathach's martial power and the superiority of her fundamentals. It should not be used by itself as definitive evidence that she can freely generate or manipulate pure Primordial Energy.

#### Victor's Confrontation with the Regulators
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] confronted the Primordial Beings over the threatened interference with Moira, he released an enormous quantity of formless power from his body. The narration described it as pure Primal power and associated it with the Energy from which everything was formed.[14]
The Energy subsequently acquired the crimson characteristics of Victor's influence over Negativity, allowing him to threaten catastrophic disruption of the native Universal structure.
Because the description emphasizes formless Energy and its relationship with the origin of Creation, this event must be interpreted alongside the distinction between Primordial Energy and Attributeless Energy established in Chapters 1107 and 1185. It is not sufficient evidence to treat the entire manifestation as ordinary Primordial Energy alone.[6][14][3]

#### Moira's Reality-Mediated Energy Transfer
Moira's accidental restoration of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] in Chapter 1184 represents one of the most unusual late applications associated with Primal Energy.
Her desire for her father's recovery caused Reality to supply him with Energy directly, bypassing the defenses that would ordinarily prevent outside influences from entering his body. Approximately twenty percent of his reserves were restored.[12]
The incident confirms that Primal Energy can be involved in a conceptual process initiated by a Being who does not necessarily control the Energy directly. The source and mechanism of the transfer were connected to Moira's extraordinary influence over Luck and Reality.

- Melida absorbed Jeanne's Primordial Energy immediately following her rebirth, despite ordinary newborns being incapable of comparable manipulation.[5]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s creation of pure Primordial Energy in Chapter 1107 demonstrated that even this high-order power could be reproduced by assigning appropriate properties to Attributeless Energy.[6]
- Isabelle's Divine Concept is Energy itself, making her relationship with Primal Energy fundamentally different from that of ordinary Gods whose powers are associated with narrower Divine Concepts.[7]
- Albedo's difficulty handling Primal Energy demonstrates that extraordinary magical knowledge, Dragon physiology and vast supernatural power do not automatically provide instinctive mastery.[9]
- The proposed paradox weapon would use Primal Energy to maintain the simultaneous existence of contradictory states against Reality's natural tendency to resolve them.[10]
- Moira's desire for Victor's recovery caused Reality to supply him with Primal Energy, restoring approximately twenty percent of his reserves without an ordinary Energy-transfer technique.[12]

- Victor acknowledged that the distinction between the two becomes almost negligible for Beings capable of manipulating Energy at its most fundamental level, although Attributeless Energy remains the more fundamental state.[3]
