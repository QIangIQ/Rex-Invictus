**[[Vampire Count Form]]** is a figure of Rex Invictus lore. (Lore adapted from *My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires*.)

### Powers & Abilities
Returns a Vampire closer to their ancient racial state while greatly amplifying their Bloodline, racial abilities, physical characteristics and power output

### Users
Vampires
The Vampire Count Form, also known as the Vampire Count Transformation, is an advanced racial transformation available to sufficiently developed Vampires, particularly the Noble Vampires descended from [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]]' sireline. Rather than being merely a conventional power-enhancing technique, the transformation progressively allows a Vampire to access a state closer to the original nature of their species. It greatly increases the Vampire's physical attributes, racial abilities, Bloodline powers, supernatural output and control, while higher stages increasingly alter the user's body to better accommodate their power.[1]
Despite its name, possession of the Vampire Count Form is not the same thing as holding the political title of Vampire Count. [[Vampire Counts]] are nobles personally recognized as Pillars of [[Nightingale]], whereas the Count Transformation is a biological and supernatural state that can be developed independently of political rank. [[Victor Elderblood]], for example, possessed incomplete Count transformations before becoming an official Vampire Count and was later granted the title while still lacking complete mastery of the transformation.[1][2] Conversely, Vampires who do not hold the position of Count can also develop the transformation.
For much of modern Vampire history, the transformation was understood simply as an extraordinary amplification possessed by the most talented Noble Vampires. [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]], however, later revealed that this interpretation was incomplete. According to Vlad, Count transformations are the remnants of an ancient racial state used by Vampires thousands of years before the current generation of Gods existed. A Vampire who completely masters the modern Count Form has not yet fully recovered the ancient race's original power; instead, they have moved one step closer to the lost origins of Vampires.[3]

### Nature
The Vampire Count Transformation develops alongside a Vampire's understanding and control of their own Bloodline. It cannot be mastered merely by accumulating large quantities of raw Energy. The Vampire must progressively understand the nature of their inherited ability, improve their control over its output and gain greater understanding of their own racial nature. Because of this, development of the Count Form normally requires centuries of training and experience.[4]
Age nevertheless remains only a general indicator rather than an absolute requirement. Most Vampires require centuries before their Bloodline, body and control become developed enough to sustain the transformation, while extraordinary individuals can awaken it far earlier. [[Victor Elderblood]] awakened a Count transformation only months after becoming a Vampire, while his personal transformation was so abnormal that even [[Scathach]] regarded him as a violation of conventional Vampire development.[5][1]
The transformation is particularly associated with Noble Vampire Bloodlines because their powers originate from inherited racial characteristics. A normal transformation reflects the user's own Bloodline and therefore differs considerably between Clans. The [[Snow Clan]] produces forms centered around Fire, the [[Scarlett Clan]] around Ice, the [[Fulger Clan]] around Lightning, while more unusual Bloodlines such as [[Adrasteia Clan]] can produce transformations heavily influenced by their Monster characteristics.

### Ancient Vampire origin
Early explanations treated the Complete Vampire Count Form as the supposed "true nature" of ancient Vampires, although even [[Scathach]] noted that modern Vampires were uncertain whether the ancient stories surrounding the transformation were completely accurate.[1] The truth was later revealed by Vlad during his battle against Yama.
According to Vlad, the Count Transformation is not fundamentally a technique invented by modern Vampire society. It is the result of talented Vampires gradually recovering characteristics belonging to their species' ancient form. The original Vampires existed before many of the Gods currently active around Earth and possessed racial powers feared even by the strongest creatures of their era. Those ancient enemies eventually united against the Vampires and crippled their Race, resulting in the weaker modern state from which current Vampires descend.[3]
Vlad consequently distinguished between the Complete Vampire Count Form and the actual True Form of the Ancient Vampires. A Vampire who reaches the complete modern transformation has approached their ancient origin, but has not necessarily completely restored everything the Race once possessed. Vlad's own True Form represented a much more direct recovery of those ancient racial characteristics and their original powers.[3]
This distinction is important because the Count Form should therefore not be treated solely as a temporary numerical multiplier. The increasing physical changes, improved racial abilities and eventual assimilation between the user's body and Bloodline power are manifestations of the Vampire becoming progressively closer to what their Race originally was.

### Development
The transformation has three primary levels.[1] Progress between them depends heavily upon Bloodline control, personal strength and understanding of the user's own nature rather than simply chronological age.[4]

#### Level 1 — Incomplete Transformation
Level 1 is the incomplete awakening of the Vampire Count Form. The user's body becomes covered or surrounded by the physical manifestation of their principal power, their ears sharpen, their body may increase slightly in height and other minor predatory characteristics begin appearing. The greatest improvement at this stage is the user's ability to access and output considerably larger quantities of Bloodline power without immediately exhausting themselves.[1]
For an ordinary Noble Vampire, Level 1 produces approximately a twofold increase in combat power. [[Scathach]] illustrated this by explaining that a Vampire with the normal strength of a 500-year-old adult could temporarily fight at approximately the level expected of a 1,000-year-old Vampire after activating the first transformation.[1]
The exact appearance depends entirely upon the user's Bloodline. Fire users may become surrounded by flames, Lightning users may become covered by electricity, and Vampires possessing stranger racial abilities can undergo correspondingly different transformations.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] represented an exceptional case. His personal Blood Count Form simultaneously joined Blood, Fire, Ice, Water and Lightning because of the abnormal ritual through which he inherited the powers of three Vampire Count Houses. Consequently, his incomplete first-level transformation increased his combat power by approximately three times rather than the conventional twofold increase, allowing his roughly 500-year-old base power at the time to rise into the range of a 1,500-year-old Vampire.[1]

#### Level 2 — Semi-Complete Transformation
Level 2 is the semi-complete Count Transformation. Rather than simply coating the Vampire in their Bloodline ability, the transformation begins physically adapting the user's body toward the nature of that power. The Vampire becomes noticeably more monstrous, their racial characteristics increase, their body becomes stronger and tougher and their reflexes improve substantially.[1][4]
Unlike what might be assumed from the physical development, Level 2 does not simply double the numerical multiplier again. The basic amplification remains approximately 2× for a conventional transformation, but the user's body becomes considerably better at actually utilizing that amplified power. Their strength, speed and toughness increase; their reflexes reach another level; control over their Bloodline becomes considerably greater; and the amount of supernatural power they can output without damaging their own body rises significantly.[4]
Violet demonstrated this development when she advanced her Snow transformation. Her body became considerably more monstrous while the heat and flames surrounding her intensified enough to begin reducing the surrounding environment to ash. Hilda immediately recognized the state as the second Vampire Count Form and was surprised that Violet had become capable of advancing so rapidly.[4]
Level 2 therefore represents more than a larger Energy reserve. It is the stage where the Vampire's physiology begins adapting itself to use the Bloodline's increased output efficiently.

#### Level 3 — Complete Transformation
Level 3, also known as the Complete Vampire Count Form or Perfect Vampire Count Form, is the completed modern transformation. It combines and perfects the principal characteristics developed during the first two levels, massively enhancing the Vampire's physical body, racial powers, supernatural output and control.[1]
Only a small number of Noble Vampires historically succeeded in reaching this stage because it requires extremely advanced mastery of both Bloodline and self. The transformation provides approximately a fourfold increase in the user's strength compared with their ordinary state, while also greatly increasing the amount of Bloodline power they can output. Their physical strength and resistance receive another major improvement, allowing the body to endure techniques and quantities of power that would severely injure or destroy the user in Base Form.[6]
This does not mean every individual statistic is mathematically multiplied by exactly four. The narration specifically establishes the overall strength multiplier while separately emphasizing improved output, physical reinforcement, resistance and supernatural control.[6]
Complete mastery can also fundamentally change how the user's Bloodline interacts with their body. Agnes, for example, becomes completely immune to Fire while using her mastered Level 3 Snow transformation despite the ordinary [[Snow Clan]] still being vulnerable to sufficiently superior Flames in Base Form.[7] Natashia, meanwhile, could use the transformation to safely channel physical and Lightning techniques that would severely damage the body of a less-developed Vampire.[6]

### Elemental assimilation
For Vampire Bloodlines based around elemental powers, mastery of the Count Transformation can progress beyond merely surrounding the body with an element. The user can begin merging their physical existence with their own power, becoming something described as closer to an Elemental Spirit than an ordinary Vampire.[8]
This phenomenon should not be applied universally to every Vampire Bloodline. The effect is possible because Clans such as Scarlett, Fulger and Snow developed elemental manipulation to extraordinary levels. A Bloodline whose ability produces a fundamentally separate object—for example, creating supernatural ropes and controlling them—would not necessarily be capable of physically becoming that power in the same manner.[8]
The transformation therefore adapts to the actual nature of the Bloodline rather than forcing every Vampire into an identical elemental state.

#### Living Element
Natashia demonstrated an especially refined version of elemental assimilation after completely mastering her Count Transformation. When she activated a refined form, her entire body became pure Lightning and she was described as effectively becoming a living element—something comparable to a Spirit possessing Vampire characteristics.[9]
Interestingly, greater mastery did not cause Natashia to become progressively more monstrous. The opposite occurred. Her previously exaggerated monster-like characteristics became less pronounced while her body more naturally embodied her Bloodline. This demonstrates that the grotesque physical changes associated with the middle stages are not necessarily the final expression of Count mastery. A highly refined Vampire can become more natural and aesthetically humanoid even while their actual integration with their power becomes considerably deeper.[9]

### Partial manifestation
Exceptional Vampires can eventually reproduce individual characteristics of their Count Form without undergoing the complete transformation. This application is distinct from the normal three levels and represents an advanced development in Bloodline control.
During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later training, his body began unconsciously using his elemental abilities as though he were already transformed. Ice automatically formed across individual portions of his body to protect him from [[Scathach]]'s attacks, allowing him to reproduce effects that normally required the increased output and assimilation granted by a Vampire Count Form.[6]
After observing Victor, Scathach attempted the same principle and briefly drew upon the Count Form's Ice assimilation without transforming her entire body. Her hand became Ice internally and externally without producing the severe pain normally associated with forcibly freezing one's own body through ordinary Cryokinesis. Scathach immediately recognized the technique as a new path through which completely mastered Count transformations could potentially be integrated into Base Form.[6]
This demonstrates that the ultimate value of the Count Transformation is not merely activating a temporary combat state. Sufficient mastery can teach the Vampire's body how to naturally interact with its Bloodline in ways previously limited to the transformed state.

### Variants and mutations
Because the Vampire Count Form reflects the user's Bloodline and racial nature, the transformation can change dramatically when the Vampire possesses an abnormal physiology, hybrid Bloodline or pre-existing supernatural characteristics.

#### Elemental Count Forms
The most conventional examples belong to [[Nightingale]]'s elemental Count Clans. Their transformations progressively turn the Vampire into a living manifestation of the Clan's element.
- [[Snow Clan]] — Fire: Snow transformations greatly amplify [[Pyrokinesis]] and Fire resistance. Complete mastery can allow a Snow Vampire such as Agnes to effectively become immune to Fire while transformed.[7]
- [[Scarlett Clan]] — Ice: Scarlett transformations greatly increase Cryokinesis, physical reinforcement and the ability to assimilate the body with Ice. [[Scathach]] possessed complete control over her Count Form and could reproduce portions of its elemental properties even outside full transformation.[6]
- [[Fulger Clan]] — Lightning: Fulger transformations dramatically increase [[Electrokinesis]], speed and the body's ability to survive extreme acceleration. Complete mastery can transform the Vampire into something comparable to a Lightning Spirit.[9]

#### Victor's Blood Count Form
[[Victor Elderblood]] developed an unprecedented personal Count Form because his transformation ritual granted him the powers of Snow, Scarlett and Fulger in addition to his own Blood Manipulation. Rather than representing a single inherited Bloodline, his Blood Count Form merged Blood, Fire, Lightning, Ice and Water into the same transformation.[1]
Its first level already granted an approximately threefold amplification rather than the standard twofold increase. The form was nevertheless extremely dangerous during Victor's youth because he lacked sufficient mastery over all of the Bloodlines composing it. [[Scathach]] consequently prohibited him from relying upon it and placed limiters upon him while he learned to properly control his powers.[1]
Victor also possessed separate Snow, Fulger and Scarlett Count transformations inherited from his wives' Bloodlines. These transformations eventually became obsolete when his Vampiric characteristics fused with his Draconic nature and evolved into the physiology of the Blood Dragon Primogenitor.

#### Demonic mutation
[[Morgana Elderblood]] possessed a heavily mutated Count Form because she had previously been a Demon before becoming a Vampire. Rather than manifesting a conventional Noble Vampire transformation, her Demonic characteristics became incorporated into the Count Form. Her skin became deep red, her hands transformed into Demon-like claws, her limbs developed natural armor, her wings darkened and spikes emerged from her body.[10]
When Morgana advanced further into the transformation, her body became increasingly monstrous, developing a larger frame, a powerful tail and additional Demonic characteristics.[11] Morgana therefore demonstrates that Count transformations do not erase a Vampire's other racial traits; sufficiently integrated characteristics can become part of the transformation itself.

#### Monster-Hybrid mutation
Members of the main [[Adrasteia Clan]] Bloodline possess a transformation influenced by the Monster blood introduced through their Clan's unique ritual. Eleanor's form consequently resembles a reptilian or Dragon-like monster rather than a conventional elemental Vampire. Her body becomes covered in dark green scales while horns, wings and a long tail emerge from her body.[12][13]
Eleanor's transformation demonstrates that the Count Form responds to the Vampire's total racial makeup. Her Monster Bloodline, itself descended from a Dragon-like creature, altered what would otherwise have been an ordinary Noble Vampire transformation.

### Power and combat applications
The primary effects of the Vampire Count Transformation can be summarized as an amplification and refinement of abilities the user already possesses rather than the automatic acquisition of completely unrelated powers.
- Bloodline Amplification: The Vampire's inherited Clan ability becomes considerably more powerful and can be used at much higher output.[1]
- Enhanced Racial Abilities: Higher stages enhance the abilities naturally possessed by Vampires rather than only their Bloodline power.[1]
- Enhanced Strength: The user's physical strength increases considerably, especially from Level 2 onward.[4]
- Enhanced Speed: The body becomes capable of substantially greater movement speed and acceleration.[4]
- Enhanced Reflexes: Level 2 explicitly takes the user's reflexes to another level, allowing the Vampire to actually react at the speeds produced by the enhanced body.[4]
- Enhanced Durability and Resistance: The transformed body becomes much tougher and more resistant to the recoil produced by its own abilities.[4][6]
- Enhanced Regeneration: As the transformation strengthens a Vampire's racial physiology, their regenerative abilities likewise become more effective.
- Enhanced Power Output: The user can release far larger quantities of Bloodline power without harming their body or becoming immediately exhausted.[4][6]
- Enhanced Control: Progression through the form is inseparable from improving control over the user's Bloodline. At the highest levels this control can become precise enough to partially assimilate the Vampire with their own power.[8][6]

### Vampire Count-class destructive power
The destructive reputation of [[Vampire Counts]] should be distinguished from the numerical amplification of the transformation itself. Experienced Vampire Counts possess enough supernatural power to casually perform attacks capable of destroying a small country. The exceptional aspect of their power is not merely producing such an attack; other sufficiently powerful beings can reach comparable destructive output. What makes Vampire Counts particularly dangerous is that attacks of that scale consume comparatively little of their reserves, allowing them to continue fighting afterward.[14]
This is why experienced Counts are repeatedly described as walking atomic bombs. Their threat comes from the combination of extreme destructive output, enormous reserves, regeneration, physical strength and the ability to repeatedly perform attacks that would exhaust weaker supernatural beings.[14]
This level of destruction is also not automatically produced merely by unlocking Level 1. The user's Base strength remains extremely important. A weak Vampire receiving a twofold increase remains vastly weaker than an Elder Vampire Count receiving the same proportional enhancement.

### Relation to Vampire age
Age and Count mastery are strongly correlated because older Vampires have normally accumulated vastly greater Energy, experience and Bloodline control. A Vampire is socially regarded as an adult at approximately 500 years of age, and adult Noble Vampires commonly possess enormously more developed racial abilities than younger Vampires.[1]
However, the Count Form is determined by development rather than age alone. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] awakened transformations before even reaching his first year as a Vampire, while several ancient Vampires still lacked the level of Bloodline control demonstrated by the great Countesses. Talent, training, Bloodline quality, personal understanding and accumulated supernatural power all influence progression.
The four original Countess Bloodlines became particularly famous because their leaders pushed their respective abilities to extreme levels over thousands of years, giving them Count transformations far beyond what an ordinary Noble Vampire could easily reproduce.

### Limitations
- Requires Bloodline Mastery: The transformation develops alongside control and understanding of the user's Bloodline. Greater raw Energy alone does not guarantee advancement.[4]
- Requires Physical Adaptation: Higher supernatural output is dangerous if the user's body cannot withstand it. Part of the purpose of Levels 2 and 3 is reinforcing the body sufficiently to safely release greater power.[4]
- Incomplete Forms Can Be Dangerous: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s personal Blood Count Form placed severe strain upon him because he had not mastered the numerous Bloodlines composing it. [[Scathach]] therefore forbade him from casually using it.[1]
- Power Depends on Base Strength: The Count Transformation multiplies and refines the Vampire's existing abilities. It does not make every transformed Vampire equal to a Vampire Countess or Elder Vampire.
- Bloodline Dependent: The appearance and effects of the transformation vary according to the user's actual Bloodline. Elemental assimilation cannot automatically be applied to unrelated abilities.[8]
- Not the Final Ancient State: Even the Complete modern transformation is only a step toward restoring the original racial powers of Ancient Vampires.[3]

### Trivia
- The expression Vampire Count Form predates [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] becoming an actual political Vampire Count, further demonstrating that the transformation and title are separate concepts.
- The first level normally grants approximately a 2× increase, while Victor's abnormal Blood Count Form granted approximately 3×.[1]
- Level 2 retains the ordinary transformation's approximately 2× raw amplification but improves physical abilities, reflexes, control and safe power output rather than simply multiplying the first level a second time.[4]
- The Complete Vampire Count Form provides approximately a 4× increase in strength, alongside much greater supernatural output and physical resistance.[6]
- The increasingly monstrous appearance seen while a Vampire develops the transformation is not necessarily permanent. Natashia became noticeably less monstrous after completely mastering her Count Form because her body began embodying Lightning far more naturally.[9]
- For elemental Vampires, a perfected transformation can make the user resemble an Elemental Spirit possessing Vampire characteristics.[8][9]
- Vlad considers the widespread belief that the transformation is merely a power-up to be fundamentally incorrect. Its deeper function is the recovery of characteristics belonging to the ancient Vampire Race.[3]
- Chapter 670 reveals that the ancestors of the modern Vampires did not originate on Earth, explaining why their oldest racial history predates many of Earth's current Gods.[3]
