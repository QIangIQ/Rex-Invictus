Destiny was one of the original [[Regulators of Existence]] and a completed Primordial Entity responsible for the Destiny, Fate and timeline/future structure of Creation. Unlike the other known Regulators, Destiny was discovered only after having already been erased so completely that even the other Primordial Beings no longer remembered that an additional member of their cosmic order had once existed.[1]
The System eventually discovered residual information showing that the known Primordial order had originally included Destiny alongside Positivity, Negativity, Infinity, [[True Death]], [[The Universal Tree]], [[The Judges of The Abyss]] and The Owner of Limbo. Destiny was responsible for the Destiny of the Universe, the timeline of Creation and the structure through which predetermined futures and branching possibilities were organized.[1]
Destiny's erasure had consequences far beyond the death of an individual. The predetermined future of Creation disappeared, alternate timelines began collapsing and converging toward the Main Timeline, and beings capable of seeing the future were left observing potential futures rather than a Reality already fixed by Creation.[2][1]
Later events reveal that Creation never completely recovered from the loss. Although the Universe remained operational, the System privately considered the future structure incomplete and unstable without a Primordial occupying Destiny's former office. This eventually caused it to seek [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]] as a replacement archetypal figure of Destiny.[3] [[Victor Elderblood]] refused, and as of Chapter 1176 the Primordial office of Destiny remains vacant.[4]

### Physical Appearance
Destiny's canonical appearance is completely unknown.
The Primordial was erased before the character was ever observed directly by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], the System or any currently known living character. Chapter 965 reveals Destiny only through traces and residual information discovered after the erasure.[1]
Consequently, both the humanoid and abstract images currently used within this article are artistic representations rather than confirmed canonical forms.
Like the other completed Primordial Beings, Destiny would have existed fundamentally as a cosmic Conceptual entity rather than being limited to an ordinary biological body, but the exact manner in which Destiny manifested while alive remains unknown.

### Personality
Destiny's personality is unknown.
The erasure was sufficiently complete that the other Primordial Beings lost their ordinary memories of Destiny, leaving no known direct conversation or behavioral record from which a reliable personality profile can be reconstructed.[1]
It is therefore unknown whether Destiny possessed an expressive individual personality comparable to [[The Universal Tree]], a function-dominated mentality similar to [[The Judges of The Abyss]], or something completely different.
Later information concerning Primordial Beings suggests that their personalities can be heavily influenced or constrained by the cosmic offices they occupy. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] concluded that many of the Regulators were created primarily as mechanisms responsible for keeping the Universe functioning, even when they subsequently developed individual egos and personalities.[3]
Whether Destiny possessed similar limitations before being erased is unknown.

### Synopsis

#### Dragon God of Chaos
The circumstances leading to the discovery of Destiny began after [[Victor Elderblood]] completed his ascension as <CHAOS>.
Victor became a Unique Being. While ordinary individuals could possess alternative counterparts spread across different timelines, Victor no longer possessed functioning parallel versions of himself. The Victor occupying the Main Timeline had become the only active expression of his existence.[5]
The System investigated the anomaly and discovered that Creation contained an enormous branching timeline structure. Different choices and possibilities produced alternative histories containing corresponding versions of individuals and even corresponding instances of the System itself.[6]
Victor's alternate selves, however, were disappearing.
The anomaly rapidly became much larger than Victor alone. Entire timelines were being erased on an enormous scale while Reality continuously converged toward a single historical structure: the Main Timeline.[2][1]
While investigating why Creation was behaving this way, the System discovered traces of something that should not have been absent.
There had once been another Primordial.
Destiny.[1]
The discovery was particularly disturbing because Destiny had not simply died. The Primordial had been erased so completely that the System itself had failed to recognize the missing cosmic office, while the other Primordial Beings no longer remembered the lost Regulator.[1]
The residual information identified Destiny as the Primordial responsible for the Destiny of the Universe, the timeline of all Creation and the predetermined future structure that once allowed certain outcomes to be cosmologically defined as things that were supposed to happen.[1]
Destiny's disappearance explained why future perception had changed. Creation no longer possessed the same fixed future that had once existed while Destiny administered it. Future-seers could observe possibilities, but those possibilities were no longer guaranteed to become Reality simply because they appeared within a future vision.[1]
The investigation eventually attracted the direct attention of Akashic. Akashic ordered the System to stop investigating the anomaly and subsequently synchronized the System instances distributed throughout the remaining timelines with the Main Timeline.[1]
The process concluded with the effective disappearance of the former branching timeline structure. Alternative timelines were erased or converged, leaving the Main Timeline as the active historical Reality while alternative futures remained possibilities rather than simultaneously existing realized worlds.[2][1]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
The consequences of Destiny's absence resurfaced long after the original discovery.
By the time [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]] was born, the System itself had changed significantly. The later narrative reveals that when Akashic unified the timelines into the Main Timeline, the System became substantially stronger. What had previously functioned primarily as an administrative structure developed a more pronounced form, ego and [[Will Solace|Will]], while Reality was rewritten so that the existing Primordials perceived the System's altered state as though it had always been normal.[4]
Moira's birth attracted the System's attention because her existence possessed an unprecedented natural relationship with Reality. The Universe responded directly to her desires, allowing her to influence outcomes without consciously possessing the formal Primordial Authority required to command Creation.[7][3]
The System initially attempted to observe Moira but could not penetrate [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Eldritch Soul, where his daughters resided. Victor recognized that the System was withholding information and forced it to reveal the actual reason for its intervention.[3]
The System admitted that the former Progenitor of Destiny had been destroyed and that the future structure of the Universe had never been properly restored.[3]
According to the System, an enormous force continued holding Creation together, but the absence of Destiny meant that the Universe lacked a properly administered future. The System viewed this as a long-term threat to stability and believed another Primordial was eventually necessary to occupy the vacant office.[3]
Moira was identified as an almost perfect candidate.
The System intended for her to become the new archetypal figure of Destiny.[3]
Moira possessed an extraordinary compatibility with Reality. Her desires could passively influence the Universe itself, and the System assessed her governing potential as being beneath only [[Primordial Chaos]] in the relevant relationship with Reality.[3]
She nevertheless lacked one component required by Destiny's former office: the appropriate Time-related Authority. The System considered this a solvable problem and believed it could assist Moira in obtaining the necessary temporal power later.[3]
Victor immediately rejected the proposal.
The System attempted to justify its actions through universal stability, but Victor refused to permit his daughter to be transformed into a Primordial mechanism whose existence would become tied to maintaining Creation.
The confrontation escalated until the System withdrew and notified the remaining Primordial Beings.[3][4]
The reaction of the Regulators revealed another important fact: they had not known that the System was searching for a new Primordial of Destiny.[4]
The Owner of Limbo directly questioned the System, pointing out that the Universe was supposedly stable and demanding an explanation for why another Primordial was suddenly necessary. [[True Death]], Infinity and the Judges of the Abyss similarly reacted negatively to the System's unexplained behavior.[4]
[[The Universal Tree]] later concluded that the System's intervention had gone far beyond its normal authority and promised Victor that he would investigate what had changed.[4]
Victor prevented the System from obtaining Moira, leaving the former office of Destiny unoccupied.
As of Chapter 1176:
- Destiny remains erased.
- Predetermined universal Destiny remains absent.
- The old branching timeline structure remains collapsed into the Main Timeline.
- The Primordial office associated with Destiny/Fate remains vacant.
- The System considers the vacancy a threat to long-term cosmic stability.
- Moira remains a candidate identified by the System, but she has not become the new Destiny.[3][4]

### Nature

#### Primordial Entity
Destiny was a completed Primordial Entity and one of the fundamental administrators upon which the original cosmology depended.[1]
A completed Primordial is fundamentally different from a God merely possessing a Divine Concept. Primordials function as components of the cosmic structure itself. Their existences are tied directly to the universal Aspects they administer.[8]
Destiny therefore did not merely possess a Divinity of Destiny.
Destiny occupied the cosmic office responsible for the Destiny of the Universe.
This distinction explains why the Primordial's erasure altered the structure of Creation itself.

#### The Eighth Primordial
Before Chapter 965, the known cosmic order appeared to contain only seven principal Primordial entities:
- Positivity
- Negativity
- Infinity
- [[True Death]]
- [[The Universal Tree]]
- [[The Judges of The Abyss]]
- The Owner of Limbo
The System's investigation revealed that the original arrangement had contained another member:
Destiny.[1]
Destiny was therefore effectively the forgotten eighth Primordial of the old structure.
The exact relationship between Destiny's former Authority and the later known System Authority ranking remains unknown because the erasure affected even the records used to identify the missing administrator.

#### Destiny and Fate
Destiny and Fate are closely related but should not automatically be treated as identical terms.
Chapter 595 describes Fate according to the cosmology understood after Destiny had already disappeared. Fate existed as an impersonal Force or Concept closer to a state of non-existence rather than a conscious Being comparable to the Regulators.[9]
Fate was explained through the analogy of a road containing branches.
An individual could encounter multiple possible paths but retained the freedom to choose which path to follow.
FATE

│     └── possible outcome
│
├── Branch B
│     └── possible outcome
│
├── Branch C
│     └── possible outcome

The Force of Fate therefore did not directly mind-control individuals into obeying a specific result.[9]
Chapter 965 retroactively changes the context of this information.
The impersonal Fate observed by modern characters exists in a Creation where the conscious Primordial responsible for Destiny had already been erased.
Thus:
Destiny = the former conscious Primordial administrator.
Fate = a metaphysical Force/structure that can continue existing in some form even after the Primordial's erasure.
The surviving Force of Fate should not be treated as evidence that Destiny itself survived.

#### Predetermined Destiny
While Destiny existed, Creation possessed a stronger form of predetermined future structure.
Chapter 965 describes certain outcomes as things effectively etched into Creation and supposed to happen.[1]
This creates an important distinction:
Concept
Meaning
Possibility
An outcome that could occur depending upon choices, probability and circumstances.
Fate
The metaphysical structure of possible paths and outcomes available to an existence.
Destiny
A future state or outcome that Creation itself had established as something meant to occur.
Destiny's erasure removed this final category in its former absolute form.
Creation consequently lost the cosmological mechanism by which the future could be predetermined as an unavoidable Reality.[1]

### Destiny and Time
Destiny possessed a direct relationship with Time, but the exact limits of that relationship are unknown.
Chapter 965 associates Destiny with the timeline of all Creation and describes the Primordial through functions involving future progression and historical branching.[1]
Chapter 1175 further confirms that temporal authority formed a necessary component of the vacant Destiny office. When the System proposed [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]] as Destiny's replacement, it acknowledged that Moira did not naturally possess the required Time-related power and intended to help her obtain it later.[3]
This establishes that Time was not merely incidental to Destiny.
However, it does not establish Destiny as the sole Primordial authority over every aspect of Time.
Other cosmic entities possess their own temporal relationships, particularly Infinity and [[True Death]]. Destiny's confirmed specialization concerns:
- timelines;
- future progression;
- branching futures;
- predetermined outcomes;
- and the temporal structure through which those futures exist.
Destiny should therefore be treated as possessing Time Authority focused upon timelines and the future rather than unrestricted ownership of every temporal phenomenon.

### Destiny and Causality
Older versions of this article listed Destiny as the Primordial of Cause and Effect.
Current source material does not explicitly establish Causality as an independent Primordial Concept belonging to Destiny.
Destiny naturally affected the relationship between actions and future outcomes because its Domain governed Fate, timelines and predetermined results. This creates considerable overlap with cause-and-effect phenomena.
However:
- causality did not cease functioning after Destiny was erased;
- physical and supernatural actions continued producing consequences;
- and no available chapter explicitly identifies Destiny as the dedicated Primordial of <Causality>.
For this reason, Cause and Effect should presently be treated as an application/overlap of Destiny's future-regulation function rather than a confirmed separate Primordial Authority.

Different choices could correspond to radically different historical realities.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] observed possibilities in which:
- he became a Werewolf rather than a Vampire;

- his Golden Blood killed him;
- and his life developed under entirely different circumstances.[6]

Timeline administration and regulation is therefore more accurate than simply claiming unrestricted timeline creation.

### Erasure of Destiny
Destiny suffered an existence-level erasure rather than an ordinary death.
The erasure was sufficiently complete that:
- Destiny's conscious existence disappeared.
- The other Primordials forgot Destiny had ever existed.
- The System initially failed to recognize that a cosmic administrator was missing.
- Only residual information eventually revealed the former Primordial.
- The Concept of predetermined universal Destiny disappeared.
- The future structure of Creation became fundamentally altered.
- The former timeline system eventually collapsed into one active Main Timeline.[1]
This makes Destiny one of the clearest demonstrations that even a completed Primordial can theoretically be removed if an attacker possesses a method capable of bypassing the Primordial's integration with its Concept.

#### Erasure of the Primordial Concept
The destruction of Destiny affected the universal function attached to the Primordial.

#### Memory Erasure
One of the most extreme consequences of Destiny's destruction was the loss of knowledge that the Primordial had ever existed.
Even Destiny's fellow Regulators did not ordinarily remember the missing entity.[1]
This suggests that the erasure affected more than a body or Soul.
The removal extended into:
- records;
- historical recognition;
- Primordial memory;
- cosmic administration;
- and the conceptual place Destiny occupied within Creation.
The System only rediscovered Destiny after investigating inconsistencies produced by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and the collapsing timeline structure.[1]

### The Akashic Records and Destiny
Akashic became directly involved when the System investigated Destiny too deeply.
The System had begun uncovering the relationship between:
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s transformation into a Unique Being;
- the disappearance of his alternate selves;
- the collapse of the timeline structure;
- and the erased Primordial Destiny.
Akashic ordered the System to stop investigating and subsequently synchronized the timeline-separated System instances.[1]
The purpose behind Akashic's intervention remains incompletely explained.
The event nevertheless had enormous consequences.
By Chapter 1176, the narration reveals that merging the timelines had strengthened the System itself. The previously more limited administrative structure gained a stronger ego, a manifested form and substantially greater autonomy, while Reality was rewritten so the Primordials perceived the altered System as though nothing had changed.[4]
This development directly connects the erasure of Destiny and the timeline collapse to the System's later attempt to replace the missing Primordial.

### Effect on Precognition
The disappearance of predetermined Destiny did not eliminate prophecy, future sight or temporal perception.
Instead, it changed what those abilities observe.

#### Before Destiny's Erasure
A future event could exist as part of a future Reality cosmologically established by Creation.
Some outcomes could effectively be predetermined.

#### After Destiny's Erasure
Future perception reveals possibilities.
A future seen through precognition is no longer automatically a Reality that Creation itself guarantees will happen.[1]
This distinction is particularly important for extremely powerful Beings whose actions can rapidly change future possibilities.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later intentionally avoids relying heavily upon distant future sight because at the level of Beings such as himself the future changes too easily for far-reaching predictions to remain reliable.

### Vacant Primordial Office
Destiny's cosmic office remains vacant.
For centuries or longer after the erasure, even the surviving Regulators did not behave as though another Primordial needed to replace Destiny because they had forgotten the missing Being altogether.[1]
The System eventually became aware that the absence was a serious long-term problem.
By Chapter 1175, its internal reasoning was explicit:
- Destiny had been destroyed.
- The future was no longer properly administered.
- Something enormously powerful was keeping the Universe together.
- The System did not know how long the existing stability would last.
- Without a governed future, the System considered long-term cosmic order endangered.[3]
The System therefore began seeking a new holder of the Destiny office.
This search was conducted without the knowledge or authorization of the other active Primordial Beings.[4]

### Moira Eutychia Elderblood
[[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]] became the first confirmed candidate selected by the System to replace Destiny.[3]
Moira was not born as Destiny.
She instead possessed an unprecedented compatibility with Reality itself.
The Universe naturally responds to Moira's desires. Her power operates passively, allowing Reality to alter conditions around her according to what she genuinely wants even before she possesses a formal Primordial office.[7][3]
The System considered this characteristic ideal for Destiny.
According to its assessment:
- Moira was naturally suited to become a Primordial Being.
- Reality already responded to her at an extraordinary level.
- Her governing compatibility was surpassed only by [[Primordial Chaos]] in the relevant comparison.
- Becoming the Primordial of Fate/Destiny would greatly stabilize the Universe.[3]
Moira nevertheless lacked a necessary part of the original office.
Time.
The System specifically acknowledged that she did not possess the required Time-related power from birth.[3]
It considered this limitation temporary and intended to provide or help Moira acquire the necessary temporal Authority later.
This provides strong confirmation that Destiny's former Primordial office intrinsically required a relationship with Time and timeline administration.

#### Victor's Refusal
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] categorically refused to surrender Moira to the System.
His objection was not simply that Moira was too young.
Victor concluded that becoming a completed Primordial Administrator could fundamentally alter a Being's mentality by binding their existence to the function they were required to perform. He compared several existing Primordials to cosmic tools whose thoughts and personalities remained heavily limited by their purpose.[3]
Victor was unwilling to risk transforming his daughter into such a mechanism regardless of the benefits to universal stability.
When the System continued pursuing the issue, Victor threatened the surrounding Reality itself rather than compromise.
The System eventually withdrew and informed the existing Primordial Beings.[3][4]

#### Reaction of the Regulators
The surviving Primordials were surprised to discover that the System considered another Primordial necessary.
The Owner of Limbo directly questioned why the System was searching for a new candidate when the Universe was supposedly stable.[4]
This demonstrates that the replacement project was not a collective decision by the Regulators.
The System had acted independently.
After the confrontation, the Universal Tree concluded that the System had exceeded its normal authority and promised [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] that he would investigate the anomaly.[4]
Moira therefore remains only a rejected candidate rather than the new Destiny.

### Who Erased Destiny?
The identity of the Being responsible for Destiny's erasure remains officially unconfirmed.
Chapter 965 explicitly establishes that the System did not know:
- when Destiny was erased;
- why Destiny was erased;
- how the erasure was accomplished;
- or who was responsible.[1]
The surrounding sequence strongly associates the anomaly with [[Victor Elderblood]], particularly a future or higher expression of Victor.
The reasons include:
- Victor becoming a Unique Being across the timeline structure.
- The disappearance of alternative Victors.
- The simultaneous collapse of alternate timelines.
- Victor's future self displaying powers extending beyond conventional temporal restrictions.
- Akashic immediately intervening when the System investigated the anomaly too deeply.
- The timeline collapse ultimately protecting the continuity surrounding the Main-Timeline Victor and his family.[5][6][2][1]
These circumstances make Future Victor the strongest implied candidate.
However, implication is not confirmation.
Until the novel explicitly identifies Destiny's eraser, the infobox and main factual text should list the responsible Being as Unknown rather than definitively stating that Victor killed Destiny.

- Rule Administration — Destiny and Timeline Laws: Chapter 965 describes Destiny as having established or administered Rules governing the destined progression of Creation.[1]
- Destiny Law Regulation: Destiny maintained the Rules through which predetermined outcomes functioned.
- Timeline Law Regulation: The branching future/timeline system operated beneath Destiny's office.
- Predestination Enforcement: While the office existed, Creation could maintain outcomes treated as cosmologically predetermined.
Later chapters identify [[Primordial Chaos]] as the Creator of the Universe and its deepest fundamental Rules. Destiny's Rule authority should therefore be applied specifically to Destiny, Fate and timeline progression rather than claiming Destiny authored every Law of Reality.)
- Timeline Awareness — Presumed Administrative Function: Destiny's Authority encompassed the complete timeline structure administered by the Primordial.[1]
Destiny never appears alive, so unrestricted Omniscience should not be listed as a demonstrated ability.)
- Fate Awareness — Presumed Administrative Function: Fate and predetermined universal outcomes existed within Destiny's conceptual jurisdiction.
- [[Primordial Energy]] Manipulation: As a completed Regulator, Destiny possessed the fundamental Energy available to Primordial-class entities.
- Fate Energy / Fate Force Authority: Destiny's Domain was associated with the metaphysical Force of Fate described throughout the series.[9]
No direct scene depicts Destiny personally using a named Fate Energy attack, so this should remain a cosmic power-source classification rather than a combat technique.)

#### Abilities Not Currently Confirmed
The following powers should not presently be treated as independently confirmed just because they are thematically related to Destiny:
- Absolute Causality Manipulation
- Probability Manipulation
- Omniscience
- Complete control over all Time
- Creation of every alternate timeline
- Mind Control through Destiny
- Absolute control over individual free will
Destiny's confirmed Domain was extraordinarily broad, but direct feats from the living Primordial do not exist.

### Weaknesses and Limitations
Very little is known about Destiny's individual limitations because the character was erased before appearing.
Several restrictions can nevertheless be established from the cosmology:
- Destiny did not eliminate free choice. Chapter 595 explains that individuals could choose among available paths.[9]
- Destiny's Time Authority was not necessarily absolute. Other Primordials possess their own temporal relationships.
- Destiny could be erased. An unknown Being successfully bypassed the persistence normally associated with a completed Primordial.
- The Universe could continue without Destiny, but imperfectly. Creation did not immediately collapse after the erasure, although the System later considered the missing office a long-term stability problem.[3]
- Fate can exist without the conscious Primordial. An impersonal Force of Fate continued to operate after Destiny disappeared.[9]
- Predestination and ordinary causality are not identical. The loss of Destiny destroyed fixed future outcomes, not the basic relationship between cause and consequence.

### Trivia
- Destiny is unique among the original Regulators because the character was introduced only after already having been erased from existence.
- Destiny's erasure was so complete that even fellow Primordials forgot that another member of their order had existed.[1]
- The System nevertheless eventually found enough residual information to reconstruct the fact that Destiny had once existed.[1]
- Destiny should be indexed as Erased / Nonexistent rather than merely Deceased because the event affected Destiny's conceptual and historical existence.
- The older infobox's attribution of Destiny's death directly to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is stronger than the confirmed source material. Future Victor remains the strongest implied candidate, but the responsible Being is officially unknown.
- The older title Primordial of Cause and Effect is also unconfirmed. Destiny affected outcomes and future progression, but no available chapter explicitly establishes <Causality> as Destiny's independent Primordial Concept.
- Destiny's disappearance did not erase the impersonal Force of Fate.[9]
- Destiny's disappearance also did not eliminate precognition. Future sight now observes possibilities rather than guaranteed cosmological outcomes.[1]
- The convergence of timelines should not be described as the Main Timeline absorbing the total Energy of all erased timelines unless later chapters explicitly establish this.
- Moira is not currently the new Destiny. The System selected her as a candidate, Victor rejected the proposal and the Primordial office remains vacant through Chapter 1176.[3][4]
- Moira's lack of native Time-related Authority confirms that Time/timeline power was an important component of Destiny's former cosmic function.[3]
- The remaining Regulators were unaware that the System considered the Destiny office urgently necessary, demonstrating that the System's search for a replacement was an independent action rather than an agreed decision of the Primordial order.[4]
- The timeline collapse indirectly transformed the System itself. After Akashic merged the timelines, the System became stronger and developed greater [[Will Solace|Will]] while the rest of Reality was rewritten to perceive the new condition as normal.[4]
