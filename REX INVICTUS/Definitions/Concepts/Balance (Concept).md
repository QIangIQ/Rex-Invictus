**[[Balance (Concept)]]** is one of the fundamental principles governing Creation and one of the cosmic Concepts represented by the Regulators of Existence. (Lore adapted from *My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** This material is adapted into the Rex Invictus world at the lowest priority: Rex Invictus wins any conflict, then the Flamel material, then MTWABV. ## MTWABV Canon

Balance is one of the fundamental principles governing Creation and one of the cosmic Concepts represented by the [[Regulators of Existence]]. Its primary representative is the Owner of Limbo, the Primordial associated with the Concept of Balance.
In its broadest sense, Balance is the condition in which the countless forces, Concepts, systems, cycles, and opposing aspects that constitute Creation are able to coexist and perform their respective functions without destabilizing the greater cosmic structure.[1]
Although Positivity and Negativity form one of the most important foundations of Balance, Balance should not be reduced to merely maintaining equal quantities of Positive Energy and Negative Energy. It concerns the continued operation of Creation itself: its Timeline, cosmic systems, Life, Death, Souls, the development of worlds and civilizations, the activity of powerful beings, the operation of the Regulators, and ultimately the completion and renewal of the universal cycle.[1]
Balance likewise does not mean peace, absolute stability, morality, or the absence of chaos. Creation naturally contains change, conflict, destruction, death, and chaos. These things can exist within a Balanced Universe so long as their existence does not interfere with the greater mechanisms that allow Creation to continue functioning.[2][1]

### Nature
Balance has two closely related meanings within the context of the Cosmos.
The first is Balance as a fundamental Concept. Balance is an actual Aspect of Creation, comparable to the Concepts represented by other Primordials such as Life, Soul, Infinity, and Death. It is not merely an idea created by mortal civilizations.
This is made clear when Azathoth explains the nature of Primordials to [[Victor Elderblood]]. Primordials are fundamental pieces of the Universe whose existence represents Aspects required for Creation to function. Killing one would be equivalent to eradicating the Aspect represented by that Primordial from the Universe.[3]
For example, removing the Primordial associated with Soul would ultimately mean removing Soul itself. Removing Life would affect Life throughout Creation. Following the same principle, the disappearance of Balance would mean the loss of one of the fundamental mechanisms that allows the Universe to function correctly.[3]
The second meaning is the state of Balance. When the Regulators speak about "maintaining the Balance" or something "breaking the Balance", they refer to whether the interconnected systems of Creation remain capable of operating without an abnormality spreading into a larger failure.[1]
Therefore, the Owner of Limbo embodies and regulates the Concept of Balance, while the greater "Balance of the Universe" refers to the functioning equilibrium of Creation as a whole.

### A Balanced Universe
The clearest explanation of what a Balanced Universe actually means is given directly by the Owner of Limbo during his conversation with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
According to Balance:
"The Balanced Universe is a Universe that has only one Timeline, where the [[Chaos]] of the stars being created and the Balance of The Systems already created is not interfered with."[1]
This explanation establishes that Balance does not require Creation to remain static.
New stars can form, bringing with them natural Chaos. Existing stellar systems can remain stable. Both states can exist simultaneously without contradicting one another because both are normal parts of the operation of the Universe.[1]
The same principle applies throughout Creation.
Stars may be born and die. Worlds may develop or be destroyed. Species may evolve. Civilizations may rise, expand, decline, or disappear. Gods may fight. Beings may kill one another. Entire supernatural societies may undergo enormous changes.
None of these events automatically represent the breaking of Balance.
What matters is whether those events begin interfering with systems beyond their natural scope and create failures severe enough to threaten the continued functioning of Creation itself.[1]
Balance is therefore not synonymous with stability.
It is more accurately described as functional equilibrium.

### Origin of Balance
The modern state of cosmic Balance is intimately connected to [[Primordial Chaos]] and its disappearance.
Originally, Primordial Chaos existed as the highest existence within Creation. At some point in the ancient past, Primordial Chaos separated, resulting in the existence of Positivity and Negativity.[1]
These two primordial forces became the opposing sides underlying the duality of Creation.
The disappearance of unified Primordial Chaos also left the highest position within the cosmic structure vacant.
The System identifies this position as Rank 0, the position corresponding to the Ruler of the Universe.
Because no unified Leader currently occupies this position, the Primordials below it are required to continually maintain their respective portions of Creation and compensate for problems that would otherwise have been handled by the supreme ruler.[1]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] compares the situation to a malfunctioning computer.
Instead of possessing one supreme administrator capable of correcting problems throughout the entire structure, the Universe effectively has several administrators constantly correcting individual errors and ensuring that the machine continues running.[1]
The Owner of Limbo accepts this comparison.
In this sense, the current Universe has never possessed a completely perfect Balance.
It has instead been continuously maintained by the Regulators since the disappearance of Primordial Chaos.[1]

### Positivity and Negativity
Positivity and Negativity are two of the most fundamental forces involved in Balance.
They once existed together within [[Primordial Chaos]] before its division, and together represent the great duality underlying Creation.[4]
Their influence exists throughout the Universe.
Positivity and Negativity are described as being present everywhere and functioning as the Balance that allows Creation to operate.[4]
They are also semi-conscious primordial existences capable of selecting Representatives.
Negativity chose [[Victor Elderblood]] as its Representative, while Positivity had already selected another individual residing within the Upper Sectors.[4][1]

#### Duality
The relationship between Positivity and Negativity demonstrates one of the fundamental functions of Balance: allowing opposing forces to coexist without one completely eliminating the other.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] reaches a deeper understanding of this principle while examining [[Cain's Dagger]].[5]
The dagger contains opposing influences and energies that should normally be considered contradictory, yet those forces remain together in a functional state.
Through studying the interaction, Victor comes to understand:
"Duality exists to protect Creation. Balance is preserved so that this duality may exist."[5]
This does not mean that every Positive and Negative force must possess an exact numerical equivalent.
Rather, the two sides must remain capable of existing and performing their respective functions.
When the relationship between opposing forces becomes sufficiently unstable that one side destroys the structure permitting the other to function, the Balance can collapse.[5]

#### Balance is not exact equality
Balance should therefore not be interpreted as a universal 50/50 division.
One world, species, individual, Pantheon, or phenomenon may contain considerably more influence from one side than another without threatening the entire Cosmos.
The important factor is not numerical equality.
It is whether the larger system remains functional.
A temporary or localized dominance of one force does not automatically constitute a cosmic violation. The danger occurs when the imbalance becomes capable of disrupting the mechanisms upon which Creation depends.

### Positivity and Negativity are not Good and Evil
Positivity and Negativity should not be treated as synonyms for moral Good and Evil.
This distinction is directly established when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] accesses the power of Negativity.
Negativity does not mean that its Representative is evil. It simply represents one side of the cosmic scale and possesses different functions from the opposing side.[4]
Victor himself is the clearest example.
Despite being selected by Negativity, the Universal Tree explicitly recognizes that Victor's nature is not inherently evil.[4]
Therefore, Concepts or phenomena connected to Death, Destruction, Darkness, Endings, or other Negative aspects should not automatically be classified as morally evil.
Likewise, something connected to Creation, Life, Light, or Beginnings is not automatically morally good.
Cosmic Balance concerns function, not mortal morality.
A morally evil individual may exist without threatening Balance, while an apparently benevolent action could theoretically threaten Balance if its consequences damaged a fundamental cosmic system.

### Balance, Order, and Chaos
Balance is also distinct from Order.
[[Chaos]] does not inherently oppose Balance.
The Owner of Limbo explicitly describes the Chaos produced during the formation of stars as part of a Balanced Universe.[1]
[[Victor Elderblood]] demonstrates this principle on an even greater level.
During his transformation in Chapter 884, the Regulators examine Victor's existence and discover an extraordinary contradiction.
Victor's existence generates Chaos, yet at the same time produces Order and Balance around itself.[2]
The Judges of the Abyss are confused by this duality because Victor simultaneously causes enormous Chaos while generating stability in everything surrounding him.
The Owner of Limbo ultimately concludes:
"He has become a fundamental Pillar of The Balance."[2]
Victor therefore demonstrates that Chaos and Balance are not mutually exclusive.
An existence may generate enormous Chaos while simultaneously preventing that Chaos from destabilizing the greater system.
Balance concerns the relationship between those forces and the effect they have on Creation, rather than simply favoring Order over Chaos.

### The Regulators of Existence
Although the Owner of Limbo represents Balance directly, preserving the Balance is a collective responsibility of the [[Regulators of Existence]].
Each Regulator governs or represents different fundamental Aspects of Creation.
- [[The Universal Tree]] represents Life and performs functions related to the maintenance of living worlds and the continuation of life throughout Creation.
- [[The Judges of The Abyss]] govern Souls and the processing of deceased beings.
- The Owner of Limbo directly represents Balance and manages the Limbo Prison.
- Infinity represents Infinity and performs functions related to the continued structure and expansion of Creation.
- [[True Death]] represents the final End.
- Positivity and Negativity represent the primordial duality from which much of Creation derives.
The Regulators intervene when events become capable of breaking Balance.[1]
However, their activity differs depending on their respective Domains.
Balance explains that those Primordials whose functions involve sentient beings—particularly himself, Soul, and Life—are much more active because abnormalities related to their Domains occur far more frequently.
Infinity and Death, by comparison, are considerably more reclusive and generally intervene only when something directly affects their Domains.[1]

### The Impartiality of the Regulators
The Regulators are expected to prioritize their cosmic responsibilities over ordinary personal interests.
This becomes especially relevant when the possibility of beings ascending toward the level of a Primordial is discussed.
A Primordial is not simply an extremely powerful God.
Their existence represents a cosmic function.
Because of this, a being attempting to occupy such a position must be capable of fulfilling the responsibilities associated with the Concept rather than merely possessing sufficient raw power.
This distinction is demonstrated through [[Kali Elderblood]] and her relationship with Destruction.
Although Kali possesses an extraordinary compatibility with Destruction, her personal attachments interfere with the impartial state expected from a true Regulator.
[[The Laws of Existence]] consequently resist her progression toward that position.[6]
The greater principle is that a Regulator's duty concerns Creation itself.
Personal preferences cannot be allowed to override the cosmic function they are responsible for maintaining.

### How Balance is Maintained
Balance is protected through several overlapping mechanisms.
The Universe is therefore much more difficult to destabilize than it initially appears.[1]

#### Natural Self-Correction
Creation possesses an inherent tendency to correct irregularities.
According to the Owner of Limbo:
"The Universe always tries to Balance itself in the end."[1]
He associates this process with the semi-conscious existence of Positivity and Negativity.
Because both primordial forces permeate Creation, they form part of the underlying mechanism through which reality naturally attempts to return toward a functional state.[1]
This means that not every irregularity requires immediate Primordial intervention.
Many smaller disturbances may be corrected naturally before developing into serious threats.

#### The System
The System is another major component involved in the administration of Creation.
The System functions as a cosmic mechanism accessible according to varying levels of Authority.
The Primordials possess administrative privileges within it, allowing them to observe and manage aspects of Creation that ordinary beings cannot access.
However, the System is not the same thing as the [[Akashic Records]].
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] investigates the nature of the Akashic Records during his contemplation following the destruction of an alternate reality.[7]
The Akashic Records are the records of everything occurring within Creation, including events across Time.
The System instead serves as a separate cosmic mechanism through which information and Authority can be processed and accessed.
Therefore, the previous interpretation that the System and Akashic Records are synonymous is incorrect.

#### Akashic Records
Although distinct from the System, the [[Akashic Records]] are deeply connected to the functioning of Creation.
Everything that occurs within Creation is recorded within them.
Positivity and Negativity, because they exist throughout the Universe, can function as information collectors connected to these Records.[4]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] demonstrates this relationship while using the power of Negativity.
By drawing upon Negativity's connection throughout Creation, he is able to retrieve records of past events from the Akashic Records and display them before the Regulators.[4]
The Records therefore preserve information regarding Creation, while the System performs a different administrative and operational function.

#### Cosmic Laws and Restrictions
The Primordials do not possess unrestricted permission to interfere with every unusual being.
The conflict surrounding [[Diablo]] demonstrates this limitation.
Despite Diablo becoming an increasingly dangerous anomaly and possessing powers that threatened the established cosmic structure, the Regulators could not simply act against him however they wished before the necessary conditions were met.[8]
The situation continues as the Balance becomes increasingly disturbed.[9]
This establishes that several conditions must be distinguished:
- Being unusual does not necessarily mean breaking Balance.
- Being powerful does not necessarily mean breaking Balance.
- Possessing the potential to threaten Creation does not necessarily mean Balance has already been broken.
- Intending to cause an imbalance does not necessarily mean the actual cosmic threshold has already been crossed.
- A genuine violation occurs when the functioning of the larger cosmic structure is actually affected.
The Regulators themselves therefore operate within restrictions imposed by Creation.

#### Direct Intervention
When an irregularity grows beyond what natural correction and ordinary systems can safely manage, the Regulators intervene directly.
Balance explains that small problems cannot simply be ignored because numerous abnormalities may accumulate or interact with one another until they create a much larger failure.[1]
His function can therefore be understood as preventative maintenance on a cosmic scale.
The Regulators deal with individual abnormalities before those abnormalities become capable of threatening the greater machinery of Creation.

### Disturbing Balance and Breaking Balance
There is an important difference between disturbing Balance and completely breaking Balance.
Creation is capable of tolerating enormous amounts of instability.
Entire worlds can be destroyed.
Pantheons can fall.
Civilizations can wage wars.
Species can spread throughout large territories.
Powerful Gods can alter worlds.
None of these events necessarily mean that the fundamental Balance of Creation has collapsed.
The Owner of Limbo even considers the possibility of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s civilization and species expanding throughout the Cosmos.
Such expansion could create substantial problems for the System and surrounding civilizations, yet that does not mean that the entire Balance would instantly collapse.[1]
A genuine catastrophic violation requires something considerably more severe.
It must interfere with fundamental systems or create an abnormality capable of propagating through Creation until the greater structure can no longer function correctly.
This is why the Regulators often intervene before a problem reaches such a stage.

### Breaking Balance
Breaking Balance is therefore not merely "doing something unnatural."
The novel contains numerous beings whose existences violate ordinary supernatural expectations without Creation rejecting them.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] himself explicitly points this out when confronting the Judges of the Abyss.
Despite repeatedly being called an irregularity, monster, or potential threat to Balance, Victor notes that his existence has been accepted by Creation.
The System does not identify his mere existence as a Bug.[4]
This demonstrates that abnormality and imbalance are not synonymous.
An existence may be unprecedented and still remain compatible with the Laws and functioning of Creation.

### Artificial Power and Opposing Aspects
The combination of opposing powers is one of the subjects most frequently associated with Balance.
However, simply possessing powers from opposing sides does not automatically break Balance.
[[Diablo]] acquired and combined numerous powers through unnatural methods and eventually became a serious threat to cosmic stability.[8][9]
This led to concern regarding artificial combinations of opposing forces.
However, Diablo's example should not be interpreted as a universal law stating that any artificial fusion immediately violates Balance.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] provides the clearest contradiction to such an interpretation.
He possesses powers and Concepts associated with different sides of Creation, yet instead of destabilizing his surroundings, his existence produces Balance.[2]
Likewise, [[Cain's Dagger]] contains opposing influences while remaining a stable artifact.[5]
Therefore, the true issue is not simply:
Natural = Balanced
Artificial = Unbalanced
The determining factor is whether the resulting structure is capable of existing without destabilizing Creation.
Artificial manipulation can certainly create an unstable result, but artificial origin alone is insufficient to prove that Balance has been broken.

### Cain's Dagger
[[Cain's Dagger]] provides one of the clearest material examples of cosmic duality existing in a stable form.
The dagger contains influences connected to both sides of Creation.
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] examines it together with [[Roxanne Elderblood]] and [[Amara Elderblood]], its nature helps him understand how opposing forces can coexist rather than automatically annihilating one another.[5]
The dagger therefore serves as an example of the principle that Balance does not necessarily require eliminating contradiction.
Instead, contradictory forces may coexist if the structure containing them remains stable.
Victor's realization regarding Cain's Dagger becomes one of the foundations of his understanding of duality:
"Duality exists to protect Creation. Balance is preserved so that this duality may exist."[5]

### Balance and the Representatives of Positivity and Negativity
The selection of Representatives by Positivity and Negativity may have enormous implications for the future Balance of Creation.
Negativity selected [[Victor Elderblood]] as its Representative.[4]
Positivity had already selected its own Representative, who resides within the Upper Sectors.[1]
The Owner of Limbo views this development as potentially connected to the future restoration of the position once occupied by [[Primordial Chaos]].
When Victor asks what the existence of the two Representatives could mean for the Cosmos, Balance suggests the possibility that a new Leader may eventually emerge, allowing the Universe to function normally again.[1]
This is not presented as an absolute prophecy.
Balance himself admits that he does not know exactly what will happen.
However, he considers the appearance of the two Representatives significant because the current Cosmos has lacked its highest governing existence since Primordial Chaos divided.

### The Universal Cycle
Balance does not merely concern maintaining the present Universe.
It also concerns allowing the Cosmos to eventually reach its proper End.
According to the Owner of Limbo, Creation is not meant to continue endlessly in the same state.
Eventually, the current cosmic cycle must conclude.[1]
At the End of Everything, only two existences are supposed to remain:
- Death
- [[Primordial Chaos]]
Death accumulates the Essence produced by the conclusion of Creation.
Primordial Chaos would then use that Essence to begin another cycle.[1]
The End is therefore not an enemy of Balance.
It is part of Balance.
A Universe that could never properly end would itself represent a failure of the cosmic cycle.
The problem with the current Creation is that unified Primordial Chaos no longer exists.
This means that one of the two beings expected to remain at the conclusion of the cycle is presently absent.[1]
The selection of Representatives by Positivity and Negativity may ultimately be related to solving this problem, although the exact outcome remains unknown.

### The Fragility of Balance
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] initially assumes that Balance must be extremely fragile because the Primordials frequently discuss the danger of breaking it.
The Owner of Limbo corrects this interpretation.
Balance is simultaneously fragile and extraordinarily difficult to completely destroy.[1]
The idea can be compared to something delicate surrounded by numerous layers of extremely durable protection.
The fundamental Balance may be vulnerable if directly compromised, but reaching that point requires overcoming the numerous systems designed to protect Creation.
These safeguards include:
- The natural self-correcting tendency of the Universe.
- Positivity and Negativity.
- The System.
- The Laws governing Creation.
- The intervention of the [[Regulators of Existence]].
- The fundamental cosmic cycle itself.
Therefore, most irregularities do not immediately threaten to destroy the Universe.
The purpose of the Regulators is to identify and correct those irregularities before they can accumulate into something capable of reaching the fundamental Balance beneath those safeguards.[1]

### Victor Elderblood and Balance
[[Victor Elderblood]] possesses an extraordinary relationship with Balance.
Throughout his development, Victor repeatedly acquires powers and Concepts that should appear contradictory from the perspective of ordinary cosmic classification.
Most notably, his existence possesses an inherent duality between Order and [[Chaos]].[2]
When the Regulators examine him, they discover that while Victor's existence generates immense Chaos, it simultaneously produces Order and Balance in the surrounding environment.
Instead of causing Creation to reject him, his existence corrects abnormalities around itself.[2]
This leads the Owner of Limbo to declare that Victor has become a fundamental Pillar of the Balance.[2]
Victor later becomes the chosen Representative of Negativity while also possessing compatibility with Concepts associated with the opposing side, most notably Beginnings.[4]
Despite this apparent contradiction, his existence remains valid within Creation.
This is one of the clearest demonstrations of the actual nature of Balance.
Balance does not judge an existence purely according to labels such as Positive, Negative, Order, Chaos, natural, artificial, destructive, or creative.
What matters is whether that existence remains compatible with the larger functioning of Creation.

### The Owner of Limbo's "Indecision"
The statement that Balance represents "indecision" should not be interpreted literally as the definition of the Concept.
During his conversation with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], the Owner of Limbo jokingly claims that Balance is about indecision while deliberately avoiding answering a personal question.[1]
Victor immediately responds that this would be imbalance, after which Balance continues joking and avoids answering him.
The exchange is therefore primarily a joke about the Owner of Limbo's personality rather than a technical explanation of cosmic Balance.
The same chapter provides the actual explanation of the Concept through the discussion of the Balanced Universe, cosmic systems, the Regulators, [[Primordial Chaos]], and the universal cycle.[1]

### Common Misconceptions
Balance means equal amounts of Positive and Negative Energy
Not exactly. Positivity and Negativity are foundational components of Balance, but Balance encompasses the operation of Creation as a whole.[4][1]
Positive means Good and Negative means Evil
Incorrect. Negativity is explicitly stated not to mean Evil. The two sides possess different cosmic functions.[4]
[[Chaos]] inherently breaks Balance
Incorrect. Chaos naturally exists within a Balanced Universe, including the Chaos involved in stellar formation.[1]
Order and Balance are the same thing
Incorrect. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] can simultaneously generate Chaos, Order, and Balance, demonstrating that these are distinct principles.[2]
Possessing opposing powers automatically breaks Balance
Incorrect. Victor possesses contradictory aspects yet became a Pillar of Balance.[2]
Artificially combining opposing forces automatically breaks Balance
Not established as an absolute rule. Artificial manipulation can cause dangerous instability, but the result and its effect on Creation determine whether Balance is actually threatened.[8][9][2]
An irregular being automatically breaks Balance
Incorrect. Victor explicitly notes that Creation and the System accept his existence despite his numerous abnormalities.[4]
The System and [[Akashic Records]] are the same entity
Incorrect. The Akashic Records preserve the Records of Creation, while the System performs a distinct cosmic administrative function.[7]
Balance means peace
Incorrect. War, destruction, death, stellar formation, and other chaotic events can occur without destroying cosmic Balance.[1]
Balance means nothing should ever change
Incorrect. Change is an intrinsic component of a Balanced Universe.[1]
The present Cosmos is perfectly Balanced
Not in the sense of being structurally complete. The absence of unified [[Primordial Chaos]] leaves the highest position in the cosmic structure vacant, forcing the Regulators to continually maintain Creation.[1]
Balance represents indecision
The Owner of Limbo makes this statement as a joke while avoiding Victor's question. It is not the actual definition of the Concept.[1]
