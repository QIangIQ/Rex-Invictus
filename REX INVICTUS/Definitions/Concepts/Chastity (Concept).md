### Appearances

#### First Mention
Chapter 675
Chastity is an ancient Angel, a Seraph and one of the Seven Heavenly Virtues serving the [[Heavenly Father]]. As her title indicates, she possesses the conceptual authority associated with Chastity, placing her above ordinary Seraphim within Heaven's hierarchy and below the highest-order Original Seraphim in authority.[1][2]
Chastity is additionally distinguished from the other known Virtues by possessing prophetic visions. Shortly before the final stage of the Demonic War, one of her visions produced the name [[Azrael]] in connection with the newly established King of [[Biblical Hell]]. The revelation initially alarmed Heaven's Angels because Azrael was their own Angel of Death, but [[Cassiel]] interpreted the name symbolically as representing Death rather than indicating that Azrael had betrayed the Heavenly Father.[3]

### Physical Appearance
Chastity's individual physical appearance has not been explicitly described in sufficient detail.
During the Heavenly scene in which her prophecy is discussed, [[Cassiel]] is accompanied by two women whose eyes are covered by white masks bearing the same star symbol displayed beneath Cassiel's eyes.[3] Chastity has commonly been identified as one of these women because she is the Virtue directly discussed in the scene; however, Chapter 675 does not individually name either masked woman. The mask should therefore not be treated as an absolutely confirmed personal characteristic unless a later chapter identifies her directly.
As a member of the Seven Virtues, Chastity is a Seraph and consequently belongs to Heaven's six-winged Angelic hierarchy, although her wings are not individually described during her first mention.

### Personality
Very little has been directly revealed about Chastity's personality.
She appears to be a relatively mysterious member of the Seven Virtues whose primary known contribution is providing prophetic information. The other Angels take her visions seriously enough to discuss their implications before beginning Heaven's large-scale offensive against Hell, indicating that Chastity's supernatural foresight is an established and respected resource within Heaven.[3]
Her visions are nevertheless notoriously difficult to interpret. [[Cassiel]] openly states that Chastity's visions are not very accurate, not necessarily because they are completely wrong, but because the information they provide can be symbolic, incomplete or misleading without interpretation.[3]
The [[Azrael]] prophecy demonstrates this clearly. Chastity correctly perceived something relating the new King of Hell to Death, but the vision produced the specific name "Azrael". Taken literally, this implied that Heaven's own Angel of Death had betrayed the [[Heavenly Father]]. Cassiel instead understood the message symbolically and concluded that the new King of Hell was an existence positioned along the boundary separating Life and Death.[3]
Because Chastity has not yet received substantial dialogue or a dedicated scene, traits such as being emotionless, detached, shy or unusually quiet should not presently be treated as confirmed aspects of her personality.

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Chastity became relevant during the final preparations preceding Heaven's renewed offensive against [[Diablo]].
Following [[Victor Elderblood]]'s conquest of [[Biblical Hell]], the political and metaphysical situation surrounding the Demonic War changed completely. Biblical Hell now possessed a new King, while Diablo lost access to resources that had depended upon his former authority over the realm.[3]
Within Heaven, Ariel prepared to enter the war alongside the other high-ranking Angels. [[Cassiel]], the Virtue of Diligence, informed Ariel that the emergence of a new King of Hell had already been foreseen by the [[Heavenly Father]].[3]
When Ariel asked whether the identity of this new ruler was known, Cassiel explained that they did not know exactly who he was.
However, Chastity had received a vision.
The name that appeared was:
[[Azrael]].[3]
The response from the surrounding Angels was immediate. Azrael was one of Heaven's most powerful and loyal Original Seraphim, and the idea that the Angel of Death had betrayed the Heavenly Father was considered almost impossible.[3]
Cassiel clarified that Chastity's visions could not always be interpreted literally. He explained that Azrael was, in a broader sense, another name for Death. Chastity's vision therefore did not necessarily predict that Azrael himself had become the King of Hell.[3]
Ariel acknowledged the problem and admitted that Cassiel was unusually capable of interpreting their sister's vague visions. She asked what Chastity's prophecy actually meant.[3]
Cassiel concluded that the new King of Hell was neither properly an Angel nor a Demon, but an unusual existence walking a fine line of Balance—the boundary known as Life and Death.[3]
The prophecy was therefore an indirect reference to Victor Elderblood, whose increasingly abnormal existence and newly acquired authority over Biblical Hell did not fit comfortably within the traditional categories governing Heaven and Hell.
Cassiel considered Victor's personal identity less immediately important than the consequences of his ascension. With Biblical Hell recognizing another King, Diablo had lost the ability to freely draw upon the Lesser Demons of the realm and had suffered an enormous military setback.[3]
The Heavenly Father then issued the order for his Angels to sound the trumpets and begin Judgment Day. Five of the highest-ranking Angels—the Virtues—emerged as Heaven mobilized for full-scale war.[3]
Chastity's vision consequently served as one of Heaven's early supernatural indicators that a fundamental shift had occurred within Hell, even though its exact meaning required Cassiel's interpretation.

### Seven Heavenly Virtue
Chastity belongs to the Seven Heavenly Virtues, an elite class of Angels possessing conceptual powers granted by the [[Heavenly Father]].
The Virtues are still Seraphim by race, but their possession of Concepts places them above ordinary Seraphim in Heaven's hierarchy. Chapter 558 explicitly explains that Concept-bearing Virtues are closer to Gods than conventional Angels and possess greater authority over normal Seraphim.[1]
The hierarchy described at the time was:
- Original Seraphim — beings such as Michael and [[Gabriel]], who possess their own Concepts and hold greater authority.
- Seven Heavenly Virtues — Concept-bearing Seraphim such as Chastity, Ariel and [[Cassiel]].
- Ordinary Seraphim — powerful six-winged Angels beneath the Virtues.[1]
Each Heavenly Virtue is also entitled to two Seraphim as direct subordinates, whose purpose includes protecting the Virtue and acting upon their commands.[1]
Later explanations from the Heavenly Father clarify that the Concepts possessed by the Virtues were granted by him. The Seven Virtues are not completely equivalent to full Gods: their Concepts were described as insufficiently developed to independently make them Gods in their own right, but they nevertheless possess enough conceptual authority to wield those powers similarly to Divine beings.[2]

### Skills & Abilities
Chastity has very few direct combat feats. Most of her confirmed powers come from her status as one of the Seven Heavenly Virtues and her uniquely demonstrated prophetic ability.

#### Angel Physiology
- Seraph Physiology: Chastity is a Seraph and possesses the supernatural spiritual and physical characteristics of Heaven's highest ordinary Angelic order.
- Supernatural Physical Attributes: As a Seraph, Chastity possesses strength, speed, durability, stamina and sensory abilities greatly exceeding ordinary Humans and lower supernatural beings.
- Extreme Longevity: Chastity has existed for an extremely long period and is counted among Heaven's ancient Angels.
- Flight: Seraphim possess six wings capable of supernatural flight, although Chastity's own wings have not received an individual physical description.
- [[Holy Energy]]: As a high-ranking Angel, Chastity possesses Heaven's Holy Energy and belongs naturally to the Positive side of the Biblical supernatural system.
- High Spiritual Authority: Chastity's position as a Heavenly Virtue places her above ordinary Seraphim because she possesses a Concept.[1]

#### Conceptual Authority
- Concept of Chastity: Chastity possesses the conceptual power corresponding to the Heavenly Virtue for which she is named. The [[Heavenly Father]] granted the Seven Virtues their respective Concepts, allowing them to wield conceptual authority in a manner comparable to Divine beings.[2]
- Conceptual Empowerment: Possessing a Concept elevates a Virtue beyond normal Seraphim and grants greater supernatural authority.[1]
- Conceptual Existence: Chastity's power is tied to the abstract principle of Chastity rather than being merely a title or moral philosophy.[2]
- The specific combat, defensive or metaphysical applications of Chastity's Concept have not yet been demonstrated. Abilities such as absolute purity, forced celibacy, desire suppression, immunity to corruption or resistance to temptation should therefore not currently be indexed as confirmed applications.

#### Prophetic Visions
- Future Sight / Prophecy: Chastity can receive supernatural visions concerning future events or major developments.[3]
- Symbolic Precognition: Her visions do not necessarily provide literal names or direct explanations. The appearance of "[[Azrael]]" in her vision referred to Death and the boundary between Life and Death rather than necessarily predicting Azrael himself as the new King of Hell.[3]
- Event Premonition: Chastity's vision successfully detected the emergence of an extraordinary new ruler of [[Biblical Hell]] before the Angels possessed a complete understanding of what had occurred.[3]
- Existential Perception through Visions: Her prophecy contained information concerning the unusual nature of the new King of Hell, indirectly indicating an existence that did not fit cleanly within the traditional Angel/Demon divide.[3]

#### Limitations of Prophecy
Chastity's visions possess a major limitation: they are difficult to interpret accurately.[3]
[[Cassiel]] explicitly states that her visions are not particularly accurate when interpreted literally. Their information can arrive through symbols, names or associations rather than straightforward descriptions.[3]
This creates several limitations:
- Symbolic Information: A name appearing within a vision may represent an associated Concept rather than the individual bearing that name.
- Interpretation Requirement: Another individual may need to interpret what the vision means. Cassiel appears especially proficient at understanding Chastity's prophecies.[3]
- Incomplete Identification: Despite Chastity's vision, Heaven still did not immediately know that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was definitively the new King of Hell.[3]
- Potential Misinterpretation: Taking her visions literally can lead to incorrect conclusions, as demonstrated when Angels initially feared [[Azrael]] had betrayed Heaven.[3]

### Relationships

#### Cassiel
[[Cassiel]] is one of Chastity's fellow Heavenly Virtues and appears to understand her prophetic ability better than most of their siblings.
When Chastity's vision produced [[Azrael]]'s name, the other Angels initially interpreted it as evidence that Azrael might have betrayed Heaven. Cassiel immediately rejected this literal interpretation and explained the symbolic meaning behind the prophecy.[3]
Ariel specifically remarked that Cassiel seemed to be one of the few people capable of understanding Chastity's visions properly.[3]

#### Heavenly Father
The [[Heavenly Father]] is Chastity's creator and sovereign.
As with the other Seven Virtues, the conceptual power associated with Chastity was granted through the Heavenly Father's authority. Later explanations establish that he is responsible for granting the Seven Virtues the Concepts they wield.[2]
Chastity remains part of Heaven's hierarchy and loyal to her creator.

#### Azrael
[[Azrael]] became indirectly connected to Chastity through her most important known prophecy.
His name appeared in her vision concerning the new King of [[Biblical Hell]], causing immediate concern among Heaven's Angels. The prophecy did not mean Azrael personally had become King. Instead, his name functioned as a symbolic representation of Death.[3]

#### Victor Elderblood
Chastity has not directly interacted with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] in the checked chapters.
Her prophecy nevertheless indirectly concerned him. After Victor became the new King of [[Biblical Hell]], Chastity perceived an image or name associated with Death, leading [[Cassiel]] to describe the new ruler as an existence walking along the boundary between Life and Death.[3]

### Power
Chastity's precise combat strength has not been directly demonstrated.
What can be established is her position within Heaven's hierarchy. As one of the Seven Heavenly Virtues, she possesses a Concept and therefore stands above conventional Seraphim in authority. Only higher-order Angelic beings such as the Original Seraphim were explicitly described as possessing greater authority during the earlier Heaven hierarchy.[1]
Later explanations describe the Seven Virtues as beings closer to Gods because of their Conceptual powers. They were compared more closely to Demigods than complete Gods because their Concepts were not independently developed to the same degree as true Divine beings, although they could still wield those Concepts similarly to Gods.[2]
No chapter currently provides enough direct combat evidence to assign Chastity a more specific power tier.
