List of Important Terms is a central glossary for terminology used throughout [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]]. It is intended as a one-stop reference for the novel's supernatural mechanics, Souls, Concepts, Divine hierarchy, Energies, cosmology, Pantheons, political titles, civilization classifications and late-story Imperial terminology.
The definitions below summarize what the novel has actually established. Where a term has multiple meanings, those meanings are separated rather than silently combined. A title, Energy type, ontological state, Threat Level and political rank are not automatically interchangeable merely because the same character possesses all of them.
Reading rule: The novel frequently reuses words such as Authority, Domain, [[Chaos]], Universe, Reality, Primordial, Core and World. Their meaning must be determined from the chapter's context rather than from the word alone.

### Metaphysical and Conceptual Terms
Term
Meaning
Soul
A Soul is the metaphysical existence associated with living and supernatural beings and forms a fundamental part of identity, death and reincarnation. Souls are not restricted to sentient organisms; the afterlife explanation establishes that plants, mountains and other existences can possess Souls which eventually enter the reincarnation process.[1] The Judges of the Abyss explain that Life provides a form while their system supplies that form with a Soul.[2] Souls can contain Bloodlines, Divine structures, memories, Concepts and other supernatural information depending upon the being.
Soul Core
A Soul Core is a deep central structure of a Soul. Damage reaching this region is vastly more serious than ordinary physical injury. After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s [[Soul Marriage]] with [[Aphrodite]], a structure representing a portion of Aphrodite's Soul Core existed within Victor's Inner World, and damaging it could directly endanger her Soul.[3] Victor's later Soul develops much more complicated internal layers, but his abnormal structure should not be assumed to represent every Soul.
Soul Quality
Soul Quality describes the qualitative difference between Souls belonging to beings at different stages of existence. Mortal, Divine and extremely advanced Souls are not identical simply because all are called Souls. This becomes especially important in the explanation of Primordial ascension, where merely strengthening a Divine Soul is insufficient; the Soul itself must begin transforming into a Concept.[4]
Essence
Essence is a recurring term for a portion of an existence which retains qualities belonging to its source. Essence can participate in creation, inheritance and transformation. Victor's Heralds could enter a deeper part of his existence because they shared [[Chaos]]-related Essence with Victor and War.[5] Azathoth later explains that even fragments or descendants of an Eldritch Chaos can retain qualities originating from that Chaos.[6] The novel has not established one universal formal "Essence system", so the exact meaning remains context-dependent.
Ego
Ego refers to the conscious self or individual identity occupying an existence. This becomes cosmologically important with [[Primordial Entities]]: Azathoth's explanation distinguishes the consciousness administering a Primordial Aspect from the Aspect itself. Destroying or replacing a Primordial's Ego does not necessarily require erasing the fundamental Concept from Creation.[6]
Concept
A Concept is an abstract principle or aspect of existence such as Life, Death, Time, Blood, War, Destruction, Infinity or Order. Gods interact with Concepts through Divinity, while true Primordial Entities can become part of fundamental Aspects of Creation itself.[7][4] A God possessing a Concept should not automatically be interpreted as the singular universal source of that Concept.
Divine Concept
A Divine Concept is a Concept expressed through a God's Divinity. The God develops an individual Divine relationship with that Concept, its Laws and its applications.[7] Multiple Gods can possess related or similarly named Concepts while differing enormously in interpretation, mastery and Authority.
Divinity
Divinity is the Divine structure and conceptual connection through which a God embodies and exercises a Concept.[7] Possession of a Divinity does not automatically grant complete knowledge of everything linguistically associated with that Concept. Later training repeatedly establishes that comprehension is essential: a God can possess enormous Energy while still understanding only a small portion of their own Divinity.[8][9]
Concept Mastery
Concept Mastery is the degree to which a Divine Being actually understands the nature and Laws of a Concept. A newly acquired Divinity and a fully mastered Divinity are therefore radically different even when the name of the Concept is identical. Victor's difficulty with Destruction and Kali's later development demonstrate that greater Energy cannot substitute for understanding what a Concept fundamentally represents.[8][9]
Laws
Laws are rules governing reality and supernatural phenomena. A God deepens a Divinity by understanding the Laws belonging to its Concept.[9] On a vastly greater scale, entire independent Cosmoses can operate according to different Fundamental Laws and Rules.[6]
Fundamental Laws of Existence
The Fundamental Laws of Existence are the foundational Rules determining how a complete cosmological system operates. Azathoth demonstrates that another Cosmos can possess different Rules, Primordials and even a different Chaos from Victor's native Creation.[6]
Conceptual Authority
Conceptual Authority is the effective control, priority and influence a being possesses over a Concept or conceptual family. It depends upon the nature of the Concept, the user's understanding, their existential state and the source of their power. A broad umbrella Concept can grant authority over subordinate phenomena without making every subordinate phenomenon an independently awakened personal Divinity.[8][10]
Conceptual Source
A Conceptual Source is the deeper existence from which a conceptual power receives its foundation or Energy. Conventional Divinities operate through the native conceptual structure of Creation. Victor later becomes a foreign conceptual source for Eldritch Goddesses such as [[Nero]] and Ophis, performing for them a role analogous to the native higher sources of ordinary Gods.[11][12]
Umbrella Concept
An Umbrella Concept is an exceptionally broad Concept encompassing many subordinate conceptual families. Negativity and Beginning are major examples. Victor's Negativity eventually governs thousands of subordinate Divinities, but specialized Gods can still possess superior precision within a narrower branch they have individually mastered.[10][13]
Conceptual Being / Conceptual Existence
A Conceptual Being is an existence whose nature has become unusually inseparable from a Concept rather than merely using it as an external ability. The term exists across more than one level: Kassandra was born as a conceptual embodiment of Destruction,[14] while a completed Primordial Entity represents a much deeper state in which the Soul becomes part of a universal Aspect.[4] Therefore, being described as "a Concept" does not automatically mean every such being possesses the full cosmological status of a Regulator.
Aspect of Existence
An Aspect of Existence is a fundamental component of Creation with which a completed Primordial Entity can become integrated. Life, Soul, Death, Infinity and Balance are examples associated with established Primordial administration.[4][6] Erasing the Aspect itself is vastly different from defeating an ordinary God who merely possesses a related Divinity.
Balance
Balance is the cosmological equilibrium which allows conflicting systems and forces of Creation to continue operating without destabilizing the greater Universe. The [[Regulators of Existence]] are expected to protect this equilibrium rather than simply pursue personal interests. The current Universe itself displays a semi-conscious tendency to restore balance because of its underlying Positive and Negative foundations.[15]
Positivity
Positivity is one of the two enormous opposing foundations produced from the incomplete division of [[Primordial Chaos]]. It is associated with a vast family of Concepts and Energies but is not synonymous with moral Good.[16][17][15]
Negativity
Negativity is the opposing foundational side of the same cosmic Balance. It governs an enormous family of negative, spiritual and destructive-related Concepts while remaining morally neutral in itself.[16][17] Victor becomes its chosen representative and eventually gains authority over thousands of compatible Divinities.[13]
Chosen Representative
A Chosen Representative is an individual selected by a semi-conscious Primordial Aspect such as Positivity or Negativity to carry its power within Creation. These foundational powers are not ordinary Divinities that can simply be learned through training; the representative must be accepted by the underlying Primordial principle.[17]
Synchronization
Synchronization is a temporary state in which a compatible chosen representative synchronizes with the Primordial Aspect behind their power and channels a vastly greater portion of its authority. The state can temporarily elevate the user's conceptual authority toward the level of the underlying Primordial, but it depends upon compatibility and is not the same thing as permanently becoming that Primordial Entity.[17]
Karma
Karma is one of the qualities considered during afterlife judgment. Souls possessing Positive Karma can be sent toward appropriate Heavens, while sufficiently corrupted Souls or negative portions separated from them can be directed to Hells for punishment, purification or recycling.[1] No canonical numerical Karma formula has been revealed.
Faith
Faith is genuine belief or conviction directed toward a deity, supernatural existence or ideal. Religious practitioners can convert such conviction into supernatural power,[18] while sustained collective worship can also influence the Divine development of the being being worshipped.[19]

### Souls, Records and Identity
Term
Meaning
Record of Existence
A Record of Existence is the information preserved by the [[Akashic Records]] concerning a being or event. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explains that events occurring throughout Creation are stored within the Records.[20] Even when a Soul has lost its old identity through the reincarnation process, an earlier Record can remain accessible; Victor used Vanessa's old information to reconstruct the identity associated with her previous existence.[21]
Soul Record
Soul Record is useful shorthand for the Akashic information associated with a Soul's identity, history and prior existence. It should not be confused with the Soul itself. Chapter 1021 demonstrates that a Soul can be emptied of its former identity while the previous Record remains separately recoverable through the Akashic structure.[21]
Identity
Identity is more than the continued existence of raw Soul material. Memories, personality, accumulated experiences and the informational pattern defining the individual can be separated from the underlying Soul during reincarnation or supernatural alteration. Vanessa's restoration demonstrates why reproducing a body or possessing an empty Soul is not automatically equivalent to restoring the original person.[22][21]
[[Soul Marriage]]
Soul Marriage is a supernatural union directly binding the Souls of its participants. [[Aphrodite]]'s marriage to Victor demonstrates that the connection is fundamentally deeper than an ordinary contractual or ceremonial marriage and cannot simply be undone by physical separation.[23] Later explanations treat the union as continuing beyond ordinary physical death and through the participants' continuing spiritual existence.[24]
Vampire Marriage Ritual
The Vampire Marriage Ritual is a different supernatural bond used by Vampires. It creates a powerful spousal relationship, including supernatural connections between the participants, but should not automatically be treated as identical to the God/Conceptual-level Soul Marriage demonstrated by Aphrodite.[25][26][23]
Soul Contract
A Soul Contract is a contract whose enforcement reaches the Soul rather than relying only upon social or material consequences. The exact severity varies enormously. High-level contracts can bind information, behavior or loyalty, while the Upper-Sector contract encountered in Chapter 1158 automatically destroyed both the host's body and Soul whenever prohibited information was revealed.[27]
Existential Contract
An Existential Contract is an extreme form of binding whose enforcement acts on the target's existence itself. The contract protecting Upper-Sector information in Chapter 1158 repeatedly annihilated the captive fragment when restricted knowledge was spoken, forcing Victor to reconstruct the victim hundreds of billions of times to circumvent the restriction.[27] The chapter does not establish that every ordinary Witch contract functions at this level.
Witch Contract
A Witch Contract is a magically binding agreement produced through Witchcraft. Vlad used such a contract when formally establishing Victor as a Vampire Count.[28] The strength, clauses and supernatural enforcement depend upon the contract; "Witch Contract" should not be treated as one single universal power level.
Inner World / Soul World
An Inner World is an internal metaphysical space associated with a sufficiently developed Soul. Victor's internal world became increasingly elaborate as his existence evolved and eventually contained Soul structures, memories and entire internal realms.[3] Victor's later internal cosmology is extraordinarily abnormal and should not be used as proof that every being possesses a universe-sized Soul world.

### Akashic Records, System and Timelines
Term
Meaning
[[Akashic Records]]
The Akashic Records are the informational Record of Creation. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explains that events occurring throughout the Universe are preserved there.[20] The Records can preserve information even after an individual's present manifestation is gone and can include alternative histories and possibilities.[29][21] They are not identical to The System.
Akashic
Akashic is the personification or conscious manifestation associated with the Akashic Records. Chapter 965 demonstrates that Akashic can directly confront the System, command it to abandon a dangerous investigation and reorganize its relationship with the alternate Systems that once existed across the branching timelines.[30]
System
The System is an administrative and informational mechanism created to assist the Primordial administrators in monitoring and managing Creation.[29] Victor compares the larger arrangement to a computer: the Akashic Records function more like the irreplaceable informational foundation/storage, while the System functions more like the processor which examines and administers that information.[29]
Authority
Authority is a heavily context-dependent word. In System discussions it means recognized administrative access. In Divine discussions it can mean conceptual control. In a Ruler's territory it can mean jurisdictional control, while in political discussion it can simply mean legal sovereignty. These uses should not be merged into one statistic.
System Authority
System Authority is the degree of administrative recognition and access granted by the System to exceptional beings who understand and interact with Creation at a sufficiently high level.[29] It governs informational and administrative privileges and is not a direct measurement of combat strength.
Authority Rank
An Authority Rank is the System's hierarchy of beings possessing administrative Authority. Higher ranks receive broader access to information and cosmic functions.[29] A lower-ranked Authority can still be more dangerous than a higher-ranked one in a particular battle because Authority Rank and combat power measure different things.
Administrator
An Administrator is a being possessing exceptionally high administrative Authority over the mechanisms of Creation. Established [[Regulators of Existence]] occupy the highest known administrative positions beneath the absent supreme [[Chaos]] authority.[6]
Timeline
A Timeline is a historical sequence generated through the events and choices of Creation. Before the convergence, effectively countless branching histories existed as active alternative timelines, each possessing its own corresponding history and System.[29][31]
Alternate Timeline
An Alternate Timeline was one of the parallel historical branches which existed before the convergence. Different choices could create histories in which familiar individuals developed differently or never reached the same events.[29] An alternate timeline is not automatically the same thing as a Dimension or an entirely independent Cosmos.
Main Timeline
The Main Timeline is the single active historical Reality remaining after the timeline convergence. Following Akashic's intervention, the formerly parallel active histories were synchronized/collapsed so that unrealized futures became possibilities rather than simultaneously existing living timelines.[30]
Timeline Convergence
Timeline Convergence refers to the collapse/synchronization of the branching timeline structure around the Main Timeline. Victor's transformation into a Unique Being exposed the anomaly, while Akashic ultimately synchronized the alternate Systems and left one active historical Reality.[29][31][30]
Unique Being
A Unique Being is an existence possessing no parallel counterpart across the alternative timeline structure. Victor became one after his ascension as <CHAOS>; the System discovered that versions of Victor which should have existed in alternate histories were no longer present as separate active counterparts.[32][29]
Destiny
Destiny was a Primordial Regulator associated with the predetermined course of Creation and its branching futures. Future Victor erased Destiny so completely that even the other Primordials largely forgot the missing Aspect.[31][30] After Destiny's removal and the timeline convergence, future events continued to exist as possibilities but were no longer cosmologically guaranteed scripts.
Fate
Fate is earlier described as a flow which can branch as circumstances and choices produce different paths.[33] It should not automatically be treated as identical to the erased Primordial Destiny. By the Ragnarök period Victor explicitly rejected the assumption that a prophecy or predicted future must occur simply because it had been foreseen.[34]
Possible Future
A Possible Future is an outcome that can occur without necessarily already existing as a separate active timeline. This distinction became especially important after Chapter 965, when the old branching timeline structure collapsed and futures remained possibilities rather than simultaneously realized worlds.[30]

### Death, Afterlife and Reincarnation
Term
Meaning
[[Reincarnation System]]
The Reincarnation System is the large automated structure responsible for recycling Souls throughout Creation.[21] Souls can pass through judgment, Heaven, Hell, purification and other processes before ultimately returning to Life according to the applicable system.[1]
Afterlife Realm
An Afterlife Realm is a supernatural Dimension used to receive, process, reward, punish or house the dead. Heavens, Paradises, Hells and Underworlds are examples. Their structures vary between Pantheons and civilizations.[1] [[World Trees]] can participate in creating and sustaining such dimensions.[35]
Heaven / Paradise
A Heaven or Paradise is generally a positive afterlife destination in which qualifying Souls may reside before eventually returning to the wider reincarnation cycle.[1] Different Pantheons possess different Heavens and rules.
Hell / [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]]
A Hell or Underworld is an afterlife Realm used for punishment, purification, Soul administration or the negative side of a Pantheon's afterlife. The word "Hell" does not always refer to one universal location; the Greek, Norse, Biblical, Shinto and other systems developed distinct Realms and administrations.[1][36]
Core of the Afterlife
A Core of the Afterlife is a metaphysical structure connecting an Underworld to the greater Soul-processing system. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] observed such a Core in the Greek Underworld and recognized that his own Hell possessed a comparable structure.[20] It acts as a gateway/interface to the greater reincarnation machinery rather than merely functioning as an Energy battery.
Ruler
A Ruler is the recognized administrator of a sufficiently developed Hell or Underworld. The position is separate from God-King. A Ruler receives jurisdictional authority over the afterlife system, access to the relevant Core and supernatural support from the Realm itself.[1][37][36] The authority is strongest inside the Ruler's legitimate domain and is not automatically absolute over independent Primordial territories located within the same wider Realm.[38]
God of Death
A God of Death in a developed Pantheon helps administer Souls beneath the Ruler's larger afterlife authority. Chapter 704 explains this as part of the structure arising when a sufficiently important Hell is integrated into the greater Soul System.[36]
Death
Death can refer to ordinary biological/spiritual death, Divine Concepts related to death or the universal Primordial authority of [[True Death]]. These are not interchangeable. Killing a body is not automatically Soul destruction, and possessing a Death Divinity does not make a God the universal source of Death.
True Death
True Death is the Primordial authority associated with the final Death and End of Creation. It supervises the regions associated with the true End and stands vastly above ordinary Gods who possess death-related Divinities.[39][40][6] True Death did not create The Abyss; the Abyss is a region produced by portions of Creation genuinely reaching their End.[40]
End / <END>
End is the principle of a phenomenon reaching its conclusion rather than merely being physically damaged. Powers derived from <END> can bypass assumptions that ordinary destruction leaves something usable behind.[41][39] Late material explicitly distinguishes <END> from Destruction because END can erase the deeper Record and place of an existence in the cosmic cycle.[14]
Destruction
Destruction is a Concept concerned with destroying or breaking existing structures. Chapter 1150 distinguishes it from END: even extremely complete Destruction can leave deeper Records, Space-Time context and a place from which Creation can eventually recycle or reconstruct what was destroyed.[14]
True End
A True End is a state in which the underlying existence of a region has genuinely concluded. Chapter 1059 contrasts ordinary Galactic destruction, which may leave matter and celestial remains, with a galaxy which has truly ended: Life, ordinary Time, Space and even the Concept of Void are absent from that dead region.[39]

### Energy Terms
Energy type, conceptual Authority and state of existence are separate things. Possessing a superior Energy does not automatically place the user's Soul at an equivalent ontological stage.
Term
Meaning
Energy
Energy is the general medium used to power supernatural phenomena. Numerous forms possess different characteristics, affinities and rules. [[Magic]] Energy, [[Divine Energy]], [[Draconic Energy]], Faith Energy, Positive Energy, Negative Energy, [[Primordial Energy]] and [[Eldritch Energy]] should not automatically be treated as interchangeable simply because all are forms of Energy.
Energy Characteristic / Attribute
An Energy Characteristic is the property which causes Energy to behave as a particular type. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later research demonstrates that characteristicless Energy can be given properties and transformed into another Energy if the user understands what must be imposed upon it.[42][43]
Magic Energy / Mana
Magic Energy, commonly expressed as Mana within Witchcraft, is the Energy used for conventional Magic and magical technology. Mana Crystals store condensed Magic Energy and are widely used as supernatural resources by Witches.[44]
Divine Energy
Divine Energy is the supernatural Energy associated with Gods and Divine existence. It can power Divine techniques and Magic but should not be confused with Divinity itself: Divinity is conceptual structure and Authority, while Divine Energy is Energy used by the Divine being.[7][45][46]
Draconic Energy
Draconic Energy is the racial Energy associated with true Dragons. A Dragon's Heart and physiology function as an enormous Energy system capable of supporting its body and abilities.[47] Dragon Progenitors possess exceptional compatibility with other Energies, but Fire, Magic or another Energy manipulated by a Dragon does not automatically become Draconic Energy merely because a Dragon is controlling it.[48]
Positive Energy
Positive Energy is Energy associated with the Positive side of the cosmic Balance. It should not be equated with moral goodness. Highly advanced civilizations such as the [[Veillon Empire]] can build entire systems around enormous concentrations of Positive Energy.[17][49]
Negative Energy
Negative Energy is Energy associated with Negativity and the corresponding side of cosmic Balance. Numerous supernatural species, Concepts and phenomena can use it without automatically being morally evil.[16][17]
Faith Energy
Faith Energy is supernatural power produced through genuine belief and religious conviction. Human religious practitioners can wield Faith without possessing Divinity themselves,[18] while later Imperial systems allow powerful believers to channel Divine powers through their relationship with Gods.[50][51]
Primordial Energy
Primordial Energy is a highly developed characterized Energy associated with Primordial-level powers. It is not identical to Primordial existence and using it does not automatically make someone a Primordial Entity. Victor later demonstrates that sufficiently understood Primordial Energy can itself be produced by assigning the required characteristics to Attributeless Energy.[42][43]
Eldritch / Nightmare Energy
Eldritch Energy or Nightmare Energy is foreign Energy associated with Victor's and other [[Outer Gods]]' Eldritch existence. [[Nero]]'s developing Apocalypse was filled through Victor's Nightmare Essence rather than through an ordinary native Divine source.[11] The novel has not established that Eldritch Energy, [[Chaos]] Energy, Primordial Energy and Creator's Energy are synonyms.
Attributeless Energy
Attributeless Energy, also called Energy Without Attributes or Creator's Energy, is Energy before a specialized characteristic has been imposed upon it. Victor's analogy identifies it as the "paint" which a Creator can give different properties when building Creation.[42] With sufficient understanding, it can be converted into specialized Energies.[43]
[[Creator's Energy Mastery]]
Creator's Energy Mastery is Victor's Higher-Level method for generating, stabilizing and manipulating Attributeless Energy. The technique allows the practitioner to assign characteristics to otherwise neutral Energy and use it to reproduce or supplement specialized powers.[43][52] Its explicitly established proficiency stages are Beginner, Proficient and Master. [[Scathach]] describes the technique itself as being above their existing Grandmaster-class [[Martial Arts]]; "Grandmaster" is not a fourth Creator's Energy stage.[52]

### Divine, Primordial and Eldritch Existences
Term
Meaning
God
A God is a Divine Being whose Soul contains Divinity and one or more Divine Concepts.[7] A God can be born Divine or ascend from another race. Godhood alone does not establish equal power: ordinary Gods, [[Combat Gods]], [[God-Kings]] and [[Primordial Gods]] can differ by enormous amounts.
Apotheosis / Divine Ascension
Divine Ascension is the process through which a sufficiently developed non-God awakens genuine Divinity. Major requirements demonstrated by the story include Soul maturation, self-understanding, acceptance of one's nature and compatibility with an emerging Concept.[53][54][55] [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s awakening of fifteen Divinities simultaneously was an extreme anomaly.[56][57]
[[Demigod]]
A Demigod is a being possessing partial Divine lineage or characteristics without necessarily being a full God. The exact origin varies; children or descendants of Gods can possess Divine traits while remaining distinct from a fully ascended Divine Being. Late Imperial examples include descendants such as [[Nikolai Francis Nichev]], whose ancestry traced back to [[Dagda]].[27]
God-King
A God-King is primarily the God who rules or represents a Pantheon.[58] The title is political and Divine rather than one perfectly fixed combat tier. Individual God-Kings can differ enormously according to Divinity, Pantheon support, artifacts, experience and racial nature.
Second-Generation Primordial God
A Second-Generation Primordial God is a Primordial-class God belonging to a later Divine generation or equivalent level of development. Kronus is one of the clearest examples.[59] Victor's Blood Dragon Primogenitor state was separately compared to this general level after Chapter 803.[48]
First-Generation / Older Primordial God
A First-Generation or Older Primordial God refers to the earliest and generally most developed Gods forming the ancient foundation of a Pantheon, such as [[Gaia]], Nyx, [[Erebus]] and [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]]. Later Author explanations distinguish these older Primordial Gods from both their descendants and true [[Primordial Entities]].
Peak Primordial God
A Peak Primordial God is a conventional Divine being who has advanced far beyond ordinary Primordial God baselines but has not necessarily undergone the qualitative Soul transformation required for Primordial-Entity existence. The term describes very high Divine development rather than a completed transformation into an Aspect of Creation.
Quasi-Primordial / Semi-Primordial
A Quasi-Primordial or Semi-Primordial is an incomplete transitional state between advanced Primordial Godhood and completed Primordial-Entity existence. Kali is explicitly described as approaching this condition while her Soul and Destruction become increasingly integrated with the greater Concept.[60]
Primordial Entity
A Primordial Entity, Primordial Being or Primordial is an ontological state in which the Soul itself becomes a Concept and integrates with an Aspect of Existence.[4] This is fundamentally different from simply being an extremely powerful Primordial God. Complete destruction of such an existence can require affecting the underlying Aspect itself.[6]
[[Regulators of Existence]]
The Regulators of Existence are the group of Primordial Entities occupying administrative roles responsible for fundamental mechanisms of Victor's Universe. They include authorities associated with functions such as Life, Soul, Death, Infinity and Balance and possess extraordinarily high System Authority.[29][6] Primordial Entity is the state of existence; Regulator is the administrative group/role. The terms should not be treated as universally identical.
Primordial [[Immortality]]
Primordial Immortality is the abnormal persistence resulting from a completed Primordial's integration with an Aspect. Azathoth explains that erasing the individual in the same manner as an ordinary God is insufficient if the underlying Aspect continues to exist; completely eliminating the Primordial can require erasing that fundamental function from Creation.[6]
Outer God
An Outer God is an Eldritch existence whose nature does not conform normally to the native cosmological framework. Azathoth originated in another Cosmos, while Victor became an Outer God despite being born within his current Creation, proving that "Outer God" cannot be defined only as "something physically born outside the Universe".[61][6]
Eldritch [[Chaos]]
An Eldritch Chaos is the foundational Chaos of an [[Eldritch Pantheon]]. Victor and Azathoth are explicitly identified as beings of this class.[61][12] An Eldritch Chaos can serve as a conceptual source for subordinate Eldritch Gods and possesses a fundamentally corruptive, contradictory nature foreign to ordinary native Creation.
Eldritch Primordial God
An Eldritch Primordial God is an Eldritch God whose starting existence is comparable to the original Primordial Gods of a conventional Pantheon. [[Yog-Sothoth]] Elderblood is the principal established example.[12]
Eldritch God
An Eldritch God is a Divine-level Eldritch Being whose conceptual nature can draw directly from an Eldritch Chaos instead of the native Primordial framework. [[Nero Elderblood]], [[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]] and [[Akasha Elderblood]] demonstrate different versions of this relationship.[11][12][62]
Eldritch Domain
An Eldritch Domain is a conceptual authority originating through an Eldritch source rather than through ordinary native Divinity. Akasha explicitly states that she does not possess a normal native Divinity; her Domain of Control exists through her connection to Victor, while her contradictory nature also produced Loss of Control.[62]
Progenitor
A Progenitor is an origin point or supreme foundational ancestor of a supernatural race or Bloodline. Progenitors can create or propagate members of their lineage and possess exceptional authority over the characteristics connected to that lineage.[63] Progenitor is a racial/origin classification and does not automatically mean God.
Primogenitor
Primogenitor is terminology used for an original first Progenitor or foundational source of a newly established lineage. Victor receives this designation after becoming the origin of the Blood Dragon/Draconic Vampire race.[48] The title describes origin and lineage rather than one universal combat tier.
Higher-Dimensional Being
A Higher-Dimensional Being is an existence described as having grown beyond the Reality which previously contained it. Azathoth uses the concept while explaining the future development of an Eldritch Chaos.[61] The novel does not provide a complete numbered "5-D/6-D/etc." hierarchy, so external dimensional labels should not be assigned without direct textual support.
Multiversal Being
A Multiversal Being is a state Azathoth associates with an Eldritch Chaos growing until its previous Reality can no longer contain its existence.[61] The term does not by itself mean omnipotence, omniscience or unrestricted control over every possible Reality.

### Pantheons and Divine Governance
Term
Meaning
Pantheon
A Pantheon is a structured Divine civilization rather than merely "a list of Gods". Depending upon context it can refer to its Gods, political faction, Divine hierarchy, celestial and infernal territories, afterlife infrastructure and associated populations. Dragon's Nest was formally registered by the greater System as a genuine Pantheon despite containing numerous races rather than only Gods.[64]
Great Pantheon
A Great Pantheon is not simply one of the strongest military Pantheons. Chapter 704 explains that the classification is tied to the development of a sufficiently significant Hell and Soul flow for the Judges to integrate that afterlife into the greater Soul System, producing a true Ruler and Death-God administration.[36] Earth historically possessed Seven Great Pantheons.
Minor Pantheon
A Minor Pantheon is a smaller Divine civilization lacking some of the full supernatural infrastructure possessed by the Great Pantheons. The [[Celtic Pantheon]], for example, possessed Divine territory but lacked the complete independent Ruler structure of the Seven Great Pantheons.[36][65]
[[Eldritch Pantheon]]
An Eldritch Pantheon is a conceptual Divine lineage founded upon an Eldritch [[Chaos]] rather than being completely dependent upon the native Divine system. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] compares himself and Azathoth to the foundational Chaos, Yog to an Eldritch Primordial God and [[Nero]]/Ophis to later Eldritch Goddesses.[12]
Pantheon Domain
A Pantheon Domain is the supernatural advantage or territorial power produced when a God fights while supported by the Pantheon/Realm with which they are deeply connected. [[Zeus]] and Ra demonstrate that this support can greatly alter the effective power of a Pantheon ruler.[66][67][68]
Territory / Divine Territory
Territory refers to an area over which a supernatural authority has imposed control or influence. Territory can be established through a Pantheon, Ruler, Divine Concept or enormous Energy investment. It should not automatically be treated as identical to an Eldritch Domain or to a character's normal physical country.[69]
God-King
The principal celestial leader or representative of a Pantheon. See [[God-Kings]].[58]
Ruler
The administrator of a sufficiently developed Hell or [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]]. See Ruler.[1][37]
Dragon's Nest
Dragon's Nest is Victor's System-recognized Pantheon and direct Divine faction. It should not be treated as synonymous with the [[Elderblood Empire]], which is a much larger political civilization containing ordinary citizens, planets, military organizations and even other politically subordinate Pantheons.[64][70][51]

### Creation, Chaos and the Cosmic Cycle
Term
Meaning
[[Primordial Chaos]]
Primordial Chaos is the Creator or supreme foundational [[Chaos]] associated with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s native Cosmos. Creator's Energy explanations compare Primordial Chaos to the Painter and Attributeless Energy to the Paint used to establish differentiated Creation.[42] It occupies a more fundamental cosmological position than an ordinary Primordial Aspect.
Creator
A Creator in the highest cosmological sense is an existence capable of establishing an entire cosmological system and its governing framework. Azathoth demonstrates that separate Cosmoses can possess different Rules, Primordials and their own corresponding Chaos.[6] A God who can create matter, species, planets or Dimensions is not automatically a Creator at this scale.
Chaos
Chaos is used in several different contexts. Primordial Chaos is the foundational Creator of Victor's native Cosmos. Eldritch Chaos is the class/nature of beings such as Victor and Azathoth. Victor's earlier title God of Chaos instead described the contradictory collection of native powers awakening within him. These uses should not be merged simply because they share the same word.[57][61][12]
Creation
Creation is a broad term for structured existence belonging to a cosmological system. Depending upon context it can encompass material space, Dimensions, Souls, Concepts, Laws, civilizations and the systems maintaining them. It can also be used more narrowly in dialogue, so scale must be inferred from context.[6]
Cosmic Cycle
The Cosmic Cycle is the intended long-term progression of a properly functioning Universe. Balance explains that the Primordials maintain Creation until its natural End; [[True Death]] then gathers what remains, while Primordial Chaos is intended to use the remaining Essence to begin another cycle.[15] Victor's current Cosmos is abnormal because its original Primordial Chaos is absent from the supreme position.
Throne of the Universe
The Throne of the Universe is the vacant supreme position above the ordinary Primordial administrators of Victor's Cosmos. Later material increasingly treats the absence of its intended Chaos as a structural problem and as an objective contested by extremely high-order beings.[6][71][27]
Creation Divinity
A Creation Divinity is a Divine Concept allowing a God to create or construct phenomena within the existing cosmological framework. It should not be confused with the state of being the Creator of an entire Cosmos. The difference is comparable to manipulating a function inside a system versus establishing the system and its fundamental Rules.

### Runes, Language and Supernatural Techniques
Term
Meaning
Blessing
A Blessing is a supernatural boon granted by another supernatural being. Its properties depend entirely upon the giver and the Blessing itself. Blessings can provide protection, affinity, power or access to abilities but are not automatically Divinities.[72][23]
Runes
Runes are supernatural symbols which impose a function through language, Intent and Energy. Early Rune instruction establishes that reproducing the visible symbol alone is insufficient: the user must understand the associated language and properly impose the desired Intent.[73] At higher stages, Runes are described as being comparable to the source code of the Universe, allowing sufficiently advanced users to interact directly with the structures underlying Reality.[74]
Intent
Intent is the intended meaning or purpose the practitioner imposes upon a supernatural technique. It is essential to Rune usage: a correctly drawn Rune without the proper Intent does not necessarily reproduce the original function.[73]
Ancient Language
The Ancient Language is a supernatural language used in the construction and understanding of many Runes. Correct words or symbols remain only one component; Energy and Intent are also required.[73]
Speech of Power / Dragon Language
Speech of Power, later commonly treated as Dragon Language, is the Draconic method of imposing will through supernatural language. The early explanation describes the principle as combining Energy, Voice and Intention.[75] By the late Imperial Era, powerful Dragons can use such language to directly impose changes upon Reality.
Draconic Runes
Draconic Runes are the Runic system developed through Draconic language and authority. They are especially suited to imposing structured instructions upon Reality. [[Scathach]] later explains that a Dragon does not require a Rune for every simple use of Energy; the Runes are most valuable when the Dragon wants to impose a deliberate conceptual instruction or rewrite upon Reality.[74][76]
Source Code of Reality
Source Code of Reality is the computational metaphor used for the deeper rule structure governing Reality. Advanced Rune theory compares Runes to the Universe's source code,[74] while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later explains that his daughters' inherited sight can eventually perceive and manipulate this underlying "code" when sufficiently developed.[77] The term should be treated as the story's own explanatory model rather than imported computer-science mechanics.
Grandmaster
A Grandmaster is an exceptionally advanced Martial Artist whose art has progressed beyond ordinary perfect technique and can begin interacting with conceptual principles. Rose and Scathach demonstrate that Grandmaster weapon arts can carry Conceptual qualities.[78] Reaching a higher state of existence can allow already-developed Grandmasters to continue progressing beyond barriers which previously limited them.[79]
Higher-Level Technique
A Higher-Level Technique is the classification Scathach applies to Victor's Attributeless-Energy method because it operates above their existing Grandmaster-class techniques.[52] This does not establish a formal universal "Above Grandmaster" [[Martial Arts]] rank for every weapon discipline.

### Bloodlines and Supernatural Society Terms
Term
Meaning
Bloodline
A Bloodline is a hereditary supernatural lineage carrying racial information, abilities and traits between generations. Some Bloodlines primarily affect the body, while others reach deeply enough to alter or become integrated into the Soul. Clan Adrasteia's Monster ritual directly demonstrates that sufficiently profound Bloodline alteration can change both body and Soul.[80][81]
Clan
A Clan is a familial and political supernatural lineage organized around shared ancestry, Bloodline, territory or service. Noble Vampire society uses Clans as major hereditary institutions, although other species can organize their lineages differently.
Noble Vampire
A Noble Vampire belongs to one of the hereditary Vampire lineages whose Bloodlines carry established supernatural characteristics and political status. Noble Blood can be used in regulated Vampire-transformation rituals, and the Clan Head's Blood historically played an important role in creating new Vampires.[82]
Plebeian Vampire
A Plebeian Vampire is generally a transformed Vampire who does not naturally belong to one of the old hereditary Noble Bloodlines. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was expected to become a Plebeian through his transformation, but the abnormal ritual, his Blood and the three Noble heiresses produced an unprecedented result.[83]
Vampire Count / Pillar
A Vampire Count is one of the great rulers historically serving as a Pillar of [[Nightingale]]. The office is functional rather than merely aristocratic: Counts control territories, armies and responsibilities required to support the kingdom.[84][85] Victor became the Fifth Pillar with responsibility for exploration and discovery.[86]
[[Sanguis Noctis Regis]]
Sanguis Noctis Regis, the Blood of the Night King, is the supernatural Human blood associated with the origins of the Vampire Progenitors. Vlad identifies Victor's RH-null/Golden Blood as this rare blood type and uses it to explain several of Victor's abnormal characteristics after transformation.[83]
Godslayer / God Hunter
A Godslayer or God Hunter is a descriptive designation for a being capable of genuinely threatening or killing Gods despite not necessarily being a God themselves. [[Scathach]]'s skill, preparation and anti-Divine weapon development allowed her to enter this category while still fundamentally originating as a Vampire.[87][88] The term is a capability description rather than one universal species or exact power rank.

### World Trees, Planetary Structures and Imperial Cores
Important naming distinction: a natural planetary Core, the Elderblood [[World Core]] artifact, a biological [[Dragon Heart]] and the Empire's artificial Dragon Heart are four different things.
Term
Meaning
World Tree
A World Tree is a supernatural life-support entity associated with the development of planets, civilizations and their spiritual systems. [[World Trees]] can nourish worlds, support supernatural ecosystems and participate in creating or sustaining afterlife Dimensions.[35] The mature planet and Tree can develop a reciprocal Energy relationship rather than the Tree indefinitely supporting the world alone.[89]
World Tree Seed / Seedling
A World Tree Seed or Seedling is an immature World Tree capable of developing alongside a suitable world. By Chapter 1088 the [[Elderblood Empire]] had encountered hundreds of seedlings during Galactic exploration, proving that the four World Trees first known to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] were not unique exceptions.[90]
World Tree Network
The World Tree Network is the Sector-wide collection of World Tree connections later exploited by the Elderblood Empire. When Victor expanded his Cosmic Consciousness throughout his Sector, millions of World Tree connections answered him. Their locations became anchors for mapping, teleportation, logistics and colonization.[91]
Planetary Core
A Planetary Core is the natural energetic/structural heart of a world. The planet and its World Tree can function as a connected living system, and sufficiently severe draining of the world's underlying Energy can cause planetary decline or death.[89] This natural Core should not be confused with the Imperial artifact called a World Core.
[[City Core]]
A City Core is an Elderblood Divine/technological artifact developed to supervise and control a defined urban territory. Its demonstrated functions include surveillance, access control and automated territorial administration.[92] It later became the predecessor of the planetary World Core.
Imperial World Core
The Elderblood World Core is a planetary-scale Imperial artifact created by upgrading the City Core. It administers an entire planet, integrates planetary infrastructure with its World Tree and distributes Energy supplied through the greater Imperial network.[93] It is an artificial planetary administrator, not the natural physical/metaphysical Core of the planet.
Biological Dragon Heart
A Dragon Heart in Draconic biology is the extraordinary Energy-producing heart of a True Dragon. It is explicitly compared to an Energy reactor and supports the enormous output required by Draconic physiology.[47] Victor later develops increasingly advanced Dragon Hearts as his Draconic race evolves.[94][48]
Imperial Dragon Heart
The Imperial Dragon Heart is a separate artificial living Energy structure created in Chapter 1004. Victor transformed Velnorah and Velina's prototype using Divine materials, Draconic Runes, [[Junketsu]]-related reinforcement and a fragment of his own Soul. The resulting artifact generates vast quantities of largely unaltered Energy which can then be given whatever properties Imperial systems require.[95]

### Cosmological Geography
Term
Meaning
Cosmos
A Cosmos is a complete cosmological system. Azathoth's explanation represents separate Cosmoses as possessing their own Universe, Rules, Primordials and [[Chaos]].[6] It therefore exists at a much greater conceptual scale than an individual planet, Pantheon, Dimension or Sector.
Universe
The Universe is the enormous native cosmic expanse containing Sectors, galaxies, civilizations, Dimensions and the systems governed by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s current Creation. The novel also uses "Universe" conversationally for smaller self-contained local Realities, so the word alone should not be treated as an exact size statement.
Local Reality
A Local Reality is a useful description for a bounded cosmological environment such as a Sector. Individual Sectors can be described in Universe/Cosmos terminology from the perspective of inhabitants while still remaining subdivisions of the greater Universe. Chapter 956 explicitly establishes that Sectors divide the larger Universe.[96]
Reality
Reality generally means an existing structure or state of Creation. The word is scale-dependent: it can refer to local physical Reality, an artificial world, a Sector-sized environment or a far larger cosmological structure. It is not automatically synonymous with Universe, Dimension or Timeline.
Dimension
A Dimension is a spatial Realm distinct from normal material space. Pantheons, afterlives, artificial training spaces and private worlds can exist as independent Dimensions within a larger Creation.[97] Several Dimensions can coexist inside a larger system.[98]
Pocket Dimension
A Pocket Dimension is a smaller artificial or enclosed dimensional space existing within and using the broader framework of its host Reality. Later Elderblood teachings distinguish creating such subordinate spaces from creating a genuinely independent Reality with its own sufficiently complete internal foundations.[77]
Independent Reality
An Independent Reality is more self-contained than an ordinary pocket dimension and requires a sufficiently complete internal structure to remain stable. During Victor's lessons to his daughters, he distinguishes merely reproducing matter or stars from establishing the conceptual Rules required for a genuine stable Reality.[77][99]
[[Dimensional Void]]
The Dimensional Void, or Void Between Dimensions, is the interdimensional expanse separating and connecting Dimensions belonging to the same greater Creation.[97] Powerful beings can navigate it in search of other dimensions; beings unable to do so can become stranded until they encounter another Realm.[34]
Nothingness
Nothingness, also called the Space of Nothingness, is the incomprehensible expanse surrounding or existing beyond the coherent influence of the [[Akashic Records]]. Away from the Records, normal chronological and conceptual relationships cease behaving conventionally; even Akashic risks becoming permanently lost if she moves too far from the Record structure.[30]
[[Void Between Cosmoses]]
The Void Between Cosmoses is the expanse separating completely independent cosmological systems. Azathoth illustrates it using two separate Cosmoses, each with different Rules, Primordials and Chaos.[6] It is not the same as the internal Dimensional Void.
Conceptual Void Between Multiverses
Conceptual Void Between Multiverses is terminology used when Victor discusses existences capable of originating from beyond his Multiverse.[100] The novel has not formally confirmed that it is exactly identical to the Void Between Cosmoses, so the two names should remain distinct unless a later chapter explicitly equates them.
[[Edge of the Cosmos]]
The Edge of the Cosmos is the frontier where the Universe continues expanding and developing new regions. It represents the side of Creation where new cosmic structures continue to form rather than a confirmed doorway into another independent Cosmos.[101]
The Abyss
The Abyss is a deep region of the Universe containing areas where galaxies have reached their genuine End.[102] These dead regions can lack Life, Time, Space and even the ordinary Concept of Void.[39] The Abyss is inhabited by beings adapted to those conditions and exists under the broader supervision of [[True Death]], although Death did not create it.[40]

### Sectors and Cosmic Regions
Term
Meaning
Sector
A Sector is a vast bounded region of the greater Universe established within the Primordial cosmological structure. Sectors can contain numerous galaxies, planets, [[World Trees]], Pantheons, civilizations and Dimensions.[96] They are large enough to function as local Realities and can differ enormously in age and development.
Sector Barrier
A Sector Barrier is the metaphysical boundary separating one Sector from surrounding cosmic regions. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] encounters the boundary during his large-scale surveys, and his Cosmic Consciousness later expands until it reaches the outer barrier of his Mid-Level Sector.[22][91] A perception feat which stops at this barrier should not automatically be treated as perception of the complete greater Universe.
Low-Level Sector
A Low-Level Sector is a comparatively underdeveloped Sector. [[Nightingale]] and Samar are explicitly associated with separate Low-Level Sector environments.[103] "Low-Level" describes the developmental state of the region rather than requiring every individual inhabitant to be weak.
Mid-Level Sector
A Mid-Level Sector is a more developed multi-Galactic Sector. Victor's native Sector had reached this general stage and was continuing to advance as its civilizations, World Trees, supernatural systems and Overlord developed.[90][91]
Upper / Higher Sectors
Upper or Higher Sectors are the highly advanced regions beyond Victor's native Sector which contain civilizations and rulers vastly beyond ordinary lower-region standards. By Chapters 1134–1135 Victor's reconnaissance had confirmed Solar-System-destroying weapons, extraordinarily advanced civilizations and rulers such as <Light>, <Darkness>, <Emperor> and <Law>.[49][100] The terms should be preserved as used by the story rather than forcing an unsupported exact equivalence between every "High-Level" and "Upper" reference.
Sector Development
Sector Development describes the collective evolution of a Sector rather than the personal strength of its strongest inhabitant. World Trees, Life, civilizations, Pantheons, technology, supernatural development, conceptual growth and the rise of beings capable of dominating the region all contribute to its condition.[103][90]
Overlord
An Overlord is a being who has established overwhelming dominance over the major powers of a Sector and effectively stands at the top of its local political/supernatural order.[104][105][19] Since Sectors differ radically in development, two Overlords are not automatically equal combatants.
Cosmic Consciousness
Cosmic Consciousness is the term used for Victor's enormous expanded awareness once ordinary "Divine Consciousness" becomes inadequate. In Chapter 1136 his awareness spreads throughout his Sector until it encounters the Sector barrier, allowing him to perceive its galaxies and millions of World Tree connections.[91] It is an extremely large perception ability, not unrestricted omniscience.

### Political and Civilization Terms
Term
Meaning
Emperor
An Emperor is a ruler whose sovereignty has expanded beyond ordinary kingship into civilization-scale authority. The title concerns rulership and institutional control rather than one mandatory personal power level.[109] [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later God-Emperor status combines enormous personal power with civilization-wide sovereignty, but "Emperor" itself remains a title rather than a biological evolution.
Overlord
An Overlord is the dominant ruler of a Sector. Victor becomes recognized as the effective Overlord of his Sector after unifying Earth's major supernatural powers and creating an order capable of standing above the region's former competing factions.[19]
Demon King
A Demon King is the supreme ruler of a major Demonic realm or civilization. The title should not automatically be equated with Ruler: [[Diablo]] was Demon King of Hell, while the Ruler position concerns recognized metaphysical administration of an afterlife system.
Vampire Count
One of [[Nightingale]]'s major Pillars. See Bloodlines and Supernatural Society Terms.
Herald
A Herald is a supernatural representative acting directly for a greater master. Victor's late Imperial Heralds function as his eyes, ears and hands; War can receive Victor's perception, carry his commands and even serve as a vessel through which the Emperor directly acts.[27] The exact abilities of each Herald depend upon how that being was created.
Herald of [[Chaos]]
A Herald of Chaos is one of the beings reconstructed to serve beneath Victor's Chaos-aligned structure. Thomas, Jimmy, Lucy and Karen were converted into Heralds rather than simply being executed, becoming servants operating beneath War and Victor.[110][5]
Kardashev Scale
The novel uses an expanded fictional Kardashev Scale for civilizations capable of controlling progressively greater astronomical and cosmological resources.[100] Its higher classifications go far beyond the original real-world scientific proposal, so external Kardashev definitions should not be substituted for the novel's own descriptions.
Civilization Level
Civilization Level measures the collective development of a society: Energy production, technology, population, infrastructure, science, supernatural integration, territorial scale and ability to manipulate large cosmic structures.[100] It is not equivalent to the personal power of every citizen.
[[Elderblood Empire]]
The Elderblood Empire is Victor's multi-racial political civilization. It encompasses ordinary citizens, planets, [[World Trees]], military forces, technology and multiple supernatural traditions. It is broader than Dragon's Nest, which is Victor's direct Pantheon.[70][51]

### Important Distinctions
Terms
Difference
Concept / Divinity
The Concept is the principle. Divinity is the Divine structure through which a God connects to and exercises that Concept.[7]
Divinity / [[Divine Energy]]
Divinity is conceptual Authority. Divine Energy is Energy used by a Divine Being.
Concept Mastery / Raw Power
More Energy does not automatically mean deeper understanding of the Concept.[9]
Primordial God / Primordial Entity
A Primordial God remains a God wielding Divinity. A Primordial Entity's Soul becomes integrated with a Concept and Aspect of Existence.[4]
Primordial Entity / Regulator of Existence
Primordial Entity is an ontological state. Regulators are the established Primordial administrators of the current Universe.
[[Primordial Energy]] / Primordial Entity
Using Primordial Energy does not automatically transform the user's Soul into a Concept.[42]
[[Primordial Chaos]] / Eldritch [[Chaos]]
Primordial Chaos is the foundational Creator of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s native Cosmos. Eldritch Chaos is the Outer-God class represented by Victor and Azathoth.[61][6]
God of Chaos / Eldritch Chaos
Victor's God of Chaos title reflects his contradictory native Divinities. His Eldritch Chaos nature is a deeper Outer-God state.[57][12]
Creator / Creation Divinity
A cosmological Creator establishes a complete Cosmos and its Rules. A Creation Divinity operates within an already existing cosmological structure.
Attributeless Energy / Primordial Energy
Attributeless Energy lacks specialized characteristics. Primordial Energy is a characterized Energy which can be reproduced from it with sufficient understanding.[43]
Creator's Energy / Primordial Chaos
Creator's Energy is the "paint". Primordial Chaos is the "painter".[42]
[[Eldritch Energy]] / Attributeless Energy
Eldritch Energy carries foreign Eldritch characteristics. Attributeless Energy is defined specifically by the absence of assigned characteristics.
Soul / Soul Core
The Soul is the greater metaphysical existence. The Soul Core is one of its deepest central structures.
Soul / Record
The Soul is the spiritual existence. The Record is preserved information concerning that existence.[21]
Soul / Essence
Soul refers to the individual's spiritual existence. Essence refers more broadly to transferable qualities or portions of an existence; the story has not declared the two interchangeable.
Ego / Aspect
The Ego is the consciousness/self. A Primordial Aspect is the greater Conceptual function with which that Ego may be integrated.[6]
Faith / Divinity
Faith is belief capable of producing supernatural power and influencing Divine development. Divinity is the God's conceptual structure.[18][19]
Blessing / Divinity
A Blessing is power granted by another being. Possessing a Blessing does not automatically make the recipient a God.
Native Divinity / Eldritch Domain
Native Divinity belongs to the conceptual framework of the current Creation. An Eldritch Domain can originate directly from an Eldritch Chaos and operate outside portions of the native framework.[11][62]
Destruction / END
Destruction can remove matter and structures while leaving deeper Records and cosmic context. END can reach a more fundamental termination.[14]
Ordinary Destruction / True End
Ordinary destruction can leave matter, Energy, Space-Time context or records behind. A true End can remove the ordinary framework itself.[39]
Death / [[True Death]]
Death can mean ordinary death or a Divine Concept. True Death is the universal Primordial authority associated with final Death/End.
Timeline / Dimension
A Timeline is a sequence of history and possibilities. A Dimension is a spatial Realm. They are not interchangeable.[29]
Dimension / Sector
A Dimension can exist inside a Sector. A Sector is an enormous local Reality which can contain galaxies, planets, Pantheons and multiple Dimensions.
Sector / Greater Universe
A Sector is a bounded subdivision/local Reality. The greater Universe contains the Sector structure.[96]
[[Dimensional Void]] / Abyss
The Dimensional Void connects/separates Dimensions. The Abyss contains regions of Creation that have genuinely reached their End.[39]
Dimensional Void / [[Void Between Cosmoses]]
The Dimensional Void exists internally between Dimensions belonging to one greater Creation. The Void Between Cosmoses separates independent cosmological systems.[6]
Nothingness / Abyss
Nothingness lies beyond coherent Akashic influence. The Abyss remains a region of the Universe whose contents reached their true End.[30][39]
Planetary Core / Imperial [[World Core]]
Planetary Core is a natural structure of a world. Imperial World Core is an Elderblood planetary administration artifact.[93][89]
Biological [[Dragon Heart]] / Imperial Dragon Heart
The biological Dragon Heart is a Draconic organ and Energy reactor. The Imperial Dragon Heart is an artificial living Energy generator created in Chapter 1004.[47][95]
God-King / Ruler
God-King generally governs the celestial Pantheon. Ruler governs the corresponding Hell or [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]].
Pantheon / Empire
A Pantheon is a Divine civilization/system. An Empire can politically contain several Pantheons, races, planets and non-Divine populations.[51]
Emperor / Overlord
Emperor concerns civilization-scale sovereignty. Overlord concerns effective dominance over a Sector.
Authority Rank / Threat Level
Authority Rank measures System access. Threat Level measures operational danger. Neither should automatically replace the other.[29][106]
Threat Level / Attack Potency
Threat Level considers total operational capability. A V-05 being does not need every casual attack to destroy a Solar System.[51]
Civilization Level / Individual Strength
Civilization Level measures a society's total development. It does not imply every individual citizen possesses corresponding combat power.
Unique Being / Omnipresence
Being unique across timelines means there is only one active counterpart of that existence. It does not mean the being is simultaneously physically present everywhere.[32][29]
Cosmic Consciousness / Omniscience
Cosmic Consciousness can grant enormous perception range, but Victor's demonstrated awareness still reached a Sector barrier rather than unrestricted knowledge of everything.[91]

### Notes
- This page is a glossary. Individual main articles should provide the full history, mechanics, applications and exceptions of each subject.
- Terms have been separated whenever the novel uses the same word for different mechanisms.
- Primordial Entity and Regulator of Existence should not be treated as exact synonyms. The first is a state of existence; the second is a specific administrative role/group.[4][6]
- [[World Core]] should not be used as the name of a planet's natural Core when discussing Elderblood technology. The Imperial World Core is an artificial planetary administrator created from the [[City Core]].[93]
- The biological [[Dragon Heart]] and Chapter 1004 Imperial Dragon Heart are separate structures.[47][95]
- Authority does not possess one universal meaning. System Authority, Conceptual Authority, Ruler Authority and political authority describe different things.
- Domain likewise changes meaning according to context.
- Shared use of the word Primordial does not make [[Primordial Chaos]], [[Primordial Energy]], [[Primordial Gods]] and [[Primordial Entities]] equivalent.
- Shared use of [[Chaos]] does not make Primordial Chaos, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s God of Chaos title and Eldritch Chaos equivalent.
- Outer God is not defined purely by birthplace. Victor develops into one despite originating within the native Creation.[61]
- An Eldritch Chaos acting as the source of another being's Concept does not automatically prove that the Chaos personally possesses every specialized Domain expressed by that subordinate being.[11][12]
- Kassandra being born as Destruction demonstrates that a being can possess an unusually conceptual nature without automatically being identical to a completed universal Primordial Entity.[14]
- The novel's words Universe, Cosmos, Reality and world are sometimes conversational rather than strict scale labels. Chapter context must determine the intended scope.
- Sector barriers are particularly important when interpreting "Universe-wide" perception from inside an isolated Sector.[91]
- The [[Magic]] World is directly useful for this distinction: it is described with "Universe" terminology while later being treated as a distinct Sector.[111][103]
- The exact numerical size of a Sector is not fixed. Victor's Mid-Level Sector contains numerous galaxies and millions of World Tree connections.[91]
- The exact relationship between the Conceptual Void Between Multiverses of Chapter 1135 and the [[Void Between Cosmoses]] explained in Chapter 1075 remains unconfirmed.[100][6]
- Attributeless Energy being theoretically convertible into another Energy does not mean the user automatically knows the characteristics necessary to reproduce every Energy in existence.[43]
- [[Creator's Energy Mastery]] is a Higher-Level Technique, not proof of a separately named universal [[Martial Arts]] stage above Grandmaster.[52]
- Faith can influence Divine development, but collective belief does not simply invent the underlying universal Concept from nothing.[19]
- A dead God's Concept does not ordinarily cease to exist merely because that God dies; the Divine connection normally returns to the greater conceptual structure.[112]
- Victor's ability to retain absorbed Divinities is exceptional and should not be presented as a normal property of all Gods.[113]
- A Ruler possesses enormous jurisdiction in their legitimate [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] but is not automatically omnipotent in every independent territory contained inside that larger Realm.[38]
- Great Pantheon status is tied to developed afterlife infrastructure rather than simply military strength.[36]
- Threat Codes measure broad operational danger. They are intentionally not perfectly precise statistics, particularly from Galactic scales upward.[106]
- V-06 contains a particularly wide range: an ordinary Galaxy-level threat and a being capable of threatening several galaxies can still fall within the same broad classification.[106]
- Cosmic Consciousness is not omniscience.[91]
- Unique existence across timelines is not omnipresence.[32][29]
- The removal of Destiny is why fixed predetermined future history should not be assumed to continue operating as it once did.[31][30]
- The current Main Timeline remains capable of producing possible futures even though those possibilities are no longer simultaneously active alternate worlds.[30]
- Chapter 1168 adds an important limitation to creation feats: producing convincing stars, matter or even an Eldritch Galaxy is not necessarily the same as creating a completely independent stable Universe. Victor explicitly distinguishes his artificial replica from a true self-sustaining cosmological creation because it lacked the complete conceptual foundation supplied by a supreme Chaos.[99]
