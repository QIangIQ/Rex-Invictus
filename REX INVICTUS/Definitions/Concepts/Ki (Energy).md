Ki is a supernatural form of Internal Energy derived directly from the vitality, or Life Force, naturally possessed by living beings. Unlike [[Senjutsu]], which draws upon [[Natural Energy]] circulating through the environment, Ki uses the practitioner's own life-sustaining Energy as the medium for supernatural techniques. Every living being possesses an internal source of Vitality, but consciously accessing and manipulating that Energy requires training, discipline and sufficient control to prevent the expenditure of Life Force from damaging the practitioner.[1][2]
Ki was identified as a distinct supernatural discipline through the research conducted by the Yokais under Otsuki Elderblood Haruna. Following the unification of Japan's principal Youkai factions, Haruna sought a way to overcome the limitations of Youki, the negative emotional Energy traditionally used by her people. Cooperation between the three great Youkai Clans led to the investigation of the planetary Dragon Veins and the discovery of Senjutsu. Once Haruna learned to absorb Natural Energy and circulate it through her body, she became capable of perceiving and manipulating her own Vitality, establishing the foundation of Ki.[1]
Although the two disciplines emerged from the same research, Ki and Senjutsu are not identical. Senjutsu employs the Natural Energy of the environment, while Ki employs the Life Force already existing within the practitioner. Their relationship is complementary: Senjutsu can support the body and reduce the Vitality consumed when employing Ki, allowing supernatural techniques to be sustained without depending exclusively upon the practitioner's finite personal reserves.[1][2]
The principal danger of Ki lies in the direct relationship between its Energy source and the user's lifespan. Excessive or undisciplined use can consume the practitioner's remaining Vitality, potentially resulting in death even among extremely long-lived supernatural species such as Vampires and Youkai. Consequently, Ki is not merely another source of combat power but a discipline requiring careful management of the Energy sustaining the practitioner's own existence.[1]

### Nature and Origin

#### Internal Life Energy
Ki originates from the Vitality naturally present within living beings. This Energy is intrinsically connected to the continued functioning of the organism and exists independently of whether an individual has learned to manipulate it through supernatural techniques. The development of Ki allows a practitioner to consciously access this internal resource rather than relying entirely upon Energy obtained from an external environment.[1]
The distinction between possessing Vitality and mastering Ki is fundamental. Living beings naturally possess the Energy necessary to sustain their existence, but that does not mean every individual can automatically channel it into supernatural techniques. Ki cultivation involves becoming aware of this internal resource and developing sufficient control to employ it deliberately without consuming an excessive portion of the user's Life Force.[1][2]
Unlike Youki, which originates from negative Human emotions, Ki does not require a particular concentration of environmental negativity. Its source exists within the practitioner, making it potentially accessible even in regions lacking the conditions necessary for traditional Youkai Energy cultivation. However, this independence introduces a different limitation: the Energy being expended belongs to the practitioner's own vitality rather than to an external supply that can be absorbed without directly sacrificing personal Life Force.[1]

#### Classification
Ki is a form of supernatural Energy associated with Life and Vitality. Within the broader classification of supernatural powers, it belongs among the attributed energies available to living beings and is distinguished from Mana, Youki, Miasma and [[Senjutsu]] through its conventional source and method of utilization.[1][3]
Its classification as Internal Energy does not mean it exists outside the greater structure of supernatural Energy. Ki, like the other attributed energies used throughout Creation, shares a more fundamental foundation with other supernatural forces. The practical differences between these systems arise from their attributes, the manner in which they are obtained and the restrictions governing their users.[3]
Ki's relationship with Life also does not automatically grant its users authority over the Divine Concept of Life. Manipulating one's internal Vitality is a different ability from possessing conceptual control over the existence, creation or fundamental principles of living beings.

#### A Sub-Art of Senjutsu
Ki was developed as a Sub-Art of [[Senjutsu]]. This classification reflects the method through which Haruna and her fellow Youkai discovered the discipline rather than indicating that Ki and Senjutsu possess an identical Energy source.[1]
The discovery of Senjutsu introduced Haruna to the [[Natural Energy]] circulating through Earth's planetary environment. By meditating and absorbing that Energy, she became aware of the Vitality within her own body. Once she understood the distinction between the two forms of Energy, she developed a method of using her internal Vitality as a separate medium for supernatural techniques.[1]
Senjutsu consequently serves as the foundation of Haruna's cultivation method, while Ki represents its internal application. The two arts complement one another because the external Energy obtained through Senjutsu can support the practitioner during the expenditure of internal Life Force.[1]

### Discovery and Development

#### The Youkai's Search for Alternative Energy
The discovery of Ki originated from the efforts of Haruna to overcome the weaknesses of traditional Youkai cultivation. Most Youkai rely upon Youki, an Energy produced through the accumulation of negative emotions generated by Humanity. The exceptional concentration of Youki within Japan provided favorable conditions for supernatural development, but ordinary Youkai could not maintain the same level of Energy availability outside regions possessing comparable environmental conditions.[1]
The unification of the principal Youkai factions allowed the three great Clans—Kitsune, Oni and Ryuji—to investigate alternatives collectively. During their research, the Ryuji revealed information concerning the planetary pathways known as Dragon Veins, through which a powerful and unfamiliar form of [[Natural Energy]] circulated.[1]
Haruna investigated one of these locations and discovered that the Energy could be absorbed through meditation. Unlike Youki, the newly discovered power possessed a fundamentally neutral character. Its use required an appropriate mental state and careful regulation, but its origin offered a means of developing supernatural abilities without depending upon Japan's accumulation of negative emotional Energy.[1]

#### Awakening Internal Vitality
Haruna's initial experiments with [[Senjutsu]] gradually revealed a second form of Energy. After learning how to draw [[Natural Energy]] into her body, she became capable of perceiving her own Vitality and understanding its potential applications.[1]
This discovery established the foundation of Ki. Instead of treating the surrounding Natural Energy as the sole resource available to a practitioner, Haruna recognized that the body itself possessed an internal Energy source that could be used to produce supernatural techniques.[1]
The resulting discipline differed from ordinary Senjutsu in both its benefits and its dangers. The external origin of Senjutsu made environmental absorption possible, whereas Ki involved consuming a resource directly connected to the practitioner's continued existence. Proper control was therefore necessary to prevent the pursuit of greater power from destroying the individual employing it.[1]

#### Accessibility
Ki and [[Senjutsu]] possess comparatively broad potential accessibility. Unlike supernatural powers restricted to particular races or Bloodlines, the underlying principles of these disciplines can be learned by beings capable of satisfying their cultivation requirements.[1]
Ki's source exists within living beings rather than within a specialized racial organ or an inherited ability. Nevertheless, access to internal Vitality does not eliminate the need for training, and the cultivation methods developed by Haruna require discipline and control.[1]
The existence of Ki should therefore be distinguished from innate racial abilities. A Vampire, Youkai or other living supernatural being may possess the necessary Vitality to cultivate Ki without automatically knowing how to manipulate that Energy.

### Cultivation and Manipulation

#### Awareness of Vitality
The foundation of Ki cultivation is the practitioner's awareness of their internal Life Force. Haruna developed this sensitivity while experimenting with [[Senjutsu]], discovering that the absorption of external [[Natural Energy]] allowed her to perceive the Vitality already existing within her body.[1]
This awareness provides the basis for conscious manipulation. Rather than treating Vitality solely as a passive characteristic of being alive, the practitioner can employ it as a supernatural resource. The effectiveness of that process depends upon the user's ability to distinguish ordinary bodily vitality from the Energy deliberately expended through a technique.[1]

#### Internal Energy Circulation
Ki techniques involve directing the practitioner's internal Vitality toward a particular supernatural application. The Energy used is drawn from the individual's own reserves rather than being supplied directly by the surrounding environment.[1][2]
Haruna's method incorporates [[Senjutsu]] into this process. She absorbs [[Natural Energy]], filters it to avoid damaging her body and then uses her internal Vitality to produce techniques. This establishes a relationship between environmental Energy intake and the controlled expenditure of Life Force.[2]
- Ki Manipulation: The practitioner can consciously utilize the Vitality naturally existing within their body as a source of supernatural Energy.[1]
- Life-Force Manipulation: Ki allows the user's own Life Force to serve as the medium for supernatural techniques.[1]
- Internal Energy Manipulation: The Energy is supplied through the practitioner's internal reserves rather than obtained directly from the surrounding environment during each application.[1][2]
- Energy Channeling: Ki can be directed toward the execution of techniques, provided that the practitioner possesses sufficient control over its expenditure.[2]
- Energy Regulation: Users must regulate the quantity of Vitality consumed to avoid damaging their lifespan.[1]

#### Mental Discipline
Ki cultivation requires careful mental and energetic control. Haruna emphasized that undisciplined use could rapidly consume the practitioner's lifespan, making proper regulation an essential part of the discipline rather than an optional refinement.[1]
Her research into [[Senjutsu]] also established the importance of maintaining a calm mental state when manipulating unfamiliar Energy. The training she developed therefore combined awareness of internal Vitality with the discipline necessary to control the amount of Energy used.[1]
The dangers of Ki are distinct from those of Senjutsu. Improper Senjutsu use can damage the body and Soul through uncontrolled [[Natural Energy]], whereas Ki directly threatens the individual's lifespan when excessive Vitality is consumed.[1]

#### Energy Expenditure
Every Ki technique requires the expenditure of internal Vitality. This makes Energy conservation particularly important, since the cost of excessive use may extend beyond ordinary fatigue or a temporary reduction in supernatural reserves.[1]
A practitioner who loses control of their consumption can exhaust the Life Force sustaining their existence. The resulting danger applies even to supernatural races that do not ordinarily die through biological aging. Their exceptional longevity does not make their internal Vitality an unlimited resource.[1]
The system consequently rewards efficient techniques and precise control over raw Energy expenditure. Greater supernatural output is not automatically beneficial if the practitioner sacrifices an excessive portion of their remaining lifespan to achieve it.

### Relationship with Senjutsu

#### Complementary Energies
Ki and [[Senjutsu]] are distinct but complementary disciplines. Senjutsu draws upon [[Natural Energy]] distributed throughout the planetary environment, while Ki uses the practitioner's own internal Vitality.[1]
The relationship between them is particularly important because Senjutsu provides a means of reducing the burden associated with Ki expenditure. By absorbing Natural Energy and incorporating it into the body, a practitioner can support the use of internal Vitality without consuming the same quantity of personal Life Force that would otherwise be required.[1][2]
This arrangement allows the two disciplines to operate as a coordinated system rather than competing for the same Energy source. The practitioner's ability to control both energies is nevertheless essential, since improperly absorbed Natural Energy introduces separate dangers that Ki cultivation alone does not resolve.

#### Natural Energy Filtration
Haruna's cultivation method relies upon filtering [[Senjutsu]] before making use of her internal Vitality. The [[Natural Energy]] obtained from Earth's Ley Lines is powerful enough to damage the body if absorbed or circulated without adequate control.[2]
To overcome this problem, she initially employs a two-stage process: absorbing and regulating Natural Energy before using her own Ki to perform techniques. This method avoids the immediate dangers associated with unrestricted use of raw Senjutsu while still allowing the practitioner to benefit from the external Energy source.[2]
With sufficient proficiency, the direct utilization of Senjutsu becomes possible. However, this approach places a greater burden upon the body than Haruna's filtered method, making the conventional relationship between Senjutsu and Ki a practical means of balancing supernatural output and personal safety.[2]

#### Victor and Roxanne
[[Victor Elderblood]] possesses the internal Vitality necessary to employ Ki, but his relationship with [[Roxanne Elderblood]] makes conventional Ki cultivation comparatively unnecessary.[2]
Roxanne exists as a World Tree directly connected to Victor's Soul. Through their extraordinary synchronization, he gained the ability to access her [[Senjutsu]] without relying upon the filtration process employed by Haruna. This provides him with a powerful source of [[Natural Energy]] that does not require the same expenditure of personal Vitality associated with Ki.[2]
Roxanne therefore advised Victor to prioritize her Senjutsu instead of consuming his own Life Force through Ki. His situation differs from Haruna's because the Energy supplied by Roxanne originates from a World Tree already bound to his existence rather than from an external planetary environment.[2]
Victor's ability to use his internal Vitality remains distinct from his preference not to rely upon it. His rejection of conventional Ki as a principal power source reflects the advantages of his unique circumstances rather than an inability to manipulate the Energy.

### Applications and Abilities

#### Internal Energy Manipulation
The principal application of Ki is the conscious manipulation of the practitioner's own Vitality. This enables living beings to employ an internal supernatural resource rather than relying exclusively upon Mana, Youki or another external Energy system.[1][2]
- Ki Manipulation: Conscious utilization of internal Life Energy for supernatural techniques.[1]
- Vitality Manipulation: The practitioner's personal Vitality serves as the resource used to produce Ki-based effects.[1]
- Energy Channeling: Internal Energy can be directed toward the execution of techniques through controlled expenditure.[2]
- Energy Regulation: The amount of Vitality consumed must be controlled to avoid exhausting the practitioner's Life Force.[1]
- Energy Conservation: Combining Ki with [[Senjutsu]] can reduce the personal Vitality consumed during supernatural activity.[1]

#### Supernatural Techniques
Ki can serve as the Energy source for techniques developed through the practitioner's mastery of internal Vitality. Haruna recognized that the discovery of [[Senjutsu]] and Ki greatly expanded the possibilities available to supernatural martial arts, although the process of developing reliable techniques required considerable experimentation and discipline.[1]
The specific effects of a Ki technique depend upon what the practitioner learns to accomplish with their internal Energy. The fundamental mechanics support supernatural applications, but Ki itself should not be automatically credited with every effect commonly associated with martial-arts energy systems.
- Energy-Based Techniques: Ki can be used as the medium for supernatural techniques developed through control of the user's internal Vitality.[1][2]
- Power Combination: Ki can function alongside Senjutsu, allowing the practitioner to employ internal Vitality while benefiting from externally acquired [[Natural Energy]].[1]
- Energy Efficiency: Controlled use can reduce unnecessary expenditure and permit the practitioner to sustain techniques without rapidly consuming their remaining Vitality.[1]
Specific abilities such as flight, elemental projection, advanced regeneration, independent Soul attacks or unrestricted physical enhancement should not be considered universal properties of Ki without corresponding demonstrations from individual practitioners.

#### Martial Applications
Ki possesses potential relevance to martial techniques because it provides an internal Energy resource capable of supporting supernatural applications. Haruna's discovery emerged within a civilization possessing highly developed martial traditions, including the swordsmanship of the [[Otsuki Clan]].[1]
Nevertheless, the techniques Haruna demonstrated during her duel against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] primarily involved Youki and her established Kitsune abilities. Her use of those powers does not independently demonstrate that the same results were produced through Ki.[4][5]
Ki should therefore be classified as an available supernatural discipline rather than being credited with all the martial feats performed by characters who also possess the ability to cultivate it.

### Limitations and Weaknesses

#### Lifespan Consumption
The greatest established weakness of Ki is its direct consumption of the practitioner's Vitality. Unlike ordinary external Energy reserves, which may be replenished from the environment, Ki draws upon a resource intrinsically associated with the continued existence of the living being employing it.[1]
Excessive use can consume the individual's remaining lifespan and cause death. The danger is particularly severe when the practitioner attempts powerful techniques without sufficient discipline, since uncontrolled expenditure may destroy the very Life Force required to remain alive.[1]
- Life-Force Consumption: Ki techniques consume the user's internal Vitality.[1]
- Lifespan Reduction: Excessive expenditure can burn through the practitioner's remaining lifespan.[1]
- Energy Exhaustion: The user's personal reserves limit how much Ki can be safely employed without additional support.[1]
- Self-Destruction Through Overuse: Undisciplined utilization can become fatal when the expenditure of Vitality exceeds what the practitioner can safely sustain.[1]

#### Longevity Does Not Guarantee Protection
Supernatural longevity does not eliminate Ki's dangers. Haruna explicitly identified Youkai and Vampires as examples of races whose extraordinary lifespans do not protect them against death caused by excessive consumption of Vitality.[1]
The distinction reflects the difference between freedom from ordinary aging and immunity to having one's Life Force consumed. A supernatural being may survive indefinitely under normal circumstances while remaining vulnerable to a technique that exhausts the Energy sustaining its existence.
Ki cultivation therefore requires discipline even among powerful or extraordinarily long-lived races.

#### Dependence on Personal Vitality
Ki cannot be treated as an unrestricted source of Energy merely because it exists naturally within living beings. Its conventional use depends upon the amount of Vitality the individual can safely expend, creating a limitation different from the environmental dependence associated with Youki or [[Senjutsu]].[1]
External Energy systems may reduce this burden, as demonstrated through the relationship between Ki and Senjutsu, but the underlying distinction remains important. A practitioner without sufficient control cannot safely compensate for limited internal reserves simply by attempting to release greater quantities of Ki.

#### Training Requirements
Although Ki is potentially accessible to different races, its proper use requires an understanding of internal Vitality and the ability to regulate its expenditure. Haruna's successful development of the discipline followed considerable research involving [[Natural Energy]], meditation and experimentation.[1]
The requirement is particularly important because the consequences of failure may extend beyond temporary fatigue. Improper utilization can consume a resource whose depletion directly threatens the practitioner's continued existence.

### Relationship with Other Energy Types

#### Ki and Youki
Youki is an Energy derived from the accumulation of negative emotions generated by Humanity. Most ordinary Youkai obtain it through environmental absorption, making their traditional supernatural abilities dependent upon the availability of suitable Energy.[1]
Ki differs by utilizing the practitioner's own internal Vitality. This allows its cultivation to avoid the same geographical dependence upon Japan's accumulated negative emotional Energy, although it introduces the separate danger of consuming the user's lifespan.

#### Ki and Senjutsu
[[Senjutsu]] is the foundational discipline from which Haruna developed Ki. The former draws upon the [[Natural Energy]] of the environment, while the latter uses the Life Force naturally possessed by the practitioner.[1]
Their complementary relationship allows externally acquired Natural Energy to reduce the burden imposed upon personal Vitality. Haruna's method incorporates filtered Senjutsu into her body before using Ki for techniques, while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] bypasses the conventional approach through Roxanne's direct supply of World Tree Energy.[2]

#### Ki and Magic
[[Magic]] is conventionally associated with Mana and the execution of supernatural spells, whereas Ki employs personal Vitality as its Energy source.
Although both systems belong to the wider classification of supernatural Energy, their conventional acquisition and utilization differ. The presence of internal Vitality does not automatically grant an individual the knowledge required to perform Magic, nor does mastery of Mana inherently establish proficiency with Ki.

#### Ki and Miasma
Miasma is the supernatural Energy associated primarily with Demons and Hell. It possesses characteristic infernal properties and can exist naturally within the environment of the infernal realms.
Ki instead originates from the internal Life Force of living beings. Its use does not inherently reproduce the poisonous or corrosive properties of Demonic Miasma, and possession of one Energy system does not automatically grant the techniques associated with the other.

#### Ki and Divinity
Ki is not inherently a [[Divine Energy]]. Its manipulation does not automatically confer Godhood, Divinity or conceptual authority over Life. The discipline operates through internal Vitality rather than through a Divine Concept.
Although powerful supernatural beings may possess access to both Ki and Divine powers, their availability arises from separate aspects of the user's existence.

#### Ki and Attributeless Energy
At a more fundamental level, Ki belongs to the same underlying system of Energy as Mana, [[Senjutsu]], Demonic Energy and the other attributed powers used throughout Creation. The distinction between these forms lies in their attributes and the particular methods through which ordinary beings employ them.[3]
[[Victor Elderblood]]'s advanced understanding of Attributeless Energy establishes that a being possessing sufficiently extraordinary control may manipulate Energy independently of the limitations ordinarily imposed by its attributes. However, this capability should not be confused with conventional Ki cultivation, which remains restricted by the practitioner's understanding and available internal Vitality.[3]

### Known Users

#### Otsuki Elderblood Haruna
[[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]] is the principal documented practitioner associated with the development of Ki. As a Nine-Tailed Kitsune and the ruler of the unified Youkai, she investigated alternative supernatural systems alongside representatives of the three great Clans.[1]
Her experiments with [[Senjutsu]] led to the discovery of internal Vitality as a manipulable source of Energy. Haruna developed a cultivation method in which externally absorbed [[Natural Energy]] could support the use of Ki, reducing the danger associated with consuming her own Life Force.[1][2]
Her extraordinary discipline and experience with complex martial techniques made her particularly suited to investigating the new Energy system. Nevertheless, her established combat feats involving Youki should not automatically be reclassified as Ki techniques.

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] possesses the internal Vitality necessary to employ Ki, but the existence of [[Roxanne Elderblood]] within his Soul makes the conventional expenditure of that Energy unnecessary under most circumstances.[2]
Rather than consuming his own Life Force, Victor can draw directly upon the [[Senjutsu]] supplied by Roxanne. This arrangement grants him access to an extraordinary source of Energy associated with a World Tree, bypassing the filtration process employed by Haruna.[2]
His access to Ki is therefore established, but its deliberate use as an independent combat system is not among his principal demonstrated techniques.

#### The Youkai
Certain Youkai possess the capacity to cultivate Ki through the methods developed under Haruna's leadership. The discipline was established as part of the effort to overcome the limitations of ordinary Youki, with the potential to provide an internal source of supernatural Energy to practitioners capable of satisfying its requirements.[1]
The ability to cultivate Ki should not be attributed automatically to every named Youkai simply because their race possesses access to the discipline. Individual proficiency and demonstrated use remain separate considerations.
