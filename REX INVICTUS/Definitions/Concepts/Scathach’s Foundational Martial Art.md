Scathach’s Foundational Martial Art, also known as [[Scathach]]'s Five Basic [[Martial Arts]], is the personal foundational combat system created by Scathach Elderblood Scarlett after centuries of studying, practicing and analyzing numerous martial disciplines. Unlike a weapon-specific school, the system is designed to strengthen the fundamental mechanics underlying virtually every form of combat: how physical force is generated, how supernatural power is incorporated into technique, how the practitioner moves, how they protect themselves and how all of those elements are controlled as one coherent whole.[1][2][3][4]
Its complete structure consists of four primary foundations—Strength, Power, Speed and Defense—followed by a fifth and final principle, Control.[2][3][5] An earlier explanation in Chapter 62 calls Ruby's fourth branch Resistance, while Victor's later detailed explanation in Chapter 422 calls the same branch Defense. The later terminology is used throughout this article, with Resistance treated as the earlier description of the defensive branch rather than a separate sixth technique.[1][5]
Victor Elderblood became the first prominently shown disciple to receive the complete five-part foundation directly from Scathach. Within approximately six months he had absorbed the basics of all five techniques, something that shocked the Scarlett sisters because Scathach had deliberately withheld the complete system from them.[2][3] Victor later used this foundation as the common structure through which he adapted numerous unrelated martial arts, fighting methods and weapon systems to his own body, ultimately carrying Scathach's foundational philosophy into his later Dragon-oriented martial arts and the [[Seven Arts of the Blood Dragon Primogenitor]].[6][7][8]

### Origin and Creation
[[Scathach]]'s martial art is the result of an exceptionally long period of observation rather than the invention of one isolated technique. By Chapter 277, the novel explicitly describes the martial art she taught [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] as an amalgamation of several martial arts.[6] Much later, Chapter 1017 clarifies this history further: Scathach developed her system by observing martial styles belonging to both supernatural races and Humans over thousands of years, with Human martial arts forming the majority of the styles she observed.[9]
Human martial systems interested Scathach precisely because Humans lacked the naturally overwhelming bodies available to Vampires, [[Werewolves]] or Dragons. Human fighters had to compensate through creativity, technique, preparation, leverage and adaptation when facing stronger opponents. Scathach spent millennia observing this behavior and incorporated the principles she found useful into an exceptionally lethal composite system.[9]

Although Scathach possesses extensive expertise with many forms of combat, the Five Basic Martial Arts should not be confused with her principal personal weapon discipline. Ruby explains in Chapter 62 that Scathach's main martial art is Sojutsu, or spearmanship, while the broader close-combat system was something she developed through her wider martial studies.[1] Scathach would later attain Grandmastery specifically through the [[Spears|Spear]], while remaining highly skilled across numerous other disciplines.

#### A Martial Art Built from Fundamentals
The significance of this origin becomes obvious when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] begins studying unrelated martial arts. Because [[Scathach]]'s own system was already a synthesis built from transferable principles, Victor could use it as a common foundation rather than treating every foreign martial art as an entirely separate structure.[6]
The system therefore functions less like a rigid school of prescribed movements and more like a martial framework. Its practitioner learns how to generate force, move efficiently, protect the body, incorporate supernatural abilities and regulate all of these functions. Once those fundamentals are established, different fighting methods can be built upon them without necessarily destroying the user's original foundation.

### Foundational Philosophy
The central philosophy of [[Scathach]]'s system is that overwhelming power is meaningless if the practitioner cannot use it correctly. Her earliest training of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] demonstrates this directly. Rather than beginning with spectacular secret techniques, Scathach first tested whether Victor could remain conscious, observant and functional while severely injured, then rebuilt his movement, distance management, strength control, Energy efficiency and weapon handling from the ground up.[11][12]
Victor possessed enormous physical advantages as a newborn Vampire, but Scathach considered those advantages dangerous if they encouraged him to substitute raw power for martial skill. Her objective was to remove unnecessary movement and inefficient habits so that his physical and supernatural capabilities enhanced correct technique rather than replacing it.[12]
Chapter 305 gives the most concise description of what Scathach's martial art is intended to accomplish:
FOUNDATION
Perfect the foundation of the practitioner.
The stronger the foundation, the more weapons, techniques and supernatural abilities can be added without destabilizing the practitioner's combat ability.
[4]
This principle became particularly important for Victor. His arsenal continued expanding at an abnormal rate, meaning that each new ability, weapon and combat method placed additional demands upon his body and technique. Scathach consequently focused heavily upon his basics because a weak foundation beneath Victor's rapidly increasing complexity would eventually become a structural weakness.[4]

#### Scathach's Definition of Combat Power
[[Scathach]]'s later observations further explain why she places so much value on foundation. In Chapter 690 she treats combat power as the combined result of numerous factors rather than simply the quantity of Energy possessed by an individual. Techniques, mentality, racial characteristics, equipment, experience and the ability to actually use one's resources can all determine the outcome of a battle.[13]

### The Five Basic Martial Arts
[[Scathach]]'s complete foundational system contains five interconnected components. The first four represent major areas of combat development, while Control is the fifth principle that allows those areas to function together efficiently.[2][3][5]
The first four branches are not cultivation stages and are not required to be learned in a fixed universal sequence. Scathach deliberately gave different first branches to each of her daughters according to their individual characteristics.[1][5] Control, however, requires familiarity with the preceding foundations because its purpose is to integrate them.

#### Strength
Strength is the branch [[Scathach]] assigned to [[Pepper Scarlett Elderblood]].[1][5] It concerns the generation and application of physical force through correct martial movement. The objective is not simply to increase the user's natural physical statistics; the user learns how to transfer existing strength into an attack efficiently.
Pepper provides the clearest early demonstration. Even while relatively inactive compared with Scathach's standards, her physical punches could generate enormous air pressure, with Chapter 62 showing the destructive effects of a casual martial strike.[1] The technique's deeper function becomes clearer later: Strength is only one portion of a complete foundation. Excessive specialization gives Pepper exceptional raw physical application but leaves deficiencies in movement, defense and the integration of her supernatural abilities.[5]
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] began training Pepper in the other branches, she noticed that learning Speed also slightly improved the expression of her Strength.[14] This demonstrates one of Scathach's central ideas: the five branches are mutually reinforcing rather than isolated skills.

#### Power
Power is the branch originally assigned to [[Siena Scarlett Elderblood]].[1][5] The term refers primarily to the practitioner's supernatural abilities and how those abilities are incorporated into combat.
The purpose of Power is not simply to increase Energy output. A supernatural fighter can possess an enormous ability while still using it inefficiently. Power teaches the practitioner to apply that racial or supernatural ability through an established martial foundation instead of treating it as something disconnected from physical combat.
This distinction becomes important for advanced practitioners such as [[Scathach]] and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], both of whom can merge supernatural effects into their movements without abandoning their martial form.[5]

#### Speed
Speed is the branch assigned to [[Lacus Scarlett Elderblood]].[1][5] It is significantly more technical than simply possessing naturally high movement speed.
While teaching Pepper, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] demonstrates that its basic mechanics involve controlled breathing, small impulses of force through the feet and legs, correct timing and efficient movement.[5] He performs the technique without Lightning and moves quickly enough that Pepper initially assumes he used supernatural enhancement.
The technique therefore improves acceleration through biomechanics and force application before supernatural abilities are introduced.

##### Basic Application
At the basic level, the practitioner synchronizes breathing with carefully controlled impulses through the lower body. The objective is to produce greater acceleration without wasting motion or force.[5]

##### Advanced Application
At higher proficiency, Speed can be directly combined with supernatural abilities. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explains that both he and [[Scathach]] can integrate their powers with the technique.[5]
Victor's own example combines the Speed foundation with Lightning, using his supernatural ability to enhance a movement system that already functions without Lightning.

#### Defense / Resistance
Defense is the later name used for the branch assigned to [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]], while Chapter 62 initially describes Ruby's foundation as Resistance.[1][5]
The branch covers defensive application rather than raw toughness alone. Ruby demonstrates this early by using her Water and Ice abilities to transform and reinforce her body, allowing attacks to strike an elemental defense rather than ordinary flesh.[1] Lacus explains that the method requires exceptionally precise control because improper transformation could damage vital portions of the body.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later explanation confirms that Defense can be integrated with other foundations and supernatural powers.[5] For long-distance movement, he can combine Ruby's defensive principle with the Speed technique and Lightning, transforming his body into an elemental state while using Speed to move as a form of living lightning.[5]
Defense therefore encompasses more than blocking. It includes the use of bodily positioning, resistance, transformation and supernatural abilities to prevent or mitigate damage.

Control is the fifth and final component of [[Scathach]]'s foundational martial system.[2][3][5] Unlike the previous four, Control is not another isolated specialization. Its function is to bring all four foundations together.
Scathach explicitly states in Chapter 68 that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] learned five basic techniques and that the prerequisite for learning the fifth was learning the previous four.[2] Chapter 69 further explains that Control focuses upon using the four techniques as efficiently as possible.[3]

#### Failure of the Original Plan
The Scarlett sisters developed differently from what [[Scathach]] expected. Instead of teaching one another until each became individually complete, they increasingly specialized in their assigned branches and compensated for their missing foundations through exceptional teamwork.[5]
As a group this made them extremely dangerous. Pepper supplied Strength, Siena supplied Power, Lacus supplied Speed and Ruby supplied Defense. The four sisters collectively reproduced much of the balanced structure Scathach intended for one practitioner.
The weakness was that the balance existed across a team rather than inside each individual.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] recognized that their teamwork had become so deeply ingrained that the sisters unconsciously limited themselves when fighting together.[5] Lacus, for example, did not fully express her speed while remaining synchronized with her sisters. ### Victor's Training
[[Victor Elderblood]] was trained very differently from [[Scathach]]'s daughters. From the beginning, Scathach intended to rebuild his entire combat foundation rather than assign him a single specialty.

#### Initial Training
[[Scathach]]'s formal training began by testing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s body rather than teaching elegant movements. She repeatedly injured him, testing his regeneration, pain tolerance and ability to remain functional while suffering damage that would kill an ordinary Human.[11] Once she understood his physical limits, she began correcting the fundamental weaknesses created by possessing enormous supernatural power without formal martial training.
Her instruction included:
- movement and positioning;
- distance management;
- efficient physical force;
- control of Strength;
- Energy efficiency;
- weapon handling;
- combat awareness while injured;
- and the elimination of unnecessary movement.[11][12]
Scathach's objective was not merely to increase Victor's power. She wanted to create a foundation strong enough that he could eventually develop a fighting style of his own.[12]

#### Learning All Five Foundations
After approximately six months, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had learned the basics of all five of [[Scathach]]'s foundational techniques.[2] Scathach describes him as absorbing her instruction like a sponge and admits that she continued teaching him simply to discover how much he could learn.

#### Readiness and Qualification
[[Scathach]] did not automatically teach the full system to every student. Chapter 62 establishes that many of her disciples were never considered qualified to inherit everything she knew.[1]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later treatment of Pepper and [[Nero]] provides additional examples of how readiness was judged.
In Chapter 421, Victor considers Pepper ready to learn the complete version of her mother's martial arts because she had reached a developmental bottleneck.[15] His recommendation was not simply to obtain greater raw power, but to improve control and refinement. He demonstrates the principle by compressing and rotating Water into a tiny high-pressure projectile capable of cutting through clouds, showing Pepper that a familiar power can become substantially more dangerous through superior control rather than greater volume.[15]
Victor then begins teaching Pepper the missing foundations—Power, Defense and Speed—so that she can eventually reach Control.[5]
Chapter 424 contrasts Pepper with [[Nero Elderblood]]. When Nero asks to learn the same martial arts, Victor refuses to begin immediately because her foundations are not yet ready: she had only recently become a full Vampire, previously relied heavily upon firearms and still needed to acclimate to her body and supernatural powers.[14]
These scenes establish that the art's practical prerequisite is foundational readiness, not simply possessing a certain amount of destructive power.

#### Control After Major Growth
The need to revisit foundation does not disappear after a practitioner becomes powerful. Chapter 619 demonstrates this when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] required approximately 48 hours of continuous physical and martial combat with [[Scathach]] to regain sufficient control over his greatly increased strength that his movements would no longer indiscriminately damage the surroundings.[16]
This reinforces one of the most important principles behind Scathach's system:

### Foundation for Other Martial Arts
One of the greatest strengths of [[Scathach]]'s foundational art is its compatibility with other martial disciplines. Chapter 277 gives the clearest demonstration through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[6]
During his later training, Victor studied the basics of numerous unrelated systems, including:
- Human martial arts;
- Werewolf martial arts;
- Tengu martial arts;
- combat methods associated with Banshee racial abilities;
- fist techniques;
- [[Spears]];
- Axes;
- Western swords;
- Katana;
- Whips;
- Naginata;
- and other weapon disciplines.[6]
Many of these martial systems had originally been designed for bodies very different from Victor's own. Tengu arts could assume the practitioner possessed wings, Werewolf techniques could assume a transformed Werewolf body and Banshee methods could rely upon racial sound abilities.
Victor's instinct was to ignore those restrictions and determine whether the underlying principle could be recreated through his own abilities.

#### The Failed Fusion
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] initially attempted to directly merge the new martial arts into [[Scathach]]'s system.
He failed.[6]
The failure demonstrated that complete martial systems cannot simply be stacked together without contradiction. Different styles possess assumptions concerning stance, body structure, movement, rhythm, weapon handling and physiology.
Victor's eventual solution was not fusion but adaptation.

#### Perfecting the Foundation
Chapter 305 demonstrates the long-term result. [[Scathach]] finds [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] increasingly difficult to categorize because fighting him feels like fighting multiple martial artists at the same time.[4] He exhibits the habits and principles of numerous fighters without those different mannerisms disrupting one another.
Scathach realizes that her foundational martial art is one of the primary reasons this is possible. The system she created was specifically designed to perfect the practitioner's foundation, allowing additional techniques to be placed upon it without immediately creating structural instability.[4]

#### God of War Potential
Observing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s trajectory, [[Scathach]] speculates that he could eventually approach the ideal of a [[Ares|God of War]]: a being who knows every martial art and can employ all of them perfectly without suffering the normal disadvantages of extreme breadth.[4]
This statement describes Victor's potential, not an automatic ability of Scathach's martial art and not proof that Chapter 305 Victor had already mastered every martial art.

### Draconic Evolution
[[Scathach]]'s transformation into a Dragon created the same problem that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had previously encountered: martial techniques developed around one physiology are not automatically perfect for a fundamentally different body.

#### Victor's Dragon-Tailored Foundation
After [[Scathach]] became a Dragon Ancestor, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] gave her an unfinished martial foundation designed specifically for Dragons fighting in humanoid forms.[7]
Victor explicitly points out that a Dragon capable of assuming humanoid form should not simply be expected to fight exactly like a Human. The martial framework he created was intended to account for Dragon physiology and the new supernatural capabilities available to the Race.[7]
The system was still incomplete—more a developed idea and usable foundation than a finished martial art—but it represented an important derivative of the philosophy Scathach had originally taught Victor.

#### Scathach's Own Dragon Adaptation
The development did not stop with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s gift. Chapter 1017 explicitly states that [[Scathach]] herself was attempting to adapt her martial art to the robust body of a Dragon.[9]
This is especially significant because the chapter simultaneously explains the historical origin of her system. Scathach's original martial art had been constructed largely through observing Human creativity—techniques created by physically weaker beings to overcome stronger opponents. As a Dragon, Scathach now faced the opposite problem: translating an art shaped by efficiency and overcoming weakness into a body possessing overwhelming natural power.[9]
The Dragon adaptation should therefore be understood as an evolution of the foundational philosophy rather than proof that the original human/Vampire-oriented movements remained perfectly optimal after her transformation.

#### Chapter Correction
The Dragon-tailored martial foundation associated directly with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and [[Scathach]] belongs to the Chapter 819 period following Scathach's earlier Dragon transformation, not Chapter 918.[7] Chapters 917–925 concern the later mass transformation of Victor's wider family into [[True Dragons]] and should not be used as the primary citation for this martial-art event.

#### Foundation and Grandmastery
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later Grandmaster Enlightenment in Chapter 1039 further reinforces the importance of this principle. Despite possessing the Seven Arts and an enormous collection of advanced techniques, Victor eventually realizes that further progress requires him to return to the most basic movements and foundations of his Greatswordsmanship.[17]
The lesson mirrors [[Scathach]]'s original philosophy: complexity never makes fundamentals obsolete.

#### Other Disciples
[[Scathach]] has taught many disciples throughout her extremely long life, including figures such as Eleonor Adrasteia. However, being Scathach's disciple does not automatically prove that the individual was taught the complete Five Basic [[Martial Arts]]. Chapter 62 explicitly establishes that Scathach did not consider every disciple qualified to inherit everything she knew.[1]
For that reason, only characters whose training in this specific foundational system is directly established should be listed as confirmed practitioners.

#### Nero
[[Nero Elderblood]] expressed interest in learning [[Scathach]]'s martial art during Pepper's training period, but [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] considered her foundations insufficient at the time because she had only recently adapted to her full Vampire physiology and previously relied heavily upon firearms.[14] She should therefore not be listed as a confirmed practitioner solely from that conversation.

### Training Method
[[Scathach]]'s personal teaching style is notoriously severe, but her brutality should be separated from the actual mechanics of the Five Basic [[Martial Arts]].
Her training of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] involved:
- repeated combat against a superior opponent;
- training while injured;
- pain conditioning;
- movement correction;
- weapon practice;
- strength regulation;
- Energy efficiency;
- combat observation;
- and continuous repetition of basic principles.[11][12]
Victor's Vampire regeneration allowed Scathach to use training methods that would permanently cripple an ordinary Human. This does not mean that severe injury or regeneration is a universal mechanical requirement of the martial art.
The same principle applies to Pepper's bottleneck. Victor considered it an appropriate moment to teach her the complete system, but the novel does not establish a formal universal rule stating that every practitioner must reach one identical "power-up bottleneck" before learning Control.
What is consistently established is that Scathach refuses to give advanced instruction to someone whose foundation is not ready.

### Technical Distinctions and Clarifications
Claim
Clarification
The five components are mastery ranks.
False. Strength, Power, Speed, Defense and Control are internal components of [[Scathach]]'s specific system. Martial Art Mastery is a separate ranking structure.
Ruby's branch is either Resistance or Defense.
Both terms appear. Chapter 62 calls it Resistance while Chapter 422 later calls Ruby's assigned branch Defense. They describe the same fourth foundational specialization.[1][5]
Control simply means suppressing Energy.
Incomplete. Control integrates all four primary foundations and regulates how they are applied together.[3][5]
Strength automatically grants greater raw physical statistics.
The branch teaches the application of existing physical force. A practitioner's underlying physical potential still depends upon the individual.
The art automatically gives [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]-level adaptation.
False. The foundation facilitates adaptation, while Victor's extraordinary learning speed and physiology are personal characteristics.[6]
Every practitioner follows Strength → Power → Speed → Defense → Control in order.
False. Scathach deliberately assigned different first branches to different daughters. Control depends upon integrating the four foundations, but no single fixed learning order for those four is established.[5]
Completing Control makes someone a Grandmaster.
False. Victor possessed the complete foundation centuries of subjective experience before his confirmed Greatsword Grandmaster Enlightenment.[2][17]
The Dragon foundation in Chapter 819 is the original Five Basic [[Martial Arts]] unchanged.
False. Victor created an unfinished derivative specifically for Dragon physiology, and Scathach later continued adapting her own system to her Dragon body.[7][9]
The Dragon-tailored foundation first appears in Chapter 918.
Incorrect. The relevant martial-art event is associated with Scathach's earlier Dragon transformation and Chapter 819.[7]
The Seven Arts are simply Scathach's Five Basic Martial Arts renamed.
False. Scathach's system forms part of the foundation, while the Seven Arts incorporate new Dragon-specific mechanics involving Reality, Energy and Soul control.[8]

### Notes
- [[Scathach]]'s personal foundational martial art is an amalgamation created from numerous combat systems rather than one inherited school.[6]
- Chapter 1017 clarifies that Scathach observed both supernatural and Human martial arts, with Human styles forming the majority of her inspiration.[9]
- Her principal weapon discipline remains Sojutsu/Spearmanship rather than the Five Basic [[Martial Arts]] themselves.[1]
- The first four foundations are Strength, Power, Speed and Defense/Resistance; the fifth is Control.[1][2][5]
- Control requires the practitioner to understand the other four foundations and use them together efficiently.[2][3]
- Scathach originally distributed one primary foundation to each daughter and expected them eventually to exchange their knowledge.[5]
- The Scarlett sisters instead compensated for their incomplete individual foundations through exceptionally effective teamwork.[5]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] learned all five foundations during approximately six months of direct training under Scathach.[2]
- Scathach still considered this only the strengthening of Victor's basics rather than the completion of his martial development.[2]
- Victor later used Scathach's complete system as the common foundation for adapting numerous weapon and racial martial arts.[6]
- Chapter 305 explicitly describes Scathach's system as perfecting the practitioner's foundation.[4]
- Victor's God-of-War potential is a prediction arising from his own development and should not be treated as an automatic property granted to all users.[4]
- Advanced versions of the foundational arts can be combined with supernatural abilities.[5]
- Pepper's later training demonstrates that improving one foundation can improve the practical expression of another.[14]
- Victor's later Dragon-tailored foundation and the Seven Arts are derivative developments rather than replacements for Scathach's original system.[7][8]
- Scathach herself continued evolving the system after becoming a Dragon.[9]
