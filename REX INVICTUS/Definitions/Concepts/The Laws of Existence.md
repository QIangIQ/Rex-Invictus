TERMINOLOGY
The term "Law" is used for several different levels of regulation in the novel. A Concept's internal Laws, a prohibition imposed by a Primordial, a Rule governing interaction between Primordial Domains and the fundamental conceptual Rules upon which a complete Reality is constructed are related forms of metaphysical regulation, but they are not automatically the same thing.
The Laws of Existence, also referred to contextually as the Rules of Creation, Rules of the Universe, Rules of Reality or simply Rules, are the metaphysical principles, restrictions and administrative conditions through which Reality and the beings existing within it are permitted to function. The term covers several related layers, ranging from Rules deliberately imposed by sufficiently powerful rulers to the deepest conceptual Rules forming the underlying architecture of an entire Cosmos.[1][2][3]
The existence of Rules was introduced long before the complete cosmological structure surrounding them became known. Code, Rule described Rules broadly as commands created by stronger beings and imposed upon weaker ones, emphasizing that Rules are continually established and erased throughout worlds, eras and Universes.[1] Later chapters progressively revealed that this principle extends far beyond political law. Gods are constrained by the Laws belonging to their Divine Concepts, [[Primordial Entities]] function beneath cosmic responsibilities and jurisdictional restrictions, [[World Trees]] possess conditions imposed upon their existence, and complete Cosmoses possess foundational Rules defining how concepts such as Life, Death, Infinity and Souls operate.[4][5][6]
The late explanation in Chapter 1168 provides the most complete framework. A genuine Universe does not become a fully established Reality merely because it contains Space, matter, Energy, stars and apparently functioning life. It requires fundamental conceptual Rules determining what those things are, how they operate and how they interact. Victor compares these fundamental Divine Concepts to the source code of the Universe. Primordial Chaos is identified as the supreme existence responsible for establishing the underlying conceptual architecture of the native Cosmos, while the established Primordials function as essential components within the resulting system.[3]

### Nature of Rules

#### Rules as Commands
The first direct explanation of Rules comes from [[Code, Rule]] in Chapter 269. Code describes a Rule as something established by a stronger existence that weaker existences are compelled to obey. He treats Rules simultaneously as methods of administration, limitations upon absolute free will and tools through which sufficiently intelligent rulers can create stable societies.[1]
His examples are deliberately broad. The Rules established by a king governing an empire and the Rules operating throughout a supernatural Reality are not necessarily identical in metaphysical scale, but both follow the same underlying principle:
CORE PRINCIPLE
Authority establishes a Rule; existences beneath that Authority are expected or compelled to operate within it.
Code also states that Rules are neither universally permanent nor unique to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Universe. Rules are created and erased across different worlds and Universes depending upon the existences capable of commanding those systems.[1] This early explanation becomes particularly important once the existence of multiple separate Cosmoses is confirmed.

#### Rules Are Cosmos-Specific
[[Azathoth Elderblood]] explains that separate Cosmoses possess their own Rules, Primordials, fundamental structures and corresponding [[Chaos]].[6] A Rule belonging to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s native Cosmos therefore should not automatically be assumed to possess absolute jurisdiction over every foreign Reality. Azathoth represents another Cosmos whose conceptual structure developed separately, while foreign Chaos entities can consume or interact with other Cosmoses precisely because those systems are distinct.
This establishes:
A Law can be fundamental to one Cosmos without being an absolute law governing all possible existence.

#### Persistence of Established Rules
Chapter 1075 describes the established Rules of a Cosmos as eternal while Azathoth explains why one ordinary Being of [[Chaos]] cannot simply replace another and inherit its position.[6] This statement should be interpreted in its specific cosmological context. The Rules established in a complete Cosmos are deeply integrated enough that an external Chaos normally cannot simply enter, declare itself the new source and occupy the existing supreme position. Instead, foreign Chaos generally consumes Cosmoses in order to expand its own.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] represents an exception because he is both an Eldritch Chaos and an existence born inside the native Cosmos. This gives him a relationship with the very Rules that would normally reject an outside Chaos and creates the theoretical possibility that he could "take over" the vacant administrative structure rather than destroying the Reality and rebuilding it from nothing.[6]
The statement that established Rules are eternal should therefore not be generalized into:
"No Rule can ever be changed."
The series repeatedly demonstrates Rules being bypassed, locally altered, removed through destruction of their governing source or overwritten by sufficiently high Authority.

Chapter 1168 introduces the deepest known level of native Rules. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explains that reproducing visible Reality is not equivalent to creating a genuine Universe. A creator could produce Space, matter, planets, stars, galaxies, Energy, apparent organisms and realistic physical interactions, yet the creation would still lack something essential if it did not possess the conceptual Rules giving those structures genuine metaphysical foundation.[3]
Victor compares this to constructing a program containing visuals and apparent functions while failing to reproduce the deeper source code through which the complete system operates.

### Destiny and the Rules of Creation
Main article: Destiny
The role of Destiny requires careful distinction from [[Primordial Chaos]].
When The System rediscovered Destiny in Chapter 965, its recovered information described him as the Primordial responsible for the timelines and Destiny of Creation and additionally stated that he was the Being who "made the rules for everything."[9]
Earlier interpretations could therefore treat Destiny as the personal creator of every fundamental metaphysical Rule in the Cosmos. The later cosmology makes that reading too broad. Chapter 1168 attributes the foundational conceptual architecture of the native Universe to Primordial Chaos, while Destiny belonged to the later structure as one of the original Eight Regulators.[9][3]
The two statements can therefore be reconciled by distinguishing:
- Foundational Reality architecture — established through Primordial Chaos.
- The operational rule structure governing Destiny, timelines and predetermined outcomes — historically administered or established through Destiny.
Destiny's erasure demonstrates how deeply a Primordial office can affect the Rules operating throughout Creation. Following his removal, fixed Destiny disappeared, predetermined outcomes ceased possessing their former cosmological certainty, alternate timelines began converging upon the Main Timeline and the surviving Primordials did not initially remember that Destiny had existed.[9]
The removal of the administrator therefore changed the structure through which Creation operated. Destiny's unusual erasure should not, however, be generalized as proof that every fundamental Primordial Aspect can be removed safely.

### Conceptual Laws
Main article: Divinity
The Laws associated with Divine Concepts form another layer of metaphysical regulation. Chapter 889 explains that a Divine Body is essentially a vessel and that the Universe contains various Laws associated with different Concepts. A God must learn the Laws belonging to their Concept and progressively apply those Laws to themselves.[4]
This process constitutes conceptual mastery.
For example:
- possessing enormous destructive power does not automatically mean understanding Destruction;
- fighting constantly can provide knowledge useful for understanding War;
- possessing a broad umbrella Concept such as Beginning requires comprehension of many subordinate principles;
- and a God can wield a Concept while still understanding only a small fraction of its Laws.[4]
Thus:
DO NOT CONFLATE
Conceptual Laws define what a particular Concept represents and how its Authority functions.
Laws of Existence describe the greater Rule structure in which those Concepts themselves operate.
The two levels interact but should not be treated as synonyms.

#### Conceptual Hierarchy
Chapter 1033 explicitly identifies one of the Rules [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had learned while studying the Cosmos: weaker Concepts cannot simply oppose Concepts occupying the highest positions of the conceptual hierarchy.[2]
Victor gives examples including:
- <Begin>;
- <End>;
- <Positivity>;
- <Negativity>.
This does not mean that every manifestation of a lower Concept automatically loses against every inexperienced wielder of a higher Concept. Divine mastery, source, Authority, compatibility and raw power remain relevant. The rule instead establishes that Conceptual Authority possesses a genuine hierarchy independent of purely physical power.

#### Authority and Perception
Chapter 1063 states that the greater the Divine Authority a Being possesses in a Concept high within the hierarchy of Divinities, the more clearly that Being can perceive the world as it truly is.[10]
This is an important property of the conceptual system, but it should not be listed as though it were a prohibition comparable to the World Tree rules or Primordial non-interference. It describes a consequence of high conceptual Authority rather than a command forbidding particular behavior.

### Primordial Functions and Cosmic Rules
Main article: [[Primordial Entities]]
The established Primordial Entities are not merely enforcers standing outside the Laws. They are themselves fundamental components of the system.[6][3]
Chapter 1075 describes Primordials as fundamental pieces of the Universe representing Aspects of Creation required for everything to function. Chapter 1168 develops this into the analogy of a cosmic machine. A true Primordial position combines conceptual embodiment, cosmic function, administrative responsibility and integration with the native Rule structure.

#### Life
[[The Universal Tree]] represents a fundamental Life-related pillar. Chapter 1168 explains that true destruction of the underlying Life component would cause Life throughout the Universe to effectively cease.[3]
This is much deeper than killing an ordinary Life Goddess. The latter destroys a representative. The former damages the Rule structure through which Life itself functions.

#### Death
[[True Death]] similarly represents a fundamental terminal function. If Death itself were removed as a cosmic component, living beings and matter would no longer reach their normal End and the energetic cycle dependent upon death would fall into severe disorder.[3]

#### Infinity
If Infinity disappeared at the fundamental level, Infinity itself would cease operating as a property of the native Cosmos and Creation would possess an end.[3]

#### Soul and Balance
Azathoth gives equivalent examples in Chapter 1075. If Soul itself vanished, Souls would cease existing. If Balance disappeared, the corresponding stabilizing Aspect would disappear from Creation.[6]
These examples establish that some Laws are not simply externally enforced commandments. They exist because the fundamental conceptual components through which Reality operates continue to exist.

### Administrative Rules

#### Primordial Impartiality
Chapter 1033 reveals that full Primordial ascension is constrained by more than power. While studying [[Kali Elderblood]], [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] concluded that she possessed the strength, Soul, conceptual development and necessary proximity to Destruction required to approach Primordial existence, yet Creation continued preventing the final transition.[2]
The missing requirement was impartiality. Victor identifies the central characteristics of the established Primordials as impartiality, prioritizing Balance and correctly performing the universal function represented by their position. Kali remained personally attached to her Pantheon and preferences. If the correct operation of Destruction required destroying something she loved, Victor doubted she would perform the duty impartially. Creation consequently did not accept her as the completed Primordial of Destruction.[2]
Chapter 1168 later generalizes the principle by explaining that becoming a Primordial Being requires more than power or conceptual personification. The wider fundamental structure of Creation must also permit the Being to occupy the corresponding cosmic position.[3]
This means Primordial impartiality is not merely an ethical expectation. It can function as a qualification enforced through Creation itself.

#### Domain Non-Interference
Chapter 1074 establishes one of the clearest explicit administrative prohibitions among the Primordials:
A Primordial is strictly forbidden from entering another Primordial's Domain without permission.[5]
This rule became relevant when the Universal Tree declared [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family to be its "children" and placed them beneath the Domain of Life. Interference with them by another Primordial would consequently have to pass through the Universal Tree rather than occurring freely.[5]
This establishes that Primordial Domains are not merely areas of influence. They possess jurisdiction recognized by other cosmic administrators.

#### Primordial Conflict
The Domain rule should not be misinterpreted to mean Primordials are metaphysically incapable of fighting one another. Chapter 1074 explicitly establishes that they can fight. The reason direct conflict is avoided is that their Aspects are fundamental parts of Creation. A serious battle between Life and Soul, for example, would make the Universe itself suffer because the systems dependent upon both functions would be affected.[5]
Disputes can therefore be settled through negotiation, jurisdictional boundaries, representatives, subordinate forces or supervised conflicts between servants.

#### Balance Intervention
During the Demonic War, [[The Judges of The Abyss]] challenged Balance when his concern over a possible future caused him to consider excessive interference before the feared event had actually occurred.[11]
The lesson was that a predicted threat is not automatically equivalent to a completed violation. Sentient beings still make choices. The Primordials are expected to consider the wider consequences instead of punishing every possible future before it happens.
Later, Balance explains the broader principle more directly: the Primordials intervene when an action genuinely threatens to Break the Balance or risks creating a cascading problem capable of harming Creation.[12]
The older formulation that Primordials simply "cannot interfere until the violation happens" is therefore too absolute. A more accurate description is:
Primordial intervention is constrained by their responsibility to preserve Balance and by the distinction between a possible future threat and an actual systemic violation.

### World Tree Rules
Main article: [[World Trees]]
Several explicit cosmic Rules concern the operation of World Trees.

#### Living Planets and World Trees
Chapter 1033 lists among the Laws [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had discovered:
A planet possessing Life must have a World Tree to sustain Life.[2]
This describes the normal native Life system. The later Empire demonstrates that Victor can reproduce or substitute portions of the functions normally performed by a World Tree, but this should be treated as an exceptional circumvention or replacement of the ordinary system rather than evidence that the original Law never existed.

#### Positive and Negative World Trees
Chapter 1088 explains the normal planetary World Tree cycle. The [[Amara Elderblood|World Tree of Positivity]] appears first and nourishes the planet and its Life. Once sentient Life emerges, the World Tree of Negativity manifests to manage the more spiritual side of the planetary ecosystem.[13]
The narration identifies this cycle as one of the fundamental Rules of the Universe enforced by the Primordial of Life.[13]
This distinction is important. The Rule is part of the universal Life system, while the Universal Tree acts as the Primordial responsible for enforcing that system.

#### World Tree Reproduction Prohibition
[[World Trees]] are normally incapable of reproduction. Chapter 1116 clarifies that this is not merely a biological consequence of their enormous Souls. It is specifically a prohibition imposed by their Creator, the Universal Tree.[7]
This makes the restriction a particularly clear example of an imposed existential prohibition rather than one of the deepest foundational rules of Reality.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] nevertheless impregnated [[Roxanne Elderblood]] and [[Amara Elderblood]], producing World Tree descendants not directly created through the Universal Tree's normal process. His success therefore demonstrates that a sufficiently anomalous existence can bypass at least some restrictions imposed upon a species.
It does not prove that Victor automatically ignores every fundamental Law of the Cosmos.

### Progenitor Rule
Chapter 1033 also identifies the rule:
Only one Progenitor can exist for each species at the same time.[2]
The existence of unusual Progenitors throughout the story demonstrates that the application of this Rule depends upon what Creation recognizes as a distinct species or racial foundation. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] repeatedly evolves beyond previous classifications and eventually establishes new races rather than remaining merely another example of an already-existing species.
The Progenitor restriction should consequently be described according to its explicit wording rather than expanded into unsupported assumptions concerning every hybrid or descendant.

### The Balanced Universe
Chapter 1130 provides Balance's description of the intended stable condition of Creation. A Balanced Universe is described as possessing a single Timeline in which the chaos involved in the creation of stars and the stability of already-existing systems can coexist without interfering destructively with one another.[12]
The Primordials maintain this system until Creation eventually reaches its natural End. At the end of the cycle:
1. Creation reaches its final stage.
2. [[True Death]] gathers the remaining Essence.
3. The intended complete [[Primordial Chaos]] remains.
4. That Primordial Chaos uses the accumulated Essence to begin the next cycle.[12]
The current Cosmos possesses a structural problem:
There is no complete Primordial Chaos occupying the intended supreme position.
The existing Primordials therefore continually maintain a system whose intended central administrator is absent.
The "single Timeline" statement should not be treated as an isolated commandment in the same category as World Tree reproduction. It describes the stable cosmic configuration Balance considers correct.

### Local Rules and Sovereignty

#### Rules Within Personal Realities
Rules do not exist only on the universal scale. An existence possessing complete sovereignty over a smaller Reality can establish local Rules within that territory.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] demonstrates this directly inside his inner Reality. When [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] attempted to invoke her crown and the power of Order, Victor declared that the power of Order was forbidden there. The ability immediately ceased functioning.[8]
This was possible because Victor possessed overwhelming ownership over the Reality containing them.
The example demonstrates:
A sufficiently complete local administrator can impose Rules upon powers existing inside that administrator's Domain.
The Rule does not automatically apply outside Victor's Reality.

#### Administrative Rights
Chapter 1168 expands this principle from local sovereignty toward universal administration. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explains that attempting to individually overpower every fundamental component of a Cosmos would be extraordinarily inefficient. A superior method would be to obtain sufficient administrator rights over the Reality itself.
With adequate administrative Authority, a ruler could change the system's operating Rules rather than defeating every component through brute force.[3]

### Conceptual Keys
Chapter 1168 describes some forms of conceptual Authority as functioning like keys. A Being may understand that a structure exists and possess enormous Energy while still lacking the appropriate conceptual access required to change it.
Fundamental Reality therefore distinguishes between:
- knowing the target exists;
- possessing enough power to affect it;
- possessing the correct conceptual Authority;
- and possessing administrative ownership over the system containing it.[3]
This helps explain why weaker Beings cannot simply reproduce the actions of Primordials by accumulating Energy. The obstacle can be one of Authority rather than quantity.

#### Existential Weight
Chapters 1167–1168 further establish that Concepts can increase the existential weight of a structure.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] demonstrates this to [[Norabell Elderblood]] by creating an ordinary subordinate dimension and a miniature galaxy supported by several higher Concepts. The ordinary dimension remains dependent upon the greater Reality and is extremely easy for Norabell to manipulate. The galaxy is far more difficult because Concepts such as <Negativity> and <Begin> are incorporated directly into its foundation.[14]
The additional conceptual foundation makes the structure metaphysically "heavier". However, Chapter 1168 clarifies that existential weight alone is not sufficient to make a complete Universe. Victor's galaxy still lacked the full conceptual Rules required for a genuinely independent Reality.[3]

### Creating Reality

#### Physical Creation
Creating matter, Energy, stars or planets does not by itself require reproducing the deepest Laws of Existence. Such objects can be created inside a Reality while relying upon the Rules already provided by that Reality.
This is how ordinary dimensions function. They borrow the deeper structure of the larger system.

#### Independent Reality
Creating a genuinely autonomous Universe requires something deeper. The creator must establish enough fundamental conceptual Rules for that Reality to function independently rather than merely existing as a structure dependent upon another Universe.[3]
This was precisely what [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Chapter 1167 miniature galaxy lacked. It looked real, possessed matter and cosmic structures, contained conceptually reinforced Eldritch replicas and had genuine existential weight, but it did not possess the complete conceptual architecture of a true independent Cosmos.[3]

#### Primordial Chaos as Creator
[[Primordial Chaos]] represents the clearest known example of the level required to establish such architecture. The original native Cosmos possesses fundamental Rules because its [[Chaos]] established them as part of Creation itself.
This is qualitatively different from an ordinary God creating an object by using powers inside an already-existing system.

### Enforcement
The Laws of Existence do not all require a conscious enforcer standing nearby. Different Rules are enforced through different mechanisms.

#### Structural Enforcement
Some Rules are embedded directly within Reality. Kali's failed Primordial ascension is an example. No visible cosmic officer physically stopped her. Creation itself refused to complete the transformation because her mentality did not satisfy the requirements of the Primordial office.[2]

#### Conceptual Enforcement
Conceptual hierarchy can prevent lower Authority from simply overriding higher-order Concepts.[2]

#### Primordial Enforcement
[[The Universal Tree]] actively enforces the World Tree planetary cycle.[13] The Primordials collectively intervene when disturbances threaten Balance.[12]

#### Domain Enforcement
The rule against unauthorized entry into another Primordial's Domain is recognized and respected by the other Primordial administrators.[5]

#### Creator-Imposed Enforcement
[[The Universal Tree]]'s prohibition prevents ordinary [[World Trees]] from reproducing.[7]

#### Administrative Enforcement
A sovereign administrator such as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] can locally prohibit specific powers inside a Reality under his control.[8]

### Circumvention and Exceptions

#### Overwhelming Power
Chapter 1168 establishes that one theoretical method of bypassing the normal conceptual "keys" is possessing enough raw power to force the issue.[3]
This should not be interpreted as meaning that any sufficiently strong attack automatically erases every metaphysical restriction. The amount and kind of power required depend upon the structure being challenged.

#### Correct Authority
A more efficient solution is to possess the appropriate Conceptual Authority. Rather than breaking a locked system, the wielder possesses the correct key.

#### Administrative Ownership
The highest approach is administration of the system itself. An administrator can potentially alter the Rules defining how the subordinate components operate.

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is among the greatest known native anomalies in relation to Rules.
Examples include:
- becoming an anomalous Progenitor through transformations that ultimately established new racial foundations;
- impregnating [[World Trees]] despite the Creator-imposed reproductive prohibition;[7]
- possessing an Eldritch nature capable of operating outside many ordinary native assumptions;
- establishing absolute local Rules within his Personal Universe;[8]
- creating conceptually reinforced structures with unusually high existential weight;[14]
- and developing toward a state in which he could theoretically acquire administrative Authority over the native Cosmos itself.[6][3]
Victor should nevertheless not be described as completely immune to every Law of Existence. His entire late cosmological development is based upon understanding which Rules can be bypassed, which require correct Authority and which demand control over the system itself.

#### Outer Gods
[[Outer Gods]] possess a natural advantage against native Rules because their deeper conceptual architecture originates outside the local Cosmos. Azathoth describes Eldritch [[Chaos]] as fundamentally different from native beings and capable of operating beyond Rules that normally bind existences born purely within the Cosmos.[6]
However, even this relationship contains nuance. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is unusual precisely because he is simultaneously an Eldritch Chaos and a Being born inside the native Reality. A completely foreign Chaos cannot normally occupy the native supreme position because the established Rules reject such replacement. Victor potentially can because those same native Rules recognize him as part of the Cosmos.[6]
This paradox is one of the primary reasons Azathoth considers Victor capable of taking over the vacant throne.

### Advanced Civilizations and Physical Laws
The technological classification introduced in the Upper-Sector material should not be confused with direct mastery of the deepest Laws of Existence.
Highly advanced civilizations can possess extraordinary control over:
- Space;
- Time;
- physical constants;
- dimensional engineering;
- artificial Universes;
- and the ordinary laws of physics.[15]
The Empire of Light and comparable civilizations operate on a technological level where manipulating Space-Time and large-scale physical systems is ordinary.
This does not automatically mean every member of such a civilization can rewrite:
- Life as a fundamental Aspect;
- Death itself;
- Infinity;
- Soul;
- or the source-code-level conceptual Rules established by [[Primordial Chaos]].

Civilization tiers demonstrate control over increasingly extreme physical and cosmological systems, while complete conceptual administration belongs to a different metaphysical level.

### Akashic Records
Main article: [[Akashic Records]]
The Akashic Records and the Laws of Existence are likewise related but distinct.
Akashic contains the Records of Creation.
The Laws determine the framework according to which Creation operates.
Chapter 965 demonstrates that Akashic possesses enough influence to prevent the System from investigating particular hidden anomalies and to synchronize its alternate manifestations after the timeline convergence.[9]
This should not be converted into the claim that Akashic personally wrote every Rule governing the Universe. The Records and the operating Rules are different cosmological functions.

### Relationship with Primordial Chaos
Main article: [[Primordial Chaos]]
The complete current model places Primordial Chaos at the deepest known level of the native Rule structure.
Primordial Chaos was not merely a powerful entity existing inside an already completed Universe. It was associated with the construction of the system itself.
The present Cosmos therefore contains two different kinds of relationships to Rules:
Beings living under the Rules.
and:
the existence responsible for establishing the framework in which those Rules operate.
The surviving [[Regulators of Existence]] belong to the first category despite their enormous Authority. They are administrators and components of the machine, but they remain bound by the greater structure whose operation they maintain.[3]
A complete Primordial Chaos occupies the much more fundamental position of the existence capable of establishing or administering the architecture as a whole.

### Relationship with Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s development increasingly revolves around understanding the distinction between using Reality and administering Reality.
Early Victor simply became powerful enough to ignore ordinary social rules. Later Victor acquired powers capable of distorting Space, Time, Souls and Reality. As a God, he learned the Laws belonging to Divine Concepts. As an Eldritch existence, he developed the ability to bypass restrictions that native beings could not. As ruler of his Personal Universe, he gained the ability to impose local operating Rules.
By Chapters 1167–1168, his focus had advanced again. Victor was studying:
- existential weight;
- conceptual foundations;
- metaphysical keys;
- fundamental cosmic components;
- and administrator rights over Reality itself.[14][3]
His objective is therefore no longer adequately described as:
becoming powerful enough to break the Rules.
The greater development is:
Understanding the Rules → obtaining their keys → obtaining Authority over the system → potentially becoming capable of rewriting the system itself.
This distinction is directly relevant to the vacant cosmic throne and Victor's possible future relationship with [[Primordial Chaos]].

### Notes
- The novel uses Rule, Law, Conceptual Law, Domain, Authority and similar terminology in different contexts. They should not automatically be treated as interchangeable.
- Chapter 269's explanation is intentionally broad and applies to Rules imposed by rulers in general; it predates the detailed cosmological distinction between local Rules and fundamental conceptual architecture.[1]
- Chapter 965 calls Destiny the Primordial who "made the rules for everything", but Chapter 1168 later identifies [[Primordial Chaos]] as the existence responsible for the foundational conceptual Rules of the Cosmos. Destiny's statement is therefore best contextualized around the old timeline/Destiny structure rather than used to identify Destiny as the ultimate source of Creation itself.[9][3]
- The greater one's Divine Authority over a high-ranking Concept, the more clearly Reality can be perceived; this is a property of high conceptual Authority, not an independent prohibition.[10]
- [[The Universal Tree]]'s World Tree reproduction restriction is explicitly described as a Creator-imposed prohibition, making it different from a fundamental Concept such as Death or Infinity.[7]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] circumventing a specific Rule does not establish blanket immunity to every Law of Existence.
- Primordial non-interference is primarily a matter of Domain jurisdiction and the catastrophic consequences of clashes between fundamental Aspects; Primordials are physically capable of fighting one another.[5]
- Other Cosmoses possess their own Rules and Primordial structures.[6]
- The statement that established Rules are eternal appears in the context of explaining why one foreign [[Chaos]] cannot simply replace the Chaos associated with another established Cosmos. It should not be generalized into the claim that no Rule can ever be altered by sufficiently high Authority.[6]
- An advanced civilization manipulating Space-Time and physical laws should not automatically be classified as rewriting the deepest source-code-level Laws described in Chapter 1168.
- Chapter 1168's computer, key and machine comparisons are explanatory analogies used by Victor. Reality is not stated to literally be computer software.
- The complete erasure of a fundamental Primordial Aspect differs from destroying the consciousness or manifestation occupying that position. The former can alter the functioning of the Cosmos itself.[6][3]
