### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 1074 (Mentioned)
Order, referred to by the cosmic designation <Order>, is a mysterious male Overlord and one of the exceptional Beings known to exist within the wider Cosmos. Very little has currently been revealed about his identity, civilization or abilities, but his existence was significant enough to attract the personal attention and fear of the Judge of the Abyss.[1]
During [[Victor Elderblood]]'s confrontation with the Primordial authorities, records from the past revealed that the Judge had repeatedly advocated eliminating extraordinary Beings whose development frightened him. Among the examples shown were <Light>, <Order> and <Endless>. In Order's case, the Judge specifically complained that he was "gaining Power in an unnatural way" and argued that the other Primordials should eliminate him.[1]
No direct appearance of Order has yet been shown, and the novel has not established the source of his unusual growth, the nature of the Sector he rules, his Race, his civilization or his present relationship with the other major cosmic factions.

### Physical Appearance
Order's physical appearance has not yet been revealed.
Unlike figures such as Light or [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]], Order has not received an on-page physical introduction in the currently available material. His species, height, facial features, clothing and supernatural form therefore remain unknown.
The masculine pronoun used by the Judge when discussing Order establishes that Order is male.[1]

### Personality
Almost nothing is presently known about Order's personality.
The only direct information concerning him comes from the Judge of the Abyss, who considered Order's increasing strength dangerous enough to advocate his elimination.[1] This statement describes the Judge's fear of Order's growth rather than Order's motives or moral character.
Consequently, it is currently unknown whether Order is benevolent, hostile, expansionist, isolationist or aligned with any of the larger cosmic powers.
The fact that his power was described as growing through an "unnatural" process should likewise not automatically be interpreted as evidence that Order was violating the Balance. During the same confrontation, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] accused the Judge of repeatedly attempting to destroy exceptional Beings because he feared them rather than because they had genuinely crossed the boundaries that normally justify Primordial intervention.[1]

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Order was first revealed indirectly during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s confrontation with [[The Judges of The Abyss]] concerning the Judge's long history of interference with exceptional existences.
Victor exposed records from the distant past showing multiple occasions in which the Judge attempted to convince the other Primordial Beings to eliminate individuals whose growth he considered threatening.[1]
Three examples were shown in rapid succession.
The first concerned Emperor <Light>, whom the Judge considered exceptionally dangerous because Light had apparently been chosen by a higher or otherwise significant figure. The second concerned Order. The Judge stated that Order was gaining power through an unnatural process and immediately proposed his elimination. The third concerned <Endless>, whose continued activity caused the Judge to complain that Death should keep him under control.[1]
These records were important because the Primordials normally claimed neutrality toward the development of ordinary existence. Direct intervention was supposed to occur only when a Being genuinely threatened the cosmic Balance. Victor argued that the Judge had repeatedly exceeded that role, targeting exceptional individuals simply because their development made him afraid.[1]
[[The Universal Tree]] recognized that the Judge had made similar complaints throughout history so frequently that the behavior had effectively become routine. Victor then exposed additional records in which some of the exceptional Beings targeted by the Judge were actually eliminated.[1]
The chapter does not establish whether Order was one of the individuals successfully killed.
No death, defeat or disappearance of Order was shown, and no later explanation has yet established what ultimately happened after the Judge proposed eliminating him. His present status should therefore remain unknown until the novel directly confirms otherwise.
Order's mention also places him within the same broader cosmic generation of extraordinary Beings that produced entities such as Light and Endless. These individuals existed far beyond the ordinary planetary Gods familiar to Victor's original world and belonged to the level of powers that increasingly became relevant after Victor's rise into an Overlord himself.

### Overlord Status
Order is classified as an Overlord, placing him within the extremely small group of Beings who reached supremacy over a cosmic Sector.
An Overlord is not merely another name for an especially powerful God. The title represents a Being who has achieved effective supremacy within their Sector, generally becoming the strongest power or ruler capable of dominating its major civilizations and competing factions.[2][3][4]
Known Overlords and former Overlords include individuals such as:
- [[Victor Elderblood]]
- <Light>
- <Darkness>

- <Law>
- <Insect>
- <Endless>
- [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]] (Formerly)

Order's exact Sector, territory, civilization and method of attaining Overlord status have not yet been revealed.

### Skills & Abilities
Order's complete abilities remain unknown. Only characteristics directly supported by his status and Chapter 1074 should presently be indexed.

#### Overlord-Level Existence
- Overlord: Order is classified among the known Overlords of the wider Cosmos. Overlords represent the apex political and supernatural powers of their respective Sectors.[2][3][4]
- Sector Supremacy: By definition, an Overlord has attained a position of dominance within a Sector rather than merely ruling an individual planet, country or Pantheon.
- Exceptional Cosmic Standing: Order was important enough for the Judge of the Abyss to specifically monitor his development and advocate his elimination.[1]
- Unknown Territory: The Sector under Order's authority has not been identified.
- Unknown Civilization: Nothing has been revealed regarding the species, Empire, Pantheon or civilization associated with him.

#### Abnormal Power Growth
- Accelerated / Unnatural Power Growth: The Judge of the Abyss explicitly stated that Order was "gaining Power in an unnatural way."[1]
- The mechanism responsible for this development has not been explained.
- It is unknown whether Order was absorbing external powers, evolving through an abnormal racial characteristic, using technology, gaining conceptual understanding, exploiting the System or progressing through an entirely different method.
- The statement should therefore be treated as evidence of unusual growth rather than assigned to a specific ability without further information.

#### Exceptional Potential
- Exceptional Existence: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s reconstruction of past events grouped Order with other extraordinary Beings whose emergence was significant enough to attract the Judge's personal attention.[1]
- Threat Recognition: The Judge considered Order dangerous enough that his response to observing Order's growth was to request his immediate elimination.[1]
- Primordial Attention: Very few ordinary Beings attract the direct concern of Primordial authorities. Order's growth was significant enough to become a subject of discussion among them.[1]

### Trivia
- Order's first known mention occurs in Chapter 1074.[1]
- The novel presents his name using the cosmic designation <Order>.
- Order is male; the Judge explicitly refers to him using masculine pronouns.[1]
- The Judge of the Abyss considered Order's growth abnormal and proposed eliminating him.[1]
- Order was mentioned alongside <Light> and <Endless> as examples of exceptional Beings who drew the Judge's attention.[1]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] used these historical records to argue that the Judge interfered with extraordinary Beings because he feared them rather than because every target genuinely threatened the Balance.[1]
- The chapter does not confirm whether the Judge's attempt to have Order eliminated succeeded.
- The Overlord Order should not be confused with [[Violet Elderblood Snow]]'s Divinity of Order.
