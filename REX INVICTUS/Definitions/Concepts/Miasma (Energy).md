Miasma, also known as Demonic Energy, is a supernatural form of Energy associated primarily with Demons, Hell and the Negative side of Creation. It is the principal energy naturally produced and manipulated by Demons, serving as both a fundamental component of their supernatural physiology and the environmental energy sustaining the infernal realms. Unlike ordinary Mana, which can be given different attributes through magical techniques, Miasma possesses characteristic corrosive, poisonous and destructive properties that vary in potency and manifestation according to its origin, concentration and the nature of its wielder.[1][2]
Miasma is not exclusively an offensive ability. It is described as the [[Natural Energy]] of Hell, performing a role comparable to the environmental Energy that sustains other supernatural worlds. Its concentration influences the development of the infernal environment, the creation and evolution of Demons, and even the passage of Time within Hell. Excessive quantities can become harmful to the dimension itself, while insufficient concentrations can interfere with the natural processes through which new Demons emerge. Consequently, Miasma is simultaneously a racial Energy, an environmental resource and an important component of infernal civilization.[3]
Although Miasma is predominantly associated with Demons, it is not restricted to beings born as members of that race. Certain [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] Gods and exceptionally versatile supernatural beings can also manipulate it, while Victor Elderblood developed control over Demonic Energy after becoming the King of Hell and incorporating multiple supernatural systems into his existence. Its relationship with other energies is particularly important: Miasma belongs to the Negative side of the cosmic spectrum, but it should not be confused with the unrestricted Negativity possessed by Victor or the Primordial responsible for that fundamental aspect of Creation.[2][3]

### Overview

#### Nature of Miasma
Miasma is a form of Energy possessing a distinct Demonic characteristic. Ordinary manifestations are commonly associated with dark or black coloration and can behave like a corrosive, venomous substance capable of entering living bodies and damaging the surrounding environment. Its destructive nature distinguishes it from ordinary Mana, which is more versatile and can be adapted to a greater variety of applications through conventional [[Magic]]. Nevertheless, the fundamental nature of all Energy remains related, allowing sufficiently knowledgeable individuals to develop unconventional applications of Miasma instead of restricting it to the abilities naturally possessed by Demons.[1][2]
The characteristics of Miasma depend upon the being generating or manipulating it. Demons possess different supernatural affinities, and the Energy produced by particularly powerful individuals may acquire characteristics associated with their personal nature. This can result in variations involving extreme toxicity, corrosion, Darkness, fire-like properties or other effects. The difference between ordinary and highly concentrated Miasma is equally important, since an attack that merely causes pain and weakness at a lower level can become fatal when produced by a sufficiently powerful Demon.[1][4][5]
Miasma also serves as a source of supernatural power. High-ranking Demons possess immense quantities of it and can use that Energy to reinforce their bodies, project attacks, sustain Demonic techniques and develop abilities that resemble Magic. Its destructive tendencies do not prevent more sophisticated applications, but manipulating it beyond ordinary racial instincts requires an understanding of its characteristics and the principles governing supernatural Energy.[2]

#### Origin and Classification
Within the cosmological framework of [[Energy Types]], Miasma is classified as a form of Demonic Energy associated with the Negative side of Creation. It is commonly grouped with Darkness and other infernal energies because they possess related characteristics and are generally opposed to the Holy and Sacred energies associated with Angels and certain Gods. This relationship does not make every manifestation of Darkness identical to Miasma, nor does it establish that all negative-aligned supernatural powers operate through the same mechanics.[2][5]
The Primordial duality of Positivity and Negativity underlies much of the cosmic structure governing supernatural Energy. Negative-aligned phenomena can encompass many different characteristics, including Darkness, decay, corruption and destructive emotional influences. Miasma represents a more specialized expression of supernatural Energy associated with Demonic existence rather than the totality of those phenomena. A Demon capable of manipulating Miasma does not automatically possess conceptual authority over Negativity itself.[2]
The same distinction applies to Divinity. Miasma can be generated and used by Demons without their possessing a Divine Concept, while Demonic Gods may combine it with the authorities granted by their Divinities. A God whose powers involve Despair, Rottenness or Corruption can therefore produce effects superficially similar to those of especially destructive Miasma without the two systems being identical. When employed together, however, Divine authority and Miasma can produce considerably more dangerous results.[5]

### Miasma in Hell

#### Natural Energy of the Infernal Realms
Miasma constitutes an important part of Hell's supernatural environment. Rather than existing solely as an Energy released by individual Demons, it naturally permeates infernal dimensions and influences the conditions under which Demonic beings live and develop. The Seven Hells associated with the Biblical afterlife possess their own particular environments, and the Demons emerging within them can develop abilities corresponding to those characteristics.[3]
The relationship between Miasma and Hell is therefore comparable in broad function to the relationship between a supernatural world and the Energy that sustains its ecosystem. Its concentration is not merely a measurement of how dangerous an area is; it influences the processes through which the realm develops and produces inhabitants. The nature of individual infernal environments also helps explain why Demons originating from different Hells may possess different racial affinities despite all belonging to the same larger Demonic system.[3]

#### Miasma and Time Dilation
One of Miasma's most unusual environmental properties is its influence upon the flow of Time within Hell. Infernal dimensions can experience substantially different temporal conditions from those prevailing on Earth, with the concentration of Miasma contributing to that difference. Extremely dense concentrations can produce undesirable temporal effects, while excessive Energy can also become harmful to the infernal environment itself.[3]
Following his assumption of authority over Hell, [[Victor Elderblood]] worked alongside [[Aline Elderblood Valefar]] and [[Vepar Elderblood]] to reduce the excessive Miasma distributed throughout the realm. Their modifications stabilized its environmental conditions and reduced the unwanted effects associated with its previous concentration. The resulting temporal relationship was approximately five years in Hell for every one year on Earth, providing a more manageable flow of Time while preserving the supernatural characteristics necessary for the realm to function.[3]
This adjustment demonstrates that Miasma can influence temporal conditions indirectly through its relationship with the infernal dimension. It does not establish that an ordinary Demon can freely manipulate Time simply by releasing Miasma, since the environmental effects involve the structure and conditions of Hell rather than unrestricted individual authority over the Concept of Time.

#### Demon Generation and Evolution
Miasma is closely connected to the processes responsible for producing new Demons within the Biblical infernal system. The Seven Hells receive condemned Souls and possess environments that gradually generate Demonic existences. Although the precise period required for a Demon to emerge is inconsistent, the surrounding Miasma concentration has a substantial influence upon the process.[3]
This relationship produced an unexpected problem when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] reduced the excessive Miasma distributed throughout Hell. Although the changes improved the realm's stability, they also reduced the rate at which the Seven Hells could generate Demons. The same substance that had contributed to excessive environmental and temporal effects was necessary for the continued development of the infernal population.[3]
Victor resolved the problem with the assistance of Aline and [[Lily Elderblood]]. Seven specially prepared spheres were created to correspond to the seven subordinate Hells, while a concentrated source of Miasma was isolated within a controlled location. By directing the accumulated Energy toward those spheres, the system channeled Miasma into the individual infernal dimensions rather than allowing it to disperse inefficiently throughout the entire realm.[3]
The resulting concentration was significantly greater than when the same Energy had been distributed across Hell. Because each sphere maintained a connection to its corresponding infernal environment, the accumulated Miasma could nourish the seven dimensions much more efficiently. This arrangement increased Demon production beyond its previous rate while allowing the larger realm to retain the improved environmental and temporal stability established by Victor's reforms.[3]
The process demonstrates that Miasma functions as more than an expendable energy reserve. Within Hell, its distribution and concentration influence the reproduction and development of the supernatural race inhabiting the realm. Increasing the total quantity indiscriminately is therefore not necessarily the most effective method of strengthening the infernal system; controlling how the available Energy reaches the locations where it is required can produce superior results.

#### The Seven Hells and Specialized Miasma
The Seven Hells do not possess completely identical environments. Their differences affect the Demons originating within them and the abilities those Demons naturally develop. The Hell associated with Wrath, for example, possesses a strong relationship with Hellfire, producing Demons particularly suited to manipulating infernal flames. The realm associated with Despair has a different nature, being dominated by profound Darkness and a specialized form of Miasma associated with that environment.[3]
Demons originating within the Hell of Despair possess an exceptional affinity for the Darkness and Miasma characteristic of their birthplace. This connection distinguishes them from the flame-oriented Demons of Wrath, even though both groups belong to the same overarching Demonic Race. The former Horseman Death possessed particularly exceptional mastery of the powers associated with Despair, illustrating how an individual's development can greatly surpass the ordinary characteristics of Demons originating from the same environment.[3]
These environmental differences should not be interpreted as proof that every Hell possesses a completely independent category of Energy. They establish that the Miasma and supernatural conditions of particular infernal regions can possess different characteristics and produce correspondingly specialized Demons.

### Properties and Effects

#### Corrosion and Poisoning
Miasma is especially dangerous because of its corrosive and venomous characteristics. When ordinary Miasma enters a living being's body, it can produce severe pain, weakness and deterioration. More powerful manifestations can be substantially more destructive, with certain advanced forms capable of killing or consuming the affected target. The extent of these effects depends upon the potency of the Miasma and the victim's ability to withstand or counteract its influence.[1]
Its effects are not limited to externally visible injuries. Miasma can remain within an affected body and interfere with its normal condition, making internal contamination particularly dangerous. Because it is a supernatural Energy rather than an ordinary chemical poison, conventional physical recovery is not necessarily sufficient to eliminate its harmful effects.[1]
- Poison Manipulation: Miasma can produce venom-like effects that weaken and inflict pain upon susceptible living beings.[1]
- Corrosion Inducement: Its destructive characteristics allow it to deteriorate physical matter and living tissue.[1][5]
- Biological Manipulation (Harmful): Miasma entering a target's body can interfere with its normal physical condition and produce continuing injuries.[1]
- Statistics Reduction: Ordinary Miasma can weaken an affected being, reducing their ability to function normally while the contamination persists.[1]
- Damage Over Time: Persistent contamination can continue harming the affected target rather than producing only the immediate damage of the initial attack.[1]
- Regeneration Negation (Limited): Miasma can interfere with supernatural recovery, although its effectiveness depends upon the attack and the resistance of the affected being.[1]

#### Environmental Corruption
Miasma can also contaminate surrounding environments. When sufficient quantities are released into an area, their destructive properties can affect the ground and other physical surroundings, producing conditions dangerous to living beings. Especially powerful Demons can release concentrations capable of transforming the immediate battlefield into hostile territory rather than merely attacking a particular opponent.[1][5]
The greatest demonstrated environmental dangers arise when Miasma possesses extraordinarily high potency or is combined with other destructive powers. During Lilith's confrontation with [[Diablo]], her concentrated Miasma was sufficiently toxic to destroy matter upon contact. The enormous quantities released threatened devastating damage to the surrounding environment, requiring a barrier maintained by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and the [[Heavenly Father]] to contain the confrontation.[5]

#### Regeneration Interference
The harmful characteristics of Miasma can obstruct the recovery of supernatural beings. Rather than functioning only as physical injury, contamination can introduce a continuing destructive influence that a victim's regenerative abilities must overcome. This is particularly relevant when fighting opponents whose natural healing normally allows them to survive injuries that would kill ordinary beings.[1]
Lilith's battle with [[Diablo]] provides a more extreme example of regeneration interference, although an important distinction must be maintained. Lilith combined her exceptionally toxic Miasma with the Divine Concepts of Rottenness, Corruption and Despair. Diablo's regeneration eventually stopped functioning, but the precise individual contribution of Miasma cannot be separated from the other supernatural effects incorporated into her attacks.[5]
The feat consequently demonstrates the danger of combining Miasma with conceptual powers rather than establishing that every ordinary Miasma attack can completely negate the regeneration of an Ancestral Demon.

### Manipulation and Applications

#### Basic Miasma Manipulation
Demons can generate and manipulate Miasma as part of their supernatural physiology. Its fundamental applications include releasing Energy from the body, directing it toward opponents, surrounding physical attacks with it and maintaining a concentration around the user. The potency and complexity of these techniques vary significantly between ordinary Demons and ancient beings possessing exceptional Energy reserves or specialized abilities.[1][5]
- Energy Manipulation: Generating and directing Demonic Miasma through supernatural control.[2][5]
- Energy Projection: Releasing Miasma toward opponents as a direct supernatural attack.[5]
- Energy Aura: Surrounding the user's body with concentrated Miasma, producing an offensive or defensive supernatural presence.[5]
- Energy Coating: Covering physical appendages, weapons or other attack surfaces with Miasma.[5]
- Environmental Manipulation: Releasing sufficient Energy to alter the conditions of a surrounding area.[5]
- Power Combination: Integrating Miasma with other supernatural systems, including [[Magic]], Hellfire and Divinity.[2][5]

#### Demonic Magic
Despite its distinct characteristics, Miasma can serve as the foundation for techniques resembling conventional [[Magic]]. [[Merlin]], one of the greatest practitioners of Magic in supernatural history, successfully developed a new magical system after becoming a Demon. His transformation deprived him of access to the original Energy he had used as a Human, but his exceptional understanding of supernatural power allowed him to adapt his knowledge to the Miasma of Hell and the Demonic Energy contained within his body.[2]
Rather than abandoning Magic entirely, Merlin recreated its principles through Demonic Energy. He produced magical circles possessing a visibly different structure and character from those employed in conventional Human Magic, developing a method of reproducing spell-like effects through a power source traditionally regarded as incompatible with that system.[2]
The fundamental principle behind his achievement was that different supernatural powers remain forms of Energy despite possessing distinct names and characteristics. Each Energy has its own implicit rules, and mastering an unfamiliar type requires understanding those rules before attempting more advanced manipulation. Merlin found Demonic Energy less subtle than the Mana he had previously employed, but he was nevertheless capable of reproducing sophisticated supernatural techniques through it.[2]
Merlin's methods demonstrate that Miasma is not inherently restricted to poison, corruption or direct destruction. Its properties may complicate certain applications, but sufficiently advanced Energy control can allow the wielder to develop an independent magical system founded upon Demonic Energy.

#### Demonic Runes and Magical Formulas
Miasma can provide the Energy necessary to sustain supernatural techniques involving magical circles, inscriptions and Demonic spells. This application is distinct from treating Miasma itself as a Rune. As explained through Runes, the function of a runic technique depends upon the relationship between its symbolic language, the user's intention and the Energy supplied to execute the desired effect.[2][6]
Demonic Runes and Demonic [[Magic]] therefore represent methods of organizing or applying supernatural power, while Miasma is one of the relevant Energy sources associated with Demonic practitioners. The two concepts are closely related in practice but are not interchangeable.

#### Constructs and Projections
Miasma can also serve as a medium for supernatural projections. War, one of the Four Horsemen serving [[Diablo]] during the Demonic War, demonstrated the ability to form visual representations through his own distinct Demonic Energy. The technique allowed him to present information concerning events that had occurred elsewhere rather than using Miasma solely as a destructive attack.[4]
The manifestation was also visually unusual. Whereas ordinary Demonic Miasma is generally associated with black coloration, War's Energy possessed a reddish quality and a fiery character corresponding to his nature as the Horseman of War. This establishes that the appearance and specialized properties of Miasma can vary between powerful Demons.[4]

### Specialized Manifestations

#### Ordinary Demonic Miasma
Ordinary Miasma is the characteristic Demonic Energy commonly generated by members of the race. It can be released offensively, used to weaken opponents and incorporated into the techniques naturally developed by Demons. Its destructive properties include pain induction, poisoning, corrosion and limited interference with regeneration, although the effectiveness of these effects varies according to concentration and the relative strength of the target.[1]

#### Concentrated Hell Miasma
Hell's environmental Miasma can accumulate into exceptionally dense concentrations. Such accumulations are important to Demon generation, infernal development and temporal conditions within the realm. Excessively widespread concentrations can also produce undesirable environmental effects, making deliberate distribution and containment important for the continued functioning of Hell.[3]
The system developed by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Aline and Lily demonstrates the practical significance of concentrated Miasma. Instead of permitting the Energy to disperse throughout the entire infernal dimension, the accumulated quantity was directed through specialized channels into the Seven Hells, increasing its efficiency as a resource for producing Demons.[3]

#### Despair-Associated Miasma
The Hell associated with Despair possesses an environment dominated by profound Darkness and a specialized manifestation of Miasma. Demons emerging within this region develop an affinity with those conditions, and particularly exceptional individuals can reach far greater mastery than ordinary members of the same infernal lineage.[3]
This form of Miasma is distinct in its environmental association with Despair and Darkness. Its existence does not automatically establish that every Demon originating from the realm possesses the Divine Concept of Despair, since a racial affinity with a supernatural environment and conceptual authority over a Divinity are separate classifications.

#### War's Miasma
War possesses a distinctive form of Demonic Energy characterized by a reddish hue and fire-like qualities. His Miasma reflects the nature of his supernatural existence and can be manipulated for purposes beyond direct physical destruction, including the creation of projections displaying events to other individuals.[4]
- Miasma Manipulation: War can generate and control the Demonic Energy associated with his original supernatural physiology.[4]
- Energy Projection: He can use Miasma to present visual information to other beings.[4]
- Energy Variation: His Miasma possesses a red-tinged and fiery appearance distinct from the conventional black manifestations associated with many Demons.[4]

#### Lilith's Miasma
[[Lilith Elderblood]], the Mother and Progenitor of Demonkind, possesses one of the most destructive demonstrated forms of Miasma. Upon entering her True Demon Form, dense corrupt Miasma spreads across her tail and body while her physical power increases considerably. The substance is extraordinarily toxic and can destroy matter upon contact, making direct engagement dangerous even to opponents capable of fighting powerful Gods.[5]
Lilith can combine this Energy with Genesis, her God-Slaying sword, and her four confirmed Divinities of Beginning, Rottenness, Corruption and Despair. The simultaneous application of Miasma and conceptual powers allows her attacks to inflict several forms of supernatural damage. [[Diablo]]'s inability to easily withstand these combined effects illustrates the effectiveness of integrating racial Energy with Divine authority.[5]

#### Abyssal Miasma
Abyssal Miasma is among the forms of corruptive Energy associated with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s increasingly advanced supernatural capabilities. During the confrontation involving his younger clone and the [[Veillon Empire]], the interaction between Darkness, Abyssal Miasma and Soul manipulation was used against an enemy commander, producing a corruptive effect that the target could not counter through ordinary defensive measures.[7]
This application should be distinguished from ordinary Hell Miasma. The corruption was produced through the interaction of several powers, and the extent to which Abyssal Miasma alone could reproduce the same result is not independently established.

#### Liriel's Hell Miasma
[[Liriel Elderblood]], daughter of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Lilith, inherited an extraordinary combination of True Demon and True Dragon characteristics. Her Demonic powers include exceptionally toxic Hell Miasma, which she uses alongside Hellfire, Dragonfire and other inherited abilities. Her powers are particularly dangerous when multiple adverse conditions accumulate upon an opponent, allowing her to exploit the combined effects of her Demonic and Draconic heritage.[8]
Liriel's inherited Miasma demonstrates that Demonic Energy can remain a functional part of a hybrid supernatural existence even when combined with the physiology and Energy systems of another powerful race. Her abilities do not require her to abandon either heritage; she instead develops combat techniques integrating both.[8]

### Relationship With Other Energy Types

#### Miasma and Negativity
Miasma is associated with the Negative side of Creation but is not identical to the fundamental Concept of Negativity. Ordinary Demons manipulate a specific supernatural Energy associated with their race and environment, while the greater Concept of Negativity encompasses a much broader range of phenomena and Divine authorities.[2]
This distinction is particularly evident in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], whose authority over Negativity developed beyond the conventional capabilities of a Demon King. His ability to manipulate fundamental cosmic forces cannot be generalized to every Demon simply because they possess Miasma. The two forms of power remain related through their negative characteristics, but their scope and authority are fundamentally different.

#### Miasma and Magic
[[Magic]] and Miasma are separate forms of supernatural Energy, but their applications can overlap when manipulated by sufficiently knowledgeable practitioners. Conventional Magic is associated with Mana and its versatility, while Miasma possesses Demonic characteristics that make it naturally suited to more corruptive and destructive effects. [[Merlin]]'s development of Demonic Magic demonstrates that those limitations do not prevent one Energy from reproducing techniques traditionally associated with another system.[2]
The efficiency and characteristics of the resulting techniques depend upon the user's understanding of the Energy employed. Merlin recognized that Demonic Energy lacked the subtlety of his previous Mana, but his knowledge allowed him to adapt magical circles and techniques to the available power source.[2]

#### Miasma and Divine Energy
Miasma and [[Divine Energy]] are distinct systems, although a Demon who attains Godhood can employ both. Lilith demonstrates their compatibility by using her ordinary Demonic powers alongside the Divine Concepts of Beginning, Rottenness, Corruption and Despair. The resulting attacks possess effects that extend beyond the capabilities of ordinary Demonic Energy.[5]
The combination does not transform every manifestation of Miasma into a conceptual attack. Conceptual effects arise from the wielder's Divine authority, while Miasma remains the Demonic component of the technique unless the source explicitly establishes a more fundamental transformation.

#### Miasma and Holy Energy
Angels and Demons possess traditionally opposing supernatural characteristics. [[Holy Energy]] is particularly dangerous to Demonic beings, while powerful manifestations of Miasma can create hostile conditions for their Angelic opponents. The incompatibility reflects their respective supernatural natures, although individual strength, resistance and specialized abilities can alter the outcome of direct contact.[5]
[[Diablo]]'s attempt to combine Demonic and Angelic characteristics demonstrates that the two systems can interact under exceptional circumstances. During his battle against Lilith, he employed the [[Spears|Spear]] of Longinus while coating it in Miasma and subsequently attempted a more extreme transformation involving a Demonic heart and the hearts associated with Michael and [[Gabriel]]. His actions were intended to overcome the limitations of his original Demonic existence rather than representing an ordinary technique available to every Demon.[5]

### Limitations and Weaknesses
Miasma's effectiveness depends upon its density, the characteristics of the wielder and the resistance of the target. Ordinary concentrations can weaken or poison susceptible beings, but they do not automatically reproduce the effects of exceptionally powerful manifestations such as Lilith's corrosive Miasma. A sufficiently resistant supernatural being may withstand effects that would incapacitate an ordinary Human, while particular forms of Holy or Divine power can be especially dangerous to Demonic existences.[1][5]
Its corruptive tendencies also impose limitations upon more sophisticated applications. [[Merlin]] found Demonic Energy less subtle than conventional Mana, requiring him to adapt his understanding of magical principles to the distinct rules governing his new source of power. The successful development of Demonic [[Magic]] therefore depended upon exceptional knowledge rather than Miasma automatically possessing every property associated with ordinary Magic.[2]
Environmental concentration presents another limitation. Excessive Miasma distributed throughout Hell can damage the realm's stability and produce undesirable temporal conditions, while reducing its availability too severely can interfere with Demon generation. Maintaining appropriate quantities and directing the Energy where it is required is consequently essential for the continued development of infernal civilization.[3]
Miasma also should not be treated as inherently capable of unrestricted conceptual corruption, Soul destruction, Time manipulation or Reality Warping. Individual characters may achieve such effects by combining Miasma with Divinity, specialized supernatural techniques or other forms of Energy, but those combinations do not establish the same abilities for every ordinary Miasma user.
