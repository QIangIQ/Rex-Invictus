## MTWABV Canon

Eldritch Energy is a foreign form of Energy originating outside the native Universe. [[Victor Elderblood]] directly describes it as being comparable to the Attributeless Energy of another Universe: because it does not belong to the Reality in which Victor was born, its use naturally produces corruption and distortion, forcing affected matter, Energy, Rules, Concepts or Reality to increasingly follow the logic of the external cosmological system from which the Energy originates.[1] At sufficiently high levels it can corrupt existing Rules, alter conceptual systems, sustain Eldritch beings, power foreign Divinities, distort Reality, transmit dangerous information and contaminate native existence on physical, spiritual, conceptual and ontological levels.[2][3]

The most important canon clarification concerning Eldritch Energy came much later than its initial introduction. Earlier chapters primarily explained Eldritch beings through their foreign nature, corruption and incompatibility with native Rules. Victor eventually defined the Energy itself more precisely: Eldritch Energy comes from outside the Universe and does not belong to native Reality. He compared it to Attributeless Energy belonging to another Universe, with its corruptive nature arising because a foreign foundational Energy is being expressed inside a cosmological system whose Rules were not designed around it.[1]
This does not mean that every being who possesses Eldritch Energy was physically born outside Victor's Universe. Victor Elderblood was born within native Creation and later developed an Eldritch nature, his daughters inherit Eldritch characteristics through him, and native-born beings can become contaminated by foreign Eldritch influence. The Energy's cosmological origin and the birthplace of its current user are therefore separate questions.[2][3][1]

### Nature

#### External Origin
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] ultimately explained that ordinary Energy forms such as [[Divine Energy]], Demonic Energy, Mana, Ki and Spirit Energy are fundamentally different expressions of Energy produced inside the same Creation. Even native Attributeless Energy belongs to the Universe because it was the foundational Energy from which that Creation was shaped.[1]
Eldritch Energy is one of the exceptions.
External Universe → Foreign Foundational Energy → Enters Native Reality → Native Rules cannot treat it as ordinary internal Energy → Corruption / Distortion occurs
Victor describes it as the Attributeless Energy of another Universe. This analogy is especially important because it corrects the interpretation that Eldritch Energy is simply an extremely specialized native Energy. Its defining difference is cosmological origin.[1]

#### Corruptive Logic
Because Eldritch Energy does not belong to native Reality, its use causes the affected target to begin following rules and logic associated with the foreign system rather than remaining completely obedient to the local Creation.[3][1]
This corruption is not inherently moral. It does not mean "Evil", Demonic Miasma, sin or poison. The corruption is ontological: something is being rewritten by information and Energy whose natural operating assumptions come from somewhere else.
At lower levels, the result can be contamination or mutation. At higher levels it can affect Souls, Concepts, Divine authorities, environmental Rules or universal systems. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Chapter 1176 threat represents the extreme case: corrupting the native Concept of Negativity with Eldritch Energy until the corruption propagated through the rest of Reality like a virus.[7]

#### Eldritch Energy and Source Characteristics
The Energy's external origin does not prevent individual Eldritch beings from imprinting their own characteristics upon what they produce. [[Azathoth Elderblood]], [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and their descendants repeatedly demonstrate that Eldritch power becomes heavily colored by the Soul, Essence, Concepts and nature of its user.[2][3]
This distinction is important:
- Eldritch Energy itself is foreign foundational Energy originating outside the native Universe.[1]
- Eldritch Essence is the deeper identity and existential information belonging to a particular Eldritch being.[4]
- Individual Eldritch powers arise when that Energy and Essence are expressed through a specific Soul, Concept, bloodline or purpose.

### Eldritch Energy and Eldritch Essence
Eldritch Energy and Eldritch Essence are deeply connected but not identical.
Eldritch Energy
A foreign Energy belonging to an external cosmological system. When used in native Reality its incompatibility naturally produces corruption and distortion.[1]
Eldritch Essence
The deeper metaphysical information defining an Eldritch individual. Essence determines what the being is and can transmit specific characteristics, affinities and existential structure.[4]
[[Akasha Elderblood]] demonstrates the difference. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] did not merely supply her with Energy. He incorporated his Eldritch Essence into her construction so she would develop as an Eldritch Goddess whose existence was not entirely dependent upon the native Divine framework. Her resulting authority centered around Control while also containing an opposing tendency toward freedom or loss of control, reflecting Victor's intention that the administrator who controlled his civilization should herself remain free.[4]
[[Yog-Sothoth]] Elderblood was similarly created through the Essence of both Victor and Azathoth, making her a genuine fully fledged Eldritch Goddess rather than merely a native Goddess carrying a temporary reserve of foreign Energy.[8][9]

### Eldritch Contamination
Repeated exposure to Eldritch existence can gradually introduce Eldritch Energy into native beings. Azathoth observed that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s developed wives had accumulated approximately one-quarter Eldritch Energy through prolonged interaction with him.[2]
The change made them considerably more compatible with Eldritch phenomena. Rather than immediately losing their minds when encountering [[Outer Gods]] or foreign cosmic horrors, Victor's family could increasingly tolerate forms, information and Energies which would overwhelm ordinary native beings.[2]
Known consequences include:
- Eldritch Compatibility: Increased ability to contain and interact with Eldritch Energy.[2]
- Madness Resistance: Increased tolerance toward otherwise incomprehensible Eldritch forms and information.[2]
- Existential Adaptation: Eldritch characteristics become progressively less foreign to the target.[2]
- Energy Hybridization: Native Divine, Draconic, Vampiric or racial powers can coexist with Eldritch properties.[2]
- Potential Eldritch Development: A sufficiently Eldritch Soul may eventually develop genuine Eldritch Divinities or higher Eldritch states.[2][1]
Contamination is not automatically beneficial. Victor's family underwent the process while being protected by Victor and gradually adapting to his existence. Hostile or uncontrolled contamination can instead destroy identity, destabilize Reality or corrupt the target beyond recovery.

### Inheritance and the Elderblood Lineage
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s descendants represent a more stable route toward Eldritch development. Rather than acquiring foreign Energy only after birth, his daughters can possess Eldritch characteristics in their Soul and body from their original development.[2][1]
By Chapter 1171, the Elderblood family explicitly recognized that all of Victor's daughters possess an Eldritch component, although the degree varies significantly. Jeanne describes the daughters as partly Eldritch Goddesses and partly Primordial Goddesses, while only a smaller number possess Divinities specifically belonging to the [[Eldritch Pantheon]].[6]
Victor later confirms the same principle while teaching them about Energy: all of his daughters possess Eldritch Energy, some more than others, with [[Yog-Sothoth]] Elderblood and [[Nigredo Elderblood]] standing out particularly strongly.[1]
This produces several different developmental paths:
- Eldritch-Dominant Development: Yog-Sothoth is a fully fledged Eldritch Goddess.[9]
- Strong Eldritch Divinity: Nigredo possesses a Divinity deeply connected to the Eldritch.[14][9]
- Created Eldritch Existence: Akasha was deliberately formed with Victor's Eldritch Essence.[4]
- Hybrid Eldritch Concept: Moira combines Luck with Victor's Eldritch inheritance.[15]
- Potential Eldritch Primordialization: Kassandra can potentially become an Eldritch Primordial force of Destruction rather than competing for the native Primordial office.[1]
- Latent Eldritch Nature: Other daughters possess Eldritch Energy without necessarily developing a specifically Eldritch Divinity.[6][1]

#### Eldritch Primordial Forces
Chapter 1185 establishes that Eldritch development extends beyond ordinary Eldritch Godhood.
Kassandra Elderblood was born as an incarnation of Destruction. Under normal native development this would eventually place her in competition with Kali for the single native Primordial position corresponding to Destruction. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explains that Kassandra does not need to follow that route. Because she is half Eldritch in Soul and body, she can instead become an Eldritch Primordial force of Destruction—functionally the same type of fundamental Concept, but belonging to another Universe or Eldritch cosmological framework.[1]
Victor further states that Kali could potentially follow the same path in the future, although the transformation would be easier for Kassandra because the decisive factor is the Soul and Kassandra was born with an Eldritch Soul.[1]
This establishes several important principles:
- Eldritch cosmology can support its own Primordial Concepts.[1]
- An Eldritch Primordial version of a Concept does not necessarily need to replace the native Primordial of that Concept.[1]
- The Soul is crucial to Eldritch Primordial development.[1]
- Existing native beings may theoretically transition toward an Eldritch Primordial path, although this is more difficult than being born Eldritch.[1]

### Notable Eldritch Divinities and Expressions

#### Yog-Sothoth's Unreality and Nothingness
[[Yog-Sothoth]]'s early powers manifested through Space, Intangibility, Nullity and Reality instability. As she matured, the nature of her authority became much clearer.[8][5]
By Chapter 1160, Yog's current power is described as the manipulation of Unreality and Nothingness. She can control, dissolve or erase the validity of effects, Concepts and Realities, allowing her influence to override Rules established by the native [[Primordial Chaos]] within the range of her authority.[10]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family gives a direct example: if Yog wished to create Life on a planet which normally required a World Tree, she could simply remove the Rule requiring that World Tree. The result would not be ordinary native Life, however, because everything Yog affects becomes corrupted by her cosmic-horror nature and the resulting Life would emerge distorted.[10]
Yog is consequently one of the clearest examples of how Eldritch power does not merely overpower a Rule—it can invalidate the Rule's applicability.

#### Nigredo's Eldritch Darkness
[[Nigredo Elderblood]], daughter of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]], was born as a Primordial Goddess of Darkness whose nature is explicitly Eldritch.[14]
Her Darkness is described as the Darkness of a black hole in which being and non-being collapse together. A black hole was created inside Victor's Personal Reality as a consequence of her birth, and Nigredo later treats that singularity as her own.[14][16]
Nigredo's exceptionally strong Eldritch compatibility is why she, alongside Yog, could observe Victor's artificial Eldritch galaxy and regard the cosmic horrors within it as beautiful rather than instinctively recoiling from them.[17][9]

#### Nero's Eldritch Apocalypse
[[Nero Elderblood]] developed an Eldritch Divinity associated with Apocalypse, catastrophe and the circumstances producing an ending. Her Divinity emerged through the Eldritch side of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s lineage rather than solely through the native Divine framework.[2][18]

#### Akasha's Control
[[Akasha Elderblood]] was deliberately created using [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Eldritch Essence. Her primary Divinity developed around Control, with an opposing tendency relating to freedom and loss of control because Victor refused to create an administrator who could herself be completely controlled.[4]

#### Moira's Eldritch Luck
[[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]] combines [[Tyche]]'s extraordinary Luck with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Eldritch inheritance. Her Divinity, I Am the Privileged One, manifests through Eldritch Luck.[15]
Unlike ordinary Luck, which manipulates probability so an attack misses or Reality changes to keep the favored user safe, Moira's Eldritch Luck is aggressive and chaotic. An attacker may be devoured by spontaneously created Eldritch beings or eliminated as the surrounding world corrupts itself to satisfy Moira's preferred outcome.[15]
This is not a generic ability granted by Eldritch Energy. It is a specialized hybrid created from Luck plus Moira's Eldritch inheritance.

### Interaction with Reality

#### Reality Distortion
High-order Eldritch manifestations can destabilize Reality simply by existing without the source consciously casting separate techniques for every individual effect.
During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s investigation of The Abyss Realm, exposing an extremely small portion of his deeper Eldritch existence affected Space, gravity, temporal perception and the surrounding environment while overwhelming lower Eldritch beings with the difference in existential hierarchy.[12]
Observed manifestations include:
- Spatial Distortion[12]
- Gravity Distortion[12]
- Temporal Perception Distortion[12]
- Environmental Instability[12]
- Instinctive Terror[12]
- Existential Pressure[12]
The effect scales with the source. Ordinary low-level Eldritch creatures do not continuously destroy their environments merely by existing, while an Eldritch [[Chaos]] can distort local Reality through partial manifestation alone.

#### Reality Stabilization
Compatibility can be as important as raw strength when dealing with Eldritch phenomena. When Yog's unstable powers distorted Reality shortly after her arrival, ordinary Draconic authority did not completely solve the problem. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] accessed his deeper Eldritch side and used a compatible form of influence to stabilize her.[8]

#### Rule Corruption
Eldritch Energy can corrupt Rules rather than merely attack objects governed by those Rules.
Chapter 1183 makes this explicit. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family designed the [[Absolute Paradox]], Eldritch Energy was rejected because the project required two contradictory Rules to remain simultaneously valid. Eldritch Energy would instead corrupt the existing Rules, defeating the intended mechanism.[11]
Chapter 1185 explains why: Eldritch Energy belongs to another cosmological system and naturally distorts native Reality toward foreign logic.[1]

#### Native System Resistance
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Eldritch Soul demonstrates resistance to native universal observation. When The System attempted to inspect Moira while she remained within Victor's Soul, it was unable to penetrate his Eldritch existence despite being designed as the administrator of the native Universe.[19]
This should not be treated as an automatic property of every low-level Eldritch creature. Victor is described as a cosmic horror of the highest order, and the feat reflects his existential scale as much as the foreign nature of his Soul.[19]

### Madness and Information Hazards
The madness produced by Eldritch beings is not simply conventional telepathic Mind Manipulation. A sufficiently high Eldritch existence contains information, structure and Concepts which lesser minds are not capable of processing safely.
During the Abyss investigation, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] exposed his deeper nature for only an extraordinarily short period and surrounding creatures were immediately affected. [[Hela Elderblood]] required protection despite her own enormous supernatural status.[12]
Potential consequences include:
- Madness Inducement[12]
- Cognitive Overload[13]
- Soul Overload[13]
- Fear Inducement[12]
- Forbidden Knowledge Exposure[13]
- Psychological Collapse[12]
- Information Hazard[13]

#### Knowledge Through Observation
When [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]] observed [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s higher Eldritch form, perception itself began transferring genuine information and understanding into her consciousness. The process could have greatly increased her knowledge, but her mind and Soul could not safely process the quantity involved and Victor had to terminate the exposure.[13]
This demonstrates that high Eldritch beings can simultaneously function as sources of knowledge and cognitive hazards.

### Eldritch Runes
Main article: Runes
Eldritch Runes are the Runic expression of an external Eldritch language. Their defining characteristic is not simply greater Energy output. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explains that they function according to the same broad principle as other Runes—a language issues instructions to Reality—but because the language belongs to an external God, its symbols distort the surrounding world according to foreign Rules.[1]
This makes extensive Eldritch Runecraft dangerous even for its user. Victor states that he normally employs only a small number of Eldritch symbols at once because overusing them would cause the surrounding Reality to become excessively distorted or "messed up".[1]
Earlier manifestations already demonstrated this problem. Advanced Eldritch inscriptions could contain enough information to become cognitive hazards for beings attempting to understand them without sufficient mental or spiritual capacity.[20]
Victor also demonstrated constructive applications. During the Chapter 1159 strategic meeting, he used Eldritch-language Runes to provide mental protection and filter dangerous memories before allowing his family to observe information obtained through his unrestricted Eldritch manifestation.[21]
Later development produced Draconic-Eldritch Runecraft. [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]]'s Heptagrammakos mixed Draconic and Eldritch authority so thoroughly that an entire reinforced dimension became frozen and submerged in absolute darkness, ordinary spellcasting failed and the environment manifested thousands of Eldritch eyes.[22]
General distinction:
Native Rune → Gives a command through the language of native RealityEldritch Rune → Gives a command through an external language and distorts native Reality toward foreign Rules

### Abhoth and Programmable Corruption
[[Abhoth]] represents one of the clearest examples of intentionally engineered Eldritch corruption. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Azathoth created him while discussing methods of protecting Imperial forces from hostile corruption and mental or spiritual subversion.[21]
Victor describes Abhoth as living corruption. Everything touched by him can become corrupted, but unlike uncontrolled contamination, Victor can determine what the resulting corruption does.[21]
His intended Imperial function operates as a two-layer defensive system:
- Reverse Corruption: If an outside force attempts to corrupt a soldier carrying Abhoth's influence, the hostile source can be corrupted instead.[21]
- Detection: Victor is informed when the protected individual is corrupted or betrays the Empire.[21]
- Counter-Control: Through Abhoth's fragments, Victor can potentially establish control over the opposing side which initiated the corruption.[21]
- Distributed Protection: Victor planned to integrate Abhoth's influence into Imperial soldiers and citizens.[21]
The ability is derived from Abhoth and Victor's control rather than proof that every Eldritch user can arbitrarily program corruption.

### Eldritch Cosmogenesis
Chapters 1167–1168 greatly expanded the known creative applications of Eldritch power.
While teaching his daughters about dimensions, Concepts and existential weight, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] created an artificial galaxy using Negativity, Beginning and Eldritch to give the structure existence and Reality.[17]
The galaxy contained stars which, under deeper observation, were revealed as gigantic Eldritch cosmic horrors. Yog and Nigredo regarded them naturally while most of their sisters instinctively recoiled from the sight.[17]
Victor then clarified that the galaxy was not a truly independent living Reality. Its cosmic organisms were extremely convincing replicas, but they lacked the deeper Conceptual anchor possessed by genuine living Eldritch beings. Victor summoned genuine creatures, including [[Abhoth]], and allowed his daughters to compare them directly.[9]
For a complete Universe to exist independently, Victor explained that it requires the full collection of conceptual Rules supplied by a supreme foundation such as a [[Primordial Chaos]]. Victor did not yet possess that complete authority on his own, so the galaxy remained a sophisticated imitation rather than an autonomous Cosmos.[9]
He privately recognized that combining directly with Azathoth could potentially allow them to create a genuine Eldritch galaxy, but rejected the experiment because producing something on that scale could attract attention from greater external powers.[9]

### Conceptual and Systemic Corruption
The greatest stated destructive application of Eldritch Energy occurred during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s confrontation with the native Primordial Beings over [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]].[7]
Rather than attempting to defeat every Primordial Being individually, Victor threatened the system they existed to protect. Negativity permeated roughly half the native Universe and was deeply interconnected with Positivity. Victor proposed destroying his connection to Negativity and flooding the Concept with Eldritch Energy.[7]
The corruption would then spread through every universal function dependent upon Negativity.
Conventional Destruction
Damages or removes components while the surviving system continues operating according to the same underlying Rules.
Eldritch Corruption
Changes the Rules governing those components until the system itself begins functioning according to foreign logic.
Victor compares the process to a virus progressively infiltrating the functions of a system until everything is transformed into something else.[7]
He did not complete the action. The feat therefore remains a directly stated and seriously treated capability rather than a completed universal corruption event.
The cost would also have been extreme. Negativity had become deeply integrated with Victor's Soul; destroying and corrupting it would have effectively torn apart a massive portion of his own spiritual structure. Victor nevertheless believed his Eldritch nature was sufficient for him to survive and eventually recover.[7]

### Eldritch Chaos
An Eldritch [[Chaos]] is a foundational high-order Eldritch existence capable of supporting subordinate Eldritch beings, Concepts and Divine development.[2]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Azathoth are both associated with this classification, but the later chapters establish an important distinction between Victor's developing state and Azathoth's completed cosmological status.
[[Azathoth Elderblood]] is explicitly described as the [[Primordial Chaos]] of the [[Eldritch Pantheon]] and creator of its cosmic horrors.[6]
Victor, while extraordinarily high within the Eldritch hierarchy, admits during his cosmogenesis lesson that he does not yet possess the complete authority necessary to independently produce a fully anchored living Universe. Cooperation or union with Azathoth could overcome that limitation.[9]
Known characteristics associated with an Eldritch Chaos include:
- Pantheon Foundation: Can serve as the source around which Eldritch Gods and Divinities develop.[2]
- Assimilative Growth: Energy, information, matter and supernatural characteristics can be consumed and incorporated.[2]
- Divinity Consumption: Divine powers can be used as nourishment for further development.[2]
- Cosmological Independence: Development is not completely dependent upon the native Divine hierarchy.[2]
- Corruptive Creation: Creations naturally risk inheriting the source's Eldritch characteristics unless deliberately restrained.[3]
- Primordial Development: A mature Eldritch cosmological system can possess its own equivalent of Primordial Concepts and foundational forces.[1]
- Cosmic Expansion: Growth can eventually extend beyond the limits of a single native Reality.[2]

When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] entered the Abyss, he encountered enormous numbers of low-level Eldritch organisms. Their shared nature did not make them remotely comparable to Victor. ### Interaction with Native Creation
Native Reality and foreign Eldritch existence naturally conflict because they are based upon different cosmological foundations.
Azathoth originally entered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Cosmos from outside its native structure. The primordial administrators could confront and ultimately imprison her, but her existence could not simply be processed like an ordinary native Goddess because the Rules governing Life, Death and other native systems had not created her.[23]
Victor represents a unique hybrid case. He was born inside native Creation, developed through its races and Divine systems and simultaneously evolved into a high-order Eldritch existence. This gives him relationships with both native and external cosmological frameworks.[2][3]
By Chapter 1176, Victor himself describes the consequences of abandoning the native side completely: losing Negativity and the Divinities connected to it would weaken his authority over the current Universe, but he believed he would simply become more completely Eldritch and move closer to Azathoth's side of existence.[7]

#### Attributeless Energy
Earlier interpretations treated Eldritch Energy and Attributeless Energy as nearly opposite because Attributeless Energy lacks defined characteristics while manifested Eldritch power frequently appears highly distorted. Chapter 1185 provides the more precise explanation and supersedes that simplification.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] states that Eldritch Energy can be understood as the Attributeless Energy of another Universe.[1]

### Limitations
- Hierarchy Matters: Possessing Eldritch Energy does not make a being comparable to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] or Azathoth. Low-Level Eldritch organisms remain enormously beneath an Eldritch [[Chaos]].[12]
- Eldritch Energy Is Not an Automatic Ability Package: Unreality, Luck, Darkness, Apocalypse and Control belong to specific users rather than Eldritch Energy itself.
- Corruption Can Be Resisted: Sufficiently compatible or powerful beings can endure Eldritch influence, and repeated controlled exposure can increase resistance.[2]
- Foreign Does Not Mean Automatically Stronger: An Eldritch Concept is not guaranteed to defeat every native Concept. Strength, understanding, conceptual weight and existential hierarchy remain relevant.
- Information Can Harm Allies: Eldritch forms and Runes can injure friendly observers if their minds or Souls cannot process the information involved.[13]
- Runic Overuse Destabilizes Reality: Victor deliberately limits how many Eldritch symbols he uses because excessive external instructions would distort the surrounding Reality too severely.[1]
- Eldritch Energy Is Unsuitable for Some Precision Systems: It could not serve the [[Absolute Paradox]] because it would corrupt the Rules the weapon needed to preserve simultaneously.[11]
- High Concentrations Can Be Unsafe for Native Life: Victor rejected personally supplying Mars with his Energy because it had become too potent and too heavily infused with Eldritch characteristics to distribute casually through future generations.[24]
- Large-Scale Corruption Can Harm the User: Corrupting Negativity would have devastated Victor's own Soul because Negativity had become integrated deeply into his existence.[7]
- Cosmic Creation Requires Conceptual Anchors: Victor could imitate a living Eldritch galaxy but could not independently create a completely autonomous one without the full conceptual foundation associated with [[Primordial Chaos]].[9]
- Native Primordial Powers Can Oppose Eldritch Beings: Azathoth was successfully imprisoned by the native cosmic order despite her foreign origin.[23]
- Victor Is an Exceptional Hybrid: His ability to interact with both native and Eldritch systems should not be generalized to every Outer God.[3]

### Canon Clarifications
- Eldritch Energy explicitly comes from outside the native Universe. Chapter 1185 directly establishes this and supersedes interpretations treating its origin as purely existential rather than cosmological.[1]
- A native-born being can still possess Eldritch Energy. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his daughters demonstrate that the location of a being's birth and the cosmological origin of the Energy it carries are separate matters.[1]
- Eldritch Energy is analogous to the Attributeless Energy of another Universe. It is not simply the opposite of Attributeless Energy.[1]
- Its corruption comes from foreign incompatibility. The Energy does not belong to native Reality and therefore distorts that Reality according to external logic.[1]
- Eldritch corruption is not Demonic corruption. It is cosmological and ontological rather than moral or Miasma-based.
- All of Victor's daughters possess Eldritch Energy to some degree. Only some develop Divinities specifically belonging to the [[Eldritch Pantheon]].[6][1]
- The Soul is critical to deeper Eldritch development. Victor identifies Kassandra's Eldritch Soul as the main reason she can more easily become an Eldritch Primordial force.[1]
- Eldritch Primordial Concepts can coexist with native equivalents. Kassandra can become an Eldritch Primordial Destruction without necessarily replacing the native Primordial position Kali seeks.[1]
- Yog's developed authority is broader than her early Space/Intangibility/Nullity description. Her later power is identified as Unreality and Nothingness capable of invalidating Rules, effects, Concepts and Realities.[10]
- Nigredo's Eldritch Darkness is specifically tied to a black-hole state where being and non-being collapse.[14]
- [[Abhoth]]'s programmable counter-corruption is a specialized created ability. It should not be given automatically to every Eldritch user.[21]
- Moira's Eldritch Luck is unique to Moira. Eldritch Energy itself does not automatically grant Luck Manipulation.[15]
- Heptagrammakos is a Draconic-Eldritch Runic technique. Its spell suppression and dimensional freezing should not be generalized to every Eldritch being.[22]
- Victor's Chapter 1176 universal corruption was threatened, not completed. It demonstrates a stated capability and its intended mechanism rather than a finished feat.[7]
- Azathoth currently represents the completed Eldritch Primordial-[[Chaos]] foundation. Victor remains an Eldritch Chaos of immense rank but acknowledged in Chapter 1168 that he could not yet independently anchor a genuine Universe.[9][6]

### Development
The Eldritch system became relevant near the end of Volume 7 when Azathoth's origin outside the native Cosmos was revealed. Her foreign existence could not be treated as merely another native Goddess, and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] discovered that his own deeper nature was developing toward the same general class of being.[23][8]
[[Yog-Sothoth]] then demonstrated that foreign Eldritch characteristics could be combined with Victor's Essence to create a genuine descendant whose Divinities did not function according to ordinary native assumptions.[8][5]
In Volume 8, Azathoth explained the larger implications. Victor was developing into an Eldritch [[Chaos]], his descendants could inherit the same foundation, and his wives were gradually becoming contaminated through long-term exposure. [[Nero]]'s Apocalypse and Akasha's deliberately engineered Eldritch existence demonstrated that the system could support both inherited and created Divinities.[2][4]
Victor's Abyss expedition expanded the system from conceptual explanation into direct environmental demonstration. Higher Eldritch existence could distort Space, gravity, temporal perception and Reality while overwhelming lower minds and Souls through sheer informational incompatibility.[12]
Later chapters expanded Eldritch Runecraft, information hazards and the ability to combine Eldritch properties with other systems. Victor's interaction with Albedo demonstrated that perception of a high-order Eldritch being could transmit genuine knowledge while simultaneously threatening the observer's mind and Soul.[20][13]
Volume 9 dramatically expanded practical Eldritch applications. Victor used Eldritch Runes during his interrogation of a foreign God, while Victor and Azathoth created [[Abhoth]] as programmable living corruption capable of protecting Imperial citizens against hostile corruption.[21]
Yog's mature Unreality and Nothingness demonstrated direct Rule invalidation, while Nigredo revealed an Eldritch form of Darkness corresponding to a singularity where being and non-being collapse.[10][14]
Victor later incorporated Eldritch power into artificial cosmogenesis, creating a fake Eldritch galaxy and explaining the difference between reproducing form and creating a genuinely conceptually anchored Universe.[17][9]
The Elderblood family subsequently confirmed that all of Victor's daughters possess some Eldritch component, although only certain daughters develop explicitly Eldritch Divinities.[6]
[[Scathach]] demonstrated mature Draconic-Eldritch Runecraft through Heptagrammakos, Moira manifested Eldritch Luck and Victor threatened to infect Negativity until corruption propagated through the native Universe.[22][15][7]
Chapter 1183 explicitly clarified that Eldritch Energy corrupts existing Rules, while Chapter 1185 finally provided the most precise definition of the Energy itself: it is a force from outside the native Universe, comparable to the Attributeless Energy of another Universe, whose incompatibility causes native Reality to distort according to foreign logic.[11][1]
The same lesson also established the existence of Eldritch Primordial development, revealing that Kassandra could become an Eldritch Primordial force of Destruction and that external cosmological systems can possess their own equivalents of native Primordial Concepts.[1]
