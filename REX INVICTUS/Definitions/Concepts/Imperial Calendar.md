Only dates explicitly supported by the novel are treated as exact dates. Undated developments are preserved in their proper sequence and contextual period rather than being assigned invented years.
The Imperial Calendar is the official chronological system of the Elderblood Empire. Its epoch begins with the formal foundation of the Empire under [[Victor Elderblood]], when the former political order of Earth was abolished, the planet was renamed [[Yggdrasil]] and Imperial history began counting from Year One.[1]
The Calendar later became particularly important during the Empire's greatest temporal-development project. Victor, Natalia Elderblood, [[Jeanne Elderblood]] and the other Empresses established a civilization-scale Time acceleration intended to give the Empire approximately 200,000 years of internal development while roughly one year passed outside the ritual.[2] The Imperial Calendar consequently records the historical Time actually experienced by the Empire rather than automatically corresponding to Time experienced by observers outside the accelerated region.
The system chronicles the transformation of Victor's civilization from a newly unified supernatural planet into an interplanetary and eventually Galactic Empire. Within its first millennium it developed specialized Legions and civilization-wide institutions; by approximately the 30,000th year its mother galaxy had been broadly populated; from the 30,000th year onward genetic development radically increased the baseline of Imperial citizens; from approximately Year 70,000 the Imperial galaxy itself began being converted into a defensive weapon; and by Year 100,000 the Empire had become a mature Galactic civilization preparing for war against the rulers of the Upper Sectors.[3][4][5] Near the end of the project, Mars replaced Yggdrasil as the Imperial capital, a World Tree network was established to make communication and transport effectively instantaneous across Imperial territory, and the lower Sectors containing [[Nightingale]], Samar and Arcane were drawn into Victor's Middle Sector.[6][7][8][9] Chapter 1183 then explicitly places the ritual at approximately 190,000 years, with the original demographic target already achieved but Victor refusing to stop before the planned deadline.[10]
Only dates explicitly supported by the novel are treated as exact. Events whose specific Imperial year is unknown are placed within their confirmed chronological period rather than being assigned invented dates.

### Early Imperial Expansion
The exact Imperial year of most developments immediately following the first week is not specified. The novel therefore supports their sequence, but not arbitrary year assignments.

#### Milky Way Survey
After becoming Overlord, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] immediately recognized that possessing [[Yggdrasil]] was insufficient for a civilization expected to face the Upper Sectors. By Chapter 1088, the Milky Way had been practically completely mapped. Imperial expansion discovered numerous living worlds, approximately 300 World Tree seedlings and another three more developed [[World Trees]], which were connected to the growing network associated with Victor.[22]
The survey simultaneously revealed a demographic problem. Victor's Sector contained far more habitable and strategically useful territory than the existing Imperial population could occupy. Even cloning alone could not immediately populate the available galaxies. This population shortage became one of the reasons Victor treated Time itself as a strategic resource.[22]

#### Planning the Time Project
Between Chapters 1089 and 1093, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], his wives and Imperial researchers began developing the mechanisms necessary to accelerate the development of an entire civilization. The system required far more than Natalia simply using her Time Divinity on individual people. The [[Dragon Heart]], [[World Trees]], Divine Authorities, Runes, technology, spatial infrastructure and enormous quantities of Energy had to operate together across astronomical distances.[23]
The objective was to solve a problem Victor could not overcome through personal strength alone. An Emperor could become extraordinarily powerful in a short period, but a civilization needed generations of researchers, soldiers, administrators, engineers, populations and cultural development. The Empire therefore intended to manufacture the historical Time required for those institutions to mature.[22][24]

#### Expansion Beyond Yggdrasil
Imperial operations increasingly moved beyond the original supernatural territories. By Chapter 1112, another alien world was being conquered and secured by Imperial soldiers and orbital forces rather than requiring [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to personally appear on every battlefield.[25]
[[Akasha Elderblood]] and the enormous network of subordinate Artificial Intelligences became increasingly important as territorial expansion accelerated. Administrative, military, scientific and economic information could be coordinated across worlds while Victor and the Empresses concentrated upon decisions requiring their direct authority.[26][27]
The Empire also began formalizing long-term reforms concerning education, racial representation, merit, population policy, military organization and resource allocation. The political structure was therefore being designed for continued interstellar expansion rather than functioning merely as a temporary coalition of Victor's personal allies.[26][27]

#### The Upper-Sector Threat
Evidence of hostile foreign interference became increasingly difficult to ignore. By Chapter 1126, Imperial forces had encountered surveillance and biological experimentation connected to civilizations beyond [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s immediate territory.[28]
Victor subsequently created an independent scouting clone whose deeper metaphysical connections were deliberately severed so that its destruction could not provide an enemy with a path toward his true body or family.[29] The clone eventually encountered the Empire of Light. The encounter ended with foreign military infrastructure destroying not merely the clone but the surrounding Solar System, proving that Upper-Sector warfare operated on a scale far beyond the old conflicts of Earth.[30]
Victor consequently identified rulers or supreme powers associated with <Light>, <Darkness>, <Emperor> and <Law> and concluded that eventual war with the civilizations beyond his Sector was unavoidable.[31]

#### Sector-Wide Mapping
In Chapter 1136, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] extended his Cosmic Consciousness throughout his Mid-Level Sector until he encountered its external barrier. He then called to the [[World Trees]] distributed throughout the region and received millions of connections.[32]
Using those connections as reference points, Victor constructed a map of the galaxies throughout his Sector and transmitted the information to Akasha. Teleportation nodes were subsequently established upon World Tree-bearing worlds, giving the Empire a pre-existing network through which future colonization and military logistics could operate.[32]
Imperial worlds could now be assigned specialized purposes, including:
- population centers;
- agricultural worlds;
- resource-extraction worlds;
- automated industrial planets;
- military strongholds;
- research territories;
- and strategic teleportation or logistical nodes.[32]
Victor could temporarily substitute for the Life-supporting role of a World Tree and maintain large numbers of planets personally, but he preferred worlds capable of operating independently rather than forcing the entire civilization to depend permanently upon his direct attention.[32]
By this period, moving or manipulating stars, black holes and other astronomical structures had become a practical subject of Imperial engineering rather than purely theoretical astrophysics.[32]

### The Great Accelerated Era

#### Beginning of the 200,000-Year Project
Once the Sector had been mapped and the Empire possessed the infrastructure necessary for large-scale expansion, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] activated the greatest temporal project in Imperial history.
The declared objective was:
approximately 200,000 Imperial years
experienced during
approximately one external year.[2]
Natalia governed the temporal component of the ritual while Victor functioned as its principal Energy source and stabilizing anchor. Jeanne and the other Empresses contributed Energy, conceptual support and specialized abilities, while Divine avatars allowed them to continue participating in Imperial affairs without abandoning the ritual.[2][11]
Victor initially described the process as beginning with the Empire's mother galaxy and expanding outward until a larger super-galactic Imperial structure could be developed.[2]
The project was not intended merely to create stronger soldiers. It was designed to provide enough history for entire civilizations to reproduce, evolve, conduct research, establish institutions and develop generations of specialists before Upper-Sector enemies could properly respond.

#### Energetic Cost
The ritual proved enormously more expensive than accelerating isolated individuals or dimensions. Stars continued evolving, planets moved, populations reproduced, industries consumed resources and countless physical and supernatural processes had to remain synchronized within the altered temporal flow.[11]
After approximately seven weeks of maintaining the system, even [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] began showing signs that the expenditure could eventually strain his enormous reserves.[11] Victor and Jeanne therefore remained among the principal anchors, Natalia concentrated upon controlling Time and the other Empresses rotated between maintaining the structure and using avatars inside the accelerated civilization.

#### Reorganization of Death and Reincarnation
A civilization intended to experience two hundred thousand years required an afterlife system capable of processing generations of deaths. The Empire therefore reorganized its Soul infrastructure during the early accelerated era.[11]
Hell remained available for sinners, Demonic existences and appropriate punishments, while additional spaces and systems were developed for Soul screening, rest, reincarnation and exceptional individuals. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] assumed direct responsibility for the most difficult aspects of Soul circulation because his Authority over Souls exceeded that of the other members of Dragon's Nest.[11]
Ordinary citizens were not universally granted indefinite immortality. The Imperial system deliberately preserved the cycle of birth, life, death and reincarnation. Most reincarnated citizens did not retain complete memories of earlier lives, preventing a civilization containing trillions of immortal minds from stagnating beneath the accumulated psychological burden of eternity.[3]
Individuals who accomplished exceptional deeds for the Empire could receive different treatment, allowing valuable knowledge, loyalty and ability to be preserved when the Imperial authorities considered it worthwhile.[3]

#### Around Imperial Year 30,000
By approximately the 30,000th year of the Imperial Calendar, the civilization had changed beyond recognition compared with Year One.[3]
- The population had expanded throughout the Empire's mother galaxy.[3]
- World Tree-bearing planets supported permanent Imperial settlements.[3]
- Teleportation networks and Space-Time-based spacecraft had reduced planetary distance to an ordinary logistical concern.[3]
- Copies of Babylon had been constructed across newly populated worlds and connected to the same larger training network.[3]
- Gods, Demigods, Mortals and numerous supernatural species openly lived within the same civilization.[3]
- Stable hybrid descendants of previously incompatible supernatural races had become common because Divine intervention and Imperial biotechnology prevented many of the problems that historically accompanied mixed bloodlines.[3]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Velnorah had conducted long-term racial development. Human-descended populations became dramatically stronger, while later generations of Vampires and [[Werewolves]] increasingly approached ancestral characteristics that earlier members of their races required special awakenings to achieve.[3]
- A civilization-wide martial culture had developed without eliminating the importance of science, administration, craftsmanship and other non-combat professions.[3]

#### The Genetic Era — Year 30,000 Onward
Chapter 1145 provides an important correction to the precise chronology of Imperial biological development. The Empire is stated to have fully embraced genetics from approximately the 30,000th-year point onward.[4]
Within less than another 20,000 years, the average Imperial citizen had evolved still further. An ordinary citizen could effortlessly lift what the narration describes as an Old World building, while average life expectancy had reached approximately 300 years. The Empire expected this figure to approach approximately 400 years by Year 100,000.[4]
This means the extreme biological baseline described in Chapter 1145 belongs approximately to the period after Year 30,000 but before roughly Year 50,000. It should not be placed at Year 20,000.
The improvement was civilization-wide rather than restricted to a tiny Divine aristocracy. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] deliberately wanted even ordinary citizens to become increasingly capable rather than creating an Empire consisting entirely of overwhelmingly powerful rulers governing biologically helpless populations.[4]

#### Imperial Year 70,000
Beginning around the 70,000th year, the Empire launched two gigantic projects associated with the defense and development of its Galactic territory.[4]
Chapter 1145 directly describes one of these projects in detail. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] maintained the stability of the surrounding galaxy while the Dragon Goddesses used their Reality-shaping abilities to remodel astronomical structures including:
- asteroids;
- moons;
- stars;
- nebulae;
- and black holes.[4]
Creatures originally associated with [[Hela Elderblood]] and the war against [[Asgard]] were distributed strategically across the galaxy. Victor and [[Azathoth Elderblood]] strengthened these beings without pushing them so far that their development would unnecessarily attract outside attention.[4]
Each was capable of monitoring enormous territories, with individual creatures described as capable of watching an entire Solar System. The resulting defense network embedded dangerous Eldritch entities throughout asteroids, stars, black holes and other astronomical structures, transforming the Imperial galaxy itself into a hostile environment for unauthorized invaders.[4]
Victor further modified the creatures so that they no longer depended upon ordinary feeding for survival. They could remain operational indefinitely and consume hostile forces when opportunities arose without threatening the stability of the civilization they were meant to protect.[4]
The same passage states that two gigantic projects began around Year 70,000, but only one is directly described in the available Chapter 1145 text. The second project should therefore remain unidentified until the novel explicitly names or explains it.

#### Imperial Year 100,000
Imperial Year 100,000 became one of the largest symbolic milestones in the Empire's history.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] descended through avatars and joined Imperial forces in an enormous parade through the Imperial Capital. The celebration was transmitted to every connected Imperial planet.[4]
[[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]], who had deliberately remained outside the accelerated civilization, observed that the first 100,000 Imperial years had passed in approximately six months for him.[4] This provides one of the few direct comparisons between a major Imperial Calendar milestone and external Time.
The military display demonstrated how radically Imperial standards had changed. Even members of the Imperial Guard had reached levels that ancient rulers such as Vlad considered absurd, while the Empire's overall military buildup reflected the scale of the enemies expected beyond the Sector.[4]
During the same hundred-thousand-year period, Victor publicly addressed the Empire concerning the coming conflict. He warned that the civilization still lived inside a comparatively protected cosmic region and that war would begin once the barrier separating them from the greater powers beyond the Sector ceased protecting them.[37]
Victor simultaneously established a deeper bond between himself and Imperial citizens. He declared that even if his physical body were destroyed, the Souls of those belonging to his Empire would remain protected with him. Crimson power accompanied the declaration and even Divine observers recognized that the statement had created a genuine supernatural connection rather than functioning as simple political rhetoric.[37]
Victor stated that approximately another 100,000 internal years remained before the intended deadline of the project.[37]

#### The Hundred-Thousand-Year Development Boom
The Year-100,000 address triggered another period of extraordinary development. Chapter 1148 describes an Imperial technological and scientific boom so rapid that new discoveries again appeared almost constantly.[5]
Faith, Imperial pride and fear of the coming war encouraged citizens to work, train, research and reproduce more aggressively. Fertility-related Goddesses led by [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood intensified population growth until families possessing more than one hundred children were no longer automatically considered exceptional.[5]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] believed that if the new pace continued, several of the Empire's intended goals might be reached within approximately another 50,000 years, earlier than the full 200,000-year deadline. He therefore slightly increased the rate of temporal acceleration.[5]
This adjustment is important when interpreting the Calendar: the Time ratio was actively controlled and could be modified. Individual Imperial dates therefore should not be converted into exact external dates through a simple permanent mathematical ratio unless the novel directly provides the comparison.

#### Polaris and the Year-100,000 Military Generation
By Chapter 1159, Polaris is described as a secret project dating from the 100,000th-year Imperial milestone.[38]
Polaris represents one of the clearest examples of what one hundred thousand years of accelerated research produced. It is a Galaxy [[Destroyer]]-class Divine Artifact constructed in the form of an enormous spacecraft capable of manipulating Reality, Space and Time through systems associated with Destruction and compatible with Eldritch forces.[38]
Its most dangerous weapon channels <END> through beings such as [[Fenrir]], [[Typhon]] and Jormungand. The Concept is destructive enough that the firing structure cannot survive a complete shot; rather than solving the material problem directly, Imperial scientists designed large numbers of replaceable cannons.[38]
[[Hela Elderblood]] was designated as its principal intended operator, while Velnorah, Aline, Ruby and later Pepper were ordered to continue improving the weapon.[38]

### The Mature Galactic Era

#### After Imperial Year 100,000
The exact Imperial years of Chapters 1149–1157 are not stated. They occur after the Year-100,000 milestone and before the Empire reaches approximately the 170,000-year point.
This period demonstrates that the Empire had become culturally, politically and biologically stable across a span longer than the organized existence of many ancient civilizations.
The older Pantheons increasingly functioned as integrated or subordinate parts of the Elderblood political order. [[Ariel Elderblood]] could move naturally between [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family and Heaven, while [[Kali Elderblood]] represented the Empire in dealings with the [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]].[39][40][41]
The Elderblood second generation simultaneously matured into adults whose natural abilities exceeded many of the Gods who once ruled Earth. The existence of daughters connected to Destruction, Space-Time, Energy, Infinity, Unreality and other fundamental phenomena illustrated how radically Victor's dynasty itself had developed during the accelerated centuries.[42]
Despite these changes, Victor continued preserving independent thought throughout the Empire. Dissatisfied groups were monitored rather than immediately exterminated simply for disagreeing with him. Victor considered a controlled opposition useful because discontented individuals would naturally gather together where they could be observed, while actual betrayal remained grounds for elimination.[5]

### Around Imperial Year 170,000
Chapter 1158 provides the latest major approximate Imperial-year marker presently revealed in the novel.
By approximately the 170,000th year of the accelerated project, the Empire finally obtained one of the direct clues concerning Upper-Sector powers that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had been seeking since the ritual began.[43]

#### Expansion to the Sagittarius Dwarf Galaxy
The incident occurred in the Sagittarius Dwarf Elliptical Galaxy, approximately 80,000 light-years from [[Yggdrasil]], upon a moon orbiting a planet already under Imperial control.[43]
The event proves that by the late accelerated era, Imperial territory and security operations extended far beyond the immediate stellar neighborhood of Yggdrasil.
[[Nikolai Francis Nichev]], a [[Demigod]] descended from [[Dagda]] whose mother had been born within Imperial civilization, became involved with a hostile external power operating inside Imperial territory.[43]

#### The Emperor's Herald
The operation attracted War, described as [[War (Demon)|The Emperor's Herald]] and the strongest among [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Heralds. War suppressed the hostile operation and delivered surviving participants to the Third Legion.[43]
When the moon hosting the compromised installation became expendable, War compressed and destroyed it before creating a replacement and restoring the astronomical structure without damaging the Imperial planet beneath it.[43]
The event demonstrates how radically the meaning of strategic destruction had changed within the mature Empire: even the destruction and replacement of moons could occur as part of security cleanup conducted by Victor's principal servants.

#### First Major Upper-Sector Intelligence
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] transferred the captured foreign existence to an isolated dimensional facility positioned near the edge of his Sector. The location was separated from ordinary Imperial territory and reinforced with extensive Draconic and Eldritch Runes.[43]
An existential Soul Contract attempted to permanently destroy the prisoner's body and Soul whenever restricted information was revealed. Victor repeatedly reconstructed the captive through his authorities over Negativity, Life, Beginning, Time, Space, Souls, Eldritch power, Draconic Reality distortion and prepared Runes.[43]
The process ultimately repeated more than 623 billion times, allowing Victor to extract what useful information the comparatively low-ranking operative actually possessed.[43]
The intelligence confirmed that hostile Upper-Sector forces possessed active interest in Victor, his Bloodline and the powers he had accumulated, shifting Imperial strategy from preparation against abstract future enemies toward preparation against identifiable factions.[43]

#### Black Eclipse
The information obtained from the foreign operative revealed the faction associated with <Darkness>, identified as the [[Black Eclipse Pantheon]].[38]
The hierarchy included <Darkness> at the highest level, followed by the [[Seven Black Thrones]] and additional classes of Gods and subordinate forces. The information remained incomplete because [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had extracted it from an operative who did not belong to the faction's highest leadership.[38]
Imperial estimates therefore treated the available threat classifications as provisional intelligence rather than perfect measurements of the enemy's entire military strength.

#### Civilization-Wide Corruption Security
The encounter also caused [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to strengthen Imperial defenses against conceptual or Eldritch corruption.
Through the Eldritch existence known as [[Abhoth]] or the Little One, fragments could remain connected to Victor while being carried by Imperial personnel. An outside attempt to corrupt or subvert a protected individual could consequently expose the attacker to Victor through the same connection.[38]
The system could function as:
- a corruption detector;
- a counter-corruption mechanism;
- an alert against betrayal;
- a metaphysical path toward the hostile source;
- and part of a distributed security network incorporating Victor's parallel consciousnesses and connected [[World Trees]].[38]
Violet proposed eventually extending the protection to every citizen of the Empire, potentially turning a military anti-corruption measure into civilization-wide spiritual security.[38]

### Late Accelerated Era
Chapters 1160–1169 occur after the approximately 170,000-year marker, but no later exact Imperial year is explicitly supplied. These chapters should therefore remain grouped as the Late Accelerated Era rather than being assigned invented dates.

#### Maturity of the Second Generation
By Chapter 1160, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters had matured into powerful Goddesses and conceptual beings. Despite being born with overwhelming advantages, they remained subject to rules preventing them from casually using supernatural powers against one another and were expected to continue training rather than relying entirely upon inherited strength.[44]
The development of the second generation is historically significant because it represents the first generation raised almost entirely inside Victor's mature cosmic civilization rather than inheriting the social assumptions of old Earth, [[Nightingale]] or the ancient Pantheons.

#### Scientific Compartmentalization
Chapter 1161 demonstrates the scale reached by Imperial information security. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] allowed his daughters access to enormous amounts of classified information because he expected many of them eventually to participate in governing the Empire, but certain projects remained compartmentalized even from the Royal Family.[45]
Projects such as Polaris and Pepper's private research were deliberately restricted to individuals with direct need-to-know access. Imperial advancement had therefore become sufficiently complex that even members of the Emperor's own family no longer automatically possessed access to every major weapon or experiment.

#### Pepper's Artificial-Star Research
In Chapter 1162, Pepper's private laboratory revealed one of the more extreme examples of late-era research.
Pepper had successfully created and stabilized an artificial star as part of a project she casually called "Creating a supernova in the palm of my hand."[46]
A later phase intended to continuously feed the structure until it could produce an output compared to approximately a billion exploding suns. Multiple nested dimensions surrounded the project so that catastrophic failure could be absorbed before threatening the inhabited environment outside.[46]
Pepper's private databases contained hundreds of millions of research records, despite science not being her principal public responsibility. The discovery implied an even greater accumulation of research beneath specialists such as Ruby, Velnorah and Aline.[46]

#### Imperial Territory in Andromeda
Chapter 1163 depicts an Imperial military operation in the Andromeda Galaxy. A fleet of more than fifty ships operated around a World Tree-bearing planet affected by an aggressive newly emerged species.[47]
The operation involved:
- an Explorer-class flagship;
- orbital weaponry;
- [[The Spartans]];
- Fourth Legion requests for biological samples;
- [[Frederick Winter]] representing the mature Imperial Onmyō tradition;
- and [[Andrew Walter]] representing the reorganized Imperial Inquisition.[47]
Both Fred and Andrew were classified as V-05 threats under Imperial assessment despite their ordinary appearance compared with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family. The designation represented overall strategic danger at approximately the Solar-System category rather than claiming every ordinary attack they performed destroyed a Solar System.[47]
The chapter contains wording translated as the planet having belonged to the Empire since the "thousandth Imperial millennium". Because the entire great development project is explicitly structured around approximately 200,000 Imperial years, that phrase is chronologically incompatible if interpreted literally as one million years. It should therefore not be used to assign an exact date to the annexation of that planet without clarification from the original text.

#### Late-Era Threat Standards
Chapter 1164 further clarifies how radically Imperial standards had changed.
Within the mature Empire:
- planetary combat capability could be regarded as merely above average;
- Solar-System-level threats were treated as elite;
- Galaxy-level beings were classified among the super-elites;
- and the highest beings in categories such as V-06 could differ enormously from those merely entering the same broad category.[48]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Azathoth, Jeanne and [[Scathach]] are discussed as exceptional existences capable of operating beyond ordinary single-galaxy standards, emphasizing that Imperial classification measures threat potential rather than functioning as a perfectly linear destructive-power scale.[48]

#### Engineered Research Populations
Velnorah also began designing a new population of adaptive technomages intended specifically to assist with research.[48]
Her objective was not to produce another elite warrior species. The Empire already possessed enormous numbers of powerful combatants. Instead, she wanted researchers who could:
- be born with large quantities of useful knowledge;
- perform advanced scientific and magical work;
- protect themselves without requiring permanent military escorts;
- and participate in compartmentalized projects whose true purpose might be known only to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Velnorah.[48]
The project reflects another transition in Imperial development: by this stage, entire specialized populations could be engineered around particular scientific or strategic functions.

#### True Dragon Population
Despite the extraordinary growth of the Empire, [[True Dragons]] remained deliberately rare. Chapter 1164 places their total population at only approximately forty to fifty, even after including [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s enormous family.[48]
This was not caused by an inability to reproduce. Victor and his wives deliberately restricted the expansion of a species whose natural capabilities were considered destabilizing if multiplied without restraint.[48]
The [[Elderblood Bloodline]] and True Dragon nature consequently remained defining characteristics of the Imperial Family rather than biological upgrades distributed throughout ordinary Imperial society.

#### Leviathan Population and Star Animals
Chapter 1166 reveals another measure of the Empire's late biological and military scale.
Billions of Leviathans inhabited their designated world, with the largest examples capable of consuming a planet comparable to Earth.[49]
Working collectively, these creatures killed a Star Animal whose corpse was larger than [[Jupiter]]. Ruby, Velnorah and Aline treated the body simultaneously as food, a scientific specimen and a possible biological template.[49]
[[Scathach]] also possessed a recording of another young Star Animal capable of swallowing a star. Its measured length was approximately 1,670,040 kilometres.[49]
Ancient Star Animals surviving for billions of years were theorized to potentially reach Solar-System scales or beyond, leading Velnorah to order future forces to prioritize capturing suitable specimens alive whenever possible.[49]

#### Late-Era Knowledge
Chapters 1167–1168 demonstrate that Imperial education within the Royal Family had progressed beyond ordinary astrophysics or Divine combat into the conceptual structure of Reality itself.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] taught [[Norabell Elderblood]] the difference between ordinary subordinate dimensions and structures possessing greater existential weight. He demonstrated how Divine Concepts, Eldritch power and fundamental Rules could become part of the foundation of a constructed Reality rather than merely functioning as abilities applied inside it.[50][51]
The lesson also distinguished powerful Gods from genuine Primordial components of the Cosmos, explaining that a true Primordial Being occupies a functional position within universal architecture rather than merely possessing enormous Energy.[51]
These chapters do not provide a new Imperial year, but they demonstrate the intellectual environment in which the Empire's later generation matured: subjects concerning cosmic laws, conceptual structures and the source code of Reality had become part of family education.

#### Approaching the 200,000-Year Anniversary
By Chapter 1178, the novel explicitly states that the 200,000-year anniversary of the Imperial Calendar was approaching. This is the first direct late-stage dating statement after the approximately 170,000-year marker and places the following reforms close to the end of the original temporal project.[6]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] decided that [[Yggdrasil]] should no longer remain the continuously expanding population center of the Empire. He wanted the original homeworld preserved as a protected garden world, the historical cradle and jewel of Imperial civilization, with its sentient population eventually relocated elsewhere. Mars was selected as the new gathering point and political center.[6]
Victor, Natalia, [[Anna Elderblood]] and Zaladrac Elderblood completely reconstructed Mars. The planet was enlarged into a megaplanet capable of supporting trillions of inhabitants and deliberately designed as more than a comfortable capital. Victor created high-gravity and weightless regions, magma and eternal-ice zones, Miasma-rich environments, floating islands, oceans containing smaller Leviathans, mutated primeval species and other dangerous ecosystems so that the Imperial center would continue challenging its inhabitants rather than becoming a harmless paradise.[6]
The most important infrastructural development was the installation of a distributed World Tree Network. Rather than forcing a single World Tree on Mars to process the information of the entire Empire, the [[World Trees]] connected to Victor extended their roots through a common core placed on the planet. This turned Mars into a central communications and transportation nexus, allowing information and travel through the network to become effectively instantaneous across Imperial territory rather than requiring even the previous several-minute delays between extremely distant galaxies.[6]
The World Trees themselves remained protected inside Victor's inner existence, meaning destruction of the physical capital would not automatically destroy the true network. As long as the Trees remained intact, the planet could theoretically be restored. Victor also embedded automated planetary defenses and traps intended to activate against hostile intruders rather than ordinary citizens.[6]
The reconstruction required approximately seven subjective months while Natalia prevented outside Time from advancing around the operation. This localized Time manipulation should not be confused with the larger Imperial Calendar ratio; it demonstrates that the family could create additional isolated temporal frames inside the already accelerated era.[6]
Once the work was complete, [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] issued an Empire-wide proclamation declaring Mars the new capital of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Yggdrasil remained the symbolic place where the Imperial era began, while Mars became the center from which the late Empire intended to advance into its next historical phase.[6]

#### Integration of the Three Lower Sectors
Immediately afterward, the Empire began another territorial project involving the lower Sectors containing Arcane, [[Nightingale]] and Samar. By this point all three had developed heavily under Elderblood influence. Nightingale had become a multiracial world integrated with Imperial technology, Samar had expanded to roughly three times the size of Old Earth and possessed an enormous capital, and Arcane had been reorganized under the Witch civilization.[7]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] placed fragments of his own power inside each lower Sector through special spheres carried by Albedo, Ophis and Tasha. These fragments saturated the Sectors and created the metaphysical connection required to pull them toward his own Middle Sector. The method resembled the way higher-sector rulers seeded smaller Sectors so that developing regions would later emerge near their own spheres of influence.[7][52][8]
When the merger began, Victor used his full Draconic scale to draw all three lower Sectors into his domain while the Universal Tree prevented neighboring lower Sectors from being dragged along accidentally. Victor's Sector barrier expanded and the younger galaxies of the incoming Sectors appeared inside the larger region.[8]
Victor then reorganized the newly integrated galaxies rather than leaving them to collide or develop chaotically. Entire galaxies and billions of stars were moved while their internal systems remained stable. Nightingale and Samar were placed within separate Solar Systems only a few hundred light-years apart in one of the Empire's central protected regions, while the younger galaxies received enough separation to continue maturing safely. [[The Universal Tree]] described the result as comparable to watching Victor recreate the universe and agreed to help the youngest galaxies stabilize after their rapid relocation.[9]
This event marks one of the greatest territorial changes recorded by the Imperial Calendar: three previously separate lower Sectors and their galaxies ceased existing as isolated Imperial dependencies and became part of the same broader Sector governed by Victor.[8][9]

#### Imperial Year 190,000
Chapter 1183 provides the latest explicit internal-Time marker currently available: approximately 190,000 years had passed. Anna states that the Empire had already reached its intended population target and argues that the ritual should be terminated, while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] refuses because he considers the remaining years strategically important.[10]
By this point the cumulative Energy cost had become severe. Victor had previously expended more than eighty percent of his available strength during the late-stage preparations; by Chapter 1183 the expenditure is described at approximately ninety-six percent. He was not physically injured, but the ritual consumed Energy faster than he regenerated it, causing systems directly connected to him—including his planet, the [[Dragon Heart]] and the [[World Trees]]—to function more slowly. Roxanne, Amara and the World Trees supplied additional Energy, but the contribution was only a temporary relief compared with the scale of the ritual.[6][10]
The integration of three additional Sectors also created a new structural problem. Victor's Middle Sector was already densely populated with developed powers and galaxies; after the merger, the narration described the region through the recurring "aquarium" analogy as increasingly crowded with large powers. Imperial expansion therefore did not end with successful annexation—the Empire now had to manage a much denser cosmic environment inside the same larger Sector.[10]
The final phase also produced the conceptual superweapon project known as the [[Absolute Paradox]]. Velina derived the original idea from Schrödinger's cat, after which Velnorah, Aline and Ruby developed it and Victor designed a possible architecture involving a contradiction core, a stabilizing Energy source and a transfer mechanism capable of applying mutually incompatible states to beings, objects, laws or Concepts. Victor considered the remaining years of the ritual important enough to continue despite his exhaustion partly because he wanted the project realized before the deadline.[10]
The weapon remained in the idea and experimental-planning stage, but its appearance demonstrates how far late Imperial research had moved beyond conventional astronomy or warfare: the Empire was now designing systems intended to attack the logical consistency of Reality itself.[10]

### Dating and Translation Notes
- The Imperial Calendar begins at Year One, not Year Zero.[1]
- Events occurring before Chapter 1086 should remain Pre-Imperial unless a later chapter explicitly assigns them an Imperial date.
- Chapter 1087 provides unusually precise dating for the first week of the Empire, including reforms on Days Two through Five and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s recognition as Overlord at the end of Day Seven.[12]
- The exact Imperial year of most events between the first week and the beginning of the accelerated era is not provided.
- Chapter 1139 retrospectively supplies several important dates: Years 20, 50, 1,000 and approximately 30,000.[3]
- Older versions of this article placed the Chapter 1145 biological uplift around Year 20,000. This is unsupported. Chapter 1145 states that the Empire fully embraced genetics from approximately Year 30,000 and that the later physical baseline was achieved within less than another twenty thousand years.[4]
- Chapter 1145 explicitly states that two gigantic projects began around Year 70,000 but directly describes only the Galactic-remodeling/Eldritch-defense project in the available text. The second should not be identified through speculation.[4]
- Year 100,000 is explicitly shown and corresponds to approximately six months for Vlad, who remained outside the accelerated civilization.[4]
- Chapter 1159 describes Polaris as a secret project dating from the Year-100,000 stage of Imperial development. Its origin should therefore be cited to Chapter 1159 rather than Chapter 1158.[38]
- The translated Chapter 1145–1169 text occasionally uses "millennium" where the surrounding chronology clearly concerns the numbered Imperial-year milestone. Because the entire ritual is designed around approximately 200,000 internal years, phrases such as "100,000th millennium" should not automatically be multiplied by one thousand.
- Chapter 1163 contains particularly problematic wording describing an Andromeda world as Imperial territory since the "thousandth Imperial millennium". Taken literally this would require a million Imperial years and contradict the established 200,000-year project. Until the original-language wording or a corrected version clarifies the phrase, the planet's exact annexation year should remain unspecified.[47]
- Chapters 1159–1177 occur after the approximately Year-170,000 milestone but do not provide a reliable later exact Calendar year.
- Chapter 1178 explicitly states that the 200,000-year anniversary is approaching and establishes Mars as the new Imperial capital.[6]
- Chapters 1179–1182 record the merger of the Arcane, [[Nightingale]] and Samar lower Sectors into Victor's Middle Sector. These events occur near the end of the ritual but are not assigned a precise year.[7][8][9]
- Chapter 1183 explicitly states that 190,000 years have already passed, making approximately Year 190,000 the latest reliable internal-Time marker.[10]
- The same Chapter 1183 narration also loosely says that Victor had maintained the ritual for 200,000 years. Because this conflicts with Anna's precise statement that 190,000 years have passed and with Chapter 1178 saying the 200,000-year anniversary is still approaching, this article treats the 200,000-year wording as a rounded description of the project rather than evidence that the endpoint has already been reached.[6][10]
- No unique Imperial month, week or replacement day names have yet been revealed.

### Historical Significance
The Imperial Calendar is not merely a replacement for Earth's former dating systems. Year One marks the point at which [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s territories, allied populations, conquered Pantheons and supernatural races ceased functioning primarily as separate civilizations and began being reorganized beneath a single Imperial identity.[1][12]
Its earliest dates record the deliberate construction of that identity. Within seven days, the Empire replaced the previous calendar, currency, educational system and planetary political structure; created a common language; restored medical and cultural infrastructure; demonstrated extraterrestrial expansion; and publicly established Victor's overwhelming military superiority.[1][12]
The later Calendar records something much larger. The great temporal ritual allowed processes that normally require geological, biological or civilizational ages to occur within approximately one external year. Military institutions matured across millennia, species evolved across generations, worlds were populated and engineered, the afterlife became part of state administration, and the Imperial population expanded enough to support Galactic colonization.[2][11][3]
By Year 70,000, Imperial planning was no longer centered upon defending individual planets; the galaxy itself was being converted into a defensive structure.[4] By Year 100,000, Victor ruled a mature Galactic civilization whose ordinary citizens possessed capabilities far beyond Pre-Imperial Humanity and whose military research had begun producing weapons such as Polaris.[4][38]
By approximately Year 170,000, the purpose of the entire project became explicit. The Empire was no longer preparing merely to defend [[Yggdrasil]], [[Nightingale]] or the Milky Way. Imperial territory reached distant Galactic regions, Upper-Sector agents were operating inside that territory and Victor had begun acquiring actionable intelligence concerning rulers capable of threatening his position at the highest levels of the Cosmos.[43][38]
As the 200,000-year anniversary approached, the Calendar recorded a second civilizational transition. Yggdrasil ceased being treated as the permanent population center and was reserved as the future garden-world cradle of the Empire, while Mars became the new Imperial capital and the World Tree Network turned the capital into a communications and transportation heart for worlds spread across enormous Galactic distances.[6] The subsequent merger of Arcane, Nightingale and Samar transformed three lower-sector dependencies into territories physically integrated into Victor's Middle Sector, requiring the Emperor to rearrange entire galaxies to preserve their development and security.[7][8][9]
By approximately Year 190,000, the original population objective had already been fulfilled, yet Victor was still sustaining the ritual at extraordinary personal cost because the final period had become strategically valuable for projects such as the [[Absolute Paradox]] and for consolidating the newly expanded Sector.[10] The Calendar therefore does not merely measure how long the Empire existed; it records how manufactured Time was converted into population, institutions, scientific knowledge, cosmic infrastructure and eventually direct control over the architecture of entire Sectors.
The Imperial Calendar therefore records the transformation of the [[Elderblood Empire]] from a newly unified supernatural planetary civilization into a Galactic and multi-Sector power deliberately manufacturing centuries of history in preparation for war over the future order of Reality itself.
