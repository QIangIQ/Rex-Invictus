This article covers the principal inhabited world commonly called the [[Magic]] World or Planet of the Witches. The planet itself should not be confused with the complete Lower Sector containing it.
The Magic World, also known as the World of Magic, Witches' Ancestral World and later the Planet of the Witches, is the principal known inhabited world of a separate Lower Sector historically connected to Arcane through a dimensional portal. It is deeply associated with the ancestral origin of Witches, the supernatural Energy known as Magic and the ancient race called the Drakyo. When [[Victor Elderblood]] first investigated the Witches' dimensional experiments, he discovered that their portal had not simply reached another distant planet but an entirely separate Universe-like Reality.[2] Later explanations incorporated that Reality into the wider cosmology of Sectors, and Chapter 1179 explicitly identifies the Witch World as belonging to the Lower Sectors.[1]
At first contact, the world possessed a magically advanced civilization superficially resembling a medieval society. Its armies included Paladins equipped with powerful artifacts and multiple Divine Blessings, while its Gods operated under a much more unified political system than Earth's historically divided Pantheons.[3][4] Victor's intrusion triggered intervention from the [[Regulators of Existence]], after which approximately 100,000 years passed within the foreign Sector while roughly 30 minutes passed externally. The civilization consequently advanced from a magical medieval society into a mature techno-magical civilization with an enormous population capable of pursuing Divinity.[5][6]
The world's political situation later changed completely. Victor eventually ordered [[Evie Moriarty Elderblood]] to take control of the World of Magic, and Chapter 1179 confirms that the campaign succeeded. Evie became the Witch Queen and ruler of the planet, the Witches assumed control of its civilization with the support of the [[Elderblood Empire]], and the formerly foreign Lower Sector became one of the Imperial-controlled territories selected for integration into Victor's own Sector.[7][1]

The [[Magic]] World and the Lower Sector containing it are related but distinct structures. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] originally perceived the destination of the Arcane portal as another Universe because the dimensional connection crossed out of the Reality in which Earth existed rather than simply traversing conventional astronomical distance.[2] Later information clarified that such separated cosmic environments are organized within the greater structure of Sectors.

#### Lower Sector Classification
Chapter 1179 removes the earlier uncertainty concerning the world's classification by explicitly presenting the location beneath the heading "Lower Sectors: Planet of the Witches".[1] The Witch Sector had also reached the maximum level of development possible under its existing conditions by the beginning of the merger operation. Ruby attributed this to the extensive connection the [[Elderblood Empire]] had established with the controlled Sectors, allowing them to reach their developmental limits much faster than they otherwise would have.
Velnorah later explained the structure of Sectors through an aquarium analogy. Lower Sectors resemble several smaller aquariums intentionally left relatively isolated by the Primordial Beings so Life can develop without immediately being exposed to stronger civilizations. A Middle Sector resembles a much larger aquarium, while entering the Upper Sectors is comparable to emptying that aquarium into an open ocean populated by dangerous predators.[1] This protection explains why [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s original intrusion into the [[Magic]] World was cosmologically significant: he represented a Being from a dramatically superior civilization entering an environment intentionally protected from such interference.

#### Sector Merger
By Chapter 1179 the [[Magic]] World's isolation was coming to an end. The Elderblood leadership decided to physically integrate the controlled Lower Sectors with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Sector rather than leave them as disconnected external Realities.[1] Albedo delivered a mysterious dark-purple sphere to Evie, while equivalent objects had been assigned to Ophis at [[Nightingale]] and Tasha at Samar. Ruby explained that these spheres would draw the associated Sectors toward Victor's Sector, functioning as anchors for the merger while the holders themselves did not need to perform the complex cosmological manipulation.
The operation did not automatically advance Victor's Sector into a higher classification. When Anna asked whether absorbing the smaller Sectors would cause such an evolution, Ruby answered that it would instead result in the creation or incorporation of additional galaxies.[1] Velnorah described the process as moving smaller aquariums toward the edge of a larger one. Victor would personally ensure that the newly positioned Galactic structures did not collide during integration.

### Connection to Witches
The [[Magic]] World is fundamentally connected to the origin of Witches and their ability to manipulate Magic. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Albedo investigated Witch Magic, the capacity to properly manipulate this Energy was shown to depend on more than simply memorizing spells: Witches possess a metaphysical compatibility connected to their ancestry and Soul.[9]
Magic is a highly adaptable neutral Energy. It lacks the inherent qualitative superiority of forces such as [[Divine Energy]] or the [[Natural Energy]] associated with powerful [[World Trees]], but compensates through extreme versatility. Witches can shape it into elemental spells, rituals, enchantments, barriers, contracts, curses, transformation, dimensional techniques and numerous specialized applications.[9] The Magic World represents the deeper ancestral environment from which this connection originated.
By Chapter 1179 the link between the world's population and Witch Magic had become much more pervasive. While inspecting the conquered world, Albedo observed that magical blood flowed through everything and considered its continued spread desirable.[1] The exact biological mechanics of this statement are not separately explained, but it demonstrates that Witch-associated magical characteristics had become widespread throughout the civilization rather than remaining restricted to a small ruling class.

### World Trees
Like other naturally developed inhabited worlds, the [[Magic]] World participates in the greater World Tree structure associated with [[The Universal Tree]]. Its original Life-bearing environment was supported by a positive World Tree, while the world eventually developed the complementary negative side necessary for mature metaphysical balance.[6]

#### World Tree of Positivity
The positive World Tree represents the Life-bearing foundation of the world. Its role is cosmological and ecological rather than political: it supports the planetary environment and the growth of Life instead of functioning as a conventional monarch.

#### World Tree of Negativity
A complementary World Tree of Negativity later existed alongside the positive Tree.[6] [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] did not directly create this Tree. His intrusion accelerated circumstances surrounding the Sector, but the development of positive and negative [[World Trees]] belongs to the larger balancing mechanisms of Creation.

### History

#### Arcane's Dimensional Experiment
The first direct connection resulted from experiments conducted by Albedo, Evie and the Witches of [[The Arcane Realm]]. Their research into dimensional travel unexpectedly produced a passage to an unknown inhabited Reality.[8][2] [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] investigated the dimensional thread and discovered that its destination lay outside the familiar cosmic environment entirely, describing it as a different Universe.[2]
The discovery demonstrated that the Witches had accidentally created an inter-Sector route rather than a conventional portal between distant planets.

#### First Contact
The connection worked in both directions. Inhabitants of the [[Magic]] World discovered the passage from their side, creating an inter-Reality security crisis.[3] Among those who crossed into Arcane was Alfey, a Human Paladin equipped with Divine armor, a supernatural sword and Blessings associated with multiple Gods. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] pursued him through the portal and entered the foreign world directly.
The civilization Victor encountered resembled an advanced medieval fantasy society. Its warriors could fly, use Magic and employ sophisticated Divine artifacts, while its Gods coordinated their response rather than operating as isolated competing Pantheons.[3][4] The inhabitants identified Victor as a Drakyo, revealing that Dragon-like Beings already occupied an important place in the world's ancient history.

#### The Drakyo
At first contact the Drakyo appeared to have vanished from contemporary civilization. Their exact relationship to ordinary [[True Dragons]] remained unclear, and the term should not be equated automatically with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later [[Blood Dragons]].
Chapter 1179 significantly expands the available information. Evie possesses a small white Dragon identified as the last Drakyo, and she tells Albedo that the Drakyo were a race said to have created the world.[1] This establishes both a surviving member and a much more important place for the species in the planet's ancient history, although "said to have created this world" is presented as an inherited historical claim rather than a fully explained cosmological account.
The surviving Drakyo appeared visibly intimidated by Albedo, who casually stroked its head. Its name, age, exact power and relationship to the original creators remain unrevealed.

#### Victor and Alfey
Alfey demonstrated how the pre-acceleration civilization combined several systems at once. His power relied upon physical training, [[Magic]], Divine Blessings and specialized equipment rather than the overwhelming personal power common among Earth's highest supernatural figures.[3] His sword and armor could interact directly with supernatural Energy, and his position as a Paladin connected him to the unified Divine establishment.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] overwhelmed Alfey and the defenders relatively easily, but concluded that the real danger lay in organization. A weaker civilization whose Gods cooperated could mobilize far more effectively than Earth's stronger but historically divided Pantheons.[4] He warned the Witches that their uncontrolled dimensional experiment could easily have resulted in Arcane being invaded by a coordinated foreign civilization.
Alfey survived the original incident and later became important to Evie's development. Chapter 1179 confirms that he eventually died, but also reveals that Evie learned everything she could from him before his death.[1] The exact circumstances and date of Alfey's death have not yet been given.

#### Unified Pantheon
Before the Witch conquest, the world's Gods operated with considerably greater unity than most traditional Earth Pantheons. Multiple Divine powers cooperated under a common order and supported warriors such as Alfey through Blessings and artifacts.[3][4]

#### Primordial Intervention
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s intrusion created a problem extending far beyond diplomacy. The [[Magic]] World belonged to a developing Lower Sector protected from exploitation by civilizations operating at dramatically higher levels.[6] The [[Regulators of Existence]], particularly the Universal Tree and [[The Judges of The Abyss]], intervened to prevent the imbalance created by Victor's presence and the continuing Arcane connection.
Their solution involved an extreme manipulation of Time rather than simply destroying the portal.

#### One Hundred Thousand Years
The foreign Sector underwent approximately 100,000 years of internal development while only around 30 minutes elapsed from Earth's external perspective.[5]

Unlike ordinary Time Manipulation affecting an individual or isolated room, the Regulators altered the development of an entire Sector environment on a civilizational scale.[5][6]

#### Temporal Leakage
Because the Arcane portal remained open while the two Realities experienced radically different Time Flows, temporal effects began leaking through the dimensional connection.[6] The portal consequently became a cosmological hazard rather than merely a transportation route, forcing further intervention from the Universal Tree and Judges of the Abyss.
The incident demonstrated why uncontrolled inter-Sector travel could destabilize more than the locations immediately surrounding a portal.

#### Post-Acceleration Civilization
After 100,000 years of accelerated development, the [[Magic]] World had transformed enormously. Its society no longer resembled the civilization [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] originally encountered. Magic, Divine systems and technology increasingly fused into a single techno-magical structure, while the population's supernatural potential expanded dramatically.[6]

Approximately 99% of the later population was described as possessing enough potential or Divine Essence to potentially pursue Godhood, creating the opposite problem from Earth's historical Divine civilizations: suitable candidates were no longer rare, forcing local authorities to restrict actual ascension to preserve stability.[6]

#### Evie's Conquest
Much later, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] gave [[Evie Moriarty Elderblood]] greater supernatural authority, granted her the Divine Concept associated with <Research> and ordered her to take over the World of [[Magic]] on her own.[7] The previous article could only record that command because the outcome had not yet been shown.
Chapter 1179 confirms that Evie's campaign succeeded.[1] By the time Albedo returned to the planet, the Witches had assumed control and Imperial influence had transformed the civilization. Evie was explicitly described as the Witch Queen and ruler of an entire planet. When Albedo asked whether she had won the war, Evie effectively confirmed it while dismissing the difficulty of the conflict, noting that with the [[Elderblood Empire]] supporting her it would have been embarrassing to lose.
The conquest appears to have been extremely one-sided by its later stages. Evie's own recollection of the "war" consisted largely of her people dominating their opposition, while the former Paladin Alfey was already dead.[1] The exact sequence of battles, fate of the former Pantheon and political terms imposed after victory have not yet been revealed.

#### Planet of the Witches
Under Evie's rule the world became recognizably dominated by Witch culture. Albedo observed entire streets filled with magical devices, women moving through the sky through magical circles, talking animals and enormous creatures wandering freely.[1] Despite the apparent visual chaos, the society remained highly organized and had become significantly better administered under Witch control.
The spread of magical blood throughout the population particularly pleased Albedo, and she detected even Demigods among the inhabitants.[1] Children played openly in the cities, indicating a stable civilian society rather than a world still suffering from active conquest. The result was not simply occupation by an external army: Witch culture, Imperial organization and the native magical civilization had begun blending into a single planetary society.
Emilly Moriarty, Evie's daughter, had also reached adulthood by this period and resided on the conquered world. When greeting Albedo she welcomed her to Arcane, suggesting that the Witch polity continues to use the Arcane identity within its new planetary civilization.[1] The line does not yet establish whether the entire planet has officially been renamed Arcane, so [[Magic]] World and Planet of the Witches remain the safer location names.

#### Preparations for the Merger
Evie's conquest was not the final stage of the world's integration. Albedo's actual reason for visiting in Chapter 1179 was to prepare the Lower Sector for a larger Imperial merger operation.[1] She gave Evie a dark-purple sphere so unusual that attempting to comprehend it directly caused Evie a severe headache. Albedo immediately warned her to stop examining it or risk losing her mind, while admitting that she herself did not understand the object's deeper nature.
Equivalent spheres were connected to operations at [[Nightingale]] and Samar. The individuals positioned there only needed to keep the spheres with them while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and the Imperial leadership performed the actual cosmological manipulation.[1]
Ruby explained that the controlled Sectors had already reached their current developmental limits and that the next step was to draw the smaller Sector structures toward Victor's Sector. The process would create or add galaxies rather than directly increasing the classification of Victor's Sector. Velnorah described the operation as moving a small aquarium to the edge of a larger aquarium, with Victor ensuring that the Galactic structures created during the process did not collide.[1]
This marks the transition of the [[Magic]] World from a conquered external civilization into a territory undergoing direct cosmological integration with the [[Elderblood Empire]].

### Civilization

#### Original Civilization
Before the 100,000-year acceleration, the [[Magic]] World resembled a magically advanced medieval civilization. Known features included large cities, organized supernatural military forces, Paladins, magical flight, Divine Blessings, enchanted weapons and armor, knowledge of Gods and a unified Pantheon capable of coordinating planetary defense.[3]
Its architecture and social appearance should not be confused with technological primitiveness. Alfey's equipment and the combined Divine-Magical systems employed by the civilization already exceeded conventional Human technology by enormous margins.

#### Techno-Magical Civilization
After the Primordial acceleration, [[Magic]] and technological development increasingly merged rather than competing with one another.[6] The world developed a civilization in which magical principles, Divine systems, artifacts and engineered technology could function as parts of the same infrastructure.

#### Witch Administration
The conquest under Evie produced another major transformation. By Chapter 1179, Witch influence was described as effectively omnipresent throughout the planet.[1] Administration had become more organized, Witch-style magical devices were integrated into urban life and aerial movement through magical circles appeared common.
The environment included:
- magical streets and devices;
- Witches flying through magical circles;
- talking animals;
- giant supernatural creatures;
- children possessing widespread magical blood;
- Demigods;
- and a highly organized planetary society despite its visually chaotic magical character.[1]
The civilization should therefore no longer be described simply as the same local society observed in Chapters 832–835. It is now an Imperial Witch civilization built upon the foundations of the original [[Magic]] World.

#### Divine Development
The world's enormous 100,000-year advancement produced a population with extremely high Divine potential. Approximately 99% of its inhabitants were described as theoretically capable of eventually reaching Godhood, forcing the previous Divine authorities to regulate ascension.[6]
Chapter 1179 confirms that actual Demigods now exist openly within the Witch-controlled civilization.[1] It does not establish how many exist, whether the former Godhood restrictions remain unchanged under Evie or how the Elderblood administration currently regulates Divine advancement.

### Magic
The world's defining supernatural resource remains [[Magic]], a neutral and highly flexible Energy capable of being shaped into a vast range of phenomena through sufficient understanding, compatibility and spellcraft.[9]
Known applications across Witch traditions include:
- elemental manipulation;
- barriers;
- dimensional Magic;
- enchantment;
- contracts;
- curses;
- rituals;
- Spatial Magic;
- transformation;
- information gathering;
- artifact construction;
- flight;
- and specialized magical engineering.
The modern Witch-controlled world demonstrates the natural conclusion of this versatility: rather than restricting Magic to individual spellcasters, supernatural principles are incorporated into transportation, infrastructure, urban technology and the biology of the population itself.[1]

### Military and Power

#### Paladins
The best documented original military class was the Paladin. Alfey combined martial training, [[Magic]], Divine Blessings, enchanted armor and a supernatural sword, demonstrating the multi-system nature of the world's original military.[3]
His equipment included defenses capable of interacting with supernatural attacks and a sword capable of manipulating Magic. Paladins could also benefit from the cooperation of several Gods rather than depending exclusively upon one patron.
Chapter 1179 confirms that Alfey eventually died sometime after the original contact, but not before Evie learned extensively from him.[1] Whether the Paladin order still exists under Witch rule has not yet been established.

#### Collective Warfare
The civilization's greatest original strength was organization. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] observed that a unified society could deploy supposedly weaker Gods far more efficiently than Earth's stronger but fragmented Pantheons.[4] Ironically, this principle was later surpassed by the [[Elderblood Empire]] itself, whose support allowed Evie's Witch forces to dominate the world during their eventual conquest.[1]

#### Modern Power
The precise military strength of the modern [[Magic]] World remains unquantified, but Chapter 1179 confirms that Demigods exist among the population and that Evie herself has become powerful enough to rule the entire planet.[1] Even so, the gap between a developed Lower Sector and the highest Elderblood Dragons remains enormous. Albedo—despite considering herself comparatively weak among the Dragons involved in the merger operation—had to seal part of her own power merely to safely enter the Lower Sector.[1]
This provides a direct demonstration of why Lower Sectors historically required protection from Beings originating in more developed cosmic environments.

### Known Inhabitants

#### Evie Moriarty Elderblood
[[Evie Moriarty Elderblood]] is the current Witch Queen and ruler of the [[Magic]] World.[1] [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] originally ordered her to take over the World of Magic in Chapter 1093, and the success of that campaign is confirmed in Chapter 1179. By then Evie rules the entire planet from a Witch-controlled capital and regards the original conflict as so one-sided under Imperial support that she hesitates to call it a genuine war.
Evie also inherited considerable knowledge from Alfey before his death and presides over a civilization containing Demigods, widespread magical blood and extensive Arcane technology.[1]

#### Emilly Moriarty
Emilly Moriarty is Evie's daughter and a member of the ruling Witch family. By Chapter 1179 she has grown into an adult woman residing within the Witch civilization and refers to their territory as Arcane.[1]

#### Alfey
Alfey was one of the first documented native inhabitants and a Paladin of the original civilization. His encounter with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] supplied much of the earliest information regarding the world's Divine military, artifacts and Pantheon.[3]
Alfey later became a source of knowledge for Evie. By Chapter 1179 he is confirmed to be dead, although the cause and date of death remain unrevealed.[1]

#### Unified Pantheon
The original civilization possessed a largely unified Divine hierarchy whose Gods coordinated their military and Blessings.[3][4] Most individual Gods remain unnamed. At least one God associated with Space was referenced, but there is insufficient evidence to identify that individual as the sole ruler of the original world.
The status of the former Pantheon following Evie's conquest has not yet been explained.

#### Drakyo
The Drakyo are an ancient Dragon-like race associated with the world's earliest history. The original defenders recognized [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] as one, indicating that Drakyo mythology or historical records survived even after the species appeared to have vanished.[3]
Chapter 1179 reveals that the race is not completely extinct. A small white Dragon kept near Evie's family is identified as the last Drakyo, and Evie states that the Drakyo are said to have created the world.[1] The exact meaning of "created" remains unknown: the available chapter does not establish whether they physically constructed the planet, terraformed it, founded civilization or played some other primordial role.
The Drakyo should not currently be categorized as [[Blood Dragons]], since Victor created that lineage much later and no direct biological relationship has been established.

### Relationship with the Elderblood Empire
The [[Magic]] World is no longer merely an external civilization observed by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. It is now under the control of the Elderblood-aligned Witch civilization.
Victor's order in Chapter 1093 established the intended conquest, while Chapter 1179 confirms its completion.[7][1] Evie rules the planet, the Witches have taken charge of its government, Imperial organization has reshaped the civilization and the population has been incorporated deeply enough into the Empire's sphere that its entire Lower Sector is being prepared for cosmological integration.
The precise administrative term for the planet—province, client world, Imperial territory or another designation—has not yet been stated, so the page should distinguish confirmed control from an invented bureaucratic title.

### The Merger of the Lower Sectors
Chapter 1179 marks the beginning of a new cosmological phase for the [[Magic]] World.
The Imperial leadership concluded that the controlled Sectors had reached the limits of their present development and decided to integrate them directly with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Sector.[1] Dark-purple spheres were placed in the relevant territories, with Albedo carrying the Witch World's sphere to Evie. Similar preparations occurred at [[Nightingale]] and Samar.
Ruby explained that the spheres would draw the Sectors toward them, while Victor handled the dangerous cosmological rearrangement. The movement could result in new Galactic structures appearing inside or along the edge of the receiving Sector, but it would not automatically cause the Sector to evolve into a higher category.[1]
Velnorah's explanation provides one of the clearest descriptions of the Sector hierarchy:

It represents the deliberate removal of a Lower Sector from the isolated environment in which the Primordial Beings originally allowed it to develop and its integration into a civilization powerful enough to survive outside that protection.

### Current Status
As of Chapter 1179, the [[Magic]] World is a fully active Witch-controlled planet within a Lower Sector under the political dominance of the [[Elderblood Empire]].[1]
Cosmologically, its Reality is explicitly a Lower Sector that has reached its current developmental limit and is being prepared for merger with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Sector.
Politically, [[Evie Moriarty Elderblood]] has completed Victor's order to take control of the world and now rules the planet as Witch Queen.[7][1]
Civilizationally, the world has undergone two enormous transformations: first the 100,000-year Primordial acceleration that produced a techno-magical society, and later the Witch conquest that spread Arcane culture and Imperial organization throughout the planet.[5][6][1]
Magically, Witch characteristics have become widespread enough that Albedo describes magical blood as flowing through the world, while Demigods and numerous magical species live openly throughout its cities.[1]
Historically, the last known Drakyo survives there, preserving a direct living connection to the race said to have created the world.[1]
Imperially, the planet is no longer merely a target of future conquest. It is an Elderblood-controlled territory whose entire Sector is now participating in the Empire's next phase of expansion.[1]

### Notes
- The [[Magic]] World is the planet; the Lower Sector is the much larger Reality containing it.
- Chapter 1179 explicitly confirms its classification among the Lower Sectors.[1]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] originally perceived the destination as an entirely different Universe, terminology later contextualized by the Sector cosmology.[2]
- Approximately 100,000 years passed inside its Sector while roughly 30 minutes passed externally.[5]
- The 100,000-year development did not itself prove that the Sector had evolved beyond Lower-Sector status; Chapter 1179 still explicitly identifies it as a Lower Sector.
- The Sector had nevertheless reached its existing developmental limit by the time of the merger.[1]
- Victor did not personally create the World's negative World Tree.
- Evie's conquest is now confirmed and should no longer be described as an unresolved mission.[7][1]
- Evie is explicitly described as the Witch Queen and ruler of the entire planet.[1]
- Alfey is confirmed dead by Chapter 1179, although the circumstances of his death remain unknown.[1]
- Evie learned extensively from Alfey before his death.[1]
- A surviving white Dragon is identified as the last Drakyo.[1]
- The Drakyo are said to have created the world, but the exact nature of that creation has not yet been explained.[1]
- The Drakyo should not automatically be classified as [[Blood Dragons]].
- Witch influence has become widespread throughout the modern population, with magical blood, Demigods, talking animals, giant magical creatures and extensive Arcane technology observed directly by Albedo.[1]
- Albedo had to suppress part of her own power simply to safely enter the Lower Sector, demonstrating the enormous difference between its native power environment and the current Elderblood Dragons.[1]
- The dark-purple sphere given to Evie is an anchor used in the Sector-merger operation, although its exact composition and nature remain unexplained.[1]
- Attempting to directly comprehend the sphere caused Evie pain, and Albedo warned that continuing could damage her mind.[1]
- The merger draws the smaller Sector toward Victor's Sector and can create or incorporate additional galaxies; it does not automatically increase Victor's Sector classification.[1]
- Victor is responsible for preventing Galactic collisions during the merger.[1]
- Velnorah compares Lower Sectors to isolated aquariums intentionally left by the Primordial Beings so Life can safely develop, while the Upper Sectors resemble an open ocean filled with predators.[1]
