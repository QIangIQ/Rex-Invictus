### Users
- [[Violet Elderblood Snow]]
Violet's Secret Techniques are a collection of ninety-nine magical techniques developed by Violet Elderblood Snow together with several members of her extended family. Violet describes the collection as something that she and her "sisters" developed, while [[Victor Elderblood]] correctly deduced that [[Hela Elderblood]] and curse-specialist [[Alice Moriarty]] had contributed to their creation; Violet then revealed that [[Persephone Elderblood]], whose extensive experience involved Souls and the [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]], had assisted as well.[1]
Despite their intentionally absurd names, the techniques are not simply jokes. They are designed around a serious combat principle: a living body, its Soul and its mental state contain vulnerabilities that can be exploited without needing to overwhelm the target through conventional destructive power.[1] Several attacks interfere with the Soul Information governing the body's physical form, others manipulate basic physiological processes, while Techniques 90–99 specialize in debilitating states of mind and mental illnesses.[1]
Victor initially reacted to the techniques as though Violet had created an absurd collection of useless attacks, but rapidly changed his assessment after considering their military application. He recognized that a user with Violet's scale of power could potentially inflict such effects across an entire enemy formation, destroying morale and combat capability without needing planet-destroying attacks.[1]

### Origin and Development
The techniques originated from what [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] immediately recognized as one of Violet's characteristically excessive ideas. Violet and her collaborators had developed an entire catalogue of specialized attacks, many carrying deliberately embarrassing or ridiculous names. Their initial appearance made the collection seem more like an elaborate family joke than a legitimate school of warfare.[1]
That impression changed almost immediately when Violet demonstrated what the techniques actually targeted.
The first two attacks did not merely cause conventional physical injury. They directly interfered with Soul Information—the information through which the Soul defines and maintains the corresponding physical body.[1] This allowed Violet to bypass the logic of ordinary external damage. Changing the physical body alone could become useless when the Soul itself continued supplying the altered information from which that body was maintained.
Other techniques were designed around the opposite principle: rather than modifying the Soul's information, they exploited ordinary biological functions that even tremendously powerful living beings still possessed. Violet summarized her philosophy by treating the physical body itself as a weakness. Disease, psychological fear, mental attack and involuntary physiological processes represented possible attack vectors regardless of how destructive the target might be.[1]

Victor ultimately concluded that the techniques had genuine military value and praised Violet for the result. He then requested a full list so that he could begin formulating defenses for soldiers of the Empire, showing that he considered the techniques dangerous enough that friendly forces required dedicated countermeasures.[1]

### Combat Philosophy
Violet's Secret Techniques are an example of asymmetric supernatural warfare. Their purpose is to bypass the assumption that the correct response to an overwhelmingly powerful opponent is simply to create a more destructive attack.
CONVENTIONAL APPROACH
Overpower the Target
Increase force, Energy and destructive scale until the enemy's defenses fail.
→
VIOLET'S APPROACH
Attack the Weakness
Identify a bodily, Soul-based or mental function that remains vulnerable and make continued combat impossible.
Violet's argument is particularly dangerous in a setting populated by beings whose raw durability can reach extraordinary levels. A Dragon, God or similarly powerful entity might survive attacks capable of devastating ordinary battlefields, but survival does not automatically mean immunity to every Soul, physiological or mental mechanism.
Her example of Explosive Diarrhea is intentionally comedic but illustrates the logic clearly. Violet argues that even a powerful Dragon remains a living organism with basic physiological processes. If those processes are disrupted badly enough, the enemy does not need to be physically destroyed to become combat ineffective.[1]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] immediately extrapolates the same principle toward warfare. If Violet can spread a debilitating technique across an army, then thousands of combatants could potentially be neutralized without the collateral damage created by conventional large-scale attacks.[1]

05SCALEApply an individually debilitating effect across battlefield-sized groups.

### Secret Technique 01: Testicular Torsion
Secret Technique 01: Testicular Torsion is the first Secret Technique Violet demonstrates against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. She manifests a violet [[Magic]] Circle and fires the technique toward him, immediately triggering Victor's instincts strongly enough that he chooses to defend rather than allow the attack to make contact.[1]
The technique directly attacks the Soul Information responsible for generating the male reproductive organs. Its purpose is to produce the greatest possible pain in a male target rather than simply inflicting ordinary external physical damage.[1]

#### Mechanism
The important component is the target's Soul Information. The technique does not need to physically manipulate the organs through conventional contact. It attacks the information responsible for their existence and physical manifestation.[1]
This distinction makes the technique fundamentally different from simply striking a vulnerable organ. Its attack vector is spiritual/informational rather than conventional physical force.

#### Confirmed Defense
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] blocks the technique because he possesses the ability to control his Soul. The narration specifically identifies an individual like Victor—someone capable of controlling the Soul—as able to stop the attack.[1]

### Secret Technique 02: Phallic Reduction
After Testicular Torsion fails, Violet immediately follows with Secret Technique 02: Phallic Reduction. The technique is projected as a beam of Energy and, like Technique 01, directly targets the target's Soul Information.[1]

#### Soul Information and the Physical Body
The technique demonstrates an important relationship between Soul and body. If the attack successfully modifies the relevant information, simply changing the flesh afterward is insufficient. The Soul fails to recognize the attempted physical correction and continues imposing its altered information upon the body.[1]
This gives the technique a form of body-modification bypass. A target cannot necessarily solve the problem by shapeshifting, regenerating or otherwise modifying the physical organ if the underlying Soul Information remains unchanged.

#### Restoration
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] identifies a Soul Master as the type of individual capable of repairing the damage.[1] This implies that successful restoration requires correcting the spiritual/informational alteration itself rather than merely reconstructing flesh.

### Secret Technique 90: Deep Depression
Secret Technique 90: Deep Depression demonstrates the psychological branch of Violet's Secret Techniques. Instead of firing a narrow beam, Violet releases a miasma from her body that spreads throughout the surrounding area.[1]
The miasma directly attacks the Souls of those within the field, inducing an extreme depressive state characterized by overwhelming loss of motivation and emotional collapse. The effect can become severe enough that victims may develop suicidal impulses.[1]

### Underlying Mechanics
The ninety-nine techniques do not all appear to operate through one identical supernatural mechanism. The revealed techniques instead demonstrate several different attack vectors.

#### Soul Information
Techniques 01 and 02 establish Soul Information as one of the collection's most dangerous mechanisms.[1]
In this context, the Soul does more than contain consciousness. It carries information corresponding to the physical body. If that information is attacked or modified, manipulating the resulting flesh alone may fail to solve the problem because the underlying spiritual blueprint remains altered.
SOUL INFORMATION
Defines the relevant physical state
↓
PHYSICAL BODY

↓
ORDINARY CORRECTION
Can fail if the Soul still retains the altered state
The two revealed Soul Information attacks nevertheless behave differently. Testicular Torsion primarily uses the attack vector to generate extraordinary pain, while Phallic Reduction alters information in a manner that persists against ordinary body modification.[1]

#### Physiological Exploitation
Technique 05 establishes a second branch: attacking biological processes directly.
This approach does not necessarily require permanent Soul alteration. Its purpose is to exploit the fact that living combatants possess bodies with involuntary processes that can be turned against them.[1]

#### Soul-Targeted Mental Manipulation
Technique 90 combines mental-status manipulation with direct Soul interaction. Its miasma attacks Souls throughout the affected area and produces an altered emotional state.[1]
The technique therefore should not be reduced to conventional intimidation. Deep Depression is a supernatural status-inducing attack rather than Violet simply frightening or verbally demoralizing her targets.

#### Magic and Energy Projection
The revealed techniques are activated through magical/supernatural Energy. Violet manifests a violet [[Magic]] Circle for Testicular Torsion and fires several techniques as Energy beams, while Deep Depression uses an emitted miasma.[1]

### Military and Battlefield Application
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s reaction is one of the most important pieces of information concerning the Secret Techniques. He initially focuses upon their absurdity, but the moment Violet explains their battlefield use he recognizes them as legitimate strategic weapons.[1]

#### Mass Incapacitation
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] realizes that Violet possesses enough power to affect an entire army. A large-scale deployment of an effect such as Testicular Torsion against compatible targets could collapse enemy morale without the need for conventional bombardment.[1]
The tactical advantage is obvious:
Conventional Large-Scale Attack
Violet's Secret-Technique Doctrine
Attempts to destroy enemy bodies or defenses.
Attempts to make those bodies incapable of fighting.
Often requires enormous raw output.
Can exploit specialized weaknesses instead.
May devastate terrain and infrastructure.
Certain techniques can neutralize combatants without comparable environmental destruction.
Strong durability can reduce effectiveness.
Some techniques target Soul Information, physiology or mental condition rather than ordinary toughness.
Victory is achieved through destruction.
Victory can be achieved through incapacitation and collapse of morale.
This does not mean that the Secret Techniques universally ignore durability or function against every possible existence. Rather, their strategic value comes from attacking dimensions of the opponent that ordinary durability may not protect.

#### Anti-Army Warfare
Explosive Diarrhea is specifically discussed as an anti-army technique. Violet asks whether an enemy army could properly concentrate while every soldier was overwhelmed by the need to use the bathroom.[1]
Deep Depression potentially serves an even broader role because its miasma can affect multiple Souls across the surrounding area.[1]

#### Morale Warfare
The collection is deliberately designed to humiliate and psychologically destabilize as well as physically incapacitate. Even an enemy that survives the techniques may lose cohesion, concentration or willingness to continue fighting.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] summarizes the strategic value by recognizing that conventional planet-cracking power is unnecessary if an opponent can instead be completely demoralized through a much more targeted effect.[1]
99
BATTLEFIELD DOCTRINE
Destroy Combat Effectiveness, Not Necessarily the Combatant
The series weaponizes Soul structure, involuntary biology and mental condition to collapse an enemy formation from inside its own bodies and minds.

#### Against Non-Living Enemies
Violet acknowledges an obvious limitation in her philosophy: machines do not possess the same biological vulnerabilities as living beings. Her response is not that the Secret Techniques themselves automatically affect machines, but that Velnorah can deal with technological opponents through her own technological abilities.[1]
This distinction should be preserved. Velnorah's technological control is a complementary family resource, not an inherent property of Violet's ninety-nine techniques.

#### Soul Control
Soul control is the clearest demonstrated defensive principle. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ability to control his Soul allows him to stop Testicular Torsion rather than being helpless against the informational attack.[1]
This should not be expanded into the claim that all Soul manipulation automatically grants immunity. The level of control needed is not quantified.

#### Soul Restoration
Phallic Reduction provides a different interaction. Once its Soul Information alteration occurs, ordinary manipulation of the physical body is insufficient; a Soul Master is required to properly restore the affected information.[1]

[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] avoids Techniques 03–05 instead of testing their effects directly, demonstrating that conventional evasion remains useful when a technique is delivered through a projectile or beam.[1]
His protection of Hilda during Deep Depression also demonstrates the value of defensive barriers or other methods that prevent the hostile miasma from reaching the target.[1]

#### Unknown Compatibility
The techniques have not been demonstrated against every Race, form of artificial life, incorporeal being or higher-dimensional existence. Their effectiveness should therefore be indexed according to confirmed targets and mechanisms rather than automatically assumed to apply without limit.

### Powers & Abilities
The following abilities are derived from the confirmed mechanics demonstrated by the Secret Techniques.
- Soul Manipulation / Soul Information Manipulation: Techniques 01 and 02 directly target information contained within the Soul that governs physical characteristics. Technique 90 also directly attacks exposed Souls through its miasma.[1]
- Biological Manipulation / Physiological Manipulation: The collection includes attacks designed to exploit biological processes rather than conventional physical durability, with Explosive Diarrhea being the clearest demonstrated example.[1]
- Pain Inducement: Testicular Torsion is explicitly designed to cause the greatest possible pain to a male target through an attack upon reproductive Soul Information.[1]
- Bodily Alteration: Phallic Reduction modifies Soul Information in a manner that changes the corresponding physical state.[1]
- Limited Healing / Body Modification Negation: Physical modification alone cannot properly reverse the effect of Phallic Reduction because the altered Soul does not recognize the attempted correction; restoration instead requires a Soul Master.[1]
- Emotion Manipulation / Depression Inducement: Deep Depression induces an extreme depressive state and destroys motivation by attacking the Souls of those within its miasma.[1]
- Mental Status Inducement: Violet confirms that Techniques 90–99 are primarily devoted to debilitating states of mind and mental illnesses.[1]
- Miasma Generation: Deep Depression is distributed through miasma emitted from Violet's body.[1]
- Area-of-Effect Attacks: Deep Depression affects an area rather than one isolated target, and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] recognizes that Violet possesses the scale necessary to deploy certain Secret Techniques against armies.[1]
- Combat Incapacitation: The collection is fundamentally designed to make targets incapable of continuing effective combat through pain, physiological disruption, Soul alteration or mental collapse.[1]
- Psychological Warfare: Violet deliberately values the techniques for their ability to demoralize enemy forces, a military application Victor ultimately agrees is highly effective.[1]

### Imperial Countermeasure Program
After recognizing the military value of the ninety-nine techniques, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] asks Violet to provide him with the complete list so he can formulate countermeasures for Imperial soldiers.[1]
This reaction is significant for two reasons. First, Victor does not dismiss the collection as comedy once he understands its mechanics. Second, he immediately treats it as a security problem that his own forces need to be prepared against.

### Notes and Clarifications
- There are ninety-nine Secret Techniques. This number is directly stated by Violet.[1]
- Only six are presently named in the revealed explanation: 01, 02, 03, 04, 05 and 90.[1]
- Techniques 03 and 04 do not receive detailed mechanical explanations. Hormonal, neural or sensitivity-based mechanisms should not be treated as confirmed unless later canon explains them.
- Technique 01 directly targets Soul Information associated with male reproductive organs and is intended to inflict extreme pain.[1]
- Technique 02 alters Soul Information strongly enough that ordinary body modification cannot properly reverse the result.[1]
- A Soul Master can repair Technique 02's alteration.[1]
- Explosive Diarrhea is primarily an incapacitation technique, not a high-damage attack.[1]
- Deep Depression directly attacks Souls through an area-covering miasma.[1]
- Techniques 90–99 specialize primarily in debilitating states of mind and mental illnesses.[1]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] can defend against the Soul-targeting techniques because of his advanced Soul control.[1]
- Victor shields Hilda from Deep Depression rather than allowing the miasma to affect her.[1]
- Violet is the only directly demonstrated user in Chapter 1094. Hela, [[Alice Moriarty]] and [[Persephone]] are confirmed collaborators in the creation of the techniques, but collaboration alone should not automatically be indexed as mastery of all ninety-nine.
- "Victor's Wives" should not be used as a blanket confirmed-user entry unless later chapters explicitly establish that the complete harem can execute the techniques.
- Velnorah's ability to handle machines is not one of Violet's Secret Techniques. Violet mentions it as the family's solution to enemies whose mechanical bodies would not possess the same physiological weaknesses.[1]
- Victor recognized genuine wartime value in the techniques and requested the complete list so defenses could be developed for Imperial personnel.[1]

### Trivia
