### Appearances

#### Debut
Arrives after [[Vaelthar]] has restored the three
Well, it's true, this faction is among the weakest and the most isolated.
—Justice, to [[Victor Elderblood]]
Justice is the envoy who comes for War in the name of the patron aligned with <Emperor>. He is faceless, he wears a suit, and he has sharp teeth. The presence he brings is at the level of a Throne. That weight is not a seat among the seven Thrones, and he is not one of the Chief Gods who closed around the wounded three. His patron wants War. [[Elarion]] wants him for a different patron. War refuses to be handed to either.[1]
He does not join the attack on the projection. He is still standing when it is over, in a cold sweat, beside Elarion. Victor Elderblood asks him, not Elarion, what he makes of these famous factions of the upper sectors. Justice calls Black Eclipse one of the weakest and the most isolated. Victor says that soon it will no longer exist, and that he will do Justice the favor. Justice thanks him without knowing what he has just said. What he wants is to be gone.[2]

### Physical Appearance
Faceless. A suit. Sharp teeth. Nothing else is given. No height, no eyes, no hair, no skin, no weapon.[1]
[[Elarion]], who comes in behind him, is the God of Goodness in golden armor. [[Vaelthar]], already on the field, is ivory and violet, four-armed and four-eyed, near three meters. Justice is the suited one. The presence that arrives with that body sits at the level of a Throne. After the projection fight he is still in the suit, standing with Elarion. Both of them are in a cold sweat. The suit is not used as an attack. The teeth are not used as one either.[1][2]

### Personality
He speaks for his patron, and then he tries to leave. The Emperor-aligned patron wants War. Justice says so. He does not greet [[Vaelthar]], he does not rebuke the Chief Gods, and he does not argue when War refuses to be portioned out.[1]
The attack is not his, and he does not pretend it is. Asked what the factions amount to, he does not defend Black Eclipse and he does not flatter [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. His answer is that this faction is among the weakest and the most isolated. The thanks that follows is not a bargain he understands. "Eh? Thanks, I think...?" He no longer knows what he is saying. He wants to get away. The message back to a leader is given to [[Elarion]], not to him. Victor calls Elarion a dog. He does not call Justice one.[2]

### Synopsis

#### Those Who Rule Reality
The duel inside [[Gol-goroth]]'s domain is already over when he arrives. [[Azrakar]] has been erased. War, Famine, Death, and Pestilence have driven [[Kaelith]], [[Nerath]], and [[Vhalor]] to the point of execution. [[Vaelthar]], Throne of Origin, has come through at the head of the relief. War has judged him stronger, seen no way out, and shut the domain down. Vaelthar has put the three back with a beam of pure darkness and told them they disappointed the [[Vharzath|Great Darkness]]. They have bowed.[3][1]
Justice arrives first. Faceless, suited, sharp-toothed, a presence at the level of a Throne. [[Elarion]] follows, God of Goodness, in golden armor. Each says that his own patron wants War. They are not part of the ten who closed around the wounded three. Those ten were nine galactic-level threats and Vaelthar, the one true God at a Throne's level in that count. The two who come after are envoys of the other patrons.[1]
The claim on the herald has three sides. Darkness is already on the field, under Vaelthar. The other two patrons have sent Justice and Elarion. One of those patrons is aligned with <Emperor>. The other is Light. Justice is the Emperor faction's envoy. Elarion is not his colleague in any chain of command. A divinity of <BEGIN> flares while the two of them make their claims. It is Vaelthar's. War is the one who notes it. It matches the Emperor's, and it feels corrupted. It is not Justice's power, and it is not a clean copy of the divinity it resembles. The corruption has a maker. Vharzath put his own Darkness into that Throne.[1][4]
War refuses to be decided over. He names himself the incarnation of war and the Dragon Emperor's herald, and he says he will kill anyone in his way. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] answers, "Well said, my herald," and appears as a giant dragon holding the whole galaxy. Light, Darkness, and the Emperor-aligned patron turn toward him. Darkness is overjoyed. Negativity has been found. Justice is the envoy of that third patron. He is not the patron, and he is not the one the text shows rejoicing.[1]
Victor praises War. War sees that his father has fully recovered. Victor calls the Gods a disappointment and kills the restored Kaelith by the Soul. The body is erased. He holds the Soul. Only the patrons perceive the method. Justice is not named as the hand, and he is not named as one of the patrons who see it. Victor then names Vharzath and declares extinction. The planets of Black Eclipse are already being hit from every side. Vaelthar orders the attack anyway, with their chance of living through Victor under one percent. Justice is not given that order.[1]
The attack spends itself on a projection. The true body is the dragon, still holding the galaxy, and it hardly moves. Vaelthar's restraints, his blow, and his domain prison fail. He is left under a foot, the sword at his head, and the sword does not come down in that moment. Justice and Elarion are still standing. Victor studies the two enforcers of the other factions. Both are in a cold sweat.[2]
He asks what these famous factions of the upper sectors are. Children with too much power, who think they are strong, or simply weak. The question is for Justice. Justice says this faction is among the weakest and the most isolated. Victor accepts it. Soon, he says, they will no longer exist. He will do Justice the favor. Justice's answer is "Eh? Thanks, I think...?" He does not know what he is saying. He wants to leave.[2]
Vharzath does not come out. Victor had meant the appearance as a lure, and the leader's pride is not enough to take it. The living of Black Eclipse are ordered taken prisoner, and the remains of the fallen are to be collected. Elarion is the one told to inform his own leader that Victor will visit soon, because that leader has something that belongs to him. Justice is not given that errand. He is not named among the prisoners, and he is not named among the dead. The projection leaves with War and the fallen Gods. Elarion watches them go. Justice's departure is not described.[2]
He is not seen again when <Emperor> bargains with Vharzath, and he is not named when that same skull later asks for the Enforcers to be spent against the Thrones. The field called him one of the two enforcers of the other factions. It does not show him answering that later call.[2][5]

### Skills & Abilities
He does not strike, heal, or open a domain. What he brings onto the field is the weight of his presence, his patron's claim, and one sentence about Black Eclipse once the fight is already lost.

#### Presence
- Throne-level Presence: He arrives at the level of a Throne. That weight is his. It is not a seat among the seven Thrones of Black Eclipse, and it is not [[Vaelthar]]'s office. [[Elarion]], who follows, is not given the same line.[1]
- The Body: Faceless, a suit, sharp teeth. None of the three is spent as an attack.[1]

#### The Envoy
- The Claim: His patron wants War. [[Elarion]] makes the same kind of demand for another patron. The two do not share a master. War refuses both, and refuses Black Eclipse with them.[1]
- Not the Reinforcement: He stands outside the ten who closed on the wounded three, and outside the order [[Vaelthar]] gives to attack. He is still on his feet when that attack has failed.[1][2]
- The Assessment: Asked what the famous factions amount to, he places this one among the weakest and the most isolated. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] takes that as enough, and promises the faction will soon be gone. Justice does not understand the thanks he gives.[2]

#### Not Shown
No weapon, no Concept, no domain, and no personal blow. God of Justice is not a title the text gives him. A Throne-level presence does not make him a Primordial God, an Overlord, or one of <Emperor>'s Thrones. The later request that the Enforcers be sent against [[Vharzath]]'s Thrones does not name him as one of the beings who would go.[1][5]

### Trivia
- The name on the field is Justice. He is not called God of Justice, and he is not given a second name.[1]
- He arrives before [[Elarion]]. He has the Throne-level presence. Elarion has the title God of Goodness and the golden armor. Each speaks for his own patron.[1]
- He is the envoy of the patron aligned with <Emperor>. Elarion is not that patron's man. Light and Darkness are the other two who turn when the dragon appears.[1]
- He is faceless, suited, and sharp-toothed.[1]
- He is not part of [[Vaelthar]]'s reinforcement. The ten who stand with the wounded three are counted without him.[1]
- A Throne-level presence is not a seat on the seven Thrones. Vaelthar is the Throne named on this field.[1]
- The <BEGIN> that flares while he and Elarion claim War is Vaelthar's. War notes that it matches the Emperor's and that it feels corrupted. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is not the one who makes that note. The stain is Vharzath's Darkness.[1][4]
- When the dragon appears, Light, Darkness, and the Emperor-aligned patron all turn. Darkness is the one overjoyed at Negativity. Justice is the third patron's envoy, not the patron.[1]
- Only the patrons perceive the method by which Victor kills [[Kaelith]]. Justice is not named among them.[1]
- War refuses to be given to him, and in the same breath names himself the Dragon Emperor's herald. Victor's answer is "Well said, my herald."[1]
- He does not join the attack. He and Elarion are the two enforcers of the other factions, still standing at the end, in a cold sweat. Victor's question is asked of him.[2]
- His answer is that Black Eclipse is among the weakest and the most isolated. Victor says he will do him the favor of ending it. Justice thanks him and wants to leave.[2]
- He is not given Elarion's errand. Elarion is told to warn his own leader. Victor calls that envoy a dog. Justice is not called one, and he is not named among the prisoners or the dead.[2]
- He is not named again when <Emperor> speaks to Vharzath, or when the Enforcers are asked for. The skull and this suited envoy are not shown in the same place.[4][5]
