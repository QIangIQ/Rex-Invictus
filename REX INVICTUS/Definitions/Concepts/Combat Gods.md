**[[Combat Gods]]**, also referred to as Battle Gods or Fighting Gods, are Gods whose practical fighting ability is developed far beyond that of ordinary deities of comparable standing. (Lore adapted from *My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** This material is adapted into the Rex Invictus world at the lowest priority: Rex Invictus wins any conflict, then the Flamel material, then MTWABV. Events of the MTWABV timeline (Victor's conquests, the fall of the pantheons, the [[Elderblood Empire]]) are distant background or rumor unless a chapter puts them on the page. COMBAT GODS
The specialized fighting hierarchy of Divine beings
Combat Gods, also referred to as Battle Gods or Fighting Gods, are Gods whose practical fighting ability is developed far beyond that of ordinary deities of comparable standing. Unlike the baseline God hierarchy, which primarily describes the development of Godhood and Divinities, the Combat God system evaluates how effectively a Being converts their Energy, Divine abilities, body, martial skill, weapons, experience and instincts into actual battle performance.[1]

### Overview
The term Combat God does not simply mean "a God who fights". Nearly every deity is capable of defending themselves, and many ordinary Gods possess offensive Divinities. A Combat God is distinguished by the degree to which their complete power set has been refined for actual confrontation.
The Author's power-ranking explanation establishes this distinction particularly clearly through [[Victor Elderblood]]. Around Chapter 630, Victor already possessed quantities of Energy comparable to a low God-King, yet his ordinary effective combat classification was only that of a Low-Level Combat God. By perfectly controlling his body and techniques he could fight opponents in the general Mid-Level God range, while his Vampire Count transformation allowed him to confront High-Level Combat Gods such as [[Ares]] and [[Thor]].[1]
The result establishes one of the most important rules of Divine combat:
ENERGY RESERVE ≠ COMBAT CLASS
Possessing enough Energy to rival a God-King does not mean the user can fight like a God-King.
Combat God classification consequently represents effective fighting capability: how much of a Being's theoretical power can actually be converted into usable force against another powerful opponent.

### Nature of the Classification
A Combat God's strength is produced by the interaction of many different factors rather than by one numerical statistic.
DIVINITY
Combat applicability, mastery and understanding of the God's Concepts.
TECHNIQUE
[[Martial Arts|Martial arts]], weapon handling, movement, timing and technical efficiency.
BODY
Physical strength, durability, senses, regeneration and racial advantages.
EXPERIENCE
Battle history, adaptability, instinct, tactical judgment and psychological warfare.
EQUIPMENT
Divine Artifacts, weapons, armor and specialized tools capable of changing a matchup.
ENERGY CONTROL
How efficiently available Energy is converted into attacks, defense and sustained battle.
The Author explicitly warns against treating supernatural battles as equations in which an individual with "2,000 Power" automatically defeats someone with "1,000 Power". Skill, experience, psychological state, weaknesses, equipment, battlefield circumstances and the interaction between abilities can overturn a simple difference in raw Energy.[1]
[[Aphrodite]] is one of the examples used to demonstrate the problem. Her straightforward combat ability historically fell below that of [[Ares]], but her authority over Love and Beauty could still dominate Beings with greater direct Power if they lacked an appropriate resistance.[1]

### Low-Level Combat God
Low-Level Combat God — Entry Fighting-God Class
Low-Level Combat Gods represent the beginning of the specialized Divine combat hierarchy. They should not be confused with ordinary Low-Level Gods. The latter describes Divine development, while Low-Level Combat God indicates that the Being's overall battle package has become sufficiently efficient to occupy a more dangerous fighting bracket.
Around Chapter 630, the Author explicitly classified [[Victor Elderblood]] as a Low-Level Combat God despite his Energy reserves already approaching the God-King range.[1] Victor's condition demonstrates that entry into this class is not determined by how much Energy a combatant possesses, but by how effectively that Energy can actually be controlled and weaponized.
The class remained important far later in the story. When Victor explained the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s military threat system before the Upper-Sector war, he stated that the minimum power necessary to intervene in the coming battles was that of a Lower-Level Fighting God. Imperial systems classified a Being of that level as CODE: V-03 — Continental Level, capable of destroying entire continents.[2]

#### Characteristics
- Entry level of the formal Combat God ladder.
- Practical combat capability is already considerably more important than raw Energy alone.
- Can be reached by combatants whose fighting methods are optimized enough to exceed ordinary Divine opponents.
- The later Imperial threat system places the Lower-Level Fighting God benchmark at CODE: V-03.[2]
- V-03 should not be treated as an exclusive Combat God code; the threat system and Divine combat hierarchy measure different properties.

### Mid-Level Combat God
Mid-Level Combat God — Established Divine Warrior
Mid-Level Combat Gods possess substantially greater practical fighting development than the entry Combat God class. Their abilities, body, Energy control, technique and combat experience are sufficiently refined that simple comparisons based upon raw [[Divine Energy]] become increasingly unreliable.
[[Victor Elderblood]] provides a direct canonical example of progression into this range. In Chapter 650, an enemy report concluded that his combat prowess had increased to the level of a Mid-Level Combat God. The same report simultaneously noted that Victor's Vampire Count transformation could generate Energy at the level of a God-King or possibly higher, once again separating Energy output from actual fighting classification.[3]
The report further concluded that Victor's speed in that transformation had become dangerous enough that only High-Level Combat Gods were expected to handle him reliably.[3] A Mid-Level Combat God can therefore possess specific advantages capable of threatening the next class without automatically belonging to that class overall.

#### Characteristics
- Established specialized Divine combatant.
- Greater integration between body, technique, Divinity and Energy than the lower Combat God class.
- Can threaten stronger nominal opponents through speed, specialized powers, artifacts or favorable matchups.
- Does not require God-King levels of raw Energy.
- Conversely, possessing God-King-level Energy does not automatically move a Mid-Level Combat God into the God-King combat bracket.[3]

### High-Level Combat God
High-Level Combat God — Apex Conventional Divine Warrior
High-Level Combat Gods represent the highest demonstrated conventional Combat God stage before the separate God-King hierarchy.
The Author explicitly uses [[Ares]] and [[Thor]] as benchmarks for this class. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s [[Vampire Count Form]] around Chapter 630 was described as capable of fighting High-Level Combat Gods like Ares and Thor, while complete control over that form was expected to allow Victor to confront several [[God-Kings]].[1]
Chapter 650 reinforces the same distinction from another direction. Even after Victor's normal combat prowess had risen to Mid-Level Combat God, his extreme speed in Count Form led his enemies to conclude that only a High-Level Combat God could properly contend with him in that state.[3]
This class should consequently be treated as the apex of conventional Divine combat specialization, not as a synonym for every powerful or famous war deity. A God's mythological association with War, Battle, Hunting or Weapons does not automatically establish High-Level Combat God status.

#### Characteristics
- Highest conventional Combat God class.
- [[Ares]] and [[Thor]] are explicit Author benchmarks.[1]
- Combines highly developed fighting instincts, Divine abilities, physical capability and technical efficiency.
- Can remain dangerous to [[God-Kings]] through specialized advantages or favorable matchups without automatically possessing God-King status.
- Being a famous God of War does not independently prove membership in this class.

### Tyr's Classification
The previous version of this article placed Tyr among High-Level Combat Gods. A source audit does not support that placement.
Tyr is unquestionably a [[Ares|God of War]] and a combat-oriented deity, but a combat-related Divinity is not enough to equate him with Ares or [[Thor]]. In Chapter 961, Leonidas directly calls Tyr a fighting God before challenging him, yet treats him as dramatically inferior and defeats him with overwhelming confidence.[6]
For this reason, Tyr is indexed here conservatively as a Low-Level Combat God rather than preserving the old unsourced High-Level classification. This also reinforces an important rule of the system:
God of War ≠ High-Level Combat God.
A Divinity describes what a God represents. Combat God rank describes how dangerous that Being actually is in battle.

### The "Minor Combat God" Terminology
Chapter 889 describes Violet, Sasha and Ruby as having progressed from the combat effectiveness of a Minor Combat God to that of a High-Level Combat Focused God after their experiences inside the Tower of Nightmare.[4]
This wording establishes that Minor Combat God can be used descriptively as a comparison for comparatively weak Divine fighting power. However, the principal Author ranking and the repeatedly used formal combat ladder establish Low-Level, Mid-Level and High-Level Combat God as the primary conventional stages. For that reason, Minor Combat God is not treated here as a separate formal fourth tier unless future material defines it as such.

### Advancement
Unlike ordinary Divine advancement, Combat God progression does not require the acquisition of a new Concept or a change of Divine generation. A fighter can advance simply by becoming dramatically better at using what they already possess.
Important methods include:
- Energy Control: Reducing wasted output and converting a greater portion of available Energy into useful attacks or defenses.
- [[Martial Arts]]: Improving movement, timing, positioning, counters and the ability to fight opponents with superior raw statistics.
- Weapon Mastery: Learning to use Divine Artifacts or racial weapons as genuine extensions of the body instead of relying on their inherent Power.
- Divinity Integration: Combining Divine Concepts naturally with combat rather than treating them as isolated supernatural attacks.
- Battle Experience: Learning to read unfamiliar powers, exploit mistakes and adapt under lethal pressure.
- Physical Development: Strengthening the body, senses, regeneration, durability and reaction speed supporting Divine abilities.
- Transformation Control: Learning to access powerful forms without losing precision or exhausting the body unnecessarily.
- Psychological Discipline: Preventing rage, fear, arrogance or manipulation from destroying otherwise superior technical performance.
- Specialized Counters: Developing methods against Soul attacks, Conceptual powers, spatial abilities, mind influence, regeneration and other unusual threats.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s movement from Low-Level Combat God around Chapter 630 to explicitly assessed Mid-Level Combat God by Chapter 650 demonstrates that combat rank can improve extremely rapidly when an abnormal fighter gains greater control over existing Power.[1][3]

### Temporary Power and Transformations
A Combat God classification should distinguish between a character's ordinary state and temporary states produced by transformations, external buffs or artifacts.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] again provides the clearest example. His ordinary combat level and his [[Vampire Count Form]] occupied radically different practical ranges even though both belonged to the same individual.[1] Similarly, Violet, Sasha and Ruby's Vampire Count Forms after the Tower of Nightmare allowed them to confront opponents such as [[Thor]] and [[Ares]] even though their overall development was continuing independently.[4]
A character should therefore not automatically be assigned a permanent higher Combat God class because:
- one transformation temporarily produces greater output;
- a Divine Artifact allows one attack above the user's ordinary level;
- an ally provides a temporary amplification;
- a specialized ability bypasses the normal defenses of a stronger opponent;
- or the character wins a single favorable matchup.
The form or circumstance responsible for the higher performance should be recorded separately whenever possible.

### Common Misconceptions
Combat classification errors
“Every [[Ares|God of War]] is a High-Level Combat God.”
No. A Divinity indicates the Concept represented by a God, not their level of practical fighting development. Tyr is the clearest reason not to make this assumption.[6]
“A God-King amount of Energy makes someone a God-King fighter.”
No. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] possessed approximately God-King quantities of Energy while his ordinary combat classification remained Low-Level Combat God.[1]
“A Mid-Level Combat God cannot threaten a High-Level Combat God.”
Not necessarily. Victor's Chapter 650 combat prowess was Mid-Level Combat God, yet his transformation and extreme speed required High-Level Combat Gods to contend with him reliably.[3]
“Combat God is a Divine generation.”
No. It is a fighting classification. Age, lineage and Divine generation do not automatically decide placement.
“Non-Gods cannot reach Combat God levels.”
They can reach equivalent fighting strength. The Author explicitly places Vampires, [[Werewolves]], Demons, Angels and other supernatural beings in brackets comparable to Combat Gods.[1]
“Low-Level Combat God means V-03 in every circumstance.”
V-03 is a later operational threat designation. Chapter 1149 explicitly uses Lower-Level Fighting God as the V-03 baseline, but the two systems measure different characteristics and should not be treated as permanently interchangeable.[2]
“Minor Combat God is definitely a formal fourth stage.”
Chapter 889 uses the expression as a combat-effectiveness comparison, but the established hierarchy otherwise uses Low-Level, Mid-Level and High-Level Combat God. Until the story formally defines Minor Combat God as a separate tier, it is best treated as descriptive wording.[4]

High-Level Combat God

Next Boundary
God-King — a separate hierarchy rather than a fourth Combat God stage.
Combat God classification is ultimately a measurement of what a Being can actually accomplish in a fight. Energy, Divinity, physical power and status provide resources, but combat class reflects how effectively those resources are converted into victory. This is why Victor could possess God-King Energy while remaining a Low- or Mid-Level Combat God, why transformations could temporarily let him challenge High-Level Combat Gods, why Ares and Thor serve as stronger combat benchmarks than many politically important Gods, and why non-Divine warriors such as [[Scathach]] or Rose could historically occupy equivalent fighting ranges.[1][3]
