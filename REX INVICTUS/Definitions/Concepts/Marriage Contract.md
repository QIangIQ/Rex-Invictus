## MTWABV Canon

This is a temporary secrecy contract that functions as a preliminary agreement before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]’s full [[Soul Marriage]].
Its purpose is to protect confidential information about Victor, his family, and the [[Elderblood Imperial Family]]. The contract contains essentially one absolute condition: the signer must not reveal anything they learn about the Emperor, his family, or anything connected to him to anyone outside the recognized Elderblood family.
The contract is written partly in Eldritch language, with the surname “Elderblood” specifically written in Eldritch script. [[Tyche]] is unable to properly read that portion and immediately suffers a severe headache when attempting to do so, suggesting that the language itself contains information or power beyond what she can presently comprehend.
The text also explains that Victor has placed an Eldritch order upon the Elderblood surname. Only people whom Victor personally recognizes are permitted to use it. This acts as a supernatural safeguard against outsiders falsely claiming membership in his family.
Only ones who may have had a contract like this or adjacent before Soul Marriage were Tyche Elderblood, [[Dun Scaith Elderblood]], [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]], [[Kali Elderblood]] and likely [[Ariel Elderblood]].
