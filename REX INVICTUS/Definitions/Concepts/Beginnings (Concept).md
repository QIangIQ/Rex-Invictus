BEGIN, also called Beginning and Beginnings, is the Concept of the start, and of existence continuing, in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]]. It is as unique as END. Like Negativity, it covers smaller Concepts under it. The ones named at the awakening are life, and the perpetuation of existence.[1]
On the positive side the order of importance is Positivity, then BEGIN, then the Concept of Creation. On the negative side it is Negativity, then END, then Destruction. BEGIN is second only to Positivity. END is its only rival, and END is warded off only by BEGIN.[2][1][3][4]
END was given by Death. BEGIN was not. It was given by the positive sub-consciousness of existence itself. Exercising it should have put Victor on the positive side of the scale alone. That is not where he stopped.[1]
Not the same word
BEGIN is the Greater Concept, second only to Positivity. The Concept of Creation sits under it and is narrower: bringing a thing forth. The word "creation" is also used for the universe. That cosmos is not this page, and it is not the Concept under BEGIN. A Progenitor's Beginning is the start of one race. Lilith holds that kind for demons. Vaelthar holds a BEGIN that Darkness stained. None of those is a clean copy of Victor's.

### What It Is
BEGIN awoke out of the status of Progenitor. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was the beginning of everything that status meant. The Concept covers other, smaller Concepts. Life is one. The perpetuation of existence is another. His proficiency in it, at the moment it awoke, was nil. It is not a simple Concept to understand.[1]
It is a Concept directly related to Positivity. On the scale of importance it is second only to the Divinity of Positivity itself. Five major Divinities awoke with it, and ten minor ones. Two of those majors were the most important Divinities in existence. War came first, and he was already the highest-ranking [[Ares|God of War]]. Destruction awoke at a beginner's grasp. Negativity awoke as the only God who represented that side. Blood he already dominated. BEGIN was the one whose proficiency was nothing.[1]
Positivity and Negativity sit at the top defensively, because without them nothing would exist. They are [[Primordial Gods]] in a semi-conscious state, and they can still decide things that affect the cosmos. Before more energy and density were put into the Concept, Positive and Negative Energy were not that high on the scale of importance. END and Destruction could surpass them if something like a World Tree was attacked. The matter changed when the density did. The order of importance stated beside that fact starts with [[Primordial Chaos]], then Negativity and Positivity, then Infinity, Death, The Universal Tree, the Judges of the Abyss, and The Owner of Limbo.[1]
A later count, after the long span inside the Inner World, still has Beginnings as a major Divinity, and only Intermediate. Negativity was still a beginner. Years of that subjective time moved Beginnings about twenty percent, and Negativity about three. Fifteen Divinities were mastered. Beginnings was not one of them. The Divinity of Beginnings takes in the categories tied to the making of life, and to life itself: Fertility, Life, Love, Reproduction, Sex, and what belongs with them. That phrase is the life-making scope of BEGIN. It is not the Concept of Creation, and it is not a claim that every one of those names was awakened as its own Divinity.[5]
Weaker Concepts do not get to go against the ones at the top. Begin, End, Positivity, and Negativity are those. Dream and Madness can turn a dream toward reality. They still cannot use that dream to interfere with the laws of the cosmos, and this priority is one of those laws.[6]

### The Concept of Creation
The Concept of Creation is the bringing-forth. BEGIN is above it. A Dragon can control that Concept. A Dragon does not create from scratch unless many conditions are met. BEGIN is not a shortcut past that sentence. When a world of living things is spoken into being, the Concept of Creation is the material. Begin is the boost.[9][10]
Joined to the Progenitor of Vampires and of Dragons, and to Negativity's access to Souls, the God of BEGIN can make life the way a God of Creation makes it. The sentence uses a God of Creation as the measure. BEGIN does not become that Concept by being above it.[9]
Understanding the wider cosmos, its duality and its balance, raises proficiency in Begin and in Negativity. That gain is comprehension of the universe. It is not mastery of the Concept of Creation, and it did not finish BEGIN. Beginnings was still Intermediate long after.[11][5]
Destruction is second only to END. The Concept of Creation can contradict Destruction. So can a being who holds BEGIN, or END. So can an absurd amount of Positive and Negative Energy, because those two cover every existing Concept, BEGIN and END included. That quantity belongs to [[World Trees]]. Against Kali, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] added Begin and Positivity to the list of counters, and then did not call any of them. The body held.[12][13]

### The Holder
[[Victor Elderblood]] is the God of BEGIN. He is the beginning of everything that title is pointing at. Because of the Concept of Beginning, the Concepts he already held entered the realm of Life as well. He was God of Blood, Murder, and all Wars, and also of Family and of Nature. He would protect Life and Nature, and he could kill them if he had to. The God of Beginnings, with Martial Honor and Nature, pulled him to look at a killing with more neutral eyes. In the literal sense, the mix made him chaotic.[9][14][15]
The stronger a being is, the harder it is for them to have children. That rule does not apply to him. As a Progenitor and a God of Beginnings, he can impregnate whomever he wants, whenever he wants, and with as many children as he wants. He does not. He leaves it to nature.[16]
A daughter of a Dragon and a demon inherits both, because the Dragon blood takes what it meets and makes a power of its own. Souls are part of that. The Beginning aspect is what solved the rest of the problem. A Dragon God of his line does not have a child who is not a True Dragon. The genes do not allow the inferior result.[17]
Of the people standing at Polaris, only [[Hela Elderblood]] and Victor had resistance to END. His is because he holds BEGIN, the opposite Divinity, and because he was one step from living Negativity.[18]
In theory, END energy is supported only by someone who has BEGIN. A newborn END being leaks a little of it anyway, and that leak can make someone resistant without the Divinity. That is Hela. It is not the Concept.[19]
Being chosen by Negativity does not strip this side. Creation and Beginning are the examples still in him. Positivity and Negativity are not enemies. Together they are the balance. He grew into both.[20]

### He Who Creates Monsters
The leviathans were not an old sea rewritten. The Concept of Creation was the resource. Begin was the extra boost. What he spoke was a birth.
From the water, they were born, and with water, they brought both life and destruction.
They are immense, they dwell in the depths, they have the pride of a Dragon, and they are not Dragons. They were born from the essence of the Progenitor of the [[Blood Dragons]], and they carry his will. The words after that were Let There Be Life.[10]
The Primordial of Life accepted. The Primordial of Soul accepted, and did not want to. Life and the Judges of the Abyss both refused those Souls a place in reincarnation. A creature with no conscience of its own, bound to its creator, dies with the creator.[10]
The binding failed once. The Concept required was not there, so one was made. He who creates monsters was created, and bound to the Greater Concept BEGIN as a sub-concept. Anyone other than [[Chaos]] who wants that Divinity has to meet four conditions.[10]
Requirements, other than Chaos
1. The Greater Concept of BEGIN.
2. The Concept of Creation.
3. Dragon Progenitor of that sector.
4. Enough energy to carry the reincarnation cycle and the lives of the creatures.
The Universal Tree's conclusion was that no one but [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] meets that list. Carrying the weight of hundreds of creatures is Primordial work. In energy, he was approaching one. BEGIN is the first line. It is not the whole list. The Concept of Creation is the second line, and it is not included inside BEGIN.[10]

### Uses
- Developmental guidance. After [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]] forced a tenth tail before the Soul could carry it, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] used the authority of Beginnings on the damage. Golden energy surrounded her. The wall in the way of her progress cracked. Beginnings can guide a being toward a compatible path. It does not hand them a finished Divinity, and it does not make them the holder.[21]
- A heavier reality. A plain cube of dimension borrows the rules of this reality and stays light. A galaxy anchored on Negativity and Beginning has more levels of existence, more Divine Concepts in it, and a different weight. Norabell could take the cube, because he allowed it. She could not take the galaxy. The only ones he named who could, without his letting go, were [[Azathoth Elderblood]] and Jeanne. The stars inside it were Eldritch horrors, and the lives in it were replicas. It was not a finished universe. A stable universe still wants a [[Primordial Chaos]], and he did not have that power yet. Beginning was an anchor, not that missing whole.[22]
- Hybrid inheritance. The Beginning aspect, with the Soul and the predatory Dragon blood, is why a child of two races keeps both and still comes out a True Dragon.[17]
- The answer to END. A beam of END erases without remainder. The region it crosses is gone. The Upper Sectors know the ward. Only BEGIN answers it. [[Vharzath]] had already lost the Throne who held a stained copy. The Emperor had no subordinate who could.[4]

### Other Beginnings

#### Lilith
[[Lilith Elderblood]] is the goddess of demons, and specifically the Concept of Beginning. Hers was formed because she is a Progenitor. She has complete authority over demons. She can force that authority on a demon whose will is weaker. She can create demons if she wants to.[23]
That is the Beginning of a race. It is not the statement that she holds the Greater Concept second only to Positivity.

#### Vaelthar
[[Vaelthar]] holds BEGIN. It is stained by [[Vharzath]]'s Darkness. War felt a divinity that matches the Emperor's and is corrupted. It is kin to that divinity. It is not a clean copy, and Vaelthar does not spend it as a blow. He was built as a candidate Chosen of Negativity, begun in the light and ruined by Darkness. He was even given a portion of Vharzath's power. Negativity did not choose him. When END was fired from a ship, that Throne was already gone, and nothing on that side could ward the shot.[24][25][4]
A self-contained universe still wants a starting point. That is what the BEGIN Throne was for, on Velnorah's reading of the offices Darkness had tried to assemble. The other Thrones covered time, space, reality, existence, the end, and a false chaos. Life, death, and infinity were the gaps she and Jeanne named. Negativity and Positivity would still be required to change the laws underneath. A stained Beginning is a starting point. It is not those two halves.[20]

#### Not Ariella
[[Ariella Victoria Elderblood]] was born with the Concept of Creation, and with the potential to also possess Life. The second presence in the womb was that Concept preparing its host. She is not the holder of BEGIN. Life under BEGIN and Life under the Universal Tree are not the same office. The seven Heavenly Virtues she was born with are not BEGIN either.[26][27][2]

### Powers and Abilities
- Beginning. The start of a thing, a race, or a lineage. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s own status as Progenitor is what the Divinity was born from. He is the beginning of everything that awakening was naming.[1]
- Perpetuation of existence. Existence going on is one of the smaller Concepts the umbrella covers. Life is the other one named with it.[1]
- Life-category umbrella. Fertility, Life, Love, Reproduction, Sex, and the related categories sit inside Beginnings. They are not each a separate major. The Concept of Creation is not one of them.[5]
- Life creation. As God of BEGIN, joined to both Progenitor lines and to Negativity's access to Souls, he can create life to serve him, in the way a God of Creation creates.[9]
- Creation boost. When a world is spoken into being, the Concept of Creation is the material and Begin is the boost. The Dragon limitation remains.[9][10]
- Monster creation. He who creates monsters is a sub-concept bound to BEGIN. The leviathans are the beings it was made for. Pride of a Dragon. Not Dragons. Bound to their creator. No reincarnation.[10]
- Race origination. A Progenitor's Beginning is authority over that race. Lilith's is demons: command, compulsion of the weak-willed, and the making of more demons.[23]
- Reproduction override. The rule that a stronger existence has a harder time having children does not bind a Progenitor who is also a God of Beginnings. He can, and he usually does not.[16]
- Inheritance. The Beginning aspect lets a child of two races keep both lineages and still be a True Dragon.[17]
- Guidance. Beginnings can crack the wall in front of a compatible path. It cannot grant the finished Divinity, and the person guided does not become the holder.[21]
- Conceptual anchoring. Beginning is one of the anchors that gives a handmade galaxy more existential weight than a pocket dimension. Negativity and the Eldritch side were the other pieces he named. The result was still a realistic fake, not a universe.[22]
- END opposition. BEGIN is the only ward against END. Resistance to the Energy, in theory, belongs to the one who holds it.[3][19][18][4]
- Destruction opposition. A bearer of BEGIN can contradict Destruction. END can. The Concept of Creation can. A great mass of Positive and Negative Energy can.[12]
- Top-Concept priority. A weaker Concept does not overturn Begin, End, Positivity, or Negativity.[6]

### What BEGIN Is Not
- Not the Concept of Creation. That Concept is beneath it. Creation is the material of a made thing. BEGIN is the start and the boost. The monster sub-concept asks for both.[2][10]
- Not the universe. The cosmos, "all of creation," and the fuel Jeanne was counting are not this Divinity. Comprehending that cosmos raises Begin's proficiency. It does not replace the Concept.[11][7]
- Not Positivity. It loses to Positivity on the scale of importance. Positivity is the half. BEGIN is the Concept under that half, given by that half's subconscious.[1][2]
- Not the Universal Tree's Life. Life as a smaller Concept under BEGIN is not the office that runs life, [[World Trees]], and reincarnation.[2]
- Not END, and not a moral opposite of erasure. It is the rival. It is the only ward.[3][4]
- Not mastery on the day it awoke, and not a mastered major later. Proficiency was nil. After the Inner World it was Intermediate.[1][5]
- Not Lilith's authority, and not [[Vaelthar]]'s stained copy. Both use the word. Neither is the clean Greater Concept. The stained copy did not answer the beam.[23][4]
- Not a permission to create from nothing whenever a Dragon feels like it. The conditions are still there. The monster sub-concept has four of them, and the Tree did not think anyone else could meet them.[9][10]
- Not Ariella's birthright. She was born with the Concept of Creation, and with the potential for Life. She was not born holding BEGIN.[27]

### Limitations
- Understanding does not arrive with the Divinity. The proficiency was nothing, and years of subjective time moved it about twenty percent, to Intermediate.[1][5]
- Creation from scratch still needs conditions. A Dragon controls the Concept of Creation. That is not the same as making a thing from nothing. BEGIN boosts. It does not erase the limit.[9]
- The monster sub-concept will not bind to a person who lacks BEGIN, the Concept of Creation, that sector's Dragon Progenitor status, and the energy to carry the lives.[10]
- [[The Universal Tree]]'s Life office is not included in the gift.[2]
- Guidance is not a transfer. Haruna did not receive the Concept.[21]
- An anchor is not a universe. The galaxy held on Beginning still had no real life in it, because he did not yet have the power of a [[Primordial Chaos]].[22]
- A stained copy does not do the work of the clean Concept. [[Vaelthar]]'s did not answer the beam.[4]
- Holding it is not the same as being Positivity's Chosen. That seat is Light's. BEGIN is the Concept under the half, and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] holds the Concept.[1][28]
