[[Martial Arts]] are the collective combat disciplines, fighting systems and methods of physical technique practiced throughout [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]]. They encompass unarmed combat, weapon arts, movement systems, specialized racial fighting methods, personalized combat styles and advanced techniques capable of incorporating supernatural powers, Energy, Souls, Reality Distortion and even Concepts.
Martial Arts are not a single supernatural power. At their most fundamental level they concern the efficient use of the practitioner's body: stance, breathing, footwork, positioning, timing, balance, force distribution, weapon handling, defense, movement and control. As a practitioner develops, those fundamentals can be adapted to their individual physiology, preferred weapon, supernatural abilities and state of existence. This becomes particularly important in a setting containing Humans, Vampires, [[Werewolves]], Gods, Demons and Dragons, whose bodies and natural capabilities differ radically from one another.[1][2][3][4]
Martial Art Mastery is the proficiency system used to classify how deeply a practitioner understands a particular martial discipline. Swordsmanship, Spearmanship, Greatswordsmanship, Katana Arts, Close Combat, [[Scathach’s Foundational Martial Art]] and the [[Seven Arts of the Blood Dragon Primogenitor]] are martial disciplines or systems, while ranks such as Expert, Master and Grandmaster represent the practitioner's level of development within them.[5][6]
At the upper reaches of Martial Arts, technical skill undergoes a qualitative transformation. A Grandmaster must experience a personal form of Enlightenment that fundamentally changes how they understand their chosen discipline. Sufficiently developed Grandmaster Martial Arts can apply conceptual properties through weapons and techniques and can eventually become capable of directly severing Concepts.[7][8] Grandmastery itself is not the absolute endpoint. Later developments establish continued progression, existential prerequisites and techniques explicitly placed above previously established Grandmaster Martial Arts.[9][10][11][12]

### Nature of Martial Arts
[[Martial Arts]] fundamentally concern the efficient expression of whatever capabilities the practitioner already possesses. Extraordinary physical strength, speed or supernatural power can make an individual dangerous, but none automatically provide equivalent technical skill. Proper Martial Arts teach the fighter how to direct those advantages with the least unnecessary movement, correct timing and greatest practical efficiency.
This distinction was central to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s original training. When [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] began seriously teaching him, Victor possessed exceptional Vampiric physical abilities but almost no true combat foundation. [[Scathach]] deliberately prevented him from relying entirely upon instinct and raw power, repeatedly correcting inefficient movement and forcing him to establish the fundamentals upon which his later development would depend.[1]
As the story progresses, Martial Arts become increasingly broader than conventional Human fighting systems. Practitioners adapt techniques around supernatural anatomy, transformations, Bloodlines and unique abilities; Grandmasters can apply Concepts through Martial Arts; and Victor later develops systems designed specifically around Dragon physiology.[7][4][13][14]

The seven levels of the Seven Arts of the Blood Dragon Primogenitor, for example, represent development through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s personal martial system and should not be equated directly with Expert, Master or Grandmaster.

### Fundamental Principles

#### Foundation
A strong foundation determines how much later knowledge can be added without making a practitioner's fighting style unstable or inefficient. [[Scathach]] compared a proper foundation to the supporting structure of a building: the stronger its base, the more martial knowledge can safely be built upon it.[15]
This became central to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s development. He accumulated knowledge from numerous fighting systems but was able to integrate them because Scathach had first created a broad and stable foundation within him. When Victor eventually encountered the barrier preventing him from reaching Grandmaster, the answer was once again found in his fundamentals rather than greater complexity. He returned to the first basic Greatsword stance Scathach had taught him and realized that he could not continue advancing while the supporting pillars of his [[Martial Arts]] remained imperfect.[16]

#### Control
Control allows a practitioner to use strength, speed and supernatural abilities without wasting them. This can involve the amount of force placed into a movement, how Energy is distributed, how powers are incorporated into movement and how the different parts of a fighting system interact.
[[Scathach]]'s foundational system culminates in a principle explicitly called Control, integrating Strength, Power, Speed and Defense into a unified whole rather than leaving them as isolated specialties.[17][18][19]

#### Efficiency
Superior [[Martial Arts]] are not defined merely by producing greater destruction. Efficiency means accomplishing the intended effect without unnecessary movement, wasted force or excessive Energy expenditure. Advanced practitioners shorten preparatory movements, improve timing, transfer force more precisely and synchronize their bodies with their supernatural capabilities.
At Grandmaster Level this becomes exceptionally pronounced. During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Enlightenment, he realized that tremendous force was not supposed to be the sole objective of a correct strike; it emerged naturally because the movement itself had been properly executed.[16]

#### Adaptation
[[Martial Arts]] are not universally rigid systems. A practitioner can study the useful principles behind a technique and reconstruct them around their own body.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] provides the clearest example. After collecting Martial Arts belonging to numerous weapons and even different races, he initially attempted to directly combine everything he knew. That approach failed. Rather than force incompatible systems together, Victor began translating their useful fundamentals through [[Scathach]]'s already mastered foundation.[2]
This produced an adaptive method through which Victor could change weapons and styles while retaining abnormal levels of efficiency. Scathach herself had needed centuries of martial development to acquire comparable breadth, demonstrating the abnormality of Victor's learning speed.[2]

#### Personalization
A technique ideal for one body is not necessarily ideal for another. Height, proportions, supernatural physiology, transformations, preferred weapons and racial capabilities all change how a Martial Art should ideally be performed.
[[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] provides an important example. Maya had already constructed a fighting style suited specifically to her own body and deliberately avoided simply adding [[Scathach]]'s complete style to it because incompatible instincts could lower her efficiency. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], meanwhile, openly described his own fighting method as Scathach's style modified for his own needs.[3][20]
Grandmastery represents an extreme development of this principle. During Victor's Enlightenment, his stances, breathing, footwork, positioning and posture were refined specifically around his present body rather than remaining perfect reproductions of movements originally taught by Scathach.[16]

### Scathach's Foundational Martial Art
Main article: [[Scathach’s Foundational Martial Art]]
[[Scathach]]'s Foundational Martial Art is one of the most important generalized martial systems in the series. Scathach created it from the enormous amount of combat knowledge she accumulated throughout her life, deliberately constructing a balanced foundation capable of supporting different weapons, fighting styles and Vampire Bloodlines.[3]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] began serious training in the system shortly after Scathach accepted him as her disciple. During this period the Greatsword became his first principal weapon.[1] After roughly six months of training, Victor had learned the fundamentals of all five major components of Scathach's system, including its fifth principle, Control.[17][18]

[[Scathach]] deliberately divided the first four principles among her daughters. She expected them to exchange what they had learned and eventually discover Control for themselves. Instead, the daughters became heavily specialized in their individual branches and compensated by fighting together as a coordinated team.[21][19]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later began correcting this problem by teaching Pepper the remaining branches. During that training he explained the mechanics of Speed, Defense, Power and Control and demonstrated how the individual principles could be combined with supernatural abilities such as Lightning.[19]

### Adaptive and Multi-Weapon Martial Arts
Main article: [[Victor Elderblood]]/Martial Arts
Victor's adaptive approach illustrates one of the most advanced uses of a foundational Martial Art.
During the extended period in which he could not return to [[Nightingale]], Victor acquired [[Martial Arts]] material from many different sources. He studied systems involving [[Spears]], axes, fists, western Swords, Katanas, whips and other weapons, as well as techniques developed around supernatural races different from Vampires.[2]
Victor had not instantly mastered every style. Chapter 277 indicates that he initially learned their fundamentals. His first attempt was to directly combine all of those Martial Arts with [[Scathach]]'s system, but incompatible movements and instincts prevented the fusion from functioning correctly.[2]
Victor solved the problem by reversing the process. Instead of forcing complete systems together, he used Scathach's Martial Art as the common foundation and translated useful principles from each new discipline through it. This allowed him to change weapons and combat methods while retaining an abnormal degree of efficiency.[2]
The development continued across several stages of the story. During his battle with Anderson, Victor repeatedly changed weapons and fighting approaches while testing the results of his extended training.[22][23][24] By Chapter 305, Scathach observed that fighting Victor increasingly resembled fighting several different Martial Artists simultaneously because numerous combat principles existed within him without interfering with one another.[15]
Scathach described the possible endpoint of Victor's path as something resembling a [[Ares|God of War]] capable of learning and wielding all forms of combat without suffering the normal incompatibilities between them. This was a prediction of Victor's potential rather than a statement that he had already perfectly mastered every Martial Art at the time.[15]
Victor himself remained aware of how much training such a path required. By Chapter 421 he acknowledged that fully developing every Martial Art he possessed would require centuries even with his abnormal talent and temporarily concentrated more heavily upon the Odachi, which had become one of his strongest weapon disciplines.[21]
His adaptive capability continued progressing after becoming a Dragon. During later combat he further refined the ability to change weapons without losing the flow and rhythm of his movements.[25]

### Unarmed and Mixed Combat
Unarmed [[Martial Arts]] use the practitioner's own body as the primary weapon. They can include striking, grappling, throws, footwork and transitions between multiple close-combat methods.
[[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] is one of the clearest dedicated examples. Although she possessed weapon experience, Maya preferred treating her body itself as the weapon. Her personal Martial Art was built around explosive movement and attacks capable of transmitting destructive force through an opponent rather than relying exclusively upon external weapons.[3]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually became an exceptional mixed close-combat fighter. During his confrontation with [[Adam William Lykos]], he was explicitly treated as a Close Combat Master and shifted between multiple combat approaches while heavily restricting his supernatural abilities. Adam and Maya observed Victor repeatedly changing stance and technique without being dragged back toward a single dominant martial instinct.[3][20]
Not every resemblance to a real-world Martial Art should automatically be treated as confirmation that Victor formally mastered a separate named school. Adam initially interpreted one of Victor's stances as Bajiquan, but Victor himself identified the style he was using as his own modification of [[Scathach]]'s Style.[3] Similar descriptions involving Boxing-like strikes, grappling or kicks should therefore be treated as combat applications unless the novel explicitly identifies them as separate disciplines.

### Weapon Martial Arts
Weapon [[Martial Arts]] remain discipline-specific. Extraordinary skill with one weapon does not automatically produce an identical rank with another.
Chapter 589 provides one of the clearest comparisons. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly described both his Greatsword and Odachi proficiency as Expert Level, while [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]] had already reached Master Level in Katana Arts.[5]

### Breadth and Specialization
[[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] and [[Rose Adrasteia Elderblood]] demonstrate two fundamentally different approaches to [[Martial Arts]].
[[Scathach]] deliberately pursued breadth. She learned numerous weapons and fighting methods because she believed a warrior should possess more than one answer in battle. Her enormous range of knowledge made her exceptionally adaptable, but she nevertheless achieved true Grandmastery specifically through the [[Spears|Spear]].[26][7][6]
Rose pursued depth. Rather than divide her attention among numerous unrelated disciplines, she dedicated herself overwhelmingly to the Sword. Her specialization eventually allowed her Swordsmanship to reach a realm Scathach herself had not achieved with a Sword despite Scathach possessing far broader martial knowledge.[26]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s path combines aspects of both philosophies. He accumulated an enormous variety of Martial Arts and learned to translate them through one common foundation, but his eventual Enlightenment still emerged through a specific discipline: the Greatsword.[16]

### Racial and Physiological Martial Arts
[[Martial Arts]] can be created specifically around the body and capabilities of a particular race.
This becomes increasingly important after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and members of his family become Dragons. A Dragon does not possess the same physical limitations, movement options or relationship with Reality as a Human or ordinary Vampire. Continuing to use a Martial Art constructed entirely around a previous body can therefore restrict development.

#### Dragon-Tailored Martial Art
In Chapter 819, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] gave [[Scathach]] the foundation of a Martial Art specifically designed for Dragons.[4]
Victor himself acknowledged that calling it a complete Martial Art was premature. What he had created was an unfinished collection of ideas, movements and principles that he expected both himself and Scathach to develop further. The system retained obvious influence from Scathach's teachings but modified its mechanics around Draconic existence.[4]
One major difference involved aerial movement. Victor used a Dragon's ability to distort Reality to make surrounding space behave as though it provided solid footing, allowing terrestrial concepts of stance and footwork to be reconstructed for three-dimensional aerial combat.[4]
The creation of this system reinforced a recurring principle throughout Victor's development: when the practitioner's body fundamentally changes, their [[Martial Arts]] must be reevaluated rather than preserved unchanged.

#### State of Existence
The relationship between body and Martial Art becomes even more important among advanced Grandmasters.
After Rose evolved from a Noble Vampire into a Dragon, her Swordsmanship began progressing rapidly even without the same amount of conventional training. The narration explains that her former state of existence had apparently been restricting her development. [[Scathach]] experienced the same phenomenon.[9]
The situation is described as though an invisible prerequisite existed: sufficiently advanced Grandmasters could only continue progressing once they themselves reached a higher state of existence.[9]
Chapter 880 develops the same idea from Rose's perspective. Her Dragon body had finally become capable of supporting the extreme path she had chosen, in which the distinction between herself and her Sword became increasingly irrelevant; her body and Soul themselves could function as the weapon. Rose and Scathach were consequently approaching a new martial realm beyond the limitations which previously restrained them.[10]

### Seven Arts of the Blood Dragon Primogenitor
Main article: [[Seven Arts of the Blood Dragon Primogenitor]]
The Seven Arts of the Blood Dragon Primogenitor are [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s most advanced original Martial Art system and one of the clearest examples of a racial and supernatural Martial Art.
Victor created the Seven Arts from the accumulated knowledge of his entire martial path. By this period he possessed enormous experience from his own training, numerous absorbed beings and approximately two thousand years of personal development within his accelerated training environment. Haruna explicitly recognized him as a master of various blade [[Martial Arts]] during the creation of the system.[13]
The Seven Arts were divided into seven progressive levels. Their earlier stages focus upon transforming the practitioner's body into a complete weapon and combining numerous Martial Arts into an exceptionally advanced close-combat framework. Later levels increasingly incorporate Victor's supernatural abilities and the nature of a Blood Dragon rather than remaining purely physical techniques.[13][14]
The complete system demands exceptional control over multiple aspects of the practitioner, including physical Martial Arts, Draconic physiology, Energy, gravity, Reality Distortion, supernatural movement and eventually the Soul.[13][14]
Victor's development of the Seven Arts also revealed a teaching problem: even an extremely powerful Martial Art can become impractical if it lacks an accessible beginning. [[Scathach]] observed that a practitioner would probably require a previous Martial Art—particularly a foundation similar to her own—before properly understanding Victor's system. Victor therefore recognized the need for a preliminary Level 0 capable of teaching Dragons how to move their bodies and properly interact with Reality before entering the Seven Arts themselves.[14]
The Seven Arts are especially important because Victor created them before completing his own Greatsword Grandmaster Enlightenment. This demonstrates that the sophistication of an original Martial Art is not automatically identical to the personal mastery rank of its creator.

### Martial Art Mastery
Martial Art Mastery measures the practitioner's level of understanding within a specific discipline.
Chapter 749 explicitly states that [[Martial Arts]] possess ranks extending from Apprentice to Grandmaster. It further explains that, in the greater scheme, the most important higher ranks are Master and Grandmaster, because only a relatively small number of practitioners ever reach Master and an even smaller number attain Grandmaster.[6]
The chapter does not comprehensively enumerate every lower rank between Apprentice and Grandmaster. Chapter 589 independently confirms Expert Level as a formal stage below Master.[5]
Apprentice → Lower / Developing Ranks → Expert → Master → Grandmaster → Higher Realm

#### Apprentice and Lower Ranks
Apprentice is explicitly identified as the lower end of the Martial Art ranking system in Chapter 749.[6]
The story spends comparatively little time defining lower Martial Art ranks because many major combatants are already exceptionally experienced. The lower stages should therefore be treated conservatively as progressive levels of martial development rather than being assigned abilities or universal characteristics that the novel has not directly established.

#### Expert
Expert Level is an advanced rank below Master.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Greatsword and Odachi proficiency were explicitly Expert Level in Chapter 589, while Haruna's Katana Arts were Master Level.[5]

#### Master
Master Level represents an exceptionally advanced degree of conventional martial proficiency.
A person can become Master-level in several separate disciplines. [[Scathach]] is the clearest example: Chapter 749 states that she had reached Master Level in several [[Martial Arts]] while attaining Grandmastery only with the [[Spears|Spear]].[6]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later reached Master-level ability in multiple areas. His confrontation with Adam establishes him as a Close Combat Master, while by the creation of the Seven Arts he was recognized as a master of various blade Martial Arts.[3][13]

#### Grandmaster
Grandmaster is the highest Martial Art rank whose basic advancement mechanics have been extensively explained.
Only an extremely small number of beings had reached the stage by Chapter 749. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] considered the number of beings who had entered Grandmaster Rank small enough to be counted on both hands, illustrating the rarity of the achievement even among Gods and ancient supernatural warriors.[6]
The defining difference is that Grandmastery cannot reliably be reached merely through continued conventional training. Advancement requires personal Enlightenment.[7][6]

### Master and Grandmaster
Master
- Exceptional technical skill and experience.
- Can be reached through extreme training and refinement.
- A practitioner may become Master in multiple disciplines.
- Technique remains primarily learned and deliberately refined.
- Continued conventional training can still produce improvement.
Grandmaster
- Requires personal Enlightenment.
- Represents a qualitative transformation in understanding.
- Previously invisible flaws become perceptible.
- Execution becomes increasingly natural and personalized.
- [[Martial Arts]] can begin developing Conceptual properties.[7]
The difference between the two stages was described by [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] as comparable to heaven and earth.[7]
Vlad himself demonstrates that age and experience do not guarantee Grandmastery. Despite his immense lifespan, combat experience and Martial Arts training, he admitted that he had never reached Grandmaster because he had never experienced the necessary Enlightenment.[7]

### Enlightenment
Enlightenment is the defining breakthrough separating Master from Grandmaster.[7][6]
It is not a standardized ritual, fixed amount of training or universal sensation. Enlightenment is personal to the practitioner and their martial path.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] previously asked [[Scathach]] what Enlightenment felt like. Her answer was deliberately vague: he would understand once he experienced it himself. Chapter 749 explains why. If Scathach described her own Enlightenment in detail, Victor might begin unconsciously searching for the same realization despite following a fundamentally different martial path, potentially obstructing his own development.[6]
Consequently:
- Enlightenment cannot simply be taught by another Grandmaster.
- It cannot reliably be copied through imitation.
- Enormous amounts of experience do not guarantee it.
- Each practitioner's realization can differ fundamentally.
- Attempting to reproduce another person's Enlightenment may hinder one's own progression.[6]

#### Victor's Enlightenment
Main article: [[Victor Elderblood]]/Martial Arts
Victor's breakthrough provides the most detailed known demonstration.
By Chapter 1039, Victor had accumulated immense Martial Art knowledge, mastered numerous disciplines and already created the Seven Arts. Despite this, he encountered a barrier preventing further progress. The answer was not another new technique or an even more complicated system.[16]
Victor created a Greatsword and returned to the first basic stance [[Scathach]] originally taught him. He realized that continuing to build upward while the foundation supporting everything remained imperfect was fundamentally flawed.[16]
His perception of his existing [[Martial Arts]] changed. His stance, breathing, footwork, positioning, posture, balance and basic movements all began undergoing refinement.[16]
The narration distinguishes this process from conventional learning. Victor was not acquiring an entirely new Martial Art; he had become capable of recognizing imperfections he previously could not perceive and was refining what he already possessed.[16]
The process transformed Victor into a Grandmaster of the Greatsword. Rose subsequently described the achievement as his first steps into the Grandmaster Realm, demonstrating that the breakthrough was the beginning of his Grandmaster development rather than its final endpoint.[16]

#### Natural Execution
One of the defining characteristics of Grandmastery is how natural the Martial Art becomes.
Chapter 1039 compares the state to instinctively understanding how to move one's own body. The practitioner no longer needs to struggle with every fundamental component of an ordinary movement because stance, balance, breathing and positioning have become deeply integrated.[16]
This does not mean Grandmasters fight without thought. Strategy, specialized techniques, supernatural powers and responses to opponents remain deliberate. Rather, the underlying physical mechanics no longer contain the same degree of unnecessary effort.

#### Continuous Improvement
Grandmastery possesses substantial internal progression.
Rose referred to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s breakthrough as his first steps into the Realm, while [[Scathach]] immediately encouraged him to continue until he reached its summit.[16] Chapter 874 later demonstrates that sufficiently advanced Grandmasters can encounter an entirely different limitation: their state of existence itself can become insufficient to support further development.[9]
A newly Enlightened Grandmaster should therefore not automatically be treated as technically equivalent to a Grandmaster who has continued refining their discipline for a much greater period.

### Conceptual Martial Arts
Grandmaster [[Martial Arts]] can eventually transcend ordinary physical interaction and begin applying Conceptual properties.

#### Applying a Concept
Chapter 681 explains that Grandmaster-level [[Martial Arts]] can apply a Concept through the practitioner's weapon.[7]
Rose's Swordsmanship is used as one of the clearest examples. Her danger does not come merely from the physical sharpness of her Sword or her supernatural strength. Her Martial Art itself has reached a level where a Conceptual quality is expressed through the blade, making the technique dangerous even to Gods.[7]
This should not be interpreted as every Grandmaster possessing an identical Concept. The final expression remains connected to the individual practitioner, discipline and level of development.

#### Severing Concepts
Chapter 801 demonstrates a more advanced application.
When [[Scathach]] attacked [[Nocturnus]], she entered a [[Martial Arts]] stance and released an apparently simple strike. The narration explicitly identifies the technique as Grandmaster-level Martial Arts where the Martial Arts themselves could sever a Concept.[8]
The resulting path of destruction erased everything caught along the line of the attack, but the exact effect and scale should not automatically be treated as a universal passive ability possessed identically by every Grandmaster. It was Scathach's Grandmaster Martial Art expressed through Scathach's own extraordinary power.[8]
Physical Technique → Advanced Refinement → Grandmaster Enlightenment → Conceptual Application → Concept Severing

### Martial Skill and Raw Power
Martial Art Mastery and general combat power are related but not identical.
A practitioner can possess superior technique while having a weaker body, or possess overwhelming physical strength while remaining technically inferior. The final result of a Martial Art depends upon both the quality of the technique and the capabilities of the person performing it.

#### Victor's First Grandmaster Slash
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s first Greatsword attack after completing his Enlightenment illustrates this distinction.
Victor performed a technically simple Greatsword slash using his newly refined Martial Art. The attack generated an enormous rift across his Inner Cosmos and affected the surrounding dimensional structure.[16]
Victor recognized that the result exceeded what his previously created Martial Art would ordinarily accomplish without deliberately adding mechanisms such as additional Energy or Reality Distortion. However, the narration also clarifies that the effectiveness of [[Martial Arts]] depends heavily upon the user. Victor's extraordinary body and overall level were major reasons the refined slash produced such an extreme result.[16]
Grandmaster Greatswordsmanship + Victor's Existing Power = Victor's Result
The feat should not be interpreted as a universal destructive benchmark automatically granted to every Grandmaster.

### Perception and Advanced Execution
Extremely developed [[Martial Arts]] can become difficult for inferior observers to perceive.
Chapter 874 provides one of the clearest examples. After becoming a Dragon, Rose performed a Sword attack so efficiently that [[Loki]] and most of the Gods present failed to properly perceive it. [[Thor]], [[Taranis]], Susanoo and [[Azrael]] sensed only a glimpse of what occurred, while the physical world visibly reacted only after Rose had already finished the movement and returned her Sword to its sheath.[9]
Only [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Velnorah and [[Scathach]] clearly followed the action, with Scathach's own advanced martial understanding contributing to her ability to perceive it.[9]
This should not be treated as an automatic "perception negation" ability belonging to every Grandmaster. It was a demonstration of Rose's highly advanced Swordsmanship combined with her Dragon state and personal abilities.

#### Grandmaster's Existential Barrier
Chapter 874 establishes that [[Scathach]] and Rose reached a point where further development was restricted by their previous states of existence. After both became Dragons, their [[Martial Arts]] began advancing again, as though an invisible prerequisite required a sufficiently advanced Grandmaster to possess a higher form of existence before progressing further.[9]
This establishes that high-level Grandmaster development eventually encounters requirements beyond ordinary technical training.

#### A New Martial Realm
Chapter 880 expands upon Rose's development. Her Dragon body had become sufficiently powerful for the path she had chosen, with Rose increasingly treating her own body and Soul as the Sword rather than depending entirely upon an external weapon. The narration presents Rose and [[Scathach]] as approaching a further martial realm beyond the limits they previously encountered.[10]
The universal terminology and complete advancement requirements of this realm remain incompletely explained, but the chapter reinforces that Grandmaster is not the final possible state of [[Martial Arts]].

#### Above the Grandmaster
The idea receives its clearest later terminology in Chapters 1107–1108, both titled Above the Grandmaster.[11][12]
While experimenting with Attributeless Energy, [[Scathach]] recognized the method as a development beyond what they had previously been using. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] initially considered terminology comparable to Grandmaster-tier Energy Control, but Scathach instead proposed Higher-Level Technique, explicitly placing the method one level above their existing Grandmaster [[Martial Arts]] and Victor's previous Martial Art techniques.[12]
Established

- A higher state of existence can permit renewed progress.[9]

- Higher-Level Techniques exist above their previous Grandmaster Martial Arts.[12]
Still Unknown
- The complete number of post-Grandmaster stages.
- Whether all Grandmasters encounter identical requirements.
- Whether another form of Enlightenment is necessary.
- The official universal names of every higher subdivision.
- Whether Victor's Greatswordsmanship had itself entered a formally named higher rank by Chapter 1108.
For encyclopedia purposes, Above Grandmaster is therefore retained as the broad next level of Martial Art development, while its internal structure remains incomplete.

### Creator's Energy Mastery and Higher-Level Techniques
Main article: [[Creator's Energy Mastery]]
The Higher-Level Technique introduced in Chapters 1107–1108 should be connected to Above-Grandmaster development without being treated as identical to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Greatsword rank.
The technique concerns the manipulation of Attributeless Energy. Victor and [[Scathach]] discussed classifying its degree of control using terminology similar to [[Martial Arts]], but Scathach ultimately placed the technique one level above their Grandmaster Martial Arts.[12]
The Energy method also possesses its own internal proficiency progression beginning with a practitioner who can barely manipulate the Energy and advancing toward much greater control.[12]
Therefore:
Above-Grandmaster Martial Development — the broader higher realm existing beyond ordinary Grandmaster limits.
Higher-Level Attributeless Energy Technique — a specific Energy-control method explicitly classified above their Grandmaster Martial Arts.
The latter provides strong evidence for combat techniques existing beyond Grandmaster-class Martial Arts, but does not by itself prove that Victor's Greatswordsmanship was formally assigned an entirely new rank.

### Grandmaster Martial Arts vs. Supernatural Abilities
[[Martial Arts]] should remain distinct from the supernatural abilities possessed by their practitioners.
[[Scathach]], Rose and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] can fight using their physical bodies and Martial Art skill while deliberately withholding much of their supernatural arsenals. During Victor's later training, missed physical attacks from Grandmaster-level combatants could already create extreme environmental effects because of the bodies and Martial Arts involved even before every available supernatural ability was used.[27]
Martial Art Mastery
How efficiently, precisely and naturally the practitioner fights.
Supernatural Ability
The additional powers, elements, Concepts or abilities available to the practitioner.
Physical / Existential Power
The amount of force, durability and scale the practitioner's body and existence can support.
The three frequently interact in serious combat, but they should not be treated as interchangeable.

### Martial Arts in the Elderblood Empire
[[Martial Arts]] remained strategically important even after the [[Elderblood Empire]] developed Divine technology, interstellar civilization and extremely advanced supernatural weaponry.
[[Scathach]] continued functioning as one of the Empire's greatest combat instructors, while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s descendants and elite soldiers underwent extensive physical and martial training from early in their development. The continued importance of personal Martial Arts reflects the Empire's philosophy that powerful Bloodlines, Divine abilities and technology cannot replace the ability to properly control one's body and power.
By the later Imperial era, Scathach developed an entire planet primarily as a military training environment. The world contained specialized training grounds, fortifications, arenas and infrastructure capable of supporting approximately one hundred million recruits.[28]
This represents the large-scale evolution of the same principle Scathach originally applied to individual disciples: exceptional supernatural power without discipline and technique is incomplete.

### Terminology

#### Apprentice and the Lower Ranks
Chapter 749 directly states that Martial Art ranks range from Apprentice to Grandmaster.[6] Earlier versions of the wiki used labels such as Novice and Intermediate for portions of the lower progression, but the complete lower-rank sequence has not been comprehensively enumerated in the audited Martial Art explanations. For accuracy, Apprentice should be treated as the confirmed lower end of the hierarchy, while Expert, Master and Grandmaster are independently demonstrated formal ranks.

#### Master as a Rank and Description
The word Master can occasionally be used descriptively for a highly accomplished fighter. When discussing the formal hierarchy, Chapters 681 and 749 provide the clearest distinction between Master and Grandmaster.[7][6]

#### Grandmaster and Grand Master
Translations have used both Grandmaster and Grand Master for the same martial stage. For consistency, this wiki uses:
Grandmaster

#### Above Grandmaster
Above Grandmaster is retained as the general term for the higher martial realm beyond conventional Grandmaster development. Chapters 874 and 880 establish progression beyond the limitations faced by advanced Grandmasters, while Chapters 1107–1108 explicitly use Above the Grandmaster as their titles and introduce a Higher-Level Technique classified above the characters' existing Grandmaster [[Martial Arts]].[9][10][11][12]
The exact subdivisions and complete advancement mechanics of this realm remain unrevealed.

#### Martial Rank vs. Power Level
Martial Art Mastery should not be used as a substitute for the general [[Power Hierarchy]].
A practitioner may simultaneously be:
- physically stronger but technically inferior;
- physically weaker but technically superior;
- Expert in one weapon and Master in another;
- Master in several disciplines but Grandmaster in only one;
- a newly Enlightened Grandmaster or a vastly more experienced Grandmaster;
- or capable of a Higher-Level Technique without their traditional weapon discipline being explicitly assigned a new formal rank.
