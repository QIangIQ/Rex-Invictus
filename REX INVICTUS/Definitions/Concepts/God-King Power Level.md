**[[God-King Power Level]]** is the upper conventional Divine power range above High-Level Combat Gods and below the point where Primordial-class existence becomes the normal baseline. (Lore adapted from *My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** This material is adapted into the Rex Invictus world at the lowest priority: Rex Invictus wins any conflict, then the Flamel material, then MTWABV. Events of the MTWABV timeline (Victor's conquests, the fall of the pantheons, the [[Elderblood Empire]]) are distant background or rumor unless a chapter puts them on the page. ## MTWABV Canon

Upper Divine Power Archive
GOD-KING POWER LEVEL
The upper conventional Divine bracket between [[Combat Gods]] and [[Primordial Gods]]
God-King Power Level is the upper conventional Divine power range above High-Level Combat Gods and below the point where Primordial-class existence becomes the normal baseline. It measures a Being's developed overall Power—not simply how much [[Divine Energy]] they possess—and therefore incorporates physical capability, Soul quality, Divinity development, Energy control, combat ability, racial traits, equipment, Domains and the nature of the Concepts available to them.[1]
Important distinction: God-King Power Level is a measure of personal power. God-King is also used as the title of a Pantheon ruler. The two concepts overlap frequently but are not synonymous. A Pantheon ruler may be below, inside or above the conventional God-King combat bracket, while a Being may possess God-King-class power without ruling a Pantheon.[2]

### Overview
The God-King bracket represents the point where the difference between ordinary Divine development and the strongest rulers of Divine civilizations becomes extreme. A Being operating consistently at this level can no longer be evaluated solely through the conventional God or Combat God hierarchies.
The Author's original ranking places rulers such as [[Zeus]], [[Odin]], [[Indra]], [[Amaterasu]] and Ra within the broad God-King rank while simultaneously emphasizing that the individuals occupying that rank are not equal. Equipment, special abilities, Divine authority and the conditions of a battle can dramatically alter their effective performance.[1]
This becomes particularly clear with [[Victor Elderblood]]. Around Chapter 630, Victor already possessed a quantity of Energy roughly comparable to the God-King range, yet his ordinary practical fighting ability was still only that of a Low-Level Combat God.[1] By Chapter 690, after centuries of accelerated development in Hell and a massive increase in control, technique and experience, Victor was explicitly recognized as a God-King-level Being.[2]
GOD-KING CLASS IS NOT AN ENERGY NUMBER
A character can possess God-King quantities of Energy without possessing God-King combat ability. Conversely, specialized Concepts, artifacts, Domains or racial abilities can allow a technically weaker combatant to threaten a God-King.

### Evidence Status of the Subdivisions
The terminology surrounding [[God-Kings]] is less rigid than the lower God hierarchy. The novel clearly establishes God-King level as a power range, but it does not announce every progression between Low, Mid, High and Peak as though they were formal cultivation realms.

### What Determines God-King Power?
God-King classification reflects the complete combat-capable existence rather than a single statistic.
DIVINE ENERGY
Total reserves, quality and the amount that can be safely converted into usable Power.
SOUL
The quality, stability and development of the Soul supporting Divine existence.
DIVINITIES
Importance, conceptual scope, understanding and mastery of the user's Divine authorities.
CONTROL
How efficiently Energy and abilities are used rather than how much Power exists in theory.
COMBAT DEVELOPMENT
Martial skill, reactions, experience, tactics, weapons and practical fighting efficiency.
EXTERNAL AUTHORITY
Pantheon Domains, Ruler Authority, Divine Artifacts, Faith and external Energy sources.
[[Scathach]]'s explanation in Chapter 690 summarizes the principle: for sufficiently powerful Beings, Power is not merely Energy. It is the sum of technique, mentality, racial qualities, equipment and every other advantage the Being can meaningfully bring into battle.[2]

### Low God-King
Low God-King — Entry God-King Class
Low God-King represents the beginning of genuine God-King-class personal Power. A Being in this range has crossed beyond even the strongest conventional [[Combat Gods]] and developed enough overall strength to operate among the powerful rulers and elites of major Pantheons.
[[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]] provides the cleanest explicit progression example. After restoring her complete Godhood, becoming a High-Level Goddess, becoming the Werewolf Progenitor and then absorbing the Power accumulated by [[Volk Fenrir]], Tasha's strength increased to the level of a Low-Level God-King.[3]
The class should not be defined merely by the quantity of Energy stored inside the body. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] already possessed God-King quantities of Energy considerably before he could consistently fight at God-King level.[1]

#### Characteristics
- Entry range of God-King-class Power.
- Vastly exceeds the ordinary baseline of conventional Gods.
- Possesses enough overall development to stand above the standard High-Level Combat God hierarchy.
- Physical and Energy-based output should conservatively be treated as planetary-class or greater when the user is actually capable of expressing the class consistently.
- Conceptual abilities may operate far beyond the user's simple physical destruction scale.

### Mid God-King
Mid God-King — Established God-King Class
Mid God-King represents the broad middle of God-King development. Beings placed here have moved substantially beyond the minimum threshold but have not yet reached the upper extreme represented by High-Level and Peak God-King combatants.
Unlike Tasha's explicit Low-Level classification, the novel rarely pauses to announce that an individual has become a "Mid God-King". Development at this level is usually continuous. A God may increase the quality of their Soul, gain control over previously inefficient Energy reserves, deepen their principal Concepts, obtain superior artifacts or spend centuries improving their techniques without undergoing a named transformation.
[[Victor Elderblood]] demonstrates this continuous development especially well. Chapter 690 establishes him as a God-King-level Being, while later chapters show him continuing to develop until he could battle Primordial-class opponents. The precise instant at which he would be classified as Mid God-King is not separately announced.[2]

#### Classification Rule
Characters should only be placed in the Mid God-King range when their position is supported through progression or comparative scaling. The classification should not be assigned merely because the character is stronger than a known Low God-King.

### High God-King
High God-King — Upper God-King Class
High-Level [[God-Kings]] occupy the upper portion of conventional God-King development. The terminology is explicitly used in Chapter 1059, when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] reflects upon the recurring number seven throughout Creation and mentions seven High-Level God-Kings alongside the seven Hells, seven Celestial Heavens, Seven Deadly Sins, Seven Virtues and seven Primordial Beings.[4]
The statement confirms High-Level God-King as terminology within the setting, although it does not individually identify the exact current combat placement of every Pantheon ruler at that point in the story. Characters should therefore not automatically receive the class simply because they once occupied one of Earth's great thrones.

#### The Seven High-Level God-Kings
Earth historically possessed a recurring pattern of seven principal Divine rulers associated with its great supernatural order. The traditional great [[God-Kings]] included rulers such as [[Zeus]], [[Odin]], [[Indra]], [[Amaterasu]], the [[Heavenly Father]], [[Buddha]] and the [[Jade Emperor]], although several members of this group possessed origins or states that extended far beyond ordinary God-King physiology.
This is an important distinction. The expression High-Level God-King in Chapter 1059 establishes a powerful ruling category, but it does not erase the separate biological and metaphysical classifications of its members. For example, a ruler who is also a Primordial God remains a Primordial God even if they occupy a God-King throne.

### Peak God-King
Peak God-King — Primordial Combat Threshold
Peak God-King represents the highest conventional portion of the God-King power bracket: the point at which a sufficiently developed non-Primordial Being can begin competing directly with lower [[Primordial Gods]] in practical battle.
Peak God-King is fundamentally a combat-development threshold. It does not mean the individual has acquired Primordial physiology, become an embodiment of a fundamental Concept or changed their Divine generation.
A Peak God-King may bridge the remaining difference through overwhelming Energy, extraordinarily developed Divinities, artifacts, Soul abilities, racial advantages, Pantheon support, superior martial skill or a favorable conceptual interaction.

### Physical Destructive Power
One of the most important distinctions for this bracket is the difference between physical/energy-based destructive power and Conceptual potency.

#### Planetary Minimum
A conservative interpretation of God-King and higher combatants places their raw physical and Energy-based destructive capability at planetary levels at minimum once they can properly express the Power of the class.
This is supported by several independent events.
During the [[Greek Underworld]] conflict, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] fought Kronus, [[Erebus]], [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] and other upper Divine opponents while still operating before his later Blood Dragon Primogenitor evolution. Chapter 800 explicitly states that the destruction being produced across the battlefield had reached a level capable of destroying Earth several times over. The reason the Greek Underworld remained intact was its unusual durability together with reinforcement attributed to [[Gaia]].[5]
Earlier, Victor's final clash with the newly ascended [[Demon God]] War caused [[Biblical Hell]] to flash with their collision and shake across the entire dimension, with Beings even in isolated regions feeling the shockwave. Their battle also caused permanent damage to Hell, something Demons believed even [[Diablo]], Lilith and [[Lucifer]] had never accomplished through ordinary destruction.[6] Biblical Hell is an enormous supernatural dimension and was later described at a scale vastly larger than Earth, reinforcing how far the setting's upper physical combat had progressed even before Victor's later Divine evolution.
Scaling note: "Planetary at minimum" is a conservative physical and Energy-output baseline for the God-King bracket and above. It is not intended as the maximum scale of every attack, nor does it describe the maximum scope of their Concepts.
This distinction also prevents environmental durability from being mistaken for low attack power. Pantheons, Hells and Divine dimensions are frequently much more resilient than ordinary planets. A battle occurring inside one of these realms can therefore produce attacks that would devastate a normal world without destroying the battlefield immediately.[5]

### Conceptual Power
Conceptual Power is not restricted to the physical destruction scale of the user.
A God-King whose punches, weapons or raw Energy are conservatively planetary can possess a Divinity whose authority operates across a much greater metaphysical structure.
Concepts such as Time, Death, Soul, Space, Destruction, Creation, Love, Luck or similar authorities do not function simply as larger explosions. Their effectiveness is determined by what the Concept governs, how deeply the user understands it, whether the target possesses resistance and what jurisdiction applies to the authority.
The Author's later mechanics explanation emphasizes that the importance of a Concept, its development and interactions with opposing authorities become decisive at the upper levels of Divine combat.[7]
PHYSICAL / ENERGY SCALE
Measures direct force: punches, beams, explosions, shockwaves, weapon strikes and raw Energy discharge.
CONCEPTUAL SCALE
Measures authority over phenomena such as Souls, Time, Space, Death or Destruction. Its scope can greatly exceed direct physical output.
Therefore:
Planetary physical strength
does not limit
the range or potency of a God-King's Conceptual authority.
A character profile should record these separately whenever possible.

### God-King Title and Personal Power

Chapter 690 explicitly notes that God-Kings are not all equivalent. Some received the title because they led a Pantheon, while others possessed the raw and practical Power normally associated with the designation.[2]

#### Examples
- [[Zeus]] was the political God-King of [[Olympus]] and was also directly placed within the God-King power rank by the Author.[1]
- [[Odin]] was a God-King by rulership while possessing a Divine origin and existence that cannot be understood merely from the political title.
- [[Heavenly Father]] functioned as the supreme ruler of the [[Biblical Pantheon]] but belongs to an immensely higher primordial order.
- [[Persephone]] entered the realm of a God-King after becoming the Ruler of the [[Greek Underworld]] despite not becoming the ruler of Olympus.[11]
- Tasha achieved Low-Level God-King personal Power through her own racial and Divine evolution, independent of the conventional Pantheon throne system.[3]

### Ruler Authority and Domains
The power of a God-King cannot always be separated cleanly from the realm they control.
Chapter 641 explains that the Ruler of an [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] possesses authority comparable to that of a God-King within the territory they govern. The realm itself supports the Ruler's existence and greatly amplifies their Power.[11]
[[Persephone]] demonstrates this directly. Before the Ruler trial, she was merely a Low-Level Goddess despite possessing numerous Concepts because she lacked proficiency. After passing the trial, she entered the realm of a God-King while being supported by the entire Greek Underworld.[11]
This produced an enormous jump:

The same chapter states that [[Hades]] had been stronger than [[Zeus]] in the Greek Underworld. This should not automatically be interpreted as Hades possessing a permanently higher base classification; his position as Underworld ruler, accumulated treasures and territorial advantages were integral to that superiority.[11]
The same principle applies to other Divine rulers. A God-King fighting inside their own Pantheon may possess:
- additional Energy supplied by the realm;
- greater authority over local Rules;
- improved compatibility with their Divinities;
- environmental regeneration or protection;
- access to Pantheon artifacts;
- support from Faith and subordinate Gods;
- and direct control over parts of the realm itself.
Domain-amplified performance should therefore not automatically be used as the character's unaided base class.

### Divine Artifacts
Divine Artifacts are one of the largest sources of variation inside the God-King bracket.
[[Zeus]] is the Author's primary example. Zeus normally belonged to the God-King rank, but his greatest Divine weapon could raise his effective combat strength enough to compete within the same broad rank as his father Kronus, a Primordial God.[1]
Zeus without greatest weapon = God-King-class combatant.
Zeus with his greatest Divine weapon = capable of reaching Primordial-relevant combat territory.[1]
This does not mean that Zeus permanently becomes a Primordial God. It means his weapon allows his practical fighting capacity to rise beyond his normal baseline.
The same rule applies to:
- Anti-Divine weapons;
- Divine armor;
- Pantheon treasures;
- Ruler artifacts;
- external Energy sources;
- temporary blessings;
- contracts;
- and transformations.

### Primordial Overlap
Main article: [[Primordial Gods]]
The upper God-King range and lower Primordial combat range overlap heavily, but their underlying existences remain different.
A God-King normally reaches their position through development: accumulating and refining Power, mastering Divinities, strengthening the Soul, obtaining artifacts and improving combat ability.
A Primordial God possesses an additional existential quality tied to their origin and relationship with a fundamental Concept.
Consequently:
Defeating a Primordial God
≠
becoming a Primordial God
A sufficiently powerful God-King can defeat a Primordial through overwhelming combat ability, a superior artifact, a favorable Concept, Soul destruction, specialized racial abilities or a direct weakness without sharing the Primordial's metaphysical nature.

#### Victor and Kronus
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s victory over Kronus is the clearest example. Victor had already reached God-King-class combat capability and continued evolving until he could defeat the Second-Generation Primordial God.[9]
That victory demonstrates that Victor had entered the uppermost God-King / Primordial-overlap combat region. It does not mean his race or Divine generation retroactively changed into that of Kronus.
His subsequent Blood Dragon Primogenitor evolution in Chapter 803 represented a separate existential advancement.[10]

### The Seven High-Level God-Kings
Chapter 1059 contains one of the strongest later confirmations that the God-King bracket possesses internal upper divisions. While exploring a region populated by Eldritch Beings, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] notices another recurrence of the number seven and internally lists:
Seven Hells
Seven Celestial Heavens
Seven Deadly Sins
Seven Virtues
Seven High-Level [[God-Kings]]

This statement confirms that High-Level God-Kings existed as a recognized category associated with Earth's previous supernatural structure.
However, the statement should not be used to assume that every historically important Pantheon ruler remained exactly High-Level throughout all eras. Rulers died, evolved, weakened, obtained artifacts or belonged to separate Primordial generations.

### Physical Power vs Conceptual Authority
This separation becomes mandatory from the God-King bracket onward.
Attribute
Interpretation
Physical Strength
Raw bodily force. At God-King and greater ranges this can reach planetary-class consequences without relying upon Conceptual manipulation.
Energy Output
Destruction caused through [[Divine Energy]], Miasma, Elemental Power or similar direct release.
Conceptual Authority
Manipulation of the phenomenon represented by a Divinity. This may affect Souls, Space-Time, Death, Reality or an entire Divine jurisdiction without corresponding directly to explosive yield.
Attack Potency
The amount of force that can be concentrated into a target. This can exceed the visible area destroyed.
Area of Effect
The amount of territory directly affected. A highly concentrated attack may possess extreme potency without destroying the surrounding environment.
Domain Authority
Additional influence available inside the user's own Pantheon, Heaven, Hell or territory.
A God-King possessing a sophisticated Time Divinity does not need to physically blow up a dimension in order for their Time authority to affect that dimension. Likewise, a Soul or Death-based authority may kill something vastly more durable than the amount of physical terrain the attack destroys.
For this reason, physical destruction feats should never be used as an upper limit for God-King Conceptual powers.

### Environmental Durability
Divine realms frequently survive battles that would annihilate normal planets.
Chapter 800 states directly that the [[Greek Underworld]] battle was producing destruction sufficient to destroy Earth several times over, yet the region survived because the Pantheon possessed greater resilience and because [[Gaia]] appeared to be reinforcing the ground.[5]
The same phenomenon appears in [[Biblical Hell]]. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and War caused the entire dimension to shake and permanently altered part of its landscape, but Hell itself endured due to its supernatural structure and connection to higher cosmic systems.[6]
Therefore:
Pantheon survives attack
does not automatically mean
attack is below planetary level.
The environmental durability of the realm must be considered.

### Relationship with the Later Threat System
Main article: [[Power Hierarchy]]
The [[Elderblood Empire]]'s later Threat Level system classifies danger according to the scale of destruction or strategic catastrophe an entity can produce.
In that system:
CODE V-03 = Continental-class threat.
CODE V-04 = Planetary / World-Ender-class threat.
CODE V-05 = Solar-System-class threat.
CODE V-06 = Galactic-class threat.
God-King Power Level and Threat Level are not the same system.
Based upon demonstrated God-King-era physical feats, the bracket can conservatively be associated with at least planetary-class direct combat output, placing true combat-capable [[God-Kings]] at or above the broad destructive standard represented by V-04.
This is an extrapolated correspondence, not a direct canonical equation.
The novel does not state that "Low God-King = V-04", "Mid God-King = V-05" or any similar one-to-one conversion.
This is especially important because later characters can reach very high V-codes through specialized abilities without belonging to the God-King Divine hierarchy at all.

### Why God-Kings Differ So Much
Two [[God-Kings]] can occupy the same broad class while having radically different matchups because Divine combat becomes increasingly asymmetric at this level.
For example:
- A God-King specializing in War may possess superior direct battle ability.
- A God-King specializing in Time may possess weaker physical force but far more dangerous battlefield control.
- A Ruler inside their own [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] may receive overwhelming territorial amplification.
- A God-King equipped with a superior Divine Artifact can temporarily fight above their usual class.
- A Progenitor possessing Soul-destroying abilities may kill a God-King despite possessing lower ordinary physical strength.
- A God-King with poor combat technique can lose to a physically weaker but vastly more skilled opponent.
The Author's ranking explicitly rejects the concept that a Being with more Energy automatically wins.[1]

### Advancement
Progress within the God-King bracket normally occurs through the cumulative improvement of the entire existence rather than through a single mandatory transformation.
Important factors include:
- increasing [[Divine Energy]] reserves;
- increasing Energy quality;
- improving Energy control;
- strengthening the Soul;
- developing principal Divinities;
- acquiring or awakening additional Divinities;
- deepening Conceptual understanding;
- becoming more synchronized with represented Concepts;
- improving physical strength and durability;
- mastering martial arts;
- improving weapon skill;
- accumulating battle experience;
- obtaining stronger Divine Artifacts;
- increasing Faith;
- acquiring Ruler Authority;
- gaining Domain support;
- evolving racial physiology;
- and eliminating weaknesses that prevent existing Power from being expressed efficiently.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is the clearest example of this process. His Energy reached God-King quantities long before his practical control did. His later training did not simply give him "more Energy"; it allowed him to use the enormous Power he already possessed with increasingly absurd efficiency.[1][2]

### Common Misconceptions
God-King classification errors
“God-King is just the title of a Pantheon ruler.”
The term has both political and power-level uses. Chapter 690 explicitly explains that [[God-Kings]] are not all equally powerful and that some receive the name because they lead Pantheons.[2]
“Every God-King ruler has God-King-class combat ability.”
No. Political authority must be separated from individual combat capability.
“More [[Divine Energy]] automatically means a higher God-King class.”
No. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] possessed God-King quantities of Energy while still fighting at Low-Level Combat God level.[1]
“Low, Mid, High and Peak are biological transformations.”
No. They are broad development brackets. A Being can progress continuously without visibly transforming.
“A Peak God-King is a Primordial God.”
No. Peak God-King describes achieved practical Power. Primordial Godhood includes a distinct existential origin and relationship with Divinity.
“Killing a Primordial automatically makes someone Primordial.”
No. Victor defeated Kronus before his later Blood Dragon Primogenitor evolution.[9][10]
“God-Kings are only planetary.”
Planetary is used here as a conservative raw physical/Energy lower bound. It is not a ceiling. Upper God-King and Primordial-overlap battles already exceed a single-Earth destruction benchmark.[5]
“A planet-scale physical feat limits Conceptual abilities to planet scale.”
No. Physical destruction and Conceptual authority are separate measurements. Conceptual powers can operate across Souls, Time, Space, Death, Pantheon structures or other metaphysical systems without reproducing the same result as an explosion.[7]
“If the Pantheon survived, the attack cannot have been planetary.”
Incorrect. Chapter 800 explicitly describes Earth-destroying levels of destruction while explaining that the [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]]'s greater resilience and [[Gaia]]'s reinforcement prevented equivalent environmental collapse.[5]
“God-King rank guarantees victory over a High-Level Combat God.”
Not absolutely. Matchup, artifacts, Conceptual abilities, experience and technique remain relevant.[1]

Highest conventional God-King combat region, overlapping with lower [[Primordial Gods]] without granting Primordial existence.
Physical Floor
Conservatively planetary or greater for combat-capable God-Kings; upper examples demonstrate destruction sufficient to destroy Earth multiple times.[5]
Conceptual Ceiling
Not determined by physical destruction. Divinities can possess substantially greater metaphysical reach depending upon Concept, mastery and jurisdiction.[7]
God-King Power Level is therefore best understood as a broad upper-Divine continuum rather than a rigid numerical cultivation system. The bracket begins when a Being's complete usable Power has moved beyond conventional [[Combat Gods]], progresses through increasingly developed Souls, Divinities and combat capability, and culminates where the greatest God-Kings can contend directly with Primordial Gods. Their raw physical and Energy-based strength can conservatively be treated as planetary at minimum, but this physical scale should never be mistaken for the maximum scope of their Conceptual abilities, Domains, Soul powers or other Divine authorities.[1][2][5]
