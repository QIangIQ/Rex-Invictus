### Organization

#### Creator
[[Aphrodite]]
The Godly Chat Group, also referred to as the Gods' Chat Group, is a private online group created and administered by Aphrodite. Unlike a Divine council, treaty organization or formal alliance, it was originally a personal social space containing only individuals Aphrodite considered worthy of joining. Its five original members were Aphrodite, [[Freyja|Freya]], [[Loki]], Susanoo and Kali, Gods belonging to four separate Pantheons who possessed considerable influence within their respective Divine societies.[1]
The group became considerably more important when Aphrodite invited [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] Alucard despite him not being a God at the time. Victor joined under the username The Sigma Male, becoming the first confirmed non-God admitted into Aphrodite's circle. What began primarily as a place for gossip, jokes and arguments consequently developed into an unusual cross-Pantheon communication channel connecting Victor with major figures of the Greek, Norse, Hindu and Shinto Divine worlds.[2]
Years later, after Victor had become the Demon King and one of the strongest active supernatural rulers, he specifically asked whether the group remained functional before using it to contact Loki. The later conversations demonstrated that the Godly Chat Group had outgrown its original role as a Divine social circle and could function as an informal diplomatic and intelligence backchannel between people who otherwise belonged to competing Pantheons.[3][4][5]

### Overview
The Godly Chat Group should not be confused with the later private communication networks used by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wives. [[Aphrodite]]'s group existed independently and significantly earlier, having already connected Gods from otherwise politically separate Pantheons before Victor was invited.[1]
Aphrodite maintained direct administrative control over the group. She could invite members, change their display names without their permission and effectively dictate the atmosphere of the channel. The members nevertheless treated the group less like an official institution and more like an ancient circle of friends whose interpersonal relationships happened to span some of the most powerful Divine factions on Earth.[1][4]
Its importance came precisely from that informality. [[Freyja|Freya]], [[Loki]], Susanoo and Kali could communicate outside the ceremonies and restrictions of their respective Pantheons; Aphrodite could maintain friendships independent of [[Olympus]]; and Victor later gained a direct line to Gods whom he otherwise might have encountered only through formal negotiations or hostile supernatural incidents.[2][5]

### Interface and Communication
⌁ Channel Properties
Observed Functions
- Online status — the interface visibly announces when members enter or leave the chat.[2]
- Display names — members use custom usernames rather than their real identities, although several later return to their actual names.[1][4]
- Administrator privileges — [[Aphrodite]] can rename members directly and repeatedly abuses this privilege for amusement.[1][4]
- Images — members can upload photographs, and the group possesses a private collection of member images accessible to the participants.[2]
- GIFs and reactions — the Gods routinely use reaction images, GIFs, emojis and simple responses such as "+1".[1][2]
- Soul communication — Kali's participation is abnormal. Her visible contribution can appear simply as ellipses while the actual meaning is perceived at the Soul level. Aphrodite could understand her, and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] shocked the other members by immediately understanding Kali as well.[1][2]

### Member Dynamics

#### Aphrodite — The Social Center
[[Aphrodite]] is the foundation of the group. The members are not gathered because their Pantheons are allies but because they individually possess relationships with her. Her ability to maintain friendships across radically different Divine societies allowed a Greek Goddess to casually communicate with major figures from the Norse, Hindu and Shinto Pantheons long before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction began unifying supernatural powers on a larger scale.[1]
Her administrator behavior also defines the atmosphere. Aphrodite frequently changes usernames simply because she finds the reaction entertaining, openly teases her friends and has little concern for the dignity normally expected from ancient Gods. That lack of ceremony is precisely what distinguishes the group from an official Divine council.[1][4]

#### Freya and Loki
[[Freyja|Freya]] and [[Loki]] possess one of the most volatile relationships within the channel. [[Aphrodite]] later reveals that their relationship had once been sufficiently hostile that Freya could barely tolerate encountering Loki. The existence of their shared friendship network and the Godly Chat Group gave them repeated social contact without the political pressures surrounding [[Asgard]], gradually allowing them to interact without every disagreement becoming a genuine conflict.[5]
Their argument over Hela demonstrates that this did not make them automatically agree. Freya openly opposed Loki when she believed his paternal instincts were preventing him from acknowledging Hela's independence, while Loki remained intensely protective of his daughter.[5]

#### Kali's Unusual Presence
Kali is simultaneously one of the least conventionally talkative and most important members. Her overwhelming connection to Destruction makes ordinary interaction fundamentally different from that of the other Gods. In the early group scenes, much of her communication is conveyed directly at the level of the Soul rather than by ordinary written text.[1][2]
[[Aphrodite]] being capable of understanding Kali already distinguished her from most people. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s immediate comprehension of Kali therefore attracted enormous attention when he first entered the group. That first contact eventually became more significant when Victor returned years later as a vastly stronger Being and openly announced his intention to surpass her.[2][4]

### Role in the Story
Although the Godly Chat Group appears only occasionally, it performs several distinct narrative functions:
- Cross-Pantheon social network: it demonstrates that personal friendships between Gods can exist outside formal Pantheon politics.[1]
- Introduction of major Gods: [[Freyja|Freya]] and Kali are introduced to the story through the group before their later physical appearances.[1]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s growing reputation: the difference between how the members treat Victor in Chapters 449 and 725 provides a direct measurement of how dramatically his status changed.[2][4]
- Kali and Victor's first connection: Victor's ability to understand Kali immediately establishes an unusual connection long before their later relationship develops.[2]
- Informal diplomacy: Victor uses the channel to contact [[Loki]] regarding Hela and the developing Norse conflict without involving official representatives.[3][5]
- Character relationships: the conversations reveal [[Aphrodite]]'s friendship network, Loki's paternal instincts, Freya's temperament, Susanoo's humor and Kali's normally distant personality in circumstances where Divine titles matter far less than personal familiarity.[1][4][5]

### Status
The Godly Chat Group is explicitly confirmed to remain active in Chapter 720, and Chapters 725–726 show all of its established members still possessing access to the channel.[3][4][5] No later event in the currently indexed material explicitly states that [[Aphrodite]] deleted or permanently dissolved the group. Its present operational status should therefore be treated as unknown rather than assuming that the channel ceased to exist.
The group should also be distinguished from the later wives' group chat used by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family. The wives' network is an Elderblood household communication system with a vastly larger membership, whereas the Godly Chat Group originated as Aphrodite's personal circle of Divine friends.[1]

### Trivia
- [[Aphrodite]] originally restricted membership to people she personally considered worthy of joining.[1]
- The original five members represented four different Pantheons: Greek, Norse, Shinto and Hindu.[1]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was not a God when he joined. Aphrodite nevertheless openly believed that he could eventually become one.[2]
- Victor's original username, The Sigma Male, was chosen by Aphrodite rather than by Victor himself.[2]
- [[Loki]] and Susanoo immediately understood why Aphrodite had chosen the name after Victor casually left instead of accepting her invitation.[2]
- The chat possesses a private member-photo archive.[2]
- Aphrodite repeatedly uses administrator privileges to humiliate the other members by changing their usernames.[1][4]
- [[Freyja|Freya]] temporarily received both Bitch 01/Bitch 001 and Whore as administrator-assigned usernames.[1][4]
- Susanoo temporarily received the username Lolicon after the other members discussed Inari.[1]
- Loki's best-known original username was AManOfCulture. Aphrodite later temporarily renamed him Chicken.[1][4]
- Kali's early username was TheMostBadssGoddess, an identity she rarely showed any interest in changing until Victor's later return to the group.[4]
- By the time the members restored their actual names in Chapter 725, Victor had gone from being the only non-God curiosity in the group to a Being stronger than multiple original members.[2][4]
- Aphrodite credits her friendship and the chat group with helping Freya and Loki interact without Freya constantly attempting to attack the God of Lies.[5]
