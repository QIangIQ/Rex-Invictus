The Reincarnation System is the universal mechanism responsible for returning processed Souls to the cycle of Life after death. It operates as one of the central pieces of the metaphysical infrastructure maintained by the Regulators of Existence, working primarily through The Universal Tree, who governs Life and Reincarnation, and The Judges of The Abyss, who govern Souls, judgment and the distribution of the dead.[1][2]
The system should not be confused with Hell, Heaven, the Judges of the Abyss or the broader System. Heavens and Hells are afterlife environments in which Souls are processed, rewarded, punished or recycled; the Judges determine how Souls are handled; and the Universal Tree represents the fundamental authority over Life and Reincarnation. The Reincarnation System is the automated structure that connects these processes and ultimately places processed Souls back into Life.[3][4]
Its operation is extraordinarily broad. Everything possessing a Soul participates in the cycle, including ordinary Humans and supernatural creatures as well as non-sentient life such as plants and even entities such as mountains that possess traces of a Soul. Once the appropriate afterlife processes have been completed, the recycled Soul is returned to the Universal Tree's side of the system and eventually receives a new beginning.[1][3]

### Creation
The Reincarnation System was created by [[The Universal Tree]], the Regulator responsible for Life, Reincarnation and the continued maintenance of living existence throughout the Universe. The system allows the reincarnation process to continue throughout countless worlds and Sectors without requiring the Universal Tree to personally supervise every individual Soul passing through the cycle.[4]
The system was created after [[Jeanne Elderblood]] lost her memories. Because of this, Jeanne originally possessed no personal knowledge of the Reincarnation System despite her ancient connection to the Universal Tree. After recovering herself, her powers and her relationship with her brother, Jeanne once again became capable of investigating the structure he had created.[4]
The system effectively automated one of the Universal Tree's most important cosmic responsibilities. Rather than personally selecting a new life for every Soul that reached him, the Universal Tree established an organized mechanism capable of processing enormous numbers of Souls simultaneously across the Universe.

### Purpose
The fundamental purpose of the Reincarnation System is to ensure the continuity of Life through the recycling and reincarnation of Souls. Death therefore does not automatically represent the destruction of the Soul. In ordinary circumstances, death begins a larger metaphysical process through which the Soul is judged, processed through the appropriate afterlife and eventually returned to Life in a new form.[1][3]

The exact route is not identical for every Being. Ordinary Souls, powerful supernatural Souls, Gods and non-sentient Souls can be processed differently, while individual Pantheons possess their own Heavens and Hells with unique rules. What remains consistent is that the greater system exists to prevent Souls from accumulating indefinitely in the afterlife and to return recyclable Souls to the living cycle.[3]

### The Cycle of Reincarnation

#### Death
The cycle begins when a living Being genuinely dies and its Soul separates from its previous existence. Death alone does not immediately cause reincarnation. The Soul must first pass through the structures responsible for determining what happens to it after death.[1]
The exact circumstances can vary substantially depending upon the nature of the Being. Ordinary Mortals are processed differently from exceptionally powerful supernatural creatures, and Gods may undergo a much more decisive judgment because they exist on a higher level than ordinary Mortal Souls.[3]

#### Judgment and Soul Sorting
Main article: [[The Judges of The Abyss]]
The Judges of the Abyss occupy the central position between death and reincarnation. Despite their title, their everyday work is not limited to conducting conventional moral trials. Their role is more accurately described as sorting, separating and administrating Souls before those Souls enter the appropriate afterlife.[3]
For ordinary Mortals, the Judges can separate the positive and negative aspects of the Soul. Positive portions can be directed toward the Heaven associated with the individual's beliefs, while negative or corrupted portions can be sent toward Hell. Souls burdened by sufficiently severe Negative Karma can be sent directly to Hell for rehabilitation rather than being separated normally.[1][3]
This judgment stage is distinct from the Reincarnation System itself. The Judges determine the Soul's condition and destination; the Reincarnation System becomes relevant once that Soul has completed the necessary afterlife process and is ready to return to Life.

#### Heaven
Heavens do not merely function as eternal rewards. They are part of the greater recycling structure surrounding Souls.
The positive aspect of an ordinary Soul can be sent to the Heaven corresponding to the Being's belief or spiritual system. The Soul remains within that Paradise until it becomes satisfied with its personal Heaven. After reaching that state, it returns to the Cycle of Reincarnation and can eventually begin another life.[3]
Different Pantheons implement this principle differently. The Norse system provides one of the clearest examples. Warriors who die with honor may enter Valhalla. If they later die within Valhalla, their Souls are sent directly toward reincarnation rather than remaining there permanently.[3]
Heaven is therefore not necessarily the final destination of a Soul. Within the natural cosmic order, it can function as one stage of the Soul's progression before reincarnation.

#### Hell
Main articles: [[Biblical Hell]], [[Greek Underworld]], [[Nordic Hell]]
Hells likewise exist as part of the recycling process rather than functioning exclusively as eternal prisons for evil Beings. Souls carrying negative aspects, severe sins or undesirable Karma can enter a Hell, where the precise method of punishment and rehabilitation depends upon the Pantheon and the structure of that Underworld.[1][3]
If rehabilitation succeeds, the processed Soul can leave the Hell system and pass toward the Universal Tree so that it may eventually reincarnate.[1]
The importance of Hells to the reincarnation infrastructure is enormous. When a Hell ceases to function correctly, Souls that should normally be processed begin accumulating. This places pressure on the Pantheon's Divine authorities and can interfere with the larger circulation of Souls.[1]
The process is usually sufficiently automatic that even a King of Hell does not personally inspect every Soul. The ruler's responsibility is principally to keep the Underworld's system functioning properly so that the automated recycling mechanisms can continue operating.[3]

#### Return to the Universal Tree
After a Soul completes the necessary Heaven, Hell or recycling process, it passes toward the Primordial responsible for Life and Reincarnation: The Universal Tree.[1]
This division of responsibilities is one of the foundations of the cosmic system. The Judges govern the Soul itself, while the Universal Tree governs the continuation of Life.
The relationship was directly explained by the Judges:
Life creates the form, while the Judges provide it with a Soul.[2]
Their functions are therefore complementary rather than interchangeable. The Judges help create and administer Souls, while the Universal Tree provides the living framework into which those Souls are eventually placed.

#### Reincarnation
Once the Soul has been sufficiently processed, it enters the reincarnation stage and is prepared for another existence.
Reincarnation is not ordinary resurrection. The previous individual does not simply wake up again in a younger body with their old identity intact. The purpose of the process is to provide a new beginning. Previous-life information and identity are normally removed or emptied as part of the Soul's preparation for another existence, preventing ordinary reincarnated Beings from naturally retaining complete knowledge of their former lives.[3][4]
The recycled Soul then becomes associated with a new living form, restarting the cycle.

### Types of Souls
The Reincarnation System does not process every Soul identically. The method depends heavily upon the nature, strength and condition of the Soul.

#### Ordinary Mortal Souls
Ordinary Mortal Souls constitute the largest portion of the natural cycle.
When an ordinary Mortal dies, the Judges can separate different aspects of the Soul and send them toward the appropriate Heaven or Hell. After those portions complete their respective processes, the Soul can eventually be recycled and returned to Life.[1][3]
The system is heavily automated at this level. [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] rulers are not expected to personally judge every Human, animal or other ordinary creature that dies.

#### Powerful Supernatural Souls
Powerful supernatural Beings are handled differently because their Souls possess considerably greater strength and integrity.
Nyx theorized that forcibly separating the positive and negative portions of such a powerful Soul could damage it badly enough that it would become incapable of returning to the reincarnation cycle. Consequently, powerful supernatural Mortals—such as sufficiently strong Vampires and [[Werewolves]]—can be sent directly to Heaven or Hell without having their Souls divided in the same manner as ordinary Mortals.[3]
These Souls receive greater direct attention from the system because the consequences of incorrectly processing them are much more significant.
It is therefore inaccurate to state simply that all supernatural Souls bypass the Reincarnation System. Powerful supernatural Souls receive different judgment and afterlife treatment, but they can still ultimately return to the reincarnation cycle once the appropriate conditions have been satisfied.[3]

#### Gods
Gods receive an even more decisive form of judgment.
Unlike powerful Mortals, who may still be sent into Heaven or Hell according to their actions, a dead God brought before the Judges can face outcomes involving the very continuation of their existence. A Lesser God, for example, might be judged and either cease to exist or be allowed to return to the Cycle of Reincarnation and receive an entirely fresh beginning.[3]
This difference exists because Gods are Higher Existences rather than ordinary Mortal lifeforms. Their deaths and Souls therefore cannot always be processed according to the same rules used for common Mortals.

#### Non-Sentient Souls
The reincarnation cycle extends far beyond Humans and supernatural creatures.
Everything in existence can possess some form or trace of a Soul, including plants and even mountains. When a tree dies, the small Soul associated with it passes into Hell, is automatically recycled and returns to the broader system so that another tree can eventually be born.[1][3]
These Soul traces are processed so rapidly and automatically that even [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] rulers generally do not notice individual cases.
The existence of this process demonstrates that the Reincarnation System is not merely an afterlife for intelligent species. It is part of the fundamental recycling of Life throughout Creation.

### Heavens and Hells as Soul Zones
Individual Heavens and Hells can be understood as local Soul Zones connected to the larger universal system.
Different Pantheons created different cultural interpretations of the afterlife, but those differences do not alter their underlying metaphysical purpose. One Hell may punish sinners through physical torment, another may employ a completely different rehabilitation method, and one Heaven may function differently from another, yet all remain mechanisms through which Souls are prepared for eventual recycling.[3]
This explains why apparently contradictory afterlife traditions can coexist. The Reincarnation System does not require every Pantheon to use the same method. It requires only that the relevant Soul eventually be correctly processed and returned to the greater cycle.

#### Norse Example
The [[Norse Pantheon]] demonstrates how unusual local rules can still feed into the same universal process.
Those who die honorably may be taken into Valhalla. When such warriors later die again, they enter the Reincarnation Cycle directly. Those who die ordinary deaths can instead enter Helheim, where their Souls undergo the recycling process before being returned to reincarnation.[3]
The difference therefore concerns the route taken through the afterlife rather than the ultimate existence of an entirely separate reincarnation system.

### Rulers of the Underworld
Kings and Queens of Hells possess a limited administrative connection to the greater Soul System.
A genuine [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] Ruler is expected to maintain the local machinery responsible for processing Souls and must be capable of judging Souls with sufficient impartiality. [[Persephone Elderblood]] had to undergo a trial administered through the authority of [[Hades]]' Helm before she could become the true ruler of the Greek Underworld. Passing the test connected her to the system responsible for Souls.[3][2]
This does not place a Hell King above the Judges of the Abyss. Nyx explicitly described Hell rulers as possessing only a lesser capacity to judge Souls compared to the Judges themselves.[3]
Their responsibility is primarily administrative:
- maintain the local Soul Zone;
- ensure Souls continue flowing correctly;
- prevent the Underworld from becoming blocked;
- administer local judgment where their authority permits;
- maintain the punishment or rehabilitation systems;
- and return processed Souls to the greater reincarnation cycle.
A ruler abandoning these responsibilities can cause large numbers of Souls to accumulate and force higher Divine authorities to compensate for the failure.[1]

### The Core of the Afterlife
Main article: [[The Core of the Afterlife]]
Individual Underworlds possess metaphysical mechanisms connected to the wider Soul System. The [[Greek Underworld]] contains an enormous Soul Core through which countless Souls continuously flow. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] identified this structure as something constructed by the Primordial authorities so that the Underworld's Soul-processing system could function.[5]
Similar structures exist in other properly developed Hells. Their external appearance and local environment may differ, but the basic mechanism is connected to the same deeper Soul infrastructure.[5]
The Core is therefore not the Reincarnation System itself. It functions more like a local interface or processing hub attached to a much greater structure.

### Reincarnation Queue
Reincarnation is not always instantaneous.
Chapter 1021 reveals that the Reincarnation System requires appropriate conditions before a Soul can receive another body. In ordinary circumstances, the system reincarnates a Soul on the same planet on which its previous body existed, but this process depends upon enough new Beings being born for reincarnation opportunities to become available.[4]
Consequently, a planet that suffers enormous population loss can accumulate Souls waiting for new lives. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] concluded that [[Vanessa Alekerth]]'s Soul could still be waiting in the reincarnation queue because the mass deaths caused by his predecessor drastically reduced the number of suitable births available for the waiting Souls.[4]
This establishes several important characteristics of the system:
- reincarnation possesses an actual queue rather than occurring instantly;
- reincarnation normally maintains a connection between a Soul and its previous planet;
- population growth affects the speed at which waiting Souls can return;
- catastrophic depopulation can create substantial reincarnation delays;
- and a Soul can remain waiting within the system for an extended period without having already received a new incarnation.[4]
The exact algorithm determining which Soul receives which new body has not been explained.

### Memory and Identity
One of the Reincarnation System's most important functions is preventing the new incarnation from simply becoming a continuation of the previous person's ordinary life.
A recycled Soul normally receives a new beginning. Information forming the previous individual's personal identity is removed or emptied during processing so that the new incarnation does not naturally begin life carrying complete memories and knowledge from its predecessor.[3][4]
This distinction is important because a Soul can persist while the previous personality does not.
Same Soul ≠ Same conscious individual.
A Soul that enters the completed reincarnation process should therefore not automatically be treated as the original dead person returning from death. The original life has ended; the Soul has been recycled into another existence.
This is also why resurrection and reincarnation are treated as fundamentally different supernatural processes.

### Reincarnation and Resurrection
Resurrection attempts to restore an individual who has died while preserving enough of that person's identity, Soul information and existence for them to remain recognizably the same Being.
Reincarnation intentionally moves in the opposite direction. The Soul completes the death cycle, has its previous existence processed and eventually begins another life with a new identity.

Once a Soul has progressed sufficiently far through reincarnation, restoring the original individual becomes fundamentally more complicated because the old identity is no longer simply waiting intact within an afterlife.

### System Automation
The greatest practical advantage of the Reincarnation System is automation.
The number of Souls continuously entering the death cycle throughout the Universe is far too great for the Universal Tree, Judges or individual [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] rulers to manually process one at a time. A tree dying somewhere in Creation does not require a personal appearance from a Primordial. Its Soul trace can pass through the local recycling mechanism automatically and be returned to the system almost immediately.[1]
Likewise, an ordinary King of Hell is not expected to individually sentence every Soul entering their Underworld. Their role is to keep the structure functional while the system performs the vast majority of routine processing.[3]
Direct intervention becomes increasingly important when a Soul is abnormal, extraordinarily powerful, cosmologically significant or otherwise incapable of being processed safely by ordinary mechanisms.

### Abnormal and Powerful Souls
The Reincarnation System is built around the ordinary continuation of Life, but extremely powerful Souls can require special treatment.
Powerful Mortals may be too spiritually robust to undergo ordinary division without damage. Gods can receive direct judgments determining whether they are permitted to reincarnate at all. Other exceptional Beings may possess Souls connected to unique Concepts, Bloodlines, Divine authorities or external systems that make routine processing unsuitable.[3]
The stronger and more unusual a Soul becomes, the less appropriate it is to assume that the automatic procedure used for an ordinary Human will apply unchanged.
This is one of the principal reasons the Judges of the Abyss remain necessary despite the existence of an automated Reincarnation System.

### Soul Creation
The Judges of the Abyss do more than process already-existing Souls. They are also involved in their creation.
During [[Persephone]]'s trial, the Judges explicitly stated that one of their responsibilities is creating Souls. They explained their partnership with the Universal Tree through a division of cosmic labor: Life creates the form, while the Judges provide the Soul.[2]
The Reincarnation System therefore participates in a larger structure involving both newly created Souls and recycled Souls.
Not every living Being must necessarily be the reincarnation of a previously dead person. New Souls can be created as Life expands, while existing Souls can simultaneously return through reincarnation.
This prevents the reincarnation cycle from implying a permanently fixed number of Souls throughout an expanding Universe.

### Relation to the Broader System
Main article: System
The Reincarnation System is connected to the greater metaphysical infrastructure commonly called the System, but the two terms should not be treated as synonyms.
The broader System records and regulates numerous aspects of supernatural existence, while the Reincarnation System specifically concerns the processing and return of Souls into Life.
[[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] rulers can establish connections with the Soul-processing portion of the System, and metaphysical structures such as the Core of the Afterlife can function as interfaces to deeper universal records and mechanisms.[3][5]
The Reincarnation System is therefore best understood as a specialized subsystem within the wider cosmic administration maintained by the [[Regulators of Existence]].

### Relationship with the Regulators of Existence
The Reincarnation System is not the responsibility of one Regulator acting entirely alone. The complete death-and-rebirth cycle depends upon several cosmic functions.

### Importance
The Reincarnation System is one of the mechanisms that prevents the Universe from becoming overwhelmed by dead Souls.
Without functioning Hells, Heavens and Soul-processing structures, the dead would accumulate faster than individual Gods could manually process them. Without the Judges, Souls would lack the cosmic administration required to determine how they should be handled. Without the Universal Tree and the Reincarnation System, processed Souls would lack the mechanism needed to return to Life.[1][3]
Its existence also means that the Universe's population is not composed entirely of newly created Souls. Creation continuously uses both newly produced Souls and Souls returning from previous lives.

### Known Rules
- Everything possessing a Soul can enter the recycling cycle, including non-sentient lifeforms.[1][3]
- Heavens and Hells ultimately function as Soul-processing environments rather than simply permanent reward and punishment dimensions.[3]
- Ordinary Souls can be divided into positive and negative aspects before being processed.[1][3]
- Powerful supernatural Souls are normally not divided in the same manner because of their greater spiritual integrity.[3]
- Gods can receive direct judgment with consequences more decisive than those applied to Mortals.[3]
- Processed Souls are eventually transferred toward the Universal Tree for reincarnation.[1]
- Reincarnation provides a new beginning rather than a conventional resurrection.[3]
- Previous-life identity and information are normally removed during the reincarnation process.
- The system can maintain queues when suitable new lives are temporarily unavailable.[4]
- Reincarnation normally attempts to return a Soul to Life on the same planet associated with its previous body.[4]
- Large-scale depopulation can greatly slow reincarnation because fewer suitable births exist for Souls waiting in the system.[4]
- Local [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] rulers are responsible for keeping their Soul-processing systems functional but do not manually perform every reincarnation.[3]
- The Judges and Universal Tree can directly intervene when ordinary automated processing is insufficient.[2]

### Notes
- The term Reincarnation System describes a specialized cosmic mechanism and should not automatically be treated as another name for the complete System.
- [[The Universal Tree]] created the Reincarnation System only after Jeanne had already lost her memories, explaining why she originally had no personal knowledge of its construction.[4]
- Chapter 1021 confirms that reincarnation possesses a queue and that population availability can delay a Soul's return to Life.[4]
- The exact method used to match a Soul with a new body has not yet been explained.
- The story has not established that reincarnated Beings naturally retain memories of their previous existence; the ordinary process instead emphasizes a fresh beginning.
- Individual Heavens and Hells can possess radically different methods while remaining connected to the same greater reincarnation cycle.[3]
- The Reincarnation System operates on a scale far larger than sentient civilization because plants and other non-sentient entities also possess Souls that are recycled.[1][3]
- [[The Core of the Afterlife]] is a local interface to the Soul-processing infrastructure and should not be confused with the Reincarnation System itself.[5]
- The separation of responsibilities between The Universal Tree and [[The Judges of The Abyss]] can be summarized as Life creates the form; Soul supplies the existence that inhabits it.[2]
