Creator's Energy Mastery is an exceptionally advanced Energy manipulation technique developed by Victor Elderblood and formalized with Scathach Elderblood Scarlett. It originated from Victor's investigation of the extraordinary natural abilities possessed by his daughter, Melida Charlotte Elderblood, whose instinctive control allowed her to manipulate Energy at a level preceding the distinction between its various supernatural forms. Through Melida, Victor discovered a method of accessing the same foundational Energy that the original Creator employed to construct the Universe.[4][1]
The principle underlying the technique is the distinction between Energy itself and the characteristics assigned to Energy. [[Divine Energy]], Demonic Energy, [[Primordial Energy]], Mana, Ki and other supernatural Energies possess different properties, functions and requirements. Their differences nevertheless arise from the characteristics through which Energy is expressed. Attributeless Energy exists beneath these distinctions as a foundational medium that has not yet received a specific identity. A sufficiently knowledgeable practitioner can use that medium to reproduce other Energies by imposing their corresponding characteristics.[1][2][5]
For most qualified practitioners, Creator's Energy Mastery functions as a means of supplementing and strengthening existing powers. Attributeless Energy can be introduced into an established technique and given the same characteristics as the Energy already being used, greatly increasing the resulting output while reducing the quantity of specialized Energy required. At higher levels, the practitioner can produce differentiated Energies directly from the Attributeless foundation rather than depending entirely upon existing reserves of each separate type.[3]
Victor's mastery extends beyond ordinary supplementation. His exceptional control, parallel thought processes and Eldritch nature allow him to maintain Attributeless Energy as a principal foundation of his own power. Instead of continuously sustaining numerous independent Energy systems, he can characterize the underlying Energy according to his immediate requirements. This restructuring significantly improved the efficiency of his abilities and contributed to the enormous expansion of his unrestricted True Dragon form.[3][6]
The technique is classified as a Higher-Level Technique, standing above the family's established Grandmaster [[Martial Arts]] classification. Its formal progression consists of three stages: Beginner, Proficient and Master. Because even an unsuccessful preliminary attempt can fundamentally alter the user's body or interfere with the connection between physical existence and the Soul, Creator's Energy Mastery is maintained as a restricted Clan Elderblood Family Secret.[2][3]

### Terminology
The foundational Energy is described through several related names, including Attributeless Energy, Non-Attributed Energy, Energy Without Attributes, Characterless Energy and Characteristicless Energy. These designations refer to the same essential condition: Energy that has not been assigned the qualities distinguishing one supernatural manifestation from another.[1][2]
Creator's Energy emphasizes its cosmological origin and relationship with [[Primordial Chaos]], while Creator's Energy Mastery identifies the technique developed within the Elderblood family. The description of the Energy as the Creator's Paint originates from an analogy in which the original Creator is the Painter, Attributeless Energy is the material used for painting, and assigned characteristics represent the colors through which Creation acquires its differentiated forms.[1][7]
The terminology must also be distinguished from [[Primordial Energy]]. Although both possess an intimate relationship with Creation's original foundations, Primordial Energy is an attributed form of Energy, whereas Attributeless Energy exists before attributes are assigned. The distinction is functionally important for practitioners whose control remains limited to established Energy systems. For [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Melida, whose understanding reaches the underlying foundation, the difference between manipulating the attributed and unattributed states becomes practically insignificant.[5]
FUNDAMENTAL DISTINCTION
Attributeless Energy is not synonymous with every manifestation of [[Chaos]] Energy. Primordial Chaos was the original Creator associated with the foundational Energy, but the Creator, his Authority, his divided aspects of Positivity and Negativity, and the Energy he used to shape Creation are distinct. Likewise, [[Eldritch Energy]] originates from a different cosmological foundation and is not merely another name for the native Universe's Attributeless Energy.[1][5]

### Cosmological Foundation

#### The Creator and the Foundation of Creation
The native Universe originated through the power of [[Primordial Chaos]], the original Creator whose existence encompassed the Reality he produced. His ability to create was not restricted to manipulating the established material and supernatural structures familiar to ordinary Gods. Attributeless Energy served as the foundational medium through which the characteristics, structures and differentiated Energies of Creation could be established.[1][5]
In its original condition, Attributeless Energy possesses no predetermined identity. It does not inherently correspond to a particular element, Divine Authority or racial power. Its importance lies in being the medium upon which such characteristics can be imposed. The Creator could employ this Energy to shape the conditions and structures of his Universe, giving the underlying foundation the necessary characteristics through which Creation would acquire form and function.[1][5]
The significance of the foundational Energy therefore extends beyond its usefulness as an extraordinarily versatile source of supernatural power. It occupies a position near the beginning of the native Universe's creation process, preceding the differentiation of the Energy systems employed by its inhabitants. The many forms of Energy found throughout Creation can be understood through their connection to this common foundation, even when the individual manifestations possess radically different properties and applications.[8][5]
This does not mean that Attributeless Energy is identical to the totality of Creation or that possessing it automatically grants the powers of the Creator. The Energy is a medium employed in the creation process, while the knowledge, Authority and nature of the Being wielding it determine what can actually be accomplished. The original Creator's abilities consequently cannot be transferred in their entirety to another individual merely through access to his foundational Energy.

#### The Painter, Paint and Colors
The relationship between Attributeless Energy and differentiated Creation can be represented through the analogy of a Painter producing a work of art. The analogy identifies four connected elements: the Painter, the Paint, the Colors and the Painting. Each represents a distinct part of the creative process rather than a separate physical substance.[1][7]

#### The Common Origin of Energy
The supernatural world contains an enormous variety of Energy systems. [[Divine Energy]], Demonic Energy, Spirit Energy, Mana, Ki, [[Senjutsu]] and other manifestations possess different characteristics, respond to different forms of control and support different categories of supernatural abilities. Nevertheless, the distinctions between them do not change their fundamental nature as Energy.[8][5]
The differences arise through the characteristics that define their behavior. Divine Energy possesses qualities associated with Divinity, Demonic Energy carries Demonic characteristics, while other systems operate according to their own supernatural requirements. Such properties determine the effects, compatibility and applications of each Energy, but they do not eliminate the underlying relationship between them.[5]
Ordinary supernatural beings generally interact with Energy through the systems made available by their race, spiritual structure, training or Divine Authority. A God may naturally produce Divine Energy, a Demon may possess Demonic Energy and a magician may manipulate Mana. These abilities do not necessarily grant an understanding of the common foundation from which the characterized Energies originate.[5]
Beings possessing exceptional comprehension can operate beneath those distinctions. Instead of relying exclusively upon Energy already carrying the desired characteristics, they can employ a more fundamental medium and assign the properties required for a particular application. This allows them to bypass portions of the ordinary separation between supernatural systems, although doing so demands far greater precision than simply using an Energy type already compatible with their existence.[1][5]

#### Primordial Chaos, Positivity and Negativity
Main article: [[Primordial Chaos]]
Main article: Positivity & Negativity
Primordial Chaos was the original unified Primordial Being whose existence formed the foundation of the native Universe. The concepts of Positivity and Negativity represent the two divided aspects of that original existence. Their separation allowed the Universe to retain its stability while preserving the fundamental positive and negative foundations underlying Creation.[9][10][5]
The two aspects permeate Creation and participate in the composition of its phenomena. Positivity is predominantly associated with material, physical and concrete existence, while Negativity possesses a stronger relationship with spiritual, emotional and nonphysical phenomena. Their distribution is not entirely exclusive: physical objects and abstract Concepts alike contain both influences, with the relative predominance differing according to their nature.[5]
Even Concepts such as Death, Destruction, Time and Space possess both Positive and Negative components. A phenomenon may be predominantly associated with one side without being completely devoid of the other. Spiritual and emotional Concepts such as Fear, Hatred and Kindness possess a greater Negative proportion, while physical phenomena generally possess a greater Positive proportion. Their shared participation reflects the foundational duality established through the original Creator.[5]
This duality occupies a higher metaphysical position than the Attributeless Energy produced by Primordial Chaos. Positivity and Negativity are not merely two additional characteristics of equal standing with ordinary Divine or elemental Energies. They are the divided aspects of the Creator himself, while Attributeless Energy is the medium he created and employed in shaping the Universe.[10][5]
COSMOLOGICAL DISTINCTION
Primordial Chaos is the original Creator and unified Primordial Being. Positivity and Negativity are his divided aspects, whose influences permeate the native Universe. Attributeless Energy is the foundational Energy produced and employed by the Creator. The importance of Attributeless Energy within the creative process does not place it above the Being responsible for producing it or the fundamental aspects of his existence.[10][5]

#### The Creator and His Created Energy
The original [[Primordial Chaos]] is described as the embodiment of the Universe itself, with Creation existing within his own being. His supreme jurisdiction over that Reality includes attributes of omnipotence, omniscience and omnipresence within the Universe that constitutes his existence.[5]
These characteristics belong to the Creator and his relationship with his own Reality. They are not inherent abilities automatically acquired by every individual capable of manipulating Attributeless Energy. The technique provides access to a foundational medium, but the practitioner remains dependent upon personal understanding, available Energy, compatible Authority and the ability to control the resulting manifestation.[3][5]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s own exceptional proficiency is closely connected to his unusual existence as a Blood Dragon Primogenitor, True Dragon and Eldritch God. His position permits a degree of compatibility that ordinary Gods cannot reproduce merely through increased training. Even so, his ability to wield the Creator's Energy does not make him literally identical to the original Primordial Chaos or automatically grant him every attribute belonging to that Being.[3]

### Discovery

#### Melida Charlotte Elderblood
Main article: [[Melida Charlotte Elderblood]]
Melida Charlotte Elderblood possesses an extraordinary natural affinity for the manipulation and understanding of Energy. Her talent differs from conventional mastery because she instinctively approaches Energy before the properties distinguishing its various supernatural manifestations have been assigned. Rather than treating [[Divine Energy]], Demonic Energy, [[Primordial Energy]] or Mana as completely separate foundations, Melida can manipulate an undefined Energy state and give it the characteristics necessary for her intended action.[4][1]
Her unusual nature became apparent immediately after her birth and transformation under [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s influence. Her body displayed compatibility with numerous supernatural Energies, including Hell's Miasma, Divine power, Positive and Negative Energy, [[Magic]] and Primordial Energy. She could absorb surrounding Energy and imitate the properties she observed, even drawing a portion of the Primordial Energy controlled by [[Jeanne Elderblood]] into herself.[1]
Melida's capacity was especially remarkable because she possessed almost no accumulated personal experience. Most extraordinary supernatural practitioners develop their abilities through years of training, observation and accumulated knowledge. Melida instead demonstrated a natural understanding of Energy's underlying behavior, allowing her to manipulate its characteristics instinctively despite being newly born.[1]
The distinction became apparent when she imitated Victor's Energy manipulation. Victor had previously developed his abilities by employing existing Energies already contained within his body or gathered from the surrounding environment. Melida worked from the opposite direction. Her power originated from an undefined foundation that could subsequently receive the required properties.[1]
This natural method revealed a principle that Victor had possessed the necessary knowledge to understand but had not previously organized into a complete technique. Melida's instinctive manipulation supplied the missing connection between his enormous collection of supernatural powers and the deeper Energy state underlying their differentiation.

#### Victor's Breakthrough
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s understanding of Energy had developed through centuries of combat, training, experimentation and the assimilation of powers belonging to numerous supernatural beings. His accumulated knowledge included Divine Energies, Draconic powers, Demonic systems, [[Primordial Energy]], World Tree influences, the effects of Divine Concepts and the foreign properties of Eldritch existence.[1]
These experiences provided an enormous collection of reference points concerning the characteristics of differentiated Energy. What remained missing was a method of organizing those observations around a single underlying foundation. Melida's natural manipulation allowed Victor to recognize that the properties distinguishing various supernatural Energies could be imposed upon a state that initially possessed none of them.[1]
Victor rapidly connected the discovery with the role of Attributeless Energy in Creation. The Energy represented a condition preceding differentiation, allowing characteristics to be assigned according to the user's understanding. Rather than requiring each supernatural Energy to be independently acquired and maintained, the same underlying foundation could potentially support numerous different manifestations.[1]
His existing knowledge made the breakthrough unusually rapid. Melida possessed the instinctive understanding necessary to demonstrate the method, while Victor possessed the accumulated experience required to identify its implications. His comprehension of [[Chaos]] also improved during the discovery, reflecting the relationship between his developing understanding of Creation and the fundamental principles underlying his powers.[1]

#### Development into a Formal Technique
The discovery was subsequently transformed into a structured training system through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s work with [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]]. [[Scathach]] possessed exceptional control over numerous supernatural Energies and was among the most accomplished martial artists in the Elderblood family, making her a suitable practitioner for examining the technique's requirements and dangers.[2][3]
Her initial attempt revealed that producing Attributeless Energy required considerably more than ordinary control. Even a tiny quantity could become unstable and fundamentally alter an existing physical structure. Victor used the experience to refine the method, clarify the importance of preventing unintended characterization and establish requirements that would allow other family members to train without repeating the same mistakes.[2]
The resulting technique was classified above conventional Grandmaster [[Martial Arts]] and divided into three levels of mastery. Victor and Scathach also established restrictions upon its transmission, recognizing that unrestricted experimentation could endanger even exceptionally powerful members of their family.[3]

### Nature of Attributeless Energy

#### Energy Before Characteristics
Attributeless Energy is defined by its absence of a predetermined characteristic. It is not naturally Fire, Ice, Lightning, [[Divine Energy]], Demonic Energy, Mana, [[Primordial Energy]] or another established supernatural manifestation. The characteristics responsible for such distinctions are applied to the Energy rather than existing as permanent properties of its original state.[1][2]
A practitioner who possesses sufficient knowledge and control can give the Energy an appropriate attribute. The resulting manifestation then behaves according to the properties associated with that characteristic. This allows a common foundation to support numerous differentiated forms without requiring each form to originate from an entirely independent primary source.[2][5]

The underlying Energy serves as the foundational medium, while characteristics function as instructions defining the eventual result. #### Formless and Colorless State
Attributeless Energy is associated with a formless and colorless condition, reflecting its lack of assigned properties. The Energy can nevertheless be perceived or manifested through supernatural means, as demonstrated by the small colorless spheres produced during [[Scathach]]'s training and Violet's application of Order.[2][7]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s own advanced manipulation demonstrates the relationship between the colorless foundation and the characteristics subsequently imposed upon it. When he releases the underlying Energy supporting his powers, it initially possesses no distinguishing color or form. Upon allowing his Authority over Negativity to permeate the Energy, its appearance changes to deep red, reflecting the nature of the Concept applied to the foundational medium.[11]
The resulting transformation illustrates that the Energy's appearance and supernatural properties can change according to characterization. The colorless state represents the foundation before differentiation, while the characterized state reflects the influence of the power being expressed.

#### Higher-Order Energy
Attributeless Energy possesses a foundational relationship with conventional supernatural systems, allowing it to be regarded as a Higher-Order Energy. Its properties cannot be safely controlled through the same methods used to dominate ordinary attributed Energies.[7]
[[Violet Elderblood Snow]] demonstrated this distinction through her Divine Authority over Order. She successfully manifested a tiny quantity of the Energy by directing Order toward the desired result, but the process was extraordinarily inefficient. Forcibly compelling the Energy to obey did not provide the efficient manipulation required for ordinary practical use.[7]
The demonstration also affected the supernatural powers of nearby observers. [[Dun Scaith Elderblood]] experienced unusual behavior from the Runes incorporated into her clothing, while [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]] perceived substantial changes in her [[Divine Energy]] and [[Magic]]. [[Kali Elderblood]] and [[Hela Elderblood]] recognized the significance of the foundation being displayed, with Hela distinguishing the phenomenon from her existing understanding of The End.[7]
Their reactions established the exceptional nature of Attributeless Energy, but its higher-order classification does not mean that any arbitrary quantity automatically surpasses every Concept, Divine Authority or foreign cosmological power. Its effects remain dependent upon the form in which it is used and the capabilities of the practitioner.

### Mechanics

#### Creating Attributeless Energy
Practitioners who lack Melida's instinctive affinity must deliberately approach the Attributeless state through Energies they already control. The process involves gathering mastered Energies and progressively removing the characteristics that define them until the remaining Energy no longer possesses an assigned attribute.[2]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s initial training method required [[Scathach]] to collect the Energies under her control, form a sphere and gradually transform that sphere toward a state without characteristics. The difficulty lay not in the conceptual complexity of the instruction, but in the extraordinary precision necessary to perform it without destabilizing the result.[2]
The practitioner's own existence presents one of the greatest obstacles. Race, personality, Divinities, natural affinities, instincts and established supernatural powers can influence the Energy during conversion. Even after removing an existing characteristic, the resulting foundation may immediately receive another property originating from the practitioner. Such unintended characterization can produce effects radically different from those of properly stabilized Attributeless Energy.[3]
Successful creation therefore requires the practitioner to maintain an unusually neutral interaction with the Energy while preventing both the original and newly introduced characteristics from defining it prematurely. This demand explains why exceptional ordinary Energy control is a prerequisite for even beginning the technique.

#### Attribute Imposition
Once the Attributeless state has been properly stabilized, the practitioner can deliberately assign an understood characteristic. The resulting Energy acquires the properties associated with that characteristic and becomes suitable for the intended application.[2]
Knowledge is essential to this process. The practitioner must possess sufficient reference points to understand what is being reproduced. Simply knowing the name of an Energy or having witnessed its appearance does not necessarily provide the comprehension required to accurately recreate its properties.[2]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ability to produce pure [[Primordial Energy]] demonstrates the importance of accumulated references. His experience observing Primordial Energy through Jeanne, his daughters and the [[Primordial Entities]] gave him sufficient information to reproduce its characteristics using the Attributeless foundation.[2]
The same principle applies to [[Divine Energy]]. Victor can draw a portion of the foundational Energy and give it the characteristics of Divine Energy without relying exclusively upon an independently maintained Divine reserve. Such conversion illustrates the relationship between a common Energy foundation and the differentiated systems that emerge through characterization.[5]

#### Supplemental Characterization
Attributeless Energy can also be used to reinforce an already established supernatural technique. Instead of requiring the practitioner to generate an entirely new Energy manifestation from the beginning, the Attributeless foundation can receive the characteristics of an existing attack or ability and become an additional source of power for it.[3]
This approach is considerably more practical for most qualified practitioners than [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s complete internal restructuring. The original technique supplies the defining characteristic, while the supplementary Attributeless Energy provides additional volume and output. The user can therefore achieve a result that would ordinarily require a much larger quantity of specialized Energy.[3]
The effect is particularly valuable when the characterized Energy is difficult to produce or maintain. By reducing the quantity of specialized Energy required, the practitioner can conserve reserves while increasing the scale of an attack.

#### Return to the Beginning
The conversion process can also operate in the opposite direction. Instead of giving characteristics to Attributeless Energy, an existing characterized structure can lose the properties defining its current state and return toward the underlying foundation.[2]
This phenomenon became apparent during [[Scathach]]'s first attempt to produce Attributeless Energy. Her conversion destabilized, causing the Energy to explode and alter the superficial layer of her skin. The affected tissue did not suffer ordinary burning or disintegration. Instead, its established physical state was removed as the skin returned to an Attributeless Energy condition.[2]
The result was far more dangerous than a conventional wound. Scathach possessed extraordinary regeneration and resistance to physical injury, but the affected area could not be restored through ordinary healing. Its original state had been disrupted at a level deeper than the replacement of damaged tissue.[2]
The phenomenon is described as a return to the beginning because the affected structure loses the characteristics defining its current manifestation. It is not equivalent to simply breaking matter apart or reducing a substance to another recognizable physical form.

Scathach's injury demonstrated that even an exceptionally powerful Dragon Goddess and Grandmaster could encounter consequences beyond ordinary regeneration. Although Victor initially attempted to produce replacement tissue through several of his Divinities, the new material did not properly correspond to the original portion of her body. The affected area behaved as though the Record associated with that part of Scathach's existence had been disrupted.[2]
The problem concerned more than physical appearance. Her body and Soul were connected through the structure of her existence, and the altered tissue could not be treated as an ordinary wound. A replacement created through conventional Divine techniques remained different from the original structure because it lacked the appropriate existential connection.[2]
Victor ultimately addressed the injury by manipulating the same foundational Energy involved in the accident. Rather than merely creating another layer of skin, he restored the structure through a process compatible with the level at which the original damage had occurred.[2]

#### Unintended Characterization
Attributeless Energy does not naturally possess a fixed Return characteristic. The reversion effect observed during [[Scathach]]'s accident resulted from the interaction between the unstable Energy and the characteristics imposed upon it through her own existence.[3]
The user's nature can influence the resulting Energy even when that influence is not consciously intended. Scathach's destructive characteristics affected her failed conversion, turning what should have been a neutral foundational state into a dangerous manifestation with destructive consequences.[3]
This distinction is essential because it prevents the accidental effect from being treated as a universal property of every properly controlled application. An Attributeless foundation can support numerous different characteristics, and the result depends upon what has been imposed upon it.

#### Fundamental Reconstruction
Creator's Energy Mastery possesses considerable reconstructive potential when the practitioner understands the foundational structure being manipulated. The restoration of [[Scathach]]'s injury required more than recreating visible tissue; [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had to restore the appropriate connection between the affected physical structure and her existing state.[2]
The reconstruction potential differs from ordinary healing because it concerns the definition of the structure rather than simply repairing damage within its established form. A sufficiently capable practitioner may be able to address problems that conventional regeneration or Divine Creation cannot resolve, provided they possess the necessary understanding and control over the relevant foundation.[2]
However, the ability to repair Scathach's injury does not automatically establish unrestricted restoration of every erased being, Concept or Soul Record. The demonstrated application involved a specific consequence of an unstable Attributeless conversion, resolved through Victor's exceptional understanding of the process responsible.

#### Difference from The End
Main article: [[True Death]]
The reversion associated with Attributeless Energy bears a superficial resemblance to the effects of The End, the Divine Authority governing termination and erasure. Both can produce results that cannot be corrected through ordinary regeneration, but their mechanisms are not established as identical.[2]
The End imposes the authority of termination upon a target. Attributeless reversion instead removes the characteristics defining an existing structure and returns the affected Energy toward the foundational state preceding differentiation.[2]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] considered the possibility that The End might preserve or employ a specialized aspect of the process through which characterized Energy returns to its original condition. Such a relationship would help explain certain similarities between the observed effects, but it remains a proposed interpretation rather than a confirmed equivalence between The End and Attributeless Energy.[2]

### Mastery System
Creator's Energy Mastery possesses three established levels: Beginner, Proficient and Master. These stages describe progressively greater control over the foundational Energy. The technique as a whole is separately classified as a Higher-Level Technique, placing it above the Elderblood family's established Grandmaster [[Martial Arts]] category.[3]

#### Beginner
The Beginner stage introduces the practitioner to the direct production and stabilization of Attributeless Energy. Entry into this level requires an extraordinary degree of pre-existing Energy control, approaching perfection in the user's established supernatural powers.[3]
Training involves suppressing the characteristics of familiar Energies while preserving a stable foundation. Because a failed conversion can fundamentally alter the user's own body or surroundings, the practitioner must learn to recognize instability and maintain control before attempting greater quantities.[2][3]
[[Scathach]]'s experience demonstrates that exceptional physical power and martial accomplishments do not remove these requirements. Her status as a Grandmaster and Dragon Goddess provided considerable experience but did not prevent a dangerous failure during her first attempt.[2]
The Beginner stage is consequently not equivalent to ordinary introductory training. It represents a level of precision attainable only by individuals whose mastery of conventional Energy systems is already extraordinarily advanced.

#### Proficient
The Proficient stage permits the practitioner to employ Attributeless Energy as a supplement to established supernatural abilities. Rather than requiring the user to recreate every desired Energy from the foundational state, the existing technique supplies the appropriate characteristics, which are then applied to the supplementary Energy.[3]
This significantly increases the efficiency of attacks and other applications. A comparatively small quantity of specialized Energy can establish the required properties, while a larger quantity of Attributeless Energy supplies additional power after receiving those characteristics.[3]

The result illustrated the possibility of achieving a much larger effect while conserving specialized Energy that would otherwise have been consumed in producing it.[3]
This stage is regarded as the most practical objective for qualified Elderblood family members. Its advantages are substantial without requiring the practitioner to reconstruct the fundamental architecture of their own existence.

#### Master
The Master stage involves maintaining Attributeless Energy as a primary foundation rather than merely an external supplement. The practitioner can stabilize the undefined Energy continuously and characterize it according to the needs of their established abilities.[3]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] possesses the necessary compatibility to employ this method because of his exceptional existence, vast knowledge and capacity for parallel mental processing. His control allows him to manipulate a foundation that would ordinarily be contaminated by the natural characteristics of the individual attempting to wield it.[3]
The requirements are sufficiently extreme that unrestricted Master-level use is compared to the position of a [[Primordial Chaos]] capable of creating an entire Universe. This describes the degree of compatibility necessary to operate the foundational Energy without suffering the consequences normally associated with such manipulation.[3]
For ordinary practitioners, simply increasing Energy control does not guarantee eventual access to [[Poseidon|the Master]] stage. The fundamental nature of the user must also permit direct interaction with an Energy foundation that resists ordinary characterization and can destabilize structures when improperly controlled.

[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and [[Scathach]] placed Creator's Energy Mastery above their existing Grandmaster [[Martial Arts]] classification because its requirements and effects operated beyond ordinary advances in combat technique. ### Victor's Special Compatibility

#### Parallel Thoughts
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] possesses the ability to divide his mental activity into enormous numbers of parallel thought processes. This allows him to sustain several complicated supernatural operations while maintaining awareness and control over his other responsibilities.[3]
During the early development of Creator's Energy Mastery, a substantial portion of his parallel mental processing was devoted exclusively to preserving the Attributeless foundation. These processes prevented Victor's own characteristics from contaminating the Energy and allowed him to impose different properties whenever required.[3]
The mental demand was considerable even for Victor. Maintaining the foundational Energy reduced the amount of processing capacity available for unrelated tasks, demonstrating that Master-level control was not initially effortless despite his exceptional advantages.[3]
Repeated use gradually increased his familiarity with the process. Operations that originally required conscious allocation of parallel thoughts became increasingly instinctive, allowing him to devote less deliberate attention to maintaining the Energy's stable condition.[6]

#### Eldritch Nature
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Eldritch existence provides an unusual degree of compatibility with powers that would normally conflict with the established rules of native Creation. His nature as an Eldritch God allows him to exist through contradictions that ordinary beings cannot safely accommodate.[3]
This is particularly important when controlling Attributeless Energy. Ordinary practitioners are required to suppress the characteristics defining their own existence, an operation that becomes increasingly difficult as their supernatural nature grows more powerful. Victor can preserve his individuality while simultaneously maintaining a foundational Energy state free from those characteristics through his parallel processing and unusual existential structure.[3]
His Eldritch nature therefore supplies an advantage independent of conventional skill. Even beings possessing comparable Energy reserves or long experience as Gods may lack the necessary compatibility to reproduce his methods.

#### Position Analogous to Primordial Chaos
Within the Great [[Chaos]], [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] occupies the supreme position associated with the Creator of his own Eldritch order. His relationship with the native Universe's Attributeless Energy resembles that of a new Painter acquiring the tools previously employed by the original Creator.[3]
This correspondence does not make Victor literally identical to the original [[Primordial Chaos]]. Rather, his status provides a functional relationship with foundational Energy that ordinary native Gods do not naturally possess. He can manipulate the Creator's medium without abandoning his own identity in the manner that would ordinarily be required to suppress every defining characteristic.[3]
Victor considers this privilege central to his Master-level efficiency. Without such compatibility, even he would have regarded Attributeless Energy primarily as a supplemental resource rather than attempting to rebuild his Energy system around it.[3]

#### Accumulated Knowledge
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s enormous collection of supernatural powers supplies the reference points necessary to characterize Attributeless Energy into numerous different forms. His experience with Divine, Demonic, Draconic, Primordial, Positive, Negative and other Energy systems allows him to understand the characteristics required for their reproduction.[1][2]
Melida's natural talent revealed the foundational principle, but Victor's accumulated experience permitted immediate practical development. His knowledge of existing Energy systems became considerably more valuable after he discovered that they could be understood as differentiated manifestations of a shared foundation.[1]
This advantage is not equivalent to automatic knowledge. Victor can reproduce Energies he sufficiently understands, while unfamiliar systems may still require observation, experimentation or the acquisition of additional reference points.

The scope and reliability of deliberate offensive reversion remain dependent upon the practitioner's control and the characteristics involved.

The same foundational understanding that permits reversion can support reconstruction of affected structures. This application operates at a deeper level than ordinary replacement of damaged matter, allowing the original relationship between a structure and its existence to be restored when the practitioner possesses sufficient understanding of the alteration.[2]

### Relationship with Runes and the Akashic Records
Main article: Runes
Main article: [[Akashic Records]]

#### Access to Creation's Foundation
Attributeless Energy and Runes are related through their connection to the underlying structure of Creation, although they are not the same technique. Attributeless Energy provides a foundational medium from which characterized Energy can be produced, while Runes are languages of power through which practitioners issue instructions to Reality.[5]
A Rune operates by expressing a command through the user's understanding of the world. Its effectiveness depends upon the nature of the language, the characteristics of the race employing it, the practitioner's knowledge and the Energy or Authority supporting the instruction.[5]
The relationship with the [[Akashic Records]] provides another means of understanding the process. The Records preserve information concerning the structures and functions of Reality. A practitioner using an appropriate Rune can access or invoke a portion of that information to produce the intended effect.[5]
For example, a Fire Rune employs the relevant supernatural language to produce an effect associated with Fire. The outcome depends upon the practitioner's understanding and the mechanism through which the Rune accesses Reality's structure. This differs from directly producing Fire-characterized Energy through the Attributeless foundation, although both operations benefit from deeper knowledge of the phenomenon being expressed.[5]

#### Conventional Runes
Conventional Rune systems generally operate within the restrictions established by their respective supernatural traditions. Norse Runes can assign properties, reinforce objects or establish protections, while exceptionally accomplished practitioners can integrate Rune instructions into complex techniques and personal abilities.[5]
[[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] possesses sufficient mastery to employ Runes far beyond the applications normally demonstrated by conventional practitioners. Her expertise allows her to integrate supernatural instructions directly into her combat techniques and other powers.[5]
The effectiveness of these systems remains dependent upon the practitioner's understanding rather than merely possessing the appropriate symbols or language.

#### Draconic Runes
Draconic Runes originate from the language and supernatural nature of Dragons. Because the Dragon race possesses exceptional compatibility with the structures existing within native Creation, its language can be employed to influence Reality at a more fundamental level than many conventional Rune systems.[5]
Sufficiently advanced Draconic Rune practitioners can alter the structure of Reality through commands supported by their knowledge and Authority. [[Anna Elderblood]] is identified as having mastered this application, demonstrating that Runes can extend beyond ordinary magical inscriptions and become a means of directly affecting the underlying rules of existence.[5]
The versatility of Draconic Runes is therefore connected to the nature of Dragons and their compatibility with Creation rather than being an automatic property of every language of power.

#### Eldritch Runes
Eldritch Runes originate from the language and supernatural rules of Eldritch beings. Unlike native Rune systems, they operate through principles associated with existences originating outside the ordinary structure of the native Universe.[5]
Their use can impose foreign logic upon the surrounding Reality, distorting the local environment according to rules that do not naturally belong to it. Even a small number of such symbols can produce significant effects because they express the influence of an existence governed by a different cosmological framework.[5]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] consequently exercises caution when employing Eldritch Runes, limiting their number when unnecessary distortion of his surroundings would create additional complications.

#### Relationship with Magical Enchantment
[[Magic]], particularly Enchantment, can operate through principles comparable to Rune use. A magical inscription or enchantment provides instructions that influence an object or phenomenon according to the practitioner's understanding and the rules governing the relevant system.[5]
Such applications are generally less fundamental than advanced Draconic or Eldritch Runes. Nevertheless, they belong to the broader family of supernatural methods through which knowledge, language and Energy interact to influence Reality.

#### Understanding and Divine Authority
Possessing a Divine Concept associated with language does not automatically grant complete mastery of every Rune system. The effectiveness of Rune manipulation depends heavily upon understanding the structure of Reality and the principles through which the relevant language expresses commands.[5]
A God whose Divinity concerns language may possess abilities relevant to communication and symbolic expression without automatically acquiring the ability to rewrite Reality through unfamiliar Rune systems. The practitioner's knowledge remains essential.

#### Education of the Imperial Princesses
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters possess exceptional supernatural talents and are capable of using advanced Runes, but practical proficiency does not necessarily entail a complete understanding of the principles governing those techniques.[5]
Their education initially emphasized the use and mastery of Runes, with deeper theoretical instruction intended to follow once their practical development had sufficiently advanced. This approach allowed the princesses to employ sophisticated abilities before studying the complete relationship between Rune languages, the [[Akashic Records]] and the fundamental structure of Creation.[5]
The distinction also applies to Creator's Energy Mastery. Being capable of using advanced Runes or possessing extraordinary natural talent does not establish that an individual has mastered Attributeless Energy or qualifies for one of Victor's formal mastery stages.

Instead of maintaining every Energy as a separate principal source, he could assign characteristics to the common foundation according to the needs of his existing abilities.[6]

Main article: [[Victor Elderblood]]
The restructuring of Victor's Energy system contributed to a considerable increase in the scale of his unrestricted True Dragon form. Creator's Energy Mastery remained part of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s active power system after the initial discovery and training period. #### Sustained Expenditure and Physical Rest
The efficiency gained through Creator's Energy Mastery does not remove the need to replenish Energy. During the prolonged ritual surrounding the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s development and the maintenance of its Sector, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] experienced a condition in which his expenditure exceeded his replenishment.[13][14]
The strain arose from several simultaneous responsibilities, including sustaining the ritual and forcibly preventing the Sector from advancing through its weakened barrier. The resulting demand affected Victor's Energy reserves and the systems connected to his body, including his [[Dragon Heart]] and associated [[World Trees]].[13][14]
Victor responded by allowing his physical body to rest while continuing to operate through his Soul. This reduced the expenditure associated with his physical existence without requiring him to abandon the essential responsibilities he was maintaining.[14]
[[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]] subsequently restored a portion of his reserves through her extraordinary wish-related power and the Universe's response. Approximately twenty percent of Victor's Energy was replenished, but the reserves continued to decline because the underlying expenditure remained greater than the rate of recovery.[14]
The incident demonstrates that improved Energy efficiency and unified control are not equivalent to limitless self-generation. Even a Master capable of directly manipulating the Creator's foundational Energy remains subject to expenditure when sustaining sufficiently demanding operations.

#### Attributeless Energy and Primordial Energy
The principal distinction between Attributeless and [[Primordial Energy]] concerns the presence of assigned characteristics. Attributeless Energy remains undefined, while Primordial Energy possesses properties associated with making Creation function.[5]

### Limitations and Risks
Creator's Energy Mastery provides exceptional versatility and efficiency, but its operation is subject to significant limitations.
- Knowledge Requirement: The practitioner must possess sufficient understanding of an Energy's characteristics before accurately reproducing it. Attributeless Energy does not grant automatic knowledge of every supernatural system.[2]
- Extreme Control Requirement: Nearly perfect ordinary Energy control is necessary even to begin safely manipulating the foundational state.[3]
- Unintended Characterization: Race, personality, ideology, Divinities, instincts and existing powers can unintentionally impose characteristics upon the Energy, producing effects different from those intended.[3]
- Instability: An unsuccessful conversion can release destructive Energy or alter existing structures in ways conventional supernatural defenses cannot easily correct.[2]
- Physical and Existential Damage: [[Scathach]]'s failed attempt altered her skin at a foundational level, demonstrating that the consequences can extend beyond ordinary bodily injury.[2]
- Soul and Record Interference: Severe reversion may disrupt the normal relationship between a structure and its existential Record, preventing conventional reconstruction from restoring the original state.[2]
- Mental Processing Demand: Sustained Master-level control originally required [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to devote a substantial portion of his parallel thoughts to maintaining the Attributeless foundation.[3]
- Existential Compatibility: Complete Master-level use requires more than conventional skill. The practitioner's fundamental existence must be compatible with maintaining an Energy state free from unintended characterization.[3]
- Foreign-Cosmology Uncertainty: Beings originating outside the native Universe, including Azathoth, are not automatically confirmed to possess compatibility with its Creator's Energy.[3]
- Restricted Progression: The Beginner and Proficient stages require proper supervision, while Master-level experimentation is subject to additional safeguards because of its risks.[3]
- Energy Expenditure: The technique improves efficiency but does not eliminate the consumption of Energy. Sustained operations can exceed even Victor's replenishment rate.[13][14]
- No Automatic Conceptual Authority: Reproducing a form of Energy does not automatically grant ownership of every Divine Concept associated with that Energy.[5]
- No Automatic Omnipotence: Access to the Creator's foundational Energy does not confer the Creator's omnipotence, omniscience or omnipresence upon its practitioner.[5]
- No Universal Superiority: Its status as Higher-Order Energy does not establish that every manifestation automatically defeats stronger beings, foreign cosmological systems or all conceptual powers.[7]
- Dependence upon the User: The ultimate scale of any application depends upon the practitioner's available Energy, knowledge, control, Authority and compatibility with the characteristic being imposed.[2][3]

### Canon Clarifications
Expand terminology and mechanical distinctions
Creator's Energy and [[Chaos]] Energy are identical. — The original Creator bears the identity of [[Primordial Chaos]], but the Attributeless Energy employed in Creation is not automatically identical to every later manifestation of Chaos Energy.[1]
Positivity and Negativity are simply two names for Attributeless Energy. — Positivity and Negativity are divided aspects of the original Creator. Attributeless Energy is the medium produced and employed by that Being, not the complete identity of his two halves.[10][5]
Attributeless Energy naturally possesses the characteristic of Return. — The reversion observed during [[Scathach]]'s accident resulted from unstable characterization and the influence of her own destructive nature. Return is not established as a permanent defining attribute of the properly stabilized foundational Energy.[3]
Attributeless Energy and The End perform identical operations. — Their effects can resemble one another, but the mechanisms are distinguished. Any deeper relationship between The End and foundational reversion remains an interpretation rather than a confirmed identity.[2]
Grandmaster is an additional stage of Creator's Energy Mastery. — The formal stages are Beginner, Proficient and Master. Higher-Level Technique is the classification assigned to the system as a whole.[3]
Every Elderblood family member possesses Creator's Energy Mastery. — The technique is a Family Secret, but access to knowledge, qualification for training and practical mastery remain different matters.[3]
Violet has mastered Creator's Energy. — Violet produced a small quantity through Order but recognized that her method was extremely inefficient. Her demonstration does not establish formal Master-level proficiency.[7]
Azathoth is a confirmed user of the native Creator's Energy. — Her exceptional nature suggests possible compatibility, but her origin in another Cosmos prevents that compatibility from being treated as confirmed.[3]
Every Universe Creator is confirmed to possess the Elderblood technique. — The requirements for Master-level compatibility are compared with the position of a Primordial Chaos, but the formal Creator's Energy Mastery system was developed by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Scathach. No complete list of equivalent practitioners across other Universes has been established.[3]
Every use of Attributeless Energy erases Soul Records. — Record-related consequences were observed during Scathach's unstable conversion. They are not established as an unavoidable result of every properly controlled use.[2]
Creator's Energy alone can destroy half of the Universe. — Victor's enormous conceptual threat originated from his Authority over Negativity, with Attributeless Energy serving as the foundation through which that power was characterized and expressed.[11]
[[Eldritch Energy]] is simply another ordinary native Energy attribute. — Eldritch Energy originates outside the native Universe and can impose foreign rules upon its Reality. Its comparison with a foundational Energy from another Universe does not make it identical to the native Creator's Energy.[5]
[[Primordial Energy]] and Attributeless Energy are completely unrelated. — The two are distinguished by their attributed and unattributed states, while their connection is sufficiently fundamental that Victor and Melida can manipulate the difference with exceptional ease.[5]
Rune mastery confirms Creator's Energy Mastery. — Runes and Attributeless Energy are related through Creation's foundations, but they employ different methods. A practitioner may possess sophisticated Rune abilities without qualifying for Victor's formal Energy mastery stages.[5]
Attributeless Energy grants omnipotence. — The Energy is the foundational medium employed by the original Creator, but its practitioners remain constrained by knowledge, control, compatibility, available power and the nature of the intended manifestation.[5]

### Development Summary
The development of Creator's Energy Mastery encompasses several distinct stages, beginning with the recognition of a common Energy origin and progressing toward the incorporation of that foundation into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s own existence.
Development
Significance
Recognition of a Common Origin
The relationship between the many differentiated supernatural Energies and the original Creator's foundational Energy becomes apparent through the principle that Energy remains fundamentally Energy despite the characteristics separating its manifestations.[8]
Melida's Natural Manipulation
Melida demonstrates the ability to begin with undefined Energy and assign characteristics instinctively, revealing the missing foundation behind Victor's accumulated knowledge.[4][1]
Foundational Conversion
Victor develops a deliberate method of producing Attributeless Energy and demonstrates its transformation into [[Primordial Energy]].[2]

[[Scathach]]'s failed attempt reveals the dangers of unstable conversion and the possibility of restoring damaged structures through the same foundational Energy.[2]
Formal Mastery Classification
Creator's Energy Mastery becomes a restricted Higher-Level Technique with Beginner, Proficient and Master stages.[3]
Order-Assisted Interaction
Violet demonstrates the Energy's Higher-Order nature and establishes the inefficiency of attempting to dominate it through brute-force conceptual Authority.[7]
Unified Internal Foundation
Victor reorganizes his Energy infrastructure around the Attributeless state, greatly improving efficiency and contributing to the expansion of his unrestricted True Dragon form.[6]
Advanced Cosmic Application
Non-Attributed Energy remains an active component of Victor's abilities, including his use of [[Junketsu]] against the boundary separating his Sector from the Higher Sectors.[12]

Victor imposes Negativity upon his colorless foundational Energy, demonstrating compatibility with an exceptionally high-order Concept.[11]

Victor directly produces [[Divine Energy]] from Attributeless Energy and clarifies its relationship with Primordial Energy, foreign [[Eldritch Energy]], Runes and the [[Akashic Records]].[5]
