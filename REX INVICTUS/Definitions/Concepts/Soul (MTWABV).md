### Souls

### Ownership

#### Creator
[[The Judges of The Abyss]]
A Soul is the metaphysical foundation and personal record of an existence in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]]. Rather than containing only consciousness or memories, a Soul stores the information constituting the Being itself: personality, emotions, thoughts, desires, experiences, physical characteristics, racial characteristics and the deeper information composing the individual's existence.[1][2][3]
The physical body and Soul are closely connected but are not the same thing. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] describes the Soul as the record of the Being and the body as its receptacle.[2] Consequently, sufficiently advanced Soul Manipulation can do far more than alter memories: it can modify biology, race, physical characteristics, supernatural qualities, Divinity and other aspects of existence by changing the information from which the Being is defined.[4][5][3]
Souls are also one of the fundamental components of Creation's cycle of Life, Death and Reincarnation. The Judges of The Abyss, the Regulator associated with Souls, create, judge and administer Souls together with the systems maintained by The Universal Tree and the various afterlives.[6][7]

### Nature of the Soul

#### Record of an Existence
The most consistent description of a Soul is that of a record. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] originally described a Soul as a set of information storing everything accumulated during a Being's life.[1] He later expressed the principle more directly: the Soul is the record of the Being, while the physical body is the receptacle in which that record manifests.[2]
Victor's later mastery of Soul Manipulation revealed the breadth of this information. A Soul contains an individual's personality, emotions, thoughts, desires, physical aspects and core information of existence.[3] Memories are therefore only one component of a Soul. The Soul represents the complete metaphysical information defining what the individual presently is.

This informational nature explains why Soul-level injuries can persist even when a body is physically reconstructed. If the underlying record has been deleted, reproducing the material that once occupied that location does not necessarily restore what was lost.[5]

#### Soul and Identity
A Soul and the identity currently recorded within it are related but not completely synonymous. The distinction is most clearly demonstrated by [[Vanessa Alekerth]]. During ordinary reincarnation processing, almost all of the information constituting "Vanessa Alekerth" had already been removed from her surviving Soul. The Soul remained in existence, but the memories and information defining Vanessa as that particular person had nearly disappeared.[8]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] restored Vanessa because the [[Akashic Records]] still preserved the historical information belonging to her former existence. By returning that information to the surviving Soul, he reconstructed the identity that had nearly been erased.[8]

#### Soul Memory and Instinct
Conscious memory and deeper Soul information can also be distinguished. [[Melida Charlotte Elderblood]], [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s reincarnation of [[Merlin]], did not consciously remember everything belonging to her previous existence, yet Victor observed that her Soul remembered the path she had previously walked. This manifested as instinctive familiarity and accelerated development despite the absence of equivalent conscious memories.[10]
This does not mean normal reincarnated individuals automatically retain usable memories or abilities. Melida was an exceptional, deliberately constructed reincarnation. It does, however, establish that information embedded in a Soul can influence instinct and aptitude without appearing as ordinary conscious recollection.

#### Universality
Souls are not exclusive to Humans, supernatural races or even conventionally sentient life. During the explanation of the afterlife system, it is stated that everything possesses a Soul, including Humans, supernatural beings, animals, plants and even existences such as mountains.[6]
Conscious thought is therefore not the requirement that produces a Soul. Souls are tied more fundamentally to existence and to the machinery through which Creation maintains Life, Death and Reincarnation.

### Cosmological Origin and Function

#### The Primordial Authority of Soul
Main article: [[The Judges of The Abyss]]
The Judges of The Abyss are the Regulator associated with the universal function of Soul. Although commonly introduced as the entities responsible for judging the dead, judgment is only one component of their work. The Judges directly state that one of their responsibilities is creating Souls.[7]
Their work is closely linked to Life. The division described by the Judges is:
LIFE
The Universal Tree creates and maintains the living form and the greater continuation of Life.
＋
SOUL
The Judges of The Abyss provide the form with a Soul and administer its spiritual existence.
[7]
The two functions cooperate without being identical. Life produces and maintains living existence, while Soul provides the metaphysical record participating in that existence.

#### Soul as an Aspect of Existence
The importance of Soul extends beyond individual beings. Regulators are not merely users of their respective powers; they represent essential functions required by the Universe. [[Azathoth Elderblood]] explained that eliminating the Primordial function of Soul would cause Souls themselves to cease existing, in the same manner that removing Life would cause Life to cease and removing Balance would eliminate Balance.[11]
COSMIC PRINCIPLE
The existence of individual Souls is therefore supported by a deeper universal function. "Soul" is not merely a supernatural organ shared by living beings; it is one of the fundamental operations of the native Cosmos.[11]

### Structure and Properties

#### Soul Core
A deeper region of spiritual existence is repeatedly described as the Soul Core. The complete internal anatomy of an ordinary Soul has never been exhaustively mapped, and exceptional Souls can possess structures that should not be generalized to everyone. Nevertheless, the Core is consistently treated as a particularly fundamental region.
Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood's [[Soul Marriage]], a structure genuinely connected to a portion of Aphrodite's Soul Core manifested inside Victor's Inner World. Damage to that structure was expected to directly harm Aphrodite herself.[12]
For Gods, the importance is even clearer. Divinity is located within the Core of a Divine Soul, and forcibly removing that Divinity is effectively equivalent to killing the individual as a God.[13] Victor later demonstrated this principle upon Ra, directly editing the Primordial God's Soul, extracting his Solar Divinity and reducing him to a mortal state before repairing enough of the remaining Soul to keep him alive.[4]

#### Size, Quality, Strength and Maturity
Souls do not possess a single universal level of development. They can differ in size, quality, strength, resilience and maturity.
Vampire Progenitors provide an important distinction between size and quality. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Progenitor Soul possessed an enormous capacity and was described as potentially larger than the Soul of a God, yet its originally mortal nature meant that a true Divine Soul remained superior in quality.[12]
A larger Soul is therefore not automatically metaphysically superior in every respect.
Very old supernatural existences can also display increasingly mature Souls. When examining [[Zaladrac Zeovnur Elderblood]], Victor observed that ancient supernatural Souls become increasingly solid and brilliant, allowing spiritual perception to reveal the maturity accumulated across an immense lifespan.[14] This observation concerned extraordinary long-lived supernatural beings and should not be treated as a universal visual aging formula applying identically to every race.

#### Soul Form
A Soul can possess a form reflecting the underlying nature of the Being. This becomes especially explicit among Eldritch existences.
Following [[Nero Elderblood]]'s Eldritch transformation, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] inspected her Soul and found it healthy but still stabilizing. [[Nero]] possessed an actual Soul Form reflecting her deeper Eldritch nature and her connection to Apocalypse.[15]
Victor possesses an even stranger Soul Form. His deeper Eldritch existence is effectively formless and capable of assuming radically different shapes, while his preferred humanoid state reflects the identity he chooses to maintain rather than an absolute limitation upon what his Soul can become.[15]
EXCEPTIONAL PROPERTY
The existence of Eldritch Soul Forms does not establish that every ordinary Soul possesses an equally elaborate physical-looking form. Nero and Victor are abnormal higher existences whose spiritual nature visibly reflects what they have become.

#### Inner Worlds and Internal Structures
Powerful Souls can develop internal spiritual spaces, but their complexity varies enormously between individuals. Vampire Progenitors possess enough capacity to absorb and contain other Souls, while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Inner World eventually developed into a complete cosmological structure.[12][16]
Victor's later Soul contains superficial territories, personal worlds, [[World Trees]], multiple internal layers, a Soul Core and an even deeper Core associated with his Eldritch existence.[16] This architecture is an exceptional property of Victor rather than the confirmed standard anatomy of every Soul.

### Soul and Physical Existence

#### The Body as Receptacle
The physical body functions as the receptacle through which the information of a Soul is manifested.[2] Death or destruction of the body therefore does not automatically imply destruction of the Soul, while damage directly affecting the Soul can remain even when a body is physically repaired.
One of the clearest demonstrations occurred when unstable Attributeless Energy erased part of the information representing [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]]'s skin from her Soul. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was capable of recreating the missing tissue physically, but the replacement was not truly recognized as [[Scathach]]'s because the spiritual record connecting that tissue to her existence had disappeared. The defect remained even between different bodily forms because the error existed in the Soul rather than the flesh.[5]

#### Race and Biology
Race is not encoded exclusively in conventional biology. Racial characteristics can exist as information within a Soul and can consequently be rewritten by sufficiently advanced Soul Manipulation.[2][3]
Vampire Progenitors possess a particularly intuitive relationship with this mechanism. Their Blood functions as a medium through which body, Life and Soul can be influenced, allowing a Progenitor to alter both physical and spiritual characteristics.[2][17]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually reduced racial alteration to an informational process. Because the Soul contains the information defining the complete individual, transforming one race into another can be accomplished by altering or adding the required information and supplying enough Energy to sustain the resulting existence.[3]
Previous characteristics do not necessarily disappear completely. After Victor's conversion from Human to Vampire, [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] observed that qualities associated with Humanity—particularly adaptability and extraordinary potential—remained embedded within Victor's Soul despite his new racial state.[18]
By Chapter 1168, this principle had expanded into Victor's own reproductive Bloodline. Victor explained that the universal compatibility of Dragons, his predatory Bloodline and his powers related to Soul and Beginning allow his descendants to absorb and retain characteristics from both parental races rather than simply inheriting one side at the expense of the other.[19]

#### Soul-to-Vessel Compatibility
A Soul cannot necessarily be inserted into any available body. The vessel must possess sufficient compatibility with the nature and strength of the Soul it contains.
This became especially important during experiments involving fragments of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul. Even extremely small fragments retained enough of his nature to become hazardous to ordinary bodies and materials.[20] Further experimentation established that ordinary supernatural durability alone was not enough; a vessel required genuine compatibility with the Soul itself.[21]
Victor's wives and descendants were considerably more suitable because their existences were already connected to his Blood, Energy, Soul or Bloodline.[21]
A Soul fragment is therefore not equivalent to a neutral quantity of spiritual Energy. It remains a portion of the original Being and carries properties belonging to that existence.

#### Mortal and Supernatural Souls
Ordinary living creatures possess non-[[Divine Souls]] participating in the conventional cycle of Life and Reincarnation. Supernatural races can possess stronger and more developed Souls than ordinary Humans, and exceptionally old supernatural beings can display considerable spiritual maturity.[14]
Raw spiritual strength is nevertheless different from existential quality. An extraordinary supernatural Soul can be enormous without automatically becoming Divine.[12]

#### Progenitor Souls
Vampire Progenitors possess abnormally large Souls designed to interact with and contain other Souls. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s original Progenitor state allowed him to absorb Souls, retain their information and maintain an Inner World capable of processing the enormous amount of spiritual material accumulated within him.[1][12]
Their natural Soul authority is so intuitive that Victor later compared the Progenitor's access to stored Soul information with an extremely limited form of the [[Akashic Records]], restricted specifically to information accessible through Souls.[9]

#### Divine Souls
Main article: Gods
Main article: Divinity
Ascending into Godhood is not simply an increase in Energy. The entire Soul undergoes a metamorphosis. The characteristics accumulated through the individual's previous existence combine into a qualitatively new Divine state described as resembling a spiritual rebirth.[22]
A God therefore differs from a powerful mortal at the level of existence itself. Their Soul becomes Divine and their Divinity exists within its Core.[13][22]
The nature of the original Soul influences which Divine Concepts an individual can properly awaken. Personality, mentality, history, emotions and self-understanding contribute to conceptual compatibility. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] emphasized this while guiding [[Nero Elderblood]] toward her Divinity of Apocalypse: she could not properly embody the Concept without accepting the experiences and qualities making her who she was.[23]

#### Primordial Transformation
Main article: [[Primordial Entities]]
The difference between a Primordial God and a true Primordial Entity is fundamentally tied to the Soul.[24]
A Primordial God remains a Divine Being possessing and wielding a Concept. Progressing into a true Primordial Entity requires a qualitative transformation through which the Soul itself begins becoming the Concept. The individual ceases merely controlling the phenomenon and increasingly becomes integrated with the Aspect of Existence represented by it.[24]
[[Kali Elderblood]] was the clearest early example. Her mastery of Destruction had progressed so deeply that her Soul was gradually transforming into Destruction itself.[24]
ONTOLOGICAL CHANGE
Primordial transformation is not simply a higher quantity of [[Divine Energy]]. The Soul changes what kind of existence it is, eventually becoming inseparable from a universal function.[24][11]

#### Eldritch Souls
Main article: [[Outer Gods]]
Eldritch existence follows a different path from native Divine development. Becoming Eldritch does not inherently mean losing the Soul.
After [[Nero]]'s transformation, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] found her Soul present, powerful and robust, although it required time to stabilize after the enormous alteration to her existence.[15] Both Nero and Victor possess true Soul Forms reflecting their Eldritch nature.
Victor and [[Azathoth Elderblood]] possess an even more exceptional protection. Their Souls are described as inviolable to the native Primordial framework because they are foreign forms of [[Chaos]] that do not properly belong to the system governing the local Cosmos.[11]
This does not mean every Outer God has identical protection or Soul anatomy. It is a property specifically established for Victor and Azathoth's foreign Chaos nature.

#### Soul Restructuring
A sufficiently abnormal Soul can be reorganized on a massive scale without being destroyed. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] provides the clearest example.
During the development of his Inner Cosmos, [[Roxanne Elderblood]] and [[Amara Elderblood]] reorganized Victor's stored Powers and internal structures until his Soul contained separate regions associated with Powers, Connections and his deepest self. The sudden restructuring was significant enough to cause Victor physical injury in the outside world because the foundation of his existence itself was changing.[25]
His Soul changed again by Chapter 1111. Its previous binary structure had been reorganized around a single immense black central star containing his converged Energies, while his Divine Concepts existed around it as a connected stellar cluster.[26]
These structures are Victor-specific and should not be interpreted as standard stages through which ordinary Souls evolve.

### Soul Manipulation
Soul Manipulation encompasses powers capable of interacting directly with the Soul rather than merely affecting its physical receptacle. Because the Soul contains the information defining the individual, advanced Soul Manipulation can alter memories, identity, body, race, Powers, Divinity, spiritual integrity and even the Being's continued participation in Reincarnation.
The greatest native authority over Souls belongs to [[The Judges of The Abyss]].[6][7] Other beings—including Vampire Progenitors, Gods associated with Souls and highly advanced conceptual users—possess more limited or specialized methods.

#### Progenitor Soul Authority
Vampire Progenitors possess a natural affinity for dealing with Souls. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] describes Blood as a supernatural bargaining chip through which a Progenitor can interact with the body and Soul.[2]
Known applications include:
- Soul Absorption: Consuming another Being's Soul and obtaining access to accumulated memories, knowledge, experiences and techniques.[1][27]
- Soul Storage: Containing foreign Souls inside the Progenitor's own spiritual existence.[12][13]
- Soul Reading: Accessing information stored within absorbed or accessible Souls.[1][9]
- Soul Editing: Altering information contained within the Soul, including race and physical characteristics.[4][3]
- Soul Repair: Reconstructing damaged spiritual information when the manipulator possesses enough knowledge and appropriate Powers.[28][29][4]
- Soul Separation: Dividing composite, attached or fragmented spiritual structures into independent existences under appropriate circumstances.[29][30][31]

#### Soul Absorption
Consuming a Soul can provide far more than raw Energy. The accumulated experiences of the deceased remain recorded within it and can be accessed by the absorber.[1][27]
The process is dangerous. During the Demonic War, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] consumed numerous powerful Souls in rapid succession to acquire memories, techniques and Authority. Roxanne warned that processing too many at once could damage Victor's Soul without her support.[27]
Foreign Souls can also influence the absorber. Adonis' extraordinarily valuable Soul produced qualitative changes within Victor, while Victor's ability to safely isolate or filter later Souls became increasingly important as the quantity he contained grew.[1][27]
Soul consumption should therefore not be treated as a consequence-free method of acquiring memories or Powers.

#### Soul Repair and Reconstruction
Soul damage can be repaired when enough of the original existence remains and the user possesses sufficient knowledge and authority.
[[Metis]] Elderblood provides an early example. Her Soul had been damaged so severely that it began absorbing the Energy surrounding it in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Inner World in order to recompose itself. Roxanne helped stabilize the process, resulting in Metis recovering with a Dragon-shaped Soul and the Essence necessary for her new existence.[28]
Victor later repaired the spiritual damage affecting Natashia and the Souls attached to her, and he repaired enough of Ra's Soul to preserve his existence after forcibly removing his Divinity.[29][30][31][4]
The most extreme example occurred in Chapter 1158. A foreign entity was bound by an existential-level Soul Contract that annihilated both its body and Soul when it attempted to reveal restricted information. Victor refused to accept the destruction as final. Using Soul, Life, Beginning, Negativity, Time, Space, Eldritch power, Draconic Reality distortion and a prepared Runic environment, he forced the shattered Soul and body to reassemble and restored the entity to life.[32]
EXTREME EXCEPTION
Chapter 1158 does not establish that ordinary Soul destruction is casually reversible. The reconstruction required Victor to combine numerous fundamental and conceptual Powers inside a deliberately isolated laboratory prepared specifically to contain the target.[32]

#### Soul Division and Fragments
Souls can be separated into portions without every fragment immediately becoming neutral Energy.
The Judges themselves divide aspects of ordinary mortal Souls during afterlife processing.[6] Artificial fragmentation is also possible. Tiny fragments of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul used during Imperial research retained his nature so strongly that ordinary vessels could not safely contain them.[20][21]
A fragment can consequently preserve properties of the original Being despite representing only a tiny percentage of the complete Soul.

#### Independent Souls from Fragmentation
Spiritual fragmentation can develop into independent existence in exceptional cases.
Natashia contained multiple spiritual abnormalities. A surviving fragment belonging to [[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]] remained connected to her, while the personality that became Natasha developed into its own independent existence. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] repaired and separated the structures involved.[29][30][31]
Chapter 1169 later clarifies the distinction more explicitly: Natasha originally developed as an alternate personality but eventually formed her own Soul and form. Victor contrasted this with [[Roberta Elderblood]] and [[Medusa]] Elderblood, who were already two different individuals and Souls sharing the same body rather than one personality gradually becoming another Soul.[33]
This demonstrates that, under highly abnormal conditions, a sufficiently developed independent identity can become a genuinely separate spiritual existence.

#### Soul Damage
Damage directed at the Soul is more fundamental than ordinary bodily injury because it attacks the information from which the individual is defined.
Demonstrated consequences include:
- Destruction or loss of memories and identity.[9][8]
- Deletion of information defining physical characteristics.[5]
- Spiritual instability and fragmentation.[28][29]
- Loss or extraction of Divinity.[13][4]
- Incompatibility between Soul and body.[20][21]
- Damage severe enough to interfere with the normal reincarnation process.[6]
- Complete spiritual destruction.[32]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] previously treated truly shattering his own Soul as functionally equivalent to killing himself.[9] His later Eldritch development made his Soul vastly more resilient, but even Chapter 1176 retains this basic principle: detonating the Negativity integrated into his existence was described as effectively blowing apart half of his Soul, something that would seriously injure him despite his expectation that his enormous Eldritch Soul could eventually recover.[34]
CHARACTER THEORY
Nyx theorized that the Judges do not divide sufficiently powerful supernatural Souls into Positive and Negative portions because forcibly separating such strong Souls may cause severe spiritual damage and prevent their return to the Reincarnation Cycle. Nyx explicitly presented this as her own theory rather than a confirmed technical explanation from the Judges.[6]

### Incomplete, Composite and Altered Souls

#### Demons
Demons demonstrate what can happen when only part of a Soul remains.
During ordinary afterlife recycling, aspects associated with the negative side of a mortal Soul can remain within Hell after the useful or positive portion has been removed. Ordinary Demonic existence can arise from these spiritual remnants.[6][27]
Chapter 665 directly explains that Demons are incomplete Beings possessing only the bad portion of a Soul. A Demon must develop the missing half and become a more complete existence before naturally gaining the possibility of Divine Ascension.[27]
This does not mean every living person naturally possesses two fully independent "good" and "evil" Souls. The separation occurs as part of the processing and recycling of a previously unified Soul.

#### Empty Souls
An empty Soul is a Soul that remains after the information defining its former identity has largely been removed.
Vanessa's surviving Soul had reached approximately this condition before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] intervened. The underlying Soul still existed, but the information defining Vanessa had almost disappeared.[8]
This establishes that the spiritual foundation can survive the loss of its previous identity and later serve as the basis for another existence.

#### Composite Souls
Multiple spiritual structures can coexist within a single body under exceptional conditions. Natashia, Natasha and Carmila demonstrate one route through fragmentation and development, while Roberta and [[Medusa]] demonstrate two genuinely separate Souls sharing one physical vessel.[29][30][31][33]
These cases should not be treated as identical forms of "multiple personality." The novel distinguishes psychological identity, Soul fragmentation and multiple independent Souls.

#### Conceptual Pseudo-Souls
Chapter 1177 reveals that not every spiritual structure perceived as an apparent second Soul is necessarily a living individual.
During the pregnancies that produced Ariella Elderblood and [[Helvarya Elderblood]], [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his family initially believed the unusual spiritual structures indicated twins. Further investigation revealed that the apparent "other Soul" was not alive at all. It was the power of Creation and END respectively preparing an ideal host for the Concept.[35]
CHAPTER 1177 DEVELOPMENT
A sufficiently advanced Concept can therefore produce a developing spiritual structure that resembles a Soul closely enough to be mistaken for another living individual. Spiritual detection alone does not necessarily prove that every soul-like presence possesses independent life or identity.[35]

### Death, Judgment and the Afterlife
Main article: [[The Judges of The Abyss]]
Main article: [[The Core of the Afterlife]]
Physical death does not normally destroy the Soul. Instead, the Soul enters the larger infrastructure responsible for judgment, punishment, purification and eventual return to Life.[6]
Individual Pantheons possess Hells, Heavens, Paradises and Underworlds with different local rules, but these systems participate in the greater machinery administered beneath the authority of the Regulators.

#### Ordinary Mortal Processing
For ordinary mortal Souls, the Judges can separate different aspects of the Soul and direct them toward the appropriate destinations. Negative portions can undergo punishment and recycling in Hell while positive portions may enter a Heaven or Paradise until they are ready to return to the Reincarnation Cycle.[6]
The exact experience differs by Pantheon. Valhalla, for example, follows different procedures from the Greek or Biblical systems.[6]

#### Powerful Supernatural Souls
Sufficiently powerful supernatural beings can be treated differently. Their Souls are generally kept complete and judged as whole existences rather than automatically divided in the same manner as ordinary mortals.[6]
Gods receive an even more specialized judgment because they are Higher Existences. Depending upon the circumstances, a deceased God's Soul may be erased or returned to the Reincarnation Cycle rather than simply receiving the same destination as an ordinary mortal.[6]

### Reincarnation
Main article: [[Reincarnation System]]
Reincarnation does not ordinarily return the deceased person unchanged.
Before returning to Life, the information belonging to the previous identity is removed so that the Soul can become the foundation of a new existence rather than merely continuing its former life.[8]
Supernatural Souls are given particular attention because allowing powerful individuals to freely retain previous memories, identities and supernatural knowledge across repeated lives would create serious problems for the normal order.[8]
The general process is therefore:
Life → Death → Judgment → Afterlife / Purification → Identity Removal → Reincarnation → New Life
The system is maintained through the combined functions of the Judges, The Universal Tree, local afterlives and the Reincarnation System.[6][7][8]

#### Exceptions and Preserved Identity
Normal reincarnation deliberately removes previous identity information, but exceptions can be created.
Vanessa's identity was restored by returning her information from the [[Akashic Records]] to the surviving Soul.[8] Melida was deliberately created as [[Merlin]]'s reincarnation and retained Soul-level instinct even without equivalent conscious memory.[10]
These are artificial or extraordinary exceptions rather than the default behavior of Reincarnation.

### Soul and the Akashic Records
Main article: [[Akashic Records]]
The Akashic Records and Souls both preserve information, but they perform fundamentally different roles.
A Soul is the personal metaphysical record forming the active individual.[2] The Akashic Records preserve information concerning Creation itself, including the histories of existences whose active Souls may have changed, been processed or disappeared.[9][36]
The distinction is particularly clear in two cases.
[[Adonis Snow]] — [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] did not recover Adonis' original surviving Soul. Instead, he used information preserved within the Records to produce an informational recreation containing Adonis' recorded memories and personality. The result was closer to a living historical record than the conventional resurrection of the original Soul.[9]
[[Vanessa Alekerth]] — Vanessa's original Soul still existed, but its identity information had nearly been erased. Victor used the Akashic Records to restore that missing information to the actual Soul, allowing Vanessa herself to be reconstructed.[8]

### Soul Bonds

#### Soul Marriage
Main article: [[Soul Marriage]]
A Soul Marriage creates an exceptionally deep metaphysical connection between two otherwise independent Souls. It does not merge both people into one existence. Instead, their Souls become permanently bound in a manner intended to persist through death and reincarnation.[37]
The ritual can be performed by appropriate Divine or Conceptual Beings and incorporates Fate into the relationship. As long as the Concept of Soul continues existing, individuals joined by a genuine Soul Marriage are destined to find one another again even across subsequent lives.[37]
The connection also affects Vampiric Bloodlust. Two Vampires connected through Soul Marriage can remain spiritually satisfied by one another's presence to an extent that significantly reduces the severity of their normal hunger, although Bloodlust itself does not disappear.[37]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and [[Aphrodite]] provide one of the clearest structural demonstrations. After their Soul Marriage, a structure connected to Aphrodite's Soul Core appeared inside Victor's own Inner World, demonstrating that the union produced an actual metaphysical connection rather than a purely symbolic bond.[12]

#### Other Soul Connections
Not every Soul connection is a [[Soul Marriage]].
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul developed an entire region associated with his connections to other people, planets and [[World Trees]].[25] His wives can also perceive powerful changes in Victor's emotions through the Soul-level connections linking them to him.[38]
Such phenomena depend strongly upon the individuals involved and should not be treated as universal anatomy shared by all Souls.

### Soul Contracts and Ownership

#### Existential Soul Contracts
Soul Contracts can operate at a level far beyond ordinary promises or magical agreements.
The foreign entity captured in Chapter 1158 was bound by an existential-level Soul Contract. Attempting to disclose restricted information automatically destroyed both its physical body and Soul.[32]
The contract was powerful enough to activate repeatedly even after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] reconstructed the entity, forcing Victor to repeatedly restore the destroyed existence whenever forbidden information triggered the restriction.[32]
This establishes that sufficiently advanced contracts can be attached directly to the target's existence rather than merely punishing the body.

#### Imperial Soul Safeguards
The later [[Elderblood Empire]] incorporated Soul protection directly into military and civil infrastructure.
Imperial clone systems were designed so that their Souls belonged to the Emperor's structure. If a clone genuinely betrayed its purpose, safeguards could destroy the compromised existence and return the Soul to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] rather than allowing hostile forces to seize it.[39]
Victor also refused to allow soldiers serving him to have their Souls captured by enemy civilizations. By Chapter 1159, protection against spiritual capture had become an explicit part of Imperial planning, with the intention of extending similar safeguards throughout the Empire.[39]
These mechanisms are applications of Victor's enormous Soul authority and Imperial technology rather than properties naturally possessed by ordinary Souls.

### Exceptional Souls

#### Victor Elderblood
Main article: [[Victor Elderblood]]/Personal Universe
Victor Elderblood possesses the most extensively developed individual Soul presently demonstrated in the series.
His Soul began with the abnormal capacity of a Vampire Progenitor, allowing the absorption and storage of numerous Souls.[1][12] The integration of [[Roxanne Elderblood]], a World Tree rooted directly within him, dramatically accelerated its development by continuously supporting his body, Soul and Inner World.[12]
The Soul later developed real planetary and cosmological structures, was reorganized through the interaction of Roxanne and Amara, and eventually became sufficiently enormous to be described as a Universe of its own.[25][16]
By Chapter 1124, its known hierarchy included:
- More superficial spiritual territories containing portions of Victor's civilization and domains.
- A deeper personal planetary region.
- Regions containing his [[World Trees]].
- His Soul Core.
- A still deeper hidden Core housing his true Eldritch Form and Heralds.[16]
Because much of Victor's administration exists as part of his Soul, he possesses overwhelming awareness throughout these internal territories.[16]
His later Soul is also extraordinarily difficult to observe from outside. In Chapter 1175, even the System was incapable of penetrating Victor's Eldritch Soul to directly inspect [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]].[38]
The same chapter states that his Soul could easily house tens of trillions of beings if he desired, although Victor deliberately reserves its most protected regions primarily as a home and safe haven for his family.[38]
VICTOR-SPECIFIC
Victor's planets, stars, internal universe, World Trees, layered Cores, Eldritch protections and civilization-scale Soul capacity are not the standard anatomy of Souls in the setting. They are the result of his unique development as a Progenitor, True Dragon, God, Eldritch [[Chaos]] and host of multiple fundamental Powers.

#### Primordial Entities
True [[Primordial Entities]] represent the most extreme native transformation of Soul demonstrated within the Cosmos.
Instead of remaining an individual who merely possesses a Concept, a developing Primordial Entity transforms the Soul until it becomes part of that Concept and therefore part of an Aspect of Existence.[24]
Once fully integrated, destroying the Being in the conventional sense is no longer comparable to killing an ordinary individual. Its function has become embedded in the universal machinery itself.[11]

#### Outer Gods
Main article: [[Outer Gods]]
Outer Gods demonstrate that native Soul rules do not necessarily encompass every form of existence.
[[Nero]] retained a powerful Soul after becoming Eldritch and developed a Soul Form reflecting her new existence.[15] [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Azathoth possess Souls whose foreign [[Chaos]] nature prevents native Primordial authorities from interacting with them in the same way they could interact with normal inhabitants of the Cosmos.[11]
Outer existence therefore does not inherently mean being soulless; instead, the Soul itself can become foreign to the Rules normally governing native spiritual existence.

### Known Properties
Information
- Stores the information defining the individual.[1][3]
- Includes memories, personality, emotions, thoughts, desires and physical information.[3]
- Can preserve racial and supernatural characteristics.[2][3]
Existence
- The body functions as the Soul's receptacle.[2]
- Physical death does not normally destroy the Soul.[6]
- Soul-level damage can persist across bodily restoration or transformation.[5]
Development
- Souls vary in size, quality and maturity.[12][14]
- Godhood metamorphoses the entire Soul.[22]
- Primordial development can transform the Soul into a Concept.[24]
Manipulation
- Souls can be absorbed and stored.[1][13]
- Souls can be read, edited, repaired, divided and reconstructed.[9][29][4][32]
- Soul fragments preserve characteristics of their original source.[20][21]
Cosmological Role
- Everything in Creation can possess a Soul, including non-sentient existences.[6]
- The Judges create and administer Souls.[7]
- Soul is a fundamental universal function represented by a Regulator of Existence.[11]
Afterlife
- Souls are judged and processed after death.[6]
- Previous identity information is normally removed before reincarnation.[8]
- Akashic information can survive independently from the active Soul.[9][8]
