## MTWABV Canon

Nothingness, also referred to as the Space of Nothingness, is the infinite and incomprehensible expanse existing outside the influence of the [[Akashic Records]]. Unlike ordinary empty space or the [[Dimensional Void]], Nothingness does not function according to the laws, concepts, chronology, or structure that govern normal Creation. It is the surrounding state beyond the coherent reality recorded and maintained by the Akashic Records.[1]
Within Nothingness, concepts do not simply behave differently; their normal meanings and functions cease to remain reliable. Time does not function away from the Records, events do not necessarily possess a coherent chronological order, and concepts only maintain recognizable influence near the sphere of the Akashic Records. Farther away, even the Akashic Records themselves cannot properly interpret what occurs.[1]

### Nature
Nothingness is fundamentally different from conventional space. Although it is described as an infinite expanse, ordinary ideas such as distance, duration, chronology, and conventional physical law cannot be assumed to operate within it.[1]
When Akashic observed [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and the [[Outer Gods]] moving to an isolated location away from the Records, she was unable to determine how much time passed. From the perspective of Creation, their activity could have taken only several minutes or thousands of years because the Concept of Time did not function independently within that region.[1]
This absence of conventional chronology extends beyond merely being "timeless." Events outside the Records do not necessarily follow a fixed sequence recognizable from within Creation. Something may simultaneously possess apparently contradictory states, with existence and nonexistence becoming difficult to distinguish. Emptiness and Nothingness are described as intertwined while concepts are either scrambled or completely absent.[1]
The [[Akashic Records]] are what give coherence to this otherwise incomprehensible state. Within the area under their influence, concepts, events, histories, timelines, and the rest of recorded Creation possess understandable relationships. Beyond that influence, such coherence can no longer be taken for granted.[1]

### Relationship with the Akashic Records
The [[Akashic Records]] exist as a structured sphere surrounded by Nothingness.[1] All of the histories, possibilities, realities and timelines governed by the Records possess coherence because of their existence, whereas the surrounding Nothingness is not governed in the same manner.
Despite personifying the Records themselves, Akashic cannot freely wander throughout Nothingness. She explicitly recognized that moving too far away from the Records and beyond the protected opening surrounding them could result in her becoming lost with the possibility of never finding her way back.[1]
This demonstrates that Nothingness is not simply another location stored within the Records. The Records exert influence into their immediate surroundings, but that influence is not described as extending without limit throughout Nothingness.[1]

### Victor's Future Defenses
In the future, [[Victor Elderblood]] transformed the region surrounding the [[Akashic Records]] into an enormous defensive system against beings attempting to invade or interfere with his cosmology.[1]
Because guarding such an enormous surrounding expanse directly was inefficient, Victor reinforced the structure around the Records and reduced viable entry into his home to a single controlled opening. This created a funnel through which intruders would be forced to pass rather than allowing them to approach the Records from countless directions.[1]
Victor then used the characteristics of the [[Outer Gods]] to make the surrounding emptiness actively hostile. [[Shub-Niggurath]] was capable of generating abnormal forms of life on a cosmic scale, allowing entire living universes, galaxies, planets and stars to populate the previously empty region around the Records. These creations functioned as cosmic horrors rather than conventional celestial bodies and were intended to attack beings attempting to advance toward the main Records.[1]
Behind these creatures was another layer of protection based around the Concept of END. The Akashic Records were protected by a barrier composed of more than one thousand layers of different lethal traps intended to erase powerful intruders that succeeded in reaching the Records themselves.[1]
Consequently, while Nothingness is naturally an empty and incoherent expanse, the immediate region surrounding Victor's Akashic Records in the future is no longer completely empty. Victor and his family deliberately populate and fortify portions of it as a defensive zone.[1]

### Relationship with Creation
Nothingness should not be confused with ordinary regions of the Universe. Locations such as planets, Sectors, Pantheon dimensions, the [[Dimensional Void]], the [[Edge of the Cosmos]], and the different realities recorded by the [[Akashic Records]] remain associated with structured Creation.
Nothingness begins beyond the coherent influence of the Records themselves.[1] Because concepts only retain recognizable functionality near the Records, it represents a fundamentally different cosmological environment from locations that merely possess unusual spatial or temporal properties.
The distinction is especially important with the Dimensional Void. The Dimensional Void exists as the medium separating and connecting dimensions belonging to Creation, whereas Nothingness exists outside the Records responsible for giving those dimensions and their histories coherence.[1]

### Difference from the Void Between Cosmos
Nothingness and the Void Between Cosmos are not presented in the story as interchangeable terms.
Nothingness is described in relation to the [[Akashic Records]] of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s cosmology: the Records exist within an otherwise incomprehensible surrounding expanse.[1]
By contrast, the Void Between Cosmos is introduced when [[Azathoth Elderblood]] compares two completely separate cosmoses, each possessing its own Universe, Rules, Primordials and [[Chaos]]. The Void Between Cosmos is the region or distance that must be crossed to travel from one such cosmos to another.[2]
Therefore, Nothingness is the outer environment surrounding the Records of a cosmology, while the Void Between Cosmos concerns travel between separate cosmological systems.

### Known Associated Beings
- [[Victor Elderblood]] — In the future, Victor operates outside the Records and protects them against foreign intruders.[1]
- Akashic — The embodiment of the Records and the entity whose existence gives coherence to the recorded Creation.[1]
- [[Azathoth Elderblood]] — One of the primary [[Outer Gods]] associated with Victor's future existence outside the Records.[1]
- [[Yog-Sothoth]] Elderblood — Participates in Victor's protection of the Records and the surrounding region.[1]
- [[Nyarlathotep]] — Participates in Victor's protection of the Records and its different defensive layers.[1]
- [[Shub-Niggurath]] — Creates living universes and other cosmic life used to populate and defend the space surrounding the Records.[1]

### Trivia
- Nothingness is not merely another name for outer space or the [[Dimensional Void]]. Its defining characteristic is the breakdown or absence of the concepts which normally provide coherent structure to Creation.[1]
- Even Akashic cannot safely navigate indefinitely away from the Records despite embodying the Records themselves.[1]
- The natural Nothingness is described as empty, but [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s future defensive measures deliberately fill the region surrounding his Records with hostile Eldritch creations.[1]
- Time cannot be reliably measured within Nothingness because the Concept of Time does not properly function away from the Records.[1]
