Absolute Paradox is a theoretical superweapon project of the [[Elderblood Empire]] conceived by [[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]] and subsequently developed with [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]], [[Aline Elderblood Valefar]], [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] and [[Victor Elderblood]]. Unlike conventional weapons, the Absolute Paradox is not intended to destroy a target through physical force, Energy, Soul destruction or ordinary Conceptual attacks. Its proposed function is to force two mutually contradictory states to simultaneously exist as equally valid truths, preventing Reality from resolving which state should actually exist.[1]
The project originated from Velina studying the ancient thought experiment known as Schrödinger's cat. Rather than reproducing the experiment itself, she proposed taking its underlying idea of mutually exclusive possibilities to an extreme and creating an actual paradox. Velnorah immediately recognized the wider implications, gathered her research group and developed the concept into a possible weapon. [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] approved the project after learning its potential, while Victor later converted the broad idea into a proposed three-component architecture.[1]
Despite its potential, the Absolute Paradox does not currently exist as a completed weapon. By the end of its introduction, the project remained in the conceptual stage, with small-scale experiments being considered to determine whether the principle could function without Reality immediately eliminating it.[1]

### History
While the [[Elderblood Empire]] continued developing during the enormous temporal ritual, Velina became bored and began manually browsing ancient Human material through a bookshelf connected to the Empire's database. She discovered an account of Schrödinger's cat and became fascinated by the possibility represented by the thought experiment. Remembering that Velnorah had recently complained about a difficult classified project, Velina brought the idea directly to her mother and proposed taking the principle to its logical extreme by creating a large-scale paradox.[1]
Velnorah immediately moved beyond the original thought experiment and began examining how its underlying principle could be applied universally. She gathered her usual research group and concluded that some form of practical implementation might be possible. The proposal was then brought before Violet, who approved it after understanding what such a weapon could theoretically accomplish. Violet considered it another means of ensuring that even beings on the level of the Primordials could not easily threaten [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[1]
The project later became important enough that Victor was willing to continue supporting the Empire's exhausting 200,000-year temporal-development ritual despite operating at heavily reduced energetic efficiency. The Absolute Paradox had become one of the projects he considered important for the Empire's future preparations.[1]
Victor subjected Velina's proposal to his own analysis and established the basic architecture that a functional Absolute Paradox would require. The principal obstacle was no longer understanding what the weapon should do, but creating something that Reality itself would regard as impossible and therefore naturally attempt to eliminate.[1]

### Principle
The Absolute Paradox operates upon the idea that Reality normally resolves contradictions into a single coherent state. Something either exists or does not exist; an event either occurred or did not occur; a person is either alive or dead. The weapon is intended to reject this exclusivity by creating a second contradictory state and forcing both states to remain true simultaneously.[1]
A successful strike would therefore not necessarily damage its target in any conventional sense. Instead, the target would be placed into a condition where two incompatible descriptions of its existence were simultaneously recognized by Reality. Neither state would overwrite, negate or replace the other.[1]
For example, if the Absolute Paradox were successfully applied to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Reality could simultaneously recognize Victor as alive and Victor as dead. A weapon could simultaneously be held by someone and have never been present. A person could simultaneously have been born and never have been born, while an event could both have occurred and never have occurred.[1]
The significance of the contradiction determines the disturbance created within Reality. Applying the effect to increasingly fundamental targets would consequently create increasingly severe consequences.[1]

### Proposed Architecture
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] determined that a functional Absolute Paradox would require three principal systems.[1]
Component
Function
Current Problem
Contradiction Core
The central component would contain two states that normally cannot coexist, such as existence and nonexistence. Both conditions would have to remain active simultaneously without either cancelling the other.
Reality itself would attempt to eliminate the Core because its existence violates the normal structure of the universe.
Stabilizing Energy Source
A continuous source of Energy would prevent Reality from naturally correcting the contradiction. Victor considered Primal Energy suitable because it could reinforce the paradox strongly enough to force Reality to temporarily tolerate something inherently impossible.
The Energy requirement has not been established, and the contradiction would continuously be opposed by Reality.
Paradox Transfer Mechanism
The final system would determine where the contradiction is imposed. In theory, it could transfer the paradox from the Core into people, objects, Space, Time, laws or Concepts.
No functional transfer system had been completed when the project was introduced.
The Core creates the contradiction, the Energy source keeps both states from collapsing into one valid outcome, and the transfer mechanism applies the resulting paradox to the intended target.[1]

### Capabilities
All abilities of the Absolute Paradox are currently theoretical. No completed version of the weapon has demonstrated these effects in combat.[1]

#### Paradox Manipulation
The weapon's fundamental ability is the creation and preservation of mutually exclusive states. Rather than replacing one truth with another, both truths would coexist.[1]
- Life and Death: A target could simultaneously be alive and dead.
- Existence and Nonexistence: Something could exist while simultaneously being treated as though it had never existed.
- Presence and Absence: An object could occupy a location while simultaneously never having been present there.
- Event Contradiction: An event could have occurred while simultaneously never having occurred.[1]

#### Space-Time Manipulation
Applying the paradox to Space or Time would move the effect beyond ordinary targets and into the framework surrounding them.[1]
- Temporal Contradiction: Something could simultaneously have happened and never have happened.
- Spatial Contradiction: Two locations could remain separate while simultaneously occupying the same point in Space.[1]
Unlike conventional Time manipulation, the proposed mechanism would not simply erase, reverse or rewrite an event. Both mutually incompatible versions would remain valid simultaneously.[1]

#### Death Manipulation
If applied directly to Death, the paradox could create a state in which an individual is dead while simultaneously continuing to live. The proposal therefore attacks the logical state represented by Death rather than simply resurrecting a dead target afterward.[1]

#### Conceptual and Law Manipulation
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s proposed transfer mechanism was intended to apply the contradiction not only to physical targets but also to laws and Concepts. In principle, the weapon could therefore create an opposing state within an abstract principle and require Reality to recognize both forms simultaneously.[1]

#### Reality Destabilization
The Absolute Paradox differs from normal Reality Warping because its purpose is not merely to change Reality into a desired configuration. It instead attempts to force Reality to preserve mutually exclusive configurations at once. Reality would then be unable to resolve the contradiction according to its normal structure.[1]

#### Universal Paradox
The weapon's maximum theoretical application is directed at the universe itself. If successful, it could establish both of the following as simultaneously valid truths:
- The universe exists.
- The universe never existed.
Because neither condition would be permitted to negate the other, Reality would be unable to determine which state was correct. The resulting contradiction was predicted to produce a fundamental universal failure, causing Reality to effectively collapse or implode from within.[1]
This is why the concept was compared to an ultimate red button: instead of overpowering everything contained within the universe, the weapon would attack the logical consistency required for the universe itself to continue functioning.[1]

### Relationship with Negativity
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s understanding of Negativity is central to the theoretical project. His knowledge of the universe's fundamental structure allowed him to consider attacking not merely objects inside Reality but the elements necessary for Reality to remain internally coherent.[1]
The intended result is not simply conventional Conceptual Destruction. Rather than personally destroying every component of Reality, Victor intended to create a contradiction severe enough that Reality would become incapable of reconciling its own states and effectively destroy itself through the resulting flaw.[1]
This distinguishes the Absolute Paradox from weapons that rely entirely upon greater destructive output. Its theoretical danger comes from attacking the consistency of Reality rather than overpowering everything within Reality through raw Energy.[1]

### Difference from Eldritch Corruption
[[Eldritch Energy]] was specifically rejected as the foundation of the weapon. Eldritch power could corrupt existing Rules, but the researchers did not want the Absolute Paradox merely to corrupt Reality.[1]
The intended distinction is:
Method
Interaction with Reality
Eldritch Corruption
Corrupts, distorts or alters existing Rules and structures.
Absolute Paradox
Leaves the relevant framework sufficiently intact to force it to acknowledge two contradictory truths simultaneously.
For this reason, simply flooding a target with Eldritch Energy would not reproduce the intended effect of the Absolute Paradox.[1]

### Weaknesses and Limitations
The Absolute Paradox currently possesses several major theoretical obstacles.[1]
- Unbuilt Weapon: The complete Absolute Paradox had not been constructed when it was introduced. Its advertised capabilities remain theoretical.
- Reality Rejection: The moment a true Contradiction Core came into existence, Reality would naturally attempt to eliminate it.
- Continuous Stabilization: The impossible states would require an Energy source capable of preventing Reality from correcting the paradox.
- Primal Energy Requirement: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] proposed Primal Energy as a possible means of sustaining the contradiction, but this remained part of the design rather than a completed system.
- Cosmic Detection: Creating such an impossible object within the universe could alert or mobilize the very cosmic Order responsible for maintaining the structure established by [[Primordial Chaos]].
- Construction Location: Victor concluded that safely constructing the true weapon might require a location outside the universe so that the universe could not immediately suppress its formation.
- Unknown Exit from the Universe: The Empire had no established method for simply leaving the universe entirely, creating another fundamental engineering problem.
- Scaling Risk: The greater the target and contradiction, the greater the effect upon Reality. A universe-level application would consequently carry catastrophic consequences for everything.
- Experimental Uncertainty: The researchers had not yet determined whether Reality could actually be forced to preserve the intended contradiction in practice.[1]

### Development Status
Despite the immense theoretical potential attributed to the Absolute Paradox, the project remains at the earliest stage of development. Rather than immediately constructing the proposed full-scale weapon, the Imperial researchers intended to begin with much smaller experiments and observe how Reality responded to controlled contradictions.[1]
The narration explicitly establishes that the concept remained in the idea phase, with the experiments themselves yet to begin. The final weapon may therefore differ significantly from the initial design—or the project may develop in an entirely different direction depending upon the results.[1]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] briefly considered whether [[Yog-Sothoth]] Elderblood and her abnormal powers could provide a solution, but dismissed the idea rather than immediately incorporating her into the weapon's design.[1]

### Trivia
- The idea for the Absolute Paradox originated from the ancient Human thought experiment Schrödinger's cat, although the completed concept has little to do with the original experiment beyond inspiration from mutually exclusive states.[1]
- The weapon is not designed primarily around raw destructive force. Its theoretical maximum effect comes from making Reality incapable of resolving mutually contradictory truths.[1]
- A target does not need to be physically destroyed for the weapon to affect it; the proposed effect instead operates through the state by which Reality recognizes that target.[1]
- Its proposed transfer mechanism is not restricted to living beings and could theoretically target objects, Space, Time, laws and Concepts.[1]
- [[Eldritch Energy]] was considered unsuitable as the primary mechanism because it would corrupt the Rules rather than produce the exact contradiction required by the project.[1]
- The researchers considered construction outside the universe because Reality itself would attempt to destroy a genuine Contradiction Core.[1]
- No successful combat use, completed prototype or full-scale test of the Absolute Paradox has currently occurred.[1]
