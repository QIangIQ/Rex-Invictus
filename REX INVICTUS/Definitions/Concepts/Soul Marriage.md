## MTWABV Canon

Soul Marriage is a form of supernatural marriage in which two individuals are joined directly through their souls, creating an eternal connection between their existences. Unlike an ordinary marriage or a conventional magical contract, a Soul Marriage is recognized on a metaphysical level and binds the participants through their souls and Fate.[1]
Once the connection is established, the two individuals become soulmates and are destined to remain connected. Even if they are separated, Fate will eventually cause them to encounter one another again. Death itself is normally incapable of permanently separating those connected through a Soul Marriage, as they are destined to meet again in another life. According to [[Aphrodite]], as long as the concept of the Soul continues to exist within the universe, beings connected through this form of marriage will remain destined to be together.[1]

### Description
Soul Marriage is fundamentally a union between souls rather than merely between physical bodies. The marriage establishes an actual metaphysical connection between the participants, allowing their existences to remain connected regardless of ordinary distance or separation.[1]
The conventional form of Soul Marriage is primarily associated with Gods and special beings who possess a sufficient relationship with a Concept. Gods are capable of establishing such a connection because they possess a limited degree of access to the System. Through this access, a sufficiently powerful and genuine commitment between two individuals can become something more than a spoken promise and instead manifest as an actual connection between their souls.[2]
Unlike most conventional marriage rituals, a Soul Marriage does not necessarily require a predetermined ceremony, spell, or ritualistic process. In certain circumstances, the sincere intent of those involved is sufficient. A God promising to belong eternally to another individual can cause a Soul Marriage to form even if the individual did not deliberately begin a formal ritual.[2]
Because of this, Soul Marriage should not necessarily be considered a single standardized ritual. Different races, Concepts and supernatural abilities can create comparable soul bonds through different means, with the resulting connection functioning as a marriage between the souls of the individuals involved.[3][4][5]

### Requirements
The primary requirement for the conventional version of Soul Marriage is normally Godhood, or alternatively being a special existence sufficiently connected to a Concept. [[Aphrodite]] stated that Gods and special beings related to Concepts were capable of performing Soul Marriage.[1]
The reason Gods can establish such a union is their limited access to the System. Their words, intentions and promises can possess metaphysical significance when backed by their existence as Gods. Because of this, an eternal promise between compatible individuals can result in the System recognizing the connection between their souls.[2]
Godhood is not necessarily the only possible method of achieving an equivalent bond. Certain supernatural races possess their own innate soul-binding traditions and abilities. Roxanne's Dryad marriage and Zaladrac's Dragon bond demonstrate that beings with unique racial characteristics can form extremely deep soul connections through methods different from the conventional Divine Soul Marriage.[3][4]

### Mechanics

#### Soul Connection
The most fundamental effect of Soul Marriage is the direct connection of the participants' souls. After [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood completed their Soul Marriage, Victor immediately became capable of feeling Aphrodite connected to him at the level of his soul. He compared the sensation to the connection he already possessed with Roxanne.[1]
The connection is not merely symbolic. Similar marriages have shown that the participants can become capable of perceiving one another through the bond. When [[Medusa]] tied her Fate to Victor and he accepted it, her spirit became connected to his and she became capable of sensing his emotions.[6]
Tasha's union with Victor similarly manifested through their souls becoming bound after she accepted him as her husband.[7]

#### Fate
Soul Marriage also operates through Fate. Once two souls are joined, their Fate becomes connected strongly enough that separation is not necessarily permanent. Even if circumstances divide the participants, Fate will eventually influence events so that they encounter one another again.[1]
This aspect was particularly visible when [[Medusa]] declared that both her own Fate and the Fate of her people would forever be tied to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Victor accepted her Fate, completing the connection between them.[6]

#### Death and Reincarnation
One of the greatest characteristics of Soul Marriage is its ability to persist beyond death. [[Aphrodite]] explained that even death would not permanently separate two beings joined through Soul Marriage because they would eventually encounter one another again in another life.[1]
Therefore, the connection is not restricted to the lifespan of the participants' current physical bodies. The marriage exists at the level of their souls and continues to influence their Fate through reincarnation.[1]
The continued existence of the bond is dependent upon the existence of the concept of the Soul itself. As long as that concept continues to exist within the universe, those joined by Soul Marriage remain destined to reunite.[1]

#### Soul Anchor
In [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s case, Soul Marriage has an additional manifestation within his own soul. Because Victor is normally the strongest party in his marriages, his wives are generally the ones whose souls establish the connection toward him. As a consequence, a trace of the wife's soul becomes present within Victor's soul.[2]
These traces function as anchors for the souls of Victor's wives. Because their soul traces remain preserved within Victor, they possess an additional degree of protection should something happen to them.[2]
This phenomenon can physically manifest within Victor's internal Personal Universe. When Victor formed a Soul Marriage with [[Persephone]], a new representation associated with her appeared within his inner world, allowing Roxanne and Amara to recognize that another Soul Marriage had occurred.[2]

#### Emotional Connection
Depending on the individuals involved, the bond can allow one participant to sense aspects of the other. [[Medusa]] became capable of clearly feeling [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s emotions after connecting herself to him.[6]
Victor's connection with his wives has similarly been portrayed as something that allows an unusually intimate spiritual awareness between those involved. The exact extent of this connection can differ depending on the nature of the individuals, their powers, and the method by which their souls were joined.

### Effects on Vampires
Soul Marriage possesses a particularly useful effect when applied to Vampires. Traditional Vampire marriage rituals help regulate the instincts and desires of the participants, but the ritual can become increasingly problematic when one individual maintains too many such connections.[1]
Soul Marriage removes this particular disadvantage. Because the partners are connected directly through their souls, their presence provides a form of spiritual satisfaction that reduces the severity of a Vampire's bloodlust.[1]
This does not completely eliminate bloodlust. A Vampire connected through Soul Marriage can still desire blood, but the craving becomes significantly less overwhelming. [[Aphrodite]] compared the resulting hunger to the relatively minor hunger experienced by a human who had simply gone several hours without eating, rather than an unbearable emptiness.[1]

### Forms and Variations
Although the term Soul Marriage generally refers to the Divine soul-binding ritual explained by [[Aphrodite]], several different supernatural methods have produced comparable connections.

#### Divine Soul Marriage
The conventional Soul Marriage is performed by Gods or special beings connected to Concepts. It can be initiated simply through mutual declarations of intent, after which their supernatural powers and souls establish the connection.[1]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and [[Aphrodite]] are one of the clearest examples. Aphrodite requested a marriage of the soul, the two declared their intentions to accept one another, and their respective powers reacted and merged. Once the process was complete, Victor could directly feel Aphrodite connected to his soul.[1]

#### Dryad Marriage
Dryads possess their own form of marriage based around exchanging and consuming parts of one another. Roxanne explained that in Dryad culture, if both individuals consume a part of the other, they are considered married.[3]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] unintentionally fulfilled this condition with Roxanne when he consumed the fruit containing her core while Roxanne had consumed Victor's blood. The resulting connection between them became extremely deep, and when Victor later Soul Married [[Aphrodite]], he recognized the sensation of that Divine Soul Marriage as being very similar to his existing connection with Roxanne.[3][1]
Because of this similarity, Roxanne's racial marriage can be regarded as a Dryad method of creating a comparable soul-level union, despite the process being different from the conventional Divine ritual.

#### Dragon Soul Bond
Dragons possess a natural method of creating a soul connection with an individual they choose. Zaladrac instinctively selected [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and created an initial connection with him, identifying him as her chosen partner or "knight".[4]
The Dragon Soul Bond develops progressively. During its initial stage, Victor and Zaladrac became capable of sharing their vision with one another. Zaladrac explained that the bond would continue to deepen as the trust between them increased.[4]
They later completed further steps of the bonding process, causing Victor to become capable of feeling Zaladrac more clearly and strengthening her presence within his soul-space. Unlike [[Aphrodite]]'s Soul Marriage, the Dragon bond possesses distinct stages and abilities associated specifically with the relationship between a Dragon and its chosen partner.[4]
For this reason, Zaladrac's connection is best understood as a racial variation of soul bonding rather than being completely identical in procedure to the conventional Divine Soul Marriage.

#### Conceptual Soul Binding
A powerful Divinity or conceptual Authority can also establish a Soul Marriage directly.
When [[Amaterasu]] agreed to become [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wife, Victor repeatedly confirmed that she understood the permanent nature of that decision. Once she genuinely accepted, her soul suddenly became bound to Victor's.[5]
Amaterasu was surprised that even her soul as a God-Queen could be connected so easily. Victor explained that the phenomenon occurred through his Authority encompassing the Concept of Yandere.[5]
This demonstrates that a sufficiently powerful conceptual Authority can directly establish the soul connection required for marriage without following the ordinary Divine ceremony.[5]

### Known Soul Marriages
The following are notable Soul Marriages or equivalent soul-bonding marriages directly demonstrated in the story:
- [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Roxanne Elderblood]] — Dryad marriage formed through both individuals consuming a part of the other.[3]
- Victor Elderblood and [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood — Divine Soul Marriage performed through mutual declarations of marriage.[1]
- Victor Elderblood and [[Medusa]] Elderblood — Medusa permanently tied her Fate to Victor, after which their spirits became connected.[6]
- Victor Elderblood and [[Zaladrac Zeovnur Elderblood]] — Dragon Soul Bond created through Zaladrac choosing Victor and establishing a progressively deepening connection.[4]
- Victor Elderblood and [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]] — their souls became bound after Tasha accepted her position as Victor's wife.[7]
- Victor Elderblood and [[Amaterasu]] Elderblood — Soul Marriage established through Victor's Yandere Divinity after Amaterasu accepted becoming his wife.[5]
- Victor Elderblood and [[Persephone Elderblood]] — Soul Marriage whose manifestation within Victor's soul was observed by Roxanne and Amara.[2]

### Limitations
Soul Marriage does not make the participants literally the same individual. Although their souls are deeply connected, they maintain their own personalities, consciousnesses, powers, and individual existences.
The bond also does not mean that the participants must remain physically beside one another at all times. The purpose of Fate within the marriage is precisely to ensure that separation is ultimately temporary.[1]
Likewise, the protection granted by leaving a trace of a wife's soul within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] should not be confused with automatic resurrection. Chapter 930 establishes that the soul trace offers protection and remains preserved within Victor, but does not state that any wife who dies is automatically and immediately resurrected.[2]

### Trivia
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul Marriage with [[Aphrodite]] was the first occasion in which the mechanics of the conventional Divine Soul Marriage were directly explained in detail.[1]
- Victor immediately compared his Soul Marriage connection with Aphrodite to his pre-existing connection with Roxanne.[1]
- A Soul Marriage can occur without a deliberate formal ceremony if a God makes a sufficiently genuine promise of eternal belonging.[2]
- In Victor's case, portions or traces of his Soul-Married wives can manifest within his internal soul world.[2]
- Soul Marriage can greatly reduce the bloodlust experienced by Vampires, although it does not completely eliminate their desire for blood.[1]
- Different races can possess their own methods of creating comparable soul-level relationships, such as the Dryad marriage ritual and the Dragon Soul Bond.[3][4]
