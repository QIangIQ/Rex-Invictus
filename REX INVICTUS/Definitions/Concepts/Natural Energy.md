Natural Energy, also known as Nature Energy, World Tree Energy or the Energy of Nature, is a fundamental form of supernatural Energy responsible for sustaining the natural environments and living ecosystems of planets throughout the Universe. Its principal generators are World Trees, extraordinary beings connected to the existence of inhabited worlds. Their Energy circulates through planetary environments, nourishes the conditions necessary for Life and provides the supernatural foundation for several distinct energy systems, including [[Senjutsu]] and the internal Natural Energy generated by Werewolves.[1][2]
Natural Energy possesses an exceptionally close relationship with the fundamental forces of Life and the planetary systems maintained by World Trees. Unlike conventional Mana or the racial energies produced by many supernatural beings, the Energy generated by a World Tree is ordinarily intended to sustain an entire planet rather than serve exclusively as a combat resource. Its distribution contributes to the maintenance of the environment, the development of natural ecosystems and the continuation of the supernatural processes necessary for a living world to function.[1][3]
Although Natural Energy possesses a fundamentally neutral foundation, its characteristics can change according to the World Tree generating it and the individual utilizing it. World Trees associated with the positive side of Creation generate Natural Energy possessing positive characteristics, while their negative counterparts produce Energy associated with Negativity. These differences do not make the underlying power an entirely unrelated energy system; instead, they represent different attributed expressions of Natural Energy.[4]
The first detailed explanation of Natural Energy as a usable supernatural resource emerged through the discoveries of Otsuki Elderblood Haruna and the united Youkai factions. Their investigations into the planetary Ley Lines, also called Dragon Veins, led to the development of Senjutsu, a discipline allowing practitioners to absorb Natural Energy from the environment and employ it through supernatural techniques. Victor subsequently discovered that his connection to Roxanne Elderblood, a World Tree existing within his Soul, provided him with a direct supply of the same fundamental Energy without relying upon ordinary environmental absorption.[5][1]
Natural Energy is therefore broader than Senjutsu. The former is the supernatural power circulating through planetary systems, whereas the latter represents a method of absorbing and using that power. [[Werewolf Energy]] is another specialized manifestation, naturally generated within Werewolf bodies and particularly suited to physical reinforcement and elemental integration.[1][2][6]

### Nature and Origin

#### The Energy of Nature
Natural Energy is the supernatural power associated with the maintenance of planetary Nature and Life. Its existence is inseparable from the role performed by [[World Trees]], which function as the spiritual and energetic foundations of living planets. A World Tree generates the Energy necessary for a planet to develop and maintain environments capable of supporting living beings, making it considerably more important than an ordinary supernatural organism possessing Nature-related abilities.[7][1]
The Energy operates throughout the planetary environment rather than remaining confined to the physical body of the World Tree. It circulates through the planet, nourishes its natural systems and becomes available within specialized locations containing significant concentrations of that power. Haruna identified these channels as Ley Lines, describing them as the veins through which the planet's Energy circulates.[1]
The relationship between Natural Energy and living environments explains why World Trees possess an essential role in the development of habitable planets. A world may possess the physical materials necessary for an ecosystem, but its continued development into a genuinely living planet requires the supernatural support provided through the World Tree system.[7][3]
Natural Energy consequently serves both an environmental and a supernatural function. It sustains planetary systems while also providing an extraordinary resource for individuals capable of perceiving, absorbing or manipulating it through suitable techniques.

#### Origin in the World Trees
[[World Trees]] are the principal natural generators of Natural Energy. Their existence originates from [[The Universal Tree]], the Primordial Being responsible for the broader system of Life throughout Creation. Each World Tree performs a specialized function associated with a planet, generating and distributing the Energy necessary to sustain its natural environment.[8][9]
A World Tree is not merely an exceptionally powerful plant. Its existence is connected to the planet as a whole, including the environment, natural resources and the development of living organisms. World Trees possess the capacity to nourish planets over extraordinary periods, eventually creating the conditions necessary for civilizations to emerge.[7][9]
This process can require millions of years. [[Aurora Seraphina Evergreen]], the [[Amara Elderblood|World Tree of Positivity]] associated with Samar, explained that the first World Tree to emerge upon a planet is ordinarily its positive counterpart. Through the continuous nourishment of the environment, this Tree contributes to the development of Life. Once sentient beings emerge and the planet develops the corresponding negative characteristics, a negative counterpart can gradually form.[9]
The resulting World Trees possess different relationships with the same planet. Their Energies reflect the positive and negative characteristics associated with their respective existences, while both remain components of the larger planetary system.

#### The Universal Tree
[[The Universal Tree]] is the Primordial source associated with the creation and maintenance of the World Tree system. Its function extends across the Universe, connecting the existence of individual [[World Trees]] to the broader mechanisms through which Life develops and continues.[8]
The Universal Tree does not merely possess the abilities of an ordinary World Tree on a greater scale. Its existence is connected to universal systems, whereas individual World Trees ordinarily maintain planets and the living environments associated with them.
The relationship provides Natural Energy with a fundamental position within the cosmology. Rather than representing an isolated magical substance, it belongs to a distributed system connecting living worlds to a greater supernatural structure.
The existence of this connection does not make ordinary Natural Energy identical to the [[Primordial Energy]] wielded by the [[Regulators of Existence]]. The powers of a World Tree and the fundamental authority of the Universal Tree remain distinguishable despite their relationship.

### Planetary Energy System

#### Planetary Nourishment
The primary function of Natural Energy is the nourishment and maintenance of planets. A World Tree generates Energy that supports the conditions necessary for Life, allowing a planet to develop and sustain natural environments over enormous periods.[1][9]
The scale of this function is extraordinary. Unlike the supernatural reserves produced by an ordinary Mortal, a World Tree supplies Energy to an entire planetary system. Its power supports not merely individual organisms but the environment upon which those organisms depend.
[[Roxanne Elderblood]] explained that her Energy was originally intended to nourish a planet. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] discovered how to use it directly, she emphasized that employing her power as a personal combat resource represented an unusual application of an Energy system ordinarily dedicated to planetary maintenance.[1]
- Planetary Energy Generation: [[World Trees]] generate the Natural Energy necessary to sustain living planetary environments.[1][9]
- Environmental Nourishment: Natural Energy supports the continuation and development of planetary ecosystems.[1][3]
- Life Sustenance: World Trees utilize their Energy to maintain conditions compatible with living organisms.[9]
- Planetary Restoration: Natural Energy can contribute to the recovery of damaged or underdeveloped environments.[3][10]
- Energy Distribution: Natural Energy circulates throughout the planet through specialized environmental channels.[1]
The continued existence of this Energy supply is important to the planet's long-term stability. Serious disruption of the relationship between a World Tree and its planet can threaten the entire environment.

#### Planetary Ley Lines
Ley Lines, also known as Dragon Veins or the blood vessels of Mother Earth, are channels through which Natural Energy circulates throughout a planet. They connect the environmental supply of Energy with the larger planetary system and can contain substantial concentrations of supernatural power.[5][1]
Haruna's discovery of these channels provided the foundation for [[Senjutsu]]. By identifying a location where the Energy circulated in significant quantities, she developed a method of absorbing it through meditation and subsequently employing it through supernatural techniques.
The Energy flowing through the Ley Lines does not originate from the practitioner. It belongs to the planet's environmental system and can be drawn into the body when the practitioner develops sufficient control and compatibility.
The relationship was demonstrated when Haruna introduced [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to a cavern containing a concentration of Natural Energy. The Energy circulating through the location immediately reacted to him, gathering around his body in quantities far exceeding what Haruna expected.[1]
- Planetary Energy Circulation: Ley Lines distribute Natural Energy throughout a planet.[1]
- Environmental Energy Concentration: Certain locations contain significant quantities of circulating Natural Energy.[5][1]
- Energy Absorption: Compatible practitioners can draw Energy from these channels into their bodies.[1]
- Senjutsu Cultivation: Ley Lines provide an important environmental source for practitioners of Senjutsu.[5]

#### Planetary Symbiosis
The relationship between [[World Trees]] and their planets is fundamentally symbiotic. The World Tree initially nourishes the planet through Natural Energy, allowing the environment to develop. As the world matures, its systems return and store Energy, supporting the continued operation of the relationship.[10]
The importance of this cycle became apparent during the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s investigation of a dying planet controlled by the [[Skaarith Empire]]. The civilization had been extracting Energy directly from the planetary core without maintaining the natural process through which the World Tree and the planet supported one another.
Velnorah recognized that the extraction was depriving the World Tree of the Energy necessary to continue nourishing the planet. The resulting imbalance caused the Tree's vitality to deteriorate and threatened the continued existence of the entire planetary environment.[10]
This establishes that World Tree Energy is not literally unlimited under every circumstance. Although World Trees possess enormous reserves and can generate quantities of Energy far exceeding those available to ordinary beings, their planetary systems depend upon the continuation of a functional exchange.
- Energy Recycling: The planetary system can return and store Energy after receiving nourishment from its World Tree.[10]
- Symbiotic Energy Supply: A World Tree and its planet support one another through an ongoing energetic relationship.[10]
- Environmental Energy Depletion: Excessive extraction of planetary Energy can disrupt the cycle and weaken the World Tree.[10]
- Planetary Decline: Severe depletion can cause the natural environment to deteriorate and eventually threaten planetary Life.[10]
The planetary system therefore possesses extraordinary regenerative potential, but it is not immune to sustained exploitation or catastrophic disruption.

#### Artificial Planetary Nourishment
Natural Energy can also be employed to establish or restore planetary conditions through deliberate supernatural intervention.
During the construction of a new world, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] directed Roxanne and [[Jeanne Elderblood]] to supply Energy to a newly assembled celestial body. Roxanne's negative-aligned Energy nourished the environment, while Jeanne's [[Primordial Energy]] accelerated and supported the process.[3]
The procedure required the creation of channels through which the Energy could circulate. These artificial veins fulfilled a function comparable to the systems naturally developed by [[World Trees]] on established planets.
The resulting process demonstrates that Natural Energy is not limited to maintaining an already inhabited world. Under suitable circumstances, it can help establish the environmental foundation necessary for Life to develop in a previously unsuitable location.
- Environmental Creation: Natural Energy can contribute to establishing environments capable of supporting Life.[3]
- Planetary Energy Infusion: Large quantities of World Tree Energy can be introduced into a developing world.[3]
- Artificial Ley Line Formation: Energy circulation channels can be established through sufficiently advanced supernatural techniques.[3]
- Environmental Restoration: Natural Energy can participate in repairing or reconstructing planetary conditions.[3]
These applications required the cooperation of exceptionally powerful individuals. The feat should not be treated as evidence that an ordinary [[Senjutsu]] practitioner can independently create a habitable planet.

### Properties and Characteristics

#### Neutral Foundation
Natural Energy possesses a fundamentally neutral nature before acquiring the characteristics of its source or practitioner. During the original discovery of [[Senjutsu]], Haruna explained that the new Energy was neither inherently good nor evil and that its manifestation depended upon the characteristics of the individual utilizing it.[5]
This neutrality distinguishes Natural Energy from powers whose ordinary manifestations possess specific corruptive or Sacred characteristics.
However, neutrality does not imply that every manifestation of Natural Energy remains completely without attributes. [[World Trees]] can generate positive- or negative-aligned expressions of the Energy, while practitioners can influence its manifestation through their own supernatural characteristics.[4]
The distinction is particularly important when comparing the green Natural Energy associated with Haruna and Earth's World Tree with the blood-red manifestation utilized by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] through Roxanne.

#### Positive Natural Energy
Positive Natural Energy is generated by [[World Trees]] associated with the positive side of Creation. Its properties are connected to the development, maintenance and constructive functions of planetary Life.[4][9]
[[Yggdrasil]], the World Tree associated with Earth, and [[Aurora Seraphina Evergreen]], the [[Amara Elderblood|World Tree of Positivity]] of Samar, provide important examples of this alignment.
The Natural Energy circulating through Earth's Ley Lines possesses a light-green appearance, while Samar's positive Natural Energy can manifest through green supernatural constructs and specialized World Tree techniques.[1][11]
Positive Natural Energy is particularly associated with the nourishing functions of World Trees. Its properties can support environmental development and certain protective or restorative supernatural techniques, although the precise effects depend upon the user and the method through which the Energy is manipulated.
- Positive Energy Attribution: Natural Energy can acquire characteristics associated with the positive side of Creation.[4]
- Environmental Nourishment: Positive World Trees use their Energy to support planetary development.[9]
- Protective Energy Constructs: Positive Natural Energy can be used through specialized techniques to establish protective barriers.[11]
- Energy-Based Regeneration: Certain constructs sustained through positive Natural Energy can regenerate after sustaining damage.[11]
Positive Natural Energy should not be regarded as identical to the complete Primordial authority of Positivity. The former represents an attributed planetary Energy, while the latter encompasses a far broader aspect of Existence.

#### Negative Natural Energy
Negative Natural Energy is the manifestation generated by [[World Trees]] associated with the negative side of Creation. Its characteristics differ from positive Natural Energy while retaining the same underlying relationship with the World Tree system.[4][9]
Roxanne provides the clearest example. Her Energy manifests as a blood-red supernatural power possessing characteristics influenced by her relationship with Negativity and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s own existence.
When Haruna observed Victor using Roxanne's Energy, she recognized that it was fundamentally similar to [[Senjutsu]] despite its unusual appearance and the intense sensation of Blood and Killing Intent surrounding it. The Energy retained its connection to Nature and Life rather than becoming an unrelated Demonic power.[1]
Roxanne's negative alignment influences the effects she can produce. During the creation of Volk's protective barrier, Roxanne explained that an equivalent construct generated through her own Energy would possess a destructive rather than protective character.[11]
- Negative Energy Attribution: Natural Energy can acquire characteristics associated with Negativity.[4]
- Blood-Red Energy Manifestation: Roxanne's Natural Energy can appear as an intense red supernatural power.[1]
- Destructive Energy Constructs: Negative Natural Energy can support constructs whose functions differ from the regenerative defenses associated with positive manifestations.[11]
- Personalized Energy Characteristics: The Energy can reflect the supernatural nature of its source and user.[1]
The negative alignment does not automatically make the Energy evil. Haruna recognized that Victor's manifestation retained the fundamental characteristics of Nature despite its ominous appearance.[1]

#### Energy Density
The density and quality of Natural Energy can differ significantly between [[World Trees]]. Older and more developed World Trees can generate Energy possessing greater density than that of comparatively young individuals.
Roxanne explicitly identified the Natural Energy of Earth's World Tree as denser than her own because of the difference in their development. This distinction became apparent when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] encountered Earth's Natural Energy before switching to the power supplied through his personal World Tree.[1]
The density of Natural Energy is important because the power was originally intended to nourish planetary environments. Such Energy can place considerable strain upon ordinary supernatural bodies when forcibly concentrated within them.
The distinction between total reserves and instantaneous output is also significant. A World Tree can possess an enormous quantity of stored Energy while retaining practical limitations concerning how much it can release or transfer through a particular technique at once.[9]

### Generation and Reserves

#### World Tree Generation
[[World Trees]] possess the natural ability to generate enormous quantities of Natural Energy. Their reserves are vastly greater than those ordinarily produced by Mortal supernatural beings because they are responsible for sustaining entire planetary environments.[1][9]
This abundance makes World Trees exceptionally valuable to powerful supernatural factions. A direct relationship with a World Tree can provide access to a supply of Energy whose potential greatly exceeds the ordinary reserves available through conventional racial development.
However, the available quantity remains subject to the World Tree's own condition and the resources supporting its existence. Planetary depletion can reduce its ability to continue nourishing the environment.[10]

#### Energy Absorption
Natural Energy can be absorbed from the environment by practitioners possessing appropriate techniques and compatibility.
Haruna developed such a method after studying the Energy circulating through Earth's Ley Lines. She discovered that successful absorption required careful meditation and a state of mental tranquility, allowing the Energy to enter her body without producing the uncontrolled reactions associated with improper cultivation.[5][1]
The amount absorbed depends upon the practitioner's skill, the available environmental Energy and the body's ability to withstand the process.
Natural Energy absorption therefore differs from ordinary racial Energy generation. A practitioner can draw upon an external planetary supply rather than relying entirely upon Energy already produced within their own body.

#### Direct World Tree Access
A direct supernatural connection with a World Tree can provide access to Natural Energy without requiring the same cultivation methods used by ordinary [[Senjutsu]] practitioners.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] demonstrated this through his bond with Roxanne. When attempting to absorb Earth's Natural Energy, he recognized the similarity between the environmental power and the Energy already available through his own Soul. He subsequently began using Roxanne's Energy directly.[1]
The relationship allowed him to bypass the ordinary process through which Haruna filtered environmental Natural Energy before utilizing it.
However, the direct supply created its own difficulties. The density and quantity of the Energy exceeded what Victor's body could safely handle at his earlier stages of development, requiring substantial physical adaptation and improved control.[12]

#### Energy Synchronization
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Roxanne developed a deeper connection through the synchronization of their Souls. This process allowed Roxanne to nourish Victor's existence more directly and significantly increased his access to her Natural Energy.[1]
The synchronization was extraordinary because a World Tree is normally connected to a planet rather than an individual being. Victor's exceptional Soul and Progenitor characteristics allowed him to sustain a relationship that would ordinarily be impossible for a conventional supernatural creature.
The resulting union improved his physical capabilities and provided access to certain abilities associated with Roxanne's existence. However, the synchronization also exposed Victor to an Energy supply far greater than his previous body could comfortably withstand.[1][12]
- Energy Synchronization: A compatible individual can establish a direct connection with a World Tree through exceptional supernatural circumstances.[1]
- Energy Transfer: The World Tree can supply Energy directly to its partner.[1]
- Passive Physical Enhancement: The connection can reinforce the recipient's body through the continuous supply of Natural Energy.[1]
- Shared Supernatural Abilities: An exceptionally developed connection can permit access to certain World Tree powers.[1]
The process is not an ordinary Natural Energy technique available to every practitioner. It depends upon the unusual relationship between Victor and Roxanne.

### Abilities and Applications

#### Natural Energy Manipulation
Natural Energy can be manipulated through techniques developed by [[World Trees]], [[Senjutsu]] practitioners and certain supernatural races possessing compatible physiology.
Its applications vary according to the user's relationship with the Energy. World Trees employ it primarily to sustain environments, while Mortals and other supernatural beings may adapt it for combat, physical enhancement or specialized techniques.
- Natural Energy Generation: World Trees produce enormous quantities of Natural Energy, while certain races generate specialized internal manifestations.[1][2]
- Energy Absorption: Practitioners can absorb environmental Natural Energy through appropriate cultivation methods.[5][1]
- Energy Manipulation: Natural Energy can be consciously controlled and directed through supernatural techniques.[1][11]
- Energy Projection: Natural Energy can be released beyond the user's body through specialized attacks.[11]
- Energy Constructs: The Energy can sustain supernatural structures, including barriers.[11]
- Energy Reinforcement: Natural Energy can enhance physical capabilities when properly integrated with the body.[1][6]
- Energy Attribution: Natural Energy can acquire characteristics associated with positive or negative World Trees and certain elemental affinities.[4][6]

#### Life Sustenance
The most fundamental application of Natural Energy is its ability to sustain Life through the World Tree system.
[[World Trees]] generate and circulate Energy necessary for planetary environments, providing the conditions through which ecosystems can develop and persist.[1][9]
At the individual level, an exceptionally powerful supply of Natural Energy can also sustain a compatible supernatural body. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s connection to Roxanne eventually provided enough Energy to maintain his physical condition continuously, eliminating his ordinary need to consume Blood for nourishment.[13]
This effect arose from the extraordinary quantity of Energy continuously supplied by a World Tree and the changes occurring within Victor's existence. It should not be interpreted as an ordinary ability automatically granted to every [[Senjutsu]] practitioner.

#### Environmental Restoration
Natural Energy can restore or improve environments by supplying the supernatural nourishment necessary for their recovery.
The restoration of planetary conditions is particularly important after damage to a world's natural systems. A World Tree can continue supporting the environment over extended periods, allowing natural development to resume when suitable conditions remain available.[3][10]
The ability also possesses considerable value for planetary colonization and reconstruction. By introducing Natural Energy and establishing appropriate circulation channels, exceptionally powerful practitioners can accelerate processes that would ordinarily require enormous periods of natural development.

#### Nature Manipulation
[[World Trees]] possess powers associated with Nature and the non-sentient components of their planetary environments.
Roxanne explained that she could control and encourage the development of non-sentient natural elements, including plants, trees and other environmental structures. She possessed a particularly strong affinity with plant Life, allowing her to influence vegetation and related natural phenomena.[1]
- Plant Manipulation: World Trees can manipulate plants, trees and other forms of vegetation.[1]
- Plant Growth: Natural powers can accelerate or direct the development of vegetation.[1]
- Environmental Manipulation: World Trees can influence certain non-sentient components of Nature within their domain.[1][14]
- Earth Manipulation: World Trees possess influence over natural materials and environmental structures associated with their domain.[1]
These abilities belong to the supernatural nature of World Trees and should not automatically be assigned to every practitioner capable of absorbing Natural Energy.

#### Physical Enhancement
Natural Energy can reinforce a compatible supernatural body, improving its performance through the circulation and application of Energy.
The effect is particularly evident in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and the [[Werewolves]]. Victor's synchronization with Roxanne substantially increased his physical capabilities, while Werewolves naturally employ their internal form of Natural Energy to strengthen their bodies during combat.[1][6]
- Enhanced Strength: Natural Energy can reinforce physical attacks and muscular performance.[1][6]
- Enhanced Speed: Energy reinforcement can improve movement and combat acceleration.[6]
- Enhanced Durability: Natural Energy can help sustain and reinforce the body against physical strain.[1][6]
- Energy Coating: Compatible practitioners can surround themselves with Natural Energy to increase their combat performance.[11]
- Physical Regulation: Advanced control can influence how extraordinary strength interacts with the surrounding environment.[12]

#### Energy-Based Regeneration
Natural Energy can support recovery and regeneration through specialized applications.
Roxanne demonstrated this capacity by using her Energy to sustain [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] after he was struck by an exceptionally dangerous poison. Although Victor's body continued deteriorating under the poison's effects, Roxanne supplied enough Energy to maintain his condition while working to remove the harmful substance.[15]
The regenerative properties of Natural Energy are also demonstrated through Volk's defensive barrier, which could restore damage after sustaining powerful attacks.[11]
These effects demonstrate the Energy's potential for restoration, but they depend upon the abilities and control of the individual employing it. Natural Energy does not universally guarantee immunity to injury, poisoning or supernatural deterioration.

#### Energy Barriers
Natural Energy can sustain protective barriers through specialized supernatural techniques.
[[Volk Fenrir]] demonstrated this application by creating an enormous green barrier around a combat arena. The structure possessed regenerative properties and could withstand several powerful attacks from the [[Lycaon|Werewolf King]] himself.[11]
The barrier drew upon Natural Energy associated with Samar's World Tree system. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] recognized the underlying power as pure Natural Energy and considered whether Roxanne could produce a comparable construct.
Roxanne explained that her negative-aligned Energy could generate a related manifestation, but its characteristics would be destructive rather than protective.[11]
- Barrier Creation: Natural Energy can form large protective structures.[11]
- Barrier Regeneration: Certain positive-aligned constructs can restore damage sustained during combat.[11]
- Area Protection: Natural Energy barriers can protect extensive regions from supernatural attacks.[11]
- Attributed Constructs: The characteristics of a Natural Energy construct depend upon the nature of its source.[11]

#### Energy-Based Transportation
Natural Energy can support transportation systems through specialized World Tree techniques.
During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s journey to Samar, a Werewolf named Liza activated markings resembling tree branches and used green Natural Energy to construct a gateway connecting Earth with Samar.[4]
Victor recognized the power as similar to Roxanne's Natural Energy but possessing positive characteristics. He also identified the markings as part of a system providing access to the destination world through its connection with the World Tree.
The technique demonstrates that Natural Energy can support interplanetary transportation when combined with appropriate supernatural mechanisms.
- Portal Creation: Specialized Natural Energy techniques can produce gateways connecting distant locations.[4]
- Spatial Transportation: World Tree-based supernatural systems can facilitate travel between planets.[4]
- Energy-Based Markings: Certain techniques employ markings or inscriptions connected to their Natural Energy source.[4]
These applications depend upon specialized techniques rather than ordinary possession of Natural Energy automatically granting Space Manipulation.

### Senjutsu
Main article: [[Senjutsu]]
Senjutsu is a supernatural discipline developed through the absorption and manipulation of environmental Natural Energy. Its discovery among the Youkai represented an important advancement because it provided a source of power independent of the negative emotional Energy traditionally used by many members of that race.[5]
Haruna discovered Senjutsu after investigating the planetary Ley Lines identified by members of the Youkai factions. By meditating and establishing a sufficiently tranquil mental state, she learned to absorb the Energy circulating through the environment and incorporate it into her own supernatural techniques.
The process was initially difficult because the Nature-derived Energy conflicted with the Youki already present within her existence. Careful regulation was therefore necessary to prevent the two systems from interacting destructively.[5]

#### Environmental Absorption
Conventional [[Senjutsu]] involves drawing Natural Energy from the environment through a cultivated connection with the surrounding planetary systems.
The process requires an appropriate location, sufficient available Energy and the user's ability to maintain a state suitable for absorption.
Haruna demonstrated this method during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s training within a cavern containing a concentration of Natural Energy. The surrounding Energy could be perceived as a light-green supernatural power circulating through the area.[1]

#### Energy Filtering
Natural Energy must ordinarily be filtered and controlled before it can be safely employed by practitioners whose bodies are not naturally adapted to its direct use.
Haruna developed a method allowing her to absorb Natural Energy and subsequently utilize her internal vitality to produce techniques. Direct use of unfiltered Natural Energy was possible with sufficient proficiency but placed greater demands upon the practitioner's body.[1]
This distinction separates the environmental source from the techniques produced through it. The Energy itself belongs to Nature, while the cultivation method determines how the practitioner receives and employs it.

#### Victor's Direct Senjutsu
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s method differed fundamentally because he possessed a World Tree within his Soul.
Rather than relying upon the ordinary environmental filtering process, he could receive Energy directly from Roxanne and manipulate it through the connection established between their existences.[1]
The resulting power possessed a blood-red appearance associated with Roxanne's negative characteristics. Although it differed greatly from Haruna's green [[Senjutsu]], its underlying foundation remained Natural Energy.
Victor's direct access also eliminated the need to consume his own vitality when a suitable supply could be obtained through Roxanne.
However, the method exposed his body to considerable strain because the Energy being supplied was ordinarily intended to sustain an entire planet.[12]

### Werewolf Energy
Main article: [[Werewolf Energy]]
Werewolf Energy is a specialized internal manifestation of Natural Energy naturally produced by [[Werewolves]]. Its fundamental origin is connected to the World Tree system, but it possesses characteristics suited to the physiology and fighting styles of the Wolf Race.[2]
Unlike ordinary [[Senjutsu]], which involves environmental absorption, Werewolves generate a diluted form of Natural Energy within their bodies.
The Energy can acquire elemental characteristics according to the practitioner's affinity and is particularly effective when employed for physical enhancement.[6]

#### Internal Natural Energy
The internal Natural Energy of [[Werewolves]] is an inherent racial characteristic rather than a separate form of environmental cultivation.
Maya explained that all Werewolves possess an Energy capable of acquiring elemental attributes, although the specific element depends upon the individual's compatibility. The Bloodlines of powerful Alpha families can provide advantages in training and refining the Energy.[6]
The adaptability of this system differs from the more fixed elemental inheritance commonly associated with Noble Vampire Bloodlines.

#### Physical Reinforcement
[[Werewolves]] principally utilize their Natural Energy to strengthen their bodies.
This approach complements their naturally powerful physical characteristics and allows them to incorporate elemental attributes directly into their combat movements.[6]
The technique can improve physical strength, speed and durability while allowing the user to perform specialized elemental attacks through their limbs or weapons.

#### Elemental Attribution
[[Werewolf Energy]] can be converted into an element compatible with the user.
[[Adam William Lykos]] demonstrated this ability by converting green Natural Energy into Lightning and incorporating it into his physical movements. His advanced techniques allowed him to alter the ordinary process through which elemental attacks manifested, producing rapid localized effects that were difficult for opponents to anticipate.[11]
Such mastery depends upon personal training and affinity rather than being universally available to every Werewolf.

#### Residual Werewolf Natural Energy
[[Nero Elderblood]] retained a small portion of her original Werewolf Soul after becoming a Noble Vampire.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] discovered that this remaining fragment continued producing Natural Energy that enhanced her physical abilities. The manifestation resembled [[Senjutsu]] but possessed the characteristics associated with the internal Energy generated by [[Werewolves]].[2]
Her condition demonstrates that Nature-derived racial Energy can remain active even after an extraordinary transformation changes the individual's dominant supernatural physiology.

### World Tree Manifestations

#### Yggdrasil
[[Yggdrasil]] is the World Tree associated with Earth and one of the most important sources of Natural Energy within the original supernatural world.
The Natural Energy circulating through Earth's Ley Lines possesses a light-green appearance and can be absorbed by practitioners of [[Senjutsu]].[1]
Yggdrasil's considerable age makes its Energy denser than that of younger [[World Trees]] such as Roxanne, providing an important demonstration of how World Tree development influences the characteristics of the Natural Energy generated.[1]

#### Roxanne Elderblood
[[Roxanne Elderblood]] is a World Tree associated with negative Natural Energy.
Her Energy possesses a blood-red appearance and can be supplied directly to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] through their extraordinary Soul connection.
Unlike the environmental Energy circulating through Earth's Ley Lines, Roxanne's power is closely synchronized with Victor's existence and can continuously reinforce his body while supplying the supernatural resources necessary for his techniques.[1][13]
Roxanne also possesses the Nature-related abilities common to [[World Trees]], including control over certain non-sentient natural elements and the development of plant Life.[1]

#### Aurora Seraphina Evergreen
[[Aurora Seraphina Evergreen]] is the [[Amara Elderblood|World Tree of Positivity]] associated with Samar.
Her Natural Energy possesses positive characteristics and contributes to the maintenance of the planet's living environment. She is also connected to the supernatural systems employed by the Werewolf Royal Family, including specialized transportation and protective techniques.[4][9]
Aurora explained that her existence is deeply connected to Samar, allowing her to perceive the environment through the natural systems belonging to her planetary domain.[14]
These capabilities arise from her nature as a World Tree rather than being universal powers granted to every individual capable of manipulating Natural Energy.

#### Aria Seraphina Evergreen
[[Aria Seraphina Evergreen]] is the World Tree of Negativity associated with Samar.
She represents the negative counterpart of Aurora and emerged through the development of the planet's sentient Life and corresponding negative characteristics.[9]
Her existence demonstrates the complementary relationship between positive and negative [[World Trees]]. Although the two possess different supernatural alignments, both belong to the same planetary system.

#### Amara Elderblood
[[Amara Elderblood]] is a World Tree associated with the positive side of Creation and an important source of Nature-derived power within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s expanding supernatural existence.
Her relationship with Roxanne reflects the complementary roles performed by positive and negative [[World Trees]]. Both can participate in the nourishment of a shared supernatural environment while contributing different energetic characteristics.

### Relationship with Other Energy Types

#### Natural Energy and Ki
Ki is the internal vitality or life-force Energy employed through specialized cultivation techniques.
Natural Energy differs because its ordinary source is the planetary environment and the [[World Trees]] sustaining it.
Haruna's original method involved absorbing Natural Energy and subsequently using her own vitality to produce techniques. The process demonstrates that environmental Natural Energy and internal Ki can interact without being identical.[1]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s direct access to Roxanne's Energy allowed him to avoid unnecessarily consuming his own vitality.

#### Natural Energy and Youki
Youki is the characteristic supernatural Energy used by many Youkai, traditionally associated with negative emotions and the conditions supporting its accumulation.
Natural Energy possesses a different foundation. Its ordinary environmental manifestation is neutral, and it can be absorbed from the planetary systems sustained by [[World Trees]].[5]
Haruna developed [[Senjutsu]] partly because the dependence upon Youki imposed restrictions upon the expansion and development of the united Youkai factions.
However, the two energies initially conflicted within her existence, requiring careful control to prevent harmful interactions.

#### Natural Energy and Vampiric Energy
[[Vampiric Energy]] is the characteristic racial power generated by Vampires.
Natural Energy originates principally from [[World Trees]] and the planetary systems they sustain.
The distinction is especially important for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], whose original Vampiric powers coexisted with the Nature-derived Energy supplied by Roxanne. His ability to use Natural Energy did not mean that ordinary Vampiric Energy had become identical to [[Senjutsu]].[1]
His subsequent development allowed the different energies to interact in increasingly complicated ways, contributing to exceptional growth but also placing substantial demands upon his body.

#### Natural Energy and Miasma
Miasma is the characteristic Demonic Energy associated with Hell and the negative side of Creation.
Although negative-aligned Natural Energy can possess characteristics associated with Negativity, it is not identical to Demonic Miasma.
Roxanne's power retains the fundamental properties of Nature-derived Energy even when it possesses an ominous or destructive manifestation.[1]
The distinction prevents every negative manifestation of Natural Energy from being classified as Demonic corruption.

#### Natural Energy and Divine Energy
[[Divine Energy]] is the supernatural power generated by Gods.
Like Natural Energy, it can possess a neutral foundation before receiving particular attributes. However, the conventional source and function of the two energies differ.
Natural Energy is generated principally through planetary World Tree systems, while Divine Energy belongs to the supernatural existence of Gods.
The ability to use Natural Energy does not automatically grant Godhood or authority over Divine Concepts.

#### Natural Energy and Positivity
Positive Natural Energy possesses characteristics associated with the positive side of Creation.
However, it represents an attributed manifestation produced through the World Tree system rather than the entirety of the Primordial Concept of Positivity.[4]
Positive [[World Trees]] possess an important relationship with planetary development, but their Energy should not be treated as unrestricted control over every positive Concept.

#### Natural Energy and Negativity
Negative Natural Energy possesses characteristics associated with the negative side of Creation.
Roxanne provides an important example through her blood-red Energy, which can support destructive manifestations while retaining its fundamental relationship with Nature and Life.[1][11]
The Energy remains distinguishable from pure Negativity, which encompasses a far broader supernatural foundation.

#### Natural Energy and Draconic Energy
[[Draconic Energy]] is generated through the [[Dragon Heart]] and employed by Dragons.
Natural Energy ordinarily originates from [[World Trees]] or specialized Nature-derived racial systems.
The two can coexist within extraordinary beings possessing multiple supernatural characteristics, but their conventional sources and properties remain distinct.
The possession of Draconic Energy does not automatically confer the full planetary nourishment abilities of a World Tree.

#### Natural Energy and Attributeless Energy
Attributeless Energy represents the more fundamental foundation from which different attributed supernatural energies can arise.
[[Melida Charlotte Elderblood]] demonstrated exceptional control over this source, allowing her to transform otherwise unattributed Energy into different supernatural manifestations.[16]
Natural Energy belongs among the specialized attributed energies existing throughout Creation.
The distinction is important because Natural Energy's underlying neutrality does not make it identical to the original Attributeless Energy. The latter occupies a more fundamental position and can be manipulated by exceptionally advanced beings capable of producing different energy attributes directly.

### Limitations and Weaknesses

#### Physical Strain
Natural Energy can place extraordinary strain upon bodies that are not sufficiently developed to contain or manipulate it.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] experienced continuous pain after beginning to utilize Roxanne's Energy directly. The Energy was so dense and powerful that it repeatedly damaged his body, forcing his regeneration to repair the resulting injuries.[12]
The problem arose because the Energy was intended to sustain an entire planet rather than circulate through an ordinary supernatural body.

#### Energy Control
The ability to absorb Natural Energy does not guarantee mastery over its applications.
Haruna required careful meditation and refinement to utilize environmental Natural Energy safely, while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] initially struggled to control the enormous quantity supplied through Roxanne.[5][12]
The effectiveness of a practitioner therefore depends upon their proficiency, compatibility and capacity to manage the Energy available.

#### Energy Incompatibility
Natural Energy may conflict with other supernatural energies already present within the body.
Haruna encountered this problem because the neutral Nature-derived Energy used in [[Senjutsu]] was not immediately compatible with the Youki upon which her previous supernatural techniques depended.[5]
The resulting difficulty required careful cultivation and regulation rather than unrestricted simultaneous use of both powers.

#### World Tree Development
The quality and density of Natural Energy depend partly upon the development of the World Tree producing it.
Roxanne's Energy was less dense than the Energy generated by Earth's older World Tree, demonstrating that younger [[World Trees]] do not automatically possess identical energetic characteristics to their more developed counterparts.[1]

#### Planetary Energy Depletion
World Tree Energy is not limitless under all circumstances.
The destruction of the energetic relationship between a World Tree and its planet can weaken the Tree and compromise the planetary environment. Excessive extraction of Energy from the planetary core can deprive the system of the resources necessary to continue functioning.[10]

#### Specialized Applications
Not every Natural Energy practitioner possesses the complete abilities demonstrated by [[World Trees]].
Planetary nourishment, advanced environmental manipulation and the construction of enormous supernatural systems depend upon powers and resources exceeding those ordinarily available to Mortal cultivators.
Similarly, specialized Werewolf techniques belong to the individual practitioners who developed them rather than representing universal abilities of Natural Energy itself.

- Discovery of [[Senjutsu]]: Haruna and the Youkai factions developed a method of absorbing Natural Energy from Earth's planetary Ley Lines.[5]
- Environmental Energy Absorption: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] attracted enormous quantities of Earth's Natural Energy while training within a Ley Line cavern.[1]
- Negative Natural Energy: Victor manifested Roxanne's blood-red Natural Energy, which Haruna recognized as fundamentally similar to Senjutsu despite its different characteristics.[1]
- World Tree Synchronization: Victor and Roxanne synchronized their Souls, enabling the continuous transfer of Natural Energy and increasing Victor's physical capabilities.[1]
- Physical Overload: The density of Roxanne's Energy repeatedly damaged Victor's body until his regeneration and continued development improved his compatibility.[12]
- Sustained Physical Nourishment: Roxanne's continuous Energy supply maintained Victor's body sufficiently that he no longer required ordinary Blood consumption for nourishment.[13]
- Residual [[Werewolf Energy]]: [[Nero]] retained the ability to generate diluted Natural Energy through a surviving fragment of her original Werewolf Soul.[2]
- Positive Natural Energy Transportation: A Werewolf serving Anderson constructed a gateway to Samar through specialized Natural Energy techniques.[4]
- Natural Energy Barrier: Volk created a regenerative barrier surrounding a combat arena using positive Natural Energy.[11]
- Elemental Conversion: Adam converted internally generated Natural Energy into Lightning and employed it through his body and weapons.[11]
- World Tree Duality: Aurora explained the relationship between Samar's positive and negative World Trees, including the different conditions governing their development.[9]
- Planetary Creation: Roxanne supplied negative Natural Energy to a newly assembled celestial body while Jeanne used [[Primordial Energy]] to support the establishment of a life-compatible environment.[3]
- Planetary Energy Depletion: The [[Skaarith Empire]]'s exploitation of a planetary core disrupted the exchange of Energy between the planet and its World Tree, threatening the survival of the environment.[10]
