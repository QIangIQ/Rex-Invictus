Onmyoujutsu is a broad supernatural system historically practiced by Japanese Onmyōji. Although its best-known applications rely upon Faith and the invocation of Divine Beings, Onmyoujutsu is not a single Faith spell tradition. Abe-no-Seimei explicitly describes Faith-based Onmyoujutsu as only one of many areas of the Onmyō Arts, while his rival Ashiya Doman developed an independent tradition centered upon Yōkai and the Yin or Dark side of supernatural existence.[1][2][3]
At its broadest, the system functions as a method of translating an external supernatural principle into a usable technique. Depending upon the branch, that principle may come from a God, a Heroic Spirit, a Yōkai, a prepared talisman, an enchanted weapon or another supernatural source. The resulting art can produce effects ranging from weapon creation and physical reinforcement to weather manipulation, illusion, binding, paralysis, summoning, conceptual Divine effects and large-scale battlefield attacks.[4][3][5][6]
The system continued evolving far beyond its original Human Japanese context. Mizuki Elderblood retained and expanded her Onmyō abilities after her racial evolution, while Frederick Winter continued using Onmyoujutsu after being reconstructed as a complete Noble Vampire. By the Imperial Era the art had become an organized military-religious profession whose practitioners served the [[Elderblood Empire]] alongside the Imperial Inquisition.[7][8][9]

### Nature of Onmyoujutsu
Onmyoujutsu is better understood as a framework for supernatural application than as one standardized collection of spells. Its practitioners use specific ritual structures to access, imitate, borrow, summon or channel supernatural characteristics that do not necessarily originate within their own bodies.
This explains the extraordinary variety of demonstrated techniques. Mizuki can request a weapon from Bishamonten, borrow [[Abe-no-Seimei]]'s wisdom, hide herself through Nurarihyon, produce duplicates through Tanuki, restrict violence through Kamainu, combine numerous Yōkai into Hyakki Yagyō and use Susanoo's characteristics to generate a battlefield-scale storm.[4][3][5]
Fred later demonstrates the same underlying adaptability on a much larger Imperial scale. Rather than remaining restricted to Japanese Gods, he invokes [[Victor Elderblood]] through <Fear>, later calls upon Divine power associated with War, and participates in an invocation of [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] through <Order>.[6][9][10]

#### Magic as a Tool
[[Abe-no-Seimei]]'s explanation to Mizuki is particularly important to understanding the system. He rejects the idea that Onmyoujutsu itself is inherently good or evil. [[Magic]] is a tool, and its moral direction comes from the person using it.[2]
This philosophy becomes essential when Mizuki begins studying Dōman's arts. Techniques previously condemned as Dark Arts are not mechanically unusable simply because they were socially forbidden. The danger lies in their source, method and potential corruption rather than a universal rule that only Seimei's Divine tradition qualifies as legitimate Onmyoujutsu.

#### Divine / Faith Arts
The Divine branch is the tradition most strongly associated with [[Abe-no-Seimei]] and with the early depiction of Onmyoujutsu.
Its central principle is the establishment of a supernatural connection between the practitioner and a Divine source through Faith. A suitable technique then allows the Onmyōji to temporarily manifest a characteristic, blessing or fraction of that God's power.[1][6]
Early Mizuki demonstrates this process through Japanese Divine figures. She calls upon Bishamonten to materialize a combat weapon and invokes Abe-no-Seimei's wisdom through a talisman.[4]
The Imperial version demonstrates that this structure was never intrinsically limited to Japanese Gods. Fred eventually invokes [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], and Chapter 1163 explicitly states that his mature Onmyō techniques can harness the power of Gods belonging to other Pantheons.[6][9]

#### Dark / Yin / Yōkai Arts
The Dark Arts are most strongly associated with [[Ashiya Doman]], Seimei's historical rival.
Dōman developed the art around Yōkai rather than relying principally upon Gods. Chapter 475 explains the underlying contrast directly: Gods were associated with the Yang side used heavily by conventional Onmyōji, while Yōkai represented Yin and the negative supernatural side of the tradition.[3]
Dōman's method went considerably further than merely summoning friendly Yōkai. When he killed Yōkai, they could become part of the supernatural power available to his Dark Arts rather than simply disappearing. The enormous diversity of Yōkai species consequently granted his system an equally enormous variety of possible effects.[3]
Importantly, Dōman was still capable of using Divine Onmyō methods. [[Scathach]]'s recollection describes him as a talented Onmyō Mage whose aptitude simply leaned much more strongly toward the Dark side than Seimei's did.[3]
Mizuki eventually learned techniques from both traditions, making her one of the clearest examples of an Onmyōji who transcended the old separation between Divine and Dark methods.

#### Other Areas
Seimei tells Mizuki directly that the Faith branch she had focused upon was only one of many areas of Onmyoujutsu.[2]
No complete list of those areas has yet been provided. Divine/Faith Arts and Dark/Yōkai Arts should therefore be treated as the two best-explored traditions, not the complete limits of the system.

### Faith and Divine Invocation

#### Faith as Connection
Faith is the central spiritual mechanism behind Divine Onmyoujutsu.
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] questioned Mizuki about her magic, its broad principle was compared to the techniques of the Inquisition. Both traditions allow a mortal practitioner to access power associated with Gods through belief and ritual.[1]
Faith in this context is not simply factual knowledge that a God exists. Chapter 1118 demonstrates the distinction through Frederick. Fred personally knew Victor, understood that he was a God-Emperor and had witnessed overwhelming evidence of his power, yet he initially struggled to establish the kind of Faith required by his Onmyoujutsu because he continued instinctively perceiving Victor as his childhood friend.[6]
Victor solved this problem by exposing Fred, Andrew and Edward to a fraction of the reality of his existence. Once Fred emotionally and spiritually reconciled the familiar Victor with the God Victor had become, his invocation succeeded.[6]

#### Borrowed Power, Not Permanent Divinity
The Divine characteristics manifested through Onmyoujutsu remain borrowed power.
Fred's <Fear> invocation temporarily manifests power derived from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Divine aspect as concentrated crimson Negativity. It does not transform Fred into the permanent owner of Victor's <Fear> Authority.[6]
The same distinction applies generally. An Onmyōji may reproduce properties connected to a God without automatically becoming the God of that Concept or acquiring the invoked Divinity permanently.

#### Divine Permission
Onmyoujutsu does not grant unrestricted access to a God's power.
Fred explicitly states that the amount of Divine power available to a technique depends upon both his Faith and the God's permission.[6] Chapter 1163 later treats permission from the relevant God as a normal requirement of Imperial Onmyō techniques.[9]
Ordinary practitioners therefore cannot simply select an unwilling God and forcibly drain their Authority.

#### Cross-Pantheon Invocation
Traditional Japanese Onmyoujutsu commonly worked with the Gods of the [[Shinto Pantheon]], but the underlying system is not restricted to them.
Fred proves this directly in Chapter 1118 by successfully replacing his former focus with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[6] By Chapter 1163, this flexibility is stated as a general advantage of mature Onmyoujutsu: Fred's techniques can draw upon any compatible God, including Gods belonging to other Pantheons, subject to the relevant Divine permission.[9]

### Casting Methods
Onmyoujutsu has several demonstrated methods of structuring and delivering supernatural effects. None should be mistaken for the ultimate source of the system.

#### Talismans
Paper talismans are the most iconic Onmyō medium and are used extensively by both Mizuki and Fred.
Talismans can:
- initiate Divine invocation;
- carry prepared supernatural effects;
- manifest Yōkai;
- anchor formations;
- reinforce bodies or weapons;
- structure seals and restraints;
- and serve as the focal point through which a spell is released.[4][3][11][6]
Fred's Chapter 1118 <Fear> spell demonstrates a typical advanced use. He holds a talisman while invoking [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], throws it forward and strikes it to release the accumulated crimson Negativity as a projectile.[6]
Talismans are therefore important tools, but Chapter 1163 proves they are not an absolute requirement. Fred casts his God-of-War invocation without even retrieving a talisman.[9]

#### Incantations
Spoken prayers and invocations traditionally identify the source and intended nature of the spell.
Mizuki calls directly upon beings such as Bishamonten and Susanoo, while Fred's later invocations address [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and other Gods through their relevant Divine aspects.[4][5][6]
At extremely advanced levels, incantations cease being mechanically necessary. By Chapter 1163, Fred and Andrew had long since learned how to activate their respective religious techniques without spoken chants. Fred continued using them primarily because he enjoyed the dramatic ritual.[9]
言
Incantation is a method, not the source.
The late Imperial form of Onmyoujutsu proves that a sufficiently experienced practitioner can abandon both the spoken chant and, in some circumstances, the talisman while still invoking Divine power.

#### Magic Circles and Formations
Onmyoujutsu can create supernatural geometric formations both externally and within the practitioner's own body.
Mizuki has demonstrated magical patterns appearing in her eyes, large floor formations and the distinctive ten-pointed structure associated with Dōman's Dark Arts.[4][3]
These formations can organize multiple simultaneous effects. Hyakki Yagyō, for example, combines immobilization and multiple Yōkai within a single large Dark Art rather than functioning as one simple projectile.[3]

#### Enchantments
Onmyoujutsu can reinforce the practitioner directly.
Fred used enhancement techniques while still Human, compensating for the physical limitations of his body before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later reconstructed him as a Vampire.[12]
The system can also reinforce weapons or transform them into different supernatural forms. At Imperial levels, the distinction between enchantment and Divine transformation becomes increasingly small: Fred's Chapter 1163 invocation covers his body in violet Energy and transforms his technological sword into an enormous greatsword formed from [[Divine Energy]].[9]

### Dark Arts and Yōkai Integration
Dōman's Dark Arts are among the most mechanically unusual aspects of Onmyoujutsu.
Rather than treating Yōkai merely as creatures to exorcise, Dōman transformed them into supernatural resources. [[Scathach]] recalls that Yōkai killed by him could become part of his power, allowing their characteristics to be reproduced through his techniques.[3]

This made Dōman's arsenal extraordinarily versatile because Yōkai themselves exist in countless varieties.

#### Nurarihyon
Yami Art: Nurarihyon manipulates environmental perception rather than simply making the user's physical body transparent. When Mizuki uses it against [[Scathach]], Scathach can still sense her supernatural presence despite being unable to visually perceive her normally.[3]
The technique is therefore best classified as supernatural concealment and perception interference rather than simple optical transparency.

#### Order: Tanuki
Order: Tanuki manifests ten raccoon-like entities that subsequently take Mizuki's appearance, creating multiple functional copies surrounding the enemy.[3]
The word Order in this technique's name should not be confused with Violet's later Divine Concept of <Order>. Chapter 475 predates that Imperial invocation and presents Tanuki as part of Mizuki's Onmyō repertoire.

#### Order: Kamainu
Order: Kamainu manifests a canine Yōkai whose supernatural effect prevents a target from deliberately using their power to inflict harm.[3]
[[Scathach]] remains capable of producing her power, but the technique interferes when she attempts to use that power violently. This is therefore a form of behavioral or action restriction rather than conventional power nullification.

#### Yami Art: Hyakki Yagyō
Yami Art: Hyakki Yagyō is one of the most complex Dark Arts shown.
Mizuki produces a ten-pointed magical formation and employs multiple Yōkai-derived effects simultaneously. One supernatural entity prevents movement while others contribute to the larger trapping structure.[3]
[[Scathach]] identifies the historical technique as Dōman's method for using several Yōkai to:
- immobilize the target;
- trap the target;
- and eventually eliminate the target.[3]
Its danger comes primarily from layered effects. A combatant unfamiliar with the technique can be restrained by one Yōkai while another component prepares the killing mechanism.

#### Jorōgumo
Jorōgumo uses a black talisman to manifest a spider-woman Yōkai. Mizuki commands it to mark General Kurtz and turn him into its prisoner. The summoned Yōkai restrains him while a smaller spider enters his body.[11]
The technique demonstrates several Dark-Art functions at once: summoning, marking, restraint and imprisonment.

### Catalysts and Property Transfer
One of the most important advanced mechanics is demonstrated by Mizuki during the [[WarFall]] battle in Chapter 653.
Mizuki uses an Odachi lent by [[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]] not primarily as a sword, but as a spell catalyst. Multiple talismans cover the blade while her Energy flows through it.[5]
[[Abe-no-Seimei]] explains the principle directly: when Mizuki channels her spell through the weapon, characteristics belonging to the blade are transmitted into the magic produced through it.[5]

The principle dramatically expands Onmyoujutsu's theoretical versatility. A suitable catalyst can modify the behavior of an existing spell by introducing supernatural characteristics the practitioner may not naturally possess.

#### Wrath of the Heavens
Mizuki's most extensive demonstrated Human-era Onmyō spell is Wrath of the Heavens.[5]
She floats above the [[WarFall]] battlefield with eight differently glowing talismans and invokes Susanoo. The spell creates localized storm clouds, torrential rain and repeated Lightning strikes before causing the accumulated water to gather into eight approximately twenty-metre-tall samurai giants.[5]
The giants are not identical. They manifest different weapons—including swords, polearms and even a water-based firearm—and independently attack different classes of Monster.

### Power, Scaling and Efficiency
Onmyoujutsu does not possess one fixed level of destructive power.
The same system can produce relatively small support techniques or effects powerful enough to restructure battlefields depending upon the practitioner, supernatural source and spell being used.

#### Variables
Known factors include:
- the practitioner's Faith;
- the power of the invoked God;
- the amount of power the God permits the practitioner to borrow;
- the branch of Onmyoujutsu;
- the sophistication of the technique;
- the user's own body and supernatural vessel;
- the user's control and experience;
- the selected catalyst;
- and the compatibility between source, technique and practitioner.[6][5]

#### Fraction of Divine Power
Chapter 1118 provides the clearest numerical description.
Fred states that the basic Onmyoujutsu he used drew only a fraction of a percent of the invoked God's power.[6]
Even that minute fraction of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s <Fear> generated a huge sphere of crimson Negativity that crossed from the planet into space within seconds before detonating.[6]
Fred immediately points out that more advanced Onmyō spells can request a greater fraction of the God's power, although Faith and Divine permission continue limiting how much can actually be used.[6]

#### The Caster as a Vessel
Fred also notices that the Chapter 1118 enchantment became significantly stronger because he was no longer Human.[6]
This demonstrates that an Onmyōji is not merely an empty pipe through which [[Divine Energy]] passes. The strength and quality of the practitioner's own supernatural body affects the resulting technique.
That principle is also visible much earlier through Mizuki. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s blood drastically improved her body and supernatural potential, allowing her to maintain Wrath of the Heavens for more than thirty minutes when [[Abe-no-Seimei]] stated that even he could only maintain comparable magic for approximately one minute at his peak.[5]

#### Imperial Scale
By Chapter 1163, Fred had developed to a completely different level.
He invokes a God-of-War aspect while fighting on an alien world without even retrieving a talisman. Violet-colored [[Divine Energy]] covers his body, his technological sword transforms into a gigantic Energy greatsword and one ranged slash creates a vast crater while splitting continental geography.[9]
The same chapter describes Fred and Andrew as possessing approximately planetary-scale personal ability while assigning each a V-05 threat classification, a level associated with the potential to destroy a Solar System when their entire arsenal and Divine access are considered.[9]
This classification should not be interpreted as meaning every ordinary Onmyō spell is Solar-System level. Fred's own demonstrated casual battlefield attack is vastly smaller, and Chapter 1164 explicitly explains that the reason Fred and Andrew can reach such extreme threat levels is partly because the Gods they can call upon are vastly stronger than they are personally.[10]

### Racial Compatibility
Onmyoujutsu originated as a Human supernatural system, but later events demonstrate that the art is not biologically restricted to Humans.

#### Mizuki's Evolution
Mizuki had spent much of her Human life building her Onmyō foundation. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later began preparing her transformation into a Dragon, one reason she accepted the possibility was that his method could preserve and integrate those pre-existing supernatural characteristics rather than simply erase them.[7]
Her later Dragon state continued supporting evolved Onmyō abilities rather than replacing the system.
This establishes an important principle: racial evolution can modify the vessel without requiring the practitioner to abandon Onmyoujutsu.

#### Frederick's Reconstruction
Fred provides an even clearer example.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] reconstructs him into a complete Noble Vampire while deliberately preserving the Onmyō abilities Fred developed as a Human.[8]
By Chapter 1118 Fred directly remarks that his spell is stronger partly because he is no longer Human.[6]
His Vampire physiology therefore becomes a stronger vessel through which the existing supernatural system operates.

### Imperial Onmyoujutsu
By the late Imperial Era, Onmyoujutsu had transformed from a comparatively obscure Earth tradition into a formal supernatural profession of the [[Elderblood Empire]].

#### Imperial Onmyō Warriors
Chapter 1163 confirms the existence of organized Onmyō Warriors.[9]
The profession had become popular through the patronage and influence of [[Mizuki Elderblood]] and [[Amaterasu]] Elderblood. Frederick was described as the most famous and highest-ranking among them and as the leader associated with Mizuki's warriors.[9]

Fred's religious status had also become extraordinary. Because of his personal closeness to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his position within the Empire's spiritual hierarchy, believers regarded him almost like a saint and treated him as an unofficial equivalent of a Pope.[9]
This reverence is social and religious rather than evidence that Fred himself had become the supreme God of the religion.

#### Military Role
Imperial Onmyō Warriors were not isolated temple mystics. They operated as combatants alongside Imperial fleets, Spartans and the Imperial Inquisition.[9]
Both religious warrior groups served beneath High Priestess [[Valeria Alekerth]], the Emperor's apprentice and head of the Empire's official religion.[9]
Their practical combat ability was described as comparable, although the mechanics underlying their powers differed.

#### God-of-War Invocation
The first detailed demonstration of mature Imperial Onmyoujutsu occurs when Fred descends upon an alien world in Chapter 1163.
Without retrieving a talisman, he invokes the Emperor in a martial Divine aspect:
[[Ares|God of War]] — Command[9]
Violet Energy envelops his body and transforms his Western technological sword into a huge Energy greatsword. Fred then fights directly through the Divine reinforcement before firing an Energy slash powerful enough to carve an enormous crater and divide continental geography.[9]
This demonstrates how far Onmyoujutsu had evolved from its early paper-talisman presentation. At Imperial levels the art can function as an immediate battlefield transformation integrated directly into modern equipment and martial combat.

#### Invocation of <Order>
Fred later joins Andrew in invoking [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] through her relationship with <Order>.[9]
Their combined ritual manifests a crown and a translucent Divine projection of Violet.
The event is especially important because the invoked Goddess does not remain a passive battery. Violet:
- directly communicates with the practitioners;
- reads their memories to understand the situation;
- actively completes the unfinished spell;
- examines the surrounding Solar System;
- and personally applies <Order> to the world.[9][10]
In Chapter 1164 her projected staff strikes the ground and a golden-white force spreads over the entire planet. Hostile creatures become docile, the violent climate calms and the atmosphere itself becomes purified or ordered.[10]

### Known Practitioners

#### Abe-no-Seimei
[[Abe-no-Seimei]] is one of the most important historical Onmyō Masters and the principal teacher of Mizuki.
His tradition emphasizes Divine/Faith-based techniques, although he possesses knowledge of other branches and of Dōman's methods. Even after death, Seimei continued advising Mizuki as a Heroic Spirit.[13][2]
By Chapter 1081 [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] restored Seimei to a living body as a biologically immortal High Human, allowing the historical master to continue observing the Empire and his disciple's development.[16]

#### Ashiya Doman
[[Ashiya Doman]] was Seimei's friend, rival and the principal historical master of Dark/Yin Onmyoujutsu.
Where Seimei possessed greater aptitude for the Divine side, Dōman's talents were particularly suited to Yōkai and negative supernatural forces. His techniques allowed him to incorporate Yōkai properties into his own combat system, giving him an exceptionally wide range of strange abilities.[3]
[[Scathach]] considered his accumulated Yōkai arsenal dangerous enough that, with sufficient preparation and training, he could threaten even Elder Vampires who were not properly prepared for his methods.[3]

#### Mizuki Elderblood
[[Mizuki Elderblood]] is one of the most accomplished modern Onmyō practitioners.
She began primarily through Seimei's Divine tradition but eventually studied Dōman's opposing Dark Arts rather than remaining trapped within one historical school.[2][3]
Her demonstrated repertoire includes:
- Divine weapon invocation;
- Heroic Spirit invocation;
- Lightning and weather manipulation;
- Yōkai concealment;
- duplication;
- violence restriction;
- paralysis and layered binding;
- marking and imprisonment;
- mass summoning;
- weapon-catalyst spell conversion;
- and large-scale battlefield magic.[4][3][11][5]
Her exceptional performance with Wrath of the Heavens is specifically identified as abnormal even among Onmyō Mages.[5]
She later retains the system through Dragon evolution and becomes one of the principal Imperial patrons associated with the Onmyō warrior profession.[7][9]

#### Frederick Winter
[[Frederick Winter]] began training much later than traditional Onmyōji but displayed exceptional aptitude.
Seimei offered him the path after Fred became interested in acquiring a supernatural system of his own.[1][17]
Fred eventually continued Onmyoujutsu after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] reconstructed him as a complete Noble Vampire while intentionally preserving his existing supernatural abilities.[8]
His later development is particularly important because it demonstrates the full flexibility of mature Onmyoujutsu:
- he can invoke Victor instead of relying exclusively upon Shinto Gods;[6]
- he can draw power from Gods belonging to different Pantheons;[9]
- he no longer requires incantations, although he prefers using them;[9]
- he can perform at least some advanced invocations without retrieving a talisman;[9]
- he serves as the most famous and highest-ranking Imperial Onmyō Warrior;[9]
- and he leads the warriors associated with Mizuki's tradition.[9]

#### Imperial Onmyō Warriors
By Chapter 1163, Onmyō practitioners had become an organized Imperial profession rather than an isolated collection of individual Japanese mystics.[9]
They operate alongside Imperial Inquisitors, possess broadly comparable military capabilities and serve within [[Valeria Alekerth]]'s religious command structure.
The average Imperial Onmyō Warrior should not automatically be assumed to possess Fred's personal access, threat classification or relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].

### Powers and Abilities
The following abilities have been demonstrated through Onmyoujutsu. Individual practitioners do not automatically possess every ability in the system.
- [[Magic]]: Onmyoujutsu is an expansive magical and spiritual system incorporating Divine invocation, Yōkai Arts, enchantments, talismans, formations and supernatural catalysts.[2][3]
- Faith Empowerment: Genuine Faith strengthens the connection between a Divine Onmyōji and the invoked God.[1][6]
- Power Borrowing: Divine practitioners can temporarily access a fraction of a God's characteristics or power.[6]
- Cross-Pantheon Power Borrowing: Mature Onmyoujutsu can access Gods from Pantheons other than Shinto when the relevant conditions and permission are satisfied.[9]
- Divine Invocation: Gods can be invoked through their identities, roles or Divine characteristics; extremely advanced invocations can cause the God to actively answer the call.[6][9][10]
- Talisman Magic: Talismans function as spell containers, triggers, summoning media, seals and ritual anchors.[4][3][11]
- Incantation Bypass: Highly advanced users can operate without spoken incantations.[9]
- Talisman Bypass: At least some late-stage spells can be invoked without physically retrieving a talisman, as demonstrated by Fred.[9]
- Summoning: Onmyō techniques can manifest spirits, Yōkai and constructed supernatural entities.[13][3][5]
- Weapon Creation / Transformation: Mizuki invokes Bishamonten to transform her fan into an Odachi; Fred later transforms a technological sword into a [[Divine Energy]] greatsword.[4][9]
- Weapon Enhancement: Energy and supernatural properties can be channelled through weapons.[4][5]
- Property Bestowal / Property Transfer: A weapon used as a catalyst can transmit its relevant supernatural characteristics into a spell.[5]
- Power Augmentation: Onmyoujutsu can reinforce the practitioner's combat capabilities through Divine or magical enhancement.[12][9]
- Statistics Amplification: Fred has used the system to overcome the limitations of his original Human body and later receives substantially stronger enhancement through Divine invocation.[12][9]
- Elemental Manipulation: The system can channel elemental characteristics through Gods and other supernatural sources.
- Water Manipulation: Wrath of the Heavens creates and controls enormous quantities of water and shapes it into autonomous combat giants.[5]
- Lightning Manipulation: Susanoo-based techniques create Divine Lightning across the battlefield.[14][5]
- Weather Manipulation: Mizuki produces localized storm clouds and torrential rainfall through Susanoo's invocation.[5]
- Illusion Manipulation / Perception Manipulation: Nurarihyon alters environmental perception so that Mizuki effectively disappears from normal sight.[3]
- Invisibility / Concealment: Several techniques can conceal the practitioner from normal observation or detection.[3]
- Duplication: Order: Tanuki produces multiple copies of Mizuki through Yōkai manifestations.[3]
- Behavioral Manipulation / Violence Inhibition: Order: Kamainu prevents the affected target from deliberately performing harmful actions with their powers.[3]
- Paralysis Inducement: Hyakki Yagyō incorporates a Yōkai capable of immobilizing the target.[3]
- Binding: Dark Arts can restrain targets through layered Yōkai effects and supernatural formations.[3]
- Sealing: Several Dark Arts prevent escape or meaningful movement once the target is caught.[3][11]
- Marking: Jorōgumo marks the selected victim before converting them into its prisoner.[11]
- Imprisonment: Jorōgumo can place a victim under the supernatural control of the summoned Yōkai.[11]
- Creature Manipulation: Dōman's system incorporates and deploys Yōkai-derived supernatural properties.[3]
- Fear Manipulation: Fred channels [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s <Fear> through Divine Onmyoujutsu.[6]
- Negativity Manipulation: Fred's <Fear> invocation materializes as concentrated crimson Negativity.[6]
- Order Manipulation: Fred later invokes Violet through <Order>; the resulting Divine projection completes a planetary-scale pacification effect.[9][10]
- Energy Projection: Divine Energy can be shaped into large ranged attacks, including Fred's <Fear> sphere and later greatsword slash.[6][9]
- Explosion Inducement: Fred's <Fear> projectile detonates after leaving the planet.[6]
- Large-Scale Environmental Manipulation: At high levels Onmyoujutsu can alter battlefield weather or act as the invocation framework through which a God affects an entire planet.[5][10]
- Limited Healing: Mizuki was observed recovering injuries through an unidentified supernatural technique during the Chapter 29 battle; the novel does not clearly establish which Onmyō branch produces the effect.[13]
- Information Analysis: Mizuki later displays analytical capabilities after her evolution, but this is an evolved Mizuki-specific application and should not automatically be assigned to all Onmyōji.[18]

### Trivia
- Onmyoujutsu was one of the first Human supernatural systems shown capable of allowing physically inferior Humans to threaten much stronger supernatural beings through preparation, Divine power and technical knowledge.[4]
- Seimei and Dōman represent opposing specializations rather than simple “good” and “evil” versions of the art.[2][3]
- Dōman did not completely abandon Divine techniques; his natural aptitude was simply stronger toward the Yin/Yōkai side.[3]
- Mizuki eventually became capable of using techniques connected to both rivals.
- Wrath of the Heavens is one of the clearest demonstrations of how Onmyoujutsu can combine Divine invocation, talismans, environmental magic, summoning and an enchanted weapon catalyst within a single operation.[5]
- Fred's Chapter 1118 <Fear> invocation was considered only basic Onmyoujutsu despite the size of the resulting attack.[6]
- Fred continued using dramatic spoken incantations long after he became skilled enough to cast without them simply because he enjoyed doing so.[9]
- Imperial Onmyō Warriors and Imperial Inquisitors routinely serve together despite accessing Divine power through different systems.[9]
- By the Imperial Era, Onmyoujutsu had expanded from a Japanese spiritual tradition into a multi-Pantheon military art capable of functioning alongside interstellar technology.[9]
- The direct manifestation of Violet proves that an invocation can become an active interaction with the Divine source rather than remaining a one-way transfer of Energy.[9][10]
