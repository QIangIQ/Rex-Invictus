Power Hierarchy encompasses the systems used to classify the strength, supernatural abilities and combat capabilities of beings throughout My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires. The setting features several overlapping hierarchies, including an original comparative ranking established by the author, the formal Threat Level classification developed by the Elderblood Empire, the power hierarchy of Gods and the individual structures governing supernatural races such as Vampires, [[Werewolves]], Witches and Demons.
The most comprehensive modern system is the Imperial Threat Level, which divides supernatural danger into seven categories ranging from V-01 (Urban Level) to V-07 (Universal Level). Established by the Elderblood Empire, the system classifies an entity according to the greatest credible threat it can represent through its complete abilities, equipment, supernatural authorities and available sources of power. It is particularly useful for comparing beings belonging to different races or civilizations, since a conventional racial rank does not necessarily describe the full destructive potential of its holder.[1]
An earlier hierarchy established in the author's auxiliary material provides a separate comparative framework. It places the Primordial Beings responsible for maintaining universal existence above the ordinary Divine hierarchy, followed by the original [[Primordial Gods]] and a series of supernatural ranks containing Gods, Progenitors, Demons, Vampires, Werewolves, Witches and other exceptional beings. This system places considerable importance upon the combat effectiveness of an individual, the supernatural advantages provided by their abilities and their potential to defeat stronger opponents through technique or specialized powers.[2]
The two hierarchies describe different aspects of supernatural strength. The author's original system arranges powerful beings into comparative combat ranks, whereas the Imperial system evaluates the maximum scale of danger they can credibly represent. They are presented separately because authorial Rank 5 and Imperial V-05 are not equivalent classifications. The appearance of the number five in both systems does not establish the same destructive scale.

### Overview
Power within the supernatural world depends upon several interacting characteristics, principally physical strength, supernatural Energy, racial physiology, combat proficiency, specialized abilities and conceptual authority. These factors can differ enormously between individuals belonging to the same species or occupying similar political positions. A powerful Vampire may defeat a God through superior combat skills or specialized weapons, while a God with comparatively modest physical strength may possess a Divine Concept capable of affecting opponents far beyond their conventional durability.[2]
The distinction is particularly significant among beings whose abilities involve the manipulation of Souls, Reality, Space, Time, Destruction or other fundamental phenomena. Such powers can influence the outcome of a confrontation independently of ordinary physical comparisons, although their actual effectiveness remains dependent upon their demonstrated properties and the resistances possessed by the target.

### Imperial Threat Hierarchy
The Imperial Threat Level System is the standardized power classification established by the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Its seven categories describe the maximum credible scale of destruction or supernatural danger a target can produce. The system applies to supernatural beings, military personnel, hostile entities, Divine Artifacts and other assets whose abilities represent a significant strategic threat.[1]
The classification considers the complete capabilities available to the subject rather than exclusively measuring its ordinary physical strength. Supernatural abilities, Divine authority, transformations, advanced weapons, equipment and accessible external powers can therefore influence the assigned category. An individual possessing Planetary personal strength may qualify as a Solar-System-level threat when their available techniques can invoke powers capable of producing substantially greater destruction.[3][4]

#### CODE: V-01 — Urban Level
V-01
URBAN LEVEL
Localized Supernatural Combatants
The lowest formal classification, encompassing individuals whose abilities pose a localized threat without extending to the destruction of an entire metropolitan area.
V-01 encompasses trained Human fighters, inexperienced supernatural beings and individuals possessing equipment or abilities capable of creating significant local destruction. The category includes ordinary combatants whose physical characteristics or supernatural powers exceed those of average Humans without providing the means to destroy an entire city.[1]
Examples associated with this general range include inexperienced Vampires, lower-ranking Inquisitors, trained martial artists and newly awakened supernatural beings. Early appearances of relatively weak Vampires such as [[Corneliu Funar]] and [[Luan Davis]] provide useful examples of the lower supernatural hierarchy, while inexperienced Human combatants such as Jimmy and Thomas represent the importance of training and equipment.
Despite its position at the beginning of the system, an Urban-class supernatural being may possess a decisive advantage over an ordinary Human. The classification is comparatively low only when measured against the vastly greater destructive capabilities of higher supernatural ranks.

#### CODE: V-02 — City Level
V-02
CITY LEVEL
Metropolitan Destruction
A combatant capable of destroying or collapsing a city through supernatural strength, [[Magic]], weapons or specialized abilities.
V-02 encompasses individuals capable of producing destruction on a metropolitan scale. The category represents the transition from localized supernatural incidents to combatants whose complete capabilities can endanger entire cities. It can be reached through personal power, large-scale Magic, specialized weapons or supernatural abilities capable of producing equivalent consequences.[1]
During the earliest portions of the story, the ordinary or restrained manifestations of powerful individuals could operate within this general range. [[Victor Elderblood]] possessed extraordinary potential from his awakening, but his initial practical control and available techniques remained far below the power he eventually achieved. [[Mizuki Elderblood]] and [[Scathach]], when operating under considerable restrictions, were also used among the illustrative comparisons for lower threat scales during the establishment of the Imperial system.[1]
The classification describes the output available under the relevant conditions rather than the maximum strength of those characters in their later or unrestricted forms.

#### CODE: V-03 — Continental Level
V-03
CONTINENTAL LEVEL
Continental and National Annihilation
The general range associated with powerful supernatural elites capable of devastating nations, continental regions or producing comparable destruction.
V-03 encompasses beings whose abilities extend beyond ordinary city destruction and into continental-scale devastation. During the earlier supernatural era, powerful Noble Vampires, trained Lesser Gods, elite Witches and other exceptional Mortal combatants belonged broadly around this category or its lower boundaries.[1]
The traditional [[Vampire Counts]] provide particularly useful comparisons. [[Agnes Elderblood Snow]] and Natashia Elderblood Fulger possessed the strength to devastate countries through their powers, while [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] was recognized as the strongest of [[Nightingale]]'s Counts and possessed exceptional combat experience extending far beyond that of most Nobles.
[[Scathach]]'s combat ability placed her at the upper end of the original Vampire Count hierarchy. Her ordinary destructive output and the full danger represented by her equipment were distinct, because her specialized spear could threaten Gods whose durability surpassed her conventional attacks. Consequently, her original complete threat potential could extend above the basic Continental classification even when her ordinary fighting strength belonged around the upper reaches of this range.[1][2]
The scale of V-03 takes on a different significance among advanced Upper-Sector civilizations, whose ordinary soldiers may possess Continental-level combat capabilities. The Empire prepared for the possibility of fighting enormous armies of V-03-class enemies, including forces numbering in the billions.[5][6]

#### CODE: V-04 — Planetary Level
V-04
PLANETARY LEVEL
World-Class Destruction
A subject capable of destroying or dominating an entire planet through personal power, [[Magic]], Divine abilities or advanced technology.
V-04 encompasses supernatural beings and assets whose complete power can endanger an entire planet. The category includes direct planetary destruction as well as supernatural domination or comparable world-scale consequences. Powerful Gods, exceptional Progenitors and advanced supernatural combatants may qualify through different combinations of raw power, specialized abilities and equipment.[1]
During the original Imperial assessment, powerful Gods such as Kali, [[Shiva]], [[Heavenly Father]], [[Erebus]] and [[Gaia]] were used as illustrative Planetary examples. Those comparisons concerned their known capabilities within the original assessment and do not define the permanent maximum strength of their later forms or subsequent development.[1]
The classification is particularly significant for beings possessing unusual abilities whose range may exceed their conventional physical statistics. Certain Gods can affect extensive portions of a world through their Divine authorities, while other combatants rely upon weapons or complex supernatural techniques capable of producing Planetary consequences.
The mature [[Elderblood Empire]] eventually regarded Planetary combat power as relatively ordinary among its more developed personnel. Nevertheless, V-04 remains a world-ending classification when evaluated against civilizations without access to higher supernatural forces.[4]

#### CODE: V-05 — Solar System Destroyer Level
V-05
SOLAR SYSTEM DESTROYER LEVEL
Stellar-System Threat
A being or weapon capable of destroying an entire Solar System through its complete available capabilities.
V-05 represents the power necessary to threaten complete Solar Systems. Unlike Planetary combatants, whose maximum destructive effects concern individual worlds, Solar-System-class beings can produce destruction extending across an astronomical system containing a central star and numerous planetary bodies.[1]
The classification includes exceptionally powerful supernatural beings, Divine techniques and technological assets whose destructive potential reaches this scale. A subject may qualify through its own Energy or through an available power system capable of invoking stronger beings.

[[Frederick Winter]] and [[Andrew Walter]] are explicitly classified as V-05 in the mature [[Elderblood Empire]]. Both possess approximately Planetary personal combat strength, but their respective Onmyō and Inquisition techniques allow them to access powers belonging to Gods whose abilities exceed their ordinary physical limitations. Their full arsenals therefore represent Solar-System-level danger.[3][4]

#### CODE: V-06 — Galactic Level
V-06
GALACTIC LEVEL
Galaxy-Class Destruction
A being or asset capable of destroying entire galaxies or producing comparable astronomical destruction.
V-06 encompasses individuals and assets whose power can threaten entire galaxies. This category represents an enormous escalation beyond ordinary Solar-System destruction and includes exceptional Gods, [[True Dragons]], powerful technological weapons and other supernatural beings whose available capabilities operate on a Galactic scale.[1][6]
The classification has a particularly broad internal range. An individual capable of destroying one Galaxy and another capable of destroying several galaxies may both remain within V-06 because the system does not introduce a separate formal category for every difference in scale.[4]

#### CODE: V-07 — Universal Level
V-07
UNIVERSAL LEVEL
Universe-Class Power
The highest formal Imperial classification, encompassing the creation, destruction or fundamental alteration of entire Universes.
V-07 is the highest category established within the official Imperial Threat Level system. Its defining scope extends to entire Universes and the fundamental laws governing their existence. Beings capable of creating, destroying or reconstructing a complete Universe, or otherwise exercising power with equivalent consequences, belong within the general scope of this classification.[1]
The category is relevant to exceptionally powerful cosmic existences, including the [[Regulators of Existence]], certain manifestations of [[Outer Gods]] and other beings whose abilities operate through the foundations of Reality itself.
Universal destructive capability and universal conceptual authority can possess different properties. A being capable of destroying enormous quantities of Matter does not automatically possess the ability to create an autonomous Universe with complete systems of Life, Souls and natural law. Conversely, a being whose authority governs a foundational universal process may exercise extraordinary influence without demonstrating the same method of direct physical destruction as another V-07 entity.[9]
The classification encompasses a broad range of powers associated with Universal-scale existence. Its upper boundaries therefore require consideration of the actual capabilities demonstrated by individual beings, particularly when their powers involve unusual relationships with Space, Time, Reality or the underlying structure of Creation.

The system is particularly valuable because it identifies named characters at specific comparative levels, including [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]], [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett|Scathach Scarlett]], Rose Adrasteia, [[Volk Fenrir]], Evie Moriarty, Agnes Snow, Natashia Fulger and numerous Gods and Demons.

#### Primordial Beings
The seven Primordial Beings exist outside the conventional comparative ranks of Gods and Mortals. Their importance derives from their connection to the maintenance of the Universe and its foundational systems. The original auxiliary hierarchy places [[Primordial Chaos]] at the highest level of importance, followed by Positivity and Negativity, Infinity, Death, the Universal Tree, the Judges of the Abyss and the Guardian of Limbo.[2]
The hierarchy reflects cosmic significance rather than a conventional duel-based ranking. These beings embody or regulate aspects of Reality whose continued existence is integral to the operation of Creation, and their powers cannot be evaluated solely through the physical strength of their manifestations.

#### Rank 0 — Primordial Gods
Rank 0 encompasses the original [[Primordial Gods]] of the individual Pantheons and their exceptionally powerful descendants. The classification includes Nyx Elderblood, [[Gaia]] Elderblood, [[Erebus]], [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] and comparable Divine beings associated with the earliest Pantheon generations.[2]
The hierarchy recognizes substantial differences between beings occupying the same rank. Original Primordial Gods possess greater foundational authority than many of their descendants, even when both belong to the wider classification of Primordial Divinities. The difference between Nyx and a younger descendant such as [[Thanatos]] can be enormous despite their placement within the same general category.

#### Rank 1 — Destruction and End
Rank 1 includes powerful beings whose supernatural authorities involve Destruction, Apocalypse or the Concept of <END>. [[Shiva]] and entities such as [[Fenrir]] and [[Typhon]] are representative examples.
The powers of these beings can be exceptionally dangerous because they involve Concepts capable of bypassing ordinary physical durability. However, the ranking does not establish equal strength among all its members. Shiva possesses a much broader and more developed supernatural existence than certain beings whose principal danger arises from one exceptionally powerful Concept.[2]

#### Rank 2 — Creator Gods
Rank 2 includes major Creator Gods such as the [[Heavenly Father]] and Uranus. Their powers possess important relationships with the creation of supernatural structures and the maintenance or generation of existence within the limits of their respective Divine authorities.
Their abilities can differ substantially from the destructive powers associated with Rank 1. A Creator God may possess greater versatility or creative authority while not reproducing the same combat methods as a deity whose principal Concept is Destruction.[2]

#### Rank 3 — God-Kings
Rank 3 encompasses powerful [[God-Kings]] such as [[Zeus]], [[Odin]], [[Indra]], [[Amaterasu]] and Ra. The classification also includes exceptional beings capable of competing with major Pantheon rulers through unusual supernatural characteristics.[2]
The effectiveness of a God-King can increase considerably through specialized Divine Artifacts. Zeus, for example, possesses [[Poseidon|the Master]] Bolt, whose powers substantially enhance his combat capabilities and permit him to approach opponents whose underlying Divine existence may otherwise be superior.
Vlad is particularly significant because his Progenitor abilities provide an advantage against Gods through the manipulation and destruction of Souls. His complete threat can therefore exceed the limitations suggested by his ordinary physical statistics.

#### Rank 4 — Supreme Supernatural Beings
Rank 4 encompasses exceptionally powerful supernatural rulers and ancient beings whose full combat capabilities can threaten major Gods. The author's examples include [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]], [[Diablo]], Lilith, [[Hades]] and other supreme rulers of Hell.[2]
Vlad occupies an unusual position because his normal physical capabilities belong around this general range, while his Progenitor authority allows him to threaten beings ranked above him. Diablo similarly possesses corruptive powers capable of weakening enormously powerful Divine opponents, demonstrating how specialized abilities can increase a combatant's danger beyond their conventional statistics.

#### Rank 5 — Apex Supernatural Combatants
Rank 5 encompasses the strongest conventional combatants belonging to the major supernatural races, together with powerful combat-oriented Gods and exceptional Demonic or Angelic beings.
The author explicitly placed [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett|Scathach Scarlett]], Rose Adrasteia, [[Volk Fenrir]] and Evie Moriarty within this category. Powerful Gods such as [[Ares]] and [[Thor]] also belong here, alongside high-ranking Angels, the Seven Heavenly Virtues, powerful Demonic Dukes, the Seven Deadly Sins and the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse.[2]
[[Scathach]] occupies the rank through her extraordinary combat skill, supernatural strength and mastery of the [[Spears|Spear]]. Rose Adrasteia belongs within the same range because her swordsmanship reached an exceptionally advanced level, with her specialization granting her an advantage in that particular discipline. Volk represents the immense strength available through Werewolf royal authority and Pack-based supernatural development, while Evie is included principally because of the versatility, complexity and effectiveness of her [[Magic]].
Agnes and Natashia also reach this rank in their complete Vampire Count forms, demonstrating the enormous difference between a Count's ordinary abilities and the power available through their advanced racial transformations.

#### Rank 6 — Elite Supernatural Beings
Rank 6 encompasses powerful supernatural elites, including Mid-Level Gods, older Noble Vampires such as Agnes and Natashia in their ordinary forms, elite Werewolf Generals, some daughters of the Witch Queen and advanced Angelic or Demonic combatants.[2]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s comparative placement around the auxiliary reference point also illustrates the importance of separating available Energy from combat proficiency. His exceptional relationship with Roxanne and the passive benefits associated with his Progenitor status provided him with reserves comparable to powerful Gods, while his ability to employ those resources efficiently remained substantially less developed. His complete potential therefore exceeded what could be demonstrated through his ordinary control at that stage.

### Exceptional Power and Outliers
Certain supernatural beings possess abilities or demonstrate feats substantially beyond the normal expectations associated with their rank, species or Divine generation. These cases are important when evaluating the upper limits of individual power because the effects may extend far beyond conventional physical destruction or exploit properties of Reality that ordinary combatants cannot manipulate.

#### Ikor-Kar-VI — Cross-Universal Displacement
EXCEPTIONAL COSMIC FEAT • CHAPTER 884
[[Ikor-Kar-VI]] — Hundreds of Universes
During the final stages of his battle with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Ikor-Kar-VI released a concentrated attack whose trajectory was altered through spatial manipulation. The attack struck Victor and sent him through hundreds of universes, demonstrating extraordinary cross-universal displacement and the ability to affect a target across immense cosmological boundaries. The feat represents an exceptional application of power associated with an individual Elder God rather than the baseline of all Primordial or Elder Gods.[10]
Ikor-Kar-VI was an exceptionally powerful Elder God whose final confrontation with Victor occurred at the [[Edge of the Cosmos]]. The battle involved supernatural powers operating under conditions where the ordinary laws of Space and Time did not function as they did within the stable regions of Creation.
After Victor awakened his Divinities and underwent his transformation into the Dragon God of [[Chaos]], Ikor attempted to overwhelm him through a final concentrated attack. Rather than relying exclusively upon a conventional straight-line projectile, Ikor manipulated the surrounding spatial conditions so that his power appeared directly before Victor, striking him in the chest and sending him through hundreds of sectors.[10]
The feat is exceptional for several reasons. First, its range extended through numerous distinct universes rather than remaining confined to the astronomical structures of one ordinary Sector. Second, the attack incorporated advanced manipulation of Space, allowing Ikor to redirect his power through a cosmological environment where normal spatial relationships were unstable. Third, the movement inflicted stress through the process of crossing universal boundaries, even though Victor's newly developed protection prevented the attack itself from inflicting meaningful direct damage.
The feat provides evidence that Ikor possessed an exceptional capacity to manipulate power and spatial displacement across a scale far beyond ordinary Planetary, Solar-System or Galactic destruction. Its demonstrated cross-universal reach can be considered extraordinary even among higher supernatural beings, and it deserves recognition as an individual outlier relative to the conventional expectations of the older God hierarchy.
However, the attack did not demonstrate that Ikor destroyed the hundreds of universes through which Victor passed. Its greatest demonstrated characteristics were the immense displacement, spatial manipulation and the consequences of moving a target through universal boundaries. A direct comparison between that feat and the total destructive output of a V-07 entity therefore depends upon the nature of the abilities being compared.

#### Exceptional Progenitor Abilities
[[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] represents a different form of exceptional power. His ordinary physical capabilities were placed around Rank 4 of the author's original hierarchy, but his Progenitor abilities could threaten [[God-Kings]] through the manipulation or destruction of Souls. The author explicitly acknowledged that these supernatural characteristics allowed Vlad to occupy a higher effective combat position than his physical statistics would ordinarily suggest.[2]
[[Victor Elderblood]] subsequently demonstrated the extraordinary potential of Progenitor authority through his progressive development, including powers associated with Blood, Souls and the fundamental Concept of Beginning. These advantages extended far beyond ordinary physical strength, allowing him to threaten opponents possessing exceptional Divine characteristics.

#### Aphrodite's Conceptual Advantage
[[Aphrodite]] illustrates how specialized Divine powers can overcome differences in ordinary combat ability. Despite possessing less direct fighting strength than a combat-oriented deity such as [[Ares]], her powers associated with Love and Beauty can influence exceptionally powerful Divine beings. The author's auxiliary ranking specifically identifies her Charm as a dangerous ability even against [[God-Kings]], while noting that beings with suitable powers may counter or negate its effects.[2]
Aphrodite's danger therefore derives principally from the nature of her Divine abilities rather than a conventional comparison of physical statistics.

#### Agares and Dimensional Combat
[[Agares]] possesses exceptional abilities involving the manipulation of dimensions and access to the [[Black and White World]]. The nature of those powers can allow him to overcome opponents occupying comparable or even higher conventional rankings when they lack the appropriate defenses or are caught unprepared.[2]
His abilities demonstrate that positional advantages, dimensional manipulation and the exploitation of an opponent's vulnerabilities can significantly alter the result of a battle.

#### Combat Gods
[[Combat Gods]] possess particularly developed fighting capabilities compared with Gods whose powers principally concern noncombat functions. The classification includes Low-, Mid- and High-Level Combat Gods, distinguishing Divine combat ability from the broader nature of Godhood.[2]
Gods such as [[Ares]] and [[Thor]] demonstrate the effectiveness of specialized Divine combatants, whose martial abilities, weapons and supernatural characteristics can threaten beings possessing considerable raw Energy or important Divine positions.

#### God-Kings
[[God-Kings]] are exceptional Divine beings associated with rulership over Pantheons or possession of powers comparable to their strongest members. Major examples include [[Zeus]], [[Odin]], [[Indra]], [[Amaterasu]] Elderblood and Ra.
The strength of a God-King can be influenced by their Divine Artifacts and the systems supporting their Pantheon. Zeus, for example, possesses a [[Master Bolt]] capable of substantially increasing his offensive capabilities and allowing him to confront opponents who would otherwise surpass his conventional strength.[2]

#### Primordial Gods
[[Primordial Gods]] are ancient Divine existences associated with the original or earliest generations of their Pantheons. Their classification includes original beings such as [[Gaia]], Nyx, [[Erebus]] and [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]], as well as powerful descendants whose supernatural origins preserve their Primordial characteristics.[2][11]

#### Semi-Primordial Beings
Certain exceptional Gods occupy a transitional category commonly described as Semi-Primordial. Such beings possess Divine capabilities approaching those associated with [[Primordial Gods]] without necessarily belonging to the same foundational generation.
Examples of exceptionally developed Divine beings include Kali, [[Gaia]], [[Amaterasu]] and other powerful members of the Elderblood Pantheon whose supernatural evolution substantially altered their original characteristics. The classification concerns the nature and development of the individual rather than functioning as a universally fixed numerical power stage.

Primordial Beings occupy a separate cosmic category associated with the foundational structure of the Universe. Their existence encompasses the fundamental mechanisms through which Creation operates, including the systems sustaining Life, Death, Souls and other universal functions.[9]

### Vampire Hierarchy
Main article: Vampires
The Vampire Hierarchy encompasses racial development, Bloodline strength, advanced Vampiric transformations and the exceptional authority of Progenitors. Its traditional political structure developed beneath [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]], the First Vampire Progenitor and former ruler of [[Nightingale]], but combat power among Vampires varies considerably according to individual experience, inherited characteristics and personal growth.

#### Ordinary and Noble Vampires
Ordinary transformed Vampires possess supernatural physical characteristics and racial abilities whose strength depends upon their origin and the power of their master. Noble Vampires possess living bodies, inherited Bloodlines and the ability to reproduce, establishing a distinction between their biological existence and that of Vampire Slaves.
The supernatural power of Nobles can vary enormously. Ordinary members of the great Clans may possess considerably less strength than their ruling Counts, whose experience and advanced transformations allow them to confront opponents far above conventional Vampire standards.

#### Vampire Count-Class Combatants
The traditional Counts of [[Nightingale]] represented some of the strongest Mortal supernatural beings of their era. Agnes Snow and Natashia Fulger possessed destructive powers capable of devastating countries, while [[Scathach]] was recognized as the strongest of the Counts through her extraordinary physical strength, martial proficiency and mastery of supernatural techniques.
The author's auxiliary ranking places Agnes and Natashia at Rank 6 in base form and Rank 5 in complete Vampire Count form. Scathach also occupies Rank 5, but her combat effectiveness is exceptional because of her skill with the [[Spears|Spear]] and the properties of her specialized weaponry.[2]
The Count transformations dramatically increase the combat effectiveness of their users, allowing them to fight powerful Gods and other supernatural beings whose ordinary statistics exceed those of most Noble Vampires.

#### Scathach Scarlett
[[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett|Scathach Scarlett]] was traditionally the strongest Vampire Count of [[Nightingale]]. Her strength arose from a combination of exceptional physical capabilities, thousands of years of experience, advanced Bloodline control, mastery of the [[Spears|Spear]] and specialized supernatural weapons capable of inflicting serious injuries upon Gods.
Within the original author hierarchy, [[Scathach]] belonged to Rank 5, alongside exceptionally powerful Mortal-race combatants such as Rose Adrasteia, [[Volk Fenrir]] and Evie Moriarty. In the early Imperial V-system comparison, her ordinary fighting strength belonged around the upper Continental range, while her specialized weaponry could raise her complete threat beyond that baseline.[2][1]
Her subsequent transformation into a Dragon and continued development of martial and Divine powers placed her far above her former Vampire Count state. Her later capabilities reach Galactic and potentially greater scales through exceptional techniques and the resources available to her.[4]

#### Vlad Tepes
[[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] is the First Vampire Progenitor and the strongest non-Dragon Noble Vampire identified in the late Imperial comparison. His extraordinary authority over Blood and Souls provides offensive capabilities substantially beyond those of ordinary Noble Vampires.
The auxiliary hierarchy places Vlad around Rank 4 in ordinary physical comparison, with the ability to threaten Rank 3 [[God-Kings]] through his Progenitor powers. His Soul-based abilities are particularly dangerous because they can permanently destroy Divine beings whose physical durability would otherwise protect them against conventional attacks.[2]
By the mature Imperial era, Vlad possesses Solar-System-class combat capabilities and belongs among the Empire's elite beings capable of destroying entire Solar Systems.[7]

#### Tatsuya Rider
[[Tatsuya Rider]] developed into the second strongest Noble Vampire beneath Vlad without accepting a transformation into one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Dragons. His continued training and development elevated his existence far beyond the conventional limits associated with ordinary Noble Vampires.
Tatsuya possesses Solar-System-class destructive capabilities and is recognized among the Empire's elite combatants. His development is particularly significant because it demonstrates the exceptional heights attainable through personal strength, discipline and supernatural growth without relying upon Victor's Draconic lineage.[7]

### Werewolf Hierarchy
Main article: [[Werewolves]]
The Werewolf Hierarchy is based upon Pack dominance, Alpha authority, inherited Bloodlines and the supernatural advantages associated with commanding subordinate Werewolves. Unlike Vampire aristocracy, whose political offices do not necessarily correspond to personal fighting strength, Werewolf leadership possesses a more direct relationship with supernatural dominance.

#### Alpha Werewolves
Alpha [[Werewolves]] possess supernatural authority over their Packs. Their strength depends upon individual development, Bloodline and the number and quality of the Werewolves recognizing their leadership. The ruler of a large Pack can obtain considerable advantages over an otherwise comparable individual possessing fewer subordinates.[12]

#### Werewolf Generals
Werewolf Generals represent the upper levels of the traditional military hierarchy. Their capabilities substantially exceed those of ordinary Pack members and may approach the strength of powerful Noble Vampires or advanced Witches.
The author placed elite Werewolf Generals within the general range of Rank 6, although individual differences remain important because of Bloodline strength and exceptional combat development.[2]

#### Volk Fenrir
[[Volk Fenrir]] historically ruled Samar as the King of [[Werewolves]]. His Pack authority extended over the greater Werewolf civilization, providing extraordinary supernatural advantages associated with the enormous number of Werewolves beneath his rule.
The author placed Volk within Rank 5, alongside [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett|Scathach Scarlett]], Rose Adrasteia and Evie Moriarty. His fighting strength and the advantages provided by his Pack made him one of the most powerful conventional Mortal-race rulers of the original supernatural world.[2]

#### Edward
Edward is one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s childhood friends and a powerful Werewolf associated with the Imperial supernatural world. His development places him among the notable Werewolf combatants of the mature Empire.
A V-05 classification is a reasonable comparative possibility when considering the extraordinary growth achieved by Victor's closest companions and the demonstrated V-05 ratings of Frederick and Andrew. However, Edward's precise Imperial Threat Level is not explicitly established in the verified passages used for this comparison.
His classification is therefore best maintained as an estimate rather than an official recorded V-code until a passage confirms his Solar-System-class capabilities.

#### Master Witches
Master Witches possess advanced knowledge and control over [[Magic]], allowing them to perform complex supernatural techniques whose effectiveness may extend beyond their ordinary physical durability.
The author's auxiliary hierarchy includes established Master Witches among the powerful Mortal combatants occupying the lower portions of the original ranking, with exceptionally powerful members of the race reaching higher positions through their individual knowledge and capabilities.[2]

#### The Witch Queen's Daughters
The daughters of the Witch Queen occupy elite positions within Witch society. Their magical proficiency and political authority place them above ordinary practitioners, although the strength of each individual depends upon their own development and specialization.
The author placed some of the Queen's daughters within Rank 6, alongside elite Werewolf Generals, older Noble Vampires and Mid-Level Gods.[2]

#### Evie Moriarty
Evie Moriarty is the Witch Queen and one of the most powerful conventional Witches of the original supernatural world. Her strengths lie principally in the sophistication of her [[Magic]], versatility, extensive knowledge and ability to employ supernatural techniques in ways that can overcome opponents possessing greater raw physical strength.
The author explicitly placed Evie within Rank 5, alongside [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett|Scathach Scarlett]], Rose Adrasteia and [[Volk Fenrir]]. Her placement was associated primarily with her magical capabilities and intelligence rather than conventional melee strength.[2]
An exact Imperial V-05 classification for Evie cannot be derived from this authorial Rank 5 alone. Her later development and Imperial resources may substantially increase her effective danger, but those capabilities require their own demonstrated Solar-System-scale evidence before receiving a confirmed V-05 designation.

#### High-Ranking Demons
High-ranking Demons possess extraordinary supernatural strength, with the most powerful representatives capable of confronting advanced Noble Vampires, elite Angels and Gods. Their powers vary considerably according to individual lineage and specialized abilities.

#### The Seventy-Two Dukes
The Seventy-Two Dukes of Hell represent an important political and supernatural classification within [[Biblical Hell]]. Powerful Dukes such as [[Baal]] and [[Agares]] possess abilities capable of threatening other high-ranking supernatural beings, although their specialized characteristics differ considerably.
The author placed certain exceptional Dukes within Rank 5, recognizing their ability to compete with the strongest Mortal-race combatants and powerful Gods.[2]

#### The Seven Deadly Sins
The Seven Deadly Sins represent exceptional supernatural beings whose powers are associated with the Concepts of the principal Sins. Their abilities may involve destructive Energy, corruption, emotional influence or other specialized effects whose potency depends upon the individual.
Powerful members of the group were included within the author's Rank 5 comparisons, reflecting their position among the greatest Demonic combatants.[2]

#### The Four Horsemen
The Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse occupy a special position within infernal power classifications. Their supernatural roles include War, Pestilence, Famine and Death, providing powers whose effectiveness cannot be reduced exclusively to ordinary physical strength.
The author included the exceptional Horsemen among the beings occupying Rank 5, although their individual capabilities depend upon the nature of their respective powers.[2]

#### Diablo and Lilith
[[Diablo]] and Lilith represent exceptional Demonic existences standing beyond the ordinary hierarchy of Dukes and Generals. Their supernatural powers and origin-level characteristics place them among the most dangerous beings associated with Hell.
The author assigned both to Rank 4, alongside other supreme supernatural rulers. Diablo's corruptive powers could influence Gods whose conventional strength exceeded his own, while Lilith possessed the extraordinary characteristics associated with the origin of Demonkind.[2]

### Other Supernatural Hierarchies

#### Angels
Main article: Angels
The Heavenly hierarchy includes ordinary Angels, advanced Angelic orders, Archangels, Seraphim and the Seven Heavenly Virtues. Their strength depends upon their racial origin, [[Holy Energy]], specialized abilities and their positions within the Heavenly system.
Exceptional Angels such as Michael, [[Gabriel]] and other members of the highest Heavenly orders were placed among the author's Rank 5 combatants, reflecting the immense strength available to the upper levels of the Angelic hierarchy.[2]

#### Humans and Hunters
Main article: Humans
Humans generally possess less physical power than the principal supernatural races, but advanced combat training, supernatural weapons, [[Magic]], Faith and specialized techniques can allow exceptional individuals to confront vastly stronger opponents.
[[Andrew Walter]] is among the strongest known Human-origin [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] associated with the Imperial Inquisition. His personal strength is Planetary, while his ability to access Divine power through the Imperial Inquisition raises his complete operational classification to V-05.[3]
[[Frederick Winter]] possesses a comparable operational classification through his Onmyō techniques, although his specialization belongs to a different supernatural discipline. The two therefore represent important examples of Human-origin practitioners capable of reaching Solar-System-class threat potential through advanced training and access to Divine power.

#### Dragons
Main article: Dragons
Dragons possess an exceptional racial power system based upon Draconic physiology, the [[Dragon Heart]], Dragon Energy, inherited characteristics and the ability to impose supernatural changes through the Draconic Language. [[True Dragons]] can possess abilities surpassing those of conventional Gods, but their actual strength varies significantly according to individual development.
The Dragon lineage established through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] includes beings whose powers reach Galactic and greater scales, especially among his most developed wives and daughters. Their capabilities cannot be accurately represented through the traditional rankings governing ordinary Vampires or Gods because their existence combines several exceptionally powerful supernatural systems.

#### Outer Gods
Main article: [[Outer Gods]]
Outer Gods possess supernatural characteristics fundamentally different from those of ordinary beings belonging to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s native cosmology. Their powers can involve [[Eldritch Energy]], unusual conceptual authorities and methods of interacting with Reality beyond those available through conventional Divine systems.
Their individual Threat Levels depend upon their demonstrated abilities, since belonging to the Outer God classification does not establish identical strength among all members of the race.

#### Overlords
Main article: Overlords
Overlords are exceptional beings associated with supremacy over Sectors and the civilizations contained within them. Their strength can encompass enormous supernatural reserves, powerful Divine authorities and the military or technological resources necessary to dominate vast astronomical territories.
Overlord status identifies a position within the broader cosmic hierarchy, while the Imperial Threat Level describes the maximum scale of danger represented by the individual.

### References and Terminology
The Imperial Threat Level codes and the original authorial hierarchy are independent systems, each possessing its own classification criteria. Their respective ranks are most accurately understood through the abilities and comparisons associated with the individuals occupying them. Racial classifications such as Noble Vampire, Alpha Werewolf, Witch Queen and Primordial God describe particular supernatural characteristics or positions, while the Imperial V-codes represent the scale of danger an individual can produce through the complete powers available to them.
Exceptional beings may possess capabilities extending beyond the ordinary expectations of their race or historical ranking. Their placement is therefore determined by the specific powers demonstrated, including extraordinary combat techniques, specialized artifacts, conceptual authorities and unusual interactions with the structure of Reality.
