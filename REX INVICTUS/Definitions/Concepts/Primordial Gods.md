**[[Primordial Gods]]** are the uppermost conventional class of Divine Beings, existing beyond ordinary Gods and the standard God-King hierarchy. (Lore adapted from *My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** This material is adapted into the Rex Invictus world at the lowest priority: Rex Invictus wins any conflict, then the Flamel material, then MTWABV. Events of the MTWABV timeline (Victor's conquests, the fall of the pantheons, the [[Elderblood Empire]]) are distant background or rumor unless a chapter puts them on the page. ## MTWABV Canon

Upper Divine & Conceptual Archive
PRIMORDIAL GODS
The highest conventional Divine existences below true [[Primordial Entities]]
Primordial Gods are the uppermost conventional class of Divine Beings, existing beyond ordinary Gods and the standard God-King hierarchy. Their [[Divine Souls]], Energy, bodies and relationships with their Divinities have reached a level where they can serve as foundational Gods of Pantheons, wield extremely developed Conceptual authority and survive or produce physical forces that would erase ordinary worlds. The class is enormously broad: its lower physical benchmark is conservatively planetary / CODE V-04, while developed Primordial-tier combatants can reach Stellar, Solar-System and CODE V-06 Galactic threat ranges or higher.[1][2]
Scope of this article: This page covers conventional Primordial Gods, their generational and achieved power differences, the secondary Dragon God branch of Primordial-tier development, and Quasi-Primordial Entities approaching true Primordial Entity existence. True universal Primordial Entities are the next ontological order and are not merely stronger Primordial Gods.
PHYSICAL SCALE ≠ CONCEPTUAL SCALE
All destructive benchmarks on this page—planetary, Stellar, Solar-System or Galactic—refer principally to raw physical, Energy-based or directly destructive capability. They are not ceilings for a Primordial Being's Conceptual authority. A Primordial God associated with Time, Death, Space, Luck, Destruction, Life or another high-order Concept may affect structures vastly beyond the area they can destroy through brute force alone.[3]

### What Is a Primordial God?
Primordial Gods are Divine Beings whose existence has surpassed ordinary Godhood to such a degree that their Souls, Divinities and Energy exist on an entirely different scale. Many original Primordial Gods emerged near the foundation of their Pantheons and consequently possess extraordinarily deep relationships with the Divine systems surrounding them.
Common characteristics include:
- enormous Divine or equivalent Energy reserves;
- extremely developed Souls;
- powerful physical bodies;
- advanced Conceptual authority;
- greatly developed primary Divinities;
- resistance to conventional supernatural attacks;
- Divine immortality and extreme regeneration;
- deep interaction with Pantheon systems or natural laws;
- the ability to survive planetary or greater levels of physical force;
- and the potential to affect phenomena far beyond their raw destructive scale through Concepts.
Primordial Godhood does not mean that the individual is the absolute universal incarnation of their Concept. Kronus, for example, possessed Primordial authority over Time, but [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly rejected Kronus' arrogance in acting as though he were Time itself.[4]
A Primordial God can:
possess, embody or personify a Divinity
without being:
the universal Aspect from which that Concept fundamentally originates.
That distinction separates conventional Primordial Gods from true [[Primordial Entities]].

### Generation and Power
The terms First-Generation and Second-Generation Primordial God originally describe lineage and origin, but they are also useful comparative power benchmarks. They should never be treated as permanent ceilings.

#### Second-Generation Primordial Gods
Second-Generation Primordial Gods form the lower conventional Primordial class. Many are children or later descendants of older Primordial Gods, although sufficiently evolved outsiders can attain equivalent strength.
Examples traditionally associated with this broad generation include Kronus, [[Rhea]], [[Hypnos]], [[Thanatos]], [[Nemesis]] and other descendants of original Primordial lineages.
The Author's early ranking makes clear that being Primordial does not make all such Beings remotely equal. The gap between younger Primordial Gods such as Hypnos or Thanatos and an older existence such as Nyx could be enormous.[1]
Therefore:
same generation ≠ same power
same Concept category ≠ same mastery
same Primordial status ≠ same combat capability

##### Peak God-King Overlap
The bottom of the Primordial hierarchy overlaps with the upper God-King bracket.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was already a God-King-level Being by Chapter 690,[5] yet later defeated Kronus, a genuine Primordial God, before undergoing his next major racial transformation.[4]
This means:
Peak God-King
can possess enough combat capability to defeat
a lower Primordial God
without becoming Primordial in origin or physiology.
Victor's subsequent Blood Dragon Primogenitor evolution in Chapter 803 provided a more appropriate direct Second-Generation Primordial baseline for his new existence.[6]

#### First-Generation / Older Primordial Gods
First-Generation, Original and Older Primordial Gods refer to the foundational generation of Pantheon Gods and later Beings whose complete development has reached a comparable standard.
Common examples include:
- [[Gaia]];
- Nyx;
- [[Erebus]];
- [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]];
- [[Ouranos]];
- [[Heavenly Father]];
- [[Izanagi]];
- [[Izanami-no-Mikoto]];
- and other Creator or foundational Gods.
Older Primordial God is therefore both an origin category and, in appropriate contexts, a comparative power benchmark.
The Author specifically described a later [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] as operating around the level of an older Primordial God while emphasizing that Victor still was not a true Primordial Being representing a Universal Aspect.[3]
This is one of the clearest pieces of evidence for keeping the two concepts separate:
Older Primordial God
≠
Universal Primordial Entity

#### Peak Primordial Gods
Peak Primordial Gods are Beings who have progressed beyond ordinary Older Primordial standards while still remaining fundamentally within conventional Divine or equivalent existence.
The term is most useful for individuals who can decisively dominate normal Primordial Gods but have not yet begun the ontological merger that characterizes a Quasi-Primordial Entity.
By Chapter 1085, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] demonstrated this type of superiority when his unsuppressed existence caused lesser Gods to die and made normal Primordial Gods struggle merely to endure his presence.[7] Kali—herself extraordinarily powerful—was not confident that she could casually defeat him.
Peak Primordial development may result from:
- further growth of the Soul;
- greater Energy reserves and Energy quality;
- complete or near-complete mastery of primary Divinities;
- stronger physical or racial evolution;
- accumulation of secondary Concepts;
- superior control;
- enormous combat experience;
- Divine or racial artifacts;
- and increasingly direct synchronization between the individual and their principal Concept.
Peak Primordial is still fundamentally a power/development state. It does not automatically mean the Soul has become part of the Concept itself.

### Dragon Gods
Dragon Gods and Dragon Goddesses are indexed as a secondary Primordial-tier branch. They combine genuine Divinity with Draconic physiology and the exceptional adaptability of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Dragon line.
Their importance to the hierarchy is that Dragon Godhood is not merely "ordinary Godhood plus Dragon traits". [[True Dragons]] possess a fundamentally different relationship with Reality and Energy. Chapter 1171 explains that True Dragons are effectively manifestations or embodiments of Nature itself and possess compatibility with an immense variety of phenomena, while ordinary Dragons are weaker members of that broader lineage.[8]
Later teachings to [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]] expand this principle. A True Dragon does not require a spell or Rune simply to command ordinary Energy; broad Energy interaction is part of its biology. Draconic Runes become particularly important when the Dragon wishes to impose Concepts, laws or alterations directly upon Reality.[9]

#### Draconic Hierarchy
The late Elderblood hierarchy distinguishes:
Lesser Dragon
→ Dragon
→ True Dragon
→ Dragon God / Dragon Goddess
with additional variations produced by lineage, Divinity and individual evolution.
The difference is enormous. Lesser Dragons remain dramatically superior to most conventional species, ordinary Dragons possess greater Draconic qualities, and [[True Dragons]] are described as embodiments of Nature with extraordinary compatibility.[8]
A Dragon God combines this racial framework with genuine Divine development.

#### Planetary Baseline
The lowest demonstrated Dragon-God-associated benchmark is already planetary.
[[Adam Tepes]], Jeanne's son, is described as theoretically possessing the status of a Dragon God from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s lineage. Despite enormous potential, he failed to develop himself properly and was mocked by his own family for being comparatively weak. After passing the family's trials and developing from Lesser Dragon to Dragon, his threat level was still described as "only planetary-level".[8]
Within the Elderblood household, this was considered embarrassingly low.
DRAGON-GOD LOW-END BENCHMARK
Adam Tepes provides a conservative lower Draconic example: planetary-level physical threat. This should not be interpreted as the maximum of Dragon Godhood; it is explicitly portrayed as unusually weak for an individual possessing that lineage.[8]
[[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]] provides another useful benchmark. Despite being criticized as badly trained and inefficient, Albedo possessed the body and Energy of a Dragon Goddess and could produce spells capable of destroying planets.[10] Her problem was not insufficient raw Power but her continued habit of fighting like a conventional Human magician.
[[Violet Elderblood Snow]], a high-ranking Dragon Goddess of Order, demonstrated the enormous disparity that can exist above that floor. Even a remote projection of Violet could spread Order across an entire planet and pacify its hostile creatures and environment, while the actual Goddess was vastly more powerful than the projection.[2]
Thus:
Dragon God status has a very high minimum
but
Dragon God status does not determine a fixed maximum

### Quasi-Primordial Entities
Quasi-[[Primordial Entities]] are Beings who have already surpassed the conventional limits of Primordial Godhood and are in the process of becoming true Primordial Entities.
This is the most important correction to the older hierarchy.
A Quasi-Primordial is not simply a particularly powerful Peak Primordial God.
Their development has begun to become ontological.
THE QUASI-PRIMORDIAL THRESHOLD
A conventional Primordial God uses and embodies a Concept through Divinity. A Quasi-Primordial Entity has progressed far enough that their body, Soul and existence are beginning to merge with the Concept itself. They have surpassed ordinary Primordial Gods but have not yet completed the transformation into the universal Primordial Entity responsible for that Aspect.
The progression is therefore more accurately represented as:
Peak Primordial God
→ Concept increasingly permeates body and Soul
→ existence begins synchronizing with Concept
→ Quasi-Primordial Entity
→ completed Conceptual integration
→ Primordial Entity
Older wiki terminology sometimes used Semi-Primordial Being for this region. For consistency, this article uses Quasi-Primordial Entity for the actual transitional state.

#### Kali Elderblood
[[Kali Elderblood]] is the clearest confirmed example.
Kali's understanding of Destruction advanced so far that ordinary Primordial Goddess classification ceased to describe her development adequately. By Chapter 1096, she is explicitly identified as a Quasi-Primordial Entity of the Universe.[11]
Her development was not simply an increase in Energy. Destruction had begun permeating and transforming her existence.
This distinction is crucial:
more [[Divine Energy]]
does not create
a Quasi-Primordial Entity
The transition requires a much deeper integration between Soul, existence and Concept.

#### Tyche Elderblood
[[Tyche]] provides an extraordinary non-combat example of a Primordial Goddess approaching the same transition.
By Chapter 1170, Tyche explained that she could feel the Concept associated with Happiness increasingly flooding her body. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] recognized the process immediately: her body was preparing to merge with her Concept as a Primordial Being, directly comparing what was happening to Tyche with Kali's earlier evolution.[12]
Tyche had not yet completed the transformation. She should therefore be indexed as:
Primordial Goddess
→ approaching Quasi-Primordial transition
rather than prematurely listed as a completed Primordial Entity.
Her physical development is equally revealing. Victor's castle contains enormous doors designed so that even young Dragons can eventually operate them. Tyche attempted to open one and struggled visibly—but succeeded. The door's effective weight under Earth gravity was stated to equal approximately the mass of [[Pluto]]: 13 sextillion kilograms.[12]
The narration explicitly emphasizes that an ordinary Primordial Goddess would never be capable of the feat.[12]
NON-COMBAT PRIMORDIAL ≠ PHYSICALLY WEAK
Tyche is not presented as a martial specialist. Nevertheless, her late physical body could move a door with a stated effective weight comparable to Pluto, and the narration places the feat beyond an ordinary Primordial Goddess. Her limitations concern practical combat training and technique rather than a lack of enormous raw physical capability.[12]
Tyche is therefore one of the best examples of why combat specialization and Primordial development must remain separate.

The late story explains that true Primordial Beings are not merely Gods with more power. They are effectively living universal functions. Destroying a Primordial responsible for Life, Death or another necessary universal Aspect without replacing that role could cripple the Cosmos itself.[13][14]
Chapter 1177 reinforces the divide even more severely: ordinary mortals and conventional Divine Beings cannot simply defeat Beings that literally exist as universal Concepts through ordinary combat.[15]
This is why Quasi-Primordial Entity is an important transitional classification. A Quasi-Primordial has begun crossing the ontological boundary but has not yet become the completed universal mechanism.

### Physical Destructive Baseline
Primordial Godhood should be assigned a conservative physical baseline of at least planetary / V-04.
This represents raw bodily, Energy or directly destructive output, not maximum Conceptual authority.
The baseline is supported by several independent comparisons:
- The God-King and Primordial-overlap battles around the [[Greek Underworld]] were already producing destructive force explicitly described as sufficient to destroy Earth several times over.[16]
- Weak or underdeveloped members of the later Draconic Divine line are still treated as planetary threats.[8]
- A poorly trained Dragon Goddess such as Albedo can nevertheless produce planet-destroying magic.[10]
- By the mature Elderblood era, planetary combat prowess is casually described as merely above average within the Imperial elite ecosystem.[2]
For wiki indexing:
Conservative Primordial physical floor: CODE V-04 / Planetary.
This is a minimum benchmark for raw destructive or physical capability, not a universal statement that every Primordial God fights identically or possesses the same attack style.

### Overlords
Main article: Overlords
Overlord is primarily a title describing sovereignty over a Sector or equivalent civilization-scale domain. It is not itself a formal Primordial generation.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became recognized as the effective Overlord of his Sector after consolidating its major civilizations beneath the [[Elderblood Empire]].[18][19]
However, the strongest Overlords encountered in the wider Cosmos also occupy extraordinarily high personal power ranges. The Author's later mechanics notes describe the highest-ranked Beings of the System's wider list as Overlords wielding extremely developed Divine Concepts and identifies only a tiny number of individuals as realistic one-on-one threats to the contemporary Victor.[3]

#### Light and Darkness
The Upper-Sector hierarchy introduces supreme rulers associated with <Light>, <Darkness>, <Emperor> and <Law>.[20]
Light rules the [[Veillon Empire]] and represents one of the greatest known Upper-Sector authorities. His civilization already possessed weapon systems capable of erasing an entire Solar System during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s first reconnaissance encounter, and his personal conceptual association with Positivity places him among the highest-order Overlord threats introduced outside the universal [[Primordial Entities]].[21][20][3]
Darkness occupies a corresponding upper-cosmic position associated with the opposing hierarchy.
For the purposes of this page, Beings such as Light and Darkness should conservatively be indexed as:
Overlord
+
upper Primordial-tier / V-06-at-minimum threat context
unless a chapter provides a more exact personal classification.
Overlord ≠ Primordial stage. An Overlord can possess whatever personal power class their own development supports. Light and Darkness are relevant here because the known upper Overlords stand within the same broad cosmic battlefield as the strongest Primordial and V-06 Beings, not because every Sector ruler is automatically a Peak Primordial God.

### Conceptual Power
At Primordial levels, the Concept can matter more than the amount of Energy being released.
The Author's later mechanics explanation explicitly emphasizes that Divine battles at this scale depend heavily upon:
- the importance of the Divinity;
- the degree of development;
- compatibility between opposing Concepts;
- artifacts;
- combat experience;
- and the user's ability to impose their authority.[3]
Concepts such as:

Creation
Destruction
Positivity
Negativity
Life
Death
Infinity
Time
Space
Luck / Happiness
can produce effects wildly disproportionate to the user's visible explosive output.

#### Physical Output Is Not a Conceptual Ceiling
A V-04 Primordial God is not limited to affecting only one planet with every ability.
For example:
- A Death authority may directly attack Souls rather than terrain.
- Time may act on temporal structures without destroying surrounding matter.
- Space may manipulate or separate dimensions without producing an explosion.
- Luck can influence causal outcomes despite possessing little conventional destructive use.
- Destruction may erase matter or structures at the Conceptual level.
- Negativity and Positivity can permeate vast layers of Reality independently of the user's ordinary physical punches.
Contemporary [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually became the chosen successor of <Negativity>. By Chapter 1176, threatening the Concept itself would have endangered approximately half of the native Universe and destabilized its inseparable relationship with Positivity.[22]
That is a Conceptual consequence. It should not be rewritten as evidence that every ordinary physical attack Victor throws automatically destroys half a Universe.
This rule applies throughout the upper hierarchy.

### Combat and Non-Combat Primordials
Primordial rank describes the Being's overall Divine/existential development, not whether their abilities were designed for battle.

#### Combat-Oriented Primordials
Combat-oriented Beings convert their immense Primordial resources efficiently into direct confrontation. Their advantages may include:
- martial arts;
- weapons;
- combat Divinities;
- high-speed adaptation;
- battle experience;
- specialized killing abilities;
- Draconic Runes;
- or extreme physical evolution.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and [[Scathach]] represent the extreme end of this philosophy. Their threat cannot be understood from reserves alone because their technique allows them to extract far more combat value from the same amount of Power.

#### Non-Combat Primordials
A non-combat Primordial can possess extraordinary physical and Conceptual development while lacking refined martial technique.
[[Tyche]] is the clearest late example. Her principal authority concerns Luck/Happiness rather than direct destruction, yet she can physically overcome a [[Pluto]]-weight benchmark that an ordinary Primordial Goddess could not match.[12]
Her weakness relative to combat specialists is therefore:
combat experience and training
not:
insufficient Primordial existence
This distinction is essential when comparing Primordial Gods.

### Pantheons and Primordial Gods
Many original Primordial Gods are deeply connected to the metaphysical stability of their Pantheons.
The collapse of the Greek Primordial hierarchy during the [[Olympus]] conflict demonstrated why removing foundational Divine Beings can affect far more than an individual battlefield. The oldest Primordial Gods may support:
- local cosmological structures;
- Underworlds and Heavens;
- Divine Rules;
- natural cycles;
- the stability of a Pantheon;
- or the manifestation of important Concepts within that Pantheon's territory.
This should not be confused with universal [[Primordial Entities]]. A Pantheon Primordial may be foundational to one Divine system without being the universal source of the represented Concept.

### Primordial Evolution Is Not Linear
A Primordial Being does not have to follow a single predetermined route.
Possible paths include:
DIVINE DEVELOPMENT
Second-Generation → Older-equivalent → Peak Primordial through Soul, Divinity and Energy development.
DRACONIC EVOLUTION
Dragon Godhood provides a parallel high-level route through Draconic physiology, compatibility and Divinity.
CONCEPTUAL ASCENSION
Peak Primordial → Quasi-Primordial when the individual's existence begins merging with a Concept.
A Dragon God can pursue Conceptual ascension. A born Second-Generation Primordial can surpass First-Generation Gods. A Human can evolve until reaching Primordial-equivalent power. Generation describes origin; it does not dictate destiny.

### Common Misconceptions
Primordial classification errors
“Every Primordial God is a Primordial Entity.”
No. A conventional Primordial God still uses Divinity to embody or control a Concept. A completed Primordial Entity is integrated with a fundamental Aspect of Creation.
“Quasi-Primordial just means a very strong Peak Primordial.”
No. A Quasi-Primordial has begun an ontological transition in which the Concept itself increasingly permeates or merges with the Soul and existence.[11][12]
“Semi-Primordial and Quasi-Primordial should be separate power tiers.”
There is insufficient reason to treat them as independent consecutive stages. This article uses Quasi-Primordial Entity for the transitional Concept-merger state and treats "Semi-Primordial" as older or alternate terminology for the same broad boundary.
“Dragon God comes after First-Generation Primordial God in a strict ladder.”
No. Dragon God is a secondary Draconic Primordial-tier branch. An individual Dragon God can occupy lower, middle, upper or Quasi-Primordial ranges depending upon development.
“A Dragon God is automatically V-06.”
No. Adam provides a very low-end planetary example, while vastly stronger members of the same broader Draconic Divine lineage can reach Galactic or greater threat levels.[8][2]
“Planetary is the maximum for Primordial Gods.”
No. Planetary / V-04 is the conservative physical floor. Developed Beings can reach V-05 and V-06, with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Jeanne and [[Scathach]] among rare multiple-galaxy-level examples.[2]
“V-06 means Quasi-Primordial.”
No. Threat Level measures operational danger and destruction. Quasi-Primordial describes ontological progress toward becoming a universal Conceptual Entity.
“A non-combat Primordial is physically weak.”
No. [[Tyche]] is explicitly non-specialized in combat yet can move a [[Pluto]]-weight door beyond the capacity of an ordinary Primordial Goddess.[12]
“A planetary physical limit also limits Conceptual abilities.”
No. Concepts operate according to their nature, mastery and jurisdiction. Soul, Death, Time, Space, Luck or universal polarity authorities can affect systems much larger than the user's straightforward destructive output.[3]
“First-Generation is always stronger than Second-Generation.”
Not necessarily. Generation describes origin. Exceptional later Beings can grow past their original generation or achieve equivalent Power through evolution.
“Overlord is a Primordial God stage.”
No. Overlord describes Sector-scale sovereignty. The strongest known Overlords happen to possess extremely high personal Power, but the political title and personal class remain separate.
“Light and Darkness are automatically true [[Primordial Entities]].”
No. They are extraordinarily high Upper-Sector authorities and Overlords associated with fundamental powers, but they should not be equated with completed universal Primordial Entities without direct evidence establishing that ontological state.

### Summary
Second-Generation Primordial
Entry conventional Primordial class; overlaps the highest God-King combat ranges.
Older / First-Generation
Foundational Pantheon Gods and Beings attaining equivalent complete development.
Peak Primordial
Primordial God developed beyond ordinary Older Primordial standards while remaining below true Concept-merger.
Dragon God
Secondary Primordial-tier Draconic branch. Weakest demonstrated members can be planetary, while elite Dragon Gods reach vastly higher levels.[8][2]
Quasi-Primordial Entity
Has surpassed ordinary Primordial Gods; body/Soul are beginning to merge with the represented Concept. Kali is confirmed; [[Tyche]] is approaching this state.[11][12]
Physical Floor
Conservatively V-04 / Planetary for Primordial-tier combat-capable Beings.
Upper Conventional Examples
V-06 / Galactic and multiple-galaxy physical threats exist, including [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Jeanne and [[Scathach]].[2]
Conceptual Scale
Evaluated separately and can greatly exceed direct physical destruction depending upon the Concept.[3]
Primordial Godhood is therefore not a single fixed strength. It is an enormous upper-Divine ecosystem beginning at a conservative planetary physical threshold and extending through ancient Pantheon founders, high Draconic Gods, Galactic-class elites and Beings beginning to transcend Godhood entirely. Quasi-[[Primordial Entities]] stand at the upper boundary because their growth is no longer merely quantitative: their Souls and existence are approaching union with the Concepts they represent. Beyond that boundary are true Primordial Entities, whose existence is tied to the fundamental machinery of Creation itself.
