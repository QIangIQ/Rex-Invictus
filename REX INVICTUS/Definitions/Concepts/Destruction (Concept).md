Destruction is the Concept that breaks what exists so that what was broken can be made again, in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]]. It is second only to END among the Concepts of ending. Shiva's Destruction destroys a thing so a God of Creation can use it later. END deletes the thing so that it never exists again.[1][2]
On the negative side the order of importance is Negativity, then END, then Destruction. On the positive side it is Positivity, then BEGIN, then the Concept of Creation. Destruction is the third of its side, not the top of it. Negativity is the Energy that drives it.[3][4]
Not the same word
Destruction recycles. The body, the building, and the space can go. The place in Time, the place in Space as a record, and the entry in the [[Akashic Records]] stay, so the matter can be used again. END takes the record. The Concept of Creation is the positive-side Divinity under BEGIN. It can contradict Destruction, and a God of that Concept can use what Destruction leaves. It is not this Power, and it is not the rival of END. When the novel says Destruction exists so that creation can occur, that sentence is the return of broken matter, not a claim that this Concept is the Concept of Creation. Creation as the Universe, the System, or all of existence is a third use of the word.

### What It Does
A destroyed thing is not a blank. In Nature, nothing is lost and nothing is erased. It changes from one form into another, and Destruction is that saying made into a Concept. A laugh of [[Kali Elderblood]]'s destroyed the temple around her. She could not stop it while she was laughing. When she stopped, the temple regenerated as if Time had run backward. Unlike END, which erases a thing from existence, Destruction destroys so that creation can occur once more.[2]
[[Shiva]] reduced an Elder God to particles with presence alone. Those particles were going to join creation. The sentence is the leftover matter, and a God of Creation is who that matter is for. It is not a deleted name.[1]
The same rule, said against the records, is why [[Victor Elderblood]] was relieved his daughter was not born as END. Destruction does not erase a person from Time, from Space, and from the [[Akashic Records]] as if they had never existed. It recycles them so that creation can use that matter. END is true conceptual erasure. The record ceases.[5]
The job of a Primordial of Destruction is that sentence made into an office. Destroy, so that creation can occur. The being in that seat works with [[The Universal Tree]]. The word creation in that job is the making that follows the breaking. The Universe does not need a Primordial who will not do the job.[6]
Everything that exists is Positivity and Negativity together, near half and half, with leans. A strongly physical Concept can sit near seventy percent Positivity. A strongly spiritual or emotional one can sit near seventy percent Negativity. Destruction is not given that seventy. It is named with Death, Time, and Space as a Divine Concept that still holds both halves, whichever side the office serves. The side this one serves is the negative one, and Negativity is the Energy that drives the Power.[7][4][3]

The Concept of Creation sits under BEGIN, on the positive side. It is not the third step under END. A bearer of it can contradict Destruction. The pieces Destruction leaves are still only matter.
A completed Primordial is not a strong God of the Concept. He is the incarnation. He stops being the God of Destruction and becomes Destruction. With the consent of the Primordials already in place, he becomes a piece of the Universe, and every God of Destruction draws from him. If that being disappeared, the Concept of Destruction would disappear with him. Hitting a thing hard enough would no longer be enough to destroy it, because Destruction would no longer exist.[7]
Under the present order there is one native Primordial to a Concept. The Judges are not three offices. They are one Being in three. The rule is the current rule. If the Creator wants it changed, it changes.[7]
Weaker Concepts do not overturn the ones at the top. Begin, End, Positivity, and Negativity are those. Destruction is beneath END, and those four sit above both.[6]
[[Shiva]]'s own list of energies is a separate thought, and it is not the order above. In his mind, first comes [[Primordial Energy]], which only Primordial Beings use. Then comes the Negativity and Positivity that only the [[World Trees]] use. Then come the Concepts of the Gods, with BEGIN and END at the top of those. Destruction is high. It is not that top.[1]
Moving from a lower Sector into a higher one does not change what Destruction is. A God of Destruction from a lower Sector is still a God of Destruction above. What changes for a normal God is the crowd. Hundreds can share the Concept, and a local peak stops being the peak. This family is not that normal God. Being born as the incarnation, or standing one step from becoming the Concept, is already past what a middle Sector produces.[7]

### Two Ways to Hold It

#### Always on
If the influential Gods are asked who the strongest male God is, they answer [[Shiva]]. If they are asked who the strongest female God is, they answer Kali, the Goddess who embodies the aspects of Active Destruction. Shiva can turn his Divinity on and off. Hers does not turn off. Speaking can destroy a weaker Being. In the pure Concept she stands above him. They have not fought to settle which of them is stronger, and a fight between them would be a catastrophe. Control is its own strength. Power without control is a weakness, and on that measure he can be called the stronger of the two.[2]
A God who takes a Concept into themselves the way she did becomes more than a Primordial God. A Primordial Entity is a Soul turned into a Concept, a piece of an Aspect of Existence. She is the only Goddess known, at that time, to be one step from it. She expected the rest of the climb to take a few more millennia. She was still one step from it long after.[2][6][7]
When she is seen in that isolation, the hair is long and blood-red, and it floats, and every strand carries an absurd amount of Energy. The eyes are dark red, without irises, a lake of blood full of beings. A red aura sits on her like natural armor. Touch it, and what touched it is destroyed.[2]
The merge of Soul and Concept, while he watched her meditate, was happening in an artificial way. The strength, the Soul, and the mentality were already there. The gap that looks small is the gap between the Sun and a planet the size of Earth. A Primordial is impartial and keeps the balance. She will defend her Pantheon. The ideals do not line up, so the transition stays unfinished. Wearing the Soul down against that wall is not the same as crossing it.[6]
By the visit to Shiva she is a Dragon Goddess, 476 centimeters, hair of pure Destruction down to the thighs. Anything that comes too close is destroyed. The visit's wording is destruction from existence. The same visit then draws the line: that destruction still recycles. It does not take the record the way END does. Easing the hold, even a little, starts eating the Space around her. Few Beings can stand in that presence.[5]
When she fights, the pure form starts on the arms and the legs and then covers the body. That is perfect control of a Power that used to fire when she laughed. The step after it is to become the Concept. Wrapped in it, distance stops being an obstacle. Dimensional passages are swallowed before they settle. A barrier of overlapping dimensions breaks if the fist lands before it closes, and the Destruction spreads through the crack faster than the body can put itself back. Her presence alone pulls temporal structures apart. She does not have to strike to stop a retreat. [[Oryndal]] read her as a high Goddess of Destruction, elite, and one step from a Primordial Being. Had his spatial defense not been on the first clash, the narration's words are that he would have been wiped from existence. That wipe is still this Concept. It is not END.[8][9]

#### Turned off
[[Shiva]]'s Destruction is the same Concept with a hand on the switch. If he stopped controlling it, everything around him would be destroyed, and his Divinity still does not compare to hers. He did not chase more of it. He could see the end of her road: an existence that is Destruction, and a time when her own Power would not let her stand next to anyone. The reason he stayed was loneliness.[1]
He does not treat the Power as invincible. The Concept of Creation contradicts it. [[Primordial Energy]] contradicts it. A bearer of BEGIN contradicts it. A bearer of END contradicts it. An absurd quantity of Positive and Negative Energy contradicts it, because those two cover every Concept, BEGIN and END included, and that quantity belongs to [[World Trees]]. The Heavenly Father resists because the Concept of Creation is in him. Against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Shiva did not know whether a counter was there. He was not going to assume there wasn't.[1]

### The Incarnation
[[Kassandra Kashvi Elderblood]] was born as Destruction. Both parents carry the Power, and she came out as the Concept itself. What her mother has spent an existence walking toward, the daughter had by being born. The aptitude to become the native Universe's Primordial of Destruction was there at birth. Neither parent wanted that seat filled yet, so she stayed on the personal planet inside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul, and she left only after most of the Power was sealed.[5]
She does not have her mother's control. A sneeze, or a cough, with that Power loose, evaporates a planet, and that is not all of it. The predatory Blood did not treat a reincarnated Concept as something too high to eat. It devoured Destruction and assimilated it into the biology. Her Draconic powers carry the aspects of Destruction. A command in the Dragon Language carries the damage with it, and the cost in Energy is small. The text calls that an erasure. The next lines are the correction. If she had been born with END, the person would be deleted forever, as if they had never existed. Destruction does not do that.[5]
She also has his Eyes, and traces of Negativity, so that Concept can be walked later. Her hair, like her mother's, is pure Destruction, black-red. Most of the Power sits behind a limiter of the same kind he once wore. The limiter is not a cut to the ceiling. It is a limit on how much of the ceiling she can touch before the control exists.[5]

### The Office, and the Second One
There is one native Primordial to a Concept. Kassandra taking the native seat would close it to Kali. She does not have to take that seat. She is half Eldritch, Soul and body, and the Soul is the part that matters. An Eldritch Primordial of Destruction is the same office from another universe. Kali cannot take that road now. Later, she can. Kassandra would rather belong to her father's order than to someone else's, and she believes her mother would too.[7]
The [[Primordial Chaos]] of this Universe is a different embodiment again. A Primordial of Destruction is the incarnation of one Concept. Primordial Chaos is the embodiment of the Universe itself, and all of creation, in the sense of the cosmos, is in it. That is not this Concept, and it is not the Concept of Creation under BEGIN.[7]

### Victor
Destruction awoke with the rest of the major Divinities, at a beginner's grasp. War came with it. Negativity came as the only representation of that side. BEGIN came out of the Progenitor status, and the proficiency in BEGIN was nothing. Blood was already dominated.[10]
After the long span inside the Inner World, the count was seventeen. Five were major. War, Destruction, and Blood were mastered. Negativity was still a beginner. Beginning was intermediate. The Concept of Creation sat with the twelve minor Divinities, and those were mastered too. Holding the Concept and the things that contradict it in the same body does not turn his Destruction into END, and it does not turn it into Creation.[11]
When Kali fought him to find what she still lacked, she opened with about half her strength and half her Destruction, a blow that could have made [[Shiva]] suffer, and he caught it. Her reading of the defense was that the Divinity of Destruction is answered by a greater Divinity of Destruction or by the Divinity of Creation. He added BEGIN and Positivity to that list. He used none of them. Negativity is the Energy that drives Destruction. If his hold on that greater Divinity were complete, her Destruction would not harm him, because the Energy under the Power would already be his. That immunity was a condition, not a fact of the fight. What could still harm him, on that reading, was exceptional destruction at her full strength, the Powers of END Gods, and a daughter who can turn the unreal into the real.[4]
She let the Power go. The hair became pure Destruction Energy, and the body was covered in pure Energy. Without his reinforcements, her presence alone would have destroyed the moon. He read her as a hybrid of a Goddess and a Primordial made entirely of Energy, still walking toward the step. When she fired a concentrated beam, he punched the air. Reality distorted, and the Power coming at him went back to her. He did not bend Space to do it, and he did not call Destruction, Creation, BEGIN, or Positivity.[4]

### A Ship, and a War
Polaris bends reality, space, and time using concepts of destruction, and it can be changed with Eldritch power. That is this Concept put into a hull. The ship's worse function is separate. It can channel END, and END eats the cannon. Bending a battlefield with Destruction is not deleting the record.[12]
Against the Throne of Space, the same Concept eats the structures the Throne is made of. Passages collapse. Rings fall. A layered barrier does not get to finish. Repair loses the race, because the Destruction is already in the crack. Presence alone is enough to pull a temporal structure apart. None of that is a second Concept. It is Destruction, controlled, wrapped around a body that has not yet become the office.[8][9]

### Powers and Abilities
- Matter Destruction: a body, a temple, a plate of armor. What the Concept touches comes apart. An Elder God went to particles under presence alone.[1][2]
- Existential Recycling: the pieces are for making again. They are not deleted from Time, from Space as a record, or from the [[Akashic Records]]. A destroyed temple can stand again, as if Time ran backward. Particles join creation, in the sense of leftover matter a God of Creation can use. In Nature, nothing is lost. It is transformed.[2][1][5]
- Presence Destruction: Kali does not need a technique for a weaker Being. Speaking can destroy one. Standing close enough destroys what approaches. Easing the hold eats Space. In a fight, presence alone pulls temporal structures apart.[2][5][9]
- Spatial Destruction: Space itself comes apart around her when the restraint loosens. Distance stops being an obstacle once Destruction is wrapped around the body. She crosses the layers instead of walking the gap.[5][9]
- Dimensional Structure Destruction: overlapping dimensions, opened passages, and barriers break, and the Destruction spreads through the damage before the structure can reform.[9]
- Controlled Release: [[Shiva]] turns the Divinity off. Kali's does not turn off. Her later fight-form is perfect control of a Power that used to fire when she laughed. The step past that control is becoming the Concept.[2][1][8]
- Conceptual Embodiment: taking the Concept into the self moves a God past the rank of Primordial God, toward a Soul that is the Concept. Kali is one step from it, and the merge she was forcing was artificial. Kassandra was born on the other side of that sentence, as Destruction incarnate, and the Blood then ate the Concept into the biology.[2][6][5]
- Construct: Kali can form a belt of pure Destruction. The object is the Concept, not a separate craft.[5]
- Dragon-Carried Destruction: once the Concept is in the biology, Draconic powers carry its aspects. A reality command in the Dragon Language hits with that damage, at a small cost in Energy. That hit is still recycling, not END.[5]
- Negativity as Fuel: Negativity is the Energy that drives the Power. A completed hold on Negativity would sit above ordinary Destruction and keep it from landing. Kali's practical knowledge of the Concept was still far ahead of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s hold on that Energy.[4]
- What the Office Feeds: the Primordial's work is to destroy so that creation can occur, beside the Universal Tree. Without that return, there is nothing to make from. If the Primordial vanished, the Concept would vanish, and brute force would stop being enough to break a thing.[6][7]
- Conceptual Priority: second only to END. Contradicted by the Concept of Creation, by [[Primordial Energy]], by BEGIN, by END, and by a World Tree's mass of Positive and Negative Energy. A greater Divinity of Destruction also answers it.[1][4]
- Technological Use: a ship can bend reality, space, and time through concepts of destruction. That function is not the END cannon on the same hull.[12]

### What Destruction Is Not
- Not END. END takes the record. Destruction leaves it. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s relief that his daughter was born as one and not the other is the whole distinction. A sentence that says a weaker being was destroyed from existence is still this Concept unless the record itself is gone.[2][5]
- Not the Concept of Creation. That Concept sits under BEGIN. A God of Creation can use what Destruction leaves, and the Divinity of Creation is one of the counters. The lowercase creation in the rule, the making that occurs once more, is the return of matter. It is not a second name for this Power, and it is not the Universe.[2][1][6][5]
- Not Negativity. Negativity is above it, and it is the Energy that drives it. Holding the Energy is not the same as Kali's understanding of the Concept.[3][4]
- Not automatic invincibility. [[Shiva]] is not invincible because the Concept is high. The Concept of Creation, BEGIN, END, [[Primordial Energy]], a greater Destruction, and a great mass of Positive and Negative Energy all contradict it.[1][4]
- Not a bent Space. When Victor sent Kali's beam back, reality distorted and the Power returned. He did not call Destruction to do it, and he did not bend Space.[4]
- Not mastery on the day it appears. Victor's awoke at a beginner's grasp. Kali's was always on, and that was the weakness, not the proof she had finished it.[10][2]
- Not a second native office. One Concept, one native Primordial. A daughter who takes the seat closes it to the mother. The Eldritch road is a different universe's version of the same job, not a duplicate of this one.[7]
- Not gone when the holder learns manners. The Power is the existence. Control decides whether a room survives her. It does not turn Active Destruction into an ordinary element. Easing the hold still eats Space.[2][5]
- Not Dragon Language by itself. Kali can bend reality and open a dimension with the Dragon Language. The belt she formed in that room was pure Destruction. The two are not the same tool.[5]

### Limitations
- The record survives. A victory that requires the target never to have existed is END's work, not this one.[5]
- The Concept of Creation, BEGIN, END, [[Primordial Energy]], a greater Destruction, and a World Tree's Positive and Negative Energy contradict it.[1][4]
- Negativity drives it. A completed hold on that Energy would keep ordinary Destruction from landing. The condition was stated. It was not the state of the fight.[4]
- An always-on holder is a danger to everything weaker than she is, including a temple she did not mean to break.[2]
- Control without the Concept is not the office, and the Concept without impartiality does not enter the office. Kali has spent the existence one step short for that reason. Forcing the Soul into the Concept wears the Soul down. It does not finish the step.[6]
- A child born as the Concept has no control. The limiter is the only reason a cough is not a dead planet.[5]
- One native Primordial. Taking it removes it from everyone else of this Universe. If that Primordial disappeared, Destruction would disappear with him.[7]
- Begin, End, Positivity, and Negativity are above it. A weaker Concept does not overturn them.[6]
