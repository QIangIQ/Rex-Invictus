Divine Energy, also known as Divine Power or Godly Energy, is the characteristic supernatural Energy generated and utilized by Gods. It serves as the energetic foundation of their Divine abilities, providing the power through which they express their supernatural nature and exercise abilities associated with their Divinity. Unlike ordinary Mana or the racial energies employed by many Mortal supernatural beings, Divine Energy possesses an intrinsically neutral character before its user imposes an attribute upon it. In its unmodified state, it is colorless and lacks the distinctive characteristics normally associated with the powers of a particular God.[1]
The defining property of Divine Energy is its capacity to receive the characteristics of the God wielding it. A God associated with Death can express the Energy through negative-aligned powers, while a God associated with Life can express it through positive-aligned powers. Its initial neutrality therefore does not prevent it from manifesting as Light, Darkness, Fire, Death, Life, [[Magic]] or another supernatural influence. Those manifestations depend upon the attributes, affinities and Divine authorities of the individual user rather than Divine Energy possessing every conceivable characteristic simultaneously.[1]
Divine Energy is closely related to Divinity but is not synonymous with it. Divine Energy is the power supplied and manipulated by the God; Divinity is the conceptual authority through which that power acquires a specific supernatural function. A Goddess of Love and a God of Death can both possess Divine Energy, yet the resulting abilities differ because the Concepts governing their respective existences are different. Similarly, possessing enormous quantities of Divine Energy does not automatically confer mastery over an unrelated Concept.[2][1]
Divine Energy occupies an important position within the supernatural energy systems of Creation. It is generally superior in raw quality to conventional Mortal energies such as Mana, although the outcome of a particular technique also depends upon its complexity, efficiency and the user's understanding. Its neutral foundation allows it to operate through either side of the positive-negative duality, with the God's nature determining the characteristics of the resulting power.[2][1][3]

### Nature and Origin

#### The Energy of Gods
Divine Energy is the supernatural power naturally associated with Godhood. Although different Pantheons possess their own histories, Divine Concepts, techniques and governing authorities, their members share an underlying capacity to generate and utilize Divine Energy. Its existence is one of the important distinctions between Gods and ordinary supernatural beings whose powers derive primarily from their racial physiology, inherited abilities or externally acquired energy systems.[1]
The Divine nature of the user determines how the Energy is ordinarily employed. A God whose existence is associated with War may direct it into techniques suited to battle, while a Goddess associated with Beauty may incorporate it into supernatural attraction or influence over appearance. Such abilities arise from the interaction between the available Divine Energy and the supernatural characteristics of the user. The same underlying Energy therefore supports substantially different applications across individual Pantheons.[1]
The association between Divine Energy and Godhood does not make it impossible for other beings to possess traces of that power. An individual may inherit Divine characteristics, obtain a blessing or acquire the remnants of a Divine being without personally becoming a fully developed God. The resulting circumstances can allow Divine Energy to remain within an otherwise Mortal supernatural existence.[4][5]

#### Neutrality
The most important fundamental characteristic of Divine Energy is its neutrality. Albedo demonstrated this property while explaining the nature of supernatural Energy to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. By manifesting several distinct forms of power, she distinguished the Miasma of Demons, the Sacred Light of Angels, the [[Natural Energy]] associated with Nature and the Mana used by Witches from an additional colorless Energy. Victor recognized the final manifestation as the supernatural power belonging to Gods.[1]
Unlike the other demonstrated energies, the Divine Energy Albedo produced possessed no inherent color or elemental characteristic. Its attributes were determined by the being employing it. The neutral state consequently represents an important difference between the original Divine power and the effects produced after it has been adapted to a God's particular nature.[1]
- Energy Neutrality: Divine Energy initially possesses neither the explicitly positive nor the explicitly negative characteristics associated with specialized supernatural powers.[1]
- Colorless Manifestation: Unmodified Divine Energy lacks an inherent visible color, distinguishing it from the characteristic appearances of energies such as Miasma, Sacred Light and Mana.[1]
- Energy Attribution: The God utilizing Divine Energy can impart characteristics associated with their supernatural nature and powers.[1]
- Conceptual Compatibility: Divine Energy can support the expression of Divine abilities associated with the user's Concepts.[2][1]
- Alignment Adaptation: The expression of Divine Energy may belong to either side of the positive-negative balance, depending upon the nature of the God wielding it.[1]
Divine Energy's neutrality should not be confused with the entirely different nature of the original Attributeless Energy used in the creation of the Universe. Both can lack ordinary characteristics, but they are not identical in origin, scope or authority.[6][7]

#### Energy Attribution
Divine Energy acquires its functional properties through the being utilizing it. This process explains why Gods with radically different supernatural natures can rely upon the same underlying category of Energy while producing entirely different results.[1]
For example, a God associated with Death may produce a negative-aligned manifestation, while a God associated with Life may produce a positive-aligned manifestation. These attributes influence the powers that the Energy can support and the supernatural effects resulting from its use.[1]
The process is not equivalent to an ordinary God gaining unrestricted control over all attributes. The nature of the resulting Energy remains connected to the user's own supernatural existence, including their affinities, Divinities and limitations.
This distinction is particularly important when discussing the fundamental Concepts of Positivity and Negativity. A God's Energy acquiring positive or negative characteristics does not mean that the God possesses direct authority over the Primordial forces governing those characteristics throughout Creation.

### Divine Energy and Divinity

#### Energy and Conceptual Authority
Divine Energy and Divinity form related but distinguishable components of Divine power. Divine Energy provides the supernatural power used to produce an effect, while Divinity establishes the particular Concept or aspect of Reality over which the God possesses authority.[2][1]
A [[Hecate|Goddess of Magic]] may use her Divine Energy to produce magical effects. The existence of Divine Energy supplies the power necessary for the technique, but the authority and specialization associated with [[Magic]] determine the character of the resulting ability.
The distinction also applies to Gods possessing several Divinities. Their Divine Energy can be employed through different Concepts without each Concept necessarily representing an entirely independent reserve of ordinary Energy.

#### Divine Power and Conceptual Expression
The characteristics of Divine Energy become especially important when a God employs their Conceptual abilities. A Divine technique is not defined solely by its quantity of Energy; the user's authority over the associated Concept also affects its nature and effectiveness.[3][8]
A God possessing the Concept of Fire can produce effects related to Fire, while a Goddess possessing the Concept of Beauty may exercise abilities associated with Beauty. These applications require the relevant authority rather than Divine Energy independently granting the entire range of possible supernatural effects.
Consequently, the possession of Divine Energy should not be used as evidence that an individual automatically possesses Conceptual Manipulation over every element, emotion, natural phenomenon or supernatural force.

#### Power and Authority
The quantity of Divine Energy possessed by a God and the degree of authority they exercise over a Concept are related aspects of Divine strength, but neither should be treated as an absolute substitute for the other.[3][8]
A God with enormous reserves may possess a significant advantage in prolonged combat or techniques requiring exceptionally large quantities of Energy. However, a being possessing superior conceptual authority may produce effects that are difficult to overcome through ordinary Energy expenditure alone.
The importance of this distinction becomes especially apparent among Gods wielding higher-order Concepts. Destruction, Creation, Beginning and End possess characteristics that cannot be accurately represented through ordinary measurements of Energy quantity.[3]

#### Godhood and the Divine Body
Godhood involves a transformation of the individual's existence. For Mortals who ascend into Gods, the relationship between the body and Soul changes as the individual's existence becomes increasingly spiritual and capable of embodying Divine authority.[9]
The Divine Body provides the foundation through which a God can sustain and employ their supernatural power. However, the transformation into a God is distinct from merely possessing traces of Divine Energy.
An ordinary supernatural being who inherits a limited quantity of Divine power does not automatically undergo the same transformation as a genuine Ascendant God. Similarly, the possession of a powerful blessing does not independently demonstrate complete God physiology.[4][9]

### Generation and Reserves

#### Divine Energy Generation
Gods naturally possess Divine Energy as part of their supernatural existence. Their reserves, output and capacity to exercise that power vary according to the individual and their development.[1]
The amount available can differ enormously between minor Gods, powerful [[Combat Gods]], [[God-Kings]] and beings approaching the conditions associated with higher states of existence. Nevertheless, differences in rank cannot be reduced exclusively to Divine Energy quantity because authority, experience, Conceptual proficiency and other characteristics also influence a God's overall strength.[3]
The distinctions between Divine Energy reserves and Conceptual authority become especially significant when a God undergoes an evolution that fundamentally alters their supernatural existence. Such changes may improve the individual's ability to wield Energy while simultaneously expanding the authority associated with their Divinity.

#### Divine Energy Density
Divine Energy generally possesses greater raw potency than conventional Mortal energies such as Mana. This difference is directly observable when comparing the abilities of [[Hecate]], Goddess of Magic, with those of ordinary Witches.[2]
Despite both systems producing effects recognized as [[Magic]], Hecate's techniques depend more heavily upon Divine power. Her Magic consequently possesses greater raw potency, while the conventional Magic of Witches remains more versatile and capable of supporting a wider range of complicated applications.[2]
This establishes that the superior raw quality of Divine Energy does not guarantee superiority in every aspect of supernatural technique. Control, specialization and the method through which Energy is applied remain important.

#### Increased Divine Reserves
Divine Energy can increase through exceptional transformations and the acquisition of additional supernatural support. [[Persephone Elderblood]] provides an example through her appointment as the true Ruler of the [[Greek Underworld]].[10]
After completing the trial administered by the [[The Judges of The Abyss|Three Judges]] of the Abyss, [[Persephone]] received the authority associated with ruling Greek Hell. Her new position allowed the infernal realm itself to support her existence, resulting in a tremendous increase in power. A Goddess who had previously occupied a comparatively minor position within the [[Greek Pantheon]] became an existence possessing the strength and authority of a God-King.[10]
Her development also demonstrates the importance of external supernatural systems. The increase was not merely the result of ordinary training or accumulating Energy over time; it was associated with the authority and support granted through the legitimate government of an infernal realm.
The distinction should be preserved because becoming a Ruler involves responsibilities and supernatural systems beyond ordinary Divine Energy manipulation.

#### Divine Energy as a Resource
Divine Energy can function as a resource for exceptionally demanding supernatural undertakings. Unlike ordinary Mana, whose availability limits the practical scale of conventional Witchcraft, the accumulated power of an entire Pantheon can provide Energy sufficient for projects that would otherwise be impossible through ordinary magical reserves.[11]
Evie Moriarty and [[Emily Moriarty]] considered the Divine Energy of an entire Pantheon sufficient for their plan to locate a new habitable world. Their ordinary Mana reserves could not accomplish the same objective, demonstrating the importance of both the quality and enormous quantity of Divine power available through a collection of Gods.[11]
This example establishes that Divine Energy can be valuable beyond direct combat. However, the ability to obtain an enormous energy supply is not itself proof that an individual possesses the knowledge, compatibility or authority necessary to employ it successfully.

### Physical and Supernatural Properties

#### Appearance
Pure Divine Energy is colorless. Its visible appearance changes when the user imposes attributes associated with their supernatural nature.[1]
This explains the wide variety of colors associated with Divine techniques. Energy employed through a Goddess of Love may possess a pink manifestation, while the powers of a God associated with Death or Darkness may appear considerably different.
The coloration reflects the characteristics of the active supernatural technique rather than proving that every God possesses a fundamentally unrelated form of Divine Energy.
- Colorless Divine Energy: The unmodified Divine Energy generated by Gods possesses no inherent color.[1]
- Attributed Manifestation: The Energy can acquire visible characteristics associated with the God utilizing it.[1]
- Colored Divine Constructs: A Divine ability may appear as a colored supernatural construct or projection after the Energy has received the characteristics of the relevant power.[5][12]

#### Divine Energy Signatures
Divine Energy can be recognized by supernatural beings possessing sufficiently developed senses. Its presence may remain detectable even when the quantity is comparatively small.[4]
[[Victor Elderblood]] retained traces of Divine Energy after absorbing the existence of [[Adonis Snow]]. During an encounter with beings capable of recognizing supernatural characteristics, this Energy was identified despite Victor not having undergone the complete transformation into a God.[4]
The residual power associated with Adonis was sufficiently distinct to be recognized alongside other supernatural influences affecting Victor, including the Curse imposed upon Adonis by [[Persephone]].[4]
- Divine Energy Detection: Individuals possessing appropriate supernatural senses can recognize Divine Energy within another being.[4]
- Residual Energy Recognition: Traces of Divine power can remain detectable even when inherited or absorbed by an individual who does not naturally belong to the Divine Race.[4]
- Energy Differentiation: Exceptionally knowledgeable practitioners can distinguish Divine Energy from Mana, Miasma, Sacred Light and other attributed energies.[1]
- Energy Analysis: Advanced Energy users can identify the characteristics of Divine power and understand how they differ from those of other supernatural systems.[1][6]

#### Compatibility
Divine Energy is fundamentally neutral but normally functions through the nature of the God wielding it. Its neutrality therefore does not guarantee universal compatibility with every user.[1]
The existence of blessings, inherited Divine characteristics and specialized energy manipulation demonstrates that beings outside the ordinary Divine Race may obtain or handle Divine Energy under exceptional circumstances. Nevertheless, the conditions permitting these applications vary considerably.[4][5][6]
An individual who acquires a limited Divine influence should not automatically be classified as possessing complete access to the original Energy reserves, Divine Body or conceptual authority of its source.

### Abilities and Applications

#### Divine Energy Manipulation
The fundamental ability associated with Divine Energy is its conscious generation, circulation and manipulation. Gods utilize this power as the energetic foundation for many of their supernatural abilities.[1]
- Energy Generation: Gods naturally produce Divine Energy through their Divine existence.[1]
- Energy Manipulation: Divine Energy can be controlled and directed through supernatural techniques.[1]
- Energy Attribution: Gods can impart characteristics associated with their supernatural nature to otherwise neutral Divine Energy.[1]
- Energy Projection: Divine power can be released offensively or as part of a specialized supernatural technique.[3][8]
- Energy Constructs: Divine Energy can be incorporated into supernatural constructs whose effects depend upon the user's abilities.[5][12]
- Energy Reinforcement: Divine Energy can support the extraordinary abilities and supernatural output of its user.[2]

#### Divine Energy Projection
Divine Energy can be released as a supernatural attack. Its effects depend upon the user, the attributes imposed upon the Energy and the nature of the technique employed.
The battle involving [[Shiva]] and the Elder God Nur demonstrates the interaction between Divine attacks possessing different characteristics. Shiva's Destruction power overwhelmed Nur's elemental Fire attack, with the superior nature of his technique becoming decisive in the exchange.[3]
Similarly, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s awakening into Godhood produced a substantial change in the supernatural Energy surrounding him. As his Divine nature developed, the resulting power became increasingly associated with the Concepts emerging within his existence.[8]
- Energy Projection: Gods can release concentrated Divine power through offensive techniques.[3][8]
- Destructive Energy Manipulation: Divine attacks can possess extraordinary destructive power when combined with appropriate attributes.[3]
- Conceptual Energy Attacks: A God can employ Divine power through the characteristics associated with their Divinity.[8]
The specific effects of an attack should be attributed to the power actually demonstrated rather than to Divine Energy universally possessing every effect.

#### Divine Energy Constructs
Divine Energy can serve as a foundation for supernatural constructs. Their form and effects depend upon the user's specialized abilities.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] demonstrated a related application after learning to control the Divine influence associated with [[Aphrodite]]'s Blessing. Once he became capable of distinguishing that Energy from his other supernatural reserves, he could consciously manipulate it and form constructs instead of allowing it to act only as an uncontrolled influence.[5]
Aphrodite likewise demonstrated the manifestation and release of her Divine power as a visible pink aura, which could expand through a surrounding area and impose the effects associated with her abilities upon those exposed to it.[12]
- Energy Construct Creation: Divine power can be formed into supernatural manifestations through appropriate control.[5]
- Energy Aura: Divine Energy may surround the body of its user and influence the surrounding environment.[12]
- Area-of-Effect Projection: Certain Divine techniques can release their power across a substantial area rather than toward a single target.[12]
- Power Combination: Divine Energy may operate alongside other supernatural energies and techniques when their user possesses the necessary compatibility.[1]

#### Flight
Gods possess the ability to fly using their supernatural powers, with Divine Energy contributing to the functions of their Divine physiology.
This characteristic is associated with the broader abilities of Gods rather than requiring every deity to possess a separate Concept of Flight. The manner in which individual Gods manifest flight can differ according to their supernatural nature and abilities.
The general capacity for flight should nevertheless be distinguished from unrestricted control over Gravity, Space or Motion, which require separate supporting abilities.

#### Divine Magic
Divine [[Magic]] is one of the most important specialized applications of Divine Energy. Unlike ordinary Magic, which typically utilizes Mana, Divine Magic can employ the Energy generated by Gods as its principal supernatural power source.[2]
[[Hecate]], Goddess of Magic, provides the clearest comparison between the two systems. Although both Hecate and Witches employ abilities recognized as Magic, the fundamental source of their power differs. Hecate relies considerably more heavily upon Divine Energy, granting her techniques greater raw potency than comparable ordinary magical applications.[2]
The advantage is not absolute. Witch Magic possesses exceptional versatility and complexity, permitting elaborate applications that can involve numerous elements and supernatural attributes. Hecate's Divine Magic possesses superior raw strength in the demonstrated comparison but does not automatically exceed the flexibility of every Witch technique.[2]
- Divine Magic Manipulation: Divine Energy can be employed to perform supernatural techniques associated with Magic.[2]
- Magic Empowerment: Divine power can provide greater raw potency than conventional Mana when used through an appropriate magical system.[2]
- Energy Attribution: Divine Magic can acquire the characteristics associated with the user's supernatural powers.[2][1]
- Energy-Based Spellcasting: Divine Energy can serve as the power source for specialized magical techniques.[2]
[[Merlin]]'s discoveries concerning the common foundation of supernatural Energy further establish that the effectiveness of a technique depends upon the understanding and control of its user, not simply the name assigned to its Energy source.[2]

#### Blessing Empowerment
Divine Energy can be associated with the supernatural power granted through Divine blessings. A blessing allows a God to impart a particular influence or ability to another being, although the power made available remains connected to the domain of the granting deity.[5]
[[Aphrodite]]'s Blessing of Beauty provides a direct example. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] initially struggled to distinguish the influence from his ordinary supernatural powers. He perceived Fire, Water, Ice, Lightning and Blood as separate internal sources, while the Blessing existed as another influence that had to be recognized and controlled.[5]
Through Aphrodite's instruction, Victor learned to distinguish the Blessing's Energy and manipulate it consciously. The process demonstrated that a Divine influence transferred to another being can be controlled independently when the recipient possesses sufficient understanding and compatibility.[5]
- Blessing Empowerment: Divine power granted through a Blessing can provide another being with access to supernatural effects associated with the granting God.[5]
- Energy Transfer: Divine influence can be imparted to a recipient without the recipient necessarily becoming a God.[5]
- Power Bestowal: A Divine being can grant selected abilities associated with their supernatural authority.[5]
- Energy Recognition: A recipient can learn to distinguish and regulate the Divine influence present within their own body.[5]
- Energy Construct Creation: Victor demonstrated the ability to shape the Energy associated with Aphrodite's Blessing after learning to control it.[5]
The existence of a Divine blessing does not establish that every blessed being possesses unrestricted access to the entirety of the granting God's Energy or Divinities.

### Divine Energy Beyond Gods

#### Divine Heritage
Traces of Divine Energy can exist within individuals who possess Divine ancestry or have absorbed the existence of another being carrying Divine characteristics.[4]
[[Adonis Snow]] possessed Divine characteristics associated with his supernatural heritage. After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] absorbed Adonis, the resulting transformation left a small but recognizable amount of Divine Energy within the Vampire Progenitor.[4]
This inherited Energy remained distinguishable from Victor's original Vampiric powers and from the supernatural effects associated with Adonis' Curse. Its presence demonstrates that Divine Energy can persist through an extraordinary transfer of existence rather than disappearing simply because the recipient is not yet a God.[4]
- Divine Energy Inheritance: Divine characteristics can persist within the existence of individuals possessing appropriate supernatural heritage.[4]
- Residual Divine Energy: Traces of Divine power may remain after the absorption of another being's supernatural existence.[4]
- Energy Detection: Such traces can be identified by beings with appropriate supernatural senses.[4]

#### Demigods
Demigods may possess Divine characteristics inherited from their Divine ancestors. However, their relationship with Divine Energy varies according to individual heritage, development and the extent of the supernatural characteristics received.
The possession of Divine ancestry alone should not be interpreted as evidence that every [[Demigod]] can freely generate the same quality or quantity of Divine Energy as a fully developed God.

#### Transferred Divine Power
Gods can grant portions of their supernatural abilities through blessings and other special circumstances. However, different methods of transferring Divine power produce different results.
A blessing may provide access to a particular ability without changing the recipient into a Divine being, while the absorption of another individual's existence can introduce supernatural characteristics belonging to the original owner.[4][5]
The recipient's Soul, physical condition and compatibility remain relevant because possessing a Divine influence is not equivalent to possessing the complete physiology of a God.

### Divine Energy and Faith

#### Faith as a Separate Power
Faith Energy is a supernatural power arising from belief, conviction and the relationship between worshippers and the objects of their devotion. It is not identical to the Divine Energy naturally generated by Gods.
Religious practitioners can obtain supernatural power through their Faith, and the resulting techniques may be associated with a particular Divine being. Such abilities include the sacred techniques of the Inquisition and the supernatural systems used by certain Onmyoji.[13][14]
However, the Energy wielded by a worshipper should not automatically be classified as pure Divine Energy. The method through which the power is accessed, the identity of the patron deity and the conditions established by the relevant supernatural system remain important.

#### Faith and the Growth of Gods
Faith can influence the development of Divine power, particularly through the relationship between widespread belief and the emergence of new Divine Concepts.[15]
A sufficiently widespread association between an individual and a particular idea can contribute to the development of a corresponding Divinity. This becomes relevant when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] begins deliberately cultivating the public identities of members of his Pantheon, recognizing that recognition and worship can influence the Concepts associated with them.[15]
This relationship does not establish that Gods require a continuous supply of Faith to remain alive. Divine existence and the development of particular Divine authorities should be treated as related but distinguishable phenomena.

#### Faith and Divine Energy Are Not Interchangeable
Divine Energy is the characteristic Energy associated with Gods, while Faith Energy arises from belief and its supernatural effects.
Their interaction can be important when a God empowers a follower or develops a greater relationship with their worshippers, but the two powers should not be treated as a single undifferentiated resource.
A faithful Mortal does not automatically possess the complete Divine Energy of the God they worship, just as an existing God does not automatically cease generating Divine power merely because their following changes.

### Relationship with Other Energy Types

#### Divine Energy and Mana
Divine Energy and Mana are both supernatural energies capable of powering complicated techniques, but they differ in their conventional sources and characteristics.
Mana is principally associated with conventional [[Magic]], while Divine Energy is naturally associated with Gods. Divine Energy possesses greater raw potency in the demonstrated comparison between [[Hecate]]'s Divine Magic and ordinary Witch Magic, but Mana remains exceptionally versatile and suitable for elaborate supernatural applications.[2]
The distinction therefore involves more than a simple difference in strength. Divine Magic and ordinary Magic can produce superficially similar effects while relying upon different sources and methods of control.

#### Divine Energy and Holy Energy
Divine Energy is not inherently identical to [[Holy Energy]]. Holy Energy possesses characteristics associated with the Sacred Light employed by Angels and related supernatural beings, while pure Divine Energy is initially neutral and receives its attributes from the user.[1]
A God associated with positive or Holy characteristics may employ Divine Energy in ways resembling Sacred Light. This does not mean that every God produces Holy Energy or that the Energy generated by a God of Death possesses the same properties as Angelic Light.
The distinction is particularly important when discussing Demons, Vampires and other supernatural beings whose weaknesses may depend upon the actual attributes of the power affecting them.

#### Divine Energy and Miasma
Miasma is the characteristic Demonic Energy associated with Demons and Hell. Its ordinary manifestations possess corrosive and toxic properties, whereas Divine Energy is initially neutral.[1]
Both energies belong to the larger structure of supernatural Energy but possess distinct conventional properties. Albedo demonstrated her ability to produce and manipulate both, illustrating that exceptionally knowledgeable beings can control multiple energy systems without treating their ordinary characteristics as identical.[1]
The possibility of manipulating both forms also demonstrates that Divine Energy is not automatically the conceptual opposite of Demonic Energy in every circumstance. Their interaction depends upon the actual attributes and abilities employed by the users.

#### Divine Energy and Positivity
Positivity is one of the fundamental powers underlying the structure of Creation. Divine Energy can acquire positive characteristics according to the nature of its user, but this does not automatically grant the God authority over the fundamental Concept of Positivity.[1][8]
A Goddess of Life can utilize positive-aligned powers without possessing the complete authority of the Primordial source governing Positivity throughout the Universe.

#### Divine Energy and Negativity
Negativity is the complementary fundamental power associated with the negative side of Creation. Divine Energy can manifest negative characteristics through Gods whose powers involve Death, Darkness, Despair, Corruption or similar Concepts.[1]
However, negative-aligned Divine Energy remains distinguishable from the pure power associated with the fundamental Concept of Negativity.
This distinction is particularly important for [[Victor Elderblood]], whose possession of a direct relationship with Negativity provides authority beyond the ordinary negative-aligned powers of Gods.[8]

#### Divine Energy and Primordial Energy
[[Primordial Energy]] occupies a more fundamental position within the supernatural hierarchy than ordinary Divine Energy. Its characteristics and the beings capable of manipulating it differ substantially from those associated with conventional Godhood.[3]
The higher-order nature of Primordial Energy becomes especially important when comparing supernatural attacks based upon Divine Concepts. Superior Energy sources and exceptional conceptual authorities can counter abilities that would otherwise overwhelm ordinary Gods.[3]
This hierarchy should not be interpreted as guaranteeing a particular result in every confrontation. The nature of the technique, its authority, the user's control and the available quantity of Energy remain relevant.

#### Divine Energy and Attributeless Energy
Divine Energy possesses an unmodified, colorless and neutral state. However, it must not be confused with the Attributeless Energy representing the fundamental origin of the energies employed throughout Creation.[6][7]
The distinction became apparent through [[Melida Charlotte Elderblood]], whose extraordinary innate understanding of Energy allowed her to manipulate an unaltered source and transform it directly into energies possessing particular characteristics.[6]
Unlike conventional Gods who generate Divine Energy and exercise it through their supernatural nature, Charlotte can manipulate Energy at a much more fundamental level, creating the desired attributes rather than merely depending upon an existing supply of a particular type.[6]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later demonstrated this distinction while teaching his daughters. He manifested several different types of Energy, including Divine Energy, Demonic Energy, Spirit Energy, Mana and others, around a central representation of Attributeless Energy. He then drew power from the central source and converted it into Divine Energy, demonstrating that Divine Energy is one of the attributed expressions arising from that more fundamental foundation.[7]
- Energy Conversion: Divine Energy can be produced from a more fundamental energy source by individuals possessing sufficiently advanced control.[6][7]
- Energy Attribution: The characteristics associated with Divine Energy can be established through the transformation of more fundamental Energy.[7]
- Energy Differentiation: Divine Energy remains a recognizable category even to individuals capable of manipulating multiple types of supernatural power.[6][7]
The conversion demonstrated by Victor does not establish that every ordinary God can independently create Attributeless Energy or freely convert any energy into Divine Energy.

#### Divine Energy and Draconic Energy
[[Draconic Energy]] is the supernatural power associated with Dragons and their Dragon Hearts. Its origin and conventional functions are distinct from Divine Energy, although the two may coexist within a being possessing both Draconic and Divine characteristics.
Dragon Gods demonstrate this compatibility through their combined existence as Dragons and Gods. Their ability to utilize several supernatural systems does not eliminate the distinction between the Energy produced through their Draconic physiology and the power associated with Godhood.
The same distinction becomes relevant to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his descendants, whose extraordinary heritage allows several energies to coexist within the same individual.

### Advanced Energy Manipulation

#### Albedo's Understanding of Energy
[[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]] possesses exceptional understanding of supernatural Energy. Her studies revealed that the distinctions conventionally maintained between Mana, Miasma, Divine Energy and other forms of power do not represent entirely unrelated foundations.[1]
By manifesting multiple types simultaneously, Albedo demonstrated that the characteristics of an Energy can be understood and manipulated independently. Her explanation helped [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] recognize that his previous assumptions about the separation between supernatural systems were incomplete.[1]
Nevertheless, the ability to recognize the common origin of different energies does not automatically grant unrestricted control over the source from which all of them derive.

#### Melida's Attributeless Energy
[[Melida Charlotte Elderblood]] possesses an exceptional talent for the understanding and manipulation of Energy. Immediately after her transformation, she demonstrated the ability to recognize and interact with numerous distinct supernatural energies, including Divine Energy, Miasma, Sacred Light, [[Magic]] and the powers associated with [[World Trees]].[6]
Her existence provided [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] with further insight into the relationship between ordinary attributed energies and their underlying origin. Unlike individuals who manipulate the energies already present within themselves or the surrounding environment, Charlotte could transform Attributeless Energy into a desired form.[6]
Divine Energy consequently became one of the many types she could recognize, produce or reproduce through her extraordinary abilities.

#### Victor's Higher Energy Control
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s understanding developed beyond the conventional distinction between individual Energy systems. During his instruction of the Imperial Princesses, he demonstrated that Divine Energy, Demonic Energy, Mana, Ki and other supernatural powers could be understood as attributed expressions of Energy itself.[7]
He emphasized that ordinary beings must work within the limitations imposed by the energies available to them, while individuals possessing exceptional control can manipulate the underlying power more directly.
By converting Attributeless Energy into Divine Energy, Victor demonstrated that the latter is not the ultimate source from which every other power originates. Rather, it possesses a recognizable place within the wider structure of Creation's supernatural energies.[7]

### Known Users

#### Gods
Gods are the principal natural users of Divine Energy. Their Divine nature allows them to generate and manipulate the power, while their individual Divinities and supernatural characteristics determine the effects they can produce.[1]
- [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood: A Goddess whose Divine Energy commonly manifests through pink supernatural power associated with her abilities involving Love, Beauty and other related influences. Her Blessing allowed [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to obtain and manipulate a corresponding Divine influence.[5][12]
- [[Hecate]]: The Goddess of Magic, whose techniques rely heavily upon Divine Energy. Her Divine [[Magic]] possesses greater raw potency than ordinary Witch Magic in the demonstrated comparison, although it lacks some of the versatility available through conventional Mana-based techniques.[2]
- [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]]: A Goddess and exceptionally knowledgeable practitioner of Magic who demonstrated control over pure colorless Divine Energy alongside several other supernatural energy types.[1]
- [[Persephone Elderblood]]: A Goddess whose supernatural reserves and power increased enormously after she obtained the authority associated with ruling the [[Greek Underworld]].[10]
- [[Kali Elderblood]]: A Goddess whose supernatural abilities are associated with Destruction. Her Divine nature provides the foundation for the expression of her destructive powers.
- [[Shiva]]: A God associated with Destruction whose abilities demonstrate the importance of higher-order conceptual power when confronting other Divine opponents.[3]
- [[Amaterasu]] Elderblood: A Sun Goddess whose Divine powers are associated with the Solar characteristics of her existence.
- [[Hestia]] Elderblood: A Goddess whose supernatural powers are associated with the Hearth, [[Rex Invictus|Home]] and Sacred Fire.
- [[Gaia]] Elderblood: A primordial Goddess whose supernatural existence is associated with Earth and Nature.
- [[Heavenly Father]]: A powerful God whose Divine abilities include the supernatural systems associated with Creation and the [[Biblical Pantheon]].

#### Ascendant Gods
Ascendant Gods are individuals who did not originally belong to the Divine Race but subsequently attained Godhood. Their Divine Energy exists alongside the supernatural characteristics inherited from their previous existence.
- [[Victor Elderblood]]: An Ascendant God whose existence encompasses several supernatural races and energy systems. He can generate, manipulate and convert Divine Energy while also possessing exceptional authority over the fundamental forces of Creation.[8][6][7]
- [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]]: A former Witch who attained Godhood while retaining extraordinary knowledge of [[Magic]], [[Alchemy]] and other supernatural energy systems.[1]

#### Inherited and Exceptional Users
- [[Adonis Snow]]: Possessed Divine characteristics that remained detectable after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] absorbed his existence.[4]
- Anderson: Possesses supernatural heritage associated with Divine ancestry through [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]]. His Divine characteristics should be distinguished from the ordinary Energy used by [[Werewolves]].[16]
- [[Melida Charlotte Elderblood]]: Possesses extraordinary Energy Manipulation capable of reproducing Divine Energy alongside other supernatural powers through Attributeless Energy.[6]
- Demigods: Individuals possessing Divine ancestry may inherit characteristics associated with Divine Energy, although the degree of access and control depends upon the individual.

### Limitations and Weaknesses

#### Conceptual Restrictions
Divine Energy's neutral foundation does not automatically grant its users authority over every Concept. The abilities accessible to a conventional God remain connected to their supernatural nature, Divinities and individual understanding.[1]
The ability to generate Divine Energy is therefore not sufficient to reproduce every supernatural technique employed by other Gods.

#### Energy Quantity
Although Divine Energy is generally more potent than ordinary Mana in the demonstrated comparison, its effectiveness remains subject to the quantity available and the user's proficiency.[2]
A God possessing comparatively small reserves cannot automatically perform techniques requiring an enormous expenditure merely because their Energy belongs to the Divine classification.
The Divine Energy of an entire Pantheon can provide an exceptional resource for large-scale supernatural undertakings, demonstrating that accumulated quantity remains important despite the superior quality of Divine power.[11]

#### Energy Control
Advanced manipulation requires understanding and control. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] initially struggled to recognize the Divine influence associated with [[Aphrodite]]'s Blessing despite possessing extraordinary general power.[5]
The problem was not a lack of raw strength but difficulty identifying and controlling an unfamiliar source within his existing supernatural reserves. Instruction from Aphrodite allowed him to overcome that limitation.

#### Compatibility
The ability to detect, inherit or receive Divine Energy does not automatically grant unrestricted access to its original applications.
Inherited traces and blessings may exist within an otherwise Mortal supernatural being, but their effects remain subject to the nature of the transfer and the recipient's compatibility.[4][5]

#### Higher-Order Energy
Divine Energy is not the highest form of supernatural Energy. [[Primordial Energy]] and the fundamental forces associated with the creation and regulation of the Universe possess characteristics extending beyond those of ordinary Gods.[3][7]
The existence of such energies establishes that even powerful Divine techniques can encounter opposition from beings whose supernatural authority operates at a more fundamental level.

### Notable Demonstrations
- Residual Divine Energy: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] retained a recognizable trace of Divine Energy after absorbing Adonis' existence. The Energy was detected despite Victor not yet possessing the complete physiology of a God.[4]
- Divine Blessing Control: Victor learned to identify and manipulate the Energy associated with [[Aphrodite]]'s Blessing, transforming an initially uncontrolled supernatural influence into a power he could consciously employ.[5]
- Pantheon-Level Energy Supply: Evie and Emily considered the accumulated Divine Energy of an entire Pantheon sufficient to support a project requiring far more power than their available Mana reserves.[11]
- [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] Empowerment: [[Persephone]] obtained the support of Greek Hell after completing the trial necessary to become its legitimate Ruler, increasing her supernatural power to a level comparable with a God-King.[10]
- Divine [[Magic]]: [[Hecate]] demonstrated a magical system relying upon Divine Energy rather than conventional Mana, possessing greater raw potency but less versatility than the Magic of Witches.[2]
- Pure Divine Energy: Albedo manifested a colorless form of Divine Energy and explained how Gods impart their own characteristics to an otherwise neutral power.[1]
- Divine Energy Attribution: Albedo demonstrated the distinction between the neutral foundation of Divine Energy and the positive or negative characteristics acquired through its user.[1]
- Godhood [[Awakening]]: Victor's ascension produced major changes in his supernatural existence as the powers associated with his emerging Divinities awakened.[8]
- Attributed Divine Power: [[Shiva]] demonstrated the destructive effects of his supernatural authority in battle against Nur, illustrating the difference between ordinary elemental attacks and powers supported by a higher Divine Concept.[3]
- Multi-Energy Manipulation: [[Melida Charlotte Elderblood]] demonstrated the ability to recognize and interact with Divine Energy, Miasma, Sacred Light, Magic and other supernatural powers through her exceptional understanding of Energy.[6]
- Fundamental Energy Conversion: Victor transformed Attributeless Energy into Divine Energy while demonstrating the underlying relationship between the energies employed throughout Creation.[7]

### Trivia
- Pure Divine Energy is colorless and possesses no inherent elemental or positive-negative attribute. Its characteristics are determined by the God utilizing it.[1]
- Divine Energy is the characteristic supernatural Energy associated with Gods, but it is not identical to Divinity or Divine Concepts.
- Divine Energy can acquire positive or negative characteristics without granting its user direct authority over the Primordial Concepts of Positivity or Negativity.[1]
- Divine Energy possesses greater raw potency than conventional Witch Mana in the comparison between [[Hecate]]'s Divine [[Magic]] and ordinary Magic, although Witch Magic remains more versatile.[2]
- A small quantity of Divine Energy can remain detectable within an individual who has inherited or absorbed Divine characteristics.[4]
- Blessings can provide access to selected supernatural effects associated with a God without independently transforming their recipient into a fully developed God.[5]
- The enormous accumulated Divine Energy of an entire Pantheon can serve as a resource for exceptional supernatural projects.[11]
- Divine Energy is distinct from Faith Energy, even though Faith and worship can interact with the development of Divine powers.[15]
- Possessing Divine Energy does not automatically grant universal authority over all supernatural Concepts.
- Divine Energy can be produced through the manipulation of a more fundamental energy source by individuals possessing exceptional control, as demonstrated by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and [[Melida Charlotte Elderblood]].[6][7]
- The colorless neutrality of Divine Energy should not be confused with Attributeless Energy, which represents the origin from which the different attributed energies of Creation arise.[7]
