Senjutsu is a supernatural energy-manipulation discipline centered upon the absorption, circulation and utilization of [[Natural Energy]], the life-sustaining power distributed throughout a planet by its World Tree. Unlike supernatural systems that primarily rely upon the practitioner's internally generated Energy, Senjutsu draws upon an external source: the planetary energy circulating through the natural world and its Ley Lines. The Energy itself is fundamentally neutral and supports the continued existence of planetary life, but its concentration and extraordinary potency make it exceptionally dangerous when absorbed without sufficient physical and spiritual control.[1][2][3]
Senjutsu became particularly important among the Yokais of Japan following their political unification under Otsuki Elderblood Haruna. The traditional supernatural power employed by the major Youkai Clans, Youki, depended upon their relationship with the negative emotions and spiritual influences generated by Humanity. That dependence imposed practical limitations upon where the Youkai could obtain suitable Energy and how reliably they could maintain their supernatural abilities. The discovery of Senjutsu and its related discipline, Ki, offered a means of overcoming those restrictions through the utilization of Natural Energy and the practitioner's own vitality.[1]
Although introduced as a technique developed by the Youkai, Senjutsu is not exclusive to their race. Its underlying Energy originates from the planetary World Tree system, making it accessible in principle to beings capable of perceiving and properly manipulating that Energy. Victor Elderblood demonstrated a particularly extraordinary application after discovering that Roxanne Elderblood, the World Tree bound to his Soul, could supply him directly with her own form of Senjutsu. Unlike the light-green Energy naturally circulating through Earth's Ley Lines, Roxanne's Energy manifested as blood-red Senjutsu carrying characteristics associated with Victor's Blood, murderous intent and supernatural nature while retaining its fundamental identity as an Energy of Life.[2]

### Nature and Origin

#### Natural Energy
Senjutsu is associated with the [[Natural Energy]] that sustains a planet and its living environment. Rather than being a supernatural substance originating within the practitioner's body, this Energy exists throughout the planetary environment and circulates through its natural energy pathways. These pathways are commonly described as Ley Lines, or the planet's Dragon Veins, and are understood as channels through which its underlying Energy flows.[1][2]
The existence of Natural Energy on a planetary scale is directly connected to the functions of [[World Trees]]. A World Tree exists as part of the supernatural foundation sustaining its planet, maintaining the conditions necessary for Life and distributing the Energy associated with that responsibility. Senjutsu is therefore not merely an arbitrary system of Nature [[Magic]] developed by mortals. It represents the utilization of a genuine planetary resource ordinarily dedicated to supporting an entire world.[2]
This distinction explains both Senjutsu's exceptional potential and its dangers. The Energy available through the World Tree system is vastly greater than the quantities that ordinary supernatural beings can safely contain within their bodies. Although a sufficiently skilled practitioner may draw upon that power, possessing access to it does not automatically confer the durability, control or spiritual capacity necessary to use it without suffering damage.[2][3]

#### World Tree Energy
The connection between Senjutsu and [[World Trees]] establishes its underlying origin. [[Natural Energy]] circulating through a planet originates from the supernatural life-sustaining functions of its World Tree, with different Trees capable of generating manifestations possessing distinct characteristics.[2]
[[Yggdrasil]] is associated with the Natural Energy circulating through Earth, while Roxanne possesses a different manifestation originating from her own World Tree existence. Although both produce Senjutsu, their Energy does not necessarily possess identical visual or supernatural qualities. A World Tree's nature can influence the form in which its Energy appears without eliminating its underlying connection to Life and the maintenance of a planet.[2]
Roxanne's explanation of her original function further establishes that this power was not initially intended to serve as an ordinary personal weapon. Her Energy was meant to nourish and maintain a planet. After becoming bound to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul, however, she instead directed that extraordinary resource toward sustaining and empowering him, transforming a planetary energy system into a highly unusual source of individual supernatural strength.[2]

#### Neutrality
Senjutsu is fundamentally a Neutral Energy. This classification describes its underlying nature rather than an absolute absence of characteristics or effects. The same Energy that peacefully nourishes a planetary environment can become dangerous when forced through a body incapable of sustaining it, and its manifestation can acquire characteristics associated with the World Tree producing it.[2][3]
The contrast between Earth's light-green Senjutsu and Roxanne's blood-red variant illustrates this distinction. Earth's Energy naturally conveys the qualities of Life and Nature, whereas Roxanne's manifestation carries a heavy presence associated with Blood and killing intent. Haruna nevertheless recognized [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Energy as Senjutsu because its underlying qualities of Nature and Life remained present despite its radically different appearance.[2]
The presence of violent or negative characteristics does not automatically transform Senjutsu into Miasma or make the Energy inherently evil. Roxanne's role as a World Tree associated with Negativity does not eliminate the neutrality of her life-sustaining function, just as a positive-aligned World Tree does not necessarily produce Energy that is exclusively suited to offensive Holy techniques.[2]

### Ley Lines and Planetary Energy

#### Ley Lines
Ley Lines are natural pathways through which the Energy of a planet circulates. Among the supernatural communities familiar with Senjutsu, they are also associated with the terminology of Dragon Veins. Their importance arises from their role in transporting the [[Natural Energy]] maintained by the planetary World Tree rather than simply functioning as convenient locations for supernatural rituals.[1][2]
Areas situated along particularly powerful Ley Lines contain greater concentrations of Natural Energy and are therefore suitable for Senjutsu cultivation. The availability of such locations allows practitioners to gather Energy directly from the environment, although the increased concentration also requires careful control to prevent harmful absorption.[2]
Haruna introduced [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to Senjutsu within a cave intersected by Earth's Ley Lines. The surrounding Natural Energy manifested as a light-green radiance while flowing toward the young Progenitor in quantities that shocked his instructor. The phenomenon demonstrated how a naturally concentrated source could provide enormous amounts of Energy without requiring its user to generate that power internally.[2]

#### Planetary Life Support
Senjutsu exists because [[Natural Energy]] performs an essential function beyond combat. The supernatural energy supplied by [[World Trees]] sustains the conditions of a living world and contributes to the continued existence of its natural environment. Its ordinary purpose is therefore planetary maintenance rather than individual empowerment.[2]
A World Tree possesses an Energy supply and function suited to the scale of a planet. An individual attempting to draw directly upon that source is not merely acquiring another reserve of Mana or Demonic Energy; they are exposing their body to a power normally distributed throughout an entire planetary environment. This accounts for the considerable difference between the ordinary use of Senjutsu by Youkai and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s direct access to Roxanne.[2][3]

### Cultivation and Training

#### Natural Energy Absorption
The conventional practice of Senjutsu begins with sensing and drawing [[Natural Energy]] from the surrounding environment. A practitioner must become aware of the Energy circulating through the planet and attract it into their body without losing control of the quantity being absorbed. The process requires considerable discipline because the same Energy that sustains the natural world can damage a living organism when introduced at an excessive concentration.[1][2]
Meditation is particularly useful during this process. Maintaining a calm and controlled mental state allows the practitioner to concentrate upon the surrounding Energy and regulate its absorption rather than attempting to force enormous quantities into the body through uncontrolled exertion. The dangers involved extend beyond ordinary muscular fatigue because the Energy interacts with both the physical and spiritual aspects of the practitioner.[1]
- Energy Sensing: Practitioners can perceive Natural Energy circulating through their surroundings and identify locations where it is sufficiently concentrated for cultivation.[2]
- Energy Absorption: Senjutsu allows its users to draw external Natural Energy into their bodies instead of relying exclusively upon Energy generated internally.[2]
- Energy Channeling: Absorbed Natural Energy can be directed through the practitioner's body for subsequent supernatural applications.[2]
- Energy Regulation: Successful practitioners must control the quantity and circulation of absorbed Energy to avoid overwhelming their bodies.[1][2]
- Meditation: Mental concentration and tranquility facilitate the controlled absorption and handling of Natural Energy.[1][2]

#### Energy Filtration
Ordinary practitioners cannot freely circulate unlimited quantities of raw [[Natural Energy]] through their bodies. Its density and intrinsic power can place enormous strain upon an organism, creating the need for a controlled process through which externally absorbed Energy becomes suitable for use.[2]
Haruna's method involves attracting Natural Energy into her body and filtering it before attempting more advanced applications. This process allows her to avoid the direct damage that unrestricted circulation might produce. The filtered Energy can support her use of Ki and provide a foundation for the continued development of her martial and supernatural techniques.[2]
The process is not entirely without limitations. Filtration requires control and introduces additional steps between drawing upon Natural Energy and employing its full potential. A practitioner with sufficient proficiency can eventually attempt to use Senjutsu more directly, but doing so places a greater burden upon the body.[2]

#### Direct Utilization
Advanced Senjutsu practitioners may utilize [[Natural Energy]] without relying exclusively upon the ordinary filtration process. Direct utilization grants access to a more immediate and powerful form of the Energy, but it requires a body capable of enduring the increased strain.[2]
Haruna recognized direct utilization as a possible result of greater proficiency, although it remained physically demanding. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] bypassed the conventional process entirely through his Soul connection with Roxanne. Rather than absorbing Earth's Natural Energy and adapting it for his body, he accessed a source already fundamentally connected to his existence.[2]
The distinction is important: Victor did not merely develop a more efficient version of Haruna's established technique. His circumstances created a different pathway to Senjutsu, with advantages and dangers specific to his relationship with a World Tree existing inside his Soul.

### Senjutsu and Ki

#### Relationship
Ki is a closely related energy-manipulation discipline that utilizes the vitality naturally existing within living beings. Senjutsu and Ki complement one another, but they are not identical.
Senjutsu draws upon external [[Natural Energy]], whereas Ki is associated with internally possessed Life Energy or Vitality. Ordinary practitioners can use the controlled absorption of Senjutsu to assist in developing and employing Ki, creating a relationship between the planetary Energy available in their surroundings and the personal Energy generated or sustained by their own bodies.[1][2]
Haruna's training method illustrates this relationship. She absorbs and filters Natural Energy before employing the corresponding vitality-based techniques. Direct Senjutsu utilization is possible at sufficiently high proficiency, but it places greater strain upon the practitioner than using the safer intermediate process.[2]

#### Victor's Relationship with Ki
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] possesses personal Vitality like other living beings, but his connection with Roxanne makes conventional Ki cultivation comparatively unnecessary. The World Tree within his Soul provides a direct supply of Senjutsu that greatly exceeds the limited quantities of personal Vitality he would otherwise need to expend.[2]
Roxanne therefore advised him to use her Senjutsu instead of relying upon his internal Ki. This did not mean Victor lacked Life Energy or was incapable of learning Ki; rather, the existence of an extraordinary external source rendered the conventional expenditure of his personal Vitality inefficient under his particular circumstances.[2]

### Senjutsu and Youki

#### Youki
Youki is a supernatural Energy traditionally associated with powerful Youkai. Its properties are linked to the emotional and spiritual influences generated by Humanity, particularly negative emotions that certain Youkai can exploit. Although powerful, this relationship limits the availability of suitable Energy in environments lacking the necessary emotional conditions.[1]
These geographical and environmental limitations became increasingly important as Haruna unified the major Youkai factions and sought ways to strengthen their civilization beyond its traditional territories. Senjutsu and Ki offered alternative systems whose operation did not require the same dependence upon hostile Human emotions.[1]

#### An Alternative to Traditional Youkai Energy
Senjutsu allows Youkai to draw upon the [[Natural Energy]] circulating through their environment. This provides an alternative to conventional Youki cultivation and expands the possibilities available to beings capable of learning the discipline.[1]
The systems are not mutually exclusive. Haruna continued using her established Youki abilities and combat techniques while learning Senjutsu and Ki, demonstrating that acquiring Natural Energy does not require abandoning a practitioner's existing racial powers.[4]
Senjutsu also introduces a different developmental challenge. While powerful Youkai may already possess considerable mastery over their natural racial abilities, their experience with Youki does not automatically provide equivalent control over externally absorbed planetary Energy. Haruna herself continued studying the new discipline despite her extraordinary combat experience and status as a Nine-Tailed Kitsune.[4]

### Applications and Abilities

#### Energy Empowerment
One of the principal benefits of Senjutsu is the enhancement of the practitioner's physical and supernatural capabilities. [[Natural Energy]] can circulate through the body and reinforce its ordinary functions, increasing the amount of power available for physical activity and other techniques.[2][3]
The effectiveness of this enhancement depends heavily upon the Energy's concentration and the user's ability to withstand it. A higher Energy output is not automatically beneficial if the resulting strain exceeds the practitioner's physical or spiritual tolerance.
- Statistics Amplification: Senjutsu can reinforce the practitioner's physical capabilities by supplying additional Natural Energy.[2][3]
- Enhanced Strength: Natural Energy can substantially increase the power available to the user's body when properly controlled.[3]
- Enhanced Durability: Senjutsu can contribute to greater bodily resilience, although excessive quantities may damage the same body they are intended to strengthen.[3]
- Enhanced Stamina: A sufficiently stable supply of Natural Energy can reduce the burden imposed upon the practitioner's ordinary Energy reserves.[2][5]
- Energy Reinforcement: Senjutsu can circulate through the body and augment its natural characteristics.[3]
- Power Amplification: Natural Energy may increase the output of a practitioner's existing supernatural abilities when incorporated into their Energy system.[5]

#### Physical Regulation
Senjutsu can also influence how the practitioner's body interacts with its environment. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] demonstrated an unusual passive application in which the Energy circulating through his body prevented his extraordinary physical weight and strength from causing excessive damage to the ground beneath him.[3]
His transformed physiology had become exceptionally dense and powerful, with physical testing placing his apparent bodily weight at approximately six to eight tonnes depending upon how he supported himself. Despite that extraordinary condition, he could remain standing without automatically sinking into the surrounding terrain because the Senjutsu circulating through his body regulated its interaction with the environment.[3]
- Physical Regulation: Senjutsu can assist in controlling the external effects of extraordinary physical characteristics, as demonstrated by Victor's ability to stand without excessively damaging the ground.[3]
- Energy Field: The [[Natural Energy]] circulating through Victor produces a passive effect that moderates his physical interaction with the environment.[3]
- Enhanced Body Control: Controlled application of Senjutsu can support the management of extraordinary physical power, although acquiring an excessively powerful source may initially worsen the user's control.[3]
This effect is demonstrated through Victor's unusual physiology and should not be interpreted as proof that every beginner can independently manipulate gravity, eliminate physical mass or generate an identical passive field.

#### Sustenance and Vitality
As an Energy associated with planetary Life, Senjutsu possesses the potential to sustain and nourish living beings. Its ordinary function within a World Tree is to support an entire planet, but [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s connection with Roxanne allowed that life-sustaining function to operate directly through his body.[2]
After adapting to Roxanne's continuous Energy supply, Victor no longer experienced the ordinary need to consume Blood as a Vampire. The Energy circulating through his body maintained him in excellent condition and eliminated his immediate Bloodlust, effectively providing the nourishment necessary to sustain his physiology without the same dependence upon external feeding.[5]
- Self-Sustenance: Victor's direct connection with Roxanne provides sufficient [[Natural Energy]] to maintain his body without its ordinary requirement for Blood consumption.[5]
- Life-Force Empowerment: Roxanne's Energy continually nourishes Victor's body and reinforces his supernatural existence.[2][5]
- Energy Supply: The World Tree bound to Victor provides an extraordinary continuing source of power rather than requiring him to rely exclusively upon his own reserves.[2][5]
- Statistics Amplification: Victor receives passive improvements to his physical characteristics through the deeper synchronization of his existence with Roxanne.[2]
These effects arise from Victor's exceptional Soul bond with a World Tree. Ordinary Senjutsu practitioners have not been established as automatically possessing freedom from biological nourishment, unlimited stamina or the same continuous Energy supply.

### World Tree Variations

#### Earth's Senjutsu
The [[Natural Energy]] circulating through Earth's Ley Lines manifests as a light-green radiance associated with Life and Nature. This Energy is connected to the planetary World Tree system and can be absorbed by practitioners who possess the necessary sensitivity and control.[2]
Haruna's conventional Senjutsu belongs to this environmental system. She draws the Energy from the surrounding world and must regulate its absorption before utilizing it effectively. The process reflects the ordinary relationship between a practitioner and the Natural Energy available within their planetary environment.[2]

#### Roxanne's Senjutsu
[[Roxanne Elderblood]] possesses a distinct manifestation of Senjutsu generated through her existence as a World Tree. Unlike the green Energy circulating through Earth's Ley Lines, Roxanne's Energy manifests as blood-red [[Natural Energy]] carrying a heavy presence associated with Blood and murderous intent. Despite those characteristics, its underlying nature remains recognizable as Senjutsu rather than Demonic Miasma or an inherently evil Energy.[2]
Roxanne's unusual characteristics arise partly from her own nature and partly from her profound connection with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. The two share an exceptionally close Soul bond, enabling their existences to synchronize in a manner unavailable to ordinary practitioners. Through this connection, the Natural Energy generated by Roxanne can be directed into Victor without requiring him to employ the same environmental absorption method used by Haruna.[2]

#### Victor's Senjutsu
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Senjutsu is a direct expression of his relationship with Roxanne. While initially attempting to absorb Earth's green [[Natural Energy]], he recognized its familiarity and realized that the same underlying form of power already existed within his Soul through the World Tree bound to him.[2]
Abandoning the external Energy source, Victor accessed Roxanne's Senjutsu directly. The resulting manifestation astonished Haruna because it possessed the unmistakable qualities of Natural Energy while simultaneously conveying a presence unlike anything she associated with conventional Senjutsu. The Energy appeared red, carried a heavy sensation of Blood and murderous intent, and yet retained the fundamental characteristics of Life and Nature.[2]
The distinction between Victor and ordinary practitioners is not merely a difference in Energy quantity. Haruna must absorb externally available Natural Energy and regulate it within her body, whereas Victor possesses a living World Tree within his Soul that can supply him directly. This arrangement removes the ordinary intermediate process but creates an entirely new challenge: safely controlling an Energy source originally intended to sustain a planet.[2]

### Victor's Synchronization with Roxanne

#### Soul Synchronization
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s access to Roxanne's Senjutsu developed through the synchronization of their existences. Their connection allowed them to share memories, emotions and the experiences that had shaped their respective lives, further deepening a bond already established at the level of the Soul.[2]
The synchronization was far more significant than an ordinary transfer of Energy. Roxanne's World Tree nature became more directly accessible to Victor, reducing the need for intermediaries when utilizing powers associated with her existence. The process also reinforced Victor's overall physical and supernatural potential, introducing a continuing source of nourishment and empowerment.[2]
However, the abilities made accessible through this synchronization must be distinguished from the ordinary properties of Senjutsu. Roxanne possesses numerous powers associated with World Tree physiology, including the manipulation of Nature, non-sentient Life and her own Negative characteristics. Victor's access to those abilities results from his connection with Roxanne rather than every Senjutsu practitioner automatically possessing the entire range of a World Tree's supernatural powers.[2]

#### Passive Empowerment
The continuous flow of Roxanne's Energy substantially increased [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s base capabilities. His body began receiving an extraordinary supply of power, and the Energy circulating through his physiology continually reinforced his physical characteristics.[2][3]
The advantages became increasingly apparent after he regained control over his altered body. Victor could maintain an extraordinary level of physical activity, sustain himself without his former immediate dependence upon Blood, and operate with an Energy supply that vastly exceeded what conventional Vampires could naturally generate.[5]
The quality of the Energy also influenced his Blood, producing a highly nourishing supernatural substance that contributed to the growth of [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] during their intense training and Blood exchanges. Victor's transformation therefore affected not only his own physiology but also the quality of the supernatural resources his body produced.[5]

#### Amplification of Existing Powers
Roxanne's Senjutsu also interacted with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s preexisting Progenitor abilities. The Energy nourished his supernatural powers and increased their potential, strengthening characteristics that were already present within his Bloodline rather than simply granting him an unrelated collection of techniques.[3]
The interaction became especially apparent when Victor attempted to employ his Vampire Count transformations. The enormous increase in Energy associated with those states combined with his Senjutsu, producing a much greater output than he had previously been capable of controlling.[5]
While this amplification represented substantial growth, it also prevented Victor from safely employing every transformation immediately. His Base Form became manageable before his higher-output states because the latter imposed a far greater energetic burden upon his body.[5]

### Dangers and Limitations

#### Energy Overload
Senjutsu's greatest danger is the enormous density and potency of its [[Natural Energy]]. Although the Energy is fundamentally neutral, its ordinary purpose is to sustain a planetary environment rather than circulate unrestricted through the body of a comparatively small supernatural being.[3]
A practitioner who absorbs more Natural Energy than their body can safely withstand risks catastrophic physical or spiritual consequences. Successful cultivation therefore requires careful regulation, while direct utilization demands substantially greater tolerance than conventional filtered methods.[1][2]
- Physical Overload: Excessively concentrated Natural Energy can damage the practitioner's body when its power exceeds the organism's capacity.[3]
- Spiritual Overload: The Soul must also be capable of enduring the Energy circulating through the practitioner's existence, making physical durability alone insufficient protection.[3]
- Energy Instability: Raw Senjutsu is exceptionally dense and volatile when concentrated within an individual body.[3]
- Loss of Physical Control: Sudden increases in Energy output can disrupt the practitioner's ability to regulate ordinary movements and physical strength.[3]
- Transformation Incompatibility: Combining powerful transformations with unrestricted Senjutsu may exceed the practitioner's tolerance, even when either power can be used separately.[3][5]

#### Continuous Bodily Damage
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s direct use of Roxanne's Senjutsu demonstrated the consequences of introducing planetary-scale Energy into a body that had not fully adapted to its concentration. Despite his extraordinary Vampire Progenitor physiology and regenerative capabilities, the Energy circulating through him repeatedly damaged his body, which was then continuously reconstructed by his regeneration.[3]
The process occurred rapidly enough that Victor could continue functioning, but it produced persistent discomfort and revealed that his body had not yet developed sufficient tolerance to contain the Energy without injury. His regeneration prevented the damage from becoming immediately fatal while the repeated destruction and reconstruction contributed to his body's gradual adaptation.[3]
The same process would have been fatal to Victor at an earlier stage of his development. His improved physiology and previous bodily transformations were essential prerequisites for surviving the unrestricted flow of Roxanne's Senjutsu.[3]
This does not establish that Senjutsu universally grants Regeneration. Victor survived the process because he already possessed exceptional regenerative abilities, which allowed him to recover from injuries caused by the Energy itself.

#### Loss of Control
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s sudden increase in strength initially destroyed much of the fine physical control he had developed through prolonged martial training. Ordinary movements began producing disproportionate effects, and even standing or exerting small amounts of strength risked damaging the surrounding environment.[3]
His concern was particularly severe because an accidental movement could injure family members who approached him without realizing how much his strength had changed. Rather than exploiting the increase immediately, Victor returned to basic training and devoted considerable effort to recovering precise control over his physical capabilities.[3]
Working closely with [[Scathach]], Victor eventually regained full control of his Base Form after approximately one week of intense training. His transformed states remained more difficult to manage because their greater Energy output interacted with Senjutsu on a substantially larger scale.[5]

#### Transformation Restrictions
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s initial attempt to combine Senjutsu with his highest-output Progenitor transformations presented an especially severe risk. His Alter warned that the accumulated burden would exceed what his body and Soul could safely withstand, potentially resulting in his death.[3]
The problem was not an inherent incompatibility between Senjutsu and Vampiric powers. Roxanne's Energy had already strengthened Victor's Progenitor characteristics, demonstrating that the systems could coexist. The danger arose from attempting to combine excessively powerful expressions before his body had developed sufficient control and tolerance.[3][5]

### Relationship with Other Energy Types

#### Senjutsu and Ki
Senjutsu draws upon [[Natural Energy]] supplied through the planetary environment, whereas Ki employs the practitioner's internal Vitality. Both belong to related life-energy disciplines and can support the development of techniques based upon the manipulation of supernatural Energy.[1][2]
Their greatest practical distinction lies in their source and associated dangers. Improper Senjutsu absorption can overwhelm the practitioner's body and Soul, while excessive Ki expenditure threatens the user's personal Life Force.

#### Senjutsu and Youki
Youki is associated with the supernatural characteristics of Youkai and the emotional conditions that nourish their traditional powers. Senjutsu provides an alternative source based upon planetary [[Natural Energy]] rather than the same dependence upon Human emotions.[1]
The two systems can coexist within a practitioner, but mastering one does not automatically confer mastery over the other.

#### Senjutsu and Nature Energy
Although Senjutsu is a form of [[Natural Energy]], its specific method of operation should not be confused with every supernatural ability bearing the name of Nature. Certain [[Werewolves]] possess powerful affinities with Nature and can use a formless Energy to reinforce their bodies, while Fairies and [[World Trees]] possess other supernatural relationships with living environments.
These abilities may share a relationship with the broader Energy systems supporting Life, but their mechanisms, racial characteristics and demonstrated techniques are not automatically identical to the Senjutsu discipline practiced by Haruna and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].

#### Senjutsu and Magic
[[Magic]] and Senjutsu represent different conventional methods of utilizing supernatural Energy. Ordinary Magic is associated with Mana and the systematic execution of spells, whereas Senjutsu emphasizes externally acquired [[Natural Energy]] and its circulation through a practitioner's body.[1][2]
At a more fundamental level, however, these powers belong to the same larger reality of supernatural Energy. Individuals possessing sufficiently advanced control may overcome many distinctions ordinarily imposed by racial affinities and specialized energy systems.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later explanation of Attributeless Energy establishes that Mana, Ki, Senjutsu, [[Divine Energy]] and Demonic Energy are different attributed expressions of Energy. Their ordinary differences remain important to practitioners who can use only the forms compatible with their bodies and supernatural systems, but those limitations become far less significant to beings capable of manipulating Energy at its underlying level.[6]
This does not make the normal Senjutsu techniques of Youkai equivalent to unrestricted Attributeless Energy manipulation. It establishes their place within a broader supernatural framework rather than eliminating the practical distinctions between them.

#### Senjutsu and Divinity
Senjutsu is not inherently a Divine Concept or an ability restricted to Gods. Ordinary supernatural beings can learn to manipulate it through training, although [[World Trees]] possess a considerably deeper relationship with its source.[1][2]
A Divine being may possess access to Senjutsu or similar life-sustaining Energy through other characteristics, but merely manipulating [[Natural Energy]] does not automatically grant a practitioner Divinity, Godhood or conceptual authority over Life.

### Known Users

#### World Trees
- [[Yggdrasil]]: A World Tree associated with Earth's planetary energy system. The [[Natural Energy]] distributed throughout Earth's Ley Lines is connected to the supernatural functions of its World Tree.[2]
- [[Roxanne Elderblood]]: A World Tree bound to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul whose Senjutsu manifests as blood-red Natural Energy carrying characteristics associated with Blood and killing intent. She can supply that Energy directly to Victor through their Soul connection.[2]
- [[World Trees]]: The broader species possesses the ability to generate and distribute the Energy necessary to sustain planetary environments. The characteristics of that Energy can vary according to the Tree producing it.[2]

#### Individual Practitioners
- [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]]: A Nine-Tailed Kitsune who cultivated Senjutsu and Ki as alternatives to traditional Youki. Her methods involve absorbing and filtering externally available [[Natural Energy]], with direct utilization requiring greater proficiency and physical tolerance.[1][4][2]
- [[Victor Elderblood]]: A Vampire Progenitor who gained direct access to Roxanne's Senjutsu through their Soul synchronization. His exceptional version possesses an enormous continuing Energy supply, substantially amplifies his existing powers and allows him to draw upon the abilities associated with his bonded World Tree.[2][3][5]
- Yokais: Certain Youkai possess the knowledge necessary to cultivate Senjutsu and Ki. The disciplines were developed as alternatives to the limitations of their traditional racial Energy system.[1]

### Notable Demonstrations
- Planetary Energy Absorption: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] attracted an enormous quantity of light-green [[Natural Energy]] from Earth's Ley Lines during his initial Senjutsu training. The concentration greatly exceeded Haruna's expectations and demonstrated his exceptional compatibility with the planetary source.[2]
- World Tree Synchronization: Victor established direct access to Roxanne's Senjutsu through their Soul connection, bypassing the conventional filtration process employed by Haruna.[2]
- Blood-Red Senjutsu: Roxanne's Energy manifested through Victor as a blood-red supernatural presence associated with Blood and killing intent while retaining the qualities of Life and Nature.[2]
- Continuous Regeneration Under Overload: Victor's body repeatedly suffered damage from the excessive Natural Energy circulating through it, with his Vampiric regeneration restoring the damaged tissue while he gradually adapted.[3]
- Physical Regulation: The Senjutsu circulating through Victor helped prevent his extraordinary weight and physical power from damaging the ground beneath him without deliberate intent.[3]
- Base-Form Recovery: After intensive training with [[Scathach]], Victor regained control over his enhanced Base Form despite the dramatic increase in Energy output.[5]
- Transformed-State Amplification: Victor's Senjutsu interacted with the Energy released through his Vampire Count transformations, producing an output far greater than his earlier physiology could safely manage.[5]
- Sustenance Through Natural Energy: The continuous supply of Energy from Roxanne maintained Victor's body sufficiently that he no longer experienced his previous immediate need to consume Blood.[5]
- Blood Quality Enhancement: The extraordinary Energy circulating through Victor improved the quality of his Blood, contributing to the development of Scathach's physical condition and supernatural potential during their repeated training and Blood exchanges.[5]

### Limitations
Senjutsu's potential depends upon the nature of its source, the practitioner's compatibility, the quantity of Energy absorbed and the ability to control its circulation. Ordinary practitioners must train carefully to acquire and regulate [[Natural Energy]], while uncontrolled direct utilization can produce devastating physical and spiritual consequences.[1][2][3]
Access to a powerful World Tree does not automatically eliminate these limitations. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] possessed a uniquely favorable relationship with Roxanne, yet the raw Energy still overwhelmed his body and forced him to retrain his physical control. The same power that continuously nourished him could have killed him if accessed before his previous transformations had sufficiently strengthened his existence.[3]
The most exceptional benefits demonstrated by Victor, including continuous personal nourishment, access to Roxanne's abilities and the massive amplification of his Progenitor powers, depend upon his extraordinary Soul bond rather than representing guaranteed abilities of every Senjutsu practitioner.[2][5]

### Trivia
- Senjutsu is one of the supernatural disciplines developed by the Youkai as an alternative to the geographical and energetic limitations of traditional Youki.[1]
- Earth's [[Natural Energy]] manifests as a light-green radiance, while Roxanne's Senjutsu manifests as blood-red Energy possessing characteristics associated with Blood and killing intent.[2]
- The Natural Energy used in Senjutsu originates from the planetary World Tree system rather than being generated exclusively by its practitioners.[2]
- Senjutsu and Ki are closely related but distinct disciplines. The former utilizes external Natural Energy, while the latter involves the user's own Vitality.[1][2]
- The extraordinary density of Senjutsu arises partly from its original function as an Energy capable of sustaining an entire planet.[3]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s body was initially incapable of safely handling the unrestricted Energy provided by Roxanne, even after his transformation into a Vampire Progenitor.[3]
- Victor's use of Senjutsu initially disrupted his control over ordinary movements, requiring him to repeat fundamental martial training.[3]
- Victor's improved Energy circulation eventually eliminated his immediate need for Blood consumption, although this benefit is associated with Roxanne's unique continuous supply rather than ordinary Senjutsu cultivation.[5]
- At the fundamental level of supernatural Energy, Senjutsu belongs to the same general system as Mana, Ki and other attributed energies, but its conventional acquisition and application remain distinct.[6]
