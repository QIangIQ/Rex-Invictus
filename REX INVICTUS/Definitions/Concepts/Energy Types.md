Energy Types is the collective classification of the different forms, sources, attributes and supernatural manifestations of Energy throughout The Universe in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]]. Energy is the underlying medium through which supernatural beings exercise their powers, Gods manifest their Divinities, World Trees sustain planetary environments, Dragons impose their will upon Reality, and supernatural civilizations develop [[Magic]], artifacts and advanced technologies. Although these powers can appear fundamentally different because of their characteristics and methods of utilization, they ultimately share a common energetic foundation within the native cosmological system.
The principle was initially proposed by Merlin, whose understanding of supernatural power led him to theorize that seemingly unrelated energies were actually different expressions of the same fundamental phenomenon. Albedo Moriarty Elderblood subsequently demonstrated this relationship by simultaneously manifesting Demonic Miasma, Angelic Sacred Energy, [[Natural Energy]], Magic Energy and [[Divine Energy]]. Her explanation established that these energies differ principally through the characteristics imposed upon them, the supernatural sources producing them and the beings utilizing them.[1][2]
This interpretation developed further through Melida Charlotte Elderblood, whose exceptional understanding of Energy enabled her to manipulate its underlying propertyless state. By observing her abilities, Victor identified Attributeless Energy as the foundational medium from which attributed supernatural energies originate. Rather than creating an unrelated substance for each supernatural phenomenon, a sufficiently knowledgeable being can impose information upon Energy, giving it characteristics such as Fire, Ice, Life, Death, Magic or Primordial power. Victor subsequently demonstrated that even [[Primordial Energy]] could be produced by assigning the appropriate characteristics to this underlying medium.[3][4]
The most comprehensive explanation of the relationship between Energy Types was provided by Victor during his instruction of his daughters. He established that Divine Energy, Demonic Energy, Spirit Energy, [[Senjutsu]], Mana and Ki are not independently existing substances separated by insurmountable metaphysical barriers. They represent different expressions and applications of Energy whose final characteristics depend upon their source and the information governing them. Ordinary beings are generally restricted to the energies compatible with their existence, whereas individuals possessing extraordinary understanding can manipulate Energy closer to its fundamental state.[5]
The system nevertheless contains an important exception: [[Eldritch Energy]]. This power originates outside the native Universe and introduces the influence of a foreign Reality whose underlying Rules differ from those governing ordinary Creation. Its corruptive and contradictory characteristics arise from the interaction between foreign supernatural information and the established structure of the native Cosmos. Consequently, Eldritch Energy must be distinguished from the internal hierarchy governing the powers generated within Victor's Universe.[6][5]

### Overview
Energy can be classified according to its origin, fundamental characteristics, cosmological position, method of generation and application. These classifications frequently overlap. [[Divine Energy]], for example, is naturally generated by Gods but initially possesses a neutral state before receiving characteristics associated with the God utilizing it. [[Natural Energy]] originates principally from [[World Trees]] and is normally distributed through planetary environments, yet a specialized version is also generated internally by [[Werewolves]]. Similarly, an elemental power such as Fire may be produced through [[Magic]], an inherited Vampire Bloodline, a God's Divinity, Draconic physiology or the deliberate attribution of Attributeless Energy.[7][8][2][4]
The classification of an Energy must therefore be distinguished from the technique utilizing it. [[Senjutsu]] is a discipline for absorbing and manipulating Natural Energy, while Runes are structured commands that use Energy to produce supernatural effects. Neither automatically represents a new foundational Energy substance. The same distinction applies to Divine Concepts, whose supernatural authority can be expressed through Energy without the Concept itself becoming synonymous with the medium through which its effects are manifested.[9][10][5]

### The Universal Energy Hierarchy
The native hierarchy of Energy is explained through several increasingly comprehensive discoveries. [[Shiva]] initially described the universal ordering of supernatural powers in terms of [[Primordial Energy]], the Positive and Negative duality, and the Concepts governing Divine existence. Albedo later expanded the explanation by distinguishing neutral Divine and [[Natural Energy]] from comparatively weaker Mortal energies. Finally, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s discovery of Attributeless Energy identified the common foundation underlying the entire structure.[11][2][3][5]

The pyramid consolidates the native hierarchy discussed by Shiva and Albedo with Victor's later explanation of Attributeless Energy. Its specialized branches overlap; not every energy below the polarities is directly generated through every preceding category.

#### Attributeless Energy — The Origin
Attributeless Energy is the foundational medium before the imposition of any specialized characteristics. It does not initially possess the defining properties of Fire, Ice, Miasma, [[Divine Energy]] or any other particular manifestation. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] compared it to an empty canvas upon which a creator applies information, with the resulting attributes determining the behavior of the Energy.[3][4]
In Chapter 1185, Victor explicitly distinguished Attributeless Energy from [[Primordial Energy]] by explaining that the former provides the foundation from which things can be created, while the latter possesses the characteristics required for Creation's operation. Primordial Energy can therefore be produced by assigning the appropriate attributes to the more fundamental medium. Their practical distinction becomes extremely small for beings capable of directly manipulating Energy at its origin, but remains essential to the structure of the hierarchy.[5]

#### Primordial Energy — The Operational Foundation
[[Primordial Energy]], also rendered Primal Energy, is the high-order attributed power associated with the native Universe's fundamental operations. [[Shiva]] described it as the highest energy within the hierarchy known to him before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s discovery of Attributeless Energy, placing it above the Positive and Negative polarities and the ordinary Divine Concepts.[11]
The later explanation establishes that Primordial Energy is not itself entirely without attributes. It possesses the characteristics necessary for the operation of Creation, while Attributeless Energy is the medium from which those characteristics can be generated. Exceptional beings such as Victor and Melida can manipulate the distinction directly, whereas less developed beings must rely upon the energies their existence can safely generate or control.[4][5]

#### Positivity and Negativity — The Cosmic Duality
The Positive and Negative aspects represent the fundamental duality governing native Creation. Their authority encompasses innumerable physical, spiritual, emotional and conceptual phenomena. [[Shiva]] described their energy as capable of opposing extremely powerful conceptual attacks, including Destruction, when utilized in sufficient quantities.[11]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later explained that both polarities are present throughout Existence, although their proportions vary according to the nature of the phenomenon. Physical structures and more abstract spiritual or emotional phenomena may possess different balances, but neither polarity is entirely absent from the normal Universe.[5]

#### Divine and Specialized Energy
[[Divine Energy]] and the more specialized supernatural energies exist within the broader structure established by the fundamental polarities. Gods produce Divine Energy whose characteristics reflect their own nature and Divinities. Other races and supernatural systems produce powers possessing more specialized attributes, including Miasma, [[Holy Energy]], [[Vampiric Energy]], Mana and Youki.[2]
The distinction between these branches concerns their source and the characteristics imposed upon them, not the existence of completely unrelated supernatural substances.

#### Eldritch Energy — The External Branch
[[Eldritch Energy]] is a foreign manifestation whose source lies outside the native Universe. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] described it as comparable, in principle, to the underlying Energy of another Universe. Because it originates from a different cosmological structure, its influence may distort the native environment according to incompatible Rules.[5]
Eldritch power therefore cannot be placed beneath Attributeless Energy or above [[Primordial Energy]] through the same simple ordering used for native energies. Its defining advantage is the introduction of foreign supernatural information rather than automatic superiority in every confrontation.

### Fundamental Energies
Fundamental Energy describes the underlying medium and the highest-order energetic states through which native Creation is established and maintained.

#### Attributeless Energy
Attributeless Energy, also called Energy Without Attributes or Characteristic-less Energy, represents the origin-state medium from which different supernatural energies can be produced. Its defining characteristic is the absence of specialized properties until a sufficiently knowledgeable user imposes them.[3]
Melida instinctively demonstrated the ability to convert Attributeless Energy into other manifestations, including Demonic Miasma, Angelic Light and [[Divine Energy]]. Her capacity differed from ordinary energy conversion because she was not simply transforming an existing attributed power into another familiar element. She could operate directly upon the foundational medium and give it the required properties.[3]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] developed a comparable ability after studying her. In Chapter 1107, he deliberately generated [[Primordial Energy]] by assigning its characteristics to Attributeless Energy, proving that the latter existed beneath even the high-order power employed by Primordial Beings.[4]
The Energy can also return attributed phenomena toward their origin by removing the characteristics sustaining them. This process is dangerous when applied directly to physical matter or living bodies because the information defining the target's existence can be affected. [[Scathach]] discovered the danger when attempting to imitate Victor's technique and inadvertently damaged her own hand.[4]
Victor later explained that the apparent destructive effect is influenced by the nature of the user. Attributeless Energy itself does not inherently possess the destructive properties of Scathach's ordinary powers, but imperfect control allows the practitioner's characteristics to affect the result.[12]
Demonstrated applications: Energy attribution, energy conversion, attribute removal, reinforcement of conventional abilities, efficient manipulation of supernatural phenomena and access to the foundational medium of Creation. Known users: Victor Elderblood, Melida Charlotte Elderblood and selected highly developed family members trained in the technique.[3][4][12]

#### Primordial Energy
[[Primordial Energy]] is a high-order form of Energy possessing the characteristics associated with the fundamental operations of native Creation. The terms Primal Energy and Primordial Energy are used interchangeably in the later material and should be indexed as names of the same Energy rather than separate substances.[11][5]
Primordial Energy was initially described as the power associated with the highest native supernatural beings, including the [[Regulators of Existence]]. It can resist or counter powerful Divine Concepts whose ordinary effects would overwhelm lesser energies, as demonstrated through [[Shiva]]'s explanation of Destruction and its limitations.[11]
The Energy can also support extremely advanced supernatural techniques involving the structure of Reality. During the Empire's research into the creation of a universal paradox, Primordial Energy was considered an appropriate source for sustaining contradictions that ordinary Reality would otherwise correct. Its role was to provide sufficient high-order supernatural support for a phenomenon whose continued existence conflicted with the expected operation of the Universe.[13]
Demonstrated properties: High-order conceptual compatibility, resistance to certain forms of Divine Destruction, support for advanced Reality-related powers and compatibility with the workings of native Creation. Known users: The Regulators of Existence, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], [[Jeanne Elderblood]], [[Melida Charlotte Elderblood]] and exceptional beings possessing the appropriate training or supernatural characteristics.[11][4][5]

#### Creator's Energy
Creator's Energy is a contextual designation for the Attributeless foundation utilized in the creation of the Universe. It refers to the original medium associated with the work of [[Primordial Chaos]], rather than necessarily identifying another Energy substance separate from Attributeless Energy.[3][5]
The distinction is important because the Creator's authority and the Energy employed through it are not automatically the same thing. The Creator can impose characteristics upon the fundamental medium, and those characteristics allow the resulting structures and supernatural systems to function. The name consequently describes the Energy's role in the act of Creation rather than establishing a second origin-state substance.

#### Chaos Energy
[[Chaos]] Energy is an ambiguous designation because the novel employs Chaos in several different contexts. [[Primordial Chaos]] represents the creator associated with the origin of native Creation, whereas Eldritch Chaos belongs to the supernatural nature of beings such as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and [[Azathoth Elderblood]]. These are related through their importance to existence but cannot be reduced to one universally interchangeable Energy Type.[14][15]
Chaos-related power can be expressed through Energy, but a manifestation must be classified according to its actual source. The Attributeless Energy associated with native Creation is not automatically identical to the Eldritch power originating from outside the native Universe.

### Cosmic Polarity Energies

#### Positive Energy
Positive Energy represents the positive aspect of the fundamental duality underlying the native Universe. Its authority extends across the positive-aligned families of supernatural phenomena and Concepts. [[Shiva]] recognized that sufficiently enormous concentrations of Positive Energy could oppose even the destructive influence of his Divinity.[11]
[[World Trees]] associated with Positivity possess a natural relationship with this power. Positive [[Natural Energy]] is particularly important in the nourishment of planetary environments, although the Natural Energy generated by a World Tree remains distinguishable from pure cosmological Positivity.
The [[Veillon Empire]] also demonstrated extensive technological exploitation of positive-aligned Energy, utilizing an immense source of pure Positive Energy within its infrastructure. This establishes that Positive Energy can serve as a strategic resource for sufficiently advanced civilizations rather than existing exclusively as an abstract philosophical principle.[16]
Positive Energy is not synonymous with [[Holy Energy]] or moral goodness. The latter is a specialized sacred attribute associated principally with the Heavenly system, whereas Positivity is a foundational cosmological category.

#### Negative Energy
Negative Energy represents the negative aspect of the fundamental duality. It is associated with numerous negative-aligned Concepts, powers and supernatural phenomena without being reducible to any one of them.[11]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly demonstrated the distinction between pure Negative Energy and Demonic Miasma when he simultaneously manifested a sphere of Negative Energy and a separate sphere of dense, pitch-black Miasma. Their different appearances and supernatural effects established that the two are related but not identical forms of Energy.[17]
Negative Energy also contributes to the development of Youki. Humans generate negative emotional Energy, which Youkai can absorb and refine into their characteristic supernatural power. This transformation produces a specialized racial Energy rather than simply leaving the accumulated negativity unchanged.[18]

#### The Interaction of the Polarities
Positivity and Negativity are complementary aspects of the native Universe rather than entirely independent cosmological systems. Their relationship underlies the supernatural balance, and their relative influence contributes to the characteristics of many Concepts and energies.
In Chapter 1185, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explained that the native Universe contains both polarities throughout its physical and conceptual structure. Their distribution is not always equal in practical expression, with more abstract or emotional phenomena potentially exhibiting a greater proportion of Negativity and many physical manifestations tending toward Positivity.[5]
The interaction of these forces is therefore considerably more extensive than a simple opposition between Holy and Demonic powers. Divine Concepts, spiritual phenomena, Nature-derived energies and the physical structure of the Cosmos can all possess relationships with both sides of the duality.

### Eldritch Energies
FOREIGN COSMOLOGICAL ENERGY
[[Eldritch Energy]] operates through an origin outside the native Universe. Its foreign attributes can introduce corruption, distortion and supernatural effects whose behavior cannot be explained solely through the ordinary native hierarchy.[5]

#### Eldritch Energy
[[Eldritch Energy]] is the characteristic supernatural power associated with [[Outer Gods]] and other Eldritch existences. Its defining property is its foreign origin. Unlike the ordinary energies generated within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s native Universe, Eldritch Energy originates from an external Reality possessing its own supernatural Rules and foundational information.[6][5]
Victor explained that Eldritch Energy can be considered analogous to the Attributeless Energy of another Universe, but its actual manifestation within the native Cosmos acquires the characteristic of distortion because the two systems are incompatible. The resulting power alters local phenomena according to foreign information, producing corruption and other effects that ordinary native supernatural beings may struggle to interpret.[5]
This characteristic allows Eldritch Energy to serve as an alternative source supporting powers that would ordinarily derive from native Primordial authorities. Victor demonstrated this when he replaced the connection sustaining Ophis' developing Infinity Divinity with his own Eldritch power, preventing the native Primordial Infinity from acting as its source.[19]
Eldritch Energy can also contaminate the underlying relationships supporting native Concepts. Its danger therefore extends beyond ordinary physical corruption because its influence can spread through the supernatural foundations connected to an affected phenomenon.

#### Nightmare Energy
Nightmare Energy is a specialized Eldritch-aligned manifestation associated with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Nightmare nature and the powers of certain members of his family.
[[Nero]]'s developing Apocalypse Divinity initially drew upon Victor's Nightmare existence instead of the native authority normally supporting comparable supernatural Concepts. Ophis subsequently acquired a similar relationship when Victor redirected her Infinity authority away from the native Primordial source.[19]
Nightmare can also function as an attribute imposed upon Attributeless Energy, demonstrating that a particular manifestation may be reproduced through deliberate energy attribution without erasing the distinction between the original Eldritch source and a constructed energetic imitation.[12]
Nightmare Energy is consequently indexed as a specialized expression associated with the broader Eldritch category rather than an entirely independent cosmological foundation.

#### Eldritch Chaos
Eldritch [[Chaos]] is associated with the nature and existence of beings such as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Azathoth. Its supernatural manifestations can possess extraordinary properties connected to foreign Reality, Eldritch Concepts and the manipulation of existence beyond the conventional limitations of native supernatural beings.[15]
The expression should be distinguished from Attributeless Energy and the [[Primordial Chaos]] responsible for the creation of Victor's native Universe. Eldritch Chaos represents a foreign state of existence and its associated powers rather than automatically identifying the original unmodified medium of native Creation.

#### Eldritch Nightmare and Conceptual Attribution
Eldritch influence can be incorporated into the power supporting a Concept. [[Nero]]'s Apocalypse and Ophis' Infinity provide direct demonstrations of this interaction, since their developing Divinities received power from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Eldritch nature rather than relying exclusively upon conventional native sources.[19]
A Concept sustained through [[Eldritch Energy]] can acquire characteristics different from those of an equivalent Concept sustained through ordinary native authority. This distinction concerns the source and supernatural structure supporting the power rather than the mere name of the Concept.

### Divine and Spiritual Energies

#### Divine Energy
[[Divine Energy]] is the characteristic supernatural power generated by Gods. Albedo demonstrated that Divine Energy naturally possesses a colorless and initially unattributed state before the God gives it characteristics reflecting their own supernatural existence.[2]
A God associated with Life may produce positively aligned manifestations, while a deity associated with Death or other negative Concepts can give the same fundamental Divine Energy different properties. The result depends upon the God's nature, Divinity and capacity to control the Energy.
Divine Energy is distinct from Divinity. Energy provides the supernatural medium through which power is exercised, while Divinity represents a metaphysical connection or authority associated with a Concept. A God can produce Divine Energy without automatically possessing every possible Divine Concept.
Divine Energy should also be distinguished from Attributeless Energy. Both may initially lack conventional elemental attributes, but the latter represents the more fundamental medium beneath the specialized Divine state.[3][5]

#### Faith Energy
Faith Energy is generated through belief, conviction and devotion. Its source is the strength of Faith rather than a particular race or physical organ. Religious supernatural systems can utilize it to empower their practitioners, sustain blessings and invoke Divine assistance.[20]
Faith does not require unquestioning obedience to one particular deity. Conviction in an ideal, a person or one's own beliefs can also possess supernatural significance. This makes Faith fundamentally different from [[Holy Energy]], which is a specialized attributed power associated principally with Angels and the Heavenly system.
The mature [[Elderblood Empire]] demonstrated the extraordinary quantity of Faith capable of accumulating across an enormous civilization. Trillions of inhabitants contributed to the supernatural power available through the Empire's religious structure, allowing sufficiently developed practitioners to obtain exceptional benefits from their relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[21][22]

#### Holy Energy
[[Holy Energy]], also called Sacred Energy or Holy Light, is the characteristic sacred power associated with Angels, Saints and the Heavenly system. It possesses a specialized alignment connected to the positive side of Creation, but it is not identical to pure Positivity.[2]
Holy Energy can support purification, supernatural healing, exorcism and techniques particularly effective against certain Demonic and Vampiric beings. The destructive Holy Fire employed through Jeanne's Judgment of Israel demonstrates its ability to destroy Demonic magical constructs, purify Miasma and establish a sanctified environment hostile to Demons.[23]
Ordinary elemental Light should not be treated as synonymous with Holy Energy. [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]] possesses a Light affinity that does not automatically confer the Sacred properties associated with the traditional Vampiric weakness.[24]

#### Soul Energy
Soul Energy is supernatural power directly associated with Souls and spiritual existence. It is particularly relevant to beings and systems capable of interacting with the underlying information preserved within a Soul.
The Tower of Nightmare employs traces of Soul Energy alongside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood to nourish the Souls of trainees repeatedly exposed to simulated death and supernatural reconstruction. This establishes Soul Energy as a directly utilized resource rather than simply another name for ordinary Mana.[25]
Soul Energy also possesses connections to the larger cosmic administration of spiritual existence, including the roles performed by [[The Judges of The Abyss]] and the Universal Tree. The Judges oversee the passage of Souls into the Akashic system, while the Universal Tree participates in the recycling of Energy and the generation of new Life.[26]
Soul Energy should not automatically be equated with unrestricted Soul Manipulation or the authority to create, destroy or reincarnate every Soul. Those are specialized abilities dependent upon the individual possessing them.

#### Spirit Energy
Spirit Energy is explicitly included among the named Energy Types in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s explanation of the common foundation of supernatural power. He classified it alongside [[Divine Energy]], Demonic Energy, [[Senjutsu]], Mana and Ki, establishing it as a recognized expression of Energy.[5]
Spirit Energy belongs to the broader category of spiritual powers, but the novel's available explanations do not provide a fully standardized independent system specifying its precise generation rate, ordinary reserves or universal applications.
Its distinction from Soul Energy should therefore be preserved. Both are associated with spiritual phenomena, but the direct naming of Spirit Energy does not prove that every manifestation of Soul Energy and Spirit Energy is identical.

#### Dream Energy
Dream Energy is the supernatural power associated with Dreams, consciousness and related conceptual phenomena. Its existence is explicitly demonstrated when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] manipulates a small amount of Dream Energy to interfere with Hela's consciousness, allowing him to enter her mind without relying upon conventional physical contact.[27]
The technique combined Dream Energy with Victor's advanced understanding of Reality, demonstrating that a relatively small quantity of the appropriate attributed power could produce a highly specialized mental effect.
Dream can also be imposed as an attribute upon Attributeless Energy through [[Creator's Energy Mastery]].[12]
Dream Energy is distinct from Nightmare Energy. Although both interact with dream-related phenomena, Nightmare possesses a specific association with Victor's Eldritch existence and can introduce characteristics extending beyond ordinary native Dream authority.

### Racial Energies

#### Draconic Energy
[[Draconic Energy]] is the characteristic supernatural power generated and manipulated by Dragons. It sustains their exceptional physiology and is closely associated with the [[Dragon Heart]], an extraordinarily powerful biological Energy system capable of supplying the resources required for advanced Draconic abilities.
A Dragon's relationship with Energy is broader than the mere generation of Draconic Energy. [[True Dragons]] possess exceptional compatibility with the supernatural structure of Reality and can manipulate many forms of Energy through their nature and understanding.[28][29]
Chapter 1173 explicitly demonstrates that True Dragons should not require Runes or [[Magic]] Circles simply to perform ordinary Energy manipulation. Their Draconic existence already provides compatibility with Energy, while Runes are particularly useful when imposing more advanced instructions or conceptual effects.[29]
The artificial Dragon Heart created during the development of Imperial technology also demonstrates that not every Energy produced by a Draconic-style generator must automatically possess Draconic attributes. Its output initially lacked Positive, Negative and Draconic characteristics, functioning instead as a source of nearly unaltered Energy.[30]

#### Vampiric Energy
[[Vampiric Energy]] is the characteristic supernatural power associated with the Vampire Race. It varies between individuals according to quantity, density and coloration, reflecting differences in their supernatural development and inherited characteristics.[31]
Vampiric Energy can manifest externally as an aura and can operate alongside the supernatural powers associated with a Vampire's Bloodline. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s early dark Energy manifestation was sufficiently unusual that the Demon [[Belial]] questioned its resemblance to Demonic Energy, demonstrating that Vampiric power can acquire appearances distinct from those of ordinary Vampires.[31]
Vampiric Energy should not be merged with pure Negative Energy, Miasma or the physical substance of Vampire Blood. These systems can interact within one being while retaining different origins and supernatural properties.

#### Werewolf Energy
[[Werewolf Energy]] is a specialized internally generated manifestation of [[Natural Energy]] possessed by [[Werewolves]]. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] identified its similarity to World Tree Energy after observing the remaining Werewolf characteristics within [[Nero]]'s Soul.[8]
Unlike conventional [[Senjutsu]], which ordinarily draws Natural Energy from the environment, Werewolves naturally produce a related and comparatively diluted Energy through their own physiology.
The Energy can acquire elemental characteristics according to the practitioner's affinity. [[Adam William Lykos]] demonstrated advanced Lightning manipulation through his internal Energy, while [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] explained that the principal combat advantage of the system lies in reinforcing the Werewolf's naturally powerful body.[32][33]

#### Youki
Youki is the supernatural Energy accumulated and refined by Youkai. Its traditional source is negative emotional Energy generated by Humans, which Youkai absorb and transform into their own characteristic power.[18]
The relationship can be represented as:
Human Negative Emotional Energy → Absorption and Refinement → Youki
Youki possesses properties and applications distinct from pure cosmological Negativity. Its accumulated emotional characteristics also caused difficulties when Haruna attempted to combine it with the comparatively neutral [[Natural Energy]] used by [[Senjutsu]].[18]

#### Miasma
Miasma is the characteristic Demonic Energy associated with Demons and the infernal environments they inhabit. It possesses corrosive, toxic and weakening properties whose effects vary according to concentration, quality and the supernatural development of the being producing it.
Miasma is particularly important within [[Biblical Hell]], where it functions as an environmental resource necessary for the continued operation of the Realm and the development of its inhabitants. Removing excessive quantities can disturb the environment rather than merely eliminating a harmful supernatural contaminant.[34]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s direct manifestation of Miasma beside pure Negative Energy establishes that it is a specialized form rather than the entirety of cosmological Negativity.[17]

#### Adrasteia Hereditary Energy
Adrasteia Hereditary Energy is a descriptive classification for the unusual supernatural power possessed by Clan Adrasteia. Its most significant demonstrated property is its effectiveness against the exceptional regeneration and immortality of [[Nightingale]]'s Monsters.[35]
The relevant Energy can enable fatal attacks against Monsters that would otherwise survive damage inflicted through conventional methods. Its characteristics are therefore important to the historical role of Clan Adrasteia as protectors of Nightingale.
The novel has not established a universally standardized independent name for this Energy. It is retained under a descriptive title rather than presented as a confirmed new fundamental energy species.

### Environmental and Cultivation Energies

#### Natural Energy
[[Natural Energy]], also called Nature Energy or World Tree Energy, is the supernatural power generated and distributed by [[World Trees]] to sustain planetary environments. It circulates through planetary Ley Lines and can be absorbed by beings possessing the appropriate cultivation techniques.[18][7]
Natural Energy possesses a fundamentally neutral foundation but can acquire different characteristics according to its source. Positive World Trees generate positively aligned manifestations, while negative World Trees produce Energy possessing different attributed properties. Roxanne's blood-red Natural Energy and the green environmental Energy circulating through Earth's Ley Lines provide contrasting examples.[7]
Its principal functions include planetary nourishment, environmental development, support for Life and the maintenance of natural ecosystems. It can also be used for physical enhancement, Energy barriers, specialized transportation and combat techniques when manipulated by compatible supernatural beings.

#### Mana / Magic Energy
Mana, also called [[Magic]] Energy, is the supernatural power conventionally used by Witches, Mages and other practitioners of Magic. Albedo described it as a comparatively weak neutral form of Energy, sharing the same underlying foundation as Divine and [[Natural Energy]] while possessing different practical limitations and applications.[2]
Mana can be shaped into numerous supernatural phenomena through spell construction, Magic Circles, enchantments and other methods. Its versatility allows practitioners to manipulate elemental attributes, construct barriers, perform transformations and create specialized magical effects.
Magic is the system through which Mana is manipulated. Mana itself is the Energy utilized by those techniques.

#### Ki
Ki is an internally utilized Energy associated with the vitality of living beings. Haruna identified it as a distinct cultivation method developed alongside [[Senjutsu]], with the principal difference being the practitioner's reliance upon their own vitality rather than an external environmental supply.[18]
Excessive use can consume the practitioner's life force and produce serious physical consequences, including a reduction of lifespan. This makes Ki fundamentally different from the direct utilization of a sufficiently abundant external source such as a World Tree.
Ki is also explicitly included in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s explanation of the unity of supernatural Energy, demonstrating that its apparent distinction from Mana or [[Divine Energy]] arises from the characteristics and applications imposed upon the same underlying medium.[5]

#### Environmental Miasma
Miasma possesses both racial and environmental functions. Demons generate and manipulate it through their physiology, while the infernal environment itself depends upon its continued presence.
The relationship resembles the importance of [[Natural Energy]] to living planets, although the characteristics of the two energies differ considerably. Miasma possesses corrosive and negative-aligned attributes rather than the neutral foundation characteristic of ordinary environmental Natural Energy.[34]

#### Stellar and Planetary Energy
Stellar Energy and Planetary Energy can describe the power contained within stars, planets and other astronomical structures. Their existence is particularly important to the cosmic recycling system governed by the Primordial Beings.
Certain beings associated with Death consume Energy released by dying stars, planets and galaxies. The distribution of these resources is governed through the functions performed by Primordial Death and the broader systems sustaining the Universe.[27]
[[The Universal Tree]] also explained that the death of an enormous galaxy can release sufficient Energy to contribute to the formation of numerous younger galaxies elsewhere within Creation. This establishes the role of astronomical Energy within the cosmic cycle rather than merely treating stars and planets as inert physical objects.[26]
Stellar and Planetary Energy are indexed as recognized sources and manifestations, but the novel does not establish one universally standardized mystical substance called Stellar Energy that must be identical across every astronomical or supernatural context.

### Elemental Energies
Elemental Energies are attributed forms of Energy possessing characteristics corresponding to particular natural or supernatural elements. The same elemental attribute can emerge from different energy sources, meaning an attack composed of Fire does not automatically reveal whether its user relied upon Mana, [[Divine Energy]], [[Vampiric Energy]] or another supernatural system.

#### Elemental Energy and Divine Concepts
An elemental manifestation is not necessarily equivalent to the Divine Concept governing that element. A Witch manipulating Fire through Mana does not automatically possess the Divinity of Fire, just as a powerful God capable of projecting Lightning is not necessarily limited to ordinary electrical phenomena.
The distinction becomes particularly important at higher levels of supernatural development, where the user's authority may extend beyond the physical characteristics of an element and influence its underlying conceptual properties.

#### Composite Elemental Energy
Multiple elemental characteristics can be imposed upon Energy to create combined manifestations. Such effects may arise naturally from a user's physiology, through structured [[Magic]] or through advanced manipulation of the underlying Energy itself.
The existence of combined attributes does not mean they constitute independently existing foundational energies. They are generally specialized expressions derived from the interaction between established elemental properties.

### Conceptual Energy Manifestations
A Conceptual Energy Manifestation occurs when Energy expresses properties associated with a Concept or Divine authority. The resulting power may possess effects that cannot be explained solely through conventional physical damage. Nevertheless, the Concept, its Divine authority and the Energy carrying that authority remain distinct components of the supernatural system.

#### Destruction Energy
Destruction Energy is directly associated with the power utilized by [[Shiva]]. Its conceptual characteristics allow it to destroy conventional elemental attacks and other phenomena within the scope of his authority.[11]
Destruction can be opposed by certain higher-order or incompatible powers, including Creation, [[Primordial Energy]] and sufficiently developed expressions of the Positive and Negative polarities.
The distinction between Destruction and END is particularly important. Destruction breaks down existing structures, while the Concept of END possesses a more fundamental relationship with the conclusion of existence and the termination of phenomena.[36]

#### Creation Energy
Creation Energy describes Energy expressing the authority of Creation. It can be utilized to generate or reconstruct supernatural structures, although the resulting capabilities depend upon the authority, understanding and available Energy of the individual.
[[Shiva]] identified Creation as a natural contradiction to Destruction, demonstrating that the interaction between the two Concepts is not reducible to the ordinary amount of Energy released.[11]
Creation Energy should be distinguished from Attributeless Energy. The former expresses a particular Concept, whereas the latter precedes the imposition of such attributes.

#### BEGIN-Attributed Energy
BEGIN is one of the highest-order Divine Concepts discussed in the novel. It possesses a relationship with origin, beginning and the initiation of existence, and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] developed an exceptionally powerful connection to it through his Divine evolution.
BEGIN-related manifestations can utilize Energy carrying the authority of that Concept, but BEGIN itself remains a metaphysical principle rather than a conventional independent racial Energy.[11][37]

#### END-Attributed Energy
END is the Concept associated with the conclusion of an existence or phenomenon. Its finality distinguishes it from ordinary Destruction and makes it exceptionally dangerous when applied through sufficiently developed supernatural authority.[11][36]
The power of END can be incorporated into advanced attacks and technological systems. The development of Polaris included an Imperial weapon capable of channeling END-related power through appropriate conductors, demonstrating that a Concept can be expressed through engineered systems as well as direct Divine techniques.[38]

#### Life-Attributed Energy
Life Energy can describe manifestations associated with the maintenance, generation and development of living existence. Its meaning depends upon context.
[[Natural Energy]] sustains living planets through [[World Trees]], while the Divinity of Life can produce effects associated with a God's conceptual authority. Ki is additionally connected to the vitality of individual living beings. These systems share relationships with Life without necessarily constituting one universally interchangeable Energy substance.[7][26]

#### Death-Attributed Energy
Death Energy can describe powers carrying the characteristics of Death or the supernatural influence of the Primordial system governing the termination of existence.
The distinction between Death as a Concept, the Primordial Being responsible for cosmic functions and the specific Energy employed by an individual remains essential. A God possessing a Death-related Divinity does not automatically exercise every power belonging to Primordial Death.

#### Infinity-Attributed Energy
Infinity is a fundamental Concept whose power can be expressed through specialized Energy. Ophis' awakening demonstrated that the source sustaining an Infinity-related Divine authority can be altered under exceptional circumstances, allowing an Eldritch source to replace the ordinary native relationship.[19]
Infinity-Attributed Energy should therefore be indexed according to the particular source supporting it rather than being treated as a universally identical substance across all users.

#### Order-Attributed Energy
Order represents a conceptual authority associated with the establishment and enforcement of Rules. Its manifestations can interact with the structure of Reality and can provide advantages against certain forms of supernatural irregularity.
[[Violet Elderblood Snow]] demonstrated an exceptional relationship with Order and the ability to utilize it in conjunction with advanced Energy manipulation. Nevertheless, Order remains a Concept and source of authority rather than automatically constituting a separate fundamental Energy family.[39]

#### Void-Attributed Energy
Void is associated with the absence or emptiness of conventional existence. [[Nocturnus]] possesses supernatural characteristics connected to the Void and an unusual neutrality relative to the ordinary Positive and Negative aspects.[40]
Void-related power must be distinguished from the Eldritch Concept of Nullity, whose origin and effects belong to a foreign cosmological system.

#### Luck and Fate
Luck and Fate represent supernatural forces capable of influencing probability, outcomes and the paths through which events develop. Their conceptual manifestations can interact with Reality in ways that ordinary physical attacks cannot reproduce.
These forces are genuine components of the supernatural setting, but their existence does not independently establish one standardized reservoir called Luck Energy or Fate Energy available to every practitioner.[41][42]

#### Balance
Balance is a fundamental principle associated with the maintenance of equilibrium throughout Existence. Its authority is connected to The Owner of Limbo, whose function involves preserving the relationships necessary for a stable Cosmos.
Balance can be expressed through supernatural power, but the Concept itself should not be classified as merely another elemental Energy substance.

#### Reincarnation
Reincarnation is a universal process connecting Life, Souls, Death and the recycling of Energy.
The Universal Tree explained that the Energy contained within deceased beings is returned through the cosmic cycle, contributing to the generation of new Life. The [[Akashic Records]] and the Judges of the Abyss are essential to the administration of this process.[26]
Reincarnation is therefore a system utilizing and redistributing Energy rather than a confirmed independent form universally known as Reincarnation Energy.

### Hybrid and Specialized Energies

#### Demonic-Divine Energy
Demonic-[[Divine Energy]] is a descriptive classification for an attributed manifestation combining Demonic and Divine characteristics. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] used the example of his Dragon Goddess daughters possessing Demonic characteristics to explain that apparently incompatible energies can coexist and be manipulated through a common foundational medium.[5]
The combination should not be understood as a permanently separate fundamental Energy Type. It is a hybrid attributed manifestation whose precise behavior depends upon the individual generating it.

#### Abyssal Miasma
Abyssal Miasma is a specialized manifestation of Demonic Energy carrying characteristics associated with the Abyss.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] demonstrated its use through a scouting clone that combined Darkness, Abyssal Miasma and Soul Manipulation to corrupt an enemy commander associated with the Veillon forces.[16]
The manifestation is sufficiently distinct to warrant its own entry within the Energy index, although the available explanation does not conclusively establish whether it represents a completely separate foundational Energy or an advanced attribute imposed upon conventional Miasma.

#### Holy Fire
Holy Fire is a specialized manifestation of [[Holy Energy]] incorporating fiery and Sacred characteristics.
Jeanne's Judgment of Israel demonstrated its ability to destroy Demonic magical structures, disperse Miasma and impose a hostile environment upon Demons and certain Vampires.[23]
Holy Fire is therefore an attributed technique and manifestation of Sacred Energy, not a separate fundamental energy source.

#### Dark Light
Dark Light is a specialized inherited power associated with the union of Demonic and Fallen Angelic characteristics, particularly within [[Lily Elderblood]].
Its existence demonstrates that apparently opposing supernatural attributes can coexist within an exceptional lineage. However, Dark Light should be understood according to the characteristics of the individual possessing it rather than being automatically classified as a universal foundational Energy.

#### Blood-Attributed Energy
Blood-Attributed Energy describes supernatural manifestations carrying Blood-related properties.
Vampires can produce attacks and constructs whose effects depend upon Blood Manipulation or the characteristics of their inherited Bloodlines. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s development further introduced powers associated with Progenitor Blood, Draconic physiology and Divine authority over Blood.
Such manifestations should be distinguished from ordinary [[Vampiric Energy]]. Blood is an attribute and supernatural medium utilized through particular abilities, while Vampiric Energy is the characteristic racial power associated with Vampires.

#### Moon and Solar Manifestations
Moon- and Sun-associated supernatural abilities can possess distinctive Energy manifestations connected to the relevant celestial bodies or Divine authorities.
Haruna's powers incorporate characteristics associated with the Moon, while numerous Gods and other supernatural beings possess powers involving sunlight, stars and celestial phenomena.
These are retained as specialized attributed expressions unless a particular chapter establishes an independent universal Energy substance possessing a separate generation system.

### Energy Mechanics

#### Energy Attributes
An Energy Attribute is the information determining how a particular manifestation of Energy behaves. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s discovery of Attributeless Energy established that attributes are not necessarily inseparable from the underlying medium. A sufficiently advanced practitioner can impose characteristics, transform them or remove them to return Energy toward its origin-state condition.[3][4]

#### Energy Generation
Energy can be generated through several mechanisms. [[World Trees]] supply [[Natural Energy]] to planets, Gods generate [[Divine Energy]], supernatural races produce their characteristic powers, and living beings generate or accumulate specialized internal resources. Religious conviction can additionally produce Faith, while advanced technology can generate or store Energy through artificial systems.[7][2][30][21]

#### Energy Absorption
Energy can be absorbed externally rather than generated entirely within the user's body. [[Senjutsu]] practitioners obtain [[Natural Energy]] from planetary Ley Lines, Youkai accumulate Human-generated negative emotional Energy, and certain supernatural artifacts absorb power from their surroundings.
Melida demonstrated an exceptionally advanced version of this capacity when she absorbed numerous different energies following her rebirth, including traces of [[Primordial Energy]] that would ordinarily be difficult for a newly created being to handle.[3]

#### Energy Conversion
Energy Conversion is the transformation of one attributed manifestation into another. Conventional methods depend upon the user's compatibility and the properties of the available Energy.
[[Creator's Energy Mastery]] provides a more fundamental approach because the practitioner can begin with Attributeless Energy and deliberately impose the desired characteristics.[4]

#### Energy Purity
Purity describes the extent to which a manifestation retains its intended energetic characteristics without contamination or unwanted attributes. [[Natural Energy]] can become influenced by the characteristics of the being absorbing it, while Demonic and Eldritch contamination can introduce additional properties to an existing manifestation.
Purity is therefore separate from quantity. A large supply of Energy does not automatically retain the same properties after passing through an incompatible body or being exposed to a foreign supernatural influence.

#### Energy Density
Density concerns the concentration of Energy within a particular manifestation. Higher density can increase the effect produced by the same apparent volume of power and may create greater strain upon incompatible bodies.
Roxanne identified the Energy of Earth's older World Tree as denser than her own, demonstrating that development can influence energetic density even among sources belonging to the same general category.[7]

#### Energy Efficiency
Efficiency concerns the amount of Energy required to produce a particular result. Extraordinary reserves are advantageous but do not replace the ability to use those reserves properly.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s development of Attributeless Energy dramatically improved the efficiency of his supernatural systems because his body no longer needed to sustain numerous separate attributed energies simultaneously. Instead, he could operate through a common foundation and apply the required characteristics as needed.[21]

#### Energy Compatibility
Compatibility determines how effectively a being can generate, absorb and utilize an Energy Type. A supernatural race may possess natural compatibility with its own characteristic Energy while experiencing difficulties when attempting to manipulate an incompatible form.
[[True Dragons]] possess exceptionally broad compatibility with Energy, allowing them to employ powers beyond their ordinary racial manifestation. Nevertheless, this does not guarantee perfect mastery of every energy. Personal understanding, control and experience remain important even among Dragon Goddesses.[28][29]

#### Energy Recycling
Energy is recycled through the universal processes governed by the Primordial Beings. [[The Universal Tree]] explained that the information belonging to deceased beings enters the Akashic system, while their accumulated Energy is eventually redistributed through the Cycle of Reincarnation.[26]
The quantity of Energy associated with a being affects the time required for this process. Exceptionally powerful existences possess such enormous reserves that recycling their Energy can require extraordinarily long periods, while ordinary beings are processed comparatively quickly.
The recycling system also extends to astronomical structures. The death of stars and galaxies releases Energy that can contribute to the development of new cosmic structures, demonstrating that the cycle concerns the Universe as a whole rather than only sentient organisms.[26]

### True Dragons and the Nature of Energy
[[True Dragons]] possess an unusually direct relationship with the supernatural structure of Reality. Their compatibility extends beyond the ordinary racial Energy generated through their Dragon Hearts, allowing sufficiently developed individuals to manipulate different forms of Energy without relying upon the elaborate external methods required by conventional practitioners.[28][29]
During Albedo's training, [[Scathach]] emphasized that a Dragon Goddess should not continue fighting as a conventional Human magician whose abilities depend upon extensive spell preparation and the construction of [[Magic]] Circles. A True Dragon can employ Energy through its own supernatural nature, reserving Draconic Runes and other structured techniques for more advanced purposes.[29]
This compatibility does not eliminate the importance of knowledge. Albedo's extraordinary reserves and Draconic physiology did not initially provide her with the technique or understanding necessary to utilize her powers as efficiently as the more experienced members of the Elderblood family.

#### The Unified Energy Foundation
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s discovery of Attributeless Energy further extended the potential of advanced [[Draconic Energy]] Manipulation. Rather than maintaining numerous incompatible attributed reserves within his body, he developed a unified energetic foundation from which different characteristics could be imposed as required.[21]
This development greatly improved his efficiency and control, demonstrating the importance of understanding Energy as a common medium rather than a collection of fundamentally unrelated supernatural substances.

### Cosmic Energy Cycle
The Universe contains an immense recycling system through which the Energy of living beings, Souls and astronomical structures returns to the larger mechanisms sustaining Creation. [[The Universal Tree]] explained that the death of an existence releases Energy that can be processed and redistributed to support new Life.[26]
The cycle involves several Primordial functions. The Universal Tree governs the development and renewal of Life, the Judges of the Abyss oversee the passage of spiritual existence through the Akashic system, and Death is responsible for the conclusion and recycling of larger cosmic structures.
This process extends far beyond biological organisms. The death of stars and galaxies contributes Energy to the formation of new astronomical structures, allowing the Universe to maintain a continuing relationship between creation, decline and renewal.[26]
The recycling of Energy does not necessarily preserve its former attributes unchanged. The process concerns the larger energetic foundation supporting existence, while the information associated with individual beings is handled through the systems responsible for Souls and the [[Akashic Records]].

### Notes
- Energy is the fundamental supernatural medium underlying the native system of Creation, while attributes determine the characteristics through which that Energy manifests.[3][5]
- Attributeless Energy is the foundational medium rather than another ordinary elemental or racial manifestation.[4]
- [[Primordial Energy]] possesses characteristics and is not identical to the completely unattributed state.[5]
- Primal Energy is an alternate rendering of Primordial Energy, not a separately established Energy Type.
- Positivity and Negativity constitute the native Universe's fundamental duality and are not interchangeable with [[Holy Energy]] and Miasma.[2]
- [[Eldritch Energy]] has a foreign cosmological origin and cannot be accurately placed as merely another tier within the native hierarchy.[5]
- Spirit Energy is explicitly recognized in the late explanation of supernatural Energy, although its individual mechanics remain less developed than those of the major racial and environmental systems.[5]
- Soul Energy is directly utilized through the Tower of Nightmare and the wider supernatural structures associated with Souls.[25]
- Dream Energy is explicitly demonstrated through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s manipulation of consciousness.[27]
- Nightmare Energy can operate through Victor's Eldritch existence and provide an alternative source for developing Divine Concepts.[19]
- [[Werewolf Energy]] is a specialized internally generated form of [[Natural Energy]].[8]
- Holy Fire, Abyssal Miasma and Demonic-[[Divine Energy]] represent specialized manifestations rather than automatically constituting entirely new foundations.
- Runes, [[Senjutsu]] and Onmyōjutsu are methods of manipulating supernatural power rather than universally established independent energy substances.[18][9][5]
- The origin-state and cosmological hierarchy should not be interpreted as a guaranteed ranking of combat effectiveness. Quantity, control, Conceptual authority, compatibility and the abilities of the practitioner remain decisive factors.
