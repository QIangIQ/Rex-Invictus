Infinity, registered by the System as <Infinity>, is one of the original [[Regulators of Existence]] and the primordial existence governing Infinity, Space, the continuous expansion of Creation and part of the universal characteristics associated with Time. Infinity is not merely a God possessing an Infinity or Space Divinity; he is the greater primordial Aspect from which those Concepts and their corresponding Divine powers derive within Creation.[1][2]
His defining cosmic function is the perpetual expansion of the Universe. Unlike the Primordials whose principal responsibilities concern Souls, Life or the inhabited regions of Creation, Infinity's work primarily occurs at the extreme frontier of existence. Chapter 964 describes him as the literal embodiment of infinite expansion, states that he is effectively the Universe in constant expansion, and identifies the edges of Creation as his Domain.[3]
Infinity is consequently one of the most important Primordials in the administrative hierarchy. The author's original importance scale places him immediately beneath [[Primordial Chaos]] and Positivity/Negativity and above [[True Death]], while the later System hierarchy registers <Infinity> at Authority Rank 2. These placements represent cosmological importance and administrative Authority rather than an ordinary combat ranking between Primordials.[4][5]

### Physical Appearance
Infinity does not possess a conventional biological body. His manifested form is described as a Being that seemed to be made of galaxies, reflecting his nature as the embodiment of cosmic expansion rather than an ordinary living organism.[3]
When he manifested during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ascension, Infinity initially appeared through violet Energy. His association with violet Energy is consistent with the appearance of Infinity-derived power elsewhere in the story, including the Energy that began forming around [[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]] when her <Infinity> Divinity awakened.[1][6]
The Infinity
The galaxy-filled form should not necessarily be treated as the complete physical limits of Infinity's existence. As a true Primordial, his consciousness is integrated into a universal Aspect, while the form perceived by other Beings functions as a manifestation through which that Aspect interacts with Creation.[5]

### Personality
Infinity is extremely reclusive, detached and generally uninterested in events occurring throughout ordinary Creation. His appearance during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ascension surprised the other Primordials because, much like [[True Death]], Infinity rarely involves himself in cosmic affairs unless something directly concerns his Domain or genuinely attracts his attention.[1]
He possesses a calm, neutral and analytical demeanor. When observing Victor's ascension, Infinity did not display hostility toward him despite the unprecedented nature of Victor's developing existence. Instead, he expressed surprise, examined the contradiction between Victor's cosmic-horror characteristics and his nature as a Dragon representing Life and Existence, and noticed the System beginning to correct its own errors around Victor.[1]
Infinity's detachment is closely connected to his responsibilities. Chapter 964 explains that his area of work exists far from most inhabited Creation at the expanding edge of the Universe, meaning the System rarely needs to directly involve itself in his duties. As a result, Infinity is explicitly described as possessing more free time than any of the other Primordials.[3]
The Owner of Limbo later similarly described Infinity and Death as the most secretive members of the group, explaining that the two usually interfere only when something affects their Domains. Unlike Life, Soul and Balance, whose functions constantly interact with civilizations and living beings, Infinity's normal work concerns the continued cosmic expansion occurring far beyond most ordinary societies.[7]

### Nature and Cosmic Role
Infinity is one of the primordial Aspects produced through the structure established after the division of [[Primordial Chaos]]. He represents a universal function rather than merely a powerful supernatural individual.
The author's auxiliary material identifies Infinity as one of the seven original primordial administrators and places him third in the original importance scale, following Primordial Chaos and Positivity/Negativity. The same auxiliary states that the seven Primordials possess infinite power and complete omnipotence within their own realms, with only other Primordials capable of properly dealing with one another.[4]
Infinity's primary responsibility is simple in description but cosmological in scale: Creation must continue expanding. Chapter 964 describes Infinity directly as the Universe in constant expansion and explains that his Domain lies along the edges of Creation. His activity therefore continuously pushes the cosmic frontier outward while the remainder of Creation develops behind it.[3]

#### Infinity, Space and Time
Infinity's conceptual authority includes three major characteristics:
- Infinity — the fundamental principle of endless extension and limitless continuation.
- Space — the spatial framework within which Creation and its structures exist.
- Time — a partially shared primordial characteristic rather than an authority belonging exclusively to Infinity.
The later author explanation clarifies that Death, END and some characteristics of Time are connected to [[True Death]], while Infinity is responsible for Infinity and Space and shares characteristics associated with Time. This unusual overlap exists because multiple higher Aspects can influence related Divine Concepts.[2]
Time therefore should not be described as belonging exclusively to either Infinity or Death. Both possess primordial influence over different or overlapping characteristics of the Concept.[2]
The later revelation concerning the erased Primordial Destiny further complicates the history of temporal Authority. Destiny originally represented the Universe's Destiny, timelines and the predetermined structure of events before being erased from Creation. Infinity's present relationship with Time must therefore be understood within a cosmology whose original temporal architecture has already been fundamentally altered.

#### Continuous Expansion
Infinity does not simply enlarge individual objects or stretch conventional distances. His primordial existence is tied to the actual continuous expansion of Creation itself.
Chapter 964 explicitly states:
Infinity is the Universe in constant expansion, and his Domain lies at the edges of Creation.[3]
The [[Edge of the Cosmos]] is consequently the location most directly associated with his normal cosmic function. It represents the frontier where established Creation continues expanding outward and where the normal structures of mature Reality have not necessarily reached their final stabilized condition.

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Infinity became directly involved in the story during [[Victor Elderblood]]'s ascension into the Dragon God of [[Chaos]]. Victor's awakening produced an unprecedented combination of major and minor Divinities, including Negativity, BEGIN, Destruction, Blood, War and several Concepts born directly from his nature. The disturbance attracted all of the principal Primordial administrators.[1]
Infinity manifested through violet Energy, surprising [[The Universal Tree]] because even the normally reclusive Infinity had chosen to personally observe Victor. His appearance also provided the first direct explanation of his cosmic responsibility: Infinity was identified as the Primordial Entity responsible for the continuous expansion of Creation.[1]
Upon examining Victor, Infinity initially identified him simply as a God of Chaos. The Universal Tree corrected him, explaining that Victor had become something considerably stranger: a Chaos Dragon God simultaneously carrying powers connected to both sides of the Positive/Negative scale.[1]
Infinity continued observing as Victor's true nature manifested. He regarded Victor as an enigmatic contradiction—simultaneously resembling a cosmic horror capable of causing madness while also appearing as a majestic Dragon connected to Life and Existence. When the Judges argued that Victor's existence was destabilizing Balance, Infinity and the other Primordials examined him more carefully and discovered the opposite: Victor's existence was actively forcing nearby instability toward equilibrium.[1]
Infinity was then the first of the group to notice another unprecedented effect. The System itself had begun correcting thousands of minor errors automatically because of Victor's presence. Infinity's observation drew the attention of the remaining Primordials and helped establish that Victor's ascension had transformed him into something fundamentally significant to the operation of Creation.[1]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Infinity's nature and normal responsibilities were explained much more directly during the System's investigation into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. After speaking with Victor, the System returned to the space between existence, also called the [[Abode of the Primordials]], where Infinity was present.[3]
Infinity immediately noticed that the System had remained inactive for approximately ten seconds—an abnormal delay for an entity designed to continuously supervise Creation. After learning that Victor had caused the System unusual difficulty, Infinity reviewed the recording of their conversation and acknowledged Victor's exceptional intuition.[3]
The interaction revealed several important facts concerning Infinity. His visible manifestation appeared to be composed of galaxies, while the narration directly identified him as the literal embodiment of infinite expansion. It also explained why Infinity rarely interfered with the System despite having participated in its creation: his normal duties occurred at the Edge of Creation, far away from the regions where most System intervention was necessary.[3]
Infinity advised the System to avoid using its main Ego during future contact with Victor because Victor's connection with Creation made him unusually capable of noticing hidden details. The System ultimately explained that the [[Akashic Records]] had specifically ordered it to use its primary consciousness when communicating with Victor, providing Infinity with further evidence that unusual forces were deliberately allowing Victor greater access than an ordinary Being should possess.[3]
The most important direct demonstration of Infinity's powers occurred through the history of [[Azathoth Elderblood]]. Long before Victor encountered her personally, the foreign Eldritch Being entered the native Cosmos and grew by consuming enormous quantities of Energy and other beings. Her presence eventually attracted several Regulators, including Infinity, Death, the Universal Tree and the Owner of Limbo.[8]
The native Primordials attempted to deal with Azathoth through the authorities of their cosmology, but her origin outside their system prevented several normal methods from functioning as expected. During the confrontation, Infinity imposed an infinite distance between Azathoth and the Primordials. Despite Azathoth's natural ability to distort surrounding Reality, she was unable to simply cross or overwrite the separation.[8]
When destroying the foreign Being proved impractical, the Primordials changed strategies. Infinity participated in removing Azathoth from the battlefield and confining her within an extraordinary closed dimensional prison constructed with Primordial authorities. The prison combined the properties of Infinity and END, creating an internal region that was effectively endless while still remaining enclosed.[8]
Azathoth attempted to escape by expanding her enormous body and distorting the surrounding Reality, but those methods proved insufficient to immediately overcome the combined structure. She instead began developing long-term methods of interacting with the outside Cosmos through pieces of her Essence.[8]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Infinity's conceptual influence resurfaced when [[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]] awakened her own Divinity. Kali detected traces of <Infinity> developing within Ophis and immediately recognized the Concept as exceptionally dangerous because of the enormous number of applications an Infinity authority could potentially produce.[6]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] perceived something deeper: Ophis' developing Divinity was beginning to draw Energy from Primordial Infinity. Refusing to allow another Primordial to possess even indirect influence over his daughter, Victor used the Energy of his Nightmare Form to sever the connection and substitute himself as the source feeding Ophis' Concept. Her <Infinity> consequently developed into an Eldritch-derived version connected to Victor rather than continuing to depend upon the native Primordial Infinity.[6]
This event provides one of the clearest demonstrations of Infinity's role as a Conceptual Source. A native God awakening <Infinity> naturally begins drawing Energy from the primordial Aspect corresponding to that Concept unless another sufficiently fundamental power changes the relationship.[6]
Infinity's administrative position was later confirmed when Victor opened the System's Authority hierarchy. The ranking placed <Infinity> at Rank 2, immediately beneath Positivity and Negativity and above <Death>, <Life>, <Soul> and <Balance>.[5]
Azathoth also explained the structural immortality shared by Infinity and the other true Primordials. A Primordial cannot simply be killed like an ordinary God because its existence represents a functioning Aspect of the Universe. Destroying Infinity completely would therefore mean attempting to remove the universal Aspect associated with Infinity from Creation itself. It may be possible to destroy a Primordial's ego or consciousness while allowing the Aspect to continue functioning, but this is fundamentally different from eradicating the underlying Conceptual function.[5]
The Owner of Limbo later confirmed Infinity's extremely passive lifestyle. Infinity and Death were described as secretive, non-sentient-oriented Primordials who usually interfere only when their Domains are directly affected, leaving them with substantially more free time than Life, Soul and Balance.[7]

### Authority and Hierarchy
Infinity occupies one of the highest administrative positions within Creation.
The author's original importance scale was:
1 — [[Primordial Chaos]]
2 — Positivity & Negativity
3 — Infinity
4 — [[True Death]]
5 — [[The Universal Tree]]
6 — [[The Judges of The Abyss]]
7 — The Owner of Limbo[4]
This scale was explicitly an importance ranking, not a declaration that Primordials can be reduced to ordinary numerical combat tiers. Each represents a fundamentally different universal function and possesses complete supremacy within the appropriate Domain.[4]
The later System Authority hierarchy records:
Rank 0 — Unknown / vacant supreme position
Rank 1 — Positivity & Negativity
Rank 2 — <Infinity>
Rank 3 — <Death>
Rank 4 — <Life>
Rank 5 — <Soul>
Rank 6 — <Balance>[5]
Infinity is therefore the highest-ranked currently individualized Regulator beneath Positivity and Negativity in the System's visible administrative structure.

### Relationship with the Edge of the Cosmos
The [[Edge of the Cosmos]] is the region most directly associated with Infinity's normal duties.
Chapter 964 states that Infinity's work is far from Creation because his Domain lies at the edges of Creation. This wording reflects the unusual nature of the Edge: it is part of the expanding cosmic structure, but unlike mature inhabited regions it exists at the active frontier where Creation continues extending outward.[3]
Infinity's relationship to the Edge can be represented as:

Infinity should not automatically be credited with personally constructing every galaxy, World Tree, civilization or Law that later develops within the expanded Cosmos. His demonstrated primordial responsibility is the continuity and spatial expansion of Creation itself; other universal systems govern Life, Souls, Balance and the development of structures inside that expanding framework.

### Relationship with the System
Infinity is one of the Primordials responsible for the creation of the System, but unlike several of his companions he rarely needs to interact with it directly.[3]
The reason is structural rather than political. Infinity's Domain lies at the edge of Creation, where the Universe expands naturally through his primordial function. The System primarily supervises developed Creation, detects anomalies and reports problems requiring intervention. Infinity therefore requires comparatively little routine System assistance.[3]
This distance does not diminish his Authority over the System. His later Rank 2 classification places him above every individualized active Primordial other than the semi-conscious Positivity/Negativity pair.[5]
Chapter 964 additionally demonstrates that Infinity is not automatically aware of everything known by the System. He needed the System to show him its conversation with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] before analyzing the interaction. The System itself had also developed over eons into something sufficiently complex that portions of its thought process were beyond Infinity's immediate comprehension.[3]
This is important when describing Primordial omniscience: Infinity possesses overwhelming and effectively complete Authority within his own primordial Domain, but his conscious personality does not automatically process every independent piece of information recorded by separate cosmic entities at all times.

### Relationship with Azathoth
Infinity's confrontation with [[Azathoth Elderblood]] is his most important known direct combat/regulatory feat.
Azathoth was a foreign Being born from a tangle of [[Chaos]] outside the normal native framework. After entering the Cosmos and consuming enormous quantities of Energy, she became an existential threat that the native Regulators could not resolve through their usual methods.[8]
Infinity's contribution occurred in several stages:
1. He imposed infinite distance between Azathoth and the Regulators.
2. Azathoth's Reality distortion failed to immediately bypass that distance.
3. After direct elimination proved ineffective, Infinity participated in transporting and confining Azathoth.
4. The resulting closed prison incorporated the properties of Infinity and END.
5. Azathoth could expand and distort the surrounding Reality but remained unable to immediately reach or break the prison boundary.[8]
The feat demonstrates the distinction between Infinity and ordinary Space Gods. He did not merely create a very large room or teleport Azathoth far away. He imposed an actual Infinity-based spatial condition upon the prison.
It should nevertheless remain classified as a joint Primordial containment feat. Death, Limbo and the other Regulators participated in dealing with Azathoth, and the prison should not be presented as something Infinity independently created through his power alone.[8]

### Relationship with Ophis
[[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]] provides the clearest example of how Infinity interacts with native Gods possessing his Concept.
When Ophis awakened <Infinity>, the new Divinity naturally began drawing Energy from Primordial Infinity. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] perceived the connection and immediately replaced the Primordial source with his own [[Eldritch Energy]], transforming Ophis' developing Divinity into an Eldritch version connected to him instead.[6]
The event establishes several important points:
- <Infinity> can exist as a Divine Concept in lesser Gods.
- Native Infinity Divinities naturally connect to Primordial Infinity as their greater source.
- This relationship can theoretically be severed by a sufficiently fundamental external authority.
- Severing Ophis' connection did not damage Primordial Infinity himself; it changed the Energy source feeding Ophis' individual Divinity.[6]
Ophis therefore should not be treated as possessing Infinity's complete primordial Authority. Her <Infinity> is an individual Divine Aspect whose original native source would have been Infinity before Victor converted its underlying Energy.

### Limitations and Clarifications
- Infinity is explicitly described by the author as possessing infinite power and complete omnipotence within his own realm. The former statement that Infinity was simply "not omnipotent" was incorrect.[4]
- This omnipotence is Domain/realm-specific. Infinity should not automatically be assigned source-level supremacy over Death, Soul, Life, END, Balance, Positivity, Negativity or other independent Primordial Aspects.
- Infinity does not possess exclusive Authority over Time. [[True Death]] shares primordial characteristics associated with Time, while the erased Destiny historically governed timelines and Destiny itself.[2]
- Infinity's ability to stop Azathoth's Reality distortion through infinite distance does not prove unrestricted nullification of every form of Reality Warping. The confirmed feat is superior Infinity/Spatial Authority preventing Azathoth from crossing the imposed separation.[8]
- Infinity's cosmic expansion should not automatically be labeled unrestricted Creation Manipulation. He expands Creation's spatial frontier; the Universal Tree, Soul, Balance and other systems govern different processes within the resulting Cosmos.
- Infinity's galaxy-like body should not be given an exact number of galaxies. Chapter 964 describes him as a Being that seemed to be made of galaxies; the chapter does not provide a precise count.[3]
- Infinity's conscious ego is not absolutely identical to the continued operation of the Aspect itself. Chapter 1075 establishes that a Primordial consciousness may theoretically be destroyed while the universal function remains active.[5]
- Infinity did not independently create the entire prison containing Azathoth. It is a joint structure involving multiple Primordial authorities.[8]
- Ophis drawing Energy from Infinity does not prove that Infinity was deliberately attempting to control or possess her. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] interpreted the natural conceptual connection as unwanted influence and severed it.[6]

### Notes
- Infinity is one of the most reclusive Regulators and normally appears only when something unusually important affects Creation or interests him personally.[1][7]
- The author's original auxiliary places Infinity above Death on the Primordial importance scale, although this should not be treated as an ordinary combat ranking.[4]
- The System similarly registers <Infinity> above <Death>, at Rank 2 compared with Death's Rank 3.[5]
- Infinity's manifested Energy is associated with violet coloration.[1][6]
- Chapter 964 identifies Infinity as both the literal embodiment of infinite expansion and effectively the Universe in constant expansion.[3]
- His principal Domain lies at the Edge of Creation rather than within the densely inhabited regions managed more actively by Soul, Life and Balance.[3]
- Infinity participated in the creation of the System but later became comparatively detached from its routine operation because direct System intervention in his work was generally unnecessary.[3]
- The System's ten seconds of inactivity after talking with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was sufficiently abnormal for Infinity to immediately question what had happened.[3]
- Infinity advised the System not to use its primary Ego around Victor because Victor's connection with Creation made him unusually perceptive.[3]
- Despite being one of the System's creators, Infinity acknowledged that the entity had evolved across the eons into something whose reasoning was not always immediately comprehensible even to him.[3]
- Infinity and Death both possess influence over Time, but neither should automatically be treated as the sole primordial origin of every temporal phenomenon.[2]
- The historical existence of Destiny demonstrates that the modern distribution of temporal responsibilities does not necessarily reflect the original configuration of Creation.
