Creation is the Divine Concept of bringing a thing forth, in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]]. On the positive side the order of importance is Positivity, then BEGIN, then Creation. On the negative side it is Negativity, then END, then Destruction. Creation is the third of its side. BEGIN is the start and the going-on. Creation is the making. There is no good and no evil in that order. The two sides are energy.[1]
The same word is used for the universe, for existence, and for everything that was ever made. This page is not that. A World Tree is not this Concept. Attributeless Energy is not this Concept. A technique that borrows the word is not this Concept.
The three
BEGIN is the start. Creation is the bringing-forth under that start. Destruction breaks a thing so this Concept has matter to make with. END does not feed it. END takes the thing so that it never exists again.

### What It Does
A Dragon can control Creation. A Dragon does not create from scratch unless many conditions are met. That limit was already on [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] before any essence changed hands. His control was enough, in his own weighing of it, to shatter a world's economy. It was still not the act of making a thing out of nothing.[2]
The God of BEGIN can make life the way a God of Creation makes it, if that Divinity is joined to both Progenitor lines and to Negativity's access to Souls. The sentence uses a God of Creation as the measure. BEGIN does not replace the Concept. It can be aimed at the same result when the other powers are there.[2]
When the leviathans were spoken into a dimension of water, the Divinity of Creation was the primary resource. Begin was only the extra boost. Before that day, the usual making had been BEGIN and the Progenitor qualities, with only rare bits of Creation. This time the order was reversed.[3]
Destruction exists so Creation can occur. [[Shiva]]'s Destruction breaks a thing for a God of Creation to use later. The particles are material. They were going to join creation. END deletes the thing so that it never exists again. The Concept of Creation contradicts Destruction. So does [[Primordial Energy]]. So does a bearer of BEGIN, and a bearer of END. So does an absurd amount of Positive and Negative Energy, because those two cover every existing Concept, BEGIN and END included. That quantity is not something an ordinary God carries. [[World Trees]] are the beings it belongs to. The Heavenly Father resists Shiva because the Concept of Creation is in him.[4]
The office of a Primordial of Destruction is that sentence made into a job. Destroy, so Creation can occur, working with [[The Universal Tree]]. A Primordial has to be impartial and has to do the work. [[Kali Elderblood]] has the strength, the Soul, and the mentality, and she is still short of the office, because her ideals do not line up with that impartiality. Creation does not need a Primordial who will not do the job.[5]
Against Kali herself, the counters were named and then not used. A fist wrapped in Destruction can be answered by a greater Divinity of Destruction or by the Divinity of Creation. Victor added Begin and Positivity to that list, and then said he had used none of them. What held was the body: a monstrous body, already adapted to the energies constantly poured into it, and covered by the sheer amount of energy it carries. When the concentrated beam came, he punched the air. Reality distorted. The power returned to her. Holding the Concept is not the same as having to speak it.[6]

Destruction leaves matter. END leaves nothing to bring forth. Creation is not the universe those two sides sit inside.
Death and the Universal Tree are opposites, and neither wields the other's aspect. A God who holds the negative side and the positive side in one body breaks that rule. The Heavenly Father felt a greater Divinity of Destruction in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and, with it, a fragment of Creation, and called him a God of Destruction as the primary and a God of Creation in the making. None of the Primordials had come to the door.[7]
BEGIN is the second most important positive Divinity. It takes in the categories tied to the making of life: Fertility, Life, Love, Reproduction, Sex, and what belongs with them. Negativity takes in every Divinity energized by the negative side. Those two are not isolated subjects. Creation, the Concept, is not one of the things BEGIN swallows. The monster-making sub-concept later asks for both.[8][3]
Being chosen by Negativity does not strip the other side. Creation and Beginning are the examples of that other side still sitting in him. Positivity and Negativity are not enemies. Together they are the balance. Victor grew into both. [[Vaelthar]] is what a forced version of both poles looks like when the growth is not natural.[9]

### The Fragment
[[Amaterasu]] Elderblood wanted more than a subordinate's place. She opened her hand on a sphere of energy. The Power of Creation was in it, and it was not as great as the Heavenly Father's. She called it the essence of her father and her mother, the gods who created her pantheon. The essence of a God of Creation. She offered it as an investment, a bet on a man who had not been born into that power.[2]
A small amount of that essence had already given her unprecedented authority. It could be spent in an emergency: something sturdy enough to defend, or something dangerous enough to annihilate. To gain a Concept of Creation any other way, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] would have had to kill and absorb a God of Creation. The only God then living with those characteristics was the Heavenly Father, and Victor would not harm him.[2]
The fragment did not wait to be finished before it pulled. As the God of Nature, Life, [[Rex Invictus|Home]], Family, and the newly acquired Creation, he wanted to preserve life. Murder, Revenge, Negativity, and Blood pulled the other way. War, Strength, Yandere, and Beauty sat more neutrally. He called himself a God of [[Chaos]]. The pull is not the same thing as a finished Divinity.[10]
Asked whether he wielded Creation, Victor said a fraction, and that it could not even be called a Divinity yet. What he opened his hand on was the essence Amaterasu had given him. The Heavenly Father's answer was that a small spark can cause a great fire, and that the understanding he needed was already his as a Blood Dragon. Watching another God do the work would not hand him that God's path. The advice after that was narrower still: start with the Divinities he identified with most.[7]

### A Mastered Minor
The fragment did not stay a fragment. After the span inside the Inner World, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] held seventeen Divinities: five major and twelve minor. Fifteen were mastered. The majors were War, Destruction, and Blood, mastered; Negativity, still a beginner; Beginning, intermediate. Years of that subjective time had moved Negativity about three percent and Beginnings about twenty. The minor Divinities were the ones that finished.[8]
Creation is named among those mastered minors, with Strength, Beauty, Revenge, Martial Honor, [[Rex Invictus|Home]], Family, Nature, Yandere, Dream, and Madness. The chapter counts twelve minor Divinities. It does not print a twelfth name beside those eleven. Creation is not one of the five majors. Mastery of a major can open a road toward a Primordial, and the requirements differ by Divinity. That sentence is not a statement that Creation had become major.[8]

### The World of Water
[[Amaterasu]] did not expect the fragment of her father's Divinity to go as far as it did. [[Gaia]] set the sight next to the making of a pantheon, the kind of work that asks for all the Primordials, and said it was like seeing the [[Heavenly Father]] again. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was doing it alone.[3]
The Heavenly Father felt <Creation> being used. Victor's authority in the Concept grew until it stopped just below his own. The growth was not gradual. It was an upgrade. A being who had not been around for a million years had reached that height. In front of him was a blue sphere the size of a basketball. He called it a world of water, Victor's first Creation, and wondered whether existence would accept it.[3]
The proclamation was a birth. From the water, they were born, and with water, they brought both life and destruction. The voice of the creator went through the planet: plants, seas, mountains, clouds, the core, the wives, the servants, and the System. What he described next were immense leviathans of the depths. They have the pride of a Dragon, and they are not Dragons. They were born from the essence of the Progenitor of the [[Blood Dragons]], and they carry his will. [[Roxanne Elderblood]] and [[Amara Elderblood]] fed the cost as his energy fell.[3]
Let There Be Life came after, in a speech only Jeanne and the Primordials understood as language. Everyone else still felt the meaning. Time stopped for everyone except the Primordials. The Primordial of Life accepted. The Primordial of Soul accepted, unwillingly. The Judges of the Abyss refused the souls a place in reincarnation, and Life refused with them. The leviathans have no conscience of the kind that would load the cycle. Bound to their creator, they die with him if they die, or if the energy fails.[3]
The System then failed to bind them for lack of a necessary Concept, and made one. <He who creates monsters> was created for the need and bound as a sub-concept of the greater <BEGIN>. The leviathans are beings created by <[[Chaos]]>, in an aquatic dimension. Anyone other than Chaos who wants that Divinity needs four things:[3]
Requirements, other than Chaos
1. The Greater Concept of BEGIN.2. The Concept of Creation.3. Dragon Progenitor of that sector.4. Enough energy to carry the reincarnation cycle and the lives of the creatures.
The Universal Tree's judgment was that only Victor could meet them. The weight of hundreds of those creatures is a Primordial's weight. In energy, he was approaching one. Creation is only the second line of that list. It does not, by itself, make the monsters.[3]

### The Heavenly Father
The Concept is in him. That is why [[Shiva]] does not treat him as a God who can be evaporated inside a sphere of Destruction.[4] His craft, shown to [[Ariella Victoria Elderblood]], is the same office with no wasted energy. A gesture made a swallow, and the swallow landed on his hand. More animals followed until a simple house had become a small garden. He said his strength was not combat. He is a creator. Practice had perfected it. He also said that even her father could not surpass him when it comes to creation.[11]
Ariella did not grant him the comparison. She answered that her father is the best: he made her sisters, [[Akasha Elderblood]], [[Metis]] Elderblood, Melida, the women who help the Empire, and several planets. He conceded rather than make a political problem out of a child defending her father.[11]
[[Ariel Elderblood]] later put a golden sphere in his hand. Inside it was knowledge from [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]], [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]], and [[Aline Elderblood Valefar]] on angel biology and how to bring that biology closer to gods, together with the power of creation her husband had given her for him. That is a gift of usable power and knowledge passed onward. It is not [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] handing the Concept away. The [[Heavenly Father]] read it as an ally being strengthened.[11]
During the World of Water, his authority in the Concept remained the ceiling. Victor's stopped just under it.[3]

### Ariella
At that visit she was already carrying traces of her father's Divinity of Creation. She was also born with all seven Heavenly Virtues as Divine Concepts of their own: Chastity, Charity, Temperance, Diligence, Patience, Benevolence, and Humility. Those Virtues are not this Concept. The traces are.[11]
The birth, told later, is sharper. The pregnancies of Ariel and [[Hela Elderblood]] looked like twins. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] wanted twins. The two souls merged into one, and the power of both grew. The other presence was not a living soul. It was the power of Creation preparing its host, as END did for Helvarya. Ariella was born with the Concept of Creation, and with the potential to also possess Life. The two Concepts are exceptional even among their own kind.[12]
Life under that gift is a potential. It is not a second Divinity already in hand, and it is not the Universal Tree's office of Life. She was not born holding BEGIN.

### The Medium
Attributeless Energy is the blank the bringing-forth is painted on. It is not the Concept. Charlotte Elderblood turned that blank into the properties she wanted. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had been changing energy he already held. He took the blank for the origin of all energy, primordial energy included.[13]
He put the same split in front of his daughters as a white sun with the other energies orbiting it. Divine energy, demonic energy, spirit energy, senjutsu, mana, and ki are one Energy under different names. An ordinary being is limited to energy as reality hands it over. Someone with the control he was describing can skip that step. The white sun is attributeless energy, the origin. A thread drawn from it becomes divine energy, or whatever else is required.[14]
[[Primordial Chaos|The Creator]] of this universe shaped all creation with that blank. It can loosely be called primordial energy, because things were created from it. One has attributes and one does not. For Victor, or for Melida, the difference is trivial. For everyone else it is the whole craft. Attributeless energy creates and brings things forth. Primal energy makes what was brought forth work. Attributeless energy comes first, because everything was brought forth from it. Handling it is access to the source of the making.[14]
Eldritch energy is the attributeless energy of another universe. It does not belong to this reality, and it distorts what it touches according to that other logic. It is not this Concept, and it does not obey this Concept.[14]
Positivity and Negativity sit above that blank. They are the split of the Primordial Chaos who made the energy, kept divided so the universe would stay stable. The Concept of Creation is not either half. It is a Concept under BEGIN, on the positive side of the order of importance.[1][14]

### Later Use
Creating is the hard act. Shaping what already exists is easier. Destroying it is cheap. When the Empire's galaxies had to become one supergalaxy, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] refused the first of those three. He was not making a galaxy out of nothing. He was merging galaxies that were already his. Even for him, a galaxy from nothing was an enormous burden.[15]
[[Aurethys Vaelthion]], Primordial God of Light and Chosen of Positivity, watched that work and recognized attributeless energy. Positivity had inherited the knowledge and the reason of the former self. Negativity had inherited instinct. Positivity had taught the use of that energy to Aurethys, and refused to believe it could be learned. Even Primordials do not know how to use it correctly, except the one who holds that knowledge. Aurethys's conclusion was that Victor had learned it alone.[15]
By then his creator Divinity had been freed of its former restrictions. The text does not say what lifted them, or when. What he did with the freedom was still reorganization. Attributeless energy took the scattered regions because it is compatible with everything, and a master of it makes the work easier than it first appears. He wrapped the finished supergalaxy in his energy. Inside that wrap he knows every corner when he chooses to look. Negativity, as a feminine projection of red energy, called it a first attempt. The precision was not high enough. She is instinct, not a teacher. The key sits with Positivity.[16]

### Powers and Abilities
- Creation: the bringing-forth itself. The Concept under BEGIN. The measure a God of BEGIN is compared against when life is made.[1][2]
- Controlled Creation: a Dragon controls it. From scratch is a different sentence, and it waits on conditions. The control [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] already had was enough to wreck an economy. It was still not creation from nothing.[2]
- Creation Essence: a fragment of a God of Creation can be given. [[Amaterasu]]'s was her parents', the gods who created her pantheon. In his hand it was not yet a Divinity. It could still be spent to defend or to annihilate, and even a small amount carries authority.[2][7]
- Life-ward Pull: before it could be called a Divinity, the newly acquired Creation already pulled toward preserving life, alongside Nature, Life, [[Rex Invictus|Home]], and Family.[10]
- Matter Formation: Creation is the primary resource when a world of living things is spoken into being. Begin boosts it. The leviathans are the result: pride of a Dragon, not Dragons, born from water, carrying life and destruction together.[3]
- Life Creation: joined to BEGIN, both Progenitor lines, and Negativity's access to Souls, the making of life reaches what a God of Creation does. The Concept alone is not that whole list.[2]
- Sub-Concept Support: <He who creates monsters> will not bind to a being who lacks Creation, even if the other conditions are met. The other three are BEGIN, the Dragon Progenitor of that sector, and the energy to carry the lives and the cycle.[3]
- Authority in the Concept: use of it can raise a holder's authority in a jump rather than by slow growth. Victor's rose until it stopped just below the Heavenly Father's.[3]
- Destruction Opposition: the Concept contradicts Destruction. A holder resists a Destruction that would otherwise unmake them. The Heavenly Father is the example. A greater Destruction, Begin, and Positivity are the other counters Victor named. He did not need to call any of them to take Kali's fist or to turn her beam.[4][6]
- Recycling Partner: Destruction leaves matter so this Concept can use it. END leaves nothing. The Primordial of that office would destroy so Creation can occur, beside the Universal Tree.[4][5]
- Conceptual Hosting: the Concept can enter a womb and prepare a host. Ariella is that host. The second presence was the Power, not a sibling. Life remains a potential on top of it.[12]
- Passed Power: a measure of the power of creation can be given onward as a usable gift, with knowledge, without the giver losing the Concept. Ariel carried Victor's gift to the Heavenly Father in that form.[11]
- Reshaping, once unrestricted: with the creator Divinity free of its former restrictions, existing matter can be reorganized through attributeless energy. That is not the same feat as making a galaxy from nothing, which he still treated as the hardest act and did not do.[15][16]

### What Creation Is Not
- Not the universe. "All of creation," the cosmos, and the system [[Primordial Chaos]] made are not this Divinity. Comprehending that cosmos, its duality and its balance, raises proficiency in Begin and Negativity. That gain is not mastery of this Concept.[17]
- Not BEGIN. BEGIN is above it. BEGIN is the start and the perpetuation, and it takes in the life-making categories. Creation is the bringing-forth. A God of BEGIN can imitate a God of Creation only with the other powers added. The monster sub-concept asks for both.[1][2][8][3]
- Not Positivity. Positivity is the half. Creation is the third Concept on that side. Positivity inherited the knowledge of how attributeless energy is used. That knowledge is not this Divinity.[1][15]
- Not Destruction, and not its absence. Destruction is the partner that feeds it. A thing broken by Destruction is material. A thing taken by END is not. Negativity is the energy that drives Destruction. Creation is one of the things that can contradict it.[4][6]
- Not Life, and not the Universal Tree. Ariella has the potential for Life. She was not born holding that office. The Tree's work, and the Primordial of Life's permission, are not this Concept.[3][12]
- Not the Heavenly Virtues. Chastity, Charity, Temperance, Diligence, Patience, Benevolence, and Humility are Divine Concepts Ariella was born with. They sit beside the traces of Creation. They are not it.[11]
- Not Attributeless Energy, and not Primal Energy. The blank brings forth. Primal energy makes the made thing run. The Concept is the bringing-forth those energies serve. Eldritch energy is a blank from another universe.[13][14]
- Not a World Tree. [[World Trees]] counter Destruction because of the mass of Positive and Negative Energy they carry. That is not this Concept sitting inside them.[4]
- Not a permission to make anything, at any time, from nothing. The Dragon limit is still there. The monster sub-concept has four conditions, and Creation is only one of them. A galaxy from nothing stayed the act he would not spend, even after the creator Divinity's former restrictions were gone.[2][3][15][16]
- Not a major Divinity in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s count. It is mastered. It is minor. War, Destruction, and Blood are the mastered majors beside it.[8]
- Not a technique's name. Three Steps to Creation was ice joined to ancient Runes, a continent-breaking art from the time [[Scathach]] was a respected vampire, later driven further. Look! Creation is beautiful is her exclamation over a Big Bang she holds in a chosen space. The words, the ice, the Runes, and the local birth of a universe are not this Divinity.[18][19][20]
- Not Runes. A Rune issues an instruction to reality. Understanding of the cosmos, and compatibility with it, decide how far that instruction goes. A god of language does not become a supreme runemaster by the title. That dependence is not proof of this Divinity.[14]
- Not Akasha, and not every daughter he has made. A being made from his essence, for a purpose, is his creation in the ordinary sense of the word. That is not the same sentence as holding the Concept.

### Limitations
- Control is not creation from scratch. The conditions remain.[2]
- A fragment is not a Divinity. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s was not, on the day he showed it. Mastery came later, and the Divinity stayed minor.[7][8]
- BEGIN does not include it. The monster sub-concept asks for both, and for the Progenitor line and the energy besides.[3]
- END is not a feedstock. There is no matter to bring forth from a deleted thing.[4]
- Another God's method is not a path. The [[Heavenly Father]] refused to hand his own over. The understanding he pointed at was the one a Blood Dragon already has.[7]
- The Heavenly Father's authority in the Concept sat above Victor's at the World of Water, and his own claim at the later visit was that he had not been surpassed in the craft.[3][11]
- Being born with it is not the same as holding Life, or holding BEGIN, or holding the office of a Primordial.[12]
- The souls of the leviathans were refused by Life and by the Judges of the Abyss. They are not fed into reincarnation.[3]
- A Concept does not change when its holder changes sectors. What changes is influence. A normal god at the top of a Concept in a low sector is no longer that top when the same Concept is crowded with rivals. A Primordial is the Concept itself, and if that being vanishes, the Concept vanishes from the universe. The rule of one Primordial to a Concept is the current rule, and the Creator can change it. None of that makes Creation a major Divinity, and none of it makes Victor its Primordial.[14]
- What lifted the former restrictions on his creator Divinity is not said. The freedom did not turn reshaping into creation from nothing.[16]
