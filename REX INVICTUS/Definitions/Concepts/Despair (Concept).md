### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 561
Despair is an ancient Demonic Horse originally belonging to Death, one of the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse. He later became the personal mount of [[Victor Elderblood]] after Victor killed Death and consumed his Soul during his conquest of [[Biblical Hell]].[2]
Despair is not an ordinary infernal beast. He was born within the same Hell associated with Death, a region where Darkness reigned, and spent an unknown period exposed to its Miasma and supernatural environment.[1] Under Death, he appeared as a black Demonic warhorse whose eyes, hooves and tail burned with eerie greenish flames. After falling under Victor's authority, Despair gradually became larger, healthier and more muscular before undergoing a far greater Evolution following the restructuring of Hell.[2][1]
His Evolution in Chapter 858 was triggered largely by his refusal to allow Victor to replace him with another mount. Returning to the Hell in which he had originally been born, Despair absorbed its abundant Miasma and Pure Darkness, transformed his flames from green to black and finally to neon violet, greatly enlarged his body, and manifested two enormous wings composed entirely of Pure Darkness.[1]
Despair remains Victor's personal mount and one of the few companions inherited directly from an enemy whom Victor personally devoured.

### Physical Appearance

#### Original Form
Despair originally appeared as a large black Demonic Horse with an ominous and distinctly supernatural appearance.
His body was dark, while his eyes, hooves and tail burned with green-tinted flames. When Death required his mount, Despair could appear from Death's shadow, immediately forming beneath his master as a fully manifested Demonic warhorse.[3]
Even in this state, Despair did not resemble a normal horse. His burning eyes and hooves, Demonic presence, and connection to one of the Horsemen made him visibly infernal.
After [[Victor Elderblood]] acquired him, Despair's body gradually changed. He became noticeably more muscular, larger and healthier while existing beneath Victor's authority.[2]

#### Evolved Form
Despair's current form was achieved after he returned to the Hell where he had originally been born.[1]
As the Miasma of the Realm surrounded him, his previous green flames first became deep black. His physical body rapidly increased in size while masses of Pure Darkness and shadow merged directly into his form.[1]
His eyes subsequently began glowing a deep violet and his black flames transformed again, becoming a brilliant neon violet.[1]
The most dramatic change was the appearance of two enormous wings composed of Pure Darkness. These wings allow Despair to fly and leave trails of neon-violet, black and crimson Energy across the sky.[1]
His neigh also changed following the Evolution. Rather than sounding like an ordinary horse, his voice became sufficiently powerful and bestial to resemble the roar of a supernatural monster.[1]

### Personality
Despair is extraordinarily loyal, proud and possessive of his position as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s mount.
Although he cannot communicate through ordinary spoken language, his behavior clearly demonstrates intelligence and an ability to understand conversations occurring around him.
The strongest example occurred when Victor discussed obtaining new infernal beasts that could serve as mounts for himself and his armies. Despair immediately reacted to the implication that Victor might acquire a replacement.[1]
The possibility was completely unacceptable to him.
Despair emerged before Victor and insisted through his behavior that the only mount Victor required was him. When Victor recognized what he wanted and opened a portal into Hell, Despair entered without hesitation and began deliberately seeking the power necessary to improve himself.[1]
His Evolution was therefore not an accidental biological process. It occurred in direct response to his determination to remain useful to Victor and preserve his position as his Master's personal mount.[1]
Despair also displays absolute confidence when carrying Victor. During Victor's conquest of Hell, he calmly transported his new Master through hostile cities and Demon territories while Victor's rapidly growing army marched behind them.[4]
Unlike many Demons who submitted to Victor because of fear, ambition or political advantage, Despair's relationship with him appears far more personal and instinctive. Once Victor became his Master, the Demonic Horse accepted that position completely.

### History

#### Under the Horseman of Death
Despair was originally born within the same Hell associated with the [[Horseman of Death]]. Chapter 858 describes this homeland as a Hell in which Darkness reigned and identifies it as the place from which both Death and Despair originated.[1]
By the time of the Demonic War, Despair already served as Death's personal warhorse.
He first appeared during the conflict involving the Four Horsemen, the Vatican and Heaven. When Death and War decided to withdraw and report developments to [[Diablo]], Death summoned his mount from the shadows.[3]
A black horse emerged with its eyes, hooves and tail burning in greenish flames. Death mounted Despair and departed alongside War.[3]
The scene established Despair not as a random Hell Beast but as the personal mount used by one of the Four Horsemen themselves.

#### Demonic War
Despair's ownership changed dramatically when [[Victor Elderblood]] was trapped inside [[Biblical Hell]] and began slaughtering [[Diablo]]'s forces.
Victor eventually confronted the Horsemen directly. During the battle against Death, Pestilence, Famine and War, Victor killed and consumed several of the Horsemen.[5][2]
Death was among those whose Soul Victor completely consumed.[2]
As a consequence, Death's personal Demonic Horse came under Victor's authority. Despair accepted Victor as his new Master rather than disappearing alongside his former owner.[2]
Victor's influence immediately began affecting him. Despair appeared physically healthier and more muscular after entering Victor's service, suggesting that being attached to Victor or existing beneath his authority benefited the Demonic Beast's development.[2]
Despair subsequently became Victor's primary mount during the remainder of his conquest.
After War's defeat, Victor advanced deeper into Biblical Hell while riding Despair. Demon cities fell one after another as Victor's surrendered Legions followed behind him, turning the image of the Vampire Progenitor riding Death's former horse into part of his growing reputation as Alucard the Conqueror.[4]
Despair carried Victor through the middle levels of Hell toward the Demon King's capital. His burning hooves left flaming footprints across the ground as the expanding Demonic army followed behind them.[4]
The symbolism was particularly striking: one of the mounts formerly associated with the Four Horsemen now carried the man systematically dismantling Diablo's rule.
Victor eventually reached the Demon King's Castle, claimed the throne of Hell and became its new sovereign, while Despair remained at his side throughout the campaign.[6]

#### King Among Kings
Following the Demonic War, Despair remained [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s personal mount.
As Victor's territories and family expanded, Despair was increasingly treated less like captured property and more like one of Victor's personal supernatural companions.
His greatest development occurred following Victor's restructuring of the Hells and the creation of the larger supernatural system surrounding Dragons Nest.
Victor began considering the infernal wildlife of his newly controlled Hells. Demonic Beasts naturally grew by feeding upon Miasma, and Victor considered cultivating the strongest of them into useful animals and military mounts for his forces.[1]
The suggestion immediately attracted Despair's attention.
A neigh echoed through the area and the black warhorse emerged from the ground before Victor. Victor understood his reaction almost immediately. Despair had no intention of allowing another creature to become his Master's principal mount.[1]
Victor accepted his insistence and opened a portal leading into Hell.
Despair immediately entered.
The moment he arrived, he began galloping toward the horizon while the enormous quantities of Miasma surrounding the Realm converged upon him.[1]
His green flames turned completely black.
His body expanded.
Pure shadows began fusing into his physical form.[1]
The transformation was connected to Despair's origin. He had originally been born in the same Hell as Death—the Realm dominated by Darkness—and returning home allowed him to access the conditions necessary for a genuine Evolution.[1]
Despair's desire to remain Victor's only personal mount provided the motivation driving the process.
His eyes became deep violet, followed by his black flames changing into bright neon violet. His neigh became a beast-like roar as the Demonic Horse completed another stage of development.[1]
Finally, shadows extended from his body and formed two enormous wings composed entirely of Pure Darkness. Despair took flight, painting the sky in neon violet, black and faint crimson as his new Power spread around him.[1]
The event transformed Despair from Death's former infernal warhorse into a significantly more powerful Demonic Beast directly adapted to Victor's new supernatural order.

### Skills & Abilities
Demonic Horse Physiology: Despair is an ancient Demonic Beast in the form of a warhorse. His physiology has been shaped by prolonged exposure to Hell, Miasma and the Darkness-dominated Realm from which he originated.[3][1]

#### Supernatural Physical Characteristics
- Supernatural Strength: Despair possesses the physical strength necessary to serve as a combat mount for existences such as Death and later [[Victor Elderblood]].
- Supernatural Speed: Despair can travel rapidly through the enormous territories of Hell while carrying Victor. During Victor's conquest he repeatedly transported him between Demonic cities and toward the Demon King's capital.[4]
- Enhanced Stamina: As a Demonic warhorse, Despair can sustain long-distance supernatural travel without displaying ordinary biological exhaustion.
- Enhanced Durability: His body is adapted to the extreme environment of Hell and has become increasingly powerful through Miasma exposure and later Evolution.[1]

#### Demonic Flames
Despair naturally manifests supernatural flames from his:
- Eyes.
- Hooves.
- Tail.[3]
Originally these flames possessed a green tint.
During his Evolution, the flames became black before finally transforming into neon violet.[1]
The flames visibly remain active while Despair moves, allowing his hooves to leave burning tracks behind him.[4]
The novel has not yet shown Despair projecting these flames offensively at range, so their confirmed use should remain limited to their demonstrated manifestation around his body.

#### Miasma Absorption
Despair can absorb and benefit directly from the Miasma of Hell.
When he returned to his home Realm, enormous amounts of ambient Miasma began circling around him and entering the process of his Evolution.[1]
The event demonstrates that Miasma functions not merely as an environmental condition for Despair but as a source capable of strengthening and altering his Demonic physiology.

#### Darkness Assimilation
Despair possesses an intrinsic affinity with Darkness because he was born in a Hell where Darkness reigned.[1]
During his Evolution, Pure Shadows physically merged with his body, increasing his size and transforming his supernatural flames.[1]
This culminated in the creation of wings composed entirely of Pure Darkness.

#### Reactive Evolution
Despair possesses the capacity to Evolve when exposed to appropriate environmental conditions and sufficiently strong motivation.
His Chapter 858 transformation occurred in direct response to his desire to remain worthy of being [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s mount.[1]
The process required him to return to the Hell in which he had been born, where the abundant Miasma and Darkness provided the resources necessary for the Evolution.[1]
The transformation produced:
- Increased body size.
- Greater musculature.
- Assimilation of Pure Shadows.
- Black flames.
- Neon-violet flames.
- Deep-violet eyes.
- Pure Darkness wings.
- Flight.[1]

#### Flight
After his Evolution, Despair gained two enormous wings constructed from Pure Darkness and became capable of true aerial flight.[1]
His first flight produced a trail of neon-violet fire, Pure Darkness and crimson shades across the sky.[1]

#### Environmental Empowerment
Despair is significantly empowered by returning to the Hell in which he originated.
Although he had already lived for an immense period and served two exceptionally powerful Masters, he required his original Darkness-based Hell to trigger the Evolution shown in Chapter 858.[1]
This suggests a particularly deep compatibility between Despair's physiology and the Miasma, Darkness and environmental laws of his birthplace.

#### Intelligence
Despair possesses intelligence considerably beyond that of an ordinary animal.
He can:
- Understand [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s conversations.
- Recognize the implication that Victor was considering acquiring another mount.
- Express disagreement through his behavior.
- Deliberately seek a method of becoming stronger.
- Understand Victor's instructions.
- Independently travel to the location necessary for his Evolution.[1]
His behavior demonstrates self-awareness, pride and a clear understanding of his relationship with Victor.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Despair's current Master.
Their relationship began when Victor killed and consumed Death. Because Death's Soul and authority were absorbed, Despair subsequently came beneath Victor's control.[2]
Unlike many trophies Victor obtained by defeating his enemies, Despair developed into a genuine companion.
Victor used him extensively during the conquest of Hell and rode him while advancing through Demon cities toward [[Diablo]]'s throne.[4]
Despair's loyalty eventually became strong enough to influence his own Evolution.
When Victor began discussing the possibility of breeding powerful Hell Beasts as mounts, Despair immediately objected to the idea of his Master acquiring another principal mount. He deliberately returned to his birthplace and Evolved specifically because he believed Victor needed no mount other than him.[1]
Victor understood Despair's intention immediately and willingly provided the portal required for him to return home.[1]
The scene demonstrates that their modern relationship extends beyond simple magical ownership.

#### Horseman of Death
Death was Despair's original Master.
Despair originated from the same Darkness-dominated Hell as Death and served as his personal Demonic Horse for an unknown length of time.[1]
Death could summon Despair directly from his shadow, as demonstrated during the events of Chapter 561.[3]
Their relationship ended when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] killed Death and consumed his Soul in Chapter 664.[2]
Despair subsequently accepted Victor as his new Master.
