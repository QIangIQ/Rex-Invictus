Law, registered within the System under the designation <Law>, is a mysterious high-ranking Authority in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]]. Originally occupying Rank 10 in the System's Authority Ranking, Law belongs among the exceptionally advanced Beings recognized for their understanding of Creation. The designation appears immediately beneath <Emperor>, <Darkness> and <Light> in the hierarchy revealed during [[Victor Elderblood]]'s investigation of the greater Cosmos. Unlike those of several other high-ranking Authorities, Law's identity, physical appearance and individual supernatural abilities have not been revealed.[1]
Law's position became particularly significant when Victor's developing understanding of Reality elevated his own System designation, <[[Chaos]]>, to the tenth rank. During Chapter 1113, both Law and Chaos occupied Rank 10 simultaneously, demonstrating that the System could recognize multiple existences at an equivalent level of Authority. However, the ranking measures a Being's understanding and authority over Creation rather than functioning as a direct comparison of destructive power, meaning that sharing the same numerical position does not establish identical supernatural abilities or combat strength.[2]
During Victor's preparations for contact with the Upper Sectors, Law became one of four exceptionally important non-Administrator Authorities considered potential rulers of the region's dominant factions. Velnorah proposed that Light, Darkness, Emperor and Law might correspond to the four principal powers governing the Upper Sectors. However, [[Scathach]] Elderblood cautioned against treating the interpretation as definitive because the identities, relationships and complete political structures of those factions had not been verified.[3]
A subsequent revelation from the Universal Tree introduced an important distinction within the political hierarchy of the Upper Sectors. During a conversation with Victor, the Primordial of Life explained that the Primordial of Balance maintained a Herald who commanded one of the strongest factions in the Higher Sectors. Unlike the other major powers, this organization existed to execute the instructions of its Primordial superior and maintain the broader equilibrium of the Cosmos. Victor consequently recognized that the four major factions did not necessarily represent four conventional rival civilizations. Instead, three operated as independent competing powers, while the fourth fulfilled a mediating or regulatory function. The Universal Tree confirmed the interpretation and added that the fourth faction was comparatively inactive, with the other faction leaders generally avoiding conflicts against it.[4]
This information makes Law a plausible candidate for the unidentified Herald of Balance. Among the four originally recorded high-ranking non-Administrator Authorities, Law's designation possesses an obvious thematic relationship with the enforcement of rules, regulations and the preservation of cosmic order. Light and Darkness are associated with identifiable expansionist civilizations, while Emperor and Law remained comparatively mysterious when the four-faction hypothesis was proposed. The possibility that Law represents the Herald commanding Limbo's mediating faction would therefore provide a coherent explanation for the unusual position occupied by the fourth major power.[1][3][4]
However, this identification remains a theory rather than confirmed canon. The Universal Tree identifies the existence and responsibilities of Limbo's Herald but does not disclose the Herald's name, System designation or personal Divinity. The conversation also does not identify which of the four previously recorded Authorities commands the mediating faction. Although Law is a particularly plausible candidate, the available evidence does not eliminate Emperor or an as-yet-unidentified individual as alternative possibilities. Consequently, Law's relationship with the Primordial of Balance, leadership of the fourth faction and potential role in enforcing the laws of Reality must remain unconfirmed until explicitly established by the narrative.[3][4]

### Physical Appearance
Law's physical appearance has not been revealed. The Being is known primarily through the designation recorded within the System's Authority Ranking and references made during discussions concerning the powerful existences occupying the Upper Sectors. No confirmed description establishes Law's height, facial features, hair, eyes, clothing, bodily constitution or potential alternate manifestations.[1][3]
[[The Universal Tree]]'s explanation concerning Limbo's Herald does not provide additional physical information. The Herald is discussed through the responsibilities of the faction under their command rather than through an individual description, and the narrative does not explicitly identify that figure as Law. Consequently, neither the Herald's unknown appearance nor the manifestation of the Primordial of Balance can be assigned to Law.[4]
Law's gender, race and species also remain unknown. Although the Being possesses an exceptionally high Authority Ranking, this alone does not establish whether Law is a conventional God, a Primordial God, an Overlord or another form of supernatural existence.

### Personality
Law's individual personality remains largely unknown because the Being has not been directly depicted speaking, expressing opinions or interacting with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and the other identified characters. Their temperament, personal ambitions, moral outlook and attitude toward the [[Elderblood Empire]] have not been established through an individual scene.[3]
If Law is ultimately confirmed as the Herald of Balance, their responsibilities would involve serving the Primordial associated with Limbo and overseeing a faction dedicated to maintaining equilibrium rather than pursuing unrestricted territorial conquest. Such a position would require considerable political authority and responsibility, particularly because the Universal Tree describes the organization as one of the strongest powers in the Higher Sectors and notes that the competing faction leaders generally avoid confronting it. However, these characteristics presently belong to the unidentified Herald's institutional role and cannot be treated as demonstrated elements of Law's personal temperament.[4]
The possible association with Balance also does not establish that Law is benevolent, morally neutral, impartial in every circumstance or unwilling to employ violence. The Primordial of Balance possesses responsibilities concerning the stability of the Cosmos, but the individual methods through which its Herald executes instructions have not been described. It remains unknown whether the Herald's organization relies primarily upon diplomacy, supernatural enforcement, military intervention or a combination of these methods.[4]
Similarly, the incomplete rumors concerning Law obtained during Victor's investigation of foreign Divine interference do not provide a reliable basis for evaluating the Being's intentions. The supernatural restrictions protecting the information prevented the foreign fragment from revealing the complete circumstances behind its statements.[5]

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Law was first identified when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] examined the System's Authority Ranking during a discussion with Azathoth concerning Creation, the original [[Primordial Entities]] and the vacant supreme throne governing Reality. Their conversation addressed the fundamental responsibilities of the Primordial Beings and the consequences of destroying existences responsible for maintaining essential cosmic functions. The destruction of such a Being could threaten the stability of the Universe itself, prompting Victor to consider whether separating an individual's consciousness from its cosmic responsibility would be more practical than eliminating the underlying function.[1]
The System displayed a hierarchy of exceptionally advanced Authorities, including the original Primordial representatives responsible for fundamental aspects of Creation. Beneath these original authorities appeared four additional designations: <Light> at Rank 7, <Darkness> at Rank 8, <Emperor> at Rank 9 and <Law> at Rank 10. Their positions established that the System recognized them as possessing extraordinary understanding of Reality, although the list did not reveal their individual identities, supernatural abilities or political allegiances.[1]
Victor's own understanding of Reality continued advancing as he investigated the structure of the Cosmos and developed his powers beyond the limitations normally associated with a Being originating from the Middle Sectors. His designation, <[[Chaos]]>, eventually reached the tenth position in the System's hierarchy, sharing the rank with Law during Chapter 1113.[2]
This development occurred during Victor's experiments concerning movement between different Sectors. When he attempted to send a weakened clone into a Lower Sector, the System warned that a high-level entity designated <Chaos> was attempting to enter territory where Beings of his Authority were restricted. The limitation remained applicable despite the clone possessing only a fraction of Victor's power, demonstrating that the System's classification concerned the authority and understanding associated with his existence rather than exclusively his immediate physical strength.[2]
The incident established an important distinction between high-ranking Authorities and the original Primordial Administrators. Although a Being such as Victor could achieve an extraordinary ranking, this did not automatically grant the administrative privileges or complete freedom possessed by those whose cosmic responsibilities were directly integrated into the System. Law's placement within the same hierarchy consequently demonstrated considerable recognized Authority without proving that the Being possessed unrestricted access to the System or direct control over its governing mechanisms.[1][2]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Law became increasingly relevant to the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s strategic considerations after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] encountered evidence of civilizations possessing technological and supernatural capabilities far beyond those familiar to his native Sector. The destruction of a reconnaissance clone by the Empire of Light demonstrated that the Upper Sectors contained advanced military powers capable of deploying weapons on astronomical scales. The encounter prompted Victor and his family to investigate the political structure of the Higher Sectors and the identities of the greatest powers occupying them.[3]
During these discussions, Velnorah examined the System's Authority Ranking and identified Light, Darkness, Emperor and Law as four exceptionally important non-Administrator entities. She proposed that their positions might correspond to the rulers of the four dominant factions occupying the Upper Sectors. The interpretation was based upon the exceptional status of the four Beings and the evidence concerning civilizations already encountered by Victor's faction.[3]
The Empire of Light supplied a relatively direct connection between the System designation <Light> and an identifiable civilization. Information concerning the supernatural forces associated with the rescue of [[Niklaus Horseman]] also suggested the existence of a separate faction connected to Darkness. However, comparatively little was known about the political structures associated with Emperor and Law, whose identities remained concealed despite their proximity to the other Authorities within the ranking.[3]
Velnorah also considered the possibility that the advanced military forces previously encountered by her own civilization belonged to one of these unknown factions. The soldiers possessed sophisticated standardized equipment and military infrastructure, but the surviving information did not definitively identify the supreme authority commanding them. Emperor and Law consequently remained possible associations rather than confirmed rulers of the civilization involved.[3]
[[Scathach]] challenged the assumption that four exceptionally high-ranking System designations necessarily corresponded to four conventional empires. The relationship between the System's Authority Ranking and the political hierarchy of the Upper Sectors had not been established, while additional powerful civilizations could exist outside the four names already identified. The distinction became increasingly important as Victor continued acquiring information about the greater Cosmos.[3]
Law's name reappeared during Victor's investigation of supernatural corruption originating from the Upper Sectors. Through the compromised existence of [[Nikolai Francis Nichev]], the Emperor obtained access to a fragment of a foreign God's consciousness and began extracting information concerning the ambitions of powerful outside factions. The fragment revealed that several major authorities possessed an interest in extraordinary supernatural powers associated with Victor's existence and the vacant supreme throne of Reality.[5]
During the interrogation, the fragment attempted to disclose protected information involving Darkness, Emperor and Law. An exceptionally powerful Soul Contract immediately activated, destroying the captive before the information could be revealed. Victor subsequently reconstructed the prisoner's existence through his supernatural abilities and continued the investigation, eventually obtaining considerable information concerning the civilization governed by Darkness. However, the incomplete statement involving Law did not establish the Being's actual intentions, supernatural powers or possible relationship with the other authorities.[5]
A much more significant revelation occurred during Victor's conversation with the Universal Tree concerning the structure and responsibilities of the original Primordial Beings. While observing Victor's preparations to incorporate [[Nightingale]], Samar and Arcane into his existing Sector, the Universal Tree explained the relationship between Life, Death, the Judges of the Abyss, the [[Akashic Records]] and the broader cycle responsible for maintaining the Cosmos. Life originated through the Universal Tree, the Judges supervised the spiritual information entering the Akashic structure, Death governed the conclusion and decay of existences, and the resulting Energy eventually returned to the cycle through which new Life emerged.[4]
The Universal Tree subsequently discussed the other Primordial responsibilities. Infinity sustained the expansion of the Universe, while the Being associated with Limbo and Balance monitored the conduct of sentient existences, preventing powerful individuals or civilizations from destabilizing the greater cosmic order. The Primordial of Balance therefore occupied a regulatory position concerned with maintaining equilibrium rather than performing the same functions as Life, Death or the Judges of the Abyss.[4]
During this explanation, the Universal Tree revealed that the Primordial of Balance possessed a Herald who led one of the strongest factions in the Higher Sectors. The faction's members existed to carry out the instructions of their Primordial superior, making their political and military responsibilities fundamentally different from those of civilizations primarily seeking territorial expansion or supremacy over neighboring powers.[4]
Victor immediately recognized the significance of this information. Rather than four entirely independent powers competing for dominance, the Upper Sectors effectively possessed three major competing factions and a fourth organization responsible for mediation or enforcement. The Universal Tree confirmed this interpretation, adding that the fourth faction was comparatively inactive and that the other leaders generally avoided conflict with it. The information was sufficiently sensitive that the Universal Tree indicated only the faction leaders normally possessed knowledge of the arrangement.[4]
The revelation provided a possible explanation for the otherwise mysterious fourth Authority originally identified during Victor's investigation of the System Ranking. Since Light and Darkness had already been associated with identifiable civilizations, and Emperor and Law remained insufficiently understood, the existence of an independent regulatory organization raised the possibility that one of those two designations belonged to the Herald of Balance. Law is an especially plausible candidate because its designation suggests an association with the principles governing order, rules and enforcement. If the identification proves correct, the Being registered as <Law> would not merely represent another competing Upper-Sector ruler, but an authority operating under the direction of Limbo's Primordial.[1][3][4]
However, the Universal Tree did not explicitly identify the Herald as Law. The conversation confirmed the existence of the Herald and the regulatory function of the fourth faction without establishing a direct connection to any particular System designation. It therefore remains possible that Emperor holds the position, or that the Herald is another powerful Being not directly identified by the four names previously considered by Velnorah. Until further information becomes available, the possible association between Law and the Primordial of Balance remains an interpretation supported by the political structure described in Chapter 1181 rather than a confirmed revelation.[4]
The Universal Tree's explanation also caused Victor to develop a broader understanding of the possible purpose behind the original Primordial hierarchy. He reasoned that the fundamental cosmic offices formed a coordinated structure through which external threats could be detected and internal instability controlled. The Universal Tree and its countless [[World Trees]] monitored Life throughout the Universe, the Judges supervised spiritual information, Death governed the conclusion of existences, Infinity maintained the expanding frontier and Limbo's Primordial prevented internal disturbances from undermining the stability of the greater system.[4]
Within this interpretation, the Herald of Balance and the faction under their command would provide a practical means of enforcing the Primordial's objectives throughout the Higher Sectors without requiring direct intervention in every dispute. Such a role could explain why the competing faction leaders avoided confronting the fourth organization, but the exact structure, membership and military capabilities of this enforcement faction remain undisclosed.[4]

### Skills & Abilities
Law occupies an exceptionally high position within the System's Authority Ranking, establishing that the Being possesses extraordinary recognition within the hierarchy governing the understanding of Creation. However, Law's individual supernatural abilities have not been directly demonstrated. The possible identification as the Herald of Balance provides a plausible explanation for the Being's political significance but does not independently establish the powers available to Law.[1][4]

#### Confirmed Characteristics
- High-Ranking System Authority: Law originally occupied Rank 10 in the System's Authority Ranking, placing the Being immediately beneath Emperor, Darkness and Light among the highest recorded non-Administrator Authorities.[1]
- Advanced Understanding of Creation: The System's ranking recognizes extraordinary understanding and authority over Reality rather than merely measuring physical strength or destructive power.[1][2]
- Shared Authority Rank: Law shared Rank 10 with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s designation <[[Chaos]]> during Chapter 1113. Their placement at the same rank does not establish identical Divinities, supernatural abilities or combat strength.[2]
- Possible High-Level Factional Authority (Unconfirmed): Law was considered one of four significant non-Administrator Authorities who might represent the rulers of major Upper-Sector factions. The exact political affiliation of Law remains unidentified.[3]

#### Possible Herald of Balance
[[The Universal Tree]]'s revelation provides the strongest available basis for a theory concerning Law's actual identity. The existence of a Herald commanding the fourth major faction is confirmed, as is that organization's responsibility for executing the instructions of the Primordial associated with Limbo and Balance. However, the narrative does not identify the Herald by name or disclose their individual supernatural powers.[4]
If Law is ultimately confirmed to be the Herald, the position would establish a direct relationship with a primordial cosmic regulator and responsibility for commanding an exceptionally powerful organization devoted to maintaining equilibrium. This could potentially involve the enforcement of cosmic regulations and intervention against civilizations threatening universal stability. Nevertheless, the specific techniques, mechanisms and limitations associated with such responsibilities have not been demonstrated, and none should be assigned to Law as confirmed personal abilities.[4]

#### Unconfirmed Divine Abilities
Law's System designation suggests a possible association with the Concept of Law, but the exact nature of that association has not been revealed. No directly depicted scene demonstrates unrestricted Reality Manipulation, the alteration of universal laws, the cancellation of other Gods' Divinities or the ability to impose binding cosmic regulations through individual supernatural commands.
Similarly, the authority exercised by the Primordial of Balance cannot automatically be inherited by its Herald. Even if Law is eventually identified as the Herald, the Primordial's responsibility for maintaining equilibrium would not establish that Law independently possesses the same fundamental powers or unrestricted control over the Cosmos.[4]
Law has not been shown creating or destroying celestial bodies, manipulating Souls, modifying the System, generating supernatural weapons or engaging an opponent in direct combat. The extraordinary Soul Contract encountered during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s interrogation of a foreign Divine fragment also cannot be attributed to Law, because the source of that restriction remains unidentified.[5]
The Being's individual strength, speed, durability, stamina, regeneration, resistance to supernatural techniques and effective combat range consequently remain unknown.

### Power
Law occupies an exceptionally significant position within the System's hierarchy of Authorities. Originally recorded at Rank 10, the Being was placed beneath the original Primordial representatives and three other high-ranking designations: Light, Darkness and Emperor. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s designation <[[Chaos]]> subsequently shared the tenth rank with Law during Chapter 1113, demonstrating that the hierarchy could accommodate multiple Beings with equivalent recorded levels of Authority.[1][2]
The numerical ranking represents the System's recognition of a Being's understanding and Authority over Creation rather than an unconditional measurement of destructive strength. Consequently, Law's placement establishes extraordinary conceptual standing but does not independently demonstrate that the Being possesses greater attack potency, physical strength or durability than every lower-ranked entity.[2]
The political interpretation concerning Law's power became more significant after the Universal Tree revealed the existence of the Herald of Balance. According to the Primordial of Life, the Herald commands one of the strongest factions occupying the Higher Sectors, while the other faction leaders generally avoid confronting that organization. Its exceptional standing appears connected to its responsibilities under Limbo's Primordial rather than merely the accumulation of territory or ordinary military expansion.[4]
Should Law be confirmed as the Herald, this position would provide a possible explanation for the Being's extraordinary Authority Ranking and influence among the major Upper-Sector powers. The role would involve command over a civilization or institution whose existence is connected to the regulation of cosmic stability. However, these organizational characteristics do not establish the Herald's personal maximum destructive strength, nor do they demonstrate that Law can independently reproduce the authority of the Primordial of Balance.[4]
Law's precise combat capabilities, current numerical ranking, maximum conceptual authority and relationship with the original Primordial hierarchy therefore remain incompletely established.

### Relationships

#### The Owner of Limbo
The Owner of Limbo is the Primordial associated with Balance and the regulation of internal cosmic stability. According to the Universal Tree, this Being monitors sentient existences to prevent individuals or civilizations from disrupting the balance of the Universe. Unlike the original Primordial functions associated with Life, Death and Infinity, Balance is concerned with maintaining equilibrium among the Beings inhabiting the Cosmos.[4]
The Universal Tree also confirms that the Primordial of Balance possesses a Herald who commands one of the strongest factions in the Upper Sectors. This organization exists to execute the Primordial's instructions and serves as a mediating or regulatory power between the other major factions, which generally avoid conflicts with it.[4]
Law is a possible candidate for this Herald, particularly because the System designation suggests an association with order or the enforcement of rules. Among the four high-ranking non-Administrator names previously considered possible Upper-Sector faction leaders, Law is one of the two whose political affiliation remains comparatively obscure. The discovery of a fourth faction possessing a distinct regulatory function therefore provides a plausible explanation for Law's potential role in the greater cosmic hierarchy.[1][3][4]
Nevertheless, no direct interaction between Law and the Owner of Limbo has been depicted, and the Universal Tree does not explicitly identify Law as the Primordial's Herald. Their relationship must therefore remain unconfirmed.

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is the Dragon Emperor and the Being registered within the System as <[[Chaos]]>. Law became relevant to Victor when the Emperor examined the Authority Ranking and discovered the exceptionally powerful non-Administrator entities occupying its highest positions. Victor subsequently reached Rank 10 and temporarily shared that position with Law, placing the two within the same numerical category of System Authority.[1][2]
Their relationship remains indirect, as Victor has not personally encountered Law. However, his conversation with the Universal Tree introduced the possibility that one of the mysterious high-ranking Authorities could occupy a position directly subordinate to a Primordial regulator. If Law is ultimately identified as the Herald of Balance, the two would possess an important institutional connection through Victor's unusual relationship with the Owner of Limbo and the broader cosmic regulations governing powerful existences.[4]
No confirmed evidence establishes that Law personally considers Victor an enemy, ally or potential subordinate. Their shared Authority Ranking and possible positions within the Upper Sectors do not independently demonstrate a personal rivalry.

#### Light, Darkness and Emperor
<Light>, <Darkness> and <Emperor> are three exceptionally high-ranking Authorities listed immediately above Law in the original System ranking. Their positions place them among the most significant non-Administrator existences recognized during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s examination of the greater cosmic hierarchy.[1]
During Velnorah's analysis of the Upper Sectors, these three names and Law were considered possible designations for the rulers of four major factions. The Empire of Light supplied a recognizable civilization associated with Light, while information concerning foreign supernatural powers suggested a separate faction connected to Darkness. Emperor and Law remained comparatively mysterious because their identities and political affiliations had not been definitively established.[3]
[[The Universal Tree]] subsequently clarified that one of the four major Upper-Sector organizations serves the Primordial of Balance and acts as a mediator rather than as another conventional competing faction. This creates a possible distinction between Law and the other recorded Authorities, although the conversation does not identify which of the four designations belongs to the Herald. The possibility that Law commands this organization remains an inference rather than an established relationship with Light, Darkness or Emperor.[4]
The proximity of the four names within the ranking does not prove that they possess equivalent personal power, share a common origin or maintain permanent alliances.

#### The Universal Tree
[[The Universal Tree]] is the Primordial associated with Life and the continuation of the universal cycle of reincarnation. During his conversation with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], he explains how the original Primordial functions cooperate to maintain the Cosmos and identifies the Herald of Balance as the leader of one of the Higher Sectors' strongest factions.[4]
The information reveals the regulatory purpose of the fourth major faction and provides the principal basis for the theory identifying Law as its possible leader. However, the Universal Tree does not personally mention Law by name during the explanation, and no confirmed relationship between the two Beings has been demonstrated.

#### The System
The System is the cosmic mechanism through which Law's designation and Authority Ranking are known. It records exceptionally advanced existences according to their understanding and authority over Creation while distinguishing the original Primordial Administrators from other high-ranking Beings.[1][2]
Law's presence within the System ranking establishes recognition by that cosmic structure but does not independently demonstrate direct administrative control over its operations. No confirmed interaction has shown Law modifying System regulations, issuing commands to its administrators or exercising the privileges belonging to the original Primordial Beings.

### Current Status
Law's present condition remains unknown. The Being has not received a confirmed physical introduction, and the examined chapters do not establish their location, individual activities or direct participation in the developing conflict between the [[Elderblood Empire]] and the Upper-Sector factions.
[[The Universal Tree]]'s explanation establishes that the fourth major Upper-Sector faction serves the Primordial of Balance and is commanded by an unidentified Herald. Its regulatory responsibilities and extraordinary political standing distinguish it from the three conventional competing powers, while its comparatively limited activity suggests that the organization does not ordinarily participate in the same territorial struggles as the other factions.[4]
Law remains a plausible but unconfirmed candidate for the Herald of Balance. The theory is supported by the correspondence between the four principal faction positions and the earlier Authority Ranking, together with Law's designation suggesting an association with order and regulation. However, the Herald's identity has not been explicitly established, and no new individual abilities or relationships can be confirmed for Law solely through the description of that office.[1][3][4]
