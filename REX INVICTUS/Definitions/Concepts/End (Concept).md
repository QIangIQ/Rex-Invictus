END is the Concept of final erasure in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]]. It is not a stronger form of Destruction. Destruction breaks a thing and leaves the matter so it can be used again. END deletes the record. The person, the place, the stretch of space, and the fact that any of it was ever there, go with it.[1]
On the negative side the order of importance is Negativity, then END, then Destruction. On the positive side it is Positivity, then BEGIN, then the Concept of Creation. END has one rival. That rival is BEGIN, not Creation.[2][3][4]
The power originates from True Death. Death, END, and part of Time draw from that Primordial. The Primordials themselves come from the divided Positivity and Negativity of [[Primordial Chaos]]. Both statements stand. END sits under Negativity in the order of importance, and it draws its power from True Death.[2][3]
Not the same word
END is this Concept. Destruction recycles. The Concept of Creation is the positive-side Divinity under BEGIN, the bringing-forth, and it is not the rival of END. When the novel says destroyed matter is left so creation can use it, that sentence means the reuse of matter, not that Divinity. Creation as the Universe, the System, or "all of existence" is a third use of the word and is not this page. Death is the Primordial office and also a separate Divinity. The Throne of the End, [[Xytherion]], is an office of [[Vharzath]], not the Concept. Abyss creatures in a galaxy that has already reached its end are not Gods of END.

### What It Does
A being born with END does not merely kill. [[Kassandra Kashvi Elderblood]] was born as Destruction. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was relieved. If she had been born with END, she would not erase someone and leave the pieces. She would delete that person from existence forever, as if they had never been there. Destruction does not take a person out of time, out of space, and out of the [[Akashic Records]]. It recycles the matter so that matter can be used again. END is true conceptual erasure. The record of the existence ceases.[1]
The first time the Concept is shown in a fight, Hassan names it over the trail [[Fenrir]] leaves. A single roar erases everything in the path. What was there is deleted. The surrounding nature can correct the damage afterward, and that correction is not a complete rebirth. It is an overlay. Victor knew that if the roar had hit him, his entire existence would have been erased, and he would not have been able to do anything about it. Dragon Fire does not delete in that way. It sits a level below, and in its early stages it rivals the Concept of Destruction. It is not END.[5]
A later roar, in a spar, deletes Energy and objects in a straight line. Space and time in that stretch cease. What is left is the darkness of an abyss. A battlefield used this way cannot be trained on. The ground is no longer there.[3]
Victor's own reading of a successful deletion is not "it becomes nothing and that is the whole story." When END deletes something, it does not simply vanish. It goes somewhere he does not know. A thing already Deleted cannot be Recreated. For it to exist again, that item would have to be made over from the start. Death's restoration is not that making. He looked at the deleted stretch, gestured, and everything that had been deleted reappeared, the way a stroke is undone.[3]
When the power is fired as a beam, everything the beam strikes is erased without remainder. The target does not get to oppose it. A region of a galaxy can be gone the way a mark is gone after an eraser. Nothing exists there until the place recovers. The Primordial Beings repair that kind of wound. The emptied region begins to fill again while they work. The repair is theirs. It is not proof that END failed to delete what it hit.[4]

Death, END, and part of Time draw their power from [[True Death]]. Life, nature, reincarnation, and the maintenance of life draw from The Universal Tree. The Creation under BEGIN is the Concept, not the Universe.
Divine Concepts draw their power from Positivity and Negativity. Those two are the divided [[Primordial Chaos]]. The Primordials come from them. END is high on the negative list, and its specialized source is still True Death.[2]
Destruction is second only to END. [[Shiva]]'s Destruction destroys a thing so that a god of creation can use it later. The particles left in the sky are material that will eventually join that reuse. END deletes so the thing never exists again. A holder of the Concept of Creation can contradict Destruction. So can a being who holds BEGIN, and so can a being who holds END. So can [[Primordial Energy]], and so can an absurd amount of Positive and Negative Energy, because those two cover every existing Concept, BEGIN and END included. That quantity is not something an ordinary God walks around with. [[World Trees]] are the beings that energy belongs to.[6]
Shiva's own list of energies is a separate thought, and it is not the order above. In his mind, first comes the energy of Primordial Chaos, Primordial Energy, which only Primordial Beings use. Then comes the Negativity and Positivity that only the World Trees use. Then come the Concepts of the Gods, with BEGIN and END at the top of those as the most important. The Heavenly Father resists Shiva because the Concept of Creation is in him. Shiva did not know whether [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had a counter, and he refused to assume the answer.[6]
BEGIN is the ward against END itself. [[Fenrir]] holds END, and that Concept has only one rival, BEGIN. When END is bound to a weapon and fired, the same rule is what the Upper Sectors know. END is warded off only by BEGIN. [[Vharzath]] had lost his BEGIN Throne. Emperor had no subordinate who could supply the counter.[3][4]
Victor Elderblood's resistance, among the people standing at Polaris, is this opposition. Of everyone present, only [[Hela Elderblood]] and Victor had resistance to END. His is because he holds BEGIN, the opposite Divinity, and because he was one step from becoming living Negativity. He does not wield END as his own Concept.[7]

### Purpose
Death does not treat END as a toy. After [[Fenrir]] deleted a stretch of space in a spar, Death told him not to abuse the power. It has a purpose. He was not to go against it. Beings who hold the Concept have a greater purpose in the scheme of things. While training with the Progenitor, Fenrir was told to leave END unused. He would improve faster that way.[3]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s image for why the erasure has to exist at all is a machine with too much cache. Too much cache freezes the machine or slows it. END has to happen for existence to continue. Watching the deleted stretch put back, he named the other half of that balance. For a beginning to arise, an end has to come.[3]
Ragnarök is one of the events that has to happen. Not because a script says so. Trying to change it does not stop it. It happens because Death wished it. Death does not explain the wish. Fenrir's guess is balance. The Primordials are obsessed with it.[3]
An END being is born already leaking a little of the Energy. An ordinary God's Divinity is found when they come into the world. An END being emits END from the beginning. Those small amounts were enough to make Hela, and perhaps Angrboda, resistant to an Energy that in theory only someone with BEGIN can support.[8]

### How It Is Carried
The same Concept does not sit in every body the same way. [[Fenrir]], [[Typhon]], and [[Jormungandr]] channel the Energy of The End through their bodies, so claws, fangs, and skin are imbued with it. Because they embody the end of everything, that saturation makes them genderless beings who cannot initiate Life. Hela was not born with the Powers of The End. She channels them through a staff, and later through a cannon, and that channel is not the same as wearing the Concept in the flesh.[9]

#### Fenrir
[[Fenrir]] is combat-oriented. The Energy is concentrated inside him, and he can throw it. It sits in the claws, the sharp teeth, and the inside. The body, the tail, and the top of the head are not covered. On the outside, the Concept does not armor him. It applies to his interior and to his prey. The source [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] found was not a stomach. When Fenrir yawned, the inside of the throat was an abyss. That is where the released power comes from.[5][3]
He can ignore concepts that govern the world. A God whose whole style is one fixed Concept has a bad fight against that. Victor did not. He was reading five seconds ahead, kicked Fenrir in the face, and drove the strike [[Scathach]] built, one punch and one million hits, up to one billion hits inside a burst of red plasma-like power. Fenrir opened his mouth and roared. The Energy and the objects in a straight line were gone. Space and time in that line were gone. Victor stopped the spar. Any field Fenrir used END on became unusable.[3]
His Divine Form is a different distribution. In that form he is no longer limited to the fangs and the inside. It is the form tied to the annihilation of the [[Norse Pantheon]]. Without the claws, the fangs, and the inner power, he is much easier to seal. In the territory of a Primordial, The Owner of Limbo would not have a hard time with him as an individual. The danger is the Concept, not an uncovered body that cannot be touched.[5][3]

#### Typhon
[[Typhon]] carries END in the skin, a natural armor. He cannot throw bursts of it. As long as he is not provoked, and as long as [[Gaia]] holds him, he is not the same problem [[Fenrir]] is. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s reading was that Typhon had been quite young when he was sealed. He could not see that being losing to the Greek Gods. Typhon was the antithesis of everything that existed. Sealing him is a different problem from sealing Fenrir. Throw him into an endless abyss where he has no support to move, and the seal is the easy part.[3]

#### Jormungandr
[[Jormungandr]] is the other child Angrboda bore as an END being, and one of the three conductors Polaris uses. With [[Fenrir]], he loaded Hela's second cannon. The beam erased the dimension and the enemy together.[8][7][9][4]

#### Nidhogg
[[Nidhogg]] is a True Dragon and an End Dragon, and he is still a Being of Nature. Death groups him with [[Fenrir]] as a bearer who can actually throw the problem around. [[Typhon]] cannot. The Norse dragon can.[3]
He spent his existence in a dimension of his own, the size of former Russia, small by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s measure. END Energy covered it. The roots of the World Tree there were gnawed through, and they were not connected to [[Yggdrasil]]. END Energy deletes everything. A dimension that strong can repair itself, and beings without access to END Energy cannot enter it. The place sat entirely outside the [[Akashic Records]].[10]
It did not erase itself, because the mix was wrong for a clean deletion. The roots leaked Positive and Negative Energy, and those energies merged with END. [[Draconic Energy]] was in the place as well. Nidhogg fed on the Positive and the Negative. What he expelled was END mixed with [[Natural Energy]], and that mixture is what made the ground around him so strong. Positive, Negative, and END should not sit together. The unexpected mix produced a state where both life and non-life began to exist. Hela's sacrificed Time Energy fueled the dimension and kept the Positive and Negative from being deleted. The balance held because he lived there. When he left, it collapsed. The World Tree abandoned those roots, stopped nourishing the dimension, and stopped nourishing some of [[Asgard]]'s dimension with them.[10]
Victor used the Divinity of Beginnings to look deeper. A fragment of a Soul was growing. Positive Energy, Negative Energy, Death Energy, and Nature Energy had made a chaotic environment, held up by that Time Energy, and the soul-trace sat in the middle of it. A Primordial Norse God was coming into existence inside the dimension, feeding on the dimension and on the abandoned roots, and controlling the END Energy so the fragile balance did not break.[10]

#### Hela Elderblood
At the time [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] walked into Hel, [[Hela Elderblood]] was not an END goddess. The lair was protected by three END gods, and he had walked into a lair of three END beasts alone. The staff was made from a brother's fangs. A being without END should not have been able to touch it. The resistance came from Angrboda's line, the mother of two END beings, and from growing up in the slight emission those newborns already give off. It did not come from holding BEGIN. She has a very strong connection to death and time. In Victor's words, the Primordial Death likes that family a great deal.[8]
She was not born with the Powers of The End. She was born resistant because she shared the womb with her brothers. She channels those Powers through the staff, closer to the way Primordial Death uses the Energy, and that lets her use it in a more creative way than only ending whatever she touches. A strike from the staff opens a hole in Reality. She later wields the same Concept as a measured load in a cannon. She has resistance to being erased by it. The ship Polaris was given to her.[9][7][4]

#### Helvarya Elderblood
[[Helvarya Elderblood]] was not a twin who vanished. Hela's pregnancy showed two Souls. So did Ariel's. As the pregnancies went on, each pair merged into one child, and the power of that child grew. [[Ariella Victoria Elderblood]] was born with the Concept of Creation, and with the potential to also possess Life. Helvarya was born with Death and END. The other presence was not a living Soul. It was the power of Creation in one womb, and the power of END in the other, each preparing its host. Those two Concepts are exceptional even among their own kind. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had wanted twins. He did not get them.[11]
Helvarya's birth is not the body-channel of [[Fenrir]], [[Typhon]], and [[Jormungandr]], and it is not Hela's staff. The Concept prepared her. She was born holding Death and END. Ariella's Concept is Creation. Neither girl is a substitute for the other, and neither one is BEGIN.[11]

### Dead Galaxies
To reach the realm of Abyss creatures, Hela uses her End ability as a guide. Those beings are not Gods of The End. They exist in galaxies that have already had their end. They are what remains when an End God erases a galaxy and only the darkness of the Sector is left.[12]
The later account of that same realm is more precise, and it does not turn those creatures into wielders of the Concept. A galaxy can die and still leave stars, supernovas, and a black hole that goes on pulling. A galaxy that meets its true End does not. What is left is a dark environment around supermassive black holes. Life is not there. Time is not there. Space is not there. Even Void is not there. In plain words, that remainder is the Universe's trash. The Primordials, when they cut the Universe into Sectors, did not spend much care on those dead galaxies. Creatures thrive in them anyway. They are born from darkness, from True Darkness, formed out of fragments of Azathoth's power, and they are Eldritch. The novel's word for why the Primordials left them is that they are part of Creation, meaning the Universe and the System, not the Concept of Creation. They were accepted by the System. When a galaxy is dead, they drink the remaining Energy so that aged Energy does not spread into other Sectors. They are a trash depot. The place became the domain of Primordial Death, and his task is to oversee them and end them if they have to be ended. They are still dangerous if they reach a galaxy that has life.[9]
Hela can enter because it is the Domain of The End. She does it more efficiently than her brothers, because she does not channel the Energy the way a body of claws and fangs does. The creature she had summoned before that visit was not an adult of that realm. It sat at the bottom of the food chain there.[12][9]

### Weapons
Polaris is a galaxy-destroyer Divine Artifact, begun in secret in the 100,000th millennium of the Imperial calendar. The ship bends reality, space, and time, uses Destruction, and can be transformed with Eldritch power. The worst of it is that it can channel END. [[Fenrir]], [[Typhon]], and [[Jormungandr]] are the conductors. The final attack erases what it hits from reality. The cannon withstands the shot once, then disappears. They stopped trying to build a barrel that could survive, and built hundreds of cannons that can be replaced. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] gave Hela the ship to learn. Of the people in that room, only she and he could stand in front of the Energy.[7]
The same method was used in the war, without waiting on that ship. A weapon of Darkness drank a system, the fleets, and the soldiers. Hela grew a cannon from the bow of her vessel, loaded the quantity Velnorah had calculated, and fired. The beam was pure existential erasure. It opened a hole through the creature. It could not oppose the shot. After the Souls of the Imperial soldiers were pulled out, Velnorah boxed what remained in a square dimension. Fenrir and Jormungandr loaded the next cannon with her. The full beam erased the dimension and the weapon of Darkness together. A region of that galaxy no longer existed. It looked as if part of the Universe had been rubbed out with an eraser. An Imperial force was told to stay clear of it until it recovered.[4]
Emperor, seeing END joined to technology, said the faction had to disappear. The two of them had no answer. The enemy held the button for total erasure, and only BEGIN wards it off. [[Xytherion]] was there for that silence. He is the Throne, not the Concept. While they watched, the Primordials were already repairing the erased zone, and it had begun to fill.[4]
[[Violet Elderblood Snow]] wanted a weapon of another scale after the Primordials came for Moira. Something that could threaten everything, and if it had to, tear the Universe apart, so Victor would not be the one to spend half his Soul. [[Eldritch Energy]] and END were both on the table for that work, with Azathoth and the researchers. It was a preparation, not a finished gun.[11]

### Powers and Abilities
- Existence Erasure: what the beam strikes is erased without remainder. A monster cannot oppose the shot. A dimension and the thing inside it can be erased together. A roar that lands would erase the target's entire existence.[5][4]
- Record Erasure: the record of the existence ceases. This is the difference from Destruction, which leaves the matter to be used again. END takes the being out of time, space, and the [[Akashic Records]], as if they had never existed.[1]
- Space-Time Erasure: a straight line of the attack leaves space and time deleted. The stretch is an abyss of darkness.[3]
- Energy Erasure: a roar deletes the Energy thrown at him along with the objects in the line.[3]
- Conceptual Negation: an END being can ignore concepts that govern the world. A style that depends on one fixed Concept has no clean answer to that.[3]
- Erasure Projection: [[Fenrir]] throws the power. The claws, the teeth, and the inside carry it. The throat is an abyss, and that is the source of what he releases.[3]
- Erasure Armor: [[Typhon]] keeps the power in the skin. He does not throw it. Contact with the body is the danger, not a beam.[3]
- Divine Form: Fenrir's Divine Form is no longer limited to the fangs and the interior. The distribution of the Concept widens with that shape.[5]
- Living Conductor: Fenrir, Typhon, and [[Jormungandr]] can be used as conductors so a ship can fire the Concept as a final attack.[7]
- Measured Imposition: Hela loads a specific quantity, the amount Velnorah marked, and the beam goes where it was aimed. The first shot opened a hole. The second, fully loaded, erased the prison and the prisoner.[4]
- Staff Channel: Hela puts the Energy through a staff made from a brother's fangs. She can open a hole in Reality with it, and she can use the Energy in a way closer to Primordial Death than to a claw that only ends what it touches.[8][9]
- Weapon Self-Erasure: the cannon that fires END withstands the shot once, then disappears. The workaround is a replaceable barrel, not a barrel that survives.[7]
- Technological Binding: the Concept can be joined to a ship and fired as a weapon. That joining is what made Emperor decide the faction had to disappear.[4]
- Environmental Erasure: a region of a galaxy can be removed. Until it recovers, nothing exists there. The Primordials repair that kind of wound, and the zone begins to fill.[4]
- Incomplete Natural Overlay: ordinary nature can lay new ground over a deleted path. That overlay is not a full rebirth of what was deleted.[5]
- Dimensional Exclusion: [[Nidhogg]]'s dimension sat outside the Akashic Records, covered in END, and beings without END Energy could not enter it.[10]
- Self-Repairing END Domain: that same dimension could repair itself. The repair was part of how the place was made, not a failure of the Concept.[10]
- Hostile Mixing: Positive Energy, Negative Energy, and END do not sit together cleanly. Time Energy spent by Hela kept the first two from being deleted. The mix produced both life and non-life, and a new god could feed on the balance while controlling the END Energy.[10]
- Innate Emission: a newborn END being already emits a slight END Energy. An ordinary God's Divinity is only found when they come into the world.[8]
- Resistance through Exposure: growing up in that slight emission, including sharing the womb with END brothers, can make someone resistant without BEGIN. That is how Hela could touch a staff made from an END being's fangs.[8][9]
- Resistance through BEGIN: the Energy, in theory, is supported only by someone who holds BEGIN. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s resistance is that opposite Divinity, together with how close he was to living Negativity.[8][7]
- Domain of The End: Hela uses the Divinity of END as a guide into the realm where a galaxy's true end has left only darkness. She enters it more cleanly than her brothers.[12][9]
- Host Preparation: the Concept can stand in a womb as a second presence that is not a living Soul, merge with the child, and leave that child born with END. Helvarya is that host. She was also born with Death.[11]
- Restoration by the Source: [[True Death]] puts a deleted stretch back with a gesture. That reversal is not the recreation of a Deleted thing. A Deleted thing, on Victor's reading, has gone somewhere unknown, and it cannot be Recreated. It would have to be made again.[3]
- Primordial Repair: damage to the wider Universe is repaired by the Primordial Beings. The erased region starts to exist again under that work.[4]

### What END Is Not
- Not Destruction. Destruction is second to END, and it recycles. [[Shiva]] destroys so a god of creation can use the pieces later. END deletes the record.[6][1]
- Not the Concept of Creation, and not its opposite. The Concept of Creation sits under BEGIN. It brings forth. END's rival is BEGIN. A sentence about matter being recycled for creation is the reuse of matter, not that Divinity. "Creation" as the Universe, or as the System that accepted the Abyss creatures, is the cosmos, not the Concept either.[2][3][9][1]
- Not ordinary Death. Death is the office and the Primordial. END is the erasure that draws from him. A Goddess of Death is not automatically a wielder of END. Hela was not an END goddess when she could already touch the staff.[8][2]
- Not BEGIN. BEGIN is the ward and the only rival. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] resists END because he holds BEGIN. He does not fire END.[3][7]
- Not Dragon Fire, and not a Dragon-Language command. Dragon Fire deletes less completely than [[Fenrir]]'s roar and sits nearer Destruction. Kassandra's commands erase because she was born as Destruction. That erasure still leaves a record Creation, in the sense of reuse, can take. END would not.[5][1]
- Not Negativity, and not something every Negative Concept can do. Negativity is above it. Destruction is beneath it. Neither one deletes the Akashic record by being itself.[2][1]
- Not the Throne of the End. [[Xytherion]] is [[Vharzath]]'s office. Watching an END beam does not make the Throne into the Concept.[4]
- Not the Abyss creatures. They live in what a true end leaves behind. They are not Gods of The End.[12][9]
- Not safe to fire from a permanent barrel. The shot eats the weapon.[7]
- Not a free attack for training. Death forbade the abuse, and he forbade it in spars with Victor. The field does not survive the use.[3]
- Not moral evil, and not the end of a chapter. When the word is this power, it is the Concept.

### Limitations
- BEGIN is the ward. Without it, a faction that can bind END to a ship has no answer.[4]
- The source can undo a misuse. Death put [[Fenrir]]'s line back, and told him not to do it again. He also told him not to use END while training with the Progenitor.[3]
- What is Deleted is not freely rebuilt by the one who deleted it. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] could not see where it went, and a Deleted thing cannot be Recreated. It would have to be made again. Death's gesture is a reversal, not that making.[3]
- The Primordials repair the galaxy-scale wound. The region does not stay a permanent hole in the Universe by default. Ordinary nature's overlay of a roar's path is even less of a rebirth.[5][4]
- A body is not fully covered just because it holds END. Fenrir's tail, torso, and the top of his head are open, until the Divine Form changes the distribution. Without claws, fangs, and the inner power, he is sealable.[5][3]
- [[Typhon]] cannot project it. An endless abyss with nothing for him to brace against is enough to seal him.[3]
- The cannon dies with the shot.[7]
- Positive and Negative Energy do not calmly share a space with END. The mix either deletes them or, if Time Energy holds the deletion off, curdles into a balance that should not exist.[10]
- Resistance is not the Concept. Hela could touch the staff long before she was firing beams.[8]
- Body-channelers who embody the end of everything do not initiate Life. That limit is Fenrir, Typhon, and [[Jormungandr]]. It is not a description of Hela's staff, and it is not a description of Helvarya's birth.[9][11]
- Using it in a spar ruins the spar. Death was explicit.[3]
- There is no perfect bearer. Victor's conclusion, after watching both wolves, was that every one of them has a weakness if it is looked for in the right place.[3]
