### Physical Appearance
He is a man with long black hair that reached the ground, has a muscular body that was covered in strange tattoos that looked like runes, and these tattoos seemed to cover his entire body as if following some sort of pattern. He wasn't wearing anything from the waist up. The only thing he was wearing was torn prisoner's pants and two black cuffs that looked very old on his wrists.[1]

### Personality
He seemed tired of the same monotonous life of a prisoner and an administrator of the Limbo prison, and had an look devoid of joy and life itself.

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes and War
He appeared for the first time during when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Natashia visited Limbo to get captives and prisoners to feed it to [[The Universal Tree|The Tree]]. He showed Victor multiple universes where beings had conquered there cosmos and how they ruled and how they had there ending and as well as how they created there own rules to rule the masses. he showed Victor, three scenarios of rulers, first being a warrior ruler from mediaeval lands, second one was an elf surrounded by her people and green trees, and lastly was a galactic emperor who traveled in his gigantic spaceship peacefully on a journey to explore every corner of the universe. He then pulled Victor out from the 'space', a space outside of the 'universe' itself. He then showed Victor the three rulers, how there ruling worked and what happened after there death. The reason he showed the three rulers are because all three made rules for there kingdom, empires and so on but the outcomes were vastly different for each of them.
1. "Rules, a set of words made by a stronger being and which smaller and weaker beings are obliged to follow."
2. "Rules, the way a king or ruler found to control and administrate his people."
3. "Rules, chains created to imprison a 'little' of the free will that the creator gave to all beings."
—Code, Rule to Victor, Chapter 269
He later said to Victor that 'In all ages, in all universes, the rulers think they are invincible and that nothing can kill them. When gaining power, in gaining a status that is above ordinary beings, he thinks he is untouchable.' Its complete arrogance. He then added, even though he thinks rules are the definition of insanity but if used correctly it could become something amazing.
Remember, as you fall into the deepest abyss, only order will be your guide. Nothing can be born from chaos, only more chaos will be born. Only when a tiny spark of 'Order' exists, only then can sentient beings show their full potential.
—Code, Rule to Victor, Chapter 269
He then gave full access of Vampire designed criminals to The Second Progenitor of Vampires. Criminals from Level 5 to 7 were given to Victor.
"May order accompany you, child loved by the chaos."
—RULES to Victor, Chapter 269

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
He was mentioned by The Owner of Limbo while talking with [[Victor Elderblood]], that he is very interested in what action Victor would take in the future events.[2]

### Skills & Abilities
- Time Manipulation: He has the power to completely stop time around him and around the being he desire. This effect can effect entire [[The Limbo Realm]].[1]
- Perception Manipulation: He can manipulate other beings perception of his existence around him and only beings who he desire could see him.[1]
- Personal Non-Existence: He exists but at the same time he doesn't exist. No one can perceive his existence, even those who could see souls.[1]
- Eternal Life: He exist in the Limbo which has Endless Loop concept baked in the prison which grants every prison complete immortality.
- Self-sustenance: He is completely self sustainable and can go on without food or sleep for eternity.

### Trivia
- He is a prisoner as well as an administrator of [[The Limbo Realm]] prison and has authority second only to The Owner of Limbo himself.
- He is one of the highest level prisoner of The Limbo Realm who was directly captured by The Limbo Realm himself.[3]
