Holy Energy, also known as Sacred Energy or Holy Light, is a supernatural form of Energy associated primarily with Angels, the Heavenly Father and the positive side of Creation. It constitutes the characteristic Energy of the Biblical Heavenly system and serves as the supernatural foundation through which Angels exercise their racial powers. Its applications include the generation of Sacred Light, purification of Demonic corruption, the establishment of Holy territories, healing, exorcism and the execution of specialized Heavenly techniques. Unlike ordinary elemental Light, Holy Energy possesses a sacred attribute that is particularly harmful to beings whose existence is associated with the negative side of the supernatural balance.[1][2][3]
Holy Energy is not merely an offensive manifestation of Light. It is also an essential environmental resource for the Heavenly Race. Powerful Angels require sufficient concentrations of compatible Sacred Energy to maintain their full strength, and prolonged activity outside an appropriate environment can gradually weaken them. This dependence resembles the relationship between Demons and the corruptive Energy of Hell. Consequently, the creation and maintenance of sanctified territory is an important part of Angelic activity outside Heaven, allowing Angels to descend upon the Mortal World and exercise their abilities without suffering the ordinary limitations imposed by an incompatible environment.[1]
Although closely associated with the Heavenly Father, Holy Energy is distinct from the neutral [[Divine Energy]] generated by Gods. Pure Divine Energy initially possesses no attribute, while Holy Energy already possesses a positive and sacred nature. The distinction arises from the characteristics of the power itself: Divine Energy acquires its attributes through the God utilizing it, whereas Holy Energy is an attributed supernatural power directly associated with the positive side of Creation and the Heavenly Father's Divine nature.[3]
Holy Energy also possesses considerable versatility. The Heavenly Father can employ it through devastating Judgment techniques, while Angels and Saints use it for sanctification, healing and the suppression of Demonic powers. Particularly exceptional individuals can retain or acquire Holy Energy even after becoming members of races ordinarily vulnerable to it. Jeanne Elderblood demonstrates this through her ability to invoke Sacred power despite her former Vampiric physiology and her later development as a Dragon whose elemental affinities include Holy Light and Wind.[4][5]

### Nature and Origin

#### Angelic Energy
Holy Energy is the principal supernatural Energy associated with Angels. Their ability to generate and manipulate it forms part of their racial physiology, distinguishing them from Demons, Vampires, [[Werewolves]] and other supernatural beings whose ordinary powers arise through different energetic foundations.[1][3]
The relationship between Angels and Holy Energy extends beyond a simple affinity with Light. The Biblical Heavenly system incorporates an environment naturally compatible with Angelic existence, allowing Angels to maintain their powers without the restrictions encountered in regions lacking suitable Sacred Energy. Their bodies, abilities and supernatural development are therefore closely connected to the Energy associated with Heaven.[1]
This relationship resembles the dependence of Demons upon Hell's supernatural environment. However, the two races possess opposing characteristics, with Holy Energy being associated with the Sacred and positive side of Creation while Miasma possesses the corruptive characteristics associated with Demonic existence.
The differences between the two energies do not mean that every individual Angel possesses identical abilities. Their ranks, innate characteristics, specialized powers and individual experience determine how effectively they can manipulate Holy Energy and which advanced applications are available to them.

#### Origin in the Heavenly Father
The [[Heavenly Father]] is the principal Divine being associated with the creation of the Biblical Heavenly system and the Angels inhabiting it. Holy Energy is directly connected to his Divine nature and the supernatural foundation of Heaven.[3]
[[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]] explained that the Energy of Angels belongs to the positive side of the cosmic balance. Unlike Demons, whose characteristic Energy is associated with the Miasma of Hell, Angels obtain their Sacred characteristics through a power connected directly to the Heavenly Father's Divinity. His nature as a God associated with Creation is particularly significant because Creation belongs to the positive side of the balance.[3]
This establishes an important distinction between the origin of Holy Energy and the manner in which ordinary Angels employ it. Although the Heavenly Father serves as the fundamental source associated with the Heavenly system, Angels possess their own supernatural characteristics and can generate Holy Energy through their racial physiology. Their powers are therefore not limited to actively borrowing an individual technique from the Heavenly Father every time they employ Sacred Light.
Certain techniques, particularly the Heavenly Judgments, nevertheless retain a more direct dependence upon his authorization. Those abilities operate through a specialized Divine system rather than being ordinary applications available to every Angel.[4][6]

#### Positive Alignment
Holy Energy is naturally associated with the positive side of Creation. The supernatural balance distinguishes the positive and negative characteristics underlying existence, with various attributed energies representing particular expressions of those foundations.[3]
Angelic Holy Energy possesses a direct relationship with the positive side, while Demonic Miasma possesses a corresponding relationship with the negative side. This distinction explains the natural incompatibility between their ordinary manifestations and the severe damage that sufficiently powerful Sacred techniques can inflict upon Demons.[7][3]
However, Holy Energy should not be treated as identical to the complete primordial Concept of Positivity. The fundamental positive side of Creation encompasses numerous Concepts and supernatural expressions, while Holy Energy is a more specialized form associated with the Heavenly Father's Divine nature and Angelic existence.
- Positive Energy Affinity: Holy Energy possesses an intrinsic association with the positive side of the cosmic balance.[3]
- Sacred Energy: Its supernatural characteristics are associated with the Heavenly system and are especially harmful to incompatible negative-aligned beings.[2][5]
- Energy Generation: Angels possess the ability to generate the Sacred power associated with their racial existence.[1][3]
- Energy Manipulation: Holy Energy can be controlled and employed through Angelic techniques, sanctified territories and supernatural attacks.[4][6]
- Energy Purification: Sacred techniques can remove Demonic Miasma and destroy supernatural effects sustained through Demonic [[Magic]].[4]
The Energy's positive alignment does not independently establish authority over every positive Concept. A being capable of generating Holy Light does not automatically possess the Divinities of Life, Creation, Beginning, Purity or every other positive-aligned phenomenon.

### Relationship with the Heavenly Environment

#### The Natural Energy of Heaven
The Heavenly realms possess a supernatural environment naturally compatible with Angels. Holy Energy is an essential component of that environment, allowing members of the Heavenly Race to maintain their strength and utilize their abilities without the limitations they ordinarily encounter when descending into an incompatible world.[1]
This is especially important for high-ranking Angels. Although exceptionally powerful, they cannot necessarily maintain their full strength indefinitely in an environment lacking suitable Sacred Energy. Their supernatural physiology depends upon the conditions associated with Heaven in much the same way that powerful Demons benefit from the corruptive Energy of Hell.[1]
The environmental dependence affects even powerful members of the race, including high-ranking Angels and Archangels. Their ability to descend upon Earth does not remove the problem; instead, they commonly establish or utilize territories where compatible Sacred Energy can be maintained.

#### Environmental Dependence
Angels possess a fundamental dependence upon an environment containing sufficient Holy Energy. When high-ranking Angels enter Earth without an appropriate territory, their strength gradually decreases because the surrounding conditions do not provide the Energy required to maintain their full supernatural output.[1]
This weakness is especially significant because the reduction does not require an enemy to attack the Angel directly. The environment itself imposes a limitation upon the continued exercise of their powers.
- Environmental Energy Dependence: Angels require an environment containing compatible Holy Energy to sustain their full supernatural strength.[1]
- Environmental Empowerment: Sacred environments allow Angels to operate without the weakening ordinarily produced by unsuitable surroundings.[1]
- Energy-Based Weakening: High-ranking Angels gradually lose strength when operating outside compatible territories.[1]
- Territorial Empowerment: Sanctified locations can compensate for environmental incompatibility and allow Angels to exercise their normal abilities.[1]
The environmental requirement does not mean Angels are incapable of fighting on Earth or within other realms. Rather, their performance can deteriorate without an appropriate means of maintaining compatible supernatural conditions.

#### Churches as Holy Territory
Churches serve as important locations for Angelic activity because they provide territory compatible with the Heavenly Race. The existence of such locations historically allowed Angels to descend upon Earth and maintain their strength without suffering the ordinary disadvantages of an unsuitable environment.[1]
A Church therefore possesses significance beyond its social and religious functions. Within the Biblical supernatural system, it can serve as a sanctified location where the Heavenly Race can establish the conditions necessary for the exercise of its powers.
The relationship between Churches and Angelic descent is especially important during supernatural conflicts, since controlling an appropriate territory can allow an Angel to fight effectively while preventing the weakening associated with prolonged exposure to incompatible surroundings.[1]

#### Territory Declaration
Territory Declaration is one of the most important applications of Holy Energy associated with Angels and Saints. It permits the establishment of a sanctified area possessing conditions suitable for the Heavenly Race.[1]
The [[Heavenly Father]] grants certain Humans the status of Saints, and these individuals can establish Holy territories through their supernatural abilities. Such territories function as locations where Angels can descend and utilize their full strength rather than being weakened by Earth's ordinary conditions.[1]
- Holy Territory Creation: Saints and qualified Heavenly beings can establish sanctified regions suitable for Angelic activity.[1]
- Environmental Alteration: Territory Declaration creates local conditions compatible with Holy Energy.[1]
- Angelic Empowerment: Angels operating within a properly established Holy territory avoid the weakening normally caused by an unsuitable environment.[1]
- Area-of-Effect Empowerment: The resulting territory can support Heavenly beings throughout the affected region rather than empowering only its creator.[1]
The basic purpose of Territory Declaration is environmental compatibility. Additional abilities, including the suppression of Demons and destruction of Miasma, depend upon the particular technique used to establish or affect the territory.

#### Holy Energy and Dimensional Separation
The concentration of Holy Energy within Heaven also influences interactions between the Heavenly and infernal dimensions. The Biblical Heavens and Hells contain exceptionally different supernatural environments, with Heaven possessing enormous concentrations of Sacred Energy and Hell being saturated with Demonic Miasma.[7]
This difference creates practical difficulties for communication between the dimensions. The [[Heavenly Father]] provided [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] with a specialized device capable of establishing contact despite the separation and the distinct energetic conditions of the two realms.[7]
Victor described the contrast through the characteristics of elemental Energy. Miasma was compared to Water because of its density and heaviness, while Sacred Light was compared to Wind because of its light and free-flowing nature.[7]
The comparison establishes that Holy Energy and Miasma possess fundamentally different energetic characteristics, beyond their tendency to harm beings associated with the opposing supernatural alignment.

### Properties and Characteristics

#### Sacred Light
Holy Energy frequently manifests as Sacred Light, an attributed supernatural Light possessing characteristics associated with Heaven. Unlike ordinary Light, Sacred Light can exert supernatural effects upon Demons, Vampires and other creatures whose existence is incompatible with its properties.[2][5]
The manifestation of Sacred Light can include golden illumination, supernatural radiance and energy projections. Powerful Heavenly techniques may produce massive beams of Light or enormous areas surrounded by golden flames.[4][6]
Its appearance alone is not what defines the Energy. A beam of ordinary Light and a beam of Holy Light may appear superficially similar while possessing different supernatural properties.

#### Light and Sacred Light
One of the most important distinctions within the supernatural energy system is that ordinary Light and Holy Light are not identical.
[[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]] possesses an elemental affinity for Light despite belonging to the [[Otsuki Clan]], whose supernatural characteristics are principally associated with Darkness. During her duel with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], this unusual affinity caused concern among some spectators because Light was mistakenly associated with the Sacred element capable of harming Vampires.[2]
[[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] directly corrected this misunderstanding, distinguishing the ordinary Light element from the Holy or Sacred attributes to which Vampires are vulnerable. The distinction was further supported by [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]], who recognized that the powers of the [[Snow Bloodline]] provided substantial resistance to Sacred attacks even though their characteristics were different from ordinary elemental Light.[2]
This establishes that ordinary Light Manipulation does not automatically grant Holy Energy. A practitioner can manipulate Light through [[Magic]], racial affinities or other supernatural systems without possessing the Sacred attribute associated with Angels.
Conversely, Holy Energy can manifest as Light while carrying effects unrelated to ordinary illumination.

#### Energy Density
Holy Energy possesses characteristics fundamentally different from those of Miasma. Its relationship with the positive side of Creation gives it an energetic nature associated with lightness and freedom of movement, while Demonic Miasma possesses comparatively dense and heavy characteristics.[7]
The distinction influences the behavior of the two energies within their respective supernatural environments and contributes to the practical difficulties involved in establishing communication between Heaven and Hell.
- Light Energetic Characteristics: Sacred Light possesses comparatively light and free-flowing properties.[7]
- Environmental Concentration: Heaven contains enormous quantities of Holy Energy, influencing the conditions of the realm.[7]
- Energy Differentiation: Holy Energy possesses characteristics distinguishable from Demonic Miasma, ordinary Mana, [[Natural Energy]] and neutral [[Divine Energy]].[3]
- Sacred Attribute: Holy Energy can exert supernatural effects against beings vulnerable to the Sacred element.[2][5]
The comparison between Holy Energy and Wind describes its energetic characteristics. It does not mean that every Angel automatically possesses Wind Manipulation or that Holy Energy is an ordinary elemental form of Wind.

#### Incompatibility with Negative-Aligned Beings
Holy Energy possesses particularly harmful effects against Demons and other supernatural beings whose existence is associated with the negative side of the cosmic balance.[2][7]
Demons are especially vulnerable to Sacred Light because of the incompatibility between their corruptive physiology and the positive attributes of the Energy. Vampires can also be harmed by Holy techniques, although their degree of resistance varies according to their individual Bloodlines, supernatural development and protective abilities.[2]
The incompatibility does not mean every quantity of Holy Energy automatically destroys every negatively aligned being. The strength of the user, the potency of the Energy, the resistance of the target and the characteristics of the particular technique remain important.
Powerful Demons can survive attacks that would immediately destroy lesser members of their race, while exceptional Vampires may possess enough resistance to withstand Sacred attacks without suffering fatal damage.

### Abilities and Applications

#### Holy Energy Manipulation
The fundamental application of Holy Energy is the generation and manipulation of Sacred supernatural power. Angels possess this capacity through their racial physiology, while Saints and exceptional beings may receive access to it through other supernatural circumstances.[1][4]
- Holy Energy Generation: Angels can generate the Sacred Energy associated with their supernatural existence.[1][3]
- Holy Energy Manipulation: Users can direct Holy Energy through supernatural techniques, attacks and sanctified territories.[4]
- Energy Projection: Holy Energy can be released as beams, auras and other offensive manifestations.[6]
- Energy Reinforcement: Compatible Holy environments can support the supernatural output of Angels and prevent the weakening caused by unsuitable surroundings.[1]
- Energy Constructs: Sacred Energy can be incorporated into supernatural weapons and specialized Heavenly techniques.[4]
- Area-of-Effect Manipulation: Powerful Sacred techniques can influence extensive regions rather than being restricted to individual targets.[4][6]

#### Purification
Purification is one of the most important offensive and defensive applications of Holy Energy. Its Sacred characteristics allow it to counter Demonic Miasma and destroy certain supernatural effects sustained through Demonic [[Magic]].[4]
A particularly clear demonstration occurred when [[Jeanne Elderblood]] invoked the [[Heavenly Father]]'s Judgment of Israel. The resulting golden flames spread across the Royal Capital and destroyed the magical circles established by [[Paimon]], simultaneously eliminating the Miasma emanating from the Demons present.[4]
- Purification: Sacred Energy can remove harmful Demonic influences from an affected area.[4]
- Miasma Purification: Holy techniques can destroy or disperse Demonic Miasma.[4]
- Demonic Magic Negation: Powerful Sacred attacks can dismantle magical circles and supernatural effects created through Demonic power.[4]
- Environmental Purification: Holy techniques can transform a contaminated battlefield into a region temporarily dominated by Sacred power.[4]
- Energy Suppression: Purification can interfere with the supernatural Energy used by Demons, weakening their ability to maintain certain techniques.[4]
The effectiveness of purification depends upon the strength of the technique and the resistance of the opposing power. Jeanne's Judgment represents a particularly powerful authorized technique and should not be treated as the ordinary output available to every Angel.

#### Suppression of Demonic Powers
Holy Energy can weaken Demons through its incompatible supernatural characteristics. Within Jeanne's Judgment of Israel, Demons suffered substantial reductions in strength while the Sacred flames remained active throughout the surrounding territory.[4]
The effect also extended to Vampires, although Demons suffered more severely. The difference reflects the particular effectiveness of Sacred power against the Demonic characteristics of the affected beings.[4]
- Demonic Power Suppression: Sacred Energy can reduce the supernatural strength of Demons within an affected area.[4]
- Statistics Reduction: Demons exposed to powerful Holy territories may experience a reduction in their physical and supernatural capabilities.[4]
- Energy Negation: Holy techniques can destroy certain Demonic supernatural constructs and suppress the Energy sustaining them.[4]
- Area-of-Effect Weakening: Sacred techniques can affect numerous negatively aligned beings simultaneously.[4]
The suppression does not function as an unconditional command over Demonkind. It arises from the interaction between the Sacred Energy and the incompatible supernatural characteristics of the affected targets.

#### Exorcism
Holy Energy is associated with the expulsion of Demons from vessels and the disruption of Demonic possession. The effectiveness of exorcism depends upon the relative power of the Angel or practitioner and the Demon being affected.
The vulnerability of Demons to Holy Energy makes Sacred power particularly suitable for this function. Nevertheless, the ability to damage a Demon does not automatically guarantee successful exorcism against every form of possession.
- Exorcism: Compatible Holy techniques can expel Demonic beings from possessed vessels.
- Demonic Energy Suppression: Sacred power can interfere with the Demonic characteristics supporting an infernal presence.[4]
- Purification: Holy techniques can remove Miasma and certain Demonic magical effects associated with the affected environment.[4]
Exorcism should be distinguished from Soul Destruction. Expelling a Demon from a vessel does not necessarily destroy its underlying Soul or prevent its return through another supernatural mechanism.

#### Healing
Holy Energy can be used through specialized Heavenly and Saintly abilities to restore injuries and support living beings. This application reflects the constructive potential of Sacred power rather than its destructive characteristics against Demons.
Healing is associated with the standard abilities of Angels and certain Saints, although the strength and extent of the effect depend upon the particular practitioner.
- Healing: Qualified Holy Energy users can employ Sacred power to treat injuries.
- Energy-Based Restoration: Healing techniques can apply Holy Energy toward the recovery of a compatible recipient.
- Support Techniques: Sacred power can serve a beneficial function rather than being used exclusively for combat or purification.
Healing should not be confused with unrestricted regeneration, resurrection or the ability to cure every possible supernatural condition. Those effects require separate demonstrations and may depend upon the specialized abilities of individual Angels, Saints or higher-ranking Divine beings.
The healing applications associated with Holy Energy also do not establish that the same techniques are automatically safe for Vampires or Demons. Their ordinary incompatibility with the Sacred element remains an important limitation.

#### Holy Fire
Holy Fire is a specialized manifestation of Sacred Energy associated with powerful Heavenly techniques. Unlike ordinary Fire, its harmful effects are not restricted to the destructive properties of heat and combustion. Its Sacred characteristics allow it to interfere directly with Demonic Miasma and negatively aligned supernatural powers.[4]
Jeanne's Judgment of Israel provides a direct demonstration. Golden flames spread throughout the surrounding territory, destroying Demonic magical circles and removing Miasma while weakening Demons and Vampires.[4]
- Holy Fire Manipulation: Specialized Sacred techniques can manifest Holy Energy as supernatural flames.[4]
- Sacred Combustion: Holy Fire can inflict damage through both its fiery manifestation and its Sacred attributes.[4]
- Miasma Purification: Golden Holy flames can destroy Demonic Miasma within an affected area.[4]
- Demonic [[Magic]] Negation: Holy Fire can eliminate certain magical constructs sustained through Demonic Energy.[4]
- Area-of-Effect Suppression: Sacred flames can establish a region that weakens negatively aligned supernatural beings.[4]
Holy Fire should be distinguished from ordinary [[Pyrokinesis]] and from Fire produced through neutral elemental Magic. Its Sacred characteristic is essential to its effectiveness against certain supernatural beings.

#### Sacred Light Projection
Holy Energy can be projected as destructive Light. The [[Heavenly Father]] demonstrated the extraordinary potential of this application through a Judgment technique that released numerous beams of Sacred Light across the battlefield during the Demonic War.[6]
The resulting attack devastated the Demonic forces, destroying numerous powerful opponents and demonstrating the exceptional effectiveness of large-scale Sacred attacks against armies composed primarily of negatively aligned beings.
- Sacred Light Projection: Holy Energy can manifest as beams of destructive supernatural Light.[6]
- Area-of-Effect Attacks: Particularly powerful techniques can cover enormous battlefields with numerous simultaneous energy projections.[6]
- Demonic Destruction: Sacred beams can destroy Demons unable to resist their supernatural properties.[6]
- Energy-Based Judgment: Sacred Light can serve as the destructive medium for specialized Heavenly Judgment techniques.[6]
The destructive scale of the Heavenly Father's Judgment is associated with his extraordinary power and the nature of the technique. Ordinary Angels should not automatically be assigned equivalent attack potency.

### Heavenly Judgments

#### Nature of the Judgments
Heavenly Judgments are specialized supernatural techniques associated with the [[Heavenly Father]]. They are based upon his historical acts of Divine judgment and represent exceptionally powerful manifestations of Heavenly authority.[8]
Their foundations are connected to significant events in Biblical history, including the Flood of Noah, the destruction associated with Israel's Judgment by Fire and the banishment of rebellious Angels into Hell.[8]
The Judgments are not ordinary abilities automatically available to every Angel or Saint. Their use depends upon the Heavenly Father's authorization, and certain techniques are accessible only to qualified individuals recognized by the Heavenly system.[4][8]

#### Judgment of Israel
Judgment of Israel is a Holy technique bestowed through the [[Heavenly Father]]'s authority. [[Jeanne Elderblood]] invoked it during the Demonic War against the forces of [[Paimon]], [[Baal]] and their allies.[4]
Jeanne possessed the remnants of her former Saintly powers despite having become a Vampire. When she invoked the Judgment, the Heavenly Father responded and granted permission for her to employ the technique. He warned her that her Vampiric body was fundamentally incompatible with the Sacred power being invoked and that excessive use could erase her existence.[4]
Golden wings manifested behind Jeanne, and a Sacred sword associated with Michael appeared in her hand. After she proclaimed the Judgment, golden flames spread through the Royal Capital, forming an enormous circle that destroyed the Demonic magical circles within its range and dispersed the Miasma surrounding the enemy forces.[4]
The technique also established a hostile environment for Demons. Their strength was significantly reduced, and the surrounding Sacred Energy restricted their ability to operate normally. Vampires were affected as well, although the Demons suffered considerably more severely.[4]
- Territory Declaration: Judgment of Israel establishes a sanctified area dominated by Holy Energy.[4]
- Holy Fire Manipulation: The technique manifests enormous golden flames that spread throughout the affected region.[4]
- Purification: Demonic Miasma is destroyed within the territory.[4]
- Demonic [[Magic]] Negation: Magical circles created by Demonic practitioners can be dismantled by the Sacred effect.[4]
- Power Suppression: Demons and certain other negatively aligned beings suffer reductions in their supernatural capabilities.[4]
- Weapon Manifestation: The authorized technique permits the appearance of a Sacred sword associated with Michael.[4]
- Divine Authorization: Jeanne's ability to execute the Judgment depends upon permission granted by the Heavenly Father.[4]
After using the technique, Jeanne suffered considerable physical strain. The Sacred power gradually dissipated from her body, and the consequences demonstrated the dangers of invoking incompatible Heavenly Energy through a Vampiric vessel.[4]

#### Heavenly Judgment
Heavenly Judgment is a large-scale execution technique employed by the [[Heavenly Father]] during the Demonic War. Its activation produced an enormous manifestation of Sacred Light above the battlefield before releasing destructive beams toward the Demonic forces.[6]
The attack inflicted devastating losses upon the Demon army, destroying many powerful combatants and dramatically changing the balance of the conflict. Despite the extraordinary destructive output, certain exceptionally powerful beings managed to resist or survive parts of the attack through their durability, defensive techniques or concentrated supernatural Energy.[6]
Sloth, for example, survived by remaining protected within an exceptionally dense concentration of Miasma. Even so, some Sacred Light penetrated her defenses and inflicted injuries, demonstrating the effectiveness of the attack without establishing that its destructive properties were literally irresistible.[6]
- Massive Energy Projection: The technique releases numerous destructive beams of Holy Light across a large area.[6]
- Area-of-Effect Destruction: Its destructive range is suitable for devastating a large military formation.[6]
- Demonic Destruction: The Sacred attribute is particularly effective against Demonic beings.[6]
- High Energy Expenditure: Heavenly Judgments require substantial supernatural power to perform.[6]
- Resistance-Dependent Damage: Exceptional defensive abilities and sufficiently concentrated Energy can partially mitigate the effects of the attack.[6]

#### Other Judgments
The [[Heavenly Father]]'s Judgments also include techniques associated with the Flood of Noah and the banishment of rebellious Angels.[8]
These are distinct Heavenly abilities reflecting different historical acts of judgment. Their existence demonstrates the variety of supernatural effects that may be associated with the Heavenly Father's authority, but it does not establish that every Judgment operates exclusively through Holy Fire or Sacred Light.
The individual effects of these techniques depend upon the specific Judgment being invoked.

### Specialized Manifestations

#### Ordinary Angelic Holy Energy
Ordinary Angelic Holy Energy is the characteristic Sacred power generated by members of the Heavenly Race. It sustains their supernatural abilities and can be employed through techniques suited to the practitioner's rank and specialization.[1][3]
Its effectiveness depends upon the available Energy, the user's development and the environment in which it is employed. High-ranking Angels possess greater supernatural capabilities than ordinary members of the race, but their full strength remains influenced by environmental compatibility.

#### Seraphic Holy Energy
Seraphim are among the highest-ranking members of the Heavenly Race and possess exceptional supernatural power. Their abilities include advanced applications of Sacred Light and Holy Fire, while certain members possess special authority or concepts associated with their roles.
The powers of the highest Seraphim should not be treated as ordinary abilities possessed by every Angel. Their greater status and specialized capabilities allow them to produce effects extending considerably beyond common Holy Energy techniques.
- Advanced Sacred Energy Manipulation: High-ranking Seraphim possess exceptional control over the Holy Energy associated with their race.
- Holy Fire: Certain Seraphic abilities involve the generation and manipulation of Sacred flames.
- Heavenly Combat Techniques: Elite Angels can employ powerful Sacred attacks and weapons suited to confronting high-ranking Demons.
- Specialized Heavenly Authority: Certain original Heavenly beings possess supernatural characteristics extending beyond ordinary Angelic Energy.

#### Jeanne's Saintly Holy Energy
[[Jeanne Elderblood]] possesses an unusual relationship with Holy Energy because of her former existence as a Saint and her subsequent transformations. Although she became a Vampire, her original Saintly characteristics were not completely extinguished.[9][4]
She retained the ability to establish contact with the [[Heavenly Father]] and invoke certain powers associated with her former role. However, the relationship was physically dangerous: even communicating with the Heavenly Father through Sacred power caused her Vampiric body discomfort.[9]
Her invocation of Judgment of Israel demonstrated the extent of the incompatibility. The Heavenly Father explicitly warned that the technique could destroy her existence if she attempted to use too much of its power.[4]
The danger arose from the conflict between her body and the Sacred element rather than an absence of the necessary Saintly authority.

#### Jeanne's Holy Light Affinity
Jeanne's later development produced a fundamentally different relationship with Holy Energy. Following her transformation into a Dragon, she manifested Holy Light and Wind as her principal elemental affinities.[5]
Jeanne discovered that the Sacred power formerly associated with the [[Heavenly Father]] had become part of her own supernatural affinity. She demonstrated this by manifesting Holy Light directly from her hand, producing a Sacred radiance capable of threatening Demons and other creatures of the night.[5]
The reaction of [[Lilith Elderblood]] and [[Lily Elderblood]] confirmed the danger of this power. Both withdrew from Jeanne while she manifested the Light and remained visibly uncomfortable until she dismissed it.[5]
The transformation did not merely permit Jeanne to tolerate an occasional borrowed Heavenly technique. Holy Light had become an established characteristic of her own supernatural existence.
- Holy Light Affinity: Jeanne possesses an intrinsic affinity with Sacred Light after her transformation.[5]
- Holy Energy Generation: She can manifest Holy Light directly rather than requiring a separate invocation of Judgment of Israel.[5]
- Sacred Light Manipulation: Jeanne can consciously control the Holy Energy she produces.[5]
- Demonic Weakness Exploitation: Her Sacred Light remains particularly dangerous to Demons, including extremely powerful individuals such as Lilith.[5]
- Multi-Attribute Physiology: Holy Light coexists with Jeanne's Wind affinity and her Draconic nature.[5]
Her development demonstrates that Holy Energy is not restricted exclusively to the original physiology of Angels. An exceptional being can acquire it as an innate supernatural characteristic through an extraordinary transformation.

#### Diablo's Hybrid Holy Light
[[Diablo]] temporarily obtained the ability to wield Holy Light after undergoing an extreme transformation incorporating characteristics associated with Angels, Fallen Angels and Demons.[10][11]
The transformation produced an existence combining ordinarily opposing supernatural attributes. Diablo demonstrated the ability to manifest dark Demonic Miasma and Angelic Holy Light simultaneously, creating attacks that incorporated characteristics of both sides of the balance.[11]
This ability was particularly dangerous because it allowed him to exploit the vulnerabilities of different supernatural races. His Miasma remained effective against Angels, while his Holy Light provided a means of injuring Demons whose ordinary physiology was highly resistant to conventional Demonic Energy.[7]
- Dual Energy Manipulation: Diablo could wield Holy Light and Demonic Miasma simultaneously after his transformation.[11]
- Hybrid Energy Physiology: His transformed body incorporated characteristics normally associated with incompatible supernatural races.[11]
- Sacred Energy Projection: Diablo could manifest Angelic Holy Light as an offensive supernatural power.[11]
- Energy Combination: He could combine Holy and Demonic characteristics within his attacks.[11]
- Demonic Vulnerability Exploitation: Holy Light provided a means of attacking powerful Demons through a supernatural weakness distinct from ordinary physical force.[7]
Diablo's transformation represents an exceptional case and does not establish that ordinary Demons can naturally generate Sacred Energy through their normal physiology.

#### Lily's Dark Light
[[Lily Elderblood]] possesses an unusual Demonic power known as Dark Light, associated with the union of the Demonic lineage of Lilith and the Fallen Angelic lineage of [[Lucifer]] Morningstar.
The power incorporates characteristics associated with Darkness and traces of the Sacred Light originally possessed by Lucifer before his fall. Its existence demonstrates that Angelic characteristics can persist or reappear within a lineage whose ordinary racial nature is Demonic.
Dark Light should nevertheless be treated as a specialized inherited supernatural power rather than being classified as ordinary Holy Energy. Its characteristics arise from the extraordinary lineage of its wielder and do not establish that all descendants of Fallen Angels possess the same abilities.

### Holy Energy and Supernatural Races

#### Angels
Angels possess the strongest natural relationship with Holy Energy. It serves as their characteristic racial Energy, supports their supernatural abilities and provides the conditions necessary for their full strength within Heaven or an established Sacred territory.[1][3]
Their dependence upon Holy Energy is comparable to the dependence of Demons upon compatible infernal environments, although their powers possess opposing supernatural characteristics.

#### Demons
Demons are among the beings most vulnerable to Holy Energy. Their negative-aligned physiology is particularly incompatible with Sacred Light, making sufficiently powerful Holy attacks extremely dangerous.[4][7]
Holy Energy can also counter Demonic Miasma and suppress the powers used by Demons within a sanctified territory. The effectiveness of this interaction depends upon the potency of the Sacred power and the resistance of the affected Demon.[4]
Although exceptionally powerful Demons may survive particular Holy attacks, the fundamental vulnerability remains relevant even to beings possessing great physical strength and supernatural durability.

#### Vampires
Vampires possess a vulnerability to Sacred attributes, although the degree of that weakness depends upon their Bloodline and individual characteristics.[2]
The attack Haruna performed using ordinary Light did not possess the Sacred attributes necessary to exploit the ordinary Vampiric weakness. This distinction demonstrates that the vulnerability is not simply a reaction to illumination.[2]
The [[Snow Bloodline]] possesses significant resistance to Fire and Sacred attributes, granting its descendants greater protection against attacks that would otherwise be particularly dangerous to Vampires.[2]
Jeanne's former Vampiric physiology provides an especially clear demonstration of incompatibility. Her Saintly Soul retained a connection with Holy Energy, but the Sacred power associated with her former abilities caused physical pain and threatened to destroy her existence when used excessively.[9][4]

#### Werewolves
[[Werewolves]] are among the supernatural races traditionally threatened by Sacred powers associated with the Inquisition and other religious organizations.
However, their individual resistance varies considerably, particularly among powerful Royal Werewolves and supernatural beings possessing exceptional protective characteristics.
The vulnerabilities of Werewolves should not automatically be assumed to function identically to those of Demons or Vampires, since their racial physiology and supernatural Energy systems differ.

#### Saints
Saints are Humans recognized and empowered through the Biblical Heavenly system. Their supernatural abilities can include the declaration of Holy territories suitable for Angelic descent.[1]
The relationship between Saints and Holy Energy allows them to serve as intermediaries between Heaven and the Mortal World. Their abilities can create conditions under which Angels are capable of exerting their full strength despite the ordinary limitations of Earth's environment.[1]
The power available to an individual Saint depends upon their blessing, supernatural characteristics and the authorization required for the technique involved.

#### Gods
Gods possess [[Divine Energy]] through their supernatural existence, but that power is not automatically identical to Holy Energy.
A God associated with the positive side of Creation may possess powers that interact favorably with Sacred characteristics. However, the nature of their Divine Energy and the supernatural authority of their Divinities remain important distinctions.[3]
[[Hecate]], for example, can employ powerful Divine [[Magic]] without requiring every manifestation of her power to possess the Angelic Sacred attribute. Similarly, Gods associated with Death or Darkness may exercise powerful Divine authorities whose energetic characteristics belong to the negative side of the balance.

### Relationship with Other Energy Types

#### Holy Energy and Divine Energy
Holy Energy and [[Divine Energy]] are related through the [[Heavenly Father]]'s Divine nature but are fundamentally distinct in their ordinary characteristics.
Pure Divine Energy possesses no inherent attribute. A God gives that Energy the characteristics associated with their supernatural existence. Holy Energy, by comparison, already possesses a positive and Sacred attribute associated with Heaven and the Angelic Race.[3]
The Heavenly Father's role as the Divine source associated with Angels explains their relationship without making the two energies interchangeable.
A God can possess Divine Energy without being capable of generating Holy Light, while an Angel can possess Holy Energy without having undergone the transformation necessary to become a genuine God.

#### Holy Energy and Positivity
Holy Energy belongs to the positive side of Creation and derives its Sacred characteristics from that alignment. However, the fundamental Concept of Positivity represents a far broader supernatural authority than ordinary Angelic Energy.[3]
The positive side encompasses numerous concepts and attributed energies. Holy Energy represents one of its specialized expressions rather than the entirety of the positive spectrum.
A practitioner capable of producing Holy Light does not automatically possess unrestricted authority over Life, Creation, Beginning, Love or other positive Concepts.

#### Holy Energy and Negativity
Negativity represents the opposite fundamental side of the cosmic balance. Holy Energy's incompatibility with many negative-aligned beings arises from its positive Sacred characteristics.[3]
The relationship is particularly important in conflicts between Angels and Demons, where their characteristic energies possess opposing supernatural qualities.
However, the existence of exceptional beings capable of manipulating both sides demonstrates that these categories are not absolute restrictions applying to every possible supernatural physiology.

#### Holy Energy and Miasma
Miasma is the characteristic Demonic Energy associated with Hell and the negative side of Creation. Its properties include corruption, toxicity and supernatural deterioration, while Holy Energy possesses Sacred characteristics suited to purification and the destruction of certain Demonic powers.[4][7]
The energies also differ in their environmental behavior. Miasma possesses comparatively dense and heavy characteristics, whereas Sacred Light is light and free-flowing.[7]
The incompatibility between the two explains the harmful effects they can produce against members of the opposing race, although exceptionally powerful beings may possess the resistance necessary to survive contact.

#### Holy Energy and Ordinary Light
Ordinary Light is a neutral elemental phenomenon unless additional supernatural attributes are incorporated into it. A practitioner can possess Light Manipulation without the Sacred properties associated with Angels.[2]
Haruna demonstrates this distinction through her exceptional Light affinity despite originating from the Darkness-associated [[Otsuki Clan]].
Sacred Light differs because its positive Holy attribute produces supernatural effects against vulnerable beings. Its effects are therefore not reducible to illumination, brightness or the physical properties of ordinary Light.[2][5]

#### Holy Energy and Fire
Ordinary Fire and Holy Fire can produce superficially similar destructive effects, but their supernatural characteristics differ.
Fire is a conventional elemental power available through numerous racial and magical systems, while Holy Fire combines a fiery manifestation with the Sacred properties associated with Heavenly Energy.[4][2]
This distinction is important for Vampires, who may possess vulnerabilities to both Fire and the Sacred element even though the two are not identical.

#### Holy Energy and Faith Energy
Faith Energy is associated with supernatural power arising from belief, devotion and religious conviction. It can be utilized by members of the Inquisition and other individuals whose abilities are connected to their religious systems.
Holy Energy is the characteristic Sacred Energy associated with Angels and Heaven.
Although the two can interact through the [[Heavenly Father]] and his followers, their sources and conventional methods of utilization are not identical.
A Human possessing Faith Energy does not automatically become an Angel or acquire the ability to generate unrestricted Holy Light. Conversely, an Angel does not require the same cultivation methods employed by Human Faith practitioners to generate its ordinary racial Energy.
The distinction also applies to the specialized abilities of Saints. Their access to Holy techniques depends upon the Heavenly system rather than Faith alone universally granting the full range of Angelic powers.

#### Holy Energy and Onmyojutsu
Onmyojutsu is a supernatural discipline associated with Japanese Onmyoji and their relationship with the Divine powers of the [[Shinto Pantheon]].
Although some techniques may employ Sacred or Divine influences, Onmyojutsu is not synonymous with Angelic Holy Energy. Its characteristics depend upon the patron deities, supernatural techniques and Energy utilized by the practitioner.
The existence of sacred techniques within different Pantheons does not automatically make their energetic foundations identical to those of the Biblical Heavenly system.

#### Holy Energy and Attributeless Energy
Attributeless Energy represents a more fundamental source from which attributed supernatural energies can arise.
Holy Energy possesses an established Sacred characteristic, placing it among the attributed energy systems rather than identifying it with the original unaltered foundation of supernatural Energy.[3][12]
Beings possessing exceptional understanding of Energy may manipulate or reproduce multiple attributed forms without belonging naturally to the races most closely associated with them.
[[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]] demonstrates the ability to distinguish Holy Energy from other supernatural powers, while [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Melida Charlotte Elderblood]] possess exceptionally advanced capabilities involving the manipulation of Energy beyond ordinary racial limitations.[3][12]
Such capabilities do not establish that every ordinary Angel can independently generate Attributeless Energy.

### Known Users

#### The Heavenly Father
[[Heavenly Father]] is the Divine being most directly associated with Holy Energy through his creation of Heaven and the Angelic Race.
His supernatural abilities include the creation of Sacred environments and the use of exceptionally powerful Heavenly Judgments. During the Demonic War, his direct intervention devastated large portions of the Demonic army through an enormous attack involving Holy Light.[6]
His authorization is also necessary for certain Judgment techniques employed by Saints, as demonstrated through Jeanne's use of Judgment of Israel.[4]

#### The Angelic Race
- Michael: One of the highest-ranking Angels and a principal commander of the Heavenly forces. His Sacred power and weapons represent the exceptional combat capabilities of the original Heavenly hierarchy.
- [[Gabriel]]: An original member of the Heavenly hierarchy whose powers were associated with the Sacred characteristics of the Angelic Race before his subsequent changes.
- [[Ariel Elderblood]]: A Heavenly Virtue originally associated with Generosity and the Sacred powers of Heaven. Her development into a Dragon did not eliminate the significance of her inherited Angelic characteristics.
- [[Azrael]]: The Angel of Death, possessing the supernatural characteristics associated with the Heavenly Race alongside abilities specific to his role.
- [[Uriel]]: A powerful member of the Heavenly hierarchy associated with its characteristic Sacred powers.
- [[Cassiel]]: An Angel belonging to the Biblical Heavenly system and possessing its supernatural characteristics.
- Seraphim: The elite ranks of the Heavenly Race, possessing exceptional mastery over Sacred abilities and specialized Heavenly techniques.
- Angels: The wider race naturally associated with the generation and manipulation of Holy Energy.[1][3]

#### Saints
- [[Jeanne Elderblood]]: A former Saint who retained access to Heavenly powers after becoming a Vampire. She invoked Judgment of Israel through the [[Heavenly Father]]'s authorization and later acquired Holy Light as an intrinsic Draconic affinity.[9][4][5]
- Saints of the Heavenly Father: Humans recognized by the Biblical Heavenly system who can declare Holy territories suitable for Angelic descent.[1]

#### Exceptional Users
- [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]]: An exceptionally knowledgeable Goddess capable of generating and distinguishing Holy Energy alongside Miasma, Mana, [[Natural Energy]] and neutral [[Divine Energy]].[3]
- [[Diablo]]: An Ancestral Demon who temporarily acquired Angelic Holy Light through a hybrid transformation incorporating ordinarily incompatible supernatural characteristics.[11][7]
- [[Lily Elderblood]]: Possesses a specialized inherited Dark Light ability associated with the union of Lilith's Demonic lineage and [[Lucifer]]'s former Angelic characteristics.
- [[Victor Elderblood]]: A supernatural being possessing exceptional Energy Manipulation and the ability to interact with attributed energies beyond ordinary racial limitations.[12]
- [[Melida Charlotte Elderblood]]: Possesses extraordinary understanding and manipulation of supernatural Energy, including the ability to interact with multiple attributed energy systems through her exceptional innate capabilities.[12]

### Limitations and Weaknesses

#### Environmental Dependence
High-ranking Angels require a compatible Holy environment to maintain their full supernatural strength. Without an appropriate territory, their powers gradually weaken when operating in unsuitable locations.[1]
The limitation can be overcome through an established Sacred territory, including Churches and regions created through Territory Declaration.

#### Racial Incompatibility
Holy Energy is particularly harmful to certain negatively aligned supernatural beings. The incompatibility may prevent them from utilizing Sacred powers safely even when they retain a connection to the Heavenly system.[9][4]
Jeanne's former Vampiric body demonstrates this danger. Her continued possession of Saintly abilities did not eliminate the physical consequences of invoking Sacred power, and the [[Heavenly Father]] warned that excessive use could erase her existence.[4]

#### Power and Resistance
Holy Energy is not universally irresistible. Exceptionally powerful beings may possess enough resistance to survive attacks that would destroy weaker members of their race.
The [[Heavenly Father]]'s Judgment inflicted enormous damage upon the Demonic forces, but certain powerful opponents survived through extraordinary durability or defensive techniques.[6]
Similarly, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s exceptional Bloodline provided substantial resistance to Sacred attributes that would otherwise pose considerable danger to ordinary Vampires.[2]

#### Energy Expenditure
The effectiveness of Holy techniques depends partly upon the Energy available to the practitioner. Particularly powerful Judgments require substantial supernatural resources and cannot automatically be reproduced by ordinary Angels or Saints.[6]
Even when the required technique is available, the user must possess sufficient control and physical compatibility to endure its operation.

#### Authorization Requirements
Certain Heavenly techniques cannot be invoked solely through the possession of Holy Energy. The Heavenly Judgments represent specialized powers associated with the [[Heavenly Father]]'s authority and may require his permission.[4][8]
Jeanne's use of Judgment of Israel depended upon authorization rather than her Saintly characteristics alone guaranteeing unrestricted access.

#### Limited Conceptual Authority
Holy Energy does not independently grant possession of the complete Concept of Holiness or every positive-aligned Divinity.
Angels may possess specialized supernatural authorities associated with their roles, particularly among the highest ranks, but these abilities should be distinguished from the general properties of the Holy Energy supporting their existence.

- Angelic Environmental Dependence: High-ranking Angels require Holy environments to exercise their full power, while unsuitable surroundings gradually weaken them.[1]

- Judgment of Israel: Jeanne established an enormous circle of golden Holy Fire that destroyed Demonic magical circles, dispersed Miasma and weakened Demons and Vampires throughout the affected territory.[4]
- Sacred Energy Incompatibility: Jeanne's Vampiric body suffered considerable strain when she invoked Judgment of Israel, and the Heavenly Father warned that excessive use could erase her existence.[4]
- Light and Sacred Distinction: Haruna's ordinary Light affinity did not possess the Sacred characteristic responsible for the ordinary Vampiric weakness to Holy Energy.[2]
- Heavenly Judgment: The Heavenly Father unleashed massive beams of Sacred Light across the Demonic War battlefield, destroying numerous powerful Demons and significantly changing the course of the conflict.[6]
- Resistance to Heavenly Judgment: Certain exceptionally powerful beings survived the large-scale Holy attack, including Sloth, whose concentrated Miasma prevented most of the Light from reaching her.[6]
- Hybrid Holy Energy: [[Diablo]] obtained the ability to manipulate both Holy Light and Demonic Miasma through his transformation into a hybrid supernatural existence.[11]
- Holy and Demonic Energetic Contrast: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] recognized the difference between the dense, heavy qualities of Miasma and the light, free-flowing properties of Sacred Light while discussing communication between Heaven and Hell.[7]
- Jeanne's Holy Light Affinity: Jeanne manifested Holy Light after becoming a Dragon, demonstrating a Sacred power sufficiently dangerous that Lilith and Lily maintained their distance until she dismissed it.[5]
- Energy Classification: Albedo demonstrated Holy Energy alongside Miasma, [[Natural Energy]], Mana and neutral [[Divine Energy]], distinguishing their characteristics and explaining their relationships with the wider supernatural balance.[3]
- Inherited Heavenly Characteristics: [[Ariella Victoria Elderblood]], daughter of Victor and Ariel, inherited extraordinary Angelic characteristics associated with the Seven Heavenly Virtues alongside traces of her father's Creation authority.[13]
