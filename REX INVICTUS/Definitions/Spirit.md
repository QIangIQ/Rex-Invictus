Spirits are a broad class of supernatural beings whose existence is fundamentally spiritual rather than biological. Unlike conventional mortal races whose Souls inhabit physical bodies, many Spirits are essentially powerful Souls or manifestations of natural forces that can exist independently of mortal flesh. Their nature varies enormously: some are connected to elements or aspects of Nature, some can evolve into much higher spiritual existences, and beings such as Gods and Demons possess existences closely related to Spirits despite having developed into distinct supernatural races.[1][2]
The term Spirit should not be treated as synonymous with every ordinary Soul. Chapter 861 specifically describes Nature Spirits as exceptionally powerful Souls whose spiritual essence is sufficiently fundamental that even after being transformed into another species, a tiny remnant of that essence can continue influencing the individual's Soul Core and descendants.[2] Spirits can also emerge through evolution. In Chapter 1079, [[Alexios Alioth]] was beginning to become a Time Elemental after prolonged exposure to pure Time Energy; [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explained that if the process continued, Alexios would cease being Human and become a Spirit-like existence completely synchronized with Time.[3]
Higher forms of Spirits exist much closer to fundamental forces of Creation than ordinary mortal species. Becoming such an existence links the Spirit to the wider structure of Creation and Nature, placing limitations and responsibilities upon its existence comparable in principle to those placed upon [[World Trees]].[3]

### Nature
Spirits differ fundamentally from physical races because their primary existence is their Soul or spiritual essence. Chapter 861 describes Nature Spirits as being "essentially Souls"—but extremely powerful Souls whose characteristics can persist even after a racial transformation.[2]
This distinction becomes particularly important when Spirits acquire physical bodies. A sufficiently advanced Spirit can possess a body belonging to another race while retaining a completely separate spiritual identity. [[Jeanne Elderblood]] explains that she originally existed as a Spirit before receiving a physical body through the power of the [[Heavenly Father]]. She was subsequently transformed into a Noble Vampire by [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]], yet her true Spirit essence remained independent from her Vampire body.[4]
Jeanne explicitly explains that, as a High-Level Spirit, she can assume another Race without her spiritual essence merging with that physical Race. Her body can therefore become Human, Vampire or another species while her underlying spiritual nature remains distinct.[4]
This characteristic also affects reproduction. A High-Level Spirit possessing a physical racial body does not automatically transmit its full spiritual nature to biological descendants. Jeanne explains that her children would inherit characteristics from her physical Race rather than the [[Primordial Energy]] and spiritual characteristics bound specifically to her Soul.[4]

### Spiritual Essence and the Soul Core
A Spirit's original nature can remain embedded within the Core of the Soul even after the individual becomes another species. This became important in understanding both [[Morgana Elderblood]] and the [[Fulger Clan]].
When an individual changes species, characteristics from the original species can remain in the Soul Core. In extreme spiritual species, even a tiny surviving portion can exert enormous influence. Chapter 861 explains that even approximately 1% of a Nature Spirit's original essence was enough to continue influencing the entire Core of a being despite transformation through a Vampire Progenitor.[2]
This effect is much more pronounced for spiritually powerful species than for ordinary physical races. [[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]] originated as a Spirit before becoming a Noble Vampire, and her residual Spirit essence became an inherited characteristic of the Fulger lineage. Even thousands of years later, that spiritual foundation continued influencing descendants such as Natashia Elderblood Fulger and [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]].[5][6][2]
Similarly, Morgana Elderblood retained characteristics of her original Demonic existence after becoming a Noble Vampire because Demons themselves possess an extremely spiritual nature.[1][2]

### Evolution
Spirits possess their own form of spiritual evolution. Chapter 861 establishes that a Nature Spirit reaching a sufficiently high level of power naturally begins undergoing the process of becoming a High-Level Spirit.[2]
This evolution can occur even when the original Spirit has already changed Race. Carmila's transformation into a Noble Vampire did not completely erase her Spirit essence; consequently, descendants carrying that inherited spiritual component could eventually reach a level where the dormant Spirit nature attempted to evolve again.[2]
This became a serious problem for Clan Fulger. Because their ancestral Spirit characteristics remained within their Soul Cores while their primary species had become Noble Vampire, sufficiently powerful members risked undergoing an uncontrolled spiritual evolution that their current existence was not naturally designed to support. Victoria eventually learned that their family had effectively possessed an inherited limit: reaching sufficient strength could trigger the evolution toward a High-Level Spirit and potentially result in death if the process could not be stabilized.[2]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood greatly accelerated this process within Natashia and Sasha. His Blood had continuously nourished their potential, and after Victor merged with [[Roxanne Elderblood]] and later became a Blood Dragon, the nourishment increasingly affected their Souls rather than merely their Vampire bodies. Combined with the Tower of Nightmare strengthening their spiritual foundations, the dormant Spirit characteristics within the Fulger lineage began developing much more rapidly.[2]

### Connection to Creation
Becoming a sufficiently developed Spirit carries consequences beyond simply gaining greater supernatural power. Chapter 1079 explains that a Spirit becomes linked to all of Creation and effectively becomes part of Nature itself.[3]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] compares this condition to the existence of the [[World Trees]]. A Spirit connected in this manner no longer possesses the unrestricted freedom of an ordinary mortal being. Instead, it acquires a role within the machinery of Creation and is expected to perform that role according to the natural order.[3]
This was the primary reason Victor prevented Alexios from completing his transformation into a Time Spirit. Although the transformation would have given Alexios extraordinary affinity with Time, Victor understood that Alexios valued serving Vlad through his own free choice. Becoming a Spirit would have forced him into a more restrictive existential position tied to Creation itself.[3]
The comparison to World Trees does not mean Spirits and World Trees are the same species. Rather, both can function as spiritually integrated parts of the cosmic ecosystem whose existence carries specific duties toward Creation.[3]

### Types of Spirits
Spirit is an extremely broad classification. The novel has demonstrated or described several different spiritual states.

#### Nature Spirits
Nature Spirits are powerful spiritual beings whose existence is fundamentally composed of a powerful Soul associated with Nature or a natural phenomenon. Chapter 861 describes Nature Spirits as essentially very strong Souls.[2]
Their original spiritual essence can remain within the Soul Core even after their species is changed, and sufficiently developed Nature Spirits can naturally evolve into High-Level Spirits.[2]
[[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]] belonged to this general category before becoming a Noble Vampire, and the residual characteristics of her spiritual existence continued influencing the [[Fulger Bloodline]] thousands of years after her transformation.[2]
The [[Ahishahu]] discovered during the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s expansion are another closely related example of naturally occurring spiritual life. They are described as manifestations of Nature itself that took shape, with Rose and Eleonor comparing their nature to Divine or Nature Spirits.[7]

#### Elemental Spirits
Elemental Spirits are Spirits whose existence becomes fundamentally synchronized with a specific element or force. Rather than merely manipulating an element externally, becoming an Elemental means becoming completely attuned to that force.[3]
High-Level Elemental Spirits are powerful enough to be discussed alongside Gods and other exceptional spiritual existences. When discussing Jeanne's compatibility with other species, [[Scathach]] specifically mentions High-Level Elemental Spirits as beings existing within the same broad spiritual tier.[8]
Known or theoretical varieties include Spirits associated with Lightning, Ice and Time.

##### Time Spirits
A Time Spirit is an Elemental Spirit completely synchronized with Time. Alexios nearly evolved into one after his Soul continuously absorbed Natalia's extraordinarily pure Time Energy.[3]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] stopped the process before completion, but his analysis revealed several characteristics that such a Spirit would possess. Because the Spirit would effectively perceive Time directly, it could access the past contained within the [[Akashic Records]] while receiving limited information from the future.[3]
The future remains changeable under the rules of the Universe, preventing unrestricted knowledge of future events. A Time Spirit would consequently perceive only a short distance into the future while potentially observing the entirety of the already-recorded past.[3]

#### High-Level Spirits
High-Level Spirits are advanced Spirits whose spiritual existence has evolved far beyond ordinary Spirit states. Nature Spirits can naturally begin evolving toward this state after accumulating sufficient power.[2]
Their Souls and spiritual characteristics are sufficiently developed that they can interact with physical racial systems in ways ordinary beings cannot. Jeanne states that a High-Level Spirit can assume another Race while keeping its spiritual essence separate from the body belonging to that Race.[4]
The physical body therefore functions more like an additional racial vessel rather than replacing the Spirit's underlying existence.
Gods are closely connected to this classification. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly explains that if the Concept were removed from a God, what remained would essentially be a High-Level Spirit. He further describes Gods as technically being High-Level Spirits, which helps explain their unusual compatibility with physical species when producing offspring.[1]
This does not mean every High-Level Spirit is a God. Godhood requires additional characteristics—most importantly a Concept and Divinity—while a High-Level Spirit represents the spiritual foundation beneath that Divine structure.[1]

#### Primordial/Highest-Level Spirits
Certain Spirits exist substantially above conventional High-Level Spirits. [[Jeanne Elderblood]] is one of the clearest examples.
Jeanne serves as the Guardian of [[The Universal Tree]], one of the [[Primordial Entities]], and her unique Energy is bound directly to her Soul. Her position is stated to be considerably higher and more flexible than the ordinary Guardian relationship between [[Diablo|Big Guy]] and Roxanne.[4]
[[Scathach]] later refers to Jeanne as an exceptionally high-order Spirit whose existence exceeds that of ordinary Gods and most conventional High-Level Spirits.[8]
This extraordinary state should not be generalized to Spirits as a race. Jeanne's characteristics originate from her unique relationship with the Universal Tree and from an existence predating many of the supernatural systems used by later civilizations.

#### Evil Spirits
Evil Spirit is used for spiritual existences associated with the negative side of the supernatural spectrum. Demons are repeatedly compared with this category, although the relationship should be described carefully.
Chapter 637 states that Demons are similar to Evil Spirits in essence. Demons originate from the negative or "bad" portion of mortal Souls sent to Hell and consequently possess a primarily spiritual rather than mortal physical nature.[1]
Chapter 861 similarly calls Demons essentially malevolent Spirits when explaining why Morgana's Demonic characteristics remained so strongly embedded within her Soul after she became a Noble Vampire.[2]
However, Demons possess their own racial systems, Hell connections, Miasma and resurrection mechanics and should therefore remain a distinct species rather than simply being categorized as ordinary Spirits.
[[Asmodeus]] is a particularly ancient example. He is referred to as an Ancient Spirit and as an exceptionally high-ranking Evil Spirit despite also being one of Hell's Demons.[9]

### Relationship with Gods
Main article: Gods
The relationship between Spirits and Gods is considerably closer than the relationship between Gods and ordinary physical species.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly states that Gods are technically High-Level Spirits. A God's Concept and Divinity transform that spiritual foundation into a Divine existence; if that Concept were removed, what remained would be a High-Level Spirit.[1]
This spiritual nature explains several unusual characteristics of Gods. They do not possess conventional "mortal flesh" in the same manner as Humans or ordinary supernatural races, and their spiritual composition allows them to reproduce with otherwise incompatible physical species with fewer genetic problems than would normally occur.[10][1]
[[Viviane]] is one example. As the daughter of a God and a Fairy, she could exist as a stable Perfect Hybrid largely because her Divine parent's Godly nature was fundamentally closer to that of a High-Level Spirit than that of a conventional biological species.[1]
Gods should nevertheless not simply be listed as another ordinary Spirit subtype. Their Concepts, Divinities, [[Divine Souls]] and Pantheon systems produce an existence substantially different from Spirits that have not ascended into Godhood.

### Relationship with Demons
Main article: Demons
Demons possess a spiritual existence comparable to Evil Spirits. They originate from the negative portions of mortal Souls and lack conventional mortal flesh unless they acquire a body through some external process.[1]
This is why [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] compares Gods and Demons despite their enormous differences: both are fundamentally spiritual existences rather than ordinary biological organisms. Gods resemble High-Level Spirits empowered through Concepts, while Demons resemble Evil Spirits produced from incomplete negative Souls.[1]
The distinction becomes particularly important regarding Concepts. A normal Demon cannot naturally contain a Concept because its Soul is incomplete. Jeanne explains that a Demon would first require a complete Soul before it could properly contain such authority.[1]
Morgana eventually became an exception after acquiring a complete physical and spiritual existence through her transformation into a Noble Vampire.[1]

### Relationship with World Trees
Main article: [[World Trees]]
Spirits and World Trees are separate forms of life but share several metaphysical characteristics.
A sufficiently developed Spirit becomes linked to Creation and Nature and acquires an assigned role within the natural order. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly compares this condition to World Trees when explaining why he prevented Alexios from becoming a Time Spirit.[3]
Spirit Guardians can also be directly associated with World Trees. [[Diablo|Big Guy]] serves as Roxanne's Guardian, while [[Jeanne Elderblood]] occupies a substantially greater version of the same position as Guardian of the Universal Tree.[4]
Jeanne remains connected to the Universal Tree even while possessing an independent physical body, demonstrating that a Spirit's metaphysical relationship can survive transformations of its physical Race.[4]

### Physical Incarnation
A powerful Spirit is capable of possessing a physical racial existence without necessarily ceasing to be a Spirit.
Jeanne is the clearest example. Her original existence was spiritual, but the [[Heavenly Father]] provided her with a physical body. Vlad later transformed that physical existence into a Noble Vampire.[4]
Despite those changes, Jeanne explains that her true spiritual essence remained bound to her Soul and did not mix with the Race of her physical body. She could therefore possess a Vampire body while remaining a High-Level Spirit at her fundamental spiritual level.[4]
The same principle means that such a being should not automatically be classified as a conventional Hybrid. The physical Race and spiritual existence are not being combined into a single mixed biological species; rather, the Spirit is operating through a body possessing its own Race.[4]
A Spirit's descendants likewise inherit the characteristics of the physical body rather than automatically inheriting the Spirit's full metaphysical nature. Jeanne states that her children would inherit her current physical lineage but not the unique [[Primordial Energy]] bound specifically to her Spirit Soul.[4]
Carmila demonstrates that this separation is not always perfect after a Progenitor-level racial transformation. Although she became a Noble Vampire, approximately one percent of her Spirit essence remained embedded within her Soul Core, eventually influencing the entire Fulger lineage.[2]

### Powers and Abilities
Spirit abilities vary enormously according to the individual's type, associated natural force, degree of evolution and whether the Spirit possesses a separate physical Race. The following abilities are limited to characteristics directly established for Spirits rather than extrapolating individual feats from Jeanne, Carmila or other exceptional beings to the entire classification.

#### Standard Spirit Abilities
- Spiritual Physiology: Spirits fundamentally exist as spiritual beings rather than conventional biological organisms. Nature Spirits are described as essentially extremely powerful Souls, meaning their primary existence is spiritual instead of physical.[2]
- Incorporeality: Spirits whose existence is not currently bound to a physical body can exist independently as Souls rather than requiring conventional flesh. Carmila continued existing as a Soul long after the death of her Vampire body.[5][6]
- Non-Physical Existence: A Spirit's core identity exists within its spiritual essence. Destroying or changing a physical vessel does not necessarily erase the original Spirit characteristics contained within the Soul.[4][2]
- Soul Preservation: Powerful Spirit essence can survive major racial changes. Even after Carmila became a Noble Vampire, a residual portion of her original Spirit nature remained within her Soul Core and continued influencing her descendants.[2]
- Energy Manipulation: Spirits associated with a natural phenomenon possess an extremely intimate relationship with the corresponding Energy or force. Their interaction is deeper than merely learning an elemental spell because the Spirit's existence itself can become synchronized with that phenomenon.[3]
- Racial Transformation (High-Level Spirits): A High-Level Spirit can assume another Race while retaining its original spiritual essence. Jeanne possessed a physical body and subsequently became a Noble Vampire without her High-Level Spirit essence merging into or being replaced by her Vampire physiology.[4]
- Power Separation (Spiritual/Physical): The abilities belonging specifically to a High-Level Spirit's Soul can remain isolated from those of its physical Race. Jeanne's [[Primordial Energy]] remained bound exclusively to her Soul rather than becoming a hereditary characteristic of her Vampire body.[4]
- Resistance to Racial Alteration: Spirit essence can remain present despite supernatural transformations powerful enough to alter both body and Soul. Carmila's Spirit nature persisted as a small but influential component even after transformation through a Vampire Progenitor.[2]

#### Nature Spirit Abilities
- Nature Embodiment: Advanced Spirits can become part of Nature and the greater structure of Creation rather than merely living within it. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explains that becoming a Spirit would have linked Alexios to Creation in a manner comparable to a World Tree.[3]
- Creation Connection: Spirits that reach this state become metaphysically linked with Creation and acquire a role within its functioning.[3]
- Spiritual Evolution: Nature Spirits naturally possess the potential to evolve into High-Level Spirits after reaching sufficient strength.[2]
- Soul Empowerment: Nature Spirits possess exceptionally strong Souls. Their spiritual composition is powerful enough that a small amount of surviving essence can influence another Race's entire Soul Core.[2]

#### Elemental Spirit Abilities
- Elemental Embodiment: An Elemental Spirit becomes completely synchronized with its associated element or natural force rather than merely manipulating it externally. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] describes Alexios' incomplete transformation as becoming completely "in tune" with Time itself.[3]
- Elemental Manipulation: Elemental Spirits possess natural control and affinity with the force constituting their spiritual existence. The exact abilities depend upon the Spirit's element and level of evolution.[3]
- Elemental Perception: Becoming completely synchronized with an element can fundamentally alter how the Spirit perceives reality through that force.[3]

##### Time Spirit Abilities
- Time Manipulation: A completed Time Spirit would possess an extraordinarily intimate relationship with Time because its Soul would become fully synchronized with Time Energy itself.[3]
- Retrocognition: A Time Spirit can theoretically observe the recorded past through the [[Akashic Records]]. Because past events have already been written into the Records, the entire past would be available to its temporal perception.[3]
- Precognition: A Time Spirit can perceive a limited distance into the future. Unlike the past, the future remains mutable; consequently, the Spirit would only be capable of observing approximately several seconds ahead rather than gaining unrestricted knowledge of future events.[3]
- Cosmic Awareness (Temporal): [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] describes the awareness of a completed Time Spirit as approaching omniscience specifically in relation to Time, although the mutable nature of the future prevents true omniscience.[3]

#### High-Level Spirit Abilities
- Advanced Spiritual Physiology: High-Level Spirits possess spiritual existences substantially superior to ordinary Nature Spirits and can interact with biological Race systems without losing their fundamental identity.[4][2]
- Racial Incarnation: High-Level Spirits can assume physical [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] while keeping their Spirit essence separate from the physical body.[4]
- Non-Hybrid Physiology: Assuming another Race does not necessarily turn a High-Level Spirit into a biological Hybrid because the spiritual essence and physical racial body remain distinct systems.[4]
- Hereditary Restriction: Powers bound specifically to the Spirit's Soul are not automatically inherited by children produced through its physical Race. Jeanne explicitly states that descendants would inherit her physical lineage rather than her unique spiritual Energy.[4]

#### Spirit-Derived Divine Abilities
These abilities should not be treated as ordinary Spirit powers, but demonstrate what a Spirit-like existence can become through further evolution.
- Divinity (Gods): Gods are technically High-Level Spirits whose existence has become associated with Concepts and Divinity. Removing the Concept from a God would leave behind a High-Level Spirit.[1]
- Conceptual Manipulation (Gods): Concepts belong to the Divine layer placed above the underlying High-Level Spirit existence and are therefore not a baseline ability of ordinary Spirits.[1]

#### Evil Spirit-Related Abilities
The following characteristics principally apply to Demons and other Evil Spirit-like entities rather than every Spirit.
- Negative Energy Manipulation: Evil Spirit-like beings can possess strong affinities with negative forms of Energy. Demons, for example, are spiritual beings formed from the negative portions of mortal Souls and naturally wield Miasma.[1]
- Spiritual Persistence: Demons can survive bodily destruction and return to the Hell connected to their Souls, although this is a property of Demonic existence and its connection to Hell rather than a confirmed universal Spirit ability.[1]

### Weaknesses
- Soul Manipulation: Because a Spirit's fundamental existence is its Soul or spiritual essence, abilities capable of directly damaging, altering, consuming or destroying Souls are particularly dangerous to them.[2]
- Soul Destruction: The destruction of a Spirit's true spiritual foundation can threaten the existence itself rather than merely destroying an external physical body.
- Existential Role (Advanced Spirits): Becoming a sufficiently developed Spirit can reduce personal freedom. Chapter 1079 explains that a Spirit linked to Creation becomes part of Nature and is expected to perform its assigned role according to the order of Creation.[3]
- Physical Vessel Weaknesses: A Spirit possessing the physical body of another Race is subject to the normal properties and vulnerabilities of that body. The Spirit essence remains separate, but the vessel itself can still be injured or destroyed according to the rules of its physical Race.[4]
- Unstable Spiritual Evolution: A dormant Spirit essence attempting to evolve within a body or Soul belonging primarily to another Race can become dangerous. Clan Fulger's inherited Nature Spirit characteristics created a point at which continued growth risked triggering a High-Level Spirit evolution that could kill an unprepared descendant.[2]
- Elemental Transformation (Loss of Individual Freedom): Complete synchronization with a fundamental force can significantly alter the individual's existence and mentality. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] considered Alexios becoming a Time Spirit undesirable precisely because the transformation would turn him into a restrictive existence bound to Creation.[3]

### Known Spirits

#### Jeanne Elderblood
[[Jeanne Elderblood]] is one of the highest-order Spirits presently known. She is the Guardian of [[The Universal Tree]] and possesses a unique spiritual connection with the Primordial Entity responsible for maintaining Life, reincarnation and the [[World Trees]] throughout existence.[4]
Jeanne later acquired a physical body through the [[Heavenly Father]] and was subsequently transformed into a Noble Vampire by Vlad. Neither transformation replaced her actual Spirit essence.[4]
Her unique [[Primordial Energy]] is bound specifically to her Soul and cannot normally be inherited by descendants through her physical Race.[4]

#### Big Guy
[[Diablo|Big Guy]] is the Guardian associated with [[Roxanne Elderblood]]. Jeanne directly compares her own existence to Big Guy's, explaining that both serve as Guardians of [[World Trees]], although Jeanne's position as Guardian of the Universal Tree is substantially higher and grants considerably greater autonomy.[4]

#### Carmila Fulger Elderblood
[[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]] was originally a Spirit before becoming a Noble Vampire and founding the supernatural lineage that would become Clan Fulger.[4][2]
Her original Spirit essence was never completely erased by her Vampire transformation. A small residual component remained within her Soul and became deeply influential within the [[Fulger Bloodline]].[2]
This residual Spirit nature was responsible for the hidden evolutionary problem later discovered among sufficiently powerful Fulger descendants.[2]

#### Asmodeus
[[Asmodeus]] is primarily classified as a Demon, but his existence has also been described using high-level Spirit terminology. He has been referred to as an exceptionally high-ranking Evil Spirit and as an Ancient Spirit.[9]
His case demonstrates the extremely close relationship between powerful Demons and the broader metaphysical category of Spirits without requiring all Demons to be classified as ordinary Spirits.

### Spirit-Related Beings

#### Gods
Gods are technically High-Level Spirits whose existences possess Concepts and Divinity. Their Divine nature places them far beyond ordinary Spirits, but the High-Level Spirit serves as the underlying spiritual foundation of their existence.[1]

#### Demons
Demons are described as beings similar in essence to Evil Spirits. They originate from incomplete negative portions of mortal Souls and possess spiritual bodies rather than conventional mortal flesh.[1][2]

#### Ahishahu
[[Ahishahu]] are enormous beings described as manifestations of Nature itself that gained form. During the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s campaign on another world, Rose and Eleonor independently compared them with Divine or Nature Spirits.[7]
The civilization inhabiting their planet had captured several Ahishahu and used the beings as enormous living Energy sources until the Elderblood forces disrupted the systems suppressing them.[7]

#### World Trees
[[World Trees]] are not Spirits, but sufficiently developed Spirits share important metaphysical similarities with them. Both can become directly connected to the functioning of Creation and possess defined roles within Nature.[3]
