Monsters are artificial supernatural creatures native to the modern ecosystem of Nightingale. For most of Vampire history, the Monsters and the humanoid beings commanding them were collectively treated as Nightingale's indigenous population. This interpretation was ultimately revealed to be incorrect. After consuming [[Ikor-Kar-VI]] and obtaining access to his knowledge, [[Victor Elderblood]] learned that the actual indigenous people of Nightingale were the humanoid population later referred to as the Natives, while the creatures Vampires had spent millennia calling Monsters were artificial experiments created by combining Ikor's Divinity, Nightingale's Natives and Noble Vampires.[1]
The distinction is particularly important regarding the beings historically called Alphas. Alphas are not a superior Monster species. They are members of Nightingale's native humanoid population who held positions of military and familial authority. Only adult males who became leaders of families could obtain the title of Alpha and receive authority to command the Monster armies against [[WarFall]].[1] Monsters were therefore closer to purpose-built supernatural biological weapons than a naturally developed civilization of their own.
The Monsters possess extraordinarily adaptable biology, immense physical characteristics, toxic anti-Vampire traits and, among many common classes, an abnormal form of resurrection that allows them to reconstruct themselves after apparently fatal destruction. Their physiology was subsequently incorporated into Clan Adrasteia through a dangerous ancient ritual, producing the Vampire-Monster hybrids whose unique Monster-derived power allows them to permanently kill the otherwise resurrecting creatures.[2][3]

### Origin
For thousands of years, Vampires knew extremely little about the true origin of the creatures living beyond the territories they controlled on [[Nightingale]]. The common understanding was that the Monsters were simply the indigenous creatures of the planet and that the intelligent humanoids referred to as Alphas represented an advanced Monster class. Clan Adrasteia, despite possessing more information about Monster anatomy than virtually any other Vampire family, likewise lacked a complete understanding of the civilization and powers controlling them.[4][5]
This misunderstanding persisted because the western territories were locked in continuous warfare. The forces operating from the wilderness attacked [[WarFall]] repeatedly, while Clan Adrasteia responded by killing and harvesting Monster bodies for armor, weapons and research. Contact with the actual native settlements was uncommon and generally occurred through hostile Alpha commanders accompanied by Monster armies.[4][6]
The truth was uncovered only after the defeat of the Elder Gods. Once [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] consumed [[Ikor-Kar-VI]], he inherited knowledge concerning the experiments performed throughout Nightingale. He subsequently revealed that the Monsters were artificial creations produced through a combination of three primary components: Ikor's Divinity, biological material from Nightingale's Natives and Noble Vampire characteristics.[1]
This revelation also means that the Monsters in their known form cannot simply be described as the people who inhabited Nightingale before [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] arrived. The Natives were the original inhabitants. The Monsters were experimental organisms created through processes that included Noble Vampire material and were subsequently developed into increasingly specialized anti-Vampire weapons.[1]

### Nightingale Natives and the Alphas
The [[Nightingale]] Natives are a separate humanoid population and should not be classified as Monsters. They possess their own families, settlements, laws, technology, reproductive population and social organization. During the long war against the Vampires, the military leaders among them were referred to as Alphas.[7][1]
Chapter 886 reveals that the title was restricted rather than biological: only adult men who became leaders of families could obtain the title of Alpha and gain the right to command Monsters in attacks against [[WarFall]].[1] The Alpha title should therefore be understood as a leadership and military rank within the native population rather than a Monster evolutionary stage.
This explains several apparent contradictions in the early WarFall chapters. Alpha commanders are humanoid, capable of complex speech, strategy, family attachment, inheritance laws and technological operation, while ordinary Monsters behave much more like controlled biological weapons. Alphas themselves even refer to the Monsters as "abominations" and do not consider themselves part of the same species.[8][5]
Ken and Kal, consequently, are Nightingale Natives rather than notable members of the Monster species. When their father died, Ken inherited leadership of the village according to native custom and began preparing his younger brother Kal as a potential successor.[7]

#### Native Technology
The advanced technology seen controlling the Monsters belongs to the Natives and their Alpha commanders rather than to the largely animalistic Monsters themselves. Native settlements possess surveillance devices capable of remotely observing Vampire expeditions, giving commanders information about the movements of Clan Adrasteia and other invaders.[9]
They also possess an unusual fusion of technological and supernatural engineering. During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s expedition, Ken operated a futuristic yet visually archaic control system connected to containment chambers holding specialized Monsters. Through this system he could release specific engineered Types according to his authorization level, including Types 4, 5 and 6, while Type 7 required the authority of a Commander.[10][7]

### Nature and Biology
Monsters display enormous biological variation. Their bodies range from comparatively small humanoid Predators to enormous Behemoths and [[Titans]] exceeding fifty meters in height. Some are terrestrial quadrupeds, others are gigantic humanoids or arthropod-like organisms, while several classes possess wings and reptilian characteristics.[6][10][3]
Despite their extreme differences in appearance, several biological characteristics recur throughout the artificial Monster population. Their bodies are highly compatible with supernatural modification, many common specimens possess powerful regenerative and resurrective mechanisms, and their physiology has gradually developed traits particularly effective against Vampires.[2][3]
The race's genetic adaptability is especially abnormal. During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s examination of Clan Adrasteia, it is explained that Monster genes possess a degree of adaptability surpassing even Human genetics. This allows their biological material to merge unusually well with Vampire Blood, which made the creation of stable Vampire-Monster hybrids possible despite the normally incompatible nature of supernatural species.[3]
This adaptability should not be confused with instantaneous combat adaptation. The statement concerns the flexibility and compatibility of Monster genetics rather than every Monster spontaneously developing immunity to attacks during battle.

### Anti-Vampire Evolution
Monsters have spent thousands of years fighting the Vampires occupying [[Nightingale]]. During this period, the creatures developed increasingly specialized characteristics intended to counter Vampire physiology.[2]
One of the most significant is a supernatural Corruption transmitted through Monster attacks. When a Monster successfully wounds a Vampire, this Corruption enters the victim's body and interferes with their natural regeneration. Until the foreign influence is purged, the Vampire's normally exceptional healing ability can be disabled.[2]
Monster flesh and blood are similarly toxic to ordinary Vampires. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] discovered this after consuming Monster flesh during the [[WarFall]] expedition. The poison was sufficiently severe that normal Noble Vampires could die from consuming it, while members of Clan Adrasteia are naturally resistant because their own bodies already contain Monster genetics.[9][6]
The strength of this toxicity varies according to the Monster involved. Victor could progressively develop resistance to the weaker poison after consuming lower-level Monsters, but the venom of stronger specimens remained dangerous to him.[8]

### Immortality and Resurrection
Many of [[Nightingale]]'s standard combat Monsters possess an exceptional form of biological immortality. Ordinary physical death is frequently temporary: decapitated, bisected, frozen and otherwise destroyed Monsters can reconstruct their bodies and resume fighting.[2]
During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s first confrontation with a major horde, he escalated from dismemberment to completely incinerating the creatures until their bodies were reduced to ash. Even that was insufficient. Flesh began forming again from apparently empty space while a green supernatural substance accompanied the reconstruction, eventually rebuilding the destroyed Monsters.[2]
Eleonor Adrasteia consequently describes them as an "immortal army", explaining that regardless of the normal method used to kill them, they simply return.[2]
This characteristic does not apply to the Nightingale Natives or Alphas. An Alpha's death is treated as permanent, and the death of Ken's father immediately triggered the native succession system in which the eldest son inherited his leadership position.[8][7]

### Classification
The different names used for Monsters are primarily battlefield classifications used by Clan Adrasteia and the native commanders rather than evidence that every class constitutes a completely independent natural species. Their extreme artificial biological diversity makes exact taxonomy difficult.

#### Minions
Minions are among the most common low-level Monsters deployed in hordes. They serve as basic infantry and normally appear in great numbers alongside stronger organisms such as Ogres and Behemoths.[5][10]
Individually, these creatures represent comparatively little danger to elite Vampires, but their resurrection, toxic attacks and sheer numbers make prolonged combat against large hordes dangerous.

#### Ogres
Ogres are large terrestrial Monsters specializing primarily in physical combat. Their bodies possess considerable strength and durability, and some specimens wield enormous weapons capable of threatening ordinary Vampires.[2]
They remain below Behemoths in the general [[WarFall]] threat structure but are substantially more dangerous than common Minions.

#### Behemoths
Behemoths are enormous terrestrial Monsters and one of the major heavy-combat classes encountered around [[WarFall]]. They possess massive bodies, tremendous physical strength and considerable durability.[4][2]
Behemoths also fulfill a support role within Monster armies. Their roars release a pressure-like aura over surrounding Monsters, causing their eyes to glow red and significantly increasing their power. Clan Adrasteia therefore prioritizes eliminating Behemoths during large battles rather than allowing them to continuously empower the horde.[2]
Behemoth remains are among the numerous Monster materials harvested by WarFall for armor, weapons and other military equipment.[4][2]

#### Wyvern Class
Wyverns are aerial Monsters encountered in large groups above native settlements. Their presence gives Monster hordes a considerable aerial component and requires Clan Adrasteia's ranged combatants to continuously clear the skies during expeditions.[6]
They are considered a lower aerial classification than Dragon Class Monsters.[6]

#### Dragon Class
Despite the name, Dragon Class Monsters are not confirmed to be members of the true Dragon race. The term is a [[WarFall]] classification given to enormous flying Monsters possessing characteristics reminiscent of Dragons.
The specimen observed by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] possessed enormous wings, a long tail and two heads equipped with sharp teeth. Clan Adrasteia describes Dragon Class as being superior to Wyverns and approximately equivalent to Behemoths in general threat level, while their ability to fly makes them particularly troublesome opponents.[6]
They also possess powerful breath attacks, although the exact nature of the breath varies by specimen.[6]

#### Predators
Predators are specialized stealth Monsters. They move with almost complete silence and possess camouflage capable of making them effectively invisible to ordinary visual observation. Clan Adrasteia regards them as dangerous ambush predators because a target can be killed before becoming aware of their presence.[5][10]
The later [PREDATOR] Type 4 appears to be a deliberately enhanced or formally numbered version of this combat role.

#### Engineered Types
The native military developed numbered Monster models beyond the ordinary battlefield classifications. Their existence demonstrates that Monster production and modification continued long after Ikor created the original biological foundation.[10]

##### [PREDATOR] Type 4
[PREDATOR] Type 4 is a bipedal Monster covered in dark-green scales. It possesses large wings and long claws from which an unidentified liquid continuously drips.[10]
Groups of these Monsters display advanced camouflage, becoming effectively invisible while approaching their targets.[10]

##### [HUNTER] Type 5
[HUNTER] Type 5 is an enormous centipede-like Monster approximately 30 meters long.[10]
During its confrontation with Rose Adrasteia, the creature attacked by launching numerous acidic needles from range.[10]

##### [DESTROYER] Type 6
[DESTROYER] Type 6 is a roughly 10-meter-tall four-armed gorilla-like Monster with horns and four golden-green eyes.[10]
Its danger is considerably greater than its physical appearance suggests. The [[Destroyer]] can abruptly relocate through space and generates an effect around itself that dramatically interferes with movement and the flow of Time. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] initially compared its movement to Natalia's spatial abilities and described the suppression surrounding it as though Time itself had stopped.[10][7]
Type 6 was dangerous enough to cause serious difficulty for Victor despite his Count-level speed and combat abilities.

##### Type 7
Type 7 is the highest numbered Monster explicitly revealed during the [[WarFall]] expedition. Its physical appearance and complete abilities remain unknown because Ken was prevented from releasing it.[7]
The Native military describes Type 7 as a highest-level Monster specifically created to kill the invaders they called "Elder Vampires". It was stated to be stronger than the Type 6 that was already giving [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] considerable trouble and required Commander-level authorization to release.[7]

#### Titans
[[Titans]] are among the largest conventional Monster classes encountered in [[Nightingale]]'s wilderness. The specimens seen during the [[WarFall]] expedition exceeded 50 meters in height, with twenty individuals visible simultaneously from an enormous distance.[3]
Titans rank above Behemoths. Their enormous bodies grant exceptional physical strength and durability, but their movement is comparatively slow. A large concentration of Titans is additionally dangerous because combat around them tends to attract numerous other Monsters into the area, making an extended confrontation increasingly difficult to control.[3]
Even [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and the elite WarFall expedition judged engaging twenty Titans simultaneously without a larger force and established outpost to be unnecessarily reckless.[3]

### Powers and Abilities
Monster Physiology: The artificial nature of the Monster race produced extreme biological diversity, meaning individual classes can differ radically in size, anatomy and supernatural abilities. The following powers describe broadly demonstrated racial characteristics or abilities belonging to clearly identified Monster classes.

#### Standard Abilities
- Superhuman Strength: Monsters possess physical strength far beyond ordinary Humans and weaker supernatural beings. Ogres and Behemoths specialize heavily in raw physical power, while [[Titans]] possess even greater strength due to their enormous bodies.[2][3]
- Superhuman Durability: Monster bodies can withstand severe supernatural trauma and repeated attacks from Vampires. Their durability increases dramatically according to class, with Behemoths and Titans possessing particularly resistant bodies.[2][3]
- Superhuman Endurance: Monsters can continue fighting despite extreme bodily destruction. Their ability to tolerate injury is further amplified by their regeneration and resurrection.[2]
- Enhanced Stamina: Monsters can remain active through prolonged supernatural combat and repeatedly resume fighting following regeneration or resurrection.[2]
- Regeneration: Many Monsters can rapidly restore severe bodily damage, including dismemberment and destruction of substantial portions of their bodies.[2]
- Resurrection: Numerous common Monster classes possess an abnormal survival mechanism that allows them to return after conventional death. Even complete incineration and reduction to ash can fail to permanently destroy them.[2]
- Body Reconstruction: A green supernatural substance accompanies their resurrection and reconstructs a new body even when little or none of the original physical form remains.[2]
- Corruption Inducement: Monster attacks can transmit a supernatural substance or influence described as Corruption into the bodies of Vampires.[2]
- Regeneration Negation: Monster Corruption interferes with a Vampire's natural regeneration, preventing wounds from healing normally until the foreign influence is removed.[2]
- Poison Manipulation (Biological): Monster flesh and blood contain substances that are naturally poisonous to ordinary Vampires. Stronger Monster classes can possess more dangerous toxins.[9][6][8]
- Corrosion Inducement: The green substance associated with Monster resurrection possesses extremely corrosive properties. Contact with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] caused his flesh to begin melting and forced him to amputate the affected limb before the effect spread further.[2]
- Biological Adaptation: Monster genetics possess extraordinary flexibility and compatibility with foreign supernatural biology. Their genes can merge with Vampire Blood more successfully than most supernatural species, forming the foundation of Clan Adrasteia's hybrid Bloodline.[3]
- Natural Weaponry: Depending on their class, Monsters possess claws, fangs, horns, wings, tails, massive jaws, hardened scales or other anatomical weapons.[4][6][10]

#### Class-Specific Abilities
- Flight: Wyverns, Dragon Class Monsters, Predator Type 4 and other winged Monsters possess natural aerial mobility.[6][10]
- Statistics Amplification (Behemoth): Behemoths can release a roar and pressure-like aura that strengthens nearby Monsters, making them priority targets during large engagements.[2]
- Camouflage (Predators): Predators possess highly advanced camouflage capable of making their bodies effectively invisible to ordinary sight.[5][10]
- Stealth Mastery (Predators): Predator Monsters move with extremely little sound and specialize in approaching targets without detection.[5]
- Acid Manipulation (Hunter Type 5): Hunter Type 5 can launch numerous acidic needle-like projectiles from its massive centipede-like body.[10]
- Spatial Manipulation ([[Destroyer]] Type 6): Destroyer Type 6 can abruptly shift through Space and appear beside targets without relying upon conventional physical movement.[10][7]
- Teleportation: Its spatial movement allows it to disappear from one position and manifest elsewhere almost instantly.[10]
- Time Manipulation (Destroyer Type 6): The area surrounding Type 6 produces an abnormal suppression effect that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] compared to Time being stopped. Even his enhanced movement and Lightning were severely impeded within its influence.[10][7]
- Breath Attack (Dragon Class): Dragon Class Monsters possess dangerous breath-based attacks. Their exact properties vary according to the specimen involved.[6]

### Relationship with Clan Adrasteia
Main article: Clan Adrasteia
Clan Adrasteia developed in continuous warfare against the Monster armies occupying western [[Nightingale]]. Ordinary Vampires were poorly suited for this conflict because conventional lethal attacks could not permanently kill many Monsters and Monster poison was dangerous to Vampire physiology.[2]
The early Adrasteia family eventually performed a dangerous rebirth ritual that forcibly combined Noble Vampire and Monster genetics, creating a stable Vampire-Monster hybrid lineage.[3]
The ritual possessed an extremely low survival rate, stated to be below approximately 5% even among Vampires with powerful regeneration. Monster genetics nevertheless proved unusually compatible because of their extraordinary adaptability.[3]
The main members of Clan Adrasteia and their elite warriors possess substantially more Monster characteristics than distant descendants. High-purity members can assume genuine Monster transformations, while weaker descendants may only display partial traits such as altered eyes or transformed limbs.[3]
Most importantly, Clan Adrasteia developed a Monster-derived power that prevents the resurrection of Monsters. When Eleonor imbued her weapon with the blue-and-red Energy produced by her hybrid Bloodline, Monsters struck by the attack ceased regenerating and remained permanently dead.[2]
Because their own bodies contain Monster genetics, members of Clan Adrasteia also possess natural resistance to the toxins present in Monster flesh and blood.[9][6]

### Weaknesses
- Clan Adrasteia's Monster-Derived Power: The specialized Energy produced through Clan Adrasteia's Monster Bloodline can permanently kill Monsters by preventing their resurrection.[2]
- Command Dependency: Most conventional Monsters possess limited intelligence and historically operated as organized armies only when directed by Alpha commanders or Elder Gods. Chapter 886 confirms that Alphas were specifically authorized to command Monster forces against [[WarFall]].[1]
- Limited Intelligence (Most Classes): Many standard Monsters behave primarily through instinct and aggression rather than advanced strategy. Their effectiveness as armies therefore depends heavily upon commanders and specialized classes.[4][5]
- Class-Specific Weaknesses: Different artificial Monster forms possess different disadvantages. [[Titans]], for example, possess enormous power and durability but comparatively poor speed and reaction time.[3]
- Superior Authority: Monsters created under Ikor's system could be controlled by the native Alpha commanders and the Elder Gods who stood above them in the artificial hierarchy.[1]

### History

#### War with the Vampires
When [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] and the Vampires settled [[Nightingale]], they entered a world already inhabited by its native humanoid civilization. Conflict between the newcomers and the existing inhabitants eventually developed into a prolonged war.
The Monster population became one of the principal weapons used against the Vampires. Over thousands of years, the artificial creatures developed and were modified around anti-Vampire warfare, including Corruption capable of preventing Vampire regeneration.[2]
The Vampires eventually established [[WarFall]] as the primary fortress separating their civilization from the western wilderness and the forces operating beyond the [[Gate of Tartar]]. Clan Adrasteia was assigned responsibility for defending this frontier.

#### Creation of Clan Adrasteia's Hybrid Bloodline
Because conventional Vampires could repeatedly defeat Monsters without permanently killing them, the ancestors of Clan Adrasteia sought another solution. Through a dangerous rebirth ritual, they combined their Vampire Bloodline with Monster genetics.[3]
The resulting hybrids became exceptionally effective Monster hunters. They inherited resistance to Monster toxins, the ability to transform portions of their bodies into Monster forms and, most importantly, the supernatural power necessary to stop Monster resurrection.[2][3]
Monster remains also became valuable military resources. [[WarFall]] developed methods of processing their skin, bones and other biological materials into equipment used by Clan Adrasteia's warriors.[4][2]

#### Victor's First Encounter
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] first encountered the large-scale Monster armies while visiting Eleonor's territory. At the time, he understood almost nothing about their immortality and initially treated the battle as another opportunity for combat.[4][2]
During the fight, he learned that Monster attacks could prevent Vampire regeneration and witnessed Behemoths strengthening surrounding creatures. He then attempted progressively more destructive methods of killing the Monsters, eventually reducing them to ash through fire.[2]
The apparently destroyed Monsters still returned. Their bodies reconstructed through the mysterious green substance until Eleonor used Clan Adrasteia's hybrid power to permanently kill the horde.[2]

#### WarFall Expedition
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later joined Eleonor and her Valkyries on an expedition far beyond [[WarFall]]. The group encountered increasingly diverse Monster classes, including Wyverns, Dragon Class creatures, Predators and the experimental numbered Types maintained by the [[Nightingale]] Natives.[9][6][5][10]
The expedition exposed the existence of native surveillance technology, laboratory infrastructure and Monster containment systems. Alpha commanders monitored Vampire movements remotely and released specialized Monsters according to specific authorization levels.[9][5][10][7]
Despite these discoveries, Victor and Clan Adrasteia still lacked enough historical context to realize that Alphas were not simply intelligent Monsters.

#### Elder God War
The Monster armies later appeared on a much larger scale during the war against the Elder Gods. Large numbers of artificial creatures accompanied the Elder Gods and attacked [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s forces throughout [[Nightingale]].[11][12][13][14]
After defeating and consuming [[Ikor-Kar-VI]], Victor inherited knowledge concerning Nightingale's ancient history and the Elder God's experiments. He subsequently discovered the surviving native civilians and finally learned the actual relationship between the Natives and Monsters.[1]
Victor explained that the Natives were Nightingale's original humanoid people, that Alpha was a title reserved for adult male family leaders authorized to command the Monsters, and that the Monsters themselves were artificial creations produced by combining Ikor's Divinity, Native biology and Noble Vampire characteristics.[1]
Approximately 1,700 surviving Natives remained within the refuge discovered after the battle. Victor declined to punish the civilians for the crimes committed by Ikor and the military Alpha factions and instead placed their future relationship under the supervision of Clan Adrasteia.[1]

### Trivia
- The early [[WarFall]] chapters intentionally present Alphas as if they were intelligent members of the Monster race, while Chapter 886 later reveals that the two populations are fundamentally separate.[2][1]
- The Alpha commanders themselves refer to the artificial creatures under their control as abominations, providing an early indication that they did not regard the Monsters as members of their own species.[8]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] suspected during the WarFall expedition that the enormous number and specialization of Monster types could not have developed naturally.[10]
- Type 7 was never released, leaving its physical appearance and supernatural abilities unknown.[7]
- Monster genetics possess sufficient adaptability to produce one of the setting's most stable deliberately created hybrid Bloodlines in Clan Adrasteia.[3]
- The Monster resurrection mechanism is sufficiently powerful that complete destruction and incineration of the body does not guarantee permanent death.[2]
- [[Ikor-Kar-VI]] extensively experimented on [[Nightingale]]'s Native population and used his Predation ability to acquire useful powers or traits that emerged from his experiments.[1]
- Only approximately 1,700 members of Nightingale's original native population remained together in the refuge discovered by Victor after the Elder God conflict.[1]
