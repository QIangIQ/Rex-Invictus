Kitsune are a species of fox Yokai originating from Japan and one of the most prestigious and powerful Yokai lineages shown in the story. Unlike many Yokai species whose individual growth does not possess an immediately visible racial indicator, a Kitsune's development is reflected directly through its tails. Kitsune are normally born possessing a single tail, with additional tails manifesting as the fox grows stronger. A Nine-Tailed Kitsune represents the normal pinnacle of mortal Yokai development and stands immediately beneath the threshold of Godhood.[2]
Kitsune possess several racial characteristics that distinguish them even from other Yokai. They are natural masters of disguise, possess highly developed fox senses and gain additional elemental affinities as their tails increase. Chapter 601 establishes that every new tail grants the Kitsune another element it can control, with the specific element depending upon the individual fox and its natural affinities.[4] Exceptionally developed Kitsune can therefore become extraordinarily versatile elemental combatants, with Nine-Tailed Kitsune potentially possessing nine different elemental affinities.
Kitsune should not be described as beings created from Human negativity. Like other Yokai, they historically absorb Youki, which itself is accumulated Negative Energy generated by Human emotions, but the actual biological or spiritual origin of the Kitsune race has never been revealed.[5]

### Description
Kitsune are supernatural foxes capable of possessing both humanoid and more explicitly fox-like appearances. Their most recognizable physical characteristics are their fox ears and tails, although sufficiently skilled Kitsune can conceal these characteristics completely and pass as ordinary Humans.[6]
Their tails are considerably more important than simple physical ornaments. Tail number directly reflects a Kitsune's supernatural development, while each additional tail is associated with the acquisition of another elemental affinity.[2][4] The difference between a one-tailed Kitsune and a Nine-Tailed Kitsune is consequently not merely one of age or appearance but represents an enormous difference in accumulated supernatural development.
Kitsune also possess different racial colorations or lineages whose symbolism is associated with different supernatural characteristics. Chapter 365 identifies Golden, White, Red and Black foxes, with Red and Black Kitsune being considerably rarer than the more common Golden and White populations.[7]
The race should not be treated as uniformly malicious, Demonic or "evil". The term "Demonic Fox" may be used descriptively because of Human mythology, but Kitsune are Yokai rather than members of the Demon race.

### Tail System
The tail system is the defining evolutionary characteristic of Kitsune.

#### One-Tailed Kitsune
A Kitsune is normally born with one tail.[2] The first tail represents the beginning of the fox's supernatural development and provides the foundation upon which subsequent evolution occurs.
[[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]] herself was once known to Vlad as a small one-tailed Black Kitsune before eventually developing into one of the most powerful Nine-Tailed Kitsune in Japan.[7]

#### Additional Tails
As a Kitsune becomes stronger, additional tails emerge.[2] This growth reflects actual supernatural development rather than simply biological aging.
Chapter 601 further reveals that gaining a tail provides the Kitsune with another element it is capable of controlling. The specific element depends upon the individual fox, its lineage and its existing affinities.[4]
This creates an important distinction between raw strength and versatility. A Kitsune possessing many tails has not simply increased its reserves of Energy; it has progressively accumulated additional elemental possibilities.

#### Nine-Tailed Kitsune
A Nine-Tailed Kitsune represents the ordinary pinnacle of the Kitsune race. [[Nero]] describes nine tails as the peak of Yokai strength for foxes and states that such a being is only one step away from Godhood.[2]
Nine-Tailed Kitsune are extremely rare and possess enormous supernatural reserves compared with younger foxes. Known examples include [[Hashimoto Genji]], [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]], [[Otsuki Yoichi]] and [[Otsuki Hana]].
The existence of nine tails does not make every Kitsune equally powerful. Haruna, for example, possessed vastly greater Youki and combat ability than Genji despite both being Nine-Tailed Kitsune.[8]

#### Ten-Tailed Kitsune
A Ten-Tailed Kitsune represents a Divine evolutionary state beyond the normal nine-tailed limit.
[[Inari-Omikami]] is the clearest stable example. Chapter 361 explicitly identifies Inari as a ten-tailed fox who reached Godhood, with her Divine evolution granting complete control over the basic elements.[9]
Haruna later demonstrated that the same evolutionary boundary could be reached by another Kitsune. During the Elder God War, she violently forced her own spiritual development beyond its normal limit, underwent a massive metamorphosis and manifested a tenth tail. The accompanying transformation referred to her as a Lunar Goddess and a fox that had surpassed ordinary Divine expectations.[10]
Haruna's transformation was not a safe or stable example of natural ascension. [[Jeanne Elderblood]] explicitly criticized her for forcibly evolving into a Ten-Tailed Fox before her Soul was properly prepared and warned that failure could have completely destroyed her Soul. Haruna survived principally because her spiritual foundation had been enormously strengthened within the Tower of Nightmare.[10]
After the battle, Haruna was exhausted and her ten tails reverted to nine, demonstrating that her Chapter 882 transformation was a temporarily forced Divine state rather than an immediately permanent racial evolution.[10]
The tenth tail should therefore be understood as a threshold associated with Divine Kitsune evolution rather than an ordinary tenth stage every fox automatically reaches through age.

### Kitsune Types
Kitsune possess several major color variants. These colors are not cosmetic alone; Chapter 365 associates them with different supernatural characteristics and affinities.[7]

#### Golden Kitsune
Golden Kitsune are one of the more common Kitsune variants. Their color is associated with the natural elements.[7]
[[Inari-Omikami]] is the most prominent known Golden Kitsune, although her later Divine abilities vastly exceeded those of a normal fox and should not be treated as the baseline for the variant.

#### White Kitsune
White Kitsune are another comparatively common form and symbolize Purity.[7]
[[Hashimoto Genji]] is a known Nine-Tailed White Kitsune.

#### Red Kitsune
Red Kitsune are considerably rarer than Golden or White foxes. They are associated with War, Blood and Lust.[7]
The exact supernatural abilities universally possessed by Red Kitsune have not yet been comprehensively demonstrated, and the symbolic association should not automatically be interpreted as proof that every Red Kitsune possesses Blood Manipulation, War Manipulation or Lust Manipulation.

#### Black Kitsune
Black Kitsune are an extremely rare fox variant associated with Death and Darkness.[7]
Because of this association, historical folklore developed the belief that encountering a Black Kitsune was an omen of misfortune. Chapter 365 presents this specifically as an old story or superstition rather than proof that Black Kitsune automatically produce bad luck.[7]
The [[Otsuki Clan]] is principally composed of Black Kitsune. Known members include [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]], [[Otsuki Hana]] and [[Otsuki Yoichi]].
Black Kitsune normally possess strong Darkness affinity. Haruna represents a major anomaly because, despite originating from a Clan dominated by Darkness-aligned foxes, her nine elemental affinities eventually included Light. Yoichi explicitly considers this something that should not have been possible according to normal Kitsune elemental rules.[4]

### Otsuki Clan
Main article: [[Otsuki Clan]]
The Otsuki Clan is an exceptionally rare Black Kitsune lineage whose characteristics extend beyond ordinary Kitsune physiology.
Chapter 365 explains that members of Clan Otsuki possess an unusually deep instinctive bond with one another. Unlike [[Werewolves]], whose collective amplification is centered upon an Alpha, Otsuki Kitsune derive their strength from family itself.[7]
When Otsuki foxes gather together, their real power becomes more fully expressed. Their strength increases, their unique powers gain a much greater effective range and the Clan begins functioning almost as though several foxes were parts of one existence.[7]
The bond operates unconsciously as well. Members of the lineage naturally seek one another, and the connection can influence supernatural abilities across great distances. Vlad identifies this bond as the reason Ophis' unstable Teleportation drew her specifically toward Haruna in Japan: Ophis carried characteristics originating from her mother [[Otsuki Hana]], causing the Otsuki lineage to resonate with Haruna.[7]
These characteristics belong specifically to the Otsuki Clan and should not be listed as universal abilities of every Kitsune.

### Relationship with Inari
[[Inari-Omikami]] was historically one of the most important Divine figures associated with the Kitsune.
[[Hashimoto Genji]], formerly recognized as the leader of all Kitsune, was directly blessed by Inari and served as the gatekeeper controlling access to the Divine Realm associated with the foxes.[2]
Inari herself represented what a Kitsune could become after crossing the threshold into Divinity. As a Ten-Tailed Fox Goddess, she possessed a Divine body and complete control over the basic elements.[9]
This relationship should not be interpreted as every Kitsune automatically being a subordinate of Inari. Haruna openly challenged the old political structure, built her own [[Hyakki Yakō]] and eventually unified the Yokai under authority independent from Inari's direct control.[2]

### Disguise and Shapeshifting
Kitsune possess an instinctive talent for disguising themselves.
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] questioned how another Kitsune had appeared completely Human, Haruna explained that Kitsune, like Tanuki, are masters of disguise and that the skill exists within their instincts. They can conceal their Yokai characteristics easily despite many foxes considering their ears and tails a source of pride.[6]
This allows a Kitsune to move through Human society without openly exposing its supernatural nature.
The ability should not be exaggerated into unlimited Shapeshifting. The verified statement establishes exceptional disguise and concealment of racial characteristics, but does not prove that every Kitsune can transform into any imaginable creature or object.

### Senses
Kitsune possess highly developed fox senses, particularly hearing.
Haruna possesses both Human-like ears and supernatural fox ears. She explains that her fox ears provide extremely sharp and refined animal-like hearing, while her Human ears are useful for receiving sound at lower intensity.[11]
A sufficiently experienced Kitsune can deliberately suppress its fox hearing to avoid being overwhelmed. Haruna can effectively shut off the enhanced ears when necessary.[11]
This heightened perception also creates a weakness: an enemy aware of the fox's extreme auditory sensitivity can exploit powerful sound against them.[11]

### Elemental Affinity
Elemental development is fundamentally connected to a Kitsune's tails.
Chapter 601 states that when a fox gains another tail, it receives another element it can control. The specific element varies between individuals.[4]
A Nine-Tailed Kitsune can therefore theoretically possess nine different elemental affinities. During Haruna's battle against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], her nine tails represented:
- Fire
- Water
- Earth
- Wind
- Metal
- Wood
- Lightning
- Darkness
- Light[4]
Haruna's exact combination should not be treated as mandatory for every Nine-Tailed Kitsune. The chapter explicitly states that the element obtained depends upon the individual fox.[4]
Normal affinities also appear to possess restrictions. A fox born with a Darkness affinity should ordinarily not obtain the directly opposed Light affinity, making Haruna's possession of both an anomaly even among her own species.[4]
Ten-Tailed Divine Kitsune can exceed the ordinary elemental limitations of the mortal race. Inari possessed complete command of the basic elements after reaching Godhood.[9]

### Mating and Bonding
Kitsune possess a supernatural method of marking their chosen partners.
Chapter 730 compares this behavior with Werewolf mate-marking. While [[Werewolves]] mark their partner through a bite recognizable by other members of their race, Kitsune perform a similar action while additionally injecting Youki into the bite.[12]
Haruna used this form of marking upon [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
This establishes a supernatural mate-marking mechanism but does not establish the old page's uncited statement that every Kitsune is biologically monogamous and can choose only one mate during its entire existence. Until a chapter explicitly states such a universal restriction, it should not be presented as canon.

### Powers and Abilities
Kitsune Physiology: Kitsune are among the more powerful Yokai species, but their abilities vary enormously according to number of tails, lineage, individual elemental affinities and training. Nine-Tailed and Ten-Tailed foxes should not be used as the baseline statistics of newly born Kitsune.

#### Standard Racial Abilities
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Kitsune possess supernatural strength, speed, reflexes, agility, durability and stamina far beyond ordinary Humans. These physical characteristics increase drastically as the fox develops more tails and accumulates greater supernatural power.[2][8]
- Longevity / Agelessness: Like other Yokai, Kitsune are described as essentially immortal until killed. They do not naturally die from old age under ordinary circumstances but remain vulnerable to lethal supernatural or physical damage.[5]
- Youki Manipulation: Kitsune can manipulate Youki, the accumulated Negative Energy produced by Human emotions that serves as the traditional supernatural Energy source of Yokai.[5]
- Energy Absorption (Youki): Yokai absorb environmental Youki to strengthen themselves; as Yokai, Kitsune share this relationship with the Energy.[5]
- Statistics Amplification (Youki): Absorbed Youki reinforces a Kitsune's supernatural power and can be used to support combat techniques.[5][8]
- Shapeshifting (Disguise): Kitsune can alter or conceal their external racial characteristics and appear entirely Human. Haruna describes this as an instinctive racial skill.[6]
- Enhanced Senses: Kitsune possess animal-like sensory characteristics superior to ordinary Humans.
- Enhanced Hearing: Their fox ears provide extremely sharp and refined hearing. Experienced Kitsune can deliberately suppress this sense when necessary.[11]
- Natural Weaponry: Kitsune possess fox characteristics such as claws, fangs and tails that can function as natural weapons depending upon their manifested form.
- Elemental Manipulation: Each additional tail grants the Kitsune another elemental affinity. The element depends upon the individual fox rather than following one mandatory progression.[4]
- Power Evolution (Tail Growth): Kitsune physically manifest additional tails as their supernatural development increases. Tail number therefore serves as a visible indication of racial evolution.[2]

#### Advanced Kitsune Abilities
The following abilities have been demonstrated by exceptionally advanced Kitsune and should not automatically be attributed to every young member of the species.
- Illusion Creation / Perception Manipulation: Advanced Kitsune techniques can confuse an opponent's perception through supernatural deception. During Haruna's battle against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], she repeatedly disappeared from his ordinary sight and reappeared like a mirage; Victor compared the phenomenon with illusion abilities while recognizing that the mechanism was not completely identical.[8]
- Duplication: Haruna demonstrated the ability to divide herself into multiple bodies during her Nine-Tailed techniques. She produced eight additional manifestations so that the original and clones collectively represented her nine tails.[4]
Because Chapter 601 demonstrates this through Haruna's advanced technique rather than explicitly stating that every Kitsune can duplicate itself, it should be treated as an advanced Kitsune ability rather than an unconditional racial power.
- Flight: High-level Kitsune such as Haruna and Inari are capable of sustained aerial movement. The verified material does not establish that every newly born Kitsune automatically possesses Flight.
- Enhanced Elemental Manipulation: A Nine-Tailed Kitsune can possess as many as nine different elemental affinities, with each tail corresponding to an element the fox has acquired during its evolution.[4]

#### Ten-Tailed Divine Abilities
A Ten-Tailed Kitsune has crossed or temporarily forced itself across the boundary separating the mortal Yokai state from Divinity.
- Divine Power: Stable Ten-Tailed Kitsune such as [[Inari-Omikami]] possess [[Divine Energy]] and the powers associated with Godhood.[9]
- Divinity: The tenth-tail state is associated with a Kitsune crossing into a Divine existence. Inari existed permanently as a Fox Goddess, while Haruna temporarily forced the transformation during Chapter 882.[9][10]
- Elemental Manipulation (Complete Basic Elements): Inari, as a Ten-Tailed Fox who had reached Godhood, possessed complete control over the basic elements.[9]
- Transformation (Divine Fox Form): Divine Kitsune can manifest enormous fox bodies far exceeding their ordinary humanoid form. Inari and Haruna both demonstrated colossal Ten-Tailed transformations.[9][10]
The exact Divine abilities beyond these depend upon the individual's Divinities, Concepts and personal evolution and should not automatically be assigned to every theoretical Ten-Tailed Kitsune.

#### Otsuki Clan Abilities
The following characteristics belong specifically to the rare Black Kitsune of the [[Otsuki Clan]].
- Statistics Amplification (Familial Resonance): Members of Clan Otsuki become stronger when gathered with other members of their family.[7]
- Power Amplification: Otsuki abilities can operate over a significantly greater range when members of the Clan are together.[7]
- Hive Mind / Spiritual Link (Limited): Vlad compares the gathered Otsuki to several foxes functioning almost like one being. This should not be interpreted as literal permanent telepathy, but demonstrates an unusually deep supernatural familial resonance.[7]
- Extrasensory Perception (Familial Attraction): Otsuki descendants instinctively seek one another. This connection was powerful enough to influence Ophis' uncontrolled Teleportation and draw her toward Haruna despite Ophis not consciously knowing Haruna's location.[7]
The old page listed Teleportation as a universal exclusive ability of Black Kitsune, but the currently verified chapters do not directly establish that every Black Kitsune possesses natural Teleportation. Ophis' event in Chapter 365 involved her own Teleportation being influenced by Otsuki resonance, which is not the same statement.

#### Learnable Abilities
The following abilities are not Kitsune racial powers. They are supernatural disciplines that Kitsune can learn.
- [[Natural Energy]] Manipulation ([[Senjutsu]]): Haruna helped develop a method of absorbing neutral Nature Energy circulating through Earth's Dragon Veins/Ley Lines.[5]
- World Tree Energy Manipulation: Chapter 607 later reveals that the Energy involved in Earth's Senjutsu system originates from remnants of the power circulating through [[Yggdrasil]], Earth's World Tree.[13]
- Life-Force Manipulation (Ki): Ki allows the user to deliberately manipulate their own vitality. Haruna discovered the system while learning to perceive the interaction between Nature Energy and her own life force.[5]
Neither Senjutsu nor Ki is exclusive to Kitsune or Yokai. Haruna explicitly states that anyone who fulfills the necessary requirements can learn them.[5]

### Weaknesses
- Mortality: Kitsune are ageless rather than absolutely immortal. Sufficiently severe attacks can kill them.[5]
- Head Destruction / Decapitation: Mortal Yokai can be permanently killed through catastrophic destruction of the head.[14] A Kitsune who has already attained genuine Godhood may possess the resurrection mechanics of a God rather than surviving the original injury itself.
- Enhanced Hearing: A Kitsune's extraordinarily sensitive fox ears can become a liability when confronted by sufficiently intense sound. Haruna explicitly acknowledges that an enemy could exploit this weakness and can suppress her fox hearing to compensate.[11]
- Youki Dependency: Traditional Kitsune abilities depend heavily upon Youki, an environmental Energy produced through accumulated Human negativity. Yokai leadership began researching [[Senjutsu]] specifically because relying exclusively upon Youki created strategic limitations outside regions rich in the Energy.[5]
- Senjutsu-Youki Conflict: Senjutsu's neutral Nature Energy naturally conflicts with negative Youki when both are maintained in the same body. Even Haruna initially possessed only limited control over the interaction.[5]
- Senjutsu Instability: Improper use of Senjutsu while emotionally disturbed can damage the body internally and, in extreme circumstances, injure the Soul.[5]
- Ki Overuse: Ki directly consumes the user's vitality. Reckless use can exhaust the entire lifespan of even an otherwise ageless Yokai and cause death.[5]
- Forced Evolution (Ten-Tailed State): Attempting to prematurely force the transition into a Ten-Tailed Divine Kitsune can destroy the user's Soul. Haruna survived such an attempt only because her Soul had already received extraordinary strengthening through the Tower of Nightmare.[10]

### History

#### Traditional Kitsune Society
Kitsune historically formed one of the most powerful components of Japan's fragmented Yokai civilization. Their society was divided among multiple fox Clans and color lineages, while [[Hashimoto Genji]] eventually came to be recognized as the leader of all Kitsune.[2]
Genji was a Nine-Tailed White Kitsune blessed by [[Inari-Omikami]]. He guarded access to the Divine Realm and possessed sufficient political and supernatural authority that other Kitsune recognized his position over the species.[2]
The Otsuki Black Kitsune existed alongside this system as an exceptionally rare family lineage whose members possessed unusually powerful internal bonds.[7]

#### Rise of Haruna
[[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]] eventually challenged the old Yokai political system while building a [[Hyakki Yakō]] containing numerous unrelated species.
Her campaign placed her in direct conflict with Genji because defeating and recruiting the leader of the foxes would bring an enormous portion of Kitsune society under her growing coalition.[2]
Haruna's objective extended beyond simply becoming the strongest Kitsune. She wanted to unify Japan's fragmented Yokai populations into a single faction capable of protecting itself from Gods, Vampires, Demons, [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] and other supernatural powers.
Her eventual success caused the political leadership of the Kitsune to shift away from Genji and into the larger Yokai structure commanded by Haruna.

#### Alliance with Victor Elderblood
Haruna's faction eventually entered negotiations with [[Victor Elderblood]], producing an alliance between the Yokai and Victor's expanding coalition.[15]
The relationship also exposed Victor to Kitsune techniques on a much deeper level. Haruna personally fought him using her Nine-Tailed abilities and later taught him the principles of [[Senjutsu]].[8][4][13]
During this period, Haruna and Victor developed a supernatural mate connection. Like [[Werewolves]], Kitsune possess a marking ritual associated with their partners; Haruna bit Victor and injected Youki into the mark.[12]

#### Ten-Tailed Evolution
During the war against the Elder Gods, Haruna fought alongside [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] against Shur.
Unable to immediately overcome the Elder God's extraordinary survival ability and enraged by the circumstances surrounding her sister's death, Haruna deliberately forced herself beyond the Nine-Tailed limit.[10]
Her body transformed into an enormous fox approximately 270 meters tall, her existing tails expanded to enormous size and a tenth tail manifested. The transformation was described as the ascension of the Moonlight Fox and carried clearly Divine characteristics.[10]
Haruna used this state to call attacks from the sky resembling controlled falling stars and redirect Shur's attacks through her tails. The transformation consumed an enormous amount of power and immediately collapsed once the battle ended.[10]
Jeanne subsequently revealed that Haruna had violently forced an evolution her Soul was not yet naturally prepared to complete. Without the strengthening she had undergone inside the Tower of Nightmare, the transformation could have destroyed her Soul entirely.[10]

#### Dragon Transformation
Haruna initially refused [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s offer to become a Dragon because she was intensely proud of the Kitsune identity she had cultivated throughout her life.[16]
She nevertheless later underwent a greater transformation and became a Blood Dragon and Dragon Goddess. By Chapter 938, her default racial body was explicitly Draconic rather than Kitsune, although she could continue reproducing her former Kitsune appearance through shapeshifting.[3]
Haruna should therefore be listed as a former biological Kitsune while remaining the political leader historically recognized by the Yokai and fox populations beneath her.

### Relationship with the Yokai
Main article: Yokais
Kitsune are one of the Three Great Yokai groups involved in Haruna's later supernatural research, alongside the Oni and Ryuji.[5]
They possess particularly high political prestige because of their tail-based development and the existence of Nine-Tailed foxes standing immediately below Godhood.[2]
Their importance nevertheless does not mean every Yokai answers naturally to a Kitsune. Haruna's authority was established through her personal strength, [[Hyakki Yakō]] and political unification rather than an automatic racial hierarchy placing foxes above all other Yokai.

### Relationship with Humans
Kitsune historically inhabit regions containing significant Human populations because Youki is generated through accumulated negative Human emotions.[5]
This relationship concerns their Energy supply, not their creation. Kitsune are capable of biological reproduction and possess family lineages such as the [[Otsuki Clan]]; there is no canonical statement that new Kitsune spontaneously appear whenever Humans experience negative emotions.
Their instinctive disguise abilities also allow Kitsune to enter Human society without openly revealing their nature.[6]

### Misconceptions
- Kitsune are born from Human negative emotions: False. Human negative emotions generate Youki, which Yokai absorb as an Energy source. The actual racial origin of Kitsune remains unknown.[5]
- Every Kitsune begins with several tails: False. Kitsune are normally born possessing one tail and gain additional tails as their strength develops.[2]
- Nine tails means the Kitsune is already a God: False. A Nine-Tailed Kitsune represents the pinnacle of ordinary Yokai development and stands one step away from Godhood.[2]
- Every Nine-Tailed Kitsune controls the same nine elements: False. Each additional tail grants another elemental affinity, but the specific elements depend upon the individual fox.[4]
- Every Black Kitsune possesses Teleportation: Not currently established. Chapter 365 demonstrates that Otsuki familial resonance can influence an existing Teleportation ability, but it does not directly state that Teleportation is an inherent ability possessed by every Black Kitsune.[7]
- Every Kitsune possesses Haruna's regeneration: False. Much of the extraordinary healing displayed during the [[Hyakki Yakō]] conflict resulted from Haruna's unique connection to the subordinates beneath her rather than a universal Kitsune racial regeneration ability.[17]
- Inari's physical statistics are the standard for Kitsune: False. Inari was already a Ten-Tailed Fox Goddess, meaning many of her feats derived from God physiology and Divinity rather than ordinary Kitsune biology.[9]
- Ten tails simply means another ordinary tail: False. The tenth-tail state is associated with transcending the normal mortal Kitsune limit and entering Divine evolution.[9][10]
- All Kitsune are monogamous and can select only one mate during their lifetime: Not currently supported by a verified chapter. The novel does establish a supernatural Kitsune mate-mark through a Youki-infused bite, but this does not by itself establish universal lifelong monogamy.[12]
- Black Kitsune automatically bring bad luck: False. Chapter 365 describes the Black Fox as having historically become associated with bad omens because of its connection to Death and Darkness; this is presented as folklore, not demonstrated supernatural Luck Manipulation.[7]
- Haruna is still biologically a Kitsune: False. She retained Kitsune characteristics and can reproduce her old appearance, but by Chapter 938 her actual Race had become Blood Dragon/Dragon God.[3]

### Trivia
- Kitsune are normally born with one tail and gain more as their strength increases.[2]
- A Nine-Tailed Kitsune is described as standing only one step away from attaining Godhood.[2]
- Each new tail grants a Kitsune an additional elemental affinity.[4]
- Haruna's possession of both Darkness and Light affinities violated what Yoichi understood to be a normally absolute Kitsune elemental restriction.[4]
- Golden and White Kitsune are more common than Red and Black Kitsune.[7]
- Golden Kitsune symbolize natural elements, White Kitsune symbolize purity, Black Kitsune are associated with Death and Darkness, and Red Kitsune are associated with War, Blood and Lust.[7]
- The [[Otsuki Clan]] is sufficiently rare that Vlad estimated the living members of their unusual fox variant could be counted on two hands during Chapter 365.[7]
- Otsuki Kitsune grow stronger when together and can extend the effective range of their individual powers through their familial resonance.[7]
- Kitsune and Tanuki are explicitly described as instinctive masters of disguise.[6]
- A Kitsune's fox ears possess much greater auditory sensitivity than its Human-like ears, and an experienced fox can deliberately suppress the enhanced hearing.[11]
- Kitsune mark mates by biting them and injecting Youki into the mark.[12]
- Inari demonstrates a stable Ten-Tailed Divine Kitsune, while Haruna demonstrates that an exceptionally powerful Nine-Tailed Kitsune can forcibly approach the same evolutionary boundary at enormous risk to the Soul.[9][10]
- Haruna's tenth tail disappeared after her forced transformation ended, reducing her back to nine tails until her later racial evolution.[10]
