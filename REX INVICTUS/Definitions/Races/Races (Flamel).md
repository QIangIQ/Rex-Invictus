**[[Races (Flamel)]]** is a figure of Rex Invictus lore. (Lore adapted from *The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** Flamel material is adapted into the Rex Invictus present (c. 2021). Rex Invictus wins any conflict; [[Robespierre]] fills [[Dr. John Dee]]'s role and hunts the Philosopher's Stone rather than [[The Codex]]. See [[Flamel Series#Integration rules]].

## Flamel Series Canon (SOFTINF)

> Race is a term which can be used to describe a group of similar entities belonging to a single species. Since the Secrets of the Immortal [[Nicholas Flamel]] universe draws on dozens of mythologies from around the world, it includes references to scores of creatures and civilizations. Many of these races make appearances, though many more are mentioned only in passing.

### List of Races
Main article: Races/List of Races

### Subraces
Certain races seem to change substantially between epochs. For example, the [[Great Elders]], [[Elders]], and [[Next Generation]] are usually treated as completely different species.

### Inter-species Races
It is evidently possible that certain humanoid races are capable of producing viable offspring. For example, the [[Cucubuths]] are the children of Vampires and [[Torc Madra]], even though the two are radically different species. (Vampires being [[Next Generation]] and Torc Madra being of the Were clans.)

### Trivia
- Though [[Ancients]] are said to be one of the four [[Great Races]] (the others being [[Elders]], [[Archons]], and [[Earthlords]]), no Ancient is ever seen or even mentioned by name in the series.
