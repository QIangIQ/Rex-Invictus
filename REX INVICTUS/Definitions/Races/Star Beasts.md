#### Origin

Star Animals, also called Star Beasts or Star Creatures, are an exceptionally rare kind of naturally occurring cosmic lifeform in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]]. Unlike creatures inhabiting ordinary planets, they live in outer space and feed directly upon stars, accumulating enormous amounts of Energy within their bodies. Even specimens regarded as young can exceed [[Jupiter]] in size, while an animal encountered near the outer reaches of [[Victor Elderblood]]'s Sector was recorded swallowing an entire star. Their known physical characteristics include fur resembling the fabric of Space, an extraordinarily resilient exterior, and a stomach compared to a black hole because of its unusual properties and immense concentration of Energy.[1]
Despite the potential devastation associated with their feeding habits, Star Animals are not presented as inherently aggressive or malicious. [[Scathach]] observed a larger specimen at the edge of the Sector without being attacked, preserving a recording of its behavior in the Imperial archives. Only a few were known to live within the Sector, and the individuals observed there were considered immature. Velnorah theorized that exceptionally old Star Animals might inhabit the Upper Sectors and could eventually grow to the scale of entire Solar Systems, although neither the existence nor the dimensions of such mature individuals have been directly verified.[1]
The species became an object of scientific interest when an enormous young individual, previously killed by the Leviathans serving the [[Elderblood Empire]], was brought to an Elderblood family gathering. While several family members intended to prepare the animal as food, Ruby, Velnorah and Aline recognized the opportunity to study its unusual biology and collected anatomical samples before the carcass was butchered. The creature was later prepared for the traditional banquet, and the event continued in Chapter 1169, when Rosemary identified the Star Beast to other members of the family using Scathach's archived report.[1][2]

### Biology and Physiology

#### External Structure
Star Animals possess an unfamiliar physiology adapted to an environment without ordinary planetary conditions. The young specimen brought before the Elderblood family was described as larger than [[Jupiter]], and its body appeared to be enveloped in or fused with Space itself. Rather than displaying a recognizable outer surface comparable to that of ordinary terrestrial animals, its fur seemed to be composed of the surrounding spatial fabric. This gave the carcass the appearance of belonging to the Cosmos rather than merely occupying a region within it. Ruby was immediately fascinated by the creature's appearance, and even Eve could still sense an exceptional amount of power from the body despite its death.[1]
These observations establish an unusual spatial association with the animal's physical body, but they do not demonstrate that every member possesses unrestricted Space Manipulation, dimensional travel, or an intangible form. The description concerns the appearance and structure of the specimen being examined; its fur's seeming connection with Space is not explained through a demonstrated mechanism. The species' exact external anatomy, including whether the observed surface is ordinary biological material transformed by cosmic exposure or a fundamentally different form of matter, remains unknown.[1]

#### Internal Anatomy and Energy Storage
The carcass retained identifiable internal structures despite its unusual exterior. Ruby, Velnorah and Aline specifically collected samples from its heart, brain, eyes, bone marrow and stomach, confirming the presence of several recognizable organs or tissue types within the enormous organism. Their examination was exploratory rather than a completed anatomical classification, and the chapter does not establish the precise function of every sampled component. The existence of a brain and eyes likewise does not reveal the animal's intelligence, sensory range or cognitive abilities without additional observation.[1]
The stomach attracted particular attention. Ruby compared it to a black hole after discovering an extraordinary concentration of Energy inside, and Aline explained that such reserves were understandable in an organism whose food consisted of stars. The comparison is a description of its appearance or apparent physical properties, not confirmation that a literal astronomical black hole exists inside every Star Animal. The narrative does not establish a true event horizon, singularity, black-hole gravitational field, or the specific biological processes through which stellar matter is converted into stored Energy. Nevertheless, the observation identifies the digestive system as one of the most unusual and potentially valuable aspects of the species for future scientific investigation.[1]

#### Durability and Material Properties
The dead animal's body was extremely resistant to conventional cutting equipment. When the family began preparing the specimen for cooking, ordinary knives could not properly penetrate its exterior, forcing the participants to use tools made from Divine and Demonic materials. Sasha employed the Dragon Language to fashion the specialized knives, allowing the group to proceed with the butchering. The incident demonstrates the extraordinary resilience of the organism's tissues even after death, although it does not specify a quantitative durability tier or establish resistance to every type of supernatural attack.[1]

### Stellar Diet and Natural Ecology
The defining known feeding behavior of Star Animals is the literal consumption of stars. [[Scathach]]'s recording showed a specimen engulfing an entire star, confirming that stellar consumption is not simply a figure of speech used to describe a powerful cosmic creature. This behavior also explains the tremendous Energy detected in the younger animal's remains: the digestive system must be capable of accommodating or processing resources far beyond the capacity of ordinary biological life. Whether Star Animals consume other kinds of matter, how frequently they feed, and how their feeding affects the survival of nearby celestial bodies remain unrecorded.[1]
A Star Animal does not need to intentionally attack a civilization to become an existential threat to its inhabitants. The loss of a star can imperil the worlds dependent upon it, making the species' natural feeding habits dangerous even in the absence of hostility. The chapter explicitly contrasts this danger to ordinary civilizations with the response of the Elderblood family, whose vastly greater supernatural resources allowed them to consider the dead creature a rare ingredient and research specimen rather than an insurmountable threat. This distinction concerns the consequences of stellar consumption and should not be misread as evidence that every Star Animal possesses an independently demonstrated combat technique capable of instantly destroying entire Solar Systems.[1]
The encounter near the edge of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Sector suggests that Star Animals may spend substantial periods traveling or feeding without displaying territorial aggression toward nearby observers. Scathach did not report being attacked or pursued, and she recorded the specimen because its behavior appeared peaceful. However, a single encounter cannot establish that the species is universally docile, herbivorous in the ordinary sense, or harmless to beings approaching it. Its reproductive cycle, breeding habitats, predators, communication, social organization and natural causes of death remain unknown.[1]

### Growth and Size
Star Animals appear to grow over timescales far longer than those of ordinary animals. The corpse retrieved by the Leviathans was already larger than [[Jupiter]], yet both [[Scathach]] and the researchers considered it young. Scathach had also observed another individual significantly larger than the carcass, and the recorded creature was still regarded as young after Velnorah and Aline assessed its size. Thus, the two observed individuals demonstrate that remarkable size alone does not indicate full maturity in this species.[1]
When asked how large the oldest Star Animals might become, Velnorah suggested that they could reach the size of an entire Solar System, perhaps even larger. She estimated that attaining such dimensions might require billions of years or longer. These statements are informed speculation by an experienced scientist, not observations of a confirmed ancient specimen. The maximum size, age at physical maturity and actual lifespan of the species therefore remain unknown.[1]

#### The Recorded Measurement
Ruby measured the larger specimen in [[Scathach]]'s recording at approximately 1,670,040 kilometres, while the same dialogue describes it as about 120 times larger than Earth's Sun. These two numerical descriptions do not agree when interpreted as corresponding linear measurements. The article retains both statements exactly as recorded rather than silently replacing either with a reconstructed figure. The chapter does not clarify whether one value was mistranscribed, whether different measures were being compared, or whether the original statement contained another error. Neither reading should be used to invent a precise maximum species size.[1]

### Distribution and Behavior
The confirmed distribution of Star Animals is limited to a handful of individuals known within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Sector, including the animal obtained by the Leviathans and the one observed by [[Scathach]] at the Sector's edge. Velnorah stated that only a few had been identified locally and that all were young, supporting the conclusion that the species is rare in the regions investigated by the Empire. She speculated that genuinely ancient individuals may live in the Upper Sectors, where significantly older cosmic beings could persist, but no Upper-Sector Star Animal was individually identified in the examined passages.[1]
One known specimen showed no hostility toward Scathach despite being capable of swallowing a star. Scathach treated it as an unusual natural animal, recorded it in the Imperial archives and left it undisturbed. Conversely, the younger specimen brought to the family gathering had been killed by Imperial Leviathans. These are two different encounters: the dead carcass was prey, while the recorded animal was an unharmed subject of observation. The identity, sex and exact ages of the individuals are not known.[1]

### Relationship with the Elderblood Empire

#### The Leviathans
At least one Star Animal has been successfully hunted by the Leviathans serving the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Although the retrieved specimen exceeded [[Jupiter]] in size, the Imperial household already possessed creatures capable of attacking enormous cosmic prey. [[Anna Elderblood]] explained that the Leviathans normally hunt cooperatively, helping them defeat animals larger than an individual hunter. The chapter also describes billions of Leviathans living on their designated world and the largest being capable of swallowing an Earth-sized planet. These population and size statements concern the Leviathans, not Star Animals, and do not establish the number of hunters involved in the specific kill.[1]
Following the examination of the carcass, Velnorah argued that future encounters should prioritize capturing Star Animals alive rather than killing them. She intended to communicate this direction to the Leviathans so the Empire could investigate the biology of living individuals instead of repeatedly destroying rare specimens. The proposal does not establish that a live Star Animal was subsequently captured or successfully maintained in captivity.[1]

#### Scientific Research and Cloning
The species attracted particular interest from Ruby, Velnorah and Aline because of its unusual structure and ability to contain stellar quantities of Energy. The three researchers collected samples of several organs and tissues before permitting the carcass to be processed for the family's meal. Kaguya asked whether the creature could be cloned, and Velnorah responded that she could attempt it. The possibility of reproducing the animal through Imperial scientific or supernatural techniques was therefore raised, but no successful cloning experiment or created specimen is confirmed in the examined chapters.[1]
This interest distinguished the scientific view of the animal from [[Scathach]]'s more pragmatic approach. Scathach had preserved a recording of the larger creature because it had not threatened her, but she had not attempted to capture it. Velnorah considered the missed research opportunity wasteful, emphasizing the value of observing an intact living specimen and investigating its unusual physiology more thoroughly.[1]

### Known Abilities and Traits
The following abilities reflect the characteristics actually demonstrated or described for the species. Where a feat belongs to one specimen, or where a mature-stage characteristic is merely predicted, that limitation is stated explicitly.
- Cosmic Physiology: Star Animals naturally inhabit the vacuum of space and do not require the ordinary planetary environment of terrestrial animals to survive. This is an observed ecological characteristic, not proof of unrestricted adaptation to every possible environment.[1]
- Stellar Consumption: At least one Star Animal was recorded swallowing an entire star as food. The feat confirms literal stellar feeding, although the mechanism, time required and energetic conversion remain unexplained.[1]
- Gigantic Size: A young recovered specimen was larger than [[Jupiter]], and a second young individual recorded near the Sector's edge was substantially larger. The ultimate size of a fully mature specimen is unknown.[1]
- Growth over Geological and Cosmic Timescales: Velnorah estimated that the species might need billions of years or longer to reach its greatest size. This remains a projected maturation period rather than a measured age of an individual.[1]
- Spatially Associated Anatomy: The examined corpse appeared enveloped in or fused with Space, while its fur resembled spatial fabric. Direct manipulation of surrounding space was not separately demonstrated.[1]
- Enhanced Durability: Ordinary cutting tools failed to penetrate the dead specimen's exterior; specialized blades made from Divine and Demonic materials were required to prepare it. Its resistance to direct attacks from powerful supernatural combatants has not been quantified.[1]
- Extraordinary Internal Energy Reserves: The examined digestive system contained immense stores of Energy consistent with the species' diet of stars. The exact capacity and mechanism of storage remain unknown.[1]
- Black-Hole-Like Digestive Anatomy: Ruby compared the specimen's stomach to a black hole. This is a descriptive comparison, not a confirmed ability to produce an actual gravitational singularity.[1]
- Extreme Natural Longevity (Inferred): The projected possibility of growth over billions of years suggests an extraordinary lifespan, but no individual of confirmed billion-year age has been directly observed.[1]
- Nonaggressive Behavior (Observed individual): The larger specimen encountered by [[Scathach]] exhibited no hostility toward her. This establishes behavior in that encounter, not universal pacifism across the species.[1]

### Power and Threat Assessment
A Star Animal is an extreme environmental threat to civilizations dependent upon a stable stellar system, because its ordinary feeding behavior can remove the star sustaining an inhabited world. [[Scathach]]'s recording of one swallowing a complete star establishes the practical basis for that danger. Nevertheless, consuming a star is not automatically the same feat as instantly destroying one through a conventional attack, and no direct battle involving a Star Animal has been depicted in sufficient detail to measure its striking strength, reaction speed, range of supernatural attacks or combat intelligence. Its maximum individual combat capabilities therefore remain unquantified.[1]
The Leviathans' successful hunt confirms that a young specimen can be overcome by exceptionally powerful Imperial creatures, while its resistance to ordinary cutting implements shows that the carcass was extraordinarily tough by mundane standards. The novel does not establish whether the Leviathans relied upon numbers, special attacks, a prolonged pursuit or other conditions during that particular kill. Comparisons between Star Animals and individual Gods, [[True Dragons]], Leviathans or adult Solar-System-sized animals consequently require additional direct feats rather than scaling solely from bodily dimensions or Energy stored in the stomach.[1]

### History

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Star Animals became relevant to the Elderblood household during preparations for a traditional family dinner after the gathering of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wives and daughters. The women chose to prepare the meal themselves rather than assign the work to servants, with Kaguya coordinating the preparations. When the question of ingredients arose, [[Scathach]] suggested using a Star Beast recently killed by the Leviathans. The suggestion astonished family members unfamiliar with the animal, since its body exceeded [[Jupiter]] in size and the species was known to feed upon stars. Albedo expressed interest in tasting one for the first time, while other family members treated the prospect with a mixture of curiosity and enthusiasm.[1]
The carcass was too large for the ordinary kitchen space, prompting Anna to expand the available region until three Jupiter-sized planets could fit side by side. Scathach then manifested the enormous body before the assembled women. Ruby admired its appearance, while Sasha lamented that it had already been killed because it might have made an interesting pet. Kaguya raised the possibility of cloning it, and Velnorah agreed that an attempt might be possible. The dead creature appeared partially merged with Space and possessed fur resembling the surrounding Cosmos; its unusual form prompted Eve to praise the Leviathans' ability to hunt such a creature.[1]
When Scathach explained that the dead individual was relatively young, she revealed that she had observed an even larger Star Animal during an excursion to the edge of Victor's Sector. The group accessed an Imperial archive recording showing the creature swallowing an entire star. Ruby calculated a measurement of 1,670,040 kilometres and also described the animal as 120 times larger than Earth's Sun, two statements that cannot be reconciled as a single ordinary linear size. Velnorah and Aline nevertheless concluded that the animal was still young, with Velnorah suggesting that truly ancient individuals might reach Solar-System scale and require billions of years to mature. She also noted the scarcity of known specimens within the Sector and theorized that older animals might be found in the Upper Sectors.[1]
Velnorah immediately recognized the potential scientific value of the species and instructed that future Leviathan encounters should aim to capture the animals alive. She criticized Scathach for not capturing the larger specimen when the opportunity arose, but Scathach explained that she had simply encountered a peaceful natural animal and recorded it without interfering. Meanwhile, Ruby, Velnorah and Aline collected the carcass's heart, brain, eyes, bone marrow, stomach and other tissues for examination. The stomach's extraordinary Energy reserves impressed Ruby, who compared it to a black hole, while Aline attributed the concentration to the species' stellar diet.[1]
The family then turned to preparing the remaining body for dinner. Ordinary knives proved inadequate against its exterior, so Natasha suggested crafting specialized cutting implements from Divine and Demonic materials. Sasha used the Dragon Language to create the blades, allowing the butchering to proceed. [[Amaterasu]], initially astonished by the enormous creature, was asked to apply her Solar Divinity to help cook it. Although momentarily taken aback by the prospect of serving as the family's source of heat, she agreed and joined the preparations. Anna suggested preparing dishes from several culinary traditions rather than simply roasting the entire animal, emphasizing the unusual contrast between the creature's cosmic scale and its role in an ordinary family celebration.[1]
During the later gathering in Chapter 1169, Rosemary explained the Star Beast to sisters who had not yet studied Scathach's report, while Gina expressed far more interest in how it would taste. The family subsequently gathered for the banquet. Victor transformed the dining setting into an extravagant cosmic environment, creating furniture from astronomical material and arranging the celebration among the stars in recognition of the rare creature chosen for the meal. Kaguya served the prepared dishes, and the banquet commenced. The narrative does not provide a completed laboratory analysis of the species or a systematic assessment of its flavor, so neither detailed culinary qualities nor scientific conclusions beyond the earlier examination should be invented.[2]
