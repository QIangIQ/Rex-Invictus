Yokai are a highly diverse collection of supernatural species historically concentrated in Japan. Rather than representing a single uniform biological species, the term encompasses numerous supernatural populations with radically different bodies, natural abilities and traditions, including Kitsune, Oni, Ryuji, Nekomata, Tanuki and many other varieties. Chapter 337 explicitly notes that Yokai come from different Clans and species and that each possesses different characteristics and areas in which they excel.[1]
For much of their history, the Yokai were divided among independent Clans and local rulers rather than operating as a single political race. This began changing under [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]], who gathered Yokai belonging to numerous unrelated species beneath her banner and established one of the most powerful known [[Hyakki Yakō]]. Through the traditional Yokai system of challenges, alliances and demonstrations of strength, Haruna progressively consolidated much of Japan's Yokai population beneath her authority.[1][4]
The principal Energy historically associated with Yokai is Youki, a negative form of Energy that accumulates from negative emotions produced by Humans. Yokai absorb this Energy and use it to strengthen themselves, with Japan possessing unusually favorable conditions for Youki because of an ancient supernatural event involving the death of an unidentified monster.[5] This does not mean Yokai themselves are created from Human negativity; the novel establishes Human negative emotions as the source of Youki, not the origin of the Yokai race.
The limitations of Youki eventually became a major political and military concern. Because Haruna intended for the Yokai to become a faction capable of operating outside Japan rather than remaining geographically dependent upon their homeland, the Three Great Yokai Clans began searching for alternative sources of power. Their research resulted in the rediscovery and development of [[Senjutsu]] and Ki, two systems based respectively upon Nature's Energy and the user's own vitality.[5] These developments, combined with Haruna's alliance with [[Victor Elderblood]], eventually brought the Yokai into [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]] and later the [[Elderblood Empire]].[6][2][7]

### Nature
Yokai are not one physically standardized race. The classification encompasses many distinct supernatural species whose anatomy can range from largely humanoid to animalistic, monstrous or draconic. Some possess tails, claws, fangs, horns or other natural weapons, while others can appear almost entirely Human unless they deliberately reveal their supernatural characteristics.[1][4]
Consequently, statements concerning one Yokai species should not automatically be generalized to every other Yokai. A Kitsune's tails and supernatural development, an Oni's physical characteristics, a Ryuji's draconic body and a Nekomata's feline characteristics belong to their respective species rather than representing a single universal Yokai physiology.
This diversity also extends to strength. Some Yokai are relatively minor supernatural beings, while ancient Clan leaders and exceptionally evolved individuals can compete with the upper ranks of Earth's supernatural world. Haruna, Genji, [[Shuten Douji]] and other major leaders are therefore not appropriate representatives of an average Yokai's physical statistics.

#### Longevity
Yokai possess extraordinarily long lives and are described as being effectively immortal under ordinary circumstances. While explaining the dangers of Ki, Haruna states that Yokai, like Vampires, are essentially immortal until they are killed.[5]
This should be understood primarily as agelessness rather than absolute immortality. Yokai can still die through sufficient physical destruction, supernatural attacks, misuse of their own vitality or other means capable of killing their particular species.[8][5]

### Youki
Youki is the traditional supernatural Energy utilized by the Yokai and was historically the foundation of their power.
Chapter 564 explains that Youki is an accumulation of negative Energy produced by Humans. Yokai absorb this Energy from their surroundings and use it to strengthen themselves.[5]
Japan possesses an unusually large concentration of Youki. According to Haruna's explanation, the death of an unidentified monster in ancient times altered the region in such a way that Japan became particularly favorable for the accumulation of this negative Energy.[5]
This circumstance allowed Japanese Yokai civilization to flourish but simultaneously created one of its greatest strategic weaknesses. A race that developed while surrounded by a regional supply of Youki could not easily carry the same environmental advantage into foreign territories.

#### Territorial Limitation
By the time of the third [[Supernatural Gathering]], Haruna had already recognized that continued dependence on Youki was dangerous. The Yokai were developing from scattered Japanese Clans into an international faction, yet their principal Energy was strongly tied to particular environmental conditions.[5]
This problem motivated Haruna to search for a new Energy system. Her objective was not necessarily to completely eliminate Youki but to provide the Yokai with a source of supernatural power that did not leave them crippled simply because they left Japan.[5]

#### Relationship with Negative Emotions
Youki's connection with negative Human emotion should not be confused with Demonic Miasma. Both may possess negative characteristics, but they belong to different supernatural systems and are produced through different mechanisms.
Likewise, Yokai are not established as creatures born directly from Human fear, hatred or negative thought. Human negativity creates and accumulates Youki; the exact original creation of the Yokai race remains unexplained in the novel.

### Hyakki Yakō
The [[Hyakki Yakō]] is one of the most important social and supernatural institutions of Yokai civilization.
Although the term can literally describe a parade or gathering of supernatural creatures, among the Yokai it represents considerably more than a ceremonial procession. Chapter 337 describes the Hyakki Yakō as proof of a ruler. A Yokai who manages to gather several Yokai from different Clans beneath their own banner fulfills the requirements necessary to lead one.[1]
The variety of species present is itself significant. Because Yokai Clans traditionally possess their own loyalties, cultures and rulers, successfully bringing many unrelated groups together demonstrates the leader's political influence, charisma and personal strength.

#### Authority and Power
A leader's [[Hyakki Yakō]] is directly connected to their power. During Haruna's appearance in Chapter 337, it is explained that the Yokai present who acknowledged her as their superior became part of the strength supporting her.[1]
This relationship is not identical to a Werewolf Alpha's biological pack system, but it similarly demonstrates that Yokai leadership can possess a supernatural component rather than being purely political.

#### Challenge System
A [[Hyakki Yakō]] can also be used to challenge other Yokai leaders. Chapter 338 explains that the gathering serves as a recruitment and battle event: a leader possessing a Hyakki Yakō can demand a confrontation with another qualified leader, and defeat can obligate the defeated party to join the victor's side.[4]
Haruna used this system during her attempt to recruit [[Hashimoto Genji]], the leader of the Fox Yokai. Her objective was not simply to defeat rivals but to systematically consolidate Japan's strongest Yokai beneath a single authority.[4]

#### Haruna's Hyakki Yakō
Haruna developed an exceptionally advanced connection with the Yokai under her command.
During the conflict surrounding her [[Hyakki Yakō]], members of Haruna's side who suffered mutilation or otherwise severe injuries rapidly recovered. At the same time, corresponding injuries accumulated across Haruna's own body before she healed them through her dark power.[8]
This is important because the scene has sometimes been interpreted as evidence that every Yokai possesses extreme regeneration. The chapter instead directly associates the phenomenon with Haruna and the supernatural relationship between her and the army beneath her. Yokai on opposing sides are shown dying normally during the same conflict.[8]
Haruna's demonstrated Hyakki Yakō abilities should therefore remain primarily on her individual article rather than being generalized as standard racial regeneration.

### Political Structure
Historically, Yokai society was fragmented between numerous independent Clans and species. Strength and reputation played important roles in determining authority, while individual Clan leaders frequently possessed enormous autonomy.
Haruna changed this structure by building a sufficiently powerful [[Hyakki Yakō]] to bring otherwise independent populations under one banner. Her status eventually progressed beyond leader of the [[Otsuki Clan]] and became that of the principal ruler and Supreme Commander of the Yokai aligned with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[1][2]
Even after Haruna herself later ceased being biologically Yokai following her transformation into a Blood Dragon and Dragon Goddess, her political relationship with the race continued. Her transformation changed her species, not the loyalty of the population she had spent centuries gathering beneath her authority.[3]

#### Three Great Yokai Clans
By Chapter 564, three groups are explicitly identified as the Three Great Yokai Clans: the Oni, the Ryuji and the Foxes/Kitsune.[5]
These Clans were gathered together by Haruna when the Yokai began searching for an alternative to Youki. Their combined knowledge ultimately led to the discovery of [[Senjutsu]] and Ki.

##### Kitsune
Main article: Kitsune
Kitsune are fox Yokai and one of the Three Great Clans. They are among the most prestigious and powerful Yokai species shown in the story.
Their development is strongly associated with their tails. Chapter 338 explains that the number of tails possessed by a Kitsune reflects its level of supernatural development, with a nine-tailed fox representing the pinnacle normally associated with the Yokai race and standing extremely close to Godhood.[4]
The exact circumstances through which tails develop can vary. Nine-tailed individuals are extraordinarily rare, and later material establishes that unusual Kitsune such as Haruna and Genji possessed exceptional natural characteristics from birth.[9]
Kitsune are also natural masters of disguise. Haruna explains that hiding their Yokai characteristics is instinctive for them, allowing a Kitsune to appear completely Human even though revealing ears and tails can be a source of pride.[9]
Known powerful Kitsune include:
- [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]]
- [[Hashimoto Genji]]
- [[Otsuki Hana]]
- [[Otsuki Yoichi]]

##### Oni
Oni are one of the Three Great Yokai Clans and are represented by powerful warriors such as [[Shuten Douji]] and [[Ibaraki Douji]]. They are generally portrayed as highly physical and combat-oriented Yokai, possessing imposing bodies and a strong warrior culture.
The Oni were important enough that Haruna specifically gathered their Clan alongside the Kitsune and Ryuji when deciding the future of Yokai supernatural development.[5]
Statements from traditional mythology concerning every Oni eating Humans, possessing identical magical powers or behaving as uniformly violent creatures should not automatically be treated as novel canon.

##### Ryuji
Ryuji are a draconic Yokai Clan and one of the Three Great Yokai Clans. They possess long draconic bodies and powerful breath attacks, with Haruna describing members of the Clan as valuable soldiers capable of causing significant destruction on a battlefield.[5]
The Ryuji also discovered the system of planetary Energy channels they called Dragon Veins. They were aware that an enormous source of power circulated through these fissures but had never successfully developed a safe method for using it themselves.[5]
Their discovery ultimately became the foundation of Haruna's [[Senjutsu]] research.
Despite their name and draconic appearance, Ryuji should not automatically be classified as members of the setting's True Dragon race. Within the novel they are introduced as a Yokai Clan whose members possess draconic forms. No verified statement establishes that they possess the full racial nature, Soul, status or physiology of [[True Dragons]].

#### Other Yokai Species
Numerous other Yokai species exist outside the Three Great Clans. The novel repeatedly emphasizes that Japan contains many Clans and species with their own specialized characteristics.[1]

##### Nekomata
Nekomata are feline Yokai recognizable by their cat-like features and multiple tails. [[Yotsuba Kuroka]], Haruna's second-in-command and one of her most trusted subordinates, is a prominent example.
Kuroka's status demonstrates that political importance among the Yokai is not restricted to the Three Great Clans.

##### Tanuki
Tanuki are Yokai particularly associated with disguise and transformation. Chapter 587 directly compares their instinctive disguise abilities with those of Kitsune.[9]
Tanuki can conceal their supernatural characteristics and imitate Human appearances, making appearance alone especially unreliable when attempting to identify the power or species of a Yokai.
Other Yokai forms undoubtedly exist, but classifications based entirely upon real-world Japanese folklore should not be added as novel canon unless the corresponding species actually appears or is identified within the story.

### Senjutsu
[[Senjutsu]] is a supernatural discipline developed by Haruna's faction after the Yokai began searching for an Energy source capable of overcoming Youki's territorial limitations.[5]
Rather than drawing strength from Human negativity, Senjutsu uses Nature's Energy as its medium. The amount that can be controlled depends upon the practitioner's stamina, proficiency and ability to maintain the necessary mental state.[5]
Haruna first gained access to this system through the Dragon Veins revealed by the Ryuji Clan. After entering one of these locations, she discovered an enormous neutral Energy flowing through the planet. By meditating and achieving complete emotional calm, she became capable of allowing this Energy to enter her body.[5]

#### Connection to the World Tree
The true source of [[Senjutsu]] became clearer when Haruna later taught the system to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
The so-called Dragon Veins are planetary Ley Lines, described as Mother Earth's blood vessels through which Energy circulates across the world. When Victor absorbed the Energy contained within one, he recognized it as strangely familiar.[10]
[[Roxanne Elderblood]] subsequently identified the power Haruna had been using as remnants of the Energy belonging to Earth's World Tree, [[Yggdrasil]].[10]
Senjutsu therefore represents a method through which a practitioner draws upon Nature's Energy circulating through a World Tree's planetary system.

#### Energy Absorption and Filtering
Haruna does not initially use the raw planetary Energy directly. She draws Nature's Energy into herself and filters it through her body before using it safely.[10]
With sufficient proficiency, direct use of [[Senjutsu]] becomes possible, but doing so places considerably greater strain upon the practitioner's body.[10]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] represented an exceptional case because he possessed his own World Tree within his Soul. Rather than relying upon the residual Energy of Earth's [[Yggdrasil]] in the same manner as Haruna, he could access the corresponding source directly through Roxanne.[10]

#### Mental Requirements
[[Senjutsu]] requires a state of extraordinary calm.
Haruna discovered that the Energy would enter her properly only while she was meditating with her heart and mind completely settled. Her own martial arts already used similar principles, making her unusually compatible with the discipline.[5]
Attempting to control Senjutsu while mentally turbulent can cause severe internal damage. Haruna states that misuse can injure the body and, in extreme circumstances, damage the user's Soul. Several Ryuji died after attempting to force the Energy without the required control.[5]

#### Conflict with Youki
[[Senjutsu]] and Youki naturally conflict.
Youki is negative Energy accumulated from Humans, whereas Senjutsu uses neutral Nature Energy. Placing both within the same body therefore causes opposing Energies to interfere with one another.[5]
When the system was first introduced, Haruna was the only Yokai who had managed to control both Energies to even a limited degree, and she acknowledged that she remained far from mastering the interaction.[5]
This makes the old interpretation that the entire Yokai race simply "replaced Youki with Senjutsu" inaccurate. Senjutsu began as a newly discovered discipline with demanding requirements and serious risks.

### Ki
Ki is the second discipline discovered during the Yokai's [[Senjutsu]] research. Unlike Senjutsu, which draws upon Nature's external Energy, Ki uses the practitioner's own vitality or Life Energy as its medium.[5]
Once Haruna learned to perceive the Nature Energy entering her body, she simultaneously became aware of her own internal vitality and discovered that it could be deliberately manipulated.

#### Relationship with Senjutsu
Haruna describes Ki as effectively a sub-art developed from [[Senjutsu]].
Senjutsu forms the beginning of the system by using Nature Energy, while Ki applies similar principles through the practitioner's own life force. The two complement one another: incorporating Senjutsu into the body reduces the amount of vitality consumed when Ki is used.[5]

#### Lifespan Consumption
Ki is considerably more dangerous than ordinary Energy reserves because the practitioner is directly consuming their own vitality.
Using it recklessly can burn through the user's entire lifespan and result in death. Haruna explicitly notes that even ageless Yokai are not exempt from this consequence.[5]
The technique therefore requires strict control and should not be treated as an unlimited source of power simply because Yokai ordinarily do not die from aging.

#### Universal Learnability
Neither [[Senjutsu]] nor Ki is biologically exclusive to Yokai.
Haruna explicitly states that anyone can learn the two disciplines provided they satisfy the necessary requirements.[5]
The Yokai are historically important because Haruna and the Three Great Clans were responsible for developing the practical system used during this period, not because Nature Energy or Ki belongs exclusively to their Race.

### History

#### Fragmented Yokai Society
For much of recorded supernatural history, Japan's Yokai population was divided among numerous Clans, species and independent authorities. The race lacked the unified political structure possessed by groups such as [[Nightingale]]'s Vampires or Samar's [[Werewolves]].
Powerful figures commanded their own territories and followers, with [[Hyakki Yakō]] traditions providing one of the principal mechanisms through which a strong ruler could demonstrate authority and absorb rival groups.
The Shinto Gods simultaneously held enormous supernatural authority over Japan, but Yokai society was not simply synonymous with the [[Shinto Pantheon]]. Certain Yokai maintained relationships with the Gods while others followed independent leaders.

#### Rise of Haruna
[[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]] emerged as one of the strongest leaders among the Yokai and began systematically gathering the race beneath her own banner.
By Chapter 337, Haruna commanded a [[Hyakki Yakō]] containing Yokai from numerous Clans and species. Her appearance in Shibuya served both as a demonstration of overwhelming political influence and as part of her campaign to recruit other powerful leaders.[1]
Haruna then challenged [[Hashimoto Genji]], recognized as the leader of the Fox Yokai and one of their greatest figures. Under Hyakki Yakō tradition, defeating another qualified commander could force that leader to join the victor's faction.[4]
This process gradually transformed Haruna from the head of one Kitsune Clan into the Supreme Commander of a much broader Yokai faction.

#### Conflict and Alliance with Victor
Haruna's growing faction eventually came into contact with [[Victor Elderblood]] and the Vampire forces surrounding him.
Despite early political conflict and mutual manipulation, Victor and Haruna progressively developed a relationship built upon respect for one another's strength, ambition and willingness to improve their people.
By the third [[Supernatural Gathering]], the Yokai were already treated as an independent supernatural faction capable of participating in negotiations alongside Vampires, [[Werewolves]], Gods, Witches and other world powers.[11][12]
Haruna later formally aligned her people with Victor. This relationship became increasingly important after the Demonic War and the destabilization of Earth's previous supernatural order.

#### Search for a New Energy
Haruna understood that international expansion was impossible if the Yokai remained completely dependent upon Japan's Youki.
She consequently gathered the Oni, Ryuji and Fox Clans—the Three Great Yokai Clans—to find a solution.[5]
The Ryuji revealed the existence of the Dragon Veins, allowing Haruna to begin experimenting with the neutral Energy circulating through the Earth. After repeated trial and error, she developed the disciplines later named [[Senjutsu]] and Ki.[5]
These discoveries represented one of the most significant changes in modern Yokai development because they offered the race a path toward supernatural power that was not completely dependent upon accumulated Human negativity.

#### Integration into Victor's Faction
As [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction became increasingly multi-racial, the Yokai were formally incorporated into its political structure.
By Chapter 730, [[Yotsuba Kuroka]] served as the Yokai representative in Victor's Council of [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] because Haruna herself frequently needed to remain within Yokai territory to manage her political responsibilities.[6]
Haruna nevertheless remained the principal leader of the faction and continued to be identified as the authority representing the Yokai among Victor's allied races.[2]

#### Relocation to the Former Egyptian Pantheon
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] conquered the [[Egyptian Pantheon]], he ordered allied populations lacking independent homelands comparable to Samar to move into the newly acquired territory.
The Yokai under Haruna were explicitly among the populations relocated there, alongside allied Vampires, Humans and Goddesses.[7]
This event ended the race's exclusive geographical identification with Japan. Japan remained their historical homeland, but the Yokai had become part of a much larger supernatural civilization under Victor.
The former Egyptian territory later developed into one of the principal population centers associated with Victor's faction and eventually the [[Elderblood Empire]].

#### Elderblood Empire
The Yokai later became one of the many supernatural populations incorporated into the [[Elderblood Empire]].
Their historical identity, Clans and traditions continued to exist, but political allegiance increasingly shifted toward [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s multispecies civilization rather than the isolated supernatural order that had previously dominated Japan.
Haruna herself eventually underwent a complete racial transformation and became a Blood Dragon and Dragon Goddess. Chapter 938 explicitly shows that her default body had become Draconic and that she was no longer biologically a Kyūbi no Kitsune, even though she could still instinctively shapeshift into her former fox appearance.[3]
Her personal transformation should not be confused with the evolution of the Yokai race as a whole. There is no canon statement that every Yokai was transformed into a Blood Dragon.

### Powers and Abilities
Yokai Physiology: Yokai encompass numerous supernatural species rather than one physically identical race. Their abilities therefore vary greatly depending on species, Clan, age, individual development, Energy reserves and training. The abilities below distinguish broadly shared characteristics from species-specific and learned powers.

#### General Racial Abilities
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Yokai possess supernatural bodies whose strength, speed, durability, reflexes and stamina exceed those of ordinary Humans. The degree of enhancement varies enormously between species and individuals; a minor Yokai should not be equated with ancient leaders such as Haruna, Genji or [[Shuten Douji]].[1][4]
- [[Immortality]] (Agelessness): Yokai are described as being essentially immortal until killed, allowing them to live indefinitely without dying naturally from old age under ordinary circumstances.[5] This does not prevent death from violence, supernatural attacks, Soul damage or misuse of Ki.
- Energy Manipulation (Youki): Yokai traditionally manipulate Youki, the negative Energy accumulated from Human emotions within their environment.[5]
- Energy Absorption (Youki): Yokai absorb accumulated Youki from their surroundings and incorporate it into their supernatural power.[5]
- Statistics Amplification (Youki): Youki is absorbed specifically to strengthen Yokai, increasing the supernatural power available to them.[5]
- Shapeshifting: Numerous Yokai species can alter their physical appearance. The degree varies by species, with some possessing Human forms, supernatural forms or alternate animalistic appearances.[1][9]
- Natural Weaponry: Many Yokai possess anatomical weapons appropriate to their species, including claws, fangs, horns, tails and other supernatural body structures. These characteristics are species-dependent rather than universal.
- Enhanced Senses: Many animalistic Yokai possess sensory abilities beyond ordinary Humans. The extent depends upon species; Kitsune, Nekomata and other animal-associated Yokai should not automatically be assumed to possess identical senses.

#### Species-Specific Abilities
The following powers are demonstrated by particular Yokai species and should not be assigned automatically to the entire race.
- Disguise Mastery (Kitsune and Tanuki): Kitsune and Tanuki possess instinctive mastery over disguising their supernatural characteristics. Haruna explains that members of both species can easily conceal their Yokai traits and present themselves as Humans.[9]
- Power Evolution (Kitsune): A Kitsune's tails are associated with its supernatural development. Increasing tail count reflects progressively greater power, with nine-tailed foxes representing an extraordinarily rare level close to Godhood.[4]
- Flight (Certain Species): Yokai possessing wings or suitable supernatural abilities can fly. Flight is not a universal Yokai racial ability.
- Breath Attack (Ryuji): Ryuji possess draconic breath attacks powerful enough for Haruna to regard them as highly valuable battlefield soldiers.[5]
Different Yokai species may possess additional individual abilities, but powers derived solely from traditional Japanese mythology should not be attributed to their novel counterparts without chapter evidence.

#### Hyakki Yakō-Related Abilities
- Power Bestowal / Statistics Amplification ([[Hyakki Yakō]] Leader): Yokai gathered beneath a qualified Hyakki Yakō leader become part of the supernatural strength supporting that ruler.[1]
The exact expression of this relationship depends upon the commander. Haruna demonstrated considerably more advanced abilities than the basic requirements of a Hyakki Yakō.
- Damage Transferal (Haruna): Haruna could receive injuries corresponding to damage suffered by Yokai serving beneath her, allowing otherwise mutilated subordinates to recover rapidly.[8]
- Healing (Haruna): Haruna repeatedly healed the injuries transferred onto her body using her own dark power.[8]
These two abilities are demonstrated specifically through Haruna's command structure and should not be listed as universal Yokai powers.

#### Learnable Abilities
The following systems were developed by the Yokai but can theoretically be learned by other races.
- [[Natural Energy]] Manipulation ([[Senjutsu]]): Senjutsu allows a practitioner to draw upon neutral Nature Energy circulating through planetary Ley Lines. Haruna developed the system after discovering how to absorb the Energy contained within the Ryuji's Dragon Veins.[5]
- Energy Absorption (Senjutsu): Practitioners draw external Nature Energy into their bodies. Haruna initially needed to absorb and filter this power before safely using it.[5][10]
- World Tree Energy Manipulation (Senjutsu): Chapter 607 reveals that the Energy accessed through Earth's Ley Lines consists of remnants of the power belonging to [[Yggdrasil]], Earth's World Tree.[10]
- Life-Force Manipulation (Ki): Ki allows a practitioner to perceive and consciously manipulate their own vitality.[5]
- Statistics Amplification (Ki/Senjutsu Techniques): Senjutsu and Ki can be incorporated into supernatural techniques to enhance the practitioner's capabilities. The exact effect depends upon the technique developed by the individual.[5]

### Weaknesses
- Mortality: Yokai are ageless but not absolutely immortal. They can be permanently killed through sufficient damage or supernatural means.[8][5]
- Decapitation / Head Destruction: Ordinary Yokai can die from severe destruction of the head or complete decapitation. Yokai are shown dying from such injuries during the conflict surrounding Haruna's [[Hyakki Yakō]].[8][13] Exceptional individuals possessing unusual regeneration or additional racial transformations may not necessarily share the same limitation.
- Youki Dependency: Traditional Yokai power is strongly dependent upon access to Youki, an environmental Energy accumulated from Human negativity. This makes relying exclusively upon Youki strategically disadvantageous when operating beyond regions where sufficient quantities are available.[5]
- Youki-[[Senjutsu]] Incompatibility: Youki and Senjutsu naturally conflict because one is negative Energy while the other is neutral Nature Energy. Attempting to maintain both simultaneously requires exceptional control.[5]
- Senjutsu Instability: Senjutsu requires complete mental calm. Attempting to manipulate Nature Energy while emotionally turbulent can damage the user's internal body and, in extreme cases, injure the Soul.[5]
- Ki Overuse: Ki consumes the user's own vitality. Reckless use can burn the practitioner's entire lifespan and kill them, including otherwise ageless Yokai.[5]
- Species-Specific Weaknesses: Because Yokai comprise many different species, particular weaknesses may apply only to a specific species and should not automatically be generalized across the entire race.

### Relationship with the Shinto Pantheon
Yokai and the [[Shinto Pantheon]] historically occupied the same supernatural territory but should not be treated as a single faction.
Certain Yokai maintained close relationships with Shinto Gods. [[Hashimoto Genji]], for example, was directly associated with [[Inari-Omikami]] and held an important position connected to the Divine Realm.[4]
Other Yokai operated independently beneath their own Clan leaders. Haruna's rise increasingly created a secular Yokai faction whose loyalty centered around her rather than direct service to the Shinto Gods.
This distinction became more politically important as Haruna sought alliances outside Japan and eventually aligned her people with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction.

### Relationship with Humans
Humans play an unusually important role in the supernatural ecosystem of the Yokai because their negative emotions create Youki.[5]
This does not mean Yokai require Human flesh or blood for survival. Rather, Human civilization historically provided the emotional and environmental conditions responsible for generating their traditional Energy source.
Japan's extraordinarily dense Human population and supernatural history consequently made it a valuable habitat for Youki-dependent Yokai.
After [[Senjutsu]] and Ki were discovered, the race gained the possibility of becoming progressively less dependent upon this Human-generated source.

### Relationship with Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s relationship with the Yokai began through political and Clan conflicts but eventually became one of the most important alliances in the race's modern history.
Haruna viewed Victor as both a dangerous rival and an extraordinarily useful ally. Victor, in turn, recognized the Yokai as a large supernatural population whose unusual cultures, martial traditions and Energy systems had considerable potential.
The alliance eventually became strong enough for Yokai representatives to participate directly in Victor's government.[6] Haruna remained one of the racial leaders identified within his faction by Chapter 848.[2]
After Victor acquired the former [[Egyptian Pantheon]], the Yokai were among the allied populations ordered to relocate into the newly controlled territory.[7] Their later incorporation into the [[Elderblood Empire]] transformed the race from a primarily Japanese supernatural faction into part of a much larger interspecies civilization.
Haruna's own eventual transformation into a Blood Dragon further deepened this connection without changing the species of her subjects.[3]

### Trivia
- Yokai are explicitly described as consisting of numerous different Clans and species rather than a single standardized supernatural body.[1]
- The [[Hyakki Yakō]] is considered proof of rulership because a commander must first gather Yokai belonging to several different Clans beneath their banner.[1]
- A Hyakki Yakō can function as a recruitment challenge in which the defeated ruler is obligated to join the victor's side.[4]
- Fox Yokai are identified in Chapter 338 as one of the most prestigious and powerful Yokai groups, with nine-tailed Kitsune standing close to Godhood.[4]
- Kitsune and Tanuki possess instinctive disguise abilities that allow them to conceal their Yokai characteristics and appear Human.[9]
- The three Great Yokai Clans explicitly gathered during Haruna's Energy research were the Oni, Ryuji and Fox/Kitsune Clans.[5]
- The Ryuji were responsible for discovering Earth's Dragon Veins, but Haruna was the one who successfully developed the practical method that became [[Senjutsu]].[5]
- Youki and Senjutsu possess naturally opposing characteristics: Youki is negative Energy while Senjutsu is based on neutral Nature Energy.[5]
- The Energy used by Haruna's Senjutsu was later revealed to originate from remnants of the Energy belonging to Earth's World Tree, [[Yggdrasil]].[10]
- Ki can kill an otherwise ageless Yokai if they consume too much of their own vitality.[5]
- Haruna stated that Senjutsu and Ki could theoretically be learned by any race capable of satisfying the disciplines' requirements.[5]
- [[Yotsuba Kuroka]] later represented the Yokai within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s early Council of [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] because Haruna's responsibilities prevented her from personally attending every governmental meeting.[6]
- The Yokai population under Haruna relocated to the former [[Egyptian Pantheon]] after Victor ordered his allied populations without independent territories to establish themselves there.[7]
- Haruna remained the recognized leader of the Yokai even as her personal racial development eventually carried her beyond the Yokai race itself.[2][3]
