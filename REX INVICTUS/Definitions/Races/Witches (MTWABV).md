Witches are a Human-derived supernatural lineage naturally compatible with [[Magic]] Energy. Although their physical bodies are fundamentally Human, true Witches possess a metaphysical connection to Magic that extends through their Blood, Essence and Souls. Later investigation revealed that this compatibility did not originate naturally from Earth's native Humanity: the ancestral source of Witch Magic came from beings outside Earth's original universe/Sector, and ordinary Humans who lack the required spiritual connection cannot naturally access the same Energy.[1]
Witches existed long before they became a unified political faction, but organized Witch society is comparatively young when compared with ancient supernatural civilizations such as the Vampires and [[Werewolves]]. The modern Witch Kingdom began consolidating during the European Witch Hunts between approximately the fifteenth and seventeenth centuries, when older Witches gathered persecuted members of their kind and the woman later recognized as the Witch Queen united them into a single supernatural community.[2] The resulting society adopted an extremely successful mercenary economy, selling magical products, contracts, rituals, Talismans, Artifacts and specialized services to other supernatural factions until Witch products became deeply integrated into the supernatural market.[3][2]
Their principal historical homeland was Arcane, a magically isolated territory created by [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]].[4] Arcane developed into a highly sophisticated society centered on magical research, scholarship, Artifacts and supernatural engineering rather than merely functioning as a mercenary headquarters.[5][6] After later events surrounding [[Emily Moriarty]] and the discovery of the ancestral source of Witch Magic, Arcane ceased operating as a fully independent kingdom. In Chapter 990, [[Victor Elderblood]] physically transferred the entire Witch kingdom into the technocratic city governed by Velnorah Xyphora Thaloria II, formally integrating the organized Witch population into his wider civilization.[7]

### Origin
For most of their history, Earth's Witches did not know why certain Humans were naturally capable of using [[Magic]] while ordinary Humans were not. This question was finally addressed during the ritual performed by Evie and [[Emily Moriarty]] in Chapter 831. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], after attaining a level of perception that allowed him to better understand different forms of Energy, recognized that the "Magic" used by Witches was an Energy possessing a specific requirement: the user normally needed Blood or Essence originating from the outsiders connected to the ancestral source of Witch-kind.[1]
Albedo further clarified that the requirement is not merely conventional genetics. The connection is metaphysical and tied to the Soul itself, explaining why Witch Magic remains associated with particular lineages despite Witches otherwise appearing biologically Human.[1] This ancestral connection ultimately led to the discovery of a distant civilization associated with the origin of Witch Magic, commonly referred to as the Magic World. The exact cosmological classification of that world should be treated separately on its own page, but Chapter 831 establishes that the source of Witch Magic originated outside Earth's native supernatural environment.[1]
The rule is not completely impossible to bypass. [[Merlin]] was capable of using the same form of Magic despite lacking the normal natural compatibility because Albedo personally Blessed him with the ability after becoming interested in his theories concerning Energy.[1] Merlin should therefore not be classified as an ordinary male Witch; his use of Witch Magic was an externally granted exception.

### Biology
Witches retain fundamentally Human bodies. They require food, rest and ordinary physical sustenance and do not naturally possess the extreme physical strength, speed, regeneration or durability of races such as Vampires, [[Werewolves]] and Dragons.[8] Their supernatural advantage instead comes from their compatibility with [[Magic]], allowing them to compensate for their physically weaker bodies through barriers, prepared spells, Artifacts, Territory, enchantments and other magical systems.
This Human-derived physiology makes instantaneous attacks particularly dangerous. Selena explained that Witches are vulnerable to assassinations and sudden attacks that prevent them from activating their Magic, which is one reason the Witch faction historically regarded members of the [[Blank Clan]] as especially troublesome opponents.[8]
Despite possessing mortal Human-derived bodies, powerful Witches can extend their lives far beyond ordinary Human limits using Magic. Several of Evie's highest-ranking daughters were already more than two thousand years old while remaining physically youthful.[9] Magical longevity should not be confused with the natural biological immortality of Vampires; it is maintained through supernatural methods rather than being an inherent ageless racial physiology.

### Gender and Magical Compatibility
Most natural Witches shown throughout the story are women, and Chapter 831 describes the normal compatibility requirement in terms of women carrying the Blood of the outsiders associated with Witch [[Magic]].[1] However, this should not be presented as an absolute cosmological law stating that no man can ever use Witch-derived Magic.
Two important exceptions exist.

#### Merlin
[[Merlin]] was capable of using Witch [[Magic]] because Albedo personally Blessed him with the necessary compatibility. Albedo explicitly explains this in Chapter 831 after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] questions how Merlin could use the same Magic as Witches despite lacking the normal ancestral requirement.[1]
Merlin is therefore a Magic user who bypassed the racial limitation rather than evidence that every Human male can naturally become a Witch.

#### Clan Alioth
[[Clan Alioth]] represents a different irregularity. Their Bloodline originally derived from [[Magic]] and specializes exclusively in Time and Space-related powers.[10] Unlike conventional Witch lineages, however, the Alioth abilities evolved over time until they became closer to a unique inherited supernatural ability than ordinary Witch spellcasting. Chapter 386 explicitly notes that calling their modern abilities "Magic" is no longer entirely accurate.[10]
The Clan also demonstrates that male descendants can inherit this Magic-derived lineage, as [[Alexios Alioth]] possessed the family's Space and Time abilities.[10] For this reason, Clan Alioth is best described as a Variant Witch-related Bloodline rather than proof that conventional Witch Magic naturally manifests identically in men and women.

### History

#### Before the Witch Kingdom
Individual Witches existed long before they possessed a unified state. Several of the oldest known Witches are thousands of years old, and figures such as [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]], [[Evie Moriarty Elderblood]] and the oldest of Evie's daughters predate the modern Witch political faction by many centuries.[9][2] Witches originally existed in scattered groups rather than under one recognized supernatural government.

#### Witch Hunts and Unification
The European Witch Hunts became the major event responsible for unifying the scattered practitioners. Between approximately the fifteenth and seventeenth centuries, older Witches began gathering and protecting members of their kind who were being persecuted by Human society.[2]
Evie eventually transformed this loose collection into a functioning kingdom. Her charisma, power and commercial instincts allowed a community of suspicious and highly independent magical practitioners to become a centralized supernatural faction.[2]
The faction's comparatively young political age is an important distinction. Witches as supernatural beings existed long before the fifteenth century, but the organized Witch Kingdom began only after the Witch Hunts.[2]

#### Mercenary Witch Kingdom
Evie's political and economic model turned the Witch Kingdom into a supernatural mercenary power. Rather than attempting to compete directly with physically superior races through conventional warfare, Witches monetized the thing they possessed in abundance: magical knowledge.[2]
Their products and services became useful enough that the other supernatural factions increasingly depended upon them. Witch-made contracts, transformation rituals, Talismans, Artifacts and specialized magical services became part of the infrastructure of supernatural society.[3][11][12]
By Chapter 267, the Witch faction effectively held a monopoly over significant portions of the supernatural magical market. Their enormous network of clients and political connections also functioned as deterrence; attacking the Witch Kingdom risked antagonizing numerous other factions that depended upon Witch products.[2]
This commercial history created the widespread stereotype that Witches would do virtually anything for the correct payment. While many Witches did work professionally as mercenaries, this reputation should not be treated as the occupation or personality of every individual Witch.

#### Search for a Permanent Homeland
One of the major historical problems facing Witch society was territory. Vampires possessed [[Nightingale]], [[Werewolves]] possessed Samar, Demons possessed Hell and major Gods possessed Pantheon dimensions, while organized Witches remained confined to a hidden region of Earth.[2]
By Chapter 267, Arcane was suffering from a population and territorial crisis, and Witch leadership actively searched for a world capable of sustaining Human-derived life without requiring impossible amounts of permanent environmental [[Magic]].[2] This desire explains several later actions by Evie and the Witch faction.
The old objective of "Find their own world" should now be described as a historical goal rather than a current one.

#### Arcane
Arcane became the central homeland and political capital of Witch society. [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]] was its creator and possesses deeper control over its magical structure than Evie despite Evie historically acting as its public ruler.[4][13]
Arcane should not be described as a naturally independent universe. Chapter 705 clarifies that it is a protected land isolated through extremely powerful [[Magic]] rather than an entirely separate naturally occurring dimension.[14]
The territory nevertheless functions effectively as an isolated magical realm because of its extensive barriers and access restrictions. It contains magical infrastructure, transportation, housing, research institutions and environmental systems constructed specifically for Witch society.[5][6]

#### Discovery of the Ancestral Source
Evie eventually attempted a massive ritual designed to establish contact with the source connected to Witch [[Magic]]. The ritual became the central event of Chapters 829–833 and resulted in direct confirmation that Witch Magic originated from outsiders beyond Earth's native supernatural environment.[15][1]
Emily possessed strong doubts about the ritual and correctly sensed that whatever existed beyond the connection was not simply a harmless ancestral homeland.[1] [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Albedo observed the process and later intervened as the situation became politically dangerous.
The event fundamentally changed Witch understanding of their own species. Witches were no longer merely Humans who inexplicably possessed Magic; their supernatural compatibility had a concrete external ancestral source.[1]

#### Integration into Victor's Civilization
The independent political status of Arcane eventually ended. Following later conflicts involving Emily and several hostile Gods, Evie placed herself and her kingdom under [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s authority in exchange for his assistance.[7]
Victor then transferred the entire Arcane kingdom into the technocratic city ruled by Velnorah, where the Witch population would live as part of his civilization.[7] Evie retained the title of Witch Queen and continued to represent her people, but Arcane was no longer an independent sovereign supernatural kingdom operating outside Victor's authority.
This makes several entries on the older Witch page outdated:
- Base: Arcane remains historically correct, but Arcane itself is now integrated into Victor's civilization.
- Leader: Victor is politically accurate at the highest imperial level, while Evie remains the internal Witch Queen.
- Goal: Find their own world is obsolete.
- Occupation: Hired Mercenaries is too narrow to describe modern Witch society.

### Magic
[[Magic]] is the defining Energy of Witches. It is an exceptionally flexible Energy capable of producing a vast range of supernatural effects depending upon the caster's knowledge, Mana capacity, preparation and specialization.[16]
Witch Magic should be distinguished from abilities that happen to be called Magic but originate from different Energies. [[Hecate]]'s Divine Magic, for example, uses Divine Power and possesses superior raw quality, while conventional Witch Magic possesses far greater flexibility and can interact with a wider variety of phenomena.[16]
Chapter 790 specifically establishes that Witch Magic is sufficiently neutral and versatile to interact with multiple Elements, including opposing forces such as Light and Darkness.[16] This does not mean every Witch automatically knows every Elemental spell; it means the Energy system itself is capable of supporting those applications.

### Powers and Abilities
Witches do not possess one fixed combat ability comparable to the hereditary Element of a Noble Vampire Clan. Their capabilities are determined primarily by knowledge, natural talent, Mana reserves, specialization, preparation and accumulated magical research.[5][6] The abilities below represent confirmed disciplines demonstrated by members of the species; an ordinary Witch should not automatically be assumed to know every discipline.
Ability / Discipline
Description
[[Magic]] Manipulation
The fundamental supernatural ability of Witches. Their ancestral Soul/Blood compatibility allows them to naturally generate and manipulate Magic Energy.[16][1]
Spellcasting
Witches convert Magic into predetermined effects through spells, incantations, Magic Circles, gestures or other structured methods. Extremely familiar simple spells can eventually be activated with very little effort; Yue, for example, reduced a hair-color spell to a casual gesture after mastering it.[6]
Magic Circles
Witches can encode complex magical effects into structured Circles. Magic Circles are used in rituals, barriers, transformation systems, portals and numerous other applications. Ancient Witch Circles became an important part of Vampire transformation rituals.[3][1]
Elemental Magic
Witch Magic can interact with multiple conventional Elements rather than restricting the entire species to one hereditary Element.[16]
Light Magic
The neutral versatility of Witch Magic allows it to reproduce or interact with Light-related effects despite Witches not possessing Angelic or inherently Holy physiology.[16]
Darkness Magic
Witch Magic can likewise interact with Darkness-related phenomena, demonstrating that its Energy system can encompass normally opposed Elements.[16]
Barrier Magic
Witches can construct magical barriers for defense, concealment and territorial protection. Esther surrounded the Lost Club with numerous magical barriers.[17]
Prepared Magical Territory
Witches can layer a location with barriers, Circles and predetermined spells before a confrontation, making a prepared Witch considerably more dangerous than the same individual caught without her magical infrastructure.[17]
Bounded Fields
Witches can establish supernatural boundaries around territory that impose entry restrictions. A Witch Bounded Field capable of preventing lesser Demons from entering is explicitly referenced in Chapter 933.[18]
Contract Magic
Witches can create magically binding contracts between consenting parties. The black magical contract Vlad used when appointing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] as a Vampire Count was explicitly recognized as Witch Magic.[11]
Black Contracts
Exceptionally powerful contracts associated with the Witch Queen. Chapter 126 states that the black contract used by Vlad would normally require the Witch Queen's authority to break.[11]
Talisman Creation
Witches manufacture magical Talismans capable of reproducing predetermined supernatural effects. The artificial Full Moon Talismans used by [[Werewolves]] were Witch-made products.[12]
Artifact Creation and Replication
Advanced Witches can research and manufacture supernatural Artifacts. A Clandestine Witch successfully studied and produced numerous inferior copies of [[Hephaestus]]' Divine Hammer.[19][20]
Investigation / Analysis Magic
Witches can construct spells designed to analyze unfamiliar magical objects or supernatural structures. June used investigation Magic while examining a Dimensional Bag and attempted to understand how it could be reproduced.[5]
Health Magic
Witches can use Magic to maintain physical health and bodily condition. Yue explicitly states that she continuously uses Health Magic on herself.[6]
Appearance Alteration
Simple Magic can alter physical traits such as hair color. Yue developed a spell allowing her to change her hair color at will.[6]
Memory Magic
Specialized Witches can interact directly with memories. White possessed Magic capable of entering other Witches' memories, stealing magical knowledge despite mental defenses and converting memories into externally usable magical records.[21]
[[Necromancy]]
Certain Witches specialize in Magic involving the dead and related supernatural phenomena. [[Selena Moriarty]] is explicitly identified as a Necromancer.[22]
Emotional Regulation
Selena demonstrated Magic capable of regulating her own mental and emotional condition, allowing her to remain calm when interacting with overwhelming supernatural presences.[23]
Insect / Bug Magic
Individual Witches can develop highly specialized magical fields; Evie's Sixth Daughter specialized in insect-related Magic.[6]
Magical Longevity
Witches can use Magic to extend their Human-derived lifespan for centuries or millennia. Several of Evie's oldest daughters were already over two thousand years old.[9]

#### High-Level Magic
The scale of Witch [[Magic]] depends enormously on the practitioner. Ordinary Witches rely heavily on individual spells and prepared Circles, while exceptional beings can manipulate Magic on vastly larger scales.
[[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]] is the most extreme known example. As one of the greatest Witch researchers and later a Goddess, she can produce enormous quantities of Magic Circles almost instantaneously and employ Heroic-Class and large-scale strategic Magic capable of affecting Pantheon-level structures.[24][13]
These feats should not be generalized as abilities every Witch can perform; they demonstrate the upper potential of the magical system when used by an extraordinarily powerful practitioner.

### Specializations
Witches commonly devote themselves to particular fields rather than attempting to master every form of [[Magic]]. Knowledge and research are central to Witch strength, meaning centuries of study in a narrow discipline can produce abilities that a Witch with greater raw Mana may still be incapable of reproducing.[5][6]
Known specializations include memory Magic, [[Necromancy]], insect Magic, barriers, contracts, Artifact research, investigation Magic and other personalized fields. Arcane's culture encourages this specialization because new magical research provides both political prestige and economic value.[5]

### Variant Witch Lineages

#### Clan Alioth
[[Clan Alioth]] is an irregular [[Magic]]-derived Bloodline historically associated with Witch-kind but fundamentally different from ordinary Witches. Chapter 386 states that Witch Magic runs through the veins of Clan Alioth, but their lineage is restricted specifically to Space and Time-related abilities and cannot freely learn the broader Magic practiced by conventional Witches.[10]
The Alioth Bloodline changed across generations until its abilities became closer to an inherited unique power than conventional Magic.[10] This evolution explains why [[Alexios Alioth]] could possess the family's abilities despite being male and why characters questioned whether modern Alioth powers should still technically be called Witch Magic.
Known members include:
- Alexios Alioth
- Natalia Elderblood Alioth
Clan Alioth should therefore be treated as a Variant Witch-related lineage rather than ordinary members of the modern Witch species.

#### Externally Granted Witch Magic
A person lacking the natural Witch Bloodline can potentially use Witch [[Magic]] if a sufficiently powerful being grants the required compatibility. [[Merlin]] is the confirmed example. Albedo Blessed him because she was interested in his theories concerning Energy, allowing him to use the same fundamental Magic system despite lacking the ordinary ancestry.[1]
This does not transform the recipient into a conventional Witch; it grants access to the Energy.

### Arcane
Arcane was the center of organized Witch civilization and represents one of the strongest examples of [[Magic]] being developed into a complete societal infrastructure.

#### Structure
Arcane combines natural environments with Human-like architecture and extensive magical construction. Witches use flying brooms, flying carpets, enchanted vehicles and other [[Magic]]-powered forms of transportation.[5] The region's climate and seasons are regulated through Magic, demonstrating that Witch Magic is used for large-scale civilian infrastructure rather than merely individual combat.[5]
Complex Magic Circles are incorporated directly into buildings and private residences. June's home, for example, possessed sophisticated magical systems surrounding its structure and research areas.[5]

#### Research Culture
Research is one of the central pillars of Witch society. Magical discoveries possess enough social and economic importance that disagreements over research can become major disputes.[5]
Witches can resolve such conflicts through:
- Magical duels.
- Competitions of [[Magic]] research.
- Arbitration by one of the Witch Queen's daughters.[5]
A daughter serving as arbitrator is expected to remain neutral. Favoring one participant because of personal relationships can result in the loss of her title.[5]
This structure demonstrates that Witch society values magical competence and intellectual achievement alongside political rank.

### Clandestine Witches
Clandestine Witches are Witches who operate outside the authority and institutional structure of the Witch Queen. The term is fundamentally political rather than biological. Esther and White are examples of practitioners associated with the Lost Club rather than Arcane's official government.[17][21]
The older description stating that Clandestine Witches are "usually women who deceive supernatural beings who trust them" is too broad. Some rogue Witches engage in criminal behavior, but independence from the Witch Queen does not automatically establish one personality type or criminal method.

### Mercenary System
The organized Witch faction historically earned enormous wealth and influence through supernatural contracts and services. Its members could work for Vampires, [[Werewolves]], Gods, [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] and other supernatural factions as long as the assignment did not violate Witch law.[2]
Two important professional laws are explicitly established:
1. A Witch must not deliberately enter direct conflict with another Witch because their employers are enemies. If two contracts would require Witches to fight one another, both are expected to abandon the conflicting assignment.[25]
2. After accepting the initial payment for an approved mission, a Witch cannot simply abandon the contract without appropriate cause.[25]
These restrictions protected Witch society from being destroyed by outside factions hiring Witches against one another.

### Weaknesses and Limitations
Weakness / Limitation
Description
Human-Derived Physiology
Witches do not naturally possess the extreme physical bodies of Vampires, [[Werewolves]] or Dragons and can be killed through comparatively ordinary physical trauma if their [[Magic]] does not protect them.[8]
Instantaneous / Surprise Attacks
Selena identifies sudden attacks and assassinations as one of the most dangerous weaknesses of Witches because they can prevent the target from deploying defensive Magic.[8]
[[Blank Clan]]
Witches historically regarded the Blank Clan as particularly dangerous because their Shadow-based assassination methods exploit the race's vulnerability to surprise attacks.[8]
Mana Exhaustion
Completely exhausting a Witch's Mana source can cause unconsciousness, coma or death.[26]
Spell Backlash
Certain spells become dangerous once casting has progressed sufficiently far. Improperly stopping such a spell before completion can produce lethal backlash against the caster.[26]
Dependence on Knowledge
Witch Magic is extraordinarily versatile, but an individual practitioner can only perform applications she has learned or developed. Raw Mana alone does not automatically provide mastery of every magical discipline.[5][6]

### Relationship with Vampires
Witches possess an ancient commercial and magical relationship with Vampires. Witch [[Magic]] helped create several systems fundamental to Noble Vampire society, including controlled transformation rituals and other supernatural agreements.[3]
Witch-made magical contracts also became standard tools of Vampire politics; Vlad used a black Witch contract when formally establishing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] as the Fifth Vampire Count.[11]
The relationship was traditionally transactional rather than based on racial friendship, although individual Witch and Vampire factions later became closely integrated under Victor.

### Relationship with Werewolves
Witches historically supplied [[Werewolves]] with magical equipment and services. One of the clearest examples is the artificial [[Full Moon Talisman]] capable of reproducing the empowering effects of the Moon for Werewolves outside the natural lunar cycle.[12]

### Relationship with the Inquisition
Witches were historically persecuted by Human religious organizations and [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]], with the Witch Hunts playing a direct role in forcing scattered Witches to unite politically.[2] The Inquisition therefore became one of the traditional enemies of organized Witch society.
The relationship became substantially more complicated in later eras as the political structure surrounding Humans and supernatural races changed.

### Albedo Moriarty and the Witch Queen
The older page incorrectly risks conflating two different founding roles.
[[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]] is the creator of Arcane and possesses a deeper structural connection to the realm than Evie.[4][13] She is also Evie's mother and teacher and one of the greatest [[Magic]] researchers known in the setting.[15][24]
[[Evie Moriarty Elderblood]], however, is the woman responsible for consolidating the modern organized Witch faction into a successful political kingdom during and after the Witch Hunts.[2]
Therefore:
- Albedo should not simply be listed as "Founder of Witches".
- Albedo is the Creator of Arcane.
- Evie is the historical Witch Queen who unified and governed the modern Witch Kingdom.
- Witch-kind itself existed before either political organization was established.

### Current Status
Witches remain an active supernatural lineage, but the political status of organized Witch society has changed drastically from its early-story portrayal. Arcane is no longer an entirely independent mercenary kingdom hidden from the other supernatural powers. In Chapter 990, Evie accepted [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s authority and Victor transferred the entire Witch kingdom into Velnorah's technocratic city.[7]
Evie continues to hold the title of Witch Queen and serves as one of Victor's important magical specialists, while the Witch population remains culturally distinct within the wider civilization.[7] Albedo likewise operates inside Victor's sphere while continuing her research.
Consequently, the modern Witch faction is best understood as:
A distinct magical people and scholarly culture integrated into Dragons Nest and Victor's wider civilization, with Evie remaining their internal Witch Queen.

### Trivia
- The old statement that Witches are simply "female Humans who somehow acquire [[Magic]]" is incomplete. Their compatibility with Magic is connected metaphysically to an ancestral outsider lineage through their Blood/Essence and Soul.[1]
- Most ordinary natural Witches are women, but access to Witch-derived Magic is not absolutely impossible for males; [[Merlin]] received it through Albedo's Blessing, while [[Clan Alioth]]'s evolved Magic-derived Bloodline can manifest in men.[10][1]
- Clan Alioth's modern Time and Space powers are no longer considered ordinary Witch Magic despite originally deriving from it.[10]
- Not every Witch is a mercenary. Arcane contains researchers, craftsmen, magical technicians and ordinary inhabitants in addition to professional contractors.[5]
- Organized Witch society is considerably younger than the Witch species itself; the kingdom only consolidated around the fifteenth to seventeenth centuries.[2]
- Albedo created Arcane, while Evie created and governed the successful modern Witch political faction. These are separate roles.[2][4]
- Arcane is not a naturally separate universe; it is an isolated magical territory.[14]
- The goal of finding an independent world for Witches was historically important because Arcane lacked sufficient territory, but it is no longer an accurate current faction goal after the discovery of the ancestral Magic connection and Arcane's integration into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s civilization.[2][1][7]
- Witch Magic and [[Hecate]]'s Divine Magic are different systems. Hecate's possesses greater raw Divine power, while Witch Magic possesses much broader versatility.[16]
- Witches can use Magic corresponding to both Light and Darkness.[16]
- Witches developed supernatural products used extensively by other races, including Werewolf Full Moon Talismans and Vampire-related rituals.[3][12]
- A Clandestine Witch succeeded in mass-producing inferior copies of [[Hephaestus]]' Divine Hammer.[19][20]
- Arcane was physically relocated into Velnorah's city by Victor in Chapter 990.[7]
