The Humani, or whom call themselves humans, are a race created by [[Prometheus]], that became the dominant race over the world after the sinking of [[Danu Talis]]. The term humani was an insulting label created for their race by opposing [[Elders]] since the [[De Danann Empire]]. Eventually, humani became the norm scientific classification accepted by immortal humans, [[Next Generation]], Elders (Normal and Dark), [[Archons]] and [[Earthlords]].

### Description

#### Original History
Humani are naturally mortal but can become immortal by various means such as it being gifted by an Elder. Humani with strong enough auras are able to shape the magics once they have been awakened, but many do not survive to awakening process. Originally on [[Danu Talis]] the humani were slaves to the [[Elders]] however they were rescued during the sinking by [[Mars Ultor]] and others. [[Prometheus]] is shown to care a lot about his creation but came to dislike them because of how they treated him, even when he was the one who created them.

#### Parallel History
[[Virginia Dare]] and [[Dr. John Dee|John Dee]] were sent by [[Marethyu]] to lead the enslaved humans of the Earth Shadowrealm to freedom near the time of the Fall of [[Danu Talis]], the end of the [[De Danann Empire]].

#### First People
The first generation were called the [[First People]], and the first humans were [[Prometheus]]'s adoptive children: [[Tsagaglalal]] the Watcher, wife of [[Abraham the Mage]], and [[Gilgamesh]] the King.
