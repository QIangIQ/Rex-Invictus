Elementals are creatures that are heavily tied to a certain elemental magic. There are fire, air, water, and earth elementals, each of which embodies the element it represents. Elementals have some degree of power to influence and shape its respective element. Dr. John Dee set a fire elemental on [[Nicholas Flamel|Nicholas]] and Perenelle Flamel, however he lost control over the creature which ultimately led to the Great Fire of [[London]], destroying much of the city.
