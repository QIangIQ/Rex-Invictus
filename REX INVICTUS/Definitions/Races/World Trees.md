World Trees are ancient supernatural existences created by The Universal Tree to nourish, maintain and regulate life-bearing planets throughout Creation. They are not simply enormous magical plants: a World Tree is integrated into the biological, energetic and spiritual systems of the world under its care, with its Energy spreading throughout the planet and indirectly influencing the development of Life.[1]
Under the normal Rules governing Creation, a planet capable of sustaining Life requires the presence or equivalent function of a World Tree. Chapter 1136 explicitly identifies this as an absolute Rule created by the Primordials. A sufficiently exceptional existence can substitute for the function—as Victor Elderblood later planned to do for barren Imperial worlds by directly nourishing them with his own Energy—but this is an exception to the ordinary planetary system rather than proof that World Trees are unnecessary.[2]
World Trees ordinarily develop as complementary Positive and Negative aspects. The Positive World Tree begins the planetary life cycle and nourishes the emergence of Life, while the Negative World Tree develops once sentient life appears and regulates the Negative side of the world's natural and spiritual cycle.[3][4] Although aligned with opposite Energies, World Trees themselves are fundamentally neutral existences whose primary function is maintaining the planet and the Balance required for Life.[3]

### Nature

#### Planetary Higher Existences
World Trees are existences whose natural scale is comparable to the planet they support.
When [[Roxanne Elderblood]] reached adulthood, it was explained that the ordinary role of a World Tree is to sustain an entire planet. Because Roxanne instead existed inside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul, the same fundamental function was redirected toward supporting Victor's existence itself, producing a substantial improvement in his physical and spiritual foundation.[6]
The true importance of a World Tree therefore comes less from conventional destructive power and more from its existential function.
A World Tree serves as:
- a planetary source of Life-supporting Energy;
- a regulator of the world's natural systems;
- an anchor for the planet's supernatural ecosystem;
- a participant in the Positive/Negative Balance;
- and part of the infrastructure connecting material Life with spiritual and dimensional systems.
World Trees possess enormous Souls appropriate to this role. Chapter 1116 describes their Souls as essentially being on the scale of the planets they support.[5]

#### Daughters of the Universal Tree
[[The Universal Tree]] is not merely another ordinary planetary World Tree.
When the Universal Tree assumed responsibility for maintaining Life throughout the Universe, it created seeds that would become its daughters. Each daughter was assigned the function of supporting a planet capable of developing Life.[5]
Thus:

All naturally created World Trees are therefore sisters in the sense that they originate from the same Primordial source.
Chapter 1116 also establishes that they are all female from Creation, although their concept of femininity does not originally function in the same social or reproductive manner as that of Mortal women or Goddesses. They were created as components of an enormous cosmic system rather than as a naturally reproducing species.[5]

#### Age
A naturally assigned World Tree is approximately as ancient as the planet it supports.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explains that individual World Trees have existed long enough to witness the emergence of Gods and later Mortal civilizations on their worlds.[5]
Their perception of time is consequently radically different from that of short-lived species.

#### Dryad Form
World Trees can manifest humanoid forms generally identified as Dryads.
The Dryad is a sentient representation through which the World Tree can:
- communicate;
- interact with other Beings;
- move independently within its sphere;
- form relationships;
- and exercise its abilities with greater precision.
The existence of the humanoid avatar does not mean that the colossal Tree and the Dryad are separate Beings. They are expressions of the same World Tree existence.
[[Aurora Seraphina Evergreen]] demonstrates this clearly: despite appearing before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] in humanoid form, her consciousness and existence remained spread throughout Samar through her connection to the planet.[7]

#### Positive World Trees
The Positive World Tree is normally the first World Tree associated with a developing life-bearing planet.
Aurora explains that the Positive World Tree is effectively the first Being to establish itself upon the world. Over immense periods—potentially millions of years—it nourishes the planet until Life spreads throughout it.[4]
Its functions are aligned primarily with the Positive side of Creation.
Known Positive World Trees include:
- [[Yggdrasil]];
- [[Amara Elderblood]];
- [[Aurora Seraphina Evergreen]];
- [[Amaya Elderblood]];
- [[Diana]].
Their responsibilities include filtering and cycling Positive Energy, supporting Life and maintaining the conditions required for biological and supernatural development.[3]

#### Negative World Trees
Once sufficiently developed sentient life begins existing on a planet, the corresponding Negative World Tree gradually emerges.[4]
Negative World Trees regulate the opposite but equally necessary side of planetary Life.
Known Negative World Trees include:
- [[Qliphoth]];
- [[Roxanne Elderblood]];
- [[Aria Seraphina Evergreen]];
- [[Rosalyn Elderblood]];
- Diane.
Their function is not to destroy Life.
Roxanne explains that the Negative side filters the corruption and Negative Energy produced throughout the planet and returns the necessary portions to the planetary cycle. This allows processes such as:
- death;
- decomposition;
- decay;
- and other Negative aspects necessary for continued Life
to function normally.[3]
A world consisting only of unchecked Positive processes would therefore be no more naturally Balanced than one dominated entirely by Negativity.

#### Relationship Between the Two
Positive and Negative World Trees are simultaneously independent individuals and complementary parts of the same planetary system.
Aurora describes them as effectively the same overall function divided between distinct Beings.[4]
The older Positive World Tree normally possesses far greater experience because it exists before the Negative counterpart develops. The Positive World Tree is therefore expected to teach the younger Negative Tree how to fulfill its planetary role.[4]
[[Roxanne Elderblood]] became an unusual exception. Her natural development was altered after bonding with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] before receiving normal instruction from [[Nightingale]]'s corresponding Positive World Tree. This contributed to her developing rapidly while lacking much of the ordinary knowledge expected of a mature World Tree.[4]

### Functions

#### Planetary Nourishment
The fundamental function of every World Tree is to nourish its planet.
Its Energy travels throughout the world, indirectly influencing the development and maintenance of Life.[1]
This does not mean a World Tree individually designs every organism or consciously controls every evolutionary event. Their function is to establish and sustain the underlying planetary conditions in which Life can develop naturally.
By Chapter 1136, this principle is explicitly treated as a universal Rule:
Normal planetary Life requires World Tree support.[2]
A barren planet can therefore not simply be terraformed through ordinary technological manipulation while ignoring this metaphysical requirement.

#### Terraforming
World Trees can transform hostile environments into worlds capable of supporting Life.
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] first learned Roxanne's true nature, Ruby explained that planting a World Tree on an otherwise unsuitable planet would eventually allow the Being to terraform the environment.[8]
This process is not instantaneous.
A World Tree may require enormous periods to establish the planetary ecosystem, distribute its Energy and produce stable conditions for Life.[4]
The later explanation in Chapter 1136 confirms why ordinary technological terraforming alone is insufficient within the native Rules of Creation: the planet must still possess a World Tree or another Being capable of fulfilling the same metaphysical function.[2]

#### Positive and Negative Energy Regulation
World Trees continuously interact with the fundamental duality of Positivity & Negativity.
Roxanne describes the process as a planetary filtration system:
- the Positive side filters Positive forces and cycles them back into the world;
- the Negative side filters corruption and Negative forces and cycles the necessary Negativity back into the planetary system.[3]
Their role is therefore not to eliminate either side.
They maintain the circulation and Balance necessary for a living world.

#### Creation of Heavens and Hells
When a planet produces more Positive or Negative Energy than the material world can naturally process, World Trees can redirect the excess into separate dimensions associated with that planet.[3]
Excess Negativity can contribute to the formation of Hells.
Excess Positivity can similarly contribute to the formation of Heavens or Paradise-like dimensions used in the wider spiritual system.[3]
The simplified process is:
Living Planet
│
├── Excess Positive Energy
│        │
│        ▼
│   Heavenly / Paradise Realms
│
└── Excess Negative Energy
│
▼

These realms are supernatural dimensions connected to the planetary system rather than ordinary physical continents.
The exact number, organization and internal Laws of such dimensions can differ drastically between planets and Pantheons.

#### Pantheon and Dimensional Infrastructure
World Trees can support supernatural territories that later become associated with Pantheons.
Early explanations state that given sufficient time, a World Tree can establish a territory capable of becoming the basis of a Pantheon.[8]
This does not make a World Tree the property of whichever Pantheon later occupies those dimensions.
Their loyalty is fundamentally directed toward the planet and their cosmic function rather than toward one specific collection of Gods.
[[Yggdrasil]], for example, is popularly associated with the [[Norse Pantheon]] but is ultimately a World Tree of Earth rather than a Being whose existence belongs exclusively to the Norse Gods.[9]

#### Planetary Awareness
A mature World Tree possesses an extraordinary awareness of the planet to which it is connected.
Aurora describes herself as:
anywhere and nowhere
within her sphere of influence, calling the condition a form of semi-omnipresence.[7]
Through her planetary bond she could perceive the entire world because she was connected to:
- Nature;
- water;
- earth;
- and the wider planetary environment.[7]
This awareness should not be interpreted as unrestricted omnipresence throughout the Universe.
Its demonstrated scope is the world or system to which the World Tree is connected.

#### Influence over Developing Life
World Trees possess enormous amounts of instinctive knowledge concerning what they may safely influence.
Roxanne explains that their Energy naturally travels throughout the planet and affects developing Life indirectly. Because careless manipulation could interfere with Balance, the Universal Tree provides its daughters with the knowledge needed to understand their limitations.[1]
This knowledge may be directly implanted by their father or become available automatically as the World Tree reaches the appropriate developmental stage.[1]

### Guardians
Young World Trees can possess Guardians responsible for protecting them while they are vulnerable.
[[Diablo|Big Guy]] served as Roxanne's Guardian while she existed as a young World Tree, protecting the area surrounding her before she possessed the power and maturity to defend herself independently.[11]
[[The Universal Tree]] likewise possesses [[Jeanne Elderblood]] as his Guardian, although the scale of that relationship is vastly greater than the planetary example.[2]
Chapter 640 describes the typical early existence of a World Tree as one in which the young Tree may spend vast periods alone on its planet with only its Guardian while waiting for the world to develop sufficiently for Life.[1]
Guardians therefore serve as external protectors during stages when the World Tree's enormous potential has not yet translated into mature practical power.

### Contracts and Bonds
World Trees can establish supernatural connections with other Beings.

#### World Tree Contracts
A conventional World Tree contract requires the acceptance of the World Tree and, in at least Roxanne's case, the approval of her Guardian.[11]
Such a relationship should not be confused automatically with ownership.
A World Tree remains an independent sentient existence whose cooperation must normally be obtained rather than a supernatural artifact that can simply be claimed.

#### Deep Soul Connection
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s bond with Roxanne developed far beyond an ordinary contract.
After Victor fed the young World Tree his Blood and later consumed the fruit she produced, the two established an increasingly deep spiritual relationship.[12][13]
Roxanne eventually came to reside directly within Victor's Soul.
Because a World Tree normally supports a planet, this altered her natural function:
Normal World Tree → supports a planet.
Roxanne inside Victor → supports Victor's existence.[6]
Her growth consequently improves Victor's physical and spiritual foundation.
The two can also Synchronize, allowing memories, information and abilities to pass between them.[14]

#### World Tree as Part of a Soul
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s condition demonstrates that the fundamental "world" supported by a World Tree does not absolutely need to be an ordinary external planet if an abnormal Soul is capable of containing the Tree.
Roxanne's chosen "planet" effectively became Victor himself.
Later, [[Amara Elderblood]] was similarly removed from her original planetary role and integrated into Victor's internal structure, establishing a Positive/Negative World Tree pair inside his Soul.
The growth and Balance of Roxanne and Amara became increasingly intertwined with Victor's own physical, energetic and spiritual development.[10][15][16]

### Reproduction
Normal World Trees cannot reproduce biologically.
Although every naturally created World Tree is female, Chapter 1116 reveals that their inability to become pregnant is ultimately the result of a prohibition placed upon their existence by [[The Universal Tree]].[5]
Their enormous planetary-scale Souls would already make ordinary reproduction extraordinarily difficult, but [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] internally clarifies that Soul size is not the true fundamental restriction:
their Creator prohibited natural reproduction.[5]
This means the ordinary World Tree population is produced through the Universal Tree's cosmic system rather than biological childbirth.

#### Victor's Exception
[[Victor Elderblood]] broke this Rule.
He successfully impregnated both [[Roxanne Elderblood]] and [[Amara Elderblood]], producing:
- [[Rosalyn Elderblood]];
- [[Amaya Elderblood]].
These daughters are unprecedented because they are World Tree descendants not directly created by the Universal Tree.[5]
Victor concludes that this became possible because his abnormal existence is capable of distorting Rules established within Creation, allowing him to bypass a restriction that should normally prevent World Tree reproduction.[5]
The event should therefore not be generalized into a normal reproductive ability of World Trees.

### The Universal Tree
Main article: [[The Universal Tree]]
The Universal Tree is the creator and higher source of the planetary World Tree system.
He is one of the [[Regulators of Existence]] and possesses a cosmological function vastly greater than that of his daughters.
The relationship can broadly be understood as:

The Universal Tree creates the seeds that become planetary World Trees, provides them with the knowledge required to perform their functions and maintains the larger Life-related system to which they belong.[1][5]
He should therefore not simply be listed as another ordinary World Tree alongside [[Yggdrasil]], Roxanne or Aurora.
He is their Primordial progenitor and creator.

### World Trees and the Elderblood Empire
World Trees eventually became a major part of the infrastructure of the [[Elderblood Empire]].
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s relationship with them differs dramatically from the normal relationship between a civilization and a planetary World Tree.
Rather than treating them as resources to be exploited, Victor repeatedly forms personal connections with them and integrates compatible World Trees directly into his wider supernatural network.

#### Roxanne and Amara
[[Roxanne Elderblood]] became part of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul long before his rise to Godhood, making her development directly affect Victor.[6]
[[Amara Elderblood]] was later incorporated as her complementary Positive World Tree and rapidly evolved into an Elder World Tree through their connection.[10]
Together they created a more complete Positive/Negative World Tree structure within Victor.

#### Sector-Wide World Tree Network
By Chapter 1136, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had become powerful enough to issue a Divine Order to the World Trees throughout his entire Sector.
Millions of white threads converged upon him, after which Roxanne observed thousands of World Tree avatars inside Victor's Soul and confirmed that he had called the World Trees from across the Sector.[2]
The exact number of World Trees is therefore not definitively established by the chapter. The millions of incoming threads should not automatically be interpreted as millions of individual Trees.
Victor subsequently placed teleportation nodes upon the planets associated with those World Trees and incorporated their locations into his Sector-wide Imperial planning.[2]
This transformed his Soul into a central hub linking World Trees and planetary infrastructure from throughout his territory.

#### Victor as a Substitute World Tree
Chapter 1136 also reveals one of the most important late-story extensions of the World Tree system.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] wished to terraform barren planets that did not possess World Trees. Because native Universal Rules prevent ordinary planetary Life from existing without World Tree support, he concluded that those planets would have to connect directly to him and receive Energy from his existence.[2]
In doing so:
Victor himself would perform the function of their World Tree.
He estimated that his available Energy was sufficient to nourish hundreds of additional planets.[2]
This does not make Victor biologically a World Tree.
Rather, his Life Authority, Energy capacity and Reality-altering existence allow him to reproduce the cosmic function normally assigned to one.

### Skills and Abilities
The abilities below describe powers demonstrated or explicitly identified as belonging generally to World Trees. Powers unique to individual Trees, racial hybrids or [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]-linked evolutions should be listed on their respective character pages rather than assumed to apply universally.
- World Tree Physiology: World Trees are planetary-scale supernatural existences created specifically to sustain living worlds. Their Souls and Energies are fundamentally adapted to planetary Life support.[6][5]
- Higher Existence: Even young World Trees possess an unusually high existential quality compared with conventional Mortal species. Their natural function operates on a planetary rather than individual scale.[6][14]
- [[Immortality]] / Agelessness: World Trees can exist for planetary timescales and are described as being as ancient as the worlds assigned to them.[5] This does not make them impossible to kill.
- Enhanced Soul: A World Tree possesses an enormous Soul appropriate for an existence responsible for sustaining an entire planet. Chapter 1116 describes their Souls as essentially planetary in scale.[5]
- Nature Manipulation: World Trees possess natural control over the non-sentient Life and natural systems within their influence. Roxanne explicitly states that this is an ability shared by every World Tree.[14]
- Plant Manipulation: World Trees possess particularly strong affinity with plants, trees and other "green" Life and can grow and manage vegetation directly.[14]
- Earth Manipulation: Their Nature Authority extends beyond vegetation to natural materials such as rocks, earth and similar non-sentient components of the environment.[14]
- Life Manipulation: Positive World Trees nourish the emergence and continuation of planetary Life over enormous periods.[4]
- Planetary Life Support: The defining World Tree ability. A World Tree supplies the metaphysical support required for a planet to sustain Life under the Rules of native Creation.[6][2]
- Terraforming: Given sufficient time, a World Tree can transform an otherwise hostile world into an environment capable of supporting Life.[8][2]
- Environmental Manipulation: Through their connection with the world, mature World Trees can influence Nature, water, earth and the broader planetary environment.[7]
- Planetary Awareness: A mature World Tree can observe the world to which she is connected through the natural environment itself.[7]
- Limited Omnipresence: Aurora describes the planetary World Tree state as a type of semi-omnipresence inside its own sphere of influence, allowing the Tree's presence and awareness to extend throughout its connected world.[7] This ability is limited to the World Tree's appropriate domain rather than being universal omnipresence.
- Immense Energy Reserves: World Trees possess extraordinarily large Energy reserves capable of sustaining entire planets. They are compared to inexhaustible Energy batteries, with the total quantity of Energy they possess described as immeasurable. However, a World Tree has a finite limit to how much of that Energy it can release at any one moment.[17]
- Energy Generation: World Trees possess extraordinary quantities of Energy generated and recycled through their planetary functions.
- Energy Absorption: World Trees absorb and filter Energies associated with the planetary system, particularly Positive or Negative Energy according to their alignment.[3]
- Energy Recycling: Rather than merely consuming planetary Energy, World Trees filter it and return usable portions to the natural cycle.[3]
- Energy Bestowal: World Trees can supply Energy to Beings or systems directly connected to them. Roxanne and Amara continuously provide Negative and Positive Energy to Victor through their Soul connection.[10]
- Energy Reserves: World Trees possess effectively immense or inexhaustible long-term Energy reserves through their planetary cycle, but their instantaneous output is still limited. Aurora explicitly distinguishes the amount of Energy a World Tree possesses from how much it can release at one time.[4]
- Positive Energy Manipulation (Positive World Trees): Positive World Trees naturally produce, absorb, filter and redistribute Positive Energy as part of their planetary function.[3]
- Negative Energy Manipulation (Negative World Trees): Negative World Trees naturally produce, absorb, filter and redistribute Negative Energy, including corruption and other Negative phenomena generated by living Beings.[3]
- Decay and Death Regulation (Negative World Trees): Negative World Trees support natural processes such as death, decay and decomposition by maintaining the Negative side of the planetary Life cycle.[3]
- Dimensional Creation / Dimensional Support: Excess Positive or Negative Energy can be redirected into separate supernatural dimensions associated with the planet, producing structures such as Heavens and Hells.[3]
- Dimensional Anchoring: World Tree roots and Energies participate in maintaining the supernatural dimensional infrastructure attached to a developed world and its Pantheons.
- Spiritual Energy Manipulation: Their planetary function extends beyond ordinary biological ecosystems into the spiritual systems produced by sentient Life.[3]
- [[Natural Energy]] Manipulation: World Trees possess exceptional compatibility with Natural Energy. Victor's connection with Roxanne allowed Natural Energy to be drawn naturally toward him during his development of [[Senjutsu]].[14]
- Senjutsu Generation: A World Tree's relationship with Nature allows it to generate and interact naturally with the Energy used in Senjutsu.[14]
- Instinctive Knowledge: World Trees possess inherited knowledge concerning their powers, cosmic function, planetary Life and the limitations imposed by Balance. Roxanne states that this information originates from the Universal Tree or becomes available automatically as a World Tree matures.[1]
- Avatar Manifestation: World Trees can manifest humanoid Dryad bodies through which they directly interact with other Beings while remaining connected to their greater existence.
- Synchronization (through sufficiently deep bonds): A World Tree can synchronize with a compatible Being to share memories, information, Energy and abilities. Roxanne and Victor demonstrate an exceptionally deep version through their Soul connection.[14] This degree of synchronization should not automatically be assumed for every ordinary World Tree contract.
- Soul Enhancement (through integration): When a World Tree exists directly within another Being's Soul, its natural supporting function can nourish and strengthen that Soul. Roxanne's growth repeatedly improved Victor's spiritual and physical foundation.[6]
- Physical Enhancement (through integration): The same World Tree support can improve the physical body of a Being whose existence is deeply integrated with the Tree. Victor received a major increase when Roxanne reached adulthood despite not personally undergoing a separate evolution at that moment.[6]

### Weaknesses and Limitations
- Core Destruction: The World Tree's Core is a vital component of its existence. Complete destruction of the Core is treated as a method capable of permanently killing a World Tree.[18]
- Limited Immediate Output: World Trees possess enormous long-term Energy reserves, but they cannot release their entire supply at one instant. Aurora compares their nature to an effectively inexhaustible battery whose output still possesses a maximum limit.[4]
- Developmental Vulnerability: Young World Trees can spend enormous periods in vulnerable stages before reaching full maturity, which is one reason Guardians exist to protect them.[11][1]
- Creator's Reproductive Restriction: Normal World Trees cannot become pregnant because their Creator placed a prohibition upon their reproductive system.[5]
- Planetary Responsibility: A normal World Tree's priorities and natural instincts are heavily influenced by the world she exists to maintain. Protecting planetary continuity can take precedence over protecting individual Mortal civilizations or Pantheons.
- Balance Restrictions: World Trees possess instinctive knowledge concerning what they should and should not influence because excessive direct interference with developing Life can disturb the Balance of the planetary system.[1]

### Role in the Wider Cosmology

Their role exists between ordinary biological ecosystems and the higher cosmological systems administered by the [[Regulators of Existence]].
The general hierarchy can be illustrated as:

By the later stages of the novel, World Trees are also shown to be cosmologically significant enough that the number, maturity and distribution of World Trees form part of the development of entire Sectors rather than merely individual planets.[2]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Sector-wide summoning demonstrates the scale of this network: World Trees existed throughout the Sector on numerous inhabited planets, and integrating them into his Soul allowed him to immediately connect their worlds to the infrastructure of the [[Elderblood Empire]].[2]
