Fairies are a race of supernatural Spirits born from the [[Natural Energy]] of [[World Trees]]. Their existence is fundamentally tied to Nature and World Tree ecosystems rather than being merely a humanoid species with an affinity for plants. [[Roxanne Elderblood]] directly describes Fairies as Spirits born from a World Tree, placing them within the broader family of spiritual lifeforms whose existence originates from the natural systems maintained by World Trees.[1]
Fairies historically existed in much greater numbers, but their unusual beauty, purity, spiritual abilities and close relationship with World Trees made them highly desirable to other supernatural beings. Over time, greed from Humans, Gods and other powerful factions reduced their population to the point that the surviving Earth Fairies were considered an endangered race.[3][1]
The surviving population was eventually protected by [[Viviane]], the Lady of the Lake, who concealed herself and the remaining Fairies within an isolated forest in England. During the Demonic War, they abandoned that sanctuary and entered [[Victor Elderblood]]'s growing faction after learning that Victor was married to an actual World Tree, Roxanne Elderblood.[3][4]
Their recovery began after Roxanne restored a suitable environment for them. New Fairies once again began the slow process of being born through World Tree Natural Energy, and the species later moved under Roxanne's protection inside Victor's Inner World.[2][5][6]

### Nature
Fairies are fundamentally World Tree Spirits.
When discussing their nature, Roxanne directly explains that Fairies are related to [[World Trees]] in a manner similar to a World Tree's Guardian: they are Spirits born from a World Tree and consequently possess powerful instinctive reactions toward beings of that class.[1]
Their relationship with a World Tree is not merely religious worship or cultural tradition. It is built into their nature.
Even [[Viviane]], despite being half-God and possessing a Divine Soul, found her Fairy instincts overwhelmingly drawn toward Roxanne. She instinctively regarded Roxanne as an existence of extremely high order and had difficulty treating her casually despite Roxanne repeatedly asking her to do so.[1]
This explains why World Trees occupy such a central position in Fairy society. They are not simply allies of the race; they represent the natural source from which Fairy existence originates.

#### Purity
Fairies possess an unusually pure spiritual nature.
[[Scathach]] describes them as a race that naturally hates impurity and is strongly connected to Nature. Their presence can affect the environment and those around them, making them particularly incompatible with corruptive entities.[1]
This purity should not be confused with moral perfection.
Fairies can be mischievous, irritating, curious, selfish or emotional like other intelligent beings. The term purity primarily describes the nature of their Energy and spiritual existence rather than guaranteeing that every Fairy possesses an innocent personality.
[[Viviane]] herself repeatedly demonstrates curiosity, irritation, prejudice and other ordinary emotions despite embodying the general purity associated with the race.[3][2]

#### Nature Affinity
Fairies possess an extremely intimate relationship with Nature.
Their natural habitat consists of healthy forests and regions saturated with [[Natural Energy]]. [[Viviane]] immediately feels most comfortable within environments created or maintained by [[World Trees]], and she describes living inside Roxanne's forest as the best home her people could have obtained.[2]
Fairies are also sensitive to the distinction between natural negativity and genuine corruption. Despite Roxanne functioning as [[Nightingale]]'s Negative World Tree, Fairies were not harmed by her Energy because World Tree Negativity is a natural part of planetary balance rather than Demonic corruption.[1]
Roxanne explains that positive and negative World Trees are fundamentally neutral components responsible for maintaining planetary balance. Fairies therefore do not automatically reject Negative Energy when that Energy is part of the natural order.[1]

### Creation and Reproduction
Fairies possess two known methods of reproduction.

#### World Tree Birth
The primary and most common method is through the [[Natural Energy]] of a World Tree.[2]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly states that Fairies are born from Natural Energy, while [[Viviane]] and [[Hestia]] confirm that this is the normal method through which new members of the race appear.[2]
A World Tree or sufficiently powerful branch releases and accumulates Natural Energy until the conditions necessary for new Fairy life are created.
The process is extremely slow.
Even after Roxanne restored the surviving Fairy population's environment, Viviane estimated that it could take several decades before the first new generation of Fairies appeared.[2]
This low reproductive speed contributed significantly to the difficulty of recovering from their near-extinction.

#### Tree of Creation
The surviving Earth Fairies possessed a structure known as a Tree of Creation.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later discovered that this "Tree of Creation" was actually an Original Branch of Earth's World Tree, [[Yggdrasil]]. The branch had gradually lost much of its Power over time before Roxanne restored it with World Tree Energy.[2]
New Fairy life could be produced through the [[Natural Energy]] accumulated within this branch.
This demonstrates that a Fairy population does not necessarily require direct residence beside the main body of a World Tree as long as a sufficiently powerful connected branch or compatible World Tree structure exists.
Nevertheless, [[Viviane]] considered living directly beside Roxanne's Main Body vastly superior to using the depleted branch.[2]

#### Natural Reproduction
Fairies can also reproduce biologically with one another like many other species.
However, [[Viviane]] explains that this is not the common method. Fairies normally emerge through World Tree [[Natural Energy]] rather than relying primarily upon conventional reproduction.[2]
This explains why restoration of a World Tree environment was considered essential for rebuilding their population even though biological reproduction remained possible.

### World Tree Dependency
Fairies depend heavily upon [[World Trees]] for their long-term development.
Before discovering Roxanne, the surviving Earth Fairies were trapped in a weakened and declining state. Natashia specifically used their inability to properly develop without a World Tree as one of the arguments convincing [[Viviane]] to abandon her isolation.[4]
The arrival of Roxanne fundamentally changed their circumstances.
Her [[Natural Energy]]:
- restored their environment;
- recharged their Tree of Creation;
- allowed the surviving population to recover;
- and created the conditions necessary for new Fairies to begin appearing again.[1][2]
This dependency should not be interpreted as meaning that a Fairy instantly dies when separated from a World Tree. Viviane and the surviving population lived for extended periods without direct access to [[Yggdrasil]]'s Main Body.
The limitation instead concerns growth, reproduction and long-term prosperity.

#### World Tree Instinct
Fairies possess an instinctive reverence toward [[World Trees]].
When [[Viviane]] first encountered Roxanne, her reactions were comparable to those of an obsessive admirer meeting the highest form of existence associated with her Race.[1]
Roxanne found this behavior somewhat uncomfortable, but Viviane explained that even her Divine half could not completely suppress her Fairy instincts.
This reverence does not appear to be an externally imposed compulsion. It originates naturally from the relationship between the species and the beings responsible for creating them.

### Appearance
Fairies possess an ethereal supernatural appearance closely associated with Nature.
Individual Fairies can appear as small points or orbs of light when moving through their environment. When Natashia, Sasha and Tatsuya entered the hidden Fairy territory in England, numerous glowing lights appeared throughout the forest while Fairy voices and laughter surrounded them.[3]
Fairies can also manifest humanoid bodies.
Their race is widely regarded as exceptionally beautiful. [[Scathach]] specifically identifies their beauty and "special" nature as one of the reasons they historically became targets for exploitation by stronger races.[1]
The claim that Fairies are objectively more beautiful than every other race in existence is not established and should not be treated as an absolute racial rule.

#### Wings
Fairies possess wings associated with their manifested forms.
[[Viviane]] is repeatedly shown using her wings for movement and Flight.[2]
The exact number, appearance and size of the wings may vary according to the Fairy, but wings are one of the principal visual characteristics associated with their Race.

### Behavior
Fairies are naturally playful and curious.
When Tatsuya and Sasha entered their hidden territory, numerous Fairies repeatedly spoke, laughed and whispered directly around or inside their perception, irritating the visitors despite displaying no actual hostility.[3]
Natashia specifically warns them that Fairies are playful by nature and that attacking one because of its irritating behavior would cause serious problems.[3]
Their playful behavior should not be exaggerated into universal stupidity or naivety.
The race was historically exploited because of its relative weakness and valuable abilities, but the surviving Fairies demonstrated enough caution to remain hidden from supernatural factions for centuries.
[[Viviane]]'s entire policy of isolation was specifically intended to prevent Humans and Gods from exploiting what remained of her people.[3][4]

### History

#### Early Fairy Population
Fairies were once substantially more common throughout Earth.
[[Scathach]] remembers a period in which encountering Fairies was comparatively normal, demonstrating how dramatically their population declined over later centuries.[1]
Their beauty, rarity, spiritual powers and relationship with Nature made them valuable targets.
Powerful individuals sought Fairies themselves or the unique services they could provide, particularly their ability to bless supernatural items and weapons.[1][2]
Over time, this exploitation pushed the race toward near-extinction.

#### Isolation in England
The surviving Fairies eventually isolated themselves within a hidden region of England under [[Viviane]].
Their sanctuary was concealed behind powerful supernatural effects. Even [[Tatsuya Rider]], who possessed significant experience with illusions, failed to perceive the true Fairy territory until he was allowed to enter.[3]
The surrounding forest initially appeared completely ordinary.
After the Fairies revealed themselves, lights emerged throughout the environment, flowers bloomed and the apparent forest was replaced by a magical Fairy domain.[3]
Viviane maintained this isolation both to protect her people and because of her own increasing exhaustion with repeatedly losing companions across her extremely long life.[4]

#### Demonic War
The Demonic invasion forced the surviving Fairies to reconsider their isolation.
Natashia Elderblood Fulger, [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] and [[Tatsuya Rider]] traveled to England to locate [[Viviane]] and warn her of the rapidly deteriorating supernatural situation.[3]
Viviane initially showed little interest in becoming involved with yet another supernatural conflict.
Her attitude changed after learning that:
- [[Diablo]]'s war was spreading across multiple territories;
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had become the Second Vampire Progenitor;
- multiple powerful factions were joining his coalition;
- and most importantly, Victor was married to [[Roxanne Elderblood]], an actual World Tree.[4]
Natashia produced a concealed leaf originating from Roxanne.
The presence of genuine World Tree Energy affected the entire Fairy Kingdom and immediately provoked an intense emotional response from Viviane, who associated it with the long-lost presence of [[Yggdrasil]].[4]
Viviane subsequently agreed to evacuate.

#### Sanctuary in Nightingale
The Fairies were initially moved into [[Nightingale]] and hidden within Clan Adrasteia's territory because the region was isolated and rarely visited by outsiders.[1]
Their presence also provided practical benefits.
[[Scathach]] specifically pointed out that Fairies could identify liars and that their pure natural presence could repel corruptive creatures, making them extremely useful for identifying spies and protecting settlements.[1]
[[Viviane]] accepted Clan Adrasteia's laws in exchange for sanctuary and access to Roxanne.
The Fairies then began living under the protection of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction.

#### Recovery Under Roxanne
Once Roxanne established a suitable forest and restored the Fairies' Tree of Creation, the population began recovering.
By Chapter 719, [[Viviane]] confirmed that the Fairies were stable and satisfied with their new home.[2]
Their greatest remaining problem was population growth.
New Fairies had not yet appeared because World Tree-born reproduction could take decades, but the restored environment meant the process had finally begun again.[2]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] then offered to accelerate the recovery by supplying additional [[Natural Energy]] through his own connection to Roxanne.
Because of his relationship with the World Tree, Victor could manipulate enormous quantities of Natural Energy and use it to replenish the Fairies' reproductive tree.[2]

#### Political Integration
The Fairies gradually became one of the recognized racial groups within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s expanding faction.
When Victor created his early Council of [[Races (Flamel)|Races]], [[Viviane]] became the official Fairy representative.[7]
Chapter 730 also makes the political hierarchy clear: Viviane represented her people directly, while the Fairy faction ultimately existed under [[Roxanne Elderblood]] due to the race's World Tree relationship.[7]
Their alliance therefore developed from simple sanctuary into formal incorporation within Victor's multi-racial government.

#### Victor's Inner World
Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s evolution into the Primogenitor of the Draconic Vampires, his Inner World expanded enormously.
Roxanne brought [[Viviane]] into the world and assigned her responsibility for helping regulate the environment by reducing excess Negativity so that greater amounts of plant life could flourish.[5]
Roxanne also informed Viviane that the remaining Fairies would eventually be moved into the Inner World.[5]
By Chapter 820, Viviane was explicitly ordered to retrieve her people and bring the Fairy population into Victor's Inner World.[6]
This gave the race an unprecedented sanctuary:
- direct protection from Victor;
- continuous access to Roxanne;
- enormous quantities of [[Natural Energy]];
- and an environment inaccessible to ordinary external factions.
The migration effectively ended the period in which the surviving Fairies were forced to hide in isolated Earth territories merely to avoid extinction.

### Powers and Abilities
Fairy Physiology: Fairies are spiritual lifeforms created from the [[Natural Energy]] of [[World Trees]]. Their abilities strongly revolve around Nature, purity, perception and World Tree Energy. Not every feat performed by [[Viviane]] should automatically be treated as a baseline racial ability because she is also a powerful [[Demigod]].

#### Standard Racial Abilities
- Spirit Physiology: Fairies are directly described as Spirits born from [[World Trees]] rather than ordinary biological creatures.[1]
- Nature Affinity: Fairies possess an exceptionally strong natural connection with Nature and prefer environments saturated with healthy [[Natural Energy]].[3][1]
- World Tree Affinity: Fairies possess an instinctive connection with World Trees. Their bodies and Spirits react extremely strongly to the presence of World Tree Energy.[4][1]
- Natural Energy Absorption: Fairies are born from and develop through Natural Energy produced by World Trees. Their reproductive cycle relies directly upon accumulating this Energy.[2]
- World Tree Empowerment: Access to a healthy World Tree environment allows the race to recover, develop and reproduce properly. Separation from such an environment severely limits long-term racial growth.[4][2]
- Purity: Fairy spiritual nature is described as exceptionally pure and naturally opposed to corruptive influences.[3][1]
- Lie Detection: Fairies possess the ability to detect dishonesty. [[Scathach]] specifically identifies knowing when someone is lying as one of the useful abilities of the race.[1]
- Corruption Repulsion: A Fairy's pure natural presence can repel corruptive creatures. Scathach states that certain "monsters" would be unable to approach a settlement protected by Fairies.[1]
- Telepathy / Mental Communication: Fairies can communicate directly through voices perceived within another person's mind. Sasha and Tatsuya repeatedly heard multiple Fairy voices and laughter without ordinary visible speakers while entering the hidden Fairy Kingdom.[3]
- Light Form: Fairies can manifest as small points or orbs of supernatural light. Numerous Fairies appeared in this form around Natashia's group when their hidden territory was revealed.[3]
- Flight: Fairies possess wings that allow aerial movement. [[Viviane]] repeatedly uses her wings for Flight.[2]
- Supernatural Beauty: Fairies possess unusually beautiful supernatural appearances. Their beauty and rarity contributed to the historical exploitation of the race.[1]

#### Illusion and Perception Abilities
- Illusion Manipulation: Fairy territories can be concealed through extremely powerful illusions. The surviving Fairy Kingdom in England remained completely hidden even from Tatsuya, an experienced illusion user.[3]
- Perception Manipulation: Fairy illusions can alter how a target perceives the surrounding environment. Upon being admitted into their domain, the ordinary forest around Natashia's group seemingly disappeared and was replaced by the true magical landscape.[3]
The strongest illusion demonstrated in Chapter 577 may have been maintained principally by [[Viviane]], who is a Fairy-[[Demigod]]. Consequently, the scale of the hidden Fairy Kingdom should not automatically be attributed to every individual Fairy.

#### Purification Abilities
- Purification: Fairies possess abilities associated with removing corruptive or negative supernatural influences.[4]
- Status Effect Removal: Fairy purification can remove supernatural influences affecting another being, including certain forms of mental manipulation.[4]
- Mind Manipulation Negation: Fairy purification can counter charm-like effects affecting the mind.[4]
[[Viviane]] demonstrated this ability at an exceptionally high level and could purify influences with Divine origins. Because Viviane is also half-God, her Divine-level purification should not automatically establish the limits of an ordinary Fairy.

#### Item Blessing
- Blessing: Fairies can spiritually bless items, one of the abilities that historically made them extremely valuable to Humans, Gods and other supernatural factions.[2]
- [[Holy Energy]] Infusion: Fairy blessings can infuse equipment with Holy Energy.[2]
- Weapon Enhancement: A Fairy blessing can enhance the supernatural properties of a weapon or item. The desire of supernatural beings to obtain Fairy-blessed equipment contributed significantly to the race being hunted and exploited.[2]
This ability is more accurately described as item blessing than generic Weapon Manipulation.

### Advanced and Individual Abilities
Certain abilities associated with the Fairy article originate primarily from [[Viviane]] and should not be generalized to every member of the race.

#### Viviane
[[Viviane]] is a Fairy-[[Demigod]] rather than a pure baseline Fairy.
Her abilities include extremely advanced:
- Illusion Manipulation;
- Purification;
- Lie Detection;
- spiritual perception;
- Truth perception;
- Soul perception;
- and near-ageless longevity.
Her extraordinarily long lifespan is specifically attributed to the combination of her Fairy and God heritage, meaning her near-immortality cannot be used as definitive evidence that every normal Fairy possesses an infinite lifespan.[4]
Her ability to perceive Souls and the Truth of the World likewise originates partly from her Divine heritage and should remain on her individual page.[8]

### Relationship with World Trees
Main article: [[World Trees]]
World Trees stand at the center of Fairy existence.
Fairies:
- are born from their [[Natural Energy]];
- depend upon their Energy for population recovery;
- instinctively revere them;
- and can use World Tree branches as reproductive centers.[1][2]
This relationship exists regardless of whether the World Tree governs Positivity or Negativity.
Roxanne specifically explains that World Trees themselves are neutral beings fulfilling necessary planetary functions. Her status as a Negative World Tree therefore does not make her harmful to Fairies.[1]
Fairies recognize the distinction between natural Negativity and corruptive Energy.

#### Yggdrasil
Earth's surviving Fairy population originated through the systems associated with [[Yggdrasil]], Earth's World Tree.
Their Tree of Creation was eventually revealed to be one of Yggdrasil's Original Branches that had gradually lost its original Energy.[2]
[[Viviane]] still possessed connections to Yggdrasil's wider system and could communicate with beings such as Ratatoskr to travel through the World Tree's branches.[2]
However, directly requesting Yggdrasil's aid was politically dangerous because [[Odin]] guarded the World Tree and would likely attempt to seize control of the surviving Fairies.[2]

#### Roxanne Elderblood
[[Roxanne Elderblood]] became the most important World Tree in the modern history of the surviving Fairy population.
Her arrival:
- restored their Tree of Creation;
- created a new forest habitat;
- provided sufficient [[Natural Energy]] for population recovery;
- and eventually gave them access to her own Main Body inside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul.[1][2][5]
Politically, [[Viviane]] remained the representative and Queen of the Fairies while Roxanne became the higher authority responsible for the World Tree/Fairy faction inside Victor's organization.[7]

### Relationship with Gods
Fairies possess a complicated relationship with Gods.
Gods are spiritually closer to High-Level Spirits than ordinary mortal races, making them naturally compatible with Fairy biology in ways many physical supernatural races are not.[3]
This is demonstrated by [[Viviane]], who was born a stable Fairy-God Hybrid without the genetic instability normally associated with incompatible supernatural reproduction.
However, the historical relationship between Fairies and Gods was far from universally peaceful.
Powerful Gods coveted:
- Fairies themselves;
- Fairy blessings;
- and access to the [[World Trees]] associated with them.
Viviane consequently spent centuries hiding her people partly to prevent Gods from enslaving or exploiting them.[3][2]
Her refusal to openly contact [[Yggdrasil]] was specifically based upon the fear that [[Odin]] would discover the surviving population and claim control over them "for their own good".[2]

### Relationship with Humans
Humans were another major historical threat to the race.
The unique abilities and supernatural beauty of Fairies made them desirable targets, while legends surrounding beings such as [[Viviane]] increased interest in locating their hidden settlements.
This exploitation contributed to their population collapse and was one of the main reasons Viviane withdrew the remaining Fairies from Human civilization.[3][1]
This does not mean Fairies are inherently enemies of all Humans.
Their modern allegiance to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction eventually placed them within a civilization containing large Human populations.

### Relationship with Spirits
Fairies should not merely be listed as "allies" of Spirits.
They are themselves a specific form of Spirit.
Roxanne explicitly describes Fairies as Spirits born from a World Tree.[1]
They therefore belong within the broader metaphysical classification of spiritual life while possessing a more specialized origin and relationship with [[World Trees]] than ordinary Spirits.

### Relationship with Victor Elderblood
The Fairies originally joined [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction indirectly through Natashia and Roxanne.
[[Viviane]] had little initial interest in Victor personally but became interested in his faction after discovering that:
- he was the Second Vampire Progenitor;
- multiple ancient powers were joining him;
- and his wife was an actual World Tree.[4]
Victor later became increasingly involved in their survival.
He offered:
- political protection;
- access to Roxanne;
- [[Natural Energy]];
- a hidden sanctuary;
- and eventually a permanent home inside his own Inner World.[2][5][6]
Viviane subsequently became the official representative of the Fairy population within Victor's early Council of [[Races (Flamel)|Races]].[7]
By Chapter 803, Viviane herself had been incorporated directly into Victor's Inner World under Roxanne's authority, and the remaining Fairies followed soon afterward.[5][6]

### Weaknesses
- World Tree Dependency: Fairy population growth and long-term development are heavily dependent upon access to a World Tree or sufficiently powerful World Tree branch. Their near-extinction worsened while separated from a healthy World Tree environment.[4][2]
- Slow Reproduction: World Tree-born Fairies reproduce extremely slowly. [[Viviane]] estimated that even under restored conditions the first new generation could require several decades to appear.[2]
- Limited Population: Centuries of exploitation reduced Earth's Fairy population to dangerously low levels. This made individual losses considerably more damaging to the race than they would be for more populous supernatural species.[3][1]
- Comparatively Limited Combat Power: Fairies possess extremely valuable supernatural abilities but historically lacked enough raw military power to prevent stronger races from exploiting them. [[Scathach]] directly attributes much of their decline to being "beautiful and special" without sufficient strength to protect themselves.[1]
- Purity / Corruption Aversion: Fairies naturally dislike corruption and impure supernatural environments because of their exceptionally pure spiritual nature.[3][1] The degree to which corruption causes direct physical damage has not been universally established.
- Physical and Spiritual Destruction: Fairies are not established as absolutely indestructible. Their near-extinction demonstrates that sufficiently powerful beings can capture or kill them.
The old page's claim that simple "naivety" is a universal biological weakness is too broad. Fairies can be playful and curious, but the surviving population demonstrated considerable caution and successfully remained hidden from major supernatural factions for centuries.
