True Dragons are an ancient and exceptionally powerful supernatural race in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]], representing the highest complete natural expression of the Dragon Race. Their distinction from ordinary Dragons is not merely a matter of greater physical strength, larger bodies or superior Energy reserves, but concerns the fundamental nature of their existence. A True Dragon possesses a genuine True Dragon Soul, a complete Draconic body and an extraordinary natural compatibility with Creation and the structure of Reality. This integration is so profound that True Dragons are described as embodiments of Nature itself, whereas ordinary Dragons remain supernatural members of the Draconic Race without possessing the same completeness of existence.[1][2]
True Dragons possess a naturally developed relationship with the forces governing existence, allowing them to interact with supernatural Energy and the underlying structure of Creation in ways unavailable to ordinary races. Their complete spiritual and biological characteristics include a [[Dragon Heart]], specialized Draconic veins, highly developed senses, [[Draconic Energy]], an authentic Dragon Form and a natural affinity for the ancient Draconic Language and Runes. These qualities form a unified racial foundation rather than a collection of unrelated enhancements. The possession of individual Dragon organs or powers does not necessarily establish complete True Dragonhood, as demonstrated when Victor Elderblood obtained nearly every major physical characteristic of a Dragon through his connection with Zaladrac Zeovnur Elderblood but continued to identify himself as incomplete because he lacked a genuine Dragon Soul.[1][3]
The extraordinary relationship between True Dragons and Creation grants their Race an inherent advantage in the manipulation of supernatural phenomena. [[Roxanne Elderblood]] and [[Amara Elderblood]] described a fundamental racial ability to interact with Creation, comparing Draconic Runes to the Codes of Creation. Later explanations clarified that True Dragons do not require Runes for every ordinary manipulation of Energy because their biology already allows them to command such forces instinctively. Runes instead become especially valuable when imposing more elaborate changes, Concepts or instructions upon Reality. This distinction separates the inherent abilities of a True Dragon from techniques that require extensive knowledge, Runic construction or additional Divine authority.[4][5]
Despite their extraordinary natural abilities, True Dragons are not inherently omnipotent, nor does their existence grant every member unrestricted mastery over Reality. The effectiveness of their powers depends upon the individual's Soul, physical and spiritual development, intelligence, talent, experience, available Energy and understanding of the phenomena being manipulated. Victor explicitly acknowledged that even among True Dragons there could be individuals who lacked exceptional aptitude for particular disciplines. Their racial superiority provides an extraordinary foundation, but the abilities developed from that foundation remain subject to individual differences.[6]
True Dragons are also extraordinarily rare. During the later Imperial Era, [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]] estimated that only approximately forty to fifty True Dragons existed throughout Victor's entire Sector, even after the creation of numerous members of the Imperial Draconic lineage. Their low population is associated with prolonged natural development, difficult reproduction, extraordinary spiritual requirements and, in Victor's case, deliberate restrictions imposed upon the expansion of his family. Although the Emperor possessed the means to create additional members, he intentionally refrained from distributing the complete True Dragon foundation indiscriminately after receiving warnings concerning the consequences of rapidly increasing the population of such powerful beings.[7]

### Overview
True Dragonhood describes a complete Draconic condition in which an individual's physical, energetic and spiritual characteristics correspond to a genuine Dragon existence. The most decisive characteristic is the True Dragon Soul, which forms the metaphysical foundation of the Race and distinguishes complete Dragons from beings possessing only partial Draconic characteristics. Although an incomplete Dragon-related being may possess scales, Dragon Eyes, a [[Dragon Heart]] or supernatural powers inherited from another Dragon, these features alone do not necessarily establish True Dragonhood.[1]
The distinction became particularly important as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] developed beyond his original Vampire existence. His connection with Zaladrac granted him a body possessing numerous Draconic characteristics long before he completed his transformation. [[Scathach]] identified his Dragon skin, senses, Heart and specialized veins, describing him as practically a Dragon except for his Soul. Victor nevertheless rejected the description because he regarded the Soul as the essential element defining the Race. A similar distinction appeared when he later created Dragonoids whose bodies possessed Draconic properties without the full spiritual and physical completeness of True Dragons.[1][8]
The Race also possesses a relationship with Nature extending beyond conventional elemental affinity. A True Dragon is naturally compatible with the surrounding structure of Reality, enabling the individual to manipulate Energy, interpret Draconic phenomena and develop abilities in accordance with the environment in which they mature. This compatibility does not mean that every Dragon possesses all supernatural abilities simultaneously. Rather, it provides a foundation capable of interacting with an unusually broad range of natural and supernatural influences without ceasing to be fundamentally Draconic.[1][9][2]

### Classification
The novel distinguishes several degrees and expressions of Draconic existence. These categories must not be treated as interchangeable because physical Draconic characteristics, complete True Dragonhood, Blood Dragon lineage and Godhood describe different aspects of an individual's nature. The Imperial lineage later introduced additional distinctions between Lesser Dragons, ordinary Dragons and True Dragons, with the latter possessing a fundamentally superior relationship with Nature and Reality.[8][2]

### Nature and Existence

#### Embodiments of Nature
True Dragons are described as embodiments of Nature itself, a distinction emphasizing their extraordinary integration with Creation. Ordinary Dragons possess supernatural characteristics inherited through the Dragon Race, but True Dragons are naturally compatible with Reality on a much more fundamental level. Their relationship with Nature allows them to interact with supernatural Energy and the environment without requiring an external system of magic for every basic action. This should be understood as a racial condition rather than a personal Divinity of Nature automatically possessed by every member.[2][5]
Velnorah described True Dragons as an anomalous race extraordinarily well adapted to Existence itself. Her assessment occurred after studying numerous advanced species and comparing their strengths and limitations with those of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family. Even races previously considered close to ideal by the standards of highly developed civilizations appeared incomplete beside True Dragons. This unusual compatibility partly explains why their existence is regarded as qualitatively superior to ordinary biological enhancement or the simple accumulation of supernatural Energy.[7]
The correspondence between a True Dragon and the surrounding world also provides unusual flexibility during supernatural development. Different individuals can acquire characteristics associated with particular environments, elements and inherited lineages while retaining their Draconic nature. However, the Race's compatibility with Nature should not be confused with unrestricted immunity to hostile environments or automatic mastery of every power available within the Universe. The exact outcome depends upon the individual, their development and the forces with which they interact.[1][9]

#### Compatibility with Reality
The relationship between True Dragons and Reality becomes especially important in the use of [[Draconic Energy]] and Runes. [[Scathach]] later explained that a True Dragon should be capable of commanding ordinary Energy through their own existence instead of relying upon complex magical structures for every simple action. The Race's natural compatibility allows Energy to respond to the Dragon's will, while Runes provide a means of imposing more specific changes upon the governing conditions of Reality.[5]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] likewise explained that the exceptional power of Dragon Gods partly originates from the same racial foundation. A powerful God and a Dragon God may possess superficially comparable supernatural status, yet the latter benefits from an existence naturally harmonized with Reality. The superiority demonstrated through this relationship is qualitative rather than merely a numerical difference in raw Energy, although individual Divinity, combat experience and understanding continue to influence the result.[12]
A True Dragon is therefore not automatically a universal administrator. Their natural compatibility does not grant the institutional authority possessed by the original [[Primordial Entities]], whose existence is integrated into fundamental cosmic functions. A Dragon may manipulate Reality through Energy, Draconic Runes, personal Divinities or sufficiently developed abilities without becoming responsible for maintaining the underlying structure of the Cosmos.[12]

#### Connection with Creation
True Dragons possess an inherent ability to interact with Creation. During the examination of [[Anna Elderblood]]'s newly developed Draconic abilities, Amara and Roxanne connected the effectiveness of her Runes with the basic nature of the Race. They explained that True Dragons naturally possess a degree of control over Creation and that Draconic Runes are closely associated with the Codes of Creation. Anna's unusually powerful results were further enhanced by her ability to use Positive and Negative Energy and by her individual talent, demonstrating how a common racial foundation can produce extraordinary outcomes when combined with exceptional personal characteristics.[4]
The ability to interact with Creation does not mean that True Dragons automatically possess every Concept, Divine Authority or fundamental Law. Advanced applications require sufficient knowledge, intent, Energy and an understanding of the changes being attempted. A naturally talented Dragon can exploit this connection more effectively, while another individual may need additional training despite possessing the same general racial foundation.[6]

### True Dragon Soul
The True Dragon Soul is the defining spiritual foundation of a complete True Dragon. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] identified the Soul as the most important component of Draconic existence because it contains the fundamental characteristics determining what the individual is. Before acquiring a genuine Dragon Soul, Victor had already inherited Dragon skin, Dragon senses, a [[Dragon Heart]], Draconic veins and multiple Dragon-related powers through his connection with Zaladrac. Nevertheless, he still considered himself a Noble Vampire possessing Dragon characteristics rather than a complete Dragon.[1]
This distinction demonstrates that Draconic physiology can exist independently of complete Draconic spiritual identity. An individual may obtain powerful biological traits through a supernatural bond, transformation or inheritance without automatically acquiring the Soul necessary for True Dragonhood. A complete Dragon's physical and energetic systems correspond to the deeper characteristics of its Soul, forming a unified racial existence rather than an assortment of unrelated supernatural organs.[1]
The creation of Dragonoids reinforced this principle. Victor explained that a Dragonoid could possess Draconic characteristics and eventually learn Dragon abilities while lacking both the genuine Soul and the authentic Dragon Form associated with complete True Dragons. This does not make such beings ordinary mortals, but it establishes that their Draconic nature differs fundamentally from the full racial condition.[8]
The True Dragon Soul is also central to reproduction. Natural conception involves the Souls of both parents participating in the formation of the child's Soul, making spiritual compatibility essential rather than merely physical compatibility between reproductive bodies. The extraordinary importance of the Soul consequently influences racial inheritance, fertility and the possibility of producing offspring with other supernatural species.[13]

### Physiology

#### Complete Draconic Body
A True Dragon possesses a body whose physical and energetic structures are naturally adapted to the Race's extraordinary supernatural power. The Draconic Heart, specialized veins, scales, senses and other organs support the existence established through the Dragon Soul. Their physiology is capable of sustaining immense quantities of Energy and expressing that power through physical strength, Draconic Fire, Dragon Breath, Runes and other individually developed abilities. The exact appearance and characteristics of these systems vary according to lineage, age, environment and personal development.[1]
The complete Draconic body is fundamentally different from the partial transformations or engineered enhancements available to inferior Draconic beings. Although Dragonoids and Lesser Dragons may possess several similar characteristics, they do not automatically possess the complete spiritual and existential foundation of a True Dragon. The exact biological distinctions between all lower forms have not been exhaustively documented, and abilities demonstrated by the highest-ranking Dragons should not be assigned indiscriminately to every subordinate Draconic classification.[8][2]

#### Dragon Heart
Main article: [[Draconic Energy]]/Dragon Heart
The [[Dragon Heart]] is one of the most important organs in a True Dragon's physiology, functioning as a supernatural Energy reactor. [[Scathach]] identified the Heart as a principal component of the Draconic characteristics [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] inherited through Zaladrac, emphasizing its importance in generating and supporting the enormous power required by a Dragon body. The Heart operates alongside specialized Draconic veins and the Dragon's wider physical structure, forming part of the integrated system through which Draconic Energy is produced, contained and distributed.[1]
The output and efficiency of Dragon Hearts vary considerably. Anna, for example, demonstrated exceptional energetic properties following her transformation, with her Heart supporting unusually powerful manifestations of Draconic Runecraft. During the testing of her abilities, expenditure of Energy was followed by rapid recovery through her Heart, illustrating how the organ contributes directly to supernatural endurance and the continued use of powerful techniques.[4]
Possessing a Dragon Heart does not independently establish complete True Dragonhood. Victor acquired a functioning Draconic Heart while still lacking a True Dragon Soul, demonstrating that the Heart is a major biological component but not the ultimate determinant of the Race.[1]

#### Draconic Energy
Main article: [[Draconic Energy]]
True Dragons naturally generate, contain and manipulate Draconic Energy through their complete racial physiology. Their bodies are adapted to enormous supernatural reserves, while their Heart and veins support the circulation and expression of that Energy through the physical body. Draconic Energy can reinforce their movements, power their natural abilities and support the use of Draconic Runes and the Speech of Power. The specific properties of the Energy vary according to the Dragon's lineage, personal affinities and other supernatural influences.[1][3]
A particularly important later clarification concerns the difference between ordinary Energy control and the advanced Runic manipulation of Reality. [[Scathach]] explained that True Dragons naturally possess the ability to command Energy without needing Runes for basic techniques. Their biology already supports direct interaction with supernatural power, and the development of this instinctive ability is an essential aspect of combat training. Draconic Runes become particularly valuable when the Dragon wishes to impose a Concept, alter a law or create a more complicated supernatural effect, rather than merely performing actions their physiology already permits.[5]
This distinction also explains why an individual with extensive magical education can remain inefficient when first learning to use complete Dragonhood. Reliance upon external magical constructions or elaborate Runes for every action may prevent the individual from developing the instinctive Energy control expected of a mature True Dragon. Scathach's training of Albedo directly addressed this problem, requiring her to return to the fundamentals rather than compensate for weak practical control through increasingly elaborate magical displays.[5]

#### True Dragon Form
A complete True Dragon possesses an authentic Dragon Form corresponding to the nature of its Soul and racial foundation. This is distinct from a humanoid individual acquiring a partial Draconic transformation or the artificial characteristics of a Dragonoid. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s explanation of the Dragonoids specifically distinguished their lack of a genuine True Dragon Soul and authentic Dragon Form from the completeness possessed by the higher Race.[8]
True Dragons may also possess humanoid manifestations, as demonstrated by Zaladrac and numerous members of the Elderblood family. Their humanoid appearances do not remove their underlying Draconic existence, nor do they necessarily represent the maximum size, strength or anatomical complexity of their complete Dragon bodies. The enormous differences between individual Dragon Forms further demonstrate that the Race does not possess a single universal coloration, structure or elemental affinity.[1][11]
The size and appearance of a Dragon Form can change considerably with growth and individual development. However, enormous physical dimensions do not automatically mean that a Dragon possesses complete spiritual maturity. The Dragon Soul continues developing over extended periods, and the apparent age of a humanoid manifestation may differ greatly from the actual age or developmental condition of the Dragon.[14]

#### Dragon Eyes and Senses
Main article: [[Draconic Energy]]/Dragon Eyes
True Dragons possess highly developed senses capable of perceiving supernatural phenomena beyond the limitations of ordinary physical sight. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] inherited Dragon senses through his connection with Zaladrac before acquiring complete True Dragonhood, demonstrating that Draconic perception is partly supported by biological and energetic structures that can be transferred through an exceptional supernatural bond.[1]
The Dragon Eyes and other Draconic sensory characteristics can assist in perceiving supernatural Energy and the effects produced through Runes. Victor's later experiments with Draconic abilities demonstrated that supernatural phenomena could be observed through appropriately developed Dragon Eyes, allowing a sufficiently skilled user to perceive changes ordinarily invisible to conventional senses.[3][4]
The degree of perception varies with individual development. A naturally powerful Dragon possesses exceptional sensory potential, but its effectiveness is affected by Soul strength, available Energy and personal understanding. Feats demonstrated by Victor or other extraordinarily developed Dragons cannot automatically be assigned in full to every member of the Race.

#### Energy Sustenance
True Dragons possess an unusual relationship with the Energy of Nature, allowing them to sustain their existence without relying upon conventional food in the same manner as ordinary biological species. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explained that members of his Draconic family could survive through the Energy available in Nature itself, making ordinary meals unnecessary for basic biological maintenance. Food nevertheless remains relevant for pleasure, tradition and social interaction, as demonstrated by the numerous meals and celebrations shared by the Elderblood family.[15]
This characteristic represents a form of supernatural sustenance rather than an absolute absence of energetic needs. True Dragon existence continues to depend upon the maintenance of its extraordinary physical and spiritual systems, while the environment can provide the Energy necessary to support those functions. The ability does not establish that every individual possesses unlimited Energy or can operate without restriction in every possible supernatural environment.

### Draconic Language
Main article: Runes
The Draconic Language is the ancient language of the Dragon Race, possessing a history described as being as old as the languages of the Gods. It is more than an ordinary method of communication because it can be used to produce supernatural effects through the combination of the user's voice, Energy and intention. Zaladrac identified this art as the Speech of Power, explaining that Dragons could employ the language to influence the world directly rather than relying exclusively upon externally constructed magical systems.[3]
The fundamental principle of this technique is the interaction between Energy, Voice and Intention. When a Dragon speaks with sufficient power and appropriate intent, the surrounding world can respond to the command. Zaladrac demonstrated this ability through spoken instructions that produced immediate supernatural consequences, including an application of decay and another that reduced the effective weight of her body. These examples demonstrate the language's versatility, but the resulting effects remain dependent upon the Energy and understanding of the individual using it.[3]
Draconic speech and Runes are closely related because both employ ancient language, intention and supernatural power to impose effects upon Reality. However, they should not be described as automatically identical in every application. Spoken commands may be suitable for immediate effects, while complex Runic constructions can establish sustained or permanent supernatural conditions through carefully arranged instructions.[3][6]
The language also possesses enormous cultural significance. Zaladrac explained that Dragons historically guarded knowledge of their language and rarely transmitted it to outsiders. For Dragon Riders, receiving the language could represent the final test of trust within the bond between Rider and Dragon. Some Dragon Riders spent their entire lives alongside their partners without being taught it, demonstrating that the knowledge was regarded as a profound expression of personal confidence rather than a routine benefit granted to every Rider.[3]

### Draconic Runes
Main article: Runes
Draconic Runes are supernatural instructions associated with the Dragon Race's ancient language and natural compatibility with Creation. Their application allows a sufficiently skilled practitioner to influence physical and supernatural phenomena through the use of appropriate language, Energy and intention. During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s examination of Anna's powers, Draconic Runes were described as being closely associated with the Codes of Creation, emphasizing their relationship with the structures underlying Reality.[4]
A True Dragon possesses a natural racial affinity for Runecraft. Victor explained that Runes came naturally to him because of his Draconic nature and that even an otherwise untalented member of his complete Blood Dragon lineage would possess the foundation necessary to create basic Draconic Runes. This does not eliminate differences in personal skill. Some of his daughters struggled with constructing complicated Runes despite possessing extraordinarily powerful Divine Concepts, demonstrating that Runic talent and conceptual affinity are not always distributed equally.[6]
The distinction between simple and advanced applications becomes especially important in the later Imperial Era. [[Scathach]] explained that the natural abilities of a True Dragon should be sufficient for ordinary Energy manipulation, while Draconic Runes are especially valuable when the user attempts to impose more complicated changes upon Reality. The purpose of advanced Runecraft is therefore not merely to reproduce basic magical effects, but to establish instructions capable of altering conditions beyond those easily controlled through ordinary instinctive Energy use.[5]
Advanced Runes may be used for seals, barriers, enchantments, reinforcement, alterations to environmental conditions and restrictions affecting Space or other supernatural phenomena. Victor demonstrated the creation of a spatial seal through spoken Draconic commands, while his later instruction of [[Dún Scáith|Dun Scaith]] emphasized that complicated Runic structures could combine multiple languages and effects. However, such results depend upon knowledge, precise construction and sufficient power; the simple possession of a True Dragon Soul does not grant an individual unrestricted mastery over every possible Runic technique.[4][6]
The natural connection between True Dragons and Runes also does not replace the importance of conventional training. Draconic Runecraft possesses an enormous range of potential applications, but an inexperienced user may still produce inefficient or unstable effects. The ability to manipulate Reality through Runes remains distinct from unrestricted ownership of the underlying laws of the Universe.

### Environmental Adaptation
A True Dragon's development can be permanently influenced by the environment in which it grows. Zaladrac provides a confirmed example because her childhood took place within Hell, where she consumed Negative Energy and Demonic Miasma as part of her natural development. Those substances entered her metabolism and altered the characteristics of her growing body, producing flames that became considerably more destructive than they otherwise would have been. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] subsequently inherited these characteristics through the supernatural connection joining his existence with hers.[1]
This process demonstrates that Draconic development depends upon more than inherited biological characteristics. The surrounding environment, available Energy, location, consumed substances and supernatural conditions can shape the abilities that develop as the Dragon matures. A True Dragon raised within an environment saturated by particular supernatural influences may consequently express characteristics different from those of another member of the Race raised under different conditions.[1]
The adaptation demonstrated through Zaladrac's childhood is a developmental process rather than unrestricted instantaneous adaptation. Her Demonic characteristics emerged naturally as her body matured in Hell, not because she possessed the ability to immediately acquire complete control over every hostile Energy encountered. Although the Race's broad compatibility with Creation contributes to such development, its results remain connected to the Dragon's personal circumstances and the duration and nature of the exposure.

### Ancestral Knowledge and Racial Inheritance
True Dragons can inherit extraordinary amounts of knowledge through their racial lineage. Zaladrac, who was born naturally through the ancient Dragon Race, possessed millions of pieces of information inherited from previous generations. This knowledge included historical information and accumulated understanding preserved through the experiences of earlier Dragons. The inheritance was especially valuable because Dragons had been hunted extensively by other supernatural beings interested in their valuable bodies and supernatural characteristics.[16]
The existence of inherited knowledge does not automatically grant immediate mastery over every subject contained within it. Zaladrac still needed considerable time to process the accumulated information, contributing to her tendency to sleep for prolonged periods. The knowledge represented an extraordinary inherited resource, but its practical application remained dependent upon assimilation, understanding and experience.[16]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later explained that naturally born Dragons and transformed Dragons could possess different forms of inherited knowledge. Zaladrac benefited from an ancient lineage through which information had been passed down over many generations, while individuals transformed into Dragons did not automatically acquire the complete archive belonging to the natural Dragon ancestry. Their transformation nevertheless produced genuine Draconic beings rather than artificial imitations.[16]
A further development became evident through Victor's direct descendants. By Chapter 1174, Victor had become an Ancient Dragon possessing an enormous accumulated legacy, allowing his daughters to inherit basic knowledge of the world alongside dormant information concerning techniques and abilities. This inheritance did not mean that every daughter could immediately use the complete collection of techniques at full effectiveness. The inherited abilities required further personal development and practice before their potential could be properly expressed.[17]
The two forms of inheritance should therefore be distinguished. Natural-born Dragons such as Zaladrac can inherit the accumulated information of an ancient biological lineage, while Victor's direct descendants inherit knowledge and latent abilities through the established legacy of their exceptionally developed father. Neither process guarantees immediate proficiency, and transformed individuals do not automatically acquire the historical memories belonging to a natural Draconic ancestry they never possessed.[16][17]

### Growth and Maturation
True Dragons possess an exceptionally prolonged natural developmental cycle compared with Humans and most ordinary supernatural races. Their physical development, spiritual maturity and the condition of their Soul do not necessarily progress at an identical rate. Zaladrac could possess an enormous Dragon body while still remaining comparatively young according to the standards of her Race because her Soul had not fully matured.[14]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later indicated that a Dragon would ordinarily require approximately 100,000 years to complete its natural development under conventional circumstances. This period establishes the extraordinary timescale associated with Draconic maturation, although it should not be treated as an identical biological requirement for every individual. Exceptional lineage, Progenitor influence, additional Divinities, environmental conditions, available Energy and other supernatural interventions may alter the process significantly.[18]
The Imperial True Dragon lineage demonstrates how extensively these natural limitations can be modified. Victor's presence and Progenitor authority supported the development of his descendants, while the extraordinary characteristics of their mothers contributed to differences in gestation, appearance and individual supernatural potential. Dragon Gods may also be born with bodies resembling older children or adults rather than conventional infants, depending upon the nature of the powers inherited during their development.[18][17]
The birth of [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]] provided an especially unusual example. Most of Victor's earlier True Dragon daughters required more than 1,000 years of gestation, while Moira was born after approximately 300 years. Victor attributed this accelerated development to the extraordinary relationship between her inherited Luck and the surrounding Reality, which appeared to favor her existence. Her abnormal developmental period therefore represents an exceptional individual case rather than a new universal standard for True Dragon reproduction.[17]
The species' prolonged natural development should also be distinguished from combat experience. A Dragon may possess enormous innate power and extensive inherited information while lacking the practical training necessary to exploit those advantages efficiently. This distinction remains important even within the Imperial Family, where powerful Dragon Goddesses can still require intensive instruction to develop proper Energy control, martial fundamentals and the use of their own Divinities.[6][5]

### Reproduction

#### Soul-Based Conception
True Dragon reproduction involves the direct participation of the Souls of the parents. Chapter 1102 explains that when two ordinary True Dragons reproduce, their Souls unite during the process that produces the child's Soul. The spiritual nature of conception is therefore fundamental to Draconic reproduction, making the strength and compatibility of the parents' Souls decisive factors rather than merely the compatibility of their physical bodies.[13]
The process resembles reproduction among Gods in its reliance upon spiritual interaction, but natural non-Divine True Dragons possess a different method of developing the child's body. The resulting Dragon Soul is placed into an egg, allowing the physical body to form around the spiritual foundation. This distinction is important because True Dragonhood is not defined exclusively by biology, and the complete Dragon Soul must correspond to the body that eventually develops.[13]

#### Egg Formation
In ordinary non-Divine True Dragon reproduction, the mother returns to her True Dragon Form and lays an egg after the child's Soul has been formed. The Soul is deposited within the egg, which subsequently develops the child's physical body. Zaladrac was born through this natural process, which differs from the reproduction of sufficiently powerful Gods whose offspring may develop directly through their spiritual existence.[13]
The period required for the egg to hatch varies according to the potential and characteristics of the parents and child. Although natural Dragon reproduction is generally extremely prolonged, the narrative does not establish a single fixed incubation period applicable to every True Dragon egg. Exceptional circumstances, parental Divinities and the nature of the offspring may affect development in ways that ordinary Draconic reproduction does not.[13]

#### Soul Compatibility
Physical compatibility alone is insufficient for natural True Dragon reproduction. Because the child's Soul is formed through the interaction of the parents' Souls, an extremely weak spiritual existence cannot necessarily participate in conception with a True Dragon. Chapter 1102 specifically identifies ordinary Humans as incompatible reproductive partners under normal circumstances because their Souls lack the required strength. Gods and other beings possessing sufficiently powerful Souls may be compatible despite belonging to different races.[13]
This clarification affects the interpretation of an earlier hypothetical discussed in Chapter 989. The earlier example considered what would happen if an ordinary Human could somehow survive reproduction with a True Dragon, suggesting that the resulting child would inherit a complete True Dragon foundation because of the extraordinary dominance of the Draconic Race. Chapter 1102 subsequently established that such conception would not ordinarily occur because the disparity between the Souls prevents the natural reproductive process from functioning in the first place.[9][13]

#### Dominant Draconic Inheritance
True Dragon characteristics possess extraordinary dominance during reproduction. Their natural compatibility with Creation allows the Draconic foundation to preserve its identity while interacting with characteristics inherited from another parent. Under ordinary circumstances, a child possessing a genuine True Dragon foundation does not become an evenly divided combination of two weaker races. Instead, the Draconic nature tends to establish the fundamental racial identity of the offspring.[9]
Exceptional results may occur when the other parent possesses a similarly extraordinary lineage or Progenitor characteristics. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s children with powerful Progenitor mothers demonstrated that certain inherited traits could survive within a complete Draconic foundation rather than being entirely suppressed. The resulting beings could possess genuinely hybrid characteristics because of the compatibility of the Dragon Race and the exceptional nature of both parents.[9]
These exceptional combinations belong primarily to the development of Victor's Blood Dragon lineage rather than to every naturally occurring True Dragon. His Blood Dragon existence possesses additional traits associated with Noble Vampire Progenitorhood and other supernatural characteristics that ordinary True Dragons do not automatically share.

#### Dragon God Reproduction
A True Dragon possessing genuine Godhood may reproduce differently from a non-Divine Dragon. Gods are spiritual beings whose reproductive processes involve their Souls, and sufficiently powerful Dragon Goddesses may develop offspring through those Divine characteristics rather than relying upon the ordinary physical egg process used by natural non-Divine True Dragons. The exact process depends upon the nature of the individuals involved and should not be generalized to every member of the Race.[13]
The extraordinary reproduction of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family demonstrates that Divine and Progenitor characteristics can substantially change the expected limitations of Draconic development. However, the unusual circumstances of the Imperial Family should not be treated as evidence that ordinary True Dragons naturally reproduce at the same rate or possess identical methods of conception.

#### Gestation, Twins and Birth Rate
Natural Dragon reproduction is exceptionally slow and difficult. Chapter 987 refers to approximately 50,000 years as a historical benchmark for the shortest naturally recorded Dragon gestation discussed in that context. This figure should be understood as a statement concerning natural Draconic reproduction rather than a universal requirement governing every artificially assisted pregnancy or the offspring of extraordinary Progenitors.[19]
Chapter 1102 also establishes that twins are possible within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family, demonstrating that the Race is not inherently restricted to producing a single child. The extraordinary difficulty associated with multiple offspring originates partly from the spiritual and energetic requirements of conception and development, which can place tremendous strain upon the mother. Even Victor's Progenitor status does not eliminate every reproductive requirement because the nature and potential of the other parent remain important.[13]
Chapter 1164 further clarifies that the Imperial Family's common pattern of producing one daughter per mother was largely deliberate rather than the result of biological impossibility. Multiple Dragon children would require significantly greater Energy and impose additional demands upon their mothers, but the family possessed the means to survive such circumstances. Their restraint also reflected Victor's decision not to rapidly multiply the number of extraordinarily powerful True Dragons while higher cosmic authorities were observing the development of his lineage.[7]

### Rarity and Population
True Dragons are among the rarest major supernatural races known within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Sector. By Chapter 1164, Velnorah estimated that approximately forty to fifty members existed throughout the region despite the tremendous expansion of the Elderblood family. This number reflects the exceptional scarcity of the Race relative to ordinary supernatural populations and demonstrates that even the Emperor's efforts had not produced a large True Dragon civilization by numerical standards.[7]
Their rarity is associated with several factors. Natural Dragons develop across extremely long periods and possess demanding reproductive requirements, while complete True Dragonhood requires a spiritual foundation that cannot be created through ordinary biological enhancement alone. Historical persecution also affected the development of the Race because Dragons possessed highly valuable supernatural organs, materials and accumulated knowledge sought by other powerful beings.[16][13]
Victor's Imperial lineage represents an additional circumstance because its population is intentionally regulated. Although Victor could theoretically create many more complete Dragons, he refrained from distributing his Blood or the full Draconic foundation outside his immediate family. Primordial authorities had warned him concerning the possible consequences of multiplying such an extraordinarily powerful Race too rapidly, and the Emperor regarded the resulting restrictions as necessary until his civilization possessed sufficient strength to manage the consequences.[7]
The comparatively small population therefore does not establish that True Dragons are biologically incapable of having numerous descendants. Rather, natural reproductive difficulty and the Imperial Family's deliberate policies both contribute to the rarity observed during the later chapters.

### Relationship with World Trees
True Dragons possess an ancient relationship with [[World Trees]], recognizing the essential role these Beings play in the development and maintenance of Life. When Zaladrac encountered Roxanne's developed World Tree manifestation, she displayed an unusual degree of respect despite her ordinarily proud disposition. The encounter reflected knowledge inherited through the Dragon Race concerning the importance of World Trees within Creation.[20]
According to the historical understanding discussed in Chapter 824, Dragons who ordinarily possessed conflicts with one another could temporarily set aside their differences when a World Tree was genuinely threatened. The preservation of a World Tree could therefore become an overriding concern capable of uniting otherwise hostile Dragons against a common threat.[20]
This behavior does not necessarily constitute worship. Instead, it reflects the Dragons' recognition of the role played by World Trees within the natural structure of Reality and their exceptional sensitivity toward the foundations of Life. The relationship is consistent with the broader description of True Dragons as beings intimately connected with Creation, although no universal alliance between every Dragon and every World Tree has been independently established.

### Culture and Knowledge
The ancient Dragon Race historically guarded its knowledge and supernatural traditions carefully. Zaladrac explained that many details concerning Dragons were intentionally concealed from other races rather than merely becoming obscure through the passage of Time. The Draconic Language was particularly protected because of its extraordinary supernatural applications and the importance of the knowledge to Draconic culture.[3]
The transmission of the language to a Dragon Rider could serve as an exceptional demonstration of trust. A Rider might remain bound to a Dragon for an entire lifetime without being taught the language, while another could receive it after establishing sufficient mutual understanding. Zaladrac's decision to teach [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] represented such confidence and also allowed him to develop the supernatural applications of the language through his own studies.[3]
The accumulation and transmission of ancestral information provided another important means of preserving racial knowledge. Naturally born Dragons could inherit vast archives of previous generations' experiences, reducing their dependence upon external civilizations while maintaining historical awareness of the threats that had endangered their Race. However, the inherited information still required individual effort to understand and apply.[16]
The later Imperial Draconic lineage introduced a different cultural environment. Victor encouraged the development of individual talents and expected his family to master their extraordinary abilities rather than rely solely upon inherited strength. The education of his daughters and wives consequently involved Draconic Runes, martial arts, Divine Concepts, scientific knowledge and direct understanding of supernatural phenomena. The ability to learn quickly did not remove the need for practical training, particularly when an individual possessed powers capable of damaging Reality through uncontrolled use.[6][5]

### Powers and Abilities
True Dragons possess the complete abilities associated with the Dragon Race, together with additional advantages arising from their genuine Dragon Soul and exceptional compatibility with Creation. The following list distinguishes confirmed racial characteristics from powers demonstrated only by particular individuals. Specific Divine Concepts, Eldritch abilities, Vampire Progenitor characteristics and advanced feats performed through exceptional personal techniques must not be treated as universal powers of every True Dragon.

#### Racial Abilities
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics: True Dragons possess extraordinary physical strength, speed, durability and stamina supported by their complete Draconic physiology. The exact magnitude varies with age, development and individual circumstances.[1][2]
- True Dragon Physiology: Their existence combines a complete Draconic body, genuine Dragon Soul, [[Dragon Heart]], specialized veins, supernatural organs and natural [[Draconic Energy]] into a unified racial foundation.[1]
- Soul-Based Existence: A genuine True Dragon Soul forms the fundamental basis of their racial identity and distinguishes them from beings possessing only partial Draconic traits.[1][8]
- Draconic Energy Manipulation: True Dragons naturally generate and manipulate Draconic Energy through their bodies, employing it to support physical power, supernatural abilities and advanced techniques.[1][5]
- Energy Generation: Their Dragon Heart functions as a supernatural Energy reactor, supporting the enormous reserves and continuous expenditure associated with Draconic abilities.[1][4]
- Enhanced Energy Control: True Dragons possess instinctive control over ordinary Energy, allowing them to manipulate supernatural power directly rather than relying upon complex Runes for every basic action.[5]
- Enhanced Senses: Complete Draconic physiology includes highly developed perception capable of detecting supernatural phenomena beyond ordinary mortal awareness.[1]
- Extrasensory Perception: Dragon Eyes and other Draconic sensory characteristics can perceive manifestations of supernatural Energy and Runic effects that conventional sight cannot observe.[3][4]
- Transformation: Complete True Dragons possess an authentic Dragon Form and can also display humanoid manifestations without abandoning their underlying Draconic existence.[8]
- Natural Weaponry: Their complete Dragon bodies possess natural offensive structures such as claws, fangs, scales and other physical characteristics appropriate to the individual's Draconic form.
- Dragon Breath: Complete Dragons can release concentrated supernatural Energy through Dragon Breath. Its element, properties and effectiveness vary according to the individual.[1]
- Flight: True Dragons can employ the wings and supernatural movement characteristics available through their Draconic bodies. Their exact methods of movement vary according to form and development.
- Energy Sustenance: True Dragons can sustain their bodies through the Energy of Nature without requiring ordinary food for survival.[15]
- Extreme Longevity: Their natural development occurs across extraordinarily long periods, with approximately 100,000 years cited as a general benchmark for complete natural Draconic maturation.[18]
- Environmental Adaptation: The Energy and supernatural conditions encountered during growth can permanently influence a Dragon's physiology and abilities, as demonstrated by Zaladrac's childhood in Hell.[1]
- Enhanced Learning Capacity: The Race possesses extraordinary potential for acquiring and developing supernatural knowledge, although actual learning and practical mastery remain dependent upon individual effort.[16][17]
- Ancestral Knowledge: Naturally born Dragons may inherit extensive knowledge accumulated through previous generations of their lineage.[16]
- Inherited Techniques: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s direct descendants can inherit basic knowledge and dormant information concerning techniques associated with his accumulated Draconic legacy.[17]
- Draconic Language: True Dragons possess the ancient language of their Race, whose supernatural applications depend upon Energy, voice and intention.[3]
- Speech of Power: Draconic commands can impose supernatural effects upon the surrounding world through the appropriate application of intention and Energy.[3]
- Runic Affinity: True Dragons possess an innate affinity toward Draconic Runes, allowing them to develop basic Runic abilities more naturally than ordinary races.[6]
- Runic Reality Manipulation: Skilled users can employ Draconic Runes to impose instructions affecting Reality, with the scope determined by knowledge, Energy and individual talent.[4][6]
- Creation Interaction: True Dragons possess a fundamental racial capacity to interact with Creation, contributing to the effectiveness of their Draconic Runes and other supernatural techniques.[4]
- Reality Compatibility: The Race exists in exceptional harmony with Reality, providing a broad natural compatibility with supernatural phenomena and the structure of Creation.[12][2]
- Racial Adaptability: Their Draconic foundation can incorporate compatible supernatural characteristics from other sources without necessarily losing its underlying racial identity.[9]
- Dominant Inheritance: True Dragon characteristics possess exceptional dominance during reproduction, ordinarily establishing the offspring's fundamental racial foundation.[9]
- Soul-Based Reproduction: True Dragon conception involves the direct participation of the parents' Souls in the formation of the child's Soul.[13]
- Egg Production: Natural non-Divine True Dragon mothers can lay eggs containing the child's developing Soul, allowing the physical body to form around it.[13]

#### Draconic Rune Applications
Draconic Runes provide a specialized means of influencing Reality through language, intention and supernatural Energy. The following applications are supported by demonstrated Draconic techniques, but their inclusion does not establish that every True Dragon possesses immediate mastery over every listed effect.
- Reality Manipulation: Draconic Runes can impose changes upon the surrounding world by constructing supernatural instructions that Reality responds to.[3][6]
- Law Manipulation: Advanced Runic constructions can establish conditions and restrictions affecting the operation of particular phenomena within a defined region, depending upon the user's understanding and Energy.[6][5]
- Spatial Manipulation: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] demonstrated Draconic commands capable of sealing a region of Space and preventing unauthorized entry or departure.[4]
- Gravity Manipulation: Zaladrac and Victor discussed the potential use of Draconic commands and Runic techniques to alter gravitational conditions and physical movement.[3]
- Weight Manipulation: Zaladrac demonstrated a spoken Draconic command that reduced the effective weight of her body, illustrating how the Speech of Power could alter physical conditions.[3]
- Decay Inducement: Zaladrac demonstrated a Draconic command causing a tree to decay rapidly, revealing the direct offensive potential of the language.[3]
- Barrier Creation: Draconic Runes can establish supernatural barriers and restrictions according to the instructions and Energy supplied by the user.[4]
- Sealing: Draconic Runecraft can restrict access to a selected region, as demonstrated when Victor sealed Space during Anna's ability testing.[4]
- Enchantment: Zaladrac identified the enhancement of possessions and treasures among her ordinary uses of the Speech of Power.[3]
- Physical Reinforcement: Draconic commands and Runes can enhance physical characteristics when properly constructed, although their effectiveness depends upon the practitioner.[3]
- Environmental Manipulation: Sufficiently advanced Draconic techniques can alter the characteristics of an affected environment rather than being restricted to directly attacking an opponent.[3][5]
- Conceptual Manipulation (Through Advanced Runic Effects): The later explanation of Draconic Runes identifies their specialized role in imposing Concepts or changes upon Reality, although this does not grant unrestricted command over every Divine Concept.[5]

#### Special Characteristics
- World Tree Awareness: Dragons possess inherited knowledge concerning the significance of [[World Trees]] and the importance of preserving them within Creation. Their historical willingness to defend threatened World Trees reflects this cultural and racial understanding.[20]
- Draconic Knowledge Inheritance: Natural Dragon lineages can preserve extraordinary quantities of information across generations, although the recipient must still assimilate the inherited knowledge.[16]
- Exceptional Developmental Potential: True Dragonhood provides an unusually advanced starting foundation, but the abilities ultimately achieved depend upon the individual's training, talent, Soul and other supernatural characteristics.[6]
- Extraordinary Spiritual Compatibility: True Dragon reproduction and racial inheritance depend upon the compatibility of powerful Souls, creating significant differences between their reproductive limitations and those of ordinary biological species.[13]

### True Dragon Gods
A True Dragon God is a True Dragon who additionally possesses genuine Divinity. True Dragonhood describes the complete Draconic condition, whereas Godhood represents another supernatural aspect of existence. The two may coexist within a single individual, producing a being whose Draconic physiology and exceptional relationship with Reality support the exercise of Divine Concepts. However, the acquisition of True Dragonhood does not automatically grant every individual a Divinity, and the personal authority possessed by a Dragon God cannot be assigned to ordinary members of the Race.[10][12]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explained that the qualitative difference between an ordinary powerful God and a True Dragon God could be enormous even when their conventional supernatural status appeared comparable. During his discussion concerning [[Tyche]], he used a simplified numerical analogy in which a God possessing a particular level of power would exist at a lower qualitative level than a True Dragon God possessing otherwise comparable power. Victor explicitly clarified that the numbers were illustrative rather than formal classifications, meaning the explanation should not be converted into an additional numerical power hierarchy.[10]
The difference arises from the True Dragon foundation itself. Their natural compatibility with Creation enhances the manner in which supernatural power can be expressed, while the individual's Divinity provides additional authority associated with a particular Concept. A Dragon Goddess of Order, Destruction, Space-Time or another Concept consequently possesses powers reflecting both her Race and personal Divine existence. Those individual Concepts remain personal and should not be indexed as universal True Dragon racial abilities.[12]
The importance of practical experience remains unchanged. Even a tremendously powerful Dragon Goddess may need training to use her abilities efficiently, particularly when her previous fighting style relied heavily upon conventional magic or externally constructed techniques. [[Scathach]]'s instruction of Albedo demonstrated that the fundamentals of Draconic existence remain essential regardless of the individual's Divine status or the quantity of supernatural Energy available.[5]

### The Imperial True Dragon Lineage
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s rise created an extraordinary development in the history of the True Dragon Race, but he did not create its ancient natural origin. Dragons such as Zaladrac existed long before his transformation, possessing their own historical lineages, traditions and ancestral knowledge. Victor instead became a complete True Dragon through his own evolutionary path and subsequently combined that foundation with his pre-existing Noble Vampire Progenitor existence, producing the separate Blood Dragon Race.[1][11]
His transformation made it possible to establish a new Imperial Draconic lineage through which members of his family could acquire complete Dragonhood. The resulting transformations did not simply overwrite the recipients with a standardized racial template. Their previous characteristics continued influencing their new bodies, affinities and supernatural powers, producing Dragons whose individual abilities reflected their earlier existences alongside Victor's inherited foundation.[11]
This lineage also demonstrated the possibility of creating complete True Dragon existences through transformation rather than natural birth. Victor recognized the transformed members of his family as genuine Dragons, although they did not automatically inherit the ancient ancestral memories possessed by naturally born Dragons such as Zaladrac. The distinction concerns the history of the lineage rather than the authenticity of the resulting Draconic condition.[16]
The expansion of the Imperial Family was intentionally limited. Victor refused to distribute his Blood indiscriminately, while his wives established demanding requirements for relatives seeking access to the broader Draconic lineage. The strongest condition of True Dragonhood remained associated with the immediate Imperial Family, whereas extended relatives might receive Lesser Dragon or ordinary Dragon states if they successfully completed the necessary trials.[7][2]
These restrictions should not be interpreted as evidence that the ancient natural True Dragons were subordinate to Victor from the beginning of their Race. The Imperial lineage represents a later branch created through Victor's extraordinary evolutionary development and regulated by his authority as the founder of the Blood Dragon Race.

### Distinction from Blood Dragons
Main article: [[Blood Dragons]]
True Dragons and Blood Dragons possess a closely related foundation but should be indexed as separate races. The True Dragon Race existed naturally before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s birth, while the Blood Dragon Race originated through his fusion of complete True Dragon characteristics with the nature of a Noble Vampire Progenitor. The resulting Race retains its complete Draconic foundation while acquiring additional characteristics associated with Victor's Vampiric existence and later supernatural evolution.[11][9][6]

### Strength and Potential
True Dragons possess an extraordinarily high racial foundation, but the ultimate strength of an individual depends upon much more than Race. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly explained that even among exceptional races there could be individuals lacking particular talents or the motivation required to achieve their full potential. A True Dragon may possess a genuine Dragon Soul, natural [[Draconic Energy]], a powerful Heart and exceptional compatibility with Reality while still being comparatively inexperienced or untalented in certain disciplines.[6]
The importance of this distinction became evident through the different talents of Victor's daughters. Some demonstrated extraordinary Runic aptitude, while others possessed stronger affinities with their Divine Concepts or greater potential in martial arts and direct combat. These differences did not undermine their common Draconic foundation; they demonstrated how a naturally superior Race could still produce individuals whose strengths developed in different directions.[6]
The later Imperial Era further emphasized the difference between raw power and practical understanding. Albedo possessed enormous supernatural Energy and advanced magical knowledge but relied too heavily upon elaborate magical constructions. [[Scathach]]'s training forced her to develop the instinctive Energy control expected of a True Dragon and to use Runes for their appropriate purpose rather than treating them as substitutes for her own biological and spiritual abilities.[5]
True Dragons therefore represent an extraordinary developmental foundation rather than a single fixed combat tier. Their natural compatibility with Creation provides exceptional potential, but their final achievements continue to depend upon their Soul, age, personal Divinities, knowledge, available Energy and accumulated experience.

### Weaknesses and Limitations
Despite their extraordinary nature, True Dragons possess limitations that prevent their racial advantages from functioning as unrestricted authority over every supernatural phenomenon.
- Individual Differences: True Dragons vary in talent, intelligence, motivation, combat experience and supernatural proficiency. Membership in the Race does not guarantee mastery of every discipline.[6]
- Energy Requirements: Draconic Runes and the Speech of Power require sufficient Energy, and inefficient instructions can demand considerably greater expenditure.[3][6]
- Runic Complexity: Natural affinity does not automatically provide the knowledge necessary to construct complicated Runes or impose advanced changes upon Reality.[6]
- Developmental Time: Natural Draconic growth occurs over extraordinarily long periods, with approximately 100,000 years cited as a general maturity benchmark.[18]
- Difficult Reproduction: Natural conception requires sufficient compatibility between the Souls of the parents, preventing ordinary Humans from reproducing with True Dragons through conventional means.[13]
- Long Gestation: Natural Dragon reproduction may require enormous periods of Time, although exceptional Progenitor and Divine circumstances can substantially alter development.[19][17]
- Environmental Dependence During Growth: A Dragon's childhood environment can permanently influence its development, as demonstrated by Zaladrac's acquisition of Demonic characteristics within Hell.[1]
- Knowledge Assimilation: Inherited ancestral information does not produce immediate mastery and may require considerable time to process.[16]
- Combat Experience: Even powerful True Dragon Goddesses can require extensive training to use their natural abilities efficiently.[5]
- Incomplete Universal Authority: True Dragons are exceptionally compatible with Creation but do not automatically possess every Divine Concept, unrestricted control over Reality or the administrative authority of the original [[Primordial Entities]].[4][12]
- Godhood Is Not Automatic: Complete True Dragonhood does not inherently grant genuine Divinity. Dragon Godhood remains an additional supernatural condition.
- Mortality: True Dragons can be killed despite their extraordinary durability and potential. Their historical persecution contributed to the preservation of Draconic knowledge through ancestral inheritance.[16]

### Known True Dragons

#### Natural-Born
- [[Zaladrac Zeovnur Elderblood]] — A naturally born True Dragon whose childhood in Hell produced distinctive Demonic characteristics. She possesses extensive knowledge inherited from ancient Dragon generations and provides one of the clearest demonstrations of complete natural Dragonhood. Her later association with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] also connects her to the separate Blood Dragon lineage.[1][16]

#### Imperial True Dragon Lineage
The Elderblood family contains the majority of the currently identified True Dragon-level beings within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Sector. However, many belong specifically to the Blood Dragon Race created through Victor's evolution. Their Draconic foundation is therefore relevant to True Dragon classification, while their complete racial characteristics must also be distinguished from those of natural True Dragons.
- Victor Elderblood — Acquired complete True Dragonhood through extraordinary supernatural evolution and subsequently established the Blood Dragon Race by combining his Draconic foundation with his Noble Vampire Progenitor existence.[11]
- [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] — A True Dragon Goddess and Empress whose supernatural abilities are enhanced by the complete Draconic foundation. Her personal Divinity of Order is distinct from generic True Dragon powers.[12]
- [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] — A True Dragon Goddess whose extraordinary martial mastery and understanding of Draconic fundamentals make her one of the most accomplished combatants within the Imperial Family.[5]
- [[Anna Elderblood]] — A True Dragon Goddess whose extraordinary Runic abilities demonstrate the interaction between the Race's natural Creation affinity and exceptional individual talent.[4]
- [[Tyche]] Elderblood — A Goddess of Luck who subsequently acquired complete True Dragon characteristics. Her later development demonstrates that an existing Divine Being can gain the qualitative advantages of True Dragonhood through transformation.[10][17]
- [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]] — A naturally born member of Victor's direct Draconic lineage whose unusually rapid development and extraordinary Luck-based powers reflect her personal inheritance rather than the ordinary abilities of the Race.[17]
The complete roster of the Imperial lineage belongs primarily on the [[Blood Dragons]] article and the individual character pages. A character's possession of a Draconic body or Dragon-related title should not automatically be treated as proof of complete True Dragonhood without corresponding information concerning their spiritual and racial condition.
