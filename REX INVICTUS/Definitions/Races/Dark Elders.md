**[[Dark Elders]]** is a figure of Rex Invictus lore. (Lore adapted from *The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** Flamel material is adapted into the Rex Invictus present (c. 2021). Rex Invictus wins any conflict; [[Robespierre]] fills [[Dr. John Dee]]'s role and hunts the Philosopher's Stone rather than [[The Codex]]. See [[Flamel Series#Integration rules]].

---

## Flamel Series Canon (SOFTINF)

> Dark Elders are [[Elders]] who wished to return the Earth to rule and became Dark Elders. They became the masters of immortal humani to gain [[The Codex]], which was bound in spells to stop beings of the Elder race from going near it. Those Elders who were content with the humani, waged a great battle but lost to the Dark Elders.

### Trivia
- The term "Dark Elders" was first coined by [[Abraham the Mage]] in a conversation with [[Chronos]] and [[Tsagaglalal]]. He used it describe those [[Elders]] who believed that working with humani would reduce their own power.[1]
