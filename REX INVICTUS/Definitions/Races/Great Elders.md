**[[Great Elders]]** is a figure of Rex Invictus lore. (Lore adapted from *The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** Flamel material is adapted into the Rex Invictus present (c. 2021). Rex Invictus wins any conflict; [[Robespierre]] fills [[Dr. John Dee]]'s role and hunts the Philosopher's Stone rather than [[The Codex]]. See [[Flamel Series#Integration rules]].

---

## Flamel Series Canon (SOFTINF)

> Great Elders are the oldest and likely the wisest of the Elder Race. Most of them, if not all of them, helped in raising [[Danu Talis]] from the seabed using elemental magic.  Some of the Great Elders in the [[Nicholas Flamel]] Series include
> - [[Benzaiten]]- The Japanese goddess of flowing rivers, waters, music, art, and serpents.
> - [[Thoth]]- The Egyptian god of knowledge and magic, along with [[Isis]]
> - [[Typhon]] - From Greek mythology, father of all monsters and feared greatly by the Olympians.
> - [[Nereus]]- From Greek mythology, father of the [[Nereids]]
> - [[Abraham the Mage]] (possibly) - From Abrahamic theology
