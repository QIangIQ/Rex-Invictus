**[[Utlaga]]** is a figure of Rex Invictus lore. (Lore adapted from *The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** Flamel material is adapted into the Rex Invictus present (c. 2021). Rex Invictus wins any conflict; [[Robespierre]] fills [[Dr. John Dee]]'s role and hunts the Philosopher's Stone rather than [[The Codex]]. See [[Flamel Series#Integration rules]].

---

## Flamel Series Canon (SOFTINF)

> Utlaga is a term used by the [[Elders]] to describe someone that they banish/or set a bounty on. It's literal translation in Latin is "an outlaw", or "wolf's head." which is essentially its meaning in the series. [[Dr. John Dee]], [[Billy the Kid]], Niccolò [[Niccolo Machiavelli|Machiavelli]] and [[Mars Ultor]] are the only known beings to be declared Utlaga, by their masters for disappointing them.
> It means Oath Breaker in the language of [[Danu Talis]].
