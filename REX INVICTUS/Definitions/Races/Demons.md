Demons are a broad class of supernatural beings associated with infernal realms, Negative Energy and the darker portions of the Soul. The most extensively explored group are the Biblical Demons inhabiting Hell, whose existence is connected to the reincarnation and Soul-recycling system maintained by [[The Judges of The Abyss]]. Although Demons were historically treated by most supernatural civilizations as creatures synonymous with evil, later revelations establish that they are sapient beings capable of individual personalities, loyalty, civilization and moral choice. Under the reign of Victor Elderblood, Demons who obey his Laws are formally treated as citizens rather than monsters, with punishment being directed toward individual actions instead of Race alone.[1][2]
The fundamental nature of a Biblical Demon is closer to an Evil Spirit than a conventional living organism. Demons are created from the Negative side of mortal Souls and are therefore already spiritually "dead" by ordinary standards. Killing their physical manifestation does not normally destroy them permanently; unless their Soul itself is destroyed, absorbed or severed from its Hell, they eventually return to the infernal realm and can be reborn.[3][4] Later explanations further clarify that an ordinary Demon is effectively an accumulation of Negative Energy lacking its corresponding Positive component, a structural imbalance that explains both their natural affinity for Negativity and several limitations associated with their Souls.[5]
The term Demon is also used for supernatural populations outside [[Biblical Hell]], most notably the Asuras associated with the [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]]. Because the novel gives substantially more information about Biblical Demons, the Soul-recycling system, Hell hierarchy and most racial abilities described on this page primarily concern that branch unless otherwise stated.

### Description
Biblical Demons are manifestations of the darker aspects discarded from mortal Souls. Early descriptions presented them as creatures rejected by God for being too corrupt and as the embodiment of the sins produced by mortal civilization.[6] Later explanations provide the more precise metaphysical interpretation: when the reincarnation system processes a condemned Soul, the positive portion can be separated from the accumulated corruption and the remaining Negative existence may form a Demon. Because of this origin, Demons naturally resonate with Negative Energy, Miasma, corrupted environments and the infernal dimensions responsible for receiving condemned Souls.[6][3][5]
This does not mean every Demon possesses the same personality or degree of malice. Hell's ancient political system rewarded aggression, strength, treachery and Energy Cannibalism, producing a civilization where cruelty became an effective survival strategy. This environment heavily influenced Demonic culture, but [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later demonstrated that the Race itself was capable of functioning under organized laws. When other supernatural leaders described Demons as inherently monstrous, Victor directly rejected that classification. His position was that Humans, Gods, Vampires and other sapient [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] were equally capable of atrocities; therefore, the correct distinction was between a citizen capable of obeying society and an individual who behaved like a monster.[1][2]
Demons vary enormously in appearance. Some possess primarily humanoid bodies while others possess horns, tails, wings, unusual skin colors, claws or other bestial traits. Their physical characteristics are also influenced by lineage and subtype, with Succubi, Imps, the Ars Goetia, the Four Horsemen and other Demonic groups exhibiting drastically different appearances and abilities.

### Creation

#### Soul Recycling
The ordinary creation of a Biblical Demon is tied to the judgment and reincarnation of deceased Souls. Mortal actions generate sin and Negative Energy; murder motivated by rage, envy, theft, destructive pride, uncontrolled greed, gluttony, extreme lust and similar actions were among the examples associated with the accumulation of corruption. A deceased Soul is ultimately subject to the judgment system overseen by [[The Judges of The Abyss]], beings historically described by supernatural society as the eyes, hands and mouth of God because their decrees can determine the destination of Souls.[6]
When a Soul is deemed excessively corrupted, it can be sent into Hell and processed. The portion regarded as suitable for reincarnation is separated while the accumulated Negative side remains behind. The resulting existence is what becomes a Demon. This is why ordinary Demons possess incomplete or heavily Negative Souls compared with living beings and why they are described as beings that are already dead.[6][3]
[[Diablo]] was the first Demon produced when the [[Heavenly Father]] implemented this particular Soul-recycling system and was consequently associated with humanity's earliest sin. This does not make Diablo the oldest entity to have existed in Hell; ancient beings such as Albu and [[Zahal]] existed before [[Lucifer]]'s reign and served Hell itself rather than the later political factions created within it.[7]

#### Demon Progenitor
Lilith is the Progenitor of Demons and possesses a racial privilege different from ordinary Soul recycling. A Demon Progenitor can create Demons directly without requiring the standard process through which a corrupted deceased Soul is judged and reconstructed.[8] Consequently, not every Demon needs to be individually born through the same method used to produce beings such as [[Diablo]]; a Progenitor can become the beginning of further Demonic bloodlines and descendants.
Lilith's existence is itself highly abnormal. She began as a Human connected to Genesis before becoming a Demon and eventually the Demon Progenitor, allowing her to retain characteristics and spiritual potential unavailable to ordinary Demons. Her exceptional Soul also helped make possible her later ascension into genuine Divinity.[9][10]

#### Fallen Angels
Fallen Angels and ordinary Demons should not be treated as completely synonymous. [[Lucifer]] was originally one of Heaven's greatest Angels before falling and becoming the first Demon King, demonstrating that Angelic beings can transition into infernal existences. This is a different origin from the mortal Soul-recycling process responsible for ordinary Biblical Demons.[6]

### Nature and Physiology
A Demon's true existence is spiritual rather than purely biological. Their bodies allow them to physically interact with the environment, but destroying the body does not necessarily destroy the underlying Demon. Their affinity with Negative Energy also causes them to function considerably better in Hell and other corrupted environments than in places dominated by incompatible energies.[3][11]
The absence of a complete Positive component within an ordinary Demon's Soul has significant consequences. A normal Demon's spiritual foundation is incomplete, making it substantially more difficult for one to progress toward the same kind of conceptual existence achieved by a God. This was eventually described as a form of self-imposed limitation: becoming a Demon can remove many weaknesses of mortal flesh, but sacrificing the Positive half of one's existence can cripple later spiritual progression.[5] This limitation is not absolutely irreversible. Exceptional Demons can reconstruct or compensate for the missing portions of their Souls, although doing so is extremely difficult.[12] Beings such as Lilith and War demonstrate that Demonic origins do not make higher evolution fundamentally impossible.

### Growth
The traditional method through which Demons increase their strength is Energy Cannibalism. Training is possible, but Hell's culture developed around a considerably faster method: killing a stronger Demon and absorbing the Energy accumulated by that individual. The repeated consumption of other Demons was one of the principal mechanisms through which Hell's ancient legendary beings and Demonic Pillars reached their positions.[13]
Before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s conquest, rank among the Ars Goetia depended on a combination of wealth, influence, personal power and lineage. Wealth and political influence could carry a Demon relatively high within Hell's aristocracy, but reaching the upper positions required genuine strength and an exceptional bloodline. The divide became particularly important among the highest twenty Keys of Solomon.[14]
This system was eventually abolished by Victor. After taking the throne, he rejected the idea that the old titles should automatically determine authority and reorganized his followers around usefulness, capability and loyalty. Demons who proved themselves could receive greater authority and rewards regardless of the rank they had possessed under [[Diablo]], while betrayal or incompetence could result in punishment regardless of inherited status.[15][16][17]

### History

#### Ancient Hell
Long before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s birth, Hell developed into the largest infernal power associated with the Biblical system. Albu and [[Zahal]], the ancient Keepers of Hell, existed before the fall of [[Lucifer]] and were responsible for protecting the realm itself and preventing an unsuitable King from destroying it.[18]
Lucifer, originally one of Heaven's greatest Angels, eventually became the first Demon King and the guardian responsible for maintaining order within Hell and preventing its inhabitants from freely entering the Human World. He became associated with the Sin of Pride and unified the realm beneath his authority. Lucifer eventually abandoned the position and divided the [[Key of Hell]], leaving portions of his former authority with Lilith and [[Diablo]].[6][19]
This division created the two dominant factions that controlled [[Biblical Hell]] for generations. Lilith, the Mother and Progenitor of Demons, commanded her own followers and carried an overwhelming hatred toward Heaven and the God who had rejected her. Diablo controlled much of the traditional Demonic aristocracy, including large portions of the Ars Goetia, Seven Deadly Sins and Four Horsemen. Diablo eventually defeated and enslaved Lilith through supernatural means, effectively collapsing the old balance between the two rulers and leaving his faction dominant.

#### Diablo's Era
Under [[Diablo]], Hell was organized around strength, hierarchy and exploitation. The 72 Keys of Solomon acted as powerful Demon nobles commanding personal territories and Legions, while the Seven Deadly Sins and Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse stood among the greatest military forces available to the throne. Rival Demons continually fought, consumed one another and competed for greater positions because remaining weak in Hell made an individual vulnerable to predation by stronger beings.[13]
Diablo's long-term objective extended far beyond ruling Hell. He sought influence over the Human World, manipulated multiple supernatural factions and ultimately prepared the large-scale Demonic War. Demonic forces infiltrated Earth, attacked [[Nightingale]] and invaded important supernatural territories while Diablo used political agreements, hostages, corrupted organizations and alliances to prevent the supernatural factions from forming a unified response.[20][21][22][23]
The invasion eventually became one of the largest supernatural wars in Earth's modern history. Nightingale, the Vatican, Youkai territories, supernatural organizations and multiple Pantheons were dragged into the conflict. High-ranking Demons demonstrated why the ancient Pillars were so feared, while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction increasingly dismantled Diablo's strategy by freeing hostages, killing Demon commanders and turning several of Diablo's supposed allies against him.[24][25][26][27]

#### Victor's Conquest of Hell
[[Diablo]]'s greatest strategic mistake was arranging for [[Victor Elderblood]] to be forcibly transported into Hell. Rather than becoming trapped and exhausted by an infinite supply of hostile Demons, Victor regarded the realm as a source of combat experience, Souls, blood, knowledge and Negative Energy. He slaughtered Demonic armies and defeated increasingly powerful rulers while consuming useful information from his enemies.[28][29][13]
One of Victor's earliest major victories was against [[Vine]], the Forty-Fifth Key of Solomon and ruler of thirty-eight Demonic Legions. After destroying most of her army, Victor spared Vine when she submitted and realized that conquering useful Demons would be more productive than simply exterminating everything in the realm.[30][31] His policy rapidly became simple: Demons who surrendered and proved useful could live and serve him; those who continued resisting would be destroyed and converted into resources.[32][33][34]
Victor continued descending through Hell, defeating armies, Demon rulers and eventually the Four Horsemen of The Apocalypse. With most of Diablo's strongest forces committed to the war on Earth, the old Demon King was unable to return without sacrificing the invasion infrastructure he had spent years constructing. Entire cities and Legions consequently surrendered to Victor as his army expanded behind him.[35][36][37][38]
The conquest culminated when Victor entered the Demon King's castle. Hell's ancient guardians recognized him as a legitimate sovereign and the throne itself accepted him, changing to accommodate its new ruler. Diablo sensed from Earth that the authority of his kingdom had been taken from him.[39][40] Victor immediately began reorganizing Hell instead of treating the throne as a ceremonial prize. He elevated capable followers, dismantled portions of Diablo's obsolete hierarchy and established a system based around strength, competence, reward and loyalty.[15][16]
Demons understood those principles considerably more easily than abstract Human morality. Victor rewarded successful subordinates and subjected traitors to terrifying punishment, publicly demonstrating both possibilities until the enormous Legions surrounding him voluntarily acknowledged his rule. By the end of the conquest, Hell proclaimed Victor Alucard as the new King of Demons.[17][41]

#### Fall of Diablo
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] returned to the Demonic War commanding the very Legions [[Diablo]] had expected to remain loyal to him. The battlefield was consequently divided between Demons serving the former King and Demons serving Victor, while Heaven, [[Nightingale]] and Victor's other allies continued attacking Diablo's increasingly isolated forces.[42][43][44]
Diablo's authority then collapsed. Yama abandoned him, multiple Demon nobles switched allegiance, Lily and Victor's infiltrators betrayed Diablo's remaining command structure and several of his greatest servants were killed. Victor also broke the supernatural control Diablo had imposed upon Lilith, restoring the Mother of Demons' free will and turning one of Diablo's strongest weapons into another enemy.[45][46][47]
Desperate to overcome the coalition surrounding him, Diablo attempted to transcend normal Demonic limitations by combining Demonic and Angelic characteristics into an artificial Nephalem state. The transformation failed to reverse the battle. Victor ultimately defeated and consumed Diablo, ending the old King's reign and leaving [[Biblical Hell]] completely under Victor's authority.[48][49][50][51]

#### Modern Demon Civilization
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] did not intend to preserve Hell as the violent feudal structure he conquered. Over the following centuries experienced from Hell's distorted temporal perspective, he and his subordinates reorganized the realm, developed its cities, trained its armies and transformed its political and economic institutions. By the time Victor returned from his extended period in Hell, he had accumulated roughly seven centuries of experience governing the Demonic realm.[52]
The philosophical change became explicit during the later [[Supernatural Gathering]]. When representatives of other factions dismissed Demons as monsters, Victor declared that the Race would be judged according to the same principle as everyone else. A Demon capable of respecting his Laws was a citizen; a Human, God, Vampire or Demon who behaved like a monster would be treated as one. Victor also began implementing methods through which ordinary Demons could be identified and held responsible for their actions outside Hell, while keeping the identities of military and intelligence operatives secret.[1][2]
Hell subsequently became one of the principal civilizations of the [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]]. Its inhabitants were no longer isolated subjects of an independent Demonic kingdom but part of Victor's multi-racial Pantheon alongside Vampires, Dragons, Gods, [[Werewolves]], Witches, Humans and other supernatural peoples.[53]
The scale of the transformation became particularly apparent when Victor invited the [[Heavenly Father]] and Ariel into Hell. Cities such as Alcantara had become enormous economic centers while Abaddon functioned as an organized capital possessing its own currency, security infrastructure, commerce, technological development and entertainment. Shadow Demons were used to maintain public order, demonstrating how far the realm had developed from the lawless civilization formerly associated with [[Diablo]].[54][55]
Thousands of years of hatred between Angels and Demons did not disappear merely because Victor desired cooperation. Both civilizations retained legitimate grievances and cultural hostility toward the other. Victor therefore favored gradual cooperation based upon shared interests rather than pretending that peace could be imposed immediately through royal decree. His objective was to make Heaven and Hell capable of coexistence in preparation for enemies far beyond the old conflicts of Earth.[56][57]

### Society

#### Old Demonic Society
Traditional Hell operated according to an extreme interpretation of survival through strength. Demons accumulated resources, armies and personal power while consuming weaker or defeated rivals to accelerate their own growth. Demon cities were separated by enormous distances and ruled by powerful nobles whose personal military strength determined how securely they could hold territory. Even titles such as "King" were ultimately meaningless if the individual lacked the power necessary to enforce them.[13][58][59]
The Ars Goetia system reflected this culture. Wealth and political influence mattered, particularly among lower-ranked Pillars, but the highest ranks could not be maintained without powerful lineages and overwhelming individual strength.[14] Personal armies were equally important; powerful Keys of Solomon commanded thousands or millions of subordinate Demons and could effectively operate as independent rulers within their territories.

#### Victor's Order
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] abolished the assumption that the old Ars Goetia rank automatically entitled a Demon to authority. His system rewards capability, loyalty and results. Ambition is not prohibited; instead, ambitious Demons are encouraged to become useful enough that the King benefits from rewarding them. Betrayal is discouraged through overwhelming punishment while competence produces wealth, authority and opportunities for advancement.[15][16][17]
This system gradually transformed Demonic society from a collection of competing feudal territories into a centralized civilization. Victor's Generals, administrators and specialists oversee military training, logistics, technological development and internal government, while the ancient Keepers continue protecting the existence and history of Hell itself.

### Abilities

#### Standard Demonic Abilities
The exact abilities of a Demon vary according to age, lineage, rank and subtype. The following are abilities commonly associated with Biblical Demons.
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics: Demons possess physical strength, speed, reflexes, stamina and durability greatly exceeding ordinary Humans. The difference between low-level Demons and members of the ancient Demonic aristocracy is enormous; high-ranking Demons can treat attacks capable of annihilating ordinary supernatural beings as comparatively insignificant.
- Agelessness: Demons do not die from old age, making the passage of time largely irrelevant to them. High-ranking Demons can therefore survive for extraordinarily long periods; [[Sitri]], for example, had accumulated riches over eons.[60][61]
- Evil Spirit Physiology: A Demon's fundamental existence is similar to an Evil Spirit created from the Negative portion of a Soul. They are therefore already considered spiritually dead and do not function exactly like ordinary biological life.[3]
- Resurrection: Destroying a Demon's physical body normally causes its Soul to return to the Hell from which it originated. Depending upon the individual's strength, natural reconstruction may require thousands of years. A sufficiently powerful King of Hell can accelerate the process through equivalent exchange, traditionally using Souls as payment.[4]
- Regeneration: Demons can regenerate damage to their true bodies to varying degrees. Their capacity depends heavily upon rank, Energy and individual abilities.
- Possession: Demons can inhabit suitable mortal vessels, historically allowing them to manifest in the Human World without transporting their true infernal bodies. More powerful Demons generally require correspondingly better vessels.[6]
- Soul Consumption: Demons can consume Souls as food or supernatural resources. Souls were also historically valuable for possession, resurrection and infernal rituals.[6]
- Negative Energy Absorption: Demons naturally interact with Negative Energy and can increase their power by consuming compatible Energy. Traditional Demonic growth frequently developed into Energy Cannibalism, in which the accumulated power of defeated Demons was absorbed by the victor.[13]
- Miasma: Demons can produce and manipulate Miasma, a corruptive form of Demonic Energy capable of contaminating the environment and harming living beings. More advanced Miasma can inflict severe pain, weaken victims, interfere with regeneration and, depending upon the user, possess considerably more destructive properties.[62]
- Demonic Scriptures: Demons possess an ancient language in which words themselves carry supernatural power. Demonic Scripture can be used for contracts, Laws and other supernatural effects. As King of Hell, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] can combine this language with his infernal authority and effectively bypass portions of the conventional Rune-creation process.[63]
- Natural Weaponry: Many Demons possess horns, claws, fangs, tails, wings or other supernatural anatomical weapons. These traits are not universal and vary by subtype.
- Flight: Winged Demons such as Succubi are naturally capable of flight.
- Enhanced Senses: Demons generally possess senses superior to Humans, while particular subtypes can perceive additional phenomena associated with their nature.

#### Succubi
Succubi are a specialized Demonic bloodline associated strongly with desire, dreams and the extraction of Essence. Their powers vary according to age and individual strength.
- Dream Manipulation: Succubi can enter or manipulate dreams. Powerful individuals can reshape dream environments according to the desires and fears of their targets and dramatically alter the target's subjective experience of time.[64]
- Essence Extraction: Succubi can extract another being's Essence and use it as nourishment or as a supernatural resource.[64]
- Illusion Manipulation: High-level Succubi can manipulate perception to create extremely convincing illusions. Morgana demonstrated illusions capable of deceiving high-ranking Demons of the Ars Goetia.[65]
- Charm and Lust Inducement: Succubi naturally possess supernatural influence related to attraction and desire, allowing sufficiently powerful members of the Race to manipulate the emotions and sexual impulses of weaker targets.
- Lust Empowerment: Succubi can use lust and sexual Energy as a source of nourishment and recovery.
- Life and Essence Absorption: Sexual contact can be used by Succubi to drain vitality and Essence from another being.
- Bodily Fluid Manipulation: Succubi can control and manipulate their own bodily fluids for supernatural purposes.[66]

### Weaknesses
- Soul Destruction: Destroying a Demon's Soul results in true death. Once the Soul itself has been erased, the normal resurrection process becomes impossible.[4]
- Soul Absorption: Completely absorbing the Soul likewise prevents it from returning to Hell. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] repeatedly used this principle during the Demonic War to permanently eliminate enemies who would otherwise eventually resurrect.[4][67]
- Severing the Connection to Hell: A Demon whose spiritual connection with its native Hell is completely severed cannot use the ordinary resurrection mechanism.[4]
- [[Holy Energy]]: Holy powers are naturally dangerous to ordinary Demons because of the opposition between their spiritual natures. Powerful Angels and holy weapons are consequently among the most effective conventional methods for permanently or severely harming Demonic beings.
- Faith Energy: The Faith-based techniques used by the Inquisition are particularly effective against infernal and corrupted supernatural beings.
- Divine Power: Sufficiently powerful Divine abilities can damage and destroy Demons, although the effectiveness depends heavily upon the relative strength and nature of the individuals involved.
- Incomplete Soul: Ordinary Demons are heavily biased toward Negativity and lack a balanced Positive spiritual component. This can limit spiritual development and make conventional ascension toward Godhood extremely difficult without reconstructing the missing portions of the Soul or undergoing an abnormal evolution.[68][5]
- Unsuitable Environments: Demons operate most naturally within locations rich in corruption, Miasma and Negative Energy. Manifesting far from such environments can restrict the amount of power an ordinary Demon can comfortably access.[69]
- Vessel Destruction: A Demon manifesting through a possessed body can be expelled from the Human World if that vessel is destroyed. This does not normally constitute permanent death for the underlying Demon.
- Exorcism: Specialized holy and spiritual techniques can force a possessing Demon from its host, functioning similarly to destroying or rejecting the vessel.

### Restrictions

#### Historical Restrictions on Earth
Before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s reign, Biblical Demons were prevented from freely entering Earth by the systems separating Hell from the Human World. [[Lucifer]] originally served as the Guard of Hell and was responsible for preventing the infernal population from simply leaving the realm. After Lucifer broke the [[Key of Hell]] and abandoned his position, instability created gaps through which lower-level Demons could occasionally reach the Human World.[6]
Powerful Demons could not normally pass through these breaches in their full state. Their available methods included deliberately suppressing their power until they resembled low-level Demons or using a recently deceased Human as a vessel. Because deliberately weakening themselves made them vulnerable to rivals, possession became the preferred method among many high-ranking Demons.[6]
These restrictions should primarily be understood in the context of the old [[Biblical Hell]]. [[Diablo]]'s later invasion relied upon prepared portals, sacrifices, agreements and outside assistance capable of circumventing protections that ordinary Demons could not bypass on their own.[70][71]

### Trivia
- [[Diablo]] was the first Demon produced by the Biblical Soul-recycling system, but he was not the oldest being inhabiting Hell. Albu and [[Zahal]] existed before the fall and reign of [[Lucifer]] Morningstar.[73]
- Lilith is simultaneously the Progenitor of Demons and a Goddess, demonstrating that a sufficiently exceptional Demon can overcome the normal spiritual limitations of the Race.[74][75]
- [[Dantalion]] persecuted the Incubus population and was responsible for driving the Race to extinction.
- Demons are technically closer to Evil Spirits than ordinary biological species because their bodies are manifestations of Souls that have already passed through death.[3]
- [[Morgana Elderblood]] ceased to be an ordinary Demon when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Progenitor Blood fused her Demonic and Vampiric characteristics into a stable Demonic Vampire subspecies.[76][3]
- Victor is an exceptional case among Demon Kings because he conquered and ruled Hell without originally being a Demon. Hell itself nevertheless acknowledged him as its legitimate King.[77][41]
- Victor's reign represents the first demonstrated attempt to make ordinary Demons internationally recognized as citizens rather than automatically classified as monsters.[1][2]
- The old claim that Demons universally possess no capacity for anything resembling "goodness" became increasingly obsolete as the story developed. Their Souls are naturally dominated by Negativity, but their behavior and loyalties are not predetermined solely by Race.[1][5]
