Angels are a supernatural Celestial race created by the Heavenly Father and one of the principal populations of the Biblical Pantheon. They form the military and administrative force of Biblical Heaven, serving as the Heavenly Father's children, soldiers, messengers and representatives throughout Creation. Although Human religions commonly portray Angels as embodiments of absolute good, the novel later clarifies that this is an oversimplification. Angels and Demons occupy opposite sides of the Biblical supernatural structure and naturally wield opposing forms of Energy, but neither race is automatically morally good or evil. Angels are capable of violence, warfare and atrocities when acting under orders just as Demons are capable of loyalty, affection and civilization.[1]
The Angels are primarily beings associated with [[Holy Energy]], Light and the positive side of the supernatural balance. Their highest members considerably blur the distinction between an Angel and a God: Original Seraphim and the Seven Heavenly Virtues possess authority over Concepts despite not being ordinary Gods, while sufficiently powerful high-ranking Angels can fight beings occupying the upper levels of the Divine hierarchy.[2][3]

### Description
Angels are the principal Celestial race of the Seventh Heaven and were directly created by the [[Heavenly Father]]. They possess humanoid bodies, supernatural beauty, an inherently Holy presence and, among higher ranks, multiple large feathered wings. Seraphim such as Michael and [[Gabriel]] are shown possessing three pairs of wings, while their bodies naturally emanate such intense Light and holiness that even their facial features can become obscured behind their Celestial presence.[4]
Angels traditionally possess an extremely ordered society centered around the Heavenly Father's authority. Their military organization is highly hierarchical, and Angels are accustomed to following instructions from beings above them in the Celestial hierarchy. This does not make every Angel identical in personality. Later chapters increasingly demonstrate considerable individuality among them, particularly after Heaven's long isolation from large-scale warfare began ending. Ariel, for example, displays pride, frustration, envy, affection, fear and eventually romantic desire despite having initially embodied the image of a cold, perfectly controlled Angel.[3][5][6]
The traditional conflict between Angels and Demons originates primarily from their fundamentally opposing supernatural natures. Angels wield [[Holy Energy]] and powers aligned with the positive Celestial side of the Biblical structure, while Demons generate and inhabit regions saturated with Miasma and Corruption. Angelic attacks are consequently exceptionally dangerous to Demons, while sufficiently concentrated Demonic Miasma is equally dangerous to Angels.[7][8]
This opposition does not mean the two species are metaphysically required to remain enemies forever. By the later Imperial era, Heaven had entered a comparatively neutral geopolitical position and the Heavenly Father acknowledged that the traditional Demonic enemy was no longer necessarily a threat under [[Victor Elderblood]]'s rule of Hell.[3]

### Creation and Souls
The nature of Angelic Souls changed significantly throughout the history of the race.

#### The First Angels
The earliest Angels created by the [[Heavenly Father]] were fundamentally different from most of the modern Angel population. The first generation of Archangels was created with complete Souls. Known examples include [[Lucifer]] Morningstar, Michael and [[Gabriel]], with other extremely ancient Angels such as [[Azrael]] belonging to the same general Original Seraphim class.[9]
Possessing a complete Soul was extraordinarily important. A complete Soul allowed these early creations to possess the same fundamental spiritual completeness necessary for eventually developing genuine Divinity and becoming a God. Lucifer's later evolution into an Elder God was therefore possible in part because his existence had never possessed the fundamental spiritual incompleteness characteristic of later Angels.[9]
The disaster surrounding Lucifer and the rebellion of the earliest Angels caused the Heavenly Father to change his approach to creating Celestial beings. Rather than continuing to create independent life with complete Souls, the Angels produced after the first generation were created only with the good portion of a Soul.[9]

#### Incomplete Souls
Most modern Angels therefore possess incomplete Souls. Chapter 911 directly explains that after the "debacle" involving the Original Angels, the [[Heavenly Father]] stopped creating complete Souls and constructed subsequent Angels using only their positive or "good" portion.[9]
This characteristic is closely related to the wider metaphysics of [[Biblical Heaven]]. The Seventh Heaven receives the purified good portions of Souls that enter the Biblical afterlife. The Heavenly Father deliberately minimizes inequality, excessive material possession and other circumstances that could stimulate envy, greed and similar characteristics in these purified Souls.[10]
The incompleteness of an ordinary Angel's Soul produces a significant limitation: they do not naturally possess the same straightforward path toward becoming true Gods that beings with complete Souls possess. This is why the discovery that Demons and Angels could potentially overcome this limitation was so surprising to both Ariel and the Heavenly Father.[9]
It should therefore not be stated that every modern Angel is literally a deceased Human whose good half was transformed into an Angel. Chapter 904 distinguishes between the purified Soul portions residing within Heaven and the Seraphim themselves, while Chapter 911 describes later Angels as beings created using only the good portion of a Soul.[10][9]

### Nature and Morality
Angels are strongly associated with concepts such as order, purity, holiness and the positive side of the supernatural balance. Historically, this caused Humans and even many supernatural civilizations to equate Angels with "good" and Demons with "evil". The actual cosmology is considerably less simplistic.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] directly reflects that Angel and Demon are not synonymous with Good and Evil. Angels can commit mass violence when commanded by the [[Heavenly Father]] because they function as Heaven's army, while Demons are capable of forming families, governing civilizations and displaying genuine loyalty or affection. The distinction is therefore largely factional and energetic rather than an automatic moral judgment.[1]
The Heavenly Father himself similarly rejects overly simplistic racial morality. By the later story, Heaven becomes increasingly pragmatic and comparatively neutral toward supernatural factions that it would once have treated automatically as enemies. Ariel describes Heaven's modern international position as neutral, specifically noting that the Demon race was no longer an immediate problem after Victor became Demon King.[3]
This should also be distinguished from the Angelic concept of purity. Purity in the Biblical supernatural system concerns the composition of their Soul and Energy rather than guaranteeing moral perfection. Angels can experience anger, jealousy, pride, resentment and desire, and sufficiently destabilized Angels can even Fall.[11][5][6]

### Hierarchy
Angelic society possesses a strict military and supernatural hierarchy. Although every individual rank has not been fully explained, several of its highest levels have been directly established.

#### Heavenly Father
Main article: [[Heavenly Father]]
The Heavenly Father stands above the entire Angelic race as their creator, father and sovereign. He is not himself an Angel but an Original Primordial God who created the race through his authority over Creation.[9]
All traditional Angelic military authority ultimately descends from him. Even the strongest Seraphim functioned as commanders operating under his [[Will Solace|Will]] rather than independent rulers.

#### Original Seraphim
The Original Seraphim are among the earliest and highest-ranking Angels created by the [[Heavenly Father]]. They are distinguished from ordinary Seraphim not merely by age or raw strength but by possessing direct associations with Concepts and, among the first generation, complete Souls.[4][2][9]
The first three known major Angels were Michael, [[Gabriel]] and [[Lucifer]] Morningstar. Michael became the supreme military commander of Heaven and was explicitly introduced as the strongest Seraphim and Supreme Chief of the Eternal Father's army.[4] Gabriel stood alongside him as an extremely high-ranking Seraph associated with Justice and serving as the one who announced the Heavenly Father's words.[4] Lucifer became the first of the Angels to rebel and Fall.
[[Azrael]], the Angel of Death, also belongs to the Original Seraphim and represents one of Heaven's greatest combatants. Following Michael and Gabriel's deaths during the Demonic War, Azrael slaughtered nearly every remaining Pillar-ranked Demon on the battlefield before the arrival of additional opposition forced Heaven to reorganize.[7]
Known Original Seraphim include:
- Michael † — Former Supreme Chief
- Gabriel † — Fallen
- Lucifer Morningstar † — Fallen; former Angel
- Azrael

#### Seven Heavenly Virtues
The Seven Heavenly Virtues are seven extraordinarily powerful Seraphim who embody and wield authorities associated with the Seven Virtues. They exist above ordinary Seraphim in Heaven's hierarchy but normally beneath the Original Seraphim.[2]
Unlike ordinary Angels, the Virtues possess direct access to Concepts. Chapter 558 explains that their conceptual nature brings them closer to Gods than ordinary Seraphim and gives them superior standing within the Celestial hierarchy. Chapter 1041 further describes Angels such as the Seven Virtues as beings approaching Divine status: they do not possess fully developed Divinities in the same manner as ordinary Gods, but their authority is developed enough that they can wield their Concepts like Gods.[2][3]
Each Heavenly Virtue is entitled to two Seraphim acting as direct subordinates and bodyguards. Ariel, while serving as the Virtue of Generosity, commanded the Seraphim [[Zaniel]] and Daniel.[2]
Known Virtues include:
- Generosity: Ariel (Formerly)
- Chastity: Chastity
- Diligence: [[Cassiel]]
- Patience: [[Uriel]]
- Kindness: Unknown
- Humility: Unknown
- Temperance: Unknown
The exact identities of every Virtue have not yet been revealed in the novel and should not be filled using mythological assumptions.

#### Seraphim
Seraphim are the highest regularly encountered order of Heaven's Angelic forces beneath the exceptional Original Seraphim and Seven Heavenly Virtues. They possess six wings and substantially greater Holy power than ordinary Angels.[4][2]
Seraphim can command large numbers of ordinary Angels and are commonly entrusted with important military, defensive and diplomatic responsibilities. [[Zaniel]] and Daniel are examples of standard Seraphim serving directly beneath Ariel.[2]
Known Seraphim include:
- Zaniel
- Daniel

#### Ordinary Angels
The majority of Heaven's military forces consist of Angels beneath Seraphim rank. Although far weaker than Heaven's highest Celestial beings, they remain supernatural combatants possessing [[Holy Energy]], wings and the fundamental physical and spiritual characteristics of their race.
Large numbers of ordinary Angels serve as Heaven's standing army and can be deployed under the command of Seraphim, Virtues or the [[Heavenly Father]]'s Supreme Commander.

#### Fallen Angels
Fallen Angels are former Angels who have severed or lost their original position within Heaven. Falling is not simply a change of political affiliation; it can fundamentally alter the Angel's supernatural alignment and eventually transform their nature toward that of a Demon.
The most famous example is [[Lucifer]] Morningstar, whose rebellion against the [[Heavenly Father]] resulted in his Fall and the Fall of numerous Angels who followed him. Those forces eventually became deeply associated with the Demonic side of the Biblical supernatural system.
[[Gabriel]] demonstrates that an Angel can also be forcibly pushed toward a Fallen state through sufficient Demonic corruption. During the Demonic War, Gabriel was struck by a special substance supplied through [[Diablo]]'s conspiracy. His Angelic presence became contaminated by intense Miasma, his voice and nature became increasingly Demonic, and the other Angels immediately recognized that he was undergoing a Fall.[12]
Fallen Angels should therefore not automatically be treated as identical to natural Demons. "Fallen Angel" describes the transitional or altered state of a former Angel, while individuals such as Lucifer later proceeded further and became genuine Demons or even changed species again.

### Powers and Abilities
Angel Physiology: Angels are powerful Celestial beings created by the [[Heavenly Father]] and naturally possess bodies and Souls adapted to [[Holy Energy]]. The exact strength of these traits varies enormously according to rank; an ordinary Angel and an Original Seraphim should not be treated as physically equivalent.

#### Common Racial Abilities
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Angels possess physical capabilities vastly beyond ordinary Humans, including enhanced strength, speed, reactions, agility and durability. High-ranking Angels are capable of participating directly in battles involving Demon Pillars, [[Vampire Counts]] and Gods, while Heaven's strongest members can contend with high-ranking Divine beings.[2][7][3]
- Superhuman Strength: Angelic bodies possess supernatural physical strength. Higher-ranked Angels can physically contend with elite Demons and other major supernatural combatants rather than relying exclusively upon ranged Holy attacks.[2][7]
- Superhuman Speed: Angels can move and attack at speeds vastly exceeding Human perception. High-level Angelic battles routinely occur on the same battlefield as Vampire Counts, Demon Pillars and Divine combatants.[7]
- Enhanced Reflexes: Angels possess supernatural reaction speed compatible with their enhanced movement, allowing elite Angels to respond to attacks from high-ranking Demons during large-scale combat.[2][7]
- Enhanced Agility: Their supernatural physiology and wings allow Angels to rapidly maneuver through the air and reposition during combat.[2]
- Enhanced Stamina: Angels possess supernatural stamina significantly beyond ordinary mortals. High-ranking Angels can remain active throughout extended supernatural wars and continue fighting after sustaining significant injuries.[11][7]
- Superhuman Durability: Angels possess bodies substantially more resistant to injury than those of ordinary Humans. Their durability nevertheless varies greatly according to rank and can be bypassed by materials and Energies specifically hostile to their Celestial nature.[13][11]
- Regeneration: Angels possess supernatural healing capable of recovering from injuries beyond normal Human limits. This regeneration is not absolute; sufficiently severe damage, Demonic corruption or specialized anti-Angel materials can overcome it.[11][12]
- [[Immortality]] (Agelessness): Angels do not appear to suffer normal Human aging and can remain alive for extremely long periods. The oldest Angels have existed since before modern Human civilization. This does not make them impossible to kill, as multiple ancient Seraphim have died through supernatural combat.[4][12]
- Enhanced Senses: Angels possess supernatural perception and are particularly sensitive to Energies associated with their natural opposite. High concentrations of Miasma are immediately recognizable and repulsive to Celestial beings such as Ariel.[9]
- Flight: Winged Angels can naturally fly through use of their Celestial wings. Seraphim possess three pairs of wings and use them freely during combat.[4][2]
- Natural Weaponry (Wings): Angelic wings are physical manifestations of their Celestial nature and can withstand the stresses produced by supernatural combat. Higher-ranking Angels possess larger and more numerous wings corresponding to their rank.

#### Holy Energy
- [[Holy Energy]] Manipulation: Angels naturally generate and manipulate Holy Energy, the Celestial Energy corresponding to their position on the positive side of the Biblical supernatural structure. Holy Energy is exceptionally dangerous to beings strongly aligned with Demonic Miasma and can inflict disproportionately severe injuries upon Demons.[7]
- Energy Projection: Angels can release Holy Energy externally as destructive beams, waves and other attacks. Ariel destroyed large numbers of Demons by releasing her Holy power across the battlefield.[7]
- Light Manipulation: Angelic Holy Energy commonly manifests as supernatural Light. Michael's presence was described as completely permeated by holiness and Light, while high-level Angels can actively shape Light into offensive techniques.[4]
- Weapon Creation: Powerful Angels can form weapons directly from their Holy/Light Energy. Ariel creates and throws a [[Spears|Spear]] of Light capable of killing Pillar-ranked Demons.[7]
- Healing: Angels are highly proficient in supernatural healing. Ariel explicitly identifies Angels as specialists in healing while examining [[Fenrir]]'s condition, although even Angelic healing has limits when confronted with damage produced by sufficiently powerful Conceptual forces.[14]
- Purification: Holy Energy naturally opposes and suppresses Demonic Miasma and Corruption. This makes Angelic attacks particularly effective against Demons and other beings whose supernatural existence is heavily aligned with the negative side of the Biblical structure.[7]
- Miasma Resistance: Angels possess some resistance to ordinary exposure to Demonic Energy by virtue of their opposing Holy nature, allowing them to fight Demons directly. This resistance has limits; sufficiently concentrated Miasma can poison, corrupt or force them from a battlefield.[12][8]

#### Seraphim Abilities
Seraphim possess all common Angelic abilities at substantially higher levels and display additional supernatural characteristics associated with their rank.
- Enhanced [[Holy Energy]] Manipulation: Seraphim generate Holy Energy vastly more powerful than ordinary Angels. Their mere presence radiates intense holiness, and their attacks can threaten high-ranking Demons.[4][2]
- Multiple Wings: Seraphim possess three pairs of wings, for a total of six, distinguishing them physically from lower Angelic ranks.[4]
- Aura Generation: High-ranking Seraphim passively release powerful Holy presences capable of making their status immediately obvious to supernatural observers.[4]
The former page listed Holy Fire Manipulation as an inherent Seraphim-exclusive ability. The chapters presently checked establish Seraphim's enormous Holy and Light manipulation but do not clearly establish that every Seraph automatically possesses a unique racial "Holy Fire" ability. It should therefore not be treated as a universal Seraphim power without a direct chapter demonstration.

#### Conceptual Angels
The Original Seraphim and Seven Heavenly Virtues possess powers that extend beyond ordinary Angel physiology.
- Conceptual Manipulation: Certain high-ranking Angels possess authority over Concepts granted to them by the [[Heavenly Father]]. The Seven Heavenly Virtues embody the Virtues they represent, while Original Seraphim likewise possess individual Conceptual authorities.[2][3]
- Concept Embodiment: A Heavenly Virtue is supernaturally associated with the Virtue represented by their position. Ariel, for example, represented Generosity.[2]
- Conceptual Empowerment: Possessing a Concept places the Virtues above ordinary Seraphim and brings their existence closer to that of true Gods.[2][3]
- Limited Divinity: Chapter 1041 explains that their Concepts are not fully developed enough to make the Virtues true Gods in the normal sense. They nevertheless possess sufficient authority to wield those Concepts in a manner resembling genuine Gods.[3]
- Divine Power (Original Seraphim): The oldest Angelic creations possess spiritual foundations considerably closer to Gods than ordinary Angels. Because the earliest Archangels were created with complete Souls, they naturally possessed the potential to progress into true Divinity in a manner unavailable to most later Angels.[9]

#### Individual Conceptual Examples
These abilities belong to specific Angels and should not be generalized to the entire race.
- Death Manipulation: Azrael, the Angel of Death, possesses powers and authority associated with Death.
- Justice Manipulation: [[Gabriel]] represented Justice while functioning as one of the Original Seraphim.
- Generosity Manipulation: Ariel, as the former Heavenly Virtue of Generosity, could wield the Concept associated with Generosity.[2]
- Diligence Manipulation: [[Cassiel]] represents the Heavenly Virtue of Diligence.[15]
- Chastity Manipulation: Chastity represents the Heavenly Virtue of Chastity.[15]

### Environmental Dependency
Powerful Angels cannot necessarily use their complete strength everywhere in the same manner they can within Heaven. Like high-ranking Demons, high-ranking Angels possess a degree of environmental dependency connected to the Energy supporting their existence.
On Earth, a high-level Angel or Archangel gradually weakens if they remain outside a sufficiently Holy environment. [[Alexios Alioth]] explains that the problem is fundamentally the same as the difficulty experienced by high-ranking Demons: Demons require regions saturated with Corruption and Miasma, while Angels require regions containing sufficient [[Holy Energy]].[16]
The Angelic faction solved this problem through Holy Territories. Churches can function as territories saturated with the necessary Energy, while Saints blessed by the [[Heavenly Father]] can declare temporary territories in which Angels are able to exert their complete strength without suffering environmental weakening.[16]
This does not necessarily mean every Angel personally possesses Territory Creation. The chapter specifically identifies Churches and Saints as the methods normally used to establish the necessary environment.

### Falling
An Angel can lose their original Celestial alignment and become a Fallen Angel. Falling is strongly associated with separation from the [[Heavenly Father]]'s order, severe spiritual corruption and the development of characteristics incompatible with the Angel's original Holy state.
[[Lucifer]] is the most historically significant example. Once one of the Heavenly Father's earliest and greatest creations, Lucifer rebelled against Heaven and became the First Fallen, eventually progressing from Fallen Angel into a genuine Demon before later abandoning that existence as well.
[[Gabriel]] provides the clearest modern example of an externally induced Fall. During the Demonic War, a conspiracy involving the Elder Gods and [[Diablo]] contaminated him with an exceptionally powerful Demonic substance. Dark Miasma began corrupting his Holy body, his Angelic voice became increasingly Demonic and the surrounding Angels immediately understood that one of Heaven's purest members was being transformed.[12]
Falling therefore represents more than social exile. It can alter the supernatural composition of the Angel itself.

### Weaknesses
- Miasma / Demonic Energy: Miasma is fundamentally hostile to an Angel's Holy nature. Moderate exposure can injure or weaken them, while sufficiently concentrated Miasma can contaminate their bodies, corrupt their Celestial Energy or make an entire region unsuitable for Angelic forces.[12][8]
- Environmental Dependency: High-level Angels cannot freely exert their full strength in every environment. On Earth they gradually weaken without sufficient [[Holy Energy]] and therefore rely upon Churches, Saints or other Holy Territories when descending in their true bodies.[16]
- Hell's Metal: Hell's Metal possesses properties specifically dangerous to Angelic beings. A projectile constructed using Hell's Metal penetrated and severely injured Ariel despite her status as one of the Seven Heavenly Virtues.[13][11]
- Enchanted Hell's Metal: The projectile that injured Ariel was considerably more dangerous than ordinary metal; it was combined with supernatural enhancements, including runic modifications and Demonic power. The feat should therefore not be interpreted as every ordinary fragment of Hell's Metal automatically killing every Seraphim.[13][11]
- Demonic Corruption: An Angel's Holy nature can be forcibly contaminated by sufficiently powerful Demonic substances. [[Gabriel]]'s Fall demonstrates that extreme corruption can alter even an Original Seraphim.[12]
- Incomplete Soul (Most Modern Angels): Angels created after the first generation possess only the "good" portion of a Soul rather than a naturally complete Soul. This restricts their normal ability to independently progress toward true Godhood.[9]
- Falling: Separation from Heaven's Celestial nature or sufficiently severe corruption can cause an Angel to Fall, resulting in fundamental changes to their powers, Energy and species alignment.
- [[Spears|Spear]] of Longinus: [[The Spear of Longinus]] was created from the event that killed Jesus and is described by [[Lucifer]] as a weapon capable of killing creations of the [[Heavenly Father]]. [[Diablo]] consequently regarded it as an exceptionally effective weapon against Lucifer despite Lucifer's numerous transformations since leaving Heaven.[17]
- Vital Organ Destruction: Even extremely powerful Angels can be killed through sufficient damage to vital structures. Michael and Gabriel's deaths during the Demonic War demonstrate that Seraphim do not possess unconditional bodily immortality, and their hearts were subsequently used by Diablo as components in his transformation.[12][18]

### Relationship with Demons
Angels and Demons historically function as supernatural opposites. [[Holy Energy]] is particularly lethal to Demons, while Demonic Miasma is correspondingly dangerous to Angels. During the Demonic War, this energetic advantage allowed Heaven's forces to cause severe damage even to powerful Demons whenever they successfully landed attacks.[7]
Their rivalry nevertheless extends beyond simple Energy interaction. [[Lucifer]]'s rebellion, the ancient wars between Heaven and Hell, Fallen Angels and the repeated interference of Demons in Human civilization created grievances lasting thousands of years. Even after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] reorganized Hell and established peaceful relations with the [[Heavenly Father]], neither ruler expected the populations beneath them to instantly abandon their hatred.[19]
By the later era, however, the conflict was no longer absolute. Heaven considered itself broadly neutral on the international stage, while the Demons under Victor's authority were no longer treated as an automatic enemy.[3]

### Relationship with Humanity
Angels have maintained an extensive relationship with Humanity throughout recorded history, generally through the [[Biblical Pantheon]], the Church, Saints and the Inquisition. High-ranking Angels rarely descend freely onto Earth in their complete bodies because of their environmental restrictions, making Human intermediaries particularly useful.[16]
Churches function as stable Holy Territories where Angelic beings can descend without rapidly losing strength, while Saints possess the ability to temporarily declare similar Holy territory. This historically allowed Heaven to operate on Earth without permanently transforming the surrounding environment.[16]
The Inquisition likewise developed as a Human military institution using Faith, blessed weapons and other resources associated with the Biblical faction. Despite this relationship, Angels are not automatically obligated to support every action performed by Human religious institutions, and the [[Heavenly Father]] has repeatedly demonstrated willingness to allow Humans to face the consequences of their own choices.

### History

#### Creation of the Angels
The Angel race originated through the [[Heavenly Father]]'s use of Creation. His earliest Angels were complete beings possessing full Souls and considerably greater evolutionary freedom than their later descendants. These Original Angels became some of the most powerful beings of the [[Biblical Pantheon]] and formed the basis of Heaven's earliest hierarchy.[9]
The most important among them included Michael, [[Gabriel]] and [[Lucifer]]. Michael became Heaven's greatest military commander, Gabriel acted as the Heavenly Father's herald and Lucifer became one of the brightest and most exceptional of Heaven's children.

#### Lucifer's Rebellion
The greatest catastrophe in early Angelic history was the rebellion of [[Lucifer]] Morningstar. Lucifer rejected the [[Heavenly Father]]'s order and turned against Heaven, convincing enormous numbers of his Angelic siblings to follow him. The conflict produced the first Fallen Angels and permanently transformed the relationship between Heaven and the supernatural forces that eventually populated Hell.
The rebellion also influenced how the Heavenly Father created future Angels. After seeing the consequences created by complete independent Souls, he stopped freely creating new Angels with the same spiritual completeness. Later generations were instead created using only the positive or "good" portion of the Soul.[9]

#### Demonic War
Angels later became heavily involved in the conflict against [[Diablo]] and the Demonic armies invading Earth. Heaven deployed large Angelic armies under Michael, the Seven Heavenly Virtues and numerous Seraphim.
The war initially favored Heaven whenever their Holy attributes could be properly used against Demons, but Diablo's forces deliberately targeted Heaven's command structure. Ariel was severely wounded by an enhanced Hell's Metal projectile during the earlier fighting, demonstrating that even a Heavenly Virtue could be badly injured by appropriately prepared anti-Angel weaponry.[13][11]
The conflict escalated further when [[Gabriel]] was forcibly corrupted and began Falling. Michael's emotional reaction destabilized Heaven's command, and both brothers were subsequently killed. Their deaths severely damaged Angel morale until Ariel assumed command of the remaining army.[12][7]
[[Azrael]] subsequently entered the battlefield and massacred the majority of the remaining Pillar Demons. Heaven's advantage was later disrupted when enormous amounts of Miasma contaminated the battlefield, creating conditions increasingly hostile to Angelic forces and eventually contributing to their withdrawal.[7][8]

#### Ariel's Command
After Michael's death, Heaven's military leadership eventually passed to Ariel. By Chapter 873 she is explicitly identified as the current Commander of the Angels and holder of Michael's former military position.[20]
Ariel served alongside [[Azrael]] during the coalition assembled against the Elder Gods and remained one of Heaven's principal representatives throughout the following era.

#### Alliance with Victor Elderblood
Relations between Heaven and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction progressively changed after the Demonic War. Victor's conquest and reorganization of [[Biblical Hell]] removed the previous Demonic leadership that had repeatedly threatened Heaven, causing the [[Heavenly Father]] to adopt a much more neutral attitude toward Hell under its new King.
By Chapter 1041, Ariel described Heaven as possessing no significant active problems with the other major factions and explicitly stated that Demons were no longer the same threat because of the new Demon King.[3]
Relations became considerably closer when the Heavenly Father developed a genuine mutual respect with Victor. Victor visited the Seventh Heaven, while the Heavenly Father later toured Hell and observed the civilization Victor had constructed there. Both rulers acknowledged that the old hatred between Angels and Demons could not disappear immediately but agreed that endless repetition of ancient wars was strategically meaningless in the face of larger future threats.[10][19]
The relationship eventually became familial when Ariel entered Victor's household. She subsequently ceased being purely an Angel after her transformation into a Blood Dragon, although her Angelic heritage, Concepts and relationship with Heaven remained fundamental parts of her existence.

### Myths and Misconceptions
- Angels are inherently morally good: False. Angels are Celestial beings aligned with [[Holy Energy]] and the positive side of the Biblical supernatural structure, but Chapter 1045 explicitly rejects the idea that Angel automatically means Good or Demon automatically means Evil.[1]
- Demons are currently the automatic enemies of all Angels: No longer entirely true. They were historical mortal enemies, but Heaven later adopted a neutral position toward Hell after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became its ruler.[3]
- Every Angel possesses a complete Soul: False. The first generation of Archangels possessed complete Souls, but the [[Heavenly Father]] deliberately stopped creating later Angels in the same way. Most later Angels possess only the positive portion of a Soul.[9]
- Every Angel can naturally become a God: False. The incomplete Soul of most modern Angels severely limits their normal path toward true Godhood. Original Angels with complete Souls and Concept-bearing exceptional Angels represent special cases.[9]
- Every Seraphim possesses a Concept: False. Chapter 558 specifically distinguishes ordinary Seraphim from the Seven Virtues and Original Seraphim because the latter groups possess Concepts.[2]
- Churches merely serve religious purposes: False. Within the supernatural system, Churches can function as Holy Territories that allow powerful Angels to descend onto Earth without suffering the environmental weakening normally caused by insufficient Holy Energy.[16]

### Trivia
- Michael was explicitly introduced as the strongest Seraphim and Supreme Chief of the [[Heavenly Father]]'s army.[4]
- The Original Seraphim and Seven Heavenly Virtues possess Concepts despite technically belonging to the Angel race rather than being normal Gods.[2]
- Each member of the Seven Heavenly Virtues is entitled to two Seraphim as subordinates.[2]
- [[Zaniel]] and Daniel served as Ariel's personal Seraphim while she was the Virtue of Generosity.[2]
- The Heavenly Father's decision to create later Angels with incomplete Souls was a direct reaction to the problems created by the original generation of Angels.[9]
- Chapter 911 treats the possibility of ordinary Angels overcoming their incomplete-Soul limitation and progressing toward Divinity as a surprising development even to the Heavenly Father and Ariel.[9]
- The Heavenly Father's Heaven deliberately minimizes material inequality because the Souls inhabiting Paradise are composed of their purified positive portions, and he wishes to prevent characteristics such as envy and greed from developing unnecessarily.[10]
- Angelic Holy attacks possess a natural advantage against Demons, but the reverse is also true: Demonic Miasma is one of the most dangerous Energies an Angel can encounter.[7][8]
- By the later story, Heaven's ancient conflict with Hell had become sufficiently reduced that Ariel described Heaven as broadly neutral on the world stage.[3]
