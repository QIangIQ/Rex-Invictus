Humans are one of the principal mortal races of the Universe and the dominant mortal species historically associated with Earth, later renamed [[Yggdrasil]]. Compared with most supernatural races, ordinary Humans possess extremely weak natural bodies, short lifespans and almost no innate supernatural combat capability. Their weakness, however, conceals the two characteristics that the novel repeatedly identifies as the defining inheritance of Humanity: extraordinary Potential and Adaptability. These traits originated with Adam, the first Human and original Human Progenitor, and survived in greatly diminished form throughout his descendants.[2]
Humanity is consequently unusual because the weakest ordinary Human and the highest possible expression of the Human archetype differ enormously. Adam himself was later revealed to have belonged to the superior state known as a High Human, a "perfect" Human considerably closer to the supernatural world than modern Humanity. Ordinary Humans represent a much weaker form, while High Humans, Witches, environmentally empowered Imperial Humans and the later Primordial Humans demonstrate different ways in which the Human racial foundation can develop.[3][4]
Humanity should also be distinguished from the various supernatural professions practiced by Humans. [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]], Onmyo Mages, Saints and Blessed Humans ordinarily remain members of the Human race despite gaining access to Faith, blessings, [[Holy Energy]] or supernatural techniques. Their powers represent external or learned systems rather than independent species unless the individual undergoes an actual racial transformation.

### Origin and Creation
Humanity's exact creation history is more complicated than the traditions of any single Pantheon suggest. Within the Biblical account, the [[Heavenly Father]] directly created Adam, described as the first Human and Humanity's Progenitor. Chapter 365 identifies Adam as the beginning from which Human Potential and Adaptability passed into later generations, while the Heavenly Father describes Humanity as having been fashioned according to his design.[2]
Later information complicates the idea that the entirety of Humanity can be reduced to one Pantheon's creation story. By Chapter 1045, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] possessed memories originating from numerous ancient Gods, Demons and other beings connected to Humanity's development. From this accumulated information, he understood that the Heavenly Father was not the only Divine being involved with Humans; different Creator Gods belonging to Earth's Pantheons had participated in the creation or development of Humanity within their respective regions.[1]
For this reason, Earth/Yggdrasil is a more accurate general racial origin than treating the Garden of Eden as the uncontested birthplace of every Human population. Eden remains specifically connected to Adam and the Biblical tradition.

#### Adam and the Original Human State
Adam was not simply the first example of modern Homo sapiens. He was the Human Progenitor and possessed Humanity's defining characteristics at a level ordinary descendants could no longer reproduce naturally.
Vlad explains the difference through the metaphor of a seed. In an ordinary Human, Potential and Adaptability exist like an extremely small seed. Adam, being the Progenitor from whom those traits originated, possessed the equivalent of an already fully developed tree. Modern Humanity inherited those characteristics from him, but only in drastically diminished form.[2]
Chapter 1079 later reveals that Adam's physical species was itself superior to modern Humanity. He belonged to the race known as High Humans, described by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] as perfected Humans who stood much closer to the supernatural world. Adam was stronger still because he combined High Human physiology with his unique status as Humanity's Progenitor.[3]

#### The Weakening of Modern Humanity
Modern Humans are substantially inferior to the state represented by Adam. Ordinary Humans can be born with genetic disorders, suffer mundane illnesses, possess fragile bodies and die from injuries that many supernatural creatures can casually survive.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually found this discrepancy suspicious. After examining the memories and histories of numerous beings connected to Human creation, including [[Lucifer]] Morningstar, [[Diablo]] and many Gods, he reasoned that the enormous difference between Adam and modern Humanity appeared too extreme to have occurred accidentally. He consequently suspected that Humanity had been deliberately created or developed in a weakened state by the Creator Gods involved with its history.[1]
This remains Victor's deduction rather than an explicitly confirmed statement from every Creator God responsible for Humanity. He himself noted that obtaining the complete answer would require knowledge directly from the relevant Human-creating Gods.

### Biology
Ordinary Humans are biological mortal beings. They naturally age, require food, water, oxygen and sleep, and possess none of the effortless immortality, regeneration or supernatural durability common among many other species. Severe blood loss, damage to the heart or brain, disease, organ failure and ordinary physical trauma can all kill a Human.
From the perspective of natural combat ability, Humans are explicitly described as the weakest major race encountered during the early story. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] notes that even unusually powerful Humans generally become dangerous because of weapons, supernatural systems, external gifts or abilities they discover rather than because the natural Human body rivals Vampires, [[Werewolves]] or other supernatural beings.[5]
The weakness of the Human body does not reflect the limitations of the Human archetype itself. Humanity's development is instead centered around overcoming those weaknesses through learning, cooperation, technology, supernatural disciplines, adaptation and evolution.

#### Potential
Potential is one of the two principal racial characteristics inherited from Adam. It represents Humanity's extraordinary capacity to develop beyond its initial condition.[2]
In ordinary Humans, this Potential exists at an extremely low level compared with Adam. Vlad compares it to a tiny seed that requires the correct conditions to grow. Humans are naturally social beings, and their progress becomes considerably more significant when they cooperate, exchange knowledge and build upon the achievements of previous generations.[2]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s own existence provides an extreme demonstration of how dangerous Human Potential can become when freed from an ordinary Human vessel. Although his Race changed after becoming a Vampire, his Soul had originally been Human and retained Humanity's characteristics. His transformation into a Progenitor caused his inherited Potential to develop toward the Progenitor-level state associated with Adam rather than disappearing.[2]
Potential does not mean every ordinary Human possesses unlimited immediate power. The individual still requires a body, Soul, resources, circumstances and methods capable of supporting further development.

#### Adaptability
Adaptability is Humanity's second defining racial characteristic. Humans possess an exceptional capacity to adjust to new environments, circumstances and systems of power.[2]
At the civilization level, this manifests through Humanity's ability to survive radically different climates, technological eras, political systems and supernatural conditions. At the individual and biological level, Humans can interact with a remarkable variety of supernatural systems and can undergo transformations into other races with comparatively few conceptual restrictions.
Human adaptability becomes particularly apparent under extreme supernatural conditions. Once Humans began living permanently inside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s territories alongside [[True Dragons]] and enormous concentrations of [[Natural Energy]], even citizens who had undergone no formal racial evolution became substantially stronger than old-Earth Humans.[6]

#### Ingenuity and Progress
Humanity's natural weakness encouraged the race to compensate through intelligence, invention, social cooperation and continuous development. Chapter 516 explicitly contrasts Human civilization with supernatural societies that remained culturally stagnant for centuries or millennia. Despite having little natural power, Humans created advanced technology, worldwide communication, modern societies and extensive martial arts traditions.[5]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] specifically identifies Humanity's ingenuity as a characteristic that powerful supernatural beings should not dismiss. The techniques developed by Humans even influenced beings such as [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], whose martial arts incorporated principles derived from methods created by mortal civilizations.[5]
Witches similarly retained this tendency toward progress because, underneath their supernatural [[Magic]], they remained fundamentally connected to Humanity. Victor considered their ability to continually advance one of the clearest demonstrations that "progress" remained embedded within the Human foundation.[5]

### Human Variants
The term Human can refer either specifically to ordinary modern Homo sapiens or more broadly to the Human racial archetype. Several significantly different Human states and lineages have appeared throughout the story.

#### Ordinary Humans
Ordinary Humans represent the baseline form of Humanity familiar from old Earth. They possess Potential and Adaptability but express those traits through bodies dramatically weaker than supernatural species.
Most ordinary Humans lack innate Energy manipulation, supernatural senses, regeneration or enhanced physical characteristics. Their greatest advantages historically came from numbers, cooperation, technology, learned combat systems and compatibility with external supernatural sources.
The Human body is nevertheless highly responsive to improved environments. This became increasingly obvious after Humanity entered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s growing civilization.

#### Environmentally Empowered Imperial Humans
Before the creation of Primordial Humans, ordinary Humans living within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s supernatural territories had already begun changing through prolonged environmental exposure.
By Chapter 1058, Hela observed that an otherwise "ordinary" Human living in Victor's city was approximately ten times stronger than an ordinary Human from old Earth. A sixteen-year-old casually lifting a one-ton vehicle was sufficiently normal that local Humans themselves did not consider it remarkable.[6]
This development was not yet a formal racial evolution. These people remained Humans, but generations of exposure to enormous concentrations of [[Natural Energy]], [[True Dragons]] and other supernatural beings continuously strengthened their bodies.
Their lifespans improved as well. By Chapter 1138, a Human living within the Empire could naturally reach approximately 150–160 years while retaining the physical condition of a healthy sixty-year-old. This resulted from supernatural saturation, extremely rich Natural Energy and the Empire's advanced medical environment.[7]
The Empire possessed technology capable of extending life considerably further, but such methods were deliberately restricted because Victor's government wanted birth, life, death and reincarnation to continue rather than creating a civilization composed almost entirely of immortal beings.[7]

#### High Humans
High Humans are the perfected superior state of Humanity represented by Adam before the weakening of later Human generations. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] did not originally invent the High Human Race; rather, he rediscovered and became capable of reconstructing people into this older and considerably more complete Human form.[3]
Victor first explicitly demonstrated this when preventing [[Alexios Alioth]] from completing his transformation into a Time Spirit. Instead of allowing Alexios to become an existence bound to the natural duties imposed upon Spirits, Victor strengthened and reconstructed his Human nature until Alexios became a High Human.[3]
Victor subsequently explained that Adam had originally possessed the same general species. High Humans are "perfect" Humans positioned far closer to the supernatural world than ordinary Humanity. Adam himself remained superior to a standard High Human because he was also Humanity's Progenitor.[3]
High Humans possess exceptionally strong physical and spiritual foundations. Adam did not suffer ordinary disease, possessed enormous natural physical strength, could recover from injuries fatal to ordinary Humans after approximately a day of rest, and existed in sufficient harmony with Nature to use [[Natural Energy]] in a manner compared with modern Youkai.[3]
Their defining Human Adaptability is also vastly more pronounced. Victor compares High Humans conceptually with fantasy Elves: Elves possess a greater natural connection with Nature, while High Humans possess considerably more extreme adaptability.[3]
Victor later transformed additional Humans into this state, including [[Abe-no-Seimei]], [[Andrew Walter]] and [[Liena Walter]].[8][9]
High Humans should not automatically be classified as biologically immortal. Victor can separately modify individuals to eliminate their natural expiration date. Abe-no-Seimei received biological immortality in addition to his High Human transformation, and Andrew was later told that he no longer possessed a natural expiration date after Victor altered him.[8][9] These modifications demonstrate what Victor can add to a High Human rather than establishing immortality as an unavoidable racial ability.

#### Primordial Humans
Primordial Humans are a separate advanced Human branch created during the mass evolutionary programs of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. They should not be confused with High Humans.[4]
Following the establishment of the Empire, [[Victor Elderblood]] and Velnorah initiated systematic programs to improve the species under Imperial rule. Through biological engineering, Divine assistance, supernatural resources and controlled evolution, the Human population was developed into the Race known as Primordial Humans.[4]
Their baseline physical superiority is enormous compared with old Humanity. Chapter 1139 states that even children belonging to the Primordial Human race can easily lift an entire building.[4]
Primordial Humans therefore represent a deliberate future evolutionary branch of the Empire's Human population. High Humans, by contrast, represent the perfected Human state associated with Adam and can be restored through Victor's direct manipulation of the individual's Race and Soul.

#### Witches
Main article: Witches
Witches are a supernatural Human lineage naturally capable of interacting with [[Magic]]. Early in the story, Witches are directly described as Human women who possess the Energy known as Magic.[10]
Later revelations establish that their supernatural nature is much more complicated than an ordinary Human simply learning spells. Witch Magic is tied to inherited characteristics originating from beings whose essence came from outside Earth's ordinary universal system, and those characteristics are rooted deeply enough to affect the Soul itself.[11]
Witches are therefore better understood as a supernatural Human lineage rather than merely a profession comparable to Hunter or Onmyo Mage. Their physical bodies historically remained much closer to Humans than to naturally physical supernatural races, but their magical potential radically distinguishes them from ordinary Humanity.

#### Amazonians
Amazonians are a specialized Human population historically associated with the [[Greek Pantheon]]. Unlike High Humans, Primordial Humans or Witches, the currently established material does not require treating Amazonian identity as a completely separate biological Human subspecies.
Their distinction is principally civilizational and martial. Amazonian society developed around warfare and survival under the patronage of Greek Goddesses, producing highly trained Human warriors capable of fighting supernatural enemies in organized armies.[12]
During the Demonic invasion, Amazonians fought large numbers of Demons directly despite suffering heavy casualties themselves, demonstrating how dangerous highly trained and supernaturally supported Human populations could become.[12]

### Supernatural Human Paths
Many Humans gain supernatural abilities without changing Race. These individuals remain Humans unless the supernatural process explicitly alters their species.

#### Hunters
Main article: Inquisition
[[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] are Humans trained specifically to fight supernatural creatures. The Inquisition historically represented the largest and most organized Hunter institution associated with the [[Biblical Pantheon]].
Their principal supernatural foundation is Faith. Chapter 153 explains Faith as absolute conviction or belief cultivated to such an extreme that it can produce supernatural phenomena and even bend reality through access to the "Source".[13]
The ability was described as originally accessible principally to Humans, giving otherwise physically weak mortals a method of fighting beings whose natural statistics vastly exceeded their own.[13]
Hunter is therefore a combat profession and supernatural discipline, not an independent Race.

#### Onmyo Mages
Onmyo Mages are Humans practicing [[Onmyoujutsu]]. Their system is closely related to the Faith-based supernatural techniques employed by [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]].
Mizuki explains that Onmyoujutsu operates through Faith directed toward the Gods and spiritual traditions of her homeland rather than the exact religious structure used by Biblical Hunters.[13]
Onmyo Mages can employ talismans, seals, barriers, rituals, spiritual entities and numerous specialized techniques, but practicing Onmyoujutsu does not by itself change the practitioner's Human Race.

#### Saints
Saints are Humans who receive exceptional blessings from the [[Heavenly Father]] and the Biblical supernatural system.
Unlike ordinary believers, Saints possess sufficiently powerful Holy authority to directly assist Angels on Earth. Chapter 433 establishes that Saints can declare Holy Territories, creating environments filled with [[Holy Energy]] in which Angels can operate at their proper strength without suffering the environmental weakening normally experienced outside Heaven.[14]
Individual Saints can possess additional unique blessings. Jeanne d'Arc, for example, possessed extraordinary abilities associated with her status as a Saint and Oracle, but such individual gifts should not automatically be attributed to every Saint.

#### Blessed Humans
Blessed Humans are ordinary or supernatural Humans who have received power directly from a God or another being capable of granting a Blessing.
The exact effect varies enormously according to the giver and recipient. A Blessing can increase strength, grant unique abilities, provide protection or connect the Human to a particular supernatural authority.
Receiving a Blessing does not necessarily alter the recipient's Race. Unless the underlying body and Soul are actually transformed into another species, the individual remains Human.

### Powers and Abilities
Human abilities vary dramatically between ordinary Humans, High Humans, Primordial Humans and supernatural Human disciplines. The following sections separate actual Human racial traits from powers obtained through specialized lineages or external systems.

#### Ordinary Human Racial Abilities
- Accelerated Development (Potential): Potential is one of Humanity's two defining racial characteristics. Humans possess the capacity to develop far beyond their weak initial condition when provided with suitable knowledge, resources, training and circumstances. In ordinary Humans this characteristic exists only as a small "seed", while Adam possessed its fully developed Progenitor-level expression.[2]
- Adaptation (Adaptability): Humanity possesses exceptional adaptability inherited from Adam. This allows Humans to adjust rapidly to changing environments, cultures, technologies and supernatural conditions and contributes to the enormous variety of evolutionary paths available to the species.[2]
- Supernatural Adaptation: Humans can successfully interact with numerous supernatural systems that did not originate from their ordinary physiology, including Faith, [[Magic]]-derived lineages, Divine Blessings, racial transformations and artificially induced evolution.
- Accelerated Technological Development: Humans compensate for poor natural physical ability through science, technology, weapons, medicine, organization and cumulative knowledge. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly contrasts this continual advancement with the long-term stagnation found throughout numerous supernatural societies.[5]
- Accelerated Learning / Skill Development: Humanity's dependence upon acquired skill rather than racial power encouraged the development of extensive martial arts, sciences and specialized professions. Human-created combat systems became sophisticated enough to influence supernatural martial artists such as [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]].[5]
- Environmental Adaptation: Human physiology can gradually improve when permanently exposed to exceptionally rich supernatural environments. Humans raised in Victor's supernatural city became approximately ten times stronger than equivalent old-Earth Humans without undergoing a formal racial transformation.[6]

#### High Human Abilities
High Humans possess the defining Human traits at a dramatically higher baseline than ordinary modern Humanity.
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: High Humans possess naturally enhanced strength, durability, stamina, speed and general physical ability compared with ordinary Humans. Adam is described as naturally extremely strong even independently of his later significance as Humanity's Progenitor.[3]
- Enhanced Regeneration: Injuries that would be fatal to modern ordinary Humans could be healed by a High Human after approximately one day of proper rest.[3]
- Disease Immunity: High Humans do not naturally suffer the ordinary diseases that afflict weakened modern Humanity. Adam is specifically stated not to have become sick.[3]
- Nature Energy Manipulation: High Humans possess a natural connection to Nature and can use its Energy. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] compares this ability with the way modern Youkai interact with Nature Energy.[3]
- Nature Affinity: Their existence is considerably more synchronized with the natural supernatural environment than that of modern ordinary Humans.[3]
- Enhanced Adaptation: High Humans retain Humanity's defining Adaptability at an enormously superior level. Victor describes their adaptability as the principal characteristic distinguishing them from similarly Nature-aligned races such as Elves.[3]
- Enhanced Potential: As the perfected Human state, High Humans possess a physical and spiritual foundation capable of supporting far greater supernatural development than an ordinary Human body.
- Longevity / Biological [[Immortality]] (Optional Modification): Victor can modify a High Human so that the individual no longer possesses a natural expiration date. This was explicitly done to [[Abe-no-Seimei]] and later [[Andrew Walter]]. It is an additional alteration and should not automatically be attributed to every High Human.[8][9]

#### Primordial Human Abilities
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Primordial Humans possess enormously superior baseline bodies compared with ordinary Humanity as a result of the Empire's deliberate racial-evolution program.[4]
- Superhuman Strength: Even Primordial Human children can easily lift entire buildings, establishing a racial baseline vastly beyond ordinary old-Earth Humans.[4]
- Enhanced Physiology: Primordial Humans were engineered and evolved to function within the supernatural environment of the [[Elderblood Empire]] rather than retain the physical limitations of historical Humanity.[4]
- Enhanced Adaptation: As a developed branch of Humanity, Primordial Humans retain the fundamental adaptability of their ancestral race while possessing a body better suited to exploiting that characteristic.

#### Imperial Human Enhancements
These characteristics apply to Humans developing inside the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s supernatural environment and should not automatically be assigned to historical Humans from old Earth.
- Environmental Empowerment: Continuous exposure to supernatural beings, [[Natural Energy]] and superior planetary conditions passively strengthens Imperial Humans. By Chapter 1058, ordinary local Humans had become approximately ten times stronger than historical equivalents.[6]
- Enhanced Strength: A Human teenager living under these conditions could casually lift an approximately one-ton vehicle before Humanity's later mass evolution into Primordial Humans.[6]
- Longevity: By Chapter 1138, an Imperial Human could naturally live approximately 150–160 years while maintaining the body of a healthy sixty-year-old.[7]
- Disease Resistance (Technological / Environmental): Advanced Imperial medicine and improved living conditions made ordinary diseases increasingly irrelevant. This is principally a civilizational benefit rather than an innate power possessed by historical Humanity.[7]
- Advanced Medical Restoration: Imperial medicine can restore lost limbs and resolve bodily damage that would previously have permanently disabled or killed ordinary Humans.[7]
By the Empire's later development period, its biological standards had increased even further. Chapter 1145 states that an ordinary Imperial citizen could easily lift an old-world building and possessed an average lifespan around 300 years, with approximately 400 years projected by the end of the hundred-thousandth Imperial year.[15] These statistics describe citizens produced by tens of thousands of years of Imperial development and should not be retroactively attributed to old Humanity.

#### Faith-Based Abilities
The following powers belong to Human supernatural disciplines rather than Humanity's ordinary biological state.

##### Hunters
- Faith Manipulation: [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] cultivate absolute belief and conviction into supernatural power. Faith can be directed toward a God, ideal or the individual themselves.[13]
- Reality Warping (Through Faith): Faith cultivated to extreme levels can produce phenomena capable of bending reality by allowing the practitioner to access the supernatural "Source".[13]
- [[Holy Energy]] Manipulation: Hunters connected to the [[Biblical Pantheon]] can channel Holy/Faith-based power through techniques and weapons designed to harm supernatural creatures.
- Weapon Empowerment: Hunter weapons can be empowered through Faith and blessings, allowing otherwise mundane weapons to injure beings resistant to normal physical attacks.

##### Onmyo Mages
- Faith Manipulation: [[Onmyoujutsu]] uses the same broad supernatural principle of Faith that supports Hunter abilities, although the belief is traditionally connected to Japanese Gods and spiritual systems.[13]
- Onmyoujutsu: Practitioners use ritual techniques, talismans, seals, barriers, spiritual familiars and other supernatural methods associated with the Onmyo tradition.

##### Saints
- Divine Blessing: Saints possess exceptional Blessings granted through the Biblical Divine system.
- [[Holy Energy]] Manipulation: Saints can wield supernatural Holy power deriving from their connection with the [[Heavenly Father]] and Heaven.
- Territory Creation (Holy Territory): Saints can declare a Holy Territory, saturating an area with Holy Energy. This permits Angels to manifest their proper power even in environments that normally lack sufficient Holy Energy.[14]

##### Blessed Humans
- Divine Empowerment: A God or sufficiently powerful supernatural being can grant a Human additional supernatural abilities through a Blessing.
- Power Bestowal (External): The exact abilities available to a Blessed Human depend entirely upon what the higher being grants rather than a fixed Human racial power.

### The Human Progenitor
Main article: Adam
Adam was the original Human Progenitor and represented Humanity at a level fundamentally above ordinary modern Humans.
His Potential and Adaptability were not merely stronger versions of the characteristics possessed by his descendants; as the racial origin, he possessed those traits in the fully developed state from which Humanity's diminished versions descended.[2]
Chapter 1079 additionally establishes that Adam possessed High Human physiology. This means his existence combined three separate advantages: the superior body of the perfected Human race, the defining Human characteristics of Potential and Adaptability, and the exceptional existential status of a Progenitor.[3]
Adam therefore should never be used to establish the baseline statistics of an ordinary Human.
The implications of a Human Progenitor are enormous. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later considered how dangerous a Human Progenitor possessing the relentless mentality and combat drive of someone such as [[Scathach]] could have become, emphasizing that Humanity's weak modern baseline should not be confused with a low ultimate ceiling.[16]

### Humanity and Racial Transformation
Humans possess exceptional compatibility with supernatural transformation. Throughout history, Humans have become Vampires, [[Werewolves]], Gods, Demons and other supernatural existences through rituals, infection, Divine intervention, Soul manipulation or other mechanisms.
This does not mean every transformed Human remains biologically Human. When the transformation changes the individual's fundamental Race, the person becomes a former Human whose original Human characteristics may or may not remain embedded within the Soul.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is the most extreme example. He began life as an ordinary Human before becoming a Vampire, yet Vlad determined that Humanity's Potential and Adaptability remained present because Victor's Soul had been born Human. His later Progenitor nature amplified those inherited traits rather than deleting them.[2]
The same principle explains why Human origin can remain relevant even after the individual's present Race becomes radically different from Humanity.

### Population and Historical Development

#### Old Earth
Humanity dominated the mundane population of Earth and numbered approximately eight billion before the catastrophic events surrounding the Second Genesis.
Most ordinary Humans remained unaware of the supernatural world despite living alongside Gods, Vampires, [[Werewolves]], Witches, Angels, Demons and numerous other hidden civilizations.

#### Second Genesis War
The large-scale Demonic invasion devastated Humanity. More than six billion Humans died, representing nearly eighty percent of the planetary population and reducing the approximate eight-billion population to around two billion survivors.[17]
Human civilization suffered further population decline and instability in the centuries that followed. By Chapter 986, the remaining Human population of Earth was approximately one billion.[18]

#### Integration into Victor's Civilization
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s expanding faction gradually absorbed the surviving Human population into territories shared with powerful supernatural races. The resulting environment immediately began affecting Humanity.
Long before deliberate racial engineering began, Humans living around [[True Dragons]] and supernatural concentrations of Energy had become dramatically more powerful than historical Humans.[6]
Following Victor's conquest and political consolidation of Earth, the planet was renamed [[Yggdrasil]] and brought fully beneath the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Victor consequently became the sovereign governing Humanity alongside every other race incorporated into his Imperial civilization.[19][20]

#### Evolution into Primordial Humans
The Empire eventually stopped relying upon environmental improvement alone. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Velnorah formally initiated racial-evolution projects throughout Imperial civilization, turning Humans into Primordial Humans while other species received their own corresponding evolutionary improvements.[4]
The Human population subsequently spread throughout the Empire's mother galaxy rather than remaining confined to [[Yggdrasil]]. Humanity therefore transformed from a fragile planetary civilization into one component of an enormous multispecies Galactic Empire.

#### Later Imperial Humanity
Tens of thousands of years of controlled development fundamentally changed what an "ordinary citizen" of Human descent could accomplish. Imperial biology was shaped through genetics, Divine Blessings, supernatural environments, selective development, medicine and interracial integration.[15]
By this era, feats that old Humanity would have considered explicitly superhuman had become mundane among ordinary citizens, while expected lifespans had increased into centuries.[15]
The enormous difference between pre-supernatural Earth Humans and later Imperial Humanity demonstrates Humanity's defining Adaptability on a civilizational scale.

### Society and Civilization
Historically, Humanity lacked the racial political unity common to many supernatural species. Vampires possessed [[Nightingale]] and its monarchies, [[Werewolves]] organized around packs and Samar, Witches recognized centralized magical authorities, while Gods organized themselves through Pantheons.
Humans instead developed thousands of cultures, nations, religions, languages and political systems. This fragmentation frequently weakened them in supernatural politics but also encouraged an enormous diversity of ideas and technological approaches.
Their dependence upon cooperation played directly into their racial Potential. Chapter 365 specifically emphasizes that ordinary Humans are social beings whose diminished Potential and Adaptability produce results most effectively when Humans unite and operate together.[2]
By Chapter 516, Human technological and societal advancement had become particularly remarkable when compared with supernatural civilizations that possessed vastly greater personal power but had culturally stagnated for centuries. Humanity's physical weakness continuously forced the race to compensate through invention and progress.[5]
The establishment of the [[Elderblood Empire]] eventually replaced Humanity's fragmented planetary political order with a multispecies Imperial system. Human identity remained culturally important, but citizenship under the Empire increasingly superseded race as the principal political identity.[19][20]

### Relationship with Other Races

#### Vampires
Humans historically possessed one of the most complicated relationships with Vampires. Human blood serves as a principal food source for Vampires, and many Humans were historically treated as prey by predatory Vampire factions.
Humans are also one of the principal populations capable of undergoing vampiric transformation. [[Victor Elderblood]] himself began as an ordinary Human before his transformation permanently altered the balance of supernatural power.
The relationship changed radically under the [[Elderblood Empire]], where Humans and Vampires became citizens of the same political civilization rather than automatically functioning as predator and prey.

#### Witches
Witches share a particularly close relationship with Humanity because they are themselves descended from and fundamentally connected to Humans.
Their [[Magic]] gives them a supernatural status ordinary Humans lack, but [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] emphasizes that their Human foundation is one of the reasons Witch civilization continually develops rather than remaining completely stagnant like many ancient supernatural societies.[5]

#### Angels
Humans historically maintained deep cultural and supernatural ties with Angels through the [[Biblical Pantheon]], Christianity, the Church, Saints and the Inquisition.
The [[Heavenly Father]] directly participated in Human creation and maintained extensive influence over Human religious civilization. Nevertheless, Angels should not be interpreted as unconditional protectors of Humanity; the policies of Heaven and Human religious institutions have not always aligned perfectly.

#### Demons
Demons historically represented one of Humanity's most destructive supernatural threats. The Second Genesis invasion resulted in the deaths of billions of Humans and nearly destroyed modern Human civilization.[17]
Under [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later rule of Hell and the [[Elderblood Empire]], the traditional assumption that Humans and Demons must remain permanent enemies ceased to apply universally. Both became populations governed under the same Imperial political structure.

#### Gods
Humans and Gods possess a mutually influential relationship. Human belief produces Faith, while Gods can answer that Faith with Blessings, protection, miracles and supernatural knowledge.
Earth's numerous Human civilizations historically developed alongside different Pantheons, producing regional mythologies, religions and Divine traditions.
At the same time, Humanity was frequently treated as a resource by Gods who benefited from mortal Faith. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later Imperial system greatly reduced the ability of Pantheons to independently control Human populations for their own interests.

### Weaknesses

#### Ordinary Humans
- Mortality: Ordinary Humans possess finite natural lifespans and eventually die from aging.
- Aging: Human bodies gradually deteriorate with age unless supernatural, technological or evolutionary intervention slows the process.
- Disease Susceptibility: Ordinary modern Humans can contract mundane illnesses, infections and genetic disorders. This is specifically contrasted with the original High Human state represented by Adam.[3]
- Physical Fragility: Humans possess extremely weak natural durability compared with most supernatural races. Damage to vital organs, severe trauma and blood loss can easily be fatal.
- Limited Regeneration: Ordinary Humans possess only natural biological healing and cannot rapidly regenerate catastrophic injuries.
- Low Natural Physical Characteristics: Chapter 516 explicitly describes Humanity as the weakest race in terms of natural baseline physical power among the major races being considered.[5]
- Limited Innate Supernatural Power: Most ordinary Humans possess no natural combat Energy or racial ability capable of allowing them to fight supernatural beings directly.
- Reliance on External Development: Humans generally require training, technology, weapons, Faith, Blessings, environmental improvement or racial evolution before they can compete directly with powerful supernatural creatures.

#### Potential Limitations
Human Potential does not automatically make an ordinary Human infinitely powerful. Potential represents the capacity for development; the individual's body, Soul, mentality, resources and circumstances determine whether that Potential can actually be realized.
An ordinary Human vessel remains dramatically more restrictive than a High Human, Progenitor or evolved supernatural body.

#### Faith Users
- Faith Dependency: Hunter and Onmyo abilities are dependent upon genuine conviction and belief. Because Faith derives from an individual's absolute certainty, severe psychological collapse or loss of belief can interfere with the supernatural system supporting their techniques.[13]

#### Blessed Humans
- External Power Dependency: A Blessing originates from another supernatural being and may consequently depend upon the conditions imposed by that source.
The exact restrictions vary according to the specific Blessing and should not be universally generalized to every Blessed Human.

### Trivia
- Humanity's two defining racial traits are Potential and Adaptability.[2]
- Adam possessed these traits in their full Progenitor state rather than the drastically diminished form inherited by ordinary Humans.[2]
- Humanity is explicitly described as naturally the weakest race during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Chapter 516 analysis, but also one of the most ingenious because weakness continuously forces Humans to compensate and progress.[5]
- Victor considered the continued advancement of Witches evidence of their Human foundation, noting that progress remained part of their nature even after [[Magic]] transformed their civilization.[5]
- High Humans possess enough natural connection to Nature to use [[Natural Energy]] similarly to modern Youkai.[3]
- High Humans were compared with fantasy Elves: Elves possess a superior natural relationship with Nature, while High Humans possess vastly superior adaptability.[3]
- By Chapter 1058, ordinary Humans living in Victor's supernatural environment had become approximately ten times stronger than old-Earth Humans without formally changing Race.[6]
- By Chapter 1138, ordinary Imperial Humans could live approximately 150–160 years while retaining the physical condition of a healthy sixty-year-old.[7]
- The Empire deliberately avoided granting universal immortality to its mortal population because Victor's government considered death and reincarnation necessary for preventing long-term civilizational stagnation.[7][4]
- Primordial Human children can casually lift buildings.[4]
- By the later Imperial development period, even an ordinary Imperial citizen could lift an old-world building and live approximately 300 years, with further biological improvement still underway.[15]
- Humanity's original planetary population fell from approximately eight billion to around two billion during the Second Genesis catastrophe after more than six billion people were killed.[17]
- By Chapter 986, Earth's remaining Human population had fallen to approximately one billion.[18]
