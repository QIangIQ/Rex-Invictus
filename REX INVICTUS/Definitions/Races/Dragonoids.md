**[[Dragonoids]]**, more specifically Blood Dragonoids, are a lesser Draconic race derived from the Blood Dragon lineage of Victor Elderblood. (Lore adapted from *My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** This material is adapted into the Rex Invictus world at the lowest priority: Rex Invictus wins any conflict, then the Flamel material, then MTWABV. Events of the MTWABV timeline (Victor's conquests, the fall of the pantheons, the [[Elderblood Empire]]) are distant background or rumor unless a chapter puts them on the page. Dragonoids, more specifically Blood Dragonoids, are a lesser Draconic race derived from the Blood Dragon lineage of Victor Elderblood. They are genuine Dragon-related beings, but unlike full [[Blood Dragons]] they do not possess the complete existence of a True Dragon. Victor describes the state as essentially that of a Humanoid Dragon: a being possessing significant Draconic characteristics and exceptionally high potential while deliberately lacking several of the qualities that make a True Dragon a qualitatively superior existence.[1]
Dragonoids became particularly important after the development of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Full True Dragonhood remained an extraordinarily restricted privilege because Victor could not indiscriminately transform entire populations into [[True Dragons]] without disturbing the Balance and attracting the attention of greater cosmological authorities. The Dragonoid state provided a solution: Victor could create powerful beings carrying a portion of his Blood Dragon foundation without elevating every soldier, servant or citizen into the same racial state as the Imperial Family.[2][1]
By the Imperial Era, Dragonoids had become one of the Empire's principal enhanced military populations. They were developed alongside Imperial technology, Runes, supernatural engineering and Victor's Bloodline to produce soldiers capable of participating in wars that had already progressed beyond the scale of ordinary planetary civilizations.[3]

### Overview
Dragonoids occupy an intermediate position within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Draconic hierarchy.
They are far more than ordinary Humanoids with cosmetic Dragon traits, yet remain fundamentally incomplete when compared with a True Dragon. The distinction is spiritual as much as physical.
A Dragonoid possesses:
- a Humanoid Draconic body;
- substantial Dragon characteristics;
- compatibility with Draconic systems;
- the potential to learn Draconic Runes;
- the potential to learn and use the Draconic Language;
- and supernatural potential vastly superior to most ordinary Mortal races.[1]
However, a Dragonoid does not automatically possess:
- a complete True Dragon body;
- a True Dragon Soul;
- a genuine True Dragon Form;
- the instinctive understanding of Draconic Runes possessed by a full True Dragon;
- or the complete natural relationship with Creation belonging to True Dragon physiology.[1]
Victor's Blood Dragonoids should therefore be understood as a deliberately reduced branch of his greater Blood Dragon lineage rather than as defective [[True Dragons]].
Dragonoid = a Humanoid Draconic existence derived from Victor's Blood Dragon lineage, possessing genuine Dragon characteristics while lacking the complete body, Soul, instincts and transformation state of a True Dragon.

### Origin

#### The Blood Dragon Lineage
The existence of Blood Dragonoids became possible only after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] created the Blood Dragon Race.
During the destruction of the [[Greek Pantheon]], Victor's Vampire and True Dragon characteristics completely fused into a new Race. The resulting existence was recognized as neither an ordinary Vampire nor an ordinary True Dragon, but as an entirely new lineage with Victor as its Primogenitor.[4]
As Primogenitor, Victor became capable of spreading his racial Essence to others.
Full Blood Dragon conversion, however, involves the reconstruction of the recipient's racial existence into a genuine True Dragon-level state. Victor later explained that the unrestricted creation of enormous numbers of [[True Dragons]] would disturb the Balance. For that reason, complete True Dragonhood was primarily reserved for those sufficiently close to him, while people outside that group could receive lesser Draconic states instead.[2]
This limitation created an important distinction:
Blood Dragon
Complete member of Victor's Race possessing the full True Dragon foundation.
Blood Dragonoid
Lesser descendant of that same lineage possessing a reduced Humanoid Draconic existence.

#### First Explicit Demonstration
The clearest early explanation of Dragonoids occurs in Chapter 960 when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] personally modifies [[Yuki Snow]].
Yuki had originally been a Vampire maid associated with the [[Snow Clan]]. Victor examined her, granted her a lesser form of his Draconic lineage and transformed her into a partial Blood Dragonoid. Her new body developed visible Draconic characteristics, including white Dragon horns and altered Draconic eyes.[1]
Rather than turning Yuki into a full Blood Dragon, Victor intentionally created a reduced state.
This scene establishes that a Dragonoid is not simply a Human-shaped True Dragon. Victor directly distinguishes the condition from genuine True Dragonhood by explaining that Dragonoids lack several of the racial components naturally possessed by full Dragons.[1]

#### Humanoid Dragon
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s description of a Dragonoid as a Humanoid Dragon is particularly important.[1]
Their default physiology is structured around a humanoid body rather than the enormous True Dragon bodies possessed by full Dragons.
Visible characteristics can include:
- Dragon horns;
- Draconic eyes;
- other Dragon-like physical traits;
- enhanced supernatural physiology;
- and characteristics inherited from the individual's original Race.
Yuki, for example, retained a Human-like appearance while developing white Dragon horns after her transformation.[1]
The exact appearance of every Dragonoid is not stated to be identical.

#### Blood Dragonoid
The term Blood Dragonoid identifies Dragonoids specifically derived from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood Dragon Essence.
This distinguishes them from the theoretical possibility of unrelated Humanoid Dragon races that might exist elsewhere in the Universe.
Within the currently established Elderblood system, the relevant Dragonoids are products or descendants of Victor's Draconic-Vampiric lineage.

### True Dragon Soul
One of the most important differences between a Dragonoid and a True Dragon is the Soul.
Full [[True Dragons]] possess a spiritual foundation corresponding to their Race. Their Dragon nature is not simply biological; it extends into the structure and quality of their Souls.
Blood Dragonoids explicitly lack a True Dragon Soul.[1]
This means their reduced state is not merely the result of possessing a smaller body.
A Dragonoid is incomplete on a metaphysical level when compared with a True Dragon.
The distinction can be simplified as:
TRUE DRAGON
Body ─────── True Dragon
Soul ─────── True Dragon
Instinct ─── True Dragon
Form ─────── Full Dragon Form
DRAGONOID
Body ─────── Humanoid Draconic
Soul ─────── Not a True Dragon Soul
Instinct ─── Reduced / learned
Form ─────── No True Dragon Form

### True Dragon Form
Blood Dragonoids do not possess the genuine True Dragon Form belonging to complete members of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Race.[1]
This separates them from [[Blood Dragons]] such as [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], [[Morgana Elderblood]], [[Jeanne Elderblood]] and other members of the Imperial Family who can exist as full Dragons.
A Dragonoid may display horns, eyes or other Draconic traits, but those features should not be confused with the ability to transform into a complete True Dragon.

### Connection to Creation
[[True Dragons]] possess an exceptional natural compatibility with Creation.
Their racial nature gives them unusually broad interaction with the world's supernatural systems and contributes to their instinctive understanding of Draconic supernatural principles.
Blood Dragonoids possess Draconic nature but do not possess this complete True Dragon foundation.[1]
Their relationship with Draconic systems is consequently more dependent upon:
- education;
- training;
- experience;
- Runic study;
- Energy control;
- and individual talent.
This distinction is particularly clear in their relationship with Dragon Language and Runes.

### Draconic Language
[[True Dragons]] possess an innate racial relationship with their own Language of Power.
Blood Dragonoids do not inherit the same complete instinctive understanding.
They can nevertheless learn the Draconic Language because they remain genuine Draconic beings.[1]
This distinction is important.
True Dragon
Uses Draconic systems from a natural racial foundation.
Dragonoid
Possesses sufficient Draconic nature to study and develop those systems, but does not receive the same complete instinctive knowledge automatically.
A talented and highly trained Dragonoid can therefore become considerably more sophisticated than an untrained member of the race, but this development requires actual learning.

### Draconic Runes
Main article: Runes
The same principle applies to Draconic Runes.
Full [[Blood Dragons]] possess natural compatibility with Draconic Runic systems. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later states that even relatively unexceptional complete Blood Dragons possess enough racial affinity to create basic Draconic Runes.[5]
Dragonoids do not possess that same instinctive racial knowledge.
They can still learn Draconic Runes through education and practice because their bodies and supernatural existence remain Draconic.[1]
This makes Runic education particularly valuable to Imperial Dragonoids.

### Transformation and Previous Race
Blood Dragonoids do not necessarily begin existence as a completely separate population born without previous racial characteristics.
Yuki demonstrates that an existing supernatural Being can be modified into a Dragonoid.[1]
She began as a Vampire and received a lesser portion of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Draconic lineage rather than being replaced by an entirely unrelated artificial identity.
This is consistent with the broader behavior of Victor's Blood Dragon lineage, which is capable of incorporating pre-existing racial and supernatural characteristics rather than automatically deleting everything that existed before transformation.[2]
However, the exact amount of previous Essence retained by every Dragonoid has not been directly quantified.
The approximately one-percent retained-Essence explanation given for full Progenitor conversion should not automatically be applied as an exact biological percentage to every Dragonoid unless directly confirmed.

### Purpose
The Dragonoid state solves one of the principal problems created by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s status as the Blood Dragon Primogenitor.
Full [[True Dragons]] are extraordinarily powerful and possess enormous long-term potential.
Creating them indiscriminately would be dangerous both cosmologically and politically.[2]
Victor nevertheless needed:
- soldiers capable of surviving supernatural warfare;
- personnel whose potential vastly exceeded ordinary Mortal limits;
- beings capable of using advanced Imperial supernatural systems;
- a military population directly connected to his civilization;
- and troops capable of functioning during the Empire's expansion beyond individual planets.
Dragonoids provide this middle layer.
True Dragons were too valuable and too restricted to become ordinary mass-produced soldiers. Dragonoids allowed the Empire to industrialize part of the advantage of Draconic existence without granting the complete Blood Dragon state to every warrior.

### Role in the Elderblood Empire
Following the establishment of the [[Elderblood Empire]], Dragonoids became one of its principal enhanced military populations.[3]
The Empire was no longer preparing solely for wars between supernatural factions on Earth.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had become an Overlord, and his civilization needed armed forces capable of:
- planetary deployment;
- interplanetary operations;
- conquest of hostile worlds;
- territorial occupation;
- protection of Imperial infrastructure;
- operations in environments unsuitable for ordinary Humans;
- and participation in conflicts involving Gods, advanced civilizations and extraterrestrial species.
Dragonoid forces were developed as part of this transition from supernatural planetary warfare to interstellar Imperial warfare.[3]

#### Interstellar Expansion
By Chapter 1112, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] no longer needed to personally descend onto every world encountered by the Empire.
John and newly developed Imperial forces participated in the conquest and securing of another planet, demonstrating the military system Victor intended to use during expansion. Specialized soldiers could invade, occupy, investigate and stabilize newly acquired territory while Victor remained available for threats actually requiring the personal intervention of the Emperor.[6]
Dragonoids were an important part of the class of military forces designed for this purpose.
Their existence allowed the [[Elderblood Empire]] to expand without making every military campaign dependent upon:
- Victor;
- his wives;
- the Dragon Goddesses;
- or other irreplaceable high-level individuals.

### Dragonoids and the Imperial Military
Dragonoids should not automatically be treated as synonymous with The Emperor's Soldiers.
Main article: [[The Emperor’s Soldiers]]
The Emperor's Soldiers were a specially designed military lineage created by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] beginning with seven unique original creations.[7][8]
The original seven were:
- [[Clare Bloodhunter]]
- [[Laura Bloodhunter]]
- [[Kiana Bloodhunter]]
- Conan
- [[Caelus Hunter]]
- [[Kaleb Hunter]]
- John
Victor intended these individuals to become the foundations of the Empire's future military command structure.[9]
By Chapter 1080, approximately 25,000 Emperor's Soldiers had already been produced.[10]
Dragonoids existed within the same rapidly developing Imperial military environment and became a standard enhanced military population.[3]
However:
Dragonoid = racial / physiological classification.
Emperor's Soldier = military creation, soldier lineage or organizational classification.
The available material should therefore not be used to declare that every Emperor's Soldier is automatically a Blood Dragonoid unless the individual is explicitly identified as such.
Likewise, membership in one of the later Imperial Legions does not determine a person's Race.

### Relationship with the Imperial Family
Full Blood Dragonhood increasingly became associated with the ruling dynasty of the [[Elderblood Empire]].
By the late Imperial Era, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood and True Dragon lineage were treated as identifying characteristics of the Imperial Family itself. The total number of complete [[True Dragons]] remained extremely small despite the enormous population of the Empire.[11]
The continued existence of Dragonoids therefore also preserves the separation between:
Imperial Bloodline
Extremely restricted True Dragon-level members of Victor's lineage.
and
Imperial Draconic Forces
Lesser Draconic beings who can benefit from Victor's lineage without becoming equivalent to the ruling [[Blood Dragons]].
This distinction became even more important as the Empire expanded across enormous populations and multiple worlds.

### Dragonoids and Lesser Dragons
Later chapters introduce additional Draconic classifications within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s expanded Bloodline.
Chapter 1171 explicitly distinguishes:
- Lesser Dragons
- Dragons
- [[True Dragons]][12]
Extended members of Victor's greater family can receive lesser forms of his lineage without automatically becoming True Dragons.
These later Lesser Dragons should not automatically be treated as another name for Blood Dragonoids.
Both classifications describe incomplete Draconic states connected to Victor's Bloodline, but the story has not explicitly confirmed that:
Blood Dragonoid = Lesser Dragon
as an exact interchangeable biological classification.
The currently demonstrated distinction is:
Classification
Established Meaning
Blood Dragonoid

Lesser Dragon

### Physical Characteristics
The exact physical appearance of Dragonoids varies.
Their defining body plan is humanoid rather than that of a full True Dragon.[1]
Known visible Draconic characteristics can include:
- horns;
- Draconic eyes;
- enhanced physique;
- and other traits determined by the individual's lineage or transformation.
[[Yuki Snow]] developed white Dragon horns after receiving [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Dragonoid transformation.[1]
There is no evidence that all Dragonoids possess white horns or an identical appearance.
Coloration and other traits should therefore be indexed individually.

### Abilities
The exact powers of a Dragonoid depend upon the individual. The Dragonoid state provides a Draconic foundation, but it does not grant every Blood Dragon ability or every power personally possessed by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
- Partial Draconic Physiology: Dragonoids possess genuine Dragon characteristics incorporated into a Humanoid body. They are supernatural Draconic beings rather than Humans merely wearing Dragon-like features.[1]
- Enhanced Physical Characteristics: Their Draconic transformation substantially improves the quality of their physical existence and contributes to their suitability as elite Imperial soldiers.[1][3]
- Draconic Traits: Dragonoids can develop physical features such as horns and Dragon Eyes. Yuki developed white horns following her transformation.[1]
- Blood Dragon Lineage: Elderblood Dragonoids derive from Victor's Blood Dragon foundation rather than from an unrelated natural Dragon species.[1]
- Draconic Language Affinity: Although they lack the complete instinctive understanding of a True Dragon, Dragonoids possess sufficient Draconic nature to learn the Draconic Language.[1]
- Runic Affinity: Dragonoids are capable of learning Draconic Runes despite lacking the natural Runic instincts available to full [[True Dragons]].[1]
- Supernatural Growth Potential: Victor deliberately created the Dragonoid state as a powerful lesser Draconic condition. Their potential remains extremely high despite being below the complete True Dragon state.[1]
- Previous-Race Trait Retention: The transformation can be applied to pre-existing supernatural beings rather than requiring the individual to be created as a Dragonoid from birth. Yuki's transformation demonstrates a pre-existing Vampire receiving the Dragonoid condition.[1]
- Advanced Supernatural Compatibility: Dragonoids can be integrated into Imperial training, Runic systems, technology and other supernatural military programs, contributing to their role as soldiers of the [[Elderblood Empire]].[3]

### Limitations
Dragonoids possess several major limitations when compared with complete [[Blood Dragons]].
- No True Dragon Soul: Dragonoids explicitly lack the full spiritual foundation of a True Dragon.[1]
- No True Dragon Form: They do not possess the complete racial transformation of a True Dragon.[1]
- Reduced Draconic Instincts: Draconic Language and Runic knowledge must be learned rather than being available through the same instinctive foundation possessed by complete [[True Dragons]].[1]
- Incomplete Connection to Creation: They do not possess the complete racial compatibility with Creation that defines full True Dragon existence.
- Lower Racial Ceiling: Their starting racial foundation is deliberately below the full Blood Dragon state.[1]
- Not Automatically Divine: Becoming a Dragonoid does not make the individual a God, grant Divinity, or provide Conceptual Authority.
- Not Automatically a True Dragon Candidate: No universal automatic progression from Dragonoid to True Dragon has been established.
- Individual Variation: A Dragonoid's actual combat ability depends upon training, Energy, prior Race, abilities, equipment, Runic knowledge and personal development.

### Known Dragonoids

#### Yuki Snow
[[Yuki Snow]] is the clearest individually confirmed Blood Dragonoid.
Originally a Vampire maid of the [[Snow Clan]], Yuki was personally examined and transformed by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] in Chapter 960. She developed white Dragon horns and Draconic eyes and was elevated into a partial Blood Dragonoid rather than being given full True Dragonhood.[1]
Victor subsequently placed greater responsibility upon her within his growing household and organization.

#### Unnamed Imperial Dragonoids
Large numbers of unnamed Dragonoids later served within the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s military structure.
By the Empire's early expansion era, Dragonoids were treated as one of the standard populations intended for advanced Imperial warfare rather than a one-person experiment.[3]
The complete population has never been given as a reliable fixed number.
Any older numerical statement concerning another military organization, including the approximately 25,000 Emperor's Soldiers existing in Chapter 1080, should not automatically be treated as the Dragonoid population.[10]

### Importance
Dragonoids represent one of the most practical applications of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s status as Primogenitor.
Victor's immediate family could be elevated into beings possessing overwhelming Draconic potential, but an interstellar Empire could not rely upon a few dozen [[True Dragons]] to perform every military task.
Dragonoids made it possible to spread a controlled portion of the Blood Dragon advantage through the Imperial military without destroying the exclusivity of full Dragonhood or recklessly creating enormous numbers of True Dragons.
They therefore occupy an important position between:
ordinary Imperial citizens and soldiers
and
the True Dragon ruling lineage
while allowing Victor's military civilization to exploit Draconic physiology, Runic education and Bloodline engineering on a much larger scale.

- Blood Dragonoids should not be listed as full [[True Dragons]].

- Abilities unique to individual [[Blood Dragons]], Dragon Gods or Victor himself should not automatically be assigned to Dragonoids.
