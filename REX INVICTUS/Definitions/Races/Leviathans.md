Leviathans are an artificially created supernatural species produced by Victor Elderblood and native to the World of Water, a separate water-filled Dimension contained within Victor's greater Personal Universe. They were created as one of Victor's first genuine original lineages rather than being modified descendants of an already existing race. Their creation required the interaction of Victor's powers over Beginning, Creation and his authority as a Progenitor, ultimately resulting in the System recognizing a specialized Concept responsible for the existence and reincarnation of the species.[1]
Unlike the Emperor's Soldiers, Dragons or elite members of the Seven Legions, Leviathans were not designed primarily for precision warfare or duels against individual elite Gods. Victor instead conceived them as civilization-scale biological weapons: the equivalent of an Imperial "nuclear option" that could be released when he wanted a hostile army, planet or even an entire Galactic civilization destroyed rather than conquered.[2]
Their development became increasingly extreme during the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s long accelerated era. The original population numbered in the hundreds of thousands, while later generations expanded into the billions.[2][3] Their bodies also possess extraordinary growth potential: early specimens already reached planetary sizes, Victor knew the species could naturally advance toward [[Jupiter]]-scale bodies and exceptional individuals possessed the potential to approach the size of a star. Later members became powerful enough for the largest among them to consume an Earth-sized planet, while groups of Leviathans successfully hunted a Star Animal larger than Jupiter.[2][3]

### Origin
The Leviathans originated after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] completed his dealings with [[Poseidon]] and turned his attention toward exercising Creation and Beginning on a fundamentally greater level. Rather than merely altering an existing animal, strengthening a pre-existing race or reconstructing something from inherited Records, Victor established the World of Water as a new Dimension and initiated an entirely new lineage within it.[1]
The World of Water is distinct from the main Dimension containing [[Etherea]]. Although both exist inside Victor's enormous Soul, the inhabitants of his primary world were transported into a different environment when the new Creation was established, confirming that Victor had formed another independent Dimension rather than merely transforming an ocean on his existing planet.[1]
Victor's declaration associated the new life with both Life and Destruction. The resulting Leviathans became enormous aquatic creatures whose natural development reflected both functions: they formed a viable living species capable of reproduction and long-term growth, but their scale and predatory nature also made them extraordinarily effective weapons.

#### First-Generation Souls
The Leviathans possess genuine first-generation Souls. They are not reincarnated beings whose Souls previously belonged to another species, nor are they simple copies reconstructed from the [[Akashic Records]]. Their spiritual existences originated with the new lineage [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] created.[1]
The act of creating such beings forced the System to recognize a new metaphysical responsibility associated with Victor. His powers of Beginning and Creation, combined with his Progenitor nature and immense Energy reserves, produced the specialized Concept commonly identified as <He Who Creates Monsters>.[1]
Through this authority, Victor became responsible for the life and reincarnation cycle of the creatures he had created. For the Leviathan lineage, functions ordinarily associated with larger cosmic systems governing Life, Souls and reincarnation could therefore be maintained through Victor himself.[1]

### Physiology
Leviathans are enormous supernatural aquatic organisms whose bodies possess an extraordinary capacity for growth. Their morphology has not been described in sufficient anatomical detail to establish one precise universal body plan, but they are consistently presented as gigantic sea-monster-like beings rather than humanoid creatures.
Their bodies vary enormously in size because the species contains individuals at very different stages of growth.

#### Aquatic Physiology
Leviathans are fundamentally aquatic beings. Their native environment is a Dimension composed almost entirely of water, and their lineage was explicitly created around the relationship between Water, Life and Destruction.[1]
Their enormous growth demonstrates that they are not constrained by the ordinary biological limits governing conventional aquatic animals. Specimens can reach planetary dimensions while remaining functional living organisms, and the species can maintain a population numbering in the billions despite such extreme individual growth.[3]

#### Supernatural Physical Characteristics
Even without individually named supernatural techniques, Leviathans possess physical characteristics far beyond ordinary biological life. Their enormous bodies provide correspondingly extreme strength, durability, predatory capacity and destructive potential.
Their ability to hunt and kill a Star Animal larger than [[Jupiter]] demonstrates that the species' combat effectiveness cannot be measured solely from body size. The dead Star Animal retained enough power after death to impress members of the Elderblood family, yet a group of Leviathans had successfully brought it down.[3]

#### Soul Connection to Victor
The Souls of the Leviathans belong to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] through the metaphysical system responsible for their lineage. He explicitly considers their deaths temporary because a slain Leviathan's Soul returns through the cycle under his authority rather than becoming permanently lost.[2]
This makes the species unusually suitable for catastrophic warfare. Victor can deploy them in situations where casualties would be unacceptable for conventional forces because destruction of an individual body does not necessarily represent the permanent end of that Leviathan.

### Behavior and Ecology

#### Collective Hunting
Leviathans naturally hunt in groups. Their pack behavior compensates for differences in size and individual strength and allows multiple specimens to attack cosmic prey that would otherwise be extremely difficult to kill.[3]
This hunting pattern was demonstrated when a group successfully killed a Star Animal whose corpse alone was larger than [[Jupiter]]. The creature belonged to a species capable of feeding upon stars and possessed an abnormal body integrated with Space itself, yet the Leviathans were capable of overwhelming it collectively.[3]

#### Predatory Behavior
The species is highly predatory. Mature individuals are capable of consuming planetary bodies, while groups can hunt gigantic cosmic organisms.[3]
The largest Leviathans were specifically described as capable of easily devouring a planet comparable to Earth. Their predation therefore operates on an astronomical scale rather than being restricted to ordinary marine ecosystems.[3]

#### Relationship with the Elderblood Family
The Leviathans are not treated merely as disposable ammunition. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wives cared for the species during its early development, something Victor explicitly noticed when he returned to accelerate their evolution.[2]
The Elderblood family nevertheless has an extremely unusual relationship with creatures of this scale. A Star Animal killed by the Leviathans was later taken as food for a family gathering, while Ruby, Velnorah and Aline harvested scientific samples from its organs before the meal.[3]

#### Population
The Leviathan population demonstrates one of the greatest examples of biological expansion inside the [[Elderblood Empire]].
Initially, hundreds of thousands inhabited the World of Water.[2] After the civilization's enormous accelerated development period, their population had increased to billions.[3]
The exact reproductive mechanism, gestation period and rate of natural birth have not yet been explained. The increase therefore should not be used to invent egg-laying, live birth or another specific method until the novel directly establishes one.

### Skills and Abilities
The following abilities are directly demonstrated or necessarily associated with the Leviathan lineage. Abilities belonging only to experimental Leviathan hybrids are not included as normal racial powers.
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics: Leviathans possess strength, durability and bodily power vastly exceeding ordinary biological organisms. Groups can kill cosmic animals larger than [[Jupiter]] whose bodies remain saturated with extraordinary Energy even after death.[3]
- Aquatic Physiology: The species was created specifically for the water-filled environment of the World of Water and can exist naturally throughout that Dimension.[1]
- Gigantic Size: Leviathans can grow from already enormous specimens into planetary and potentially stellar-scale organisms. Early individuals reached Earth-like dimensions, the species can develop toward Jupiter-like sizes and especially exceptional individuals possess stellar-scale growth potential.[2]
- Continuous Growth: Leviathans are not born at one permanently fixed maximum scale. Their bodies continue developing over time, with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] capable of accelerating their evolutionary growth as creator of the lineage.[2]
- Planet Consumption: The largest mature Leviathans can easily devour an entire planet comparable in size to Earth.[3]
- Mass Devouring: Leviathans can consume enormous quantities of matter rather than merely destroy targets externally. Victor's intended extermination doctrine specifically relies upon them consuming the contents of hostile Galactic territory.[2]
- Collective Hunting: Leviathans instinctively hunt in groups, allowing multiple individuals to overwhelm prey above the comfortable limits of one specimen.[3]
- Cosmic-Megafauna Predation: A Leviathan hunting group killed a Star Animal larger than Jupiter, establishing that they can prey upon organisms whose normal diet consists of stars.[3]
- Galactic Devastation (Collective): Victor considers a released Leviathan population capable of consuming an entire hostile galaxy within a matter of hours if he has no intention of preserving that galaxy for conquest.[2] This is a deployment scenario involving the race collectively and should not be misrepresented as every individual Leviathan possessing single-attack Galaxy destruction.
- First-Generation Soul: The original Leviathan lineage possesses newly created Souls rather than recycled Souls carrying previous-life Records.[1]
- Reincarnation / Revival: Their Souls fall under Victor's specialized reincarnation authority. Even if their current bodies are destroyed, Victor can return them through the cycle governing the species.[1][2]
- Soul Bond to Victor: The Leviathans' Souls fundamentally belong beneath the authority of their creator, preventing ordinary battlefield death from separating the species permanently from Victor.[2]
- Accelerated Evolution (External): Victor can accelerate the development and evolutionary growth of the race.[2] This is an ability exercised upon the Leviathans by their creator rather than an independent power every individual can activate.

### Military Role
The Leviathans are among the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s greatest biological military assets.

#### Strategic Extermination Weapons
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly distinguishes them from soldiers intended to fight elite Gods or perform precise operations. The Emperor's Soldiers and other specialized combatants exist for tactical warfare; Leviathans exist for situations where preservation of the battlefield is no longer important.[2]
Their intended role is comparable to a biological superweapon:
- destroy enormous hostile armies;
- overwhelm mass defensive formations;
- consume planetary infrastructure;
- devastate populated astronomical systems;
- and erase unwanted Galactic civilizations through sustained predation.[2]
For this reason, they function less like ordinary military units and more like a mobile extinction mechanism.

#### The Nuclear Option
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] directly conceptualized the species as his "Nuclear" option.[2]
If he wishes to conquer a civilization, more precise forces can be used. If conquest is unnecessary and only destruction matters, Leviathans can be released instead. This distinction reflects the broader Elderblood military doctrine of preserving useful resources whenever possible while retaining disproportionately destructive alternatives for enemies Victor considers unworthy of annexation.

#### Hindu Pantheon
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later deployed the Leviathans as part of the overwhelming force surrounding the [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]]. Blue power spread through the sky before enormous Leviathans manifested overhead, with even the smallest visible individuals comparable to mountains.[4]
Their appearance accompanied Victor's Heralds, his Demonic armies and the Dragon Goddesses of his household, making the Leviathans part of the demonstration that resisting annexation would result in annihilation rather than a conventional war.
[[Shiva]] ultimately submitted, after which Victor dismissed the Leviathans together with the rest of the invasion force.[4]

#### Mature Imperial Military
By the later Imperial era, Leviathans remained important enough to be named alongside clones, Dragons, Eldritch beings and thousands of Gods when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his Empresses evaluated the military resources available for future Upper-Sector warfare.[5]
They therefore remained relevant even after ordinary Imperial troops had advanced toward Planetary and Solar-System threat categories and weapons such as Polaris had reached Galaxy [[Destroyer]]-class standards.

#### Military Benchmark
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later considered creating a new generation of Eldritch beasts specifically strong enough to surpass Leviathans, Dragons and the Imperial Guard.[6]
This places Leviathans among the established high-level biological benchmarks of the mature Empire. The comparison does not establish that every Leviathan equals every Dragon or Imperial Guard member; each group contains enormous internal differences. It instead demonstrates that the race as a military category remains significant enough for Victor to use it when defining the desired strength of a completely new class of war-beast.

### World of Water
Main article: World of Water
The World of Water is the native homeland and principal breeding territory of the Leviathans. It is an independent Dimension contained within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul rather than an ocean located on [[Etherea]].[1]
The Dimension is composed almost entirely of Water and was created together with the metaphysical conditions necessary to support Victor's new lineage. The exact physical size of the realm has never been given. Any numerical estimate based merely upon how many Leviathans inhabit it would therefore be speculative.
By the later Imperial era, the realm supported billions of Leviathans despite many mature individuals reaching astronomical scales.[3]

### Experimental and Derived Forms

#### Eldritch-Leviathan Hybrids
The Leviathan lineage later became material for experiments conducted by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters. During one of their unsupervised projects, Yog Elderblood, [[Nigredo Elderblood]], Leone Luna Elena Elderblood and several sisters combined Leviathan characteristics with Eldritch lifeforms and their own supernatural influences.[6]
The resulting creatures were explicitly identified as hybrids between Leviathans and Eldritch beings.[6]
These experimental creatures displayed abilities not demonstrated by ordinary Leviathans, including:
- Space Swimming: Nigredo modified the creatures so they could swim through Space itself.[6]
- Black-Hole Survival: Yog granted them the ability to survive within black holes.[6]
- Extreme Adaptation: Leone modified them to exist under an extraordinarily broad range of environmental conditions.[6]
- Additional Power Integration: The sisters subsequently experimented with Dragon Fire, Dragon Lightning, Ice, Solar power, Truth and enhanced intelligence while continuing to alter the organisms.[6]
These powers must not be indexed as normal Leviathan abilities. They belong to a separate experimental derivative produced through multiple Elderblood daughters, Eldritch influences and Victor's materials.
