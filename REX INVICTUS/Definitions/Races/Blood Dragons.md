Blood Dragons, also known as Draconic Vampires, Vampyric Dragons and True-Blood Dragons, are a supernatural race originating from the evolution of Victor Elderblood, the first and supreme Primogenitor of the lineage. The Race came into existence during the conflict surrounding [[Olympus]] when Victor's Vampire Progenitor nature and increasingly complete Draconic existence ceased functioning as separate supernatural systems and merged into a single, fundamentally new form of life. Rather than remaining a Vampire who happened to possess Dragon characteristics, Victor became the origin of a new race whose defining foundation combined the complete properties of a True Dragon with those of a Noble Vampire.[1][2][3]
The creation of Blood Dragons represented a new stage in supernatural racial evolution. The cosmic authorities observing Victor's transformation recognized that his former condition as an additional Vampire Progenitor had been an anomaly, while the emergence of a genuinely independent Draconic-Vampiric race resolved that contradiction. Victor was acknowledged as the First and Beginning of a lineage standing above the existing Dragon and Vampire subspecies, establishing a separate racial identity rather than another branch belonging exclusively to either older race.[1][2]
The Blood Dragon foundation possesses an exceptional capacity to integrate and preserve previously existing supernatural characteristics. A recipient's Bloodline, original Race, Elements, mutations, spiritual properties and personal abilities need not disappear during transformation. Instead, compatible traits can be reconstructed within the new Draconic existence, often becoming substantially stronger or acquiring additional properties through their interaction with the complete Dragon body. This characteristic explains why Blood Dragons exhibit radically different physical appearances, Dragon Forms and supernatural affinities despite descending from the same Primogenitor.[4][5][6]
Although Blood Dragons possess the complete True Dragon foundation, they must be distinguished from natural [[True Dragons]], ordinary Dragons and lesser beings originating from Victor's Bloodline. Blood Dragons belong to an independent Progenitor race, while ordinary Dragons and Lesser Dragons possess weaker stages of Draconic existence. Blood Dragonoids represent another incomplete Draconic condition whose members lack a genuine True Dragon Soul and authentic True Dragon Form. These distinctions became increasingly important as Victor expanded his Empire and established explicit restrictions governing access to the highest levels of his lineage.[7][8]
By the later Imperial Era, the Race had become inseparable from the ruling dynasty of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Victor's original transformations established the first generation, while his children inherited highly individualized expressions of his mature Blood Dragon nature. Despite the possibility of expanding the species far beyond its existing population, Victor deliberately restricted the distribution of his Blood. Approximately forty to fifty True Dragons were known throughout his entire Sector in Chapter 1164, including members of the Imperial Family and other complete True Dragons. This figure describes the broader True Dragon population rather than an exact census of Blood Dragons alone.[9]

### Origin and Creation

#### The Birth of the Blood Dragon Race
The foundations of the Blood Dragon Race were established long before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s completed transformation. Through his relationship with [[Zaladrac Zeovnur Elderblood]], he gradually acquired increasingly complete characteristics associated with Dragon existence, including Dragon skin, Draconic senses, specialized veins, a [[Dragon Heart]] and supernatural powers influenced by Zaladrac's development within Hell. Despite these additions, Victor remained fundamentally a Vampire Progenitor because the spiritual foundation of his existence had not yet developed into a complete True Dragon Soul. The distinction between possessing Draconic organs and becoming a genuine Dragon was therefore established before his eventual racial evolution.[10]
During the confrontation surrounding [[Olympus]], Victor's body underwent repeated destruction and reconstruction under the pressure of attacks from extraordinarily powerful opponents. His regeneration continuously rebuilt the damaged structures, but the resulting body became increasingly Draconic rather than simply restoring its previous Vampire condition. The scales that had once functioned as a protective covering gradually became part of his actual biological structure, and his expanding Draconic characteristics approached the point where the two supernatural foundations could no longer remain separate.[11][1]
The transformation reached its decisive stage when Victor consumed [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]], an extraordinarily ancient Primordial God associated with the [[Greek Pantheon]]. His existing power underwent another violent evolution, causing his Vampiric and Draconic characteristics to fuse completely. The resulting Being was no longer accurately described as a Vampire Progenitor with an additional Dragon form or a Dragon Rider possessing unusual Vampiric biology. His entire supernatural identity had become the foundation of a new species, with the characteristics of both lineages operating as one coherent existence.[1]
The moment of this evolution was recognized by the Primordial authorities observing the disturbance. The Judges of the Abyss, the Owner of Limbo, the Universal Tree and other cosmic Beings became aware of the emergence of a new Progenitor. Their discussion established that the anomaly surrounding Victor's former position as an additional Vampire Progenitor had been resolved through the creation of a separate race. Victor became the Primogenitor of the Draconic Vampires, an existence recognized as standing above the established subspecies of Dragons and Vampires.[2]
This recognition did not mean that all [[True Dragons]], ordinary Dragons or Vampires were instantaneously transformed into Victor's descendants. The event established the legitimate existence and racial standing of the new lineage, while its actual population initially consisted of Victor himself. Additional members would emerge later through conversion or through children born directly from his Blood Dragon foundation.[2][4]

#### Recognition by Creation
The creation of Blood Dragons resolved an extraordinary contradiction in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s previous existence. He had originally become the Second Vampire Progenitor while [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] continued occupying the position associated with the original Vampire Progenitor. The emergence of a new Draconic-Vampiric race removed the need for Victor to remain classified within exactly the same Progenitor lineage as Vlad. His identity instead became that of the first and founding Being of a new supernatural species.[2]
This event also established that Blood Dragons possess a genuine racial Essence rather than representing a temporary combat transformation. Their existence is not sustained merely by the activation of a Dragon form or the possession of a particular external blessing. A complete Blood Dragon possesses its own coherent spiritual, biological and energetic foundation, with Victor's Primogenitor Essence serving as the origin from which subsequent members are derived.[2][4]
The Primordial authorities' recognition should nevertheless be distinguished from a claim that every Blood Dragon personally possesses the same cosmic authority as Victor. The establishment of an exceptionally high racial hierarchy concerns the nature and potential of the lineage, while the abilities available to an individual member continue to depend upon personal development, Soul strength, acquired powers and the degree to which that member can utilize the inherited foundation.[3]

#### Victor as Primogenitor
[[Victor Elderblood]] is the creator, first member and Primogenitor of the Blood Dragon Race. His Essence forms the origin of the lineage, and subsequent transformations and descendants derive their basic Draconic-Vampiric characteristics from him. His authority also distinguishes him from ordinary members because he possesses the ability to initiate the conversion of compatible Beings, reconstruct their racial characteristics and establish subordinate branches of the wider lineage.[2][4]
Victor's personal power is not an appropriate minimum standard for other Blood Dragons. By the later Imperial Era, his existence incorporated multiple Divinities, advanced Creator-related abilities, the Concept of Beginning, Negativity, accumulated supernatural characteristics, Eldritch powers, extraordinary martial mastery and numerous other developments independent of the original Blood Dragon foundation. These additional powers allow him to perform feats that ordinary members cannot automatically reproduce merely because they share his Race.[12]
In Chapter 1186, Victor explicitly distinguished his racial and combat foundation from the additional Authority supplied by Negativity. He explained that his strength was built upon his own combat skills, his lineage as the Primogenitor of the Blood Dragons, his Eldritch nature and his control over the abilities accumulated throughout his life. The same discussion confirmed that his predatory capacity to consume Beings and assimilate their powers originated from characteristics already present in his former Vampire existence, although it had become substantially more powerful through subsequent evolution and assimilation.[12]
These distinctions establish that Victor's Primogenitor-level predation, the authority he gained by consuming other Beings and the powers supplied by his individual Divinities must not automatically be assigned to ordinary Blood Dragons. Their Race shares the foundation from which his development emerged, not the complete collection of supernatural abilities he personally acquired.

### Racial Nature

#### Fusion of True Dragon and Noble Vampire
The defining characteristic of a Blood Dragon is the complete integration of a True Dragon foundation with the supernatural properties of a Noble Vampire. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] directly explained the nature of his Race during his instruction of [[Dún Scáith|Dun Scaith]], stating that its characteristics consisted of everything belonging to a True Dragon perfectly combined with those of a Noble Vampire. He identified himself as a True-Blood Dragon, emphasizing that the new condition represented a complete racial existence rather than two separate races sharing the same body.[3]
The True Dragon foundation provides complete Draconic physiology, extraordinary supernatural strength, a genuine Draconic spiritual basis, [[Dragon Heart]], [[Draconic Energy]], instinctive compatibility with Runes and an exceptional relationship with Creation. The Noble Vampire component contributes the supernatural characteristics inherited through Victor's original Vampiric existence, including its powerful regenerative foundation and the capacity for unusually complex Bloodline development. The resulting body operates through a unified system rather than requiring its two original components to compete for control.[10][4][3]
A Blood Dragon is therefore not an ordinary Noble Vampire who happens to possess Dragon scales, a Dragon Heart or the ability to breathe supernatural Fire. Those traits can exist in incomplete Dragon-related forms without representing full Blood Dragonhood. The defining difference lies in the complete reconstruction of the individual's physical and spiritual existence around the independent racial foundation established by Victor.[7][3]

#### Relationship with True Dragons
Main article: [[True Dragons]]
Natural True Dragons existed long before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s birth. Their Race possesses its own ancient history, reproductive characteristics, inherited knowledge and relationship with Creation. Blood Dragons emerged only after Victor acquired a complete Draconic foundation and merged that foundation with the characteristics of a Noble Vampire Progenitor. Consequently, Blood Dragons inherit the essential properties of True Dragonhood without being identical to the original natural Race.[10][1][2]
True Dragons are described as embodiments of Nature itself, possessing a profound compatibility with the structure of Reality. Blood Dragons retain that natural advantage through their Draconic foundation while expressing additional supernatural characteristics derived from the Vampire component and Victor's personal Bloodline. Their unusual adaptability partly originates from this compatibility, allowing inherited powers and previous racial characteristics to be incorporated into the new existence without necessarily producing instability.[4][8]
This does not establish that every Blood Dragon automatically possesses unrestricted control over Creation. The Race provides an exceptional foundation for interacting with supernatural phenomena, but the use of advanced Draconic Runes, Divine Concepts and other specialized abilities continues to require knowledge, Energy, talent and experience.[3]

#### Relationship with Noble Vampires
Blood Dragons also retain a fundamental relationship with the Noble Vampire Race, although they are no longer ordinary Noble Vampires. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s original Vampire Progenitor nature became part of the new racial foundation during his evolution, allowing Vampiric characteristics to exist alongside complete Draconic properties without remaining a separate competing system.[1][2]
This relationship is particularly important for converted members originating from established Vampire families. The Snow, Scarlett, Fulger and other Bloodlines possess hereditary abilities that were not simply erased during transformation. Their characteristic powers could survive and evolve as part of the resulting Dragon existence, producing individuals whose Dragon Forms continued reflecting the traits of their previous families.[4][6]
The survival of Vampiric characteristics does not mean that converted Blood Dragons remain subject to every ordinary Vampire limitation in exactly the same manner. Their entire physiology has been reconstructed through the Draconic foundation, changing the conditions under which inherited weaknesses and strengths operate. However, neither racial evolution nor the fusion of superior lineages establishes immunity to every supernatural attack or weakness specifically designed to affect Dragons.[4][13]

#### Racial Essence
The Blood Dragon Race possesses its own supernatural Essence originating from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Through this Essence, the Primogenitor can initiate transformations that affect far more than an individual's external appearance. A successful complete conversion restructures the recipient's biological foundation and incorporates corresponding changes to the Soul, supernatural Energy system, inherited traits and the manner in which previous powers function.[4][5]
The process is therefore fundamentally different from merely introducing Dragon Blood into another species. A recipient may retain recognizable traits from their original existence, but the resulting Being operates through a new racial foundation rather than simply carrying temporary enhancements supplied by an external source.[4]

### Conversion and Metamorphosis

#### Conversion Through the Primogenitor
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] possesses the ability to create additional Blood Dragons through the transmission of his Primogenitor Essence. The process was used repeatedly following his evolution, initially affecting selected individuals before expanding into a much larger transformation of his family. Rather than making conversion mandatory, Victor generally offered the transformation to those who wished to share his new Race, allowing their individual preferences and previous identities to influence the decision.[14][4]
[[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]] provides a notable example of an individual who declined complete Dragonhood. Despite recognizing the immense advantages associated with Victor's Race, she valued her identity as a Nine-Tailed Kitsune and the supernatural heritage accumulated throughout her life. Victor accepted her decision rather than forcing a transformation upon her. [[Mizuki Elderblood]], by contrast, showed interest because the Blood Dragon foundation could integrate the Onmyō abilities she had developed rather than requiring their complete abandonment.[14]
The broader principle demonstrated through these choices was that racial evolution did not necessarily require the destruction of every characteristic associated with the recipient's previous existence. Victor intended the transformation to improve the individual's abilities while preserving qualities that had become part of their personal identity.[14][4]

#### Preservation of Previous Racial Essence
A Progenitor conversion may preserve a small portion of the recipient's previous racial Essence. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explained that approximately 1% of an individual's former Essence could survive the transformation, allowing particular characteristics of their original Race to remain integrated into the new foundation. The small numerical proportion should not be mistaken for an insignificant effect, since deeply rooted supernatural traits may continue expressing themselves through the complete Draconic body.[4]
Victor illustrated this principle with a hypothetical Werewolf undergoing Blood Dragon conversion. The resulting Being would predominantly possess the new Draconic-Vampiric foundation, but surviving characteristics from the former Werewolf Essence could preserve Wolf-related traits through the natural adaptability and shapeshifting capabilities of Dragon physiology. The exact result would depend upon the recipient and the characteristics capable of surviving the reconstruction.[4]
Victor himself represented an exceptional case. By the time his Blood Dragon existence was fully established, the Human racial remnant remaining from his earlier transformation into a Vampire had been absorbed by his subsequent metamorphoses. This was a consequence of his particular development and should not be treated as a mandatory result for every individual transformed through his lineage.[4]

#### Large-Scale Transformation
The most extensive early expansion of the Race occurred when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] initiated a large-scale transformation of members of his family who had accepted Dragonhood. By this time, Victor had advanced beyond his earlier Mortal condition and possessed genuine Godhood, making the process considerably different from the transformations performed before his Divine ascension. His Essence and Divinities participated in reconstructing the recipients, while the resulting changes affected their bodies and the supernatural characteristics already integrated into their Souls.[15][4][5]
The women undergoing the transformation began expressing different supernatural characteristics as their new bodies developed. Their Dragon Forms, Elements, horns, wings and other physical traits did not follow a standardized template. Instead, the process incorporated existing Bloodlines, previous affinities and individual spiritual characteristics, producing Dragons whose new powers reflected their former identities through a more advanced racial foundation.[4][6]
The transformation also required caution when affecting Divine Beings. Because a God's Divinity is intimately associated with their Soul and identity, recklessly activating Divine powers while the transformation remained incomplete could interfere with the reconstruction process. Victor warned the participants against such actions, demonstrating that even his advanced Essence needed to interact carefully with the supernatural structures already present within the recipients.[5]

#### Dragon Eggs
During the mass transformation, enormous Dragon eggs formed around the developing recipients. These eggs possessed different appearances and colors according to the individuals contained within them, reflecting the fact that their new bodies were not being created from an identical model. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explained that the process represented the complete reconstruction of the recipients' physical forms rather than an unsuccessful transformation.[5]
The appearance of eggs surprised members who had undergone earlier transformations without such a stage. Morgana specifically observed that their own bodies had changed gradually rather than being reconstructed inside eggs. [[Aphrodite]] concluded that Victor's newly acquired Divinities were influencing the process, accelerating the transformation and allowing the recipients to develop complete Dragon bodies through a different method.[5]
These eggs should therefore be understood as a demonstrated reconstruction mechanism rather than an absolute requirement for every Blood Dragon conversion. Earlier members had acquired complete Draconic characteristics without the same visible metamorphic stage, and natural-born descendants follow reproductive processes distinct from conversion through Victor's Essence.[5][16]

### Physiology

#### Complete Blood Dragon Physiology
A full Blood Dragon possesses a complete Draconic body integrated with Noble Vampire characteristics. The new physical foundation includes the extraordinary strength, durability, sensory abilities and supernatural organs associated with [[True Dragons]], while the Vampiric component contributes its own compatible characteristics. This combination grants the Race an exceptionally high physical baseline, although individual members vary in actual combat power according to their age, development and additional supernatural abilities.[4][3]
The difference between complete Dragon physiology and the bodies of many ordinary supernatural races can be enormous. [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]], whose previous Werewolf physiology already possessed considerable strength, recognized that genuine Draconic characteristics represented a major improvement. Similar benefits applied to individuals originating from other races, although their new bodies could still retain specialized characteristics associated with their original existence.[4]
The complete Draconic body possesses a natural capacity to contain extraordinary quantities of supernatural Energy. Physical strength, durability, regeneration, transformation and the use of Draconic techniques are supported by a specialized internal structure rather than being dependent entirely upon conventional magical enhancement. The effectiveness of these characteristics nevertheless varies, and the full abilities of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] or highly developed Dragon Goddesses should not be treated as the normal minimum available to every newly created Blood Dragon.[4][3]

#### Blood Dragon Soul
The complete spiritual foundation of the Race is as important as its physical body. Genuine True Dragonhood requires a corresponding Draconic Soul, distinguishing full Blood Dragons from lesser Draconic beings whose physical characteristics do not possess the same spiritual completeness. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly established this distinction while creating Dragonoids, whose Dragon-related bodies lacked both an authentic True Dragon Soul and a genuine Dragon Form.[10][7]
However, the transformation of an individual with an exceptional Soul does not necessarily involve replacing that Soul with a standardized Draconic version. [[Jeanne Elderblood]] provides a particularly important example. Following her transformation, she possessed a complete Dragon body, but her original spiritual existence remained extraordinarily powerful, exceeding the conventional Soul structure of ordinary Dragons. The new body gradually integrated with her existing Soul, allowing her to recover previously restricted capabilities associated with her spiritual nature.[17]
Jeanne's transformation demonstrates that the Blood Dragon foundation can accommodate extraordinary pre-existing Souls instead of necessarily erasing them. The defining principle is the completeness and integration of the resulting existence, not the forced replacement of every unusual spiritual characteristic with an identical racial template.[17]

#### Dragon Heart
Main article: [[Draconic Energy]]/Dragon Heart
The [[Dragon Heart]] is a fundamental component of complete Draconic physiology. It functions as a supernatural Energy reactor supporting the body's extraordinary physical and energetic demands. The Heart works alongside specialized Draconic veins and other internal structures to generate, circulate and sustain the Energy required for movement, regeneration, physical reinforcement and the use of supernatural techniques.[10]
The quality and output of Dragon Hearts are not identical across the Race. Individual development, Soul characteristics, personal affinities and other supernatural influences can substantially alter their capabilities. Blood Dragons whose original existences already possessed extraordinary Energy systems may therefore display characteristics different from those of individuals transformed from comparatively weaker races.[4]
The Dragon Heart is nevertheless distinct from the True Dragon Soul. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] acquired a functional Dragon Heart before becoming a complete Dragon, demonstrating that possession of the organ alone is insufficient to establish full Blood Dragonhood.[10]

#### Draconic Energy
Main article: [[Draconic Energy]]
Blood Dragons naturally generate and manipulate Draconic Energy through their complete racial physiology. This Energy supports their physical strength, defensive characteristics, transformations and specialized powers, while the [[Dragon Heart]] and internal veins provide the structure necessary to sustain and distribute it. The Race's supernatural adaptability also allows compatible Energy systems associated with previous racial characteristics or personal powers to remain incorporated into the resulting body.[10][4]
Depending upon the individual, additional Energy systems may include Vampiric, Demonic, Divine, spiritual, magical or elemental characteristics. Their integration does not mean that every Blood Dragon automatically possesses all possible types of Energy. The Race provides an unusually compatible foundation, but the individual must still actually possess, inherit or acquire the relevant supernatural characteristic.[4][6]
An important later clarification concerns the natural Energy control available through complete True Dragon biology. [[Scathach]] explained that a True Dragon does not need to construct elaborate Runes merely to manipulate ordinary supernatural Energy. The Race's physical and spiritual foundation already provides the capacity for instinctive control, while Runes become particularly important when imposing more complex instructions, Concepts or alterations upon Reality. Since full Blood Dragons inherit this complete Draconic foundation, the same distinction applies to their racial development.[18]

#### Regeneration and Durability
The combination of complete Dragon physiology and Noble Vampire characteristics gives Blood Dragons extraordinary physical resilience. Their Dragon bodies possess substantially greater durability than most ordinary supernatural organisms, while the Vampiric foundation contributes powerful regenerative characteristics. These properties can complement one another, forcing an opponent to overcome the durability of Draconic flesh and scales while also contending with the supernatural recovery available through the Blood Dragon's integrated existence.[4][3]
Regeneration is not identical in effectiveness between every member. The rate at which injuries are restored and the damage that can be survived depend upon the individual's strength, Energy, Soul and any additional supernatural powers. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s extraordinary regeneration and later conceptual restoration abilities cannot be treated as universal Blood Dragon standards without direct evidence.[3]
Their regenerative advantages also do not establish unrestricted immortality or immunity to every supernatural attack. Powerful techniques capable of affecting Dragons, Souls or other fundamental components of existence may remain dangerous, while an individual with insufficient Energy or development may fail to recover from damage that a considerably stronger Blood Dragon could survive.[13]

#### True Dragon Form
Full Blood Dragons possess authentic Dragon Forms corresponding to their complete Draconic foundation. The Dragon Form is an expression of their actual racial existence rather than a temporary illusion or superficial transformation. Although many members commonly appear humanoid, their complete forms can reveal enormous differences in size, scales, wings, horns, coloration and elemental characteristics.[4][7]
The characteristics of a Dragon Form are influenced by the individual's inherited Bloodline, previous Race, Soul, supernatural affinities and personal development. A member originating from a powerful Fire-related lineage may display a form fundamentally different from another member whose abilities are associated with Space, Light or Darkness. These manifestations reflect the individualized nature of the Race rather than the existence of numerous formally established biological subspecies.[4][6]
The distinction between full Blood Dragons and Blood Dragonoids is especially clear here. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly described Dragonoids as lacking a genuine Dragon Form despite possessing Dragon-related characteristics. A full Blood Dragon, by contrast, possesses the complete racial foundation necessary for an authentic True Dragon manifestation.[7]

#### Shapeshifting
Blood Dragons inherit the natural adaptability and transformative characteristics of Dragons. This allows them to maintain humanoid appearances while expressing physical traits associated with their Draconic existence or inherited racial characteristics. Scales, wings, horns, claws, fangs and other structures may be displayed according to the individual's abilities and preferred manifestation.[4]
This flexibility also contributes to the preservation of characteristics inherited from previous races. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s example of a transformed Werewolf demonstrated that surviving Wolf-related traits could continue existing within the new Draconic foundation rather than requiring the recipient to abandon every familiar physical characteristic.[4]
The specific range of transformations available to an individual remains dependent upon their racial development and personal powers. A Blood Dragon possessing exceptional shapeshifting abilities before conversion may retain specialized transformations unavailable to other members, while the existence of one such individual does not establish unrestricted shapeshifting as a universal racial power.

#### Dragon Breath
Main article: [[Draconic Energy]]/Dragon Breath
Dragon Breath forms part of the complete Draconic foundation inherited by Blood Dragons. It allows supernatural Energy to be released directly through the Dragon body, producing attacks whose characteristics depend upon the individual's Energy, Bloodline and supernatural affinities. Although Dragon Breath is frequently associated with Fire, the Race is not restricted to one universal elemental expression.[19][6]
The nature of a Blood Dragon's Breath may reflect previous racial characteristics, mutations, inherited powers or additional Divine properties. Eleonor's Corruptive Fire provides a notable example because its effects extend beyond ordinary physical destruction and can continue damaging the Soul of a target that initially survives the attack. Such specialized effects arise from the individual's particular supernatural characteristics rather than belonging automatically to every Blood Dragon Breath.[6]
Consequently, Dragon Breath should be indexed as a common Draconic racial ability, while individual forms such as Corruptive Fire, Holy Light or other specialized attacks must be attributed to their demonstrated users.

#### Advanced Dragon God Physiology
Some Blood Dragons later acquired genuine Godhood and developed bodies whose composition differed considerably from that of ordinary biological beings. Chapter 1006 describes highly developed Dragon Gods whose internal blood possessed characteristics resembling concentrated golden liquid Energy rather than conventional biological fluid. Despite this development, important Draconic organs and structures remained functional because they continued serving necessary supernatural purposes.[20]
This condition represents an advanced supernatural development associated with Dragon Godhood rather than the default physiology of every Blood Dragon. Ordinary members do not automatically possess bodies composed predominantly of [[Divine Energy]] simply because they share [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s racial foundation.[20]

### Relationship with Creation
The Blood Dragon Race inherits the extraordinary compatibility with Creation belonging to its complete True Dragon foundation. This relationship makes the Race unusually well adapted to the structure of Reality and capable of incorporating supernatural characteristics that would be difficult for less compatible beings to contain within a single existence.[4][3]
The advantages of this compatibility extend beyond ordinary physical strength. A complete Blood Dragon possesses a natural foundation for manipulating Energy, understanding Draconic Runes and developing powers that interact with the structure of Reality itself. However, the individual ability to create complicated supernatural effects remains dependent upon knowledge, intention, available Energy and the ability to impose appropriate instructions upon the environment.[3][18]
This compatibility also contributes to the Race's extraordinary adaptability during reproduction and conversion. Instead of automatically rejecting characteristics associated with another powerful supernatural lineage, the Draconic foundation may integrate those traits while maintaining its own essential identity. The specific results differ considerably according to the individuals involved.[4][21]
Blood Dragons should nevertheless not be described as universally possessing complete Creation Manipulation. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s advanced ability to create, reconstruct or consume extraordinary supernatural phenomena results from the combination of his Race, Primogenitor status, individual Divinities, accumulated powers and later developments rather than the ordinary capacities of every member.[12]

### Draconic Language
Main article: Runes
Blood Dragons inherit the supernatural language associated with complete Dragon existence. The Draconic Language is an ancient system whose extraordinary properties extend beyond conventional communication, allowing a sufficiently developed Dragon to influence the surrounding world through supernatural commands. Zaladrac described its application as the Speech of Power, a technique involving the interaction of Energy, Voice and Intention.[22]
Fundamental Principle of the Speech of Power
Energy + Voice + Intention
A Draconic command imposes a supernatural effect through the user's Energy and [[Will Solace|Will]].
The most important aspect of the Speech of Power is the intention accompanying the command. Identical words can produce different results when used with different purposes, while forcing a word to express an effect poorly aligned with its ordinary meaning can require substantially greater Energy. The ability is therefore not merely the repetition of a fixed magical phrase; it depends upon the individual's understanding and the supernatural authority expressed through the language.[22][3]
Converted Blood Dragons also demonstrate access to relatively simple spoken Draconic effects. After the transformations of the Elderblood family, several members began exploring the direct imposition of commands upon Reality, while Anna demonstrated an exceptionally powerful version of an ability related to the same general Draconic foundation. These effects established that the language is relevant to more than its oldest natural-born users, but they did not make Anna's extraordinary abilities a universal Blood Dragon characteristic.[23]

### Draconic Runes
Main article: Runes
Blood Dragons possess an exceptional natural affinity for Draconic Runes through their complete True Dragon foundation. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explained that Runecraft came naturally to him because of his Race and that even comparatively untalented members retained the foundation necessary to create basic Draconic Runes. This racial advantage does not mean every individual possesses the intelligence, experience or understanding necessary to construct complicated Runic arrangements.[3]
Draconic Runes are especially significant because of their relationship with Creation. They allow a practitioner to impose instructions affecting supernatural phenomena, potentially modifying environmental conditions, physical properties, Energy flows or more complicated structures of Reality. However, the effectiveness of the resulting technique depends upon the user's available Energy, understanding, accuracy and the nature of the effect being attempted.[22][3]
The later Imperial Era provided an important clarification concerning their proper role. During [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]]'s training of [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]], [[Scathach]] explained that a True Dragon should not require elaborate Runes to perform every ordinary manipulation of Energy. Their complete racial biology already supports instinctive control, while Runes are especially valuable when attempting to impose Concepts or change the deeper conditions governing a phenomenon.[18]
A Blood Dragon may consequently possess the natural ability to manipulate Energy without possessing advanced Runic skill. Complex Draconic Runes remain a specialized discipline requiring training, understanding and personal development rather than a fixed power automatically mastered by every member.

### Power Preservation and Evolution

#### Assimilation of Previous Abilities
One of the most important properties of Blood Dragon conversion is its ability to preserve and reconstruct supernatural characteristics originating from a recipient's previous existence. The process can incorporate inherited Bloodlines, racial characteristics, Elements, mutations, magical abilities and other compatible powers rather than simply replacing them with a standardized Dragon ability set.[4][6]
This does not mean that every previous ability remains completely unchanged. The new Draconic foundation can alter the manner in which the power is generated, expressed and sustained, sometimes increasing its output or allowing it to interact with supernatural phenomena that the previous body could not effectively affect. The actual result depends upon the nature of the original ability and how successfully it integrates with the new racial Essence.[6]
The transformations of the Elderblood family demonstrate this principle through numerous different Bloodlines. Members of the Fulger family retained their Lightning characteristics, while Eleonor's pre-existing powers associated with Space, Earth and Corruptive Fire became incorporated into her complete Dragon existence. Other individuals similarly developed new expressions of abilities that had previously been limited by weaker physical or spiritual foundations.[6]

#### Adaptive Evolution
The adaptation produced through Blood Dragon transformation is not identical to unrestricted instantaneous evolution. The Race possesses an unusually compatible foundation through which powers can be preserved, strengthened and further developed, but individual results still depend upon the recipient's existing characteristics and subsequent growth.[4][6]
A power may become substantially more effective after receiving the support of a complete Dragon body, while another may acquire a different expression because of its interaction with [[Draconic Energy]]. Certain abilities may remain closely associated with their previous racial origin, whereas others become almost unrecognizable after being integrated into the Blood Dragon foundation.[6]
The same flexibility continues through natural inheritance. Children descended from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] can inherit maternal powers that become integrated into the dominant Draconic foundation, producing forms of supernatural development that differ from both parents rather than following a fixed template.[21]

#### Hybridization
Blood Dragons possess extraordinary potential for incorporating powerful secondary lineages. This characteristic becomes particularly evident when a Blood Dragon reproduces with another Progenitor or an exceptionally powerful supernatural Being whose Essence contains deeply established racial properties.[21]
Under ordinary circumstances, the True Dragon foundation is extremely dominant during inheritance. However, characteristics originating from an unusually powerful parent may survive and integrate with the Draconic nature, producing offspring whose abilities reflect both lineages rather than simply inheriting a diminished copy of either.[21]
These offspring may possess Demonic, Werewolf, Divine, Eldritch or other supernatural characteristics according to the identities and abilities of their parents. Such traits should be attributed to the actual individuals possessing them rather than indexed as universal Blood Dragon powers.

#### Predatory Bloodline
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s mature Blood Dragon lineage possesses particularly aggressive inheritance characteristics. Chapter 1168 describes his genes as extraordinarily predatory, preventing a direct descendant of his mature Dragon lineage from simply being born as an inferior racial hybrid. The child's foundation remains complete Draconic existence, while compatible maternal characteristics are absorbed into that foundation rather than replacing it.[24]
The explanation concerns Victor's exceptionally developed personal Bloodline and should not automatically be applied as an identical reproductive rule to every hypothetical Blood Dragon born outside his direct lineage. Nevertheless, it establishes one of the most important characteristics of his Imperial descendants: their Draconic nature is not weakened merely because a parent possesses a different supernatural Race.[24]
Chapter 1186 also reinforces the predatory basis of Victor's development. He explains that the ability to devour and assimilate other Beings originated from his Vampiric foundation and had become substantially more powerful through his later evolution. However, his ability to assimilate extraordinary powers without relying upon the same processes as other characters reflects his personal development and should not be treated as evidence that every Blood Dragon can consume any Being and freely acquire all of its abilities.[12]

### Individual Blood Dragon Manifestations
Blood Dragons do not possess one standardized Dragon Form or universal elemental affinity. Their manifestations reflect the characteristics of their previous existences, personal Souls, inherited Bloodlines and additional supernatural powers. Names such as Red Dragon, Light Dragon, Dream Dragon or Corruptive Fire Dragon generally describe an individual's appearance and abilities rather than automatically establishing a separate formally recognized biological subspecies.[25][17][6]

### Blood Dragon Gods
A Blood Dragon God is a Blood Dragon who additionally possesses genuine Godhood. Blood Dragonhood and Godhood are related but distinct supernatural conditions: the former describes the individual's racial existence, while the latter concerns the acquisition of Divinity and the corresponding spiritual properties of a God.[20][3]
Numerous members of the Elderblood family eventually acquired Divine Concepts, allowing them to combine complete Draconic physiology with supernatural authorities associated with Order, Destruction, Space-Time, Nature and other individual Concepts. These combinations can be extraordinarily powerful, but the specific Divinity remains personal rather than becoming a universal racial ability.[24]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explained the qualitative advantage of True Dragon Godhood through comparisons between ordinary powerful Gods and Dragons possessing comparable Divine development. The difference concerned the existential foundation of the Dragon rather than simply the amount of raw Energy available. A Dragon God possesses an unusual natural harmony with Reality, providing additional advantages in the application of supernatural power.[26][24]
The same distinction explains why a Blood Dragon Goddess may still require additional training despite possessing extraordinary power. Individual Divinity and inherited abilities do not automatically grant complete control over martial arts, [[Draconic Energy]] or Runic construction. [[Scathach]]'s instruction of Albedo demonstrated that even an exceptionally powerful Dragon Goddess could possess serious weaknesses in practical combat fundamentals if she relied excessively upon the magical methods associated with her previous existence.[18]
Blood Dragon Godhood must also be distinguished from the Primordial state of existence possessed by the original cosmic administrators. A Dragon Goddess may wield tremendous Divine power without becoming responsible for maintaining the underlying laws of Creation.

### Inheritance and Reproduction

#### Natural-Born Blood Dragons
The first Blood Dragons were created through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s transformation or the direct transmission of his Essence, but later generations were born naturally into the Imperial lineage. Victor's daughters inherited complete Draconic characteristics together with supernatural properties originating from their mothers, producing an exceptionally diverse generation of Blood Dragons whose individual abilities varied considerably.[27][21]
The inherited traits could include Bloodlines, mutations, elemental affinities, Divine characteristics, specialized spiritual properties or the influence of another powerful Progenitor. Because the Draconic foundation is highly dominant and compatible with supernatural inheritance, these characteristics could survive without reducing the child to an incomplete Dragon-related hybrid.[21][24]
Despite their shared paternal foundation, the daughters did not become identical copies of Victor. Their bodies, Souls, Divine affinities and developing abilities reflected the characteristics of both parents and the unusual circumstances surrounding their births.[21]

#### Dominance of the Primogenitor's Lineage
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s mature Bloodline possesses extraordinary reproductive dominance. In Chapter 1168, he explained that the inherited Draconic foundation of his direct descendants was so predatory that it prevented the child from naturally developing into an inferior non-Dragon state. The offspring remained a complete True Dragon-level Being while the maternal characteristics were integrated into the resulting existence.[24]
This feature is particularly important when the mother possesses another exceptional racial or Progenitor lineage. The child may inherit significant properties from both parents, but those characteristics do not necessarily destroy the dominant Draconic foundation. The result is a Blood Dragon whose individuality reflects multiple supernatural inheritances without being reduced to an ordinary mixture of incomplete races.[21][24]
The principle must nevertheless be distinguished from unrestricted inheritance of every parental power. A child may receive a powerful foundation or dormant characteristics requiring further development, while the complete abilities possessed by either parent remain dependent upon the child's own growth and personal circumstances.

#### Soul-Based Reproduction
Because full Blood Dragons possess complete True Dragon-level foundations, their reproduction may involve the spiritual characteristics associated with natural True Dragon reproduction. Chapter 1102 explains that ordinary True Dragon conception involves the Souls of the parents participating directly in the formation of the child's Soul. Natural non-Divine True Dragon mothers subsequently lay eggs in which the physical body develops around the resulting spiritual foundation.[16]
The Elderblood family includes numerous Gods, Progenitors and other exceptional supernatural Beings whose reproductive characteristics may differ from those of ordinary natural [[True Dragons]]. The existence of a standard natural Dragon reproductive mechanism therefore does not establish that every child born within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family must develop through exactly the same process.[16]
Soul compatibility remains important because the enormous spiritual requirements of True Dragon reproduction prevent ordinary Humans from naturally reproducing with complete True Dragons under conventional circumstances. Sufficiently powerful spiritual beings may be compatible despite originating from another Race, while the nature and development of the offspring depend upon the participating parental Souls.[16]

#### Multiple Offspring and Energetic Cost
Blood Dragons are not inherently restricted to producing one child at a time. The Imperial Family's ability to have twins or multiple offspring is directly established, but carrying several exceptionally powerful children places enormous demands upon the mother. The greater the potential of the developing offspring, the more significant the Energy and supernatural resources required for pregnancy and development.[16][28]
Chapter 1164 further clarifies that the common pattern of one daughter per wife was largely deliberate rather than an unavoidable biological restriction. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his family possessed the strength to survive pregnancies involving multiple Dragon children, but the increased strain and the Emperor's concern over the rapid growth of an extraordinarily powerful Race encouraged restraint.[9]
This limitation therefore involves both reproduction and political circumstances. The family was aware that higher cosmic authorities were observing its development, and Victor had already received warnings concerning the possible consequences of multiplying complete [[True Dragons]] too rapidly.[9]

#### Inherited Knowledge
The later Imperial generation inherited more than ordinary biological characteristics. Chapter 1174 explains that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s development into an Ancient Dragon had established an extensive personal legacy, allowing his daughters to inherit basic knowledge of the world and dormant information concerning supernatural techniques and abilities. These characteristics could become accessible as the individual developed, but were not necessarily available for immediate use at full effectiveness upon birth.[29]
This inheritance differs from the ancient ancestral knowledge possessed by naturally born Dragons such as Zaladrac. Her lineage preserved accumulated information transmitted through numerous Dragon generations, while Victor's daughters inherited knowledge through the legacy established by their own father. Both forms of inheritance provide extraordinary advantages without guaranteeing immediate mastery of every inherited technique.[29]

#### Exceptional Maturation
Blood Dragon development can vary enormously according to the inherited characteristics and powers of the parents. The later Imperial daughters demonstrate this flexibility, with individual gestation periods and physical development differing from the natural timescales associated with ordinary [[True Dragons]].[30][29]
[[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]] provides an exceptional example. Unlike the earlier daughters, whose gestation commonly exceeded one thousand years, Moira was born after approximately three hundred years. Her accelerated development reflected the extraordinary characteristics of her personal inheritance and her unusual relationship with Luck and Reality rather than a universal reduction in the maturation period of every Blood Dragon.[29]
The biological and spiritual circumstances of these exceptional children should not be treated as evidence that every naturally born member possesses identical abilities or developmental speed.

### Population and Rarity
Blood Dragons remain an exceptionally limited supernatural race concentrated within Clan Elderblood and the ruling dynasty of the Empire. Although [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] possesses the ability to create additional members through his Essence, he has deliberately refrained from expanding the population indiscriminately.[9]
By Chapter 1164, Velnorah estimated that approximately forty to fifty [[True Dragons]] existed throughout the entire Sector. This population included members of Victor's family, but it was not presented as an exact census limited exclusively to the Blood Dragon Race. The distinction matters because natural True Dragons predate Victor's lineage and remain a separate racial classification.[9]
Velnorah considered the rarity of True Dragons one of the few significant disadvantages associated with their otherwise extraordinary nature. Their compatibility with Existence, immense racial potential and superior supernatural characteristics made them unusually powerful, but their small population could become a strategic vulnerability without a civilization capable of protecting them.[9]
Victor's Imperial resources allowed him to overcome many practical disadvantages created by the small population, while his authority as Primogenitor gave him a means of increasing it. Nevertheless, he deliberately restricted the distribution of his Blood after receiving warnings from Primordial Beings concerning the consequences of rapidly multiplying such powerful existences.[9]
The Race consequently developed as an Imperial Bloodline rather than a mass-produced enhancement granted to every citizen. Its rarity is the result of natural reproductive difficulty, the enormous requirements of complete True Dragonhood and the deliberate decisions of Victor and his family.

### The Imperial Bloodline
By the later Imperial Era, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood Dragon lineage had become one of the principal foundations of the ruling dynasty. The Emperor's Blood represented membership in an extraordinarily powerful racial inheritance and the direct continuation of his personal supernatural existence. This status distinguished the immediate Imperial Family from the enormous populations of ordinary Vampires, Dragons, Demons, Humans and other species living within the Empire.[9]
The Empire nevertheless contained additional branches of Victor's wider Draconic lineage. These included Lesser Dragons and ordinary Dragons who possessed portions of the Emperor's inherited characteristics without becoming complete True-Blood Dragons. Their existence allowed the Empire to strengthen selected individuals and extended family members without granting them the highest available racial foundation.[8]

#### Restrictions on Conversion
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wives established demanding conditions for individuals seeking entry into his Draconic lineage. Membership in the extended family did not automatically qualify a person to receive the Emperor's Blood, and even relatives closely connected to the Imperial household could be required to pass difficult trials before obtaining a transformation.[8]
Violet and the other Empresses argued that the privilege had to be earned rather than distributed solely through family relationships. They compared these requirements with the demanding standards imposed upon the Empire's most trusted servants, emphasizing that even prestigious personal connections did not remove the need to demonstrate loyalty, competence and suitable character.[8]
The distinction between full True Dragonhood and lower Draconic classifications remained particularly strict. Complete [[True Dragons]] were generally confined to the Emperor's immediate family, while extended relatives could receive the weaker Lesser Dragon or Dragon conditions when approved.[8]

#### Lesser Dragons and Ordinary Dragons
The Imperial lineage possesses a hierarchy encompassing Lesser Dragons, ordinary Dragons and complete [[True Dragons]]. Each classification represents a different level of racial development and completeness, with True Dragons possessing the highest natural foundation and the strongest compatibility with Reality.[8]
Lesser Dragons remain substantially inferior to complete True Dragons but can still possess extraordinary abilities compared with many other supernatural races. Ordinary Dragons stand above Lesser Dragons, while the difference between an ordinary Dragon and a True Dragon is described as exceptionally great, exceeding the familiar distinction between ordinary Vampires and Noble Vampires.[8]
The Emperor's lesser Draconic descendants are not necessarily equivalent to unrelated members of the ancient Dragon Race. They can retain characteristics originating from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s combined Draconic and Vampiric inheritance, making them especially powerful even when they have not attained complete True Dragonhood.[8]

#### The Case of Adam Tepes
[[Adam Tepes]] demonstrates that receiving an advanced Draconic lineage does not guarantee exceptional personal development. He succeeded in the household's trials and progressed from Lesser Dragon into ordinary Dragonhood, but remained far below the extraordinary potential associated with his new Race. His development was limited by insufficient motivation and the failure to cultivate his supernatural characteristics to their expected level.[8]
The example establishes that racial potential and actual combat power are separate. An individual may possess a formidable Bloodline without reaching the level normally expected from someone who has fully developed that inheritance. The same principle applies to complete Blood Dragons, whose extraordinary foundation does not eliminate differences in talent, training and practical experience.[3][8]

### Blood Dragonoids
Blood Dragonoids are incomplete Draconic beings created through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wider Blood Dragon lineage. They possess significant Dragon-related characteristics and supernatural potential but lack the complete foundation required for genuine True Dragonhood. Victor explicitly distinguished Dragonoids from full Dragons while transforming Luna, explaining that a Dragonoid does not possess a genuine True Dragon Soul or authentic Dragon Form.[7]
Although Dragonoids lack the instinctive understanding of the Draconic Language and Runes available to complete [[True Dragons]], they retain the potential to learn those abilities through study and development. Their reduced racial condition therefore does not make them ordinary mortals; they remain powerful supernatural beings possessing valuable characteristics derived from the Draconic lineage.[7]

### Racial Abilities
Blood Dragons possess the complete racial properties of their Draconic-Vampiric foundation, together with individual characteristics inherited through conversion or natural reproduction. The abilities listed below represent confirmed common properties, direct consequences of complete Dragonhood or explicitly demonstrated characteristics of the lineage. Individual feats must remain associated with the characters who actually performed them.

#### Physical and Spiritual Abilities
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics: Full Blood Dragons possess extraordinary strength, speed, durability, endurance and reflexes through their complete Draconic physiology, combined with compatible properties inherited from Noble Vampires.[4][3]
- Blood Dragon Physiology: Their bodies combine True Dragon and Noble Vampire characteristics into one coherent supernatural racial existence rather than two incompatible systems.[1][2][3]
- True Dragon-Level Spiritual Foundation: Full members possess the complete Draconic foundation distinguishing genuine [[True Dragons]] from lesser Draconic beings. Exceptional existing Souls can remain integrated rather than being replaced by a standardized Soul.[17][7]
- [[Dragon Heart]]: Complete Draconic physiology includes a supernatural Heart functioning as an Energy reactor capable of supporting the extraordinary requirements of the Dragon body.[10]
- [[Draconic Energy]] Manipulation: Blood Dragons naturally generate, circulate and manipulate Draconic Energy through their complete racial foundation.[10][18]
- Instinctive Energy Control: True Dragon physiology allows direct manipulation of ordinary supernatural Energy without requiring elaborate Runic constructions for every basic application.[18]
- Enhanced Durability: Complete Dragon bodies possess extraordinary biological resilience, while individual protection may be strengthened through additional supernatural abilities.[4]
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Blood Dragons retain regenerative characteristics associated with their Noble Vampire foundation while benefiting from the durability of complete Draconic physiology. The actual extent varies between individuals.[4][3]
- Enhanced Senses: Blood Dragons inherit the extraordinary sensory capabilities associated with complete Dragon physiology.[10]
- True Dragon Transformation: Full Blood Dragons possess genuine Dragon Forms corresponding to their complete Draconic existence.[7]
- Shapeshifting: Their Draconic foundation permits humanoid manifestations and the expression of physical characteristics associated with Dragons or compatible former racial traits.[4]
- Natural Weaponry: Dragon bodies can possess scales, claws, fangs, horns, wings and tails capable of serving offensive or defensive functions according to the individual's form.[4]
- Dragon Breath: Complete Draconic physiology permits the release of concentrated supernatural Energy through Breath attacks. Additional elements and specialized effects depend upon the individual.[19][6]
- Flight: Draconic bodies possess natural aerial movement and can develop other supernatural methods of travel according to personal powers and transformation.[4]
- Longevity: Blood Dragons inherit the extraordinarily long developmental scale associated with True Dragons, although individual maturation may be altered by Progenitor, Divine and other exceptional characteristics.[30]
- Energy Sustenance: Their complete True Dragon foundation provides the capacity to sustain the body through the Energy of Nature rather than depending exclusively upon conventional biological nutrition.[31]

#### Draconic and Reality-Related Abilities
- Draconic Language: Full Blood Dragons inherit the natural compatibility with the ancient supernatural language of the Dragon Race.[22][3]
- Speech of Power: Draconic commands can impose supernatural effects upon the surrounding world through the interaction of Energy, Voice and Intention.[22]
- Draconic Runecraft: Blood Dragons possess an instinctive affinity for Draconic Runes, with even comparatively untalented members capable of using the foundation required for basic Rune creation.[3]
- Creation Interaction: Their True Dragon foundation grants an exceptional natural relationship with Creation, allowing interaction with supernatural structures through Energy and Runes.[3][18]
- Reality Compatibility: Complete True Dragonhood provides a profound natural harmony with Reality, supporting interaction with supernatural systems beyond the restrictions encountered by many ordinary races.[24][8]
- Runic Reality Manipulation: Skilled Blood Dragons can employ Draconic Runes to impose instructions affecting the surrounding world. This is dependent upon individual mastery rather than automatically providing unrestricted control.[3][18]
- Conceptual Interaction Through Runes: Advanced Draconic Runes can be used to impose conceptual instructions or alter the conditions governing particular phenomena, provided the practitioner possesses the necessary understanding and Energy.[18]
- Environmental Adaptation: Draconic development can be shaped by surrounding supernatural Energy and conditions, as demonstrated by Zaladrac's development within Hell.[10]

#### Inheritance and Adaptive Abilities
- Power Preservation: Blood Dragon conversion can preserve compatible supernatural characteristics belonging to the recipient's previous Race, Bloodline or spiritual existence.[4]
- Power Assimilation Through Conversion: Existing abilities can become incorporated into the complete Draconic foundation rather than operating as separate and potentially incompatible systems.[4][6]
- Adaptive Evolution: Preserved powers may become stronger or develop new Draconic expressions after being integrated into the Blood Dragon body.[6]
- Hybridization: The lineage can incorporate sufficiently powerful supernatural inheritance from another parent without necessarily losing its complete Draconic foundation.[21]
- Dominant Draconic Inheritance: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s mature lineage possesses highly predatory reproductive characteristics that preserve the True Dragon foundation within direct descendants.[24]
- Soul-Based Reproduction: Full Blood Dragon reproduction may involve the spiritual mechanisms associated with [[True Dragons]], in which the Souls of the parents participate directly in creating the offspring's Soul.[16]
- Ancestral and Inherited Knowledge: Direct descendants of Victor can inherit basic knowledge and dormant information concerning supernatural techniques through his accumulated Draconic legacy.[29]
- Racial Individualization: Each member can express different Dragon Forms, Elements and inherited abilities according to their personal Essence, Soul, Bloodline and development.[4][6]

#### Individualized Powers
The Blood Dragon Race can accommodate numerous additional powers, but their presence in one member does not establish that every member possesses them. Examples include Eleonor's Corruptive Fire, Jeanne's Holy Light and Wind, Morgana's Dream and Radiation characteristics, specialized Scarlett and Fulger powers, and the various Divine Concepts inherited by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters. These abilities belong to the individuals possessing them and should be documented on their respective pages.[17][6][21]
Similarly, the exceptionally powerful Dragon forms associated with Victor's later cosmic development do not automatically establish that every Blood Dragon can grow to Galactic size, devour other Beings, manipulate every Divine Concept or exercise the same powers as an Outer God.

### Resistances and Limitations
Blood Dragons possess an exceptionally advanced racial foundation, but the combination of True Dragon and Noble Vampire characteristics does not render every individual invulnerable. Their resistances depend upon their physical and spiritual development, additional supernatural powers and the nature of the attack encountered.
- Resistance to Physical Damage: Their complete Draconic bodies possess extraordinary biological durability, while the integrated Vampire component provides additional regenerative potential.[4]
- Resistance to Ordinary Environmental Hazards: The natural capabilities of complete Dragons allow them to operate under conditions that would threaten conventional biological organisms. The exact environmental limits depend upon the individual.[10]
- Compatibility with Multiple Energies: The Blood Dragon foundation can incorporate different supernatural characteristics without necessarily producing the incompatibilities expected in weaker races.[4]
- Limitations of Regeneration: The combination of durability and healing does not demonstrate unlimited recovery from every kind of physical, spiritual or conceptual attack.[13][3]
- Vulnerability to Anti-Dragon Effects: Complete Blood Dragons remain genuine Draconic Beings and may be affected by sufficiently powerful weapons, Runes and supernatural effects specifically designed to harm Dragons.[13]
- Individual Talent: Blood Dragons do not possess identical intelligence, martial skill, Runic aptitude or Divine affinity. A superior racial foundation does not remove the possibility of mediocrity in particular disciplines.[3]
- Complex Runic Requirements: Advanced Draconic Runes continue requiring Energy, knowledge, study and suitable intention. Natural affinity does not guarantee the ability to construct every possible supernatural effect.[3]
- Reproductive Demands: Producing powerful Blood Dragon offspring can require enormous quantities of Energy and impose considerable strain upon the mother, particularly when multiple children develop simultaneously.[16][9]
- Developmental Requirements: Complete Draconic potential does not automatically mature or manifest at its maximum level. Individual growth may require training, experience and extended periods of development.[30][18]
- Conversion Complexity: Transforming a Being with complicated Divinities, Souls or supernatural systems requires the conversion to accommodate those structures rather than simply overwriting them.[5]
- Godhood Is Separate: A Blood Dragon does not automatically possess genuine Divinity or the spiritual condition of a God merely because of their Race.[20]
- Primogenitor Authority Is Not Universal: The ability to create the Race, reconstruct other Beings and perform [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s extraordinary forms of predation belongs to the Primogenitor and his personal development, not automatically to every descendant.[2][12]

### History

#### King Among Kings
The foundations of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s eventual Blood Dragon existence emerged through his relationship with Zaladrac. His body gradually acquired Dragon characteristics, including enhanced senses, specialized internal structures and supernatural Fire influenced by Zaladrac's childhood in Hell. Despite these changes, Victor remained spiritually incomplete as a Dragon because his Soul did not yet possess the foundation defining genuine True Dragonhood.[10]
During the conflict surrounding [[Olympus]], the difference between his Vampire and Draconic characteristics gradually disappeared. His body was repeatedly destroyed and reconstructed, and the developing Dragon foundation eventually merged completely with his Vampire Progenitor existence. The decisive transformation occurred when he consumed [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]], releasing an extraordinary surge of supernatural power that announced the birth of a new Progenitor.[11][1]
The surrounding Primordial authorities recognized the event as the emergence of a legitimate new Race. Victor ceased occupying the same racial position as Vlad's Vampire lineage and became the Primogenitor of the Draconic Vampires, establishing the foundation of the Blood Dragon Race.[2]
The new lineage began expanding soon afterward. [[Metis]] was reconstructed through extraordinary circumstances involving Victor's internal world and became a Draconic Goddess whose Soul and appearance displayed characteristics connected to him. [[Scathach]] subsequently underwent transformation through Victor's Essence, developing a complete Draconic existence while preserving the martial characteristics and supernatural affinities that had defined her previous life.[32][25]
Additional transformations followed, including Jeanne's acquisition of a Draconic body that gradually integrated with her extraordinary pre-existing Soul. Her resulting abilities included Holy Light, Wind and returning characteristics associated with her spiritual existence, demonstrating that the new Race could accommodate exceptional Souls rather than requiring every member to possess exactly the same spiritual structure.[17]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Following his rise as the Dragon God of [[Chaos]], [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] decided to expand his Draconic lineage among members of his family who wished to become Dragons. He refused to make the transformation mandatory, allowing individuals such as Haruna to preserve their original racial identities while offering others the opportunity to acquire complete Dragonhood through his Essence.[14]
The mass transformation of Chapters 917–919 demonstrated a more advanced method of racial reconstruction. Victor's newly acquired Divinities influenced the process, producing enormous Dragon eggs around several recipients while their bodies were rebuilt from the ground up. The different appearances of these eggs reflected the individualized nature of the resulting Dragons rather than a standardized method of producing identical members.[15][4][5]
The results were particularly important for established Vampire Bloodlines. The Fulger family's Lightning remained significant, while Eleonor retained her abilities associated with Space, Earth and Corruptive Fire. Other women similarly discovered that Dragonhood did not erase their existing supernatural characteristics, but could elevate and reconstruct them into stronger Draconic expressions.[6]
The development of these abilities continued through training and experimentation. The Draconic Language and Runes became important subjects of study, while Anna demonstrated unusually powerful effects related to the Race's natural interaction with Creation. The emergence of these powers established the extraordinary potential of the Blood Dragons without proving that every individual possessed Anna's particular degree of Reality manipulation.[23]
The creation of Blood Dragonoids further expanded the Empire's Draconic taxonomy. Victor demonstrated that he could produce powerful Dragon-derived beings possessing a substantial portion of Draconic characteristics without granting the complete Soul and Dragon Form associated with True Dragonhood. This distinction allowed the Empire to develop subordinate lineages without automatically multiplying the number of complete Blood Dragons.[7]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
The expansion of the Imperial Family introduced the first major generation of natural-born descendants carrying [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s mature Blood Dragon inheritance. Their powers reflected the interaction between their father's extremely dominant Draconic foundation and the racial, spiritual or Divine characteristics of their mothers. The resulting generation demonstrated enormous diversity, with different daughters possessing individualized abilities rather than identical copies of Victor's powers.[27][21]
The same period clarified several limitations associated with Dragonhood. Victor's family discussed the enormous developmental timescale of ordinary Dragons, the spiritual requirements of natural reproduction and the difference between complete True Dragonhood and weaker Draconic conditions. Their experience demonstrated that Victor's Progenitor status and extraordinary resources could overcome many natural limitations without making every Blood Dragon's growth identical.[30][16]
Victor later explained the natural Runic compatibility of his Race while teaching [[Dún Scáith|Dun Scaith]]. He emphasized that even comparatively untalented Blood Dragons possessed advantages unavailable to ordinary supernatural beings, but that advanced Runic skill, Divine affinity and personal development could vary greatly from one member to another.[3]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
By the later Imperial Era, [[True Dragons]] had become a defining characteristic of the Emperor's immediate family. Velnorah estimated that approximately forty to fifty existed throughout the Sector, while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] deliberately restricted further expansion after receiving warnings from Primordial authorities concerning the consequences of rapidly multiplying such powerful Beings. The household's limited reproductive growth was therefore partly strategic rather than evidence of an inherent inability to produce multiple children.[9]
The direct descendants of Victor's mature Bloodline also inherited increasingly aggressive Draconic characteristics. He explained that his genes were too predatory to permit the birth of an inferior non-Dragon child under the conditions of his established lineage. Maternal powers could still survive and develop, but the foundation of his direct descendants remained complete Draconic existence.[24]
The Empire's broader Draconic hierarchy was subsequently clarified through the distinction between Lesser Dragons, ordinary Dragons and True Dragons. The highest condition remained restricted to the Emperor's immediate family, while extended relatives could acquire weaker forms of the lineage after passing demanding trials. The example of [[Adam Tepes]] demonstrated that entering a powerful Draconic lineage did not eliminate the need for personal motivation and training.[8]
[[Scathach]]'s training of Albedo further emphasized the practical fundamentals of True Dragonhood. Despite possessing an advanced magical foundation, Albedo relied excessively upon complex spells and Runes. Scathach explained that the Race naturally possessed the ability to command ordinary Energy without using Runic constructions as a substitute for every basic action. Advanced Runes were intended for imposing deeper changes upon Reality, not compensating for inadequate mastery of the user's own Draconic biology.[18]
The birth of [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]] revealed additional characteristics of Victor's established Draconic legacy. His daughters inherited dormant knowledge and techniques through his lineage, while Moira's exceptionally rapid gestation demonstrated how individual supernatural inheritance could alter ordinary developmental expectations. Her extraordinary Luck-related abilities nevertheless remained personal and were not established as common powers belonging to every Blood Dragon.[29]
Victor later distinguished his racial strength from the separate Authority associated with Negativity. He explained that even without the conceptual advantages provided by that power, his foundation as the Blood Dragon Primogenitor, his martial skill, his Eldritch development and his mastery of accumulated powers remained fundamental to his strength. His explanation also clarified that his predatory assimilation originated from his earlier Vampire nature and had become increasingly powerful through evolution.[12]
The Imperial military continued distinguishing complete True Dragons from ordinary Dragons belonging to Victor's broader lineage. During the mobilization preceding the war against the Upper Sectors, the Emperor retained his Dragons and Leviathans as powerful strategic assets, while the Dragons intended for later deployment were specifically distinguished from the considerably rarer True Dragons. Their possession of the Emperor's lineage nevertheless made them exceptional compared with unrelated ordinary Dragons.[33]

#### Natural-Born Imperial Generation
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later daughters were born directly into his mature Blood Dragon lineage rather than being converted from another Race. Their supernatural characteristics reflect the combination of their father's dominant Draconic foundation and the abilities inherited from their mothers. Individual daughters may also possess Divine, Progenitor or Eldritch characteristics unrelated to the basic racial abilities of Blood Dragons.[27][21][24]
The complete second-generation roster belongs on the Imperial children and individual character pages. Their inherited specialties, including powers associated with Reality, Space-Time, Destruction, Darkness, Lightning, Nature, Happiness or other Concepts, should be documented as individual characteristics rather than assumed to belong to every member of the Race.

### Power and Racial Potential
Blood Dragons possess an exceptionally high racial foundation, but their ultimate power cannot be represented accurately through one fixed combat classification. Their complete True Dragon characteristics provide enormous advantages over ordinary supernatural races, while their Noble Vampire inheritance and compatibility with additional powers can further increase their potential. Nevertheless, actual combat strength continues to depend upon the individual's development, experience, personal abilities and supernatural resources.[4][3]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly explained that even among superior races there could be individuals lacking exceptional talent. A Blood Dragon might possess relatively poor aptitude for advanced Draconic Runes while developing an extraordinary affinity toward a personal Divine Concept, whereas another might become a formidable martial artist without possessing equivalent proficiency in conceptual abilities. These differences demonstrate that the Race establishes potential without guaranteeing identical accomplishments.[3]
The Imperial Family provides numerous examples of this principle. Some daughters became extraordinarily accomplished with Runes, others developed unusual Divine affinities, and others possessed martial or supernatural talents differing considerably from those of their siblings. The shared Draconic foundation did not erase these individual differences, nor did it guarantee that every descendant would eventually reach the same level of power as the Primogenitor.[3][8]
The lower Draconic branches reinforce the same distinction. [[Adam Tepes]] possessed tremendous potential after progressing through the Imperial lineage, but his comparatively limited development demonstrated that even a very powerful inherited foundation requires training and motivation to produce its full practical advantages.[8]
Victor represents the extraordinary upper development of the Race rather than its ordinary standard. His personal evolution incorporates numerous powers gained through Godhood, Predation, Eldritch development, martial mastery and the assimilation of other supernatural existences. Those abilities must be assessed individually instead of simply attributing them to the Blood Dragon Race as a whole.[12]

### Limitations of Racial Attribution
Several powerful characteristics demonstrated by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] or other Blood Dragons should not be treated as universal racial abilities without direct evidence. These include complete control over Divinity, unrestricted Soul Manipulation, the creation of new races, the powers of the original [[Primordial Entities]], universal-scale Reality Manipulation, complete Conceptual Authority, the abilities of mature [[Outer Gods]] and Victor's personal mastery of Beginning or Negativity.[12]
The same restriction applies to specialized elemental and Bloodline abilities. Eleonor's Corruptive Fire, Jeanne's Holy Light, Morgana's Dream-related powers and the exceptional Divine abilities of Victor's daughters remain individual supernatural characteristics even though the Blood Dragon foundation can support their existence.[17][6][21]
The Race's extraordinary compatibility with Reality also does not mean that every member possesses unlimited Energy, immunity to every supernatural attack or instant mastery over Draconic Runes. Those characteristics must be demonstrated through individual feats rather than inferred from the general superiority of the species.[3][18]

### Trivia
- The Blood Dragon Race originated during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s transformation in Chapter 802 and was formally recognized through his emergence as the Primogenitor of the Draconic Vampires in Chapter 803.[1][2]
- The new Race resolved the anomaly surrounding Victor's previous position as a second Vampire Progenitor while Vlad remained the original Progenitor of Vampires.[2]
- Blood Dragons combine the complete characteristics of [[True Dragons]] and Noble Vampires into one coherent Race rather than existing as an incomplete hybrid.[3]
- The terms Draconic Vampire, Vampyric Dragon and True-Blood Dragon have been used in connection with Victor's racial foundation.[1][3]
- Victor can create additional members of the Race through his Essence, but he does not require every member of his family to accept the transformation.[14]
- Haruna rejected Dragonhood because she wished to preserve her Nine-Tailed Kitsune identity, while Mizuki was interested in acquiring Draconic characteristics without sacrificing her Onmyō abilities.[14]
- Approximately 1% of an individual's former racial Essence may survive a Progenitor conversion, allowing deeply established previous characteristics to remain incorporated into the new Race.[4]
- Victor's own Human racial remnant was eventually absorbed by his subsequent evolutions, but this result does not establish that every converted Blood Dragon loses all characteristics associated with their former Race.[4]
- The mass transformations of Chapters 917–919 produced enormous Dragon eggs whose appearances varied according to the individuals developing inside them.[15][5]
- Dragon eggs are not mandatory for every Blood Dragon conversion because several earlier members acquired complete Draconic characteristics without undergoing the same reconstruction process.[5]
- The transformation of a Divine Being requires special care because the recipient's Divinity is connected to their Soul and can interfere with the process if activated recklessly.[5]
- Jeanne retained her unusually powerful original Soul while developing a complete Draconic body, demonstrating that Blood Dragon conversion does not necessarily replace every exceptional spiritual characteristic.[17]
- Eleonor's Corruptive Fire became particularly dangerous after transformation because its effects could continue damaging the Soul of a target that survived the initial attack.[6]
- Blood Dragonoids lack both a genuine True Dragon Soul and an authentic Dragon Form, although they possess the potential to learn Draconic Runes and Language.[7]
- The complete True Dragon foundation grants an instinctive affinity with Draconic Runes. Even comparatively untalented Blood Dragons possess advantages allowing the creation of basic Runes.[3]
- Advanced Runic skill is not universal. Some of Victor's daughters possess extraordinary affinity with their Divine Concepts while remaining less accomplished with complicated Draconic Runes.[3]
- [[Draconic Energy]] control is a natural characteristic of complete True Dragon biology, while advanced Runecraft is especially valuable for imposing Concepts or deeper alterations upon Reality.[18]
- Blood Dragon conversion can preserve previous Bloodlines and allow them to develop stronger Draconic expressions rather than removing their original supernatural characteristics.[4][6]
- Blood Dragon descendants can inherit exceptional characteristics from another powerful Progenitor without automatically losing their dominant Draconic foundation.[21]
- Victor's mature Bloodline possesses unusually predatory inheritance, preventing direct descendants from developing into inferior non-Dragon conditions under the circumstances he described in Chapter 1168.[24]
- The ability to assimilate supernatural powers through predation is part of Victor's personal development and should not automatically be assigned in its entirety to ordinary Blood Dragons.[12]
- True Dragon reproduction depends upon the Souls of the parents, making sufficiently powerful spiritual foundations necessary for natural conception.[16]
- Blood Dragons are not inherently incapable of producing twins or multiple offspring. The extraordinary Energy requirements and Imperial restrictions explain much of the household's comparatively limited reproduction.[16][9]
- Natural-born Imperial Blood Dragons can inherit knowledge and dormant techniques from Victor's accumulated Draconic legacy, but still require personal development to make full use of that inheritance.[29]
- [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]] was born after approximately three hundred years of gestation, much sooner than Victor's earlier daughters. Her unusually rapid development was an exceptional personal characteristic rather than a universal change to Blood Dragon reproduction.[29]
- By Chapter 1164, approximately forty to fifty True Dragons were known throughout Victor's Sector. This number does not represent an exact Blood Dragon census because the original True Dragon Race existed independently of Victor.[9]
- Victor deliberately restricts the distribution of his Bloodline, partly because of warnings concerning the consequences of rapidly multiplying such powerful Beings.[9]
- The Imperial Draconic hierarchy includes Lesser Dragons, ordinary Dragons and complete True Dragons. Blood Dragonoids are another incomplete Draconic classification but have not been explicitly identified as equivalent to Lesser Dragons.[7][8]
- The wives of Victor established trials for extended relatives seeking entry into the Emperor's Draconic lineage, and complete True Dragonhood remained generally restricted to the immediate Imperial Family.[8]
- [[Adam Tepes]] advanced from Lesser Dragon to Dragon but remained comparatively underdeveloped despite his exceptional inherited potential.[8]
- Blood Dragonhood is not synonymous with Godhood. A Blood Dragon God possesses genuine Divinity as an additional supernatural characteristic.[20]
- Advanced Dragon Gods can possess internal structures composed of highly concentrated Energy, but this condition does not represent the minimum physiology of ordinary Blood Dragons.[20]
- The ordinary Imperial Dragons prepared for later military deployment are distinct from the restricted True Dragon population, although their descent from Victor makes them powerful members of the wider lineage.[33]
- The personal Divinities, Eldritch abilities and cosmic transformations demonstrated by Victor and his family must be distinguished from common Blood Dragon racial characteristics.[12]
