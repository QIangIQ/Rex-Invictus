Gods are emergent embodiments of physical or metaphysical qualities present within a universes. 
Their sentience arises from same latent pool of awareness as all other life. Manifesting as avatars that posses power and dominion over the aspects of existence they embody
it is nearly impossible to kill a god, while their specific personalities or awareness can be expunged, the qualities they embody can rarely be destroyed. Thus a new avatar will always be reborn in time, unless seized by the being that destroyed  their previous divine representative, or an appointed heir. Their main source of fuel is the belief of its denizens, who also assist in shaping its ideals and ego.

Gods, also known as Deities, Divine Beings and Higher Beings, are supernatural existences whose Souls possess genuine Divinity and establish metaphysical relationships with one or more Divine Concepts. Godhood is not simply a combat tier, a large reserve of [[Divine Energy]] or a religious title. Genuine ascension fundamentally restructures the Soul, creating a Divine existence capable of sustaining Divinity, Divine Energy, Conceptual Laws and the Authority required to interact with metaphysical principles of Reality.[1][2]

Godhood is best understood as a Divine state of existence rather than a single biological species. Conventional Gods are generally Higher-Level Spirits whose visible bodies are manifestations of their Souls, but Godhood can also be added to an already physical supernatural Race. Dragons, Vampires, [[Werewolves]] and other sufficiently developed Beings can attain Divinity without necessarily abandoning their previous physiology.[3] Victor Elderblood, for example, retained the foundations of his Blood Dragon, Vampire Progenitor, Demon King and other accumulated states when he became the Dragon God of [[Chaos]].[4][5]
The term God also covers an enormous range of power. A newly ascended deity, an ancient God-King, a First Generation Primordial God, a Dragon God and a highly trained Higher-Sector deity may all qualify as Gods while possessing radically different Souls, Concepts, Energy reserves, artifacts, racial advantages and combat experience. The novel repeatedly separates existential classification from battle capability, and even Beings without Divinity can surpass ordinary Gods if their Race or individual development is sufficiently extreme.[6]

### Nature of Godhood
The defining transformation of conventional Godhood occurs in the Soul. A Being does not become a true God simply by gathering enough Energy, receiving worship or becoming strong enough to defeat existing deities. Divine Ascension changes the metaphysical foundation that carries identity, power and Conceptual Authority.[2]
Chapter 687 describes the process as a form of rebirth. The traits accumulated throughout the previous existence are gathered, merged and reconstructed into a qualitatively different Soul capable of sustaining Divine structures.[2] This explains why ascended Gods often awaken Divinities strongly related to the lives they lived before ascension. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood, Family, [[Rex Invictus|Home]], Martial Honor and other Concepts did not appear arbitrarily; they crystallized characteristics and paths already embedded deeply within his existence.[4][5]

Godhood can coexist with pre-existing racial systems. [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]] retained her Werewolf nature after awakening as a Goddess connected to the entire Wolf Race and its subspecies.[8] Victor retained his complete Blood Dragon foundation after ascending, and Divine Beings later reconstructed into Dragons preserved their compatible Divinities rather than losing them.[4][3]

#### Higher Spiritual Existence
Conventional naturally born Gods are fundamentally high-order spiritual existences. [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]] explains that normal Gods are comparable to extremely elevated Spirits whose visible humanoid bodies are manifestations created through the Soul.[3] Their bodies are therefore substantially more malleable than those of ordinary physical species.
A conventional God can alter physical appearance, bodily proportions, size and sex with much greater ease because the manifested body follows the spiritual foundation.[3] The actual Divine Soul remains the important structure underneath the visible form.
Dragons are specifically contrasted with this physiology. A Dragon possesses an actual supernatural body and an exceptionally robust Soul. When an existing Goddess such as [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood becomes a Dragon, she discovers that the underlying Soul can no longer be reshaped as casually as when her existence was primarily spiritual.[3]

### Divine Soul
The Divine Soul is the core structure supporting conventional Godhood. Chapter 687 describes Divine Ascension as a complete metaphysical metamorphosis rather than an ordinary power-up.[2]

A Divine Soul can support Divinity, Conceptual Laws, [[Divine Energy]] and much larger spiritual structures than an ordinary Mortal Soul. [[Divine Souls]] nevertheless differ enormously in quality. A newly ascended Minor God and an ancient Primordial God are both Divine, but their Soul density, capacity, stability and natural Conceptual compatibility can be separated by massive qualitative gaps.[9]
The Soul also explains why the visible destruction of a God is not always permanent. If the Divine Soul and Divinity remain sufficiently intact, the God can potentially rebuild or remanifest their body. Conversely, direct attacks upon the Soul, Divinity or conceptual foundation can permanently kill a deity even when ordinary bodily destruction would not.[10][1]

#### Divinity as an Internal Structure
Divinity is integrated deeply into the Soul rather than functioning as a detachable external spell. Chapter 622 treats the destruction of Divinity as a direct threat to Divine existence, and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later demonstrates the principle with Ra. Instead of merely killing Ra, Victor attacks the ancient God's spiritual foundation and extracts the Sun Divinity from his Soul, leaving the former Sun God alive but reduced to a Mortal existence.[11]
Ra therefore demonstrates that death and loss of Godhood are not automatically identical. A Divine foundation can theoretically be removed while the remaining Being survives if the process is sufficiently precise.

#### Soul Quality and Other Races
Dragon Gods provide the clearest comparison between different Soul types. Velnorah explicitly states that Dragon Souls are more robust and less malleable than conventional [[Divine Souls]].[3] This increases resistance against Soul interference but also means Dragons do not possess the same effortless spiritual reshaping characteristic of normal Gods.
Other supernatural species can likewise bring pre-existing Soul characteristics into Godhood. Divine Ascension adds a Divine structure to what already exists rather than necessarily standardizing every deity into one identical spiritual species.

[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s early Divine development demonstrates the difference between possession and understanding particularly well. His Blood Divinity awakened at an extraordinarily advanced state because his entire supernatural life had revolved around Blood, Bloodlines and Vampirism. Beginning awakened with extremely little mastery despite its greater conceptual importance because Victor possessed much less understanding of what Beginning fundamentally represented.[4]
His initial attempts to develop Destruction produce the same lesson. Victor could already annihilate objects physically, yet that did not mean he understood the Concept itself. Divine progression required comprehending what Destruction was, what its purpose was and how it differed from simply inflicting damage.[12]

#### Conceptual Interpretation
A Divinity is shaped partly by the Being expressing it. Personality, experience, history, desires, worldview, specialization and comprehension determine how the Concept is manifested in practice.
Multiple Gods can therefore share War without becoming identical War Gods. [[Athena]] represented strategic and tactical War, while [[Ares]] represented a more violent and direct aspect. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s own War Divinity emerged from a life encompassing personal combat, large-scale warfare, rulership, conquest, defense and martial culture.[4][12]
The same principle applies to other Concepts. A Goddess of Beauty does not merely become physically attractive; Beauty becomes a metaphysical component of her identity. A God of [[Rex Invictus|Home]] does not merely own a house; the Conceptual meaning of sanctuary, belonging or household can become part of the Divine structure.

The author’s cosmology explanation places ordinary native Divinity inside a much larger conceptual structure. Divine Concepts do not exist as disconnected powers floating independently through Creation. Their ultimate metaphysical foundation descends from [[Primordial Chaos]], its divided aspects Positivity & Negativity, and the specialized [[Primordial Entities]] representing major functions of the Universe.[7]

The author gives several direct examples. Concepts associated with Life, Nature, Reincarnation and the maintenance of living existence are tied to Life. Concepts associated with Soul correspond to [[The Judges of The Abyss]]. Infinity governs Infinity and Space while sharing portions of Time-related structure with Death, whose sphere includes Death, End and terminal processes.[7]
Balance is unusual because Balance does not act as a large family of subsidiary Divine Concepts in quite the same way as Life or Death. If a God possesses a genuine Balance Divinity, however, that Concept still ultimately corresponds to the Primordial foundation represented by Limbo.[7]

#### Positivity and Negativity
The terms Positivity and Negativity do not mean simple moral good and evil. They describe complementary cosmological polarities. The mechanics explanation gives fear as an example of a Concept incompatible with Positivity and kindness as an example naturally compatible with the Positive side.[7]
Later chapters refine the idea further. Positivity and Negativity are present throughout Creation, but individual phenomena can lean much more strongly toward one side. Spiritual, emotional and abstract phenomena are heavily associated with Negativity, while material and concrete phenomena lean more strongly toward Positivity.[15][13]
This is important because the energetic or conceptual source underlying a Divinity can affect compatibility. A God of Fear and a God of Kindness can both be legitimate Gods while their Concepts naturally relate to different portions of the Creator-level Duality.

#### The Universe Nourishes Divine Concepts
Chapter 1186 adds another layer: the Universe itself nourishes Divine Concepts. Concepts exist throughout the Universe even when no current God is actively using them.[16]
Godhood therefore does not require a deity to invent a Concept from nothing. The Divine Being establishes a relationship with a metaphysical possibility already supported by Reality. The God’s individuality then influences how that Concept is interpreted, expressed and developed.

### Conceptual Hierarchy
Divine Concepts are not equal in scope or cosmological importance. Some describe extremely specific identities or properties; others govern enormous portions of Reality.
Chapter 885 explicitly distinguishes [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s more significant Divinities from smaller ones, noting that certain major Concepts can affect numerous lesser Divinities.[5] Chapter 1186 later describes Life, Death, Time, Space and Nature as examples of primary Concepts, contrasting them with much narrower Divine possibilities.[16]
Victor even theorizes that virtually every adjective might potentially correspond to some Divine Concept, pointing toward unusual examples such as his own Yandere Divinity and highly specialized Shinto deities.[16] This "every adjective" formulation remains Victor's theory rather than a confirmed exhaustive law, but the existence of extremely narrow Concepts is directly established.

#### Destruction and END
Later chapters clarify one particularly important distinction. Destruction can eliminate matter and structures while still leaving deeper Records, Space-Time context and the cosmic recycling system intact. END represents a more absolute terminal principle capable of ending an existence at a deeper level.[17]
The two Concepts are therefore related without being identical. The same principle applies broadly across the Divine system: Concepts that appear linguistically similar can occupy different metaphysical roles.

### Conceptual Competition Across Sectors
The wider Universe contains many Gods who can possess the same Divine Concept. Earlier encounters first revealed this when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] discovered distant Gods associated with Night and Darkness despite Nyx Elderblood never having treated them as ordinary local competitors.[18]
Chapter 1185 provides the later explanation. The Concept itself does not become a different Concept when a God enters another Sector. A God of Destruction remains connected to Destruction. What changes is the amount of influence that individual God possesses over the Concept because additional representatives are participating in the same metaphysical competition.[13]

Victor compares Higher-Sector conceptual competition to an immense tug-of-war. A God who monopolizes a Concept inside a small region may discover that their previous dominance means far less after entering a civilization containing hundreds of equally legitimate representatives.[13]
The user's personal strength, Conceptual comprehension and development determine how much influence they can exercise in this struggle.[13] The author's mechanics explanation adds Divine Artifacts and battle experience as additional factors in actual God-level warfare.[7]

#### Higher-Sector Training
Higher-Sector Gods also reveal the importance of structured Divine education. Earth's ancient Pantheons had often spent millennia as dominant local powers and therefore lacked pressure to refine combat systems continuously. More competitive civilizations developed better organized training, greater cooperation, more advanced supernatural technology and stronger practical understanding of Divine power.[18]
Age consequently does not guarantee superiority. An ancient God who stagnates can be surpassed by a younger deity whose civilization systematically trains Conceptual mastery and combat application.

### Origins of Godhood
Gods can arise through several different mechanisms. These paths lead into the same broad Divine class but can produce radically different Souls, innate potential and relationships with Concepts.

#### Natural Divine Birth
Gods can descend naturally from Divine lineages. Their children can inherit Divine potential, conceptual affinities and extremely high-quality Souls while remaining independent existences rather than exact copies of their parents.[19]
Inheritance can include parental Concepts, related Concepts or entirely new combinations shaped by the child's individual Soul. The Elderblood children later demonstrate this at a much higher level: some are born already carrying several Divine Concepts, while others develop unusual conceptual identities produced through the interaction of two extraordinarily powerful parents.
[[Ariella Victoria Elderblood]], for example, is born carrying all Seven Heavenly Virtues as Divine Concepts while also possessing True Dragon biology and traces of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Creator-related nature.[20]
[[Kassandra Kashvi Elderblood]] goes further and is born as an incarnation of Destruction itself, demonstrating that a Divine child can inherit such extreme conceptual compatibility that the normal distinction between possessing a Concept and embodying it begins collapsing from birth.[17]

#### Ascended Gods
Mortals and members of other supernatural [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] can become genuine Gods through Divine metamorphosis. This process requires far more than accumulating raw Energy. The Soul must possess enough development and Conceptual compatibility to survive becoming a Divine existence.[2]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ascension provides the clearest detailed example. After prolonged battle at the [[Edge of the Cosmos]], his existing life paths—Vampire, Progenitor, Dragon, Demon King, warrior, family head and ruler—began crystallizing into Divinities.[21][4]
His transformation produced fifteen Divinities. Ariel and [[Azrael]] note that ordinary Mortals successfully ascending into Godhood normally awaken only two or three Divinities, making Victor's simultaneous awakening an extreme anomaly even before accounting for the unusual importance of several Concepts involved.[5]

#### God-King Assisted Ascension
A God-King can assist another Being's path toward Godhood through a Spark of Divinity.[22] The spark does not instantly turn the recipient into a fully developed deity. It begins a process of Soul refinement and Conceptual development that can eventually culminate in genuine Divine metamorphosis.
Candidate → Spark of Divinity → Soul Refinement → Conceptual Development → Divine Metamorphosis → God

#### Reality-Assisted Exception
[[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]] demonstrates that conventional Divine rules can be bypassed by a Being with sufficiently abnormal Authority over Reality. In Chapter 1174 she desires the ability to shine, consumes a replicated star and develops a minor Solar Divinity within approximately two minutes. When she later decides she no longer wants the effect, the Divinity disappears as though it had never existed.[23]
The Gods witnessing this are shocked precisely because Divinity should not normally be acquired and discarded so casually. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explains that Moira's existence causes Reality itself to modify the conditions necessary to fulfill her genuine desires.[23] Her case therefore represents an anomaly rather than a standard path to Godhood.

### Faith and Divine Identity
Faith is a genuine supernatural force connected closely to Divine existence, but it is not the sole origin of every God. Many Gods exist independently of current mortal worship, and naturally born or ascended deities can possess Divinity without first being invented by religion.
Faith nevertheless has several important functions: it can allow Mortals to channel Divine power, strengthen religious systems, reinforce a deity's identity and eventually help cultivate entirely new Divinities.

#### Faith as Supernatural Power
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] first receives a clearer explanation of Faith in Chapter 153. [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]], priests and other religious practitioners can channel supernatural power associated with Divine Beings through conviction, prayer and sacred techniques.[24]
Chapter 156 reinforces that the visible ritual is not the true source. An experienced Faith user can act without an elaborate spoken prayer because the actual power originates from conviction rather than the external ceremony.[25]
This makes Faith more than worship in a sociological sense. It is an actual metaphysical system through which a Mortal can connect with higher Divine power.

#### Myth and Divine Identity
Chapter 703 explains another mechanism in which supernatural events become stories, stories become myths and repeated collective belief gives those myths increasing metaphysical definition.[26]
Supernatural Phenomenon → Story → Myth → Collective Faith → Defined Divine Identity
This does not mean all ancient Gods were fictional thoughtforms created entirely by mortals. Faith can act upon an already existing supernatural Being, strengthening or reshaping how their Divine identity is expressed.

#### Faith Can Cultivate New Divinity
By Chapter 1087, the mechanism is used deliberately inside the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Siena initially dismisses titles such as Goddess of Culture or Goddess of Art because those were not yet actual Divinities, but [[Aphrodite]] explains that continuous worship under a stable identity can eventually cause a compatible deity to awaken the corresponding Concept.[27]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] exploits this intentionally by shaping the public images of the women around him. A goddess repeatedly worshipped as a healer, artist, ruler or cultural icon can eventually accumulate enough Faith and conceptual association for the identity to become metaphysically real.[27]
FAITH AND GODHOOD
Faith can channel Divine power, reinforce an existing Divine identity and help create new Divinities without being the universal source from which all Gods originate.

### Borrowing Divine Power
Gods can allow non-Gods to draw upon Divine power without transforming the recipient into a deity. Early Faith techniques already establish this principle, but the [[Elderblood Empire]] later develops it into highly organized military systems.
Chapter 1164 shows two mature approaches. Fred uses Onmyō techniques capable of harnessing the power of Gods from different Pantheons when the relevant deity grants permission, while [[Andrew Walter]] channels power directly from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Pantheon through an Imperial Inquisition system requiring immense Faith.[28]
Pantheon membership matters. Andrew can draw upon Victor, Violet and other Gods belonging directly to Dragon's Nest, including Dragon Goddesses such as [[Amaterasu]] and Kali after their integration into the Pantheon. He cannot automatically borrow [[Shiva]]'s or Susanoo's powers merely because their Pantheons are politically subordinate to the Elderblood Empire; the technique still recognizes them as belonging to different Divine systems.[28]

#### Understanding Over Raw Energy
The most repeated principle of Divine development is that Energy cannot substitute completely for understanding. A God can possess enormous reserves and still use their Divinity poorly. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood and Beginning Divinities demonstrate the opposite ends of the scale immediately after ascension.[4]
The late Dragon God training of [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]] provides an even clearer demonstration. Albedo possessed enormous Energy and could cast spells capable of devastating planets, yet Victor and Anna considered her weak relative to what she should have been because she remained dependent upon old Human magician habits.[29][30]
Anna explains that a Dragon Goddess should use imagination and understanding rather than treating every effect as an external spell requiring complicated preparations. When Albedo tried to reproduce Life-like structures through elaborate magic, Anna demonstrated that a Divine Being could instead use a proper conceptual medium and manifest the relevant qualities much more directly.[30]

#### Lifestyle and Conceptual Development
Divinity can also stagnate when the God stops living in ways related to the Concept. Anna directly criticizes Albedo's isolation, explaining that her lack of engagement with the family and Empire had weakened the practical influence and development of her Concept.[30]
A Divine Concept therefore develops partly through experience. A War God who never encounters War, a ruler who never rules or a deity whose identity contradicts their own Concept can possess the Divinity while failing to refine it effectively.

### Divine Generations
Divine generation describes lineage and age of origin rather than an absolute combat ranking.

#### First Generation Primordial Gods
First Generation [[Primordial Gods]], also known as Original Primordial Gods, belong to the oldest foundational Divine generations of established Pantheons. Known examples include Nyx Elderblood, [[Gaia]] Elderblood, [[Erebus]], [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]], [[Ouranos]], [[Heavenly Father]], Ra and comparable ancient deities.
They generally possess exceptionally high-quality [[Divine Souls]], immense Energy reserves, ancient conceptual compatibility and important relationships with the construction or early history of their Pantheons. The author's mechanics explanation uses "older Primordial God" specifically to distinguish this Divine class from a true Primordial Being that exists as an Aspect of the Universe.[7]
Despite the title, these Gods are not automatically [[Primordial Entities]]. A First Generation Primordial God still possesses and wields a Divine Concept through a Divine Soul rather than necessarily becoming the universal Concept itself.[9]

#### Second Generation Primordial Gods
Second Generation [[Primordial Gods]] originate directly from, or through, the First Generation while retaining exceptionally high innate Divine quality. Examples include Kronus, [[Rhea]], [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood, Seth, [[Hypnos]] and [[Thanatos]].
Aphrodite represents an unusual case because she emerged from the remains of [[Ouranos]] instead of conventional Divine reproduction.[31]
Second Generation status provides exceptional potential but does not establish an unbreakable ceiling. A younger deity can surpass an older generation through superior Conceptual progress, better training, stronger artifacts, new racial transformations or greater combat experience.

#### Later Generations
Later Divine generations descend from older Gods or emerge after the foundational period of a Pantheon. [[Zeus]], [[Hades]], [[Poseidon]], [[Hera]], [[Demeter]] and [[Hestia]] represent familiar examples within the Greek structure.
As generations continue, ancestry becomes progressively less reliable as a practical measurement of strength. Higher-Sector civilizations demonstrate that disciplined younger Gods can surpass extremely old but complacent deities.[18]

### Pantheons
Gods traditionally organize into Pantheons, which function simultaneously as Divine civilizations, political institutions, religious systems and supernatural states. A Pantheon can include Gods, Demigods, Mythological Beasts, priests, Divine armies, mortal believers, artifacts, Underworlds, Faith networks and dedicated supernatural territories.

Pantheon affiliation is not biologically immutable. Gods can abandon one Pantheon, join another, overthrow a ruler, survive the destruction of their homeland or establish entirely new Divine structures.
Dragon's Nest provides one of the clearest examples of a modern Pantheon. Victor officially registers it with the greater System despite its population being deliberately multiracial rather than limited to one mythological culture or Divine species.[32] Dragons, Vampires, Demons, Angels, [[Werewolves]], Youkai, Humans and extraterrestrial species can all exist under the same broader political structure.

#### Pantheon Identity vs Political Subordination
The later [[Elderblood Empire]] demonstrates that political submission does not necessarily erase Pantheon identity. The Shinto, Hindu and Biblical Pantheons can be subordinate to the Emperor while remaining metaphysically distinguishable from Dragon's Nest.[28]
This distinction becomes directly relevant to Divine channeling. Andrew's Inquisition technique identifies Gods of the Emperor's own Pantheon differently from politically subordinate Gods belonging to another Divine structure.[28]

### God-Kings
Main article: [[God-Kings]]
God-King is primarily a title of sovereignty. The God-King serves as the highest recognized Divine ruler of a Pantheon and can possess political, religious and metaphysical authority over the civilization's Gods, Realms and institutions.
The title is separate from Divine generation. A later-generation God can become God-King, a Primordial God can become God-King and an ascended God can establish a completely new Pantheon.

### Dragon Gods
Dragon Gods are Dragons who possess genuine Godhood while retaining true Draconic bodies, Dragon Souls, Dragon Hearts, [[Draconic Energy]] and racial powers. They are not a separate Divine generation; they represent the fusion of Dragon existence and Godhood.[3]

Dragon Gods possess important advantages over ordinary Higher-Spirit Gods. Their physical bodies are genuine rather than spiritual manifestations, and their Dragon Souls are explicitly described as more robust and resistant to manipulation.[3]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became the Dragon God of [[Chaos]] while already being the Blood Dragon Primogenitor. His ascension awakened fifteen Divinities without erasing the Draconic, Vampiric, Progenitor or Demonic characteristics accumulated before Godhood.[4][5]
Existing Gods can also become Dragons. [[Aphrodite]] retains her Divinities after reconstruction into a Dragon Goddess, proving that compatible Divine structures can survive major racial metamorphosis.[3]

#### Dragon God Development
Later chapters make clear that possessing Dragon God physiology does not automatically make the individual skilled. [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]] possesses enough raw power to destroy planets but is criticized for continuing to fight like a fragile Human magician dependent upon range, preparation and external spell structures.[29][30]
Anna explains that Dragon Gods should use their own existence, imagination and understanding as conceptual media rather than relying constantly upon external crutches.[30] This reflects the extraordinary natural compatibility [[True Dragons]] possess with Reality.
Chapter 1185 later uses an ordinary high-ranking God versus Dragon God comparison to explain that these are not equivalent existential foundations. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] describes a sufficiently developed Dragon God as qualitatively superior because True Dragons possess much deeper compatibility with Reality, in addition to the Divine structures shared with ordinary Gods.[13]

### Primordial Gods
[[Primordial Gods]] are extremely high-order Gods distinguished by exceptionally powerful Souls, ancient lineage and unusually deep relationships with their Divinities. They remain fundamentally different from true [[Primordial Entities]].
A Primordial God still possesses and manipulates a Concept through Divinity. A completed Primordial Entity advances beyond ordinary Divine possession until the Soul and existence become the Concept itself.[9]

Kali demonstrates the transition most clearly. She was already a Primordial Goddess before her Soul began transforming toward the Concept of Destruction itself.[9][14] By Chapter 1096 she is explicitly described as a Quasi-Primordial Entity of the Universe.[35]
[[Nocturnus]] likewise demonstrates that a Second Generation Primordial God can possess only the potential to become a future Primordial Entity associated with the Void of Existence rather than already occupying that state.[36]

### Outer Gods
Main article: [[Outer Gods]]
Outer Gods are Divine or conceptual existences whose metaphysical foundations originate outside the ordinary native structure of the Universe. Their existence demonstrates that native Divinity is not the only possible conceptual power system.
A conventional native God connects to Concepts supported by the local Universe. An Outer God can instead derive conceptual authority from an Eldritch [[Chaos]] or another foreign cosmological foundation.[37][38]
[[Nero Elderblood]] develops the foreign Concept Apocalypse through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Nightmare/Eldritch foundation rather than relying solely upon native Creation.[37] [[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]] originally begins forming a native connection to Infinity before Victor replaces the developing source with his own Essence.[37]
[[Akasha Elderblood]] later states explicitly that she does not possess an ordinary native Divine Concept. Her Domain of Control comes through Victor, while her Eldritch nature simultaneously produces Loss of Control.[39]
Outer Godhood therefore represents a parallel conceptual system rather than merely the next numerical tier above normal Godhood.

### Elder Gods
Elder Gods are a distinct external group encountered during the conflict immediately preceding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ascension. Their armies and supernatural systems differed considerably from the familiar Pantheons of Earth, demonstrating that the wider Universe contains Divine civilizations that do not fit neatly into Earth's mythological categories.[40][41][42]
Known Elder Gods include [[Ikor-Kar-VI]], [[Var-Kar-VI]], Nur and Shur. Several eventually fight Victor at the [[Edge of the Cosmos]], where the unstable conditions of Space and Time allow the battle to continue across an effectively immeasurable subjective period.[43][21]
The term Elder God should not be treated as synonymous with Primordial God or Primordial Entity. It identifies a separate external class involved in the Elder God conflict.
Victor later demonstrates that biological or supernatural characteristics acquired from Elder Gods can be reproduced through his evolved body without replacing his own Blood Dragon core.[44]

### Divine Realms
Many Pantheons possess supernatural dimensions broadly described as Divine Realms. These are not merely palaces inhabited by Gods; they can function as entire metaphysical states containing Divine infrastructure, armies, temples, Faith systems, supernatural ecosystems, portals, [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] connections and Conceptual anchors.
Examples include [[Olympus|Mount Olympus]], [[Asgard]], [[Biblical Heaven]], [[Takamagahara]], Aaru and Svarga.
High-ranking Gods can develop profound relationships with their Realms. A Pantheon ruler may gain access to territorial barriers, accumulated power, Faith networks and local Authority unavailable outside the domain.
Ra benefits heavily from the Egyptian Divine structure while confronting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[34][11] [[Persephone Elderblood]] becomes dramatically more powerful after being formally recognized as Ruler of the Greek Underworld, with the Realm itself supporting her Authority to the point that she can expel Nyx from the territory.[45]
Kronus demonstrates an even deeper conceptual anchor. His permanent death causes forward temporal progression in Mount Olympus to stop because of his relationship with Time inside the local Divine system.[46] This does not erase Time throughout the entire Universe; it reveals how a high-order God can become foundational to a specific Divine Reality.

#### Pantheon Infrastructure
Divine Realms can themselves become strategic assets. During the fall of [[Olympus]], [[Zeus]] draws upon the Pantheon's Authority and even sacrifices [[Poseidon|the Master]] Bolt to obtain greater power, damaging the very infrastructure supporting the Greek Realm in the process.[47][48]
The [[Norse Pantheon]] later becomes sufficiently separable as territory and infrastructure that Hela can sell what remains after Ragnarök.[49] Pantheons therefore have both metaphysical and political existence.

### Divine Artifacts
Divine Artifacts are one of the major external factors affecting Divine combat. The author's mechanics explanation explicitly lists Divine Artifacts alongside Divinity, conceptual development and battle experience when discussing what determines the outcome of battles between Gods.[7]
A Divine Artifact can provide specialized abilities, amplify a Concept, improve durability, increase energetic output or give the user a tool capable of interacting with opponents their ordinary techniques might not affect efficiently.
The Greek [[Master Bolt]] is one of the most prominent examples. [[Zeus]] uses it as both a weapon and a component deeply tied to his Pantheon's authority, eventually sacrificing it to draw more deeply upon [[Olympus]] during the Greek conflict.[47][48]
The [[Hammer of Hephaestus]] demonstrates another aspect of Divine artifacts. Even substantially inferior copies retain enough supernatural value to function as powerful weapons, while reproducing even part of the original artifact's function is treated as remarkable by knowledgeable supernatural researchers.[52][53]
By the later story, Divine artifacts are no longer restricted to mythological swords, hammers or spears. Technological constructs such as [[Shiro-Hime]] demonstrate that sufficiently advanced civilizations can combine Divine principles with high technology and create strategic weapons that belong in the same broader category of high-order Divine equipment.[54][55]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] also crafts specialized Divine weapons for the Valkyries, tailoring individual artifacts to their fighting styles rather than treating artifacts as generic stat amplifiers.[56]

Combat between Gods is not decided by raw destructive capacity alone. The author explicitly identifies several variables that matter simultaneously: battle experience, the Divinity itself, the Concept's level of importance, how far the Divinity has been developed and the Divine Artifacts available to the user.[7]
The story adds several other practical variables repeatedly: Soul quality, racial physiology, Energy reserves, environmental support, Pantheon Domains, Conceptual counters and the number of competing users sharing the Concept.

### Skills and Abilities
The following terminology uses a Superpower Wiki-style organization. These abilities describe broad characteristics associated with Godhood; individual Gods possess additional powers derived from their personal Divinity, underlying Race, artifacts, training and Pantheon.

#### General God Powers
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics: Gods possess strength, speed, durability, stamina, reflexes and endurance vastly beyond ordinary Mortals and lower supernatural Beings. The exact scale differs enormously among individual Gods.
- Divine Physiology: Genuine Godhood reconstructs the Soul into a Divine existence capable of sustaining Divinity, [[Divine Energy]] and Conceptual Authority.[2]
- Spiritual Physiology: Conventional Gods are Higher-Level Spirits whose ordinary bodies are manifestations generated through their Souls.[3] Ascended physical species can preserve genuine racial bodies.
- Enhanced Soul: [[Divine Souls]] are qualitatively superior to Mortal Souls and can contain Divinity, enormous Energy reserves and complex Conceptual structures.[2][9]
- Agelessness: Gods do not normally suffer deterioration through Mortal aging and can remain active for immense periods.[57]
- [[Immortality]]: Destroying the manifested body of a conventional God does not necessarily cause permanent death while the Divine Soul remains intact.
- Resurrection: Gods whose Souls and Divine foundations survive can eventually remanifest after apparent death. Depending upon the damage, full restoration can require years or centuries.[10]
- Regeneration: Gods possess powerful supernatural recovery and can survive damage fatal to ordinary biological creatures. Soul destruction and Conceptual attacks can bypass or suppress this regeneration.
- Shapeshifting: Conventional spiritual Gods can alter their manifested forms because the visible body originates from the Soul.[3]
- Body Modification: Conventional Gods can alter size, sex, bodily proportions and appearance more freely than ordinary biological species.[3]
- Flight: Gods commonly possess supernatural Flight or Levitation independent of biological wings.
- Divine Energy Manipulation: Gods naturally manipulate Divine Energy for reinforcement, techniques, manifestations and supernatural effects.
- Aura: Gods can release overwhelming spiritual and Divine pressure. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ascension produced enough pressure to affect other high-level supernatural Beings without a deliberate attack.[4]
- Conceptual Manipulation: Through Divinity, Gods manipulate phenomena governed by metaphysical Concepts such as War, Beauty, Time, Destruction, Blood, Nature and Beginning.[12]
- Law Manipulation: Gods exercise Laws contained within their personal Concepts. This does not grant unrestricted authority over all Laws of Existence.[12]
- Conceptual Representation: Divinity integrates a Concept into Divine identity, making the deity a genuine representative or limited personification of the Concept.[9]
- Conceptual Awareness: Gods can perceive sufficiently large disturbances involving Concepts to which they possess relevant affinity or Authority. Creation-related Gods detected Victor's large-scale use of Creation when he established the World of Water.[58]
- Conceptual Resistance: Sufficient Authority can grant resistance against the same, subordinate or closely related Concepts. Relative Authority and understanding still determine the outcome.[59]
- Conceptual Empowerment: Circumstances strongly aligned with a Divinity can increase its practical effectiveness.
- Conceptual Suppression (Authority-dependent): A sufficiently superior representative can interfere with weaker users of the same Concept. [[Aphrodite]]'s dominance over Beauty demonstrates this principle.[12]
- Faith Empowerment: Worship and collective conceptual association can strengthen a Divine identity and eventually cultivate new Divinities in compatible Gods.[27]
- Power Bestowal: Gods can grant portions or expressions of their powers to other Beings.
- Divine Channeling: Gods can allow authorized practitioners to borrow Divine power through systems such as Faith or Onmyō techniques.[24][28]
- Blessing Inducement: Gods can grant Blessings corresponding to their Divine Concepts.[60][61]
- Curse Inducement: Gods possessing compatible Divinities can impose Divine curses.
- Extrasensory Perception: Gods can perceive Souls, Divine Energy, supernatural presences and Conceptual phenomena beyond normal physical senses.
- Resistance to Biological Manipulation: Conventional spiritual Gods do not rely entirely upon ordinary anatomy because their manifested bodies originate from their Souls.[3]
- Resistance to Age Manipulation: Their ageless Divine state makes ordinary biological aging effects far less relevant.
- Divinity-Specific Abilities: Individual Gods additionally possess powers based upon their Concepts, including:
- Time Manipulation: Kronus possessed deep Authority over Time within the Greek Divine system. His death stopped temporal progression inside [[Olympus|Mount Olympus]].[46]
- Destruction Manipulation: [[Kali Elderblood]] developed Destruction to such an extreme degree that the Concept could influence even apparently simple actions.[9]
- Existence Erasure (Concept-dependent): Kali's advanced Destruction could erase weaker targets rather than merely physically destroy them.[9]
- Creation: Victor used Creation-related Authority during the establishment of the World of Water.[58]
- Life Manipulation: Beginning and Creation-related powers can assist the establishment or manipulation of living systems.[58]
- War Manipulation: [[Athena]], [[Ares]] and Victor demonstrate different interpretations of War.[4][12]
- Love Manipulation / Emotion Manipulation / Desire Manipulation: Aphrodite Elderblood possesses Divine Authority over Love, Sexuality and closely associated emotional phenomena.
- Beauty Manipulation: Aphrodite possesses extremely high Authority over Beauty and can suppress weaker representatives.[12]
- Racial Authority: [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]] possesses Divine Authority over the Wolf Race and its related subspecies.[8]

#### God-King Powers
[[God-Kings]] possess ordinary Godhood together with advantages derived from Pantheon rulership.
- Divine Rulership: God-Kings command the political and Divine institutions of their Pantheon.
- Dimensional Lordship: God-Kings can possess control over the Divine Realm associated with their Pantheon.
- Domain Empowerment: Pantheon rulers can receive major advantages inside Realms metaphysically tied to their sovereignty.[34][11]
- Power Bestowal: God-Kings can implant Sparks of Divinity into compatible candidates.[22]
- Apotheosis Inducement: Divine Sparks and Pantheon mechanisms can cultivate suitable Beings toward full Godhood.
- Energy Bestowal: God-Kings can provide [[Divine Energy]] or Pantheon resources to subordinates.
- Authority Bestowal: Pantheon rulers can assign offices, territories and Divine responsibilities.
- Barrier Creation: Divine Realms can possess powerful barriers maintained through Pantheon Authority.
- Portal Creation / Dimensional Travel: Pantheons commonly maintain supernatural passages between Divine territories and other Realms.
- Faith Manipulation: God-Kings can influence large religious networks through temples, priesthoods and institutional worship.
- Resource Manipulation: Pantheon rulers control Divine artifacts, armies, materials, Faith and accumulated supernatural resources.

#### Dragon God Powers
Dragon Gods possess general God abilities together with genuine Dragon physiology.[3]
- Dragon God Physiology: Dragon Gods combine physical Dragon bodies, Dragon Souls, Dragon Hearts and [[Draconic Energy]] with Divinity and [[Divine Energy]].[3]
- Enhanced Supernatural Physical Characteristics: Their Dragon bodies provide extraordinary physical output and durability in addition to Divine reinforcement.
- Enhanced Soul: Dragon Souls are explicitly more robust and less malleable than conventional [[Divine Souls]].[3]
- Resistance to Soul Manipulation: Greater Soul stability increases resistance against spiritual alteration.[3]
- Resistance to Soul Destruction: Their robust Souls are significantly harder to destroy than those of ordinary spiritual Gods.
- Physical Body: Unlike conventional Higher-Spirit Gods, Dragon Gods possess genuine supernatural bodies.[3]
- Transformation / Metamorphosis: Dragon Gods can shift between humanoid and Draconic forms through racial Metamorphosis.
- Draconic Energy Manipulation: Dragon Gods retain Draconic Energy generated through the [[Dragon Heart]].[62]
- Divine Energy Manipulation: Dragon Gods simultaneously possess genuine Divine Energy.
- Dragon Heart: The Dragon Heart remains a central Energy source and processor after Divine Ascension.[62]
- Energy Amplification: Dragon bodies can contain and release enormous quantities of Energy.
- Energy Sustenance: Highly developed Dragons can sustain themselves through supernatural Energy.[63]
- Elemental Manipulation: Dragon Gods preserve personal elemental affinities.
- Breath Attack: Dragon Gods retain supernatural Draconic breath weapons.[64]
- Creation Affinity: [[True Dragons]] possess exceptionally high compatibility with Reality and the systems of Creation.[65]
- Power Integration: Dragon physiology can integrate compatible pre-existing supernatural powers rather than automatically replacing them.[65][66][67]
- Elemental Adaptation: Individual Dragons can preserve or develop different supernatural attributes based on prior existence and environment.[66][67][68]
- Draconic Language: Dragon Gods retain the Language of Dragons, whose words can carry supernatural meaning and [[Will Solace|Will]].[69][70]
- Draconic Runes: Dragons possess a Runic system derived from their language and exceptional compatibility with Reality.[70][71][72]
- Speech of Power: Draconic language can impose effects through meaning, Will and Energy.[70]
- Conceptual Manipulation: Dragon Gods possess normal Divine Concepts in addition to Dragon powers.
- Law Manipulation: Dragon Gods can combine Divine Laws with Draconic Runes and racial Reality compatibility.
- Divine-Draconic Power Combination: Divine Authority and Dragon physiology can function as one integrated combat system.[4][5]
- Imagination-Based Conceptual Application: Highly developed Dragon Gods can use their own existence and understanding as conceptual media rather than depending entirely upon external spells or structures.[30]
- Enhanced Conceptual Resistance: Divine resistance combines with the natural stability of a Dragon Soul.
- Enhanced Divine Durability: A real Dragon body provides an additional defensive foundation beyond a conventional spiritual manifestation.

#### Primordial God Powers
[[Primordial Gods]] possess the general abilities of Gods at a substantially higher qualitative level.
- Enhanced Divine Physiology: Primordial Gods possess exceptionally powerful [[Divine Souls]] and much greater innate supernatural potential.[9]
- Enhanced Supernatural Physical Characteristics: Their manifestations can sustain far greater force than those of ordinary Gods.
- Enhanced [[Immortality]]: Primordial Gods are exceptionally difficult to kill permanently but remain fundamentally killable.[46]
- Enhanced Regeneration: Their superior Divine Souls support recovery from catastrophic damage.
- Enhanced Energy Manipulation: Primordial Gods possess enormous [[Divine Energy]] reserves.
- Enhanced Conceptual Manipulation: Their Souls possess unusually deep relationships with their Divinities.[9]
- Enhanced Conceptual Representation: Their identity can become profoundly associated with their Concept without yet becoming a true Primordial Aspect.
- Conceptual Anchoring: Certain Primordial Gods can become major anchors for local Divine systems, as demonstrated by Kronus and Time in [[Olympus]].[46]
- Passive Conceptual Manipulation: Exceptionally developed Primordial Gods can manifest their Concept passively.
- Passive Destruction (Kali; exceptional): Kali's advanced Destruction can affect her surroundings through ordinary actions.[9]
- Existence Erasure (Concept-dependent): Sufficiently advanced destructive Divinity can erase targets beyond ordinary physical damage.[9]
- Enhanced Aura: Primordial Gods possess vastly greater Divine and spiritual pressure.
- Enhanced Resistance to Soul Manipulation: Their exceptional Souls provide stronger resistance against spiritual attacks.
- Enhanced Resistance to Conceptual Manipulation: Their deeper Authority makes weaker conceptual interference less effective.
- Reality Alteration (Concept-dependent): High-level Divine Authority can alter large-scale functions inside compatible Divine Realities.
- Environmental Manipulation (Concept-dependent): Their Divinity can reshape surrounding conditions according to the Concept.
- Conceptual Assimilation (Primordial transition): Primordial Gods capable of progressing toward true Primordial existence can begin merging their Souls with the Concept.[14]
- Law Embodiment (Primordial transition): At the highest stage, the distinction between the God and Concept begins disappearing.[9]
- Primordial Entity Potential: Exceptionally developed Primordial Gods can evolve toward genuine [[Primordial Entities]].[9][36]

### Immortality and Death
Gods are naturally difficult to kill because destruction of the visible body does not always eliminate the true Divine existence. For conventional spiritual Gods, the Soul is the primary foundation. If the Soul and Divinity survive, the deity can potentially remanifest.[10]
Permanent death can occur through Soul destruction, Divinity destruction, complete consumption of the Soul, sufficiently powerful Conceptual attacks or specialized God-killing weapons.[1]
The Greek war demonstrates the importance of these deeper attacks. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul-targeting abilities prevent Gods he kills from simply entering the temporary incapacitated states through which Divine Beings can normally recover, converting ordinary battlefield deaths into genuine permanent kills.[47]

#### Primordial Gods Can Die
Kronus is permanently killed despite being a Second Generation Primordial God.[46] His death proves directly that Primordial Godhood is not equivalent to the Conceptual immortality of a true Primordial Entity.
Kronus is exceptional because his death also disrupts Time inside [[Olympus|Mount Olympus]] due to his role as a local metaphysical anchor. Time throughout the wider Universe continues to exist.

#### Loss of Godhood
Ra demonstrates that a God can survive while losing Godhood. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] extracts the ancient Sun Divinity from his Soul and reconstructs the remaining Being as a Mortal.[11]
This separation shows that Divine existence contains several layers: the manifested body, Soul, Divinity and personal consciousness can all be affected differently.

### Weaknesses and Limitations
- Soul Damage: The Soul is the foundation of conventional Godhood, making direct spiritual attacks especially dangerous.
- Divinity Destruction: Removing or destroying Divinity can permanently cripple, kill or strip a deity of Godhood.[1][11]
- God-Killing Weapons: Specialized Divine artifacts can bypass normal Divine durability, immortality or regeneration.
- Incomplete Conceptual Mastery: [[Awakening]] a Divinity does not grant total understanding. Newly awakened Gods can possess only a fraction of the Concept's potential.[12]
- Conceptual Competition: Many Gods can share the same Concept, and stronger representatives can reduce a weaker user's relative influence.[13]
- Superior Authority: A God with much greater mastery of the same or a hierarchically superior Concept can resist or suppress weaker Divine effects.
- Conceptual Counters: Certain Concepts interact favorably against others. The outcome depends on importance, development, Authority and implementation rather than functioning as a simple elemental chart.
- Dependence on Understanding: Raw [[Divine Energy]] cannot replace comprehension. A deity with larger reserves can remain conceptually inferior.
- Cosmic Restrictions: Conventional Gods remain subject to higher cosmological structures such as the [[Regulators of Existence]], [[Primordial Entities]] and [[The Laws of Existence]].
- Restricted Concepts: Dangerous use of Time and other fundamental principles can destabilize Reality and attract intervention from higher authorities.[73]
- Sector Competition: A God dominating a Concept in one region can lose much of that relative supremacy in a Sector containing numerous stronger representatives.[13]
- Foreign Cosmological Systems: Native Divinity does not automatically grant perfect Authority over Eldritch or foreign systems originating outside the local Universe.[37][38]
- Complacency: Immense lifespans and local dominance can produce stagnation. Higher-Sector Gods demonstrate the advantage of continuous institutional training.[18]
- Poor Practical Application: Enormous Divine Energy does not compensate automatically for weak combat habits. Albedo's late training demonstrates that a powerful Dragon Goddess can still perform far beneath her theoretical potential.[29][30]

### Relationship with Mortals
Godhood does not inherently make a Being omniscient, morally superior or emotionally detached. Gods retain love, hatred, ambition, greed, jealousy, compassion, arrogance, fear, possessiveness, loyalty and personal bias.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] repeatedly rejects the idea that Divine status alone makes someone wiser than Mortals.[74] The histories of the Greek, Norse, Egyptian and other Pantheons repeatedly demonstrate that Gods can preserve psychological flaws for thousands of years.
Gods have interacted with mortal civilizations as rulers, protectors, teachers, conquerors, religious icons, judges, tyrants, benefactors and distant observers. The relationship depends upon the deity and civilization rather than upon Godhood itself.
Faith creates a two-way metaphysical relationship. Mortals can channel Divine power through belief and authorized systems, while large populations can reinforce the conceptual identity of the God they worship.[24][27][28]

### Notable Divine Archetypes

#### Creation Gods
Creation-related Gods include [[Victor Elderblood]], Heavenly Father, [[Ouranos]], [[Prometheus]], [[Izanagi]] and [[Izanami-no-Mikoto]]. Their interpretations and degrees of Authority differ enormously.
The Heavenly Father is particularly notable for exceptional efficiency in creating Life, impressing even Ariella before his comparison with Victor immediately triggers the latter's intense filial loyalty.[20]

#### Destruction Gods
Destruction-related Gods and Beings include [[Victor Elderblood]], [[Shiva]], [[Kali Elderblood]], [[Fenrir]], [[Typhon]], [[Jormungandr]], Nidhoggr and [[Apep]], although the exact Concepts possessed by these characters are not necessarily identical.
Kali possesses the most extensively demonstrated conventional development of the actual Divine Concept of Destruction, progressing until her Soul begins transforming toward true conceptual embodiment.[9][14]

#### War Gods
War has several valid interpretations. [[Athena]] embodies strategy and organized War, [[Ares]] represents a more violent expression and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] develops a much broader War Divinity through his history as warrior, ruler and conqueror.[4][12]

#### Racial Gods
Divinity can govern entire species rather than only elements, emotions or philosophical principles. Tasha's Divine Authority extends over the Wolf Race and its subspecies.[8]

### Godhood in the Wider Cosmos
Godhood exists throughout the Universe rather than being unique to Earth's mythological civilizations. Developed Sectors can possess their own Gods, Pantheons, Divine Realms, Faith systems, artifacts and highly organized Divine militaries.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s encounters with foreign civilizations demonstrate that Earth represents only a small and comparatively isolated Divine environment. Some other societies combine Divinity with advanced science, large-scale military coordination and conceptual education that Earth's Pantheons never developed.[18]
The later cosmology establishes several broader principles:
Principle
Meaning
The Universe nourishes Divine Concepts
Concepts can exist even when no current God uses them.[16]
Concepts have cosmological sources
Native Divinities ultimately exist within the structure descending from [[Primordial Chaos]], Positivity/Negativity and major Primordial functions.[7]
Concepts vary enormously in importance
Primary Concepts such as Life, Death, Time and Space possess far broader cosmological scope than highly specialized Divinities.[16]
Multiple Gods can share one Concept
Gods compete for relative influence rather than each Sector possessing completely unrelated versions of the same Concept.[13]
Combat depends upon several factors
Divinity, conceptual importance, advancement, battle experience and artifacts all matter.[7]
Native Divinity is not the only conceptual system
Eldritch and foreign cosmologies can produce conceptual powers outside ordinary native Divine rules.[37][38]
Godhood is below completed Primal Force
Ordinary Gods wield Concepts; a completed Primordial becomes the Concept's fundamental universal foundation.[13]
DIVINE PRINCIPLE
Godhood determines what kind of existence a Being has become; the actual strength of that God depends upon the quality and development of everything built upon that foundation.
Soul quality, Conceptual importance, understanding, Energy, Faith, artifacts, Pantheon infrastructure, combat skill, Race and external cosmological systems can all radically change the practical capabilities of two Beings who are both simply called “Gods.”
