A Vampire is an Elder, typically of the [[Next Generation]], belonging to one of the various vampire clans. Vampires are known in folklore as blood-drinkers, however this is only true of some vampiric clans. Other vampires draw sustenance from drinking auras, memories, or even feelings such as pain. Vampires are born immortal - resembling young adults despite a physical age often measured in millennia - and are extremely difficult to kill without special weaponry ([[Clarent]] and [[Excalibur]] are among the few weapons able to easily kill a vampire).

Vampires are an ancient supernatural race belonging to the Creatures of the Night, distinguished by their dependence on Blood, extremely powerful physical bodies, regeneration, biological immortality, heightened senses and emotions, supernatural racial techniques, and the ability to develop hereditary Bloodline powers. Although the majority of Vampires encountered throughout the story belong to the lineage governed by [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]], later revelations establish that Vlad is not the literal first Vampire to have ever existed. Ancient Vampire Bloodlines and Vampire Progenitors existed before him, with surviving examples such as the Asanbosam descending from a lineage that predates Vlad himself.[1]
The modern Vampires of Nightingale represent a diminished form of a considerably older species. According to Vlad, the original Vampires existed on another world and possessed an ancestral form powerful enough that other ancient Monsters collectively acted to cripple their race.[2] The modern [[Vampire Count Form]] is a partial return toward this original state rather than merely an artificial transformation; Victor later confirmed that the Count Form represents what Vampires originally looked like before their racial evolution became weakened by the environment in which later generations developed.[2][3][4]
Vampire society is extraordinarily diverse. The race contains living Noble Vampires, converted Plebian or Slave Vampires, ancient sirelines, racial mutations, natural Hybrids and several derived subspecies such as Ghouls, the Monster-influenced [[Adrasteia Clan]] and the Demonic Vampire lineage created through [[Morgana Elderblood]].[5][6] Victor Alucard later became the Second Vampire Progenitor and established another Vampire sireline before transcending Vampirism and becoming the Primogenitor of the [[Blood Dragons]].[7][8] Thousands of years later, Victor also created the first officially identified Daywalker, [[Frederick Winter]], a superior mutation of the Noble Vampire race specifically designed to overcome several weaknesses of modern Vampires.[9]

### Origin
The exact beginning of the Vampire species has not yet been completely revealed, but it is explicitly established that the dominant [[Nightingale]] lineage is not the beginning of Vampirism itself. Vlad stated that Vampires did not originally come from Earth and reminded Yama that countless worlds possess their own native supernatural histories. The original Vampire race existed thousands of years before many of the current Gods and possessed a much more complete ancestral physiology than modern Vampires.[2]
Vlad's title of First Vampire Progenitor should therefore be understood within its historical context. During a meeting involving the rediscovery of the South African Vampires, Vlad explained that "First Progenitor" was a title given to him by supernatural society rather than proof that he was literally the first Vampire Progenitor in all of history. According to Vlad, every age can produce a Progenitor of a race; earlier Vampire Progenitors existed before him, but their lineages failed to survive or establish civilizations comparable to the society Vlad later created.[1]
The survival of the Asanbosam provides direct evidence of this. [[Scathach]] identified them as descendants of an Ancient Vampire Bloodline that predates Vlad, having encountered the isolated group during her travels long before their later introduction into the wider supernatural community.[1] Other lost or obscure Bloodlines descended from ancient Vampire Progenitors likewise survived in small numbers, including the ancestry connected to Vlad's later [[Vampire Queen]].
Vlad's historical importance therefore comes not from being the literal biological first Vampire, but from being the Progenitor who successfully survived, expanded his Bloodline, founded an enduring civilization and became recognized by the wider supernatural world as the Vampire King.[1]

### Ancient Vampires
Ancient Vampires represent the ancestral state from which the diminished modern Vampire race descends. They should not be confused simply with an extremely old modern Vampire; "Ancient Vampire" in this context refers to the original racial physiology possessed by Vampires before the species lost part of its ancestral potential.[2][4]
Vlad revealed his true Ancient Vampire form during his battle against Yama. His body developed enormous wings made from Blood, grey skin, black sclera surrounding glowing crimson irises, sharpened ears, a mouth filled entirely with predatory teeth, monstrous claws on his hands and feet, and black tattoo-like patterns moving across his body.[2] Vlad described this appearance as the true form of Ancient Vampires and explained that a Vampire who completes the modern Vampire Count Transformation is only beginning to return toward this original racial state.[2]
The ancestral Vampires were considerably more powerful than their modern descendants. Vlad stated that their original power was feared by the strongest Monsters of the ancient era, eventually causing those beings to unite against the Vampire race and cripple its development.[2] The exact mechanism through which this racial decline occurred has not yet been fully explained.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later clarified the biological reason that the [[Vampire Count Form]] resembles the ancestral race. Modern Vampires experienced an evolutionary reduction caused by the environment in which their later generations developed, much like the modern Werewolf race. According to Victor, the Vampire Count Form is effectively what Vampires actually looked like in the past.[4]
This distinction is especially important when discussing [[Frederick Winter]]. Becoming a Daywalker did not automatically make Frederick an Ancient Vampire. Chapter 1117 explicitly distinguishes the two traits: Frederick was a Daywalker mutation, while Victor had additionally altered him so that his Vampire lineage possessed the Ancient Vampire state as well, making him an Ancient Daywalker Vampire.[4]

### Sirelines
A sireline is a Vampire lineage ultimately connected to a particular Progenitor or ancestral Bloodline. Most known Vampires descend directly or indirectly from the Blood of a Progenitor, although several independent lineages have existed throughout history. Membership in one sireline can affect a Vampire's racial qualities, potential, inherited abilities and relationship with the Progenitor at its source.
The influence of a Progenitor can be changed through sufficiently advanced Bloodline rituals. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] incorporated Kaguya Blank into his own lineage, he perceived the remaining influence of Vlad inside her existence, removed it and replaced it with his own Progenitor influence while integrating his Bloodline into her Soul.[10] Because Kaguya was already a Noble Vampire, Victor explained that his ritual differed from creating a Vampire from a non-Vampire and primarily altered the Bloodline foundation already present within her.[10]

#### Pre-Vlad Ancient Sirelines
Several Vampire Bloodlines existed before Vlad's rise. Most were destroyed, assimilated or became too small to significantly affect modern Vampire civilization.[1] The best-known surviving example is the Asanbosam lineage.

#### Asanbosam Sireline
The Asanbosam are an isolationist group of Noble Vampires inhabiting South Africa. They descend from an Ancient Vampire Bloodline older than Vlad rather than from Vlad's sireline.[1] Their survival demonstrates that ancient Vampire lineages can persist independently for thousands of years even after the rise of the dominant Romanian/Nightingale lineage.
Their society remained comparatively small because of Vampire reproductive limitations, with Vlad estimating their population at approximately one to two hundred individuals during the period in which they were rediscovered.[1] Bomani is one of their known leaders.

#### Vlad's Sireline
Vlad's lineage became the dominant Vampire population and forms the basis of [[Nightingale]]'s traditional Noble Vampire civilization. The [[Tepes Clan]], Royal Vampires and the majority of Nightingale's Noble Clans ultimately descend from this sireline.
The modern [[Vampire Count Form]] is explicitly associated with this Romanian/Nightingale Vampire lineage.[11][2] It should therefore not automatically be assumed that every unrelated pre-Vlad Vampire population possesses the exact same transformation system.

#### Oriental and Japanese Vampires
Independent Noble Vampire communities also existed throughout East Asia. Japanese Noble Vampire Clans existed for centuries before being largely destroyed by [[Mizuki Elderblood]] and other anti-Vampire forces.[12] Some Japanese Vampires treated Humans as livestock rather than participating in the more regulated Blood economy developed in [[Nightingale]].[13]
Japanese Noble Vampires possessed another unusual characteristic: they had learned methods of using Youki, an Energy normally associated with Youkai. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] discovered this during his activities in Japan and became sufficiently interested that he ordered the surviving Noble Vampires captured alive so their techniques could be studied.[14] Their scrolls contained Youki-related techniques, demonstrating that Vampire Bloodlines are capable of adapting to or learning supernatural systems beyond ordinary [[Vampiric Energy]].
This should not be generalized to all Vampires; Youki use was specifically associated with these Japanese Noble Vampires and their accumulated techniques.

#### Victor's Vampire Sireline
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became the Second Vampire Progenitor after his unusual transformation, development and relationship with his Bloodline reached the level required for an independent Progenitor lineage.[7] His existence did not replace Vlad; both Progenitors existed simultaneously.
Vampires directly incorporated into Victor's lineage possessed extremely high potential because Victor's Blood carried his own abnormal qualities, including the influence of his Human Potential, Night King Blood and several Noble Vampire Bloodlines. Existing Noble Vampires brought into his lineage generally had their pre-existing powers and potential strengthened rather than receiving an unrelated ability from nothing.[10][15]
Chapter 635 demonstrates this especially clearly with Sasha and Natashia. Their existing [[Fulger Bloodline]] became substantially stronger after interaction with Victor's lineage, while traits inherited from Sasha's paternal family—particularly enhanced perception of time—were also strengthened rather than erased.[15]
Victor later ceased being a Vampire after evolving into the Primogenitor of the [[Blood Dragons]], but his former Vampire Progenitor characteristics were incorporated into his new race rather than disappearing entirely.[8] The Vampires he previously created did not retroactively cease being Vampires simply because their Progenitor evolved.

### Classification

#### Noble Vampires
Noble Vampires are the true living and reproductive branch of the modern Vampire race. Their bodies are biologically alive, they possess functioning Souls and reproductive systems, and they can naturally produce children.[16][5] Noble children inherit Vampire physiology together with supernatural characteristics belonging to their parents' Bloodlines.
A Noble Vampire's inherited power is normally instinctive. Young Nobles naturally understand the basic method of activating their Bloodline ability, while training is required to regulate output, improve efficiency and develop advanced applications.[17] Powerful Noble families deliberately combine Bloodlines through marriage when two abilities possess useful synergy. The Fulger family, for example, deliberately introduced a minor time-perception Bloodline into Sasha's ancestry because accelerated perception worked exceptionally well with Lightning-enhanced speed.[15]
Noble Vampires are fundamentally distinct from converted Vampire Slaves. Chapter 342 explicitly describes Noble Vampires as a complete species capable of reproduction, while a conventional Slave Vampire is biologically dead and incapable of producing offspring.[5]

#### Plebian / Slave Vampires
Plebian Vampires, also called Vampire Slaves in several contexts, are normally Humans transformed by Noble Vampires through the traditional conversion system. Unlike true Noble Vampires, their core biological structure is not completely rewritten into that of the Noble species.[5]
The traditional conversion ritual was developed with the assistance of ancient Witches and uses Noble Vampire Blood, ritual components and [[Magic]] to control the process of transformation.[18][19] Ordinary uncontrolled conversion carries a substantial failure rate, while properly constructed rituals greatly improve the probability of success.[19]
Plebian Vampires are biologically dead, cannot reproduce and are closer to an undead state than true Nobles.[5] Their powers can develop according to the individual's personality rather than simply reproducing the hereditary Bloodline of the Vampire who converted them.[16]

#### Progenitor-created Noble Vampires
A Vampire Progenitor can perform a fundamentally deeper racial transformation than an ordinary Noble Vampire. Chapter 342 explains that only a Progenitor can completely rewrite another being's core genetic structure into that of a Noble Vampire.[5]
When a humanoid supernatural being is converted by a Vampire Progenitor, approximately 99.99% of their biological structure can be overwritten into Noble Vampire physiology while a minute portion of the previous race remains.[5] Those remaining traits may preserve parts of the individual's previous powers, appearance or nature.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s transformations later demonstrated that this retained racial information can evolve into entirely new Vampire Bloodlines or subspecies rather than remaining a simple remnant.[6]

#### Daywalkers
Daywalkers are a deliberately engineered superior mutation of Noble Vampires created by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] from a reconstructed version of his former Vampire Progenitor Blood. [[Frederick Winter]] became the first officially identified Daywalker in Chapter 956.[9]
Victor explicitly designed the Daywalker lineage to retain the advantages of Noble Vampire physiology while eliminating several common weaknesses. A Daywalker possesses all normal Noble Vampire characteristics, but has no weakness to sunlight, can consume ordinary food in addition to Blood, possesses stronger basic physical and racial abilities than an ordinary Noble Vampire, and receives a stronger version of the [[Vampire Count Form]].[9] Daywalkers still require Blood periodically to properly sustain their Vampire physiology.[9]
Victor created the lineage with the possibility that Frederick and his Noble Vampire partner could eventually establish an entirely new Vampire Clan descended from the mutation.[9]

#### Ghouls
Ghouls are a lower subspecies related to Vampires. Chapter 637 explicitly places Ghouls within the Vampire evolutionary family rather than treating them as an entirely unrelated species.[6] [[Maria Elderblood]] was originally Human, became a Ghoul, and was later transformed into a Noble Vampire by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Initially, her Soul retained approximately 1% Human information, 1% Ghoul information and 98% Noble Vampire information.[6]
Because Ghoul physiology is already closely related to Vampires, Maria's Ghoul characteristics integrated into her Noble Vampire existence particularly efficiently. Her Ghoul abilities eventually became a true Bloodline rather than remaining a weakened residual racial trait, allowing her to create and command Ghouls.[6]

#### Adrasteia Vampire Subspecies
The [[Adrasteia Clan]] represents another example of Vampire evolution producing a specialized subspecies. Their ancestors incorporated the characteristics of [[Nightingale]]'s Monsters into their Vampire lineage over generations, creating a stable Vampire-Monster Bloodline rather than remaining ordinary Nobles.[20][6]
Members possess abnormal physical characteristics, Monster-derived transformations and a specialized Energy capable of permanently killing otherwise immortal Monsters native to Nightingale.[20] Their lineage also possesses resistance or immunity to Monster flesh and Blood that would ordinarily be poisonous to conventional Vampires.[21][22]
The Adrasteia lineage demonstrates that Vampire biology is sufficiently adaptable for traits originating from another species to become fully integrated over long periods of Bloodline evolution.

#### Demonic Vampires
Demonic Vampires are a Noble Vampire subspecies created when Demonic characteristics become fully integrated into Vampire physiology. [[Morgana Elderblood]] is the principal demonstrated example.[6]
Morgana had originally been a Demon before becoming a Vampire. Rather than permanently remaining a conventional Noble Vampire with a small amount of weakened Demonic residue, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Bloodline caused her Vampire side to absorb and incorporate her Demonic nature, turning the previous racial characteristics into part of her Vampire existence.[6] Ruby and [[Scathach]] therefore classified Morgana not as a conventional Hybrid but as a new Noble Vampire subspecies created from Victor's Bloodline.[6]
This process parallels the long-term evolution of the [[Adrasteia Clan]], except Victor's Progenitor Blood reproduced the result within an extremely short period instead of requiring thousands of years of natural Bloodline development.[6]

### Hybrids
Vampires are biologically distinct from most supernatural races, but natural or artificial Hybrids can exist under exceptional circumstances. [[Nero Elderblood]] is one of the most important examples of a naturally viable Vampire-Werewolf Hybrid.[23]
A Hybrid should not automatically be treated as a Vampire subspecies. Unlike Demonic Vampires or the [[Adrasteia Bloodline]], in which foreign characteristics have been assimilated into a fundamentally Vampire racial Core, a true Hybrid maintains meaningful characteristics from two distinct species.

### Clan Initiation and Bloodline Integration
High-level Noble Vampire Clans possess Blood rituals capable of incorporating another Noble Vampire into the family's Bloodline. Such rituals can grant access to traits associated with the main Clan and deepen the individual's supernatural connection to it.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Progenitor version of this process demonstrates the principle at a much deeper level. When transforming Kaguya's sireline, Victor removed traces of Vlad's Progenitor influence and inserted his own Bloodline into her existence.[10] Because she was already a Noble Vampire, the process strengthened and altered the Vampire foundation already present rather than rebuilding her entire species from the beginning.
Victor later explained that when already-established Noble Vampires entered his lineage, his Blood tended to strengthen what they already possessed rather than randomly granting unrelated abilities.[15] This differed from Humans or other species directly transformed by him, whose racial reconstruction could produce completely new Bloodline characteristics.

### Biology

#### Living and Undead Vampires
Not every Vampire is undead. Noble Vampires are living beings, while conventional Vampire Slaves are biologically dead.[5] Noble Vampires possess reproductive systems, living Souls and functioning biological processes, whereas Plebian/Slave Vampires cannot naturally reproduce.[16][5]

#### Physical Appearance
Modern [[Nightingale]] Vampires generally possess pale skin, supernatural beauty, red eyes when using racial power or experiencing Bloodlust, retractable fangs and predatory physical characteristics. Transformation from Human to Vampire can significantly improve the individual's physical appearance and bodily condition; Frederick became visibly more attractive and pale after his Daywalker transformation, while his eyes changed to crimson as a consequence of entering [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Vampire lineage.[9]
The appearance of Ancient Vampires is substantially more monstrous and predatory, as demonstrated by Vlad's true form.[2]

#### Physical Ability
Vampire bodies possess extraordinary strength, speed, agility, reflexes, durability, stamina and resistance compared with ordinary Humans. Even newborn Vampires can display strength far beyond Human limits, while centuries of training and racial growth can increase these characteristics enormously.[25][26]
Physical ability varies according to age, Bloodline, Progenitor proximity, training and mutation. A Royal Vampire or Count-line descendant can possess dramatically better biological performance than a normal young Noble.

#### Regeneration
Regeneration is one of the race's most important natural advantages. Ordinary Noble Vampires can regenerate severe internal-organ damage in approximately thirty seconds to one minute, Count Clan descendants generally require around thirty to forty seconds, and newborn members of Vlad's Royal family can regenerate comparable injuries in roughly ten to fifteen seconds.[26] Exceptional Vampires can far exceed these averages; even before extensive development, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] regenerated comparable damage in under ten seconds, while [[Scathach]] could regenerate a severed arm almost instantly.[26]
Later chapters clarify the actual lethal condition for a Noble Vampire more precisely than the earlier idea that simply destroying the head is enough. A Noble Vampire can regenerate if either the brain or heart remains intact; permanent physical death requires both organs to be destroyed simultaneously or the regeneration to otherwise be completely overcome.[3]
Victor theorized that the heart acts as an important Energy center for mortal supernatural beings because transforming his heart during his Elemental Count Form caused a significant reduction in output. The novel explicitly presents this as Victor's theory rather than a confirmed universal law.[3]

#### Biological Immortality
Vampires possess biological immortality and do not die from ordinary aging after reaching maturity. This should not be confused with the higher metaphysical concept of True [[Immortality]]; Vampires can still be killed through sufficient physical, spiritual or conceptual destruction.

#### Heightened Emotions and Desires
Vampire transformation greatly amplifies existing emotions and suppressed desires. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] repeatedly describes Vampires as naturally selfish, possessive and driven beings, while newly transformed Vampires can lose control when previously suppressed urges become overwhelmingly intense.[27]
The transformations of Leon and Anna demonstrate this clearly. Victor warned that becoming a Noble Vampire would amplify underlying desires, and Leon temporarily lost control after his rebirth because impulses suppressed during Human life became far stronger once he became a Vampire.[28]

#### Bloodlust
Bloodlust is an instinctive Vampire hunger for Blood. Powerful Vampires can control it through discipline, feeding arrangements or specialized bonds, but prolonged deprivation can destabilize their emotional state. Noble Vampire society historically used Witch-created marriage rituals to redirect Bloodlust toward a chosen partner, reducing the need for indiscriminate feeding.[18]

#### Diet
Blood is the natural supernatural nourishment of conventional Vampires. Its quality matters, particularly to powerful Nobles whose bodies require more potent Blood. Daywalkers are a notable exception because [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] deliberately modified their physiology to allow ordinary food consumption while retaining a periodic Blood requirement.[9]
[[Nightingale]] developed a regulated Blood economy in which certain Humans voluntarily supplied Blood in return for long-term financial support. This differed dramatically from several Japanese Noble Vampire groups, who treated captive Humans more directly as livestock.[13]

### Powers and Abilities
Vampire racial abilities exist in a hierarchy. Plebeian Vampires possess the fundamental abilities of the Vampire Race but are incomplete undead Vampires created through transformation rituals. Noble Vampires are the complete living Vampire species and possess additional biological, Bloodline and evolutionary characteristics. Vampire Progenitors stand above both, possessing the racial characteristics of Vampires at vastly superior levels alongside their unique authority over Souls.[29][30][31]

#### Vampire Progenitors
- Vampire Progenitor Physiology: Vampire Progenitors represent the highest natural position within the Vampire hierarchy and possess the racial capabilities of Noble Vampires at a substantially greater level. Their bodies, Blood and Souls possess capabilities fundamentally beyond ordinary Vampires.[30][32]
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Progenitors possess supernatural strength, speed, agility, reflexes, endurance and durability vastly exceeding ordinary Vampires.
- Enhanced Senses: Their Vampiric senses operate at a correspondingly greater level, allowing extremely precise perception of their surroundings and Blood.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: They possess the extraordinary regeneration characteristic of Vampires at a greatly enhanced level.
- Natural Weaponry: They retain the retractable fangs and claws characteristic of Vampires.
- Night Vision: They retain perfect Vampiric vision in darkness.
- [[The Mist|Mist]] Mimicry — Fog Walking: Progenitors retain the ability to disperse or transform themselves into mist.
- Animal Morphing — Bat Transformation: They retain the ability to transform into bats or swarm-like forms.
- Hypnosis — Vampiric Charm: Progenitors possess Vampiric Charm at an enormously higher level than ordinary Vampires. The effectiveness remains dependent upon the difference between the Progenitor and the target.[33]
- Shapeshifting: The basic Vampiric ability to alter one's body becomes vastly more developed at Progenitor levels, with the known Progenitors displaying extremely fluid control over their physical forms.[34][35]
- [[Vampiric Energy]] Manipulation: Progenitors can generate and manipulate the supernatural Energy inherent to Vampires.
- Enhanced Soul: Vampire Progenitors naturally possess exceptionally large and robust Souls. Their Souls are designed to interact with, absorb and contain other Souls, requiring far greater spiritual capacity than an ordinary Vampire.[36]
- Large Soul Capacity: A Progenitor Soul can possess greater size than the Soul of an ordinary God, although a mortal Progenitor Soul does not automatically possess the same qualitative nature as a Divine Soul.[36]
- Inner World: Vampire Progenitors naturally develop an internal spiritual space used in the management of Souls contained within themselves.[36]
- Soul Manipulation: The defining racial ability of Vampire Progenitors is an extraordinary intuitive authority over Souls. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] describes Blood and Souls as fundamental to the existence of a Progenitor, while later directly stating that Vampire Progenitors can destroy and edit Souls but cannot independently create entirely new Souls.[30][31][37]
- Soul Perception: Progenitors can directly perceive and examine Souls and their condition.
- Soul Interaction: They can physically and supernaturally interact with otherwise incorporeal Souls.
- Soul Editing: Progenitors can modify the information contained within a Soul. Because the Soul functions as the record of the Being, sufficiently advanced editing can alter the individual on both spiritual and physical levels.[31][38]
- Soul Destruction: Vampire Progenitors can directly destroy Souls, potentially killing targets at a level beyond destruction of their physical bodies.[37]
- Soul Absorption: Progenitors can absorb the Souls of other beings into themselves. Both known Vampire Progenitors have demonstrated the ability to accumulate Souls belonging to defeated beings.[39][40][35]
- Soul Storage: Absorbed Souls can be preserved inside the Progenitor's own Soul/Inner World rather than being immediately destroyed.[36][39]
- Information Assimilation: Because Souls contain the records accumulated throughout a Being's existence, a sufficiently skilled Progenitor can acquire memories, experiences, knowledge and techniques from Souls they successfully absorb.[41][40]
- Biological Manipulation: Manipulating the information of a Soul allows an advanced Progenitor to alter the physical characteristics generated from that Soul. Victor eventually described this ability as being comparable to manipulating the "code" defining Life.[38]
- Species Transformation: At advanced mastery, Soul editing allows a Progenitor to alter a target's racial characteristics rather than merely their outward body.[30][38]
- Genetic Manipulation: When a Vampire Progenitor turns a compatible humanoid into a Vampire, the target's genetic foundation is rewritten to approximately 99.99% Noble Vampire, while approximately 0.01% of their previous racial information remains as a trace preserving aspects of their former existence.[30]
- Vampire Creation: Unlike ordinary Noble Vampires, who normally create Plebeians through the transformation ritual, a Vampire Progenitor can directly transform compatible humanoids into genuine Noble Vampires by rewriting their racial foundation.[30]
- Hybridization: Because the original racial trace is not completely erased, Progenitor transformations can preserve characteristics belonging to a target's former species, potentially producing Noble Vampires possessing traits inherited from a previous existence.[30]
- Divinity Preservation (Theoretical): Because a sufficiently powerful Vampire Progenitor can store the complete Soul of a God within their own Soul, [[Aphrodite]] theorized that this could prevent the God's Divinity from immediately returning to the greater cosmological system.[39]
- Divinity Transfer (Theoretical): Aphrodite further theorized that a Progenitor sufficiently powerful and skilled in Soul Manipulation could potentially transfer a stored God's Divinity to another Being. This remained a theoretical application when originally discussed.[39]
- Blood as a Metaphysical Medium: Blood possesses special importance to Vampire Progenitors beyond ordinary Vampiric feeding. Victor describes Blood as the "bargaining chip" through which Progenitors alter the relationship between body and Soul.[31]
- Blood-Based Transformation: Progenitor Blood can be used as the physical medium through which racial and spiritual transformations are performed.
- Racial Empowerment: Even a small quantity of Progenitor Blood can improve a Noble Vampire's racial quality and allow greater growth as a species.[38]
- Power Bestowal: Vampires who receive sufficient compatible Progenitor Blood can acquire enhancements and characteristics deriving from that Progenitor's lineage.[38]
- Accelerated Development: Progenitors undergo the age-based evolutionary growth possessed by Noble Vampires, but the benefits of each developmental threshold are considerably greater. An ordinary Progenitor who reaches the Old Vampire stage is already described as a monster by Vampire standards.[32]
- Enhanced Vampiric Charm: The known Vampire Progenitors possess Charm vastly beyond ordinary Noble Vampires, allowing them to overcome targets whose racial or supernatural resistance would normally protect them from lesser Vampiric hypnosis.[33]
- Ancestral Vampire Transformation: The highest known Vampire forms move progressively closer to the ancient/original state of the race. Vlad's True Form demonstrates a highly fluid, living Blood-like body capable of continuously changing shape while retaining combat functionality.[35] This should be treated as a demonstrated high-level Progenitor expression rather than assuming every hypothetical Progenitor must possess an identical appearance.

##### Known Night King Blood Traits
Both known Vampire Progenitors, [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] and [[Victor Elderblood]], possess Blood of the Night King. The novel specifically attributes several additional properties to this Blood rather than simply to generic Vampire physiology.[33]
- Accelerated Learning: The Blood substantially increases the rate at which the bearer learns and assimilates new skills and information.[33]
- Accelerated Development: The bearer grows and improves considerably more efficiently than ordinary Vampires.[33]
- Enhanced Adaptation: The Blood greatly improves the ability to adapt to new powers, techniques and circumstances.[33]
- Mental Resistance: The Blood provides exceptional resistance against mental interference and mind-related supernatural effects.[33]
- Enhanced Hypnosis: Vampiric Charm is greatly strengthened by the Blood of the Night King.[33]
These traits are demonstrated by the known Night Kings, but the novel generally associates them with [[Sanguis Noctis Regis]]. They should therefore be distinguished from the explicitly universal Progenitor ability of Soul Manipulation.

#### Noble Vampires
- Noble Vampire Physiology: Noble Vampires are the complete, naturally living form of the Vampire species. Unlike Plebeian Vampires, they possess genuine reproductive biology, hereditary Bloodlines and the ability to undergo the full racial development of the Noble Vampire species.[29][30]
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Noble Vampires possess supernatural strength, speed, agility, reflexes, endurance and durability vastly beyond Humans. Even young Noble Vampires can perform physical feats impossible for ordinary Humans.[42]
- Enhanced Senses: Their hearing, vision, smell and other senses are vastly superior to those of Humans, with Blood being particularly easy for Vampires to detect.[42][43]
- Accelerated Thought Process: Vampire physiology allows the brain to process sensory and combat information substantially faster than an ordinary Human.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Vampires possess exceptionally powerful regeneration, explicitly described as far superior to that of [[Werewolves]]. With sufficient age, strength and training, a Vampire can regenerate severe injuries such as lost limbs almost instantaneously.[44]
- Pain Tolerance: Although not automatically immune to pain, the Race's extreme regeneration allows trained Vampires to develop extraordinary tolerance to injuries as part of their fighting style.[44]
- Natural Weaponry: Vampires possess natural predatory weapons.
- Claw Retraction: Vampires can harden and extend their fingers into supernatural claws.[45]
- Fangs: Vampires possess retractable predatory fangs used for drinking Blood and combat.
- Night Vision: One of the explicitly identified basic Vampire abilities, allowing Vampires to see clearly in darkness.[45]
- [[The Mist|Mist]] Mimicry — Fog Walking: One of the basic racial abilities of Vampires. The Vampire can transform or disperse into mist, allowing them to obscure themselves and move in a less materially defined state.[45]
- Animal Morphing — Bat Transformation: Vampires can transform into bats or a swarm-like collection of bats. This is explicitly identified as one of the Race's basic abilities.[45]
- Hypnosis — Vampiric Charm: Vampires can use supernatural Charm to influence weaker targets. Effectiveness depends strongly upon relative strength, while certain races and sufficiently powerful individuals can resist ordinary Noble-level Charm.[46]
- Memory Manipulation (Advanced): At greater mastery, Vampiric mental influence can be used to interfere with the memories of substantially weaker targets, including suppressing or erasing memories.
- Shapeshifting: Vampires possess a natural ability to modify their bodies. Simple applications such as enlarging a limb or extending claws are described as things ordinary Vampires can learn, while more powerful Vampires can develop full-body transformations.[34]
- Intangibility — Density Shifting (Advanced): A developed Vampiric technique produced by combining principles of Fog Walking and Bat Transformation. It allows the user to reduce or alter the material solidity of their body sufficiently to pass through obstacles or attacks. Mastery requires training rather than being automatically perfected at birth.[47]
- [[Vampiric Energy]] Manipulation: Vampires possess their own form of supernatural Energy and can learn to generate, release and manipulate it. The amount, density and appearance vary between individuals.
- Levitation (Developed): Vampires who train their basic racial abilities sufficiently can suspend themselves in the air and glide without requiring physical wings.
- Blood Empowerment: Drinking Blood replenishes and strengthens a Vampire's physical condition and can accelerate recovery from injuries.
- Enhanced Emotions: Vampirism enormously amplifies the intensity of emotions and desires. Love, anger, jealousy, possessiveness and similar emotions can become much more difficult to suppress.[48]
- Bloodlust: Vampires possess a powerful instinctive desire for Blood. Uncontrolled Bloodlust can drive even otherwise intelligent Vampires into a berserk or irrational state.[49]
- Biological [[Immortality]]: Noble Vampires are living supernatural beings whose bodies do not undergo ordinary Human aging toward natural death. They can continue living indefinitely unless killed.[30]
- Reproduction: Unlike Plebeian Vampires, Noble Vampires can biologically reproduce and give birth to new Noble Vampires.[29][30]
- Hereditary Power Inheritance: Noble Vampires naturally inherit supernatural characteristics belonging to their parents and Bloodlines. A child inherits the characteristics of the Noble parent rather than developing an unrelated personal ability in the manner of a Plebeian Vampire.[29]
- Bloodline Ability: Noble Clans can develop distinctive supernatural powers that become hereditary, such as the [[Snow Clan]]'s Fire, [[Fulger Clan]]'s Lightning, [[Scarlett Clan]]'s Ice or Alioth Clan's Space-related abilities. The specific power is not a general Vampire ability; the general racial perk is the ability to inherit and transmit a Bloodline power.[29]
- Instinctive Ability Knowledge: Noble Vampires are born with an instinctive understanding of how their inherited Bloodline ability functions. Their principal early difficulty is controlling its output rather than discovering the ability from nothing.[45]
- Age Empowerment — Racial Metamorphosis: Noble Vampires undergo major developmental thresholds as they age. Training, accumulated experience and growth from the previous period are converted into a substantial increase in raw power when the next stage is reached.[32]
- Newborn Stage: Approximately birth to 500 years.[32]
- Adult Stage: Approximately 501 to 1,500 years.[32]
- Old Vampire Stage: Begins after approximately 1,500 years, with further development continuing afterward.[32]
- Experience Conversion: A Vampire who trains extensively before a developmental threshold receives a larger increase because accumulated experience contributes to the power obtained during the transition.[32]
- Vampire Count Transformation (Romanian/Nightingale Lineage): An advanced ancestral transformation explained primarily in relation to the Romanian Vampires of Vlad's lineage. It is theoretically accessible to them, although most Vampires never achieve the complete state. The transformation amplifies both ordinary racial powers and the user's individual/Bloodline ability.[50]
- Level 1 — Incomplete Transformation: The Vampire becomes covered by the manifestation of their primary power, their ears sharpen and their body changes slightly. An ordinary Noble Vampire receives roughly a twofold overall increase and can output considerably more of their primary ability without exhaustion.[50]
- Level 2 — Semi-Complete Transformation: The user becomes more monstrous and gains further enhancement to physical strength, toughness, speed, reflexes, racial abilities and control over their primary supernatural power.[50]
- Level 3 — Complete Transformation: The previous stages are fully integrated, substantially increasing physical attributes, regeneration, racial powers, supernatural output and control. Very few Noble Vampires naturally reach this state.[50]
- Ancestral Reversion: The completed Count Form is later explained as a Vampire moving closer to the ancient/original state of the Race rather than simply performing an arbitrary power-up transformation.[35]

#### Plebeian Vampires
- Plebeian Vampire Physiology: Plebeian Vampires are normally Humans transformed through the Vampire ritual rather than naturally born members of the complete Noble Vampire species. They retain the basic racial toolkit of Vampires but lack several of the biological and hereditary characteristics belonging to Noble Vampires.[29][30]
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Plebeian Vampires possess supernatural strength, speed, reflexes, agility, endurance and durability beyond normal Humans.
- Enhanced Senses: Their senses become far sharper than those of ordinary Humans, particularly their ability to detect Blood.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Plebeians possess the powerful regenerative capability characteristic of Vampires, although their overall racial quality is normally inferior to high-level Noble Vampires.
- Natural Weaponry: They possess Vampiric fangs and can produce supernatural claws.
- Night Vision: Plebeians possess the basic Vampiric ability to see clearly within darkness.[45]
- [[The Mist|Mist]] Mimicry — Fog Walking: Plebeians retain the basic racial ability to assume or move through a mist-like state.[45]
- Animal Morphing — Bat Transformation: Plebeians possess the basic racial ability to transform into bats or a bat-like swarm.[45]
- Hypnosis — Vampiric Charm: They possess the ordinary mental Charm characteristic of Vampires, although effectiveness depends heavily upon their strength relative to the target.
- Shapeshifting: Basic Vampiric alterations of the body can be developed through training.[34]
- Intangibility — Density Shifting (Advanced): By mastering and combining basic Vampire abilities, a sufficiently skilled Vampire can develop the ability to pass through solid obstacles or attacks.[47]
- [[Vampiric Energy]] Manipulation: Plebeians possess and can manipulate Vampiric Energy.
- Blood Empowerment: Drinking Blood restores and temporarily improves their physical condition and regenerative ability.
- Enhanced Emotions: Their emotions and desires are amplified through Vampirism.[48]
- Bloodlust: Plebeians possess the same fundamental hunger for Blood characteristic of the Race and can lose rational control when that hunger becomes sufficiently severe.[49]
- Undead Physiology: Unlike Noble Vampires, Plebeian/Slave Vampires are explicitly described as dead beings. Ruby compares them to Ghouls possessing considerably better control rather than a completely living species.[30]
- [[Immortality]]: Their undead state prevents conventional biological aging and natural Human death, although they can still be permanently killed through sufficient supernatural damage or Vampire weaknesses.
- Personalized Power Manifestation: Plebeian Vampires do not normally inherit the Bloodline power of the Noble Vampire who created them. Instead, the supernatural characteristics they develop are influenced by their own personality and individual nature.[29]
- Non-Reproductive Physiology: Plebeian Vampires cannot naturally reproduce or create biological offspring. This is one of the principal biological differences separating them from Noble Vampires.[29][30]
Note: The Noble Vampire 500-year racial metamorphosis is explicitly described in Chapter 361 as a characteristic of Noble Vampires and is separately stated to apply even more strongly to Progenitors. It should therefore not automatically be assigned to Plebeian Vampires without additional evidence.[32]

### Vampire Count Form
The [[Vampire Count Form]], or Vampire Count Transformation, is an advanced racial transformation explicitly associated with the Romanian/Nightingale Vampire lineage. Although the name originated from [[Vampire Counts]], possession of a Count title is not the true source of the transformation; Vampires of the lineage theoretically possess the potential to awaken it, with ordinary members generally requiring centuries of development.[11]
The Count Form is fundamentally a return toward the ancestral Vampire state. Vlad later revealed that the transformation represents talented modern Vampires recovering a portion of the Ancient Vampire form and moving one step closer to their original racial power.[2] [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] subsequently explained that activating the Count Form causes the Vampire's Soul to begin returning toward its ancestral state, while information describing the normal humanoid form is preserved within the Soul and restored once the transformation ends.[3]

#### Level 1
The first stage is an incomplete transformation. Physical features become more predatory, the Vampire's Bloodline ability spreads across the body and overall combat ability increases sharply. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s early Blood Count Form and Violet's Fire Count Form are examples.[11][51]

#### Level 2
The intermediate form pushes the Vampire farther toward the ancestral transformation, further amplifying physical ability, regeneration, Bloodline output and control. The exact physical appearance varies depending on the user's inherited power.

#### Complete Count Form
The complete form represents the closest state most modern Vampires achieve toward their Ancient Vampire ancestry. Physical strength, speed, reflexes, agility, durability, regeneration, racial abilities, Energy output and resistance all increase simultaneously.[2] The complete transformation is described as approximately quadrupling the Vampire's overall combat ability compared with the ordinary state.[2]

#### Perfect Elemental Count Form
Vampires possessing sufficiently developed Elemental Bloodlines can go farther and merge the transformed body directly with their Element. Chapter 254 explains that a perfected Elemental Count Form can bring the Vampire closer to an Elemental Spirit than an ordinary flesh-and-blood being.[24]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later developed this concept substantially further by combining Count Form power with Vampire shapeshifting. He converted almost his entire body into Fire while preserving his heart and brain, making conventional physical attacks largely useless against the transformed sections of his body.[3]
This application is an advanced technique created through mastery of the Count Form and should not be treated as something automatically possessed by every Vampire.

### Racial Creation
Ordinary Noble Vampires and Progenitors possess fundamentally different transformation authority.

#### Ordinary Noble Transformation
A conventional Noble Vampire can create Plebian/Slave Vampires through the traditional transformation system. The process normally requires Blood and a Witch-developed ritual if the creator wishes to maximize stability and success.[18][19] [[Scathach]] explained that simply attempting transformation without the proper process possesses a high failure rate, whereas the controlled ritual was developed to prevent uncontrolled Vampire population growth and conflict with Humans.[19]

#### Progenitor Transformation
A Vampire Progenitor can directly rewrite another humanoid being into the Noble Vampire species rather than merely producing a Slave Vampire.[5] The process replaces approximately 99.99% of the target's racial structure while retaining a minute amount of their original information.[5]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s transformations of Leon and Anna demonstrate the physical process: his Progenitor Blood and venom entered the target, killed the original Human state and rebuilt the individual as a Noble Vampire connected to Victor's lineage.[28]
The reborn Vampire's suppressed desires and personality remain present and can manifest more intensely because of Vampire emotional amplification.[28]

### Racial Evolution and Subspecies Formation
Vampire biology is unusually capable of assimilating useful characteristics from other species. Chapter 637 establishes that if foreign racial traits become fully integrated into the Vampire Core rather than remaining a separate competing lineage, a new Vampire subspecies or Bloodline can form.[6]
Maria and Morgana demonstrate two versions of this process. Maria's previous Ghoul physiology was closely enough related to Vampires that it ultimately merged into her Noble Vampire Bloodline, whereas Morgana's Demonic nature was forcibly incorporated by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood, producing a new Demonic Vampire subspecies.[6]
Ruby compared this process with the [[Adrasteia Clan]], whose ancestors achieved a similar result gradually through more than two thousand years of evolution and incorporation of Monster characteristics.[6]
The Vampire race should therefore be understood as highly adaptable rather than biologically static.

Vampire weaknesses differ substantially between lineages, age groups and mutations. Ancient Vampires, Daywalkers, powerful Elder Vampires and unusual Bloodlines can overcome weaknesses that remain lethal to ordinary young Vampires.

#### Noble Hierarchy
[[Nightingale]]'s Noble Vampire society historically functioned through aristocratic family status and personal power. Royal Vampires and Count families occupied the highest social positions, followed by other Noble houses and their subordinates. A Vampire's family Bloodline, age, strength and political connections often had as much significance as a formal title.
Women were not automatically required to abandon their surname after marriage, particularly when preserving an important Bloodline. A child could inherit the surname and lineage of whichever parent represented the politically or supernaturally more important family.[53]

#### Blood Economy
[[Nightingale]] developed systems allowing Humans to provide Blood without simply being treated as disposable livestock. Certain Human Blood suppliers were supported financially in exchange for maintaining specific conditions and donating Blood to Vampire society.[13]
This was not universal Vampire culture. Japanese Noble Vampire groups were shown maintaining considerably more exploitative systems in which Humans were treated as cattle.[13]

### Relationship with other races

#### Humans
Vampires historically preyed upon Humans and frequently maintained secrecy to prevent large-scale conflict. The relationship became increasingly regulated in [[Nightingale]] and later changed substantially as Humans and Vampires entered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s broader multi-racial civilization.

#### Werewolves
Vampires and [[Werewolves]] possess a long history of rivalry and warfare. Their natural combat profiles differ, with Werewolves emphasizing overwhelming physicality and pack-based power while Vampires rely heavily on regeneration, inherited Bloodlines and specialized techniques. Later alliances under [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and other rulers ended the idea that the two species are universally permanent enemies.

#### Witches
Vampires maintain an ancient professional relationship with Witches. Witches helped develop Vampire conversion rituals, marriage systems and supernatural contracts, making them historically important to Noble Vampire society.[18][19]

#### Hunters and the Inquisition
The Inquisition and other [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] historically specialized in exterminating hostile Vampires through Faith, blessed weapons and supernatural techniques.[12] This relationship eventually changed as political alliances and the supernatural order surrounding Earth were reorganized.

### Historical Development

#### Ancient Era
The original Vampire race developed outside Earth and possessed the Ancient Vampire form later demonstrated by Vlad.[2] Ancient Progenitors produced independent Bloodlines, many of which were subsequently destroyed or reduced to small isolated communities.[1]

#### Rise of Vlad
Vlad became the most successful Vampire Progenitor of his era, established the dominant modern sireline and built [[Nightingale]]'s long-lasting Vampire civilization.[1] His success resulted in supernatural society calling him the First Vampire Progenitor despite the existence of older lineages.

#### Second Vampire Progenitor
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s transformation created another Progenitor lineage within the same race.[7] His Bloodline rapidly developed into one of the most abnormal Vampire lineages in existence and demonstrated how quickly Progenitor Blood could strengthen or restructure other Nobles.[10][15]

#### Blood Dragon Transition
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later evolution into the Blood Dragon Primogenitor ended his personal existence as an ordinary Vampire while carrying his Vampiric traits into the new Draconic race.[8] Numerous members of his immediate family later followed him into the Blood Dragon lineage.
[[Blood Dragons]] should consequently be classified as a successor species descended partly from Vampirism, not as ordinary modern Vampires.

#### Daywalker Restoration
Thousands of years later, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] deliberately revisited the Vampire racial framework while helping [[Frederick Winter]]. Using a recreated form of his former Vampire Progenitor Blood, he designed the Daywalker mutation as a stronger Noble Vampire variant adapted for the increasingly dangerous wider Cosmos.[9]
Victor further revealed that he had restored Frederick's lineage toward the Ancient Vampire state rather than leaving him as merely a modern Daywalker, demonstrating that the lost ancestral Vampire condition can be intentionally reconstructed by a sufficiently powerful being with the necessary understanding of the race.[4]

### Current Status
Vampires remain an extant and widespread supernatural race, but there is no longer a single political or biological definition capable of describing every Vampire population. Vlad's [[Nightingale]] lineage remains one of the largest surviving Vampire civilizations, pre-Vlad Bloodlines such as the Asanbosam continue to exist, foreign Vampire groups developed independently, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s original Vampire sireline produced multiple evolved Bloodlines, and Daywalkers demonstrate that entirely new Vampire variants can still be created.[1][9]
The race's modern history also makes the old description of Vampires as simply "the species ruled by Vlad and five Counts" obsolete. Many formerly prominent Nightingale Vampires joined Victor's civilization, several evolved into [[Blood Dragons]], and the wider supernatural world now recognizes Vampire lineages whose origins predate Vlad by thousands of years.
