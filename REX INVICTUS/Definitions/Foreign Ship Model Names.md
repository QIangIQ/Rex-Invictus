**[[Foreign Ship Model Names]]** lists ship pattern names used outside the [[UOE Navy]]. Several appear in the [[War of Princes]]: the Voss defenders flew Mastodon light frigates and Icarus corvettes, and scrapped Vanguard and Mastodon wrecks for their void shields when [[Eurobium]] ran short. See [[Ship Classification]].
