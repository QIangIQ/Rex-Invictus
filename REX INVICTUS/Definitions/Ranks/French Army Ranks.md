**[[French Army Ranks]]** underlie the rank ladder of [[M.A.I.D]], which uses feminized forms (Colonelle, Capitaine, Sergente and so on). Since a 2019 French government circular, feminine rank titles may be used in the French armed forces.

| [[M.A.I.D]] form | French Army rank | NATO code | U.S. Army equivalent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Maréchale-Commandante | Maréchal de France (honorary dignity) | OF-10 | General of the Army |
| Générale de Corps | Général de corps d'armée | OF-8 | Lieutenant general |
| Générale de Division | Général de division | OF-7 | Major general |
| Générale de Brigade | Général de brigade | OF-6 | Brigadier general |
| Colonelle | Colonel | OF-5 | Colonel |
| Lieutenante-Colonelle | Lieutenant-colonel | OF-4 | Lieutenant colonel |
| Commandante | Commandant | OF-3 | Major |
| Capitaine | Capitaine | OF-2 | [[John-000\|Captain]] |
| Lieutenante | Lieutenant | OF-1 | First lieutenant |
| Sous-Lieutenante | Sous-lieutenant | OF-1 | Second lieutenant |
| Adjudante-Cheffe | Adjudant-chef | OR-8 | [[Dun Scaith Elderblood\|Master]] sergeant |
| Adjudante | Adjudant | OR-7 | Sergeant first class |
| Sergente-Majore | Sergent-major (historical; today sergent-chef) | OR-6 | Staff sergeant |
| Sergente | Sergent | OR-5 | Sergeant |
| Caporale-Cheffe | Caporal-chef | OR-4 | Corporal |
| Caporale | Caporal | OR-3 | Private first class |

In France the general officer ranks are styled by the number of stars (two to five). Maréchal is a state dignity, last given to Marie-Pierre Kœnig posthumously in 1984.
