A Demigod is a supernatural being possessing both a Divine lineage and the racial characteristics of a non-God species. The most familiar Demigods are children born between a God and a Human, but the classification is not restricted to Humanity. Known examples also include Fairy-Demigods such as [[Viviane]] and Werewolf-Demigods such as Anderson, demonstrating that the non-Divine side of a Demigod can belong to other supernatural species.
Demigods occupy an unusual position between ordinary mixed-race beings and full Gods. They are born carrying a portion of their Divine parent's power and Divinity, but they are not automatically Gods themselves. Chapter 627 directly explains that Demigods already possess their parent's Divinity within their bodies, which allows them to ascend toward Lesser Godhood considerably faster than beings beginning without Divine ancestry.[1]
The exact nature of a Demigod varies enormously according to the Divine parent, the other parent's species, the compatibility of both racial foundations, the strength of the inherited Divinity and whether the Divine parent was still an active God when the child was conceived. A Demigod therefore does not possess one universal physical power level or one standardized set of supernatural abilities.

### Nature
Demigods are fundamentally mixed Divine beings.
Unlike a normal God, whose existence revolves around a Divine Soul, Divinity and Concept, a Demigod possesses those Divine characteristics only as one part of a larger racial existence. The remaining part originates from the other parent.
This can produce radically different results.
A Human-Demigod may possess a primarily Human body strengthened by Divine ancestry, while a Werewolf-Demigod can combine the physiology and racial abilities of a Werewolf with inherited [[Divine Energy]]. A Fairy-Demigod such as [[Viviane]] combines Fairy spiritual physiology with a Divine heritage strong enough to grant extraordinary longevity and a highly stable Soul.[2][3]
The term Demigod consequently describes Divine ancestry rather than one completely independent biological species.

#### Relationship with Hybrids
Many Demigods are technically a form of Hybrid, but Divine hybridization operates differently from the unstable combinations seen between certain physical supernatural species.
Chapter 637 explains that Gods are fundamentally comparable to High-Level Spirits beneath their Conceptual structure. This spiritual nature makes Divine beings considerably more compatible with physical races than two unrelated biological supernatural species may be.[3]
[[Viviane]] is the clearest example. She was born from a God and a Fairy and is explicitly described as a Perfect Hybrid. Her Godly and Fairy sides coexist without the severe racial instability demonstrated by combinations such as Vampire-Werewolf Hybrids.[3]
This does not mean every Demigod must automatically be described as an identical Balanced Hybrid. The exact stability and inherited characteristics still depend upon the parents involved.

### Creation
The most conventional Demigod is created through reproduction between a God and a member of another race.
Because the child possesses Divine ancestry from birth, part of the parent's Divinity already exists inside the Demigod rather than needing to be created from nothing later.[1]
This provides Demigods with an important advantage when pursuing Godhood.
Ordinary supernatural beings attempting to become Gods generally need to establish the necessary connection with a Concept and develop a Divine existence capable of supporting it. A Demigod already possesses a Divine foundation inherited from the parent and can therefore progress into Lesser Godhood more easily.[1]

#### Children of Fallen Gods
A Divine parent does not necessarily need to remain seated within a Pantheon for Divine ancestry to persist.
[[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]] is an important example. Tasha was originally an Egyptian Goddess who later became a Fallen God after losing her formal Divine position. Despite this, she retained access to her Concepts and could still legitimately be regarded as a Goddess.[4]
Her children with [[Volk Fenrir]] consequently possess Werewolf bodies alongside Divine ancestry originating from Tasha.
Anderson is explicitly referred to as a Demigod in Chapter 268 and demonstrates the ability to produce an attack containing [[Divine Energy]] despite otherwise being an Alpha Werewolf.[5]
The same family background applies to Tasha's other children, including Fenir and [[Thomas Fenrir]], who belong to the Werewolf royal bloodline while also descending from a Fallen Goddess.
A Fallen God should not be confused with a normal mortal who simply lost all connection to Divinity. Tasha still possessed her Divine Concepts even while outside her former Pantheon.[4]

### Divine Heritage
A Demigod's most important characteristic is the portion of Divine nature inherited from the Godly parent.
This does not normally make the child equivalent to that parent.
Gods possess fully developed Divinities, [[Divine Souls]], Concepts and the ability to generate and use [[Divine Energy]] as part of their natural existence. A Demigod begins with only an inherited Divine foundation and must develop that foundation further if they wish to become a complete God.

#### Inherited Divinity
Chapter 627 explicitly states that Demigods already possess their parent's Divinity inside their bodies.[1]
This should not be interpreted as meaning a child automatically inherits the parent's complete Conceptual authority at full strength.
Rather, the inherited Divine component gives the Demigod:
- a natural connection to Divine power;
- greater compatibility with [[Divine Energy]];
- the possibility of manifesting abilities related to the parent's Divine nature;
- and a considerably easier path toward ascending into Godhood.
The exact amount and expression vary from one Demigod to another.

#### Divine Energy
Demigods can possess and use [[Divine Energy]] despite not being complete Gods.
Anderson provides a direct example. During the period before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became a fully Divine being, Anderson produced an attack involving an enormous wolf charged with Divine Energy.[5]
Demigod Divine Energy should nevertheless not be assumed equal in quality or quantity to that produced by a fully developed God.

#### Conceptual Affinity
Because a Demigod carries the parent's Divinity, the child can possess an affinity toward the Divine parent's Concept or associated authorities.
This should not be confused with immediately possessing complete Conceptual Manipulation.
Full Conceptual authority generally belongs to an actual God who has developed the corresponding Divinity. A Demigod may instead inherit partial powers, affinity or supernatural effects related to the parent's Divine nature until achieving full ascension.

### Ascension to Godhood
One of the defining advantages of Demigods is their unusually direct path toward becoming Gods.
[[Aphrodite]] explains in Chapter 627 that Demigods do not face the same beginning as ordinary beings because their parent's Divinity is already present within them. For this reason, they can ascend into Lesser Godhood faster than beings possessing no Divine foundation.[1]
This process is historically well established.
Numerous mythological Demigods eventually became Gods, with Heracles being one of the best-known examples referenced in the setting.[1]
Once a Demigod fully ascends and becomes a genuine God, the individual should no longer be treated as merely a Demigod for racial classification purposes.

#### Heracles
Heracles originated as a Demigod before eventually ascending to true Godhood.
His progression serves as one of the clearest examples of the advantage described by [[Aphrodite]]: a Demigod begins with the Divine foundation necessary to make eventual Godhood substantially easier.[1]

### Compatibility
The characteristics of the non-Divine parent have an enormous effect on the resulting Demigod.

#### Physical Races
When a God produces offspring with a physical species, the resulting child generally combines Divine heritage with the physiology of that species.
Examples include Werewolf-Demigods such as Anderson.
The individual retains the racial foundation of the physical parent while also possessing Divine ancestry.

#### Spiritual Races
Demigods born from Gods and highly spiritual beings can possess particularly unusual compatibility.
[[Viviane]] was born from a God and a Fairy. Chapter 637 explains that her existence was naturally stable because the Godly side of her heritage was fundamentally similar to a High-Level Spirit, allowing it to coexist extremely well with a Fairy's spiritual nature.[3]
This made Viviane a Perfect Hybrid rather than a genetically unstable mixed-race being.

#### Opposing Existences
The old article claimed that Gods and beings of an "opposite nature" such as Demons are universally incapable of producing Demigods.
The currently verified chapters do not establish this as an absolute biological law.
Gods and Demons possess opposing energetic and metaphysical characteristics in many contexts, but no sufficiently direct statement has been identified proving that reproduction between every God and every Demon is categorically impossible.
That claim should therefore not be presented as a universal Demigod rule without additional chapter evidence.

### Lifespan
Demigods possess considerably greater longevity than ordinary Humans but are not normally immortal simply because they possess Divine ancestry.
Chapter 1138 provides the clearest general benchmark, stating that even a Demigod normally lives approximately 300–500 years.[6]
This figure describes ordinary Demigods rather than every possible combination.
The lifespan can change dramatically depending upon the other parent species.

#### Viviane
[[Viviane]] is an extreme exception.
Her combined Fairy and God heritage grants her an almost infinite lifespan. Chapter 578 states that she can continue living until she is killed or the nature of the planet itself dies.[2]
This longevity should not be attributed to all Demigods.
It arises specifically from a combination of her Fairy and Divine heritage.
A Demigod whose non-Divine parent belongs to an exceptionally long-lived or effectively immortal species can therefore possess far greater longevity than a typical Human-Demigod.

### Powers and Abilities
Demigod Physiology: Demigods possess characteristics inherited from both Divine ancestry and their non-Divine racial foundation. Their exact powers vary too greatly for every ability below to be universal.

#### General Divine Abilities
- Divine Physiology (Partial): Demigods possess a partial Divine foundation inherited from their Godly parent. They are not complete Gods but carry Divine characteristics from birth.[1]
- Divinity: A Demigod possesses a portion of the parent's Divinity within their body from birth.[1]
- [[Divine Energy]] Manipulation: Demigods can possess and manipulate Divine Energy to varying degrees. Anderson explicitly manifested an attack containing Divine Energy despite remaining a Werewolf-Demigod.[5]
- Conceptual Affinity: Demigods can inherit affinities or abilities associated with the Divine parent's Concept. The degree differs by individual and should not automatically be equated with the complete Conceptual authority of a full God.
- Accelerated Divine Ascension: Because the Divine foundation already exists within them, Demigods can reach Lesser Godhood substantially faster than ordinary beings attempting to ascend without Divine ancestry.[1]
- Divine Weapon Compatibility: Demigods can possess sufficient Divine affinity to interact with or wield Divine artifacts when the weapon itself and its conditions permit. This is not an unconditional racial right to use every Divine weapon.

#### Inherited Racial Abilities
- Composite Physiology: A Demigod retains characteristics belonging to the non-Divine parent race alongside its Divine heritage.
- Racial Ability Inheritance: Demigods can inherit the supernatural abilities associated with their non-Divine parent. A Werewolf-Demigod therefore remains capable of displaying Werewolf physiology, transformations and racial Energy alongside Divine power.
- Racial Weakness Inheritance: The non-Divine side may also retain its corresponding vulnerabilities unless the Divine heritage specifically compensates for them.

#### Physical Enhancement
Demigods generally possess superior physical foundations to ordinary members of weaker mortal races because Divine ancestry reinforces their existence.
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Human-Demigods and similarly mortal-origin Demigods can possess strength, speed, stamina, durability and reactions far beyond ordinary members of the mortal parent race.
- Enhanced Strength: Divine ancestry can significantly increase the natural physical power of the non-Divine body.
- Enhanced Speed: Demigods can possess movement speed beyond the ordinary limits of the parent species.
- Enhanced Reflexes: Their Divine inheritance can reinforce perception and reaction capability.
- Enhanced Durability: Demigods can possess bodies significantly harder to injure than ordinary mortals.
- Enhanced Stamina: The inherited Divine component can improve physical endurance and Energy reserves.
- Enhanced Regeneration: Some Demigods possess greater healing ability than ordinary members of the non-Divine parent species.
The scale of these enhancements varies too greatly for one fixed racial statistic. A Fairy-Demigod, Werewolf-Demigod and Human-Demigod should not automatically be expected to possess identical physical performance.

#### Longevity
- Longevity: An ordinary Demigod can live approximately 300–500 years, vastly exceeding the historical lifespan of an ordinary Human.[6]
- Extended Longevity (Species Dependent): If the non-Divine parent belongs to an unusually long-lived species, the Demigod's lifespan can increase far beyond the standard range.
- Near-[[Immortality]] ([[Viviane]]): Viviane's Fairy-God heritage grants an almost unlimited natural lifespan, lasting until she is killed or the planetary Nature supporting her existence dies.[2]

### God-Fairy Demigods

#### Viviane
[[Viviane]] is one of the most unusual known Demigods.
She is the child of a God and a Fairy and consequently possesses both Divine and Fairy spiritual heritage.
Chapter 637 identifies her as a Perfect Hybrid, explaining that the spiritual nature of Gods allows her Divine side to coexist naturally with her Fairy existence without producing the instability often found in incompatible Hybrid species.[3]
Her Fairy heritage provides an intimate relationship with Nature and [[World Trees]], while her Godly heritage contributes to her exceptionally powerful Soul and extraordinary lifespan.
Viviane is therefore a poor baseline for an average Human-Demigod.
Her combination is exceptionally compatible and spiritually advanced.

### Werewolf Demigods
Tasha's children demonstrate that Demigods are not limited to Human-God reproduction.
[[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]] originated as an Egyptian Goddess before becoming a Fallen God and later a Werewolf. Even while Fallen, she retained her Concepts and remained sufficiently Divine to still be considered a Goddess.[4]
Her children with [[Volk Fenrir]] consequently possess Werewolf physiology together with inherited Divine ancestry.

#### Anderson
Anderson is explicitly identified as a Demigod in Chapter 268.[5]
He remains an Alpha Werewolf and possesses Werewolf racial characteristics, but he can additionally use Divine power.
One of his attacks manifested as an enormous wolf infused with [[Divine Energy]], demonstrating that the Divine component of his ancestry is operational rather than merely genealogical.[5]

#### Fenir
Fenir is the eldest son of Tasha and Volk.
Like his brothers, his maternal lineage originates from a Fallen Goddess while his physical racial foundation is Werewolf.
His exact Divine abilities have not been explored as extensively as Anderson's and should not be invented from mythology or from the powers of other family members.

#### Thomas Fenrir
[[Thomas Fenrir]] is another son of Tasha and Volk and therefore shares the same mixed Divine/Werewolf ancestry.
He additionally received particular attention and protection from [[Fenrir]], although a God's Blessing should be distinguished from the Divine heritage inherited through Tasha.

### Human Demigods
Historically, Human-Demigods represent the most famous examples of the classification.
Greek mythology and the Pantheon system produced numerous individuals descended from Gods and Humans, many of whom became famous heroes before either dying or ascending into Godhood.
Known or historically recognized examples include:
- Perseus
- Helen of [[Troy]]
- Heracles (Formerly; ascended to Godhood)
Their abilities varied according to their Divine parent and should not be reduced to one universal set of powers.

### Relationship with Gods
Demigods possess an ambiguous relationship with Gods.
They are part Divine but usually lack the full authority, Conceptual development and immortality systems of complete Gods.
Their Divine blood makes eventual ascension easier, yet until that process is completed they remain fundamentally incomplete compared with a genuine God.[1]
The old claim that Gods generally despise all Demigods is too broad and is not sufficiently established as a universal law.
Individual Pantheons and Gods may treat Divine children differently according to culture, politics, parentage and personal relationships.

### Relationship with Perfect Hybrids
Main article: Hybrids/Balanced Hybrids
Demigod and Perfect Hybrid are overlapping but distinct classifications.
Demigod describes Divine ancestry.
Perfect Hybrid describes successful coexistence between multiple racial natures without the biological or spiritual defects seen in unstable Hybrids.
[[Viviane]] possesses both classifications:
- she is a Demigod because one parent was a God;
- and a Perfect Hybrid because her God and Fairy natures coexist harmoniously.[3]
A Demigod therefore does not need to be discussed as genetically defective simply because it is technically mixed-race.

### Weaknesses
There is no universal weakness shared by every possible Demigod beyond the fact that they have not necessarily completed the transition into full Godhood.
- Incomplete Godhood: A Demigod possesses Divine ancestry and inherited Divinity but lacks the fully developed Divine existence of a genuine God unless they ascend.[1]
- Mortality: Most Demigods remain mortal and can die from age or injury. Chapter 1138 places ordinary Demigod lifespans around 300–500 years rather than the effectively limitless lifespans of Gods.[6]
- Inherited Racial Weaknesses: A Demigod can retain weaknesses associated with the non-Divine parent species. The exact weaknesses depend upon the individual.
- Limited Divine Authority: Inheriting Divine power does not automatically grant the complete Conceptual authority, resurrection systems or [[Divine Energy]] reserves possessed by a fully developed God.
- Variable Compatibility: Although Gods' spiritual nature makes Divine hybridization unusually compatible, the stability and abilities of the resulting child still depend upon the races and circumstances involved.
The former article's claim that the majority of Demigods suffer from mental illness, aggression and anger problems has been removed because the cited chapters do not establish this as a general racial condition.
