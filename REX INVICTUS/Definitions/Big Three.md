**[[Big Three]]** are the three sons of [[Kronos]] who divided the world: [[Zeus]] (sky), [[Poseidon]] (sea) and [[Hades]] ([[Greek Underworld|Underworld]]). In [[Percy Jackson and the Olympians]], after World War II, which their children fought on opposite sides, they swore an oath on the [[Styx]] to have no more demigod children, because such children are too powerful and the [[Great Prophecy]] concerned one. [[Zeus]] broke it with [[Thalia Grace]], [[Poseidon]] with [[Percy Jackson]], and [[Hades]] had [[Bianca di Angelo]] and [[Nico di Angelo]] from before. See [[Oath (ambiguous)]].
