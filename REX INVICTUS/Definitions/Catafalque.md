A **[[Catafalque]]** is a raised platform or bier that holds a coffin or body during a funeral or lying in state. The Lincoln Catafalque, built in 1865 for Abraham Lincoln, has since held many people lying in state in the U.S. Capitol rotunda.

In the vault the word fits the funerary naming of the SNV designations (such as 041-SNV-EPITAPHIOS and 099-SNV-SEPULCRUM) in [[(Potential) Pilot Names]].
