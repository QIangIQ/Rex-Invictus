The **[[Major Arcana]]** are the 22 trump cards of the tarot, numbered 0 to XXI in the Rider-Waite-Smith order (1909) used by [[Tarot card reading]]. Tarot began as a card game in 15th-century Italy; its occult reading dates from the 18th century (Antoine Court de Gébelin, 1781) and the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn.

## The Fool
0. Beginnings, innocence, a leap of faith. Upright: spontaneity. Reversed: recklessness.

## The Magician
I. Willpower and skill; all four suits on the table. Reversed: manipulation.

## The High Priestess
II. Intuition, hidden knowledge, the unconscious. Reversed: secrets withheld.

## The Empress
III. Fertility, abundance, nurture. Reversed: dependence.

## The Emperor
IV. Authority, structure, the father. Reversed: tyranny or rigidity.

## The Hierophant
V. Tradition, institutions, teaching. Reversed: rebellion against convention.

## The Lovers
VI. Union, choice, values. Reversed: disharmony.

## The Chariot
VII. Victory through control and will. Reversed: loss of direction.

## Strength
VIII. Courage and gentle control (a woman closing a lion's jaws). Reversed: self-doubt. Some decks swap it with Justice (XI).

## The Hermit
IX. Introspection, solitude, guidance. Reversed: isolation.

## Wheel of Fortune
X. Cycles, fate, turning points. Reversed: bad luck.

## Justice
XI. Fairness, truth, cause and effect. Reversed: dishonesty.

## The Hanged Man
XII. Surrender, a new perspective, suspension. Reversed: stalling.

## Death
XIII. Endings and transformation, rarely literal death. Reversed: resistance to change.

## Temperance
XIV. Balance, moderation, patience. Reversed: excess.

## The Devil
XV. Bondage, addiction, materialism. Reversed: release.

## The Tower
XVI. Sudden upheaval and revelation. Reversed: disaster averted or delayed.

## The Star
XVII. Hope, renewal, serenity. Reversed: despair.

## The Moon
XVIII. Illusion, fear, the subconscious. Reversed: confusion clearing.

## The Sun
XIX. Joy, success, vitality. Reversed: temporary setbacks.

## Judgement
XX. Reckoning, rebirth, a call. Reversed: self-doubt.

## The World
XXI. Completion and fulfilment. Reversed: unfinished business.
