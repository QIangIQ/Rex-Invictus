## Description

**Hyperium** is the foundational energy medium of the [[UOE]], produced by suspending a **trace admixture (<1%) of Titan ichor derived from [[Hyperion]]** within a stabilized, ultra-reactive fuel lattice.

Unlike conventional fuels, Hyperium is not consumed through combustion. It operates as a **divine catalytic amplifier**, where the ichor component forces near-complete energy extraction from the surrounding medium.

### Visual Signature

- Base coloration: **faint silver (Titan ichor)**
    
- Active state: **red-silver luminescence**
    
- Fully energized: **deep crimson core with silver filamentation**
    

The red component originates from **energetic excitation of the propellant matrix**, while the silver persists as the ichor’s intrinsic signature.

---

### Catalytic Behavior

Hyperium functions through:

1. **Excitation of carrier propellant**
    
2. **Ichor-mediated amplification of reaction pathways**
    
3. **Near-total matter-energy conversion under constraint**
    

The Titan ichor acts as a **causality anchor**, stabilizing reactions that would otherwise result in uncontrolled annihilation.

## Harvesting Doctrine

### Source: [[Hyperion]] (Titan-Class Entity)

Hyperion is not dead. The Titan is **biologically partitioned and perpetually sustained** in a controlled state of forced regeneration.

---

### Structural Condition

- Body divided into **countless isolated biomass sectors**
    
- Each sector undergoes:
    
    - Continuous over-regeneration
        
    - Tissue hypertrophy
        
    - Tumorous expansion
        

These growths are:

> **Engineered extraction masses — biologically alive, structurally controlled, and optimized solely for ichor yield.**

---

### Harvesting Process

1. **Containment Stabilization**
    
    - Divine suppression fields prevent systemic awakening
        
2. **Localized Growth Induction**
    
    - Tissue stimulated to exceed natural regeneration limits
        
3. **Ichor Extraction**
    
    - High-pressure siphoning from vascularized tumor masses
        
4. **Immediate Fractionalization**

5. **Transport to Refinement Forges**
    
    - Delivered under Aegis-locked containment
