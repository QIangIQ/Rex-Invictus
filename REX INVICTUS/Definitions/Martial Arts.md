**[[Martial Arts]]** are codified systems of combat practiced for war, self-defense, sport or self-cultivation.

---

## History (IRL)

Ancient examples include Greek pankration and wrestling at the Olympic Games, Roman gladiatorial schools and the Celtic warrior training of the [[Ulster Cycle]]. Chinese wushu, Japanese kenjutsu, jujutsu and karate, Korean taekwondo, Indian kalaripayattu and European fencing traditions all developed their own schools and lineages. In Chinese fiction the *wuxia* and *xianxia* genres join martial arts to the cultivation of inner energy, the model for the [[Cultivation Levels]] of the [[Martial Arts Orthodox Alliance]] and [[Heavenly Demonic Sect]].

## Rex Invictus

[[Scathach]] is credited with founding structured martial arts in mythological attribution, as the teacher of [[Cuchulain]] at [[Dún Scáith]]. See also [[Melee]] and [[Titan Melee]].
