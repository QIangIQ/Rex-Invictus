**[[Aunt Agnes]]** is a figure of Rex Invictus lore. (Lore adapted from *The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** Flamel material is adapted into the Rex Invictus present (c. 2021). Rex Invictus wins any conflict; [[Robespierre]] fills [[Dr. John Dee]]'s role and hunts the Philosopher's Stone rather than [[The Codex]]. See [[Flamel Series#Integration rules]].

---

## Flamel Series Canon (SOFTINF)

> Aunt Agnes is the alias of [[Tsagaglalal]], one of the first [[Humani]] created by [[Prometheus]]. She was known by her alias to [[Sophie Newman|Sophie]] and [[Josh Newman]], who believed her to be their biological aunt. However, it was later revealed that they were not, in fact, related.
