Elle is [[Sophie Newman]]'s best friend, mentioned in [[The Alchemyst]] and briefly in [[The Necromancer]]. Elle is from New York, and Sophie rarely sees her. They stay in contact using e-mail, mobile phone, and social media. Elle is never physically present in the series.
