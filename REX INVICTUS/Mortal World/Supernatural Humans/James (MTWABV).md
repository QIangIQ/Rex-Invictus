### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 30
James, commonly referred to as General James, is a supporting antagonist in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]]. He was formerly one of the Generals of the Inquisition serving beneath Alexander, but secretly pursued increasingly extreme research involving Vampires, Humans, Faith and artificial Hybrids. He later abandoned the Inquisition following the intervention of the Angels and became one of the core members of [[New Dawn]], eventually holding the organization's position of Second Seat.[1]
James was one of the primary architects behind the artificial Hybrid experiments encountered by [[Victor Elderblood]] during his early years as a Vampire Count. Rather than confronting Victor personally, James repeatedly treated him as both an enemy and an invaluable experimental benchmark, deploying increasingly powerful creations against him to observe the limits of Victor's strength and gather data for future projects.[2][3][4] His continued association with Radu, Fenir and other enemies of Victor eventually placed him among the most persistent surviving conspirators opposing the growing Elderblood faction.
James was eventually captured during Victor's campaign against New Dawn and its allied factions. Rather than immediately executing him, Victor ordered James to be imprisoned and instructed his forces to seize the entirety of his accumulated research.[5]

### Physical description
James is a relatively short man standing approximately 170 cm tall. He has black hair and black eyes and was originally seen wearing the black priestly robes associated with high-ranking members of the Inquisition. His overall appearance was described as dark and unremarkable compared to the more physically imposing [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] surrounding him.[6]
One of James' more distinctive characteristics is his expression. He commonly maintains a lifeless or exhausted-looking face even during dangerous situations, contributing to his detached and difficult-to-read demeanor.[6]

### Personality and traits
James is an intelligent, calculating and deeply unethical researcher whose greatest strength lies considerably more in preparation and experimentation than direct combat. Unlike many [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] who rely primarily upon personal strength or religious conviction, James approaches supernatural beings as subjects that can be studied, dissected, reproduced and eventually surpassed through controlled experimentation. He prefers operating remotely through laboratories, surveillance equipment, subordinates and disposable test subjects instead of unnecessarily exposing himself to combat.[2]
His scientific curiosity is combined with an extreme form of religious fanaticism. James was willing to experiment upon Humans and other supernatural beings if doing so could advance his objective of developing weapons against Vampires. When questioned over the morality of using Humans as experimental material, James justified his actions through the belief that defeating what he considered the "evil" threatening mankind permitted him to dirty his own hands. He believed that God would ultimately forgive his actions because they were performed for what he considered a righteous purpose.[2]
This allows James to commit actions that would normally contradict the teachings supposedly represented by the Inquisition without perceiving himself as hypocritical. His devotion is centered less upon Church doctrine and more upon his own interpretation of Humanity's survival. Even the corruption and political problems within the Church failed to discourage him from continuing his experiments; once he left the Inquisition, he instead viewed the absence of its restrictions as an opportunity to pursue his research with greater freedom.[1]
James displays a pronounced tendency to objectify his experimental subjects. Individuals such as S-000 and A-000 were often discussed in terms of their usefulness, completion and performance rather than simply as people. By the time of the Japanese conflict, James considered the pair among his most successful or nearly perfected products and prioritized preserving them partly because of their immense value to his research.[7]
Despite his arrogance regarding his work, James is capable of recognizing when his calculations are incorrect. His encounters with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] repeatedly forced him to revise his understanding of what was possible. Artificial combatants designed to contend with ancient Vampires proved incapable of properly challenging Victor, and even stronger creations continued failing regardless of James' theoretical estimations.[3][4]
This eventually caused James' attitude toward Victor to become a strange mixture of hostility, fear, respect and scientific fascination. He continued regarding Victor as an enemy and an existence that should not have been possible, but he also became increasingly impressed by the rate at which Victor exceeded every prediction made about him. Rather than diminishing his interest, Victor's abnormal growth only intensified James' desire to observe, understand and eventually create something capable of opposing him.[7]
James is also highly pragmatic. He has little interest in sacrificing himself for ideological appearances and routinely avoids direct confrontation when the probability of success is poor. This trait made him compatible with Radu Tepes, whose own preference for planning, manipulation and strategic retreat greatly contrasted with battle-oriented individuals such as Victor and [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]].[1]

### Synopsis

#### The Awakening
James first appeared during [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett|Scathach Scarlett]]'s assault upon the headquarters of the Inquisition. After [[Scathach]] descended upon Vatican territory in retaliation for the attack on [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]], Alexander ordered Generals James and Leonardo to prepare for battle.[6]
James was presented alongside the highest-ranking combatants of the organization, though his reaction already distinguished him from more emotional [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]]. Upon seeing the situation surrounding Scathach, he sarcastically questioned Mizuki regarding what she had done before following Alexander's instructions and preparing for the possible confrontation.[6]

#### A New Pillar
James' more important activities were initially conducted away from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s direct awareness. By the time Victor began investigating the experimental Hybrid designated B-002, evidence pointed toward an extensive research network attempting to artificially combine characteristics belonging to Humans, Vampires and users of Faith. Unlike natural Hybrids, these artificial creations frequently possessed severe biological defects, shortened lifespans or infertility, but some retained Vampire characteristics while simultaneously remaining capable of using powers normally associated with [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]].[8]
Information surrounding B-002, [[Carlos Reiss]] and the other experiments increasingly pointed toward General James as one of the individuals controlling the project. Two especially important creations were designated S-000 and A-000, later identified as Sylvie and August. Carlos himself also attracted James' interest because of his extreme hatred of Vampires and his willingness to indiscriminately kill them, making him useful to the General's plans.[8]
The true extent of James' involvement became apparent when Victor confronted the forces surrounding Carlos. Rather than personally appearing, James observed the confrontation remotely and deliberately treated Victor as a live experimental subject. He deployed multiple enhanced subjects against Victor in progressively more dangerous stages, studying how the recently established Vampire Count reacted to their abilities and attempting to determine his upper limits.[2]
The experiments were not insignificant. James had developed combatants capable of threatening Vampires centuries older than Victor and believed that their transformations would eventually force Alucard to reveal more of his power. Instead, Victor systematically overwhelmed them while remaining considerably more comfortable than James had predicted.[3]
James escalated the experiment again, including methods that combined or mutated his surviving subjects into a considerably stronger creature. The process increased their raw power but also sacrificed much of their rationality and combat effectiveness. Victor consequently destroyed the resulting monster far more easily than James' calculations suggested should have been possible.[4]
The encounter exposed one of the central weaknesses in James' research philosophy. He could artificially increase physical ability and combine supernatural characteristics, but numerical power alone could not reproduce Victor's accumulated martial skill, instincts, adaptability or ability to employ numerous different powers together.
Victor's faction subsequently invaded and investigated the laboratory network involved in the experiments. Rather than simply destroying everything, [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] recognized the scientific value of the collected information and ordered useful research, equipment and records preserved for later study.[9][10] When Victor later extracted additional information from Carlos, he finally obtained a much clearer connection between the experimental program and General James, turning the previously distant researcher into a directly identified enemy.[11]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
James continued monitoring [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] after the destruction and exposure of several of his earlier experiments. During the conflict in Japan, he observed Alucard's actions from a distance while attempting to preserve S-000 and A-000, who had become two of the most valuable products remaining from his research.[7]
By this point James had become considerably more cautious about Victor. The Vampire's growth had exceeded even the already abnormal predictions created from previous observations, and James recognized that attempting to directly recover his experiments while both Victor and [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] were nearby would be effectively suicidal. He therefore released restrictions placed upon S-000 and A-000 while instructing them to avoid exposing their capabilities until an opportunity to escape appeared.[7]
James also continued utilizing covert equipment supplied through [[Niklaus Horseman]], including surveillance methods that allowed him to follow developments without personally entering the battlefield. Rather than relying upon brute force, he attempted to manipulate the wider political situation and create distractions through other [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]], including Leonardo, while keeping his own involvement hidden.[7]
The events also exposed the degree to which James remained obsessed with solving the instability of artificial Hybrids. The existence of [[Nero]], a naturally born and comparatively stable Hybrid, immediately attracted his scientific interest because studying a successful natural Hybrid could potentially provide information capable of correcting the defects within his own artificial creations.[7]
Victor himself produced an even stronger reaction. Although James continued believing that Alucard represented an existence hostile to his ideals, he could no longer dismiss him as merely another Vampire. Victor's continuing evolution inspired genuine respect, fear, admiration and awe within the scientist precisely because every encounter forced James to expand what he believed a supernatural being could accomplish.[7]

#### Demonic War
Following the increasing involvement of the Angels in the affairs of the Church, James separated himself from the Inquisition before he could be caught in the subsequent cleansing of its corrupted elements. His foresight allowed him to avoid being present when the organization came under direct attack, and he later considered the loss of his former position beneficial because he was no longer required to obey the restrictions imposed upon his research by the Church.[1]
James subsequently became one of the principal members of [[New Dawn]]. Alongside [[Niklaus Horseman]], Fenir, [[Bael]] and other conspirators, he participated in a meeting concerning the increasingly dangerous threat represented by [[Victor Elderblood]]. Rather than treating Victor as an inconvenience that could be dealt with later, the group recognized that his abnormal growth meant every delay would make killing him considerably more difficult.[1]
When Fenir argued that challenging Victor directly would be foolish, Niklaus agreed and formally entrusted the operation against Alucard to James, addressing him as New Dawn's Second Seat. James accepted responsibility and emphasized that, unlike during his time within the Inquisition, he was no longer constrained by the organization's rules or limitations.[1]
From that point onward, James' scientific expertise, experience with Victor and willingness to employ unconventional methods made him one of New Dawn's primary figures responsible for finding methods of opposing the rapidly evolving Progenitor.

#### King Among Kings
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] defeated [[Diablo]] and absorbed information belonging to the Demon King and several of his subordinates, the secrecy protecting [[New Dawn]] began collapsing. The memories Victor obtained connected previously scattered enemies into a coherent network and identified James, Niklaus and Fenir among the conspirators whose activities had repeatedly intersected with Victor and his family.[12]
It was later revealed that New Dawn had originally begun with a much smaller core than the organization it eventually became. The initial group consisted primarily of [[Niklaus Horseman]], General James and Fenir, whose interests increasingly aligned as Victor's overwhelming presence disrupted the existing supernatural order. As their conflicts with Victor intensified, the organization expanded and sought support from increasingly powerful external factions.[13]
This made James not merely a later recruit but one of New Dawn's original core members. His position as Second Seat reflected the importance he had accumulated inside the organization through his knowledge, experiments and ability to develop plans without personally entering direct combat.
New Dawn's secrecy ultimately became irrelevant when Victor began openly eliminating organizations and Pantheons that had accumulated grievances against him. During the war involving New Dawn, the [[Egyptian Pantheon]] and the [[Elder Gods Pantheon]], James was successfully located and captured by Victor's forces.[5]
Unlike many of Victor's enemies, James was not immediately killed. Once informed of his capture, Victor ordered that he remain restrained inside the dungeon and specifically instructed his subordinates to confiscate all of James' research. Members of the [[Blank Clan]] subsequently began securing the research and materials accumulated throughout his years of experimentation.[5]
James therefore survived the destruction of New Dawn's immediate operations but lost both his freedom and control over the scientific knowledge he had spent years developing.

### Skills & Abilities
- Faith Manipulation: As a Human Hunter and former General of the Inquisition, James possesses access to the supernatural power of Humanity known as Faith. While he has never been shown seriously fighting on-screen, his normally black eyes have explicitly been shown glowing gold when his religious conviction and power surfaced.[14][15] Because James has never demonstrated the extensive offensive applications displayed by [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] such as Leonardo, more specific abilities such as energy projection, barriers or weapon creation should not currently be attributed to him without further evidence.
- Supernatural Scientific Expertise: James' greatest demonstrated ability is his extraordinary knowledge of supernatural biology and his attempts to combine it with scientific experimentation. He studied the physiology and powers of Vampires, Humans, Hunters and Hybrids, eventually creating artificial Human-Vampire Hybrids capable of simultaneously possessing Vampire characteristics and access to Human Faith.[16][17]
- Artificial Hybrid Creation: James successfully developed methods of artificially combining Human and Vampire traits. Although many of his creations suffered from instability, infertility, shortened lifespans or other biological defects, they were nevertheless genuine functional Hybrids rather than complete failures. His experiments could possess Vampire traits such as enhanced physical attributes, regeneration, claws, wings and Charm alongside Hunter abilities derived from Faith.[18][19]
- Supernatural Enhancement: James is capable of artificially enhancing already existing beings and producing experimental subjects significantly stronger than ordinary Humans. The combatants A-010, A-011 and A-012 were modified until their combined abilities allowed them to contend with powerful Vampires, while further transformations could dramatically increase their raw power.[20][21]
- Power Restriction and Release: James developed or possessed a method of restricting the supernatural abilities of his experiments and remotely removing those restrictions when necessary. During the conflict in Japan, he remotely released the restrictions placed upon S-000 and A-000, although he initially ordered them not to expose their full abilities while [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] were nearby.[22]
- Remote Experiment Control: James can remotely trigger processes and transformations incorporated into his experimental subjects. During his observation of Victor, he activated additional stages of A-010, A-011 and A-012's modifications from his laboratory, deliberately forcing their bodies into increasingly powerful states to test Victor's limits.[23][24]
- Genetic and Biological Modification: James possesses extensive knowledge of supernatural physiology and can modify living subjects to reproduce traits belonging to other species. His research attempted to reproduce the natural compatibility seen in beings such as [[Nero Elderblood]], with James believing that studying a stable natural Hybrid could help eliminate the defects present within his artificial creations.[25]
- Experimental Design: James is capable of designing specialized supernatural combatants around particular combinations of abilities. His artificial Hybrids demonstrated powers including Vampire physiology, regeneration, bat-like wings, claws, Charm, illusion abilities and unusual applications of golden Faith-based flames, showing that James could reproduce or artificially integrate several supernatural traits into individual subjects.[26]
- Scientific Analysis: James possesses exceptional analytical ability and repeatedly uses combat as an opportunity to collect data. Rather than simply ordering his experiments to kill Victor, he deliberately deployed increasingly powerful subjects in stages so that he could estimate Victor's physical strength, fighting ability, powers and upper limits.[27][28]
- Supernatural Research: James possesses considerable knowledge regarding Vampires, Hunters, Faith and supernatural physiology. He was able to evaluate the approximate level of Vampires his creations should theoretically be capable of confronting and continually adjusted his experiments according to the results he observed.[29]
- Surveillance: James is highly skilled at remotely observing supernatural conflicts. During the Japanese incident, he monitored Victor Elderblood through a miniature black insect-like surveillance device supplied by [[Niklaus Horseman]] and secretly placed on S-000.[30]
- Tactical Planning: James rarely engages powerful enemies personally and instead constructs circumstances in which other combatants, experiments or allies confront them while he observes from a safer location. His ambush involving Carlos and the artificial Hybrids was deliberately structured as a multi-stage test intended to obtain information on Victor rather than simply assassinate him.[31]
- Manipulation: James is proficient at exploiting other people's hatred, ideology and circumstances for his experiments. Individuals such as [[Carlos Reiss]] were useful to him precisely because their hatred of Vampires made them willing participants in increasingly extreme modifications and operations.[32][33]
- High Intelligence: James is an exceptionally intelligent scientist and strategist capable of coordinating laboratories, long-term experimentation programs, artificial supernatural beings and covert operations. His expertise was valuable enough that after James was captured, Victor specifically ordered his forces to confiscate all of his research rather than simply destroy it.[34]

### Trivia
- James was one of the three original core members of [[New Dawn]], alongside [[Niklaus Horseman]] and Fenir.[13]
- By the events of Chapter 595, James held the position of Second Seat within New Dawn.[1]
- James' two most prominent experimental subordinates, S-000 and A-000, are Sylvie and August, respectively.[7]
- Although James' experiments frequently suffered from instability, infertility, limited lifespans or other defects, describing his attempts to combine science and the supernatural as complete failures is inaccurate. He successfully produced artificial Human-Vampire Hybrids, enhanced combatants capable of using supernatural abilities and subjects powerful enough to threaten experienced Vampires. Their greatest failure was their inability to reach the level James expected when confronted by extraordinary individuals such as [[Victor Elderblood]].[8][2][3]
- James regarded Victor as both one of his greatest enemies and one of the most valuable sources of experimental data available to him. His feelings toward Victor eventually developed beyond simple hatred and included scientific fascination, respect, fear and awe at his abnormal growth.[2][7]
- Unlike several combat-oriented antagonists, James generally preferred to observe battles remotely and send experimental subjects, [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] or other allies into direct confrontations. This allowed him to survive multiple conflicts that destroyed many of Victor's earlier enemies.
