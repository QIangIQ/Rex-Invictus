### Appearances

#### Debut
[[Ch10 - Rex Invictus III|Chapter 10]]
June, better known by her Witch Codename The Greedy Witch, is a Human Witch and a Titled Witch of [[The Arcane Realm]]. She specializes in contracts, information brokerage, magical construction, supernatural logistics and solving problems that cannot conveniently be handled through ordinary Human institutions. She is one of the earliest Witches associated with [[Victor Elderblood]]'s household, having originally worked for [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] before Victor's transformation brought him into her clientele.[2]
June earned the title The Greedy Witch for good reason. Profit is one of the strongest motivations in her life: she negotiates aggressively, treats information as merchandise, hoards valuable objects and researches useful Artifacts partly for the possibility of reproducing or monetizing them. Her greed is nevertheless accompanied by considerable competence and self-preservation. Once she accepts a contract she generally performs it reliably, openly states when something exceeds her reach and refuses to throw her life away in conflicts between beings vastly stronger than herself.[3][4][1]
Although she never becomes one of Victor's primary combatants, June repeatedly proves useful during the early development of his faction. She built and reinforced parts of the Walker underground residence, created Victor's early covert infrastructure, investigated Lucy and [[Horseman Clan]], helped recover the Horseman experiments and research, continued taking specialized construction commissions from Victor, and later provided one of the clearest perspectives on the position of ordinary Witches during the Demonic War.[5][6][7]

### Physical Appearance
June is a relatively short woman with fair skin, western facial features, black hair and striking golden eyes. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] first met her he estimated her height at approximately 155–160 centimeters, noting that she was slightly shorter than [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]]. Chapter 529 later gives a more precise comparison by describing Yue as 165 centimeters tall and exactly five centimeters taller than June, placing June at approximately 160 centimeters.[2][1]
At her debut, June wore her black hair short and dressed casually in a white shirt bearing a beach image, denim shorts and sandals.[2] Her appearance changes somewhat over time; by the raid on the Horseman experimental facility her black hair is described as long, showing that she had allowed it to grow considerably between her earliest appearances and Chapter 198.[6]
June generally dresses like an ordinary modern woman rather than adopting the stereotypical appearance expected of a mysterious supernatural Witch. When Victor unexpectedly contacted her while she was preparing to sleep in Japan, she arrived wearing an oversized sweatshirt and short shorts. Once their meeting became formal business, [[Magic]] Circles surrounded her body and transformed the casual outfit into a professional women's suit.[3] Her golden eyes are especially expressive whenever wealth is involved, with her reactions frequently becoming exaggerated the moment an opportunity for profit appears.

### Personality
June is greedy, opportunistic, practical, curious, cautious and unusually honest about all of those qualities. She makes no effort to pretend that her prices are altruistic; when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] directly calls her greedy, she proudly introduces herself as The Greedy Witch. She habitually attempts to extract the greatest possible payment from a commission, although the intimidating presence of clients such as Violet can persuade her to reconsider a particularly ambitious price almost instantly.[3]
Her attitude toward information is identical to her attitude toward physical wealth. Knowledge is something to acquire, protect and sell. During her investigation of Lucy and [[Horseman Clan]], June demonstrates that Witch society possesses an extensive informal information economy and bluntly explains that supposedly confidential information often stops being confidential once sufficient money is offered to the correct Witch. At the same time, she is professional enough to distinguish what she actually discovered from what she merely suspects, openly informing Natalia when further investigation would require contacts, risks or expenses beyond what she could reasonably provide.[4]
June's greed is accompanied by powerful survival instincts. She understands her own position in the supernatural hierarchy and has no desire to imitate warriors who deliberately challenge beings far beyond them. During the Demonic War, Yue asks whether June intends to help Victor after Arcane is sealed. June immediately rejects the idea, reasoning that Victor does not need a relatively ordinary Witch fighting on his behalf and that their relationship is based upon a contract rather than a promise that she will die for him. She values the money Victor's household gives her, but values remaining alive enough to continue enjoying that money even more.[1]
This does not make June unreliable. Her caution is part of the same practical mentality that makes her useful. She fulfills the work she accepts, warns her clients about professional restrictions before taking payment, admits when she cannot accomplish something and prefers preparation over reckless heroics. When Arcane enters lockdown, her immediate response after learning that the situation involves Evie, [[Diablo]], Divine Artifacts and Pantheon-level forces is to prepare a contingency rather than investigate powers capable of erasing her.[1]
June dislikes being uninformed. Even when she has no intention of participating in a conflict, she wants enough information to understand the danger and prepare an escape plan. This curiosity occasionally works against her; after Yue shows her evidence connected to Evie's dealings with Diablo, June immediately realizes that she has acquired information dangerous enough that ignorance might actually have been safer.[1]
Her greed also manifests as extreme hoarding. June accumulates clothes, books, magical research, Artifacts and anything else she considers potentially useful or valuable. A dimensional storage bag containing approximately 500 square meters of internal storage is, from June's perspective, frustratingly small because most of it is already occupied by her collection.[7] During the supposed invasion of Arcane, her concern for the Realm is quickly accompanied by horror at the thought that Demons might destroy the wealth she has spent years accumulating. She becomes sufficiently enraged by the possibility to imagine personally hunting down whoever damages her property.[7]
Her desire to possess things extends naturally into research. June tries to understand useful magical objects instead of merely storing them and attempts to reverse-engineer the unknown Runic structure of her dimensional bag. Her magical curiosity is therefore not purely academic: discovering how an expensive Artifact functions potentially gives her a way to reproduce it, improve it or profit from the knowledge.[7]
Despite being heavily motivated by self-interest, June possesses genuine personal relationships. Her old friendship with Yue is particularly close. Yue treats her almost like a younger sister, casually hugging her, dragging her into conversations, worrying about her safety and intentionally calling her increasingly incorrect names simply to irritate her. June complains constantly but trusts Yue enough to seek her out during Arcane's lockdown and accepts sensitive information from her.[1]
June's view of Victor is more complicated than a simple employer-employee relationship. She considers him an extremely profitable contractor and protects information surrounding him even while joking with Yue about his eccentricities. Chapter 529 also makes clear that June personally recognizes Victor's extraordinary attractiveness, but there is no established romantic relationship between them. Her long-term connection to Victor is principally that of a recurring contractor, information broker and supernatural business associate.[1]

### Synopsis

#### The Awakening
June first entered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s life shortly after his transformation into a Vampire. Violet had already hired her to transform the ordinary basement beneath the Walker household into a hidden supernatural residence large enough to accommodate servants, prisoners, equipment and the increasingly abnormal life developing beneath Victor's parents' home.[2]
When Victor returned home and questioned whether excavating so much ground beneath the property was safe, Violet introduced the Witch responsible. Victor recognized June as biologically Human but observed an unusual blue supernatural aura around her. June requested her usual payment from Violet—approximately one million dollars—before demonstrating how dramatically Witchcraft could alter a structure. Through several hand gestures and [[Magic]] Circles, the rough cavern was remodeled with white ceramic flooring, reinforced brick walls, furniture, wooden coffins and finished rooms until the underground area resembled part of a black-and-white mansion rather than an excavation site.[2]
Violet explained that June was particularly useful because she could handle services ordinary Human businesses could not perform openly or legally. Victor immediately tested that reputation by asking for an alternate identity complete with forged identification, a Social Security number, financial documentation and a bank account in a tax haven. June combined her contacts and supernatural methods to arrange the necessary infrastructure within minutes. Victor selected the deliberately generic identity James Smith, giving himself a mundane legal-looking persona that could be used separately from Victor Walker.[2]
The encounter also demonstrated June's mobility. Once her business was finished, she formed a Magic Circle and disappeared, mentioning that she had other responsibilities elsewhere. From the beginning, June therefore occupied a role very different from Victor's warriors: she was a logistical specialist capable of constructing spaces, arranging documents, using supernatural transportation and bridging the gap between ordinary Human society and the hidden supernatural economy.[2]
June returned when Victor required physical equipment for the James Smith identity. At the time she was relaxing in Japan and arrived dressed for sleep rather than business. Her first instinct was to charge Victor an enormous price, but Violet's reaction convinced her to dramatically reduce the amount. June then formally introduced her Witch Codename—The Greedy Witch—while Magic Circles transformed her clothing into a business suit and she produced a golden professional card for Victor.[3]
Victor commissioned a deliberately conspicuous black costume and mask that could conceal his identity while operating outside ordinary society. June created and adjusted the clothing according to his requests, demonstrating that her construction abilities were not limited to architecture. Natalia then gave her a far more serious commission: investigate Lucy, determine what he had stolen from the Inquisition and uncover whatever could be discovered about the people supporting him.[3]
Before accepting, June explained important rules governing Witch contracts. She required fifty percent of her payment in advance, and if two Witches discovered that they had been contracted by opposing clients, Witch law required both to withdraw rather than directly fight one another over their employers. A Witch was not supposed to create internal conflict within their race merely because outside factions happened to be enemies.[3]

#### Vampire Society
June later returned with a substantial intelligence package. Using a large [[Magic]] Circle, she projected images and information concerning Lucy and Karen, identifying Lucy as a cautious and ambitious Noble Vampire while revealing that Karen was both his lover and a Noble talent scout associated with [[Horseman Clan]]. The name initially meant little to Ruby and Sasha until June explained that Horseman was the Clan that had obtained the Count title previously belonging to the Fulger family.[4]
Natalia was surprised by how much supposedly confidential information June had obtained. June explained that secrecy within the Witch information economy was highly dependent upon money: she had located the Witch working for Horseman Clan and purchased information directly from her. Her investigation also identified the object stolen from the Church as a white coffin associated with the sacred relic known as the Finger of Saint Mary.[4]
June did not pretend to know everything. The deeper secrets surrounding the relic and Lucy's ultimate objective required access and risks beyond what she had been paid to provide, so she separated confirmed information from the areas she had failed to penetrate. She nevertheless supplied a flash drive containing Lucy's plans and other intelligence, earning a payment from Natalia large enough to reach ten digits. Sasha subsequently convinced the group to use June's greed and professional network for a broader investigation into Horseman Clan itself.[4]

#### A New Pillar
June remained close enough to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household that she was present around the period in which he returned from the Royal Capital as [[Nightingale]]'s new Fifth Vampire Count.[8][9] When Victor later returned to Earth, the underground residence beneath his parents' home had developed into a considerably larger and more functional supernatural base through June's work.[10]
June used wide-area [[Magic]] Circles to clean and reorganize the long-unused sections of the underground complex rapidly.[10] More importantly, she constructed a specialized training chamber deep beneath the residence so that Victor and his wives could use supernatural abilities without immediately destroying the house above them. The room was covered with multiple layers of magical reinforcement intended to endure the attacks of Noble Vampires, with the narration indicating that several conventional missiles would be required merely to scratch its protections.[5]
Victor's growth immediately made June regret her assumptions. While testing Blood Manipulation and his elemental abilities, he produced attacks powerful enough to damage a chamber constructed around the expected strength of Noble Vampires, sending tremors through the complex and forcing June to confront how quickly Victor was outgrowing infrastructure that should have remained useful for far longer.[5]
Her Horseman investigation concluded around the same period. June initially reported that she had discovered almost nothing obviously abnormal, an answer that became extremely uncomfortable after the fortune she had been paid. The investigation nevertheless produced one valuable fragment: a magical recording or listening device had captured part of a hidden conversation involving [[Nero Elderblood|Number Zero]], references to a supposedly perfect experimental subject, failure and an unexplained explosion before the surveillance method was discovered and destroyed.[11]
The fragment convinced Ruby that the absence of visible evidence around [[Horseman Clan]] was itself suspicious. She took the information and began pursuing it through contacts capable of reaching places June could not, making June's incomplete report one of the early clues leading Victor's faction toward the experiments hidden beneath the Horseman conflict.[11]
That investigation eventually culminated in the discovery of a laboratory filled with failed hybrid experiments, equipment and research. When Victor offered Ruby the choice between destroying everything and taking everything useful first, Ruby chose the latter. Victor contacted June through the golden card she had given him; he barely finished telling her there was profitable work available before a large Magic Circle appeared and June arrived immediately, demonstrating how efficiently the card functioned as a direct magical contact method.[6]
June examined the laboratory and judged the extraction comparatively straightforward. Her primary concern was destroying or bypassing the enchantments protecting the facility; once those magical defenses were removed, she and Victor's maids could systematically transport the experimental equipment, research records, specimens and other valuable materials back to Clan Alucard's underground base. The operation expanded June's established role from construction into supernatural dismantling and logistical recovery.[6]
Ruby explicitly warned June not to exploit Victor's willingness to pay by inflating the price excessively. June, unusually, was willing to be somewhat restrained because simply maintaining a profitable connection to one of Nightingale's [[Vampire Counts]] carried long-term value beyond a single commission. After the valuable contents were recovered, the remaining laboratory could be destroyed without wasting resources useful to Victor's growing faction.[6]

#### Discoveries, Changes, and New Beginnings
June's construction work remained useful even after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s resources expanded beyond the Walker household. When [[Roxanne Elderblood]] evolved and Victor learned that she was a young World Tree, Roxanne preferred an environment surrounded by nature rather than being confined inside an ordinary mansion. Victor therefore contacted June and commissioned a residence designed around Roxanne's preferences.[12]
June accepted the project and began directly questioning Roxanne about the type of home and environment she wanted, showing that her construction work could be customized around supernatural clients rather than merely reproducing ordinary Human architecture. The circumstances were intimidating—Roxanne's massive guardian was present—but a profitable commission remained enough to keep June working.[12]

#### Demonic War
June was in Clan Snow territory when the Demonic War reached [[Nightingale]] and the surrounding supernatural civilizations. During the fighting she received an emergency notice from Arcane claiming that Demonic portals had opened inside the Realm and that Demon Legions were attacking Witch territory. All Witches operating outside Arcane were ordered to return immediately.[7]
June requested permission from Violet and returned using a Witch teleportation crystal. The possibility of Arcane being invaded worried her, but her thoughts very quickly turned toward something more personal: nearly all of her accumulated treasure was stored there. The idea that Demons might burn or steal years of carefully hoarded wealth enraged June enough that she imagined hunting the responsible attackers herself.[7]
She arrived at her residence and found Arcane unexpectedly peaceful. Before investigating the larger political situation, June checked that her treasures were intact and reinforced the magical defenses protecting her property. Her home contained an enormous collection of clothes, books, magical research and Artifacts, including work connected to Beauty [[Magic]].[7]
Among those possessions was a leather dimensional storage bag acquired through her Vampire connections. Although small enough to carry normally, the interior contained approximately 500 square meters of storage space. June considered this capacity inadequate because her hoarding had already filled much of it. The bag was covered in unknown Runes, and June cast an Investigation Spell in an attempt to understand and reproduce the technology, but the Runic system remained beyond her ability to decipher.[7]
June eventually learned that the supposed invasion was not what it appeared to be. The Witch Queen and [[Diablo]] had arranged an artificial large-scale battle as part of their cooperation, using Demon forces against a constructed Witch city while evaluating Arcane's response. The revelation did not reassure June; it instead made her more suspicious about what Evie was planning and how deeply the Witch Queen had become involved with the Demon side of the war.[7]
June soon encountered her old friend Yue, the Titled Master Witch known as the Smile Witch. Yue immediately resumed an old habit of calling June deliberately incorrect names—first Judy, then Jonas, then Julieta—before dragging her away for a private conversation. Their exchange establishes that Yue is 165 centimeters tall and exactly five centimeters taller than June, clarifying June's height as approximately 160 centimeters. When Yue teased her about gaining weight, June indignantly replied that such a thing was impossible because she continuously used Health Magic.[1]
Yue revealed that Evie had sealed Arcane itself. Ordinary Witches could no longer leave the Realm; only the Witch Queen and her daughters retained permission to bypass the restriction. June personally tested the barrier by breaking another Witch teleportation crystal. A Magic Circle appeared beneath her as expected, but the teleportation failed, confirming that the lockdown operated at a level superior to ordinary Witch transportation Magic.[1]
When Yue asked whether June would attempt to return to her contractors and help them fight, June immediately refused. She respected [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and valued the extraordinary amount of money his faction paid her, but correctly understood that a conflict involving the Vampire King, Demon armies, Pantheons and the Witch Queen was far beyond the battlefield role of a Titled Witch such as herself. Her relationship with Victor was contractual and she saw no reason to die for a client who did not require her combat assistance.[1]
What June did demand was information. She hated being trapped inside Arcane without understanding what was happening and wanted enough knowledge to prepare for the worst possible outcome. Yue eventually showed her covert footage of an enormous stockpile containing rare items, pills, Artifacts and Divine equipment that appeared connected to Evie's dealings with Diablo. June could recognize Divine Artifacts among the collection and noticed that some objects were contaminated by Miasma, but immediately accepted Yue's conclusion that the situation belonged to powers far above them.[1]
Rather than investigate recklessly, the two Witches agreed upon the philosophy that had allowed them to survive: remain informed, maintain a backup plan and prepare for the worst possible outcome. Yue believed the resources obtained by Evie could substantially strengthen Arcane and perhaps move the Witches closer to their old dream of possessing a secure world of their own. June was willing to wait and observe rather than risk interfering with whatever larger strategy the Witch Queen was pursuing.[1]
Their conversation eventually shifted toward Victor. Yue had heard about the extraordinary appearance of the new Vampire Count and asked June for information about him. June initially refused to provide even casual personal information without payment and became suspicious that Yue might try to steal one of her most profitable contractors. She nevertheless eventually discussed Victor's eccentric personality and romantic preferences at length, demonstrating both how familiar she had become with the household and how firmly she continued treating information—even gossip about a client—as something valuable.[1]

### Skills & Abilities
June is a Titled Witch, meaning she personally earned a recognized Witch Codename from the Witch Queen. She remains below [[Poseidon|the Master]] Witch status held by Yue, but the title distinguishes her from ordinary members of her race and reflects her accumulated magical competence, professional reputation and authority within Arcane.[1]

#### Witchcraft
- Mana Manipulation: As a Witch, June uses Mana as the basis of her spellcasting through gestures, [[Magic]] Circles, enchanted objects and specialized Witch tools.
- Magic Circle Creation: Magic Circles are one of June's most commonly demonstrated methods of casting, appearing in her construction, teleportation, clothing alteration, information projection and investigative work.[2][3][4]
- Construction Magic: June can rapidly construct and remodel interior structures. During her debut she transformed an unfinished underground cavern into a furnished, reinforced mansion-like space through Magic.[2]
- Materialization/Creation Magic: Her construction work can produce or place flooring, walls, furniture, coffins and other required furnishings as part of the magical remodeling process.[2]
- Large-Scale Structural Modification: June expanded the Walker residence into a sprawling subterranean complex capable of supporting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s supernatural household.[2][10]
- Cleaning Magic: Wide-area Magic Circles allow June to clean and reorganize large sections of the underground residence extremely quickly.[10]
- Protective Magic: June can place powerful magical defenses and reinforcement upon structures. Victor's underground training room was protected strongly enough that several conventional missiles would have been required merely to scratch its defenses.[5]
- Enchantment: June can place multiple magical protections over structures and specialized rooms.[5]
- Counter-Enchantment / Enchantment Breaking: During the recovery of the Horseman laboratory, June identifies the facility's protective enchantments as the principal obstacle and is tasked with removing or destroying them so Clan Alucard can extract the contents.[6]
- Teleportation Magic: June can travel through Magic Circles and Witch teleportation methods, disappearing from one location and appearing in another. Ordinary Witch teleportation can nevertheless be overridden by superior spatial restrictions, as demonstrated when Evie sealed Arcane and June's teleportation crystal failed.[2][7][1]
- Clothing Transformation: June can use Magic Circles to transform her clothing almost instantly, changing from casual sleepwear into a professional business suit.[3]
- Magical Tailoring: She can create and adjust supernatural clothing according to a client's requirements, including Victor's early James Smith disguise.[3]
- Projection Magic: June can create large magical projections for displaying images and intelligence during reports.[4]
- Health Magic: June explicitly states that she continuously uses Health Magic to maintain her physical condition.[1]

#### Magical Construction and Engineering
Magical construction is one of June's most consistently demonstrated professional specialties. Unlike combat-oriented Witches, she repeatedly earns money by creating, modifying and protecting supernatural infrastructure.
- Walker Underground Complex: June helped transform the basement beneath [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Human home into an extensive concealed supernatural residence.[2]
- Interior Creation and Furnishing: She magically completed flooring, walls, rooms, furniture and other portions of the underground complex.[2]
- Supernatural Training Chamber: June constructed the heavily reinforced room later used by Victor to test his growing Blood and elemental abilities.[5]
- High-Level Defensive Reinforcement: The training chamber's enchantments were designed around attacks from Noble Vampires and exceeded the durability of ordinary Human military construction by an enormous margin.[5]
- Custom Supernatural Architecture: Victor later hires June to create a residence specifically suited to Roxanne's preferences as a nature-oriented Dryad and developing World Tree.[12]
- Magical Dismantling: June can reverse the normal construction role by breaking enchantments and preparing hostile supernatural facilities for systematic extraction.[6]
- Supernatural Logistics: June coordinated with Victor's maids to remove equipment, research materials, specimens and other valuable contents from the Horseman laboratory and relocate them to Clan Alucard's base.[6]

#### Investigation and Analysis
- Investigation Spell: June possesses a dedicated spell for examining unfamiliar magical objects and attempting to determine their construction.[7]
- Runic Analysis (Limited): She used Investigation [[Magic]] on the unknown Runes covering her dimensional bag in an attempt to understand and reproduce them, but failed because the Runic system exceeded her knowledge.[7]
- Artifact Recognition: June can recognize valuable supernatural equipment and identify Divine Artifacts when examining Evie's stockpile.[1]
- Energy Contamination Recognition: She notices Miasma contaminating some of the Divine equipment shown to her by Yue.[1]
- Magical Research: June independently studies useful Magic and Artifacts, including dimensional storage and Beauty Magic.[7]
June's investigative abilities have clear limits. Possessing an analysis spell does not allow her to automatically comprehend every supernatural language, Rune system or Artifact she encounters.[7]

#### Intelligence Gathering
June possesses an extensive professional network extending through Witch society and other supernatural contacts.
- Information Brokerage: June buys, sells and gathers information as part of her professional work.[4]
- Supernatural Contacts: She was able to locate the Witch contracted by [[Horseman Clan]] and purchase otherwise confidential information directly from her.[4]
- Target Investigation: June successfully gathered extensive information about Lucy, Karen and Horseman Clan.[4]
- Artifact Investigation: Her intelligence work identified the Church relic stolen by Lucy as the Finger of Saint Mary.[4]
- Operational Intelligence: She obtained Lucy's plans and delivered the information digitally to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household.[4]
- Surveillance: Her later Horseman investigation obtained a partial hidden recording concerning [[Nero Elderblood|Number Zero]] and the Clan's experiments before the surveillance method was detected and destroyed.[11]
- Professional Risk Assessment: June identifies when further investigation would require resources or danger beyond the terms of her commission and openly informs clients rather than fabricating results.[4]

#### Modern-World Networking
June maintains mundane and supernatural contacts capable of rapidly arranging difficult or illegal services within Human society. For [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] she obtained a false identity, forged identification, a Social Security number, a bank account in a tax haven and supporting records under the name James Smith, completing the process within minutes.[2]
This is not necessarily a single supernatural power. Much of June's effectiveness in the modern world comes from combining [[Magic]] with an extensive professional network and the willingness to operate outside ordinary legal systems.

#### Contract Knowledge
June possesses detailed knowledge of the customs and restrictions governing professional Witches. She explains payment requirements, contractor obligations and the rule that Witches serving opposing parties are expected to withdraw rather than fight one another directly on behalf of their clients.[3]
Her adherence to contract culture also shapes her morality. June does not equate accepting payment with becoming personally loyal to the client's wars; unless a contract explicitly requires something, she does not consider herself obligated to perform it.[1]

### Possessions

#### Golden Business Card
June gave [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] a golden professional card after formally introducing herself as the Greedy Witch.[3] The card is not merely decorative. It functions as a direct magical contact device: during the Horseman laboratory operation, Victor uses it while announcing that he has profitable work for June, and she manifests through a large [[Magic]] Circle before he can even finish explaining the job.[6]
The card therefore provides Victor with an exceptionally rapid method of contacting or summoning June when he requires her professional services.

#### Dimensional Storage Bag
June owns a leather bag whose interior possesses approximately 500 square meters of storage space despite its ordinary external size.[7] The Artifact is covered with an unfamiliar Runic system and was obtained through June's Vampire connections rather than created by June herself. She attempted to reverse-engineer it using Investigation [[Magic]] but could not decipher the Runes.[7]
June considers the storage capacity inadequate because the enormous collection of clothes, books, research material and other valuables she hoards already occupies much of the available space. The older claim that the bag contains 500 square kilometers is incorrect; Chapter 528 gives the capacity as approximately 500 square meters.[7]

#### Research Collection
June maintains a large personal archive of books, magical research, clothes, Artifacts and miscellaneous treasures inside her Arcane residence. Known research interests include dimensional storage, unknown Runes and Beauty [[Magic]].[7] She rarely discards something once she has decided it possesses value.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is one of June's longest-standing and most profitable clients. They met shortly after his transformation when Violet introduced June as the Witch responsible for remodeling the Walker residence. Victor immediately recognized the usefulness of someone capable of handling both supernatural problems and illegal mundane logistics, commissioning his James Smith identity during their first meeting.[2]
Their business relationship expanded rapidly. June created his early disguise, investigated Lucy and [[Horseman Clan]], constructed infrastructure beneath his home, helped recover valuable material from the Horseman laboratory and continued accepting specialized construction work as Victor's faction grew.[3][4][5][6][12]
June frequently tries to profit from Victor's extraordinary wealth, while Victor generally accepts the arrangement because she provides services he considers useful. Ruby eventually warns June not to exploit his willingness to spend too excessively, and June recognizes that maintaining a long-term connection with an increasingly powerful Vampire Count is more valuable than maximizing every individual invoice.[6]
June does not mistake their professional history for absolute loyalty. During the Demonic War she makes clear that she will not enter an apocalyptic conflict merely because Victor is one of her contractors. She correctly assumes that he is vastly more capable of handling such enemies himself.[1]
By Chapter 529 she knows enough about Victor's personality and household to give Yue a lengthy description of his eccentric preferences. Yue's interest also makes June immediately suspicious that another Witch might attempt to take one of her lucrative clients. Although June blushes when Victor's beauty is mentioned, no romantic relationship between June and Victor has been established; their canonical connection remains primarily professional and friendly.[1]

#### Violet Elderblood Snow
[[Violet Elderblood Snow]] was June's principal connection to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household before Victor himself entered supernatural society. Violet hired June after arriving in the United States and repeatedly used her for construction and other work that ordinary Human companies could not perform.[2]
Their relationship is familiar enough that June understands Violet's moods extremely quickly. When she attempts to overcharge Victor in Violet's presence, a threatening look is enough to reduce the price dramatically without further negotiation.[3] June nevertheless continues accepting Snow-family work because Violet and Victor's household consistently pay extremely well.

#### Natalia Elderblood
Natalia handled several of the serious financial and intelligence negotiations between June and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household. She commissioned June to investigate Lucy and accepted June's requirement that half of the payment be supplied in advance.[3]
When June returned with useful information, Natalia paid her an enormous ten-digit sum.[4] The later Horseman investigation makes clear that June is considerably more frightened of disappointing Natalia than her casual professional manner suggests; announcing that an expensive investigation apparently found nothing immediately becomes terrifying when Natalia and the other women begin reacting to the result.[11]

#### Yue
Yue, the Smile Witch, is one of June's oldest confirmed friends. Like June, Yue personally earned a Witch title from the Queen, although Yue has additionally reached the higher rank of Master Witch. Yue is 165 centimeters tall, five centimeters taller than June.[1]
Their friendship is highly informal. Yue intentionally calls June names such as Judy, Jonas and Julieta, hugs her without permission, teases her about her weight and treats herself as the older sister in their relationship. June responds with irritation and threats but clearly trusts her.[1]
Yue's genuine concern becomes apparent during Arcane's lockdown. She is relieved when June returns safely and specifically checks whether her friend intends to involve herself in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s war. Once June confirms that she has enough sense to stay away from Pantheon-level combat, Yue relaxes and shares highly sensitive information concerning Evie's activities.[1]
The two possess similar survival philosophies. Both believe Witches are frequently targeted for exploitation by stronger factions, making secrecy, contingency planning and preparing for the worst possible outcome essential to remaining alive.[1]

#### Evie Moriarty
June is a recognized Titled Witch living under the authority of Evie Moriarty, the Witch Queen of Arcane. Her title was personally granted by the Queen, establishing June as a Witch who has earned formal recognition within their civilization.[1]
June nevertheless does not possess privileged knowledge of Evie's plans. During the Demonic War she is as surprised as most other Witches when Arcane is sealed and the Queen's cooperation with [[Diablo]] becomes apparent. June respects the enormous difference between herself and Evie enough to avoid interfering once she realizes the matter involves Divine Artifacts and major supernatural factions.[1]

### Trivia
- June's official Witch Codename is The Greedy Witch.[3]
- She is a Titled Witch whose title was personally recognized by the Witch Queen, but she has not been identified as a Master Witch.[1]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] initially estimated June's height at 155–160 centimeters. Chapter 529 later places Yue at 165 centimeters and exactly five centimeters taller than June, establishing June at approximately 160 centimeters.[2][1]
- June's hair was short during her debut but is described as long by Chapter 198.[2][6]
- She originally worked for Violet before becoming one of Victor's recurring contractors.[2]
- June's early fee for her work on the Walker residence was approximately one million dollars.[2]
- She helped create Victor's false James Smith identity and produced the associated mundane documentation.[2]
- Her golden business card doubles as a rapid magical contact method rather than functioning only as a normal business card.[3][6]
- June's investigation of Lucy exposed Karen's connection to [[Horseman Clan]] and identified the stolen Church relic as the Finger of Saint Mary.[4]
- She helped uncover the earliest evidence of Horseman's hidden experiments through the recording concerning [[Nero Elderblood|Number Zero]].[11]
- During the later raid on the Horseman laboratory, June was brought in specifically to handle magical enchantments and assist with recovering valuable research and equipment.[6]
- Victor later hired her to construct a residence adapted to Roxanne after learning that Roxanne was a developing World Tree.[12]
- June is an extreme hoarder and regards approximately 500 square meters of dimensional storage as insufficient.[7]
- She keeps independent research concerning Beauty [[Magic]].[7]
- June attempted to reverse-engineer her dimensional bag but could not decipher its Runes.[7]
- She continuously uses Health Magic to maintain her physical condition.[1]
- Yue deliberately calls her Judy, Jonas and Julieta despite knowing that her name is June.[1]
- The alias Janos found in some older versions of the article appears to originate from a transcription mistake; Chapter 529 uses Jonas.[1]
- June openly prioritizes survival over contractual loyalty when a conflict exceeds what she was actually hired or capable of handling.[1]
- Despite finding Victor extremely attractive, June has never been established as one of his romantic partners; she is therefore not indexed as one of Victor's love interests.[1]
