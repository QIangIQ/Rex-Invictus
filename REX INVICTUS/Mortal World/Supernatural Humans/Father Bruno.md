### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 161
Father Bruno, designated B-002, was an Inquisition priest, Hunter and artificially created Human-Vampire Hybrid. He was one of the Church's early attempts to combine the supernatural abilities of a Human Hunter with the passive physical characteristics of a Vampire, retaining access to Faith while gaining enhanced strength, durability and regeneration.[1][2]
Bruno became directly involved in the conflict surrounding [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]]'s former mansion after [[Victor Elderblood]] massacred several of the [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] and Human authorities operating around the area. Accompanied by several subordinate priests and Hunters, Bruno confronted Victor and demonstrated sufficiently advanced Faith techniques to briefly interest the newly appointed Vampire Count.[3]
Despite Bruno's considerable power compared with ordinary Hunters and Vampires, his artificial Hybrid physiology was fundamentally unstable. Later examination identified him as experiment B-002 and revealed that the incompatible combination of Human and Vampire biology was slowly destroying his body.[2]

### Physical Appearance
Very little detailed information concerning Bruno's physical appearance is explicitly provided by the novel. He is consistently described and addressed as a priest and is seen wearing clothing appropriate to a member of the Inquisition.[3]
His Hybrid nature was not outwardly comparable to a full Vampire transformation. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] examined him, he noted that the Vampire blood incorporated into Bruno had primarily enhanced his physical abilities rather than giving him the complete supernatural characteristics of a Vampire.[1]
Following his defeat, Bruno was left without his arms and legs after Victor severed all four limbs. [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] subsequently encased his remaining body in ice for transport and interrogation.[1]

### Personality
Bruno is deeply religious and possesses genuine faith in God despite the contradictions inherent in his existence as an artificial Human-Vampire Hybrid.
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] directly questioned why Bruno believed so strongly in a God he had never personally met, Bruno answered without hesitation that faith did not require evidence and originated from the heart. His response indicates that his religious conviction was not simply an act performed for the Inquisition but something he sincerely believed.[1]
At the same time, Bruno displays the moral hypocrisy common among the more extreme members of the Inquisition. During his fight with Victor, his subordinate [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] created a massive Faith-based attack that threatened the Humans still present in the area. When Victor questioned whether Bruno was willing to kill civilians in order to defeat him, Bruno dismissed their deaths as a "necessary sacrifice".[3]
Bruno therefore placed the Inquisition's objectives and the destruction of perceived supernatural enemies above the lives of ordinary Humans supposedly protected by the Church.
He is also highly hostile toward Vampires, repeatedly calling Victor a Demon and treating the conflict in religious terms rather than acknowledging him merely as an enemy combatant.[3]
Despite this fanaticism, Bruno was not completely fearless. Once Victor entered his [[Vampire Count Form]], Bruno's Vampire-derived instincts recognized the enormous difference between them. He began instinctively backing away as Victor approached, demonstrating that the Vampire blood inside his body responded to Victor as a superior predator even when Bruno consciously attempted to continue fighting.[1]
His reaction frustrated Victor, who viewed Bruno and the other artificial Hybrids as fundamentally flawed beings. From Victor's perspective, Bruno possessed neither the complete advantages of a Vampire nor the independence required to become something genuinely new, instead functioning as an unstable experiment created by the Church.[1]

### Synopsis

#### A New Pillar
Bruno became involved in the aftermath of the Inquisition operation surrounding [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]]'s former mansion.
The original ambush had already left Kaguya critically injured and several Church agents captured or killed. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] responded by arriving at the mansion and systematically interrogating and killing the [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] responsible.[4]
The situation escalated when Human law enforcement arrived. Victor, already enraged by Kaguya's condition and the attack on his family, slaughtered the armed forces that attempted to confront him rather than maintaining supernatural secrecy.[5]
Father Bruno subsequently appeared with a separate group of Inquisition priests and Hunters and confronted Victor directly.[3]
Bruno's subordinates immediately assisted him through coordinated Faith techniques. Several Hunters entered prayer positions and created a massive golden magical circle in the sky while invoking the judgment of the Archangel Michael.[3]
The circle generated numerous enormous golden swords which descended toward Victor and the surrounding battlefield. Victor responded by creating a gigantic shield of ice to protect himself and prevent the weapons from indiscriminately striking the surrounding Humans.[3]
When Victor questioned whether Bruno intended to sacrifice civilians in the process, Bruno coldly answered that they were a "necessary sacrifice".[3]
Bruno also demonstrated his own direct control over Faith by firing golden energy toward Victor. The energy transformed into a golden sword as it approached its target. Victor simply caught the weapon despite being burned by its holy properties and subsequently covered it in fire before using it to destroy the larger golden formation in the sky.[3]
The ability surprised Bruno because Hunter weapons were inherently effective against Vampires. Victor nevertheless returned the sword and continued fighting despite the burns it caused.[3]
The battle continued while [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]], Sasha Elderblood Fulger, [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] and the others worked to erase evidence of Victor's massacre and prevent the supernatural incident from becoming public knowledge.[3][6]
Victor eventually paused the fight to question Bruno directly about his Faith. Having already encountered actual Gods through his supernatural life, Victor wanted to understand why a Human could possess absolute belief in an entity he had never personally encountered.[1]
Bruno insisted that his belief was genuine and that Faith came from the heart rather than requiring proof.[1]
Victor then examined Bruno's Hybrid nature more closely. He concluded that the Vampire blood inside Bruno had failed to produce a proper fusion. Bruno possessed regeneration and enhanced physical capabilities but lacked the more distinctive powers normally associated with Vampires.[1]
Victor further realized that Bruno's two racial components were actively incompatible and that his body was slowly collapsing because Human and Vampire blood were not naturally suited to coexist in this manner.[1]
Despite those flaws, Bruno remained extremely powerful by conventional standards. Victor estimated that Bruno could easily kill an adult Vampire approximately five hundred years old, while Bruno and his subordinate Hunters working together could potentially threaten Vampires approaching eight hundred years of age.[1]
Such strength was meaningless against Victor. Once Victor entered his [[Vampire Count Form]], the enormous increase in power immediately overwhelmed Bruno's composure. The Vampire-derived portion of Bruno's body instinctively recognized Victor's superiority and responded with fear.[1]
Bruno ordered his surviving subordinates to begin another spell, but Victor immediately generated pillars of fire and burned several Hunters to death before they could complete it.[1]
Victor prepared to kill Bruno as well, but Violet intervened and reminded him that the priest could provide information concerning the people responsible for the attack against their family.[1]
Victor accepted her reasoning but refused to leave Bruno unharmed. He used a sword of fire to sever both of Bruno's arms and both legs before dropping the mutilated priest onto the ground.[1]
Ruby then froze Bruno's entire body, preserving him for later interrogation and experimentation.[1]
The capture became a serious problem for the Church. The Inquisition had underestimated how violently Victor would respond to the operation, and Bruno's loss gave Clan Alucard possession of both another Church combatant and additional information regarding the organization's experiments.[7]
Bruno was eventually transported to Victor's underground residence and became one of the subjects examined by his household.
By Chapter 186, the Hybrid had been restrained inside a room containing extensive medical and experimental equipment. Victor found Bruno unconscious and discovered a written research report detailing his true condition.[2]
The report identified him as B-002 and confirmed that Bruno had not been born as a Hybrid. His entire genetic structure had been artificially altered and was described as being in complete chaos.[2]
Bruno himself apparently no longer remembered when he had been born, preventing even the researchers examining him from determining his age.[2]
His condition was terminal. Human blood and Vampire blood were inherently incompatible within his artificial body, meaning that the very transformation which had given Bruno his supernatural strength was slowly killing him. The report estimated that he had only a few years remaining even if nothing else killed him first.[2]
His altered biology had also rendered him completely sterile.[2]
The analysis of Bruno became one of Victor's earliest direct sources of information regarding the Inquisition's artificial Hybrid research. His existence helped confirm that someone within the Church had been deliberately attempting to create beings combining Human Faith with Vampire physiology, contributing to Victor and Ruby's eventual discovery of James' larger experimentation program.[2]
Bruno's exact death was not directly depicted in the identified chapters. Later records nevertheless classify him as deceased after his period of captivity and experimentation.

### Skills & Abilities
As the artificial Hybrid designated B-002, Bruno retained the supernatural abilities of a Human Hunter while possessing several passive Vampire characteristics. His greatest demonstrated strength was his unusually advanced control over Faith.[2]

#### Human / Hunter Abilities
- Faith Manipulation: Bruno possessed access to Faith, the supernatural power generated through Human belief. His Faith was specifically described as unusually powerful for an artificial Hybrid.[3][2]
- High-Level Faith: The research report examining B-002 explicitly classified Bruno as capable of using a high level of Faith power despite his altered Hybrid physiology.[2]
- Energy Projection: Bruno could directly fire concentrated golden Faith energy from his body.[3]
- Weapon Manifestation: Bruno could transform projected Faith energy into solid golden weapons. During his battle with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], one of his golden attacks transformed into a sword immediately before reaching its target.[3]
- Michael's Judgment: Working together with his subordinate [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]], Bruno participated in a large-scale Faith technique invoking the Archangel Michael. A massive golden magical formation appeared above the battlefield and generated numerous gigantic golden swords which rained downward upon Victor.[3]
- Coordinated Ritual Casting: Bruno was capable of leading and fighting alongside multiple subordinate Hunters whose combined prayers greatly increased the scale of the Faith techniques they could produce.[3]

#### Hybrid Abilities
- Artificial Human-Vampire Hybrid Physiology: Bruno's body had been artificially altered through the introduction of Vampire biology into a Human Hunter. Unlike naturally stable Hybrids, his genetic structure remained severely incomplete and unstable.[2]
- Superhuman Strength: The Vampire component of Bruno's body greatly enhanced his physical strength. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] judged him powerful enough to easily kill Vampires approximately five hundred years old under ordinary conditions.[1]
- Superhuman Speed: Bruno possessed physical speed vastly beyond that of ordinary Humans and could directly engage a supernatural opponent in close combat.[1]
- Superhuman Durability: Bruno inherited increased physical resistance through his Vampire physiology, allowing him to survive injuries that would be immediately fatal to an ordinary Human.[1][2]
- Regeneration: Bruno possessed supernatural regenerative abilities derived from his Vampire blood. The ability was identified as one of the passive Vampire characteristics successfully reproduced within B-002.[1][2]
- Enhanced Physical Attributes: Victor determined that Bruno's Vampire blood primarily served to amplify his physical characteristics rather than granting him the more specialized racial abilities of a genuine Vampire.[1]
- Hunter Power Immunity: Bruno displayed an unusual immunity to the supernatural powers normally used by [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] against Vampires. The researchers examining him were unable to determine the origin of this resistance and specifically noted that further study was required.[2]

### Weaknesses
- Unstable Hybrid Physiology: Bruno's greatest weakness was the incompatibility between his Human and Vampire components. His genetic structure was described as chaotic, and his body was continuously deteriorating because the two types of blood could not naturally coexist.[2]
- Limited Lifespan: Even without suffering additional injuries, Bruno was expected to survive only a few more years because his Hybrid physiology was gradually killing him.[2]
- Sterility: Bruno's artificial combination of two incompatible races rendered his reproductive system completely infertile.[2]
- Fire Weakness: Bruno inherited the Vampire weakness to fire. His research report specifically identified fire as one of the effective methods of damaging B-002.[2]
- Incomplete Vampire Abilities: Bruno could not utilize the active or specialized powers normally associated with Vampires. His Vampire side provided only passive benefits such as increased strength, durability and regeneration.[2]
- Predatory Hierarchy Instinct: Bruno's Vampire-derived physiology instinctively reacted with fear when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] entered his [[Vampire Count Form]], recognizing the overwhelming superiority of the higher-ranking Vampire even when Bruno consciously attempted to continue resisting.[1]

### Trivia
- Bruno's experimental designation was B-002.[2]
- Unlike naturally born Human-Vampire Hybrids, Bruno was explicitly described as an artificial Hybrid.[2]
- Bruno's biological age is unknown because he himself apparently could not remember when he was born.[2]
- Despite being part Vampire, Bruno could still use extremely powerful Human Faith.[3][2]
- His Vampire characteristics were almost entirely passive. He possessed enhanced physical attributes and regeneration but could not access the distinctive active powers of a genuine Vampire.[2]
- Bruno was unusually immune to Hunter power despite possessing Vampire blood. Even the people studying him did not understand why this immunity existed.[2]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] considered Bruno dangerous by conventional standards and estimated that he could easily kill a roughly five-hundred-year-old Vampire. Bruno and his subordinate [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] together could potentially threaten significantly older Vampires.[1]
- The Michael's Judgment attack was not purely Bruno's individual ability. It was a coordinated technique involving the prayers and Faith of his subordinate Hunters, so it should not be treated as a completely independent personal spell.[3]
- The earlier claims that Bruno could bench press exactly five tons, possessed bullet-timing speed, had a special psychic shield, or displayed glowing red eyes beneath black glasses are not supported by the identified novel chapters and have therefore been removed.
- Bruno did not lead the original assault that critically injured Kaguya. His direct confrontation with Victor occurred afterward, during the Church's attempt to deal with the consequences of the failed operation.[8]
- His exact death has not been shown in the chapters currently identified, although later character records classify him as deceased.
