### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Vanessa was resurrected by [[Victor Elderblood]] as a reward for [[Valeria Alekerth]] for her loyalty and all the work she put into for leading the [[Blood God Religion]]. Victor traded 1000 supernatural souls for her positive and negative aspect of her soul from [[The Judges of The Abyss]].[2] She is also aware and retain her past memories of the fact that she had died previously and how she died and was resurrected by Victor.

### Trivia
- Even though Vanessa is a 'superior human', this is not to be confused with the actual 'High Human' variant (Alexios, Liena & Andrew), high humans are the true version of Humans originated from the Progenitor of Humans, Adam.
