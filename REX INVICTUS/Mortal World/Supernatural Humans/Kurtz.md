Kurtz is a supporting antagonist in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]] and one of the Generals of the Inquisition. He originally served directly beneath Pope Alexander alongside James and Leonardo, occupying one of the highest positions available to a Human Hunter within the organization.[1]
Among the Inquisition's senior members, Kurtz represents its most uncompromising ideological extreme. He possesses an intense belief in the supposed "purification" of Humanity's enemies, interpreting that purification as the extermination of supernatural races rather than merely hunting individual supernatural beings who threaten Humans.[2]
Although Kurtz possesses the authority and supernatural power expected from an Inquisition General, relatively little of his personal fighting style has been shown directly. His characterization instead emphasizes his absolute obedience to the Church, fanaticism toward its original anti-supernatural doctrine and willingness to follow its hierarchy even when personally afraid of the beings confronting it.[1][2]

### Physical description
Kurtz is a Human man standing approximately 180 cm tall. He has brown hair and brown eyes and is generally seen wearing the white priestly robes associated with the upper ranks of the Vatican.[1]
Although Kurtz physically resembles a man approximately twenty-five years old, this is not his actual chronological age. When Mizuki observed Kurtz, James and Leonardo together, she was already aware that the senior [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] were considerably older than their youthful appearances suggested.[1]
Unlike Leonardo, whose appearance becomes dramatically altered by the golden manifestations of his Faith, no distinct combat transformation or specialized equipment has yet been directly described for Kurtz.

### Personality and traits
Kurtz is an extremely disciplined, emotionless-seeming and obedient individual. He commonly speaks in a neutral tone and rarely questions direct orders from those he recognizes as legitimate superiors. When Alexander summoned him and ordered the activation of a Level 6 alarm, Kurtz did not ask why the order had been issued; he merely asked which alert level was required and immediately prepared to carry it out.[1]
His outward composure should not be confused with an absence of fear. When Alexander confirmed that [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] had arrived at the Vatican, Kurtz remained outwardly neutral, but Alexander could tell that the General was actively concealing his fear. Rather than flee or protest, Kurtz continued following orders, demonstrating considerable discipline even when confronted by an enemy he understood to be vastly beyond him.[1]
Kurtz also displays the attitude of an experienced senior Hunter. Before leaving to confront [[Scathach]], he specifically warned Mizuki not to interfere in the coming confrontation, addressing her as "Young one". Mizuki was not offended by the comment because she understood that Kurtz and the other senior members of the Inquisition were substantially older than their appearances suggested.[1]
His defining characteristic, however, is his fanaticism. Mizuki personally regarded Kurtz as a psychopath and remembered him as an individual who sincerely believed that "purifying" the world was necessary for Humanity's good. In Kurtz's interpretation, purification meant eliminating supernatural beings as a whole rather than distinguishing between hostile and peaceful individuals.[2]
This places Kurtz ideologically closer to the extremist elements of the old Inquisition than figures such as Mizuki. While Mizuki's hatred of Vampires originated from personal trauma and eventually became more nuanced through her relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and other supernatural beings, Kurtz appears to have internalized the Inquisition's institutional doctrine to such an extent that extermination itself became a righteous objective in his mind.[2]
Despite his fanaticism, Kurtz is not characterized as loud or emotionally unstable. His behavior is normally restrained and controlled, making the extremity of his beliefs more disturbing: he discusses dangerous situations and follows orders with the same neutral demeanor regardless of what those orders may ultimately require.[1]

### Synopsis

#### The Awakening
Kurtz first appeared at the headquarters of the Inquisition shortly after [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] arrived above Vatican territory in retaliation for the attack upon her daughter, [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]]. Realizing the severity of the situation, Pope Alexander personally summoned Kurtz to his office.[1]
Kurtz entered respectfully and addressed Alexander as "[[Alexander (MTWABV)|Your Holiness]]". Alexander immediately ordered him to raise the Vatican's alarm, prompting Kurtz to calmly ask what level of alert was required. When Alexander answered Level 6, Kurtz immediately understood who was approaching and asked whether the "demon" was coming.[1]
Although Kurtz maintained his characteristic neutral tone, Alexander could see that he was suppressing fear. [[Scathach]]'s reputation was already well established among the Inquisition, and even its highest-ranking [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] understood that confronting her was fundamentally different from fighting an ordinary Vampire.[1]
Scathach's arrival caused an enormous impact around the Vatican as she descended into the area and created an ice throne while waiting for Alexander. As Alexander and the Generals prepared to confront her, Kurtz warned Mizuki not to become involved, calling her a "Young one". Mizuki accepted the warning without complaint because she knew the apparently youthful Generals were far older than they looked.[1]
Kurtz subsequently accompanied Alexander and the other senior Hunters during the confrontation with Scathach, though he did not personally attempt the kind of reckless provocation displayed by Leonardo.

#### Demonic War
Kurtz later participated in the conflict surrounding the Vatican during the large-scale Demonic War. While the battlefield became increasingly chaotic through the intervention of Demons, [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]], Angels and [[Victor Elderblood]], Kurtz was severely injured at some point during the fighting.[2]
Following Victor's intervention and the near-collapse of cooperation between the Angels and the Inquisition, Mizuki infiltrated deeper into the Vatican in search of useful information. While passing through one of its medical areas, she discovered Kurtz lying in a bed with much of his body covered in bandages.[2]
Mizuki immediately recognized him as General Kurtz. Her first instinct was to question whether she should simply kill him, remembering him as an extremist whose definition of purification involved exterminating supernatural races for what he considered the benefit of Humanity.[2]
After consulting [[Abe-no-Seimei]], Mizuki ultimately decided that Kurtz remained potentially useful. Rather than killing him, she produced a black talisman and summoned a Youkai known as a Jorōgumo.[2]
Mizuki ordered the Jorōgumo to mark Kurtz as her target and turn him into its prisoner. The spider-woman climbed onto Kurtz's bed, forced open his mouth and released a small spider which crawled directly inside him. Although Kurtz's wounded body began struggling against the process, the Youkai physically restrained him until the parasite entered successfully.[2]
The technique effectively placed the injured General under Mizuki's control and gave her a method of using one of the Inquisition's highest-ranking surviving members as a captive asset rather than immediately executing him.[2]
Kurtz's ultimate activities following this incident have not been explored in comparable detail, though he survived the events surrounding the Vatican and was not shown being killed during the Demonic War.

### Skills & Abilities
As one of the Generals of the Inquisition, Kurtz is a highly experienced Hunter and practitioner of Faith. However, unlike Leonardo or Mizuki, Kurtz has not yet received a detailed on-screen battle demonstrating specialized techniques unique to him. Consequently, abilities belonging generally to high-ranking [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] should not automatically be treated as individually demonstrated Kurtz techniques.
- Faith Manipulation: Kurtz possesses access to Faith, the supernatural energy utilized by the Hunters of the Inquisition. Faith is generated through powerful belief and can strengthen Humans sufficiently to fight supernatural beings. Kurtz is specifically recognized as one of its users.[3]
- Enhanced Physical Attributes: As a high-ranking Hunter and Inquisition General, Kurtz possesses physical capabilities significantly exceeding those of an ordinary Human through the reinforcement provided by Faith. Hunters can use Faith to enhance their strength, speed and general physical condition sufficiently to confront weaker supernatural beings.[4]
- Enhanced Longevity: High-level Hunters can maintain their bodies far beyond normal Human lifespans through supernatural means associated with Faith. Kurtz physically resembles a man of approximately twenty-five despite being explicitly understood by Mizuki to be considerably older than he appears.[1][5]
- Supernatural Awareness: Kurtz possesses extensive experience identifying and evaluating supernatural threats. Upon hearing that Alexander had ordered a Level 6 alert, he immediately inferred that [[Scathach]] was approaching before Alexander explicitly confirmed it.[1]
- Hunter Training: As one of Alexander's Generals, Kurtz possesses the extensive training, experience and knowledge expected from the highest operational rank of the Inquisition beneath its Pope. The exact weapons and personal combat disciplines used by Kurtz have not yet been directly demonstrated.
- Strong Willpower and Discipline: Kurtz was capable of suppressing visible panic even when confronted with Scathach's arrival, maintaining his duties despite understanding the overwhelming difference in power between them.[1]

### Trivia
- Kurtz is one of the three Generals introduced alongside Alexander in Chapter 30, the others being James and Leonardo.[1]
- Although he appears approximately twenty-five years old, Mizuki explicitly knew that Kurtz and the other senior [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] were older than their physical appearances suggested.[1]
- Kurtz concealed his fear when [[Scathach]] arrived at the Vatican, but Alexander was able to notice it despite his otherwise neutral demeanor.[1]
- Unlike Leonardo, who openly provoked Scathach and nearly had his head removed, Kurtz did not attempt to antagonize her during their first shown encounter.
- Mizuki personally considered Kurtz a psychopath because of his belief that supernatural races should be "purified" for Humanity's benefit.[2]
- Kurtz survived the Demonic War's Vatican conflict despite sustaining injuries serious enough to leave him heavily bandaged in the medical wing.[2]
- Rather than killing Kurtz when she found him vulnerable, Mizuki used a Jorōgumo talisman to mark him as a prisoner and implant a controlling spider inside his body.[2]
