### Synopsis

#### Demon Wars
Jonah is revealed to be a traitor, working with the demons. He ambushes Ariel, shooting her with a specially crafted Hell’s Metal bullet, which leaves a severe wound in her stomach. Instead of showing remorse, Jonah taunts the angels, mockingly asking how it feels to be harmed by a “mere ant.”
Despite his arrogance, Jonah underestimates how quickly the angels would react. [[Zaniel]] immediately kills him, slicing him in half before he can give any further information. His betrayal deeply shakes Leonardo, who cannot believe that someone from the Inquisition would side with Demons​.[1]
