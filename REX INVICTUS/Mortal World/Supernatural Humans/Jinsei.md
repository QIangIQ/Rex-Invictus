Jinsei is a Human Witch and one of [[Johnny Lykos]]' long-term lovers and companions. She is a member of Johnny's unconventional household alongside Judy, Jessica Horseman and, formerly, Roberta.[1][2] Although only a minor character, Jinsei possesses a distinctive form of [[Magic]] capable of influencing another person's emotional state through her voice, accompanied by her normally sapphire-blue eyes turning gold with reptilian slit pupils.[1]
Jinsei is notably hostile toward Vampires as a race, to the point that accepting Jessica—a Noble Vampire—as another member of Johnny's household was initially difficult for her. She nevertheless suppressed that hostility over time for Johnny's sake and eventually became capable of treating Jessica as part of their unusual family rather than an enemy.[2]

### Physical Appearance
Jinsei is a tall, voluptuous woman standing approximately 177 centimeters tall. She possesses chocolate-colored skin, long straight white hair, sapphire-blue eyes and a curvaceous physique with a particularly prominent bust described as G-cup.[1]
Her most unusual physical characteristic appears when she uses [[Magic]]. Jinsei's ordinary sapphire-blue eyes transform into golden eyes with reptilian slit pupils. The transformation accompanies the magical influence she demonstrates through her voice when calming Johnny.[1] Despite the reptilian appearance of her magical eyes, Jinsei has never been confirmed to possess Dragon ancestry or Draconic physiology; the novel only directly demonstrates the altered eyes as part of her magical activation.

### Personality
Jinsei is generally composed, affectionate and considerably calmer than Johnny. During her debut, she becomes concerned when Johnny begins beating another patron badly enough that he might kill him and immediately responds when Madam asks her to intervene. Rather than confronting Johnny physically, Jinsei uses [[Magic]] and her relationship with him to redirect his attention and end the violence.[1]
She appears genuinely affectionate toward Johnny. When she magically draws his attention away from the fight, the two immediately greet one another warmly and Jinsei admits that she missed him. She is nevertheless willing to establish personal boundaries, becoming annoyed and telling Johnny to stop when he becomes overly physical with her in public.[1]
Jinsei also tends to act as one of the more level-headed women within Johnny's household. She gently criticizes Judy for refusing to be honest about her feelings and later explains Jessica's behavior to Johnny when the Noble Vampire's prejudices and emotions cause tension between them.[1][2]
Her attitude toward Vampires is much harsher. Jinsei openly hates Vampires in general and originally struggled to coexist with Jessica because Jessica was a Noble Vampire. Continuous conflict between the two women eventually diminished because Johnny disliked seeing his lovers fight, and Jinsei gradually learned to accept Jessica personally despite retaining her broader hostility toward the species.[2]
Jinsei is also perceptive concerning the prejudices of supernatural races. When Jessica reacts badly to the possibility of producing a Hybrid child with Johnny, Jinsei understands that Jessica's reaction is connected to the deeply ingrained Vampiric hatred of Hybrids rather than simply a lack of affection for Johnny. She compares this prejudice with similar attitudes held by older [[Werewolves]], demonstrating that she is familiar with the social tensions between major supernatural races.[2]

### Synopsis

#### Vampire Society
Jinsei is first mentioned shortly before her proper debut when Johnny returns to [[The Lost Club]]. Because Johnny is notorious for causing trouble, Madam considers calling Jinsei, Judy, Jessica or Roberta to calm him before another incident occurs.[3]
She appears personally in Chapter 59 while Johnny is violently beating a patron inside the club. Jinsei, described as a chocolate-skinned woman with sapphire-blue eyes and long straight white hair, worries that Johnny will kill the man if he continues. Madam consequently asks Jinsei to stop him rather than allowing another corpse to be created inside the establishment.[1]
Jinsei closes her eyes briefly before reopening them with a dramatic magical change: her sapphire eyes become gold with reptilian slit pupils. She then addresses Johnny with a magically seductive voice. The effect is immediate; Johnny stops attacking the patron, turns toward Jinsei and his predatory expression softens considerably. Jinsei successfully draws him away from the confrontation without needing to fight him physically.[1]
Their interaction also establishes Jinsei as one of Johnny's genuine lovers. Johnny embraces her and tells her that he missed her, while Jinsei reciprocates his affection before objecting when he becomes too physically familiar in public. Unlike the later revelations surrounding Roberta, there is no indication in these scenes that Jinsei's relationship with Johnny is an artificial memory.[1]
Jinsei is also shown to be comfortable around Johnny's other women. When Judy becomes irritated during a conversation concerning Johnny's intervention in her past, Jinsei lightly asks why she cannot simply be more honest about her feelings. Judy responds by giving Jinsei the middle finger, suggesting an established and informal relationship between them rather than genuine hostility.[1]
When Johnny decides to visit Roberta upstairs, Jinsei accompanies him alongside Jessica and Judy. The following discussion reveals the complicated balance existing within Johnny's household. Jessica informs them that her brothers are working with Lucy and that members of [[Horseman Clan]] have entered the area for a larger operation. Jinsei reacts particularly negatively to the mention of Vampires.[2]
The narration explains that Jinsei hates Vampires. Her relationship with Jessica was originally difficult because Jessica is a Noble Vampire, and the two women frequently fought. Over time Jinsei learned to tolerate and eventually accept Jessica because Johnny disliked their constant conflict. Her accommodation is therefore personal rather than a reversal of her general prejudice; Jinsei continues to dislike other Vampires.[2]
Jessica eventually reveals that Horseman forces are targeting a man named [[Adam William Lykos]]. Johnny shocks Jinsei and the other women by revealing that Adam is his father, information he had apparently never shared with them. Jessica explains that her family wants the Blood of an Alpha Werewolf as part of its experiments, making the situation immediately personal for Johnny.[2]
The conversation becomes increasingly uncomfortable when Jessica refers contemptuously to Hybrids before realizing that any child she produced with Johnny would itself be a Vampire-Werewolf Hybrid. Embarrassed and conflicted, Jessica warns Johnny not to involve himself recklessly before leaving the room.[2]
Afterward, Jinsei helps Johnny interpret Jessica's reaction. She explains that Jessica is a Noble Vampire and that deeply ingrained hatred of Hybrids is part of the culture and instincts she was raised around. Being emotionally involved with a Werewolf and potentially producing a Hybrid child therefore creates a contradiction Jessica cannot simply discard overnight. Jinsei compares this with similar prejudices among older [[Werewolves]] and implicitly encourages Johnny to understand the problem instead of treating Jessica's behavior as proof that she does not care about him.[2]

#### A New Pillar
By the time [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later personally visits [[The Lost Club]], Jinsei remains part of Johnny's household. Johnny specifically notes that Judy and Jinsei had already gone home before his encounter with Victor, while he remained at the club waiting for Roberta.[4]
Jinsei consequently does not personally participate in Victor's confrontation with Johnny and the mystery surrounding Roberta. Her name nevertheless becomes relevant when everyone attempts to reconstruct Roberta's history. Eddy remembers Roberta appearing around the same period as Jinsei and initially believes that Roberta had arrived together with her, only to discover that neither he, Johnny nor Esther can form a reliable memory of how Roberta actually entered their lives.[5]
Victor eventually determines that the memories surrounding Roberta are abnormal. Johnny's supposed relationship with Roberta and even the Werewolf "mark" associated with her begin disappearing once the mysterious influence surrounding Roberta is disrupted.[6] This revelation distinguishes Roberta's artificial place in Johnny's household from Jinsei's normally demonstrated relationship with him.

### Skills & Abilities
Jinsei is a Witch and therefore possesses the supernatural ability to perform [[Magic]]. Unlike more extensively explored Witches such as Albedo, Evie, June or Esther, the full breadth of Jinsei's magical knowledge has never been demonstrated. Her confirmed abilities should therefore be limited to what she actually performs on-page.

#### Magic
- [[Magic]]: Jinsei is explicitly identified as a Witch and demonstrates supernatural spell use during her debut.[1]
- Magical Eye Transformation: When activating her demonstrated Magic, Jinsei's sapphire-blue eyes transform into gold with vertically slit, reptilian pupils.[1]
- Voice Manipulation: Jinsei can channel Magic through her voice. When she calls to Johnny, the narration explicitly describes her seductive voice traveling toward him and visibly affecting him.[1]
- Magical Suggestion: Jinsei's voice can redirect another person's attention and behavior. Johnny immediately abandons the man he was beating after Jinsei activates her Magic and calls him over.[1]
- Emotion Manipulation / Pacification: Johnny's expression changes from predatory aggression to a gentle demeanor almost immediately after Jinsei's magical intervention, allowing her to peacefully end a potentially lethal confrontation.[1]
- De-escalation: Jinsei combines her Magic with her personal familiarity with Johnny to calm him without direct violence.[1]
The scene does not demonstrate unrestricted Mind Control. Johnny is already Jinsei's lover and willingly interacts with her once his attention is redirected. The safest classification of the feat is magical influence, suggestion and emotional pacification rather than complete control over another person's mind.[1]

#### Supernatural Knowledge
- Vampire Knowledge: Jinsei is familiar with the cultural and instinctive prejudices found among Noble Vampires, particularly their hatred of Hybrids.[2]
- Werewolf Knowledge: Through living with Johnny and his Pack, she understands that comparable prejudices also exist among older [[Werewolves]].[2]
- Hybrid Knowledge: Jinsei understands the social consequences surrounding relationships between incompatible supernatural species and immediately recognizes the emotional conflict Jessica experiences over the possibility of producing a Hybrid child with Johnny.[2]
- Emotional Insight: Jinsei is capable of interpreting Jessica's hostility more accurately than Johnny, explaining that Jessica's behavior results from ingrained racial beliefs conflicting with her feelings for him.[2]

### Relationships

#### Johnny Lykos
[[Johnny Lykos]] is Jinsei's lover and the central member of the household with which she is associated. Their relationship is openly affectionate rather than merely professional. During Jinsei's debut, Johnny abandons a violent confrontation after she calls him, immediately embraces her and tells her that he missed her; Jinsei responds that she missed him as well.[1]
Jinsei appears unusually capable of handling Johnny's impulsive temperament. Madam specifically asks her to intervene when Johnny is about to kill another patron, and Jinsei's [[Magic]] combined with their relationship is enough to calm him almost immediately.[1]
She does not behave submissively toward him. Jinsei openly establishes boundaries when Johnny becomes overly physical in public and is willing to explain uncomfortable truths concerning his relationships. After Jessica leaves following the discussion of Hybrids, Jinsei explains the psychological conflict behind Jessica's actions rather than simply agreeing with Johnny.[1][2]
Jinsei is also willing to coexist with Johnny's other lovers despite her own prejudices. Her eventual acceptance of Jessica is specifically attributed to Johnny disliking the constant fighting between them.[2]

#### Jessica Horseman
Jessica Horseman originally had a difficult relationship with Jinsei because Jessica was a Noble Vampire and Jinsei strongly disliked Vampires. The two women frequently fought before gradually learning to coexist, largely because Johnny disliked the hostility inside his household.[2]
Jinsei eventually comes to understand Jessica well enough to explain her behavior to Johnny. After Jessica reacts badly to the subject of Hybrids, Jinsei recognizes that Jessica's upbringing and Vampiric instincts conflict with her genuine affection for Johnny. Jinsei therefore does not interpret Jessica's reaction as simple rejection and encourages a more nuanced understanding of her situation.[2]
This represents a significant exception to Jinsei's general anti-Vampire attitude: although she continues hating Vampires broadly, she has learned to accept Jessica as an individual.[2]

#### Judy
Judy is another of Johnny's lovers and appears to have a familiar, informal relationship with Jinsei. Jinsei gently teases her for refusing to be honest about her feelings toward Johnny, to which Judy responds with an obscene gesture rather than genuine anger.[1]
Jinsei, Judy and Jessica are later established as genuine long-term members of Johnny's household, unlike Roberta's relationship with him, which was eventually revealed to have been supported by manipulated memories.[5][6]

#### Roberta Elderblood
Roberta was believed to be another member of Johnny's group and regularly interacted with Jinsei, Judy and Jessica.[1][2] Jinsei initially treats her as naturally as the rest of the household, with no indication that she realizes anything supernatural is wrong with Roberta's history.
Later events reveal that memories surrounding Roberta had been manipulated. Eddy vaguely remembers that Roberta appeared together with Jinsei but cannot remember when or how this actually happened, while Johnny and Esther suffer similar gaps in memory.[5] [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s investigation ultimately exposes the abnormal influence surrounding Roberta and causes the false portions of Johnny's relationship with her to disappear.[6]
There is no evidence that Jinsei knowingly participated in Roberta's manipulation.

#### Esther
Madam knows Jinsei through [[The Lost Club]] and appears sufficiently familiar with her abilities to call upon her when Johnny becomes violent. Rather than sending security or attempting to restrain the Alpha Werewolf herself, Esther specifically asks Jinsei to calm him, and Jinsei succeeds immediately.[1]

### Trivia
- Jinsei is first mentioned in Chapter 58 when Madam considers which of Johnny's women could calm him, but physically debuts in Chapter 59.[3][1]
- Her normal eyes are sapphire blue, but they become gold with reptilian slit pupils when she activates her demonstrated [[Magic]].[1]
- Despite this reptilian appearance, Jinsei has never been confirmed to possess Dragon ancestry or actual Draconic physiology.
- Her confirmed demonstrated Magic revolves around magically influencing and pacifying Johnny through her voice.[1]
- Jinsei is one of the few people capable of stopping an enraged Johnny without physically fighting him.[1]
- She strongly dislikes Vampires but eventually learned to accept Jessica personally because of their shared relationship with Johnny.[2]
- Jinsei understands that anti-Hybrid prejudice is not exclusive to Vampires and notes that older [[Werewolves]] possess comparable attitudes.[2]
- By Chapter 206, Jinsei and Judy still remain associated with Johnny, although both had already returned home before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s confrontation with him at [[The Lost Club]].[4]
- The previous version of this article stated that Jinsei was formerly enslaved by a Vampire and rescued by Johnny. The Chapter 59 scene commonly used to support that statement actually identifies Judy as the woman Johnny rescued from a degrading market; Jinsei only comments on Judy's reluctance to admit her feelings.[1] No equivalent enslavement backstory for Jinsei is directly established in the cited chapters.
- The previous claim that Jinsei was specifically "marked" by Johnny is also not directly demonstrated in her available scenes. Chapter 208 discusses Johnny's supposed mark on Roberta, which disappears when the manipulated memories surrounding Roberta collapse.[6] Until a direct chapter statement concerning Jinsei's mark is located, it should not be treated as confirmed.
- Unlike Roberta's false relationship with Johnny, Jinsei's affection and interactions with him are shown directly without being identified as part of Roberta's memory manipulation.[1][5][6]
