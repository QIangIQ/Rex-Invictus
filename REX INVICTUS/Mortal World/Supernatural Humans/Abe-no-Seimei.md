Abe-no-Seimei is a legendary Japanese Onmyō master, the longtime master of [[Mizuki Elderblood]], and the former Heroic Spirit who resided within her. During his original Human life, Seimei became one of the greatest practitioners of Onmyōjutsu in Japan and developed a long-standing relationship of both friendship and rivalry with fellow Onmyō master [[Ashiya Doman]]. Even after his physical death, he survived as an exceptionally powerful spirit and continued practicing, teaching and developing the mystical arts.[2][1]
Seimei eventually encountered the young Aikyo Mizuki after she escaped the Noble Vampires who had enslaved her. Recognizing a level of talent in Onmyōjutsu that could eventually exceed even his own and Dōman's, he accepted her as his disciple and remained beside her for the following century, guiding her from an abused Human child into one of the most powerful Onmyō practitioners of the modern era.[3][4]
He initially existed in the modern era as a Heroic Spirit closely connected to Mizuki, frequently lending her knowledge and power during battle while continuing to function as her teacher. Through Mizuki's eventual relationship with [[Victor Elderblood]], Seimei became progressively more involved with Victor's faction and later Dragons Nest.[5][6]
Much later, Victor offered Seimei an opportunity to transcend his existence as a Spirit. Although Seimei was satisfied with his condition and had no great ambitions for greater power, he expressed the desire to continue observing Victor and discover what kind of civilization the Emperor would create over immense spans of time. Victor consequently recreated him as a biologically immortal High Human and strengthened his mind so that he could survive an effectively endless lifespan without the psychological deterioration normal Humans would experience.[7]

### Physical Appearance
As a Heroic Spirit, Abe-no-Seimei appeared as an elderly Japanese man with white hair. His body possessed the translucent quality characteristic of a manifested spirit, and he commonly carried a traditional folding fan bearing a pentagram-like symbol associated with his Onmyō arts.[2][6]
Seimei had actually gone bald during his original Human life. When Mizuki mocked him as a "bald old man", Seimei instinctively touched the white hair of his spiritual body and expressed relief that becoming a Spirit had restored the hair he had lost due to stress during his lifetime.[1]
His Spirit form retained the appearance of an old man even though he was capable of manifesting independently around Mizuki. When appearing to others he was visibly transparent, making his non-physical nature immediately apparent.[6]
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] reconstructed him as a High Human, Seimei acquired a fully living physical body once again. His new body was noticeably younger than his spiritual manifestation, possessing the appearance of a middle-aged man rather than an elderly one. Victor additionally granted the body biological immortality, eliminating natural aging as a limitation upon his future existence.[7]

### Personality
Abe-no-Seimei is an experienced, pragmatic and exceptionally patient teacher whose personality frequently alternates between the dignity expected of an ancient master and the exasperation produced by his disciples. He commonly calls Mizuki his "foolish disciple" and has little hesitation about lecturing her whenever he believes she has acted irresponsibly.[2][1]
Despite his constant criticism, Seimei possesses deep paternal affection and pride toward Mizuki. His reprimands generally originate from concern for her survival rather than a desire to control her. During her first confrontation with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], he immediately criticized her for recklessly entering a battle in foreign territory without first properly understanding her opponent, reinforcing the principle that information and preparation were essential to survival.[2]
He is similarly willing to acknowledge when his disciple has surpassed him. During the war at [[WarFall]], Seimei watched Mizuki sustain an extremely advanced Onmyō spell for more than thirty minutes and privately admitted that even at the peak of his own power he could only have maintained something comparable for approximately one minute. Rather than being jealous, he was openly proud that Mizuki could reach heights beyond his own.[6]
Seimei possesses a notably pragmatic philosophy concerning magic. He rejects the idea that a school of Onmyōjutsu can inherently be classified as righteous or evil, arguing that magic is fundamentally a tool and that morality originates from the intentions of the individual using it. This outlook allowed him to encourage Mizuki to study the more violent enhancement techniques associated with his friend and rival [[Ashiya Doman]] even though she was initially reluctant to practice them.[1]
He also dislikes excessive dependence upon external blessings. Although Mizuki's Onmyōjutsu frequently invokes the Shinto Gods, Seimei repeatedly taught her that power obtained too easily could disappear just as easily. Following [[Inari-Omikami]]'s temporary death, he used her weakened blessings as another example of why Mizuki needed to develop her own foundations rather than becoming completely dependent upon Divine support.[1]
Seimei's worldview became increasingly cynical regarding supposedly righteous institutions. He originally believed the Inquisition was genuinely dedicated to protecting Humanity and had even encouraged Mizuki to expand her horizons beyond Japan after they destroyed the Noble Vampire families responsible for her past suffering. Discovering that [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] were experimenting upon Humans disillusioned him severely, and he openly rejected Alexander as a legitimate Pope, describing him instead as a man whose ideals had become distorted.[1]
Nevertheless, Seimei is not generally vindictive. When Mizuki encountered the critically wounded Kurtz during the Demonic War and considered killing him, Seimei simply told her that the choice belonged to her. He did not attempt to impose his own judgment and had become accustomed to allowing his adult disciple to determine her own course.[8]
This attitude also governs his response to Mizuki's personal relationships. Seimei recognized that she had lived long enough to make decisions concerning romance and deliberately refused to interfere excessively in her developing connection with Victor. Although Victor's overwhelming nature occasionally gave the old Spirit an existential crisis, Seimei gradually came to trust him because of the positive changes he observed in Mizuki.[1]
He displays the same attitude toward Mizuki's decision to abandon her Human race and become a Blood Dragon. Seimei did not approve of the decision and preferred that she remain Human, but ultimately accepted that Mizuki was an adult capable of determining her own future.[9]
Over time, Seimei developed genuine respect for Victor. By Chapter 1081, his principal ambition was no longer personal power or advancement. He simply wanted to continue watching Victor's journey and see whether the Emperor would remain the same person after thousands, millions or potentially even greater spans of time. When Victor later granted him immortality and asked him to provide advice if he ever strayed from his path, Seimei agreed to do so out of gratitude for the man who had helped his disciple escape her former suffering.[7]

### Synopsis

#### The Awakening
Abe-no-Seimei was first referenced during Mizuki's battle against the newly transformed [[Victor Elderblood]]. Recognizing that Victor's abnormal Vampire transformation made him dangerous despite his lack of combat experience, Mizuki used one of her talismans and invoked Seimei directly, requesting that he lend her his wisdom to defeat the Oni.[5]
The talisman dissolved into blue light which enveloped Mizuki's body and caused a magical circle to appear within her eyes. Empowered by Seimei's knowledge and her own Onmyōjutsu, Mizuki successfully struck Victor with an attack capable of inflicting extraordinary pain and weakening the fire surrounding his Vampire transformation. The technique became one of Victor's earliest experiences with supernatural attacks specifically designed to harm beings such as Vampires.[5]
Seimei manifested directly shortly afterward. His appearance immediately attracted Kaguya's attention, and the pentagram symbol upon his fan allowed her to recognize the elderly Spirit as the legendary Abe-no-Seimei.[2]
Rather than congratulating Mizuki for nearly defeating Victor, Seimei immediately began lecturing her. He criticized her for traveling into unfamiliar territory and engaging an unknown enemy without properly investigating him first, regarding her recklessness as evidence that she still required considerably more training.[2]
When Victor's wives successfully escaped with him, Mizuki complained that Seimei's interference had prevented her from killing them. Seimei instead praised her eventual decision to retreat and privately reflected upon the much larger danger surrounding the conflict. Despite having fought countless Oni during his Human life and remaining extremely powerful even as a Spirit, Seimei was deeply wary of [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], whom he referred to internally as an Akuma. Learning that [[Scathach]] remained alive and had produced a daughter made the ancient exorcist pessimistically wonder what kind of era they had entered.[2]

#### A New Pillar
Seimei remained beside Mizuki while she interacted more frequently with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his Human friends.
When Fred became interested in acquiring supernatural powers but discovered that the restrictive lifestyle of the Inquisition did not appeal to him, Seimei suggested an alternative: Fred could become an Onmyō Mage instead.[10]
Seimei immediately recognized considerable potential within Fred and predicted that he could eventually become an excellent practitioner. Because Onmyō Mages were not bound by the same restrictions imposed upon Church [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]], the path also suited Fred considerably better than formal membership in the Inquisition.[10]
His enthusiasm for the idea quickly became part of the group's usual comedy when Victor supported the proposal for considerably less scholarly reasons, particularly after realizing that Fred becoming an Onmyō practitioner might eventually give him access to Mizuki and Seimei's techniques. Seimei nevertheless remained serious regarding Fred's potential and effectively became another teacher connected to Victor's Human circle.[10]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Following the exposure of the Inquisition's Human experimentation and other corruption, Seimei attempted to help Mizuki process the collapse of an institution she had once believed protected Humanity.
He admitted that Mizuki was not alone in having been deceived. Seimei himself had believed the public image presented by the Inquisition and therefore refused to hypocritically condemn his disciple for failing to discover what was occurring behind the organization.[1]
Seimei was especially contemptuous of Alexander. When Mizuki referred to Alexander as the Pope, Seimei immediately rejected the title, declaring that a man so distorted by his ideals did not qualify to hold such a position.[1]
Their conversation also revealed more about Seimei's philosophy as an Onmyō master. He encouraged Mizuki to expand her training into techniques created by his old friend and rival [[Ashiya Doman]]. When Mizuki expressed reluctance because Doman's branch of Onmyōjutsu possessed a more violent reputation, Seimei explained that there was no inherently good or evil Onmyōjutsu; magic itself was merely a tool whose morality depended upon the person wielding it.[1]
He further explained that the Faith-based techniques Mizuki already used represented only one branch of the much broader system of Onmyōjutsu. Because [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s blood had restored Mizuki's body to its physical prime, Seimei concluded that she was finally capable of enduring the extensive additional training necessary to study Doman's methods.[1]
Seimei personally examined the biological effects Victor's blood had produced within Mizuki and demonstrated that even something as simple as one of her hairs had become abnormally resistant to fire. Rather than reacting with hostility toward Victor, Seimei was fascinated, recognizing that the Vampire Progenitor's blood effectively functioned as something resembling a fountain of youth for a Human compatible with it.[1]
He ultimately promised to teach Mizuki everything he could, while warning that his training would become even stricter now that her rejuvenated body was capable of handling it.[1]

#### Demonic War
Seimei continued accompanying Mizuki after she separated herself from the Inquisition and aligned increasingly closely with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction.
During the conflict at the Vatican, Mizuki discovered the heavily injured General Kurtz. She briefly considered killing him because of his genocidal beliefs toward supernatural races. Seimei responded that the decision belonged entirely to her, while also observing that Victor would probably give Mizuki the same answer.[8]
Mizuki ultimately decided Kurtz was still useful and used a Jorōgumo talisman to turn him into a controlled prisoner. Seimei watched his disciple's increasingly ruthless behavior with visible unease, privately recognizing how much more merciless she had become whenever matters involving Victor were concerned.[8]
Later, during the enormous battle at [[WarFall]], Seimei appeared openly beside the combatants while Mizuki used her Onmyō arts against the invading monsters. Mizuki invoked Susanoo, generated a localized storm, manipulated enormous quantities of water and created eight twenty-meter samurai-like constructs through her technique Wrath of the Heavens.[6]
When the warriors observing the battle struggled to understand how her spell worked, Seimei explained it himself. Mizuki was using an Odachi lent to her by [[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]] as a magical catalyst rather than merely a conventional weapon. Talismans covering the blade allowed the characteristics carried by the sword to spread through the spells she channeled through it.[6]
Watching Mizuki maintain the enormous technique filled Seimei with pride. He admitted privately that even at his own peak he could only have sustained comparable magic for approximately one minute, whereas Mizuki was capable of maintaining it for more than half an hour.[6]
Seimei emphasized that Mizuki's achievement was not representative of ordinary Onmyō Mages. Her power had become anomalous even by the standards of their tradition, and the only other practitioner Seimei personally remembered being capable of similar feats was [[Ashiya Doman]].[6]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
By the time Dragons Nest had become the center of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s growing civilization, Mizuki began seriously considering Victor's offer to transform her into a Blood Dragon.
Unlike Haruna, who initially rejected changing her race because of pride in her Nine-Tailed Fox lineage, Mizuki saw obvious practical advantages in becoming a Dragon. The transformation would provide an overwhelmingly superior body and talent for Energy manipulation without preventing her from continuing to use the Onmyō techniques she had spent her life mastering.[9]
Seimei did not approve of the decision as readily as Mizuki did. Having trained and accompanied her throughout most of her Human life, he preferred that his disciple remain Human. Nevertheless, Mizuki understood that her master could not dictate her future indefinitely, and Seimei ultimately respected the fact that she was old enough to make her own decisions.[9]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
The history of Seimei and Mizuki was explored in considerably greater detail after Mizuki became part of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family.
As a child, Mizuki had been kidnapped and enslaved by Japanese Noble Vampires after witnessing the destruction of her family. She eventually escaped while gravely injured and collapsed before a temple associated with one of Abe-no-Seimei's descendants, where Seimei's Spirit was resting.[3][4]
Seimei recognized extraordinary natural talent within the child. Mizuki's affinity toward the mystical arts was so exceptional that he considered her potential in Onmyōjutsu superior even to his own and that of [[Ashiya Doman]]. He accepted her as his disciple and began the training that would define the remainder of her Human life.[4]
Mizuki absorbed his teachings at an extraordinary pace. As she matured and discovered the connection between the Noble Vampire families and the suffering of her childhood, Seimei helped provide the knowledge and training through which she eventually became powerful enough to eradicate the Vampire families responsible.[4]
Despite possessing the ability to exist independently as a Heroic Spirit, Seimei continued remaining with Mizuki through the centuries, acting not merely as a source of supernatural power but as her teacher, adviser and closest link to the Onmyō tradition.[3]
Much later, as Victor expanded his Imperial Domain, Seimei had become one of the individuals directly operating within Dragons Nest. Victor summoned Seimei alongside [[Oda Blank]], [[Hassan-i-Sabbah]] and [[Yggdrasil]] while reorganizing elements of the Empire, but deliberately asked Seimei to remain behind after the others departed.[7]
Victor asked whether Seimei was genuinely satisfied remaining a Spirit. Seimei answered that he was. His ambition was not to acquire greater power; he simply wanted to observe the civilization Victor would create and discover whether the man who had risen from an ordinary Vampire into the Emperor he now knew would remain fundamentally the same after an immense passage of time.[7]
Victor pointed out that even a powerful Spirit could not necessarily survive the millions of years required to answer that question. Without further ceremony, he gave Seimei life again.[7]
Seimei immediately realized that he possessed a living physical body and had become noticeably younger. Victor explained that he had transformed the ancient Spirit into a High Human, granted him biological immortality and strengthened his mind so that an effectively endless lifespan would not destroy his psyche.[7]
When Seimei questioned why Victor had done this, Victor told him to continue watching. He also asked the old master to speak to him if he ever changed so drastically that he lost sight of the person he currently was.[7]
Seimei initially joked that Victor already possessed enough wives to correct him whenever necessary, but ultimately agreed. He considered it the least he could do for the man who had helped Mizuki escape the miserable condition that had defined much of her earlier life.[7]
Seimei therefore ceased being a Heroic Spirit and began a new life as a biologically immortal High Human within Victor's Empire.

### Trivia
- Seimei was already more than 500 years old by Chapter 366.[1]
- He had gone bald during his original Human life. Becoming a Spirit restored his hair, which appears white in his Heroic Spirit form.[1]
- According to Seimei, his baldness was caused by stress associated with a certain red-haired woman, though her identity was not revealed in the chapter.[1]
- [[Ashiya Doman]] was simultaneously Seimei's friend and rival. Despite the differences between their respective styles of Onmyōjutsu, Seimei respected Doman's techniques enough to insist that Mizuki study them.[1]
- Seimei considers the classification of magic itself as "good" or "evil" fundamentally incorrect. In his view, magic is merely a tool and responsibility belongs to its user.[1]
- Although Mizuki frequently invokes Shinto Gods through Onmyōjutsu, Seimei repeatedly taught her not to become excessively dependent upon Divine blessings.[1]
- Following [[Inari-Omikami]]'s death, Seimei estimated that it would take the Goddess thousands of years to awaken again.[1]
- Seimei once recommended that [[Frederick Winter]] become an Onmyō Mage after recognizing considerable potential within him.[10]
- During the [[WarFall]] battle, Seimei stated that Mizuki's level of Onmyōjutsu had exceeded his own peak in at least sustained magical output. A technique he believed he could have maintained for roughly a minute was being maintained by Mizuki for more than half an hour.[6]
- Despite initially disapproving of Mizuki's decision to become a Blood Dragon, Seimei did not attempt to forcibly stop her because he recognized that she was capable of making her own decisions.[9]
- As a Heroic Spirit, Seimei was satisfied with remaining a Spirit and did not personally request resurrection. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] independently chose to reincarnate him because Seimei wished to observe the future of his Empire over extremely long periods of time.[7]
- Victor transformed Seimei into a High Human with biological immortality and increased mental fortitude, allowing him to survive enormous spans of time without suffering the psychological problems an ordinary Human would experience.[7]

#### Skills & Abilities
Abe-no-Seimei is one of the most accomplished Onmyo Mages shown in the series and was considered one of the great masters of Onmyōjutsu alongside [[Ashiya Doman]]. His knowledge encompasses considerably more than the Faith-based branch commonly used by modern practitioners, and even centuries after his death he remained capable of teaching, analyzing and transmitting Onmyō techniques as a Heroic Spirit.[1][2]
Following his rebirth, Seimei additionally possesses the physiology of a High Human, biological immortality and enhanced mental fortitude granted directly by [[Victor Elderblood]].[3]

##### Onmyōjutsu
- Onmyōjutsu Mastery: Seimei is a legendary master of Onmyojutsu, having spent centuries studying and practicing the mystical arts. His knowledge is extensive enough that he trained [[Mizuki Elderblood]] from childhood and continued teaching her even after becoming a Heroic Spirit. Seimei explained that the Faith-oriented techniques Mizuki originally practiced represented only one branch among the numerous fields contained within Onmyōjutsu.[4][1]
- Faith Manipulation: Like other Human Onmyō practitioners, Seimei possesses an extensive understanding of Faith and the supernatural phenomena produced through absolute belief. He explained that sufficiently intense and unwavering faith allows Humans to access a supernatural "Source" capable of producing phenomena that bend ordinary reality.[4]
- Reality Alteration (Through Faith): Seimei explained that Faith cultivated to an extreme level grants access to the Source and allows its user to produce phenomena capable of bending reality. This is the underlying principle responsible for many Onmyō and Hunter techniques rather than a separate unrestricted reality-warping ability.[4]
- Talisman Arts: Seimei possesses expert knowledge concerning the creation, use and application of magical talismans. Talismans are among the fundamental tools of Onmyōjutsu and can function as catalysts for invocation, empowerment, barriers and other supernatural effects.[5][6]
- Magical Catalyst Usage: Seimei possesses advanced knowledge regarding the use of physical objects as magical catalysts. He explained how Mizuki could cover Eleanor's Odachi with talismans and use the weapon similarly to a mage's staff, allowing properties carried by the blade to propagate throughout the spells channeled through it.[2]
- Protective Arts: Seimei possesses knowledge of defensive Onmyō techniques. When Mizuki created the Shisa-based protective field around [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ritual chamber, Seimei identified the spell as a successful combination of his own Arts and those belonging to [[Ashiya Doman]].[6]
- Barrier Creation: Seimei's branch of Onmyōjutsu includes methods capable of creating supernatural defensive barriers. Mizuki used techniques originating partly from Seimei's Arts to cover an enclosed room with a red protective force field.[6]
- Enhancement Techniques: Seimei possesses extensive knowledge of magical enhancement techniques and was capable of instructing Mizuki in additional forms of physical and supernatural reinforcement. He also understood the enhancement-oriented Arts of Ashiya Doman sufficiently well to incorporate them into Mizuki's continued training.[1]
- Multiple Branches of Onmyōjutsu: Seimei's knowledge is not restricted to the Shinto Faith-based Onmyōjutsu most frequently demonstrated by Mizuki. He explicitly explained that this was only one field within a much larger system of mystical study.[1]
- Mysticism Knowledge: Seimei considers magic a tool for understanding mysticism and possesses centuries of accumulated theoretical knowledge concerning supernatural phenomena, Faith, Spirits, Gods, Youkai and magical techniques.[1]

##### Wisdom and Power Bestowal
- Wisdom Bestowal: Seimei can temporarily lend his knowledge and supernatural insight to Mizuki through a specialized talisman. During Mizuki's first battle with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], she invoked Seimei by asking him to "lend [her] his wisdom to defeat this Oni". The talisman dissolved into blue light which enveloped her body, while magical circles appeared within her eyes and her combat capability increased dramatically.[5]
- Supernatural Empowerment: Through the same invocation, Seimei was capable of empowering Mizuki sufficiently for her to seriously injure the newly transformed Victor despite the significant difference between him and ordinary Vampires.[5]
- Knowledge Transfer: Seimei's connection with Mizuki allows her to access supernatural knowledge accumulated by him. His invocation in Chapter 28 did not merely supply raw Energy but specifically granted her his wisdom, enabling her to better confront an unfamiliar supernatural opponent.[5]
- Magical Instruction: Seimei is an exceptional teacher capable of transmitting complex magical techniques to others. Mizuki eventually became powerful enough to surpass Seimei's own peak sustained magical output, something he openly regarded with pride rather than jealousy.[2]

##### Former Spirit Abilities
The following abilities belonged to Seimei while he existed as a Heroic Spirit.
- Heroic Spirit Physiology: Following his original Human death, Seimei continued existing as a powerful Heroic Spirit for centuries. He retained his identity, memories, magical knowledge and ability to interact with living individuals.[7][3]
- Spiritual Manifestation: Seimei could emerge from Mizuki and visibly manifest his spiritual body in the physical world. His form appeared translucent and could be perceived by supernatural beings around her.[7][2]
- Independent Spiritual Existence: Although Seimei commonly remained within or near Mizuki, he was not completely dependent upon her body for conscious existence. His Spirit form could manifest independently and interact with other people.[7]
- Spiritual Longevity: Seimei survived for centuries after the death of his Human body as a Heroic Spirit. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later explained that even a Spirit as powerful as Seimei would not naturally survive the millions of years Seimei wanted to observe, which motivated his eventual transformation into a High Human.[3]
- Telepathic Communication: Seimei could communicate directly with Mizuki without needing to speak aloud. Mizuki frequently heard her master's voice within her mind while Seimei remained in his spiritual state.[7][6]
- Spiritual Concealment: Seimei could remain unmanifested within Mizuki rather than continuously maintaining a visible form, only emerging physically when he chose to interact directly with others.[4]

##### High Human Abilities
After Chapter 1081, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] recreated Seimei as a living High Human.[3]
- High Human Physiology: Victor transformed Seimei from a Heroic Spirit into a living High Human, granting him a true biological body once again.[3]
- Biological [[Immortality]]: Victor explicitly granted Seimei biological immortality. His new body no longer ages toward natural death, allowing him to pursue his desire to observe Victor's Empire across extremely long periods of time.[3]
- Enhanced Mental Fortitude: Victor strengthened Seimei's mind alongside his immortal body. The enhancement was specifically intended to prevent the psychological deterioration that could occur when a Human consciousness experiences thousands, millions or potentially greater spans of time.[3]
- Agelessness: As a consequence of his biological immortality, Seimei's High Human body is no longer subject to ordinary age-related deterioration.[3]
- Reincarnated Physical Body: Seimei's transformation returned him from a nonphysical spiritual existence to a fully living body. His new form appeared middle-aged rather than elderly and no longer possessed the translucent characteristics of his former Spirit body.[3]

##### Intelligence & Skills
- Genius Intelligence: Seimei is one of the greatest mystical scholars produced by Japan. His knowledge encompasses Onmyōjutsu, Faith, spiritual phenomena, supernatural races, Gods, Youkai and numerous branches of magic developed over centuries of study.[4][1]
- Master Onmyō Mage: Seimei reached a level of magical proficiency regarded as exceptional even among Onmyō practitioners. He stated that at his peak he was capable of maintaining a highly advanced large-scale spell comparable to one later used by Mizuki, although only for approximately one minute.[2]
- Supernatural Analysis: Seimei can quickly analyze magical techniques and explain their underlying mechanisms. He accurately identified how Mizuki's catalyst weapon interacted with her talismans and spells during the battle at [[WarFall]].[2]
- Supernatural Knowledge: Seimei possesses extensive knowledge concerning Vampires, Youkai, Gods, Spirits and magical systems accumulated throughout more than five centuries of existence.[7][1]
- Teaching Mastery: Seimei successfully trained Mizuki from childhood into a practitioner who eventually surpassed his own peak magical output. He also recognized [[Frederick Winter]]'s potential and offered Onmyōjutsu as a possible supernatural path for him.[8][2]

#### Limitations
- Inferior Sustained Output to Modern Mizuki: Although Seimei was an extraordinarily powerful Onmyō master at his peak, he explicitly acknowledged that Mizuki eventually surpassed him in sustained magical output. A spell he believed he could maintain for approximately one minute could be sustained by Mizuki for more than thirty minutes after her later enhancements.[2]
- Former Spirit Lifespan: Heroic Spirits are extremely long-lived but not truly eternal. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] stated that Seimei's former Spirit existence would eventually expire and could not naturally survive the millions of years he wished to observe.[3]
