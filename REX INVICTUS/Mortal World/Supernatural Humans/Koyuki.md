Koyuki was a Human captive who lived alongside the young Aikyo Mizuki within a mansion belonging to Japanese Vampires near Kyoto. Despite presenting himself as a kind and comforting fellow victim, Koyuki secretly cooperated with Mizuki's captors and betrayed her when she attempted to escape. He survived her escape and was eventually transformed into a Vampire Slave, but was later killed by Mizuki after she became an accomplished Onmyō practitioner and returned to destroy the Vampires responsible for her childhood suffering.[1][2]

### Physical Appearance
Koyuki was male but possessed an unusually androgynous appearance. When Mizuki first knew him among the mansion's captives, he was described as a beautiful boy whose appearance and presence were comforting to the other children. Despite his delicate features, he possessed a visibly muscular physique. By the time of Mizuki's attempted escape three years later, Koyuki had developed into an androgynous young adult while retaining the pleasant appearance that helped conceal his true character.[1][2]
Koyuki commonly presented himself with a gentle smile and approachable demeanor. This outward appearance was one of the principal reasons the young Mizuki failed to recognize him as dangerous. When he finally betrayed her, that friendly expression briefly disappeared and exposed the cold individual hidden beneath it before he resumed his familiar smile.[2]
His appearance after being transformed into a Vampire Slave was not described in detail.

### Personality
Koyuki's defining characteristic was the enormous disparity between the person he presented to others and his actual behavior. Among the children imprisoned within the Vampire mansion, he appeared gentle, dependable and reassuring. His appearance and demeanor gave the captives a sense of comfort within an environment dominated by fear, and Mizuki consequently came to regard him as a genuine friend.[1]
That kindness was ultimately a carefully maintained mask. Koyuki understood how to make himself appear harmless while concealing predatory intentions from someone considerably younger than himself. When Mizuki entrusted him with the escape plan she had spent years developing, he accepted without revealing any objection and allowed her to believe they would flee together. He instead waited until she was vulnerable before attacking her from behind.[2]
Koyuki was also disturbingly willing to cooperate with the very Vampires who had imprisoned him. Mizuki later described him as both a victim and one of the monsters. His own victimization did not prevent him from exploiting another captive; rather than helping the nine-year-old girl escape, he had negotiated with their captors for the opportunity to abuse her himself. When confronted after betraying her, he displayed no meaningful remorse for what he had done.[2]
His actions became one of the most important lessons of Mizuki's childhood. The Vampires surrounding her were openly monstrous and therefore comparatively easy to identify as threats, while Koyuki demonstrated that cruelty could hide behind kindness, beauty and familiarity. Mizuki's failure to recognize his deception became a mistake she never forgot.[2]

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Koyuki's story is revealed when [[Mizuki Elderblood]] brought [[Victor Elderblood]] to an abandoned mansion hidden in the mountains near Kyoto. The location was connected to Mizuki's childhood and to the hatred of Vampires that eventually drove her to become one of Japan's greatest supernatural hunters.[1]
When Mizuki was six years old, a Vampire Slave attacked her family, murdered her father and brutalized her mother before taking Mizuki away. She was brought to an old mansion controlled by Japanese Vampires, where she discovered numerous other Human children who had similarly been reduced to possessions. The Vampires treated the captives as livestock and entertainment, allowing them to grow until they reached an age suitable for their captors' purposes before consuming or abusing them.[1]
Among these captives was Koyuki. Although male, Koyuki possessed such beautiful and androgynous features that his appearance could initially make his gender difficult to determine. He was muscular despite his beautiful appearance and possessed a comforting presence that made the children around him feel comparatively safe. Mizuki consequently became close to him and regarded him as her friend during the years they spent imprisoned together.[1]
The young Mizuki survived by observing everything around her. The Vampires saw no reason to conceal their habits from children they regarded merely as pets, allowing Mizuki to memorize their sleeping schedules, personalities, routines and reactions. She learned when individual Vampires became angry, when they were distracted and how security around the mansion changed according to the presence of its Noble Vampire master.[2]
After approximately three years of captivity, Mizuki understood that she was running out of time. Despite being only nine years old, her abnormal development and unusual physical potential were making her increasingly conspicuous. She had already watched other children disappear as they grew older and understood that remaining within the mansion would eventually result in the same fate.[1][2]
Mizuki eventually discovered what she believed to be a reliable opening in the Vampires' defenses. At the beginning of each month, the Noble Vampire routinely left the mansion on Friday night. His absence caused the remaining Vampires to become less disciplined and concentrate upon their own activities, producing the best opportunity Mizuki had ever found to escape.[2]
Rather than flee alone, Mizuki made what she later considered one of the greatest mistakes of her childhood. She told Koyuki everything. She explained the Vampires' routines, the weakness in their defenses and the precise time at which she intended to leave, then asked the person she considered her friend to escape with her. Koyuki smiled and agreed.[2]
Mizuki failed to recognize what Koyuki had hidden throughout their relationship. Although he had himself been one of the mansion's victims, Koyuki had become willing to participate in the predators' behavior. His kindness and charm concealed someone capable of exploiting the trust of the other captives while remaining close to the Vampires controlling them.[2]
When the time came for Mizuki's escape, Koyuki attacked her from behind with a knife. The strike produced a serious wound through her abdomen before she even understood that the attack had come from the person she trusted. Mizuki initially could not comprehend why her friend had betrayed her.[2]
Koyuki then revealed that his interest in Mizuki had never been innocent. Rather than wishing to escape with her, he had already approached the Vampires and requested that he be allowed to personally abuse her before they did. The revelation finally destroyed the false image Mizuki had maintained of him and taught her that the most dangerous monster in the mansion had not necessarily been the one openly displaying fangs.[2]
Koyuki nevertheless underestimated Mizuki as completely as the Vampires had. Mizuki had been favored by supernatural forces long before she consciously understood them, and the terror and desperation produced by his betrayal caused her dormant connection to Faith to manifest instinctively. Despite possessing no talisman and having received no formal instruction, she released golden supernatural power and blasted Koyuki away from her.[2]
Already grievously wounded, Mizuki used the opening to run. Her abnormal physical potential allowed the nine-year-old child to continue moving through the dense forest despite an injury that should have incapacitated an ordinary Human. She eventually collapsed near a temple connected to the descendants of [[Abe-no-Seimei]], placing her on the path that would eventually transform her into an extraordinary Onmyō practitioner and Vampire hunter.[2]
Koyuki remained behind. At some unknown point following Mizuki's escape, he was transformed from a Human into a Vampire Slave. The circumstances surrounding that transformation are never explained, leaving it unknown whether he requested the change, was rewarded by the Vampires for his cooperation or was transformed for another reason.[2]
Mizuki never forgot what happened inside the mansion. Under the guidance of Abe-no-Seimei, she developed an extraordinary talent for Onmyō techniques and investigated the supernatural society responsible for her childhood. After reaching adulthood and becoming sufficiently powerful, she returned against the Japanese Vampire forces connected to her suffering.[2]
Koyuki was among those she killed. By this point he had become a Vampire Slave, placing him among the same type of supernatural creatures that had once imprisoned them both. Mizuki exterminated the Vampire family responsible for the mansion and personally ensured that Koyuki did not survive her vengeance. The exact technique she used to kill him is never shown or described.[2]
His death closed one of the earliest chapters of Mizuki's life, but the betrayal itself continued shaping her long afterward. Her experiences with Koyuki and the Vampires were fundamental to the hatred that eventually made Mizuki one of Japan's most dangerous Vampire hunters and explain why her later ability to accept individual Vampires such as Victor represented such an enormous change in her worldview.[2]

### Skills & Abilities

#### Human
- Deception: Koyuki successfully maintained the image of a kind and trustworthy fellow captive for years. Even Mizuki, who had become exceptionally observant after studying every behavior of the Vampires around her, failed to recognize his true intentions until he directly betrayed her.[1][2]
- Social Manipulation: His appearance, gentle behavior and comforting presence allowed him to gain the trust of the mansion's younger captives. He exploited Mizuki's trust sufficiently that she revealed the entirety of her carefully prepared escape plan to him without suspecting that he would inform or cooperate with their captors.[1][2]
- Acting: Koyuki was capable of concealing his intentions behind a convincing friendly persona. He agreed to Mizuki's plan while behaving normally enough that she noticed nothing suspicious before his attack.[2]
- Stealth: Koyuki successfully approached and attacked Mizuki from behind before she could react, despite Mizuki already possessing unusually sharp observational abilities from years spent studying her captors.[2]
- Weapon Usage: He used a knife to ambush Mizuki and inflicted a severe abdominal wound with a single surprise attack.[2]
- Physical Fitness: Even before becoming a Vampire, Koyuki was explicitly described as muscular despite his delicate and androgynous appearance.[1]

#### Vampire
- Vampiric Physiology: Koyuki was eventually transformed into a Plebeian Vampire and specifically identified as a Vampire Slave by the time Mizuki returned as an adult. His transformation would have removed him from ordinary Human physiology, but no individual post-transformation combat feats or unique Vampiric abilities were demonstrated before Mizuki killed him.[2]
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: As a Vampire Slave, Koyuki possessed the enhanced physiology associated with his race. The exact extent of his strength, speed, durability and regeneration is unknown because his confrontation with the adult Mizuki was not shown in detail.[2]

### Weaknesses
- Overconfidence: Koyuki regarded the nine-year-old Mizuki as sufficiently helpless that he attacked her without anticipating any meaningful supernatural resistance. He was completely unprepared when her dormant Faith manifested and blasted him away.[2]
- Limited Demonstrated Power: Although he later became a Vampire Slave, Koyuki has no meaningful post-transformation feats. He should not be scaled to Noble Vampires or the more powerful Japanese Vampires Mizuki later fought solely because he belonged to their household.[2]

### Trivia
- Koyuki was originally a Human captive just like Mizuki. He only became a Vampire Slave sometime after her escape.[2]
- When Mizuki first knew him, his comforting presence made him one of the easiest people in the mansion for the other children to trust.[1]
- By the time of Mizuki's attempted escape, Koyuki was described as an androgynous young adult.[2]
- Koyuki's betrayal occurred when Mizuki was approximately nine years old, around three years after she had first been abducted.[1][2]
- His exact age is unknown, although he was considerably more physically developed than Mizuki during her childhood.
- The circumstances of Koyuki's transformation into a Vampire Slave are never explained.[2]
- Although Mizuki eventually killed him, the exact method of Koyuki's death was never revealed.[2]
- Koyuki is an important example behind Mizuki's belief that appearances could be deceptive. The openly predatory Vampires were obvious enemies, while the person she trusted as another victim proved capable of comparable cruelty.[2]
