Leonardo is a high-ranking Human Hunter and one of the Generals of the Inquisition, serving beneath Alexander alongside James and Kurtz. Although initially introduced as a comparatively unimpressive Hunter from the perspective of ancient beings such as [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], Leonardo later demonstrated himself to be an exceptionally powerful practitioner of Faith, capable of fighting without incantations, constructing independent bodies from divine energy, manifesting weapons and armor, regenerating severe damage through those manifestations and directly confronting [[Victor Elderblood]].[1][2][3]
Unlike James, whose principal strengths revolve around experimentation and preparation, Leonardo is primarily a frontline combatant. His Faith manifests as an enormous quantity of golden energy that he can shape into bodies, wings, armor, shields and bladed weapons. His belief is unusually internalized compared with [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] who depend upon prayers or lengthy incantations; Leonardo explicitly stated that his Faith came directly from his heart and therefore required no chant to invoke.[2][3]

### Physical description
Leonardo is a white-skinned man standing approximately 185 cm tall. He possesses short red hair and striking bright-blue eyes. Despite his true age being unknown, Leonardo physically resembles a man approximately twenty-five years old, sharing the unnatural youthfulness displayed by several of the Inquisition's highest-ranking [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]].[1]
During the Demonic War, Leonardo was additionally seen wearing heavy armor resembling that of a paladin. Combined with his red hair, blue eyes and stern expression, the armor gave him a far more martial appearance than the ordinary priestly clothing associated with many members of the Inquisition.[4]
When utilizing large quantities of Faith, Leonardo's body becomes surrounded by brilliant golden energy. He can manifest two enormous golden wings from his back, golden armor around his body and a pair of golden swords in his hands. His artificial manifestations possess an unusual internal structure in which ordinary flesh, blood and organs are replaced by concentrated golden energy.[2][3][5]

### Personality and traits
Leonardo is confident, proud and strongly devoted to Humanity and the Inquisition. During his earliest appearance, this confidence bordered on arrogance. He casually commented upon [[Scathach]]'s beauty despite knowing her reputation and initially behaved as though his position as a General afforded him a degree of security. That confidence immediately collapsed when Scathach directed her killing intent toward him, demonstrating that Leonardo is capable of recognizing overwhelming danger despite his pride.[1]
Like many [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]], Leonardo possesses a deeply ingrained distrust and hostility toward supernatural beings, particularly Vampires. He considers individuals such as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] dangerous creatures that should have been eliminated before they were allowed to grow beyond Humanity's ability to control them.[4] His prejudice is not entirely absolute, however. When he arrived in Japan and discovered S-000 and A-000 in danger, he immediately attempted to save them because he considered them companions. Learning that they were artificial Hybrids visibly disturbed him, but he did not simply attack or abandon them upon discovering their nature.[2]
Leonardo is markedly more straightforward than either James or Alexander. Rather than constructing elaborate experiments or operating exclusively through political manipulation, he is comfortable directly entering combat and confronting enemies personally. At the same time, he is not recklessly suicidal. His experience with Scathach and Victor taught him to recognize when an opponent exists beyond his current ability, and he obeyed James' order to withdraw after realizing that continuing his fight against Victor would accomplish nothing.[5]
His defining characteristic is his extraordinary personal Faith. Leonardo does not regard his supernatural strength as something produced primarily by ritualistic words or external religious ceremonies. When Victor commented that Hunters normally relied upon incantations, Leonardo rejected the comparison and pointed directly toward his own chest, declaring that his Faith originated from his heart. So long as he continued breathing, he considered his devotion to God unshakeable.[2][3]
Leonardo is also intensely competitive and possesses considerable pride in Humanity's potential. Victor's continuous growth deeply frustrates him not merely because Victor is an enemy, but because Leonardo despises the disparity between what exceptional supernatural beings can accomplish and what even Humanity's strongest representatives struggle to match. During the Demonic War, he openly wondered why Humans could not become monsters comparable to Victor.[4]
Despite this frustration, Leonardo is capable of acknowledging strength honestly. After Victor destroyed half of his manifested body in Japan, Leonardo did not attempt to deny the difference between them. Instead, he directly asked whether Victor had fought at full strength and, after receiving his answer, acknowledged him as a monster before withdrawing.[5]
He also possesses a strong sense of responsibility toward those under his command. During the Vatican conflict, Leonardo immediately attempted to preserve the alliance between Humanity and the Angels after his own assistant Jonah betrayed them and critically injured Ariel. Rather than distancing himself from the incident, Leonardo attempted to explain the situation and prevent the actions of one infiltrator from causing open conflict between the two allied forces.[6][7]

### Synopsis

#### The Awakening
Leonardo first appeared at the Vatican as one of the three principal Generals serving under Pope Alexander, alongside James and Kurtz. When [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] personally invaded Vatican airspace in retaliation for the attack upon [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]], Alexander ordered his Generals to prepare for battle.[1]
Unlike James, who immediately treated [[Scathach]]'s presence as a serious problem, Leonardo initially responded with arrogance. After seeing her, he openly commented upon her beauty and lamented that she was a "demon." His attitude disappeared the moment Scathach focused her killing intent upon him. Leonardo immediately became terrified, and Scathach lost whatever interest she might have possessed in evaluating him, regarding the General as an insect beneath her attention.[1]
When Leonardo attempted to object to her dismissive treatment, Scathach called him a "dog" and ordered him to remain silent while she spoke to Alexander. Leonardo attempted to respond anyway, prompting Scathach to move instantly and attempt to sever his head. He survived only because Alexander intercepted her wrist before the attack connected.[1]
The experience left Leonardo sweating and unable to meaningfully respond. Although he internally insulted Scathach, he understood that openly provoking her again could result in his immediate death. He subsequently remained present while Scathach and Alexander discussed the consequences of the Inquisition's attack on Ruby.[1]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Leonardo re-entered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s story during the crisis surrounding Ophis and [[Nero Elderblood]] in Japan. James, attempting to recover the artificial Hybrids S-000 and A-000 before Victor or [[Scathach]] captured them, contacted Leonardo and presented Victor's massacre as an uncontrolled supernatural attack. Leonardo immediately agreed to intervene.[2]
Scathach noticed Leonardo approaching through the sky and was surprised to see him, remembering that she had apparently killed the man at some point in the past and briefly wondering whether he had somehow been revived. The precise circumstances surrounding this earlier incident were not shown.[2]
Leonardo entered the battlefield at enormous speed and struck Victor directly in the face. The resulting golden explosion destroyed the surrounding structure and vaporized the nearby corpses while leaving the individuals Leonardo intended to protect unharmed, demonstrating considerable control over the destructive effects of his Faith.[2]
Victor blocked the blow and immediately noticed something abnormal about Leonardo. Through his supernatural vision he saw an enormous pillar of golden light emerging from Leonardo's body and discovered that the man's internal structure appeared to consist almost entirely of energy rather than ordinary biological organs.[2]
Additional Leonardos then appeared around the battlefield. Victor initially questioned whether they were twins before recognizing them as clones. One manifestation attempted to remove S-000 and A-000 from the battlefield while the others distracted Victor. Leonardo stated that he was rescuing them because they were his companions, only learning from Victor during the confrontation that the two [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] were actually Hybrids created through James' experiments.[2]
Although visibly disturbed by this revelation, Leonardo still prepared to intervene when Victor captured them. He stopped only after simultaneously feeling the attention of both Victor and Scathach, understanding instinctively that attempting to force the issue would result in his death.[2]
Victor then became interested in Leonardo's Faith after noticing that the General manifested his abilities without prayers or incantations. Leonardo dismissed ordinary Hunters who depended upon such methods and explained that he required no chant because his Faith came directly from his heart.[2][3]
Their confrontation escalated into a full aerial battle. Victor tore the heart from one Leonardo, but the body simply collapsed and disappeared into golden light. Another manifestation immediately attacked him, demonstrating that destroying an individual Leonardo did not necessarily defeat the true General.[3]
Leonardo manifested two golden swords and a pair of golden wings before attacking Victor through high-speed aerial combat. He also created a large golden shield capable of blocking Victor's elemental greatsword and generated additional clones to attack from several directions simultaneously.[3]
One clone detonated its own body in concentrated golden power after Victor grabbed it. The explosion completely destroyed Victor's hand and temporarily slowed his normally extreme regeneration, demonstrating the effectiveness of Faith against Vampiric physiology.[3]
Leonardo subsequently used golden energy to restore another damaged manifestation. He continued producing shields and blades while Victor progressively increased his speed. When Victor's modified Fulger Count Form became too fast for Leonardo to perceive properly, Leonardo concentrated his energy defensively and repeatedly generated shields in an attempt to survive the assault.[3]
After Victor cut through one of his barriers, Leonardo sacrificed an arm to prevent the strike from becoming fatal. He then regenerated the missing limb within moments through golden energy. As Victor prepared a massive lightning attack, Leonardo responded by covering himself in golden armor, enlarging his wings and concentrating his remaining power into his twin swords.[3]
Their weapons collided in an enormous explosion. Leonardo survived but lost approximately half of his body. Even then, the exposed portion did not contain conventional blood or organs; it released golden energy, causing Victor to finally suspect that the opponent he had been fighting was itself another artificial manifestation.[5]
Leonardo asked Victor whether he had used his complete strength during their battle. After concluding that Victor had still been holding back, Leonardo acknowledged his opponent as a monster. James then contacted him and ordered an immediate withdrawal.[5]
Victor attempted to prevent his escape, but Leonardo revealed that his true body had never been present. The damaged manifestation dissolved entirely into golden light, leaving behind only the communication device, which self-destructed when Victor attempted to examine it.[5]

#### Demonic War
Leonardo later participated directly in the Demonic War as one of Humanity's principal military representatives working alongside the Angels. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] entered the battlefield surrounding the Vatican, Leonardo immediately recognized that the Vampire had grown enormously stronger since their encounter in Japan.[4]
Victor's presence alone created enough pressure to paralyze combatants from both armies. Leonardo attempted to suppress his instinctive fear, but internally acknowledged that although he had previously believed he might be capable of fighting Victor, he could no longer confidently imagine defeating him alone. The realization deeply frustrated him, particularly because Victor had achieved such extraordinary power despite his extremely young age.[4]
Leonardo warned Ariel that if Alucard chose to support the Demons, Humanity's forces would lose the battle. He deliberately avoided speaking Victor's name aloud, irrationally fearing that even doing so might attract the Vampire's attention toward him.[4]
Victor then released a large-scale attack that annihilated more than seventy percent of the Demons outside the Vatican's walls within moments. Leonardo completely lost his normal composure at the sight and openly cursed in front of the Seraphim, while even the Angels themselves were too stunned by Victor's display to reprimand him.[6]
The alliance between the [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] and Angels immediately deteriorated when Leonardo's assistant, Jonah, unexpectedly attacked Ariel using a projectile made from Hell's Metal and concentrated Demonic poison. Leonardo was horrified by the betrayal, having apparently possessed no knowledge that the subordinate working directly beneath him was an infiltrator.[6]
With Ariel critically wounded and the Angels turning their weapons toward Humanity, Leonardo attempted to prevent the situation from escalating. He insisted that Jonah had been a Demonic infiltrator and that the Inquisition itself had not ordered the attack. [[Zaniel]], however, angrily pointed out that the attacker had been Leonardo's own assistant and refused to accept his assurances.[7]
Leonardo attempted to approach the injured Ariel but was ordered away. Recognizing that forcing the issue would transform the Angels into Humanity's enemies, he reluctantly complied. Victor subsequently passed Leonardo and casually knocked him off balance merely by brushing his shoulder against him, further emphasizing how dramatically their physical disparity had grown since Japan.[7]
Victor then succeeded in removing the Demonic projectile and restoring Ariel, something the Angels themselves had been unable to accomplish immediately. The incident left Leonardo and the Human forces in an increasingly uncomfortable political position: their alliance had been sabotaged from within, while the Vampire they considered one of Humanity's greatest threats had become the person responsible for saving one of Heaven's Seraphim.[7]
The relationship between Humanity and the Angels remained severely damaged afterward. Leonardo wished to continue assisting during the conflict surrounding Alexander, but the Seraphim Daniel physically prevented him from interfering because the Angels no longer trusted the Inquisition enough to allow its representatives freedom of action.[8]
Leonardo could therefore do little but watch as Alexander and the wider Inquisition suffered the consequences of the corruption that had accumulated within the organization, leaving the proud General in the position he despised most: a Human unable to meaningfully intervene while vastly greater supernatural powers determined the outcome around him.[8]

### Skills & Abilities
As one of the Inquisition's Generals, Leonardo is among the strongest known Human [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]]. His fighting style centers almost entirely upon his exceptionally developed Faith, which manifests as brilliant golden energy. Unlike most Hunters, Leonardo does not require spoken incantations to access his power, claiming that his Faith is maintained directly within his heart.[2][3]
- Faith Manipulation: Leonardo possesses an exceptionally powerful form of Faith, generating immense quantities of golden energy through his absolute belief in God. His Faith is sufficiently developed that he can invoke it without incantations or lengthy rituals.[2][3]
- Incantationless Casting: Unlike ordinary Hunters who frequently employ prayers, chants or other ritualistic methods, Leonardo can immediately utilize his Faith in battle. He attributes this ability to the strength and sincerity of his internal belief.[2]
- Clone Creation: Leonardo can create multiple physically independent duplicates of himself composed of Faith energy. These clones possess his appearance, weapons and combat capabilities and can operate simultaneously in different locations around a battlefield.[2][3]
- Remote Manifestation: Leonardo can apparently operate through an artificial Faith-created body without placing his true body upon the battlefield. The Leonardo fought by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] in Japan was eventually revealed to have been a manifestation or clone rather than Leonardo's actual body. Even after losing half of itself, the manifestation remained functional before dissolving into golden light when Leonardo withdrew.[5]
- Energy-Based Physiology (Clones/Manifestations): Leonardo's artificial bodies possess an internal structure composed principally of golden Faith energy. Victor removed the heart from one body without meaningfully harming the true Leonardo, while another manifestation lost half its torso without exposing conventional blood or organs.[3][5]
- Weapon Manifestation: Leonardo can create solid weapons directly from Faith, most prominently a pair of golden swords which he uses simultaneously in close combat.[3]
- Barrier Creation: Leonardo can manifest large golden shields capable of resisting powerful supernatural attacks. His barriers blocked Victor's elemental greatsword and temporarily endured repeated attacks from Victor's modified Fulger Count Form.[3]
- Armor Manifestation: When preparing to withstand Victor's strongest attack, Leonardo surrounded his entire body in dense golden armor, considerably increasing the defensive concentration of his Faith.[3]
- Flight: Leonardo can manifest large golden wings and use them for high-speed aerial movement and combat. His wings can increase in size when he concentrates additional Faith into his body.[3]
- Energy Projection: Leonardo can project Faith outward as destructive golden energy. His attacks can create enormous explosions and shockwaves, while swings from his manifested weapons release arcs of concentrated golden power.[2][3]
- Explosive Energy Release: Leonardo can deliberately detonate concentrated Faith from his body or clones. One clone exploded after Victor grabbed it, destroying Victor's hand completely and forcing his regeneration to proceed considerably more slowly than usual.[3]
- Regeneration (Faith Manifestations): Leonardo's manifested bodies can repair severe physical damage through Faith energy. After sacrificing an arm against Victor, Leonardo reconstructed the missing limb within moments. Another damaged body was restored after golden energy was directly channeled into it.[3]
- Regeneration Inhibition: Damage inflicted by Leonardo's concentrated Faith temporarily interfered with Victor's Vampiric regeneration. After a golden-energy explosion destroyed Victor's hand, even Victor noted that the injury was recovering considerably more slowly than normal.[3]
- Superhuman Strength: Leonardo possesses enormous physical power while reinforced by Faith. His initial punch against Victor created an explosion powerful enough to vaporize corpses and destroy the building around them, although Victor himself blocked the strike without significant injury.[2]
- Superhuman Speed: Leonardo is capable of extremely rapid aerial combat and was able to exchange attacks with Victor before the latter entered his modified Fulger Count Form. His movements were fast enough to surprise ordinary observers and coordinate attacks between multiple clones.[2][3]
- Superhuman Durability: Through Faith reinforcement, Leonardo's manifested bodies can withstand attacks that would immediately kill ordinary Humans and continue functioning even after catastrophic injuries.[3][5]
- Enhanced Longevity / Youth: Leonardo appears physically approximately twenty-five years old despite contextual evidence indicating that he is considerably older than he appears. The highest levels of the Inquisition possess methods of maintaining youth through Faith, although Leonardo's exact chronological age has not been stated.[1]
- Combat Skill: Leonardo is an experienced Hunter capable of fighting with two swords simultaneously, coordinating multiple clones and engaging in high-speed aerial combat. Although Victor considered him weaker than Mizuki during their first direct battle, Leonardo was still powerful enough to make Victor become genuinely interested in continuing the fight.[3]
- Precise Energy Control: Leonardo possesses considerable control over the destructive output of his Faith. His entrance into the Japanese battlefield produced an explosion capable of destroying the surrounding structure and corpses while avoiding the individuals he was attempting to rescue.[2]

### Trivia
- [[Scathach]]'s first on-screen attempt to kill Leonardo in Chapter 30 was stopped by Alexander, meaning Leonardo did not die during that particular encounter.[1]
- When Leonardo appeared again in Chapter 345, however, Scathach remembered having killed him previously and briefly wondered whether he had been revived. The exact circumstances of the death she remembered have not been depicted or properly explained.[2]
- Leonardo's declaration that his Faith originates from his heart distinguishes him from [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] who require explicit incantations or prayers before using their abilities.[2][3]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] initially mistook Leonardo's duplicates for possible twins before realizing that they were supernatural clones.[2]
- The Leonardo who fought Victor in Japan was not his true body. The entire manifestation disappeared into golden light when James ordered him to retreat.[5]
- Despite his strong prejudice against supernatural creatures, Leonardo attempted to save S-000 and A-000 because he believed them to be fellow Hunters and companions. His reaction upon discovering that they were artificial Hybrids showed clear hesitation rather than immediate hostility.[2]
- Leonardo is one of the few Human Hunters whose personal Faith output was powerful enough to make Victor's normally extreme regeneration noticeably slower.[3]
