### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 315
Brown is the codename given by [[Victor Elderblood]] to a mysterious Witch whose real name has never been revealed. She is one of the clandestine Witches living and operating beneath [[The Lost Club]] under the protection and leadership of Esther.[1][2]
Unlike many of the Witches associated with Esther, Brown developed an unusually friendly relationship with Victor during the period in which he was forced to remain on Earth. Victor himself was surprised by how rapidly the two became comfortable with one another, especially because this occurred during a period in which his emotions were highly unstable. Brown's cheerful, uncomplicated behavior reminded him strongly of Pepper, and by the time Victor returned to The Lost Club in Chapter 316 she was comfortable enough to immediately jump onto him, hug him and happily allow him to pat her head despite the dramatic changes in his appearance.[2]
Brown works alongside White and the other clandestine Witches concealed beneath The Lost Club. The hidden Witch community operates separately from ordinary Witch society and uses Esther's heavily protected establishment as a safe base from which its members can live, study and conduct magical research away from hostile Witches and unwanted supernatural attention.[1][2]

### Physical Appearance
Brown possesses the physical appearance of a teenage girl and stands approximately 165 cm tall. She has brown hair, blue eyes and pale skin.[1][2]
Her relatively small stature became particularly noticeable when she examined [[Junketsu]]. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s enormous Odachi appeared disproportionately large beside her, with the narration directly identifying Brown as only 165 cm tall while comparing her height with the considerably taller White, Natalia and Mizuki.[2]
Brown's clothing has not received enough detailed description in the novel to establish a consistent canonical outfit.

### Personality
Brown is cheerful, affectionate, curious and exceptionally carefree. Among Esther's clandestine Witches, she stands out for how naturally she interacts with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and how little concern she appears to have for the intimidating supernatural phenomena surrounding him.
Her personality is notably airheaded. When Victor returned to the hidden Witch quarters after undergoing significant physical and supernatural changes, White froze and stared at him in shock. Brown instead immediately leaped toward Victor and hugged him as though nothing about his appearance had changed. The narration explicitly contrasts Brown's reaction with the more normal response displayed by White, describing very few people as sufficiently airheaded to simply ignore how dramatically Victor had changed.[2]
Victor is unusually comfortable with Brown. He affectionately stroked her head after she greeted him, causing Brown to simply laugh happily rather than react with embarrassment or discomfort. Victor privately compared her to Pepper, whose similarly energetic and uncomplicated personality Brown strongly reminded him of.[2]
Brown is also highly curious. She immediately became fascinated by [[Junketsu]] after noticing the enormous weapon beside Victor and questioned him about it. Rather than displaying the instinctive unease that the weapon generated in most of the women present, Brown stared at it with shining eyes and obvious interest.[2]
Despite her carefree behavior, Brown is not ignorant of supernatural affairs. She immediately recognized [[Natalia Elderblood née Alioth]] from reports available to Esther's organization, demonstrating that she keeps at least some awareness of the intelligence circulating through [[The Lost Club]].[2]
Brown is similarly willing to assist Victor without hesitation. When he explained that he urgently required help from the Witches, Brown and White exchanged a glance before immediately agreeing.[2]

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Brown became associated with [[The Lost Club]] as one of several clandestine Witches operating beneath the establishment under Esther. Unlike the public nightclub above, the Witches occupied a magically concealed underground area accessible through a secret passage hidden within the room reserved for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]]. The Witches could observe people approaching the hidden area and decide whether they wished to reveal themselves, allowing the location to function as both a refuge and a secure magical base.[1]
During the period in which Victor remained on Earth, he became acquainted with Esther's clandestine Witches and developed a particularly easy friendship with Brown. Victor later remembered being surprised by how quickly they got along, especially because his emotions were considerably more unstable at the time. Brown's personality reminded him of Pepper, giving Victor an immediate sense of familiarity with her.[2]
Victor later returned to The Lost Club with [[Natalia Elderblood née Alioth]] and [[Mizuki Elderblood]]. After Natalia opened a portal directly into Esther's office, Victor moved through the Club's protected private areas and entered the concealed section occupied by the Witches. Most of Esther's Witches remained hidden because they were suspicious of Natalia and Mizuki, but Brown and White came forward to meet him.[1][2]
Brown reacted to Victor's return very differently from White. Victor's appearance and presence had changed enormously since the Witches had last seen him, leaving White temporarily stunned, but Brown ignored the difference completely and immediately jumped onto Victor in a hug. Victor greeted both women by the codenames White and Brown and affectionately stroked Brown's head while she laughed happily. Mizuki correctly realized that neither designation represented the Witches' real names.[2]
Brown soon became distracted by the enormous Odachi accompanying Victor. She noticed that the weapon produced a sensation similar to the one she associated with Victor himself and asked what it was. Victor introduced the weapon as [[Junketsu]], the Sword of a Progenitor. Although Junketsu remained sheathed, most of the women present instinctively sensed danger from it; Brown was the notable exception and instead stared at the weapon with obvious fascination.[2]
Victor then introduced Mizuki as Junketsu's creator. Brown and White immediately looked at Mizuki with respect, reasoning that anyone capable of creating such a dangerous weapon must herself be exceptionally capable. When Victor introduced Natalia, Brown already knew who she was because Natalia had appeared in the reports available to Esther's network.[2]
Victor had come to the Witches for assistance with memories he had acquired from Adonis and the numerous beings previously absorbed through his Progenitor abilities. Brown and White immediately agreed to help him. The highly specialized magical work itself was performed primarily by White, whose unique Memory [[Magic]] allowed Victor to convert a memory into a magical recording and begin organizing the enormous collection of memories existing within him.[2]
Brown remained part of the clandestine Witch community beneath The Lost Club, but the novel has not subsequently revealed her true name, individual magical specialization or later fate in detail.

### Skills & Abilities
Brown is a clandestine Witch and therefore possesses the supernatural ability to use Mana and Witchcraft. Unlike White, whose specialization in Memory [[Magic]] is extensively explained, Brown's personal school of Magic has never been revealed. Powers unique to other Witches should therefore not automatically be attributed to her.

#### Witch Physiology
- [[Magic]]: As a Witch, Brown possesses the ability to use supernatural Magic through Mana.[1][2]
- Mana Manipulation: Brown is capable of interacting with Mana as part of her nature and training as a Witch.
- Spellcasting: Brown possesses magical training as a clandestine Witch, although no named spell or personal specialization has been directly demonstrated.
- Magical Knowledge: Brown belongs to a hidden group of independent Witches who live and conduct magical work beneath [[The Lost Club]].[1][2]
Brown's exact specialization remains unknown. She should not currently be listed with Memory Manipulation, Illusion Manipulation, Barrier Magic or other specialized Witch abilities unless later chapters directly demonstrate them.

#### Supernatural Perception
Brown possesses enough supernatural sensitivity to recognize unusual qualities in magical or supernatural objects.
When examining [[Junketsu]], Brown noticed that the weapon gave her a sensation similar to the one she experienced from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] himself, correctly recognizing a connection between the Progenitor and his weapon before Victor explained Junketsu's nature.[2]
This can be indexed as limited:
- Extrasensory Perception
- Supernatural Detection
The exact mechanism has not been explained and should not be exaggerated into unrestricted Energy, Soul or Aura perception.

#### Information Gathering
Through her position beneath [[The Lost Club]], Brown has access to at least part of Esther's supernatural intelligence network. She immediately recognized Natalia from existing reports despite having no indication of a previous personal meeting with her.[2]
This is a professional skill rather than a supernatural Power.

#### Clandestine Witch Training
Brown lives among Witches who deliberately operate outside conventional Witch society. The hidden enclave beneath [[The Lost Club]] is protected by concealment, magical barriers and Esther's political connections, allowing its residents to conduct research in relative safety.[1][2]
The novel does not establish how much of the enclave's shared magical knowledge Brown personally possesses.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Brown's closest known friend outside the Witch community.
The two became acquainted during Victor's extended stay on Earth. Victor was surprised by how quickly Brown became comfortable around him, particularly because the friendship developed while his emotional state was unstable. Her behavior reminded him strongly of Pepper.[2]
Their familiarity is demonstrated when Victor later returned to the hidden Witch base. Brown completely ignored the enormous change in Victor's appearance and immediately jumped onto him in a hug. Victor responded affectionately by stroking her head while she laughed.[2]
Brown also appears to trust Victor considerably more than many of the other clandestine Witches. When Natalia and Mizuki accompanied him into [[The Lost Club]], most of the Witches deliberately remained concealed because they did not trust the unfamiliar women. Brown nevertheless greeted Victor openly alongside White and immediately agreed when he requested their help.[2]
Victor calls her Brown because her real name has never been revealed.

#### White
White is Brown's fellow clandestine Witch and closest confirmed Witch associate.
Both women live or work inside the secret Witch enclave beneath [[The Lost Club]] and emerged together when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] visited in Chapters 315–316.[1][2]
Their personalities differ considerably. White is restrained, neutral and cautious, while Brown is highly expressive and immediately affectionate toward Victor. The contrast becomes obvious when Victor returns in his changed appearance: White is stunned while Brown immediately hugs him without concern.[2]
The two nevertheless appear accustomed to working together. When Victor asked whether they could help him, Brown and White exchanged a glance before simultaneously agreeing.
White later performed the specialized Memory [[Magic]] Victor required, while Brown's personal magical specialty remained unrevealed.[2]

#### Esther
Esther is Brown's superior and the leader responsible for the clandestine Witch community operating beneath [[The Lost Club]].
Brown resides or works inside the hidden territory protected by Esther and recognizes the structure established around her authority.[1][2]
Esther's alliance with Ruby and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] also provided the clandestine Witches with indirect protection from far more powerful supernatural forces while allowing them to conduct their own magical work in relative safety.
