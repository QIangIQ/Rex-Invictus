### Physical description
A 190 cm tall beautiful woman with emerald green eyes and black hair. She was wearing a white dress with gold accents. Her hair was styled in braids.

### Personality and traits
She's a prideful and arrogant woman. Meya tends to overestimate her own ability and underestimate her opponent's, she's also rash and reckless but she's loyal to her people and has a sense of duty. Meya is against her people's 'tradition', seeing it as barbaric and savage act. She tried to change it but the Amazon [[Elders]] refused.

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
When Demons attacked Amazons, Meya Neyku fought their leader but was defeated. She was saved by [[Scathach]] and her team.[1]
