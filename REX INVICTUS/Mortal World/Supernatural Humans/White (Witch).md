### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 315
White is the codename given by [[Victor Elderblood]] to a Witch specializing in exceptionally delicate Memory [[Magic]]. She is one of the clandestine Witches living beneath [[The Lost Club]] under Esther's protection and is one of the most dangerous known specialists in magical memory manipulation despite originally possessing what was considered a relatively ordinary branch of Witchcraft.[1][2]
White originally lived openly within the Witch Realm, where she specialized in psychology and used Memory Magic to help Witches suffering from trauma originating from their lives as Humans. What initially appeared to be ordinary memory-reading magic eventually developed into something considerably more dangerous. White discovered that she could enter another Witch's mind, bypass their mental defenses and extract the memories containing their magical knowledge. She began secretly exploiting this ability, luring Witches whose research interested her into situations where she could perform the necessary ritual and steal everything she wanted to learn.[2]
By the time her activities were discovered, White had accumulated the memories and magical knowledge of more than fifty Witches. The discovery made her an extremely valuable target within the Witch Realm. Other Witches wanted her Magic, research and accumulated knowledge, while powerful authorities could have forced her to repeatedly provide her abilities for free. Rather than surrender her unique Magic, White fled the Witch Realm and eventually received shelter from Esther.[2]
Under Esther, White became one of the clandestine Witches hidden beneath The Lost Club. She and the other Witches were eventually placed beneath a magical agreement connecting them to [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] and Victor. In exchange for loyalty and the restriction that their research could not harm the protected parties, they received security, freedom to continue their studies and protection powerful enough to make life outside the Witch Realm substantially safer.[2]
White later became particularly useful to Victor when he needed to organize the enormous number of memories he had absorbed through his Progenitor abilities. Using a specialized ritual, White created metaphysical "doors" within Victor's mind that allowed him to access organized groups of memories through meditation, effectively helping construct a mental archive from knowledge that would otherwise have required a much longer period for Victor to sort naturally.[2]

### Physical Appearance
White possesses the physical appearance of a teenage girl and stands approximately 170 cm tall. Her appearance is almost completely monochromatic: she has extremely pale skin, pure white hair, white eyes and normally wears an entirely white dress.[1][2]
Her appearance is so uniformly white that the narration jokes that if White stood inside a completely white room, it would be difficult to notice she was present at all.[2]
White is slightly taller than Brown, who stands approximately 165 cm, but remains considerably shorter than many of the women normally accompanying [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].

### Personality
White is quiet, reserved, intelligent and considerably more emotionally restrained than Brown. Whereas Brown reacts openly and enthusiastically to most situations, White generally maintains a neutral expression, speaks calmly and displays her emotions through small changes in expression rather than exaggerated reactions.
Her reaction to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s return demonstrates this difference clearly. Brown immediately ignored how drastically Victor's appearance had changed and jumped onto him, while White simply stared at him in shock. Victor considered White's reaction the normal one, since the physical difference between the Victor she remembered and his current appearance was enormous.[2]
Despite her restrained demeanor, White appears genuinely fond of Victor. After recovering from her surprise, she greeted him politely and displayed a small smile. She continued calling him "Count" even after Victor reminded her that he had already told her not to address him so formally.[2]
White is also highly intelligent and technically skilled. Her [[Magic]] requires extensive preparation, precise rituals and delicate manipulation of the mind, all of which demand considerably more control than ordinary destructive Witchcraft. Her former profession dealing with psychological trauma also indicates a developed understanding of the mind before she began abusing the same knowledge for personal gain.[2]
Like many Witches, White is extremely greedy regarding magical knowledge. Once she realized her ability could steal another Witch's understanding, she deliberately began targeting Witches who possessed Magic she wanted. She would trick them into visiting her home, place them in the conditions necessary for her ritual and steal the relevant memories containing their research and magical knowledge.[2]
Her greed is nevertheless accompanied by strong hypocrisy regarding ownership. White was perfectly willing to steal other Witches' knowledge, but refused to allow anyone else to take her own research or learn the unique Memory Magic she had developed. The possibility that the Witch Realm's authorities could force her to surrender her Magic and accumulated knowledge was one of the principal reasons she fled.[2]
This behavior reflects the fiercely individualistic nature attributed to many Witches within the story. White values magical knowledge enormously, but values her personal freedom and ownership over that knowledge even more.
White is also cautious and practical. Unlike Brown, she does not recklessly approach unfamiliar supernatural beings, and she understands the danger represented by individuals such as Natalia, Mizuki and Victor. Her willingness to live beneath Esther and accept contractual restrictions from Ruby and Victor demonstrates that she considers limited obligations preferable to losing her freedom entirely within the Witch Realm.[2]

### Background
White originally lived as an ordinary Witch within the Witch Realm. Her specialty was psychology, supported by a relatively common form of [[Magic]] capable of reading memories.[2]
She initially used this ability constructively. Many Witches had originally been Humans and retained severe trauma from their mortal lives, and White used Memory Magic to examine their experiences and assist them psychologically.[2]
Over time, however, she discovered that her apparently ordinary Magic possessed extraordinary potential.
White's spell could penetrate another Witch's mind and extract knowledge directly from their memories. Conventional mental defenses were unable to prevent the process once the ritual had been successfully established.[2]
White began exploiting the ability for herself. Whenever she encountered a Witch possessing magical research or knowledge that interested her, she would lure the target into visiting her home and secretly perform the ritual needed to copy or steal the relevant memories.
Through this method, White accumulated the magical knowledge of more than fifty Witches.[2]
The ability was extremely powerful but not convenient enough to use casually. White required considerable preparation, specialized materials and enough time to complete the ritual. The target also needed to remain asleep throughout the process, and White became exhausted once the ritual was completed.[2]
Eventually, other Witches discovered what White had been doing.
Her Magic immediately made her a target. If captured by the Queen's faction or other powerful Witches, White expected to lose her freedom and be forced to use her Memory Magic for them, surrender her accumulated research and potentially teach others how her unique version of Memory Magic worked.[2]
Unwilling to accept that outcome, White fled the Witch Realm.
Esther eventually found or accepted her and provided shelter within the clandestine Witch community hidden beneath [[The Lost Club]]. White accepted the arrangement because living under Esther—and later beneath the indirect protection of Ruby and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]—was substantially preferable to returning to a society where other Witches coveted her Magic.[2]

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
White became associated with [[The Lost Club]] as one of the clandestine Witches living in the magically concealed area beneath Esther's establishment. The underground enclave could be reached through a hidden passage concealed within the private room reserved for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]], allowing Esther's Witches to remain separated from the supernatural patrons occupying the nightclub above.[1]
Victor became familiar with White and Brown during the extended period in which he remained on Earth. When he later returned to The Lost Club alongside [[Natalia Elderblood née Alioth]] and [[Mizuki Elderblood]], most of the clandestine Witches remained hidden because they did not trust the unfamiliar women accompanying him. White and Brown nevertheless came forward to greet Victor personally.[1][2]
Victor's appearance had changed enormously since White had last seen him. Brown ignored the difference and immediately embraced him, but White remained frozen for several moments while attempting to reconcile the Victor she remembered with the man standing in front of her. After recovering, she greeted him politely as "Count", despite Victor reminding her that he had previously asked her not to address him by his title.[2]
Victor introduced the women accompanying him. White already recognized Natalia through reports circulated through Esther's information network and identified her as an Alioth. She and Brown also became visibly interested in Mizuki after learning that she had created [[Junketsu]], the Progenitor weapon whose presence they could sense beside Victor.[2]
Victor had two specific reasons for seeking the clandestine Witches. The first was to transform one of Adonis' memories into a magical recording. He intended to use the memory of Adonis' meeting with Vlad's Queen while censoring identities that could unnecessarily draw another powerful faction toward his wives' Clans. Converting the memory into a magical recording was comparatively simple and was completed without major difficulty.[2]
His second objective was substantially more complicated. During previous uses of his Progenitor abilities, Victor had absorbed large numbers of beings and unintentionally inherited enormous quantities of their memories. He wanted to organize these memories into a structured mental archive resembling a library or mind palace, allowing him to revisit the knowledge of individual beings, extract anything useful and discard irrelevant information.[2]
White was chosen to perform the procedure because Memory [[Magic]] was her specialization. She prepared Victor inside a complicated magical pentagram and began a ritual involving several Magic Circles that appeared across her arms. As the circles moved toward her wrists, a white sphere of magical power formed between her hands.[2]
White explained that she would create several metaphysical "doors" inside Victor's mind. By meditating, Victor would later be capable of accessing those doors and navigating the organized memories stored behind them.[2]
Victor technically did not require White's assistance permanently. His Progenitor abilities would eventually allow him to sort the absorbed memories instinctively once his control improved. At the time, however, properly mastering that aspect of his Progenitor form would have taken far too long, making White's ritual an effective shortcut.[2]
While White performed the ritual, Victor reflected upon her history and the nature of Witches generally. Although he maintained a friendly relationship with White and the other clandestine Witches, he did not completely trust them. White's own history—helping traumatized Witches before later exploiting the same Memory Magic to steal magical knowledge—was an ideal example of why Victor remained cautious around their Race.[2]
White nevertheless remained protected under Victor and Ruby's sphere of influence. After Esther gave her refuge, White and the other clandestine Witches entered a magical agreement of loyalty. In exchange, they were allowed to conduct their own research freely so long as that research did not harm Victor, Ruby or other protected parties. For White, this arrangement gave her exactly what she wanted: security, access to magical research and freedom from the Witches who wished to seize her unique ability.[2]
No later chapter has revealed White's real name or provided a substantial update concerning her current status.

### Skills & Abilities
White is an exceptionally specialized Witch. Rather than focusing upon direct offensive [[Magic]], her expertise lies in delicate mental and memory-based Witchcraft. Her abilities are extremely dangerous because they can acquire information that would normally be protected by another Witch's mental defenses.

#### Witch Physiology
- [[Magic]]: As a Witch, White possesses the ability to use Mana and perform supernatural Witchcraft.[2]
- Mana Manipulation: White can gather, shape and direct Mana through Magic Circles and ritual structures.
- Spellcasting: She is capable of performing highly complicated ritual Magic requiring multiple stages, Magic Circles and specialized materials.[2]
- Magical Knowledge: White possesses her own extensive magical education in addition to knowledge stolen from more than fifty other Witches.[2]

#### Memory Magic
White specializes in an exceptionally powerful form of Memory [[Magic]]. What originally appeared to be common memory-reading Magic developed into a highly invasive mental ability capable of accessing and extracting knowledge from supernatural minds.[2]
- Memory Reading: White can enter and examine another person's memories. This was the original application of her Magic and formed the basis of her work helping psychologically traumatized Witches.[2]
- Memory Manipulation: White can directly interact with the structure and organization of memories rather than merely observing them.
- Memory Extraction: She can extract specific information contained within another person's memories.[2]
- Knowledge Extraction: White can target memories specifically connected to magical study, research and learned techniques, allowing her to take another Witch's accumulated knowledge.[2]
- Knowledge Theft: White repeatedly used Memory Magic to secretly steal the magical knowledge of other Witches for herself.[2]
- Memory Copying: Memories can be copied without requiring the original experience to be physically recreated.
- Memory Recording: White's Magic can assist in converting a memory into a magical recording capable of being viewed separately from the original mind.[2]
- Memory Organization: She can restructure large quantities of disorganized memories into more manageable mental systems.[2]
- Mind Palace Creation: White's ritual allowed [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s vast collection of absorbed memories to be reorganized into an internal library-like structure that could be deliberately accessed.[2]
- Mental Door Creation: White can create metaphysical "doors" within a person's mind which serve as access points to organized memories during meditation.[2]
- Traumatic Memory Analysis: Before becoming a clandestine Witch, White used her Magic together with her knowledge of psychology to help Witches suffering from trauma originating from their Human lives.[2]

#### Mental Defense Bypassing
White's most dangerous demonstrated characteristic is her ability to bypass the mental defenses of other Witches.
Once the conditions required for her ritual were successfully established, ordinary mental protections did not prevent her from accessing and extracting the target's memories.[2]
This grants her:
- Mental Defense Negation (Memory [[Magic]])
- Resistance Bypassing (Mental Defenses)
- Protected Memory Access
This ability should not be interpreted as proof that White can automatically bypass the defenses of every being regardless of power. Her known feats specifically concern other Witches and depend upon an elaborate prepared ritual.

#### Magical Knowledge Assimilation
White retains and uses information extracted from other Witches.
By the time she fled the Witch Realm, she possessed memories and magical knowledge taken from more than fifty Witches.[2]
This gives her an unusually large theoretical knowledge base despite the novel never cataloguing every spell contained within those stolen memories.
The individual abilities of those fifty Witches should not automatically be indexed as White's personal powers unless she directly demonstrates that she can reproduce them. What is confirmed is that she possesses their memories and magical knowledge.

#### Psychology
Before becoming a fugitive, White specialized in psychology and helped Witches suffering from trauma.[2]
Her professional knowledge includes:
- Psychological Analysis
- Trauma Recognition
- Memory-Assisted Therapy
- Mental State Analysis
This knowledge was originally used to help patients before White began exploiting her access to other Witches' minds for personal gain.

#### Ritual Magic
White's strongest Memory [[Magic]] depends heavily upon prepared rituals.
During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s memory-organization procedure, she required:
- a complicated magical pentagram;
- ritual preparation;
- multiple Magic Circles;
- incantations;
- and concentrated magical power.[2]
For her historical knowledge-stealing ritual, additional materials and extensive preparation were required.
White therefore possesses considerable proficiency in:
- Ritual Magic
- Magic Circle Creation
- Incantation
- Magical Preparation
- Delicate Mana Control

#### Limitations
White's Memory [[Magic]] is extremely powerful but possesses major practical limitations.[2]
- Preparation Time: Her strongest memory-extraction techniques require considerable advance preparation.
- Material Requirements: Various magical materials are needed to strengthen and stabilize the ritual.
- Stationary Target Requirement: The target must remain asleep and cannot awaken while the process is being performed.
- Ritual Dependence: White cannot casually steal the complete magical knowledge of another Witch simply by looking at them.
- Exhaustion: Completing the memory-stealing process leaves White heavily fatigued.
- Power Difference Unknown: The novel has never demonstrated whether White's Memory Magic could penetrate beings whose Souls and mental defenses exist on the level of modern [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] or other exceptionally powerful Divine/Conceptual entities.
- Knowledge Does Not Equal Mastery: Possessing memories concerning another Witch's Magic does not automatically prove White can reproduce every ability at the same level as its original owner.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is one of White's closest known allies and one of the powerful figures responsible for protecting the clandestine Witches living beneath [[The Lost Club]].
White became acquainted with Victor during his extended period on Earth and developed enough familiarity with him that Victor asked her not to address him formally as "Count". White nevertheless continued doing so out of habit, although her small smile upon seeing him again demonstrates that her formality does not indicate emotional distance.[2]
Victor trusts White's technical skill sufficiently to allow her to perform delicate Memory [[Magic]] directly upon his mind. He relied upon her when he needed to organize the enormous number of memories absorbed through his Progenitor powers, something that would be extraordinarily dangerous to entrust to an ordinary Witch.[2]
His trust nevertheless has limits. Victor explicitly reflected that he did not completely trust White or Witches generally, considering magical contracts necessary precautions even toward individuals with whom he maintained friendly relationships.[2]
White's current safety is partially derived from Victor's protection. In exchange, she remains bound by the contractual restrictions established around the clandestine Witches.

#### Brown
Brown is White's closest confirmed companion among Esther's clandestine Witches.
Both women live or operate within the hidden Witch enclave beneath [[The Lost Club]] and frequently appear together.[1][2]
Their personalities contrast strongly. Brown is energetic, affectionate and somewhat airheaded, while White is restrained, analytical and cautious. This contrast is demonstrated during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s return: Brown immediately leaps onto him while White remains frozen in surprise at his changed appearance.[2]
The two nevertheless appear close and accustomed to communicating nonverbally. When Victor asked them for help, Brown and White exchanged a glance before agreeing together.

#### Esther
Esther is White's superior, protector and the person responsible for giving her refuge after she fled the Witch Realm.
After White's Memory [[Magic]] became known and other Witches began coveting her ability, Esther allowed her to live under the protection of the clandestine community beneath [[The Lost Club]].[2]
This arrangement allowed White to preserve ownership of her research and Magic while avoiding forced service to more powerful factions within the Witch Realm.
White consequently operates beneath Esther's authority and remains one of the specialist Witches associated with her organization.

#### Ruby Elderblood Scarlett
[[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] is one of the major powers indirectly responsible for White's continued safety.
Following Ruby's takeover of Esther's information network and her growing association with the clandestine Witches, White and the others were bound through magical agreements restricting betrayal and harmful research.[2]
In exchange, White gained the protection associated with Ruby, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and their wider faction.
For a fugitive Witch being sought for her unique [[Magic]], living beneath the protection of a Vampire Count was considerably safer than remaining exposed within the Witch Realm.
