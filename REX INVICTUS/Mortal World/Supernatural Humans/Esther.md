### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 59
Esther, more commonly addressed as Madam, is a clandestine Witch, information broker and the owner of [[The Lost Club]], a supernatural establishment located in Brooklyn. Although outwardly operated as a nightclub and bar, the Lost Club serves as neutral territory for different supernatural races and supports a much larger underground business involving information gathering, information trading and the employment of supernatural mercenaries.[1][2]
Esther originally operated independently from the Witch Queen and mainstream Witch society. Her decision to become a clandestine Witch freed her from the regulations and contracts imposed upon ordinary Witches serving the Queen, allowing Esther to build her own intelligence network around the Lost Club. Over time, her information network became sufficiently valuable that [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] chose to incorporate Esther into her own plans rather than attempt to reproduce the network from nothing.[1][3]
Although Esther initially entered into a partnership with Ruby, Ruby did not trust her enough to rely solely upon an agreement. Remembering that Esther had deceived her in the past and distrusting Witch contracts, Ruby bit Esther and used an experimental technique she had developed from Vampiric Charm. The technique allowed Ruby to detect Esther's intention to betray her and, should Esther actually attempt betrayal, compel her to kill herself. Consequently, Esther's cooperation with Ruby became far more binding than an ordinary business arrangement.[3]
Through Ruby, Esther's information network became one of the intelligence resources available to [[Victor Elderblood]] and his faction. Victor later became personally acquainted with her after accidentally arriving inside the Lost Club, and the establishment continued functioning as a recurring point of contact for Victor, his allies and several clandestine Witches.[2][4][5]

### Physical Appearance
Esther is a tall woman standing approximately 175 cm in height. She has short silver hair and a visible golden eye, while her right eye is normally concealed beneath an eye patch that covers a significant portion of that side of her face. Her appearance is mature and sophisticated, complementing the image she maintains as the proprietor of one of Brooklyn's most important supernatural establishments.[6]
Esther usually wears stylish formal clothing. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] encountered her directly at the Lost Club, she wore a fitted black suit beneath a fur coat, maintaining the elegant appearance associated with her position as "Madam."[2] She is also known to smoke unusual black cigarettes that appear to have been specially manufactured rather than being ordinary commercial cigarettes.[6]

### Personality
Esther is calculating, secretive and strongly values control. Information is one of the principal tools through which she maintains that control, explaining both her occupation and the enormous effort she invested into developing the Lost Club into an intelligence hub. Rather than relying on personal combat strength alone, Esther prefers possessing enough information about the surrounding supernatural world to understand the people entering her territory and profit from conflicts between larger factions.[1][2]
Her attitude toward personal relationships reflects the same controlling tendency. Esther enjoys maintaining the dominant position in a relationship and has shown a preference for treating men more like possessions or personal toys than equal partners. This disposition is reflected in her relationship with Eddy, who serves as both her bartender and lover.
Esther is nevertheless pragmatic enough to recognize when she is dealing with people considerably more dangerous than herself. Ruby's return to the Lost Club demonstrated this clearly. Esther was willing to negotiate with Ruby, provide intelligence and accept a long-term partnership once she understood the benefits involved. After Ruby placed her under the modified Vampiric Charm, Esther was furious about having her freedom restricted but quickly understood that openly challenging Ruby would achieve nothing.[3]
She displays a similar mixture of caution and curiosity around [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Esther understood the enormous reputation surrounding the newly appointed Fifth Vampire Count and recognized that his arrival immediately changed the atmosphere of her establishment. Even so, her natural curiosity remained strong enough that the magic connected to her territory attempted to examine Victor, causing her to detect several distinct life forces inside him and completely misunderstand the reason for their presence.[2]
Despite her independence and earlier willingness to deceive others, Esther is not recklessly disloyal once the difference in power becomes obvious. Following her arrangement with Ruby, she continued supplying information and allowing the Lost Club to function as a useful contact point for Victor's faction rather than attempting to escape the arrangement.

### Synopsis

#### Vampire Society
Esther was already operating [[The Lost Club]] in Brooklyn during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s early months as a Vampire. The establishment had become an important gathering point for supernatural beings, mercenaries and individuals seeking information outside the official organizations of their respective races. [[Johnny Lykos]], the White Wolf, was among the supernatural figures regularly associated with the club during this period.[6]
By then, Esther had separated herself from normal Witch society and operated as a clandestine Witch, giving her substantially greater freedom than Witches directly affiliated with the Witch Queen. Rather than functioning primarily as a spellcaster for hire, Esther focused her efforts on the intelligence business and gradually transformed the Lost Club into the center of her personal information network.

#### A New Pillar
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] returned from [[Nightingale]] as the newly appointed Fifth Vampire Count, [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] and Natalia visited the Lost Club specifically to meet Esther. Ruby wanted a reliable intelligence network capable of gathering information independently of the Witch Queen's organization, and Esther already possessed much of the infrastructure Ruby needed.[1]
[[The Lost Club]] was nominally neutral territory where Vampires, [[Werewolves]], Witches and other supernatural races could interact, hire mercenaries and purchase information. Ruby nevertheless demonstrated that neutrality existed only so long as the people involved respected the strength of those around them. When Johnny insulted and challenged her, Ruby humiliated him despite being inside Esther's establishment, making it clear that the Lost Club's rules alone could not restrain someone of her status and power.[1][7]
Ruby and Esther eventually reached an agreement concerning their intelligence operations. Esther possessed contacts and an established network, while Ruby possessed the resources, influence and long-term ambitions necessary to expand that network dramatically. Esther even provided Ruby with information concerning Clan Horseman as a gesture of good faith toward their new partnership.[3]
Ruby, however, had no intention of trusting Esther completely. Esther had deceived her once before, and Ruby considered both Esther and conventional Witch agreements unreliable. After accepting the partnership, Ruby suddenly froze the room, moved behind Esther and bit her before Esther could properly react. Rather than transforming her into a Vampire, Ruby used a technique she had developed by applying Vampiric Charm through her bite. The ability functioned as "insurance": Ruby would become aware if Esther seriously intended to betray her, and could force Esther to kill herself with a thought. Ruby then instructed Esther simply not to betray her if she wished to remain alive.[3]
During the same conversation, Esther became one of the first outsiders to hear the scale of Ruby's ambitions. Ruby explained that merely attempting to repair the existing supernatural political order was pointless; she intended to exploit the conflicts between the major factions, accumulate power and eventually reshape the world into something safe enough to offer entirely to Victor. Esther considered Ruby's plan insane, particularly because successfully carrying it out could throw the supernatural factions into chaos, but Ruby regarded that instability as an opportunity rather than a problem.[3]
Some time later, Victor accidentally entered the Lost Club after stepping through a portal before Natalia had finished organizing everyone's transportation. By that point, Victor's reputation as Count Alucard, [[Scathach]]'s disciple and one of the supernatural world's most abnormal new figures was already widespread. Esther immediately recognized him and personally began showing him through the establishment.[2]
Victor learned directly that the Lost Club was considerably more than a nightclub. Esther explained that supernatural beings used the establishment for a variety of dealings, with its principal businesses involving the hiring of mercenaries and the gathering and sale of information. Victor acknowledged Esther's competence but also recognized that her network remained smaller than the enormous international network controlled by the Witch Queen.[2]
While guiding Victor, the magic connected to Esther's establishment reacted to her curiosity and examined him. Esther detected what appeared to be five separate life forces within Victor and mistakenly concluded that the newly appointed Count possessed multiple souls inside his body. In reality, Kaguya, Bruna and Eve were concealed with Victor alongside the mysterious existence connected to Eve.[2]
Esther also received a direct call from Ruby warning her against attempting to seduce Victor. During the same visit, Victor encountered Eddy, Johnny Lykos and the mysterious Roberta. Victor became increasingly suspicious of Roberta when inconsistencies appeared in the memories surrounding her, eventually discovering that something associated with Roberta could interfere with memory itself.[2][8][9]
Victor's confrontation with Roberta escalated inside the Lost Club until the mysterious entity associated with her defended her. Rather than destroying Roberta, Victor became interested in her potential and decided to take her with him under a magical contract that restricted whom she could manipulate or harm. Esther's establishment survived the incident, although Victor's presence and the resulting confrontation left those around him increasingly convinced that Count Alucard attracted trouble wherever he appeared.[9][10]

#### Volume 4: Discoveries, Changes, And War
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later became unable to return normally to [[Nightingale]], Esther once again became useful as an information source. Victor and Ruby consulted her after conventional attempts to understand the problem failed. Esther herself did not know the cause of Nightingale's isolation, but she suggested other individuals who might possess information unavailable through her own network, including members of the Witch Queen's family and the Goddess [[Aphrodite]].[11]
Victor subsequently returned to the Lost Club and encountered Aphrodite there, demonstrating that Esther's establishment remained one of the locations where powerful figures from otherwise unrelated supernatural factions could cross paths.[11][12]
By the time Victor returned to the Lost Club with Natalia and Mizuki, Esther's organization also sheltered and employed other clandestine Witches. Two of the most notable were the women Victor nicknamed Brown and White.[4]
White possessed an exceptionally dangerous form of memory magic. Although it originally appeared to be an ordinary ability for entering and examining memories, she had developed it to the point that she could bypass the mental defenses of other Witches and steal their magical knowledge. After stealing knowledge from dozens of Witches, White became an enemy of mainstream Witch society and eventually operated under Esther's protection at the Lost Club.[5]
Victor made use of these clandestine Witches for specialized magical work, including converting memories into magical recordings and reorganizing the enormous quantity of memories he had accumulated into a more manageable mental structure. Their presence demonstrated that Esther's organization had grown beyond a simple mercenary bar and information brokerage, functioning as a refuge and workplace for rogue specialists whose abilities could be valuable to Victor and Ruby.[4][5]
Esther consequently remained an indirect but useful member of Victor's faction. While she was never one of Victor's frontline combatants, her value came from maintaining contacts, gathering information, operating neutral territory and providing access to clandestine Witches and other supernatural specialists whom Victor's family could employ when conventional resources were insufficient.

### Skills and Abilities
As a clandestine Witch, Esther possesses the fundamental magical abilities associated with Witches, although she is considerably more notable for the magical infrastructure surrounding the Lost Club and her intelligence network than for direct combat feats.
- [[Magic]]: Esther is capable of manipulating Mana and performing Witchcraft. Her precise magical specialization has not been fully revealed, but she possesses enough magical knowledge and experience to operate independently from the Witch Queen's organization and maintain the extensive enchantments surrounding the Lost Club.[2]
- Barrier Creation: Esther established multiple magical barriers and protective enchantments around the Lost Club. These barriers are intended to prevent outsiders from freely observing or spying upon activities occurring inside the establishment.[4]
- Magical Surveillance: [[The Lost Club]] contains magic connected directly to Esther as its owner. When Esther became curious about [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], the magic of the establishment reacted automatically in an attempt to provide her with information about him.[2]
- Life-Force Detection: Through the magic operating inside her territory, Esther was capable of detecting multiple separate life forces concealed within Victor. She mistakenly interpreted what she detected as Victor possessing several souls because she was unaware that several of his maids were hidden with him.[2]
- Information Gathering: Esther's greatest demonstrated specialty is intelligence rather than direct combat. She maintains contacts throughout supernatural society and built an independent network capable of gathering and selling information concerning Vampires, [[Werewolves]], Witches and other factions.[1][3]
- Supernatural Networking: Through the Lost Club, Esther maintains relationships with mercenaries, rogue Witches and members of multiple supernatural races. This allows her to connect clients with individuals possessing abilities or information that would otherwise be difficult to obtain.[2][4]
- Business Management: Esther successfully manages the Lost Club as several businesses simultaneously: a nightclub, supernatural meeting point, mercenary hub and information brokerage. Its continued importance despite Esther's separation from mainstream Witch society demonstrates considerable organizational ability.[1][2]

### Relationships

#### Ruby Elderblood Scarlett
[[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] is Esther's principal superior and the person who incorporated her intelligence network into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wider faction. Their relationship began from mutual usefulness rather than trust. Ruby needed a capable independent information network, while Esther benefited from the resources and protection associated with one of [[Nightingale]]'s most important Vampires.[1][3]
Ruby nevertheless remembered that Esther had deceived her previously and refused to depend upon goodwill alone. She consequently placed Esther under an experimental Vampiric Charm technique that served as a lethal safeguard against betrayal.[3] Despite the coercive nature of their arrangement, Esther continued working with Ruby and became an important component of the intelligence structure Ruby was building for Victor.

#### Victor Elderblood
Esther initially knew [[Victor Elderblood]] primarily through his rapidly spreading supernatural reputation and through Ruby's plans. Their first major direct interaction occurred when Victor accidentally arrived inside the Lost Club. Esther immediately recognized him as the Fifth Vampire Count, [[Scathach]]'s disciple and a supernatural celebrity, and personally showed him around her establishment.[2]
Victor regarded Esther as competent, particularly in information gathering, although he remained naturally distrustful of Witches. Their relationship eventually developed into one of practical cooperation, with Esther and the clandestine Witches under her protection providing information and specialized magical services whenever Victor required them.[2][4][5]

#### Eddy
Eddy is Esther's bartender, subordinate and lover. He assists in operating the Lost Club and serves supernatural clients directly from the establishment's bar. Their personal relationship reflects Esther's dominant personality and preference for maintaining control within her relationships.

#### Brown and White
Brown and White are clandestine Witches affiliated with Esther and the Lost Club. Both operate outside the normal authority of the Witch Queen and eventually became useful contacts for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction.[4][5]
White is particularly valuable because of her extremely advanced memory magic, while Brown is another rogue Witch protected within Esther's organization. Their presence demonstrates that Esther's influence extends beyond ordinary employees and mercenaries to Witches who cannot safely return to mainstream Witch society.[5]

### Trivia
- Esther is usually addressed as Madam by patrons and employees of the Lost Club.
- Her information network is independent from the much larger international network maintained by the Witch Queen.[2]
- Ruby's control over Esther is not an ordinary Witch contract. Ruby specifically distrusted Witch contracts and instead used a modified form of Vampiric Charm as insurance against betrayal.[3]
- Esther had deceived Ruby sometime before their Chapter 151 agreement, which was one of the principal reasons Ruby refused to trust her without a safeguard.[3]
- When Esther's magic examined [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] in Chapter 206, it detected five life forces and caused Esther to mistakenly believe that several souls existed inside him.[2]
- [[The Lost Club]] eventually became associated with several clandestine Witches, including Brown and White.[4][5]
