### Appearances

#### Debut
[[Ch14 - Rex Invictus VII|Chapter 14]]
Thomas is a former Human Hunter and Exorcist of the Inquisition who later became a covert intelligence agent serving [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] and [[Victor Elderblood]]. He was one of the first [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] encountered by Victor after his transformation into a Vampire, operating alongside his longtime partner and closest friend Jimmy. Unlike Jimmy's cautious and calculating personality, Thomas was hot-headed, straightforward and intensely loyal, frequently attempting to fight enemies far beyond his level rather than retreat.[1][2]
After being captured by Victor and [[Violet Elderblood Snow]], Thomas and Jimmy were eventually bound by magical contracts created by Ruby Elderblood Scarlett, forcing them to operate as spies inside the Inquisition. Although their service originally began through coercion, Thomas gradually became genuinely loyal to Victor's faction. Unlike Jimmy, whose later loyalty was partially motivated by ambition and the advantages of standing beside a rising power, Thomas was primarily won over by a much simpler principle: Victor's faction consistently rewarded useful work, while the organization Thomas had originally served demanded sacrifice from him without giving anything meaningful in return.[3][4]
The money Thomas earned through his intelligence work was used to support the orphanage where he had been raised. He eventually financed Witch-created protective barriers around the orphanage, allowing it to survive attacks from Lesser Demons during the Demonic invasion. His years of useful service and genuine loyalty were ultimately recognized by Victor, who sent War to reward Thomas and Jimmy. Both were transformed using the Essence of [[Chaos]] and became the first members of the supernatural race known as the [[Heralds of Chaos]].[4][5]

### Physical Appearance

#### Human
Thomas was a young Human man with black hair and brown eyes. During his years as an Exorcist, he wore the clothing of an Inquisition field Hunter and carried religious equipment intended for combat against supernatural beings.[1]
One of the most important items in his equipment was a crucifix worn around his neck. When empowered through Faith, the crucifix could transform into a Western-style sword whose blade was coated in golden [[Holy Energy]].[1]

#### Herald of Chaos
Thomas' physical appearance changed radically after his transformation by War. His Human facial features disappeared beneath complete living darkness, leaving his face as an empty black surface from which no ordinary features could be seen. His former clothing was replaced by supernatural armor resembling War's own equipment, although his armor was noticeably less massive than that of his Progenitor.[5]
A pair of wings composed entirely of pure darkness also emerged from his back. His new form therefore bears little resemblance to the Human Hunter he once was, instead visually identifying him as one of War's [[Chaos]]-born Heralds.[5]

### Personality
Thomas is straightforward, emotional, courageous, loyal and extremely impulsive. Where Jimmy habitually evaluates danger and searches for the safest solution, Thomas has a tendency to act according to his immediate emotions. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly recognized this contrast shortly after capturing the pair, considering Jimmy intelligent and survival-oriented while describing Thomas as a loyal but hot-headed man who frequently acted before thinking.[2]
His recklessness was particularly apparent during his first encounter with Violet. Even after Jimmy realized that they were confronting a Noble Vampire capable of walking freely in sunlight and repeatedly ordered a retreat, Thomas refused. From his perspective, encountering a Vampire meant that it was his responsibility as a Hunter to destroy it. When Victor appeared, Thomas immediately attacked him as well rather than reassessing the situation.[1]
Thomas' hatred toward Vampires was considerably stronger than Jimmy's. Victor noticed the intensity of his reaction and suspected that Thomas had experienced some form of personal tragedy involving Vampires in the past, although the exact event responsible for this hatred has never been revealed. Therefore, while a traumatic history is implied, its details should not be treated as confirmed.[2]
Despite his lack of caution, Thomas is deeply loyal to the people he considers companions. When Victor successfully used Charm on Jimmy, Thomas immediately became furious and attempted to attack Victor despite already knowing the enormous difference between them. His reaction was not tactical; he simply saw his friend being manipulated and responded immediately.[2]
Thomas is also somewhat simple-minded and frequently fails to recognize dangerous implications as quickly as Jimmy. During their imprisonment, Jimmy immediately understood what Ruby's magical contract meant, while Thomas remained confused about why his partner had become so distressed. This repeated dynamic eventually led the narration to refer to Thomas as the "Shounen Protagonist", contrasting his emotional, straightforward and heroic mentality with Jimmy's much more cynical pragmatism.[3][6]
However, Thomas' simplicity is not the same as stupidity. He possesses a clearly defined personal morality and responds strongly to fairness. This became one of the central reasons he eventually became genuinely loyal to Victor. Thomas had grown dissatisfied with the Inquisition because the organization constantly demanded that its [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] sacrifice themselves in the name of its cause while providing little meaningful support in return. Ruby's faction, despite initially forcing him into service, consistently rewarded useful intelligence according to its value.[4]
Those rewards were personally important to Thomas because he was an orphan. Rather than spending his earnings entirely on himself, he sent resources to the orphanage where he had grown up and eventually paid for magical protections capable of shielding it during the Demonic invasion. This demonstrates that beneath his aggressive and impulsive exterior, Thomas possesses a strong sense of responsibility toward the people and place that raised him.[4]
By the time War offered him greater power, Thomas no longer required threats or a magical contract to remain loyal. His reasoning was extremely simple: if diligent work, loyalty and continued improvement would be recognized and rewarded, he was satisfied serving the person responsible for that system.[4]

### Synopsis

#### The Awakening
Thomas first appeared alongside Jimmy while the pair were searching for the rogue Vampire Lucy. Their mission formed part of a larger Inquisition operation involving more experienced [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] such as [[Carlos Reiss]], Maria and [[Mizuki Elderblood]].[1][2]
When Thomas and Jimmy sensed Violet's killing intent, Jimmy immediately began preparing defensive prayers. Golden light descended upon both Hunters, after which Thomas grasped the crucifix around his neck and declared himself a messenger chosen by God. His crucifix transformed into a Western sword whose edge was surrounded by golden supernatural Energy.[1]
Violet attacked almost immediately, but the golden defensive barrier surrounding the Hunters successfully stopped her first strike and briefly injured her foot before her Vampire regeneration repaired the damage. Thomas recognized Violet as a Vampire and responded with immediate enthusiasm rather than caution. Jimmy, however, noticed the far more important detail: Violet was walking beneath broad daylight without suffering any harm.[1]
Jimmy correctly realized that they had encountered a member of an extraordinarily dangerous Noble Vampire lineage and repeatedly ordered Thomas to retreat. Thomas refused, insisting that because a Vampire was standing before them they should simply kill her.[1]
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] appeared, Thomas escalated the confrontation again. He invoked God to bless his body with greater physical strength and resistance to fatigue while increasing the amount of golden Energy surrounding his sword. Ignoring Jimmy's increasingly desperate warnings, Thomas launched himself toward Victor at greatly enhanced speed and attempted to decapitate him.[1]
Victor avoided the physical blade, but the [[Holy Energy]] extending from Thomas' attack still grazed his face. The resulting wound produced an intense burning sensation and briefly surprised Victor because he believed he had completely avoided the strike. Jimmy immediately criticized Thomas for failing to properly control the Energy, noting that he had wasted an opportunity.[1]
Thomas had exhausted almost all of his available Energy in the attack. Nevertheless, Victor recognized that the technique was genuinely dangerous: Thomas' control was crude because he was still a novice, but Victor believed an experienced Hunter using the same type of attack might have been capable of taking his head off. The strike was also stated to possess enough Holy power to permanently injure an ordinary Vampire.[1]
Unfortunately for Thomas, injuring Victor enraged Violet. Her killing intent exploded and she released an enormous pillar of fire after realizing that Thomas had wounded her husband. Even Thomas finally became frightened by the scale of her power, while Jimmy angrily reminded him that he had repeatedly warned against fighting them.[1]
Thomas and Jimmy attempted to escape once Victor calmed Violet, but Victor personally intercepted Thomas. Thomas tried to speak before Victor kicked him unconscious and carried him away. Both Hunters were taken to an abandoned building for interrogation.[1]
Jimmy immediately began cooperating when Victor promised that answering questions could preserve their lives. Thomas objected to his partner revealing Inquisition information, but Jimmy angrily silenced him and took control of the negotiation.[1][2]
Victor quickly recognized Thomas as the emotional and loyal member of the pair. When Victor experimented with his developing Vampiric Charm and successfully enthralled Jimmy despite the Inquisition's anti-enchantment protections, Thomas became furious and threatened to kill Victor for what he had done to his friend. Before Thomas could invoke another prayer, Victor seized him by the neck and forced him to look into his eyes, successfully charming Thomas as well.[2]
The two Hunters were subsequently transferred to Violet's mansion rather than being released.

#### Vampire Society
Thomas and Jimmy remained imprisoned while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] traveled to [[Nightingale]]. Their captivity was considerably less severe than might have been expected; when [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] later inspected them, the [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] had food and were playing cards inside the room where they were being held.[3]
Ruby initially considered both men too weak and inexperienced to be particularly valuable. However, she eventually realized that two low-ranking Hunters who could return to the Inquisition without attracting significant suspicion could function as intelligence assets. She therefore obtained magical contracts created by Witches and offered them an extremely simple choice: become her spies or die.[3]
The contract contained three principal clauses:
- Betrayal and Death: betraying Ruby would result in death.
- Master and Confidentiality: revealing the existence of their arrangement or allowing the magical contract to be exposed would result in death.
- Espionage: Thomas and Jimmy would operate as Ruby's spies inside their organization.[3]
Jimmy immediately understood the implications and became visibly depressed. Thomas, by contrast, initially did not even understand why his partner was upset. Jimmy was forced to tell him to remain silent before Thomas accidentally made their situation worse.[3]
Neither man willingly chose Victor's side at this stage. Their intelligence work began because Ruby had made refusal equivalent to execution.

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Thomas and Jimmy remained embedded within Hunter society for years, gradually becoming some of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Ruby's longest-serving Human intelligence assets. Their position allowed them to regularly transmit information concerning the Inquisition and its internal activity without openly revealing their allegiance.[6]
By Chapter 312, the two were explicitly identified as the first [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] Victor had encountered and as Ruby's spies. They had also begun providing intelligence to [[Mizuki Elderblood]], who had separated herself from her former organization but retained numerous contacts and continued investigating suspicious Hunter activity.[6]
The narration identified Jimmy as "Dick Head" and Thomas as "The Shounen Protagonist", reflecting the extreme contrast between their personalities. Jimmy remained the cynical and calculating member of the partnership, while Thomas retained the direct, passionate and comparatively idealistic mentality that had characterized him since his debut.[6]
Their intelligence became particularly useful when Mizuki discovered that Vampires, [[Werewolves]] and Demons—species that normally possessed little reason to cooperate—were coordinating attacks. Because she suspected Hunters were also involved, Thomas and Jimmy provided a secure method of monitoring developments inside the Inquisition. Ruby and Victor permitted Mizuki to receive information from them in exchange for her own cooperation and silence concerning their identities.[6]
Over the years, Thomas' attitude toward his forced employment changed substantially. Ruby's faction paid the two spies according to the quality and usefulness of the intelligence they supplied. Thomas had grown increasingly dissatisfied with the Inquisition because it constantly demanded sacrifice in the name of duty while providing little in return. Victor's faction, by contrast, rewarded results.[4]
Thomas directed a considerable portion of that money toward the orphanage where he had grown up. His support eventually extended beyond simple financial donations; he acquired protective bounded fields created by Witches, which helped prevent Lesser Demons from advancing into the orphanage during the Demonic invasion.[4]
By this point, what had begun as forced espionage had become genuine service.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Years later, after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s transformation into the Dragon God of [[Chaos]], War began gathering individuals connected to Victor's past. Some were brought before him for punishment, but Thomas and Jimmy were summoned for an entirely different reason.[4]
War appeared before the gathered prisoners and then summoned the two Human Exorcists through darkness. Both initially reacted with instinctive fear toward the unknown Being standing before them. War identified them by name and informed them that Victor was pleased with the work they had performed over the years and intended to reward them.[4]
The statement genuinely confused both men. They knew their betrayal of the Inquisition had originally occurred only because they had been forced into it, and neither believed that coerced service should have earned Victor's gratitude. War immediately addressed this concern by explaining that Victor understood their true feelings perfectly.[4]
According to War, both Thomas and Jimmy had originally felt disgust and hatred toward their situation. However, each time they received news concerning Victor's accomplishments and observed his continuous rise in power, their attitudes slowly changed. Their espionage eventually became increasingly enthusiastic, especially after Victor received open recognition from the Gods during the [[Supernatural Gathering]]. What began through coercion had transformed into genuine loyalty.[4]
War offered them power as a reward. Jimmy immediately asked whether their espionage assignment was still necessary, and War explained that it was obsolete; Victor's senses had advanced enough that he could observe the entire planet if necessary, while more specialized beings now handled intelligence operations.[4]
Thomas then raised his hand and asked a much more direct question: if Victor already possessed them as effectively enslaved agents, why bother rewarding them at all rather than simply continuing to use them?[4]
Jimmy immediately elbowed Thomas for asking something he considered dangerously unnecessary, but War considered the question legitimate. He explained that Victor's system operated according to a simple principle. Those who worked diligently, demonstrated results, remained loyal and continually improved themselves would be rewarded regardless of whether they began as slaves, ordinary citizens or members of the highest levels of society. Those who opposed Victor would receive the opposite treatment.[4]
The answer resonated strongly with Thomas. Unlike the Inquisition, which he believed constantly demanded sacrifice without returning anything meaningful, Victor's faction had repeatedly compensated him for his work. For Thomas, whose worldview was considerably simpler than Jimmy's, that fairness was enough.[4]
He immediately accepted War's offer. Jimmy followed him.
Darkness swallowed the two Humans as War's power invaded and reconstructed their bodies. Their Human existence was replaced by a new supernatural nature derived from the same Essence of Chaos associated with War himself.[4][5]
War formally declared himself the Bearer of the [[Will Solace|Will]] of the Chaos Dragon God and commanded them to rise as [[Heralds of Chaos]]. Thomas and Jimmy emerged with faces composed entirely of darkness, dark supernatural armor and wings formed from pure darkness.[5]
The newly created Heralds were then transported to the boundary where Reality and Imagination exerted extraordinary influence. Countless galaxies surrounded them as they instinctively and perfectly synchronized themselves before kneeling toward Victor's incomprehensible Cosmic form.[5]
Victor's presence distorted Reality, appeared to consume everything around it and simultaneously generated strange new galaxies. Although Thomas and Jimmy retained the consciousness of their former Human selves, the transformation had already significantly altered that Human element without either fully realizing it.[5]
Lucy and Karen subsequently accepted the same transformation, increasing the number of Heralds to four. Roxanne later clarified that the Heralds had been created from the same Chaos Essence involved in War's creation and were therefore genuinely connected to Victor, although their connection was considerably shallower than hers or Amara's.[5]
The four Heralds later received Victor's will simultaneously and departed to pursue additional enemies from his past, beginning Thomas' new role as a supernatural agent directly serving the Chaos Dragon God.[5]

### Skills & Abilities
Thomas' abilities differ radically between his existence as a Human Hunter and his current form as a Herald of [[Chaos]]. His Human abilities were based upon Faith, religious equipment and anti-supernatural combat techniques, while his current physiology originates from War's Chaos Essence.

#### Herald of Chaos
- Herald of [[Chaos]] Physiology: Thomas was transformed by War into one of the first members of the [[Heralds of Chaos]]. The process completely reconstructed his Human body and granted him a supernatural existence directly associated with War and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[4][5]
- Chaos Essence: Roxanne confirmed that Thomas and the other Heralds were created through the same Essence of Chaos involved in War's creation. This gives Thomas a genuine supernatural connection with Victor Elderblood, although the connection is shallower than those possessed by beings such as Roxanne and Amara.[5]
- Supernatural Empowerment: Thomas' transformation raised him far beyond the level of the novice Human Hunter he once was. War's offer was explicitly a reward intended to grant the two men a superior existence and greater power.[4][5]
- Human Consciousness Retention: Thomas retains the memories and consciousness of his former Human identity following transformation. However, the Human aspect of his mind and existence was stated to have been significantly altered by the process even though the Heralds themselves had not yet recognized the extent of the change.[5]
- Darkness Manifestation: Thomas' transformed face and wings consist of supernatural darkness. The complete offensive and defensive applications of this darkness have not yet been individually demonstrated.[5]
- Wing Manifestation: Thomas possesses a pair of wings made from pure darkness after becoming a Herald.[5]
- Flight: The Heralds are capable of freely existing and moving within open Space after their transformation.[5]
- Space Survival: Thomas remained completely functional in an open cosmic environment containing countless visible galaxies without requiring air, pressure protection or conventional life-support equipment.[5]
- Connection to Victor's [[Will Solace|Will]]: Thomas and the other Heralds can receive Victor's will through their supernatural connection. All four responded simultaneously when Victor ordered them to pursue additional enemies.[5]
- Dimensional/Cosmic Translocation: Thomas was capable of disappearing from War's location and reappearing inside Victor's deeper internal cosmic region. The exact degree to which this movement is Thomas' personal ability rather than a function of War or his connection to Victor remains unclear.[5]

#### Former Abilities
- Human Physiology: Thomas was originally an ordinary Human who underwent Inquisition training for combat against supernatural creatures.[1]
- Hunter Training: Thomas possessed formal combat training, knowledge of Vampires and experience using Faith-based techniques and blessed weapons against supernatural opponents.[1]
- Faith Manipulation: Thomas could channel Divine/Holy Energy through prayer, religious declarations and blessed equipment.[1]
- Physical Enhancement: Thomas could invoke a blessing intended to strengthen his body and reduce or remove fatigue, allowing him to temporarily increase his physical performance during combat.[1]
- Speed Enhancement: After empowering himself through Faith, Thomas could explosively increase his movement speed enough to momentarily surprise the recently transformed [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[1]
- Stamina Enhancement: Thomas specifically prayed for a body capable of fighting without fatigue, although the enormous amount of Energy consumed by his strongest early attack still exhausted him afterward.[1]
- Holy Sword Creation: Thomas could transform the crucifix worn around his neck into a Western sword through Divine power.[1]
- [[Holy Energy]] Coating: The blade was covered by golden Holy Energy particularly effective against Vampires.[1]
- Holy Energy Projection: Thomas could extend the Energy beyond the physical edge of the sword. Victor physically avoided the blade itself but was still grazed by its supernatural Energy.[1]
- Anti-Vampire Damage: Contact with Thomas' Holy Energy produced severe internal burning pain in Victor despite his abnormal physiology. His strongest strike was described as capable of causing permanent injury to an ordinary Vampire.[1]
- Energy Manipulation: Thomas possessed limited control over the Holy Energy surrounding his sword. His control was still poor as a novice, and Jimmy criticized him for wasting a potentially successful attack because he failed to direct the Energy properly.[1]
- Resistance to Vampiric Charm: Like other Inquisition members, Thomas possessed protection intended to prevent Vampires from controlling his mind. Victor's abnormal Charm nevertheless bypassed the protection and enthralled him almost immediately.[2]
- Espionage: Thomas spent years operating as a covert intelligence asset for Ruby and Victor while remaining connected to Hunter society.[6][4]
- Information Gathering: His continued position within the Inquisition allowed him to provide intelligence regarding Hunter movements, internal activity and possible conspiracies.[6]

### Relationships

#### Jimmy
Jimmy is Thomas' closest known friend and longtime partner. Their relationship predates their encounter with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], and according to Jimmy the Inquisition itself paired the two together rather than allowing them to choose their own partner.[2]
The organization apparently considered their opposing personalities complementary. Thomas was loyal, emotional and straightforward, whereas Jimmy was cautious, cynical and willing to compromise for survival. Victor believed the partnership may have been deliberately arranged in the hope that Thomas' loyalty would influence Jimmy over time.[2]
Despite constantly being called an idiot by Jimmy, Thomas clearly cares about him. When Victor charmed Jimmy, Thomas immediately attempted to attack Victor in retaliation, even though doing so was strategically suicidal. Jimmy in turn repeatedly protects Thomas from his own mouth by interrupting or elbowing him whenever Thomas is about to say something dangerous.[2][4]
The pair remained together through imprisonment, forced espionage, years of intelligence work and their eventual transformation into [[Heralds of Chaos]].[3][6][4][5]

#### Ruby Elderblood Scarlett
[[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] transformed Thomas from a prisoner into a covert operative by binding him through a Witch-created magical contract. At the beginning, Thomas had no meaningful choice in the matter; refusal would simply have resulted in his death.[3]
Over time, however, the practical relationship became considerably less hostile. Ruby's faction rewarded useful intelligence, and Thomas increasingly focused on his espionage assignment because those rewards allowed him to improve both his own life and the lives of the children in the orphanage where he had been raised.[4]

#### Victor Elderblood
Thomas originally regarded [[Victor Elderblood]] as an enemy and hated him as a Vampire. During their first meeting he attempted to decapitate Victor with a Holy sword, and later became enraged when Victor mentally controlled Jimmy.[1][2]
His attitude evolved gradually through years of indirect service. Victor's continuous growth certainly affected Thomas' perception, but his eventual loyalty was based on more than fear. Thomas became convinced that Victor's faction rewarded effort fairly in a way the Inquisition had failed to do.[4]
The fact that Victor eventually remembered and rewarded two comparatively insignificant Human spies after years of service reinforced precisely the principle that had won Thomas over. Through War, Victor elevated him into a Herald of [[Chaos]] rather than continuing to use him as an expendable agent.[4][5]

#### War
War is Thomas' current Progenitor and immediate superior within the [[Heralds of Chaos]]. Acting as the bearer of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s will, War personally summoned Thomas and Jimmy, explained why Victor had recognized their loyalty and offered them supernatural power as compensation for their service.[4]
War subsequently used [[Chaos]] Essence to transform the two Humans and formally declared them Heralds of Chaos.[5]
