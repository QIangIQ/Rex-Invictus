### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 709
Jonathan Eric Gonzales was a 32-year-old Human criminal and serial offender operating in England. He was wanted for numerous serious crimes, including trafficking women, sexual violence, child abuse and the murders of disabled men and women whom he regarded as "defective". His final known victim before his capture was the sixteen-year-old [[Vanessa Alekerth]], daughter of [[Valeria Alekerth]].[1]
Jonathan survived the catastrophe surrounding the Demonic War despite being a wanted criminal, but was eventually captured by followers of the newly developing [[Blood God Religion]]. He was brought before Valeria during a ritual intended to contact Hell, where his identity and crimes were publicly recited. After the ritual successfully summoned Valefar, Jonathan was personally thrown through a portal into Hell and condemned to continued punishment at the hands of Demons, while Valeria was given a supernatural Orb through which she could observe his fate.[1]
Jonathan has no demonstrated supernatural powers and is important primarily because his crimes became one of the events that shaped Valeria's later devotion to Alucard, eventually helping transform her into one of the most important Human leaders of the Blood God Religion.

### Physical Appearance
Jonathan's physical appearance is not described in detail. The novel identifies him as a 32-year-old Human man but does not establish his height, build, hair color, eye color, clothing or other distinguishing physical characteristics.[1]

### Personality
Jonathan was remorseless, emotionally detached and profoundly lacking in empathy. Even after being captured and brought into a ritual chamber where other condemned criminals were being killed around him, he remained unusually calm. Rather than panicking immediately, he watched the blood-filled [[Magic]] Circle and casually asked why he was being killed in what he described as a Hellish ritual.[1]
His reaction toward [[Valeria Alekerth]] demonstrates the depth of his emotional detachment. When Valeria recited his name, age and crimes with obvious hatred, Jonathan did not initially recognize her and casually wondered whether she had been one of his victims. Once she introduced herself as [[Vanessa Alekerth]]'s mother, he remembered Vanessa as his "last target" and asked whether Valeria's actions were motivated by revenge. His indifferent attitude toward the suffering he had caused enraged Valeria and the other women present.[1]
Jonathan's crimes were not portrayed as impulsive acts. He possessed an openly supremacist worldview and murdered disabled men and women while claiming that he was cleansing the world of people he considered "defective". This indicates that he rationalized at least part of his violence through an ideology that dehumanized his victims rather than regarding his actions as morally wrong.[1]
Despite his depravity, Jonathan was not unintelligent. After Roxanne began preaching to the women participating in the ritual, Jonathan internally recognized that she was deliberately manipulating their existing hatred and quickly concluded that Roxanne and Valefar were cooperating. He essentially acknowledged himself to be a terrible person while simultaneously realizing that the religious gathering surrounding him was being carefully directed toward a specific objective.[1]
His composure finally broke when the ritual actually succeeded. Jonathan had initially assumed the women intended to summon a Demon, but became visibly shocked when he realized they were connected to the supernatural forces serving the being Humanity had branded the "Evil God". His fear became considerably more pronounced once Valefar appeared and the reality of his intended punishment became unavoidable.[1]

### History
Before the events of the story, Jonathan became a wanted criminal in England. His known offenses included trafficking women, sexual violence, child abuse and multiple murders. He specifically targeted disabled men and women because of his belief that killing them amounted to removing "defective" people from the world.[1]
One of his victims was sixteen-year-old [[Vanessa Alekerth]]. Jonathan sexually assaulted and murdered Vanessa before dismembering her remains. Vanessa became the final victim he specifically acknowledged during his appearance in Chapter 709, although the exact circumstances surrounding Jonathan's later capture are not shown.[1]
Vanessa's death devastated her mother, [[Valeria Alekerth]]. After the Demonic War transformed Human society and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] Alucard became worshipped by an emerging underground religion, Valeria eventually became involved with women who had similarly lost family members to violent criminals. Jonathan was subsequently captured and selected for punishment during one of the religion's early rituals.[1]

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Jonathan was introduced inside an old mansion in England where several women dressed entirely in black were conducting a blood ritual. Three captured criminals had been positioned around a [[Magic]] Circle, with four hooded women acting as the ritual participants. The first two prisoners were killed and their blood allowed to flow into the Magic Circle while the women invoked the Blood God and described their sacrifices as the murder of sinners in the name of the God of Murderers.[1]
Jonathan was the third prisoner. Unlike the terrified second victim, he remained remarkably calm and watched the Magic Circle begin glowing blood-red before asking why he was apparently going to die in a Hellish ritual. One of the women responded by identifying him completely: Jonathan Eric Gonzales, age 32, a wanted criminal responsible for trafficking women, sexual violence, child abuse and murdering disabled people under the justification that he was cleansing the world of those he considered defective.[1]
Jonathan noticed the intense hatred in the woman's voice and casually asked whether she had been one of his victims. The woman removed her hood and introduced herself as [[Valeria Alekerth]], mother of sixteen-year-old [[Vanessa Alekerth]]. Jonathan recognized Vanessa's name and recalled her as his last target, responding to Valeria's grief with such indifference that both Valeria and the other women became increasingly enraged. Another cultist had to remind Valeria to control herself and complete the ritual rather than simply killing Jonathan immediately.[1]
Valeria could have killed Jonathan directly, but simple death was not what she wanted. The ritual was intended to inflict a far greater punishment and simultaneously establish contact with the supernatural forces worshipped by the women. The participants explained that the blood and deaths of sinners would feed the ritual enough to summon a Being. Jonathan correctly guessed that they were attempting to call a Demon from Hell, only to learn that the group believed themselves connected to the forces serving the ruler Humanity commonly regarded as the Evil God.[1]
The ritual required another component in addition to the blood already offered. Valeria deliberately cut her own hand and contributed the Blood of a Faithful Devotee given willingly. Her blood completed the process, causing the Magic Circle to intensify while the temperature of the room fell. A portal then opened and Valefar, a powerful female Demon serving [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Hell, emerged in response to an old contract.[1]
The ritual also revealed the identity of another participant. A red-haired woman removed her hood and introduced herself as Roxanne, an Archbishop responsible for spreading the teachings of the Blood God throughout the Mortal World. Roxanne presented Victor not as the "Evil God" feared by the surviving Human population but as a protector who had helped save Humanity from the apocalypse, a God who valued family, home, martial honor and revenge against those who harmed innocents or his followers.[1]
Jonathan recognized that Roxanne was intentionally exploiting the grief and anger of the women surrounding him. He internally noted that despite being a terrible person himself, he was not stupid and could recognize manipulation. He also correctly deduced that Roxanne and Valefar were coordinating with one another rather than acting independently.[1]
His realization did nothing to alter his fate. Valefar grabbed Jonathan and threw him directly through the portal into Hell. The women were then shown an image of numerous Demons beginning his punishment. Valefar informed Valeria that Jonathan would remain there indefinitely rather than receiving the quick death Valeria had originally been capable of giving him.[1]
Valeria asked whether she could continue watching. Valefar responded by giving her a supernatural Orb linked to Jonathan's punishment. By sacrificing a portion of her stamina to activate the Artifact, Valeria could observe what was happening to him and even provide suggestions regarding his punishment. The gift became a direct manifestation of the [[Blood God Religion]]'s emerging doctrine that Victor represented not merely Blood and War but Revenge against those who deliberately harmed the innocent or members of his community.[1]
Jonathan's punishment consequently became part of one of the earliest directly shown ceremonies of the Blood God Religion. The assembled devotees ended the ritual by repeatedly proclaiming their loyalty to Alucard, while Jonathan disappeared from the Mortal World permanently.[1]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Jonathan himself never returned to the story, but the consequences of his crime against Vanessa remained important centuries later. [[Valeria Alekerth]] rose from the grieving mother shown during Jonathan's punishment into one of the most devoted and important leaders of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s religion. Despite everything Victor's faction eventually gave her, Valeria never demanded that her God somehow repay her for Vanessa's death.[2]
Victor nevertheless remembered what had happened to his follower. By this point his authority over Souls and access to the greater systems surrounding the [[Akashic Records]] had developed enough for him to investigate Vanessa's fate. Although much of the information that once constituted Vanessa's personal identity had already been stripped from her Soul by the normal processes surrounding death and reincarnation, the Akashic system still retained the historical Record of the person she had been.[3]
Victor therefore reconstructed Vanessa's identity and eventually reunited her with Valeria, undoing the permanent loss Jonathan had inflicted upon the Alekerth family even though the historical crime itself remained part of their past.[2][3][4]
Jonathan's own condition was not revisited. His last confirmed fate remains the punishment imposed upon him in Hell after Valefar removed him from the Human World.[1]

### Skills & Abilities
Jonathan was an ordinary Human with no demonstrated [[Magic]], supernatural Bloodline, Divine ability or specialized power. His appearance serves primarily as a narrative example of the kinds of Human criminals targeted by the early [[Blood God Religion]] rather than as a supernatural combatant.
- Human Physiology: Jonathan possessed the ordinary physical characteristics and limitations of a Human. Nothing suggests that he was a High Human, Hunter, Mage or member of another supernatural group.[1]
- Normal Human Physical Capabilities: No superhuman strength, speed, durability, regeneration or supernatural senses were demonstrated.
- Composure: Jonathan remained unusually calm while restrained inside the ritual chamber even after witnessing the deaths of the other prisoners. His composure only substantially weakened once the supernatural ritual succeeded and an actual Demon appeared.[1]
- Situational Awareness: Jonathan quickly recognized that Roxanne was manipulating the grief and hatred of the women participating in the gathering and correctly deduced that she and Valefar were cooperating.[1]
- Criminal Cunning: Jonathan had accumulated numerous serious offenses while remaining alive until the post-Demonic-War era despite being a wanted criminal. The novel does not provide enough information to determine whether this resulted from planning, luck, institutional collapse following the apocalypse or outside assistance.
- No Supernatural Resistance: Jonathan possessed no demonstrated protection against Magic, Demons, Hell portals, Soul-based phenomena or other supernatural effects and was completely helpless once Valefar appeared.[1]

### Crimes
Jonathan's known crimes were explicitly listed by the followers of the Blood God during Chapter 709.[1]
- Human Trafficking: Jonathan was wanted for trafficking women.
- Sexual Violence: He was wanted for multiple sexual offenses.
- Child Abuse: Child abuse was included among his known crimes.
- Serial Murder: Jonathan murdered multiple victims.
- Targeting Disabled People: Some of his victims were disabled men and women whom Jonathan deliberately targeted because he considered them "defective".
- Murder of [[Vanessa Alekerth]]: Vanessa, Valeria's sixteen-year-old daughter, was one of Jonathan's victims and the specific crime that eventually led Valeria to personally participate in his punishment.[1]
The novel does not provide an exact total number of Jonathan's victims.

### Relationships

#### Valeria Alekerth
[[Valeria Alekerth]] was the mother of one of Jonathan's victims and the person most directly responsible for arranging his punishment. Jonathan did not initially recognize Valeria when she revealed herself during the ritual, which suggests that he had never meaningfully interacted with her before Vanessa's death.[1]
Valeria hated Jonathan intensely but deliberately restrained herself from simply killing him. She considered death insufficient punishment and participated in the Blood God ritual specifically so that he could be delivered to Hell instead. Valefar later gave Valeria an Orb allowing her to observe Jonathan's continuing punishment.[1]
Jonathan's crime became one of the defining traumas behind Valeria's life. Her later devotion to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and rise within the [[Blood God Religion]] cannot be reduced entirely to revenge, but the religion's willingness to punish the man responsible for Vanessa's death was one of the earliest events shown in her relationship with the faith.

#### Vanessa Alekerth
[[Vanessa Alekerth]] was Jonathan's final specifically identified victim before his capture. She was sixteen years old when Jonathan sexually assaulted and murdered her.[1]
Jonathan displayed no remorse when confronted by Vanessa's mother and simply remembered Vanessa as his previous "target". His attitude toward her death helped convince the women participating in the ritual that he felt no regret for his crimes.[1]
Centuries later, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] reconstructed Vanessa's identity through his advanced Soul-related abilities and information retained within the Akashic system, eventually returning her to Valeria.[2][3][4]

#### Valefar
Valefar was the Demon summoned by the [[Blood God Religion]]'s ritual. She personally seized Jonathan and threw him through the portal into Hell, where Demons began carrying out his punishment.[1]
Jonathan initially expected the ritual to summon an ordinary Demon but became visibly frightened after realizing the extent of the supernatural organization supporting Valeria and the other devotees.

#### Roxanne
Roxanne was the Archbishop directing the religious gathering and using Jonathan's punishment as both an act of revenge and a demonstration of the developing [[Blood God Religion]]'s beliefs. Jonathan correctly recognized that Roxanne was deliberately amplifying the emotions of the women around her and cooperating with Valefar.[1]

### Legacy
Jonathan was an otherwise insignificant ordinary Human, but his actions indirectly influenced the development of one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s most important Mortal followers. Vanessa's death became the trauma that brought Valeria into direct contact with the emerging [[Blood God Religion]], and Jonathan's punishment became one of the earliest examples of the religion presenting Victor as a God of Revenge who protected his followers while condemning those who preyed upon innocents.[1]
The later resurrection of Vanessa substantially altered that legacy. Jonathan had permanently taken Vanessa from her mother under ordinary Mortal conditions, but Victor's later command over Souls and the Records of existence allowed him to reconstruct the girl Jonathan had murdered and return her to Valeria.[2][3][4]
Jonathan himself gained no supernatural importance from this. He remained what he had always been: an ordinary Human criminal whose significance came entirely from the damage he inflicted upon other people and the consequences that followed.
