#### Debut

Yue, better known by her Witch Codename Smile Witch, is a Human Witch, a Titled Witch and one of [[Poseidon|the Master]] Witches of Arcane. She is an old friend of June and one of the high-ranking Witches serving under Evie Moriarty, the Witch Queen.[1]
Unlike June, who earned a personal Title but remained below the rank of Master Witch, Yue has attained both distinctions. Her Codename derives from the distinctive fox-like smile she almost constantly wears, a habitual expression that persists even when she becomes serious, worried or frightened.[1] Yue is also an experienced information broker with contacts inside Arcane, knowledge of the political methods employed by high-ranking Witches and access to intelligence concerning the Witch Queen's secret dealings during the Demonic War.[1]

### Physical Appearance
Yue is a beautiful, youthful-looking woman standing approximately 165 centimeters tall, making her exactly five centimeters taller than June. She possesses a slim, balanced physique described as neither particularly full nor excessively thin.[1]
At her debut, Yue had long green hair. As she walked, however, the hair gradually changed into a violet-pink color. This is not a natural biological trait but the result of a simple Witch spell that allows its user to alter their hair color at will. Yue mastered the spell so thoroughly that she can perform it casually and apparently without meaningful effort simply because she considers being able to change her hair whenever she wants convenient.[1]
Yue dresses considerably more like a modern Human woman than the stereotypical image of an ancient Witch. During her introduction she wore black pants, a white shirt exposing her shoulders and midriff, and a pair of earrings.[1]
Her most recognizable facial characteristic is her persistent smile. The narration describes it as resembling a fox's smile more than an ordinary Human expression. The smile is sufficiently characteristic of her identity that it is directly associated with the Title Smile Witch; even June can distinguish when genuine seriousness is concealed beneath Yue's usual amused expression.[1]

### Personality
Yue is playful, mischievous, perceptive, cautious, opportunistic and considerably more serious than her perpetually amused expression initially suggests. She enjoys teasing June relentlessly, intentionally calling her incorrect names such as Judy, Jonas and Julieta despite obviously remembering June's real name. She also treats herself as the older sister in their friendship, casually pulling June into hugs, commenting on her weight and physically dragging her away for private conversations while ignoring June's complaints.[1]
Her teasing demeanor does not prevent genuine affection. Yue was sincerely worried about June when she learned that Arcane was being sealed during the Demonic War. Once she confirmed that June had returned safely and had no intention of recklessly entering a conflict between vastly more powerful factions, Yue's concern immediately diminished. Her understanding of June is deep enough that she trusts the Greedy Witch's instinct for self-preservation once she knows June possesses the relevant information.[1]
Yue shares the pragmatic survival mentality common among experienced Witches. She believes Witches have historically survived by anticipating danger, maintaining contingency plans and preparing for the worst possible outcome because stronger supernatural factions frequently attempt to exploit them. She consequently advises June not to become involved in the political game surrounding the Witch Queen, [[Diablo]] and Divine Artifacts, describing it as a conflict between major players whose consequences ordinary Witches would be foolish to invite upon themselves.[1]
Despite this caution, Yue is extremely curious. She maintains contacts capable of acquiring secret information, follows rumors concerning the Witch Queen's activities and is immediately interested in learning about [[Victor Elderblood]] once she hears about the newly risen Vampire Count. June's increasingly bizarre description of Victor's personality and romantic preferences shocks Yue rather than discouraging her; her curiosity only becomes stronger, and she insists upon hearing more.[1]
Like many Witches, Yue also possesses a transactional approach to information. When June asks what she knows about Evie's secret activities, Yue instinctively asks how much June is willing to pay. She eventually provides the information for free because it would have been comparatively easy for June to obtain independently, demonstrating that Yue is opportunistic without being unreasonable toward a close friend.[1]
Yue displays considerable confidence in Evie Moriarty. Although she does not know the full extent of the Witch Queen's plans, she refuses to assume Evie has acted irrationally merely because [[Poseidon|the Master]] Witches were excluded. Yue reasons that Evie became Witch Queen for a reason and would simply order the Master Witches to act if she actually required their assistance.[1]
She is similarly loyal to Arcane as a civilization. Yue believes that whatever private objectives Evie possesses, the Witch Queen will ultimately act with Arcane's interests in mind. Rather than objecting to the enormous payment Evie receives for cooperating with Diablo, Yue becomes impressed by how effectively the Queen has exploited the situation to strengthen the Witches before the larger war has even fully unfolded.[1]
Despite her composure, Yue possesses an intense fear and hatred of insects, particularly spiders. June's threat to throw her into a nest of giant insects causes Yue's body to tremble despite her maintaining the same smiling expression. She becomes openly agitated when reminded that one of Evie's daughters possesses insect-related [[Magic]].[1]

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Yue first appears in Arcane shortly after the apparent Demonic invasion of the Witch Realm. The attack had actually been part of a larger arrangement involving Evie Moriarty and [[Diablo]], but most ordinary Witches were not given the complete details. Arcane's sudden change in behavior and the recall of Witches operating among outside factions consequently generated confusion throughout the Realm.[1]
While June observed the unusual activity around her, Yue approached and immediately began annoying her old friend by calling her Judy. Yue was introduced as a 165-centimeter-tall Witch with long green hair that changed toward violet-pink as she walked. The hair transformation was revealed to be a simple [[Magic]] Spell Yue had mastered to the point of casual activation. More importantly, she was identified as Yue, Codename Smile Witch, an old friend of June who had personally earned a Title from the Witch Queen and, unlike June, had also attained the rank of Master Witch.[1]
Yue continued deliberately misnaming June as Jonas and Julieta before pulling the five-centimeter-shorter Witch into a hug. Her teasing rapidly shifted once she confirmed June was safe. Yue revealed that she had been genuinely worried because she knew the entire Arcane Realm was about to be closed, and she invited June back to her house so they could speak privately.[1]
Inside Yue's home, she explained the situation she understood. The Witch Queen was cooperating with Demons during the ongoing war, a decision dangerous enough to threaten the traditional neutrality maintained by Arcane toward other supernatural factions. More seriously, Evie had completely sealed the spatial boundaries of the Arcane Realm: ordinary Witches could no longer leave, with only the Witch Queen and her daughters exempt from the restriction.[1]
June initially questioned whether even [[Poseidon|the Master]] Witches had been informed of Evie's true objective. Yue confirmed that they had not; the Witch Queen's actual plans were restricted to herself and her daughters. Rather than taking this exclusion as an insult, Yue reasoned that Evie would have ordered the Master Witches to participate if she required them. Her response demonstrated both her acceptance of the Witch hierarchy and her recognition that the Queen possessed power and information far beyond their own.[1]
After confirming that June only wanted information so she could prepare rather than recklessly assist her outside contractors, Yue became willing to share what she knew. She initially jokingly attempted to charge June for the information before deciding that the rumors were easy enough to obtain that accepting payment would be unfair.[1]
Yue explained that Evie's secrecy was probably intended both to protect the Witches and prevent information from leaking to other factions. Even restricting the truth to Titled and high-ranking Witches would be dangerous because Witches were independent and opportunistic enough that some could sell valuable information if sufficiently rewarded. Since Evie's cooperation with Diablo could damage Arcane's neutrality, containing knowledge of the arrangement was strategically sensible.[1]
Yue then demonstrated that she possessed considerably more than rumors. With movements of her hands, she produced multiple Magic Circles throughout her house before taking an orb from her pocket and using it to project secret footage obtained through one of her contacts. The contact had acquired the recording using a new form of espionage that the Witches were developing.[1]
The footage showed a warehouse containing enormous numbers of rare Artifacts, pills, equipment and other supernatural resources. Among them were numerous Divine Artifacts. Yue believed the collection represented at least part of the payment Evie had received for assisting Diablo, while June noticed that some of the items were contaminated by Miasma.[1]
Yue immediately warned against investigating further. She described the affair as a game belonging to much greater powers and believed interfering would only bring disastrous consequences upon them. She trusted that Evie would continue acting in Arcane's interests and advised June to do what experienced Witches traditionally did when confronted with forces beyond their control: remain silent, gather enough information to prepare and always maintain a second plan in case the worst occurred.[1]
The two also discussed the Divine equipment itself. Yue understood that simply possessing a Divine Artifact did not mean an ordinary mortal could wield it effectively. Divine equipment placed an enormous load upon non-Divine bodies, and sufficiently powerful items were fundamentally designed for Gods rather than ordinary mortals.[1]
Nevertheless, Yue believed that the resources Evie had acquired could dramatically increase Arcane's overall strength. She was impressed enough to speculate that the Witch Queen had secured sufficient resources to allow Arcane to challenge some existing Pantheons. Yue recognized the operation as an extraordinarily advantageous move in which Evie had obtained a major portion of Earth's supernatural resources before the broader invasion had even properly begun.[1]
This possibility connected to an older aspiration of the Witches. Yue expressed hope that Arcane might eventually grow powerful enough for Witches to obtain a genuine planet of their own, comparable to the civilizations possessed by Vampires and [[Werewolves]], where they could finally live in relative peace rather than constantly fearing exploitation by stronger races.[1]
After the serious discussion ended, Yue immediately returned to teasing June. She had heard rumors concerning the exceptional beauty of the newly appointed Vampire Count [[Victor Elderblood]] and wanted to know whether those claims were true. June's visible reaction confirmed the rumors well enough for Yue to demand details.[1]
June initially refused to provide information without payment, accusing Yue of attempting to steal one of her valuable contractors. Yue freely admitted that working with Victor might be profitable; as both a Master Witch and a Titled Witch, she reasoned that her Magic might eventually prove useful to him. More importantly, she was personally curious about the man.[1]
The discussion briefly exposes Yue's greatest irrational fear when June threatens to throw her into a nest of giant insects. Yue physically trembles while maintaining her smile and angrily declares that insects—particularly spiders—should be eradicated. She becomes even more irritated when reminded that the Witch Queen's sixth daughter possesses insect-based Magic.[1]
When an announcement later informed Arcane that Queen Evie had returned from the supernatural leaders' meeting, June expected Yue to react immediately. Yue instead told her to continue the story about Victor. From her perspective, Arcane remained sealed and neither woman could meaningfully influence the Queen's actions; behaving normally was therefore the most sensible option.[1]
June eventually described Victor's unusual preferences and relationships in enough detail to leave Yue momentarily speechless. Rather than losing interest, Yue became even more curious and asked June to continue. Their conversation consequently ended with the two drinking together while June prepared to give Yue a much longer account of the new Vampire Count.[1]

### Skills & Abilities
Yue is both a Titled Witch and a Master Witch, placing her above ordinary Witches and above Titled Witches such as June who have not attained [[Poseidon|the Master]] rank. Her exact combat abilities have not been demonstrated, but Chapter 529 establishes several forms of [[Magic]], expertise and professional knowledge she possesses.[1]

#### Magic
- Mana Manipulation: As a Witch, Yue possesses the ability to perform [[Magic]] and has developed sufficiently to attain the rank of Master Witch.[1]
- Master-Level Witchcraft: Yue has progressed beyond ordinary and merely Titled Witches to become a Master Witch. The exact requirements of the rank are not detailed during her appearance, but June explicitly treats Master Witches as a distinct higher group within Arcane's hierarchy.[1]
- Magic Circle Creation: Yue can generate multiple Magic Circles with casual hand movements. Before displaying her secret intelligence to June, she rotates her hands and causes several Magic Circles to appear throughout her house.[1]
- Hair Color Manipulation: Yue has mastered a simple Witch spell that allows her to alter the color of her hair at will. She has refined the Magic enough to activate it through casual gestures while walking, with her hair visibly transitioning from green to violet-pink during her debut.[1]
- Casual Spellcasting: Yue's mastery of her hair-changing spell demonstrates enough technical familiarity that a spell which normally requires deliberate Witchcraft can be maintained or altered through insignificant movements.[1]

#### Information Gathering
- Information Brokerage: Yue treats information as a tradable commodity and instinctively attempts to charge June when asked to reveal what she knows concerning the Witch Queen's activities.[1]
- Intelligence Network: Yue maintains contacts capable of gathering secret information from locations associated with the Witch Queen's operations.[1]
- Espionage Access: One of Yue's contacts acquired covert footage through a new form of espionage being developed by the Witches, giving Yue access to information that had not been distributed even among Arcane's Master Witches.[1]
- Information Security: Yue understands the risks created when sensitive intelligence spreads among independent Witches and recognizes why Evie sealed Arcane and restricted knowledge of her plans.[1]
- Operational Secrecy: Yue deliberately shares sensitive information only after determining June's intentions and makes clear that revealing what she has shown could create serious consequences.[1]

#### Magical and Supernatural Knowledge
- Divine Artifact Knowledge: Yue can recognize the importance and limitations of Divine Artifacts and understands that ordinary mortals cannot safely or efficiently wield powerful Divine equipment because of the strain it places upon their bodies.[1]
- Divine Power Knowledge: Yue understands the qualitative difference between mortal [[Magic]] and Divine equipment sufficiently to explain why such Artifacts are fundamentally intended for Gods.[1]
- Miasma Awareness: During the investigation of Evie's stockpile, Yue and June are capable of recognizing the presence and implications of Demonic contamination affecting supernatural Artifacts.[1]
- Supernatural Politics: As a Master Witch, Yue possesses a sophisticated understanding of Arcane's neutrality, the relationships between major supernatural factions and the consequences that would follow if Evie's cooperation with [[Diablo]] became publicly known.[1]
- Strategic Assessment: Yue is capable of examining Evie's acquired resources and judging their larger geopolitical significance, concluding that the Witch Queen's rewards could substantially strengthen Arcane and potentially elevate it toward the military standing of established Pantheons.[1]
- Misdirection and Political Cover: Yue notes that Master Witches are familiar with methods of spreading responsibility and redirecting blame away from Arcane when conducting politically dangerous operations.[1]
- Contingency Planning: Yue follows the traditional Witch philosophy of always preparing a second plan and considering the worst possible outcome before acting.[1]

### Possessions

#### Intelligence Orb
Yue possesses an orb capable of projecting recorded information as a holographic image. After surrounding her home with [[Magic]] Circles, she places the orb upon a table and uses it to show June covert footage of the enormous collection of supernatural resources associated with Evie's dealings.[1]
The footage itself was not personally recorded by Yue; it was supplied by one of her contacts using experimental Witch espionage methods.[1]

### Relationships

#### June
June, the Greedy Witch, is one of Yue's oldest confirmed friends. Both women personally earned Titles from the Witch Queen, although Yue later advanced to the higher rank of Master Witch.[1]
Yue behaves toward June like an irritating older sister. She deliberately calls her the wrong name, hugs her without permission, comments on her weight, drags her into conversations and continuously provokes reactions for her own amusement. June responds with threats and insults but clearly understands Yue well enough to recognize when genuine concern is hidden beneath her fox-like smile.[1]
Their friendship becomes particularly apparent during Arcane's lockdown. Yue was worried specifically because June had been operating outside the Realm when the closure was announced and is visibly relieved once she sees her safely inside Arcane. Before revealing dangerous information, Yue also confirms that June has no intention of recklessly involving herself in the war.[1]
The two possess similar professional instincts. Both understand the value of information, expect compensation for useful intelligence and prioritize survival when confronted with conflicts far beyond their power. Yue openly points out that June would also have attempted to charge her for equivalent information, an accusation June immediately admits is true.[1]
Their personalities nevertheless differ considerably. June's greed is direct and frequently dominates her behavior, while Yue is more playful and socially manipulative. Yue is willing to provide information without payment when she considers charging unfair and appears more concerned with understanding Arcane's long-term position than maximizing immediate profit.[1]

#### Evie Moriarty
Evie Moriarty is Yue's ruler and the Witch who personally recognized her Title. Yue is therefore simultaneously a Titled Witch acknowledged by the Queen and a Master Witch within Arcane's hierarchy.[1]
Despite her high rank, Yue is not privy to Evie's deepest plans. During the Demonic War, even [[Poseidon|the Master]] Witches were excluded from the actual details surrounding the Queen's cooperation with [[Diablo]]; the information was apparently restricted to Evie and her daughters.[1]
Yue does not interpret this secrecy as disrespect. She trusts Evie's competence enough to assume that the Queen would simply command the Master Witches if their assistance were required. Yue also believes Evie will ultimately act in Arcane's interests even when pursuing private objectives and becomes openly impressed when she realizes how much wealth and Divine equipment the Queen has obtained through her negotiations.[1]
Her admiration is practical rather than blind. Yue understands the political danger created by Evie's cooperation with Demons and recognizes why the Queen must suppress information capable of damaging Arcane's neutrality.[1]

#### Victor Elderblood
Yue has not been shown personally meeting [[Victor Elderblood]] during her debut chapter, but becomes intensely curious about him after hearing rumors concerning [[Nightingale]]'s newly appointed Vampire Count.[1]
She initially focuses upon his famous appearance, asking June whether Victor truly possesses beauty comparable to the most handsome Gods. After June confirms the rumors through her reaction, Yue begins seeking information about Victor's personality and relationships.[1]
June becomes suspicious that Yue intends to steal one of her most profitable contractors. Yue does not entirely dismiss the possibility, reasoning that her status as a Titled Master Witch and her [[Magic]] could potentially make her useful enough for Victor to employ. However, her principal reaction by the end of the conversation is simple curiosity; the stranger June's stories become, the more Yue wants to hear.[1]

### Weaknesses
- Entomophobia: Yue possesses an extreme fear and hatred of insects, with spiders receiving particular hostility. Merely being threatened with a nest of giant insects causes her body to tremble despite maintaining her characteristic smile.[1]
- Limited Political Access: Despite being a Master Witch, Yue does not automatically receive access to the Witch Queen's highest-level plans. During the Demonic War, Evie's true operation remained hidden from [[Poseidon|the Master]] Witches.[1]
- Mortal Limitations: Yue explicitly recognizes that powerful Divine Artifacts are too burdensome for ordinary mortals to use effectively. Nothing shown in Chapter 529 suggests Yue herself is exempt from this limitation.[1]
