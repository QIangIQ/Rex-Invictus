### Appearances

#### Debut
[[Ch04 - Rex Natus II|Chapter 4]]
Karen is a former Plebeian Vampire and longtime partner of Lucy who later became one of the first [[Heralds of Chaos]] created by War. During the early period of [[Victor Elderblood]]'s Vampire life, Karen and Lucy operated together within the criminal side of supernatural society and spent centuries searching for a method to transcend their status as Plebeian Vampires and become true Noble Vampires.[1][2][3]
Karen was considerably more than Lucy's passive assistant. She helped supervise their hidden base, independently investigated breaches in their security, participated directly in negotiations involving [[Horseman Clan]] and was sufficiently knowledgeable about their operation to catch strategic mistakes that Lucy himself overlooked. Her greatest weakness was not incompetence but impatience; when her instincts warned her that their opportunity was disappearing, Karen abandoned Lucy's slower approach and personally entered their experimental Noble-Vampire ritual before him.[4][2][3]
The ritual increased her strength but failed to transform her into a Noble Vampire because Karen and Lucy did not know that the final ingredient required the blood of a Vampire Progenitor. Their ambition therefore remained unrealized for centuries.[3]
Much later, after Victor became the Dragon God of [[Chaos]], Karen and Lucy were gathered alongside several other individuals connected to Victor's past. War judged that their actions had primarily been motivated by desire rather than a Sin warranting their destruction and offered them exactly what both had always wanted: greater Power. Karen and Lucy immediately accepted eternal servitude in exchange for advancement and were transformed using the same Essence of Chaos involved in War's existence, joining Jimmy and Thomas as the first four subordinate Heralds of Chaos.[5][6]

### Physical Appearance

#### Plebeian Vampire
Karen originally appeared as a relatively tall woman with short black hair and an ordinary professional appearance. Her hair reached approximately the level of her neck and was cut in a style [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] immediately associated with the stereotypical hairstyle of a "Karen", humorously matching her actual name.[1]
She wore a normal work suit during her first appearance and stood approximately 170 cm while wearing heels. Unlike Lucy's expensive white suit and deliberately sophisticated appearance, Karen's clothing was comparatively plain and functional.[1]
Her expression during her first encounter with Victor was noticeably predatory. Victor observed that she was staring at him as though he were prey, demonstrating the instinctive interest Vampires could show toward his extraordinary blood even before his true nature became known.[1]

#### Herald of Chaos
Karen's former Vampire appearance was fundamentally altered after accepting War's offer. She underwent the same [[Chaos]] transformation used on Jimmy, Thomas and Lucy, replacing her Plebeian Vampire constitution with that of a Herald of Chaos.[6]
Her face became engulfed in pure darkness, hiding her ordinary features, while her clothing was replaced by supernatural armor resembling War's own equipment but with a less massive construction. A pair of wings formed from pure darkness manifested behind her.[6]
The transformation was not a disguise or temporary combat state. Roxanne later confirmed that the Heralds had genuinely been recreated using the same Chaos Essence connected to War, permanently tying Karen's new existence to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[6]

### Personality
Karen is fiery, blunt, impatient and exceptionally quick to anger. She contrasts heavily with Lucy, whose usual response to danger is to remain outwardly calm, manipulate the situation or hide his fear behind diplomacy. Karen instead expresses frustration openly and frequently screams, insults Lucy or simply abandons the conversation when she becomes sufficiently irritated.[2][3]
Her temper became one of the longest-running jokes in her relationship with Lucy. Even centuries later, Lucy continued deliberately provoking her by making stereotypical jokes about her name and personality because he enjoyed watching her become angry. Karen repeatedly demanded that he stop, while Lucy showed no intention of doing so.[5]
Despite her explosive personality, Karen is intelligent and observant. When their underground base was infiltrated, she independently determined that the intruder was most likely a powerful Witch, reaching the same conclusion as Lucy from the available evidence. She also monitored the political movements of Clan Horseman and understood the implications of its three heirs suddenly being sent to their base.[4]
Karen has also demonstrated greater practical judgment than Lucy on several occasions. After Lucy discarded a stolen Church artifact inside Violet's territory, Karen immediately recognized that the plan could still be traced back to him because the [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] already knew Lucy had stolen it. She systematically explained the flaws in his reasoning until Lucy was forced to admit—although not openly—that she was correct.[2]
However, Karen's intelligence is undermined by impatience. Once she decides that waiting is dangerous or pointless, she is willing to act without Lucy. Her strongest example occurred when she grew furious over Lucy's decision to trust [[Johnny Lykos]] with an important task. Believing that further delay could destroy the opportunity she had pursued for centuries, Karen took her personal vial of Noble Vampire blood and began the transformation ritual without waiting for Lucy.[3]
Her instinctive caution should also not be underestimated. Before entering the ritual, Karen felt an increasingly powerful sense that something was wrong and that if she did not act immediately, she might never achieve her goal. Although she misunderstood the exact danger, her instincts were correct that the situation surrounding the ritual was rapidly changing.[3]
Karen is deeply ambitious. Her desire to become a Noble Vampire had persisted for centuries and was strong enough that she accepted dangerous Witchcraft, innocent Human sacrifices and political cooperation with the [[Horseman Clan]] for the chance to improve her existence. When [[Einer Horseman]] revealed genuine Progenitor blood, Karen's body reacted with overwhelming thirst despite simultaneously sensing instinctive fear.[2][3]
This fundamental desire never disappeared. Centuries later, War explicitly categorized Karen and Lucy as individuals motivated primarily by desire. When offered Power in exchange for servitude, Karen did not hesitate or attempt to negotiate. Becoming more than she currently was had always been one of her defining goals.[6]
Karen nevertheless appears genuinely close to Lucy. Their constant arguments resemble the interactions of two people who have spent centuries together rather than genuine hatred. They operate together, plan together, pursue the same ambitions and ultimately accept their transformation into [[Heralds of Chaos]] together.[4][2][3][6]

### Synopsis

#### The Awakening
Karen first appeared alongside Lucy when the pair visited [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] shortly after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s transformation into a Vampire. Lucy wanted permission to bring approximately fifty subordinate Vampires into Violet's territory because the Inquisition had begun hunting him.[1]
Victor immediately noticed Karen staring at him like prey. At the time Victor's extraordinary blood already generated unusually strong reactions among Vampires, although neither Karen nor Lucy understood what he truly was.[1]
Lucy and Karen also attempted to provoke the newborn Vampire by treating his marriage to Violet dismissively and emphasizing his apparent status as a Plebeian Vampire. Their assumptions were based upon ordinary Vampire hierarchy: from their perspective, Victor was a Human transformed into a Vampire who had somehow married the heiress of one of [[Nightingale]]'s most prestigious Clans.[1]
The meeting ultimately ended with Violet refusing Lucy's request before Victor overruled her and allowed the Vampires into the city under strict restrictions. Unknown to Karen and Lucy, Victor had no intention of protecting them out of generosity. He wanted to use Lucy as bait to draw the Inquisition into the area so that he could observe Vampire [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] for himself.[7]
Their activities subsequently helped trigger Victor's first direct encounter with Jimmy and Thomas, members of the Hunter force assigned to locate Lucy.[8][9]

#### Vampire Society
Karen and Lucy continued working toward a much larger objective while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] trained in [[Nightingale]]. Both despised the biological and social limitations imposed upon Plebeian Vampires and had spent an enormous period of time searching for a method of entering Vampire nobility themselves.[4][2]
Their project operated from a concealed underground base. When an unknown intruder infiltrated the facility and stole information, Karen immediately informed Lucy that they had been compromised. She had already analyzed the attack sufficiently to determine that the intruder had taken only their basic plans and had bypassed protections without leaving any conventional trace.[4]
Karen and Lucy independently reached the same conclusion: only a sufficiently powerful Witch was likely to have infiltrated an armored underground vault in that manner. [[Werewolves]] would have left physical traces, while powerful Noble Vampires would have little reason to hide rather than simply force their way inside.[4]
Karen also informed Lucy that the [[Horseman Clan]] had sent all three of its heirs as reinforcements. She understood that the family's political position had become increasingly unstable after seizing Clan Fulger's former holdings, noting the continued loyalty of Fulger's allies, the expected challenge from Victoria and rumors that Agnes Snow opposed the sudden shift in Nightingale's balance of power.[4]
Neither Karen nor Lucy cared primarily about Horseman's politics. Their real concern was completing their ritual before the Noble heirs could interfere with or claim the power they had been preparing.[4]
By Chapter 53, their ritual chamber had been completed. Its center contained a white coffin marked by a black cross and surrounded by red magical symbols apparently drawn in blood. Lucy had spent almost his entire fortune hiring a corrupt Witch to design the procedure.[2]
The plan required filling the coffin with the blood of innocent Humans and Noble Vampires. If their information was correct, they believed entering the completed ritual would reconstruct them into Noble Vampires.[2]
Karen also questioned Lucy regarding the stolen sacred artifact they had acquired from the Church. She was shocked when he casually admitted to throwing it away. Lucy argued that they could not use the artifact themselves, keeping an anti-Vampire Holy weapon nearby was stupid and selling it through Witches risked exposing their identities to [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]].[2]
Karen accepted part of his reasoning but immediately recognized a much larger problem: Lucy had discarded the artifact in Violet's territory, apparently hoping that investigators would blame Clan Snow. Karen pointed out that the Hunters already knew Lucy had stolen the object, knew the three major Noble Clans were unlikely to require such an artifact and could logically reconstruct what had happened. Lucy had therefore created a trail leading directly back toward himself.[2]
After hearing her reasoning, Lucy backed down and agreed to retrieve the object and dispose of it somewhere safer. The scene demonstrated that despite Karen's explosive temper, she was capable of identifying major strategic flaws that Lucy overlooked.[2]
Their disagreements reached a breaking point shortly afterward. Lucy had employed [[Johnny Lykos]] for an important task, insisting that the mercenary was trustworthy because they had worked together previously. Karen considered the idea absurd and furiously questioned in what world a mercenary should be considered trustworthy.[3]
After Lucy continued dismissing her concerns, Karen decided that she was finished waiting. She announced that she would accelerate the ritual herself. They had gathered almost everything required, and Karen had already obtained a vial of Noble Vampire blood for her own use.[3]
When Lucy asked what he was supposed to use, Karen simply told him to find another vial and proceeded without him. Her supernatural instincts were warning her so strongly that Karen believed delaying the ritual any further would permanently cost her the thing she had desired for centuries.[3]
Karen poured the Noble Vampire blood into the blood-filled coffin. The magical circle surrounding it immediately activated, convincing her the procedure was working. She entered the coffin alone and declared that she would be reborn.[3]
When the ritual ended, Karen emerged and immediately examined her body. She was devastated to discover that nothing obvious had changed. Her first instinct after being approached by an unfamiliar man was to punch him, allowing [[Zwei Horseman]] to intercept her fist and confirm that Karen had at least become physically stronger.[3]
However, [[Einer Horseman]] revealed that the actual transformation had failed. Karen and Lucy had misunderstood the ritual. The blood of an ordinary Noble Vampire was insufficient; the procedure required the blood of the Vampire King himself—a true Progenitor.[3]
Einer then revealed a vial containing [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]]' blood. Karen and Lucy reacted immediately. Their throats became dry and they stared at the vial with overwhelming thirst while simultaneously feeling instinctive fear toward the power contained inside it.[3]
Einer explained that it was not the blood of Vlad's descendants, wives or ordinary Noble family members. It was the blood of the Progenitor who had ruled Vampire society for thousands of years—the missing ingredient capable of completing their desired evolution.[3]
When Einer asked whether they wanted it, both Karen and Lucy immediately reached for the bottle. Their desperate desire for advancement allowed the Horseman heirs to place them exactly where they wanted them.[3]
Karen therefore remained a Plebeian Vampire. Her centuries-long attempt to force her way into Vampire nobility never reached its intended conclusion.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Karen resurfaced centuries later during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s settlement of unresolved enemies and acquaintances. She and Lucy had been transported into an apparently inescapable room alongside [[Luan Davis]], Cornelius Funar and eventually [[Kurama]].[5]
When Kurama awakened, Lucy attempted to handle the introductions formally. Karen clearly had far less patience for the process. When Lucy gestured toward her, she rolled her eyes with an openly disdainful expression before simply stating her name.[5]
The group's theory was that everyone inside the room had somehow angered Victor or harmed someone important to him. Kurama refused to accept the situation and attempted to escape despite the others already understanding that the room could not be breached.[5]
Karen openly mocked his failure. When describing Kurama, however, she incorrectly used the term "Kyuubi Kitsunes", prompting Lucy to deliberately fake a cough and correct her. Karen immediately growled at him and told him to stop correcting her.[5]
Lucy responded by making the same joke about her temper that he had apparently repeated for years. Karen demanded to know whether he had finally become tired of the joke; Lucy admitted that he had not. Their exchange demonstrated that even after an enormous period of time their relationship remained fundamentally recognizable.[5]
The argument ended when both Karen and Lucy suddenly sensed something approaching. Lucy quietly announced that "he" had arrived, and a Being emerged from the darkness.[5]
The entity was War, now transformed into a Progenitor created through Victor's [[Chaos]]. Neither Karen nor Lucy understood what he was, but their instincts immediately recognized that they were confronting an existence far outside their previous experience.[5]
War first summoned Jimmy and Thomas. He explained that the former Human Exorcists had gradually changed from unwilling spies into genuinely loyal servants and rewarded them by transforming both into [[Heralds of Chaos]].[5][6]
Afterward, War turned toward Lucy and Karen.
Unlike Cornelius and Kurama, the pair were not condemned. War declared that their previous actions had primarily been motivated by desire, and that desire itself was not a Sin because desire was one of the forces capable of driving a Being forward.[6]
He therefore extended the same offer to them:
Power in exchange for servitude.[6]
Karen and Lucy accepted immediately.
Their motivation required no complicated justification. They had always wanted to become more than they currently were and had spent centuries pursuing greater strength. War was now offering them the thing they had failed to obtain through their Noble-Vampire ritual at a scale vastly beyond anything they had previously imagined.[6]
Both seized War's hand and underwent the same transformation as Jimmy and Thomas. Their former existences were reconstructed through the Essence of Chaos, turning them into subordinate Heralds beneath War and ultimately Victor.[6]
Karen's face became pure darkness, supernatural armor replaced her previous clothing and wings of darkness emerged behind her. Together with Lucy, Jimmy and Thomas, she became one of the four original Heralds of Chaos.[6]
The Heralds were transported into the extraordinary region where Victor's transformed Cosmic existence could be perceived. Countless galaxies surrounded them while they automatically knelt before the God they had pledged themselves to serve.[6]
Victor's mere presence altered Reality and distorted nearby astronomical structures. When he later punished Kurama, hundreds of stars were affected strongly enough that Karen and the other Heralds would have been harmed merely by witnessing the event without protection from [[Roxanne Elderblood]] and [[Amara Elderblood]].[6]
Roxanne explained that the Heralds were capable of existing in this environment because they had been created using the same Essence of Chaos that created War. That Essence genuinely connected Karen to Victor, although the bond was significantly less profound than the connection Victor possessed with Roxanne or Amara.[6]
Once Victor finished dealing with the prisoners, Karen and the other three Heralds simultaneously received his will. All four answered together and disappeared to pursue additional enemies on Victor's behalf, beginning Karen's existence as one of the direct supernatural executors of the Chaos Dragon God's authority.[6]

### Skills & Abilities
Karen's current capabilities are fundamentally different from those she possessed as a Plebeian Vampire. Her original race granted the standard supernatural characteristics of a transformed Vampire, while her Herald form originates from War's [[Chaos]] lineage.

#### Herald of Chaos
- Herald of [[Chaos]] Physiology: Karen was fundamentally reconstructed by War and became one of the first four members of the [[Heralds of Chaos]]. Her former Plebeian Vampire constitution was replaced by a Chaos-derived supernatural existence.[6]
- Chaos Essence: [[Roxanne Elderblood]] explicitly confirms that Karen and the other Heralds were created using the same Essence of Chaos involved in War's creation. This directly connects Karen's existence to War and [[Victor Elderblood]].[6]
- Supernatural Empowerment: Karen's transformation granted her a level of existence vastly superior to her former Plebeian Vampire body. Greater Power was the explicit reward War offered in exchange for servitude.[6]
- Altered Existence: The transformation changed more than Karen's appearance. Her underlying supernatural nature was reconstructed through Chaos Essence.[6]
- Darkness Manifestation: Karen's Herald form manifests supernatural darkness across her body, most visibly through her featureless dark face and wings.[6]
- Wing Manifestation: Karen possesses wings composed entirely of pure darkness.[6]
- Flight: Karen can exist and move in open Space after becoming a Herald.[6]
- Space Survival: She remained fully functional in a cosmic environment containing countless galaxies without conventional air, pressure protection or biological life-support systems.[6]
- Connection to Victor: The Chaos Essence forming Karen's new existence establishes a genuine supernatural link to Victor.[6]
- Reception of Victor's [[Will Solace|Will]]: Karen and the other Heralds simultaneously received and understood Victor's command without ordinary spoken communication.[6]
- Dimensional/Cosmic Translocation: Karen participates in the Heralds' ability to disappear from one location and manifest in Victor's unusual internal/cosmic space. The novel has not yet clarified whether this is Karen's independent ability, an authority granted through War or a function of their bond with Victor.[6]

#### Former Abilities
- Plebeian Vampire Physiology: Karen was formerly an older Plebeian Vampire and possessed the standard enhanced body of her race.[1]
- Enhanced Strength: Karen possessed physical strength far beyond an ordinary Human. Her failed transformation ritual increased that strength further, with Zwei explicitly noting that she had become stronger after intercepting her punch.[3]
- Enhanced Senses: As a Vampire, Karen possessed supernatural sensory capabilities superior to those of ordinary Humans.
- Enhanced Speed and Agility: Her Vampire physiology granted physical mobility beyond Human limitations, although no major individual speed feat was directly demonstrated.
- Longevity: Karen and Lucy had pursued their transformation project for centuries, establishing that she was already an old supernatural Being before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] entered Vampire society.[2][3]
- Bloodlust: Karen experienced the extreme instinctive attraction Vampires feel toward exceptional blood. Vlad's Progenitor blood caused immediate overwhelming thirst despite simultaneously inspiring fear.[3]
- Supernatural Instinct: Karen's instincts warned her that delaying the Noble-Vampire ritual could cause her to permanently lose the opportunity. Although she did not know precisely what was approaching, her intuition correctly recognized that the situation was becoming increasingly dangerous.[3]
- Investigation and Deduction: Karen independently analyzed the breach of Lucy's hidden base and correctly concluded that a powerful Witch was the most likely infiltrator.[4]
- Security Analysis: She understood what information had been stolen and recognized that the intruder had bypassed physical security in a manner inconsistent with [[Werewolves]] or ordinary Noble Vampires.[4]
- Political Awareness: Karen monitored Clan Horseman's situation and understood the implications of the three heirs suddenly arriving at their base.[4]
- Strategic Reasoning: She identified the flaws in Lucy's attempt to redirect suspicion for the stolen Church artifact toward Clan Snow and convinced him to remove it from Violet's territory.[2]
- Ritual Knowledge: Karen possessed enough knowledge of the Plebeian-to-Noble Vampire ritual to personally activate and undergo it without Lucy.[2][3]
- Blood Ritual Activation: She correctly poured Noble Vampire blood into the prepared coffin and activated the crimson magical formation surrounding it.[3]
- Partial Empowerment: Although the ritual failed to change Karen's race, it successfully increased her physical strength.[3]

### Relationships

#### Lucy
Lucy is Karen's oldest known friend, partner and former superior. Karen is frequently described as his assistant, but their actual relationship is considerably more equal than that title suggests. She openly criticizes his decisions, independently handles important portions of their operation and can simply ignore his objections when she believes he is wrong.[4][2][3]
The two share essentially the same greatest ambition: escaping the limitations of Plebeian Vampirism and becoming more powerful. They spent centuries pursuing the Noble-Vampire ritual together, cooperated with the [[Horseman Clan]] and reacted identically when presented with Vlad's Progenitor blood.[2][3]
Their personalities constantly clash. Lucy enjoys mocking Karen's temper, while Karen becomes furious at his jokes, arrogance and tendency to dismiss her concerns. Their arguments continued centuries later during Chapter 933, indicating that the behavior had become a permanent part of their relationship rather than genuine hostility.[5]
Ultimately they made the same defining choice. When War offered Power in exchange for servitude, Karen and Lucy answered together and immediately took his hand, becoming [[Heralds of Chaos]] side by side.[6]

#### Victor Elderblood
Karen first encountered [[Victor Elderblood]] when he was an extremely young Vampire and initially viewed him as prey, unaware that the supposedly ordinary newborn would eventually become the supreme existence ruling her life.[1]
She was indirectly involved in circumstances that brought the Inquisition into conflict with Victor's household through Lucy's criminal activities and their stolen Church artifact.[7][2]
Centuries later, Karen's relationship with Victor changed from distant former adversary to direct subordinate. War offered her a place under the [[Chaos]] Dragon God's authority, and Karen willingly exchanged permanent service for the Power she had always desired.[6]
She now operates as one of Victor's Heralds and receives his will directly.

#### War
War is Karen's Progenitor and immediate superior within the [[Heralds of Chaos]]. Rather than condemning Karen for her past greed and ambition, War explicitly identified her actions as being driven primarily by desire and stated that desire was not inherently sinful.[6]
War offered Karen exactly the type of bargain most likely to appeal to her: greater Power in exchange for absolute servitude. She accepted without hesitation.[6]
His Essence subsequently reconstructed her into a Herald of [[Chaos]].

#### Horseman Clan
Karen and Lucy worked with the [[Horseman Clan]] during their attempt to escape Plebeian Vampire status. Karen monitored the Clan's political situation closely and became suspicious when all three Horseman heirs were suddenly sent to their base.[4]
The Horseman brothers possessed crucial knowledge that Karen and Lucy lacked. Einer eventually revealed that their supposedly complete ritual could never create a true Noble Vampire using ordinary Noble blood; the missing ingredient was the blood of a Vampire Progenitor.[3]
Karen's overwhelming reaction to Vlad's blood gave the Horseman heirs considerable leverage over her and Lucy.
