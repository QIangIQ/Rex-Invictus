### Physical Appearance
Nobunaga is depicted as a powerful warlord with a commanding presence. His very name evokes an image of a ruthless, charismatic leader who sought to unify Japan.

### Personality
Nobunaga is a charismatic, eccentric, and pragmatic leader, willing to bathe Japan in blood to achieve his vision of unification. He recognizes the power of deception and manipulation, making use of individuals like [[Kurama]] and the [[Kuroyami Clan]], a group of vampire assassins, to achieve his goals. He is both feared and respected by his subordinates.

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes and War
Nobunaga led Japan into a brutal era of war, aiming to unify the country under his rule. [[Kurama]], drawn to his charisma and ruthlessness, aligned with him, becoming his great general and using his fox-like abilities to aid in the war. With the help of the [[Kuroyami Clan]], Nobunaga’s forces achieved dominance, and Japan was finally united. However, his triumph was short-lived.
Just three days after Japan’s unification, Nobunaga was betrayed by his most trusted subordinates—[[Akechi Mitsuhide]], [[Toyotomi Hideyoshi]], and Kurama himself. The betrayal led to the Honnō-ji Incident, where Nobunaga was forced to commit seppuku (ritual suicide). His death marked a turning point, as his former subordinates vied for power in the ensuing chaos.[1]
