August, designated A-000, is an artificial Human-Vampire Hybrid, Inquisition Hunter and one of General James' most successful experimental creations. Alongside S-000, August was considered one of James' strongest and closest-to-perfect products and eventually served as a Commander within the Inquisition's forces.[1][2][3]
August was first known only through the designation A-000, which appeared in information recovered from one of James' earlier experimental subjects. He was later promoted to replace the deceased Commander [[Carlos Reiss]] and operated alongside Sylvie as part of the force commanding James' personal [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]].[1][2]
During the incident involving Ophis Tepes and [[Nero Elderblood]] in Japan, August deliberately accelerated the attempt to capture Ophis and personally mutilated both girls. His actions directly triggered [[Victor Elderblood]]'s retaliation. August was overwhelmingly defeated, tortured and subsequently captured alongside Sylvie. Rather than being killed, the pair were frozen by [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] and transported to [[Nightingale]] for research.[4][5][3][6]

### Physical Appearance
August is a tall man with long white hair and a face marked by two distinct scars. He wears priest-like clothing designed to allow considerable freedom of movement, reflecting his role as an active combatant rather than a ceremonial member of the Church.[4]
His principal weapons are a pair of golden swords composed entirely of supernatural energy. During his first direct appearance, August was already holding both weapons while approaching Ophis and [[Nero Elderblood]].[4]
As an artificial Human-Vampire Hybrid, August also possesses visibly Vampiric characteristics. When angered during his confrontation with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], his eyes glowed blood-red, displaying the supernatural traits incorporated into his altered physiology.[3]

### Personality
August is impatient, ruthless and highly self-confident. Rather than allowing the other supernatural factions pursuing Ophis and [[Nero]] to continue their prolonged attempt to capture the girls, he personally intervened because he considered the operation to be taking too long. His first direct words upon entering the conflict were effectively a criticism of the others for wasting time.[4]
He is also extremely brutal in pursuit of his objectives. August showed no hesitation in severing both of Nero's arms before decapitating Ophis despite her young appearance. The attack was not performed during a desperate battle for survival; August deliberately "accelerated the process" because the other participants had failed to complete the capture quickly enough.[4]
August was willing to cooperate with supernatural beings despite belonging to the Inquisition. He promised the supernatural factions assisting the hunt that they would receive a portion of the Vampire King's daughter once Ophis had been captured, illustrating the hypocrisy and pragmatism that had developed within the organization by this period.[4]
He nevertheless appears to possess genuine loyalty toward the Church as an institution. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] deliberately mocked the religious symbolism of the Inquisition by crucifying August and Sylvie upon structures of ice, August reacted angrily and accused Victor of insulting the Church.[3]
August also attempted to defend the moral position of the Inquisition during Victor's accusations of institutional hypocrisy. He pointed out that Victor himself had massacred large numbers of Humans and supernatural beings and accused him of preparing to commit genocide across the Japanese supernatural community merely because his daughter had been harmed.[3]
However, August was unable to meaningfully respond when Victor distinguished between their behavior by openly accepting responsibility for his own actions rather than attributing them to God's will. The exchange demonstrated that August still perceived himself and the Inquisition as morally justified despite the organization's increasingly extreme methods.[3]
August's arrogance disappeared rapidly when confronted with an opponent overwhelmingly stronger than himself. Once Victor arrived after Ophis' decapitation, August immediately recognized the danger and attempted to flee rather than continue fighting. His first instinct was to escape and reunite with Sylvie.[4]
This reaction also suggests that Sylvie was personally important to him rather than merely another experiment serving under James. Even while surrounded by allied supernatural forces, August specifically thought about escaping to meet Sylvie once Victor appeared.[4]
Despite his fear, August remained defiant after being captured. Even while severely mutilated and crucified on Victor's ice construct, he continued arguing with Victor and condemning the massacre occurring around them rather than silently accepting defeat.[3]

### Synopsis

#### A New Pillar
August was first mentioned through his experimental identification A-000. While examining information obtained from one of the Inquisition's artificial Hybrids, [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] discovered references to two unusually important experimental subjects connected to General James: S-000, identified as female, and A-000, identified as male.[1]
The subject providing the information could not remember their appearances or names, but the existence of S-000 and A-000 immediately distinguished them from the large number of lower-grade artificial Hybrids produced through the Inquisition's experimentation program.[1]
Ruby suspected that these individuals might have been associated with some of the covert operations previously directed against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his household, though August's true identity would not become clear until considerably later.[1]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
August eventually emerged publicly during the crisis surrounding Ophis Tepes and [[Nero Elderblood]] in Japan.
Following the death of Commander [[Carlos Reiss]], the Inquisition promoted a replacement Commander and dispatched a significantly smaller Hunter force to Japan than the army accompanying General Leonardo. That new Commander was August, who operated alongside Sylvie. The two were also identified as recruits responsible for commanding General James' personal forces.[2]
While multiple Vampires, Youkai, [[Werewolves]], Witches and [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] pursued Ophis because of her identity as the daughter of [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]], [[Nero]] continuously protected the younger girl from the increasingly frequent attacks.[2]
August eventually intervened personally when he became dissatisfied with how slowly the supernatural factions were progressing. Nero had successfully resisted repeated attempts to capture Ophis despite accumulating serious injuries, causing August to decide that the operation needed to be accelerated.[4]
He appeared behind Ophis and Nero wielding two golden swords composed of pure energy. His first attack severed both of Nero's arms before she could properly defend herself.[4]
When the Youkai [[Nura]] questioned his interference, August simply replied that the others were taking too long. He then reminded them of their agreement, promising that they would receive a portion of the Vampire King's daughter once the objective was completed.[4]
August immediately followed this statement by decapitating Ophis.[4]
The attack caused Ophis to release an agonized supernatural scream powerful enough to temporarily incapacitate many of the surrounding beings, including August himself. Before the Hunters and supernatural factions could recover, [[Victor Elderblood]] arrived.[4]
August immediately recognized the danger. Rather than trying to confront Victor directly, he decided that he needed to flee and reunite with Sylvie. He barely managed to take a step before Victor attacked him faster than August could perceive.[4]
By the time August understood what had happened, half of his body had already been severed. The attack was so fast that August did not initially feel pain and only realized he had been struck when his body collapsed.[4]
Victor subsequently healed Ophis and Nero before turning his attention toward everyone responsible for their condition. The surrounding supernatural factions were slaughtered while August remained unable to escape.[4][5]
Victor then subjected August to extensive physical punishment rather than killing him immediately. The Hunter's body was repeatedly damaged and mutilated while Victor systematically punished those who had participated in the attack upon his daughters.[5]
Sylvie eventually intervened when Victor was preparing to finish August. She attacked Victor with her twin golden daggers and appeared to cut his body into numerous pieces, only for Victor to reveal that the attack had failed to meaningfully harm him.[5]
Victor then mutilated Sylvie as well, leaving both of James' most successful experiments incapacitated on the battlefield.[5]
General James, who had been observing the conflict remotely, became alarmed at the possibility of losing A-000 and S-000. He described the pair as his strongest and near-perfect products and remotely removed restrictions that had previously been placed upon their powers.[3]
James nevertheless ordered them not to reveal their newly unrestricted strength immediately. With both Victor and [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] present, James calculated that their chances of successfully escaping remained extremely low even at full power. Instead, August and Sylvie were instructed to quietly regenerate while James prepared a distraction.[3]
The two began slowly repairing their bodies, attempting to conceal their recovery from Victor. Their efforts failed almost immediately because Victor noticed what they were doing.[3]
Victor created enormous stakes of ice and impaled both Hunters upon them. He then reshaped the structures until August and Sylvie were suspended in a deliberate imitation of religious crucifixion.[3]
August reacted furiously to the symbolism and accused Victor of spitting in the face of the Church. Victor responded that August and Sylvie did not represent Christianity or genuine Catholic faith, instead calling them products of a corrupted organization that used God's name to justify atrocities.[3]
August attempted to counter Victor's criticism by pointing toward the thousands of corpses surrounding them. He accused Victor of hypocrisy for condemning the Inquisition while simultaneously slaughtering both Humans and supernatural beings throughout Japan.[3]
Victor did not deny the accusation. Instead, he openly admitted that he was willing to destroy the Japanese supernatural community because Ophis was his daughter and stated that he would personally accept responsibility for everything he did rather than claiming that God had ordered it.[3]
August continued accusing him of preparing to commit mass genocide, but the argument did nothing to change Victor's behavior or improve the Hunters' situation.[3]
James subsequently summoned Leonardo to create an opportunity for their escape. Leonardo arrived through golden energy, attacked Victor and used one of his clones to seize the ice structures containing August and Sylvie.[3]
Leonardo attempted to throw both captives through an opening in the battlefield while distracting Victor. Sylvie tried to coordinate their escape with August, but Victor intercepted them before they could leave.[3]
Victor grabbed both Hunters by the neck and threw them directly toward [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]], jokingly presenting them to his wife as "two fresh Hybrids".[3]
Ruby immediately accepted the gift. She seized August and Sylvie with enormous hands of ice before completely freezing their bodies inside blocks of ice.[3]
Leonardo considered attempting another rescue but abandoned the idea when both Victor and [[Scathach]] silently warned him against interfering. He understood that continuing would result in his death.[3]
Several hours later, Ruby had already returned to [[Nightingale]]. She was explicitly described as being unable to contain her enthusiasm to begin researching the two artificial Hybrids Victor had given her.[6]
August's final fate following his capture has not been directly shown. Contrary to the previous version of this article, Chapters 343-347 never depict Victor killing August. His last explicitly confirmed state is as a living, frozen prisoner transported to Nightingale by Ruby for research.[3][6]

### Skills & Abilities
August was one of James' strongest and most advanced artificial Human-Vampire Hybrids. James explicitly described A-000 and S-000 as his most powerful and nearly perfected products at the time of the Japanese incident.[3]
- Artificial Hybrid Physiology: August is an artificially created Human-Vampire Hybrid produced through James' experimentation. His physiology combines Human Hunter characteristics with several passive traits normally associated with Vampires.[1][3]
- Faith Manipulation: August retains access to the supernatural Human power known as Faith. His preferred weapons manifest as golden energy swords, consistent with the golden supernatural energy utilized by high-ranking Inquisition [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]].[4]
- Energy Weapon Creation: August can manifest and wield a pair of golden swords composed entirely of supernatural energy. He used these weapons as his primary armaments when attacking [[Nero]] and Ophis.[4]
- Dual Swordsmanship: August fights using two swords simultaneously. His initial attack with the weapons severed both of Nero's arms before she could successfully defend herself.[4]
- Superhuman Strength: As an artificial Hybrid, August possesses physical strength significantly exceeding that of an ordinary Human.
- Superhuman Speed: August moved quickly enough to appear behind Ophis and Nero and mutilate Nero before she could properly respond. He subsequently decapitated Ophis within only a few moments of entering the confrontation.[4]
- Enhanced Reflexes: August possesses the combat reactions expected from a high-ranking Hunter, although [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s speed exceeded his perception so completely that August failed to detect the attack that severed half of his body.[4]
- Enhanced Durability: August survived injuries that would immediately kill an ordinary Human, including having approximately half of his body severed by Victor.[4]
- Regeneration: August possesses Vampire-derived regenerative abilities. Even after sustaining catastrophic injuries from Victor, he remained alive and was capable of gradually repairing his body.[4][3]
- Accelerated Recovery: After James removed the restrictions placed upon A-000's powers, August deliberately began using his abilities to heal the damage Victor had inflicted. He was capable of controlling the rate of his regeneration enough to attempt to conceal the process.[3]
- Enhanced Senses: August possesses heightened supernatural senses as part of his Hybrid physiology. These enhanced senses were strong enough that Ophis' supernatural scream temporarily overwhelmed him when he was standing near her.[4]
- Vampiric Eyes: August's eyes can glow blood-red when his Hybrid nature becomes more pronounced. This was visibly demonstrated while he angrily confronted Victor.[3]
- Pain Tolerance: August remained conscious and verbally defiant despite being bisected, extensively mutilated and later impaled through multiple portions of his body upon Victor's ice construct.[4][5][3]
- Combat Experience: August was trusted with the rank of Commander inside the Inquisition and placed among the individuals directing James' personal forces, implying significant field experience and combat competence.[2]
- Restricted Power (Formerly): James deliberately installed restrictions limiting August's complete abilities. During the conflict with Victor, James remotely removed these restrictions because A-000 and S-000 were in danger of being lost. August never received an opportunity to fully demonstrate the extent of his unrestricted strength before being captured.[3]

### Weaknesses
- Artificial Hybrid Instability: August was an artificial rather than naturally born Hybrid. James himself described A-000 and S-000 as only near-perfect products, indicating that neither represented a completely stable or perfected Hybrid.[3]
- Hunter/Vampire Hybrid Limitations: James' artificial Human-Vampire Hybrids inherited only selected characteristics of both races rather than the complete abilities of either. Earlier subjects studied by Ruby possessed Vampire passive traits and Human Faith but suffered serious genetic and physiological defects.[1]
- Overconfidence and Impatience: August's impatience directly contributed to the catastrophe in Japan. Rather than waiting for Sylvie or allowing the operation to proceed as planned, he personally accelerated the capture of Ophis and attacked before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s arrival, resulting in his immediate defeat once Alucard reached the battlefield.[4]

- A-000 was first mentioned as early as Chapter 186, considerably before August's direct appearance in Japan.[1]
- Chapter 341 identifies August as the new Commander sent by the Inquisition after the death of [[Carlos Reiss]]. General Leonardo was the General operating separately in Japan. August therefore should not be listed with the class or rank of General.[2]
- August and S-000 were described by James as his strongest and nearly perfected artificial Hybrid products.[3]
- August's preferred weapons are a pair of golden energy swords.[4]
- August was directly responsible for severing both of [[Nero Elderblood]]'s arms and decapitating Ophis, making him one of the principal individuals responsible for triggering [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Crimson Nightmare massacre.[4]
- When Victor arrived, August's immediate objective was to escape and reunite with Sylvie rather than attempt to fight Alucard.[4]
- August continued defending the Inquisition's moral position even while being tortured by Victor, accusing Victor of hypocrisy and mass genocide.[3]
- August's ultimate fate after being taken to [[Nightingale]] has not been explicitly shown in the chapters currently identified. His status should therefore remain Unknown unless a later direct statement confirms his death.
