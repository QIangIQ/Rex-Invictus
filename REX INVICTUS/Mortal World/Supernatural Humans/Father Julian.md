### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 155
Father Julian was a Human priest and veteran Hunter of the Inquisition who served beneath General James. He was the field leader of the Hunter force sent to [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]]'s former mansion with orders to capture the Fulger heiress and obtain her blood for the Inquisition's experimental program.[1][2]
Julian was notorious among supernatural beings for participating in the extermination of multiple Vampire communities and commonly operated alongside his close companion [[Zandriel]]. Unlike Zandriel, who had been artificially altered into a Human-Vampire Hybrid, Julian himself was presented as a Human Hunter whose combat abilities were derived from Faith.[3][4]
After the operation failed and [[Victor Elderblood]] discovered the condition in which the [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] had left [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]], Julian was captured, tortured and mentally dominated. Victor extracted information concerning General James and the Church's interest in Sasha's blood before killing Julian along with the surviving members of his operation.[2][5]
Julian's usefulness continued even after death. [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] later constructed an elaborate cover story which blamed him for the entire incident, publicly transforming the deceased priest into an international terrorist supposedly connected to a secret organization operating from the Vatican.[6]

### Physical Appearance
Julian was an exceptionally tall man standing approximately 195 cm. He had spiked blonde hair and wore the priestly clothing associated with the combat personnel of the Inquisition. His eyes were concealed beneath a black bandanna, preventing their natural color from being properly seen.[1]
He carried two western-style swords at his waist and wielded both simultaneously when preparing for battle. When invoking his Faith, Julian's body became surrounded by brilliant golden energy which spread across both swords.[1]
His imposing stature and unusual blindfolded appearance were significant enough for Kaguya to immediately identify him as the most dangerous-looking member of the Hunter force before he even entered combat.[1]

### Personality
Julian was an intensely fanatical and uncompromising follower of the Inquisition's anti-supernatural ideology. He sincerely considered [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] to be "God's chosen ones" and viewed Vampires and other supernatural beings as demons or abominations whose destruction represented a sacred responsibility rather than merely a military objective.[1]
His fanaticism was accompanied by an outwardly calm and almost courteous demeanor. When initially confronting Maria, Julian spoke with a gentle smile and claimed that the Church could still "save" her despite her transformation into a Vampire. Maria immediately understood that this supposed salvation meant death.[1]
Julian's religious devotion directly fueled his supernatural abilities. Before fighting, he openly prayed to God and described himself as the Almighty's faithful instrument before asking for the strength necessary to eliminate his enemies. His prayer immediately caused golden Faith to surround both his body and weapons.[1]
He possessed an extreme hatred of Vampires and had developed a reputation for accepting missions involving the destruction of Vampire communities. Maria recognized him specifically because of his history of participating in such extermination operations.[3]
Despite this fanaticism, Julian was not merely an uncontrolled berserker. He understood and followed mission parameters, repeatedly emphasizing that Sasha had to be captured rather than simply slaughtered. When [[Zandriel]] became distracted by his enthusiasm for fighting Kaguya, Julian reminded him that the Fulger heiress was the actual objective.[1]
Julian also displayed significant loyalty toward Zandriel. He openly praised Zandriel's combat performance and became genuinely concerned whenever his companion suffered severe injuries.[3][4]
His respect for hierarchy was selective. Julian obeyed the superior who had actually assigned his mission—later revealed to be General James—but refused to explain the operation even to General Mizuki. When Mizuki demanded answers, Julian dismissively reminded her that although she held the rank of General, she was not his superior.[4]
Julian also showed poor judgment when evaluating Vampires outside the ideological assumptions of the Inquisition. When Sasha remained outwardly calm after discovering Kaguya's mutilated body, Julian incorrectly assumed the maid meant little to her and believed her lack of visible rage indicated emotional indifference. In reality, Sasha was desperately suppressing her anger while fearing what [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] would do once he discovered Kaguya's condition.[4]
Whatever confidence Julian normally possessed completely collapsed before Victor's rage. Victor's presence frightened even experienced Hunters, and Julian openly called him a monster after witnessing the abnormal transformation of his expression and aura.[4]
His religious training also provided no meaningful protection against Victor's mental influence. Once Victor focused his Charm upon him, Julian's eyes immediately lost focus and he began obediently responding to Victor as "Master".[4]

### Synopsis

#### A New Pillar
Julian first appeared during the second Inquisition operation targeting [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]].
Sasha had returned to her former mansion alongside [[Maria Elderblood]] to recover an item associated with her ancestor, Carmila Fulger. Unknown to them, more than twenty [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] had already infiltrated the mansion and were waiting for an opportunity to capture the former Count Clan's heiress.[1]
Sasha detected the intruders before they could attack and ordered Maria and Kaguya to capture them while she entered a secret Fulger chamber.[1]
The enemy force consisted not only of ordinary Hunters but also specialized combatants, including a mercenary Werewolf, [[Zandriel]] and Father Julian. Kaguya immediately sensed that the unusually tall priest positioned behind the others was considerably more dangerous than the ordinary Hunters surrounding him.[1]
Julian initially addressed Maria directly. Seeing that the former Hunter had become a Vampire, he expressed disappointment before claiming that the Church would "save" her. Maria rejected his offer immediately, understanding that his version of salvation consisted of killing her.[1]
When the mercenary Werewolf attempted to negotiate with Kaguya and Maria rather than unnecessarily fight people outside his contract, Julian reacted with outrage. He declared that they had encountered both a Demon and a traitor before stepping forward personally.[1]
Julian then issued direct orders to Zandriel. Maria and Kaguya could be killed, but Sasha Fulger had to be captured because acquiring the heiress remained the mission's primary objective.[1]
Preparing for battle, Julian drew the two western swords at his waist and began praying. He declared himself God's faithful instrument and requested Divine strength to eliminate the supernatural beings standing before him. Golden Faith immediately enveloped his body and both swords before he loudly completed the prayer with "AMEN!".[1]
The ensuing battle demonstrated that Zandriel was the principal frontline fighter of Julian's group. After Zandriel successfully injured Kaguya, Julian openly praised his companion while the other Hunters attempted to restrain Maria and support the operation.[3]
Maria recognized Julian during the confrontation. She remembered hearing of a priest responsible for eliminating multiple Vampire communities and realized that the man fit the reputation of Father Julian, who was rumored to frequently operate beside Zandriel.[3]
Julian defended Zandriel when Maria described him as insane, simply responding that his friend was perfectly normal despite the latter's extreme religious fanaticism and violent behavior.[3]
The Hunters initially appeared to gain the advantage. Kaguya was forced into an increasingly difficult battle against Zandriel while Maria dealt with the larger Hunter group. Maria's threads eventually allowed her to entrap several opponents and interfere with their ability to properly use Faith, but Julian continued attempting to coordinate the surviving Hunters.[3]
By the time Sasha returned from the Fulger chamber, the situation had become catastrophic. Maria had been rendered unconscious while Kaguya had suffered extensive mutilation: her arms and legs were gone, her abdomen had been torn open, one eye was missing and much of her face had been burned.[4]
Julian calmly welcomed Sasha back and initially attempted to continue the operation. When Sasha repeatedly demanded to know who had harmed Kaguya, Julian answered that whoever had done so was simply carrying out God's command to punish sinners.[4]
He then ordered Zandriel to attack Sasha from behind.
The ambush failed when Sasha entered her Lightning state and easily avoided Zandriel's swords before kicking him through the mansion wall. Sasha warned Julian that the Hunters had made an enormous mistake because someone far more dangerous than herself cared deeply about Kaguya.[4]
That person arrived moments later.
[[Victor Elderblood]] descended directly through the mansion roof after sensing Sasha's distress through their bond. Julian initially failed to recognize him and angrily demanded his identity while Victor ignored everyone and concentrated entirely upon Kaguya.[4]
Mizuki subsequently arrived and was surprised to discover Julian and the additional Hunters operating without her knowledge. When she asked why he was there, Julian refused to explain his orders, stating that even though Mizuki was a General, she was not his superior.[4]
After Victor stabilized Kaguya using his own blood, his attention turned toward the Hunters. The pressure released by him terrified nearly everyone present, including Julian, who openly described Victor as a monster.[4]
Victor brutally mutilated Zandriel and discovered the man's abnormal physiology, including Vampire bite marks combined with a Human heart and powerful regeneration. Julian watched in horror and repeatedly called out to his companion.[4]
Victor then turned toward Julian.
Recognizing that Julian appeared to be the leader of the operation, Victor used his Vampiric mental influence and ordered him to reveal everything he knew. Julian's resistance collapsed immediately; his eyes became unfocused and he obediently addressed Victor as "Master".[4]
Victor nevertheless did not immediately begin questioning him. Enraged by Kaguya's injuries, he first subjected Julian to prolonged torture severe enough that Mizuki could hear the priest screaming even after she fled far from the mansion.[4]
By the time Victor finally interrogated him, Julian's limbs had been twisted into unnatural positions and his eyes appeared lifeless from the physical and psychological trauma.[2]
Under Victor's control, Julian revealed that General James had personally sent the operation.
James wanted the blood of the heir of the former Vampire Count Clan Fulger. Julian explained that because Clan Fulger had lost its Count status, the Inquisition believed the family could be targeted without suffering the retaliation normally expected from one of [[Nightingale]]'s great Pillars.[2]
He further revealed that James intended to use Sasha's superior blood as material for creating additional "faithful" individuals. The stronger the Vampire bloodline used in the process, the better the resulting subjects were expected to become.[2]
This information helped Victor understand that Zandriel's unusual Vampire-Human condition was not an isolated phenomenon but part of a broader Church experimentation program.[2]
Julian did not survive his capture. Chapter 159 establishes that after completing Julian's interrogation, Victor moved on to Zandriel and the mercenary Werewolf. By the conclusion of the interrogation sequence Victor was sitting beside the three corpses, confirming Julian had been killed along with the other surviving members of the operation. The exact method by which Victor delivered Julian's final death was not directly shown.[5]
Julian's corpse subsequently became useful to Victor's household for an entirely different reason.
As [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]], Sasha, [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] and Natalia attempted to conceal the supernatural catastrophe surrounding the mansion, Julian's body and known connection to the Vatican provided the perfect foundation for a fabricated Human explanation.[7]
Ruby constructed a narrative portraying Julian as the terrorist responsible for the massacre. Rather than revealing that a Vampire Count, the Inquisition and supernatural beings had fought in the area, the public was told that Father Julian had caused the incident and successfully escaped from American authorities.[6]
News reports claimed witnesses had seen Julian boarding an aircraft back toward the Vatican while shouting "Hail The Inquisition". The story additionally credited SWAT [[Commander David]] with forcing Julian to retreat, creating both a convenient villain and a Human hero for the public narrative.[6]
The Vatican could not effectively refute the accusation without creating further problems. Alexander officially denied the existence of the Inquisition, forcing the Church to publicly treat Julian as nothing more than a rogue priest and terrorist despite his genuine connection to the Vatican.[6]
The fabricated scandal therefore allowed Julian to damage the organization he had faithfully served even after his death.

### Skills & Abilities
Julian was an experienced Human Hunter whose supernatural abilities derived from Faith. Unlike [[Zandriel]], no evidence identified Julian as an artificial Human-Vampire Hybrid.
- Faith Manipulation: Julian could access the supernatural energy generated through Human belief. When invoking God before combat, brilliant golden Faith visibly enveloped his entire body.[1]
- Prayer-Based Empowerment: Julian activated his supernatural abilities through prayer. He invoked God as the Almighty, described himself as His faithful instrument and requested strength to destroy the supernatural beings before him. His Faith immediately intensified following the prayer.[1]
- Weapon Empowerment: Julian could channel golden Faith into both of his swords, covering the weapons in supernatural energy and increasing their effectiveness against supernatural beings such as Vampires.[1]
- Dual Swordsmanship: Julian carried and fought with two western-style swords simultaneously. His preferred fighting equipment and stance indicate substantial experience using paired blades in supernatural combat.[1]
- Enhanced Physical Attributes: As an experienced Hunter capable of channeling Faith through his body, Julian possessed physical capabilities exceeding those of an ordinary Human while empowered.
- Vampire Hunting Expertise: Julian had considerable experience hunting Vampires and had become notorious for participating in the extermination of several Vampire communities before his encounter with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction.[3]
- Leadership: Julian functioned as the field leader of the mansion operation. He commanded more than twenty [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] and associated combatants, issued mission parameters to Zandriel and coordinated the attempt to capture Sasha Fulger.[1]
- Tactical Knowledge: Julian understood the strategic objective behind the operation and repeatedly prioritized the capture of Sasha over unnecessary fighting, recognizing that her blood rather than the deaths of Maria or Kaguya was the true goal.[1]

### Weaknesses
- Human Physiology: Despite his use of Faith, Julian remained Human and lacked the extreme regeneration and survivability possessed by the Hybrid [[Zandriel]].[4]
- Vulnerability to Vampiric Mental Manipulation: Julian demonstrated no effective resistance to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Charm. Once Victor focused his influence upon him, Julian immediately lost focus and became obedient enough to reveal highly sensitive Inquisition information.[4][2]
- Religious Fanaticism: Julian's absolute belief in the righteousness of the Inquisition caused him to underestimate the moral and strategic consequences of his actions. His dismissal of supernatural beings as demons prevented him from accurately understanding the people he was confronting.
- Inferiority to High-Level Vampires: Whatever combat ability Julian possessed was completely irrelevant against a Vampire of Victor's level. Victor subdued and mentally dominated him without Julian demonstrating any meaningful ability to resist.[4]

### Trivia
- Julian stood approximately 195 cm tall, making him unusually tall for a Human character.[1]
- He had spiked blonde hair and permanently covered his eyes with a black bandanna during his shown appearances.[1]
- Julian's primary weapons were two western swords which he could empower with golden Faith.[1]
- Maria recognized Julian by reputation because of his involvement in the destruction of several Vampire communities.[3]
- Julian and [[Zandriel]] were known to frequently operate together, and Julian displayed genuine concern for him during their final mission.[3][4]
- Contrary to the previous version of this article, Julian has never been established as a Human-Vampire Hybrid. Zandriel was the Hybrid member of the team, possessing Vampire bite marks, a Human heart and regeneration.[4]
- General James was Julian's actual superior and personally ordered the Fulger Mansion operation.[2]
- Julian's mission was to obtain Sasha's blood so that the Inquisition could create additional and potentially stronger experimental "faithful".[2]
- Although Julian is confirmed to have been killed by [[Victor Elderblood]], his exact killing blow occurred outside the directly narrated portion of the interrogation. Chapter 159 confirms his death by referring to Julian, Zandriel and the mercenary Werewolf collectively as three corpses.[5]
- Ruby later transformed Julian into an international terrorist in the official Human cover story surrounding the incident, falsely claiming that he had escaped back toward the Vatican.[6]
- The fabricated report also claimed that Julian shouted "Hail The Inquisition" before his supposed escape, greatly damaging the Vatican's public reputation.[6]
