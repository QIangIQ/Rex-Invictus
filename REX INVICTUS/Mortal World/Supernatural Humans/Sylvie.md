Sylvie, designated S-000, is an artificial Human-Vampire Hybrid created by former Inquisition General James. She is one of the most successful products of James' long-running Hybrid experiments and was described by him, alongside A-000, as one of his strongest and nearly perfected creations.[1][2]
Unlike many of James' earlier experimental subjects, Sylvie retained a stable personality, considerable independence and enough self-awareness to openly question the morality and effectiveness of her creator's actions. She continued operating as a Hunter affiliated with the Inquisition and later served alongside August among the new recruits commanding James' personal forces.[3][4]
Sylvie later became involved in the incident surrounding Ophis Tepes and [[Nero Elderblood]] in Japan. After attempting to save August from [[Victor Elderblood]], she was brutally defeated and subsequently captured. Victor eventually entrusted both Hybrids to [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]], who froze them and returned to [[Nightingale]] with the intention of researching their unusual physiology.[5][2][6]

### Physical Appearance
Very little information concerning Sylvie's physical appearance is directly provided by the novel. She is identified as a woman and appears physically human despite being an artificial Human-Vampire Hybrid.[7]
In combat, Sylvie is associated with a pair of golden daggers which she wields simultaneously. These weapons complement her rapid and precise close-range fighting style, allowing her to deliver numerous slashing attacks in extremely short periods of time.[5]

### Personality
Sylvie is considerably more independent-minded than many of James' experimental subjects. Although she operates under his authority and participates in missions on behalf of the Inquisition, she does not display unquestioning obedience toward either James or the institution that created her. She openly criticizes James, questions his decisions and shows awareness of the moral corruption surrounding his experiments.[1][3]
She possesses a dry and somewhat dismissive personality. When James referred to her as one of his successful experiments and discussed the continuing problems created by his other subjects, Sylvie was considerably more interested in avoiding another cleanup operation so she could return to watching her Korean dramas. This comparatively mundane hobby contrasts heavily with the violent circumstances of her existence as an artificial supernatural weapon.[1]
Sylvie is also observant and analytically minded. While watching [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] fight James' experimental combatants, she disagreed with James' tendency to evaluate Alucard primarily according to his accumulated powers. Sylvie instead recognized that Victor's greatest danger came from his mentality, combat instincts, adaptability and the degree of control he possessed over his abilities. She even considered Victor representative of a wider generational change occurring throughout the supernatural world.[3]
Her observations frequently proved more perceptive than James' numerical evaluations. When James continued expending valuable experimental subjects merely to obtain information on Victor, Sylvie questioned whether the data justified wasting so many resources.[3]
Despite being one of James' creations, Sylvie appears uncomfortable with some of his methods. She directly questioned whether Alexander knew James was experimenting upon Humans and internally regarded the people surrounding the project as fundamentally rotten. While James justified his actions through his belief that God would forgive anything done for Humanity's protection, Sylvie was considerably less convinced, noting that even if God forgave him, ordinary people—and particularly Victor—certainly would not.[3]
Sylvie possesses a close relationship with August. Before the Japanese incident, she warned James that A-000 had begun acting independently and feared that his recklessness might provoke [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], demonstrating both concern for August and an accurate understanding of how dangerous the situation could become.[1] When Victor later prepared to kill August, Sylvie intervened without hesitation despite the overwhelming danger and attempted to cut Victor apart before he could finish him.[5]
She is also remarkably defiant. Even after Victor gouged out her eyes, severed one of her arms, shattered her knees and later impaled her upon an ice structure, Sylvie remained conscious enough to insult him and secretly attempt to regenerate rather than simply surrender.[5][2]

### Synopsis

#### A New Pillar
Sylvie was initially known only through her experimental designation S-000. While examining information recovered from the artificial Hybrid B-002, [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] discovered references to two significantly more important subjects: S-000, identified as a woman, and A-000, identified as a man. Their connection to the wider activities surrounding the Inquisition and James' experiments immediately made them subjects of interest.[7]
Sylvie later appeared personally beside James while he observed [[Carlos Reiss]]. James regarded Carlos as another failed or expendable experiment and contrasted him with S-000, whom he considered an example of what his work could achieve when successful.[1]
Sylvie showed little interest in receiving praise from her creator. Instead, she complained about repeatedly having to clean up the consequences of his experiments and expressed a desire to return to watching her K-drama. Their exchange demonstrated that despite being designated by an experimental number, Sylvie possessed a distinct personality and was comfortable speaking casually—and even disrespectfully—to James.[1]
She also warned James about A-000. August had begun acting independently while searching for the individual responsible for an earlier incident, and Sylvie correctly recognized that his actions could eventually draw the attention of [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]]. Rather than dismissing the concern, James understood the danger represented by provoking the ancient Vampire Countess.[1]
Sylvie was later present while James observed [[Victor Elderblood]] fighting several artificial combatants. Unlike James, who concentrated heavily upon Victor's individual abilities and measurable combat output, Sylvie argued that simply cataloguing Alucard's powers missed the most dangerous aspect of him.[3]
She believed Victor's mentality was what truly made him abnormal: his desire for combat, his adaptability and his ability to control several unrelated supernatural powers as though they naturally belonged together. From Sylvie's perspective, Victor potentially represented the beginning of a wider change in the supernatural world's established order rather than merely another unusually powerful Vampire.[3]
Sylvie also questioned James' willingness to continuously sacrifice expensive experimental subjects for additional information, particularly when the gap between Victor and those experiments had already become obvious. Their conversation eventually turned toward the Human subjects James used in his research. Sylvie questioned whether Alexander truly knew what the General was doing, while James responded that the Pope had permitted the very circumstances that had transformed Sylvie herself.[3]
Although she continued working beside him, Sylvie internally recognized the hypocrisy surrounding the project. James believed his actions would be forgiven by God because they were performed for Humanity, while Sylvie concluded that regardless of divine forgiveness, ordinary people would not overlook what he had done—especially someone like Victor.[3]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Sylvie and August were eventually deployed as part of the Inquisition forces operating in Japan. By this point, the pair were described among the new recruits responsible for commanding James' personal army.[4]
August became directly involved in the conflict surrounding Ophis and [[Nero Elderblood]], while Sylvie initially remained elsewhere. As the situation deteriorated, August repeatedly thought about escaping the battlefield and reuniting with Sylvie, demonstrating that the pair were accustomed to operating together.[8]
The situation became catastrophic once [[Victor Elderblood]] arrived. Enraged by what had happened to Ophis and [[Nero]], Victor hunted the responsible [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] and eventually captured August. He tortured the Hybrid and prepared to crush his head, apparently ending the confrontation.[5]
Sylvie intervened at the final moment. Appearing directly in front of Victor with two golden daggers, she launched an extremely rapid series of slashes that seemingly divided his body into numerous pieces. Believing the attack had succeeded, she declared that she could not allow Victor to kill August yet.[5]
Her confidence disappeared almost immediately. What Sylvie believed to be Victor's body dissolved into the dark-red mist associated with his regeneration and movement, and Victor appeared directly before her. He grabbed Sylvie by the head and drove his thumbs into both of her eyes before she could respond.[5]
Victor proceeded to tear one of her arms from her body and destroy both of her knees, leaving her unable to properly resist. Sylvie's attempt to rescue August therefore ended with both of James' prized Hybrids completely at Victor's mercy.[5]
James continued remotely monitoring the situation and became increasingly concerned about losing S-000 and A-000, whom he described as his strongest and nearly perfected products. Recognizing that both Hybrids needed every possible advantage, he remotely released restrictions that had previously been placed upon their powers.[2]
Sylvie and August immediately began using their enhanced recovery to repair their injuries as slowly and discreetly as possible, hoping Victor would fail to notice the gradual changes occurring within their bodies. Victor nevertheless perceived their attempts almost immediately.[2]
Rather than simply killing them, Victor created large structures of ice and impaled both Hybrids upon them in a deliberate mockery of religious crucifixion. Sylvie remained conscious despite her injuries and angrily referred to Victor as a "crazy bastard" while continuing to endure the pain of her regeneration.[2]
General Leonardo eventually arrived after being contacted by James and attempted to remove the two Hunters from the battlefield. Victor prevented the rescue and seized both Sylvie and August before Leonardo could take them away.[2]
Victor then presented the two Hybrids to [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]], jokingly calling them a gift and instructing her to treat the "fresh Hybrids" gently. Ruby immediately became interested in examining them, producing large hands of ice to take custody of both captives.[2]
Sylvie and August recognized Ruby's expression as disturbingly similar to the look James displayed whenever he discovered something new he wanted to experiment upon. Their situation worsened when Ruby completely encased both of them inside blocks of ice and removed them from the battlefield.[2]
Leonardo considered intervening but abandoned the attempt after simultaneously sensing the attention of both Victor and [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]]. Continuing the rescue would have required fighting two beings far beyond what he was prepared to confront merely to recover the captured Hybrids.[2]
After Leonardo's subsequent battle with Victor, it was revealed that Ruby had already returned to [[Nightingale]] several hours earlier because she was unable to contain her enthusiasm to begin researching the two Hybrids Victor had given her.[6]
Sylvie's ultimate fate following her capture has not been explicitly shown. Contrary to some earlier accounts, Chapters 344-347 do not depict Victor killing either Sylvie or August; their last directly confirmed state is as frozen captives taken to Nightingale by Ruby for study.[2][6]

### Skills & Abilities
As S-000, Sylvie is one of James' most advanced artificial Human-Vampire Hybrid creations. James explicitly considered S-000 and A-000 his strongest and closest-to-perfect products at the time of the Japanese incident.[2]
- Artificial Hybrid Physiology: Sylvie possesses characteristics derived from both Humans and Vampires as a result of James' experimentation. Unlike many of his earlier experimental subjects, she remained stable enough to operate independently as an Inquisition Hunter and field commander.[7][1][2]
- Enhanced Physical Attributes: Sylvie possesses physical abilities considerably beyond those of an ordinary Human. She was capable of entering an active supernatural battle, intercepting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] while he was preparing to kill August and performing a rapid sequence of attacks before an ordinary combatant could have reacted.[5]
- Regeneration: Sylvie possesses a Vampire-derived healing factor. Despite having both eyes destroyed, one arm torn from her body and both knees shattered, she remained alive and gradually began recovering from the damage. After James removed the restrictions placed upon her abilities, her regenerative capability noticeably increased.[5][2]
- Survivability: Sylvie can survive catastrophic injuries that would immediately kill an ordinary Human. She remained conscious after extensive mutilation by Victor and continued functioning while impaled upon his ice construct.[5][2]
- Pain Tolerance: Sylvie displayed exceptional tolerance for extreme physical trauma. Even after Victor mutilated her and subjected her to a deliberately painful restraint, she remained capable of speaking, observing her surroundings and attempting to recover.[5][2]
- Dual Dagger Wieldership: Sylvie fights using two golden daggers simultaneously. She demonstrated enough proficiency with them to launch a rapid succession of slashes against Victor immediately after entering the battlefield.[5]
- Enhanced Speed and Agility: Sylvie is capable of rapid battlefield movement and sudden interception. She appeared before Victor while he was preparing to kill August and immediately initiated a close-range attack with her twin daggers.[5]
- Combat Skill: As a Hunter serving among James' elite forces, Sylvie possesses considerable combat experience. Her fighting style emphasizes rapid movement and close-range attacks with her twin daggers rather than heavy or prolonged exchanges.[4][5]
- Combat Analysis: Sylvie possesses notable analytical intelligence. While observing Victor, she identified that his true danger lay not merely in the quantity of powers he possessed but in his mentality, combat instinct and ability to seamlessly control those powers together.[3]
- Tactical Awareness: Sylvie recognized the danger created by August's independent behavior and specifically warned James that provoking [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] could have disastrous consequences. She similarly questioned whether James' continued expenditure of experimental subjects against Victor was strategically worthwhile.[1][3]
- Restricted Power (Formerly): James had deliberately placed restrictions upon the abilities of both S-000 and A-000. During the Japanese conflict he remotely removed those restrictions in an attempt to increase their chances of surviving Victor. The complete nature and maximum extent of Sylvie's unrestricted powers were never demonstrated before her capture.[2]

### Trivia
- Sylvie's experimental designation is S-000.[7]
- She was first mentioned in Chapter 186 as a mysterious female experimental subject associated with James' Hybrid project, considerably earlier than her appearance during the Crimson Nightmare.[7]
- Sylvie first appears directly by Chapter 191, where she converses with James concerning Carlos and August.[1]
- Despite being one of James' most advanced artificial supernatural weapons, Sylvie enjoys watching Korean dramas and complained that dealing with James' problems interfered with her viewing time.[1]
- James regarded Sylvie and August as his strongest and nearly perfected experimental products.[2]
- Sylvie appeared considerably more skeptical of James' methods than a blindly loyal subordinate would be. She questioned his Human experimentation and privately regarded the people involved with the project as corrupt.[3]
- Her decision to attack [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] in Chapter 344 was specifically motivated by preventing him from killing August, demonstrating that their relationship extended beyond simply being two experiments serving the same creator.[5]
- The previous claim that Sylvie was killed by Victor Elderblood during the Crimson Nightmare is not supported by Chapters 344-347. Victor instead captured her and August, gave them to [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]], and Ruby subsequently returned to [[Nightingale]] with both Hybrids for research.[2][6]
