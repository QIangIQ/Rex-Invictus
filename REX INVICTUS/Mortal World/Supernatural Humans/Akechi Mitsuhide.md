### Physical Appearance
He is historically known as a samurai and high-ranking general under [[Oda Nobunaga]].

### Personality
Akechi is portrayed as a calculating and pragmatic strategist, willing to betray his master for his own reasons.

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes and War
Akechi Mitsuhide was one of Nobunaga’s most trusted subordinates, but he ultimately turned against him, aligning with [[Toyotomi Hideyoshi]] and [[Kurama]] to stage the Honnō-ji Incident. Following Nobunaga’s forced seppuku, Akechi vanished, choosing not to remain in the ensuing power struggle. The last known information about him is that he became the lord of some land, though Kurama expresses no interest in his fate.[1]
