Adam was the first Human created by the Heavenly Father, the original Human Progenitor of Earth and the beginning of the Human bloodline that would eventually spread across the planet. Unlike modern Humans, whose defining racial gifts of Potential and Adaptability generally exist in comparatively undeveloped forms limited by their bodies, Souls and circumstances, those qualities were already fully developed within Adam as the Progenitor of the race.[1]
Adam was also one of the exceedingly rare beings whom the Heavenly Father directly created as a complete lifeform with a complete Soul. Long after Adam's death, the Heavenly Father revealed that the last occasions on which he had used Creation to make life in the truest sense—body, life and Soul together—were the creation of Adam and Lilith. Eve was created differently, using Adam's Soul as a foundation together with another pre-existing Soul possessed by the Heavenly Father.[2]
Although Adam was a Progenitor, he possessed an unusual limitation not shared by most known members of that class. Most Progenitors can transform beings of another race into members of their own species, but Adam is explicitly identified as the confirmed exception: he could not transform another species into a Human.[3] Humanity instead spread through ordinary reproduction and inherited progressively lesser expressions of the extraordinary qualities present in its original ancestor.
Adam died long before the events of the main story and has never physically appeared in the present narrative. Most information regarding him is reconstructed through the knowledge of ancient beings such as [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]], explanations from the Heavenly Father and later analysis by [[Victor Elderblood]]. Despite this limited direct portrayal, later revelations repeatedly establish Adam as something radically superior to the ordinary Humans of the modern era. The difference was so extreme that Victor eventually questioned whether modern Humanity had been deliberately reduced from the condition represented by its original Progenitor.[4]

### Physical Appearance
Adam has never physically appeared in the present-day narrative, and the novel has not provided a sufficiently detailed canonical description of his hair, eyes, clothing, height or other identifying features. The existing portraits used on the wiki are artistic interpretations and should not be treated as evidence for his canonical appearance.
What is established is that Adam represented an extraordinarily superior form of Humanity compared with ordinary modern Humans. Later comparisons between Adam and advanced Human variants describe him as closer to an ideal or perfected original Human state, although even the most advanced modern Human evolutions should not be considered directly equivalent to Adam because he additionally possessed the unique metaphysical status of a Progenitor.[5][6]

### Personality
Very little is known about Adam's individual personality because he died long before the main story and has never been shown through a direct scene, memory or extended first-person account. Consequently, personality traits commonly associated with the biblical Adam or with Progenitors in general should not automatically be attributed to him without support from the novel.
The most important characteristic attached to Adam's existence is Free [[Will Solace|Will]]. The [[Heavenly Father]] considers freedom of choice one of the fundamental principles granted to the beings he created and repeatedly refuses to treat his creations as entities whose every decision should be predetermined by their creator.[7] Adam therefore belonged to the earliest generation of Humanity capable of independently choosing its own path, including choices that could directly contradict the intentions or expectations of its creator.
His status as a Progenitor also implies extraordinary independence compared with ordinary members of his species. Adam was not simply an early Human placed at the beginning of a genealogical tree; he represented Humanity at its original maximum starting condition, possessing racial qualities that his descendants would inherit only in diminished or undeveloped forms.[1]

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Adam was first discussed when [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] attempted to understand the increasingly abnormal growth of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Victor had originally been Human before becoming a Vampire, and his transformation into a Progenitor caused qualities inherited from Humanity to interact with his new existence in ways Vlad considered highly unusual. To explain the phenomenon, Vlad reflected upon Humanity's two exceptional racial characteristics: Potential and Adaptability.[1]
For ordinary Humans, those abilities functioned more like seeds than completed powers. Their potential could theoretically allow enormous development, but the body, Soul, lifespan, environment and personal drive of an ordinary Human usually prevented that potential from ever reaching its theoretical limit. Human adaptability likewise allowed the species to survive and develop across changing circumstances despite being physically weak compared with most supernatural races. Adam was different. As the first Human and the Progenitor from whom those traits originated, what existed as undeveloped seeds within later Humans was already comparable to a fully grown tree within Adam.[1]
This comparison became important to understanding Victor because his original Human foundation had not completely disappeared when he became a Vampire. The Progenitor state allowed portions of his inherited Human Potential and Adaptability to develop far beyond their previous limitations, causing Vlad to compare the process with the condition Adam had possessed naturally from the beginning.[1]

#### King Among Kings
Later research into the nature of Progenitors clarified one of Adam's greatest abnormalities within his own class. A fundamental advantage possessed by most known Progenitors is the ability to expand their race by converting individuals of another species into members of their own bloodline. Vampire Progenitors can create Vampires, Werewolf Progenitors can create [[Werewolves]] and similar racial founders possess equivalent reproductive or transformative authority over their species.
Adam was explicitly identified as the exception to this rule. Despite being the Progenitor of Humanity, he possessed no ability to take a non-Human being and transform it into a Human.[3] Humanity therefore developed differently from many supernatural races whose Progenitors could directly manufacture new members. Adam's descendants expanded principally through reproduction, eventually producing the enormous Human population that came to dominate Earth numerically despite the species' comparatively poor individual combat ability.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s visit to the [[Biblical Heaven]] provided considerably more context regarding Adam's origin. Within the [[Heavenly Father]]'s domain remained the Garden of Eden, preserving structures associated with Humanity's earliest history, including the Tree of Life and the Tree of Knowledge of Good and Evil. The latter's fruits possessed an unusual relationship with both positive and negative forces and contained knowledge considered dangerous even by Divine standards.[8]
During his conversation with Victor, the Heavenly Father explained the philosophical relationship between a creator and the form of a creation. He used Adam as the principal example: God made man in his own image, and Adam was the result. Adam was therefore not simply assembled as an arbitrary biological organism; the first Human was created according to an image derived from the Heavenly Father's understanding of himself.[9]
Further revelations concerning Soul creation made Adam's origin even more exceptional. The Heavenly Father explained that the creation of entirely new life possessing a completely new Soul eventually became heavily regulated by the higher mechanisms governing Life, Death and the universal system. Following the earliest generations of his creations, even the Heavenly Father could no longer indiscriminately manufacture complete Souls without oversight. Adam and Lilith were among the last beings he personally created as complete lives with complete Souls.[2]
Eve's creation was specifically different. Rather than creating another completely new Soul in the same manner as Adam and Lilith, the Heavenly Father used Adam's Soul as a foundation and combined it with another Soul already available to him.[2] This distinction makes Adam one of the most direct expressions of the Heavenly Father's original Creation power within Humanity.
Adam and Lilith subsequently becoming Progenitors should not be interpreted to mean that the Heavenly Father could simply manufacture Progenitors whenever he desired. Progenitor status is a much deeper phenomenon tied to becoming the beginning of a race or bloodline. Adam was created as complete life by the Heavenly Father and then occupied the unique position of Humanity's Progenitor, making him both a Divine creation and the biological-metaphysical origin point of his species.

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
As [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s understanding of races and biological evolution increased, Adam became an increasingly important benchmark for evaluating Humanity. Later discussions established that the superior original state represented by Adam possessed extraordinary longevity compared with ordinary Humans. A Human existing at such a level could live for thousands of years rather than being restricted to the extremely short lifespan of modern Humanity.[10]
The enormous disparity between Adam and his descendants eventually caused Victor to question the accepted assumption that Humans had simply always been naturally weak. Modern Humans possessed fragile bodies, short lifespans, susceptibility to disease and limited supernatural defenses despite descending from a Progenitor as extraordinary as Adam. Based upon the accumulated memories he had consumed from [[Lucifer]] Morningstar, [[Diablo]] and hundreds of Gods, Victor considered it likely that Humanity's present weakness had been deliberately produced by the various Divine creators involved with different Human civilizations.[4]
Victor did not treat this conclusion as absolute proof. He acknowledged that completely confirming the theory would require information directly from beings responsible for creating or modifying Humanity, such as Human-creator Gods whose memories he had not yet personally obtained.[4] Adam's extraordinary original condition nevertheless remained one of the strongest reasons Victor considered the modern weakness of Humans suspicious.
The theoretical ceiling of a Human Progenitor was addressed again when Victor considered how dramatically the result would change if a being possessing Adam's racial foundation also possessed the relentless mentality of someone such as [[Scathach]]. Victor judged that a Human Progenitor with that degree of obsession toward continuous improvement could readily become an Overlord of an entire Sector.[11] The statement concerned potential rather than a confirmed feat personally performed by Adam, but it demonstrated how highly Victor evaluated the ceiling of the species' original Progenitor.
Victor's later creation and study of High Humans provided another indirect comparison. High Humans represented Humanity raised toward a far more complete biological state, possessing immense physical attributes, accelerated recovery, greater resistance to disease, greatly improved adaptability and a far stronger foundation for supernatural growth. They were compared with the ideal represented by Adam, but remained fundamentally different because Adam was not simply a highly evolved Human—he was the Human Progenitor.[5][6]

### Skills & Abilities
Adam was the original Human Progenitor and consequently cannot be evaluated according to the limitations of ordinary modern Humans. Most of his known abilities are racial and metaphysical rather than combat techniques because the story has never shown Adam participating directly in battle.

#### Human Progenitor Physiology
- Progenitor Physiology: Adam was the first Progenitor of Humanity and the beginning of Earth's principal Human bloodline. His existence represented the racial foundation from which later Human generations inherited their defining characteristics.[1]
- [[Superior Human Physiology]]: Adam's original Human body was vastly superior to the fragile physiology associated with modern ordinary Humans. Later comparisons repeatedly use Adam as the benchmark for Humanity in an ideal or original condition.[4][5]
- Enhanced Physical Attributes: Although no direct combat scene provides exact measurements for Adam's strength, speed or durability, his status as a Human Progenitor and the later comparison between Adam and advanced Human forms establish physical capabilities enormously beyond ordinary Humanity.[5][6]
- Extreme Longevity: Adam's superior original Human condition allowed a lifespan extending far beyond ordinary Humanity, with later discussions indicating that Humans of such a level could survive for thousands of years.[10]
- Complete Soul: Adam was created as a complete life possessing a complete Soul rather than being constructed from only a fragment, recycled component or incomplete spiritual foundation.[2]
- Created Life: Unlike ordinary Humans born through reproduction, Adam was personally created through the [[Heavenly Father]]'s Creation power as one of his earliest complete Human lifeforms.[2]
- Divine Image: Adam was created according to the Heavenly Father's own conceptual "image", making his original form an intentional expression of how the Heavenly Father understood the Human life he wished to create.[9]

#### Human Potential
- Unparalleled Potential: Potential is one of Humanity's two defining racial advantages. So long as a Human possesses a body and Soul capable of supporting further development, their theoretical room for growth can become extraordinary.[1]
- Fully Developed Potential: Ordinary Humans inherit Potential in a largely undeveloped state, comparable to a seed whose growth is restricted by their physical and spiritual limitations. Adam, as the Progenitor, possessed this characteristic at a fundamentally superior level from the beginning.[1]
- Continuous Growth Potential: Adam's racial foundation did not possess the same inherently low ceiling associated with ordinary modern Humanity. The later evaluation of Human Progenitors demonstrates that sufficient [[Will Solace|Will]], training and opportunity could drive such an existence toward the highest levels found within a Sector.[11]
- Sector Overlord Potential: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] judged that a Human Progenitor possessing a mentality comparable to [[Scathach]]'s relentless pursuit of improvement could readily become the supreme Overlord of a Sector.[11] This is a statement regarding the potential of an entity such as Adam rather than evidence that Adam himself historically achieved Sector-Overlord status.

#### Human Adaptability
- Superior Adaptability: Adaptability is Humanity's second defining racial characteristic. Humans possess exceptionally high capacity to adjust to new conditions, environments and circumstances, with Monsters being among the few races noted for even greater natural adaptability.[1]
- Fully Developed Adaptability: Just as with Potential, the Adaptability inherited by later Humans existed within Adam in a far more complete state because he was the Progenitor from whom the racial characteristic originated.[1]
- Environmental Adaptation: Humanity's survival despite its later physical weakness is attributed substantially to this capacity to adjust, innovate and continue functioning under unfavorable conditions. Adam possessed the original and superior foundation of the same racial characteristic.[1]
- Developmental Flexibility: Adam's racial foundation allowed substantially greater room for biological and supernatural development than the condition demonstrated by ordinary Humans.

#### Free Will
- Free [[Will Solace|Will]]: Free Will is one of the central principles associated with the Humanity created by the [[Heavenly Father]]. The Heavenly Father explicitly treats beings making their own decisions and suffering the consequences of those decisions as a fundamental aspect of his creations.[7]
- Independent Will: As one of the Heavenly Father's earliest complete creations, Adam was not designed merely as an extension or puppet of his creator. His ability to make choices independently was integral to the philosophical foundation upon which Humanity was created.
- Resistance to External Control: Human racial lore associates the Free Will granted to Adam, Eve and Lilith with increased resistance against attempts by supernatural beings of comparable standing to completely dominate their wills. This should not be interpreted as absolute immunity to every form of Mind, Soul or Will manipulation, particularly from entities overwhelmingly superior to the target.

#### Progenitor-Level Growth
- Immense Original Power: Adam was explicitly remembered as enormously more powerful than ordinary modern Humans. The difference was significant enough for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to question how descendants of such an extraordinary Progenitor had become physically so weak.[4]
- Exceptional Racial Ceiling: Adam's importance lies less in a demonstrated named attack and more in the sheer developmental ceiling of his existence. A Human Progenitor provided with sufficient motivation and training was considered capable of rising to the level required to dominate an entire Sector.[11]
- Superior Biological Foundation: High Humans created or developed much later in the story were likened to the idealized state represented by Adam while still being acknowledged as inferior to a genuine Human Progenitor.[5][6]

#### Racial Limitation
- Race Creation Demerit: Adam is the confirmed exception to the normal Progenitor ability of transforming beings from another species into members of the Progenitor's own race. He could not transform another species into a Human.[3]
- This limitation does not prevent Humanity from reproducing naturally. Adam instead became the genealogical origin of later Humans through descendants produced by the original Human family.
- Adam's inability to convert other species should not be interpreted as evidence that he was an incomplete Progenitor. The story explicitly recognizes him as Humanity's Progenitor while identifying the absence of racial conversion as an unusual Human-specific limitation.[3]

### Weaknesses
- Progenitor Conversion Limitation: Unlike most other known Progenitors, Adam lacked the ability to convert members of another race into Humans.[3]
- Mortal Nature: Despite his extraordinary power and lifespan, Adam remained fundamentally a mortal rather than a God. His existence was superior to ordinary Humanity but did not grant unconditional Godly immortality.
- Loss of Original State: Adam's eventual consumption of the Forbidden Fruit is recorded as the event that resulted in the loss of his previous superior condition and his transition into ordinary mortality. He subsequently died through natural causes rather than remaining an immortal or indefinitely long-lived Progenitor.
- Potential Requires Development: Possessing extraordinary Potential does not automatically grant its theoretical maximum. Later commentary concerning Human Progenitors specifically emphasizes the importance of mentality and continuous self-improvement when discussing their possible rise to Sector-Overlord status.[11]

### Relationships

#### Heavenly Father
[[Heavenly Father]] was Adam's creator and is repeatedly treated as his father in the broader Biblical lineage. Adam was not born through biological reproduction; the Heavenly Father personally created him as a complete lifeform possessing a complete Soul.[2]
Adam held an exceptional place among the Heavenly Father's creations. The creation of complete new Souls eventually came under increasing regulation from the higher mechanisms governing Life and Death, making the circumstances under which Adam had been produced increasingly difficult to reproduce. The Heavenly Father later identified Adam and Lilith among the last beings he had directly created in the complete sense of producing new life together with its own Soul.[2]
The Heavenly Father's statement that man was created in God's "image" specifically uses Adam as the example. Adam's original Human condition was therefore based upon an image the Heavenly Father derived through his own self-understanding rather than being an accidental product of natural evolution.[9]

#### Lilith Elderblood
Lilith was Adam's first wife and another exceptional complete creation of the [[Heavenly Father]]. Both Adam and Lilith were created with complete Souls and later became Progenitors, Adam of Humanity and Lilith of Demons.[2]
Their marriage ultimately ended in divorce. The novel has revealed considerably more about Lilith's later life than about the period of her relationship with Adam, and the currently available material does not provide enough direct detail to reconstruct their entire marriage without relying upon external mythology.
Their shared origin remains significant. They represent two of the Heavenly Father's rarest acts of complete Life Creation and eventually became the racial starting points of two radically different populations.

#### Eve
Eve became Adam's later wife and the woman through whom the principal Human bloodline continued. Her origin differed substantially from Adam's. Rather than creating another entirely independent Soul from nothing, the [[Heavenly Father]] formed Eve using Adam's Soul as a base together with another Soul already in his possession.[2]
This makes Eve spiritually related to Adam at a deeper level than ordinary reproduction while still remaining an independent person. Adam and Eve's descendants eventually formed the foundation of Earth's enormous Human population.

#### Humanity
Adam stands at the beginning of Humanity's principal lineage and is the Progenitor from whom later Humans inherited their extraordinary Potential and Adaptability.[1] Those traits became progressively restricted by weaker bodies, Souls, short lifespans and ordinary living conditions, meaning the average modern Human bears little resemblance to the level represented by Adam himself.
Even so, the underlying qualities were never completely lost. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s own Human origin demonstrated how dramatically those inherited traits could develop when placed inside a sufficiently powerful body and Progenitor foundation, while the later existence of Primordial and High Human variants showed that Humanity could still move toward states substantially closer to its superior origins.[1][5][6]

### Trivia
- Adam is one of the oldest known Progenitors connected to Earth and the original Progenitor of Humanity.
- Although Adam was created directly by the [[Heavenly Father]], his status as a Progenitor should not be interpreted as proof that the Heavenly Father can intentionally manufacture Progenitors at will. Adam was created as complete life and subsequently occupied the metaphysical position of Humanity's beginning.[2]
- Adam and [[Lilith Elderblood]] were among the last complete lifeforms whom the Heavenly Father created together with entirely complete Souls.[2]
- Eve was not created through exactly the same process. The Heavenly Father used Adam's Soul as a foundation and combined it with another Soul already in his possession.[2]
- The statement that "God made man in his image" is directly used in the novel to explain Adam's creation rather than existing only as an external biblical reference.[9]
- [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] used Adam as the benchmark when attempting to explain why [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s original Human Potential and Adaptability reacted so strongly after Victor became a Progenitor.[1]
- Adam is the confirmed exception to the usual Progenitor racial-conversion ability. He could not transform beings of another race into Humans.[3]
- Adam's inability to create Humans through racial conversion helps distinguish Humanity from species such as Vampires and [[Werewolves]], whose Progenitors can directly transform outsiders into members of their race.[3]
- The preserved Garden of Eden still contains the Tree of Life and Tree of Knowledge of Good and Evil during Victor's era.[8]
- Adam's original power was so far removed from ordinary modern Humanity that Victor suspected Humans had been deliberately weakened at some point by the Divine beings involved in creating and managing Human civilizations.[4]
- Victor's theory that Humanity was intentionally weakened remains an informed deduction rather than completely confirmed history. His opinion was based upon the enormous number of memories he had absorbed from beings such as [[Lucifer]], [[Diablo]] and numerous Gods, but he acknowledged that obtaining definitive proof would require information from a deity directly responsible for Human creation or alteration.[4]
- According to Victor, a Human Progenitor possessing a mentality comparable to [[Scathach]] could easily develop into an Overlord of an entire Sector.[11]
- That statement describes the potential ceiling of a Human Progenitor and should not be misrepresented as evidence that Adam himself historically became a Sector Overlord.
- Later High Humans were compared with the superior state represented by Adam, but the comparison was explicitly imperfect because even an exceptionally advanced Human is not automatically equivalent to a genuine Progenitor.[5][6]
