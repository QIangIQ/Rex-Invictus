Merlin Ambrosius was an ancient Human mage associated with Arthur Pendragon, one of the greatest known specialists in the manipulation and understanding of supernatural Energy, and later a high-ranking Asura Demon who served as both General and teacher to Yama. Merlin was anomalous even before becoming a Demon: while the form of [[Magic]] practiced by Earth's Witches was normally exclusive to women, Merlin was the only known Human male capable of naturally accessing and using the same system without an external Blessing or racial modification.[1]
His defining talent was not merely possessing an enormous catalogue of spells, but understanding the principles underlying supernatural Energy itself. Merlin recognized that Magic, Miasma, Demonic Energy and other apparently incompatible powers were fundamentally forms of Energy operating according to different properties and rules. After transforming into a Demon and losing access to the Energy source that supported his Human Magic, he applied that understanding to create an entirely new spellcasting system using his Demonic Energy and Hell's Miasma, personally naming it Demon Magic.[1]
Merlin was an ancient acquaintance of [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], who had helped train Arthur and had even trained Merlin on occasion. Although the two regularly insulted and irritated one another, [[Scathach]] respected Merlin enough to treat practically everything he said or did as potentially part of a larger strategy. Centuries later she still considered him an absolute monster within his field of expertise.[1][2]
After centuries serving within the Hindu [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]], Merlin's pursuit of knowledge eventually placed his usefulness above his loyalty to any faction. His own student Yama ultimately betrayed him by revealing his location to the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Merlin was hunted by [[Violet Elderblood Snow]], Nyx Elderblood and [[Hilda Snow Elderblood]], who systematically removed his ability to manipulate Energy before killing him. His extraordinary potential was deliberately preserved rather than discarded, and the remaining foundation of his Soul was later reconstructed by [[The Universal Tree]]. The process changed the Soul so fundamentally that the resulting girl, [[Melida Charlotte Elderblood]], was recognized as a new individual rather than Merlin simply returning in another body.[3][4]

### Physical Appearance

#### Human
During his original Human life, Merlin was remembered as an elderly man. By the period of Arthur Pendragon and Camelot, he already possessed the appearance historically associated with an old mage.
The novel has not provided a sufficiently detailed canonical description of Merlin's original Human height, facial features, hair color or clothing to establish them with certainty. His Human artwork should therefore not be treated as a complete canonical reference for characteristics that have never been described.

#### Demon
Merlin's transformation into an Asura completely altered his body. His Demonic form stood approximately ten meters tall and possessed red skin, four eyes and four arms, creating an enormous physique almost completely unrecognizable compared with the elderly Human [[Scathach]] remembered.[5][6]
His appearance changed so extensively that Scathach did not immediately identify him visually. Instead, Merlin's speech patterns, knowledge, irritating mannerisms and familiarity with her gradually exposed his identity.[6][1]
Although his new physiology granted the advantages of a powerful Demon, it also introduced the racial incompatibility between Demons and sufficiently concentrated [[Divine Energy]]. Merlin had already begun developing equipment intended to reduce this disadvantage, demonstrating his characteristic refusal to simply accept a weakness as permanent.[1]

### Personality
Merlin was extraordinarily intelligent, manipulative, eccentric and almost pathologically curious. Knowledge was the closest thing he possessed to an overriding personal allegiance. Political interests, danger and even the interests of whichever ruler he happened to serve could become secondary if Merlin believed an opportunity offered access to genuinely new information.[7]
[[Scathach]] considered conversation with Merlin exhausting because practically nothing he said could safely be interpreted only at face value. He regularly embedded additional information, warnings and implications into apparently ordinary statements, sometimes revealing useful intelligence while simultaneously pursuing objectives known only to himself.[1]
His confidence was primarily intellectual rather than based upon raw physical superiority. Merlin knew that his mind was exceptional and once described himself as a "hard-working genius". Scathach considered even that self-description misleadingly modest because ordinary effort could not explain his capacity to rebuild entire supernatural systems through understanding alone.[1]
Merlin's obsession with understanding unfamiliar phenomena was both his greatest strength and one of his most exploitable weaknesses. The clearest example occurred when Yama attempted to negotiate access to a newly discovered world controlled by the Witches. Merlin prepared an agreement so disproportionately favorable to Arcane that Yama immediately realized his teacher was effectively sacrificing his own faction's political and economic advantage purely to guarantee Merlin access to whatever knowledge existed beyond the portal.[7]
Even under mortal danger, Merlin's mind continued analyzing. During his final hunt he was physically exhausted, unable to access Energy, unable to use Space and unable to activate his Runes, yet instead of collapsing into panic he continuously examined the phenomena affecting him. He correctly identified the nature of Violet's conceptual authority, considered what categories of power could theoretically oppose it and detected irregularities revealing that an unseen third party was interfering with the battlefield before Nyx revealed herself.[3]
Violet found this characteristic sufficiently impressive that even while hunting him she recognized the extraordinary value of Merlin's mind.[3]
Merlin also retained several strong moral convictions from his Human life despite becoming a Demon. He possessed an intense hatred of sexual violence and explicitly believed that people who enjoyed committing such crimes deserved Hell regardless of gender. After entering Yama's service, he deliberately obtained responsibility for punishing such sinners.[6]
His concept of punishment, however, was thoroughly Demonic. Merlin believed that people guilty of inflicting extreme suffering should personally experience the suffering they had caused. He was therefore capable of displaying both genuine moral disgust toward an action and extreme cruelty toward the person responsible for it.[6]
This contradiction summarized much of Merlin's morality. He possessed principles, but mercy was not necessarily one of them. He disliked hypocrisy and had little sympathy for individuals who committed atrocities while sheltering behind authority, morality, status or supposed righteousness.
Merlin's arrogance ultimately contributed to his death. During his final moments he admitted that he had failed to prepare some countermeasures because he had become confident that nobody would ever be capable of completely severing his connection to Energy. Violet's Order demonstrated that his assumption was wrong.[3]

### History

#### Camelot
During his Human life, Merlin became one of the most important figures surrounding Arthur Pendragon. He acted as Arthur's adviser, guardian and teacher, helping prepare him for the responsibilities associated with becoming King.[1]
Arthur's development also brought Merlin into direct contact with [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]]. [[Scathach]] participated in Arthur's martial training and occasionally trained Merlin himself. Their long history explains why Scathach could eventually recognize Merlin despite his complete transformation into an Asura.[1]
Merlin respected Arthur and continued speaking of him long after Camelot had disappeared. He also acknowledged that Scathach succeeded in areas where his own teaching had failed, particularly in forcing Arthur to mature sufficiently to accept the path and responsibilities awaiting him. Such an admission was significant because Merlin rarely acknowledged his own shortcomings openly.[1]
His Human existence was already supernatural in an unprecedented way. The [[Magic]] practiced by Witches was naturally tied to women, yet Merlin possessed direct access despite being male. He did not require a Divine Blessing, artificial alteration or assistance from a Witch to bypass the restriction.[1]
This distinction is particularly important when comparing Merlin with [[Victor Elderblood]]. Victor eventually gained access to Witch Magic through supernatural alteration and the influence of Albedo Moriarty, while Merlin's compatibility existed naturally.[1]
Merlin also accumulated advanced knowledge of the Soul. [[Viviane]] later remembered being taught principles concerning Soul structure by Merlin despite possessing her own natural sensitivity toward such phenomena.[8]

#### Transformation into a Demon
At an unknown point after the age of Camelot, Merlin ceased being Human and became an Asura Demon.
The complete procedure through which the transformation occurred has not been revealed. [[Scathach]] nevertheless strongly suspected Merlin had arranged the transformation himself rather than being forcibly corrupted or accidentally converted. In her experience, voluntarily changing his species simply to explore another supernatural system was entirely consistent with his personality.[1]
The transformation had an unexpected consequence: Merlin lost access to the Energy he had previously used for his Human [[Magic]].[1]
Rather than regard this as the end of his magical career, Merlin studied the properties of Hell's Miasma and the Demonic Energy produced by his new body. By understanding how these Energies differed from the source he had previously used, he reconstructed the fundamental process of spellcasting around his new physiology.[1]
The result was Demon Magic, a magical system of Merlin's own creation powered through Demonic Energy and Miasma rather than the original Energy associated with Witch-style Magic.[1]
This achievement became one of the strongest demonstrations of Merlin's underlying philosophy:
Energy is Energy.
To Merlin, supernatural Energies were not completely unrelated substances. They were different expressions of a broader phenomenon, each governed by particular characteristics and implicit rules. A sufficiently knowledgeable practitioner could therefore understand the rules of a new Energy source and develop methods appropriate to it rather than remaining permanently restricted to a single inherited system.[1][9]
Merlin eventually became connected to the Hindu [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] and entered the service of Yama. His position was unusual: officially he was one of Yama's Generals and subordinates, yet Merlin simultaneously acted as the younger ruler's teacher and possessed vastly greater knowledge in areas such as Magic, Energy and supernatural theory.[10]

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Merlin made his first direct appearance during the Demonic invasion of the Amazon Realm. Acting as one of Yama's major commanders, he led a Demonic force into Amazon territory during the expanding global conflict.[5]
The attack resulted in enormous casualties and revealed that the invading army was supported by something highly unusual for Demons. Hundreds of thousands of interconnected [[Magic]] Circles infused with Miasma had been distributed across the battlefield, creating a massive magical formation whose scale immediately indicated the involvement of an exceptional practitioner.[5][6]
Demons ordinarily relied upon Miasma and racial Demonic powers rather than the complex external spell structures associated with Witches. The presence of such an enormous magical infrastructure therefore became one of the first clues that the commander responsible was fundamentally different from an ordinary Demon.[6]
When [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], Natashia, Agnes and their allies entered the conflict, [[Scathach]] gradually began recognizing familiar traits in the gigantic four-armed Demon. His manner of speaking, knowledge of ancient events and habit of irritating her eventually confirmed his identity as Merlin.[6]
Merlin explained that his transformation had not erased the principles he carried from his Human life. He had specifically arranged to become responsible for punishing offenders guilty of sexual violence in Yama's Hell because his hatred of such crimes remained unchanged. His methods were brutal: Merlin believed such sinners should be forced to understand the suffering they had caused by experiencing comparable suffering themselves.[6]
Scathach soon discovered that Merlin could still perform sophisticated Magic despite being a Demon. Merlin explained that this was technically no longer the same Magic he had once practiced as a Human. Losing his original Energy source had forced him to build another system around Miasma and Demonic Energy, producing what he called Demon Magic.[1]
Their conversation evolved into a discussion of Energy itself. Merlin explained that different Energies operated according to their own characteristics, but the underlying principle remained the same: Energy could be studied, understood and manipulated if the practitioner learned the rules governing it.[1]
Merlin also provided Scathach with strategic intelligence. He identified several categories of beings or organizations capable of discovering major supernatural movements despite attempts at secrecy, including Nyx, cosmic forces responsible for maintaining Balance, beings capable of examining possible futures and [[New Dawn]].[1]
According to Merlin, New Dawn had developed into a multinational and multi-species organization containing Vampires, [[Werewolves]], Hybrids, Demons, Elder Gods and numerous other supernatural assets. Their information-gathering capacity meant that the movements of major factions could no longer be treated as reliably secret.[1]
Merlin warned Scathach that the conflict occurring around them was larger than a normal war between Heaven and Hell. Grudges, plans and political tensions that had accumulated for thousands of years were converging, threatening the traditional assumptions separating good from evil, Heaven from Hell and the supernatural world's established order.[1]
Despite having little reason to assist [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction directly, Merlin gave Scathach this warning because of their ancient relationship. He departed through a dark portal rather than allow their encounter to develop into a battle.[1]
Merlin later participated in the operation surrounding Yama's confrontation with [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]]. He had been instrumental in training Yama to suppress the appearance of his true strength, allowing the younger ruler to appear only somewhat stronger than major combat Gods while hiding abilities Merlin considered strategically important.[10]
When Yama became too excited during his battle against Vlad and began revealing hidden powers, Merlin personally intervened. He reprimanded Yama for treating an intelligence-gathering operation like a genuine battle to the death and eventually forced the younger King to withdraw once the objective had been accomplished.[10]
Merlin's magical influence was also displayed through the establishment of a Diabolic Zone, an area controlled and saturated through his Demonic Magic.[10]
His expertise saved Yama when the Demon King attempted to directly withstand Dragon Breath produced using an Ancient Dragon's power. Merlin recognized that the attack's unusual damage properties could make the resulting wounds exceptionally difficult to heal and redirected it before Yama received the attack directly.[10]
The larger war eventually turned overwhelmingly against [[Diablo]] and his coalition. Merlin remained beside Yama until Victor's conquest of [[Biblical Hell]] invalidated Yama's agreement with Diablo. Yama and his forces withdrew rather than die defending a King with whom their contract no longer applied.

#### King Among Kings
Merlin survived the Demonic War and continued expanding his understanding of supernatural Energy. By this period [[Scathach]] still regarded him as an absolute monster in the field of Energy control.[2]
His importance increasingly came from the principles behind his techniques rather than any single spell. Merlin had already demonstrated that changing races and losing an entire Energy source did not prevent him from rebuilding a functional magical system through another one. His theories therefore anticipated discoveries later made by other high-level researchers concerning the common foundations underlying apparently separate supernatural systems.[9]
His older teachings also continued influencing other characters. [[Viviane]] retained the Soul knowledge Merlin had taught her, while Yama continued applying lessons concerning power concealment, Energy and strategy learned from his teacher.[8]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Merlin later accompanied Yama to [[The Arcane Realm]] after the Witches discovered a portal connected to another world.[7]
The mere existence of an unexplored world immediately attracted Merlin's interest. Evie Moriarty recognized him despite his Demonic transformation and warned the surrounding Witches not to casually cast [[Magic]] in his presence.[7]
Evie's assessment placed Merlin among the greatest known geniuses in Energy control. His mastery was sufficiently advanced that he could interfere with Magic after another practitioner had already begun using it.[7]
She demonstrated the danger personally when attempting to use Evaluation Magic upon Yama and Merlin. Merlin immediately detected the spell and destroyed it before the Magic could successfully analyze either of them.[7]
This was not ordinary resistance to a status-reading ability. Merlin recognized and disrupted the Energy structure of another mage's spell directly, demonstrating that his theoretical understanding translated into highly dangerous counter-magic.[7]
Merlin was also secretly responsible for the first contract Yama presented to the Witches. The agreement was so favorable to Arcane that Evie immediately suspected Merlin's involvement. When Yama actually examined the terms, he realized Merlin had effectively sacrificed his King's negotiating position merely because the arrangement would grant Merlin access to the unexplored world and its knowledge.[7]
Merlin responded by avoiding responsibility and looking away while Yama discarded the agreement and produced a second proposal.[7]
The incident demonstrated the hierarchy of Merlin's priorities more clearly than almost any battle: the discovery of genuinely new knowledge could outweigh political advantage, factional loyalty and even the interests of his own King.
Evie rejected Yama's revised agreement as well, and the two Demons eventually left Arcane rather than risk provoking [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] by attempting to take the portal through force.[7]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Merlin's long relationship with Yama eventually ended through betrayal.
By Chapter 1097, Yama had concluded that the expanding [[Elderblood Empire]] offered greater opportunities for his future than remaining politically isolated. He therefore provided information concerning Merlin's location to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family.[3]
Merlin quickly realized that the hunt targeting him was unlike anything he had previously experienced. His access to Energy disappeared, Space was sealed and even his Runes refused to operate. From these conditions alone, Merlin correctly concluded that whoever was pursuing him possessed authority beyond Yama and potentially far beyond ordinary Gods.[3]
The primary hunter was [[Violet Elderblood Snow]].
Violet used her Divine authority over Order to impose conditions upon the surrounding Reality that prevented Merlin from properly interacting with Energy. This directly targeted the foundation of practically every supernatural technique he possessed.[3]
Merlin immediately began analyzing the phenomenon despite his condition. He recognized that an ordinary magical countermeasure would accomplish nothing and reasoned that opposing Violet's effect would require either superior Conceptual authority or a power capable of fundamentally contradicting Order itself.[3]
The analysis impressed Violet. Merlin had been deprived of the ability around which he had constructed his entire supernatural existence and remained focused on understanding the mechanism responsible rather than simply panicking.[3]
Merlin also detected inconsistencies in the surrounding Energy and correctly suspected another hidden participant, although he could not locate the source. Nyx Elderblood eventually revealed herself, explaining why Merlin's perception had failed.[3]
[[Hilda Snow Elderblood]] was the third participant.
Deprived of [[Magic]], Demon Magic, Runes and effective Energy manipulation, Merlin attempted to use an emergency weapon: a concealed dagger forged from Demonic [[Iron]], a material capable of killing ordinary Gods. Hilda removed and destroyed it effortlessly, illustrating how little conventional anti-God equipment mattered against the level reached by the Dragon Gods of Victor's family.[3]
The women were not hunting Merlin because he had personally committed some major offense against Victor. His fate was instead connected to the extraordinary value [[Scathach]] assigned to his talent. Scathach considered permanently wasting a mind such as Merlin's unnecessary when his potential could instead be recycled into something useful.[3]
Violet therefore intended to preserve the important foundation of Merlin's Soul while ending Merlin Ambrosius as an individual.
Merlin was killed by Violet, Nyx and Hilda after the hunters completely dismantled the supernatural systems through which he normally fought.[3]
Yama appeared after his teacher's defeat and admitted that he had provided the information enabling the hunt. Although some attachment to Merlin remained, Yama valued his own future more highly and used the betrayal as part of his effort to gain admission into the Elderblood Empire.[3]
Merlin's Soul was later delivered to [[The Universal Tree]]. By this point, millennia of Demonic existence had altered and damaged its deeper structure enough that simply reincarnating Merlin unchanged was not considered desirable.[4]
The Universal Tree reconstructed the fundamental Core of the Soul itself. The alterations were extensive enough that even Victor could not interpret the entire process normally, and the resulting existence could no longer meaningfully be described as Merlin resurrected in another body.[4]
Victor consequently treated the resulting girl as a completely new person rather than imposing Merlin's memories or identity upon her.
He named her [[Melida Charlotte Elderblood]] and accepted her as his daughter.[4]
Charlotte retained an extraordinary natural aptitude for understanding Energy derived from the potential preserved through Merlin's Soul foundation, but her consciousness, identity, race, body and existence were her own.[4]
Her instinctive understanding later contributed to one of Victor's greatest breakthroughs in Energy theory. Through observing Charlotte, Victor recognized that Fire, Time, Space, [[Divine Energy]] and numerous other specialized Energies could be understood as different characteristics imposed upon a more fundamental substrate. This realization led toward his understanding of Attributeless Energy and [[Creator's Energy Mastery]].[11]
Merlin therefore did not personally acquire Victor's later Attributeless Energy. His legacy was instead the exceptional Energy-comprehension potential preserved in Charlotte, which helped another generation push Merlin's old principle—Energy is Energy—far beyond what he had achieved during his own life.[11]

### Relationships

#### Arthur Pendragon
Arthur was Merlin's student, King and one of the most important people from his Human life. Merlin served as Arthur's guardian and adviser while attempting to prepare him for the responsibilities of kingship.[1]
Merlin remained capable of acknowledging his own failures regarding Arthur. He credited [[Scathach]] with forcing Arthur to mature in ways his own instruction had been unable to accomplish, demonstrating genuine respect for both Arthur's development and Scathach's role within it.[1]

#### Scathach Elderblood Scarlett
Merlin and [[Scathach]] had known one another since the age of Arthur. Scathach trained Arthur directly and occasionally trained Merlin as well.[1]
Their interactions were characterized by mutual irritation, insults and genuine respect. Scathach called Merlin an "old bastard", while Merlin considered her one of the people most worthy of his respect. More importantly, Scathach never underestimated him merely because his direct combat strength was lower than hers.[1]
She understood that attacking Merlin simply because he appeared vulnerable could itself be part of a trap and once stopped Agnes from acting rashly for precisely that reason.[1]
Centuries later, Scathach's assessment of Merlin's potential was one of the principal reasons [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family chose to preserve his Soul foundation rather than erase him completely.[3]

#### Yama
Merlin's relationship with Yama reversed the normal structure between monarch and subordinate. Merlin officially served Yama as a General, yet simultaneously acted as the younger King's teacher and repeatedly reprimanded him like an unruly student.[10]
Merlin trained Yama to conceal his true power and helped maintain the false image surrounding the King's strength. He also intervened when Yama's battle with Vlad threatened to expose too much of their hidden arsenal.[10]
Merlin nevertheless possessed little conventional loyalty toward Yama's political interests. His obsession with the Arcane portal led him to write an agreement so favorable to the Witches that Yama openly questioned whether his own teacher was attempting to sell him out.[7]
Yama ultimately became the one who betrayed Merlin. He disclosed Merlin's location to the Elderblood faction and used the resulting hunt as political capital when requesting entry into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Empire.[3]

#### Melida Charlotte Elderblood
[[Melida Charlotte Elderblood]] is not Merlin restored to life.
Her existence originated from the Soul foundation left after Merlin's death, but the Universal Tree reconstructed the Core and fundamental structure so extensively that the resulting being was recognized as a new individual.[4]
Charlotte inherited the extraordinary potential for Energy comprehension associated with Merlin, yet possesses her own consciousness, identity, family and future. She should therefore not be treated as merely a female form or continuation of Merlin.[4][11]

### Skills & Abilities
Merlin was among the greatest magical and Energy-manipulation geniuses produced by Earth's supernatural history. His principal advantage was not overwhelming raw power but his ability to understand the rules governing supernatural phenomena and then exploit those rules through technique, preparation and experimentation.

#### Human Abilities
- Natural [[Magic]] Affinity: Merlin was the only known Human male naturally capable of using the form of Magic normally restricted to Witches.[1]
- Witch-Style Magic: During his Human life, Merlin could utilize Magic Circles, spell construction and the external manipulation of magical Energy through the same broad system employed by Witches.[1]
- Independent Magical Access: Merlin did not require a Blessing, artificial ritual or racial modification to use Magic. His access existed naturally.[1]
- Magic Circle Creation: Merlin possessed exceptional mastery over Magic Circles and later retained enough understanding of their underlying principles to reproduce spell structures with Demonic Energy.[5][1]
- Runic Knowledge: Merlin possessed extensive knowledge of Runes and incorporated them into his supernatural arsenal. During his final hunt, Violet's Order specifically prevented his Runes from functioning alongside his other Energy-based techniques.[3]
- Soul Knowledge: Merlin understood Soul structure deeply enough to teach principles concerning Souls to [[Viviane]], herself a supernatural being naturally sensitive to such phenomena.[8]
- Teaching Mastery: Merlin trained major historical and supernatural figures, most notably Arthur Pendragon and Yama.[1][10]

#### Asura Physiology
- Asura Physiology: After abandoning his Human body, Merlin became a high-ranking Asura Demon possessing an enormous ten-meter supernatural body with four arms and four eyes.[5][6]
- Demonic Energy: Merlin's Asura body naturally produced Demonic Energy, which became one of the two primary foundations of Demon [[Magic]].[1]
- Miasma Manipulation: Merlin could manipulate Hell's Miasma independently and integrate it directly into his spells and battlefield formations.[6][1]
- Demonic Longevity: His transformation allowed Merlin to survive for centuries beyond the period of Camelot without suffering the ordinary lifespan limitations of his former Human existence.
- Demonic Resistance: Merlin possessed the supernatural durability and environmental tolerance expected from a high-ranking Asura, although his physiology remained vulnerable to sufficiently powerful [[Divine Energy]].[1]

#### Energy Manipulation
- Extraordinary Energy Control: Merlin's greatest ability was his understanding and control of Energy. [[Scathach]] described him as a monster in this field, while Evie regarded him as one of the greatest Energy-control geniuses known to history.[2][7]
- Energy Analysis: Merlin could study unfamiliar supernatural Energy, identify the rules governing it and determine how those rules differed from systems he already understood.[1]
- Energy-System Reconstruction: After losing access to his original magical Energy, Merlin successfully reconstructed spellcasting through Demonic Energy and Miasma instead of attempting to regain his previous system.[1]
- Cross-System Adaptation: Merlin could apply principles learned from one supernatural system to another after identifying the relevant differences in their underlying Energy rules.[1]
- Energy Disruption: Merlin could interfere with supernatural techniques by disrupting the Energy structure sustaining them.[7]
- [[Magic]] [[Radar Warning Receiver (RWR)|Interception]]: Evie specifically warned Witches against casually casting Magic around Merlin because he could interfere with or intercept spells after they were activated.[7]
- Magic Nullification: When Evie attempted to use Evaluation Magic upon Merlin and Yama, Merlin immediately detected and destroyed the spell before it could complete its analysis.[7]
- Energy Detection: Merlin could notice extremely subtle disturbances and inconsistencies in surrounding Energy. Even while Nyx concealed herself from him, he detected enough anomalies to suspect another participant was present.[3]
- Supernatural Analysis: Merlin could rapidly analyze unfamiliar abilities in active combat situations. Despite being deprived of his own powers, he correctly identified the conceptual nature of Violet's Order and theorized what categories of Authority would be necessary to oppose it.[3]

#### Demon Magic
- Demon [[Magic]]: A magical system personally created by Merlin after his transformation into a Demon. It reproduced many principles of conventional spellcraft using Hell's Miasma and Merlin's own Demonic Energy as its primary sources.[1]
- Demonic Magic Circles: Merlin could construct specialized Magic Circles compatible with Demonic Energy and Miasma rather than relying upon the original Energy he possessed as a Human.[6][1]
- Miasma-Infused Spellcasting: Miasma could be incorporated directly into Merlin's spells, formations and environmental effects.[6][1]
- Strategic-Scale Magic: Merlin could prepare magical networks composed of hundreds of thousands of interconnected Magic Circles covering enormous battlefield areas.[5][6]
- Dimensional Lockdown: The magical infrastructure within the Amazon Realm was dangerous enough that careless interference risked triggering effects capable of restricting or sealing the surrounding dimension.[5][6]
- Diabolic Zone: Merlin could establish an area saturated and controlled through his Demonic Magic, creating environmental conditions favorable to his supernatural techniques.[10]
- Portal Creation: Merlin could create dark magical portals for transportation and escape.[1]
- Contract Magic: Merlin possessed sufficient knowledge to construct supernatural contracts with complex political conditions.[7]
- Trap Creation: Merlin regularly incorporated hidden conditions and traps into his magical preparations. [[Scathach]] considered his apparent vulnerability sufficient reason for caution because attacking without understanding the surrounding spell structure could activate prepared countermeasures.[1]

#### Intelligence and Non-Combat Skills
- Genius Intelligence: Merlin was one of the greatest magical intellects known in the series. Creating an original system of [[Magic]] after changing species is his clearest demonstration of theoretical genius.[1]
- Supernatural Scholarship: Merlin accumulated extensive knowledge concerning Magic, Demons, Miasma, Souls, Gods, supernatural organizations, Energy systems, Runes and dimensional phenomena.
- Strategic Intelligence: Merlin routinely operated through layered plans and intentionally concealed his complete intentions, even from allies.[1]
- Deductive Reasoning: During his final hunt he correctly inferred numerous properties of Violet's Order, recognized that his enemies possessed power above Yama and conventional Gods and detected evidence of Nyx before seeing her.[3]
- Information Gathering: Merlin possessed detailed knowledge regarding organizations such as [[New Dawn]], including information concerning its mixed supernatural membership and surveillance capabilities.[1]
- Political Manipulation: Merlin could manipulate negotiations toward outcomes serving his personal interests even when those interests conflicted with the faction he officially represented.[7]
- Leadership: Merlin commanded large Demonic forces as one of Yama's Generals during the Demonic War.[5]
- Teaching: Merlin taught or advised Arthur, Yama and [[Viviane]] in different supernatural disciplines.[1][8]

### Equipment
- Anti-Divine Armor: Merlin possessed or was developing specialized armor intended to reduce the natural vulnerability of his Demonic body to powerful [[Divine Energy]].[1]
- Demonic [[Iron]] Dagger: Merlin carried a concealed dagger forged from Demonic Iron as an emergency weapon. The material was dangerous enough to kill ordinary Gods, but Hilda destroyed the weapon effortlessly during his final hunt.[3]

### Weaknesses
- [[Divine Energy]] Vulnerability: Merlin's Asura body was naturally disadvantaged against sufficiently concentrated Divine Energy. He attempted to compensate through specialized protective equipment.[1]
- Energy Dependency: Almost every major aspect of Merlin's fighting style depended upon his ability to perceive, access or manipulate Energy. Violet's Order specifically exploited this reliance and reduced his supernatural options almost completely.[3]
- Knowledge Obsession: Merlin's desire to acquire new knowledge could override loyalty and political self-interest. His conduct during the Arcane negotiations endangered Yama's interests simply because Merlin wanted access to an unexplored world.[7]
- Arrogance: Merlin eventually admitted that he had grown too confident in his Energy mastery and failed to prepare adequately for an opponent capable of simply denying him access to Energy altogether.[3]
- Preparation Reliance: Many of Merlin's most dangerous applications involved [[Magic]] Circles, traps, environmental setup or prior analysis. Preventing access to his prepared systems drastically reduced his options.[3]

### Legacy
Merlin's greatest legacy was not a specific spell but his approach toward Energy.
Long before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] developed [[Creator's Energy Mastery]], Merlin had already recognized that the categories supernatural society treated as fundamentally separate were often different expressions of Energy operating under different rules.[1][9]
His response to becoming a Demon embodied that philosophy. Instead of mourning the permanent loss of his original [[Magic]], he analyzed a new Energy source and built another magical system around it.
The potential responsible for this extraordinary comprehension survived the reconstruction of Merlin's Soul. [[Melida Charlotte Elderblood]] inherited an even more extreme natural capacity for understanding Energy, and observing her eventually helped Victor perceive the deeper substrate beneath specialized Energies and reach his understanding of Attributeless Energy.[4][11]
This does not make Charlotte Merlin. Merlin Ambrosius died. What survived was a reconstructed Soul foundation containing enough of his extraordinary potential to become one component in the creation of an entirely new Elderblood daughter.[4]

### Trivia
- Merlin was the only known Human male naturally capable of using the form of [[Magic]] ordinarily associated with Witches.[1]
- Contrary to earlier interpretations of the character, [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]] did not grant Merlin his ability to use Magic. Merlin's Human magical affinity existed naturally; [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was the individual whose access required outside intervention.[1]
- [[Scathach]] personally knew Merlin during the age of Arthur and occasionally trained him.[1]
- Merlin openly admitted that Scathach succeeded in aspects of Arthur's development where he had failed.[1]
- His transformation into a Demon radically changed his body from an elderly Human into a roughly ten-meter-tall, four-armed, four-eyed Asura.[5][6]
- Scathach suspected Merlin deliberately arranged his own transformation into a Demon.[1]
- Becoming a Demon caused Merlin to lose access to his original Human Magic rather than simply granting Demonic powers in addition to everything he already possessed.[1]
- He personally created and named Demon Magic to compensate for that loss.[1]
- Merlin's central Energy philosophy can be summarized as "Energy is Energy."[1]
- He retained his hatred of sexual violence after becoming a Demon and deliberately became involved in punishing offenders guilty of such acts within Yama's [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]].[6]
- Merlin taught [[Viviane]] principles concerning Souls.[8]
- He served simultaneously as Yama's subordinate and teacher.
- Merlin was one of the principal people responsible for hiding Yama's real strength from [[Diablo]].[10]
- Evie considered Merlin's control sufficiently dangerous that she warned Witches against casually using Magic in his presence.[7]
- Merlin immediately destroyed Evie's Evaluation Magic when she attempted to use it upon him.[7]
- His obsession with the unknown world discovered by Arcane caused him to write an agreement absurdly favorable to the Witches despite officially representing Yama.[7]
- Yama eventually betrayed Merlin by revealing his location to the Elderblood faction.[3]
- During his final hunt, Merlin continued analyzing Violet's Order even after being completely denied access to his own Energy.[3]
- Merlin carried a Demonic [[Iron]] dagger capable of threatening ordinary Gods, but Hilda destroyed it without difficulty.[3]
- Scathach's assessment of Merlin's talent was one of the reasons the Elderblood family chose to preserve his Soul foundation rather than erase it.[3]
- [[Melida Charlotte Elderblood]] is not Merlin with a new body. [[The Universal Tree]] reconstructed the Soul Core so extensively that Victor recognized Charlotte as an entirely new person.[4]
- Charlotte nevertheless inherited the extraordinary Energy-comprehension potential associated with Merlin's Soul foundation.[4][11]
- Merlin himself never acquired Victor's later Attributeless Energy or [[Creator's Energy Mastery]]; Charlotte's existence merely helped Victor reach the breakthrough that produced those abilities.[11]
