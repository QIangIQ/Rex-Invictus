### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 59
Judy is a Beta Werewolf, former Human and one of the long-standing lovers and pack members of [[Johnny Lykos]]. Before meeting Johnny, Judy worked as a stripper dancer in order to pay for medical treatment for her seriously ill father. Johnny eventually helped her escape that life, began a relationship with her and brought her into his personal pack.[1]
By Chapter 187, Judy is explicitly subject to Johnny's authority as her Alpha, confirming her position as a Werewolf within his pack.[2] Although she has received relatively little individual focus, Judy is important to Johnny's personal life and was one of the people he refused to abandon when Anderson pressured him to fully return to [[Adam William Lykos]]' pack.[2]

### Physical Appearance
Judy is a tall young woman with long blonde hair and blue eyes.[1]
Her original article listed her height as approximately 180 cm, although the reviewed Chapter 59 and Chapter 187 material does not independently restate an exact measurement.
No detailed description of her current clothing, transformed Werewolf appearance or other distinguishing physical traits has been provided.

### Personality
Judy is strong-willed, outspoken and considerably more assertive than her former circumstances might suggest. Although Johnny can intimidate her when his Alpha instincts become serious, she is normally comfortable arguing with him, insulting him and openly refusing to behave as a submissive partner.[1]
She is particularly sensitive about her previous occupation. Judy did not become a stripper because she enjoyed the work; she entered the profession because her father's illness required money she had no other practical means of obtaining. She remembers that period with disgust and prefers not to dwell upon the experiences she endured while working there.[1]
After entering a relationship with Johnny, Judy stopped working as a stripper. When a customer at [[The Lost Club]] mistakenly assumed she was offering sexual services, Judy immediately rejected him and became angry when Johnny initially suspected she had returned to her former work.[1]
Despite Johnny's possessive behavior, Judy clearly cares for him. Their relationship involves frequent arguing and teasing, but she continues living alongside him and accepts him as her Alpha after becoming part of his Werewolf pack.[1][2]
Judy also possesses a rebellious streak. Even when Johnny gives her instructions for her own safety, her first instinct can be to protest. In Chapter 187, she attempted to argue after Johnny ordered her to remain behind him when he sensed danger. It was only after Johnny directly invoked his Alpha authority that her Werewolf instincts compelled her to obey.[2]

### Synopsis

#### Vampire Society
Judy first appeared at [[The Lost Club]] while [[Johnny Lykos]] was speaking with Eddy about [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]]' growing relationship with the newly transformed [[Victor Elderblood]]. During their conversation, Johnny heard Judy scream and immediately turned toward the disturbance.[1]
A large man had grabbed Judy and demanded sexual services from her. Judy angrily explained that the establishment was not a prostitution club, but the man continued attempting to force the issue. Johnny immediately intervened by throwing a chair at him and began brutally beating him for touching Judy.[1]
Other customers treated the confrontation almost as entertainment because Johnny's violent protectiveness toward Judy was apparently already familiar to them. When someone casually called Judy Johnny's wife, she loudly denied the title despite being openly involved with him.[1]
Jinsei eventually used her abilities to calm Johnny before he killed the man. Once the confrontation ended, Johnny questioned Judy because he believed she might have resumed her old work. Judy angrily denied it and explained that she had stopped working after becoming involved with him; the man had simply seen her coming downstairs and assumed she was available for sexual services.[1]
Their conversation revealed Judy's past. She had previously worked as a stripper because her father's serious illness required expensive treatment. Judy hated the work and the exploitative environment surrounding it but believed she had little alternative because she needed money for her family.[1]
Johnny eventually intervened in her life and helped her leave that environment. He also bit Judy's sick father, restoring the man's health and bringing him into Johnny's pack. Johnny promised Judy that despite her father technically becoming one of his pack members, he would not force the man to perform duties simply because of their supernatural hierarchy.[1]
Judy sincerely thanked Johnny and admitted that without his intervention she would probably still be trapped in her former life. Johnny dismissed the gratitude, declaring that once he had become interested in her he had already decided that she would become his woman.[1]
Judy subsequently accompanied Johnny, Jinsei and [[Jessica Horseman Snow]] deeper into The Lost Club during the events surrounding Johnny's mercenary work and family.

#### A New Pillar
By Chapter 187, Judy had become a Beta Werewolf and belonged directly to Johnny's personal pack.[2]
She accompanied Johnny while the two walked near [[Adam William Lykos]]' residence. Johnny suddenly noticed that the normally populated streets had become unnaturally silent. Judy initially failed to understand what had changed, but after looking around she realized that everyone who had been present only moments earlier had disappeared.[2]
Johnny immediately recognized the situation as dangerous and ordered Judy to stay behind him. Judy attempted to object, but Johnny looked directly at her with glowing sapphire-blue eyes and commanded:
"No argument."[2]
Judy's eyes briefly glowed gold and her Werewolf instincts responded automatically to the authority of her Alpha. Despite being Johnny's lover, the supernatural hierarchy between Alpha and Beta forced her to obey, and she moved behind him.[2]
The source of the disturbance was soon revealed to be Anderson, accompanied by Liza, Julian, Juan and Yuran. Anderson confronted Johnny over his increasingly independent status and questioned his refusal to properly return to Adam's pack.[2]
Johnny avoided provoking a fight because Judy was present and he understood that attempting to confront Anderson and four additional [[Werewolves]] while protecting her would be reckless. His behavior during the encounter demonstrated the importance Judy held for him: Johnny's normally aggressive Alpha pride was secondary to ensuring her safety.[2]
Anderson eventually warned Johnny that fully separating from Adam while establishing himself as an independent Alpha could cause the Werewolf hierarchy to regard him as a rival. He encouraged Johnny to return completely to Adam's pack.[2]
Johnny refused.
One of the principal reasons was that returning under Adam's complete authority would require Johnny to abandon the women and pack he had built for himself, including Judy. He was unwilling to sacrifice the people he considered his own simply to make his position within the traditional Werewolf hierarchy easier.[2]
Judy has not received another substantial individual storyline in the currently documented material. Johnny remains associated with his established household in later volumes, but the biological mothers of his later-confirmed children have not been individually identified, so none should currently be assigned to Judy without further confirmation.

### Skills & Abilities
Judy has not participated in a dedicated combat scene and has demonstrated very few individual supernatural abilities. Her known powers primarily derive from her status as a Beta Werewolf.

#### Beta Werewolf Physiology
- Beta Werewolf Physiology: By Chapter 187, Judy is a Beta Werewolf belonging to Johnny's personal pack.[2]
- Superhuman Strength: As a Werewolf, Judy possesses strength exceeding that of an ordinary Human.
- Superhuman Speed: Her Werewolf physiology provides enhanced movement speed and reflexes.
- Superhuman Durability: Judy possesses greater physical resilience than an ordinary Human.
- Enhanced Stamina: [[Werewolves]] possess significantly enhanced endurance.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: As a Werewolf, Judy possesses accelerated supernatural healing.
- Enhanced Senses: Her Race grants superior smell, hearing and predatory awareness.
- Longevity: Werewolf physiology provides a substantially extended lifespan compared with ordinary Humans.
- Werewolf Transformation: Judy possesses the racial capacity to manifest Werewolf characteristics, although her individual transformed form has not been shown.
- Natural Weapons: Werewolf transformations provide enhanced claws and fangs.

#### Pack Bond
- Beta Pack Bond: Judy belongs to [[Johnny Lykos]]' personal pack and recognizes him instinctively as her Alpha.[2]
- Alpha Command Response: A sufficiently serious command from Johnny can trigger Judy's Werewolf instincts and compel her to obey the established Alpha–Beta hierarchy.[2]
- Golden-Eyed Response: Judy's eyes briefly glow gold when her instincts respond to Johnny's Alpha command.[2]
This Alpha response should not be interpreted as conventional Mind Manipulation performed by Judy. It is a manifestation of Werewolf pack hierarchy acting upon her as a Beta.

#### Unconfirmed Abilities
Judy has not been confirmed to possess:
- Alpha Werewolf physiology.
- True Werewolf physiology.
- Complete Werewolf Transformation.
- An individualized Element.
- Nature Energy techniques.
- Godhood or Divinity.
- A named combat technique.
- The Elizabeth Trait.
- Membership in the biological [[Lykos Clan]].
The exact circumstances under which Judy changed from a Human into a Werewolf have also not been directly shown in the reviewed chapters.

### Relationships

#### Johnny Lykos
[[Johnny Lykos]] is Judy's lover and Alpha.
Johnny encountered Judy while she was still struggling with the circumstances surrounding her father's illness and her former occupation. After becoming attracted to her, he helped remove her from that life and eventually incorporated her into his personal household and pack.[1]
Johnny is intensely possessive and protective of Judy. When another man grabbed her and demanded sexual services, Johnny nearly beat him to death and later made it clear that the man would not be permitted to return.[1]
His feelings toward Judy also influence his decisions as an Alpha. During his confrontation with Anderson, Johnny avoided unnecessary escalation because Judy was beside him and later refused to fully return to Adam's pack because doing so would mean abandoning the women who had become his own family.[2]
Their relationship is not entirely equal within Werewolf hierarchy. Judy is a Beta while Johnny is an Alpha, meaning direct Alpha commands can trigger a compulsory instinctive response from her.[2] Outside such serious situations, however, Judy freely argues with Johnny and frequently refuses to indulge his possessive attitude.

#### Unnamed Father
Judy's unnamed father suffered from a serious illness that required expensive medical treatment. His condition was the primary reason Judy entered stripping in the first place.[1]
Johnny eventually bit him, and his health recovered afterward. He became connected to Johnny's pack, although Johnny promised Judy that he would not force her father to perform duties merely because he was now subordinate to him.[1]

#### Jinsei
Jinsei was one of the women closely associated with Johnny during Judy's introduction.
Jinsei appears comfortable with Judy and helped defuse Johnny's violent outburst after he attacked the man who grabbed her.[1] The two women were also part of the same broader household surrounding Johnny.

#### Jessica Horseman Snow
[[Jessica Horseman Snow]] was another woman associated with Johnny during Judy's early appearances. Jessica immediately checked whether Judy was unharmed after the confrontation at [[The Lost Club]], suggesting a friendly relationship between the two women.[1]
