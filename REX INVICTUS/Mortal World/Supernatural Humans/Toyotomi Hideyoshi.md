### Physical Appearance
He is historically known as a powerful and ambitious military leader who took over after Nobunaga’s death.

### Personality
Toyotomi is depicted as an ambitious and opportunistic leader, eager to seize control following Nobunaga’s fall. Unlike [[Kurama]], who thrives on endless war, Toyotomi seeks to consolidate power and rule Japan in the wake of his master’s demise.

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes and War
Toyotomi Hideyoshi played a role in the betrayal of [[Oda Nobunaga]], siding with [[Akechi Mitsuhide]] and [[Kurama]] during the Honnō-ji Incident. With Nobunaga gone, he assumed power, aiming to carry on his master’s work under his own rule. However, Kurama, ever the manipulator, accused him of treason, igniting a war against him and ensuring that bloodshed would continue in Japan.[1]
