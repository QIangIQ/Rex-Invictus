### Appearances

#### Debut
[[Ch04 - Rex Natus II|Chapter 4]]
Lucy is a former Plebeian Vampire, criminal blood dealer and human trafficker who later became one of the first [[Heralds of Chaos]] directly subordinate to War and [[Victor Elderblood]]. Long before Victor became a major supernatural power, Lucy operated a network that kidnapped Humans and supplied them as "food" to Noble Vampires who desired blood outside their usual partners. His connections with Noble families made him useful within the Vampire underworld, but his activities also attracted the attention of the Inquisition.[1]
Lucy deeply resented the inferior social and biological position of Plebeian Vampires. Together with his assistant and longtime partner Karen, he spent considerable resources pursuing an artificial ritual intended to transform them into Noble Vampires. The ritual required stolen Church materials, Witchcraft, the blood of innocent Humans and Noble Vampire blood, but its true final requirement was something nearly impossible for ordinary Vampires to obtain: the blood of a Vampire Progenitor.[2][3]
Centuries later, after Victor had become the Dragon God of [[Chaos]], Lucy and Karen were brought before War during Victor's settlement of old enemies and acquaintances. Unlike several others gathered there, War judged Lucy and Karen primarily by their overwhelming desire for advancement rather than by some unforgivable offense against Victor. Both immediately accepted War's offer of greater power in exchange for permanent servitude and were recreated through the same Essence of Chaos used in War's existence, becoming Heralds of Chaos and direct executors of Victor's will.[4][5]

### Physical Appearance

#### Plebeian Vampire
Lucy was a tall man standing approximately 190 cm with short black hair slicked backward. He habitually wore glasses and, during his first appearance, dressed in an immaculate white suit. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] thought his overall appearance resembled that of a successful lawyer rather than a criminal responsible for kidnapping and selling Humans.[6]
His respectable appearance strongly contrasted with his actual occupation. Lucy deliberately cultivated a polite and controlled demeanor when dealing with more powerful Vampires, allowing him to function as an intermediary between the Human World and Noble Vampire clients.[6][1]

#### Herald of Chaos
Lucy's appearance changed completely after his transformation by War. The same process used to transform Jimmy and Thomas was repeated with Lucy and Karen, replacing their previous supernatural existence with that of [[Heralds of Chaos]].[5]
As a Herald, Lucy's face became engulfed in pure darkness, obscuring his ordinary facial features. His previous clothing was replaced by supernatural armor resembling War's own, although the armor of the subordinate Heralds was less bulky than their Progenitor's. A pair of wings composed entirely of pure darkness also manifested behind him.[5]

### Personality
Lucy is calculating, opportunistic, ambitious and extremely concerned with his own survival. During their first interaction, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] quickly identified him as a "scared fox" who constantly searched for ways to profit from whatever situation surrounded him. Lucy openly admitted that he was frightened of dying and wanted to live for thousands of additional years, making self-preservation one of his clearest motivations.[6][1]
He normally presents himself through a calm, polite and professional façade. He speaks formally to powerful individuals, adjusts his behavior according to the balance of power and can suppress his anger quickly when he realizes that displaying it would endanger him. When the killing intent of Violet's servants surrounded him, Lucy immediately abandoned his previous aggression and returned to an apologetic, diplomatic demeanor.[1]
However, this calmness is partly a social mask. Victor's mockery during their first meeting caused Lucy's polite expression to collapse, revealing a considerably more aggressive and sadistic temperament underneath. He openly threatened Victor's life before remembering that he was standing inside Violet's mansion surrounded by her servants.[1]
Lucy is also highly ambitious and deeply dissatisfied with weakness. His greatest early objective was to escape his status as a Plebeian Vampire and become a Noble Vampire. He was willing to spend almost his entire fortune, hire a corrupt Witch, steal from the Church and sacrifice innocent Humans if doing so provided even the possibility of altering his race.[2][7]
His desire for advancement is more important to him than ideology. War later directly characterized Lucy and Karen as beings primarily driven by desire. When offered an opportunity to obtain much greater power in exchange for serving Victor, neither required persuasion; they immediately accepted because they still wanted the same fundamental thing that had motivated them centuries earlier—to become more than what they currently were.[5]
Despite constantly arguing with Karen, Lucy appears genuinely accustomed to her presence and trusts her as his closest associate. Their conversations frequently resemble those of an old pair who have spent so long together that irritation, insults and cooperation have become inseparable. Lucy deliberately continues making jokes that annoy her simply because he finds her reactions entertaining.[3][4]
By Chapter 933, Lucy retained the controlled monotone demeanor he displayed during his early life. Even while imprisoned somewhere he could not escape and waiting for an unknown cosmic entity to arrive, he remained comparatively composed, introduced the other prisoners to [[Kurama]] and calmly observed the latter's unsuccessful attempts to escape.[4]

### Synopsis

#### The Awakening
Lucy first appeared shortly after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s transformation when he visited [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] accompanied by Karen. He had learned that the Inquisition was operating in the area and requested permission to move a large number of Vampires into Violet's territory for protection.[6]
Violet immediately objected. Her territory did not normally permit unrelated Vampires to settle there, and she interpreted Lucy's request as a potential challenge to Clan Snow's authority. Lucy quickly clarified that he had no intention of provoking a Count Clan and openly admitted that his request was motivated by fear of being killed by the Inquisition.[6]
His meeting also became Victor's first introduction to Lucy. Lucy and Karen attempted to provoke the newborn by commenting on Violet marrying a Plebeian Vampire, apparently expecting Victor's newly amplified emotions to make him lose control. Instead, Victor mocked Lucy extensively until the older Vampire's calm façade broke and Lucy threatened to kill him.[1]
The killing intent of the Vampires hidden throughout Violet's mansion immediately reminded Lucy of his position. He apologized and returned to diplomacy.[1]
Victor unexpectedly overturned Violet's refusal and granted Lucy permission to bring his Vampires into the city. Lucy stated that he intended to bring approximately fifty Vampires, identifying all of them as his slaves. Victor restricted them from entering the area surrounding Violet's mansion and his own family's home.[1]
Lucy attempted to assert himself one final time by increasing the strength of his handshake with Victor, apparently intending to crush the newborn's hand. Victor effortlessly reversed the attempt and shattered Lucy's hand instead. Lucy nevertheless withdrew without creating another confrontation.[1]
Victor had never actually trusted him. He deliberately allowed Lucy into the territory because he intended to use the older Vampire as bait to draw the Inquisition closer and observe the capabilities of Human Vampire [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] firsthand.[1]
After Lucy left, Violet explained his real business. Lucy specialized in supplying Humans to Noble Vampires who had become bored with drinking only the blood of their spouses. He kidnapped Humans and delivered them to Noble clients as new "flavors", giving him extensive ties to Vampire nobility while simultaneously making him a target of the Church.[1]
The strategy worked. When Victor later captured the novice Hunters Jimmy and Thomas, Jimmy revealed that their group had entered the area specifically to hunt Lucy. The operation included much more dangerous Hunters such as [[Carlos Reiss]] and [[Mizuki Elderblood]], demonstrating that the trouble Lucy attracted was considerably more serious than ordinary Human law enforcement.[8]

#### Vampire Society
Lucy's ambitions eventually expanded beyond wealth and survival. He and Karen began developing a method of escaping the biological limitations of Plebeian Vampires and transforming themselves into Noble Vampires.[9]
Their operation became sufficiently important that they maintained a concealed underground base and cooperated with outside supernatural factions. When information concerning their plans was stolen by an unknown infiltrator, Lucy correctly narrowed the likely culprit to a powerful Witch by analyzing the manner in which the intruder had bypassed the base's defenses. He nevertheless remained confident that only the basic portions of the plan had been compromised.[9]
Lucy also learned that the [[Horseman Clan]] was sending additional personnel. Rather than regarding their involvement entirely as a threat, he saw the deteriorating political position of the Horseman family as an opportunity because the Noble Clan was becoming increasingly desperate for useful allies.[9]
By Chapter 53, Lucy and Karen had completed most of the physical framework for their race-changing ritual. Their underground base contained a white coffin marked with a black cross and surrounded by magical symbols written in blood. Lucy had spent almost his entire fortune hiring a corrupt Witch to construct the ritual.[2]
The ritual they believed would transform them required the coffin to be filled with the blood of innocent Humans and Noble Vampires. Lucy had also obtained a sacred artifact stolen from the Church as part of the process, although after determining that neither he nor Karen could use the weapon and that selling it to Witches risked exposing them to the Inquisition, Lucy simply threw it away. Karen was openly stunned by how casually he discarded such a valuable supernatural object.[2]
Information concerning Lucy's project spread through supernatural mercenary circles. [[Johnny Lykos]] learned that Lucy was a common Plebeian Vampire with enormous ambitions who was abducting innocent men and women to fuel a ritual intended to make him a Noble Vampire. He had also begun working with a relatively new Noble Vampire house.[7]
Lucy later hired Johnny for an important task despite Karen's distrust of mercenaries. Lucy insisted that Johnny was reliable because he had worked with him several times before, once again demonstrating the extensive criminal and mercenary connections Lucy had developed over his long life.[3]
Additional Noble Vampires connected to Clan Horseman became involved in the situation. The Horseman brothers were interested in Lucy's ritual for their own reasons, while another element of their activity involved obtaining Alpha Werewolf blood for a separate Hybrid-related objective.[10]
Karen eventually grew tired of waiting and decided to accelerate the ritual using Noble Vampire blood she had already acquired. Lucy immediately became worried that she would proceed without him and asked what he was supposed to do, only for Karen to tell him to find another vial for himself.[3]
Karen entered the ritual first. When she emerged from the coffin, she initially believed nothing had happened. Although she had become physically stronger, she had not been transformed into a Noble Vampire. [[Einer Horseman]] explained that the ritual itself was not fundamentally incorrect; it was missing its true central ingredient.[3]
According to Einer, changing a Plebeian Vampire into a Noble Vampire required the blood of a Vampire Progenitor. He produced a vial containing the blood of [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] himself—the King of Vampires and First Progenitor. Merely seeing the blood caused both Lucy and Karen to experience overwhelming thirst mixed with instinctive fear. Both immediately reached for it when offered the possibility of completing their transformation.[3]
Einer then closed the case and refused to simply hand the blood over, revealing just how dependent Lucy and Karen had become upon the Noble Vampires manipulating the situation. Their attempt to transcend Plebeian status therefore remained incomplete, and Lucy continued existing as a Plebeian Vampire rather than becoming the Noble Vampire he desperately wanted to be.[3]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Lucy resurfaced much later after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had already become the Dragon God of [[Chaos]]. He and Karen were among several people mysteriously gathered in an apparently inescapable room alongside [[Luan Davis]], Cornelius Funar and eventually [[Kurama]].[4]
Lucy assumed the role of informal spokesman among the prisoners. When Kurama awakened, Lucy calmly questioned his identity and explained that anyone brought to the room was probably connected to Victor by having harmed him or someone close to him. After Kurama refused to cooperate, Lucy introduced himself, Karen, Luan and Cornelius before asking Kurama what "sin" he had committed against Victor.[4]
Kurama attempted to escape but discovered that neither the door nor the windows could be damaged. Lucy simply wished him luck and watched with amusement alongside the others as the Youkai repeatedly failed. Unlike Kurama, Lucy had apparently already accepted that whatever force had transported them there was far beyond anything they could presently oppose.[4]
His interactions with Karen remained almost unchanged despite the enormous passage of time. When Karen incorrectly pluralized Kurama's species, Lucy immediately fake-coughed and corrected her terminology. Karen told him to stop, while Lucy deliberately repeated an old joke about her temper simply because he still found irritating her entertaining.[4]
The entire room eventually fell silent when Lucy sensed something approaching. He quietly announced that "he" had arrived before War emerged from the darkness. Lucy and Karen did not actually know what kind of Being War had become, but both instinctively recognized that they were dealing with something vastly beyond ordinary supernatural existence.[4]
War initially summoned Jimmy and Thomas and rewarded the former Human Exorcists for their years of service by transforming them into [[Heralds of Chaos]]. After dealing with them, War turned toward Lucy and Karen.[4][5]
Unlike individuals such as Kurama or Cornelius, Lucy and Karen were not immediately condemned. War stated that their past actions had primarily been motivated by desire, and that desire itself was not a Sin because it was one of the forces that drove living Beings forward. He therefore extended the same offer given to Jimmy and Thomas.[5]
War offered them Power in exchange for servitude.
Lucy and Karen accepted immediately.
Neither required a speech about loyalty or justice. Their motivation was exactly the same desire that had defined their lives as Plebeian Vampires: both wanted to become stronger and become more than they already were. They grabbed War's hand and underwent the same fundamental transformation as Jimmy and Thomas.[5]
Lucy consequently became one of the four original subordinate Heralds of Chaos. His face transformed into pure darkness, his body was clothed in armor resembling War's and wings of darkness manifested behind him.[5]
The four Heralds were transported into the extraordinary region connected to Victor where countless galaxies surrounded them. They automatically knelt before the incomprehensible Cosmic existence of the Dragon God of Chaos, whose presence distorted Reality, consumed surrounding existence and simultaneously generated abnormal new galaxies.[5]
[[Roxanne Elderblood]] later explained that Lucy and the other Heralds had been created using the same Essence of Chaos that created War. They were therefore genuinely connected to Victor's existence, although their connection was considerably less intimate than the bonds possessed by Roxanne and [[Amara Elderblood]].[5]
The danger of Victor's unrestricted presence became immediately apparent when his power affected hundreds of stars and would have harmed the four Heralds had Roxanne and Amara not intervened with a protective barrier.[5]
After the event ended, Lucy, Karen, Jimmy and Thomas simultaneously received Victor's will. The four acknowledged the command together and disappeared to hunt additional enemies who remained unresolved from Victor's past, beginning Lucy's new existence as a direct supernatural executor of the Chaos Dragon God's authority.[5]

### Skills & Abilities
Lucy's abilities changed completely following his transformation. As a Plebeian Vampire, his supernatural attributes were relatively modest compared to powerful Noble Vampires. As a Herald of [[Chaos]], his existence was reconstructed through War's Chaos Essence and connected directly to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].

#### Herald of Chaos
- Herald of [[Chaos]] Physiology: Lucy is one of the first four subordinate [[Heralds of Chaos]] created beneath War. His former Plebeian Vampire existence was fundamentally transformed through the same process applied to Jimmy, Thomas and Karen.[5]
- Chaos Essence: Roxanne explicitly confirmed that the four Heralds were created using the same Essence of Chaos that created War. This establishes a direct supernatural connection between Lucy, War and [[Victor Elderblood]].[5]
- Supernatural Empowerment: Becoming a Herald granted Lucy a completely superior form of existence compared to his former Plebeian Vampire body. The transformation was explicitly offered as a means of obtaining greater Power.[5]
- Darkness Manifestation: Lucy's transformed body manifests supernatural darkness, most visibly through his featureless dark face and wings.[5]
- Wing Manifestation: Lucy possesses wings formed entirely from pure darkness following his transformation.[5]
- Flight: The Heralds can freely operate in open Space and are shown kneeling and moving within a cosmic environment without conventional support.[5]
- Space Survival: Lucy can remain functional in the vacuum of Space without breathing equipment, pressure protection or any ordinary biological support system.[5]
- Connection to Victor: The Chaos Essence used to create Lucy establishes a direct connection to Victor, allowing Lucy to exist within spaces tied closely to Victor's Soul and receive his will.[5]
- Reception of Victor's [[Will Solace|Will]]: Lucy and the other Heralds simultaneously understood Victor's command without conventional verbal communication and immediately departed to execute it.[5]
- Dimensional/Cosmic Translocation: Lucy can participate in the same disappearance and reappearance demonstrated by the other Heralds, including movement into Victor's extraordinary inner cosmic space. Whether this is an independent ability or movement facilitated by War/Victor remains unspecified.[5]
- Altered Existence: The transformation into a Herald is not merely cosmetic. The original forms and supernatural constitutions of the transformed individuals were fundamentally altered through Chaos Essence.[5]

#### Former Abilities
- Plebeian Vampire Physiology: Before becoming a Herald, Lucy was an older Plebeian Vampire. He possessed the standard supernatural constitution of a transformed Vampire but remained biologically and socially inferior to Noble Vampires, a fact he deeply resented.[1][3]
- Enhanced Strength: Lucy possessed strength considerably above an ordinary Human and attempted to crush [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s hand during their first meeting. However, even the newly transformed Victor overwhelmingly surpassed him and shattered Lucy's hand with little effort.[1]
- Enhanced Senses: Lucy possessed heightened Vampiric senses. Karen shouting beside him was loud enough to temporarily overwhelm his hearing.[3]
- Longevity: Lucy was already considered an older Vampire during Victor's early life and explicitly intended to continue living for several thousand additional years.[6][1]
- Bloodlust: Lucy retained the intense attraction to powerful blood characteristic of Vampires. Merely being shown a small vial of Vlad's Progenitor blood caused his throat to become dry and generated an overwhelming desire to consume it despite simultaneously sensing danger from it.[3]
- Vampire Creation / Slave Siring: Lucy possessed approximately fifty Vampire slaves whom he identified as his own subordinate Vampires when requesting entry into Violet's territory.[1]
- Criminal Networking: Lucy maintained extensive connections with Vampire nobility, mercenaries and criminal elements. His business supplying Humans to Noble Vampires gave him access to clients and information unavailable to ordinary Plebeians.[1][3]
- Human Trafficking Network: Lucy specialized in abducting Humans and transporting them to Noble Vampires seeking alternative sources of blood.[1]
- Leadership: Lucy commanded numerous subordinate Vampires and maintained a concealed underground base staffed by Vampire servants.[1][3]
- Strategic Analysis: Lucy could analyze supernatural intrusions and identify likely culprits from the methods used. After an infiltrator bypassed the protections surrounding his operation, he correctly reasoned that a powerful Witch was the most plausible explanation.[9]
- Ritual Knowledge: Through a corrupt Witch and his research with Karen, Lucy acquired knowledge concerning an artificial ritual capable of strengthening a Plebeian Vampire and potentially reconstructing one into a Noble Vampire if the required Progenitor blood was supplied.[2][3]

### Relationships

#### Karen
Karen is Lucy's oldest known companion, assistant and closest associate. The two operated their Human trafficking business and Noble Vampire transformation project together for years, sharing the same dissatisfaction with their Plebeian existence.[6][2]
Their relationship is highly argumentative. Karen regularly becomes furious with Lucy's decisions, while Lucy enjoys provoking her and deliberately repeats jokes he knows she hates. Despite this constant friction, they repeatedly act together and clearly trust one another more than almost anyone else in their old lives.[3][4]
Both ultimately made the same defining decision. When War offered Power in exchange for servitude, Lucy and Karen answered simultaneously and immediately accepted, becoming [[Heralds of Chaos]] together.[5]

#### Victor Elderblood
Lucy met [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] when the latter was only days into his life as a Vampire. He initially underestimated Victor as Violet's newly transformed Plebeian husband and attempted to provoke him, only to discover almost immediately that the newborn possessed physical power far beyond Lucy's own.[6][1]
Victor distrusted Lucy from their first conversation and deliberately used him as bait to attract the Inquisition. He considered Lucy a frightened opportunist who attempted to profit from every situation rather than a person worthy of genuine trust.[1]
Their relationship changed radically much later. By the time Victor became the Dragon God of [[Chaos]], Lucy no longer possessed any realistic ability to challenge him and, more importantly, had no reason to refuse an opportunity for advancement. He willingly accepted permanent service to Victor in exchange for the Power offered through War.[5]
Lucy now functions as one of Victor's direct Heralds and executors.

#### War
War is Lucy's Progenitor and immediate superior among the [[Heralds of Chaos]]. War evaluated Lucy's history and determined that his dominant motivation was desire rather than an offense deserving destruction.[5]
He offered Lucy and Karen the same fundamental opportunity given to Jimmy and Thomas—greater Power in exchange for absolute servitude. Lucy accepted instantly and was recreated through the [[Chaos]] Essence associated with War.[5]

#### Horseman Clan
Lucy cooperated with members of the [[Horseman Clan]] during his attempt to become a Noble Vampire. He viewed their declining political circumstances as an opportunity because a Noble Clan desperate for allies could potentially provide resources unavailable to an ordinary Plebeian.[9]
The relationship was never equal. The Horseman brothers understood details of Lucy's ritual that Lucy himself did not, including the necessity of Progenitor blood. Einer eventually demonstrated this advantage by revealing a vial of Vlad's blood and using Lucy and Karen's overwhelming desire for it to manipulate the situation.[3]
