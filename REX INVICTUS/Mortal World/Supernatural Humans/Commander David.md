### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 161
David is a minor Human character in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]] and a Commander of a SWAT unit operating in the United States. He became unintentionally involved in the supernatural world when his unit was ordered to deploy to the scene surrounding [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]]'s former mansion following the intervention of General James.[1]
David and the surviving members of his unit were subsequently placed under Sasha's Vampiric influence. Sasha erased their memories concerning the supernatural events at the mansion and deliberately left David conditioned to follow additional instructions from her in the future.[1]
Through a cover story subsequently constructed by [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]], Natalia, Sasha and Violet, David was publicly portrayed as the heroic SWAT Commander responsible for forcing the supposed terrorist [[Father Julian]] to retreat. The fabricated narrative successfully redirected public attention away from [[Victor Elderblood]] and the supernatural forces actually responsible for the incident.[2]

### Physical Appearance
David's physical appearance is not described in meaningful detail within his identified novel appearances.
He is a Human male serving as the Commander of a SWAT unit and was therefore present at the mansion incident wearing the tactical equipment associated with his unit.[1]
The earlier description of David as a middle-aged Caucasian man with a military buzz cut, stocky physique and customized tactical vest is not directly supported by the identified chapters and should therefore not be treated as canon unless another source explicitly describes him in this manner.

### Personality
Relatively little of David's natural personality is shown because almost immediately after his introduction he is placed under Sasha's mental control.
Before being manipulated, David appears to be a professional law-enforcement officer accustomed to commanding armed personnel. He was leading the surviving SWAT agents operating around the mansion and continued coordinating his subordinates through radio communications.[1]
David also readily answered Sasha's questions once she took control of him, revealing that General James had ordered the operation and explaining the surveillance capabilities of the SWAT vehicles. These actions should not be interpreted as voluntary cooperation, however, because Sasha had already placed him under her Vampiric influence.[1]
Following the manipulation, David's behavior was no longer fully representative of his own personality. Sasha erased his memories of the supernatural incident and implanted the belief that his team had simply fallen asleep while on duty due to exhaustion. She additionally ordered him to wait for future instructions, effectively turning the unaware Commander into a potential Human asset for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction.[1]

### Synopsis

#### A New Pillar
David became involved in the conflict surrounding [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]]'s former mansion after General James ordered the rapid mobilization of SWAT forces toward the area.[1]
The timing of their arrival immediately appeared suspicious to Sasha. Normal police forces should not have mobilized so quickly toward an incident involving supernatural beings, particularly when the Inquisition already had agents operating in the vicinity. Sasha therefore suspected that someone possessing advance knowledge of the operation had deliberately directed the Human authorities toward the mansion.[1]
By the time Sasha encountered David's unit, [[Victor Elderblood]] had already slaughtered a large number of the armed Humans who attempted to confront him. Several SWAT agents nevertheless remained alive away from Victor's immediate battlefield.[3]
Sasha appeared before the surviving agents and immediately used her Vampiric influence upon the entire group. After commanding the ordinary agents to forget everything they had witnessed, she turned specifically toward their Commander and demanded his identity and the source of his deployment orders.[1]
David introduced himself and revealed that General James had ordered the operation. He subsequently explained everything he knew concerning their deployment.[1]
The information confirmed Sasha's suspicion that the Human response had not occurred naturally. James had deliberately caused the SWAT unit to enter an active supernatural conflict connected to the Church's operation against Victor's faction.[1]
Sasha then questioned David about whether the vehicles had recorded the events surrounding the mansion. David confirmed that several vehicles possessed forward-facing cameras recording the area.[1]
Realizing that the recordings could expose Victor and the supernatural world, Sasha generated golden lightning and destroyed the [[Semiconductor Devices|electronic systems]] of the SWAT vehicles, eliminating the footage before it could leave the scene.[1]
She additionally questioned David about other personnel communicating with the unit and obtained information concerning their locations and communication channels.[1]
Before departing, Sasha completely altered the surviving officers' recollection of the incident. She ordered David and his subordinates to forget everything that had occurred and to believe that they had simply fallen asleep while working because they were exhausted.[1]
Sasha placed one additional command upon David that she did not give the others: he was to wait for further orders from her. She deliberately preserved him as a controllable Human asset because she anticipated that Ruby would eventually find some practical use for a SWAT Commander under their influence.[1]
Once Sasha disappeared, David immediately resumed commanding his unit and ordered the surviving officers to return to their base, completely unaware of what had actually happened.[1]
In practical terms, Sasha's intervention also saved David's life. Had she not removed the surviving SWAT personnel from the area, they could easily have encountered Victor while he was still massacring the Humans and [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] surrounding the mansion.[1]
During the following days, [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]], Natalia, Sasha and Violet constructed an elaborate public explanation for the incident. Their objective was to conceal Victor's supernatural actions while simultaneously damaging the Church responsible for provoking him.[1][2]
The resulting narrative transformed the deceased [[Father Julian]] into an international terrorist supposedly responsible for the massacre. Fabricated witnesses claimed that Julian had fled toward the Vatican while proclaiming allegiance to an organization known as the Inquisition.[2]
David was assigned the opposite role within the cover story.
News reports described him as the "hero of SWAT", claiming that Commander David's efforts had successfully forced Father Julian to retreat. The story therefore transformed a Commander whose unit had actually been manipulated by Vampires into the Human hero responsible for resolving the entire incident.[2]
The fabricated narrative proved extremely effective because it provided ordinary Humans with a believable explanation involving terrorism and law enforcement rather than Vampires, Hunters and supernatural warfare. As Ruby observed through the success of the operation, people were considerably more willing to accept a story involving an American police hero and an international terrorist than the supernatural reality.[2]
The Church itself was unable to effectively counter the story. Alexander publicly denied the existence of the Inquisition, meaning that the Vatican could not openly explain Julian's actual role without exposing the supernatural organization it was attempting to keep hidden.[2]
David consequently became an unwilling public symbol of Ruby's cover-up despite having no knowledge of the supernatural events that actually produced his newfound reputation.[2]

### Skills & Abilities
David is an ordinary Human and has never demonstrated supernatural powers.
- SWAT Training: As a Commander of a SWAT unit, David possesses the professional law-enforcement training required to operate within and command a heavily armed tactical response team.[1]
- Leadership: David commands multiple SWAT agents and is capable of issuing orders to his unit during an active operation. After Sasha completed her manipulation, David immediately ordered the surviving officers to return to their base.[1]
- Tactical Operations Knowledge: David possessed detailed knowledge concerning his unit's deployment, communications and vehicle equipment, including which vehicles were recording the mansion through forward-facing cameras.[1]
- Firearms Training: As a SWAT Commander, David is trained to operate alongside armed tactical personnel, although he has not personally demonstrated any notable supernatural-level marksmanship or combat feats in the novel.

### Weaknesses
- Ordinary Human Physiology: David possesses none of the supernatural physical characteristics of Vampires, [[Werewolves]], [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] or other empowered beings. His survival during the mansion incident depended largely upon avoiding direct confrontation with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
- Vulnerability to Vampiric Mental Manipulation: David possessed no demonstrated resistance to the mental influence of a sufficiently powerful Noble Vampire. Sasha placed him under her control almost immediately, extracted information from him, erased his memories and implanted commands without noticeable resistance.[1]

### Trivia
- David's full name has not been revealed.
- He held the rank of Commander within the SWAT unit involved in the mansion incident.[1]
- David's unit was mobilized under orders ultimately originating from General James.[1]
- Sasha considered the speed with which the SWAT unit arrived suspicious because ordinary police rarely responded to incidents with such efficiency. This helped her realize that someone connected to the supernatural operation had deliberately mobilized them.[1]
- Sasha deliberately left David conditioned to obey future commands because she believed Ruby would eventually find a use for him.[1]
- David was publicly transformed into the "hero of SWAT" through Ruby's fabricated account of the mansion incident.[2]
- The media story falsely credited David with forcing [[Father Julian]] to retreat, despite Julian already being dead and David having no meaningful role in defeating him.[2]
- The previous claim that David participated in staged hero interviews is unsupported by the identified chapters. Chapter 164 only confirms that news reports publicly credited him as the hero responsible for Julian's retreat.[2]
- The previous detailed physical description of David as a middle-aged Caucasian man with a buzz cut and stocky build is not supported by Chapters 161 or 164 and has therefore been removed.
