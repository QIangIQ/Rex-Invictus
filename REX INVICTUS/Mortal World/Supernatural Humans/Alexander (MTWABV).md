Alexander was the Pope of the Vatican, the supreme leader of the Inquisition, and one of the most powerful Human [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] of his era. Having lived for more than nineteen centuries while maintaining the youthful appearance of a man in his twenties, Alexander stood at the top of the Church's supernatural military hierarchy and commanded Generals such as James, Kurtz and Leonardo.[2][1]
Alexander possessed an extraordinary connection to Faith and derived much of his supernatural authority from the Heavenly Father. This relationship allowed the Inquisition under his leadership to perform feats that ordinary Human organizations could never reproduce, including restoring severe injuries and resurrecting high-ranking Hunters who had been killed.[3]
Despite presenting himself as a calm, respectable and devout Pope, Alexander knowingly tolerated severe corruption within the organization he governed. General James explicitly revealed that Alexander had authorized the circumstances that produced experimental subjects such as S-000, including experimentation upon Humans.[1] His willingness to permit such atrocities ultimately caused the Heavenly Father to judge both Alexander and the Inquisition as having fundamentally betrayed the organization's original purpose.[4]
Alexander was ultimately killed during the Demonic War when War and Death infiltrated the Vatican and overwhelmed him. At the moment of his defeat, the Heavenly Father withdrew the supernatural power supporting him, causing Alexander's preserved youth to disappear and his body to rapidly return to its true decrepit age. After his physical death, the Heavenly Father personally rejected his soul from Heaven and condemned him to Limbo.[4]

### Physical description
Alexander was an exceptionally tall Human man standing approximately 195 cm. He possessed a muscular and well-developed physique that remained clearly visible even beneath his white papal robes.[2]
He had long golden-blonde hair and golden eyes. When using his Faith, Alexander's eyes glowed more intensely with golden light and his body became surrounded by a similarly colored aura.[2]
Despite being more than nineteen hundred years old, Alexander retained the appearance of a man approximately twenty-five years old. His youthful appearance was the result of the supernatural longevity available to extremely powerful [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] through Faith rather than his natural Human lifespan.[2][1]
During the final battle of his life, Alexander was seen wearing armor resembling that of a heavily armed paladin while fighting War and Death. Once the [[Heavenly Father]] withdrew his support, however, Alexander immediately lost the supernatural preservation of his body. His appearance rapidly deteriorated until he resembled a decrepit old man reflecting his true chronological age.[4]

### Personality and traits
Alexander was generally calm, composed and diplomatic, preferring negotiation and careful decision-making over unnecessary confrontation. Even when [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] personally descended upon the Vatican, Alexander did not panic or recklessly order his forces into battle. He immediately raised the Vatican to a Level 6 alert, recognized the seriousness of the threat and personally approached [[Scathach]] to attempt a peaceful resolution.[2]
Unlike many lower-ranking [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]], Alexander possessed a realistic understanding of the supernatural hierarchy. He did not believe the Vatican was invincible and was well aware that individuals such as Scathach represented threats capable of annihilating conventional Hunter forces. He even remembered a previous General who had attempted to harass Scathach and was immediately killed for it, making Alexander extremely conscious of what provoking her could cost.[2]
His calm demeanor also remained intact when confronted with information contradicting ordinary religious expectations. When Scathach revealed that she had personally met Jesus Christ, Alexander responded with curiosity rather than outrage and genuinely asked what Jesus had been like. This demonstrated that despite his religious authority, Alexander was capable of separating historical reality from idealized Church doctrine when dealing with beings old enough to have personally witnessed those events.[2]
Alexander was also courageous. When negotiations with Scathach failed, he did not attempt to flee. His eyes began glowing golden, Faith surrounded his body and he openly prepared to fight despite understanding the tremendous difference between them. He similarly confronted War and Death during the Demonic War even though the two Horsemen were overwhelmingly powerful opponents.[2][4]
However, Alexander's public image as a righteous and responsible Pope concealed a much darker pragmatism. He was willing to permit morally indefensible actions if he believed they benefited Humanity or strengthened the Inquisition. James explicitly stated that Alexander was aware of the experimentation performed upon Humans and had personally allowed the circumstances that created subjects such as S-000.[1]
Alexander therefore allowed his subordinates considerable freedom, even when their activities directly violated the ideals the Inquisition was supposedly created to uphold. This institutional permissiveness became one of his defining failures. When the [[Heavenly Father]] finally judged him, the deity explicitly condemned not only Alexander's personal actions but also the atrocities he knowingly allowed his subordinates to commit.[4]
Despite this corruption, Alexander genuinely believed in God. His internal confidence in the Heavenly Father's continued support was so complete that after Scathach killed his Generals, he regarded their deaths as temporary inconveniences because they could simply be resurrected while both God and the Pope remained present.[3]
This faith made his final judgment particularly devastating. When the Heavenly Father withdrew his power, Alexander did not react primarily with rage or fear; instead, he was emotionally crushed by his God's disappointment. Alexander internally acknowledged that he knew many of his actions—and the actions he had permitted—contradicted the purpose for which the Inquisition had originally been created.[4]
After the Heavenly Father declared that Alexander was neither welcome in Heaven nor deserving of the comparatively merciful punishment of Hell, Alexander ceased resisting completely. His motivation disappeared, and he passively allowed the forces of Limbo to take his soul.[4]

### Synopsis

#### The Awakening
Alexander first appeared at the Vatican after Mizuki returned from the confrontation involving [[Victor Elderblood]], [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]], [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] and [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]]. Alexander had originally ordered the Inquisition to locate and capture Lucy, the Vampire responsible for stealing one of the Church's sacred artifacts, but instead his [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] had become involved with several powerful Vampire families.[2]
When Mizuki reported that Ruby Scarlett had been attacked, Alexander immediately understood the true danger of the situation. Ruby's mother, [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], had warned the supernatural world eighteen years earlier that harming her daughter would bring direct retaliation from her.[2]
Alexander summoned General Kurtz and ordered the Vatican placed under a Level 6 alert. Even before the others fully understood what was happening, Alexander sensed that [[Scathach]] had already arrived.[2]
He then explained the seriousness of the situation to Mizuki. A previous Inquisition General had attempted to harass Scathach while she was shopping in Vatican territory and had been killed for his behavior. Alexander therefore understood that Scathach's arrival was not an empty diplomatic threat.[2]
Scathach descended upon the Vatican and froze an enormous surrounding region, effectively creating her own territory inside the headquarters of the Church. Alexander personally approached her accompanied by James, Kurtz and Leonardo.[2]
Scathach casually addressed Alexander as "Kratos", prompting him to correct her and state that his name was Alexander. Their familiarity demonstrated that the two had known one another for a considerable period of time, with Scathach openly commenting upon how much stronger Alexander had become over the centuries.[2]
During their discussion Scathach revealed that she had once personally encountered Jesus Christ. While the Generals reacted with shock, Alexander became genuinely interested and asked her what Jesus had been like.[2]
The relatively calm conversation ended once Scathach made clear that there would be no peaceful compromise concerning the attack upon Ruby. Alexander activated his Faith, causing his eyes to glow gold and an aura of golden energy to surround his body. Scathach responded by preparing for battle.[2]
The complete battle occurred outside the chapter's immediate viewpoint. Its consequences were revealed months later: Scathach had killed all of Alexander's Generals and Mizuki had lost an arm during the confrontation. Alexander nevertheless survived and subsequently restored the operational leadership of the Inquisition.[3]

#### A New Pillar
Approximately six months after [[Scathach]]'s attack, Alexander received news concerning [[Victor Elderblood]] becoming [[Nightingale]]'s new Vampire Count. Rather than dismissing Victor as an inexperienced newborn, Alexander immediately treated his rise as extremely bad news for the Vatican.[3]
By this point Alexander's Generals had already returned despite having been killed by Scathach. Alexander internally regarded their deaths as a manageable loss because, as long as God and the Pope remained present, the Generals could be resurrected. He similarly considered Mizuki's lost arm a comparatively minor injury that could be restored.[3]
The resurrection of his senior [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] demonstrated the extraordinary supernatural resources available to Alexander through his position and Faith, while simultaneously reinforcing his confidence that ordinary casualties among the Inquisition's elites did not necessarily have to remain permanent.[3]
Alexander's darker role within the organization became clearer through James' experimental program. While observing artificial Human-Vampire Hybrids, S-000 questioned whether the Pope actually knew that James was using Humans as experimental material.[1]
James laughed at the assumption that Alexander was unaware and revealed that the Pope had personally permitted the circumstances that had allowed S-000 herself to exist. James argued that nobody could survive for nineteen centuries while maintaining such authority without becoming morally compromised.[1]
This established that the Hybrid program and other atrocities occurring beneath the Inquisition were not simply the actions of rogue subordinates hidden from the Pope. Alexander knowingly tolerated at least portions of the program and therefore shared responsibility for the experiments carried out under his authority.[1]

#### Demonic War
The accumulated consequences of Alexander's leadership eventually returned during the Demonic War. The Vatican became one of the primary battlefields between the Inquisition, the forces of Heaven and [[Diablo]]'s Demons.[5]
Diablo's forces specifically targeted Alexander because eliminating the Pope would cripple the Inquisition's leadership. War and Death, two of the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse, infiltrated the Vatican while the larger battle distracted its defenders.[5][4]
The operation became possible after the Demonic infiltrator Jonah, who had served as Leonardo's assistant, betrayed the Humans and critically wounded the Seraphim Ariel with a projectile made from Hell's Metal and Demonic poison. The betrayal damaged cooperation between Heaven and the Inquisition at exactly the moment War and Death required.[5]
Alexander personally confronted the two Horsemen rather than fleeing. Wearing paladin-like armor and armed for battle, he fought both War and Death simultaneously while [[Victor Elderblood]] observed the battle from a distance.[4]
Despite his considerable power, Alexander was completely outmatched. Victor recognized that War and Death were not only enormously powerful but possessed master-level martial skill. Alexander could do little more than defend himself while the two Demons systematically overwhelmed him.[4]
The Seraphim did not immediately intervene. Unknown to Alexander, the [[Heavenly Father]] had already decided to use the Demonic attack as an opportunity to purge the corruption that had spread throughout the Inquisition.[4]
After several minutes, Death severed both of Alexander's arms with his twin scythes. War then drove his enormous greatsword through the Pope's heart and declared the end of the Inquisition.[4]
At that moment Alexander felt the supernatural power sustaining him disappear. The Heavenly Father withdrew everything Alexander had been borrowing through his position and Faith. Without that support, Alexander's preserved youth immediately collapsed and his body rapidly aged until only a decrepit elderly man remained.[4]
Confused and devastated, Alexander internally asked why his Father had abandoned him. To his surprise, the Heavenly Father directly answered him.[4]
The Heavenly Father explained that He had granted free will to Humanity and allowed that freedom to extend into the Inquisition. Alexander and his subordinates had abused that freedom, committing sins so extreme that even the Demons of Hell would be astonished by them. Rather than expressing hatred or anger, the Heavenly Father simply declared Himself disappointed.[4]
Alexander understood the accusation. He knew that both what he had personally done and what he had knowingly allowed his subordinates to do contradicted the original purpose of the Inquisition. His emotional reaction showed that divine disappointment affected him more deeply than hatred or punishment would have.[4]
The Heavenly Father then declared that Alexander would not be accepted into Heaven. He further stated that even Hell would effectively be a paradise compared with the punishment Alexander deserved and sentenced him instead to [[The Limbo Realm]].[4]
A gigantic gate to Limbo appeared behind Alexander. Thousands of hands emerged from the darkness, pierced his aged body and forcibly tore his visible soul from his corpse. Alexander offered no resistance, having completely lost the motivation to fight after receiving his God's judgment.[4]
Following Alexander's death, Ariel publicly announced the Heavenly Father's judgment upon the organization. The Inquisition lost the independent free will it had previously possessed, the entire institution would be investigated, and those responsible for its atrocities would ultimately be condemned to Limbo alongside Alexander.[4]
Alexander's death therefore marked more than the elimination of the Pope. It represented the end of the autonomous Inquisition he had governed for centuries and the beginning of direct Heavenly intervention into the corrupted organization.[4]

### Skills and abilities
As the Pope and supreme leader of the Inquisition, Alexander was one of the most powerful Human [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] of his era. His supernatural abilities revolved around extraordinarily developed Faith and his relationship with the [[Heavenly Father]].
- Faith Manipulation: Alexander was an extremely powerful user of Faith, the supernatural force generated through absolute belief. When preparing to fight [[Scathach]], his eyes glowed golden and his body became enveloped by a large golden aura.[2]
- Divine Empowerment: A significant portion of Alexander's supernatural strength was ultimately dependent upon power supplied or permitted by the Heavenly Father. When God withdrew His support in Chapter 561, Alexander immediately lost his supernatural strength and his preserved youth collapsed.[4]
- Resurrection: Alexander possessed the extraordinarily rare ability to restore dead Hunters to life through the power available to the Pope and God. Scathach killed all of Alexander's Generals during her attack on the Vatican, yet six months later they had been resurrected and returned to active service. Alexander specifically considered the resurrection of his Generals possible so long as both God and the Pope remained present.[3]
- Healing and Restoration: Alexander and the supernatural system available under his authority were capable of restoring otherwise catastrophic injuries. After Scathach's attack, Alexander regarded Mizuki losing an entire arm as a comparatively minor problem that could be cured.[3]
- Eternal Youth: Through Faith and divine support, Alexander maintained the appearance and physical condition of a man in his twenties despite being more than nineteen hundred years old. Once the Heavenly Father removed that support, Alexander immediately aged to his natural elderly state.[2][1][4]
- Enhanced Longevity: Alexander survived for more than nineteen centuries despite remaining biologically Human, making him one of the oldest known Human Hunters in the story.[1]
- Superhuman Physical Attributes: As a high-level Hunter reinforced by Faith, Alexander possessed strength, speed, durability and reflexes vastly beyond those of an ordinary Human. When Scathach attempted to decapitate Leonardo in Chapter 30, Alexander was fast enough to intercept her movement and physically catch her wrist before the attack connected.[2]
- Enhanced Strength: Alexander could physically restrain Scathach's wrist during her attempted attack on Leonardo, an exceptional feat considering the enormous physical power possessed by the ancient Vampire Countess.[2]
- Enhanced Reflexes: Alexander reacted quickly enough to Scathach's sudden movement to prevent Leonardo's immediate decapitation.[2]
- Combat Skill: Alexander possessed centuries of combat experience and was capable of personally confronting ancient supernatural beings. Although overwhelmed, he survived for several minutes while simultaneously fighting War and Death before the Horsemen finally executed him.[4]
- Swordsmanship: During his final battle Alexander fought while equipped as a paladin against two Horsemen who themselves possessed master-level martial skill. Although he was vastly inferior to them, he was sufficiently skilled to defend himself for a period of time.[4]
- Supernatural Perception: Alexander displayed awareness of powerful supernatural presences before ordinary observers could properly react. During Scathach's approach to the Vatican, he recognized that she had already arrived while his eyes briefly glowed with golden power.[2]
- Leadership: Alexander controlled the highest levels of the Inquisition for centuries, commanding its Generals, Hunters, military resources and worldwide operations from the Vatican.
- Strategic Intelligence: Alexander possessed considerable experience dealing with supernatural politics and powerful beings. Rather than blindly ordering attacks upon Scathach, he recognized the difference in power, attempted negotiation first and only prepared for combat after diplomacy became impossible.[2]

### Weaknesses
- Dependence on the [[Heavenly Father]]: Alexander's greatest supernatural strength was not entirely independent. His longevity, youthful state and much of his power depended upon continued divine support. When the Heavenly Father rejected him, these benefits disappeared almost instantly.[4]
- Moral Corruption: Alexander's willingness to tolerate increasingly extreme actions within the Inquisition ultimately became the cause of his downfall. The Heavenly Father specifically condemned Alexander for both his own actions and the atrocities he knowingly permitted his subordinates to commit.[1][4]

### Trivia
- [[Scathach]] repeatedly called Alexander "Kratos", but Alexander immediately corrected her and stated that his name was Alexander. There is currently no clear evidence that Kratos was genuinely his second name; it appears to have been Scathach either forgetting or deliberately misnaming him.[2]
- Alexander and Scathach had known one another for centuries. During their Chapter 30 meeting, Scathach openly remarked that Alexander had grown stronger over the approximately nineteen centuries of his life.[2]
- Alexander had personally known enough about Scathach's history to be curious about her experiences, including her claim that she once met Jesus Christ.[2]
- Scathach killed all of Alexander's Generals during her attack on the Vatican, but Alexander subsequently resurrected them.[3]
- Alexander knowingly permitted at least part of James' artificial Hybrid experiments rather than being an innocent superior unaware of what his subordinate was doing.[1]
- Despite Alexander's immense devotion to God, the [[Heavenly Father]] ultimately considered him unworthy of both Heaven and Hell and personally sentenced his soul to [[The Limbo Realm]].[4]
- The Heavenly Father used the Demonic attack upon the Vatican as an opportunity to remove Alexander and begin cleansing the corruption inside the Inquisition.[4]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s assessment after Alexander's death was that someone could not ultimately progress very far while relying upon borrowed power.[4]
