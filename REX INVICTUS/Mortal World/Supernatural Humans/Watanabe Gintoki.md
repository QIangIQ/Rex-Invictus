### Physical description
He looks like a normal salaryman with the black glasses, he was wearing a simple suit and holding a briefcase.

### Synopsis
He has a peculiar constitution, he absorbs the luck around him, bringing bad luck to everyone around him, making him very lucky. He also owns one of the luxury house on Earth, Japan as he work directly under [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Dragons Nest so he makes a lot of money through it.

### Equipment
- Orb: He is one of the very few non family member of [[Victor Elderblood]] who has the means to directly contact him.[1]

### Trivia
- His luck is in the realm of complete reality warping. It was said by Violet that one time he was shot at by a gunman and the bullet changed its trajectory and ricochetted the bullet and it hit the gunman.[1]
- Its also said that he is not blessed by a Japanese God of Luck but another foreign entity.[1]
- With his luck, Gintoki managed to single handedly destroy an entire Yokai Clan.[2]
