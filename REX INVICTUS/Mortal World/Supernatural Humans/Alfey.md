### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 832
Alfey was a powerful Blessed Human, Paladin and the World Champion of the [[Magic]] World, an inhabited world located within a separate Lower Sector. He served as one of the strongest known mortal defenders of his civilization and was empowered by several Gods belonging to both sides of his world's supernatural balance. Unlike most champions who relied upon the Blessing of a single deity, Alfey combined multiple Divine Blessings, Magic and a complete set of supernatural equipment, allowing him to wield both positive and negative-aligned forces and counter many of the techniques used by the Witches of [[The Arcane Realm]].[2]
Alfey became the first important native warrior encountered after the Witches accidentally opened a dimensional route between Arcane and the Magic World. His appearance immediately demonstrated that the civilization on the opposite side was considerably more dangerous than the Witches had anticipated. He could cut through their magical defenses, reflect supernatural attacks, interfere with summoned creatures and draw power from several Divine sources simultaneously. His greatest importance, however, was not his individual strength. Through Alfey, [[Victor Elderblood]] discovered that the Magic World's Pantheon operated as a genuinely unified military organization whose Gods coordinated their Blessings and forces rather than constantly competing with one another.[2][3]
Although Alfey survived his original encounter with Victor, his later history remained unknown for a long period. Chapter 1179 finally confirms that Alfey eventually died. By that point [[Evie Moriarty Elderblood]] had conquered and become Queen of the Magic World, and when [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]] asked what had happened to the Paladin, Evie answered simply that he was dead. Albedo then asked whether Evie had learned everything from him, which Evie confirmed.[1] The circumstances of Alfey's death, his exact lifespan and the nature of his later relationship with Evie have not yet been revealed.

### Physical Appearance
Alfey was an exceptionally tall Human standing approximately 200 centimetres in height. He had long golden hair reaching his waist, green eyes and a broad, square-shaped face. During his confrontation with the Witches he wore an ornate full suit of silver-and-gold armor accompanied by a pure white cape. The cape carried a distinctive crest consisting of a circle crossed by an X with a sword piercing through its center.[2]
His equipment gave him the appearance of a heavily armed Divine knight rather than an ordinary Human soldier. He carried a golden Divine sword and a large shield, both of which possessed supernatural properties specifically suited for confronting [[Magic]] and other Energy-based abilities.[2]

### Personality
Alfey was disciplined, confident and strongly committed to defending his world. When the Arcane portal unexpectedly connected the two civilizations, he did not hesitate to confront the unknown Witches and treated the dimensional breach as a hostile incursion. His confidence was supported by genuine experience and considerable supernatural power: as the World Champion, he was accustomed to standing among his civilization's strongest mortal warriors and possessed the direct support of several Gods.[2]
He was nevertheless pragmatic rather than blindly courageous. Alfey recognized when the difference between himself and an opponent became impossible to overcome. His reaction to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was fundamentally different from his response to the Witches; once he understood that Victor existed on a level far outside anything he could reasonably defeat, Alfey attempted to retreat rather than throw his life away in a meaningless confrontation.[2] This should not be interpreted simply as cowardice. Victor himself later judged the civilization dangerous despite its individual warriors being weaker than him because its Gods and military understood cooperation and warfare far better than most of Earth's traditional Pantheons.[3]
The final information concerning Alfey also suggests that his role eventually extended beyond fighting. By Chapter 1179, Evie states that she had learned everything from him before his death.[1] The novel has not yet explained whether Alfey willingly taught her, was defeated and interrogated, became an ally after the original conflict or developed some other relationship with the Witch Queen. It therefore cannot yet be stated that he was formally Evie's master, only that she obtained his knowledge before he died.

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Alfey first appeared after the dimensional experiment conducted by [[Evie Moriarty Elderblood]], [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]] and the Witches of [[The Arcane Realm]] unexpectedly established contact with an inhabited Reality beyond their own Sector. The portal had initially been treated as an extraordinary research breakthrough, but a massive surge of foreign Energy revealed that something on the opposite side had also discovered the connection.[2]
The portal stabilized and Alfey emerged into Arcane wearing his complete Divine equipment. Rather than appearing as an ordinary explorer, he immediately represented an organized military response from the civilization on the opposite side. His golden sword and shield easily interacted with the Witches' [[Magic]], and his various Divine Blessings allowed him to answer several different supernatural attacks. Alfey was identified as his world's World Champion, a powerful Blessed Human whose position represented the combined support of Gods belonging to both the positive and negative sides of his world's balance.[2]
The Witches quickly discovered that his equipment had been specifically designed to counter supernatural forces. His sword could cut through magical barriers and absorb Magic, while his shield could repel or reflect hostile Energy and curses. His armor supplied another defensive layer against supernatural attacks. Alfey also demonstrated the ability to seize control of creatures generated or summoned by the Witches, turning their own constructs against them.[2]
His Divine Blessings were equally unusual. Alfey could draw upon powers associated with both sides of his world's supernatural balance, allowing him to alternate between positive/light-aligned and negative/dark-aligned Energy. He could not freely use both opposing states at exactly the same moment and instead shifted between them according to the situation.[2] This versatility, combined with his Magic, weapons and experience, made him considerably more dangerous than a conventional Human warrior.
Evie eventually forced Alfey back toward the portal with her own Magic, but the confrontation had already attracted the full attention of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Albedo. Victor had been observing the experiment and immediately understood that the Witches had accidentally opened their Realm to an organized foreign civilization rather than an empty world. When Victor decided to investigate personally, Alfey attempted to retreat, but the difference between the two was overwhelming.[2]
Victor crossed into the Magic World and encountered the civilization Alfey represented. The world possessed large magical cities, organized supernatural soldiers and a Pantheon capable of responding collectively to an external threat. Victor defeated the immediate opposition without difficulty, but the encounter changed his assessment of the world. The individual Gods were not necessarily superior to Earth's strongest Gods; several were below beings such as [[Zeus]] in direct individual strength. Their danger came from unity. The entire Pantheon operated together, understood warfare and coordinated its Divine resources instead of wasting most of its strength through internal rivalries.[3]
After returning to Arcane, Victor warned Evie that she had been extremely fortunate he was present. Had the portal been opened without someone of his level nearby, Alfey's civilization possessed enough organized military power to invade Arcane and potentially kill its Witches.[3] Alfey's encounter consequently became the first practical demonstration of the dangers involved in uncontrolled contact between civilizations belonging to separate Sectors.
The incident soon escalated far beyond Alfey himself. Because Victor represented a vastly superior Being entering a protected developing Sector, the [[Regulators of Existence]] intervened and accelerated Time within the Magic World's Reality. Approximately 100,000 years subsequently passed there while only around 30 minutes passed relative to Earth.[4][5] Alfey's personal activities during this enormous period were never shown, and there is no reliable basis for assigning him an age of 100,000 years merely because his Sector experienced that amount of elapsed Time.

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Long after first contact, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] ordered [[Evie Moriarty Elderblood]] to take control of the World of [[Magic]]. By Chapter 1179, the conquest had already been completed: Evie ruled the planet as its Witch Queen, the Witches had taken political control with support from the [[Elderblood Empire]], and the civilization had become one of the Lower-Sector territories preparing for merger with Victor's Sector.[1]
During Albedo's visit to the conquered world, she noticed the last surviving Drakyo and vaguely remembered Alfey from the original dimensional incident. She asked Evie what had happened to "that paladin." Evie's answer was direct: "Dead."[1] No explanation was given for how, when or where Alfey died.
Albedo then asked Evie whether she had learned everything from him before his death. Evie answered that she had.[1] This establishes that Alfey remained relevant to Evie's later development even after their original hostile encounter. Whatever their relationship became, his accumulated knowledge was successfully passed to or acquired by the Witch Queen before his death.
The revelation also resolves Alfey's previously uncertain current status. He did not simply disappear during the Magic World's enormous period of accelerated development; he survived long enough to become a significant source of knowledge for Evie before eventually dying. The story has not established whether his death occurred naturally, during the Witch conquest, in another battle or through some unrelated cause.

### Skills & Abilities
As the World Champion of the [[Magic]] World, Alfey was an exceptionally powerful Blessed Human whose abilities were derived from personal skill, Magic, Divine Blessings and specialized supernatural equipment. Although completely inferior to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], he was powerful enough to overwhelm ordinary Witch defenses and served as one of the clearest examples of the Magic World's pre-acceleration military system.[2]
- Enhanced Physical Condition: Alfey possessed physical capabilities far beyond an ordinary Human as a Blessed warrior empowered by multiple Gods. His strength, speed, durability and combat reactions allowed him to fight supernatural Witches and their creations directly.[2]
- Divine Blessing Empowerment: Alfey carried several Divine Blessings supplied by the Gods of his world rather than relying upon a single Divine patron. These Blessings reinforced both his physical and supernatural abilities and were one of the reasons Victor considered him an unusually well-supported mortal champion.[2]
- Light Manipulation / Positive Energy Manipulation: Alfey could draw upon the positive-aligned side of his Divine Blessings and generate golden supernatural Energy for offensive and defensive applications.[2]
- Darkness Manipulation / Negative Energy Manipulation: Alfey could also access power associated with the opposite, negative-aligned side of his world's balance.[2]
- Duality Manipulation: Alfey possessed access to supernatural powers belonging to both opposing sides of his world's balance. He could alternate between these powers depending upon the situation, although Chapter 832 indicates that he could not freely employ both opposing states simultaneously.[2]
- Energy Manipulation: He could generate, control and release supernatural Energy through his Blessings and equipment, including concentrated golden Energy used during combat.[2]
- Magic: Unlike ordinary warriors who relied solely upon Divine Blessings, Alfey could also manipulate Magic, giving him another independent supernatural system to use alongside his Divine powers.[2]
- Weapon Mastery: Alfey was highly proficient with his Divine sword and shield. His status as World Champion and the manner in which he responded to the Arcane Witches demonstrate extensive practical combat experience with both weapons.[2]
- Divine Sword: Alfey's golden sword was constructed from a supernatural material unfamiliar to Earth's known Pantheons. It possessed absorptive properties and could cut through dense magical barriers with little difficulty.[2]
- Energy Absorption: The sword could absorb Magic and supernatural Energy directed against or encountered by its blade.[2]
- Barrier Bypassing: Its absorptive properties allowed Alfey to cut through magical defenses that would normally resist conventional physical attacks.[2]
- Divine Shield: Alfey carried a large supernatural shield capable of channeling golden Energy and reflecting incoming attacks.[2]
- Attack Reflection: The shield could repel or redirect supernatural Energy attacks rather than simply endure them through raw durability.[2]
- Curse Reflection: The shield's defenses also extended to hostile curse-type effects, allowing certain supernatural afflictions to be reflected away from Alfey.[2]
- Divine Armor: His complete silver-and-gold armor possessed powerful defensive properties against supernatural Energy, supplementing the protection supplied by his Blessings and shield.[2]
- Summoning Interference / Minion Control: Alfey demonstrated the ability to seize control of creatures summoned or created through Witch techniques and redirect them against their original controllers. This included necromantic creatures associated with [[Alice Moriarty]].[2] This feat is better classified as interference with summoned/created entities than as unrestricted Mind Control over ordinary living targets.
- Enhanced Senses / Danger Perception: Alfey possessed enough combat awareness to recognize the enormous difference between himself and Victor. Once confronted with Victor's true level of danger, he abandoned a conventional engagement and attempted to escape rather than continue an unwinnable battle.[2]
- Leadership: Alfey occupied a high-ranking military position and represented his civilization as its World Champion. His role placed him among the warriors entrusted with responding to existential or interdimensional threats against the Magic World.[2]
- Tactical Judgment: Alfey did not insist upon fighting when survival became impossible. His attempted retreat after evaluating Victor demonstrates the ability to abandon an engagement when the tactical situation becomes hopeless.[2]
- Possible Last-Resort Techniques: Victor and Albedo theorized that a champion carrying so many Divine Blessings and such an extensive set of equipment would likely possess emergency or self-sacrificial methods if pushed into an extreme situation.[2] No such technique was actually demonstrated, so its exact nature and capabilities remain unknown.

### Relationships

#### Evie Moriarty Elderblood
Alfey and [[Evie Moriarty Elderblood]] initially encountered one another as enemies. Alfey emerged through the Arcane portal as a defender of the [[Magic]] World, while Evie and her Witches were responsible for opening the dimensional route that his people interpreted as an intrusion.[2] Their first confrontation therefore consisted of direct combat, with Evie eventually forcing him back toward his own side of the portal.
Their relationship later changed in a manner that has not yet been fully shown. By Chapter 1179, Evie confirms that she learned everything from Alfey before his death.[1] This establishes a significant transfer of knowledge but does not clarify whether Alfey willingly became her teacher, cooperated after being defeated, was interrogated following the Witch conquest or passed on his knowledge through some other method. He should therefore not yet be formally classified as Evie's master without additional confirmation.

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] was the opponent who completely overturned Alfey's understanding of the dimensional incident. Alfey could contend with Arcane's ordinary Witches through his Blessings and equipment, but Victor existed on an entirely different level and defeated him without serious difficulty.[2]
Victor did not regard Alfey as worthless. Instead, Alfey became an example through which Victor evaluated the strengths of the [[Magic]] World's civilization. His equipment, multi-God Blessings and position demonstrated how effectively a unified Pantheon could empower mortal champions, while his defeat revealed the enormous difference separating that Lower-Sector civilization from Victor personally.[2][3]

### Trivia
- Alfey was the first explicitly demonstrated Paladin-type warrior from the [[Magic]] World.
- He was the first major native combatant encountered after Arcane established contact with the foreign Sector.[2]
- His title of World Champion reflected his position as one of the primary defenders of his civilization.[2]
- Alfey was Blessed by Gods associated with both sides of his world's supernatural balance rather than by only one faction.[2]
- His ability to use positive and negative-aligned forces did not mean he could freely combine both simultaneously; he shifted between them.[2]
- His sword was composed of a material unfamiliar to the supernatural civilizations known on Earth at the time of first contact.[2]
- The old assumption that Alfey was 100,000+ years old should not be treated as confirmed. The Magic World's Sector experienced approximately 100,000 years of accelerated Time, but the novel never states that Alfey personally remained alive throughout that entire period.[4][5]
- Chapter 1179 conclusively changes his status from Unknown/Alive to Dead.[1]
- His cause of death remains unknown.
- Albedo still remembered Alfey many years after the original portal incident, referring to him simply as "that paladin."[1]
- Evie states that she learned everything from Alfey before his death, although the exact circumstances of this knowledge transfer remain unrevealed.[1]
- Alfey's encounter was one of the first events to demonstrate to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and the Witches that civilizations existing in separate Sectors could possess completely independent Pantheons, military systems and supernatural technologies.[2][3]
