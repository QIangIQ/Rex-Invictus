Events of the MTWABV timeline (Victor's conquests, the fall of the pantheons, the [[Elderblood Empire]]) are distant background or rumor unless a chapter puts them on the page. ### Appearances

#### Debut
[[Ch14 - Rex Invictus VII|Chapter 14]]
Jimmy is a former Human Hunter and Exorcist of the Inquisition who later became an intelligence agent working for [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] and [[Victor Elderblood]]. He was one of the first [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] Victor encountered after becoming a Vampire, initially confronting Victor and [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] alongside his longtime partner Thomas. Unlike the considerably more impulsive Thomas, Jimmy quickly recognized that they were hopelessly outmatched and attempted to preserve their lives by cooperating with Victor.[1][2]
After their capture, Jimmy and Thomas were eventually forced by Ruby into magical contracts that turned them into spies inside their own organization. Although Jimmy originally served Victor's faction because resistance was impossible, this relationship changed considerably over the following years. As Victor's influence, power and treatment of useful subordinates became increasingly apparent, Jimmy's reluctant cooperation developed into genuine loyalty, and he began performing his espionage work with an enthusiasm comparable to the followers of powerful [[God-Kings]].[3][4][5]
Jimmy's service was eventually recognized after Victor became the Dragon God of [[Chaos]]. War, acting as the bearer of Victor's will, personally rewarded Jimmy and Thomas by transforming them through the same Essence of Chaos connected to War himself. The two ceased being ordinary Humans and became the first members of a new supernatural race under War: the [[Heralds of Chaos]].[5][6]

### Physical Appearance

#### Human
As a Human, Jimmy was a young male Hunter with brown hair and dark eyes. During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s first encounter with him, his hairstyle was distinctive enough that Victor referred to him as the boy with the '"bowl hair" when ordering Violet to capture him.[1]
Jimmy normally dressed in clothing associated with an Exorcist and carried a small bag containing several of the tools necessary for his work, including a Bible and Holy Water. By Chapter 933, he and Thomas were still recognizably dressed as Human Exorcists despite having spent years secretly working against their original organization.[1][5]

#### Herald of Chaos
Jimmy's appearance changed fundamentally after being transformed by War. His Human features became obscured as his face turned into complete darkness, preventing any ordinary facial features from being seen. His clothing simultaneously transformed into supernatural armor resembling War's own, although significantly less bulky.[6]
A pair of wings composed entirely of pure darkness also manifested behind Jimmy. His new form consequently resembles a dark supernatural herald far more than the Human Exorcist he originally was.[6]

### Personality
Jimmy is intelligent, pragmatic, cautious and highly concerned with self-preservation. These qualities immediately distinguished him from Thomas. Whereas Thomas frequently allowed anger, hatred of Vampires and enthusiasm for battle to override common sense, Jimmy was normally the person attempting to evaluate whether a confrontation could actually be survived. During their first encounter with Violet, Jimmy immediately understood the implication of seeing a Vampire freely walking beneath the Sun and correctly concluded that they had encountered someone associated with one of the exceptionally dangerous Noble Vampire bloodlines. His repeated attempts to convince Thomas to retreat were ignored, ultimately resulting in both of them being captured.[1]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] himself quickly recognized the difference between the two men. Jimmy was considerably more willing to compromise, reveal information or abandon pride if doing so increased his chances of surviving. Victor described him internally as both cowardly and intelligent, noting that Jimmy prioritized his own life enough to reveal information regarding the Inquisition's operation against Lucy once he understood that Victor had no immediate reason to kill him.[2]
Jimmy is also highly observant. While imprisoned at Violet's mansion, he immediately recognized Ruby from her appearance and her connection to [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], while Thomas did not even know who she was. Jimmy likewise understood the purpose of Ruby's magical contract clauses before Thomas grasped what was happening and immediately began considering whether a Witch could later remove the contract.[3]
His intelligence is repeatedly contrasted with Thomas' simpler personality. Jimmy frequently stops Thomas from speaking at dangerous moments, becomes frustrated whenever Thomas asks questions that could worsen their situation and effectively operates as the brains of their partnership. War later explicitly recognized this dynamic when Jimmy was the first of the pair to question what would happen to their long-running espionage assignment after they were offered a new existence.[3][5]
Jimmy is nevertheless not motivated solely by fear. He is ambitious and strongly attracted to situations that improve his own position. This eventually became one of the primary reasons his loyalty toward Victor stopped being merely contractual. Jimmy concluded that remaining on Victor's side meant aligning himself with the eventual winner and gaining opportunities for power and influence that his old organization could never provide. War was fully aware of Jimmy's ambition but did not consider it a problem, observing that ambition and desire could become powerful sources of loyalty when they were properly rewarded.[5]
His attitude toward Victor changed gradually rather than instantaneously. Jimmy originally felt disgust and resentment because he and Thomas had effectively been coerced into betraying their organization. However, as they continued receiving information regarding Victor's accomplishments and growing power, their feelings began to change. Their espionage became increasingly sincere, and following Victor's recognition during the [[Supernatural Gathering]], Jimmy and Thomas worked even more fervently for his faction. By the time War approached them, their loyalty had become genuine enough for Victor to formally acknowledge and reward it.[5]
Jimmy retained his Human consciousness after becoming a Herald of [[Chaos]], although the transformation significantly altered it. Upon witnessing Victor's true Cosmic existence for the first time, Jimmy did not even consider attempting to oppose him. His only conclusion was that nobody involved had possessed a realistic chance of resisting Victor from the beginning.[6]

### Synopsis

#### The Awakening
Jimmy first encountered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Violet while operating as a novice Hunter alongside Thomas. The two were members of an Inquisition team searching for the Vampire Lucy, who had been abducting Humans and creating problems in the Human World. Jimmy and Thomas were arguing when Violet's hostility alerted them to her presence, immediately forcing both [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] into combat readiness.[1]
Jimmy opened his Bible and invoked his God for protection, causing golden [[Divine Energy]] to descend upon himself and Thomas. The resulting blessing provided supernatural protection against Vampires and supported the pair's combat techniques. When Violet attempted to attack Thomas, a golden barrier prevented her kick from reaching him and even temporarily injured her foot before her regeneration restored it.[1]
Jimmy immediately realized the danger of their situation when he saw Violet freely standing beneath daylight. He correctly concluded that they were dealing with a Vampire belonging to an abnormal Noble bloodline and repeatedly ordered Thomas to retreat. Thomas ignored him and attempted to fight instead. When Violet targeted Jimmy directly, he threw Holy Water onto the ground and invoked Michael, calling upon the Guardian Prince and Warrior for protection. A gigantic sword composed of golden Energy descended between Jimmy and Violet, preventing her immediate attack.[1]
The arrival of Victor made Jimmy even more certain that continuing the battle was suicidal. He attempted to warn Thomas again, but Thomas attacked Victor and managed to graze his face using [[Holy Energy]]. The injury enraged Violet enough for her to release an enormous pillar of fire. Jimmy immediately blamed Thomas for ignoring his warnings and began searching for an opportunity to escape while Victor calmed his wife.[1]
Their escape failed. Victor incapacitated Thomas while Violet captured Jimmy, and the two Hunters were taken to an abandoned building for interrogation. Jimmy quickly adapted to the situation and cooperated once Victor promised that answering his questions could keep them alive. He revealed that their actual target was Lucy and provided information regarding the Hunter group involved in the operation.[1][2]
Jimmy identified [[Carlos Reiss]], Maria, himself, Thomas and Mizuki as participants in the operation and explained the importance of the Inquisition's General-class Hunters. His reverence toward the organization's strongest warriors remained deeply religious at the time, with Jimmy describing the Generals as warriors chosen by God and attributing their abilities and longevity to Divine blessings.[2]
Victor recognized that Jimmy's personality made him far easier to manipulate than Thomas. He tested his developing Vampiric Charm on Jimmy despite Violet explaining that Inquisition Hunters possessed protection against Vampire enchantment. Victor's influence nevertheless overwhelmed that protection and successfully placed Jimmy under his control. Thomas attempted to intervene but was subsequently charmed as well, leaving both Hunters completely at Victor's mercy.[2]

#### Vampire Society
Jimmy and Thomas remained imprisoned in Violet's mansion while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was taken to [[Nightingale]]. Their captivity was comparatively comfortable, to the point that Ruby later found them playing cards while surrounded by food. Jimmy immediately recognized Ruby Scarlett and correctly identified her as [[Scathach]]'s daughter, demonstrating considerably greater awareness of the supernatural world than Thomas.[3]
Ruby originally considered the two novice [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] almost useless, but decided that their continued access to the Inquisition could provide Victor's faction with intelligence. She therefore used magical contracts created by Witches to bind them into service.[3]
The contract imposed three principal conditions:
- Betrayal and Death: betraying Ruby would result in Jimmy's death.
- Master and Confidentiality: revealing their arrangement or allowing others to discover the contract would result in death.
- Espionage: Jimmy and Thomas were required to operate as Ruby's spies within their organization.[3]
Jimmy immediately understood that the arrangement was effectively a slave contract and attempted to identify possible methods of eventually escaping it. He knew that a Witch could theoretically nullify such a contract, but also knew doing so would require considerable resources. Ruby gave them no meaningful room for negotiation: they could accept becoming spies or die.[3]
Jimmy and Thomas consequently became intelligence assets for Ruby and Victor, remaining inside Hunter society while secretly supplying information to their supposed enemies.

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Jimmy and Thomas continued operating as spies for years after their original capture. Their position eventually became sufficiently valuable that Mizuki, despite having left her old organization, received information from them with the support of both [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Ruby.[4]
By Chapter 312, Jimmy and Thomas were explicitly identified as the first [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] Victor had encountered and the men who had eventually become Ruby's subordinates through magical contracts. They regularly exchanged information with Ruby and had also begun supplying Mizuki with intelligence concerning the Hunters. The contract protecting their identities ensured that Mizuki could use their information without exposing the two agents.[4]
Their position became particularly useful when Mizuki began investigating suspicious cooperation between normally hostile supernatural races. After being attacked by a coordinated group involving Vampires, [[Werewolves]] and Demons, she suspected that elements of the Inquisition were also participating in the conspiracy. Jimmy and Thomas consequently became part of the intelligence network used to monitor the organization from within.[4]
What had begun entirely through coercion gradually changed during this period. Jimmy repeatedly witnessed Victor rise from an unusually powerful young Vampire into a Progenitor, faction leader and increasingly dominant supernatural authority. His practical nature led him to recognize that opposing Victor offered increasingly fewer benefits while serving him repeatedly produced tangible rewards.[5]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Years after Jimmy's forced recruitment, War gathered numerous people connected to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s past so their actions could finally be judged. Unlike figures such as [[Corneliu Funar]], [[Kurama]] and [[Luan Davis]], Jimmy and Thomas were not summoned for punishment. War appeared before them specifically because Victor had recognized their years of service and intended to reward them.[5]
War revealed that Victor had remained aware of their true feelings throughout their service. Jimmy and Thomas had originally hated and resented being coerced into espionage, but those emotions had slowly changed whenever they learned about Victor's accomplishments. Their work eventually stopped being the minimum required by their contract and began resembling the loyalty powerful [[God-Kings]] received from their own subordinates. Their dedication increased further after Victor was publicly acknowledged by the Gods during the [[Supernatural Gathering]].[5]
Jimmy's first response to War's offer was practical. Rather than immediately celebrating, he asked what would happen to the espionage work he and Thomas had spent years performing. War explained that their old assignment was no longer necessary. Victor's senses had advanced enough that he could observe the entire planet if he desired, while newer beings were far better suited to intelligence work.[5]
Thomas questioned why Victor would reward people who could simply have been kept as slaves. Jimmy immediately elbowed him and attempted to stop him from asking a potentially dangerous question, once again demonstrating the familiar dynamic between the cautious Jimmy and blunt Thomas. War nevertheless answered that Victor rewarded diligent work, demonstrated loyalty and continued self-improvement regardless of a person's original status. Those who served properly could advance, while those who opposed him would receive equally proportional consequences.[5]
Thomas accepted the offer first. Jimmy followed immediately. Unlike Thomas, whose motivations were comparatively simple, Jimmy accepted because he understood that becoming part of Victor's side meant joining the dominant power and gaining considerably more influence himself. War read this ambition without condemning it.[5]
Jimmy and Thomas placed their hands into War's darkness. The Energy consumed their bodies, causing both men to collapse and writhe as their Human existences were reconstructed. When the transformation ended, War identified himself as the bearer of the [[Chaos]] Dragon God's will and commanded them to rise as [[Heralds of Chaos]].[5][6]
Jimmy's face became pure darkness, supernatural armor manifested around his body and a pair of dark wings emerged behind him. He and Thomas were transported to an extraordinary region of Space where countless galaxies could be seen and where Victor's incomprehensible Cosmic form was present in the distance. Both Heralds instinctively knelt before their God.[6]
Despite the radical transformation, Jimmy retained his Human consciousness. Seeing Victor's true existence destroyed any remaining concept he might have possessed regarding meaningful resistance. Even after receiving his new power, Jimmy's instinct was not to imagine fighting Victor but to recognize that fleeing would be the only conceivable response if such a Being were ever his enemy.[6]
War subsequently extended the same offer to Lucy and Karen, creating four Heralds. Roxanne later explained that all four had been created using the same Essence of Chaos involved in War's existence and were therefore genuinely connected to Victor, although much less deeply than beings such as Roxanne herself.[6]
Victor immediately gave his Heralds new work. Jimmy, Thomas, Lucy and Karen received his will and departed together to pursue additional enemies who remained unresolved from Victor's past, beginning Jimmy's new existence as an executor of the Chaos Dragon God's authority.[6]

### Skills & Abilities
Jimmy began as a relatively inexperienced Human Hunter whose combat ability depended heavily upon Faith, holy equipment and teamwork. His transformation into a Herald of [[Chaos]] fundamentally reconstructed his existence, although the full capabilities of his new race have not yet been individually demonstrated.

#### Herald of Chaos
- Herald of [[Chaos]] Physiology: Jimmy was transformed by War from an ordinary Human into one of the first four [[Heralds of Chaos]]. His physical body, supernatural nature and connection to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] were fundamentally reconstructed during the transformation.[5][6]
- Chaos Essence: Roxanne explicitly states that Jimmy and the other Heralds were created using the same Essence of Chaos involved in War's creation. This gives Jimmy a direct supernatural connection to Victor Elderblood, although this connection is shallower than the bond Victor possesses with beings such as Roxanne and Amara.[6]
- Supernatural Empowerment: The transformation granted Jimmy power vastly beyond what he possessed as a Human Exorcist. War's entire purpose in approaching him was to reward his service by granting him a superior existence rather than leaving him as a mortal spy.[5][6]
- Human Consciousness Retention: Jimmy retained his memories, awareness and individual consciousness after transformation. The narration nevertheless states that the Human side of the transformed Heralds had been significantly altered by their new existence.[6]
- Darkness Manifestation: Jimmy's transformed face and wings are composed of supernatural darkness. The full offensive applications of this darkness have not yet been individually demonstrated.[6]
- Wing Manifestation and Flight: A pair of wings made from pure darkness appeared behind Jimmy following his transformation. He is subsequently capable of existing freely in open Space alongside the other Heralds.[6]
- Space Survival: Jimmy and the other Heralds could remain conscious and functional in an open cosmic environment where countless galaxies were visible without requiring breathing equipment or any other form of protection.[6]
- Cosmic/Dimensional Translocation: Immediately after their transformation, Jimmy and Thomas vanished from War's location and appeared within Victor's extraordinary inner/cosmic environment. The Heralds are later shown disappearing again when ordered to pursue Victor's enemies. The novel has not yet clarified how much of this movement is personally controlled by Jimmy and how much is facilitated through War or his connection to Victor.[6]
- Connection to Victor's [[Will Solace|Will]]: The Heralds can receive and respond to Victor's wishes through their supernatural connection to him. After witnessing Victor's true form, all four Heralds simultaneously acknowledged his command and departed to carry it out.[6]

#### Former Abilities
- Human Physiology: Before his transformation, Jimmy was an ordinary Human trained by the Inquisition to fight supernatural beings. Although physically inferior to powerful Vampires, he possessed sufficient training to participate in active Vampire-hunting operations.[1]
- Hunter Training: Jimmy possessed knowledge regarding Vampires, Noble bloodlines, Hunter organization, supernatural threats and Inquisition procedures. His understanding allowed him to recognize the significance of Violet's sunlight immunity almost immediately and conclude that retreat was necessary.[1]
- Faith Manipulation: Jimmy could invoke Divine or [[Holy Energy]] through prayer and religious scripture. Opening his Bible and praying caused golden Energy to descend upon both himself and Thomas before their battle with Violet.[1]
- Blessing: Jimmy's prayers could provide supernatural reinforcement to himself and an ally, coating the [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] in golden [[Divine Energy]] before battle.[1]
- Barrier Creation: The Holy Energy invoked during Jimmy's prayer generated a defensive golden barrier capable of stopping Violet's physical attack and temporarily injuring her despite her Vampire physiology.[1]
- Holy Energy Weapon Summoning: By invoking Michael as the Guardian Prince and Warrior, Jimmy summoned an enormous golden sword composed of supernatural Energy to defend himself from Violet's attack.[1]
- Holy Water Usage: Jimmy carried Holy Water among his anti-supernatural equipment and used it when attempting to defend himself from Violet.[1]
- Resistance to Vampiric Charm: Members of the Inquisition possessed supernatural protection intended to prevent Vampires from enchanting them. Jimmy carried or benefited from this protection, although it proved completely ineffective against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s abnormal Charm and was overwhelmed almost immediately.[2]
- Espionage: Jimmy spent years secretly passing information from Hunter society to Ruby, Victor and later Mizuki. His effectiveness was ultimately high enough that Victor personally acknowledged the usefulness of his work and rewarded him for it.[4][5]
- Information Gathering: Jimmy's position inside the Inquisition gave Victor's faction access to intelligence concerning Hunter operations, supernatural conspiracies and internal activity that would otherwise have been difficult to obtain.[4]

### Relationships

#### Thomas
Thomas is Jimmy's longtime partner and closest known friend. Their personalities are almost complete opposites. Jimmy is cautious, analytical and survival-oriented, while Thomas is hotheaded, stubborn and considerably more willing to fight enemies without considering whether victory is realistic.[1][2]
Jimmy frequently acts as the brains of the pair, warning Thomas against dangerous decisions and physically stopping him from saying things that could worsen their situation. Despite constantly insulting Thomas' intelligence, Jimmy remained beside him through their capture, forced recruitment, years of espionage and eventual transformation. Both men became [[Heralds of Chaos]] together.[3][5][6]

#### Ruby Elderblood Scarlett
Ruby was the person who transformed Jimmy's captivity into long-term service. Rather than killing Jimmy and Thomas, she recognized the potential intelligence value of two [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] who could return to the Inquisition unnoticed and bound both through magical contracts.[3]
Their relationship initially possessed no genuine loyalty; Jimmy considered the agreement little more than slavery and immediately searched for possible loopholes. Nevertheless, his continued work for Ruby eventually provided enough rewards and opportunities that his attitude toward [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction changed naturally over time.[3][5]

#### Victor Elderblood
Jimmy first regarded [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] as a dangerous enemy and later as the Vampire responsible for enslaving him to a hostile faction. His initial cooperation with Victor existed almost entirely because he understood that resistance would result in death.[2][5]
That attitude changed as Victor continued rising through supernatural society. Jimmy's practical worldview made him increasingly conscious that Victor consistently rewarded useful people while continually surpassing the enemies who attempted to oppose him. His coerced espionage eventually became genuine service, and by the later years Jimmy intentionally worked with a degree of loyalty comparable to the followers of a God-King.[5]
Victor ultimately rewarded this development rather than ignoring Jimmy's original hostility. Through War, he granted Jimmy a new existence as a Herald of [[Chaos]] and incorporated him directly into the hierarchy beneath his Chaos authority.[5][6]

#### War
War is Jimmy's current Progenitor and immediate superior among the [[Heralds of Chaos]]. Acting as the bearer of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s will, War personally approached Jimmy and Thomas, evaluated the motives behind their service and offered them power in recognition of their loyalty.[5]
War transformed Jimmy using [[Chaos]] Essence and formally declared him a Herald of Chaos. Jimmy consequently serves Victor through War's newly created racial hierarchy.[6]

#### Mizuki Elderblood
Although Mizuki had once belonged to the same broad Hunter organization, Jimmy later became one of her secret intelligence sources. With Ruby and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s permission, Mizuki was allowed to obtain information from Jimmy and Thomas regarding the Inquisition and other Hunter activities while remaining bound to secrecy concerning their true allegiance.[4]

### Trivia
- [[Ch15 - Rex Invictus VIII|Chapter 15]] is explicitly titled "Jimmy is a smart guy.", directly reflecting the primary personality trait that distinguishes Jimmy from Thomas.[2]
- The nickname "Dick Head" predates Jimmy's later work with Mizuki. Thomas already called him "Dick-head" during their imprisonment in Chapter 47, while later narration continued using the nickname when identifying him in Chapter 312.[3][4]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] repeatedly contrasted Jimmy's intelligence and self-preservation instincts with Thomas' emotional and impulsive personality.[2]
- Jimmy recognized Ruby immediately when she entered the basement, whereas Thomas did not know who she was.[3]
- Jimmy understood the implications of Ruby's magical contract before Thomas did and immediately began considering whether another Witch could remove it.[3]
- War later explicitly described Jimmy as the brains of his partnership with Thomas.[5]
- Jimmy's eventual loyalty to Victor was not instantaneous or created purely by supernatural control. War explicitly states that Jimmy originally felt disgust and hatred toward his forced service before gradually becoming sincerely loyal through years of observation and rewarded work.[5]
- Jimmy accepted transformation partly because of ambition. He believed that being on Victor's side meant being among the winners and gaining greater influence, a motivation War openly recognized without condemning.[5]
- Jimmy and Thomas were the first two people shown being transformed into [[Heralds of Chaos]]. Lucy and Karen accepted the same offer shortly afterward.[5][6]
- Despite becoming vastly more powerful, Jimmy's first reaction upon witnessing Victor's Cosmic form was that fighting such a Being was not even worth considering.[6]
- The novel has not yet individually demonstrated the complete combat abilities of Jimmy's Herald of [[Chaos]] form. Abilities belonging specifically to War should therefore not automatically be attributed to Jimmy unless later chapters confirm that ordinary Heralds inherit them.
