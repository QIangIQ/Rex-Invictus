Luan Davis is a minor recurring character in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]]. Originally a Human suffering from a muscular disease, he was transformed into a Plebian Vampire by the Noble Vampire [[Corneliu Funar]], curing his illness but placing him in a master-servant relationship that would define most of his supernatural life.[1]
Before either man's supernatural life became publicly relevant, Luan was one of [[Victor Elderblood]]'s most hated Human acquaintances. Both young men had suffered from serious illnesses that restricted their physical capabilities, but after Luan became a Vampire and was freed from his disease, he repeatedly tormented the still-sick Victor over limitations Luan himself had once experienced. The situation became personal when Luan insulted Victor's mother, something Victor considered far more unforgivable than the bullying directed at himself.[1]
Shortly after Victor's own transformation, the power imbalance between them reversed completely. Victor challenged Luan to basketball, deliberately defeated and humiliated him in front of the university, and exposed how little independence Luan actually possessed when Corneliu silently ordered him to surrender. Although Luan then disappeared from the main narrative for hundreds of chapters, he survived the increasingly destructive supernatural conflicts that followed and remained Corneliu's servant. When Victor later gathered unresolved enemies from his past, Luan was judged far less severely than his former master because he had primarily been a weak subordinate rather than a major threat to Victor's family.[2][3]
Luan was ultimately offered a choice between death followed by the possibility of entering the reincarnation cycle or continued life in servitude. He chose slavery and was incorporated into Victor's civilization under the designation 069L.[4] No later canonical release for Luan has been established, and he continues to be indexed among the slaves of Dragons Nest.

### Physical Appearance
Luan is a tall young man standing approximately 190 cm in height, with light brown hair, brown eyes, sharp facial features and a muscular build. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] considered his appearance reminiscent of a rebellious delinquent.[1]
His athletic physique was not natural to his Human condition. Before becoming a Vampire, Luan suffered from a disease that progressively weakened his muscles and significantly restricted strenuous physical activity. Vampirization cured the condition and reconstructed his body into a healthy supernatural form, allowing him to develop physically, participate freely in sports and eventually become captain of the basketball team.[1]
Despite the enormous amount of time and supernatural upheaval that later passed around him, Luan retained the youthful appearance expected of a Vampire. Following his sentencing in Chapter 934, his most notable new feature became the supernatural slave choker placed around his neck. The device marked him as part of Victor's penal system and was directly tied to his new designation, 069L.[4]

### Personality
Luan's personality changed considerably between his first and later appearances. During his youth he was arrogant, spiteful and cruel toward people he considered weaker than himself. His treatment of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is particularly hypocritical because Luan had personally experienced a debilitating disease before being transformed into a Vampire, yet once his own weakness disappeared he chose to ridicule Victor for suffering from another incurable condition.[1]
He frequently interfered when Victor attempted to exercise, physically shoved him down and mocked the fact that Victor's illness made ordinary athletic aspirations dangerous. His worst insult was directed at Anna, telling Victor that his mother should never have given birth to someone who was supposedly nothing more than a burden. Victor could tolerate insults directed at himself far more easily than attacks on his family, and that statement became the principal reason his resentment toward Luan survived even after his transformation.[1]
Luan's new Vampiric body only amplified his arrogance. His muscle disease disappeared, he became strong and athletic, and his connection to Corneliu gave him protection within the local supernatural hierarchy. Yet the confidence was largely superficial because Luan remained completely subordinate to his master. During the basketball match with Victor, all of his pride and anger disappeared beneath Corneliu's authority the moment the Noble Vampire ordered him to stop. Victor deliberately exploited the contradiction by calling him a dog and daring him to disobey, but Luan surrendered rather than challenge Corneliu.[2]
By Chapter 933, centuries of supernatural events had stripped away much of this youthful arrogance. Luan openly regarded himself as a Vampire Slave and understood that becoming supernatural had never actually made him free. He had entered the supernatural world earlier than Victor but remained an insignificant servant while Victor rose through Vampire society, conquered Hell, became a Dragon and eventually reached a level of existence Luan could barely comprehend.[3]
The Demonic War and the instability surrounding [[Nightingale]] also changed his outlook. Luan lost virtually everything connected to his earlier Human existence, including the ordinary life and family he once possessed, while supernatural wars repeatedly demonstrated how irrelevant someone of his level was beside Gods, Demon Kings and other high-level entities. When [[Kurama]] interpreted Luan's calm behavior in captivity as courage, the truth was considerably less impressive: Luan simply understood that struggling against Victor was pointless.[3]
He nevertheless possesses a strong survival instinct. When War offered him death or slavery, Luan chose slavery almost immediately. He reasoned that he had already spent most of his supernatural existence beneath one master, so accepting another leash was preferable to voluntarily dying. Victor's system also offered something Corneliu never had: a structured possibility of earning rewards and eventually obtaining freedom.[4]
Luan still possesses resentment toward Victor, but it is now tempered by realism. He no longer imagines himself capable of meaningful retaliation and understands that his continued existence depends upon adapting to a civilization whose weakest serious powers vastly surpass anything he once considered extraordinary.

### History
Before the beginning of the series, Luan lived in [[New York]] and suffered from a muscular disease that restricted the development and use of his body. At some point he encountered [[Corneliu Funar]], who transformed him into a Plebian Vampire. The transformation cured his illness and placed Luan under Corneliu's authority as a Vampire servant.[1]
By the time [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] encountered him after his own awakening, Luan had already been a Vampire for more than a year. His supernatural condition was hidden from ordinary students, who simply accepted that the formerly sick young man had experienced an extraordinary physical recovery. Luan took advantage of that health to become an athlete and captain of the basketball team.[1]
His supernatural rise remained extremely limited compared with Victor's. Luan never developed a Noble Bloodline, special ability or remarkable combat reputation. Instead, his existence continued revolving around Corneliu until the latter and several other surviving enemies were eventually collected for judgment under Victor's new order.[3]

### Synopsis

#### The Awakening
Shortly after becoming a Vampire, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] returned to university and encountered Luan around the basketball court. The meeting immediately revived one of the deepest grudges from Victor's Human life. Because of Victor's RH-null blood and chronic anemia, strenuous activity could leave him hospitalized, yet he had always wanted to exercise and participate in sports. Luan repeatedly used that vulnerability against him, interfering whenever Victor practiced, knocking him down and mocking his inability to function like a healthy person.[1]
Victor had refused to allow Leon, Anna or his friends to solve the problem for him because he regarded Luan as his own grievance. His Human body simply lacked the means to retaliate. The situation changed completely once Victor became a Vampire.[1]
Victor's new Vampiric senses also revealed something he had been incapable of understanding previously. Luan possessed the mark of another Vampire and had been transformed into a servant. This explained the impossible recovery from his muscular disease and eventually led Victor toward the Noble Vampire responsible: [[Corneliu Funar]].[1]
Victor challenged Luan to a one-on-one basketball match in which the first player to reach seven points would win, with a two-point lead required if the score became tied. Both agreed not to rely openly upon supernatural powers. The game quickly drew a crowd, with Ruby Scarlett observing secretly and Corneliu watching his servant from the stands.[1][2]
Despite having been a Vampire for over a year, Luan found himself completely outclassed by the newly transformed Victor. Victor deliberately restrained himself to physical performance comparable to an exceptionally trained Human rather than openly displaying his true Vampiric abilities, yet still repeatedly outplayed Luan. What had once been a relationship between an athletic bully and a physically helpless victim became a public humiliation in the opposite direction.[2]
Luan's anger eventually overcame his willingness to respect the match's restrictions. Believing Victor must secretly be using supernatural strength, he prepared to retaliate with his own Vampiric abilities. Before the confrontation escalated, Victor looked toward Corneliu, who silently ordered his servant to stop.[2]
Victor then deliberately attacked Luan's pride, comparing him to a dog and challenging him to ignore his owner's command. Luan could not. He obeyed Corneliu and publicly surrendered despite his fury, proving to Victor that whatever power Vampirism had given Luan had not made him independent.[2]
Victor left the university still dissatisfied. Humiliating Luan had repaid part of his Human grudge, but he now understood that Luan was fundamentally subordinate to another person. Victor deliberately waited in an isolated place afterward, expecting Corneliu to confront him, but the Noble Vampire never appeared. His attention consequently shifted from the servant he had humiliated toward the master hiding behind him.[5]
Luan subsequently disappeared from Victor's immediate life. The difference between them only grew from that point onward.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
By the time Luan returned to the narrative, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had long since ceased being the young Vampire who defeated him on a basketball court. Luan had remained a comparatively ordinary Plebian Vampire and continued existing beneath Corneliu, while Victor had become one of the greatest powers in the Sector.[3]
Luan's life had deteriorated considerably during the years between appearances. The Demonic War and the destruction surrounding supernatural society erased most of the stability he had once possessed. [[Nightingale]] itself became embroiled in conflicts far beyond Luan's ability to influence, and he lost the family, ordinary ambitions and Human-world life he had once known. Looking back upon his existence, Luan understood that entering the supernatural world before Victor had accomplished almost nothing for him; he remained a servant while Victor's talent, opportunities and relentless growth had carried him beyond comparison.[3]
Victor eventually decided to resolve the surviving grudges and unfinished enemies from his past. Luan was captured alongside Corneliu, Lucy, Karen and [[Kurama]] and placed in a location from which escape appeared impossible. When Kurama awoke and demanded answers, Luan was the one who explained their common situation: every prisoner had, in one way or another, made an enemy of Victor, and Victor had finally decided to collect what remained of those old debts.[3]
Kurama was surprised by Luan's apparent composure, but Luan's attitude was not bravery. He had simply spent too long seeing the overwhelming differences between supernatural beings to delude himself into believing resistance would accomplish anything. Corneliu similarly recognized that Luan's position was different from those of the more serious enemies. He had been an insignificant servant who bullied Victor in his youth, not someone responsible for catastrophic attacks against Victor's wives, children or faction.[3]
The prisoners were eventually brought before War, now one of Victor's [[Heralds of Chaos]] and an executor of his [[Will Solace|Will]]. Lucy and Karen received radically different outcomes and were transformed into Heralds, while Luan and Corneliu were judged according to their individual histories.[4]
Luan was offered two possibilities: die and enter the possibility of future reincarnation, or continue living as a slave. His crimes were not severe enough to justify the permanent Soul Erasure reserved for enemies Victor considered irredeemable. Luan chose servitude almost immediately. He had already regarded himself as Corneliu's slave and considered changing masters a better outcome than voluntarily abandoning his current life.[4]
A supernatural choker formed around his neck and Luan was transported into one of Victor's controlled settlements. The environment shocked him because advanced technological structures, desert terrain and extremely beautiful natural regions existed together, while numerous beings wearing similar collars moved under the supervision of Demonic wardens. Some prisoners were powerful enough that Luan instinctively recognized them as Gods.[4]
He was also astonished that sunlight did not burn him despite being an ordinary Plebian Vampire. The story never explains whether this resulted from environmental technology, magical protections, the nature of the region itself or another Imperial system, so it should not be treated as Luan developing sunlight immunity.[4]
A warden identified him as Number 069L rather than by name and ordered him into position. When Luan hesitated, he discovered the actual function of his collar: it could seize control of his body and force obedience. Further resistance triggered overwhelming pain intense enough to disrupt his thoughts while preventing him from even vocalizing his suffering.[4]
An enslaved Lesser God advised Luan not to oppose the system unnecessarily. Victor's penal structure was severe but operated according to recognizable rules: useful service could produce privileges and eventual release, while repeated defiance resulted in increasingly harsh consequences.[4]
Luan almost immediately witnessed proof. Former Egyptian Gods [[Upuaut]] and Kuk, previously identified as Numbers 089U and 081K, had voluntarily aided Imperial development, contributed useful information and completed the conditions of their punishment. They were publicly released, restored to their original identities and recognized as exemplary citizens.[4]
Their release changed Luan's understanding of his sentence. He was genuinely enslaved, but the sentence was not automatically eternal. If he obeyed the rules, performed useful work and demonstrated sufficient value, a path back to freedom existed.[4]
No later canonical scene has established that Luan completed this process. His latest confirmed designation therefore remains 069L, and later Imperial indexing continues treating him as enslaved.

### Skills & Abilities
Luan is an ordinary Plebian Vampire whose abilities are overwhelmingly derived from basic Vampiric physiology. He possesses no known Bloodline ability, Divinity, magical specialization, martial discipline or unique supernatural technique.

#### Vampire Physiology
- Plebian Vampire Physiology: Luan was transformed from a sick Human into a Plebian Vampire by [[Corneliu Funar]]. The transformation eliminated his muscular disease and reconstructed his body into a healthy supernatural state.[1]
- Superhuman Strength: Luan is physically stronger than an ordinary Human. His supernatural strength was nevertheless already inferior to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s immediately after Victor's transformation despite Luan having possessed more than a year of Vampiric experience.[1][2]
- Superhuman Speed and Reflexes: His Vampiric body possesses movement speed and reactions beyond conventional Human limitations.
- Enhanced Durability: Luan can endure physical damage that would seriously injure an ordinary Human.
- Regeneration: Luan possesses Vampiric healing. Victor damaged his shoulder shortly before their basketball match and observed that the injury had already recovered soon afterward.[1]
- Enhanced Stamina: His transformation removed the severe physical restrictions imposed by his Human disease, allowing him to perform intense athletic activity without the muscular deterioration that had previously limited him.[1]
- Enhanced Senses: His senses exceed those of ordinary Humans as part of his Vampiric physiology.
- Longevity: As a Vampire, Luan possesses a lifespan vastly exceeding that of an ordinary Human and retains a youthful physical appearance despite the passage of time.
- Blood Consumption: Like other Vampires, Luan can sustain himself through Blood. No unusual feeding ability or Blood-related technique has been demonstrated.
- Vampiric Presence: Luan possesses the supernatural characteristics recognizable to other Vampires and supernatural beings, including the mark identifying his previous servant relationship with Corneliu.[1]

#### Physical Skills
- Basketball Proficiency: After his transformation cured his illness, Luan became captain of his university basketball team and developed substantial experience as a player.[1]
- Athleticism: His Vampiric physique allowed him to become highly athletic relative to ordinary Humans, although this should not be confused with meaningful combat skill by later supernatural standards.
- Basic Physical Combat: Luan was willing to use physical force and his Vampiric advantages during confrontations, but he has never demonstrated a formal martial art or advanced fighting system.

#### Slave Choker
The choker imposed upon Luan by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s penal system is not one of Luan's abilities. It is an external restraint designed to control him.[4]
- Compulsory Obedience: Authorized commands can override Luan's voluntary control and force his body to perform ordered actions.[4]
- Pain Inducement: Resistance can trigger extreme supernatural pain intended to discourage disobedience.[4]
- Motor Control Override: The choker can move Luan's body even when he consciously hesitates or refuses an instruction.[4]
- Vocal Suppression: During punishment, Luan was subjected to agony while being unable to properly scream, demonstrating that the system can restrict physical responses while administering discipline.[4]
- Identification: The penal system recognizes Luan as 069L, temporarily replacing his ordinary social identity with a prisoner designation.[4]

### Weaknesses
- Low-Level Vampire: Luan possesses none of the advantages associated with Noble Vampire Bloodlines, [[Vampire Counts]], Progenitors or the later evolved races within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s civilization.
- Limited Combat Experience: Although physically superior to Humans, Luan has never demonstrated meaningful combat training capable of compensating for his low supernatural level.
- Sunlight Vulnerability: As an ordinary Plebian Vampire, Luan normally possesses the weaknesses of his race. His ability to stand in sunlight after being transported into Victor's territory is presented as surprising even to Luan and has never been established as a permanent personal resistance.[4]
- Former Master-Servant Bond: Luan spent most of his supernatural life subordinate to Corneliu and obeyed his master's commands even when doing so directly violated his pride.[2]
- Slave Choker: His current collar can force obedience, seize control of his movement and punish resistance through extreme pain.[4]
- Extreme Power Disparity: By his later appearance, Luan is functionally irrelevant in direct confrontation with Victor or any of the major Imperial powers. He understands this himself and no longer seriously considers fighting them.[3]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] and Luan began as Human acquaintances whose relationship was defined almost entirely by hostility. Luan repeatedly bullied the sick Victor and eventually crossed the line Victor cared about most by insulting Anna. Victor's transformation finally gave him the physical ability to retaliate, and he deliberately used their basketball match to reverse the humiliation that had defined their earlier relationship.[1][2]
Victor's attitude toward Luan nevertheless changed after discovering Corneliu's involvement. Luan remained someone he disliked, but Victor realized that the young Vampire was ultimately another man's servant rather than the true power behind his situation. By [[Ch10 - Rex Invictus III|Chapter 10]], his attention had already begun shifting toward Corneliu.[5]
Centuries later, the difference in their lives became enormous. Victor had become a Dragon God and sovereign of an expanding civilization while Luan remained a minor Plebian Vampire. Victor still remembered him when settling his remaining grudges, but did not consider Luan's crimes severe enough to justify permanent destruction. His punishment consequently allowed both survival and the possibility of eventual freedom.[3][4]
Luan continues to resent Victor but is fully aware that the relationship can no longer function as a rivalry. Victor exists on a level Luan cannot meaningfully challenge.

#### Corneliu Funar
[[Corneliu Funar]] was the Noble Vampire who transformed Luan, cured his Human disease and became his master.[1] The transformation gave Luan the healthy and athletic body he had previously lacked, but simultaneously exchanged one limitation for another: Luan became dependent upon Corneliu's authority.
The basketball incident demonstrates the imbalance clearly. Luan desperately wanted to continue fighting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], yet surrendered immediately when Corneliu ordered him to stop.[2] By his later appearance, Luan no longer romanticized the arrangement and simply described himself as a Vampire Slave.[3]
His eventual decision to accept Victor's slave sentence was partially shaped by this history. Luan did not view servitude as an entirely new condition; from his perspective, he had already spent most of his supernatural life wearing an invisible leash beneath Corneliu.[4]

#### War
War acted as the executor of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s judgment when Luan was finally brought before the Empire. He did not treat Luan as a significant warrior or dangerous enemy, instead presenting him with the options Victor's judgment permitted: death with possible reincarnation or continued existence through slavery.[4]
Luan chose servitude, and War's judgment effectively transferred him from Corneliu's former servant to a prisoner of Victor's civilization.

#### Kurama
[[Kurama]] and Luan were imprisoned together when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s surviving enemies were gathered. Luan was one of the first people to explain the situation to the confused Youkai, informing him that everyone present had somehow become part of Victor's unresolved past.[3]
Kurama initially misinterpreted Luan's resignation as courage. Luan was not fearless; unlike Kurama, he had simply become accustomed to being powerless within the supernatural hierarchy and understood that escape or resistance was unrealistic.[3]

#### Kuk and Upuaut
Luan did not share a known relationship with Kuk or [[Upuaut]] before entering [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s slave system, but their release became important to his future outlook. The two former Egyptian Gods completed their sentences through productive cooperation and were restored as ordinary citizens in front of Luan.[4]
Witnessing their freedom provided concrete evidence that his own sentence could eventually end rather than continuing indefinitely.

### Current Status
Luan's last confirmed condition remains that of an enslaved Plebian Vampire designated 069L within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s civilization.[4] No subsequent chapter in the currently indexed material establishes that he has earned release, died, reincarnated, received a new position or undergone another racial evolution. Current character navigation also continues to place him among the Empire's slaves.
His situation is nevertheless fundamentally different from permanent imprisonment. Chapter 934 explicitly demonstrates through Kuk and [[Upuaut]] that prisoners who satisfy their sentence and contribute productively can regain their names, freedom and citizenship. Luan therefore possesses a canonical route out of slavery even though the story has not yet shown him completing it.[4]
