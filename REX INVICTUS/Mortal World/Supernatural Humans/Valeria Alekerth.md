#### Debut
Chapter 709
... Very well... For my daughter... For my god... I will start conquering the shadow world. Everything will bear the influence of my god.
—Valeria, Chapter 826
Valeria Alekerth is an enhanced Human, the supreme High Priestess of the [[Blood God Religion]], personal disciple and representative of [[Victor Elderblood]], and one of the most important mortal administrators within Victor's civilization. She originally entered the story as a grieving mother whose sixteen-year-old daughter, [[Vanessa Alekerth]], had been raped, murdered and dismembered by [[Jonathan Eric Gonzales]]. Valeria became involved with the growing religion worshipping Victor as the Blood God, personally sacrificing Vanessa's murderer during one of its rituals and dedicating herself to spreading Victor's influence throughout the Human and supernatural worlds.[2]
Her exceptional devotion eventually brought her directly to Victor's attention. Unlike many fanatical followers, Valeria retained humility, discipline and the ability to independently evaluate what was best for the religion as a whole. Victor considered these qualities essential and personally selected her as his High Priestess and spokesman, passing over even individuals who possessed greater raw supernatural power. He subsequently forged a two-way contract with Valeria, rejuvenating her body, strengthening her potential, connecting her directly to his Energy and granting her the authority to summon the Demons of Hell under his command.[3]
From that point onward, Valeria grew from the administrator of an underground religious movement into the central mortal religious authority of Victor's civilization. She governed the Blood God Religion, coordinated its Archbishops and Acolytes, represented Victor before allied supernatural factions and later commanded a religious structure extensive enough that both the Imperial Inquisition and the Empire's Onmyō practitioners ultimately operated beneath her authority.[4][5]
Victor eventually rewarded Valeria's unwavering service with something she had never dared request: the restoration of Vanessa. Using his authority over Souls, information preserved concerning Vanessa's existence and a transaction with [[The Judges of The Abyss]], Victor reconstructed her daughter and reunited mother and child in the Paradise created for his faithful.[6][7]

### Physical Appearance

#### Before Victor's Contract
Valeria originally appeared as a mature Human woman with dirty-blonde hair, brown eyes and visibly aged features. She appeared to be somewhere between her mid-thirties and approximately forty years old, reflecting the ordinary aging process of a Human who had endured considerable emotional hardship before entering [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s supernatural faction.[2]
Her original appearance was relatively unremarkable by the standards of the supernatural women surrounding Victor, something that changed dramatically after Victor personally selected her as High Priestess.

#### After Victor's Contract
When Valeria accepted the position of High Priestess and swore eternal devotion to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], his contract immediately altered her body. Her aged appearance visibly rejuvenated, her hair became a brighter golden blonde, her body grew taller and more developed and her overall physical condition improved dramatically.[3]
The transformation was not purely cosmetic. Victor explicitly strengthened Valeria because he refused to allow the person representing him before the world to remain physically fragile or mediocre. Her new body was designed to support the supernatural Energy, responsibilities and dangers associated with serving as his personal representative.[3]
Her eyes also change when the contract or supernatural abilities connected to Victor become active. During the establishment of their pact, her eyes temporarily took on a crimson coloration while a blue Runic magic circle appeared within them, marking the successful contract with the King of Hell. Later depictions show her eyes shining with a distinctive violet supernatural radiance when using her Powers.[3]
Valeria is frequently accompanied by her personal [[Death Staff]], a black-and-gold Divine Artifact with a floating violet gem. The weapon has become both a tool of supernatural authority and a visual symbol of her position as Victor's Supreme Priestess.[8][9]

### Personality
Valeria is disciplined, hardworking, pragmatic and deeply devoted to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. However, the quality that distinguished her most strongly from the other candidates for High Priestess was her humility. When Victor first announced that she would become his spokesman, Valeria did not celebrate or assume that she deserved the position. Her first reaction was to question whether Roxanne or one of the more experienced Archbishops would be a better choice. Victor specifically identified that response as one of the reasons he had chosen her: Valeria instinctively considered what would benefit the organization rather than focusing upon the prestige she personally stood to gain.[3]
Her devotion to Victor is extreme, but it is not portrayed as mindless obedience. Victor explicitly valued the fact that Valeria could think, learn, form her own opinions and carry responsibility rather than merely repeating his commands. During her personal education, he reminded her that she was not a puppet or disposable tool and ordered her to understand supernatural society, independently evaluate situations and become the best possible version of herself.[4]
Valeria consequently developed into a highly capable administrator. She can coordinate large numbers of followers, assign logistical responsibilities, delegate authority to Archbishops and recognize when specialists should be consulted rather than personally interfering with every problem. By the later [[Blood God Religion]], much of her work consisted of managing an enormous institution rather than personally executing every task.[4][7]
She is cold and ruthless toward people she considers irredeemable. The woman who sacrificed [[Jonathan Eric Gonzales]] did so without hesitation after what he had inflicted upon Vanessa, and her later reputation as Executioner Valeria reflects her willingness to oversee punishment against those judged guilty beneath Victor's religious order.[2][9]
At the same time, Valeria is an exceptionally loving mother. Vanessa's death remained the deepest emotional wound in her life even after Valeria gained supernatural authority and status. When Victor finally restored Vanessa, Valeria's usual cold composure collapsed completely. Merely hearing her daughter's voice left her unable to properly speak, and when Vanessa embraced her, Valeria fell apart and cried uncontrollably while holding the daughter she believed she had permanently lost.[7]
Valeria is also ambitious on behalf of Victor. Her loyalty eventually expanded beyond maintaining the existing Blood God Religion; she began viewing every unexplored social, spiritual and supernatural environment as territory in which Victor's influence could be extended. After receiving the [[Death Staff]] and learning more about the spiritual world, she resolved to extend the Blood God's influence into the Shadow World itself.[8]
Despite her devotion, Valeria follows Victor's teachings regarding survival and strategy. When he questioned what she would do if confronted by an enemy she could not defeat, she answered that she would inform him, escape if necessary and prepare revenge for the future. Victor strongly approved, emphasizing that there was no shame in retreating from a superior enemy when survival allowed a later counterattack.[4]

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Valeria entered the story during the enormous social collapse following the Demonic War. With Humanity now aware of supernatural Beings and traditional institutions weakened, numerous Humans began seeking new Gods and new forms of protection. Among the religious movements emerging during this period was the rapidly growing [[Blood God Religion]], whose worship centered around [[Victor Elderblood]].[2]
Valeria had a deeply personal reason for embracing a God associated with Blood, Murder and Vengeance. Her sixteen-year-old daughter, [[Vanessa Alekerth]], had been raped, murdered and dismembered by [[Jonathan Eric Gonzales]]. Valeria eventually captured Jonathan and brought him into a ritual alongside other criminals selected for execution. She personally used the man responsible for destroying her family as a sacrifice to the Blood God she worshipped.[2]
The ritual demonstrated how far Victor's religion had already spread despite his own limited involvement in its administration. Its followers invoked Victor as the God of Blood and Murder, carried out sacrifices according to their developing religious practices and possessed enough supernatural knowledge to interact with Demonic forces and other supernatural Beings.[2]
Victor soon learned that worship of him was no longer a collection of isolated cults. The Blood God Religion had already developed Acolytes, Archbishops, temples and organized communities across several territories. Rather than allow a religion bearing his name to evolve without supervision, Victor chose to formally take responsibility for it.[10][11]
He personally manifested before hundreds of believers, demonstrating that the Being they worshipped was neither myth nor distant legend. During the gathering Victor announced that the religion would possess a genuine home and created Victorius Aeternus, a fortified Holy Capital intended as a sanctuary for his followers should another catastrophe strike the world.[3]
The greatest surprise came when Victor selected Valeria rather than Roxanne as the religion's High Priestess. Valeria herself initially believed this was a mistake and argued that Roxanne or several of the Archbishops were better qualified. Victor explained that this response was precisely why he trusted her. Her humility, devotion and instinct to consider the organization before herself were traits he found extremely rare among Gods and religious leaders.[3]
After Valeria accepted the position, Victor required her to swear eternal devotion to him. He then invoked an ancient custom belonging to Hell and created a two-way supernatural contract between them. In exchange for Valeria's undying devotion, Victor's Energy would preserve her youth and she would gain the authority to summon Demons belonging to him as King of Hell. The contract would be broken if Valeria ever genuinely lost that devotion.[3]
The pact immediately rejuvenated Valeria, strengthened her body and marked her eyes with supernatural Runes. Even Roxanne was shocked by the extent of the authority Victor had given one Human, since Valeria could theoretically summon any Demon subordinated to Victor so long as she possessed enough Energy to sustain the invocation.[3][4]
Valeria immediately began reorganizing the expanding religion. Victor's resources transformed the Blood God Religion from a clandestine movement into a genuine supernatural power. Victorius Aeternus was connected to the outside world, Demons were summoned for logistics, Witches and portal specialists were incorporated into transportation plans and Victor's wives began coordinating the religion's influence across Human society.[4]
Victor personally taught Valeria for seven days. Rather than merely teach combat, he attempted to rebuild her entire understanding of the supernatural world. He provided ancient books from the Snow, Scarlett and Fulger Clans, supernatural historical texts, a spatial storage bag and a communication Orb linked to his allied network. He ordered Valeria to study, form her own opinions and never tolerate mediocrity simply because she possessed his favor.[4][12]
Victor also established the principles she was expected to enforce. Those serving him were to work hard and demonstrate results rather than depend upon blind Faith for advancement. Kindness shown to the faction was to be remembered, while hostility would eventually be returned. Valeria internalized these teachings and developed into the central Human representative of Victor's growing faction.[4]
As Victor's network expanded, Valeria's authority became increasingly extraordinary for a Human. Through Victor she possessed access not merely to the Blood God Religion but to Demons from Hell, the Vampire Count Clans, Noble Vampires, Witches, [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]], Fairies, Greek Goddesses, Amazons, Youkai and the other supernatural factions allied to him. She was effectively the mortal official authorized to speak with the support of one of the strongest supernatural coalitions on Earth.[4]
Following Victor's transformation into the Blood Dragon Primogenitor and the creation of his new Pantheon, Valeria received another major reward. Victor, [[Thanatos]] and [[Hephaestus]] collaborated to create a Divine Artifact specifically for her: the [[Death Staff]]. The Staff contained a fraction of Death and dramatically expanded Valeria's ability to interact with Souls and the dead.[8]
Rather than become satisfied with controlling the religion's existing territories, Valeria interpreted the Staff as another responsibility. Motivated by both Vanessa and Victor, she resolved to extend her God's influence into the Shadow World and declared that everything would eventually bear his influence.[8]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
As [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s authority continued expanding, the [[Blood God Religion]] ceased being merely a Human cult centered on an unusually powerful Vampire. Victor became a genuine God and later a Dragon God whose Pantheon incorporated numerous Divine, Demonic and supernatural systems. Valeria's position consequently evolved from the priestess of a local religion into the mortal representative of a rapidly expanding God Emperor.[1]
Victor explicitly continued regarding Valeria as belonging to him and referred to the disciple managing his religion when discussing the people and factions he considered part of his personal domain.[1]
Her personal romantic relationship with Victor remained far less developed than those of his established wives, but her absolute devotion, direct contract and position as his chosen disciple made her one of the women repeatedly identified as a potential future member of his harem.

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Years later, Valeria remained the central administrator of the [[Blood God Religion]]. By this period she had acquired a reputation as an exceptionally cold and ruthless woman toward criminals and enemies, while remaining respected among [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faithful as the Supreme Priestess.[9]
The religion itself had grown enormously. Its headquarters included major bases within Victor's expanding civilization, while Valeria coordinated numerous Archbishops and followers distributed through increasingly large territories.[9]
During one operation, Valeria's faction recovered a suspicious supernatural artifact connected to hostile Divine activity. Rather than recklessly interact with it, Valeria followed established procedures and had the object examined by [[Violet Elderblood Snow]]. Violet identified a curse attached to the object and subsequently supplied the Blood God Religion with adapted [[City Core]] technology that could monitor its territory while still allowing hostile infiltrators enough apparent opportunity to expose themselves.[9]
Valeria's religious instincts remained as aggressive as ever. When Victor's colossal Dragon form became visible on a scale that shocked the world, Valeria immediately recognized the event as an opportunity to spread her Master's faith into territories dominated by other Gods. She ordered her followers to mobilize and preach the God Emperor's influence wherever traditional Divine authorities had weakened.[7]
Although Valeria privately viewed worship of weaker Gods as irrational when Victor demonstrably existed, she understood the importance of public relations. The official Blood God Religion maintained rules requiring its followers to respect others rather than openly insult foreign religions, while Valeria kept her own contempt for weaker deities primarily within the religion's upper circles.[7]
At the same time, Victor prepared the greatest reward he had ever given her.
Valeria had never demanded Vanessa's resurrection. Her daughter had been dead for years, and restoring a destroyed Human Soul was far beyond what most supernatural Beings could accomplish. Victor nevertheless remembered Valeria's service and decided that loyalty of such quality deserved repayment.[13][6]
Victor negotiated with [[The Judges of The Abyss]], exchanging one thousand supernatural Souls to recover the Positive and Negative components necessary for Vanessa's restoration. Using his authority over Souls and the information preserving Vanessa's previous existence, Victor reconstructed her identity and brought her back to Life.[6]
He then summoned Valeria into the Paradise he had created for his faithful. Valeria arrived expecting another assignment and initially assumed the blonde teenager beside Victor might be one of his daughters. The instant she heard the girl's voice, her composure began collapsing. When Victor addressed the girl as Vanessa, Valeria lost the strength in her body and fell to her knees.[7]
Vanessa recognized her mother and embraced her. Valeria, who had survived years of grief by burying the pain beneath work, discipline and devotion, finally broke down completely and cried while holding her daughter. Victor remained nearby and allowed mother and daughter to reunite without interference.[7]
Valeria was subsequently permitted to abandon her responsibilities temporarily and spend approximately seven days with Vanessa inside a house prepared for them in Paradise. Victor personally assumed a portion of the administrative work normally handled by his High Priestess during her absence, giving him direct experience with just how much responsibility Valeria carried on his behalf.[14][15]
The resurrection permanently reinforced the ideology upon which Valeria had built the Blood God Religion: Victor remembered both service and betrayal. Valeria had never demanded payment, yet he remembered her loyalty years later and returned something that should have been impossible to restore.[6][15]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Valeria remained alive and active after the enormous temporal development of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. More than one hundred thousand internal years after the early era of the [[Blood God Religion]], she still occupied the position of High Priestess and remained the highest religious administrator beneath [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[5]
By this period the religion had evolved far beyond its original Human temples. Victor's Empire possessed several formal religious and combat traditions drawing power from the Gods incorporated into its civilization.
The most prominent included the Imperial Onmyō practitioners, whose patronage was strongly associated with [[Mizuki Elderblood]] and [[Amaterasu]] Elderblood, and the reorganized Imperial Inquisition, whose members invoked Victor and the Gods of [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]] through Faith and Imperial Energy.[5]
[[Frederick Winter]] became the most famous representative of the Onmyō practitioners, while [[Andrew Walter]] occupied a similarly prestigious position within the Imperial Inquisition. Despite their immense power and religious importance, both institutions ultimately remained subordinate to Valeria Alekerth, confirming that the Human woman once chosen from an underground cult had remained Victor's supreme priestly authority throughout the entire development of his Galactic civilization.[5]

### Skills & Abilities
Valeria began as an ordinary Human woman with no indication of exceptional combat ability. Nearly all of her supernatural capabilities originate from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s direct contract, his training, the Divine Artifact created for her and the institutional authority granted through her position as High Priestess.

#### Victor's Contract and Blessing
- Enhanced Human Physiology: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s contract physically reconstructed and strengthened Valeria. Her body became younger, stronger and capable of supporting substantially more supernatural Energy than an ordinary Human.[3]
- Enhanced Strength and Physical Condition: Valeria explicitly identifies possessing a stronger body as one of the Powers Victor granted her following the contract.[4]
- Rejuvenation: The contract reversed much of Valeria's visible aging, transforming her original mature appearance into a younger and healthier form.[3]
- Perpetual Youth: Valeria uses Victor's Energy to maintain her youth indefinitely. Victor specifically clarified that this does not make her absolutely immortal; she simply will not naturally continue aging while the contract and Energy supply remain functional.[3][4]
- Extended Lifespan: Because her youth is maintained supernaturally rather than biologically, Valeria can theoretically continue living far beyond an ordinary Human lifespan so long as the conditions sustaining her contract remain intact.[3]
- Supernatural Energy Access: The pact links Valeria directly to Victor's Energy for specific contract functions, most explicitly the maintenance of her youthful body.[3]
- Runic Contract Mark: A supernatural Rune appeared within Valeria's eyes when Victor completed the pact, marking her successful contract with the King of Hell.[3]
- Demon Summoning: One of the most extraordinary benefits of Valeria's contract is the ability to summon Demons subordinated to Victor as King of Hell.[3]
- Broad Summoning Authority: Because the pact connects Valeria directly to Victor, she can theoretically call upon any Demon beneath his authority as long as she possesses enough Energy to support the summoning.[4]
- Army Summoning Potential: Ruby immediately recognized that this authority potentially gave Valeria access to entire Demonic armies rather than merely individual low-ranking Demons.[4]
- Specialized Summoning: The type of Demon summoned can be influenced by Valeria's intent and the task she wants performed. When she focused heavily on logistical problems, messenger-type Demons answered her summons.[4]
- Connection to Victor: Valeria's position and contract establish a supernatural link between disciple and God.
- Remote Communication: Victor can directly contact Valeria mentally across distance, demonstrated when he summoned her during the period surrounding Vanessa's resurrection.[7]
- Devotion-Based Contract: The pact is explicitly tied to Valeria's genuine devotion. Victor defined the loss of that devotion as the condition capable of breaking their contract.[3]

#### Death Staff
Valeria's primary supernatural weapon is the [[Death Staff]], a Divine Artifact forged through the combined efforts of [[Victor Elderblood]], [[Thanatos]] and [[Hephaestus]]. It is a black staff decorated with golden elements and a floating violet gem.[8]
- Death Authority Channeling: The Staff contains a fraction of the power of Death, allowing a mortal such as Valeria to interact with phenomena far beyond normal Human capabilities.[8]
- Soul Perception: [[True Death|The Death]] Staff allows Valeria to perceive Souls and spiritual phenomena that ordinary Humans cannot naturally observe.[8]
- Soul Interaction: Valeria can interact with Souls through the Staff rather than merely perceive them.[8]
- Communication with the Dead: The Staff can function as a medium through which Valeria communicates with deceased individuals and other spiritual existences.[8]
- Merciful Death / Soul Transfer: The Artifact can bring death to members of the [[Blood God Religion]] and direct their Souls toward Victor's spiritual authority rather than leaving them to ordinary post-mortem processes.[8]
- Power Amplification: Merely taking hold of the Staff causes supernatural Power to surge through Valeria's body, demonstrating that the Artifact also functions as a direct amplifier and channel for her supernatural abilities.[9]
- Spiritual Administration: The Staff's abilities made Valeria an important intermediary between Victor's religion and the dead, contributing to her determination to spread his influence into spiritual territories such as the Shadow World.[8]

#### Religious Authority
- Supreme Priesthood: Valeria possesses the highest mortal religious office beneath [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and speaks publicly as his chosen representative.[3]
- Institutional Authority: Her authority extends over Archbishops, Acolytes and the organized personnel of the [[Blood God Religion]].[4]
- Imperial Religious Command: By the later [[Elderblood Empire]], major religious combat traditions such as the Imperial Inquisition and Imperial Onmyō practitioners ultimately fall beneath Valeria's religious administration.[5]
- Religious Influence: Victor's direct appointment makes Valeria's words carry his institutional authority among the faithful. Her position allows her to coordinate mass movements, missions and religious expansion across enormous territories.[4][7]

#### Leadership & Mental Abilities
- Exceptional Leadership: Valeria can command large groups of highly devoted followers and rapidly mobilize them toward a unified objective. Her commands immediately restored organization among believers shocked by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s appearance.[7]
- Administrative Expertise: She manages the logistical, political and religious responsibilities of a supernatural institution spanning numerous species and territories.[4][15]
- Delegation: Valeria does not attempt to personally perform every task. She distributes responsibilities to Archbishops and specialized subordinates while maintaining overall strategic control.[7]
- Rapid Learning: Victor personally subjected Valeria to an intensive seven-day educational period and expected that short period to fundamentally transform her capabilities. Her contract and determination allowed her to absorb an enormous amount of supernatural information in a very short time.[4][12]
- Supernatural Knowledge: Valeria was given access to ancient records belonging to the Snow, Scarlett and Fulger Clans and educated in the history, cultures and political structures of supernatural society.[4]
- Strategic Prudence: Valeria understands that survival takes priority when confronting an overwhelmingly superior enemy. She follows Victor's principle of retreating, informing stronger allies and preparing retaliation rather than dying meaninglessly.[4]
- Charisma: Valeria's authority and conviction allow her to inspire large groups of believers into immediate action, even during situations of widespread confusion or panic.[7]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is Valeria's God, Master, benefactor and the central figure around whom she structures her life. Her devotion began before they personally established their teacher-disciple relationship, when she worshipped him as the Blood God associated with vengeance against those who harmed the innocent.[2]
Victor's decision to appoint Valeria High Priestess completely changed her existence. He chose her specifically because her devotion coexisted with humility and independent thought, traits he considered more valuable than simple fanaticism. He subsequently established a direct two-way contract with her and personally spent seven days educating her.[3][4]
Valeria views Victor with absolute reverence, but Victor repeatedly discouraged her from reducing herself to a mindless servant. He told her directly that she was not his puppet or tool and that because she had offered him her devotion, he would repay that trust by transforming her into the best possible version of herself.[4]
His greatest repayment came with Vanessa's resurrection. Valeria had never demanded that Victor restore her daughter, but Victor remembered the tragedy that had driven her into his religion and eventually accomplished what Valeria believed no one could do. The event deepened a relationship already based upon nearly absolute loyalty.[6][7]
Victor later openly included the disciple managing his religion among the people he considered his, further establishing Valeria as part of his personal sphere rather than merely an employee administering an independent church.[1]

#### Vanessa Alekerth
[[Vanessa Alekerth]] is Valeria's daughter and the most emotionally important person in her life. Vanessa was sixteen when [[Jonathan Eric Gonzales]] raped, murdered and dismembered her, an atrocity that permanently changed Valeria's life and became one of the major reasons she embraced [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s religion of Blood, Murder and Vengeance.[2]
Valeria personally sacrificed Jonathan in Victor's name and spent years believing Vanessa was permanently gone.[2]
Victor later reconstructed Vanessa as a reward for Valeria's unwavering loyalty. The reunion destroyed Valeria's usual emotional restraint; she collapsed to her knees merely upon realizing who the girl beside Victor was and openly wept while embracing Vanessa.[6][7]
Victor subsequently gave mother and daughter seven uninterrupted days together in Paradise.[15]

#### Rena
Rena is one of the senior figures operating beneath Valeria within the [[Blood God Religion]]. She was already an Archbishop during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s formal assumption of responsibility over the religion and later continued serving within its hierarchy.[3]

#### Lizbet Greygrave
[[Lizbet Greygrave]] is another important religious subordinate associated with Valeria's administration. As the religion expanded to include numerous supernatural races, individuals such as Lizbet allowed Valeria's command structure to extend beyond ordinary Humans.
