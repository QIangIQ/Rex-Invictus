Siddhartha Gautama, more commonly known as the Buddha, is an ancient Human-born God, founder of Buddhism and the Divine ruler associated with the [[Buddhist Pantheon]]. Unlike many of Earth's ancient Gods who originated as members of naturally Divine races, Buddha began as a mortal Human and followed a path of spiritual development that eventually placed him among the greatest Divine authorities connected to Earth.
The defining aspect of Buddha's Divinity is The Journey, a Concept intrinsically connected to self-discovery, spiritual progress and the path by which an individual comes to understand their own existence. Hestia Elderblood specifically identifies The Journey as the strongest Aspect of Buddha's Divinity and recognizes his philosophy as unusually effective at assisting mortals attempting to reach Enlightenment and eventually Divinity.[1]
Buddha's most important confirmed action in the story occurred long before the present era through his relationship with [[Hassan-i-Sabbah]]. Hassan was originally Human and received a Divine Blessing from Buddha. Rather than directly giving him the answers required for ascension, Buddha repeatedly pushed Hassan toward discovering his own Truth, appearing only at crucial moments when the Human strayed from his pilgrimage. This guidance eventually helped Hassan complete an extraordinarily rapid ascent from Human to a High-Level God in roughly one thousand years, something that shocked even an ancient Goddess such as Hestia.[2][1]
Although Buddha has never physically appeared in the present narrative, he is repeatedly treated as a major figure within Earth's Divine history. Buddhism also possesses one of Earth's largest religious populations, giving its associated supernatural structures enormous scale; the Buddhist Hell is explicitly described as one of the largest Hells on Earth alongside [[Biblical Hell]].[3]

### Physical Appearance
Buddha has not physically appeared within the main narrative, and the novel has not provided a detailed canonical description of his present Divine appearance. His hair, eyes, height, clothing and other visual characteristics are therefore currently unknown within MTW canon.
The artwork used for the article should consequently be regarded as an artistic interpretation rather than evidence of his canonical design.
What is known is that Buddha originated as a mortal Human before reaching Divinity. [[Hestia]] refers to him as the Ancient Prince of the Sakia Dynasty, Siddhartha Gautama, confirming that the Divine figure worshipped as Buddha is the same Human individual who began the philosophy that later became Buddhism rather than an unrelated God who merely inherited the title.[1]

### Personality
Buddha is portrayed indirectly as an extraordinarily eccentric, patient, mysterious and philosophical individual. Much of what is known about his behavior comes from Hassan's description of the God who guided him toward Enlightenment rather than from a direct appearance.[1]
According to Hassan, Buddha almost never explained himself. During Hassan's entire pilgrimage, the God spoke to him directly only twice. On both occasions, he refused to provide a ready-made answer and instead instructed Hassan to search for his own Truth. At other times Buddha simply appeared silently when Hassan began deviating from the path he needed to follow, allowing his presence alone to function as a correction.[1]
This method reveals a defining element of Buddha's mentality: he does not appear interested in manufacturing disciples who mechanically repeat his own conclusions. His guidance is designed to make the individual discover themselves. Hassan's eventual Divinity was therefore not a collection of powers handed to him by Buddha; it emerged from Hassan understanding his own identity, beliefs and purpose through the path Buddha encouraged him to walk.[1]
[[Hestia]] immediately recognized the philosophy behind Hassan's story. The combination of pilgrimage, the pursuit of personal Truth and spiritual development reminded her specifically of Buddha, suggesting that his unusual approach is sufficiently famous among ancient Gods to be recognizable from methodology alone.[1]
Buddha also appears to possess considerable restraint. Despite having enough authority to Bless a Human and influence his development toward Godhood, he did not simply force Hassan into a predetermined form. The Journey could assist progression, but Hestia emphasized that there are no shortcuts to Divinity; the person still has to understand themselves and complete the process through their own effort.[1]
This makes Buddha fundamentally different from Divine beings who attempt to monopolize followers by dictating every aspect of their lives. His strongest Concept appears to function less as an answer and more as a path.

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Buddha himself does not directly appear during the Demonic War, but the supernatural structures created around Buddhism reveal how enormous his religious legacy had become. While discussing the great infernal dimensions connected to Earth, the Buddhist Hell was identified alongside [[Biblical Hell]] as one of the largest Hells associated with the planet. The reason given was straightforward: Buddhism and Christianity possessed two of the largest populations of mortal believers, producing correspondingly enormous spiritual systems associated with their religions.[3]
The Buddhist afterlife was governed separately from Buddha's position as the principal Divine figure of Buddhism. [[Enma]], identified as the Buddhist Hell King, exercised authority over the Buddhist Hell. This distinction is important because the mortal religion, its celestial Divine authorities and the rulers responsible for its infernal afterlife did not necessarily operate as a single individual hierarchy.
During [[Diablo]]'s wider campaign, Enma was counted among the remaining supernatural rulers connected to Diablo's alliances, together with the Ancient Chinese faction and the Hindu Hell King Yama. However, by the later stages of the conflict Enma was occupied restructuring his own Hell and was considered unlikely to provide Diablo with meaningful immediate assistance.[4]
Nothing in the presently verified chapters establishes that Buddha personally joined Diablo, personally fought during the Demonic War or ordered Enma's decisions. Older summaries that treat the actions of the Buddhist Hell King as direct actions by Buddha therefore conflate two different authorities within the Buddhist supernatural system.

#### King Among Kings
Buddha became directly relevant to the narrative through [[Hassan-i-Sabbah]]. While Hassan trained members of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction, his extraordinary Divine development attracted attention because he had achieved something considered almost absurd by normal Divine standards. Hassan had been born Human, yet within approximately one thousand years he had progressed far enough to become a High-Level God.[2][1]
For comparison, [[Hestia]] explained that even Gods born as Divine beings could spend vastly longer periods attempting to reach the same level. Hassan's development was therefore unusual enough that Hestia immediately wanted to understand what had allowed a former Human to progress so quickly.[1]
Hassan explained that he had once encountered the original master whose title he eventually inherited and that his own path ultimately brought him into contact with an eccentric God. That God Blessed Hassan, but the Blessing did not instantly transform him into a deity. Instead, Hassan was placed upon a long pilgrimage of personal and spiritual development.[1]
The God spoke directly to Hassan only twice. His principal instruction was for Hassan to search for the Truth—specifically, Hassan's own Truth. During the remainder of the pilgrimage, the God normally remained silent and only manifested when Hassan began straying away from the path of self-discovery.[1]
Hestia recognized the description almost immediately. The emphasis on pilgrimage, individual Truth and spiritual understanding led her to identify the mysterious God as Buddha. She then explained why Buddha's involvement had been so effective.[1]
According to Hestia, a mortal who wishes to naturally ascend toward Divinity must undertake a profound journey of self-discovery. Questions that ordinary people can spend their entire lives avoiding become fundamental to the ascension process: Who am I? What is my purpose? Where am I going? What do I truly want? What is the meaning of my existence?[1]
When an individual truly resolves these questions and reaches the necessary level of self-understanding, the distinction between the physical body and Soul begins changing. The Soul and body increasingly merge, the physical body becomes spiritualized and the Soul effectively becomes the individual's true body. This is one of the natural paths through which a mortal can become a God.[1]
Ascension is not the end of that process. Once an individual becomes Divine, they must continue understanding themselves and the Concepts connected to their existence. Their Divine development therefore remains another Journey, only operating at a higher level.[1]
Buddha's philosophy was uniquely well suited to this process. Hestia directly stated that the philosophy created by the Ancient Prince Siddhartha Gautama greatly assists beings attempting to attain Divinity because its fundamental principles naturally encourage the kind of introspection and self-realization required for spiritual evolution.[1]
This does not mean that every Buddhist automatically becomes a God. Hestia explicitly rejected such an interpretation. There are effectively infinite paths toward Enlightenment, and simply following an existing doctrine without truly understanding oneself does not complete the process.[1]
Hassan's case succeeded because his own personality, Faith and experiences allowed him to use Buddha's guidance effectively. His strongest Divinity ultimately became Faith, accompanied by other authorities such as Military and Truth. Buddha did not copy his own Divinities into Hassan; he helped Hassan discover the Concepts that genuinely belonged to Hassan himself.[2][1]
When Ruby questioned why Buddha's assistance had accelerated the process so dramatically, Hestia revealed the crucial detail: the strongest Aspect of Buddha's own Divinity was The Journey.[1]
The Journey could aid a being's progression through self-discovery and Enlightenment. It did not eliminate the work required, nor did it automatically grant Divinity. Instead, it made Buddha uniquely suited to guiding those already attempting to understand themselves, helping them remain on a path capable of producing genuine spiritual development.[1]
Hassan consequently became one of the clearest demonstrations of Buddha's Divine philosophy in practice. A Human who might otherwise have required vastly longer periods—or never succeeded at all—became a High-Level God in around a millennium while still developing Divinities genuinely reflective of his own existence rather than becoming a copy of his Divine teacher.[1]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
As [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s understanding of Earth's Divine hierarchy expanded, the importance of [[God-Kings]] became increasingly distinct from the ordinary classification of Gods. The original supernatural order of Earth contained a limited number of major Pantheon rulers whose authority extended over entire religious civilizations rather than merely individual Divine domains.
Later cosmological discussion identified seven High-Level God-Kings as part of Earth's old supernatural structure.[5] Buddha's position as the God-King associated with Buddhism places him within this ancient class of rulers alongside the principal leaders of Earth's other Great Pantheons.
His importance does not depend only upon personal combat strength. Buddhism's enormous mortal following, the size of the Buddhist afterlife and the effectiveness of his philosophy in facilitating spiritual development make Buddha one of Earth's most influential Gods even without a direct combat appearance.[3][1]

### Skills & Abilities
Buddha has never directly participated in an on-page battle, meaning his complete combat arsenal remains unknown. His confirmed abilities instead revolve around Divinity, Enlightenment, spiritual development and the Concept of The Journey. Claims based exclusively upon real-world Buddhist traditions should not be treated as MTW abilities unless the novel explicitly establishes them.

#### Divine Physiology
- God Physiology: Buddha is an ancient Human-born God who reached the highest political class of Earth's Divine civilization as a God-King. As a Divine being, his existence possesses the supernatural spiritual foundation characteristic of Gods.
- [[Divine Energy]]: Buddha possesses Divine Energy appropriate to his status as an ancient God.
- Divine Soul: His mortal existence underwent the spiritual development necessary for true Divinity, resulting in a state where the distinction between body and Soul no longer follows ordinary Human limitations.[1]
- [[Immortality]]/Longevity: Buddha has existed for more than two millennia without suffering ordinary Human aging.
- Spiritual Body: [[Hestia]]'s explanation of natural ascension states that the process causes body and Soul to merge until the Soul effectively becomes the individual's true body. Buddha's Human origin and Divine state make him an example of the class of beings produced through such spiritual ascension, although the exact historical steps of his own transformation have not been shown directly.[1]

#### The Journey
The Journey is the strongest confirmed Aspect of Buddha's Divinity and the most important supernatural authority associated with him in the novel.[1]
- Journey Divinity: Buddha possesses Divine authority over the Concept of The Journey, particularly journeys involving personal development, self-discovery and spiritual progress.[1]
- Self-Discovery Facilitation: The Journey can assist individuals attempting to understand their own identity, purpose and direction.[1]
- Spiritual Progression Assistance: Buddha's Concept can make the process of spiritual development more effective for someone already walking a legitimate path toward Enlightenment.[1]
- Enlightenment Guidance: His philosophy and Divinity can guide individuals toward the realizations required to reach Enlightenment without simply placing the answers inside their minds.[1]
- Ascension Assistance: The Journey can indirectly facilitate the natural transformation of a mortal into a God by helping them advance through the self-discovery required for Divine ascension.[1]
- Pilgrimage Guidance: Buddha monitored Hassan's spiritual pilgrimage and appeared when Hassan deviated significantly enough to require correction.[1]
- Path Correction: Rather than continuously controlling Hassan, Buddha intervened selectively when his disciple began straying away from the Journey he needed to complete.[1]
- Individualized Development: Buddha's method does not force disciples to inherit his own Concepts. Hassan emerged with Faith, Military and Truth because those Divinities belonged to Hassan's identity, demonstrating that Buddha guides the discovery of an individual's personal path rather than creating replicas of himself.[2][1]
- Conditional Acceleration: The Journey can greatly accelerate spiritual development under the right circumstances, but it does not bypass the requirement for genuine understanding. Hassan's extraordinary thousand-year rise demonstrates its effectiveness, while [[Hestia]] explicitly states that no true shortcut to Divinity exists.[1]

#### Blessing
- Divine Blessing Bestowal: Buddha personally Blessed [[Hassan-i-Sabbah]] while Hassan was still Human.[1]
- Long-Term Spiritual Influence: The effects of Buddha's guidance continued across Hassan's extended pilgrimage rather than functioning as a temporary combat enhancement.
- Developmental Blessing: The confirmed result of Buddha's involvement was not an immediate increase in destructive power but the creation of conditions under which Hassan could develop himself toward Enlightenment and Godhood.[1]
The exact mechanical properties of Buddha's Blessing have not been fully described. It should therefore not automatically be assigned powers such as absolute protection, resurrection, fate manipulation or direct power transfer without additional chapter evidence.

#### Enlightenment and Spiritual Knowledge
- Enlightenment: Buddha possesses profound understanding of the spiritual state required for mortals to transcend their original limitations.
- Self-Knowledge: His philosophy centers upon understanding the self rather than obtaining external power alone.
- Spiritual Philosophy: The philosophy created by Siddhartha Gautama is explicitly recognized by [[Hestia]] as unusually effective at helping beings move toward Divinity.[1]
- Teaching: Buddha can guide others without directly revealing the conclusions they need to reach, allowing their development to remain genuinely their own.
- Truth Guidance: His principal instruction to Hassan was to seek his own Truth, emphasizing that an individual's path to Divinity must reflect their own existence.[1]
- Conceptual Understanding: As an ancient God-King whose primary Divinity is linked to spiritual progression itself, Buddha possesses extensive knowledge concerning the relationship between Souls, identity, Concepts and ascension.

#### God-King Authority
- God-King Status: Buddha stands as the principal Divine ruler associated with the [[Buddhist Pantheon]] and belongs to the class of beings who ruled Earth's major Pantheons.
- Religious Authority: As founder of Buddhism and its central Divine figure, Buddha possesses enormous spiritual importance to a religion with one of Earth's largest populations of believers.[3]
- Massive Faith Base: Buddhism's global population contributes to a supernatural infrastructure enormous enough for the Buddhist Hell to stand among Earth's largest afterlife dimensions.[3]
- Pantheon Influence: Buddha's teachings continued shaping mortal and Divine development thousands of years after his original Human life.

#### Limitations
- The Journey Is Not an Instant Ascension: Buddha cannot simply turn every follower into a God. [[Hestia]] explicitly states that no shortcut can replace genuine self-discovery.[1]
- Individual Compatibility: The success of The Journey depends strongly upon the individual walking it. The same philosophy does not produce identical results for every practitioner.[1]
- No Automatic Concept Granting: Buddha's disciples do not necessarily receive Buddha's Divinities. Hassan developed Faith, Military and Truth rather than The Journey itself.[2][1]
- Unknown Combat Arsenal: Buddha has not fought on-page, so specific offensive techniques, defenses, weapons and destructive feats remain unconfirmed.
- Unconfirmed Secondary Divinities: Older versions of this article listed Faith, Energy, Mindfulness, Concentration and Wisdom as Buddha's personal Divinities. The currently verified novel material does not explicitly confirm this set. Chapter 792 directly identifies The Journey as the strongest Aspect of Buddha's Divinity, while Hassan—not Buddha—is explicitly established as possessing Faith as his strongest Divinity.[2][1]

### Relationships

#### Hassan-i-Sabbah
[[Hassan-i-Sabbah]] is the most important character directly connected to Buddha within the current narrative. Hassan was born Human and eventually encountered an eccentric God who Blessed him and placed him upon a pilgrimage toward his own Truth.[1]
Buddha deliberately avoided becoming a conventional teacher who dictated every answer. He spoke to Hassan only twice, repeatedly directing him toward self-discovery rather than dependence. When Hassan drifted too far away from the necessary path, Buddha would appear without explanation, allowing his presence to function as a warning that Hassan needed to reconsider his direction.[1]
The method proved extraordinarily effective. Hassan eventually reached Enlightenment and ascended into a High-Level God in approximately one thousand years, developing Divinities centered upon Faith, Military and Truth. [[Hestia]] considered that rate of development exceptional even compared with naturally born Gods.[2][1]
Hassan therefore serves as the clearest demonstration of The Journey's practical value. Buddha did not make Hassan powerful by simply handing him Divine authority; he guided him toward becoming the kind of existence capable of creating and understanding his own Divinity.

#### Hestia Elderblood
[[Hestia]] Elderblood has not been shown directly interacting with Buddha in the present story, but she possesses enough knowledge of him to recognize his methods almost immediately.
When Hassan described the strange God who appeared during his pilgrimage, Hestia connected the philosophy, the search for personal Truth and the nature of the pilgrimage to Buddha before Hassan needed to identify him directly.[1]
Hestia also understood the nature of Buddha's strongest Divinity and explained The Journey to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family, indicating that Buddha's Concept and philosophical path were known among ancient Divine beings.[1]

#### Enma
[[Enma]] is the ruler associated with the Buddhist Hell. Although both exist within supernatural structures generated around Buddhism, Enma and Buddha should not be treated as the same authority.
Buddha is associated with the Divine philosophy and Pantheon of Buddhism, while Enma governs its infernal afterlife. During the Demonic War, Enma was involved in [[Diablo]]'s wider alliance and later became occupied with restructuring his Hell.[4]
No verified chapter currently establishes that Buddha personally ordered Enma to assist Diablo or that the two shared the same political position during the war.

#### Buddhist Followers
Buddhism eventually became one of the largest mortal religions on Earth. The sheer number of believers was sufficient for its associated Hell to become one of the largest afterlife dimensions on the planet, comparable in scale to [[Biblical Hell]].[3]
Buddha's relationship with those followers should not be confused with the personal relationship he had with Hassan. Most believers follow the philosophy and religion associated with Buddha; they are not individually confirmed to have received a direct Blessing or personal instruction from him.

### Religious and Divine Legacy
Buddha's greatest demonstrated legacy is not a weapon, conquest or military victory, but a philosophy that interacts unusually well with the actual mechanics of Divine ascension in MTW.
[[Hestia]] explains that becoming a God naturally requires an individual to understand themselves at increasingly fundamental levels. The Soul and physical existence must eventually cease functioning as two loosely connected components and become a unified spiritual existence. After ascension, growth continues through increasingly deep understanding of one's Concepts.[1]
Buddhism, as established by Siddhartha Gautama, naturally encourages many of the mental and spiritual processes necessary for that progression. This does not make Buddhism a guaranteed God-production system; rather, it provides a philosophical structure particularly compatible with the requirements of self-discovery.[1]
Buddha's own Divinity magnifies this compatibility. The Journey allows him to assist others in moving through the spiritual process without invalidating the essential requirement that the individual discover their own answers.
Hassan's ascension is consequently one of the most important pieces of evidence for Buddha's power. Rather than merely creating a follower who worshipped him more intensely, Buddha helped produce an independent High-Level God with his own Truth and Divinities.
