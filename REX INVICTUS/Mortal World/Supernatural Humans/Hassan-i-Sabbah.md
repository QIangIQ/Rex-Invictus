Hassan-i-Sabbah is a Human-born Ascended God, a High-Level God whose strongest Concept is Faith, the successor to the original Hassan-i-Sabbah, and the longtime master of [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]].[3][2][1] Originally a mortal Human, Hassan followed an extraordinary path of self-discovery until he attained Divinity and subsequently developed far enough to become a High-Level God, an achievement described as exceptionally difficult even for beings who are born Divine.[1]
Hassan inherited the name Hassan-i-Sabbah from his master approximately a thousand years before his introduction. He eventually surpassed the level his predecessor had reached, declaring that through the inherited name and the path he followed afterward, he attained a height even his master had never achieved.[2] Despite being associated with the historical Hassan-i-Sabbah, the current Hassan is not the original [[Abe-no-Seimei|Old Man]] of the Mountain; he was already an adult when he encountered the man who later became his teacher.[1]
At some point during his mortal pilgrimage, Hassan received a Blessing from [[Buddha]]. The blessing was connected to Buddha's Divine Aspect known as The Journey, an authority that assists a person in the process of self-discovery rather than simply granting Divinity without effort. Through his own Faith, experiences, hardship, enlightenment and Buddha's guidance, Hassan eventually awakened as a God and continued developing until reaching the High-Level God range.[1]
Hassan later became the master of Tasha Fenrir Elderblood, teaching her assassination, espionage, emotional discipline, self-control and the philosophy that would eventually underpin much of her rule over the hidden side of Samar.[3][4] He lived in seclusion under an agreement with Tasha until [[Victor Elderblood]] acquired his services for one year and brought him to [[Nightingale]] to train members of his family.[5]
Hassan initially questioned what he could possibly teach a household already containing elite martial artists, assassins, ancient warriors and Dragons. After examining Victor's family, however, he identified what he believed most of them lacked: Faith in themselves and their own existence. He consequently chose not to teach them another fighting style, but the Philosophy of Self-Discovery, intending to strengthen the foundations of their Souls and guide their future paths toward Divinity and greater understanding of themselves.[2]

### Physical Appearance
Hassan's complete physical appearance has not yet been described in sufficient detail to establish his height, hair color, eye color or exact clothing.
During his first direct appearance, Hassan emerged as a shadow behind Tasha, reflecting his extraordinary ability to remain concealed even while operating near powerful supernatural beings.[3] Whether this shadow-like presentation was an innate supernatural transformation or simply the result of his concealment techniques has not been fully explained.
When Hassan later revealed his Divine status before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family, a powerful golden aura erupted from his body and spread through the surrounding area. The manifestation immediately allowed Agnes to recognize him as a High-Level God.[2]

### Personality
Hassan is calm, emotionally disciplined, analytical, private and exceptionally difficult to provoke.
His emotional control is one of the defining characteristics of both his personality and his teachings. During his first meeting with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family, Hassan remained almost completely expressionless despite being surrounded by some of the most dangerous women in [[Nightingale]]. The principal exception occurred when he learned that Victor already possessed daughters, information significant enough to momentarily break his otherwise neutral composure.[2]
Hassan deliberately refuses to allow intense emotions to control his judgment. He later taught the same principle to Tasha, instructing her not to suppress her feelings entirely but never to become enslaved by them. According to Hassan, an effective assassin must be capable of acknowledging emotional turmoil while preventing it from reducing lethality or distorting rational judgment.[4]
His teachings emphasize personal responsibility. Hassan's interpretation of the maxim "Nothing is True, Everything is permitted" is not permission for meaningless violence or amorality. Rather, it is an observation about the instability of civilization and the consequences of individual choice.[4]
For Hassan:
- Nothing is True means understanding that the foundations upon which societies are built are fragile and that individuals must ultimately become the shepherds of the civilizations they construct.[4]
- Everything is permitted means that individuals are the architects of their own actions and must accept the consequences of those actions, whether glorious or tragic.[4]
- Failure should not be endlessly blamed upon others. A person must understand their mistakes, learn from them and evolve so that the same mistakes are not repeated.[4]
This philosophy strongly influenced Tasha and became one of the mental tools she relied upon when dealing with [[Volk Fenrir]]'s betrayal.[4]
Hassan is also highly observant. During his first meeting with Victor's family, he rapidly assessed everyone present and immediately recognized [[Zaladrac Zeovnur Elderblood]] and [[Jeanne Elderblood]] as the two women who produced the greatest sense of danger.[2]
Despite his extraordinary Divine status, Hassan is not driven by ego. When Violet asked what he could possibly teach women already surrounded by several of the world's greatest martial and assassination instructors, Hassan admitted that he had asked himself the same question. Rather than becoming offended by Violet's challenge, he treated it as legitimate and considered Victor's reason for choosing him carefully.[2]
He is equally private regarding his own past. When Victor's family questioned him about the God who had Blessed him and the price he paid, Hassan provided only limited answers. Once the questions became too personal, he calmly refused to reveal more.[1]
Hassan is a harsh instructor. When asked to train Ophis and [[Nero]], his initial objection was not that he lacked the ability, but that his methods would inevitably cause pain, injury and mental hardship. He assumed their family would object to such treatment, only to discover that Victor's household already considered severe training completely normal.[2]

### History

#### The Successor of Hassan-i-Sabbah
The man currently known as Hassan-i-Sabbah was originally an ordinary Human.
At some point while already an adult, he encountered the original Hassan-i-Sabbah, the legendary [[Abe-no-Seimei|Old Man]] of the Mountain, and became his disciple.[1]
He later inherited his master's name and legacy.
Approximately one thousand years before his appearance in [[Nightingale]], Hassan formally became the successor to that identity. However, rather than merely reproducing the achievements of his predecessor, he continued developing beyond them.[2]
By his own admission:
"I inherited this name from my Master a thousand years ago, and through it, I reached a height that even he couldn't attain."[2]
The exact details of his early training under the First Hassan have not been fully revealed, but his later mastery of assassination, espionage, concealment, psychological discipline and information warfare reflects the tradition he inherited.

#### The Journey
During his life as a Human, Hassan encountered an eccentric God who placed him upon a far more extraordinary path.
The God rarely spoke to him. According to Hassan, the Being directly addressed him only twice, and on both occasions told him to search for Truth—specifically his own Truth.[1]
On other occasions, the God merely appeared without speaking, usually when Hassan had begun drifting away from the path of his pilgrimage.[1]
[[Hestia]] Elderblood recognized the description.
The God was [[Buddha]].[1]
Buddha Blessed Hassan through the Divine Aspect known as The Journey. Unlike a power that simply handed Hassan Divinity or strength, The Journey assisted his process of self-discovery. Hassan himself was still required to experience hardship, question his own existence, understand his purpose and develop the Faith necessary to awaken as a God.[1]
When asked what he paid in exchange for Buddha's Blessing, Hassan answered simply:
His Faith.[1]
The deeper meaning of this price remains unexplained, particularly because Faith later became Hassan's strongest Concept and the foundation of his Divinity.
Through his pilgrimage, Hassan eventually reached Enlightenment and transformed from a Human into a God.[1]
His growth did not stop there.
Over roughly the millennium associated with his identity as Hassan-i-Sabbah, he continued developing his Divinity until reaching the level of a High-Level God. Hestia considered this extraordinary because even natural-born Gods frequently struggle to reach such a level, while Hassan had begun as a mortal Human.[1]

#### Master of Tasha
At an unspecified point in the past, Hassan became the master of [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]].
Tasha was already an extraordinarily experienced Goddess and political ruler, but Hassan trained her in areas where she eventually became exceptional: assassination, espionage, covert operations, information gathering and emotional discipline.[3]
His teachings became the foundation of the methods Tasha later used to manage the dark side of Samar.
Rather than simply teaching her how to kill, Hassan taught Tasha how to observe, question appearances, search for hidden Truth, control emotional responses and act only after properly understanding the circumstances surrounding a target.[3][4]
Their relationship eventually developed into a long-term agreement.
Tasha gave Hassan a place where he could spend his extraordinarily long life in peace and seclusion. In return, Hassan agreed to assist her and help defend Samar whenever necessary.[3]

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Hassan made his first direct appearance while [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]] secretly monitored [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s political negotiations on Samar.
Tasha had already spent considerable effort attempting to detect Victor's hidden agents and surveillance network, but every method she used failed. Seeking a more experienced perspective, she apologized for disturbing Hassan's seclusion and requested his assessment.[3]
A shadow appeared behind Tasha.
The figure was Hassan-i-Sabbah—her master, a legendary assassin and spy, and the Human who had once trained a Goddess in the arts of assassination and espionage.[3]
Hassan reminded Tasha of the arrangement between them: she had provided him with somewhere peaceful to spend his long existence, while he helped protect her country when required.[3]
After examining the situation, Hassan admitted that even he could not detect Victor's hidden operatives.
Rather than concluding that they did not exist, Hassan recognized the opposite: if Victor had spies present and neither he nor Tasha could locate them, then the operatives were simply extraordinarily competent.[3]
Hassan also corrected Tasha's interpretation of Victor himself.
Tasha was so accustomed to politics, deception and hidden intentions that she continued searching for another layer behind Victor's behavior. Hassan advised her not to become trapped by appearances and to continue searching for the Truth.[3]
His assessment of Victor was unusually direct. Victor was confident, charismatic, overbearing and arrogant, but his arrogance did not blind him. Hassan believed that Tasha was overcomplicating the situation by assuming every truthful statement necessarily concealed another scheme.[3]
This encounter established Hassan as one of the few individuals Tasha consulted when her own intelligence experience failed to produce an answer.
Later, after Victor's negotiations on Samar progressed, he acquired Hassan's services for an entire year.[5]
Victor bluntly told Hassan that for that year he effectively belonged to him and would be used until exhausted. Once it became clear Victor meant training rather than literal enslavement, Hassan agreed under several conditions.[5]
Hassan would train whoever Victor selected, but he retained the right to return to Samar regularly and could permanently terminate the arrangement if an emergency threatened the country. Victor accepted these conditions and allowed Hassan to return to Samar approximately once per month during the contract.[5]
Hassan was consequently brought to [[Nightingale]].
Before being permitted near Victor's family, however, he underwent an exhaustive investigation conducted by Agnes and Natashia. Even after passing their investigation, he remained under continuous surveillance.[2]
Several Demons of Despair remained concealed around him, while women from the [[Blank Clan]] monitored his movements. [[Zaladrac Zeovnur Elderblood]] personally warned Hassan that any harmful action, wrong movement or dangerous thought toward Victor's family would result in his existence being erased.[2]
Hassan took the Dragon's warning completely seriously.
His first sight of Victor's family left even the emotionally disciplined assassin briefly speechless. He was astonished by the number of extraordinarily beautiful women Victor had married and even more surprised when he discovered that the Demon King already possessed daughters.[2]
Hassan's threat assessment immediately identified Zaladrac and [[Jeanne Elderblood]] as the most dangerous individuals present. Both produced an instinctive feeling of danger greater than the other women around him.[2]
Agnes introduced Hassan to the women he would be training and informed him that he could teach them however he considered appropriate.
When Ophis and [[Nero]] were presented, Hassan initially refused.
His objection was practical: his training methods were harsh, and he expected his students to suffer injuries and mental strain. He assumed Victor's family would interfere the moment the girls were hurt.[2]
Instead, the women made it clear that brutal training was already routine within their household.
Violet subsequently asked what Hassan could possibly teach them. Their family already possessed elite martial artists, ancient warriors, Dragons and accomplished assassins. Hassan himself admitted that he had wondered exactly the same thing since Victor recruited him.[2]
After observing the group, the answer became clear.
Faith.
Hassan realized that the principal weakness he could address was not another missing fighting technique, but the internal foundation of their identities.
Compared with Victor, most of the women were "lost" in this respect. The exceptions he identified were Zaladrac, Jeanne and [[Hestia]] Elderblood, whose understanding of themselves already met the standard he was evaluating.[2]
Hassan explained that Faith, in this context, was not simply religious worship.
It was conviction.
Belief in oneself.
Faith in one's own existence.[2]
According to Hassan, this belief nourishes the Soul and increases an individual's worth within existence. The path toward Divinity therefore requires more than collecting Energy or becoming physically powerful. It requires hardship, enlightenment, understanding and knowledge of oneself and the world around them.[2]
As Hassan spoke, a golden Divine aura erupted from his body.
Agnes immediately recognized the level of Power he had concealed:
A High-Level God.[2]
Hassan revealed that he had inherited the Hassan-i-Sabbah name from his master approximately one thousand years earlier and subsequently climbed higher than his predecessor ever had.
He then revealed the foundation of his Divine existence:
he attained Divinity through Faith.[2]
Rather than attempting to replace the martial training Victor's family already possessed, Hassan announced that he would teach them the Philosophy of Self-Discovery. His objective was simple: whatever paths his students eventually chose, by the end of his lessons they should understand themselves more deeply than they had before.[2]
Several hours later, Hassan began personally training Ophis and [[Nero Elderblood]].[1]
He began with fundamentals and subsequently had the sisters spar against one another while observing their individual strengths and weaknesses. His approach demonstrated that the Philosophy of Self-Discovery was not merely abstract discussion; Hassan also intended to understand each student practically before deciding how their development should proceed.[1]
While watching Hassan, Hestia identified the Concepts he had developed:
Military, Truth and Faith.
Of the three, Faith was his strongest Concept.[1]
The revelation prompted the women to reconsider Hassan's age and history. Natashia initially estimated that he must be approximately a thousand years old because the historical Hassan-i-Sabbah had lived around that general period.
Hassan corrected her.
Although he had indeed been the original Hassan's disciple, he was already an adult when he encountered his future master.[1]
He was therefore considerably older than their initial estimate suggested.
When Sasha asked what Race could possess such longevity, Hassan answered simply:
Human.[1]
Zaladrac immediately added the missing detail.
Hassan was a Human who had been Blessed by a God.[1]
Hassan confirmed that the price of the Blessing had been his Faith.
The God responsible had spoken to him directly only twice, repeatedly urging Hassan to search for his Truth. At other times, the Being appeared silently whenever Hassan began deviating from his pilgrimage.[1]
Hestia recognized the description as [[Buddha]].
She explained that Buddha's strongest Divine Aspect was The Journey. The ability did not simply transform mortals into Gods. Instead, it assisted them in the process of self-discovery necessary for genuine Divine ascension.[1]
Hassan had still been required to walk that path himself.
Through Faith, hardship and enlightenment, his understanding eventually became sufficient for his body and Soul to transcend their ordinary mortal state and awaken as a Divine existence. After attaining Godhood, his Journey continued through the discovery and development of the Concepts that defined him.[1]
Hassan's philosophy continued influencing Tasha even while he trained Victor's family.
When Tasha later discovered the full scale of Volk's betrayal, her emotional state nearly collapsed into rage and grief. At the critical moment, she remembered Hassan's teachings.[4]
He had taught her:
- Do not ignore intense feelings, but do not become their slave.
- Calm the mind.
- Conceal internal turmoil.
- Sharpen the mind.
- Never lose control.[4]
Tasha applied those lessons immediately, bringing her explosive emotions under control and transforming her rage into focused determination.
She also remembered Hassan's maxim:
“Nothing is True, Everything is permitted.”[4]
The lesson became central to the way she interpreted Volk's betrayal. Rather than allowing herself to remain trapped in grief or blame, Tasha accepted responsibility for her future decisions and began constructing the civilization and political order she intended to rule herself.

### Skills & Abilities
Ascended High-Level God Physiology: Hassan began life as a mortal Human and later achieved Divinity through self-discovery, Enlightenment and Faith, eventually reaching the rank of a High-Level God.[2][1] This achievement is particularly exceptional because natural-born Gods themselves frequently struggle to reach the same stage.
- [[Divine Energy]]: Hassan possesses Divine Energy corresponding to his status as a High-Level God. When he openly revealed his Divinity, his Power manifested as an intense golden aura that spread through the surrounding area.[2]
- Divine Soul: Hassan completed the mortal path toward Divinity, fundamentally transforming his existence from that of an ordinary Human into a genuine God.[1]
- Immense Longevity: Hassan has lived for well over a millennium. He had already reached adulthood before becoming the disciple of the original Hassan-i-Sabbah and subsequently continued living long enough to attain and develop Divine status.[1]

#### Concepts
Hassan possesses at least three explicitly identified Divine Concepts.[1]
- Faith: Hassan's strongest Concept and the foundation through which he attained Divinity.[2][1] Hassan defines Faith as conviction in oneself and one's own existence—a belief capable of nourishing the Soul and increasing an individual's existential worth.[2]
- Truth: One of Hassan's developed Concepts.[1] The search for personal Truth also formed a central part of the pilgrimage encouraged by [[Buddha]]'s Blessing.
- Military: One of Hassan's developed Concepts.[1] Its exact supernatural applications have not yet been shown.
The precise offensive, defensive or reality-altering applications of these Concepts have not been demonstrated in enough detail to assign additional abilities without speculation.

#### Buddha's Blessing
Hassan was Blessed by [[Buddha]] during his mortal life.[1]
- The Journey: Buddha's principal Divine Aspect assisted Hassan's path of self-discovery.[1] It did not simply grant Hassan Godhood; it supported a pilgrimage through which he still had to discover his own answers, understand himself and reach Enlightenment.
- Guidance toward Truth: Buddha repeatedly directed Hassan toward the discovery of his own Truth rather than providing fixed answers.[1]
- Path Correction: Hassan recalled that Buddha sometimes appeared silently when he began deviating from the course of his pilgrimage.[1]
- Price — Faith: Hassan states that the price he paid for Buddha's Blessing was his Faith.[1] The exact metaphysical meaning of this price has not been explained, particularly because Faith later became Hassan's strongest Concept.

#### Master Assassin
Hassan is an exceptional assassin and inherited the assassination tradition associated with the original [[Abe-no-Seimei|Old Man]] of the Mountain.
His mastery was sufficient for him to train [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]], an ancient Goddess, in assassination techniques.[3]
- Assassination Arts: Hassan possesses extensive knowledge of covert killing, target assessment and lethal efficiency.
- Emotional Discipline: Hassan teaches that an assassin must acknowledge emotions without allowing those emotions to interfere with lethality or judgment.[4]
- Threat Assessment: Hassan rapidly identified [[Zaladrac Zeovnur Elderblood]] and [[Jeanne Elderblood]] as the most dangerous women surrounding him during his first meeting with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family.[2]
- Combat Analysis: While training Ophis and [[Nero]], Hassan began by observing them spar so that he could identify each girl's strengths and weaknesses before progressing their training.[1]

#### Master Espionage
Hassan is explicitly described as a Master Spy and was responsible for developing much of Tasha's extraordinary espionage skill.[3]
- Counter-Espionage: Hassan understands how to search for hidden observers and infiltrators even when direct evidence of their presence is absent.[3]
- Intelligence Analysis: When neither he nor Tasha could detect [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s spies, Hassan correctly refused to assume that no spies existed. Instead, he concluded that Victor's operatives were simply sufficiently skilled to evade detection.[3]
- Deception Analysis: Hassan can distinguish between genuine deception and a target whose personality simply appears suspicious. He recognized that Tasha was overanalyzing Victor and searching for manipulation even when Victor was speaking truthfully.[3]
- Concealment: Hassan first appeared from behind Tasha as a shadow-like presence, demonstrating extraordinary stealth and concealment.[3] The exact supernatural mechanism behind this presentation has not been fully explained.

#### Psychological Analysis
Hassan possesses exceptional skill at reading individuals.
After observing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] only briefly, Hassan accurately identified several of his defining characteristics: confidence, charisma, arrogance and an overwhelming personality that nevertheless did not allow arrogance to blind his judgment.[3]
His analysis was sufficiently reliable that Tasha sought his opinion specifically when her own political instincts became uncertain.

#### Philosophy of Self-Discovery
Hassan developed a method of instruction centered upon helping individuals understand their identity, convictions and personal place in existence.[2]
The philosophy is intended to support:
- Self-awareness.
- Emotional discipline.
- Personal conviction.
- Spiritual growth.
- Development of Faith in one's own existence.
- Preparation for the path toward Divinity.
- Understanding of one's eventual Concepts.[2][1]
Hassan does not present the philosophy as a shortcut to power. Its purpose is to provide students with the internal foundation required to discover their own path rather than imitate someone else's.

#### Master Instructor
Hassan is capable of adapting his methods to students with radically different backgrounds.
His known students include:
- An ancient former Egyptian Goddess and assassin, [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]].
- Young Divine/Eldritch existences such as [[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]] and [[Nero Elderblood]].
- Experienced Noble Vampires and warriors from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household.
When teaching Ophis and [[Nero]], Hassan deliberately returned to basics before analyzing their sparring performance, demonstrating that he does not assume supernatural power eliminates the need for fundamental training.[1]

### Relationships

#### First Hassan
First Hassan was Hassan's master and predecessor.
The current Hassan encountered him after already reaching adulthood and became his disciple.[1]
Approximately one thousand years before the present story, he inherited the name Hassan-i-Sabbah from his teacher and later surpassed the level his master had reached.[2]
The relationship is one of succession and mentorship rather than confirmed blood ancestry.

#### Buddha
[[Buddha]] was the Divine Being who Blessed Hassan during his mortal pilgrimage.[1]
Unlike a conventional patron who simply granted power, Buddha rarely interfered directly.
He spoke to Hassan on only two remembered occasions, telling him to seek the Truth—specifically Hassan's own Truth. At other times, he appeared silently when Hassan strayed from his Journey.[1]
Buddha's Aspect of The Journey helped Hassan progress through self-discovery until he eventually attained Enlightenment and Divinity.[1]
Hassan nevertheless attributes his ascension primarily to his own Faith rather than treating Buddha's Blessing as something that completed the process for him.

#### Tasha Fenrir Elderblood
[[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]] is Hassan's most important known disciple.
Hassan trained Tasha extensively in assassination, espionage, covert politics and emotional discipline.[3][4]
These teachings became fundamental to Tasha's later role managing the hidden side of Samar's political system. Even thousands of years into her life, she continued consulting Hassan when faced with intelligence problems her own experience could not resolve.[3]
Their modern relationship also includes a reciprocal agreement. Tasha allows Hassan to live peacefully within Samar, while Hassan assists in protecting her nation when his expertise or power is required.[3]
Hassan addresses Tasha with the familiarity expected of a longtime teacher, while Tasha continues to respect him sufficiently to apologize before disturbing his seclusion.
His influence remained evident during the collapse of her marriage to Volk. When Tasha was nearly consumed by rage after discovering Volk's betrayal, Hassan's lessons allowed her to stabilize her emotions and convert them into deliberate action.[4]

#### Victor Elderblood
Hassan first knew [[Victor Elderblood]] indirectly through Tasha's intelligence operations.
His initial analysis of Victor was unusually accurate despite limited contact. Hassan described him as overwhelmingly confident and arrogant but emphasized that Victor was not blinded by that arrogance and was often far more straightforward than Tasha expected.[3]
Victor later acquired Hassan's services through a one-year agreement.[5]
During that period Hassan agreed to train whoever Victor selected, provided that he retained regular access to Samar and the right to permanently return if an emergency threatened the country.[5]
Hassan initially did not understand why Victor had chosen him when Victor's household already contained so many exceptional instructors. Only after meeting the family did Hassan identify the answer himself: Victor wanted someone capable of teaching them the internal foundation of Faith and self-discovery rather than simply another combat system.[2]

#### Ophis Tepes Elderblood and Nero Elderblood
[[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]] and [[Nero Elderblood]] were among the principal younger students placed directly under Hassan during his one-year contract.[2][1]
Hassan initially hesitated to train them because his methods were harsh and he expected them to suffer injuries and psychological strain. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family assured him that such hardship was acceptable and necessary.[2]
Hassan subsequently began with fundamentals before ordering the sisters to spar while he studied their respective strengths and weaknesses.[1]
