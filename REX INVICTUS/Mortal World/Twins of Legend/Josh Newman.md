Josh Newman, also known as Marethyu, is the younger of a set of twins that are part of a prophecy with his sister Sophie. They both have blonde hair and blue eyes. Josh's aura is gold and has the scent of oranges. He was Awakened by Mars Ultor. Since young, he suffered from "astral projection" moments in his sleep due to his existence watching over another.

### Description
- (UK [[The Enchantress]]) Josh Newman was born on December 21st, 1991, just seconds after his twin sister [[Sophie Newman|Sophie]]. Tall, athletic, with blond hair and blue eyes, Josh is quite impulsive, hates snakes, rats, spiders and scorpions and sometimes suffers claustrophobia. After falling under the influence of [[Dr. John Dee]] and [[Virginia Dare]], Josh has travelled back in time to [[Danu Talis]], accompanied by Sophie. Josh's magical aura is gold, with a scent of orange.

### Past
The Newman twins' adoptive parents are [[Richard and Sara Newman]], who at the end of [[The Warlock]] are revealed to be the [[Earthlords]] Osiris and Isis. Osiris and Isis later confirm that they didn't give birth to the twins and there wasn't a blood relation between them. The twins were told that they were born on December 21, 1991, but Josh was actually born to a prehistoric human tribe around 30,000 BCE. He was moved to a timeless Shadowrealm by [[Isis]] and [[Osiris]] and eventually joined by [[Sophie Newman|Sophie]].
Before meeting [[Dr. John Dee|John Dee]], Josh had worked nearly two months at Nick Flaming's The Small Book Shop.

### History
After [[Dr. John Dee]] tries to take the Codex from [[Nicholas Flamel]] Josh, who was working at the bookshop Flamel owned in [[San Francisco]], rips two pages out of the Codex and goes with Flamel alongside [[Sophie Newman|Sophie]]. They stay with [[Scathach]] until they are forced to flee to [[Hecate|Hekate]]. When Sophie's aura is Awakened by Hekate, there is no time for Josh's aura to also be Awakened as well as an invading army led by [[Bastet]], [[The Crow Goddess|the Morrigan]] and Dee freeze and shatter the world tree, [[Yggdrasil]]. Eventually in [[Paris]], Flamel gives him [[Clarent]], the Sword of Fire, to protect himself. With it he slays [[Nidhogg]] and sees its memories. Josh also has the memories of [[Mars Ultor]], as it was he who Awakened Josh in the Catacombs of Paris. Josh is also taught the Water [[Magic]] by [[Gilgamesh]].
After returning to San Francisco Josh learns Fire Magic from the Elder [[Prometheus]] in his Shadowrealm named [[Hades]]. After learning Fire Magic Josh sides with Dee and is tricked into releasing [[Coatlicue]]. Then Josh goes to [[Alcatraz]] with Dee and his associate [[Virginia Dare]] to unleash monsters on San Francisco. Before they do Virginia teaches Josh Air Magic. They release the [[Lotan]] and succeed in releasing the rest of the monsters from their cells until Mars, Hel, and [[Odin]] race to Alcatraz to stop them.
Dee uses the [[Swords of Power]] to create a leygate, which makes them traveling back in time to [[Danu Talis]] before it had sunk, taking Josh, Dare, and Sophie with him. Josh and Sophie meet [[Isis]] and [[Osiris]], who take them in. Soon they are to be presented to the [[Elders]] as the successors of [[Aten]]. Sophie and Josh leave and go to the roof of the [[Pyramid of the Sun]]. They see Isis and Osiris and start to argue. During the battle it is revealed that Josh and Sophie are not brother and sister. Isis and Osiris went looking for people with pure gold and silver auras, finding Josh at a prehistoric human camp. They stole him as a baby and raised him as their own alongside Sophie. Soon Isis and Osiris decide to kill the twins and reveal that they are [[Earthlords]]. Josh kills them and realizes that he is the twin to destroy Danu Talis. He fuses the four swords and becomes [[Marethyu]], which can be translated as death in the lost language of Danu Talis. He also created a Shadowrealm modeled after the Pleistocene Epoch, but with four moons and no snakes.
His hook/sickle is the four swords of power fused together.
"Today, I am become Death, the [[Destroyer]] of Worlds."
— Josh, becoming Marethyu, [[The Enchantress]] (Chapter 78)

### Personality
Josh is protective at the beginning of the series and becomes more so during [[The Alchemyst]]. He grows jealous when he does not get Awakened and [[Sophie Newman|Sophie]] does. He was also stubborn and brave, more so that sometimes he didn't know what he was doing, like when he was finding [[Scathach]] in [[The Magician]]. He finds it getting harder to trust [[Nicholas Flamel]] as the series proceeds due to all the secrets the alchemyst has been keeping from them. As the story continues, Josh is Awakened and is secretly jealous of the fact that Sophie can do more than him.
He loved the mingled smell of parched curled papers' dry and musty with the richer aroma of old leather bindings and dusty cobwebs: he its was warm and comforting.
During his eight days of adventure then becoming [[Marethyu]], Josh matured through the encounters of empowerment, loss, rational, love, and choice. In the end, he completed the multi-time line loop with the birth of his hook.

### Powers & Abilities
- Awakened Senses: Josh Newman has been awakened, or has learned to harness the power of their aura. He has significantly sharper senses, better reflexes, and greater stamina than most humani.
- Aura Manipulation: Josh Newman has learned to control his aura to a certain extent. He is able to shape the aura around his body, and uses it to power his magic.
- Legendary swords: Has wielded a sword of power.
- Master Tactician & Strategist: After being Awakened by [[Mars Ultor]], the Elder grants Josh all his skills as a tactician and strategist.
- [[Necromancy]]: Josh learns necromantic magic from [[Dr. John Dee|John Dee]]. His first usage, under the [[Magician (Flamel)|Magician]]'s thrall, was to summon [[Coatlicue]].
- [[Immortality]]: Josh Newman is Immortal and does not age. Though an immortal may be poisoned or slain in battle, they may live for thousands or millions of years.
Master of Air
- Aeromancy: Masters of air can greatly compress and decompress air. Air can be manipulated to form tornadoes, walls, and shields. Highly pressurized air can be shaped into blades, orbs, blasts, and explosions.
- Exnomophis: Masters of air can alter the balance of the components that make up air.
- Flight: Masters of air can fly by manipulating air currents.
- Manipulation of Weather: Masters of air can create powerful tornadoes, hurricanes, twisters, whirlwinds etc.
- Thermokinesis: Masters of air can change the temperature of air, either freezing it or heating it to extreme temperatures.
- Typhokinesis: Masters of air can manipulate air particulates, such as smoke, mist, and clouds.
Master of Water
- Hydromancy: Masters of water can create, move, and shape water into nearly any shape or form imaginable.
- Manipulation of Bodies of Water: Masters of water can manipulate rivers and oceans, such as impacting tidal currents, causing floods, and creating waves.
- Thermokinesis: Masters of water can manipulate the temperature of water, causing it to boil or freeze.
- Weather Manipulation: Masters of water can create rain, clouds, and storms.
Master of Fire
- Healing: Masters of fire can heal certain types of wounds with fire by fusing and reforming tissue.
- Pyromancy: Masters of fire can create, extinguish, manipulate, move, and throw fire. Fire can be formed into shapes.
- Thermokinesis: Masters of fire can greatly heat an object or enemy with a touch.
- Welding: Masters of fire can melt and fuse stone, metal, and many other substances.

### Trivia
- Josh is awakened by [[Mars Ultor]], an Elder known in most cultures as a god of war, whereas [[Sophie Newman|Sophie]] is awakened by [[Hecate|Hekate]], an Elder known as a goddess of magic. Ironically, the siblings' skillsets throughout the series seems to mirror this, with Sophie gaining quicker access to the Elemental Magics and Josh gaining Mars' tactical knowledge and being better at combat.
- Josh and Sophie were awakened by a pair of siblings: a younger brother and an older sister, respectively.
- Josh's final quote before turning into [[Marethyu]] is the same one Robert Oppenheimer used during his first tests with atomic bombs. He in turn has taken this quote from the Bhagavad Gita.
- [[The Codex]] is revealed in [[The Lost Stories Collection]] to be his diary and encyclopedia, used to guide and support the auspicious time line: [[Flamel Series|The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel]].
- He nicknamed [[Scathach]] and [[Aoife]] the "Shadow Twins" and had been their voyeur for over 10,000 years.
SPOILER:
- There seems to be a paradox in the fact that Josh becomes Marethyu: at certain moments in time both Josh and Marethyu exist, while they are the same person (as revealed at the end of [[The Enchantress]]). The most probable explanation for this is that they are in fact both Josh Newman, yet not the same person because the story assumes different timelines. As stated, one of Marethyu's powers is traveling between timelines (A & B). It is therefore not unthinkable that Josh becomes Marethyu in timeline A, Marethyu works his schemes in another timeline B, and when Josh becomes Marethyu after forging the four swords in timeline B, Marethyu returns to his original timeline (A) where he already sunk [[Danu Talis]]. It is also can be possible due to the Hook of [[Aether]] "binding" him to a specific point of parallel existence and empowering Marethyu's omniscience.
- Josh becoming Marethyu cements the Auspicious Time line.
- Josh doesn't appear to be humani. [[Prometheus]] created humani out of clay roughly 10,000 years before the Modern era, but Josh was born in a Neanderthal camp 30,000 years ago. This, however, could simply be a plot hole. This inconsistency also made Prometheus teaching a "humani" Josh illogical.
