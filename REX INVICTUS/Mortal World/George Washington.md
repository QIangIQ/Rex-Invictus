**[[George Washington]]** (1732 to 1799) commanded the Continental Army in the American Revolutionary War and was the first President of the United States (1789 to 1797).

---

## History (IRL)

A Virginia planter and surveyor, he served in the French and Indian War, took command of the Continental Army in 1775, held it together through Valley Forge and won at Yorktown in 1781 with French help. He presided over the Constitutional Convention and set the two-term precedent for later presidents.
