**[[The Sphinx]]** is a creature that has a lion's body, the wings of an eagle, and the head of a beautiful woman. (Lore adapted from *The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** Flamel material is adapted into the Rex Invictus present (c. 2021). Rex Invictus wins any conflict; [[Robespierre]] fills [[Dr. John Dee]]'s role and hunts the Philosopher's Stone rather than [[The Codex]]. See [[Flamel Series#Integration rules]].

## Real-World Background (IRL)

The Great Sphinx of Giza is a limestone statue with a lion's body and a human head, built around 2500 BC under the pharaoh Khafre. The Greek Sphinx at Thebes posed the riddle that Oedipus solved.

The Sphinx is a creature that has a lion's body, the wings of an eagle, and the head of a beautiful woman. It is also one of the daughters of Echidna. They feed on auras, so magic is almost impossible around them. There is a sphinx trapped on [[Alcatraz]] which was used to keep [[Nicholas Flamel|Perenelle]] imprisoned. It is unknown how many sphinxes there are for only one is mentioned in the books.
The sphinx was later killed in [[The Enchantress]] by [[Billy the Kid]] using two of the spears that were imprisoning [[Areop-Enap]] under Alcatraz.
