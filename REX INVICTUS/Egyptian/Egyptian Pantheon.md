The Egyptian Pantheon was one of the major Divine factions of Earth, composed of Gods connected to the Egyptian branch of the supernatural world. It was ruled by Ra, who was explicitly recognized as a true God-King, and was noted to be considerably more unified than the [[Greek Pantheon]] at the time [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] began directly moving against the Gods.[1][2][3]
Like the other Pantheons, the Egyptian Pantheon functioned as a Divine power bloc composed of Gods, subordinate Divine beings, and their own protected territory. Its known members include Ra, [[Anubis]], [[Thoth]], [[Khons]], and Seth.[4][5][6] During the later conflicts surrounding Victor Elderblood, the faction became one of the Pantheons directly opposing or acting against him and his family, which ultimately led to its downfall and the transformation of its territory into the former Egyptian Pantheon.[2][6][7][8]

### Description
The Egyptian Pantheon was one of Earth's recognized Pantheons and represented the Egyptian Divine system within the supernatural world.[1][2] It was led by Ra, whose authority over the Pantheon was significant enough that he could seal the Pantheon's Dimension more completely than [[Zeus]] could seal [[Olympus]], a feat specifically attributed to the fact that Ra was a true God-King.[2]
Unlike the [[Greek Pantheon]], which had already been fractured by internal conflict and external pressure, the Egyptian Pantheon was explicitly described as undivided when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] prepared to move against it.[3] This meant that Victor treated it as a more coordinated and potentially more dangerous target than some of the other Pantheons.
The Pantheon also included Gods associated with death, knowledge and other Divine domains. [[Anubis]] was identified as an Egyptian God and considered troublesome by [[Aphrodite]],[4] while [[Thoth]] was recognized by Albedo as a God whose area overlapped with her own sphere of knowledge and research.[5]

### Territory
The Egyptian Pantheon was connected to Earth through the Egyptian desert and possessed its own sealed Divine territory or Dimension.[2] Ra had extremely high influence over this territory and used that authority to close off the Pantheon when threatened by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and the allied Pantheons.[2]
However, even this strong control was no longer absolute after the large-scale merger of Paradises, which left cracks and openings that could still be exploited.[2]
After the Pantheon's fall, the territory was no longer treated as an independent Divine domain and instead began to be referred to as the former Egyptian Pantheon.[7][8]

### History

#### Earlier status
Before its collapse, the Egyptian Pantheon existed as one of the major Divine factions on Earth, ruled by Ra and composed of Egyptian Gods under his leadership.[1][2] Compared with some other Pantheons, it appears to have remained politically stable and internally coherent for longer, as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later noted that it was still undivided when he targeted it.[3]

#### Connection to New Dawn
By the time [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his allies were investigating the Gods acting against his side, one of the three suspicious Gods identified through Jeanne's observations was recognized by [[Aphrodite]] as [[Anubis]].[4] This linked the Egyptian Pantheon to the wider network of hostile Divine movements and placed it among the Pantheons being watched carefully after the escalation caused by the Gods and [[New Dawn]].[4]
Later commentary by readers and the story context surrounding this period also align the Egyptian side, particularly Anubis, with the cluster of death-related Divine figures that became relevant around the New Dawn conflict, but the page should remain limited to what is directly established in the chapters unless a dedicated New Dawn/Egyptian connection chapter is sourced more explicitly.[4]

#### Conflict with Victor
The Egyptian Pantheon eventually became one of the factions directly facing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. By Chapter 874, Victor and his forces were physically in the Egyptian desert, preparing to deal with the faction while Ra had already sealed the Pantheon in response to the threat posed by Victor and the other Pantheons.[2]
Victor considered the Egyptian Pantheon dangerous precisely because, unlike the [[Greek Pantheon]], it was not politically divided.[3] This forced him to approach the conflict more carefully and to bring several elite allies with him rather than relying on a fragmented enemy structure to weaken the faction from within.[3]
During the confrontation, Ra personally faced Victor and attempted to justify events by shifting blame toward Seth, stating that Seth was the one responsible for the actions taken.[6] Victor rejected the excuse entirely and made it clear that, as leader of the Pantheon, Ra was equally culpable once the faction had moved against his wives and daughters.[6] This exchange effectively marked the destruction of any possibility of reconciliation.

#### Fall
The Egyptian Pantheon ultimately ceased to exist as an independent Pantheon and its territory became known as the former Egyptian Pantheon.[7][8] By Chapter 953, groups under [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s authority were already organizing the transfer of new inhabitants into the former Egyptian Pantheon, indicating that the area had passed fully under his control and was being repurposed administratively.[7]
By Chapter 955, Victor was casually walking through the former Egyptian Pantheon with several of his wives and companions, further confirming that the area was now part of his sphere rather than a hostile Divine territory.[8]

### Structure
The Egyptian Pantheon was ruled by Ra, whose status as a true God-King is explicitly stated in the novel.[2] This strongly implies a hierarchical structure in which the Pantheon was organized around Ra's central authority rather than functioning as a loose alliance of fully independent Gods.
The faction also included other Egyptian Gods acting under or alongside this structure. Confirmed members include:
- Ra — the ruler and God-King of the Egyptian Pantheon.[1][2]
- [[Anubis]] — an Egyptian God specifically identified by [[Aphrodite]].[4]
- [[Thoth]] — the God of Knowledge, known by Albedo.[5]
- [[Khons]] — a God of the Egyptian Pantheon who appeared alongside Thoth.[5]
- Seth — a major member of the Pantheon and the one Ra blamed for the hostile actions that triggered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wrath.[6]
Because the source material has not yet given a full internal governmental breakdown for the Pantheon, it would be inaccurate to invent councils, priesthood structures or officer ranks that have not been directly shown.

### Power and Position
The Egyptian Pantheon was one of the more formidable Pantheons on Earth during the period immediately before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s campaign against the Gods. Ra's ability to almost completely seal the Pantheon and Victor's explicit caution about the Pantheon's undivided structure both indicate that this was not a weak or fragmented Divine faction.[2][3]
The Pantheon's importance is also reflected in the fact that recognized figures such as [[Thoth]] and [[Anubis]] were directly relevant to the larger conflict involving Victor, the other Pantheons, and the Gods acting behind the scenes.[4][5]
At the same time, its final defeat and later conversion into territory administered by Victor shows that, despite its strength, it could not withstand the later balance of power once Victor directly chose to eliminate it.[6][7][8]

### Current Status
The Egyptian Pantheon no longer exists as an independent Divine faction. Its territory was conquered and is repeatedly referred to in later chapters as the former Egyptian Pantheon.[7][8]
After its fall, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s subordinates began organizing the transfer of new residents into the area, indicating that it had already been absorbed into his broader domain and was being reused rather than destroyed or abandoned.[7] Victor himself later moved through the territory openly and without resistance, confirming that the Pantheon had fully ceased functioning as a hostile sovereign faction.[8]
