**[[Amenhotep]]** is a figure of Rex Invictus lore. (Lore adapted from *The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** Flamel material is adapted into the Rex Invictus present (c. 2021). Rex Invictus wins any conflict; [[Robespierre]] fills [[Dr. John Dee]]'s role and hunts the Philosopher's Stone rather than [[The Codex]]. See [[Flamel Series#Integration rules]].

---

## Real-World Background (IRL)

Several Egyptian pharaohs bore the name Amenhotep. Amenhotep III (c. 1388–1351 BC) ruled at the height of the New Kingdom; his son Amenhotep IV became Akhenaten.

---

## Flamel Series Canon (SOFTINF)

> Amenhotep is the former Lord of [[Danu Talis]]. He is son of [[Thoth]], husband of [[Bastet]], father of [[Aten]] and [[Anubis]], and grandfather of [[Tutankhamun]].

### Abilities
[[Immortality]]: Amenhotep is Immortal and does not age. Though an immortal may be poisoned or slain in battle, they may live for thousands or millions of years.
