**[[Anubis]]** is an ancient Egyptian God, a Major God of the Egyptian Pantheon associated principally with Death, the Afterlife, Mummification and the protection of the dead. (Lore adapted from *The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** Flamel material is adapted into the Rex Invictus present (c. 2021). Rex Invictus wins any conflict; [[Robespierre]] fills [[Dr. John Dee]]'s role and hunts the Philosopher's Stone rather than [[The Codex]]. See [[Flamel Series#Integration rules]].

## Real-World Background (IRL)

Anubis is the jackal-headed Egyptian god of mummification and the dead, who weighs the heart against the feather of Ma'at. In the Kane Chronicles he is a young god who befriends [[Sadie Kane]].

Anubis is the brother of Aten, Lord of [[Danu Talis]]. His father is Amenhotep, who also was Lord of Danu Talis.
He is jealous of his brother, Aten, and he and his mother, Bastet, plot to overthrow him. They succeed, though that is revealed to be allowed by Aten. Anubis then plans to be presented as Lord of Danu Talis, but he does not get the chance due to intervention by [[Isis]] and [[Osiris]]. Anubis is in control of an army, which is composed of Anpu, jackal-headed creatures created by himself, and various other monsters. He finds great amusement in creating more human-animal hybrids in the labs under The [[Pyramid of the Sun]] and putting them into action. He is a strategist and more than a capable warrior: he fought [[Earthlords]] with his brother, though his greatest failing might have been being controlled by his mother.

### Abilities
- [[Immortality]]: Anubis is Immortal and does not age. Though an immortal may be poisoned or slain in battle, they may live for thousands or millions of years.

### In Mythology
Anubis is the Egyptian god of embalming, his father was either [[Osiris]] or Set, Osiris' brother, and his mother was Nephthys. Anubis is the Greek name for him, his Egyptian name is [[Anpu]].

### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 702
Anubis is an ancient Egyptian God, a Major God of the [[Egyptian Pantheon]] associated principally with Death, the Afterlife, Mummification and the protection of the dead. He was one of the more dangerous Third Generation Gods of Egypt and was described by [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood simply as a "quite troublesome guy" when she identified him from an Ice reconstruction created by [[Victor Elderblood]].[1]
Long before Victor openly went to war with Egypt, Anubis was secretly involved with supernatural factions operating through [[New Dawn]]. Alongside Gods such as [[Taranis]], Shinigami and [[Erebus]], he was connected to experiments intended to artificially reproduce the conditions under which Gods are born from mortal Faith. The experiment discovered by Victor above the Bermuda Triangle isolated Human populations, fabricated myths and manufactured miraculous events in order to accumulate enough belief around artificial Divine identities to eventually create entirely new Gods.[2][3]
Anubis later accompanied Ra and Seth to the third major [[Supernatural Gathering]], where Victor publicly exposed the Egyptian Pantheon's involvement with the enemies targeting his family. Anubis attempted to deny the accusations, but Victor presented recordings proving secret Egyptian negotiations and subsequently demanded the entire Pantheon's surrender. When the Egyptian Gods refused, Victor formally declared war upon the Egyptian Pantheon, the Elder Gods and New Dawn.[4][5]
The war proved catastrophically one-sided. When Victor invaded the sealed Egyptian Pantheon, Velnorah suppressed the Gods' Divinities and simultaneously captured 76 selected Egyptian Gods before they could properly resist. Anubis was among the Egyptian Divine targets taken prisoner, while Ra was left for Victor to personally confront.[6] Anubis survived the conquest but became one of Victor's enslaved Divine enemies, while the former territory of the Egyptian Pantheon was incorporated into Victor's rapidly expanding faction.[7]

### Physical Appearance
The novel has not yet provided a sufficiently detailed canonical description of Anubis' appearance.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] directly observed Anubis during the investigation of the hidden Bermuda Triangle facility and was able to reproduce his appearance accurately enough through an Ice sculpture for [[Aphrodite]] to immediately identify him.[1] However, the narration did not specify his hair color, eye color, height, skin tone or clothing.
The jackal-headed Divine form traditionally associated with Anubis and the Human appearance used in the current article artwork should therefore be treated as artistic representations unless future chapters explicitly establish those features.

### Personality and Traits
Anubis is considerably more controlled, cautious and calculating than many of the Gods with whom he worked. This is most clearly demonstrated through his interactions with [[Taranis]] after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] destroyed their Bermuda Triangle research facility. While Taranis immediately lost his temper upon seeing their work erased, Anubis repeatedly told him to calm down and instead focused on understanding what had happened.[8]
His calm demeanor does not make him benevolent. Anubis was an active collaborator in experiments that manipulated isolated Human populations for generations, deceiving them into believing that the outside world had been destroyed so their fear and Faith could be concentrated toward artificial Gods.[3] He also participated in a conspiracy that discussed abducting useful Gods when their abilities could improve the experiment, including the possibility of kidnapping [[Demeter]] to solve food-production problems within the isolated settlements.[8]
Anubis is also wary of abnormalities he cannot properly quantify. After identifying the remnant Energy of Victor's Dragon Breath, he referred to Victor as an "irregular abomination" and reflected that he had never encountered someone so unnatural. Even from the residual Energy alone, Anubis remembered the immense amount of power Victor contained and the effects of the Negativity he had previously released.[8]
Despite his cautious nature, Anubis was still willing to participate in deception when necessary. During the [[Supernatural Gathering]], after Victor produced the wanted posters issued against his family, Anubis attempted to act innocent and deny the [[Egyptian Pantheon]]'s involvement. Victor immediately exposed the lie with recordings obtained through his intelligence network.[5]
[[Aphrodite]]'s assessment of Anubis as "troublesome" further indicates that his danger was not based solely upon raw Divine power. His intelligence, association with Death and willingness to cooperate with covert organizations made him an especially inconvenient opponent even among Gods.[1]

### History

#### Association with New Dawn
At some point before Chapter 702, Anubis became associated with the network of Gods and supernatural factions connected to [[New Dawn]].
Whether Anubis should be considered a formal member of New Dawn or an allied Divine collaborator has never been explicitly clarified. However, he was directly involved with at least one of the organization's hidden research programs and personally knew about additional research sites.[8]
The project [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] discovered involved artificially creating Gods through accumulated Faith. Rather than waiting for myths to develop naturally over centuries or millennia, the conspirators manufactured the social conditions necessary for Divine creation.
Their method involved:
- isolating entire Human communities inside concealed Dimensions;
- convincing the inhabitants that civilization outside their settlement had been destroyed;
- periodically releasing Monsters or manufactured threats;
- creating fictional heroic Gods who would supposedly protect the settlement;
- placing shrines throughout the community;
- redirecting accumulated Faith into specialized Divine containers;
- and allowing fabricated myths to strengthen over successive generations.[3]
One example was an artificial heroic deity named Thorron, represented as a powerful man wielding an axe. The intended result was for accumulated belief, mythology and Faith to eventually cause a genuine Divine Being corresponding to Thorron to emerge.[3]
Earlier versions of this article incorrectly described the project as accumulating Fate Energy. The actual resource emphasized by the experiment was Faith.

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Anubis first entered the story during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s investigation of a hidden island and pocket Dimension located around the Bermuda Triangle.
Victor, [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], [[Jeanne Elderblood]], [[Morgana Elderblood]] and their companions discovered a Human settlement living beneath artificial supernatural conditions. The inhabitants believed most of Humanity had been destroyed and depended upon supposed Gods for survival.[2]
Victor's enhanced perception detected three Divine presences associated with the operation. Because his group did not recognize every God present, Victor later reconstructed their appearances with Ice and asked [[Aphrodite]] to identify them.[1]
Aphrodite identified the Eastern deity as the replaceable Shinto Death God commonly referred to as Shinigami, the Celtic deity as [[Taranis]] and the Egyptian deity as Anubis. Her reaction toward Anubis was immediate: she described him as a particularly troublesome God.[1]
Further investigation revealed that the entire settlement existed as a controlled Faith-production experiment. The Humans were being manipulated into creating myths and worshiping false Divine identities so that enough belief could accumulate to artificially birth new Gods.[3]
Victor's group evacuated the Human population, removed useful information and materials from the facility and then destroyed the entire island and pocket Dimension through Victor's Dragon Breath.[9]
After Victor departed, Anubis and Taranis returned to the destroyed site.
Unlike Taranis, who immediately shouted in anger over the destruction of their work, Anubis remained calm and repeatedly told him to control himself. Their conversation was interrupted when [[Erebus]] manifested beside them.[8]
Anubis asked Erebus whether he understood what had happened. Erebus examined the residual Energy and identified the attack as Dragon Breath belonging to Victor.
Anubis reacted by calling Victor an irregular abomination. He privately reflected that throughout his entire existence he had never encountered someone so abnormal. Victor possessed enough Energy to fuel what Anubis considered an apocalyptic inferno, and the Egyptian God could still clearly remember the effects of the Negativity Victor had released during an earlier conflict.[8]
The group concluded that Victor had discovered one of their research operations and that their remaining facilities needed greater secrecy. They also discussed logistical problems affecting their Human experiments, particularly food production. The possibility of kidnapping an agriculture-related deity was raised, with [[Demeter]] becoming an obvious candidate because the [[Greek Pantheon]] was trapped in civil war. Erebus informed them that Demeter had already entered Victor's faction, once again making Victor an obstacle to their plans.[8]
Anubis and Taranis subsequently departed to warn their allies about the destruction of the facility.
Anubis reappeared during the third major [[Supernatural Gathering]], arriving alongside Ra and Seth.[4]
By this point Victor already possessed extensive intelligence concerning the [[Egyptian Pantheon]]'s activities. His family had been targeted through wanted posters offering enormous rewards for Victor, Scathach Elderblood Scarlett, [[Agnes Elderblood Snow]] and others, while several of Victor's female relatives and allies were specifically ordered to be captured alive for reproductive purposes.[5]
When Victor displayed the posters, Ra, Seth and Anubis immediately became visibly uncomfortable.
Anubis attempted to deny responsibility, presenting himself and the Egyptian Pantheon as uninvolved. Victor silenced the denial by striking the Gathering's supposedly indestructible table hard enough to crack it and activating an Orb containing secret recordings.[5]
The evidence revealed conversations within the Egyptian faction and negotiations involving servants of the [[Elder Gods Pantheon]]. The Egyptian leadership intended to exploit information concerning Victor and his allies in order to weaken or eliminate them.[5]
Victor demanded only one form of compensation: the complete surrender of the Egyptian Pantheon and recognition of himself as its new God-King.
The Egyptians refused.
Victor immediately declared war upon the Egyptian Pantheon, the Elder Gods of [[Nightingale]] and [[New Dawn]].[5]

#### Dragon God of Chaos

##### Conquest of the Egyptian Pantheon
The [[Egyptian Pantheon]] attempted to isolate itself after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s declaration.
Ra completely sealed the Pantheon's Dimension, hoping to prevent Victor's coalition from entering. The defense was considerably stronger than [[Olympus]]' previous isolation because Ra possessed much greater direct authority over his Pantheon.[6]
It did not matter.
Victor's forces identified a weakness in the dimensional barrier and forced their way inside. Velnorah entered the Pantheon first and immediately activated technology capable of suppressing Divine abilities across the region.[6]
Her armor released a pulse and announced:
Divine Concept denied.
The Egyptian Gods abruptly discovered that they could no longer properly invoke their Divinities.
Velnorah then marked the Divine targets Victor had selected and deployed a spatial-capture system. Threads of Energy spread throughout the Pantheon, seized the targeted Gods and forcibly dragged them into Victor's portable prison system before the other members of the invading coalition could even begin their planned competition.[6]
In total, 76 selected targets were captured almost instantaneously.
Anubis was among the Egyptian Gods captured during the operation, alongside figures such as [[Amon]] and Seth. Only Ra remained outside the mass capture because Victor intended to confront the God-King personally.[6]
The result was particularly humiliating for the Egyptian Pantheon. Victor had assembled elite warriors from several Pantheons and intended the invasion to become a coordinated hunt, yet Velnorah neutralized and captured almost the entire target list by herself in seconds.
Ra was subsequently confronted separately by Victor and stripped of his Divine status.[10][11]
Following Egypt's defeat, Anubis did not die.
He became one of the Egyptian Gods captured and enslaved beneath Victor's authority, alongside Amon, Seth, [[Thoth]], [[Khons]] and other surviving Divine enemies.
The former Egyptian territory ceased functioning as an independent political power. Victor began relocating members of his growing faction into the conquered Pantheon, including allied Vampire Clans, Humans, Youkai, Goddesses and Demonic forces.[7]
The once-independent lands of Egypt were therefore transformed into territory belonging to Victor's civilization, while Anubis' status changed from autonomous Major God to enslaved Divine subordinate.

### Skills & Abilities
Anubis is a Third Generation Major God associated with the negative side of the Egyptian Divine system. Despite receiving very little direct combat exposure, he was considered sufficiently dangerous for [[Aphrodite]]—herself an ancient and exceptionally experienced Goddess—to immediately describe him as troublesome.[1]
- God Physiology: As an ancient Egyptian God, Anubis possesses a Divine body and Soul, immense longevity, supernatural physical attributes, [[Divine Energy]] and the fundamental characteristics shared by natural Gods.
- Divine Energy Manipulation: Anubis can wield the Divine Energy generated by his existence and Divinities. His exact total reserves have never been individually quantified.
- Death Manipulation: Death is one of Anubis' principal Divine authorities. As an Egyptian Death God, his existence is associated with death and the processing of deceased Souls. The novel has not yet shown the full offensive applications of this Divinity.
- Afterlife Manipulation: Anubis possesses Divine association with the Egyptian Afterlife and the movement of the dead through the Egyptian supernatural system.
- Soul Interaction: His Death and Afterlife authorities imply direct interaction with Souls as part of his Divine function. However, the novel has not demonstrated enough of his personal techniques to define the exact upper limits of his Soul Manipulation.
- Mummification: Anubis is associated with Mummification within the Egyptian Divine system. No dedicated combat technique based upon this authority has been shown.
- Protection of the Dead: Anubis' Divine functions include protection connected to graves and the dead. The exact supernatural applications remain unrevealed.
- [[Immortality]]: As a God, Anubis does not age like an ordinary Mortal and is extremely difficult to permanently eliminate through conventional physical damage.
- Regeneration: Divine Beings can recover from injuries that would ordinarily kill Mortals or lesser supernatural species. Permanently killing a God generally requires sufficient power to affect the deeper Divine existence rather than merely destroying the physical body.
- Enhanced Strength: Anubis possesses the physical strength of a developed Major God, although the novel has not provided an isolated physical-strength feat for him.
- Enhanced Speed and Reflexes: His Divine physiology grants supernatural movement and reaction speeds, though these were never meaningfully demonstrated because Velnorah disabled and captured the Egyptian Gods before they could fight during the invasion.
- Enhanced Durability: Anubis possesses the Divine durability of a Major God.
- Supernatural Senses: He could perceive supernatural Energies remaining after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s attack and recognize the unusual nature of Victor's existence and Negativity.[8]
- Faith Knowledge: Anubis possessed detailed practical knowledge concerning Faith and the processes by which mortal mythology can contribute toward the birth of new Gods. He participated in [[New Dawn]]'s project to artificially recreate those conditions.[3][8]
- Artificial God Creation Knowledge: Through the hidden research program, Anubis and his allies developed a method of accelerating Divine formation through fabricated myths, controlled populations, staged miracles and concentrated Faith.[3]
- Strategic Intelligence: Anubis displayed greater caution and emotional control than [[Taranis]] and quickly focused upon protecting the remaining research operations after Victor discovered one of their facilities.[8]

### Capture and Enslavement
Anubis' defeat is unusual because he was never given an individual combat scene against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
Victor entered the Egyptian campaign intending to capture the Gods implicated in the conspiracy rather than simply destroy the entire Pantheon indiscriminately. Before the assembled allied warriors could begin hunting their targets, Velnorah used her technology to deny Divine Concepts across the region and capture the designated Gods simultaneously.[6]

### Relationships

#### Ra
Ra was the God-King of the [[Egyptian Pantheon]] and Anubis' political superior.
Anubis accompanied Ra during the third [[Supernatural Gathering]] and remained on the Egyptian side when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] exposed the Pantheon's conspiracy.[4][5]
When Victor demanded complete surrender, the Egyptian leadership refused. That decision led directly to the invasion and loss of the Pantheon.
Ra was the only principal Egyptian target not taken during Velnorah's mass capture, as Victor deliberately confronted him separately.[6]

#### Seth
Seth was one of Anubis' principal allies within the hostile Egyptian faction.
Seth was heavily involved in the plot against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], and secret recordings revealed him negotiating plans intended to exploit Victor's perceived weaknesses.[5]
Both Seth and Anubis were subsequently captured during Victor's conquest and remained alive as enslaved Gods.
The claim that Seth is Anubis' father originates from some versions of real-world Egyptian mythology and has not been independently established through the cited novel scenes.

#### Taranis
[[Taranis]] worked alongside Anubis within the network connected to [[New Dawn]].
Their personalities contrasted strongly. Taranis was emotionally volatile and immediately raged when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] destroyed their research facility, while Anubis remained calm and repeatedly told him to control himself.[8]
Both were aware of the artificial-God experiments and additional hidden research operations.
Taranis later separated politically from Anubis when [[Sucellus]] ordered him to participate in Victor's hunt against the factions that had targeted Victor's family.

#### Erebus
[[Erebus]] was another major Divine collaborator associated with the conspiracy.
Anubis treated the Original Primordial God with considerably more composure than [[Taranis]] and directly asked him to identify the Energy remaining after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] destroyed their facility.[8]
Erebus recognized Victor's Dragon Breath and confirmed that Alucard had discovered the operation.
The exact depth of the relationship between Erebus and Anubis remains unknown.

#### Shinigami
Shinigami was one of the three Gods detected by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] during the Bermuda Triangle investigation and was therefore associated with the same artificial-God project.[2][1]
Unlike Anubis, Shinigami's position as a Death God was described as an occupational Divine office subordinate to the Shinto [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] rather than an irreplaceable personal identity.[1]

#### Victor Elderblood
Anubis initially knew [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] as an increasingly dangerous obstacle interfering with [[New Dawn]]'s operations.
After Victor destroyed the Bermuda Triangle facility, Anubis regarded him as an "irregular abomination" whose growth, Energy reserves and connection to Negativity violated the normal expectations Anubis had developed over his eons of existence.[8]
Their hostility became open during the third [[Supernatural Gathering]].
When Victor accused the [[Egyptian Pantheon]] of placing bounties upon his family, Anubis attempted to deny responsibility. Victor exposed the faction's secret dealings and demanded complete submission.[5]
After the refusal, Victor declared war.
Anubis was later captured during the conquest and became one of Victor's enslaved Divine enemies.[6]

### Current Status
Anubis is alive but enslaved.
The [[Egyptian Pantheon]] ceased operating as an independent supernatural power after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s conquest. Its lands were claimed and repurposed as one of the principal territories of Victor's expanding faction, receiving Vampires, Humans, Youkai, Goddesses, Demons and other populations aligned with him.[7]
Anubis, Seth, [[Amon]], [[Thoth]] and [[Khons]] survived the conflict but are indexed among Victor's enslaved Divine enemies rather than autonomous members of the former Egyptian political order.
