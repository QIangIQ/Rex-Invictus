Seth, also known as Set, Setekh and Setesh, is an ancient Egyptian God and a Second Generation Primordial God belonging to the [[Egyptian Pantheon]]. Unlike the large mythology-derived list previously associated with him, the novel directly identifies the primary Divinities residing within Seth's Soul as Death, Weather and Animals. When [[Victor Elderblood]] examined Seth through his evolved perception, those three Concepts were immediately recognized as the principal characteristics of his Divine existence.[1]
Seth was regarded as dangerous even among other [[Primordial Gods]]. When [[Amon]] proposed summoning him as reinforcement against Victor, [[Erebus]] objected specifically because Seth had difficulty distinguishing between ally and enemy, making his presence dangerous even to his own side. Amon nevertheless believed that adding Seth to Erebus and [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] would give their faction three Primordial Gods and overwhelm the Demon King.[2]
Seth entered the war inside the [[Greek Underworld]] in Chapter 797 and later attempted to assassinate [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] from behind while she was occupied fighting other Primordial Gods. Victor intercepted the attack and subsequently made Seth one of the few enemies he deliberately chose not to kill. Instead, after the Greek conflict ended, Victor systematically destroyed Seth's body and Soul while keeping the God alive, intending to return him to Egypt in a permanently crippled condition as punishment for targeting his wife.[3][4]
The punishment did not render Seth literally unconscious forever. By the third [[Supernatural Gathering]], he had recovered enough to walk, speak, think and participate in events, although the damage inflicted by Victor remained impossible for even Egyptian healing Gods to properly repair. Despite having already survived Victor's retaliation once, Seth became one of the principal figures behind the Egyptian conspiracy against Victor's family and the [[Blood God Religion]]. Ra later directly blamed him for the entire affair and Victor acknowledged the possibility that Seth had convinced or manipulated the Egyptian God-King into participating.[5][6][7]
Following Victor's declaration of war, Seth was eventually captured together with the other selected Egyptian Gods when Velnorah disabled the Pantheon's Divine Concepts and imprisoned Victor's seventy-six targets in a spatial containment artifact. He survived the conquest and remains one of Victor's enslaved Divine enemies.[8]

### Physical Appearance
Seth is a tall man with gray skin and white hair. These traits were explicitly described when he physically entered the Greek conflict after being summoned by [[Amon]].[9]
Upon appearing, Seth displayed immediate interest in the battle occurring before him. The narration did not provide a detailed description of his clothing, eye color or exact height.
Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s punishment after the destruction of [[Olympus]], Seth's appearance deteriorated dramatically. By the time he entered the later [[Supernatural Gathering]] alongside Ra and [[Anubis]], he was explicitly described as a very broken Seth, reflecting the extreme physical and spiritual damage Victor had inflicted upon him.[5]
Despite that condition, Seth remained conscious. He could walk alongside the Egyptian delegation, speak, observe his surroundings and internally react to Victor, demonstrating that the earlier description of Victor turning him into a "vegetable" represented the intended severity of his crippling rather than Seth's permanent literal state.[4][5]

### Personality and Traits
Seth is an aggressive, opportunistic and dangerously unpredictable God. [[Erebus]]' reaction to [[Amon]]'s plan to summon him provides one of the clearest assessments of his nature: Seth was powerful enough to be desirable reinforcement, but unreliable enough that Erebus questioned whether calling someone incapable of properly distinguishing ally from enemy was rational.[2]
His first appearance reinforces this characterization. Amon summoned Seth and simply directed his attention toward the ongoing Greek war. Rather than expressing concern regarding the extremely complicated conflict or asking why he had been called, Seth smiled and immediately admitted that the situation interested him.[9]
Seth is also willing to exploit openings dishonorably. During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s battle with several [[Primordial Gods]], Seth did not challenge [[Scathach]] directly. He appeared behind her while she was occupied with [[Nocturnus]] and [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] and attempted to stab her in the back. The act immediately provoked an extreme response from Victor and ultimately became the principal reason Seth was tortured rather than simply released after the war.[3][4]
Despite his recklessness, Seth is neither stupid nor incapable of evaluating danger. He possesses considerable supernatural knowledge and correctly identified one of Victor's techniques as Strategic-Class [[Magic]] associated with Albedo, even recognizing that Victor had modified the original spell. Once Seth's instincts warned him that Victor was still watching him, he immediately stopped attempting to move rather than blindly continuing his escape.[1]
Seth also displays a strong instinct for self-preservation. Once captured after [[Olympus]], he attempted to invoke the threat of war with the [[Egyptian Pantheon]] to force Victor to release him. When the intimidation failed, he attempted to use his Soul Powers before Victor tore off his arm and began systematically destroying him.[4]
His greatest flaw is that fear does not permanently correct his behavior. Even after Victor left him with damage that Egyptian Gods could not heal, Seth later became involved in another conspiracy targeting Victor's faction. By the time Victor arrived at the [[Supernatural Gathering]], Seth was visibly terrified and internally regretted not remaining quiet. Ra's reaction toward him made it equally clear that the God-King blamed Seth heavily for their current situation.[5]
Seth therefore combines several seemingly contradictory characteristics: he can accurately recognize overwhelming danger when directly confronted by it, yet his hostility and schemes repeatedly drive him into provoking those same dangers after the immediate crisis has passed.

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Seth was first mentioned during the final war of the [[Greek Pantheon]].
As [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s involvement transformed the conflict and began overwhelming forces that had originally expected to control events from the background, [[Amon]] considered calling additional reinforcement. He proposed Seth to [[Erebus]].[2]
Erebus immediately questioned the idea because Seth was notorious for failing to distinguish between allies and enemies when fighting. Amon nevertheless considered the risk acceptable. With Erebus, [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] and Seth operating simultaneously, he believed even Victor would be unable to withstand three [[Primordial Gods]].[2]
Seth physically appeared shortly afterward. He manifested as a tall gray-skinned man with white hair and asked Amon what was sufficiently interesting to justify summoning him. After Amon directed his attention toward Victor's battlefield, Seth smiled and became interested in the conflict.[9]
His presence represented a significant escalation. Seth was not an ordinary Egyptian Major God summoned to provide numerical support; the conspirators specifically counted him as another Primordial-class combatant capable of participating in a battle already involving some of the strongest Gods of Greece.[2]
Seth's most consequential action occurred while [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] was occupied fighting [[Nocturnus]] and Tartarus.
[[Scathach]] possessed enough martial skill and lethality that the two Greek Primordial Gods were already forced to fight cautiously. Seth exploited her divided attention and appeared behind her, attempting to stab her from the rear.[3]
Victor saw the attack through his enhanced Draconic vision.
The sight of another Primordial God attempting to assassinate his wife enraged Victor enough that he unconsciously invoked the Dragon Language. With the command "Stop", Victor temporarily froze the battlefield, including the Primordial Gods and even the surrounding [[Olympus]] environment, long enough to remove Scathach from danger.[3]
Victor then unleashed an enormous combined attack upon Seth, Nocturnus and Tartarus. Seth recognized the danger quickly enough to escape the most destructive portion of the attack, as did Nocturnus, while Tartarus suffered much more severe damage.[3]
The failed assassination attempt was not forgotten.
After Victor's perception evolved further, he looked directly at Seth and became capable of identifying the Divine Concepts residing within the Egyptian God's Soul.
The principal characteristics he observed were:

Victor immediately focused upon Death. A sufficiently powerful Death Divinity meant Seth possessed authority capable of interacting directly with Souls, including the possibility of destroying them. To Victor, this meant Seth was one of the comparatively rare Gods on the battlefield with a power capable of posing a genuine existential danger to him and his wives.[1]
Seth also demonstrated considerable magical knowledge during this period. He recognized one of Victor's attacks as Strategic-Class [[Magic]] originating from Albedo's system but immediately noticed that Victor had modified the spell rather than reproducing it unchanged.[1]
Recognizing how rapidly the situation was deteriorating, Seth intended to withdraw. His instincts suddenly screamed at him that Victor was still watching, causing him to immediately stop moving rather than risk attracting the Demon King's attention at the wrong moment.[1]
Seth was also among the limited number of individuals capable of following Victor's increasingly extreme speed to some degree. While the Primordial Gods could properly perceive Victor's movements, Seth and [[Jeanne Elderblood]] could still observe them to a lesser extent, placing his supernatural perception considerably above that of ordinary Gods.[10]
Seth survived the destruction of Olympus but did not escape Victor.
After the major Greek enemies had been eliminated, Seth demanded his release and attempted to use the possibility of war with the [[Egyptian Pantheon]] as leverage. Victor had no intention of killing him, but not because he intended to show mercy.[4]
Victor reminded Seth that he had personally witnessed the God attempting to stab Scathach in the back.
When Seth attempted to activate his Soul Powers, Victor tore off his arm before he could properly respond. He then explained that Seth would indeed be returned to the Egyptian Pantheon alive—but deliberately ruined.[4]
Victor used his increasingly advanced Soul Manipulation to attack the underlying structure of Seth's Soul. His objective was not simple Soul destruction. Instead, he intended to shred the Soul while leaving it technically intact, producing a condition in which it remained present but permanently dysfunctional and incapable of healing correctly.[4]
The punishment included systematic damage to:
- Seth's physical body;
- his nervous system and ability to function normally;
- his spiritual structure;
- and, most importantly, his Soul.
Victor's methods were partially inspired by torture techniques associated with Lily [[Baal]], modified for use against an immortal Divine target.
Seth was then returned to his Pantheon alive.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s intention had been to leave Seth in an effectively vegetative condition, but the later story demonstrates that Seth recovered partially.
The [[Egyptian Pantheon]] possessed powerful healing resources, and Seth eventually regained enough functionality to walk, speak, reason and participate in political events. However, the core Soul damage inflicted by Victor proved beyond their ability to properly repair.[5]
Reports obtained by Victor's faction indicated that Seth had received extensive treatment after being returned to Egypt, yet his condition remained fundamentally broken.
Rather than withdrawing permanently from conflict, Seth became involved in the escalating conspiracy surrounding the [[Blood God Religion]], Victor's family and factions hostile to his influence.
Members associated with Victor's religion were targeted, internal betrayal and instability were encouraged, and the larger operation eventually became connected to the Egyptian leadership, [[New Dawn]] and the [[Elder Gods Pantheon]]. At first, Victor's faction considered several possibilities: Seth may have acted independently, Ra may have supported him for internal political reasons, or the Egyptian Pantheon may have been participating more broadly.[5]
Later information established Seth's importance much more clearly.
Seth arrived at the third major [[Supernatural Gathering]] alongside Ra and [[Anubis]]. Despite his recovery, the narration still described him as visibly broken.[5]
He remained mentally functional enough to observe Victor's newly developed civilization and compare it with his own barren Divine territory. However, his composure deteriorated as soon as Victor himself arrived.
Victor's presence imposed overwhelming pressure upon the entire Gathering, but the Egyptian delegation reacted particularly badly. Ra repeatedly looked toward Seth with an expression promising severe consequences, while Seth internally cursed his previous decisions and wished that he had simply remained quiet.[5]
The situation became progressively worse as one major faction after another sided with Victor. Seth grew increasingly nervous as it became apparent that the Egyptian Pantheon had badly miscalculated the balance of power.
When Victor directly focused his attention upon the Egyptians, Seth physically shrank beneath his gaze. Ra was furious not only at the crisis itself but at his own decision to listen to Seth in the first place.[6]
Victor subsequently revealed evidence connecting the Egyptian faction to the attempts against his family.
Rather than indiscriminately declaring war upon every Egyptian God, Victor refined his declaration and specifically named Ra, Anubis, Seth and their associates who planned the conspiracy as his targets.[6]
Seth was therefore not merely an incidental member of the Egyptian delegation; he was explicitly identified among the individuals responsible for provoking the war.
Ra later attempted to save himself by shifting responsibility onto Seth.
During his confrontation with Victor, Ra directly stated that Seth had been responsible for what occurred. Victor did not dispute that Seth had influenced events. Instead, he responded that it did not matter whether Ra had been manipulated, persuaded by Seth or simply failed to recognize the consequences of his own decisions. Ra was still the ruler who permitted action against Victor's family.[7]
The exchange establishes that Seth played a central role in persuading or manipulating Ra into the conspiracy, although it does not absolve the rest of the Egyptian leadership.
This makes Seth's actions particularly reckless. He had already personally experienced what Victor did to individuals who threatened [[Scathach]], suffered permanent Soul damage from that retaliation and then became involved in another scheme targeting Victor's family.
After the Gathering, Victor's declared war became a coordinated hunt.
Ra sealed the Egyptian Pantheon in an attempt to isolate it from invasion, but Victor's faction located an opening in the dimensional defenses. Rose Elderblood widened the path and the coalition entered.[8]
Before the assembled Gods and warriors could begin fighting, Velnorah asked Victor whether he wanted the targets killed or captured quickly. Victor explicitly required them alive because he intended to deal with them personally.[8]
Velnorah entered the Egyptian territory and deployed technology capable of interfering with Divine authority throughout the Pantheon.
Her system announced:
Divine Concept denied.
The Egyptian Gods suddenly found themselves unable to properly invoke their Divinities. Velnorah immediately marked Victor's designated enemies and deployed a mass spatial-capture system. Within moments, 76 selected targets had been isolated and secured inside Victor's portable prison artifact.[8]
Seth was among the Egyptian conspirators ultimately captured and enslaved after the Pantheon's defeat. Ra alone received a separate personal confrontation with Victor, during which he attempted to blame Seth before Victor stripped him of his Sun Divinity and reduced him to a Mortal.[7]
Seth consequently survived the Egyptian conquest but lost his remaining independence.

### Skills & Abilities
Seth is an ancient Second Generation Primordial God possessing significantly greater supernatural power and existential weight than an ordinary Third Generation God. His most important confirmed abilities derive from the three primary Divinities [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] directly observed within his Soul: Death, Weather and Animals.[1]
- God Physiology: Seth possesses the Divine body, Soul, Energy and supernatural longevity inherent to natural Gods. His physiology grants him vastly superhuman physical capabilities, longevity and resistance to ordinary forms of injury.
- Primordial God Physiology: Seth is repeatedly treated as a Primordial-class God. [[Amon]] specifically counted his addition alongside [[Erebus]] and [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] as producing a force of three [[Primordial Gods]], while Victor later perceived him as a foreign Primordial God when Seth attempted to attack [[Scathach]].[2][3]
- [[Divine Energy]] Manipulation: Seth can generate and control Divine Energy associated with his Concepts and Divine existence.
- Death Manipulation: Death is one of Seth's three principal Divinities. Victor immediately considered this authority dangerous because it potentially allowed Seth to bypass ordinary durability and directly affect the life and Soul of a target.[1]
- Soul Manipulation: Victor explicitly connected Seth's Death authority with the capacity to influence and destroy Souls. Seth later attempted to use his own Soul Powers against Victor after being captured, confirming that his abilities extend directly into spiritual interaction.[1][4]
- Soul Destruction: Seth's Death Divinity gives him the potential to destroy Souls rather than merely kill physical bodies. This was the aspect of his power that most immediately alarmed Victor.[1]
- Weather Manipulation: Weather is another of Seth's principal confirmed Divinities.[1] The complete range of this authority has never been demonstrated in an individual combat scene, so specific atmospheric techniques should not be attributed to him without further evidence.
- Animal Manipulation: Animals constitute Seth's third principal confirmed Divinity.[1] The story has not yet shown the exact manner in which Seth applies this authority.
- Enhanced Strength: As a Primordial-class God, Seth possesses physical power far beyond ordinary supernatural Beings, although his individual raw-strength ceiling has never been isolated through a dedicated feat.
- Enhanced Speed: Seth can move and react at speeds appropriate for combat between high-level Gods.
- Enhanced Reflexes: His reactions were sufficient for him to escape the primary impact of Victor's massive retaliatory attack after the attempted assassination of Scathach.[3]
- Enhanced Durability: Seth survived fighting within the catastrophic environment created by Victor during the destruction of [[Olympus]] and remained alive even after Victor deliberately subjected him to extreme physical and spiritual torture.
- Enhanced Perception: Seth was one of the few non-central combatants capable of perceiving Victor's high-speed movements to some extent when ordinary Gods could not properly follow them.[10]
- Danger Sense / Supernatural Instincts: Seth possesses highly developed combat instincts. During the Greek conflict, his instincts warned him that Victor was still observing him and that attempting to move would immediately place him in danger.[1]
- [[Magic]] Analysis: Seth possesses enough knowledge of high-level Magic to recognize Strategic-Class Magic associated with [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]] and immediately identify that Victor had modified the original technique.[1]
- Combat Experience: Having existed for eons, Seth possesses extensive supernatural combat experience. His instinctive reactions during the Greek war allowed him to survive situations in which less experienced Gods were rapidly eliminated.
- Stealth / Surprise Attack: Seth successfully positioned himself behind Scathach while she was occupied with [[Nocturnus]] and Tartarus and nearly attacked her without a conventional frontal confrontation. Victor's enhanced perception was required to identify the approaching threat.[3]

### Divinities

#### Death
Death is one of Seth's primary Divine Concepts and the most dangerous one directly identified by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[1]
Unlike simple destructive force, Seth's Death authority is capable of interacting with the spiritual components of a Being. Victor immediately interpreted the Concept as meaning Seth could influence Souls and potentially destroy them completely.
This made Seth especially dangerous against supernatural Beings whose normal immortality depends upon their Soul surviving physical destruction.

#### Weather
Weather is Seth's second directly confirmed major Divinity.[1]
The novel has not yet provided a dedicated demonstration of its full applications. Consequently, broader mythological associations such as Sandstorms, Deserts, Sky or generic Storm authority should not automatically be indexed as distinct canonical Divinities.
Weather itself can encompass numerous atmospheric phenomena, but until Seth demonstrates those applications they remain extensions of a confirmed Concept rather than individually established powers.

#### Animals
Animals is the third primary Divinity [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] observed within Seth's Soul.[1]
The exact scope of this Concept remains unknown. Seth has never received an extended battle in which he directly demonstrates command over animals, transformation into animal forms or the creation of animal-based manifestations.

### Irreversible Soul Damage
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s punishment produced one of the most unusual conditions suffered by a surviving God.
Ordinary Divine regeneration is capable of repairing damage that would permanently kill most Mortals. Simply mutilating Seth's body would therefore have been temporary.
Victor instead attacked the underlying structure of his Soul.[4]
His method was deliberately paradoxical: Seth's Soul was damaged severely enough that it could no longer function or naturally recover correctly, yet Victor avoided destroying it completely. Seth therefore remained alive without receiving the clean release of death.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] became Seth's greatest enemy after the events of the Greek war.
Their hostility was initially impersonal. Seth was summoned by [[Amon]] as an additional Primordial God intended to help suppress Victor. The relationship became deeply personal when Seth attempted to stab [[Scathach]] in the back.[2][3]
Victor did not forget the attack even after the broader Greek conflict ended. When Seth later demanded his release, Victor specifically cited the attempted assassination of his wife before beginning his punishment.[4]
Rather than killing Seth, Victor considered prolonged crippling more appropriate. He destroyed Seth's body and Soul in a manner intended to prevent normal Divine recovery and sent him back to Egypt alive.[4]
Seth nevertheless later became involved in another conspiracy targeting Victor's family and religious organization. This transformed their hostility from an incident of war into a repeated pattern.
At the [[Supernatural Gathering]], Seth's earlier confidence was completely absent. He shrank beneath Victor's attention and visibly feared what would happen once the Egyptian conspiracy was exposed.[5][6]
Victor eventually named Seth directly among the individuals against whom he was declaring war.

#### Scathach Elderblood Scarlett
[[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] was the target of Seth's most consequential attack.
During her battle against [[Nocturnus]] and [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]], Seth appeared behind her and attempted to stab her while she was occupied with the other [[Primordial Gods]].[3]
[[Scathach]] herself was capable of fighting the existing opponents, but [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] reacted before Seth could complete the ambush.
The attack permanently defined Victor's treatment of Seth. Even after the Greek conflict had effectively ended, Victor explicitly remembered Seth attempting to strike his wife from behind and used the incident as justification for destroying his Soul.[4]

#### Ra
Ra was Seth's God-King and eventually one of the principal figures influenced by his actions.
By the third [[Supernatural Gathering]], Ra clearly regretted listening to Seth. As [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s power and political support became increasingly obvious, Ra repeatedly directed furious looks toward the already-broken God.[5]
Later, when Victor confronted Ra directly, Ra attempted to shift blame by stating that Seth had caused the entire situation. Victor's response acknowledged that Seth may have convinced or manipulated Ra but refused to accept that as an excuse for the God-King's responsibility.[7]
Their relationship therefore appears to have deteriorated significantly by the time of the [[Egyptian Pantheon]]'s fall.

#### Amon
[[Amon]] was responsible for calling Seth into the Greek conflict.
Amon believed Seth's addition would provide enough power to overwhelm [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and specifically counted him as the third Primordial God alongside [[Erebus]] and [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]].[2]
This decision ignored Erebus' warning regarding Seth's indiscriminate nature.

#### Erebus
[[Erebus]] knew Seth well enough to distrust his behavior.
When [[Amon]] proposed summoning him, Erebus objected because Seth could not reliably distinguish ally from enemy during conflict.[2]
Despite the objection, Seth was summoned and joined the larger conspiracy surrounding the Greek conflict.

#### Anubis
[[Anubis]] and Seth later appeared together as part of the hostile Egyptian leadership at the [[Supernatural Gathering]].
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually named Ra, Anubis and Seth together as principal Egyptian targets responsible for the conspiracy against his family.[6]
Both Seth and Anubis survived the eventual conquest and became enslaved Divine enemies.

### Capture and Enslavement
Seth's second defeat was considerably less dramatic than his first because he was never permitted to properly fight.
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] invaded the sealed [[Egyptian Pantheon]], Velnorah deployed technology capable of interfering directly with Divine Concepts. A pulse spread throughout the Pantheon and prevented its Gods from properly accessing their Divinities.[8]
She then marked Victor's selected enemies and captured seventy-six targets simultaneously through a network of spatial Energy threads.[8]
The Egyptian conspirators were required alive because Victor intended to determine their punishment personally.
Seth survived the operation and became one of the Egyptian Gods held under Victor's authority. Current Imperial records consequently classify him as enslaved rather than deceased. His already damaged Soul also means his condition differs substantially from the other captured Egyptian Gods.
