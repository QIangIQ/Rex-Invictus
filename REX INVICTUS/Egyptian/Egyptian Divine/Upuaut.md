Chapter 934: A Just God. 2
Upuaut, also known as Wepwawet, Apuat and Ophois, is an Egyptian God whose confirmed Divine Concept encompasses War. Formerly a member of the [[Egyptian Pantheon]], he survived the Pantheon's defeat and was subsequently incorporated into [[Victor Elderblood]]'s civilization as a prisoner under the numerical designation 089U. Rather than continuously resisting the new regime, Upuaut cooperated with its development alongside fellow Egyptian Goddess Kuk, voluntarily assisting society, revealing useful locations previously unknown to the Empire and completing the conditions imposed upon his sentence. By Chapter 934, both Gods had earned their freedom, had their original identities formally restored and were publicly recognized as exemplary citizens of Victor's civilization.[1]
The older version of Upuaut's page classified him as a Third Generation Major God and listed Seth, Nephthys and [[Anubis]] as his immediate family. The currently verified novel material does not establish those genealogical relationships or explicitly state his Divine generation. They should therefore not be presented as confirmed MTW canon merely because similar relationships or associations exist within Egyptian mythology.

### Physical Appearance
Upuaut is directly described in Chapter 934 as a dark-skinned God.[1] No further canonical description of his hair, eyes, height, build, clothing, armor, weaponry or Divine appearance has presently been located. His current artwork should therefore be treated as an artistic interpretation beyond his confirmed gender and complexion.
Traditional representations of Wepwawet commonly use a canine or jackal-like form, but the novel has not established that Upuaut possesses an animal head, jackal transformation or canine true form.

### Personality
Upuaut receives little dialogue, but his conduct during imprisonment provides a clearer picture of his personality than the original stub contained. Following the destruction of the [[Egyptian Pantheon]]'s independent power, Upuaut did not spend his captivity attempting continuous rebellion. Instead, he and Kuk voluntarily helped [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s new society develop, performed their assigned duties without complaint and provided information concerning previously unknown locations that could materially benefit the Emperor.[1]
His behavior demonstrates pragmatism and adaptability. Whatever his opinion of Victor's conquest may originally have been, Upuaut recognized the reality of his position and chose productive cooperation over pointless resistance. This cooperation went beyond merely following commands under compulsion; the Imperial overseer specifically praised the pair for voluntarily contributing to society.[1]
When his freedom was formally restored, Upuaut responded with only a slight smile, although his expression made his relief obvious.[1] His restrained response suggests considerable composure and self-control. He did not use his release as an excuse to lash out at his former captors and instead accepted his new status as a citizen operating beneath Imperial law.
His eventual recognition as an exemplary citizen further indicates that his behavior during captivity was consistently acceptable rather than representing a single useful act.[1]

### History
Upuaut was originally one of the Gods belonging to the [[Egyptian Pantheon]], one of Earth's major Divine civilizations. The Pantheon contained numerous important Gods, including Ra, [[Amon]], [[Apep]], Seth, [[Anubis]], [[Thoth]], [[Ptah]], [[Khons]] and Kuk.
The Egyptian Pantheon eventually entered direct conflict with [[Victor Elderblood]] and his rapidly expanding faction. After diplomacy failed, Victor openly declared war upon the forces he considered hostile, and the Egyptian Pantheon's independent political structure was subsequently destroyed.[2] Its surviving population, resources and Divine territory were gradually absorbed into Victor's civilization, with the former Egyptian lands eventually developing into the region associated with Elvenorah.[3]
Upuaut survived the conflict but lost his former political freedom. He became one of the supernatural prisoners placed under Victor's punishment system and was stripped of his ordinary identity for the duration of his sentence, receiving the designation Number 089U.[1]
He served within the same system as Kuk, whose designation was 081K. Their status as Gods did not exempt them from punishment; Victor's developing civilization applied its laws and sentences according to allegiance and conduct rather than assuming Divine beings were automatically above accountability.[1]
Upuaut eventually distinguished himself through cooperation rather than resistance. Together with Kuk, he voluntarily participated in the development of the new society, worked without unnecessary complaints and revealed useful locations that had previously been unknown to the Empire.[1] These contributions ultimately fulfilled the requirements of his sentence and resulted in the restoration of his freedom.

### Synopsis

#### Dragon God of Chaos
By Chapter 934, Upuaut had already become a prisoner within the civilization constructed from territories conquered by [[Victor Elderblood]]. Prisoners were identified through numerical designations and controlled by enchanted collars that enforced obedience to the wardens responsible for administering them.[1]
The system was shown through the arrival of [[Luan Davis]], who was assigned the designation 069L. Luan was shocked to discover that the society contained enslaved Gods and quickly learned that Divine status provided no special exemption from Imperial punishment.[1]
An Ice Demon serving as one of the wardens eventually approached two Egyptian Gods standing nearby: Upuaut and Kuk. Upuaut was identified as Number 089U, while Kuk was Number 081K.[1]
The warden announced that the pair had accumulated significant accomplishments during their sentences. Unlike prisoners who merely obeyed because their collars compelled them, Upuaut and Kuk had voluntarily helped the society develop, carried out their responsibilities without complaining and revealed previously unknown locations that could provide considerable value to Victor and his civilization.[1]
Their contributions resulted in the termination of their sentences. The warden formally announced that Numbers 089U and 081K were now free and permitted to use their original identities again.[1]
Upuaut's identity was then publicly restored:
God Upuaut, with Concepts encompassing War.
Kuk was similarly restored as the Goddess whose Concepts encompassed the Unknown and Darkness. Both were officially proclaimed exemplary citizens and instructed to continue obeying the laws established by the Emperor.[1]
The surrounding Demons and prisoners applauded the announcement. Upuaut and Kuk responded with slight smiles, while their expressions made their relief at finally regaining their freedom obvious.[1]
Their release served a wider narrative purpose. Luan had questioned whether Victor's claims of justice actually meant anything when prisoners were subjected to forced servitude. Seeing two Gods genuinely complete their punishment and regain their freedom proved that the system was not designed exclusively as eternal enslavement. A prisoner who fulfilled the conditions imposed upon them could actually leave the system and become an ordinary citizen.[1]
Following his release, Upuaut ceased being a numbered slave and became a recognized member of Victor's civilization. Current character indexing consequently treats his [[Egyptian Pantheon]] membership as former while placing him within the broader population associated with Dragons Nest.

### Skills & Abilities
Upuaut has no direct on-screen combat scene, so his exact fighting ability, physical statistics and techniques remain unknown. Chapter 934 nevertheless provides one extremely important confirmation: Upuaut is explicitly a God whose Concepts encompass War.[1]

#### God Physiology
- Divine Physiology: Upuaut is a genuine God and therefore possesses a Divine body and supernatural existence fundamentally different from those of ordinary mortals.
- [[Divine Energy]] Manipulation: As a God, Upuaut possesses Divine Energy that can be used in conjunction with his Divinity. The appearance, quantity and personal characteristics of his Divine Energy have not been shown.
- Divine Soul: His Soul is capable of sustaining Divinity and Divine Energy and possesses the spiritual characteristics associated with Gods.
- Agelessness: Divine beings are not subject to ordinary mortal aging. Upuaut's exact chronological age has never been directly stated, so the previous value of Eons should be treated as unverified rather than a precise canonical age.
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: His Divine nature grants physical capabilities beyond those of ordinary Humans, but no individual feat currently establishes his exact strength, speed, durability, endurance or regeneration.
- Divinity: Upuaut possesses confirmed Divine authority associated with the Concept of War.[1]

#### War
War Divinity: Chapter 934 directly identifies Upuaut as a God whose Concepts encompass War.[1] This establishes War as his principal confirmed Divine Domain rather than simply a ceremonial title.
- War Manipulation: As a God whose Divinity encompasses War, Upuaut possesses supernatural authority tied to the Concept. The precise limits and applications of this authority have not been demonstrated.
- Conceptual Manipulation — War: Divinities function through the Concepts represented by their Gods. Upuaut can therefore interact with War conceptually through his Divine nature.
- [[Divine Energy]] Attribution: His Divine Energy can be shaped through his War Divinity and acquire properties corresponding to his Concept, although no specific manifestation has been shown.
- War Authority: Upuaut possesses Divine authority associated with War itself. This should not be confused with automatically possessing every power ever associated with warfare.
Potential applications such as battlefield enhancement, conflict inducement, weapon empowerment, army strengthening, combat precognition, tactical amplification or control over hostility remain unconfirmed until Upuaut actually demonstrates them.

#### Conceptual Manipulation
Conceptual Manipulation: Through his Divinity, Upuaut possesses a supernatural relationship with the Concept of War.[1] This does not grant unrestricted manipulation of unrelated Concepts and should be indexed specifically as War-based conceptual authority.

#### Divine Energy Attribute Manipulation
Gods can express their Divinities through [[Divine Energy]], giving otherwise neutral Divine Energy characteristics derived from their Concepts. Upuaut can therefore conceptually attribute his Divine Energy through War, although no named application or attack has yet appeared.

#### Supernatural Knowledge
As a former Egyptian God, Upuaut possesses knowledge accumulated through existence within one of Earth's major Pantheons. His usefulness to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s society demonstrates that this knowledge remained valuable even after the Empire gained access to substantially more advanced civilizations.[1]

#### Exploration and Intelligence Contribution
Upuaut and Kuk identified locations that were previously unknown to the Empire and which were considered potentially highly beneficial to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[1] The chapter does not explain whether Upuaut found these areas personally, remembered them from Egyptian records or discovered them through some supernatural ability. The accomplishment should therefore be indexed as demonstrated knowledge and intelligence contribution rather than an invented tracking power.

#### Adaptability
Upuaut demonstrated substantial psychological and social adaptability. He went from being a God of an independent Pantheon to a numbered slave under another ruler, yet adapted well enough to become officially recognized as an exemplary citizen. His behavior shows an ability to function effectively even after a complete collapse of his former political status.[1]

#### Combat Ability
Despite being the [[Ares|God of War]], Upuaut has not received a direct battle in the novel. No confirmed weapon, martial art, fighting style, Divine Artifact or named attack has been revealed.
His War Divinity logically makes him a combat-oriented God, but the available evidence does not establish whether his actual fighting ability falls within the Mid-Level God, High-Level God, Combat God, God-King or Primordial ranges. A conceptual association with War alone is not sufficient to assign a specific tier.

#### Unverified Abilities and Classification
The previous article classified Upuaut as a Third Generation God and Major God. No currently verified chapter found during this audit directly establishes either classification, so they should not be presented as confirmed facts until a source is located.
Likewise, the following powers should not be automatically added merely because they are associated with Wepwawet in Egyptian mythology:
- Jackal or Wolf Physiology;
- Animal Transformation;
- Path Manipulation;
- Portal Creation;
- [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] Manipulation;
- Death Manipulation;
- Soul Guidance;
- Hunting Manipulation;
- Tracking;
- Light Manipulation;
- Royal Authority;
- or unrestricted Weapon Mastery.
The name Wepwawet is traditionally interpreted as "Opener of the Ways", but this mythological title is not sufficient by itself to grant MTW's Upuaut Space Manipulation, dimensional travel or conceptual path-opening powers.

### Power
Upuaut's exact power level is unknown. He is a genuine God with a directly confirmed War Divinity, but the novel has not provided a fight or comparison allowing his overall combat strength to be placed accurately within the Divine hierarchy.[1]
His former status within the [[Egyptian Pantheon]] indicates that he was an established deity rather than a newly ascended God, but his generation and combat rank remain unverified. The article should therefore avoid describing him as a Third Generation Major God unless a direct chapter statement is found.

### Relationships

#### Kuk
Kuk is the character most closely associated with Upuaut in his only substantial appearance. Both survived the fall of the [[Egyptian Pantheon]], were imprisoned under [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s regime and received numerical identities: Upuaut became 089U while Kuk became 081K.[1]
They cooperated throughout their imprisonment, voluntarily contributed to societal development and supplied valuable information to the Empire. Their accomplishments were evaluated together, and both completed their sentences at the same time.[1]
During their release ceremony, their Divine identities were publicly restored side by side: Upuaut as the God associated with War and Kuk as the Goddess associated with the Unknown and Darkness. Both became exemplary citizens of the same civilization.[1]
The novel does not establish whether the two possessed a particularly close relationship before their imprisonment, but their shared circumstances make Kuk Upuaut's clearest known companion during the post-Egyptian period.

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] became Upuaut's sovereign following the destruction of the [[Egyptian Pantheon]]'s independent political authority. Upuaut initially entered Victor's civilization as a prisoner rather than as a voluntary follower.
Victor's punishment system nevertheless provided a route back to freedom. Upuaut fulfilled those conditions through productive service and was formally released rather than being permanently enslaved.[1]
His eventual status as an exemplary citizen demonstrates that Victor did not consider his original Egyptian allegiance an unforgivable offense once his sentence had been completed.

#### Egyptian Pantheon
Upuaut formerly belonged to the [[Egyptian Pantheon]] alongside Ra, [[Amon]], [[Apep]], Seth, [[Anubis]], [[Thoth]], [[Ptah]], [[Khons]] and Kuk. The Pantheon was eventually conquered and absorbed into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s expanding civilization.[2][3]
Current navigation records classify Upuaut as a former Egyptian Pantheon member, reflecting the loss of the faction's previous political structure and his later integration into Dragons Nest.

#### Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II
[[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]] played a major role in the operations surrounding the defeated Egyptian territories and later oversaw the development of Elvenorah. Older wiki material attributes Upuaut's capture specifically to Velnorah during the Egyptian hunt. Chapter 934 confirms that Upuaut was already an Egyptian prisoner within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s civilization but does not itself narrate the moment of capture, so the exact circumstances should remain unstated unless the relevant battle chapter is located.

#### Seth and Anubis
The previous article identified Seth as Upuaut's father, Nephthys as his mother and [[Anubis]] as his brother. These relationships have not been directly verified in the novel material reviewed for this page and appear to originate from mythology-based genealogy. They should therefore not remain in the canonical infobox without a chapter explicitly establishing them.

### Mythological Background
In Ancient Egyptian religion, Wepwawet—also rendered Upuaut, Apuat or Ophois—was a canine-associated deity whose name is commonly interpreted as the "Opener of the Ways". He was strongly connected to warfare, royal processions, hunting and the opening of paths for armies and rulers. Wepwawet was often depicted as a standing canine or a canine-headed deity associated with standards carried into battle.
Some later traditions and interpretations connect Wepwawet with other canine Egyptian deities such as [[Anubis]] and occasionally provide varying genealogies involving Egyptian Gods. These traditions are not uniform and should not automatically be transferred into MTW continuity.
The novel's most important confirmed adaptation is considerably simpler: Upuaut is explicitly identified as a God whose Concepts encompass War.[1]
