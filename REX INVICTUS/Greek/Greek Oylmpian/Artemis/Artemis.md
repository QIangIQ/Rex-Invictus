Wife of [[Percy Jackson]]

Sister of [[Apollo]]
Tolerable relationship. She respects his power but finds his antics immature. Prefers to keep her distance unless celestial duty demands cooperation. They share deep unspoken loyalty despite frequent disagreement.

Daughter of [[Zeus]]
She honors his position as king but attempts to maintain a sharp emotional distance. Strongly opposed to his treatment of mortal women and considers many of his decisions beneath divine dignity.

Daughter of [[Leto]]

Niece of [[Poseidon]]
They rarely interact nowadays, only due to matters concerning [[Him within the moon]]. She views his realm as chaotic and his temperament unstable. Nonetheless, she does not provoke him without cause.

Niece of [[Hades]]
Works closely with him to contain the billions of monsters exiting [[Tartarus (Realm)]] daily around the UOE

Niece of [[Demeter]]
Mild tension. Artemis appreciates nature but dislikes Demeter's obsession with growth cycles and fertility. They have had philosophical disputes over wilderness vs. agriculture.

Niece of [[Hera]]
She avoids Hera whenever possible.

Cousin of [[Ares]]
Open dislike. She considers Ares crude and dishonorable.

Cousin of [[Athena]]
Mutual respect. Though ideologically different, both share a fierce independence and disdain for male arrogance. Artemis trusts Athena more than most Olympians. Also one of the only factions not to implement the [[Répertoire du Savoir Athénien]]

Cousin of [[Hephaestus]]
Neutral alliance. Regular trade is to be conducted for arms resupply.

Cousin of [[Hermes]]
Mild amusement. Artemis tolerates him but remains wary of his trickster nature. His jokes rarely land, but he is useful.

Cousin of [[Aphrodite]]
Veiled animosity. Their divine philosophies are fundamentally opposed.
