**[[Festus]]** is a bronze automaton dragon built in [[Hephaestus' Cabin|Bunker 9]] at [[Camp Half-Blood]].

---

## PJO Canon

Before the camp had magical borders, he guarded the woods, said to be a gift from the [[Hephaestus' Cabin|Hephaestus campers]] to [[Hephaestus]]. He went rogue after his control disk failed, and in [[The Lost Hero]] Leo Valdez repairs him and names him Festus ("happy" in Latin). His head later becomes the figurehead of the *Argo II*, and Leo rebuilds him again to fight Gaea in [[The Blood of Olympus]].
