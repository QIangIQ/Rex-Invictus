The Three Sisters of Fate, also referred to as the Three Destiny Sisters and the Goddesses of Fate, are three ancient Goddesses associated with the [[Greek Pantheon]] who govern and preserve the Past, Present and Future of the beings connected to their mythology. In addition to serving as temporal custodians, the sisters possess Divine authority over Fate and are capable of producing prophecies by observing the Threads of Fate and the possible futures branching from the choices of living beings.[1][2]
Their purpose extends far beyond ordinary fortune-telling. Each sister continuously maintains one temporal division—Past, Present or Future—and their work is directly connected to the temporal continuity of the Greek mythological system. Kairos states that abandoning these responsibilities would cause the Past, Present and Future of the beings belonging to their mythology to fall apart.[1]
The sisters also possess a close relationship with the metaphysical force known as Fate. Fate exists as a non-sentient Conceptual force composed of a principal flow with innumerable branching possibilities. Individuals choose among those branches through their actions, meaning the future observed by Fate users is generally a collection of possibilities rather than an absolutely predetermined outcome.[3][2]
Through Fate Energy, the Sisters can observe temporal information, interpret possible futures and issue prophecies. Their abilities therefore occupy an intersection between Fate Manipulation, Clairvoyance, Precognition and temporal maintenance, although they should not be confused with the former Primordial Destiny, whose Authority once governed predetermined Destiny and the greater timeline structure of Creation.

### Overview
The Three Sisters of Fate are among the more unusual Divine Beings of the [[Greek Pantheon]] because their responsibilities are not primarily political or military. Rather than continuously participating in [[Olympus]]' internal disputes, each sister remains dedicated to a fundamental temporal responsibility connected to Greek mythology.[1]
Their three functions divide temporal continuity into:
Sister
Domain
Function
First Sister
Past
Maintains and preserves the Past of the beings connected to the Greek mythological system, ensuring that their established history remains part of the temporal continuity.[1]
Second Sister
Present
Continuously maintains the Present. She is the sister specifically discussed by [[Kairos]] when [[Aphrodite]] asks for help investigating [[Nightingale]].[1]
Third Sister
Future
Maintains the Future and its possible developments. Because the future remains influenced by present choices, this responsibility should not be interpreted as preserving one absolutely immutable future.[1][2]
The novel has not currently provided their individual names within the story. The three are primarily identified according to the temporal functions they maintain.

### Nature of Fate
The Sisters' powers are connected to the supernatural force of Fate.
Fate is described as a Conceptual force rather than a sentient administrator similar to The Owner of Limbo or other [[Regulators of Existence]]. It exists as a flow resembling a straight path from which countless branches emerge.[3]
These branches represent the different directions that can arise from decisions.
A person's existence can therefore be represented conceptually as:
┌── Possible Future A
│
Past ───── Present ────┼── Possible Future B
│
├── Possible Future C
│
└── Countless further possibilities
The individual determines which branch becomes relevant through their choices. This is why Fate-based prophecy is not equivalent to an absolute declaration that one event must occur.[3][2]
The Sisters are capable of interpreting this structure through their Divine authority.

### Fate and Prophecy
The Three Sisters are explicitly associated with prophecy.
They draw upon Fate Energy to perceive information contained within the Threads of Fate, allowing them to observe the Past, understand the Present and look toward possible Futures.[2]
This prophetic sight can reveal events that may occur if the relevant circumstances continue developing in a particular direction.
It does not automatically reveal one unavoidable future.
Chapter 657 clarifies that even Gods connected to Fate do not see the future as a perfectly completed object. Future events have not yet completely occurred and can be changed by the decisions made in the Present.[2]
Their prophetic system therefore follows the principle:
FATE ENERGY
│
▼
THREADS OF FATE
│
├── Past information
├── Present circumstances
└── Possible future branches
│
▼
PROPHECY
A prophecy represents a possible development observed through those Threads rather than unrestricted knowledge of everything that will ever happen.

### Temporal Function
The Sisters possess an additional responsibility beyond ordinary prophecy: maintaining the temporal continuity of the Greek mythological system.
During Chapter 247, [[Aphrodite]] asks [[Kairos]] about one of the Sisters. Kairos explains that she remains occupied maintaining the Present while her sisters perform equivalent responsibilities for the Past and Future.[1]
Aphrodite questions why the Sisters cannot simply abandon their posts temporarily and enjoy themselves.
Kairos explains that doing so would cause the Past, Present and Future of the beings belonging to their mythology to collapse.[1]
This establishes that their work is not ceremonial.
Their continued activity serves as part of the metaphysical infrastructure supporting the [[Greek Pantheon]] and its associated mythological Reality.

#### Past
The sister responsible for the Past maintains the historical continuity belonging to the Greek mythological system.[1]
The Past contains events that have already occurred and therefore differs from the uncertain nature of future possibilities.
Her precise method of maintaining historical continuity has not been demonstrated directly.

#### Present
The sister associated with the Present continuously maintains the current temporal state of the mythology.[1]
She is the only Sister indirectly involved with the main plot during their introductory chapter. [[Kairos]] contacts her while investigating the temporal abnormality surrounding [[Nightingale]].
Her connection to the Present apparently allows information regarding ongoing temporal conditions to be investigated even when those conditions exist inside a heavily isolated supernatural Realm.

#### Future
The final sister maintains the Future.[1]
Because later explanations establish that future events are not completely fixed, maintaining the Future should not be interpreted as forcing every Being toward a predetermined Destiny.
Instead, Fate contains numerous potential branches whose relevance changes according to decisions made in the Present.[3][2]
Her role therefore concerns preserving the temporal framework through which future possibilities can develop.

### Relationship Between Fate and Destiny
Fate and Destiny are related concepts within the cosmology but should not be treated as identical.

The distinction became especially important after Destiny was erased.
The removal of the Primordial Destiny eliminated the universal system of absolute predetermination, but prophecy and future sight continued functioning. Beings could still observe possible future events through Fate, Time and the Akashic Records; what disappeared was the requirement that Reality necessarily proceed toward one predetermined cosmological outcome.
The Three Sisters therefore remain prophetic Fate Goddesses rather than replacements for Primordial Destiny.

### Relationship with the Akashic Records
Fate-based perception overlaps heavily with information preserved within the [[Akashic Records]].
The Records contain the information of Creation across Past, Present and possible Future states. The author later describes the Akashic Records as a vast database containing the future, present and past of Creation.[4]
Fate users can access information resembling portions of this temporal structure by examining Threads of Fate.
However, the Three Sisters are not established as owners or administrators of the Akashic Records.
Their confirmed jurisdiction is the Greek Fate system and the Past, Present and Future associated with their mythology.

### Synopsis

#### A New Pillar
The Three Sisters become relevant during [[Victor Elderblood]]'s attempt to discover why [[Nightingale]] had suddenly become inaccessible.
After Nightingale was sealed by [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]], conventional portals and communications stopped functioning. Victor eventually sought information from [[Aphrodite]].[1]
Aphrodite did not possess the necessary temporal information herself, so she contacted [[Kairos]], the Greek God associated with Opportune Time.[1]
Kairos recognized Nightingale as Vlad's kingdom but needed assistance from someone with greater access to the relevant temporal information. He therefore contacted one of his acquaintances among the Sisters of Fate.[1]
During their conversation, Aphrodite asked how the Goddess was doing.
Kairos answered that she remained occupied performing the same responsibility she had maintained for an enormous period of time: preserving the Present.[1]
Aphrodite then asked about the other two Sisters.
Kairos explained that they were similarly occupied preserving the Past and Future.[1]
Aphrodite questioned why the three could not simply stop working and leave temporarily.
Kairos rejected the idea and explained that if one of the Sisters abandoned her responsibility, the Past, Present and Future of the beings belonging to their mythology would begin falling apart.[1]
Despite understanding why their responsibilities were necessary, Kairos admitted that spending thousands of years performing the same task sounded extremely boring.[1]
Through this connection, information concerning Nightingale was eventually obtained. Kairos later informed Aphrodite that Vlad had deliberately closed the Realm and altered its temporal relationship with the outside world.[5]
Their indirect involvement therefore provided Victor with the first reliable confirmation that Nightingale's disappearance was the result of intentional Time manipulation rather than an ordinary magical malfunction.

### Limits
Despite their extraordinary responsibilities, the Sisters should not be interpreted as possessing unlimited authority over Time, Fate or Destiny.
- Pantheon/Mythology Scope: [[Kairos]] specifically describes their responsibility in relation to the beings belonging to their mythology. Their known temporal-maintenance function should therefore not automatically be expanded to the entire Universe.[1]
- Future Is Mutable: The future has not yet been completely created. Present choices alter which potential branch becomes relevant.[2]
- Prophecy Is Not Absolute: A prophecy describes an observed future possibility and can become irrelevant if circumstances change.
- Fate Is Not Absolute Destiny: The branching flow of Fate described in Chapter 595 differs from the predetermined universal Destiny that existed under the former Primordial Destiny.[3]
- Not [[Primordial Entities]]: No evidence identifies the Sisters as [[Regulators of Existence]]. They are Greek Divine Beings operating through Fate-related Divinity.
- Not Universal Time Itself: Their responsibilities involve temporal maintenance, but they are not established as embodiments of universal Time.
- Duty-Bound: Their positions severely restrict their freedom. Chapter 247 implies that simply abandoning their duties would threaten the temporal structure they maintain.[1]
- Unknown Combat Capability: No direct battle involving the three Sisters has currently been shown, so their combat statistics and offensive applications cannot be reliably determined.

### Relationships

#### Kairos
[[Kairos]] possesses a personal connection with the Sisters and is sufficiently familiar with at least one of them to contact her directly when temporal information is required.[1]
He understands the burden of their responsibilities and displays subtle sadness when discussing how long they have remained bound to their respective duties.
Despite accepting that their work is necessary, Kairos agrees with [[Aphrodite]] that performing the same task for thousands of years sounds extremely monotonous.[1]
His relationship with them makes him an intermediary between more conventional Greek Gods and the Divine authorities responsible for Fate and temporal continuity.

#### Aphrodite Elderblood
[[Aphrodite]] is familiar enough with the Sisters to ask [[Kairos]] about their wellbeing during an otherwise unrelated investigation.[1]
Her reaction indicates that their endless duty is well-known among at least some ancient members of the [[Greek Pantheon]].
Aphrodite questions why they do not simply leave temporarily, demonstrating that even other Gods do not necessarily understand the full consequences attached to the Sisters' positions until those consequences are explained.

#### Greek Pantheon
The Sisters are part of the metaphysical infrastructure of the [[Greek Pantheon]].
Unlike political Gods who rule cities, armies or populations, their responsibility concerns the continuity of the mythology itself.
The fact that their abandonment could destabilize Past, Present and Future demonstrates that certain Gods perform structural roles within a Divine Reality rather than existing merely as powerful inhabitants of it.[1]
