A Satyr (sometimes called a faun) is a hybrid animal with the upper body of a human and the lower body of a goat. They are very pale, with large heads, forked tongues, and small sharp teeth. Satyrs feed on fear.

#### Phobos and Deimos
Main article: [[Phobos]] and [[Deimos]]
The only known satyrs are Phobos and Deimos (the Greek words for "fear" and "dread" respectively), who accompany [[Mars Ultor]]. In [[The Magician]], they try to feed on [[Josh Newman]] when [[Dr. John Dee]] brings him to be awakened.
