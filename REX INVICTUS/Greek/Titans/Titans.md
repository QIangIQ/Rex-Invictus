**[[Titans]]** are the elder gods, children of [[Gaia]] and [[Ouranos]], who ruled before the [[Olympians]] until the [[Titanomachy]]. See also the general definition [[Titan]].

## The twelve
- [[Oceanus]] and [[Tethys]]
- [[Hyperion]] and [[Theia]]
- [[Koios]] and [[Phoebe (Titan)]]
- [[Kronos]] and [[Rhea]]
- [[Mnemosyne]], [[Themis]], [[Krios]], [[Iapetus]]

## Second generation and allies
[[Atlas]], [[Helios]], [[Selene]], [[Leto]], [[Perseus (Titan)]], [[Clymene]], [[Hecate]] (Titan-born), [[Hesperide|Hesperides]]

## Their brothers
The [[Cyclopes]] ([[Arges]], [[Brontes]], [[Steropes]]) and the [[Hecatoncheires]] ([[Briares]], [[Cottus]], [[Gyges]])

## Canon
In [[Percy Jackson and the Olympians]] [[Kronos]] leads the Second [[Titanomachy|Titan War]] from [[Mt. Othrys]]. In [[Rex Invictus]] Kronos stays unseen and [[Atlas]] falls at Othrys.
