### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 795
Helios is an ancient God, a member of the Titan race and a Second Generation Primordial God whose primary Divinity is the Sun. He belonged to the faction of Kronus during the final conflict of the [[Greek Pantheon]] and stood among the most powerful surviving [[Titans]] alongside [[Atlas]].[1][2]
As a Solar deity of Primordial-God caliber, Helios possessed considerably greater Divine power than an ordinary Olympian. His Sun Divinity was significant enough that [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett|Scathach Scarlett]] deliberately selected him as her opponent after [[Victor Elderblood]] empowered her spear with his Soul-severing Blood, wanting to test whether she could metaphorically "freeze the Sun."[1]
Despite initially behaving arrogantly toward Victor and openly lusting after [[Aphrodite]], Helios quickly understood the overwhelming danger presented by Victor's Blood Age. After witnessing the Demon King's Immortal Army, Seven [[Blood Dragons]], the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse and numerous Demonic entities emerge from the Sea of Blood, Helios abandoned his earlier aggression and attempted to negotiate. Victor refused, reminding him that Helios himself had declared that anything standing in their way would simply be annihilated.[3]
Helios survived the initial massacre of the Greek Gods and remained among the surviving elite forces during the later stages of the war. Chapter 799 explicitly identifies him and Atlas as examples of the Titans present who possessed the power of Second-Generation [[Primordial Gods]].[2]

### Physical Appearance
Helios' most immediately recognizable characteristic is his flaming head. When he first appeared among the combined Greek forces, his head was visibly covered in flames, making his identity as a Solar deity immediately apparent.[3]
During his confrontation with [[Scathach]], Helios wore Divine clothing capable of surviving the environment of the [[Greek Underworld]] and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood Age. Scathach nevertheless cut through all of his Divine clothes so quickly that Helios initially failed to understand what had happened.[1]
When threatened, Helios could visibly cover his body with the Divinity of the Sun, surrounding himself with his Solar Divine Power.[1]

### Personality
Helios initially displayed the arrogance commonly associated with the older Greek Gods and [[Titans]]. When [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood attempted to peacefully remove [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his faction from the conflict between [[Olympus]], the Titans and [[Persephone Elderblood]], Helios rejected her proposal without hesitation. He looked at Aphrodite lustfully and declared that Victor was standing in their way and would therefore be annihilated.[3]
His behavior also demonstrated a pronounced lack of restraint toward Aphrodite. Even while facing Victor directly, Helios openly desired the Goddess of Beauty in front of her husband. Victor remembered the insult after activating Blood Age and specifically cited Helios' desire for Aphrodite as one of the reasons the [[Greek Pantheon]] would cease to exist.[3]
However, Helios was not suicidally prideful once the difference in power became undeniable. The appearance of Victor's enormous army caused his attitude to reverse almost immediately. Faced with creatures that were permanently killing Gods, resurrecting the fallen into Victor's own army and preventing the Greek deities from properly using their Concepts, Helios attempted to negotiate with the Demon King rather than continuing to blindly provoke him.[3]
Helios was also capable of recognizing genuine skill regardless of the opponent's race. During his duel with [[Scathach]], he was shocked upon realizing that she had reached the level of a Grandmaster despite being a Mortal-born Vampire. His reaction was disbelief rather than dismissing her achievement, showing that he understood the significance of high-level martial mastery.[1]

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings

##### The Final War of the Greek Pantheon
Helios entered the story during the decisive battle occurring within the [[Greek Underworld]]. The conflict had originally involved the Olympian forces of [[Zeus]], the Titan faction of Kronus and the Underworld faction led by [[Persephone Elderblood]], but the confrontation changed completely after [[Victor Elderblood]] arrived to protect his family from [[Erebus]] and [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]].[3]
Hundreds of Gods and [[Titans]] gathered against Victor. While many reacted cautiously to the overwhelming Negative Energy surrounding him, several of the most arrogant Divine Beings remained confident. After [[Apollo]] was silenced merely by Victor turning his gaze toward him, [[Aphrodite]] attempted to prevent further conflict and declared that Victor's group had no reason to participate in their war.[3]
Helios immediately rejected her proposal. With a lustful expression directed toward Aphrodite, he argued that Victor was already standing in their path and should therefore simply be annihilated. Aphrodite responded by releasing her enhanced Divine Charm across the entire battlefield. Helios, like virtually every other God present, was immediately placed beneath her influence until an unknown participant dispelled the effect.[3]
Once diplomacy failed, Victor activated Blood Age. The Greek Underworld was transformed into a vast hellscape of Blood, with red clouds forming overhead and Blood raining continuously across the battlefield. The Sea of Blood carried overwhelming Negative Energy capable of interfering with the Gods' Divine Concepts.[3]
Helios and Apollo, despite both possessing Solar Divinities, attempted to summon the Sun but were unable to do so. They were already disadvantaged because they were fighting inside the Greek Underworld rather than [[Olympus]], meaning their usual territorial reinforcement was absent, while Victor's Blood continuously interfered with their ability to spread their Concepts.[3]
The situation rapidly deteriorated. Creatures formed from Victor's Blood slaughtered Gods throughout the battlefield, while the Souls contained within Victor's existence manifested through the Sea of Blood. Victor created enormous Dragons composed of corpses and Souls, summoned the Horsemen of the Apocalypse, manifested previously consumed Demonic Beings and constructed a gigantic Blood Castle from which he commanded the battlefield.[3]
The destruction finally broke Helios' earlier confidence. Seeing the forces surrounding him, he shouted toward Victor and attempted to negotiate. Victor immediately reminded Helios of his previous statement that anyone standing in their way should simply be annihilated. Helios' expression darkened as he realized that Victor had no intention of accepting a ceasefire.[3]
Victor then specifically recalled that Helios had openly desired Aphrodite in front of him. He declared that the [[Greek Pantheon]] would cease to exist and ordered his Immortal Army and Horsemen to begin their massacre.[3]

##### Battle Against Scathach
As Gods began dying permanently under [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul-severing attacks, Victor summoned and empowered additional members of his faction. He covered [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]]'s spear with his Blood, granting the weapon the ability to sever the Souls of Gods and thereby permanently kill Divine Beings rather than merely destroying their physical vessels.[1]
[[Scathach]] entered her [[Vampire Count Form]] and immediately selected Helios as her opponent. Looking toward the Titan God of the Sun, she openly wondered whether she could freeze the Sun and launched herself toward him using a platform of Ice as leverage.[1]
Her opening thrust targeted Helios' heart and produced several sonic booms from the force of her acceleration. Helios reacted quickly enough to avoid being impaled, demonstrating why he was classified as a Second-Generation Primordial God, but he was unable to escape completely and received a deep wound across his chest.[1]
Confused by the unfamiliar Vampire attacking him, Helios asked whether Scathach was another of Victor's lovers. Scathach corrected him, declaring that Victor did not have lovers but wives and openly identifying herself as one of them—and, in her own words, the strongest one.[1]
Scathach attacked again. Helios prepared to dodge, only to realize that she had not directly struck his body. Instead, all of his Divine clothing had already been cut apart before he could perceive the exact sequence of her movements. Surprised by both her speed and technique, Helios retreated several steps while covering himself with the Divinity of the Sun.[1]
Helios then recognized Scathach as a Grandmaster. The realization visibly shocked him because Scathach was a Mortal-born existence rather than an ancient Divine Being, yet she had reached a level of martial mastery capable of threatening a Second-Generation Primordial God.[1]
The complete conclusion of their individual exchange was not directly shown because the narrative moved between the numerous simultaneous battles occurring throughout the [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]]. Helios nevertheless survived the encounter. After [[Zeus]] used the destroyed [[Master Bolt]] to temporarily impose [[Olympus]]' territory over part of the Greek Underworld, Helios remained among the surviving elite Gods and [[Titans]], although he had been significantly weakened by the fighting.[4]

##### Collapse of the Titan Faction
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s initial offensive devastated both Greek armies. More than seventy percent of Kronus' forces were killed during Blood Age, while [[Zeus]]' side suffered losses exceeding ninety percent. Only the elites remained, many of whom were already injured. Helios was specifically identified alongside [[Ares]] as one of the powerful Gods who had been weakened during Victor's assault.[4]
The situation grew even worse after Victor personally killed both Zeus and Kronus, destroying the leadership of the two principal surface factions. With both [[God-Kings]] dead, command fell primarily to figures such as [[Atlas]] and Ares while [[Persephone]]'s [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] forces surrounded the surviving Olympians and [[Titans]].[2]
Despite the enormous losses, several exceptionally powerful beings remained. The remaining Titan forces included multiple Second-Generation [[Primordial Gods]], with the narration explicitly identifying Atlas and Helios as examples of this class. Helios therefore survived not only his confrontation with [[Scathach]] but also Victor's initial Blood Age massacre and the deaths of the two God-Kings.[2]
The war eventually resulted in the destruction of the old Greek political order. [[Olympus]] ceased functioning as an independent Pantheon, and Victor later used his authority over its Dimension as the foundation for the new [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]].

### Skills & Abilities
Helios is considerably stronger than the average Greek God. As a Titan belonging to the Second-Generation Primordial God class, his Divine power places him within the Primordial-God range rather than among ordinary Major Gods.[1][2]
- God Physiology: Helios possesses the supernatural characteristics of a natural-born God, including [[Divine Energy]], immense longevity, supernatural physical capabilities and a Divine Soul.
- Titan Physiology: As one of the [[Titans]], Helios belongs to the older Divine generation descending from the primordial founders of the [[Greek Pantheon]]. Titans such as Helios and [[Atlas]] can reach the level classified as Second-Generation [[Primordial Gods]].[2]
- Second-Generation Primordial God Physiology: Helios possesses the Divine power of a Second-Generation Primordial God. His classification is explicitly stated during his confrontation with [[Scathach]] and again when the surviving forces of [[Olympus]] are evaluated following the deaths of [[Zeus]] and Kronus.[1][2]
- Divine Energy Manipulation: As a Primordial-class God, Helios possesses enormous quantities of Divine Energy and can manifest that Energy through his Divinity, physical reinforcement and supernatural abilities.
- Sun Manipulation: The Sun is Helios' defining Divine authority. He possesses the ability to exert his Divinity over Solar phenomena and attempted to summon the Sun while fighting within the [[Greek Underworld]].[3]
- Sun Summoning: Helios normally possesses enough authority over his Concept to invoke or summon the Sun. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood Age prevented him from doing so because its Negative Energy interfered with Divine Concepts while Helios was already outside the favorable environment of Olympus.[3]
- Solar Divinity Manifestation: Helios can directly surround his body with the Divinity of the Sun. He used this ability defensively after recognizing the danger posed by Scathach's Grandmaster-level techniques.[1]
- Solar Energy Manipulation: Helios can channel the supernatural Energy connected to his Sun Divinity through his body. The exact full applications of this Energy were not displayed before the Greek war ended.[1]
- Heat Generation: Helios' physical manifestation is naturally associated with immense Solar heat, with his head permanently appearing engulfed in flames.[3]
- Enhanced Strength: Helios possesses physical power befitting a Second-Generation Primordial God. Although the novel has not shown a dedicated strength feat from him, his classification places his body and Divine reinforcement far above ordinary Gods.[1][2]
- Enhanced Durability: Helios remained active despite the highly corrosive environment of Blood Age, the weakening effects of the Greek Underworld, Scathach's initial chest wound and the enormous destruction occurring across the battlefield.[3][1]
- Enhanced Speed and Reflexes: Helios reacted quickly enough to Scathach's opening Grandmaster-level attack to avoid being impaled through the heart. He could not completely evade the strike, but his reaction limited the attack to a deep chest wound.[1]
- Extrasensory Perception: Helios could perceive the level of Scathach's martial mastery after exchanging only a small number of movements with her, correctly recognizing that she had reached the level of a Grandmaster.[1]
- Divine Combat Experience: Helios has existed for eons and fought as one of the elite Titans during the Greek civil conflict. His reaction to Scathach's lethal opening strike demonstrates considerable experience dealing with sudden high-level attacks.[1]

### Divinity

#### Sun
The Sun is Helios' primary confirmed Divinity. His identity as a Titan God is directly connected to Solar authority, and his power is sufficiently developed for him to be classified as a Second-Generation Primordial God.[3][2]
Helios' Solar Divinity allows him to summon the Sun and manifest Solar Divine Power around himself. However, the final Greek war demonstrated that the effectiveness of even a Primordial-class Divinity can depend heavily upon environment and conceptual interference.
During Blood Age, Helios was unable to summon the Sun because:
- he was fighting within the [[Greek Underworld]] rather than [[Olympus]];
- he lacked the territorial reinforcement normally supplied by the Pantheon;
- the Greek Underworld naturally favored Chthonic Gods connected to its environment;
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Sea of Blood was saturated with overwhelming Negativity;
- and the Blood continuously disrupted the Concepts of the opposing Gods.[3]
This did not mean Helios permanently lost his Divinity. When directly confronted by [[Scathach]] shortly afterward, he was still capable of covering his body with the Divinity of the Sun.[1]

### Relationships

#### Kronus
Kronus was the God-King leading the Titan faction during the Greek civil war. Helios fought alongside the Titan forces beneath his broader command and remained one of the faction's most powerful surviving members until Kronus was killed by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[2]
After Kronus' death, [[Atlas]] became one of the principal figures capable of maintaining order among the remaining Titan forces.[2]

#### Atlas
[[Atlas]] and Helios were among the most powerful [[Titans]] still present during the final destruction of [[Olympus]]. Both were explicitly identified as Second-Generation [[Primordial Gods]].[2]
Atlas appears to have occupied the more important battlefield-command role, particularly after Kronus' death, while Helios functioned primarily as one of the faction's elite combatants.

#### Aphrodite Elderblood
Helios displayed immediate physical desire toward [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood when she attempted to prevent the Greek factions from fighting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. His openly lecherous expression and refusal to cooperate contributed directly to the situation escalating into a massacre.[3]
Aphrodite's enhanced Charm was powerful enough to instantly overwhelm Helios and most of the other Gods on the battlefield. He remained under her control until the Charm was externally dispelled.[3]
Helios' open desire for Aphrodite was also taken personally by Victor. When Helios later attempted to negotiate, Victor explicitly recalled that he had dared to desire his wife in front of him before declaring the destruction of the [[Greek Pantheon]].[3]

#### Scathach Elderblood Scarlett
[[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] personally selected Helios as her opponent after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] empowered her spear with Soul-severing Blood. She considered the confrontation an opportunity to test whether her Ice could metaphorically "freeze the Sun."[1]
Helios' superior Divine classification allowed him to survive [[Scathach]]'s initial surprise attack, but she still inflicted a deep wound across his chest. He became increasingly shocked as the fight continued, particularly after Scathach effortlessly destroyed his Divine clothing and he realized that she had reached the level of a Grandmaster despite originating as a Mortal.[1]
Contrary to earlier summaries of the battle, Scathach was not shown killing Helios. He survived their confrontation and remained among the Titan elites during the subsequent stages of the war.[2]

#### Victor Elderblood
Helios' first direct interaction with [[Victor Elderblood]] was openly hostile. He rejected [[Aphrodite]]'s attempt to avoid conflict and declared that because Victor stood in the Greek Gods' path, he would simply be annihilated.[3]
Victor later turned Helios' own words against him when Helios became terrified by Blood Age and attempted diplomacy. Victor reminded him that Helios had already chosen annihilation as the solution when he believed himself to possess the advantage.[3]
Victor additionally took offense at Helios openly desiring Aphrodite in front of him. The incident became one of the personal provocations Victor cited immediately before ordering the complete slaughter of the Greek forces.[3]

### Weaknesses
- Hostile Territory: Helios' Solar authority is significantly less effective outside an environment favorable to his Pantheon. Inside the [[Greek Underworld]] he did not receive the reinforcement [[Olympus]] normally supplied.[3]
- Conceptual Suppression: Powerful hostile Energy capable of interfering with Divine Concepts can prevent Helios from using his full Solar authority. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood Age completely prevented him from summoning the Sun.[3]
- Martial Skill Gap: Despite possessing vastly greater Divine power than an ordinary Mortal, Helios had difficulty following the movements of Grandmaster-level [[Scathach]]. Raw Divine status alone did not compensate for the difference in martial technique.[1]
- [[Aphrodite]]'s Charm: Helios was vulnerable to Aphrodite Elderblood's Divinity and fell beneath her Charm alongside the majority of the Greek Gods.[3]

### Trivia
- Helios is one of the comparatively few explicitly identified Second-Generation [[Primordial Gods]] within the [[Greek Pantheon]].[1][2]
- The novel specifically pairs Helios and [[Atlas]] as examples of Titan Second-Generation Primordial Gods.[2]
- Helios and [[Apollo]] both possessed Solar authority. Both attempted to summon the Sun during Blood Age and were simultaneously prevented from doing so.[3]
- Helios was one of the first Greek Gods to openly reject [[Aphrodite]]'s attempt to peacefully disengage [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] from the war.[3]
- He was also one of the first major Gods to attempt diplomacy once Victor revealed the full scale of Blood Age.[3]
- Victor specifically remembered Helios' lust toward Aphrodite and used it as an additional reason not to show him mercy.[3]
- [[Scathach]] chose Helios because she wanted to discover whether she could metaphorically "freeze the Sun."[1]
- Helios immediately recognized Scathach as a Grandmaster after witnessing her movements, and was shocked that a Mortal-born existence had reached that level.[1]
- Earlier versions of this article listed dozens of spouses, lovers and children taken directly from real-world Greek mythology. These relationships have not been individually established within the novel and are therefore not treated as canonical character relationships on this page.
- Earlier summaries also implied that Scathach defeated or killed Helios during Chapter 796. The chapter only shows her wounding and overwhelming him in their initial exchange; Chapter 799 later confirms that Helios remained alive among the surviving [[Titans]].[1][2]
