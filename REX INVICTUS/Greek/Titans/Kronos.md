**[[Kronos]]** is the lord of the [[Titan|Titans]], son of [[Ouranos]] and [[Gaia]], husband of [[Rhea]] and father of the elder [[Olympians]]. He wields [[Kronos' Scythe]] and is the main antagonist of [[Percy Jackson and the Olympians]].

## Greek History (IRL)

Kronos castrated [[Ouranos]] with an adamantine sickle given by [[Gaia]] and ruled the Golden Age. Warned that his child would overthrow him, he swallowed his children. [[Zeus]] survived, made him disgorge them and defeated the Titans in the [[Titanomachy]]. Kronos was imprisoned in [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] or, in other traditions, rules the Isles of the Blessed. He was later conflated with [[Chronos]] (time). Roman counterpart: Saturn.

---

## PJO Canon

Kronos' remains rest in [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] and he whispers to [[Luke Castellan]]. He rebuilds his body in the golden coffin on the *Princess Andromeda* ([[The Sea of Monsters]]), possesses [[Luke Castellan|Luke]] ([[The Battle of the Labyrinth]]) and attacks [[Olympus]] in [[The Last Olympian]]. Luke stabs himself to destroy him, and Kronos is scattered.

## Rex Invictus (Present Day)

In Rex Invictus Kronos is unseen. His forces were repulsed at Long Island in [[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]], and Article VII of the [[Anabasis Accord]] binds the Imperials to fight him.

#### Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **The present-day Kronos** is the only on-stage relevance. He is never seen directly, only through [[Luke Castellan|Luke]] and through his time-field.
- **Not [[Chronos]]:** [[Chiron]] insists Kronos is "the Titan of harvest, not to be mistaken with the Primordial of time Chronos" ([[Ch03 - Rex Natus I|Ch3]]). Yet in [[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]], [[Minerva (AI)|Minerva]] attributes the momentum-stealing field to the "only one being in this world [with] authority over time", implying Kronos. Separately, Chiron says Eleni bears "the mark of my father's namesake. Chronos", and future demigods may "punch through Chronos' Hourglass" ([[Ch15 - Rex Invictus VIII|Ch15]]).
- **Future:** in the old timeline, [[Percy Jackson|Percy]]'s war against Kronos is history. The future [[Thalia Grace|Thalia]] "had faced Kronos" ([[Ch23 - Interlude I|Ch23]], remembering the distant past; this is not a UOE-era appearance).

#### Appearance
- Unseen. [[Chiron]]'s class shows a statue of him "eating his own children" and notes he is "always" depicted "with a scythe" ([[Ch03 - Rex Natus I|Ch3]]).
- His presence in [[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]: "a shimmer… faint and golden and old" around the ship. [[Poseidon]] feels the cold: "He had felt it once before, a long time ago, when his father's scythe came up out of the dark." ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]])

#### Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Time manipulation:** a golden field around the *Princess Andromeda* "takes speed away". DMAPFSDS rail rounds fired at eleven times the speed of sound hang "like insects caught in amber" and drop harmlessly. [[Minerva (AI)|Minerva]]: "time runs differently around that ship… The faster it is going, the more it loses." It also catches lightning ("your uncle's lightning") ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]). [[Peltast]] javelins, designed with space-time anchoring, pierce it and create "a wobbly unstable rift".
- **Armies:** thousands of monsters on the *Princess Andromeda*: hellhounds, dracaenae, giants, sea serpents, harpies, Stymphalian birds, telkhines ([[Ch19 - Rex Invictus XII|Ch19]], [[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]).
- **The Great Stirring:** the monster awakening that [[Dr. Thorn]] invokes ([[Ch15 - Rex Invictus VIII|Ch15]]). Its link to Kronos is implied.
