**[[Metis]]** is the Titaness of wisdom and cunning, daughter of [[Oceanus]] and [[Tethys]]. She gave [[Zeus]] the emetic that made [[Kronos]] disgorge his children. [[Zeus]] married her, then swallowed her when it was prophesied her son would overthrow him; she was already pregnant with [[Athena]], who was born from Zeus's head.

Hmm... I gave a cup to an evil god, I talked to some people, I advised many people. I participated in a war, I loved someone... I was betrayed... And I... I woke up in a dark place? And gave advice to someone, then I suddenly woke up here... Hmm...
—Metis to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Chapter 817
Metis Elderblood is a Blood Dragon, True Dragon and Dragon Goddess of Wisdom belonging to the Elderblood Clan and the Imperial Family of Victor Elderblood. In her former life, she was an ancient Second Generation Primordial Goddess of the [[Greek Pantheon]], the first wife and advisor of [[Zeus]] and the mother of [[Athena]]. Zeus destroyed her original body by swallowing her, yet her Soul persisted in two fragments within Zeus and Athena for an immense span of time. Victor reunited the fragments following his conquest of [[Olympus]], but the damage to the surviving Soul was so severe that its reconstruction required substantially more than the Goddess's remaining Essence. Placed inside Victor's Personal Universe, Metis absorbed the [[Draconic Energy]] saturating her surroundings and emerged as a new Dragon Goddess whose Soul possessed the form of a Dragon and the Essence of a Goddess.[5][6][1][7]
This reconstruction gave Metis a relationship with Victor that did not exist in her former incarnation. Her newborn Draconic Soul instinctively recognized the source of its existence and called Victor Father, even though her memories belonged to a woman who had lived for eons. The contradiction between her former self and her new racial identity troubled her until she accepted that neither set of experiences needed to invalidate the other. Victor's recognition of Metis as one of his eldest daughters helped her reach that conclusion, allowing the previously conflicting portions of her Soul to begin integrating.[7][8][9] Following extensive subjective time within Victor's household, Metis also came to be recognized among his wives; her daughterhood and marriage are both acknowledged by the later narrative rather than being interchangeable descriptions of a conventional biological relationship.[10][3]
Metis' defining strengths remain her judgment and understanding. Victor identifies her modern Divinities as Good Advice, Planning, Cunning and Wisdom, naming Cunning and Wisdom as her greatest authorities.[11] She assists in teaching the Elderblood daughters about opposing Energies and cosmic Balance and later works with [[Hestia]] Elderblood on Imperial education. Her experience and intellectual abilities eventually make her a promising Imperial commander, although the story does not establish her as the commander of a particular Legion.[12][13][14]

### Physical Appearance

#### Original Appearance
Before her destruction, Metis was a blonde-haired Goddess from the ancient Greek Divine lineage. The distinction between her former blonde hair and the black hair of her reborn body is established when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] examines her reconstructed existence. Most of the other details of her original appearance are not extensively described in the accounts of her rebirth.[1]

#### Reborn Dragon Goddess
At the beginning of her rebirth, Metis was small enough to resemble a fairy or doll, with long black hair, pale skin, crimson Draconic eyes and the horns associated with her new Race. Her resemblance to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] immediately revealed how extensively his Energy had influenced her reconstructed body, while the shape of her Soul confirmed that the transformation involved far more than an outward alteration.[1][7] Although she carried the memories and knowledge of a mature Goddess, the new body initially behaved like a recently born Dragon. She could manifest Draconic features such as wings through instinct even when she could not explain those movements through memories of her former existence.[7]
Metis matured at an extraordinary pace as reconstruction continued. Two days after awakening, she had already grown to approximately 150 cm and was capable of floating into a room while speaking to Victor. He estimated that she could regain her adult physical development in about another week.[2] By the time of the conflict between [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]] and [[Volk Fenrir]], she had assumed the appearance of a mature, imposing Dragon Goddess. Her long black hair, Draconic eyes and horns remained distinguishing traits of her new form.[15][11]

### Personality and Traits
Metis is exceptionally observant, intelligent, prudent and cunning, traits that defined her long before she became a Dragon. Even after [[Zeus]] swallowed her, she continued advising him from within his mind. [[Aphrodite]] described her presence as a protective influence that helped preserve Zeus' mental stability despite his tendency to disregard the advice she gave him.[5] As an Elderblood, she remains inclined to examine motives, contradictions and long-term implications before drawing conclusions. These qualities are reflected in her modern Divinities, especially Wisdom and Cunning, but they are also products of the experiences and judgment accumulated in her former life.[11]
Her otherwise decisive mind was initially troubled by her new identity. Metis instinctively called [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] Father, yet her ancient memories associated maturity, marriage and motherhood with herself; describing Victor only as her Progenitor seemed emotionally incomplete, while calling him Master wrongly implied servitude. [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] teasingly suggested that she simply call him Husband. Although this did not immediately settle her question, it illustrates why Metis regarded the familiar labels used by mortals as inadequate to describe a Soul restored in such unusual circumstances.[2][8] Metis eventually concluded that the woman betrayed by Zeus belonged to a former existence, whereas the Dragon Goddess born through Victor was a new individual who nonetheless legitimately inherited that woman's memories and identity. Once she accepted both parts, her Soul began integrating instead of remaining in conflict.[16][9]
Metis is proud of her current identity and particularly dislikes being introduced as Zeus' wife. When a Valkyrie describes her by her former marriage, Metis immediately corrects her to ex-wife; both the ancient and reborn sides of her personality agree completely on that point.[11] Despite her immense racial power, she is capable of accepting corrections. While observing the fighting between Tasha and Volk, she initially considers them weak by her own standards. [[Scathach]] warns that dismissing an opponent's skill because of a power advantage can create dangerous openings, and Metis takes the lesson seriously rather than stubbornly defending her initial assessment.[15]
The Dragon Goddess has a strong instructional disposition, particularly toward [[Nero Elderblood]] and [[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]]. She can explain the difficult distinction between natural and artificially imposed combinations of opposing Energies, identify the dangers of disturbing cosmic Balance and discuss the Primordial Beings responsible for regulating aspects of Creation. She is also perceptive enough to notice when Ophis has neglected her lessons and is merely pretending to understand. Metis does not wish the girls to spend eternity dependent upon Victor's reputation and resources; however, her attempt to encourage them to become more than sheltered daughters creates an amusing misunderstanding about the meaning of that advice.[12]
Metis possesses deep resentment toward [[Athena]]. She acknowledges Athena's intelligence but considers her pride destructive and resents that Athena made no serious effort to help the fragment of her mother residing within her, even if Athena did not completely understand the circumstances. This bitterness influences her comments about having children in Chapter 1005. Her later relationship with Victor and their daughter must therefore be understood alongside an unresolved and sometimes contradictory narrative chronology, rather than by assuming that every statement about her intention to become a mother occurs before all references to her actual child.[17][18]

### History
Metis belonged to the ancient Greek Divine order and became [[Zeus]]' first wife and principal advisor. Her original Divinities included Health, Protection, Cunning, Prudence and Virtues. Her extraordinary intelligence and the part she played in Zeus' decision-making also led to her association with Wisdom, although after being reborn she initially identifies that title separately from the list of her original authorities.[7] She possessed enough importance to Zeus' reign that her guidance remained influential even while he ignored much of it, and her imprisoned presence served as a stabilizing force within the mind of the ruler who had betrayed her.[5]
A prophecy concerning their descendants brought that marriage to an end. Metis was expected to have a daughter whose wisdom would surpass her own and, afterward, a son stronger than Zeus who could depose him as King of the Greek Gods. Fearing the pattern of succession that had already overthrown earlier Greek rulers, Zeus swallowed Metis and destroyed her physical independence. His act failed to annihilate her Soul. A fragment remained inside him, while another existed within [[Athena]], who became the daughter associated with the prophecy's first stage.[5][6] The fragments continued existing separately for eons, depriving Metis of an independent body without entirely erasing her consciousness or capacity to advise Zeus.
The continuing influence of the imprisoned Metis first becomes relevant to the modern story when the [[Greek Pantheon]] begins to suffer from Zeus' increasingly unstable behavior. [[Aphrodite]] warns [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s circle that Zeus turning on his own subordinates is especially troubling because Metis has long acted as a safeguard for his mind. The incident helps explain how much of the competence attributed to Zeus was sustained by the very woman he had deprived of freedom.[5]

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Following the defeat of [[Olympus]], [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] confronts [[Athena]] and identifies a golden Soul fragment hidden within her. He recognizes it as part of Metis, whom he regards as the real Goddess of Wisdom behind many of [[Zeus]]' intelligent decisions. Victor also possesses the other fragment through what remained inside Zeus after his defeat. When brought together, the two fragments respond to one another and begin fusing without Victor needing to force them into a new arrangement. The resulting Soul is damaged, small and incomplete, far from capable of immediately resuming its former existence. Victor recognizes how extraordinary it is that she survived for so long, attributing some of that resilience to the enduring nature of a Second Generation Primordial God's Soul.[6][1]
Victor sends the recovering Soul into his Personal Universe for protection. The environment has been saturated by the Energy of his newly evolved Blood Dragon existence, and the injured Metis instinctively draws upon what surrounds her to replace what the ancient fragmentation has left missing. Roxanne assists the Soul's recovery, but does not deliberately produce the racial conversion and rejects the suggestion that she somehow planned it. When Metis awakens, Victor discovers a young girl resembling him with a Dragon-shaped Soul and a Goddess's Essence. She looks at him and spontaneously calls him Father, producing a familial bond neither Victor nor Roxanne originally set out to create.[1][7]
Questioned about her past, Metis recalls advising people, participating in a war, falling in love, being betrayed, surviving in darkness and continuing to provide advice. She also remembers her older Divine authorities but cannot immediately reconcile the title Goddess of Wisdom with the way she lists her original Divinities. At the same time, her body displays Draconic instincts she never had as an ancient Greek Goddess. She generates clothing and manifests wings through her new nature rather than memories acquired in the [[Greek Pantheon]]. Victor accepts that the daughter before him possesses both the identity of the old Metis and an equally genuine new existence.[7]
After Victor returns to [[Nightingale]], the newly reborn Metis encounters [[Zaladrac Zeovnur Elderblood]], whose far older Dragon nature provokes an instinctive response. Metis lowers her head and withdraws from Victor under Zaladrac's territorial pressure, demonstrating that her Draconic instincts are embedded in her Soul rather than constituting a temporary disguise.[19] Within two days her body has grown from fairy-sized to approximately 150 cm, and although she sometimes begins to address Victor as Father, she attempts to replace the title with Progenitor because she is still struggling with its implications. Victor openly invites her to use Father if she wishes.[2]
Metis later watches the confrontation between Tasha and Volk and comments on the comparatively low strength of the opponents from a Dragon's perspective. [[Scathach]] uses the occasion to explain that underestimating supposedly weaker opponents is a mistake even for exceptionally powerful individuals. The lesson is relevant to a Goddess who has inherited immense raw power without personally spending every year of an ordinary Dragon's life adapting to her newly acquired body.[15] During a subsequent discussion with Scathach, Metis reveals her uncertainty about the proper term for Victor. Father, Progenitor and Master each seem wrong in a different way, leading Scathach to offer Husband as a teasing alternative. For Metis, the problem is less about finding the correct word than accepting that the new Soul's feelings do not erase the memories and independence of its former life.[8]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] visits the Valkyries alongside Metis, Ophis and [[Nero]], he introduces Metis without hesitation as his daughter and a Dragon Goddess of Good Advice, Planning, Cunning and Wisdom. His introduction surprises both the Valkyries and Metis, who is simultaneously embarrassed and happy that Victor acknowledges her place in his family so naturally. She also corrects the description of her as [[Zeus]]' wife to ex-wife with obvious displeasure, an occasion when her former and current personalities agree fully.[11]
Metis accompanies Victor and the other girls on his visit to the [[Heavenly Father]] and the reorganized Hell. Their travels demonstrate that the daughter newly reconstructed from a divided ancient Soul is already being treated as a member of the family accompanying Victor in matters involving the highest rulers of the Sector.[20][16] In the Demon King's castle, the Demons address Metis, Ophis and Nero as Princesses. Metis, who remembers having held a much higher status in her former life, initially finds the title strange, yet the recognition also appeals to her new Draconic identity. Victor treats the three as his eldest daughters and charges them with looking after their younger sisters in situations where he or their mothers cannot do so.[9]
This experience resolves the conflict that has followed Metis since awakening. She realizes that the original Greek Goddess was betrayed and lost her independent life, while the Soul brought into being through Victor now carries the memories of that ancient self without being required to repeat its fate. Metis accepts the identity of Metis, Dragon Goddess of Wisdom, daughter of Victor, after which the divided aspects of her Soul begin moving toward genuine integration. Victor recognizes the change and is pleased that his consistent acknowledgment of her daughterhood has helped her find a place within the family.[16][9]
Metis later assumes the responsibility of explaining Victor's unusual relationship with the contradictory forces underlying his [[Chaos]] powers to Nero and Ophis. She draws a distinction between entities that disturb Balance artificially and Victor's abnormal nature as a Blood Dragon Primogenitor who also possesses authority associated with Beginning and Negativity. The Primordial regulators, the cosmic functions of the Universal Tree and the Judges of the Abyss, and the danger of possessing powers contradictory to the normal order of Existence all enter her explanation. She also distinguishes Victor's capacity to create beings with his own characteristics from an ordinary God's limited control over a single native Divinity. When she notices that Ophis has not understood the lessons, Metis reproaches the girls for failing to make better use of the extraordinary instruction and resources available to them.[12]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
After the family's accelerated development, Metis is shown among the Dragon Goddesses discussing Imperial technology and the protective suits created under the direction of [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]] and [[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]]. Her analytical response contrasts with [[Nero]] and Ophis' more immediate observations and reflects her familiarity with the strategic value of new equipment. When the subject of children arises, Metis jokes about wishing to enjoy her status as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughter for several more millennia. Her inner thoughts reveal that resentment toward [[Athena]] still makes the prospect of repeating her former experience as a mother distressing.[17] The account contrasts with other passages that already identify Metis among the mothers who completed lengthy pregnancies and later confirm her own daughter; the conflicting statements do not negate that child.[18][4]
Metis' long-term importance extends beyond her private place within the Elderblood family. As the [[Elderblood Empire]] develops its administrative systems, education becomes a priority for a civilization incorporating many different races and territories. A reformed educational and talent-development system is entrusted to the Emperor's wives, with Metis identified alongside [[Hestia]] Elderblood as particularly responsible for overseeing education. Hestia's authority over the [[Rex Invictus|Home]] and Metis' Wisdom make them suitable custodians of an institution intended to develop citizens' knowledge, capability and prospects rather than leave advancement entirely to ancestry or privilege.[13]
Her position among the Imperial daughters remains unusual. Some of Victor's biological daughters do not treat Metis, Nero and Ophis as exactly equivalent to themselves, since their origins involve different forms of adoption or creation. They recognize Metis as their father's daughter, but are also aware that she retains a mature Greek Goddess's identity and has become a potential romantic rival for Victor's attention. The resulting jealousies do not sever their familial bonds; they help explain the distinctive way the more recent generation reacts to its elder sisters.[10] Metis is also one of the mothers whose pregnancy lasted approximately 1,900 years during the earlier time-accelerated period. This unusually long gestation reflected the difficulty of developing a child with high-order Divine potential, not a simple guarantee that a longer pregnancy always produces a more talented daughter.[18] At least one unnamed daughter of Victor and Metis is later confirmed among his natural children.[4]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Metis' contributions continue during the Imperial expansion and preparations for confrontation with the Upper Sectors. In discussions about the increasingly capable second generation, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his wives recognize that Metis and [[Akasha Elderblood]] have progressed far enough to be considered promising commanders. They distinguish such potential from the responsibilities Akasha already performs in managing the Empire's Shadow-related systems; Metis is not assigned a specific named Legion in the relevant passage.[14]
During a gathering of more than thirty-five Imperial daughters, Metis arrives with Akasha and [[Melida Charlotte Elderblood]]. While several sisters visibly react to their arrival, Metis understands the jealousy and rivalry behind the reactions without being drawn into the dispute. She exchanges remarks with [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]] regarding the extraordinary talent of Melida, whose inherited proficiency with Primal Energy has progressed beyond what many far older researchers achieve through prolonged study.[21] When Anna and [[Scathach]] join the gathering, even the older daughters, Metis included, address them as Mothers and respond with the disciplined formality instilled through years of family education and Scathach's demanding training. Their conduct illustrates that Metis' immense age and Wisdom have not exempted her from the family traditions that govern the younger Elderblood generation.[21]
Metis is later specifically contrasted with creations such as Akasha that were brought into being for an assigned function. While Akasha was created to protect the Imperial Family and fulfill an important administrative role, Metis and Melida were given lives as daughters without a predetermined purpose. This distinction helps explain why Victor prefers to allow Metis to grow, learn and decide how she will contribute rather than treating the extraordinary abilities of his daughters as obligations they must repay through forced service.[22]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is the source of Metis' present Draconic existence and the central figure of the family she joins after rebirth. He recovered both surviving Soul fragments from [[Zeus]] and [[Athena]] and gave them the opportunity to reunite, but did not intentionally set out to produce a new Dragon daughter. Her injured Soul absorbed his Energy of its own accord within his Personal Universe, leading her to address him as Father immediately after awakening.[6][7] Victor's willingness to recognize that title rather than reduce her to the ancient Greek woman he had rescued becomes crucial to Metis' eventual acceptance of herself. His introduction of Metis as his daughter to the Valkyries and his designation of her, [[Nero]] and Ophis as eldest sisters demonstrate how consistently he treats the newly reborn Dragon as family.[11][9]
The story later also recognizes Metis as one of Victor's wives. Their relationship is unusual because her former adult identity and the daughter created through his Essence coexist; the later chapters explicitly recognize both rather than describing Metis as a biological child born to Victor. Later discussion counts her among Victor's wives, without independently describing the ceremony that established that status.[10][3] They are also parents to an unnamed daughter.[18][4]

#### Zeus
[[Zeus]] was Metis' original husband. She loved him, advised him and continued safeguarding his mental faculties even after he swallowed her, while Zeus routinely ignored much of the advice he received. His fear that a child of Metis could surpass and depose him led him to destroy her body and imprison the surviving Soul within the divided conditions that lasted until [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s intervention.[5][6] The current Metis firmly refuses to be defined by her former husband and corrects anyone who continues to refer to her as Zeus' wife, explicitly identifying him as an ex-husband.[11]

#### Athena
[[Athena]] is Metis' daughter from her first marriage and formerly held one of the two surviving fragments of her mother's Soul. The bond between them is not a warm one. Metis considers Athena intelligent but crippled by pride and deeply resents the absence of meaningful effort to aid her imprisoned mother. By the time she discusses Athena with [[Nero]] and Ophis, Metis shows little sympathy for her daughter's subsequent suffering at the hands of [[Lily Elderblood]] and [[Medusa]] Elderblood, regarding the outcome as fitting for the disappointment she became. Her resentment shapes her ambivalence about experiencing motherhood again, although later chapters confirm she eventually has a daughter with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[6][17][4]

#### Roxanne Elderblood
[[Roxanne Elderblood]] helped stabilize Metis' shattered Soul during its recovery inside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Personal Universe. When Victor observes the resulting Dragon form and questions her about the transformation, Roxanne explicitly denies causing it intentionally. Her intervention helped Metis recover, but the Draconic substance incorporated into the new Soul originated from Victor's Energy, not from a deliberate racial alteration performed by Roxanne. She is therefore an important participant in Metis' rebirth, though calling her Metis' biological mother would be inaccurate.[7]

#### Nero Elderblood and Ophis Tepes Elderblood
[[Nero Elderblood]] and [[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]] are Metis' fellow eldest daughters in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household. Victor entrusts the three with responsibilities toward their younger sisters, making Metis' place within the family explicit.[9] Metis frequently functions as their teacher, explaining complex subjects that their fathers and mothers have already covered and urging them not to remain satisfied with dependence on Victor's power. [[Nero]] engages more directly with her explanations; Ophis sometimes agrees without understanding and is quickly discovered by Metis.[12] By the later Imperial era, the younger sisters also recognize the unusual adult and marital status of all three women, a distinction that complicates ordinary sibling rivalry.[10]

#### Scathach Elderblood Scarlett
[[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] becomes an informal mentor during Metis' early life as a Dragon. She cautions Metis not to confuse overwhelming strength with invulnerability and jokingly suggests Husband as an answer to Metis' uncertainty about what to call [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Both interventions help show that Metis' inherited intelligence does not automatically provide experience with every aspect of her new existence.[15][8] Metis later participates in the same family training culture that [[Scathach]] imposes upon the younger generation.[21]

#### Zaladrac Zeovnur Elderblood
[[Zaladrac Zeovnur Elderblood]] is among the first established Dragons to encounter Metis following her rebirth. Her presence brings out the younger Dragon Goddess's new instincts: Metis instinctively acknowledges the older Dragon's territorial authority before consciously deciding how to react. The encounter underscores the genuineness of Metis' racial transformation.[19]

#### Hestia Elderblood
[[Hestia]] Elderblood is another Greek Goddess integrated into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s dynasty. Both women bring knowledge acquired through Divine history to the Emperor's growing civilization, and they are specifically identified together as the Imperial wives responsible for education. Their shared work is a meaningful contribution to the Empire independent of their family status or combat potential.[13]

### Abilities

#### Skills and Abilities
Metis Elderblood is an ancient Goddess reborn as a Blood Dragon and True Dragon, possessing the Divine foundation of a Second Generation Primordial Goddess and a new Draconic existence originating from [[Victor Elderblood]]. Her greatest strengths are her Divinities of Wisdom and Cunning, supported by the additional authorities of Good Advice and Planning. Her original existence also possessed the Divine authorities of Health, Protection, Cunning, Prudence and Virtues, although the individual applications of those former authorities have not all been demonstrated following her rebirth.[1][2]
Metis' rebirth was fundamentally different from the deliberate Dragon transformations later performed upon Victor's wives. Her original Soul had survived divided between [[Zeus]] and [[Athena]], but the fragments were so severely damaged that reconstructing an independent existence required an extraordinary amount of Energy. Once Victor reunited them and placed the recovering Soul inside his Personal Universe, Metis instinctively absorbed the [[Draconic Energy]] saturating that environment. Rather than simply returning to her previous Divine body, she reconstructed herself as a new Being whose Soul possessed the form of a Dragon and the Essence of a Goddess. [[Roxanne Elderblood]] assisted the recovery but did not intentionally perform the transformation.[3][4][1]
The resulting Dragon Goddess was considerably more powerful than the ancient Metis. [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] explicitly compared the two incarnations and stated that their levels were completely different. This was particularly remarkable because the original Metis had already possessed the status of a Second Generation Primordial Goddess and participated in the ancient [[Titanomachy|Titan War]]. Nevertheless, Metis is primarily distinguished by her intellect, understanding and judgment rather than by a documented record of overwhelming offensive techniques.[5][2]

#### Blood Dragon Goddess Physiology
Metis' present existence combines the supernatural qualities of a high-ranking Goddess with the racial foundation of a True Dragon. Her Draconic nature is not an external transformation or temporary enhancement: the structure of her reconstructed Soul itself became Draconic, while the Essence of her original Goddess existence survived within it. This makes her a genuine Dragon Goddess belonging to the Blood Dragon lineage founded by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[1]

##### Physical Characteristics
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Metis possesses the extraordinary physical constitution associated with [[True Dragons]] and powerful Divine Beings. Her reconstructed body is vastly superior to her original incarnation, despite the latter already belonging to the ancient Greek Divine hierarchy.[5]
- Superhuman Strength: Her True Dragon physiology provides physical strength greatly exceeding that of ordinary supernatural beings. This racial advantage is supported by the fact that Metis' present state is described as being on an entirely different level from her former Primordial Goddess incarnation.[5]
- Superhuman Durability: Her Draconic and Divine constitution provides exceptional physical resilience. This is a consequence of her confirmed racial nature rather than a claim that every conceivable attack is ineffective against her.[1][6]
- Superhuman Speed: Metis possesses the enhanced movement potential of a True Dragon. Her ability to float and move through the air was already apparent during her early development, although the story does not provide a numerical measurement of her maximum speed.[7][8]
- Superhuman Reflexes: Her Draconic body possesses the enhanced reactions characteristic of powerful Dragons, supported by the heightened awareness associated with her Divine nature.[1][5]
- Superhuman Stamina: Her combined Divine and Draconic constitution provides the capacity for sustained supernatural exertion, although her individual endurance has not been quantified through a prolonged battle.[1]
- Enhanced Senses: As a True Dragon, Metis possesses the supernatural perception associated with the Race. Her newly acquired senses allow her to recognize Draconic pressure and assess the comparative power of other supernatural beings.[7][5]
- Extrasensory Perception: Metis can perceive supernatural power rather than relying exclusively upon ordinary sight. During the confrontation between [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]] and [[Volk Fenrir]], she assessed the power of both participants and was surprised by how little danger they represented to her.[5]
- Aura Sensing: She can perceive the overwhelming supernatural pressure produced by another Dragon. Zaladrac's display of dominance caused Metis to react instinctively even though she possessed no previous experience of the encounter.[7]
- Power Level Analysis: Her ability to recognize differences in supernatural strength is demonstrated when she evaluates the [[Lycaon|Werewolf King]] and Tasha. Her assessment is observational rather than proof of perfect knowledge concerning every hidden ability an opponent possesses.[5]
- [[Immortality]]: Metis' original existence belonged to an exceptionally ancient Divine generation, and her Soul survived for an immense period even after the destruction of her body. Her new Dragon Goddess existence additionally possesses the supernatural longevity associated with True Dragons. Ordinary mortal aging is therefore not an established threat to her existence.[3][4][1]
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Metis belongs to a lineage whose supernatural physiology possesses exceptional restorative potential. Her own most important recorded recovery feat concerns the reconstruction of her shattered Soul and should be distinguished from a demonstrated ability to regenerate every conceivable bodily injury during combat.[1][6]
- Energy Manipulation: Her existence combines [[Divine Energy]] and the [[Draconic Energy]] that formed her new Soul. She possesses the foundation necessary to wield power belonging to both natures, although the precise proportions and limits of her individual Energy reserves have not been established.[1]
- Energy Absorption: Metis' damaged Soul instinctively absorbed the enormous concentration of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Energy surrounding it during reconstruction. This was a survival response rather than a consciously demonstrated offensive Energy-draining technique.[1]
- Energy Assimilation: The Energy absorbed during her recovery did not function solely as temporary nourishment. It became incorporated into her reconstructed existence, producing a new Draconic nature derived from Victor's Blood Dragon Essence.[1]
- Adaptation: Metis' Soul adapted to the unusual supernatural conditions of Victor's Personal Universe by incorporating his Draconic Energy during its reconstruction. The feat establishes extraordinary spiritual adaptability under those circumstances, not unrestricted instantaneous adaptation to every attack or phenomenon.[1]

##### Draconic Anatomy
- Dragon Physiology: Metis is a genuine True Dragon rather than a Dragonoid. Her Soul possesses an authentic Draconic nature, distinguishing her from lesser Dragon variants that lack a complete True Dragon Soul.[1][9]
- Dragon Transformation: Full [[True Dragons]] possess genuine Dragon Forms, unlike the Dragonoids described by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Metis belongs to the former category, although the exact dimensions, appearance and combat characteristics of her complete Dragon Form have not been individually established in the cited chapters.[1][9]
- Shapeshifting: Metis' Draconic existence is capable of maintaining a humanoid form rather than remaining permanently restricted to the anatomy of a large Dragon. Her reconstructed form retained clearly recognizable Draconic characteristics while developing from a small newborn appearance into an adult body.[1][5]
- Natural Weaponry: Her True Dragon foundation includes the natural anatomical potential associated with Draconic bodies, such as claws and other combat-oriented physical structures. The story has not established a complete catalogue of natural weapons Metis personally employs.[1][9]
- Flight: Metis displayed Draconic wings and the ability to float shortly after her rebirth. These characteristics were instinctive despite her lack of previous experience living as a Dragon.[1][7]
- Dragon Breath: Dragon Breath is part of the supernatural combat system of True Dragons, with individual attributes depending upon the Dragon's nature and powers. Metis possesses the relevant racial foundation, but the precise Element, conceptual property and destructive capabilities of her own Dragon Breath have not been explicitly demonstrated.[10]
- Draconic Instincts: Her new existence possesses racial instincts that were absent from her previous life. These include reactions to other Dragons, territorial pressure and the relative hierarchy of powerful members of the Race.[7]
- Draconic Pride: Metis acquired the naturally prideful tendencies associated with True Dragons. Her reaction to the perceived weakness of the [[Lycaon|Werewolf King]] and Tasha illustrates how her new racial perspective affected her judgment, although she retained enough prudence to recognize the danger of dismissing weaker opponents.[5]

##### True Dragon Soul
The possession of a complete True Dragon Soul is one of the fundamental distinctions between full [[True Dragons]] and lesser Draconic variants. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly explains that Dragonoids lack the true Dragon Soul and the instinctive knowledge associated with it. Metis' Soul was directly identified as Draconic upon her awakening, placing her within the complete True Dragon category.[1][9]
- Soul Physiology: Metis possesses a genuine Draconic Soul integrated with the surviving Divine Essence of a Primordial Goddess.[1]
- Soul Adaptation: Her Soul reconstructed itself through the absorption and incorporation of Victor's Energy, changing its fundamental nature rather than merely restoring the previous spiritual structure.[1]
- Supernatural Energy Compatibility: The combination of Divine Essence and Draconic Soul demonstrates that Metis can sustain both within one existence without the incompatibility that might ordinarily accompany an abnormal racial reconstruction.[1]
- Instinctive Knowledge: True Dragon Souls possess instinctive comprehension of their racial language and Runes. This knowledge is an inherent characteristic of the complete Draconic foundation, distinguishing True Dragons from Dragonoids, who must learn the same systems without that instinctive starting point.[9]

#### Divine Physiology
Metis retains her status as a genuine Goddess after reconstruction. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] directly observes that although her Soul has acquired the form of a Dragon, it still possesses the Essence of a Goddess. Her present existence is therefore neither a conventional mortal Dragon nor an ordinary Goddess merely using Draconic abilities.[1]
- God Physiology: Metis possesses the supernatural constitution of a Divine Being, integrated with her new True Dragon physiology.[1]
- Primordial God Physiology: Her original existence belonged to the Second Generation of [[Primordial Gods]]. Its spiritual foundation survived within the Soul used to reconstruct her current body. This establishes her ancient Divine origin without implying that she automatically possesses the authorities of the [[Primordial Entities]] responsible for regulating the Cosmos.[3][4]
- Divinity: Metis possesses genuine Divine Concepts. Her current authorities are explicitly identified as Good Advice, Planning, Cunning and Wisdom, with Cunning and Wisdom representing her greatest Divinities.[2]
- [[Divine Energy]] Manipulation: Her retained Goddess Essence supplies a Divine foundation alongside her Draconic existence. The story confirms that both coexist, although her individual offensive applications of Divine Energy remain comparatively unexplored.[1]
- Divine Aura: Metis possesses the presence and supernatural pressure associated with her Dragon Goddess status. The Valkyries felt uncomfortable before her even though she was not actively participating in combat.[2]
- Conceptual Power: Her Divinities concern abstract principles rather than ordinary Elements. Wisdom, Cunning, Planning and Good Advice are confirmed domains of authority, but naming those Concepts does not by itself establish unrestricted manipulation of every phenomenon linguistically associated with them.[2]

#### Soul Abilities
Metis possesses an unusually resilient Soul whose survival and reconstruction are among her most important demonstrated supernatural characteristics. These feats originate from her former Divine foundation and the extraordinary circumstances of her rebirth rather than from an explicitly established resurrection technique that she can freely activate whenever desired.[3][4][1]
- Soul Resilience: Metis' Soul survived for an enormous period despite the destruction of her original body and its division into two fragments. One portion existed within [[Zeus]] while the other remained with [[Athena]].[3]
- Soul Fragmentation Survival: Her existence persisted in separate spiritual fragments without immediately dissolving. Both fragments retained enough of the original Metis to allow their eventual reunion.[3][4]
- Soul Reunification: When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] brought the two fragments together, they naturally began recombining. He did not need to manually construct the Soul's entire internal structure for the reunion to occur.[3]
- Soul Reconstruction: The reunited Soul reconstructed an independent existence through the Energy available inside Victor's Personal Universe. The final result differed from Metis' original Divine body because the reconstruction incorporated Victor's Draconic nature.[1]
- Energy Absorption: Her severely damaged Soul instinctively drew upon the Energy saturating its surroundings to support recovery. Roxanne explicitly identifies this process as the reason Metis' reconstruction developed so unexpectedly.[1]
- Energy Assimilation: The absorbed Energy became part of Metis' newly formed spiritual foundation. This assimilation explains why she was reborn as a Dragon Goddess rather than returning to her previous Titan Goddess constitution.[1]
- Spiritual Adaptation: Her Soul altered its reconstruction in response to the Energy available, preserving the Essence of a Goddess while developing an authentic Draconic form.[1]
- Memory Retention: Metis retained memories of her former existence, including her ancient experiences, relationship with Zeus, participation in war and subsequent spiritual imprisonment. Her memories remained identifiable despite the severe damage suffered by her Soul.[1]
- Dual Identity Integration: The ancient memories and the instincts of her new Dragon Soul initially produced an internal conflict concerning her identity. After accepting herself as both the inheritor of the ancient Metis' memories and a daughter created through Victor, those conflicting aspects began integrating more completely.[11][12]
Limit of the feat: Metis' survival depended upon the exceptional resilience of her ancient Divine Soul, the recovery of both surviving fragments, Victor's intervention and an environment saturated with the Energy necessary for reconstruction. These circumstances do not establish that Metis can regenerate from complete Soul destruction, resurrect herself without assistance or survive every form of spiritual annihilation.

#### Divinities
Metis possesses a combination of original Divine authorities and the modern authorities established following her reconstruction as a Dragon Goddess. The distinction is important because her original Goddess existence did not initially possess Wisdom as one of the native Concepts she identifies. Although she eventually became recognized as the Goddess of Wisdom, that association originally developed through her accomplishments and advisory role. Following her rebirth, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] recognizes Wisdom as one of her actual greatest Divinities.[1][2]

##### Current Divinities
Confirmed Dragon Goddess authorities: Wisdom, Cunning, Good Advice and Planning.[2]

###### Wisdom
Wisdom is one of Metis' two greatest Divinities and the Concept most closely associated with her identity. Her ancient reputation for Wisdom was already so extensive that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] identified her as the true Goddess responsible for many of [[Zeus]]' intelligent decisions. Following her rebirth, Wisdom became an actual Divine authority of her current existence rather than remaining merely a title earned through reputation.[3][1][2]
- Wisdom Manipulation: Metis possesses genuine conceptual authority over Wisdom. The demonstrated importance of this Divinity lies primarily in her superior understanding, judgment, advice and ability to analyze complex situations. Specific external techniques capable of directly manipulating another Being's Wisdom have not been established.[2]
- Enhanced Intelligence: Metis possesses an exceptionally developed intellect supported by her Wisdom Divinity and the accumulated knowledge of her original existence. Her intellectual abilities allow her to analyze matters involving supernatural Energy, Divine systems, technology and complex relationships between different powers.[13][14]
- Analytical Thinking: She can examine a situation, identify its underlying conditions and distinguish relevant information from superficial appearances. During her explanation of opposing supernatural Energies, Metis analyzes why certain combinations violate the ordinary expectations of cosmic Balance.[13]
- Conceptual Understanding: Metis understands the relationship between Divine Concepts, supernatural Essences and the cosmic principles governing their interaction. She is capable of explaining why Victor's existence differs fundamentally from beings that obtain incompatible powers through artificial means.[13]
- Information Analysis: Her reasoning allows her to evaluate the implications of information rather than simply memorize it. When discussing the Elderblood combat suits, she considers their current functions, limitations and potential future development.[14]
- Critical Thinking: Metis does not automatically accept that an existing technological limitation will remain permanent. She questions whether future Imperial technology might surpass the capabilities of [[Junketsu]], even when [[Nero]], Ophis and Zaladrac regard that possibility as impossible.[14]
- Teaching: Her understanding allows her to explain advanced supernatural subjects to younger members of the Imperial Family. Victor leaves Nero and Ophis in her care while she discusses the relationship between [[Chaos]], opposing Energies and the beings responsible for preserving Balance.[13]
- Intuitive Aptitude: Metis demonstrates a strong ability to recognize the significance of developments involving her family. During the gathering of the Imperial Princesses, she understands the emotions and intentions underlying her sisters' behavior without requiring a direct explanation.[15]
Metis' Wisdom does not constitute omniscience. Her explanation concerning artificially unbalanced beings includes reasoning based upon limited examples, and she recognizes that certain conclusions cannot be established with complete certainty. Her Divinity provides exceptional understanding without granting knowledge of every fact, future event or hidden circumstance.[13]

###### Cunning
Cunning is the other of Metis' two greatest Divinities. Unlike Wisdom, which primarily concerns understanding and judgment, Cunning emphasizes the intelligent application of knowledge toward advantageous solutions. The Concept was already among her original Divine authorities and remains explicitly present within her modern set.[1][2]
- Cunning Manipulation: Metis possesses genuine Divine authority associated with Cunning. Although the precise range of direct conceptual applications has not been demonstrated, Cunning is explicitly one of her greatest Divinities.[2]
- Strategic Intelligence: Her intellect and Cunning provide a strong foundation for examining circumstances, anticipating difficulties and constructing effective responses. Her former position as [[Zeus]]' advisor and her later suitability for Imperial command support her strategic expertise.[3][16]
- Tactical Analysis: Metis can consider relative power, available information and possible weaknesses when judging supernatural confrontations. Her observations during the conflict on Samar demonstrate her ability to evaluate the apparent strength of powerful opponents.[5]
- Political Intelligence: Her ancient experience advising Zeus gives her substantial knowledge of Divine government, political decision-making and the behavior of supernatural rulers.[3]
- Problem Solving: Metis approaches unfamiliar problems analytically and remains willing to reconsider assumptions when additional information becomes available.[13][14]
Cunning should not be confused with automatic mind control, guaranteed deception, probability manipulation or the ability to compel an opponent into making strategically unfavorable decisions. Those effects have not been individually demonstrated as applications of Metis' Divinity.

###### Planning
Planning is a separate confirmed Divinity possessed by Metis following her rebirth. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly names it among her modern Divine authorities when introducing her to the Valkyries.[2]
- Planning Manipulation: Metis possesses conceptual authority associated with Planning. The novel establishes the Divinity's existence but has not demonstrated an independent technique that directly alters another person's plans or guarantees a particular outcome.[2]
- Strategic Planning: Her conceptual affinity and accumulated experience make her well suited to evaluating future objectives, administrative problems and the actions required to achieve a desired result.[2][16]
- Long-Term Planning: Her ancient experience allows her to consider matters over periods far exceeding an ordinary mortal lifespan. Her subsequent participation in an Empire developing over immense periods further broadens the circumstances in which this knowledge is useful.[3][17]
- Organizational Skills: Her contribution to the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s educational administration provides a practical application of her intellect and suitability for large-scale institutional planning.[17]

###### Good Advice
Good Advice is another independent Divinity explicitly attributed to Metis. It closely corresponds to the role she performed during her former existence as [[Zeus]]' advisor and the responsibilities she later assumes within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family.[3][2]
- Advice Manipulation: Metis possesses Divine authority concerning Good Advice. Her confirmed applications center upon understanding circumstances and providing useful counsel rather than forcing others to follow her recommendations.[2]
- Expert Advice: She has extensive experience advising powerful rulers and explaining complicated situations. During the reign of Zeus, she repeatedly provided guidance that improved his decisions despite his own shortcomings.[3]
- Knowledge Transmission: Metis can communicate complicated supernatural principles in a form that others can understand. Her instruction concerning Victor's [[Chaos]]-related nature demonstrates this skill.[13]
- Decision Support: She can evaluate available information and provide recommendations based upon experience, knowledge and an understanding of likely consequences.[3][13]
- Mentorship: Metis encourages [[Nero]] and Ophis to develop their abilities and ambitions rather than remaining entirely dependent upon Victor's strength and protection. Her advice motivates them to pursue further training, although their reaction also demonstrates that even Metis' carefully intended guidance can be misunderstood.[13]

##### Original Divinities
Before becoming a Dragon Goddess, Metis identifies her Divine authorities as Health, Protection, Cunning, Prudence and Virtues. These are not speculative mythological associations; they are explicitly named by Metis following her rebirth. They should therefore remain recorded as part of her confirmed Divine history, even though the precise state and applications of several authorities in her reconstructed existence are not established.[1]

###### Health
- Health Manipulation: Original Divine authority. Metis explicitly identifies herself as a Goddess of Health. The domain is therefore established for her former existence, but the available scenes do not demonstrate its precise use upon herself or another individual.[1]
- Health-Related Divine Authority: As a Goddess associated with Health, Metis possessed a conceptual connection to that domain. The exact scope of the authority, including whether she could restore damaged bodies, cure illnesses or alter biological conditions, has not been directly demonstrated.[1]
The existence of this Divinity does not independently establish unlimited healing, disease immunity, biological reconstruction of other beings or the ability to manipulate every aspect of life and death.

###### Protection
- Protection Manipulation: Original Divine authority. Metis identifies Protection as one of her original Divinities. Its existence is confirmed, but no individually documented technique establishes its offensive, defensive or conceptual limits.[1]
- Protective Divine Authority: Metis possessed a conceptual connection to Protection in her former life. Her role in helping preserve [[Zeus]]' mental stability demonstrates an important protective contribution, although that event should not automatically be classified as a direct activation of her Protection Divinity.[18][1]
Abilities such as absolute defense, invulnerability, unrestricted barrier creation and conceptual immunity cannot be assigned to Metis solely from the name of this domain.

###### Prudence
- Prudence Manipulation: Original Divine authority. Prudence is explicitly included among Metis' original Divinities.[1]
- Prudent Judgment: Metis displays the restraint and consideration associated with Prudence. She recognizes that an apparent difference in power does not eliminate the danger of a weaker opponent exploiting unexpected circumstances.[5]
- Risk Assessment: Her reasoning naturally incorporates the limitations of available information. While discussing Balance and beings possessing contradictory Energies, she avoids treating a theory based upon limited examples as absolute certainty.[13]
Her demonstrated prudence does not establish unrestricted foresight, precognition or automatic protection against mistakes.

###### Virtues
- Virtue Manipulation: Original Divine authority. Metis identifies Virtues as one of her original Divine domains, establishing that her former Goddess existence was conceptually associated with Virtues.[1]
- Virtue-Related Divine Authority: The novel does not define the individual Virtues governed by Metis or demonstrate an explicit technique using this domain. Its precise scope therefore remains unspecified.[1]

###### Cunning
- Cunning Manipulation: Cunning is the only authority explicitly named both among Metis' original Divinities and within her modern confirmed set. It is consequently a demonstrated point of continuity between the ancient Goddess and the Dragon Goddess created through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[1][2]

##### Development of Wisdom
Metis was originally recognized as a Goddess of Wisdom because of her extraordinary intelligence and the role she played in advising [[Zeus]], not because Wisdom was initially one of the native Concepts she identified as her own. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] recognizes this distinction after Metis awakens and becomes confused upon recalling that she was known as a Goddess of Wisdom.[1]
Her modern existence is different. Victor subsequently identifies Wisdom as one of her two strongest actual Divinities, alongside Cunning. This establishes that her new Dragon Goddess identity possesses genuine conceptual authority over Wisdom rather than merely retaining the historical reputation of the original Metis.[2]

#### Draconic Language and Runes
Metis possesses the instinctive connection to Draconic Runes and Draconic Language associated with a complete True Dragon Soul. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly distinguishes full [[True Dragons]] from Dragonoids by explaining that the latter do not possess the instinctive understanding of Runes and Draconic Language, even though they retain the potential to learn those systems through training.[9]
- Language Comprehension: Metis possesses the instinctive understanding of Draconic Language belonging to genuine True Dragons. This comprehension comes from her reconstructed racial foundation rather than knowledge acquired during her former existence as a Greek Goddess.[1][9]
- Rune Comprehension: Her complete Draconic Soul provides the instinctive understanding of Draconic Runes that lesser Dragonoids lack.[9]
- Rune [[Magic]]: Metis possesses the racial foundation necessary to learn and employ Draconic Runes. The precise level of her individual technical mastery and the advanced Rune structures she can personally create have not been established through specific demonstrations.[9]
- Reality Warping: Draconic Runes and Dragon Language belong to supernatural systems through which Dragons can impose commands upon Reality. Metis possesses the racial foundation of those systems, but unrestricted Reality Warping or mastery comparable to Victor should not be attributed to her without an individual feat.[19][9]
- Supernatural Language: True Dragon speech is more than ordinary communication. Its relationship with Energy, intent and authority allows Draconic language to function as a supernatural instrument when properly understood and employed.[19]
Distinction: Instinctive knowledge is not identical to complete technical mastery. Metis can possess the natural understanding of a True Dragon without automatically equaling the Rune expertise accumulated by Victor, [[Zaladrac Zeovnur Elderblood]], [[Scathach]] or other dedicated practitioners.

#### Creation Affinity
[[True Dragons]] possess an exceptional connection to Creation and supernatural phenomena. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later explanations distinguish genuine True Dragons from Dragonoids by their complete Souls and greater capacity to interact with existence itself. Metis inherits the genuine True Dragon foundation through her reconstruction, but the most extreme applications demonstrated by Victor should not automatically be attributed to her.[6][9]
- Creation Affinity: Metis possesses the natural relationship with Creation belonging to her True Dragon Race. This racial affinity exists alongside her separate Divine Concepts rather than replacing them.[1][6]
- Supernatural Adaptability: Her reconstruction illustrates the ability of a genuine Draconic existence to incorporate another supernatural foundation. Her surviving Goddess Essence and the [[Draconic Energy]] acquired from Victor became integrated into one existence.[1]
- Energy Compatibility: The successful coexistence of her Divine Essence and Draconic Soul demonstrates a level of compatibility that would not be expected from an ordinary damaged Goddess Soul undergoing reconstruction.[1]
- Racial Power Integration: Blood Dragon transformations can preserve and develop powers associated with a Being's previous nature. In Metis' case, her Goddess Essence survived the formation of the new Draconic Soul, although the reconstruction occurred without an intentional Dragonization ritual.[1][6]
- Power Evolution: Metis' current state is superior to her former incarnation. [[Scathach]] explicitly identifies the extraordinary difference between the original Goddess and the Dragon Goddess produced through Victor's Essence.[5]
Metis has not been shown personally creating a complete universe, rewriting arbitrary cosmic laws or freely producing living beings through a Creation Divinity. Such feats require independent evidence and cannot be assigned to her merely because Victor possesses them.

#### Intellectual and Strategic Skills
Metis' intellectual abilities are among her defining characteristics. Her knowledge comes from both the memories of her ancient existence and the development of her modern Divinities, while her practical responsibilities within the [[Elderblood Empire]] establish that her intellect is valuable beyond abstract supernatural discussion.[3][2][17]
- Genius Intelligence: Metis was already an extraordinarily intelligent Goddess before her rebirth. Her reputation as the true source of many of [[Zeus]]' intelligent decisions demonstrates that her intellect was exceptional even among ancient Greek Gods.[3]
- Ancient Knowledge: Her memories preserve knowledge of the ancient Greek Divine world, the [[Titanomachy|Titan War]] and the supernatural institutions that existed during her former life.[1][5]
- Expert Advisor: She served as an advisor to Zeus for an immense period. Even after her body was destroyed, her surviving spiritual presence continued influencing and advising him.[18][3]
- Strategic Intelligence: Her Divinities of Wisdom, Cunning and Planning provide a strong conceptual foundation for strategy. Her later development makes her a potential Imperial commander, although no particular Legion is confirmed as being under her command.[2][16]
- Tactical Analysis: Metis can recognize differences in supernatural power and assess opponents accordingly. She evaluates Tasha and Volk during their confrontation, while [[Scathach]] cautions her against overreliance upon a superiority in raw strength.[5]
- Conceptual Analysis: Metis understands how opposing Essences and the requirements of Balance influence the development of supernatural beings. She explains the distinction between artificial violations of Balance and the unusual nature of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s existence.[13]
- Divine Knowledge: Her understanding encompasses Gods, Divinities, Essences and the role of powerful entities responsible for preserving cosmic stability.[13]
- Technological Analysis: Metis can evaluate advanced supernatural technology. While observing the Imperial combat suits, she recognizes their intended protective function and considers how their development might compare with [[Junketsu]].[14]
- Comparative Analysis: She can compare technologies and artifacts possessing very different operating principles, distinguishing the functionality of equipment created through Draconic Runes, Technomancy and Divine or Demonic materials from the evolving nature of Junketsu.[14]
- Teaching: Metis instructs [[Nero]] and Ophis concerning supernatural principles and the nature of their father's existence. She is also entrusted with important responsibilities involving the education of future Imperial generations.[13][17]
- Mentorship: She encourages younger members of Victor's family to develop their own ambitions and improve themselves rather than relying entirely upon their father's accomplishments.[13]
- Administration: Metis possesses experience and aptitude applicable to large-scale government institutions, as demonstrated by her responsibility for the Imperial education system alongside [[Hestia]].[17]
- Leadership: Metis' development eventually attracts attention as a potential commander. [[Jeanne Elderblood]] remarks that both Metis and [[Akasha Elderblood]] are progressing well and may become suitable for command positions.[16]

##### Imperial Education
As the [[Elderblood Empire]] expanded, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] established a new educational system intended to accommodate numerous supernatural [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] within the same civilization. Traditional academic structures were replaced with a system emphasizing the acquisition and demonstration of knowledge rather than the accumulation of grades. Imperial Academies accepted students of different backgrounds, while especially talented individuals could be transferred into specialized programs designed to develop their abilities.[17]
Responsibility for the Empire's educational structure was placed under two of Victor's wives: [[Hestia]] Elderblood, the Dragon Goddess of the [[Rex Invictus|Home]], and Metis, the Dragon Goddess of Wisdom. This appointment demonstrates that Metis' knowledge and judgment were considered suitable for directing an institution operating across the Empire's territories rather than merely advising individuals within the Imperial household.[17]
- Educational Administration: Metis shares responsibility for the Imperial academic system, including the institutions responsible for educating future generations.[17]
- Curriculum Development: Her administrative position places her among those responsible for a system emphasizing genuine comprehension and the development of individual talent. The novel does not separately identify every policy or curriculum decision as Metis' personal creation.[17]
- Knowledge Management: Her role requires applying intellectual judgment to an educational structure intended to serve many different Races and levels of supernatural development.[17]
- Institutional Leadership: The Imperial education system provides a confirmed example of Metis exercising responsibility over a major civilian institution within Victor's civilization.[17]

##### Command Potential
During the Empire's later development, Jeanne identifies Metis and Akasha as individuals whose progress could make them suitable commanders. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] acknowledges their development while emphasizing that their ultimate responsibilities should depend upon their own interests and choices.[16]
- Leadership Potential: Metis' continued development makes her a candidate for higher command responsibilities within the Empire.[16]
- Strategic Aptitude: Her intellectual Divinities and ancient advisory experience provide a natural foundation for command, although these characteristics do not establish a record of independently commanding large military campaigns.[2][16]
- Organizational Competence: Her established Imperial education responsibilities demonstrate practical experience overseeing an institution of considerable scale.[17]

#### Combat Abilities
Although Metis is primarily associated with Wisdom and advisory expertise, the original Goddess was not defenseless. [[Scathach]] explicitly states that Metis participated in the ancient [[Titanomachy|Titan War]], while her reconstructed body possesses physical and supernatural capabilities far exceeding those of the original Goddess.[5]
- Combat Experience: Metis possesses memories of participating in the Titan War and therefore has experience involving conflicts between ancient Divine Beings.[5]
- Superhuman Combat: Her current True Dragon physiology provides a considerably stronger physical foundation than her former Divine body, making her dangerous even without invoking the full range of her Divinities.[5]
- Combat Analysis: Metis can assess the relative power of opponents and recognize when an individual appears unlikely to present a serious threat.[5]
- Tactical Judgment: She understands the danger of underestimating an opponent solely because that opponent possesses less raw power. Her response to Scathach's warning demonstrates that she was surprised by the strength difference rather than completely ignorant of tactical caution.[5]
- Battlefield Awareness: Her supernatural perception allows her to evaluate powerful combatants from outside a confrontation, although the novel does not establish that she possesses unrestricted battlefield awareness or perfect knowledge of hidden techniques.[5]
- Draconic Combat Potential: Her complete True Dragon body possesses the anatomical and supernatural foundation necessary for the combat capabilities of her Race. The individual techniques she has mastered have not been catalogued in the same detail as those belonging to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] or Scathach.[1][9]

##### Relative Power
Metis' current existence is explicitly much stronger than her original Primordial Goddess incarnation. During the confrontation between Tasha and Volk, she perceives the two as comparatively weak, while [[Scathach]] explains that Metis was born directly through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Draconic Soul Essence and the remnants of an exceptionally ancient Divine Soul.[5]
The difference is significant because both Tasha and Volk are powerful [[Werewolves]]. Metis' reaction establishes her considerable superiority in perceived raw power, but it is not a completed combat victory over either of them. Likewise, Scathach's assessment of Metis' new incarnation does not establish that she can defeat every Second Generation Primordial God, Dragon Goddess or higher-ranking Imperial combatant.[5]

#### Resistances and Survivability
Metis possesses exceptional survivability through her Divine foundation, True Dragon physiology and the resilience of her reconstructed Soul. However, the specific immunities and resistances associated with her Race should be distinguished from abilities she has personally demonstrated under controlled circumstances or in battle.

##### Demonstrated Spiritual Resilience
- Soul Resistance: Her original Soul survived severe fragmentation and remained viable for an enormous period despite the loss of her independent body.[3][4]
- Resistance to Soul Deterioration: The surviving fragments retained enough identity and spiritual information to undergo eventual reconstruction, although they remained badly damaged and required outside assistance.]][3][1]
- Spiritual Survivability: The destruction of her former body did not immediately eliminate her spiritual existence. Her survival depended upon the unusual conditions involving [[Zeus]], [[Athena]] and the strength of her Divine Soul.]][3]
- Energy Compatibility: Metis' damaged Soul successfully incorporated the [[Draconic Energy]] of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Personal Universe without losing the Goddess Essence that survived within it.]][1]
- Spiritual Adaptation: Her Soul reconstructed itself into a different racial form in response to the Energy available during recov]]ery.[1]

##### Racial Resistances
- Supernatural Durability: Metis possesses the superior bodily resilience expected from a genuine True Dragon. The precise magnitude of her resistance against physical, magical and conceptual attacks has not been individually tested in the relevant chapters.[1][6]
- Immortal Physiology: Her Divine and Draconic nature provides survival beyond the limitations of ordinary mortal lifespans.[3][1]
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Metis possesses the restorative foundation associated with her Race, while the strongest directly recorded regenerative event is the reconstruction of her damaged Soul under extraordinary circumstances.[1][6]
- Racial Adaptability: Her current existence demonstrates the compatibility of Divine and Draconic foundations. This should not be interpreted as universal resistance to every supernatural effect or unrestricted adaptation during battle.[1]

##### Anti-Dragon Weakness
Despite the overwhelming power of [[True Dragons]], the Race possesses a fundamental vulnerability to Anti-Dragon properties. Metis herself explains that one of the principal purposes of the advanced suits developed by Velnorah is to address this weakness.[14]
The weakness is especially significant because even [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s attempts to alter Reality did not completely remove it from his wives. Their vulnerability could be reduced, but the underlying limitation remained difficult to eliminate. Metis' True Dragon status consequently makes this weakness relevant to her unless sufficient protective measures are available.[14]
Her personal resistance to Anti-Dragon effects should not be treated as equivalent to Victor's exceptional resistance simply because she belongs to his lineage.

#### Equipment

##### Personalized Elderblood Combat Suit
Metis is among the Imperial women for whom personalized combat equipment was developed through the work of [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]] and [[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]]. During the discussion of the new equipment, Metis receives notification through her communicator that her own suit is ready and subsequently leaves to obtain it.[14]
The combat suits combine advanced Imperial technology with supernatural engineering and are designed to protect [[True Dragons]] against weaknesses that cannot simply be overcome through overwhelming raw power.
- Power Armor: Metis has access to a personalized combat suit belonging to the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s advanced equipment program.[14]
- Anti-Dragon Countermeasures: The primary purpose of the armor project is to compensate for the vulnerability of True Dragons to Anti-Dragon properties. The equipment is intended to mitigate that weakness rather than merely provide conventional armor protection.[14]
- Technomancy: The suits incorporate Technomancy alongside advanced technological engineering.[14]
- Rune Technology: Draconic Runes form part of the equipment's construction, allowing supernatural principles to operate together with advanced machinery.[14]
- Divine Materials: The suits incorporate supernatural materials associated with Divine power as part of their construction.[14]
- Demonic Materials: Demonic materials are also used within the equipment, contributing to its unusual supernatural composition.[14]
- Personalized Equipment: Each suit is developed according to the characteristics and requirements of its intended wearer, rather than being a completely standardized system.[14]
- Equipment Evolution: The initial suits belong to the Mark I generation. Metis considers how future versions might develop as Imperial technological knowledge advances.[14]
The exact weapons, autonomous functions, defensive values and special systems installed within Metis' individual suit have not been independently demonstrated. Features shown by other wearers, including technological wings and plasma weapons, should not automatically be attributed to Metis without confirmation.

#### Former Existence
Metis' former Divine abilities should not be treated as an unrelated set of powers belonging to an entirely separate character. Her surviving ancient Soul formed the foundation of the Dragon Goddess she became, while the original memories and Divine Essence remained present within her new existence.[1]

##### Original Primordial Goddess
Before being swallowed by [[Zeus]], Metis was a Second Generation Primordial Goddess associated with the ancient Greek Divine lineage. She possessed the original Divinities of Health, Protection, Cunning, Prudence and Virtues, along with the supernatural constitution of an ancient Goddess.[3][1]
- God Physiology: Metis possessed the supernatural body and Divine foundation of a powerful ancient Goddess.[3]
- Primordial God Physiology: Her original existence belonged to the Second Generation of [[Primordial Gods]], granting her a Divine foundation remarkable enough that portions of her Soul survived catastrophic fragmentation.[4]
- [[Immortality]]: Metis existed across enormous spans of Divine history and spiritually survived the destruction of her original body.[3]
- Divinity: Her original authorities were Health, Protection, Cunning, Prudence and Virtues.[1]
- Genius Intelligence: Metis possessed extraordinary intelligence and advised Zeus throughout his reign.[3]
- Ancient Combat Experience: She participated in the [[Titanomachy|Titan War]] despite not being primarily known as a frontline combatant.[5]
- Soul Resilience: Her spiritual existence survived being divided between Zeus and [[Athena]] for an immense period.[3][4]

##### Abilities Preserved Through Rebirth
Metis' reconstructed Soul retained the Essence of a Goddess and her ancient memories while developing a genuine Draconic form. Her previous Divine constitution therefore contributed to the formation of her current existence rather than being completely discarded.[1]
The strongest confirmed continuity between her original and modern Divinities is Cunning, which appears in both lists. Wisdom, Good Advice and Planning are independently confirmed as current authorities, while the original domains of Health, Protection, Prudence and Virtues remain part of her documented Divine history without individually established modern applications.[1][2]
