**[[Prometheus]]** is an ancient Titan God of the Greek Pantheon and one of the known Gods of Creation. (Lore adapted from *The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel*.)

## Rex Invictus Integration

[[Rex Invictus]] follows PJO canon for Prometheus' identity: he is the Titan of *The Last Olympian*. The [[Flamel Series|Flamel series]] makes him an Elder whose Shadowrealm is called [[Hades (Shadowrealm)|Hades]]; keep that only as background where it does not contradict PJO.

## Real-World Background (IRL)

In Greek myth Prometheus is a Titan, son of [[Iapetus]], who stole fire from the gods for humanity. [[Zeus]] chained him to the Caucasus, where an eagle ate his liver each day, until Heracles freed him ([[Hesiod]], *Theogony*; Aeschylus, *Prometheus Bound*). In PJO canon he sides with [[Kronos]] in *The Last Olympian* and gives Pandora's pithos to [[Percy Jackson|Percy]].

"My uncle appears in the mythology of many races. He has many names, but the story is the same: Prometheus created the first humani out of mud using an ancient technology that was so advanced it seemed magical. Some of the other [[Elders]] created beasts, but Prometheus went one step further. A step too far, for many. That was the reason the Elders hated him and banished him, and why he was sentenced to a long drawn-out death in the [[Hades]] Shadowrealm."
— Aoife on her Great-uncle Prometheus
Prometheus the Firelord is an Elder, the brother of the Witch of Endor, warrior comrade-in-arms to Huitzilopochtli and father-in-law to [[Abraham the Mage]]. A Master of Fire, he created the first humani; molding them from clay and accidentally animating them by the power of his aura. He also taught humani how to use fire for cooking and creation. Tsagaglalal and Gilgamesh were simultaneously the first of the [[First People]] to be animated by his aura and allowed them perpetual, renewable youth. His blood-red aura smells of sweet anise, but with the creation of the humani, it was permanently changed to be bitter and foul anise. He is friends with Niten, his future grandson-in-law.

### Appearance
Prometheus
Prometheus is portrayed as a very brawny, tall, as stated by [[Josh Newman|Josh]] to be at least six feet and seven inches tall, weighing at least 300 pounds, all of it pure muscle. He also has bright green eyes, a fiery red hair of tight curls and a beard streaked with gray and silver. He usually wears jeans and a deeply faded shirt and hiking boots. His face also possesses several tiny scars from his time of imprisonment. His auric armor is an ornate red metal shimmering crimson. It would pale, becoming crystalline and transparent as his aura overall decreased from his body by overdraft.
In [[Zephaniah]]'s memories when the first humani were created, Prometheus wore rust-colored leather armor, his hairy arms bare, and wielded two double-headed axes hanging on his waist belt.
On [[Danu Talis]], Prometheus was a tall redheaded warrior in shimmering crimson armor and metal-gloved hands. When [[Scathach]] first met him, he was a huge red-haired warrior in shimmering crimson armor. While piloting the Rukma vimana, the big Elder grinned from the controls, teeth white against his beard. For the civil uprising, Prometheus was dressed from head to foot in ornate red armor, and a massive red-bladed sword was strapped to his back, the hilt projecting over his left shoulder. On the top of the [[Pyramid of the Sun]], Prometheus wore burnished red armor.
Until Tuesday 5 June, his appearance looked middle-aged. By Wednesday 6 June, his hair has become graying-white and he looked tired and ill with more wrinkles due to supporting the Sorceress's ritual for dying the Alchemyst and fishing the [[Lotan]]. On Thursday 7 June, his self-healing with aura caused him to age, his hair turning snow-white, lines etching into his forehead, deep groves forming alongside his nose and at the corners of his mouth. He also restored the two spear holes on his auric armor. Dead, but saved by his daughter, his armor was in ruins.

### Personality
Prometheus appears to have a very kind yet cruel personality. He loathes [[Mars Ultor]] (up until [[The Warlock]]). He apparently is also very sensitive when it comes to betrayal, such as when he was, in his mind, betrayed by his sister, and when he was betrayed by his brother in arms Mars. He was also deeply depressed when [[Josh Newman|Josh]] turned over to the [[Dark Elders]]. Despite also being very loving and caring, he appears to have a very strict and cruel side; he isn't afraid to kill in cold blood. He also apparently has a more sneaky and cunning side to his personality.
Francis, on many occasions, has been said to have stole the secret of Fire from Prometheus. This prompted the Firelord to no longer train others for along time until Josh. While Prometheus was training Josh in the magic of Fire, he said that he'd forgiven St. Germain for stealing the secret of Fire because he had stolen it himself beforehand.
Even if he created the humani, Prometheus only favored a few ([[Tsagaglalal]] and [[Gilgamesh]]) and set strict paternal responsibility upon himself: only acting for their future independence, survival and rise as the next dominant race of Earth, but being indifferent to many enslaved or experimented humani on [[Danu Talis]]. After the Fall of Danu Talis, Prometheus helped many humans, but most of them were betrayed after he taught them, the latest was Francis. These many betrayals caused the Firelord to despise most humani (except [[Niten]]).
His warrior discipline ensured that when he looked at Abraham, his face was impassive and his posture was straight. He has a daredevil penchant for aviation, lots of firepower and explosives, even when he lived on Danu Talis. By modern day ten thousand years later, he habitually accepts surprise hugs from his "nieces" [[Scathach]] and [[Aoife]] with great love every time. For Aoife, he used his only revival chance to resurrect Niten.
He speaks English with a strong Southern accent. He has always been fond of the color red, and partial to green. He hates noisy, filthy creatures like parrots.

### Powers & Abilities
Prometheus is stated by many to be the greatest Master of Fire [[Magic]] to ever live. His power in this area was so great that beings such as [[Mars Ultor]], who feared nothing, was terrified of him. He is able to perform feats such as creating massive fireballs, raining fire from the sky, surrounding his body with fire, heating the temperature in a room to burning levels, burning and setting things on fire by touching them, projecting blasts of fire from his hands, creating a massive Supernova explosion that encompasses everything, and an infinite list of more possibilities. He was stated by [[Aoife]] to be one of the most powerful [[Elders]] in existence.
- [[Immortality]]: Prometheus is Immortal and does not age. Though an immortal may be poisoned or slain in battle, they may live for thousands or millions of years.
- Can Grant Immortality: Prometheus can grant Immortality to [[Humani]].
- Strength: Prometheus is physically a top warrior full of pure muscle, with a massive chest and thick arms. He can bodily push a vimana off a cliff.
- Martial Arts: Prometheus is a top warrior, skilled hand axes and a massive long sword.
- Driving: Prometheus learns to drive cars with the rise of the 20th century. He owns a  and drove an ancient VW Bug.
- Piloting: Prometheus is the finest flier on [[Danu Talis]]. He used his flight training and the environment to defeat a hundred vimana. However, he has a record of crashing into the original Yggdrasill repeatedly.
Master of Fire
- Healing: Masters of fire can heal certain types of wounds with fire by fusing and reforming tissue.
- Pyromancy: Masters of fire can create, extinguish, manipulate, move, and throw fire. Fire can be formed into shapes.
- Thermokinesis: Masters of fire can greatly heat an object or enemy with a touch.
- Welding: Masters of fire can melt and fuse stone, metal, and many other substances.

### The Magician
Prometheus was briefly mentioned by [[Nicholas Flamel]] as one of the [[Elders]] he could find to teach [[Sophie Newman|Sophie]] the art of Fire [[Magic]]. He is mentioned as the nemesis of "Francis" [[Comte de Saint-Germain]] for the latter being a thieving apprentice.

### The Necromancer
Prometheus plays a much larger role in [[The Necromancer]]. At some point, he escaped from his Shadowrealm prison and fled to make his home in the Shadowrealm of [[Hades]] at Point Reyes, California. [[Nicholas Flamel]], Perenelle, [[Sophie Newman|Sophie]], [[Josh Newman|Josh]], [[Aoife]] and [[Niten]] all traveled to this Shadowrealm in hopes that Prometheus could train Josh in Fire [[Magic]]. At first Prometheus refused, but after Sophie used her Silver Aura to create a copy of Zephanniah's armor around herself and used her memories to become almost exactly like the Witch, calling him the father of the humani, and that as their father he had responsibility to them. This convinced him to train Josh.
They all then travelled to a cottage that Prometheus had in his Shadowrealm where he taught Josh the secrets of Fire Magic. After Josh left the cottage while under the control of [[Mars Ultor]], Nicholas, Aoife, Niten, Sophie, and Perenelle Flamel used an [[Archon Skull]] to watch and follow Josh's every movement. When he arrived at [[Dr. John Dee|Dee]]'s, however, the connection was broken. Prometheus was appalled that Nicholas had even brought the skull and refused to touch it. He soon came around though, and used his Aura to fuel the skull.
Soon after, Prometheus got a call from Niten saying that a fight had ensued and Josh had gone over to the [[Dark Elders]].

### The Warlock
Prometheus is first shown in [[The Warlock]] driving out of his collapsing Shadowrealm with [[Nicholas Flamel|Nicholas]] and Perenelle. They go to [[Tsagaglalal]]'s house, where they stay with [[Sophie Newman|Sophie]] and [[Niten]]. When Hel, [[Odin]], and Mars arrive, Mars tries to attack Prometheus. However, after Tsagaglalal gives them both messages from [[Abraham the Mage]], they agree to become friends again. Prometheus, along with Mars, Hel, Odin, and [[Black Hawk]], go to [[Alcatraz]] to stop [[Dr. John Dee|Dee]] from unleashing monsters onto [[San Francisco]].
He also appears on [[Danu Talis]]'s northeast shores, first climbing Abraham's uncomfortable crystal spire near the city of [[Murias]]. He reports about [[Scathach]]'s group strangely arriving on the hills south of Murias and is commanded by Abraham to rescue them from the imperial court's dungeons. He proclaims himself as the finest flier in Danu Talis.

### The Enchantress
Prometheus fought alongside [[Niten]] against the Drakon, he was defeated and was revived by [[Tsagaglalal]], but asked for Niten to be revived rather than him. His daughter gave up her renewable youth and accepted mortal aging to revive him.
On [[Danu Talis]], Prometheus, Tsagaglalal, and [[Scathach]]'s group fought on top of the pyramid against [[Richard and Sara Newman|Osiris and Isis]]'s minions to help the [[Twins of Legend]]. Prometheus used white fire magic to control the field. The entire defense group survived and left the pyramid with [[Sophie Newman|Sophie]] on the [[Earthlords]]' vimana, leaving [[Josh Newman|Josh]] to complete the Prophecy, [[The Codex]] and become the singularity [[Marethyu]].

##### Death
Prometheus and [[Niten]] are stabbed by [[Spartoi]] Warriors in the end of [[The Enchantress]]. [[Tsagaglalal]] comes, and can only save one; when she saves Prometheus, he tells her to take the aura back and save Niten. Before he dies, he says "Tell Niten to find [[Aoife]] and ask the question. Tell him... she will say yes."

### Appearances

#### First Mention
Chapter 521
Prometheus is an ancient Titan God of the [[Greek Pantheon]] and one of the known Gods of Creation. Unlike many Gods whose authority is restricted to an Element, social institution or comparatively narrow natural phenomenon, Prometheus possesses influence over the fundamental Concept of Creation. [[Shiva]] explicitly identified him alongside himself, [[Borr]] and [[Odin]] as Gods who possessed influence over Creation and used that authority to produce various forms of existence, including Humans.[2]
Prometheus was later directly identified as one of the Gods responsible for the creation of Humanity. While considering why modern Humans were physically much weaker than supernatural races despite the extraordinary nature of their earliest ancestors, [[Victor Elderblood]] concluded that the exact answer could only be obtained from a God who had participated directly in Human creation, specifically naming Prometheus and Epimetheus. Victor was unable to question either Titan because both were already missing by that point in the story.[1]
Despite his importance to Humanity's origins and the cosmological history of the Greek Pantheon, Prometheus has never directly appeared in the present storyline. His location, current allegiance following the destruction of the old Greek political order and ultimate fate remain unknown.[1]

### Physical Appearance
Prometheus has not physically appeared in the novel, and no canonical description of his appearance has been provided. His height, build, hair color, eye color, clothing, Divine manifestation and other identifying physical characteristics are currently unknown.

### Personality
Prometheus has not received direct dialogue or sufficient on-page characterization to establish his personality. Traditional mythological descriptions of Prometheus as a rebellious benefactor of Humanity, trickster, prophet or enemy of [[Zeus]] should not automatically be treated as events or personality traits confirmed within the novel.
What is canonically established is that Prometheus was deeply involved with the creation of Humanity. Whether this involvement originated from affection toward Humans, Divine responsibility, experimentation or another motivation has never been explained.[2][1]

### History
Prometheus is an ancient Titan associated with the [[Greek Pantheon]]. He existed long before the modern supernatural era and belonged to the group of Gods whose influence extended into the Concept of Creation.[2]
At some point in ancient history, Prometheus participated in the creation of Humans. The novel later groups him with Epimetheus as Gods who directly created Humanity, making the brothers part of the wider collection of Creator Gods responsible for Human populations associated with different Divine civilizations.[1]
The precise process by which Prometheus created Humans has never been revealed. It is therefore unknown whether he created complete Human Souls, only constructed their bodies and biological characteristics, modified an existing prototype, collaborated with Epimetheus or acted through a larger Divine mechanism. His confirmed achievement is that he was sufficiently involved in Humanity's creation for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to regard consuming Prometheus as a possible method of obtaining first-hand knowledge concerning why the Human Race had been designed in its present form.[1]
Much of the mythology traditionally associated with Prometheus—including stealing fire from [[Olympus]], deceiving [[Zeus]] over sacrificial offerings, being chained while an eagle consumed his regenerating liver and later being released by Heracles—has not been independently confirmed within the novel and should not be presented as established MTW history without a corresponding chapter.

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Prometheus was first mentioned during the political gathering preceding the full escalation of the Demonic War. [[Diablo]] and the enslaved Lilith appeared before representatives of multiple supernatural factions and demanded a temporary non-aggression agreement under which the other Pantheons, Vampires, Witches, [[Werewolves]] and Youkai would refrain from interfering in the coming conflict between Demons, Angels and Humanity.[2]
The proposal was immediately challenged by [[Shiva]], who pointed out that Humanity was not exclusively the creation of the [[Heavenly Father]]. The Concept of Creation existed among Gods belonging to multiple Pantheons, and Shiva explicitly named himself, Prometheus, [[Borr]] and even [[Odin]] as Divine beings who possessed influence over Creation. Through this authority, such Gods had produced various creations, including Human beings.[2]
This connection made Diablo and Lilith's demand politically unrealistic. The conflict involved a Race in whose creation numerous Divine civilizations had participated, giving other Pantheons both ideological and practical reasons to interfere. Prometheus himself did not appear during the meeting or participate directly in the war within the material shown.[2]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Prometheus became relevant again centuries later while [[Victor Elderblood]] was observing the [[Heavenly Father]] in Heaven. Victor began considering the unusual weakness of ordinary Humans compared with nearly every supernatural Race. Even relatively weak Demons, Vampires, [[Werewolves]] and Witches possessed naturally healthy bodies and resistance to mundane illnesses, while Humans could be born with serious illnesses or disabilities despite descending from extraordinarily powerful early Humans such as Adam.[1]
Victor suspected that Humanity's limitations might not have been accidental. Information obtained from [[Lucifer]] Morningstar, [[Diablo]] and the hundreds of Gods Victor had consumed suggested that the Heavenly Father and the Gods responsible for creating Humans in other regions were the most likely sources of the Race's original design.[1]
To determine the exact answer rather than relying upon second-hand information, Victor considered consuming one of the Gods who had personally created Humans and examining their memories. The two examples he immediately identified were Epimetheus and Prometheus. That solution was unavailable because both [[Titans]] were described as missing.[1]
This establishes Prometheus's current canonical status more precisely than the older article: he is not confirmed deceased. He is missing, and even Victor did not possess his location or direct access to him at the time.[1]

### Skills & Abilities
Prometheus is a Third Generation Titan God and, more importantly, a genuine Creator God. Although he has never fought on-page, the novel directly establishes his relationship with Creation and his participation in the creation of Humanity.

#### Divine Physiology
- God Physiology: Prometheus is a genuine Divine Being of the [[Greek Pantheon]] and possesses the fundamental supernatural characteristics associated with Gods.
- Higher-Spirit Physiology: Conventional Gods are fundamentally high-order spiritual existences whose visible bodies are manifestations of their [[Divine Souls]] rather than ordinary mortal biology.
- Divine Soul: Prometheus possesses a Soul capable of containing Divinity and conceptual authority.
- [[Divine Energy]] Manipulation: As a God, Prometheus possesses and manipulates Divine Energy to sustain his Divine existence and exercise his authorities.
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Prometheus possesses the strength, speed, durability, stamina, reflexes and senses inherent to Divine existence, although none of these attributes have been individually demonstrated.
- Regeneration: Gods possess supernatural recovery far beyond ordinary mortal beings. Prometheus's individual regenerative limit has never been demonstrated.
- Agelessness: Prometheus has existed for eons without conventional biological aging.
- Godly [[Immortality]]: Prometheus is not subject to natural mortal death through aging. This does not make him absolutely impossible to kill, as Gods can still be permanently destroyed through sufficient damage to their Souls, Divinities or complete existence.
- [[Divine Presence]]: As an ancient God, Prometheus possesses the supernatural presence characteristic of Divine beings, although he has never directly manifested it on-page.
- Blessing Bestowal: Gods can grant supernatural effects derived from their Divine authorities. No Prometheus-specific Blessing has yet been shown.
- Curse Bestowal: Divine authority can also be expressed through negative conceptual effects related to a God's domains, although Prometheus has never demonstrated an individual Curse.

#### Creation Divinity
- Divinity — Creation: Prometheus possesses genuine influence over the Concept of Creation. [[Shiva]] directly named him among Gods who held influence over Creation and used that authority to create different forms of existence.[2]
- Creation Manipulation: Prometheus can use Divine authority to produce new creations rather than merely reshaping pre-existing mundane material.[2]
- Conceptual Creation: His power originates from a Divine relationship with Creation itself rather than solely from conventional craftsmanship or physical construction.
- Creation Energy Manipulation: Creation Gods can utilize the supernatural Energy associated with Creation to produce phenomena and living beings. Creation Energy belongs to a higher-order creative system used by beings whose authority genuinely extends into Creation.[3]
- Matter Creation: The Concept of Creation allows its users to create physical phenomena and objects, although Prometheus has not personally demonstrated the maximum complexity or scale of objects he can create.
- Life Creation: Prometheus directly participated in the creation of Humans, confirming that his Creative authority extends to living organisms rather than only inanimate matter.[2][1]
- Human Creation: Prometheus is explicitly identified as one of the Gods who created Humanity. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later refers to Prometheus and Epimetheus specifically as Gods who created Humans.[1]
- Species Creation: His involvement in Humanity's origin demonstrates the ability to participate in creating an entire sapient species rather than merely producing isolated artificial organisms.[1]
- Biological Design: Victor's speculation concerning why Humans possess particular biological weaknesses implies that the Gods who created them had influence over the characteristics incorporated into the Human Race. The exact degree to which Prometheus personally determined those characteristics remains unknown.[1]
- Creation Knowledge: Having directly participated in Human creation, Prometheus possesses first-hand knowledge concerning the processes and decisions involved. Victor specifically wanted access to Prometheus's memories because they could provide answers unavailable through later observers.[1]
- Conceptual Authority: Prometheus's Creation Divinity grants him authority over phenomena genuinely belonging to his Concept. The extent of that authority relative to other Creation Gods such as the Heavenly Father has never been established.
- Conceptual Resistance (Creation): A God's deep connection to a Concept provides supernatural resistance to effects operating through the same or subordinate conceptual framework. Prometheus's exact resistance has never been tested.

#### Humanity Creation
Prometheus's most important confirmed feat is his involvement in creating Humanity. Chapter 521 establishes that the [[Heavenly Father]] was not uniquely responsible for Humans: numerous Gods from different Pantheons possessed influence over Creation and produced Human beings associated with their respective Divine civilizations. Prometheus is specifically included among those Gods.[2]
Chapter 1045 later makes this considerably more explicit. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] refers to Prometheus and Epimetheus as Gods who created Humans and considers consuming one of them specifically because their memories would contain direct knowledge of Humanity's original construction.[1]
The available material does not explain the division of labor between Prometheus and Epimetheus. Consequently, the following should not presently be treated as individually confirmed:
- Prometheus single-handedly creating all Humans;
- Prometheus independently creating the Human Soul;
- Prometheus creating Humans specifically from clay;
- Prometheus giving Humans Fire;
- Prometheus being solely responsible for Human intelligence;
- Prometheus deliberately designing Human illnesses and disabilities;
- or Prometheus creating Adam.
The novel only confirms that Prometheus was one of the Creator Gods directly involved in Human creation.[2][1]

#### Unconfirmed Traditional Associations
Prometheus is traditionally associated in mythology with Fire, Forethought, crafty counsel and the advancement of Human civilization. The older version of this article consequently listed Fire as one of his principal Divinities.
However, the reviewed novel material directly confirms only his relationship with Creation and Humanity's creation. Prometheus has not personally demonstrated Fire Manipulation, a Fire Divinity, Future Sight or a separate Divinity of Forethought in the story.
These abilities should therefore not be placed among his confirmed powers unless a chapter directly establishes them.

### Power
Prometheus's exact combat level is unknown because he has never participated in an on-page battle. His significance comes primarily from the conceptual importance of his Divinity rather than any demonstrated martial feat.
Creation is a broad and exceptionally important Concept. Gods possessing authority over Creation can produce phenomena and living beings through Divine power rather than depending entirely upon natural reproduction or ordinary physical construction.[2][3] Prometheus's participation in Humanity's creation consequently establishes a level of conceptual specialization far beyond an ordinary minor Divine ability.
This does not automatically make Prometheus equal to every other Creation God. The Heavenly Father, for example, possesses enormous experience and efficiency with Creation, while later beings such as [[Victor Elderblood]] developed substantially more extensive forms of Creative authority. No direct comparison between Prometheus and those beings has been provided.

### Relationships

#### Epimetheus
Epimetheus is another Titan Creator God associated with Prometheus. By Chapter 1045, both are directly identified as Gods who created Humans, and both are missing.[1]
The exact division of responsibilities between them during Humanity's creation remains unknown. Neither Titan was available for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to question, preventing him from obtaining their first-hand memories of the process.[1]

#### Humanity
Prometheus possesses one of the deepest possible historical connections to Humans because he directly participated in their creation.[2][1]
The novel does not establish whether Prometheus currently feels affection, responsibility, indifference or hostility toward modern Humanity. His role as a Human Creator should therefore not automatically be expanded into the traditional mythological portrayal of Prometheus as Humanity's eternal champion.

#### Greek Pantheon
Prometheus belongs to the [[Greek Pantheon]] and is a Titan rather than one of the later Olympian Gods. Despite the destruction and reorganization of much of the Greek Divine civilization, Prometheus was not identified among the Gods participating in the major battles surrounding the collapse of [[Olympus]].
By Chapter 1045, his location was explicitly unknown.[1]

### Current Status
Prometheus should currently be indexed as Unknown (Missing), not deceased.
By Chapter 1045, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] possessed extensive information gathered from [[Lucifer]], [[Diablo]] and hundreds of consumed Gods yet still described both Prometheus and Epimetheus as missing. He specifically noted their absence as the reason he could not obtain a definitive answer concerning Humanity's original design directly from one of its creators.[1]
No reviewed chapter establishes Prometheus's death, identifies a killer or confirms that his Soul was destroyed. Any death marker appearing in older navigation templates or unsourced indexes should therefore not override Chapter 1045's explicit statement.
