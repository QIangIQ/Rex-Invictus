Themis is an Elder and the Greek Titaness of divine law. Like all titans, her parents are Uranus and Gaea. She is briefly mentioned as having the gift of foresight.

### Abilities
- [[Immortality]]: Themis is Immortal and does not age. Though an immortal may be poisoned or slain in battle, they may live for thousands or millions of years.
- Foresight: Themis is mentioned as having the gift of foresight.
