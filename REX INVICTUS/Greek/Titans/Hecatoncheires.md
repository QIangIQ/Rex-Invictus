**[[Hecatoncheires]]** are the three hundred-handed, fifty-headed sons of [[Gaia]] and [[Ouranos]]: [[Briares]] (Briareos), [[Cottus]] and [[Gyges]]. [[Ouranos]] imprisoned them, [[Zeus]] freed them, and they won the [[Titanomachy]] by hurling boulders, then guarded the [[Titans]] in [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]]. In [[The Battle of the Labyrinth]] [[Briares]] is freed from [[Alcatraz]] and helps defend [[Camp Half-Blood]].
