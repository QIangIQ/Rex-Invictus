### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 627
Thetis is an ancient Greek Goddess, Sea Nymph and Titan associated with the sea and water. She was formerly a member of the [[Greek Pantheon]] and was among the fifty Goddesses evacuated from the collapsing Olympian political situation by [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood during the Demonic War. After being brought into [[Victor Elderblood]]'s sphere, Thetis eventually became one of the Greek deities incorporated into Victor's Faction and later the [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]].[1][2]
Among the Goddesses Aphrodite rescued, Thetis was notable for possessing a properly developed Major Divinity rather than the incomplete or lesser Divinities possessed by many of the minor Greek Goddesses. Victor also specifically counted her alongside [[Hestia]], Nike and Aphrodite as one of the few members of the rescued Goddess group who could be regarded as meaningful military power.[1][3]
Despite that standing, Thetis is considerably less battle-hungry than figures such as Victor, [[Scathach]] or Hestia. When the rescued Goddesses were asked to volunteer for an operation against Demonic forces, Thetis deliberately looked away and began whistling when attention turned toward her, making it obvious that she had no desire to volunteer herself for the mission.[4]

### Physical Appearance
Thetis is a mature and beautiful Goddess with distinctive blue hair. Her appearance is sufficiently mature that the narration groups her together with the more visibly adult Goddesses such as [[Demeter]] rather than the younger-looking members of the Greek Divine faction. During her introduction she is directly identified as the blue-haired Sea Goddess among [[Aphrodite]]'s rescued group.[1][4]
Her exact eye color, height and other physical measurements have not yet been stated in the verified novel chapters.

### Personality
Thetis is comparatively relaxed, expressive and somewhat playful for an ancient Goddess. Although she possesses enough Divine development for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to classify her among the more militarily useful members of [[Aphrodite]]'s group, she does not share the extreme enthusiasm for combat displayed by many of Victor's closest warriors. When [[Hestia]], Nike and Aphrodite all turned toward her while discussing who would join a dangerous mission, Thetis avoided eye contact and began innocently whistling rather than volunteer herself.[3][4]
She can also display surprisingly childish reactions despite her immense age. After the surviving members of Victor's faction began discussing what they wanted constructed on his newly created planet, Thetis casually requested a waterfall. Aphrodite immediately declared that she had no right to make requests because she was not Hestia, prompting Thetis to pout and openly complain about the obvious favoritism.[5]
Her reaction to Victor's Nightmare Tower also demonstrates that her avoidance of unnecessary battle should not be mistaken for complete disinterest in growing stronger. When the Goddesses unrelated to Victor were forbidden from entering the Tower, Thetis was specifically singled out alongside Nike as one of the women who pouted at being excluded. Most of the other non-combat-oriented Goddesses were relieved that they would not have to experience the Tower's repeated deaths and extreme training, while Thetis was visibly dissatisfied at losing access to the opportunity.[6]
Thetis has nevertheless adapted to the hierarchy established by Victor. She and the other resident Greek Goddesses understand that Victor stands at the top of the faction with his wives immediately beneath him. Although some of them occasionally complain about the privileges given to Hestia and [[Tyche]], they continue contributing their Divinities and assistance to Victor's growing civilization.[6]

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
The [[Greek Pantheon]] entered a catastrophic period after the release of [[Typhon]], whose existence and Divinity made the Olympians realize that their ordinary forces were incapable of reliably stopping him. Simultaneously, the longstanding dysfunction and internal conflicts of [[Olympus]] made its political situation increasingly unstable. Rather than remain and allow every useful Goddess to be consumed by a war many of them had little desire to participate in, [[Aphrodite]] began gathering Goddesses she considered worth saving.[7]
Aphrodite ultimately brought approximately fifty Goddesses into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s territory. When Victor finally met the complete group in the [[Snow Clan]] mansion, Thetis was identified among its most important members alongside [[Hestia]], [[Tyche]], Nike, [[Demeter]], [[Rhea]], [[Iaso]], [[Hygea]] and the other prominent Greek Goddesses.[1]
Victor's assessment provided the first substantial canonical information regarding Thetis. She was explicitly identified as a Sea Nymph, the Goddess of the Sea and a Titan. More importantly, Thetis was one of the comparatively small number of rescued Goddesses who possessed a Major Divinity developed around a Concept. The majority of the fifty were Minor Goddesses whose Divinities had never fully developed, placing Thetis considerably above the ordinary members of the refugee group in Divine quality.[1]
The newly arrived Goddesses were not immediately allowed to interact freely with Victor's entire faction. Aphrodite and Hestia understood how arrogant Gods could become toward Mortals and consequently placed their companions in an isolated section of Snow territory while they were taught to treat members of the wider faction with appropriate respect.[3]
During discussions over protecting and relocating the Amazons from Demon-infested territory, Victor questioned which of the rescued Goddesses were actually capable of functioning as military assets. Based on the information provided to him, he identified only Hestia, Nike, Aphrodite and Thetis as serious military power among the group.[3]
Thetis' personal reaction complicated this assessment. After Hestia and Nike became involved in the planned operation, Aphrodite asked whether anyone else wished to accompany them. Hestia, Nike and Aphrodite all looked directly toward the blue-haired Sea Goddess. Thetis simply turned her face away and began whistling, clearly pretending that she had not noticed them and communicating that she did not intend to volunteer.[4]
This distinction is important to Thetis' character. She possesses recognized combat potential and a developed Divinity, but unlike the battle maniacs surrounding Victor, she does not actively search for dangerous conflicts merely because she is capable of participating in them.

#### King Among Kings
The political structure surrounding the surviving Greek Gods changed permanently after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s conquest of [[Olympus]]. The old [[Greek Pantheon]] ceased functioning under its previous rulers and Victor established a new Pantheon under his authority, later known as Dragons Nest. The Greek deities who had already allied themselves with him consequently became part of the much larger multi-racial faction growing around the Blood Dragon Progenitor.[2]
Thetis was among the Goddesses living within Victor's newly established territory when his family began examining and developing the enormous planet created for them. The planet was an extreme environment representing numerous aspects of Victor's own Powers, containing abnormal gravity, enormous oceans, hostile biomes and areas connected to the different elements and Concepts he controlled.[5]
When members of the family began proposing structures they wanted built, [[Hestia]] requested a large hearth, while Thetis suggested adding a waterfall. [[Aphrodite]] immediately told Thetis that she had no right to make requests. When Thetis confusedly asked why, Aphrodite explained that Hestia was an exception simply because she was "Bestia."[5]
Thetis responded by pouting and complaining that Aphrodite's favoritism did not explain anything. The exchange demonstrated the comparatively informal relationship the surviving Greek Goddesses had developed inside Victor's faction; despite their Divine age and former status within Olympus, they could now participate in ordinary domestic arguments around Victor's family rather than functioning exclusively as distant objects of worship.[5]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s continued evolution, he created the Nightmare Tower, an extraordinarily dangerous training environment capable of forcing its users to repeatedly confront their weaknesses, die and improve. Numerous members of Victor's family and military forces voluntarily entered the Tower in order to increase their strength.[6]
The Greek Goddesses who were not personally connected closely enough to Victor were prohibited from entering. Most of them were secretly relieved; many specialized in domestic, social or utility Divinities rather than direct combat and had no desire to die countless times merely to become stronger.[6]
Thetis was an exception. She and Nike were specifically noted to pout because they were not allowed access, showing that despite Thetis' earlier reluctance to volunteer for the Amazon operation, she still possessed an interest in improving her power when the opportunity was available on terms she found worthwhile.[6]
By this period Thetis and the other resident Greek Goddesses had fully adjusted to Victor's internal hierarchy. They were housed on his planet, contributed their Divinities and Blessings to the faction and assisted Victor's growing organization. Their continued usefulness was the reason Victor permitted them to remain under his protection despite not possessing the same intimate position as his wives or direct family.[6]

### Skills & Abilities
Thetis is an ancient Goddess possessing a developed Major Divinity related to the sea and water. Although [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] considers her one of the few rescued Greek Goddesses with genuine military value, the novel has so far provided relatively few direct combat demonstrations of her individual abilities. Abilities below therefore distinguish explicitly established traits from applications implied by her Divine authority.
- God Physiology: Thetis is a full Divine Being rather than a [[Demigod]] or mortal supernatural creature. She possesses [[Divine Energy]], a Divinity and the supernatural constitution expected of an ancient Greek Goddess.[1]
- Extreme Longevity: Thetis has existed for an immense period of time and dates back to the ancient Greek Divine era.
- Divine Energy: As a Goddess, Thetis possesses and manipulates Divine Energy through her Divinity. Her Divine development is significantly greater than the Minor Goddesses [[Aphrodite]] rescued from [[Olympus]].[1]
- Titan Physiology: The novel explicitly classifies Thetis as a Titan, placing her in the same broad Divine category referenced for figures such as [[Rhea]] and Aphrodite during her introduction.[1] The exact biological or generational reason for this classification has not yet been separately explained.
- Sea Nymph Physiology: Thetis is additionally identified as a Sea Nymph.[1] The story has not yet provided a complete list of racial abilities exclusive to [[Sea Nymphs]].
- Major Divinity: Thetis possesses one of the properly developed Major Divinities among the fifty Goddesses brought by Aphrodite. This distinguishes her from the numerous Minor Goddesses whose Divinities had never developed to the same level.[1]
- Sea/Water Divinity: Thetis' principal Divine authority is associated with the Sea and Water, reflected in her titles as Goddess of the Sea and Goddess of Water.[1]
- Water Manipulation: Through her Sea/Water Divinity, Thetis possesses supernatural authority over water. The precise upper limit, range and specialized techniques of this ability have not yet been demonstrated directly in the verified chapters.
- Conceptual Authority: Chapter 627 explicitly places Thetis among the Goddesses whose Major Divinities are developed around a Concept. The exact structure and limitations of her Concept have not yet received the detailed explanation given to more central Gods such as Nike.[1]
- Blessing Bestowal: By the period surrounding the Nightmare Tower, Thetis was among the resident Greek Goddesses contributing to Victor's faction through her Divinity and Divine Blessings. The exact individual effect of Thetis' personal Blessing on Victor has not yet been separately described.[6]
- Combat Proficiency: Victor's assessment of the rescued Greek Goddesses placed Thetis alongside [[Hestia]], Nike and Aphrodite as one of the only members who could be treated as meaningful military power.[3] This establishes that Thetis is not merely a ceremonial or domestic deity even though she is noticeably less enthusiastic about battle than Hestia or Victor.
- Ancient Experience: As an extremely old Divine Being belonging to the Greek supernatural world, Thetis possesses far greater experience than ordinary mortals and young supernatural beings. However, no detailed martial style has yet been shown.
- Combat Reluctance: Thetis' military capability should not be confused with eagerness to fight. When asked to volunteer for the Amazon operation, she conspicuously avoided doing so.[4]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
Thetis became one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Divine subordinates after [[Aphrodite]] removed her from [[Olympus]] and brought the rescued Goddesses into his sphere. Victor immediately recognized Thetis as one of the more valuable members of the group because of her Major Divinity and military potential.[1][3]
Their relationship is considerably more distant than Victor's relationships with [[Hestia]], Aphrodite or the Goddesses who later became his wives. Thetis does not possess unrestricted access to Victor's most important resources, something demonstrated when she was forbidden from entering the Nightmare Tower because she lacked the intimate connection required for access.[6]
Despite this, Thetis remains loyal to Victor's faction and contributes her Divine power and Blessings to its development.[6]

#### Aphrodite Elderblood
[[Aphrodite]] Elderblood was responsible for removing Thetis and dozens of other Goddesses from the increasingly catastrophic situation surrounding [[Olympus]]. Aphrodite subsequently became the principal representative of the rescued Goddesses within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction, while [[Hestia]] functioned as their internal leader.[7][3]
Thetis appears comfortable enough around Aphrodite to complain openly when she believes she is being treated unfairly. Their exchange over Thetis' requested waterfall demonstrates a relationship far more informal than the old political atmosphere of Olympus.[5]

#### Hestia Elderblood
[[Hestia]] and Thetis were both among the comparatively small number of rescued Greek Goddesses [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] regarded as substantial military assets.[3]
Hestia nevertheless enjoys a uniquely privileged position inside Victor's faction that Thetis does not share. Thetis openly complained when [[Aphrodite]] allowed Hestia to request modifications to Victor's planet while denying Thetis the same privilege.[5]

#### Nike
Thetis and Nike share several similarities within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction. Both possessed developed Divinities, were considered among the more useful military Goddesses and later became the two non-family Greek Goddesses specifically disappointed when Victor prohibited them from entering the Nightmare Tower.[3][6]
Their personalities nevertheless differ in combat motivation. Nike is an acknowledged pacifist who trained principally to protect herself, while Thetis simply demonstrated reluctance to volunteer for at least one dangerous operation despite her recognized fighting potential.[3][4]

### Trivia
- Thetis' first named appearance occurs in Chapter 627, although [[Aphrodite]]'s plan to evacuate useful Greek Goddesses begins during the [[Olympus]] crisis in Chapter 622.[7][1]
- The novel explicitly calls Thetis both a Sea Nymph and a Titan.[1] This differs from common Classical Greek mythology, where Thetis is generally classified as a Nereid rather than one of the [[Titans]].
- Classical mythology traditionally describes Thetis as the daughter of [[Nereus]] and Doris, the wife of [[Peleus]] and the mother of [[Achilles]]. These relationships have not yet been meaningfully explored in the novel and should therefore not be used to invent additional MTW plot history.
- Thetis is one of the comparatively few rescued Greek Goddesses confirmed to possess a Major Divinity developed around a Concept.[1]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly counted Thetis as one of only four important military powers among the rescued group, alongside [[Hestia]], Nike and Aphrodite.[3]
- Despite that assessment, Thetis immediately avoided volunteering when the other Goddesses looked toward her for the Amazon operation, turning away and whistling.[4]
- Thetis wanted a waterfall constructed on Victor's planet, but Aphrodite denied her request because she did not possess Hestia's special privileges.[5]
- Thetis responded to Aphrodite's explanation by pouting and complaining about the obvious favoritism.[5]
- Thetis and Nike were specifically dissatisfied when they were forbidden from entering Victor's Nightmare Tower, whereas most of the other independent Greek Goddesses were relieved to avoid its brutal training.[6]
- Thetis' complete combat techniques and the full capabilities of her Sea/Water Divinity remain largely unexplored. Abilities derived only from Classical mythology should not be automatically attributed to her MTW incarnation without novel evidence.
