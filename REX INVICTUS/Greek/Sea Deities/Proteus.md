**[[Proteus]]** is an early Greek sea god, one of the figures called the [[Abe-no-Seimei|Old Man]] of the Sea. He herds [[Poseidon]]'s seals, knows the future and changes shape to avoid giving prophecy.

## Greek History (IRL)

- In Homer's *Odyssey* (Book 4), Menelaus is stranded on the island of Pharos off Egypt. Proteus' daughter [[Eidothea]] tells him to hold her father fast through every shape, and Proteus then tells him how to get home.
- Herodotus makes Proteus a king of Egypt who kept Helen during the [[Trojan War]].
- His name gives the English word "protean".

## UOE (Far Future)

A resident of [[Karcharonexus]], with the other sea deities: [[Nereus]], [[Phorcys]], [[Ceto]], [[Glaucus]], [[Galene]] and [[Benthesikyme]].
