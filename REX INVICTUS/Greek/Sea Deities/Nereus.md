Nereus is a Great Elder who tried to prevent [[Nicholas Flamel|Perenelle Flamel]] from leaving [[Alcatraz]]. He was worshipped by the ancient Greeks as the original Old Man of the Sea. In [[The Sorceress]], Perenelle mentions that the last time he visited the human world was in in the year 1912; as the Titanic sank in the April of that year, this suggests that he may have been involved in that tragedy.
He was killed by Black Hawk in [[The Enchantress]].

### Appearance
The [[Proteus|Old Man of the Sea]] as seen by [[Nicholas Flamel|Perenelle Flamel]] on [[Alcatraz]] in [[The Sorceress]] is a stocky, surprisingly-normal looking man with a head of thick curly hair flowing to his shoulders. He also has webbed fingers and a short beard twisted into two tight curls. Nereus wears a sleeveless jerkin of overlapping kelp leaves and strands of green seaweed and is accompanied by the stench of rotting fish. In his left hand he bears a wickedly spiked stone trident. He has no lower limbs; instead, he has eight suckered, octopus-like, tentacles.

### Abilities
- [[Immortality]]: Nereus is Immortal and does not age. Though an immortal may be poisoned or slain in battle, they may live for thousands or millions of years.
