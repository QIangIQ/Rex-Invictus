### Appearances

#### First Mention
Chapter 778
Morpheus is a Greek God, one of the numerous children of [[Hypnos]], and the divine Personification of Dreams. Unlike an ordinary deity who merely possesses an ability related to dreaming, Morpheus awakened the actual Divinity of Dreams, establishing Dreams as the central Concept governing his Divine existence.[1]
Morpheus is particularly unusual even among the Greek Gods because his awakening affected Hypnos as well. After Morpheus became the Personification of Dreams, Hypnos inexplicably developed an extraordinarily accurate supernatural instinct resembling a sixth sense. Morpheus possesses the same characteristic to an even greater degree, allowing him to sense approaching danger and unfavorable future events with such reliability that Hypnos' siblings eventually came to regard the phenomenon as a form of prophetic perception.[1]
The relationship between Morpheus and Hypnos is itself anomalous. Despite being Hypnos' son, Morpheus reportedly resembles him so completely that observers compared them to long-lost twin brothers rather than parent and child. Morpheus also does not normally regard Hypnos as a father, instead treating him as an equal. [[Thanatos]] and [[Nemesis]] considered the connection strange enough to suspect that something deeper existed between their Divine existences, although its actual cause has never been explained.[1]

### Physical Appearance
Morpheus has not yet received a detailed physical description in the novel. However, his resemblance to [[Hypnos]] is stated to be extraordinary. [[Thanatos]] and [[Nemesis]] considered Morpheus effectively a copy of his father, to the point that seeing the two together was compared to looking at long-lost twin brothers rather than a father and his son.[1]
The exact details of Morpheus' hair, eyes, height, clothing and Divine manifestations have not presently been described.

### Personality
Very little of Morpheus' personality has been directly shown because his first canonical information is delivered through the observations of his relatives rather than a personal appearance.
The clearest established trait is his unusual attitude toward [[Hypnos]]. Despite being Hypnos' biological son, Morpheus does not treat him with the conventional relationship expected between parent and child. Instead, he apparently regards Hypnos as an equal Being. Combined with their nearly identical appearance and the supernatural connection that emerged after Morpheus awakened his Divinity, this behavior contributed to the suspicions held by [[Thanatos]] and [[Nemesis]] regarding the true nature of their relationship.[1]
Like Hypnos, Morpheus also possesses an exceptionally reliable instinct regarding future danger. His version of this ability is stated to be even more developed than his father's, suggesting that he possesses an unusually strong sensitivity toward events and phenomena that have not yet fully occurred.[1]

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Morpheus was first mentioned during the events surrounding [[Victor Elderblood]]'s faction entering the [[Greek Underworld]]. While [[Thanatos]], [[Hypnos]] and [[Nemesis]] secretly observed Nyx Elderblood, [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood, [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] and [[Morgana Elderblood]], Hypnos suddenly experienced a powerful feeling that something terrible was about to happen.[1]
The reaction prompted Thanatos and Nemesis to recall the origin of Hypnos' unusual instincts. Hypnos had not always possessed such extraordinary perception. The change occurred after one of his thousands of children, Morpheus, matured and awakened the Divinity of Dreams, becoming the Personification of Dreams. From that point onward, Hypnos' instincts sharpened drastically and began functioning like a supernatural sixth sense.[1]
The same phenomenon existed within Morpheus himself, except his version was even more advanced. Neither Thanatos nor Nemesis understood why Morpheus' awakening had altered Hypnos, since Divine abilities were normally individual to each God. To them, the connection resembled something deeper than biological inheritance.[1]
Their suspicions were reinforced by Morpheus' resemblance to Hypnos. Morpheus was described as essentially a copy of his father, making the pair appear more like identical brothers than members of two generations. Morpheus himself also treated Hypnos as an equal rather than as his father. Despite recognizing that something unusual existed between the two, Thanatos and Nemesis chose not to investigate because every child of Hypnos possessed their own responsibilities and the matter did not directly concern them.[1]
Hypnos' warning was immediately validated. He sensed that Thanatos would soon regret arrogantly claiming that Death could not experience fear, and that Hypnos himself would somehow become involved. Moments later, [[Scathach]] detected their hidden group and launched her spear directly through Thanatos' head before he could properly respond. The incident further reinforced the reliability of the supernatural instinct shared between Hypnos and Morpheus.[1]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Following the destruction and restructuring of the old [[Greek Pantheon]], its surviving territories and inhabitants ceased existing under the former Olympian political order. The Greek Divine structures were subsequently incorporated into the much larger supernatural civilization developing beneath [[Victor Elderblood]], with the former Greek territories eventually becoming connected to Dragons Nest and Victor's reorganized domains.
Morpheus remains alive and is counted among the surviving Gods associated with this new order, although he has not yet received an extended individual scene following the collapse of the old Greek Pantheon.

### Skills & Abilities
Morpheus is a Major God whose principal Divine authority is the Concept of Dreams. Because his direct appearances remain extremely limited, only powers directly established through Chapter 778 or through the later demonstrated properties of Dream Energy are listed below.
- God Physiology: As a natural-born Greek God, Morpheus possesses the supernatural constitution of a Divine Being, including [[Divine Energy]] and the longevity and supernatural nature associated with Gods.
- Dream Manipulation: Morpheus awakened the Divinity of Dreams and became the Personification of Dreams itself. Dreams therefore constitute the fundamental Divine Concept over which his authority operates.[1]
- Conceptual Manipulation — Dreams: Morpheus is not simply described as a God who can create dreams; he specifically became the Personification of Dreams. His existence is therefore directly associated with the Divine Concept governing Dreams.[1]
- Dream Energy Manipulation: As the God embodying Dreams, Morpheus' Divine authority operates through the supernatural Energy associated with the Dream Concept. Dream Energy is later demonstrated to be capable of directly interacting with consciousness and mental states.[1][2]
- Consciousness Manipulation: Dream Energy can subtly interfere with consciousness. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later demonstrated that a sufficiently precise application could cause a target to lose consciousness momentarily without realizing that they had actually fallen asleep.[2] As the Personification of Dreams, Morpheus possesses the Divinity from which this category of Dream-based phenomena originates.[1]
- Mind Manipulation: Dream-based power can exploit the temporary lowering of a target's mental defenses to gain access to their mind. Victor demonstrated this property by using Dream Energy to enter [[Hela Elderblood]]'s mind after briefly disrupting her consciousness.[2]
- Mental Information Acquisition: Once access to the target's mental space is achieved through Dream Energy, information and memories contained within the mind can be observed. Victor used this application to obtain Hela's personal history without her initially realizing that her consciousness had been compromised.[2]
- Extrasensory Perception: Morpheus possesses an extraordinary instinct described as being significantly more advanced than the supernatural sixth sense possessed by [[Hypnos]].[1]
- Precognition: The instincts shared by Morpheus and Hypnos can warn them of dangerous or unfavorable events before those events occur. [[Thanatos]] and [[Nemesis]] had observed the phenomenon for long enough that they effectively considered it a prophetic ability capable of sensing bad events approaching either themselves or people around them.[1]
- Danger Sense: The prophetic aspect of Morpheus' instincts allows him to sense impending danger. Hypnos' weaker version warned him immediately before [[Scathach]] attacked Thanatos, while the narration explicitly states that Morpheus possesses an even more advanced version of the same characteristic.[1]
- Divine Connection: Morpheus possesses an unexplained supernatural connection with Hypnos. His awakening of the Divinity of Dreams directly caused Hypnos' instincts to become extraordinarily enhanced despite Divine abilities normally being individual to their respective Gods. Thanatos and Nemesis considered the link to be far deeper than an ordinary father-son relationship.[1]

### Divinity

#### Dreams
Morpheus' defining Divine Concept is Dreams. He awakened this Divinity after reaching maturity and consequently became the Personification of Dreams.[1]
Dream-based power is shown later in the novel to operate considerably deeper than producing ordinary sleeping visions. [[Victor Elderblood]] demonstrated that Dream Energy can influence consciousness so subtly that a target can be rendered unconscious without realizing it, allowing the user to bypass mental defenses and enter the target's mind.[2]
This makes Dream authority particularly dangerous against opponents who rely entirely upon consciously maintained mental defenses. The target does not necessarily need to experience an obvious conventional dream; Dream Energy can instead manipulate the transition between consciousness and unconsciousness and exploit the resulting opening.[2]

### Relationship with Hypnos
[[Hypnos]] is Morpheus' father and the Personification of Sleep. Morpheus is only one among Hypnos' enormous number of children, yet their relationship is uniquely different from that of Hypnos and his other offspring.[1]
When Morpheus matured and awakened the Divinity of Dreams, Hypnos underwent an unexplained change. His instincts became dramatically sharper and began allowing him to sense events before they occurred. This was particularly strange because the supernatural abilities of Gods were understood to be individual; Morpheus awakening a Divinity should not normally have altered Hypnos' abilities.[1]
The link appears to operate in both directions. Morpheus possesses the same prophetic instinct, but at a considerably more advanced level. Because of this shared characteristic, their almost identical appearances and Morpheus' refusal to treat Hypnos as a conventional father, [[Thanatos]] and [[Nemesis]] considered the pair to resemble two equal Entities rather than parent and child.[1]
The actual explanation for this connection remains unrevealed.

### Family
Morpheus belongs to the lineage descending from two of the most ancient Greek [[Primordial Gods]]. His father, [[Hypnos]], is the Personification of Sleep and a son of Nyx Elderblood and [[Erebus]].[1]
Through Hypnos, Morpheus is therefore a descendant of the Greek Primordial family associated with Night, Darkness and several fundamental aspects of existence.

### Trivia
- Morpheus is explicitly only one among approximately one thousand children of [[Hypnos]], yet his awakening uniquely affected his father.[1]
- Morpheus' awakening of the Divinity of Dreams somehow enhanced Hypnos' instincts even though Divine powers are normally individual to each God.[1]
- Morpheus possesses an even more advanced version of the prophetic sixth sense displayed by Hypnos.[1]
- His relatives describe Morpheus as essentially a physical copy of Hypnos, making the pair resemble twin brothers rather than father and son.[1]
- Morpheus does not treat Hypnos as his father and instead regards him as an equal.[1]
- [[Thanatos]] and [[Nemesis]] suspected that Morpheus and Hypnos were connected on a level deeper than an ordinary familial relationship, but neither investigated the mystery.[1]
- The source of the shared prophetic ability between Morpheus and Hypnos remains unexplained.
- Morpheus' Dream Divinity should not be confused with Hypnos' authority over Sleep. The two Concepts are closely related but are treated as distinct Divine authorities within the novel.[1]
