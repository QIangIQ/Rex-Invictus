**[[Phobos]]** is the god of fear, son of [[Ares]] and [[Aphrodite]], twin of [[Deimos]]. He rode into battle in his father's chariot. In [[The Lightning Thief]]-era books and *The Son of Neptune* he and [[Deimos]] appear as [[Ares]]' bikers. Mars' larger moon is named for him.

Phobos and Deimos are a pair of Satyrs who accompanied and served Mars Ultor both on [[Danu Talis]] and in the catacombs of [[Paris]]. Like all satyrs, they have the upper body of a human and the lower body of a goat. They feed on fear and panic.
