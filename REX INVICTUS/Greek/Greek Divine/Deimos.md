**[[Deimos]]** is the god of terror and dread, son of [[Ares]] and [[Aphrodite]], twin of [[Phobos]]. Mars' smaller moon bears his name, which matters in [[Rex Invictus]] now that four million Imperial soldiers are garrisoned on Mars.
