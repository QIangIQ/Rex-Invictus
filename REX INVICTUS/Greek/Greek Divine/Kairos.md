Kairos, also known as Caerus, is a third-generation God of the [[Greek Pantheon]] and the Divine authority associated with Opportune Time, representing the favorable or decisive moment in which an opportunity can occur. He is an old acquaintance of [[Aphrodite]] and maintains connections with the Three Sisters of Fate, whose responsibilities are associated with the Past, Present and Future.[1]
Kairos first becomes relevant when [[Victor Elderblood]] attempts to discover why [[Nightingale]] has become completely inaccessible. Aphrodite contacts Kairos because his Divine Domain and connections make him one of the few known Gods capable of investigating an abnormality involving Time. Through his connection to one of the temporal Fate sisters, Kairos later confirms that [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] deliberately sealed Nightingale and altered the realm's temporal relationship with the outside world.[1][2]
Despite his connection to temporal phenomena, Kairos should not be treated as a general God of Time. His confirmed Divine authority is specifically associated with opportune time and favorable moments, while the broader temporal investigation involving Nightingale relied partly upon his connections with the Fate sisters.[1]

### Physical Appearance
Kairos has not received a canonical physical description in the novel.
His debut occurs entirely through a remote conversation with [[Aphrodite]], and he does not physically appear during the scene.[1] His current artwork should therefore be treated as an artistic interpretation rather than a canonical depiction.

### Personality
Kairos is portrayed as calm, restrained and notably less energetic than [[Aphrodite]]. When she contacts him, he responds in a dry and matter-of-fact manner despite Aphrodite speaking to him with the familiarity of an old friend.[1]
He also appears reliable. When Aphrodite asks him to investigate [[Nightingale]], Kairos accepts the request without unnecessary argument and immediately contacts someone more directly connected to the temporal issue.[1]
His conversation also shows that he possesses sympathy for the Three Sisters of Fate. When Aphrodite asks about them, Kairos explains that the sisters remain bound to the responsibilities of preserving the Past, Present and Future. His tone briefly carries sadness when discussing the endless nature of their work, although he understands that abandoning those duties would have catastrophic consequences for the beings connected to their mythology.[1]
Kairos agrees with Aphrodite that remaining in the same place performing the same cosmic duty for thousands of years would be extraordinarily boring, showing that despite his Divine age he retains a relatively ordinary appreciation for freedom and variety.[1]

### Synopsis

#### A New Pillar
After [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] sealed [[Nightingale]], [[Victor Elderblood]] became unable to return to the Vampire world. Conventional portals, communications and other methods of entry stopped functioning, leaving Victor separated from several members of his family.[3]
After considering several possibilities, Victor eventually sought information from [[Aphrodite]]. When Victor asked whether she knew why Nightingale had suddenly become inaccessible, Aphrodite admitted that she did not know but immediately contacted Kairos.[1]
During the conversation, Kairos was identified as the God associated with opportune time. Aphrodite asked him to investigate the situation surrounding Nightingale, and after recognizing the realm as Vlad's kingdom, Kairos agreed to help.[1]
Kairos explained that he needed to contact one of his own acquaintances before he could provide a reliable answer. Aphrodite then asked about that individual, prompting a discussion about the three sisters responsible for maintaining temporal continuity.[1]
One of the sisters continuously maintained the Present, while the other two were associated with the Past and Future. Aphrodite questioned why they could not simply leave their responsibilities temporarily, but Kairos explained that doing so would cause the Past, Present and Future of the beings belonging to their mythology to collapse.[1]
Although he understood the necessity of their duties, Kairos admitted that he agreed with Aphrodite that performing the same task continuously for thousands of years sounded extremely boring.[1]
Kairos later contacted Aphrodite again after completing the investigation. He confirmed that Vlad Dracula Tepes had deliberately closed Nightingale and altered its temporal relationship with the outside world. Time inside the realm was moving differently from Time outside it, explaining why Victor could not regain access through ordinary means.[2]
The information confirmed that Nightingale's disappearance was neither accidental nor the result of a simple portal malfunction, but a deliberate action performed by Vlad using extremely high-level temporal measures.[2]

### Divinity

#### Opportune Time
Kairos' confirmed Divine Domain is Opportune Time.[1]
The Concept concerns the appropriate, favorable or decisive moment in which an action or opportunity becomes possible. This is distinct from unrestricted control over the complete Concept of Time.
The full applications and combat limitations of this Divinity have not yet been demonstrated.

### Skills & Abilities
- God Physiology: As a third-generation God, Kairos possesses the fundamental supernatural characteristics of Divine Beings.
- [[Immortality]]: Kairos has existed for an immense period of Time and does not age according to ordinary mortal biology.
- [[Divine Energy]] Manipulation: He can generate and manipulate Divine Energy through his Divine nature.
- Enhanced Physical Attributes: His Divine physiology grants strength, speed, durability, stamina and senses vastly superior to ordinary mortal beings.
- Regeneration: As a God, Kairos possesses supernatural regenerative capabilities.
- Conceptual Manipulation: His primary powers originate from a Divine Concept rather than ordinary elemental magic.
- Opportune Time Divinity: Kairos possesses Divine Authority associated specifically with Opportune Time.[1]
- Opportunity Manipulation: His Divine Concept is associated with favorable opportunities and the decisive moments in which they can occur.
- Timing Manipulation: Kairos possesses supernatural authority over the principle of proper or advantageous timing.
- Temporal Affinity: [[Aphrodite]] specifically contacts Kairos when information regarding an abnormal temporal phenomenon is required.[1]
Kairos has not directly demonstrated unrestricted Time Manipulation such as stopping, reversing, accelerating or freely traveling through Time. These abilities should not be assigned without additional evidence.
- Temporal Knowledge: Kairos possesses extensive knowledge regarding Beings and systems associated with Time.[1]
- Temporal-Anomaly Investigation: He assisted in investigating [[Nightingale]]'s abnormal temporal state and returned with accurate information concerning Vlad's actions.[1][2]
- Temporal-System Knowledge: Kairos understands the functions performed by the three sisters responsible for the Past, Present and Future.[1]
- Consequential Awareness: He understands that disrupting those temporal duties would cause severe consequences for beings connected to their mythology.[1]
- Divine Connections: Kairos possesses a useful network among ancient Greek Gods.
- Aphrodite: Aphrodite can contact him casually and trusts him with sensitive supernatural requests.[1]
- Three Sisters of Fate: Kairos possesses enough familiarity with the sisters to contact at least one directly and obtain information through her temporal authority.[1]
- Information Gathering: Kairos is capable of using his Divine contacts to obtain information outside his own immediate area of expertise. Rather than pretending to personally know Nightingale's condition, he consulted the individual best suited to investigate the phenomenon.[1][2]

### Relationships

#### Aphrodite Elderblood
[[Aphrodite]] is an old friend and acquaintance of Kairos.
Their conversation contains little formal Divine etiquette. Aphrodite contacts him casually, asks him for assistance and quickly shifts into asking about mutual acquaintances.[1]
Kairos appears accustomed to her personality and cooperates without complaint. When she asks for information concerning [[Nightingale]], he immediately agrees to investigate the matter.[1]
Their familiarity suggests a relationship extending far beyond the immediate events of the novel.

#### Three Sisters of Fate
Kairos possesses a direct relationship with the Divine sisters responsible for maintaining temporal continuity within the Greek supernatural system.
One sister preserves the Present, while the other two are responsible for the Past and Future.[1]
Kairos shows subtle sadness when discussing their endless duties and agrees that their existence sounds monotonous, although he understands that their responsibilities cannot simply be abandoned.[1]
When [[Nightingale]]'s altered flow of Time required investigation, Kairos relied upon this connection to obtain information that [[Aphrodite]] herself could not access.[1][2]

#### Zeus
[[Zeus]] is identified as Kairos' father and the former God-King of the [[Greek Pantheon]].
No substantial direct interaction between the two has currently been shown, and Kairos does not play a major role in Zeus' principal on-page conflicts.

### Trivia
- Kairos is introduced entirely through a remote conversation in Chapter 247 and therefore has no canonical physical description.[1]
- His current image is consequently an artistic interpretation.
- The novel specifically associates Kairos with opportune time, which is more accurate than simply describing him as a generic God of Luck.[1]
- His name and Divinity are strongly associated with the idea of the decisive or favorable moment rather than unrestricted chronological Time.
- Despite his association with Time, Kairos has not shown complete Time Manipulation.
- He possesses direct connections to the sisters responsible for preserving the Past, Present and Future.[1]
- Kairos' first significant contribution to the story is confirming that [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] deliberately altered [[Nightingale]]'s temporal relationship with the outside world.[2]
- Kairos' tone is noticeably dry compared with [[Aphrodite]]'s more expressive manner.[1]
- He appears sympathetic toward the extremely repetitive responsibilities imposed upon the Fate sisters.[1]
- No direct combat feat has yet been shown for Kairos, making precise combat scaling unreliable.
