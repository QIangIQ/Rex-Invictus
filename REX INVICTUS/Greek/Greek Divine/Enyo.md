**[[Enyo]]** is the Greek goddess of destructive war, companion of [[Ares]]. The Romans identified her with [[Bellonna|Bellona]].

---

## Greek History (IRL)

Homer pairs her with [[Ares]] on the field before [[Troy]], where she carries Kydoimos (Uproar) with her. Some sources make her Ares' mother, sister or nurse, and the god's epithet Enyalios may come from her name. She delights in sacking cities and is one of the deities [[Hesiod]] and later poets set beside [[Phobos]], [[Deimos]] and Eris. She also shares her name with one of the [[Gray Sisters]].
