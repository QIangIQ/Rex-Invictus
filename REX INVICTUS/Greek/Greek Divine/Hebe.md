**[[Hebe]]** is the goddess of youth, daughter of [[Zeus]] and [[Hera]], cupbearer of the gods until Ganymede replaced her. She married the deified [[Hercules]]. Her Roman name is Juventas. [[Camp Half-Blood]] has a cabin for her children after [[The Last Olympian]].
