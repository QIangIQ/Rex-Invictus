**[[Iris]]** is the goddess of the rainbow and a messenger of the gods, especially [[Hera]]. In Riordan's books she runs Iris-messaging, with her assistant Fleecy handling calls. This note also covers Fleecy.

---

## Hierarchical Placement

- [[Thaumas]] and Electra
    - [[Iris]]
    - [[Harpy|Harpies]] (her sisters)

---

## Greek History (IRL)

Iris, daughter of Thaumas and the Oceanid Electra, carries messages between gods and mortals, travels on the rainbow and fills the clouds with water. She carried a jug of [[Styx]] water used for divine oaths. She is the wife of Zephyrus and sister of the [[Harpy|Harpies]]. Sources: Hesiod's *Theogony*, Homer's *Iliad*.

---

## PJO Canon

Demigods send Iris-messages by throwing a golden drachma into a rainbow. In [[The Sea of Monsters]] she relays the message that lets [[Chiron]] rescue the questers, and [[Chiron]] is her longtime friend. In [[The Battle of the Labyrinth]] [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] receives a message from [[Bianca di Angelo]]'s ghost without paying. In [[The Last Olympian]] Percy's request gets her children a cabin, and in [[The Lost Hero]] her son Butch is a camper. In [[The Son of Neptune]] she burns the monster army with a rainbow, runs Rainbow Organic Foods & Lifestyles in Berkeley and advises Frank. Her employee Fleecy now handles Iris-messaging and gives Percy green tea for his memory. In [[The Chalice of the Gods]] she is cleared as a suspect in the chalice theft.

#### Appearance
- Not physically described. She manifests as a voice in the shimmer of the rainbow ("Her voice whispered from the light").
- The rainbow "thinned into a narrow stream", "cracked and dissolved" and finally "died in silence" ([[Ch18 - Rex Invictus XI|Ch18]]).

#### Personality
- **Exasperated but pleased by over-payment:** "You really do not understand subtlety. When you use that many coins, it feels like you are throwing gold bars at my head." She then "materialized faster than usual" ([[Ch18 - Rex Invictus XI|Ch18]]).
- **Empathic busybody:** she feels [[Percy Jackson|Percy]]'s emotions "through the rainbow" and tells him to work on his mental barriers ([[Ch18 - Rex Invictus XI|Ch18]]).
- **Gossip-loving:** "When can I tell [[Hephaestus]] TV about you two?" and "I'm going to combust from the emotional pressure in here." ([[Ch18 - Rex Invictus XI|Ch18]])
- **Perceptive and amused:** "Young one… you should not doubt what you heard. That went both ways." She has handled [[Artemis]]'s calls "for centuries" and knows how the goddess sounds "when she is trying not to show something" (Ch18).
- **Mercenary discretion:** she accepts bribes for silence ("Good"), but teases anyway: "Whatever you say, child." (Ch18)

#### Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Iris messages:** paid with drachmae scraped or offered into the light. Bandwidth is limited ([[Ch18 - Rex Invictus XI|Ch18]]).
- **Emotional sensing** through the rainbow link ([[Ch18 - Rex Invictus XI|Ch18]]).
- **Observational instruments:** she has surveillance channels on [[Percy Jackson|Percy]]. His [[Ch03 - Rex Natus I|Ch3]] barrier pulse neutralises them "As long as we do not speak their standard names". Percy avoids saying "Iris", which is why she is "The one of Many Colors".

Iris, know as the goddess of the rainbow due to her multicoloured aura, is one of the [[Elders]]. She is very powerful, having access to the Shadowrealm waters of the River [[Styx]]. She was mentioned in [[The Sorceress]], having given Nicholas Flamel two multicoloured friendship bracelets in return for a favour he did for her once. One of these was used to defeat the Hooded Ones, using the power of the River Styx to knock them unconscious for (according to legend) a year and a day.

### Abilities
- [[Immortality]]: Iris is Immortal and does not age. Though an immortal may be poisoned or slain in battle, they may live for thousands or millions of years.
