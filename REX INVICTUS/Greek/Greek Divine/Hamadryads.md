**[[Hamadryads]]** are a type of dryad, or tree spirit, that are believed to be the actual tree given life, instead of just a spirit bonded to the tree.[citation needed]  Hamadryads used to live on Earth, but moved to Tammuz's Shadowrealm, The Grove of Eridhu, when the humani began to wreak havoc on the environment. (Lore adapted from *The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel*.)

## Real-World Background (IRL)

In Greek myth hamadryads are tree nymphs bound to a single tree; they die when it dies. PJO canon has dryads such as Juniper at [[Camp Half-Blood]].
