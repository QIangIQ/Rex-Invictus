Events of the MTWABV timeline (Victor's conquests, the fall of the pantheons, the [[Elderblood Empire]]) are distant background or rumor unless a chapter puts them on the page. ### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 1111
Ion is a God explicitly identified as a Primordial God and the Leader of the unnamed Pantheon governing the planet formerly sustained by [[Diana]] and Diane.[1]
He was Diana's greatest and long-time confidant before she absorbed [[Victor Elderblood]]'s leaked Energy, abandoned him as her Champion and sided with the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Ion was critically wounded by Diana and nearly executed by the twin [[World Trees]] before Rose and Eleanor intervened because the Primordial God was considered more useful alive.[1]
Ion and the remaining Gods of his Pantheon were subsequently detained as the Elderblood Empire took control of their civilization.[1]

### Physical Appearance
Ion's physical appearance has not been canonically described in sufficient detail.
During his debut, he was already gravely wounded and on the ground attempting to close a hole that had been opened directly through his heart. [[Diana]] later stabbed him in one eye with a Cursed Dagger.[1]
His canonical hair color, eye color, height, clothing and other distinguishing features remain unknown. His current artwork should therefore be treated as an artistic interpretation.

### Personality
Ion placed enormous trust in [[Diana]].
The narration identifies Diana as his greatest confidant, and Ion himself had apparently known and trusted her long enough that her betrayal was immediately incomprehensible to him.[1]
Even after Diana opened a hole through his heart, Ion's first conclusion was not that she had consciously betrayed him. He instead believed that an unknown enemy was controlling her.
This demonstrates the extraordinary confidence he had in her loyalty.
Ion reacted with genuine fury toward the presumed controller and demanded that the responsible Being reveal themselves. His certainty was shattered when Diana explicitly denied being controlled and informed him that she had simply found something she desired more than her previous existence.[1]
Ion's brief appearance also suggests pride in his position as the planet's Divine protector. Diana's declaration that she no longer needed his services because she had found a Champion capable of protecting the entire Galaxy directly rejected the role Ion had previously fulfilled for her.
Because his entire canonical appearance occurs while wounded, betrayed and captured, little is currently known about Ion's normal governing style, morality or temperament outside the crisis.

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Ion was the Primordial God leading the Pantheon of an unnamed inhabited planet associated with the twin [[World Trees]] [[Diana]] and Diane.[1]
Diana was the planet's Positive World Tree and Ion's greatest confidant.
The political balance of their world changed after an enormous quantity of Energy released by [[Victor Elderblood]] spread into the surrounding Galaxy. World Trees on other planets absorbed portions of that Energy, and Diana and Diane were among those affected.[1]
Victor's Energy carried his increasingly abnormal Eldritch nature while simultaneously remaining highly natural and compatible with World Trees. The affected Trees developed an intense dependency upon his Energy and began changing their priorities toward joining him.[1]
The [[Elderblood Empire]] had already contacted Diana and Diane in preparation for a future alliance. Once Victor's Energy reached them, the negotiations changed completely.
Ion first appeared after Diana had already betrayed him.
He fell to the ground while attempting to stop the damage from a hole opened through his heart by the World Tree he trusted most.[1]
Diana apologized but stated that the attack was not personal. She had found something more desirable than ordinary self-preservation and now wished to live for her "new planet".
Ion looked at the black-eyed, heavily flushed Diana and immediately concluded that she was being controlled.
He demanded that the responsible party reveal themselves.
Diana responded by taking out a Cursed Dagger supplied by the Elderblood forces and stabbing him in one eye.[1]
She then directly denied being controlled.
Diana thanked Ion for his previous service and informed him that she had found a new Champion capable of protecting not only her planet but the entire Galaxy.
The new Champion was Victor Elderblood.[1]
Diane then joined her sister.
The twins approached Ion together with daggers and prepared to kill him.
Rose and Eleanor stopped the execution.
Rose explained that Ion was more useful alive. In her private thoughts, she regarded him as a possible "ingredient" for Victor.[1]
Diana and Diane accepted the decision, signed their contracts and entered Victor's Soul.
The Imperial operation then concluded successfully. Rose reported that all mission objectives had been completed, every target had been captured and no one had escaped.[1]
Eleanor ordered the Gods to be detained because they could serve as ingredients for Victor while the Imperial forces moved to place the civilization under Elderblood rule.[1]
Ion's individual fate after his capture has not been shown in later material currently available in the project sources.

### Skills & Abilities
Ion's personal Divinity, combat style and named techniques remain unknown. His article should therefore distinguish confirmed feats from assumptions based solely on his species.
- Primordial God Physiology: Ion is directly identified by the narration as a Primordial God.[1]
Chapter 1111 does not identify Ion as a First-Generation Primordial God, Original Primordial God or Second-Generation Primordial God. His generational classification is currently unconfirmed.
- Divine Nature: As a God, Ion possesses a Divine existence and the supernatural characteristics inherent to Gods. His individual Divinity has not been revealed.
- Extraordinary Vitality: Ion remained alive and conscious after [[Diana]] opened a hole directly through his heart.[1]
- Severe Injury Survival: He remained capable of speaking and reacting despite catastrophic damage.
- Pain Tolerance: Ion continued responding even after Diana subsequently stabbed a Cursed Dagger into one of his eyes.[1]
The scene does not show Ion regenerating either injury, so a specific Regeneration level should not be assigned from this feat alone.
- Pantheon Leadership: Ion was explicitly the Leader of his planet's Pantheon.[1]
- Divine Authority: His position placed him at the head of the local Divine hierarchy.
- Civilizational Influence: His Pantheon formed part of the supernatural leadership of the planet before its conquest.
- Champion Status: Diana previously relied upon Ion as her Champion before replacing him with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[1]

### Relationships

#### Diana
[[Diana]] was Ion's greatest and long-time confidant.[1]
Ion trusted her to such a degree that even after she pierced his heart, he assumed she was being controlled rather than acting voluntarily.
Diana destroyed that assumption by directly denying any outside control and abandoning him as her Champion.
She then stabbed him through one eye and attempted to kill him alongside Diane before Rose stopped them.[1]

#### Diane
Diane was the Negative World Tree paired with [[Diana]] on Ion's planet.
The exact personal relationship between Ion and Diane is not described, but Diane joined Diana in abandoning the former order and was willing to participate in Ion's execution.[1]

#### Victor Elderblood
Ion has not been shown directly interacting with [[Victor Elderblood]].
Victor nevertheless became indirectly responsible for the collapse of Ion's former relationship with his [[World Trees]]. Energy released by Victor reached [[Diana]] and Diane, causing the psychological change that led them to abandon their former world order.
Diana explicitly replaced Ion with Victor as her Champion.[1]
Rose also chose to keep Ion alive because she believed a captured Primordial God could be useful to Victor.
