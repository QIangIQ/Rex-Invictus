**[[Tyche]]** is the Greek goddess of fortune and chance. Her Roman counterpart is [[Fortuna]].

---

## Greek History (IRL)

[[Hesiod]] lists her among the Oceanids, daughters of [[Oceanus]] and [[Tethys]]. Other poets make her a daughter of [[Zeus]]. From the Hellenistic age every city worshiped its own Tyche, shown wearing a mural crown of city walls and holding a cornucopia, a rudder or a wheel. The Tyche of Antioch by Eutychides was the best-known statue type.

## PJO Canon

After [[The Last Olympian]] she is one of the minor gods given a cabin at [[Camp Half-Blood]].

Tyche Elderblood, also known as [[Fortuna]], is an ancient Greek Goddess and current True Dragon Goddess who governs Luck and Fortune. Her evolved Concept is repeatedly associated with Happiness, probability, possible outcomes and the manner in which favorable possibilities become Reality. She is the daughter of the [[Titans]] [[Oceanus]] and [[Tethys]], the wife of [[Victor Elderblood]], and the mother of [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]].[1][2]
Tyche originally lived as a comparatively unimportant Minor Goddess within the [[Greek Pantheon]]. Unlike visually destructive authorities such as Lightning, War or the Sea, the effects of Luck could easily be mistaken for coincidence, and [[Olympus]] never developed her Concept to its theoretical potential. Tyche herself also deliberately remained distant from [[Zeus]] and the political center of Olympus, correctly judging that remaining close to him would only drag her into his endless problems.[1]
After entering Victor Elderblood's faction, her circumstances changed completely. Victor immediately recognized that even a relatively weak authority over Luck could influence virtually every uncertain process, including experimentation, research, evolution, warfare and political development. He consequently invested heavily in Tyche, supplied her with Faith and resources, instructed her to focus upon her Divinity and assigned multiple Shadow Demons to permanently protect her.[3]
Across the following eras, Tyche evolved from a Minor Goddess into one of the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s most valuable Divine beings. Her Luck developed into direct Probability and Outcome Manipulation, her passive fortune became capable of altering Reality to prevent harm, and her body began gradually merging with the Concept she represented.[1][4]
By Chapter 1174, Tyche had become a True Dragon Goddess. Her transformation granted her the characteristic Dragon horns of Victor's wives and transformed her already unusual golden eyes into golden Dragon eyes. Her Luck increased dramatically enough that she was stated to have reached the same general level as Kali, while remaining only one step away from completely becoming one with her own Concept.[2]

### Physical Appearance
Tyche is an exceptionally beautiful woman approximately 180 cm tall with pale skin, an evenly proportioned feminine figure and extremely long brown hair reaching the floor. She generally favors immaculate white clothing, giving her an appearance closer to a personification of purity than the stereotypical image of a Goddess of Fortune. Her composed and gentle presence is frequently compared to the warmth associated with [[Hestia]] Elderblood.[1]
Before becoming a True Dragon, Tyche's most distinctive physical characteristic was a pair of brilliant golden eyes. These eyes developed as a mutation caused by her gradual Conceptual assimilation rather than being the original source of her Divinity.[4]
Tyche normally concealed her eyes beneath a white supernatural blindfold created by [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]] and [[Aline Elderblood Valefar]]. Her probability perception had developed to the point that looking normally at the world could expose her to enormous quantities of branching possibilities simultaneously, creating severe mental strain. Tyche later revealed that the eyes could also perceive another person's happiness or fortune and alter it through thought, making accidental activation another reason she preferred restraining them.[1][4]
After her transformation into a True Dragon, Tyche developed the physical traits characteristic of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Dragon wives, including a pair of Dragon horns and golden Dragon eyes.[2]
During the Elderblood family banquet, Victor temporarily transformed Tyche's clothing into a gown formed from the light of white stars.[5]

### Personality
Tyche is calm, intelligent, mature and highly perceptive. Her demeanor often resembles [[Hestia]] Elderblood, giving others the impression of a gentle older sister or mother. Beneath this composure, however, Tyche is extremely cautious and prefers evaluating risks before committing herself to potentially dangerous situations.[1]
This caution was already present during her life in [[Olympus]]. Tyche understood that becoming deeply involved with [[Zeus]] would expose her to his political, romantic and Divine disasters, so she intentionally remained on the outskirts of the Pantheon. Unlike many Gods who sought greater prestige around Zeus, Tyche considered obscurity preferable to becoming another victim of his behavior. Her judgment ultimately helped her survive Olympus' collapse.[1]
Tyche is capable of waiting centuries or millennia for an advantageous opportunity instead of recklessly acting simply because she possesses power. Her supernatural Luck further reinforces this behavior, allowing her to determine whether potential courses of action are likely to ultimately benefit or harm her.[4]
She possesses considerable resentment toward Zeus, his brothers and several members of the old Olympian royal family because of what happened to her parents and many of her sisters. While she previously lacked the strength necessary to retaliate, her later growth caused her to openly state that she would volunteer to execute Zeus herself if he were still alive.[1]
Tyche is deeply grateful toward [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Olympus treated her as a relatively unimportant Minor Goddess, while Victor immediately understood how strategically valuable Luck could become and deliberately gave her the resources necessary to develop. She considers him a substantially superior ruler and recognizes that his investment allowed her to reach heights she would never have achieved under Zeus.[3][1]
Despite her intelligence and confidence in most matters, Tyche is remarkably shy romantically. She remained attracted to Victor for an extended period without approaching him directly, partly because his possessive Dragon Goddesses intimidated her. Even after being openly accepted as his future wife, relatively minor compliments from Victor could leave her visibly embarrassed.[1][4]
Tyche also has a strong appreciation for quiet, peaceful and natural environments. When Victor created a private pocket Dimension for her, she spent approximately seven hours exploring its forests, animals, Fairy Spirits, waterfalls and sea before remembering that Victor himself was waiting for her.[4]
Motherhood revealed a more visibly protective and exasperated side of Tyche. Unlike several of Victor's older wives, who had already become accustomed to raising extraordinarily durable Dragon children, Tyche was horrified when Victor casually threw Moira into the stratosphere for entertainment. She also quickly discovered how difficult disciplining their daughter could become when Moira simply refused her instructions and climbed back onto Victor instead.[2]

### Background
Tyche was born to [[Oceanus]] and [[Tethys]], two members of the ancient Titan generation of the Greek Divine lineage. Despite her ancestry, Tyche belonged to a later generation of Greek Gods and spent most of her ancient existence in a relatively insignificant political position.
Her Divinity concerned Luck and Fortune. Unlike powers whose supernatural effects were immediately obvious, the influence of Luck could manifest through apparently natural events: a successful experiment, a coincidental meeting, an unlikely escape or an enemy making precisely the wrong decision.
This subtlety contributed to [[Olympus]] underestimating her. Neither the Pantheon nor Tyche herself developed the Concept anywhere near its ultimate potential during her early life.[1]
Tyche instead remained deliberately removed from [[Zeus]]' inner circle and concentrated primarily upon remaining outside his problems. The decision eventually saved her when the [[Greek Pantheon]] collapsed.
After entering [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s civilization, Tyche obtained protection, Faith, resources and enormous amounts of Time. Her once-subtle Divinity gradually developed into a power capable of selecting probabilities, influencing Reality and transforming Tyche herself into something approaching a living embodiment of her Concept.

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
When the [[Greek Pantheon]] deteriorated during the conflicts involving [[Typhon]] and [[Zeus]], [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood evacuated numerous Greek Goddesses who possessed no reason to die defending [[Olympus]]. Tyche was among the Goddesses who survived and eventually entered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s protection.[6][7]
Approximately fifty Greek Goddesses ultimately reached [[Nightingale]]. Tyche initially appeared relatively unremarkable compared with several more famous members of Olympus, but Victor soon recognized the exceptional potential of her Divinity.
By Chapter 630, Victor was already interested in eventually obtaining Tyche's Blessing. He nevertheless refused to simply demand it and allowed trust between them to develop naturally.[8]

#### King Among Kings
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later began deliberately investing in Tyche's development. Rather than attempting to transform a Goddess of Luck into a conventional frontline fighter, he ordered her to concentrate upon strengthening and understanding her Divinity.[3]
Tyche received security normally reserved for highly important strategic assets. Multiple Shadow Demons were permanently assigned to her protection, demonstrating that Victor regarded her authority as far more valuable than her original status as a Minor Goddess suggested.[3]
The usefulness of this decision became evident during Victor's research involving [[The Tower of Nightmares]]. Victor required a system capable of identifying particular Souls and automatically reacting to them without his direct intervention. The probability of the mechanism working exactly as intended was extremely poor, yet Tyche's Luck influenced the experiment strongly enough for the required result to occur.[3]
Victor could not completely explain why it had succeeded. That uncertainty became part of Tyche's value: Luck did not need to create an entirely new phenomenon when it could instead cause the extremely improbable successful possibility to become the Reality that occurred.
Victor also determined that Tyche's Divine Luck did not appear to function like forms of fortune that produce a beneficial result by transferring equivalent misfortune onto unrelated people.[3]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Tyche eventually granted [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] her Complete Blessing of Luck.[9][10]
Victor came to consider investing in Tyche one of his most successful long-term decisions. Looking back upon his life, he recognized that effort and potential alone could not completely explain the circumstances that repeatedly placed exceptional opportunities in his path. His meetings with beings such as [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], [[Roxanne Elderblood]] and [[Zaladrac Zeovnur Elderblood]] each opened evolutionary paths that talent alone could not guarantee.[9][4]
Victor concluded that Tyche's Luck primarily operated through probability combined with subtle Reality distortion. A possibility did not need to be naturally likely; it merely needed to exist for Luck to dramatically increase the chance that it became the realized result.[9]
Tyche's authority continued growing through Faith. As more beings worshipped or supported her, the density and influence of her Concept increased accordingly.[10]
By this period, Tyche had become completely loyal to Victor and willingly entrusted him with the full version of her Blessing rather than merely a temporary Divine favor.[10]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
During the accelerated Imperial Era, Tyche became one of the most strategically important Goddesses within the [[Elderblood Empire]]. She lived within the immense Greek Divine territory administered by [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood and the surviving Greek Goddesses, possessing her own Temple and access to an enormous source of Faith.[1]
Her Divinity had evolved to the point that Tyche could perceive different possible outcomes simply by looking at an event and then influence which possibility would become Reality. Her passive fortune had also become so developed that Reality itself was described as behaving as though it did not want to harm her.[1]
This development created a serious drawback. Without restraint, Tyche continuously perceived probability information surrounding everything she observed. The constant influx threatened to overwhelm her mind, so [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]] and [[Aline Elderblood Valefar]] created a limiter for her. Tyche personally requested that the device take the form of a blindfold because she considered it more charming than the alternatives.[1]
Around the same period, Tyche revealed more of her ancient history to [[Violet Elderblood Snow]]. She explained that she had intentionally avoided [[Zeus]] throughout most of her life and deeply hated him, his brothers and numerous members of his family because of what had happened to [[Oceanus]], [[Tethys]] and many of Tyche's sisters.[1]
Violet's true purpose was determining why Tyche had not yet joined [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family. Tyche admitted that she respected Victor greatly and was attracted to him, but most of their previous interactions had concerned Imperial or Divine matters. She had also avoided potential relationships with other men because her Luck consistently indicated that choosing someone else would eventually create problems.[1]
Tyche's greatest hesitation was Victor's wives. Although she could see that they were genuinely happy, she also understood that several were extraordinarily possessive True Dragon Goddesses. She therefore remained passive rather than attempting to approach Victor herself.[1]
Victor secretly observed part of the conversation. At this stage Tyche was described as the closest known native approximation to a hypothetical Primordial Being of Good Fortune, but she had not yet reached the existential state of a true Primordial Being. An illustrative analogy placed Tyche below an equally powerful True Dragon Goddess and substantially below genuine Primordial Beings; the chapter explicitly stated that these numerical examples were not literal rankings.[11]
Before receiving more sensitive information regarding Victor's family, Tyche signed a secrecy agreement containing Eldritch language she could not safely examine directly.[11]
Violet eventually proposed that Tyche enter Victor's family. Tyche was receptive, but Victor rejected treating her like a strategic resource to be acquired simply because her power was useful. Instead, he proposed that the two spend Time together personally and determine whether a genuine relationship developed.[12]
By Chapter 1169, Tyche attended an enormous Elderblood family gathering. Victor's daughters immediately examined her with their violet Dragon eyes, leaving Tyche uncomfortable until Victor instructed them to stop intimidating the woman who would soon become one of their new mothers.[5]
[[Anna Elderblood]] reassured Tyche that Victor's wives already accepted her. Anna even wondered whether Tyche's own Luck had helped arrange the unusually favorable circumstances through which she was entering the family.[5]
Victor later transformed the gathering into a banquet among celestial bodies, casually converting stars, supernovae, nebulae and other astronomical phenomena into furniture and decoration. Tyche's gown was created from the radiance of white stars, and the scale of Victor's Reality Manipulation left her completely stunned.[5]
Victor and Tyche subsequently spent private Time together. Rather than appearing in his overwhelming Imperial state, Victor assumed a more familiar form resembling his Vampire appearance and deliberately attempted to make Tyche comfortable.[4]
Their conversation eventually returned to Tyche's golden eyes. She explained that they could perceive another person's happiness and change it through thought. Because she had previously activated this effect accidentally, Tyche considered the ability dangerous enough to justify wearing her limiter.[4]
Tyche further explained that the eyes were only a side effect of a larger process. She could feel the Concept of Happiness gradually permeating her body. Victor recognized this as a process similar to Kali's merger with Destruction: Tyche's existence was progressively preparing to become one with the Concept she governed.[4]
Victor believed Tyche's comparatively stable Concept would create fewer difficulties than Kali's Destruction, but Tyche's existing race was poorly suited for complete Conceptual assimilation. He therefore intended to eventually solve the problem by transforming Tyche into a True Dragon.[4]
Victor then showed Tyche the private room prepared for her within the wives' section of the Elderblood castle. The entrance was a Dragon-scale door whose effective weight under Earth-like conditions was compared to the mass of [[Pluto]], approximately 13 sextillion kilograms. Despite possessing comparatively little physical training, Tyche successfully forced the door open through raw strength.[4]
Behind the door was not an ordinary bedroom but a private pocket Dimension approximately comparable in area to South America. It contained a white Temple, forests, hundreds of Fairy Spirits, numerous animals, an enormous waterfall and a large sea filled with fish. Victor had created the environment specifically around Tyche's preference for peaceful natural locations.[4]
Tyche spent approximately seven hours exploring the Dimension before returning with a snow leopard and discovering that Victor had patiently remained waiting for her. She began to understand more personally why his wives had become so deeply attached to him.[4]
When Tyche attempted to force their relationship forward because she believed that was expected of her, Victor stopped her. He insisted that they possessed effectively unlimited Time and that their relationship should proceed naturally. Tyche eventually fell asleep while resting against him.[4]
Victor had altered the local flow of Time enough that the two could theoretically spend approximately 10,000 years together while only a moment passed externally. His primary attention remained focused upon Tyche even while a clone continued managing Imperial matters, and Victor explicitly regarded her as his future wife.[4]
Their relationship subsequently continued developing naturally for approximately two subjective years. Victor deliberately refused to rush her despite the ability to manipulate Time, while his wives quietly observed the developing relationship and expected Tyche's Luck, Aphrodite's influence and Victor's own fertility to make conception effectively certain.[13]
By Chapter 1173, Tyche's transformation into a True Dragon had become an established part of the family's plans. Anna stated that after becoming a True Dragon, Tyche would undergo the same fundamental training used to teach newly transformed Dragon Goddesses how to properly understand their bodies, powers and mindset.[14]
By Chapter 1174, the transformation had been completed. Tyche now possessed the same major Draconic characteristics as Victor's other transformed wives, including Dragon horns and golden Dragon eyes. Her authority over Luck had increased dramatically enough that she was stated to have reached the same general level as Kali, and she was now only one step away from completely becoming one with her Concept.[2]
Tyche and Victor's daughter, [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]], was also born during this period. Unlike Victor's other True Dragon daughters, whose gestation commonly lasted more than one thousand years, Moira was born after only approximately 300 years. Victor believed Reality itself had accelerated the pregnancy because of Moira's extraordinarily favorable existence.[2]
Moira inherited Tyche's Luck but combined it with Victor's Draconic and Eldritch characteristics, producing an aggressive ability known as Eldritch Luck. The comparison between mother and daughter further clarified Tyche's own passive protection: when Tyche is attacked, chance can cause the attack to miss, and if the attack cannot naturally be avoided, Reality itself can alter events so that Tyche remains unharmed.[2]
Tyche's first visible experiences as a mother quickly demonstrated that she was considerably less accustomed to Victor's parenting style than his older wives. She nearly panicked when Victor threw Moira into the stratosphere for amusement, only for their daughter to return unharmed and enthusiastically demand that he do it again. Tyche later attempted to call Moira toward her, but the girl simply refused and immediately climbed back onto Victor.[2]

### Skills & Abilities
Tyche's power is unusual because her greatest value has never depended upon conventional destructive output. Her Concept directly influences whether events occur, which possible result becomes real and how Reality itself responds to unfavorable circumstances.
Originally a Minor Goddess, Tyche gradually evolved through training, Faith and Conceptual development until she reached Primordial-class power. Her later transformation into a True Dragon dramatically increased both the existential quality of her body and the authority of her Luck.[1][2]

#### True Dragon Goddess Physiology
After joining [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family, Tyche was transformed into a True Dragon Goddess.[2]
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics: Tyche possesses physical attributes vastly beyond her former Divine body. Even before becoming a Dragon she could move a door with an effective Earth-weight comparable to [[Pluto]] despite lacking specialized physical training.[4]
- True Dragon Physiology: As a True Dragon, Tyche belongs to a race described as an embodiment of nature/Reality with exceptional compatibility toward supernatural powers and Concepts.[14]
- [[Draconic Energy]]: Tyche now possesses the supernatural Energy and biological foundation of Victor's True Dragon race.[2]
- Divine Physiology: Her transformation did not remove her Divinity; she remains a Goddess whose Draconic body carries and amplifies her Concept.[2]
- Conceptual Compatibility: Victor specifically transformed Tyche because True Dragon physiology is substantially more suited to her progressing merger with her Concept than her former Greek Divine body.[4][2]
- Enhanced Conceptual Development: Following her Dragon transformation, Tyche's Luck increased dramatically and she advanced to the same general level as Kali, leaving her only one step away from becoming completely united with her Concept.[2]
- Dragon Eyes: Tyche's pre-existing golden conceptual eyes developed into golden Dragon eyes following her transformation.[2]
- Dragon Horns: Tyche possesses the Dragon horns characteristic of Victor's transformed wives.[2]

#### Divinity — <Luck / Fortune / Happiness>
Luck is Tyche's primary Divine authority. Its later development is repeatedly described through terms such as Fortune, Good Fortune and Happiness. Chapter 1170 specifically states that the Concept of Happiness is permeating Tyche's body, while later chapters continue treating this process as the evolution of her existing authority rather than introducing an unrelated second Divinity.[1][4]

##### Luck Manipulation
- Supernatural Luck: Tyche can cause events and circumstances to develop in unusually favorable ways.[3]
- Good Fortune Inducement: Beneficial possibilities become substantially more likely around Tyche and those affected by her authority.[1]
- Misfortune Avoidance: Unfavorable or harmful possibilities can be reduced or prevented from occurring.[1]
- Opportunity Generation: Tyche's Luck can create circumstances in which otherwise improbable opportunities become accessible.[9][4]
- Low-Probability Success: Extremely unlikely but still possible favorable outcomes can become the result Reality ultimately produces.[3]
- Luck Evaluation: Tyche can consult her own Luck to determine whether a future action is likely to ultimately benefit her. She used this ability when evaluating [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s planned training and determined that it would be beneficial despite involving considerable suffering.[4]

##### Probability Manipulation
- Probability Increase: Tyche can increase the likelihood of a desired possibility.[1]
- Probability Reduction: She can make undesirable possibilities increasingly unlikely.[1]
- Probability Alteration: Events possessing extremely small natural probabilities can become realistically achievable.[3]
- Probability Favoring: Tyche's mature Concept continuously biases Reality toward favorable possibilities.[1]
- External Probability Manipulation: Her authority can influence projects, experiments and beings other than herself.[3]

##### Probability Perception
Tyche's evolved eyes allow her to perceive the possibilities surrounding events.[1]
- Possible Outcome Perception: Tyche can see different results that may emerge from an event.[1]
- Probability Analysis: She can perceive the likelihood attached to different possibilities.[1]
- Precognition: Her perception functions as a probabilistic form of future sight by revealing multiple possible futures rather than one fixed future.[1]
- Continuous Probability Awareness: Without sufficient restraint, the amount of probability information entering Tyche's perception can become mentally overwhelming.[1]

##### Outcome Manipulation
Tyche can influence which possible result becomes the Reality that actually occurs.[1]
- Outcome Selection: Tyche can favor one result among several possible conclusions.[1]
- Event Manipulation: The final outcome of an event can be altered through her authority over Fortune.[1]
- Possible Reality Actualization: A favorable possibility can be pushed into becoming the realized Reality.[1]
- Unfavorable Outcome Suppression: Harmful possible outcomes can be prevented from becoming real.[1]

##### Reality Warping
Tyche's Luck possesses a subtle but extremely powerful Reality-altering component.[9]
- Probability-Based Reality Warping: Reality can rearrange circumstances so that favorable possibilities occur.[9]
- Possible-Reality Selection: Tyche can influence which possible version of events becomes real.[1]
- Passive Reality Favoring: Reality continuously tends to operate in ways favorable to Tyche.[1]
- Reality Protection: Her mature Luck became powerful enough that Reality was described as actively refusing to harm her.[1]
- Unavoidable-Harm Negation: Chapter 1174 explains that if ordinary chance cannot make an attack miss Tyche, Reality itself can alter the situation so that she remains unharmed.[2]

##### Passive Fortune
Tyche's mature Luck does not require continuous conscious activation.
- Automatic Probability Adjustment: Favorable possibilities can be selected passively.[1]
- Automatic Misfortune Avoidance: Harmful events naturally become less likely to affect her.[1]
- Passive Good Fortune: Beneficial circumstances continuously surround her existence.[1]
- Passive Reality Favoring: Reality itself responds favorably toward Tyche.[1]
- Passive Damage Avoidance: An attack may simply miss because chance shifts in her favor.[2]
- Passive Reality Protection: If avoiding an attack through probability is impossible, Reality may change so that Tyche remains unharmed.[2]

##### Fate Manipulation
Tyche's Luck is explicitly described as an unpredictable force capable of interfering with and, when necessary, breaking Fate.[1]
- Fate Interference: Developments that appear predetermined can be disturbed by Luck.[1]
- Destiny Manipulation: Future paths can be altered by changing the probabilities leading toward them.[1]
- Predetermined Outcome Breaking: A seemingly inevitable result can potentially be displaced if another possibility still exists.[1]
- Causality Interference: The chain of circumstances leading toward a particular outcome can subtly change through supernatural fortune.[1]
This should not be interpreted as unrestricted control over all Destiny or Causality throughout Creation.

##### Golden Dragon Eyes
Tyche's golden eyes originally emerged as a mutation caused by her developing Conceptual assimilation and later became golden Dragon eyes after her True Dragon transformation.[4][2]
- Happiness Perception: Tyche can perceive the happiness or fortune surrounding another person.[4]
- Happiness Manipulation: She can alter another person's happiness merely through thought.[4]
- Fortune Perception: Her ocular ability is directly connected to her broader authority over Luck and favorable outcomes.[4]
- Probability Sight: Tyche can visually perceive branching possibilities and probability information.[1]
- Accidental Activation: Tyche has unintentionally altered other people's happiness before, motivating her to restrain her eyes.[4]
- Conceptual Mutation: The original golden eyes were explicitly identified as a physical side effect of Tyche's progressing merger with her Concept.[4]
- Draconic Evolution: Following her transformation, the eyes developed the characteristics of True Dragon eyes while retaining their golden coloration and Conceptual function.[2]

##### Conceptual Embodiment
Tyche is progressively ceasing to be merely a Goddess who uses Luck and is instead becoming increasingly indistinguishable from the Concept itself.[1][4]
- Conceptual Assimilation: Tyche's Concept is gradually permeating her physical existence.[4]
- Conceptual Physiology (Developing): Her body is changing as it prepares for complete integration with the Concept she governs.[4]
- Near-Conceptual Embodiment: Tyche was already described as approaching the condition of Happiness/Good Fortune itself before becoming a True Dragon.[1]
- Conceptual Mutation: Her golden eyes developed as a physical consequence of this process.[4]
- Primordial Evolution (In Progress): [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] recognized Tyche's transformation as comparable to the process through which beings such as Kali approach complete merger with their Concepts.[4]
- Near-Complete Conceptual Merger: After becoming a True Dragon, Tyche reached the same general level as Kali and was stated to be only one step away from becoming one with her Concept.[2]
Tyche has not yet completed this final merger and therefore should not be treated as a fully realized Primordial Entity.[2]

##### Blessing of Luck
Tyche can grant other beings access to her Luck through Divine Blessings.
[[Victor Elderblood]] possesses her Complete Blessing.[10]
Known effects include:
- Supernatural Luck[10]
- Probability Favoring[10]
- Opportunity Inducement[9]
- Favorable Circumstance Generation[9]
- Limited Reality Distortion[9]
- Passive Probability Manipulation[10]
Victor attributes numerous important opportunities throughout his development partially to the influence of Luck, including circumstances that allowed him to encounter beings and resources that opened entirely new evolutionary paths.[9][4]
The Blessing does not grant unrestricted wish fulfillment. It primarily operates by manipulating existing possibilities and the circumstances surrounding them.

##### Faith Amplification
Tyche's Divinity becomes denser and stronger as Faith directed toward her increases.[10]
By Chapter 1170, Tyche was empowered by Faith associated with trillions of beings throughout the [[Elderblood Empire]].[4]
This enormous Faith network was one of the principal reasons her physical and Conceptual capabilities became incomparable to the Minor Goddess she had once been.

#### Primordial-Class Power
Tyche's original birth as a later-generation Greek Goddess no longer accurately represents her current strength.
Before becoming a True Dragon, Chapter 1155 already treated Tyche as the closest known equivalent to a hypothetical Primordial Being of Good Fortune while emphasizing that an enormous existential difference still separated her from a genuine Primordial Being.[11]
By Chapter 1170, her Divine authority was already treated as surpassing what she once understood as the level of ordinary ancient Greek Primordial Goddesses in several respects.[4]
After becoming a True Dragon, her authority increased again. Chapter 1174 directly states that she had reached the same general level as Kali and remained only one step away from becoming one with her Concept.[2]

#### Superhuman Strength
Tyche possesses extraordinary physical strength even though physical combat historically received comparatively little of her attention.
Before becoming a Dragon, she successfully opened a door whose effective Earth-weight was stated to equal approximately the mass of [[Pluto]], around 13 sextillion kilograms.[4]
The narration specifically emphasized that:
- Tyche possessed relatively little specialized physical training;[4]
- her body was enormously empowered by Faith;[4]
- that Faith extended across trillions of beings;[4]
- and an ordinary Primordial Goddess would not have been able to reproduce the feat in the same manner.[4]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] nevertheless considered her physical conditioning inadequate by Elderblood standards and decided that she would undergo proper training. After her True Dragon transformation, Anna likewise confirmed that Tyche would receive the same fundamental Draconic training used to teach Victor's wives how to properly exploit their new bodies and powers.[4][14]

#### Strategic Utility
Tyche is one of the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s most valuable strategic specialists because Luck applies to nearly every uncertain process.
- Research Support: Tyche can increase the probability that difficult experiments succeed.[3]
- Soul Experiment Support: Her Luck helped [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] complete an extremely improbable Tower mechanism involving automatic Soul recognition.[3]
- Technological Development Support: Experimental technology can benefit from probability manipulation.[3]
- Magical Development Support: [[Magic]] and Runic experimentation can benefit from favorable probabilities.[3]
- Divine Development Support: Dangerous Divine experiments can be shifted toward more favorable possible results.[3]
- Evolution Support: Luck can influence circumstances surrounding transformations and breakthroughs.[9]
- Opportunity Generation: Tyche's Blessing can create situations offering advantages that would otherwise be extraordinarily improbable.[9]
- Civilizational Utility: The usefulness of Luck increases as the Empire undertakes greater numbers of projects across larger territories and populations.
- Strategic Risk Reduction: Unfavorable outcomes surrounding important Imperial operations can become less likely.
Victor's confidence in Tyche's strategic value became so great that after another incident involving exceptionally bad luck within the Empire, his immediate reaction was to order Natalia to increase Tyche's budget.[15]

### Equipment

#### Divinity-Suppressing Blindfold
Tyche historically wears a white supernatural blindfold created through the combined expertise of [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]] and [[Aline Elderblood Valefar]].[1]
Its known functions include:
- Divinity Suppression: Restrains the constant expression of Tyche's evolved Concept.[1]
- Probability Perception Suppression: Prevents Tyche from continuously perceiving branching probabilities surrounding everything in her field of vision.[1]
- Information Overload Prevention: Protects her mind from excessive probability information.[1]
- Happiness Manipulation Suppression: Reduces the risk that Tyche will accidentally alter another person's happiness through her eyes.[4]
- Conceptual Ocular Limiter: Restrains powers produced by her progressing Conceptual assimilation.[4]
- Removable Restraint: The blindfold suppresses Tyche's abilities rather than permanently weakening them.[1]
Velnorah originally preferred a subtler limiter such as earrings or a bracelet, but Tyche personally chose a blindfold because she believed it added charm to her appearance.[1]
After Tyche became a True Dragon, Chapter 1174 confirmed that her eyes had developed into golden Dragon eyes, but did not clarify whether she still requires the blindfold under ordinary circumstances.[2]

### Residence

#### Temple of Tyche
Tyche possesses her own Temple upon the enormous Greek Divine world administered by [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood and the surviving Greek Goddesses of the [[Elderblood Empire]].[1]
The Temple served as her principal residence before she formally entered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household.

#### Private Pocket Dimension
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later created a private dimensional residence for Tyche within the wives' section of the Elderblood castle.[4]
The ordinary-looking entrance conceals a pocket Dimension approximately comparable in area to South America.[4]
The Dimension contains:
- a peaceful paradisiacal landscape;
- a large white Temple;
- a statue of Tyche;
- extensive forests;
- hundreds of Fairy Spirits responsible for maintaining the environment;
- large populations of animals;
- a massive waterfall;
- a large sea;
- and numerous species of fish.[4]
Victor designed the Dimension around Tyche's preference for peaceful natural environments.
Despite its continental size, Victor considered it a relatively normal personal room by the standards of the Elderblood household. Several of his wives possess private rooms concealing similarly enormous internal Dimensions, while [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]]'s residence approaches planetary scale.[4]
Victor originally intended for Tyche to customize the Dimension herself after becoming a Dragon Goddess and learning to properly use Draconic systems.[4]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Tyche's husband, Emperor, benefactor and the recipient of her Complete Blessing of Luck.
Their relationship originally possessed no romantic component. Victor simply recognized the enormous strategic value of Tyche's authority and considered allowing such an important Concept to remain undeveloped wasteful. He supplied her with resources, protection, Faith and freedom despite her insignificant position within the former [[Greek Pantheon]].[3]
Tyche eventually became completely loyal to Victor and granted him her Complete Blessing.[10]
Her gratitude gradually developed into romantic attraction, but Tyche remained too intimidated by Victor's wives to initiate a relationship herself. She also acknowledged that despite admiring Victor, the two barely knew one another personally because most of their earlier encounters involved Imperial or Divine matters.[1]
[[Violet Elderblood Snow]] eventually attempted to accelerate the situation by proposing Tyche as another wife. Victor rejected treating Tyche as a strategic acquisition and instead insisted that they first spend Time together and allow a genuine personal relationship to form.[12]
By Chapter 1169, Victor openly introduced Tyche to his daughters as someone who would soon become one of their new mothers.[5]
Their first major private meeting occurred shortly afterward. Victor deliberately reduced his presence, created an enormous private Dimension for Tyche and spent Time learning about her rather than immediately pushing the relationship forward.[4]
When Tyche attempted to accelerate their intimacy because she believed it was expected, Victor stopped her and reassured her that they possessed effectively unlimited Time. He wanted her actions to emerge naturally rather than from pressure created by the expectation that she would become his wife.[4]
Victor altered Time around them so dramatically that approximately 10,000 years could theoretically pass for them while only a moment elapsed externally. He simultaneously maintained Imperial responsibilities through another body while focusing his primary attention upon Tyche.[4]
Approximately two subjective years later, their relationship had progressed sufficiently for the Elderblood wives to expect Tyche to conceive Victor's child.[13]
Tyche subsequently became one of Victor's wives, underwent transformation into a True Dragon Goddess and gave birth to their daughter [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]].[2]
Victor's transformation of Tyche also directly accelerated her Conceptual evolution. Her previous Greek Divine body had been one of the principal obstacles preventing complete integration with Luck/Happiness, while True Dragon physiology brought her to the same general level as Kali and left her only one step from complete Conceptual merger.[4][2]

#### Moira Eutychia Elderblood
[[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]] is Tyche and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughter and, at the time of her birth, the youngest child of the Elderblood family.[2]
Moira inherited the foundation of Tyche's Luck together with Victor's Draconic and Eldritch characteristics. The combination produced the unique Divinity I Am the Privileged One and the power known as Eldritch Luck.[2]
Where Tyche's Luck generally protects her by causing danger to fail or Reality to prevent unavoidable harm, Moira's version is considerably more aggressive. Reality can directly eliminate threats, create Eldritch beings to consume attackers and spontaneously fulfill Moira's desires.[2]
Tyche is noticeably more cautious with Moira than Victor. She nearly panicked when Victor casually threw their daughter into the stratosphere and became visibly frustrated when Moira ignored her command to come over and instead returned to Victor's shoulders.[2]
Ruby reassured Tyche that she would eventually become accustomed to the tendency of Victor's daughters to become extraordinarily attached to their father.[2]

#### Violet Elderblood Snow
[[Violet Elderblood Snow]] was primarily responsible for forcing Tyche and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s unresolved attraction into the open.
Violet considered Tyche remaining outside the Elderblood family increasingly absurd because Luck had become one of the Empire's most strategically important authorities. She also viewed such a powerful independent ability as a possible security vulnerability.[1]
Tyche and Victor both criticized Violet when her attempts to arrange the relationship became too aggressive.[12]
Despite this, Violet's interference ultimately caused Victor and Tyche to begin interacting personally, eventually leading to Tyche becoming part of the family.

#### Anna Elderblood
[[Anna Elderblood]] reassured Tyche during the Elderblood family banquet that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wives accepted her and considered her future integration into the family natural.[5]
Anna also questioned whether Tyche's own Luck had influenced the unusually favorable circumstances surrounding her relationship with Victor.
Later, Anna was among the wives observing Tyche and Victor's developing relationship and predicted that Tyche's Luck made conception virtually certain.[13]
Anna also stated that Tyche would receive proper fundamental Draconic training following her True Dragon transformation so that she could learn to fully utilize her new race rather than relying upon habits developed as an ordinary Goddess.[14]

#### Aphrodite Elderblood
[[Aphrodite]] Elderblood was one of the primary reasons Tyche survived the destruction of [[Olympus]].
Aphrodite helped evacuate Tyche and numerous other Greek Goddesses before the Pantheon's collapse and later brought them beneath [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s protection.[6][7]
Tyche eventually settled within the immense Greek Divine territory administered by Aphrodite and the surviving Goddesses.
Aphrodite's fertility-related authority was also one of the reasons Victor's wives considered Tyche's eventual pregnancy effectively guaranteed once she and Victor decided to have a child.[13]

#### Hestia Elderblood
Tyche shares a comfortable relationship with [[Hestia]] Elderblood. Her mature and reassuring presence is repeatedly compared to Hestia's.[1]
Tyche also regularly experienced a sense of peace when visiting Hestia and later used that feeling as the closest comparison for the comfort she experienced while resting against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[4]

#### Zeus
[[Zeus]] was Tyche's former God-King and one of the beings she deliberately avoided throughout most of her ancient life.
Tyche considered his behavior repulsive and correctly concluded that remaining close to him would inevitably bring trouble. Her resentment later developed into outright hatred because of what Zeus, his brothers and several members of his family had done to Tyche's parents and sisters.[1]
After obtaining her current strength, Tyche stated that she would willingly volunteer to execute Zeus herself if he were still alive.

#### Oceanus and Tethys
[[Oceanus]] and [[Tethys]] are Tyche's parents and belong to the ancient Titan generation of Greek Gods.[1]
Tyche's hatred toward the old Olympian ruling family is strongly connected to the suffering experienced by her parents and many of her sisters.[1]
By Chapter 1170, Tyche had grown powerful enough to reflect that many events of the past could have unfolded completely differently had she possessed her modern authority at the time.[4]
