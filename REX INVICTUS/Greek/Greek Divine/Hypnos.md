### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 467
Hypnos is a Second-Generation Primordial God, the son of Nyx Elderblood and [[Erebus]], brother of [[Thanatos]] and [[Nemesis]], and the Divine personification of Sleep within the former [[Greek Pantheon]]. He spent most of his known history as one of the principal Divine Beings residing within the [[Greek Underworld]], first under [[Hades]] and later under [[Persephone Elderblood]] after she became the legitimate Ruler of the realm.[2][3]
Although Hypnos belongs to the broader Primordial God class, he is a Second-Generation Primordial rather than one of the original foundational Greek Primordials such as Nyx, Erebus, [[Gaia]] or [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]]. The difference between those generations can be enormous; beings such as Hypnos and Thanatos are explicitly placed within the Primordial range, but the original [[Primordial Gods]] possess vastly deeper development and considerably greater overall Power.[4]
Hypnos is also notable for his unusual connection with his son [[Morpheus]], the Personification of Dreams. After Morpheus awakened his own Divinity, Hypnos unexpectedly developed a highly reliable instinct resembling a sixth sense capable of warning him when disastrous events were approaching. Their relationship is considered abnormal even by their Divine relatives; Morpheus resembles Hypnos so closely in nature that Thanatos and Nemesis described them more like two equivalent Beings or twins than a conventional father and son.[1]
Following the destruction of the old Greek Pantheon and the creation of [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]], Hypnos became one of the surviving Greek Gods incorporated beneath [[Victor Elderblood]]'s authority.

### Physical Appearance
Hypnos' exact physical appearance has not been described in sufficient detail within the verified novel chapters to provide a reliable canonical profile.
Unlike [[Nemesis]], whose appearance is directly described during Chapter 778, Hypnos is primarily identified through his identity, Divinity and family relationships rather than detailed physical narration. Therefore, specific hair color, eye color, skin color, height or clothing should not be treated as canonical unless explicitly established in a later chapter.

### Personality
Hypnos is cautious, pragmatic and significantly less eager for direct confrontation than several of his relatives. When [[Persephone Elderblood]] ordered him, [[Thanatos]] and [[Nemesis]] to investigate [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], Nyx Elderblood, [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood and Morgana, Hypnos immediately recognized the absurdity of attempting to kill them. He openly pointed out that their opponents included a God Slayer, an Original Primordial Goddess, the Goddess of Beauty and a former Demon General, concluding that a direct battle was effectively impossible.[1]
This cautious attitude should not be mistaken for cowardice. Hypnos responds quickly when the [[Greek Underworld]] itself is endangered and is willing to mobilize troops, act upon [[Persephone]]'s orders and enter large-scale Divine conflicts when necessary.[2][3][5]
Hypnos appears deeply accustomed to obeying the hierarchy of the Underworld. During [[Hades]]' absence he readily accepted Persephone's authority, consistently addressed her as his Queen and performed whatever task she assigned him. When [[Cerberus]] disappeared, Hypnos immediately reported the incident rather than attempting to hide or independently resolve a problem capable of destabilizing the entire Underworld.[2]
He also respects Thanatos, who frequently acts as the practical commander among Persephone's Divine servants. Hypnos accepts his brother's instructions in combat and administrative emergencies, although he is willing to question Thanatos when he believes a plan is unrealistic or dangerous.[3][1][5]
Hypnos' relationship with his mother is more complicated. He clearly respects the tremendous gap between himself and Nyx and knows how dangerous confronting her would be. Unlike Nemesis, however, Hypnos does not display the same explosive resentment toward Nyx for her absence. During Chapter 778, his primary concern is not familial anger but the impossibility of fighting her and the danger posed by the group traveling beside her.[1]
He also possesses a somewhat dry attitude toward his unusual prophetic instincts. When his warning about Thanatos proved correct almost immediately, Thanatos complained that he sometimes hated Hypnos' instincts. Hypnos merely replied that he heard that frequently.[1]

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Hypnos first appeared during the growing crisis inside the [[Greek Underworld]]. [[Persephone Elderblood]] and [[Thanatos]] were attempting to maintain the realm's Soul-processing system while [[Hades]] was absent and numerous supernatural events were destabilizing the [[Greek Pantheon]].[2]
Hypnos abruptly entered [[Persephone]]'s castle and announced that [[Cerberus]] had disappeared. Thanatos immediately reprimanded him for entering so abruptly, but Hypnos apologized and insisted that the situation was urgent.[2]
Cerberus' disappearance represented an existential problem for the Underworld because he guarded its Gates. Without him, Souls could potentially escape while outside Beings could enter the realm freely. Thanatos warned that if the Underworld ceased operating correctly, Greek death itself could become dysfunctional, leaving Souls wandering without proper destination or processing.[2]
Persephone consequently reorganized the Underworld's defenses. Thanatos was temporarily assigned to replace Cerberus as guardian of the Gates, while Hypnos was ordered to travel to [[Olympus|Mount Olympus]] and personally inform [[Zeus]] of the emergency.[2]
Hypnos immediately accepted the order.

#### Demonic War
The crisis eventually escalated when the ancient prisoners of [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] began escaping.
The [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] forces attempted to restrain the disaster. [[Persephone Elderblood]], [[Thanatos]], Hypnos, Empusa and even the Underworld's torturers were shown attempting to contain the damage, but their combined efforts were insufficient to completely stop the entities emerging from Tartarus.[6]
Among the returning Beings were the Hecatonchires and other ancient creatures associated with the primordial conflicts of Greek mythology. Their release threatened not merely [[Persephone]]'s territory but the entire political stability of [[Olympus|Mount Olympus]], potentially creating another Greek Divine civil war.[6]
The conflict demonstrates the limitations of Hypnos' status as a Second-Generation Primordial God. Although he is vastly beyond ordinary Gods, the entities and forces associated with the deepest primordial layers of the Underworld could still overwhelm the defenses he helped maintain.

#### King Among Kings
After [[Persephone Elderblood]] successfully passed the trial required to become the legitimate Ruler of the [[Greek Underworld]], her authority and Power increased enormously. Her ascension shook the entire realm and immediately alerted Hypnos and [[Thanatos]].[3]
When the brothers approached [[Persephone]]'s chamber, they discovered that Nyx Elderblood had enclosed the area behind a barrier formed from her Divine authority over Concealment. Hypnos immediately recognized his mother's Power and realized something serious had occurred.[3]
Neither Hypnos nor Thanatos could penetrate Nyx's barrier. Hypnos became increasingly frustrated with remaining inactive and insisted that they needed to do something even though neither brother possessed an obvious method of intervening. Thanatos subsequently ordered the mobilization of Underworld forces and instructed Hypnos to help secure Persephone's castle.[3]
Hypnos also prevented Thanatos from wasting time asking [[Hecate]] to observe Nyx's actions through prophecy, reminding him that their mother was the Mother of Concealment and could hide herself from such methods.[3]
This exchange demonstrates that Hypnos possesses significant practical knowledge regarding his mother's Divine authorities and the limitations of other Gods when attempting to observe her.

##### Scathach's Entry into the Underworld
Much later, [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood, Morgana and Nyx entered the [[Greek Underworld]] for reasons [[Persephone]] did not initially understand.
Persephone dispatched [[Thanatos]], Hypnos and [[Nemesis]] to observe the group, determine its objective and deal with the situation according to Thanatos' judgment.[1]
Hypnos immediately questioned how they were supposed to confront such a dangerous combination of Beings. He pointed out that attacking them would mean fighting a God Slayer, an Original Primordial Goddess, the Goddess of Beauty and a former high-ranking Demon.[1]
Thanatos agreed that direct battle was unnecessary and decided that their priority should be learning why the group had entered the Underworld.
During the discussion, Thanatos claimed that as the embodiment of Death he did not experience fear. Hypnos immediately experienced an intense bad feeling.[1]
Thanatos and Nemesis took the reaction seriously because Hypnos' instincts had developed an extraordinary reputation among his siblings. Since [[Morpheus]] awakened the Divinity of Dreams, Hypnos had possessed a mysterious sixth sense that occasionally warned him of approaching disasters.[1]
Hypnos explained that he sensed Thanatos would soon bitterly regret what he had just said and that Hypnos himself would somehow become involved.
The warning proved almost immediately correct.
[[Scathach]] detected the hidden Gods and launched her spear with such speed that Thanatos had no time to react. Hypnos attempted to warn him, but the spear pierced Thanatos' skull before his sentence could even be completed.[1]
Thanatos survived because his Divine existence as the embodiment of Death allowed his body to effectively reset after being destroyed by the attack. Upon recovering, he complained about Hypnos' instincts, while Hypnos calmly noted that people complained about them frequently.[1]
Nyx then appeared directly among her children.
Rather than fight them, she dismissed the three Gods like troublesome children, covered Thanatos, Hypnos and Nemesis with a veil resembling the night sky and instantly transported all three back to Persephone's castle.[1]
The encounter reinforced the enormous difference between Hypnos' Second-Generation Primordial status and Nyx's existence as an Original Primordial Goddess.

##### Fall of Olympus
When the final war over the [[Greek Pantheon]] erupted, Hypnos fought as part of [[Persephone]]'s [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] forces rather than beside the Olympians.
Persephone ordered her army to kill the Greek Gods supporting [[Olympus]] while avoiding [[Victor Elderblood]] and the forces of the Demon King. [[Thanatos]] immediately began distributing responsibilities among their siblings and subordinates.[5]
[[Nemesis]] and the Furies were sent directly into battle. Thanatos then turned toward Hypnos and told him that he already knew what his task was.
Hypnos immediately agreed and called [[Morpheus]] to accompany him.[5]
The scene establishes that Hypnos and Morpheus were intended to operate together during the Underworld's attack upon Olympus, although the precise technique or battlefield assignment they subsequently performed is not individually explained in sufficient detail to safely invent additional combat feats.
Following the destruction of the old Greek Pantheon, Hypnos survived and later became incorporated into Victor Elderblood's [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]], alongside numerous other surviving Greek deities.

### Skills & Abilities
Hypnos is a Second-Generation Primordial God whose principal Divine authority is Sleep. Despite belonging to the Primordial class, his individual abilities remain less extensively demonstrated than those of his brother [[Thanatos]] or his parents Nyx and [[Erebus]].

#### Primordial God Physiology
- Second-Generation Primordial God: Hypnos belongs to the second major generation of Greek [[Primordial Gods]], being the son of Nyx Elderblood and [[Erebus]].[4]
- Primordial [[Divine Energy]]: As a Primordial God, Hypnos possesses Divine Energy and a Soul far beyond ordinary Gods.
- Enhanced Physical Attributes: His Primordial physiology grants supernatural strength, speed, durability, reflexes and stamina well above mortal supernatural races.
- Divine Longevity: Hypnos has existed for an enormous period of time and does not naturally die through ordinary aging.
- Divine Regeneration: Primordial Gods possess extremely advanced regenerative capabilities compared with normal Divine Beings.
- Divine Senses: Hypnos possesses senses appropriate to a high-ranking God and can perceive supernatural disturbances within the [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]].
- Conceptual Existence: Hypnos' existence is deeply linked with his principal Divinity of Sleep. However, like other Primordial Gods, he should not automatically be treated as the absolute Universal source of the Concept itself; Primordial Gods personify and wield Concepts within their Divine structure without necessarily becoming the true cosmic origin of that phenomenon.

#### Divinity of Sleep
- Sleep Divinity: Hypnos possesses the Major Divinity and Divine Concept associated with Sleep. He is repeatedly identified as the Primordial God and personification of Sleep.
- Sleep Manipulation: Hypnos possesses supernatural authority over the state of sleep and can influence sleeping Beings through his Divinity.
- Sleep Inducement: His conceptual authority allows him to cause sleep through supernatural means.
- Sleep Suppression: By the inverse application of his authority, Hypnos can interfere with or deny ordinary sleep.
- Divine Sleep Authority: As a Primordial God rather than an ordinary minor deity of sleep, Hypnos' Power functions on the level of Divine conceptual authority rather than conventional biological sedation.
The novel has not yet demonstrated the complete upper limit of Hypnos' Sleep Divinity against [[Primordial Gods]], Dragons, Progenitors or Beings possessing equivalent conceptual resistance. Claims of absolute or unavoidable sleep should therefore be avoided unless later chapters establish them.

#### Sixth Sense
Hypnos possesses an unusual supernatural intuition that developed after [[Morpheus]] awakened as the Personification of Dreams.[1]
- Danger Premonition: Hypnos can experience powerful instinctive warnings when dangerous or disastrous events are approaching.
- Future Misfortune Detection: His instincts can indicate that a specific person is about to experience something negative even when Hypnos does not know exactly what will occur.[1]
- Self-Involvement Detection: During Chapter 778, Hypnos sensed both that [[Thanatos]] would regret his words and that Hypnos himself would become involved in the resulting event.[1]
- Exceptional Accuracy: Thanatos and [[Nemesis]] regarded these instincts seriously because previous experience had demonstrated their reliability.[1]
- Limited Precognition: The ability does not provide Hypnos with complete visions of the future. It behaves more like an instinctive warning or prophetic sense rather than conventional future sight.

#### Connection to Morpheus
Hypnos possesses an unexplained supernatural relationship with [[Morpheus]] that extends beyond their ordinary father-son relationship.[1]
- Shared Dream-Related Trait: Hypnos' sixth sense emerged only after Morpheus awakened the Divinity of Dreams.
- Mirrored Existence: Morpheus is described as effectively a copy of Hypnos, comparable to a long-lost twin rather than an ordinary child.[1]
- Comparable Instinctive Ability: Morpheus possesses the same unusual sixth-sense characteristic at an even more advanced level.[1]
- Unexplained Divine Link: Even [[Thanatos]] and [[Nemesis]] considered the relationship unusual because Divine abilities are normally individual and should not simply transfer between parent and child.[1]
The precise metaphysical reason for this connection has not yet been explained.

#### Underworld Knowledge
- [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] Awareness: Hypnos understands the functions of the Greek Underworld sufficiently well to recognize threats to its Soul-processing system and defensive structure.[2]
- Primordial Authority Knowledge: He possesses extensive knowledge of the abilities and limitations of his mother Nyx, particularly her Concealment authority.[3]
- Military Coordination: Hypnos can participate in mobilizing Underworld forces and carrying out [[Persephone]]'s military orders.[3][5]

### Power and Rank
Hypnos' classification requires an important distinction between Divine birth class and actual combat capability.
He is unquestionably a Second-Generation Primordial God. This places him above ordinary Gods by nature and Divine development.[4]
However:
Second-Generation Primordial God ≠ Original Primordial God
The Author explicitly treats the gap between Gods such as Hypnos and [[Thanatos]] and ancient beings such as Nyx, [[Erebus]], [[Gaia]] and [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] as enormous.[4]
Hypnos himself recognizes this difference. When confronted with the possibility of fighting Nyx in Chapter 778, he considers the task effectively impossible rather than assuming shared Primordial classification makes them comparable.[1]
His article should therefore not describe him merely as a weak God, but neither should it scale him automatically to the level of his Original Primordial parents.

### Relationships

#### Nyx Elderblood
Nyx Elderblood is Hypnos' mother and one of the Original Greek [[Primordial Gods]].
Hypnos possesses great respect for her Power and understands her abilities extremely well. He immediately recognized Nyx's barrier around [[Persephone]]'s chamber and reminded [[Thanatos]] that conventional prophecy was unlikely to bypass the Mother of Concealment.[3]
During Chapter 778, Hypnos considered defeating Nyx essentially impossible even before accounting for [[Scathach]], [[Aphrodite]] and Morgana accompanying her.[1]
Unlike [[Nemesis]], he does not openly rage at Nyx for neglecting her children, although the wider family had once been significantly closer before Nyx and [[Erebus]]' relationship collapsed.[1]

#### Erebus
[[Erebus]] is Hypnos' father.
Hypnos and [[Thanatos]] both recognize the enormous superiority of their parents. During [[Persephone]]'s confrontation with Nyx, Thanatos specifically stated that only Erebus among those immediately available could cross Nyx's concealment barrier.[3]
The relationship received comparatively little direct emotional exploration before Erebus' death.

#### Thanatos
[[Thanatos]] is Hypnos' brother and frequent companion within the [[Greek Underworld]].
The two regularly operate together under [[Persephone]]. Thanatos generally assumes the more authoritative and combat-oriented role, while Hypnos provides observations, warnings and support.[2][3][1]
Hypnos respects Thanatos but is willing to criticize unrealistic decisions. He repeatedly questioned how they were supposed to fight Nyx's group during Chapter 778 and attempted to warn Thanatos moments before [[Scathach]]'s spear struck him.[1]
Their familiarity is considerable enough that Thanatos complains openly about Hypnos' prophetic instincts, while Hypnos responds with casual resignation.[1]

#### Nemesis
[[Nemesis]] is Hypnos' sister.
The siblings operated together while investigating [[Scathach]]'s group. Nemesis takes Hypnos' sixth sense seriously and immediately believed him when he warned that [[Thanatos]] was about to suffer because the family had already learned that his instincts were unusually reliable.[1]

#### Persephone Elderblood
[[Persephone Elderblood]] became Hypnos' Queen and primary superior after [[Hades]] disappeared.
Hypnos obeys [[Persephone]] willingly and refers to her as his Queen. When [[Cerberus]] disappeared, he reported the problem directly to her and immediately accepted her command to warn [[Olympus]].[2]
After Persephone became the legitimate Ruler of the [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]], Hypnos continued serving her and helped secure her castle during the confrontation with Nyx.[3]
He later participated in Persephone's military operations during the final war against Olympus.[5]

#### Morpheus
[[Morpheus]] is one of Hypnos' more than one thousand children and the Personification of Dreams.[1]
Their relationship is one of Hypnos' most unusual characteristics. Morpheus is described as essentially a copy of his father, to the point that their relatives compare them more to twins or equal Entities than father and son.[1]
Morpheus also does not treat Hypnos like an ordinary parent, instead interacting with him more as an equal. Neither Hypnos nor the rest of the family understands why their Divine natures are so closely connected.[1]
During the final war against [[Olympus]], Hypnos specifically chose Morpheus to accompany him when carrying out his battlefield assignment.[5]

#### Victor Elderblood
Hypnos originally had no meaningful personal relationship with [[Victor Elderblood]].
Following the destruction of the old [[Greek Pantheon]], surviving Greek Gods who remained under [[Persephone]]'s authority were eventually absorbed into Victor's expanding Divine structure. Hypnos consequently became one of the Gods subordinate to the ruler of the [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]] rather than remaining part of the former Olympian system.

- The connection between Hypnos and Morpheus is considered abnormal even by other Gods because Divine abilities are normally individual rather than automatically shared between relatives.[1]

- The precise physical appearance of Hypnos has not yet been sufficiently described in the verified novel text, so the AI artwork should not be treated as a canonical appearance reference.
