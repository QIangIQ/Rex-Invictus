**[[Eileithyia]]** is the goddess of childbirth and labor pains, daughter of [[Zeus]] and [[Hera]]. [[Hera]] once kept her from [[Leto]] to delay the birth of [[Apollo]] and [[Artemis]]. Her role overlaps [[Artemis]]'s epithet [[Upis & Melissa & Locheia & Genetyllis|Locheia]].
