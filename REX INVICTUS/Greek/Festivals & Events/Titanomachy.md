**[[Titanomachy]]** is the ten-year war in which [[Zeus]] and the [[Olympians]], from Mount [[Olympus]], overthrew [[Kronos]] and the [[Titans]] on [[Mt. Othrys]]. [[Zeus]] freed the [[Cyclopes]], who forged his thunderbolt, [[Poseidon]]'s trident and [[Hades]]' helm, and the [[Hecatoncheires]], who buried the [[Titans]] under boulders. The defeated Titans were locked in [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]], and [[Atlas]] was made to hold the sky. Sources: [[Hesiod]]'s *Theogony*. A lost epic called *Titanomachia* was attributed to Eumelus of Corinth. The second war in Riordan's books is fought in [[The Last Olympian]].
