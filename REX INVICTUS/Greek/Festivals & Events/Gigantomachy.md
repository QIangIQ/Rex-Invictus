**[[Gigantomachy]]** is the war between the [[Olympians]] and the [[Giant (Greek)|Giants]], children of [[Gaia]] born from the blood of [[Ouranos]]. A prophecy said the Giants could only be killed by gods and a mortal hero together, so [[Hercules]] fought beside the gods. [[Porphyrion]] was killed by [[Zeus]] and Heracles, [[Ephialtes]] by [[Apollo]] and Heracles, [[Polybotes]] by [[Poseidon]], who crushed him under the island of Nisyros, and [[Mimas]] by [[Hephaestus]]. It was a favorite subject of Greek art, on the Pergamon Altar and the Siphnian Treasury. In [[The Heroes of Olympus]] the war is refought with the Seven.
