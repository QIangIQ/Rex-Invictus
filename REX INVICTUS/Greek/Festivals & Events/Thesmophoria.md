**[[Thesmophoria]]** was an all-women festival of [[Demeter]] Thesmophoros ("law-bringer") and [[Persephone]], held in autumn across the Greek world. Women threw piglets into pits and later mixed the rotted remains with seed grain to ensure fertility.
