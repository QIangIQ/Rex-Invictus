**[[Battle of Manhattan]]** is the climax of [[The Last Olympian]]. While the gods fight [[Typhon]], [[Kronos]] attacks the Empire State Building, the entrance to [[Olympus]]. [[Percy Jackson]] leads [[Camp Half-Blood]], the [[Hunters of Artemis]] and the [[Party Ponies]] to defend the bridges and tunnels. [[Silena Beauregard]], [[Michael Yew]] and [[Ethan Nakamura]] fall. [[Luke Castellan]] destroys [[Kronos]] with [[Annabeth Chase|Annabeth]]'s knife. In [[Rex Invictus]] the old timeline's Battle of Manhattan is part of [[Percy Jackson|Percy]]'s memories.
