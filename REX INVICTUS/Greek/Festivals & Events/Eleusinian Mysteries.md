**[[Eleusinian Mysteries]]** were the secret initiation rites of [[Demeter]] and [[Persephone]] held at Eleusis near Athens for nearly two thousand years, until Theodosius I closed the sanctuary around 392 AD. Initiates walked the Sacred Way from Athens, fasted, drank the barley drink kykeon and saw something in the Telesterion that promised a better afterlife. Their content was never revealed. Listed among the traditions in [[Legum Magicae (XVIII-Alpha)]].
