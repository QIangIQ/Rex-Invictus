### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 632
I am Medusa, The First Known Progenitor of the 'Gorgon' Species. And I bow to my Master. From today onwards, for all eternity, my Fate, and that of my kind, will be tied to the Fate of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] Alucard.
—Medusa pledging herself and the [[Gorgons]] to Victor Elderblood, Chapter 633
Medusa Elderblood is the original Queen and first known Progenitor of the Gorgons, a Heroic Spirit, a Divine Spirit and one of Victor Elderblood's wives through a [[Soul Marriage]]. Originally a Human priestess serving [[Athena]], Medusa's mortal life was destroyed after [[Poseidon]] sexually assaulted her within Athena's temple. Instead of protecting her priestess, Athena blamed and punished Medusa, transforming her into the monster remembered throughout Greek history. The transformation ultimately produced the Gorgon lineage and the power for which the Race became infamous: a gaze capable of transforming living beings into stone.[4]
Medusa was eventually hunted and killed by Perseus, but death did not end her existence. Her extraordinary fame, suffering and the new Race associated with her allowed Medusa to attain greatness after death. She persisted as a Heroic Spirit and simultaneously developed into a Divine Spirit, with her Soul imposing the Concept of the Beginning of a Race. Through that Concept Medusa became recognized as the first known Gorgon Progenitor despite no longer possessing her original physical body.[5][1]
At some point after her death Medusa's surviving Spirit became connected to one of her descendants, [[Roberta Elderblood]]. Medusa remained hidden within Roberta for years, influencing her powers and psychology while protecting her from supernatural threats. Victor detected the unusual existence residing inside Roberta long before learning her identity, initially comparing the entity to a Heroic Spirit similar in general nature to [[Abe-no-Seimei]].[6]
Medusa finally revealed her identity during the events surrounding [[Persephone Elderblood]]'s ascension as Ruler of the [[Greek Underworld]]. She summoned surviving Gorgons, declared herself their Queen, revealed her Progenitor status and permanently tied both her Fate and that of the Gorgon Race to Victor. The women surrounding Victor immediately recognized the declaration as essentially another Soul Marriage, comparable to the bond Victor had previously formed with [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood.[5][1]
Medusa's allegiance to Victor was deeply personal. Victor had already promised to bring Athena and Poseidon to her so that she could personally exact revenge upon the two Gods who destroyed her mortal life. His willingness to fulfill that promise earned a level of trust from Medusa that Roberta considered extraordinarily rare for someone whose existence had been defined by betrayal.[5][1] Victor eventually fulfilled the promise following the destruction of [[Olympus]], personally placing the defeated Athena and Poseidon under Medusa's authority.[4]
Although she continues sharing Roberta's body, Medusa remains an independent consciousness rather than merely one of Roberta's personalities. Victor later offered to use his powers to separate their Souls and create an independent body for Medusa, but both women refused because they had become comfortable sharing one existence and neither wished to live separately from the other.[4]

### Physical Description

#### Original Appearance
Medusa's original Human body has not been described in extensive physical detail within the main story beyond the fact that she was considered extraordinarily beautiful. Her beauty was famous enough that Mortals compared her directly to [[Athena]], a comparison that contributed to the Goddess' jealousy toward her own priestess.[4]
After Athena cursed her, Medusa was transformed into the first known Gorgon. Her hair became serpentine and her appearance acquired the monstrous traits historically associated with [[Gorgons]], most importantly eyes capable of petrifying those exposed to their power.
The original body was eventually killed by Perseus and no longer exists.

#### Divine Spirit
Medusa currently exists without an independent physical body. She survives as a Heroic and Divine Spirit connected directly to her descendant [[Roberta Elderblood]].[1]
Medusa can communicate internally with Roberta or temporarily take primary control of their shared body. When she manifests more strongly through Roberta, visible changes occur. Roberta's eyes become distinctly reptilian, her extraordinarily long black hair moves as though composed of living serpents and dark crack-like markings appear around her eyes. Her skin can become noticeably paler, and when both women speak simultaneously the resulting voice possesses an unnatural dual resonance.[5]
Her hair also reacts visibly to Medusa's emotions. Excitement, possessiveness or murderous intent can cause it to move more actively and coil around nearby people, particularly [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[1][4]

#### Roberta's Blood Dragon Body
After [[Roberta Elderblood]] became a True Blood Dragon, Medusa continued residing inside her. The Draconic transformation did not transform Medusa herself into a Blood Dragon; instead, Medusa remains a Gorgon Progenitor and Divine Spirit using a body that is now Draconic.[7]
The distinction is important. Roberta's Dragon body incorporated several characteristics originating from Medusa, particularly Petrification, but Medusa's own Race and spiritual nature remained unchanged. The later appearance commonly associated with "Dragon Goddess Medusa" is therefore Medusa manifesting through Roberta's evolved body rather than Medusa possessing an independently transformed True Dragon body.

### Personality and Traits
Medusa possesses an intensely proud, seductive, vengeful and dangerous personality. As a Progenitor, she has a powerful sense of pride in both herself and the Gorgon Race. Nevertheless, her pride does not prevent her from willingly acknowledging someone she genuinely respects. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] fulfilled the conditions necessary to earn her trust, Medusa voluntarily knelt before him and called him her Master despite being a Progenitor herself.[1]
This submission should not be confused with weakness. Medusa's decision was entirely voluntary and was immediately accompanied by her declaration that she would not tolerate herself and Roberta remaining merely Victor's maids. She deliberately tied her Fate to Victor's in a marriage-level spiritual bond, making her intentions toward him unmistakable.[1]

#### Trust
Trust is particularly important to Medusa because betrayal defined her original life. She faithfully served [[Athena]] only for the Goddess to abandon and punish her after [[Poseidon]]'s assault. She was subsequently hunted by the very heroic civilization that portrayed her as the monster.[4]
Because of this history Medusa is exceptionally suspicious of promises and loyalty. When Roberta privately questioned whether she truly wanted to tie herself permanently to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Medusa's answer was simply that Victor was trustworthy. Roberta immediately recognized this as one of the highest compliments Medusa was capable of giving another person.[1]
Victor earned that trust primarily because he repeatedly demonstrated that his promises were not empty. When he told Medusa that Athena and Poseidon would eventually be placed in her hands, she believed him—and he later fulfilled the promise completely.[5][4]

#### Vengeance and Sadism
Medusa's hatred toward the Gods responsible for her suffering is one of her defining characteristics. She possesses particularly intense hatred toward [[Poseidon]], [[Athena]] and Perseus. Simply hearing Perseus' name centuries after her death was enough to make Medusa openly growl with rage.[4]
Her desire for revenge gradually developed into a pronounced sadistic streak. While observing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] employ increasingly sophisticated methods of torture, Medusa became fascinated rather than disturbed. During later events she enthusiastically asked Victor to teach her the techniques so that she could use them against Poseidon and Athena, and Victor agreed.[8]
Medusa later became visibly captivated while Victor tortured [[Zeus]] during the destruction of [[Olympus]]. The suffering of the Greek Gods produced intense satisfaction for her because she interpreted it as long-delayed retribution against the civilization that had hunted and desecrated her and her people.[9]
Her vindictiveness can occasionally become irrational. After Olympus fell, Medusa attempted to include [[Artemis]] among the Gods she intended to punish even though Artemis had played no role in Medusa's original tragedy. When Victor questioned her reasoning, Medusa openly admitted that she simply disliked Artemis and had been annoyed because the Goddess repeatedly avoided her attacks during the battle. Victor consequently released Artemis from Medusa's punishment while retaining Athena and Poseidon, the two people directly responsible for her suffering.[4]

#### Protectiveness Toward Roberta
Despite being a major source of Roberta's more violent psychological tendencies, Medusa is deeply protective of her descendant. She does not view Roberta merely as a convenient vessel.
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] handed [[Athena]] and [[Poseidon]] to Medusa, he explicitly instructed her to protect Roberta's mind if the punishments became too graphic. Medusa immediately assured him that she would protect Roberta as she always had.[4]
Their refusal to separate further demonstrates the depth of their relationship. Victor possessed the ability to separate their Souls and create Medusa an independent body, but both women rejected the offer. Roberta admitted that she did not know what she would do without Medusa inside her, while Medusa agreed that their current existence was preferable.[4]

### History

#### Ancient Greece
Medusa was originally a Human woman renowned for exceptional beauty and served as a priestess within [[Athena]]'s temple. Mortal admirers eventually began comparing Medusa's beauty with that of Athena herself, provoking the Goddess' jealousy.[4]
Her life was destroyed when [[Poseidon]] raped her within Athena's temple. Rather than protecting her priestess or punishing Poseidon, Athena blamed Medusa and used the violation of her chastity as justification to transform her into a monster. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later directly condemned Athena for abandoning Medusa after the assault and stated that Athena's real motivation had been jealousy over Mortals considering Medusa more beautiful.[4]
The curse transformed Medusa into the being that would become the foundation of the Gorgon Race. Her hair became serpents, her appearance became monstrous and her gaze acquired the ability to petrify living beings.
Medusa and the [[Gorgons]] were thereafter treated as monsters and hunted by Heroes connected to [[Olympus]]. Medusa was eventually killed by Perseus, whose name continued to provoke intense hatred from her even after thousands of years.[4]
Death, however, produced an outcome Olympus had not anticipated.
Medusa's Soul persisted as a Heroic Spirit and then achieved Divine status. Her Soul imposed the Concept of the Beginning of a Race, elevating her into a Divine Spirit and the first known Progenitor of the Gorgon species. Medusa herself later described this transformation as achieving greatness in death while the Gods who hunted and desecrated her believed the threat had been permanently eliminated.[5][1]

#### Connection to Roberta
At some unknown point Medusa's surviving Spirit became attached to her descendant [[Roberta Elderblood]]. The exact mechanism through which the connection originated has not been completely explained.
The two gradually became inseparable. Medusa possessed her own consciousness, memories, hatred and powers, but centuries of cohabitation caused elements of her personality to influence Roberta's psychology.
[[Scathach]] later theorized that the vengeful emotions carried by the Spirit combined with Roberta's personal abandonment and family trauma, contributing to the development of Roberta's sadistic protective personality.[10]
Medusa nevertheless remained an independent Being rather than merely a personality created by Roberta.

### Synopsis

#### Vampire Society
Medusa was already residing within Roberta when the latter began appearing around [[Johnny Lykos]] and [[The Lost Club]], although her identity remained completely hidden.
When [[Victor Elderblood]] encountered Roberta and realized that Johnny's memories had been manipulated, he tested the mysterious woman directly. The unknown presence residing inside Roberta responded defensively, revealing to Victor that he was dealing with something significantly more dangerous than an ordinary Witch.[6]
Victor compared the entity with a Heroic Spirit, but he could not determine its identity. Rather than destroy Roberta and the mysterious Spirit, Victor became interested in their potential and brought Roberta into his sphere under a magical contract restricting whom she was permitted to manipulate.[6][11]
Medusa therefore entered Victor's household long before anyone knew her name.

#### A New Pillar
Roberta eventually became one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Battle Maids, bringing Medusa with her. Although the Progenitor did not openly reveal herself, members of Victor's household gradually became familiar with several abilities associated with her.
By the time Medusa finally revealed her identity, Kaguya, Eve, Bruna and Maria were already aware that Roberta possessed access to abilities such as Petrification. What shocked them was not the existence of Medusa but the revelation that the Spirit inside their fellow maid was an actual Progenitor.[1]
[[Scathach]]'s examination of Roberta also revealed the degree to which Medusa's hatred interacted with her host's psychological trauma. At that stage, however, neither Scathach nor most of Victor's household knew the Spirit's true history.[10]
Medusa continued observing Victor throughout this period. His willingness to protect his people, challenge Gods and honor promises gradually earned her respect and eventually her trust.

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Medusa's relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became increasingly personal as she observed his development. Victor likewise became aware that Roberta's hostility toward the Gods was not entirely her own and that the entity within her possessed an ancient grievance connected to [[Olympus]].
Victor eventually promised that he would personally defeat [[Poseidon]] and [[Athena]] and give both of them to Medusa so she could decide their fate.[5]
The promise became one of the foundations of Medusa's loyalty toward him. She had spent an enormous period carrying hatred toward the Gods responsible for her mortal suffering, and Victor was the first person capable of offering something other than sympathy: the realistic possibility of vengeance.

#### Demonic War
Medusa finally revealed herself openly during the events surrounding the reorganization of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Clan.
While Victor was formally accepting Maria, Bruna and Roberta into his Bloodline, Roberta's eyes became reptilian, her hair moved as though alive and Medusa spoke through the body shared by the two women. When Victor reaffirmed his promise to defeat [[Athena]] and [[Poseidon]], Medusa decided that merely remaining hidden was no longer appropriate.[5]
She summoned ten Gorgon women through a large green [[Magic]] Circle and announced:
I am a Heroic Spirit; my name is Medusa! But I am also something else. I am a Gorgon; I am The Queen of The [[Gorgons]].
—Medusa revealing her identity, Chapter 632
Medusa explained that despite having been turned into a monster, hunted by Olympian Heroes and physically killed, death had elevated rather than erased her. She had become both a Heroic and Divine Spirit, and the Concept imposed by her Soul was the Beginning of a Race.[5]
The revelation immediately identified her as a Progenitor.
She subsequently declared herself the first known Progenitor of the Gorgon species and voluntarily knelt before Victor. The ten Gorgons followed their Queen's example.[1]
Medusa then made a declaration with far greater implications than ordinary political submission: for all eternity, both her Fate and the Fate of the Gorgon Race would be tied to Victor's Fate. Victor's wives immediately recognized the act as essentially the same kind of [[Soul Marriage]] previously created between Victor and [[Aphrodite]].[1]
Victor accepted.
Medusa immediately felt her Spirit connect directly with his and gained the ability to perceive his emotions through that bond. Roberta privately asked whether Medusa genuinely wanted such a permanent union. Medusa confirmed that she did, describing Victor as a trustworthy man and making clear that she had no intention of allowing the two women to remain merely Victor's maids.[1]
Victor simultaneously established an important political distinction. He refused to absorb the Gorgon Race itself into Clan Alucard. Medusa could be his wife and the Gorgons could bind their political Fate to his faction, but the Gorgons would remain their own Race under Medusa's leadership instead of losing their identity inside Victor's Clan.[1]
The result established Medusa in three simultaneous positions: Victor's Soul-Married wife, Queen and Progenitor of the Gorgons, and one of the supernatural leaders whose people had become permanently aligned with Victor's expanding faction.

#### King Among Kings
Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s conquest of Hell, Medusa became increasingly interested in the more brutal skills he had accumulated during centuries of warfare.
When Victor began designing the defenses and institutions of his developing territories, Medusa enthusiastically suggested training specialists in torture, considering such knowledge perfectly suited to interrogation and punishment.[12]
Her interest intensified after observing Victor employ the torture techniques he had learned from Lily. During one particularly gruesome display, Roberta could no longer tolerate watching and surrendered primary control of their body to Medusa. Medusa reacted in exactly the opposite manner: she watched Victor's work with open fascination and excitedly asked him to teach her everything so she could eventually use the techniques upon [[Athena]] and [[Poseidon]].[8]
Victor agreed.
Medusa's opportunity finally came during Victor's invasion of the [[Greek Pantheon]]. She accompanied his forces against [[Olympus]] alongside the Battle Maids, [[Gorgons]] and other factions personally wronged by the Olympian Gods.[13]
The war placed Medusa directly on the battlefield against the civilization that had once hunted her. By Chapter 799 she was openly recognized among Victor's major forces as the Progenitor of the Gorgons.[14]
Medusa took particular pleasure in Victor's treatment of [[Zeus]]. As Victor dismantled and tortured the God-King, Medusa watched with adoration, love and lust. For her, the suffering of the Greek Gods was the fulfillment of vengeance accumulated across millennia.[9]
Olympus ultimately fell, and Victor survived his own transformation into an even greater Draconic existence. Once the remaining Gods had been captured, he turned his attention toward promises made long before he possessed the strength to fulfill them.[15]
Poseidon and Athena were brought before Victor and Medusa.
Victor directly reminded Athena why this punishment existed. Poseidon had raped Medusa, while Athena had abandoned her own priestess and transformed her into a monster because of jealousy. Victor made clear that his treatment of Athena was not abstract justice on behalf of everyone she had ever harmed; it was personal retaliation for what she had done to his wife.[4]
Medusa initially attempted to include [[Artemis]] as another victim, but when Artemis pointed out that she had no involvement in Medusa's original tragedy, Victor questioned Medusa directly. Medusa admitted that she simply disliked Artemis and had been annoyed by her during the war. Victor consequently released Artemis from that punishment, leaving Athena and Poseidon as the two people legitimately connected to Medusa's revenge.[4]
Victor then handed both Gods to Medusa as promised.
Medusa summoned members of the Gorgon Race and had Natalia transport her prisoners to a hidden location. Victor additionally ordered that a Goddess of Medicine remain available to heal Athena and Poseidon whenever necessary, ensuring Medusa could continue their punishment without accidentally allowing them to escape through death.[4]
The promise made years earlier had been completely fulfilled.

#### Refusing Separation
Immediately before Medusa departed with [[Athena]] and [[Poseidon]], [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] offered both women something neither had seriously expected.
His newly developed Natural Powers were sufficient to separate Medusa and Roberta's Souls and create an independent physical body for Medusa.[4]
After privately considering the offer, both women refused.
Roberta stated that she no longer knew what she would do if Medusa left her body, while Medusa agreed that she preferred their existing arrangement. Victor accepted their choice and promised that the option would remain available if they ever changed their minds.[4]
Their continued shared existence is therefore no longer a limitation forced upon Medusa by lack of alternatives. By this stage, remaining within Roberta had become a deliberate choice made by both women.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Medusa herself did not become a Blood Dragon when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] created his new Race. Instead, her host and descendant [[Roberta Elderblood]] underwent the transformation.[7]
The distinction nevertheless had major consequences for both women because Roberta's new Dragon body integrated characteristics originating from Medusa.
Roberta's eyes acquired Medusa's Petrification as an inherent Draconic power. Rather than requiring Medusa to take control, Roberta herself could now petrify beings simply through her gaze and consciously determine when the power activated.[7]
Her Memory Manipulation also became drastically stronger.
Medusa continued existing as a Gorgon Progenitor and Divine Spirit inside the enhanced vessel. She therefore gained the practical advantages of sharing an immensely stronger body without her own spiritual Race being replaced by Victor's Blood Dragon lineage.

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
The enormous periods of accelerated Time experienced by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family allowed Medusa and Roberta to remain with one another for roughly two additional millennia while their relationships within Clan Elderblood continued developing.
Both women eventually desired children with Victor. Because they continued sharing a single body, later narration regularly refers to the maternal side collectively as Roberta/Medusa rather than attempting to separate them during reproductive or familial discussions.
Chapter 998 states that some of Victor's wives had twins and explicitly identifies Roberta/Medusa as one of those cases.[2]
Later, however, Chapter 1130 refers to Victor's child with Roberta/Medusa in the singular while discussing the children produced with mothers who possessed Progenitor lineages.[3]
Because those two statements conflict, the exact number of Roberta/Medusa's daughters is currently uncertain. The safest interpretation is that at least one biological daughter is confirmed, while Chapter 998 specifically supports the existence of twins.
The same later discussion establishes that Medusa's Progenitor status does not override Victor's Blood Dragon heredity. The daughter born through Roberta/Medusa is fundamentally a True Blood Dragon, although Medusa's Gorgon Progenitor lineage remains part of the child's inherited foundation.[3]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
Medusa's relationship with [[Victor Elderblood]] developed primarily through trust, vengeance and mutual possessiveness.
She observed Victor for a considerable period from inside Roberta before revealing her identity. Unlike most people who entered his faction, Medusa had already experienced catastrophic betrayal from the Goddess she once faithfully served and therefore placed considerably more importance upon whether Victor's words matched his actions.
Victor's promise concerning [[Athena]] and [[Poseidon]] became decisive. He did not dismiss Medusa's desire for vengeance or lecture her about forgiving people who had destroyed her life. He promised that when he became powerful enough, both Gods would be defeated and handed directly to her.[5]
This willingness to accept the darker parts of her personality contributed heavily to Medusa trusting him.
When Medusa finally revealed her Progenitor status, she voluntarily bowed to Victor and addressed him as Master. Roberta privately questioned whether Medusa truly wanted to surrender so much independence to another person after everything she had endured. Medusa answered that Victor was trustworthy.[1]
She then escalated the relationship herself. Refusing to remain merely a maid or subordinate, Medusa bound her Fate and Spirit to Victor in a [[Soul Marriage]] and became his wife.[1]
Her feelings subsequently became considerably more openly romantic. When Victor agreed to teach her methods of torture for use upon Athena and Poseidon, Medusa enthusiastically declared her love for him, receiving the same declaration in return.[8]
Victor's eventual fulfillment of his promise further validated everything Medusa had placed in him. He conquered [[Olympus]], defeated Athena and Poseidon and delivered them alive into her hands.[4]
Their relationship therefore represents almost the inverse of Medusa's relationship with Athena. Athena received her service and betrayed her; Victor received her trust and repeatedly fulfilled the promises through which he had earned it.

#### Roberta Elderblood
[[Roberta Elderblood]] is Medusa's descendant, host and closest companion.
The two women share the same physical body but possess separate consciousnesses. They can communicate privately, exchange control and speak simultaneously when both are actively expressing themselves.[1]
Medusa's presence influenced Roberta's psychological development, particularly through the hatred and desire for revenge carried by the Divine Spirit. [[Scathach]] believed this influence combined with Roberta's personal trauma and contributed to the formation of her crueler protective personality.[10]
Despite this influence, Medusa cares deeply about Roberta. She repeatedly protects her descendant and takes control when Roberta is psychologically incapable of tolerating particular situations. During the extreme torture Medusa wanted to study, for example, Roberta withdrew and allowed Medusa to handle the experience instead.[8]
Their relationship became sufficiently close that neither wanted independence when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] offered it. Both preferred continuing to live within the same body rather than separating into two physical existences.[4]

#### Athena
[[Athena]] was once Medusa's Goddess and the deity whose temple she served as a priestess.
That devotion ended in complete betrayal.
After [[Poseidon]] raped Medusa inside Athena's temple, Athena did not defend her. Instead, she punished the victim, transforming Medusa into a monster. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later directly accused Athena of acting from jealousy because Mortals had claimed Medusa's beauty exceeded Athena's own.[4]
Medusa's hatred consequently survived her physical death and persisted throughout her existence as a Divine Spirit.
Following the destruction of [[Olympus]], Victor handed Athena directly to Medusa. The former Goddess and former priestess therefore experienced a complete reversal of their original positions: Athena became Medusa's helpless prisoner while Medusa possessed complete freedom to decide how her former mistress would be punished.[4]

#### Poseidon
[[Poseidon]] is one of the principal causes of Medusa's tragedy.
He raped Medusa during her Human life, beginning the sequence of events that resulted in [[Athena]]'s punishment, Medusa's transformation into a Gorgon and her eventual death.[4]
Medusa carried intense hatred toward him for thousands of years and repeatedly expressed her desire to personally torture him once [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became capable of defeating the Olympian God.
Victor eventually fulfilled that desire and delivered Poseidon to her alongside Athena.[4]
Poseidon should not be listed as one of Medusa's former lovers. Their encounter was sexual assault and never represented a consensual relationship.

#### Perseus
Perseus was the Hero responsible for killing Medusa's original physical body.
Medusa continues to hate him even after becoming a Divine Spirit. During Chapter 808, merely hearing [[Artemis]] mention Perseus caused Medusa to openly growl, demonstrating that her hatred of the man who killed her has never disappeared.[4]

#### Gorgons
Medusa is the Queen and first known Progenitor of the [[Gorgons]].
Her importance to the Race goes beyond political leadership. Her Soul possesses the Concept of the Beginning of a Race, directly establishing her Progenitor status.[5]
When she revealed herself to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Medusa summoned ten Gorgon women who immediately followed her lead and knelt to Victor after their Queen pledged the Race's Fate to his own.[5][1]
Victor nevertheless refused to erase the Gorgons' racial autonomy. They remained a distinct species under Medusa instead of being absorbed directly into Clan Alucard.[1]
Medusa later summoned additional Gorgons when taking [[Athena]] and [[Poseidon]] away for punishment.[4]

### Skills and Abilities

#### Gorgon Progenitor Physiology
Gorgon Progenitor: Medusa is the first known Progenitor of the Gorgon Race. As a Progenitor, she stands fundamentally above ordinary members of her species and represents the point around which the Race's lineage is organized.[5][1]
Unlike several other known Progenitors, Medusa achieved formal Progenitor status after physical death, when her Spirit attained greatness and imposed a Concept directly upon reality.[1]

#### Divine Concept
Beginning of a Race: Medusa's Soul imposes the Concept of the Beginning of a Race. This is the Divine Concept directly connected to her status as the Gorgon Progenitor.[5]
The novel has not yet demonstrated a broad list of named offensive applications derived specifically from this Concept, so the page should not attribute speculative Conceptual abilities to Medusa beyond what has been shown.

#### Heroic Spirit Physiology
Heroic Spirit: Medusa survived the death of her physical body as a Heroic Spirit. Her fame, history and supernatural achievements allowed her consciousness and identity to continue existing long after Perseus killed her.[5]
- Post-Mortem Existence: Destruction of Medusa's original physical body did not erase her Soul or consciousness.
- Spiritual Manifestation: Medusa can manifest through Roberta and directly interact with the physical world despite lacking an independent body.
- Possession / Vessel Control: She can assume control of Roberta's body when necessary.
- Internal Communication: Medusa and Roberta can communicate mentally without outsiders hearing the conversation.
- Dual Manifestation: Both women can speak simultaneously through the same body, producing a dual-layered voice.[5]

#### Divine Spirit Physiology
Divine Spirit: Medusa is simultaneously a Heroic Spirit and a Divine Spirit. Her ascension following death elevated her beyond an ordinary deceased Hero.[5][1]
She consequently possesses [[Divine Energy]] and the spiritual foundation of a Goddess despite no longer maintaining an independent physical vessel.

#### Petrification
Petrification: Medusa's signature ability allows her to transform targets into stone. The power originates from her Gorgon nature and can be invoked through her eyes.[1]
Roberta could access portions of this ability even before becoming a Blood Dragon. After Roberta's Dragon transformation, Petrification became an inherent characteristic of Roberta's own Draconic Eyes as well.[7]

#### Serpentine Hair Manipulation
Living Hair: When Medusa manifests strongly through Roberta, the latter's hair moves like living snakes. The hair responds to Medusa's emotions, murderous intent and possessiveness and can coil around nearby objects or individuals.[5][4]

#### Barrier Creation
Barrier: Before Medusa's identity was known, the Spirit residing within Roberta demonstrated enough defensive power to respond when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] tested Roberta directly, preventing him from simply eliminating her.[6]
The barrier/protective response was originally attributed only to the mysterious Spirit and was later understood to be connected to Medusa.

#### Gorgon Summoning
Gorgon Summoning: Medusa can summon members of the Gorgon Race through large [[Magic]] Circles. During her reveal she summoned ten Gorgon women directly into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s presence.[5]
She repeated the ability after [[Olympus]] fell, summoning [[Gorgons]] to assist with transporting and administering the punishment of [[Athena]] and [[Poseidon]].[4]

#### Soul Marriage
[[Soul Marriage]]: Medusa permanently connected her Spirit and Fate to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Her declaration was immediately recognized by Victor's other wives as the same type of Soul-level marriage established between Victor and [[Aphrodite]].[1]
The connection allows Medusa to directly sense Victor's emotions and binds their [[Fates]] at the spiritual level.[1]

#### Shared Abilities Through Roberta
Because Medusa resides within [[Roberta Elderblood]], she can make practical use of the body and racial advantages currently possessed by her descendant.
This does not mean Medusa herself should be categorized as a Vampire or Blood Dragon.
Roberta's current body provides:
- True Blood Dragon physiology.[7]
- Draconic physical capabilities.
- Draconic flight and senses.
- Roberta's evolved Memory Manipulation.
- Petrifying Draconic Eyes derived from Medusa herself.[7]
These are abilities available through their shared vessel rather than Medusa's independent racial physiology.

### Trivia
- Medusa is the first known Progenitor of the Gorgon Race rather than merely its Queen.[1]
- She achieved Progenitor status after her physical death, when her existence developed into both a Heroic Spirit and Divine Spirit.[5][1]
- The exact wording of Medusa's Concept is "the Beginning of a Race".[5]
- Medusa herself is not a Blood Dragon. [[Roberta Elderblood]] is the Blood Dragon; Medusa remains a Gorgon Progenitor and Divine Spirit inhabiting her body.[7]
- For the same reason, Medusa should not be listed as formerly Human, Witch, Noble Vampire and currently True Dragon simply because Roberta passed through those racial states.
- [[Poseidon]] should not be listed as Medusa's former lover. His encounter with her was rape.[4]
- Medusa considers [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] being trustworthy one of the highest compliments she can give anyone because of how severely she was betrayed during her first life.[1]
- Despite being a Progenitor, Medusa voluntarily bowed to Victor and called him Master after he earned her trust.[1]
- Medusa immediately escalated her relationship with Victor from subordinate to wife because her pride would not allow her and Roberta to remain merely his maids.[1]
- Her [[Soul Marriage]] also tied the Fate of the Gorgon Race politically to Victor, although Victor deliberately allowed the Race to remain independent under Medusa's leadership rather than absorbing them into his Clan.[1]
- Medusa has an extreme interest in torture. After witnessing Victor's techniques, she enthusiastically asked him to teach her so that she could use them upon [[Athena]] and Poseidon.[8]
- Medusa reacted to Victor's torture of [[Zeus]] with adoration, love and lust because the suffering of the Greek Gods satisfied her ancient desire for vengeance.[9]
- Medusa's hatred can become irrational. She attempted to punish [[Artemis]] despite Artemis having no involvement in her original tragedy, admitting that she simply disliked her. Victor corrected this and released Artemis from Medusa's revenge.[4]
- Hearing Perseus' name still causes Medusa visible anger thousands of years after he killed her.[4]
- Victor eventually fulfilled his promise and personally handed Athena and Poseidon to Medusa.[4]
- Victor offered to separate Medusa and Roberta's Souls and create Medusa an independent physical body. Both women refused because they preferred continuing to share one existence.[4]
- This means Medusa's lack of an independent body is currently a choice, not simply a condition she is incapable of overcoming.
- Chapter 998 states that Roberta/Medusa had twins.[2] Chapter 1130 later refers to Victor's child with Roberta/Medusa in the singular.[3] Until the inconsistency is clarified, the exact number of their daughters should be treated as uncertain.
- Their child or children inherit Victor's Blood Dragon lineage as the dominant Race despite Medusa herself being a Progenitor.[3]
