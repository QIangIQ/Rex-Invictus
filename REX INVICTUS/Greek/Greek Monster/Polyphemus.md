**[[Polyphemus]]** is a [[Cyclopes|Cyclops]], son of [[Poseidon]] and the nymph Thoosa.

---

## Greek History (IRL)

In Book 9 of the *Odyssey* he traps Odysseus and his men in his cave and eats six of them. Odysseus gives his name as "Nobody", gets him drunk and blinds him with a heated stake, so that when the other [[Cyclopes]] come he cries that Nobody is hurting him. Odysseus escapes under the bellies of the sheep, and Polyphemus' prayer to his father begins [[Poseidon]]'s long persecution of Odysseus. Theocritus and Ovid tell of his hopeless love for the Nereid [[Galatea]].

## PJO Canon

In [[The Sea of Monsters]] he keeps the [[Golden Fleece]] on his island in the [[Sea of Monsters]] and tries to marry [[Grover Underwood]], who is disguised as a female [[Cyclopes|Cyclops]]. [[Percy Jackson]], [[Annabeth Chase]], [[Clarisse]] and [[Tyson]] take the Fleece back to save [[Thalia's Pine Tree]].
