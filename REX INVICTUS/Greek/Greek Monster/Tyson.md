**[[Tyson]]** is a young [[Cyclopes|Cyclops]], son of [[Poseidon]] and a nature spirit, and half-brother of [[Percy Jackson]].

---

## PJO Canon

He meets [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] as a homeless classmate at Meriwether College Prep in [[The Sea of Monsters]], where his strength and immunity to fire save the quest. [[Poseidon]] claims him, and he goes to work in the [[Cyclopes]]' forges under the sea. In [[The Last Olympian]] he leads the Cyclops army into the [[Battle of Manhattan]] against [[Typhon]], and he is later made a general. He loves peanut butter, ponies and the harpy [[Ella]], and he befriends a [[Hippocampi|hippocampus]] he names Rainbow.
