**[[Karkinos]]** is a giant crab brought to Alcatraz in The Enchantress by Xolotl. (Lore adapted from *The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** Flamel material is adapted into the Rex Invictus present (c. 2021). Rex Invictus wins any conflict; [[Robespierre]] fills [[Dr. John Dee]]'s role and hunts the Philosopher's Stone rather than [[The Codex]]. See [[Flamel Series#Integration rules]].

## Real-World Background (IRL)

In Greek myth Karkinos is the giant crab [[Hera]] sent to distract Heracles during his fight with the Hydra. He crushed it, and Hera placed it in the sky as the constellation Cancer.
