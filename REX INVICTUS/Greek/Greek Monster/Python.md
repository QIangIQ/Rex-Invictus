**[[Python]]** is the serpent-dragon that guarded Delphi for [[Gaia]] until the young [[Apollo]] killed it and took over the oracle; the Pythian Games and the Pythia priestess are named after it. In [[The Trials of Apollo]] Python has retaken Delphi, and [[Apollo]] defeats it in [[The Tower of Nero]]. See [[Oracle of Delphi]].
