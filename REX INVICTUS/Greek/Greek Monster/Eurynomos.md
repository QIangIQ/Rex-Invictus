**[[Eurynomos]]** (plural eurynomoi) is a corpse-eating daemon of the [[Greek Underworld]].

---

## Greek History (IRL)

Pausanias describes him in Polygnotus' painting of the [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] at Delphi: blue-black like a carrion fly, baring his teeth and sitting on a vulture skin, eating the flesh of the dead down to the bones. The guides at Delphi told Pausanias he was one of the daemons of [[Hades]]. He appears in no surviving poem.

## PJO Canon

In [[The Tyrant's Tomb]] eurynomoi serve the emperors' undead army; a wound from their claws slowly turns the victim into a zombie, as happens to [[Apollo]].
