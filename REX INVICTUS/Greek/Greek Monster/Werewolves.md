Werewolves are a supernatural race of shapeshifting Night Creatures and one of the major supernatural species of the setting. Although large Werewolf populations exist on Earth, their race did not originally evolve there. Their ancestors came from another world, and the modern Werewolves encountered throughout most of the story are later, environmentally diminished descendants of the far more complete beings now identified as Ancient Werewolves.[4]
Werewolves are primarily distinguished by their overwhelming physical constitution, connection to nature, transformations and pack-based racial structure. [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett|Scathach Scarlett]] explicitly describes Vampires as a physically weaker breed by comparison, explaining that Vampires compensate through vastly superior regeneration.[5] Werewolf society consequently developed heavily around physical strength, Alphas, packs and the instinctive authority exerted by stronger members of the race.
Their principal civilization is located on Samar, a world governed for millennia by the Werewolf monarchy. [[Volk Fenrir]] formerly ruled the species as its King alongside [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]], but after Tasha awakened as the new Werewolf Progenitor, defeated Volk and stripped him of his racial position, she became the highest authority of the race.[1][2][6]

### Origin and History
Werewolves are not Humans suffering from a supernatural curse. They are a genuine supernatural species with their own Bloodline, Soul characteristics, reproductive lineages and ancestral evolutionary state. While Werewolves have lived on Earth for an extremely long period and are classified among its Night Creatures, later revelations establish that the species itself possesses extraterrestrial ancestry. Samar became the center of modern Werewolf civilization, while Werewolf communities also developed on Earth and potentially other worlds capable of sustaining supernatural life.[7][8][4]
The first known true Werewolf Progenitor was Licael. Over the generations that followed, however, the race's original characteristics were progressively diminished by the environments in which its descendants developed. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later compares this degradation to what occurred with Noble Vampires: just as the ordinary humanoid state of modern Vampires is not the complete form possessed by their ancestors, modern Werewolves are not identical to the original Werewolves of their ancestral planet.[4]
The race remained structured around Alphas and their packs throughout modern history. On Samar, this principle reached its largest expression under [[Volk Fenrir]], whose effective pack encompassed the Werewolves of an entire world. The sheer number of Werewolves beneath him substantially increased his strength, demonstrating that the political and biological concepts of a "pack" are closely connected for the species.[9]
The racial structure fundamentally changed when [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]] defeated Volk and awakened as a new Progenitor. Rather than merely becoming another exceptionally powerful Alpha, Tasha became the source and central authority of the modern Werewolf race. Every Werewolf became connected to her through the characteristics of the Progenitor's Soul, giving her the ability to alter positions within the hierarchy, withdraw power, expel individuals from the racial system and appoint new Alphas.[1][2][6]

### Biology
Werewolves possess naturally powerful bodies specifically adapted around physical combat. Their musculature, bones, senses and overall constitution are superior to ordinary Humans and generally possess greater raw physical performance than comparable Vampires. Their regenerative ability, however, is considerably less impressive than that of Vampires, meaning Werewolves rely much more heavily on the durability of their bodies rather than recovering from otherwise fatal injuries afterward.[5]
Werewolves retain humanoid forms but can progressively manifest more of their racial characteristics. Depending on the individual and degree of development, transformations can include wolf ears, tails, fur, claws, altered legs, elongated teeth, enlarged bodies, Hybrid Forms and eventually complete bipedal or fully realized Werewolf states.[10][11][12]

#### Awakening
Not every naturally born Werewolf manifests the full characteristics of the species immediately. Some descendants can remain effectively Human or display only dormant Bloodline characteristics until their Werewolf nature is triggered. Leona Lykos is one example of a naturally born Werewolf whose complete racial abilities awakened later in life.[13]
Werewolves are also capable of converting Humans through their venom. Leona had previously infected [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] with Werewolf venom with the expectation that he could become part of her pack, although the venom was subsequently neutralized by the Vampire transformation performed upon him. Adam specifically notes that Wolf venom is not normally easy to neutralize and recognizes Leona's venom as especially potent.[13]
Converted Werewolves generally enter the racial hierarchy as Betas rather than naturally becoming Alphas. When discussing what would have happened had Victor successfully become a Werewolf, it is specifically stated that his unusual nature would have caused him to bypass the ordinary result and become a True Alpha, indicating that such a result is exceptional rather than standard.[14]

#### Physical Characteristics
Although their individual appearances vary considerably, several physical characteristics repeatedly appear among Werewolves.
- Large Physique: Werewolves generally possess large, heavily developed bodies, particularly after transformation. Their transformed states can substantially increase their height and muscular mass.[10]
- Fangs and Claws: Partial and complete transformations produce powerful claws and enlarged teeth used as natural weapons. These characteristics can be manifested without necessarily assuming a completely transformed state.[11][12]
- Distinctive Scent: Werewolves possess a recognizable racial scent that supernatural beings with sufficiently developed senses can distinguish. Vampires frequently mock this scent by comparing it to a wet dog.[15]
- Predatory Eyes: The eyes of Werewolves may change when their Bloodline or Alpha authority becomes active. Adam's eyes, for example, glow bright blue while asserting his authority over Leona.[13]
- Heightened Aggression: Manifesting more of the Werewolf Bloodline can make the individual more aggressive and instinct-driven. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] specifically observes this personality shift when Leona enters her Hybrid Form.[12]

#### Diet
Werewolves are capable of consuming ordinary food and do not possess a specialized dietary requirement comparable to a Vampire's dependence upon Blood. Their enhanced physiques and racial development can, however, dramatically increase their appetite and the amount of food necessary to maintain their bodies.[16]

#### Lifespan
Werewolves possess significant Longevity, but modern Werewolves are fundamentally mortal and should not be classified as naturally immortal or immune to aging. Ordinary Betas and Omegas possess an expected lifespan of approximately 500 years.[17] An Alpha possesses an expected lifespan of approximately 1,000 years, with stronger Alphas being capable of extending that period. Even an extraordinarily empowered Werewolf such as Volk was ordinarily expected to remain within an approximate upper lifespan of 3,000 years, with exceptional Blessings capable of modifying the normal limit.[9]
Unlike Vampires, Werewolves therefore do not automatically become stronger simply by continuing to exist for enormous periods. Their development is more strongly connected to Bloodline quality, training, transformation, individual talent, pack size and position within the racial hierarchy.[17][9]

### Pack Structure and Hierarchy
The pack is not merely a social tradition among Werewolves. It is integrated into the supernatural characteristics of the race itself. Modern Werewolves receive tangible advantages from gathering beneath an Alpha, while the Alpha also receives power from the Werewolves recognized as members of their pack.[18][9][1]

#### Alpha
Alphas are Werewolves capable of forming and leading packs. They generally possess greater inherent strength and authority than Betas and can have numerous subordinate Werewolves beneath them. A pack is not necessarily restricted to a single individual born as an Alpha; several Alphas can acknowledge a sufficiently powerful Alpha as their superior, as demonstrated by the Werewolf Generals serving the Royal Family and families containing several Alpha-class members.
An Alpha's strength is partially dependent upon the pack supporting them. The number and quality of followers both contribute to the boost: Edward estimated that gathering approximately 1,000–1,500 Betas would elevate his overall strength significantly, while Volk received an enormous amplification because virtually every Werewolf on Samar belonged to his pack.[9] This also creates a weakness. Chapter 849 explains that ordinary Alphas lose power when followers are removed and can theoretically fall toward Beta-level strength after losing the entirety of their pack.[1]

#### True Alpha
A True Alpha is an exceptional Alpha possessing the potential and qualities necessary to rise to the position of King. The distinction is treated as considerably rarer than merely possessing Alpha status. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s [[Sanguis Noctis Regis]] would have caused him to become such an Alpha immediately had his Werewolf transformation succeeded.[14]
[[Volk Fenrir]] was one of the principal examples of a True Alpha before his demotion, with his authority eventually extending across nearly the entire Werewolf civilization of Samar.

#### Beta
Betas form the majority of Werewolves beneath an Alpha. They are generally weaker than Alphas and operate as members of a pack rather than independent pack leaders. Their connection to their Alpha is supernatural in nature rather than merely political, making the presence of a powerful Alpha capable of improving their combat performance.[18]
Betas are also subjected to the authority of their Alpha. The hierarchy can compel obedience to direct orders, making opposition against one's recognized Alpha considerably more difficult than an ordinary subordinate refusing a command.[19]

#### Omega
Omegas are Werewolves existing outside the ordinary support structure of a pack, traditionally because they were expelled or separated themselves from one. Because pack connections contribute directly to modern Werewolf strength, becoming an Omega can cause an enormous reduction in power.
The most extreme example is [[Volk Fenrir]]. After becoming the Werewolf Progenitor, Tasha explicitly expelled Volk from her racial structure and declared him an Omega, severing his connection to the power system supporting the race and reducing the former [[Lycaon|Werewolf King]] to an extraordinarily weakened state.[2]

### The Moon
The Moon possesses an important relationship with Werewolf physiology and transformation. A full moon greatly enhances the racial power of modern Werewolves and can assist them in accessing stronger transformed states. During the battle involving Kaguya and a group of Werewolves, an elder specifically notes that the natural moon was not full before using a specialized talisman to reproduce the missing condition.[10]
After the talisman was activated, a large moon-shaped magical formation appeared in the sky and the surviving Werewolves underwent much stronger transformations, becoming larger and considerably more dangerous. This establishes that the condition can be artificially simulated through supernatural tools rather than requiring the physical Moon itself to naturally be full.[10]
The importance of lunar conditions is not necessarily permanent for every advanced member of the race. Werewolves who recover more complete ancestral characteristics can overcome limitations suffered by ordinary modern Werewolves, meaning lunar dependence should not be treated as an absolute law applying equally to every evolutionary state of the species.

### Ancient Werewolves
Ancient Werewolves are the original evolutionary state of the Werewolf race and represent what their ancestors actually looked like on their home planet. Their existence was properly explained after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] transformed [[Edward Jonathan Elizabeth Lykos]] into one by restoring the relevant ancestral characteristics of his race.[4]
The principal difference between Ancient and modern Werewolves is the relationship between personal development and the pack. Modern Werewolves became increasingly dependent upon pack structure, particularly at the Alpha level. Ancient Werewolves possessed no such fundamental restriction: each individual could continue growing stronger through their own power without requiring a pack. Gathering beneath an Alpha still provided an additional enhancement, meaning the original race retained the benefits of pack cooperation without sacrificing independent growth.[4]
Victor explicitly compares this situation with the degradation suffered by Noble Vampires. Environmental conditions had effectively "nerfed" the later generations of both species, leaving their modern descendants with incomplete versions of the traits naturally possessed by their ancestors.[4]

### Powers and Abilities
Werewolf Physiology: Werewolves are physically oriented supernatural beings whose racial abilities emphasize their bodies, Bloodline, transformation, natural Energy and pack structure. The abilities below describe characteristics demonstrated as part of the race itself; individual Werewolves can possess additional Bloodline abilities, Blessings, Divinities or unique powers that should not automatically be attributed to the entire species.

#### Common Racial Abilities
- Superhuman Strength: Werewolves possess physical strength far beyond Humans and comparable supernatural species. [[Scathach]] explicitly identifies Vampires as a physically weaker breed than Werewolves, with Werewolves relying on their superior bodies while Vampires compensate through much stronger regeneration.[5] Even shortly after awakening her Bloodline, Leona possessed enough physical power to violently launch another Werewolf with a strike.[13]
- Superhuman Speed: Their enhanced musculature and supernatural physiology grant Werewolves movement speed vastly beyond Humans. Edward was capable of rapidly scaling a thirty-story building, while transformed Alphas such as Anderson can physically contest high-level Vampires in close combat.[20][21]
- Enhanced Reflexes: Werewolves possess reaction speed appropriate to their greatly enhanced movement. Experienced Alphas can react to attacks occurring too quickly for ordinary observers to properly perceive.[22][23]
- Enhanced Agility: Their balance, coordination and jumping capability scale with their enhanced physique. High-level Werewolves can launch themselves through enormous distances through pure physical force, with Leona's jump during Chapter 645 producing a crater beneath her.[12]
- Enhanced Stamina: Werewolves can sustain levels of physical exertion and combat that would rapidly exhaust ordinary Humans. Their bodies are naturally structured around prolonged direct combat rather than relying exclusively upon supernatural techniques.[5][24]
- Superhuman Endurance: Werewolves can continue functioning through extreme physical punishment. Their species' combat philosophy and physiology are centered on enduring direct physical exchanges that would incapacitate weaker supernatural creatures.[5]
- Superhuman Durability: Werewolf skin, muscle and bone possess supernatural resistance to physical damage. An ordinarily armed firearm was unable to penetrate the skin of a partially transformed Werewolf in Chapter 342, forcing [[Nero]] to rely upon ammunition containing Wolfsbane instead.[11] This durability varies enormously according to the Werewolf's rank, Bloodline and transformation and should not be treated as universal invulnerability.
- Regeneration: Werewolves heal physical injuries considerably faster than Humans. Their regeneration is, however, explicitly inferior to Vampiric regeneration.[5] Serious wounds can leave permanent scars, and destruction of vital structures such as the brain or heart can kill a Werewolf before their body can recover.[25][11]
- Enhanced Senses: Werewolves possess highly developed senses, particularly hearing and smell. During Chapter 181, it is explicitly stated that Werewolf hearing and smell are considerably superior to those of Vampires, to the point that a sufficiently powerful sonic disturbance causes the Werewolves present greater discomfort than the other supernatural beings.[26]
- Enhanced Hearing: Werewolves can perceive distant and subtle sounds inaccessible to ordinary Humans.[26]
- Enhanced Smell: Their sense of smell can identify supernatural races, track individuals and distinguish scents mixed together with other odors.[27][28]
- Night Vision: As nocturnally adapted supernatural predators, Werewolves can operate effectively in darkness without the visual limitations of ordinary Humans.
- Longevity: Modern Werewolves naturally live for centuries. Betas and Omegas possess a lifespan of approximately 500 years, while an Alpha normally possesses approximately 1,000 years, increasing with strength. The normal racial ceiling discussed for even extremely powerful Werewolves is approximately 3,000 years, although external Blessings or later evolutions can alter this restriction.[17][9]
- Shapeshifting: Werewolves can manifest different degrees of their wolf characteristics rather than being restricted to a single Human and single Werewolf form.[11][12]
- Partial Transformation: Individual characteristics such as claws, fur, ears, tails, altered eyes or transformed limbs can be manifested without changing the entire body.[11][12]
- Hybrid Form: Powerful Werewolves can assume humanoid combat forms combining Human proportions with extensive lupine characteristics, greatly increasing their physical performance.[12]
- Full Werewolf Transformation: More advanced Werewolves can completely manifest their Werewolf nature, producing much larger and more bestial bodies. Lunar conditions can assist modern Werewolves in reaching these states.[10]
- Natural Weaponry: Werewolf transformations produce supernatural claws and fangs capable of being used as primary melee weapons. Even individuals possessing only a small portion of Werewolf genetics can manifest wolf-like claws when that Bloodline becomes active.[29]
- Enhanced Instincts: Werewolves possess predatory instincts derived from their racial nature. Their behavior, threat recognition and emotional state can become increasingly animalistic as more of the Werewolf Bloodline is manifested.[29][12]
- Danger Intuition: Experienced Werewolves are capable of instinctively reacting to threats and hostile presences before consciously identifying every detail of the danger.
- Sound Manipulation (Roar): A transformed Werewolf can weaponize its roar as an intense sonic attack. The Werewolf elder encountered in Chapter 181 produced a roar loud enough to force supernatural beings around him to cover their ears, while Werewolf-derived characteristics later allowed Nero to release destructive force through a similar vocalization.[26][29]
- Statistics Amplification (Lunar Empowerment): The full moon greatly amplifies a Werewolf's supernatural characteristics and can facilitate stronger transformations.[10]
- Artificial Lunar Empowerment: Special supernatural talismans can reproduce the effects normally provided by a full moon, allowing Werewolves to transform and obtain the corresponding amplification even when the natural lunar condition is absent.[10]

#### Nature and Bloodline Abilities
- Energy Manipulation (Nature Energy): Werewolves possess an unusually intimate relationship with natural Energy. When fighting, they can coat their bodies in a supernatural Energy that dramatically elevates their physical characteristics. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] specifically recalls being taught that Werewolves naturally use this method to push their already formidable physiques to extreme levels, whereas comparable bodily reinforcement is considerably less natural for ordinary Vampires.[29]
- Statistics Amplification (Nature Energy Reinforcement): The natural Energy surrounding a Werewolf can be circulated through the body to reinforce its physical abilities without producing the same internal damage experienced by Vampires attempting comparable elemental reinforcement.[29]
- Nature Affinity: Werewolves are repeatedly described as creatures unusually close to nature. During Anderson's battle, Natashia identifies the Energy he uses as deriving from an aspect of nature and explicitly contrasts the natural orientation of Wolves with Vampires.[30] Roxanne later identifies the Energy reinforcing Leona as extremely natural and similar in character to the Energy of a World Tree, though less pure and mixed with the Werewolf Bloodline.[12]
- Elemental Manipulation / Elemental Affinity: Certain Werewolves develop natural affinities corresponding to aspects of nature. These abilities are individual rather than identical across the race. Anderson, for example, possesses an affinity producing white/light-like Energy, while other Werewolves can manifest different natural attributes.[30]
- Energy Projection: Werewolves possessing sufficiently developed elemental or Bloodline Energy can project it externally as destructive attacks. Anderson released a massive blast of his white natural Energy during his fight with Victor.[30]
- Flight: High-level Werewolves with sufficient control over their Bloodline Energy can remain airborne rather than relying entirely upon physical jumping. Leona identifies the ability as basic for a Wolf capable of properly controlling their Bloodline after entering her Hybrid Form and joining Victor in the air.[12]

#### Alpha Abilities
The following abilities derive specifically from the Alpha position and should not be attributed to every Beta or Omega.
- Statistics Amplification (Alpha Presence): The presence of an Alpha strengthens subordinate Werewolves belonging to the pack. During the attack involving Kaguya, the Werewolves themselves complain that the absence of an Alpha had left them dramatically weaker and believe their performance would have been fundamentally different had one been present.[18]
- Statistics Amplification (Pack Empowerment): An Alpha receives additional strength from members of their pack. The quantity and quality of followers both influence this enhancement. Edward explains that a sufficiently large personal pack would dramatically increase his own strength, while every Werewolf on Samar being considered part of Volk's pack gave the [[Lycaon|Werewolf King]] an enormous boost.[9]
- Command Inducement (Alpha Authority): Alphas possess supernatural authority over Betas recognized as members of their pack. Their commands carry more than conventional political authority and can compel subordinate Werewolves to obey.[19]
- Social Influencing / Dominance Inducement: Werewolf instincts respond strongly to displays of superior Alpha dominance. Powerful Alphas can provoke instinctive recognition, submission or hostility from other members of the race even before a formal command is issued.[31][32]
- Enhanced Physical Characteristics: High-level Alphas dramatically exceed ordinary members of the species. At the upper levels of the modern Werewolf hierarchy, physical feats considered extreme for ordinary supernatural creatures become comparatively routine.[33]

#### Werewolf Progenitor Abilities
The following abilities represent the racial Authority demonstrated by [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]] after becoming the current Werewolf Progenitor. They are not normal abilities possessed by ordinary Alphas.
- Racial Authority: As the Werewolf Progenitor and Alpha among Alphas, Tasha possesses authority over the structure of the Werewolf race itself. Her Soul became supernaturally connected to the Werewolves beneath her, transforming the ordinary pack principle into a race-wide system.[1]
- Racial Link: Werewolves connected to the Progenitor function through a hive-like network in which supernatural power is distributed throughout the Race rather than existing solely within the Progenitor's personal body.[1]
- Statistics Amplification (Progenitor Pack): Every Werewolf connected to Tasha contributes a percentage-based increase to her power. Unlike an ordinary Alpha, losing a follower removes only that additional contribution rather than permanently decreasing her natural base power. Chapter 849 explicitly contrasts this with ordinary Alphas, who can become drastically weaker after losing their packs.[1]
- Power Absorption: Tasha can directly withdraw supernatural power from a Werewolf connected to her racial Authority. She used this against Volk, drawing his accumulated strength out of his body and condensing it into a separate mass of power.[2]
- Raw Power Absorption: Unlike [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul absorption, this process grants Tasha raw supernatural power rather than automatically acquiring the victim's memories, experience or personal techniques.[2]
- Power Nullification / Power Removal: The Progenitor can strip a Werewolf of the supernatural power sustained through their racial connection. Tasha left Volk physically alive while removing the enormous strength he had possessed as King.[2]
- Power Bestowal: Tasha can use her Progenitor Authority to elevate another member of the race and grant them a higher position within the supernatural hierarchy.[6]
- Rank Manipulation: The Progenitor can directly alter the Alpha/Beta/Omega hierarchy rather than merely recognizing positions established through birth.[2][6]
- Alpha Bestowal: Tasha can select a Beta and elevate that individual into a new Alpha.[6]
- Omega Demotion: Tasha can expel a Werewolf and reduce them to Omega status, as demonstrated upon Volk.[2]
- Soul Manipulation (Racial Connection): Expelling a Werewolf from the Progenitor's network severs the supernatural thread connecting that individual's Soul to the racial source. Volk suffered direct damage from having this connection severed rather than merely losing a political title.[2]
- Power Suppression: A Werewolf completely cut off from the Progenitor's racial structure can be prevented from properly recovering or developing the supernatural strength previously provided by that connection.[2]
- Command Inducement (Racial Dominance): Tasha's status as the Alpha above all other Alphas allows her authority to affect even Werewolves who would themselves ordinarily stand at the top of independent packs. After her ascension, her words and presence caused surrounding Werewolves—including Alphas—to react instinctively to her superior position.[6]

### Weaknesses
- Mortality: Modern Werewolves are long-lived but mortal. Unlike Vampires, they possess a finite natural lifespan rather than biological immortality.[17][9]
- Limited Regeneration: Although Werewolves regenerate supernatural injuries, their healing is explicitly inferior to that of Vampires. Damage that a sufficiently powerful Vampire could regenerate can therefore remain permanently debilitating or fatal to a Werewolf.[5]
- Vital Organ Destruction: Werewolves cannot casually regenerate the destruction of every vital organ. Destruction of the brain is fatal, as demonstrated when a Werewolf was killed by a bullet through the back of the head.[11] Destruction or removal of the heart can likewise kill a Werewolf.[34]
- Decapitation: Complete destruction or separation of the head inflicts damage beyond ordinary Werewolf regeneration and is fatal under normal circumstances.
- Wolfsbane: Wolfsbane is specifically toxic to Werewolves. A projectile containing Wolfsbane caused a transformed Werewolf's arm to convulse and begin melting immediately upon penetration, forcing him to amputate the affected limb before the substance spread further.[11]
- Faith Energy: As Night Creatures, Werewolves can be injured by supernatural Faith and weapons specifically empowered to kill beings of their classification. The effectiveness depends upon the strength of the user and weapon rather than ordinary religious symbolism alone.
- Pack Dependence (Modern Alphas): Although the pack increases an Alpha's strength, it also creates a structural weakness. Ordinary Alphas lose portions of that power when followers are lost and can become vastly weaker if deprived of their entire pack.[1] Ancient Werewolves do not possess the same fundamental limitation.[4]
- Alpha Authority (Betas): Betas are susceptible to supernatural authority from the Alpha of their recognized pack, making direct disobedience considerably more difficult.[19]
- Lunar Dependence (Certain Transformations): Modern Werewolf transformations and power can be strongly affected by lunar conditions. The full moon provides substantial empowerment, while specialized talismans are used to reproduce that condition when necessary.[10] This limitation can be overcome by sufficiently evolved Werewolves and should not be treated as universal to Ancient Werewolves.
- Unawakened Bloodline: Naturally born Werewolves whose Bloodline has not yet awakened lack access to most of their supernatural racial abilities and can consequently possess vulnerabilities much closer to ordinary Humans.

### Comparison with Vampires
Werewolves and Vampires are frequently presented as biological and cultural opposites. In direct physical capability, Werewolves generally possess the superior natural bodies. [[Scathach]] explicitly states that Vampires are physically weaker than Werewolves, while emphasizing that Vampires compensate with regeneration greatly superior to that of Wolves.[5]
Their broader development follows the same contrast. Werewolves naturally reinforce their bodies with racial Energy and derive enormous advantages from cooperation, hierarchy and packs.[29][1] Vampires, by comparison, historically emphasize individual power accumulation much more heavily. Chapter 849 directly describes numbers and pack structure as a defining characteristic of Werewolves while contrasting it with the more individually centered nature of Vampires.[1]
Their senses also differ. Although both species possess supernatural perception, Werewolf hearing and smell are specifically identified as superior to those of ordinary Vampires.[26] Vampires instead possess advantages such as considerably stronger regeneration and numerous Bloodline-specific abilities.
This rivalry should not be interpreted as every individual Werewolf automatically defeating every individual Vampire. Rank, age, Bloodline, training, transformation, special abilities and individual talent can create enormous differences between members of either race.

### Samar
Main article: Samar
Samar is the primary homeland and political center of modern Werewolf civilization. The world possesses substantially stronger gravity than Earth and developed a harsh society in which personal strength, Bloodline, pack hierarchy and the authority of the Werewolf monarchy historically played major roles.[35][36]
For most of the known era, Samar was ruled jointly by [[Volk Fenrir]] and [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]]. Volk functioned as the recognized [[Lycaon|Werewolf King]] and one of the greatest Alphas of the modern race, while Tasha maintained her own considerable political and military influence. Their rule ended after Tasha's ascension to Werewolf Progenitor and Volk's subsequent expulsion from the racial hierarchy.[1][2]
Samar is also sustained by [[Aurora Seraphina Evergreen]], its World Tree, whose existence makes the planet an important supernatural center independently of Werewolf civilization.[37]

### Myths and Misconceptions
- Werewolves are cursed Humans: False. Werewolves are an independent supernatural species with their own ancestral race, Bloodline and evolutionary history. Humans can be converted into Werewolves, but this does not make the race itself a Human curse.[13][4]
- Every Werewolf requires a full moon to exist or transform: False. The Moon strongly affects the race and can enhance transformation, but Werewolves possess their supernatural physiology independently of the natural full moon. Advanced or ancestral individuals can overcome even more of the lunar limitations.[10][4]
- Every Werewolf possesses the same natural element: False. The race has a broad affinity with nature, but the specific attribute manifested by an individual varies according to Bloodline and personal characteristics. Anderson's light-like Energy is an individual expression rather than the universal Energy of every Werewolf.[30]
- Werewolves are physically superior to Vampires in every possible category: False. Their natural physique is explicitly stronger, but Vampires possess far superior regeneration and individual Bloodlines can completely alter the result of a confrontation.[5]
- Modern Werewolves represent the complete ancestral race: False. Chapter 1117 directly identifies modern Werewolves as environmentally diminished descendants of the Ancient Werewolves that existed on their original home planet.[4]

### Trivia
- Werewolves and Noble Vampires underwent comparable evolutionary degradation after their ancestors developed in environments different from those of their original worlds.[4]
- Ancient Werewolves retain the cooperative advantages of a pack while removing the modern race's restriction requiring pack dependence for personal growth.[4]
- The current Progenitor system means the Alpha/Beta/Omega hierarchy is no longer determined entirely by birth. Tasha can directly elevate a Beta into an Alpha through her Authority.[6]
- Volk was historically regarded as the supreme Werewolf ruler and possessed the traits of a True Alpha, but Tasha's ascension established a much more fundamental form of authority by making her the actual Progenitor of the race.[1][2]
- The Werewolf Progenitor's Soul operates according to principles nearly opposite to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s. Victor's nature emphasizes absorbing and accumulating power within himself, while the Werewolf Progenitor distributes power through a network of followers and becomes stronger through that collective structure.[1]
