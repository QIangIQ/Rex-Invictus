**[[Cyclopes]]** are one-eyed giants. [[Hesiod]]'s elder Cyclopes, [[Arges]], [[Brontes]] and [[Steropes]], were sons of [[Gaia]] and [[Ouranos]] who forged [[Zeus]]'s thunderbolt; [[Apollo]] killed them after [[Zeus]] struck down [[Asclepius]]. Homer's Cyclopes are a lawless shepherd race, including Polyphemus, son of [[Poseidon]], blinded by Odysseus. In [[Percy Jackson and the Olympians]] [[Percy Jackson|Percy]]'s half-brother Tyson is a young Cyclops who becomes a general of [[Poseidon]]'s forges.
