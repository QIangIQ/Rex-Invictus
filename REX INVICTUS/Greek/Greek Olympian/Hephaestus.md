**[[Hephaestus]]** is the [[Olympians|Olympian]] god of the forge, fire, metalworking, craftsmen and technology. He is the son of [[Hera]] (alone, or with [[Zeus]]), husband of [[Aphrodite]] in most myths and maker of the gods' weapons. His Roman counterpart is [[Vulcan]]. In the Empire he is the [[Divine Being 'Forgemaester'|Forgemaester]], and the [[Forge of Hephaestus]] drives Imperial industry.

## Greek History (IRL)

**Birth and fall.** [[Hera]] bore him and, disgusted by his lameness, threw him from [[Olympus]]. He fell into the sea and was raised by [[Thetis]] and Eurynome. In another version [[Zeus]] threw him down for siding with Hera, and he fell for a full day onto the island of Lemnos. He took revenge on Hera with a golden throne that trapped her until [[Dionysus]] brought him back to Olympus drunk.

**Works.** He forged the [[Ageis|aegis]], [[Achilles]]' armor, [[Hermes]]' winged helmet, the chariot of [[Helios]], the chains of [[Prometheus]], Pandora (the first woman) and the bronze giant Talos. He built the palaces of Olympus and staffed his forge with golden automaton maidens. His forges lay under volcanoes such as Etna.

**Marriage.** Married to [[Aphrodite]], he caught her with [[Ares]] in an invisible net and exposed them. He split Zeus' head with an axe to release [[Athena]].

**Primary sources:** Homer's *Iliad* XVIII, *Odyssey* VIII, Hesiod, the *Homeric Hymn to Hephaestus*.

Hephaestus is an Olympian God and a divinely specialized Forge God formerly belonging to the [[Greek Pantheon]]. For most of his existence he served as [[Olympus]]' principal blacksmith, producing Divine Artifacts, weapons and specialized equipment for the Greek Gods. His craftsmanship was regarded as fundamentally different from that of an ordinary God who simply learned smithing; Hephaestus' very Divine specialization was centered around creation and forging.[2]
Unlike the majority of the Olympians, Hephaestus refused to participate in the war against [[Victor Elderblood]] and remained within his forge while Olympus collapsed. His refusal was not born from loyalty to Victor at the time, but from centuries of resentment toward [[Zeus]] and the Olympian Gods who had exploited both him and his talent. After conquering Olympus, Victor personally approached Hephaestus and commissioned restraints capable of suppressing the surviving Gods. Rather than enslaving the Forge God, Victor provided him with extraordinary Divine materials, created a forge containing his own Blood Dragon Progenitor Fire and promised Hephaestus freedom so long as he always gave his best work. Deeply affected by Victor's treatment of him, Hephaestus voluntarily swore an Oath of Eternal Servitude and dedicated his craft to Victor, becoming one of the earliest Olympian Gods to willingly enter Victor's faction.[3]
He subsequently developed from an Olympian blacksmith into one of the principal supernatural craftsmen supporting Victor's rapidly expanding civilization. His later work included Divine weapons for Victor's family and soldiers, the upgraded Tower of Nightmares, enormous dimensional-storage artifacts and engineering components for projects operating on Galactic scales.[4][5][6][1][7][8]

### Physical Appearance
Such a perfect job, Hephaestus... My son, though you were born so ugly, it is a fact that your hands are gifts given by primordial chaos itself... It is a pity that you are ugly
—[[Hera]]'s thoughts about Hephaestus, Chapter 746
The novel has never provided an especially detailed physical description of Hephaestus. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] first personally encountered him, Hephaestus was simply seen sitting inside his forge while staring into the embers of its fire.[3] His mother, Hera, repeatedly considered him physically ugly despite regarding his hands and craftsmanship as extraordinary gifts.[9]
The most distinctive expression associated with Hephaestus appears when his attention is captured by an interesting creation or material. Victor later recognized the same intensely focused, almost manic expression in [[Dún Scáith|Dun Scaith]], immediately comparing her craft-obsessed eyes to those he had previously seen in Hephaestus.[10]

### Personality and Traits
Hephaestus is first and foremost a craftsman. His pride is attached far more strongly to the quality of his creations than to Olympian prestige, and he is unwilling to deliberately produce something poor or incomplete merely to finish a commission faster. His work for the Amazon Queen Callisto was used as an example of this mentality: although the armor was designed for a mortal lineage rather than a God, Hephaestus still created an extraordinarily sophisticated artifact because his pride would not permit him to make something "half-assed."[11]
His enthusiasm becomes especially apparent when he encounters unusual materials or unprecedented engineering problems. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] produced a massive quantity of Divine metal for his first commission, Hephaestus became visibly fascinated by the resources and was willing to work without conventional payment simply for the opportunity to use them. The Blood Dragon forge Victor subsequently created fascinated him even further because it gave Hephaestus access to a form of flame and craftsmanship impossible within his former Olympian environment.[3]
Behind that passion was an enormous amount of resentment. Hephaestus did not hate the Dimension of [[Olympus]] itself; he considered the Realm innocent and recognized the immense resources it still possessed. What he despised were many of the Beings who inhabited and ruled it. After thousands of years of being exploited and looked down upon, he wanted [[Zeus]] dead and took satisfaction in seeing the arrogant Olympians reduced to the same helpless position in which they had repeatedly placed him.[3] Victor later rejected the idea that Hephaestus had already emotionally matured simply because he behaved calmly, explaining that the Forge God was still carrying a tremendous amount of understandable hatred.[12]
Victor's treatment of him produced an unusually strong response precisely because it contrasted with Olympus. Hephaestus expected another powerful ruler to simply exploit his abilities, yet Victor gave him materials, a superior forge, creative freedom and only demanded that anything Hephaestus made for him represent the craftsman's best effort. Hephaestus interpreted that behavior as the conduct of a true King and voluntarily offered far more loyalty than Victor had demanded.[3]
This devotion did not eliminate his independent personality. Even much later, Hephaestus remained excited by difficult technical problems and was willing to recommend other specialists when doing so would improve the final result. During the construction of the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s enormous temporal mechanism, for example, Hephaestus personally recommended involving Velnorah because her Technomancy could accelerate construction. His interest was not threatened by another expert's involvement; he was primarily concerned with seeing the project successfully brought into existence.[8]

### Synopsis

#### A New Pillar
Hephaestus' influence entered the story long before his physical debut. One of the Divine Artifacts encountered by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was a replicated [[Hammer of Hephaestus]], a copy of the Forge God's original hammer. Even the imitation possessed considerable destructive power and could release Divine fire, although Victor immediately recognized that it was inferior to what the genuine artifact should have been capable of doing.[13]
Hephaestus was later discussed during [[Aphrodite]]'s explanation of her past. Contrary to the traditional myth in which Aphrodite is presented as Hephaestus' wife, the novel's continuity establishes that [[Zeus]] attempted to force Aphrodite to marry Hephaestus and she refused the arrangement.[14]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
The [[Hammer of Hephaestus]] became important again when [[Aphrodite]] hired Esther to investigate the theft of the original artifact. A Witch operating in Greece had obtained Hephaestus' true hammer and produced numerous replicas, explaining why copies had begun appearing among supernatural combatants.[15] [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually acquired sixty-nine copied Hammers of Hephaestus. Although they were inferior to the genuine Divine Artifact, every copy retained substantial destructive power, enough that Victor considered using them as supernatural projectiles or missiles and later offered the collection as potentially useful weaponry.[16]

#### Demonic War
During the [[Supernatural Gathering]], another of Hephaestus' old creations became relevant: [[Alsyr]], the Divine chains once associated with [[Aphrodite]]. The artifact had been created during the old dispute surrounding [[Zeus]]' attempt to force Aphrodite into marriage with Hephaestus. The chains possessed an especially dangerous anti-divine property and were capable of drastically suppressing a God's power, making them useful even against extremely powerful Divine targets.[17]
The Gathering also reinforced Hephaestus' standing as something more than a God who happened to understand smithing. When Divine professions were discussed, Hephaestus was used as an example of a Divinely Specialized craftsman: while lesser Gods could learn trades and improve through experience, a deity whose Divinity directly corresponded to a craft operated on an entirely different level. Several Divine weapons carried by the Greek refugees were likewise products of Hephaestus' forge.[2]
One of his most sophisticated older creations was revealed through Callisto. Hephaestus had produced an armor set for the first Amazon Queen composed of armor, sword and shield. The equipment incorporated a Bloodline-based restriction and could absorb and transform Energy according to the user's needs. Its construction blurred the usual distinction between Mortal and Divine Artifacts, possessing characteristics expected from a Divine Artifact despite having been designed for a mortal lineage. The work was explicitly treated as the sort of achievement only a Forge God of Hephaestus' caliber could create.[11]

#### King Among Kings
Shortly before the fall of [[Olympus]], [[Hera]] retrieved another old Hephaestian weapon: a golden scythe personally forged for her. Despite constantly insulting her son's appearance, Hera internally acknowledged the perfection of his work and regarded his hands as an extraordinary gift.[9]
Hephaestus himself refused to participate in the final conflict against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. He remained in his forge and observed the war, having no desire to defend [[Zeus]] or the Olympians who had exploited him for thousands of years. When Victor conquered the Pantheon and personally appeared before him, the Forge God was initially terrified; however, Victor did not arrive to punish him. Instead, he needed someone capable of creating restraints for the surviving Olympian Gods.[3]
Hephaestus agreed but explained that the commission required appropriate materials. Victor responded by extracting an enormous mass of Divine metal and giving it to him. He then constructed a new Divine forge, inscribed it through Dragon Language and filled it with his own violet-crimson Blood Dragon Progenitor fire. The resulting forge gave Hephaestus access to Victor's personal flame—an exceptionally destructive power capable of producing weapons dangerous even to Gods and other immortal Beings.[3][18]
Victor nevertheless placed strict conditions upon the forge. Anything produced through his Dragon Fire for Victor's faction was to remain under his authority, with future safeguards preventing hostile individuals from stealing those creations or using them against his family. Outside those restrictions, Victor gave Hephaestus considerable freedom and asked only that he put his genuine effort into every commission.[3]
Having expected Victor to treat him as another exploitable craftsman, Hephaestus was profoundly affected by the offer. He knelt before Victor and voluntarily swore an Oath of Eternal Servitude, dedicating his Art and Craft to him. The oath was recognized at an existential level and was intended to endure until the End of Time, remaining valid even through Victor's death or reincarnation. Victor accepted his loyalty and welcomed Hephaestus into his family and faction.[3]
Hephaestus immediately justified Victor's confidence. Within only a few hours he produced a stock of deceptively simple-looking Divine shackles capable of preventing captured Gods from properly accessing their Divinities. Victor supplemented the construction with Dragon Language, and the restraints were used to secure surviving high-level Olympians such as [[Persephone]], [[Poseidon]], [[Athena]] and [[Artemis]].[19]
His forge was soon used for another major project. Victor and [[Thanatos]] possessed a crystallized fragment derived from the [[Greek Underworld]]'s core and needed a material capable of safely containing its Death-related properties. Hephaestus was brought into the project as the specialist responsible for refining and forging the necessary components. The completed staff became an important religious and spiritual artifact associated with [[Valeria Alekerth]] and the [[Blood God Religion]], combining the work of Victor, Thanatos and Hephaestus.[18]
Hephaestus remained emotionally scarred by his former life even after joining Victor. When Persephone suggested that the Forge God appeared mature, Victor corrected her: Hephaestus had not simply overcome the past—he was still carrying an enormous amount of hatred, even if that hatred was understandable after his treatment by Olympus.[12]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became the Dragon God of [[Chaos]], Hephaestus' role expanded alongside the growing scale of Victor's family. He and Victor jointly created a pair of [[Black Deagles]] for [[Nero Elderblood]]. These top-grade Divine firearms no longer depended upon conventional ammunition; they transformed the wielder's Energy directly into attacks and could shift between rapid condensed projectiles and enormously more powerful concentrated blasts. Their output was sufficiently extreme that Victor initially restricted [[Nero]] to a fraction of their maximum capacity while she learned to use them safely.[4]
Hephaestus and Victor subsequently equipped the Valkyries serving Victor's family with individualized Divine weapons rather than mass-produced equipment. The resulting arsenal included paired swords, firearms, spears, shields, greatswords, staffs, halberds and other weapons created according to each Valkyrie's preferred fighting style. Their edge and supernatural output were far beyond ordinary Divine equipment, reflecting the combined craftsmanship of a Forge God and Victor's Dragon-based materials and power.[5]
Victor continued collaborating with Hephaestus on weapon development afterward. When he later encountered [[Dún Scáith|Dun Scaith]]'s obsessive enthusiasm for crafting, Victor immediately recognized the expression because he had previously seen the same kind of craft mania in Hephaestus. Victor also made clear that several of his successful weapon projects were collaborative works rather than creations he had developed alone.[10]
One of Hephaestus' largest achievements during this period was the upgraded Tower of Nightmares. The later Growth Artifact was explicitly credited to Hephaestus and Victor as its principal creators, with contributions from Beings such as [[Thanatos]], [[Fenrir]] and [[Typhon]]. Unlike an ordinary training facility, the Tower incorporated multiple high-level supernatural principles and allowed users to undergo enormously prolonged cycles of combat, death, rebirth and growth while comparatively little Time passed outside. In its upgraded configuration, approximately one thousand years could be experienced internally while only seven days passed externally.[6]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
By the era of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s children, Hephaestus had become sufficiently integrated into the Elderblood family that the Emperor's daughters casually referred to him as "Uncle Hephaestus."[1]
Together with Victor and Natalia, Hephaestus created the family's advanced dimensional-storage artifacts. Even the smaller versions given to Victor's daughters contained internal spaces extending approximately 2,500 kilometers, while larger versions intended for the senior members of the family could contain areas comparable to a large country and were capable of supporting living beings. The devices could alter their outward form into bracelets, earrings, weapons or other preferred shapes while remaining extraordinarily difficult to destroy because of the Divine and Demonic materials involved in their construction.[1]
As the Elderblood civilization advanced, Hephaestus' expertise stopped being limited to personal weapons. His craftsmanship became one of the technical foundations available for large-scale Imperial projects, working alongside the Empire's Runic development, Velnorah's technology, the Nightmare Tower and other systems that Victor's civilization had accumulated.[20]
This transition became especially clear during preparations for the Empire's enormous temporal-acceleration project. Victor assembled specialists from across his civilization to create a mechanism capable of isolating and accelerating an immense Galactic region. Hephaestus was assigned to the engineering group alongside figures such as [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], [[Dún Scáith|Dun Scaith]], [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]], Evie Moriarty and [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]], helping design the physical mechanism that would function like a vast enclosing structure around the affected territory.[7]
During development, Hephaestus personally recommended that Velnorah be brought more deeply into construction because her Technomancy could greatly improve the process. He estimated that cooperation between their respective specialties could reduce a project expected to require roughly three years to approximately two. Even after everything he had already built, the prospect of seeing an unprecedented mechanism come to life still caused the Forge God to react with the same excitement that had defined him since his first meeting with Victor.[8]

### Skills and Abilities
As a Major Olympian God, Hephaestus possesses the supernatural characteristics expected of a powerful Divine Being. His defining specialty, however, is not direct combat but the creation of artifacts and supernatural technology.
- God Physiology: Hephaestus is an ancient Olympian God possessing [[Divine Energy]], immortality and the supernatural constitution associated with the Gods.[2]
- Divinely Specialized Craftsmanship: Unlike a normal deity who merely learns a craft, Hephaestus is a God whose Divine specialization is directly tied to forging. His natural skill operates on a level ordinary supernatural blacksmiths cannot reproduce through experience alone.[2]
- Master Blacksmithing: Hephaestus can forge weapons, armor, restraints and other equipment for Gods and high-level supernatural Beings. His creations range from conventional Divine weapons to highly specialized artifacts designed around particular users or Bloodlines.[11][19]
- Divine Artifact Creation: He can create genuine Divine Artifacts with supernatural functions far beyond simple durability or sharpness, including Divinity-suppressing restraints and Energy-adaptive equipment.[17][11][19]
- Artifact Enchantment: Hephaestus can incorporate supernatural conditions, Bloodline restrictions, Energy interactions and other specialized properties into equipment rather than merely forging physical weapons.[11]
- Energy Adaptation: Several of his creations can interact directly with their users' Energy. Callisto's equipment could absorb and transform Energy, while the [[Black Deagles]] convert the wielder's own Energy into ammunition and variable-output attacks.[11][4]
- Supernatural Materials Expertise: Hephaestus is capable of processing Divine metals and other substances that ordinary craftsmen would be incapable of safely manipulating. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] specifically relied upon him when material had to be refined around a fragment of the [[Greek Underworld]]'s core.[3][18]
- Dragon-Fire Forging: After joining Victor, Hephaestus gained controlled access to a forge containing Victor's Blood Dragon Progenitor Fire. Equipment produced through this forge can possess properties dangerous even to Gods and other immortal Beings, although the flame and resulting artifacts remain subject to Victor's restrictions.[3][18]
- Runic and Restriction Integration: While Victor is responsible for the Dragon Language used in their earliest joint work, Hephaestus is capable of incorporating supernatural inscriptions and restrictions into the physical construction of his artifacts, including Bloodline-linked equipment and anti-divine restraints.[11][19]
- Dimensional Engineering: Hephaestus helped create family artifacts containing enormous independent storage spaces capable of extending thousands of kilometers and, in larger models, sustaining living beings.[1]
- Growth-Artifact Engineering: He was one of the principal creators of the upgraded Tower of Nightmares, an artifact integrating multiple Divine Concepts, Soul mechanics and extreme Time dilation into a functional training system.[6]
- Large-Scale Engineering: By the Imperial era, Hephaestus could contribute to mechanisms operating on astronomical scales rather than merely individual weapons, including the infrastructure required for Victor's Galactic temporal-acceleration project.[7][8]
- Technical Collaboration: Hephaestus readily works with specialists whose expertise exceeds his own in particular fields. His major later creations involved collaboration with Victor, Natalia, [[Thanatos]], Velnorah and numerous other Divine, magical and technological experts.[18][6][1][8]

### The Dragon-Fire Forge
After recruiting Hephaestus, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] did not simply give him ordinary workshop equipment. He created a new forge from Divine materials, engraved it through Dragon Language and supplied it with his own Blood Dragon Progenitor Fire.[3]
The fire within this forge was an extension of Victor's own power rather than a flame naturally belonging to Hephaestus. It incorporated exceptionally destructive properties associated with Victor's nature and could consequently be used to forge artifacts capable of seriously affecting Gods and other immortal Beings.[18]
Victor deliberately retained authority over anything commissioned through this resource. Hephaestus could use the forge and exercise tremendous creative freedom, but artifacts created for Victor's faction were not to be distributed without permission, and Victor intended to add restrictions preventing stolen weapons from being turned against his family.[3]
The forge became a major turning point in Hephaestus' career. [[Olympus]] had possessed enormous supernatural resources, but Victor provided him with materials, flames and later collaborators that allowed his craft to move far beyond the equipment he had traditionally created for the Greek Gods.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is Hephaestus' sworn lord and the person responsible for radically changing the direction of his existence. Hephaestus initially expected Victor to exploit him in the same manner as [[Olympus]], especially after watching him conquer the [[Greek Pantheon]]. Instead, Victor asked for his craftsmanship, supplied materials and a superior forge, imposed only security restrictions and otherwise allowed him to pursue his work freely.[3]
The offer caused Hephaestus to regard Victor as a true King. Without being commanded to do so, he swore an Eternal Oath dedicating his Art and Craft to Victor and binding his service until the End of Time. Victor accepted him into his family rather than treating him merely as captured Olympian labor.[3]
Their relationship later evolved into an extensive creative partnership. Victor and Hephaestus jointly developed Divine firearms, Valkyrie weapons, the upgraded Tower of Nightmares and numerous pieces of supernatural equipment, while Hephaestus eventually became one of the major craftsmen supporting the Elderblood civilization itself.[4][5][6]

#### Hera
[[Hera]] is Hephaestus' mother. Their relationship was far from affectionate. Hera repeatedly considered her son ugly and openly demeaned his appearance, yet simultaneously possessed complete confidence in his craftsmanship. When using the golden scythe he had forged for her, she internally acknowledged that his hands possessed a nearly unmatched gift and considered the weapon's workmanship perfect.[9]

#### Zeus and the Olympians
Hephaestus' relationship with his father and many of the Olympians was dominated by resentment. He believed they had exploited him and his abilities for thousands of years while treating him as inferior. Consequently, he felt no loyalty toward the Olympian regime when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] invaded and refused to join their final war.[3]
Rather than mourning [[Zeus]]' defeat, Hephaestus had wanted his father dead and took a degree of satisfaction in seeing the arrogant Gods who had exploited him reduced to a powerless condition. Although joining Victor gave him freedom from [[Olympus]], the emotional consequences of those millennia did not immediately disappear.[3][12]

#### Aphrodite Elderblood
Despite the common mythological tradition connecting Hephaestus and [[Aphrodite]] through marriage, they were never married in the novel's continuity. [[Zeus]] attempted to arrange the marriage, but Aphrodite rejected it.[14]
Their past nevertheless involved conflict. Hephaestus created [[Alsyr]], the Divine chains later retained by Aphrodite, during events surrounding Aphrodite and [[Ares]]. The artifact ultimately became one of the old Olympian treasures used during the [[Supernatural Gathering]].[17]

### Trivia
- Hephaestus is one of the earliest Olympian Gods to willingly swear permanent loyalty to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] after the conquest of [[Olympus]].[3]
- His Oath of Eternal Servitude was considerably more extreme than the agreement Victor originally requested. Victor only demanded Hephaestus' best effort and loyalty regarding commissioned artifacts; Hephaestus voluntarily escalated this into lifelong—and potentially reincarnation-spanning—service.[3]
- Victor's daughters eventually became comfortable enough with Hephaestus to refer to him as "Uncle Hephaestus."[1]
- Hephaestus' obsession with craftsmanship is strong enough that Victor later used him as his immediate point of comparison when observing [[Dún Scáith|Dun Scaith]]'s craft mania.[10]
- The large number of copied Hammers of Hephaestus should not be interpreted as Hephaestus personally mass-producing his own Divine Artifact. The original was stolen and copied by another party.[15][16]
- The novel deliberately differs from one of the best-known versions of Greek mythology regarding [[Aphrodite]]: [[Zeus]] attempted to force a marriage between Aphrodite and Hephaestus, but Aphrodite refused and therefore was never Hephaestus' wife in the story's continuity.[14]
- Although Hephaestus began as a traditional Divine blacksmith, his work under Victor eventually expanded into dimensional engineering, growth artifacts and Galactic-scale infrastructure.[6][1][7]
