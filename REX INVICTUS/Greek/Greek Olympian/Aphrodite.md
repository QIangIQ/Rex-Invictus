**[[Aphrodite]]** is the [[Olympians|Olympian]] goddess of love, beauty, desire and procreation. In Hesiod she rose from the sea foam around the severed parts of [[Ouranos]]; in Homer she is the daughter of [[Zeus]] and Dione. She is the wife of [[Hephaestus]], lover of [[Ares]] and mother of [[Eros]], [[Phobos]], [[Deimos]], Harmonia and Aeneas. Her Roman counterpart is Venus. In the Empire she is the [[Divine Being 'Great Love'|Great Love]], "Mother Majesty" of the [[Dove's Fleet]].

## Greek History (IRL)

**Birth.** When [[Kronos]] castrated [[Ouranos]], the foam around the flesh produced Aphrodite, who came ashore at Cyprus or Cythera.

**Myths.** [[Zeus]] married her to [[Hephaestus]] to prevent rivalry. She won the golden apple in the Judgment of [[Paris]] by promising him Helen, triggering the [[Trojan War]], and protected Paris and her son Aeneas. She loved Adonis, killed by a boar, and Anchises. She cursed Hippolytus and Psyche's sisters, and [[Eros]] married Psyche. Her magic girdle compelled desire.

**Cult.** Major sanctuaries at Paphos and Cythera; Corinth was devoted to her. Symbols: dove, swan, sparrow, myrtle, rose and the scallop shell.

**Primary sources:** Hesiod, Homer, *Homeric Hymns* 5, 6, 10, Apuleius.

#### Appearance
- **Veiled:** her divine charm "had been drawn out and strengthened over eighteen thousand years of worship until it was no longer something a mortal could survive looking at directly". Even veiled it presses "like heat from an open oven". Young officers who glance at her lose minutes staring, flushed ([[Ch23 - Interlude I|Ch23]]).
- **The neural wire:**
  - A single wire "snaked out" from the nape of her neck, "slow and sinuous", tipped with a polished bronze socket that plugs into her throne.
  - Its colours follow her mood: pink when pleased; soft grey-blue when chastened; gold when delighted or excited; deep glowing rose when sensing chaos; white when thoughtful or shocked; steady quiet blue when listening and grieving; "the soft deep blue of the sea at dusk" through tears.
  - Unplugged, she wears it "coiled loosely over one shoulder like a pet snake".
- **The mechanical arm:** she descends from the ceiling iris on "a long, jointed thing of bronze and ivory, segmented like the spine of some enormous animal". She rides it "the way a queen rides a palanquin, reclining, one hand trailing lazily".
- **The throne:** rose-gold and mother-of-pearl, shaped like an open scallop shell. It "flowed around her body" to fit her.
- **Gown:** pale. When she walks through [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] mud, "it did not get muddy".
- **Voice:** "low and warm and faintly amused", and "sounded like it was standing right beside her, speaking into her ear". She giggles, "a girlish, bubbling sound".

#### Personality
- **Whimsical, childlike and theatrical.**
  - Her opening line is "Good evening, my darlings… Mother is here."
  - She loves "the numbers" and tea, and scolds the cherubs for "zooming about, it's rude."
  - She claps with delight at twelve thousand survey squads, and admits [[Athena]] was furious and that she'll give the scholars back: "Most of them."
- **Tactless, then contrite.** On arrival, telling the officers to sit, she quips "It's like some died in here!" When Sevrin reminds her, "Approximately four million people", she says "That was a silly thing for me to say, wasn't it? Val-Val, remind me not to say silly things."
- **Profoundly compassionate.** She insists every one of the 3,998,050 names be read aloud before they go to the Wall: about 92 days, read by a hundred of her own choristers. "Her Majesty feels that four million people should not go into the stone without anyone having said their names." She walks alone to the wound, asks Tullia how many legionaries she has lost (1,104) and whether she knew their names, and weeps: "Then you know," … "why I came."
- **Terrifyingly ruthless in protection.** "I want it erased from the pit so thoroughly that [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] forgets how to make it. I want it gone. I want its name gone. I want its mother to forget she ever had it." Her Spears grin.
- **Perceptive, a reader of hearts.** She knows the monsters fear "what went out" of the wound, not the wound itself. She smells "[[Chaos]]… On the other side. A great deal of it." She senses "a great deal of" love there too.
- **Teasing intimacy with [[Artemis]].** "Sister-in-law… Hello, hello. You came quickly." She teases Artemis about having been "there" 14,000 years ago and slips into "Art– ahem… The Director".
- **Hopeful.** When the verdict confirms the Lord Imperator, she "began, very quietly, to laugh", and her wire pulses "like a heart beating". She orders the line held "until the door opens again".

#### Debut
Chapter 246
If you're going to destroy the Pantheon I've lived with my entire life, I want to be present to see it. I don't care, I have no attachments, you are my home now Darling... But... I want to see it.
—Aphrodite to [[Victor Elderblood]], Chapter 796
Aphrodite Elderblood, also known by her Roman name Venus and formerly as Aphrodite Alucard, is an ancient Goddess, a Second-Generation Primordial Goddess originating from [[Ouranos]], a former member of the Twelve Olympians of the [[Greek Pantheon]], and a current Blood Dragon Goddess belonging to Victor Elderblood's True Dragon lineage. She is one of Victor's wives and among the most conceptually developed Goddesses of Dragons Nest. Although historically celebrated above all as the Goddess of Beauty, her Divine existence encompasses the major Divinities of Love, Beauty and Sexuality, alongside lesser authorities associated with Pleasure, Joy, Perpetuation of Life and War.[2][3][4]
Aphrodite is unusual even among the ancient Gods because her original existence directly descends from one of the primordial foundations of the Greek Pantheon. She emerged from the severed reproductive portion of Ouranos following his mutilation, making her a member of the ancient Divine generation associated with the [[Titans]] rather than a conventional daughter of [[Zeus]]. She existed for billions of years before encountering Victor and had already developed an enormous degree of authority over Beauty, attraction, Love and Sexuality. Her Divine nature made her dangerous even when she did not deliberately employ hostile abilities: the mere presence of her Beauty could interfere with the emotions and perceptions of beings lacking adequate resistance.[2][5]
Despite her reputation for promiscuity and her extensive history of relationships, Aphrodite spent most of her ancient existence without understanding Love as deeply as she eventually would. Her growing attachment to Victor became the decisive event in her personal and conceptual development. Genuine romantic feelings awakened a stronger expression of Love than she had previously demonstrated, eventually allowing the Goddess historically identified with Beauty to approach the authority associated with much older and more fundamental Divine existences. Nyx recognized this development during the Greek conflict, when Aphrodite's Love approached the level associated with First-Generation [[Primordial Gods]].[6][7]
Her transformation into a True Dragon greatly accelerated that progression without replacing her original Godhood. Aphrodite retained her ancient Divine Concepts while receiving the vastly superior physical, spiritual and energetic foundation of Victor's Blood Dragon lineage. After thousands of subjective years of further development, she attained the status of a High-Level Dragon Goddess and was explicitly described as almost becoming a Primordial Being of Love. The distinction is important: the statement establishes that she was approaching a higher conceptual state, not that she had already completed the transformation into a fully developed Primordial Being governing Love throughout Reality.[8][1]
Following the establishment of the [[Elderblood Empire]], Aphrodite became one of the principal Goddesses responsible for its reproductive and demographic development. Her Blessings, together with those of Goddesses associated with Fertility, Life, Motherhood, Marriage and related Concepts, contributed to an extraordinary expansion of Imperial populations during the Empire's accelerated developmental periods. She also received a world on which the surviving traditions, temples and cultural identity of the Greek Gods could be preserved under the Elderblood Empire rather than restored beneath Zeus's former political system.[9][10][11]
Aphrodite is the biological mother of [[Eros]], her son with [[Ares]], and [[Irene Elderblood]], her daughter with Victor. Her later relationship with Eros deteriorated irreparably when he repeatedly ignored her warnings concerning his conduct and violated the boundaries she expected him to respect within her new family. Aphrodite eventually disowned him, demonstrating how radically her priorities had changed since the era when she considered unrestricted desire a defining feature of her existence.[12][13]

### Physical Appearance
Aphrodite's appearance is an expression of her Divinity rather than ordinary biological attractiveness. Beauty is one of the fundamental Concepts composing her Divine existence, making her presence supernaturally captivating even without deliberate activation of Charm. Her appearance can influence how others perceive attractiveness, desire and emotional attachment, which is why [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] originally treated her as a threat despite acknowledging her extraordinary beauty.[2]
In her natural humanoid form, Aphrodite possesses exceptionally long pink hair that reaches approximately to the floor, luminous pink eyes, pale skin and a mature, proportionate figure. Her facial features are extraordinarily refined, and her body possesses the supernatural perfection associated with the Goddess of Beauty. She frequently wears flowing clothing inspired by ancient Greek fashion, although she is equally comfortable wearing modern dresses and casual clothing when interacting with members of Victor's family.[2]
Aphrodite's body remains permanently youthful despite her immense chronological age. She describes herself as possessing the appearance of an eighteen-year-old woman, reflecting the immortality and supernatural perfection of her Divine constitution rather than her actual lifespan.[14]

#### Renata
Renata is the Human identity Aphrodite used to interact with ordinary society without revealing her existence as the ancient Goddess of Beauty. In this appearance, she possesses long blonde hair and generally wears modern clothing, including professional and business attire. Although still exceptionally attractive, the disguise conceals enough of her supernatural identity for her to maintain relationships outside the Divine world.[15]
Aphrodite employed this identity for years before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] entered supernatural society. As Renata, she established a genuine friendship with Anna Walker, eventually becoming one of the few individuals with whom she could maintain an ordinary Human relationship. Their friendship continued for more than ten years before Victor discovered Renata's true identity.[16][17]
The identity became increasingly important after Victor learned the truth. Aphrodite could have abandoned Renata and created another Human identity, but doing so would have meant surrendering the friendship she had built with Anna. This possibility distressed her because Anna's affection was not founded upon Divine worship, supernatural Charm or political advantage.[18]

#### Blood Dragon Goddess
Aphrodite's appearance underwent another major transformation after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] reconstructed her into a genuine True Dragon. Her original Godhood and Divine Concepts were retained, but her physical and spiritual foundation acquired Draconic characteristics adapted to her individual nature.[19][8]
Her Dragon Goddess form possesses large feathered wings rather than the more conventional membranous wings displayed by many other Dragons. The feathers are predominantly white with pink coloration toward their tips, giving her wings an appearance consistent with her original identity as a Goddess of Beauty and Love. Her eyes become distinctly reptilian and can radiate intense neon-pink light.[20]
The transformation also intensified her natural Divine Beauty. Gods who observed her during the worldwide hunt became instinctively captivated by her appearance even though she was not consciously using Charm. This demonstrates that her Draconic evolution amplified the passive expression of her Divine nature rather than simply granting additional physical strength.[20]
By the later Imperial era, Aphrodite's natural radiance had become sufficiently pronounced that [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]] immediately noticed it upon meeting her. The newborn daughter of Victor and [[Tyche]] called Aphrodite her Shining Mama, prompting Aphrodite to deliberately dim the supernatural light surrounding her so the child could look at her comfortably.[21]

### Personality
Aphrodite is charismatic, vain, flirtatious, uninhibited and intensely proud of her appearance. Her personality reflects billions of years of existence as a Goddess whose primary authorities govern Beauty, Love and Sexuality, and she has historically approached relationships with considerably fewer inhibitions than ordinary Humans. She openly enjoys attention, frequently teases people around her and is comfortable discussing matters that other members of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family consider embarrassing. Her reputation for promiscuity is therefore not entirely undeserved, but reducing her personality to sexuality ignores her intelligence, independence, political experience, emotional vulnerability and eventual development into one of the most devoted members of Victor's household.
Personal freedom is among Aphrodite's strongest values. Throughout her ancient existence, she resisted attempts by other Gods to dictate whom she should love, how she should behave or how she was permitted to use her Divine authority. Her relationship with [[Zeus]] was particularly strained because the ruler of [[Olympus]] repeatedly attempted to impose his authority upon her despite her own ancient status and independence. Aphrodite's later decision to abandon Olympus was consequently not a sudden betrayal motivated only by attraction to Victor. It reflected centuries of resentment toward the political system surrounding Zeus and her growing realization that the Pantheon no longer represented the life she wanted to preserve.[22]
Aphrodite also possesses exceptional social intelligence. Unlike many ancient Gods who remained isolated behind temples, worshippers and political institutions, she maintained relationships across numerous Pantheons and supernatural organizations. Her friendships and acquaintances included figures such as [[Freyja|Freya]], [[Loki]], Kali and other influential Divine Beings. These connections allowed her to function as an information broker, providing knowledge concerning supernatural politics, ancient organizations and events beyond the information networks available to [[Nightingale]]'s Vampires. Her social abilities are not entirely dependent upon Charm: her friendship with Anna developed through ordinary interactions while Aphrodite was living as Renata.[17][5]
Despite receiving almost limitless attention throughout her existence, Aphrodite was deeply lonely. Being desired because of Divine Beauty did not necessarily mean being loved as an individual. Many relationships in her previous life revolved around attraction, pleasure, status or temporary fascination, leaving her unfamiliar with genuine affection that persisted independently of supernatural influence. Anna became important because she treated Renata as a friend rather than an object of worship, while Victor eventually became the first romantic partner through whom Aphrodite experienced Love deeply enough to transform the development of her own Divinity.[17][5][6]
Her initial relationship with Victor was complicated by the very powers that defined her existence. Victor regarded involuntary emotional manipulation as an intrusion upon his autonomy, especially because he considered his Love for Violet, Ruby and Sasha something no outside power had the right to alter. Aphrodite originally underestimated the severity of this objection. Accustomed to people becoming fascinated by her presence, she believed demonstrating her supernatural Beauty would reassure him of her intentions. Instead, Victor responded by inflicting a severe wound upon himself to interrupt the influence, making it clear that he preferred physical injury to losing control of his emotions.[23]
Aphrodite gradually learned that Victor's distrust could not be overcome through Beauty, seduction or the authority of her Godhood. She needed to demonstrate sincerity through her actions and allow him to decide whether he wanted her presence in his life. Her friendship with Anna complicated the situation further, forcing both Aphrodite and Victor to acknowledge that their interactions affected someone they genuinely cared about. Aphrodite was particularly distressed by the possibility of losing Anna because that friendship represented one of the few relationships she had established without relying upon her Divine identity.[17][18]
Aphrodite's relationship with Victor ultimately changed her understanding of Love. The emotions she developed for him were not merely an extension of Lust or possessive desire. She became increasingly concerned with his safety, his happiness and the well-being of the people he considered family. Her Love Divinity reacted to these feelings even when she did not consciously activate it, and its development accelerated as her understanding deepened. By the time she approached [[Hestia]] for a Blessing, Aphrodite's Love had become so powerful and emotionally substantial that Hestia was visibly affected by its presence.[5][6]
Her attachment to Victor remains intensely possessive, but it exists alongside a willingness to cooperate with the other women of his household. Aphrodite understands that competing endlessly for exclusive authority over Victor would destabilize the family she has chosen to join. She consequently uses her social intelligence to negotiate relationships, exchange information and encourage the other women to develop their own powers. Her explanation of [[Soul Marriage]] after becoming Victor's wife was partly intended to help his original wives understand that they could eventually form the same kind of eternal spiritual connection rather than regard Aphrodite's marriage as an advantage unavailable to them.[4]
Aphrodite's moral development does not erase her former wrongdoing. Her treatment of [[Adonis Snow]] remains one of the darkest aspects of her history. She cursed his mother out of jealousy, subsequently developed an obsession with Adonis and used supernatural influence against him in a relationship that violated his consent. These actions cannot be reinterpreted as harmless romantic behavior merely because Aphrodite later became a sympathetic member of Victor's family. Her subsequent development is instead characterized by a growing recognition that possessing power does not automatically grant the right to impose one's desires upon other people.[24][25]
Motherhood and family loyalty became increasingly important as Aphrodite's existence changed. Her relationship with [[Eros]] illustrates the contrast between her ancient life and the responsibilities she accepted after joining the Elderblood family. She repeatedly attempted to correct his behavior, but eventually concluded that his unwillingness to respect boundaries represented a danger to the people she loved. Her decision to disown him was therefore an expression of the priorities she had developed through Victor rather than a sudden abandonment of her former identity.[13]
By the later Imperial era, Aphrodite had become protective not only of her biological daughter but of Victor's enormous second generation. She was capable of scolding the Imperial Princesses when their experiments threatened to create dangerous supernatural consequences, even while Victor encouraged their curiosity and defended their creativity. Her irritation at his indulgence reflects the increasingly maternal role she assumed within the household.[26]
Aphrodite's understanding of family also changed her perception of Divine government. She recognized that Dragons Nest possessed many of the same proud and powerful Goddesses whose rivalries had contributed to the collapse of Olympus. However, rather than treating them as competing Divine authorities bound together only by politics, she increasingly regarded them as members of one enormous family centered upon Victor. This perspective distinguishes her current loyalty from the political relationships she previously maintained within the [[Greek Pantheon]].[27]

### Background
Aphrodite came into existence during primordial antiquity following the mutilation of [[Ouranos]]. Rather than being conceived through ordinary biological reproduction, her Divine existence originated from the severed reproductive portion of the ancient Primordial God. This origin places her within the generation associated with the [[Titans]] and establishes the basis for her classification as a Second-Generation Primordial Goddess.[2][5]
Although she belonged to the same broad ancient generation as the Titans, Aphrodite eventually became one of the Twelve Olympians and aligned herself with the Divine civilization that replaced the old Titan order. Over billions of years she developed into one of the most recognizable Goddesses of the [[Greek Pantheon]], with enormous authority over Beauty, Love and Sexuality. Her lesser Concepts included Pleasure, Joy and the Perpetuation of Life, while her travels eventually allowed her to acquire a small independent authority over War.[3][4]
Her Divine influence made her one of the most socially dangerous members of [[Olympus]]. Unlike Gods whose abilities primarily involved storms, weapons or physical destruction, Aphrodite possessed powers capable of altering attraction, emotion, relationships and behavior. Her supernatural Beauty was particularly difficult to resist because it did not always require a deliberate technique: her presence itself could influence a person's perceptions and desires.[2]
Aphrodite nevertheless remained dissatisfied with the restrictions and political expectations of Olympus. [[Zeus]]'s attempts to control the Gods around him repeatedly conflicted with her independent temperament, while the rivalries, arrogance and personal scandals of the Pantheon made her increasingly critical of its government. Her long lifespan allowed her to cultivate connections beyond Greece, giving her opportunities to learn about other Divine civilizations and experience societies that were not governed by Zeus.[5][22]
Among the most consequential events of her ancient history was her involvement with Adonis. Aphrodite became jealous after Humans praised the beauty of his mother, whom she interpreted as a challenge to her own supremacy. She cursed the woman, setting in motion circumstances that contributed to Adonis's birth. Aphrodite later developed an obsession with him and repeatedly used supernatural influence against his wishes, causing psychological damage that continued affecting him long after the events themselves.[24]
Aphrodite also developed relationships with other Greek Gods, including [[Ares]], with whom she had a son named [[Eros]]. Her romantic history was extensive, but the relationships rarely provided the emotional stability or genuine affection she later discovered through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[4][13]
At some point during the modern era, Aphrodite began living among Humans under the identity Renata. The disguise allowed her to experience Human society without the constant expectations surrounding her Godhood. Through this identity she befriended Anna Walker and became an established presence in Anna's life years before Victor became a Vampire. Their friendship eventually lasted more than a decade and remained important enough that Aphrodite feared losing it when her true identity was exposed.[17]

### Synopsis

#### A New Pillar
Aphrodite entered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s supernatural life during the period when [[Nightingale]] had been sealed by [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]]. With ordinary routes into the Vampire realm inaccessible, Victor and Ruby searched for supernatural beings possessing enough knowledge and authority to determine what had happened. Aphrodite was identified as one of the few accessible Divine figures whose connections might provide the necessary information. Ruby strongly disliked the proposal because Aphrodite's authority over Love and attraction represented a direct threat to emotions she considered sacred within her marriage.[28]
Victor nevertheless chose to investigate because remaining separated from Violet, Sasha and the rest of his family was intolerable. He approached Aphrodite while she was being pursued by [[Belial]], a Demon Duke who refused to accept her repeated rejections. Aphrodite found Demonic Energy deeply unpleasant and had little interest in Belial, but the Duke continued attempting to obtain her attention. Victor's arrival immediately attracted Aphrodite's curiosity because his supernatural nature and appearance were unusual even among powerful Vampires.[29][2]
Aphrodite's natural Beauty affected Victor's perceptions as soon as he encountered her. He began recognizing qualities resembling the women he loved, forcing him to consciously separate the Goddess standing before him from the emotional associations created by Violet, Ruby and Sasha. Victor regarded this involuntary influence as dangerous because it interfered with feelings he believed should belong exclusively to himself.[2]
The encounter became increasingly hostile when Belial insulted [[Scathach]]. Victor immediately attacked the vessel through which the Demon Duke was communicating, demonstrating that Belial's position within Hell did not protect him from retaliation for insulting someone Victor loved. Aphrodite was surprised by his willingness to provoke such a powerful enemy without considering the political consequences.[2]
After Belial's immediate interference ended, Victor asked Aphrodite for information concerning Nightingale. She contacted [[Kairos]], a Divine authority associated with Time, and obtained confirmation that Vlad had deliberately sealed the Vampire realm and altered its temporal relationship with the outside world. The information finally explained why Victor had been unable to reach his family despite searching for alternative methods of entry.[2][23]
Aphrodite attempted to reassure Victor that she was not an enemy, but her demonstration of Divine Beauty only intensified his distrust. When he recognized that her presence was affecting his emotions without permission, Victor drove ice through his own head to interrupt the influence, relying upon his Vampiric regeneration to survive. His reaction made Aphrodite realize that further attempts to influence him would create genuine hostility rather than attraction.[23]
The encounter also involved Aphrodite's hidden connection to Victor's mother. Anna was one of the few Humans Aphrodite genuinely considered a friend, and providing information to Victor allowed her to assist someone connected to that relationship. Victor did not yet fully understand that the Goddess of Beauty and his mother's friend Renata were the same person.[23]
Before departing, Victor deliberately worsened the situation between Aphrodite and Belial by encouraging the Demon Duke to believe that Aphrodite might secretly reciprocate his interest. Aphrodite eventually retaliated against Belial with a curse that rendered him impotent, humiliating the Duke and giving him another reason to resent Victor.[23][30]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] encountered Aphrodite again after returning to [[Nightingale]]. Although several months had passed since their initial meeting during the lost year, his distrust remained because her Divine nature could influence attraction and desire without deliberate hostile intent. Their subsequent interactions gradually forced both of them to recognize that their relationship could not develop through the ordinary expectations surrounding the Goddess of Beauty.[31]
During this period, Aphrodite was also searching for a Witch who had stolen the original [[Hammer of Hephaestus]]. The investigation became relevant to Victor because he had encountered copies of the Divine weapon while dealing with hostile supernatural forces. The existence of functional replicas suggested that someone had discovered an extraordinary method of reproducing at least part of a Divine artifact's power, making the missing original an important matter within supernatural politics.[32]
Aphrodite's past involvement with Adonis also became increasingly significant. Victor's connection to Adonis exposed him to the memories and trauma associated with Aphrodite's former treatment of the man, reinforcing his suspicion that she could not be trusted merely because she appeared friendly. The history also complicated her relationship with the Snow family, whose members had suffered consequences from Adonis's experiences and the Divine influences surrounding him.[24]
Victor's relationship with Aphrodite began changing more substantially after he learned that the Goddess had maintained a Human identity and had been Anna's friend for over ten years. Aphrodite appeared before Anna in her Divine form and explained that she was Renata, forcing Anna to reconcile the ordinary woman she trusted with an ancient Goddess possessing powers capable of influencing Human emotions.[16][17]
The revelation initially created distress for both women. Anna had difficulty accepting that her longtime friend was a supernatural being, while Aphrodite feared that the friendship would end because of the deception. Victor was also furious that someone possessing such dangerous authority had been close to his mother without his knowledge, and he considered removing Aphrodite from Anna's life entirely.[17][33]
Ruby persuaded Victor to examine the situation more carefully. She argued that Aphrodite's ancient wrongdoing could not be ignored, but also reminded him that many people he loved had committed acts that would be unacceptable under ordinary Human moral standards. Victor needed to decide how he intended to judge supernatural beings according to his own principles rather than automatically condemning everyone whose history conflicted with his upbringing.[34]
The issue became more complicated because Anna genuinely valued Renata's friendship. Aphrodite could abandon her existing Human identity and create another, but doing so would mean destroying a relationship that had developed independently of Divine worship or political advantage. Victor eventually recognized that removing Aphrodite from Anna's life would hurt his mother as well, forcing him to balance his desire for protection with Anna's right to maintain her own relationships.[35][18]
Aphrodite continued assisting Victor with information concerning supernatural politics. Her network of acquaintances extended across multiple Pantheons, allowing her to investigate movements among Gods and Divine organizations during the escalating tensions surrounding Nightingale and the approaching Demonic invasion. She was particularly useful because her social connections supplied information that conventional Vampire networks could not easily obtain.[5]
Her growing emotional attachment to Victor also began affecting her Divinity. While thinking about him, Aphrodite unconsciously released Love across an area of approximately thirty kilometers. People within the affected region experienced intensified romantic feelings and were driven to confess their emotions or seek those they loved. The incident demonstrated that Aphrodite's Love was developing independently of deliberate training and that her emotional state could influence the expression of her Divine authority.[5]
Aphrodite later became one of Victor's Divine instructors. Rather than continue treating every influence associated with the Goddess as something to reject, Victor asked her to help him understand the Blessing of Beauty already present within his body. Aphrodite explained that simply consuming Divine Blood could be fatal to a Creature of the Night lacking the correct compatibility and that understanding an existing Blessing was safer than attempting to force additional [[Divine Energy]] into his system.[36]
During their training, Victor began distinguishing the various powers inside himself as separate sources of supernatural Energy. Aphrodite helped him recognize the pink Divine Energy associated with her Blessing and taught him to isolate it from his Blood, Lightning, Fire, Water and Ice. Once Victor could perceive the Divine influence directly, he began constructing manifestations from it rather than allowing the Blessing to operate solely as an unconscious effect.[37]
Aphrodite also demonstrated how different Divinities could be combined in combat. She created a bow and arrows from Beauty and Love, then incorporated her lesser War authority to give the projectiles genuine offensive properties. The technique illustrated that Divine abilities were not restricted to passive blessings or emotional influence and that conceptual compatibility could allow powers with very different meanings to produce a coherent supernatural attack.[37]
The training changed Victor's perception of Aphrodite. He began recognizing that her knowledge and willingness to help were valuable independently of the attraction surrounding her Divine presence. Aphrodite, in turn, became increasingly interested in the unusual Vampire who treated her powers as something to understand rather than something to worship.[36][37]

#### Demonic War
As the supernatural crisis intensified, Aphrodite became increasingly concerned about the Divine Blessings accumulating around [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Gods frequently used Blessings to influence heroes, champions and favored Mortals, sometimes binding them to political obligations or manipulating their choices. Aphrodite understood these dangers through her experience with ancient Divine politics and feared that Victor could become another pawn in the conflicts of powerful Goddesses.[38]
Her concern increased after she learned how many influential beings had already attached powers or Blessings to him, including Nyx, [[Gaia]] and Albedo Moriarty. Aphrodite did not want Victor's freedom to become compromised by obligations imposed through Divine influence, particularly because she understood how difficult it could be for a Mortal or comparatively young supernatural being to resist the plans of ancient Gods.[39][40]
Aphrodite eventually approached Victor and asked him to allow her to protect him personally. Her request was motivated by genuine fear for his safety rather than an attempt to obtain political authority over him. Victor recognized the sincerity of her feelings and accepted the possibility of a deeper relationship, leading Aphrodite to propose a genuine [[Soul Marriage]].[4]
The ritual differed fundamentally from the marriage system commonly used by Vampires. Aphrodite explained that Soul Marriage directly connected two Souls through Fate, creating a bond that would continue beyond the death of their current bodies. As long as the Concept of a Soul continued to exist, the two individuals would remain destined to encounter one another even across future lives. The ritual was normally available only to Gods and exceptional beings possessing a sufficiently developed relationship with Concepts.[4]
Victor accepted Aphrodite's proposal, and the two completed the Soul Marriage. Their Divine and Vampiric Energies reacted to the union, and Aphrodite's Love Divinity surged as she acknowledged Victor as her husband. The marriage transformed their relationship from one between a suspicious Vampire and an outside Goddess into a permanent spiritual partnership.[4]
Aphrodite subsequently offered Victor her Blessings associated with Sexuality, Love, the Perpetuation of Life, Pleasure, Joy and War. She explained that the powers had distinct applications despite belonging to closely related areas of her Divine existence. Sexuality could enhance intimate experiences, Pleasure could alter the sensation associated with different actions and could even be used during torture, the Perpetuation of Life allowed sensitivity to living things and limited Life-related manipulation, Joy provided an empathic understanding of what made another person happy, and War supplied instinctive knowledge concerning the conduct and development of warfare.[4]
Her explanation also clarified that the War Divinity was comparatively weak. Aphrodite had acquired it during her travels rather than being born as a dedicated War Goddess, and she openly acknowledged that her understanding could not compare with [[Ares]] or other Gods whose identities were fundamentally associated with War. Nevertheless, it was sufficiently developed to provide meaningful instinctive knowledge and improve her ability to understand military conflict.[4]
Aphrodite then explained the limitations of the existing Vampire marriage ritual. Although the ritual helped regulate bloodlust and emotional connections, it had originally been intended for a comparatively small number of partners. Victor's increasingly large family created complications because the ritual could intensify dependence upon his Blood. Aphrodite proposed that the women eventually pursue Soul Marriage once they developed the necessary Divine or conceptual foundations, allowing their bonds with Victor to become more complete without preserving the same weaknesses.[4]
Her willingness to share this information helped reduce some of the hostility surrounding her sudden marriage. Although Violet and the others remained possessive, Aphrodite demonstrated that she understood the importance of maintaining a stable household rather than using her new status to place herself permanently above the women who had loved Victor before her.[4]
Aphrodite later visited [[Hestia]], hoping to obtain a Blessing for Victor and his family. Their encounter was initially hostile because Hestia remembered Aphrodite's ancient behavior, including an incident in which Aphrodite had used one of Hestia's sacred spaces for a sexual gathering. Hestia regarded the act as an insult to her authority over the [[Rex Invictus|Home]] and Family, making reconciliation difficult despite their long acquaintance.[41]
Aphrodite nevertheless persisted and revealed that she had entered a Soul Marriage. Hestia was stunned because the Aphrodite she remembered had never appeared capable of such absolute romantic commitment. As their conversation continued, Hestia recognized the extraordinary depth of Aphrodite's feelings toward Victor. The Love surrounding the Goddess had become so powerful that even Hestia needed to protect herself against its emotional influence.[6]
Aphrodite ultimately obtained Hestia's cooperation. The Goddess of the Home granted a Blessing extending to Victor's family, including future members, demonstrating that Aphrodite's changing identity could restore relationships damaged by her former behavior. The incident also brought Hestia closer to Victor's expanding faction and helped begin the process by which several Greek Goddesses separated themselves from [[Zeus]]'s political order.[6]
Aphrodite subsequently assisted Victor during the integration of additional Goddesses into his faction. Her knowledge of Divine culture, her connections to the [[Greek Pantheon]] and her developing reputation as Victor's wife made her an important intermediary between the former Olympian order and the increasingly independent civilization forming around Victor.[42]

#### King Among Kings
Aphrodite's allegiance to [[Olympus]] weakened as the [[Greek Pantheon]] entered a period of escalating instability. The struggle between [[Zeus]], [[Kronos]] and other ancient Divine authorities created opportunities for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction, while Aphrodite increasingly regarded the old political structure as something that could be dismantled without destroying every useful element of Greek civilization.[43]
She encouraged Victor to consider the enormous resources accumulated by Olympus across thousands of years. Divine materials, artifacts, knowledge, talented individuals and supernatural infrastructure could all remain useful even if Zeus's government collapsed. Rather than allow the entire civilization to disappear through war, Aphrodite believed Victor should preserve the valuable parts and incorporate them into his expanding faction.[43]
During the conflict involving the [[Greek Underworld]], Aphrodite accompanied Nyx, [[Scathach]] and Morgana into the territory governed by the Greek afterlife system. The expedition brought them into contact with increasingly dangerous Divine forces, culminating in an encounter with [[Erebus]], whose authority over Darkness threatened the group.[44][7]
When Erebus appeared, Aphrodite and Nyx immediately covered themselves with their Divinities to counteract his influence. Aphrodite manifested her Divine Raiment, and the Concepts of Beauty and Love permeated the surrounding region. The display demonstrated that her powers could affect the environment on a large scale and were sufficiently developed to participate in confrontations involving ancient [[Primordial Gods]].[7]
Nyx recognized that Aphrodite's Love had developed far beyond the expectations associated with an ordinary Olympian Major Goddess. Its conceptual authority was approaching a level comparable to that of the First-Generation Primordial Gods, despite Aphrodite's original existence belonging to a later Divine generation. The development was particularly extraordinary because it resulted from a deeper understanding of her own Concept rather than the acquisition of an unrelated power.[7]
The confrontation also demonstrated the limitations of her abilities. Aphrodite's ordinary Charm was not sufficient to simply enslave Original Primordial Gods such as Erebus and [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]], whose existence and authority occupied a much higher level than those of conventional Olympians. Nevertheless, her Love and Beauty remained extraordinarily dangerous against Gods lacking comparable resistance.[7]
During the larger confrontation between the forces of Zeus and Kronos, Aphrodite spread her Charm across the battlefield and influenced numerous Greek Gods simultaneously. The affected beings became susceptible to her commands and displayed a willingness to obey her despite the political divisions separating their factions. Her authority temporarily transformed an enormous Divine confrontation into a situation where many participants were no longer acting entirely according to their own desires.[45]
The effect was eventually disrupted by powers operating beyond the ordinary limits of her influence. Nevertheless, the scale of the feat demonstrated that Aphrodite's conceptual abilities could threaten entire groups of Gods rather than functioning solely as individual seduction or emotional manipulation.[45]
Aphrodite's position became particularly clear when Victor prepared to destroy Olympus. Rather than attempt to preserve Zeus's government, she asked to witness the end of the Pantheon in which she had spent most of her life. Although she had abandoned her loyalty to its political structure, Olympus still represented an enormous portion of her personal history. She wanted to see its destruction because the event marked the conclusion of a civilization she had known for billions of years, even though Victor had become the person she now regarded as her true home.[46]
Aphrodite's departure from Olympus also ended much of her association with the old Divine hierarchy. She no longer considered Zeus's authority relevant to her decisions and had little interest in restoring the political rivalries that had dominated the Greek Pantheon. Her priority became the preservation of the people and traditions she still valued within the new civilization forming around Victor.[47][22]
The aftermath of the Greek conflict also brought another encounter with the history surrounding Adonis. Victor eventually reconstructed an informational version of Adonis through the [[Akashic Records]], allowing several individuals connected to him to confront unresolved matters from their past. Aphrodite did not attempt to reestablish her former relationship with Adonis and remained with Victor while others pursued the closure they desired.[48][49]
Aphrodite later became one of the earliest members of Victor's family to undergo transformation into a genuine True Dragon. Her Divine nature was preserved during the process, but her physical and spiritual foundation evolved into that of a Blood Dragon. Victor consequently became her Blood Dragon Primogenitor, although he remained her husband rather than her biological father.[19][8]
The transformation reflected the individuality of each Dragon created through Victor's lineage. Aphrodite's new existence retained her principal Divine Concepts while strengthening their expression through a far more powerful body and Soul. Her unusual white-and-pink feathered wings and amplified Beauty demonstrated that her Draconic nature had adapted around the qualities that defined her as an individual.[8][20]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ascension as the Dragon God of [[Chaos]], Aphrodite became an established member of the new [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]] rather than an Olympian Goddess temporarily associated with a foreign faction. Her Divine authorities remained centered upon Love, Beauty and Sexuality, but the physical and energetic foundation supporting them had been transformed through her Blood Dragon existence.[8]
During the worldwide hunt involving powerful supernatural beings, Aphrodite displayed the passive effects of her evolved Divine Beauty. Her Draconic form possessed white feathered wings with pink tips, reptilian eyes and a radiance that instinctively drew the attention of Gods observing her. The effect occurred even when she was not consciously activating Charm, demonstrating that her conceptual presence had become increasingly difficult to separate from her natural existence.[20]
Her development continued through interactions with other advanced Goddesses. When Aphrodite discussed her growth with Kali, the Goddess of Destruction was surprised by how rapidly Aphrodite had advanced toward a more primordial state. Kali had required extraordinary periods of effort and development to refine her own authority, while Aphrodite's Love had grown at an exceptional rate through her relationship with Victor.[50]
Aphrodite attributed much of this development to the difference between possessing a Concept and genuinely understanding it. Although Love had always belonged to her Divine identity, she had never experienced it with the same depth before meeting Victor. Her emotional transformation provided a new understanding of the Concept, allowing her Divinity to develop beyond limitations that billions of years of ordinary existence had failed to overcome.[50]
Aphrodite also became increasingly involved in the ethical and political discussions surrounding Victor's faction. She participated in decisions involving Divine administration, the protection of allies and the treatment of individuals whose powers or circumstances made them vulnerable to exploitation. Her later behavior demonstrated that she no longer regarded strength as sufficient justification for treating weaker beings as disposable resources.[25]
As Dragons Nest developed, Aphrodite's connections to other Pantheons remained valuable. She understood the relationships, rivalries and histories of numerous ancient Gods, making her an important participant in the integration of former Divine factions into the civilization surrounding Victor. Her experience also allowed her to recognize the dangers of reproducing the same political structures that had contributed to [[Olympus]]'s collapse.[51]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
The enormous periods of accelerated Time experienced by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family created opportunities for development that ordinary Gods could scarcely reproduce. Aphrodite's Draconic and Divine foundations continued strengthening across thousands of subjective years, while her relationship with Victor eventually resulted in the birth of their daughter, [[Irene Elderblood]].[12]
Aphrodite carried Irene for approximately 1,900 subjective years, an extraordinary gestational period reflecting the unusual development of True Dragon offspring within Victor's family. Irene inherited the powerful lineage of the Blood Dragon Primogenitor together with Aphrodite's Divine characteristics, becoming part of the second generation of Elderblood daughters whose potential exceeded the standards of ordinary supernatural children.[12]
Motherhood became another important aspect of Aphrodite's later life. Unlike the relatively distant relationship she had maintained with [[Eros]] during the ancient Greek era, her daughter with Victor belonged to a closely connected family in which the wives collectively cared for and educated the younger generation. Aphrodite's role as a mother consequently developed alongside her responsibilities as a Goddess and member of the Imperial household.[12]
Her conceptual growth did not stagnate during this period. By the time the Imperial religious system had developed sufficiently to support enormous populations of worshippers, Aphrodite had attained the status of a High-Level Dragon Goddess. Her available [[Divine Energy]] had become so great that she believed she could bless an entire planet if necessary, even though a considerable portion of her Blessings remained connected to Victor.[1]
Aphrodite was also described as being almost a Primordial Being of Love. This represented a major advance beyond her former standing as an Olympian Major Goddess and demonstrated that the combination of genuine conceptual understanding, Draconic evolution and immense periods of development had brought her close to a higher state of existence.[1]
Faith became increasingly important to her Divine position. Aphrodite monitored the prayers received by the Goddesses of the Empire and discovered that many worshippers addressed their prayers primarily toward Victor rather than the individual Goddesses possessing the relevant authorities. Although she remained an important figure, Aphrodite recognized that newer and more active Divine personalities could surpass her in popularity if she relied solely upon her ancient reputation.[1]
She observed that [[Tyche]] and [[Hestia]] received especially strong devotion, followed by Goddesses associated with Order and Truth, while her own position remained important but not unchallenged. Aphrodite considered creating a Saint to strengthen her religious presence, demonstrating that she was willing to adapt to the developing institutions of the Empire rather than assume her ancient fame would automatically secure permanent worship.[1]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
The [[Elderblood Empire]]'s accelerated developmental project transformed the scale of Aphrodite's responsibilities. As enormous periods passed within the Empire while comparatively little Time elapsed outside, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his wives worked through avatars and other means to supervise technological, social, military and demographic development across numerous worlds.[52][9]
Aphrodite became one of the principal Divine authorities responsible for ensuring that the Empire possessed enough citizens to populate its expanding territories. Her Blessings were used alongside those of Goddesses associated with Life, Fertility, Motherhood, Marriage and related Concepts, encouraging reproduction and improving the circumstances necessary for population growth.[9]
The program was particularly important because the Empire contained many different [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] with radically different lifespans, reproductive cycles and biological limitations. Immortal or exceptionally long-lived species could not be expected to reproduce at the same rate as ordinary Mortals, and the Imperial government needed to develop demographic policies compatible with those differences. Aphrodite and the Goddesses working beneath her consequently concentrated considerable attention upon Mortal and shorter-lived populations, allowing the Empire to grow without relying exclusively upon the comparatively small number of [[True Dragons]].[9][11]
The demographic effects were extraordinary. Reproductive Blessings, advanced medicine, improved living conditions and Imperial genetic development contributed to an enormous increase in population during the accelerated period. Families containing exceptionally large numbers of children became increasingly common in parts of the Empire, while the expansion of the population also increased the number of potential worshippers supporting the Goddesses involved.[11]
Victor also provided Aphrodite and the Greek members of his family with an enormous world on which they could preserve and reconstruct elements of Greek civilization. The planet allowed temples, cultural traditions and Divine institutions associated with the ancient Pantheon to survive within the Elderblood Empire without restoring the former authority of [[Zeus]].[10]
Aphrodite's role within this Greek Divine community became increasingly prominent. The reconstructed civilization was governed by Aphrodite and the surviving ancient Greek Goddesses, allowing figures such as [[Tyche]] to maintain temples and receive worship under a new political structure. The arrangement preserved the useful cultural and religious inheritance of Greece while avoiding the rivalries and instability that had characterized [[Olympus]].[53][54]
Her relationship with [[Eros]] eventually reached its final rupture during the Imperial era. Aphrodite had repeatedly warned and punished her son for refusing to control his desires and for behaving in ways that threatened the boundaries of the Elderblood family. Eros continued treating the matter according to the permissive and self-indulgent standards of the old Greek order, failing to understand that Aphrodite no longer regarded such behavior as acceptable.[13]
When Aphrodite confronted him, her anger manifested through her vastly developed Divine existence. She ordered Eros to remain silent, and the command was enforced directly upon Reality rather than functioning as an ordinary verbal instruction. Eros found himself unable to speak despite attempting to respond, demonstrating the extraordinary authority Aphrodite could exert over a weaker Divine Being.[13]
Aphrodite ultimately disowned Eros. Her decision demonstrated that her loyalty to Victor's family was not subordinate to biological relationships inherited from Olympus and that she would not permit her son to disregard the boundaries protecting the people she loved.[13]
The late Imperial era also demonstrated how far Aphrodite's existence had progressed beyond conventional Divine standards. During a discussion of the qualitative superiority of True Dragons, Aphrodite, Anna and [[Scathach]] were used as examples of Dragon Goddesses whose racial nature provided advantages beyond what would be expected from similarly powerful non-Dragon beings. The comparison was illustrative rather than a formal numerical ranking system, emphasizing that the difference between a conventional God and a True Dragon involved more than raw Energy alone.[55]
Aphrodite remained deeply involved in Victor's enormous family during the gatherings of the Imperial Princesses. She accompanied Anna and Kaguya while visiting the daughters and participated in the increasingly familiar exchanges surrounding Scathach's severe training methods. When the daughters reacted with visible apprehension toward Scathach's unusually gentle smile, Aphrodite dryly remarked upon the emotional damage caused by her fellow wife's training, demonstrating that she retained her humor even while occupying an important maternal position within the household.[56]
During another family gathering, Aphrodite reflected upon the differences between Dragons Nest and the [[Greek Pantheon]]. The ancient Olympians had repeatedly undermined one another through personal rivalries, competition for authority and uncontrolled egos. Dragons Nest possessed many equally proud Goddesses, but their shared connection to Victor allowed them to coexist as members of one enormous family. Aphrodite concluded that the organization functioned less like a conventional Pantheon and more like a household whose members were bound together by the same central relationship.[27]
Her Divine radiance also attracted the attention of [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]], the newly born daughter of Victor and Tyche. Moira called Aphrodite her Shining Mama and complained that the light surrounding her was too bright. Aphrodite responded by dimming her natural radiance, allowing Moira to look at her comfortably. The interaction demonstrated how naturally the younger Elderblood generation regarded Victor's wives as collective maternal figures regardless of biological parentage.[21]
Moira later attempted to imitate Aphrodite's radiance by asking Victor for a star. After consuming a miniature stellar replica created by her father, Moira developed a minor Solar Divinity through the extraordinary influence of her own Luck-related nature. Although Aphrodite was not responsible for the development, the incident demonstrated how the younger generation increasingly treated the supernatural characteristics of their mothers as examples they wished to imitate.[21]
Aphrodite continued supervising the Imperial daughters while maintaining her broader responsibilities. When several of the girls experimented with the creation of new life using Victor's scale and portions of their own supernatural powers, Aphrodite encountered the activity and immediately recognized its resemblance to ancient Greek experiments such as the creation of Pandora. She attempted to stop the daughters, but they fled before she could intervene.[26]
The experiment eventually produced a new Eldritch creature, causing Aphrodite to confront Victor over his willingness to permit such dangerous creativity. Victor explained that he had monitored the process and would have intervened if genuine danger had emerged. Aphrodite nevertheless remained irritated by his tendency to indulge the daughters, recognizing that his affection often encouraged them to pursue increasingly extraordinary and potentially hazardous experiments.[26]
The incident reflected Aphrodite's position within the later Elderblood family. She was no longer merely the ancient Goddess of Beauty who pursued personal desire without concern for ordinary consequences. She had become a powerful Divine administrator, an experienced member of Victor's inner household and a protective maternal figure responsible for helping manage children whose abilities could alter the natural order of entire worlds.[26]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Aphrodite's husband, Soulmate, Blood Dragon Primogenitor and the most important romantic relationship of her later existence. Their relationship began through conflict rather than immediate affection, largely because Victor considered Aphrodite's supernatural influence over attraction a direct violation of his emotional autonomy.
During their first meeting, Aphrodite's natural Beauty caused Victor to perceive qualities associated with the women he loved, making him suspicious of her intentions. Her attempts to reassure him through further displays of Divine influence only worsened the situation, culminating in Victor injuring himself to break the effect. Aphrodite was surprised by his refusal to submit to her power and gradually became interested in the unusual Vampire who treated the Goddess of Beauty as someone who needed to earn his trust.[2][23]
Their relationship developed through repeated encounters involving supernatural information, Divine politics and Victor's family. Aphrodite's friendship with Anna forced Victor to acknowledge that she could maintain genuine relationships outside her Divine authority, while her willingness to teach him the principles of his Blessings demonstrated that she possessed knowledge and abilities valuable beyond her appearance.[17][36][37]
Aphrodite's attachment gradually developed into genuine Love. Her emotions began affecting her own Divinity, producing increasingly powerful manifestations that surprised even ancient Gods. Victor eventually recognized that her concern for his safety was sincere, particularly when she sought to protect him from the influence of other Goddesses who had attached Blessings to him.[5][4]
Their relationship became permanent through [[Soul Marriage]]. Unlike the conventional Vampire marriage ritual, the union connected their Souls through Fate and established a bond capable of persisting beyond the death of their current bodies. Aphrodite regarded the marriage as an absolute commitment and encouraged Victor's other wives to pursue similar connections once they developed the necessary conceptual foundations.[4]
Victor later became the source of Aphrodite's True Dragon existence. His Blood Dragon transformation preserved her original Godhood while granting a superior Draconic foundation, accelerating the development of her Divine Concepts and bringing her closer to a Primordial state associated with Love.[8][1]
Their marriage eventually produced [[Irene Elderblood]], Aphrodite's daughter with Victor. Across the later Imperial era, Aphrodite's devotion remained centered upon the family they had created, and she openly regarded Victor as her true home rather than the Pantheon in which she had spent billions of years.[46][12]

#### Anna Elderblood
[[Anna Elderblood]] is one of Aphrodite's closest and most genuine friends. Their relationship began while Aphrodite was living among Humans under the identity Renata and developed over more than ten years before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] entered supernatural society.[17]
The friendship was unusual because Anna interacted with Renata as an ordinary person rather than treating her as a Divine authority. Aphrodite was accustomed to worship, attraction and relationships influenced by her supernatural Beauty, making Anna's ordinary affection especially meaningful.[17]
The revelation of Aphrodite's true identity initially threatened the relationship. Anna struggled to understand that her longtime friend was an ancient Goddess, while Aphrodite feared that the deception would destroy one of the few genuine friendships she possessed. Victor also considered separating Aphrodite from his mother because of the danger represented by her powers.[17][33]
Their friendship nevertheless survived, and Anna later joined the same Elderblood household. Aphrodite and Anna continued displaying a familiar, teasing relationship throughout the Imperial era, including shared irritation at Victor's increasingly complicated family life.[56]

#### Eros
[[Eros]] is Aphrodite's biological son with [[Ares]] and one of the principal surviving connections to her former existence within the [[Greek Pantheon]].
Their relationship was considerably less intimate than the relationships commonly maintained within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family. Aphrodite's ancient lifestyle and the permissive culture surrounding [[Olympus]] meant that Eros developed attitudes concerning desire and personal entitlement that eventually conflicted with the standards she adopted after joining the Elderblood household.[13]
During the Imperial era, Aphrodite repeatedly warned and punished Eros for failing to control his behavior. She expected him to understand that being a God associated with Love or desire did not grant permission to violate another person's boundaries, particularly when members of her new family were involved.[13]
Eros continued disregarding those warnings, eventually provoking Aphrodite's anger. Her superior Divine authority was sufficient to silence him through a declaration enforced upon Reality itself. After concluding that he would not change and that his apparent interest in their relationship was not founded upon genuine respect, Aphrodite disowned him.[13]
The decision demonstrates the difference between Aphrodite's old and new priorities. Although she had once indulged many of the same impulses she condemned in Eros, she no longer considered unrestricted desire a sufficient justification for harming or exploiting other people.

#### Irene Elderblood
[[Irene Elderblood]] is Aphrodite's biological daughter with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and a member of the second generation of [[Blood Dragons]] born within the Elderblood family.
Aphrodite carried Irene for approximately 1,900 subjective years, reflecting the extraordinary developmental requirements associated with the offspring of powerful [[True Dragons]] and Divine Beings. Irene inherited the supernatural characteristics of Victor's lineage alongside those of her mother, including exceptional Beauty and the violet Dragon Eyes characteristic of Victor's daughters.[12]
Aphrodite's relationship with Irene differs fundamentally from her former relationship with [[Eros]]. Irene was born into a closely connected family whose members collectively participated in the upbringing and education of Victor's children, giving Aphrodite an opportunity to experience motherhood within a household centered upon affection and protection rather than the political expectations of [[Olympus]].

#### Hestia Elderblood
[[Hestia]] Elderblood is one of Aphrodite's oldest acquaintances and later one of her fellow wives.
Their relationship was historically difficult because Hestia strongly disapproved of Aphrodite's behavior, particularly an incident in which Aphrodite used a sacred space associated with the Goddess of the [[Rex Invictus|Home]] for a sexual gathering. Hestia regarded the event as disrespectful and remained resentful long afterward.[41]
Aphrodite nevertheless approached Hestia after marrying [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] because she wanted a Blessing capable of protecting her new family. Hestia initially reacted with hostility, but became increasingly surprised as Aphrodite explained the circumstances of her [[Soul Marriage]] and the depth of her feelings.[41][6]
Hestia recognized that Aphrodite's Love had become extraordinarily powerful and that the woman she had once regarded as incapable of genuine devotion had fundamentally changed. Their conversation resulted in reconciliation and Hestia's decision to extend a Blessing to Victor's family, including future members.[6]
The reconciliation later developed into a lasting familial relationship after both Goddesses joined the Elderblood household.

#### Kali Elderblood
[[Kali Elderblood]] is one of Aphrodite's important Divine friends and later a fellow member of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family.
Their relationship is particularly notable because Kali possesses an exceptionally advanced Divine foundation associated with Destruction and had spent enormous periods refining her own authority. She was consequently surprised by the speed at which Aphrodite's Love developed toward a higher conceptual state.[50]
Aphrodite explained that the decisive difference was not simply training or the accumulation of [[Divine Energy]]. Her genuine Love for Victor had provided a deeper understanding of the Concept she governed, allowing her authority to advance more rapidly than during the billions of years preceding their relationship.[50]

#### Adonis Snow
[[Adonis Snow]] was an ancient Vampire connected to Aphrodite through one of the darkest relationships of her former existence.
Aphrodite originally became involved with his family after Humans praised the beauty of his mother. Interpreting the comparison as an insult to her own supremacy, she cursed the woman and set in motion circumstances that contributed to Adonis's birth.[24]
Aphrodite subsequently developed an obsession with Adonis and used supernatural influence against him, repeatedly violating his consent. The relationship was therefore coercive and should not be described as an ordinary consensual romance.[24]
The consequences continued affecting Adonis long after the original events and contributed to the hostility surrounding Aphrodite's interactions with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and the Snow family. When Victor later reconstructed an informational version of Adonis through the [[Akashic Records]], Aphrodite did not attempt to resume the former relationship and remained with her husband while others confronted their unresolved feelings.[48][49]

#### Ares
[[Ares]] is the Greek God of War and Aphrodite's former lover. Their relationship produced [[Eros]] and formed part of Aphrodite's ancient romantic history within [[Olympus]].
Although Aphrodite eventually acquired a lesser War Divinity, she openly acknowledged that her authority over War was far less developed than that possessed by Ares. Her War-related powers originated from personal experience and travel rather than from the same innate conceptual identity that defined dedicated War Gods.[4]
The relationship ended long before Aphrodite's complete separation from Olympus, and Ares did not retain the central emotional significance that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later acquired.

#### Greek Pantheon
The [[Greek Pantheon]] was Aphrodite's original Divine civilization and the organization in which she spent most of her existence.
Although she became one of the Twelve Olympians, Aphrodite never regarded the political authority of [[Zeus]] as absolute. Her independent temperament and personal relationships frequently brought her into conflict with the expectations imposed by the ruler of [[Olympus]].[22]
Aphrodite's eventual allegiance to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] did not mean she hated every aspect of Greek civilization. She continued valuing certain traditions, cultural achievements, temples and relationships even after rejecting the old political structure. Her desire to witness the destruction of Olympus reflected the significance of seeing the end of a civilization that had shaped billions of years of her life.[46]
The later reconstruction of Greek Divine culture on an Imperial mega-planet demonstrates that Aphrodite rejected Zeus's government rather than the entirety of her historical identity. She and the surviving Greek Goddesses preserved elements of their old civilization within the [[Elderblood Empire]], creating a new Divine community governed according to Victor's broader political order.[10][54]

#### Dragons Nest
Dragons Nest is Aphrodite's current Pantheon and the Divine organization she regards as her true home.
Unlike [[Olympus]], Dragons Nest is not organized around rival male Gods competing for authority, worshippers and personal prestige. Its principal members are powerful Goddesses connected to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] through marriage, family or allegiance, creating a structure that Aphrodite increasingly regards as an enormous household rather than a conventional collection of competing Divine factions.[27]
Aphrodite recognizes that the personalities of its members could easily produce conflicts comparable to those that destroyed Olympus if they lacked a common center. She consequently regards Victor's position as essential to maintaining harmony among Goddesses whose pride and authority would otherwise make cooperation considerably more difficult.[27]

### Power Progression
Aphrodite's development differs from that of most supernatural combatants because the greatest changes in her power originate from deeper understanding of her existing Concepts and the integration of those Concepts with a superior racial foundation. She began as an ancient Goddess already possessing billions of years of Divine power, but her later emotional development allowed Love to progress far beyond the state it had reached during her previous existence.

### Abilities

#### Powers & Abilities
Aphrodite Elderblood, the Goddess of Love and Beauty.
Aphrodite Elderblood is an exceptionally powerful Goddess whose existence combines the ancient Divine foundation of a Second-Generation Primordial Goddess with the superior racial constitution of a genuine Blood Dragon and True Dragon. Her original Godhood, Divinities, [[Divine Energy]] and accumulated knowledge were not discarded during her transformation into a Dragon Goddess. Instead, they became integrated into a new Draconic existence capable of sustaining and developing her conceptual authorities beyond the limitations of her original Olympian state.[1][2]
Unlike Gods whose greatest abilities are primarily expressed through direct physical destruction, Aphrodite specializes in conceptual influence. Her principal Divinities are Love, Beauty and Sexuality, while her lesser authorities comprise Perpetuation of Life, Pleasure and Joy. She also independently acquired a comparatively undeveloped authority over War. These Concepts allow her to affect attraction, perception, emotional attachment, desire, sensory experience, reproduction and the behavior of supernatural beings. Her influence is especially dangerous because several effects can occur passively through her Divine nature rather than requiring a deliberate offensive technique.[3][4]
Aphrodite's combat effectiveness cannot be evaluated solely through conventional physical strength. Her Charm has demonstrated the ability to influence numerous Gods simultaneously, while her Love Divinity once affected individuals across an area of approximately thirty kilometers without conscious activation. She can combine separate Concepts to produce offensive Divine weapons, bestow Blessings conveying aspects of her authority, and influence reproduction on a scale relevant to an interstellar civilization. Her later development also allowed her to enforce a spoken declaration upon Reality itself, preventing her son [[Eros]] from speaking despite his attempts to resist.[5][6][7][8][9]
Her most significant conceptual advancement concerns Love. Although Aphrodite was born possessing authority associated with the Concept, her genuine romantic relationship with [[Victor Elderblood]] allowed her to understand and develop it to an unprecedented degree. During the Greek conflict, Nyx Elderblood recognized that Aphrodite's Love was approaching the authority associated with First-Generation [[Primordial Gods]]. After receiving Victor's Blood Dragon lineage and experiencing thousands of subjective years of additional growth, Aphrodite attained the classification of a High-Level Dragon Goddess and was explicitly described as almost becoming a Primordial Being of Love. This represents an approaching evolutionary threshold rather than confirmation that she has already attained the completed primordial state associated with that Concept.[10][11][12]

Her existing Divine authorities were preserved during the transformation, making her a Dragon Goddess whose racial nature and conceptual powers operate through the same integrated existence.[13][2]

#### Divinities
Aphrodite possesses seven explicitly established Divine authorities divided into principal and lesser Concepts. She personally identifies Love, Beauty and Sexuality as her major Divinities and Perpetuation of Life, Pleasure and Joy as lesser authorities. Her seventh authority, War, was independently developed through her travels rather than being one of her primary innate specialties.[3][4]
Her distinction between greater and lesser Divinities concerns mastery rather than the objective importance of a Concept throughout Creation. Aphrodite explains that her major authorities are those she has developed sufficiently to exert considerable influence over them, while the lesser Divinities are related but independently existing Concepts that she has not explored to the same degree. Her later progress toward a Primordial state concentrates particularly upon Love, without establishing that the remaining authorities have disappeared.[3][12]

Love is Aphrodite's most rapidly developing Divinity and the Concept most closely associated with her later personal evolution. Although it had always belonged to her Godhood, genuinely loving [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] transformed the depth of her understanding and accelerated the authority's development far beyond the level it had reached through billions of years of previous existence.[5][10][11]

###### Conceptual Development
Aphrodite's Love underwent a qualitative transformation after she began experiencing genuine romantic attachment. Her previous relationships had often involved desire, physical attraction or temporary affection, but her Love for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] created an understanding that her original Divine existence had not cultivated to the same degree.[5][10]
- Enhanced Conceptual Understanding: Aphrodite's genuine experience of Love allows her to understand and express the Concept more completely than during her former Olympian existence.[10][28]
- Rapid Divinity Development: Her Love advanced at an extraordinary rate compared with the enormous periods of time normally required for ancient Gods to refine their authority.[11][28]
- Near First-Generation Primordial Authority: Nyx recognized that the level of Love emanating from Aphrodite was approaching the authority associated with First-Generation [[Primordial Gods]].[11]
- Near-Primordial Evolution: Aphrodite eventually became a High-Level Dragon Goddess described as almost attaining the state of a Primordial Being of Love.[12]
- Enhanced [[Divine Energy]] Capacity: By her later developmental stage, Aphrodite possessed enough available Divine Energy to contemplate extending a Blessing across an entire planet.[12]

##### Beauty
Beauty is one of Aphrodite's oldest and most famous Divinities. Unlike ordinary physical attractiveness, her Beauty is a supernatural Concept capable of influencing perception and the way an individual responds to an attractive existence. It can operate both deliberately through Divine power and passively through Aphrodite's natural presence.[1][16]

###### Conceptual Authority
- Beauty Manipulation: Aphrodite possesses genuine conceptual authority over Beauty and can influence supernatural attractiveness rather than merely changing ordinary biological appearance.[1][25]
- Supernatural Beauty: Her appearance naturally expresses the Concept of Beauty, giving her an extraordinary attractiveness that exceeds conventional biological standards.[1]
- Beauty Inducement: Aphrodite can increase the attractiveness of another individual through Divine power. Adonis possessed supernatural Beauty associated with her influence.[25]
- Beauty Bestowal: She can grant a persistent Divine influence associated with Beauty, including the Blessing that eventually became connected to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[25][29]
- Beauty Suppression: The Blessing associated with Aphrodite can be used to restrain overwhelming attractiveness. Victor later employed it to reduce his supernatural Beauty to a comparatively ordinary Noble Vampire standard while traveling through Samar.[30]
- Appearance Influence: Her conceptual authority affects how observers perceive attractiveness, allowing Beauty to operate as a supernatural influence rather than an exclusively visual characteristic.[1]
- Passive Beauty Manifestation: Aphrodite's supernatural attractiveness can affect observers even without a deliberate activation of Charm.[16]
- Divine Radiance: Her Beauty can manifest as an exceptionally luminous presence, which became pronounced following her Dragon evolution.[16][20]
- Radiance Regulation: Aphrodite can reduce the outward intensity of her natural radiance to accommodate individuals uncomfortable with its brightness.[20]

###### Beauty Blessing
Aphrodite's Beauty can be transferred as a Divine Blessing rather than functioning exclusively through her own body. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] inherited a Beauty-related Blessing connected to the circumstances surrounding Adonis and later learned to manipulate its [[Divine Energy]] consciously with Aphrodite's assistance.[25][21][6]
- Beauty Blessing Bestowal: Aphrodite can grant a persistent supernatural effect associated with her Beauty authority.[25][3]
- Enhanced Physical Attractiveness: The Blessing can greatly increase the beauty of its recipient, as demonstrated through the supernatural appearance associated with Adonis and subsequently Victor.[25][29]
- Supernatural Charm Enhancement: Beauty-related Divine influence can augment the attractiveness and supernatural presence of an individual who receives the Blessing.[6]
- Beauty Regulation: The recipient can potentially learn to control the Blessing's expression. Victor eventually used Aphrodite's power to reduce the attention attracted by his supernatural appearance.[30]
- Divine Energy Transfer: The Blessing places a portion or expression of Aphrodite's Divine power within another individual, creating a distinct supernatural influence that can be identified and controlled through training.[21][6]

##### Divine Charm
Aphrodite's Charm is one of the most dangerous applications of her authorities over Love and Beauty. Unlike conventional seduction, it is a supernatural influence capable of altering emotional responses, perception, desire and behavior. Its greatest recorded combat application occurred during the Greek conflict, when Aphrodite affected numerous Gods simultaneously and caused them to respond to her commands rather than acting entirely according to their original intentions.[7]

###### Charm and Mental Influence
- Divine Charm: Aphrodite can release a supernatural attraction derived from her Divine authority, affecting the perceptions and emotional responses of those exposed to it.[1][7]
- Charm Inducement: Her power can make targets feel overwhelming attraction toward her beyond what ordinary physical appearance would produce.[1]
- Mind Manipulation: Charm can interfere with conscious judgment strongly enough to influence behavior and decision-making.[7]
- Desire Manipulation: Aphrodite can affect supernatural attraction and desire through the interaction of her Beauty, Love and Sexuality-related powers.[1][7]
- Emotional Manipulation: The Charm alters emotional responses, causing affected individuals to develop feelings and priorities that can conflict with their original judgment.[1][7]
- Perception Manipulation: Aphrodite's presence influenced [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s perception during their first encounter, causing him to perceive characteristics that reminded him of the women he already loved.[1]
- Divine-Level Charm: Aphrodite can influence other Gods rather than being restricted to ordinary Mortals or weaker supernatural species.[7]
- Mass Charm: Her Charm can affect large groups simultaneously. During the Greek conflict she spread her influence through the forces of [[Zeus]] and [[Kronos]].[7]
- Command Inducement: Targets sufficiently affected by Charm can become compelled to follow Aphrodite's instructions.[7]
- Servitude Inducement: Several affected Gods displayed submissive behavior and demonstrated willingness to obey Aphrodite during the Greek confrontation.[7]
- Battlefield Control: Her Charm can disrupt the actions of powerful combatants by influencing their emotions and priorities rather than physically overpowering them.[7]
- Passive Charm-Like Presence: Her evolved Divine Beauty can create attraction resembling a weaker passive Charm without a conscious activation of the full technique.[16]

###### Charm Resistance and Countermeasures
Aphrodite's Charm is exceptionally dangerous but not absolute. During the confrontation involving [[Erebus]] and [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]], her ordinary full-power Charm was insufficient to control Original [[Primordial Gods]]. Erebus explicitly identified their primordial nature as a reason that the technique could not simply dominate them.[11]
- Primordial Resistance Limitation: Original Primordial Gods demonstrated resistance to Aphrodite's ordinary Charm.[11]
- Conceptual Resistance Limitation: Sufficiently powerful conceptual authorities can obstruct, neutralize or disrupt her influence.[11][7]
- Charm Disruption: The large-scale influence imposed during the Greek battle was eventually disrupted by an external power.[7]
- Power-Dependent Effectiveness: The result depends upon the target's nature, authority and available resistance rather than simply whether the individual is capable of perceiving Beauty.[11]
- Amplified Power Interaction: The Greek confrontation demonstrated that the effectiveness of Divine influence could change when additional supernatural power entered the encounter. Such interactions should not be treated as proof that Aphrodite's unaided Charm automatically bypasses all Primordial resistance.[11]

##### Sexuality
Sexuality is one of Aphrodite's three major Divinities. She identifies it as a principal Concept alongside Love and Beauty, with natural relationships to Pleasure, reproduction and the continuation of Life. Her Divine Blessing associated with Sexuality can enhance intimate physical capabilities and provides an additional source of supernatural influence over the relevant bodily and emotional processes.[3][4]
- Sexuality Manipulation: Aphrodite possesses genuine Divine authority over Sexuality and can influence supernatural characteristics associated with sexual activity, attraction and desire.[3]
- Sexual Empowerment: Her Blessing can improve the recipient's sexual stamina and performance. Aphrodite explicitly describes this effect when transferring her powers to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[4]
- Sexual Desire Influence: Her principal Divinity provides supernatural influence over aspects of sexual desire and attraction, although the exact extent of direct control over another individual's desires has not been comprehensively demonstrated.[3][4]
- Reproductive Association: Sexuality possesses a natural conceptual relationship with reproduction because the creation of new life frequently involves sexual processes.[4]
- Conceptual Synergy: Aphrodite's Sexuality authority overlaps with Love, Pleasure and the Perpetuation of Life while remaining a distinct Concept.[3][4]
- Blessing Bestowal: Aphrodite can transfer an expression of her Sexuality authority to another Being as a Divine Blessing.[4]

##### Pleasure
Pleasure is one of Aphrodite's lesser Divinities and is closely related to Sexuality while remaining independently applicable to a much broader range of sensations. Aphrodite explains that Pleasure can include gentle, sexual and sadistic experiences, and that its effects are useful even in situations completely unrelated to romance.[3][4]
- Pleasure Manipulation: Aphrodite possesses Divine authority over Pleasure and can influence pleasurable sensations through supernatural means.[4]
- Pleasure Inducement: She can cause a target to experience pleasurable sensations through an application of her Divinity.[4]
- Pleasure Amplification: Her authority can increase the intensity of pleasurable sensations experienced by a target.[6][4]
- Sensory Manipulation: Aphrodite can interfere with the way sensations are experienced, including circumstances where a normally unpleasant physical experience is interpreted differently.[4]
- Pain-to-Pleasure Conversion: Aphrodite explicitly describes making a person experience Pleasure in place of the Pain ordinarily associated with torture.[4]
- Sensory Response Alteration: Her authority can change the emotional or sensory response accompanying a physical experience.[4]
- Torture Application: Aphrodite uses Pleasure as a method of torture, explaining that replacing Pain with Pleasure can become more psychologically disturbing than conventional physical suffering.[4]
- Sadistic Pleasure Inducement: Her authority encompasses pleasurable sensations arising in circumstances that are normally violent, uncomfortable or painful.[4]
- Gentle Pleasure Inducement: The same Concept can produce pleasurable experiences without requiring aggression or physical harm.[4]
- Divine Pleasure Blessing: Aphrodite can grant an expression of her Pleasure authority to another individual, allowing the recipient to develop related effects through [[Divine Energy]].[4]

##### Joy
Joy is a lesser Divinity governing happiness and related positive emotional experiences. Aphrodite describes its Blessing as an empathic power that allows the recipient to understand what makes another person happy and perceive emotions more easily.[3][4]
- Joy Manipulation: Aphrodite possesses Divine authority over Joy, a Concept associated with happiness and positive emotional experience.[3]
- Empathy: The Blessing associated with Joy allows the recipient to sense emotional information concerning other individuals.[4]
- Emotion Perception: Aphrodite's authority provides supernatural understanding of emotional responses rather than relying exclusively upon physical expressions.[4]
- Happiness Perception: The Blessing allows recognition of what another person likes or what is likely to make that individual happy.[4]
- Emotional Understanding: The power can assist in understanding another individual's emotional preferences and sources of happiness.[4]
- Joy-Related Emotional Influence: Aphrodite possesses conceptual authority over Joy, although a separately demonstrated technique forcing arbitrary targets into a permanent state of happiness has not been established.[3][4]
- Joy Blessing Bestowal: Aphrodite transferred this authority to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] as one of the Blessings granted during their [[Soul Marriage]].[4]

##### Perpetuation of Life
Perpetuation of Life is a lesser authority associated with the continuation of living beings, reproduction and the development of new generations. Aphrodite explains that this Concept is related to Sexuality because sexual reproduction is one of the principal mechanisms through which Life continues. Although she originally considered the Divinity comparatively undeveloped, its practical importance increased enormously during the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s expansion.[3][4][8]

###### Life-Related Abilities
- Limited Life Manipulation: Aphrodite possesses an authority connected to Life and its continuation. She explains that sufficient training allows the power to develop limited applications of Life Manipulation.[4]
- Life Perception: The Blessing can provide supernatural sensitivity to Life as a phenomenon.[4]
- Life Energy Affinity: Her authority has a positive connection to Life-related supernatural principles rather than being inherently aligned with Hell's negative characteristics.[4]
- Reproductive Manipulation: Aphrodite can influence reproduction through the conceptual connection between the Perpetuation of Life and Sexuality.[4][8]
- Fertility Manipulation: Aphrodite can bestow or coordinate Blessings that increase the reproductive potential of populations. Her role in the Empire's accelerated development explicitly involved making its inhabitants more fertile.[8]
- Fertility Blessing: Her Divine influence can be granted as a reproductive Blessing, allowing its effects to operate upon individuals and populations rather than requiring Aphrodite's continuous physical presence.[8]
- Population Growth Support: Aphrodite's Blessings contributed to a tremendous increase in the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s population during the accelerated development project.[31]
- Reproductive Support Across Species: The Imperial program required Blessings applicable to different supernatural [[Races (Flamel)|Races]]. The same fertility-related principle was intended to assist the [[Heavenly Father]]'s Angels in reproducing more rapidly.[8]
- Coordinated Life Blessings: Aphrodite works alongside Goddesses associated with Fertility, Life, Motherhood, Marriage and related Concepts to support reproduction and population development.[31]
- Long-Term Demographic Influence: The combination of Blessings and Imperial development contributed to a society in which exceptionally large families became increasingly common among some populations.[32]
- Individual Fertility Support: The importance of Aphrodite's Blessing remained recognizable within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family. During the preparations surrounding [[Tyche]]'s pregnancy, Anna explicitly identified Aphrodite's Blessing as one of the reasons conception was expected to succeed.[33]

###### Anti-Hell Characteristics
Aphrodite describes her Life-related authority as particularly dangerous to beings from Hell because Life is associated with positive supernatural principles and has a relationship with Light. The effect is connected to the nature of the power rather than a specifically demonstrated universal ability to destroy all Demons.[4]
- Positive Energy Affinity: Her lesser Life authority possesses a supernatural alignment that can be dangerous to beings associated with Hell.[4]
- Anti-Demonic Potential: Life-related [[Divine Energy]] can threaten beings whose supernatural nature conflicts with its positive characteristics.[4]
- Life-Light Association: Aphrodite explicitly identifies a relationship between Life and Light when explaining the consequences of her Blessing.[4]

##### War
War is Aphrodite's seventh established Divinity and an authority she independently developed during her travels. Unlike her major Concepts, it was not among her original defining specialties. Aphrodite specifically acknowledges that her comprehension of War cannot compare with dedicated War Gods such as [[Ares]], although she developed it sufficiently to provide meaningful knowledge and apply it through Divine Blessings and offensive techniques.[6][4]
- Minor War Manipulation: Aphrodite possesses genuine but comparatively undeveloped conceptual authority over War.[4]
- War Perception: Her Divinity provides an improved understanding of warfare as a supernatural Concept.[4]
- Instinctive War Knowledge: The authority can provide knowledge concerning the mechanisms through which conflicts begin, continue and end.[4]
- Military Understanding: The Blessing associated with War enhances the recipient's conceptual perception of military conflict.[4]
- War Initiation Knowledge: Aphrodite explains that the authority can provide instinctive knowledge relevant to beginning a war.[4]
- War Prolongation Knowledge: The power can assist in understanding how a conflict might be maintained or prolonged.[4]
- War Termination Knowledge: The authority can provide knowledge relevant to bringing a war to an end.[4]
- Strategic Understanding: The Divinity supplements analysis of conflict, although Aphrodite explicitly warns that its effectiveness depends upon additional knowledge and experience.[4]
- Conceptual Weapon Enhancement: Aphrodite can incorporate War into powers that are not inherently offensive. Her arrows created through Love and Beauty gained destructive properties through the addition of War.[6]
- War Blessing Bestowal: Aphrodite granted [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] an expression of the War Divinity during their [[Soul Marriage]].[4]

#### Divine Archery
Aphrodite possesses a distinctive ranged combat technique that combines several of her Divinities into one supernatural weapon. During her training with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], she demonstrated that Concepts ordinarily associated with attraction and positive emotions could become dangerous offensive powers when properly combined with an authority possessing destructive applications.[6]
- Divine Archery: Aphrodite can employ a supernatural bow and arrows formed through [[Divine Energy]].[6]
- Energy Bow Creation: She can construct a pink Divine bow rather than relying upon an ordinary physical weapon.[6]
- Conceptual Arrow Creation: Her arrows can be formed through the interaction of Beauty and Love.[6]
- War Imbuement: Aphrodite adds her minor War authority to the projectile to give it genuine offensive properties.[6]
- Multi-Divinity Combination: The technique demonstrates the ability to combine several different Concepts within a single attack.[6]
- Divine Energy Compression: Aphrodite can concentrate conceptual Energy into the relatively small form of a projectile instead of dispersing it across a wide area.[6]
- Concentrated Piercing Power: The resulting attack can concentrate destructive force into a focused impact rather than requiring a large explosion.[6]
- Controlled Offensive Output: Aphrodite's approach emphasizes focused destructive capability rather than unnecessary environmental devastation.[6]
- Non-Destructive Concept Weaponization: Her technique demonstrates that a Concept not ordinarily associated with direct physical damage can gain offensive applications through interaction with a compatible secondary Divinity.[6]

#### Blessings and Power Bestowal
Aphrodite can grant other beings supernatural powers associated with her Divinities. The transfer of Blessings does not necessarily reproduce the complete Godhood or conceptual mastery of the original Goddess; instead, the recipient receives an expression of the relevant authority that can be used or developed according to their own supernatural capabilities.[3][4]

##### Personal Blessings
- Beauty Blessing: Enhances supernatural attractiveness and provides an expression of Aphrodite's Beauty authority. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually learned to regulate the influence consciously.[29][21][30]
- Love Blessing: Grants an expression of Aphrodite's authority over Love and its associated emotional influence.[4]
- Sexuality Blessing: Enhances intimate physical capabilities and provides a connection to her Sexuality Divinity.[4]
- Pleasure Blessing: Allows the recipient to influence pleasurable sensations, including effects connected to pain and sensory responses.[4]
- Joy Blessing: Provides an empathic understanding of another individual's happiness and emotional preferences.[4]
- Perpetuation of Life Blessing: Grants sensitivity to Life and the potential for limited Life-related manipulation with appropriate development.[4]
- War Blessing: Provides enhanced understanding and instinctive knowledge relevant to beginning, prolonging or ending conflicts.[4]

##### Blessing Transfer to Victor
Following their [[Soul Marriage]], Aphrodite transferred several Blessings to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], explicitly granting him the powers associated with Sexuality, Love, Perpetuation of Life, Pleasure, Joy and War. Victor had already possessed a Beauty-related Blessing originating from the circumstances surrounding Adonis.[29][4]
- Multiple Blessing Bestowal: Aphrodite can transfer several independent expressions of her Divinities to the same recipient.[4]
- [[Divine Energy]] Transfer: Her Blessings establish supernatural Energy associated with her authorities within another Being.[21][4]
- Blessing Compatibility Instruction: Aphrodite understands how Blessings interact with the recipient's supernatural constitution and helped Victor identify the Divine Energy concealed among his other powers.[21][6]
- Power Development Guidance: Aphrodite can explain the functions and limitations of her Blessings, allowing a recipient to cultivate applications beyond the initial passive effect.[4]

##### Planetary Blessing Capacity
By her later Imperial development, Aphrodite possessed extraordinarily large reserves of [[Divine Energy]] and estimated that she could extend a Blessing over an entire planet if necessary. This statement reflects her available Divine resources and their potential application rather than proof that every Blessing automatically affects all beings across a planet without limitations.[12]
- Planetary-Scale Blessing Potential: Aphrodite possesses sufficient Divine Energy to contemplate blessing an entire planet.[12]
- Large-Scale Power Bestowal: Her developed Divine foundation can support the distribution of Blessings beyond ordinary individual targets.[12]
- Sustained Divine Influence: A considerable portion of her Blessings remained connected to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] while Aphrodite continued developing her own Divine power.[12]

##### Imperial Fertility Blessings
Aphrodite's greatest demonstrated non-combat application of Divine authority concerns the demographic expansion of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s accelerated development plan required enormous populations capable of occupying and maintaining the Empire's expanding territories. Aphrodite was explicitly assigned responsibility for improving fertility, while the [[Heavenly Father]]'s Angels were also intended to benefit from a related reproductive Blessing.[8]
- Fertility Blessing: Aphrodite can distribute supernatural support that increases reproductive potential.[8]
- Mass Fertility Enhancement: The Blessing can be applied across populations rather than remaining restricted to a single individual.[8]
- Reproductive Support: Aphrodite's power contributes to improving the likelihood of successful reproduction under the conditions established by the Imperial program.[8][33]
- Multispecies Application: The population program was intended to support different [[Races (Flamel)|Races]], including Mortal populations and Angels.[8]
- Coordinated Divine Blessings: Aphrodite and subordinate Goddesses combined their authorities over related Concepts to support the Empire's population growth.[31]
- Civilization-Wide Demographic Support: Her Blessings contributed to the extraordinary increase in population during the accelerated Imperial era.[31][32]
- Long-Term Reproductive Influence: The cumulative demographic effects persisted across extended periods of Imperial development rather than functioning as an isolated one-time phenomenon.[31][32]
- Individual Fertility Support: Within Victor's family, Aphrodite's Blessing remained relevant to pregnancies involving exceptionally powerful Divine and Draconic parents.[33]

#### Soul Marriage
Aphrodite possesses the Divine knowledge necessary to establish a genuine [[Soul Marriage]], a supernatural union operating at a deeper level than ordinary legal marriage or the standard Vampire marriage ritual. Her marriage to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] directly connected their Souls through Fate, creating a metaphysical bond that Aphrodite described as capable of enduring beyond the destruction of their current bodies.[4]

##### Soul and Fate Connection
- [[Soul Marriage]]: Aphrodite can participate in a Divine marriage ritual that connects the Souls of the participants rather than merely establishing a legal or biological relationship.[4]
- Soul Bond Creation: The ritual establishes a direct supernatural connection between the Souls of the individuals involved.[4]
- Fate Connection: Aphrodite explains that Soul Marriage links the participants through Fate, allowing their connection to persist beyond ordinary separation.[4]
- Reincarnation Bond: The connected individuals are destined to encounter one another in future lives so long as the Concept of a Soul continues to exist.[4]
- Persistent Soul Connection: Death alone does not automatically terminate a properly established Soul Marriage.[4]
- Metaphysical Union: The bond concerns the spiritual existence of the participants rather than depending exclusively upon physical proximity or biological compatibility.[4]
- Divine Ritual Knowledge: Aphrodite understands the requirements and consequences of Soul Marriage and can explain them to other supernatural beings.[4]

##### Marriage Requirements
Aphrodite explains that genuine [[Soul Marriage]] ordinarily requires Gods or exceptional beings possessing a developed relationship with Concepts. The ritual therefore cannot be treated as a common spell available to every Vampire or supernatural creature.[4]
- Conceptual Prerequisite: The participants must possess an adequate conceptual foundation or belong to a category of supernatural beings capable of sustaining the union.[4]
- Divine Compatibility: Godhood provides one of the foundations through which the ritual can be performed.[4]
- Advanced Spiritual Requirement: The bond operates upon the Souls of the participants rather than exclusively upon their physical bodies.[4]
- Marriage Instruction: Aphrodite explained the ritual to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s other wives and encouraged them to strengthen themselves until they could establish comparable connections.[4]

##### Relationship to Vampire Marriage
Aphrodite identified weaknesses within the conventional Vampire marriage system, particularly when applied to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s exceptionally large family. She regarded [[Soul Marriage]] as a deeper spiritual union that could eventually replace some of the dependence and instability associated with the existing arrangement.[4]
- Vampiric Marriage Knowledge: Aphrodite understands the supernatural consequences of the ordinary Vampire marriage ritual.[4]
- Bloodlust-Related Understanding: She recognized the complications that could arise from a large number of Vampiric spouses maintaining blood-related dependencies upon one individual.[4]
- Advanced Marriage Proposal: Aphrodite proposed that Victor's other wives eventually obtain the conceptual foundation necessary for Soul Marriage rather than remaining permanently limited to the original ritual.[4]
Limitation: Soul Marriage establishes a powerful spiritual and Fate-related connection. The ritual does not automatically grant Aphrodite control over reincarnation itself, make both participants incapable of suffering death or guarantee that no superior conceptual authority can interfere with their Souls.

#### Mass-Scale Conceptual Influence
Aphrodite possesses demonstrated abilities affecting groups, battlefields, planetary populations and Imperial civilization. These applications originate from several different Divinities and should not be combined into a single unrestricted ability to control every individual across an arbitrary distance.

##### Large-Area Emotional Influence
- Thirty-Kilometer Love Influence: Aphrodite's uncontrolled Love release affected individuals across approximately thirty kilometers.[5]
- Mass Emotional Amplification: The affected individuals experienced intensified romantic feelings, including the urge to confess hidden affection or pursue former relationships.[5]
- Passive Area Influence: The manifestation occurred through Aphrodite's own emotional state rather than through deliberate selection of every affected individual.[5]

##### Battlefield-Wide Divine Charm
- Mass Divine Charm: Aphrodite affected numerous Greek Gods during the battle involving [[Zeus]] and [[Kronos]].[7]
- Mass Behavioral Influence: The affected Gods displayed altered behavior and willingness to follow Aphrodite's directions.[7]
- Divine Battlefield Disruption: Her power interfered with the ability of powerful Gods to continue acting according to their original intentions.[7]
- Conceptual Resistance Limitation: The effect could be disrupted by sufficiently powerful opposing supernatural influence.[7]

##### Planetary and Civilizational Applications
- Planetary Blessing Potential: Aphrodite possessed enough available [[Divine Energy]] to contemplate extending a Blessing across an entire planet.[12]
- Imperial Fertility Enhancement: Aphrodite was assigned responsibility for improving reproductive potential throughout the Empire.[8]
- Population Blessing Network: Aphrodite and subordinate Goddesses distributed related Blessings across Imperial populations during accelerated development.[31]
- Long-Term Population Support: Her work contributed to the enormous demographic growth achieved throughout the Imperial era.[31][32]

#### Faith and Divine Administration
Aphrodite possesses extensive knowledge concerning the relationship between worship, Divine authority, Blessings and religious institutions. Her existence as an ancient Olympian already involved organized worship, while her later role within the [[Elderblood Empire]] placed her among the Goddesses responsible for administering Divine influence across numerous worlds.[12][31]

##### Faith
- Faith Reception: Aphrodite receives worship and prayers directed toward her Divine identity.[12]
- Prayer Reception: She can receive religious devotion from worshippers belonging to the Empire's populations.[12]
- Faith Management: Aphrodite monitors her religious standing and the amount of attention devoted to her relative to other Goddesses.[12]
- Religious Influence: Her Divine status and established reputation allow her to maintain worshippers within an expanding supernatural civilization.[12]
- Faith-Based Development: Aphrodite understands the importance of worshippers to the functioning and development of a Divine civilization.[12]
- Saint Appointment Potential: She considered establishing a Saint as a means of increasing the reach of her religion and the number of prayers directed toward her. The consideration should not be classified as proof that a particular Saint had already been appointed.[12]

##### Divine Administration
- Divine Population Administration: Aphrodite became one of the principal Goddesses responsible for Imperial reproductive development.[8]
- Subordinate Goddess Coordination: She worked alongside Goddesses associated with Life, Fertility, Motherhood, Marriage and related Concepts.[31]
- Large-Scale Blessing Administration: Aphrodite's Divine influence was integrated into an organized system affecting large populations over extended periods.[31][32]
- Religious Administration: Aphrodite possesses the experience necessary to oversee worship and Divine institutions within the Empire.[12]
- Greek Divine Community Leadership: Aphrodite became one of the leading Goddesses associated with the reconstructed Greek Divine civilization on an Imperial mega-planet.[34]
- Cultural Preservation: Her later position allowed Greek religious traditions and Divine institutions to survive without restoring the former government of [[Zeus]].[35][34]

#### Skills
Aphrodite's practical knowledge and non-combat expertise are important because her threat and usefulness do not depend exclusively upon direct applications of Divine power. She accumulated billions of years of experience interacting with Gods, rulers, supernatural societies and religious institutions, giving her an understanding of Divine politics and conceptual abilities that is unusual even among powerful members of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family.

##### Divine and Conceptual Knowledge
- Ancient Divine Knowledge: Aphrodite possesses extensive knowledge of the history, customs and supernatural systems of ancient Gods and Pantheons.[3]
- Conceptual Understanding: She understands the relationship between Divinities, [[Divine Energy]], Blessings and the development of conceptual authority.[3][4]
- Divine Energy Control: Aphrodite can recognize, regulate and manipulate the Energy associated with her different Divinities.[21][6]
- Energy Separation: She understands how to identify a particular Divine influence within a supernatural body containing several different Energy systems. This knowledge allowed her to guide [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] toward recognizing her Beauty Blessing.[21][6]
- Multi-Concept Manipulation: Aphrodite can combine different Divinities for a unified purpose, as demonstrated through her conceptual bow and arrows.[6]
- Divine Instruction: She taught Victor to perceive and consciously control a Divine Blessing rather than allowing it to remain an involuntary influence.[21][6]
- Blessing Knowledge: Aphrodite understands the powers, dangers and political consequences associated with Divine Blessings.[36][4]
- [[Soul Marriage]] Knowledge: She possesses the understanding necessary to perform and explain a Divine marriage ritual connecting Souls through Fate.[4]
- Godhood Development Knowledge: Aphrodite understands that increasing Divine power requires developing the comprehension and authority associated with a Concept rather than merely accumulating Energy.[3][28]

##### Combat Skills
- Divine Archery: Aphrodite possesses sufficient proficiency with a bow to employ a supernatural weapon constructed from [[Divine Energy]].[6]
- Conceptual Weapon Creation: She can form offensive weapons through the combination of Beauty, Love and War.[6]
- Divinity-Based Combat: Aphrodite can employ Divine influence as a combat method, including Charm, conceptual projectiles and supernatural command effects.[6][7][9]
- Battlefield Interference: Her Charm allows her to disrupt the decisions and actions of powerful combatants without requiring direct physical confrontation.[7]
- Strategic Knowledge: Her minor War authority provides instinctive information concerning military conflicts, while her ancient political experience supplies additional practical context.[4]
- Combat Support: Aphrodite's Blessings and conceptual influence can support allies by enhancing their abilities or affecting opponents.[4][7]
Aphrodite has not been established as possessing the same dedicated martial specialization as [[Scathach]], Kali or other warriors whose primary expertise centers upon close combat and destructive techniques.

##### Social and Political Skills
- Social Intelligence: Aphrodite possesses exceptional experience interacting with individuals from different supernatural cultures, including Gods, Demons, Vampires and Humans.[37][5]
- Persuasion: She can negotiate, flatter and influence individuals through ordinary social understanding independently of her supernatural Charm.[37]
- Political Intelligence: Aphrodite understands the relationships, rivalries and hidden interests of powerful Divine factions.[5]
- Diplomacy: She maintains relationships across different Pantheons and can communicate with influential Gods outside the Greek political structure.[5][24]
- Information Gathering: Aphrodite possesses a network of supernatural contacts capable of obtaining information concerning Divine activities and political developments.[5]
- Informant Coordination: During her alliance with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Aphrodite gathered intelligence through the individuals and acquaintances connected to her network.[5]
- Inter-Pantheon Networking: Her acquaintances include influential figures from Divine societies outside Greece, such as [[Freyja|Freya]] and [[Loki]].[24]
- Deception Recognition: Aphrodite is experienced with Divine political manipulation and can recognize attempts to deceive or exploit another individual.[26][5]
- Lie Detection: Aphrodite demonstrates the ability to recognize deception, although the story does not establish that she possesses an infallible truth-detection Concept capable of bypassing every concealment technique.[26]
- Strategic Judgment: Aphrodite recognized that the collapse of [[Olympus]] created opportunities to preserve useful resources, knowledge and individuals rather than allowing everything accumulated by the Pantheon to disappear.[38]
- Cultural Adaptability: Aphrodite successfully maintained a long-term Human identity as Renata and developed ordinary relationships outside Divine society.[37]

##### Imperial Administration
- Religious Leadership: Aphrodite possesses the experience necessary to oversee worship and Divine institutions.[12]
- Fertility Administration: She coordinates Divine support intended to increase reproductive potential throughout the Empire.[8]
- Population Planning: Aphrodite's responsibilities contribute directly to the demographic requirements of Imperial expansion.[8][31]
- Divine Resource Management: She understands the allocation of Blessings and the relationship between [[Divine Energy]], worship and the requirements of large populations.[12][31]
- Civilizational Leadership: Aphrodite participates in governing the reconstructed Greek Divine community within the [[Elderblood Empire]].[34]
- Supernatural Education: Her experience allows her to explain Divine powers and conceptual principles to less knowledgeable individuals.[21][4]

#### Resistances and Survivability
Aphrodite's survivability combines the supernatural characteristics of an ancient Goddess with the superior physical and spiritual foundation of a True Dragon. However, the extraordinary offensive capabilities of her Divinities should not automatically be interpreted as complete immunity to every related power. Specific resistances are best distinguished between demonstrated defensive feats and general characteristics of her current racial existence.

##### Divine Resistances
- Resistance to Divine Influence: Aphrodite can protect herself through her own Divinity against hostile supernatural influence. During the [[Greek Underworld]] confrontation, she and Nyx covered themselves with Divine power and dispelled [[Erebus]]'s initial influence.[11]
- Divinity-Based Defense: Aphrodite can employ her Divine authority defensively rather than restricting it to Charm, Blessings or emotional effects.[11]
- Supernatural Energy Resistance: Her ancient Goddess physiology provides resistance to supernatural forces beyond those an ordinary Mortal body could withstand.[1][11]
- Resistance to Aging: Her Divine existence does not suffer ordinary biological aging, and she retained this characteristic after becoming a True Dragon.[14][2]
- Divine Spiritual Resilience: Aphrodite possesses the spiritual foundation of an ancient Goddess whose Godhood survived integration into a new Draconic existence.[2]

##### Draconic Resistances
- True Dragon Durability: Aphrodite's current body possesses the superior physical resilience characteristic of genuine [[True Dragons]].[2]
- Draconic Regenerative Capacity: Her racial constitution possesses the restorative foundation associated with the Blood Dragon lineage.[2]
- Supernatural Compatibility: Aphrodite's Draconic existence can sustain her ancient Divine authorities without rejecting them as incompatible racial powers.[2]
- Immortal Physiology: The combination of her ancient Godhood and True Dragon nature provides longevity beyond mortal biological limitations.[14][2]

##### Conceptual Awareness
- Awareness of Emotional Manipulation: Aphrodite possesses exceptional knowledge concerning the powers associated with Love, Beauty, Sexuality, Pleasure and Joy.[3][4]
- Charm Expertise: Her mastery allows her to understand how supernatural attraction and Divine Charm function, making her exceptionally knowledgeable about the nature of such attacks.[1][7]
- Conceptual Defense Knowledge: Aphrodite understands that Divine influence can be resisted or nullified by sufficiently powerful opposing authorities.[11][7]
Her expertise in these Concepts does not independently establish absolute immunity to every form of emotional or mental manipulation. No separate feat demonstrates that she can resist all stronger Love authorities, Soul attacks or Reality-altering effects merely because related concepts belong to her Godhood.

#### Limitations
- Charm Is Not Absolute: Original [[Primordial Gods]] demonstrated resistance to Aphrodite's ordinary full-power Charm, and powerful external influence could disrupt her battlefield-wide effects.[11][7]
- Passive Emotional Influence: Aphrodite's Love and Beauty can manifest without deliberate activation. Their effects may require conscious restraint when she is interacting with individuals who lack adequate supernatural resistance.[5][16]
- Incomplete Primordial Evolution: Aphrodite was explicitly described as almost becoming a Primordial Being of Love. The statement does not confirm that she has already completed the transformation or possesses unrestricted universal authority over Love.[12]
- Minor War Authority: Her War Divinity is comparatively undeveloped, and Aphrodite does not regard herself as equivalent to dedicated War Gods such as [[Ares]].[4]
- Originally Limited Life Authority: Aphrodite initially described the Perpetuation of Life as a lesser Concept that she had not explored as thoroughly as her principal authorities. Its later fertility applications do not establish unrestricted Life Manipulation.[4][8]
- Soul Perception Limitation: Aphrodite could not freely examine the entirety of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s abnormal Soul because the extraordinary existence within it exceeded her ordinary perception.[15]
- Conditional [[Soul Marriage]]: Soul Marriage requires an adequate conceptual or Divine foundation and cannot be performed as an ordinary spell by any supernatural being.[4]
- Blessing Compatibility: Divine powers can be dangerous to recipients possessing incompatible supernatural constitutions. Aphrodite herself warned Victor that consuming a Goddess's Blood could be fatal to an ordinary Creature of the Night.[21]
- Conceptual Resistance: The effectiveness of Aphrodite's influence depends upon the nature and authority of the target. Possessing a Divinity does not guarantee control over every Being associated with that Concept.[11][7]
- No Established Omniscience: Aphrodite's enormous knowledge of Love, Beauty, Divine politics and emotional relationships does not grant complete knowledge of every thought, event or relationship throughout existence.
- No Established Universal Mind Control: Her Charm can influence powerful Gods, but she has not demonstrated the ability to control every Being regardless of resistance or conceptual hierarchy.[11][7]
- No Established Unlimited Reality Warping: Her command against [[Eros]] demonstrates a powerful Reality-enforced declaration, not unrestricted authority to alter every law or phenomenon in the Cosmos.[9]
- Anti-Dragon Weakness: As a genuine True Dragon, Aphrodite belongs to a Race with vulnerabilities to Anti-Dragon properties. Her individual defensive performance against specialized Anti-Dragon weapons has not been separately established.[39]
- Incomplete Runic Mastery Record: Her True Dragon foundation provides instinctive Draconic Language and Rune knowledge, but the novel does not establish that she has personally mastered every advanced Runic technique used by Victor or the Imperial specialists.[18]
- Combat Specialization: Aphrodite is exceptionally dangerous through Divine influence, Blessings and conceptual authority, but her greatest demonstrated strengths are not the same as the close-combat specializations of [[Scathach]] or Kali.

#### Former Classification
Aphrodite's original Divine existence should not be treated as an unrelated collection of abilities permanently discarded during her Dragon transformation. Her original Godhood, accumulated Concepts and spiritual foundation became integrated into the Blood Dragon Goddess she subsequently became.[2]

##### Original Second-Generation Primordial Goddess
Before her transformation, Aphrodite possessed the supernatural foundation of an ancient Greek Goddess originating from [[Ouranos]]. She developed Divine authority over Love, Beauty and Sexuality, together with lesser Concepts and an independently acquired War Divinity.[1][3][4]
- Original God Physiology: Aphrodite possessed the supernatural constitution of an ancient Divine Being.[1]
- Second-Generation Primordial Origin: Her original Divine existence originated from a primordial foundation associated with Ouranos.[1]
- [[Divine Energy]]: Her powers operated through the Divine Energy and conceptual authority of her original Goddess existence.[6]
- Major Divinities: Love, Beauty and Sexuality were her principal established authorities.[3]
- Lesser Divinities: Perpetuation of Life, Pleasure and Joy were independently existing but less-developed authorities associated with her principal Concepts.[3]
- Acquired War Divinity: Aphrodite developed a minor authority over War during her travels.[4]
- Divine Charm: Her Love and Beauty provided powerful influence over attraction, emotion and behavior even before her transformation into a True Dragon.[1][7]
- Blessing and Curse Bestowal: Aphrodite could grant Divine effects and impose supernatural Curses through her original Godhood.[25][3][4]
