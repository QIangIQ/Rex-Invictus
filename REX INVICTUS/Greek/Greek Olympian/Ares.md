**[[Ares]]** is the [[Olympians|Olympian]] god of war, violence, courage and bloodlust. He is the son of [[Zeus]] and [[Hera]], lover of [[Aphrodite]], and father of [[Phobos]], [[Deimos]], Harmonia, the Amazons and many demigods. His Roman counterpart is [[Mars Ultor|Mars]]. In the Empire he is the [[Divine Being 'Eternal Warmonger'|Eternal Warmonger]].

## Greek History (IRL)

**Character.** Unlike [[Athena]], who stands for strategy, Ares embodies the frenzy of battle. Homer's [[Zeus]] calls him the most hateful of the gods. He was wounded by Diomedes with [[Athena]]'s help in the [[Trojan War]], where he fought for Troy.

**Myths.** [[Aphrodite]]'s husband [[Hephaestus]] caught them in bed with an unbreakable net and displayed them to the gods. He killed Halirrhothius, son of [[Poseidon]], for assaulting his daughter Alcippe and was tried on the Areopagus, the first murder trial. The giants Otus and [[Ephialtes]] imprisoned him in a bronze jar for thirteen months until [[Hermes]] freed him.

**Companions and symbols.** He rode with Phobos (fear), Deimos (terror), Enyo and Eris. His animals are the dog and the vulture. Thrace was his homeland. In Sparta he was worshipped as Enyalios. The Romans honored Mars much more highly as the father of [[Romulus and Remus]].

**Primary sources:** Homer's *Iliad* and *Odyssey*, Hesiod, Aeschylus' *Eumenides*.

## PJO Canon

In [[The Lightning Thief]] Ares appears as a biker in Las Vegas and Colorado. Working under [[Kronos]]'s influence, he steals [[Master Bolt]] and [[Helm of Darkness]] to start a war, then plants them on [[Percy Jackson|Percy]]. Percy defeats him in a duel on a California beach and wounds his heel with [[Riptide]], earning his curse. In [[The Sea of Monsters]] and later books his daughter [[Clarisse]] tries to win his approval. [[Ares' Cabin]] is Cabin 5.

In [[The Heroes of Olympus]] his Roman aspect [[Mars Ultor|Mars]] is the father of [[Frank Zhang]] and appears at Camp Jupiter. In [[The Trials of Apollo]] his children fight at the battle of Camp Jupiter.

#### Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **This file:** the 21st-century war god who ran the Fort Reagan exercise and held the [[Master Bolt]].
- **Not** the far-future Ares, "beside himself" after an alpha-core overload left his son Deimos bedridden on Mars ([[Ch05 - Intermission I|Ch5]]), whose altar on ruined [[Olympus Primaris]] still receives MRE offerings in the old timeline ([[Ch01 - Rex Imperator I|Ch1]]). See `E08_Hera_Ares_Hestia_Demeter_Future.md`.
- **Aspects:** [[Mars Ultor]] is his Roman aspect (and, per the author's vault, his main title). When [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] names it, "His form changed momentarily" ([[Ch10 - Rex Invictus III|Ch10]]). Percy also taunts him as "Huitzilopotchli", the Aztec war god, and his form flickers "between his alternate forms" when [[Minerva (AI)|Minerva]] speaks. In [[Ch16 - Rex Invictus IX|Ch16]] Eleni senses Huitzilopochtli as smelling "similar to Lord Mars, his counterpart", which treats them as parallel war gods of different houses. In [[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]] **Huitzilopochtli appears in person as a separate deity** who threatens [[Olympus]] (`E18_Amaterasu_and_Foreign_Pantheons.md`). The text never states that Ares and Huitzilopochtli are one being. Percy's taunt implies a link between aspects or domains, but treat them as distinct characters.
- **Knowledge boundary:** He knows Percy has a "mechanical creep" in his head, knows the Imperials exist and pray to him, and knows the Accord. He does not know Percy's old-timeline past.

#### Appearance
- "Clad in the motorcycle leather jacket outfit that [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] remembered seeing the god in the first time". He fills the doorway, and his aviators reflect gold light. They shatter when [[Minerva (AI)|Minerva]] intervenes ([[Ch10 - Rex Invictus III|Ch10]]).
- [[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]: "leather jacket and wraparound aviator sunglasses and combat boots… grinning like it was the best day of his life". His aura is red, "flickering at the edges like a burning city". He bleeds gold when rounds hit him.
- Lounges sideways in his throne with boots on the armrest ([[Ch19 - Rex Invictus XII|Ch19]], [[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]).

#### Personality
- **Bloodthirsty joy in combat.** "Oh, hell yes," he says, facing an 11-metre Titan. He walks through rotary-cannon fire laughing ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]).
- **Bully with a tactician's mind.** Thuggish, but "his mind was working too fast, too tactically" ([[Ch10 - Rex Invictus III|Ch10]]). He weighs the risk of exposure and yields the bolt.
- **Vain, sulky and resentful.** Mocked by the council for his army's failure, he slumps on his couch sharpening a javelin ([[Ch10 - Rex Invictus III|Ch10]]). He sulks that the Imperials squat on "my planet… and nobody so much as sends me a card" ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]).
- **Sore loser who holds grudges.** "You better pray to whatever that mechanical creep's whispering in your head, Cheat… She won't be there to save you" ([[Ch10 - Rex Invictus III|Ch10]]).
- **Afraid of [[Athena]].** "Ares Theritas was always afraid of the wrath [[Athena Parthenos]] would beat into him" (Ch10). [[Minerva (AI)|Minerva]] sounding "like Sister" unsettles him.
- **Hard-line about war realism.** He refuses General Mikhail's request to halt the exercise over food poisoning: "What would the soldiers do if they fell with food poisoning in a real war?" (Ch10). He wanted minimal civilian casualties in the exercise and berated his commanders when they failed.
- **Unexpectedly moved by worship.** When the Titan pilot Keyes recites the Mars catechism to his face, "The god's grin faltered… 'Huh'" ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]). He then fiercely claims the garrison: "if any of you touch them without asking me first, you'll answer to me."
- **Grudging respect for discipline.** On Eleni: "She is disciplined" ([[Ch15 - Rex Invictus VIII|Ch15]]).

#### Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Domain suppression:** his divine pressure makes fighting him feel "slimy… as if he were fighting underwater" ([[Ch10 - Rex Invictus III|Ch10]]).
- **Weapons:** a halberd and a blade whose heat blisters skin ([[Ch10 - Rex Invictus III|Ch10]]). He catches an Imperial energy blade barehanded, burned to the bone, and tears the Titan's arm off ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]).
- **Durability:** shrugs off rotary-cannon rounds. Some don't bounce, and "Blood ran gold down his jacket" ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]).
- **Teleporting duels:** transports [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] to the empty Colosseum (Flavian Amphitheater), an "Olympian-sanctioned battleground" where "no mortal eyes would witness a duel with Ares" ([[Ch10 - Rex Invictus III|Ch10]]).
- **The Fulminus artefact:** grows stronger nearer to it, obscures the senses of meddling gods, buffs his children and soldiers (a "mist-cloaked red tinge"), and suppresses non-Ares demigod powers. Whoever captures it can demand a minor favour ([[Ch09 - Rex Invictus II|Ch9]]–10).
- **Mars:** his planet. His sons Phobos and Deimos ("Phobos and Deimons" [sic], [[Apollo]], [[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]) keep Apollo's sight off it. Ares "covers" the Morrígan's domain in the Americas (Percy, [[Ch17 - Rex Invictus X|Ch17]]).
- **Resources:** built [[Area Fulminus]], "A quarter million acres of Nevada desert", for $6.3 billion, "the second biggest military training facility in the world" ([[Ch08 - Rex Invictus I|Ch8]]). He raises the CSS *Birmingham* for [[Clarisse]] (Percy's foreknowledge in [[Ch13 - Rex Invictus VI|Ch13]], confirmed in [[Ch14 - Rex Invictus VII|Ch14]]).

Ares, also known as Mars, is an ancient Third Generation Olympian God, the son of [[Zeus]] and [[Hera]], one of the former Twelve Olympians, and the principal Greek God of War and Violence. Unlike [[Athena]], whose Divine Authority represents Strategic War, Ares' specialization is explicitly identified as Violent War, representing the direct, brutal and combat-oriented aspects of warfare.[1]
Ares is one of the strongest combat-specialized Third Generation Gods produced by the [[Greek Pantheon]]. The author's official power-ranking explanation places him among the High-Level [[Combat Gods]], alongside figures such as [[Thor]], and specifically uses Ares as one of the benchmarks for the level of power [[Victor Elderblood]] could confront after properly utilizing his [[Vampire Count Form]].[2]
His strength does not come solely from raw [[Divine Energy]]. Ares spent thousands of years learning and perfecting martial arts and weapon techniques, making him one of the most experienced conventional combat Gods of Earth. Even [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], while evaluating Victor's martial potential, used Ares as the standard for a true God of War who had mastered enormous numbers of fighting disciplines.[3]
Following the destruction of [[Olympus]], Ares survived the collapse of the Greek Pantheon but lost his former independence. He was incorporated into Victor's Faction and later the [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]] beneath Victor's authority. Although vastly surpassed by Victor and the later Dragon Gods, his developed War Concept remains sufficiently valuable that he continued to be considered for important operations in the later story.[4]

### Physical Appearance
Ares is a tall, strongly built man whose normal appearance reflects his identity as a traditional Greek warrior. During the battle for [[Olympus]], Morgana identified him immediately by the ancient Spartan-style clothing he wore.[5]
His combat equipment includes Divine armor together with a spear and shield, weapons he can summon directly when preparing for serious battle. His appearance and equipment emphasize straightforward warfare rather than the elaborate magical or conceptual style used by many other Gods.[5]

### Personality
Ares is prideful, aggressive, battle-oriented and heavily attached to an idealized concept of honorable warfare. Although his Divinity represents violent conflict, he does not embrace every possible method of fighting. During his confrontation with [[Morgana Elderblood]], Ares became genuinely irritated by her deceptive tactics and complained that his opponents possessed no honor.[5]
Morgana openly mocked this mentality, explaining that war had no obligation to be honorable and that only death and victory mattered. The exchange highlighted one of Ares' greatest contradictions: despite being the God of Violent War, he still expects combatants to adhere to his own ideas of proper battle and can become frustrated when enemies exploit deception, environmental advantages or assassination tactics.[5]
Ares is nevertheless a highly experienced warrior rather than a mindless brute. His War Divinity provides him with unusually sharp combat instincts, and he trusts those instincts immediately when they warn him of danger. When he attempted to headbutt Morgana, Ares stopped at the last moment because his instinct told him the action was foolish—correctly realizing that Morgana's entire demonic body functioned as a weapon.[5]
He is also capable of evaluating wars rationally. During the [[Typhon]] crisis, Ares and [[Athena]]—despite disliking one another and embodying very different aspects of warfare—reached the same immediate conclusion: [[Olympus]] had no realistic path to victory against the Apocalypse Beast under the existing circumstances.[6]
Ares is a natural battlefield leader. After [[Zeus]] and Kronus were killed during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s assault, Ares and [[Atlas]] immediately became the remaining Greek army's principal commanders without requiring a lengthy political transition. The narration explicitly describes both as natural leaders.[7]
Despite his martial discipline, Ares possesses many of the personal flaws associated with the old Olympian Gods. He procrastinated heavily during his long life, and despite spending thousands of years mastering martial arts, he also devoted enormous amounts of time to sexual relationships with mortals. [[Scathach]] notes that Ares fathered enough children to populate a small town—approximately 20,000 children.[3]
This tendency toward excess contributed to the contrast Scathach drew between Ares and Victor: Ares possessed immense talent and time but frequently wasted both, while Victor obsessively trained without comparable distractions.[3]

### Divine Nature
Ares' principal Divine Authority is War, but his War Concept represents a specialized branch rather than every possible form of warfare.
Chapter 884 explicitly distinguishes three major War manifestations:
[[Athena]] — Strategic War
Ares — Violent War
War — Bloody/warfare-related manifestation
[[Victor Elderblood]] — War itself in its complete overarching form[1]
Ares therefore represents the direct violent side of battle: armed conflict, aggression, combat instinct, force and the destructive confrontation between opposing sides.
This distinction became especially important after Victor awakened the complete Concept of <War>. Victor did not inherit merely Ares' violent branch or Athena's strategic branch; his Divinity encompassed all forms of War simultaneously. Ares consequently ceased to represent the highest War Authority in the Sector once Victor's complete Concept awakened.[1]

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Ares was first mentioned long before he physically entered the story.
While watching [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s martial development, [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett|Scathach Scarlett]] compared his potential with that of a true God of War. She recalled that Ares had required thousands of years to master the many martial arts he had learned, demonstrating just how absurd Victor's learning speed appeared by comparison.[3]
[[Scathach]] nevertheless noted that Ares' slow progression was partly self-inflicted. Rather than dedicating every moment of his immortal existence to training, Ares frequently procrastinated and pursued mortal women, eventually fathering roughly twenty thousand children. Victor's comparatively obsessive focus convinced Scathach that he might eventually reach or surpass the level of martial mastery represented by Ares.[3]
Ares was referenced repeatedly thereafter as one of the established benchmarks for combat Gods. When Divine Blessings were explained, Ares was used as an example of a God possessing a Greater/Major War Divinity capable of granting War-related Blessings to lesser Gods, provided the recipient accepted the connection.[8]
The author's official ranking around Chapter 630 subsequently identified Ares and [[Thor]] as examples of High-Level [[Combat Gods]]. At that point, a fully transformed Victor using his [[Vampire Count Form]] was stated to possess enough combat capability to confront beings in Ares' class.[2]

#### Demonic War
Ares physically appeared during the crisis caused by [[Typhon]].
After the Apocalypse Beast's awakening became known throughout [[Olympus|Mount Olympus]], the Greek Gods temporarily stopped their civil conflict and gathered to determine whether they could defeat Typhon. Ares and [[Athena]] represented opposing aspects of warfare and ordinarily disliked one another, yet both reached the exact same conclusion simultaneously: under the present circumstances, defeating Typhon was impossible.[6]
The agreement was significant precisely because of their rivalry. Athena governed the strategic side of warfare while Ares represented its violent side; when both Gods independently analyzed the situation and reached the same conclusion, Olympus understood the severity of the threat.[6]
Shortly afterward, Olympus received even worse news. [[Aphrodite]], Nike, [[Hestia]], [[Demeter]], [[Rhea]], [[Tyche]] and dozens of other Goddesses had abandoned the Pantheon and stolen enormous quantities of its stored resources. Athena glanced toward Ares when she realized that Nike and Tyche had left, and the two again silently agreed that Olympus' chances had deteriorated drastically now that Victory and Luck no longer supported them.[6]
Ares survived the larger Demonic War and remained loyal to [[Zeus]]' side through the final confrontation surrounding Olympus.

#### King Among Kings
Ares became one of the principal combatants during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s direct invasion of the [[Greek Pantheon]].
When Victor activated Blood Age and created an enormous Blood Dragon from the battlefield, Ares joined [[Zeus]], [[Poseidon]] and [[Apollo]] in destroying the construct. Their attack succeeded, but the Dragon's destruction caused it to explode into corrosive Blood, damaging and contaminating the Gods who had attacked it.[9]
The battlefield rapidly became almost perfectly hostile to Ares. Victor's blood covered the environment, corpses continually piled around the Greek Gods and the surrounding Blood actively corroded whatever it touched. Ares was forced to continually spend Divine Power merely using his War Concept as protection against the environment.[5]
Victor then sent [[Morgana Elderblood]] against him. Newly empowered by the claws of [[Malphas]], Morgana attacked Ares with beams of destructive Energy before closing the distance. Ares avoided the initial attacks and used War to defend himself, but the battlefield itself placed him at a constant disadvantage: Ares had to continuously expend Power protecting himself while Morgana was actively strengthened by Victor's Blood surrounding them.[5]
When Morgana attempted to stab him, Ares caught both of her claws despite having his hands severely damaged in the process. Once he recognized her demonic nature, he criticized what he considered her dishonorable style of warfare. Morgana dismissed the complaint and reminded him that war concerned victory rather than honor.[5]
Ares attempted to pull Morgana toward himself for a headbutt before his supernatural battle instinct warned him to stop. He correctly realized that Morgana's entire body could function as a weapon and immediately disengaged.[5]
Deciding to fight seriously, Ares summoned his Divine spear and shield and challenged Morgana directly. Morgana refused to fight according to his expectations. Before Ares could properly begin the confrontation, she exploited his momentary lapse in awareness and pierced his heart with her claws.[5]
Ares began losing consciousness and likely would have been killed had Zeus not intervened. Zeus struck Morgana with lightning and created a protective lightning barrier around himself and his injured son.[5]
Rather than allowing pride to dictate his response, Ares immediately warned Zeus that [[Olympus]] was losing. He urged his father to use the Pantheon's Divine treasure and bring Olympus' territorial Authority directly onto the battlefield, recognizing that the existing conditions overwhelmingly favored Victor.[5]
Ares survived but remained heavily weakened by Morgana's attack during the subsequent stages of the war.[10]
The conflict escalated further when Victor killed both Zeus and Kronus. With Olympus suddenly deprived of its two most obvious leaders, Ares and [[Atlas]] naturally assumed battlefield command of the remaining Greek army. The narration specifically notes that both were natural leaders and that the surviving forces had little difficulty accepting their authority.[7]
Ares also reacted to [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] summoning five unusual Hecatonchires. Because Ares knew the traditional Hecatonchires born from [[Gaia]] and [[Ouranos]], he immediately recognized that these creatures were fundamentally different. They retained traces associated with Gaia but felt more chaotic and resembled Tartarus himself, leading Ares to realize that Tartarus and Gaia had apparently created an entirely separate group of such beings.[7]
His reaction demonstrated both his age and his familiarity with ancient Greek Divine history. Even while wounded and surrounded by enemies, Ares remained capable of identifying differences in the origin and Divine composition of primordial creatures.[7]
The fall of Olympus eventually ended the political structure Ares had served for eons. Victor survived the war, absorbed enormous amounts of Divine Power and destroyed the Greek Pantheon as an independent faction.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Ares survived the destruction of [[Olympus]], but unlike Goddesses such as [[Hestia]], [[Aphrodite]], [[Rhea]] and Nike who had voluntarily joined [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] beforehand, Ares belonged to the defeated Olympian faction.
The surviving Gods were consequently brought beneath Victor's authority and incorporated into the supernatural structure that eventually became the [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]]. Ares' allegiance to Victor was therefore originally one of subjugation rather than voluntary loyalty.
Despite their former hostility, Ares eventually bestowed a War Blessing upon Victor. By Chapter 857, Victor explicitly identifies himself as someone blessed by the God of War, Ares. The Blessing gives Victor an enhanced awareness of approaching warfare; Victor describes himself as capable of effectively "smelling" an incoming war from enormous distances.[11]
This Blessing became strategically relevant when Victor realized that a much larger conflict was approaching and began accelerating his preparations rather than wasting time building armies through conventional long-term methods.[11]
Ares' conceptual position changed even more dramatically during Victor's later Divine ascension.
When Victor awakened War, the narration explicitly compared his new Authority against the existing specialized War Gods. [[Athena]] represented Strategic War, while Ares represented Violent War. Victor's Concept was neither branch; it encompassed War in its entirety.[1]
Because of this, Victor immediately became the highest-ranking God of War in the surrounding Divine hierarchy. Other War Gods attempting to resist his ascension sensed the Being they would need to oppose and ultimately allowed the process to continue.[1]
Ares therefore remained an exceptionally developed War God but became conceptually subordinate to Victor's broader War Authority.

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Ares continued existing within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction well after the Greek arc rather than disappearing from the narrative.
During preparations for an operation in Chapter 950, [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] specifically suggested summoning Nike, [[Tyche]], [[Gaia]], [[Metis]], Ares, [[Persephone]] and Nyx.[4]
Violet questioned whether Nike, Tyche and Ares could still contribute because none had become Dragon Gods and their raw power had fallen far behind Victor's strongest family members. Velnorah responded that their Concepts remained highly developed even if their overall strength was now comparatively low.[4]
Velnorah specifically described Ares as a High-Level Violent War God, confirming both his old combat classification and the nature of his specialized War Authority long after the destruction of [[Olympus]].[4]
The discussion demonstrates Ares' later position clearly. He was no longer one of the absolute strongest figures surrounding Victor, but his accumulated mastery of War remained strategically valuable enough that [[Scathach]] still considered him for specialized missions.
By the time Victor fully mastered the War Concept, Ares' Violent War had become one of several specialized War authorities encompassed beneath Victor's complete mastery. Victor's mature War Divinity included Strategic War, Violent War and the other known forms of warfare rather than replacing their existence individually.[12]
Ares remains alive within the civilization that eventually evolved into the [[Elderblood Empire]], although his role has shifted from independent Olympian War God to a subordinate specialist existing under the much broader War Authority of Victor.

### Relationships

#### Zeus
[[Zeus]] was Ares' father, King and commander throughout most of his life.
Despite the severe dysfunction of the Olympian family, Ares remained on Zeus' side during the final Greek war. When Morgana pierced Ares' heart and he began losing consciousness, Zeus personally intervened and saved him by striking Morgana and creating a protective lightning barrier around them.[5]
Ares immediately used the opportunity to warn Zeus that [[Olympus]] would lose unless his father deployed the Pantheon's greater territorial power.[5]
Following Zeus' death at [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s hands, Ares did not collapse emotionally or abandon command. Instead, he and [[Atlas]] immediately assumed responsibility for the surviving Olympian army.[7]

#### Hera
[[Hera]] is Ares' mother.
The novel has shown little direct interaction between the two, but their relationship places Ares among the principal children of [[Zeus]] and Hera and at the core of the former Olympian royal family.
Hera defected from Zeus before the destruction of [[Olympus]] and joined [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction, placing mother and son on opposing political sides during the final stages of the Greek conflict.

#### Athena
[[Athena]] is simultaneously Ares' sister, rival and conceptual counterpart.
Both are Gods of War, but their Divinities represent fundamentally different branches. Athena embodies Strategic War, while Ares embodies Violent War.[1]
The two historically dislike one another and frequently approach warfare from opposing perspectives. However, their shared understanding of battle also means that they can independently reach the same conclusions. During the [[Typhon]] crisis, both simultaneously declared that defeating the Apocalypse Beast was impossible under [[Olympus]]' existing conditions.[6]
When [[Aphrodite]]'s faction escaped Olympus with Nike and [[Tyche]], Athena glanced toward Ares and immediately recognized that he had reached the same conclusion as her regarding how badly Olympus' chances had deteriorated.[6]
During the final war, both served among the surviving principal Olympians and ultimately fought on the same side against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].

#### Aphrodite Elderblood
[[Aphrodite]] was one of Ares' former lovers.
Aphrodite later explained that only a very small number of men across her enormous lifespan had ever genuinely stirred significant emotions within her, with Ares being one of them. Their relationship nevertheless ended long before Aphrodite committed herself to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
The two produced at least one notable Divine child together, [[Eros]].[13]
By the time Aphrodite joined Victor, she no longer considered Ares part of her family. She explicitly prioritized Victor and her new household over the adult children and former relationships she had left behind.[13]
Ares and Aphrodite therefore fought on opposite sides during the destruction of [[Olympus]].

#### Eros
[[Eros]] is Ares' son with [[Aphrodite]].
Eros inherited the heavily sexualized behavior common among many members of the old [[Greek Pantheon]] and survived into the much later [[Elderblood Empire]] era.
The relationship between Ares and Eros has not been developed extensively on-page, and Ares should not automatically be credited with influencing all of Eros' later choices simply because he is his father.

#### Morgana Elderblood
[[Morgana Elderblood]] was Ares' principal direct opponent during the destruction of [[Olympus]].
Their battle exposed a significant difference in combat philosophy. Ares expected a warrior to confront another warrior through direct combat and complained repeatedly about dishonorable methods. Morgana considered such expectations meaningless and used every possible advantage to kill him.[5]
Although Ares was far more experienced in conventional warfare and possessed superior formal martial training, Morgana exploited [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood Age, her Demonic physiology, [[Malphas]]' claws and deceptive movement to place him at a severe disadvantage.[5]
She eventually pierced his heart and nearly killed him before [[Zeus]] intervened.[5]

#### Victor Elderblood
Ares' relationship with [[Victor Elderblood]] changed drastically across the story.
Long before meeting him, Ares served as one of the standards [[Scathach]] used when estimating the level of martial mastery Victor might someday reach.[3]
The author later used Ares as a direct power benchmark, stating that [[Vampire Count Form]] Victor had reached a level capable of confronting High-Level [[Combat Gods]] such as Ares and [[Thor]].[2]
Their first genuine conflict occurred during Victor's destruction of [[Olympus]]. Ares fought directly against Victor's forces and was nearly killed by Morgana while trying to preserve the old Greek order.[9][5]
Following Olympus' destruction, Victor became Ares' superior and absorbed the [[Greek Pantheon]] into his own growing faction.
Ares eventually granted Victor his War Blessing, giving Victor a supernatural awareness of approaching warfare.[11]
Their conceptual relationship changed once again when Victor awakened the complete Divinity of War. Ares represented only Violent War, while Victor's Concept encompassed the entirety of War and placed him above the specialized War Gods within the Sector.[1]
By the later story, Ares had become a subordinate War specialist within Victor's civilization rather than an enemy or independent Olympian.

### Power and Status
Ares is canonically one of the stronger Third Generation combat Gods of Earth.
The author's official ranking places him among the same broad class as:
- [[Thor]]
- Full-power [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]]
- other elite combat-oriented supernatural beings of the corresponding era.[2]
The author additionally states:
[[Vampire Count Form]] [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] → capable of fighting a High-Level God such as Ares or Thor.[2]
This places Ares above ordinary Mid-Level Gods and many Major Gods whose Concepts are not optimized for combat.
His position should nevertheless not be exaggerated into God-King status. Ares is a High-Level Combat God, not a God-King or Primordial God.
The late story provides an important second benchmark. In Chapter 950, after Victor's faction had produced multiple Dragon Gods, Violet referred to Ares, Nike and [[Tyche]] as comparatively weak by their new standards. Velnorah did not dispute that raw-power assessment; instead, she emphasized that Ares remained useful because his Divine Concept was highly developed and described him as a High-Level Violent War God.[4]
His progression can therefore be represented as:
Third Generation Olympian
→ Major God
→ High-Level Combat God
→ Highly developed Violent War Divinity
→ Surpassed overwhelmingly by Victor and the Dragon Gods
→ Remains strategically useful because of Concept mastery

- Despite being an enemy during Olympus' destruction, Ares eventually blessed Victor with a War-related Blessing.[11]
- The Blessing allows Victor to sense an approaching war from very far away.[11]
- By Chapter 950, Ares was still alive and sufficiently relevant that Scathach considered recruiting him for an important operation.[4]
- Velnorah continued describing him as a highly developed High-Level Violent War God even though his raw strength had been surpassed by the Dragon Gods.[4]
