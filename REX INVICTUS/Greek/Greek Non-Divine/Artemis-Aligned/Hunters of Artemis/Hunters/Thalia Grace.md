**[[Thalia Grace]]** is a [[Demigod]] daughter of [[Zeus]] and the actress [[Beryl Grace]], older sister of [[Jason Grace]], and lieutenant of the [[Hunters of Artemis]]. She spent years as [[Thalia's Pine Tree]] on Half-Blood Hill before the [[Golden Fleece]] revived her.

## PJO Canon

**Early life.** Thalia ran away from her alcoholic mother at about ten, after [[Hera]] took [[Jason Grace|Jason]] from the family. She traveled with [[Luke Castellan]] and later the seven-year-old [[Annabeth Chase]], guided to camp by [[Grover Underwood]]. Monsters sent by [[Hades]] caught them on Half-Blood Hill. Thalia held them off and died, and [[Zeus]] turned her into a pine tree whose magic protected the camp's borders.

**Revival.** In [[The Sea of Monsters]] the tree is poisoned by [[Luke Castellan|Luke]]. The [[Golden Fleece]] heals it so well that Thalia is restored to life.

**[[The Titan's Curse]].** She joins the quest to rescue [[Artemis]]. She fears heights, clashes with [[Percy Jackson|Percy]], and resists Luke's offer at Othrys. Facing the [[Great Prophecy]], she becomes Artemis' new lieutenant, which keeps her from turning sixteen.

**Later.** She leads the [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] in [[The Last Olympian]]. In [[The Heroes of Olympus]] she reunites with Jason and helps against [[Porphyrion]] and the giants. In [[The Trials of Apollo]] she fights at Camp Jupiter and mourns Jason.

**Powers.** Lightning, wind, a spear and the shield [[Ageis|Aegis]] (a replica bearing [[Medusa]]'s face). Punk style: spiked hair, chains, black eyeliner.

#### Personality
- **Irreverent and teasing.** She treats Eleni's religious hymns about [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] as entertainment: she likes the titles, wants to see the "clay chins" and calls the cult "the faithful and the completely deranged" ([[Ch15 - Rex Invictus VIII|Ch15]]).

- **Blunt and profane.** "Holy shit." "What the fuck?" She calls [[Artemis]] "a proper bitch" ([[Ch15 - Rex Invictus VIII|Ch15]]). That hostility fits a girl who has not yet joined [[Hunters of Artemis|the Hunt]].
- **Shaken underneath.** She freezes on seeing Eleni and admits that she dreamed of her while she was the tree (Ch15).
- **Social.** After Eleni's council she "spent the majority of her time connecting with the demigods around camp" (Ch15).
- **A critic of Percy's antics.** At breakfast she leans on him and mocks his new courtly voice ([[Ch20 - Rex Invictus XIII|Ch20]]).
- **Physical.** Percy notes that Eleni's sparks "hurt more than Thalia's slap" (Ch15), so Thalia has slapped him at least once off-page.

#### Continuity Notes
- [[Ch13 - Rex Invictus VI|Ch13]] describes her armour as wired and capacitor-laden and her weapon as a spear. [[Ch23 - Interlude I|Ch23]] shows Hunter gear and a bow.
- **Timeline tension:** in [[Ch23 - Interlude I|Ch23]] Thalia remembers original-timeline events (Lotus, Manhattan), while the Director remembers the Rex timeline's Long Island battle ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]). Whether the future is shared or a loop is unresolved.

This is wording drift and reconcilable: she held the bridges on the perimeter, was not in the central fight the monument commemorates, and came down afterwards to find Percy and [[Annabeth Chase|Annabeth]] on the steps. It is an original-timeline event.

## Original Vault Notes

Part of the [[Greek Pantheon]]
Thalia Grace is a Greek [[Demigod]], the daughter of [[Zeus]] and the mortal [[Beryl Grace]], and the older sister of Roman demigod [[Jason Grace]]. After she was mortally wounded by attacking monsters, she was transformed into a pine tree on Half-Blood Hill, which still stands as the official border for Camp Half-Blood to this day. She was later resurrected by the [[Golden Fleece]] under the manipulations of [[Kronos]] in an attempt to control the [[Great Prophecy]]. She is currently the lieutenant of the [[Hunters of Artemis]], leading them in the absence of the goddess after accepting Artemis' offer following a failed attempt by Kronos to use Thalia to destroy the gods.
