Part of the [[Greek Pantheon]]
Thalia Grace is a Greek [[Demigod]], the daughter of [[Zeus]] and the mortal [[Beryl Grace]], and the older sister of Roman demigod [[Jason Grace]]. After she was mortally wounded by attacking monsters, she was transformed into a pine tree on Half-Blood Hill, which still stands as the official border for Camp Half-Blood to this day. She was later resurrected by the [[Golden Fleece]] under the manipulations of [[Kronos]] in an attempt to control the [[Great Prophecy]]. She is currently the lieutenant of the [[Hunters of Artemis]], leading them in the absence of the goddess after accepting Artemis' offer following a failed attempt by Kronos to use Thalia to destroy the gods.
