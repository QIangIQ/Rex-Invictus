## UOE (Far Future)

The Pack Masters are subservient to the Hunt. A kennel-crypt keeps no fewer than 40 active Hounds under an embedded Hunter war-keeper and a Commander, and the Pack Masters run the packs inside it. They fight in hunting plate with silver leash-chains wound around their forearms. Their hounds come from the Luna lines and are reinforced from [[Lupa]] and the [[Wolf House]].

## Rex Invictus (Present Day)

**XXI, Broadway 0606 (my choice, not a ruling):** when [[Diana]] bays for the Hunt, the Phobos kennel-crypt answers down the Martian drop corridor with eighty Hounds. Bartold leads, Arrantz takes the left along the shopfronts, Jordun the right, and Grune vanishes into the fight. At 0730 Bartold reports 29 Hounds lost, Uglock dead and Herrold's leg off at the knee.
