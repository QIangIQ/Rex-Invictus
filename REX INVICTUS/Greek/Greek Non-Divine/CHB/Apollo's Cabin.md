**[[Apollo's Cabin]]** is Cabin 7 at [[Camp Half-Blood]], a golden cabin that glows like the sun, home to the children of [[Apollo]]: archers and healers. Counselors: Lee Fletcher, [[Michael Yew]], then [[Will Solace]]. Other members: Kayla Knowles, Austin Lake.
