**[[Hephaestus' Cabin]]** is Cabin 9 at [[Camp Half-Blood]], a metal workshop with smokestacks and gears, home to the children of [[Hephaestus]]. Counselors: [[Charles Beckendorf]], then Jake Mason, then [[Leo]] Valdez. It holds Bunker 9, where Leo built the Argo II.
