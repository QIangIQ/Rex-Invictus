**[[Demeter's Cabin]]** is Cabin 4 at [[Camp Half-Blood]], home of the children of [[Demeter]]. It has a grass roof and walls covered in tomato vines. Counselor: Katie Gardner. Other children: Miranda Gardiner, Billie Ng and, from [[The Trials of Apollo]], Meg McCaffrey. In [[Rex Invictus]] a dirt-covered daughter of [[Demeter]] at the strawberry fields raises the alarm in [[Ch21 - Rex Invictus XIV|Ch21]].
