[[Camp Half-Blood]] is a [[Greek]] [[Demigod]] training facility located on the north shore of [[Long Island]]. The camp is directed by the [[god (greek)]] [[Dionysus]], whom the campers call "Mr. D," and [[Chiron]], a [[Centaur]] who serves as the activities director. It is also the Greek counterpart of [[Camp Jupiter]], a Roman camp between [[Oakland, California]] and [[Berkeley, California]].

The camp has magical borders enforced by the [[Golden Fleece]], retrieved by the [[Satyr]] [[Grover Underwood]], the [[Cyclops]] [[Tyson]], and the [[Demigod]]s [[Percy Jackson]], [[Clarisse La Rue]], and [[Annabeth Chase]]. Stolen from [[Polyphemus]]' island, it was placed on [[Thalia's Pine Tree]], ensuring that no [[Monsters (Greek)]] could enter the camp. The Fleece remains on the tree, guarded by the dragon [[Peleus]], whose magical presence helps protect the camp from [[Monsters (Greek)]] and invaders.

The camp is described as the only safe place for half-bloods, although in The Heroes of Olympus it is revealed that there is a similar camp for Roman [[Demigod]]s — [[Camp Jupiter]], located near [[San Francisco]].

The half-bloods live in cabins, originally one for each of the [[Twelve Olympians]], though later additional cabins were built for minor [[gods (greek)]] and [[goddesses (greek)]], such as [[Hecate]], [[Hypnos]], [[Iris]], [[Nemesis]], [[Hebe]], [[Tyche]], and [[Nike]]. [[Hades]] also later received a cabin, despite not being a minor [[god (greek)]], after [[Percy Jackson]] requested it in [[The Last Olympian]]. The campers eat at the [[Dining Pavilion]]. The camp also contains an [[Archery Field]], a [[Sword Fighting Arena]], a [[Climbing Wall]] with lava for training, an [[Amphitheater]], [[Pegasus Stables]], an [[Armory]], and the [[Big House]].

The camp's cover name is [[Delphi Strawberry Service]], a reference to the [[Oracle of Delphi]].

## Address

[[Camp Half-Blood]], [[Half-Blood Hill]], [[Farm Road 3.141]]  
[[Long Island]], [[New York]] 11954

The camp is located near [[Montauk]], at the end of [[Long Island]]. [[Percy Jackson]], his mother [[Sally Jackson]], and [[Grover Underwood]] drive from their cabin on the beach in [[Montauk]]. It does not take them very long to reach the camp. [[Sally Jackson]] and [[Poseidon]] also frequently vacationed there, and it is where they first met. However, the camp is repeatedly described in the books as being on the North Shore of [[Long Island]], while [[Montauk]] is on the South Shore, making the camp's precise location uncertain. Percy states that the stretch of beach connecting to the camp is invisible to mortals, except for those able to see through [[The Mist]]. [[Monsters (Greek)]] and mortals cannot enter unless permitted from the inside. The address, 3.141, is generally understood to reference [[pi]], named after the Greek letter.

The main entrance to the camp is through [[Half-Blood Hill]], as stated in [[The Lightning Thief]].

## History

The first six students were brought to [[Chiron]] for training by [[Apollo]] back in [[Ancient Greece]]. Their names were [[Aeneas]], who fought for [[Troy]] in the [[Trojan War]]; the first [[Jason]], who captained the [[Argo]]; [[Achilles]], who was dipped in the [[Styx]]; [[Atalanta]], a renowned huntress; [[Asclepius]], [[Apollo]]'s son, who later became the immortal [[god (greek)]] of medicine; and the first [[Perseus]], who killed [[Medusa]].

According to various camp members, the camp later trained many famous historical figures, including some [[United States Presidents]], such as [[George Washington]], described here as a son of [[Athena]]. [[Camp Half-Blood]] originally stood in [[Ancient Greece]] at the foot of [[Mount Pelion]], which was [[Chiron]]'s home. As it grew, it moved to the base of [[Mount Olympus]], and at one point [[Apollo]] arrived with a horde of [[Satyr]]s to help [[Demigod]]s find their way to the training grounds. Through the ages, [[Camp Half-Blood]] has moved with the [[gods (greek)]] and with the center of [[Western Civilization]].

It is a sanctuary for [[Demigod]]s, but before the magical borders were established, conditions were far more dangerous. One night in [[Ancient Greece]], a massive [[Monsters (Greek)]] army stormed the camp, and it was nearly wiped from history. The army was defeated only through the combined efforts of the campers present and former campers who returned to defend it. The following morning, the [[gods (greek)]] granted [[Camp Half-Blood]] magical borders, and those protections remained in place thereafter.

Five years before [[Percy Jackson]] arrived at the camp, [[Thalia Grace]], daughter of [[Zeus]], nearly died on the border while sacrificing herself so that her friends [[Annabeth Chase]], [[Luke Castellan]], and [[Grover Underwood]] could reach safety. In her final moments, [[Zeus]] took pity on her and transformed her into a pine tree, sustaining her life-force within it. The tree's position on the border allowed her spirit to strengthen the camp's defenses. She later returned to human form and joined the [[Hunters of Artemis]].

It is also stated in The Demigod Files that before the camp had magical borders, it was guarded by [[Festus]] about fifteen years before he became defective and disappeared into the woods. The bronze dragon was said to be an offering from the [[Hephaestus Cabin]] to their father [[Hephaestus]], and it protected the camp for approximately a decade before being lost.
