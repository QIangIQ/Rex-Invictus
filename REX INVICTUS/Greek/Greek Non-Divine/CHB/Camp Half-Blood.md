[[Camp Half-Blood]] is a [[Greek Pantheon|Greek]] [[Demigod]] training facility located on the north shore of [[Long Island]]. The camp is directed by the [[god (greek)]] [[Dionysus]], whom the campers call "Mr. D," and [[Chiron]], a [[Centaur]] who serves as the activities director. It is also the Greek counterpart of [[Camp Jupiter]], a Roman camp between [[Oakland, California]] and [[Berkeley, California]].

The camp has magical borders enforced by the [[Golden Fleece]], retrieved by the [[Satyr]] [[Grover Underwood]], the [[Cyclopes|Cyclops]] [[Tyson]], and the [[Demigod]]s [[Percy Jackson]], [[Clarisse|Clarisse La Rue]], and [[Annabeth Chase]]. Stolen from [[Polyphemus]]' island, it was placed on [[Thalia's Pine Tree]], ensuring that no [[Monsters (Greek)]] could enter the camp. The Fleece remains on the tree, guarded by the dragon [[Peleus]], whose magical presence helps protect the camp from [[Monsters (Greek)]] and invaders.

The camp is described as the only safe place for half-bloods, although in The Heroes of Olympus it is revealed that there is a similar camp for Roman [[Demigod]]s — [[Camp Jupiter]], located near [[San Francisco]].

The half-bloods live in cabins, originally one for each of the [[Olympians|Twelve Olympians]], though later additional cabins were built for minor [[God (greek)|gods (greek)]] and [[God (greek)|goddesses (greek)]], such as [[Hecate]], [[Hypnos]], [[Iris]], [[Nemesis]], [[Hebe]], [[Tyche]], and [[Nike]]. [[Hades]] also later received a cabin, despite not being a minor [[god (greek)]], after [[Percy Jackson]] requested it in [[The Last Olympian]]. The campers eat at the [[Camp Half-Blood#Dining Pavilion|Dining Pavilion]]. The camp also contains an [[Camp Half-Blood#Archery Field|Archery Field]], a [[Camp Half-Blood#Sword Fighting Arena|Sword Fighting Arena]], a [[Camp Half-Blood#Climbing Wall|Climbing Wall]] with lava for training, an [[Camp Half-Blood#Amphitheater|Amphitheater]], [[Camp Half-Blood#Pegasus Stables|Pegasus Stables]], an [[Camp Half-Blood#Armory|Armory]], and the [[Camp Half-Blood#Big House|Big House]].

The camp's cover name is [[Camp Half-Blood#Delphi Strawberry Service|Delphi Strawberry Service]], a reference to the [[Oracle of Delphi]].

## Address

[[Camp Half-Blood]], [[Camp Half-Blood#Half-Blood Hill|Half-Blood Hill]], [[Camp Half-Blood#Farm Road 3.141|Farm Road 3.141]]  
[[Long Island]], [[New York]] 11954

The camp is located near [[Montauk]], at the end of [[Long Island]]. [[Percy Jackson]], his mother [[Sally Jackson]], and [[Grover Underwood]] drive from their cabin on the beach in [[Montauk]]. It does not take them very long to reach the camp. [[Sally Jackson]] and [[Poseidon]] also frequently vacationed there, and it is where they first met. However, the camp is repeatedly described in the books as being on the North Shore of [[Long Island]], while [[Montauk]] is on the South Shore, making the camp's precise location uncertain. Percy states that the stretch of beach connecting to the camp is invisible to mortals, except for those able to see through [[The Mist]]. [[Monsters (Greek)]] and mortals cannot enter unless permitted from the inside. The address, 3.141, is generally understood to reference pi, named after the Greek letter.

The main entrance to the camp is through [[Camp Half-Blood#Half-Blood Hill|Half-Blood Hill]], as stated in [[The Lightning Thief]].

## History

The first six students were brought to [[Chiron]] for training by [[Apollo]] back in [[Ancient Greece]]. Their names were [[Aeneas]], who fought for [[Troy]] in the [[Trojan War]]; the first [[Jason (Argonaut)|Jason]], who captained the [[Argo]]; [[Achilles]], who was dipped in the [[Styx]]; [[Atlanta#Greek History (IRL)|Atalanta]], a renowned huntress; [[Asclepius]], [[Apollo]]'s son, who later became the immortal [[god (greek)]] of medicine; and the first [[Perseus (Hero)|Perseus]], who killed [[Medusa]].

According to various camp members, the camp later trained many famous historical figures, including some [[George Washington|United States Presidents]], such as [[George Washington]], described here as a son of [[Athena]]. [[Camp Half-Blood]] originally stood in [[Ancient Greece]] at the foot of [[Mount Pelion]], which was [[Chiron]]'s home. As it grew, it moved to the base of [[Olympus|Mount Olympus]], and at one point [[Apollo]] arrived with a horde of [[Satyr]]s to help [[Demigod]]s find their way to the training grounds. Through the ages, [[Camp Half-Blood]] has moved with the [[God (greek)|gods (greek)]] and with the center of Western Civilization.

It is a sanctuary for [[Demigod]]s, but before the magical borders were established, conditions were far more dangerous. One night in [[Ancient Greece]], a massive [[Monsters (Greek)]] army stormed the camp, and it was nearly wiped from history. The army was defeated only through the combined efforts of the campers present and former campers who returned to defend it. The following morning, the [[God (greek)|gods (greek)]] granted [[Camp Half-Blood]] magical borders, and those protections remained in place thereafter.

Five years before [[Percy Jackson]] arrived at the camp, [[Thalia Grace]], daughter of [[Zeus]], nearly died on the border while sacrificing herself so that her friends [[Annabeth Chase]], [[Luke Castellan]], and [[Grover Underwood]] could reach safety. In her final moments, [[Zeus]] took pity on her and transformed her into a pine tree, sustaining her life-force within it. The tree's position on the border allowed her spirit to strengthen the camp's defenses. She later returned to human form and joined the [[Hunters of Artemis]].

It is also stated in The Demigod Files that before the camp had magical borders, it was guarded by [[Festus]] about fifteen years before he became defective and disappeared into the woods. The bronze dragon was said to be an offering from the [[Hephaestus' Cabin|Hephaestus Cabin]] to their father [[Hephaestus]], and it protected the camp for approximately a decade before being lost.

## Grounds

*PJO Canon, from the books and the Riordan wiki.*

### Half-Blood Hill
The hill on the camp's western edge, crowned by [[Thalia's Pine Tree]], where the magical border begins. The [[Golden Fleece]] hangs on the pine, guarded by the dragon [[Peleus]], and the [[Athena Parthenos]] was later set up here.

### Farm Road 3.141
The camp's mailing address on [[Long Island]], a joke on pi (3.141...).

### Delphi Strawberry Service
The camp's mortal cover: it sells strawberries to New York restaurants and to Olympus, named for the [[Oracle of Delphi]].

### Big House
The four-storey blue farmhouse where [[Chiron]] and [[Dionysus]] live and run the camp. The [[Oracle of Delphi|Oracle]]'s mummy was kept in its attic until [[Rachel]] took the role.

### Dining Pavilion
An open-air Greek pavilion of white columns overlooking the sea. Campers sit by cabin and burn part of each meal in the brazier as an offering to the gods.

### Amphitheater
The stone amphitheater where the [[Apollo's Cabin|Apollo cabin]] leads the nightly sing-along around the campfire, whose flames change color with the campers' mood.

### Armory
The storehouse of weapons and armor, mostly [[Celestial Bronze]], for training, capture the flag and quests.

### Archery Field
The range where the Apollo cabin trains. Its archers are matched only by the [[Hunters of Artemis]].

### Climbing Wall
A wall that shakes, sprays lava and slams its sides together when the climber reaches the top.

### Sword Fighting Arena
The arena where [[Luke Castellan]] taught Percy and the other new campers swordplay; [[Mrs. O'Leary]] later sleeps there.

### Pegasus Stables
Stables for the camp's [[Pegasus|pegasi]], used for flying lessons and quests.
