**[[Michael Yew]]** is a [[Demigod]] son of [[Apollo]] and an archer of [[Apollo's Cabin]]. His Rex Invictus profile is merged into [[Will Solace]].

---

## Hierarchical Placement

- [[Apollo]]
    - [[Apollo's Cabin]]
        - [[Michael Yew]]

---

## PJO Canon

Short and fierce, Michael leads the [[Apollo]] cabin after Lee Fletcher's death in [[The Battle of the Labyrinth]]. In [[The Last Olympian]] he holds the Williamsburg Bridge and falls when the bridge is destroyed; he is listed as missing and presumed dead.

---

## Rex Invictus (Present Day)

In Rex Invictus Michael is **dead**, killed on the Big House porch by the [[Ekdromoi]] drop of Cells Lambda-Two and Sigma-Four in [[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]] ([[00a_CANON_RULINGS]] C17). [[Dionysus]] unleashes his madness over his body, and [[Apollo]] grieves him.
