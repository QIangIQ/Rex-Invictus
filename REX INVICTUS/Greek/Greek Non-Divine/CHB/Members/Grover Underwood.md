**[[Grover Underwood]]** is a [[Satyr]] and [[Percy Jackson]]'s best friend, a protector of young demigods and later Lord of the Wild on the Council of Cloven Elders. He searches for the lost god [[Pan]].

## Greek History (IRL)

**Satyrs in myth.** Satyrs were nature spirits with horse or goat traits, companions of [[Dionysus]] and [[Pan]], fond of wine, music and nymphs. Silenus was their elder. See [[Satyr]].

---

## PJO Canon

Grover failed to save [[Thalia Grace|Thalia]] years earlier, so he carries guilt. He protects [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] at Yancy Academy, joins the quest of [[The Lightning Thief]] and receives his searcher's license. In [[The Sea of Monsters]] he is captured by Polyphemus and saved by Percy, [[Annabeth Chase|Annabeth]] and [[Clarisse]]. In [[The Titan's Curse]] he finds the di Angelos. In [[The Battle of the Labyrinth]] he finds Pan, who dies and passes his spirit to all wild things. In [[The Last Olympian]] he leads nature spirits in Manhattan and is elected Lord of the Wild. His girlfriend is the dryad Juniper. Powers: reed pipes, empathy link with Percy, nature magic.

## Rex Invictus (Present Day)

In Rex Invictus Grover holds the line with [[Bianca di Angelo]] at the Battle of [[Mt. Othrys]] ([[Ch19 - Rex Invictus XII|Ch19]]) and is teleported clear by [[Artemis]] and [[Percy Jackson|Percy]]. He is not mentioned in [[Ch20 - Rex Invictus XIII|Ch20]] to [[Ch23 - Interlude I|Ch23]].

#### Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- This is the only Grover in the story. He is the present-day satyr of the timeline [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] reincarnated into.
- Percy remembers an *old-timeline* Grover only once. In his original life he first came to camp "barely conscious, slumped over Grover's shoulder" ([[Ch07 - Rex Natus IV|Ch7]]). That earlier Grover lived the canon events. This Grover is meeting a very different Percy.
- He knows nothing of the far future. He has seen [[Minerva (AI)|Minerva]]'s manifestation only secondhand, through camp gossip; he wasn't on the [[Ch11 - Rex Invictus IV|Ch11]] bus.

#### Appearance
- Hooves and goat ears that "flatten" when worried and "flick" toward every shadow ([[Ch12 - Rex Invictus V|Ch12]], [[Ch16 - Rex Invictus IX|Ch16]], [[Ch17 - Rex Invictus X|Ch17]]).
- Carries reed pipes ([[Ch16 - Rex Invictus IX|Ch16]], [[Ch19 - Rex Invictus XII|Ch19]]).
- At Polyphemus's island he is found tied between two rocks "like some horrible parody of a scarecrow," in a sagging "fake bridal veil" ([[Ch14 - Rex Invictus VII|Ch14]]).
- Sleeps "curled up with his jacket as a pillow" ([[Ch18 - Rex Invictus XI|Ch18]]).

#### Personality
- **Loyal, anxious and brave when it counts.** He bleats nervously ([[Ch12 - Rex Invictus V|Ch12]], [[Ch15 - Rex Invictus VIII|Ch15]]), fusses, and grabs pillars during Eleni's power demonstration ([[Ch15 - Rex Invictus VIII|Ch15]]). Yet he shields friends, joins every quest, and stands beside [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] facing the [[Manticore]].
- **Earnest and dramatic.** His battle cry at Thorn, "You won't win. Not while we stand," makes Percy suppress a cringe at the cliché ([[Ch15 - Rex Invictus VIII|Ch15]]).
- **Caring.** He presses Percy to eat ("You'll pass out before tomorrow", [[Ch18 - Rex Invictus XI|Ch18]]) and notices when Percy looks bad ("Like you fought a cement mixer", [[Ch18 - Rex Invictus XI|Ch18]]).
- **Perceptive about nature and the divine.**
  - He senses the camp's "wrongness" ("You feel it too?", [[Ch12 - Rex Invictus V|Ch12]]).
  - He whispers to [[Thalia Grace|Thalia]]'s dying pine in Ancient Greek, and it twitches "Like they were listening" ([[Ch12 - Rex Invictus V|Ch12]]).
  - He senses vibrations through floorboards ([[Ch17 - Rex Invictus X|Ch17]]) and smells Othrys's "bad stench" ([[Ch19 - Rex Invictus XII|Ch19]]).
- **Thoughtful under the nerves.** After the Fleece fight he works out that it cut off Polyphemus once it rejected [[Annabeth Chase|Annabeth]], and that Percy is "the intended recipient" ([[Ch14 - Rex Invictus VII|Ch14]]).
- **Easily embarrassed.** [[Zoe Nightshade|Zoë]] jabs that the bad stench "is you," and he looks away ([[Ch19 - Rex Invictus XII|Ch19]]).

#### Voice & Speech Patterns
- Casual, breathless, nervous, often trailing off. He uses "Like…" emphases and bleats. He swears mildly or not at all. He calls [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] by name and uses the camp's usual slang.

#### Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Empathy link with [[Percy Jackson|Percy]].** [[Minerva (AI)|Minerva]] warns that "the [[Satyr]] will realize our connection too quickly if he comes too close" ([[Ch07 - Rex Natus IV|Ch7]]). Percy deliberately keeps his distance from "the Satyr's emotional link" ([[Ch12 - Rex Invictus V|Ch12]]). In canon this link forms during *Sea of Monsters*; here it already exists, or is assumed, by [[Ch12 - Rex Invictus V|Ch12]].
- **Nature magic.** His reed pipes force roots through bare stone at Othrys to tangle monster legs, "bought seconds that meant lives" ([[Ch19 - Rex Invictus XII|Ch19]]).
- **A goat-call** that times the Fleece ambush ([[Ch14 - Rex Invictus VII|Ch14]]).
- **Iris messaging.** He IMs Percy from Westover Hall, under his desk during a geometry quiz ([[Ch15 - Rex Invictus VIII|Ch15]]).
- **Satyr instincts.** [[Annabeth Chase|Annabeth]]: "He'll wake up if anything dangerous comes close. Satyr instincts." ([[Ch15 - Rex Invictus VIII|Ch15]]). He has a keen nose and sense of vibration.
- He can speak Ancient Greek to trees ([[Ch12 - Rex Invictus V|Ch12]]).

#### Backstory
- As a canon baseline, he is the satyr who protected [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] at Yancy and brought him to camp in the old timeline. In this timeline Percy skipped that: he walked to camp alone with the Minotaur's horn ([[Ch07 - Rex Natus IV|Ch7]]).
- [[Chiron]] calls him in from the forest when Percy arrives ([[Ch07 - Rex Natus IV|Ch7]]).
- In [[Ch03 - Rex Natus I|Ch3]], the [[Labyrinth]] monster camp was planning "an attack on a few demigods and a satyr". This was possibly aimed at Grover's escort mission, but the text never confirms it.

- **[[Ch07 - Rex Natus IV|Ch7]].** [[Chiron]] orders "Call Grover, a satyr in the forest." [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] recalls arriving on Grover's shoulder in his old life. [[Minerva (AI)|Minerva]] worries Grover's empathy link will expose her.
- **[[Ch08 - Rex Invictus I|Ch8]]–11.** Not shown on the Vegas/Fort Reagan trip.

  - At the dying camp his ears flatten: "You feel it too?" He whispers to [[Thalia Grace|Thalia]]'s tree, and the branches twitch.
- **[[Ch13 - Rex Invictus VI|Ch13]].**
  - Grover is "currently detained in Polyphemus' territory." [[Athena]] sends a dream-vision of his pleading voice to Percy to manipulate him into leaving.
  - The barrier breaks when "an new unnamed demigod escorted by an exhausted satyr" arrives. Whether that satyr is Grover is unclear; probably not, since Grover was already captive.
- **[[Ch14 - Rex Invictus VII|Ch14]].** Found tied up in a bridal veil. [[Annabeth Chase|Annabeth]] cuts him free, and he warns about the Fleece cloak. His goat-call distraction helps topple Polyphemus. He explains the Fleece's choice of Percy.
- **[[Ch15 - Rex Invictus VIII|Ch15]].**
  - Asleep under the sail on the way home. Annabeth: "[[Satyr]] instincts."
  - Assigned to the Eleni watch squad, he pretends to eat celery and grabs a pillar during her "two percent" display.
  - Three days before Christmas he IMs Percy: freezing at Westover Hall in Maine, sure "the vice principal suspects."
  - At the tree line: "About time… You look like you just ran a marathon." He leads the chase after Thorn and the di Angelos and faces the [[Manticore]] ("goat-boy").
- **[[Ch16 - Rex Invictus IX|Ch16]].**
  - Gasps "Percy… your sword…" as [[Riptide]] flies to [[Artemis]] and speaks.
  - Rides [[Apollo]]'s bus to camp ("Grover fussed").
  - Joins the quest west with Percy, [[Zoe Nightshade|Zoë]], [[Bianca di Angelo|Bianca]] and Eleni, "fingers twitching around his reed pipe."
- **[[Ch17 - Rex Invictus X|Ch17]].**
  - At Ammit-Sekhem's shrine: "That's… not Greek. Like, at all." He bears witness to Sariel, Manannán, [[Asmodeus]]'s fortress and the Hellway.
  - Senses vibrations in Flamel's shop.
  - Introduced in [[New Rome]] as "a companion familiar with the wilds." He crosses the Little [[Tiber]] (the text's "Mini Tiber", [[Ch17 - Rex Invictus X|Ch17]]) with Zoë and Bianca.
- **[[Ch18 - Rex Invictus XI|Ch18]].** Watches Percy's three duels from the praetorian box, pale. At dinner: "Eat." In the morning he notices Percy bleeding for the first time; Percy had given fragments of his [[Achilles]] curse to Artemis and is left at about half strength (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C13).
- **[[Ch19 - Rex Invictus XII|Ch19]].** At Othrys he and Zoë trade barbs, and his pipes tangle monsters. He holds the line below with Bianca and is pulled clear in the mass teleport.
- **[[Ch20 - Rex Invictus XIII|Ch20]]–23.** Not present.
