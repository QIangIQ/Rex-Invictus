**[[Silena Beauregard]]** is a [[Demigod]] daughter of [[Aphrodite]] and head counselor of [[Aphrodite]]'s Cabin (Cabin 10). She is the girlfriend of [[Charles Beckendorf]]. Her Rex Invictus profile is merged into his note: see [[Charles Beckendorf#Rex Invictus (Present Day)]].

- [[Aphrodite]]

---

## PJO Canon

Silena is kind and well liked. After a gift from [[Luke Castellan]], she became [[Kronos]]' spy at camp, passing information through a charm bracelet. In [[The Last Olympian]] she is revealed as the spy after her death. Ashamed, she disguised herself in [[Clarisse]]'s armor and led the [[Ares]] cabin into the Battle of Manhattan, where a drakon killed her. [[Clarisse]] avenged her. [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] keeps her secret, and the camp honors her as a hero.

---

## Rex Invictus (Present Day)

In Rex Invictus her fate is unstated (see [[00a_CANON_RULINGS]] C20). She is last mentioned in [[Chiron]]'s [[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]] order to take the [[Aphrodite]] cabin and clear the younger campers into the Big House basement.
