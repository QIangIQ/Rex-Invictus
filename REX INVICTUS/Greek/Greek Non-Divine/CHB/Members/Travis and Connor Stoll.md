**[[Travis and Connor Stoll]]** are [[Demigod]] sons of [[Hermes]], co-counselors of [[Hermes' Cabin]] after [[Luke Castellan|Luke]], and the camp's chief pranksters and thieves.

## PJO Canon

The Stolls look almost like twins; Travis is a year older. In [[The Titan's Curse]] they prank the Hunter [[Phoebe]] with a centaur-blood T-shirt. In [[The Last Olympian]] they fight in Manhattan and loot stores for supplies. Travis dates Katie Gardner of [[Demeter]]'s cabin. In [[The Heroes of Olympus]] they appear at camp, and in [[The Trials of Apollo]] Connor stays at camp while Travis goes to college.

#### Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- They are the only Stolls in the story: present-day, with no future knowledge.
- **Twins or not?** [[Ch07 - Rex Natus IV|Ch7]] calls them "near-identical demigods… the Stoll brothers," and [[Ch10 - Rex Invictus III|Ch10]] calls them "the [[Hermes]] twins." In descriptive prose they are brothers; quote "twins" only as the text's label (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C22). In canon they are not twins; Travis is the elder.
- The narration sometimes swaps their names; see Continuity Notes. When roleplaying, Travis is the bolder risk-taker at cards and Connor is the self-proclaimed card-counting teacher.

#### Appearance
- "Near-identical" ([[Ch07 - Rex Natus IV|Ch7]]). There is no other description, apart from Connor's trainers flexing on a plastic roof ([[Ch10 - Rex Invictus III|Ch10]]).

#### Personality
- **Cheeky and irreverent.** Travis tells [[Chiron]], "Well, you are getting on a bit" ([[Ch08 - Rex Invictus I|Ch8]]). Connor charges onto the off-limits royal bed on [[Zeus]]'s plane ([[Ch08 - Rex Invictus I|Ch8]]). They tongue-poke, sneer and laugh at authority.
- **Gamblers and schemers.** Travis hits on seventeen and lands blackjack ("Fortuna smiles upon me"). Connor brags, "Because I taught you how to count cards" ([[Ch08 - Rex Invictus I|Ch8]]). Camp rule three is "never bet against the Stoll brothers" (Ch8).
- **Rowdy.** In Vegas they hold an M&M battle, race "around like lunatics," flick each other with towels and blast the TV, driving [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] out of the suite (Ch8). They get "indoctrinated" by the hotel's promo channel into visiting the arcade and aquarium (Ch8).
- **Gossipy teasers.** They ambush Percy and [[Annabeth Chase|Annabeth]] with honeymoon and wedding-band jokes, then scatter "in a flurry of laughter and mock-terror" when Annabeth draws her knife ([[Ch15 - Rex Invictus VIII|Ch15]]).
- **Brave in a pinch.** Connor, laughing, rides piggyback into gunfire and drops the grenade alone after losing his brother in the smoke ([[Ch10 - Rex Invictus III|Ch10]]).
- **Appreciate good pranks.** They whistle when Percy's dodged juice soaks [[Luke Castellan|Luke]] ("Nice one", Travis, Ch8).

#### Voice & Speech Patterns
- Quick, mocking, colloquial, sometimes British-tinged in the Vegas chapters ("Get stuffed"). They lean on mock-gasps and theatrical sympathy.

#### Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- Card counting and gambling luck ([[Ch08 - Rex Invictus I|Ch8]]).
- Scouting with binoculars ([[Ch10 - Rex Invictus III|Ch10]]).
- Light-fingered [[Hermes]] stealth. Connor slips an M67 "baseball" paint grenade through a ventilation slat, timing four seconds, and paints General Mikhail neon orange ([[Ch10 - Rex Invictus III|Ch10]]). His "blood was pulsing quite strongly" to [[Percy Jackson|Percy]]'s water-sense ([[Ch10 - Rex Invictus III|Ch10]]).
- Rifles and heavy equipment packs in the war game (Ch10).

#### Backstory
- As a canon baseline: [[Hermes]] Cabin pranksters under counselor [[Luke Castellan|Luke]]. Their cabin is known for theft ("don't steal from the Hermes cabin unless you enjoy waking up glued to the ceiling", rule two, [[Ch08 - Rex Invictus I|Ch8]]).

- **[[Ch11 - Rex Invictus IV|Ch11]].** On the bus, Connor mocks with mock-gasps. "A certain son of Hermes" (probably a Stoll) begs [[Annabeth Chase|Annabeth]]: "Oh, don't ruin it, Chase. Let the mystery linger."
- **[[Ch15 - Rex Invictus VIII|Ch15]].** Lounging by the strawberry fields with two other Hermes kids, they ambush the returning Percy and Annabeth with honeymoon jokes, then flee from Annabeth's knife.
- **[[Ch16 - Rex Invictus IX|Ch16]]–23.** Not mentioned.
