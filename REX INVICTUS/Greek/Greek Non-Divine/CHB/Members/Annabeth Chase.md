**[[Annabeth Chase]]** is a [[Demigod]] daughter of [[Athena]] and the military historian [[Frederick Chase]], head counselor of [[Athena's Cabin]] and an architect. She is [[Percy Jackson]]'s closest friend and, in canon, girlfriend. In the old timeline she is "The Architect" and Vice-President of the [[Council of Olympic Sentinels]]. Her far-future legacy is [[Verrill Chase]].

## PJO Canon

**Early life.** Born in Virginia to [[Athena]] and [[Frederick Chase]], who later married a mortal woman. Feeling unwanted, Annabeth ran away at seven and joined [[Thalia Grace]] and [[Luke Castellan]], who gave her a bronze knife. She reached camp after [[Thalia Grace|Thalia]]'s death and lived there year-round.

**[[The Lightning Thief]].** She nurses [[Percy Jackson|Percy]], joins his quest and wears the Yankees cap of invisibility from her mother. **[[The Sea of Monsters]].** She hears the Sirens' song and reveals her fatal flaw, hubris. **[[The Titan's Curse]].** [[Luke Castellan|Luke]] captures her and forces her to hold the sky, which streaks her hair grey. **[[The Battle of the Labyrinth]].** She leads the quest into the [[Labyrinth]] and meets [[Daedalus]], who gives her his laptop. **[[The Last Olympian]].** She is stabbed taking a knife for Percy, gives Luke's knife back to him, and is chosen to redesign [[Olympus]].

**[[The Heroes of Olympus]].** She searches for the missing Percy, follows the Mark of Athena to recover the [[Athena Parthenos]] from [[Arachne]] ([[The Mark of Athena]]), and falls into [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] with Percy ([[The House of Hades]]).

**Traits.** Blonde curly hair, grey eyes, a strategist with a love of architecture. Weapon: [[Celestial Bronze|celestial bronze]] knife, later a drakon-bone sword.

#### Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **This file** covers the *present-day* Annabeth, a pre-teen camper who has only just met this [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] ([[Ch07 - Rex Natus IV|Ch7]]). She knows nothing about the future. All she has are clues: Percy's uncanny knowledge, the voice in his head, his blueprints, and his slip in [[Ch13 - Rex Invictus VI|Ch13]] ("Not you Annabeth, the future you").
- **Future/old-timeline Annabeth** is a separate person. That Annabeth is Percy's ex, Second-in-command of the [[Council of Olympic Sentinels]], the "High-Lord-Engineer", and "the Architect". She designed the hardware of Parlamentum Mk.I (the AI that became [[Minerva (AI)|Minerva]]), secretly re-plumbed his suit's cooling with [[Lethe]] water, and championed his [[Ageis|Aegis]] Vestments surgery. She did not design the suit itself, which is "of his own design" ([[Ch01 - Rex Imperator I|Ch1]]); see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C14. See `F02_Annabeth_Chase_Future_Architect.md`. The present Annabeth must never speak as if she remembers any of that.
- **The fake Annabeth ([[Ch08 - Rex Invictus I|Ch8]])** is [[Athena]] wearing her face through the [[The Mist|Mist]] to question Percy. That was not Annabeth. See `D03_Athena_Present.md`.
- **[[Verrill Chase]]** is a far-future legacy who looks exactly like Annabeth. She is a different person, about 14,000 years later. See `F03_Verrill_Chase.md`.
- **The Monument of the Two** ([[Ch23 - Interlude I|Ch23]]) is a far-future statue of the adult Annabeth and Percy. It is legend, not this girl.

#### Appearance
- Blonde curls and storm-gray eyes; she often stands with her arms crossed ([[Ch07 - Rex Natus IV|Ch7]], [[Ch11 - Rex Invictus IV|Ch11]]).
- She taps her fingers on her knee when she's thinking, and steeples them when she's "interrogating" ([[Ch11 - Rex Invictus IV|Ch11]]).
- She carries a knife or dagger that [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] didn't know she had ([[Ch13 - Rex Invictus VI|Ch13]]–15). She also has an invisibility cap, which she "capped" in [[Ch14 - Rex Invictus VII|Ch14]].

- After touching the [[Golden Fleece]], her palms are branded with welts shaped like tiny sunbursts, tridents and crescent moons. They fade quickly ([[Ch14 - Rex Invictus VII|Ch14]]).

#### Personality
- **Suspicious and analytical.** From her first scene she sizes [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] up "from head to toe, assessing" ([[Ch07 - Rex Natus IV|Ch7]]). She notices he is "up to something" when he asks for adrenaline and morphine ([[Ch07 - Rex Natus IV|Ch7]]). She thinks he "knew too much at all times" ([[Ch11 - Rex Invictus IV|Ch11]]). She even shoots down his ADHD excuse: "You've zoned out on us too many times for it to be your ADHD" ([[Ch13 - Rex Invictus VI|Ch13]]).
- **Bossy and protective.** She escorts the injured Percy to the infirmary, "Worried you'll do something reckless". She also thumps Beckendorf for encouraging Percy's crude jokes ([[Ch08 - Rex Invictus I|Ch8]]).
- **A brilliant tactician and engineer.**
  - She designs the three-man cell structure of the Fulminus insurgency, with the [[Athena]] cabin as the "sole connection between these cells" ([[Ch09 - Rex Invictus II|Ch9]]).
  - She runs the drone-hangar raid and flies a captured drone by joystick ([[Ch09 - Rex Invictus II|Ch9]]).
  - The narration calls her "an aeronautical engineer" ([[Ch09 - Rex Invictus II|Ch9]]). Connor calls her "a maths whiz" ([[Ch08 - Rex Invictus I|Ch8]]).
- **Ruthless in a fight.** She points a paint-loaded machine gun at a technician's face ("You wanna go blind?"). When the technician mouths off, she empties twenty rounds into her (Ch9). A fellow raider remarks: "Remind me to never ever make her mad."
- **Theatrical and dry when she wants answers.** Her bus "efficiency assessment" in [[Ch11 - Rex Invictus IV|Ch11]] is a deadpan parody of a military debrief, to [[Clarisse]]'s delight.
- **Loyal underneath the suspicion.** When Athena calls Percy "the vessel", Annabeth snaps back: "He's not a vessel. He's Percy" ([[Ch13 - Rex Invictus VI|Ch13]]). She leaves camp without a quest to save [[Grover Underwood|Grover]] ([[Ch13 - Rex Invictus VI|Ch13]]).
- **Proud, and wounded by her heritage.** When the Fleece rejects her, she says bitterly, "Because I'm my mother's daughter" and "I wasn't prepared for my bloodline to be the enemy" ([[Ch14 - Rex Invictus VII|Ch14]]).
- **Possibly vulnerable.** She insists on hearing the Sirens: "I have to see… I have to know" ([[Ch14 - Rex Invictus VII|Ch14]]). The scene cuts before we learn what she heard.
- **Squeamish about worship.** She is openly disgusted by Eleni's religious hymns to Percy ([[Ch15 - Rex Invictus VIII|Ch15]]).
- **Growth.** She starts out treating Percy as a reckless new kid. She ends up treating him as a mystery that may save the camp, and as a friend she defends to her own mother. By [[Ch20 - Rex Invictus XIII|Ch20]] she watches him in silence, unsure whether she is seeing "shenanigans, insanity, or… both."
