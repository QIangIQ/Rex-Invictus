**[[Bianca di Angelo]]** is a [[Demigod]] daughter of [[Hades]] (Roman [[Pluto]]) and the Italian mortal Maria di Angelo, older sister of [[Nico di Angelo]], and a sworn [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunter of Artemis]]. She belongs to the [[Greek Pantheon]] and passed briefly through [[Camp Half-Blood]].

She exists in two versions:

- **Present day ([[Rex Invictus]] timeline):** the frightened girl from [[Westover Hall]] who joins [[Artemis]] and, unlike canon, survives the quest to [[Mt. Othrys]].
- **Far future ([[UOE]] era):** "Bianca Di'Angelo," a godlike woman on dead [[Gaia]] near the ruins of [[Mount Everest]], whose link to the present-day girl is an open mystery.

## PJO Canon

**Origin.** Bianca was born around 1924 to [[Hades]] and Maria di Angelo, an Italian woman [[Hades]] met in Venice. Hades moved the family to Washington, D.C. in the 1930s. After the [[Oath (ambiguous)|Pact of the Big Three]] was sworn following World War II, [[Zeus]] tried to kill the children with a lightning bolt. The strike missed them and killed Maria. Hades asked the [[Fury|Furies]] to carry Bianca and [[Nico di Angelo|Nico]] to the [[Lotus Hotel and Casino]] in Las Vegas, where time barely moves. They stayed there for about seventy years without aging. A lawyer sent by Hades later pulled them out, wiped their memories with the [[Lethe]] and enrolled them at Westover Hall in Maine.

**[[The Titan's Curse]] (canon).** [[Grover Underwood]] detected the siblings at Westover Hall. [[Percy Jackson]], [[Annabeth Chase]] and [[Thalia Grace]] arrived to extract them, and [[Dr. Thorn]], a [[Manticore]], attacked. [[Artemis]] and her [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] intervened. Bianca was offered a place in the Hunt and accepted, partly to escape the burden of raising Nico alone. She received a silver bow and quickly showed skill with it.

She joined [[Zoe Nightshade]], [[Thalia Grace|Thalia]], [[Grover Underwood|Grover]] and [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] on the quest to rescue Artemis. On the way she spoke with Percy about her past, and they met [[Apollo]] and the [[Hesperide|Hesperides]]. In the junkyard of [[Hephaestus]] she took a small god figurine (a Hades statuette) for Nico from the forbidden pile. Talos woke and attacked. Bianca climbed into the giant automaton, shorted its systems and died when it fell onto power lines. Her death, partly at the hands of the [[The Titan's Curse|Prophecy]] ("one shall be lost in the land without rain"), started Nico's grudge against Percy.

**After death.** Bianca chose [[Elysium]] and later rebirth. She speaks to Nico as a ghost in [[The Battle of the Labyrinth]], tells him to stop blaming Percy and to let go of revenge. In [[The Heroes of Olympus]] it is revealed she has already been reborn, so Nico can no longer reach her.

**Appearance (canon).** Olive skin, dark eyes and dark hair, often worn under a green floppy hat. About twelve years old at death.

**Canon books:** [[The Titan's Curse]] (main), [[The Battle of the Labyrinth]] (ghost), [[The House of Hades]] and [[The Sun and the Star]] (references).

## Rex Invictus (Present Day)

In [[Rex Invictus]], the reincarnated [[Percy Jackson]] changes the Westover Hall extraction. Bianca still joins [[Artemis]]'s Hunt, still goes west on a rewritten quest, and is introduced to [[New Rome]] as a daughter of [[Pluto]]. At Othrys she holds the line with [[Grover Underwood|Grover]] below the summit ([[Ch19 - Rex Invictus XII|Ch19]]). She is **alive** as of [[Ch23 - Interlude I|Ch23]], which breaks the canon death that the author's original vault entry ("Deceased Hunter") still records. See [[00a_CANON_RULINGS]] C21 for that ruling.

She is a named member of the [[Council of Olympic Sentinels]] strike roster in the old timeline, and the [[Lotus Hotel and Casino]] note tracks how [[Hades]] slowed time for her and [[Nico di Angelo|Nico]].

#### Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **This file:** the ordinary present-day demigod girl, newly a Hunter, still "untrained" with the bow.
- **NOT** "Bianca Di'Angelo" of far-future [[Gaia]] (`C09_Bianca_DiAngelo_FarFuture.md`). That figure is a godlike woman who appears more than 30,000 years after the Empire's rise, flattens dozens of Imperial soldiers on the ruins of Mount Everest, and speaks with lofty disdain. Present Bianca knows nothing of her and shows no such power. How the two are connected is an open mystery.
- In [[Percy Jackson|Percy]]'s original timeline Bianca died on this quest (canon). The present Bianca does not know that.

#### Appearance
- "Wide eyes" full of awe and fear on meeting [[Artemis]] ([[Ch16 - Rex Invictus IX|Ch16]]).
- On the road west her "once innocent eyes" adapt "sharply to every movement along the ridges" ([[Ch17 - Rex Invictus X|Ch17]]).

#### Personality
- **Panicked at first, then brave.** She looks "panicked" when Thorn has her and [[Nico di Angelo|Nico]] cornered. She insists "[[Percy Jackson|Percy]] saved us", but [[Artemis]] tells her "You stood your ground" ([[Ch16 - Rex Invictus IX|Ch16]]).

- **Decisive under her own terms.** She asks for time ("I need to think"), then stands "taller, brushing her brother's words aside with trembling dignity" ([[Ch16 - Rex Invictus IX|Ch16]]).
- **Quickly hardening.** She keeps arrows nocked in [[San Francisco]], scans the shop shelves, and keeps watch ([[Ch17 - Rex Invictus X|Ch17]]).

- **Practical and kind.** She points the injured Percy toward the Roman healers ([[Ch18 - Rex Invictus XI|Ch18]]).
- **Adaptive in battle.** Her untrained arrows "did not arc properly, so she compensated without thinking, releasing low, fast shots" ([[Ch19 - Rex Invictus XII|Ch19]]).

The bounty hunter Octavia ([[Bounty Hunters of Dead Gaia]]) watches her flatten a squad with raw power. Nothing on the page explains how this figure connects to the present-day girl.

On the ruins of Mount Everest a lone woman stands among dozens of defeated Imperial soldiers who tried to take her. She calls herself "Bianca Di'Angelo". She is calm, haughty and contemptuous, speaking of "third-rate fools" who rely on "bizarre contraptions" and of her own "esteemed self". When roused, her hair lifts in an invisible storm and pale blue fire burns in her eyes. Her presence feels like a natural disaster, enough to make even a former Hunter of Artemis go weak at the knees. No one knows where she came from, how she survived, or why the Empire's name stirs something in her. Rumour says the "universe's greatest weapon" lies hidden on Gaia. She may be that weapon. She may be something else entirely. She may be the girl from Westover Hall, changed beyond recognition. She shares only what she chooses to.
