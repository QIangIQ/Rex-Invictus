**[[Chiron]]** is the immortal [[Centaur]] trainer of heroes, activities director of [[Camp Half-Blood]]. He is the son of the Titan [[Kronos]] and the nymph Philyra, which makes him a half-brother of [[Zeus]]. He disguised himself as Mr. Brunner at Yancy Academy.

## Greek History (IRL)

**Myth.** Unlike the wild centaurs, Chiron was wise, just and learned in medicine, music and archery, taught by [[Apollo]] and [[Artemis]]. He trained [[Achilles]], [[Jason Grace|Jason]], [[Asclepius]], Heracles, [[Actaeon]] and [[Peleus]]. Heracles accidentally wounded him with an arrow poisoned by the Hydra's blood. Unable to die and in constant pain, he gave up his immortality, some say in exchange for [[Prometheus]]' freedom, and [[Zeus]] set him among the stars as Sagittarius or Centaurus.

---

## PJO Canon

Chiron runs training at [[Camp Half-Blood]], plays pinochle with [[Dionysus|Mr. D]] and uses a magic wheelchair to hide his horse half. He gave [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] [[Riptide]]. In [[The Sea of Monsters]] he is blamed for poisoning [[Thalia Grace|Thalia]]'s tree and fired, then reinstated. He leads the [[Party Ponies]] in [[The Last Olympian]]. In [[The Heroes of Olympus]] he coordinates with Camp Jupiter.

In Rex Invictus Chiron is alive and directs [[Camp Half-Blood]] after the Battle of Long Island ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]), ordering the [[Aphrodite]] cabin to clear younger campers into the Big House basement.

#### Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **Present-day Chiron (this file).** He knows [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] only as a promising son of [[Poseidon]] with strange knowledge. He has suspected an unusual "origin" since [[Ch07 - Rex Natus IV|Ch7]], and he learns of [[Minerva (AI)|Minerva]] and of future soldiers. He does not know the full truth of Percy's past life.
- **Future/old-timeline Chiron (note only).** In Percy's original life, Chiron was "not thrilled" about Percy's surgery to integrate the [[Ageis|Aegis]] Vestments ([[Ch19 - Rex Invictus XII|Ch19]]). In the far future Percy had been at [[Camp Half-Blood]] "for many millennia" ([[Ch08 - Rex Invictus I|Ch8]] narration), which implies a camp that lasted that long and possibly a Chiron who did too. No UOE-era Chiron appears on-page.

#### Appearance
- **As Mr. Brunner:** wheelchair-bound and old, with [[Riptide]] clipped to his jacket ([[Ch03 - Rex Natus I|Ch3]]).
- **At camp:** sits at the card table with "his wheelchair tucked beneath him" ([[Ch07 - Rex Natus IV|Ch7]]). In centaur form: hooves "shifting on the porch" ([[Ch12 - Rex Invictus V|Ch12]]), a tail that "swished nervously" ([[Ch11 - Rex Invictus IV|Ch11]]), "hooves planted on the grass" ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]).
- He steeples his fingers when thinking ([[Ch07 - Rex Natus IV|Ch7]]).
- He drinks coffee "like a soldier behind siege walls" ([[Ch20 - Rex Invictus XIII|Ch20]]).
- On the war-game screen, his "blurry twenty-year-old picture" stands in for the terrorist "[[Centaur]]" ([[Ch09 - Rex Invictus II|Ch9]]).

#### Personality
- **Patient strategist.** He plays cards with [[Dionysus]] and needles him: "You haven't played your move yet" ([[Ch07 - Rex Natus IV|Ch7]]).
- **Perceptive, and keeps his own counsel.** "If this boy's origin was who Chiron suspected…" ([[Ch07 - Rex Natus IV|Ch7]]). He gives [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] "a long look that suggested he was reserving his judgment" ([[Ch20 - Rex Invictus XIII|Ch20]]).
- **Warm, with gentle teasing.** "Right on time as always" ([[Ch03 - Rex Natus I|Ch3]]). "Try not to lose it now Percy, that thing is practically a family heirloom" ([[Ch03 - Rex Natus I|Ch3]]). He jokingly threatens Travis with [[Ares]] Cabin's "four-week intensive fitness programmes" ([[Ch08 - Rex Invictus I|Ch8]]).
- **A good sport about cons.** In Vegas he poses as a posh Scot, "a distant cousin" of the Queen, while struggling to keep a straight face. He loses "eight bucks playing the five-cent slot machines" ([[Ch08 - Rex Invictus I|Ch8]]).
- **Indulgent toward Mr. D.** "Dionysius is a big boy… I'll go down and fish him out of the casino if it looks like Julio's getting the better of him" ([[Ch08 - Rex Invictus I|Ch8]]).
- **Nervous before powerful goddesses.** Facing [[Artemis]] on the plane his tail swishes nervously, and he sits "solemnly" through Percy's screams ([[Ch11 - Rex Invictus IV|Ch11]]).
- **Calm under fire.** He organises the whole camp's defence "in a voice that didn't carry any panic at all" ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]). He calls in an old favor; [[Lupa]] agrees and outvotes the Senate, and the Romans come ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]).
- **Slightly technophobic.** He wiped his self-destructing laptop twice. A twelve-year-old called him "a security risk because of my age" (Ch8).

#### Voice & Speech Patterns
- Calm, measured, courteous and lecturing; he calls [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] "boy" kindly. He is formal with gods ("Milady [[Artemis]]", "Milord"), dry with [[Dionysus|Mr. D]], and grim when the news is bad. He doesn't swear except a mild "the damned thing" ([[Ch08 - Rex Invictus I|Ch8]]).

#### Backstory
- As a canon baseline: the immortal centaur trainer of heroes, son of [[Kronos]], who disguised himself as Mr. Brunner to watch [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] at Yancy.
- In this story he "personally shadowed" Percy ([[Ch07 - Rex Natus IV|Ch7]]). He has friends in Vegas from "her short stint as a casino host" [sic: the narration uses "her"] ([[Ch08 - Rex Invictus I|Ch8]]).
- He knows Percy's mother's secret about [[Poseidon]]; he suspects it in [[Ch07 - Rex Natus IV|Ch7]] and announces it at the claiming in [[Ch08 - Rex Invictus I|Ch8]].
- **Future note.** In Percy's old life, Chiron opposed or disliked the [[Ageis|Aegis]] Vestments surgery: "Chiron was not thrilled" ([[Ch19 - Rex Invictus XII|Ch19]]).
- Author's vault note (not yet in text): canon baseline as son of Kronos, leader of the [[Party Ponies]], and teacher of [[Achilles]], [[Jason Grace|Jason]], Percy, [[Actaeon]], Ajax, [[Asclepius]], [[Hercules]] and [[Patroclus]].
- Author's vault note (PLANNED, author intent; future era, not yet in text): in the Empire, Chiron is **Strategic Director, Training Command, of [[The Citadel]]**, a 3 AU Dyson-sphere academy that trains about six billion, with strategic oversight shared with "a reduced divine fragment of [[Dionysus]]".

#### Continuity Notes
- **"She/her" in [[Ch08 - Rex Invictus I|Ch8]].** The narration uses "she" twice for Chiron ("friends she'd made", "Chiron checked over her shoulder"). These are slips; Chiron is male.
- **"Goode High."** [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] tells him the story began at Goode High ([[Ch07 - Rex Natus IV|Ch7]]), but [[Ch03 - Rex Natus I|Ch3]] places it at Yancy Academy. By [[Ch15 - Rex Invictus VIII|Ch15]] (l.289) Percy really is at Goode High. [[Ch07 - Rex Natus IV|Ch7]] is either a slip or an anticipation.
- **Status.** Confirmed alive after [[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]] (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C20).
- **Wheelchair vs hooves.** He is in the wheelchair in [[Ch07 - Rex Natus IV|Ch7]] and in full centaur form elsewhere. Both are consistent with canon.
- **[[Ch11 - Rex Invictus IV|Ch11]].** [[Annabeth Chase|Annabeth]] says "We need to tell Chiron. Now." The next line, "Tell me what? And who's this Lady?", appears to be Chiron arriving, but it is unattributed.
