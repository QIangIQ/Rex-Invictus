**[[Triptolemus]]** was a prince of Eleusis to whom [[Demeter]] taught agriculture. He flew a chariot drawn by winged serpents to spread grain across the world. In [[The Son of Neptune]]-era Riordan canon he is a minor farming god who turns [[Nico di Angelo|Nico]] into a corn plant in [[The House of Hades]].
