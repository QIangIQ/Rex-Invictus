**[[Minos]]** was the king of Crete, son of [[Zeus]] and Europa, and after death a judge of the dead.

---

## Greek History (IRL)

He failed to sacrifice the white bull [[Poseidon]] sent him, so [[Poseidon]] made his wife Pasiphaë fall in love with it and she bore the [[Minotaur]]. [[Daedalus]] built the [[Labyrinth]] to hold it, and Athens paid a tribute of youths until Theseus, helped by [[Ariadne]], killed it. Minos pursued [[Daedalus]] to [[Sicily]], where the daughters of King Cocalus killed him in his bath. In [[Hades]] he judges the dead with Rhadamanthus and Aeacus. Arthur Evans named Bronze Age Minoan civilization after him.

## PJO Canon

In [[The Battle of the Labyrinth]] his ghost guides [[Nico di Angelo]], offering to trade a soul for [[Bianca di Angelo]] while seeking revenge on [[Daedalus]]. When Daedalus dies he takes Minos' place as a judge.
