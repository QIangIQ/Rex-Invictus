**[[Icarus]]** is the son of [[Daedalus]], who flew too close to the sun.

---

## Greek History (IRL)

[[Minos]] imprisoned [[Daedalus]] and Icarus in Crete. Daedalus made wings of feathers bound with wax and warned his son to fly neither too low, where spray would soak the feathers, nor too high. Icarus soared toward the sun, the wax melted and he fell into the sea near Samos, the Icarian Sea. Daedalus buried him on the island of Icaria.

## PJO Canon

In [[The Battle of the Labyrinth]], [[Daedalus]] still grieves for him and for his nephew Perdix, whose murder made him an outcast.

## UOE (far future)

The Icarus Pattern [[Titan (Mech)|Titans]] carry his name.
