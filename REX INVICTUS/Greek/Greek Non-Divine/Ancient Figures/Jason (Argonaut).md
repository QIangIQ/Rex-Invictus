**[[Jason (Argonaut)]]** led the Argonauts on the quest for the [[Golden Fleece]]. Not to be confused with the modern demigod [[Jason Grace]].

---

## Greek History (IRL)

Son of Aeson, the rightful king of Iolcus in Thessaly, he was raised by [[Chiron]] on [[Mount Pelion]] while his uncle Pelias held the throne. [[Hera]] favored him after he carried her, disguised as an old woman, across a river and lost a sandal doing it. Pelias sent him for the Fleece in Colchis aboard the [[Argo]]. With the help of the sorceress Medea, granddaughter of [[Helios]] and priestess of [[Hecate]], he yoked the fire-breathing [[Colchis Bulls]], defeated the [[Spartoi]] sown from dragon's teeth and took the Fleece. He later abandoned Medea for a princess of Corinth, Medea killed their children, and Jason died when a rotting beam of the [[Argo]] fell on him.

## PJO Canon

Listed among [[Chiron]]'s early students at [[Camp Half-Blood]]. [[Jason Grace]] is named for him, and the *Argo II* is named for his ship.
