**[[Galatea]]** is the name of two figures in Greek and later tradition.

---

## Greek History (IRL)

**The Nereid.** Galateia, "milk-white", is one of the [[Nereids]]. Theocritus and Ovid tell how the [[Cyclopes|Cyclops]] [[Polyphemus]] courted her with clumsy love songs, and how, when she chose the shepherd Acis, [[Polyphemus]] crushed him under a rock and she turned his blood into a river.

**The statue.** The ivory woman brought to life for [[Pygmalion]] by [[Aphrodite]]. Ovid gives her no name. Writers of the 18th century, Rousseau's *Pygmalion* (1762) among them, called her Galatea, and the name stuck.
