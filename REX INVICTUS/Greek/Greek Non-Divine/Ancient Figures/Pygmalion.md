**[[Pygmalion]]** is the sculptor of Cyprus who fell in love with his own statue.

---

## Greek History (IRL)

In Book 10 of Ovid's *Metamorphoses*, disgusted by the women of Cyprus, he carved an ivory woman more beautiful than any living one. At the festival of Venus ([[Aphrodite]]) he prayed for a wife like his statue, and when he kissed it at home it warmed into a living woman. Their daughter Paphos gave her name to the city sacred to [[Aphrodite]]. Ovid leaves the statue unnamed; the name [[Galatea]] comes from 18th-century retellings. The story lies behind George Bernard Shaw's *Pygmalion* and the "Pygmalion effect" in psychology.
