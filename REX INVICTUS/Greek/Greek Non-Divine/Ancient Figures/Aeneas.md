**[[Aeneas]]** is the Trojan prince, son of [[Aphrodite]] and the mortal Anchises, whom the Romans claimed as their ancestor.

---

## Greek History (IRL)

In the *Iliad* he is the second-best fighter of [[Troy]] after Hector and is rescued from death more than once by [[Aphrodite]], [[Apollo]] and even [[Poseidon]]. Virgil's *Aeneid* follows him out of the burning city with his father on his back, through his affair with Dido of Carthage, down to the [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] and into the war in Latium. His son Ascanius (Iulus) founds Alba Longa, the line that leads to [[Romulus and Remus]], and the Julian family of [[Julius Caesar]] and [[Augustus (Roman Emperor)|Augustus]] claimed descent from him.
