**[[Hesiod]]** was a Greek poet active around 700 BC in Boeotia, a near contemporary of Homer. His *Theogony* is the main source for the genealogy of the gods, from [[Chaos]] and [[Gaia]] through the [[Titans]] to the [[Olympians]] and the [[Titanomachy]]. His *Works and Days* gives farming advice and the myths of [[Prometheus]], Pandora and the Five Ages of Man.
