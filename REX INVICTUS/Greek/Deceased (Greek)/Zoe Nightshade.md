**[[Zoe Nightshade]]** (Zoë) is a [[Hesperide]], daughter of the Titan [[Atlas]] and the Oceanid Pleione (in Riordan's canon), and for over two thousand years the lieutenant of the [[Hunters of Artemis]]. She was cast out of the [[Garden of the Hesperides]] for helping [[Hercules]], to whom she gave the weapon that became [[Riptide]].

## Greek History (IRL)

**Mythic background.** The Hesperides are the nymphs of evening who tend the golden apples in a western garden guarded by the dragon [[Ladon]]. Their parentage varies: Nyx, [[Atlas]] with [[Hesperis]], or [[Phorcys]] and [[Ceto]]. In the myth of Heracles' eleventh labor, Atlas fetched the apples while Heracles held the sky. Zoë herself is Riordan's invention built on these figures; the name means "life" in Greek.

## PJO Canon

**Origins (canon).** Zoë helped [[Hercules]] steal the golden apples by giving him the hairpin that became [[Riptide]] ([[Riptide|Anaklusmos]]). He abandoned her and took the credit. Her family disowned her, and she joined [[Artemis]].

**[[The Titan's Curse]].** Zoë leads the [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] on the quest to rescue Artemis, though she distrusts [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] and the boys. She speaks archaic English and calls Percy "boy". The quest prophecy predicts that "one shall perish by a parent's hand". On [[Mt. Othrys]] she fights her father [[Atlas]] and is mortally wounded. She makes peace with Percy before dying, and Artemis places her among the stars as a constellation of the Huntress.

**Appearance (canon).** Copper-colored skin, dark eyes, a silver circlet, Persian-style features. She carries a bow and hunting knives.

#### Appearance
- She has dark hair, worn braided, and a circlet on her brow. Her face is "so pale and so still it could have been carved" ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]).
- She wears a silver parka in winter ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]) and silver Hunter garb otherwise.
- She carries a hunter's bow, plus knives that include a throwing knife that was "small, balanced, meant for throwing rather than combat" ([[Ch18 - Rex Invictus XI|Ch18]]).
- Flamel notes that she smells "like moss and dirt" and looks like a Persian princess ([[Ch17 - Rex Invictus X|Ch17]]).
- She moves lightly, "floated quietly above the ground" in the bookshop ([[Ch17 - Rex Invictus X|Ch17]]), and watches "from the shadows" ([[Ch18 - Rex Invictus XI|Ch18]]).

#### Personality
- **Commanding and practical.** At the war table she adjusts figurines on a map of America ([[Ch04 - Rex Natus II|Ch4]]). She sends scouts, orders the pelt brought along, and keeps discipline with chores ("laundry duty for the next week", [[Ch04 - Rex Natus II|Ch4]]). One look silences [[Phoebe]].
- **Contemptuous of males, at least on the surface.** She assumes a fire means "drunken males getting rowdy" and calls [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] "that male" and "boy" ([[Ch04 - Rex Natus II|Ch4]]) and "Freak" ([[Ch16 - Rex Invictus IX|Ch16]]). Phoebe coined the idea in Ch4 ("So, a freak?"); Zoë's own first "Freak" is in [[Ch16 - Rex Invictus IX|Ch16]].
- **Sharply analytical.** From Percy's sea-green eyes she deduces that [[Poseidon]] broke the oath, guesses his age, and works out that he is the prime candidate for the [[Great Prophecy]]. She knows the history of earlier sons of Poseidon ("Churchill"). She flags his nectar control as unprecedented (Ch4). She also sees straight through his amnesia act ([[Ch06 - Rex Natus III|Ch6]]).
- **Fiercely loyal to [[Artemis]].** She prays to summon her Lady ([[Ch06 - Rex Natus III|Ch6]]) and grows "agitated" and urgent once Artemis is taken ([[Ch16 - Rex Invictus IX|Ch16]]). One gentle "Zoë." from Artemis is enough to check her fury ([[Ch19 - Rex Invictus XII|Ch19]]).
- **Grudging fairness.** She lets Percy join the quest because "our Lady seems to trust thee" and she sees "no falsehood in thine eyes" (Ch16).
- **Rule-bound.** She looks "scandalized" as Percy talks to Sariel and bulldozes "Ancient Laws" ([[Ch17 - Rex Invictus X|Ch17]]), and again when he and Artemis embrace ([[Ch19 - Rex Invictus XII|Ch19]]).
- **Dry humour.** She tests Percy with a thrown knife ([[Ch18 - Rex Invictus XI|Ch18]]) and turns [[Grover Underwood|Grover]]'s "bad stench" back on him ([[Ch19 - Rex Invictus XII|Ch19]]).
- **A lethal, quiet hunter.** In the [[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]] woods fight she kills Imperial scouts she cannot see, counting "Five" under her breath, and adapts the moment they adapt.
- **Growth.** She moves from open hostility (Ch16) to a working tolerance of Percy and teasing tests ([[Ch18 - Rex Invictus XI|Ch18]]). After Othrys her anger at him is real (Ch19). By [[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]] she is simply Artemis's blade again. When the dying Sabine speaks, she kneels and "looked like she might say something", a rare crack of softness.

#### Voice & Speech Patterns
Her English is archaic and often ungrammatical: thee/thou/thine, "-eth" and "-est" verb endings, "shouldst", "doth", "hast". She sometimes slips into plain modern speech ("5:30 Tomorrow. Don't be late"). She addresses [[Artemis]] as "Milady", "my Lady" or "Lady", [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] as "Freak", "boy" or "son of [[Poseidon]]", and her [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] by name with a title ("Huntress [[Naomi]]"). She almost never swears. Her style is terse and imperative, and she gives distances in archaic units ("Two-half stadia thousands", roughly 500 km).

#### Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- Immortal Hunter. She is a master archer and knife-fighter, with throwing knives and hunting knives.
- **"[[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II|The Lady]]'s blessing"** lets her feel where prey will be before it moves ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]). The [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] are so "saturated" in [[Artemis]]'s blessings that Imperial Geistjäger counters, thermal and ELINT cannot detect them ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]).
- She can summon Artemis by prayer ([[Ch06 - Rex Natus III|Ch6]]).
- She tracks by scent and senses at long range ("Mine senses detect something afoul brewing", [[Ch16 - Rex Invictus IX|Ch16]]).
- She carries field medicine (nectar, ambrosia, medipacks).
- She has a long history with [[Riptide|Anaklusmos]]. Artemis recalls that "Anaklusmos had always been more than bronze, Zoe had told her that long ago" ([[Ch16 - Rex Invictus IX|Ch16]]), and calls the sword "Zoë's sword".

#### Backstory
- She is a former [[Hesperide]] and [[Artemis]]'s long-serving Lieutenant. The story leans on canon for this and adds few new details.
- In canon, [[Riptide]] began as her gift. The story nods to this with "Zoë's sword" ([[Ch16 - Rex Invictus IX|Ch16]]).
- [[Percy Jackson|Percy]]'s future memory: in the original timeline she died on the Titan's Curse quest. He resolves "He couldn't let Zoe die again" ([[Ch16 - Rex Invictus IX|Ch16]]).
- **Author's vault note (not yet in text):** the vault files her as "Former Lieutenant" of Artemis, a Hesperide and daughter of [[Atlas]], cast out of the [[Garden of the Hesperides]] for betraying them by giving Riptide to [[Hercules]]; its [[Calypso]] note makes her Calypso's sister. This is canon-baseline lore the story leans on.

  - In the **old timeline** ([[Ch01 - Rex Imperator I|Ch1]]), "The Eternal Hunter Princess" taught Percy "ancient dialects when they were on their subjugation of Lord [[Atlas]] in the very beginning of his journeys", the canon *Titan's Curse* quest. The text never names her, but "Princess" (a Titan's daughter), the Atlas quest and her archaic speech point to Zoë. [[Artemis]] is the only other candidate. **Flag as a strong inference.**
  - In the **far-future Empire**, she is "on the Wall", taught to children, "Oldest Hunter of the Director", her record "ARCHIVED, DECEASED".
- The **ghost of Zoë** that "bloomed over" Percy's old future [[Riptide]] "like a golden corona" ([[Ch14 - Rex Invictus VII|Ch14]]) belongs to the sword's far-future history ("His old riptide, which he lost a few millennia ago"). This file is the owner of that entry; C04 only cross-references it. Present Zoë is alive elsewhere at that moment, so it is an echo tied to the sword, not her acting.
- [[Thalia Grace]] has been Lieutenant for ~14,000 years in the future (`C07_Thalia_Grace_Future_Lieutenant.md`), which implies Zoë's death came early in Imperial history. The text does not say when.

#### Appearance
No future-era description exists. Imperial optics identify her from her present-day look ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]): dark braided hair, a circlet, a silver parka, a pale, still face "so still it could have been carved". The [[Ch14 - Rex Invictus VII|Ch14]] ghost appears only as "a golden corona… Like a halo around a biblical archangel."

#### Personality
Known only by reputation and implication:
- **Teacher.** She taught [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] archaic dialects on the [[Atlas]] quest, a skill that later lets him understand [[Chaos]] ([[Ch01 - Rex Imperator I|Ch1]]).
- **Exemplary, revered.** She is taught "at school" across the Empire ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]).
- **Bound to the sword.** [[Riptide]] was originally hers ("Zoë's sword", [[Ch16 - Rex Invictus IX|Ch16]]). She had told [[Artemis]] "long ago" that it "had always been more than bronze" ([[Ch16 - Rex Invictus IX|Ch16]]).
- The [[Ch14 - Rex Invictus VII|Ch14]] regretful female voice in the sword ("Don't resist it. You will lose yourself.") is **unattributed**. It could be Zoë or [[Minerva (AI)|Minerva]] ("Like hers, but not her"). Do not assert it.

#### Voice & Speech Patterns
There are no future-era lines. Her known voice (present-day) is archaic English: thee, thou, thine, hast, "Thou knowest my name" ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]), and "Not yet" ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]). Roleplay of the legend should use this register.

#### Backstory
- **Old timeline:** joined [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] on the subjugation of [[Atlas]] (canon *Titan's Curse*), teaching him dialects. Her fate there is unstated; in canon she dies on that quest.
- **Author's vault note (not yet in text):** the vault makes her a [[Hesperide]] and daughter of Atlas, cast out of the [[Garden of the Hesperides]] for betraying her sisters by giving [[Riptide]] to [[Hercules]], and (in its [[Calypso]] note) Calypso's sister. Canon-baseline lore; the story only alludes to it ("former hesperide", "Zoë's sword").
- **Imperial history:** became the Director's first Lieutenant, died at an unknown time, and was memorialised on the Wall. On Long Island ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]) present-day [[Artemis]] learns, through Zoë's report of what the dying [[Sciritae]] Owl-Lead Sabine Ro told her, that Zoë "is supposed to be dead". In [[Ch23 - Interlude I|Ch23]], ~14,000 years later, the Director seals the record: "NO IMPERIAL PERSONNEL MAY DISCLOSE THE DATE OR MANNER OF HER DEATH TO ANY PARTY, INCLUDING THE DIRECTOR, IN ANY EPOCH."

#### Continuity Notes
- In canon *Titan's Curse* Zoë dies after Othrys. In this timeline a [[Hesperide]] fulfils the "parent's hand" line instead ([[Ch19 - Rex Invictus XII|Ch19]]), yet the Empire still records her as deceased, so she dies later.
- The "first" and "oldest" titles conflict slightly with [[Thalia Grace|Thalia]]'s ~14,000-year tenure, unless Zoë died early. The text gives no date.
- **Sealed, not "MISSING"** (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C7): the author's [[Plot Threads]] once said the Director marks Zoë "MISSING. NOT DEAD. LEAVE SPACE ON WALL." That wording is a superseded draft. In the published [[Ch23 - Interlude I|Ch23]], "MISSING IN ACTION… LEAVE SPACE ON WALL" applies only to the Pyrrhuiesian Reserve Echelon; Zoë's record is sealed.
- **Vault status** (C21): the vault files Zoë under "Deceased", copied from the original books. Do not carry this over to the present-day Zoë, who is alive in this story.
- **"The Eternal Hunter Princess"** ([[Ch01 - Rex Imperator I|Ch1]]) is a strong inference for Zoë, not a confirmed identification; other profiles (e.g. E16) should say "probably".
