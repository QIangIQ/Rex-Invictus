### Appearances

#### First Mention
Chapter 247
Ouranos, also known as Uranus, was an ancient God, an Original Primordial God of the [[Greek Pantheon]], a Creator God associated with the Sky, and the Divine ruler who preceded Kronus in the ancient Greek order. He belonged to the earliest generation of Greek Divinity alongside ancient Gods such as [[Gaia]], Nyx, [[Erebus]] and [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]], placing him among the foundational Gods from which much of the later Greek Divine lineage developed.[3]
Ouranos was formerly married to Gaia and was the father of at least Kronus and [[Rhea]], two members of the ancient Titan generation.[1] His Divine remains were also responsible for the unusual birth of [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood. After Ouranos was mutilated, Aphrodite emerged from his severed reproductive organ and consequently belonged naturally to the Second Generation of [[Primordial Gods]] despite later being politically associated with the Olympians.[1]
His reign ended when Kronus killed him and took the Greek throne. The succession is directly remembered in the later Greek conflict, where Kronus is described as having killed Ancient King Uranus before becoming king himself. Kronus was eventually overthrown by his own son [[Zeus]], continuing a cycle of violent succession that characterized much of the ancient Greek Divine order.[2]
Although Ouranos is associated with the Sky, the later cosmology of the series makes an important distinction between a Primordial God possessing a Concept and a true Primordial Entity embodying a universal aspect of Creation. Ouranos should therefore not be treated as the literal universal Sky itself. Like Kronus with Time, he was an extraordinarily ancient Divine user and personification of his Concept rather than the absolute cosmic source of that Concept.[4]

### Physical Appearance
Ouranos has never made a direct on-page appearance in the main narrative and currently has no canonical physical description. His height, body type, hair color, eye color, clothing, Divine manifestation and any transformation connected to his Sky Divinity remain unknown.
The image used on this page is consequently an artistic interpretation rather than an official representation of Ouranos.

### Personality
Ouranos died long before the events of the main story and has never received direct dialogue or a point-of-view scene. His individual personality is therefore largely unknown.
The novel confirms his position as an ancient ruler of the Greek Gods and the father of Kronus, but it does not presently provide enough direct material to construct a detailed psychological profile. His personal relationship with [[Gaia]] before their separation, his methods of government, his attitude toward his children and his views concerning other Pantheons have not been explored directly.
Because many traditional stories concerning Uranus exist outside the novel, mythology-specific characterization should not be treated as part of Ouranos' MTW characterization unless the story independently establishes it.

### History

#### The Original Greek Divine Generation
Ouranos belonged to the earliest generation of Gods associated with the ancient [[Greek Pantheon]]. Later explanations of the Divine hierarchy classify him alongside [[Gaia]], Nyx, [[Erebus]] and [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] as one of the Original or First Generation [[Primordial Gods]].[3]
These Beings predated the generations represented by the [[Titans]] and Olympians and possessed Divine foundations far beyond normal Gods. The broad difference between an Original Primordial God and an ordinary Second Generation Primordial God could be enormous, although belonging to the older generation did not make every individual equal in combat ability.[3]
Gaia is specifically identified in later material as the first Being born within the Greek Pantheon's ancient Divine lineage, meaning Ouranos should not be described as the first Greek God or the origin of every Greek Primordial.[5] Instead, he belonged to the same broad foundational era of Greek Divinity.
The exact origin of Ouranos himself has not been explained. The story has not established his parents, a creator responsible for his existence or the precise mechanism through which his Divinities first developed.

#### Sky and Creation
Ouranos is associated primarily with the Sky and is indexed among the ancient Creator Gods of the setting. His Divine identity therefore belonged to a considerably older conceptual structure than that of the later Olympian Sky God [[Zeus]].[3]
The precise applications of Ouranos' Sky and Creation-related powers have never been demonstrated. No canonical scene currently shows him controlling weather, manipulating stars, creating a world, producing a specific species through his Divinity or using a named Creation technique. His status as a Creator God should consequently be treated as a Divine classification rather than permission to import every ability traditionally associated with the mythological Uranus.

#### Ancient King of the Greek Gods
Before Kronus and [[Zeus]], Ouranos occupied the throne of the ancient Greek Divine order. Chapter 720 explicitly remembers him as Ancient King Uranus.[2]
His kingship makes him the earliest currently confirmed ruler in the direct succession that eventually led to the Olympian regime:

It is therefore unknown whether Mount Olympus already functioned in the same manner later associated with Kronus and Zeus, what territories Ouranos directly controlled or how the early Greek Gods divided their authorities.

#### Marriage to Gaia
Ouranos was the former husband of [[Gaia]] Elderblood. Their relationship belongs to the earliest era of Greek Divine history and produced at least Kronus and [[Rhea]], establishing Ouranos as one of the direct ancestors of the later Olympian royal family.[1]
The novel does not currently provide sufficient evidence to classify Gaia and Ouranos as biological siblings. Although both belong to the same broad Original Primordial generation, membership within that generation does not by itself establish a sibling relationship.
Similarly, traditional mythological accounts concerning every Titan, [[Cyclopes|Cyclops]], Hecatoncheir, Giant, Erinys or Meliae should not automatically be incorporated into their MTW family tree. Only relationships actually established by the novel should be indexed as canon.

#### Kronus and Rhea
Kronus and [[Rhea]] belonged to the generation following the Original Greek Primordials and are identified as children of [[Gaia]] and Ouranos.
Kronus became one of the most important descendants of the original Greek Gods. He developed into the Primordial God associated with Time and eventually ruled the [[Greek Pantheon]] before [[Zeus]].[4]
Rhea likewise survived as an ancient member of the Titan generation. Through the later family line of Kronus and Rhea, Ouranos became an ancestor of the major Olympian Gods, including Zeus, [[Hades]], [[Poseidon]], [[Hera]], [[Hestia]] and [[Demeter]].
The story has not provided a complete canon genealogy for every Titan traditionally associated with Ouranos. Consequently, only directly supported descendants are listed on this page.

#### Death and the Rise of Kronus
Ouranos' reign ended through patricide. Kronus killed his father and became the next king of the Greek Gods.[2]
The exact battle or circumstances surrounding Ouranos' death have never been shown directly. The novel does not presently describe the complete confrontation, the weapons involved, whether other Gods participated, the duration of the conflict or the precise condition in which Ouranos was defeated.
This is important when comparing their strength. Kronus killing Ouranos establishes that he successfully overthrew his father, but the story does not provide enough detail concerning the ancient event to reconstruct a complete combat sequence or prove that the two fought under perfectly equal circumstances.
Kronus later attempted to claim that the Greek throne was rightfully his. [[Aphrodite]] rejected the idea because Kronus himself had obtained the throne by killing Ancient King Uranus, while [[Zeus]] subsequently took it from Kronus through another successful overthrow.[2]
The Greek throne therefore had a long history of being transferred through force rather than possessing an unquestionable permanent owner.

#### Birth of Aphrodite
Ouranos' death also resulted in one of the most unusual births within the [[Greek Pantheon]].
[[Aphrodite]] Elderblood was not produced through conventional reproduction between two Gods. According to the explanation given during the Greek conflict, Aphrodite emerged after Ouranos' reproductive organ was severed. The Divine material produced a new Goddess, making Ouranos the unintended source of Aphrodite's existence.[1]
This origin is important to Aphrodite's classification. Despite spending much of her history among the Olympian Gods, she was naturally a Second Generation Primordial Goddess because her Divine existence originated directly from the body of an Original Primordial God.[1]
Ouranos therefore occupies an unusual position in Aphrodite's genealogy. He can be described as her Divine progenitor or unintended creator, but he did not consciously conceive, raise or personally create her through a known technique.

#### Legacy
Ouranos remained dead throughout the main narrative, but his lineage continued shaping the [[Greek Pantheon]] for eons.
His son Kronus became king and fathered the generation from which the Olympian order emerged. His daughter [[Rhea]] remained part of the surviving Titan lineage, while [[Aphrodite]] eventually became one of the most important Greek Goddesses and later joined [[Victor Elderblood]].
The conflict surrounding the Greek throne continued long after Ouranos' death. Kronus was overthrown by [[Zeus]], Zeus' regime eventually fractured under centuries of internal problems, and the final Greek civil war ended with the destruction of the old political order under Victor.
Ouranos consequently remains historically important despite never appearing directly: he was one of the foundational Gods of the Greek Pantheon, an ancient king, the progenitor of major branches of Greek Divinity and the first confirmed ruler in the succession eventually inherited by Kronus and Zeus.

### Synopsis

#### A New Pillar
Ouranos does not appear directly during this period, but his existence becomes relevant through [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood. Aphrodite's introduction establishes her as an extraordinarily ancient Goddess whose Divine origin precedes the normal Olympian generations.[6]
The exact mechanism behind her birth is explained more completely much later, establishing that her connection to Ouranos was not conventional parentage but the result of her emergence from his severed Divine remains.[1]

#### King Among Kings
The ancient history of Ouranos becomes considerably more relevant during the final collapse of the [[Greek Pantheon]]. Kronus returned to the struggle for control of Greece and attempted to present himself as the rightful ruler.
[[Aphrodite]] rejected that interpretation of history. Kronus had only become king after killing his father, Ancient King Uranus, while [[Zeus]] later overthrew Kronus and became the next ruler. The history of Greece therefore provided no basis for treating Kronus' reign as an eternal legitimate ownership of the throne.[2]
The conflict also clarified the Divine hierarchy to which Ouranos belonged. Kronus was a Second Generation Primordial God, whereas Ouranos belonged to the much older Original Primordial generation.[3][4]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s confrontation with Kronus later established another important rule relevant to understanding Ouranos. Kronus behaved as though possessing the Divinity of Time made him Time itself, but Victor rejected that assumption. [[Primordial Gods]] wield Concepts and may personify them to an extraordinary degree, while actual universal embodiment belongs to true [[Primordial Entities]].[4]
The same rule applies when interpreting Ouranos' status as the Primordial God of the Sky.

### Skills & Abilities
Ouranos has never participated in an on-page battle, and the novel has not revealed a complete ability set for him. Only powers and classifications directly supported by his established Divine nature are indexed below.

#### Original Primordial God Physiology
- God Physiology: Ouranos was a genuine God belonging to the ancient Greek Divine lineage.
- Original Primordial God Physiology: Ouranos belonged to the Original generation of [[Primordial Gods]], the ancient foundational class preceding Second Generation Primordial Gods such as Kronus.[3]
- [[Divine Energy]]: As an Original Primordial God, Ouranos possessed the Divine Energy inherent to his Godhood. The exact quantity of Energy available to him has never been stated.
- Divine Soul: Ouranos possessed the Divine spiritual foundation required for Primordial Godhood. No personal Soul technique has been demonstrated.
- Agelessness: Ouranos belonged to an ancient Divine generation predating the Olympian order and was not subject to ordinary Mortal aging.
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics: As a Primordial God, his body existed vastly beyond ordinary Mortal physiology, although no personal strength, speed or durability feat has been shown.

#### Sky Divinity
- Divinity — Sky: Ouranos' primary known Divine association is the Sky, making him the ancient Greek Primordial God connected to that Concept.
- Divine Authority over the Sky: His Godhood granted conceptual authority related to the Sky. The novel has not demonstrated its specific applications, limitations or combat techniques.
- Divine Personification: Ouranos could represent the Sky within the Greek Divine system without being the absolute Universal embodiment of the Concept. [[Primordial Gods]] remain users and Divine personifications of Concepts rather than true [[Primordial Entities]].[4]

#### Creation
- Creator God: Ouranos is classified among the ancient Creator Gods and is associated with Creation in addition to the Sky.[3]
- Creation-Related Divinity: His Divine structure possessed a relationship with Creation, although no named Creation ability or personally demonstrated act of deliberate Creation has been revealed.
- Divine Progenitor: Ouranos became one of the ancestral sources of later Greek Divinity through descendants such as Kronus and [[Rhea]].
- Divine Remnant Generation: Divine material originating from Ouranos retained enough metaphysical potency after his mutilation to give rise to [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood, a Second Generation Primordial Goddess.[1] This is a property demonstrated through Aphrodite's origin rather than a confirmed technique Ouranos consciously activated.

### Power
Ouranos was an Original Primordial God, placing him within one of the highest natural Divine generations known on ancient Earth.[3]
The story establishes a substantial difference between Original [[Primordial Gods]] and the ordinary baseline of the Second Generation. Figures such as Kronus could already possess enormously powerful Concepts and rank among Primordial Gods, yet the older generation represented a still more ancient and naturally developed class.[3]
Ouranos should nevertheless not be assigned an exact combat level beyond that classification. He has never fought on-page, and the novel provides no personal destructive feat, speed feat, durability feat, total Energy measurement or comparison placing him directly above or below a particular Original Primordial God.
Kronus killing him is a historical fact, but the exact circumstances of the ancient confrontation have not been narrated.[2] It therefore establishes Kronus' successful overthrow without providing enough information to calculate Ouranos' complete combat capabilities from the event alone.

### Relationships

#### Gaia Elderblood
[[Gaia]] was Ouranos' former wife and belonged to the same ancient era of Greek Primordial Divinity.[1]
Their union produced at least Kronus and [[Rhea]], making them two of the principal ancestors of the later Greek royal lineage. Beyond the confirmation that they were husband and wife, the novel has revealed very little about the personal nature of their relationship.
The current story does not provide sufficient evidence to describe Gaia and Ouranos as biological siblings, nor does it give a detailed account of how their marriage began or ended. These details should therefore remain unstated until additional canon information is provided.

#### Kronus
Kronus was Ouranos' son and eventual killer.
Kronus killed Ancient King Uranus and took the Greek throne for himself.[2] He subsequently became King of the [[Titans]] and the major ruler preceding [[Zeus]].
Their ancient conflict has never been depicted directly. The novel confirms its result but not a complete sequence of events, making it inappropriate to assign specific tactics, weapons, allies or motives to Ouranos' death without further evidence.
Kronus' own reign later ended through another generational overthrow when Zeus replaced him.

#### Rhea
[[Rhea]] was a daughter of [[Gaia]] and Ouranos and belonged to the Second Generation Primordial lineage.
Through the later Greek royal family, Rhea became one of the major links connecting Ouranos to the Olympian Gods. Her children with Kronus formed the generation containing [[Zeus]], [[Hades]], [[Poseidon]], [[Hera]], [[Hestia]] and [[Demeter]].

#### Aphrodite Elderblood
[[Aphrodite]] Elderblood originated from Ouranos in a fundamentally different manner from Kronus and [[Rhea]].
After Ouranos was mutilated, his severed reproductive organ produced Aphrodite. She was consequently a Goddess born directly from the Divine material of an Original Primordial God and naturally possessed the classification of a Second Generation Primordial Goddess.[1]
Ouranos can therefore be regarded as Aphrodite's Divine progenitor or unintended creator, but the two are not known to have shared an ordinary parent-child relationship. Ouranos died in the ancient era, and no interaction between them has been shown.

#### Nyx Elderblood
Nyx belonged to the same broad Original Greek Primordial generation as Ouranos.[3]
The novel does not presently establish that they were biological siblings, nor has any direct interaction between them been described.

#### Erebus
[[Erebus]] likewise belonged to the original Greek Primordial generation alongside Ouranos.[3]
No direct personal relationship between the two has been explored, and their shared generation should not automatically be interpreted as proof of a biological sibling relationship.

#### Tartarus
[[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] belonged to the same ancient Greek Primordial generation as Ouranos.[3]
Later Greek history connected Tartarus much more heavily with [[Gaia]], but the novel does not currently provide enough information concerning Ouranos and Tartarus personally to establish a direct familial or political relationship beyond their common place within the original Greek Divine era.

### Distinction from a Primordial Entity
Ouranos' status as the Primordial God associated with the Sky should not be confused with literal universal embodiment.
During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s battle against Kronus, Victor explained that [[Primordial Gods]] are not automatically the fundamental Concepts they wield. Kronus possessed extraordinary authority over Time but was not Time itself; true embodiment at that scale belongs to the [[Primordial Entities]] representing fundamental aspects of the Universe.[4]
The same principle applies to Ouranos:
Ouranos = Original Primordial God associated with the Sky
Ouranos ≠ the absolute Universal embodiment or origin of every Sky in Creation
This distinction prevents the terminology used by ancient Pantheons from being confused with the considerably higher cosmological state represented by true Primordial Entities.
