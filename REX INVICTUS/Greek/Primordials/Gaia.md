Gaia Elderblood, formerly known simply as Gaia, and also called Gaea, Terra, the Great Mother, Mother Earth, and Mother Nature, is an Original Primordial Goddess, the oldest Goddess of the [[Greek Pantheon]], one of the original Protogenoi, and a current Primordial Dragon Goddess belonging to the Elderblood Clan. As the Primordial Goddess of Earth and Nature, Gaia possesses an exceptionally ancient connection to the natural world and is associated with several fundamental aspects of terrestrial life, including Earth, Nature, Life, Motherhood, and Fertility. Her position among the original Greek Divinities made her one of the principal progenitors of the Pantheon's Divine families, whose descendants include the [[Titans]], [[Cyclopes]], Hecatonchires, Olympians, and numerous later generations of Gods.[1][4]
As the eldest Goddess of her Pantheon, Gaia belonged to the original generation of Divine beings responsible for establishing the supernatural foundations of the Greek Divine order. Her relationships with [[Ouranos]], Kronus, [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]], and [[Zeus]] contributed to several major conflicts that repeatedly transformed the Pantheon's political structure. Her opposition to the mistreatment and imprisonment of her children led her to support successive rebellions against rulers belonging to her own family, while her desire for revenge eventually contributed to the creation of catastrophic beings such as [[Typhon]]. Gaia's extraordinary maternal instincts became inseparable from her tendency to manipulate events from behind the scenes, creating and empowering other beings rather than confronting her enemies directly.[7][5]
Despite the widespread identification of Gaia with the Earth itself, her actual existence is distinct from the literal planet. She is a powerful Earth and Nature Goddess whose Divinities govern aspects of the natural world, rather than a living planetary body comparable to a World Tree. This distinction is particularly important within the novel's cosmology, where [[World Trees]] such as [[Roxanne Elderblood]] and [[Amara Elderblood]] maintain direct connections with planetary environments and their supernatural foundations. Gaia's authority over Earth and Nature allows her to influence natural processes on an enormous scale, but her connection to a particular world is not equivalent to the planetary existence possessed by a genuine World Tree.[8]
Gaia's first direct encounter with [[Victor Elderblood]] occurred during the destruction of the old Greek Pantheon. She attempted to protect the surviving members of her original family, particularly Tartarus, but was overwhelmed when Victor became the Primogenitor of the [[Blood Dragons]]. Following her defeat, Victor transported Gaia into his Personal Universe, where her connection to Nature made her valuable in developing the natural environment alongside Roxanne. Although their relationship began through hostility and captivity, Gaia gradually adapted to her new circumstances, abandoned much of her previous resentment toward the Greek Gods, and became an active member of Victor's expanding faction.[1][9]
Gaia subsequently participated in the creation of Dragon's Nest, contributing her immense terrestrial authority to the formation of the new world and the development of its natural environment. She later accepted Victor's transformation into the True Blood Dragon Race, preserving her ancient Divinities while acquiring a superior Draconic physical foundation. Her resulting existence combines the characteristics of an Original Primordial Goddess with those of a True Dragon, earning her the classification of a Primordial Dragon Goddess. Gaia eventually became one of Victor's wives and the mother of their daughter, [[Gina Elderblood]], whose inherited potential reflects the extraordinary union between a primordial Mother Goddess and the Dragon God of [[Chaos]].[10][2][3][6]

### Physical Appearance
Gaia possesses the appearance of an exceptionally beautiful, mature woman with extraordinarily long light-green hair that extends to the ground, luminous green eyes, fair skin, and a voluptuous figure. Her appearance is closely associated with the natural world she governs, and her body is ordinarily covered by living plants, leaves, and vines rather than conventional clothing. The greenery appears naturally arranged around her, emphasizing her identity as a Mother Goddess whose existence is intimately connected to vegetation and the terrestrial environment.[1]
Her presence is unmistakable to individuals familiar with the [[Greek Pantheon]]. When Gaia appeared before [[Scathach]] in the [[Greek Underworld]], the ancient Vampire immediately recognized her despite never having personally encountered her before. Gaia's unusually long green hair, plant-covered body, and overwhelming Primordial presence corresponded so closely with the traditional descriptions of the Mother Goddess that her identity was immediately apparent.[1]
During her original Divine existence, Gaia possessed a body formed around the supernatural characteristics of a First Generation Primordial Goddess. Her tremendous authority over Earth and Nature allowed the surrounding environment to respond to her emotional condition, and her appearance could become especially intimidating when she released her anger. Her normally composed expression was replaced by visible rage during her confrontation with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], while her eyes glowed with an intense green light and the terrain around her began moving violently.[1]
Following her transformation into a True Blood Dragon, Gaia retained the essential appearance and identity of her original form while incorporating the superior racial foundation of a True Dragon. The transformation reconstructed her body and Soul through Victor's Draconic power without erasing the memories, personality, or Divine characteristics that made her an Original Primordial Goddess. Her present existence is therefore not a separate individual possessing Gaia's appearance, but the continuation of the same ancient Goddess through a fundamentally altered and considerably more powerful supernatural body.[2][3]

### Personality
Gaia is an exceptionally ancient, calculating, proud, and possessive woman whose personality was originally dominated by her identity as the Mother Goddess of the [[Greek Pantheon]]. Her affection for her children is genuine, but frequently manifests through an overwhelming desire to protect them and retaliate against those she believes have harmed or betrayed them. This tendency contributed to a long history of conflict within the Greek Divine family, beginning with the rebellion against [[Ouranos]] and continuing through the struggles involving Kronus, [[Zeus]], [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]], and [[Typhon]]. Gaia repeatedly supported members of younger generations when she believed existing rulers had abused their authority, only to become hostile toward those same descendants when they eventually adopted practices she considered equally unacceptable. Her resentment accumulated across eons, gradually transforming maternal protectiveness into a destructive cycle of vengeance that threatened the very Pantheon she had helped establish.[7][4]
Unlike her brothers [[Erebus]] and Tartarus, Gaia was never primarily interested in conventional combat. Despite possessing the extraordinary supernatural power of an Original Primordial Goddess, she preferred manipulating circumstances, creating powerful offspring, influencing the environment, and arranging for other beings to act on her behalf. Her personal experience fighting directly against comparable opponents was extraordinarily limited, with the number of genuine battles she had participated in being small enough to count on one hand. This distinction became particularly apparent during her first confrontation with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and [[Scathach]], when Gaia attempted to manipulate Scathach through an apparently peaceful conversation while secretly placing a mark upon her Soul. Her ability to deceive and control situations was considerably more developed than her familiarity with direct physical confrontation.[1]
Her pride remains substantial, reflecting both her immense age and the position she occupied among the Greek [[Primordial Gods]]. Gaia was accustomed to being treated with respect even by extraordinarily powerful members of her own family, and her status as the first Goddess born within the Pantheon made openly provoking her an unusual occurrence. When Victor struck her across the face during their confrontation in the [[Greek Underworld]], she became enraged primarily by the humiliation of being physically assaulted rather than by the damage itself. Her emotions immediately affected the surrounding terrain, causing mountains to rise and fall as the land moved uncontrollably beneath her power. Her fury also caused her to attempt one of the first direct punches of her extraordinarily long existence, demonstrating how unfamiliar ordinary hand-to-hand combat remained to her.[1]
Gaia's pride does not prevent her from recognizing when a confrontation has become impossible to win. Although she is frequently vindictive, she possesses a highly developed instinct for self-preservation and understands the value of retreating when resistance no longer offers any meaningful advantage. After Victor's transformation and the defeat of Tartarus, Gaia recognized that the collapse of the Greek Primordial faction had become inevitable. Rather than sacrifice herself alongside her remaining children, she attempted to escape the battlefield. This reaction reflected a deeply pragmatic aspect of her personality that had allowed her to survive the repeated destruction of entire Divine generations.[9]
Her relationship with Nature is more complicated than her hostility toward other supernatural beings might suggest. Gaia genuinely appreciates the beauty of untouched environments, particularly landscapes whose natural development has not yet been disrupted by civilization. During Victor's planetary construction project, she became visibly pleased by the opportunity to shape mountains, oceans, forests, frozen regions, and volcanic environments without the interference of established populations. The experience reminded her how strongly she disliked watching beautiful natural worlds gradually become altered through the activities of intelligent civilizations. She even considered persuading Victor to avoid populating the newly created world, although she recognized that such an arrangement would conflict with the purpose of the project.[10]
Gaia also possesses a considerable interest in observing others, aided by her natural connection to vegetation. Plants under her influence can serve as extensions of her perception, allowing her to hear and observe events occurring within environments connected to Nature. The ability contributed to the mistrust surrounding her during the old Greek era, when Zeus avoided placing plants within [[Olympus|Mount Olympus]] because he feared that Gaia would use them to overhear private conversations. Even after joining Victor's family, her habit of gathering information through the natural world occasionally became a source of embarrassment and teasing among the other women.[11]
Gaia's personality gradually changed after she became part of Victor's faction. Although her original entry into his Personal Universe was involuntary, the environment provided her with a degree of stability she had not experienced during the prolonged conflicts of the Greek Pantheon. Working alongside Roxanne allowed her to concentrate upon the development of Nature rather than constantly planning revenge against relatives who had repeatedly disappointed or betrayed her. Over time, she acknowledged that her new circumstances were comfortable and that she no longer felt the same need to involve herself in the destructive politics of her former family. Her growing attraction toward Victor further complicated her original hostility, eventually developing into a genuine romantic relationship.[9][2]
Her integration into the Elderblood family also forced her to confront weaknesses in her approach to motherhood. Gaia had historically allowed many of her Divine children considerable freedom, frequently avoiding the responsibilities involved in directly correcting their behavior. This tendency contributed to the pride, entitlement, and destructive conduct that characterized several generations of Greek Gods. When Victor observed similar problems developing among their descendants, he criticized Gaia and Nyx for allowing their children too much freedom without sufficient discipline. The reprimand affected both Goddesses, who became more reflective and began taking their responsibilities as mothers more seriously. Gaia's development therefore involved not merely abandoning her old political grievances, but learning to balance maternal affection with the guidance necessary to prevent her children from repeating the failures of the former Pantheon.[12]
By the mature Imperial Era, Gaia had become noticeably more comfortable participating in the daily life of the Elderblood household. Her conversations with the other Greek Goddesses frequently reflected upon the destructive family conflicts that had characterized Olympus, and she openly acknowledged the relative peace enjoyed by Victor's current Pantheon. Although traces of her ancient arrogance and possessiveness remained, her new circumstances allowed her to appreciate a family whose members were generally united by their attachment to Victor rather than competing for control of the same Divine throne.[13]

### History

#### The First Greek Goddess
Gaia originated among the earliest generation of Divine beings associated with the [[Greek Pantheon]]. She was recognized as its first Goddess and belonged to the original Primordial generation alongside beings such as Nyx, [[Erebus]], [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]], and [[Ouranos]]. These original Gods possessed positions fundamentally different from those of the later Olympians because their existences were connected to the primary Divine foundations upon which the Pantheon had developed. Their relationship with the supernatural environment was sufficiently profound that battles between First Generation [[Primordial Gods]] could affect the stability of an entire Pantheon.[1][14]
As the eldest Goddess, Gaia occupied an extraordinary position within the family hierarchy. Her age and power commanded respect even among other original Primordials, while her connection to Earth and Nature gave her immense influence over terrestrial environments associated with the Greek Divine order. She also became one of the principal sources of the Pantheon's later genealogical development, producing numerous children whose inherited Divine characteristics established several important supernatural lineages.[4][1]
Despite her titles and the traditional association between Gaia and the Earth, she was not a literal embodiment of the planet itself. Her Divine authority allowed her to represent and control aspects of terrestrial Nature, but the deeper cosmological structure of the Universe distinguished such Gods from genuine [[Primordial Entities]] and [[World Trees]]. The Primordial Entities govern fundamental universal processes, while World Trees maintain the living supernatural foundations of planetary worlds. Gaia belonged to the Divine classification rather than either of those separate states of existence.[8]

#### Ouranos and the First Divine Generations
Gaia's relationship with [[Ouranos]] produced a large portion of the ancient Greek Divine family. Their descendants included the original [[Titans]], [[Cyclopes]], and Hecatonchires, whose exceptional supernatural qualities originated from the union of two Original [[Primordial Gods]]. Several of their children consequently possessed the extraordinary foundations associated with Second Generation Primordial Gods, establishing a Divine hierarchy that differed considerably from the later generations of Olympians.[4]
Among their most important descendants was Kronus, whose eventual rebellion transformed the political structure of the Pantheon. Ouranos had grown increasingly hostile toward children he considered unacceptable and imprisoned several of them rather than allowing them to exist freely. Gaia opposed this treatment and became involved in the rebellion that ultimately removed Ouranos from power. Her participation was crucial because Kronus, despite his exceptional abilities, belonged to a younger Divine generation and could not otherwise be expected to defeat an Original Primordial God through ordinary personal strength.[4]
The rebellion created a precedent that continued influencing Greek history for generations. Gaia had supported a younger ruler against a father she regarded as abusive, establishing the belief that the existing political order could be overturned when its ruler failed to protect the family. However, the consequences of Kronus' victory soon demonstrated that replacing one ruler did not necessarily eliminate the behavior responsible for the original conflict.

#### The Reign of Kronus
Kronus became one of the most important rulers of the early [[Greek Pantheon]] after overcoming [[Ouranos]]. His position as a powerful Second Generation Primordial God allowed the [[Titans]] to establish their own Divine order, but the new system eventually developed many of the same oppressive characteristics that had caused Gaia to turn against the previous generation.[4]
Gaia's expectations were repeatedly disappointed as members of her family attempted to consolidate power through violence, imprisonment, and the suppression of potential rivals. Her opposition was complicated by the fact that each new conflict involved her own descendants, forcing her to choose between children whose competing ambitions threatened to destroy the family from within. This recurring cycle became one of the defining characteristics of the Greek Pantheon's history.

#### Titanomachy and the Rise of Olympus
The conflict between the [[Titans]] and the generation led by [[Zeus]] became another major turning point in Gaia's history. The younger Olympian Gods eventually overthrew the existing Titan order, establishing a political system that displaced many of the older Divine families. The victory did not end the internal conflicts of the Pantheon, however, because the Olympians repeated the practice of imprisoning or eliminating relatives whom they considered dangerous.[7][4]
Gaia's dissatisfaction with Zeus gradually developed into intense resentment. She had already experienced the betrayal of her expectations under [[Ouranos]] and Kronus, and the Olympian regime appeared to be reproducing the same problems. The imprisonment and destruction of her descendants convinced Gaia that the new rulers had abandoned the responsibilities associated with their authority, turning her maternal protectiveness into another campaign of revenge.
Her relationship with Kronus also remained hostile despite their earlier cooperation. Although Gaia's immediate hatred was increasingly directed toward Zeus, Kronus understood that she had not forgiven him for the actions committed during his own reign. The Titan King recognized that even a successful defeat of [[Olympus]] would not guarantee his safety from Gaia's subsequent retaliation.[7]

#### The Creation of Typhon
Gaia's relationship with [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] became central to her attempts to destroy the Olympian regime. Their union produced powerful offspring possessing a more chaotic nature than many of the beings associated with Gaia's earlier relationship with [[Ouranos]]. The most dangerous of these creations was [[Typhon]], whose existence was connected to the Concept of the End and whose power threatened the stability of the Olympian order.[7]
Typhon was created as an extraordinary destructive force capable of challenging [[Zeus]] and the Gods supporting him. Unlike ordinary Divine offspring who possessed their own political ambitions, Typhon's nature was closely connected to destruction, making him particularly dangerous to a Pantheon already weakened by internal conflict. His emergence transformed the developing Greek civil war into a much greater crisis because the opposing Divine factions could no longer assume that victory over their political enemies would preserve the Pantheon itself.[7]
Gaia's relationship with Typhon reflected both her extraordinary capacity for creating powerful beings and the consequences of allowing revenge to govern her decisions. The creature possessed sufficient destructive potential to threaten enemies she could not easily defeat directly, but its existence also endangered the wider Divine family whose survival had originally motivated her actions.

#### Tartarus and the Later Primordial Offspring
Gaia and [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] continued producing powerful children after the earlier conflicts had devastated the [[Greek Pantheon]]. Their descendants possessed characteristics different from those of the original Hecatonchires and other beings produced through Gaia's relationship with [[Ouranos]]. The influence of Tartarus introduced a considerably more chaotic nature into these later generations, making them useful as weapons against the factions that had emerged victorious from the previous wars.[5]
During the final conflict surrounding [[Olympus]], Tartarus introduced five new Hecatonchires whose supernatural characteristics differed from those of the older beings of the same classification. [[Ares]] recognized the lingering Earthen influence associated with Gaia but also perceived that their paternal foundation originated from Tartarus rather than Ouranos. The appearance of these creatures demonstrated that Gaia's activities had continued beyond the older conflicts and that her partnership with Tartarus remained capable of producing powerful forces threatening the established Divine order.[5]
The continued creation of these offspring also reinforced Gaia's preference for indirect conflict. Rather than personally confront every ruler she wished to overthrow, she contributed to the production of increasingly powerful descendants capable of carrying out her revenge.

#### Conflict with the Olympians
Gaia's resentment toward the Olympian generation remained one of the principal causes of instability within the [[Greek Pantheon]]. Her opposition to [[Zeus]] and the younger Gods was rooted in their treatment of her children, particularly those who had been imprisoned or eliminated during the struggles for political control.[7]
The return of [[Typhon]] placed the Olympians in immediate danger, forcing their civil war against the [[Titans]] into a secondary position while they attempted to respond to a creature whose destructive potential threatened both sides. Kronus recognized that Gaia's hatred was not restricted to Zeus and that the Titans could not assume she would become their ally after the fall of [[Olympus]]. The wider Divine family had become trapped within a cycle of vengeance in which each generation's victory merely prepared the conditions for another rebellion.[7]
Gaia nevertheless retained an attachment to the Greek Pantheon itself. As one of its original Primordial Goddesses, she understood that the complete destruction of its Divine foundations could expose the surviving members to intervention from other Pantheons. Her desire for revenge therefore existed alongside an awareness that eliminating every powerful Greek God would ultimately endanger the family and supernatural civilization she had helped create.

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Gaia's influence upon [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] began before the two personally encountered one another. During the examination of Victor's increasingly unusual supernatural characteristics, it was revealed that he had received Blessings from several extraordinarily powerful beings whose attention he had attracted through his actions. Gaia was among these benefactors, alongside Nyx, Albedo Moriarty, the ancestral Spirits of animals, and other supernatural powers whose relationship with Victor was not yet fully understood.[15][16][17]
The Blessing demonstrated that Gaia had taken an interest in Victor despite their lack of direct contact. As a Primordial Goddess whose existence was associated with the natural world, she possessed the authority to provide supernatural assistance to individuals who attracted her attention. Victor remained cautious about relying upon powers that originated from external benefactors, recognizing that Divine Blessings could be withdrawn by their original owners and therefore should not replace the continued development of his personal abilities.[16]
Meanwhile, the [[Greek Pantheon]] descended into another period of destructive civil conflict. [[Zeus]] and his descendants opposed the surviving [[Titans]], while the appearance of [[Typhon]] threatened to transform the struggle into a disaster that neither political faction could control. The destructive Being's connection to Gaia and [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] demonstrated that the original Primordial generation remained capable of influencing events despite the political dominance of the younger Olympians.[7]
Typhon's presence forced the opposing Gods to reconsider their priorities. The Titans understood that Gaia's ultimate objective involved revenge against those who had betrayed her expectations, including members of their own faction. Although Zeus had become the most immediate target, the destruction of [[Olympus]] would not necessarily end Gaia's hostility toward Kronus and the surviving Titans. The conflict consequently developed into a struggle in which the original Primordial family's grievances threatened to destroy the political structures established by their descendants.[7]

#### King Among Kings
Gaia's direct involvement in the destruction of the old [[Greek Pantheon]] intensified during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s confrontation with its most powerful Divine factions. The conflict escalated dramatically after Victor began fighting against [[Primordial Gods]] such as [[Erebus]] and [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]], while additional forces associated with [[Olympus]], the [[Titans]], and the [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] became involved.[5]
The intensity of the battle threatened the surrounding environment. Victor noticed that the terrain was being sustained by a powerful influence that prevented the repeated impacts from producing the degree of destruction ordinarily expected from attacks involving such extraordinary Energy. The source of this protection was Gaia, whose authority over Earth and Nature allowed her to continuously reinforce the surrounding land and maintain the battlefield despite attacks from beings whose power could threaten the foundations of the Pantheon.[18]
Her intervention demonstrated that Gaia possessed the power to influence the physical environment on an enormous scale even while remaining outside the immediate center of combat. Rather than engage directly, she used her Divine authority to preserve the terrain and indirectly support the factions she wished to protect.
Gaia eventually appeared personally when Tartarus suffered catastrophic injuries. As [[Scathach]] approached the remains of the Primordial God, Gaia intercepted her and attempted to prevent her from retrieving the body. She presented herself as peaceful and expressed a desire to avoid fighting, but secretly placed a supernatural mark upon Scathach's Soul during their brief physical contact. Victor immediately detected the mark through his Draconic perception and warned his wife, preventing Gaia from exploiting the concealed influence.[1]
With deception no longer effective, Gaia employed her authority over Nature to defend herself. Vines and plants emerged from the barren terrain of the Greek Underworld, introducing vegetation into an environment where ordinary plant life had not previously existed. Scathach destroyed the approaching growth through her martial techniques, forcing Gaia to rely upon larger terrestrial constructions to obstruct her opponent.[1]
Gaia subsequently created mountains and reinforced them with her Divine authority. Her defenses were sufficiently powerful that one of Scathach's specialized spear attacks penetrated a mountain without passing completely through it, demonstrating how the Mother Goddess could strengthen an ordinary terrestrial formation into an extraordinarily durable obstacle. Nevertheless, Gaia lacked the direct combat experience necessary to confidently confront Scathach's refined martial techniques, making her dependent upon the environment and indirect methods of attack.[1]
The confrontation changed when Victor intervened. Enraged by Gaia's attempt to manipulate Scathach, he struck the Primordial Goddess in the face and sent her crashing into the ground. Gaia was infuriated by the humiliation and released her Divine power, causing the entire surrounding landscape to behave like a violent sea. Mountains rose and collapsed while the ground shifted in response to her anger, destabilizing the battlefield as she attempted to recover Tartarus' remains.[1]
Victor continued provoking Gaia while pursuing the opportunity to strengthen himself by consuming Tartarus. Gaia's anger eventually overcame her usual preference for indirect combat, causing her to attempt a direct punch against Victor. The attack successfully struck him, but Victor used the resulting movement to approach Tartarus and consume the Primordial God's remains before Gaia could recover them.[1]
The destruction of Tartarus represented a catastrophic personal loss for Gaia. Not only had she lost an extraordinarily powerful Primordial relative, but the individual who had become her partner in producing the later generations of anti-Olympian creatures had been consumed by an enemy she could not realistically defeat. Her rage contributed to the arrival of [[Typhon]], whose destructive power was intended to threaten the surviving opposition.[1]
The balance of the conflict had nevertheless shifted too far in Victor's favor. His transformation into the Primogenitor of the [[Blood Dragons]] established a new level of power within the battlefield, while his subsequent actions demonstrated that Typhon could no longer be relied upon to overturn the outcome. Gaia recognized that the collapse of the Greek Primordial faction had become inevitable and attempted to escape rather than continue a hopeless confrontation.[9]
Victor intercepted her before she could leave. His newly developed Draconic instincts reacted strongly to Nature-related beings, and he regarded Gaia's immense terrestrial affinity as especially valuable. He claimed her and transferred her directly into his Personal Universe, ending her independent participation in the Greek conflict and beginning a new period of her existence under the authority of the Dragon Primogenitor.[9]
Inside Victor's spiritual world, Gaia encountered [[Roxanne Elderblood]], the World Tree whose existence had already become integrated with the Dragon's Soul. The internal environment possessed enormous natural regions and was connected to the extraordinary supernatural structure of Victor's evolving existence. Gaia's presence introduced additional terrestrial and ecological characteristics that complemented Roxanne's forests and allowed the natural environment to develop more completely.[9]
Victor assigned Gaia to assist Roxanne rather than allowing her to remain inactive. The arrangement transformed her from a defeated opponent into a useful contributor to the development of his Personal Universe, providing her with responsibilities closely connected to the natural world she governed. Although Gaia initially remained hostile, the circumstances offered her a purpose beyond the repeated political conflicts of her original Pantheon.[9]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Gaia became increasingly important to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction as he began developing the extraordinary project that would lead to the creation of an independent planetary home for his family. Victor intended to establish a new world and Pantheon rather than remain permanently dependent upon the territories of [[Nightingale]] or other existing supernatural civilizations. Achieving this objective required cooperation between beings possessing different forms of Divine and supernatural authority, including Gaia, Nyx, Natalia, Jeanne, Roxanne, and Victor himself.[19][10]
Before the full project was undertaken, Victor had already demonstrated an ability to reproduce aspects of planetary construction through his interaction with Creation. He could manipulate existing matter and environmental characteristics to imitate the work ordinarily associated with Creation Gods, although he acknowledged that the result could not achieve the same perfection without appropriate support. Gaia's authority was particularly important because she possessed the terrestrial understanding required to establish natural environments on a functional world.[20]
When Victor began constructing the new planet, Gaia was given responsibility for manipulating the terrestrial material necessary to establish its physical environment. Working alongside Victor and the other Divine specialists, she helped gather and arrange landmasses and develop the foundations upon which the new world would be formed. Her Earth Divinity allowed her to shape immense quantities of matter and participate in processes that ordinarily required the intervention of exceptionally powerful Creation-aligned Gods.[10]
Gaia also demonstrated an important limitation of her authority. Although she could manipulate the newly gathered terrain, she warned Victor that the result could not be perfectly controlled because she lacked an established Divine connection to the planet being constructed. Her terrestrial authority remained extraordinarily powerful, but its effectiveness depended partly upon the relationship between herself and the world upon which she was acting. She was not a World Tree automatically bound to every planet, and her Divine influence therefore differed from the deeper planetary integration possessed by Roxanne.[10]
The construction process involved more than the gathering of rock and soil. Gaia participated in creating the conditions necessary for a functional planetary environment, while Nyx, Natalia, Jeanne, Roxanne, and Victor contributed their respective abilities to the manipulation of gravity, Time, primordial Energy, natural development, and the establishment of the planet's supernatural foundation. The project's complexity required coordinated intervention rather than the independent efforts of any single Goddess.[10][21]
Gaia played a particularly important role when Victor prepared to establish the planet's core. She had created an opening extending into the world's interior through which Victor could channel his Dragon Fire, using the extraordinarily powerful Energy to establish a new planetary foundation. Gaia recognized that such a core would enrich the world with rare supernatural materials, although she also warned that the resulting resources could attract the greed of other Gods. Victor nevertheless proceeded, and Gaia subsequently sealed the opening and stabilized the surrounding planetary material.[21]
Once the principal structure had been completed, the developing world was subjected to the deeper processes required for a functioning supernatural planet. [[The Universal Tree]] later examined Victor's achievement and acknowledged that Gaia would be sufficient to assist with the maintenance of the new world's natural environment alongside Victor and Roxanne, although the arrangement would require further development as the planet and its population grew.[22]
Gaia personally enjoyed the opportunity to create an environment without the scars of existing civilization. The new world's pristine landscapes and enormous natural regions reminded her of the beauty that could exist before cities, industry, and other forms of development altered the environment. Her pleasure also reflected the freedom to exercise her Divinity constructively rather than through the destructive wars that had characterized her former existence in the [[Greek Pantheon]].[10]
During the exploration of the newly established world, Gaia participated in discussions concerning its future population. She reminded Victor's family that the Balance ordinarily made reproduction increasingly difficult for exceptionally powerful beings, particularly those whose existence had developed beyond ordinary mortal limitations. This limitation presented a practical problem for a civilization intended to be inhabited by [[True Dragons]], whose extraordinary individual strength was accompanied by the corresponding difficulties of biological reproduction.[11]
Gaia's long-standing habit of using vegetation to observe events also became a source of discussion among the women. When she demonstrated knowledge of private matters involving Victor's relationships, the others recognized that her connection to the natural environment could allow her to gather information through plants and other forms of vegetation. The ability explained why [[Zeus]] had historically avoided allowing plants to grow within [[Olympus|Mount Olympus]], fearing that Gaia would use them as a means of surveillance.[11]
Gaia later joined the women who underwent Victor's transformation into the Blood Dragon Race. She was among the exceptionally powerful Primordial Goddesses whose existing Divine characteristics made the process more complex than the conversion of ordinary supernatural beings. The transformation therefore involved preserving her essential Divine identity while reconstructing her physical and spiritual foundation into a genuine True Dragon existence.[2][3]
Gaia initially acknowledged that her previous captivity had made escape from Victor unrealistic, but she no longer felt the same hostility toward her new circumstances. Life within his faction had become comfortable, she had ceased devoting herself to revenge against her treacherous descendants, and her attraction toward Victor had become increasingly apparent. Rather than resist the opportunity to evolve, she accepted the transformation and became one of the new generation of Primordial Dragon Goddesses.[2]
The process reconstructed her body and Soul while preserving the essential qualities responsible for her identity. Unlike an ordinary Dragon whose development began through a comparatively simple racial transformation, Gaia possessed ancient Divinities accumulated across eons, making it necessary to integrate those powers into a new Draconic foundation. The resulting existence combined the characteristics of a First Generation Primordial Goddess with the superior racial potential of a True Blood Dragon.[2][3]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Following her transformation, Gaia became an established member of the Elderblood family rather than a defeated Goddess held within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Personal Universe. Her relationship with Victor developed into a marriage, while the disappearance of the old [[Greek Pantheon]]'s political obligations allowed her to concentrate increasingly upon her new family and the development of the worlds under Imperial control.[2][23]
Gaia eventually became pregnant with Victor's child. The extraordinary nature of the Blood Dragon lineage and the accelerated environments used by the family meant that pregnancies involving powerful members of the household could develop over periods far longer than ordinary Human gestation. Gaia remained pregnant for approximately 1,900 subjective years, reflecting the enormous difference between conventional biological development and the circumstances governing the offspring of the Dragon God of [[Chaos]].[24]
Their daughter, [[Gina Elderblood]], inherited extraordinary potential from both parents. As the child of a Primordial Dragon Goddess and the Dragon God of Chaos, Gina possessed an exceptionally powerful supernatural foundation even when compared with other members of Victor's rapidly expanding family. Her birth established a new generation descending directly from Gaia's ancient Divine lineage and Victor's Blood Dragon Race, combining the supernatural characteristics of a Mother Goddess with those of an extraordinary Dragon progenitor.[6]
Gina developed rapidly after her birth and became recognizable for her intense personality, exceptional natural abilities, and powerful connection to biological life. Although she was considerably younger than several of her sisters, her inherited potential allowed her to develop at a remarkable rate, and her presence became especially intimidating to individuals unfamiliar with the extraordinary strength of the Imperial Princesses.[6]
The question of Gaia's true relationship with the Earth was also addressed during discussions involving Victor's daughters. Although Human mythology commonly described Gaia as the literal planet, the family recognized that she was an Earth Goddess and Mother Goddess rather than a World Tree. Roxanne and Amara possessed the much more direct planetary connection that made their existences fundamentally different from Gaia's, despite the overlap between their natural and life-related characteristics.[8]
Gaia's relationship with motherhood also developed through her participation in the upbringing of Victor's children. Her previous experience with the Greek Pantheon had been characterized by excessive freedom granted to powerful descendants, many of whom eventually became arrogant and irresponsible. Victor recognized the danger of allowing similar behavior to develop among the Imperial Princesses and confronted Gaia and Nyx over their permissive approach to parenting. The criticism caused both Goddesses to reconsider their responsibilities and attempt to provide more active guidance to their daughters.[12]
The change demonstrated that Gaia's ancient experience did not automatically make her an ideal parent. Her position as the Great Mother had given her extraordinary power and a vast number of descendants, but she had not always exercised the discipline necessary to prevent her children from abusing their privileges. Life within the Elderblood household required her to accept a different understanding of motherhood, one based not merely upon affection and protection but also upon accountability and the development of responsible individuals.[12]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
By the mature Imperial Era, Gaia had become one of the ancient Divine specialists associated with the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s expanding civilization. Her titles and Divinities remained relevant to a society whose supernatural structure included the maintenance of worlds, the development of new environments, and the creation of conditions capable of supporting enormous populations. However, the Empire's organized programs involving fertility, reproduction, and population growth were primarily administered through [[Aphrodite]] and the Divine institutions responsible for those functions rather than being exclusively governed by Gaia.[25]
The Imperial system also reflected a deliberate philosophy concerning the continuation of Life and the dangers associated with unrestricted immortality. Although the Empire possessed numerous methods of extending life, its leaders understood that a civilization composed almost entirely of immortal individuals could eventually suffer from stagnation, boredom, and destructive personal ambitions. Gaia's experience with the original [[Greek Pantheon]] provided a clear historical example of how ancient Gods could become irresponsible when their power and longevity were not accompanied by meaningful obligations.[25]
Gaia later received an unexpected gift when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] decided to preserve important aspects of Greek culture within the Empire. Although he had little affection for the political failures of [[Olympus]], he recognized that several of his wives retained sentimental attachments to the architecture, temples, and cultural traditions associated with their original homeland. He consequently provided an enormous planet, eventually made slightly larger than the old Earth, where the Greek Goddesses could establish their own temples and recreate the elements of their civilization that they wished to preserve.[26]
Gaia and Nyx initially behaved as though the gift held little importance to them, remaining outwardly indifferent while the other Greek women expressed their excitement. However, as soon as the planet became available, both Goddesses approached Victor, thanked him affectionately, and immediately departed to establish their temples. Their reaction demonstrated that Gaia retained emotional connections to her ancient homeland despite having abandoned the resentment and political conflicts that once defined her relationship with the Greek Pantheon.[26]
Gaia continued participating in the activities of the Imperial Family as Victor's civilization expanded. During a gathering involving the Emperor and several of his wives, she arrived alongside [[Persephone]], Morgana, Lilith, and Lily while the other women discussed another of the family's customary disturbances. Gaia observed that the Elderblood household at least lacked the destructive family conflicts that had plagued her former Pantheon, a sentiment shared by Aphrodite and Persephone. Their reaction reflected the extraordinary difference between the unstable political structure of Olympus and the comparative unity maintained within Victor's current household.[13]
Her role as a mother also remained significant in the later Imperial Era. Gina continued developing into an extraordinarily powerful Dragon Goddess whose behavior combined great natural talent with the intense appetites and competitiveness common among Victor's daughters. During a family gathering, Gina became fascinated by the remains of a Star Beast brought for a feast, displaying an enthusiasm that caused one of her sisters to question how she could be Gaia's daughter when their personalities appeared so different. Gaia reacted with embarrassment, privately recognizing that Gina's behavior resembled aspects of her own earlier personality more closely than she wished to admit.[27]
The growing number of Imperial descendants also required Gaia and the other mothers to consider the distribution of Dragon lineage among their children. The strongest classification of True Dragon was generally reserved for members of the Emperor's immediate family, while certain other descendants could receive lower forms of the Draconic lineage. Gaia, Persephone, Aphrodite, and Jeanne were among the mothers who exercised control over such inheritance, preventing some of their children from becoming [[True Dragons]] while allowing them to receive less powerful Dragon characteristics. The distinction preserved the extraordinary position of the Imperial True Dragons while still allowing other descendants to develop far beyond the limitations of ordinary supernatural races.[28]
Gaia's continued development therefore differs considerably from her original role as an ancient Greek Mother Goddess. Rather than producing ever more powerful offspring to overthrow rival Divine rulers, she now participates in a family whose principal responsibilities involve the upbringing of future generations, the preservation of worlds, and the management of a civilization extending across enormous regions of space. Her ancient Divinities remain part of her identity, but her responsibilities are now connected to the continuing development of the Elderblood Empire rather than the restoration of Olympus.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Gaia's current husband, the father of her daughter [[Gina Elderblood]], and the individual responsible for transforming her original Divine existence into that of a Primordial Dragon Goddess. Their relationship began through extreme hostility during the destruction of the [[Greek Pantheon]], when Gaia attempted to protect [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] and manipulate [[Scathach]] through a concealed Soul mark. Victor discovered the attempt and confronted Gaia with overwhelming anger, eventually striking her and preventing her from recovering Tartarus' remains.[1]
Following the collapse of the Greek Primordial faction, Victor captured Gaia and transported her into his Personal Universe. The initial arrangement was not a voluntary alliance, and Gaia's position within his internal world placed her under the authority of both Victor and Roxanne. Nevertheless, the environment allowed her to exercise her Divinity constructively by developing natural systems rather than continuing the cycle of revenge that had dominated much of her previous life.[9]
Their relationship gradually changed as Gaia recognized the stability and opportunities available within Victor's faction. She no longer needed to involve herself in the destructive political conflicts of her descendants, and her growing involvement in the construction of new environments allowed her to concentrate upon activities closely connected to her own preferences. Victor's immense power also provided protection against enemies who had previously threatened her position within the Greek Pantheon.[9][10]
Gaia eventually became attracted to Victor despite their earlier hostility, and the relationship developed into a marriage after she accepted her place within the Elderblood family. Victor subsequently transformed her into a True Blood Dragon while preserving her Primordial Divine characteristics, allowing her to acquire a stronger racial foundation without abandoning her original identity.[2][3]
Their daughter Gina represents the continuation of both their supernatural lineages. Gaia's ancient relationship with Life, Nature, and Motherhood combined with Victor's Draconic and Divine characteristics to produce a child possessing exceptional natural potential. Their shared parental responsibilities also forced Gaia to reconsider aspects of her previous approach to raising children, particularly after Victor criticized the excessive freedom she and Nyx had granted their descendants.[6][12]

#### Gina Elderblood
[[Gina Elderblood]] is Gaia's biological daughter with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and one of the Imperial Princesses of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Born from a Primordial Dragon Goddess and the Dragon God of [[Chaos]], Gina possesses extraordinary inherited potential and a powerful relationship with Life and biological development. Her supernatural foundation is exceptional even among the children of Victor, whose individual abilities frequently exceed the limits ordinarily associated with their races.[6]
Gina's personality differs considerably from Gaia's current restrained and calculating behavior. She is energetic, competitive, and particularly enthusiastic about unusual living beings and the prospect of extraordinary experiences. Her behavior occasionally embarrasses Gaia, although the ancient Goddess recognizes that her daughter possesses characteristics resembling her own earlier personality.[27]
Their relationship also reflects the changing standards of motherhood within the Elderblood household. Gaia's previous permissive approach to parenting had allowed many of her Greek descendants to develop excessive pride and irresponsible behavior. Following Victor's criticism, she became more conscious of the importance of actively guiding her children rather than assuming that their extraordinary natural abilities would be sufficient for their development.[12]

#### Nyx Elderblood
Nyx Elderblood is one of Gaia's original Primordial siblings and currently belongs to the same Elderblood family. Their shared origins connect them to the earliest Divine generation of the [[Greek Pantheon]], although their respective Divinities and personalities differ considerably. Gaia governs Earth and Nature, while Nyx possesses authority associated with Night and several related supernatural aspects.[16][1]
Their original relationship was influenced by Gaia's position as the eldest Goddess, whose anger and influence could intimidate even other powerful Primordials. During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s confrontation with Gaia, the other Greek Gods recognized how unusual it was for anyone to provoke her so openly, with Nyx herself having become considerably more cautious about confronting her elder sister.[1]
Following their integration into Victor's faction, Gaia and Nyx became members of the same family rather than representatives of rival political interests. Both contributed to the creation of Dragon's Nest through their respective Divinities, and both later underwent the transformation into True [[Blood Dragons]]. Their different forms of perception also became a source of humor among the other wives, who recognized that Gaia could observe through Nature while Nyx possessed extraordinary means of concealment and surveillance.[10][11][2]
Gaia and Nyx were also criticized together by Victor for their previous approach to raising children. Both women had historically allowed their descendants considerable freedom without providing enough discipline, contributing to the irresponsible behavior associated with the old Greek Pantheon. The experience prompted both Goddesses to reconsider their responsibilities within the modern Elderblood family.[12]

#### Ouranos
[[Ouranos]] was Gaia's former husband and one of the original [[Primordial Gods]] of the [[Greek Pantheon]]. Together, they produced numerous Divine offspring whose inherited characteristics established several of the earliest Greek supernatural lineages, including the [[Titans]], [[Cyclopes]], and Hecatonchires.[4]
Their relationship deteriorated when Ouranos began imprisoning children whom he considered unacceptable or dangerous. Gaia opposed his treatment of their offspring and assisted Kronus in overthrowing him, beginning the pattern of familial rebellion that would continue affecting the Pantheon across several Divine generations.[4]
The conflict demonstrated that Gaia's maternal protectiveness could overcome even her attachment to an original Primordial partner. However, her involvement in the rebellion did not create lasting peace, as subsequent rulers repeated many of the practices that had caused her to oppose Ouranos in the first place.

#### Kronus
Kronus was one of Gaia's most important sons and a powerful Second Generation Primordial God. Gaia supported his rebellion against [[Ouranos]], contributing to the removal of the earlier ruler and the establishment of the Titan regime.[4]
Their relationship eventually became hostile because Kronus continued many of the abuses Gaia had opposed. Rather than protect the younger generations of the Divine family, he became another ruler whose actions threatened the people Gaia considered her children. Her disappointment contributed to the continuing cycle of rebellion that eventually brought [[Zeus]] and the Olympians to power.[7][4]
Kronus remained aware of Gaia's resentment during the later Greek civil war. He understood that her immediate hatred of Zeus did not mean she had forgiven him and that the destruction of [[Olympus]] might simply cause Gaia to turn her attention toward the surviving [[Titans]] afterward.[7]

#### Zeus
[[Zeus]] was one of Gaia's most important descendants and the principal ruler of the Olympian generation. His rise represented another transition in the [[Greek Pantheon]]'s political structure, replacing the regime established by Kronus with a new order centered upon the Olympians.[7]
Gaia's resentment toward Zeus developed through his treatment of the older Divine families and the imprisonment of powerful beings who had opposed the Olympian regime. She regarded his actions as another betrayal of the expectations that had motivated her support for previous rebellions, eventually contributing to her decision to create [[Typhon]] as a destructive force capable of overthrowing him.[7]
Zeus was also aware of Gaia's capacity for indirect observation. His reluctance to permit plants within [[Olympus|Mount Olympus]] reflected the fear that Gaia could exploit her relationship with vegetation to overhear private conversations and gain information concerning the activities of the Olympian Gods.[11]

#### Tartarus
[[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] was one of Gaia's original Primordial siblings, her former lover, and an important partner in the creation of powerful offspring intended to oppose [[Olympus]]. Their relationship produced beings possessing a more chaotic supernatural nature than many of the children Gaia had previously created with [[Ouranos]].[7][5]
Tartarus became particularly important during the final conflict surrounding the [[Greek Pantheon]]. When he suffered catastrophic injuries during the confrontation against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Gaia attempted to recover and conceal his remains rather than allow the enemy to consume him. Her intervention exposed her to [[Scathach]] and Victor, initiating the direct confrontation that ultimately led to Gaia's defeat.[1]
The loss of Tartarus caused genuine anger and grief. Gaia's reaction demonstrated that her relationships with the original Primordials were not exclusively political, even though she frequently manipulated or opposed members of her family when their actions conflicted with her own objectives.[1]

#### Typhon
[[Typhon]] is one of Gaia's most dangerous children and a product of her relationship with [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]]. His existence is closely connected to destructive supernatural characteristics associated with the End, making him an extraordinary threat to the Olympian Gods and the Pantheon's wider political structure.[7]
Gaia created Typhon as part of her effort to retaliate against [[Zeus]] and the Olympian generation. Rather than confront the entire Olympian faction directly, she relied upon the destructive power of a child whose existence had been designed to threaten their rule.[7]
During the final conflict against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Gaia summoned Typhon after witnessing the destruction of Tartarus and the humiliation inflicted upon her. However, Victor's new Draconic power had already altered the balance of the battle, and Gaia recognized that even Typhon could no longer guarantee her survival. She ultimately attempted to escape rather than remain on the battlefield until both mother and son were destroyed.[1][9]

#### Roxanne Elderblood
[[Roxanne Elderblood]] became Gaia's principal counterpart within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Personal Universe after the Mother Goddess was captured. Both possess extraordinary connections to Nature, but their underlying existences are fundamentally different. Gaia is an Original Primordial Goddess whose Divinities govern aspects of Earth and Nature, while Roxanne is a genuine World Tree with a direct connection to the supernatural foundations of planetary life.[9][8]
Victor assigned Gaia to assist Roxanne in managing and developing his internal environment. Roxanne had already established enormous forests and other forms of vegetation, while Gaia's Earth and Nature Divinities contributed additional terrestrial characteristics necessary for a complete ecosystem. Their cooperation allowed Victor's Personal Universe to develop more efficiently than would have been possible through a single form of natural authority.[9]
Their relationship also reflects the distinction between a Goddess who possesses authority over Nature and a World Tree whose existence is structurally connected to a planet. Gaia's immense power can alter environments on an extraordinary scale, but Roxanne's nature provides a more direct connection to the processes necessary for sustaining the supernatural structure of a living world.[22][8]

#### Aphrodite Elderblood
[[Aphrodite]] Elderblood is a member of the Greek Divine family who later became one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wives. Their original relationship was influenced by the broader genealogy of the [[Greek Pantheon]], including the fact that Aphrodite's existence originated through circumstances connected with [[Ouranos]].[14]
Both Goddesses eventually became members of the Elderblood family and contributed to its expanding civilization, although their principal Divine responsibilities differ considerably. Aphrodite became especially important to the Empire's systems involving Love, reproduction, and population development, while Gaia's ancient nature remained associated with Earth, terrestrial environments, Life, and Motherhood.[25]
Their later conversations reveal a shared awareness of the destructive family conflicts that once characterized [[Olympus]]. Although the former Greek Goddesses retain different personalities and occasionally disagree, their place within Victor's household provides a degree of stability absent from the political system in which they originally lived.[13]

#### Persephone Elderblood
[[Persephone Elderblood]] is one of Gaia's Divine descendants and a current member of the Elderblood family. Their relationship originated within the complex genealogy of the [[Greek Pantheon]], where [[Persephone]] occupied an important position associated with the [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] and the generation of Gods descended from Gaia's original Divine family.[5]
Both women eventually joined [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household and became True [[Blood Dragons]], allowing their former family connection to continue within an entirely different supernatural civilization. Gaia, [[Aphrodite]], and Persephone occasionally reflect upon the destructive history of the Greek Pantheon, recognizing that their present family has avoided many of the conflicts that once made [[Olympus]] unstable.[2][13]

#### Hestia Elderblood
[[Hestia]] Elderblood is one of Gaia's Divine descendants and a member of the Elderblood family. Their relationship reflects the ancient Greek genealogy through which later Olympian Gods descended from the original Primordial generation.[4]
Hestia's position within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family is distinct from the violent political rivalries that characterized earlier generations of the [[Greek Pantheon]]. Both women eventually became members of the Dragon's Nest household and underwent the transformation into the True Blood Dragon Race, preserving their Divine characteristics while acquiring a common Draconic foundation.[2]

#### The Greek Pantheon
Gaia was one of the original foundations of the [[Greek Pantheon]] and the oldest Goddess associated with its Divine hierarchy. Her relationships with [[Ouranos]], [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]], Kronus, and the later Olympians established much of its genealogical and political history, while her repeated efforts to protect or avenge her descendants contributed to several of the conflicts that ultimately destroyed the old order.[7][4]
Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s victory over the Greek Primordial faction, Gaia ceased pursuing the restoration of [[Olympus]] as an independent political system. Her current responsibilities are connected to the [[Elderblood Empire]] rather than the former Divine hierarchy, although she retains an attachment to aspects of her original homeland and participated in the establishment of a new Greek cultural world within Victor's civilization.[26]

#### Elderblood Empire
Gaia's position within the [[Elderblood Empire]] differs fundamentally from her former status as the eldest Primordial Goddess of the [[Greek Pantheon]]. She no longer occupies the same political position above generations of Greek descendants, but instead belongs to a family whose authority is centered upon [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and whose supernatural responsibilities extend across numerous planets, civilizations, and Divine systems.[25]
Her Earth and Nature Divinities remain valuable to the maintenance and development of living environments, while her participation in Imperial family life provides her with responsibilities beyond the restoration of damaged worlds. Gaia's development as a mother, wife, and member of a much larger household illustrates how extensively her circumstances have changed since the final destruction of [[Olympus]].[12][13]

### Legacy
Gaia's legacy is inseparable from the history of the [[Greek Pantheon]]. As the first Goddess of its original Divine generation, she became the progenitor of numerous supernatural bloodlines and contributed directly to the formation of the [[Titans]], [[Cyclopes]], Hecatonchires, and later Divine families. Her immense age and position among the Protogenoi established her as one of the most important ancestral figures within the Greek supernatural world, while her Divinity of Earth and Nature gave her tremendous influence over the environmental foundations of the Pantheon.[4][1]
Her original legacy was nevertheless marked by repeated political failures. Gaia's efforts to protect her children frequently developed into campaigns of revenge that brought successive Divine generations into conflict with one another. The rebellions against [[Ouranos]], Kronus, and [[Zeus]] demonstrated that even the most powerful members of the original Pantheon could not guarantee lasting peace when their familial relationships were dominated by pride, resentment, and struggles for authority.[7][4]
The destruction of [[Olympus]] ended much of that history and forced Gaia to confront circumstances beyond the limitations of her previous Divine world. Her capture by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] initially represented the collapse of her independent influence, but it also provided an opportunity to abandon the cycle of conflict that had governed her existence for eons. Working alongside Roxanne, participating in the creation of Dragon's Nest, and becoming a True Blood Dragon allowed her ancient Earth and Nature Divinities to contribute to the development of new worlds rather than the destruction of old ones.[9][10][2]
Her marriage to Victor and the birth of Gina further transformed her legacy. Gaia became part of a new Divine family whose descendants inherited extraordinary Draconic and conceptual potential, while her earlier experiences with the Greek Pantheon served as lessons concerning the importance of responsible parenthood. Her position as the Great Mother therefore acquired a different meaning within the [[Elderblood Empire]], where preserving Life and guiding future generations became increasingly important responsibilities.[6][12]
Gaia remains one of the clearest examples of an ancient Primordial Goddess whose existence changed fundamentally after becoming part of Victor's faction. She retains her original Divine identity and the historical significance of her position among the Greek Protogenoi, but her current status as a Primordial Dragon Goddess allows her to participate in an entirely new supernatural order whose civilization, family structure, and cosmic responsibilities extend far beyond the Pantheon she originally helped create.

Gaia Elderblood is an Original First Generation Primordial Goddess, the eldest Goddess of the [[Greek Pantheon]], and a current Primordial Dragon Goddess belonging to the Blood Dragon Race. As one of the earliest Divine beings born within the Greek Pantheon, Gaia possesses an extraordinarily ancient supernatural foundation, multiple powerful Divinities, and immense authority over the terrestrial environment. Her principal Divine domains are Earth, Nature, Life, Motherhood, and Fertility, through which she can influence geological structures, generate and manipulate vegetation, sustain natural environments, and participate in the creation and development of entire planetary ecosystems.[1][2]
Unlike the majority of Gods whose supernatural abilities are primarily intended for direct confrontation, Gaia specializes in large-scale environmental manipulation, ecological development, terrestrial reinforcement, and indirect intervention. Her Divine authority is capable of affecting an entire battlefield, withstanding the destructive consequences of battles between extraordinarily powerful Divine beings, and shaping the environments of developing worlds. She also possesses considerable knowledge concerning biological life and reproduction, reflecting her identity as one of the original Mother Goddesses responsible for producing numerous generations of supernatural beings.[3][4][5]
Despite her extraordinary power, Gaia is not primarily a martial combatant. Her experience in direct combat is considerably more limited than that of her siblings [[Erebus]], [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]], and other [[Primordial Gods]] accustomed to confronting enemies personally. Gaia historically preferred influencing the environment, manipulating political circumstances, and creating or supporting powerful descendants who could accomplish her objectives. Her immense Divine rank consequently reflects her authority and the quality of her existence rather than a level of swordsmanship, hand-to-hand combat, or tactical fighting experience comparable to dedicated warriors such as [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]].[1]
Following her transformation through [[Victor Elderblood]], Gaia acquired the physiological characteristics of a genuine True Dragon while retaining the memories, consciousness, Divine Concepts, and supernatural qualities accumulated during her original existence. She is consequently a Primordial Dragon Goddess, combining the ancient Divinities of an Original Primordial Goddess with the physical constitution and developmental potential of the Blood Dragon Race. Her transformation significantly strengthened her racial foundation without replacing her connection to Earth, Nature, and Life.[6][7]

#### Racial Physiology

Gaia's current body possesses the supernatural foundation of both an Original Primordial Goddess and a True Blood Dragon. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s transformation reconstructed the physical and spiritual characteristics of the Goddesses while preserving the essential portions of their existence responsible for their individual identities. Gaia therefore retained her Divine nature rather than becoming an ordinary Dragon whose former powers had been discarded. The resulting combination grants her the advantages of an extraordinarily ancient Divine Soul, multiple established Divinities, and a superior Draconic body.[6][7]
- Blood Dragon Physiology: Gaia is a member of Victor's True Blood Dragon Race, possessing the supernatural characteristics inherited through the Dragon Primogenitor's lineage. Her original Divine nature was incorporated into this new racial foundation rather than erased.[6]
- True Dragon Physiology: Gaia possesses the physical and supernatural characteristics of a genuine True Dragon, including an exceptionally powerful body, superior Energy compatibility, an enhanced Soul, and the ability to develop beyond the restrictions associated with her former Divine physiology.[6][7]
- Primordial Dragon God Physiology: Gaia's current existence combines True Dragon characteristics with the ancient Divine foundation of a First Generation Primordial Goddess. Her status as a Primordial Dragon Goddess reflects this combination of racial and Divine qualities.[6][8]

Her Draconic transformation supplies a considerably more powerful physical foundation than the one she possessed as an ordinary Goddess.[6]
- Immense Strength: Gaia's True Dragon body possesses extraordinary physical power. However, her individual maximum lifting strength and striking power have not been separately established through a detailed combat demonstration following her transformation.[6]
- Immense Speed: Gaia possesses the enhanced speed characteristic of [[True Dragons]]. Her previous Divine existence also allowed her to move through a battlefield involving powerful [[Primordial Gods]], although her fighting style relied primarily upon Divine authority rather than speed-based martial combat.[1][6]
- Enhanced Reflexes: Her Draconic physiology provides greatly improved reflexes and supernatural physical coordination. Her personal effectiveness in direct combat nevertheless remains distinct from that of dedicated martial specialists.[6]
- Immense Durability: Gaia's current Draconic body provides a much stronger physical vessel for her ancient Divinities. Her former Goddess body could withstand supernatural punishment, but the transformation significantly elevated her physical foundation.[1][6]
- Enhanced Stamina: As a True Dragon, Gaia possesses a body capable of sustaining considerable supernatural activity and supporting the use of high-quality Energy. Her environmental powers can also operate across large areas without requiring continuous conventional physical movement.[6][5]
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Gaia possesses the regenerative foundation associated with high-ranking Divine beings and True Dragons. Her original Primordial Goddess body survived a direct attack from Victor, although the extent of her current regeneration against attacks capable of destroying her entire Soul has not been demonstrated.[1][6]
- Longevity: Gaia possesses an exceptionally long lifespan and has existed for eons as one of the original Greek Primordial Goddesses. Her transformation into a True Dragon preserves this extraordinary longevity while providing a new racial foundation.[1][6]
- Enhanced Senses: Gaia possesses the supernatural senses associated with Divine and Draconic physiology. Her perception is further supplemented by her Divinity of Nature, which allows her to observe events through natural environments under appropriate conditions.[1][9]
- Draconic Soul: Gaia's Soul was reconstructed into a True Dragon foundation while preserving her ancient memories, personality, Divine characteristics, and existing identity.[6]
- [[Draconic Energy]] Compatibility: Gaia's True Dragon body possesses the capacity to sustain and channel Draconic Energy while retaining the [[Divine Energy]] and Conceptual authority associated with her original existence.[6]
- Draconic Metamorphosis: Gaia possesses the racial foundation for the physical transformations and continued development associated with True Dragons. Her individual full Dragon form and its appearance have not been described in the same detail as Victor's transformations.[6]
- Humanoid Transformation: Gaia can maintain her recognizable humanoid appearance despite possessing the supernatural racial foundation of a True Dragon.[6]
- [[Natural Energy]] Compatibility: True Dragons possess a natural connection to the Energy of the world around them. This characteristic is especially compatible with Gaia's existing authority over Nature and terrestrial environments.[6]
- Racial Evolution: Gaia possesses the greater developmental potential associated with True Dragons, allowing her physical and supernatural characteristics to improve beyond the limitations of her former Divine body.[6]

- Basic Draconic Runecraft: True [[Blood Dragons]] possess the natural ability to perform basic Draconic Runic applications without requiring the same extensive foundational training demanded of unrelated races. Gaia's mastery beyond this baseline has not been demonstrated.[11]
- Speech of Power (Racial Aptitude): Gaia possesses the Draconic foundation for using language, Energy, and intention to produce supernatural effects. No personal specialized Speech of Power technique has yet been described.[10]

##### Original Primordial Goddess Physiology
Gaia's original existence was that of a First Generation Primordial Goddess, one of the earliest beings associated with the Divine foundations of the [[Greek Pantheon]]. Unlike ordinary Gods who achieved Divinity through ascension or inheritance from later Divine generations, Gaia belongs to the original generation from which a large portion of the Pantheon's supernatural hierarchy developed.[12][1]
Her physical transformation into a Blood Dragon did not erase the Divinities or supernatural qualities accumulated throughout her ancient existence. Gaia therefore continues possessing Primordial [[Divine Energy]] and authority over her established Concepts, now supported by a different and more powerful racial foundation.[6]
- God Physiology: Gaia possesses the characteristics of a genuine Divine being, including supernatural longevity, Divine Energy, Conceptual authority, and a spiritual foundation fundamentally different from that of ordinary mortal races.[1]
- Primordial God Physiology: Gaia belongs to the original generation of [[Primordial Gods]] associated with the establishment of the Greek Pantheon. Her Divine quality and foundational importance greatly exceed those of ordinary Gods belonging to later generations.[12][1]
- Divine Energy Manipulation: Gaia can utilize the Divine Energy associated with her existence to manifest and sustain her supernatural abilities, including environmental manipulation, Divine reinforcement, and the generation of natural structures.[4][1]
- Primordial Divine Energy: Gaia possesses Divine Energy of exceptional quality associated with her status as an Original Primordial Goddess. Her individual reserves have not been quantified, but the scale of her environmental feats demonstrates substantial supernatural output.[4][1]
- Divinity: Gaia possesses established Divine Concepts principally associated with Earth, Nature, Life, Motherhood, and Fertility. These Concepts form the foundation of her authority and determine the kinds of supernatural phenomena she can most effectively influence.[1][3]
- Divine Concept Manipulation: Gaia can exercise supernatural authority through her Divinities, allowing her to influence the terrestrial environment and natural phenomena on a scale greatly exceeding ordinary elemental magic.[4][1]
- Divine Soul: Gaia possesses an extraordinarily ancient Divine Soul capable of sustaining multiple powerful Divinities. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] preserved her original identity while reconstructing her Soul through the Blood Dragon transformation.[6]
- Divine Perception: Gaia possesses supernatural perception allowing her to observe powerful Divine and environmental phenomena. She used her Divine senses to observe Victor consuming [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]]' remains after he entered the surrounding terrain.[1]
- Divine Aura: Gaia possesses the supernatural presence associated with a First Generation Primordial Goddess, sufficient for other powerful beings to recognize her status and identity upon encountering her.[1]
- Divine Longevity: Gaia has existed across eons and is not subject to the ordinary biological lifespan of mortal species.[1]
- Divine Resistance: Gaia's Primordial physiology provides resistance to conventional forms of physical destruction, although her confrontation with Victor demonstrates that sufficiently powerful opponents can injure her.[1]
- Conceptual Existence: Gaia's Divinities form part of her supernatural identity as an Original Primordial Goddess. However, her existence is not equivalent to that of a universal Primordial Entity embodying an absolute Aspect of Creation.[1]

###### Primordial Divine Authority
First Generation [[Primordial Gods]] occupy an exceptionally important position within the supernatural structure of their respective Pantheons. Their existence is connected to the Divine foundations upon which later generations develop, and their authority can influence phenomena extending far beyond the immediate bodies of individual combatants.[12][4]
Gaia's status grants her extraordinary environmental influence, but it does not establish unrestricted control over every Divine Concept within the [[Greek Pantheon]]. Her authority remains associated primarily with her own Divinities and the characteristics developed throughout her ancient existence.
- Pantheon-Level Environmental Influence: Gaia can affect the physical environment of a Divine realm on an enormous scale, including reinforcing the battlefield against attacks involving powerful Primordial Gods.[4]
- Divine Environmental Reinforcement: Gaia can impose her Divine authority upon natural structures, increasing their durability and protecting them from otherwise catastrophic damage.[4][1]
- Divine Manifestation: Gaia can externally manifest her Divine authority through geological formations, vegetation, and other natural phenomena without depending exclusively upon conventional physical movement.[1]
- Divine Authority Projection: Gaia can affect terrestrial matter beyond her immediate body, controlling large geographical regions through her Divine influence.[4][2]

#### Divinities
Gaia's principal Divine Concepts are closely interconnected. Earth governs the terrestrial foundation upon which natural environments develop, while Nature encompasses vegetation and ecological processes. Life provides the foundation for living organisms and their continued existence, while Motherhood and Fertility relate to reproduction, descent, and the propagation of living beings. Gaia's greatest environmental accomplishments often involve more than one of these authorities operating together.[3][1][2]
However, the existence of a Divinity does not establish that every theoretically possible application of its associated Concept has been demonstrated. Gaia's confirmed powers are primarily terrestrial, ecological, and reproductive in nature. Abilities such as unrestricted Soul creation, universal biological rewriting, or absolute authority over all manifestations of Life require individual demonstrations rather than inference from her Divine titles.

##### Earth Divinity
Earth is one of Gaia's most prominent Divine authorities. Through it, she can manipulate enormous quantities of terrestrial material, reshape entire landscapes, create mountains, and reinforce geological structures against the destructive consequences of extremely powerful attacks. Her control extends beyond ordinary soil and stone manipulation because it operates through a Primordial Divinity capable of influencing the physical environment on an extraordinary scale.[4][1]
Gaia's Divine authority over Earth was particularly apparent during the final Greek conflict. She continuously reinforced the battlefield while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] confronted multiple powerful Gods, preventing the environment from suffering the full consequences of their attacks. When Victor later humiliated her, Gaia's anger caused the surrounding landscape to rise and fall like a violent sea, demonstrating how extensively her emotions could influence terrestrial matter through her Divinity.[4][1]

- Terrain Manipulation: Gaia can alter the shape and movement of large areas of land, changing the surrounding geography during combat or environmental construction.[1]
- Large-Scale Terrain Restructuring: Gaia can reshape enormous terrestrial environments, as demonstrated when the surrounding landscape became unstable and moved in response to her anger.[1]

- Mountain Manipulation: Gaia can control the formation and structure of mountains, using them as obstacles and defensive barriers during combat.[1]
- Geological Formation: Gaia can form large terrestrial structures through the direct application of her Divine authority, without waiting for ordinary geological development.[1][2]
- Earthquake Generation: Gaia can produce enormous disturbances through her control over the ground. Her anger caused the terrain to shake, rise, collapse, and lose its ordinary stability.[1]
- Seismic Manipulation: Gaia's Earth Divinity can generate movements throughout the surrounding landscape, producing effects comparable to catastrophic seismic events.[1]
- Ground Instability Inducement: Gaia can make terrain unstable, causing the land to rise and fall and disrupting the physical environment beneath an opponent.[1]
- Terrestrial Matter Manipulation: Gaia can manipulate immense quantities of solid planetary material, demonstrated through both her battlefield techniques and her participation in the construction of Victor's artificial planet.[1][2]

- Earth-Based Defense: Gaia can use created or manipulated terrain as protection against supernatural attacks.[1]
- Terrestrial Environmental Control: Gaia can affect the terrestrial foundation of an area without individually touching every object being manipulated.[1][2]

###### Earth Fortification
One of Gaia's most important individual specialties is the ability to reinforce terrestrial material with her Divine authority. Rather than merely raising ordinary mountains or moving soil, she can strengthen land to endure attacks whose supernatural power would otherwise produce catastrophic destruction.[4]
During the battle involving [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], [[Erebus]], [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]], and [[Nocturnus]], Gaia continuously reinforced the surrounding Earth. Victor recognized that the terrain was receiving external support because it remained sufficiently stable to sustain attacks that should otherwise have destroyed the environment. Gaia also created a reinforced mountain that resisted complete penetration by one of [[Scathach]]'s spear techniques, illustrating the difference between ordinary stone and terrestrial material sustained by her Divinity.[4][1]
- Earth Fortification: Gaia can increase the durability of terrestrial material through the application of her Divine power.[4][1]
- Terrain Reinforcement: Gaia can reinforce large areas of land, preventing the environment from immediately collapsing beneath powerful supernatural attacks.[4]
- Environmental Durability Enhancement: Gaia can increase the resistance of natural environments to destructive forces, allowing areas to remain stable during battles involving extremely powerful Divine beings.[4]
- Reinforced Mountain Creation: Gaia can create mountains whose durability is considerably greater than that of ordinary geological formations. A mountain reinforced through her authority resisted complete penetration by Scathach's spear thrust.[1]
- Terrestrial Barrier Creation: Gaia can use mountains and other reinforced geological structures as defensive barriers against approaching attacks.[1]
- Battlefield Stabilization: Gaia can maintain the structural integrity of a battlefield despite the release of extraordinary supernatural forces.[4]
- Large-Scale Environmental Protection: Gaia can protect terrestrial surroundings from collateral destruction by continuously reinforcing the affected land.[4]
- Divine Material Reinforcement: Gaia can imbue terrestrial matter with sufficient supernatural support to allow it to withstand attacks far beyond the durability of ordinary stone.[1]
Gaia's environmental reinforcement is not equivalent to an invulnerable barrier. Scathach's attack successfully penetrated the reinforced mountain, even though it did not pass through the entire structure. The feat establishes extraordinary durability enhancement rather than absolute immunity to damage.[1]

###### Planetary Geokinesis
Gaia's Earth Divinity extends to the manipulation of planetary environments. During the creation of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s new world, she was selected specifically to supervise the formation of its terrestrial structures, demonstrating that her authority could be applied to geographical engineering on a planetary scale.[2][5]
Gaia assisted in gathering and manipulating enormous quantities of land, reshaping the interior of the developing planet, creating functional continental environments, and preparing geological structures capable of supporting different ecosystems. Her role was distinct from the powers supplied by Victor, Nyx, Natalia, Jeanne, and Roxanne, each of whom contributed a different supernatural component to the project.[2][5]
- Planetary Earth Manipulation: Gaia can manipulate terrestrial material on the scale required to construct and develop a planet, although the complete world-building project required assistance from other specialists.[2][5]
- Large-Scale Land Manipulation: Gaia manipulated enormous portions of land taken from [[Nightingale]]'s planet, contributing them to the construction of Victor's new world.[2]
- Continental Formation: Gaia helped shape the developing planet into functional continental regions capable of supporting future ecosystems.[5]
- Planetary Interior Manipulation: Gaia reshaped portions of the planet's interior during construction, preparing its terrestrial foundation for the installation of a supernatural core.[5]
- Planetary Core Preparation: Gaia created a massive opening extending into the interior of the new planet, allowing Victor to channel Dragon Fire into the developing core.[5]
- Planetary Core Sealing: After Victor completed the Energy infusion, Gaia manipulated the surrounding terrestrial material and sealed the opening.[5]
- Geological Engineering: Gaia can organize terrestrial environments into functional geographical structures rather than creating random formations without regard for the needs of a living world.[5]
- Volcanic Region Formation: Gaia helped establish regions where volcanic activity would occur as part of the developing planet's geological systems.[5]
- Mountainous Region Formation: Gaia can create mountainous environments and arrange them as part of planetary geography.[2][5]
- Terrestrial Resource Manipulation: Gaia's authority allows her to manipulate the physical material used in world construction. The supernatural enrichment of the completed planetary core, however, resulted from Victor's Dragon Fire rather than an independently demonstrated creation of such resources by Gaia.[5]

###### Planetary Connection
Gaia's control over an environment is influenced by her relationship with the land being manipulated. Although her Earth Divinity provides enormous authority over terrestrial matter, she does not automatically possess a perfect connection to every planet in existence.[2]
During the construction of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s artificial world, Gaia explained that she could perform the required work but could not guarantee perfection because she lacked an established connection to the planet. Her statement distinguishes her Divine authority from the much more direct planetary relationship possessed by genuine [[World Trees]] such as [[Roxanne Elderblood]] and [[Amara Elderblood]].[2][13]
Gaia is therefore capable of extraordinary planetary manipulation without being the literal embodiment of every terrestrial world she encounters. Her title of Mother Earth identifies her Divine authority and historical role rather than an absolute physical identity with the planet Earth.

##### Nature Divinity
Nature is another principal aspect of Gaia's Divine existence. Her authority allows her to influence natural environments, produce and manipulate vegetation, and support the development of functioning terrestrial ecosystems. Unlike ordinary plant-based magic, Gaia's power originates from a Primordial Divinity, allowing her to manifest vegetation in environments that would ordinarily be hostile to plant life and employ Nature as an extension of her Divine influence.[1][14]

- Nature Manipulation: Gaia can influence natural environments through her Divine authority, including the generation of vegetation and the development of terrestrial ecosystems.[1][14]
- Chlorokinesis: Gaia can generate and control plants, vines, and other vegetation through her Nature Divinity.[1]
- Vegetation Generation: Gaia can cause plant life to emerge directly from the ground, including within environments that would ordinarily be unsuitable for terrestrial vegetation.[1]
- Plant Growth: Gaia can stimulate the emergence and development of vegetation through Divine influence rather than relying exclusively upon ordinary biological growth processes.[1]
- Rapid Plant Growth: Gaia generated vines almost immediately during her confrontation with [[Scathach]], demonstrating the ability to produce vegetation rapidly enough for use in combat.[1]
- [[Vine]] Manipulation: Gaia can generate and control vines, directing them toward enemies as offensive or restrictive manifestations.[1]
- Plant Restraints: Gaia attempted to attack Scathach using vines, employing vegetation to obstruct and potentially restrain her opponent.[1]
- Plant-Based Attacks: Gaia can direct created vegetation offensively, allowing her to threaten opponents without relying upon conventional physical weapons.[1]
- Barren Environment Vegetation Generation: Gaia produced plant life within the [[Greek Underworld]] despite the surrounding terrain lacking ordinary terrestrial vegetation.[1]
- Vegetation Manipulation in Hostile Environments: Her Divine authority can temporarily overcome the environmental conditions ordinarily preventing plant growth, as demonstrated by the manifestation of vegetation within the barren Underworld battlefield.[1]
- Natural Environment Alteration: Gaia can change the natural characteristics of an environment through the introduction and manipulation of vegetation.[1][14]
- Botanical Manifestation: Gaia's connection to vegetation is also reflected in her appearance, as her body is normally covered by plants, leaves, and vines rather than conventional clothing.[1]

###### Plant-Based Sensory Perception
One of Gaia's most important noncombat abilities is her capacity to perceive information through the natural environment. Vegetation associated with her Divinity can serve as an extension of her awareness, allowing her to observe and listen to events occurring within areas connected to Nature.[9]
This ability was sufficiently well known among the Greek Gods that [[Zeus]] avoided allowing plants within [[Olympus|Mount Olympus]]. He feared that Gaia could use the vegetation to overhear private conversations, granting her access to information he wished to conceal. Her observational capabilities remained relevant after joining [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family, where the other women recognized that Gaia could use plants to monitor conversations and events without being physically present.[9]
- Plant-Based Clairvoyance: Gaia can perceive events through vegetation associated with her Nature Divinity, extending her awareness beyond the immediate location of her physical body.[9]
- Plant-Based Clairaudience: Gaia can listen to conversations through plants, allowing her to gather auditory information from locations connected to Nature.[9]
- Remote Viewing: Gaia can observe events occurring away from her body through vegetation under appropriate conditions.[9]
- Remote Hearing: Gaia can use plants to hear conversations without requiring direct physical proximity.[9]
- Botanical Sensory Extension: Vegetation can function as a medium through which Gaia extends her supernatural awareness into surrounding environments.[9]
- Environmental Surveillance: Gaia can monitor events within naturally vegetated environments through her Divine connection to plant life.[9]
- Covert Information Gathering: Gaia can acquire information through plants without openly appearing before the individuals being observed.[9]
- Supernatural Espionage: Gaia's plant-based perception enables indirect intelligence gathering, a capability consistent with her historical preference for manipulation and observation rather than direct confrontation.[9]
The practical extent of Gaia's surveillance depends upon her connection to Nature. The ability is not established as unrestricted omniscience, nor does it prove that Gaia can observe every location in the Universe regardless of whether an appropriate natural connection exists.[9]

###### Planetary Biosphere Formation
Gaia contributed directly to the formation of the biosphere of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s newly created world. Her responsibility was not simply to move rock into position, but to ensure that the developing planet possessed a functional terrestrial environment capable of eventually supporting living beings.[2][5]
During construction, she reshaped the planet's interior, established continental environments, and arranged regions possessing different environmental conditions. Victor supported the process through his Creation authority, while Roxanne and Jeanne contributed Energy necessary to enrich and stabilize the developing natural systems. The project therefore demonstrated Gaia's environmental expertise while also establishing the limits of what she could accomplish alone.[5]
- Planetary Biosphere Engineering: Gaia can help construct the physical and ecological environments of a planet on an extraordinary scale.[5]
- Planetary Biome Distribution: Gaia can arrange different environmental regions across a developing world.[5]
- Ecological Infrastructure Formation: Gaia can help establish the environmental foundations necessary for future ecosystems and settlements.[5]

- Collaborative World Creation: Gaia can combine her Earth and Nature Divinities with other supernatural powers to participate in planetary construction.[2][5]
Gaia did not independently create the finished planet, its supernatural Energy veins, planetary core, Dimension, or connection to the larger structures of Creation. Those components required Victor's power, the contributions of Jeanne and Roxanne, the support of Nyx and Natalia, and the later intervention of the greater supernatural systems governing the world.[5][15]

##### Life Divinity
Life is one of the principal Divine aspects associated with Gaia's existence as an ancient Mother Goddess. Her relationship with Life is closely connected to the propagation of living beings, biological development, and the maintenance of environments capable of supporting life. However, the novel distinguishes her Divine authority from the complete creation of independent sentient organisms and Souls, which requires additional supernatural processes and powers.[3][14][5]

###### Life and Biological Influence
- Life Manipulation: Gaia possesses Divine authority associated with Life, allowing her to influence living Nature and biological environments through her established Divinities.[3][1]
- Biological Life Influence: Gaia can affect living natural systems through her Nature and Life authority, particularly vegetation and ecosystems.[1][14]
- Plant-Life Generation: Gaia can cause living vegetation to emerge within otherwise barren environments, demonstrating a direct application of her authority to plant life.[1]

###### Natural Life Generation
Gaia's most direct demonstrated generation of living organisms involves vegetation. Her Nature Divinity allows plant life to emerge rapidly even in environments where normal terrestrial growth would be difficult or impossible.[1]
- Vegetative Life Generation: Gaia can directly generate living vegetation through her Divine authority.[1]
- Plant-Life Manifestation: Gaia can produce plants and vines without relying upon ordinary planting or growth periods.[1]
- Life-Supporting Environmental Development: Gaia can contribute the natural conditions required for larger and more complex ecosystems to develop.[14][5]
The generation of vegetation should not be treated as proof that Gaia can independently create complete sentient species or their Souls. The construction of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s new world required additional supernatural support precisely because producing land and vegetation was not sufficient to establish all the systems necessary for a complete living planet.[5][15]

- Primordial Offspring Production: Gaia produced children possessing the extraordinary foundations associated with Second Generation Primordial Gods through her union with [[Ouranos]].[12]
- Supernatural Species Procreation: Gaia's descendants include multiple distinct supernatural classifications, demonstrating an unusual range of successful reproduction among powerful Divine beings.[12][16]
- Inherited Power Transmission: Gaia's offspring can inherit characteristics associated with her Earth and Nature Divinities. The later Hecatonchires produced with [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] possessed recognizable Earthen qualities inherited from Gaia.[16]
- Divine Heritage Transmission: Gaia can pass aspects of her supernatural foundation to descendants through reproduction, allowing different generations to develop their own Divine characteristics.[12]

###### Primordial Offspring Production
Gaia's relationship with different Original [[Primordial Gods]] produced offspring possessing different supernatural characteristics. Her children with [[Ouranos]] included the ancient [[Titans]] and several other powerful Divine beings, while her later relationship with [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] produced descendants displaying more chaotic traits.[12][16]
During the final Greek conflict, several new Hecatonchires appeared whose qualities reflected both parents. Their Earthen aspects originated from Gaia, while their unusually chaotic characteristics were associated with Tartarus. These beings demonstrated that the original Primordial generation could continue producing exceptionally powerful offspring even after enormous portions of the older Greek Divine families had been destroyed.[16]
- Primordial Lineage Creation: Gaia can produce new generations of supernatural beings through Divine reproduction.[12][16]
- Inherited Elemental Characteristics: Her offspring may inherit terrestrial characteristics associated with Gaia's Divinity of Earth.[16]
- Composite Divine Heritage: Gaia's offspring can inherit supernatural properties from both parents, producing beings whose characteristics differ according to their lineage.[16]
- Powerful Progeny Production: Gaia has produced descendants possessing extraordinary supernatural strength and characteristics capable of threatening major Divine factions.[17][16]
Gaia's ability to produce powerful offspring does not establish that she can instantaneously create fully developed beings from nothing or grant any desired Concept to them without conditions. Her demonstrated progeny resulted from supernatural reproduction and inherited Divine characteristics.

##### Fertility Divinity
Fertility is another major aspect associated with Gaia's status as a primordial Mother Goddess. Her extraordinary reproductive history demonstrates the capacity to produce numerous powerful offspring across different Divine lineages and generations. However, the precise independent applications of her Fertility authority remain less directly demonstrated than her powers over Earth and Nature.[12][16]
- Fertility Authority: Gaia possesses Divine authority associated with Fertility, contributing to her supernatural role as the Great Mother.[12]
- Supernatural Fertility: Gaia has produced an extraordinary number of powerful supernatural descendants across her ancient existence.[12][16]
- Cross-Species Divine Reproduction: Gaia's reproductive history includes offspring with different Original [[Primordial Gods]], producing descendants with varied supernatural characteristics.[12][16]
- Divine Reproductive Compatibility: Gaia can reproduce with extremely powerful Divine beings while maintaining her own Primordial existence.[12]
- Draconic-Divine Reproduction: After becoming a Primordial Dragon Goddess, Gaia successfully produced [[Gina Elderblood]] with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], whose own supernatural foundation had evolved into that of the Dragon God of [[Chaos]].[18][8]
- Extraordinary Gestational Capacity: Gaia remained pregnant with Gina for approximately 1,900 subjective years during the accelerated development of Victor's family, demonstrating that her reproductive physiology could sustain an unusually prolonged Dragon pregnancy.[18]
Gaia's Fertility Divinity is distinct from the organized reproductive programs administered by [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood and the relevant Divine institutions of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Although Gaia's domains overlap with some of the Concepts involved, the Empire's entire population development system should not be attributed to her personal abilities.[19]

#### Soul and Spiritual Abilities

##### Soul Marking
Gaia demonstrated a supernatural ability to place a mark directly upon another individual's Soul. During her first encounter with [[Scathach]] in the [[Greek Underworld]], Gaia approached the ancient Vampire under the appearance of peaceful negotiation and briefly held her wrist. The contact allowed Gaia to apply a concealed mark to Scathach's Soul without the latter immediately noticing the intervention.[1]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] detected the mark through his Draconic perception and warned Scathach before Gaia could exploit it. The precise purpose of the mark was not revealed because the attempt was interrupted almost immediately, leaving its full effects unknown.[1]
- Soul Marking: Gaia can place supernatural marks directly upon another person's Soul through physical contact.[1]
- Spiritual Interaction: Gaia can affect the spiritual structure of another individual rather than being restricted to physical contact with their body.[1]
- Soul-Based Imprinting: Gaia can establish a supernatural mark upon an individual's Soul, as demonstrated through her contact with Scathach.[1]
- Concealed Soul Marking: Gaia can apply a spiritual mark without producing an immediately visible physical indication detectable through ordinary observation.[1]
- Supernatural Mark Concealment: Scathach could not perceive Gaia's mark through ordinary inspection of her wrist, while Victor required his superior Draconic perception to identify the intervention.[1]
- Subtle Spiritual Interference: Gaia can perform limited spiritual manipulation during an otherwise ordinary interaction without openly announcing the application of her power.[1]
The mark's intended function remains unknown. There is insufficient evidence to identify it specifically as Mind Control, Soul Possession, Tracking, Curse Manipulation, or permanent spiritual enslavement. Only the placement and concealment of the supernatural Soul mark were directly established.[1]

##### Divine Soul and Spiritual Perception
Gaia possesses an extraordinarily ancient Divine Soul, later incorporated into her new True Dragon foundation. Her spiritual existence supports multiple powerful Divinities and retained its essential identity during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s racial reconstruction.[6]
- Divine Soul Physiology: Gaia possesses the spiritual characteristics associated with an Original Primordial Goddess, allowing her to sustain powerful Divine Concepts and extraordinarily high-quality supernatural Energy.[1][6]
- Enhanced Soul: Her transformation into a True Dragon strengthened her racial foundation while preserving her previously accumulated spiritual characteristics.[6]
- Spiritual Perception: Gaia possesses supernatural awareness of spiritual and Divine phenomena. Her demonstrated Soul marking further establishes the ability to interact with a target beyond purely physical structures.[1]
- Divine Sensing: Gaia can observe phenomena through Divine senses, including detecting Victor's actions involving [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] after the body had been concealed within the terrain.[1]

#### Divine Blessings

##### Blessing Bestowal
Gaia possesses the ability to grant Divine Blessings, allowing her to bestow a limited connection to her supernatural authority upon another individual. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was found to possess a Blessing associated with Gaia before their first direct encounter, demonstrating that she could extend her influence to someone outside her immediate personal relationships.[20]
The Blessing was one of several received by Victor from powerful supernatural beings whose attention he had attracted through his actions. [[Aphrodite]] explained that Divine Blessings allow recipients to borrow certain powers associated with their benefactors but remain subject to the authority of the God who granted them. Victor recognized that such external powers were useful but should not become the foundation of his personal strength.[20]
- Blessing Bestowal: Gaia can bestow Divine Blessings upon individuals who attract her attention or satisfy the conditions under which she chooses to provide assistance.[20]
- Divine Power Bestowal: Gaia can grant limited access to her Divine authority through a Blessing, subject to the restrictions governing Divine Blessings in the supernatural system.[20]
- Remote Blessing Bestowal: Gaia bestowed a Blessing upon Victor before the two personally encountered one another.[20]
- Minor Divine Blessing: Gaia's Blessing upon Victor belonged to the group of relatively minor Blessings he had received from powerful beings who approved of something he had done, rather than representing an unrestricted transfer of her full authority.[20]
- Divine Blessing Withdrawal (General Divine Function): Divine Blessings remain subject to the authority of their original benefactors and can be withdrawn. Gaia's personal withdrawal of a Blessing has not been demonstrated.[20]
The individual effects of Gaia's minor Blessing upon Victor were not separately isolated from the other Blessings he possessed. It should therefore not be treated as proof that she granted him complete Geokinesis, Nature Manipulation, Life Manipulation, or any other unrestricted personal Divinity.[20]

#### Environmental and Planetary Manipulation
Gaia's most distinctive practical specialty is her capacity to manipulate complete terrestrial environments. Her combination of Earth, Nature, and Life authority makes her exceptionally valuable when an ecosystem must be created, developed, stabilized, or protected from destruction. This role became particularly apparent following her integration into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction, when she assisted Roxanne in developing the environment of his Personal Universe and later participated in constructing Dragon's Nest.[14][2][5]

##### Environmental Engineering
- Environmental Manipulation: Gaia can alter terrestrial environments through the combined use of her Earth and Nature Divinities.[1][5]
- Large-Scale Environmental Engineering: Gaia can manipulate enormous quantities of natural material and establish functional geographical systems.[2][5]
- Planetary Terrain Engineering: Gaia can participate in the construction and reshaping of planetary environments through her control over terrestrial matter.[2][5]
- Ecosystem Engineering: Gaia can help establish environmental systems capable of supporting future living organisms.[14][5]
- Biome Planning: Gaia understands how different types of terrain and natural regions must be arranged to create a functional world.[5]
- Geographical Organization: Gaia can shape the distribution of terrestrial environments across a developing planet.[5]
- Environmental Fortification: Gaia can reinforce natural terrain and protect it against the consequences of large-scale supernatural destruction.[4]
- Environmental Stabilization: Gaia can assist in maintaining the structural integrity of natural systems during the application of extraordinary supernatural forces.[4][5]
- Planetary Interior Engineering: Gaia can reshape the interior of a planet and prepare geological structures for the integration of additional supernatural components.[5]
- Natural Habitat Development: Gaia can develop geographical environments that provide the physical foundation for future ecosystems.[14][5]
- Natural-System Management: Gaia can assist in maintaining and managing the environmental components of a supernatural world, as demonstrated by her assignment under Roxanne.[14]

##### Collaborative World Construction
The construction of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s new world required several different supernatural specialties. Gaia was responsible for terrestrial formation and environmental development, while other members of the family contributed their own powers to the project.[2][5]
Victor supplied his Creation authority and immense [[Draconic Energy]], Nyx contributed the supernatural isolation and gravitational support required for planetary formation, Natalia assisted through her spatial abilities, and Roxanne and Jeanne helped establish the natural and Energy systems necessary for the environment to develop. Gaia manipulated the planetary material, created functional geographical regions, and prepared the interior for Victor's Dragon Fire. The completed world was subsequently integrated into the greater supernatural system through additional processes beyond Gaia's independent authority.[5][15]
- Cooperative Creation: Gaia can combine her Earth and Nature authority with the supernatural abilities of other powerful beings to participate in the creation of a complete planetary environment.[2][5]
- Planetary Formation Support: Gaia can supply the terrestrial foundation required for large-scale world construction.[2][5]
- Planetary Material Restructuring: Gaia can reshape the matter of a developing planet, establishing the geographical foundation required for later ecological development.[5]
- Planetary Core Infrastructure: Gaia can manipulate the planet's interior to accommodate the creation of an Energy-rich core.[5]
- Planetary Sealing: Gaia sealed the opening created for Victor's Dragon Fire after the Energy had been introduced into the new planetary core.[5]
- Natural World Development: Gaia's contributions helped establish the environment of Victor's new world, allowing it to become a functional home for the expanding Elderblood family.[5][15]
Gaia cannot independently be credited with the complete creation of Dragon's Nest, the production of its Dragon Fire core, or the registration of its Dimension within the greater system of Creation. Those accomplishments were distributed across multiple participants and required powers outside Gaia's own established Divinities.[5][15]

#### Draconic Lineage and Reproductive Authority

##### Draconic Lineage Bestowal
Gaia's role within the Elderblood family eventually extended beyond ordinary reproduction and the possession of an ancient Motherhood Divinity. As a Primordial Dragon Goddess and one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wives, she participated in decisions concerning the supernatural lineage inherited by members of the Imperial family.[21]
The True Dragon lineage was preserved as an exceptional classification associated primarily with the Emperor's immediate family. Gaia belonged to the group of mothers who did not automatically permit every child or relative to become a True Dragon. Instead, certain descendants could receive less powerful Dragon lineages, preserving a distinction between the Emperor's direct True Dragon family and other individuals possessing Draconic ancestry.[21]
- Draconic Lineage Bestowal: Gaia is among the members of Victor's family capable of providing certain descendants with a lower Draconic lineage rather than unrestricted True Dragon status.[21]
- Racial Empowerment: Gaia can participate in the supernatural empowerment of descendants through the transmission or granting of Draconic characteristics.[21]
- Draconic Heritage Transmission: Gaia can pass inherited Draconic characteristics through her descendants, as demonstrated by her biological relationship with Gina and the broader lineage system of the Elderblood family.[8][21]
- Lineage Restriction: Gaia is among the mothers who deliberately restricted certain descendants from becoming [[True Dragons]], allowing lower Draconic classifications instead.[21]
- Selective Racial Bestowal: The lineage granted to a descendant can be restricted to a lower form rather than automatically matching the highest racial classification possessed by the parent.[21]
This ability should be distinguished from Victor's status as the Primogenitor of the Blood Dragon Race. Gaia's participation in lineage bestowal does not establish that she can independently create an entirely new Dragon Race or grant the complete Primogenitor authority possessed by Victor.[6][21]

#### Other Abilities and Skills

##### Supernatural Knowledge
Gaia possesses extensive knowledge accumulated throughout an existence spanning eons. Her experience includes the development of the Greek Divine lineages, the reproduction of numerous supernatural species, the maintenance of natural environments, and the interaction between ancient Gods and the ecosystems associated with their Divinities.[12][3][2]
- Ancient Knowledge: Gaia possesses knowledge accumulated across the history of the [[Greek Pantheon]], including the relationships between its original [[Primordial Gods]] and later Divine generations.[12]
- Supernatural Biology Knowledge: Gaia possesses extensive familiarity with powerful living beings and the supernatural characteristics of Divine offspring.[3]
- Reproductive Knowledge: Gaia understands numerous aspects of Divine reproduction and inherited supernatural characteristics through her extensive history as a Mother Goddess.[12][16]
- Environmental Knowledge: Gaia possesses detailed knowledge of terrestrial environments, including the natural conditions necessary to establish functional ecosystems.[2][5]
- Geological Knowledge: Gaia understands how terrestrial matter can be organized into planetary structures, demonstrated by her role in the development of Dragon's Nest.[5]
- Ecological Expertise: Gaia can identify the natural conditions required for an environment to support living organisms and sustainable development.[14][5]
- Supernatural Environmental Engineering: Gaia can apply her knowledge practically through the manipulation of land and Nature on an enormous scale.[2][5]
- Biological Condition Assessment (Knowledge-Based): Gaia was considered a possible source of information concerning [[Fenrir]]'s abnormal condition because of her status as an ancient Mother Goddess. This represents recognized expertise rather than a confirmed personal healing feat.[3]

##### Strategy and Manipulation
Gaia is considerably more experienced in indirect conflict than conventional martial combat. Her ancient history involved the manipulation of political circumstances, the creation of powerful descendants, and the use of supernatural influence to affect events without directly exposing herself to danger.[17][1]
- Strategic Intelligence: Gaia possesses the experience necessary to influence political and supernatural conflicts involving multiple generations of powerful Gods.[17]
- Political Manipulation: Gaia repeatedly supported rebellions against Divine rulers whose conduct conflicted with her interests, influencing the political development of the [[Greek Pantheon]].[12]
- Indirect Warfare: Gaia can accomplish objectives through the intervention of descendants and allied supernatural beings rather than engaging every enemy personally.[17][16]
- Psychological Manipulation: Gaia can use conversation and misleading behavior to conceal hostile intentions, as demonstrated during her attempt to mark [[Scathach]]'s Soul.[1]
- Deception: Gaia can conceal hostile intentions behind a non-threatening demeanor while preparing supernatural intervention.[1]
- Misdirection: Gaia attempted to distract Scathach through peaceful conversation while secretly applying a spiritual mark.[1]
- Supernatural Surveillance: Gaia can acquire information indirectly through her connection to vegetation, supplementing her broader strategic methods.[9]
- Threat Assessment: Gaia can recognize when an opponent's strength renders further resistance impractical. She attempted to escape once [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s evolution and the destruction of [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] made the collapse of the Greek faction inevitable.[14]
- Survival-Oriented Judgment: Gaia is willing to withdraw from a confrontation rather than continue fighting when the possibility of success has effectively disappeared.[14]
- Divine Family Knowledge: Gaia possesses extensive knowledge of the relationships, rivalries, and inherited characteristics of the Greek Divine family, reflecting her position among its original progenitors.[12]

##### Offspring Command and Indirect Support
Gaia's relationship with her descendants allows her to influence conflicts through powerful supernatural beings associated with her family. During the confrontation with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], she called upon [[Typhon]] after suffering a humiliating attack, prompting the destructive Being to approach the battlefield.[1]
- Offspring Command: Gaia can call upon certain powerful descendants to intervene in a conflict, demonstrated by her summoning cry directed toward Typhon.[1]
- Proxy Warfare: Gaia historically used powerful descendants and allied beings to oppose enemies rather than personally confronting them.[17][16]
- Divine Alliance Coordination: Gaia can influence the actions of powerful Divine relatives and descendants through their familial relationships and shared objectives.[17]
The intervention of Typhon and other offspring should not be confused with an independently demonstrated ability to create or teleport them instantaneously during combat. Gaia called upon an already existing supernatural being rather than manifesting him from nothing.[1]

#### Resistances and Immunities
Gaia possesses the exceptional durability and supernatural constitution associated with her original Primordial Goddess existence, further strengthened through the acquisition of a True Blood Dragon body. However, resistance must be distinguished from absolute immunity, particularly because Gaia was injured by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] during the Greek conflict and subsequently captured despite possessing extraordinary Divine authority.[1][6]
- Supernatural Physical Resistance: Gaia possesses high-level resistance to conventional physical damage through her Primordial Divine constitution and current Draconic physiology.[1][6]
- Enhanced Durability: Gaia can withstand physical impacts beyond the capacity of ordinary supernatural beings, although sufficiently powerful opponents can damage her.[1]
- Environmental Energy Tolerance: Gaia's current True Dragon body possesses greater compatibility with powerful supernatural Energy than her former Divine body.[6]
- Enhanced Longevity: Gaia's existence is not restricted by ordinary mortal aging, having already survived for eons before her transformation into a Blood Dragon.[1][6]
- Spiritual Resilience: Gaia possesses an extraordinarily ancient Divine Soul capable of supporting powerful Divinities and surviving the reconstruction involved in her transformation into a True Dragon. This does not establish immunity to complete Soul destruction.[6]
- Terrestrial Environmental Adaptation: Gaia's authority allows her to manipulate hostile terrestrial environments and generate vegetation within otherwise barren surroundings. This is an environmental capability rather than proof of immunity to all environmental hazards.[1]
Gaia's Divine authority over Earth and Nature should not automatically be interpreted as immunity to every Earth-based or plant-based attack. Similarly, her Divine association with Life and Motherhood does not establish unrestricted resistance to Death, Soul Manipulation, Reincarnation interference, or Conceptual Erasure without direct supporting evidence.
