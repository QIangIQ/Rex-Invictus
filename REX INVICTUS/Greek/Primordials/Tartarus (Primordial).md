### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 794
[[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] huh? I'm not impressed. You hit like a bitch, that's probably why [[Gaia]] likes you—she prefers effeminate men?
—[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to Tartarus, Chapter 794
Tartarus was one of the original [[Primordial Gods]] of the [[Greek Pantheon]], a First Generation Primordial God associated with the deepest and most chaotic regions of the [[Greek Underworld]]. He belonged to the same generation as Gaia, Nyx, [[Erebus]] and [[Ouranos]], and was explicitly identified as the second-oldest Primordial God of the Greek Pantheon after Gaia.[2]
Unlike later Greek Gods whose authority was centered around the political structure of [[Olympus|Mount Olympus]], Tartarus was one of the ancient foundational beings tied directly to the Greek supernatural system. His name was also associated with the hellish Tartarean region existing within the Greek Underworld, a deeper and more chaotic territory distinct from the domains governed by Gods such as [[Persephone]], [[Hecate]], [[Hypnos]], Nyx and Erebus.[3]
Tartarus was an exceptionally dangerous combatant even among Primordial Gods. His most defining property was an existence comparable to that of a ghost: conventional physical attacks, elemental attacks and even weapons designed specifically to kill Gods simply passed through him. [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] and [[Morgana Elderblood]] were completely unable to physically affect him during their first encounter, while Tartarus was simultaneously capable of physically restraining and burning Nyx.[4]
This apparent intangibility was not absolute. Beings capable of directly interacting with the Soul could bypass it, something Victor Elderblood demonstrated immediately after being summoned into the Greek Underworld. Victor's Negative Energy and Soul-interacting nature allowed him to grab Tartarus directly, injure his body and temporarily disable the protection that had made ordinary attacks useless.[4]
Tartarus later fought Victor together with Erebus and [[Nocturnus]], forcing the Demon King into one of the most difficult battles of his life before his Blood Dragon evolution. Tartarus served as the group's principal close-range combatant, repeatedly forcing Victor into direct physical exchanges while Erebus and Nocturnus attacked through Darkness and Void. The battle ultimately ended with Tartarus being catastrophically wounded by Victor's full-powered attack. Victor later consumed his remaining body, and the enormous amount of Power obtained from one of the oldest Primordial Gods accelerated Victor's transformation into the Primogenitor of the Vampyric Dragons.[5][6][2]

### Physical Appearance
[[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] manifested as a tall, heavily muscular man surrounded by crimson-red flames. His arrival in the [[Greek Underworld]] was marked by red fire violently erupting from the ground before his body emerged and immediately seized Nyx.[4]
His flames were not merely decorative. Tartarus could completely cover himself in them while fighting, including coating his fists and body before entering close combat. The flames were dangerous enough to burn Nyx simply through sustained physical contact.[4]
Like other high-level Gods, Tartarus possessed golden Divine blood. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] caused golden blood to burst from Tartarus' nose after striking him with a headbutt powerful enough to temporarily disrupt his ghost-like protection.[4]

### Personality
[[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] was generally calm, direct and considerably less theatrical than many of the younger Greek Gods. Even after suffering some of the worst physical damage he had experienced in an extremely long time, he discussed his condition in an almost monotonous manner, although his irritation remained obvious.[7]
He possessed considerable confidence in his own strength. After his first battle with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Tartarus questioned [[Erebus]]' decision to retreat because he believed the two of them could have killed the Demon King if they had continued fighting. Erebus was considerably more cautious and pointed out that fighting Victor was not their actual objective and that victory against such an anomaly could not be treated as completely guaranteed.[7]
Tartarus was nevertheless capable of abandoning a plan when continuing it threatened the survival of the [[Greek Pantheon]]. After witnessing [[Zeus]] destroy [[Poseidon|the Master]] Bolt and seeing the conflict rapidly destroy [[Olympus]]' remaining strength, Tartarus concluded that their original objectives were no longer worth pursuing. He proposed contacting [[Gaia]] and explicitly argued that survival came before their objectives, recognizing that allowing Olympus to collapse would simply invite the other Pantheons to exploit the weakened Greeks.[8]
This concern demonstrates that Tartarus was not indifferent to the Greek Pantheon despite operating outside ordinary Olympian politics. He was willing to cooperate with Zeus, Kronus, Erebus and foreign Gods while their interests aligned, but he considered preserving the Pantheon's existence more important than blindly following a failing conspiracy.[8]
Tartarus also appears to have viewed loyalty to the Pantheon as important. When [[Aphrodite]]'s evolving Charm temporarily affected him and allowed Nyx to escape, Tartarus' immediate reaction was not embarrassment but surprise that a daughter of [[Ouranos]] had apparently betrayed the Greek Pantheon. He concluded that Erebus had been correct that the Pantheon required reform.[4]
In battle, Tartarus was confident but not recklessly arrogant. He recognized danger when Victor prepared another Dragon Breath and immediately warned the other [[Primordial Gods]]. During their prolonged three-on-one fight, Tartarus continued attacking directly while adapting to Victor's unusual durability and increasingly dangerous combat style.[5]

### History
[[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] was one of the earliest Gods to arise within the [[Greek Pantheon]]. Chapter 802 identifies [[Gaia]] as the first-born Goddess of the Pantheon and Tartarus as the second oldest Primordial God after her, placing him among the absolute oldest Divine existences belonging to the Greek supernatural system.[2]
As an Original Primordial God, Tartarus existed long before the Olympian generation and occupied a fundamentally different position from Gods such as [[Zeus]], [[Poseidon]], [[Ares]] or [[Apollo]]. First Generation [[Primordial Gods]] were described as foundational beings whose existence helped sustain their Pantheon; direct battles between beings of that generation could damage the Pantheon itself.[4]
Tartarus also developed an intimate relationship with Gaia. The full history of their relationship has not been explained in detail, but the two cooperated in producing extremely powerful and chaotic descendants. By the time of the Greek civil war, Tartarus possessed at least five Hecatonchires created together with Gaia. These were explicitly different from the original Hecatonchires descended from Gaia and [[Ouranos]], carrying Tartarus' characteristics and appearing considerably more chaotic.[1]
The five Hecatonchires recognized Tartarus directly as their father and had undergone extensive training while awaiting a promised future conflict. When Tartarus finally summoned them, he simply informed them that the promised time had arrived and ordered them to fulfill their training.[1]
Tartarus was also aware of secrets hidden even from several other Primordial Gods. When [[Erebus]] revealed [[Nocturnus]], the forbidden God of the Void produced through a union with a Primordial Goddess from another Pantheon, nearly everyone present was shocked by the young God's existence. Tartarus was specifically noted as the only major participant who showed no surprise, establishing that Erebus had previously informed him about Nocturnus.[1]
By the period of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s conflict with [[Olympus]], Tartarus and Erebus were participating in a much larger conspiracy involving Gods from outside the Greek Pantheon. Their allies included figures such as [[Amon]] and Seth, and their plan initially relied upon manipulating the Greek civil conflict rather than destroying Olympus outright. Their exact long-term objective was not completely explained before Victor's intervention destroyed the arrangement.[7][8]

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
[[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] made his first direct appearance after Nyx, [[Scathach]], [[Aphrodite]] and Morgana encountered [[Erebus]] within the [[Greek Underworld]]. Erebus had prepared an ambush designed specifically around neutralizing Nyx, whose recent actions had made him consider her a dangerous variable.[4]
As Erebus distracted the group, crimson flames suddenly exploded from the ground directly in front of Nyx and Tartarus emerged already holding her arm. Nyx's arm began burning immediately from contact with him.[4]
Scathach reacted by thrusting her spear directly into Tartarus' head, but rather than being pierced, his head simply opened and allowed the weapon to pass through empty space. Even after Scathach activated the Runes on her weapon and added Ice, every strike continued passing harmlessly through Tartarus. Morgana attempted to physically grab him using her radiation-covered hands and experienced the same result.[4]
Scathach correctly recognized that Tartarus possessed properties similar to a ghost. Erebus then openly stated that ordinary blades and even Anti-God weapons could not harm him through normal means.[4]
Aphrodite attempted a different approach and unleashed the full strength of her Charm, ordering Tartarus to release Nyx. Initially the ability had no effect, with Erebus confidently declaring that Aphrodite's Charm could not influence [[Primordial Gods]] of their level. However, Aphrodite's anger triggered an abnormal increase in the strength of her Divinity. Tartarus hesitated for several seconds and released Nyx, giving Scathach and Morgana enough time to rescue her.[4]
After recovering, Tartarus expressed surprise that Aphrodite had turned against the [[Greek Pantheon]] and concluded that the Pantheon required even greater reform than Erebus had suggested.[4]
Nyx had already been affected by the real objective of the ambush. Tartarus and Erebus used a fragment of [[Yggdrasil]] as a countermeasure capable of sealing a Primordial God's authority. The effect reduced Nyx to approximately the level of a High-Level God and sealed much of the privilege she normally possessed as an Original Primordial Goddess.[4]
Faced with two Original Primordial Gods while Nyx was critically weakened, Scathach activated the emergency summoning artifact [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had given her. Tartarus intercepted the object himself, unknowingly activating exactly the mechanism Scathach intended. The object broke after being touched by foreign Energy and summoned Victor directly into the Underworld.[4]
Victor's arrival immediately exposed the weakness in Tartarus' apparently untouchable body. A hand of Negative Energy emerged from the summoning artifact and grabbed Tartarus' wrist. Tartarus was genuinely shocked, noting that only beings capable of directly interacting with the Soul—or individuals he voluntarily allowed—should have been capable of physically touching him.[4]
Victor threw Tartarus aside and then prevented Erebus from escaping through Darkness. Tartarus counterattacked with a fist covered in his crimson flames, striking Victor directly in the face. The impact shattered the ground behind Victor, but Victor's Dragon-enhanced body absorbed the strike without meaningful injury.[4]
Victor mocked Tartarus, grabbed his arm and pulled him into a headbutt. Golden blood burst from Tartarus' nose and his ghost-like protection temporarily failed, allowing even Scathach to physically attack him. Victor followed with a full-strength punch that launched Tartarus away and then fired a concentrated Dragon Breath of chaotic violet Energy directly at him.[4]
Tartarus survived Victor's Dragon Breath but returned to Erebus and [[Amon]] with his entire body burned. He remarked that it had been an extremely long time since his body had been damaged so severely, with the last comparable incident apparently occurring when [[Gaia]] had previously entered a state of fury. Despite his injuries, Tartarus still believed that he and Erebus could have killed Victor had they continued fighting. Erebus disagreed with wasting further time because defeating Victor was not their immediate objective and because the Demon King's abnormal abilities made the outcome uncertain. The retreat became even more frustrating when the pair realized Victor had recovered the Yggdrasil fragment used against Nyx, effectively undoing an important part of their plan. Tartarus responded with visible annoyance upon realizing their previous effort had accomplished little. The three Primordial Gods then observed Victor turn the surrounding battlefield into his Blood Domain and begin slaughtering the gathered Greek Gods and [[Titans]]. Even Amon admitted he would not willingly confront the assembled force alone, while Erebus recognized that Victor was the kind of individual who would refuse to retreat regardless of the odds.[7]
The situation rapidly deteriorated. Victor's Immortal Army transformed every God he killed into an additional weapon against [[Olympus]], while [[Zeus]]' increasingly desperate actions caused even greater damage to the Pantheon.
When Zeus finally destroyed his own [[Master Bolt]] to reclaim greater authority over Olympus, Tartarus concluded that the conflict had gone too far. He openly proposed contacting Gaia and abandoning their original objectives because the survival of the Greek Pantheon had become more important.[8]
Tartarus understood the geopolitical consequences of Olympus losing too much power. If the Greek Pantheon shattered, rival Pantheons would exploit its weakness before Tartarus and Erebus could complete whatever larger objective they had originally pursued. Erebus agreed with him, and the two temporarily departed to respond to the crisis.[8]
By the time Victor killed both Zeus and Kronus, the remaining Olympian forces had lost their two primary [[God-Kings]]. Tartarus nevertheless rejected the idea that their side had already lost.[1] He released a surge of chaotic red Power, causing flames to erupt from the ground. Five enormous beings possessing multiple heads and arms emerged in response. [[Ares]] initially mistook them for the original Hecatonchires imprisoned within Olympus, but quickly realized they were different.[1] The creatures carried Gaia's Earth-like influence, but their paternal nature was completely different. They were more chaotic than the original Hecatonchires and clearly recognized Tartarus as their father, strongly confirming that they had been created by Tartarus and Gaia rather than Gaia and [[Ouranos]].[1]
Tartarus informed the five that the promised time had finally arrived and commanded them to use the training they had received against the enemies before them. All five immediately obeyed.[1] At the same time, Erebus unveiled [[Nocturnus]], the God of the Void. Most of the gathered Gods—including Nyx and Erebus' own children—were shocked by his existence. Tartarus alone remained unsurprised, establishing that he had known about Erebus' secret son before the battle.[1]
The conflict ultimately narrowed into a battle between Victor and the combined forces of Tartarus, Erebus and Nocturnus. Although Victor possessed multiple advantages specifically suited to fighting Primordial Gods—including Negative Energy, Soul-damaging attacks, his Dragon body and Roxanne's assistance—the three Gods were still powerful enough to gradually place him at a disadvantage.[5]
Tartarus functioned primarily as the frontline combatant. Covered in his crimson flames, he repeatedly entered close combat with Victor and forced him to respond physically while Erebus and Nocturnus attacked through Darkness and Void.[5]
During one exchange, Tartarus appeared directly beside Victor and forced him to defend using [[Junketsu]] in its gauntlet form. Tartarus told Victor to accept his inevitable defeat, acknowledging that he had survived surprisingly well for someone the Gods still regarded as a Mortal.[5]
Tartarus then opened a portal beneath Victor. Hands belonging to creatures from Tartarus emerged from the opening and grabbed Victor's legs. The creatures' hands were imbued with Tartarus' flames, allowing them to function as restraints while Erebus and Nocturnus attempted to exploit the opening.[5]
Victor's body proved too durable for the flames to cause significant damage, and Erebus' Darkness also failed to penetrate him. The Gods consequently began recognizing that Nocturnus' Void was the most reliable means of bypassing Victor's defenses.[5]
When Victor prepared another Dragon Breath against Nocturnus, Tartarus immediately recognized the attack and warned the others to evade, demonstrating that he had already adapted to the threat after experiencing the technique personally during their first confrontation.[5]
The battle continued into an extended war of attrition. Tartarus repeatedly forced Victor into close-range combat while Nocturnus used the Void to restrict Victor's movement and Erebus waited for an opportunity to deliver a decisive attack. Nocturnus and Erebus concluded that continuing the same strategy would eventually exhaust Victor, but they did not know how long that process would require.[6]
During one crucial moment, Victor broke through Nocturnus' defenses and moved to strike Erebus. Tartarus intervened before Victor could land the potentially fatal blow, protecting Erebus and allowing the three Gods to maintain their coordinated assault.[6]
Despite their numerical advantage and the fact that the battle took place inside territories favorable to them, Victor continually adapted to their abilities. The strain on his own body became increasingly severe, but so did the damage accumulating on the Primordial Gods.[5][6]
The battle eventually escalated beyond the capacity of the surrounding Divine realm to comfortably contain it. Victor unleashed an enormous attack powerful enough to pierce the natural barriers protecting the Pantheon itself. The expenditure left him almost completely exhausted, but Tartarus was left catastrophically wounded, with only approximately half of his body remaining.[2]
Victor immediately recognized Tartarus' remains as an opportunity to replenish himself. Before he could reach the body, Gaia appeared and attempted to prevent Scathach from retrieving it. Gaia tried to hide Tartarus' remains beneath the Earth while fighting Victor and Scathach simultaneously.[2]
Victor ultimately reached Tartarus first.
He began physically consuming the corpse of the Primordial God while Gaia desperately ordered him to stop. Gaia did not approach while Victor ate because the increasingly dreadful aura surrounding him made direct intervention dangerous.[2]
Victor swallowed the last remaining part of Tartarus. The result was immediate. A pillar of crimson and violet Energy erupted into the sky, and everyone present instinctively understood that Victor's existence had changed.[2]
Tartarus' significance to the transformation was directly emphasized by the narration. He was the second-oldest Primordial God of the Greek Pantheon after Gaia, and consuming such an ancient Divine existence accelerated the fusion occurring between Victor's Draconic and Vampiric natures.[2]
Victor consequently evolved beyond his previous state and became the Primogenitor of the Vampyric Dragons, the First and Beginning of a new Dragon lineage standing above the conventional Vampire and Dragon species from which he had evolved.[2]
Tartarus therefore became one of the final catalysts responsible for Victor's transformation into the race later known as the [[Blood Dragons]].

### Skills and Abilities
[[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] was an Original Primordial God and one of the oldest foundational Gods of the [[Greek Pantheon]]. His demonstrated abilities center around his ghost-like existence, crimson Tartarean flames, extreme physical strength, close combat, control over creatures connected to Tartarus and the ability to create openings into his own domain. Only abilities directly demonstrated or explicitly stated in the novel are listed below.
- Original Primordial God Physiology: Tartarus was a First Generation Primordial God and the second-oldest Primordial God of the Greek Pantheon after [[Gaia]].[2] First Generation [[Primordial Gods]] existed as foundational components of their Pantheon; Nyx explicitly explains that battles between beings of their generation can cause effects throughout the Pantheon because their existences help sustain it.[4]
- Immense Divine Power: [[Scathach]] considered facing two Original Primordial Gods simultaneously while unprepared to be completely outside her normal combat range, even though she believed she could potentially handle one with sufficient preparation and appropriate weapons.[4]
- Extreme Longevity: Tartarus had existed since the earliest period of the Greek Pantheon's history and was older than every Greek Primordial God except Gaia.[2]
- Divine Blood: Like other high-order Gods, Tartarus possessed golden blood, which became visible when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] physically injured him.[4]
- Ghost-Like Existence: Tartarus possessed the most unusual defensive ability shown among the Greek Primordial Gods encountered during the battle. His body's properties were explicitly compared to those of a ghost.[4]
- Intangibility: Conventional physical attacks simply passed through Tartarus without dealing damage. His head could open around an incoming weapon rather than being pierced.[4]
- Anti-God Weapon Resistance: [[Erebus]] explicitly stated that even ordinary Anti-God weapons could not affect Tartarus through normal attacks.[4]
- Elemental Attack Resistance: Scathach combined her weapon with Ice and Runes, while Morgana covered her hands in radiation, yet both attacks still passed through Tartarus because the problem was not conventional durability but his intangible state.[4]
- Selective Interaction: Tartarus stated internally that only beings capable of directly interacting with the Soul or beings he personally allowed were normally capable of touching him.[4]
- Soul-Based Weakness: Victor's ability to interact directly with Souls allowed him to bypass Tartarus' ghost-like body. His Negative Energy-enhanced hand physically grabbed Tartarus despite the Primordial God's intangibility.[4]
- Temporary Disruption: Sufficient Soul-interacting physical damage could temporarily disable Tartarus' ghost-like protection. After Victor headbutted him and drew golden blood, even Scathach became capable of attacking him physically.[4]
- Crimson Flame Manipulation: Tartarus controlled powerful crimson-red flames strongly associated with his physical manifestation and Tartarean abilities.[4]
- Flame Manifestation: Tartarus first appeared from an eruption of red flames rising directly from the ground.[4]
- Flame-Coated Body: His body could be completely surrounded by red flames, including during close-range combat against Victor.[7][5]
- Flame-Enhanced Strikes: Tartarus coated his fist in pure Tartarean flames before striking Victor. The blow possessed enough force to shatter the surrounding terrain despite Victor absorbing the impact.[4]
- Contact Burning: Merely maintaining physical contact with Tartarus was sufficient to burn Nyx's arm.[4]
- Creature Empowerment: Creatures summoned through Tartarus could have their hands coated in his flames while restraining opponents.[5]
- Domain Portal Creation: Tartarus could create portals connected to his own Tartarean forces. During his battle with Victor, he opened a portal beneath Victor's feet through which multiple creature hands emerged and restrained him.[5]
- Creature Summoning: Tartarus could call beings associated with Tartarus through these portals and use them to support him during combat.[5]
- Hecatonchire Summoning: Tartarus summoned five Hecatonchires created with Gaia by releasing a large amount of chaotic red Power. The creatures emerged from the ground through his flames and immediately obeyed him as their father.[1]
- Created Offspring: The five Hecatonchires were not the original children of [[Ouranos]] and Gaia. [[Ares]] specifically recognized that they possessed Tartarus' characteristics and were far more chaotic than the older Hecatonchires.[1]
- Command Over Offspring: Tartarus had trained the five creatures in preparation for the conflict and could command them as military assets.[1]
- Chaotic Red Energy: Tartarus could release a distinct form of chaotic red Power from his body. This Energy accompanied the summoning of his Hecatonchires and his crimson flames.[1] The novel does not provide enough information to classify this Energy as a separate universal Concept or assign additional properties beyond what is directly shown.
- Close-Quarters Combat: Tartarus repeatedly chose to engage Victor directly at close range, serving as the physical frontline of the three-Primordial formation while Erebus and [[Nocturnus]] supported him with Darkness and Void.[5][6]
- Physical Combat Skill: Tartarus was capable of maintaining repeated exchanges against Victor, whose martial ability was already far beyond ordinary Gods and supernatural beings.[5]
- Combat Coordination: Tartarus synchronized his attacks with Erebus and Nocturnus, deliberately forcing Victor to focus on close combat while Void restricted his speed and Darkness attacked through openings.[5][6]
- Protective [[Radar Warning Receiver (RWR)|Interception]]: Tartarus was fast enough to intervene when Victor was about to land a potentially lethal blow on Erebus, preventing Victor from immediately killing his ally.[6]
- Immense Physical Strength: Tartarus' ordinary physical strikes possessed tremendous destructive force. His first punch against Victor shattered the ground behind the Demon King even though Victor's Dragon-enhanced body endured the direct impact.[4]
- Extreme Durability: Once his ghost-like defense was bypassed, Tartarus still possessed the naturally enormous durability expected from an Original Primordial God. He survived Victor's first Dragon Breath despite having his entire body severely burned.[7]
- Survival Against Dragon Breath: Victor's violet Dragon Breath left Tartarus' entire body burned but failed to kill him during their first confrontation.[7]
- High Damage Tolerance: Tartarus returned to combat despite describing that attack as some of the worst damage his body had suffered in an extremely long period.[7]
- Enhanced Senses and Combat Awareness: Tartarus quickly recognized Victor preparing a second Dragon Breath and warned Nocturnus and Erebus to evade before the attack was released.[5]
- Primordial Resistance to Charm: [[Aphrodite]]'s ordinary full-power Charm initially failed to compel Tartarus. Erebus explicitly stated that her Charm could not normally affect Primordial Gods of their level.[4] This resistance was not absolute: when Aphrodite's Divinity underwent an abnormal increase through anger and Victor's influence, Tartarus hesitated for several seconds and released Nyx.[4]

### Relationships

#### Gaia Elderblood
[[Gaia]] was [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]]' sister, former lover and one of the most important figures associated with him. Both belonged to the original generation of the [[Greek Pantheon]], although Gaia was older and explicitly identified as the first Goddess born within their Pantheon while Tartarus was the second oldest Primordial God.[2]
The two also produced children together. Five Hecatonchires summoned by Tartarus in Chapter 799 were recognized as beings created from both Tartarus and Gaia, combining Gaia's Earth-associated nature with Tartarus' more chaotic characteristics.[1]
Gaia clearly still cared about Tartarus' survival during the final Greek conflict. When Tartarus was left with only part of his body remaining, Gaia personally appeared to stop [[Scathach]] and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] from taking his remains. She attempted to hide Tartarus' body beneath the Earth while fighting them.[2]
Her intervention failed. Victor reached Tartarus' body and began consuming him while Gaia repeatedly demanded that he stop. Despite her anger, the increasingly oppressive aura surrounding Victor prevented her from recklessly approaching before Tartarus had been completely consumed.[2]

#### Erebus
[[Erebus]] was [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]]' brother and closest confirmed combat ally during the Greek civil war.
The two coordinated Nyx's ambush, with Erebus supplying the plan and the [[Yggdrasil]] fragment while Tartarus physically restrained Nyx and prevented [[Scathach]] and Morgana from freeing her through conventional attacks.[4]
Tartarus trusted Erebus' judgment enough to retreat from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] when ordered despite personally believing they could have won. Later, the two jointly decided that preserving [[Olympus]] took priority over their original objectives once [[Zeus]]' actions threatened the Pantheon's survival.[7][8]
Their cooperation continued during the final three-on-one fight against Victor. Tartarus repeatedly placed himself on the frontline and even intervened to prevent Erebus from taking a potentially fatal blow.[6]

#### Nyx Elderblood
Nyx was [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]]' sister and one of his enemies during the Greek civil war.
Tartarus personally restrained Nyx during [[Erebus]]' ambush and used his flaming body to burn her while Erebus activated the [[Yggdrasil]] fragment intended to seal her Primordial authority.[4]
There is no evidence that Tartarus possessed a personal hatred toward Nyx comparable to Erebus' marital conflict with her. His actions appear to have been motivated primarily by the larger plan he shared with Erebus and their allies.

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became the first demonstrated opponent capable of immediately bypassing [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]]' supposedly untouchable body.
Tartarus initially struck Victor with a flame-coated punch that shattered the terrain behind him, only to discover that Victor's Dragon-enhanced body had barely reacted. Victor then physically grabbed him, headbutted him hard enough to draw golden blood and temporarily removed his ghost-like protection.[4]
Tartarus survived Victor's subsequent Dragon Breath and initially believed that he and [[Erebus]] could still kill him, although Erebus considered the outcome uncertain.[7]
Their second battle became far more extensive. Tartarus, Erebus and [[Nocturnus]] together gradually pushed Victor toward exhaustion, but Victor continued adapting throughout the fight. Tartarus served as the primary close-range opponent and repeatedly forced Victor to defend rather than freely target Erebus or Nocturnus.[5][6]
Victor ultimately overwhelmed the three Gods through an attack on a level capable of piercing the Pantheon's natural barriers. Tartarus suffered the most decisive consequence and was reduced to approximately half of his original body.[2]
Victor then consumed Tartarus' remains. Because Tartarus was the second-oldest Primordial God after [[Gaia]], absorbing such an ancient Divine existence accelerated Victor's ongoing racial evolution and directly preceded the completion of his transformation into the Primogenitor of the Vampyric Dragons.[2]

#### Nocturnus
[[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] knew of [[Nocturnus]] before his public reveal, unlike almost every other God present when [[Erebus]] finally exposed his secret son.[1]
During battle, Tartarus cooperated closely with Nocturnus. Tartarus forced [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] into direct exchanges while Nocturnus used the Void to restrict Victor's speed and deliver attacks capable of bypassing defenses that Tartarus and Erebus could not reliably penetrate.[5][6]

#### Amon
[[Amon]] was one of the foreign [[Primordial Gods]] cooperating with [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] and [[Erebus]] during their larger plan. After Tartarus and Erebus withdrew from their first clash with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], they regrouped with Amon and discussed both their injuries and the deterioration of their original strategy.[7]
When Tartarus proposed abandoning the plan temporarily to preserve the [[Greek Pantheon]], Amon objected. Tartarus and Erebus nevertheless prioritized the survival of [[Olympus]] over continuing a strategy that was rapidly destroying the very Pantheon they intended to use.[8]

#### The Hecatonchires
[[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] was the father of at least five unusual Hecatonchires created with [[Gaia]]. These beings were distinct from the original Hecatonchires of Gaia and [[Ouranos]] and possessed a noticeably more chaotic nature reflecting Tartarus' influence.[1]
The five had been trained in preparation for a future conflict and immediately obeyed Tartarus when he announced that the promised time had arrived.[1]

### Trivia
- [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] was the second-oldest Primordial God of the [[Greek Pantheon]] after [[Gaia]].[2]
- The novel explicitly describes his bodily properties as being comparable to a ghost.[4]
- Ordinary blades, Anti-God weapons, [[Scathach]]'s Rune-enhanced attacks and Morgana's radiation attacks all initially passed through Tartarus without affecting him.[4]
- Tartarus' intangibility was not absolute; characters capable of directly interacting with Souls could touch him normally.[4]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was the first character shown immediately bypassing Tartarus' ghost-like defense through his Soul-interacting and Negative Energy abilities.[4]
- Tartarus was briefly affected by [[Aphrodite]]'s Charm after her Divinity suddenly became stronger, despite standard Charm initially failing against him.[4]
- The first Dragon Breath Tartarus received from Victor left his entire body burned. Tartarus stated that it had been a long time since he had taken comparable physical damage.[7]
- Tartarus believed he and [[Erebus]] could have killed Victor during their first confrontation, although Erebus considered the outcome uncertain and chose to retreat because fighting Victor was not their primary objective.[7]
- Tartarus was willing to abandon the larger conspiracy once he believed continuing it would destroy the Greek Pantheon.[8]
- The five Hecatonchires he summoned were different from the original Hecatonchires and were confirmed to possess both Tartarus' and Gaia's characteristics.[1]
- Tartarus was one of the few people aware of [[Nocturnus]] before Erebus publicly revealed him.[1]
- During the battle against Victor, Tartarus primarily acted as the frontline close-combat fighter while Erebus and Nocturnus supported him with Darkness and Void.[5][6]
- Tartarus' corpse became one of the immediate catalysts for Victor's evolution into the Primogenitor of the Vampyric Dragons.[2]
- Several older versions of this article listed separate Divinities of Evil, Ghost, Abyss and Hellfire. Tartarus demonstrably possesses ghost-like properties and crimson Tartarean flames, but the chapters covering his battle do not individually identify every one of those terms as separate named Divinities. They are therefore not presented here as confirmed standalone Concepts without an explicit chapter statement.
