[[Olympus|Mount Olympus]] may burn, the Gods may die for all I care, but the land is still ours, and foreigners will not claim this place.
—Nyx to [[Persephone]], Chapter 623
Nyx Elderblood, formerly known simply as Nyx, is an Original Primordial Goddess of the [[Greek Pantheon]], the primordial Goddess of Night, the Mother of Concealment, and one of the wives of [[Victor Elderblood]]. As a member of the First Generation of Greek [[Primordial Gods]], Nyx belongs to the oldest generation of Divine beings originating from the Greek Pantheon, possessing an existence fundamentally connected to the supernatural foundations of the realm itself. Her principal Divinities are Night and Concealment, with Darkness forming an important component of her Night-related powers. She also possesses lesser Divine associations with Death, Mystery and Terror, characteristics that became particularly evident when she openly confronted the forces responsible for the destruction of the old Greek order.[6][5]
Nyx is one of the most accomplished practitioners of concealment and supernatural information manipulation among the ancient Gods. Unlike ordinary invisibility or stealth techniques, her authority allows her to conceal individuals, locations, identities, Divine presences and information from physical and supernatural observation. Her powers can obstruct even advanced forms of Divination and future-oriented perception, while her connection to Night provides an extraordinary medium for transportation, surveillance and large-scale manipulation of the surrounding environment. In her own domain, Nyx can become almost impossible to detect by beings lacking the existential standing necessary to perceive an Original Primordial Goddess.[7][8]
Nyx was formerly married to [[Erebus]], the Original Primordial God of Darkness, with whom she established an extensive Divine lineage that included [[Thanatos]], [[Hypnos]] and [[Nemesis]]. Although their Divinities overlapped through the relationship between Night and Darkness, their conceptual authorities were not identical. Erebus represented a more fundamental form of primordial Darkness, whereas Nyx's power extended across the broader characteristics of Night, including the concealment, secrecy, mystery and psychological influence associated with it. Their marriage eventually collapsed following Erebus's betrayal, leading Nyx to distance herself from him and pursue an alternative future beyond the increasingly unstable Greek Pantheon.[9][5]
During the political collapse of Olympus, Nyx began aligning herself with Victor and the Goddesses who had abandoned the old Greek establishment. She recognized that Victor's connection to a World Tree and his capacity to establish a new Pantheon represented a genuine opportunity to escape the political and familial conflicts that had defined her previous existence. Their cooperation deepened during the confrontation with Erebus and the other conspirators attempting to overthrow the established supernatural balance, eventually leading Nyx to join Victor's faction rather than remain bound to the remnants of Olympus.[1][5][10]
Following the destruction of the old Greek order, Nyx became increasingly involved with Victor's expanding civilization. She participated in the creation of his new planetary home, carried out specialized reconnaissance missions, assisted in the capture of dangerous supernatural enemies and eventually accepted transformation into a genuine Blood Dragon. The transformation preserved her ancient Primordial identity and established Divinities while reconstructing her body and Soul through Victor's Draconic lineage. Nyx simultaneously entered a [[Soul Marriage]] with Victor, becoming Nyx Elderblood and one of the Primordial Dragon Goddesses of Clan Elderblood.[11][3]
As the [[Elderblood Empire]] developed, Nyx's ancient powers became particularly valuable for intelligence gathering, espionage, infiltration and covert warfare. She helped establish the Third Imperial Legion, the Black Hand of the Emperor, alongside Kaguya and Persephone. Kaguya became the Legion's principal commander, while Nyx was explicitly designated to replace her when necessary. The two women subsequently became recognized as the principal figures responsible for Imperial intelligence, combining Kaguya's control over Shadows with Nyx's authority over Night to conduct operations that conventional military forces could not perform.[2][12]

### Appearance
Nyx in her Primordial Goddess appearance.
Nyx possesses the appearance of an exceptionally beautiful, mature woman with pale white skin, black eyes and extraordinarily long black hair resembling the darkness of a star-filled night. Her appearance reflects the nature of her Divinity, combining elegance and mature femininity with an imposing supernatural presence. She generally favors dark clothing, particularly black dresses and accessories, whose appearance complements her identity as the Goddess of Night. Unlike the more flamboyant appearances adopted by some Greek Goddesses, Nyx's clothing typically emphasizes the quiet grandeur associated with her primordial existence.[6]
Her long hair and dark clothing frequently create the impression that the night itself has taken a humanoid form. This resemblance becomes especially pronounced when she employs a greater portion of her Divine authority, causing the environment around her to acquire the characteristics of a starry night rather than ordinary Darkness. The phenomenon is distinct from the completely lightless Darkness associated with [[Erebus]], whose manifestation represents a different and more fundamental conceptual aspect of the absence of Light.[6][9]
Nyx's appearance can change substantially when she releases her Primordial Divine power. During the final conflict surrounding [[Olympus]], her clothing transformed into a Divine Raiment resembling an immense star-filled night, while a large black scythe manifested in her hands. Her presence became considerably more threatening, reflecting the fact that her Divinities extended beyond Concealment and Night to include lesser associations with Death, Mystery and Terror. The transformation emphasized the distinction between her normally amused demeanor and the extraordinarily dangerous Primordial Goddess who emerged when personally provoked.[5]
Nyx after joining the Elderblood family.
Following her transformation into a Blood Dragon, Nyx acquired the superior physiology of a genuine True Dragon without losing the distinctive characteristics of her ancient existence. Her previous Divine foundation, memories and personality were preserved through the transformation, allowing her to continue appearing in the elegant humanoid form associated with the Goddess of Night while possessing the biological and spiritual structure of a Dragon Goddess.[3]
Nyx's connection to the night sky remained an important part of her appearance during the Imperial era. During a family celebration held within a grand environment constructed by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], the women received clothing fashioned from different celestial phenomena. Nyx was given a dress created from the starry night itself, complementing her Primordial identity. Other women received garments associated with phenomena such as stellar Light, supernovae and the fabric of the Cosmos, reflecting Victor's increasingly extraordinary ability to manipulate celestial structures and supernatural materials.[13]

### Personality
Nyx is an exceptionally intelligent, calculating and manipulative woman whose normally relaxed and seductive demeanor conceals the mentality of an ancient Primordial Goddess accustomed to observing events from positions of relative safety. Rather than approaching every conflict through direct confrontation, she prefers gathering information, identifying the weaknesses of the individuals involved and arranging circumstances so that their decisions produce the outcome she desires. Her authority over Concealment naturally complements this philosophy, allowing her to remain hidden while observing developments that other Gods cannot easily perceive. Nyx frequently presents herself as amused or indifferent even when she is actively manipulating the people around her, making it difficult for others to determine how seriously she regards a particular situation.[6][7]
Her treatment of [[Persephone]] during the crisis in the [[Greek Underworld]] provides one of the clearest examples of her methods. Recognizing that [[Hades]]'s departure had left the realm vulnerable to foreign conquest, Nyx attempted to arrange for Persephone to become its legitimate Ruler. Although the objective served the broader interests of the [[Greek Pantheon]], Nyx deliberately approached Persephone while she was emotionally and politically vulnerable, knowing that the circumstances would make it difficult for her to reject the offer. Nyx also possessed information concerning the brutal nature of the trial required for succession and understood that an individual with strong emotional attachments could emerge with considerable power. Her true expectation was that Persephone would become a sufficiently powerful sovereign to defend the Underworld while remaining politically dependent upon her guidance.[6][14][7]
Despite her extensive experience, Nyx is not incapable of miscalculation. Persephone's transformation into the legitimate Ruler of the Greek Underworld produced a far greater increase in power than Nyx anticipated, placing the newly empowered Goddess beyond the level at which she could be easily controlled. Nyx became genuinely frustrated when Persephone breached her defensive Concealment and used her new Divine weapon to sever one of Nyx's arms. The physical injury itself was comparatively insignificant to the Primordial Goddess, but the fact that Persephone had successfully deceived and surprised her was deeply irritating. Nyx was accustomed to being the individual who possessed superior information, making situations in which another person successfully concealed something from her particularly aggravating.[7]
Nyx also possesses a strong sense of territorial responsibility toward Greece, although this loyalty should not be confused with unwavering devotion to [[Olympus|Mount Olympus]] or the political system established by [[Zeus]]. She openly acknowledged that she would not particularly care if Olympus burned or numerous Greek Gods died, provided that the fundamental territory and supernatural foundations of Greece did not fall under foreign control. Her attachment was therefore directed more toward the survival and independence of her homeland than toward preserving every institution or ruler associated with it. This perspective made her willing to permit significant political upheaval when she considered the existing establishment corrupt or incapable of protecting the Pantheon's future.[6]
Her age and Primordial status contribute to considerable pride, but she is more pragmatic than many ancient Gods who treat their authority as unquestionable. Nyx recognizes that individuals capable of threatening the established supernatural hierarchy cannot be dismissed merely because they belong to younger generations. This flexibility became especially apparent in her assessment of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], whom she recognized as a potential foundation for a new Pantheon long before the complete destruction of Olympus. Rather than remain bound to the political order that had failed her, Nyx pursued the possibility of beginning again within a different supernatural civilization.[6][1][5]
Nyx's relationship with [[Erebus]] reveals a considerably more emotional aspect of her personality. Although she eventually claimed that her romantic feelings for him had disappeared, his betrayal continued provoking intense resentment, particularly after she discovered the existence of [[Nocturnus]]. The revelation that Erebus had fathered a child with a foreign Primordial Goddess was especially painful because Nyx had not believed he would go so far as to establish another Divine lineage outside their Pantheon. Her anger during the confrontation with Erebus demonstrated that beneath her habitual amusement and calculated detachment remained the capacity for powerful jealousy, grief and vengeance.[5][10]
Despite her generally manipulative disposition, Nyx possesses genuine affection for her children and extended family. Her relationships with [[Thanatos]], [[Hypnos]] and [[Nemesis]] are marked by the unusual combination of authority and familiarity typical of ancient Divine families. Although she had distanced herself from her children during the deterioration of her marriage, they continued acknowledging her extraordinary power and understanding that ordinary methods of concealment were ineffective against their mother. Nyx herself demonstrated familiarity with their individual abilities and limitations, including the unusual immortality associated with Thanatos's embodiment of Death.[15][5]
Her integration into Clan Elderblood gradually changed the circumstances of her life. Rather than remaining an isolated Primordial Goddess whose principal relationships revolved around ancient rivalries and political schemes, Nyx became part of an enormous family whose members openly expressed affection, rivalry and loyalty toward one another. She developed a genuine attachment to Victor, accepted her position among his wives and continued employing her powers in support of his Empire. Her teasing demeanor remained intact, however, and she frequently treated the disputes among the women with amusement rather than the solemnity expected from an ancient Divine being.[3][12]
Nyx also possesses considerable professional discipline when her responsibilities require it. Her ancient knowledge of secrets, covert operations and information manipulation made her exceptionally suited to Imperial intelligence work, while her willingness to cooperate with Kaguya demonstrated an ability to function within an organized command structure without demanding absolute personal control. By the Imperial era, Nyx had become one of the individuals trusted to carry out sensitive missions for Victor and to manage the covert activities of a civilization whose territory and political interests extended across enormous distances.[2][12]

### Background
Nyx originated during the earliest era of the [[Greek Pantheon]] and belongs to the First Generation of its [[Primordial Gods]], alongside beings such as [[Gaia]], [[Erebus]], [[Ouranos]] and [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]]. These ancient Gods predate the later generations of Olympians and possess existences connected to the fundamental supernatural principles governing the Greek Pantheon's domains. Their power is therefore not merely the result of accumulated worship or ordinary Divine advancement, but also derives from their relationship with the Pantheon's original foundations and the Concepts associated with their existence.[1][8]
Nyx's principal identity became associated with Night and Concealment, while her brother Erebus possessed the primordial authority over Darkness. Although the two Concepts share important characteristics, the distinction between them is significant. Erebus's Darkness represents a more fundamental condition of the absence of Light, while Nyx's Night encompasses a broader set of phenomena including secrecy, mystery, hidden presences and the psychological effects associated with darkness beneath the night sky. Their overlapping authorities allowed their powers to interact without making them identical.[6][9][5]
Nyx and Erebus eventually married and established an extensive Divine lineage. Their children included numerous Gods associated with fundamental phenomena such as Death, Sleep, Revenge and other aspects of existence, while subsequent generations produced further Divine descendants. Their relationship was significant within the structure of the [[Greek Underworld]], where several of their children eventually served important administrative and supernatural functions under [[Persephone]].[8][15][5]
Nyx also established her own Primordial territory within the Greek Underworld. Unlike the ordinary administrative regions governed through the system responsible for receiving and processing deceased Souls, this domain belonged directly to her and reflected her status as an Original Primordial Goddess. Consequently, the authority attached to the position of Underworld Ruler did not automatically grant Persephone unrestricted ownership of Nyx's personal territory. Similar circumstances applied to the domains maintained by other powerful Primordial and Divine beings residing within the Underworld.[8]
Her marriage with Erebus eventually collapsed following his infidelity and growing involvement in plans to disrupt the established supernatural balance. Nyx discovered that Erebus had betrayed her, leading her to separate from him and distance herself from much of their family. His subsequent cooperation with other Gods and involvement in the creation of [[Nocturnus]] would eventually reveal that the betrayal extended beyond an ordinary affair into political schemes involving the formation of a new Pantheon composed of negative supernatural beings.[5][10]
The deterioration of her marriage contributed to Nyx's desire for a new beginning. She became interested in the possibility of attaching herself to an individual connected to a World Tree capable of establishing a new Pantheon, eventually recognizing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] as someone whose rapidly developing supernatural existence could provide the foundation for such a future. Her decision to cooperate with him consequently emerged from a combination of personal disillusionment, political calculation and the recognition that the old Greek order was approaching a crisis it might not survive.[6][5]

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Nyx entered the modern conflict during the deterioration of the [[Greek Pantheon]]'s political structure. Following [[Hades]]'s departure from [[Olympus]] and his association with the Demonic faction, the [[Greek Underworld]] was left vulnerable because its legitimate ruler had abandoned the responsibilities attached to his position. Nyx recognized that hostile supernatural factions could exploit the situation and attempt to seize control of the realm, threatening the independence of Greece's afterlife and potentially disrupting the system responsible for processing the Souls of the dead.[6]
Nyx approached [[Persephone]], who had remained Queen of the Underworld primarily in title while lacking the authority necessary to govern the realm independently. She explained that Hades had abandoned his position and offered Persephone the opportunity to become the legitimate Ruler by inheriting the authority associated with the original Helm of Hades. The artifact had been recovered before it could leave the Pantheon and contained the supernatural mechanism necessary for determining whether a candidate possessed the impartiality required to govern the Greek Underworld.[6][14]
Nyx's proposal was not entirely altruistic. She deliberately approached Persephone when the Goddess was emotionally vulnerable and politically isolated, expecting her to accept an arrangement that would strengthen Greece while leaving Nyx with considerable influence over the new ruler. Persephone initially refused to become her pawn, but eventually recognized that allowing the Underworld to fall into foreign hands would threaten the people and territory she still considered her responsibility. She therefore agreed to undergo the trial associated with the Helm of Hades.[6][14]
During their discussion, Nyx explained important principles concerning the operation of Heaven and Hell, including the relationship between the judgment of Souls and their eventual recycling through the broader systems governing reincarnation. Persephone learned that the responsibilities of an Underworld Ruler extended far beyond political authority or the punishment of sinners. The position involved maintaining an essential supernatural institution whose operations were connected to the circulation of Souls through existence.[14]
Persephone eventually completed the trial and acquired the full authority of the Greek Underworld's legitimate ruler. However, the process required her to endure extraordinary psychological suffering involving decisions concerning the lives of individuals she loved. Her resulting transformation greatly exceeded Nyx's expectations, elevating her to the level of a God-King supported by the realm itself. Nyx had anticipated that Persephone would become a powerful but manageable ruler, not a sovereign whose authority could interfere with her own Primordial arrangements.[7]
The situation deteriorated when Persephone confronted Nyx about her manipulation. Upon learning that Nyx had understood the nature of the trial and deliberately selected her because of the emotional attachments that would make its consequences particularly severe, Persephone reacted with fury. She breached Nyx's Concealment barrier and manifested a new Divine weapon derived from the authority she had inherited. Nyx initially believed the weapon could not seriously threaten her because she associated it with [[Thanatos]], whose authority over Death could not ordinarily eliminate an Original Primordial Goddess of the same Pantheon.[7]
Persephone nevertheless succeeded in severing Nyx's arm, revealing that the weapon had been created through her newly acquired authority rather than simply borrowing Thanatos's scythe. Nyx recovered the severed limb through Telekinesis and reattached it, regarding the injury itself as relatively insignificant. What disturbed her was the realization that Persephone had concealed the true nature of her weapon and successfully surprised the Mother of Concealment. Nyx's confidence in her ability to control the situation was further undermined when Persephone banished her from the Underworld, using the authority of its legitimate ruler to establish a Law preventing her from returning without permission.[7]
Nyx accepted that she had miscalculated and withdrew rather than attempting to force her way through the newly established authority. She immediately returned her attention to Olympus, where [[Zeus]] had isolated the Pantheon through his own rulership powers. Nyx recognized the danger posed by this decision because the region was already experiencing civil conflict and the release of [[Typhon]], whose association with the Concept of the End represented an existential threat to numerous Greek Gods.[7]
While observing Olympus, Nyx overheard [[Hera]], [[Athena]] and [[Artemis]] discussing the danger facing the Amazons, whose territory was being attacked by hostile forces connected to the wider Demonic conflict. Nyx concealed the surrounding location and approached the Goddesses, offering a plan that would allow her to gather information, influence the developing crisis and potentially establish useful connections with the faction surrounding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Her actions demonstrated that losing control over Persephone had not eliminated her broader capacity to manipulate the Greek political situation.[16]
As the Demonic War continued, Nyx used her authority to conceal locations from supernatural observation and investigate the actions of individuals suspected of cooperating with the enemies of Olympus. Her information-gathering capabilities allowed her to observe conversations and activities that ordinary Gods could not easily detect, while her familiarity with the ancient Greek authorities provided important knowledge concerning the political and supernatural dangers facing the Pantheon.[16][17]

#### King Among Kings
Nyx's involvement with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction increased as the political structure of [[Olympus]] continued deteriorating. [[Zeus]] had used his authority to isolate the Pantheon, preventing ordinary Gods from entering or leaving without permission. Because Nyx belonged to the Original Primordial generation, she possessed the exceptional status necessary to bypass restrictions that prevented even powerful Space-related Gods from freely entering the sealed territory.[1]
During discussions concerning the rescue of [[Hera]] and other Greek Goddesses who wished to abandon the deteriorating political order, Nyx offered to assist with their extraction. Her involvement was initially motivated by political calculation rather than unconditional loyalty toward Victor, but her capabilities were sufficiently valuable that [[Aphrodite]] and the other women recognized the necessity of accepting her cooperation. Nyx subsequently used her supernatural influence to transfer numerous Greek Goddesses into territory controlled by Victor's faction, demonstrating the strategic importance of her mobility and Concealment during the crisis.[1][18]
Her growing connection to Victor also reflected her personal desire to escape the failing Greek order. Nyx recognized that [[Erebus]] and several other ancient Gods were pursuing objectives extending beyond the ordinary political struggles surrounding Zeus, while Victor's connection to a World Tree offered the possibility of establishing a new Pantheon. She therefore continued cooperating with the women associated with Victor even as the conflict surrounding Greece became increasingly dangerous.[1][5]
Nyx later accompanied Aphrodite, [[Scathach]] and Morgana into the [[Greek Underworld]]. The expedition required investigating the activities of powerful Gods and gathering information concerning the threats developing within Greece, while Nyx served as the group's guide through territories whose supernatural structure differed considerably from the Hell controlled by the Biblical and Demonic factions. Her familiarity with the Underworld allowed her to explain the various Divine territories maintained within the realm and the limitations imposed upon [[Persephone]]'s authority as its legitimate ruler.[8]
The expedition also demonstrated the exceptional difficulty of detecting Nyx when she employed her Concealment seriously. Her Primordial domain existed through a relationship with Night that ordinary supernatural beings could not easily perceive, and her children understood that even their own concealment techniques were generally ineffective against her senses. When [[Thanatos]], [[Hypnos]] and [[Nemesis]] attempted to observe the approaching group, Nyx detected them before they could act, despite the extraordinary abilities each possessed.[8][15]
Scathach subsequently attacked Thanatos, briefly destroying his head through an extraordinarily powerful spear strike. Nyx explained why the attack could not permanently eliminate him: as the embodiment of Death within the [[Greek Pantheon]], Thanatos possessed a form of existence that could not be ended merely through conventional anti-God enchantments. Nyx identified the conceptual conditions necessary for destroying beings of his nature, demonstrating her extensive knowledge of the relationship between Divine Concepts, immortality and the fundamental characteristics of the Greek Gods.[15]
After the confrontation, Nyx used a veil resembling the night sky to conceal her children and transport them to Persephone's castle. The operation occurred so rapidly that only Scathach clearly followed the movements involved, demonstrating both Nyx's extraordinary mobility and her ability to manipulate the night-associated space surrounding other beings. Her familiarity with her children did not prevent her from dismissing their attempts to interfere with the expedition, although she treated the confrontation with the casual authority of a mother correcting troublesome descendants rather than that of an enemy seeking their deaths.[15]
The group eventually entered the territory of [[Hecate]], the Goddess of Magic. Nyx detected an enormous hidden structure beneath the region and attempted to examine it through her Divinity, only to discover that Hecate had developed specialized countermeasures capable of interfering with the perception of Original [[Primordial Gods]]. Hecate explained that she had spent years studying methods of resisting powerful beings such as Nyx and Erebus, whose ability to intrude into other Divine territories made them especially troublesome neighbors. Nyx acknowledged the danger of provoking someone who had devoted so much effort to countering her abilities and chose not to test the defenses unnecessarily.[19]
The expedition eventually brought Nyx directly into conflict with Erebus. She detected his presence before the others and ordered him to reveal himself, recognizing that her former husband could not conceal himself from her indefinitely. Erebus responded by covering the surrounding region in primordial Darkness, forcing Nyx and Aphrodite to protect themselves through their respective Divinities and dispel the influence affecting them. The confrontation revealed that the conflict involving Olympus had become far more dangerous than an ordinary dispute among its rulers.[9]
Erebus subsequently employed a fragment of [[Yggdrasil]] to seal and drain Nyx's Primordial power. Because Original Primordial Gods were connected to the fundamental existence of their Pantheon, a material originating from the World Tree associated with its foundation could be used to interfere with powers that ordinary sealing techniques would struggle to restrain. Nyx immediately recognized the artifact and understood that it had originally been intended for emergencies involving enemies of the Pantheon, making its use against her another demonstration of Erebus's betrayal.[9]
The confrontation escalated into a battle involving Victor and several Original Primordial Gods. Erebus, [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] and [[Nocturnus]] employed extraordinary powers associated with Darkness, Gravity and the Void in an attempt to overcome Victor, whose own existence was undergoing further Draconic development. Nyx continued opposing Erebus despite the danger, eventually intervening when her former husband attempted to restrict Victor through an enormous gravitational field. She appeared beside Erebus through the starry night, seized him and dragged him away, disrupting the attack and allowing Victor to recover his freedom of movement.[20]
The battle also revealed the existence of Nocturnus, the Primordial God of the Void and the son of Erebus and a foreign Primordial Goddess. Nyx was shocked to discover that her former husband had established another Divine lineage beyond their marriage, particularly because she had not expected his betrayal to extend to fathering a child with a Goddess belonging to another Pantheon. The revelation caused her to abandon much of her usual restraint, manifesting a Divine Raiment resembling the starry night and a large black scythe while directing her anger toward the individuals responsible.[5]
Nyx's fury was accompanied by the revelation of additional Divine associations beyond her principal authority over Night and Concealment. The narration identified her as possessing lesser connections to Death, Mystery and Terror, emphasizing that her existence was considerably more dangerous than that of an ordinary Goddess whose power consisted exclusively of stealth or Darkness. Her willingness to openly confront Erebus demonstrated the distinction between the calculating Mother of Concealment and the vengeful Primordial Goddess capable of personally entering a battle involving the oldest Greek Gods.[5]
Following Victor's victory, Erebus lost much of the support previously provided by the Greek Pantheon as Victor acquired control over Olympus's fundamental authority. When Erebus became distracted by the destruction of Nocturnus, Nyx struck him with her scythe, piercing his body and throwing him before Victor. She explained that she wanted answers concerning the betrayal that had destroyed their relationship rather than simply prolonging his suffering through torture.[10]
Victor subsequently killed Erebus and absorbed his existence, obtaining access to the memories associated with the former Primordial God. He shared the relevant information with Nyx, allowing her to understand the circumstances surrounding Erebus's infidelity and involvement in the schemes connected to [[Diablo]]. The memories revealed that Erebus had pursued another woman after losing interest in Nyx and had subsequently become involved in plans to establish a new Pantheon composed of negative supernatural beings from different civilizations. His cooperation with the conspiracy was therefore driven by both personal desire and the pursuit of greater power.[10]
The conclusion of the conflict ended Nyx's relationship with the old Greek order in its previous form. Although the destruction of Erebus did not erase the resentment she had accumulated, the information Victor provided removed much of the uncertainty surrounding his betrayal. Nyx remained among the surviving Primordial Gods aligned with Victor, whose new civilization offered the possibility of establishing a future disconnected from the political failures of Olympus.[10]
Nyx subsequently participated in the construction of Victor's new planetary home. When Victor required an enormous quantity of suitable material, Nyx extended her perception beyond the planet and into space, locating a massive asteroid containing useful substances. She then shared her perception with Natalia, allowing the Space specialist to identify the distant object and create a portal capable of transporting it toward the developing world.[11]
During the construction process, Nyx spread a supernatural starry night across the enormous object, helping isolate and compress the material while preventing the escape of the Energy required for the transformation. Her work complemented [[Gaia]]'s control over the planet's physical structure, Natalia's Spatial abilities, Roxanne's Energy and Jeanne's Primordial power. The collaboration demonstrated that Nyx's authority over Night possessed applications far beyond concealment, allowing her to contribute to the creation and stabilization of an enormous planetary environment.[11]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Following the collapse of the [[Greek Pantheon]], Nyx increasingly served as a reconnaissance and intelligence specialist within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s growing faction. Her ancient experience and Primordial perception became especially valuable as the group investigated civilizations and supernatural beings originating beyond the familiar territories of Earth and [[Nightingale]].[21]
The discovery of extraterrestrial civilizations also revealed important limitations in the assumptions traditionally held by the Gods concerning Divine Concepts. Records from Velnorah's former civilization described beings possessing powers resembling Night and Darkness, despite Nyx never having perceived competition from them for her own Divine authorities. Victor concluded that conceptual monopolies associated with Gods could be limited by the jurisdiction of their Pantheon or Sector rather than automatically extending throughout all of Creation. Nyx's existence therefore demonstrated the distinction between possessing a powerful local Divine authority and exercising universal dominion over every manifestation of the corresponding Concept.[21]
Nyx was subsequently selected for an operation involving the capture of [[Agares]], a Demon whose ability to move through another plane made him extraordinarily difficult to pursue through conventional means. Victor wanted the target captured alive because his unusual abilities possessed considerable value for further investigation. Nyx participated alongside [[Oda Nobunaga]], members of the [[Blank Clan]] and Zaladrac, whose control over Space could prevent Agares from escaping through his usual method of transportation.[22][23]
During the mission, Nyx's authority over Night provided environmental advantages for the operatives acting beneath its influence. Nocturnal beings could benefit from the supernatural conditions she created, while her concealment and perception made it difficult for the target to determine the full extent of the surrounding operation. Her participation demonstrated why Victor considered her particularly suited to missions in which ordinary strength was less useful than the ability to control information, isolate an opponent and prevent escape.[22][23]
When Victor began transforming the women of his family into genuine Dragons, Nyx voluntarily accepted the opportunity. Her previous existence as an Original Primordial Goddess was not erased; instead, the transformation reconstructed her physical and spiritual foundation through the superior characteristics of the Blood Dragon lineage while preserving her accumulated Divinities, memories and individual identity. Nyx simultaneously entered a [[Soul Marriage]] with Victor, formally becoming one of his wives and acquiring the Elderblood name.[3]
Her transformation established a new stage in her existence. Nyx was no longer solely an ancient Greek Goddess whose continued power depended upon the supernatural structure of her original Pantheon. She had become a Primordial Dragon Goddess possessing the racial foundation of a genuine True Dragon while retaining the Divine authorities accumulated throughout her earlier life.[3]
During the expedition into Helheim, Nyx demonstrated the extraordinary sensory applications of her authority through Dark Pulse. She spread Darkness throughout the dimension and utilized it as a medium for observation, allowing her to examine enormous distances and gather information about the territory without physically visiting every location. The technique illustrated the value of her powers for reconnaissance operations in unfamiliar supernatural dimensions.[24]
Nyx later developed and demonstrated Eternal Night, a technique associated with the ancient fear of nights without stars. During operations involving the Dwarves following Ragnarök, she used the technique to force them to experience overwhelming nightmares drawn from their deepest fears. The psychological consequences destroyed much of their resistance and compelled cooperation without requiring prolonged conventional torture. The ability demonstrated that Nyx's association with Night could affect the minds and emotions of other beings rather than operating exclusively through Darkness, concealment or physical attacks.[25]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Following the extended periods of accelerated development experienced by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family, Nyx became a permanent member of the Elderblood household rather than remaining a Primordial ally attached to his faction. Her marriage to Victor eventually produced a daughter, [[Nikolina Nia Nefela Elderblood]], who belonged to the new generation of extraordinarily powerful children born from the union of Victor's Draconic existence and the Divine characteristics of his wives.[26][4]
Nikolina's inherited supernatural characteristics were associated with Stars, distinguishing her from a simple reproduction of Nyx's Divinity over Night. The child's development demonstrated that the descendants of Victor and his wives did not necessarily inherit identical versions of their mothers' principal Divine authorities, instead acquiring individual characteristics shaped by the interaction between their parents' supernatural existences.[4]
Nyx continued participating in the expansion and administration of Victor's civilization, particularly through her experience in reconnaissance and supernatural information gathering. Her expertise was valuable because many of the threats confronting the growing Empire possessed unusual methods of concealment, interdimensional movement or resistance to ordinary supernatural observation. Having spent an enormous portion of her life manipulating information among the Greek Gods, Nyx was well prepared to assist a civilization whose operations increasingly extended across multiple worlds and supernatural jurisdictions.[24][2]
Her Divine Blessing also became useful within the Religion of the Blood God. The Battle Sisters responsible for protecting important religious institutions and carrying out missions on behalf of the Religion received Blessings associated with several of Victor's wives, including [[Aphrodite]], [[Scathach]] and Nyx. These arrangements reflected the incorporation of the family's individual Divinities into the defensive and administrative institutions supporting the Empire.[27]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
During the accelerated development of the [[Elderblood Empire]], Nyx's responsibilities were formalized through the creation of specialized Imperial Legions. The Third Legion, the Black Hand of the Emperor, was established by Nyx, Kaguya and [[Persephone]] as the organization responsible for much of the Empire's intelligence gathering, espionage, infiltration and psychological warfare. Unlike ordinary military formations intended to capture territory through direct assaults, the Black Hand specialized in operations requiring secrecy, deception, counterintelligence and the manipulation of enemy institutions.[2]
The Legion's duties included hunting traitors, destabilizing hostile organizations and performing the covert actions necessary to protect Imperial interests. Its personnel included specialized operatives capable of utilizing Shadows, supernatural communication, Shapeshifting and other abilities valuable for intelligence operations. Kaguya became its principal commander, while Nyx was entrusted with the authority to assume command when Kaguya was unavailable.[2]
The appointment reflected Nyx's suitability for an intelligence organization operating on an interstellar scale. Her Concealment could obscure the existence and location of operatives, while her Primordial perception allowed her to identify information that ordinary surveillance methods would struggle to detect. Her authority over Night also provided extraordinary mobility through darkened environments, making her one of the few individuals whose personal abilities could support operations beyond the reach of conventional intelligence networks.[2][12]
Nyx also participated in discussions concerning the development of the Empire's afterlife infrastructure. As the Imperial population expanded, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wives recognized that the existing arrangements for processing Souls would eventually become insufficient for a civilization containing enormous numbers of supernatural beings. During planning discussions, Nyx and the other Goddesses considered how to structure the afterlife, while Hela proposed the creation of a separate realm in which worthy Souls could rest before continuing their journeys. The discussion reflected Nyx's familiarity with the older Greek afterlife system and her involvement in the supernatural institutions required by the Empire.[28]
Victor later created an enormous world intended to preserve Greek culture within the Empire. The planet allowed members of his family originating from Greece to establish territories, temples and settlements reflecting their original civilization without restoring the failed political structure of [[Olympus]]. Nyx and [[Gaia]] initially attempted to appear indifferent to the gift, but abandoned their pretense once Victor completed the project. Both women gratefully acknowledged his efforts before leaving to establish their preferred territories upon the new world.[29]
Nyx and Kaguya continued serving as the principal supernatural specialists associated with Imperial intelligence. During an incident involving the daughters of the Elderblood family, Kaguya explained the similarity between their methods of movement: she could utilize Shadows as a medium for extraordinary mobility, while Nyx could move through the dark night sky to reach the locations she desired. The comparison was directly connected to their responsibilities within the Empire, since their powers allowed them to appear, investigate and operate through environments inaccessible to ordinary agents.[12]
Nyx's authority and personal experience also remained relevant within the household itself. During the confrontation between Violet and several of Victor's daughters, Nyx accompanied Kaguya and Ruby while the younger generation challenged the Empress's authority. Rather than immediately intervening on Violet's behalf, Nyx supported the arrangement Victor had established and responded to the situation with amusement, demonstrating the comfortable familiarity she had developed with the family's internal rivalries.[12]
During a later family gathering, Nyx appeared alongside Hela, Bruna, Eve, Mizuki and Violet as the women observed the increasingly complicated activities involving Victor and the younger members of the family. Their interactions emphasized that the Empire's political and Divine institutions remained closely intertwined with the personal relationships of Victor's household, whose members were simultaneously powerful rulers, military commanders and mothers.[30]
Nyx subsequently attended a grand celebration held in an extraordinary environment constructed through Victor's control over celestial structures and supernatural matter. The decorations and furniture incorporated materials associated with stars, planets and nebulae, while the women received garments fashioned from different phenomena of the Cosmos. Nyx's dress was created from the starry night, reflecting her ancient Divinity in a form made possible by the extraordinary abilities of Victor's evolved existence.[13]
By this period, Nyx's role had changed fundamentally from that of an isolated Primordial Goddess whose interests were centered upon Greek politics. She had become a wife of the God Emperor, mother of one of his daughters, one of the Empire's principal intelligence leaders and a member of a Divine civilization whose reach extended far beyond her original Pantheon.[2][12][13]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
Nyx's connection with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] began before their personal relationship developed. During his earlier growth, Victor acquired several Divine Blessings associated with extraordinarily powerful beings, including Nyx and [[Gaia]]. [[Aphrodite]] reacted with considerable concern upon discovering these influences because Divine Blessings could establish connections between a God and a recipient, potentially allowing the Divine benefactor to observe or influence circumstances surrounding the individual. The existence of Nyx's Blessing therefore represented an early point of contact between Victor and the ancient Greek Primordial Goddess.[31]
Nyx subsequently recognized that Victor possessed something far more significant than ordinary supernatural potential. His connection to a World Tree and the possibility of establishing a new Pantheon offered a future beyond the political structure that had failed her, particularly after the deterioration of her marriage to [[Erebus]]. She initially approached Victor's faction through political calculation, but her willingness to cooperate increased as his abilities developed and the conflict surrounding [[Olympus]] exposed the extraordinary danger posed by the ancient conspirators.[6][1][5]
Their relationship became particularly significant during the final Greek conflict. Nyx opposed Erebus and assisted the forces aligned with Victor, while Victor ultimately killed her former husband and obtained the information necessary to explain his betrayal. By sharing Erebus's memories with Nyx, Victor provided answers to questions that had remained unresolved throughout the collapse of their marriage. The event strengthened their connection and allowed Nyx to move beyond the political and personal obligations associated with her former relationship.[10]
Nyx subsequently joined Victor's faction and accepted transformation into a Blood Dragon. Their relationship developed into a [[Soul Marriage]], making her one of his wives and a permanent member of Clan Elderblood. Her acceptance of the transformation reflected considerable trust because it involved the reconstruction of an existence that had remained fundamentally Primordial for an enormous period of time. Victor preserved her ancient characteristics rather than replacing them with a standardized Draconic identity, allowing Nyx to retain the Divinities and memories that defined her.[3]
Their marriage eventually produced [[Nikolina Nia Nefela Elderblood]], a daughter whose powers were associated with Stars. Nyx continued participating in Victor's affairs after becoming a mother, particularly through her responsibilities within the Empire's intelligence services. Victor entrusted her with senior authority in the Black Hand, recognizing that her combination of concealment, reconnaissance and ancient political experience made her extraordinarily valuable.[4][2]
By the Imperial era, Nyx openly displayed affection toward Victor and referred to him with the familiarity expected of his wives. Her relationship with him combined loyalty, amusement and a willingness to employ her abilities for the benefit of their family and Empire, while preserving the independent personality and manipulative intelligence that characterized her original existence.[12]

#### Erebus
[[Erebus]] was Nyx's brother, former husband and Primordial counterpart. Whereas Nyx possessed the Divinities of Night and Concealment, Erebus represented the more fundamental Concept of primordial Darkness. Their authorities overlapped without being identical, allowing the two to exist as distinct Original [[Primordial Gods]] whose powers governed related supernatural phenomena.[8][9]
Their marriage produced numerous Divine descendants, including [[Thanatos]], [[Hypnos]] and [[Nemesis]]. However, Erebus eventually betrayed Nyx by pursuing a relationship with a foreign Primordial Goddess. The betrayal contributed to their separation and caused Nyx to distance herself from their children, while her desire to escape the failed relationship became one of the motivations behind her interest in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s capacity to establish a new Pantheon.[5]
The discovery of [[Nocturnus]] made the betrayal considerably more painful. Nyx had not expected Erebus to father a child outside their Pantheon, and learning that the resulting being possessed an extraordinary Void-related existence intensified her resentment. She eventually confronted Erebus during the collapse of [[Olympus]], intervening directly in his battle against Victor and later striking him with her scythe when he became distracted.[5][20][10]
After Victor killed Erebus, he provided Nyx with the memories necessary to understand the circumstances surrounding his infidelity and involvement in the conspiracy associated with [[Diablo]]. Nyx acknowledged the information and moved forward with Victor's faction, although the resentment accumulated throughout their failed marriage remained an important part of her history.[10]

#### Persephone Elderblood
Nyx's relationship with [[Persephone]] began as one of political manipulation. Recognizing the vulnerability of the [[Greek Underworld]] following [[Hades]]'s departure, Nyx selected Persephone as the most suitable candidate to inherit its legitimate authority. She understood that Persephone's emotional attachments would make the Ruler's trial extraordinarily difficult and deliberately used that vulnerability to produce a sovereign capable of defending Greek territory.[6][14]
The plan succeeded in establishing a powerful Underworld ruler but failed in Nyx's expectation that Persephone would remain manageable. Persephone emerged from the trial with authority comparable to that of a God-King within her realm and immediately confronted Nyx for manipulating her. Their conflict culminated in Persephone severing one of Nyx's arms and banishing her from the Underworld, demonstrating the extraordinary power associated with the legitimate Ruler's position.[7]
Despite their earlier hostility, the two later cooperated through their association with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Persephone became one of the women connected to his family, while Nyx joined his faction and eventually married him. Their relationship consequently developed within a broader political and familial structure rather than continuing exclusively through the antagonism created by the Underworld succession crisis.[3]

#### Thanatos, Hypnos and Nemesis
[[Thanatos]], [[Hypnos]] and [[Nemesis]] are among Nyx's most prominent surviving children from her marriage with [[Erebus]]. Each possesses Divine characteristics associated with fundamental aspects of existence, including Death, Sleep and Vengeance. Their supernatural status is significant because they belong to the older generations of Greek Gods whose abilities differ considerably from those of ordinary Olympian descendants.[15][5]
The three demonstrated their familiarity with Nyx's powers during the events surrounding [[Persephone]]'s ascension and the later expedition into the [[Greek Underworld]]. Hypnos understood that Nyx's Concealment could interfere with future-oriented observation and warned Thanatos that requesting assistance from [[Hecate]] would not necessarily reveal information Nyx wished to hide. Their knowledge reflected the extraordinary difficulty of concealing anything from a mother whose Divine authority governed the very principle of Concealment.[7]
During the Underworld expedition, Thanatos attempted to observe Nyx's group while relying upon his supernatural concealment, but Nyx detected him almost immediately. The confrontation escalated when [[Scathach]] attacked Thanatos, prompting Nyx to explain the limitations of conventional anti-God techniques against an embodiment of Death. Nyx subsequently concealed her children beneath a night-sky veil and transported them to Persephone's castle, treating their intervention as an inconvenience rather than a genuine threat.[15]
Although Nyx had previously distanced herself from her children following Erebus's betrayal, these events demonstrate that she retained considerable knowledge of their abilities and continued exercising an authority they recognized instinctively.[5]

#### Gaia Elderblood
[[Gaia]] Elderblood is Nyx's fellow Original Primordial Goddess and one of the few surviving members of the oldest Greek Divine generation. The two possess considerably different personalities: Gaia's emotional life and priorities are strongly connected to motherhood, her descendants and the natural world, whereas Nyx historically approached supernatural society through secrecy, observation and political manipulation.[1][5]
Despite their differences, both eventually survived the destruction of the old Greek order and joined [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family. Their transformations into Primordial Dragon Goddesses preserved their individual Divine characteristics while placing them within the same new racial and familial foundation. The two consequently became part of the extraordinary collection of ancient Gods who accepted Victor's authority and contributed their powers to his growing civilization.[3]
During the Imperial era, Victor created a new world intended to preserve Greek culture and provide territories for the former members of the Pantheon. Nyx and Gaia initially attempted to conceal their enthusiasm but quickly abandoned the pretense once the planet was completed, affectionately thanking Victor before departing to establish their own temples and domains.[29]

#### Kaguya Elderblood Blank
[[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]] is Nyx's principal counterpart within the intelligence structure of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Both possess extraordinary supernatural abilities particularly suited to covert operations, although their powers originate from different racial and conceptual foundations. Kaguya specializes in Shadows and the techniques associated with her former Vampire and [[Blank Clan]] heritage, whereas Nyx possesses the ancient Primordial authorities of Night and Concealment.[2][12]
The two helped establish the Third Imperial Legion alongside [[Persephone]], creating an organization responsible for intelligence gathering, espionage, infiltration and psychological warfare. Kaguya became its principal commander, while Nyx was designated to assume command whenever Kaguya was unavailable. Their cooperation demonstrates Nyx's willingness to operate within an established command hierarchy rather than insisting upon absolute control simply because of her Primordial status.[2]
Their supernatural abilities are particularly complementary. Kaguya can move through Shadows and exploit the Darkness connected to her authority, while Nyx can utilize the dark night sky as a medium for movement and observation. These characteristics provide the Empire with extraordinary covert mobility and help explain why the two are recognized as its principal intelligence specialists.[12]

#### Nikolina Nia Nefela Elderblood
[[Nikolina Nia Nefela Elderblood]] is the daughter of Nyx and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Her birth occurred after the extensive periods of accelerated development experienced by the Elderblood family, making her part of a generation whose supernatural potential greatly exceeds that of ordinary Divine descendants.[26][4]
Nikolina inherited an affinity associated with Stars, reflecting a relationship with celestial phenomena distinct from the direct reproduction of Nyx's Divinity over Night. Her existence also established Nyx as one of the mothers of the rapidly expanding Elderblood dynasty, adding a personal responsibility beyond the political and intelligence duties that had occupied much of her ancient life.[4]

### Abilities

#### Skills and Abilities
Nyx Elderblood is an Original Primordial Goddess of the [[Greek Pantheon]], the primordial Goddess of Night, the Mother of Concealment, and one of the oldest Divine beings originating from the Greek supernatural order. Her existence belongs to the First Generation of [[Primordial Gods]], placing her among the foundational Divine beings whose powers are connected to the fundamental Concepts governing their Pantheon. Her principal Divine authorities are Night and Concealment, while Darkness forms an important component of her Night-related abilities. She also possesses lesser Divine associations with Death, Mystery and Terror, although these secondary Concepts should not be treated as equivalent to the complete authorities of Gods specializing exclusively in those principles.[1][2][3]
Nyx's greatest advantage lies in the extraordinary sophistication of her Divine authorities rather than conventional physical destruction. Through Concealment, she can hide herself, other beings, entire locations and identifying information from physical and supernatural perception. Her powers can interfere with Divine senses, obscure the information needed to locate a target and prevent certain forms of future-oriented observation from discovering what she wishes to conceal. As the Mother of Night, Nyx also possesses a separate plane of existence in which her presence becomes extraordinarily difficult for weaker beings to perceive, with the novel explaining that only beings possessing a level of existence comparable to the Original Greek Primordial Gods could ordinarily detect her when she concealed herself completely.[1][4][2]
Her authority over Night provides additional applications involving environmental manipulation, Darkness, supernatural transportation, reconnaissance and the empowerment of nocturnal beings. Nyx can cover enormous celestial structures in supernatural Night, use the darkness of her environment to detect distant targets and exploit the night sky as a medium for movement. Her sensory capabilities are similarly extraordinary, allowing her to inspect hidden magical structures, observe astronomical distances and share her supernatural perception directly with another individual. During the construction of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s new planetary home, Nyx's perception located a distant asteroid while her Starry Night helped contain and compress the enormous quantity of matter and Energy involved in creating the world.[5][6]
Nyx's powers developed further after she entered Victor Elderblood's faction. Victor improved the efficiency of her Night-related abilities through her connection to his [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]], allowing her to utilize additional supernatural Energy and develop a level of concealment capable of defeating the perception of beings who would previously have been more difficult to deceive. During the confrontation with the [[Egyptian Pantheon]], the narration described her improved concealment as capable of making her effectively cease to exist if she wished, leaving only exceptionally developed perception or suitable Runic countermeasures as reliable methods of discovering her. This advancement represents a major improvement over her already extraordinary Primordial Concealment, although the precise mechanism of this nonexistence-like state was not individually demonstrated through a detailed transformation sequence.[7]
Following her [[Soul Marriage]] with Victor, Nyx accepted transformation into a genuine Blood Dragon. Her Original Primordial Goddess existence was preserved rather than discarded, allowing her previous Divinities, accumulated power, personality and supernatural characteristics to coexist with a new True Dragon body and Soul. She consequently became a Primordial Dragon Goddess, combining her ancient conceptual authorities with the vastly superior racial foundation associated with Victor's Draconic lineage.[8]
Nyx eventually became one of the principal intelligence leaders of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Alongside [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]] and [[Persephone Elderblood]], she helped establish the Third Imperial Legion, the Black Hand of the Emperor, responsible for intelligence gathering, espionage, infiltration, counterintelligence and psychological warfare. Kaguya serves as the Legion's principal commander, while Nyx is authorized to act in her place. Their extraordinary mobility through Night and Shadows makes them particularly suited to intelligence operations on an Imperial scale.[9][10]

#### Racial and Existential Physiology

##### Primordial Dragon Goddess Physiology
Nyx currently possesses the combined characteristics of an Original Primordial Goddess and a genuine Blood Dragon. Her Draconic transformation reconstructed her biological and spiritual foundation without removing the Divine authorities associated with her ancient existence. Unlike an ordinary God who obtains Draconic characteristics through a temporary enhancement, Nyx possesses the actual racial foundation of a True Dragon while retaining the Primordial Divinities that defined her original state.[8]
- Primordial Blood Dragon Physiology: Nyx possesses the physical, spiritual and supernatural foundation of a genuine Blood Dragon while preserving her Original Primordial Goddess existence. Her transformation integrated the characteristics of her former Divine state into her new Draconic foundation.[8]
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics: Nyx possesses extraordinary strength, speed, durability, agility, reflexes and stamina. Her ancient Divine constitution already surpassed that of ordinary supernatural beings, while the True Dragon transformation provided a superior physical foundation for her continued development.[11][8]
- Enhanced Strength: Her Draconic physiology possesses the immense physical strength characteristic of genuine [[True Dragons]]. This supplements the supernatural power she previously possessed as an Original Primordial Goddess, although her individual feats remain more extensively demonstrated through Divine abilities than direct physical combat.[8]
- Enhanced Durability: Nyx possesses the exceptionally resilient body of a Blood Dragon in addition to the extraordinary endurance associated with an Original Primordial Goddess. The transformation provides a stronger physical foundation without eliminating the significance of her original Divine protections.[8]
- Enhanced Speed and Reflexes: Nyx possesses supernatural movement and reaction capabilities. Even before Dragonization, her applications of Night allowed her to relocate targets rapidly enough that only observers possessing exceptional perception could clearly follow the process.[12][8]
- Enhanced Senses: Nyx's Draconic physiology supplements her already extraordinary Primordial perception. Her principal sensory feats nevertheless originate from her Divine existence and specialized applications of Night and Concealment.[6][8][13]
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Nyx possessed powerful regenerative capabilities before becoming a Dragon. When [[Persephone]] severed her arm, Nyx recovered the limb through Telekinesis and reattached it without displaying concern that the injury would cause lasting damage. Her later Dragonization provided an additional Draconic foundation for physical recovery.[1][8]
- Supernatural Longevity: Nyx has existed since the earliest generations of the [[Greek Pantheon]] and is not subject to the ordinary biological lifespan of mortal beings. Her Blood Dragon transformation further elevated the nature of her physical existence.[11][8]
- True Dragon Transformation: Nyx possesses genuine Draconic physiology rather than merely imitating the characteristics of a Dragon. Her transformation preserved the individual supernatural qualities accumulated throughout her existence.[8]
- Humanoid Form: Nyx retains the ability to appear in the humanoid form associated with her identity as the Primordial Goddess of Night despite possessing the physiology of a True Dragon.[8]
- [[Draconic Energy]]: Nyx's current body possesses the Energy-producing and circulating foundation associated with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood Dragon lineage. Her former Divine abilities remain compatible with her transformed existence.[8]
- [[Dragon Heart]]: As a genuine True Dragon, Nyx possesses the Draconic foundation associated with the Dragon Heart. Its specific individual properties have not been separately distinguished from those of Victor's other transformed wives.[8]
- Draconic Soul: Nyx's Soul was reconstructed together with her body during Dragonization, preserving her previous Divine identity while integrating the characteristics of a genuine Dragon existence.[8]
- Draconic Adaptability: Nyx's previous abilities over Night, Concealment and associated Divine phenomena remained functional through her transformation. Victor's method preserved and elevated the characteristics of individual recipients instead of eliminating their former supernatural identities.[8]
- Flight: Nyx possesses supernatural aerial mobility and demonstrated the ability to move freely through the air before her transformation into a True Dragon.[14][8]
- Draconic Language Compatibility: Nyx possesses the racial foundation of a True Dragon, whose supernatural language allows the expression of [[Will Solace|Will]] and Energy through the principles associated with Draconic Runes. However, compatibility with the language does not independently establish that Nyx has mastered every specialized Runic technique.[15][16]
Nyx possesses a genuine True Dragon body, but the novel has not individually established the precise characteristics of a unique Dragon Breath, a named Draconic Rune technique or every advanced racial ability belonging specifically to her. General racial compatibility should be distinguished from documented personal mastery.

##### Original Primordial Goddess Physiology
Before becoming a Blood Dragon, Nyx belonged to the First Generation of the Original Greek [[Primordial Gods]]. Her existence was fundamentally different from that of ordinary later-generation Gods because her principal Divinities formed part of the supernatural foundations of the [[Greek Pantheon]]. This ancient conceptual relationship granted her extraordinary Divine power, exceptional resistance to conventional methods of killing Gods and an independent domain rooted in her own existence.[11][2]
- Original Primordial God Physiology: Nyx possesses the supernatural characteristics of one of the oldest Divine beings belonging to the Greek Pantheon. Her existence is connected to fundamental Divine Concepts rather than depending solely upon the accumulated power of a later-generation God.[11][2]
- Conceptual Divine Existence: Nyx's existence is intimately associated with Night and Concealment. These authorities define her Primordial identity and determine many of the supernatural phenomena she can manifest through her [[Will Solace|Will]].[1][3]
- Divine Authority: Nyx can impose phenomena associated with her Divinities upon the surrounding Reality. Her powers are not restricted to conventional elemental manipulation because they govern supernatural principles associated with Night and Concealment.[1][2]
- Primordial [[Divine Energy]]: Nyx possesses the exceptional Divine Energy of an Original Primordial Goddess, allowing her to manifest large-scale supernatural effects and maintain extremely sophisticated abilities.[11][3]
- [[Divine Presence]]: Nyx can manifest the supernatural pressure associated with her Primordial existence. During the final Greek conflict, she released more of her true power and assumed an imposing appearance reflecting the Night itself.[3]
- Divine Regeneration: Nyx can recover from severe physical injuries. The severing of her arm by [[Persephone]] did not produce lasting disability, and Nyx retrieved and reattached the limb through her supernatural powers.[1]
- Primordial Divine [[Immortality]]: Nyx possesses the exceptional existential resilience associated with Original Primordial Gods. Conventional Greek Death authority and ordinary physical damage cannot automatically destroy a being whose existence is supported by the foundational Divinities of the Pantheon.[1][2]
- Personal Primordial Territory: Nyx possesses a domain within the [[Greek Underworld]] whose supernatural foundation belongs directly to her rather than merely functioning as an administrative region of the ordinary afterlife system.[2]
- Divine Blessing Bestowal: Nyx can establish persistent supernatural connections with other beings through Divine Blessings. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] possessed a Blessing associated with Nyx during his earlier development.[17]
- Divine Raiment: Nyx can manifest clothing resembling a star-filled night when releasing a greater portion of her Primordial nature.[3]
- Divine Weapon Manifestation: Nyx can manifest a large black scythe while displaying her Primordial Divine Raiment.[3]

Nyx's Divinities should not be treated as a universal monopoly over every occurrence of Night and Darkness throughout Creation. Records concerning beings from other Sectors demonstrated that entities could possess comparable supernatural abilities without Nyx perceiving them as competing for the same Divine position. This distinction concerns the jurisdiction and structure of Divine Concepts across different cosmic regions rather than diminishing the power of Nyx's authority within her own supernatural foundation.[21]

#### Night Divinity
Night is one of Nyx's principal Divine Concepts and the defining characteristic of her Primordial identity. Her authority extends beyond simply producing Darkness, allowing her to impose supernatural nocturnal conditions, manifest star-filled skies and use Night as an environmental medium for perception, concealment and mobility. Its applications became especially evident during the construction of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s planetary home and later operations in which her Night empowered nocturnal beings.[6][18]
- Night Manipulation: Nyx can generate, spread and manipulate supernatural Night through her principal Divine authority. This ability operates through the Concept associated with her existence rather than functioning exclusively as ordinary Darkness manipulation.[3][6]
- Night Manifestation: Nyx can impose supernatural Night upon an environment, producing nocturnal conditions through her Divinity rather than requiring the natural astronomical arrival of nightfall.[6]
- Artificial Night Creation: Nyx can create Night over a celestial body undergoing construction. Her Starry Night covered the enormous asteroid that Victor and his family were transforming into a new planet.[6]
- Environmental Manipulation: Nyx can alter the supernatural conditions of an area through her Night authority, changing the environment in ways that favor her abilities and those of beings associated with nocturnal existence.[6][18]
- Large-Scale Night Generation: Nyx can extend her Night across enormous physical structures, including planetary material. During the construction project, the manifestation was subsequently used to contain and compress the developing planet.[6]
- Nocturnal Empowerment: Nyx can strengthen beings naturally associated with the night. During the operation against [[Agares]], she invoked her Starry Night and empowered the nocturnal creatures participating in the confrontation.[18]
- Night-Based Energy Manipulation: Nyx can manifest supernatural Energy associated with her Night, including dark manifestations used for transportation, concealment and environmental effects.[14][6]
- Night-Sky Transportation: Nyx can use the dark night sky as a medium for extraordinary movement. Kaguya explicitly compared this ability with her own use of Shadows during the Imperial era.[10]
- Night-Associated Domain: Nyx possesses a personal Primordial territory associated with her existence within the [[Greek Underworld]]. Her Night and Concealment are especially effective within this environment.[2][12]
- Night-Based Sensory Applications: Nyx can utilize the Darkness associated with Night as a means of acquiring information. Dark Pulse demonstrates the ability to spread her influence throughout an enormous environment and observe distant regions.[13]
- Night and Concealment Synergy: Nyx's principal Divinities complement one another, allowing her to establish environments in which her presence and the activities of other beings become extremely difficult to detect.[1][2]

##### Starry Night
Starry Night is one of Nyx's most significant demonstrated applications of Night. During the construction of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s new planetary home, Nyx was responsible for covering the enormous asteroid used as the project's foundation in supernatural Night. The manifestation subsequently became part of a coordinated process involving [[Gaia]]'s control over the planetary structure, Natalia's Spatial abilities, Roxanne's Energy and Jeanne's Primordial power.[6]
Nyx first covered the asteroid in a star-filled night before the other women began enriching and restructuring the material. Once the developing world had acquired its initial supernatural foundation, Nyx used the Starry Night to compress the enormous quantity of matter while helping prevent the leakage of Energy. Natalia simultaneously employed Space Manipulation to reinforce the isolation, allowing the two women to maintain the conditions necessary for Gaia and Victor to reshape the planet.[6]
- Starry Night Manifestation: Nyx can cover an enormous celestial body in supernatural Night resembling a star-filled sky.[6]
- Planetary-Scale Environmental Manipulation: Her Starry Night can envelop and affect matter on the scale of the asteroid being transformed into a planet.[6]
- Large-Scale Compression: Nyx used her Starry Night to compress the developing planetary structure during the construction process.[6]
- Energy Containment: Her Night contributed to preventing the enormous quantities of supernatural Energy circulating through the new planet from leaking into the surrounding environment.[6]
- Environmental Isolation: Nyx's authority helped maintain the stability of the developing planet while Gaia restructured its interior.[6]
- Cooperative Spatial Isolation: Natalia reinforced Nyx's work through Space Manipulation, ensuring that the developing world's Energy remained contained while its internal structure was altered.[6]
- Planetary Construction Support: Nyx's Night formed part of the combined Divine effort required to create a functioning planetary environment rather than merely a large sphere of ordinary rock.[6]

##### Nocturnal Empowerment
Nyx can use her Night to strengthen supernatural beings whose racial or individual characteristics are naturally compatible with nocturnal conditions. This effect was demonstrated during the operation to capture [[Agares]], when Nyx covered the surrounding area in Starry Night and empowered the nocturnal beings participating in the confrontation.[18]
- Power Bestowal: Nyx can provide a supernatural enhancement to beings naturally associated with Night.[18]
- Conditional Empowerment: The effect is associated with the beneficiaries' nocturnal nature and Nyx's manifestation of Night rather than functioning as a universal enhancement automatically granted to every ally.[18]
- Area-Based Empowerment: Nyx can strengthen several compatible beings within the affected area simultaneously.[18]
- Battlefield Support: The enhancement allows Nyx to contribute to a group confrontation without requiring her to perform every attack personally.[18]

#### Darkness Manipulation
Darkness forms an important component of Nyx's authority over Night. Although the two phenomena are closely connected, Nyx's principal Concept differs from the fundamental primordial Darkness once governed by [[Erebus]]. During their confrontation, the distinction became significant because Erebus could impose Darkness through his own authority, while Nyx could use her Night-related power to oppose and disperse its influence around herself.[19]
- Darkness Manipulation: Nyx can generate and control supernatural Darkness through her authority over Night.[14][19]
- Darkness Generation: Nyx can produce manifestations of dark [[Divine Energy]] capable of spreading through an environment or surrounding other beings.[14][13]
- Dark Energy Manifestation: Nyx's power can appear as active supernatural Energy rather than merely an absence of visible Light. Her influence was recognized through the dark Energy accompanying the relocation of Greek Goddesses.[14]
- Darkness Projection: Nyx can release her dark power into the surrounding environment. Dark Pulse demonstrates a controlled discharge that expands through an enormous dimension.[13]
- Darkness Dispersal: Nyx can oppose foreign Darkness through her Divine authority. During the confrontation with Erebus, she and [[Aphrodite]] used their powers to disperse the Darkness affecting their immediate surroundings.[19]
- Darkness-Based Detection: Nyx can use the Darkness she spreads through an environment as a sensory medium, identifying supernatural presences and distant regions.[13]
- Darkness-Assisted Concealment: Nyx's control over Darkness complements her authority over Concealment, allowing her to exploit nocturnal environments when suppressing presence and information.[2]
- Darkness-Mediated Transportation: Nyx's influence can be used to relocate other beings, including the Greek Goddesses transferred away from [[Olympus]].[14]
- Night-Sky Traversal: Nyx can utilize the Darkness of the night sky for rapid movement and infiltration.[10]

##### Enhanced Darkness and Concealment
Nyx's powers improved after she established a connection with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s developing Pantheon. Victor could supply additional supernatural Energy compatible with her Night-related abilities, making those abilities more efficient rather than simply granting her an unrelated new Divinity. During the confrontation with the Egyptian Gods, Victor exploited Nyx's improved capabilities to gather information from a Pantheon that believed its own defenses would prevent such surveillance.[7]
- Divine Power Enhancement: Nyx's connection with Victor's Pantheon improved the efficiency of her Darkness-related Divine abilities.[7]
- Enhanced Concealment: Her improved power allowed her to avoid detection by Gods and supernatural defenses that would otherwise have presented greater obstacles to an Original Primordial Goddess attempting covert observation.[7]
- Anti-Primordial Concealment: The narration explained that Nyx's improved concealment could defeat even the ordinary perception of powerful [[Primordial Gods]], although exceptionally developed senses and suitable Runic countermeasures remained relevant methods of detecting her.[7]
- Information-Gathering Enhancement: Nyx's upgraded powers allowed her to infiltrate the [[Egyptian Pantheon]]'s information defenses and provide Victor with knowledge the Egyptian Gods believed he did not possess.[7]
- Nonexistence-Like Concealment: The narration described Nyx as capable of effectively ceasing to exist if she wished, emphasizing the extraordinary advancement of her concealment. The statement establishes a far more advanced potential concealment state than conventional invisibility, although the exact mechanics and limitations were not separately demonstrated through an extended activation sequence.[7]

#### Concealment Divinity
Concealment is Nyx's second principal Divine Concept and the authority responsible for her title as the Mother of Concealment. Unlike ordinary invisibility, which merely prevents a target from being seen, Nyx's authority can interfere with the information and supernatural evidence necessary for an observer to perceive, identify or locate something. Her concealment can apply to individuals, areas, identities, Divine presences and information, making it extraordinarily valuable against powerful beings who do not rely exclusively upon conventional eyesight.[1][22][2]

##### Self-Concealment
- Concealment Manipulation: Nyx can suppress the information by which other beings perceive or detect her existence. This is a direct application of her principal Divinity rather than a conventional stealth technique.[1][2]
- Supernatural Invisibility: Nyx can prevent observers from visually perceiving her even when she is physically present within their surroundings.[4]
- Presence Concealment: Nyx can hide her supernatural presence from individuals who would ordinarily detect the proximity of a powerful Goddess.[4][2]
- Divine-Sense Concealment: Nyx can evade supernatural perception employed by Gods and other powerful beings. Her authority is specifically described as more sophisticated than ordinary sensory deception.[1][2]
- Perception Manipulation: Nyx can prevent an observer from consciously registering her even when she occupies a position directly in front of them.[4][2]
- Close-Range Concealment: Nyx remained extremely close to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] without being detected through his ordinary perception. Her concealment was powerful enough that she was surprised when he became aware of her presence through his unusual Draconic sight.[4]
- Energy-Signature Concealment: Nyx can suppress the supernatural indications associated with her presence, preventing ordinary Divine detection from automatically identifying her position.[1][2]
- Concealment of [[Divine Presence]]: Nyx can conceal the existence of her Divine power when she does not wish to be detected by surrounding supernatural beings.[1][2]
- Primordial-Level Concealment: The narration explains that beings below approximately the existential level of the Original Greek [[Primordial Gods]] would ordinarily be unable to perceive Nyx if she concealed herself completely.[2]
- Resistance to Detection: Nyx's concealment can defeat sophisticated sensory abilities, although her encounter with Victor demonstrated that certain exceptional forms of Draconic perception can overcome ordinary expectations concerning its effectiveness.[4]
- Advanced Anti-Detection: Her powers improved through Victor's assistance until even powerful supernatural observers could fail to detect her without exceptionally refined perception or appropriate Runic countermeasures.[7]

##### Separate Plane of Existence
Nyx's concealment is unusually effective because her status as the Mother of Night allows her to exist within a separate supernatural plane. Rather than relying exclusively upon changing the appearance of her ordinary physical body, she can position herself within a layer of existence that weaker observers cannot normally perceive. This characteristic is specifically identified while [[Persephone]] considers the difficulty of detecting Nyx in the [[Greek Underworld]].[2]
- Separate-Plane Existence: Nyx can exist within her own supernatural plane associated with the Night.[2]
- Plane-Based Concealment: Her position within that plane prevents many observers from perceiving her through ordinary physical or Divine senses.[2]
- Selective Manifestation: Nyx can reveal herself or interact with surrounding beings despite possessing the ability to remain concealed within her separate plane.[1][2]
- Existential-Level Concealment: Perceiving Nyx while she fully conceals herself ordinarily requires an existence or perception capable of overcoming the separation imposed by her Primordial nature.[2]
- Divine Perception Resistance: Nyx's separate-plane concealment differs from ordinary sensory deception because the difficulty involves the supernatural conditions under which her existence can be perceived.[2]

##### Concealment of Others
Nyx can extend her Divine authority to individuals other than herself, protecting allies or other targets from supernatural observation. Her ability to conceal several powerful Gods simultaneously demonstrates that Concealment is not limited to suppressing her personal presence.[12]
- Target Concealment: Nyx can hide another individual through her Divinity.[12]
- Multi-Target Concealment: Nyx can conceal multiple powerful Gods simultaneously.[12]
- Concealment Bestowal: Nyx can provide another being with a temporary concealed state through her own authority rather than requiring that individual to possess an equivalent Divine Concept.[12]
- Covert-Team Support: Nyx can protect several operatives or allies from observation, making her authority especially effective during infiltration or extraction missions.[14][12]
- Concealed Transportation: Nyx can conceal targets while rapidly relocating them through Night-associated power.[12]

##### Night Veil
Nyx can manifest a supernatural veil resembling a star-filled night and use it to conceal and relocate other beings. During the expedition into the [[Greek Underworld]], she covered [[Thanatos]], [[Hypnos]] and [[Nemesis]] with such a veil before removing them from the immediate area and returning them to [[Persephone]]'s castle. The technique operated rapidly enough that only individuals possessing extremely advanced perception could clearly follow the process.[12]
- Night Veil Creation: Nyx can manifest a star-filled veil of supernatural Night around selected targets.[12]
- Mass Concealment: The veil can conceal several powerful Gods at the same time.[12]
- Target Relocation: Nyx can transport those covered by her power to another location.[12]
- Rapid Transportation: The relocation occurs at extraordinary speed, making the action difficult for ordinary observers to follow.[12]
- Concealed Displacement: The targets disappear from their initial position while concealed by Nyx's Night-associated authority.[12]

##### Information and Identity Concealment
Nyx's Divinity can extend to information rather than merely physical targets. Her authority over Concealment can obscure identifying details associated with her existence, including her name. This demonstrates that her abilities operate upon the supernatural availability of information, not simply the visual appearance of an object or person.[22]
- Information Concealment: Nyx can hide information that would otherwise reveal the identity or nature of a concealed subject.[22]
- Name Concealment: Nyx can conceal information identifying her name, preventing the information from being perceived through ordinary methods.[22]
- Identity Concealment: Nyx can prevent observers from properly identifying her through the information available to them.[22]
- Information Obscuration: Nyx's Divinity can interfere with the acquisition of knowledge rather than only preventing direct sensory observation.[1][22]
- Secrecy Manipulation: Nyx can use Concealment to protect information she does not want discovered, making the ability especially useful for covert communications and intelligence operations.[23][22]

##### Anti-Divination and Future-Sight Concealment
Nyx can prevent certain forms of supernatural information gathering from revealing what she wishes to conceal. During the succession crisis involving [[Persephone]], [[Hypnos]] explicitly explained that Nyx could hide herself from future-oriented observation associated with [[Hecate]]. This establishes a direct interaction between her Concealment and forms of Divination extending beyond present-time visual perception.[1]
- Divination Resistance: Nyx can interfere with supernatural abilities intended to reveal concealed information.[1]
- Precognition Concealment: Nyx can hide from at least certain forms of future-oriented perception associated with Hecate.[1]
- Future-Sight Obstruction: Her Concealment can prevent the use of predictive observation from automatically revealing her position or actions.[1]
- Supernatural Information Denial: Nyx can obstruct methods of acquiring information that do not depend upon ordinary eyesight.[1]
- Detection Countermeasures: Nyx's abilities provide an exceptional defense against supernatural reconnaissance by individuals lacking sufficient authority or specialized methods to overcome her Concealment.[1][2]

##### Area and Territory Concealment
Nyx can apply her Concealment to locations rather than only individual beings. During the Greek crisis, she concealed areas in which sensitive conversations and operations were taking place, preventing external observers from freely examining the activity occurring inside.[23]
- Area Concealment: Nyx can conceal a location from supernatural detection.[23]
- Territory Concealment: Nyx can extend her authority across a selected region rather than restricting it to a single person.[23]
- Communication Security: Nyx can conceal conversations occurring within a protected area, reducing the risk of external surveillance.[23]
- Activity Concealment: Supernatural activities occurring inside an area can be hidden from observers who lack the means to overcome her authority.[23]
- Covert-Operations Support: Nyx can establish concealed locations suitable for intelligence gathering, planning and other activities that require secrecy.[23]

##### Concealment Barrier
Nyx can create barriers through her Divine authority capable of isolating a location from outside interference. During the confrontation with [[Persephone]], her barrier proved sufficiently powerful that [[Thanatos]] and [[Hypnos]] could not simply bypass it. Persephone breached the barrier only after acquiring the enormous authority associated with becoming the legitimate Ruler of the [[Greek Underworld]].[1]
- Barrier Creation: Nyx can manifest a supernatural barrier through her Concealment authority.[1]
- Access Denial: The barrier can prevent individuals from entering or bypassing the protected area.[1]
- Isolation: Nyx can separate a location from outside interference through the application of her Divinity.[1]
- Divine Barrier Reinforcement: The barrier possesses enough supernatural strength to resist powerful Greek Gods who lack sufficient authority to penetrate it.[1]
- Primordial-Level Protection: The barrier was described as requiring power comparable to an Original Primordial God to breach through the method demonstrated by Persephone.[1]

#### Personal Primordial Domain
Nyx possesses a personal territory within the [[Greek Underworld]] that is fundamentally connected to her Primordial existence. Unlike the ordinary regions governed by the Ruler of the Underworld for the processing and administration of Souls, Nyx's territory functions as her personal domain. Its foundation belongs directly to her, preventing [[Persephone]] from automatically acquiring unrestricted authority over the area merely through becoming the legitimate Ruler of Greek Hell.[2]
- Personal Domain: Nyx possesses her own supernatural territory within the Greek Underworld.[2]
- Primordial Territory: The domain is rooted in Nyx's existence as an Original Primordial Goddess rather than functioning solely as an administrative portion of the ordinary Underworld.[2]
- Domain Independence: The territory is not automatically transferred to Persephone through the authority attached to her position as Underworld Ruler.[2]
- Resistance to Jurisdictional Override: Persephone cannot casually seize or rewrite Nyx's personal Primordial domain through the ordinary privileges of her office.[2]
- Domain Empowerment: Nyx possesses a natural advantage within her own territory. [[Scathach]] recognized that confronting Nyx inside such an environment would be substantially more difficult than fighting her elsewhere.[12]
- Environmental Affinity: The territory is naturally associated with Nyx's Primordial existence and her powers over Night and Concealment.[2]
- Concealment Advantage: Nyx's connection to the domain makes unauthorized observation and intrusion especially difficult for beings who cannot overcome her supernatural perception and concealment.[2]

#### Transportation and Mobility
Nyx possesses several forms of supernatural movement involving Darkness, Night and the relocation of other beings. Her abilities are particularly valuable because they combine extraordinary mobility with Concealment, allowing her to move through dangerous areas or extract allies without relying upon ordinary physical travel.[14][12][10]

##### Dark Transportation
Nyx can employ manifestations of dark [[Divine Energy]] to relocate individuals over enormous distances. During the collapse of [[Olympus]], Greek Goddesses were transferred into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s territory through dark Energy originating from Nyx's influence. The process allowed numerous powerful beings to escape the isolated [[Greek Pantheon]] despite the restrictions imposed upon ordinary Divine movement.[11][14]
- Supernatural Transportation: Nyx can move other beings through applications of her Night-associated Divine power.[14]
- Long-Distance Relocation: Nyx's influence allowed Greek Goddesses to reach territory controlled by Victor after leaving Olympus.[14]
- Group Transportation: Multiple individuals can be relocated through Nyx's supernatural influence.[14]
- Remote Extraction: Nyx can assist in removing allies from hostile or politically restricted supernatural territories.[11][14]
- Dark Energy Transportation: The relocation is associated with manifestations of dark supernatural Energy originating from Nyx's authority.[14]
- Concealed Movement: Nyx can combine transportation with the concealment of the affected individuals, as demonstrated by her Night Veil in the [[Greek Underworld]].[12]

##### Night-Sky Traversal
By the mature Imperial era, Nyx's connection to Night allowed her to use the dark night sky as a medium for extraordinary movement. Kaguya explicitly compared the ability with her own use of Shadows, identifying their supernatural mobility as one of the reasons both women were exceptionally suited to leading the Empire's intelligence services.[10]
- Night-Mediated Movement: Nyx can exploit the dark night sky as a supernatural medium for travel.[10]
- Rapid Relocation: Nyx can use her Night-related authority to reach locations without relying exclusively upon conventional physical movement.[10]
- Covert Mobility: Movement through her natural medium complements her powers over Concealment, making the ability particularly effective for infiltration.[10]
- Intelligence Operations: Night-Sky Traversal allows Nyx to reach locations valuable for reconnaissance and espionage without requiring ordinary transportation routes.[10]
- Shadow-Movement Parallel: The ability operates through a supernatural medium comparable in function to the Shadows utilized by Kaguya, although their underlying authorities remain distinct.[10]

#### Divine Perception
Nyx possesses exceptionally advanced supernatural perception even among the Original Greek [[Primordial Gods]]. Her authority over Concealment provides an unusual understanding of the mechanisms through which beings hide themselves, while her connection to Night allows her to observe enormous environments and transmit information through Darkness. Her perception is sufficiently developed to detect powerful Gods, hidden magical structures and distant celestial objects beyond the reach of ordinary supernatural senses.[5][12][24][6]

##### Supernatural and Primordial Perception
- Enhanced Senses: Nyx possesses extraordinary sensory capabilities associated with her Primordial existence, allowing her to perceive phenomena inaccessible to ordinary Gods.[5][13]
- Supernatural Detection: Nyx can identify supernatural presences and abnormalities even when the beings involved possess powerful forms of concealment.[12][19]
- Concealment Detection: Nyx can recognize concealed beings through her extraordinary perception and familiarity with the Divine principles governing hiding and secrecy.[12][19]
- Primordial Detection: Nyx detected [[Erebus]] despite his attempt to remain hidden and directly confronted him, demonstrating that the concealment of another Original Primordial God does not automatically defeat her senses.[19]
- Detection of Divine Embodiments: Nyx can detect beings such as [[Thanatos]] even when they employ advanced concealment abilities.[12]
- Astral Perception: Nyx can observe supernatural phenomena beyond conventional physical sight, including the structures and Energy associated with other dimensions and Divine realms.[5][13]
- Spiritual Awareness: Nyx can perceive spiritual phenomena and recognize changes associated with Souls and supernatural existence.[5]
- [[Magic]] Perception: Nyx can detect and examine magical Energy and supernatural formations.[5]
- Magic-Circle Detection: Nyx identified enormous numbers of concealed Magic Circles and recognized the strategic danger associated with their arrangement.[5]
- Supernatural Energy Analysis: Nyx can distinguish unusual Energy characteristics associated with powerful magical structures, although specialized Magic may prevent her from immediately identifying every underlying mechanism.[5]
- Hidden-Structure Detection: Nyx can locate structures concealed beneath powerful supernatural defenses, as demonstrated while examining [[Hecate]]'s territory.[24]
- Remote Observation: Nyx can observe places far beyond her immediate surroundings through applications of her Primordial perception.[5][6]
- Threat Recognition: Nyx's perception and ancient experience allow her to identify dangerous supernatural presences and recognize when an apparent environment contains concealed abnormalities.[5][24]

##### Astronomical-Range Perception
Nyx demonstrated extraordinary long-range vision during the creation of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s new planetary home. When Victor required a massive asteroid containing suitable materials, Nyx extended her perception beyond the planet into space and located an object meeting his requirements. She then provided Natalia with the necessary information to establish a Spatial portal near the asteroid.[6]
- Astronomical Vision: Nyx can extend her perception beyond a planetary environment and examine distant objects in space.[6]
- Remote Celestial Observation: Nyx can identify celestial objects and examine their general characteristics without physically traveling to them.[6]
- Material Recognition: Nyx recognized that the selected asteroid contained materials suitable for Victor's construction project.[6]
- Astronomical Target Identification: Nyx can provide accurate information concerning distant objects to allies whose powers require a specific destination.[6]
- Cosmic Perspective: Nyx's perception provides an extraordinarily broad view of the surrounding cosmic environment. Natalia was visibly overwhelmed by the scale of the perspective temporarily shared with her.[6]

##### Vision Sharing
Nyx can directly share her supernatural perception with another individual. This ability was demonstrated when she transmitted her extraordinary vision to the Greek Goddesses during their investigation of dangerous magical structures and later when she extended Natalia's senses toward a distant asteroid required for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s planetary construction project.[5][6]
- Sensory Sharing: Nyx can transmit information obtained through her Divine senses directly to another being.[5][6]
- Remote Perception Sharing: Recipients can temporarily perceive locations and supernatural phenomena beyond the normal limits of their personal senses.[6]
- Astronomical Vision Sharing: Nyx shared her perception of distant space with Natalia, allowing her to observe the selected asteroid.[6]
- Supernatural Information Transmission: Nyx can communicate sensory information concerning hidden magical structures and dangerous supernatural formations.[5]
- Tactical Reconnaissance Support: Nyx can provide allies with information necessary for the safe examination of concealed threats.[5]
- Cooperative Spatial Targeting: Natalia used the information supplied through Nyx's vision to establish a portal connecting the developing planetary project to a distant asteroid.[6]

##### Dark Pulse
Dark Pulse is a large-scale reconnaissance technique through which Nyx releases a pulse of dark supernatural Energy and uses it to extend her perception across an enormous environment. She demonstrated the ability while investigating Helheim, where the pulse spread through the dimension and allowed her to inspect distant regions without physically traveling across the entire realm.[13]
During the expedition, Nyx examined the structure of Helheim and recognized that the realm was experiencing severe problems beyond its ordinary appearance as a frozen [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]]. She subsequently released her dark power, allowing her senses to reach the farthest regions of the dimension. Through this application, she detected several powerful beings, including [[Fenrir]] and the other supernatural entities accompanying Hela.[13]
- Dark Pulse Generation: Nyx can release a wave of dark supernatural Energy that spreads outward through an environment.[13]
- Dimension-Wide Scanning: Nyx extended her perception across Helheim and examined regions far beyond her immediate location.[13]
- Darkness-Based [[Radar Systems|Radar]]: The released Darkness functions as a medium through which Nyx obtains information concerning distant areas.[13]
- Remote Reconnaissance: Nyx can locate targets without visiting every region of an enormous supernatural dimension personally.[13]
- Supernatural Presence Detection: Nyx detected several powerful beings through the information gathered by the pulse.[13]
- Dimensional Structure Perception: Nyx could examine the supernatural structure of Helheim and recognize that the realm possessed serious underlying problems.[13]
- Large-Area Information Gathering: The technique allows Nyx to inspect an enormous territory rapidly, making it exceptionally valuable for intelligence operations.[13]

##### Detection of Concealed Magic
Nyx's perception is particularly effective against supernatural structures concealed through complex [[Magic]]. During the crisis involving the Amazons, she identified the existence of enormous numbers of Magic Circles whose arrangement presented an extraordinary strategic danger. The structures had been concealed within the environment, requiring advanced perception to recognize their scale and purpose.[5]
- Magic-Circle Detection: Nyx can perceive concealed magical formations even when the structures are embedded throughout a complex supernatural environment.[5]
- Strategic Magic Recognition: Nyx recognized that the concealed formations were connected to large-scale Magic rather than ordinary isolated spells.[5]
- Energy Signature Analysis: Nyx could detect the unusual Energy associated with the formations, although she did not immediately possess complete knowledge of every property of the Magic involved.[5]
- Environmental Threat Assessment: Nyx recognized that interfering with the concealed structures could trigger catastrophic effects and warned the other Goddesses accordingly.[5]

#### Divine Blessings
Nyx possesses the ability to establish persistent supernatural connections with other beings through Divine Blessings. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] carried a Blessing associated with Nyx during an earlier period of the story, and the connection was later removed by [[Aphrodite]] because the presence of multiple Divine influences created risks involving observation and outside interference.[17][25]
Nyx subsequently explained that a sufficiently intact Blessing could allow her to observe a recipient remotely, potentially overcoming the ordinary separation imposed by distance and dimensions. The connection therefore possesses considerable utility for information gathering, although its continued existence depends upon the Blessing remaining attached to the recipient.[5]
- Divine Blessing Bestowal: Nyx can establish a supernatural connection with another being through her Divine authority.[17]
- Persistent Divine Connection: The Blessing can continue existing after Nyx is no longer physically near the recipient.[17][5]
- Remote Observation: Nyx can use an intact Divine connection to obtain information concerning the recipient from a distance.[5]
- Interdimensional Observation: Nyx indicated that ordinary dimensional separation would not necessarily prevent her from observing a target through an established Divine Blessing.[5]
- Divine Influence: The Blessing establishes an enduring relationship between Nyx's power and the recipient's supernatural existence.[17][5]
- Removable Blessing: Aphrodite demonstrated that sufficiently powerful Divine intervention could sever the Blessings attached to Victor, including the influence originating from Nyx.[25]
- Religious Blessing Bestowal: Divine Blessings associated with Nyx later became part of the supernatural support granted to the Battle Sisters of the Religion of the Blood God.[26]

#### Lesser Divine Aspects

##### Death
Nyx possesses a lesser Divine association with Death, explicitly identified when she released a greater portion of her Primordial power during the confrontation involving [[Erebus]] and [[Nocturnus]]. Her relationship with Death is distinct from the principal authority of [[Thanatos]], whose existence directly embodies the Greek supernatural Concept of Death.[3]
- Secondary Death Divinity: Death is explicitly identified as one of Nyx's lesser Divine associations.[3]
- Death-System Knowledge: Nyx possesses extensive understanding of the supernatural structures governing Death within the [[Greek Pantheon]], including the limitations of ordinary anti-God techniques when applied to Divine embodiments.[1][12]
- Conceptual [[Immortality]] Knowledge: Nyx can identify the circumstances under which a being such as Thanatos could potentially be destroyed, distinguishing ordinary physical damage from methods capable of overcoming the conditions supporting his Divine existence.[12]
- [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] Knowledge: Nyx understands the organization and supernatural principles of the Greek Underworld, including the distinction between its administrative territory and the personal domains of Primordial beings.[2]

##### Mystery
Nyx possesses a lesser Divine association with Mystery, complementing the secrecy and hidden information associated with her principal powers over Night and Concealment. The Concept is explicitly identified as a secondary characteristic of her Primordial existence rather than a separate fully demonstrated primary Divinity.[3]
- Secondary Mystery Divinity: Mystery is explicitly identified among Nyx's lesser Divine aspects.[3]
- Concealment Compatibility: Mystery is closely related to the unknown and hidden phenomena already governed through Nyx's principal Concealment authority.[1][3]
- Night-Associated Mystery: Nyx's Divine identity connects Night with the supernatural uncertainty and hidden information associated with the unknown.[3]

##### Terror
Nyx possesses a lesser Divine association with Terror, connected to the psychological fear associated with Night. This aspect became especially significant through her development of Eternal Night, which uses the terror of a starless night to force victims into vivid experiences of their greatest nightmares.[3][20]
- Secondary Terror Divinity: Terror is explicitly identified among Nyx's lesser Divine aspects.[3]
- Fear Manipulation: Nyx can inflict overwhelming supernatural fear through applications associated with her Divine nature.[20]
- Fear Amplification: Nyx can intensify a victim's psychological response to frightening experiences through the supernatural influence of Eternal Night.[20]
- Nightmare Inducement: Nyx can force victims to experience extraordinarily vivid supernatural nightmares based upon their deepest fears.[20]
- Psychological Pressure: Nyx's powers can place targets under mental strain far beyond the effects of ordinary intimidation.[20]

#### Eternal Night
Eternal Night is one of Nyx's most dangerous psychological techniques. It originates from the ancient fear associated with a night devoid of stars, using Nyx's Divine nature to force victims into vivid nightmare experiences based upon their deepest fears. Rather than merely producing ordinary frightening images, the ability amplifies the psychological significance of those experiences until the affected individuals struggle to resist the fear imposed upon them.[20]
Nyx demonstrated Eternal Night during the events surrounding the Dwarves after Ragnarök. Following their attempts to demand valuable equipment in exchange for cooperation, Nyx subjected the offending individuals to the technique. The experience left them traumatized and exceptionally compliant, allowing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction to obtain the required cooperation without resorting to ordinary prolonged physical torture.[20]
- Eternal Night: Nyx can impose a supernatural nightmare experience derived from the fear of a starless night.[20]
- Nightmare Manipulation: The technique forces targets to experience frightening supernatural scenarios rather than ordinary waking reality.[20]
- Greatest-Fear Manifestation: The content of the nightmare is based upon the victim's own deepest fears.[20]
- Fear Amplification: Nyx's Divine influence intensifies the emotional consequences of the nightmare beyond those of ordinary dreams.[20]
- Psychological Manipulation: The technique affects a victim's mental state and ability to maintain composure or resistance.[20]
- Mental Subjugation: Victims subjected to the technique can become exceptionally compliant after experiencing the amplified nightmares.[20]
- Psychological Trauma Inducement: Eternal Night can leave lasting psychological consequences, as demonstrated by the Dwarves who remained traumatized after the experience.[20]
- Mass Application: Nyx can impose Eternal Night upon multiple individuals rather than being restricted to a single target.[20]
- Interrogation and Coercion: The ability is particularly suited to situations in which Nyx needs to overcome the resistance of captured individuals without relying entirely upon physical force.[20]

#### Additional Supernatural Abilities

##### Telekinesis
Nyx possesses the ability to manipulate objects remotely through supernatural force. After [[Persephone]] severed her arm, Nyx used Telekinesis to retrieve the detached limb before reattaching it to her body. The action was performed casually, demonstrating that the physical loss of an arm did not prevent her from using her other supernatural abilities.[1]
- Telekinesis: Nyx can remotely manipulate physical objects.[1]
- Remote Pulling: Nyx can draw a separated object toward herself without physically approaching it.[1]
- Limb Retrieval: Nyx demonstrated precise Telekinesis by retrieving her severed arm before reattaching it.[1]
The maximum range, lifting capacity and independent combat applications of Nyx's Telekinesis were not quantified in the demonstrated passage.

##### Divine Regeneration
Nyx possessed powerful regenerative capabilities before her transformation into a True Dragon. The loss of an arm to [[Persephone]] did not cause permanent disability, and Nyx treated the injury as comparatively insignificant because her Primordial existence could recover from damage that would incapacitate ordinary supernatural beings.[1]
- Regeneration: Nyx can recover from severe physical injuries through her supernatural constitution.[1]
- Limb Reattachment: Nyx can restore a severed limb after recovering it through Telekinesis.[1]
- Primordial Physical Resilience: Nyx's Original Primordial Goddess nature allows her to withstand injuries that would be far more dangerous to ordinary Gods.[1]
- Draconic Regenerative Foundation: Nyx's later transformation into a Blood Dragon provides a superior physical and spiritual foundation, although her individual regeneration limits after Dragonization have not been separately demonstrated.[8]

##### Primordial Divine Form
Nyx can release a greater portion of her Original Primordial nature, manifesting an appearance that reflects the Concept of Night itself. During the final Greek conflict, she surrounded herself in dark supernatural power resembling an immense star-filled night before appearing in clothing formed from that same celestial darkness. A large black scythe manifested in her hands as she prepared to confront [[Erebus]] and [[Nocturnus]].[3]
- Primordial Power Release: Nyx can openly manifest the extraordinary Divine power normally concealed beneath her relaxed humanoid appearance.[3]
- Divine Raiment Manifestation: Nyx can create clothing resembling a star-filled night as an expression of her Primordial power.[3]
- Divine Weapon Manifestation: Nyx can manifest a black scythe during the release of her Primordial Divine nature.[3]
- Enhanced [[Divine Presence]]: Nyx's supernatural presence becomes considerably more threatening when she openly releases the power associated with her ancient Divinities.[3]
- Night-Associated Appearance: Nyx's Primordial manifestation resembles the night sky, reflecting the fundamental supernatural characteristics of her Divine existence.[3]

#### Combat Skills
Although Nyx specializes in concealment and intelligence rather than sustained physical combat, she possesses enormous supernatural experience accumulated throughout her existence as an Original Primordial Goddess. Her abilities allow her to interfere with opponents through information denial, environmental control, Night-based mobility and psychological techniques, making her exceptionally dangerous even against enemies possessing greater conventional physical strength.[1][19][20]
- Divine Combat Experience: Nyx possesses extensive experience confronting powerful Gods and operating during conflicts involving the oldest Greek Divine generations.[19][3]
- Scythe Proficiency: Nyx can wield a large black scythe manifested through her Divine power. She later struck [[Erebus]] with the weapon during the final Greek conflict.[3][27]
- [[Divine Energy]] Combat: Nyx can release and direct enormous quantities of supernatural Energy through her Night-associated authority.[19][3]
- Concealment-Based Combat: Nyx can exploit an opponent's inability to perceive or locate her, creating opportunities for attacks and tactical movement that would be difficult to accomplish through ordinary combat.[4][2]
- Darkness Countermeasures: Nyx can oppose hostile Darkness through her own authority. During the confrontation with Erebus, she and [[Aphrodite]] dispelled the Darkness threatening them.[19]
- Battlefield Support: Nyx can strengthen nocturnal allies through her Starry Night, improving the effectiveness of groups whose abilities are compatible with her Divine authority.[18]
- Large-Scale Environmental Control: Nyx can cover enormous regions in Night and manipulate the supernatural environment, making her valuable during operations requiring isolation or control of the surrounding conditions.[6]
- Target Isolation: Nyx can prevent opponents from obtaining accurate information or escaping detection through ordinary methods, particularly when operating alongside allies capable of sealing space or preventing dimensional movement.[28][18]
- Cooperative Combat: Nyx can coordinate her Night and Concealment with other specialized supernatural powers. Her participation in [[Agares]]'s capture complemented Zaladrac's ability to restrict the opponent's escape through another plane.[28][18]
- Primordial Knowledge: Nyx possesses extensive understanding of the supernatural mechanisms supporting ancient Gods, including the limitations of conventional anti-God abilities when confronting embodiments of fundamental Concepts.[12]
- Psychological Warfare: Nyx can use Eternal Night to break the resistance of captured enemies through fear and supernatural nightmares.[20]

#### Non-Combat Skills

##### Espionage and Intelligence
Nyx possesses extraordinary experience in secrecy, information gathering, manipulation and covert observation. These abilities formed an essential part of her original activities within the [[Greek Pantheon]] and later became the foundation of her position as one of the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s principal intelligence commanders. Unlike ordinary spies, Nyx can combine supernatural stealth with remote perception, concealed transportation and the direct manipulation of information itself.[23][4][9]
- Espionage Mastery: Nyx possesses extensive experience observing and manipulating powerful supernatural beings without revealing her own presence.[23][4]
- Covert Surveillance: Nyx can remain near powerful Gods while avoiding their perception, allowing her to gather information unavailable through conventional methods.[4]
- Infiltration: Nyx can exploit Concealment and Night-based movement to enter locations that ordinary operatives would struggle to approach without detection.[4][10]
- Counterintelligence: Nyx's perception and understanding of supernatural concealment allow her to identify hidden activities and suspicious phenomena that ordinary surveillance may overlook.[5][24]
- Information Denial: Nyx can prevent opponents from perceiving selected beings, locations or information through her Concealment authority.[1][22]
- Remote Reconnaissance: Nyx can inspect distant environments through her Divine perception and Darkness-based scanning abilities.[6][13]
- Sensory Information Sharing: Nyx can transmit her perception to other individuals, allowing them to act upon information that their own senses could not obtain.[5][6]
- Concealed Transportation: Nyx can move herself or other beings through applications of Night and dark [[Divine Energy]] while minimizing the risk of detection.[14][12]
- Supernatural Investigation: Nyx can recognize unusual magical structures and identify information indicating the presence of hidden threats.[5][24]
- Interdimensional Reconnaissance: Nyx can operate within supernatural dimensions and employ Darkness to examine their distant regions, as demonstrated in Helheim.[13]
- Psychological Manipulation: Nyx possesses extensive experience influencing the decisions of others through superior information, indirect pressure and calculated manipulation.[29][1]
- Interrogation: Eternal Night provides a supernatural method of breaking psychological resistance through intensified experiences of a target's greatest fears.[20]
- Long-Term Planning: Nyx demonstrated the ability to develop and execute complex political schemes, including her attempt to establish [[Persephone]] as the legitimate Ruler of the [[Greek Underworld]].[29][1]

##### Black Hand of the Emperor
Nyx helped establish the Third Imperial Legion, the Black Hand of the Emperor, alongside Kaguya and [[Persephone]]. The organization became responsible for many of the Empire's intelligence and covert operations, functioning separately from conventional military formations whose principal responsibility involved direct territorial conquest.[9]
Kaguya serves as the Legion's principal commander, while Nyx is authorized to assume command when Kaguya is unavailable. Their abilities complement one another: Kaguya can operate through Shadows, while Nyx can utilize Night as a medium for perception, movement and concealment. The two were subsequently identified together as the principal figures responsible for Imperial intelligence.[9][10]
- Intelligence Leadership: Nyx is one of the principal authorities responsible for the Empire's intelligence operations.[9]
- Covert-Operations Command: Nyx can assume command of the Third Imperial Legion when Kaguya is unavailable.[9]
- Espionage Coordination: The Black Hand conducts intelligence-gathering operations against hostile civilizations and organizations.[9]
- Counterintelligence Operations: The Legion is responsible for identifying enemy agents and covert threats to Imperial security.[9]
- Infiltration Operations: Nyx's Night and Concealment are particularly suited to operations requiring access to secure locations without conventional military confrontation.[9][10]
- Psychological Warfare: The Black Hand employs methods intended to undermine enemy morale, manipulate information and influence hostile organizations. Nyx's Eternal Night provides a directly demonstrated example of her personal capabilities in this field.[20][9]
- Detection of Internal Threats: The Legion possesses responsibilities involving traitors and hostile individuals operating within Imperial institutions.[9]
- Cooperative Intelligence Operations: Nyx and Kaguya can employ different supernatural movement and concealment systems to support the same intelligence objectives.[10]

##### Divine and Cosmic Knowledge
Nyx's age and Primordial status grant her extensive knowledge of the [[Greek Pantheon]]'s ancient supernatural structures. Her familiarity with Gods, Divinities, Souls and the organization of the [[Greek Underworld]] is especially valuable during investigations involving unfamiliar supernatural phenomena or Divine political systems.[30][2]
- Divine Knowledge: Nyx possesses extensive understanding of the Greek Pantheon's Divine hierarchy, ancient Gods and supernatural institutions.[29][2]
- Underworld Knowledge: Nyx understands the structure of the Greek Underworld and the distinction between its administrative regions and the private domains maintained by powerful Divine beings.[2]
- Soul-System Knowledge: Nyx possesses knowledge concerning the judgment and processing of Souls within the Greek afterlife.[30]
- Conceptual Existence Knowledge: Nyx understands the relationship between fundamental Divine Concepts and the extraordinary immortality possessed by certain ancient Gods.[12]
- Magical Analysis: Nyx can recognize the structure of sophisticated [[Magic]] Circles and identify supernatural dangers associated with large-scale magical formations.[5]
- Astronomical Observation: Nyx can identify distant celestial objects and recognize potentially useful materials through her extraordinary perception.[6]
- Strategic Assessment: Nyx can evaluate the political and supernatural consequences of major changes in Divine authority, although her miscalculation concerning [[Persephone]] demonstrates that this judgment is not infallible.[1]

#### Resistances and Immunities
Nyx possesses exceptionally advanced defensive capabilities through her Original Primordial Goddess nature, her authority over Concealment and her subsequent Blood Dragon physiology. However, the novel demonstrates that these defenses are not unrestricted. [[Persephone]] breached Nyx's Concealment barrier and injured her after acquiring the authority of the [[Greek Underworld]], while [[Erebus]] used a World Tree fragment to interfere with her Primordial power. The following resistances therefore distinguish documented defensive effects from powers that have not been individually established.[1][19][8]
- Resistance to Sensory Detection: Nyx can prevent observers from perceiving her through conventional and supernatural senses, including circumstances in which she is physically present at extremely close range.[4][2]
- Resistance to Divine Perception: Nyx's Concealment can defeat the senses of powerful Gods. When fully concealed, ordinary beings below approximately the existential level of the Original Greek [[Primordial Gods]] cannot reliably perceive her.[2]
- Resistance to Precognition: Nyx can conceal herself from certain forms of future-oriented observation, including the sight associated with [[Hecate]] that [[Hypnos]] explicitly identified as insufficient to guarantee her discovery.[1]
- Resistance to Divination: Nyx's authority can interfere with supernatural methods of obtaining concealed information.[1]
- Resistance to Information Analysis: Nyx can conceal identifying information associated with herself, including her name, preventing observers from automatically obtaining information through ordinary supernatural perception.[22]
- Resistance to Supernatural Targeting: Nyx's Concealment can prevent an opponent from accurately perceiving or locating her, making it difficult to establish a reliable target for abilities requiring accurate identification of her position.[4][2]
- Resistance to Darkness: Nyx can oppose and disperse hostile Darkness through her own Night-associated authority. She and [[Aphrodite]] demonstrated this against Erebus.[19]
- Resistance to Physical Damage: Nyx possesses extraordinary Primordial durability and later acquired the physical foundation of a genuine Blood Dragon.[1][8]
- Resistance to Permanent Physical Injury: Nyx can recover from severe damage, including the loss of an arm, through her Divine regeneration and ability to reattach separated body parts.[1]
- Limited Resistance to Greek Death Authority: Nyx's Original Primordial existence is supported by foundational Divine Concepts, making conventional methods of permanently killing ordinary Gods insufficient to guarantee her destruction.[1][12]
- Enhanced Spiritual Resilience: Nyx possesses an ancient Primordial Divine Soul whose foundation was subsequently reconstructed through Blood Dragon transformation.[8]
- Enhanced Draconic Durability: Her True Dragon body provides an additional physical foundation beyond her former Primordial Goddess physiology.[8]
- Resistance to Conventional Infiltration and Concealment: Nyx's perception allows her to detect beings attempting to hide through supernatural methods, including individuals possessing extremely advanced concealment themselves.[12][19]

##### Limitations and Known Counters
- Superior Authority: [[Persephone]] breached Nyx's Concealment barrier and severed her arm after acquiring the authority of the legitimate Ruler of the [[Greek Underworld]]. This demonstrates that sufficiently powerful Divine authority can overcome her defenses.[1]
- Jurisdictional Limitations: Persephone's Underworld authority allowed her to banish Nyx from the territory governed by the legitimate Ruler, even though Nyx retained a separate private Primordial domain.[1][2]
- Exceptional Draconic Perception: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] detected Nyx at extremely close range through his unusual Draconic Eyes, surprising her because the feat exceeded her expectations for a young Dragon.[4]
- Specialized Magical Countermeasures: [[Hecate]] developed elaborate protections intended to obstruct the perception of Original [[Primordial Gods]] such as Nyx and [[Erebus]].[24]
- World Tree-Based Sealing: Erebus used a fragment of [[Yggdrasil]] to interfere with and seal Nyx's Primordial power, demonstrating that artifacts tied to the foundational supernatural structure of a Pantheon could threaten even Original Primordial Gods.[19]
- Concealment Enhancement Requirements: Nyx's more advanced concealment was improved through Victor's support and access to compatible supernatural Energy. The degree to which its most extreme descriptions can be maintained without that support was not independently quantified.[7]
- Conceptual Jurisdiction: Nyx's Divine authority over Night should not be generalized into ownership of every manifestation of Night across all Sectors of Creation.[21]
- Unconfirmed Absolute Immunities: Nyx has not individually demonstrated unconditional immunity to every form of Fate Manipulation, Causality Manipulation, Soul Manipulation, Conceptual Manipulation, Reality Warping or Existence Erasure. Her Primordial and Draconic status do not automatically establish these abilities without explicit feats or statements.

Nyx can manifest a large black scythe while releasing a greater portion of her Original Primordial nature. The weapon appeared alongside her star-filled Divine Raiment during the confrontation involving [[Erebus]] and [[Nocturnus]]. Nyx later used the scythe to attack Erebus directly, demonstrating that it functions as a genuine Divine weapon rather than merely an ornamental component of her appearance.[3][27]
- Type: Divine Weapon
- User: Nyx Elderblood
- Manifestation: Appears through Nyx's Divine power, particularly when she releases her Primordial nature.[3]
- [[Divine Energy]] Compatibility: Nyx can wield the weapon while manifesting her Primordial Divine power.[3]
- Close-Quarters Combat: The scythe can be employed directly against supernatural opponents, as demonstrated during the confrontation with Erebus.[27]
- Primordial Power Integration: The weapon appears alongside Nyx's Divine Raiment and is associated with the greater release of her ancient powers.[3]

Nyx can manifest clothing resembling an immense star-filled night as part of her Primordial Divine appearance. The Raiment reflects her authority over Night and appears when she releases a greater portion of her supernatural power.[3]
The Raiment should be treated as part of Nyx's Divine manifestation rather than as an independently named artifact possessing unconfirmed defensive enchantments or separate special abilities.
