Erebus was one of the original First-Generation [[Primordial Gods]] of the [[Greek Pantheon]], the brother and former husband of Nyx, and the father of numerous Second-Generation Primordial Gods. His primary Divine Concept was Darkness, while he also possessed an explicitly stated Greater Space Divinity.[2] He was among the ancient Divine powers secretly involved with [[New Dawn]] and the conspiracy surrounding the collapse of the old Greek order.
Erebus later became one of the principal opponents faced by [[Victor Elderblood]] during the destruction of [[Olympus]]. Alongside [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] and his hidden son [[Nocturnus]], he pushed Victor into one of the most dangerous battles of his life, using Darkness, Space and extreme gravitational manipulation in combination with Nocturnus' Void. Following Victor's evolution and conquest of Olympus, Erebus lost his connection to the Pantheon's territory, was wounded by Nyx and was ultimately killed when Victor crushed his head and absorbed his body.[1]

### Physical Appearance
Erebus' humanoid appearance was that of a tall man with a healthy complexion, long black hair and features described as oriental. He dressed in a black suit decorated with gold trim.[3]
His humanoid body was not the only shape in which he manifested. Erebus could appear as an enormous mass of supernatural Darkness and later demonstrated the ability to transform his body into Pure Darkness. In this state his physical form could dissolve into Darkness instead of behaving as conventional flesh, allowing him to disappear, evade attacks and reform elsewhere.[4][5]

### Personality
Erebus was ancient, calculating and accustomed to operating through indirect manipulation rather than exposing himself unnecessarily. This was partly a consequence of his status as a First-Generation Primordial God: direct conflicts between Beings such as Erebus and Nyx could damage the Pantheon they helped sustain, encouraging the oldest Greeks to manipulate events through other Gods, armies, artifacts and political schemes instead of constantly fighting personally. Erebus participated in a conspiracy involving [[Zeus]], [[Kronos]], [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] and outside powers, prepared specialized countermeasures against Nyx and helped create circumstances in which the different Greek factions could destroy or weaken one another.[6]
His confidence frequently developed into arrogance. Erebus initially believed the Greek crisis could still be directed according to his plans and badly underestimated how completely [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s involvement would disrupt them. Unlike many arrogant Gods, however, he was capable of recognizing that mistake. When the destruction of the Pantheon became a genuine possibility, Erebus openly admitted to himself that he had been arrogant and supported abandoning portions of the original plan in favor of contacting [[Gaia]] and preserving what remained of Greece. He rejected [[Amon]]'s objection by emphasizing that any conspiracy became meaningless if the Pantheon itself was destroyed.[7]
Erebus was nevertheless ruthless and profoundly self-serving. His marriage to Nyx eventually collapsed after he became dissatisfied with her and pursued a Primordial Goddess from another Pantheon. The relationship was not merely an affair: Erebus deliberately used the union to produce [[Nocturnus]], hoping a child born between [[Primordial Gods]] aligned with opposite sides of Balance would be naturally neutral and eventually capable of becoming the Primordial Entity representing the Void. He concealed Nocturnus from Nyx for years and later became involved with the collection of Gods absorbed into [[New Dawn]]'s larger conspiracy.[8][1]
Despite treating Nocturnus' birth as part of an experiment, Erebus was not emotionally indifferent toward his son. He coordinated closely with him in combat, immediately reacted when Nocturnus was endangered and lost his composure when Victor finally consumed him. Fear could similarly break Erebus' normal restraint. When Victor nearly struck his head with a punch powerful enough that Erebus believed he had almost died, Erebus experienced the proximity of death for the first time in his existence. Humiliation and fear drove him into a rage in which he released his Dark Power without caring whether [[Olympus]] survived the resulting destruction.[9]

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Erebus was first directly named while [[Nightingale]] was under Demonic attack. When the Demons demonstrated the ability to create passages into supernatural dimensions that should have been extraordinarily difficult to breach, Hilda used Erebus as an example of the level of conceptual authority required. She identified him as Nyx's brother and stated that he possessed a Greater Space Divinity; according to Hilda, Erebus could potentially use that authority to tear a hole through Nightingale's defenses, although accomplishing the same feat against the territory of the Witch Queen was considered beyond even him.[2]

#### King Among Kings
Erebus personally entered the story after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] began investigating the remnants of [[New Dawn]]. He manifested near [[Taranis]] and [[Anubis]] as a mass of Darkness, immediately drawing Nyx's attention. Nyx recognized the Being as both her brother and former husband and was suspicious that Erebus had supplied his Energy to a foreign God such as Taranis. Erebus examined the residual Energy left at the scene and correctly identified it as Dragon Breath associated with Alucard. His presence was sufficiently oppressive that Taranis visibly reacted to Erebus' gaze. After Anubis and Taranis departed, Erebus silently confronted Nyx with his gaze before disappearing.[4]
Much later, as Victor's faction entered the [[Greek Underworld]], Nyx sensed Erebus hiding nearby and forced him to reveal himself. Erebus covered the surroundings in supernatural Darkness through which ordinary light could not pass. Nyx and [[Aphrodite]] answered by covering themselves in their respective Divinities and dispelling his influence. Erebus explained that a direct battle between First-Generation [[Primordial Gods]] was undesirable because Beings such as himself and Nyx constituted part of the foundation supporting the [[Greek Pantheon]]. His objective was therefore not initially to duel Nyx but to exploit her arrival as part of a larger trap.[6]
The scale of that trap became apparent when Nyx observed [[Zeus]] and Kronus standing together despite their ancient hatred. [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] then seized Nyx while Erebus activated a specialized countermeasure: a fragment of [[Yggdrasil]]. Because the Greek Pantheon's existence had originally developed through the influence of a World Tree, its fragment could interfere with the Primordial Gods born alongside that system. The artifact sealed approximately half of Nyx's Power, reducing her to an output Erebus assessed as comparable to a High-Level God and, in his estimation, probably suppressing some of the privileges associated with her Primordial status as well. Aphrodite attempted to use her Charm at full force, but Erebus stated that it did not work upon Primordial Gods of their level.[6]
Erebus' plan immediately deteriorated when [[Scathach]] used Victor's emergency summoning artifact. After Victor manifested, Erebus attempted to withdraw into his Darkness, only for Victor's Negative Energy to form a hand capable of physically grasping his shadow and dragging him back out. Erebus was shocked because Darkness was his own Concept and conventional Gods could not simply seize a shadow in that fashion.[6]
Victor then attacked Erebus directly. Erebus gathered his Power and transformed into a Being of Pure Darkness, but Victor pierced through his chest and specifically attempted to destroy the Primordial God's Soul. His hand encountered an invisible spiritual barrier before the attack could completely erase Erebus, giving the God enough time for his body to dissolve. Erebus escaped while mocking Victor for believing a Primordial God could be killed so easily, although the encounter demonstrated that Victor possessed attacks capable of reaching beneath Erebus' ordinary physical manifestation.[5]
As the Greek conflict continued, Erebus was forced to reassess the situation. Victor's ability to permanently kill Gods and turn the dead into forces under his own command transformed numerical superiority into a liability for [[Olympus]].[10] Erebus ultimately agreed with Tartarus that survival and preservation of the Pantheon had become more important than blindly continuing their existing strategy. He acknowledged that he had been arrogant in his earlier calculations and supported contacting [[Gaia]] rather than allowing Olympus to be destroyed simply to protect their original conspiracy.[7]
The decisive battle revealed one of Erebus' greatest secrets. Much of [[Persephone]]'s army consisted of children born from Nyx and Erebus and Gods descended from those Second-Generation Primordials, including figures connected to Death, Sleep, Dreams and Revenge. Erebus then called forward a previously hidden son, [[Nocturnus]]. Nyx immediately realized that the young Primordial was not her child. Victor exposed the truth: Erebus had fathered Nocturnus with a Primordial Goddess from a different Pantheon whose Concept belonged to the opposite side of Balance. Erebus had hoped their offspring would emerge without a Positive or Negative alignment and eventually develop into a genuine Primordial Entity embodying the Void. The experiment produced a powerful neutral God of the Void, but potential alone had not allowed Nocturnus to cross the enormous boundary separating a Pantheon God from a true Primordial Entity.[8]
Erebus, Nocturnus and Tartarus subsequently attacked Victor together. Erebus transformed the battlefield by declaring himself the Darkness of Olympus and pouring enough Darkness from his body to engulf the area. Victor's normal vision was completely obscured, although his Dragon-enhanced sight remained capable of perceiving his opponents. Erebus' Darkness had an additional strategic effect: Nocturnus' Void Divinity became stronger while operating inside it. Nocturnus could therefore generate more dangerous spatial phenomena from inside a battlefield Erebus had shaped specifically around their compatible powers.[11]
Victor answered by covering [[Junketsu]] with Negative Energy and cutting through both Erebus' Darkness and Nocturnus' nearby spatial phenomenon. Erebus then exploited another opening by attacking Victor with Darkness-formed daggers, only to discover that the weapons could not pierce Victor's Draconic body. He dissolved back into Darkness and correctly concluded that ordinary Darkness was not sufficient to overcome Victor's defenses; Nocturnus' Void was their most promising method of inflicting meaningful damage. Erebus also used Darkness defensively, interposing it when Victor attempted to reach Nocturnus.[11]
Father and son coordinated mentally as the fight intensified. Erebus combined his Darkness with a Lesser Divinity associated with the Space where Darkness lingers and generated an enormous Gravitational Field. The initial field subjected Victor to more than one thousand times Earth's gravity. When Victor escaped and attacked Erebus, the God of Darkness vanished from the attack zone and reappeared elsewhere, but Victor reached him again before Nocturnus erected a Void barrier between them. Victor shattered the barrier and would have struck Erebus' head if Tartarus had not intervened. Even the pressure generated by the interrupted punch threw Erebus away and cut his face, making him experience the threat of death with an immediacy he had never felt before.[9]
Humiliated and enraged, Erebus transformed into Pure Darkness and released his Power without concern for the condition of Olympus. His Gravitational Field returned stronger than before, crushing Victor toward the ground hard enough to repeatedly break his bones while his regeneration repaired them. Erebus continued increasing its intensity by hundreds of times while Nocturnus attacked the immobilized Victor with Void techniques. The Power Erebus released caused Olympus itself to tremble, demonstrating both the scale of his Divinity and the reason First-Generation Primordials normally avoided unrestricted combat within their own Pantheon.[9]
The confrontation ultimately failed to kill Victor. The battle instead contributed to Victor's evolution into the Primogenitor of the Draconic Vampires, after which the entire balance of the conflict changed. Victor consumed Zeus and [[Kronos]] and acquired the position necessary to take control of Olympus. Erebus felt his ancient connection to Mount Olympus begin slipping away and was stunned by something even Zeus had been incapable of doing to him. Nyx explained that Victor had consumed the previous rulers and was now using his new Draconic authority to impose himself upon the dimension.[1]
Victor eliminated Nocturnus first. Erebus screamed when he saw his son consumed, and that momentary loss of concentration allowed Nyx to pierce his abdomen with her scythe. Rather than kill him herself, Nyx threw Erebus before Victor and requested only the truth concerning his past betrayal. Erebus began cursing Victor, but Victor silenced him with a stomp that crushed his head and killed him before absorbing the Primordial God's body.[1]
The memories Victor obtained finally exposed Erebus' motives. He had eventually become dissatisfied with Nyx and pursued another Primordial Goddess. Around the same period, he developed the idea of creating a naturally neutral offspring capable of disrupting Balance. Years later, a group of Gods recruited him into the greater plan surrounding [[Diablo]]: the construction of a Pantheon dominated by Beings associated with the Negative side of existence. Even Erebus, however, had not possessed complete information about New Dawn. Victor discovered that New Dawn had effectively merged with or operated through an older Divine organization whose membership consisted primarily—but not exclusively—of Gods aligned with the Negative side of Balance. Erebus' memories also confirmed that Nocturnus' mother was a foreign Primordial Goddess who did not belong to Earth.[1]

### Skills and Abilities
Erebus was a First-Generation Primordial God and one of the foundational Divine Beings of the [[Greek Pantheon]]. His combat abilities centered overwhelmingly upon Darkness, supported by an explicitly identified Greater Space Divinity and a Lesser spatial authority associated with regions permeated by his Darkness. Unlike a true Primordial Entity, however, Erebus had not literally become the universal Concept of Darkness. The story distinguishes Pantheon [[Primordial Gods]] from the much higher state in which an existence actually becomes its Concept; [[Nocturnus]] himself was described only as possessing the potential to reach that state.[8]

#### Primordial God Physiology
- Divine Physiology: Erebus was one of the First-Generation [[Primordial Gods]] born alongside the early [[Greek Pantheon]]. These Gods functioned as part of the supernatural foundation sustaining their Pantheon, to the point that unrestricted battles between First-Generation Primordials could damage the Divine system around them.[6]
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics: Erebus possessed the physical and energetic capabilities necessary to participate directly in combat against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]], [[Nocturnus]], Nyx and other entities operating at the highest level of the Greek Pantheon.[5][9]
- Ancient Divine Power: His unrestricted release of Dark Power was enough to make [[Olympus]] tremble while simultaneously maintaining an extreme Gravitational Field against Victor.[9]
- Primordial Soul: Erebus possessed a Soul sufficiently resilient that Victor's direct attempt to erase it did not immediately destroy him. An invisible barrier interrupted Victor's Soul-targeting strike and provided Erebus enough time to dissolve and retreat.[5]
- Pantheon Connection: Erebus possessed an intrinsic connection to Mount Olympus as one of the first Gods born within the Greek Divine system. Even [[Zeus]] could not ordinarily sever this connection. Victor only began stripping it away after consuming both Zeus and [[Kronos]], becoming the rightful authority over Olympus and gaining the ability to impose his Draconic authority upon the dimension.[1]
- [[Divine Presence]]: Erebus' presence was oppressive even to other Gods. [[Taranis]] visibly shuddered when Erebus focused his attention upon him.[4]
- Power/Energy Bestowal: Erebus could provide portions of his Energy to other Gods. Nyx specifically questioned why her brother was supplying his Energy to the foreign Celtic God Taranis.[4]

#### Divinities
- Darkness: Erebus' defining Divine Concept and the foundation of the majority of his demonstrated techniques. He could generate, manipulate, weaponize and become supernatural Darkness on an enormous scale.[6][11][9]
- Greater Space Divinity: Erebus explicitly possessed a Greater Divinity related to Space. Hilda considered his spatial authority sufficient to potentially tear a passage into [[Nightingale]] despite the dimension's protection, although she also stated that forcing such an entrance into the Witch Queen's personal territory would still be impossible for him.[2]
- Lesser Spatial Divinity — Space Where Darkness Lingers: Erebus possessed another spatial aspect associated specifically with Space occupied by Darkness. By combining this Lesser Divinity with his Darkness, he generated the extreme Gravitational Field used against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[9]

#### Darkness Manipulation
- Umbrakinesis/Darkness Manipulation: Erebus possessed overwhelming supernatural control over Darkness, treating it as both an environmental Concept and a directly manipulable form of Divine Power.[6][11]
- Darkness Generation: He could continuously generate enormous quantities of Darkness from his own body.[11]
- Large-Scale Darkness Field: Erebus could saturate a battlefield in Darkness, covering the surroundings after declaring himself the "Darkness of [[Olympus]]".[11]
- Light Negation: The Darkness he manifested in the [[Greek Underworld]] prevented ordinary light from passing through the affected area.[6]
- Vision Obscuration: Erebus' large-scale Darkness completely removed [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ordinary visual perception of the battlefield. Victor only continued tracking his enemies because his Draconic vision could perceive through the obstruction.[11]
- Concealment: Erebus could retreat into surrounding Darkness and attempt to hide his presence within it.[6]
- Darkness Weapon Creation: He could condense Darkness into weapons. During the battle against Victor, Erebus used Darkness daggers in an attempt to exploit an opening in the Dragon's defenses.[11]
- Darkness Barrier/Interposition: Erebus could place Darkness between an attacker and another target. He used his Darkness to interfere when Victor attempted to reach [[Nocturnus]].[11]
- Dark Power Explosion: After nearly being killed by Victor, Erebus erupted with a massive quantity of Dark Power before transforming into Pure Darkness and fighting without restraint.[9]
- Environmental Empowerment: Saturating the battlefield in Erebus' Darkness made the environment advantageous to Darkness-related abilities and directly increased the effectiveness of Nocturnus' Void Divinity.[11]
- Darkness-Void Synergy: Erebus' Darkness possessed exceptional compatibility with Nocturnus' Void. The son's Divinity became stronger inside his father's Darkness, allowing the two to combine battlefield obstruction, spatial phenomena, Gravity and Void attacks against Victor.[11][9]

#### Pure Darkness Form
- Elemental Mimicry (Darkness): Erebus could transform from his humanoid manifestation into a Being of Pure Darkness.[5][9]
- Body Dissolution: His physical body could dissolve into Darkness rather than remaining conventionally solid.[5][11]
- Darkness Dispersion: Erebus could disperse after an attack and subsequently continue operating through his surrounding Darkness.[5]
- Evasive Transformation: Dissolving into Darkness allowed Erebus to avoid remaining exposed as a conventional physical target after an unsuccessful attack.[5][11]
- Darkness-Based Concealment: The form could merge with Erebus' pre-existing Darkness, making conventional visual tracking substantially more difficult.[6][11]
- Non-Absolute Intangibility: Becoming Darkness did not make Erebus impossible to affect. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Negative Energy could directly seize and cut his Darkness, bypassing one of the form's most important defensive advantages.[6][11]

#### Space Manipulation
- Spatiokinesis: Erebus could manipulate Space through his Greater Space Divinity.[2]
- Dimensional Breaching: Hilda considered Erebus capable of using his Space Divinity to forcibly tear a hole into [[Nightingale]]'s protected dimensional structure.[2]
- Dimensional Limitation: The same statement explicitly established a ceiling to this ability: Hilda did not believe even Erebus could force an equivalent breach directly into the Witch Queen's territory.[2]
- Rapid Repositioning: During his fight with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Erebus vanished from the area of an incoming punch and immediately reappeared elsewhere. The scene establishes instantaneous supernatural repositioning, although it does not explicitly identify whether Erebus accomplished it through Space, Darkness or another application of his power.[9]

#### Gravity Manipulation
- Gravity Manipulation: By combining Darkness with his Lesser Divinity concerning the Space where Darkness lingers, Erebus could directly alter gravitational force across the battlefield.[9]
- Gravitational Field: Erebus created a broad field that massively increased the gravity acting upon [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[9]
- 1,000× Earth Gravity: The first explicitly quantified field exceeded one thousand times Earth's gravity.[9]
- Gravity Amplification: Erebus repeatedly increased the field when Victor continued resisting, including increases by hundreds of times beyond its previous output.[9]
- Immobilization: The field could force Victor toward the ground and severely restrict his movement, allowing [[Nocturnus]] to target him with Void attacks.[9]
- Bone Destruction: At higher output, the gravitational pressure continuously broke Victor's bones while his regeneration repaired the damage.[9]
- Area Destruction: Increasing the gravity caused the surrounding terrain to collapse around Victor.[9]
- Battlefield Suppression: When combined with Nocturnus' Void-based suppression of Victor's speed, Erebus' Gravity became one of the principal methods by which the trio temporarily restrained the Demon King.[9]

#### Power Sealing
- Power Sealing (via [[Yggdrasil]] Fragment): Erebus possessed a fragment of Yggdrasil prepared as a specialized emergency countermeasure against powerful members of his own Pantheon.[6]
- Primordial God Suppression: The fragment could interfere with First-Generation Greek Primordials because the Pantheon and its earliest Gods had developed through the influence of a World Tree.[6]
- Power Reduction: Erebus successfully sealed approximately half of Nyx's Power.[6]
- Divine Rank Suppression: After examining the result, Erebus assessed Nyx's remaining output as approximately High-Level God territory.[6]
- Primordial Privilege Suppression: Erebus believed the process had probably sealed part of the special privileges Nyx possessed as a Primordial God as well.[6]
- Artifact Dependency: This was not an innate Darkness or Space technique. The sealing depended upon the Yggdrasil fragment specifically prepared for this purpose.[6]

#### Extrasensory Perception and Analysis
- Supernatural Energy Perception: Erebus could perceive and distinguish supernatural Energy signatures with considerable precision.[4][6]
- Energy Signature Identification: After examining residual Energy, Erebus recognized it as Dragon Breath and connected it to Alucard.[4]
- Unknown-Energy Detection: Erebus immediately noticed the unfamiliar reddish-pink Energy strengthening [[Aphrodite]] and recognized that an exceptionally powerful and pure outside influence was affecting her normal [[Divine Energy]].[6]
- Power Analysis: Erebus analyzed the results of the [[Yggdrasil]] seal and estimated both Nyx's remaining level and the approximate proportion of her Power that had been suppressed.[6]
- Combat Analysis: After his Darkness failed to penetrate [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s body, Erebus rapidly concluded that [[Nocturnus]]' Void was the trio's most effective available method of overcoming Victor's Negative Energy defenses.[11][9]

#### Telepathy
- Telepathy: Erebus could communicate directly through the mind. He and [[Nocturnus]] exchanged tactical information mentally while [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] occupied [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] in close combat.[9]
- Silent Battlefield Coordination: This communication allowed father and son to plan their attacks without relying upon ordinary speech and combine Gravity, Darkness and Void more efficiently.[9]

#### Combat and Strategic Skills
- Primordial-Level Combat Experience: As an ancient First-Generation God, Erebus possessed extensive experience with Divine conflict and was capable of operating effectively alongside other [[Primordial Gods]].[6][9]
- Multi-Power Combat: Erebus could simultaneously maintain battlefield Darkness, manipulate Gravity, transform his body and coordinate with [[Nocturnus]] while fighting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[11][9]
- Tactical Coordination: Erebus understood how to use [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]]' close-range pressure and Nocturnus' Void alongside his own Darkness and Gravity, allowing three very different Divine authorities to cover one another's weaknesses.[11][9]
- Strategic Planning: Erebus participated in long-term schemes involving [[Zeus]], [[Kronos]], Tartarus, Nyx, the [[Greek Pantheon]] and forces eventually incorporated into [[New Dawn]]. His preparations included political manipulation, alliances between traditional enemies and specialized anti-Primordial artifacts.[6][7]
- Pragmatic Retreat: Erebus was willing to disengage when continued battle provided no useful advantage. After Victor breached his defenses in their first clash, Erebus dissolved into Darkness and escaped rather than allowing pride to force him into an unnecessary death.[5]
- Strategic Adaptability: When the original Greek conspiracy became unsustainable, Erebus admitted his own arrogance and supported changing objectives from completing the plan to preserving the Pantheon itself.[7]
- Target Prioritization: He quickly identified Nocturnus' Void as the most effective tool against Victor and shifted their strategy around creating openings for his son.[11][9]

#### Resistances and Defensive Capabilities
- Resistance to Charm: [[Aphrodite]] used her Charm at full power against [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] and Erebus, but Erebus stated that it did not work on [[Primordial Gods]] of their level.[6]
- Soul Manipulation/Soul Destruction Resistance (Partial): When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] pierced Erebus' heart and directly targeted his Soul, an invisible barrier prevented the attack from immediately erasing him and allowed Erebus to escape.[5]
- Limit: The barrier was not proof of absolute immunity to Soul attacks. Victor had successfully reached Erebus' deeper existence and forced him to abandon the confrontation rather than continuing to expose himself.[5]
- Darkness-Based Evasion: Erebus could avoid remaining a conventional solid target by dissolving his body into Darkness.[5][11]
- Pantheon-Supported Authority: His ancient connection to [[Olympus]] granted him a foundation that conventional rulers of the Pantheon could not simply remove. Victor required the authority obtained by consuming [[Zeus]] and [[Kronos]] together with his new Draconic state to begin stripping that connection away.[1]

#### Limitations and Counters
- Negative Energy: [[Victor Elderblood]]'s Negative Energy was an exceptionally effective counter to Erebus. It could physically seize his Darkness when he attempted to hide, and Negative Energy coating [[Junketsu]] could cut directly through the Darkness saturating the battlefield.[6][11]
- Draconic Perception: Erebus could completely obstruct Victor's normal sight, but Victor's Dragon-enhanced perception could still see through the Darkness.[11]
- Insufficient Penetration Against Victor: Erebus' Darkness daggers failed to pierce Victor's Draconic body, forcing him to rely more heavily upon [[Nocturnus]]' Void as the battle progressed.[11]
- Soul Defense Is Not Absolute: The invisible protection around Erebus' Soul stopped Victor from instantly destroying him, but it did not make spiritual attacks irrelevant; Erebus immediately chose to retreat after Victor reached that layer of his existence.[5]
- Spatial Authority Has Limits: Although Erebus' Greater Space Divinity was powerful enough to theoretically breach [[Nightingale]], Hilda explicitly considered a similar forced entry into the Witch Queen's territory impossible for him.[2]
- Pantheon Connection Can Be Overridden: Erebus believed his connection to [[Olympus]] could not be severed by conventional Greek rulers, but Victor's acquisition of the Pantheon's rightful ruling Authority allowed him to begin removing that support.[1]
- Emotional Instability Under Mortal Danger: Erebus was normally calculating, but experiencing the threat of death at point-blank range caused fear and humiliation to overwhelm his restraint. He began releasing his full Power without caring about the damage inflicted upon Olympus.[9]
- Primordial God ≠ Primordial Entity: Erebus' statements identifying himself with Darkness reflect his Divine identity and authority, not proof that he was the absolute universal Darkness Concept. The same battle establishes that becoming one's Concept and reaching the state of a genuine Primordial Entity was a separate and far higher threshold that even extraordinary Gods had not crossed at the time.[8]

#### Equipment
- Fragment of [[Yggdrasil]] (Former): A specialized fragment of a World Tree kept as an emergency countermeasure against threats connected to the [[Greek Pantheon]]. Erebus used it to suppress approximately half of Nyx's total Power and probably part of her Primordial privileges.[6]
- Pantheon Foundation Interaction: The fragment worked because [[World Trees]] are involved in the establishment of Pantheons, allowing an object from that class of existence to interfere with [[Primordial Gods]] born alongside the Pantheon's foundation.[6]
- Lost: Erebus did not retain the fragment after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] intervened and dismantled the trap surrounding Nyx.[5]

### Trivia
- Erebus' first verified direct mention is in Chapter 528, where Hilda identifies him as Nyx's brother and specifically names his Greater Space Divinity.[2]
- His first named on-page appearance occurs in Chapter 706, where he initially manifests as a mass of Darkness in the presence of [[Taranis]] and [[Anubis]].[4]
- Erebus and Nyx were both siblings and former spouses. Nyx directly recognized him as her brother and ex-husband.[4]
- Erebus' affair with the foreign Primordial Goddess was the event that ended his relationship with Nyx. Nyx knew he had betrayed her but did not know that the relationship had produced [[Nocturnus]] until the latter revealed himself during the battle for [[Olympus]].[8]
- Nocturnus' mother was a Primordial Goddess from another Pantheon and was explicitly described through Erebus' memories as a foreign Goddess who did not belong to Earth.[1]
- Nocturnus inherited neither the Positive nor Negative alignment expected from his parents and instead developed the Void, giving him the potential—but not the achieved status—of a future Primordial Entity associated with that Concept.[8]
- Erebus experienced the immediate threat of death for the first time when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] broke through Nocturnus' defense and nearly struck his head. [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] stopped the punch only centimeters before impact.[9]
- Erebus' final opening was created by his attachment to Nocturnus. When Victor consumed his son, Erebus screamed and lost focus long enough for Nyx to impale him and throw him before Victor.[1]
- Although Nyx delivered Erebus to Victor, she was not the one who killed him. Victor crushed Erebus' head with a stomp and then absorbed his body.[1]
- Victor's examination of Erebus' memories showed that Erebus himself did not possess complete knowledge of [[New Dawn]], indicating that even major ancient Gods participating in the conspiracy were compartmentalized from its entire structure.[1]
