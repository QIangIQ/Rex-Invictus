A high-order [[Divine Single Event Effect (DSEE)]] in which a surge of [[Divine Presence]] forces a system to instantiate a **partial metaphysical construct** within its operational layer.

Under baseline conditions, this remains localized and transient. During a **[[Godfall Event]]**, particularly those recorded in the [[War of Princes]], the effect escalates into a **multi-system ontological intrusion**, spreading across platforms, vessels, and networks within operational proximity.

---

### **Mechanism**

The affected system becomes an involuntary **[[Divine Anchor Node]]**, binding fragments of a collapsing [[Divine Domain]] into its architecture.

During standard exposure:

- A fragment of [[Divine Will Imprint]] embeds into system logic
- Symbolic constructs manifest within [[HUD Systems]] and control layers

During [[Godslaying]] or [[Divine Death]] events:

- The disintegrating entity propagates **[[Domain Bleedthrough]]** across nearby substrates
- Fractured authority emits **[[Authority Fragmentation Waves]]**
- Systems are subjected to **[[Mythological Rebinding]]**, attempting to preserve the god’s function through available hosts

These effects are not energy transfer, but **conceptual inheritance attempts**.

---

### **Observed Behavior**

#### **Standard Manifestation**

- [[HUD Systems]] display symbolic overlays (e.g., eyes, crowns, chains) interacting with tracked entities
- Defensive subsystems generate **non-physical constructs** resembling shields, wings, or sigils
- [[AI Subsystems]] shift into **liturgical or non-native linguistic patterns**

---

#### **Godfall-Amplified Manifestation**

Documented during the [[War of Princes]]:

- Hull surfaces and internal systems instantiate **partial [[Divine Avatars]]**
- [[Sensor Arrays]] detect entities defined by **expected presence**, not physical reality
- [[Void Shields]] reconfigure into **symbolic defense schemas**, prioritizing conceptual threats
- Command systems experience **[[Authority Override]]** by residual divine intent

Fleet-level effects include:

- Unprompted **behavioral synchronization** across vessels
- Emergence of **distributed will-patterns** consistent with the deceased entity’s domain

---

### **Range & Propagation**

During standard operation:

- Localized to system-level exposure

During [[Godfall Event]] conditions:

- Effective propagation radius: **~0.5 AU**

Within this range:

- [[Standard Vessels]] undergo immediate **cognitive and system-level compromise**
- Crew exposure results in **shared perceptual overlays** and compulsion states
- [[Command Vessels]] and heavily [[Divine-Hardened Systems]] demonstrate delayed degradation

Resistance is temporary and dependent on:

- [[Conceptual Anchoring Integrity]]
- [[Runic Insulation Density]]
- [[Authority Partitioning Depth]]

---

### **Duration**

- Standard: instantaneous to several seconds
- Godfall-amplified: persists for the duration of [[Divine Collapse]]

Residual artifacts may remain as:

- Embedded [[Metaphysical Residue]]
- Persistent [[Domain Alignment Drift]]
- Latent [[Autonomous Divine Behavior]] within affected systems
