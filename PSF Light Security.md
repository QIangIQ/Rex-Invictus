**Light Security personnel** are the first dedicated security-oriented rank above [[PSF Order/Orderlies]]. Whereas Orderlies primarily conduct mobile patrols and respond to routine civil disturbances, Light Security personnel are intended for **stationary security assignments in populated, generally peaceful environments**.

They are commonly deployed wherever a significant civilian presence creates a need for a visible, persistent security force, but where the likelihood of serious violence remains relatively low. Their presence is intended to deter crime, provide an immediate response to incidents, and prevent relatively minor disturbances from developing into larger security problems.

Typical assignments include **shopping centers, soup kitchens, public markets, transit terminals, civic buildings, recreational facilities, shelters, and other locations where large numbers of civilians regularly congregate.**

### Primary Duties

- Maintain a continuous security presence at assigned facilities.
- Guard entrances, exits, checkpoints, and restricted areas.
- Monitor civilian activity for criminal or suspicious behavior.
- Respond to disturbances occurring within their assigned facility.
- Assist [[PSF Order/Orderlies]] with incidents requiring additional personnel.
- Protect vulnerable civilian facilities and essential community services.
- Perform crowd monitoring during periods of unusually high civilian concentration.
- Prevent unauthorized entry into restricted areas.
- Conduct basic security screenings where required.
- Respond to alarms, panic signals, and security-system alerts.
- Detain individuals when legally authorized.
- Establish temporary security perimeters around incidents.
- Provide assistance during evacuations and other civilian emergencies.
- Protect civilians during the initial stages of a developing incident.
- Request higher-level PSF support when an incident exceeds their capabilities.

Light Security personnel are generally **assigned to a location rather than a patrol district**. A squad may therefore remain at the same shopping complex, public facility, or community center for an entire shift.

### Armor

Light Security personnel wear **medium protective armor**, approximately equivalent to **NIJ Level IIIA** protection in ballistic capability.

Their armor provides substantially greater protection than the lightweight thorax protection worn by Orderlies while remaining sufficiently comfortable for prolonged stationary duty.

A typical Light Security armor configuration includes:

- Level IIIA-rated thorax armor.
- Front and rear ballistic protection.
- Lightweight armored shoulder protection.
- Reinforced abdominal and flank sections.
- Protective duty helmet.
- Ballistic visor or eye protection where appropriate.
- Armored gloves.
- Reinforced boots.
- Identification and biometric systems.
- Integrated communications equipment.

The armor is primarily intended to protect against **handgun fire, fragmentation, and other common civilian threats**. It is not designed to withstand sustained rifle fire or battlefield-level weaponry.

### Standard Arms

Light Security personnel retain the relatively restrained weapon philosophy of the Orderlies but have access to somewhat greater firepower.

**Primary Weapon**

- Standard PSF service pistol.
- Secure retention holster.
- Integrated identification and biometric authorization systems.
- Weapon-mounted illumination and targeting systems where available.

**Less-Lethal Weapons**

- Stun gun or conducted electrical weapon.
- Collapsible baton.
- Other locally authorized non-lethal restraint equipment.

**Squad Equipment**

Depending on the assignment, a Light Security team may additionally possess:

- Portable barricades.
- Riot shields.
- Emergency medical equipment.
- Crowd-control equipment.
- Portable scanners.
- Dedicated communications equipment.
- Security drones.
- Portable lighting.
- Door-breaching equipment for emergency use.

Light Security units are **not normally issued rifles as standard personal weapons** in peaceful civilian environments. Heavier weapons are generally reserved for circumstances in which the unit is expecting a specific threat or for deployment of higher PSF ranks.

### Typical Assignments

Light Security is particularly common in areas where the PSF wants **more than ordinary police presence without creating the appearance of a military occupation**.

Examples include:

**Commercial Areas**

- Shopping malls.
- Markets.
- Department stores.
- Commercial plazas.
- Entertainment districts.

**Civilian Services**

- Soup kitchens.
- Food-distribution centers.
- Homeless shelters.
- Public aid offices.
- Community centers.
- Emergency relief facilities.

**Transportation**

- Bus terminals.
- Local train stations.
- Civilian landing facilities.
- Major pedestrian intersections.

**Public Spaces**

- Parks.
- Public squares.
- Recreational facilities.
- Large gathering areas.
- Queues and distribution centers.
