## Description

The **Saatverteilungs- und Zweitbefruchtungsautomatikgerät Demeter-Erweiterungssystem Mk.III** is a mechanized extension of Demeterian authority over growth, fertility, and agricultural dominance.

Worn as a **back-mounted dispersal rig with integrated forearm control manifolds**, the system automates:

- Seed deployment
    
- Soil conditioning
    
- Secondary fertilization cycles
    
- Controlled mutation pathways
    

It converts the operator from an individual cultivator into:

> **A mobile biosphere initiator capable of battlefield-scale ecological overwrite.**

---

## Core Functions

### Automated Seed Dispersal

- Multi-chamber reservoirs store:
    
    - Standard flora seeds
        
    - Combat-modified growth strains
        
- Deployment modes:
    
    - Aerosol spread
        
    - Directed burst
        
    - Subsurface injection
        

---

### Secondary Fertilization Engine

- Releases **nutrient-dense catalytic slurry**
    
- Enhances:
    
    - Growth rate
        
    - Structural density
        
    - Root network expansion
        

---

### Mutation and Control Lattice

- Encoded genetic directives applied at germination
    
- Enables:
    
    - Thorned growth
        
    - Rapid entanglement structures
        
    - Nutrient siphoning organisms
        

Operator maintains **neural-linked control** over growth patterns.

## Capabilities

- **Instant Terrain Conversion**  
    Converts barren or hostile environments into controlled vegetation zones
    
- **Area Denial via Overgrowth**  
    Rapid plant expansion restricts enemy movement
    
- **Self-Sustaining Ecosystems**  
    Deployed flora continues growth without further input
    

---

## Limitations

- Reduced effectiveness in:
    
    - Sterile or irradiated substrates
        
    - Extreme thermal environments
        
- Requires initial biological material reserves
    
- High metabolic synchronization load on user
