**Medium Security personnel** constitute the first PSF tier intended for **high-value and strategically important security assignments**. Unlike [[PSF Light Security]], which primarily protects civilian-oriented locations in relatively peaceful environments, Medium Security is responsible for safeguarding locations whose loss, compromise, or disruption could have significant governmental, economic, military, or administrative consequences.

They are commonly stationed at **government buildings, administrative complexes, major infrastructure, strategic communications facilities, courts, command centers, financial institutions, and other designated high-importance locations**.

Medium Security personnel are substantially better equipped than both Orderlies and Light Security. Their equipment allows them to respond independently to serious armed incidents rather than merely containing an incident until heavier forces arrive.

### Primary Duties

- Guard high-value government and administrative facilities.
- Secure entrances, checkpoints, restricted areas, and internal access corridors.
- Protect government officials and other designated personnel while within secured facilities.
- Monitor and control access to classified or sensitive areas.
- Respond to armed intrusions and hostile personnel.
- Defend their assigned facility against organized attacks.
- Conduct regular security patrols throughout protected complexes.
- Operate security checkpoints and identification systems.
- Monitor surveillance networks and automated security systems.
- Respond to alarms, breaches, sabotage, and attempted infiltration.
- Establish defensive perimeters during emergencies.
- Coordinate with [[PSF Light Security]] and [[PSF Order/Orderlies]] during large incidents.
- Provide security during important governmental functions and public events.
- Assist in the evacuation or relocation of protected personnel.
- Maintain control of a facility until [[PSF Heavy Security]] or specialized PSF units arrive when an incident exceeds their capabilities.

### Armor and Protection

Medium Security personnel wear **higher-tier protective armor** designed for sustained exposure to armed threats.

Their armor provides substantially greater coverage and protection than the Level IIIA-equivalent equipment used by Light Security. It is intended to protect against **rifle-caliber threats, fragmentation, and other weapons commonly encountered during serious security incidents**.

A typical Medium Security configuration includes:

- Rifle-rated ballistic chest and back protection.
- Armored side protection.
- Reinforced abdominal and pelvic protection.
- Ballistic shoulder and upper-arm protection.
- Armored thigh and knee protection where appropriate.
- Reinforced ballistic helmet.
- Ballistic visor or eye protection.
- Respiratory protection capability.
- Armored gloves.
- Reinforced combat boots.
- Integrated communications system.
- Biometric identification and authentication systems.

The armor is still designed around **security operations rather than frontline military combat**. Medium Security personnel are capable of fighting, but their primary purpose is to hold and secure strategically important locations.

### Standard Arms

Unlike Light Security, **rifles are standard equipment** for Medium Security personnel.

**Primary Weapon**

- Standard-issue PSF service rifle or carbine.
- Typically equipped with an optical sighting system.
- Weapon-mounted illumination.
- Identification and biometric authorization systems.
- Ammunition load appropriate for sustained defensive operations.

**Secondary Weapon**

- Standard PSF service pistol.
- Secure retention holster.

**Less-Lethal Equipment**

Medium Security personnel retain access to less-lethal weapons for dealing with civilians and non-combatant threats:

- Stun weapons.
- Collapsible or reinforced baton.
- Restraint equipment.
- Crowd-control equipment where appropriate.

The presence of a rifle does not automatically authorize lethal force. Medium Security personnel continue to operate under PSF rules of engagement and are expected to distinguish between ordinary civilian disorder and genuine armed threats.

### Specialist Equipment

Because Medium Security units protect important facilities, they have access to considerably more sophisticated equipment than ordinary patrol personnel.

Depending on assignment, this can include:

- Portable ballistic shields.
- Tactical drones.
- Advanced surveillance equipment.
- Motion and intrusion sensors.
- Electronic access-control systems.
- Portable communications relays.
- Chemical and biological detection equipment.
- Emergency medical equipment.
- Breaching equipment.
- Portable barriers and defensive fortifications.
- Night-vision and low-light equipment.
- Electronic countermeasure equipment.
- Facility-specific security terminals.

Particularly important government installations may have **fixed defensive systems** that Medium Security personnel are trained and authorized to operate.

### Typical Assignments

Medium Security is commonly found protecting:

**Government**

- Government ministries.
- Administrative headquarters.
- Courts.
- Legislative buildings.
- Judicial facilities.
- Official archives.
- Government communications centers.

**Strategic Infrastructure**

- Power distribution facilities.
- Major communications stations.
- Water infrastructure.
- Transportation control centers.
- High-value industrial facilities.

**Security and Intelligence**

- PSF headquarters.
- Intelligence offices.
- Evidence repositories.
- Detention facilities.
- Sensitive research installations.

**Important Civilian Facilities**

- Major financial institutions.
- Large hospitals with strategic importance.
- Central transportation hubs.
- Critical food or resource distribution facilities.
