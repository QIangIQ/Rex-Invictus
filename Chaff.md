**[[Chaff]]** is a passive [[Electronic Countermeasures (ECM)]] technique within [[Electronic Warfare (EW)]] that disperses conductive material to generate **false sensor returns**, primarily against [[Radar Systems]].

It functions by creating a **high-density reflective cloud** that produces multiple false targets, masking or displacing the true position of a platform.

---

## Hierarchical Placement

- [[Electronic Warfare (EW)]]
    - [[Electronic Countermeasures (ECM)]]
        - [[Chaff]]

---

## Composition

[[Chaff]] consists of materials engineered to resonate with detection systems:

- [[Aluminium Strips]]
- [[Metallized Fibers]]
- [[Conductive Polymers]]

These are cut to lengths proportional to the **operating wavelength** of enemy [[Radar Systems]], maximizing reflectivity and signal return.

---

## Deployment Systems

[[Chaff]] is deployed via:

- [[Aircraft Countermeasure Dispensers]]
- [[Naval EW Launch Systems]]
- [[Missile Penetration Aid Packages]]

Upon release:

- Material disperses into a **particle cloud**
- The cloud expands and drifts, forming a **distributed reflective field**

---

## Operational Function

### [[Radar Saturation]]

- Generates large volumes of false returns on [[Radar Systems]]
- Obscures true signatures of:
    - [[Aircraft]]
    - [[Missiles]]
    - [[Naval Vessels]]

---

### [[Target Decoying]]

- Disrupts [[Radar-Guided Targeting]]
- Causes:
    - Lock deviation
    - Target misidentification
    - Guidance failure

---

### [[Penetration Aids]]

Used in:

- [[Ballistic Missile Systems]]
- [[Decoy Deployment Systems]]

To:

- Overwhelm tracking networks
- Increase probability of payload survival

---

## Advanced Counter-Countermeasures

Modern systems mitigate [[Chaff]] using:

### [[Doppler Filtering]]

- Differentiates targets based on velocity
- [[Chaff Clouds]] rapidly decelerate after deployment

---

### [[Multi-Spectral Tracking]]

- Combines:
    - [[Infrared Systems]]
    - [[Radar Systems]]
    - [[Optical Tracking]]

---

### [[AI-Assisted Target Discrimination]]

- Uses pattern recognition to:
    - Identify incoherent motion
    - Reject false targets

---

## Enhanced Variants

### [[JAFF]]

- Integration of [[Chaff]] with [[Electronic Jamming]]
- Maintains Doppler-consistent signatures

---

### [[CHILL]]

- [[Chaff]] illuminated by onboard systems
- Produces **coherent, trackable false targets**
