The 7.62×54R [[Divine Material High-Explosive (DMHE)]] round “PHOEBUS-3” is a fragmentation-enhanced blast cartridge engineered around a compact high-density explosive fill with divine-reactive particulate inclusions. The projectile utilizes a cast micro-crystalline RDX/HMX composite matrix with embedded trace [[Celestial Bronze]], [[Imperial Gold]], and [[Stygian Iron]] via [[Divine-Metallurgical Binding Impurities (DMBI)]], producing dual-domain blast and metaphysical disruption upon detonation.

The projectile is optimized for anti-personnel, light cover defeat, and confined-space shock effect rather than armor penetration. The explosive cavity incorporates a scored tungsten-steel jacket to ensure controlled high-velocity fragmentation. Divine inclusions ensure that fragmentation and overpressure effects interact lethally with [[Monsters (Greek)]], disrupt regenerative cycles, and induce destabilizing resonance in [[Demigod]] and [[Legacy]] combatants. Against purely mortal targets, the round functions as a standard [[High-Explosive (HE)]] fragmentation munition.
