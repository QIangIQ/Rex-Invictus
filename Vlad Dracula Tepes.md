## Definition

**[[Vlad Dracula Tepes]]** is a **[[Vampire Progenitor]]**, reigning **[[Vampire King]]**, and one of the oldest extant **[[Primordial Immortal Entities]]**, predating or rivaling early [[God-Class Entities]]. He is the foundational origin of the majority of modern [[Vampire Lineages]], and his existence represents a continuity of a far older, pre-divine power structure that once rivaled the dominant entities of ancient eras.

## Origin & Existence

[[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] is not the original mortal figure associated with the title “Impaler,” but rather a **[[Progenitor Entity]]** that subsumed and integrated that identity, selectively retaining its characteristics. This results in a hybridized existence:

- Simultaneously:
    - Original and non-original
    - Singular and composite
- Comparable to:
    - [[Identity Overlay Phenomena]]
    - [[Multi-Form Entity Structures]]

His lineage and species are implied to originate from **extra-planetary systems**, with independent evolutionary and cultural histories separate from Earth-based [[Divine Hierarchies]].

## Physical Description

[[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] does not possess a fixed form and exhibits **[[Formless Existence]]**:

- Default manifestation:
    - A being composed of **living darkness**
- Alternate forms include:
    - A human male (~210 cm, pale skin, black hair)
    - A medieval warlord in black armor
    - A divine-aligned warrior archetype

### [[True Form]]

- Grey-toned skin
- Black sclera with crimson irises
- Large wings composed of [[Blood Constructs]]
- Mouth of exposed, continuous teeth
- Clawed limbs
- Animated, shifting black markings across the body

## Personality & Behavioral Structure

[[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] is outwardly:

- [[Stoic]]
- [[Authoritarian]]
- Intolerant of weakness

Internally, he exhibits a contradiction:

- Desires successors capable of surpassing him
- Simultaneously creates dependency in subordinates

This produces:

- Chronic dissatisfaction with lineage strength
- Rejection of descendants deemed unworthy
