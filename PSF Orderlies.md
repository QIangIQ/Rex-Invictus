**Orderlies** are the lowest operational rank within the [[Planetary Security Forces]], serving as the primary uniformed presence for routine civil-order enforcement. Their duties are broadly analogous to those of contemporary police patrol officers, with an emphasis on maintaining public order, responding to minor disturbances, regulating civilian movement, and handling the innumerable mundane incidents that occur within populated areas.

Orderlies are typically the first PSF personnel encountered by ordinary civilians. They patrol assigned districts, respond to dispatch calls, assist civilians, manage minor accidents and disputes, enforce local ordinances, and provide initial responses to incidents that do not warrant the deployment of more heavily equipped PSF personnel.

Despite their low position within the PSF hierarchy, Orderlies perform a disproportionately important role in maintaining day-to-day stability. The vast majority of incidents encountered by the PSF never progress beyond the Orderly level.

### Primary Duties

- Respond to civilian disturbance and disorder calls.
- Investigate reports of minor theft, vandalism, trespassing, harassment, and property disputes.
- Break up fights and public disturbances.
- Direct and control civilian traffic during accidents, emergencies, or infrastructure failures.
- Operate and manually override local traffic-control systems when required.
- Assist stranded or injured civilians.
- Establish temporary perimeters around minor incidents.
- Perform routine patrols of assigned streets, transit hubs, commercial districts, and residential zones.
- Conduct preliminary investigations before transferring serious incidents to higher-ranking PSF personnel.
- Issue citations and warnings for minor violations.
- Detain individuals suspected of relatively minor offenses when necessary.
- Provide crowd-control assistance during public gatherings.
- Respond to emergency alarms and determine whether escalation is required.
- Assist higher-ranking PSF personnel during arrests, searches, and evacuations.
- Maintain basic incident records and submit reports through the PSF administrative network.
- Provide directions, information, and general assistance to civilians.
- Monitor public infrastructure for obvious hazards, damage, or malfunction.
- Act as the local PSF presence during periods of heightened security.

Orderlies are **not normally expected to handle military-grade threats**. Situations involving heavily armed criminals, organized insurgents, hostile nonhuman entities, supernatural phenomena, major civil unrest, or other serious threats are escalated to [[PSF Light Security]] or higher.

### Standard Equipment

An Orderly's equipment is intentionally lightweight. They are expected to remain on patrol for extended periods and interact with civilians regularly, making excessive armor or military equipment undesirable.

**Protective Equipment**

- Lightweight [[PSF Protective Vest]], generally comparable to a modern soft-ballistic or Kevlar thorax vest.
- Limited ballistic protection concentrated around the chest and upper back.
- Minimal protection against handgun and fragmentation threats.
- Standard PSF duty helmet or reinforced patrol cap, depending on jurisdiction.
- Lightweight gloves.
- Duty boots.
- Identification badge and biometric credentials.
- Optional lightweight protective eyewear.

The armor is designed primarily to provide protection against **common civilian weapons rather than battlefield threats**. Rifle fire, military weapons, heavy fragmentation, and supernatural attacks can readily defeat it.

### Standard Arms

Orderlies are deliberately issued weapons appropriate for routine law-enforcement duties rather than combat.

**Primary Sidearm**

- Standard-issue PSF service pistol.
- Usually chambered for a relatively low-recoil handgun cartridge.
- Equipped with weapon-mounted identification and biometric systems.
- Smart-link capable where supported by local PSF infrastructure.
- Normally carried in a secured retention holster.

**Less-Lethal Weapon**

- Conducted electrical weapon/stun gun.
- Used for incapacitating violent subjects without immediately resorting to lethal force.
- Particularly common when dealing with intoxicated, mentally distressed, or otherwise non-lethal threats.

**Additional Equipment**

- Hand restraints.
- Collapsible baton.
- Personal communicator/vox unit.
- Body recorder.
- Portable identification scanner.
- Basic medical kit.
- Flashlight.
- Traffic-control beacon.
- Emergency signaling device.
- Citation and documentation terminal.

Some jurisdictions additionally issue pepper irritants, restraint foam, or other non-lethal equipment, although these are dependent upon local PSF regulations.
