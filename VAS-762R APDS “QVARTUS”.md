The 7.62×54R [[Divine Material Armor-Piercing Fin-Stabilized Discarding Sabot (DMAPFSDS)]] round “QVARTUS” is a high-velocity long-rod penetrator cartridge engineered around an [[Advanced Depleted Uranium (ADU)]] core with embedded micro-phase divine inclusions. The penetrator exploits DU adiabatic shear behavior for self-sharpening penetration while incorporating trace [[Celestial Bronze]], [[Imperial Gold]], and [[Stygian Iron]] via [[Divine-Metallurgical Binding Impurities (DMBI)]] for anti-pantheonic counters.

The projectile is optimized for defeat of light armor and partial mitigation of [[Explosive Reactive Armor (ERA)]] through extreme sectional density, reduced frontal area, and fin-stabilized flight. Divine integration provides anti-reconstitution and anti-mythic lethality against [[Monsters (Greek)]], with enhanced terminal disruption against [[Demigod]] and [[Legacy]] combatants, while remaining functionally [[Kinetic (KNTC)]] against purely mortal targets.
