Defined by the [Répertoire du Savoir Athénien](Répertoire%20du%20Savoir%20Athénien.md)

**Norse / Germanic Pantheon**  
[[Asatru]] — Revival of Norse polytheism centered on a defined pantheon of gods.  
[[Runic magic]] — Use of runes associated with Norse cosmology and divine forces.  
[[Seidr]] — Norse magical practice involving interaction with fate and divine forces.

**Greek / Hellenic Pantheon**  
[[Dionysian Mysteries]] — Greek religious rites honoring Dionysus through ecstatic worship.  
[[Eleusinian Mysteries]] — Initiatory rites centered on Demeter and Persephone and cyclical rebirth.  
[[Orphic Mysteries]] — Greek tradition focused on divine origin of the soul and its purification.  
[[Samothracian Mysteries]] — Initiatory rites devoted to protective deities.

**Roman / Greco-Roman Pantheon**  
[[Haruspex]] — Roman religious practice interpreting divine will through sacrificial omens.  
[[Imperial Cult]] — Worship of rulers as divine or semi-divine beings within a state pantheon.  
[[Magic in the Greco-Roman World]] — Religious-magical practices involving gods, spirits, and fate.  
[[Mithraic Mysteries]] — Initiatory cult centered on the god Mithras and cosmic order.

**Egyptian Pantheon**  
[[Egyptian Book of the Dead]] — Funerary corpus describing interaction with a complex afterlife pantheon.

**Mesopotamian Pantheon**  
[[Mesopotamian Divination]] — Interpretation of omens as messages from a structured pantheon.

**Celtic Pantheon**  
[[Druidry]] — Spiritual tradition inspired by ancient Celtic priesthood and nature deities.

**Japanese Pantheon**  
[[Shinto]] — Japanese religion centered on kami (spirits/deities) inhabiting nature.  
[[Onmyōdō]] — Japanese cosmological practice involving spirits, yin-yang, and elemental forces.

**Chinese / Taoist Cosmology**  
[[Dao]] — Religious-philosophical system focused on alignment with the Tao and cosmic forces.  
[[Feng Shui]] — Cosmological system based on harmonizing forces derived from Taoist metaphysics.

**Abrahamic / Monotheistic Traditions**  
[[Key of Solomon]] — Grimoire outlining invocation of spiritual entities within a divine hierarchy.  

**Universal / Cross-Pantheon Esoteric Systems**  
[[Alchemy]] — Esoteric tradition of transformation linking material processes with spiritual refinement.  
[[Animism]] — Belief that natural phenomena and objects are inhabited by spirits.  
[[Hieros Gamos]] — Ritualized sacred union representing divine masculine and feminine principles.  
[[Mysticism]] — Direct experiential union or communion with the divine.  
[[Rosicrucianism]] — Esoteric tradition linking divine knowledge, symbolism, and cosmology.  
[[Shamanism]] — Practice of mediating between human and spirit worlds.  
[[Tarot card reading]] — Divination system drawing on symbolic archetypes with spiritual associations.  
[[Theosophy]] — System integrating global traditions into a unified divine cosmology.  
[[Western Mystery School]] — Structured esoteric traditions focused on progressive divine knowledge.  
[[White Magic]] — Practice of invoking supernatural forces for benevolent purposes.  
[[Witchcraft]] — Practice involving interaction with spiritual or divine forces through ritual.
