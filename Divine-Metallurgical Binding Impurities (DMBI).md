[[Divine-Metallurgical Binding Impurities (DMBI)]] are engineered trace-phase additives incorporated into enchanted alloys to stabilize lattice coherence between mundane atomic structures and metaphysically active substrates such as [[Celestial Bronze]], [[Imperial Gold]], and [[Stygian Iron]].

Unlike accidental contaminants, these [[Binding Impurities]] are intentionally introduced in sub-5% mass fractions to:

- Improve grain boundary stability
    
- Prevent phase separation between enchanted and mundane matrices
    
- Anchor [[The Mist]] interaction layer
    
- Reduce microfracture propagation under divine stress loads
    
- Maintain spectral conductivity for [[Electronic Warfare (EW)]] systems
    

They operate simultaneously at three levels:

1. Crystalline lattice stabilization
    
2. Electromagnetic conductivity tuning
    
3. Metaphysical resonance anchoring
