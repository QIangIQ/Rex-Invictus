**PSF Subject Guards** constitute the highest conventional operational rank within the Planetary Security Forces before personnel transition into strategic, specialized, or command formations. They are deployed in environments where the density of civilians, infrastructure, political importance, and supernatural activity makes conventional PSF intervention insufficient.

Where **Enforcers** are intended to identify and eliminate high-value threats before they become major crises, Subject Guards are intended to **directly confront threats that have already exceeded conventional security capabilities**.

Subject Guards are most heavily concentrated within **high-density megacities, planetary capitals, major transportation hubs, divine population centers, and other strategically important urban environments**. These areas experience a disproportionate number of incidents involving powerful supernatural entities, enhanced individuals, rogue Legacies, terrorist organizations, and other threats capable of overwhelming ordinary security forces.

A Subject Guard is consequently expected to operate in environments where an incident can escalate from a conventional security emergency into a **supernatural disaster within minutes**.

Their defining characteristic is their combination of **extreme personal survivability, anti-divine capability, and Grid integration**.

- High-threat urban intervention
- Neutralization of rampaging enhanced individuals
- Counter-Legacy operations
- Advanced anti-divine operations
- Containment of supernatural entities
- Neutralization of rogue divine servants
- Apprehension of extremely dangerous individuals
- Protection of high-value personnel during supernatural incidents
- Counter-terrorism against supernatural organizations
- Counter-insurgency against heavily enhanced rebel formations
- Hostage rescue against supernatural threats
- Protection of critical infrastructure during major incidents
- Urban monster suppression
- Supernatural disaster response
- Containment of uncontrolled magical phenomena
- Counter-infiltration against powerful supernatural entities
- High-risk breach operations
- Rapid intervention against escalating security crises
- Reinforcement of Enforcer teams
- Command of specialized PSF assault detachments
- Coordination with military anti-supernatural formations
- Coordination with divine or pantheonic authorities when required
- Establishment of supernatural exclusion zones
- Grid-supported precision engagement
- Civilian evacuation coordination during high-threat incidents
- Recovery and containment of anomalous materials
- Elimination of threats exceeding conventional PSF capability
