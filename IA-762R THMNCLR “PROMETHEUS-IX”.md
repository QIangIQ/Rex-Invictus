The 7.62×54R [[Thermonuclear (THMNCLR)]] micro-warhead round “PROMETHEUS-IX” is a sub-caliber, hyper-miniaturized thermonuclear cartridge engineered exclusively by [[Institute Academy]]. Unlike conventional nuclear munitions, the projectile contains a stabilized micro-fusion compression lattice utilizing Institute-developed singularity-triggered initiators.

The warhead employs a dual-stage nano-compression system in which a micro-fission primer induces an ultra-brief fusion reaction confined by gravitic containment microfields. Yield is tightly bounded to prevent uncontrolled atmospheric cascade while producing catastrophic localized overpressure and radiation output.

Deployment authority is restricted to strategic command tiers within the [[UOE]].
