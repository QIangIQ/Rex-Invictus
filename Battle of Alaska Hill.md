# Strategic Context

The [[Battle of Alaska Hill]] occurred during the early consolidation phase of the **Arctic Expansion Campaigns** of the 2030s.

The objective of these campaigns was the acquisition and defense of Arctic territory across:

- [[Greenland]]
    
- [[Northern Canada]]
    
- [[Alaska]]
    

Two strategic motivations drove the conflict.

### Arctic Gold Deposits

Geological exploration had revealed extremely large gold reserves beneath Arctic crustal formations.

Gold was required for the fabrication of the next-generation computational architecture:

**[[Parlamentum Mk.II]]**

The processing arrays of this system required enormous quantities of ultra-pure gold for:

- quantum interconnect lattices
    
- cryogenic switching networks
    
- Radiation-Hardened processor matrices ([[Radiation Hardening]])

---

### Thermal Environment

The Arctic environment provided ideal conditions for the operation of massive computational arrays.

Ambient temperatures frequently below **−40 °C** significantly reduced the cooling burden of the system’s processors.

This made locations such as [[Alaska Hill]] ideal sites for **planet-scale supercomputing infrastructure**.

The Arctic theater possessed a unique metaphysical characteristic.

The region was widely referred to as **“the land beyond the gods.”**

Within this environment:

- divine presence was significantly weakened
    
- Olympian intervention was limited
    
- divine power manifestation was drastically reduced
    

Only the most powerful demigods could sustain their abilities in such conditions.

# Demigod Participation

The defense of [[Alaska Hill]] saw the direct involvement of numerous Greek and Roman demigods whose activities had previously been confined largely to smaller mythological conflicts.

The Arctic campaign represented the first large-scale deployment of demigod forces in a **modern mechanized war environment**.

These participants included members associated with:

- [[Camp Half-Blood]]
    
- [[Camp Jupiter]]
    

The battle therefore represented a rare moment of cooperation between the Greek and Roman demigod traditions.

---

# Command of [[Percy Jackson]]

The defense of the Alaska Hill construction site was led by **[[Percy Jackson]]**, son of [[Poseidon (Greek)]].

At the time of the battle Percy served as the primary field commander for demigod forces assigned to protect the installation.

Because divine influence was diminished in the Arctic theater, Perseus was one of the few demigods who retained their divine powers during the extent of the war.

# The First Extra-Pantheonic Conflict in Centuries

The Alaska Hill campaign is widely considered the **first confirmed extra-pantheonic war in nearly two millennia**.

- - 2032.2.4.1
    
    - [[Athena]] authorizes the construction of the first continental computational fortress intended to house the emerging architecture of [[Parlamentum Mk.II]].
        
    - Geological surveys confirm massive auriferous deposits beneath the Arctic crust of [[Alaska Hill Strategic Complex]].
        
    - The region is selected due to its permanent cryogenic climate, which drastically reduces thermal load on early superintelligence arrays.
        
    - Initial planning memoranda classify the site not merely as a fortress-compute nexus, but as an experimental [[Divine-Technological Integration]] zone, combining:
        
        - [[Demigod Command Elements]]
            
        - [[Machine-Assisted Strategic Computation]]
            
        - [[Electronic Warfare (EW)]]
            
        - [[Long-Range Sensor Fusion]]
            
        - [[Ritualized Divine Authority]]
            
    - Early war-college commentary later identifies this authorization as the first formal step toward a recognizable [[UOE]]-style battlespace architecture.
        
- 2032.2.18.3
    
    - [[Athena]]-aligned technical cadres begin drafting the first Arctic emitter doctrine packages for the site.
        
    - These include primitive but scalable frameworks for:
        
        - [[Signals Intelligence (SIGINT)]]
            
        - [[Electronic Intelligence (ELINT)]]
            
        - [[Communications Intelligence (COMINT)]]
            
        - [[Electronic Support (ES)]]
            
    - The original intent is defensive: to identify hostile emitters before the installation reaches full visibility to rival pantheons and mortal proxies.
        
- 2032.3.9.2
    
    - Initial excavation begins for subterranean processor vaults and geothermal heat extraction tunnels beneath [[Alaska Hill Strategic Complex]].
        
    - Early cooling towers and cryogenic processor chambers are installed to support the future operational requirements of [[Parlamentum Mk.II]].
        
    - Construction protected by combined forces from [[Camp Half-Blood]] and [[Camp Jupiter]].
        
    - Alongside physical excavation, buried cableways and shielded sensor galleries are emplaced for future [[ELINT]] arrays and hardened [[Electronic Support Measures (ESM)]] receivers.
        
- 2032.3.27.5
    
    - First Arctic [[Wideband Receiver]] fields are activated in limited test mode.
        
    - These systems are tasked with passive detection of:
        
        - [[Radar Emissions]]
            
        - [[Navigation Systems]]
            
        - [[Weapons Guidance Systems]]
            
        - foreign [[Electronic Warfare Systems]]
            
    - Analysts later mark this as the first sustained [[Olympian]] attempt to build a regional [[Electronic Order of Battle (EOB)]] using combined divine and machine-assisted analysis.
        
- 2032.4.12.4
    
    - [[Percy Jackson]] is appointed operational commander of demigod defense forces tasked with safeguarding the construction site.
        
    - Percy deploys with an experimental combat exoskeleton integrated with the strategic intelligence construct [[Parlamentum Mk.I]].
        
    - The AI construct begins assisting in:
        
        - predictive battlefield modeling
            
        - threat detection
            
        - emissions correlation
            
        - early-warning cueing
            
    - This marks one of the earliest fielded examples of a demigod commander operating in continuous cognitive partnership with a machine intelligence during an active theater deployment.
        
- 2032.4.26.2
    
    - [[Parlamentum Mk.I]] begins continuous passive collection of non-communication electromagnetic emissions across the high Arctic approach corridors.
        
    - Early [[ELINT]] packages successfully extract:
        
        - [[Frequency]]
            
        - [[Pulse Repetition Frequency (PRF)]]
            
        - [[Pulse Width]]
            
        - [[Scan Pattern]]
            
        - [[Beam Width]]
            
        - partial [[Modulation Type]]
            
    - This enables first-generation identification of hostile surveillance radars and primitive fire-control networks operating beyond the immediate Alaskan perimeter.
        
- 2032.5.3.6
    
    - First evidence of extra-pantheonic interference detected by [[Parlamentum Mk.I]].
        
    - Signals intercepted from hostile [[Chinese Cultivator]] sects operating in Siberian territory.
        
    - Intelligence predicts an imminent attempt to destroy the Arctic construction complex.
        
    - More importantly, emissions analysis confirms that several hostile systems are not relying solely on spiritual concealment; they are using disciplined [[Electronic Emissions Control]] interspersed with sudden burst-radiation events to mask approach timing.
        
    - Later doctrinal histories cite this as the first Alaska Hill indication that future wars would be decided not only by raw divine force, but by mastery of the [[Electromagnetic Spectrum (EMS)]].
        
- 2032.5.11.4
    
    - First provisional Arctic [[Emitter Libraries]] are compiled on-site.
        
    - [[Athena]]-aligned analysts and [[Parlamentum Mk.I]] jointly categorize hostile signatures into preliminary classes:
        
        - [[Chinese Cultivator]] relay emitters
            
        - [[Shinto Pantheon]] reconnaissance signatures
            
        - Russian Slavic families
            
        - unidentified contractor-grade ECM traffic
            
    - Several unidentified signatures are later retrospectively attributed to early hidden-corporate intermediaries tied to the emerging Corporations network and associated cutout entities.
        
- 2032.5.19.2
    
    - [[Shinto Pantheon]] reconnaissance elements infiltrate northern Alaska.
        
    - Several early warning radar arrays are sabotaged through spiritual interference techniques.
        
    - Counterintelligence patrols from [[Camp Jupiter]] eliminate multiple infiltrators attempting to penetrate construction command sectors.
        
    - Post-action analysis reveals that the infiltrators synchronized spiritual concealment with targeted disruption of Arctic sensor timing windows, demonstrating one of the earliest recorded forms of cross-domain concealment combining metaphysical stealth and technical emitter suppression.
        
- 2032.5.28.1
    
    - [[Parlamentum Mk.I]] identifies hostile probing against local defensive radars through adaptive jamming and deceptive echo injection.
        
    - Athenaean analysts classify the engagement cycle in later terminology as a precursor chain of:
        
        - [[Electronic Countermeasures (ECM)]]
            
        - [[Electronic Counter-Countermeasures (ECCM)]]
            
        - early [[Electronic Counter-Counter-Countermeasures (ECCCM)]]
            
    - Although not yet formally named in 2032, Alaska Hill becomes the first archive where this escalating feedback loop is preserved in operational detail.
        
- 2032.6.7.5
    
    - Russian Arctic military elements begin mobilization along northern Canadian coastlines.
        
    - Satellite imaging reveals mechanized battalions advancing toward the Alaska corridor.
        
    - [[Parlamentum Mk.I]] predicts coalition coordination between Russian forces and cultivator sects.
        
    - Concurrent [[ELINT]] sweeps identify long-range surveillance radars and probable [[Fire Control Radar (FCR)]] systems supporting the mechanized push.
        
- 2032.6.10.3
    
    - Alaska Hill command establishes a standing Arctic [[Electronic Support (ES)]] cell.
        
    - The cell is tasked with:
        
        - continuous emitter detection
            
        - threat warning
            
        - rapid [[Emitter Identification]]
            
        - preliminary [[Direction Finding (DF)]]
            
        - cueing of strike and interception teams
            
    - This is later recognized as a direct doctrinal ancestor of formalized [[UOE]] [[Electronic Warfare (EW)]] detachments.
        
- 2032.6.14.3
    
    - First skirmish between demigod reconnaissance units and [[Chinese Cultivator]] vanguard forces occurs along a glacial ridge east of Alaska Hill.
        
    - Several cultivator elders neutralized by combined Greek and Roman demigod assault teams.
        
    - Portable hostile sensor apparatus recovered from the dead reveals hybrid doctrine:
        
        - spiritual perception lattices
            
        - radio-frequency beaconing
            
        - pulse-timed seeker cueing
            
    - This confirms that the coalition is fielding blended sensor architectures rather than purely mystical detection chains.
        
- 2032.6.21.6
    
    - [[Parlamentum Mk.I]] achieves first successful multi-source geolocation of hostile emitters using:
        
        - [[Triangulation]]
            
        - [[Time Difference of Arrival (TDOA)]]
            
        - distributed bearing comparison
            
    - These geolocated contacts are used to direct demigod raids against forward coalition observation sites.
        
- 2032.6.28.1
    
    - Coalition forces begin encirclement of the Alaska Hill region.
        
    - Hostile participants identified as:
        
        - [[Chinese Cultivator]] warbands
            
        - [[Shinto Pantheon]] combat priesthood units
            
        - Russian mechanized Arctic brigades
            
        - rogue Greek demigod factions opposing Olympian expansion
            
    - Intelligence traffic also notes irregular technical support signatures inconsistent with standard state or pantheonic formations.
        
    - These anomalous signatures are later associated with hidden corporate brokerages, black-signal contractors, and proto-network assets that would eventually consolidate under or around the [[Institute Academy]]-[[Evergreen Family]] orbit.
        
- 2032.7.3.6
    
    - Percy orders construction of underground defensive tunnel networks beneath the cooling tower complexes.
        
    - Sapping attempts by enemy engineering teams attempt to collapse geothermal shafts used for thermal venting.
        
    - Parallel to the physical fortification effort, Alaska Hill command begins hardening internal networks against emitter spoofing, burst-jam saturation, and false targeting injection.
        
- 2032.7.7.4
    
    - Two enemy tunneling operations destroyed by demigod strike teams from [[Camp Jupiter]].
        
    - Coalition siege preparations continue across surrounding glacial plateaus.
        
    - Sensor logs from the period reveal rising hostile use of:
        
        - deceptive beacon placement
            
        - false target generation
            
        - intermittent radar silence
            
        - phased reactivation to exploit defensive response lag
            
- 2032.7.11.2
    
    - Alaska Hill defenders begin rotating localized [[ECCM]] routines across the complex’s sensor network.
        
    - Implemented measures include:
        
        - frequency agility
            
        - adaptive thresholding
            
        - primitive home-on-jam cue logic
            
        - timing irregularization of defensive scanning cycles
            
    - This reduces the effectiveness of the first major hostile jamming wave, but also reveals that enemy analysts are rapidly learning the defensive response patterns.
        
- 2032.7.16.2
    
    - Full-scale assault begins on the Alaska Hill complex.
        
    - Enemy forces attempt to destroy:
        
        - cryogenic cooling towers
            
        - processor vault entrances
            
        - geothermal heat exchange systems
            
    - The opening barrage is accompanied by one of the earliest large-scale Arctic [[ECM]] offensives in the theater:
        
        - barrage jamming
            
        - deception returns
            
        - guidance interference
            
        - sensor desynchronization attempts
            
- 2032.7.17.3
    
    - Russian armored battalions breach outer defensive perimeter.
        
    - Percy redeploys demigod heavy assault units along the northern ridge to prevent encirclement.
        
    - [[Parlamentum Mk.I]] shifts from pure warning support into real-time battle management, correlating kinetic maneuvers with hostile emissions and issuing threat predictions directly to field commanders.
        
- 2032.7.18.5
    
    - [[Chinese Cultivator]] forces launch direct assault on Cooling Array Alpha.
        
    - Severe casualties reported on both sides.
        
    - Cooling infrastructure damaged but remains operational.
        
    - During the engagement, hostile systems employ coherent replay techniques and waveform conformity against local tracking systems.
        
    - Later doctrine classifies these measures as an early recognizable form of [[DRFM]]-style [[ECCCM]] behavior: not merely jamming the sensor, but imitating the sensor’s adapted expectations.
        
- 2032.7.19.4
    
    - Alaska Hill analysts identify the coalition’s exploitation of [[ECCM]] adaptation latency.
        
    - Hostile forces repeatedly wait for defensive threshold adjustments, then reinsert deceptive returns within the newly accepted logic window.
        
    - This event becomes one of the foundational historical case studies for later [[UOE]] definitions of [[Electronic Counter-Counter-Countermeasures (ECCCM)]]
        
- 2032.7.20.2
    
    - Assassination attempt against [[Percy Jackson]] conducted by coalition infiltration unit.
        
    - [[Parlamentum Mk.I]] detects anomalous thermal signatures within ventilation conduits.
        
    - Four infiltrators eliminated before reaching command bunker.
        
    - Supporting evidence suggests the infiltration window was opened by synchronized physical stealth, thermal masking, and local sensor-confusion operations across multiple domains.
        
- 2032.7.22.6
    
    - Coalition forces attempt wide flanking maneuver from western glacial basin.
        
    - Percy deploys mobile demigod cavalry units across the ice plateau.
        
    - Enemy supply convoys intercepted and destroyed.
        
    - ELINT correlation from convoy escorts reveals the first strong evidence that multiple coalition elements are sharing a partially unified threat library rather than fighting as isolated forces.
        
- 2032.7.24.3
    
    - Coalition artillery begins bombardment of primary cryogenic cooling tower network.
        
    - Destruction of towers predicted to shut down future operation of [[Parlamentum Mk.II]].
        
    - Simultaneously, hostile jamming attempts saturate local tracking filters and inject target clutter into defensive fire-control nets.
        
    - Retrospective [[UOE]] analysis cites this bombardment phase as one of the earliest major demonstrations that modern war would increasingly target not only men and materiel, but the enemy’s logic-processing layer itself.
        
- 2032.7.25.4
    
    - Percy leads counteroffensive along the tower foundations.
        
    - Close-quarters combat between demigods and cultivator elites reported across cooling platforms.
        
    - In parallel, Alaska Hill defenders begin combining:
        
        - divine battlefield intuition
            
        - AI-assisted signal discrimination
            
        - field reports from demigod scouts
            
        - passive emitter collection
            
    - This fusion of mythic perception and machine-supported sensor war is later canonized as a prototype [[UOE]] command methodology.
        
- 2032.7.27.1
    
    - Demigod assault forces break through coalition forward defensive positions.
        
    - Cooling Array Alpha, Processor Vault Entrance 3, and Geothermal Shaft Complex retaken.
        
    - Several abandoned hostile devices are recovered intact, including emitter-control packages and black-box replay units later linked to covert technical facilitators beyond the visible coalition order of battle.
