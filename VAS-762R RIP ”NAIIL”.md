The 7.62×54R [[Divine Material Radically Invasive Projectile (DMRIP)]] round “NAIIL” is a frangible multi-trocar cartridge engineered around a 8 g lead-free copper monolithic body designed to segment into eight pre-machined radial trocars upon impact. The projectile departs from conventional hollow-point geometry by using controlled structural separation channels to produce multiple simultaneous wound tracks and rapid energy dump within soft targets.

The copper body is precision-machined and heat-treated for predictable fragmentation at intermediate velocities. Trace divine-phase inclusions of [[Celestial Bronze]], [[Imperial Gold]], and [[Stygian Iron]] are integrated via [[Divine-Metallurgical Binding Impurities (DMBI)]], allowing each separated trocar to retain anti-pantheonic lethality after fragmentation. The result is simultaneous mechanical trauma and distributed divine-phase disruption across multiple penetration paths.

The projectile is optimized for rapid incapacitation against unarmored or lightly protected targets rather than armor penetration. Against [[Monsters (Greek)]], each trocar functions as an independent divine-contact vector, increasing metaphysical destabilization probability. Against [[Demigod]] and [[Legacy]] combatants, distributed fragment channels reduce regenerative consolidation. Against purely mortal targets, the round functions as a high-efficiency frangible terminal-performance munition.

## Hyper-Radiological Augmentation

Unauthorized variants involve lacing or doping projectile cores with ultra-high-activity transuranic isotopes far exceeding standard [[Advanced Depleted Uranium (ADU)]] specifications.

Reported isotopic inclusions include:

- **Californium-252 (Cf-252)** — intense spontaneous neutron emitter
    
- **Polonium-210 (Po-210)** — extreme alpha toxicity at microgram levels
    
- **Curium-244 (Cm-244)** — high alpha and gamma output
    
- **Americium-241 (Am-241)** — gamma-active contaminant

### Lethality Profile

- Microgram-scale internal exposure sufficient to cause fatal radiation syndrome in mortals
    
- Rapid marrow collapse and organ failure within days
    
- Persistent neutron flux inducing secondary activation in surrounding material
    
- Long-term contamination of battlefield terrain
    

Against [[Monsters (Greek)]] and regenerative entities, neutron flux and alpha bombardment disrupt metaphysical cohesion layers, degrading divine or chthonic stabilization matrices. Regenerative attempts accelerate cellular replication under intense ionizing exposure, increasing mutation instability and catastrophic tissue breakdown.

These rounds render impact zones hazardous to friendly forces for extended periods, especially near key points in [[Tartarus (Realm)]].

## Corrosive / Parasitic Bio-Vector Coatings

More extreme variants involve coating trocars or fragment cavities with invasive biochemical agents engineered for mythic physiology destabilization.

These compounds are not conventional pathogens. They function as:

- Hyper-aggressive hemotoxic necrotic suspensions
    
- Divine-reactive enzymatic catalysts
    
- Metabolic overdrive triggers inducing uncontrolled regeneration loops
    
### Strain I — Rapid Necrotic Collapse

- Induces immediate microvascular thrombosis
    
- Enzymatic liquefaction of connective tissue
    
- Accelerated hemotoxic degradation
    

Observed effect:  
Localized impact zones undergo rapid blackening, desiccation, and structural collapse within minutes. In mortals, this resembles fulminant gangrenous necrosis. In regenerative entities, tissue attempts to regrow into an actively degrading matrix, compounding structural failure.

---

### Strain II — Hyper-Regenerative Overdraw

- Forces uncontrolled activation of regenerative pathways
    
- Amplifies mitochondrial output and cellular replication rates
    
- Disrupts regulatory apoptosis cycles
    

Observed effect:  
Tissue regrows at extreme velocity but without metabolic equilibrium. Cellular proliferation exceeds energy availability, resulting in systemic exhaustion. In divine or semi-divine targets, this induces cascading overdraw of internal vitality reserves until collapse or catastrophic rupture.

Regeneration becomes self-destructive.

---

### Strain III — Corrosive Catalytic Decomposition

- Deploys acidogenic enzymatic complexes
    
- Targets protein folding integrity
    
- Breaks down structural collagen matrices
    

Observed effect:  
Soft tissues destabilize into slurry-like breakdown. Armor interfaces and divine-infused flesh exhibit surface pitting and lattice weakening. Metallic divine inclusions within the projectile enhance catalytic propagation at the wound boundary.

---

### Strain IV — Neural Disruption Vector

- Attacks ion-channel regulation
    
- Induces uncontrolled synaptic firing
    
- Triggers seizure cascade or paralysis
    

Observed effect:  
Immediate motor dysfunction. In higher-tier entities, divine focus fractures under neural instability, reducing ability to channel regenerative or offensive power.
