## Description

The Hexenlaufgerät Erweiterte Thaumaturgische Bewegungsplattform für Runische Vorfeldkonstruktion und Kontinuierliche Glyphenvernetzungseinheit (Tochter des Elysium Serienstandard–Variante IX Beta-Null Mk.CLVI is a foundational wargear component issued to stormtroopers of [[Hecate]] lineage, designed to automate **low-order runic inscription during movement**.

Integrated into the **sole structure of standard-issue combat boots**, the system continuously engraves **pre-coded glyph chains and runic lines** into terrain at extreme temperatures, allowing the operator to externalize foundational magical work while maintaining full combat mobility.

This removes the need for manual inscription of:

- Base circle frameworks
    
- Stabilization glyphs
    
- Directional channel lines

## Operational Mechanism

### Thermal Engraving Core

- Embedded micro-reactor elevates sole interface to:
    
    - **≥ 1000°C (standard operational baseline)**
        
- Heat is channeled through patterned emitter arrays

### Glyph Projection Lattice

- Pre-loaded runic sequences are encoded into the sole
    
- Each step imprints:
    
    - Partial glyphs
        
    - Continuous line segments
        
- Movement completes full constructs over time

## Limitations

- Requires **predictable terrain contact**
    
- Ineffective in:
    
    - Zero-gravity environments
        
    - Non-solid substrates
        
- Pre-loaded glyph sets limit on-the-fly flexibility

- **Thermal Dissipation Fields**
